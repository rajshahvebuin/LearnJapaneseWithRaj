もんだい2 Q1

ことしの なつは やまへ いきます。Kotoshi no natsu wa yama e ikimasu.

EN: This summer I will go to the mountains.
HI: इस गर्मी में मैं पहाड़ों पर जाऊँगा।
GU: આ ઉનાળે હું પર્વત પર જઈશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ヨyothe katakana "yo" — not a kanjiकताकाना "यो" — कांजी नहींકાતાકાના "યો" — કાંજી નથી
2出de(ru)to go out (出る)बाहर निकलना (出る)બહાર નીકળવું (出る)
3川kawariverनदीનદી
4山yamamountain ✓पहाड़પર્વત

Why: やま = 山 "mountain". The kanji is a picture of three peaks. 出 looks like two 山 stacked, but it means "go out" (でる). 川 (かわ, river) is a picture of flowing water. ヨ is a katakana letter, not a kanji.

もんだい2 Q4

へやに しゃわーは ありますか。Heya ni shawaa wa arimasu ka.

EN: Is there a shower in the room?
HI: क्या कमरे में शावर है?
GU: શું રૂમમાં શાવર છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ツェワーtsewaawrong: ツ (tsu) not シ, small ェ not ャगलत: シ की जगह ツ, ャ की जगह ェખોટું: シ ને બદલે ツ, ャ ને બદલે ェ
2シャウーshauuwrong: ウ (u) not ワ (wa)गलत: ワ की जगह ウખોટું: ワ ને બદલે ウ
3ツャワーtsu + ya + waawrong: ツ (tsu) not シ (shi)गलत: シ की जगह ツખોટું: シ ને બદલે ツ
4シャワーshawaashower ✓शावरશાવર

Why: しゃわー = シ + small ャ + ワ + ー = シャワー. The main trap is シ (shi) vs ツ (tsu): in シ the short strokes are on the left and the long stroke goes up from the bottom; in ツ the short strokes are on top and the long stroke goes down. A long vowel in katakana is written with ー.

GU: બાજુના ઘરની બિલાડીના પગ કાળા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

2目meeyeआँखઆંખ
3手tehandहाथહાથ

もんだい2 Q6

えいがかんで おもしろい えいがを みました。Eigakan de omoshiroi eiga o mimashita.

EN: I watched an interesting film at the cinema.
HI: मैंने सिनेमा हॉल में एक दिलचस्प फ़िल्म देखी।
GU: મેં સિનેમા હૉલમાં એક રસપ્રદ ફિલ્મ જોઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見ましたmimashitawatched, saw ✓देखाજોયું
2具ましたgu + mashitawrong: 具 (ぐ, tool; ingredient) is not a verbगलत: 具 (औज़ार; सामग्री) क्रिया नहींખોટું: 具 (સાધન; સામગ્રી) ક્રિયાપદ નથી
3目ましたme + mashitawrong: 目 (め, eye) is a noun, not "see"गलत: 目 (आँख) संज्ञा है, "देखना" नहींખોટું: 目 (આંખ) નામ છે, "જોવું" નહીં

Why: みる "to see, watch" = 見る, so みました = 見ました. 見 is an eye (目) on legs (儿). The traps all start with the 目 shape: 目 alone (eye), 貝 (shellfish, 目 + two short legs) and 具 (tool, 目 + a long base). Only 見 is the verb "to see".

もんだい2 Q7

わたしの すんでいる まちは みせが すくないです。Watashi no sunde iru machi wa mise ga sukunai desu.

EN: The town where I live has few shops.
HI: जिस शहर में मैं रहता हूँ, वहाँ दुकानें कम हैं।
GU: હું જે શહેરમાં રહું છું ત્યાં દુકાનો ઓછી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1居i(ru) / kyoto be, to stay (居る) — not N5होना, रहना (居る)હોવું, રહેવું (居る)
2庁chougovernment office — not N5सरकारी दफ़्तरસરકારી કચેરી
3店miseshop ✓दुकानદુકાન
4痁(no N5 reading)a very rare character (illness radical 疒 + 占), not みせबहुत दुर्लभ अक्षर, みせ नहींખૂબ દુર્લભ અક્ષર, みせ નહીં

Why: みせ = 店 "shop". 店 = 广 (a roof with one side wall) + 占. Option 4 puts 占 under 疒 (the "sickness" roof, with two dots on the left); that is not 店. 庁 has the right roof but 丁 inside. 居 has a different roof (尸).

もんだい2 Q8

こんど、いっしょに れすとらんに 行きましょう。Kondo, issho ni resutoran ni ikimashou.

EN: Next time, let's go to a restaurant together.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ルストラソrusutorasowrong: ル (ru) not レ, ソ (so) not ンगलत: レ की जगह ル, ン की जगह ソખોટું: レ ને બદલે ル, ン ને બદલે ソ
2ルストランrusutoranwrong: ル (ru) not レ (re)गलत: レ की जगह ルખોટું: レ ને બદલે ル
3レストラソresutorasowrong: ソ (so) not ン (n)गलत: ン की जगह ソખોટું: ン ને બદલે ソ

GU: સાતોજી જાતજાતના રસ્તા જાણે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1道michiroad ✓रास्ताરસ્તો

シ shi / ツ tsushi / tsutwo lettersシ: dots on the left, long stroke goes up. ツ: dots on top, long stroke goes down.シャワー
ン n / ソ son / sotwo lettersン: long stroke goes up from bottom-left. ソ: long stroke comes down from top-right.レストラン
レ re / ル rure / rutwo lettersレ is one stroke. ル is two strokes.レストラン・エレベーター
タ ta / ク kuta / kutwo lettersタ has a short line inside; ク does not.エレベーター
ワ wa / ウ uwa / utwo lettersウ has a short stroke on top; ワ does not.シャワー
Exam trap: in 表記 the wrong options are usually not real words, so you cannot guess by meaning. Look at one part at a time: the top, the inside, the roof, the stroke direction. When the options are all real kanji (Q2 南／西／北／東, Q5 足／目／手／耳), the sentence fits every option — you must simply know the kanji.
