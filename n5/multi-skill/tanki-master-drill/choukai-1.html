課題理解 asks what someone will do (これから / はじめに). Listen for words that change the order: その前に, 先に, 〜たあとで. ポイント理解 asks for one point (where, when, which, how many). In both types, the first idea is often changed later. Wait for the final decision. (added, not in book)

Exam trap: in N5 listening, almost every wrong option is said in the talk: the first time (11時), the first place (駅), the first number (1本, 12本), the first idea (旅行会社). Do not choose the first thing you hear. Keep listening until someone says いいですね, お願いします or 大丈夫です — that is the final decision.
