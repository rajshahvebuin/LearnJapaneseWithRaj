短期マスター N5

Following 短期マスター 日本語能力試験ドリル N5 (Tanki Master JLPT Drill N5, 凡人社) section by section. It is a short drill book that covers every JLPT section in one volume: 文字・語彙, 文法, 読解 and 聴解. The 練習問題 (practice questions) come first, then a half-length まとめのテスト (review test). Every page gives the tested words or patterns, every question with the answer from the book's 別冊 key, and confusion pairs. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Work through the 練習問題 first, then take the まとめのテスト. The book suggests about 13 minutes for 文字・語彙, 25 minutes for 文法・読解 and 15 minutes for 聴解 (p.45).
  • If time is short, take the まとめのテスト first, then go back to the 練習問題 for the sections where you made the most mistakes.
  • Reading and listening pages give a short summary of each text or script in our own words, not the book's text, and quote only the lines each question needs.
