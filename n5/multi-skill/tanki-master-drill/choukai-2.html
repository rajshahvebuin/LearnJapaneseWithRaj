The pictures are the book's; our descriptions of them are ours.

もんだい3 発話表現 (p.41–43) — CD Tracks 17–22

もんだい3 1ばん CD, Track 18

Picture (described): At the front door of a house. A young girl with a school backpack and a small bag is walking out. Her mother, in an apron, smiles and waves. The arrow points to the girl.

Key line (the question)

学校に 行きます。何と 言いますか。Gakkou ni ikimasu. Nan to iimasu ka.

EN: She is going to school. What does she say?
HI: वह स्कूल जा रही है। वह क्या कहती है?
GU: તે શાળાએ જાય છે. તે શું કહે છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いってらっしゃい。Itterasshai.See you, take care.जाओ, अच्छे से आना।જાઓ, સંભાળીને આવજો.
2いってきます。Itte kimasu.I'm off! ✓मैं जाकर आती हूँ।હું જઈને આવું છું.

もんだい3 4ばん CD, Track 21

Picture (described): Outside. A young woman walks after a young man and holds out a pen, as if asking "Is this yours?". The young man, with a shoulder bag, raises one hand and looks back at her. The arrow points to the young man.

Key line (the question)

ペンを 見て 答えます。何と 言いますか。Pen o mite kotaemasu. Nan to iimasu ka.

EN: He looks at the pen and answers. What does he say?
HI: वह पेन देखकर जवाब देता है। वह क्या कहता है?
GU: તે પેન જોઈને જવાબ આપે છે. તે શું કહે છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1それ、私のじゃ ありませんよ。Sore, watashi no ja arimasen yo.That's not mine. ✓वह मेरा नहीं है।એ મારું નથી.

喫茶店で 飲み物を 頼みます。何と 言いますか。Kissaten de nomimono o tanomimasu. Nan to iimasu ka.

GU: તે કૅફેમાં પીણું મંગાવે છે. તે શું કહે છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1これ、コーヒーですよね。Kore, koohii desu yo ne.This is coffee, right?यह कॉफ़ी है न?આ કૉફી છે ને?

もんだい4 1ばん CD, Track 24

F：トイレは どこですか。Toire wa doko desu ka.

EN: Where is the toilet?
HI: शौचालय कहाँ है?
GU: શૌચાલય ક્યાં છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いいえ、いません。Iie, imasen.No, (he) isn't here.नहीं, (वह) यहाँ नहीं है।ના, (તે) અહીં નથી.
2教室ですよ。Kyoushitsu desu yo.It's a classroom.वह कक्षा है।એ વર્ગખંડ છે.
3あそこです。Asoko desu.It's over there. ✓वहाँ है।ત્યાં છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.3). Why (added, not in book): どこ asks "where?", so the reply is a place word: ここ / そこ / あそこ. Option 1 answers a yes/no question about a person (いる). Option 2 names a different room, which does not tell her where the toilet is.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Answer 2 (別冊 p.3). Why (added, not in book): 〜ても いいですか asks for permission. To give permission, say どうぞ. どうも and ありがとう are "thanks" — that is what he says after she says どうぞ, so they belong to the wrong person.
