1 Grammar Points

(added, not in book) One short point for each pattern that a question depends on, in question order. All example sentences marked "(added, not in book)" are ours.

【2-Q1】 Nは どれですか

polite
ReadingN wa dore desu ka
Meaning (EN)"Which one is N?" (choosing from three or more things)
Meaning (HI)"N कौन-सा है?" (तीन या ज़्यादा चीज़ों में से)
Meaning (GU)"N કયું છે?" (ત્રણ કે વધુ વસ્તુઓમાંથી)
Connection (接続)N＋は＋どれ＋ですか
Typical useThe topic (は) comes first, the question word どれ comes last, before ですか. Answer with これです／それです／あれです.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(added, not in book) あなたの かさは どれですか。Anata no kasa wa dore desu ka.Which one is your umbrella?आपकी छतरी कौन-सी है?તમારી છત્રી કઈ છે?

【2-Q2】 N が ＋ number-counter ＋ あります

neutral
ReadingN ga mittsu arimasu
Meaning (EN)"There are three N." The number goes after が, right before the verb.
Meaning (HI)"N तीन हैं।" संख्या が के बाद आती है।
Meaning (GU)"N ત્રણ છે." સંખ્યા が પછી આવે છે.
Connection (接続)N＋が＋三つ＋あります　／　N1が 三つと N2が あります
Typical useCounters: 〜つ for things (ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ), 〜人 for people, 〜本 for long things. Join two lists with と.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(added, not in book) つくえの 上に りんごが 二つ あります。Tsukue no ue ni ringo ga futatsu arimasu.There are two apples on the desk.मेज़ पर दो सेब हैं।ટેબલ પર બે સફરજન છે.

Meaning (GU)"(જગ્યા) ~ કરવા જવું" — に હેતુ બતાવે છે

Typical useUsed only with moving verbs: 行く, 来る, かえる. The same pattern is in もんだい3 blank 5 (食べに 来てください).

【2-Q5】 Nが 上手で、な-adj な N

neutral
Readinguta ga jouzu de, hansamu na hito
Meaning (EN)"good at singing and handsome" — な-adjective + で joins two descriptions ("and")
Meaning (HI)"गाने में अच्छा और सुंदर" — な-विशेषण + で "और" जोड़ता है
Meaning (GU)"ગાવામાં સારો અને દેખાવડો" — な-વિશેષણ + で "અને" જોડે છે
Connection (接続)Nが 上手だ → Nが 上手で　·　い-adj → 〜くて（安くて）
Typical useThe skill takes が: うたが 上手 (not を). A な-adjective before a noun keeps な: ハンサムな 人.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(added, not in book) この へやは しずかで、きれいです。Kono heya wa shizuka de, kirei desu.This room is quiet and clean.यह कमरा शांत और साफ़ है।આ ઓરડો શાંત અને સ્વચ્છ છે.

【2-Q6】 〜たり 〜たり します

neutral
Reading~tari ~tari shimasu
Meaning (EN)"do things like A and B" (a few examples, not a full list)
Meaning (HI)"A और B जैसे काम करना" (कुछ उदाहरण)
Meaning (GU)"A અને B જેવાં કામ કરવાં" (થોડાં ઉદાહરણ)
Connection (接続)V-た form ＋り：読んだ→読んだり、した→したり　＋ する（します）at the end
Typical useThe sentence always ends with する／します. Each たり comes after its own object: 本を 読んだり、さんぽを したり.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(added, not in book) よるは テレビを 見たり、おんがくを 聞いたり します。Yoru wa terebi o mitari, ongaku o kiitari shimasu.At night I do things like watch TV and listen to music.रात को मैं टीवी देखना, गाने सुनना जैसे काम करता हूँ।રાત્રે હું ટીવી જોવું, ગીતો સાંભળવાં જેવાં કામ કરું છું.

【2-Q7 · 3-[2]】 あまり 〜ない／〜ません

neutral
Readingamari ~nai / ~masen
Meaning (EN)"not very ~, not much" — あまり always needs a negative ending
Meaning (HI)"ज़्यादा ~ नहीं" — あまり के साथ हमेशा नकारात्मक
Meaning (GU)"બહુ ~ નહીં" — あまり સાથે હંમેશાં નકારાત્મક
Connection (接続)あまり ＋ な-adj では ありません／い-adj くないです／V-ません
Typical useきれいでは ありません (な-adj negative), 上手では ありません. Never あまり 上手です.

【3-[1]】 (country) から 来ました

self-introduction
Reading(Igirisu) kara kimashita
Meaning (EN)"I came from (country)" = "I'm from (country)" — past tense, because you already came
Meaning (HI)"मैं (देश) से आया/आई हूँ"
Meaning (GU)"હું (દેશ)થી આવ્યો/આવી છું"
Connection (接続)国＋から 来ました
Typical useA fixed phrase in every じこしょうかい: はじめまして。〜です。〜から 来ました。どうぞ よろしく。

【3-[3] · 3-[5]】 〜ませんか ／ 〜て ください

polite
Reading~masen ka / ~te kudasai
Meaning (EN)〜ませんか = "won't you ~? / shall we ~?" (a kind invitation). 〜て ください = "please ~" (a request).
Meaning (HI)〜ませんか = "क्या आप ~ नहीं चलेंगे?" (न्योता)। 〜て ください = "कृपया ~ कीजिए"।
Meaning (GU)〜ませんか = "શું તમે ~ નહીં આવો?" (આમંત્રણ). 〜て ください = "કૃપા કરીને ~ કરો".
Connection (接続)いっしょに ＋ V-ませんか　·　V-て ＋ ください
Typical useいっしょに ("together") is a strong sign of an invitation → 〜ませんか.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(added, not in book) いっしょに おちゃを のみませんか。Issho ni ocha o nomimasen ka.Won't you have some tea with me?क्या आप मेरे साथ चाय नहीं पिएँगे?શું તમે મારી સાથે ચા નહીં પીઓ?

【3-[4]】 そして

connector
Readingsoshite
Meaning (EN)"and, and also" — adds one more good (or bad) point in the same direction
Meaning (HI)"और" — उसी दिशा की एक और बात जोड़ता है
Meaning (GU)"અને" — એ જ દિશાની બીજી વાત ઉમેરે છે
Connection (接続)Sentence。そして、Sentence。
Typical useCompare: でも = "but" (opposite idea), だから = "so" (result), それでは = "well then" (moving on).

もんだい2 文の文法2 — put the words in order and choose the one for ★

＿★＿に 入る ものは どれですか。１・２・３・４から いちばん いいものを一つ えらんでください。★ ni hairu mono wa dore desu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: Which word goes in ★? Choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: ★ में कौन-सा शब्द आएगा? 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: ★ માં કયો શબ્દ આવશે? 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

How ★ questions work (added, not in book): there are four blanks and four word cards. Put all four cards in order to make a correct sentence. Then answer with the number of the card that sits on ★. Tip: start with small words that must touch something — particles like は, が, へ, に need a word before them.

CardJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はwa(topic particle)(विषय कण)(વિષય કણ)
2どれdorewhich one ✓कौन-साકયું
3本honbookकिताबપુસ્તક

A「あたらしい 本は どれですか。」 B「これです。」A: "Atarashii hon wa dore desu ka." B: "Kore desu."

Why: ★ = 2 (別冊 解答 p.2). Order 3 → 4 → 2 → 1 (worked out by us). わたしの needs a noun: わたしの 学校は. きれいでは ありません is the negative of きれいです, and あまり goes before it ("not very"). ★ is the 3rd slot: きれいでは.

もんだい3 文章の文法 — fill the blanks in two short texts

１から５に 何を 入れますか。ぶんしょうの いみを かんがえて、１・２・３・４から いちばん いいものを一つ えらんでください。1 kara 5 ni nani o iremasu ka. Bunshou no imi o kangaete, 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: What goes in blanks 1–5? Think about the meaning of the text and choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: 1 से 5 में क्या आएगा? लेख का अर्थ सोचकर 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: 1 થી 5 માં શું આવશે? લખાણનો અર્થ વિચારીને 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

文章の文法 — two self-introductions (じこしょうかい) by ナンシー and ワン

もんだい3 — texts

ナンシーさんと ワンさんは あした じこしょうかいを します。Nanshii-san to Wan-san wa ashita jikoshoukai o shimasu.

Summary (not the book's text) — EN: Nancy and Wang will introduce themselves tomorrow, so each wrote a short text. (1) Nancy is from the UK. She likes songs in English and Japanese, but she is not very good at Japanese songs. She wants to go to karaoke and invites everyone. (2) Wang studied a little Japanese in China, knows all the hiragana but not yet all the katakana, and from next week will work every afternoon at a restaurant near here. The food there is cheap and tasty, so Wang asks everyone to come and eat.
HI: नैंसी और वांग कल अपना परिचय देंगी, इसलिए दोनों ने छोटा लेख लिखा। (1) नैंसी इंग्लैंड से है। उसे अंग्रेज़ी और जापानी दोनों गाने पसंद हैं, पर जापानी गाने वह ज़्यादा अच्छा नहीं गाती। वह कराओके जाना चाहती है और सबको बुलाती है। (2) वांग ने चीन में थोड़ी जापानी पढ़ी। उसे सारे हिरागाना आते हैं, पर कताकाना अभी पूरे नहीं। अगले हफ़्ते से वह रोज़ दोपहर पास के एक रेस्टोरेंट में काम करेगी। वहाँ का खाना सस्ता और स्वादिष्ट है, इसलिए वह सबको खाने आने को कहती है।
GU: નેન્સી અને વાંગ કાલે પોતાનો પરિચય આપશે, એટલે બંનેએ નાનું લખાણ લખ્યું. (1) નેન્સી ઇંગ્લૅન્ડથી છે. તેને અંગ્રેજી અને જાપાની બંને ગીતો ગમે છે, પણ જાપાની ગીતો તે બહુ સારાં ગાતી નથી. તે કરાઓકે જવા માગે છે અને બધાંને બોલાવે છે. (2) વાંગે ચીનમાં થોડું જાપાની શીખ્યું. તેને બધા હિરાગાના આવડે છે, પણ કાતાકાના હજી બધા નહીં. આવતા અઠવાડિયાથી તે રોજ બપોરે પાસેની એક રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં કામ કરશે. ત્યાંનું ખાવાનું સસ્તું અને સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે, એટલે તે બધાંને જમવા આવવા કહે છે.

Key word: じこしょうかい (jikoshoukai) — self-introduction (आत्म-परिचय / સ્વ-પરિચય). (added, not in book)

もんだい3 ［1］ (ナンシー)

みなさん、こんにちは。ナンシーです。イギリスから ［１］。Minasan, konnichiwa. Nanshii desu. Igirisu kara [1].

EN: Hello, everyone. I'm Nancy. I came from England (the UK).
HI: सबको नमस्ते। मैं नैंसी हूँ। मैं इंग्लैंड से आई हूँ।
GU: બધાંને નમસ્તે. હું નેન્સી છું. હું ઇંગ્લૅન્ડથી આવી છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1来ませんでしたkimasen deshitadid not comeनहीं आईન આવી
2来ますkimasuwill come / comeआऊँगी / आती हूँઆવીશ / આવું છું
3来ましたkimashitacame ✓आईઆવી
4来ませんkimasenwill not comeनहीं आऊँगीનહીં આવું

Why: Nancy is already in Japan, so the coming is finished → past: 来ました. 〜から 来ました is the set phrase for "I'm from ~". The negatives (1, 4) make no sense, and 来ます is future.

もんだい3 ［2］ (ナンシー)

EN: But I am not very good at Japanese songs.
HI: लेकिन जापानी गाने मैं ज़्यादा अच्छा नहीं गाती।
GU: પણ જાપાની ગીતો હું બહુ સારાં ગાતી નથી.

Why: Two signs. でも ("but") shows the opposite of "I like songs". あまり must be followed by a negative. Only option 1 is negative. Option 4 is also plain style, which does not match the です／ます text.

もんだい3 ［3］ (ナンシー)

みなさん、わたしと いっそに カラオケへ ［３］。Minasan, watashi to issoni [sic, issho ni] karaoke e [3].

EN: Everyone, won't you go to karaoke with me?
HI: दोस्तो, क्या आप मेरे साथ कराओके नहीं चलेंगे?
GU: મિત્રો, શું તમે મારી સાથે કરાઓકે નહીં આવો?

Why: She speaks to みなさん and says "with me", so this is an invitation → 〜ませんか. 行きたいです is what she said in the sentence before; it does not ask the others anything. Note: the book prints いっそに; it should be いっしょに ("together"). This looks like a typo in the re-typed page (our note).

EN: And the restaurant's food is cheap and tasty.
HI: और रेस्टोरेंट का खाना सस्ता और स्वादिष्ट है।
