basic

Meaning (EN)"is not ~" for い-adjectives

もんだい1 文の文法1 — choose the best word for the blank

（　　）に 何を 入れますか。１・２・３・４から いちばん いいものを 一つ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: What goes in the blank? Choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: रिक्त स्थान में क्या आएगा? 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: ખાલી જગ્યામાં શું આવશે? 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

もんだい1 Q1

あの お店は あまり（　　）よ。Ano omise wa amari (___) yo.

Full sentence: あの お店は あまり おいしくないよ。Ano omise wa amari oishikunai yo.
EN: That shop's food is not very good.
HI: उस दुकान का खाना ज़्यादा स्वादिष्ट नहीं है।
GU: તે દુકાનનું ખાવાનું બહુ સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おいしいoishiiis tastyस्वादिष्ट हैસ્વાદિષ્ટ છે
2おいしかったoishikattawas tastyस्वादिष्ट थाસ્વાદિષ્ટ હતું
3おいしくないoishikunaiis not tasty ✓स्वादिष्ट नहीं हैસ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી
4おいしじゃないoishi janaiwrong form (not a real word)गलत रूपખોટું રૂપ

Why: あまり means "not very" and always needs a negative after it. おいしい is an い-adjective, so its negative is おいしくない. じゃない is only for な-adjectives and nouns, so 4 is wrong.

もんだい1 Q2

きょうしつが（　　）なりました。Kyoushitsu ga (___) narimashita.

Full sentence: きょうしつが しずかに なりました。Kyoushitsu ga shizuka ni narimashita.
EN: The classroom became quiet.
HI: कक्षा शांत हो गई।
GU: વર્ગ શાંત થઈ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しずかなshizuka naquiet (before a noun)शांत (संज्ञा से पहले)શાંત (સંજ્ઞા પહેલાં)
2しずかでshizuka dequiet and …शांत और …શાંત અને …
3しずかだったshizuka dattawas quietशांत थाશાંત હતું
4しずかにshizuka niquiet(ly) — form before なる ✓शांत (なる से पहले)શાંત (なる પહેલાં)

Why: "Become ~" with a な-adjective is 〜に なる: しずかに なる. な is for a noun after it, で joins to another word, だった ends a sentence. None of them can sit before なる.

もんだい1 Q3

子どもが びょうき（　　）学校を 休みました。Kodomo ga byouki (___) gakkou o yasumimashita.

Full sentence: 子どもが びょうきで 学校を 休みました。Kodomo ga byouki de gakkou o yasumimashita.
EN: My child missed school because of illness.
HI: बच्चा बीमारी की वजह से स्कूल नहीं गया।
GU: બાળક બીમારીને કારણે શાળાએ ન ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にnito, at (place, time, goal)को, परને, પર
2でdebecause of ✓की वजह सेના કારણે
3がgasubject markerकर्ता चिह्नકર્તા ચિહ્ન
4はwatopic markerविषय चिह्नવિષય ચિહ્ન

Why: びょうき (illness) is the reason for missing school. A noun + で can show a reason: びょうきで, かぜで (because of a cold). に would make びょうき a goal, which makes no sense.

Why: "Being alone" is the reason it was boring. 一人 is a noun, and "because + noun" is noun ＋ だ ＋ から: 一人だから. With only から (2), 一人から would mean "from one person". のから and にから are not correct Japanese.

もんだい3 ［5］

みなさん、［５］。Minasan, [5].

Full sentence: みなさん、いっしょに うみへ 行きましょう。Minasan, issho ni umi e ikimashou.
EN: Everyone, let's go to the sea together.
HI: सब लोग, चलो साथ में समुद्र चलें।
GU: બધા, ચાલો સાથે દરિયે જઈએ.

Why: The sentence begins みなさん、("Everyone,") — the writer is speaking to the readers, so an invitation fits: 〜ましょう "let's ~". It also fixes the problem from blank 4: alone it was boring, so "let's go together". Options 1 and 3 are about the writer only, and 4 gives a reason for nothing.
