1 Points — how each N5 reading question works

For every reading question (added, not in book)
  • Read the question first. Then read the text looking for that one thing. HI: पहले प्रश्न पढ़ें, फिर पाठ में वही एक बात खोजें। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન વાંચો, પછી લખાણમાં એ જ એક વાત શોધો.
  • Who is 「わたし」? Questions often ask what 「わたし」 (the writer) did. Don't mix the writer up with friends or family in the text. HI: प्रश्न अक्सर पूछते हैं कि "मैं" (लेखक) ने क्या किया — लेखक को दोस्तों या परिवार से न मिलाएँ। GU: પ્રશ્ન ઘણી વાર પૂછે છે કે "હું" (લેખક)એ શું કર્યું — લેખકને મિત્રો કે કુટુંબ સાથે ન ભેળવો.
  • Time words — きょう, あした, 〜か月前, 〜の あとで. A true fact at the wrong time is a wrong answer. HI: समय के शब्दों पर ध्यान दें — सही बात ग़लत समय पर हो तो उत्तर ग़लत है। GU: સમયના શબ્દો પર ધ્યાન આપો — સાચી વાત ખોટા સમયે હોય તો જવાબ ખોટો છે.
  • Reasons — 〜から and 〜が (but) show why something happened. HI: 〜から (क्योंकि) और 〜が (लेकिन) कारण बताते हैं। GU: 〜から (કારણ કે) અને 〜が (પણ) કારણ બતાવે છે.
問題4 内容理解（短文） — three short texts, one question each

EN: Each text is 2–5 lines: a diary, a description, or a note left for someone. Wrong options mix up who (friend / sister) and what (film / DVD). For a note, find the request sentences (〜て ください) and their order.   HI: हर पाठ 2–5 पंक्तियों का होता है — डायरी, वर्णन या किसी के लिए छोड़ा गया नोट। ग़लत विकल्प "कौन" और "क्या" को मिला देते हैं। नोट में 〜て ください वाले अनुरोध और उनका क्रम खोजें।   GU: દરેક લખાણ 2–5 લીટીનું હોય છે — ડાયરી, વર્ણન કે કોઈ માટે મૂકેલી નોંધ. ખોટા વિકલ્પ "કોણ" અને "શું"ને ભેળવી દે છે. નોંધમાં 〜て ください વાળી વિનંતીઓ અને તેમનો ક્રમ શોધો.

問題5 内容理解（中文） — one longer story, two questions

EN: An underlined word (here こまりました) is asked about — the reason is usually in the sentence just before it. The second question is often about what happens next (あした…).   HI: रेखांकित शब्द के बारे में प्रश्न होता है — कारण अक्सर ठीक पहले वाले वाक्य में होता है। दूसरा प्रश्न अक्सर आगे क्या होगा (कल…) पर होता है।   GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ વિશે પ્રશ્ન હોય છે — કારણ ઘણી વાર તરત પહેલાંના વાક્યમાં હોય છે. બીજો પ્રશ્ન ઘણી વાર આગળ શું થશે (કાલે…) વિશે હોય છે.

問題6 情報検索 — a flyer or notice, find the fact

EN: Don't read everything. Pick the key words from the question (here: toilet paper, meat, vegetables, the same day) and find each one on the flyer. Check the dates and days of the week: 月 火 水 木 金 土 日.   HI: सब कुछ न पढ़ें। प्रश्न से मुख्य शब्द चुनें और हर एक को पर्चे पर खोजें। तारीख़ और हफ़्ते के दिन जाँचें।   GU: બધું ન વાંચો. પ્રશ્નમાંથી મુખ્ય શબ્દો લો અને દરેકને પત્રિકામાં શોધો. તારીખ અને અઠવાડિયાના દિવસ તપાસો.

(1) A shopping day (kaimono) — p.22

Passage summary (not the book's text): The writer went shopping with a friend today. They wanted the DVD of a film they had seen a few months ago. They plan to watch the DVD with friends and their older sister.
HI (सार): लेखक आज एक दोस्त के साथ ख़रीदारी करने गया। उसे कुछ महीने पहले देखी फ़िल्म की DVD चाहिए थी। वह यह DVD दोस्तों और बड़ी बहन के साथ देखेगा।
GU (સાર): લેખક આજે એક મિત્ર સાથે ખરીદી કરવા ગયો. તેને થોડા મહિના પહેલાં જોયેલી ફિલ્મની DVD જોઈતી હતી. તે આ DVD મિત્રો અને મોટી બહેન સાથે જોશે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

How the wrong options try to catch you (all added, not in book):

QuestionTrapExampleHow to beat it
Q27Wrong person, wrong thing (2 × 2 options)友だち／姉 · えいが／DVDtomodachi / ane · eiga / DVDCheck two things: who went with "me" today, and what they did today.
Q27Right fact, wrong time3か月前に 見たsankagetsu mae ni mitaThe film was seen months ago — not "today".
Q28Now vs wish今の へや ／ 大きい 本だなが ほしいima no heya / ookii hondana ga hoshii〜が ほしい = want (not yet have). Pick the room as it is now.
Q29Copies vs bookコピーは 南さんに · 本は 机の 上にkopii wa Minami-san ni · hon wa tsukue no ue niMatch each request to its thing (は marks the topic).
Q29Whose "わたし"?わたしの 机watashi no tsukueIn a note, わたし = the person who wrote it (山口先生).
Q30True but not the reasonつかれました ／ かさが ありませんでしたtsukaremashita / kasa ga arimasen deshitaLook just before the underlined word.
Q31Money or umbrella?お金は いりませんokane wa irimasenいりません = not needed. No money changes hands.
Q32Only some items cheap金・土 やさい（にく なし）kin, do yasai (niku nashi)Every item must be cheap on the same day — tick each one.
Exam trap 1 — "it's in the text" is not enough: at N5 most wrong options use words that really appear in the text. Ask "does it answer this question?" — right person, right time, right thing.
Exam trap 2 — notes and messages: in a note, わたし is the writer, and each 〜て ください is a separate job. Number the jobs in order (copy → give copies → put the book back) before you look at the options.
Exam trap 3 — flyers: two kinds of dates mix on one flyer — fixed dates (6月11日〜14日) and every-week days (毎週 水・木). Find the days where all conditions overlap.
