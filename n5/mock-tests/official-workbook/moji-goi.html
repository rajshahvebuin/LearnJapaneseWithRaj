問題1 — 漢字読み: how do you read the kanji? (1–10)

EN: How is the underlined word written in hiragana? Choose the best one. HI: रेखांकित शब्द हिरागाना में कैसे लिखा जाता है? सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ હિરાગાનામાં કેવી રીતે લખાય? સૌથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. (p.5) — 例: 大きな → おおきな (answer 1).

Q1

先週 デパートに かいものに いきました。Senshuu depaato ni kaimono ni ikimashita.

EN: Last week I went shopping at a department store.
HI: पिछले हफ़्ते मैं डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर में ख़रीदारी करने गया।
GU: ગયા અઠવાડિયે હું ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરમાં ખરીદી કરવા ગયો.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1せんしゅsenshuathlete (選手) — short vowelखिलाड़ी (ग़लत)ખેલાડી (ખોટું)
2せんしゅうsenshuulast week ✓पिछला हफ़्ताગયું અઠવાડિયું
3ぜんしゅzenshunot a word(शब्द नहीं)(શબ્દ નથી)

Answer 2 (正答表 p.52). Why (our explanation): 先 = せん (no ゛), 週 = しゅう with a long u. Watch both traps at once: dakuten (せ／ぜ) and long vowel (しゅ／しゅう).

Q2

ごはんの 後で さんぽします。Gohan no ato de sanpo shimasu.

EN: I take a walk after meals.
HI: खाने के बाद मैं टहलता हूँ।
GU: જમ્યા પછી હું ફરવા જાઉં છું.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1つぎtsuginext (次)अगलाઆગળનું
2うしろushirobehind (後ろ — needs ろ)पीछेપાછળ
3まえmaebefore (前)पहलेપહેલાં
4あとatoafter ✓बाद मेंપછી

Answer 4. Why (our explanation): 〜の 後で (あとで) = after ~. 後 can also be read うしろ, but then it is written 後ろ (with ろ) and means "behind" — a place, not a time.

Q3

もう いちど 言って ください。Mou ichido itte kudasai.

EN: Please say it one more time.
HI: कृपया एक बार फिर कहिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને ફરી એક વાર કહો.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1いってittesay ✓कहिएકહો
2きってkittecut (切って)काटिएકાપો

Answer 1. Why (our explanation): 言う (いう) → て-form 言って (いって). All four options are real て-forms; only the kanji 言 tells you which one.

Q6

EN: I go to school together with my friend.
HI: मैं दोस्त के साथ स्कूल जाता हूँ।
GU: હું મિત્ર સાથે શાળાએ જાઉં છું.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1がこうgakoumissing small っ(छोटा っ नहीं)(નાનો っ નથી)

EN: How much is this newspaper?
HI: यह अख़बार कितने का है?

OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati

問題2 — 表記: how do you write it? (11–18)

＿＿＿の ことばは どう かきますか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを ひとつ えらんで ください。___ no kotoba wa dou kakimasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: How is the underlined word written (in kanji or katakana)? HI: रेखांकित शब्द (कांजी या काताकाना में) कैसे लिखा जाता है? GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ (કાંજી કે કાતાકાનામાં) કેવી રીતે લખાય? (p.7) — 例: こども → 子ども (answer 2; the other three are look-alike shapes).

Q11

けさ しゃわーを あびました。Kesa shawaa o abimashita.

EN: I took a shower this morning.
HI: आज सुबह मैंने शावर लिया।
GU: આજે સવારે મેં શાવર લીધો.
OptionWritten formEnglishHindiGujarati
1シャワーshawaashower ✓शावरશાવર
2シャウーshauuウ instead of ワ(ग़लत)(ખોટું)

Answer 4. Why (our explanation): two years ago = おととし. Don't confuse it with おととい (two days ago) — they differ by one kana: とし (year) vs とい.

Exam trap 1 — sounds, not meanings, in 問題1: most wrong readings differ from the right one by one small thing: a long vowel (しゅ／しゅう), a small っ (がこう／がっこう), or a ゛／゜ mark (ぼん／ぽん). Read each option slowly, mark by mark.
Exam trap 2 — fake kanji in 問題2: two or three options are often shapes that look right at a glance. Know the real kanji well enough to spot one missing or extra stroke (車, 半, 同).
Exam trap 3 — check every part in 問題4: the four sentences are identical except for one or two words. Find what changes (night/morning, always/sometimes) and test each against the underlined sentence.
