問題1 — 文法形式の判断: what goes in the brackets? (1–16)

（　　）に 何を 入れますか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in the brackets? Choose the best one. HI: कोष्ठक में क्या आएगा? सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: કૌંસમાં શું આવશે? સૌથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. (p.15) — 例: これ（は）えんぴつです。Kore (wa) enpitsu desu. (This is a pencil.) — answer 3 は.

Q1

日本（　　）ラーメンは おいしいです。Nihon ( ) raamen wa oishii desu.

EN: Japan(ese) ramen is tasty.
HI: जापान का रामेन स्वादिष्ट है।
GU: જાપાનનું રામેન સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にniin, toमें, कोમાં, ને
2のno's, of ✓काનું
3をo(object marker)(कर्म चिह्न)(કર્મ ચિહ્ન)
4へetowardsकी ओरતરફ

Answer 2 (正答表 p.52). Why (our explanation): two nouns side by side are joined with の: 日本の ラーメン = "Japan's ramen". There is no verb between them, so に, を, へ cannot work.

Q2

わたしには きょうだいが 二人 います。弟（　　）妹です。Watashi ni wa kyoudai ga futari imasu. Otouto ( ) imouto desu.

EN: I have two brothers and sisters. A younger brother (and) a younger sister.
HI: मेरे दो भाई-बहन हैं। छोटा भाई (और) छोटी बहन।
GU: મારે બે ભાઈ-બહેન છે. નાનો ભાઈ (અને) નાની બહેન.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はwa(topic)(विषय)(વિષય)
2もmoalsoभीપણ
3とtoand ✓औरઅને
4かkaorयाકે

Answer 3. Why (our explanation): there are exactly two — so it is "brother and sister": 弟と 妹. か (or) would mean only one of them, which does not match 二人 (two people).

Q3

山下「田中さん（　　）きのう どこかに 出かけましたか。」Yamashita: "Tanaka-san ( ) kinou dokoka ni dekakemashita ka."

田中「いいえ、いえに いました。」Tanaka: "Iie, ie ni imashita."

EN: Yamashita: "Tanaka-san, did you go out anywhere yesterday?" Tanaka: "No, I was at home."
HI: यामाशिता: "तानाका जी, क्या आप कल कहीं बाहर गए थे?" तानाका: "नहीं, मैं घर पर था।"
GU: યામાશિતા: "તાનાકાજી, તમે ગઈ કાલે ક્યાંય બહાર ગયા હતા?" તાનાકા: "ના, હું ઘરે હતો."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1でdeat, byमें, सेમાં, થી

Answer 2. Why (our explanation): 田中さん is the person the question is about — the topic — so it takes は. In Japanese you often use the listener's name with は instead of "you".

Q10

わたしの 母は 50さいです。父は 55さいです。母は 父（　　）5さい わかいです。Watashi no haha wa gojussai desu. Chichi wa gojuugosai desu. Haha wa chichi ( ) gosai wakai desu.

GU: મારી મમ્મી 50 વર્ષની છે. પપ્પા 55ના છે. મમ્મી પપ્પા (કરતાં) 5 વર્ષ નાની છે.

Q11

GU: બાળક: "ઇતાદાકિમાસુ (જમવાનું શરૂ)." મમ્મી: "અરે, જમતાં (પહેલાં) હાથ ધોઈ લઈએ."

外国の 本も 売って います。わたしの 国のも ［25］。Gaikoku no hon mo utte imasu. Watashi no kuni no mo [25].

EN: It also sells foreign books. (There are) ones from my country too.
HI: वहाँ विदेशी किताबें भी बिकती हैं। मेरे देश की भी (हैं)।
GU: ત્યાં વિદેશી પુસ્તકો પણ વેચાય છે. મારા દેશનાં પણ (છે).

Q26

（…りょうりの 本も 多いです。）［26］、本は いつも えきの ちかくの 本屋で 買います。(…ryouri no hon mo ooi desu.) [26], hon wa itsumo eki no chikaku no hon'ya de kaimasu.

Exam trap 1 — read the whole conversation: the answer often depends on the other speaker's line (いいえ、いえに いました in Q3; いえ in Q14; ちょっと まって ください in Q16). Read both lines before you choose.
Exam trap 2 — time words decide the tense: きょねん + あまり → past negative (Q12); 見て ください → happening now, 〜て います (Q13).
Exam trap 3 — ★ questions: build the whole sentence first, write the numbers in order, then read off the ★ slot. Find the easy pairs first: noun + particle (くだものは, 日本語が) and the piece that must touch the printed word after the blanks (あります + か, 本 + が).
