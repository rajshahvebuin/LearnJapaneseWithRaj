公式問題集 N5

Following 日本語能力試験 公式問題集 N5 (JLPT Official Practice Workbook N5) — one complete official N5 test, split into its papers: 言語知識（文字・語彙）, 言語知識（文法）, 読解 and 聴解. Every page gives every question with the book's official answer (正答表), and simple explanations in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji. The book has no explanations of its own, so all explanations are ours. Reading passages and listening scripts are summarised, not reproduced; audio tracks are referred to by track number only.
