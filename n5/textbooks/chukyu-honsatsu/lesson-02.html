第2課 — 楽しい食事・上手な買い物 (Tanoshii shokuji, jouzu na kaimono — Enjoyable meals, smart shopping)

Source
できる日本語 中級 本冊 (アルク), printed p.27–38 (PDF pages 27–38; the PDF page number is the same as the printed one)
Lesson goal
周りからいろいろな情報を得たり、自分の希望を伝えたりして、満足のいく食事や買い物をすることができる。Mawari kara iroiro na jouhou o etari, jibun no kibou o tsutaetari shite, manzoku no iku shokuji ya kaimono o suru koto ga dekiru. Get information from the people and things around you, say what you want, and enjoy meals and shopping you are happy with.

1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.27)

The opening picture is a shopping street full of signs. An izakaya くろきや (Kurokiya) advertises 生ビール半額 (nama biiru hangaku, draught beer half price) and 今月のおすすめ さんま (kongetsu no osusume sanma, this month's special: saury). A clothes shop has クリアランスSALE 30〜50%OFF (kuriaransu seeru). A takoyaki stand たこたこ (Takotako) offers ドリンクセット＋100円 (dorinku setto) and 水曜日サービスデー (suiyoubi saabisu dee, Wednesday service day). Talk about: which signs catch your eye, and how do you find good deals? HI: कौन-से बोर्ड आपका ध्यान खींचते हैं, और आप अच्छे सौदे कैसे ढूँढते हैं? GU: કયાં બોર્ડ તમારું ધ્યાન ખેંચે છે, અને તમે સારા સોદા કેવી રીતે શોધો છો?

  • 1. レストランの紹介を聞いて、お得な情報を得ることができる。Resutoran no shoukai o kiite, otoku na jouhou o eru koto ga dekiru. I can listen to a restaurant being introduced and pick up money-saving information.
    HI: मैं किसी रेस्टोरेंट का परिचय सुनकर फ़ायदे की जानकारी ले सकता हूँ। GU: હું કોઈ રેસ્ટોરન્ટનો પરિચય સાંભળીને ફાયદાની માહિતી મેળવી શકું છું.
  • 2. 友達に希望を聞いて、お薦めの店の情報と情報の探し方を紹介することができる。Tomodachi ni kibou o kiite, osusume no mise no jouhou to jouhou no sagashikata o shoukai suru koto ga dekiru. I can ask what a friend wants, recommend a shop and explain how to find such information.
    HI: मैं दोस्त की पसंद पूछकर अच्छी दुकान बता सकता हूँ और जानकारी ढूँढने का तरीका भी समझा सकता हूँ। GU: હું મિત્રની પસંદ પૂછીને સારી દુકાન બતાવી શકું છું અને માહિતી શોધવાની રીત પણ સમજાવી શકું છું.
  • 3. 看板やポスターを見て、どんな内容が書いてあるか理解して情報を得ることができる。Kanban ya posutaa o mite, donna naiyou ga kaite aru ka rikai shite jouhou o eru koto ga dekiru. I can understand what signs and posters say and get the information from them.
    HI: मैं साइनबोर्ड और पोस्टर पढ़कर समझ सकता हूँ कि उनमें क्या लिखा है। GU: હું સાઇનબોર્ડ અને પોસ્ટર વાંચીને સમજી શકું છું કે તેમાં શું લખ્યું છે.
  • 4. 店の人に希望を伝えて依頼することができる。Mise no hito ni kibou o tsutaete irai suru koto ga dekiru. I can tell shop staff what I want and make a request.
    HI: मैं दुकान वाले को अपनी इच्छा बताकर अनुरोध कर सकता हूँ। GU: હું દુકાનવાળાને મારી ઇચ્છા જણાવીને વિનંતી કરી શકું છું.
  • 5. 買い物についての経験談を周りの人と共有し、自分の買い物に役立てることができる。Kaimono ni tsuite no keikendan o mawari no hito to kyouyuu shi, jibun no kaimono ni yakudateru koto ga dekiru. I can share shopping stories with people around me and use them in my own shopping.
    HI: मैं ख़रीदारी के अनुभव दूसरों से बाँटकर अपनी ख़रीदारी में उनका फ़ायदा उठा सकता हूँ। GU: હું ખરીદીના અનુભવો બીજાઓ સાથે વહેંચીને મારી ખરીદીમાં તેનો લાભ લઈ શકું છું.

Task 2 · こんなときどうする？ (Ⓐ06)

友達に希望を聞いて、お薦めの店の情報と情報の探し方を紹介してください。Tomodachi ni kibou o kiite, osusume no mise no jouhou to jouhou no sagashikata o shoukai shite kudasai.

Role A: friend B asked you for a good place to eat. Ask what B wants, recommend a shop, and explain how you usually find shop information. Your card: 焼き肉の食べ放題「モーモーBBQ」 (yakiniku no tabehoudai "Moomoo BBQ"), near やなぎ駅, salad and dessert too, 70 minutes, ¥1,500; tips: a coupon on the shop's homepage, the site 「ぐるぐる」 where you search by dish and budget, and 口コミ (kuchikomi, reviews). Role B: a friend from home is visiting and you want all-you-can-eat yakiniku, but don't know a good place. Ask A, and ask how to find shop information.
HI: A: दोस्त B ने अच्छी खाने की जगह पूछी है; उसकी पसंद पूछो, "मोमो BBQ" (याकिनिकु असीमित, यानागी स्टेशन के पास, 70 मिनट, 1,500 येन) बताओ, और कूपन/साइट/रिव्यू से जानकारी ढूँढने का तरीका समझाओ। B: देश से दोस्त आ रहा है, असीमित याकिनिकु की जगह और जानकारी ढूँढने का तरीका पूछो।
GU: A: મિત્ર B એ સારી ખાવાની જગ્યા પૂછી છે; તેની પસંદ પૂછો, "મોમો BBQ" (યાકિનિકુ અમર્યાદિત, યાનાગી સ્ટેશન પાસે, 70 મિનિટ, 1,500 યેન) બતાવો, અને કૂપન/સાઇટ/રિવ્યૂથી માહિતી શોધવાની રીત સમજાવો. B: દેશથી મિત્ર આવે છે, અમર્યાદિત યાકિનિકુની જગ્યા અને માહિતી શોધવાની રીત પૂછો.

(our answer, not in book) B：焼き肉の食べ放題に行きたいんだけど、どこかいい店ある？

Itsumo dou yatte sagashiteru no? / A: "Guruguru" tte iu saito de, ryouri to yosan o irete kensaku suru to, iroiro na mise ga dete kuru you ni natteru n da. Kuchikomi mo sankou ni naru yo.

The reviews help too.

Task 4 · こんなときどうする？ (Ⓐ07)

店の人に服のサイズ直しを頼んでください。Mise no hito ni fuku no saizu naoshi o tanonde kudasai.

8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜うちにuchi ni
vs 〜前にmae ni		before / while stillV-ないうちに／V-る前にうちに = act while the state lasts (it will change); 前に = simple time order.温かいうちに, 忘れないうちに → うちに. 寝る前に → 前に.
〜わりにwari ni
vs 〜のにnoni (added)		although / for普通形＋わりに／のにわりに = compared with what you'd expect from the degree; のに = a plain contradiction, often with complaint.量のわりに安い (evaluation). 約束したのに来ない (complaint).
〜はずだhazu da
vs 〜だろうdarou		should / probably普通形＋はずだ／だろうはずだ = a logical expectation with a reason; だろう = a general guess.言っていたから来るはずだ (based on evidence).
〜に限りni kagiri
vs 〜だけdake		onlyN＋に限り／N＋だけに限り = formal notice language, "the offer applies only to"; だけ = everyday "only".Posters and rules → に限り. Speech → だけ.
〜につきni tsuki
vs 〜のでnode		becauseN＋につき／普通形＋のでにつき = written reason on signs, nouns only; ので = spoken or written, any clause.閉店につき (sign). 閉店するので (talk).
〜向けmuke
vs 〜向きmuki (added)		for ~N＋向け／向き向け = designed for that target; 向き = suits that person, intended or not.子ども向けの番組 (made for kids). 初心者向きの山 (suitable for beginners).
Watch out: 行っています and 来ています in this lesson are states (he is in China; I am here at the store), not actions in progress. And こそ is not just emphasis: 今年こそ means "this year, unlike the previous ones", so it carries a feeling of "finally".
