1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.207)

The warm-up is a weekly 時間割 (jikanwari, timetable) for class 3 of the first year at a Japanese junior high school (○○中学校 1年3組). Each day starts at 8:20 with 朝読書・朝の会 (asa dokusho, asa no kai: morning reading and homeroom), then six 50-minute periods of subjects such as 数学 (suugaku, maths), 英語 (eigo, English), 国語 (kokugo, Japanese), 理科 (rika, science), 社会 (shakai, social studies), 技術家庭 (gijutsu katei, technology and home economics), 道徳 (doutoku, moral education), 総合 (sougou, integrated studies), 体育 (taiiku, PE), 音楽・美術 (ongaku, bijutsu: music, art) and 学級活動 (gakkyuu katsudou, class activities). Lunch is 給食 (kyuushoku, school lunch), and the day ends with 帰りの会 (kaeri no kai, end-of-day homeroom) and 清掃 (seisou, cleaning) until 15:45. Compare it with a school timetable from your country: which subjects and activities are the same, and which are different?

(our answer, not in book) 確かに、業者の人が掃除したほうがきれいになるという考えもわからないことはありません。でも、私は生徒が掃除するのはいいことだと思います。自分で掃除をすれば、教室を汚さないように気を付けるようになるし、友達と協力することも学べるのではないでしょうか。Tashika ni, gyousha no hito ga souji shita hou ga kirei ni naru to iu kangae mo wakaranai koto wa arimasen. Task 3 · 見つけた！

Material (summary of the newspaper article, p.209)

センター試験始まるSentaa shiken hajimaru (The Center Test begins)

Setting: you found a newspaper article about university entrance exams (dated 16 January 20**).

Afterwards some said they were too nervous to give it their all, or that Japanese wasn't so hard but there wasn't enough time for English. 8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たものだta mono da
vs 〜ことだkoto da (第15課)		used to / shouldV-タ形＋ものだ／V-辞書形＋ことだたものだ looks back fondly on past habits; ことだ gives advice.Memories → たものだ. Advice → ことだ.
〜きるkiru
vs 〜ぬくnuku		to the endV-マス形＋きる／ぬくきる = completely, to the limit (also 疲れきる); ぬく = see it through despite hardship.Total state → きる. Determination → ぬく.
〜にもかかわらずni mo kakawarazu
vs 〜のにnoni (added)		despite普通形＋にもかかわらず／のににもかかわらず is formal and neutral; のに is spoken and carries surprise or complaint.News, reports → にもかかわらず. Grumbling → のに.
〜はともかくwa tomokaku
vs 〜はもちろんwa mochiron (added)		~ aside / ~ of courseN＋はともかく／はもちろんはともかく puts the first item aside; はもちろん includes it and adds more.Focus on what matters → はともかく.
〜つつtsutsu
vs 〜ながらnagara		whileV-マス形＋つつ／ながらSame meaning; つつ is written and formal.Essays, articles → つつ. Speech → ながら.
〜ないことはないnai koto wa nai
vs 〜できるdekiru		can (sort of)V-ナイ形＋ないことはないないことはない = a reluctant, partial yes, usually followed by "but…".Hedging, accepting the other view before disagreeing.
N＋次第だshidai da
vs V-マス形＋次第shidai (added)		depends on / as soon asN＋次第だ／V-マス形＋次第After a noun = "depends on"; after a verb stem = "as soon as".Results that depend on effort → N次第だ.
Watch out: 〜ないことはない is a double negative that means a weak yes ("I can, sort of"), not a strong one. And 〜にかけては is only for praising a skill: 料理にかけては自信がある, not for neutral topics (use 〜については／に関しては for those).
