中級 本冊

This course follows できる日本語 中級 本冊 (Dekiru Nihongo Intermediate, main textbook, アルク) lesson by lesson. It is the third book of the できる日本語 series, after 初級 and 初中級. Each of the 20 lessons is built around real-life tasks: you read a notice, invite a friend, listen to a forecast or introduce yourself. Then you study the grammar and expressions those tasks need, read a short text (知って楽しむ) and do a final project (できる！). Every page gives each grammar point with all of the book's examples, the tasks with answers, the reading in summary, and the full ことば list. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Each lesson starts with チャレンジ！ tasks. Try each one first with what you already know. Then study the matching 使ってみよう model and its grammar points, and do the やってみよう follow-up in real life.
  • 知って楽しむ is a light reading. Our pages summarise it and quote only the key lines; the book's text is not reproduced.
  • The ことば lists at the end of each page follow the book's order. ★ marks words printed in bold in the book, which are the core words to learn now.
