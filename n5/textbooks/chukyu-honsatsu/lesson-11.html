1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.143)

Six speech bubbles show different ways of living: living alone is carefree (「一人暮らしは気楽でいい。」Hitorigurashi wa kiraku de ii.); living with others feels safer and more fun; making your own lunch box is healthy and saves money; "I can't resist good food" (おいしいものには目がない oishii mono ni wa me ga nai) and checking food online; buying good clothes even if a bit pricey and wearing them for years; and saving money now for the future by giving up what you want. Talk about: which one is closest to you? HI: इनमें से कौन-सी जीवनशैली आपसे सबसे ज़्यादा मिलती है? GU: આમાંથી કઈ જીવનશૈલી તમને સૌથી વધુ મળતી આવે છે?

  • 1. テレビのニュース番組で調査結果の解説を聞いて、日本の大学生の平均的な生活について知ることができる。Terebi no nyuusu bangumi de chousa kekka no kaisetsu o kiite, Nihon no daigakusei no heikinteki na seikatsu ni tsuite shiru koto ga dekiru. I can learn about the average life of Japanese university students from a TV news explanation of a survey.
    HI: मैं टीवी समाचार में सर्वे के नतीजों की व्याख्या सुनकर जापानी विश्वविद्यालय छात्रों के औसत जीवन के बारे में जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું ટીવી સમાચારમાં સર્વેના પરિણામોની સમજૂતી સાંભળીને જાપાની યુનિવર્સિટી વિદ્યાર્થીઓના સરેરાશ જીવન વિશે જાણી શકું છું.
  • 2. 情報誌を読んで、衣食住について最近話題になっていることを知ることができる。Jouhoushi o yonde, ishokujuu ni tsuite saikin wadai ni natte iru koto o shiru koto ga dekiru. I can read an information magazine and learn what's in the news about clothing, food and housing.
    HI: मैं पत्रिका पढ़कर कपड़े, खाने और रहने से जुड़ी आजकल की चर्चित बातें जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું સામયિક વાંચીને કપડાં, ભોજન અને રહેઠાણ વિશે આજકાલ ચર્ચાતી વાતો જાણી શકું છું.
  • 3. 親しい人との約束を、事情を説明してキャンセルすることができる。Shitashii hito to no yakusoku o, jijou o setsumei shite kyanseru suru koto ga dekiru. I can cancel plans with a close friend, explaining the reason.
    HI: मैं क़रीबी व्यक्ति के साथ तय योजना को कारण बताकर रद्द कर सकता हूँ। GU: હું નજીકની વ્યક્તિ સાથેની યોજના કારણ જણાવીને રદ કરી શકું છું.
  • 4. 結婚に関する記事を読んで、自分の考えを話すことができる。Kekkon ni kansuru kiji o yonde, jibun no kangae o hanasu koto ga dekiru. I can read an article about marriage and give my own view.
    HI: मैं शादी से जुड़ा लेख पढ़कर अपने विचार बता सकता हूँ। GU: હું લગ્ન વિશેનો લેખ વાંચીને મારા વિચાર કહી શકું છું.

Task 2 · 見つけた！

Material (summary of the magazine article, p.144–145)

自分に合った住居スタイルを選ぶ人たち 広がるシェアハウスJibun ni atta juukyo sutairu o erabu hitotachi — hirogaru shea hausu

EN: A share house is a home where everyone lives in their own room but shares the living room and so on. A normal flat needs a lump sum (deposit, key money) even for a short stay, but share houses often don't, some charge rent weekly, and many come furnished with a TV, fridge, air conditioner and futons. Popular with foreign students for years, they are now spreading to all age groups around Tokyo. Cheapness is one reason, but another is that more people want connection with others: one single office worker, when living alone, sometimes went whole days speaking to no one but colleagues, and now the best thing is hearing "welcome home". If you want company you go to the living room; if you want to be alone you go back to your room. On the other hand, many still want a fully independent flat, as they don't want to be careful of others after a tiring day, and some dream of leaving their family to live alone. Today, people can choose a home to match their way of thinking.
HI: शेयर हाउस में हर कोई अपने कमरे में रहता है पर लिविंग रूम आदि साझा करता है। आम फ़्लैट में डिपॉज़िट, "रेकिन" जैसी बड़ी रक़म लगती है, पर शेयर हाउस में अक्सर नहीं; कुछ में हफ़्तेवार किराया और टीवी, फ़्रिज, एसी, बिस्तर सब मिलता है। पहले विदेशी छात्रों में लोकप्रिय, अब टोक्यो के आसपास हर उम्र में फैल रहा है। कारण: सस्ता होना, और लोगों से जुड़ाव चाहना (एक अकेले कर्मचारी को अब "घर आ गए?" सुनना सबसे अच्छा लगता है)। फिर भी कई लोग पूरी तरह अलग फ़्लैट चाहते हैं। आज हर कोई अपनी सोच के मुताबिक़ घर चुन सकता है।

Task 3 · こんなときどうする？ (Ⓑ03)

友達との約束を、事情を説明してキャンセルしてください。Tomodachi to no yakusoku o, jijou o setsumei shite kyanseru shite kudasai.

8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜もののmono no
vs 〜といってもto itte mo		although普通形＋ものの／といってもものの = formal "although (reality differs)"; といっても = spoken "it's not as much as it sounds".Essays → ものの. Playing something down in chat → といっても.
〜ということだto iu koto da
vs 〜とは〜ということだto wa ~ to iu koto da		I hear / means普通形＋ということだ／N＋とは…Alone = hearsay ("it is reported"); with とは = definition ("X means…").Passing on news → ということだ. Defining a term → とは〜ことだ.
〜つつあるtsutsu aru
vs 〜ているte iru		is ~ingV-ます＋つつある／V-て＋いるつつある = change still in progress (written); ている can also be a finished state.Trends in reports → つつある.
〜づらいzurai
vs 〜にくいnikui (added)		hard toV-ます＋づらい／にくいづらい = the speaker's discomfort or reluctance; にくい = objectively difficult.言いづらい (I feel awkward) · 割れにくい (hard to break).
〜んだからn da kara
vs 〜からkara		because普通形＋んだから／からんだから = "you know this, so accept it" (advice, scolding, insisting); から = neutral reason.Persuading or scolding → んだから.
〜というよりto iu yori
vs 〜よりyori		rather than / than普通形＋というより／N＋よりというより corrects a word choice; より compares two things.暖かいというより暑い ✓ · 昨日より暑い ✓.
Watch out: this lesson has two "に対して"s and two "ということ"s. 妹に対して姉は… is contrast ("whereas"), not "towards". 〜ということだ alone is hearsay, but 〜とは〜ということだ is a definition. And remember: 〜のではないでしょうか looks like a question, but it is really your opinion, said softly.
