Lesson goal
ふるさとや住んだことがある場所の地理や気候に合わせた生活を紹介して、お互いの理解を深めることができる。Furusato ya sunda koto ga aru basho no chiri ya kikou ni awaseta seikatsu o shoukai shite, otagai no rikai o fukameru koto ga dekiru. 1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.75)

The opening page is a map of Japan divided into its regions: 北海道地方 (Hokkaidou chihou), 東北 (Touhoku), 関東 (Kantou), 中部 (Chuubu), 近畿 (Kinki), 中国 (Chuugoku), 四国 (Shikoku), 九州 (Kyuushuu) and 沖縄 (Okinawa), with pictures of local products (apples, rice, hot springs, Mt Fuji, oranges, peaches, puffer fish). Two climate charts compare 札幌 (Sapporo): average 8.8°C, 1,107.6 mm of rain a year, with 那覇 (Naha): average 23.0°C, 2,063.8 mm. Talk about: what do you know about each region, and how is the climate different from your country? HI: जापान के क्षेत्रों का नक्शा, स्थानीय उत्पाद और साप्पोरो व नाहा के मौसम चार्ट; हर क्षेत्र के बारे में और अपने देश के मौसम से अंतर पर बात करें। GU: જાપાનના પ્રદેશોનો નકશો, સ્થાનિક ઉત્પાદનો અને સાપ્પોરો તથા નાહાના હવામાન ચાર્ટ; દરેક પ્રદેશ વિશે અને તમારા દેશના હવામાનથી તફાવત વિશે વાત કરો.

  • 1. 説明を読んで、日本の地理や町の様子、気候について知ることができる。Setsumei o yonde, Nihon no chiri ya machi no yousu, kikou ni tsuite shiru koto ga dekiru. I can read an explanation and learn about Japan's geography, towns and climate.
    HI: मैं विवरण पढ़कर जापान के भूगोल, शहरों और मौसम के बारे में जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું વર્ણન વાંચીને જાપાનની ભૂગોળ, શહેરો અને હવામાન વિશે જાણી શકું છું.
  • 2. 説明を聞いて、気候に合わせた建物の特徴を知ることができる。Setsumei o kiite, kikou ni awaseta tatemono no tokuchou o shiru koto ga dekiru. I can listen to an explanation and learn the features of buildings made to suit the climate.
    HI: मैं विवरण सुनकर मौसम के अनुसार बनी इमारतों की विशेषताएँ जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું વર્ણન સાંભળીને હવામાન મુજબ બનેલી ઇમારતોની વિશેષતાઓ જાણી શકું છું.
  • 3. 気候が合わなくて体調を崩した友達の話を聞いて、アドバイスすることができる。Kikou ga awanakute taichou o kuzushita tomodachi no hanashi o kiite, adobaisu suru koto ga dekiru. I can listen to a friend who got ill because the climate doesn't suit them, and give advice.
    HI: मौसम रास न आने से बीमार पड़े दोस्त की बात सुनकर मैं सलाह दे सकता हूँ। GU: હવામાન માફક ન આવવાથી બીમાર પડેલા મિત્રની વાત સાંભળીને હું સલાહ આપી શકું છું.
  • 4. 説明を読んで、町の特徴や歴史などについて知ることができる。Setsumei o yonde, machi no tokuchou ya rekishi nado ni tsuite shiru koto ga dekiru. I can read an explanation and learn about a town's features and history.
    HI: मैं विवरण पढ़कर किसी शहर की विशेषताओं और इतिहास के बारे में जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું વર્ણન વાંચીને કોઈ શહેરની વિશેષતાઓ અને ઇતિહાસ વિશે જાણી શકું છું.
  • 5. 国・ふるさとの地形や気候を利用した名物や風物詩を紹介することができる。Kuni / furusato no chikei ya kikou o riyou shita meibutsu ya fuubutsushi o shoukai suru koto ga dekiru. I can introduce local specialities and seasonal traditions of my country or hometown that make use of its landscape and climate.
    HI: मैं अपने देश या गृहनगर की भू-आकृति और मौसम का उपयोग करने वाली ख़ास चीज़ों और मौसमी परंपराओं का परिचय दे सकता हूँ। GU: હું મારા દેશ કે વતનની ભૂરચના અને હવામાનનો ઉપયોગ કરતી ખાસ વસ્તુઓ અને ઋતુગત પરંપરાઓનો પરિચય આપી શકું છું.

Task 1 · 見つけた！

Material (summary of the museum panel, p.76)

日本の国土と気候Nihon no kokudo to kikou

Setting: you are at a museum with a friend, reading a panel in the corner "Japan's land and climate".
EN: Japan is a long north–south island country surrounded by sea. Mountains and forests take up about two-thirds of the land, the terrain is complex, and the climate varies. Most areas are in the temperate zone; from June to September it is rainy and hot and humid. In winter the Pacific side has many clear, dry days, while the Sea of Japan side has a run of cloudy days and heavy snow. Because the country is so long, Okinawa can be as much as 15 degrees warmer on average than Hokkaido in some years.
HI: जापान उत्तर से दक्षिण तक लंबा, समुद्र से घिरा द्वीप देश है। पहाड़ और जंगल ज़मीन का लगभग दो-तिहाई हिस्सा हैं, भू-भाग जटिल है और मौसम भी तरह-तरह का। ज़्यादातर इलाके समशीतोष्ण क्षेत्र में हैं; जून से सितंबर तक बारिश और उमस भरी गर्मी रहती है। सर्दियों में प्रशांत तरफ़ साफ़ और सूखे दिन रहते हैं, जबकि जापान सागर तरफ़ बादल और भारी बर्फ़। कुछ सालों में ओकिनावा का औसत तापमान होक्काइदो से 15 डिग्री तक ज़्यादा होता है।
GU: જાપાન ઉત્તરથી દક્ષિણ સુધી લાંબો, સમુદ્રથી ઘેરાયેલો ટાપુ દેશ છે. પર્વતો અને જંગલો જમીનનો લગભગ બે-તૃતીયાંશ ભાગ છે, ભૂપ્રદેશ જટિલ છે અને હવામાન પણ જુદું જુદું. મોટા ભાગના વિસ્તારો સમશીતોષ્ણ કટિબંધમાં છે; જૂનથી સપ્ટેમ્બર સુધી વરસાદ અને ભેજવાળી ગરમી રહે છે. શિયાળામાં પ્રશાંત બાજુ ચોખ્ખા અને સૂકા દિવસો હોય છે, જ્યારે જાપાન સમુદ્ર બાજુ વાદળ અને ભારે બરફ. કેટલાંક વર્ષોમાં ઓકિનાવાનું સરેરાશ તાપમાન હોક્કાઇદો કરતાં 15 ડિગ્રી જેટલું વધારે હોય છે.

Key lines quoted: 山林が国土の約3分の2を占め、地形が複雑で気候もさまざまです。(Sanrin ga kokudo no yaku sanbun no ni o shime, chikei ga fukuzatsu de kikou mo samazama desu.) · 日本海側は曇りがちの日が続き、大雪が降りやすくなります。(Nihonkai-gawa wa kumorigachi no hi ga tsuzuki, ooyuki ga furiyasuku narimasu.)

Task 4 · 見つけた！

Material (summary of the article, p.78)

「横浜」という名前はどこから？"Yokohama" to iu namae wa doko kara?

EN: Yokohama is a big city of about 3.7 million people in the east of Kanagawa Prefecture. Tourist spots and business districts spread out around 関内 (Kannai), the station area and みなとみらい21. 5 知って楽しむ: 夏を快適に過ごす (p.84–85)

Summary (our words; the text is not reproduced)

夏を快適に過ごすNatsu o kaiteki ni sugosu (Spending summer comfortably)

EN: Japanese summer begins when the rainy season ends, and many people are surprised by the scorching sun and the humid nights. Air conditioning is now everywhere, but before it, people got through the heat with clever ideas. On summer nights they enjoy festivals and fireworks, and hang 風鈴 (fuurin, wind chimes) to "feel coolness through the ears"; the tinkling is the sound of a cool breeze, and the bells were once used to ward off evil. Another custom is 打ち水 (uchimizu): sprinkling water on the ground. When the water evaporates it takes heat from the ground, so the place gets cooler (a diagram notes that 1 g of evaporating water removes about 0.58 kcal). In recent years 「打ち水大作戦」 events have been held across Japan: lots of people gather and sprinkle water together to lower the temperature even a little. 8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜がちgachi
vs 〜やすいyasui		tends to / easy toV-マス形＋がち／やすいがち = it often happens (usually bad); やすい = it is easy for it to happen (good or bad).Habits and frequency → がち. Ease or likelihood → やすい.
〜に代わってni kawatte
vs 〜の代わりにno kawari ni (added)		instead ofN＋に代わって／N＋の代わりにに代わって = one thing has replaced another (often over time); written. の代わりに = everyday "instead of / in exchange".Trends and history → に代わって. Daily choices → の代わりに.
〜に備えてni sonaete
vs 〜のためにno tame ni		forN＋に備えて／のためにに備えて = getting ready for a possible future event; ために = general purpose or benefit.Disasters, exams, old age → に備えて.
〜を中心にo chuushin ni
vs 〜をはじめo hajime		centred on / starting withN＋を中心に／をはじめ中心に = X is the core and others gather around it; はじめ = X is the first and best-known item in a list.Areas, groups → 中心に. Listing famous examples → はじめ.
〜を通してo tooshite (period)
vs 〜の間no aida		throughout / duringN＋を通して／の間を通して = without a break, the whole period; の間 = at some point within or during it.1年を通して暖かい = warm all year round.
〜ことからkoto kara
vs 〜からkara		because普通形＋ことから／からことから = grounds for a name or conclusion (written); から = any reason, and allows requests and wishes.Origins of names, deductions → ことから.
Watch out: four patterns in this lesson start with を (〜を占める, 〜を中心に, 〜をはじめ, 〜を通して), and five start with に (〜に囲まれる, 〜に比べて, 〜に備えて, 〜に代わって, 〜に限る). Learn each one with its particle as a fixed chunk. 〜に限る ("nothing beats ~") is a personal opinion, so don't use it in a formal written description.
