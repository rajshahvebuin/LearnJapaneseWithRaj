第15課 — 情報社会に生きる (Jouhou shakai ni ikiru — Living in an information society)

Source

Lesson goal
(our summary of the four can-do goals) メディアを目的に応じて使い分け、情報や個人情報と上手に付き合うことができる。Media o mokuteki ni oujite tsukaiwake, jouhou ya kojin jouhou to jouzu ni tsukiau koto ga dekiru. Use different media for different purposes, and deal sensibly with information and personal data.

1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.195)

A woman wonders: 情報社会に生きるってどういうこと？ (Jouhou shakai ni ikiru tte dou iu koto?, "What does living in an information society mean?"). Speech bubbles around her give ideas: うまく利用する！ (umaku riyou suru, use it well), 楽しむ！ (tanoshimu, enjoy it), つながる！ (tsunagaru, connect), 発信する (hasshin suru, send out information), 疑う (utagau, doubt), 使い分ける (tsukaiwakeru, use different tools for different purposes), 守る！ (mamoru, protect). Talk about which of these matter most to you.
HI: "सूचना समाज में जीना" का मतलब क्या है? अच्छे से इस्तेमाल करना, आनंद लेना, जुड़ना, जानकारी फैलाना, शक करना, अलग-अलग माध्यम चुनना, और ख़ुद को बचाना। GU: "માહિતી સમાજમાં જીવવું" એટલે શું? સારી રીતે ઉપયોગ કરવો, આનંદ લેવો, જોડાવું, માહિતી ફેલાવવી, શંકા કરવી, જુદાં જુદાં માધ્યમ પસંદ કરવાં, અને પોતાની રક્ષા કરવી.

  • 1. 情報を集めるのにメディアをどのように使い分けているか、理由とともに述べることができる。Jouhou o atsumeru no ni media o dono you ni tsukaiwakete iru ka, riyuu to tomo ni noberu koto ga dekiru. I can explain, with reasons, how I use different media to gather information.
    HI: मैं कारण सहित बता सकता हूँ कि जानकारी जुटाने के लिए कौन-सा माध्यम कब इस्तेमाल करता हूँ। GU: હું કારણ સાથે કહી શકું છું કે માહિતી ભેગી કરવા કયું માધ્યમ ક્યારે વાપરું છું.
  • 2. 記事を読んでソーシャルメディアのマイナス面を知ることができる。Kiji o yonde soosharu media no mainasu men o shiru koto ga dekiru. I can read an article and learn about the downsides of social media.
    HI: मैं लेख पढ़कर सोशल मीडिया के नुक़सान जान सकता हूँ। GU: હું લેખ વાંચીને સોશિયલ મીડિયાનાં નુકસાન જાણી શકું છું.
  • 3. ラジオ番組を聞いて個人情報について考えるきっかけを得ることができる。Rajio bangumi o kiite kojin jouhou ni tsuite kangaeru kikkake o eru koto ga dekiru. I can listen to a radio programme and start thinking about personal information.
    HI: मैं रेडियो कार्यक्रम सुनकर निजी जानकारी के बारे में सोचना शुरू कर सकता हूँ। GU: હું રેડિયો કાર્યક્રમ સાંભળીને અંગત માહિતી વિશે વિચારવાની શરૂઆત કરી શકું છું.
  • 4. 自分だけでは判断できないことに対して理由を言って断ることができる。Jibun dake de wa handan dekinai koto ni taishite riyuu o itte kotowaru koto ga dekiru. I can turn down, with a reason, a request I can't decide on by myself.
    HI: जिस बात का फ़ैसला मैं अकेले नहीं कर सकता, उसे कारण बताकर मना कर सकता हूँ। GU: જે વાતનો નિર્ણય હું એકલો ન લઈ શકું, તેને કારણ જણાવીને ના પાડી શકું છું.

Task 1 · 伝えてみよう (Ⓑ19)

必要な情報をどこから集めていますか。それはどうしてですか。話してください。Hitsuyou na jouhou o doko kara atsumete imasu ka. Sore wa doushite desu ka. Hanashite kudasai.

EN: Where do you get the information you need, and why? Talk about it.
HI: ज़रूरी जानकारी तुम कहाँ से जुटाते हो, और क्यों? बताओ।
GU: જરૂરી માહિતી તમે ક્યાંથી ભેગી કરો છો, અને કેમ? કહો.

(our answer, not in book) 私は、電車の遅れや天気といった毎日の情報はスマホのアプリで見ます。すぐに確認できるからです。でも、大きなニュースは新聞のウェブサイトで読みます。SNSの情報は便利ですが、うそもあるので、信頼できる人の投稿だけを見るようにしています。目的に応じてメディアを使い分けることが大切だと思います。Watashi wa, densha no okure ya tenki to itta mainichi no jouhou wa sumaho no apuri de mimasu. Sugu ni kakunin dekiru kara desu. Demo, ookina nyuusu wa shinbun no webusaito de yomimasu. SNS no jouhou wa benri desu ga, uso mo aru node, shinrai dekiru hito no toukou dake o miru you ni shite imasu. Mokuteki ni oujite media o tsukaiwakeru koto ga taisetsu da to omoimasu.

EN: I check daily information such as train delays and the weather on smartphone apps, because I can confirm it instantly. But I read big news on newspaper websites. Social media is handy, but it has lies too, so I only read posts by people I trust. I think it's important to choose media according to your purpose.
HI: ट्रेन की देरी, मौसम जैसी रोज़ की जानकारी मैं ऐप पर देखता हूँ, क्योंकि तुरंत मिल जाती है। बड़ी ख़बरें अख़बार की वेबसाइट पर पढ़ता हूँ। SNS सुविधाजनक है पर उसमें झूठ भी होता है, इसलिए सिर्फ़ भरोसेमंद लोगों की पोस्ट देखता हूँ। मक़सद के हिसाब से माध्यम चुनना ज़रूरी है।
GU: ટ્રેનનું મોડું થવું, હવામાન જેવી રોજની માહિતી હું ઍપમાં જોઉં છું, કારણ કે તરત મળે છે. મોટા સમાચાર છાપાની વેબસાઇટ પર વાંચું છું. SNS અનુકૂળ છે પણ તેમાં જૂઠ પણ હોય છે, એટલે ફક્ત વિશ્વાસપાત્ર લોકોની પોસ્ટ જોઉં છું. હેતુ પ્રમાણે માધ્યમ પસંદ કરવું જરૂરી છે.

Model: the book's sample is the 使ってみよう 1 monologue (track Ⓑ19), summarised in section 3. It uses 〜といった and 〜を通して.

Task 2 · 見つけた！

Material (summary of the article, p.196)

SNS疲れ〜つながり続けることのプレッシャーSNS-zukare: tsunagaritsuzukeru koto no puresshaa

Setting: you have been feeling doubts about social media lately, so you read an article about it.
EN: The internet has changed how we communicate, and some people now say SNS is essential to their relationships. But a new problem has been pointed out: "SNS fatigue", where something you should be enjoying becomes a mental burden before you know it. Once you start, you want more reactions, so you feel pressed to keep posting and get tired. Comments may arrive the instant you post, or hours later, so you feel restless unless you are always checking. Seeing your number of friends can also cause stress. And SNS keeps you connected with people you would naturally have drifted away from, like old classmates or former colleagues; that seems good, but even when you no longer want contact the relationship carries on, which can feel suffocating. The writer's advice: if SNS tires you, keep your distance for a while. It's simple, because in the end these are only online relationships.
HI: इंटरनेट ने बातचीत का तरीक़ा बदल दिया, कुछ लोग SNS को रिश्तों के लिए ज़रूरी मानते हैं। पर नई समस्या है "SNS थकान": जो मज़े के लिए था, वह कब मानसिक बोझ बन जाता है पता नहीं चलता। ज़्यादा प्रतिक्रिया पाने की चाह में लगातार पोस्ट करने की जल्दबाज़ी, हर समय चेक करने की बेचैनी, दोस्तों की गिनती का तनाव, और पुराने सहपाठियों या सहकर्मियों से न चाहते हुए भी रिश्ता चलते रहना। लेखक की सलाह: थकान लगे तो कुछ समय दूरी बना लो; आख़िर ये सिर्फ़ ऑनलाइन रिश्ते हैं।
GU: ઇન્ટરનેટે વાતચીતની રીત બદલી નાખી, કેટલાક લોકો SNS ને સંબંધો માટે જરૂરી માને છે. પણ નવી સમસ્યા છે "SNS થાક": જે મજા માટે હતું, તે ક્યારે માનસિક બોજ બની જાય ખબર નથી પડતી. વધુ પ્રતિભાવની ઇચ્છામાં સતત પોસ્ટ કરવાની ઉતાવળ, દરેક વખતે ચેક કરવાની બેચેની, મિત્રોની સંખ્યાનો તણાવ, અને જૂના સહપાઠીઓ કે સહકર્મીઓ સાથે ન ઇચ્છતાં પણ સંબંધ ચાલુ રહેવો. લેખકની સલાહ: થાક લાગે તો થોડો સમય અંતર રાખો; આખરે આ ફક્ત ઓનલાઇન સંબંધો છે.

Key lines quoted: SNSを楽しんでいるはずが、いつの間にか精神的な負担になってしまう「SNS疲れ」という現象だ。(SNS o tanoshinde iru hazu ga, itsu no ma ni ka seishinteki na futan ni natte shimau "SNS-zukare" to iu genshou da.) · もし、SNSで疲れを感じたら、しばらく距離を置くことだ。(Moshi, SNS de tsukare o kanjitara, shibaraku kyori o oku koto da.)

Task 4 · こんなときどうする？ (Ⓑ22)

理由を言って断ってください。Riyuu o itte kotowatte kudasai.

Role A: a student reporter you know from your university stopped you for an interview. At the end you are asked for a friend's phone number. Give a reason and say you can't. Role B: you are a reporter for the university paper, interviewing people for an article about international students. 8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ばかりにbakari ni
vs 〜おかげでokage de (added)		because of普通形＋ばかりに／おかげでばかりに = "just because of (that one thing), something bad happened"; おかげで = thanks to, good result.Regret → ばかりに. Gratitude → おかげで.
〜かねないkanenai
vs 〜かもしれないka mo shirenai		mightV-ます＋かねない／普通形＋かもしれないかねない is only for bad outcomes and sounds like a warning; かもしれない is neutral possibility.Risks, warnings → かねない. Any guess → かもしれない.
〜わけにはいかないwake ni wa ikanai
vs 〜できないdekinai		can'tV-る＋わけにはいかないわけにはいかない = possible, but socially/morally not OK; できない = impossible.Refusing on principle → わけにはいかない.
〜てしかたがないte shikata ga nai
vs 〜てならないte naranai		terribly ~V-て／イAくて／ナAで＋…Same meaning; しかたがない／しょうがない is spoken, てならない (第14課) is written.Chat → しょうがない. Letters, essays → てならない.
〜に関してni kanshite
vs 〜についてni tsuite		aboutN＋に関して／についてSame meaning; に関して is formal and written.Reports, news → に関して. Conversation → について.
〜としたらto shitara
vs 〜たらtara		if普通形＋としたら／V-たらとしたら = "supposing it's true that…" (hypothesis, inference); たら = when/if an event happens.Reasoning from information or imagining → としたら.
Watch out: 〜かねない looks negative but means "might well happen", while 〜かねる (お答えしかねます) looks positive but means "can't". And 〜代わりに is a trade-off (便利な代わりに…), not only "instead of".
