第1課 — 新たな出会い (Arata na deai — New encounters)

Source

Lesson goal
新しい環境に自分から挑戦して、その環境で印象的に自己紹介することができる。Atarashii kankyou ni jibun kara chousen shite, sono kankyou de inshouteki ni jiko shoukai suru koto ga dekiru. Take on a new environment yourself, and introduce yourself there in a way people remember.

1 Lesson Goals (チャレンジ！ can-do list)

話してみよう (warm-up, p.15)

The opening picture shows a young man at the centre of his new life in Japan, surrounded by the people he will meet. There is his school ひまわり日本語学校 (Himawari Nihongo Gakkou), his dormitory あさひ寮 (Asahi-ryou), 地域の人たち (chiiki no hitotachi, people in the neighbourhood) and バイト先 (baito-saki, his part-time workplace). One speech bubble is empty, for you to fill. Talk about: who do you meet in a new place, and what do you say first?

  • 1. 興味のあるお知らせの情報を読み取ることができる。Kyoumi no aru oshirase no jouhou o yomitoru koto ga dekiru. I can pick out the information I need from a notice that interests me.
    HI: मैं अपनी रुचि की सूचना से ज़रूरी जानकारी पढ़कर समझ सकता हूँ। GU: હું મને રસ હોય તેવી સૂચનામાંથી જરૂરી માહિતી વાંચીને સમજી શકું છું.
  • 2. 参加するイベントの内容を話して友達を誘うことができる。Sanka suru ibento no naiyou o hanashite tomodachi o sasou koto ga dekiru. I can explain an event I'm going to and invite a friend.
    HI: मैं जिस कार्यक्रम में जा रहा हूँ उसके बारे में बताकर दोस्त को बुला सकता हूँ। GU: હું જે કાર્યક્રમમાં જવાનો છું તેની વાત કરીને મિત્રને આમંત્રણ આપી શકું છું.
  • 3. 天気予報を聞き取って自分の行動を決めることができる。Tenki yohou o kikitotte jibun no koudou o kimeru koto ga dekiru. I can follow a weather forecast and decide what to do.
    HI: मैं मौसम का पूर्वानुमान सुनकर तय कर सकता हूँ कि क्या करना है। GU: હું હવામાનની આગાહી સાંભળીને નક્કી કરી શકું છું કે શું કરવું.
  • 4. 覚えてもらえるように印象的に自己紹介することができる。Oboete moraeru you ni inshouteki ni jiko shoukai suru koto ga dekiru. I can introduce myself in a memorable way, so people remember me.
    HI: मैं ऐसा प्रभावशाली परिचय दे सकता हूँ कि लोग मुझे याद रखें। GU: હું એવો પ્રભાવશાળી પરિચય આપી શકું છું કે લોકો મને યાદ રાખે.

3 Grammar & Expressions (使ってみよう やってみよう, p.18–23)

Each task has a 使ってみよう block with the same number. Blocks 2–4 open with a model text (summarised below, not transcribed), followed by 3 points. Block 1 has no model text, because its points come from the poster. The connection (接続) is given exactly as the book prints it.

5 知って楽しむ: おしゃべりのきっかけ (p.24–25)

Summary (our words; the text is not reproduced)

EN: When you don't know what to say to someone new, or you want to lead a conversation but end up only listening in Japanese, a simple game helps. Imagine a long journey with five animals: a sheep, a horse, a monkey, a lion and a cow. Your money runs out, so you must sell them one by one. Number them in the order you'd let them go. Each animal is a symbol: the warm, soft sheep is a lover, the horse (transport) is work, the monkey is friends, the lion (protection) is parents, and the cow (milk) is wealth. The order you give them up is the order you would give up those things when life gets hard. Discuss why you chose your order, then reveal the meanings, and you'll naturally learn how you and your partner think. A second game: you are 桃太郎 (Momotarou). Besides the dog, monkey and pheasant, which companion would you take to fight the ogres, and why? That animal turned out to have a serious flaw. What was it?
HI: किसी नए व्यक्ति से बात शुरू करनी हो तो एक खेल मदद करता है। कल्पना करें कि आप पाँच जानवरों (भेड़, घोड़ा, बंदर, शेर, गाय) के साथ लंबी यात्रा पर हैं, पैसे ख़त्म हो गए और उन्हें एक-एक करके बेचना है। किस क्रम में छोड़ेंगे? भेड़ = प्रेमी, घोड़ा = काम, बंदर = दोस्त, शेर = माता-पिता, गाय = संपत्ति। जिस क्रम में आप जानवर छोड़ते हैं, उसी क्रम में आप ज़िंदगी की मुश्किल में ये चीज़ें छोड़ेंगे। फिर कारणों पर बात करें। दूसरा खेल: आप मोमोतारो हैं; कुत्ते, बंदर और तीतर के अलावा कौन-सा साथी ले जाएँगे, क्यों, और उसमें कौन-सी बड़ी कमी थी?
GU: કોઈ નવી વ્યક્તિ સાથે વાત શરૂ કરવી હોય તો એક રમત મદદ કરે છે. કલ્પના કરો કે તમે પાંચ પ્રાણીઓ (ઘેટું, ઘોડો, વાંદરો, સિંહ, ગાય) સાથે લાંબી મુસાફરી પર છો, પૈસા ખૂટી ગયા અને એક-એક કરીને વેચવા પડશે. કયા ક્રમમાં છોડશો? ઘેટું = પ્રેમી, ઘોડો = કામ, વાંદરો = મિત્રો, સિંહ = માતાપિતા, ગાય = સંપત્તિ. જે ક્રમમાં પ્રાણીઓ છોડો છો, તે જ ક્રમમાં જીવનની મુશ્કેલીમાં આ વસ્તુઓ છોડશો. પછી કારણો પર વાત કરો. બીજી રમત: તમે મોમોતારો છો; કૂતરા, વાંદરા અને તેતર સિવાય કયો સાથી લઈ જશો, કેમ, અને તેમાં કઈ મોટી ખામી હતી?

8 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜においてni oite
vs でde		in / atN＋において／でSame meaning; において is formal and written.Notices, speeches and essays → において. Talking → で.
〜上でue de
vs 〜てからte kara		after doing ~V-た上で／V-てから上で = the first step is a necessary basis; てから = simple order of events.Procedures and rules ("only after checking…") → 上で.
〜たいtai
vs 〜てほしいte hoshii		wantV-ます＋たい／V-て＋ほしいたい = I want to do it myself; てほしい = I want someone else to do it (that person + に).Asking or wishing about others → てほしい／てもらいたい.
〜から〜にかけてkara ni kakete
vs 〜から〜までkara made		from ~ to ~N＋から＋N＋にかけて／までにかけて = vague span covering the whole range; まで = exact end point.Weather, seasons, regions → にかけて. Timetables → まで.
〜っぽいppoi
vs 〜らしいrashii (added)		-ish / typical ofN＋っぽい／N＋らしいっぽい = looks like something it isn't (adult acting childish, often negative); らしい = a typical example of what it is (praise).大人なのに子どもっぽい ✗ praise · 子どもらしい子 ✓ praise.
〜と、A/Vto
vs 〜たらtara		if / when普通形現在＋と／V-たらと = natural or automatic result; no wish, request or invitation in the main clause. たら allows them.雨が降ると中止です ✓ · 雨が降ったら傘を持って行って ✓ (×降ると…持って行って).
Watch out: this lesson has two "だけ"s and two "でも"s. できるだけ／好きなだけ means "as much as possible/you like", not "only". 子どもでも作れる means "even a child", not the suggestion でも of お茶でも. Ask yourself: is there an extreme or limiting idea? If so, it's "even" or "as much as".
