【ポイント37】 V ました／ませんでした

small topic 1
ReadingV mashita / V masen deshita
Meaning (EN)"did V" / "did not V" (polite past)
Meaning (HI)V किया / V नहीं किया
Meaning (GU)V કર્યું / V ન કર્યું
How it worksTake the ます form from Lesson 3 and change only the ending: ます → ました (did), ません → ませんでした (didn't). The rest of the verb stays the same.
FormPresent / future (Lesson 3)Past (this lesson)
Affirmative行きますikimasu — go / will go行きましたikimashita — went
Negative行きませんikimasen — don't / won't go行きませんでしたikimasen deshita — didn't go
Question何をしますかnani o shimasu ka何をしましたかnani o shimashita ka — what did you do?
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
おととい、新宿へ行きました。Ototoi, Shinjuku e ikimashita.I went to Shinjuku the day before yesterday.परसों मैं शिंजुकु गया/गई।ગયા પરમ દિવસે હું શિંજુકુ ગયો/ગઈ.
昨日、勉強しませんでした。Kinou, benkyou shimasen deshita.I didn't study yesterday.कल मैंने पढ़ाई नहीं की।ગઈકાલે મેં અભ્યાસ ન કર્યો.
A：日曜日(に)、何をしましたか。 B：友達の家へ行きました。 A：そうですか。Nichiyoubi (ni), nani o shimashita ka. — Tomodachi no ie e ikimashita. — Sou desu ka.What did you do on Sunday? — I went to a friend's house. — I see. (言ってみよう 1-1)रविवार को आपने क्या किया? — मैं दोस्त के घर गया/गई। — अच्छा।રવિવારે તમે શું કર્યું? — હું મિત્રના ઘરે ગયો/ગઈ. — અચ્છા.
うちでテレビを見ました。Uchi de terebi o mimashita.I watched TV at home. (言ってみよう 1-2)मैंने घर पर टीवी देखा।મેં ઘરે ટીવી જોયું.

Japanese verbs have only two tenses: "non-past" (ます: now, usually, or future) and "past" (ました). Time words like 昨日 and 先週 make the meaning clear. Time words without a number (昨日, 週末, 今朝) don't need に. 日曜日 can take に or not: 日曜日(に).

【ポイント38】 イA かったです／くなかったです ・ ナA・N でした／じゃありませんでした

small topic 2
Readingi-A (−i) katta desu / kunakatta desu · na-A, N deshita / ja arimasen deshita
Meaning (EN)"was A" / "was not A"; "was N" / "was not N"
Meaning (HI)A था / A नहीं था; N था / N नहीं था

新宿でおすしを食べました。おいしかったです。Shinjuku de osushi o tabemashita. Oishikatta desu.I ate sushi in Shinjuku. It was delicious. (言ってみよう 1-3, topic 2)मैंने शिंजुकु में सुशी खाई। स्वादिष्ट थी।મેં શિંજુકુમાં સુશી ખાધી. સ્વાદિષ્ટ હતી.

楽しいでした is a very common mistake. With イ-adjectives the past is inside the adjective (楽しかった), and です just adds politeness. Use どうでしたか to ask "how was it?". It is the past of どうですか (ポイント33).

【ポイント39】 N が 好きです／嫌いです

small topic 3
ReadingN ga suki desu / kirai desu
Meaning (EN)"I like N" / "I dislike N"
Meaning (HI)मुझे N पसंद है / नापसंद है
Meaning (GU)મને N ગમે છે / નથી ગમતું
How it worksThe thing you like takes が, not を. Japanese says "N is likeable (to me)", just like Hindi "मुझे N पसंद है" — N is the subject, not the object. 好き and 嫌い are ナ-adjectives.

嫌いです is strong, a bit like "I hate it". To be polite about food someone offers you, say あまり好きじゃありません instead.
