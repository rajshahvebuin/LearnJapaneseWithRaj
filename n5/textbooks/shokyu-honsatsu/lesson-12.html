Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.205)

Four pictures: friends visiting a man who is sick in bed, a woman taking a pill next to a first-aid box, a medicine packet (内服薬 naifukuyaku "medicine to swallow", 1日3回 4日分 "3 times a day, for 4 days") with a thermometer and a health-insurance card, and a doctor listening to a patient's chest. Talk about it: what do you do when you catch a cold?
HI: जब आपको सर्दी-ज़ुकाम होता है, तब आप क्या करते हैं? · GU: તમને શરદી થાય ત્યારે તમે શું કરો છો?

Culture note: in Japan you take your 保険証 (hokenshou, health-insurance card) to every clinic visit. The doctor writes a 処方箋 (shohousen, prescription) and you get the medicine at a separate 薬局 (yakkyoku, pharmacy). Many people wear a mask (マスク) when they have a cold.

【ポイント104】 普通形 + んです

small topics 1 · 2 · 3
Reading(plain form) + n desu
Meaning (EN)"It's that …" — explaining a reason or situation; どうしたんですか = "What's the matter?"
Meaning (HI)"बात यह है कि …" — कारण या हालत समझाना; どうしたんですか = "क्या हुआ?"
Meaning (GU)"વાત એમ છે કે …" — કારણ કે પરિસ્થિતિ સમજાવવી; どうしたんですか = "શું થયું?"
How it worksPut the plain (dictionary / た / ない) form before んです. It sounds softer and more personal than plain です／ます: you are explaining. Someone sees you look ill and asks どうしたんですか; you answer with 〜んです. With な-adjectives and nouns, add な: 暇なんです, 風邪なんです.
Word typePatternExample
Verb (now)V辞書形 + んです熱があるんです。Netsu ga aru n desu.
Verb (past)Vタ形 + んですけがをしたんです。Kega o shita n desu.
Verb (negative)(added, not in book) Vナイ形 + んです食欲がないんです。Shokuyoku ga nai n desu.
い-adjectiveイA + んです頭が痛いんです。Atama ga itai n desu.
な-adjectiveナA + なんです暇なんです。Hima na n desu.
NounN + なんです風邪なんです。Kaze na n desu.

"痛いです" just states a fact; "痛いんです" explains: "(the reason I look like this is) it hurts". When you ask about someone's problem, always use どうしたんですか, not どうしましたか, unless you are a doctor (the doctor in the dialogue uses どうしましたか, which is also fine and a bit more formal).

【ポイント105】 Vタ形／Vナイ形 + ほうがいいです

small topic 2
ReadingV-ta / V-nai + hou ga ii desu
Meaning (EN)"You'd better (do) / You'd better not (do)" — giving advice
Meaning (HI)"…करना बेहतर है / …न करना बेहतर है" — सलाह देना
