Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.253)

Four pictures: a map of Japan headed 「きょうの天気予報」 (today's weather forecast), a man with a cup of tea watching a festival on TV, a long line of people waiting outside a shop, and a pile of travel magazines (one says 箱根, Hakone). Talk about it: what information do you get from TV and magazines? Do you ever go somewhere because you saw it there?
HI: आप टीवी और पत्रिकाओं से कौन-सी जानकारी लेते हैं? क्या कभी वहाँ देखकर कहीं गए हैं? · GU: તમે ટીવી અને મૅગેઝિનમાંથી કઈ માહિતી મેળવો છો? ક્યારેય ત્યાં જોઈને ક્યાંક ગયા છો?

Culture note: Japanese TV news always ends with a detailed 天気予報 (tenki yohou, weather forecast). Town information magazines (情報誌) list new shops, sales and events, and a shop that appears in one often gets a long queue the next day.

2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 119–124, p.280–281)

The "plain form" (普通形) used in 119 and 123 is the dictionary-style form you learned for 〜と思います in Lesson 14: 来る, 来ない, 来た; おいしい; 無料だ; 雨だ.

【ポイント119】 普通形 + そうです

small topics 1, 2
Reading(plain form) + sou desu
Meaning (EN)"I heard that … / They say that …" — passing on information
Meaning (HI)"सुना है कि … / कहते हैं कि …"
Meaning (GU)"સાંભળ્યું છે કે … / કહે છે કે …"
How it worksPut the news in plain form and add そうです. It tells the listener "this isn't my own idea, I got it from TV, a magazine or a person". With な-adjectives and nouns, add だ: 無料だそうです, 中止だそうです. Add よ (そうですよ) when you tell a friend something new.

Verb (past)Vタ形 + そうですビルが倒れたそうです。Biru ga taoreta sou desu.

Typical reply to news: へえ (oh?), 本当ですか (really?), then your feeling: 心配ですね (that's worrying), 怖いですね (scary), よかったですね (that's great). Don't mix this up with おいしそうです "looks tasty" — that is a different そう, joined to a different form (see §5).

【ポイント120】 〜たら、〜

Reading-tara, …
Meaning (EN)"If A, (then) B" / "When A happens, B"
Meaning (HI)"अगर A हो, तो B"

Biggest trap: 〜たら vs 〜ても. They look alike (both come from a verb's past or て form), but the meaning is opposite. 雨が降ったら、中止です = if it rains, it's cancelled (the rain changes things). 雨が降っても、あります = even if it rains, it's on (the rain changes nothing). Ask yourself: "Does A change the result?" Yes → たら. No → ても.
