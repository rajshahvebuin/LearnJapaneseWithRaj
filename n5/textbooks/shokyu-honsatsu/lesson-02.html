Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.31)

Four pictures: two women chatting while shopping in a supermarket, Japanese money (credit cards, 1,000-yen and 5,000-yen notes, coins), a young man paying at a shop counter, and two friends choosing food from a display case. Talk about it: where do you shop, and how do you pay?
HI: आप कहाँ खरीदारी करते हैं, और पैसे कैसे देते हैं? · GU: તમે ક્યાં ખરીદી કરો છો, અને પૈસા કેવી રીતે ચૂકવો છો?

Culture note: Japanese money is the 円 (en, yen). Coins go up to 500円, notes start at 1,000円. Shop staff greet you with いらっしゃいませ (irasshaimase) = "welcome". You don't need to answer it; a small nod is enough.

【ポイント7】 これ／それ／あれ

small topics 2 · 3
Readingkore / sore / are
Meaning (EN)"this one" (near me) / "that one" (near you) / "that one over there" (far from us both)
