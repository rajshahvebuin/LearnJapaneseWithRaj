2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 113–118, p.280)

【ポイント113】 V辞書形 + と、〜

small topic 1
ReadingV (dictionary form) + to, …
Meaning (EN)"When / if you do A, B (always) happens"
Meaning (HI)"A करने पर B (हमेशा) होता है"
Meaning (GU)"A કરીએ એટલે B (હંમેશાં) થાય"
How it worksDictionary form + と. The second half is a natural, automatic result — like how a machine works. So it's perfect for explaining how to use things. The second half can't be a request or wish (no 〜てください after this と).
FormPatternExample
MachineV辞書形 + と、〜ますボタンを押すと、開きます。Botan o osu to, akimasu.
ChangeV辞書形 + と、〜くなります足を入れると、暖かくなります。Ashi o ireru to, atatakaku narimasu.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このボタンを押すと、水が出ます。Kono botan o osu to, mizu ga demasu.When you press this button, water comes out.यह बटन दबाने पर पानी निकलता है।આ બટન દબાવીએ એટલે પાણી નીકળે છે.
あっ、そのボタンを押すと、開きますよ。A, sono botan o osu to, akimasu yo.Oh, if you press that button, it opens. (言ってみよう 1-1, p.240)अरे, वह बटन दबाओ तो खुल जाता है।અરે, એ બટન દબાવો એટલે ખૂલી જાય છે.
こたつに足を入れると、暖かくなります。Kotatsu ni ashi o ireru to, atatakaku narimasu.When you put your feet in the kotatsu, they get warm. (言ってみよう 1-2)कोतात्सु में पैर डालने पर गरमी मिलती है।કોતાત્સુમાં પગ નાખીએ એટલે ગરમ થાય છે.

Compare: 〜てから (Lesson 12) is "after A, I do B" (my choice); 〜と is "if A, then B happens by itself" (cause and effect).

【ポイント114】 Vテ形 + はいけません

small topic 2
ReadingV-te wa ikemasen
Meaning (EN)"You must not …" — a rule
Meaning (HI)"… नहीं करना चाहिए / मना है"
Meaning (GU)"… ન કરાય / મનાઈ છે"
How it worksて form + は (said "wa") + いけません. It's strong. Use it for real rules and signs. Adding よ (〜てはいけませんよ) makes it a friendly warning to a friend.
FormPatternExample
RuleVテ形 + はいけません止めてはいけません。Tomete wa ikemasen.
Friendly warningVテ形 + はいけませんよ使ってはいけませんよ。Tsukatte wa ikemasen yo.
Opposite (permission)(added, from an earlier lesson) Vテ形 + もいいです使ってもいいです。Tsukatte mo ii desu.
