Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.15)

The book opens with four photos: two people swapping business cards (名刺 meishi), a handshake, a group meeting outdoors, and an office greeting. Talk about it: how do people greet each other the first time in your country?
HI: आपके देश में लोग पहली बार मिलने पर कैसे अभिवादन करते हैं? · GU: તમારા દેશમાં લોકો પહેલી વાર મળે ત્યારે કેવી રીતે અભિવાદન કરે છે?

Culture note: in Japan people usually bow while saying はじめまして (hajimemashite). At work they swap business cards with both hands. A handshake is less common, a bit like नमस्ते / નમસ્તે being more usual than a handshake in India.

2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 1–6, p.270)

The lesson pages only say "☞ ポイント 1, 2, 3, 4" in the corner. The book gives each pattern with one to three examples at the back (p.270). Below, each one is explained in plain words, with every book example plus the model lines from the 言ってみよう drills.

【ポイント1】 N1 は N2 です

small topics 1 · 2
ReadingN1 wa N2 desu
Meaning (EN)"N1 is N2." (am / is / are)
Meaning (HI)N1, N2 है / हूँ / हैं।
Meaning (GU)N1, N2 છે / છું / છો.
How it worksは marks the topic ("as for N1…"). It is written は but always said wa. です is the polite "is". It never changes for I / you / he / she.
FormPatternExample
AffirmativeN1 は N2 です私は学生です。Watashi wa gakusei desu.
Topic left out(私は) N2 です(私は)ワンです。(Watashi wa) Wan desu.
NegativeN1 は N2 じゃありません→ see ポイント2
Past(added, not in book — Lesson 5) N1 は N2 でした学生でした。Gakusei deshita.

はじめまして。ダニエルです。オーストラリア人です。Hajimemashite. Danieru desu. Oosutoraria-jin desu.Nice to meet you. I'm Daniel. I'm Australian. (言ってみよう 1-2)आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई। मैं डैनियल हूँ। मैं ऑस्ट्रेलियाई हूँ।તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો. હું ડેનિયલ છું. હું ઑસ્ટ્રેલિયન છું.

Japanese drops anything that is already clear. When you introduce yourself, everyone knows you are talking about yourself, so 私は is usually left out: just ダニエルです. Saying 私は in every sentence sounds stiff.

【ポイント2】 N1 は N2 ですか — はい、N2 です／いいえ、N2 じゃありません

small topic 1
ReadingN1 wa N2 desu ka — hai, N2 desu / iie, N2 ja arimasen
Meaning (EN)"Is N1 N2?" — "Yes, it is N2." / "No, it is not N2."
Meaning (HI)क्या N1, N2 है? — हाँ, N2 है। / नहीं, N2 नहीं है।
Meaning (GU)શું N1, N2 છે? — હા, N2 છે. / ના, N2 નથી.
How it worksPut か at the end to make a question, like a spoken question mark. The word order does not change. For "not", change です to じゃありません.
FormPatternExample
QuestionN1 は N2 ですかBさんは学生ですか。B-san wa gakusei desu ka.
Yesはい、N2 ですはい、学生です。Hai, gakusei desu.
Noいいえ、N2 じゃありませんいいえ、学生じゃありません。Iie, gakusei ja arimasen.
No (more formal)(added, not in book) N2 ではありません学生ではありません。Gakusei dewa arimasen.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ワンさんは学生ですか。Wan-san wa gakusei desu ka.Are you a student, Ms. Wang?वांग जी, क्या आप विद्यार्थी हैं?વાંગબેન, શું તમે વિદ્યાર્થી છો?
— はい、学生です。／いいえ、学生じゃありません。— Hai, gakusei desu. / Iie, gakusei ja arimasen.— Yes, I am. / No, I'm not a student.— हाँ, विद्यार्थी हूँ। / नहीं, विद्यार्थी नहीं हूँ।— હા, વિદ્યાર્થી છું. / ના, વિદ્યાર્થી નથી.
いいえ、(私は)学生じゃありません。会社員です。Iie, (watashi wa) gakusei ja arimasen. Kaishain desu.No, I'm not a student. I'm an office worker. (言ってみよう 1-5)नहीं, मैं विद्यार्थी नहीं हूँ। मैं कंपनी कर्मचारी हूँ।ના, હું વિદ્યાર્થી નથી. હું કંપની કર્મચારી છું.

In Japanese you usually call the person you are talking to by name + さん, not "you". So "Are you a student?" becomes "Is B-san a student?" (Bさんは学生ですか). After いいえ it is natural to say the right answer as well: 学生じゃありません。会社員です。

【ポイント4】 N1 の N2

small topics 1 · 2 · 3
ReadingN1 no N2
Meaning (EN)"N2 of N1" / "N1's N2" — the student of Fujimi University, my birthday
Meaning (HI)N1 का / की / के N2 (जैसे: मेरा जन्मदिन)

Biggest trap: さん is never used for yourself. Say はじめまして。パクです。, not パクさんです. Also don't add お to your own things: 私の名前は〜, not 私のお名前. さん and お show respect to the other person, a bit like using आप / તમે for others but never for yourself.
