2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 24–36, p.272–273)

This is the "adjective lesson". Japanese has two kinds of adjectives: イ-adjectives (end in い: 大きい, 暑い) and ナ-adjectives (take な before a noun: きれいな, 静かな). They make "not" in different ways, so learn which kind each word is. Small topic 1 uses points 29–31, topic 2 uses 24, 25, 28, 32, 34, 35, and topic 3 uses 26, 27, 33, 36.

【ポイント24】 N は A です ／ イA くないです ／ ナA じゃありません

small topic 2
ReadingN wa A desu / N wa i-A (−i) kunai desu / N wa na-A ja arimasen
Meaning (EN)"N is A." / "N is not A." (A = adjective)
Meaning (HI)N, A है। / N, A नहीं है।
Meaning (GU)N, A છે. / N, A નથી.
How it worksPut the adjective before です. For "not": an イ-adjective drops its last い and adds くないです. A ナ-adjective works like a noun: じゃありません. Special: いい → よくないです.

【ポイント25】 イA + N ／ ナA な + N

small topic 2
Readingi-A + N / na-A na + N
Meaning (EN)"an A N" — a big castle, a lively place
Meaning (HI)A N (जैसे: बड़ा किला, चहल-पहल वाली जगह)
Meaning (GU)A N (જેમ કે: મોટો કિલ્લો, ધમધમતી જગ્યા)
