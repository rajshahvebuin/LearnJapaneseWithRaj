Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.221)

Four pictures: a pile of music CDs, three friends looking at photos on a camera, a night cruise / a foot massage / people in a hot spring, and a roller coaster with a Ferris wheel. Talk about it: what music, place or experience would you recommend to a friend?
HI: आप अपने दोस्त को कौन-सा संगीत, जगह या अनुभव सुझाएँगे? · GU: તમે તમારા મિત્રને કયું સંગીત, જગ્યા કે અનુભવ સૂચવશો?

Useful word: おすすめ (osusume) = a recommendation. 私のおすすめは〜です = "My recommendation is ~". Restaurants in Japan often have a board saying 本日のおすすめ (honjitsu no osusume), "today's special".

2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 108–112, p.279–280)

The lesson pages only say "☞ ポイント 108, 111, 112" and so on in the corner. The book gives each pattern with examples at the back (p.279–280). Below, each one is explained in plain words, with every book example plus the model lines from the 言ってみよう drills.

【ポイント108】 Vタ形 + ことがあります

small topic 1
ReadingV-ta koto ga arimasu
Meaning (EN)"I have (ever) done …" — talking about experience
Meaning (HI)"मैंने (कभी) … किया है" — अनुभव के बारे में
Meaning (GU)"મેં (ક્યારેય) … કર્યું છે" — અનુભવ વિશે
How it worksTake the past plain form (た form), add ことがあります. Literally "there is the fact that I did …". For "never", say ことがありません, often with 1回も (not even once). Answer the question with just あります／ありません.
FormPatternExample
Have doneVタ形 + ことがあります登ったことがあります。Nobotta koto ga arimasu.
NeverVタ形 + ことがありません行ったことがありません。Itta koto ga arimasen.
QuestionVタ形 + ことがありますか行ったことがありますか。— はい、あります。Itta koto ga arimasu ka. — Hai, arimasu.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は富士山に登ったことがあります。Watashi wa Fujisan ni nobotta koto ga arimasu.I have climbed Mt. Fuji.मैं फ़ूजी पर्वत पर चढ़ चुका/चुकी हूँ।હું ફૂજી પર્વત પર ચડ્યો/ચડી છું.
私は1回も北海道へ行ったことがありません。Watashi wa ikkai mo Hokkaidou e itta koto ga arimasen.I have never been to Hokkaido, not even once.मैं एक बार भी होक्काइदो नहीं गया/गई।હું એક વાર પણ હોક્કાઇદો ગયો/ગઈ નથી.
Bさんは沖縄へ行ったことがありますか。 — はい、あります。先月、行きました。B-san wa Okinawa e itta koto ga arimasu ka. — Hai, arimasu. Sengetsu, ikimashita.Have you been to Okinawa? — Yes, I have. I went last month. (言ってみよう 1, p.224)क्या आप ओकिनावा गए हैं? — हाँ, गया हूँ। पिछले महीने गया था।શું તમે ઓકિનાવા ગયા છો? — હા, ગયો છું. ગયા મહિને ગયો હતો.

Note the switch: the question is about experience (ことがありますか), but the details are plain past: 先月、行きました ("I went last month"). Don't say 先月、行ったことがあります: when you give a specific time, just use the past tense.

【ポイント109】 普通形 + N

small topics 2 · 3
Reading(plain form sentence) + noun
Meaning (EN)"the N that …" — a sentence that describes a noun (the magazine that I often read)
Meaning (HI)"वह N जो …" — वाक्य से संज्ञा का वर्णन (जो पत्रिका मैं अक्सर पढ़ता हूँ)
Meaning (GU)"તે N જે …" — વાક્યથી સંજ્ઞાનું વર્ણન (જે સામયિક હું વારંવાર વાંચું છું)
How it worksJapanese puts the describing part before the noun, just like an adjective: 新しい雑誌 → 私がよく読む雑誌. Use the plain form, no です／ます. Inside the describing part, the doer takes が, not は: 私がよく読む雑誌.

VerbV普通形 + N私がよく読む雑誌watashi ga yoku yomu zasshi

い-adjective(added, not in book) イA + Nサービスがいいホテルsaabisu ga ii hoteru
な-adjective(added, not in book) ナA な + N紅葉がきれいなところkouyou ga kirei na tokoro

これは私がよく読む雑誌です。Kore wa watashi ga yoku yomu zasshi desu.This is a magazine I often read.यह वह पत्रिका है जो मैं अक्सर पढ़ता/पढ़ती हूँ।આ એ સામયિક છે જે હું વારંવાર વાંચું છું.
私がよく行くレストランは渋谷にあります。Watashi ga yoku iku resutoran wa Shibuya ni arimasu.The restaurant I often go to is in Shibuya.जिस रेस्टोरेंट में मैं अक्सर जाता/जाती हूँ, वह शिबुया में है।જે રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં હું વારંવાર જાઉં છું તે શિબુયામાં છે.
今度みんなで飲み会をする店を探しています。Kondo minna de nomikai o suru mise o sagashite imasu.I'm looking for a place where we can all have a party next time.मैं ऐसी जगह ढूँढ रहा/रही हूँ जहाँ अगली बार हम सब पार्टी कर सकें।હું એવી જગ્યા શોધું છું જ્યાં આવતી વખતે આપણે બધા પાર્ટી કરી શકીએ.
もみじ屋は新鮮な魚料理を食べることができる居酒屋ですよ。Momijiya wa shinsen na sakana ryouri o taberu koto ga dekiru izakaya desu yo.Momijiya is a pub where you can eat fresh fish dishes. (言ってみよう 1, p.228)मोमिजिया एक इज़ाकाया है जहाँ ताज़ा मछली के व्यंजन मिलते हैं।મોમિજિયા એક ઇઝાકાયા છે જ્યાં તાજી માછલીની વાનગીઓ મળે છે.
Bさん、パーティーでよくするゲームは何ですか。B-san, paatii de yoku suru geemu wa nan desu ka.What games do you often play at parties? (言ってみよう 2, p.233)पार्टी में आप अक्सर कौन-सा खेल खेलते हैं?પાર્ટીમાં તમે વારંવાર કઈ રમત રમો છો?

Hindi and Gujarati use "जो … वह" / "જે … તે"; Japanese has no such word. You just put the whole description in front of the noun. Read it backwards: 私がよく読む│雑誌 = magazine ← (that) I often read.

small topic 2
ReadingV-te imasu
Meaning (EN)"is wearing …" — a state that continues after an action
Meaning (HI)"… पहने हुए है" — किसी काम के बाद बनी रहने वाली हालत

【ポイント111】 知っています／知りません

small topics 1 · 3
Readingshitte imasu / shirimasen
Meaning (EN)"I know" / "I don't know"
Meaning (HI)"मैं जानता/जानती हूँ" / "मैं नहीं जानता/जानती"
Meaning (GU)"હું જાણું છું" / "હું નથી જાણતો/જાણતી"
How it works知る means "come to know", so "know" is the state 知っています. But the negative is not 知っていません — it is 知りません. This is a special pair; learn it as a set.
FormPatternExample
