できる日本語 初級 本冊

Following できる日本語 初級 本冊 (Dekiru Nihongo, Beginner main textbook, アルク / ALC Press, 2011) lesson by lesson. Each of the 15 lessons (課) is built around one real-life goal, such as introducing yourself or shopping, and splits it into three small topics. Every lesson page gives the full word list, every sentence pattern (ポイント) with examples, the speaking drills with model answers, a short summary of the dialogue, and beginner traps. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Each lesson has three small topics. Each one goes チャレンジ！ (try the situation from the pictures) → 言ってみよう (practise the model sentences) → やってみよう (listen to the CD, then do the task with classmates).
  • The lesson ends with できる！, a real task that uses everything from the lesson, then the word list (ことば) and the script of the opening dialogue (もう一度聞こう).
  • The book does not explain grammar on the lesson pages. It only points to ポイント numbers at the back. Our lesson pages explain each ポイント in plain language.
  • Dialogues are summarised in our own words, with only the key lines quoted.
