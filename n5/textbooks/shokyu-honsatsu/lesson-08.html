Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.137)

Four pictures: parents and a daughter at a birthday cake, three old photos of two brothers growing up, a gift box with a bunch of flowers, and a wedding with friends throwing petals. Culture note: Japanese has two sets of family words — a plain set for your own family (父, 母, 兄…) and a polite set for other people's family (お父さん, お母さん, お兄さん…). This lesson teaches both. Think of it like saying "मेरे पापा" but "आपके पिताजी" / "મારા પપ્પા" but "તમારા પિતાજી".

【ポイント72】 Vて います (a state that continues: live, be married)

small topic 1
ReadingV-te imasu
Meaning (EN)"(I) live in …", "(he) is married" — something happened once and the result is still true now
Meaning (HI)… में रहता/रहती हूँ; शादीशुदा है — एक बार हुआ और अब भी वैसा ही है
Meaning (GU)… માં રહું છું; પરણેલા છે — એક વાર થયું અને હજી પણ એવું જ છે
How it worksTake the て-form of the verb (Lesson 7) and add います. With 住む (live), Japanese always says 住んでいます, never just 住みます, because you moved in once and you still live there. The place takes に: 横浜に住んでいます.
FormPatternExample
Affirmativeplace に 住んでいます横浜に住んでいます。Yokohama ni sunde imasu.
Questionどこに 住んでいますかどこに住んでいますか。Doko ni sunde imasu ka.
Negative(added, not in book) 〜ていません結婚していません。Kekkon shite imasen. — (I'm) not married.
て-form reminder(added) 住みます → 住んで · 結婚します → 結婚して結婚しています。Kekkon shite imasu. — (He) is married.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は横浜に住んでいます。Watashi wa Yokohama ni sunde imasu.I live in Yokohama.मैं योकोहामा में रहता/रहती हूँ।હું યોકોહામામાં રહું છું.
Bさんはどこに住んでいますか。B-san wa doko ni sunde imasu ka.Where do you live? (言ってみよう 1-1)आप कहाँ रहते/रहती हैं?તમે ક્યાં રહો છો?
この人は結婚していますか。Kono hito wa kekkon shite imasu ka.Is this person married? (もう一度聞こう)क्या यह व्यक्ति शादीशुदा है?શું આ વ્યક્તિ પરણેલી છે?

Careful: 結婚しています means "is married" (a state), not "is getting married right now". Hindi and Gujarati work the same way: "रहता हूँ" / "રહું છું" describes where you live now.

【ポイント73】 Vて います (what someone does these days: job, study)

small topic 1
ReadingV-te imasu
Meaning (EN)"(She) teaches / works / is studying …" — a job or activity someone does over a period of time
Meaning (HI)(वह) … पढ़ाती है / काम करता है / पढ़ाई कर रहा है (आजकल)
Meaning (GU)(તે) … ભણાવે છે / કામ કરે છે / અભ્યાસ કરે છે (આજકાલ)
How it worksSame shape as ポイント72, different use: it tells someone's job or what they are busy with these days. The place where you do it takes で: デパートで働いています. To ask about a job: 何をしていますか ("What does she do?").
FormPatternExample
Job / activityplace で V-ています高校で英語を教えています。Koukou de eigo o oshiete imasu.
Question何をしていますかお姉さんは何をしていますか。Oneesan wa nani o shite imasu ka.

Biggest trap: あげます vs くれます. In English, Hindi and Gujarati one verb (give / देना / આપવું) covers everything. In Japanese, when a present comes to you, you must say くれました: 友達がCDをくれました, never 友達がCDをあげました. Quick test: if the arrow points at me (or my family) → くれる; any other direction → あげる.
