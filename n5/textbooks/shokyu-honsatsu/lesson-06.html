Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.101)

Four pictures: three friends in front of a noodle shop (麺屋 menya), tickets for a classical concert and a soccer match, friends singing karaoke, and three women planning a trip with a Mt Fuji / Kawaguchiko magazine. Talk about it: what do you like to do with friends on your days off? How do you invite them?
HI: छुट्टी के दिन आप दोस्तों के साथ क्या करना पसंद करते हैं? उन्हें कैसे बुलाते हैं? · GU: રજાના દિવસે તમને મિત્રો સાથે શું કરવું ગમે છે? તમે તેમને કેવી રીતે બોલાવો છો?

Culture note: Japanese people rarely say a direct "no" to an invitation. Instead they say 〜はちょっと…… (… wa chotto…), "… is a little…", and let the sentence trail off. The other person understands it is a no. This lesson teaches exactly that.

【ポイント48・49】 V ませんか ／ V ましょう

small topics 1 · 3
ReadingV-masen ka / V-mashou
Meaning (EN)ませんか: "Won't you…? / Would you like to … (with me)?" — an invitation. ましょう: "Let's …!"
Meaning (HI)ませんか: क्या आप (मेरे साथ) … चलेंगे? — निमंत्रण। ましょう: चलो … करते हैं!
Meaning (GU)ませんか: શું તમે (મારી સાથે) … આવશો? — આમંત્રણ. ましょう: ચાલો … કરીએ!
How it worksTake the ます form and change ます to ませんか. It looks like a negative question ("Won't you eat?"), but it is a friendly, polite invitation. To say yes, change ます to ましょう. To say no politely, use 〜はちょっと…… and stop.

Refuse (soft)すみません。〜はちょっと……すみません。今晩はちょっと……。Sumimasen. Konban wa chotto…
Refuse + reason〜はちょっと……。N が ありますから用事がありますから。Youji ga arimasu kara. (から = because, Lesson 5)

【ポイント50】 N が あります (plans)

small topic 1
ReadingN ga arimasu
Meaning (EN)"I have N" — a plan, an appointment, a test, a job
Meaning (HI)(मेरा) N है — कोई काम, मुलाकात, परीक्षा
Meaning (GU)(મારે) N છે — કોઈ કામ, મુલાકાત, પરીક્ષા
How it worksあります means "there is". With words like 約束 (plans), 用事 (errand), テスト (test), アルバイト (part-time job), it means "I have …". が marks the thing that exists.
