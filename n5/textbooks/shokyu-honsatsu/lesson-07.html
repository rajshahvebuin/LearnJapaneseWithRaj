Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.117)

Four pictures: two friends waving to each other at station ticket gates, friends playing a block-tower game, a group sharing a hot-pot (鍋 nabe) at home, and party goods (funny glasses, party hats). Talk about it: do you hold parties at home? What do guests do to help?
HI: क्या आप घर पर पार्टी करते हैं? मेहमान मदद के लिए क्या करते हैं? · GU: શું તમે ઘરે પાર્ટી કરો છો? મહેમાનો મદદ માટે શું કરે છે?

Culture note: home parties in Japan are often potluck style. Guests bring food or drinks (持って来ます) and everyone helps to cook and clear up. Saying 手伝いましょうか ("Shall I help?") when you arrive is good manners.

【ポイント61】 N1(人) は N2(場所) に います ／ N1(物) は N2(場所) に あります

small topic 1
ReadingN1 (hito) wa N2 (basho) ni imasu / N1 (mono) wa N2 (basho) ni arimasu
Meaning (EN)"N1 is at/in N2" — saying where a known person or thing is
Meaning (HI)N1, N2 (जगह) में है
Meaning (GU)N1, N2 (જગ્યા)માં છે
How it worksJapanese has two verbs for "be there": います for living things that move (people, animals) and あります for things and places. に marks the place. Location words go after の: 駅の前, 本屋の中.
FormPatternExample
Person人 は 場所 に います私は本屋にいます。Watashi wa hon'ya ni imasu.
Thing物 は 場所 に ありますバス停はコンビニの前にあります。Basutei wa konbini no mae ni arimasu.
Question〜は どこに いますか／ありますか交番はどこにありますか。Kouban wa doko ni arimasu ka.
PositionN の 前・後ろ・上・下・中・外・横・隣・近くあのビルの後ろano biru no ushiro · AとBの間A to B no aida
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は本屋にいます。Watashi wa hon'ya ni imasu.I'm at the bookshop.मैं किताबों की दुकान पर हूँ।હું પુસ્તકની દુકાને છું.
バス停はコンビニの前にあります。Basutei wa konbini no mae ni arimasu.The bus stop is in front of the convenience store.बस स्टॉप कन्वीनियंस स्टोर के सामने है।બસ સ્ટોપ કન્વિનિયન્સ સ્ટોરની સામે છે.
あのう、すみません。交番はどこにありますか。 — あ、交番ですか。あのビルの後ろにありますよ。Anou, sumimasen. Kouban wa doko ni arimasu ka. — A, kouban desu ka. Ano biru no ushiro ni arimasu yo.Excuse me, where is the police box? — The police box? It's behind that building. (言ってみよう 1-1)माफ़ कीजिए, पुलिस चौकी कहाँ है? — पुलिस चौकी? उस इमारत के पीछे है।માફ કરજો, પોલીસ ચોકી ક્યાં છે? — પોલીસ ચોકી? પેલી ઇમારતની પાછળ છે.
もしもし、Bさん、今、どこにいますか。 — 交番の前にいます。Moshi moshi, B-san, ima, doko ni imasu ka. — Kouban no mae ni imasu.Hello, where are you now? — I'm in front of the police box. (言ってみよう 1-2)हेलो, आप अभी कहाँ हैं? — पुलिस चौकी के सामने हूँ।હેલો, તમે અત્યારે ક્યાં છો? — પોલીસ ચોકીની સામે છું.

The order is the reverse of English: 駅の前 = "station's front" = "in front of the station". Same as Hindi स्टेशन के सामने / Gujarati સ્ટેશનની સામે, so it should feel natural.

【ポイント62】 N1(場所) に N2(人・動物) が います ／ N1(場所) に N2(物) が あります

small topic 1
ReadingN1 (basho) ni N2 ga imasu / arimasu
Meaning (EN)"There is N2 at N1" — saying what is at a place
Meaning (HI)N1 (जगह) में N2 है
Meaning (GU)N1 (જગ્યા)માં N2 છે
How it worksStart with the place + に, then the new thing + が. Use this when the listener doesn't know yet what is there ("Near me there is a convenience store"). ポイント61 (は) is for a thing you are already talking about.
FormPatternExample
Person / animal場所 に 人 が いますあそこにパクさんがいます。Asoko ni Paku-san ga imasu.
Thing場所 に 物 が あります銀行の前に本屋があります。Ginkou no mae ni hon'ya ga arimasu.
Question場所 に 何が ありますか／誰が いますか近くに何がありますか。Chikaku ni nani ga arimasu ka.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あそこにパクさんがいます。Asoko ni Paku-san ga imasu.Park is over there.वहाँ पाक जी हैं।ત્યાં પાકબેન છે.
銀行の前に本屋があります。Ginkou no mae ni hon'ya ga arimasu.There's a bookshop in front of the bank.बैंक के सामने किताबों की दुकान है।બેંકની સામે પુસ્તકની દુકાન છે.
えっ？ 近くに何がありますか。 — 大きいスーパーがあります。 — わかりました。今、迎えに行きます。E? Chikaku ni nani ga arimasu ka. — Ookii suupaa ga arimasu. — Wakarimashita. Ima, mukae ni ikimasu.Huh? What's near you? — There's a big supermarket. — OK. I'll come and get you now. (言ってみよう 1-3)हैं? आसपास क्या है? — एक बड़ा सुपरमार्केट है। — ठीक है, मैं अभी तुम्हें लेने आता हूँ।હેં? આસપાસ શું છે? — એક મોટું સુપરમાર્કેટ છે. — સારું, હું હમણાં તને લેવા આવું છું.

Japanese says 迎えに行きます ("I'll go to get you") where English says "I'll come and get you". 行きます is always movement away from where the speaker is now.

【ポイント63】 Vテ形 ください

small topic 2
ReadingV-te kudasai
Meaning (EN)"Please do V" — a polite request or instruction
Meaning (HI)कृपया V कीजिए
Meaning (GU)મહેરબાની કરીને V કરો
How it worksThis is the first use of the て-form (テ形), a very important verb form. Change the ます-form into the て-form, then add ください. How you make the て-form depends on the verb group (table below).

Look at line 4: 外にいます (where he is) and 外でたばこを吸っています (where he is doing something) side by side. That one pair sums up the lesson. Listen to Track 16 again after studying §2.

Biggest trap: question words take が, never は. 誰が作りましたか, 何がありますか, どれがいいですか — not 誰は. And the answer keeps が: ワンさんが作りました. Think of it this way: は marks something you already know; a question word is exactly the thing you don't know, so it can't take は.
