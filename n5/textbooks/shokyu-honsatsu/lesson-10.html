Warm-up · 話してみよう (p.169)

Four pictures: friends getting on a tour bus, a lunch box with rice balls (おにぎり onigiri), friends eating lunch boxes under a tree, and a bus full of happy passengers. Talk about it: have you been on a bus tour? Where did you go, and what did you eat?
HI: क्या आप कभी बस टूर पर गए हैं? कहाँ गए और क्या खाया? · GU: શું તમે ક્યારેય બસ ટૂરમાં ગયા છો? ક્યાં ગયા અને શું ખાધું?

Culture note: on Japanese bus tours everyone meets at a set place and time (集合 shuugou), the leader calls names, and people bring a lunch box (お弁当 obentou). Being late is taken seriously, so this lesson has lots of "please don't…" rules.

2 Sentence Patterns (ポイント 88–97, p.277–278)

The lesson pages only flag the points in the corner: small topic 1 = 91, 92, 94, 96; small topic 2 = 88, 89, 97; small topic 3 = 90, 93, 95. The book gives each pattern with one or two examples at the back (p.277–278). Below they are in book order, each with every book example plus the model lines from the 言ってみよう drills.

Two verb forms you need here. て form (たべて, いって, かって…) you met before. ない form is the plain "not" form: group 1 changes the last -u sound to -a + ない (はいる → はいらない, すう → すわない), group 2 drops る + ない (おくれる → おくれない), and group 3 is する → しない, くる → こない. The full chart is in ポイント88.

【ポイント88】 Vない形 でください

small topic 2
ReadingV-nai-form de kudasai
Meaning (EN)"Please don't V."
Meaning (HI)कृपया V मत कीजिए।
Meaning (GU)કૃપા કરીને V ન કરશો.
How it worksTake the ない form of the verb and add でください. It is the "don't" partner of 〜てください ("please do"). Guides, staff and teachers use it a lot for rules.
Groupます form → ない form"Please don't…"
Group 1 (-u → -a + ない)(added, not in book) 入ります → 入らない · 押します → 押さない · 吸います → 吸わないhairimasu → hairanai · oshimasu → osanai · suimasu → suwanai入らないでくださいHairanai de kudasai.
Group 2 (drop る + ない)(added, not in book) 遅れます → 遅れない · 開けます → 開けないokuremasu → okurenai · akemasu → akenai遅れないでくださいOkurenai de kudasai.

small topic 2
ReadingV-te-form mo ii desu ka
Meaning (EN)"May I V?" / "Is it OK if I V?"
