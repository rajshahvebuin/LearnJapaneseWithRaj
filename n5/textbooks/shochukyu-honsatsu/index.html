初中級 本冊 (Shochuukyuu honsatsu)

These pages follow できる日本語 初中級 本冊 (Dekiru Nihongo Shochuukyuu Honsatsu) (アルク / ALC Press) one lesson at a time. It is the middle book of the できる日本語 series. Its 15 lessons are built around real situations: finding a part-time job, shopping, visiting a Japanese home, travel, health and the news. Each lesson page gives the full vocabulary list with romaji and English, Hindi and Gujarati meanings. It also explains every grammar point (ポイント) with translated examples, gives answers to the speaking drills, and summarises the listening and reading texts. The material is pre-intermediate, roughly JLPT N4 to N3.

How each lesson page works

  • Every lesson has two small topics (スモールトピック). Each one moves through the same steps: チャレンジ！ (try the scene) → 言ってみよう (speaking drills) → やってみよう (listening task and role-play).
  • The book gives the vocabulary list (ことば) without meanings, so every word on these pages has romaji plus English, Hindi and Gujarati meanings that we added.
  • The grammar is in the book's ポイント一覧 at the back (121 points). Each lesson page explains the points it uses and translates every example.
  • The listening scripts and the reading text (話読聞書) are summarised in our own words, not copied. Only key lines are quoted.
