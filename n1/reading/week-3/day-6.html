1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

省略された言葉を前の文から探そう！Shouryaku sareta kotoba o mae no bun kara sagasou!

Japanese happily drops words once they're already understood from context. When a sentence looks logically incomplete, a nearby sentence — usually the one right before it — already states the same idea in full. This book's two worked examples (explained in Chinese in this edition) show the pattern:

  • Example 1. 田中さんが選手に選ばれたそうです。なぜ田中さんなのでしょうか。Tanaka-san ga senshu ni erabareta sou desu. Naze Tanaka-san na no deshou ka. ("Tanaka was apparently chosen as a player. Why Tanaka?") → 「選手に選ばれた」が省略されている。"Senshu ni erabareta" ga shouryaku sarete iru. ("was chosen as a player" is omitted [from the second sentence].)
    HI: "तानाका जी को खिलाड़ी चुना गया, ऐसा सुना है। तानाका जी ही क्यों [चुने गए]?" → "खिलाड़ी चुना गया" वाला हिस्सा हटाया गया है। GU: "તાનાકાજીને ખેલાડી તરીકે પસંદ કરાયા, એવું સંભળાય છે. તાનાકાજી જ કેમ [પસંદ થયા]?" → "ખેલાડી તરીકે પસંદ થયા" એ ભાગ કાઢી નાખવામાં આવ્યો છે.
  • Example 2. 昨日は文法を勉強しました。今日は読解です。Kinou wa bunpou o benkyou shimashita. Kyou wa dokkai desu. ("Yesterday I studied grammar. Today [it]'s reading.") → 「勉強します」が省略されている。"Benkyou shimasu" ga shouryaku sarete iru. ("will study" is omitted [from the second sentence].)
    HI: "कल व्याकरण पढ़ा। आज पठन है [यानी पढ़ूँगा]।" → "पढ़ूँगा" वाला हिस्सा हटाया गया है। GU: "ગઈકાલે વ્યાકરણ ભણ્યો. આજે વાંચન છે [એટલે ભણીશ]." → "ભણીશ" એ ભાગ કાઢી નાખવામાં આવ્યો છે.
  • The rule. The sentence that comes before will always have the complete, non-abbreviated form somewhere in it — find it, and reuse its exact word or phrase to fill the gap.
    HI: पहले वाले वाक्य में हमेशा पूरा, बिना छोड़ा हुआ रूप मौजूद रहता है — उसे ढूँढें और उसी शब्द/वाक्यांश से खाली जगह भरें। GU: પહેલાંના વાક્યમાં હંમેશાં પૂરું, છોડ્યા વગરનું સ્વરૂપ હાજર હોય છે — તેને શોધો અને એ જ શબ્દ/વાક્યાંશથી ખાલી જગ્યા ભરો.

Q1

Passage

今、モノクロ写真がはやっているという。なぜモノクロなのか。Ima, monokuro shashin ga hayatte iru to iu. Naze monokuro na no ka.

問い　なぜモノクロなのかとはどういう意味か。Toi: Naze monokuro na no ka to wa dou iu imi ka.

EN: These days, black-and-white photography is apparently in fashion. Why black-and-white?
HI: आजकल, ब्लैक-एंड-व्हाइट फ़ोटोग्राफी चलन में है, ऐसा कहा जाता है। ब्लैक-एंड-व्हाइट ही क्यों?
GU: આજકાલ, બ્લેક-એન્ડ-વ્હાઇટ ફોટોગ્રાફી ચલણમાં છે, એવું કહેવાય છે. બ્લેક-એન્ડ-વ્હાઇટ જ કેમ?

Why: The elliptical question "なぜモノクロなのか" reuses the predicate of the sentence right before it (はやっている — "is trending"), omitted the second time around. Full form: 「なぜモノクロ[写真がはやっている]のか」. Option 1 correctly restores that predicate. Option 2 reverses the meaning entirely — the passage says monochrome IS currently trending, the opposite of "lost popularity."

Q2

Passage

上から何かをするように言われて、いつまでに、どうやってなどと質問することはあっても、どうして、と質問する大人は少ない。子どもは真っ先に「どうして」なのに。Ue kara nanika o suru you ni iwarete, itsu made ni, dou yatte nado to shitsumon suru koto wa atte mo, doushite, to shitsumon suru otona wa sukunai. Kodomo wa massaki ni "doushite" na noni.

問い　「どうして」なのにとはどういう意味か。Toi: "Doushite" na noni to wa dou iu imi ka.

EN: When told by a superior to do something, adults might ask "by when" or "how," but few ask "why." And yet children ask "why" before anything else.
HI: ऊपर से किसी को कुछ करने को कहा जाए, तो "कब तक" या "कैसे" पूछने वाले वयस्क तो होते हैं, पर "क्यों" पूछने वाले वयस्क कम ही होते हैं। जबकि बच्चे सबसे पहले "क्यों" ही पूछते हैं।
GU: ઉપરથી કોઈને કંઈક કરવાનું કહેવાય, તો "ક્યાં સુધીમાં" કે "કેવી રીતે" પૂછનારા વયસ્કો તો હોય છે, પણ "કેમ" પૂછનારા વયસ્કો ઓછા હોય છે. જ્યારે બાળકો સૌથી પહેલાં "કેમ" જ પૂછે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1どうして大人は質問しないのかと聞くのにDoushite otona wa shitsumon shinai no ka to kiku nonieven though [someone] asks why adults don't askभले ही [कोई] पूछे कि वयस्क क्यों नहीं पूछतेભલે [કોઈ] પૂછે કે વયસ્કો કેમ નથી પૂછતા

Why: The omitted verb is reused from the exact wording of the previous clause, 「どうして、と質問する大人は少ない」(few adults ASK "why"). So the full form of 「子どもは真っ先に『どうして』なのに」is 「子どもは真っ先に『どうして』と質問するのに」— reusing 質問する (asks), not a different verb like 思う (option 3), and keeping the subject as children, not adults (ruling out option 1, which wrongly shifts the subject). Given the context (being told to do something), the natural content of that "why" is "why do I have to do this" — matching option 2 in both verb and content.

Q3

Passage

おしゃれに敏感で個性的でありたい、自己表現としてのファッション、などと豪語注1しているタレントさんの着ているものが、ファッション雑誌を完ぺきにまねているものだったり、スタイリストに着せられたという感じを否めない注2ぎこちなさ注3が見えることは珍しくない。自分のものになっていないのだ。まるで着せ替え人形である。個性が聞いてあきれる。自己表現とは自分というものがあって初めて可能なのだ。自分のない者に表現もへったくれもない注4*。Oshare ni binkan de kosei-teki de aritai, jiko hyougen to shite no fasshon, nado to gougo shite iru tarento-san no kite iru mono ga, fasshon zasshi o kanpeki ni manete iru mono dattari, sutairisuto ni kiserareta to iu kanji o inamenai gikochinasa ga mieru koto wa mezurashikunai. Jibun no mono ni natte inai no da. Marude kisekae ningyou de aru. Kosei ga kiite akireru. Jiko hyougen to wa jibun to iu mono ga atte hajimete kanou na no da. Jibun no nai mono ni hyougen mo hettakure mo nai.

注1 豪語：自信を持ってばってそのことを言う Chuu 1: Gougo: jishin o motte itte sono koto o iu — "to boast with overblown confidence"　·　注2 否めない：否定できない Chuu 2: Inamenai: hitei dekinai — "cannot be denied"　·　注3 ぎこちなさ：慣れていない、不自然な感じ Chuu 3: Gikochinasa: narete inai, fushizen na kanji — "awkwardness: an unpracticed, unnatural feel"　·　注4 ～もへったくれもない：だれかの言った言葉を批判するときの表現 Chuu 4: ~mo hettakure mo nai: dareka no itta kotoba o hihan suru toki no hyougen — "an expression used to dismiss/mock something someone said"　·　* footnote: "Self-expression is only possible when you have your own ideas. You can't talk about 'expression' if you have none."

問い　個性が聞いてあきれるとはどういう意味か。Toi: Kosei ga kiite akireru to wa dou iu imi ka.

EN: It's not unusual to see, in TV personalities who boast things like "I want to be sensitive to fashion and individualistic — fashion as self-expression," clothing that's either a perfect copy of a fashion magazine or has an unmistakable awkwardness suggesting they were simply dressed by a stylist. It hasn't become their own. They're basically a dress-up doll. "Individuality" would be aghast to hear itself invoked here. Self-expression is only possible once you have a self to begin with — someone without a self has no business talking about "expression" at all.
HI: "मुझे फ़ैशन के प्रति संवेदनशील और व्यक्तिवादी बनना है, फ़ैशन आत्म-अभिव्यक्ति के रूप में" जैसी बातें दावे से कहने वाले टीवी सितारों के कपड़े, अक्सर या तो किसी फ़ैशन पत्रिका की हूबहू नक़ल होते हैं, या उनमें स्टाइलिस्ट द्वारा पहनाए जाने का साफ़ पता देने वाली एक अजीब-सी बेढंगापन दिखती है — यह कोई असामान्य बात नहीं। यह उनका अपना नहीं बन पाया है। वे बस एक कपड़े बदलने वाली गुड़िया हैं। "व्यक्तित्व" खुद यह सुनकर हैरान रह जाएगा। आत्म-अभिव्यक्ति तभी संभव है जब आपका अपना "स्व" हो — जिसका अपना "स्व" ही न हो, उसे "अभिव्यक्ति" की बात करने का कोई हक़ नहीं।
GU: "મારે ફેશન પ્રત્યે સંવેદનશીલ અને વ્યક્તિવાદી બનવું છે, ફેશન આત્મ-અભિવ્યક્તિ તરીકે" જેવી વાતો દાવાથી કહેતા ટીવી સ્ટાર્સના કપડાં, ઘણી વાર કાં તો કોઈ ફેશન મેગેઝિનની આબેહૂબ નકલ હોય છે, અથવા તેમાં સ્ટાઇલિસ્ટ દ્વારા પહેરાવવામાં આવ્યાનો સ્પષ્ટ સંકેત આપતી એક વિચિત્ર બેઢંગાપણું દેખાય છે — આ કંઈ અસામાન્ય વાત નથી. આ તેમનું પોતાનું બન્યું નથી. તેઓ ફક્ત એક કપડાં બદલવાની ઢીંગલી છે. "વ્યક્તિત્વ" પોતે આ સાંભળીને ચોંકી જશે. આત્મ-અભિવ્યક્તિ ત્યારે જ શક્ય છે જ્યારે તમારો પોતાનો "સ્વ" હોય — જેનો પોતાનો "સ્વ" જ ન હોય, તેને "અભિવ્યક્તિ"ની વાત કરવાનો કોઈ હક નથી.

Q4

Passage

来年度より、交換留学制度が廃止されるらしい。市内に住む高校生15名を3ヵ月間、オーストリアに留学させ、またオーストリアの高校生を日本に受け入れるという20年前に始まったこの制度は、この市の特色でもあった。なぜなのか。確かに費用は全部、市の負担であったが、それを削減したところで市の財政難にそれほどの効果があるとは思えない**。それどころか、市の特色がなくなるというダメージ注1のほうが大きいのではないだろうか。さまざまな部門で経費を節約しなければならないことはわかる。それならそれで、費用の一部を個人負担にすればいいではないか。この交換留学がきっかけで高校生の文化交流、とくに音楽交流が盛んになりほかの市に比べて本場のクラシック注2を聴く機会が増えたのに、これも少なくなるのだろうか。Rainendo yori, koukan ryuugaku seido ga haishi sareru rashii. Shinai ni sumu koukousei juugo-mei o san-kagetsu-kan, Oosutoria ni ryuugaku sase, mata Oosutoria no koukousei o Nihon ni ukeireru to iu nijuu-nen mae ni hajimatta kono seido wa, kono shi no tokushoku demo atta. Naze na no ka. Tashika ni hiyou wa zenbu, shi no futan de atta ga, sore o sakugen shita tokoro de shi no zaiseinan ni sorehodo no kouka ga aru to wa omoenai. Sore dokoro ka, shi no tokushoku ga nakunaru to iu dameeji no hou ga ookii no de wa nai darou ka. Samazama na bumon de keihi o setsuyaku shinakereba naranai koto wa wakaru. Sore nara sore de, hiyou no ichibu o kojin futan ni sureba ii de wa nai ka. Kono koukan ryuugaku ga kikkake de koukousei no bunka kouryuu, toku ni ongaku kouryuu ga sakan ni nari hoka no shi ni kurabete honba no kurashikku o kiku kikai ga fueta noni, kore mo sukunaku naru no darou ka.

注1 ダメージ：損害、damage Chuu 1: Dameeji: songai, damage　·　注2 クラシック：西洋の古典音楽、classical music Chuu 2: Kurashikku: seiyou no koten ongaku, classical music　·　** footnote: "even if it were cut, it wouldn't have much effect on the city's financial difficulties"

問い　なぜなのかとは、別の表現で言うと、この場合、次のどれに近いか。Toi: Naze na no ka to wa, betsu no hyougen de iu to, kono baai, tsugi no dore ni chikai ka.

EN: Starting next fiscal year, the student-exchange program is apparently going to be abolished. This 20-year-old program — sending 15 city high schoolers to Austria for three months and, in turn, accepting Austrian high schoolers into Japan — had also been one of the city's distinguishing features. Why? True, the entire cost was borne by the city, but cutting it doesn't seem likely to make much difference to the city's financial troubles. If anything, the damage of losing the city's distinguishing feature seems like the bigger loss. Various departments do need to cut costs, granted — so why not just make part of the cost the individuals' own responsibility? Thanks to this exchange, cultural exchange among high schoolers, especially musical exchange, has flourished, and compared with other cities, opportunities to hear authentic classical music have increased. Is that going to shrink too?
