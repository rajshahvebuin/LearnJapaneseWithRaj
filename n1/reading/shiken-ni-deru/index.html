試験に出る 読解 N1・N2

Shiken ni Deru Dokkai (桐原書店, 40日完成) — 40 days in three parts: 第1部 ten days of basic reading skills (who does what, modifiers, sentence skeletons, the writer's point…), 第2部 thirty days of JLPT-format practice grouped by question type, and 第3部 two reading mock tests. The book covers both N1 and N2: groups marked N2 are N2-level warm-ups, the rest are N1. Answers come from the book's own 別冊 (解答・問題を解くヒント).
