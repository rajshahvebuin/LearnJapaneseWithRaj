1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

（　）の位置が違えば、解き方も違う！( ) no ichi ga chigaeba, tokikata mo chigau!

Where the blank sits in the passage changes how you should solve it. This book (in this bilingual JP/Chinese edition) gives three approaches, translated here:

  • 1. Compare before/after the blank. Carefully weigh the relationship between the sentence(s) before the blank and the sentence(s) after it — the blank is often a connector or a paraphrase bridging the two.
    HI: रिक्त स्थान से पहले और बाद के भाग के बीच के संबंध को ध्यान से देखें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા પહેલાં અને પછીના ભાગ વચ્ચેના સંબંધને ધ્યાનથી જુઓ.
  • 2. Find the subject, then find its description. Locate the sentence's subject/topic, then find the nearby sentence that describes that same subject — the two sentences usually carry a similar meaning, so the blank can often be filled from the other sentence.
    HI: विषय (subject) खोजें, फिर उसका वर्णन करने वाला वाक्य खोजें — दोनों वाक्यों का अर्थ आमतौर पर मिलता-जुलता होता है। GU: વિષય શોધો, પછી તેનું વર્ણન કરતું વાક્ય શોધો — બંને વાક્યોનો અર્થ સામાન્ય રીતે મળતો આવે છે.
  • 3. When there are several blanks in one passage. They're always discussing the same underlying content — find the words across the passage that are parallel or contrasting in meaning. If a blank sits in a subject position, pick whichever word already appeared earlier in that same role.
    HI: कई रिक्त स्थान हों तो वे सब एक ही विषय पर बात कर रहे होते हैं — मिलते-जुलते या विपरीत अर्थ वाले शब्द ढूँढें। GU: ઘણી ખાલી જગ્યાઓ હોય તો બધી એક જ વિષય વિશે વાત કરે છે — મળતા કે વિરોધી અર્થવાળા શબ્દો શોધો.

Q1

Passage

抗生物質注の開発によって、人類は伝染病を（　）かのような錯覚を起こしている。Kousei busshitsu (chuu) no kaihatsu ni yotte, jinrui wa densenbyou o (___) ka no you na sakkaku o okoshite iru.

注 抗生物質：伝染病などを防ぐ薬の一種 Chuu: kousei busshitsu: densenbyou nado o fusegu kusuri no isshu — "antibiotics: a type of medicine that prevents infectious diseases, etc."

Full sentence: 抗生物質の開発によって、人類は伝染病を克服したかのような錯覚を起こしている。Kousei busshitsu no kaihatsu ni yotte, jinrui wa densenbyou o kokufuku shita ka no you na sakkaku o okoshite iru.
EN: Because of the development of antibiotics, humanity has fallen under the illusion that it has overcome infectious disease.
HI: एंटीबायोटिक दवाओं के विकास के कारण, मानवता को यह भ्रम हो गया है कि उसने संक्रामक रोगों पर विजय पा ली है।
GU: એન્ટિબાયોટિક દવાઓના વિકાસને કારણે, માનવજાતને એવો ભ્રમ થયો છે કે તેણે ચેપી રોગો પર વિજય મેળવી લીધો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1克服したkokufuku shitaovercame / conqueredविजय पा लीવિજય મેળવી લીધો
2広めたhirometaspreadफैला दियाફેલાવી દીધો

Why: 錯覚 ("illusion/false impression") always describes a mistaken belief that something desirable has already happened. The book's own footnote translation confirms this reading: "an illusion that the problem has been overcome." 広めた ("spread") would mean humanity mistakenly believes it spread the disease — the opposite of what antibiotics are for, so it doesn't fit the logic of the sentence at all.

Q2

Passage

（前略）かつて私たちの国では、花の美しさというように、抽象観念によって美しいものをとらえようとする言い方も乏しく、したがってそのような考え方もほとんどなかった。（　）、というようなことばや考え方を私たちに教えてくれたのは、やはり西欧舶来注のことばであり、その翻訳語だったのである。(Zenryaku) Katsute watashitachi no kuni de wa, hana no utsukushisa to iu you ni, chuushou kannen ni yotte utsukushii mono o toraeyou to suru iikata mo toboshiku, shitagatte sono you na kangaekata mo hotondo nakatta. (___), to iu you na kotoba ya kangaekata o watashitachi ni oshiete kureta no wa, yahari seiou hakurai no kotoba de ari, sono honyakugo datta no de aru.

注 西欧舶来：西洋から来た Chuu: seiou hakurai: seiyou kara kita — "brought over from the West"　·　出典: 柳父章『翻訳語成立事情』岩波書店 (Yanabu Akira, "Honyakugo Seiritsu Jijou," Iwanami Shoten)

問い　この文章の（　）の部分には、どんな内容の表現を入れることができるか。Toi: Kono bunshou no ( ) no bubun ni wa, donna naiyou no hyougen o ireru koto ga dekiru ka.

Full sentence: （花の美しさ）、というようなことばや考え方を私たちに教えてくれたのは、やはり西欧舶来のことばであり、その翻訳語だったのである。(Hana no utsukushisa), to iu you na kotoba ya kangaekata o oshiete kureta no wa, yahari seiou hakurai no kotoba datta.
EN: Once, in our country, there was hardly any way of speaking that tried to grasp beauty through abstract concepts — such as "the beauty of a flower" — and so there was almost no such way of thinking either. It was, after all, words that came from the West, and their translated forms, that taught us expressions and ways of thinking like "[the beauty of a flower]."
HI: पहले हमारे देश में सुंदर चीज़ों को अमूर्त धारणाओं के ज़रिए समझने का तरीका — जैसे "फूल की सुंदरता" — बहुत कम था, इसलिए ऐसी सोच भी लगभग नहीं थी। "[फूल की सुंदरता]" जैसे शब्द और सोच हमें सिखाने वाले, आख़िरकार, पश्चिम से आए शब्द और उनके अनुवाद ही थे।
GU: પહેલાં આપણા દેશમાં સુંદર વસ્તુઓને અમૂર્ત ખ્યાલો દ્વારા સમજવાની રીત — જેમ કે "ફૂલની સુંદરતા" — બહુ ઓછી હતી, એટલે એવો વિચાર પણ લગભગ નહોતો. "[ફૂલની સુંદરતા]" જેવા શબ્દો અને વિચારો આપણને શીખવનાર, છેવટે, પશ્ચિમમાંથી આવેલા શબ્દો અને તેમના અનુવાદ જ હતા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1花の美しさhana no utsukushisathe beauty of a flowerफूल की सुंदरताફૂલની સુંદરતા
2抽象観念chuushou kannenabstract conceptअमूर्त धारणाઅમૂર્ત ખ્યાલ

Why: This is strategy #2 from the box above — find the subject/topic, then find the sentence describing it, and reuse its wording. The first sentence already gave one concrete example of "grasping beauty via an abstract concept": 花の美しさ. The second sentence needs a concrete example again ("words/thinking like ___"), so it repeats that same example — not the umbrella category name 抽象観念, which is the term used to describe the phenomenon, not an instance of it.

Q3

Passage

昔は、子どもは親を尊敬し、親は子どもに命令し、子どもが命令されて動くことは当然の日常であった。（　）、現在は対等な立場で親子関係が築かれる世の中となった*。そして、それがあるべき愛の形であると理解している者が大半であるが、好ましくない状態であると嘆く注者も多くいるのである。Mukashi wa, kodomo wa oya o sonkei shi, oya wa kodomo ni meirei shi, kodomo ga meirei sarete ugoku koto wa touzen no nichijou de atta. (___), genzai wa taitou na tachiba de oyako kankei ga kizukareru yo no naka to natta. Soshite, sore ga aru beki ai no katachi de aru to rikai shite iru mono ga taihan de aru ga, konomashikunai joutai de aru to nageku mono mo ooku iru no de aru.

注 嘆く：とても残念だと思う Chuu: nageku: totemo zannen da to omou — "to lament: to feel it's a great shame"　·　* footnote translation: "nowadays parents and children have more equal relationships"

Full sentence: このような上下関係がいつのまにか変化し、現在は対等な立場で親子関係が築かれる世の中となった。Kono you na jouge kankei ga itsu no ma ni ka henka shi, genzai wa taitou na tachiba de oyako kankei ga kizukareru yo no naka to natta.
EN: In the past, children respected their parents, parents gave children orders, and it was simply everyday life for children to move as they were told. This hierarchical relationship changed before anyone quite noticed, and now we live in a world where parent-child relationships are built on equal footing. Most people take this to be the ideal form of love, though many also lament it as an undesirable state of affairs.
HI: पहले बच्चे माता-पिता का सम्मान करते थे, माता-पिता आदेश देते थे, और आदेश मानकर चलना बच्चों के लिए रोज़मर्रा की सामान्य बात थी। यह ऊँच-नीच वाला रिश्ता कब धीरे-धीरे बदल गया पता ही नहीं चला, और अब बराबरी के आधार पर माता-पिता-बच्चे का रिश्ता बनने लगा है। ज़्यादातर लोग इसे प्रेम का सही रूप मानते हैं, पर कई लोग इसे नापसंद स्थिति मानकर अफ़सोस भी जताते हैं।
GU: પહેલાં બાળકો માતાપિતાનું સન્માન કરતાં, માતાપિતા આદેશ આપતાં, અને આદેશ મુજબ વર્તવું બાળકો માટે રોજિંદી સામાન્ય વાત હતી. આ ઊંચ-નીચનો સંબંધ ક્યારે ધીમે ધીમે બદલાઈ ગયો ખબર જ ન પડી, અને હવે સમાનતાના ધોરણે માતાપિતા-બાળક સંબંધ બંધાવા લાગ્યો છે. મોટાભાગના લોકો આને પ્રેમનું યોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ માને છે, પણ ઘણા લોકો આને અનિચ્છનીય સ્થિતિ ગણી અફસોસ પણ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1このような上下関係は変わりようがないがkono you na jouge kankei wa kawariyou ga nai gathis hierarchy has no way of changing, butयह ऊँच-नीच बदल ही नहीं सकताઆ ઊંચ-નીચ બદલાઈ જ ન શકે
2このような上下関係がいつのまにか変化しkono you na jouge kankei ga itsu no ma ni ka henka shithis hierarchy changed before anyone noticed, andयह ऊँच-नीच धीरे-धीरे बदल गयाઆ ઊંચ-નીચ ધીમે ધીમે બદલાયું
3このような親子関係が目覚ましい進歩を遂げkono you na oyako kankei ga mezamashii shinpo o togethis parent-child relationship made remarkable progress, andयह रिश्ता ज़बरदस्त तरक्की कर गयाઆ સંબંધે અદભુત પ્રગતિ કરી
4このような親子関係の認識が逆転しkono you na oyako kankei no ninshiki ga gyakuten shithe perception of this relationship reversed, andइस रिश्ते की समझ पूरी तरह उलट गईઆ સંબંધની સમજ સાવ ઊલટાઈ ગઈ

Why: The passage moves from "hierarchy was normal" to "now it's equal" — strategy #1: compare what's before and after the blank. Option 1 flatly contradicts the next clause (it says nothing can change, but the very next words describe exactly that change). Option 3 talks about the relationship "progressing," not about a hierarchy becoming equal — wrong topic. Option 4 ("perception reversed") overstates and shifts the subject to perception itself rather than the relationship structure. Only option 2 bridges "was hierarchical" → "gradually changed" → "now equal" without contradiction.

Q4

Passage

人の脳は大きく、論理や言語を処理する左脳と、情緒注1的な感情を処理する右脳に分けられる。したがって音楽家や画家などは右脳が、学者や作家などは左脳が発達していると考えられる。一般的に、人は毎日の生活では言葉を話したり仕事を論理的に処理したりすることが多い**ため、（Ａ）をよく使うが、そんなときは音楽を聴いたりして（Ｂ）を使うようにすると、脳の血液循環注2がよくなり、同時に（Ｃ）は休息ができるのである。Hito no nou wa ookiku, ronri ya gengo o shori suru sanou to, jouchou-teki na kanjou o shori suru unou ni wakerareru. Shitagatte ongakuka ya gaka nado wa unou ga, gakusha ya sakka nado wa sanou ga hattatsu shite iru to kangaerareru. Ippanteki ni, hito wa mainichi no seikatsu de wa kotoba o hanashitari shigoto o ronriteki ni shori shitari suru koto ga ooi tame, (A) o yoku tsukau ga, sonna toki wa ongaku o kiitari shite (B) o tsukau you ni suru to, nou no ketsueki junkan ga yoku nari, douji ni (C) wa kyuusoku ga dekiru no de aru.

注1 情緒：喜び、悲しみ、怒りなどの感情 jouchou: yorokobi, kanashimi, ikari nado no kanjou — "emotion: feelings such as joy, sadness, anger"　·　注2 血液循環：血のめぐり、回り ketsueki junkan: chi no meguri, mawari — "blood circulation"　·　** footnote: "generally, people tend to talk and work rationally"

問い　（Ａ）〜（Ｃ）には、「右脳」か「左脳」が入る。その組み合わせとして最も適当なものはどれか。Toi: (A) kara (C) ni wa, "unou" ka "sanou" ga hairu. Sono kumiawase to shite mottomo tekitou na mono wa dore ka.

Full sentence: A=左脳 B=右脳 C=左脳 A = sanou, B = unou, C = sanou
EN: The human brain is broadly split into the left brain, which processes logic and language, and the right brain, which processes emotion. Since daily life involves a lot of talking and logical work, people rely heavily on the left brain (A); but if you make a habit of using the right brain (B) at such times — for example by listening to music — blood circulation in the brain improves, and at the same time the left brain (C) gets to rest.
HI: दिमाग तर्क-भाषा संभालने वाले बायें हिस्से और भावनाएँ संभालने वाले दायें हिस्से में बँटा है। रोज़मर्रा में बोलने और तार्किक काम ज़्यादा होने से लोग बायाँ मस्तिष्क (A) ज़्यादा इस्तेमाल करते हैं; पर संगीत सुनकर दायाँ मस्तिष्क (B) इस्तेमाल किया जाए तो रक्त-संचार बेहतर होता है और बायाँ मस्तिष्क (C) को आराम मिलता है।
GU: મગજ તર્ક-ભાષા સંભાળતા ડાબા ભાગ અને લાગણીઓ સંભાળતા જમણા ભાગમાં વહેંચાયેલું છે. રોજિંદા જીવનમાં બોલવાનું અને તાર્કિક કામ વધુ હોવાથી લોકો ડાબું મગજ (A) વધુ વાપરે છે; પણ સંગીત સાંભળીને જમણું મગજ (B) વાપરવામાં આવે તો રક્ત-પરિભ્રમણ સુધરે છે અને ડાબા મગજ (C) ને આરામ મળે છે.

Why: Daily life (talking, logical work) is a left-brain activity, so A = 左脳. Listening to music engages the emotional right brain, so B = 右脳. Using the right brain at that point lets the overworked left brain rest, so C = 左脳 — matching option 1. This is strategy #3: several blanks about the same underlying contrast (left vs. right brain), solved by tracking which side each clue points to.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

There's no vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, the four questions each test a different one of the strategy box's approaches. Compare which approach unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the blank doesTrap to avoid
Q1 Compare before/after Blank must match the logical direction of 錯覚 (a false belief of success) Don't pick a word just because it's topically related (広めた is about disease too) — check whether it actually fits the logic of "illusion."
Q2 Subject → its description Blank repeats the earlier concrete example, not the abstract label for it 抽象観念 sounds "more academic" and tempts you, but it's the category name, not an example belonging in that slot.
Q3 Compare before/after Blank must be consistent with the sentence that follows it, not contradict it Option 1 reads plausibly in isolation but directly contradicts the next clause — always check the sentence right after the blank before choosing.
Q4 Multiple blanks, same underlying contrast Every blank maps to one side of a single left-brain/right-brain opposition Solve A, B, C as one linked system, not three separate guesses — once A is fixed (left = daily logic use), B and C follow logically.
