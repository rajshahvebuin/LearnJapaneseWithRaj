1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

表情やことばの奥にある「気持ち」を、常識や自分の感覚で決めつけない。Hyoujou ya kotoba no oku ni aru "kimochi" o, joushiki ya jibun no kankaku de kimetsukenai.

Find the actual words in the text that describe an expression or a feeling — don't guess the feeling yourself. This book (in its Chinese explanatory prose) gives two warnings, translated here:

  • 1. Don't judge by common sense alone. The book flags a useful vocabulary pair: 表面／外見／一見 (surface / outward appearance / at a glance) usually signals that "in reality, the person's true thinking is different" — so whatever "common sense" would expect someone to feel is often exactly what the passage is about to contradict.
    HI: केवल सामान्य सोच के आधार पर भावना तय न करें — 表面/外見/一見 (सतह पर / देखने में) जैसे शब्द अक्सर बताते हैं कि असली सोच कुछ और है। GU: ફક્ત સામાન્ય સમજના આધારે લાગણી નક્કી ન કરો — 表面/外見/一見 (સપાટી પર / જોવામાં) જેવા શબ્દો ઘણી વાર બતાવે છે કે ખરું વિચારવું કંઈક બીજું જ છે.
  • 2. Don't mix in your own feelings as the reader. Likewise, 内心／本心 (inner heart / true feelings) almost always describes the opposite of whatever attitude was shown to the outside world — use the text's own emotion words, not what you personally would feel in that situation.
    HI: पाठक के तौर पर अपनी निजी भावना न मिलाएँ — 内心/本心 (मन की असली भावना) लगभग हमेशा बाहर दिखाए गए रवैये के उलट होती है, इसलिए पाठ के अपने भावना-शब्दों पर भरोसा करें। GU: વાચક તરીકે તમારી પોતાની લાગણી ભેળવો નહીં — 内心/本心 (મનની ખરી લાગણી) લગભગ હંમેશાં બહાર દેખાડેલા વલણથી ઊલટી હોય છે, એટલે લખાણના પોતાના લાગણી-શબ્દો પર જ આધાર રાખો.

Q1

Passage

彼は、娘が留学をあきらめずにいることを知って、ため息をついた。Kare wa, musume ga ryuugaku o akiramezu ni iru koto o shitte, tameiki o tsuita.

問い　彼の気持ちは次のどちらに近いか。Toi: Kare no kimochi wa tsugi no dochira ni chikai ka.

Full sentence: 彼は、娘が留学をあきらめずにいることを知って、ため息をついた（＝うれしくない気持ち）。Kare wa, musume ga ryuugaku o akiramezu ni iru koto o shitte, tameiki o tsuita (= ureshikunai kimochi).
EN: On learning that his daughter had not given up on studying abroad, he let out a sigh (= a feeling that is not happiness).
HI: यह जानकर कि उसकी बेटी ने विदेश में पढ़ाई का सपना नहीं छोड़ा, उसने एक आह भरी (यानी यह ख़ुशी की भावना नहीं है)।
GU: એ જાણીને કે તેની દીકરીએ વિદેશમાં ભણવાનું સપનું છોડ્યું નથી, તેણે નિસાસો નાખ્યો (એટલે કે આ ખુશીની લાગણી નથી).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1うれしいureshiihappyखुशખુશ
2うれしくないureshikunainot happyखुश नहींખુશ નહીં

Why: Strategy #1 — don't judge by common sense. It might seem like a parent "should" be proud that their daughter refuses to give up on her dream, but the text gives us the actual reaction: ため息 (a sigh), which conventionally signals worry, resignation, or disappointment — never joy. Go by the emotion word actually written (ため息), not by what you'd expect a supportive parent to feel.

Q2

Passage

彼女は、上司にどんな命令をされようとも、嫌な顔一つせず従った*。しかも表面的には楽しそうに仕事していたので、同僚の絵里子を除いてだれも、彼女の本心はわからなかった。Kanojo wa, joushi ni donna meirei o sareyou tomo, iya na kao hitotsu sezu shitagatta. Shikamo hyoumenteki ni wa tanoshisou ni shigoto shite ita node, douryou no Eriko o nozoite dare mo, kanojo no honshin wa wakaranakatta.

* footnote translation: "She faithfully followed her boss' orders, no matter what they were."

問い　彼女の本心とは、ここではどのようなことか。Toi: Kanojo no honshin to wa, koko de wa dono you na koto ka.

Full sentence: 表面的には楽しそうだったが、彼女の本心は仕事を嫌だと思っていた。Hyoumenteki ni wa tanoshisou datta ga, kanojo no honshin wa shigoto o iya da to omotte ita.
EN: No matter what orders her boss gave, she followed them without ever showing displeasure. And since she seemed to be enjoying her work on the surface, no one but her colleague Eriko knew her true feelings.
HI: बॉस चाहे जो भी आदेश दे, वह बिना कोई नाराज़गी दिखाए उसे मानती थी। और चूँकि ऊपर से वह काम में मज़ा लेती दिखती थी, इसलिए उसकी सहकर्मी एरीको के अलावा किसी को उसके असली मन की बात पता नहीं थी।
GU: બોસ ગમે તે આદેશ આપે, તે કોઈ નારાજગી બતાવ્યા વગર તેને માનતી. અને ઉપરથી તે કામમાં આનંદ લેતી હોય એવું લાગતું, એટલે તેની સાથી એરિકો સિવાય કોઈને તેની ખરી લાગણી ખબર નહોતી.

Why: Strategy #2 — this is exactly the 表面／本心 pair from the box above. 表面的には (on the surface) she looked like she was enjoying her work, but 本心 (her true feelings) is explicitly flagged as something nobody but one colleague could see — signalling it's the opposite of the cheerful surface. If the outward show is "enjoying it," the hidden truth is "not enjoying it" — she's simply too disciplined to let it show.

Q3

Passage

Q4

Passage

人間には、わからないことや知らないことがあって当然である。ただ私のわからないことや知らないことは、世間では常識的なことが多いように思う。だから、それがみんなが知っていることだということがわかると、つい、わかったふりをしてしまう**。どうして「わからない」「知らない」と言えないのか。自尊心注1が強いからというより、物事に対してあまりにも鈍感注2である自分をさらけ出したくないのである***。素直になりたいものである。Ningen ni wa, wakaranai koto ya shiranai koto ga atte touzen de aru. Tada watashi no wakaranai koto ya shiranai koto wa, seken de wa joushikiteki na koto ga ooi you ni omou. Dakara, sore ga minna ga shitte iru koto da to iu koto ga wakaru to, tsui, wakatta furi o shite shimau. Doushite "wakaranai" "shiranai" to ienai no ka. Jisonshin ga tsuyoi kara to iu yori, monogoto ni taishite amari ni mo donkan de aru jibun o sarakedashitakunai no de aru. Sunao ni naritai mono de aru.

注1 自尊心：自分を偉いと思う気持ち、プライド Chuu 1: jisonshin: jibun o erai to omou kimochi, puraido — "self-esteem: feeling that one is superior, pride"　·　注2 鈍感：感覚や物事に対する感じ方がにぶいこと Chuu 2: donkan: kankaku ya monogoto ni taisuru kanjikata ga nibui koto — "insensitive: having dull senses or perception"　·　** footnote: "when it seems that everyone else knows about something, I often pretend that I know it as well"　·　*** footnote: "I hesitate to openly show that I am so indifferent to things around me"

問い　筆者がいちばん言いたい気持ちは次のどれか。Toi: Hissha ga ichiban iitai kimochi wa tsugi no dore ka.

Full sentence: 「わからない」「知らない」と素直に言えないから、素直になりたいと思っている。"Wakaranai" "shiranai" to sunao ni ienai kara, sunao ni naritai to omotte iru.
EN: It's only natural for a person to have things they don't understand or don't know. But it seems to me that the things I don't understand or don't know are, more often than not, common knowledge to everyone else. So once I realize something is common knowledge, I end up, almost reflexively, pretending I know it too. Why can't I just say "I don't understand," "I don't know"? It's less that my pride is so strong, and more that I don't want to expose how insensitive I am to things. I wish I could just be honest.
HI: इंसान के लिए कुछ न समझना या न जानना स्वाभाविक है। लेकिन मुझे लगता है कि जो चीज़ें मुझे नहीं समझ आतीं या पता नहीं होतीं, वे अक्सर सबको मालूम सामान्य बातें ही होती हैं। इसलिए जब मुझे पता चलता है कि यह सबको मालूम है, तो मैं बिना सोचे-समझे जानने का दिखावा कर देता हूँ। मैं "मुझे नहीं पता," "मुझे समझ नहीं आता" क्यों नहीं कह पाता? इसकी वजह ज़्यादा घमंड नहीं, बल्कि यह है कि मैं यह ज़ाहिर नहीं करना चाहता कि मैं चीज़ों के प्रति कितना असंवेदनशील हूँ। काश मैं ईमानदार बन पाता।
GU: માણસ માટે કંઈક ન સમજવું કે ન જાણવું સ્વાભાવિક છે. પણ મને લાગે છે કે જે વસ્તુઓ મને સમજાતી નથી કે ખબર નથી, તે ઘણી વાર બધાને ખબર હોય એવી સામાન્ય વાતો જ હોય છે. એટલે જ્યારે મને ખબર પડે કે આ બધાને ખબર છે, ત્યારે હું વગર વિચાર્યે જાણવાનો ડોળ કરી દઉં છું. હું "મને ખબર નથી," "મને સમજાતું નથી" કેમ કહી શકતો નથી? તેનું કારણ વધુ પડતું અભિમાન નહીં, પણ એ છે કે હું બતાવવા માંગતો નથી કે હું વસ્તુઓ પ્રત્યે કેટલો અસંવેદનશીલ છું. કાશ હું પ્રામાણિક બની શકું.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

There's no vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which "don't be fooled" trap each question sets up:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat's being identifiedTrap to avoid
Q1 Don't judge by common sense ため息 (a sigh) signals a negative feeling despite the seemingly good news Don't assume a parent should automatically feel proud/happy about a child's persistence — trust the emotion word actually given.
Q2 Surface vs. true feeling (表面 ≠ 本心) 本心 is the opposite of her outwardly cheerful appearance Don't take 表面的には楽しそう at face value as her real feeling — 本心 always means "look again, the truth differs."
