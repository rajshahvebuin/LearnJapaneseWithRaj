問題3 — 内容理解(長文) 「できる人はどこが違うのか」 ("What makes capable people different") (p.76–77) · Q14–17

問題3 · Q14

Passage — 「できる人はどこが違うのか」 — from 斎藤孝『「できる人」はどこがちがうのか』(ちくま新書) (p.76)

Passage summary (not the book's text): The writer asks what adults should hand on to children in fast-changing Japan, and answers: the power to survive in whatever society they are thrown into — not just raw vitality, but having turned the "universal logic of getting better" into a personal skill through experience, so that you can work out for yourself how to improve at any job and dare to try new fields. His example is Mr Biri from Iran, who taught himself Japanese from TV, radio, notes and talk, and learned breakdancing just by watching good dancers and copying them; unlike some compatriots who leaned on him, he was sure he could get better at anything. "Watch the skilled and steal their skill" sounds obvious, the writer says, but whether you hold it with real conviction at the centre of your practice is what decides success.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.2, p.11). Why (added, not in book): the truck comes round from 9:00 and the notice asks for items to be gathered 玄関先などに; the item list asks for sorting and tying, so "sorted, bundled and by the front door" is complete. 1 stops at sorting — the items would still be inside the house. 2: no advance contact is requested. 3: nothing goes to a rubbish point; the only alternative is bringing items to the school by car.
