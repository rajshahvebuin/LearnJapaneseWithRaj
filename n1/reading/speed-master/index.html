スピードマスター N1 読解

Following 日本語能力試験問題集 N1読解 スピードマスター (Speed Master N1 Dokkai, Jリサーチ出版) section by section — a warm-up key-word list, PART 1 実戦練習 (practice for each of the six N1 読解 question types, each problem with a target time) and PART 2's two mock tests. Every page gives the strategy for the question type, the 別冊 word list, every question with a passage summary and the key sentence(s), and answers from the book's own 別冊 正解 key, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
