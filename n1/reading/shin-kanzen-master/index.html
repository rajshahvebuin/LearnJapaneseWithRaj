新完全マスター N1 読解

Following 新完全マスター読解 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Dokkai, スリーエーネットワーク) section by section — 第1部 essays (text structure: contrast, paraphrase, metaphor, question-led argument; then question techniques: demonstratives, who/what, underlined parts, reasons, examples), 第2部 practical texts (letters, ads, notices, instructions, tables), 第3部 exam-style 中文・長文・主張・統合・情報検索 practice, and a full 模擬試験. Every page gives the book's reading strategy, every 例題 and 練習 with a passage summary in our own words, the key sentences quoted, answers from the book's 別冊 key, and how the wrong options bend the text — in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
