1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

注意各种表格的特点！Zhùyì gèzhǒng biǎogé de tèdiǎn!

"Pay attention to the distinguishing features of each chart!" Today's task is: read a short passage of numbers/trends, then pick the ONE graph (out of several nearly-identical-looking ones) that actually matches it. The four "candidate" graphs are designed to each break the passage in one specific place.

  • 1. Precise proportion phrases matter exactly. 2分の1に満たない (less than half); 半数に達する (reaches exactly half); 半数を上回る (slightly more than half); 過半数 (50% or more). These are graded — don't treat them as interchangeable.
    HI: ठीक-ठीक अनुपात के वाक्यांश अलग-अलग अर्थ रखते हैं: "आधे से कम", "आधा", "आधे से थोड़ा ज़्यादा", "50% या ज़्यादा" — इन्हें एक जैसा न समझें। GU: ચોક્કસ પ્રમાણનાં વાક્યાંશો અલગ અલગ અર્થ ધરાવે છે: "અડધાથી ઓછું", "અડધું", "અડધાથી થોડું વધુ", "50% કે વધુ" — તેમને સરખા ન ગણો.
  • 2. Line shape and colour/style both carry meaning. A steeply rising line, a dashed middle section, a solid vs. faint line — a candidate graph can be wrong just because its line style or exact shape doesn't match, even if the general direction looks right.
    HI: रेखा का आकार और शैली (ठोस/बिंदीदार) दोनों मायने रखते हैं — सामान्य दिशा सही लगने पर भी सही शैली न होने से विकल्प ग़लत हो सकता है। GU: રેખાનો આકાર અને શૈલી (નક્કર/ટપકાંવાળી) બંને મહત્વ ધરાવે છે — સામાન્ય દિશા સાચી લાગે તોપણ સાચી શૈલી ન હોય તો વિકલ્પ ખોટો હોઈ શકે.

The book's cats bicker over exactly this: 「左の線は色が濃いね！」"Hidari no sen wa iro ga koi ne!" ("The left line is a darker colour!") — 「そうじゃなくて、形を見るんだよ。」"Sou ja nakute, katachi o miru nda yo." ("That's not it — you're supposed to look at the shape.") Both colour/style AND shape can matter, depending on what the chart is distinguishing.

Q1

Passage — 死因別死亡率 (Death rate by cause, 2008)

次の文章は日本の死因別死亡率（2008年調査）について述べたものである。Tsugi no bunshou wa Nihon no shiin-betsu shibouritsu (2008-nen chousa) ni tsuite nobeta mono de aru.

日本の主要死因は、「悪性新生物（癌）」「心疾患」「脳血管疾患」*である。その死因別死亡率は全体の60%近くを占める。その他の死因としては「肺炎及び気管支炎」や「老衰」「不慮の事故」**などがあげられる。1980年ごろ、「脳血管疾患」に代わり、「悪性新生物」が死因の一位を占めるようになった。近年「心疾患」による死亡も急激に増えており、近い将来「悪性新生物」による死亡と合わせての死亡率は、全体の50%に達するだろうと言われている。Nihon no shuyou shiin wa, "akusei shinseibutsu (gan)," "shinshikkan," "noukekkan shikkan" de aru. Sono shiin-betsu shibouritsu wa zentai no 60-paasento chikaku o shimeru. Sonota no shiin to shite wa "haien oyobi kikanshien" ya "rousui," "furyo no jiko" nado ga agerareru. 1980-nen goro, "noukekkan shikkan" ni kawari, "akusei shinseibutsu" ga shiin no ichi-i o shimeru you ni natta. Kinnen "shinshikkan" ni yoru shibou mo kyuugeki ni fuete ori, chikai shourai "akusei shinseibutsu" ni yoru shibou to awasete no shibouritsu wa, zentai no 50-paasento ni tassuru darou to iwarete iru.

* footnote translation: "Malignant growths (cancer)," "Heart disease," "Vascular problems in the brain"　·　** footnote: "Pneumonia and Bronchitis" or "Old Age," "Unanticipated Death"

EN: Japan's major causes of death are "malignant neoplasms (cancer)," "heart disease," and "cerebrovascular disease." Together their death rates account for close to 60% of the total. Other causes include "pneumonia and bronchitis," "old age," and "unforeseen accidents." Around 1980, "malignant neoplasms" overtook "cerebrovascular disease" to become the number-one cause of death. In recent years, deaths from "heart disease" have also been rising sharply, and it's said that in the near future, the combined death rate from "malignant neoplasms" and "heart disease" will reach 50% of the total.
HI: जापान में मृत्यु के प्रमुख कारण हैं "घातक अर्बुद (कैंसर)", "हृदय रोग", और "मस्तिष्क-रक्तवाहिका रोग"। इन तीनों की मिली-जुली मृत्यु दर कुल का लगभग 60% है। अन्य कारणों में "निमोनिया और ब्रोंकाइटिस", "वृद्धावस्था", और "अनपेक्षित दुर्घटना" शामिल हैं। लगभग 1980 में, "घातक अर्बुद" ने "मस्तिष्क-रक्तवाहिका रोग" को पीछे छोड़कर मृत्यु का नंबर एक कारण बनने का स्थान ले लिया। हाल के वर्षों में "हृदय रोग" से मृत्यु भी तेज़ी से बढ़ रही है, और कहा जाता है कि निकट भविष्य में "घातक अर्बुद" और "हृदय रोग" को मिलाकर मृत्यु दर कुल का 50% तक पहुँच जाएगी।
GU: જાપાનમાં મૃત્યુનાં મુખ્ય કારણો છે "ઘાતક ગાંઠ (કેન્સર)", "હૃદય રોગ", અને "મગજની રક્તવાહિની બિમારી". આ ત્રણેયનો ભેગો મૃત્યુ દર કુલનો લગભગ 60% છે. અન્ય કારણોમાં "ન્યુમોનિયા અને બ્રોન્કાઇટિસ", "વૃદ્ધાવસ્થા", અને "અણધારી દુર્ઘટના" સામેલ છે. લગભગ 1980માં, "ઘાતક ગાંઠ" એ "મગજની રક્તવાહિની બિમારી"ને પાછળ છોડી મૃત્યુનું નંબર વન કારણ બનવાનું સ્થાન લીધું. તાજેતરનાં વર્ષોમાં "હૃદય રોગ"થી મૃત્યુ પણ ઝડપથી વધી રહ્યું છે, અને કહેવાય છે કે નજીકના ભવિષ્યમાં "ઘાતક ગાંઠ" અને "હૃદય રોગ"ને ભેગું કરી મૃત્યુ દર કુલનો 50% સુધી પહોંચી જશે.

問い　次のグラフで、この文章に合うものはどれか。Toi: Tsugi no gurafu de, kono bunshou ni au mono wa dore ka.

Option悪性新生物心疾患脳血管疾患その他
130.015.911.142.9
222.525.214.338.0
320.820.08.650.6
427.123.411.038.5

Why: Three conditions from the passage must all hold at once. (a) 悪性新生物 must be the largest of the three named causes, since it's described as death cause "一位" (rank 1) — this immediately rules out option 2, where 心疾患(25.2) > 悪性新生物(22.5). (b) The three named causes together should be "60%近く" (close to 60%) — option 3's total (20.8+20.0+8.6=49.4%) is far short of 60%, ruling it out. (c) 悪性新生物+心疾患 combined should be just under 50% ("近い将来…50%に達するだろう" implies it hasn't reached 50% yet) — option 4's combination (27.1+23.4=50.5) has already passed 50%, contradicting "will reach it in the near future." Option 1 satisfies all three: 悪性新生物(30.0) is largest, the three-cause total (30.0+15.9+11.1=57.0%) is close to but under 60%, and the two-cause combination (30.0+15.9=45.9%) is still under 50%, consistent with "will reach it soon." Option 1 is also close to real published Japanese mortality statistics for cancer/heart/stroke.

Q2

Passage — 大学・短大への進学率 (University/junior-college enrollment rate)

次の文章は日本の大学・短大への進学率に関する調査の結果より、1955年から2010年までの5年ごとの推移について述べたものである。Tsugi no bunshou wa Nihon no daigaku, tandai e no shingaku-ritsu ni kansuru chousa no kekka yori, 1955-nen kara 2010-nen made no go-nen goto no suii ni tsuite nobeta mono de aru.

大学、短期大学への進学率は男女とも、1975年ごろまでは急激に伸びたが、その後女子はほぼ横ばい状態が約15年間続いた。その間、男子の進学率は低下し、1990年ごろには女子が男子を上回った。以後再び、男女とも伸び率が大きくなり、2000年には全体で49.1%となった。また、2000年には再び男女の進学率が逆転した。今後、数年で60%に達すると予想される。Daigaku, tanki daigaku e no shingaku-ritsu wa danjo tomo, 1975-nen goro made wa kyuugeki ni nobita ga, sono go joshi wa hobo yokobai joutai ga yaku juugo-nenkan tsuzuita. Sono aida, danshi no shingaku-ritsu wa teika shi, 1990-nen goro ni wa joshi ga danshi o uwamawatta. Igo futatabi, danjo tomo nobiritsu ga ookiku nari, 2000-nen ni wa zentai de 49.1-paasento to natta. Mata, 2000-nen ni wa futatabi danjo no shingaku-ritsu ga gyakuten shita. Kongo, suunen de 60-paasento ni tassuru to yosou sareru.

EN: For both men and women, university/junior-college enrollment rose sharply until around 1975. After that, women's rate stayed almost flat for about 15 years, during which men's rate declined; around 1990, women's rate overtook men's. After that, both rose sharply again, reaching an overall 49.1% by 2000 — and in 2000 the men's/women's rates reversed again (men back on top). It's expected to reach 60% within a few years.
HI: पुरुषों और महिलाओं दोनों के लिए, विश्वविद्यालय/जूनियर कॉलेज में प्रवेश दर 1975 तक तेज़ी से बढ़ी। इसके बाद, महिलाओं की दर लगभग 15 साल तक स्थिर रही, जबकि पुरुषों की दर घट गई; लगभग 1990 में, महिलाओं की दर पुरुषों से आगे निकल गई। इसके बाद दोनों फिर तेज़ी से बढ़े, और 2000 तक कुल दर 49.1% हो गई — और 2000 में पुरुष/महिला दरें फिर पलट गईं (पुरुष फिर आगे)। अनुमान है कि कुछ ही वर्षों में यह 60% तक पहुँच जाएगी।
GU: પુરુષો અને મહિલાઓ બંને માટે, યુનિવર્સિટી/જુનિયર કૉલેજમાં પ્રવેશ દર 1975 સુધી ઝડપથી વધ્યો. તે પછી, મહિલાઓનો દર લગભગ 15 વર્ષ સ્થિર રહ્યો, જ્યારે પુરુષોનો દર ઘટ્યો; લગભગ 1990માં, મહિલાઓનો દર પુરુષો કરતાં આગળ નીકળી ગયો. તે પછી બંને ફરી ઝડપથી વધ્યા, અને 2000 સુધીમાં કુલ દર 49.1% થયો — અને 2000માં પુરુષ/મહિલા દર ફરી પલટાયા (પુરુષ ફરી આગળ). અંદાજ છે કે થોડાં જ વર્ષોમાં તે 60% સુધી પહોંચી જશે.

問い　次のグラフで、この文章に合うものはどれか。Toi: Tsugi no gurafu de, kono bunshou ni au mono wa dore ka.

4 line graphs (1955–2010, %) each plotting 男子 (men) and 女子 (women). Graph 1: both lines rise smoothly with no dip or plateau. Graph 2: 男子 peaks around 1975 (~44%), declines to ~35% by 1990, then rises sharply again; 女子 rises to ~33% by 1975, stays flat around 33–37% until 1990, overtakes 男子 around 1990, then both rise, with 男子 ending higher (~58%) than 女子 (~56%) by 2010. Graph 3: labels swapped — 女子 starts as the higher line at 15%, 男子 the lower line at 5% (historically backwards). Graph 4: 男子 shows the same peak-and-dip shape as Graph 2, but 女子 ends up higher (~58%) than 男子 (~56%) by 2010.

OptionEnglish summary
1Both lines rise smoothly throughout, no plateau or decline for either sex.
2Men peak ~1975 then decline to ~1990; women stay flat 1975–1990, overtake men ~1990, then men retake the lead by 2010.
3Same shape family as graph 2/4, but women (not men) are the historically higher line from the start.
4Same peak-and-dip shape for men, but women end up higher than men by 2010.

Why: Every clause in the passage must match. Graph 1 has no plateau and no decline for either sex, contradicting both "women flat for ~15 years" and "men's rate declined" — ruled out. Graph 3 has women historically higher than men from 1955, which contradicts the well-known historical fact (and the passage's implication) that men's enrollment led before the 1990 crossover — ruled out. Graphs 2 and 4 both correctly show men peaking then dipping 1975–1990 while women stay roughly flat and then cross over around 1990 — but the passage's final clause, 2000年には再び男女の進学率が逆転した (in 2000 the rates reversed again), requires men to retake the lead after the 1990s women-led period. Graph 2 ends with men (~58%) above women (~56%) by 2010, matching this final reversal; Graph 4 ends with women (~58%) still above men (~56%), which never reverses back — failing the passage's last sentence. Graph 2 is the only one that satisfies every clause in order.
