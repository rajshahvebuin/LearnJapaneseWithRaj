1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

记住表格题中常用的说法！Jìzhù biǎogé tí zhōng chángyòng de shuōfǎ!

"Memorise the phrases commonly used in graph/chart questions!" Graph questions reuse a small, fixed vocabulary for describing shape, ranking, and proportion. Once you know these set phrases, you can match them to a graph shape instantly instead of re-deriving the meaning each time.

  • 1. Words for change over time. 移り変わり・推移・変動・動向 (all roughly "change/transition/shift"); a line that ピーク (peaks) then 下降 (declines); 一定・横ばい (flat/level); 急激に・大幅に伸びる／伸びが著しい (rises sharply/greatly/remarkably) vs 徐々に伸びる／伸びが鈍い (rises gradually/sluggishly).
    HI: बदलाव बताने वाले शब्द: उतार-चढ़ाव, प्रवृत्ति; रेखा जो ऊँचाई पर पहुँचकर गिरती है; स्थिर/समतल; तेज़ी से बढ़ना बनाम धीरे-धीरे बढ़ना। GU: ફેરફાર બતાવતા શબ્દો: ચડાવ-ઉતાર, વલણ; રેખા જે ટોચે પહોંચી પછી ઘટે; સ્થિર/સપાટ; ઝડપથી વધવું વિરુદ્ધ ધીમે ધીમે વધવું.
  • 2. Words for ranking and comparison. Ｂが1位を占める (B ranks first); ＢはＡ、Ｃを上回る (B exceeds A and C); ＣはＢを下回る (C falls below B).
    HI: तुलना के शब्द: "B पहले स्थान पर है", "B, A और C से ज़्यादा है", "C, B से कम है"। GU: સરખામણીના શબ્દો: "B પ્રથમ સ્થાને છે", "B, A અને C કરતાં વધુ છે", "C, B કરતાં ઓછું છે".
  • 3. Words for proportion. 割合／～率は80%を占める・大半を占める (accounts for 80% / accounts for the majority); 約10%にとどまる (stays at around only 10%).
    HI: अनुपात के शब्द: "अनुपात/दर 80% है", "अधिकांश हिस्सा है", "लगभग 10% तक ही सीमित है"। GU: પ્રમાણના શબ્દો: "પ્રમાણ/દર 80% છે", "મોટા ભાગનો હિસ્સો છે", "લગભગ 10% સુધી જ મર્યાદિત છે".

The book's cat jokes: 「ボクの成績は急激に落ちている。」"Boku no seiseki wa kyuugeki ni ochite iru." — "My grades are dropping sharply." — using exactly this vocabulary for a steep decline.

Q1

Graph — 国民生活に関する世論調査 (Public opinion survey on national life)

右のグラフは、日本の国民が今後の生活がどうなっていくと考えているか、について調査した結果である。Migi no gurafu wa, Nihon no kokumin ga kongo no seikatsu ga dou natte iku to kangaete iru ka, ni tsuite chousa shita kekka de aru.

Stacked bar chart, by age group (全体〈overall〉, 20〜29歳, 30〜39歳, 40〜49歳, 50〜59歳, 60〜69歳, 70歳以上), each split into 4 shares: 良くなっていく (will get better), 同じようなもの (will stay about the same), 悪くなっていく (will get worse), わからない (don't know). Overall, and in every age group, the "will stay about the same" share is clearly the largest single share (visually well over half the bar). The "will get better" share is largest for the 20s and shrinks steadily with age; the "will get worse" share is smallest for the 20s and grows steadily with age, becoming largest for the 50s–60s. (資料：内閣府大臣官房政府広報室「国民生活に関する世論調査〈2010年〉」)

問い　グラフの説明として正しくないのはどれか。Toi: Gurafu no setsumei to shite tadashikunai no wa dore ka.

EN: Question: Which of the following is NOT a correct description of the graph?
HI: प्रश्न: ग्राफ़ के बारे में निम्न में से कौन-सा विवरण सही नहीं है?
GU: પ્રશ્ન: ગ્રાફ વિશે નીચેનામાંથી કયું વર્ણન સાચું નથી?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1「わからない」という回答は、年齢が高くなるにつれて多くなる。"Wakaranai" to iu kaitou wa, nenrei ga takaku naru ni tsurete ooku naru."Don't know" answers increase as age rises."पता नहीं" वाला उत्तर उम्र बढ़ने के साथ बढ़ता जाता है।"ખબર નથી" જવાબ ઉંમર વધવા સાથે વધતો જાય છે.
2「良くなっていく」が「悪くなっていく」を上回ったのは20代のみである。"Yoku natte iku" ga "waruku natte iku" o uwamawatta no wa nijuudai nomi de aru."Will get better" exceeded "will get worse" only in the 20s."बेहतर होगा" ने "बदतर होगा" को केवल 20 के दशक (20s) में पीछे छोड़ा।"સારું થશે" એ "ખરાબ થશે"ને ફક્ત 20ના દાયકામાં જ પાછળ છોડ્યું.
3「悪くなっていく」と答えている人は50代・60代に多く見られる。"Waruku natte iku" to kotaete iru hito wa gojuudai, rokujuudai ni ooku mirareru.Those answering "will get worse" are mostly seen in their 50s and 60s."बदतर होगा" कहने वाले ज़्यादातर 50 और 60 के दशक में देखे जाते हैं।"ખરાબ થશે" કહેનારા મોટે ભાગે 50 અને 60ના દાયકામાં જોવા મળે છે.
4全体的に「同じようなもの」と答えた人は半数以上を占めている。Zentaiteki ni "onaji you na mono" to kotaeta hito wa hansuu ijou o shimete iru.Overall, more than half answered "about the same."कुल मिलाकर, आधे से ज़्यादा लोगों ने "लगभग वैसा ही" कहा।એકંદરે, અડધાથી વધુ લોકોએ "લગભગ એ જ" કહ્યું.

Why (reasoned from the visual chart — flagged as the least certain answer this week): Measuring the bar segments as carefully as the scan allows: the "will get better" (black) share is largest in the 20s and shrinks with age, while "will get worse" (grey) is smallest in the 20s and grows with age — but the crossover between the two doesn't happen cleanly at the 20s/30s boundary. The 30s bar still shows "will get better" slightly ahead of "will get worse," and only from the 40s onward does "will get worse" clearly take over. That means the 20s are not the only age group where "better" outpaces "worse" — the exclusive claim "20代のみ" (only the 20s) in option 2 overreaches. Option 4 is safely true (the "about the same" share is visibly well over half in every bar, including overall). Option 3 is also true (grey clearly grows into the 50s/60s). Option 1's broad trend (more "don't know" at higher ages) roughly holds, especially at 70+, even if it isn't perfectly smooth step-by-step — so by elimination, and because "のみ" (only) is the kind of absolute claim JLPT distractors love to plant, option 2 is the best-supported "incorrect" answer.

Q2

Graph — 年齢階級別人口構成 (Population structure by age group)

次のグラフは、日本の年齢階級別人口構成（1935年、1960年、2008年）を表したものである。Tsugi no gurafu wa, Nihon no nenrei kaisou betsu jinkou kousei (1935-nen, 1960-nen, 2008-nen) o arawashita mono de aru.

Three population pyramids (age 0–80+ on the vertical axis, population left/right), for 1935, 1960 and 2008. （資料：総務省統計局「人口統計」）

問い　上のグラフについて、正しく説明しているのはどちらか。Toi: Ue no gurafu ni tsuite, tadashiku setsumei shite iru no wa dochira ka.

EN: Question: Which of the following correctly describes the graphs above?
HI: प्रश्न: उपरोक्त ग्राफ़ के बारे में निम्न में से कौन-सा सही वर्णन करता है?
GU: પ્રશ્ન: ઉપરોક્ત ગ્રાફ વિશે નીચેનામાંથી કયું સાચું વર્ણન કરે છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglish
1若年齢層の割合は、1935年には高く、その後、近年になるほど低下し、逆に高年齢層は比率を高めている。Jaku-nenrei-sou no wariai wa, 1935-nen ni wa takaku, sono go, kinnen ni naru hodo teika shi, gyaku ni kou-nenrei-sou wa hiritsu o takamete iru.The young age group's share was high in 1935 and has since declined toward recent years, while the elderly age group's share has conversely risen.
21935年には富士山型と呼ばれるように、若年齢層が圧倒的に多かったが、年々減少し、その割合は高年齢層と逆転し、2008年には逆富士山型に変化している。1935-nen ni wa Fujisan-gata to yobareru you ni, jaku-nenrei-sou ga attouteki ni ookatta ga, nen'nen genshou shi, sono wariai wa kou-nenrei-sou to gyakuten shi, 2008-nen ni wa gyaku-Fujisan-gata ni henka shite iru.In 1935, as it's called the "Mt. Fuji shape," the young age group was overwhelmingly the largest, but it has decreased year by year, its share has reversed with the elderly group, and by 2008 it has changed into an "inverted Mt. Fuji shape."

Why: Both options describe the same real trend (young shrinking, elderly growing), but option 2 makes a much more specific — and overstated — claim: that the 2008 pyramid is a clean "inverted Mt. Fuji" (a smooth triangle wide at the top, narrow at the bottom). Japan's actual 2008 population pyramid is irregular, with bulges from the postwar baby-boom generations rather than a tidy inverted triangle, so "逆富士山型" is too strong a description to be verified from the graph. Option 1's more moderate, general description of the trend (young share down, elderly share up over time) is the safer, defensible reading — option 1.

HI: प्रश्न: उपरोक्त ग्राफ़ के बारे में निम्न में से कौन-सा सही वर्णन करता है?

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1どちらの年も若年に比べて中高年の投票率が低くなっている。Dochira no toshi mo jakunen ni kurabete chuukounen no touhyou-ritsu ga hikuku natte iru.In both years, turnout for the middle-aged/elderly is lower than for the young.
2どの年齢も1980年より2009年の投票率が低下している。Dono nenrei mo 1980-nen yori 2009-nen no touhyou-ritsu ga teika shite iru.Every age group's 2009 turnout is lower than in 1980.
3どちらの年も60歳未満は年齢の上昇とともに投票率が伸びている。Dochira no toshi mo rokujussai miman wa nenrei no joushou to tomo ni touhyou-ritsu ga nobite iru.In both years, for under-60s, turnout rises as age increases.
42009年は20代後半の投票率が半数を割った。2009-nen wa nijuudai kouhan no touhyou-ritsu ga hansuu o watta.In 2009, turnout for the late 20s fell below half.

Why: This one is fully verifiable from the printed numbers. Option 1 is backwards — turnout is actually higher, not lower, for older groups compared to the young, in both years. Option 2 fails at 65–69 (84.5%→85.0%, a rise) and 70+ (69.7%→71.06%, also a rise) — not every age group declined. Option 4 fails because 2009's 25–29 turnout was 52.1%, above half, not below. Option 3 checks out cleanly: in 1980, every age band from 20–24 up to 55–59 rises monotonically (58.3→67.0→73.3→79.2→80.5→83.2→84.8→85.7); in 2009, the same under-60 bands also rise monotonically (46.7→52.1→61.1→66.2→70.3→75.0→78.9→80.4). Option 3 is the only statement fully supported by the numbers.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare how confidently each answer can be verified:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the graph testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Compare shaded segment sizes across age groups Whether an "only ~" claim genuinely holds for every other group too Watch out for options using のみ (only) or 半数以上 (more than half) — these absolute claims are easy to falsify if you check even one extra bar.
Q2 Match a real trend against an overstated shape claim Distinguishing a moderate, defensible description from an exaggerated one 逆富士山型 (inverted Mt. Fuji shape) sounds vivid and matches the general direction, but real population pyramids rarely form a perfectly clean inverted triangle — be suspicious of dramatic, overly specific shape claims.
Q3 Check every value against the claim, not just a few Monotonic increase for under-60s in both years, verified figure by figure Options 2 and 4 are disproven by single exceptions (65–69 and 70+ rising; 25–29 staying above half) — one counter-example is enough to kill an "every/all" claim.
Exam trap: This book's answer booklet [別冊] isn't included in this scan. Q2 and Q3 are backed by numbers/labels legible in the scan, but Q1's chart is a heavily shaded, low-resolution bar chart — that answer is a best-effort visual reading, not a certainty.
