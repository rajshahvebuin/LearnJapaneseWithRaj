1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

抓住有用的信息！Zhuāzhù yǒuyòng de xìnxī!

"Grab hold of the useful information!" Bulletin-board posts (掲示板) are short and informal, but a page often shows several similar-looking posts side by side — the exam tests whether you notice what's actually different between them, not just the topic they share.

  • 1. When several similar posts are listed together, focus on the differences. Three ads about pets might look the same at a glance, but one is giving an animal away, one is looking for a lost animal, and one is asking to receive one — the verb (差し上げます／探しています／譲ってください) is what actually matters.
    HI: जब कई मिलती-जुलती सूचनाएँ एक साथ हों, तो उनके बीच के अंतर पर ध्यान दें — क्रिया (देना/खोजना/माँगना) ही असली फ़र्क़ बताती है। GU: જ્યારે ઘણી મળતી-જુલતી જાહેરાતો સાથે હોય, ત્યારે તેમની વચ્ચેના તફાવત પર ધ્યાન આપો — ક્રિયાપદ (આપવું/શોધવું/માંગવું) જ ખરો ફરક બતાવે છે.
  • 2. Grasp only the part that's actually useful to the question. A posting can be read in a few seconds once you know which single fact (what's being offered, who to contact, what condition applies) the question is actually asking about — ignore the rest.
    HI: प्रश्न से जुड़ी सिर्फ़ एक ज़रूरी बात (क्या दिया जा रहा है, किससे संपर्क करना है, कौन-सी शर्त है) पर ध्यान दें — बाक़ी को नज़रअंदाज़ करें। GU: પ્રશ્ન સાથે જોડાયેલી ફક્ત એક જરૂરી વાત (શું અપાઈ રહ્યું છે, કોનો સંપર્ક કરવો, કઈ શરત છે) પર ધ્યાન આપો — બાકીનું અવગણો.

The book illustrates this with a joke posting — 「ダメネコ♂差し上げます」"Dame neko (osu) sashiagemasu" ("Giving away one no-good male cat") — which two other cats immediately turn down: 「いらないよ！」"Iranai yo!" ("Don't want it!") and 「わたしもやめとく。」"Watashi mo yametoku." ("I'll pass too.") — a reminder that not every posting is useful to you, even if it's about the same topic.

Q1

Posting

白い中型犬、探しています！Shiroi chuugataken, sagashite imasu!

5月14日の午後、いなくなりました。赤い首輪をしている老犬です。見つけた方は連絡してください！Go-gatsu juu-yokka no gogo, inaku narimashita. Akai kubiwa o shite iru rouken desu. Mitsuketa kata wa renraku shite kudasai!

EN: Looking for a medium-sized white dog! It went missing on the afternoon of May 14. It's an elderly dog wearing a red collar. If you find it, please get in touch!
HI: एक मध्यम आकार का सफ़ेद कुत्ता ढूँढ रहे हैं! यह 14 मई की दोपहर को खो गया। यह लाल पट्टे वाला बूढ़ा कुत्ता है। अगर मिले तो कृपया संपर्क करें!
GU: એક મધ્યમ કદનું સફેદ કૂતરું શોધી રહ્યા છીએ! તે 14 મેની બપોરે ખોવાઈ ગયું. તે લાલ પટ્ટાવાળું ઘરડું કૂતરું છે. જો મળે તો કૃપા કરી સંપર્ક કરો!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1白い中型犬を飼いたいので譲ってほしい。Shiroi chuugataken o kaitai node yuzutte hoshii.Wants to keep a medium white dog, so wants someone to give one away.सफ़ेद मध्यम कुत्ता पालना चाहते हैं, इसलिए कोई दे दे।સફેદ મધ્યમ કૂતરું પાળવા માંગે છે, એટલે કોઈ આપી દે.
2飼っている犬がいなくなったので見つけてほしい。Katte iru inu ga inaku natta node mitsukete hoshii.Their own dog went missing, so they want someone to find it.उनका पाला हुआ कुत्ता खो गया है, इसलिए उसे ढूँढ दे।તેમનું પાળેલું કૂતરું ખોવાયું છે, એટલે તેને શોધી આપે.

Why: The verbs give it away: 探しています (looking for) + いなくなりました (it went missing/disappeared) + 見つけた方は連絡してください (whoever finds it, please contact) — this is a lost-pet notice, not a want-to-adopt notice. Option 1 wrongly reframes it as someone wanting to receive a dog to keep, which reverses the actual direction of the request.

Q2

Posting

ベビー用品譲ってください！！　近藤Bebii youhin yuzutte kudasai!! Kondou

6月出産予定です。ベビーベッド、ベビーカー等探しています。Roku-gatsu shussan yotei desu. Bebii beddo, bebii kaa nado sagashite imasu.

ご不要な方、譲っていただけませんか？　よろしくお願いします！Go-fuyou na kata, yuzutte itadakemasen ka? Yoroshiku onegai shimasu!

EN: Please give me baby goods!! — Kondou. I'm due to give birth in June. I'm looking for a baby bed, a stroller, and similar items. If anyone doesn't need theirs any more, could you give them to me? Thank you very much!
HI: कृपया मुझे बच्चों का सामान दें!! — कोन्दो। मुझे जून में बच्चा होने वाला है। मैं बेबी बेड, बेबी कार जैसी चीज़ें ढूँढ रही हूँ। अगर किसी को ज़रूरत नहीं है तो क्या वे मुझे दे सकते हैं? धन्यवाद!
GU: કૃપા કરી મને બાળકોનો સામાન આપો!! — કોન્દો. મને જૂનમાં બાળક થવાનું છે. હું બેબી બેડ, બેબી કાર જેવી વસ્તુઓ શોધી રહી છું. જો કોઈને જરૂર ન હોય તો શું તેઓ મને આપી શકે? આભાર!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1もうすぐ子どもが生まれるのでベビー用品をそろえたい。Mou sugu kodomo ga umareru node bebii youhin o soroetai.A baby is due soon, so she wants to gather baby items.जल्द ही बच्चा होने वाला है, इसलिए बच्चों का सामान इकट्ठा करना चाहती है।જલ્દી બાળક થવાનું છે, એટલે બાળકોનો સામાન ભેગો કરવા માંગે છે.
2ベビー用品をなくしてしまったので、さがしてほしい。Bebii youhin o nakushite shimatta node, sagashite hoshii.She lost her baby items, so wants someone to find them.बच्चों का सामान खो गया है, इसलिए उसे ढूँढ दे।બાળકોનો સામાન ખોવાયો છે, એટલે તેને શોધી આપે.
3どんなベビー用品を買えばいいか、意見がほしい。Donna bebii youhin o kaeba ii ka, iken ga hoshii.She wants opinions on which baby items to buy.कौन-सा बच्चों का सामान ख़रीदें, इस पर राय चाहिए।કયો બાળકોનો સામાન ખરીદવો, તેના પર અભિપ્રાય જોઈએ છે.
4もうすぐ子どもが生まれるので、ベビー用品をもらってほしい。Mou sugu kodomo ga umareru node, bebii youhin o moratte hoshii.A baby is due soon, so she wants someone to receive her baby items.जल्द ही बच्चा होने वाला है, इसलिए वह चाहती है कि कोई उसका सामान ले ले।જલ્દી બાળક થવાનું છે, એટલે તે ઇચ્છે છે કે કોઈ તેનો સામાન લઈ લે.

Why: 譲ってください (please give [it] to me) and 探しています (I'm looking for) both point the same direction: Kondou wants to receive items, because her baby is due (出産予定). Option 4 reverses the direction (as if she wants to give her own things away). Option 2 misreads 探しています as "lost," but she never had these items — she's looking to acquire them. Option 3 invents a request for opinions that isn't in the text.

Q3

Community board — みどり市民 掲示板 (Midori Citizens' Bulletin Board)

譲ります　＊原則無料です。有料の場合や送料負担の場合は事前によく話し合ってください。Yuzurimasu. *Gensoku muryou desu. Yuuryou no baai ya souryou futan no baai wa jizen ni yoku hanashiatte kudasai. — "Giving away. *In principle these are free. If there's a charge or shipping cost, please discuss it beforehand."

A — 家具・家電・経済関係書　卒業するので家具や家電品、東都大 経済学部の教科書や参考書などを格安で処分します。欲しい人は連絡してください。先着順に交渉に応じます。平山（TEL XX-XXXX）Kagu, kaden, keizai kankeisho. Sotsugyou suru node kagu ya kadenhin, Toutodai keizaigakubu no kyoukasho ya sankousho nado o kakuyasu de shobun shimasu. Hoshii hito wa renraku shite kudasai. Senchakujun ni koushou ni oujimasu. Hirayama (TEL XX-XXXX)

EN (A): Furniture, appliances, economics books. I'm graduating, so I'm selling off furniture, appliances, and Toto University Economics Department textbooks and reference books cheaply. If you want any, please contact me. I'll negotiate in order of first contact. — Hirayama
HI: फ़र्नीचर, उपकरण, अर्थशास्त्र की किताबें। स्नातक हो रहा हूँ, इसलिए फ़र्नीचर, उपकरण और तोतो विश्वविद्यालय अर्थशास्त्र विभाग की किताबें सस्ते में बेच रहा हूँ। जिसे चाहिए, संपर्क करें। पहले संपर्क करने वाले से पहले बात होगी। — हिरायामा
GU: ફર્નિચર, ઉપકરણો, અર્થશાસ્ત્રનાં પુસ્તકો. સ્નાતક થઈ રહ્યો છું, એટલે ફર્નિચર, ઉપકરણો અને તોતો યુનિવર્સિટી અર્થશાસ્ત્ર વિભાગનાં પુસ્તકો સસ્તામાં વેચી રહ્યો છું. જેને જોઈએ, સંપર્ક કરો. પહેલા સંપર્ક કરનાર સાથે પહેલા વાત થશે. — હિરાયામા
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1全部無料でもらえる。Zenbu muryou de moraeru.You can get everything for free.सब कुछ मुफ़्त में मिल सकता है।બધું મફતમાં મળી શકે.
2早く連絡したほうがいい。Hayaku renraku shita hou ga ii.It's better to contact him quickly.जल्दी संपर्क करना बेहतर है।જલ્દી સંપર્ક કરવો સારો.
3平山さんは不用品を捨てたい。Hirayama-san wa fuyouhin o sutetai.Hirayama wants to throw away unwanted items.हिरायामा अनुपयोगी सामान फेंकना चाहते हैं।હિરાયામા બિનજરૂરી સામાન ફેંકવા માંગે છે.
4平山さんは大学に勤めている。Hirayama-san wa daigaku ni tsutomete iru.Hirayama works at the university.हिरायामा विश्वविद्यालय में काम करते हैं।હિરાયામા યુનિવર્સિટીમાં કામ કરે છે.

Why: 先着順に交渉に応じます (I'll negotiate in order of first contact) directly implies that contacting early is an advantage — matching option 2. Option 1 is wrong because 格安で処分します means "sell off cheaply," not "give away free" (無料). Option 3 overstates 処分する ("get rid of") as 捨てたい ("want to throw away") — Hirayama is explicitly asking for money for these items, not discarding them for free, so the two aren't the same. Option 4 contradicts 卒業するので ("because I'm graduating") — he's a student, not a university employee.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which strategy from section 1 unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the post testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Check the verb direction 探しています + いなくなりました = lost pet, not "want to adopt" Don't assume every pet posting means someone wants to receive an animal — check whether it's giving away, looking for a lost one, or asking to receive one.
Q2 Check the verb direction 譲ってください = asking to receive, not to give away Option 4 flips the direction entirely — always confirm who is giving and who is receiving.
Q3 Grasp only the relevant part 先着順に交渉に応じます implies quick contact is an advantage 格安で処分する (sell off cheaply) ≠ 捨てたい (want to throw away) — selling for money is not discarding for free.
Q4 Match one need against six postings Filter by: appliance only, already has a fridge, has a car F looks generous (free!) but its "take everything together" condition and unwanted fridge make it a worse match than A+B+D.
