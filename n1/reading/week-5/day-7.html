1 Test Strategy Recap

Key idea

Before the test, a quick recap of everything Week 5 has built toward:

  • Day 1 (お知らせ・案内): skip greeting boilerplate, read the 記～以上 facts block for hard dates/places.
  • Day 2 (掲示板): when several similar posts appear together, the verb direction (give/seek/receive) is what actually differs.
  • Day 3 (リストを読む): read the question before the table, then filter by every stated condition at once.
  • Day 4 (説明を読む): match the exact scenario in the question to its matching branch/row in the rules.
  • Days 5–6 (グラフを読む): use precise proportion/ranking vocabulary, and check every clause of a passage against a graph, not just the first one that seems to fit.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1Aのほうが得。A no hou ga toku.Card A is more cost-effective.
2Bのほうが得。B no hou ga toku.Card B is more cost-effective.
3同じだけ得になる。Onaji dake toku ni naru.Both save the same amount.

Why (worked example): Six months × 1 movie = 6 tickets needed, general price ¥1,800 each (¥10,800 with no card at all). Card A: ¥100 to issue, then pay for 5 tickets (5×¥1,800=¥9,000) to earn 5 stamps, and the 6th movie is free. Total = ¥9,100. Total = ¥10,000. ¥9,100 (A) is cheaper than ¥10,000 (B), so Card A is more cost-effective.

コラム

北極と南極、どちらが寒い？ (Which is colder, the North Pole or the South Pole?)

北極と南極は、どちらも氷で覆われていて、とても寒い地域ですが、どちらのほうが寒いでしょうか？　北極の平均気温はマイナス30度で、一方、南極はマイナス60度です。南極のほうが寒いのです。なぜでしょうか。Hokkyoku to Nankyoku wa, dochira mo koori de oowarete ite, totemo samui chiiki desu ga, dochira no hou ga samui deshou ka? Hokkyoku no heikin kion wa mainasu sanjuu-do de, ippou, Nankyoku wa mainasu rokujuu-do desu. Nankyoku no hou ga samui no desu. Naze deshou ka.

これは比熱が違うからです。比熱とは1グラム当たりの物質の温度を1度上げるのに必要な熱量です。南極は氷の下に大陸があります。北極には大陸はなく、氷の海だけです。陸は海の4分の1しか比熱がありません。つまり、水は温まりにくく冷えにくく、陸は温まりやすく冷えやすいので、大陸のあるほうが冷えるのです。Kore wa hinetsu ga chigau kara desu. Hinetsu to wa ichi-guramu atari no busshitsu no ondo o ichi-do ageru no ni hitsuyou na netsuryou desu. Nankyoku wa kouri no shita ni tairiku ga arimasu. Hokkyoku ni wa tairiku wa naku, koori no umi dake desu. Riku wa umi no yon-bun no ichi shika hinetsu ga arimasen. Tsumari, mizu wa atatamarinikuku hienikuku, riku wa atatamariyasuku hieyasui node, tairiku no aru hou ga hieru no desu.

また、北極の氷の厚さは10メートルくらいですが、陸地である南極は雪が積もると解けずにどんどん重なって厚い氷になります。この氷、平均で2000メートル以上、いちばん厚いところでは、4000メートル以上にもなります。つまり場所によっては日本の富士山（3776メートル）よりも高いということです。富士山の山頂が夏でも寒いように、標高が高いところは気温が下がりますから、南極のほうがずっと寒いというわけです。Mata, Hokkyoku no koori no atsusa wa juu-meetoru kurai desu ga, rikuchi de aru Nankyoku wa yuki ga tsumoru to tokezu ni dondon kasanatte atsui koori ni narimasu. EN: Both the North and South Poles are covered in ice and are extremely cold, but which is colder? The North Pole's average temperature is −30°C, while the South Pole's is −60°C — the South Pole is colder. Why? This is because of a difference in specific heat: the heat needed to raise 1 gram of a substance by 1°C. Antarctica (South Pole) has a continent under its ice; the Arctic (North Pole) has no continent, only an icy sea. Land has only a quarter of the specific heat of water — water is slow to warm and slow to cool, while land warms and cools quickly — so the side with a continent gets colder. Also, Arctic ice is only about 10 meters thick, but on the landmass of Antarctica, snow piles up without melting and compacts into thick ice, averaging over 2,000 meters and reaching over 4,000 meters at its thickest — in places, thicker than Mt. Fuji (3,776 m) is tall. Just as Mt. Fuji's summit is cold even in summer, higher elevations mean lower temperatures, which is why Antarctica is so much colder. ☆ Does this mean that penguins in Antarctica are more cold-resistant than polar bears in the Arctic?
