【1】 Nを余儀なくされる

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o yogi naku sareru
Meaning (EN)"be forced to N (by circumstances beyond one's control)"
Meaning (HI)(हालात के कारण) N करने पर मजबूर होना
Meaning (GU)(સંજોગોને કારણે) N કરવા મજબૂર થવું
Connection (接続)N(する-noun) + を余儀なくされる  |  cause + が + N + を余儀なくさせる
Register硬 — news, reports
Typical useNouns of unwanted action: 退職・中止・変更・延期・撤退・入院. The cause is outside the subject (不況, 災害, 病気).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を余儀なくされた退職を余儀なくされたtaishoku o yogi naku sareta
Noun (causative)(added, not in book) cause が + N + を余儀なくさせる計画の変更を余儀なくさせたkeikaku no henkou o yogi naku saseta
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — needs an action noun—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 会社が経営不振で、退職を余儀なくされた。Kaisha ga keiei fushin de, taishoku o yogi naku sareta. The company was doing badly, and I was forced to resign. कंपनी की हालत ख़राब होने से मुझे नौकरी छोड़ने पर मजबूर होना पड़ा। કંપનીની હાલત ખરાબ હોવાથી મારે નોકરી છોડવા મજબૂર થવું પડ્યું.
(added, not in book) 大雨のため、試合は延期を余儀なくされた。Ooame no tame, shiai wa enki o yogi naku sareta. Because of the heavy rain, the match had to be postponed. भारी बारिश के कारण मैच को टालना पड़ा। ભારે વરસાદને કારણે મેચ મુલતવી રાખવી પડી.

余儀 = "other means"; 余儀なく = "with no other choice". 〜には及ばない ("no need to"), 〜を禁じ得ない ("can't suppress (an emotion)": 涙を禁じ得ない) and 〜べくもない ("there's no way to") don't express being forced.

【2】 Nあってのこと／Nあっての N

neutral register
ReadingN atte no koto
Meaning (EN)"(it is) only possible because of N / without N, there would be no ~"
Meaning (HI)N है तभी (यह संभव है) / N के बिना कुछ नहीं
Meaning (GU)N છે તો જ (આ શક્ય છે) / N વગર કંઈ નથી
Connection (接続)N + あっての + N  |  N + あってのことだ
RegisterNeutral to formal
Typical useStresses that N is the essential precondition: 健康あっての人生／お客様あっての商売.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + あってのことだ健康あってのことだkenkou atte no koto da
Noun(added, not in book) N1 + あっての + N2お客様あっての商売okyakusama atte no shoubai
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 仕事も趣味も、やはり健康あってのことだと思う。Shigoto mo shumi mo, yahari kenkou atte no koto da to omou. Work and hobbies — I think both are only possible if you have your health. काम हो या शौक़, मुझे लगता है सब कुछ सेहत है तभी संभव है। કામ હોય કે શોખ, મને લાગે છે બધું સ્વાસ્થ્ય હોય તો જ શક્ય છે.
(added, not in book) 今の私があるのは、家族の支えあってのことです。Ima no watashi ga aru no wa, kazoku no sasae atte no koto desu. Who I am today is only thanks to my family's support. आज मैं जो हूँ, वह परिवार के सहारे की वजह से ही हूँ। આજે હું જે છું, તે પરિવારના સાથને કારણે જ છું.

Only あって + の can modify こと. あれば／あったら／あると are conditionals and can't be followed by の.

【3】 〜とばかりに

neutral register
Reading~to bakari ni
Meaning (EN)"as if to say ~ / seizing the moment (eagerly)"
Meaning (HI)मानो कह रहा हो कि ... / मौक़ा देखकर फ़ौरन
Meaning (GU)જાણે કહેતો હોય કે ... / તક જોઈને તરત
Connection (接続)quote／plain form／imperative + とばかりに
RegisterWritten / narrative
Typical useDescribes someone else's attitude shown by action: 今だとばかりに・待ってましたとばかりに・出ていけとばかりに.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (quote)N + とばかりに今がチャンスとばかりにima ga chansu to bakari ni
Imperative(added, not in book) V-imperative + とばかりに帰れとばかりにkaere to bakari ni
Phrase(added, not in book) quote + とばかりに待ってましたとばかりにmattemashita to bakari ni
い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj + とばかりにうるさいとばかりにurusai to bakari ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 父の機嫌がよかったので、妹は、今がチャンスとばかりにおこづかいをねだった。Chichi no kigen ga yokatta node, imouto wa, ima ga chansu to bakari ni okozukai o nedatta. Dad was in a good mood, so my sister, as if thinking "now's my chance", begged him for pocket money. पापा का मूड अच्छा था, तो बहन ने "यही मौक़ा है" मानकर फ़ौरन जेबख़र्च माँग लिया। પપ્પાનો મૂડ સારો હતો, એટલે બહેને "આ જ તક છે" એમ માનીને તરત ખિસ્સાખર્ચ માગી લીધો.
(added, not in book) 店員は早く帰れとばかりに、片付けを始めた。Ten'in wa hayaku kaere to bakari ni, katazuke o hajimeta. The staff started tidying up as if to say "go home already". स्टाफ़ ने मानो "जल्दी जाओ" कहते हुए सफ़ाई शुरू कर दी। સ્ટાફે જાણે "જલદી જાઓ" કહેતા હોય તેમ સફાઈ શરૂ કરી.

とみえて is the speaker's inference from evidence, と思いきや an overturned expectation, and において a place/field marker. Only とばかりに turns "今がチャンス" into the sister's eager attitude.

【4】 〜（よ）うか〜まいか

neutral register
Reading~(y)ou ka ~mai ka
Meaning (EN)"whether to ~ or not" — hesitation before a decision
Meaning (HI)...करूँ या न करूँ
Meaning (GU)...કરું કે ન કરું
Connection (接続)V-volitional + か + V-dict／ない-stem + まい + か
RegisterSlightly formal
Typical useFollowed by 迷う・悩む・考える・決めかねる.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (Group 2)V-よう + か + V + まい + か告げようか告げまいかtsugeyou ka tsuge mai ka
Verb (Group 1)(added, not in book) V-おう + か + V-dict + まい + か行こうか行くまいかikou ka iku mai ka
Verb (する)(added, not in book) しようか + するまい／すまい + か参加しようかすまいかsanka shiyou ka su mai ka
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) 友人に真実を告げようか告げまいか悩んでいる。Yuujin ni shinjitsu o tsugeyou ka tsuge mai ka nayande iru. I'm agonising over whether or not to tell my friend the truth. मैं इस उलझन में हूँ कि दोस्त को सच बताऊँ या न बताऊँ। હું મૂંઝવણમાં છું કે મિત્રને સાચું કહું કે ન કહું.
(added, not in book) 留学しようかするまいか、まだ決められない。Ryuugaku shiyou ka suru mai ka, mada kimerarenai. I still can't decide whether to study abroad or not. विदेश पढ़ने जाऊँ या नहीं, अभी तय नहीं कर पा रहा। વિદેશ ભણવા જાઉં કે નહીં, હજુ નક્કી નથી કરી શકતો.

か〜か = a question (deciding). が〜が / と〜と = "regardless of whether" (第1回 point 12), which can't be followed by 悩んでいる. 〜ようとどうしようと = "whatever one does".

【5】 〜ものやら

neutral register
Reading~mono yara
Meaning (EN)"I wonder ~ (I really can't tell)" — with a question word
Meaning (HI)न जाने ... (समझ नहीं आता)
Meaning (GU)ખબર નહીં ... (સમજાતું નથી)
Connection (接続)疑問詞 … + plain form + ものやら
RegisterSlightly literary
Typical useClose to ことやら (第3回): どうしたらいいものやら／どこにあるものやら. Often followed by 悩む／わからない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb疑問詞 + V-たらいい + ものやらどれを選んだらいいものやらdore o erandara ii mono yara
Verb(added, not in book) 疑問詞 + V + ものやらどうなるものやらdou naru mono yara
い-adj(added, not in book) 疑問詞 + い-adj + ものやらいつまで続くものやらitsu made tsuzuku mono yara
Noun(added, not in book) not usual—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 同じような製品がありすぎて、どれを選んだらいいものやら悩んでしまう。Onaji you na seihin ga arisugite, dore o erandara ii mono yara nayande shimau. There are so many similar products that I agonise over which one I should choose. एक जैसे इतने सारे प्रोडक्ट हैं कि समझ नहीं आता कौन-सा चुनूँ। એક જેવી એટલી બધી પ્રોડક્ટ છે કે સમજાતું નથી કઈ પસંદ કરું.
(added, not in book) この雨はいつまで降り続くものやら。Kono ame wa itsu made furitsuzuku mono yara. I wonder how long this rain will go on. न जाने यह बारिश कब तक चलती रहेगी। ખબર નહીં આ વરસાદ ક્યાં સુધી ચાલુ રહેશે.

どれ + ものやら is the question + "I wonder" link. ですら ("even"), からには ("now that") and こそあれ ("though there may be ~, (no…)") don't express uncertainty.

硬 formal/written
ReadingN1 kara N2 ni itaru made
Meaning (EN)"(everything) from N1 right down/up to N2" — stresses the wide range
Meaning (HI)N1 से लेकर N2 तक (सब)
Meaning (GU)N1 થી લઈને N2 સુધી (બધું)
Connection (接続)N1 + から + N2 + に至るまで
Register硬 — written, announcements
Typical useN2 is usually the extreme end of the range. Often followed by あらゆる／すべて／みな.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN1から + N2 + に至るまで子どもから高齢者に至るまでkodomo kara koureisha ni itaru made
Noun(added, not in book) N + に至るまで細部に至るまでsaibu ni itaru made
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q6) 水泳は子どもから高齢者に至るまで、あらゆる年代の人が楽しめます。Suiei wa kodomo kara koureisha ni itaru made, arayuru nendai no hito ga tanoshimemasu. Swimming can be enjoyed by people of every age, from children right up to the elderly. तैराकी का आनंद बच्चों से लेकर बुज़ुर्गों तक, हर उम्र के लोग ले सकते हैं। તરવાનો આનંદ બાળકોથી લઈને વડીલો સુધી, દરેક ઉંમરના લોકો લઈ શકે છે.
(added, not in book) この店は家具から食器に至るまで、何でもそろっている。Kono mise wa kagu kara shokki ni itaru made, nan demo sorotte iru. This shop has everything, from furniture to tableware. इस दुकान में फ़र्नीचर से लेकर बर्तनों तक, सब कुछ मिलता है। આ દુકાનમાં ફર્નિચરથી લઈને વાસણો સુધી, બધું મળે છે.

から is printed, so the blank must close the range: に至るまで. に加え ("in addition to"), といえども ("even though") and にもまして ("even more than") break the から〜まで frame.

【7】 Nともなると／ともなれば

neutral register
ReadingN to mo naru to
Meaning (EN)"once (someone/something) is at the level of N, naturally ~"
Meaning (HI)जब N के स्तर पर हों, तो स्वाभाविक रूप से...
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે N ના સ્તરે હોય, ત્યારે સ્વાભાવિક રીતે...

RegisterNeutral to formal
Typical useN is a high status or special situation (首相・社長・プロ・年末). The main clause is what is naturally expected at that level.

NounN + ともなると一国の首相ともなるとikkoku no shushou to mo naru to

Verb(added, not in book) V-dict + ともなると結婚するともなるとkekkon suru to mo naru to
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q7) 一国の首相ともなると、発言には重大な責任がともなうものだ。Ikkoku no shushou to mo naru to, hatsugen ni wa juudai na sekinin ga tomonau mono da. When you are the prime minister of a country, your words naturally carry grave responsibility. जब कोई किसी देश का प्रधानमंत्री हो, तो उसकी बातों के साथ भारी ज़िम्मेदारी जुड़ी होती है। જ્યારે કોઈ દેશના વડાપ્રધાન હોય, ત્યારે તેમનાં નિવેદનો સાથે ભારે જવાબદારી જોડાયેલી હોય છે.
(added, not in book) 12月ともなると、街はクリスマスの飾りでいっぱいになる。Juunigatsu to mo naru to, machi wa kurisumasu no kazari de ippai ni naru. Once it's December, the town fills up with Christmas decorations. दिसंबर आते ही शहर क्रिसमस की सजावट से भर जाता है। ડિસેમ્બર આવતાં જ શહેર નાતાલની સજાવટથી ભરાઈ જાય છે.

ならでは needs の + N ("unique to"); はさておき sets N aside; じゃあるまいし ("it's not as if you're N") is casual and needs a rebuke after it. ものだ in the main clause matches ともなると's "naturally".

【8】 〜やいなや

硬 written/narrative
Reading~ya inaya
Meaning (EN)"as soon as / the instant ~"
Meaning (HI)जैसे ही ..., (तुरंत)
Meaning (GU)જેવું જ ..., (તરત)
Connection (接続)V-dict + やいなや／や
Register硬 — written narrative
Typical useReal past events; the second event follows immediately and is often unexpected. Not for intentions or commands.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (passive)V-dict + やいなや開始されるやいなやkaishi sareru ya inaya
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict + やドアが開くやdoa ga aku ya
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q8) コンサートのチケットは発売が開始されるやいなやまたたく間に売り切れた。Konsaato no chiketto wa hatsubai ga kaishi sareru ya inaya matataku ma ni urikireta. The concert tickets sold out in a flash the instant sales opened. टिकट की बिक्री शुरू होते ही कॉन्सर्ट के टिकट पलक झपकते बिक गए। ટિકિટનું વેચાણ શરૂ થતાં જ કૉન્સર્ટની ટિકિટો પલકારામાં વેચાઈ ગઈ.
(added, not in book) 彼は電話を切るやいなや、部屋を飛び出した。Kare wa denwa o kiru ya inaya, heya o tobidashita. The instant he hung up, he dashed out of the room. फ़ोन रखते ही वह कमरे से बाहर भागा। ફોન મૂકતાં જ તે રૂમમાંથી બહાર દોડ્યો.

とたん also means "the moment", but it needs V-た (開始されたとたん); と思うと／とみると also need V-た (開始されたかと思うと). With the dictionary form 開始される, only やいなや works.
