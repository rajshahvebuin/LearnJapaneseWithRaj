Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions and an answer key but no grammar explanations. Every explanation, formation table and example in §1 is added for this site; the only book sentences are the question sentences themselves (labelled "book"), and every other example is marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. Each item asks whether the verb should be plain, causative (〜させる), passive (〜される) or causative-passive (〜させられる). Find the subject and the person marked with に／を: a cause + 人に → causative; 人を + an intransitive verb → causative.

1 Grammar Points

The book has no explanation pages — the points below group the causative / passive choices this page drills, explained for this site. Book sentences are the completed question sentences; everything else is (added, not in book).

【1】 Nに言わせると（言わせれば）

neutral register
ReadingN ni iwaseru to (iwasereba)
Meaning (EN)"if you ask N / in N's opinion" — introduces a personal, often critical, view
Meaning (HI)N से पूछो तो / N की राय में
Meaning (GU)N ને પૂછો તો / N ના મતે
Connection (接続)N(person) + に言わせると／言わせれば／言わせたら
RegisterNeutral, conversational
Typical useCausative 言わせる = "let (me) say". The speaker contrasts their own frank view with what others claim (いろいろ言い訳しているが…). Plain 私に言うと is ungrammatical.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (person)N + に言わせると私に言わせるとwatashi ni iwaseru to
Noun (variant)(added, not in book) N + に言わせれば専門家に言わせればsenmonka ni iwasereba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q1) 彼はいろいろ言い訳しているが、私に言わせると、準備が足りなかっただけだ。Kare wa iroiro iiwake shite iru ga, watashi ni iwaseru to, junbi ga tarinakatta dake da. He's making all sorts of excuses, but if you ask me, he simply didn't prepare enough. वह तरह-तरह के बहाने बना रहा है, पर मेरी राय में, बस उसकी तैयारी कम थी। તે જાતજાતના બહાના બનાવે છે, પણ મારા મતે, બસ તેની તૈયારી ઓછી હતી.
(added, not in book) 母に言わせれば、最近の若者は我慢が足りないそうだ。Haha ni iwasereba, saikin no wakamono wa gaman ga tarinai sou da. According to my mother, young people these days lack patience. माँ के हिसाब से, आजकल के युवाओं में सब्र की कमी है। મમ્મીના મતે, આજકાલના યુવાનોમાં ધીરજની કમી છે.

The に marks the person allowed to speak (causative agent). That's why only the causative 言わせる works after 私に.

【2】 〜させていただく／〜させてみる

neutral register
Reading~sasete itadaku / ~sasete miru
Meaning (EN)させていただく: "(humbly) I will ~ (with your permission)".
Nに〜させてみる: "let / have N try ~".
Meaning (HI)させていただく: (विनम्रता से) मैं ~ करूँगा (आपकी अनुमति से)।
させてみる: N से ~ करवाकर देखना
Meaning (GU)させていただく: (નમ્રતાથી) હું ~ કરીશ (તમારી પરવાનગીથી).
させてみる: N પાસે ~ કરાવી જોવું
Connection (接続)V-causative-て + いただく  |  N(person) + に + V-causative-て + みる
Registerさせていただく: keigo (humble); させてみる: neutral
Typical useShop announcements: 本日は19時で営業を終了とさせていただきます. Trial: 彼にやらせてみれば (let him do it). Without the causative, 〜としていただきます would mean "please make it…" (asking the listener).
Word typeFormationExample
する verbN + と + させていただく終了とさせていただきますshuuryou to sasete itadakimasu
Verb (causative)Nに + V-させて + みる彼にやらせてみるkare ni yarasete miru
Verb (humble)(added, not in book) V-させて + いただく休ませていただきますyasumasete itadakimasu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q2) 本日は19時で営業を終了とさせていただきます。Honjitsu wa juuku-ji de eigyou o shuuryou to sasete itadakimasu. Today we will be closing at 7 p.m. आज हम शाम 7 बजे कारोबार बंद करेंगे। આજે અમે સાંજે 7 વાગ્યે વેપાર બંધ કરીશું.
(book: Q3) この仕事、彼にやらせてみればうまく行くかもしれませんよ。Kono shigoto, kare ni yarasete mireba umaku iku kamo shiremasen yo. If you let him try this job, it might go well. यह काम उससे करवाकर देखिए, शायद अच्छा हो जाए। આ કામ તેની પાસે કરાવી જુઓ, કદાચ સારું થઈ જાય.
(added, not in book) 体調が悪いので、今日は早退させていただきます。Taichou ga warui node, kyou wa soutai sasete itadakimasu. I'm not feeling well, so I'll leave early today, if I may. तबीयत ठीक नहीं है, इसलिए आज मैं जल्दी जाऊँगा (आपकी अनुमति से)। તબિયત સારી નથી, એટલે આજે હું વહેલો જઈશ (તમારી પરવાનગીથી).

Who does the action? In Q2 the shop does it and asks "permission" → causative + いただく. In Q3 he does the work and you allow it → 彼に + causative. 彼にやってみれば would mean "if (you) try doing it for him".

【3】 〜ずにはいられない／〜ずにはおかない

neutral register
Reading~zu ni wa irarenai / ~zu ni wa okanai
Meaning (EN)ずにはいられない: "can't help ~ing" (the subject's feeling).
ずにはおかない: "(something) will inevitably make people ~ / will surely ~" — an outside force.
Meaning (HI)ずにはいられない: ~ किए बिना रह नहीं सकता।
ずにはおかない: (कोई चीज़) ज़रूर ~ करवाकर रहेगी
Meaning (GU)ずにはいられない: ~ કર્યા વગર રહી શકાતું નથી.
ずにはおかない: (કોઈ વસ્તુ) જરૂર ~ કરાવીને રહેશે
Connection (接続)V-ない stem + ずにはいられない  |  V-causative-ない stem + ずにはおかない (人を感動させずにはおかない)
Registerいられない neutral; おかない 硬 (written)
Typical useWith いられない the subject is the person feeling (誰もが驚かずにはいられなかった). With おかない the subject is a thing that affects people, so the verb is often causative (この絵は人を感動させずにはおかない).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ずにはいられない驚かずにはいられなかったodorokazu ni wa irarenakatta
Verb (causative)V-させずにはおかない感動させずにはおかないkandou sasezu ni wa okanai
する verb(added, not in book) N + せずにはいられない心配せずにはいられないshinpai sezu ni wa irarenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q4) 彼の巧みな技に、誰もが驚かずにはいられなかった。Kare no takumi na waza ni, dare mo ga odorokazu ni wa irarenakatta. Everyone couldn't help being amazed at his skilful technique. उसकी कुशल कला देखकर, हर कोई हैरान हुए बिना न रह सका। તેની કુશળ કળા જોઈને, દરેક જણ આશ્ચર્ય પામ્યા વગર રહી ન શક્યા.
(book: Q5) この絵は人を感動させずにはおかない何かを持っている。Kono e wa hito o kandou sasezu ni wa okanai nani ka o motte iru. This painting has something that cannot fail to move people. इस चित्र में कुछ ऐसा है जो लोगों को भावुक किए बिना नहीं रहता। આ ચિત્રમાં કંઈક એવું છે જે લોકોને ભાવુક કર્યા વગર રહેતું નથી.
(added, not in book) その映画のラストシーンには、泣かずにはいられなかった。Sono eiga no rasuto shiin ni wa, nakazu ni wa irarenakatta. I couldn't help crying at the last scene of that film. उस फ़िल्म के आख़िरी दृश्य पर मैं रोए बिना न रह सका। તે ફિલ્મના છેલ્લા દૃશ્ય પર હું રડ્યા વગર રહી ન શક્યો.

Q4's distractor 驚かれず (passive) would mean "without being surprised (by someone)". Q5's 人を感動せず is wrong because 感動する is intransitive — to take 人を it must be causative (感動させる).

【4】 〜んがために

硬 formal/written
Reading~n ga tame ni
Meaning (EN)"in order to ~ (strong determination)" — classical, written
Meaning (HI)~ करने के लिए (दृढ़ संकल्प के साथ)
Meaning (GU)~ કરવા માટે (દૃઢ સંકલ્પ સાથે)
Connection (接続)V-ない stem + んがために（する→せん）
Register硬 — literary
Typical useん is the classical volitional. 合格させん = "to make (my son) pass". Because 息子を is the object, the verb must be causative (合格させる → 合格させん). Irregular: する → せん.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (causative)合格させ + んがために合格させんがためにgoukaku sasen ga tame ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-ない stem + んがために勝たんがためにkatan ga tame ni
する verb(added, not in book) N + せんがために成功せんがためにseikou sen ga tame ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q6) 息子を大学に合格させんがために、母親は必死だった。Musuko o daigaku ni goukaku sasen ga tame ni, hahaoya wa hisshi datta. The mother was desperate to get her son into university. बेटे को विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िला दिलाने के लिए, माँ जी-जान से जुटी थी। દીકરાને યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ અપાવવા માટે, માતા જીવ રેડીને લાગી હતી.
(added, not in book) 彼は勝たんがために、あらゆる手を使った。Kare wa katan ga tame ni, arayuru te o tsukatta. He used every trick in order to win. जीतने के लिए उसने हर तरीक़ा अपनाया। જીતવા માટે તેણે દરેક રીત અજમાવી.

Check the object: 息子を + 合格 → the mother makes him pass → causative. 合格せん would mean the mother herself passes.

【5】 Nを余儀なくさせる／Nを余儀なくされる

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o yogi naku saseru / N o yogi naku sareru
Meaning (EN)させる: "(a cause) forces (someone) to ~".
される: "(someone) is forced to ~".
Meaning (HI)させる: (कोई कारण) ~ करने पर मजबूर करता है।
される: (कोई) ~ करने पर मजबूर होता है
Meaning (GU)させる: (કોઈ કારણ) ~ કરવા મજબૂર કરે છે.
される: (કોઈ) ~ કરવા મજબૂર થાય છે
Connection (接続)Cause が + 人 に + N を余儀なくさせる  |  人 は + N を余儀なくされる
Register硬 — news, business
Typical useLook at the subject. 経営状況の悪化は (a cause) → させた. If the subject were 私達は → された.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (cause as subject)Cause は 人に Nを + 余儀なくさせる変更を余儀なくさせたhenkou o yogi naku saseta
Noun (person as subject)(added, not in book) 人は Nを + 余儀なくされる中止を余儀なくされたchuushi o yogi naku sareta
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q7) 経営状況の悪化は、私達に計画の変更を余儀なくさせた。Keiei joukyou no akka wa, watashitachi ni keikaku no henkou o yogi naku saseta. The worsening business situation forced us to change our plans. व्यापार की बिगड़ती हालत ने हमें योजना बदलने पर मजबूर कर दिया। વ્યાપારની બગડતી સ્થિતિએ અમને યોજના બદલવા મજબૂર કરી દીધા.
(added, not in book) 台風のため、イベントは中止を余儀なくされた。Taifuu no tame, ibento wa chuushi o yogi naku sareta. Because of the typhoon, the event was forced to be cancelled. तूफ़ान के कारण, कार्यक्रम रद्द करना पड़ा। વાવાઝોડાને કારણે, કાર્યક્રમ રદ કરવો પડ્યો.

In Q7 私達に marks the person made to act (causative agent) — the giveaway for させた.

【6】 〜ではすまされない

neutral register
Reading~ de wa sumasarenai
Meaning (EN)"(it) can't be dismissed as ~ / won't be allowed to end as ~"
Meaning (HI)~ कहकर टाला नहीं जा सकता / ~ से बात ख़त्म नहीं होगी
Meaning (GU)~ કહીને ટાળી ન શકાય / ~ થી વાત પતશે નહીં
Connection (接続)N(笑い事・冗談・知らなかった) + ではすまされない
RegisterNeutral, emphatic
Typical usePassive of すます: "it won't be let off as a joke". すませない (active) would mean "I won't let it end", which doesn't fit 笑い事では.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ではすまされない笑い事ではすまされないwaraigoto de wa sumasarenai
Clause(added, not in book) plain + ではすまされない知らなかったではすまされないshiranakatta de wa sumasarenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q8) そんな大事なことをだまっていたなんて、笑い事ではすまされないぞ。Sonna daiji na koto o damatte ita nante, waraigoto de wa sumasarenai zo. You kept something that important quiet? That's no laughing matter. इतनी ज़रूरी बात छिपाकर रखी? यह मज़ाक़ में टालने वाली बात नहीं है। આટલી જરૂરી વાત છુપાવી રાખી? આ મજાકમાં ટાળવાની વાત નથી.
(added, not in book) 社会人なら、「知らなかった」ではすまされない。Shakaijin nara, "shiranakatta" de wa sumasarenai. As a working adult, "I didn't know" won't cut it. नौकरीपेशा इंसान के लिए "मुझे पता नहीं था" कहना काफ़ी नहीं। નોકરિયાત માણસ માટે "મને ખબર નહોતી" કહેવું પૂરતું નથી.

Fixed: 〜ではすまされない (passive). Compare ⑧'s ずにすむ／ずにすませる — all three come from すむ.

【7】 〜ないこともない

neutral register
Reading~nai koto mo nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not that (I) won't ~ / (I) might ~ under conditions"
Meaning (HI)ऐसा नहीं कि ~ नहीं करूँगा / शर्त पर कर सकता हूँ
Meaning (GU)એવું નથી કે ~ નહીં કરું / શરતે કરી શકું
Connection (接続)V-ない + こともない（ものでもない）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA reluctant partial yes, usually with a condition (条件によっては). The book's distractor 教えさせられない (causative-passive) would mean "not be forced to tell", which is unrelated.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-てやらない + こともない教えてやらないこともないoshiete yaranai koto mo nai
Verb (plain)(added, not in book) V-ない + こともない行けないこともないikenai koto mo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q9) 条件によっては、情報を教えてやらないこともない。Jouken ni yotte wa, jouhou o oshiete yaranai koto mo nai. Depending on the conditions, I might be willing to give you the information. शर्तों के हिसाब से, मैं जानकारी दे भी सकता हूँ। શરતોના આધારે, હું માહિતી આપી પણ શકું.
(added, not in book) 少し遠いけど、歩いて行けないこともない。Sukoshi tooi kedo, aruite ikenai koto mo nai. It's a bit far, but it's not impossible to walk there. थोड़ा दूर है, पर पैदल जाना नामुमकिन नहीं। થોડું દૂર છે, પણ ચાલીને જવું અશક્ય નથી.

Same meaning as ④'s 〜ないものでもない. てやる adds a condescending "I'll do you the favour".

【8】 頼まれてくれないか

casual/spoken register
Readingtanomarete kurenai ka
Meaning (EN)"would you do me a favour?" — set phrase using the passive of 頼む
Meaning (HI)मेरा एक काम कर दोगे?
Meaning (GU)મારું એક કામ કરી આપશો?
Connection (接続)(ちょっと) + 頼まれて + くれない(か)／くれる?
RegisterCasual, spoken (to friends / juniors)
Typical useLiterally "will you be asked (by me)?" — a fixed, slightly old-fashioned way of asking a favour. 頼まされて (causative-passive, "be made to ask") makes no sense here.
Word typeFormationExample
Set phrase頼まれて + くれないか頼まれてくれないかtanomarete kurenai ka
Variant(added, not in book) 頼まれて + くれる?頼まれてくれる?tanomarete kureru?
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q10) 悪いんだけど、ちょっと頼まれてくれないか？Warui n da kedo, chotto tanomarete kurenai ka? Sorry to bother you, but could you do me a small favour? माफ़ करना, क्या मेरा एक छोटा-सा काम कर दोगे? માફ કરજો, શું મારું એક નાનું કામ કરી આપશો?
(added, not in book) ちょっと頼まれてくれる？この手紙、出してきてほしいんだ。Chotto tanomarete kureru? Kono tegami, dashite kite hoshii n da. Can you do me a favour? I'd like you to go and post this letter. एक काम कर दोगे? यह चिट्ठी डाक में डाल आओ। એક કામ કરી આપશો? આ પત્ર ટપાલમાં નાખી આવો.

This is idiomatic — learn it as a chunk.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

彼はいろいろ言い訳しているが、私に（ a 言うと ／ b 言わせると ）、準備が足りなかっただけだ。Kare wa iroiro iiwake shite iru ga, watashi ni (a iu to / b iwaseru to), junbi ga tarinakatta dake da.

Full sentence: 彼はいろいろ言い訳しているが、私に言わせると、準備が足りなかっただけだ。Kare wa iroiro iiwake shite iru ga, watashi ni iwaseru to, junbi ga tarinakatta dake da.
EN: He's making all sorts of excuses, but if you ask me, he simply didn't prepare enough.
HI: वह तरह-तरह के बहाने बना रहा है, पर मेरी राय में, बस उसकी तैयारी कम थी।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the a/b pairs the book itself set against each other — the contrasts N1 likes to test.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nに言わせるとN ni iwaseru to In N's opinion N + に + 言わせると Causative is fixed. Giving a frank view.
〜させていただく~sasete itadaku Humbly do V-causative + ていただく Speaker acts with "permission". Announcements.
〜ずにはいられない~zu ni wa irarenai Can't help ~ V-ずにはいられない Subject = person feeling. Emotions, urges.
〜ずにはおかない~zu ni wa okanai Will surely make ~ V-(させ)ずにはおかない Subject = thing affecting people. Art, events that move people.
〜させんがために~sasen ga tame ni In order to make ~ causative stem + んがために Causative when there is an object person. Determined purpose.
余儀なくさせる／されるyogi naku saseru / sareru Force / be forced Cause は 人に／人は Subject decides. News about changes.
〜ではすまされない~ de wa sumasarenai Can't be dismissed as N + ではすまされない Passive fixed. Serious matters.
頼まれてくれるtanomarete kureru Do me a favour 頼まれて + くれる Passive idiom. Casual requests.
Exam trap 1 — 人を + intransitive verb = causative: 感動する, 合格する, 驚く are intransitive. When a person is marked with を (人を感動…, 息子を合格…), the verb must be causative (感動させる, 合格させる).
Exam trap 2 — fixed voice in set phrases: 私に言わせると (causative), 〜ではすまされない (passive), 頼まれてくれないか (passive), 終了とさせていただきます (causative). Learn these as chunks.
