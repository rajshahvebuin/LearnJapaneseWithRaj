【1】 〜といったらない

neutral register
Reading~to ittara nai
Meaning (EN)"indescribably ~ / unbelievably ~" (extreme degree, often negative)
Meaning (HI)इतना ...कि कहा नहीं जा सकता
Meaning (GU)એટલું ...કે કહી ન શકાય
Connection (接続)い-adj + といったらない  |  な-adj／N + (と)いったらない
RegisterNeutral; casual variant 〜ったらない
Typical useFor emotions/evaluations of extreme degree: 悔しい, 恥ずかしい, わかりにくい, 美しい.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj + といったらないわかりにくいといったらないwakarinikui to ittara nai
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + といったらない退屈といったらないtaikutsu to ittara nai
Noun(added, not in book) N + といったらないその時の悔しさといったらないsono toki no kuyashisa to ittara nai
Verb(added, not in book) not natural — use い-adj / noun—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) この地図はいいかげんで、わかりにくいといったらない。Kono chizu wa iikagen de, wakarinikui to ittara nai. This map is so sloppy — it's unbelievably hard to understand. यह नक़्शा इतना लापरवाही से बना है — समझना बेहद मुश्किल है। આ નકશો એટલો બેદરકારીથી બનેલો છે — સમજવો અત્યંત મુશ્કેલ છે.
(added, not in book) 試合に負けたときの悔しさといったらなかった。Shiai ni maketa toki no kuyashisa to ittara nakatta. The frustration when we lost the match was indescribable. मैच हारने पर जो अफ़सोस हुआ, वह बयान नहीं किया जा सकता। મેચ હાર્યા ત્યારે જે અફસોસ થયો, તે વર્ણવી ન શકાય.

Literally "if I try to say it, (there are no words)". The blank comes before ない, so only といったら fits grammatically: といえども／といっても／といえば + ない are not phrases.

neutral register
Reading~to mo naku / ~to mo nashi ni
Meaning (EN)"without really meaning to ~ / idly ~ing"

Typical useWith perception verbs: 聞くともなく聞く, 見るともなく見る. Also どこからともなく ("from somewhere or other").

VerbV-dict + ともなく + V聞くともなく聞いてkiku to mo naku kiite

(book: 問題1 Q2) 喫茶店で隣の席の人の話を聞くともなく聞いていたら、知り合いの名前が出てきた。Kissaten de tonari no seki no hito no hanashi o kiku to mo naku kiite itara, shiriai no namae ga dete kita. While I was half-listening to the people at the next table in a café, the name of someone I know came up. कैफ़े में बग़ल की मेज़ वालों की बातें यूँ ही सुन रहा था, तभी एक परिचित का नाम आया। કૅફેમાં બાજુના ટેબલવાળાની વાતો એમ જ સાંભળતો હતો, ત્યાં એક ઓળખીતાનું નામ આવ્યું.
(added, not in book) どこからともなく、いい香りが漂ってきた。Doko kara to mo naku, ii kaori ga tadayotte kita. A lovely scent drifted in from somewhere or other. न जाने कहाँ से एक अच्छी ख़ुशबू आने लगी। ક્યાંકથી એક સરસ સુગંધ આવવા લાગી.

The repeated verb (聞く…聞いて) is the giveaway. ことなく ("without doing") would cancel the listening; とばかりに ("as if to say") and までもなく ("needless to") don't fit a repeated verb.

【3】 〜べくして

硬 formal/written
Reading~beku shite
Meaning (EN)"(happened) as it was bound to — inevitably" (〜べくして〜た)
Meaning (HI)जैसा होना ही था, वैसा ही हुआ — अनिवार्य रूप से
Meaning (GU)જેવું થવાનું જ હતું, તેવું જ થયું — અનિવાર્ય રીતે
Connection (接続)V-dict + べくして + same V-た (する → すべくして)
Register硬 — written
Typical use起こるべくして起こった (bound to happen), 勝つべくして勝った (deserved win), なるべくしてなった.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + べくして + V-た起こるべくして起こったokoru beku shite okotta
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict + べくして + V-た勝つべくして勝ったkatsu beku shite katta
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) こんな管理体制では事故が起きて当然だ。この事故は起こるべくして起こったのだ。Konna kanri taisei de wa jiko ga okite touzen da. Kono jiko wa okoru beku shite okotta no da. With management like this, an accident was only natural. This accident happened because it was bound to. ऐसी प्रबंधन व्यवस्था में दुर्घटना होना स्वाभाविक है। यह दुर्घटना तो होनी ही थी, इसलिए हुई। આવી વ્યવસ્થાપન પદ્ધતિમાં અકસ્માત થવો સ્વાભાવિક છે. આ અકસ્માત તો થવાનો જ હતો, એટલે થયો.
(added, not in book) あれだけ練習したチームだ。勝つべくして勝ったと言える。Are dake renshuu shita chiimu da. Katsu beku shite katta to ieru. That team practised so hard — you could say they won because they were meant to. उस टीम ने इतना अभ्यास किया था — कह सकते हैं कि उसे जीतना ही था। એ ટીમે આટલી પ્રેક્ટિસ કરી હતી — કહી શકાય કે તેને જીતવાનું જ હતું.

べく is the classical form of べき ("should"). The context 起きて当然だ signals inevitability. 起こればこそ ("precisely because it happens"), 起ころうとして ("about to happen") and 起こるまじとして (not a real form) don't express inevitability.

【4】 〜のやら

neutral register
Reading~no yara
Meaning (EN)"(I wonder) what/whether ~" — an embedded question the speaker can't answer
Meaning (HI)क्या ...है, (पता ही नहीं)
Meaning (GU)શું ...છે, (ખબર જ નથી)
Connection (接続)question word + plain form + のやら (+ わからない／さっぱり)
RegisterNeutral, conversational
Typical useFollowed by わからない／見当がつかない, often with irritation. Also 〜のやら〜のやら ("whether ~ or ~").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbQ-word + V-plain + のやら何が言いたいのやらnani ga iitai no yara
い-adjective(added, not in book) Q-word + い-adj + のやらどれがいいのやらdore ga ii no yara
な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) Q-word + な-adj／N + な + のやらどこが問題なのやらdoko ga mondai na no yara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) この文章は何が言いたいのやらさっぱりわからない。Kono bunshou wa nani ga iitai no yara sappari wakaranai. I have no idea at all what this text is trying to say. यह लेख आख़िर क्या कहना चाहता है, बिल्कुल समझ नहीं आता। આ લખાણ આખરે શું કહેવા માગે છે, બિલકુલ સમજાતું નથી.
(added, not in book) 息子はいつになったら帰ってくるのやら。Musuko wa itsu ni nattara kaette kuru no yara. I wonder when on earth my son will come home. न जाने बेटा कब घर लौटेगा। કોણ જાણે દીકરો ક્યારે ઘરે પાછો આવશે.

Works like 〜のか but adds a sigh of exasperation. ながら ("while") and なりに ("in its own way") can't form an embedded question; のなら ("if") would need a consequence.

【5】 〜ては〜ては

neutral register
Reading~te wa ~te wa
Meaning (EN)"(do) ~ and (undo), over and over" — repeated cycle of actions
Meaning (HI)...करते और ...करते, बार-बार
Meaning (GU)...કરતા અને ...કરતા, વારંવાર
Connection (接続)V1-ては + V2 + V1-ては + V2
RegisterNeutral, narrative
Typical useTwo alternating actions repeated many times: 書いては消し、書いては消し; 寄せては返す波.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV1-ては V2、V1-ては V2書いては消し、書いては消しkaite wa keshi, kaite wa keshi
Verb(added, not in book) V1-ては V2食べては寝るtabete wa neru
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 手紙を書いては消し書いては消ししているうちに、朝になってしまった。Tegami o kaite wa keshi kaite wa keshi shite iru uchi ni, asa ni natte shimatta. While I kept writing and erasing the letter over and over, morning came. चिट्ठी लिखते और मिटाते, लिखते और मिटाते सुबह हो गई। પત્ર લખતાં અને ભૂંસતાં, લખતાં અને ભૂંસતાં સવાર પડી ગઈ.
(added, not in book) 休みの日は食べては寝て、食べては寝ての繰り返しだった。Yasumi no hi wa tabete wa nete, tabete wa nete no kurikaeshi datta. On my day off it was just eat, sleep, eat, sleep. छुट्टी के दिन बस खाना और सोना, खाना और सोना चलता रहा। રજાના દિવસે બસ ખાવું અને સૂવું, ખાવું અને સૂવું ચાલતું રહ્યું.

The blank pairs come before 消し — so they must be te-forms + は. 書こうと／書こうと ("whether I write…"), 書きつつ ("while writing") and 書くまいと ("determined not to write") don't express a repeated undo-and-redo.

(book: 問題1 Q6) 当店のポイントサービスは、今年度限りで終了いたします。Touten no pointo saabisu wa, konnendo kagiri de shuuryou itashimasu. Our store's points service will end as of this fiscal year. हमारी दुकान की पॉइंट सेवा इस वित्त वर्ष के साथ ही समाप्त हो जाएगी। અમારી દુકાનની પૉઇન્ટ સેવા આ નાણાકીય વર્ષ સાથે જ બંધ થશે.
(added, not in book) あの選手は今シーズン限りで引退する。Ano senshu wa kon-shiizun kagiri de intai suru. That player will retire at the end of this season. वह खिलाड़ी इस सीज़न के बाद संन्यास लेगा। તે ખેલાડી આ સીઝન પછી નિવૃત્ત થશે.

(book: 問題1 Q7) 地元の人ですら見たことがないようなめずらしい鳥の撮影に成功した。Jimoto no hito de sura mita koto ga nai you na mezurashii tori no satsuei ni seikou shita. I succeeded in photographing a rare bird that even the locals have never seen. मैंने एक ऐसी दुर्लभ चिड़िया की तस्वीर लेने में सफलता पाई, जिसे स्थानीय लोगों ने भी कभी नहीं देखा। મેં એવા દુર્લભ પક્ષીનો ફોટો લેવામાં સફળતા મેળવી, જેને સ્થાનિક લોકોએ પણ ક્યારેય જોયું નથી.
(added, not in book) この問題は専門家ですら答えられない。Kono mondai wa senmonka de sura kotaerarenai. Even experts can't answer this question. इस सवाल का जवाब विशेषज्ञ भी नहीं दे सकते। આ પ્રશ્નનો જવાબ નિષ્ણાતો પણ આપી શકતા નથી.

The locals are the people most likely to have seen it — so "even they haven't" → ですら. でこそ ("precisely N") is positive emphasis; なりに ("in their own way"); ゆえに ("because of") would make no sense.

(book: 問題1 Q8) 比較するまでもなく、新型の機種は古いものよりもはるかに性能がいい。Hikaku suru made mo naku, shingata no kishu wa furui mono yori mo haruka ni seinou ga ii. There's no need even to compare — the new model performs far better than the old one. तुलना करने की ज़रूरत ही नहीं, नया मॉडल पुराने से कहीं बेहतर है। સરખામણી કરવાની જરૂર જ નથી, નવું મૉડેલ જૂના કરતાં ઘણું સારું છે.
(added, not in book) 言うまでもなく、健康が一番大切だ。Iu made mo naku, kenkou ga ichiban taisetsu da. Needless to say, health is most important. कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं, सेहत सबसे ज़रूरी है। કહેવાની જરૂર નથી, તંદુરસ્તી સૌથી મહત્ત્વની છે.

The result is obvious, so the action is unnecessary. するべく ("in order to") needs a purpose; したからには ("now that") needs a resolve; しようものなら ("if you dare") needs a bad consequence.

HI: जापानी व्यंजनों जितना सूक्ष्म और नाज़ुक कुछ नहीं है। ऐसा लगता है कि यह कच्चे माल को संसाधित करने पर नहीं, बल्कि — जैसे एक बढ़ई लकड़ी की प्राकृतिक बनावट का पूरा उपयोग करता है — मछली और सब्ज़ियों के मूल स्वाद को, मानो भीतर से बाहर निकालने पर निर्भर है। अगर सामग्री ताज़ी हो, तो सोया सॉस या नमक के साथ खाने से बेहतर कुछ नहीं। इस मायने में यह पश्चिमी या चीनी खाने से मूल रूप से अलग है, और यही कारण भी है कि कहा जाता है जापान में 'पाक-कला' है ही नहीं। सच है कि हमारे देश में विदेशों जैसी खाना पकाने की विधियाँ विकसित नहीं हुईं। क्योंकि हमें समुद्र और पहाड़ों की नेमतें ज़रूरत से ज़्यादा मिली थीं। उसी से उनके बिलकुल अलग स्वाद लेने का ढंग पैदा हुआ। हर ऋतु की ताज़ी चीज़ों का सम्मान, चाकू चलाने के तरीक़े से लेकर बर्तनों के चुनाव और परोसने के ढंग तक, प्राकृतिक स्वाद को उभारने पर बेहद ध्यान दिया जाता है।
GU: જાપાની વાનગીઓ જેટલું સૂક્ષ્મ અને નાજુક કંઈ નથી. એવું લાગે છે કે તે કાચા માલ પર પ્રક્રિયા કરવા પર નહીં, પણ — જેમ સુથાર લાકડાની કુદરતી રચનાનો પૂરો ઉપયોગ કરે છે — માછલી અને શાકભાજીના મૂળ સ્વાદને, જાણે અંદરથી બહાર કાઢવા પર આધાર રાખે છે. જો સામગ્રી તાજી હોય, તો સોયા સૉસ કે મીઠા સાથે ખાવાથી વધુ સારું કંઈ નથી. એ દૃષ્ટિએ તે પશ્ચિમી કે ચીની રસોઈથી મૂળભૂત રીતે જુદી છે, અને એ જ કારણ પણ છે કે કહેવાય છે જાપાનમાં 'રસોઈકળા' છે જ નહીં. એ સાચું કે આપણા દેશમાં વિદેશો જેવી રાંધવાની પદ્ધતિઓ વિકસી નહીં. કારણ કે આપણને સમુદ્ર અને પર્વતોની બક્ષિસો જરૂર કરતાં વધારે મળી હતી. તેમાંથી તેમનાથી સાવ જુદી સ્વાદ માણવાની રીત જન્મી. દરેક ઋતુની તાજી વસ્તુઓનો આદર, છરી ચલાવવાની રીતથી લઈને વાસણોની પસંદગી અને પીરસવાની રીત સુધી, કુદરતી સ્વાદને ઉજાગર કરવા પર ખૂબ ધ્યાન અપાય છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the distractors the book itself set against each correct answer — the pairs N1 likes to test against each other.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜といったらない~to ittara nai Indescribably ~ い-adj／N + といったらない Extreme degree; fixed with ない. Strong emotion or evaluation.
〜といっても／〜といえども~to itte mo / ~to iedomo Although (I say) ~ plain + といっても Concessive connectors — need a following clause. Downplaying: 料理といっても簡単なものだ.
〜ともなく~to mo naku Without meaning to V-dict + ともなく + V Idle, unintentional action; repeated verb. 聞くともなく聞く.
〜ことなく~koto naku Without doing V-dict + ことなく The action does not happen at all. 休むことなく働く.
〜べくして~beku shite Bound to (happen) V-dict + べくして + V-た Inevitability, same verb repeated. Looking back at a natural outcome.
〜ては〜ては／〜つつ~te wa ~te wa / ~tsutsu Repeated cycle / while V-ては V2 ／ V-ます-stem + つつ ては = repetition of a sequence; つつ = simultaneous. 書いては消し vs 歩きつつ考える.
N限りで／Nをもってkagiri de / o motte As of N (end) N(time) + 限りで Cut-off point for ending. Notices about stopping a service.
Nですら／Nでこそde sura / de koso Even N / precisely N N + ですら／でこそ すら = extreme case (usually negative); こそ = positive emphasis. Even experts can't…
〜までもなく／〜ようものなら~made mo naku / ~you mono nara No need to / if one dared V-dict + までもなく ／ V-vol + ものなら Obvious result vs threatened bad result. Obvious comparison vs warning.
Nときたら／Nとあればto kitara / to areba Speaking of N (complaint) / if it's for N N + ときたら／とあれば ときたら = critical topic; とあれば = condition for doing something. Complaining vs agreeing to act.
Nなりなんなり／Nやらなんやらnari nan nari / yara nan yara N or whatever / N and so on N + なりなんなり なりなんなり + suggestion/request; やらなんやら lists (often troubles). Offering choices vs listing chaos.
〜ずにはおかない／〜ずにはいられない~zu ni wa okanai / ~zu ni wa irarenai Cannot fail to (affect) / can't help (myself) V-ない-stem + ずには… おかない: thing affects others; いられない: speaker's urge. A work moving its audience.
〜に越したことはない／〜にあたらない~ni koshita koto wa nai / ~ni ataranai Best to ~ / no need to ~ V-dict + に… Recommendation vs "not worth (being surprised)". Advice vs downplaying.
Nに至るまで／Nをはじめ(として)ni itaru made / o hajime (to shite) Right down to N / starting with N Nから Nに至るまで ／ Nをはじめ 至るまで marks the far end of a range; はじめ marks the leading example. Exhaustive lists vs representative example.
Exam trap 1 — read the words after the blank: Q1 has ない after the blank (→ といったら＋ない), Q2 repeats the verb (→ 聞くともなく聞く), Q3 repeats the verb in the past (→ 起こるべくして起こった), Q10 ends in 〜てください (→ なりなんなり). In 問題1, the ending often decides the answer before you even look at meaning.
Exam trap 2 — から … に至るまで: when a passage has 〜から earlier in the sentence and lists items, the blank after the last item is usually に至るまで. をはじめとして goes after the first item, never the last.
