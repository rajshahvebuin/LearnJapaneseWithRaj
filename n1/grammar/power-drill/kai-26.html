【1】 〜ためしがない

neutral register
Reading~tameshi ga nai
Meaning (EN)"has never (once) ~" — said with criticism or disappointment
Meaning (HI)आज तक एक बार भी ...नहीं हुआ
Meaning (GU)આજ સુધી એક વાર પણ ...થયું નથી
Connection (接続)V-た + ためしがない
RegisterNeutral, slightly critical
Typical useComplaining that something has never happened: 約束を守ったためしがない, 当たったためしがない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + ためしがない当たったためしがないatatta tameshi ga nai
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + ためしがない時間通りに来たためしがないjikan doori ni kita tameshi ga nai
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — verb past form only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) ときどき宝くじを買っているが、残念ながら当たったためしがない。Tokidoki takarakuji o katte iru ga, zannen nagara atatta tameshi ga nai. I buy lottery tickets now and then, but sadly I've never once won. मैं कभी-कभी लॉटरी टिकट ख़रीदता हूँ, लेकिन अफ़सोस, आज तक एक बार भी नहीं लगी। હું ક્યારેક લોટરીની ટિકિટ ખરીદું છું, પણ અફસોસ, આજ સુધી એક વાર પણ લાગી નથી.
(added, not in book) 兄は時間通りに来たためしがない。Ani wa jikan doori ni kita tameshi ga nai. My brother has never once arrived on time. मेरा बड़ा भाई आज तक कभी समय पर नहीं आया। મારો મોટો ભાઈ આજ સુધી ક્યારેય સમયસર આવ્યો નથી.

ためし (例し) = "a precedent". It always follows the た-form and looks back over past experience. はずがない ("can't be") is a logical judgement about one case; までもない ("no need to") and に至らない ("doesn't reach the point of") don't follow 当たった.

【2】 〜(よ)うにも〜ない

neutral register
Reading~(y)ou ni mo ~nai
Meaning (EN)"even if one wants/tries to ~, one can't (because of a circumstance)"
Meaning (HI)...करना चाहें तो भी (किसी कारण से) नहीं कर सकते
Meaning (GU)...કરવા ઇચ્છીએ તો પણ (કોઈ કારણસર) કરી શકતા નથી
Connection (接続)V-volitional (よう／おう) + にも + (same V-potential-negative ／ obstacle clause)
RegisterNeutral, slightly written
Typical useWanting to act but being blocked: 帰ろうにも帰れない, 作ろうにも材料が手に入らない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (Group 1)V-おう + にも作ろうにもtsukurou ni mo
Verb (Group 2)(added, not in book) V-よう + にも寝ようにも寝られないneyou ni mo nerarenai
Verb (する)(added, not in book) しよう + にも連絡しようにもrenraku shiyou ni mo
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 国の料理を作ろうにも、日本ではなかなか材料が手に入らない。Kuni no ryouri o tsukurou ni mo, Nihon de wa nakanaka zairyou ga te ni hairanai. Even when I want to cook food from my country, I can hardly get the ingredients in Japan. अपने देश का खाना बनाना चाहूँ भी, तो जापान में सामग्री मुश्किल से ही मिलती है। મારા દેશનું ભોજન બનાવવા ઇચ્છું તો પણ, જાપાનમાં સામગ્રી ભાગ્યે જ મળે છે.
(added, not in book) 電話番号がわからないので、連絡しようにもできない。Denwa bangou ga wakaranai node, renraku shiyou ni mo dekinai. I don't know the phone number, so even if I want to contact them, I can't. फ़ोन नंबर पता नहीं है, इसलिए संपर्क करना चाहूँ भी तो नहीं कर सकता। ફોન નંબર ખબર નથી, એટલે સંપર્ક કરવા ઇચ્છું તો પણ કરી શકતો નથી.

The main clause must be an impossibility (手に入らない). 作るべく and 作らんがため state a purpose ("in order to make"), which would need an effort in the main clause; 作ってこそ ("only by making") states a condition for real value.

【3】 NであろうとNであろうと

硬 written
ReadingN de arou to N de arou to
Meaning (EN)"whether it's A or B (it makes no difference)"
Meaning (HI)चाहे A हो या B (कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं)
Meaning (GU)ભલે A હોય કે B (કોઈ ફરક નથી)
Connection (接続)N／な-adj + であろうと + N／な-adj + であろうと
Register硬 — formal version of 〜でも〜でも
Typical useTwo examples of a range, followed by "the situation is the same": 雨であろうと雪であろうと.

い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-かろうと い-adj-かろうと暑かろうと寒かろうとatsukarou to samukarou to
Verb(added, not in book) V-volitional と V-volitional と雨が降ろうとやりが降ろうとame ga furou to yari ga furou to
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) コンビニは、日曜であろうと年末年始であろうと買い物ができて便利だ。Konbini wa, nichiyou de arou to nenmatsu nenshi de arou to kaimono ga dekite benri da. Convenience stores are handy: you can shop there whether it's Sunday or the New Year holidays. कन्वीनियंस स्टोर सुविधाजनक हैं — चाहे रविवार हो या नए साल की छुट्टियाँ, ख़रीदारी हो जाती है। કન્વીનિયન્સ સ્ટોર સગવડભર્યા છે — ભલે રવિવાર હોય કે નવા વર્ષની રજાઓ, ખરીદી થઈ જાય છે.
(added, not in book) 社長であろうと新入社員であろうと、規則は守らなければならない。Shachou de arou to shinnyuu shain de arou to, kisoku wa mamoranakereba naranai. Whether you're the president or a new employee, you must follow the rules. चाहे अध्यक्ष हो या नया कर्मचारी, नियमों का पालन करना ही होगा। ભલે પ્રમુખ હોય કે નવો કર્મચારી, નિયમોનું પાલન કરવું જ પડશે.

The main clause states something that holds regardless (買い物ができる). だの／だの and やら／やら list things (often complaints or chaos); といい／といい ("both A and B") evaluates a single topic. None means "regardless of".

硬 written
ReadingN ni N o kasane(te)
Meaning (EN)"~ upon ~ / ~ after ~" — repeating an action over and over
Meaning (HI)बार-बार ...करके / ...पर ...करके
Meaning (GU)વારંવાર ...કરીને / ...પર ...કરીને
Connection (接続)same N + に + same N + を重ね(て)
Register硬 — written
Typical useFixed with effort nouns: 努力に努力を重ね, 改良に改良を重ね, 苦労に苦労を重ね; the result is usually an achievement.

NounN に N を重ね改良に改良を重ねkairyou ni kairyou o kasane
Noun(added, not in book) N に N を重ねて努力に努力を重ねてdoryoku ni doryoku o kasanete
Verb / Adj(added, not in book) not used — noun only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) 試作品は改良に改良を重ね、ついに実用化にこぎつけた。Shisakuhin wa kairyou ni kairyou o kasane, tsui ni jitsuyouka ni kogitsuketa. After improvement upon improvement, the prototype finally made it to practical use. प्रोटोटाइप में बार-बार सुधार करके आख़िरकार उसे व्यावहारिक उपयोग तक पहुँचा दिया गया। પ્રોટોટાઇપમાં વારંવાર સુધારા કરીને આખરે તેને વ્યવહારુ ઉપયોગ સુધી પહોંચાડ્યો.
(added, not in book) 彼は努力に努力を重ねて、医者になった。Kare wa doryoku ni doryoku o kasanete, isha ni natta. Through effort upon effort, he became a doctor. लगातार मेहनत पर मेहनत करके वह डॉक्टर बना। સતત મહેનત પર મહેનત કરીને તે ડૉક્ટર બન્યો.

The doubled noun (改良に改良) is the clue. を兼ね ("doubling as"), にたえ ("worth ~ing") and に先駆け ("ahead of") never take a repeated noun.

【5】 V-るには V-た(が)

neutral register
ReadingV-ru ni wa V-ta (ga)
Meaning (EN)"I did ~ (all right), but… (not fully / not well)"
Meaning (HI)...किया तो सही, लेकिन...
Meaning (GU)...કર્યું તો ખરું, પણ...
Connection (接続)V-dict + には + same V-た + が／けれど
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAdmits the action happened but the result was unsatisfying: 行くには行ったが, 読むには読んだが.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict には V-た が読むには読んだがyomu ni wa yonda ga

い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj には い-adj が安いには安いがyasui ni wa yasui ga
Noun / な-adj(added, not in book) rarely used in this pattern—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 薦められた本を読むには読んだが、よく理解できなかった。Susumerareta hon o yomu ni wa yonda ga, yoku rikai dekinakatta. I did read the book that was recommended to me, but I couldn't really understand it. जो किताब सुझाई गई थी, वह पढ़ी तो सही, लेकिन ठीक से समझ नहीं आई। જે પુસ્તક સૂચવાયું હતું તે વાંચ્યું તો ખરું, પણ બરાબર સમજાયું નહીં.
(added, not in book) パーティーに行くには行ったが、すぐ帰ってきた。Paatii ni iku ni wa itta ga, sugu kaette kita. I did go to the party, but I came straight back. पार्टी में गया तो सही, लेकिन तुरंत लौट आया। પાર્ટીમાં ગયો તો ખરો, પણ તરત પાછો આવી ગયો.

The same verb is repeated (読む…読んだ) with には in between. 読むこそ is ungrammatical (こそ needs 読んでこそ); 読むなり ("as soon as") needs a different second action; 読むまで読んだ is not a pattern.

(added, not in book) ベテランの彼としたことが、こんな初歩的なミスをするとは。Beteran no kare to shita koto ga, konna shohoteki na misu o suru to wa. Who'd have thought that he, a veteran, would make such a basic mistake. उसके जैसे अनुभवी व्यक्ति से इतनी बुनियादी ग़लती, कौन सोच सकता था। તેના જેવા અનુભવી માણસથી આટલી પાયાની ભૂલ, કોણ ધારી શકે.

(book: 問題1 Q7) そんなに言うなら、信じるにたる証拠を見せてくれ。Sonna ni iu nara, shinjiru ni taru shouko o misete kure. If you insist that much, show me evidence worth believing. अगर तुम इतना ज़ोर देकर कह रहे हो, तो विश्वास करने लायक़ सबूत दिखाओ। જો તું આટલા ભારપૂર્વક કહે છે, તો વિશ્વાસ કરવા લાયક પુરાવો બતાવ.
(added, not in book) 彼は信頼するにたる人物だ。Kare wa shinrai suru ni taru jinbutsu da. He is a person worthy of trust. वह भरोसा करने लायक़ इंसान है। તે ભરોસો કરવા લાયક માણસ છે.

にわたる ("spanning") follows a period or range; にたえない ("can't bear to" / "extremely") and にやまない ("never stop ~ing", 願ってやまない — needs て-form) give the wrong meaning. Only にたる makes 信じる + 証拠 mean "believable evidence".

Why: "It got hot — but not hot enough for the AC" limits the previous sentence → といって (point 13). それゆえ would make "not using the AC" a result of the heat; それどころか needs an even stronger statement; というか rephrases rather than limits.

4ものもないmono mo nai(not a set phrase)(स्थिर वाक्यांश नहीं)(સ્થિર શબ્દસમૂહ નથી)

Why: "Being late once in a while" + どうという → the fixed dismissal どうということもない (point 14), matching それでいいんだ. ことである／ものである would make it advice or a general truth; ものもない doesn't follow どうという.

Exam trap 1 — volitional + にも: 作ろうにも looks like a purpose phrase, but it is the opposite — the action is wanted but blocked. If the main clause says "can't / isn't available", choose 〜(よ)うにも; if it describes effort, choose べく／んがため.
Exam trap 2 — に＋た- verbs: にたる (worthy), にたえる／にたえない (bear / can't bear, or "deeply"), にわたる (span). They look alike; decide by the noun that follows (証拠 → worth believing → にたる).
