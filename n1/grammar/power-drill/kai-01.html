【1】 〜たところで

neutral register
Reading~ta tokoro de
Meaning (EN)"even if (you) do ~, it's no use / it won't make a difference"
Meaning (HI)चाहे ...कर भी लो, (कोई फ़ायदा नहीं / कुछ नहीं बदलेगा)
Meaning (GU)ભલે ...કરો તો પણ, (કોઈ ફાયદો નથી / કંઈ બદલાશે નહીં)
Connection (接続)V-た + ところで
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing
Typical useOften with どんなに／いくら／たとえ／何回. The main clause is a futile, negative or "only small" result (〜ない／無駄だ／〜だけだ). Never followed by a request, intention or a positive result.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + ところで片づけたところでkatazuketa tokoro de
い-adjective(added, not in book) not a natural fit — use 〜くても instead—
な-adjective(added, not in book) not a natural fit—
Noun(added, not in book) N + だったところで (rare)専門家だったところでsenmonka datta tokoro de
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) どんなに片づけたところで、すぐにまた子どもたちが散らかしてしまう。Donna ni katazuketa tokoro de, sugu ni mata kodomotachi ga chirakashite shimau. No matter how much I tidy up, the kids just mess it up again right away. चाहे कितना भी साफ़ कर लूँ, बच्चे तुरंत फिर से बिखेर देते हैं। ગમે તેટલું સાફ કરું, બાળકો તરત ફરીથી વેરવિખેર કરી નાખે છે.
(added, not in book) 今から急いだところで、終電には間に合わないだろう。Ima kara isoida tokoro de, shuuden ni wa ma ni awanai darou. Even if we hurry now, we probably won't make the last train. अब जल्दी करें तो भी, आख़िरी ट्रेन शायद नहीं पकड़ पाएँगे। હવે ઉતાવળ કરીએ તો પણ, છેલ્લી ટ્રેન કદાચ નહીં પકડી શકીએ.

Think "〜ても + it's pointless". The speaker has already decided the action won't help. Don't confuse it with 〜たところ ("when I did ~, (I found)…"), which reports a discovery, or with ところが ("however"), a plain contrast conjunction.

【2】 〜たばかりに

neutral register
Reading~ta bakari ni
Meaning (EN)"simply because ~ (a bad result followed)" — regret over a small cause with a big negative effect
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...की वजह से (बुरा नतीजा हुआ) — पछतावे के साथ
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...ને કારણે જ (ખરાબ પરિણામ આવ્યું) — અફસોસ સાથે
Connection (接続)V-た／V-ない + ばかりに  |  い-adj + ばかりに  |  な-adj-な／である + ばかりに  |  N-である + ばかりに
RegisterNeutral, slightly literary
Typical useResult clause is negative and usually past (〜てしまった). Also the fixed 〜たいばかりに ("just because one wanted to ~, (went to great lengths)").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + ばかりに遅れたばかりにokureta bakari ni
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + ばかりに背が低いばかりにse ga hikui bakari ni
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-な + ばかりに正直なばかりにshoujiki na bakari ni
Noun(added, not in book) N + である + ばかりに外国人であるばかりにgaikokujin de aru bakari ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 私が集合時間に遅れたばかりに、みんなが新幹線に乗り遅れてしまった。Watashi ga shuugou jikan ni okureta bakari ni, minna ga shinkansen ni noriokurete shimatta. Just because I was late for the meeting time, everyone missed the shinkansen. सिर्फ़ मेरे मिलने के समय पर देर से आने की वजह से, सबकी शिंकानसेन छूट गई। ફક્ત હું મળવાના સમયે મોડો પડ્યો એટલે, બધાની શિંકાનસેન છૂટી ગઈ.
(added, not in book) 一言多かったばかりに、彼女を怒らせてしまった。Hitokoto ookatta bakari ni, kanojo o okorasete shimatta. Just because I said one word too many, I made her angry. बस एक बात ज़्यादा बोल देने की वजह से, मैंने उसे नाराज़ कर दिया। બસ એક વાત વધારે બોલી જવાને કારણે, મેં તેને ગુસ્સે કરી દીધી.

The particle に is essential: ばかりに = "only because (→ bad result)". ばかりで means "only ~ and nothing else" (口ばかりで何もしない — all talk and no action), and plain 〜たばかり means "just did". N1 loves to offer all four ばかり forms together, as Q2 does.

【3】 Nにあるまじき

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni aru majiki
Meaning (EN)"(behaviour) unbecoming of / unacceptable for a N" — strong moral criticism
Meaning (HI)(किसी पद/भूमिका वाले के लिए) अशोभनीय / जो उसे बिल्कुल नहीं करना चाहिए
Meaning (GU)(કોઈ પદ/ભૂમિકા ધરાવનાર માટે) અશોભનીય / જે તેણે બિલકુલ ન કરવું જોઈએ
Connection (接続)N(role/position) + に／として + あるまじき + N(行為・発言・態度)
Register硬 — news, editorials, formal criticism
Typical useAlways attributive: あるまじき must be followed by a noun (行為, 発言, 態度, 失態). The first noun is a person's role: 教師, 政治家, 医者, 社会人.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にあるまじき + N政治家にあるまじき発言seijika ni aru majiki hatsugen
Noun (variant)(added, not in book) N + としてあるまじき + N教師としてあるまじき行為kyoushi to shite aru majiki koui
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — the pattern only attaches to a noun of role—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) その大臣は政治家にあるまじき発言をしたため、辞任することになった。Sono daijin wa seijika ni aru majiki hatsugen o shita tame, jinin suru koto ni natta. That minister made remarks unbecoming of a politician, so he ended up resigning. उस मंत्री ने एक राजनेता के लिए अशोभनीय बयान दिया, इसलिए उन्हें इस्तीफ़ा देना पड़ा। તે મંત્રીએ રાજનેતા માટે અશોભનીય નિવેદન આપ્યું, એટલે તેમને રાજીનામું આપવું પડ્યું.
(added, not in book) 患者の秘密を漏らすのは、医者にあるまじき行為だ。Kanja no himitsu o morasu no wa, isha ni aru majiki koui da. Leaking a patient's secrets is conduct unbecoming of a doctor. मरीज़ की गोपनीय बात बाहर बताना डॉक्टर के लिए अशोभनीय काम है। દર્દીની ખાનગી વાત બહાર કહેવી એ ડૉક્ટર માટે અશોભનીય કૃત્ય છે.

まじき is the classical attributive of まじ (≈ modern まい / べきではない). Remember the shape "role + にあるまじき + act-noun". あるまいし ("it's not as if ~") and ありながら ("despite being ~") look similar but can't modify 発言 directly.

【4】 〜にたえない

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni taenai
Meaning (EN)(1) "too bad/unpleasant to bear ~ing" (聞くにたえない, 見るにたえない); (2) with emotion nouns: "deeply / cannot express enough" (感謝にたえない)
Meaning (HI)(1) ...करना सहन नहीं होता (इतना बुरा है); (2) अत्यंत (आभारी आदि)
Meaning (GU)(1) ...કરવું સહન નથી થતું (એટલું ખરાબ છે); (2) અત્યંત (આભારી વગેરે)
Connection (接続)V-dict (見る・聞く・読む) + にたえない  |  N(感謝・同情・遺憾) + にたえない
Register硬 — written, formal speech
Typical useMeaning (1) uses a very small set of perception verbs. Kanji: 堪えない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + にたえない聞くにたえないkiku ni taenai
Noun(added, not in book) N + にたえない感謝にたえないkansha ni taenai
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) 彼女は人気のアイドルだが、歌は下手すぎて聞くにたえない。Kanojo wa ninki no aidoru da ga, uta wa heta sugite kiku ni taenai. She's a popular idol, but her singing is so bad it's unbearable to listen to. वह एक लोकप्रिय आइडल है, लेकिन उसका गाना इतना ख़राब है कि सुना नहीं जाता। તે લોકપ્રિય આઇડલ છે, પણ તેનું ગાયન એટલું ખરાબ છે કે સાંભળી શકાતું નથી.
(added, not in book) 皆様のご支援には感謝にたえません。Minasama no go-shien ni wa kansha ni taemasen. I cannot thank you all enough for your support. आप सभी के सहयोग के लिए मैं बेहद आभारी हूँ। આપ સૌના સહયોગ માટે હું અત્યંત આભારી છું.

Don't mix it up with 〜にたえる ("worth ~ing": 鑑賞にたえる作品) or 〜に（は）たえられない (plain "can't endure"). In Q4 the trap options are the "can't help / must" family (〜ずにはいられない, 〜ずにはすまない), which mean the opposite of "unbearable".

【5】 これはという＋N

neutral register
Readingkore wa to iu + N
Meaning (EN)"a N that makes you think 'this is it!'" — one that is truly satisfactory / outstanding
Meaning (HI)ऐसा ...जिसे देखकर लगे "बस, यही है!" — सचमुच संतोषजनक
Meaning (GU)એવું ...જેને જોઈને લાગે "બસ, આ જ!" — ખરેખર સંતોષકારક
Connection (接続)これは + という + N（もの・人・案）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useMostly in negative or searching contexts: これはというものが見つからない／これはと思う人に声をかける. Variant: これはと思う＋N.
Word typeFormationExample
Nounこれはという + Nこれはというものkore wa to iu mono
Variant(added, not in book) これはと思う + Nこれはと思う人kore wa to omou hito
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) fixed phrase — does not attach to other word types—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 新しい家に飾る絵を探しているが、なかなかこれはというものが見つからない。Atarashii ie ni kazaru e o sagashite iru ga, nakanaka kore wa to iu mono ga mitsukaranai. I'm looking for a painting to hang in the new house, but I just can't find one that feels "just right". नए घर में लगाने के लिए पेंटिंग ढूँढ रहा हूँ, लेकिन ऐसी कोई नहीं मिल रही जिसे देखकर लगे "बस, यही!"। નવા ઘરમાં લગાવવા માટે ચિત્ર શોધું છું, પણ એવું કોઈ મળતું નથી જેને જોઈને લાગે "બસ, આ જ!".
(added, not in book) これはと思う人材がいたら、ぜひ紹介してください。Kore wa to omou jinzai ga itara, zehi shoukai shite kudasai. If you find someone you think is really outstanding, please introduce them to me. अगर आपको कोई सचमुच बेहतरीन व्यक्ति दिखे, तो ज़रूर मुझसे मिलवाइए। જો તમને કોઈ ખરેખર ઉત્તમ વ્યક્તિ દેખાય, તો જરૂર મારી ઓળખાણ કરાવજો.

The は is the key: "これは (すばらしい)!" is an exclamation you would make on finding the right thing, then という turns it into a description. これだけ ("only this"), これさえ ("even this") and これか don't form this set phrase.

【6】 〜まい（と）

硬 written / firm resolve
Reading~mai (to)
Meaning (EN)"will never / determined not to ~" (speaker's own negative resolve); also "probably not ~" (negative conjecture)
Meaning (HI)कभी नहीं ...करूँगा (पक्का इरादा); या — शायद ...नहीं होगा
Meaning (GU)ક્યારેય ...નહીં કરું (પાકો નિર્ણય); અથવા — કદાચ ...નહીં થાય
Connection (接続)Group 1 V-dict + まい（吸うまい） |  Group 2 V-dict／ます-stem + まい（食べるまい／食べまい） |  する → するまい／すまい  |  来る → くるまい／こまい
RegisterWritten / formal; sounds stiff in casual speech
Typical use〜まいと思う／〜まいと決心する／〜まいと誓う; often with 二度と.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + まい吸うまいと誓うsuu mai to chikau
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-く + あるまい (conjecture only)難しくあるまいmuzukashiku aru mai
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + ではあるまい (conjecture only)簡単ではあるまいkantan de wa aru mai

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q6) もう二度とたばこは吸うまいと誓った。Mou nido to tabako wa suu mai to chikatta. I vowed never to smoke a cigarette again. मैंने क़सम खाई कि अब दोबारा कभी सिगरेट नहीं पिऊँगा। મેં સોગંદ ખાધા કે હવે ફરી ક્યારેય સિગારેટ નહીં પીઉં.
(added, not in book) 同じ失敗は繰り返すまいと、メモを取るようにしている。Onaji shippai wa kurikaesu mai to, memo o toru you ni shite iru. Determined not to repeat the same mistake, I make a point of taking notes. वही ग़लती दोबारा न करने के इरादे से, मैं नोट्स लेने की आदत रखता हूँ। એ જ ભૂલ ફરી ન કરવાના નિર્ધાર સાથે, હું નોંધ લેવાની આદત રાખું છું.

べからず (Q6 option 4) is also a "don't" form, but it is a prohibition aimed at other people (signs: 芝生に入るべからず), never your own vow. ことなしに ("without ~") and までもなく ("needless to ~") don't express resolve at all.

【7】 〜とあって

硬 written / news
Reading~to atte
Meaning (EN)"because it is (the special situation) ~, naturally …" — a notable circumstance and its expected result
Meaning (HI)चूँकि ...(ख़ास मौक़ा) है, इसलिए स्वाभाविक रूप से...
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે ...(ખાસ પ્રસંગ) છે, એટલે સ્વાભાવિક રીતે...
Connection (接続)plain form (N／な-adj: だ is dropped) + とあって
Register硬 — news reports, descriptions of observed scenes
Typical useThe result clause describes an observed fact (crowds, sell-outs), not the speaker's wish, request or will.

NounN + とあって半額セールとあってhangaku seeru to atte
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + とあって人気歌手が来るとあってninki kashu ga kuru to atte
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + とあって値段が安いとあってnedan ga yasui to atte
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj (no だ) + とあって入場無料とあってnyuujou muryou to atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q7) 全品半額セールとあって、スーパーの前には早朝から行列ができていた。Zenpin hangaku seeru to atte, suupaa no mae ni wa souchou kara gyouretsu ga dekite ita. Because it was a half-price sale on everything, there was a queue in front of the supermarket from early morning. चूँकि सभी चीज़ों पर आधे दाम की सेल थी, सुपरमार्केट के सामने सुबह-सुबह से लंबी कतार लगी थी। બધી વસ્તુઓ પર અડધી કિંમતનું સેલ હોવાથી, સુપરમાર્કેટ સામે વહેલી સવારથી લાંબી લાઇન લાગી હતી.
(added, not in book) 連休最終日とあって、高速道路はひどく渋滞していた。Renkyuu saishuubi to atte, kousoku douro wa hidoku juutai shite ita. As it was the last day of the long weekend, the expressway was terribly congested. लंबी छुट्टियों का आख़िरी दिन होने के कारण, एक्सप्रेसवे पर भारी जाम था। લાંબી રજાનો છેલ્લો દિવસ હોવાથી, એક્સપ્રેસવે પર ભારે ટ્રાફિક જામ હતો.

A stronger, more "newsy" ので: "given that it's (something special)". Distractors: とすると ("if so / supposing"), といっても ("although I say ~, actually…"), and となっては ("now that it has come to this", always followed by a helpless/negative conclusion).

【8】 Nいかんでは／Nいかんによっては

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ikan de wa / N ikan ni yotte wa
Meaning (EN)"depending on N, (a particular outcome — often a serious one — may happen)"
Meaning (HI)N पर निर्भर करते हुए, (कोई ख़ास / गंभीर नतीजा भी हो सकता है)
Meaning (GU)N પર આધાર રાખીને, (કોઈ ખાસ / ગંભીર પરિણામ પણ આવી શકે)

Q1

どんなに片づけた（　）、すぐにまた子どもたちが散らかしてしまう。Donna ni katazuketa (___), sugu ni mata kodomotachi ga chirakashite shimau.

HI: चाहे कितना भी साफ़ कर लूँ, बच्चे तुरंत फिर से बिखेर देते हैं।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ところがtokoro gahowever / but then (unexpectedly)लेकिन (तब अचानक)પરંતુ (ત્યારે અચાનક)
2ところでtokoro deeven if (it's no use)चाहे ...कर लो (कोई फ़ायदा नहीं)ભલે ...કરો (કોઈ ફાયદો નથી)

〜ずにはいられない／〜ずにはすまない~zu ni wa irarenai / sumanai Can't help ~ / must ~ V-ない-stem + ずには… You end up doing it (urge / obligation) — opposite of にたえない. Irresistible urge or social obligation.
〜まい（と）~mai (to) Determined not to ~ V-dict + まい Speaker's own resolve. Vows: 二度と〜まいと誓う.
〜べからず~bekarazu Must not ~ (prohibition) V-dict + べからず (する → すべからず) Aimed at others; signs and rules. Notices: 立ち入るべからず.
〜とあって~to atte Because it's (special) ~ plain + とあって Real circumstance + observed natural result. News-like descriptions of crowds, sell-outs.
〜となると／〜となっては~to naru to / ~to natte wa If it comes to ~ / now that it has come to ~ plain + となると／となっては となると = judgement about a new situation; となっては = helpless, often regretful (今となってはもう遅い). Weighing consequences / lamenting it's too late.
Nいかんではikan de wa Depending on N (one outcome may be…) N（の）+ いかんでは Picks one possible outcome; opposite of いかんにかかわらず. Formal warnings and conditions.
Nを皮切りにo kawakiri ni Starting with N N + を皮切りに The first of a spreading series. Tours, openings, a wave of events.
Nをもってo motte As of N / by means of N N + をもって Marks an end point or a means — not a start. 本日をもって閉店 / 書面をもって通知.
〜からこそ~kara koso Precisely because ~ plain + からこそ Stresses the true reason; ends 〜のだ. Explaining a motive.
〜からには~kara ni wa Now that ~ (must) plain + からには Leads to obligation/resolve (〜べきだ, 〜つもりだ). "Since you started, finish it."
〜ずにすませる~zu ni sumaseru Get by without ~ V-ない-stem + ずにすませる Avoiding something unwanted. できたら〜せずにすませたい.
〜かぎり~kagiri As long as ~ V-dict／V-ている + かぎり A continuing condition → inevitable result. "As long as you think like this, you'll…"
Exam trap 1 — one particle changes everything: ばかり／ばかりで／ばかりに／ばかりの (Q2) and せずにすませたい／せずにはすまない (問題3 [2]) differ by a single particle or ending but mean very different, even opposite, things. Look at what follows the blank: a bad past result → ばかりに; できたら ("if possible") → すませたい (avoid); a duty → ずにはすまない (must).
Exam trap 2 — ★ questions hide set phrases across tiles: 越したことは + ないけれど (Q4), 降ろうが + 降るまいが (Q3), ただ + 一国 + のみならず (Q2). Find the set phrase first, lock those tiles together, then fit the rest.
