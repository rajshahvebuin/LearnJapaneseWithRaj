【1】 〜からいいようなものの

neutral register
Reading~kara ii you na mono no
Meaning (EN)"it was fine/lucky because ~, but (otherwise it would have been bad)" — relief mixed with a warning or rebuke
Meaning (HI)...था इसलिए ठीक रहा, वरना (बुरा हो जाता)
Meaning (GU)...હતું એટલે સારું થયું, નહીંતર (ખરાબ થાત)
Connection (接続)plain form + からいいようなものの  |  also 〜たからよかったようなものの
RegisterNeutral; often used when scolding or reflecting
Typical useThe second clause states the bad outcome that was narrowly avoided (〜たら危なかった／〜たらどうするんだ). Core form: 〜（から）いいようなものの.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + からいいようなものの見つかったからいいようなもののmitsukatta kara ii you na mono no
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + からいいようなものの軽いからいいようなもののkarui kara ii you na mono no
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-だ + からいいようなものの無事だからいいようなもののbuji da kara ii you na mono no
Noun(added, not in book) N-だ + からいいようなものの日曜日だからいいようなもののnichiyoubi da kara ii you na mono no
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 病気が初期に見つかったからいいようなものの、もう少し発見が遅かったら命が危なかった。Byouki ga shoki ni mitsukatta kara ii you na mono no, mou sukoshi hakken ga osokattara inochi ga abunakatta. It's a good thing the illness was found at an early stage — if it had been discovered a little later, my life would have been in danger. अच्छा हुआ कि बीमारी शुरुआती दौर में पकड़ी गई, वरना थोड़ी और देर से पता चलता तो जान ख़तरे में पड़ जाती। સારું થયું કે બીમારી શરૂઆતમાં જ પકડાઈ ગઈ, નહીંતર થોડું મોડું ખબર પડી હોત તો જીવ જોખમમાં આવી જાત.
(added, not in book) けががなかったからいいようなものの、もうあんな危ない運転はしないでね。Kega ga nakatta kara ii you na mono no, mou anna abunai unten wa shinai de ne. It's fine because nobody got hurt, but please don't drive that dangerously again. चोट नहीं लगी इसलिए ठीक है, लेकिन अब ऐसी ख़तरनाक ड्राइविंग मत करना। ઈજા ન થઈ એટલે ઠીક છે, પણ હવે આવું જોખમી ડ્રાઇવિંગ ન કરતા.

Read it as "〜から、いい（の）だが…": the speaker admits the result was OK, then points at the danger. The trap options use different もの／ばかり families: というものの ("although it is said"), とばかりに ("as if to say"), かと思いきや ("contrary to what I expected"). Only ようなものの pairs with いい to mean "it's fine, but…".

【2】 Nをおいて（ほかに）〜ない

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o oite (hoka ni) ~nai
Meaning (EN)"apart from N, there is no other ~" — N is the one and only
Meaning (HI)N के अलावा और कोई नहीं / N ही एकमात्र है
Meaning (GU)N સિવાય બીજું કોઈ નથી / N જ એકમાત્ર છે
Connection (接続)N + をおいて + ほかに／ほかには + 〜ない／いない
Register硬 — speeches, writing; high praise or strong conviction
Typical useUsed to single out the best person/thing: 彼をおいてほかに適任者はいない. Kanji: 措いて (put aside). Do not confuse with 〜において (at/in).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をおいてほかに〜ない努力をおいてほかにないdoryoku o oite hoka ni nai
Noun (person)(added, not in book) N(person) + をおいて + ほかに + いない彼をおいてほかにいないkare o oite hoka ni inai
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches only to nouns—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 夢を実現させるものは、努力をおいてほかにない。Yume o jitsugen saseru mono wa, doryoku o oite hoka ni nai. The only thing that makes a dream come true is effort — there is nothing else. सपने को साकार करने वाली चीज़ मेहनत के अलावा और कुछ नहीं है। સપનું સાકાર કરનારી વસ્તુ મહેનત સિવાય બીજી કોઈ નથી.
(added, not in book) この仕事を任せられるのは、田中さんをおいてほかにいない。Kono shigoto o makaserareru no wa, Tanaka-san o oite hoka ni inai. There is nobody other than Mr Tanaka we can entrust this job to. यह काम तनाका-सान के अलावा किसी और को नहीं सौंपा जा सकता। આ કામ તનાકા-સાન સિવાય બીજા કોઈને સોંપી શકાય તેમ નથી.

The ほか(に)〜ない ending is the signal. を抜きに(して) means "leaving out N" (and is followed by 〜できない／語れない), ならでは is "unique to N" (+ の N), and いかんで is "depending on N" — none of them combine with ほかにない.

【3】 〜までもなく

neutral register
Reading~made mo naku
Meaning (EN)"without even needing to ~" — it's obvious, no need to go as far as ~
Meaning (HI)...करने की ज़रूरत ही नहीं (बिना ...किए ही साफ़ है)
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની જરૂર જ નથી (...કર્યા વગર જ સ્પષ્ટ છે)
Connection (接続)V-dict + までもなく  |  sentence-final: V-dict + までもない
RegisterNeutral to formal
Typical useCommon set phrases: 言うまでもなく (needless to say), 聞くまでもなく, 調べるまでもなく. The main clause shows the thing is already clear.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + までもなく聞くまでもなくkiku made mo naku
Verb (sentence-final)(added, not in book) V-dict + までもない説明するまでもないsetsumei suru made mo nai
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — attaches to verbs of action—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 結果を聞くまでもなく息子の表情から、不合格だったことがわかった。Kekka o kiku made mo naku musuko no hyoujou kara, fugoukaku datta koto ga wakatta. Without even needing to ask the result, I could tell from my son's face that he had failed. नतीजा पूछने की ज़रूरत ही नहीं पड़ी, बेटे के चेहरे से ही पता चल गया कि वह फ़ेल हो गया। પરિણામ પૂછવાની જરૂર જ ન પડી, દીકરાના ચહેરા પરથી જ ખબર પડી ગઈ કે તે નાપાસ થયો.
(added, not in book) 言うまでもなく、健康は何よりも大切だ。Iu made mo naku, kenkou wa nani yori mo taisetsu da. Needless to say, health is more important than anything. कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं कि स्वास्थ्य सबसे ज़रूरी है। કહેવાની જરૂર નથી કે સ્વાસ્થ્ય સૌથી મહત્ત્વનું છે.

Think "not even to the point (まで) of ~". The distractors どころか ("far from ~"), はおろか ("let alone N") and わけもなく ("for no reason / easily") cannot follow a verb like 聞く to mean "no need to".

【4】 Nときたら

neutral register
ReadingN to kitara
Meaning (EN)"as for N / when it comes to N (I'm fed up)" — topic marker loaded with criticism or exasperation
Meaning (HI)N की तो बात ही मत करो / N तो (बस…) — शिकायत के साथ
Meaning (GU)N ની તો વાત જ ન કરો / N તો (બસ…) — ફરિયાદ સાથે
Connection (接続)N + ときたら
RegisterConversational; emotional
Typical useThe topic is usually someone/something close to the speaker (うちの父, 最近の若者, この機械). The comment is negative — complaints, disbelief.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ときたらうちの父ときたらuchi no chichi to kitara
Noun (thing)(added, not in book) N + ときたらこのパソコンときたらkono pasokon to kitara
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — topic must be a noun—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) うちの父ときたら週末はゴルフばかりで、家族とのコミュニケーションがまったくない。Uchi no chichi to kitara shuumatsu wa gorufu bakari de, kazoku to no komyunikeeshon ga mattaku nai. As for my dad — every weekend it's nothing but golf, and there's no communication with the family at all. मेरे पापा की तो बात ही मत करो, हर वीकेंड बस गोल्फ़, परिवार से कोई बातचीत ही नहीं। મારા પપ્પાની તો વાત જ ન કરો, દર વીકએન્ડ બસ ગોલ્ફ, પરિવાર સાથે કોઈ વાતચીત જ નહીં.
(added, not in book) このパソコンときたら、すぐにフリーズする。Kono pasokon to kitara, sugu ni furiizu suru. This computer, honestly — it freezes all the time. यह कंप्यूटर तो बस, बार-बार हैंग हो जाता है। આ કમ્પ્યુટર તો બસ, વારંવાર હેંગ થઈ જાય છે.

Formally it is a topic marker like は, but it adds "I can't believe it". としたら ("if we suppose"), としても ("even if") and といっても ("although I say") are conditional/concessive and need a clause, not just a person as a topic of complaint.

(book: 問題1 Q5) 部屋の中にどこからともなく涼しい風が入ってきた。Heya no naka ni doko kara to mo naku suzushii kaze ga haitte kita. A cool breeze came into the room from somewhere or other. न जाने कहाँ से कमरे में ठंडी हवा आ गई। ખબર નહીં ક્યાંથી ઓરડામાં ઠંડો પવન આવ્યો.
(added, not in book) 演奏が終わると、だれからともなく拍手が起こった。Ensou ga owaru to, dare kara to mo naku hakushu ga okotta. When the performance ended, applause broke out, started by no one in particular. प्रस्तुति ख़त्म होते ही, न जाने किसने शुरू की, तालियाँ बज उठीं। કાર્યક્રમ પૂરો થતાં જ, ખબર નહીં કોણે શરૂ કરી, તાળીઓ પડવા લાગી.

どこから + ともなく is a set expression — memorise it as one chunk. はいざ知らず ("I don't know about N, but…"), はさておき ("setting N aside") and とはいえ ("although") all need a noun/clause to contrast, not a question word.

neutral register
Reading~kagiri da
Meaning (EN)"I feel extremely ~ / nothing could be more ~" — the speaker's own strong feeling
Meaning (HI)बेहद ...लग रहा है / इससे ज़्यादा ...क्या होगा

I'm so envious of my friend who spends New Year in Hawaii every year. हर साल नया साल हवाई में मनाने वाले दोस्त से मुझे बेहद जलन होती है। દર વર્ષે નવું વર્ષ હવાઈમાં ઉજવતા મિત્રની મને ખૂબ જ ઈર્ષ્યા થાય છે.

A colleague I worked with for years has quit, and I feel terribly lonely. सालों साथ काम करने वाला सहकर्मी नौकरी छोड़ गया, बहुत अकेलापन लग रहा है। વર્ષો સાથે કામ કરનાર સહકર્મી નોકરી છોડી ગયો, ખૂબ એકલું લાગે છે.

至り is the trap: 〜の至り needs a noun (光栄の至り, 若気の至り), not an い-adjective. だらけ ("covered in") follows a noun (泥だらけ), and ばかりだ means "keeps getting ~" with a verb (悪くなるばかりだ).

Exam trap 2 — check the sentence ending first: ほかにない → をおいて (Q2); 〜わけではない → からといって (問題3 [1]); おそらく + opinion → ではないだろうか (問題3 [3]). N1 often lets the end of the sentence choose the pattern.
