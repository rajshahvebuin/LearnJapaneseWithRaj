Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions and an answer key but no grammar explanations. Every explanation, formation table and example in §1 is added for this site; the only book sentences are the question sentences themselves (labelled "book"), and every other example is marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. Every item is a fixed pattern whose particle is part of the expression — you can't reason it out from the verb alone, you have to know the set phrase. Biggest trap: Q3 月末とあって vs Q6 この状況にあって (same あって, opposite answers).

1 Grammar Points

The book has no explanation pages — the points below group the particle choices this page drills, explained for this site. Book sentences are the completed question sentences; everything else is (added, not in book).

【1】 Nに即して／Nに即した＋N

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni soku shite / N ni soku shita + N
Meaning (EN)"in line with / based on (facts, reality, rules)"
Meaning (HI)(तथ्यों / वास्तविकता / नियमों) के अनुसार, उनसे मेल खाते हुए
Meaning (GU)(હકીકત / વાસ્તવિકતા / નિયમો) ને અનુરૂપ, તેને આધારે
Connection (接続)N(事実・実態・現実・法律・規則) + に即して／に即した + N
Register硬 — reports, official and business writing
Typical useThe noun is something concrete you measure against: 事実, 実態, 現状, 実戦, 法律, 規則. The verb 即する always takes に, never を.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に即した + N実戦に即した練習方法jissen ni soku shita renshuu houhou
Noun (adverbial)(added, not in book) N + に即して + V事実に即して判断するjijitsu ni soku shite handan suru
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches only to nouns—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q1) 私たちのチームは実戦に即した練習方法を取り入れている。Watashitachi no chiimu wa jissen ni soku shita renshuu houhou o toriirete iru. Our team has adopted training methods based on real match conditions. हमारी टीम ने असली मैच की परिस्थितियों के अनुसार अभ्यास के तरीके अपनाए हैं। અમારી ટીમે વાસ્તવિક મેચની પરિસ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ પ્રેક્ટિસની પદ્ધતિ અપનાવી છે.
(added, not in book) 法律に即して、公平に処分すべきだ。Houritsu ni soku shite, kouhei ni shobun subeki da. It should be dealt with fairly, in accordance with the law. क़ानून के अनुसार, निष्पक्ष रूप से कार्रवाई होनी चाहिए। કાયદા અનુસાર, નિષ્પક્ષ રીતે કાર્યવાહી થવી જોઈએ.

Fix the particle as part of the word: に即する. The trap in Q1 is を, which looks natural because 取り入れる takes を — but を belongs to 練習方法, not to 即した. Near-synonyms: に沿って (follow a plan/wish), に基づいて (based on data/evidence).

【2】 Nを境に（して）／Nを経て

neutral register
ReadingN o sakai ni (shite) / N o hete
Meaning (EN)を境に: "from N on (things changed)" — N is a turning point.
を経て: "after going through / passing N (time, a process, a place)".
Meaning (HI)を境に: N से (बदलाव शुरू हुआ) — मोड़ का बिंदु।
を経て: N से गुज़रकर / N (समय, प्रक्रिया) बीतने के बाद
Meaning (GU)を境に: N થી (ફેરફાર શરૂ થયો) — વળાંકનો મુદ્દો.
を経て: N માંથી પસાર થઈને / N (સમય, પ્રક્રિયા) વીત્યા પછી
Connection (接続)N(日・時・事件) + を境に（して）  |  N(時間・過程・場所) + を経て
RegisterNeutral to slightly formal (を経て is more written)
Typical useを境に is followed by a clear change (変わった, 増えた, 〜なくなった). を経て is used for time spans (20年の時を経て), procedures (審査を経て) and routes (香港を経て).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (turning point)N + を境にあの日を境にano hi o sakai ni
Noun (time span)N + を経て20年の時を経てnijuu-nen no toki o hete
Noun (procedure)(added, not in book) N + を経て厳しい審査を経てkibishii shinsa o hete
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + の／こと + を境に結婚したのを境にkekkon shita no o sakai ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q2) 田中さんに出会ったあの日を境に私の生活は大きく変わった。Tanaka-san ni deatta ano hi o sakai ni watashi no seikatsu wa ookiku kawatta. From the day I met Mr. Tanaka, my life changed dramatically. जिस दिन मैं तानाका जी से मिला, उस दिन से मेरी ज़िंदगी बहुत बदल गई। જે દિવસે હું તાનાકા સાહેબને મળ્યો, તે દિવસથી મારું જીવન ખૂબ બદલાઈ ગયું.
(book: Q4) あの名作が20年の時を経て、映画になってよみがえる！Ano meisaku ga nijuu-nen no toki o hete, eiga ni natte yomigaeru! After 20 years, that masterpiece comes back to life as a film! 20 साल बाद, वह महान कृति फ़िल्म बनकर फिर से जीवित हो रही है! 20 વર્ષ પછી, તે મહાન કૃતિ ફિલ્મ બનીને ફરી જીવંત થઈ રહી છે!
(added, not in book) ４月を境に、電気料金が値上がりした。Shi-gatsu o sakai ni, denki ryoukin ga neagari shita. Starting in April, electricity rates went up. अप्रैल से बिजली की दरें बढ़ गईं। એપ્રિલથી વીજળીના દર વધી ગયા.
(added, not in book) この薬は長い臨床試験を経て、ようやく発売された。Kono kusuri wa nagai rinshou shiken o hete, youyaku hatsubai sareta. After long clinical trials, this medicine was finally released. लंबे क्लिनिकल ट्रायल से गुज़रने के बाद, यह दवा आख़िरकार बाज़ार में आई। લાંબા ક્લિનિકલ ટ્રાયલમાંથી પસાર થયા પછી, આ દવા આખરે બજારમાં આવી.

Both take を because 境 and 経る are treated as "passing over" a point or span. In Q2 the trap is に (あの日に = "on that day") — but then 境に has no object. In Q4 と would mean "with time", which makes no sense; 時を経て is a fixed literary phrase.

【3】 〜とあって／Nにあって

硬 formal/written
Reading~ to atte / N ni atte
Meaning (EN)とあって: "because (it is the special situation) ~, (naturally)…" — a reason the speaker observes.
にあって: "in (the position / circumstances of) N" — a formal に + ある.
Meaning (HI)とあって: क्योंकि (ख़ास स्थिति) ~ है, (इसलिए स्वाभाविक रूप से)…
にあって: N की (स्थिति / हालात) में
Meaning (GU)とあって: કારણ કે (ખાસ પરિસ્થિતિ) ~ છે, (એટલે સ્વાભાવિક રીતે)…
にあって: N ની (સ્થિતિ / સંજોગો) માં
Connection (接続)Plain form／N + とあって  |  N(状況・立場・時代) + にあって
Register硬 — news, reports, formal speech
Typical useとあって: the main clause describes a natural, observable result (込んでいた, 人が集まった) — never the speaker's will. にあって: N is a situation, era or position (この状況, 不況下, 社長という立場); often followed by も (〜にあっても = even in ~).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (reason)N + とあって月末とあってgetsumatsu to atte
Noun (situation)N + にあってこの状況にあってkono joukyou ni atte
Verb(added, not in book) V plain + とあって人気歌手が来るとあってninki kashu ga kuru to atte
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) い-adj／な-adj(だ) + とあって (less common)連休だとあってrenkyuu da to atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q3) 月末とあって、銀行はとても込んでいた。Getsumatsu to atte, ginkou wa totemo konde ita. Since it was the end of the month, the bank was very crowded. महीने का आख़िर होने की वजह से, बैंक में बहुत भीड़ थी। મહિનાનો અંત હોવાથી, બેંકમાં ખૂબ ભીડ હતી.
(book: Q6) この状況にあって、今までと同じ生活を維持するのは難しい。Kono joukyou ni atte, ima made to onaji seikatsu o iji suru no wa muzukashii. In this situation, it is difficult to keep living the same way as before. इस हालात में, पहले जैसा जीवन बनाए रखना मुश्किल है। આ પરિસ્થિતિમાં, પહેલાં જેવું જીવન જાળવી રાખવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
(added, not in book) 人気アイドルが来るとあって、会場は若者でいっぱいだった。Ninki aidoru ga kuru to atte, kaijou wa wakamono de ippai datta. Because a popular idol was coming, the venue was packed with young people. एक लोकप्रिय आइडल के आने की वजह से, हॉल युवाओं से भरा हुआ था। એક લોકપ્રિય આઇડોલ આવવાનો હોવાથી, હૉલ યુવાનોથી ભરેલો હતો.
(added, not in book) 不況下にあっても、売り上げを伸ばす企業がある。Fukyou-ka ni atte mo, uriage o nobasu kigyou ga aru. Even in a recession, there are companies that grow their sales. मंदी के दौर में भी, कुछ कंपनियाँ अपनी बिक्री बढ़ाती हैं। મંદીના સમયમાં પણ, કેટલીક કંપનીઓ પોતાનું વેચાણ વધારે છે.

This is the classic と／に trap: the same あって, different particle, different meaning. Ask "is N a reason (月末 → crowded)?" → とあって. "Is N a place/situation you are in?" → にあって. Q3 and Q6 deliberately offer the same pair with the opposite answer.

【4】 V-dictに堪えない／Nをよそに

硬 formal/written
ReadingV-dict ni taenai / N o yoso ni
Meaning (EN)に堪えない: "too awful to (listen to / look at)" — 聞くに堪えない, 見るに堪えない.
をよそに: "ignoring / without regard for N (others' worry, opposition)".
Meaning (HI)に堪えない: (सुनने / देखने) लायक़ नहीं, इतना बुरा कि सहा न जाए।
をよそに: N (दूसरों की चिंता / विरोध) की परवाह किए बिना
Meaning (GU)に堪えない: (સાંભળવા / જોવા) લાયક નથી, એટલું ખરાબ કે સહન ન થાય.
をよそに: N (બીજાની ચિંતા / વિરોધ) ની પરવા કર્યા વગર
Connection (接続)V-dict(聞く・見る・読む) + に堪えない  |  N(心配・反対・期待) + をよそに
Register硬 — written, critical commentary
Typical useに堪えない is almost limited to 聞く／見る／読む and expresses disgust. (The other use, N + に堪えない = "can't contain (gratitude)", e.g. 感謝の念に堪えない.) をよそに: the subject does something while ignoring others' feelings — often a slightly critical tone.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dict.)V-dict + に堪えない聞くに堪えない言葉kiku ni taenai kotoba
Noun (emotion)(added, not in book) N + に堪えない = "overwhelmed with" (感謝・感激)感謝の念に堪えないkansha no nen ni taenai
Noun (others' feelings)N + をよそに周囲の心配をよそにshuui no shinpai o yoso ni
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q5) まさか彼があんな聞くに堪えない言葉を口にしようとは。Masaka kare ga anna kiku ni taenai kotoba o kuchi ni shiyou to wa. I never imagined he would say such unbearable words. मैंने सोचा भी नहीं था कि वह ऐसे सुनने लायक़ न रहने वाले शब्द बोलेगा। મેં વિચાર્યું પણ નહોતું કે તે આવા સાંભળવા લાયક ન હોય એવા શબ્દો બોલશે.
(book: Q7) 周囲の心配をよそに、父は山登りを続けている。Shuui no shinpai o yoso ni, chichi wa yamanobori o tsuzukete iru. Ignoring the worries of those around him, my father keeps on mountain climbing. आसपास के लोगों की चिंता की परवाह किए बिना, मेरे पिताजी पहाड़ चढ़ना जारी रखे हुए हैं। આસપાસના લોકોની ચિંતાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, મારા પિતાજી પર્વતારોહણ ચાલુ રાખે છે.
(added, not in book) 事故現場は見るに堪えないありさまだった。Jiko genba wa miru ni taenai arisama datta. The accident scene was too horrible to look at. दुर्घटना की जगह का दृश्य देखने लायक़ नहीं था। અકસ્માતના સ્થળનું દૃશ્ય જોવા લાયક નહોતું.
(added, not in book) 住民の反対をよそに、工事は始まった。Juumin no hantai o yoso ni, kouji wa hajimatta. Disregarding the residents' opposition, construction began. निवासियों के विरोध की परवाह किए बिना, निर्माण कार्य शुरू हो गया। રહેવાસીઓના વિરોધની પરવા કર્યા વગર, બાંધકામ શરૂ થઈ ગયું.

Particles are fixed: 堪える takes に (聞くに), and よそ ("somewhere else") takes を — literally "putting N off to the side". Q5 offers を (聞くを堪えない is not Japanese) and Q7 offers に (心配によそに is not Japanese).

【5】 Nをもって／をもちまして

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o motte / o mochimashite
Meaning (EN)"with / as of / by means of N" — here: "with this (we close)" announcing an end point.
Meaning (HI)N के साथ / N से (समापन करते हैं) — औपचारिक घोषणा
Meaning (GU)N સાથે / N થી (સમાપન કરીએ છીએ) — ઔપચારિક જાહેરાત
Connection (接続)N(以上・本日・これ) + をもって／をもちまして（polite）
Register硬 — ceremonies, announcements, official notices
Typical useTime/end point: 以上をもちまして終了します, 本日をもって閉店. Means: 書面をもって通知する ("by written notice"). をもちまして is the extra-polite form of をもって.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (end point)N + をもちまして以上をもちましてijou o mochimashite
Noun (date)(added, not in book) N + をもって3月末をもって退職するsangatsu-matsu o motte taishoku suru
Noun (means)(added, not in book) N + をもって書面をもって通知するshomen o motte tsuuchi suru
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q8) 以上をもちまして、本日は終了とさせていただきます。Ijou o mochimashite, honjitsu wa shuuryou to sasete itadakimasu. With this, we will conclude today's session. इसी के साथ, हम आज का कार्यक्रम समाप्त करते हैं। આ સાથે, અમે આજનો કાર્યક્રમ પૂર્ણ કરીએ છીએ.
(added, not in book) 当店は本日をもって閉店いたします。Touten wa honjitsu o motte heiten itashimasu. Our shop will close as of today. हमारी दुकान आज से बंद हो रही है। અમારી દુકાન આજથી બંધ થઈ રહી છે.

Learn it as a block: 以上をもちまして. 以上にもちまして does not exist; に is the distractor because "以上に" (more than) is a real phrase.

【6】 〜とすると／〜としたら

neutral register
Reading~ to suru to / ~ to shitara
Meaning (EN)"if we assume that ~ / supposing ~, then…"
Meaning (HI)अगर यह मान लें कि ~ / मान लीजिए ~, तो…
Meaning (GU)જો એમ માની લઈએ કે ~ / ધારો કે ~, તો…
Connection (接続)Plain form (N／な-adj + だ) + とすると／としたら／とすれば
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe main clause is a logical consequence or the speaker's judgment (〜なければならないだろう, 〜はずだ). The と is the quoting と: "if we take it that X".
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + だ + とすると事実だとするとjijitsu da to suru to
Verb(added, not in book) V plain + とすると彼が来ないとするとkare ga konai to suru to
い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj + とすると値段が高いとするとnedan ga takai to suru to
な-adj(added, not in book) な-adj + だ + とすると本当に安全だとするとhontou ni anzen da to suru to
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q9) その証言が事実だとすると、捜査をやり直さなければならないだろう。Sono shougen ga jijitsu da to suru to, sousa o yarinaosanakereba naranai darou. If that testimony is true, the investigation will probably have to be redone. अगर वह गवाही सच है, तो शायद जाँच फिर से करनी पड़ेगी। જો તે જુબાની સાચી હોય, તો કદાચ તપાસ ફરીથી કરવી પડશે.
(added, not in book) このまま雨が降り続くとすると、明日の試合は中止だろう。Kono mama ame ga furitsuzuku to suru to, ashita no shiai wa chuushi darou. If the rain keeps falling like this, tomorrow's game will probably be cancelled. अगर बारिश ऐसे ही होती रही, तो कल का मैच शायद रद्द हो जाएगा। જો વરસાદ આમ જ પડતો રહેશે, તો કાલની મેચ કદાચ રદ થઈ જશે.

After a plain sentence ending (〜だ), only the quotation particle と can follow. 〜だをする is ungrammatical, so b is ruled out on form alone.

【7】 Nといえども

硬 formal/written
ReadingN to iedomo
Meaning (EN)"even though (it is) N / even N…" — admitting a fact, then saying something contrary to expectation.
Meaning (HI)भले ही N हो / N होने के बावजूद
Meaning (GU)ભલે N હોય / N હોવા છતાં
Connection (接続)N／Plain form + といえども
Register硬 — written, speeches
Typical useN is something that would lead you to expect the opposite (町工場 → small and unimpressive; 子ども → can be forgiven). Often 〜といえども、〜は許されない／〜だ.

い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj + といえども寒いといえどもsamui to iedomo
な-adj(added, not in book) な-adj (だ) + といえども便利だといえどもbenri da to iedomo

(book: Q10) 町工場といえども、その技術は世界レベルだ。Machi-koujou to iedomo, sono gijutsu wa sekai reberu da. Though it's just a small local factory, its technology is world-class. भले ही यह एक छोटी स्थानीय फ़ैक्टरी है, इसकी तकनीक विश्व-स्तरीय है। ભલે આ એક નાની સ્થાનિક ફેક્ટરી હોય, તેની ટેક્નોલોજી વિશ્વ-સ્તરની છે.

いえども comes from 言う, and the quoted thing takes と ("even if you call it N"). を cannot mark a quotation. Close relatives: とはいえ (= "that said"), といっても (more casual).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

All 10 items from p.18. Answers confirmed from the 別冊 解答 (PDF p.80): 1 a · 2 b · 3 a · 4 b · 5 a · 6 b · 7 b · 8 b · 9 a · 10 a.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

私たちのチームは実戦（ a に ／ b を ）即した練習方法を取り入れている。Watashitachi no chiimu wa jissen (a ni / b o) soku shita renshuu houhou o toriirete iru.

Full sentence: 私たちのチームは実戦に即した練習方法を取り入れている。Watashitachi no chiimu wa jissen ni soku shita renshuu houhou o toriirete iru.
EN: Our team has adopted training methods based on real match conditions.
HI: हमारी टीम ने असली मैच की परिस्थितियों के अनुसार अभ्यास के तरीके अपनाए हैं।
GU: અમારી ટીમે વાસ્તવિક મેચની પરિસ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ પ્રેક્ટિસની પદ્ધતિ અપનાવી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにniin line with (に即した)के अनुसारને અનુરૂપ
bをoobject marker — not possible with 即するकर्म चिह्न — 即する के साथ संभव नहींકર્મ ચિહ્ન — 即する સાથે શક્ય નથી

Why: 即する is intransitive and always takes に: 実戦に即した (point 1). を looks tempting because the sentence later has 〜を取り入れている, but that を belongs to 練習方法.

Q2

田中さんに出会ったあの日（ a に ／ b を ）境に私の生活は大きく変わった。Tanaka-san ni deatta ano hi (a ni / b o) sakai ni watashi no seikatsu wa ookiku kawatta.

Full sentence: 田中さんに出会ったあの日を境に私の生活は大きく変わった。Tanaka-san ni deatta ano hi o sakai ni watashi no seikatsu wa ookiku kawatta.
EN: From the day I met Mr. Tanaka, my life changed dramatically.
HI: जिस दिन मैं तानाका जी से मिला, उस दिन से मेरी ज़िंदगी बहुत बदल गई।
GU: જે દિવસે હું તાનાકા સાહેબને મળ્યો, તે દિવસથી મારું જીવન ખૂબ બદલાઈ ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにnion (that day) — leaves 境に without an objectउस दिन (को) — 境に अधूरा रह जाता हैતે દિવસે — 境に અધૂરું રહે છે
bをowith (that day) as the turning point (を境に)उस दिन को मोड़ मानकर / उस दिन सेતે દિવસને વળાંક માનીને / તે દિવસથી

Why: The set pattern is Nを境に ("taking N as the boundary"), followed by a change (大きく変わった) — point 2. あの日に would be a plain time expression and leaves 境に hanging.

Q3

月末（ a と ／ b に ）あって、銀行はとても込んでいた。Getsumatsu (a to / b ni) atte, ginkou wa totemo konde ita.

Full sentence: 月末とあって、銀行はとても込んでいた。Getsumatsu to atte, ginkou wa totemo konde ita.
EN: Since it was the end of the month, the bank was very crowded.
HI: महीने का आख़िर होने की वजह से, बैंक में बहुत भीड़ थी।
GU: મહિનાનો અંત હોવાથી, બેંકમાં ખૂબ ભીડ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとtobecause it is (a special occasion) — とあってक्योंकि (ख़ास मौक़ा) हैકારણ કે (ખાસ પ્રસંગ) છે
bにniin (the situation of) — にあって(की स्थिति) में(ની સ્થિતિ) માં

Why: The end of the month is the reason the bank was crowded — a natural, observed result → とあって (point 3). にあって describes being inside a situation/position and doesn't give a reason for a one-off scene like this.

Q4

あの名作が20年の時（ a と ／ b を ）経て、映画になってよみがえる！Ano meisaku ga nijuu-nen no toki (a to / b o) hete, eiga ni natte yomigaeru!

Full sentence: あの名作が20年の時を経て、映画になってよみがえる！Ano meisaku ga nijuu-nen no toki o hete, eiga ni natte yomigaeru!
EN: After 20 years, that masterpiece comes back to life as a film!
HI: 20 साल बाद, वह महान कृति फ़िल्म बनकर फिर से जीवित हो रही है!
GU: 20 વર્ષ પછી, તે મહાન કૃતિ ફિલ્મ બનીને ફરી જીવંત થઈ રહી છે!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとtowith / and — no meaning with 経るके साथ — 経る के साथ अर्थहीनસાથે — 経る સાથે અર્થહીન
bをopassing through (time) — を経て(समय) से गुज़रकर(સમય) માંથી પસાર થઈને

Why: 経る ("pass through") is transitive: 時を経て (point 2). と経て is not a pattern; the phrase 〜年の時を経て is a fixed literary expression.

Q5

まさか彼があんな聞く（ a に ／ b を ）堪えない言葉を口にしようとは。Masaka kare ga anna kiku (a ni / b o) taenai kotoba o kuchi ni shiyou to wa.

Full sentence: まさか彼があんな聞くに堪えない言葉を口にしようとは。Masaka kare ga anna kiku ni taenai kotoba o kuchi ni shiyou to wa.
EN: I never imagined he would say such unbearable words.
HI: मैंने सोचा भी नहीं था कि वह ऐसे सुनने लायक़ न रहने वाले शब्द बोलेगा।
GU: મેં વિચાર્યું પણ નહોતું કે તે આવા સાંભળવા લાયક ન હોય એવા શબ્દો બોલશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにnitoo awful to (hear) — に堪えない(सुनने) लायक़ नहीं(સાંભળવા) લાયક નહીં
bをoobject marker — 聞くを is ungrammaticalकर्म चिह्न — 聞くを ग़लत हैકર્મ ચિહ્ન — 聞くを ખોટું છે

Why: V-dict + に堪えない = "unbearable to ~" (point 4). A dictionary-form verb can't take を directly, and 堪える takes に.

Q6

この状況（ a と ／ b に ）あって、今までと同じ生活を維持するのは難しい。Kono joukyou (a to / b ni) atte, ima made to onaji seikatsu o iji suru no wa muzukashii.

Full sentence: この状況にあって、今までと同じ生活を維持するのは難しい。Kono joukyou ni atte, ima made to onaji seikatsu o iji suru no wa muzukashii.
EN: In this situation, it is difficult to keep living the same way as before.
HI: इस हालात में, पहले जैसा जीवन बनाए रखना मुश्किल है।
GU: આ પરિસ્થિતિમાં, પહેલાં જેવું જીવન જાળવી રાખવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとtobecause it is — とあってक्योंकि ... हैકારણ કે ... છે
bにniin (this situation) — にあって(इस हालात) में(આ પરિસ્થિતિ) માં

Why: 状況 is a circumstance you are in, and the main clause is the speaker's judgment (難しい) — not an observed natural result → にあって (point 3). とあって would need a scene caused by the reason (e.g. 込んでいた).

Q7

周囲の心配（ a に ／ b を ）よそに、父は山登りを続けている。Shuui no shinpai (a ni / b o) yoso ni, chichi wa yamanobori o tsuzukete iru.

Full sentence: 周囲の心配をよそに、父は山登りを続けている。Shuui no shinpai o yoso ni, chichi wa yamanobori o tsuzukete iru.
EN: Ignoring the worries of those around him, my father keeps on mountain climbing.
HI: आसपास के लोगों की चिंता की परवाह किए बिना, मेरे पिताजी पहाड़ चढ़ना जारी रखे हुए हैं।
GU: આસપાસના લોકોની ચિંતાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, મારા પિતાજી પર્વતારોહણ ચાલુ રાખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにni(no pattern 〜によそに)(〜によそに नाम का कोई पैटर्न नहीं)(〜によそに નામની કોઈ પેટર્ન નથી)
bをoignoring, without regard for — をよそにकी परवाह किए बिनाની પરવા કર્યા વગર

Why: The pattern is Nをよそに: "putting N aside (よそ = elsewhere)" (point 4). に is a distractor only because of the following に in よそに.

Q8

以上（ a に ／ b を ）もちまして、本日は終了とさせていただきます。Ijou (a ni / b o) mochimashite, honjitsu wa shuuryou to sasete itadakimasu.

Full sentence: 以上をもちまして、本日は終了とさせていただきます。Ijou o mochimashite, honjitsu wa shuuryou to sasete itadakimasu.
EN: With this, we will conclude today's session.
HI: इसी के साथ, हम आज का कार्यक्रम समाप्त करते हैं।
GU: આ સાથે, અમે આજનો કાર્યક્રમ પૂર્ણ કરીએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにni(以上に = "more than" — wrong here)(以上に = "से ज़्यादा" — यहाँ ग़लत)(以上に = "થી વધુ" — અહીં ખોટું)
bをowith this (as the end) — をもちましてइसी के साथ (समापन)આ સાથે (સમાપન)

Why: もつ ("hold") is transitive, so 以上をもちまして, the polite form of をもって (point 5). 以上に is a real phrase ("more than"), which is why it's offered as the trap.

Q9

その証言が事実だ（ a と ／ b を ）すると、捜査をやり直さなければならないだろう。Sono shougen ga jijitsu da (a to / b o) suru to, sousa o yarinaosanakereba naranai darou.

Full sentence: その証言が事実だとすると、捜査をやり直さなければならないだろう。Sono shougen ga jijitsu da to suru to, sousa o yarinaosanakereba naranai darou.
EN: If that testimony is true, the investigation will probably have to be redone.
HI: अगर वह गवाही सच है, तो शायद जाँच फिर से करनी पड़ेगी।
GU: જો તે જુબાની સાચી હોય, તો કદાચ તપાસ ફરીથી કરવી પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとtoif we assume that — とするとअगर मान लें किજો માની લઈએ કે

Why: After a sentence-final 〜だ only the quoting と can follow: 事実だとすると = "supposing it is true" (point 6). 事実だをする is ungrammatical.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the a/b pairs the book itself set against each other — the contrasts N1 likes to test.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とあって~ to atte Because (special situation) ~, naturally… N／plain + とあって Gives a reason; result is an observed scene. 月末とあって込んでいた
NにあってN ni atte In (situation / position) N N + にあって Gives a setting; result is often a judgment (難しい) or 〜にあっても. この状況にあって／不況下にあっても
Nを境にN o sakai ni From N on (a change) N + を境に Point in time + change. "After that day, things changed."
Nを経てN o hete After passing through N N + を経て Span of time / process / route. 20年の時を経て／審査を経て
Nに即してN ni soku shite In line with N N + に即して Particle に is fixed (即する is intransitive). Following facts, reality, rules.
NをよそにN o yoso ni Ignoring N N + をよそに Particle を is fixed; often critical. Someone ignores others' worry/opposition.
〜とすると／Nといえども~ to suru to / N to iedomo If we assume ~ / Even though N plain／N + と + する／いえども Both use the quoting と; を cannot quote. Hypothesis / concession.
Exam trap 1 — the same verb, different particle: とあって (reason) vs にあって (setting). N1 often places both in one paper. Test: can you replace the phrase with "because it is ~"? → と. With "in ~"? → に.
Exam trap 2 — を vs に before a fixed word: 境に／経て／よそに／もちまして take を; 即して／堪えない take に. Memorise these as blocks (を境に, をよそに, に即して) rather than guessing from the meaning.
