日本語パワードリル N1 文法

Nihongo Power Drill N1 Bunpou (アスク出版) — a pure drill book: 30 two-page rounds in the real JLPT format (問題1 文法形式の判断, 問題2 ★ 文の組み立て, 問題3 文章の文法), interleaved with 10 one-page 集中トレーニング drills on particles, adverbs, look-alike expressions, causative/passive and repeated-word patterns. The book has no explanation pages, so every grammar explanation on these pages is added for this site; answers come from the book's 別冊 answer key.
