【1】 Nのごとき

硬 written
ReadingN no gotoki
Meaning (EN)"(people / things) like N" — with a person: humble (我々のごとき) or contemptuous (お前のごとき)
Meaning (HI)N जैसा (अपने लिए विनम्र, दूसरों के लिए तिरस्कार)
Meaning (GU)N જેવો (પોતાના માટે નમ્રતા, બીજા માટે તિરસ્કાર)
Connection (接続)N + のごとき + N ／ V-る(が)ごとき ／ N + のごとく + V
Register硬 — literary form of のような
Typical use我々のごとき庶民, 彼のごとき天才; adverbial form のごとく ("like").
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (attributive)N + のごとき + N我々のごとき一般庶民wareware no gotoki ippan shomin
Noun (adverbial)(added, not in book) N + のごとく + V風のごとく走るkaze no gotoku hashiru
Verb(added, not in book) V-る／V-た + (が)ごとき夢を見るがごとき話yume o miru ga gotoki hanashi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 我々のごとき一般庶民は、あんな高級マンションには一生住めそうもない。Wareware no gotoki ippan shomin wa, anna koukyuu manshon ni wa isshou sumesou mo nai. Ordinary folk like us will never get to live in a luxury apartment like that. हम जैसे आम लोग ऐसे आलीशान अपार्टमेंट में ज़िंदगी भर नहीं रह पाएँगे। અમારા જેવા સામાન્ય લોકો આવા વૈભવી ઍપાર્ટમેન્ટમાં જિંદગીભર રહી શકશે નહીં.
(added, not in book) 彼のごとき優秀な人材は、めったにいない。Kare no gotoki yuushuu na jinzai wa, metta ni inai. Talented people like him are rare. उसके जैसे क़ाबिल लोग बहुत कम मिलते हैं। તેના જેવા હોશિયાર લોકો ભાગ્યે જ મળે છે.

Before a noun (一般庶民) you need the attributive のごとき; ごとく is adverbial and also needs の after a noun. 並み attaches without の (人並みの); なりの means "in one's own way".

【2】 V-るが早いか

硬 written
ReadingV-ru ga hayai ka
Meaning (EN)"no sooner had (someone) ~ than …" — immediate next action
Meaning (HI)...करते ही तुरंत
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ તરત
Connection (接続)V-dict ／ V-た + が早いか
Register硬 — written, narrative
Typical useA third person's quick action in the past: ベルが鳴るが早いか、教室を飛び出した.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + が早いか乗り込むが早いかnorikomu ga hayai ka
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + が早いか席に着いたが早いかseki ni tsuita ga hayai ka
Adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — action verbs only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) その女性は、新幹線に乗り込むが早いか、すぐにバッグからパソコンを取り出した。Sono josei wa, shinkansen ni norikomu ga hayai ka, sugu ni baggu kara pasokon o toridashita. No sooner had the woman boarded the bullet train than she took her laptop out of her bag. वह महिला शिंकानसेन में चढ़ते ही तुरंत बैग से लैपटॉप निकाल बैठी। તે મહિલા શિંકાનસેનમાં ચડતાં જ તરત બૅગમાંથી લૅપટૉપ કાઢી બેઠી.
(added, not in book) 子どもたちはチャイムが鳴るが早いか、校庭へ飛び出していった。Kodomotachi wa chaimu ga naru ga hayai ka, koutei e tobidashite itta. The moment the bell rang, the children rushed out into the schoolyard. घंटी बजते ही बच्चे स्कूल के मैदान की ओर दौड़ पड़े। ઘંટ વાગતાં જ બાળકો શાળાના મેદાન તરફ દોડી ગયાં.

Can't be used for the speaker's own intention or for commands. なりに ("in one's own way") is a noun pattern; と思いきや reverses an expectation; までもなく ("without needing to") doesn't fit すぐに.

【3】 V-たが最後

neutral register
ReadingV-ta ga saigo
Meaning (EN)"once ~, (that's it — it can't be undone / it always ends badly)"
Meaning (HI)एक बार ...कर लिया, तो बस (फिर कुछ नहीं हो सकता)
Meaning (GU)એક વાર ...કર્યું, તો બસ (પછી કંઈ ન થાય)
Connection (接続)V-た + が最後 ／ V-たら最後
RegisterNeutral; often negative or exasperated
Typical use言い出したが最後, 絶対に変えない; 寝たら最後, 朝まで起きない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + が最後言い出したが最後iidashita ga saigo
Verb(added, not in book) V-たら + 最後寝たら最後netara saigo
Adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 父は頑固で、言い出したが最後絶対に考えを変えない。Chichi wa ganko de, iidashita ga saigo zettai ni kangae o kaenai. My father is stubborn — once he's said something, he never changes his mind. मेरे पिताजी ज़िद्दी हैं — एक बार कह दिया, तो बस अपनी राय कभी नहीं बदलते। મારા પપ્પા જિદ્દી છે — એક વાર કહી દીધું, તો બસ પોતાનો વિચાર ક્યારેય બદલતા નથી.
(added, not in book) このお菓子は、一口食べたが最後、やめられなくなる。Kono okashi wa, hitokuchi tabeta ga saigo, yamerarenaku naru. Once you take a bite of this snack, you can't stop. यह नाश्ता एक बार चख लिया, तो बस छोड़ा नहीं जाता। આ નાસ્તો એક વાર ચાખ્યો, તો બસ છોડી શકાતો નથી.

とたん ("just as") needs a sudden one-off change, not a permanent habit (絶対に変えない); とあっては needs a special circumstance; に至っては ("as for N, even") follows a noun.

【4】 V-こそすれ〜ない

硬 written
ReadingV-koso sure ~nai
Meaning (EN)"(it) may ~, but it certainly doesn't …" — only the first, never the second
Meaning (HI)...तो ज़रूर करता है, पर ...बिल्कुल नहीं
Meaning (GU)...તો જરૂર કરે છે, પણ ...બિલકુલ નહીં
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ／ する-noun + こそすれ + (opposite) 〜ない
Register硬 — written
Typical use感謝こそすれ、恨んではいない; 害を及ぼしこそすれ、いい影響は与えない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-stem + こそすれ及ぼしこそすれoyoboshi koso sure
する-noun(added, not in book) N + こそすれ感謝こそすれkansha koso sure
Adjective(added, not in book) adj + こそあれ (variant)程度の差こそあれteido no sa koso are
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) タバコは健康に害を及ぼしこそすれ、いい影響は与えない。Tabako wa kenkou ni gai o oyoboshi koso sure, ii eikyou wa ataenai. Tobacco may harm your health, but it certainly has no good effect. तंबाकू सेहत को नुक़सान तो ज़रूर पहुँचाता है, पर अच्छा असर बिल्कुल नहीं डालता। તમાકુ તબિયતને નુકસાન તો જરૂર પહોંચાડે છે, પણ સારી અસર બિલકુલ નથી કરતું.
(added, not in book) 先生には感謝こそすれ、恨むことなどない。Sensei ni wa kansha koso sure, uramu koto nado nai. I'm grateful to my teacher, if anything — I have no resentment at all. शिक्षक के प्रति मैं आभारी ही हूँ, कोई शिकायत बिल्कुल नहीं। શિક્ષક પ્રત્યે હું આભારી જ છું, કોઈ ફરિયાદ બિલકુલ નથી.

The second clause is always the negated opposite. 兼ねて ("doubling as") needs を; 次第で ("depending on") follows a noun; ずくめで ("nothing but") follows a noun (黒ずくめ, いいことずくめ).

【5】 V-るそばから

neutral register
ReadingV-ru soba kara
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~ (it gets undone / it happens again)" — repeated frustration
Meaning (HI)...करते ही फिर से (बार-बार)
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ ફરી (વારંવાર)
Connection (接続)V-dict ／ V-た + そばから
RegisterNeutral, slightly written
Typical useRepeated cycles: 覚えるそばから忘れる; 片付けるそばから散らかす; 返信するそばから次のメールが来る.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + そばから返信するそばからhenshin suru soba kara
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict + そばから覚えるそばから忘れるoboeru soba kara wasureru
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + そばから片付けたそばからkatazuketa soba kara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 返信するそばから次のメールが来て、いつまでたっても出かけられない。Henshin suru soba kara tsugi no meeru ga kite, itsu made tatte mo dekakerarenai. As soon as I reply, the next email comes in, so I can never get out of the house. जवाब देते ही अगला ईमेल आ जाता है, इसलिए कभी बाहर निकल ही नहीं पाता। જવાબ આપતાં જ આગળનો ઈમેલ આવી જાય છે, એટલે ક્યારેય બહાર નીકળી જ શકતો નથી.
(added, not in book) 単語を覚えるそばから忘れてしまう。Tango o oboeru soba kara wasurete shimau. I forget words as soon as I learn them. शब्द याद करते ही भूल जाता हूँ। શબ્દ યાદ કરતાં જ ભૂલી જાઉં છું.

Unlike が早いか (one quick action), そばから is a repeating cycle — いつまでたっても shows the repetition. がてら needs a noun/stem (散歩がてら); ものなら needs volitional; からこそ gives a reason.
