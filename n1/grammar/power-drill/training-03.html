【1】 限る family: Nに限ったことではない／Nに限る／Nを限りに

neutral register
ReadingN ni kagitta koto de wa nai / N ni kagiru / N o kagiri ni
Meaning (EN)に限ったことではない: "not limited to N / not just N".
に限る: "N is the best (choice)".
を限りに: "as of N (and never again) / N is the last time".
Meaning (HI)に限ったことではない: सिर्फ़ N तक सीमित नहीं।
に限る: N ही सबसे अच्छा है।
を限りに: N को आख़िरी बार मानकर / N के बाद फिर नहीं
Meaning (GU)に限ったことではない: ફક્ત N સુધી મર્યાદિત નથી.
に限る: N જ શ્રેષ્ઠ છે.
を限りに: N ને છેલ્લી વાર ગણીને / N પછી ફરી નહીં
Connection (接続)N + に限ったことではない  |  N／V-dict + に限る  |  N(今日・今回) + を限りに
RegisterNeutral (を限りに is slightly formal)
Typical useに限ったことではない: a problem is general, not special to N. に限る: personal recommendation (〜なら〜に限る). を限りに: an end point — 今日を限りに禁煙する, 今回を限りに引退する.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (limit)N + に限ったことではない今回の選挙に限ったことではないkonkai no senkyo ni kagitta koto de wa nai
Noun (best)N + に限るこの店に限るkono mise ni kagiru
Noun (end point)N + を限りに今日を限りにkyou o kagiri ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict／V-ない + に限る疲れた時は寝るに限るtsukareta toki wa neru ni kagiru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q1) 投票率が低いのは今回の選挙に限ったことではない。Touhyouritsu ga hikui no wa konkai no senkyo ni kagitta koto de wa nai. Low voter turnout is not limited to this election. कम मतदान सिर्फ़ इस चुनाव तक सीमित नहीं है। ઓછું મતદાન ફક્ત આ ચૂંટણી સુધી મર્યાદિત નથી.
(book: Q4) 今日を限りに、木村さんとはもう会えなくなってしまう。Kyou o kagiri ni, Kimura-san to wa mou aenaku natte shimau. After today, I won't be able to see Mr. Kimura anymore. आज के बाद, मैं किमुरा जी से फिर नहीं मिल पाऊँगा। આજ પછી, હું કિમુરા સાહેબને ફરી મળી નહીં શકું.
(book: Q7) 果物を買うならこの店に限る。なにより新鮮で値段も高くない。Kudamono o kau nara kono mise ni kagiru. Nani yori shinsen de nedan mo takakunai. If you're buying fruit, this shop is the best. Above all it's fresh, and it isn't expensive either. फल ख़रीदने हों तो यही दुकान सबसे अच्छी है। सबसे बढ़कर ताज़ा है और दाम भी ज़्यादा नहीं। ફળ ખરીદવા હોય તો આ જ દુકાન શ્રેષ્ઠ છે. સૌથી વધુ તો તાજાં છે અને ભાવ પણ વધારે નથી.
(added, not in book) 暑い日は冷たいビールに限る。Atsui hi wa tsumetai biiru ni kagiru. On a hot day, nothing beats a cold beer. गर्म दिन में ठंडी बीयर से बढ़कर कुछ नहीं। ગરમ દિવસે ઠંડી બીયરથી વધુ સારું કંઈ નથી.

Three uses of 限る, two particles. "Limit/best" → に限る; "end point" → を限りに. The book tests all three on one page (Q1, Q4, Q7) — check the meaning, not just the verb.

【2】 〜とみると／〜とみえて

neutral register
Reading~ to miru to / ~ to miete
Meaning (EN)とみると: "on judging that ~ (someone immediately…)".
とみえて: "apparently ~ (judging from what one sees)".
Meaning (HI)とみると: यह भाँपते ही कि ~ (तुरंत…)।
とみえて: लगता है कि ~ (दिखने से अनुमान)
Meaning (GU)とみると: એ પારખતાં જ કે ~ (તરત…).
とみえて: લાગે છે કે ~ (દેખાવ પરથી અનુમાન)
Connection (接続)Plain form + とみると  |  Plain form + とみえて／とみえる
RegisterNeutral to written
Typical useとみると: someone else judges a situation and quickly acts (態度が悪くなった). とみえて: the speaker infers a reason from visible behaviour (何も言わずに帰ってしまった). Both use the quoting と + 見る.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb／い-adjplain + とみると買う気がないとみるとkau ki ga nai to miru to
な-adj／Noun (past)だった + とみえてショックだったとみえてshokku datta to miete
Verb(added, not in book) V plain + とみえて疲れているとみえてtsukarete iru to miete
い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj + とみえて眠いとみえてnemui to miete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q2) 私に買う気がないとみると、その店員は急に態度が悪くなった。Watashi ni kau ki ga nai to miru to, sono ten'in wa kyuu ni taido ga waruku natta. As soon as the clerk decided I had no intention of buying, his attitude suddenly turned rude. जैसे ही उस सेल्समैन ने भाँप लिया कि मेरा ख़रीदने का इरादा नहीं है, उसका रवैया अचानक बिगड़ गया। જેવું તે સેલ્સમેને પારખ્યું કે મારો ખરીદવાનો ઇરાદો નથી, તેનું વર્તન અચાનક બગડી ગયું.
(book: Q10) その話がショックだったとみえて、彼女は何も言わずに帰ってしまった。Sono hanashi ga shokku datta to miete, kanojo wa nani mo iwazu ni kaette shimatta. Apparently the story was a shock to her — she went home without saying a word. लगता है वह बात उसके लिए सदमा थी — वह बिना कुछ कहे घर चली गई। લાગે છે કે તે વાત તેના માટે આઘાત હતી — તે કંઈ બોલ્યા વગર ઘરે જતી રહી.
(added, not in book) 相手が弱いとみると、彼は急に強気になる。Aite ga yowai to miru to, kare wa kyuu ni tsuyoki ni naru. Once he sees his opponent is weak, he suddenly gets aggressive. जैसे ही उसे लगता है कि सामने वाला कमज़ोर है, वह अचानक आक्रामक हो जाता है। જેવું તેને લાગે કે સામેવાળો નબળો છે, તે અચાનક આક્રમક થઈ જાય છે.

The judgment is a quotation → と. Q2 offers を (気がないをみる is ungrammatical); Q10 offers が (ショックだったがみえて — not a pattern). This is the same quoting-と logic as ①'s とすると and といえども.

【3】 Nはさておき／Nはおろか

neutral register
ReadingN wa sateoki / N wa oroka
Meaning (EN)はさておき: "setting N aside (for now)".
はおろか: "not to mention N / let alone N" — N is the easier case, the main clause the more extreme one (usually negative).
Meaning (HI)はさておき: N को अभी एक तरफ़ रखकर।
はおろか: N तो दूर की बात, (उससे आसान चीज़ भी नहीं)
Meaning (GU)はさておき: N ને હમણાં બાજુ પર રાખીને.
はおろか: N તો દૂરની વાત, (તેનાથી સહેલી વસ્તુ પણ નહીં)
Connection (接続)N + は（さて）おき  |  N + はおろか、N2 も／さえ／まで〜ない
RegisterNeutral, slightly written
Typical useさておき: postpones a topic to focus on another (内容はさておき、表紙が…). おろか: "the bigger goal (ベスト4) — forget it; we didn't even reach the smaller one (ベスト16)".
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + はさておき内容はさておきnaiyou wa sateoki
NounN + はおろかベスト4はおろかbesuto fō wa oroka
Noun (variant)(added, not in book) 冗談はさておき冗談はさておきjoudan wa sateoki
Verb / adj(added, not in book) V-dict + こと + はおろか走ることはおろかhashiru koto wa oroka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q3) この本は、内容はさておき、表紙のデザインがきれいなので、つい手に取りたくなる。Kono hon wa, naiyou wa sateoki, hyoushi no dezain ga kirei na node, tsui te ni toritaku naru. Setting the contents aside, this book has such a pretty cover design that you just want to pick it up. सामग्री को एक तरफ़ रखें तो, इस किताब के कवर का डिज़ाइन इतना सुंदर है कि उठाने का मन कर जाता है। વિષયવસ્તુને બાજુ પર રાખીએ તો, આ પુસ્તકના કવરની ડિઝાઇન એટલી સુંદર છે કે ઉપાડવાનું મન થઈ જાય છે.
(book: Q9) 目標のベスト4はおろか、ベスト16にも入れなかった。Mokuhyou no besuto fō wa oroka, besuto shikkusutiin ni mo hairenakatta. Forget our goal of the top 4 — we couldn't even make the top 16. लक्ष्य टॉप 4 तो दूर की बात, हम टॉप 16 में भी नहीं पहुँच पाए। લક્ષ્ય ટોપ 4 તો દૂરની વાત, અમે ટોપ 16 માં પણ પહોંચી ન શક્યા.
(added, not in book) 彼は漢字はおろか、ひらがなも書けない。Kare wa kanji wa oroka, hiragana mo kakenai. He can't write hiragana, let alone kanji. वह कांजी तो दूर, हिरागाना भी नहीं लिख सकता। તે કાંજી તો દૂર, હિરાગાના પણ લખી શકતો નથી.

Both take the topic particle は: the speaker picks N up only to set it aside. を／が are wrong because さておく and おろか are fixed with は in these patterns.

【4】 ここぞとばかりに

neutral register
Readingkoko zo to bakari ni
Meaning (EN)"(seizing the moment) as if to say 'now's my chance!'"
Meaning (HI)"यही मौक़ा है!" मानो ऐसा सोचकर (मौक़ा लपकना)
Meaning (GU)"આ જ મોકો છે!" જાણે એમ વિચારીને (મોકો ઝડપવો)
Connection (接続)ここぞ + とばかりに + V  |  (generally) quote + とばかりに
RegisterNeutral, expressive
Typical useとばかりに = "as if saying ~" (a quoted thought + とばかり). ここぞ = "this is it". The person acts eagerly at an opportunity.
Word typeFormationExample
Set phraseここぞ + とばかりにここぞとばかりにkoko zo to bakari ni
Quote (verb)(added, not in book) V-volitional／command + とばかりに早く帰れとばかりにhayaku kaere to bakari ni
Noun(added, not in book) N + だ + とばかりにチャンスだとばかりにchansu da to bakari ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q5) 他の人がいなくなったので、私はここぞとばかりに、彼女に話しかけた。Hoka no hito ga inaku natta node, watashi wa koko zo to bakari ni, kanojo ni hanashikaketa. Since everyone else had left, I seized the chance and started talking to her. क्योंकि बाक़ी लोग चले गए थे, मैंने "यही मौक़ा है" सोचकर उससे बात शुरू कर दी। બાકીના લોકો જતા રહ્યા હોવાથી, મેં "આ જ મોકો છે" વિચારીને તેની સાથે વાત શરૂ કરી.
(added, not in book) 店員は早く帰れとばかりに、店の片付けを始めた。Ten'in wa hayaku kaere to bakari ni, mise no katazuke o hajimeta. As if to say "go home already", the staff started cleaning up the shop. मानो कह रहे हों "जल्दी जाओ", कर्मचारियों ने दुकान समेटना शुरू कर दिया। જાણે કહેતા હોય "જલ્દી જાઓ", કર્મચારીઓએ દુકાન સમેટવાનું શરૂ કર્યું.

The と is again a quotation marker — ここぞ is the "words" behind the action. ここぞにばかりに does not exist; don't confuse with 〜たばかりに ("simply because").

【5】 Nに先駆けて

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni sakigakete
Meaning (EN)"ahead of / prior to N (being first to do something)"
Meaning (HI)N से पहले (सबसे पहले कुछ करना)
Meaning (GU)N પહેલાં (સૌથી પહેલાં કંઈક કરવું)
Connection (接続)N(発売・開幕・他社) + に先駆けて
Register硬 — news, business
Typical useUsed when one action precedes an event or others (他社に先駆けて開発した = developed before rival companies did). Not for simple "before" in daily life — use 前に there.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (event)N + に先駆けてチーム発足に先駆けてchiimu hossoku ni sakigakete
Noun (rivals)(added, not in book) N + に先駆けて他社に先駆けてtasha ni sakigakete
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used — use V-dict + のに先駆けて (rare)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q6) チーム発足に先駆けて、公式ホームページを立ち上げた。Chiimu hossoku ni sakigakete, koushiki hoomupeeji o tachiageta. Ahead of the team's official launch, we set up its official website. टीम की स्थापना से पहले, आधिकारिक वेबसाइट शुरू कर दी गई। ટીમની સ્થાપના પહેલાં, સત્તાવાર વેબસાઇટ શરૂ કરી દેવામાં આવી.
(added, not in book) 全国に先駆けて、この町でごみの有料化が始まった。Zenkoku ni sakigakete, kono machi de gomi no yuuryouka ga hajimatta. Ahead of the rest of the country, this town began charging for garbage collection. देश के बाक़ी हिस्सों से पहले, इस शहर में कचरे पर शुल्क शुरू हुआ। દેશના બાકીના ભાગ કરતાં પહેલાં, આ શહેરમાં કચરા પર ફી શરૂ થઈ.

に is fixed (先駆ける = "to lead ahead of"). と is the distractor; it gives no meaning here.

【6】 〜とあれば

neutral register
Reading~ to areba
Meaning (EN)"if it's (a special case like) ~, then (I'll gladly / I must)…"
Meaning (HI)अगर (ऐसी ख़ास बात) ~ है, तो (ज़रूर)…
Meaning (GU)જો (આવી ખાસ વાત) ~ હોય, તો (જરૂર)…
Connection (接続)N／plain form + とあれば
RegisterSlightly formal
Typical useThe condition is a special reason that justifies an effort: 子どものためとあれば, 社長の命令とあれば. The main clause is often the speaker's willingness (〜たい, 〜する).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV plain + とあれば海外の友人が来るとあればkaigai no yuujin ga kuru to areba
Noun(added, not in book) N + とあれば子どものためとあればkodomo no tame to areba
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) plain + とあれば (rare)必要だとあればhitsuyou da to areba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q8) 海外の友人が来るとあれば、いまからプランを立てていろいろなところを案内したい。Kaigai no yuujin ga kuru to areba, ima kara puran o tatete iroiro na tokoro o annai shitai. If a friend from abroad is coming, I want to start planning now and show them around lots of places. अगर विदेश से कोई दोस्त आ रहा है, तो मैं अभी से योजना बनाकर उसे कई जगहें घुमाना चाहता हूँ। જો વિદેશથી કોઈ મિત્ર આવી રહ્યો હોય, તો હું અત્યારથી યોજના બનાવીને તેને ઘણી જગ્યાએ ફેરવવા માંગું છું.
(added, not in book) お客様のご要望とあれば、何でもいたします。Okyakusama no go-youbou to areba, nan demo itashimasu. If it's what the customer wishes, we'll do anything. अगर ग्राहक की इच्छा है, तो हम कुछ भी करेंगे। જો ગ્રાહકની ઇચ્છા હોય, તો અમે કંઈ પણ કરીશું.

Compare ①'s とあって (because it is ~, a natural result happened) with とあれば (if it is ~, I will…). Both need the quoting と; 来るがあれば is ungrammatical.

Q1

投票率が低いのは今回の選挙（ a に ／ b を ）限ったことではない。Touhyouritsu ga hikui no wa konkai no senkyo (a ni / b o) kagitta koto de wa nai.

Full sentence: 投票率が低いのは今回の選挙に限ったことではない。Touhyouritsu ga hikui no wa konkai no senkyo ni kagitta koto de wa nai.
EN: Low voter turnout is not limited to this election.
HI: कम मतदान सिर्फ़ इस चुनाव तक सीमित नहीं है।
GU: ઓછું મતદાન ફક્ત આ ચૂંટણી સુધી મર્યાદિત નથી.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the a/b pairs the book itself set against each other — the contrasts N1 likes to test.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nに限ったことではないN ni kagitta koto de wa nai Not just N N + に限ったことではない Scope: the issue is general. Problems that happen widely.
Nに限るN ni kagiru N is best N／V-dict + に限る Speaker's recommendation. 〜ならNに限る
Nを限りにN o kagiri ni As of N (last time) N(今日・今回) + を限りに End point; を, not に. Quitting, retiring, closing.
Nに限りN ni kagiri Only N (special) N + に限り (added, not in book) The trap behind Q4 — "only today". Special offers: 本日に限り
NはさておきN wa sateoki Setting N aside N + はさておき Postpone N, focus elsewhere. Changing focus.
NはおろかN wa oroka Let alone N N + はおろか、N2も〜ない N is the bigger case; usually negative. Can't even do the smaller thing.
〜とみると~ to miru to On judging that ~ plain + とみると Someone judges and acts at once. Quick reactions of others.
〜とみえて~ to miete Apparently ~ plain + とみえて Speaker's inference from what is seen. Guessing a reason from behaviour.
〜とあれば~ to areba If it's (special) ~ N／plain + とあれば Condition → willingness. "For X, I'd do anything."
Exam trap 1 — 限る has three faces: に限ったことではない (scope), に限る (best), を限りに (end point), plus に限り (only). Read the main clause: "anymore" → を限りに; recommendation → に限る; "it's everywhere" → に限ったことではない.
Exam trap 2 — quoting と before みる／ばかり／ある: とみると, とみえて, とばかりに, とあれば, とあって all take a plain clause + と. A blank right after a plain verb/adjective is almost never が or を.
