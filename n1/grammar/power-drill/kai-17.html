【1】 〜限りだ

neutral register
Reading~kagiri da
Meaning (EN)"extremely ~ / I feel so ~" (speaker's strong feeling)
Meaning (HI)बेहद ...लगता है (वक्ता की भावना)
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત ...લાગે છે (વક્તાની લાગણી)
Connection (接続)い-adj + 限りだ  |  な-adj-な + 限りだ
RegisterSlightly formal
Typical useOnly with feeling adjectives: うれしい, 寂しい, うらやましい, もったいない, 心強い, 残念な.

(book: 問題1 Q1) 毎日こんなに大量の食べ物が捨てられているなんて、もったいない限りだ。Mainichi konna ni tairyou no tabemono ga suterarete iru nante, mottainai kagiri da. That this much food is thrown away every day is such a terrible waste. हर दिन इतना सारा खाना फेंका जाता है — यह बेहद अफ़सोस की बर्बादी है। દરરોજ આટલું બધું ખાવાનું ફેંકી દેવાય છે — એ અત્યંત દુઃખદ બગાડ છે.
(added, not in book) 友達がみんな帰国してしまって、寂しい限りだ。Tomodachi ga minna kikoku shite shimatte, sabishii kagiri da. All my friends have gone back home; I feel so lonely. सारे दोस्त अपने देश लौट गए, बहुत अकेलापन लगता है। બધા મિત્રો પોતાના દેશ પાછા ગયા, ખૂબ એકલું લાગે છે.

至り (光栄の至り — takes nouns), 極み (感激の極み — nouns) and 始末 ("ended up in a bad state", after verbs) look similar but none follows an い-adjective like もったいない.

【2】 〜(よ)うと(も)

neutral register
Reading~(y)ou to (mo)
Meaning (EN)"no matter (what/how) ~" — concession with a question word
Meaning (HI)चाहे जो भी ...हो
Meaning (GU)ગમે તે ...થાય
Connection (接続)question word + V-volitional + と(も)
RegisterNeutral / written
Typical useどんな〜を〜ようと, 誰が〜ようと, 何と言われようと. The main clause doesn't change.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-volitional + と受けようとukeyou to
Verb (passive)(added, not in book) V-passive-volitional + と何と言われようとnan to iwareyou to
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-かろう + とどんなに寒かろうとdonna ni samukarou to
Noun(added, not in book) N + だろう／であろう + と誰であろうとdare de arou to
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 犯人がどんな罰を受けようと、被害者の心の傷はずっと残るだろう。Hannin ga donna batsu o ukeyou to, higaisha no kokoro no kizu wa zutto nokoru darou. Whatever punishment the criminal receives, the victim's emotional scars will probably remain forever. अपराधी को चाहे जो सज़ा मिले, पीड़ित के मन का घाव हमेशा बना रहेगा। ગુનેગારને ગમે તે સજા મળે, પીડિતના મનનો ઘા હંમેશાં રહેશે.
(added, not in book) 誰に何と言われようと、私は自分の道を行く。Dare ni nan to iwareyou to, watashi wa jibun no michi o iku. Whatever anyone says to me, I'll go my own way. कोई कुछ भी कहे, मैं अपने रास्ते चलूँगा। કોઈ ગમે તે કહે, હું મારા રસ્તે ચાલીશ.

どんな is the key. 受けたなら ("if he received") needs a consequence depending on it; 受けこそすれ needs a negative contrast; 受けたつもりでも ("even if he thinks he did") doesn't fit 罰.

硬 formal/written

Meaning (EN)"because of / due to ~" (formal cause)
Meaning (HI)...के कारण / चूँकि
Meaning (GU)...ના કારણે / કારણ કે
Connection (接続)plain form + (が)ゆえに  |  N + (の)ゆえに  |  な-adj／N + である + がゆえに
Register硬 — written
Typical use〜であるがゆえに is common in formal writing. Also attributive 〜ゆえの + N.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (である)N／な-adj + である + がゆえに未熟であるがゆえにmijuku de aru ga yue ni

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 彼女の演技は未熟であるがゆえに、今後の成長が楽しみだ。Kanojo no engi wa mijuku de aru ga yue ni, kongo no seichou ga tanoshimi da. Precisely because her acting is still immature, I look forward to seeing her grow. उसका अभिनय अभी कच्चा है, इसी कारण उसके आगे के विकास का इंतज़ार है। તેનો અભિનય હજી કાચો છે, એ જ કારણે તેના આગળના વિકાસની રાહ જોવાય છે.
(added, not in book) 若さゆえの過ちだった。Wakasa yue no ayamachi datta. It was a mistake due to youth. यह जवानी के कारण हुई ग़लती थी। આ યુવાનીને કારણે થયેલી ભૂલ હતી.

Trap: ながらも ("although immature") looks natural, but 〜であるが + ながらも is not grammatical — the blank follows であるが, which only ゆえに can take. Meaning: immaturity = room to grow. よそに and ばかりに (negative result) don't fit 楽しみだ.

Typical useモデルになったつもり, 旅行したつもりで貯金する; also 〜たつもりだ ("I believe I did").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + つもりなったつもりnatta tsumori

(book: 問題1 Q4) 妹はさっきから鏡の前でポーズをとって、モデルにでもなったつもりだろうか。Imouto wa sakki kara kagami no mae de poozu o totte, moderu ni de mo natta tsumori darou ka. My little sister has been posing in front of the mirror for a while — does she think she's become a model or something? मेरी छोटी बहन काफ़ी देर से आईने के सामने पोज़ दे रही है — क्या ख़ुद को मॉडल समझ बैठी है? મારી નાની બહેન ક્યારની અરીસા સામે પોઝ આપે છે — શું પોતાને મૉડેલ સમજી બેઠી છે?
(added, not in book) 外食したつもりで、そのお金を貯金した。Gaishoku shita tsumori de, sono okane o chokin shita. Pretending I'd eaten out, I saved that money. मानो बाहर खाना खा लिया हो, यह सोचकर मैंने वह पैसा बचा लिया। જાણે બહાર જમી લીધું હોય એમ સમજીને મેં એ પૈસા બચાવ્યા.

ふり ("pretend") needs someone to deceive and would be 〜ふりをしている; もの ("(one) did used to") and ごとく ("like") don't fit 〜だろうか. つもり = her own illusion.

neutral register

Meaning (EN)"just N or so" — a small, rough amount (1カ月や2カ月)

Meaning (GU)બસ N-બે N (થોડું)
Connection (接続)number + counter + や + next number + counter + で(は)
RegisterNeutral, conversational
Typical useDownplays an amount as insufficient: 1日や2日で, 一人や二人, 1万や2万.

NumberN1 や N21カ月や2カ月ikkagetsu ya nikagetsu

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 日本語の勉強を始めて1カ月や2カ月では、新聞を読むのは難しい。Nihongo no benkyou o hajimete ikkagetsu ya nikagetsu de wa, shinbun o yomu no wa muzukashii. After just a month or two of studying Japanese, reading a newspaper is hard. जापानी पढ़ना शुरू किए बस एक-दो महीने में अख़बार पढ़ना मुश्किल है। જાપાની શીખવાનું શરૂ કર્યાને બસ એક-બે મહિનામાં છાપું વાંચવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
(added, not in book) 一度や二度の失敗であきらめるな。Ichido ya nido no shippai de akirameru na. Don't give up after failing once or twice. एक-दो बार की नाकामी से हार मत मानो। એક-બે વારની નિષ્ફળતાથી હાર ન માનો.

と would mean exactly "one month and two months"; すら ("even") and なら ("if") don't connect two numbers. や gives the vague "or so" nuance plus では ("with only that much").

(book: 問題1 Q6) 部下の苦労を知りもしないで部長はまた方針を変えた。Buka no kurou o shiri mo shinai de buchou wa mata houshin o kaeta. Without even knowing his staff's hardships, the department head changed the policy again. अपने अधीनस्थों की मेहनत जाने बिना ही विभाग प्रमुख ने फिर से नीति बदल दी। પોતાના હાથ નીચેના લોકોની મહેનત જાણ્યા વગર જ વિભાગના વડાએ ફરી નીતિ બદલી.
(added, not in book) 説明書を読みもしないで、使い方がわからないと言う。Setsumeisho o yomi mo shinai de, tsukaikata ga wakaranai to iu. He says he doesn't know how to use it without even reading the manual. निर्देश पढ़े बिना ही कहता है कि इस्तेमाल करना नहीं आता। સૂચનાઓ વાંચ્યા વગર જ કહે છે કે વાપરતાં નથી આવડતું.

知ろうにも ("want to know but can't") needs a negative ending; 知ったところで ("even if he knew, it's useless"); 知らないなりに ("in his own ignorant way") doesn't criticise.

Exam trap 1 — look at what's right before the blank: Q3 has 未熟であるが＿, and only がゆえに can follow が. ながらも feels right in meaning ("immature, but promising") but can't attach to であるが. Always test the connection before the meaning.
Exam trap 2 — 思いきや vs 思いつつ: both start with と思い-. 思いきや = "I thought…, but reality was different" (expectation broken); 思いつつ = "while thinking…" (the thought continues). In 問題3 [4] the boss's surprising reaction (あっさり許可) shows the expectation was broken.
