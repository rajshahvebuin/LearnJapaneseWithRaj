【1】 Nじゃあるまいし

casual spoken
ReadingN ja arumai shi
Meaning (EN)"it's not as if ~, so…" — dismisses an assumption, then criticises or advises
Meaning (HI)कोई ... तो नहीं है, (फिर क्यों / इसलिए)…
Meaning (GU)કંઈ ... તો નથી, (તો પછી કેમ / એટલે)…
Connection (接続)N + じゃあるまいし (written: ではあるまいし)  |  V-plain + わけじゃあるまいし
RegisterSpoken, casual; the main clause is a criticism, a command or a judgement
Typical use子どもじゃあるまいし、一人で行けるでしょう。 Not used in polite requests to superiors.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + じゃあるまいし真夏じゃあるまいしmanatsu ja arumai shi
Noun (written)(added, not in book) N + ではあるまいし子どもではあるまいしkodomo de wa arumai shi
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + わけじゃあるまいし一生会えないわけじゃあるまいしisshou aenai wake ja arumai shi
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) adj + わけじゃあるまいし高いわけじゃあるまいしtakai wake ja arumai shi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 真夏じゃあるまいし、こんな寒い日にTシャツ1枚で出かけるなんて、どうかしてるよ。Manatsu ja arumai shi, konna samui hi ni T-shatsu ichimai de dekakeru nante, dou ka shiteru yo. It's not as if it's midsummer — going out in just a T-shirt on a cold day like this, you must be crazy. कोई भरी गर्मी तो नहीं है — इतने ठंडे दिन सिर्फ़ एक टी-शर्ट में बाहर जाना, तुम्हारा दिमाग़ ख़राब है क्या। કંઈ ભર ઉનાળો તો નથી — આવા ઠંડા દિવસે ફક્ત એક ટી-શર્ટમાં બહાર જવું, તારું મગજ ઠેકાણે નથી.
(added, not in book) 子どもじゃあるまいし、自分のことは自分でしなさい。Kodomo ja arumai shi, jibun no koto wa jibun de shinasai. You're not a child — take care of your own things. तुम कोई बच्चे तो नहीं हो, अपना काम ख़ुद करो। તું કંઈ બાળક તો નથી, તારું કામ જાતે કર.

あるまい is the negative conjecture "surely is not", and し gives the reason. The 〜なんて、どうかしてるよ criticism is the clue. とばかりに ("as if to say") needs a gesture that shows a feeling; もかまわず ("without caring about") follows something one ignores; のことだから ("knowing N, (surely)") predicts from a person's character.

【2】 〜ったらありゃしない

casual spoken
Reading~ttara arya shinai
Meaning (EN)"so ~ it's unbelievable / incredibly ~" — usually a complaint
Meaning (HI)इतना ... कि हद है / बेहद ...
Meaning (GU)એટલું ... કે હદ છે / અત્યંત ...
Connection (接続)い-adj／な-adj (stem) + ったらありゃしない (= といったらない)
RegisterVery casual spoken; mostly negative feelings (汚い, うるさい, 腹が立つ)
Typical useEmotional exaggeration about a situation. Neutral equivalent: 〜といったらない.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj + ったらありゃしない汚いったらありゃしないkitanai ttara arya shinai
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + ったらありゃしない面倒ったらありゃしないmendou ttara arya shinai

Neutral form(added, not in book) い-adj + といったらない悔しいといったらないkuyashii to ittara nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 弟の部屋は何か月も掃除していないから、汚いったらありゃしない。Otouto no heya wa nankagetsu mo souji shite inai kara, kitanai ttara arya shinai. My younger brother hasn't cleaned his room for months, so it's unbelievably filthy. छोटे भाई ने महीनों से कमरा साफ़ नहीं किया, इसलिए गंदगी की हद है। નાના ભાઈએ મહિનાઓથી રૂમ સાફ કર્યો નથી, એટલે ગંદકીની હદ છે.
(added, not in book) 隣の工事がうるさいったらありゃしない。Tonari no kouji ga urusai ttara arya shinai. The construction next door is incredibly noisy. पड़ोस का निर्माण-कार्य बेहद शोर करता है। બાજુનું બાંધકામ અત્યંત ઘોંઘાટ કરે છે.

ありゃしない = ありはしない, "there is nothing (like it)". The 〜ったら already in the sentence sets it up. きりがない ("endless") follows conditionals (言い出したらきりがない); もんじゃない ("you shouldn't") follows V-dict; ものともしない needs Nを.

neutral register
Reading~mono yara
Meaning (EN)"I wonder ~ (I have no idea)" — puzzled, at a loss
Meaning (HI)पता नहीं ... (समझ नहीं आता)
Meaning (GU)ખબર નથી ... (સમજાતું નથી)
Connection (接続)question word … V-plain／V-たらいい + ものやら
RegisterNeutral, slightly literary; sentence-final
Typical useWith 何・どう・いつ・どこ: 何から始めたらいいものやら, どう説明したものやら.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbQ-word … V-たらいい + ものやら何から始めたらいいものやらnani kara hajimetara ii mono yara

い-adj(added, not in book) Q-word … い-adj + ものやらどれほど痛いものやらdore hodo itai mono yara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 引っ越しの準備をしなければならないが、いったい何から始めたらいいものやら。Hikkoshi no junbi o shinakereba naranai ga, ittai nani kara hajimetara ii mono yara. I have to get ready for the move, but where on earth should I start, I wonder. घर बदलने की तैयारी करनी है, पर पता नहीं आख़िर कहाँ से शुरू करूँ। ઘર બદલવાની તૈયારી કરવાની છે, પણ ખબર નથી આખરે ક્યાંથી શરૂ કરું.
(added, not in book) この気持ちをどう伝えたものやら。Kono kimochi o dou tsutaeta mono yara. How should I put this feeling into words, I wonder. पता नहीं यह भावना कैसे बताऊँ। ખબર નથી આ લાગણી કેવી રીતે કહું.

いったい + 何 is the signal for an "I wonder" ending. ものを ("if only… but") is regret; ものでもない ("it's not impossible that") is a guarded affirmation; ものともなく is not a real pattern.

neutral register
ReadingN nara de wa no
Meaning (EN)"unique to N / that only N can offer" — praise
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ N की ख़ास / N में ही मिलने वाला
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત N ની ખાસિયત / N માં જ મળતું
Connection (接続)N + ならではの + N  |  N + ならではだ
RegisterNeutral; common in advertising and tourism
Typical usePositive evaluation of a place, person or season: この店ならではの味, 京都ならではの風景.

NounN + ならではの + Nこの地方ならではの料理kono chihou nara de wa no ryouri

Verb / Adj(added, not in book) not used — noun only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) 海に近いこの地方ならではの新鮮な魚料理を、どうぞお楽しみください。Umi ni chikai kono chihou nara de wa no shinsen na sakana ryouri o, douzo o-tanoshimi kudasai. Please enjoy the fresh fish dishes that only this region near the sea can offer. समुद्र के पास के इस इलाक़े की ख़ास ताज़ी मछली के व्यंजनों का आनंद लीजिए। દરિયા પાસેના આ પ્રદેશની ખાસ તાજી માછલીની વાનગીઓનો આનંદ માણો.
(added, not in book) 手作りならではの温かみがある。Tezukuri nara de wa no atatakami ga aru. It has a warmth that only handmade things have. इसमें हाथ से बनी चीज़ों वाली ख़ास गर्माहट है। એમાં હાથબનાવટની ખાસ હૂંફ છે.

のごとき ("like N") compares rather than praising uniqueness; なくしての ("without N") needs a negative follow-up (努力なくしての成功はない); にあっての ("being in N") describes circumstances, not a specialty.

Typical useJudging the situation, then acting at once: チャンスとみると, 不利とみるや.

NounN + とみると美人とみるとbijin to miru to

(book: 問題1 Q5) 彼は美人とみると、必ず声をかけるような男だ。Kare wa bijin to miru to, kanarazu koe o kakeru you na otoko da. He's the kind of man who always chats up any woman he sees is beautiful. वह ऐसा आदमी है कि कोई सुंदर लड़की दिखी नहीं कि ज़रूर बात करने लगता है। એ એવો માણસ છે કે કોઈ સુંદર છોકરી દેખાઈ નથી કે જરૂર વાત કરવા લાગે છે.
(added, not in book) 相手が弱いとみるや、彼は一気に攻めた。Aite ga yowai to miru ya, kare wa ikki ni semeta. As soon as he saw that his opponent was weak, he attacked all at once. जैसे ही उसने देखा कि सामने वाला कमज़ोर है, उसने एकदम हमला कर दिया। જેવું તેણે જોયું કે સામેવાળો નબળો છે, તેણે એકદમ હુમલો કર્યો.

必ず + 〜ような男 shows a habitual reaction to a judgement → とみると. といえども ("even though") is concessive; とみえて ("it seems that") infers a cause; とばかりに ("as if to say") describes a gesture.

Nじゃあるまいしja arumai shi It's not as if N N + じゃあるまいし Rejects an assumption, then criticises. Scolding / advice in speech.

〜ものやら／〜ものでもないmono yara / mono demo nai I wonder / it's not impossible Q-word … V + ものやら ／ V-ない + ものでもない Puzzlement vs hedged affirmation. Not knowing how to start.

Exam trap 1 — て以来 + voice: 以来 only takes the て-form, so two options go immediately. Then ask who did the action: someone else sighed at the writer → passive 出されて.
Exam trap 2 — the もの family: ものやら (I wonder), ものを (if only), ものでもない (not impossible), ものだから (excuse), ものの (although), ものともせず (undaunted). A question word (何・どう) before the blank points to ものやら.
