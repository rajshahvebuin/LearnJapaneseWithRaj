【1】 Nときたら／Nといったら（ない）

casual/spoken register
ReadingN to kitara / N to ittara (nai)
Meaning (EN)ときたら: "as for N… (complaint / exasperation)".
といったらない: "the N is beyond words / incredibly ~".
Meaning (HI)ときたら: N की तो बात ही क्या… (शिकायत / झुंझलाहट)।
といったらない: N इतना ज़्यादा कि कहा नहीं जा सकता
Meaning (GU)ときたら: N ની તો વાત જ શું… (ફરિયાદ / ચીડ).
といったらない: N એટલું બધું કે કહી ન શકાય
Connection (接続)N + ときたら  |  N(〜さ) + といったら（ない／なかった）
RegisterCasual / spoken (both)
Typical useときたら introduces a topic the speaker is fed up with (うちの子, 最近の若者); the comment is negative. といったらない usually follows an 〜さ noun (多さ, 寒さ, うれしさ) and stresses an extreme degree, good or bad.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (topic)N + ときたらうちの子ときたらuchi no ko to kitara
Noun (〜さ)い-adj stem + さ + といったら人の多さといったらhito no oosa to ittara
い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj + といったらない悔しいといったらないkuyashii to ittara nai
Verb(added, not in book) not used (attaches to a noun topic)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q1) うちの子ときたら、30歳にもなって料理ひとつ作れないんです。Uchi no ko to kitara, sanjussai ni mo natte ryouri hitotsu tsukurenai n desu. As for my kid — 30 years old and can't cook a single dish. मेरे बच्चे की तो बात ही मत पूछो — 30 साल का हो गया और एक भी व्यंजन नहीं बना सकता। મારા બાળકની તો વાત જ ન પૂછો — 30 વર્ષનો થઈ ગયો અને એક પણ વાનગી બનાવી શકતો નથી.
(book: Q8) 今日の駅前の人の多さといったらなかったよ。何かあったのかな。Kyou no ekimae no hito no oosa to ittara nakatta yo. Nani ka atta no ka na. The crowd in front of the station today was unbelievable. I wonder if something happened. आज स्टेशन के सामने इतनी भीड़ थी कि कहा नहीं जा सकता। पता नहीं कुछ हुआ था क्या। આજે સ્ટેશનની સામે એટલી ભીડ હતી કે કહી ન શકાય. ખબર નહીં કંઈ થયું હતું કે શું.
(added, not in book) 部長ときたら、自分のミスを部下のせいにするんだから。Buchou to kitara, jibun no misu o buka no sei ni suru n dakara. Honestly, our manager — he blames his own mistakes on his staff. हमारे मैनेजर की तो बात ही क्या — अपनी ग़लती अपने मातहतों पर डाल देते हैं। અમારા મેનેજરની તો વાત જ શું — પોતાની ભૂલ પોતાના હાથ નીચેના લોકો પર નાખી દે છે.

Both start with the quoting と — the speaker "brings up" N. ときたら always carries annoyance; といったらない is just "extreme". In Q1, が would make うちの子 the subject of きたら ("came"), which is nonsense; in Q8 を cannot introduce a quoted topic.

【2】 Nに至る／Nにあるまじき

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni itaru / N ni aru majiki
Meaning (EN)に至る: "to reach / come to (a stage, a result)" — 発足に至った経緯 = how it came to be founded.
にあるまじき: "unbecoming of N (a role)".
Meaning (HI)に至る: (किसी चरण / नतीजे) तक पहुँचना।
にあるまじき: N (पद) के लिए अशोभनीय
Meaning (GU)に至る: (કોઈ તબક્કા / પરિણામ) સુધી પહોંચવું.
にあるまじき: N (પદ) માટે અશોભનીય
Connection (接続)N／V-dict + に至る（至った）  |  N(role) + にあるまじき + N
Register硬 — formal speech, news
Typical useに至る: 〜に至った経緯／理由, 〜に至るまで (even down to ~). にあるまじき: always followed by a noun (発言, 行為).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (stage)N + に至った発足に至った経緯hossoku ni itatta keii
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict + に至る倒産するに至ったtousan suru ni itatta
Noun (role)N + にあるまじき + N国会議員にあるまじき発言kokkai giin ni aru majiki hatsugen
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q2) この会が発足に至った経緯をご説明いたします。Kono kai ga hossoku ni itatta keii o go-setsumei itashimasu. I will explain how this association came to be founded. मैं बताता हूँ कि यह संस्था किन परिस्थितियों में स्थापित हुई। હું સમજાવું છું કે આ સંસ્થા કયા સંજોગોમાં સ્થપાઈ.
(book: Q10) A氏は国会議員にあるまじき発言をして、各方面から非難を浴びている。A-shi wa kokkai giin ni aru majiki hatsugen o shite, kaku houmen kara hinan o abite iru. Mr. A made remarks unbecoming of a Diet member and is being criticised from all sides. श्री A ने संसद सदस्य के लिए अशोभनीय बयान दिया और हर तरफ़ से आलोचना झेल रहे हैं। શ્રી A એ સંસદ સભ્ય માટે અશોભનીય નિવેદન આપ્યું અને દરેક બાજુથી ટીકાનો ભોગ બની રહ્યા છે.
(added, not in book) 話し合いを重ね、ようやく合意に至った。Hanashiai o kasane, youyaku goui ni itatta. After many discussions, we finally reached an agreement. कई बार चर्चा के बाद, आख़िरकार सहमति बन गई। અનેક વાર ચર્ચા કર્યા પછી, આખરે સહમતિ સધાઈ.

Both are に-patterns: 至る is "arrive at", so the destination takes に. Q10 offers と — but とあるまじき is not a pattern (only としてあるまじき, with して).

【3】 Nを踏まえて／Nを皮切りに

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o fumaete / N o kawakiri ni
Meaning (EN)を踏まえて: "taking N into account / building on N".
を皮切りに: "starting with N (and then many more)".
Meaning (HI)を踏まえて: N को ध्यान में रखते हुए / N के आधार पर।
を皮切りに: N से शुरुआत करके (और फिर कई)
Meaning (GU)を踏まえて: N ને ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને / N ના આધારે.
を皮切りに: N થી શરૂઆત કરીને (અને પછી ઘણા)
Connection (接続)N(事例・経験・結果) + を踏まえて  |  N(first place/event) + を皮切りに（して）
RegisterFormal — business, reports, news
Typical useを踏まえて: N is past information you base a decision on; the main clause is a plan (考えていきたい, 決定する). を皮切りに: N is the first in a series; the main clause shows the series spreading (各地で, 次々と).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (basis)N + を踏まえて様々な事例を踏まえてsamazama na jirei o fumaete
Noun (first item)N + を皮切りに東京を皮切りにToukyou o kawakiri ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + の + を皮切りにデビューしたのを皮切りにdebyuu shita no o kawakiri ni
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q3) これまでの様々な事例を踏まえて、解決策を考えていきたい。Kore made no samazama na jirei o fumaete, kaiketsusaku o kangaete ikitai. Building on the various cases so far, I want to work out a solution. अब तक के विभिन्न मामलों को ध्यान में रखते हुए, मैं समाधान सोचना चाहता हूँ। અત્યાર સુધીના વિવિધ કિસ્સાઓને ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને, હું ઉકેલ વિચારવા માંગું છું.
(book: Q5) コンサートは東京を皮切りに、世界10都市で行われる予定だ。Konsaato wa Toukyou o kawakiri ni, sekai juu toshi de okonawareru yotei da. Starting with Tokyo, the concert is scheduled to be held in 10 cities around the world. टोक्यो से शुरुआत करके, यह कॉन्सर्ट दुनिया के 10 शहरों में होने वाला है। ટોક્યોથી શરૂઆત કરીને, આ કોન્સર્ટ વિશ્વના 10 શહેરોમાં યોજાવાનો છે.
(added, not in book) アンケートの結果を踏まえて、商品を改良した。Ankeeto no kekka o fumaete, shouhin o kairyou shita. Based on the survey results, we improved the product. सर्वे के नतीजों के आधार पर, हमने उत्पाद में सुधार किया। સર્વેના પરિણામોના આધારે, અમે ઉત્પાદનમાં સુધારો કર્યો.

Both take を: 踏まえる ("step on firmly") is transitive, and 皮切り ("first cut") is treated as "using N as the opening". Q3's に and Q5's が are distractors — 東京が皮切りに is not a pattern (東京が皮切りだ would be).

【4】 Nもさることながら

硬 formal/written
ReadingN mo saru koto nagara
Meaning (EN)"N is of course (good/important), but even more so…" — N1 add-on pattern.
Meaning (HI)N तो है ही, लेकिन उससे भी बढ़कर…
Meaning (GU)N તો છે જ, પણ તેનાથી પણ વધુ…
Connection (接続)N + もさることながら、N2 も／が…
Register硬 — written, polished speech
Typical useThe first N is admitted as obviously true; the second item gets the emphasis (患者への対応がとてもよい). Usually positive evaluations.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + もさることながら技術もさることながらgijutsu mo saru koto nagara
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used (attaches to a noun)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q4) この歯医者は技術もさることながら、患者への対応がとてもよい。Kono haisha wa gijutsu mo saru koto nagara, kanja e no taiou ga totemo yoi. This dentist's skill is of course excellent, but the way he treats patients is even better. इस दंत चिकित्सक की तकनीक तो अच्छी है ही, मरीज़ों के साथ उनका व्यवहार भी बहुत अच्छा है। આ દાંતના ડૉક્ટરની ટેક્નિક તો સારી છે જ, દર્દીઓ સાથેનું તેમનું વર્તન પણ ખૂબ સારું છે.
(added, not in book) この店は味もさることながら、雰囲気がすばらしい。Kono mise wa aji mo saru koto nagara, fun'iki ga subarashii. This restaurant's food is great, of course, but the atmosphere is wonderful too. इस दुकान का स्वाद तो अच्छा है ही, माहौल भी शानदार है। આ દુકાનનો સ્વાદ તો સારો છે જ, વાતાવરણ પણ શાનદાર છે.

The も is part of the pattern ("N, too, goes without saying"). が would turn 技術 into the subject of さる (leave), which gives no meaning.

【5】 N1とN2とが相まって

硬 formal/written
ReadingN1 to N2 to ga aimatte
Meaning (EN)"N1 and N2 combine (to produce a result)"
Meaning (HI)N1 और N2 मिलकर (एक नतीजा पैदा करते हैं)
Meaning (GU)N1 અને N2 ભેગા મળીને (એક પરિણામ લાવે છે)
Connection (接続)N1 と N2 (と)が相まって  |  N1 が N2 と相まって
Register硬 — written, reviews, reports
Typical useTwo factors work together → a strong effect (素晴らしい作品, 大ヒット). 相まって is intransitive, so the factors are the subject: が.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + NounN1とN2と + が相まって映像と音楽とが相まってeizou to ongaku to ga aimatte
Noun (variant)(added, not in book) N1 が N2 と相まって才能が努力と相まってsainou ga doryoku to aimatte
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q6) 美しい映像と音楽とが相まって、素晴らしい作品に仕上がった。Utsukushii eizou to ongaku to ga aimatte, subarashii sakuhin ni shiagatta. The beautiful visuals and the music combined to make it a wonderful work. सुंदर दृश्यों और संगीत ने मिलकर इसे एक शानदार कृति बना दिया। સુંદર દૃશ્યો અને સંગીત ભેગા મળીને તે એક શાનદાર કૃતિ બની.
(added, not in book) 好天と連休が相まって、観光地はどこも人であふれた。Kouten to renkyuu ga aimatte, kankouchi wa doko mo hito de afureta. Good weather combined with the long weekend, so every tourist spot overflowed with people. अच्छे मौसम और लंबी छुट्टियों के कारण, हर पर्यटन स्थल लोगों से भरा हुआ था। સારા હવામાન અને લાંબી રજાઓને કારણે, દરેક પ્રવાસન સ્થળ લોકોથી ભરાઈ ગયું.

Remember: 相まる = "go together" (intransitive) → が. とを would need a transitive verb. The second と (音楽と) is optional; the が is not.

【6】 Nを禁じ得ない

硬 formal/written
ReadingN o kinjienai
Meaning (EN)"cannot help feeling N (anger, tears, surprise)"
Meaning (HI)N (ग़ुस्सा, आँसू, हैरानी) को रोक नहीं पाना
Meaning (GU)N (ગુસ્સો, આંસુ, આશ્ચર્ય) ને રોકી ન શકવું
Connection (接続)N(怒り・涙・驚き・同情) + を禁じ得ない
Register硬 — written, formal
Typical useOnly with emotion nouns. 禁じる is transitive ("forbid"), 得ない = "cannot": literally "cannot forbid (myself) N".
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (emotion)N + を禁じ得ない怒りを禁じ得なかったikari o kinjienakatta
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used — use 〜ずにはいられない instead—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q7) 担当者の対応のあまりのひどさに、怒りを禁じ得なかった。Tantousha no taiou no amari no hidosa ni, ikari o kinjienakatta. The staff member's handling was so terrible that I couldn't help feeling angry. ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति का व्यवहार इतना बुरा था कि मैं अपना ग़ुस्सा रोक नहीं पाया। જવાબદાર વ્યક્તિનું વર્તન એટલું ખરાબ હતું કે હું મારો ગુસ્સો રોકી ન શક્યો.
(added, not in book) 被災地の映像を見て、涙を禁じ得なかった。Hisaichi no eizou o mite, namida o kinjienakatta. Watching footage of the disaster area, I couldn't hold back my tears. आपदा-ग्रस्त इलाक़े के दृश्य देखकर, मैं अपने आँसू रोक नहीं पाया। આપત્તિગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તારના દૃશ્યો જોઈને, હું મારા આંસુ રોકી ન શક્યો.

The 〜に before the blank (ひどさに) is the cause; the emotion itself needs を as the object of 禁じる. That's why に is offered as a trap.

【7】 Nはどうあれ

neutral register
ReadingN wa dou are
Meaning (EN)"whatever N may be / regardless of N"
Meaning (HI)N जो भी हो / N चाहे जैसा हो
Meaning (GU)N ગમે તે હોય / N ગમે તેવું હોય
Connection (接続)N + はどうあれ  |  (理由・結果)はどうあれ
RegisterSlightly formal
Typical useN is something unknown or not yet decided (評価, 結果, 理由). The main clause states what matters regardless. Variant: 〜がどうであれ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + はどうあれ評価はどうあれhyouka wa dou are
Noun (variant)(added, not in book) N + がどうであれ結果がどうであれkekka ga dou de are
Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: Q9) やっとレポートが提出できた！評価はどうあれ、期限内に出せてよかった。Yatto repooto ga teishutsu dekita! Hyouka wa dou are, kigen-nai ni dasete yokatta. I finally handed in my report! Whatever the grade, I'm glad I got it in on time. आख़िरकार रिपोर्ट जमा हो गई! नंबर जो भी आएँ, समय पर जमा कर पाया यह अच्छा हुआ। આખરે રિપોર્ટ જમા થઈ ગયો! માર્ક્સ ગમે તે આવે, સમયસર જમા કરી શક્યો એ સારું થયું.
(added, not in book) 理由はどうあれ、暴力は許されない。Riyuu wa dou are, bouryoku wa yurusarenai. Whatever the reason, violence is not acceptable. कारण जो भी हो, हिंसा माफ़ नहीं की जा सकती। કારણ ગમે તે હોય, હિંસા માફ કરી શકાય નહીં.

The book's answer is は (contrastive topic: "as for the grade, however it turns out"). も would give "the grade too", which has no partner item in the sentence.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the a/b pairs the book itself set against each other — the contrasts N1 likes to test.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
NときたらN to kitara As for N (fed up) N + ときたら Topic + complaint; casual. Grumbling about someone/something.
NといったらないN to ittara nai Indescribably ~ N(〜さ)／い-adj + といったらない Extreme degree, positive or negative. 人の多さといったらない
Nに至るN ni itaru Reach N (a stage) N／V-dict + に至る Intransitive → に. How something came about.
Nを踏まえてN o fumaete Based on N N + を踏まえて Transitive → を; past info as a basis. Plans built on experience/data.
Nに基づいてN ni motozuite Based on N N + に基づいて (added, not in book) Same meaning, but に — easy to mix up. Data, rules, facts as a basis.
Nを皮切りにN o kawakiri ni Starting with N N + を皮切りに First in a series that spreads. Tours, launches, events.
NもさることながらN mo saru koto nagara N of course, and more so… N + もさることながら も is fixed; stress on the second item. Praising two qualities.
NとNとが相まってN to N to ga aimatte N and N combine N1とN2と + が相まって Intransitive → が, not を. Two factors → strong effect.
Nを禁じ得ないN o kinjienai Can't help feeling N N(emotion) + を禁じ得ない Transitive → を; emotion nouns only. Anger, tears, surprise.
Exam trap 1 — transitivity decides を vs に／が: if the fixed verb is transitive (踏まえる, 禁じる, 皮切りにする) the noun takes を; if it is intransitive (至る, 相まる) it takes に／が. When unsure, ask "can this verb have an object?".
Exam trap 2 — quoting と hides inside patterns: ときたら, といったらない, といえども, とあって all start with と. A blank directly before き／い／あ is often a と.
