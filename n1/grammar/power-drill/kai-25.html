【1】 〜ことにして

neutral register
Reading~koto ni shite
Meaning (EN)"pretending that ~ / treating it as if ~ (though it isn't true)"
Meaning (HI)...होने का बहाना बनाकर / ...मानकर
Meaning (GU)...હોવાનું બહાનું કરીને / ...માનીને
Connection (接続)V-た／V-ない／Nという + ことにする・ことにして
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAn arranged fiction: 病気になったことにして, 聞かなかったことにする.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + ことにして病気になったことにしてbyouki ni natta koto ni shite
Verb (neg)(added, not in book) V-なかった + ことにする聞かなかったことにするkikanakatta koto ni suru
Noun(added, not in book) N + という + ことにして出張ということにしてshucchou to iu koto ni shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 病気になったことにして会社を休んで遊びに行った。Byouki ni natta koto ni shite kaisha o yasunde asobi ni itta. I pretended I was sick, took the day off work and went out to have fun. बीमार होने का बहाना बनाकर मैंने दफ़्तर से छुट्टी ली और घूमने चला गया। બીમાર હોવાનું બહાનું કરીને મેં ઑફિસમાંથી રજા લીધી અને ફરવા ગયો.
(added, not in book) 今の話は聞かなかったことにしておきます。Ima no hanashi wa kikanakatta koto ni shite okimasu. I'll pretend I didn't hear what you just said. अभी जो कहा, मैं मान लूँगा कि मैंने सुना ही नहीं। હમણાં જે કહ્યું, હું માની લઈશ કે મેં સાંભળ્યું જ નથી.

つもりで ("with the intention / feeling as if") describes one's own mindset (死んだつもりで頑張る); かのごとき needs a noun after it; ことだから gives a judgement from character. Faking an excuse → ことにして.

硬 formal/written

Meaning (GU)અત્યંત ... / હદ બહારનું ...

な-adj(added, not in book) な-adj stem + 極まる失礼極まるshitsurei kiwamaru
い-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — compare N + の極み贅沢の極みzeitaku no kiwami
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) この小説は面白味に欠ける。発想からして平凡極まりない。Kono shousetsu wa omoshiromi ni kakeru. Hassou kara shite heibon kiwamarinai. This novel lacks interest. Even its basic idea is utterly ordinary. यह उपन्यास दिलचस्प नहीं है। इसकी मूल कल्पना ही बेहद साधारण है। આ નવલકથામાં રસ નથી. એની મૂળ કલ્પના જ અત્યંત સામાન્ય છે.
(added, not in book) 夜中に電話してくるなんて、非常識極まりない。Yonaka ni denwa shite kuru nante, hijoushiki kiwamarinai. Calling in the middle of the night is utterly inconsiderate. आधी रात को फ़ोन करना बेहद असभ्य है। અડધી રાતે ફોન કરવો અત્યંત અસભ્ય છે.

Connection decides it: 平凡 (な-adj stem) + 極まりない directly. 極みだ and 至りだ need の (贅沢の極み, 光栄の至り); じゃあるまい ("surely it isn't") contradicts 面白味に欠ける.

【3】 〜たら〜たで

neutral register
Reading~tara ~ta de
Meaning (EN)"if/when ~ does happen, then (there's another problem / it's fine in its own way)"
Meaning (HI)अगर ... हो भी जाए, तो (उसकी अपनी परेशानी)
Meaning (GU)જો ... થાય તો પણ, (એની પોતાની તકલીફ)
Connection (接続)V-たら + same V-たで  |  い-adj-かったら〜かったで  |  な-adj-なら〜で
RegisterNeutral, conversational
Typical useDamned if you do, damned if you don't: 行かないと怒られ、行ったら行ったで責められる.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-たら + V-たで行ったら行ったでittara itta de
い-adj(added, not in book) い-かったら + い-かったで広かったら広かったでhirokattara hirokatta de
な-adj(added, not in book) な-adj なら + な-adj で暇なら暇でhima nara hima de
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 行かないと文句を言われるし、行ったら行ったで責められるし、どうすればいいんだ。Ikanai to monku o iwareru shi, ittara itta de semerareru shi, dou sureba ii n da. If I don't go, they complain; if I do go, they blame me anyway — what am I supposed to do? न जाऊँ तो शिकायत होती है, जाऊँ तो भी डाँट पड़ती है — आख़िर करूँ क्या? ન જાઉં તો ફરિયાદ થાય છે, જાઉં તો પણ ઠપકો મળે છે — આખરે કરું શું?
(added, not in book) お金がないと困るが、あったらあったで悩みが増える。Okane ga nai to komaru ga, attara atta de nayami ga fueru. Having no money is a problem, but if you do have it, you get more worries. पैसे न हों तो परेशानी, और हों तो भी चिंताएँ बढ़ जाती हैं। પૈસા ન હોય તો તકલીફ, અને હોય તો પણ ચિંતાઓ વધે છે.

The contrast 行かないと… / 行ったら… needs the repetition 行ったら行ったで. 行くやら lists actions; 行ったり needs a pair (〜たり〜たりする); 行っては means "each time I go" and expects a repeated pattern.

【4】 Nながらに／Nながらの

硬 written
ReadingN nagara ni
Meaning (EN)"(even) as N / in the state of N" — with a few fixed nouns
Meaning (HI)N होते हुए भी / N की अवस्था में ही
Meaning (GU)N હોવા છતાં / N ની સ્થિતિમાં જ
Connection (接続)fixed N (子ども・涙・生まれ・昔・いつも) + ながらに／ながらの
Register硬 — written, set phrases
Typical use子どもながらに (even as a child), 涙ながらに (in tears), 生まれながらに (from birth), 昔ながらの (traditional).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ながらに子どもながらにkodomo nagara ni
Noun(added, not in book) N + ながらに涙ながらに語るnamida nagara ni kataru
Noun(added, not in book) N + ながらの + N昔ながらの町並みmukashi nagara no machinami
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) 大人たちの話を聞いて、この子たちも子どもながらに状況を理解したようだ。Otonatachi no hanashi o kiite, kono kotachi mo kodomo nagara ni joukyou o rikai shita you da. Hearing the adults talk, these children too seem to have understood the situation, child as they are. बड़ों की बातें सुनकर, ये बच्चे भी बच्चे होते हुए भी हालात समझ गए लगते हैं। મોટાઓની વાતો સાંભળીને, આ બાળકો પણ બાળક હોવા છતાં પરિસ્થિતિ સમજી ગયાં લાગે છે.
(added, not in book) 彼女は涙ながらに事故の様子を語った。Kanojo wa namida nagara ni jiko no yousu o katatta. In tears, she described the accident. उसने रोते हुए दुर्घटना का हाल बताया। તેણે રડતાં રડતાં અકસ્માતનું વર્ણન કર્યું.

まま ("as is") needs の (子どものまま = still a child); 手前 ("in front of / for the sake of appearances") needs の too; 並みに ("on a par with") compares levels (大人並みに). 子どもながらに = "though only a child".

neutral register
Reading~nara ~nari ni
Meaning (EN)"if (you're going to) ~, then (do it) in a way that fits ~"

Connection (接続)V-dict + なら + same V-dict + なりに  |  N + なりに
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDemanding appropriate effort/manner: やるならやるなりに, 反対するならするなりに.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict なら V-dict + なりに反対するならするなりにhantai suru nara suru nari ni
Noun(added, not in book) N + なりに子どもなりに考えたkodomo nari ni kangaeta
Adj(added, not in book) adj + なりに狭いなりに工夫するsemai nari ni kufuu suru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 反対するならするなりに、ちゃんとした理由を言ってほしい。Hantai suru nara suru nari ni, chanto shita riyuu o itte hoshii. If you're going to object, I'd like you to at least give a proper reason. अगर विरोध करना ही है, तो उसका ठीक-ठाक कारण तो बताओ। જો વિરોધ કરવો જ હોય, તો એનું યોગ્ય કારણ તો કહો.
(added, not in book) 安い店なら安いなりに、サービスの工夫がある。Yasui mise nara yasui nari ni, saabisu no kufuu ga aru. Cheap shops have their own ways of making service work, in keeping with their prices. सस्ती दुकान अपनी क़ीमत के हिसाब से सेवा में कुछ न कुछ करती है। સસ્તી દુકાન પોતાની કિંમતને અનુરૂપ સેવામાં કંઈક ને કંઈક કરે છે.

ものの ("although") follows a fact, not a conditional; そばから ("no sooner than") is for repeated frustration; かたがた ("while also") follows nouns of purpose. The repeated verb する after なら signals なりに.

NounN + とは言わないまでも海外とは言わないまでもkaigai to wa iwanai made mo

(book: 問題1 Q6) 海外とは言わないまでも、春休みはどこか旅行に行きたい。Kaigai to wa iwanai made mo, haruyasumi wa doko ka ryokou ni ikitai. Even if not abroad, I'd like to travel somewhere during spring break. विदेश न सही, वसंत की छुट्टियों में कहीं घूमने जाना चाहता हूँ। વિદેશ ભલે ન હોય, વસંતની રજાઓમાં ક્યાંક ફરવા જવું છે.
(added, not in book) 毎日とは言わないまでも、せめて週に一度は運動しよう。Mainichi to wa iwanai made mo, semete shuu ni ichido wa undou shiyou. Maybe not every day, but let's exercise at least once a week. रोज़ न सही, कम से कम हफ़्ते में एक बार तो कसरत करें। રોજ ભલે ન હોય, ઓછામાં ઓછું અઠવાડિયે એક વાર તો કસરત કરીએ.

言うばかりか ("not only say") adds; 言うことなしに ("without saying") is unnatural here; 言わないほどに ("to the extent of not saying") doesn't fit. Abroad (high) → somewhere (lower) is the までも structure.

Meaning (GU)...ની જરૂર નથી

(book: 問題1 Q7) けがは軽症で、入院するには及ばないということだ。Kega wa keishou de, nyuuin suru ni wa oyobanai to iu koto da. I hear the injury is minor and there's no need to be hospitalised. सुना है चोट मामूली है और अस्पताल में भर्ती होने की ज़रूरत नहीं। સાંભળ્યું છે કે ઈજા નજીવી છે અને હૉસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ થવાની જરૂર નથી.
(added, not in book) わざわざ来るには及びません。電話で十分です。Wazawaza kuru ni wa oyobimasen. Denwa de juubun desu. There's no need to come all the way. A phone call is enough. ख़ास तौर पर आने की ज़रूरत नहीं। फ़ोन ही काफ़ी है। ખાસ આવવાની જરૂર નથી. ફોન જ પૂરતો છે.

に至らない ("doesn't reach the point of") is possible in meaning (入院に至らない) but normally attaches to nouns; に限らない ("not limited to") and に足りない ("not worth / not enough") don't fit. 軽症 → no need → には及ばない.

Why: A 14-seater, yet 32–33 are crammed in → 14人乗りのところに (point 12). ときに is time; もとに is a basis; 場面に is a scene — none express the normal capacity being exceeded.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The table is built from the distractors the book itself set against each correct answer — the pairs N1 likes to test against each other.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ことにして／〜つもりでkoto ni shite / tsumori de Pretending that / with the feeling that V-た + ことにして ／ V-た + つもりで A fiction presented to others vs one's own mindset. Faking an excuse.
な-adj 極まりない／Nの極みkiwamarinai / no kiwami Utterly / the height of な-adj stem + 極まりない ／ N + の極み Same idea, different connection — no の vs の. 平凡極まりない vs 贅沢の極み.
〜たら〜たで／〜たり〜たりtara ta de / tari tari If it happens, (another problem) / doing things like V-たら V-たで ／ V-たり V-たり Problem either way vs listing actions. Damned if you do, damned if you don't.
Nながらに／N並みにnagara ni / nami ni Even as N / on a par with N fixed N + ながらに ／ N + 並みに State one is in vs comparison of level. 子どもながらに理解する.
〜なら〜なりに／〜もののnara nari ni / mono no Do it properly if you do it / although V なら V + なりに ／ V-た + ものの Demands fitting behaviour vs concedes a fact. 反対するならするなりに.
〜とは言わないまでも／〜ばかりかto wa iwanai made mo / bakari ka Even if not / not only N + とは言わないまでも ／ N + ばかりか Lowers the goal vs raises it. 海外とは言わないまでも.
〜には及ばない／〜に至らないni wa oyobanai / ni itaranai No need to / doesn't reach the point of V-dict + には及ばない ／ N + に至らない Unnecessary vs didn't get that far. ご心配には及びません.
V-たきり／V-たままta kiri / ta mama Did ~ and never again / left as is V-た + きり ／ V-た + まま Nothing followed vs a state continued. 買ったきり使わない.
〜ことは〜が／〜こともkoto wa ~ ga / koto mo Does ~, but / also ~ V + ことは + V + が Concession with limit vs addition. あることはあるが不便だ.
V-に V／V-て VV-i ni V / V-te V Do to the utmost / do and then V-ます-stem + に + V Emphatic repetition is fixed with に. 待ちに待った, 飛ばしに飛ばす.
〜うが〜うが／〜うが〜まいがou ga ou ga / ou ga mai ga Whether A or B / whether or not V-volitional + が ×2 ／ V-volitional + が + V-まい + が Two different alternatives vs yes/no of one. トラックが来ようがバスが来ようが.
その／あのsono / ano That (just mentioned) / that (shared memory) その + N ／ あの + N In written text, その points back to the previous sentence. Text-reference blanks.
Exam trap 1 — connection decides 極: 極まりない attaches directly to a な-adj stem (失礼極まりない); 極み and 至り need の (感激の極み, 光栄の至り). Read what's before the blank first.
Exam trap 2 — こそあ in 問題3: when the blank is a demonstrative, find what it refers to. Something just mentioned in the text → その; something both writer and reader remember from outside the text → あの.
