第12日 — 「〜たらすぐ」という意味を表す言葉 (“~tara sugu” to iu imi o arawasu kotoba — words meaning “as soon as”)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.42–43 (PDF pages 42–43)
Grammar points covered
〜(か)と思うと／〜(か)と思ったら〜たとたん(に)〜か…ないかのうちに〜が早いか〜なり〜や否や／〜や〜次第
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑪ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.3 (PDF page 169), under 第3章 "12 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
The book tags 〜が早いか, 〜なり and 〜や否や／〜や as N1; the other four are N2-level. All seven mean roughly "as soon as", so the exam tests the restrictions, not the meaning: 〜次第 is the only one that can be followed by a request, order, intention or invitation (してください, します, しましょう); the other six describe a past fact or an observed event and cannot. なり needs the same subject in both halves; たとたん often adds surprise. This day continues on the p.58 column (column-03, 「〜たらすぐ」という意味を表す言葉のつづき).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜(か)と思うと／〜(か)と思ったら

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~(ka) to omou to / ~(ka) to omottara
Meaning (EN)"no sooner had ~ than … / just when I thought ~, already …" — the next change comes immediately (book: 〜と次の瞬間 ／ 〜たら、もう… ※次への変化が早いと感じた時使う "used when you feel the change to the next thing was quick")
Meaning (HI)...हुआ ही था कि ... / अभी ...सोचा ही था कि ...
Meaning (GU)...થયું જ હતું કે ... / હજી ...વિચાર્યું જ હતું ત્યાં ...
Connection (接続)V-た + (か)と思うと／(か)と思ったら
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing.
Typical useDescribing something the speaker observed (another person, a thing, nature). Not for your own actions, and not followed by requests or intentions.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + (か)と思うと帰ってきたと思ったらkaette kita to omottara
VerbV-た + かと思ったら入学したかと思ったらnyuugaku shita ka to omottara
Noun(added, not in book) Not used for "as soon as"; N + かと思ったら means "I thought it was N, but…" — a different use.—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this meaning.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this meaning.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大きな爆音がしたかと思うと、辺りは火に包まれていた。Ooki na bakuon ga shita ka to omou to, atari wa hi ni tsutsumarete ita. No sooner was there a huge explosion than the whole area was engulfed in flames. एक ज़ोरदार धमाका हुआ ही था कि आसपास सब आग की लपटों में घिर गया। જોરદાર ધડાકો થયો જ હતો કે આસપાસ બધું આગમાં ઘેરાઈ ગયું.
息子は学校から帰ってきたと思ったら、もう外で遊んでいる。Musuko wa gakkou kara kaette kita to omottara, mou soto de asonde iru. My son had only just come home from school, and he's already out playing. बेटा अभी स्कूल से लौटा ही था कि अब बाहर खेल रहा है। દીકરો હજી શાળાએથી પાછો આવ્યો જ હતો ત્યાં તો બહાર રમવા લાગ્યો છે.
彼は忙しい。先週はアメリカに行ったかと思うと、今週は中国だ。Kare wa isogashii. Senshuu wa Amerika ni itta ka to omou to, konshuu wa Chuugoku da. He's busy. Last week he went to America, and this week it's already China. वह बहुत व्यस्त है। पिछले हफ़्ते अमेरिका गया था, और इस हफ़्ते चीन में है। તે બહુ વ્યસ્ત છે. ગયા અઠવાડિયે અમેરિકા ગયો હતો, તો આ અઠવાડિયે ચીનમાં છે.
早いものだね。この間入学したかと思ったら、もう卒業だ。Hayai mono da ne. Kono aida nyuugaku shita ka to omottara, mou sotsugyou da. Time flies, doesn't it? It feels like you only just started school, and it's already graduation. समय कितनी जल्दी बीतता है न! लगता है अभी दाख़िला लिया था, और अब ग्रेजुएशन भी आ गया। સમય કેટલો ઝડપથી જાય છે, નહીં! હમણાં જ પ્રવેશ લીધો હોય એવું લાગે છે, અને હવે તો ગ્રેજ્યુએશન આવી ગયું.

The key feeling is the speaker's surprise at how fast the next thing came. Because it reports something observed, the second half is a fact (often with もう), never 〜てください or 〜しよう. It can't describe your own actions: ×私は家に帰ったと思ったら、すぐ寝た.

【2】 〜たとたん(に)

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ta totan (ni)
Meaning (EN)"the moment ~, (something surprising happened)" / "as soon as ~, it suddenly changed" (book: 〜と次の瞬間…（驚くようなことが起きた） ／ 〜と、それまでとは急に変わって)
Meaning (HI)...ते ही (अचानक कुछ हुआ) / ...ते ही एकदम बदल गया
Meaning (GU)...તાં જ (અચાનક કંઈક થયું) / ...તાં જ એકદમ બદલાઈ ગયું
Connection (接続)V-た + とたん(に)  |  cf. とたんに (stand-alone adverb: "at that instant")
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useAn unexpected event or an abrupt change right after an action; the second half is a past fact, not a request or plan.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + とたん(に)開いたとたんaita totan
Adverb (cf.)sentence／clause, + とたんにとたんに笑顔で話し始めるtotan ni egao de hanashihajimeru
Noun(added, not in book) Not used.—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
エレベーターのドアが開いたとたん、中から猫が飛び出してきた。Erebeetaa no doa ga aita totan, naka kara neko ga tobidashite kita. The moment the elevator door opened, a cat leapt out from inside. लिफ़्ट का दरवाज़ा खुलते ही अंदर से एक बिल्ली उछलकर बाहर आई। લિફ્ટનો દરવાજો ખૂલતાં જ અંદરથી એક બિલાડી કૂદીને બહાર આવી.
その小包は、母が開けたとたん爆発したんです。Sono kozutsumi wa, haha ga aketa totan bakuhatsu shita n desu. That parcel exploded the instant my mother opened it. वह पार्सल माँ के खोलते ही फट गया। એ પાર્સલ માએ ખોલતાં જ ફાટ્યું.
大学に入るまでは一生懸命勉強するが、入ったとたんに勉強しなくなる学生が多い。Daigaku ni hairu made wa isshoukenmei benkyou suru ga, haitta totan ni benkyou shinaku naru gakusei ga ooi. Many students study hard until they get into university, but stop studying the moment they get in. बहुत से छात्र यूनिवर्सिटी में दाख़िले तक जी-जान से पढ़ते हैं, पर दाख़िला मिलते ही पढ़ना छोड़ देते हैं। ઘણા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ સુધી ખૂબ મહેનતથી ભણે છે, પણ પ્રવેશ મળતાં જ ભણવાનું છોડી દે છે.
cf. 彼はいつも先生の悪口を言っているが、先生が教室に入ってくると、とたんに先生と笑顔で話し始める。Kare wa itsumo sensei no warukuchi o itte iru ga, sensei ga kyoushitsu ni haitte kuru to, totan ni sensei to egao de hanashihajimeru. cf. He's always badmouthing the teacher, but when the teacher walks into the classroom, he instantly starts chatting with them all smiles. वह हमेशा टीचर की बुराई करता है, लेकिन टीचर के कक्षा में आते ही फ़ौरन मुस्कुराकर उनसे बातें करने लगता है। તે હંમેશાં શિક્ષકની નિંદા કરે છે, પણ શિક્ષક વર્ગમાં આવે કે તરત જ હસીને તેમની સાથે વાત કરવા લાગે છે.

たとたん has two flavours: something unexpected happens (the cat, the explosion), or a state abruptly flips (study hard → stop studying). The book's cf. shows とたんに used alone as an adverb after a と-clause. Like 〜と思ったら, the second half can't be the speaker's will: ×家に着いたとたん、電話してください (that is 次第's job — 練習問題 ⑨).

【3】 〜か…ないかのうちに

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ka ... nai ka no uchi ni
Meaning (EN)"almost at the same time as ~ / before ~ was even quite finished" (book: 〜とほとんど同時に)
Meaning (HI)...हुआ-न-हुआ कि ... / ...ख़त्म होते-होते ही
Meaning (GU)...થયું-ન-થયું ત્યાં તો ... / ...પૂરું થતાં-થતાં જ
Connection (接続)V-dict + か + V-ない + かのうちに (same verb twice: 終わるか終わらないかのうちに)  |  also V-た + か + V-ない + かのうちに (added, not in book)
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useAn action starts while the first is barely finished; the second half is a past fact.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + か + V-ない + かのうちに開くか開かないかのうちにaku ka akanai ka no uchi ni
Verb (past)(added, not in book) V-た + か + V-ない + かのうちに鳴ったか鳴らないかのうちにnatta ka naranai ka no uchi ni
Noun(added, not in book) Not used.—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生が問題を言い終わるか終わらないかのうちに、生徒たちは答えを書き始めた。Sensei ga mondai o iiowaru ka owaranai ka no uchi ni, seitotachi wa kotae o kakihajimeta. The teacher had barely finished reading out the question when the students started writing their answers. टीचर ने सवाल बोलना ख़त्म किया-न-किया कि छात्र जवाब लिखने लगे। શિક્ષકે પ્રશ્ન બોલવાનું પૂરું કર્યું-ન-કર્યું ત્યાં તો વિદ્યાર્થીઓ જવાબ લખવા લાગ્યા.
バーゲン会場の入り口が開くか開かないかのうちに、客がいっせいに入ろうとし、負傷者が出た。Baagen kaijou no iriguchi ga aku ka akanai ka no uchi ni, kyaku ga issei ni hairou to shi, fushousha ga deta. The entrance to the sale venue had scarcely opened when the customers all tried to rush in at once, and people were injured. सेल वाली जगह का दरवाज़ा खुला-न-खुला कि ग्राहक एक साथ अंदर घुसने लगे, और कुछ लोग घायल हो गए। સેલના સ્થળનો દરવાજો ખૂલ્યો-ન-ખૂલ્યો ત્યાં તો ગ્રાહકો એકસાથે અંદર ઘૂસવા લાગ્યા અને કેટલાક ઘાયલ થયા.

Picture the moment when you can't tell whether the first action has finished or not — that's the "か…ないか". The verb is repeated in affirmative + negative: 言うか言わないか, 開くか開かないか. In 練習問題 ⑦ the frame 言う＿＿ is completed by か言わないかのうちに.

【4】 〜が早いか

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ga hayai ka
Meaning (EN)"no sooner ~ than (they moved on to the next action)" (book: 〜とほとんど同時に…（次の動作に移った）)
Meaning (HI)...ते ही फ़ौरन (अगला काम कर दिया)
Meaning (GU)...તાં જ તરત (આગળનું કામ કર્યું)
Connection (接続)V-dict + が早いか  |  (added, not in book) V-た + が早いか also occurs
Register硬 — literary, used in narration and news-style writing.
Typical useA quick, often dramatic next action by someone (飛び出していった, 逃げ出した); past tense, no requests.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + が早いか見るが早いかmiru ga hayai ka
Verb (past)(added, not in book) V-た + が早いか聞いたが早いかkiita ga hayai ka
Noun(added, not in book) Not used.—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
殺人事件発生と聞くが早いか、記者たちは部屋を飛び出していった。Satsujin jiken hassei to kiku ga hayai ka, kishatachi wa heya o tobidashite itta. No sooner had they heard there'd been a murder than the reporters dashed out of the room. हत्या की वारदात की ख़बर सुनते ही पत्रकार कमरे से दौड़कर निकल गए। ખૂનના બનાવના સમાચાર સાંભળતાં જ પત્રકારો રૂમમાંથી દોડીને નીકળી ગયા.
けんかをしていた男たちは、警官の姿を見るが早いか逃げ出した。Kenka o shite ita otokotachi wa, keikan no sugata o miru ga hayai ka nigedashita. The men who had been fighting ran off the instant they saw a police officer. झगड़ रहे आदमी पुलिसवाले को देखते ही भाग खड़े हुए। ઝઘડતા માણસો પોલીસને જોતાં જ ભાગી છૂટ્યા.

Literally "which was faster — X or Y?": the two actions are so close you can't tell. The second half is a deliberate action that follows (run out, flee), not a natural phenomenon or a request. In 練習問題 ⑤ the monkey grabs the rice ball and immediately runs — 取るが早いか逃げていった.

【5】 〜なり

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~nari
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~, (and) straight away / just like that" (book: 〜と、そのまますぐ)
Meaning (HI)...ते ही, वैसे ही तुरंत
Meaning (GU)...તાં જ, એમ ને એમ તરત
Connection (接続)V-dict + なり
Register硬 — written/narrative.
Typical useThe same person does X and, without a pause, an often unexpected Y (倒れる, 泣き崩れる, 歌い始める). Third person, past tense.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + なり聞くなりkiku nari
Noun(added, not in book) Not in this meaning (N + なり = "N or …" / "in one's own way" — different forms).—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
選手はゴールインするなり、ばったり倒れてしまった。Senshu wa gooruin suru nari, battari taorete shimatta. The moment the athlete crossed the finish line, he collapsed on the spot. खिलाड़ी फ़िनिश लाइन पार करते ही धड़ाम से गिर पड़ा। ખેલાડી ફિનિશ લાઇન પાર કરતાં જ ધબ દઈને પડી ગયો.
彼女は夫の戦死の知らせを聞くなり、その場に泣き崩れた。Kanojo wa otto no senshi no shirase o kiku nari, sono ba ni nakikuzureta. As soon as she heard the news that her husband had been killed in the war, she broke down in tears right there. पति के युद्ध में मारे जाने की ख़बर सुनते ही वह वहीं फूट-फूटकर रो पड़ी। પતિ યુદ્ધમાં માર્યા ગયાના સમાચાર સાંભળતાં જ તે ત્યાં જ ભાંગી પડીને રડવા લાગી.

なり is the strictest one: the subject of both halves must be the same person. That is exactly 練習問題 ⑪ — 学生が駆け上がるなり、大声で歌い始めた (the student himself) ✓, but ×…駆け上がるなり、校長は驚いた (a different subject) ✗. It also can't take requests, intentions or the speaker's own routine.

Meaning (EN)"the instant ~ / no sooner ~ than" (book: 〜とほとんど同時に)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電車の中で、席に座るや否や携帯電話でメールを打ち始める若者をよく見かける。Densha no naka de, seki ni suwaru ya ina ya keitai denwa de meeru o uchihajimeru wakamono o yoku mikakeru. On trains you often see young people who start typing emails on their phones the instant they sit down. ट्रेन में अक्सर ऐसे युवा दिखते हैं जो सीट पर बैठते ही मोबाइल पर मैसेज टाइप करने लगते हैं। ટ્રેનમાં ઘણી વાર એવા યુવાનો દેખાય છે જે સીટ પર બેસતાં જ મોબાઇલ પર મેસેજ ટાઇપ કરવા લાગે છે.
その歌手はデビューするや、CDを600万枚も売って世間を驚かせた。Sono kashu wa debyuu suru ya, shii-dii o roppyakuman-mai mo utte seken o odorokaseta. No sooner had that singer debuted than she sold a whopping six million CDs and astonished everyone. उस गायिका ने डेब्यू करते ही 60 लाख सीडी बेचकर दुनिया को हैरान कर दिया। એ ગાયિકાએ ડેબ્યુ કરતાં જ 60 લાખ CD વેચીને લોકોને ચોંકાવી દીધા.

否 means "no", so や否や is literally "whether yes or no" — the same image as か…ないかのうちに, in classical dress. Treat it as the written twin of たとたん／が早いか: the main clause is a fact that has happened (or a habitual scene, as in example 1), never 〜てください.

【7】 〜次第

N2-level · polite/business register
Reading~shidai
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~, (I will / please …)" — followed by intention, order, request or invitation (book: 〜たらすぐ…（意志、命令、依頼、勧誘など）)
Meaning (HI)...होते ही (मैं करूँगा / कृपया कीजिए)
Meaning (GU)...થાય કે તરત (હું કરીશ / કૃપા કરીને કરો)
Connection (接続)V-stem (ます-stem) + 次第  |  (added, not in book) する-noun + 次第 (到着次第, 完成次第)
RegisterPolite; common in announcements, business mail, instructions.

次第 is the odd one out of the day: it looks forward (something hasn't happened yet) and the main clause is what the speaker will do or asks others to do. It attaches to the ます-stem (決まり次第, 戻り次第, 帰り次第). It cannot describe a past event: ×着き次第、太陽がのぞき始めました (練習問題 ⑩).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All seven forms translate as "as soon as". The exam separates them by what precedes (V-た, V-dict or stem) and what may follow (a past fact vs. a request/intention; same subject or not):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜(か)と思うと／思ったら~(ka) to omou to / omottara no sooner … than (felt as fast) V-た + (か)と思うと Observed change; speaker's feeling of speed; not own actions. Watching others/things change quickly (Q1).
〜たとたん(に)~ta totan (ni) the moment ~ (surprise / sudden change) V-た + とたん(に) Unexpected event or abrupt flip; subjects may differ. Q6, Q10 — past, unplanned results.
〜か…ないかのうちに~ka ... nai ka no uchi ni before ~ was even finished V-dict + か + V-ない + かのうちに Verb repeated; "almost simultaneous". Q7 — action begins mid-way through another.
〜が早いか~ga hayai ka no sooner ~ than (next action) V-dict + が早いか Followed by a quick deliberate action (flee, rush out). 硬. Q5 — 取るが早いか逃げていった.
〜なり~nari as soon as, and just like that V-dict + なり Same subject in both halves; often unexpected behaviour. 硬. Q4, Q11.
〜や否や／〜や~ya ina ya / ~ya the instant ~ V-dict + や否や／や Most literary; facts only. Q3 — narration, news.
〜次第~shidai as soon as ~ (I will / please) V-stem + 次第 The only one followed by request, order, intention, invitation; future, not past. Q2, Q8, Q9 — 〜てください, 〜ます, 〜ましょう.
Exam trap 1 — request / intention after the comma ⇒ 次第: if the sentence ends in 〜てください, 〜をください, 〜します (plan), 〜しましょう, the answer is 次第 (Q2, Q8, Q9). If it ends in a past event (〜た), 次第 is wrong and one of the other six fits (Q6, Q10).
Exam trap 2 — check the verb form first: V-た → かと思うと／とたん; V-dict → か…ないかのうちに／が早いか／なり／や否や; ます-stem → 次第. Q1, Q3, Q5, Q6 and Q7 are decided by the connection alone.
Exam trap 3 — なり needs one subject: 〜するなり、(same person) does Y. A different subject after the comma (×駆け上がるなり、校長は驚いた) makes なり wrong (Q11). None of these six "fact" forms can describe the speaker's own planned action.
