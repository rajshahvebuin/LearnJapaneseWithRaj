問題1 次の文の（　　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。

Mondai 1: Tsugi no bun no ( ) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — Choose the best option (1–4) for the ( ). (HI: ( ) में भरने के लिए 1–4 में से सबसे सही विकल्प चुनिए। GU: ( ) માં ભરવા માટે 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.)

Q1

このあたりは駅に近い（＿＿＿）静かですね。Kono atari wa eki ni chikai (___) shizuka desu ne.

Full sentence: このあたりは駅に近いわりに静かですね。Kono atari wa eki ni chikai wari ni shizuka desu ne.
EN: This area is quiet, considering how close it is to the station.
HI: स्टेशन के इतने पास होने के हिसाब से यह इलाक़ा काफ़ी शांत है, है न?
GU: સ્ટેશનની આટલી નજીક હોવાના પ્રમાણમાં આ વિસ્તાર ઘણો શાંત છે, નહીં?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かわりにkawari niinstead of / in exchangeके बदलेના બદલે
2とおりtoorijust asजैसा किજેમ કે
3わりにwari niconsidering, forके हिसाब सेના પ્રમાણમાં
4あまりamariso much thatइतना ज़्यादा किએટલું બધું કે

Why: "Near the station" normally means noisy, but the area is quiet — a result that goes against what you'd expect from the first part → 〜わりに (第20日). かわりに (第37日) means "instead / in return", which doesn't link "near" and "quiet". とおり (第36日) would need the same idea on both sides ("as expected"). あまり (第37日) needs an extreme cause and a (usually bad) result: 近いあまり静か makes no sense.

Q2

くわしいことが（＿＿＿）次第、また連絡します。Kuwashii koto ga (___) shidai, mata renraku shimasu.

Full sentence: くわしいことがわかり次第、また連絡します。Kuwashii koto ga wakari shidai, mata renraku shimasu.
EN: I'll contact you again as soon as I know the details.
HI: विस्तार से पता चलते ही मैं आपसे फिर संपर्क करूँगा।
GU: વિગતવાર ખબર પડતાં જ હું તમારો ફરી સંપર્ક કરીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1わかるwakaruknow (dictionary form)पता चलना (मूल रूप)ખબર પડવી (મૂળ રૂપ)
2わかったwakattaknew (た-form)पता चला (भूतकाल)ખબર પડી (ભૂતકાળ)
3わかりwakariknow (ます-stem)पता चल (ます-स्टेम)ખબર પડ (ます-સ્ટેમ)
4わかってwakatteknowing (て-form)पता चलकर (て-रूप)ખબર પડીને (て-રૂપ)

Why: "As soon as X, (I will) Y" is V-ます stem + 次第 (第12日; column-03 notes that 次第 is the one of the "as soon as" family that takes a future/intention ending like 連絡します). The other three verb forms can't attach to this 次第. Don't confuse it with N + 次第だ "depends on N" (第38日).

Q3

旅行の計画を立てた（＿＿＿）、休みが取れるかどうかわからない。Ryokou no keikaku o tateta (___), yasumi ga toreru ka dou ka wakaranai.

Full sentence: 旅行の計画を立てたものの、休みが取れるかどうかわからない。Ryokou no keikaku o tateta mono no, yasumi ga toreru ka dou ka wakaranai.
EN: I've made a travel plan, but I don't know whether I can actually get the time off.
HI: यात्रा की योजना तो बना ली, लेकिन पता नहीं छुट्टी मिल पाएगी या नहीं।
GU: પ્રવાસની યોજના તો બનાવી લીધી, પણ ખબર નથી કે રજા મળશે કે નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ことからkoto karafrom the fact thatइस बात से किએ હકીકત પરથી કે
2ことだからkoto da karasince it's (him), surely(वह) है तो ज़रूर(તે) છે એટલે જરૂર
3ものだからmono da karabecause (excuse)क्योंकि (सफ़ाई)કારણ કે (બચાવ)
4もののmono noalthough, butहालाँकि … परજોકે … પણ

Why: The two halves clash: "I made a plan" vs "I don't know if I can go" → a contrast, V-た + ものの "I did ~, but (it isn't going as it should)" (第16日). ことから (第14日) and ものだから (第16日) give a reason, not a contrast. ことだから (第14日, 〜のことだから) needs a person noun before it: 彼のことだから.

Q4

暑いですね。冷たいものでも（＿＿＿）。Atsui desu ne. Tsumetai mono demo (___).

Full sentence: 暑いですね。冷たいものでもお持ちしましょうか。Atsui desu ne. Tsumetai mono demo o-mochi shimashou ka.
EN: It's hot, isn't it? Shall I bring you something cold (to drink)?
HI: कितनी गर्मी है, न? क्या मैं आपके लिए कुछ ठंडा ले आऊँ?
GU: કેટલી ગરમી છે, નહીં? શું હું તમારા માટે કંઈક ઠંડું લઈ આવું?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1お持ちしましょうかo-mochi shimashou kashall I bring (humble)क्या मैं ले आऊँ (विनम्र)શું હું લઈ આવું (નમ્ર)
2持っていたしましょうかmotte itashimashou ka(not a correct form)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)
3お持ちになりましょうかo-mochi ni narimashou ka(honorific — for the listener's action)(आदरसूचक — सामने वाले के लिए)(આદરસૂચક — સામેની વ્યક્તિ માટે)
4持たせていただきませんかmotasete itadakimasen ka(wrong — "won't you let me be allowed…?")(ग़लत)(ખોટું)

Why: The speaker offers to do something for the listener, so the verb is the speaker's own → humble 謙譲語 お + Vます + する: お持ちする (第40日 敬語). お持ちになる is 尊敬語 (raising the listener's action) — wrong person. 持っていたす is not a real humble pattern (it should be お持ちいたす). 〜させていただきませんか mixes "asking permission" with an invitation form and doesn't make sense.

Q5

君のことを忘れていた（＿＿＿）んだけど、いろいろ忙しくて連絡できなかったんだ。Kimi no koto o wasurete ita (___) n da kedo, iroiro isogashikute renraku dekinakatta n da.

Full sentence: 君のことを忘れていたわけではないんだけど、いろいろ忙しくて連絡できなかったんだ。Kimi no koto o wasurete ita wake de wa nai n da kedo, iroiro isogashikute renraku dekinakatta n da.
EN: It's not that I'd forgotten about you — I was just busy with one thing and another and couldn't get in touch.
HI: ऐसा नहीं है कि मैं तुम्हें भूल गया था, बस तरह-तरह के कामों में व्यस्त था, इसलिए संपर्क नहीं कर पाया।
GU: એવું નથી કે હું તને ભૂલી ગયો હતો, બસ જાતજાતનાં કામોમાં વ્યસ્ત હતો એટલે સંપર્ક ન કરી શક્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1わけではないwake de wa naiit's not thatऐसा नहीं किએવું નથી કે
2ものではないmono de wa naione shouldn'tनहीं करना चाहिएન કરવું જોઈએ
3ところなtokoro na(just at the point — doesn't fit)(ठीक उस समय — फ़िट नहीं)(બરાબર એ સમયે — બંધબેસતું નથી)
4わけにはいかないwake ni wa ikanaican't (for social reasons)नहीं कर सकते (मजबूरी)ન કરી શકાય (મજબૂરી)

Why: The speaker denies one possible explanation ("forgot you") and gives the real one ("busy") → partial denial 〜わけではない (第14日). ものではない (第16日) is a moral rule ("you shouldn't forget"), not an excuse. わけにはいかない (第14日) means "I can't afford to forget", which clashes with the past ていた and the excuse. 忘れていたところなんだ would mean "I was just at the point of forgetting" — not the meaning here.

Q6

数学が得意だ（＿＿＿）、全然勉強しなかったら試験でいい点はとれないよ。Suugaku ga tokui da (___), zenzen benkyou shinakattara shiken de ii ten wa torenai yo.

Full sentence: 数学が得意だからといって、全然勉強しなかったら試験でいい点はとれないよ。Suugaku ga tokui da kara to itte, zenzen benkyou shinakattara shiken de ii ten wa torenai yo.
EN: Just because you're good at maths doesn't mean you'll get a good score if you don't study at all.
HI: सिर्फ़ इसलिए कि तुम्हारा गणित अच्छा है, अगर बिल्कुल नहीं पढ़ोगे तो परीक्षा में अच्छे अंक नहीं आएँगे।
GU: ફક્ત એટલા માટે કે તારું ગણિત સારું છે, જો બિલકુલ નહીં ભણે તો પરીક્ષામાં સારા માર્ક નહીં આવે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1からにはkara ni wanow that, since (resolve)जब … है तोજ્યારે … છે તો
2からいうとkara iu tofrom the viewpoint ofके नज़रिए सेના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી
3からしてkara shitejudging from / to begin withसे ही (देखकर)પરથી જ
4からといってkara to ittejust becauseसिर्फ़ इसलिए किફક્ત એટલા માટે કે

Why: The second half rejects the natural conclusion of the first ("good at maths → good score") with a negative (とれない) → 〜からといって (第25日). からには (第22日) leads to a duty or resolve ("since you're good, you must…"). からいうと／からして (第20日) take a noun (立場からいうと, 名前からして) and set a viewpoint, not a rejected reason.

Q7

明日は大切な試合があるが、この高熱では欠場（＿＿＿）。Ashita wa taisetsu na shiai ga aru ga, kono kounetsu de wa ketsujou (___).

Full sentence: 明日は大切な試合があるが、この高熱では欠場せざるを得ない。Ashita wa taisetsu na shiai ga aru ga, kono kounetsu de wa ketsujou sezaru o enai.
EN: There's an important match tomorrow, but with this high fever I have no choice but to sit it out.
HI: कल एक अहम मैच है, लेकिन इतने तेज़ बुख़ार में मुझे मजबूरन उससे हटना ही पड़ेगा।
GU: કાલે એક મહત્ત્વની મૅચ છે, પણ આટલા તાવમાં મારે મજબૂરીથી તેમાંથી ખસી જ જવું પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1せざるを得ないsezaru o enaihave no choice but toमजबूरन करना ही पड़ेगाમજબૂરીથી કરવું જ પડશે
2するとは限らないsuru to wa kagiranaiwon't necessarilyज़रूरी नहीं किજરૂરી નથી કે
3しなくてなんだろうshinakute nan darou(not a real form)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)
4することはないsuru koto wa naithere's no need toज़रूरत नहींજરૂર નથી

Why: 〜が (but) + この高熱では (with this fever) sets up "I don't want to, but circumstances force me" → 〜ざるを得ない (第29日; する → せざるを得ない). とは限らない (第30日) is "not necessarily", which doesn't fit a definite "this fever". でなくてなんだろう (第31日) attaches to a noun ("if this isn't love, what is?"); しなくてなんだろう is not a form. することはない (第15日) "no need" contradicts the fever.

Q8

事故で亡くなった友達のことを考えると、酒を（＿＿＿）。Jiko de nakunatta tomodachi no koto o kangaeru to, sake o (___).

Full sentence: 事故で亡くなった友達のことを考えると、酒を飲まないではいられない。Jiko de nakunatta tomodachi no koto o kangaeru to, sake o nomanaide wa irarenai.
EN: When I think about my friend who died in the accident, I can't help drinking.
HI: हादसे में गुज़र गए दोस्त के बारे में सोचता हूँ तो शराब पिए बिना रहा नहीं जाता।
GU: અકસ્માતમાં ગુજરી ગયેલા મિત્ર વિશે વિચારું છું તો દારૂ પીધા વગર રહેવાતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1飲まないこともないnomanai koto mo naiit's not that I never drinkऐसा नहीं कि कभी न पिऊँએવું નથી કે ક્યારેય ન પીઉં
2飲まないではいられないnomanaide wa irarenaican't help drinkingपिए बिना रहा नहीं जाताપીધા વગર રહેવાતું નથી
3飲むとは限らないnomu to wa kagiranaiI won't necessarily drinkज़रूरी नहीं कि पिऊँજરૂરી નથી કે પીઉં
4飲まないわけではないnomanai wake de wa naiit's not that I don't drinkऐसा नहीं कि नहीं पीताએવું નથી કે નથી પીતો

Why: 〜を考えると + a strong feeling the speaker can't hold back → 〜ないではいられない／〜ずにはいられない (第31日). Options 1 and 4 are mild partial denials (ないことはない 第15日, わけではない 第14日) that don't follow from "when I think of my dead friend". とは限らない (第30日) is a general "not always" statement.

Q9

この建物の中では写真撮影はできない（＿＿＿）。Kono tatemono no naka de wa shashin satsuei wa dekinai (___).

Full sentence: この建物の中では写真撮影はできないことになっている。Kono tatemono no naka de wa shashin satsuei wa dekinai koto ni natte iru.
EN: Taking photographs inside this building is not allowed (that's the rule).
HI: इस इमारत के अंदर फ़ोटो खींचना मना है (ऐसा नियम है)।
GU: આ ઇમારતની અંદર ફોટા પાડવાની મનાઈ છે (એવો નિયમ છે).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1わけになっているwake ni natte iru(not a real form)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)
2ものになっているmono ni natte iruhas become a thing that (wrong here)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)
3ことになっているkoto ni natte iruit is the rule thatऐसा नियम है किએવો નિયમ છે કે
4べきではないbeki de wa naione ought notनहीं करना चाहिएન કરવું જોઈએ

Why: A building rule → 〜ことになっている (第15日: a rule, custom or arrangement decided by someone). わけになっている is not a pattern. べきではない (第18日) attaches to the dictionary form (撮影するべきではない) and gives the speaker's opinion; できないべきではない is ungrammatical. ものになっている doesn't express a rule.

Q10

それは、その子の年齢にとらわれる［18］、その子の学力を見極め、"ちょうどの教材"を与えていくことだった。Sore wa, sono ko no nenrei ni torawareru [18], sono ko no gakuryoku o mikiwame, "choudo no kyouzai" o ataete iku koto datta.

Full sentence: それは、その子の年齢にとらわれることなく、その子の学力を見極め、"ちょうどの教材"を与えていくことだった。Sore wa, sono ko no nenrei ni torawareru koto naku, sono ko no gakuryoku o mikiwame, "choudo no kyouzai" o ataete iku koto datta.
EN: That meant judging the child's actual ability, without being bound by the child's age, and giving "just the right material".
HI: इसका मतलब था बच्चे की उम्र में बँधे बिना उसकी असली क्षमता परखना और उसे "ठीक उतनी" सामग्री देते जाना।
GU: એનો અર્થ હતો બાળકની ઉંમરમાં બંધાયા વિના તેની ખરી ક્ષમતા પારખવી અને તેને "બરાબર એટલું" સાહિત્ય આપતા જવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ことでkoto deby (doing)करकेકરીને
2ことがkoto gathe fact that (subject)यह बात किએ વાત કે
3こともkoto moalso the fact thatयह भी किએ પણ કે

Why: The method rejects age-based teaching and looks at real ability instead → V-dictionary + ことなく "without ~ing" (第14日). ことで would make "being bound by age" the means — the opposite of the point. ことが／ことも would need their own predicate before the comma.

Q19

［19］、一粒の種が水や日光や温度といった条件がそろった時に、ひとりでに芽を出し、成長していくように、子どもたちは可能性を開花させていく。[19], hitotsubu no tane ga mizu ya nikkou ya ondo to itta jouken ga sorotta toki ni, hitori de ni me o dashi, seichou shite iku you ni, kodomotachi wa kanousei o kaika sasete iku.

Full sentence: そうすると、一粒の種が水や日光や温度といった条件がそろった時に、ひとりでに芽を出し、成長していくように、子どもたちは可能性を開花させていく。Sou suru to, hitotsubu no tane ga mizu ya nikkou ya ondo to itta jouken ga sorotta toki ni, hitori de ni me o dashi, seichou shite iku you ni, kodomotachi wa kanousei o kaika sasete iku.
EN: When you do that, just as a single seed sprouts and grows by itself once conditions like water, sunlight and temperature are right, children make their potential blossom.
HI: ऐसा करने पर, जैसे पानी, धूप और तापमान जैसी शर्तें पूरी होने पर एक बीज अपने-आप अंकुरित होकर बढ़ता है, वैसे ही बच्चे अपनी संभावनाओं को खिलाते जाते हैं।
GU: આમ કરવાથી, જેમ પાણી, તડકો અને તાપમાન જેવી શરતો પૂરી થતાં એક બીજ જાતે જ ફૂટીને વધે છે, તેમ બાળકો પોતાની શક્યતાઓને ખીલવતાં જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1そうするとsou suru towhen you do that, thenऐसा करने परઆમ કરવાથી
2にもかかわらずni mo kakawarazuin spite ofके बावजूदછતાં
3しかしながらshikashi nagarahoweverफिर भीતેમ છતાં
4そうしないとsou shinai toif you don't do thatऐसा न करने परઆમ ન કરવાથી

Why: The previous sentence describes the method; this one gives its natural, positive result (potential blossoms) → そうすると "doing so, (this happens)". にもかかわらず (第26日) and しかしながら signal a contrast, and there is none. そうしないと would predict a bad result, but the sentence describes a good one. にもかかわらず also can't open a sentence on its own (it needs それにもかかわらず).

Q20

子どもたちは繰り返しこつこつと学習を続け、確実にステップアップして［20］、学ぶ喜びを覚える。Kodomotachi wa kurikaeshi kotsukotsu to gakushuu o tsuzuke, kakujitsu ni suteppu appu shite [20], manabu yorokobi o oboeru.

Full sentence: 子どもたちは繰り返しこつこつと学習を続け、確実にステップアップしていくことで、学ぶ喜びを覚える。Kodomotachi wa kurikaeshi kotsukotsu to gakushuu o tsuzuke, kakujitsu ni suteppu appu shite iku koto de, manabu yorokobi o oboeru.
EN: Children keep studying steadily, over and over, and by steadily stepping up they discover the joy of learning.

GU: બાળકો વારંવાર ખંતથી અભ્યાસ ચાલુ રાખે છે, અને પાકા પગલે આગળ વધતા જવાથી ભણવાનો આનંદ મેળવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いかないとikanai toif (they) don't go onअगर आगे न बढ़ें तोજો આગળ ન વધે તો
2いくことでiku koto deby going onआगे बढ़ते जाने सेઆગળ વધતા જવાથી

Why: Stepping up steadily is the means by which they come to enjoy learning → 〜ていくことで "by continuing to ~". All three other options are negative (いかない…), which would contradict 確実に (surely, steadily) and the positive 学ぶ喜びを覚える.

Q21

その過程で、自分を［21］「自信」が生まれ、自分で上へ上へと学んでいく経験から、「自学自習」を身につけていくのである。Sono katei de, jibun o [21] "jishin" ga umare, jibun de ue e ue e to manande iku keiken kara, "jigaku jishuu" o mi ni tsukete iku no de aru.

Full sentence: その過程で、自分を肯定する「自信」が生まれ、自分で上へ上へと学んでいく経験から、「自学自習」を身につけていくのである。Sono katei de, jibun o koutei suru "jishin" ga umare, jibun de ue e ue e to manande iku keiken kara, "jigaku jishuu" o mi ni tsukete iku no de aru.
EN: In that process a "confidence" that affirms oneself is born, and from the experience of learning higher and higher on their own, they acquire "self-study".
HI: इस प्रक्रिया में ख़ुद को स्वीकार करने वाला "आत्मविश्वास" पैदा होता है, और ख़ुद ऊपर-ऊपर सीखते जाने के अनुभव से वे "स्व-अध्ययन" की आदत पा लेते हैं।
GU: આ પ્રક્રિયામાં પોતાને સ્વીકારતો "આત્મવિશ્વાસ" જન્મે છે, અને જાતે ઉપર ને ઉપર શીખતા જવાના અનુભવથી તેઓ "સ્વ-અધ્યયન" ની ટેવ કેળવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1肯定しようkoutei shiyoulet's affirm / try to affirmचलो स्वीकारेंચાલો સ્વીકારીએ
2肯定しなければならないkoutei shinakereba naranaimust affirmस्वीकारना ही होगाસ્વીકારવું જ પડશે

Why: The blank is a plain noun modifier for 「自信」: "a confidence that affirms oneself" → dictionary form 肯定する. 肯定しよう (volitional) and 肯定しなければならない (obligation) express the speaker's will or duty and don't describe a quality of 自信. 肯定させる (causative) would need a third person who makes the child affirm something, but here the confidence comes from the child's own experience.

Q22

その際、誰に教わらなくても、自分で学んでいく力を身につけていれば、きっと前へ前へと力強く進むことが［22］。Sono sai, dare ni osowaranakute mo, jibun de manande iku chikara o mi ni tsukete ireba, kitto mae e mae e to chikarazuyoku susumu koto ga [22].

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The option sets in this test keep pairing the same look-alikes. These are the ones to have cold:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜からといって vs 〜からにはkara to itte / kara ni wa just because (doesn't mean) / now that (so I must) plain form + からといって + negative / plain form + からには + duty, resolve からといって rejects a conclusion; からには draws one. Q6, Q13 (both in one sentence)
〜わけではない vs 〜わけにはいかないwake de wa nai / wake ni wa ikanai it's not that / can't (socially) plain form + わけではない / V-dict + わけにはいかない Partial denial vs social impossibility. Q5 vs Q13
〜ものの vs 〜ものだからmono no / mono da kara although / because (excuse) V-た + ものの / plain form + ものだから Contrast vs reason — both start with もの. Q3
〜わりに vs 〜かわりにwari ni / kawari ni considering (unexpected) / instead of, in return plain form, N の + わりに / V-dict, N の + かわりに Only one sound apart; わりに goes against expectation. Q1
〜ことになっている vs 〜べきではないkoto ni natte iru / beki de wa nai it's the rule / one ought not plain form + ことになっている / V-dict + べきではない An external rule vs the speaker's opinion. Q9
お〜する vs お〜になるo ~ suru / o ~ ni naru humble (my action) / honorific (your action) お + Vます + する / お + Vます + になる Whose action is it? Q4
〜につれて vs 〜によってni tsurete / ni yotte as (parallel change) / by, depending on V-dict (change verb) + につれて / N + によって Gradual change (だんだん) needs につれて. Q11, Q17
Exam trap 1 — the form right before the blank: ます-stem → 次第 (Q2); potential verb + wish → ものなら (Q10); a blank before なる → 〜なく (Q12); V-dict + noun → plain modifier, not volitional (Q21). Check the connection first.
Exam trap 2 — fake forms: several distractors are not real patterns at all (持っていたす, しなくてなんだろう, わけになっている). If an option looks like a twisted version of a real form (お持ちいたす, でなくてなんだろう, ことになっている), it is almost always wrong.
Exam trap 3 — 問題3 is about direction, not just grammar: in a passage, ask whether the blank continues the writer's argument (そうすると, 〜ていくことで, できるであろう) or turns against it (しかしながら, 〜ないと). Here every answer keeps the same positive line.
