「〜たらすぐ」という意味を表す言葉のつづき (“~tara sugu” to iu imi o arawasu kotoba no tsuzuki — "as soon as" expressions, continued)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.58 (PDF page 58) — a one-page follow-up column that sits at the end of 第4章 but continues 第12日「〜たらすぐ」という意味を表す言葉 (the page header says 「p.42参照」)
Grammar points covered
〜るが早いか〜るなり〜るや／や否や〜る／たか〜ないかのうちに〜た(か)と思うと／思ったら〜たとたん〜ます形＋次第
Answer key
No separate key — the page is a worked-correction page: each correct model sentence is printed with a wrong version struck through underneath (p.58, PDF page 58). The "answers" in §2 are the book's own unstruck sentences — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Two lessons only: 1. 接続 — which verb form comes before each "as soon as" expression; 2. 文末 — what kind of sentence ending each one allows. The six "immediately after" forms (〜が早いか … 〜たとたん) describe an actual, already-happened sequence, so they cannot end in a request, command, intention, negative or ongoing state; 〜次第 is the exception and is used precisely for those future endings. Learn the meanings on 第12日; this page is the trap list.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 接続（前に来る形）に注意しよう — watch the form that comes before

The connection alone eliminates many distractors: 〜たが早いか, 〜たなり, 〜るとたん and 帰る次第 are all wrong. The only form that accepts both る and た is 〜か〜ないかのうちに, and it repeats the same verb (終わったか終わらないか).

【2】 文末（文の終わりの表現）に注意しよう — watch the sentence ending

from the book · exam trap

The book's model sentences, each with its struck-through wrong version. The point (stated here in English, added, not in book): the six "immediately" forms report something that actually happened — usually a sudden change in the past — so the second half cannot be a request, order, plan, negative, or a continuing state.

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
授業が終わったか終わらないかのうちに、学生たちは帰り始めた。Jugyou ga owatta ka owaranai ka no uchi ni, gakuseitachi wa kaerihajimeta. The class had barely ended when the students started to leave. क्लास ख़त्म हुई न हुई कि छात्र जाने लगे। ક્લાસ પૂરો થયો ન થયો ત્યાં વિદ્યાર્થીઓ જવા લાગ્યા.
× 授業が終わったか終わらないかのうちに、帰らないでください。× Jugyou ga owatta ka owaranai ka no uchi ni, kaeranaide kudasai. × "Please don't leave the moment class has barely ended." — a request: not allowed. × अनुरोध वाला अंत — ग़लत। × વિનંતી વાળો અંત — ખોટું.
うちに帰ったとたん、雨が降り出した。Uchi ni kaetta totan, ame ga furidashita. The moment I got home, it started to rain. घर पहुँचते ही बारिश शुरू हो गई। ઘરે પહોંચતાં જ વરસાદ શરૂ થયો.
× うちに帰ったとたん、宿題をしなさい。× Uchi ni kaetta totan, shukudai o shinasai. × "The moment you get home, do your homework!" — a command: not allowed. × आदेश वाला अंत — ग़लत। × આદેશ વાળો અંત — ખોટું.
そのニュースを聞くや否やみんな立ち上がった。Sono nyuusu o kiku ya ina ya minna tachiagatta. The instant they heard the news, everyone stood up. वह ख़बर सुनते ही सब उठ खड़े हुए। એ સમાચાર સાંભળતાં જ બધા ઊભા થઈ ગયા.
× そのニュースを聞くや否やみんな立ち上がらなかった。× Sono nyuusu o kiku ya ina ya minna tachiagaranakatta. × "…everyone didn't stand up." — a negative: not allowed. × नकारात्मक अंत — ग़लत। × નકારાત્મક અંત — ખોટું.
彼は国へ帰ったかと思ったら、またすぐオーストラリアに留学した。Kare wa kuni e kaetta ka to omottara, mata sugu oosutoraria ni ryuugaku shita. No sooner had he gone back home than he went off to study in Australia. अभी वह अपने देश लौटा ही था कि फ़ौरन फिर पढ़ने ऑस्ट्रेलिया चला गया। હજી તે વતન પાછો ફર્યો જ હતો કે તરત ફરી ભણવા ઑસ્ટ્રેલિયા ચાલ્યો ગયો.
× 国へ帰ったかと思ったら、結婚するつもりです。× Kuni e kaetta ka to omottara, kekkon suru tsumori desu. × "…I intend to get married." — an intention/plan: not allowed (and it describes the speaker's own action). × इरादे वाला अंत — ग़लत। × ઇરાદા વાળો અંત — ખોટું.
その男は財布をとるが早いか、走って逃げた。Sono otoko wa saifu o toru ga hayai ka, hashitte nigeta. The man had no sooner grabbed the wallet than he ran off. वह आदमी बटुआ झपटते ही दौड़कर भाग गया। એ માણસ પાકીટ ઝૂંટવતાં જ દોડીને ભાગી ગયો.
× 財布をとるが早いか、走って逃げましょう。× Saifu o toru ga hayai ka, hashitte nigemashou. × "…let's run away." — an invitation/proposal: not allowed. × प्रस्ताव वाला अंत — ग़लत। × પ્રસ્તાવ વાળો અંત — ખોટું.
その子は私の顔を見るなり泣き出した。Sono ko wa watashi no kao o miru nari nakidashita. The child burst into tears the moment she saw my face. वह बच्चा मेरा चेहरा देखते ही रो पड़ा। એ બાળક મારો ચહેરો જોતાં જ રડી પડ્યું.
× 私の顔を見るなり泣いていた。× Watashi no kao o miru nari naite ita. × "…was crying." — a continuing state: not allowed; it must be a sudden new action (泣き出した). × चलती हुई अवस्था — ग़लत। × ચાલુ સ્થિતિ — ખોટું.

The book double-underlines the endings that matter: 帰り始めた, 降り出した, 立ち上がった, 留学した, 逃げた, 泣き出した — all past, affirmative, sudden actions. The struck versions end in ないでください, しなさい, なかった, つもりです, ましょう, ていた.

【3】 〜次第 — the one that takes future endings

from the book · N2-level

The book's last box shows 〜ます形＋次第 with exactly the endings the other forms reject (double-underlined: つもりです, てください, ましょう). No struck-through versions are printed for this box.

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
国へ帰り次第、就職するつもりです。Kuni e kaeri shidai, shuushoku suru tsumori desu. I intend to get a job as soon as I go back home. देश लौटते ही नौकरी करने का इरादा है। વતન પાછો ફરતાં જ નોકરી કરવાનો વિચાર છે.
品物がそろい次第、発送してください。Shinamono ga soroi shidai, hassou shite kudasai. Please send the goods as soon as they are all in. सामान पूरा होते ही भेज दीजिए। માલ પૂરો થતાં જ મોકલી આપજો.
向こうにつき次第、写真撮影ということにしましょう。Mukou ni tsuki shidai, shashin satsuei to iu koto ni shimashou. Let's plan on taking photos as soon as we get there. वहाँ पहुँचते ही फ़ोटो खींचने का कार्यक्रम रखते हैं। ત્યાં પહોંચતાં જ ફોટા પાડવાનું રાખીએ.

〜次第 is for planned sequences: "the moment A is done, B will be / should be done". So it pairs with intentions, requests and proposals, and it sounds businesslike. It is not used to narrate a sudden past event (× 家に帰り次第、雨が降り出した — added, not in book).

文末チェック — which sentence is natural?

Pair 1 — 〜たか〜ないかのうちに

授業が終わったか終わらないかのうちに、＿＿＿。Jugyou ga owatta ka owaranai ka no uchi ni, ___.

EN: The class had barely ended when the students started to leave.
HI: क्लास ख़त्म हुई न हुई कि छात्र जाने लगे।
GU: ક્લાસ પૂરો થયો ન થયો ત્યાં વિદ્યાર્થીઓ જવા લાગ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a学生たちは帰り始めたgakuseitachi wa kaerihajimetathe students started to leaveछात्र जाने लगेવિદ્યાર્થીઓ જવા લાગ્યા
b帰らないでくださいkaeranaide kudasaiplease don't leaveकृपया मत जाइएકૃપા કરી ન જાઓ

Why: 〜か〜ないかのうちに narrates something that happened almost at the same moment, so it ends in a past fact (帰り始めた). A request (〜ないでください) is struck out by the book. To make a request, use 〜たらすぐ／〜次第.

Full sentence: うちに帰ったとたん、雨が降り出した。Uchi ni kaetta totan, ame ga furidashita.
EN: The moment I got home, it started to rain.
HI: घर पहुँचते ही बारिश शुरू हो गई।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a雨が降り出したame ga furidashitait started to rainबारिश शुरू हो गईવરસાદ શરૂ થયો

Full sentence: そのニュースを聞くや否やみんな立ち上がった。Sono nyuusu o kiku ya ina ya minna tachiagatta.
EN: The instant they heard the news, everyone stood up.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All seven forms mean "as soon as", but they split cleanly by connection and by what can follow:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜が早いか~ga hayai ka no sooner … than V-dict + が早いか Written, N1; past narration only. 財布をとるが早いか逃げた
〜なり~nari the moment (same subject) V-dict + なり Same subject, sudden reaction; not ていた. 顔を見るなり泣き出した
〜や／や否や~ya / ya ina ya the instant V-dict + や(否や) Very formal; no negative ending. 聞くや否や立ち上がった
〜か〜ないかのうちに~ka ~nai ka no uchi ni barely … when V-dict／V-た + か + V-ない + かのうちに Repeats the verb; the two events almost overlap. 終わったか終わらないかのうちに
〜た(か)と思うと／思ったら~ta (ka) to omou to / omottara just when … (then) V-た + (か)と思うと／思ったら Observing someone else; not one's own plans. 帰ったかと思ったら留学した
〜たとたん~ta totan the instant (unexpected) V-た + とたん Followed by a surprise; no commands. 帰ったとたん雨が降り出した
〜次第~shidai as soon as (planned) V-ます stem + 次第 The only one for future requests/intentions; not for past narration. 帰り次第就職するつもりです
Exam trap 1 — check the ending first: if the second half is 〜てください／〜なさい／〜ましょう／〜つもりだ／〜たい, the only "as soon as" form that fits is 〜次第 (or plain 〜たらすぐ). The other six need a past, affirmative, actually-happened event.
Exam trap 2 — the connection decides: dictionary form → が早いか／なり／や否や; た-form → とたん／たかと思うと; ます-stem → 次第; both → 〜か〜ないかのうちに. A distractor with the wrong form (× 見たなり, × 帰るとたん) can be rejected without reading further.
Exam trap 3 — ongoing states (added, not in book): なり／とたん／や否や want a change-of-state verb (〜出す, 〜始める, 立ち上がる). 〜ていた, いる, ある after them sound wrong — the book's 泣いていた example.
