基本文法 — やる、あげる、もらう、くれる (Kihon bunpou — yaru, ageru, morau, kureru (giving and receiving verbs))

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.76 (PDF page 76) — a one-page basic-grammar column at the end of 第6章
Grammar points covered
あげる・やる (give: me → others)くれる (give: others → me／my side)もらう (receive)〜てあげる／〜てやる〜てくれる〜てもらう／〜ていただく〜てもらえる (possibility)
Answer key
The column prints its own 解答 at the foot of p.76 (PDF page 76) — confirmed, not guessed: 1. b、a  2. a  3. b、c  4. a  5. c  6. b. (Item 1 has two blanks — first b, second a; in item 3 both b and c are accepted.)
Notes
A basic-grammar review of giving and receiving (授受表現), not N1 grammar as such — but N1 sentences constantly hide them inside longer chains (〜てやっていただけないでしょうか, 〜てもらってきてあげる), and choosing the wrong direction makes the sentence mean something else. The instruction is 「正しいものを選んでください。一つだけとは限りません。」 ("choose the correct one; there may be more than one") — so some items accept two options. The column has no ●-points or examples; §1 below is a short explanation (added, not in book) built only on what the six items use. Option letters a／b／c are the book's.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Who gives to whom — the three directions

N2-level basics · (added, not in book)

(added, not in book) Japanese picks the verb by the direction of the giving, seen from the speaker. やる is the casual／downward version of あげる (to children, animals, plants); さしあげる, くださる and いただく are the polite forms.

VerbDirectionMeaning (EN)Hindi / Gujarati
あげる (さしあげる, やる)ageru (sashiageru, yaru)giver = me／my side → someone else (A は B に あげる)give (away from me)देना (मैं → दूसरे को)
આપવું (હું → બીજાને)
くれる (くださる)kureru (kudasaru)someone else → me／my side (A が 私に くれる)give (to me)(मुझे) देना
(મને) આપવું
もらう (いただく)morau (itadaku)receiver's viewpoint: 私は A に／から もらうreceive, get(किसी से) पाना / लेना
(કોઈ પાસેથી) મેળવવું / લેવું

The quick test: is the thing (or favour) coming towards me／my group? → くれる. Going away from me? → あげる／やる. Am I (or the subject) the receiver, with the giver marked by に? → もらう. All three verbs work the same way with a て-form attached to describe a favour.

【2】 The favour forms 〜てあげる／〜てくれる／〜てもらう

N2-level basics · (added, not in book)

(added, not in book) Attaching the giving verb to a て-form turns the action into a favour. Illustrated with the column's own sentences:

JapaneseWhy this verb (EN)HindiGujarati
中山さんに見せてもらってください。Nakayama-san ni misete moratte kudasai. Ask Nakayama-san to show it to you (you receive the favour of being shown). नाकायामा-सान से दिखवा लीजिए। નાકાયામા-સાન પાસેથી બતાવડાવી લો.
川田さんにパンフレットを見せてあげてください。Kawada-san ni panfuretto o misete agete kudasai. Please show the pamphlet to Kawada-san (do him the favour). कावादा-सान को पैम्फ़लेट दिखा दीजिए। કાવાદા-સાનને પેમ્ફલેટ બતાવી દો.
花に水をやってくれる人hana ni mizu o yatte kureru hito someone who will water the flowers for me (やる = give water to a plant; くれる = for me). मेरे लिए पौधों को पानी देने वाला મારા માટે ફૂલોને પાણી પાનાર
息子に数学を教えてやっていただけないでしょうか。Musuko ni suugaku o oshiete yatte itadakenai deshou ka. Could you possibly teach my son maths (for him)? क्या आप मेरे बेटे को गणित पढ़ा देंगे? શું આપ મારા દીકરાને ગણિત શીખવી આપશો?

Two rules make the column's chains work. (1) When the favour goes to my own family member (息子に), the speaker uses 〜てやる (humble towards the listener), then adds 〜ていただけないでしょうか to ask the listener for it. (2) 〜てもらえる (potential of もらう) means "you can have it done for you" — so a service can be described either as 届けてくれる (they deliver for us) or 届けてもらえる (we can have it delivered).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

The column's exercise (p.76): 「正しいものを選んでください。一つだけとは限りません。」 — choose the correct one; there may be more than one. Six items, seven blanks (item 1 has two). The option letters a／b／c and their wording are the book's; the correct rows follow the on-page 解答: 1. b、a  2. a  3. b、c  4. a  5. c  6. b.

1-①

川田さん、旅行のパンフレットがないんですか。じゃあ、中山さんが持っていますから、｛a 見せてあげてください。／ b 見せてもらってください。｝Kawada-san, ryokou no panfuretto ga nai n desu ka. Jaa, Nakayama-san ga motte imasu kara, {a misete agete kudasai. / b misete moratte kudasai.}

Full sentence: 川田さん、旅行のパンフレットがないんですか。じゃあ、中山さんが持っていますから、見せてもらってください。Kawada-san, ryokou no panfuretto ga nai n desu ka. Jaa, Nakayama-san ga motte imasu kara, misete moratte kudasai.
EN: Kawada-san, you don't have the travel pamphlet? Well, Nakayama-san has one, so ask him to show it to you.
HI: कावादा-सान, आपके पास यात्रा का पैम्फ़लेट नहीं है? तो नाकायामा-सान के पास है, उनसे दिखवा लीजिए।
GU: કાવાદા-સાન, તમારી પાસે પ્રવાસનું પેમ્ફલેટ નથી? તો નાકાયામા-સાન પાસે છે, તેમની પાસેથી બતાવડાવી લો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見せてあげてください。Misete agete kudasai.please show it (to someone)दिखा दीजिएબતાવી દો
b見せてもらってください。Misete moratte kudasai.please have him show it to youउनसे दिखवा लीजिएતેમની પાસેથી બતાવડાવી લો

Why: The speaker is talking to Kawada, who lacks the pamphlet — Kawada is the receiver of the favour, Nakayama the giver. So "receive the favour of being shown" — 見せてもらって. a would tell Kawada to show something to someone, but he has nothing to show.

1-②

中山さん、川田さんに旅行のパンフレットを｛a 見せてあげてください。／ b 見せてもらってください。｝Nakayama-san, Kawada-san ni ryokou no panfuretto o {a misete agete kudasai. / b misete moratte kudasai.}

Full sentence: 中山さん、川田さんに旅行のパンフレットを見せてあげてください。Nakayama-san, Kawada-san ni ryokou no panfuretto o misete agete kudasai.

GU: નાકાયામા-સાન, કાવાદા-સાનને પ્રવાસનું પેમ્ફલેટ બતાવી દો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見せてあげてください。Misete agete kudasai.please show it (to him)दिखा दीजिएબતાવી દો

Why: Now the speaker is talking to Nakayama, the one with the pamphlet: he is the giver, Kawada (marked with に) the receiver — 見せてあげて. With b, 川田さんに would become the giver ("have Kawada show it to you"), the reverse of the situation.

2

今、あの化粧品を買うと、ショッピングバッグを｛a くれますよ。／ b もらいますよ。｝Ima, ano keshouhin o kau to, shoppingu baggu o {a kuremasu yo. / b moraimasu yo.}

Full sentence: 今、あの化粧品を買うと、ショッピングバッグをくれますよ。Ima, ano keshouhin o kau to, shoppingu baggu o kuremasu yo.
EN: If you buy those cosmetics now, they'll give you a shopping bag.
HI: अभी वह कॉस्मेटिक ख़रीदोगे तो वे शॉपिंग बैग देंगे।
GU: અત્યારે એ કોસ્મેટિક ખરીદશો તો તેઓ શોપિંગ બેગ આપશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aくれますよ。Kuremasu yo.they'll give (you／us)वे (आपको) देंगेતેઓ (તમને) આપશે
bもらいますよ。Moraimasu yo.(someone) receives(कोई) पाएगा(કોઈ) મેળવશે

Why: The unstated subject is the shop, giving to the customer (the listener, on "our" side) — くれます. もらいます would need the receiver as subject and states a plain fact about one's own action, which sounds odd as advice; もらえますよ ("you can get one") would have worked, but it is not an option.

3

このスーパーの会員になると、買った物を家まで｛a 届けてあげる／ b 届けてくれる／ c 届けてもらえる｝そうですよ。Kono suupaa no kaiin ni naru to, katta mono o ie made {a todokete ageru / b todokete kureru / c todokete moraeru} sou desu yo.

Full sentence: このスーパーの会員になると、買った物を家まで届けてくれる（届けてもらえる）そうですよ。Kono suupaa no kaiin ni naru to, katta mono o ie made todokete kureru (todokete moraeru) sou desu yo.
EN: I hear that if you become a member of this supermarket, they deliver what you buy to your home (you can have it delivered).
HI: सुना है इस सुपरमार्केट का मेंबर बनने पर ख़रीदा हुआ सामान घर तक पहुँचा देते हैं (पहुँचवा सकते हैं)।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે આ સુપરમાર્કેટના સભ્ય બનો તો ખરીદેલો માલ ઘર સુધી પહોંચાડી આપે છે (પહોંચાડાવી શકાય છે).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a届けてあげるtodokete agerudeliver (for someone else)(किसी और के लिए) पहुँचा देना(બીજા માટે) પહોંચાડી દેવું

Why: The favour comes from the store to us (members). Seen from the giver's side towards us: 届けてくれる; seen from our side as something we are able to receive: 届けてもらえる — the key accepts both. 届けてあげる would mean the speaker's side delivers to someone else.

4

中田さん、実はちょっとお願いがあるんですけど……。うちの息子に数学を｛a 教えてやっていただけないでしょうか。／ b 教えていただいてもらえないでしょうか。／ c 教えてあげていただかないでしょうか。｝Nakata-san, jitsu wa chotto onegai ga aru n desu kedo…. Uchi no musuko ni suugaku o {a oshiete yatte itadakenai deshou ka. / b oshiete itadaite moraenai deshou ka. / c oshiete agete itadakanai deshou ka.}

Full sentence: 中田さん、実はちょっとお願いがあるんですけど……。うちの息子に数学を教えてやっていただけないでしょうか。Nakata-san, jitsu wa chotto onegai ga aru n desu kedo…. Uchi no musuko ni suugaku o oshiete yatte itadakenai deshou ka.
EN: Nakata-san, actually I have a small favour to ask… Could you possibly teach my son maths?
HI: नाकाता-सान, दरअसल एक छोटी-सी गुज़ारिश है…… क्या आप मेरे बेटे को गणित पढ़ा देंगे?
GU: નાકાતા-સાન, ખરેખર એક નાની વિનંતી છે…… શું આપ મારા દીકરાને ગણિત શીખવી આપશો?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a教えてやっていただけないでしょうか。Oshiete yatte itadakenai deshou ka.could you teach (him) for me?क्या (उसे) पढ़ा देंगे?શું (તેને) શીખવી આપશો?

Why: Two favours stacked: Nakata teaches my son (a family member, so the humble 〜てやる), and I ask Nakata for that favour (〜ていただけないでしょうか — potential negative = polite request). b stacks two receiving verbs (いただいて＋もらえない); c uses 〜ていただかない (plain negative, not potential), which is not a request.

5

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The column's traps are all about direction — who gives, who receives, and whose side the speaker is on:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
あげる ↔ くれるageru ↔ kureru give (outwards) ↔ give (to me) A が B に あげる / A が 私に くれる くれる only when the receiver is me／my side; 僕が…くれる is impossible. Q2, Q6.
くれる ↔ もらうkureru ↔ morau give to me ↔ I receive A が くれる / 私が A に もらう Same event, different subject; もらえる (potential) = "can get". Q3 (届けてくれる＝届けてもらえる).
〜てあげる ↔ 〜てもらうte ageru ↔ te morau do for someone ↔ have someone do V-て + あげる／もらう Depends on whether the listener is the giver or the receiver. Q1-① vs Q1-②.
あげる ↔ やるageru ↔ yaru give ↔ give (to plants／animals／my family) N に V-て + やる やる for plants, pets and, humbly, one's own children. Q4 (息子に教えてやって), Q5 (花に水をやる).
〜ていただけない ↔ 〜ていただかないte itadakenai ↔ te itadakanai could you? ↔ won't receive V-て + いただけないでしょうか Only the potential negative is a polite request. Q4.
Trap 1 — who is the listener? The same situation takes 〜てもらって when you speak to the receiver and 〜てあげて when you speak to the giver (item 1). Identify the addressee before choosing.
Trap 2 — くれる never has "I" as giver: 僕が／私が…くれる is always wrong; use あげる (item 6).
Trap 3 — stacked favours (N1 extension, added, not in book): N1 options love chains like 〜てやっていただけないでしょうか. Read them from the inside out: 教えて (action) → やって (for my son) → いただけない (from you, as a request). Any chain with two receiving verbs in a row (いただいてもらう) is wrong.
