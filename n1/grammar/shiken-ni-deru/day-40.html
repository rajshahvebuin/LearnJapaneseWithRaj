Notes
The book gives no N1 tag anywhere in this unit, so every point is shown as N2-level — but 敬語 still turns up in N1 文法 and 読解 items, usually as "whose action is it?" traps. The intro (p.124) defines 敬語 as words that show respect to the listener by raising the other person's actions (尊敬語) or lowering your own (謙譲語), plus ていねい語. The three habitual traps: using a humble verb for a superior's action (×先生が参ります), using humble お〜する for someone else, and confusing いただけませんか (request) with いただきませんか (not a request) — see the メモ and §3.

Always ask "who is doing it?" before choosing. The same plain verb splits into two words: 先生がいらっしゃる vs 私が参る; 先生がご覧になる vs 私が拝見する. Note that 伺う covers both "go/visit" and "ask/hear", and いらっしゃる covers いる, 行く and 来る.

召す only has an honorific use — there is no humble partner (＊ in the book's table). The three set phrases are best learned whole; お年を召した方 is the polite way to say "an elderly person", much softer than 年寄り.

【3】 〜でいらっしゃる／〜でございます (honorific / polite です)

N2-level · 敬語 polite/formal
Reading~de irassharu / ~de gozaimasu
Meaning (EN)Polite versions of 〜です: 〜でいらっしゃる raises the person you are describing (尊敬語); 〜でございます is the humble/very polite form for yourself or things
Meaning (HI)〜です का आदरसूचक (सामने वाले के लिए) / अति विनम्र (अपने लिए) रूप — "हैं / हूँ"
Meaning (GU)〜です નું આદરસૂચક (સામેની વ્યક્તિ માટે) / અતિ નમ્ર (પોતાના માટે) રૂપ — "છે / છું"
Connection (接続)N／な-adj + でいらっしゃる  |  N／な-adj + でございます
RegisterFormal: phone calls, reception, customer service.
Typical useChecking someone's identity (〜さんでいらっしゃいますか) and naming yourself (〜でございます).

山田さんでいらっしゃいます。（〜です）Yamada-san de irasshaimasu. This is Mr Yamada. (honorific, about him) ये यामादा जी हैं। (आदरसूचक) આ યામાદા સાહેબ છે. (આદરસૂચક)
山田でございます。（〜です）Yamada de gozaimasu. This is Yamada (speaking). (humble, about myself) मैं यामादा बोल रहा हूँ। (विनम्र) હું યામાદા બોલું છું. (નમ્ર)

Same です, two directions: with a name + さん you are talking about the other person → でいらっしゃる; your own name (no さん) → でございます. Mixing them up (×川田さんでございますか) treats the listener as if they were your own side — 練習問題 ⑥ tests exactly this.

【4】 お／ご〜になる (regular honorific pattern)

N2-level · 尊敬語 honorific
Readingo / go ~ ni naru
Meaning (EN)The standard way to make someone else's action honorific (尊敬語)
Meaning (HI)सामने वाले/बड़े व्यक्ति की क्रिया को आदर से कहने का सामान्य तरीक़ा
Meaning (GU)સામેની/મોટી વ્યક્તિની ક્રિયાને આદરથી કહેવાની સામાન્ય રીત
Connection (接続)お + V-stem (和語) + になる  |  ご + する-noun (漢語) + になる
RegisterPolite; the default honorific when a verb has no special word.
Typical use先生がお書きになった本, ご利用になれます (potential: お／ご〜になれる).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (和語)お + V-stem + になるお書きになるo-kaki ni naru
する-noun (漢語)ご + N + になるご利用になるgo-riyou ni naru
Potentialお／ご〜 + になれるご利用になれますgo-riyou ni naremasu
い／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used — see 【7】.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生がお書きになった本ですね。Sensei ga o-kaki ni natta hon desu ne. This is the book you (the teacher) wrote, isn't it? यह वही किताब है न जो सर ने लिखी है? આ એ જ પુસ્તક છે ને જે સાહેબે લખ્યું છે?
どなたでもご利用になれます。Donata demo go-riyou ni naremasu. Anyone may use it. कोई भी इसका इस्तेमाल कर सकते हैं। કોઈ પણ તેનો ઉપયોગ કરી શકે છે.

お goes with native Japanese verbs (書く, 待つ, 帰る), ご with Sino-Japanese する-nouns (利用, 連絡, 出席). The ending になる is what makes it honorific — the same お／ご with する is humble (【6】), so check the ending, not the prefix.

【5】 れる／られる (honorific passive-form)

N2-level · 尊敬語 honorific (lighter)
Readingreru / rareru
Meaning (EN)The passive form used as a light honorific for someone else's action
Meaning (HI)कर्मवाच्य जैसा रूप, पर अर्थ है हल्का आदर (सामने वाले की क्रिया)

何時に出発されますか。Nanji ni shuppatsu saremasu ka. What time will you be leaving? आप कितने बजे रवाना होंगे? આપ કેટલા વાગ્યે નીકળશો?

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習問題 item today comes down to one question: whose action is it? The other person's → 尊敬語; mine / my side's → 謙譲語. These are the look-alike pairs the items actually test:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
いらっしゃる vs 参るirassharu / mairu go / come special verbs いらっしゃる raises the subject; 参る lowers it. 先生がいらっしゃる ／ 私が参る (Q1).
ご覧になる vs 拝見するgoran ni naru / haiken suru see, watch special verbs Honorific vs humble. The other person watches ／ I watch (Q2).
お〜になる vs お〜するo~ni naru / o~suru regular patterns お／ご + stem／N + になる／する Same prefix; only the ending tells you the direction. お聞きになりませんか (you) ／ お持ちします (me) (Q5).
〜でいらっしゃる vs 〜でございます~de irassharu / ~de gozaimasu polite です N + でいらっしゃる／でございます Other person vs own side / things. 川田さんでいらっしゃいますか ／ 山田でございます (Q6).
申す vs 申し上げるmousu / moushiageru say (humble) 〜と申します ／ 〜を申し上げます 申す for names and general statements; 申し上げる for saying something up to a superior. 林と申します ／ 意見を申し上げます (Q6, メモ).
〜ていただけませんか vs 〜ていただきませんか~te itadakemasen ka / ~te itadakimasen ka request vs not a request V-て + いただけ／いただき + ませんか Only the potential いただける makes a request. Asking a favour (Q7).
〜していただく vs 〜させていただく~shite itadaku / ~sasete itadaku you do it (for me) vs let me do it V-て／causative て + いただく Causative = I act with your permission. Offering help or asking leave (Q3, Q9).
〜てくださる vs 〜ていただく~te kudasaru / ~te itadaku (you) kindly do / (I) receive your doing V-て + くださる／いただく くださる: the giver is subject; いただく: the receiver is subject. お医者様は…診てくださる (Q11).
お目にかかる vs お目にかけるo-me ni kakaru / o-me ni kakeru meet vs show humble set phrases かかる = 会う; かける = 見せる. お目にかかれるのを楽しみに (Q8).
お暑うございます vs ×暑くてございますo-atsuu gozaimasu it is hot (very polite) い-adj 〜く → 〜う + ございます The い-adjective sound change is obligatory. Letter openings (Q8).
Exam trap 1 — find the subject first: in Q2, Q6 and Q8 one sentence has two or three different subjects (the teacher appears, I watch; you come, I meet you). Mark each verb's subject before choosing — 尊敬語 for the other person, 謙譲語 for yourself.
Exam trap 2 — "your side" includes family and company: when you talk to an outsider, your own family member or boss is lowered (春子は出かけております), but the caller raises the same person (春子さんはいらっしゃいますか). Q6's third blank tests this.
Exam trap 3 — one kana decides it: いただけませんか (request) vs いただきませんか; お目にかかる (meet) vs お目にかける (show); 〜していただく vs 〜させていただく. Read the options letter by letter.
