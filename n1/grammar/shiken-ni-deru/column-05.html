Notes
A basic-grammar review: 「〜(さ)れる、〜(さ)せる、〜(さ)せられる」の練習です。正しいものを選んでください — choose the correct voice from the bracket. The column has no ●-points or examples; §1 below is a short explanation (added, not in book) built only on what the 16 items test. N1 relevance: items 13–14 are the N1 expression 余儀なくされる／余儀なくさせる (taught properly on 第32日), and items 6–8 test the "spontaneous" causative-passive 考えさせられる／教えられる vs plain 感動する — a favourite N1 nuance trap. The options are printed unnumbered, in the order shown; they are labelled a, b, c here in that printed order.

1 Grammar Points

Quick test: is the subject doing it (plain), suffering / receiving it (passive), making someone else do it (causative), or being forced to do it (causative-passive)? Words such as 強制的に, いやだった point to the causative-passive; 〜てしまった with a victim (妹に) points to the passive.

【2】 Three traps the column tests

N2/N1 nuance · (added, not in book)

(added, not in book) Illustrated with the column's own answer sentences:

JapaneseWhy this voice (EN)HindiGujarati
町が発展するにつれて…
高速道路が開通して…Machi ga hatten suru ni tsurete… / Kousoku douro ga kaitsuu shite…		 発展する, 開通する are already intransitive する-verbs ("develop", "open to traffic") — no passive needed. 発展する／開通する ख़ुद ही अकर्मक हैं — कर्मवाच्य की ज़रूरत नहीं। 発展する／開通する પોતે જ અકર્મક છે — કર્મણિની જરૂર નથી.
電気が発明されて…Denki ga hatsumei sarete… 発明する is transitive (someone invents it), so with 電気が as subject it must be passive. 発明する सकर्मक है, इसलिए 電気が के साथ कर्मवाच्य चाहिए। 発明する સકર્મક છે, એટલે 電気が સાથે કર્મણિ જોઈએ.
いろいろと考えさせられる／教えられた／とても感動したiroiro to kangaesaserareru / oshierareta / totemo kandou shita 考えさせられる and 教えられる express a thought or lesson that arises in me from what I saw (spontaneous nuance). But 感動する is already "be moved" — 感動された／させた would be wrong. 考えさせられる／教えられる = अपने-आप सोचने/सीखने पर मजबूर होना; 感動する पहले से ही "भावुक होना" है। 考えさせられる／教えられる = આપોઆપ વિચારવા/શીખવા પ્રેરાવું; 感動する પહેલેથી જ "ભાવુક થવું" છે.
辞任を余儀なくされた／辞任を余儀なくさせたjinin o yoginaku sareta / jinin o yoginaku saseta Person as subject → 余儀なくされた ("was forced to"); the cause (疑惑の発覚は) as subject → 余儀なくさせた ("forced him to"). व्यक्ति कर्ता → された (मजबूर हुआ); कारण कर्ता → させた (मजबूर किया)। વ્યક્તિ કર્તા → された (મજબૂર થયો); કારણ કર્તા → させた (મજબૂર કર્યો).

The N1 point is items 13–14: the same fact can be told from the person's side (passive) or from the cause's side (causative). And remember the "gratitude" version: 親に食べさせてもらっている (I am fed by my parents, thankfully) — not the forced 食べさせられて.

基本文法 — choose the correct voice

1

電車が遅れて、友達を30分も［待って／待たせて／待たされて］しまいました。Densha ga okurete, tomodachi o sanjuppun mo [matte / matasete / matasarete] shimaimashita.

Full sentence: 電車が遅れて、友達を30分も待たせてしまいました。Densha ga okurete, tomodachi o sanjuppun mo matasete shimaimashita.
EN: The train was late, and I ended up keeping my friend waiting for a whole 30 minutes.
HI: ट्रेन लेट हो गई और मुझे दोस्त को पूरे 30 मिनट इंतज़ार करवाना पड़ा।
GU: ટ્રેન મોડી પડી અને મારે મિત્રને આખી 30 મિનિટ રાહ જોવડાવવી પડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a待ってmattewaited (for)इंतज़ार कियाરાહ જોઈ
b待たせてmatasetekept (him) waitingइंतज़ार करवायाરાહ જોવડાવી
c待たされてmatasaretewas kept waitingइंतज़ार करना पड़ाરાહ જોવી પડી

Why: My train was late, so I made my friend wait: 友達を + causative 待たせる. 友達を待って would mean I waited for him; 待たされて would make me the victim, but I was the late one.

2

町が発展［する／される／させる］につれて、生活も豊かになってきた。Machi ga hatten [suru / sareru / saseru] ni tsurete, seikatsu mo yutaka ni natte kita.

Full sentence: 町が発展するにつれて、生活も豊かになってきた。Machi ga hatten suru ni tsurete, seikatsu mo yutaka ni natte kita.
EN: As the town developed, people's lives also became more prosperous.
HI: जैसे-जैसे शहर का विकास हुआ, जीवन भी समृद्ध होता गया।
GU: જેમ જેમ શહેરનો વિકાસ થયો, તેમ જીવન પણ સમૃદ્ધ બનતું ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aするsurudevelopsविकसित होता हैવિકસે છે
bされるsareruis developedविकसित किया जाता हैવિકસાવાય છે

Why: 発展する is intransitive ("develop"), and 町が is its subject, so the plain form is right. There is no agent doing the developing in this sentence; 町を発展させる would need someone as subject.

3

GU: લાગે છે કે પહેલાં તેની પાસે જબરદસ્તીથી રાસાયણિક હથિયારો પર સંશોધન કરાવાતું હતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

6

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: Mother (subject) + 赤ちゃんに (the one who actually drinks) + ミルクを → causative 飲ませる "give to drink". Hindi/Gujarati have the same causative pair: पीना → पिलाना, પીવું → પિવડાવવું.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The column's traps are all "same verb, different voice". Compare:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
待つ／待たせる／待たされるmatsu / mataseru / matasareru wait / keep waiting / be kept waiting 人を待つ / 人を待たせる / 人に待たされる Who was late decides it. item 1
発展する・開通する vs 発明されるhatten suru · kaitsuu suru vs hatsumei sareru develop · open (自) vs be invented (他→受身) Nが自動詞する / Nが他動詞される Intransitive する-verbs need no passive. items 2, 4 vs 5
〜させられる (forced) vs 〜させられる (spontaneous)saserareru be made to / be led to (think) V-させられる 強制的に・いやだった → forced; 考えさせられる → thought arises in me. items 3, 9 vs 6
教えられる vs 感動するoshierareru vs kandou suru learn from / be moved 〜に教えられる / 〜に感動する 感動する is already "be moved": no voice change. items 7 vs 8
取られる vs 取らせるtorareru vs toraseru have taken (victim) / let take 人に取られる / 人に取らせる Regret (てしまった) → passive. item 10
余儀なくされる vs 余儀なくさせるyoginaku sareru vs yoginaku saseru be forced to / force (someone) to 人は〜を余儀なくされる / 原因は人に〜を余儀なくさせる Person as subject vs cause as subject. items 13 vs 14
食べさせられる vs 食べさせてもらうtabesaserareru vs tabesasete morau be forced to eat / be fed (favour) 人に V-させられる / 人に V-させてもらう Suffering vs gratitude. item 15
Trap 1 — is the verb already intransitive? 発展する, 開通する, 感動する already describe what happens to the subject. Adding される／させる is the distractor. Only transitive verbs (発明する) need the passive when the thing becomes the subject.
Trap 2 — 考えさせられる／教えられる are not "forced": with verbs of thinking and learning, the causative-passive or passive expresses a natural reaction to what you saw or heard (いろいろと考えさせられる). N1 reading passages use this constantly.
Trap 3 — the N1 pair 余儀なくされる／させる (added, not in book: see 第32日): read the subject. Person + は → された; cause + は + person + に → させた.
