Notes
This day has no new grammar: it trains the JLPT 問題2 format, where four tiles fill four blanks and you answer with the number of the tile on ★. The ★ is not always in the same slot (here it moves between the 1st and 4th blanks). The N2 set is labelled N2-level and the N1 set N1 as in the book. The items recycle forms from earlier days — こそ, ほど, にもかかわらず, とたん, かねない, かたわら, なり, というより — so §1 lists which form each item turns on.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 How a ★ question works — the book's 例題 (p.132)

from the book

例題 次の文の ＿★＿ に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。 (Reidai: tsugi no bun no ★ ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.) — Choose the best tile for the ★ blank. (HI: ★ वाली ख़ाली जगह के लिए 1–4 में से सबसे सही टुकड़ा चुनिए। GU: ★ વાળી ખાલી જગ્યા માટે 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય ટુકડો પસંદ કરો.)

あそこで ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ はジョンさんです。Asoko de ___ ___ ★ ___ wa Jon-san desu.

Tiles: 1 本 (hon)　2 読んでいる (yonde iru)　3 を (o)　4 人 (hito)

解答の仕方 (how to answer, the book's steps):

1. 正しい文はこうです。 (Tadashii bun wa kou desu.) — The correct sentence is: 1 本 → 3 を → 2 読んでいる → 4 人.

2. ★ に入る番号を解答用紙にマークします。 (★ ni hairu bangou o kaitou youshi ni maaku shimasu.) — Mark the number of the tile on ★ (here 2) on the answer sheet. ※この本では解答用紙を省略しています (this book leaves out the answer sheet).

1 本→3 を→★ 2 読んでいる→4 人

あそこで本を読んでいる人はジョンさんです。Asoko de hon o yonde iru hito wa Jon-san desu.

EN: The person reading a book over there is John. HI: वहाँ जो व्यक्ति किताब पढ़ रहा है, वह जॉन है। GU: ત્યાં જે વ્યક્તિ પુસ્તક વાંચે છે, તે જૉન છે.

Strategy (added, not in book): start from the tile with the strictest connection — a particle that needs a noun before it (を), a form that needs a specific verb shape (とたん needs た-form, なり needs dictionary form), or a set pair (〜というより, 〜こそ…べき). Lock that pair, then place the rest, and only at the end read off the ★ tile.

In ★ items the "trap" is usually a tile that seems to fit two places. The connection rules below are the ones today's items depend on — knowing what each form must attach to decides the order.

Exam trap 1 — ★ moves around: in this set the ★ is in the 1st slot (N2 Q10, Q13; N1 Q2, Q9, Q12, Q13), 2nd, 3rd or 4th. Build the whole sentence first, then count to the ★ — never assume it is the 3rd blank.
Exam trap 2 — the fixed text after the blanks: often decides the last tile — ことにした needs ことに (N2 Q10), かねない needs a stem (N1 Q7), なり needs a dictionary-form verb (N1 Q10), にあこがれる needs に (N1 Q16). Work backwards from it.
Exam trap 3 — two tiles can swap without changing ★: in N1 Q1 その時の生活環境に and 体を can be ordered either way; the ★ (合わせる) does not move. If two orders both seem fine, check whether the ★ tile actually changes before worrying.
