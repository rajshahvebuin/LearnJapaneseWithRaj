Notes
A basic-grammar review, not N1 grammar as such — but choosing the right member of a 自動詞／他動詞 pair is something N1 still punishes, especially inside longer patterns (〜てある vs 〜ている, passive vs intransitive). The column has no ●-points or examples; the instruction is 「（　）の動詞の適当な方を選んで、＿＿に正しい形にして入れてください」 — pick the right verb of the pair and conjugate it. §1 below is a short explanation (added, not in book) built only on the five pairs the column uses.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 自動詞 vs 他動詞 — the five pairs in this column

N2-level basics · (added, not in book)

(added, not in book) A 自動詞 (intransitive verb) describes something that happens by itself — its subject takes が. A 他動詞 (transitive verb) is something a person does to an object — the object takes を. The column's five pairs:

自動詞 (intransitive) — が他動詞 (transitive) — をMeaning (EN)Hindi / Gujarati
〜が見つかる~ga mitsukaru〜を見つける~o mitsukerube found ↔ findमिलना / मिल जाना ↔ ढूँढ निकालना
મળવું / મળી જવું ↔ શોધી કાઢવું
〜が始まる~ga hajimaru〜を始める~o hajimeru(something) begins ↔ begin (something)शुरू होना ↔ शुरू करना
શરૂ થવું ↔ શરૂ કરવું
〜が開く~ga aku〜を開ける~o akeru(something) opens ↔ open (something)खुलना ↔ खोलना
ખૂલવું ↔ ખોલવું
〜が入る~ga hairu〜を入れる~o irerugo in, fit in ↔ put inअंदर जाना / समाना ↔ डालना
અંદર જવું / સમાવું ↔ મૂકવું / નાખવું
〜が上がる~ga agaru〜を上げる~o agerugo up, rise ↔ raiseऊपर जाना / बढ़ना ↔ बढ़ाना
ઉપર જવું / વધવું ↔ વધારવું
〜が下がる~ga sagaru〜を下げる~o sagerugo down, fall ↔ lowerनीचे आना / घटना ↔ घटाना
નીચે આવવું / ઘટવું ↔ ઘટાડવું

Sound pattern worth noticing: 〜かる／まる／がる verbs are intransitive (見つかる, 始まる, 上がる, 下がる), their 〜ける／める／げる partners are transitive. 開く／開ける and 入る／入れる follow the u-verb (自) ↔ 〜eru (他) pattern. All of this is general knowledge, not printed in the book.

【2】 How to choose — three quick tests

N2-level basics · (added, not in book)

(added, not in book) Every blank in the column can be solved with these checks, illustrated with the column's own sentences:

JapaneseWhy this verb (EN)HindiGujarati
いいアパートが見つかりました。Ii apaato ga mitsukarimashita. が + 自動詞: the result is in focus — "a good flat turned up". अच्छा फ़्लैट मिल गया। (परिणाम पर ज़ोर) સારો ફ્લેટ મળી ગયો. (પરિણામ પર ભાર)
友達が見つけてくれた。Tomodachi ga mitsukete kureta. 人が + を + 他動詞: someone did it — "my friend found it for me". दोस्त ने ढूँढ दिया। (करने वाले पर ज़ोर) મિત્રે શોધી આપ્યો. (કરનાર પર ભાર)
ふたが開かない。Futa ga akanai. 自動詞 negative: "the lid won't open (by itself, however I try)". ढक्कन नहीं खुल रहा। ઢાંકણું ખૂલતું નથી.
ふたを開けてみてください。Futa o akete mite kudasai. Request to a person → 他動詞 (〜てください, 〜てくれる, 〜てあげる, 〜ていただく). ढक्कन खोलकर देखिए। ઢાંકણું ખોલી જુઓ.

(1) Particle: が before the blank → usually 自動詞; を (stated or understood) → 他動詞. (2) Requests and favours (〜てください／〜てくれる／〜てあげる／〜ていただく) need a 他動詞 — a person must act. (3) Things changing on their own (taxes rising, salaries not rising, a lid that won't open, a bag that is too full for things to fit) take a 自動詞.

基本文法 — choose the verb and put it in the right form

1-①（見つける・見つかる）

A：いいアパートが＿＿＿か。A: Ii apaato ga ___ ka.

Full sentence: A：いいアパートが見つかりましたか。A: Ii apaato ga mitsukarimashita ka.
EN: A: Did you find a good apartment? (lit. Has a good apartment turned up?)
HI: A: क्या अच्छा फ़्लैट मिल गया?
GU: A: સારો ફ્લેટ મળી ગયો?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見つかりましたmitsukarimashitaturned up (自)मिल गयाમળી ગયો
b見つけましたmitsukemashitafound (他)ढूँढ लियाશોધી લીધો

Why: アパートが + blank: the apartment is the subject, so the intransitive 見つかる — polite past question 見つかりましたか.

1-②

B：ええ、おかげさまで。でも、なかなか＿＿＿ませんでした。B: Ee, okagesama de. Demo, nakanaka ___ masen deshita.

Full sentence: B：ええ、おかげさまで。でも、なかなか見つかりませんでした。B: Ee, okagesama de. Demo, nakanaka mitsukarimasen deshita.
EN: B: Yes, thank you. But it took a long time to find one.
HI: B: हाँ, शुक्रिया। पर मिलने में काफ़ी वक़्त लगा।
GU: B: હા, આભાર. પણ મળવામાં ઘણો સમય લાગ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見つかりmitsukari(wouldn't) turn up (自)नहीं मिल रहा थाમળતો નહોતો
b見つけmitsuke(couldn't) find (他)ढूँढ नहीं पायाશોધી ન શક્યો

Why: なかなか…ませんでした "it just wouldn't (turn up)" describes the situation, not an action — 見つかり + ませんでした. (The ます-stem is what fits before ませんでした.)

1-③

友達がいろいろ探して、＿＿＿くれたんです。Tomodachi ga iroiro sagashite, ___ kureta n desu.

Full sentence: 友達がいろいろ探して、見つけてくれたんです。Tomodachi ga iroiro sagashite, mitsukete kureta n desu.
EN: A friend looked all over and found one for me.
HI: एक दोस्त ने बहुत ढूँढा और मेरे लिए ढूँढ निकाला।
GU: એક મિત્રે ઘણું શોધ્યું અને મારા માટે શોધી આપ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見つけてmitsuketefound (for me) (他)ढूँढ दियाશોધી આપ્યો
b見つかってmitsukatteturned up (自)मिल गयाમળી ગયો

Why: A person (友達が) did the searching, and 〜てくれた is a favour done by someone — transitive 見つけて.

1-④

A：そうですか。＿＿＿て、よかったですね。A: Sou desu ka. ___ te, yokatta desu ne.

Full sentence: A：そうですか。見つかって、よかったですね。A: Sou desu ka. Mitsukatte, yokatta desu ne.
EN: A: I see. I'm glad you found one.
HI: A: अच्छा। मिल गया, यह अच्छा हुआ।
GU: A: એમ? મળી ગયો, એ સારું થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見つかっmitsuka(tte)it turned up (自)मिल गयाમળી ગયો
b見つけmitsuke(te)you found (他)ढूँढ लियाશોધી લીધો

Why: The speaker is glad about the result (an apartment has been found), not about anyone's action — 見つかって、よかった. The て is printed, so the answer is 見つかっ.

2-①（始める・始まる）

A：Bさん、会議が＿＿＿よ。早く行きましょう。A: B-san, kaigi ga ___ yo. Hayaku ikimashou.

Full sentence: A：Bさん、会議が始まりますよ。早く行きましょう。A: B-san, kaigi ga hajimarimasu yo. Hayaku ikimashou.
EN: A: B, the meeting is starting. Let's hurry.
HI: A: B जी, मीटिंग शुरू होने वाली है। जल्दी चलिए।
GU: A: B ભાઈ, મીટિંગ શરૂ થવાની છે. જલદી ચાલો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a始まりますhajimarimasustarts (自)शुरू होगीશરૂ થશે
b始めますhajimemasustart (it) (他)शुरू करेंगेશરૂ કરીશું

Why: 会議が — the meeting is the subject, so 始まる; polite non-past 始まります for an imminent event.

2-②

Full sentence: B：すみませんが、先に始めていただけませんか。すぐ行きますので。B: Sumimasen ga, saki ni hajimete itadakemasen ka. Sugu ikimasu node.
EN: B: Sorry, but could you start without me? I'll be right there.
HI: B: माफ़ कीजिए, क्या आप पहले शुरू कर देंगे? मैं अभी आता हूँ।
GU: B: માફ કરજો, તમે પહેલાં શરૂ કરી દેશો? હું હમણાં આવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: 〜ていただけませんか asks a person to act, so the transitive 始めて (会議を始める).

2-③

A：わかりました。じゃ、先に＿＿＿います。A: Wakarimashita. Ja, saki ni ___ imasu.

Full sentence: A：わかりました。じゃ、先に始めています。A: Wakarimashita. Ja, saki ni hajimete imasu.
EN: A: All right. We'll go ahead and start, then.
HI: A: ठीक है। तो हम पहले शुरू कर देते हैं।
GU: A: સારું. તો અમે પહેલાં શરૂ કરી દઈએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a始めてhajimete(we'll) be starting (他)शुरू कर देंगेશરૂ કરી દઈશું
b始まってhajimatte(it) will have started (自)शुरू हो चुकी होगीશરૂ થઈ ગઈ હશે

Why: A is talking about what we will do (先に始めている "we'll have started") — the speaker acts, so 他動詞 始めて. 始まっています would describe the meeting itself, not A's promise.

3-①（開ける・開く）

A：このびんのふた、どうしても＿＿＿んです。A: Kono bin no futa, doushitemo ___ n desu.

Full sentence: A：このびんのふた、どうしても開かないんです。A: Kono bin no futa, doushitemo akanai n desu.
EN: A: The lid of this jar just won't open.
HI: A: इस बोतल का ढक्कन किसी भी तरह नहीं खुल रहा।
GU: A: આ બરણીનું ઢાંકણું કોઈ રીતે ખૂલતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a開かないakanaiwon't open (自)नहीं खुल रहाખૂલતું નથી
b開けないakenaidon't open (他)नहीं खोलताખોલતો નથી

Why: The lid is the topic and it resists — "it won't open" → 開かない. (開けない would mean "I don't open it", and 開けられない "I can't open it" is a different verb form.)

3-②

HI: B जी, ज़रा खोलकर देखिए।

b開いてaiteopen by itself (自)खुलकरખૂલીને

Why: A request to B (〜てみてください) — B must act on the lid: 開けて.

Full sentence: B：ええ……。はい、開きましたよ。B: Ee…. Hai, akimashita yo.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The column's traps are all "same root, different transitivity". Compare:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
見つかる ↔ 見つけるmitsukaru ↔ mitsukeru turn up ↔ find Nが見つかる / Nを見つける Result vs someone's search. 見つかってよかった vs 見つけてくれた
始まる ↔ 始めるhajimaru ↔ hajimeru begin ↔ start (it) Nが始まる / Nを始める Event vs person in charge. 会議が始まります vs 先に始めています
開く ↔ 開けるaku ↔ akeru open (itself) ↔ open (it) Nが開く / Nを開ける Report the result with 開く. 開かない／開きました vs 開けてみて
入る ↔ 入れるhairu ↔ ireru go in / fit ↔ put in Nが入る / Nを入れる 入らない = won't fit. かばんに入らない vs 入れてあげる
上がる／下がる ↔ 上げる／下げるagaru/sagaru ↔ ageru/sageru rise/fall ↔ raise/lower Nが上がる / Nを上げる Prices and pay change by themselves; governments act. 税が上がる vs 税金を下げてほしい
Trap 1 — favours and requests need a 他動詞: 〜てください, 〜ていただけませんか, 〜てくれる, 〜てあげる, 〜てほしい (aimed at a person) all require someone to act — 始めていただけませんか, 開けてみてください, 見つけてくれた, 入れてあげる, 下げてほしい.
Trap 2 — reporting a result: Japanese usually reports the outcome with a 自動詞 (開きました, 見つかりました) even when a person caused it. Using the 他動詞 (開けました) puts the focus on "I did it".
Trap 3 — the N1 extension (added, not in book): the same choice reappears in 〜てある (他動詞: 窓が開けてある — someone opened it on purpose) vs 〜ている (自動詞: 窓が開いている — it is open). Get the pair right first, then the aspect.
