第4日 — 〜あっての、〜とあって、〜とあれば、〜にあって、〜から…にかけて、〜にわたって、〜に至る、〜に至って、〜に至っては (~atte no, ~to atte, ~to areba, ~ni atte, ~kara ... ni kakete, ~ni watatte, ~ni itaru, ~ni itatte, ~ni itatte wa)

Grammar points covered

Answer key

Notes
A heavy N1 day: seven of the nine items carry the book's N1 tag (あっての, とあって, とあれば, にあって, に至る, に至って, に至っては); only から…にかけて and にわたって are N2-level. Two look-alike families: the ある-based forms (あっての／とあって／とあれば／にあって) and the 至る-based forms (に至る／に至って／に至っては). Most are written/formal. Today has no メモ box.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜あっての

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~atte no
Meaning (EN)"B exists only because of A / no B without A" — the book's gloss is 〜があるからこそ…（〜がある） (precisely because there is ~, there is …)
Meaning (HI)...के होने से ही ... है / ...के बिना ... नहीं
Meaning (GU)...હોવાથી જ ... છે / ...વગર ... નથી
Connection (接続)N₁ + あっての + N₂
Register硬 — speeches, editorials, mottos.
Typical useExpressing that someone/something is indispensable: ファンあってのプロ野球, お客様あっての商売, 健康あっての人生.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN₁ + あっての + N₂お客様あっての商売okyakusama atte no shoubai奥さんあっての成功okusan atte no seikou
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — only nouns.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ファンあってのプロ野球なのだから、観客の楽しめる試合をしなければならない。Fan atte no puro yakyuu na no da kara, kankyaku no tanoshimeru shiai o shinakereba naranai. Professional baseball only exists because of its fans, so teams must play games the spectators can enjoy. पेशेवर बेसबॉल प्रशंसकों की वजह से ही है, इसलिए ऐसे मैच खेलने चाहिए जिनका दर्शक आनंद ले सकें। વ્યાવસાયિક બેઝબૉલ ચાહકોને લીધે જ છે, તેથી પ્રેક્ષકો માણી શકે એવી મેચ રમવી જોઈએ.
デパートはお客様あっての商売、お客様は何より大切です。Depaato wa okyakusama atte no shoubai, okyakusama wa nani yori taisetsu desu. A department store is a business that depends on its customers; customers matter more than anything. डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर ग्राहकों के दम पर चलने वाला व्यापार है, ग्राहक सबसे ज़्यादा अहम हैं। ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર ગ્રાહકોના બળે ચાલતો ધંધો છે, ગ્રાહક સૌથી મહત્ત્વના છે.
彼は奥さんの協力なしには成功しなかった。奥さんあっての成功だ。Kare wa okusan no kyouryoku nashi ni wa seikou shinakatta. Okusan atte no seikou da. He would not have succeeded without his wife's help. His success is owed to her. पत्नी के सहयोग के बिना वह सफल नहीं होता। उसकी सफलता पत्नी की बदौलत है। પત્નીના સહકાર વગર તે સફળ ન થાત. તેની સફળતા પત્નીને આભારી છે.

Read N₁あってのN₂ as "N₂ that is possible only with N₁". Example 3 spells it out: 協力なしには成功しなかった → 奥さんあっての成功. 練習問題 ③ (会社あっての自分) is the "company man" mindset: "I exist only thanks to the company". Only nouns go before it.

【2】 〜とあって

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~to atte
Meaning (EN)"because it is (the special situation) ~" — the book's gloss is （〜という場合／状態）だから
Meaning (HI)चूँकि (ख़ास बात) ... है, इसलिए
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે (ખાસ વાત) ... છે, એટલે
Connection (接続)N + とあって  |  V-plain + とあって
Register硬 — news reports, descriptions of scenes.
Typical useA special circumstance → a natural, observed reaction (売り切れた, 人出だ, 真剣だ, 賑わっている). Not for the speaker's own will.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + とあってコンサートとあってkonsaato to atte
VerbV-plain + とあって出たばかりとあってdeta bakari to atte迫っているとあってsematte iru to atte
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + とあって安いとあってyasui to atte
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj (だ) + とあって無料とあってmuryou to atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
人気歌手のコンサートとあって、チケットは一日で売り切れた。Ninki kashu no konsaato to atte, chiketto wa ichinichi de urikireta. It being a concert by a popular singer, the tickets sold out in a single day. लोकप्रिय गायक का कॉन्सर्ट होने के कारण टिकटें एक ही दिन में बिक गईं। લોકપ્રિય ગાયકનો કૉન્સર્ટ હોવાથી ટિકિટો એક જ દિવસમાં વેચાઈ ગઈ.
ボーナスが出たばかりとあって、繁華街は大変な人出だ。Boonasu ga deta bakari to atte, hankagai wa taihen na hitode da. With bonuses just paid out, the shopping district is packed with people. बोनस अभी-अभी मिला है, इसलिए बाज़ार में भारी भीड़ है। બોનસ હમણાં જ મળ્યું હોવાથી બજારમાં ભારે ભીડ છે.
大切な試験が間近に迫っているとあって、皆真剣そのものだ。Taisetsu na shiken ga majika ni sematte iru to atte, mina shinken sono mono da. With an important exam just around the corner, everyone is deadly serious. अहम परीक्षा बिल्कुल नज़दीक है, इसलिए सब पूरी गंभीरता से लगे हैं। મહત્ત્વની પરીક્ષા એકદમ નજીક હોવાથી બધા પૂરી ગંભીરતાથી લાગ્યા છે.

とあって is a reporter's "since it's (such a special occasion)". The reason must be something out of the ordinary (a star's concert, bonus day, an exam), and the result is what you can see happening — crowds, sell-outs, tension. ④ この店は、ブランド品が格安で買えるとあって、いつも女性客で賑わっている is the model.

【3】 〜とあれば

N1 · 硬 formal
Reading~to areba
Meaning (EN)"if it's for ~ / if it's a case of ~, (I'll do anything)" — the book's gloss is 〜ならば…（どんなことでもする）
Meaning (HI)अगर ...के लिए हो, तो (कुछ भी करूँगा)
Meaning (GU)જો ...માટે હોય, તો (કંઈ પણ કરીશ)
Connection (接続)N + とあれば  |  V-dictionary + ため + とあれば  |  N + のため + とあれば
RegisterFormal-sounding; used in speech too for emphasis.
Typical usePurpose/request → willingness to do anything: 何でもする, どんな〜もする, 〜も辞さない.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + とあれば君の頼みとあればkimi no tanomi to areba
Noun (purpose)N + のため + とあれば子供のためとあればkodomo no tame to areba
Verb (purpose)V-dictionary + ため + とあれば当選するためとあればtousen suru tame to areba
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Rare; 必要とあれば is a set phrase.必要とあればhitsuyou to areba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子供のためとあれば、何でもしてやりたいというのが親の心情だ。Kodomo no tame to areba, nan demo shite yaritai to iu no ga oya no shinjou da. Parents naturally feel that, if it's for their children, they'd do anything. अगर बच्चों के लिए हो, तो कुछ भी कर देना — यही माता-पिता की भावना होती है। જો બાળકો માટે હોય, તો કંઈ પણ કરી છૂટવું — એ જ માતાપિતાની લાગણી હોય છે.
彼は選挙で当選するためとあれば、実行不可能な公約もするだろう。Kare wa senkyo de tousen suru tame to areba, jikkou fukanou na kouyaku mo suru darou. If it gets him elected, he'll probably even make campaign promises he can't keep. चुनाव जीतने के लिए वह ऐसे वादे भी कर देगा जिन्हें पूरा करना असंभव है। ચૂંટણી જીતવા માટે તે એવાં વચનો પણ આપશે જે પૂરાં કરવાં અશક્ય હોય.
君の頼みとあれば、ぼくは何でもする。Kimi no tanomi to areba, boku wa nan demo suru. If it's you asking, I'll do anything. अगर तुम कह रही हो, तो मैं कुछ भी करूँगा। જો તું કહેતી હોય, તો હું કંઈ પણ કરીશ.

The pair to remember: とあって = "because it's ~" (fact → observed result); とあれば = "if it's for ~" (condition → I'll go to any length). The main clause of とあれば is typically extreme: 何でもする, どんな汚い手も使う (練習問題 ①). Often preceded by 〜ため.

【4】 〜にあって

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni atte
Meaning (EN)"in (the situation of) ~ / being in ~" — the book's gloss is 〜状態の時に、〜状態のところにいて (when in the state of ~, being somewhere in the state of ~)
Meaning (HI)...की स्थिति में / ...के बीच रहते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ની સ્થિતિમાં / ...ની વચ્ચે રહીને
Connection (接続)N + にあって（は／も）
Register硬 — essays, speeches, literary style.
Typical useSpecial times/conditions: 情報の洪水の中, 非常時, 逆境, この不況下, 物の豊かな時代.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にあって（は／も）非常時にあってはhijouji ni atte wa逆境にあってもgyakkyou ni atte mo
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — only nouns.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
情報の洪水の中にあって、正しい情報を選び出すのは大変だ。Jouhou no kouzui no naka ni atte, tadashii jouhou o erabidasu no wa taihen da. In the midst of a flood of information, picking out the correct information is hard. सूचनाओं की बाढ़ के बीच सही जानकारी चुनना मुश्किल है। માહિતીના પૂરની વચ્ચે સાચી માહિતી પસંદ કરવી મુશ્કેલ છે.
非常時にあっては、上に立つ者の指導力が問題となる。Hijouji ni atte wa, ue ni tatsu mono no shidouryoku ga mondai to naru. In an emergency, the leadership of those at the top is what counts. आपात स्थिति में ऊपर बैठे लोगों की नेतृत्व-क्षमता की परीक्षा होती है। કટોકટીની સ્થિતિમાં ઉપર બેઠેલા લોકોની નેતૃત્વક્ષમતાની કસોટી થાય છે.
彼女はどんな逆境にあっても、決して希望を失わなかった。Kanojo wa donna gyakkyou ni atte mo, kesshite kibou o ushinawanakatta. No matter what adversity she was in, she never lost hope. चाहे कितनी भी विपरीत परिस्थिति रही हो, उसने कभी उम्मीद नहीं छोड़ी। ગમે તેવી વિપરીત પરિસ્થિતિમાં પણ તેણે ક્યારેય આશા ન છોડી.

Think of にあって as a weighty において for situations rather than places: "being in such a time/condition, (it is hard / X is needed / she still…)". Often the main clause states a difficulty or a contrast (〜にあっても = "even in"). ⑦ この物の豊かな時代にあって、…教えるのは難しい is a perfect fit.

【5】 〜から…にかけて

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kara ... ni kakete
Meaning (EN)"from ~ through to ~" (an approximate span) — the book: ※時間的、空間的な範囲を示す (shows a range in time or space)
Meaning (HI)...से ... तक (के दौरान / के इलाक़े में)
Meaning (GU)...થી ... સુધી (દરમિયાન / વિસ્તારમાં)
Connection (接続)N₁ + から + N₂ + にかけて  |  N₁ から N₂ にかけての + N
RegisterNeutral — weather forecasts, news, everyday speech.
Typical useBody parts (肩から腕), regions (東北から関東), times (7時から8時半, 夏から秋).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (place)N₁ から N₂ にかけて肩から腕にかけてkata kara ude ni kakete
Noun (time) + nounN₁ から N₂ にかけての + N7時から8時半にかけての時間帯shichi-ji kara hachi-ji han ni kakete no jikantai
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — only nouns of time/place.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
昨日、重い荷物を運んだので、肩から腕にかけて痛くてたまらない。Kinou, omoi nimotsu o hakonda node, kata kara ude ni kakete itakute tamaranai. I carried heavy luggage yesterday, so it hurts terribly from my shoulders down through my arms. कल भारी सामान उठाया था, इसलिए कंधे से बाँह तक बहुत दर्द हो रहा है। કાલે ભારે સામાન ઊંચક્યો હતો, એટલે ખભાથી હાથ સુધી ખૂબ દુખે છે.
昨夜、東北地方から関東地方にかけて、大きな地震がありました。Sakuya, Touhoku chihou kara Kantou chihou ni kakete, ookina jishin ga arimashita. Last night there was a big earthquake across the area from Tohoku to Kanto. कल रात तोहोकू से कांतो क्षेत्र तक एक बड़ा भूकंप आया। ગઈ રાતે તોહોકુથી કાંતો વિસ્તાર સુધી મોટો ભૂકંપ આવ્યો.
首都圏の電車は、朝7時から8時半にかけての時間帯が一番込む。Shutoken no densha wa, asa shichi-ji kara hachi-ji han ni kakete no jikantai ga ichiban komu. Trains in the Tokyo area are most crowded in the period from 7 to 8:30 in the morning. टोक्यो महानगर क्षेत्र की ट्रेनें सुबह 7 से 8:30 बजे के बीच सबसे ज़्यादा भरी रहती हैं। ટોક્યો મહાનગર વિસ્તારની ટ્રેનો સવારે 7થી 8:30 વાગ્યા દરમિયાન સૌથી વધુ ભરચક હોય છે.

から…まで marks exact start and end points; から…にかけて is vaguer — "somewhere over the stretch from A to B". That's why it suits weather and pain. Before a noun: 〜から〜にかけての時間帯. It usually appears as a pair with から; にかけて alone (週末にかけて) also exists.

【6】 〜にわたって／〜にわたる

N2-level · slightly formal
Reading~ni watatte / ~ni wataru
Meaning (EN)"over / throughout (the whole of) ~" — the book: ※時間的、空間的範囲の広がりを示す (shows the spread of a time or space range)
Meaning (HI)...तक (लगातार) / पूरे ... में
Meaning (GU)...સુધી (સતત) / આખા ...માં
Connection (接続)N + にわたって／にわたり  |  N + にわたる + N
RegisterSlightly formal — reports, news.
Typical useDurations (10カ月, 20年, 3日間), wide areas (広い範囲, 全国), many fields (多方面).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にわたって／にわたる N10カ月にわたってjukkagetsu ni watatte20年にわたる研究nijuu-nen ni wataru kenkyuu
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — only nouns.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
古墳の発掘調査は、10カ月にわたって行われた。Kofun no hakkutsu chousa wa, jukkagetsu ni watatte okonawareta. The excavation of the ancient tomb was carried out over ten months. प्राचीन टीले की खुदाई का सर्वेक्षण दस महीनों तक चला। પ્રાચીન કબરના ટેકરાનું ખોદકામ દસ મહિના સુધી ચાલ્યું.
昨夜、東北から関東の広い範囲にわたって、大きい地震があった。Sakuya, Touhoku kara Kantou no hiroi han'i ni watatte, ookii jishin ga atta. Last night a big earthquake struck across a wide area from Tohoku to Kanto. कल रात तोहोकू से कांतो तक के विशाल क्षेत्र में बड़ा भूकंप आया। ગઈ રાતે તોહોકુથી કાંતો સુધીના વિશાળ વિસ્તારમાં મોટો ભૂકંપ આવ્યો.
20年にわたる研究がやっと学会で認められた。Nijuu-nen ni wataru kenkyuu ga yatto gakkai de mitomerareta. Research spanning 20 years was finally recognised by the academic society. 20 साल तक चले शोध को आख़िरकार विद्वत् परिषद में मान्यता मिली। 20 વર્ષ સુધી ચાલેલા સંશોધનને આખરે વિદ્વત્ પરિષદમાં માન્યતા મળી.

Compare the two earthquake examples: から…にかけて marks two end points (東北から関東にかけて); にわたって attaches to a single noun expressing the whole extent (広い範囲にわたって, 10カ月にわたって) and stresses how wide/long it is. ⑤ combines both: 九州から近畿地方にかけての広い範囲にわたって.

【7】 〜に至る／〜に至るまで

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni itaru / ~ni itaru made
Meaning (EN)"(leading) to ~ / reaching as far as ~ / even down to ~" — the book's gloss is 〜までとどく、〜まで達する (reach as far as ~)
Meaning (HI)...तक पहुँचने वाला / ...तक (यहाँ तक कि ... भी)
Meaning (GU)...સુધી પહોંચતું / ...સુધી (ત્યાં સુધી કે ... પણ)
Connection (接続)N₁ から N₂ + に至る + N  |  N₁ から N₂ + に至るまで
Register硬 — written description, advertising copy.

Q7 (⑦)

この物の豊かな時代＿＿＿、子供に物を大切にすることを教えるのは難しい。Kono mono no yutaka na jidai ___, kodomo ni mono o taisetsu ni suru koto o oshieru no wa muzukashii.

EN: In this age of material abundance, it is hard to teach children to take care of things.

GU: ભૌતિક સમૃદ્ધિના આ યુગમાં બાળકોને વસ્તુઓની કદર કરતાં શીખવવું મુશ્કેલ છે.

Q8 (⑧)

週刊誌は急に有名になった彼女の私生活から過去の秘密＿＿＿まで書き立てた。Shuukanshi wa kyuu ni yuumei ni natta kanojo no shiseikatsu kara kako no himitsu ___ made kakitateta.

Full sentence: 週刊誌は急に有名になった彼女の私生活から過去の秘密に至るまで書き立てた。Shuukanshi wa kyuu ni yuumei ni natta kanojo no shiseikatsu kara kako no himitsu ni itaru made kakitateta.
EN: The weekly magazines splashed everything about her, newly famous as she was — from her private life right down to the secrets of her past.
HI: साप्ताहिक पत्रिकाओं ने अचानक मशहूर हुई उस महिला की निजी ज़िंदगी से लेकर उसके अतीत के राज़ तक सब कुछ छाप दिया।
GU: સાપ્તાહિક સામયિકોએ અચાનક પ્રસિદ્ધ થયેલી તે સ્ત્રીના અંગત જીવનથી માંડીને તેના ભૂતકાળનાં રહસ્યો સુધી બધું છાપી નાખ્યું.

Why: まで is already printed, and the pattern is AからBに至るまで — "from A all the way to (the surprising) B". にかけて is for time/space ranges and doesn't combine with まで; にわたる needs a following noun; に至って + まで isn't a pattern.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two families of near-identical forms: four built on ある, three built on 至る, plus the two range expressions.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜あっての (N1)~atte no B only exists thanks to A N₁ + あっての + N₂ Links two nouns; A is indispensable. お客様あっての商売.
〜とあって (N1)~to atte because it's (special) N／Plain + とあって Fact-reason → observed result. Crowds, sell-outs, tension.
〜とあれば (N1)~to areba if it's for N／〜ため + とあれば Condition → "I'll do anything". 何でもする, どんな手も使う.
〜にあって (N1)~ni atte being in (a situation) N + にあって（は／も） Formal "in such a time/state". 非常時, 逆境, 〜の時代.
〜から…にかけて~kara ... ni kakete from…through to N₁ から N₂ にかけて Two vague end points. Weather, pain, time bands.
〜にわたって~ni watatte over the whole of N + にわたって／にわたる N One noun = the whole extent. 10カ月, 広い範囲, 20年.
〜に至る（まで） (N1)~ni itaru (made) reaching to / even to A から B に至る N／に至るまで B is the far, surprising end. Routes; "from A even to B".
〜に至って (N1)~ni itatte only when it came to V-dict + に至って（初めて） Extreme event → belated action. + 初めて／やっと.
〜に至っては (N1)~ni itatte wa as for (the worst) N + に至っては Topic marker for the most extreme example. After listing bad cases.
Exam trap 1 — とあって vs とあれば: both look like "with ~", but とあって = because (a fact; result is something observed), とあれば = if (a condition; result is "I'd do anything"). Check the main clause: 賑わっている／売り切れた → とあって; 何でもする／どんな手も使う → とあれば.
Exam trap 2 — に至って vs に至っては: に至って takes a verb and is followed by 初めて／やっと (a late reaction); に至っては takes a noun (person/case) and introduces the worst example in a list. Q2 and Q6 test exactly this pair.
Exam trap 3 — にかけて vs にわたって: にかけて needs から (two end points); にわたって attaches to a single span noun and stresses its size. Before a noun: 〜にかけての N, 〜にわたる N (Q9).
