【1】 （あの時）〜ていたら／〜なかったら…た（実際には〜ではなかった）

N2-level basics · neutral
Reading(ano toki) ~te itara / ~nakattara ... ta (jissai ni wa ~ de wa nakatta)
Meaning (EN)Counterfactual past: "if I had (not) done ~ (back then), … would have happened" — but in reality it didn't (book heading: 実際には〜ではなかった). ※「〜たら、〜ば、〜なら」のどれも使うことができます — たら, ば and なら can all be used.
Meaning (HI)(added, not in book) अगर उस समय ... किया होता / न किया होता, तो ... होता (असल में ऐसा नहीं हुआ)
Meaning (GU)(added, not in book) જો એ વખતે ... કર્યું હોત / ન કર્યું હોત, તો ... થયું હોત (ખરેખર એવું ન થયું)
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) V-ていたら／V-なかったら (also V-ていれば／V-なければ, V-ていたなら) … V-ていた／V-ただろう／V-たのに
Register(added, not in book) Neutral; the main clause often ends in 〜ていた, 〜ただろう(に), 〜たのに (regret).
Typical use(added, not in book) Regret and "what if" about the past: プロポーズしていたら幸せになっていた.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (affirmative)V-て + いたら …たプロポーズしていたらpuropoozu shite itara
Verb (negative)V-なかったら …た言わなかったらiwanakattara
Verb (ば)(added, not in book) V-ていれば …た乗っていればnotte ireba
い／な-adj, Noun(added, not in book) 〜かったら／〜だったら …た元気だったらgenki dattara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの時、思い切って彼女にプロポーズしていたら、今ごろは幸せになっていた。Ano toki, omoikitte kanojo ni puropoozu shite itara, imagoro wa shiawase ni natte ita. If I had plucked up the courage and proposed to her back then, I would be happy by now. अगर उस समय हिम्मत करके मैंने उसे प्रपोज़ किया होता, तो आज मैं ख़ुश होता। જો એ વખતે હિંમત કરીને મેં એને પ્રપોઝ કર્યું હોત, તો આજે હું ખુશ હોત.
現実→（あの時プロポーズしなかったから、今、幸せではない）Genjitsu → (ano toki puropoozu shinakatta kara, ima, shiawase de wa nai) Reality → (Because I didn't propose then, I'm not happy now.) हक़ीक़त → (उस समय प्रपोज़ नहीं किया, इसलिए आज ख़ुश नहीं हूँ।) હકીકત → (એ વખતે પ્રપોઝ ન કર્યું, તેથી આજે ખુશ નથી.)

(added, not in book) The trick is that the past didn't happen that way. Use 〜ていたら for something you did not do, and 〜なかったら for something you did do. The result clause is often 〜ていた or 〜ただろう(に) — and ending in 〜のに／〜だろうに adds regret, as in §2 Q1.

【2】 〜ところだった

N2-level basics · neutral
Reading~tokoro datta
Meaning (EN)"almost ~ / nearly ~ (but didn't)" — something (usually bad) very nearly happened. ※「もう少しで」といっしょに使うことが多い — often used with もう少しで.
Meaning (HI)(added, not in book) ...होते-होते बचा / लगभग ... हो गया था
Meaning (GU)(added, not in book) ...થતાં થતાં બચ્યું / લગભગ ... થઈ ગયું હતું
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) (もう少しで／危うく) + V-dictionary + ところだった
Register(added, not in book) Neutral; conversational.
Typical use(added, not in book) Near-misses: 乗り遅れるところだった, 傘を忘れるところだった.
Word typeFormationExample
Verbもう少しで + V-dictionary + ところだった乗り遅れるところだったnoriokureru tokoro datta
Verb (with 危うく)(added, not in book) 危うく + V-dictionary + ところだった危うく死ぬところだったayauku shinu tokoro datta
Adjectives / Noun(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日は寝坊してしまって、もう少しでいつもの電車に乗り遅れるところだった。Kyou wa nebou shite shimatte, mou sukoshi de itsumo no densha ni noriokureru tokoro datta. I overslept today and very nearly missed my usual train. आज देर तक सोता रहा और अपनी रोज़ की ट्रेन छूटते-छूटते बची। આજે મોડે સુધી સૂતો રહ્યો અને રોજની ટ્રેન છૂટતાં છૂટતાં બચી.

(added, not in book) The verb is in the dictionary form and the whole is past (だった) — it tells a story about a moment that passed safely. Don't confuse it with 〜ところだ "just about to" (present) or 〜たところだ "just did". Negative forms like ×ところではなかった don't express a near-miss.

【3】 〜なければ

N2-level basics · neutral
Reading~nakereba
Meaning (EN)"if not ~ / unless ~" — a necessary condition: without it, the result is impossible.
Meaning (HI)(added, not in book) अगर ... न हो तो / जब तक ... न हो
Meaning (GU)(added, not in book) જો ... ન હોય તો / જ્યાં સુધી ... ન હોય
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) N が + なければ  |  い-adj-く + なければ  |  な-adj／N + で + なければ  |  V-なければ (+ negative result)
Register(added, not in book) Neutral.
Typical use(added, not in book) Requirements: 経験がなければ, 安くなければ, 好きでなければ.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + がN が + なければ経験がなければkeiken ga nakereba
い-adjectiveい-adj-く + なければ安くなければyasuku nakereba
な-adjectiveな-adj + で + なければ好きでなければsuki de nakereba
VerbV-ない → V-なければできなければdekinakereba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この仕事は、10年以上の経験がなければプロになることはできません。Kono shigoto wa, juunen ijou no keiken ga nakereba puro ni naru koto wa dekimasen. In this job, you can't become a professional unless you have more than ten years' experience. इस काम में दस साल से ज़्यादा का अनुभव न हो, तो पेशेवर नहीं बन सकते। આ કામમાં દસ વર્ષથી વધુનો અનુભવ ન હોય, તો વ્યાવસાયિક બની શકાતું નથી.
駅から近い部屋でも、安くなければ借りません。Eki kara chikai heya demo, yasuku nakereba karimasen. Even if a room is near the station, I won't rent it unless it's cheap. कमरा स्टेशन के पास हो तब भी, सस्ता न हो तो किराए पर नहीं लूँगा। રૂમ સ્ટેશનની નજીક હોય તો પણ, સસ્તો ન હોય તો ભાડે નહીં લઉં.

(added, not in book) 〜なければ is followed by a negative result (できません, 借りません, 続かない). Watch the な-adjective form: 好きでなければ, 上手でなければ — that's what §2 Q3 and the fill-ins test. Compare 好きでなくても ("even if you don't like it"), which would need a positive result.

1.

あの日はたまたま一台早い電車に乗った。いつもの電車に乗って＿＿＿、こんな事故には遭わなかっただろうに。Ano hi wa tamatama ichidai hayai densha ni notta. Itsumo no densha ni notte ___, konna jiko ni wa awanakatta darou ni.

Full sentence: あの日はたまたま一台早い電車に乗った。いつもの電車に乗っていたら、こんな事故には遭わなかっただろうに。Ano hi wa tamatama ichidai hayai densha ni notta. Itsumo no densha ni notte itara, konna jiko ni wa awanakatta darou ni.
EN: That day I happened to catch a train one earlier than usual. If I had been on my usual train, I wouldn't have been in an accident like this.
HI: उस दिन मैं संयोग से एक ट्रेन पहले वाली में चढ़ गया। अगर मैं अपनी रोज़ की ट्रेन में होता, तो ऐसी दुर्घटना में न फँसता।
GU: એ દિવસે હું સંજોગવશાત્ એક ટ્રેન વહેલી પકડી બેઠો. જો રોજની ટ્રેનમાં હોત, તો આવા અકસ્માતમાં ન ફસાયો હોત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いないとinai toif not (general)न हो तो (सामान्य)ન હોય તો (સામાન્ય)
2いなくてはinakute waunless (must)न हो तो (ज़रूरी)ન હોય તો (જરૂરી)

4いるとiru towheneverजब भीજ્યારે પણ

Why: Counterfactual past (review point 1): he did not take the usual train, so "if I had taken it" → 乗っていたら + 遭わなかっただろうに. 〜と is for general or habitual results, not a past what-if; いなくては means "must".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The column's traps are all about matching the condition form to the real situation and to the result clause:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ていたら…た~te itara ... ta if I had done (but didn't) V-ていたら + 〜ていた／ただろう(に) Counterfactual past; たら／ば／なら all OK. Regret about something not done.
〜なかったら…た~nakattara ... ta if I hadn't done (but did) V-なかったら + 〜ていた／ただろう Counterfactual past, negative. Regret about something done.
〜と~to whenever / if (general) V-dict + と Natural consequence, not a past what-if. Rules, habits.
〜ところだった~tokoro datta nearly happened もう少しで + V-dict + ところだった Near-miss in the past. With もう少しで／危うく.
〜ところではない~tokoro de wa nai no situation for V-dict + ところではない Not a near-miss. "Busy, no time for ~".
〜でなければ~de nakereba unless な-adj／N + でなければ + negative Necessary condition. Requirements.
〜でなくても~de nakute mo even if not な-adj／N + でなくても + positive Concession, not a condition. "It's fine even without ~".
Exam trap 1 — reverse the reality: for a counterfactual, flip what actually happened. Took an earlier train → いつもの電車に乗っていたら; he said something hurtful → 言わなかったら (Q1, 1-②, 1-③).
Exam trap 2 — dictionary form before ところだった: 忘れる／食べる／声をかける ところだった = nearly. A た-form (食べたところ) means "had just done" — the opposite of a near-miss (Q2, 2-②).
Exam trap 3 — なければ needs a negative result: 続かない, できない, 難しい. If the result is positive, look at 〜たら／〜ても instead; and remember な-adjectives take で (好きでなければ) (Q3, 3-③, 3-④).
