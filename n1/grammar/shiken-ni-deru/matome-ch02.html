Notes
A mixed review of 第7日–第11日. Every item gives four printed options (1–4), JLPT 問題1 style. Many distractors are real forms from the same chapter in the wrong slot, so the deciding clue is usually the connection (what form comes before the blank) or one word later in the sentence (ない, まで, さえすれば, だ). Levels: the forms tested mix the chapter's N1-tagged items (すら, ばこそ, のみならず, までだ, までもない, ないまでも, ばそれまでだ, んばかり, とは, ならでは) with N2-level ones.

まとめの問題 — choose the best option (1・2・3・4)

(1)

彼＿＿＿正直な男はいない。自分に不利なことでも決して隠さない。Kare ___ shoujiki na otoko wa inai. Jibun ni furi na koto demo kesshite kakusanai.

Full sentence: 彼くらい正直な男はいない。自分に不利なことでも決して隠さない。Kare kurai shoujiki na otoko wa inai. Jibun ni furi na koto demo kesshite kakusanai.
EN: There's no man as honest as him. He never hides anything, even if it's to his disadvantage.
HI: उसके जितना ईमानदार आदमी कोई नहीं। अपने नुक़सान की बात भी वह कभी नहीं छिपाता।
GU: તેના જેટલો પ્રામાણિક માણસ કોઈ નથી. પોતાને નુકસાન થાય એવી વાત પણ તે ક્યારેય છુપાવતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1こそkosopreciselyहीજ
2くらいkuraias … as (nobody)जितनाજેટલો
3ばかりbakarionlyसिर्फ़ફક્ત
4までmadeeven / untilतकસુધી

Why: N + ＿ + adj + 男はいない is the superlative frame 〜くらい…はいない (第8日). こそ would need an affirmative "he is the honest one"; ばかり／まで don't form a comparison.

(2)

仕事より家庭の方が大切だ。家族を犠牲にして＿＿＿働くつもりはない。Shigoto yori katei no hou ga taisetsu da. Kazoku o gisei ni shite ___ hataraku tsumori wa nai.

Full sentence: 仕事より家庭の方が大切だ。家族を犠牲にしてまで働くつもりはない。Shigoto yori katei no hou ga taisetsu da. Kazoku o gisei ni shite made hataraku tsumori wa nai.
EN: Family matters more than work. I have no intention of working to the point of sacrificing my family.
HI: काम से ज़्यादा परिवार ज़रूरी है। परिवार की बलि देकर तक काम करने का मेरा कोई इरादा नहीं।
GU: કામ કરતાં પરિવાર વધુ મહત્ત્વનો છે. પરિવારનો ભોગ આપીને કામ કરવાનો મારો કોઈ ઇરાદો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1こそkosoonly by (てこそ)...करके ही...કરીને જ
2さえsaeevenतकપણ
3までmade(てまで) going as far as...तक करके...સુધ્ધાં કરીને
4ばかりbakarionlyसिर्फ़ફક્ત

Why: V-て + まで + negative intention (〜つもりはない) — the てまで "I wouldn't go that far" use (第10日). てこそ would mean "only by sacrificing the family can I work", the opposite of the speaker's view.

(3)

恋なんて、もうしたくない。傷つく＿＿＿だから。Koi nante, mou shitaku nai. Kizutsuku ___ da kara.

Full sentence: 恋なんて、もうしたくない。傷つくだけだから。Koi nante, mou shitaku nai. Kizutsuku dake da kara.
EN: I never want to fall in love again. All it does is hurt.
HI: प्यार-व्यार अब दोबारा नहीं करना। उससे सिर्फ़ चोट ही मिलती है।
GU: પ્રેમ-બેમ હવે ફરી નથી કરવો. એનાથી ફક્ત ઘા જ મળે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1までmade(までだ) merelyबसબસ
2ほどhododegreeजितनाજેટલું
3きりkirionly / sinceसिर्फ़ (के बाद से)ફક્ત (ત્યાર પછી)
4だけdakeonly, nothing butसिर्फ़ફક્ત

Why: "Love only brings pain" — V-dictionary + だけだ "it only results in ~". 傷つくまでだ would mean "I'll simply get hurt (as a resolve)", which doesn't fit; ほどだ needs a degree; きり is not a chapter-2 form and doesn't fit V-dictionary + だ here.

(4)

5キロ＿＿＿だれでも歩けるよ。なんでもないさ。Go-kiro ___ dare demo arukeru yo. Nan demo nai sa.

Full sentence: 5キロくらいだれでも歩けるよ。なんでもないさ。Go-kiro kurai dare demo arukeru yo. Nan demo nai sa.
EN: Anyone can walk a mere 5 km. It's nothing.
HI: 5 किलोमीटर तो कोई भी चल सकता है। कोई बड़ी बात नहीं।
GU: 5 કિલોમીટર તો કોઈ પણ ચાલી શકે. કંઈ મોટી વાત નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1くらいkuraia trivial amount likeतो (मामूली)તો (મામૂલી)
2ほどhodoaboutलगभगઆશરે
3だけdakeonlyसिर्फ़ફક્ત
4までmadeup toतकસુધી

Why: なんでもないさ shows 5 km is being downplayed — くらい use (b) (〜のようなたいしたことのないもの), where ほど is impossible (第8日). だけ would mean "only 5 km can be walked".

(5)

できることは全部やった。あとは、ただ神に祈る＿＿＿だ。Dekiru koto wa zenbu yatta. Ato wa, tada kami ni inoru ___ da.

Full sentence: できることは全部やった。あとは、ただ神に祈るのみだ。Dekiru koto wa zenbu yatta. Ato wa, tada kami ni inoru nomi da.
EN: I've done everything I can. Now all that's left is to pray to God.
HI: जो कर सकता था, सब कर दिया। अब बस भगवान से प्रार्थना करनी है।
GU: જે થઈ શકે એ બધું કર્યું. હવે બસ ભગવાનને પ્રાર્થના કરવાની છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1などnadothe likes ofवग़ैरहવગેરે
2のみnominothing butबसબસ
3こそkosopreciselyहीજ
4ほどhododegreeजितनाજેટલું

Why: あとは、ただ V + ＿だ = ただ〜のみだ "all that remains is ~" — almost word-for-word the book's 第9日 example. こそだ can't follow a bare dictionary verb; などだ／ほどだ give the wrong meaning.

(6)

子供ほど大切なものはない。今がんばれるのも、子供がいる＿＿＿だ。Kodomo hodo taisetsu na mono wa nai. Ima ganbareru no mo, kodomo ga iru ___ da.

Full sentence: 子供ほど大切なものはない。今がんばれるのも、子供がいるからこそだ。Kodomo hodo taisetsu na mono wa nai. Ima ganbareru no mo, kodomo ga iru kara koso da.
EN: Nothing is as precious as my children. The reason I can keep going now is precisely because I have them.
HI: बच्चों से ज़्यादा क़ीमती कुछ नहीं। आज मैं मेहनत कर पा रहा हूँ, तो वह भी बच्चों की वजह से ही।
GU: બાળકો કરતાં વધુ કીમતી કંઈ નથી. આજે હું મહેનત કરી શકું છું, એ પણ બાળકો છે એટલે જ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1のみならずnomi narazunot onlyन सिर्फ़ફક્ત ... નહીં
2とばかりにto bakari nias if to sayमानो कह रहा होજાણે કહેતો હોય
3どころかdokoro kafar fromतो दूरતો દૂર
4からこそkara kosoprecisely becauseइसीलिए तोએટલે જ

Why: 〜のも、…からこそだ: "the reason X is precisely Y" — からこそ can close a sentence with だ (第7日). のみならず／どころか／とばかりに all need a following clause.

(7)

初めてのスピーチ大会なので心配したが、思った＿＿＿緊張しなかった。Hajimete no supiichi taikai na node shinpai shita ga, omotta ___ kinchou shinakatta.

Full sentence: 初めてのスピーチ大会なので心配したが、思ったほど緊張しなかった。Hajimete no supiichi taikai na node shinpai shita ga, omotta hodo kinchou shinakatta.

HI: पहली भाषण प्रतियोगिता थी, इसलिए फ़िक्र थी, पर जितना सोचा था उतनी घबराहट नहीं हुई।
GU: પહેલી વક્તૃત્વ સ્પર્ધા હતી એટલે ચિંતા હતી, પણ ધાર્યું હતું એટલી ગભરામણ ન થઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ほどhodonot as … asजितना … नहींજેટલું … નહીં
2くらいkurai(not possible here)(यहाँ नहीं)(અહીં નહીં)
3だけdakeonlyसिर्फ़ફક્ત
4までmadeuntilतकસુધી

Why: 思った + ＿ + negative = 〜ほど…ない "not as much as I thought" (第8日: 思ったほど高くない). In this negative comparison くらい is ruled out.

(8)

どんなに努力しても、途中で＿＿＿それまでなんだから、最後までがんばろう。Donna ni doryoku shite mo, tochuu de ___ sore made na n da kara, saigo made ganbarou.

Full sentence: どんなに努力しても、途中でやめればそれまでなんだから、最後までがんばろう。Donna ni doryoku shite mo, tochuu de yamereba sore made na n da kara, saigo made ganbarou.
EN: However hard you work, if you quit halfway it's all over — so let's keep going to the end.
HI: कितनी भी मेहनत करो, बीच में छोड़ दिया तो सब बेकार, इसलिए आख़िर तक डटे रहें।
GU: ગમે તેટલી મહેનત કરો, વચ્ચે છોડી દીધું તો બધું નકામું, એટલે છેલ્લે સુધી ટકી રહીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1やめてもyamete moeven if you quitछोड़ दो तब भीછોડી દો તો પણ
2やめずにyamezu niwithout quittingछोड़े बिनाછોડ્યા વગર

(9)

ここは、バイクぐらいなら置けても、自動車を置く＿＿＿スペースはなさそうだ。Koko wa, baiku gurai nara okete mo, jidousha o oku ___ supeesu wa nasasou da.

Full sentence: ここは、バイクぐらいなら置けても、自動車を置くだけのスペースはなさそうだ。Koko wa, baiku gurai nara okete mo, jidousha o oku dake no supeesu wa nasasou da.

GU: અહીં મોટરસાઇકલ તો મૂકી શકાય, પણ કાર મૂકી શકાય એટલી જગ્યા લાગતી નથી.

(10)

(12)

(13)

宝くじを買った。1億円とは言わない＿＿＿、1000万円くらい当たらないかなあ。Takarakuji o katta. Ichioku-en to wa iwanai ___, senman-en kurai ataranai ka naa.

4従えばこそだshitagaeba koso daprecisely because I obeyमानने की वजह से हीમાનવાને કારણે જ

Why: An excuse that minimises one's own action: V-た + までだ "I merely did ~" (第10日: 上司に強制されてやったまでだ). までもない needs the dictionary form and means "no need"; とは would be surprise at himself.

(26)

話し合いでは自分の意見を何も言わないでいて、後で文句を＿＿＿あきれた。Hanashiai de wa jibun no iken o nani mo iwanaide ite, ato de monku o ___ akireta.

Full sentence: 話し合いでは自分の意見を何も言わないでいて、後で文句を言うとはあきれた。Hanashiai de wa jibun no iken o nani mo iwanaide ite, ato de monku o iu to wa akireta.
EN: He said nothing at all during the discussion and then complained afterwards — I was appalled.
HI: चर्चा में अपनी कोई राय नहीं दी और बाद में शिकायत करने लगा — मैं तो दंग रह गया।

Why: Clause + ＿ + あきれた (I was appalled): an emotional reaction to an unexpected fact → とは (第11日). どころか needs a further clause; ばかりに would make complaining the cause of the speaker's shock in a regret sense, which is not the book's use; ないまでも makes no sense.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The option sets in this review keep pairing the same look-alikes. These are the ones to have cold:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜までもない vs 〜ないまでもmade mo nai / nai made mo no need to / even if not, at least V-dict + までもない / V-ない + までも Polarity of the verb decides. (11) (24) vs (13)
〜までだ vs 〜ばそれまでだmade da / ba sore made da merely did / if ~, it's over V-た + までだ / V-ば + それまでだ Excuse vs dead end. (25) vs (8)
〜どころか vs 〜どころではないdokoro ka / dokoro de wa nai far from (more/opposite) / no time for + clause / sentence-final どころか needs a following clause. (14) (20) vs (23)
〜だけのことはある vs 〜からこそだdake no koto wa aru / kara koso da lives up to / precisely because さすが… + だけのことはある / 〜のは…からこそだ Praise vs emphasised reason. (10) (22) vs (6)
〜くらい vs 〜ほどkurai / hodo trivial / not as … as くらい: downplay, superlative / ほど: negative comparison くらい can't do 〜ほど…ない. (1) (4) vs (7)
〜なんて vs 〜なんかnante / nanka such … as (+N) / the likes of (+particle) なんて + noun / なんか + particle Book's メモ (第11日). (17) (19)
〜でさえ vs 〜こそde sae / koso even (extreme) / precisely (spotlight) N + でさえ / N + こそ Implication of "others too" vs exclusion. (15) vs (16)
Exam trap 1 — the form before the blank decides: stem → さえすれば (12); て-form → てまで (2) (21); ば-form → それまでだ (8); V-ない → までも (13); V-dict → までもない (11) (24). Check the connection before thinking about meaning.
Exam trap 2 — fixed chunks: 〜のことはある can only be だけのことはある (10); 〜の + N for "unique to" can only be ならではの (18); あとはただ〜＿だ is のみ (5). If the frame is fixed, the meaning check is a formality.
Exam trap 3 — surprise vs other feelings: とは (26) and なんて (17, 19) both carry emotion, but とは is surprise at a fact (often followed by 驚いた／あきれた／すごい), while なんて also belittles a noun (1級なんて無理だ).
