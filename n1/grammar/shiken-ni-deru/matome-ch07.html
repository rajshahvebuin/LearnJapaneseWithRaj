Notes
A mixed review of 第24日–第28日 ("connecting expressions, part 2"). Every item gives four printed options (1–4), JLPT 問題1 style. Items (1)–(23) are blanks inside the sentence; (24)–(27) give the first half and ask you to choose the natural ending — they test what a connector forces on the rest of the sentence (〜ながらも → an unexpected result, 〜たきり → "not since", 〜といえども → "even X can't", 〜と思いきや → the opposite happened). As in the earlier chapter reviews, many distractors are real forms from the same chapter, so the connection before the blank is often the decider.

Pattern of the chapter: these are all connectors that link two clauses, and each one fixes the logical relation between them — contrast (一方, 反面, とはいえ), sequence (上で, 末, あげく), concession (からといって, といっても, ながらも), disregard (を問わず, にもかかわらず). The four "choose the ending" items (24)–(27) test exactly that relation.

(1)

暑い＿＿＿エアコンをつけっぱなしで寝てしまうと、風邪をひきますよ。Atsui ___ eakon o tsukeppanashi de nete shimau to, kaze o hikimasu yo.

Full sentence: 暑いからといってエアコンをつけっぱなしで寝てしまうと、風邪をひきますよ。Atsui kara to itte eakon o tsukeppanashi de nete shimau to, kaze o hikimasu yo.
EN: Just because it's hot, if you fall asleep with the air conditioner left on, you'll catch a cold.
HI: सिर्फ़ इसलिए कि गर्मी है, अगर AC चलता छोड़कर सो गए तो सर्दी लग जाएगी।
GU: ફક્ત ગરમી છે એટલે જો AC ચાલુ રાખીને સૂઈ જશો, તો શરદી થઈ જશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1といったらto ittarawhen it comes toजब बात ... की होજ્યારે વાત ... ની હોય
2のもかまわずno mo kamawazuwithout caring thatकी परवाह किए बिनाની પરવા કર્યા વગર
3にかかわらずni kakawarazuregardless ofचाहे ... हो या न होભલે ... હોય કે ન હોય
4からといってkara to ittejust becauseसिर्फ़ इसलिए किફક્ત એટલે કે

Why: The speaker warns that a reason (it's hot) doesn't justify the action — からといって (第25日) "just because ~, (you shouldn't)". のもかまわず would be "not caring that it's hot" (the opposite logic); にかかわらず needs a noun of variety (天候にかかわらず); 暑いといったら would be an exclamation "it's so hot!".

(2)

綿密な市場調査をした＿＿＿、どこに支店を出すか決めるつもりだ。Menmitsu na shijou chousa o shita ___, doko ni shiten o dasu ka kimeru tsumori da.

Full sentence: 綿密な市場調査をした上で、どこに支店を出すか決めるつもりだ。Menmitsu na shijou chousa o shita ue de, doko ni shiten o dasu ka kimeru tsumori da.

GU: ઝીણવટભર્યું બજાર-સર્વેક્ષણ કર્યા પછી જ અમે નક્કી કરીશું કે શાખા ક્યાં ખોલવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1最後saigo(たが最後) once ~, it's hopelessएक बार ... तो ख़त्मએક વાર ... તો ખતમ
2上でue deafter doing ~ (and based on it)... करने के बाद (उसके आधार पर)... કર્યા પછી (એના આધારે)
3あげくagekuafter all that (bad result)आख़िरकार (बुरा नतीजा)આખરે (ખરાબ પરિણામ)

Why: V-た上で + a future decision (決めるつもりだ) = "first do ~, then decide on that basis" (第24日). 末／あげく report a past outcome after a long struggle (末に決めた), not a plan; たが最後 needs a hopeless result.

(3)

「今度、大阪支店に転勤になったんです。」「大阪支店に転勤＿＿＿今度は支店長ですか。」"Kondo, Oosaka shiten ni tenkin ni natta n desu." "Oosaka shiten ni tenkin ___ kondo wa shitenchou desu ka."

Full sentence: 「今度、大阪支店に転勤になったんです。」「大阪支店に転勤というと今度は支店長ですか。」"Kondo, Oosaka shiten ni tenkin ni natta n desu." "Oosaka shiten ni tenkin to iu to kondo wa shitenchou desu ka."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1というとto iu tospeaking of / so that meansयानी / ... का मतलबએટલે કે / ... નો અર્થ
2といってもto itte moalthough I say ~कहने को तो ..., परકહેવા માટે તો ..., પણ
3とはいえto wa ieeven soफिर भीછતાં

(4)

Full sentence: 英語は世界の共通語と言われるが、数の上では中国語を母国語とする人の方が多い。Eigo wa sekai no kyoutsuugo to iwareru ga, kazu no ue de wa Chuugokugo o bokokugo to suru hito no hou ga ooi.

(5)

(8)

(10)

リゾートホテル建設計画は、周囲の景観を考慮＿＿＿、推し進められている。Rizooto hoteru kensetsu keikaku wa, shuui no keikan o kouryo ___, oshisusumerarete iru.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The chapter's distractors come in look-alike families. The ones this review actually pits against each other:

〜上で vs 〜末 vs 〜あげくue de / sue / ageku then (planned step) / finally (long process) / after all that (bad end) V-た + 上で／末（に）／あげく; N の 末 上で → future decision; 末 → hard-won (often good) result; あげく → bad or reluctant end. (2) vs (17) vs (6)

Exam trap 1 — "choose the ending" items: for (24)–(27) ask what the connector forces. ながらも → a result against expectation; たきり → "not since"; どんなに〜といえども → "even X cannot"; と思いきや → the expectation was wrong. Eliminate any option that confirms the expectation.
Exam trap 2 — する-nouns before the blank: 考慮＿＿ (10) and 成功＿＿がために (12) need an option that supplies する's form — しつつ, せん(がため). Options without し／せ cannot attach directly to a する-noun.
Exam trap 3 — good or bad ending: 末 can end in success (無罪を勝ち取った, 17); あげく usually ends badly or with a reluctant choice (6). 反面 needs opposite qualities of the same person (15); 一方で handles parallel actions (7).
