Notes
The second and last of the book's two N1 mock tests. Several items test a form's required follow-up: ないでもないが → a condition (Q7), ているうちに → a gradual change (Q8), くらいなら → ほうがましだ (Q12). Q2 and Q15 use extended N1 chunks (とは言わないまでも, てからというもの…一度として…ない). The book's target: 15 / 20 or more. The 問題3 passage (on why time seems to pass faster with age; the book notes it was written with reference to a 2009 日本経済新聞 column) is not reproduced: only the sentence holding each blank is quoted, with our own short summary.

Q1

彼らは周囲の心配を（＿＿＿）、ヨットで世界一周をすると言って出発した。Karera wa shuui no shinpai o (___), yotto de sekai isshuu o suru to itte shuppatsu shita.

Full sentence: 彼らは周囲の心配をよそに、ヨットで世界一周をすると言って出発した。Karera wa shuui no shinpai o yoso ni, yotto de sekai isshuu o suru to itte shuppatsu shita.
EN: Ignoring the worries of those around them, they set off, saying they would sail around the world in a yacht.
HI: आसपास के लोगों की चिंता को अनदेखा करके, वे यह कहकर निकल पड़े कि याट से दुनिया का चक्कर लगाएँगे।
GU: આસપાસના લોકોની ચિંતાને અવગણીને, તેઓ યૉટથી દુનિયાની પ્રદક્ષિણા કરશે એમ કહીને નીકળી પડ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1限らずkagirazu(に限らず) not onlyसिर्फ़ … ही नहींફક્ત … જ નહીં
2問わずtowazu(を問わず) regardless ofकी परवाह किए बिनाને ધ્યાનમાં લીધા વિના
3たびにtabi nievery timeहर बारદર વખતે
4よそにyoso ni(をよそに) paying no heed toको अनदेखा करकेને અવગણીને

Why: Other people's worry (心配) is something they ignored → N + をよそに (第27日), typically with 心配／反対／期待. を問わず (第26日) is for neutral categories (年齢・性別を問わず), not for people's feelings being disregarded. に限らず (第23日) needs に, not を. たびに (第13日) needs の + N or a verb and means "every time".

Q2

この漢字を全部書けるように、とは（＿＿＿）までも、せめて読めるようになってほしい。Kono kanji o zenbu kakeru you ni, to wa (___) made mo, semete yomeru you ni natte hoshii.

Full sentence: この漢字を全部書けるように、とは言わないまでも、せめて読めるようになってほしい。Kono kanji o zenbu kakeru you ni, to wa iwanai made mo, semete yomeru you ni natte hoshii.
EN: I won't go as far as saying "be able to write all these kanji", but I'd like you to at least be able to read them.
HI: मैं यह तो नहीं कहूँगा कि ये सारे कांजी लिख पाओ, पर कम से कम पढ़ ज़रूर पाओ।
GU: હું એમ તો નહીં કહું કે આ બધા કાંજી લખી શકો, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછા વાંચી તો શકો એવું ઇચ્છું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1言ったittasaidकहाકહ્યું
2言ってittesayingकहकरકહીને
3言わないiwanai(I) won't sayनहीं कहूँगाનહીં કહું
4言わざるiwazaru(classical negative + noun)(पुराना निषेध रूप)(જૂનું નકાર રૂપ)

Why: 〜までも + せめて "at least" = V-ない + までも "even if not (as much as) ~, at least…" (第10日). The fixed chunk is 〜とは言わないまでも. The た-form and て-form can't attach to までも in this meaning, and 言わざる is a noun-modifying classical form (言わざるを得ない), not a までも connector.

Q3

環境問題（＿＿＿）人口問題（＿＿＿）、何か自分でテーマを決めて調べてください。Kankyou mondai (___) jinkou mondai (___), nani ka jibun de teema o kimete shirabete kudasai.

Full sentence: 環境問題なり人口問題なり、何か自分でテーマを決めて調べてください。Kankyou mondai nari jinkou mondai nari, nani ka jibun de teema o kimete shirabete kudasai.
EN: Pick a theme yourself — environmental problems, population problems or whatever — and research it.
HI: पर्यावरण की समस्या हो या जनसंख्या की, कोई भी विषय ख़ुद चुनकर उस पर शोध कीजिए।
GU: પર્યાવરણની સમસ્યા હોય કે વસ્તીની, કોઈ પણ વિષય જાતે પસંદ કરીને તેના પર સંશોધન કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1やら・やらyara・yarawhat with … and …कहीं … कहीं …ક્યાંક … ક્યાંક …
2なり・なりnari・narieither … or (choose)चाहे … चाहे …… હોય કે …
3といい・といいto ii・to iiboth … and (evaluation)… भी, … भी (तारीफ़)… પણ, … પણ (મૂલ્યાંકન)
4につけ・につけni tsuke・ni tsukewhether … or (every time)… हो या …, हर बार… હોય કે …, દર વખતે

Why: Examples offered for the listener to choose from, followed by a request (調べてください) → A なり B なり (第28日). やら…やら (第28日) lists a jumble of things that happened, often with a complaint. といい…といい (第28日) evaluates one thing from two angles. につけ…につけ (第28日) pairs opposites (いいにつけ悪いにつけ).

Q4

もっと早く病気に気が（＿＿＿）、こんなに長い間入院しなくてもすんだのに。Motto hayaku byouki ni ki ga (___), konna ni nagai aida nyuuin shinakute mo sunda noni.

Full sentence: もっと早く病気に気がついていれば、こんなに長い間入院しなくてもすんだのに。Motto hayaku byouki ni ki ga tsuite ireba, konna ni nagai aida nyuuin shinakute mo sunda noni.
EN: If only I'd noticed the illness sooner, I wouldn't have had to stay in hospital for so long.
HI: अगर बीमारी का पहले ही पता चल गया होता, तो इतने लंबे समय तक अस्पताल में न रहना पड़ता।
GU: જો બીમારીની વહેલી ખબર પડી ગઈ હોત, તો આટલો લાંબો સમય હૉસ્પિટલમાં ન રહેવું પડત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1つくならtsuku naraif you're going to noticeअगर पता चलना है तोજો ખબર પડવાની હોય તો
2ついていればtsuite irebaif (I) had noticedअगर पता चल गया होताજો ખબર પડી ગઈ હોત
3つくにしてもtsuku ni shite moeven if (I) noticeभले पता चलेભલે ખબર પડે
4ついたとしてもtsuita to shite moeven if (I) had noticedभले पता चल जाताભલે ખબર પડી જાત

Why: A counterfactual regret: "if (in fact I hadn't) ~, then … のに" → 〜ていれば…のに (compare その他の基本文法② 〜ていたら…た, "it didn't actually happen"). としても／にしても (第21日) are concessive "even if", which would say the stay would be long anyway — contradicting しなくてもすんだのに. なら can't express a past unreal condition here.

Q5

「今からやっても間に合わないでしょう。」「いや、（＿＿＿）から、やるだけやってみたらどうですか。」"Ima kara yatte mo maniawanai deshou." "Iya, (___) kara, yaru dake yatte mitara dou desu ka."

Full sentence: 「今からやっても間に合わないでしょう。」「いや、間に合うとも限らないから、やるだけやってみたらどうですか。」"Ima kara yatte mo maniawanai deshou." "Iya, maniau to mo kagiranai kara, yaru dake yatte mitara dou desu ka."
EN: "Even if I start now, I won't make it in time." "No — you might just make it, so why not do what you can and give it a try?"
HI: "अब शुरू भी करूँ तो समय पर नहीं हो पाएगा।" "नहीं, हो सकता है समय पर हो ही जाए, इसलिए जितना हो सके कोशिश तो करके देखो।"
GU: "હવે શરૂ કરું તો પણ સમયસર નહીં થાય." "ના, બની શકે કે સમયસર થઈ પણ જાય, એટલે જેટલું થાય એટલું કરી તો જુઓ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1間に合おうとする限りだmaniaou to suru kagiri da(not a real pattern)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)
2間に合うに限ったことではないmaniau ni kagitta koto de wa naiit's not limited to making itसिर्फ़ … तक सीमित नहींફક્ત … પૂરતું સીમિત નથી
3間に合わないとも限らないmaniawanai to mo kagiranaiyou can't say you won't make it (= you might)यह नहीं कह सकते कि नहीं होगा (= हो सकता है)એમ ન કહી શકાય કે નહીં થાય (= થઈ શકે)
4間に合うとも限らないmaniau to mo kagiranaiit's not certain you'll make itज़रूरी नहीं कि हो जाएજરૂરી નથી કે થઈ જાય

Why: V-ない + とも限らない (第30日) = "you can't say it won't happen" → "it might well happen". So 間に合わないとも限らない = "you can't be sure you won't make it" — exactly the encouraging reply to "I won't make it in time". Careful: the ない belongs to the verb, and the whole phrase is positive in meaning (like 事故が起きないとも限らない "an accident could happen"). Option 4 間に合うとも限らない would mean "you can't be sure you'll make it" — discouraging, and the usual form for that is 〜とは限らない. 限りだ (第30日) expresses the speaker's strong feeling (うれしい限りだ), and 〜に限ったことではない (第30日) means "not limited to ~"; neither fits.

Q6

研究を続けていくためには、いい協力者（＿＿＿）、資金も欠かせない。Kenkyuu o tsuzukete iku tame ni wa, ii kyouryokusha (___), shikin mo kakasenai.

Full sentence: 研究を続けていくためには、いい協力者もさることながら、資金も欠かせない。Kenkyuu o tsuzukete iku tame ni wa, ii kyouryokusha mo saru koto nagara, shikin mo kakasenai.
EN: To keep research going, good collaborators are important of course, but funding is also indispensable.
HI: शोध जारी रखने के लिए अच्छे सहयोगी तो ज़रूरी हैं ही, पैसा भी उतना ही अनिवार्य है।
GU: સંશોધન ચાલુ રાખવા માટે સારા સહયોગીઓ તો જરૂરી છે જ, પણ ભંડોળ પણ એટલું જ અનિવાર્ય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にかかわりなくni kakawari nakuregardless ofकी परवाह किए बिनाની પરવા કર્યા વિના
2もさることながらmo saru koto nagaranot only (of course), but alsoतो है ही, साथ हीતો છે જ, સાથે
3のいかんにかかわらずno ikan ni kakawarazuregardless of how ~ isचाहे … कैसा भी हो… ગમે તેવું હોય
4をものともせずにo mono to mo sezu niin defiance ofकी परवाह न करते हुएની પરવા કર્યા વગર

Why: 資金も signals "A, and B too", with A acknowledged first → N + もさることながら (第22日). にかかわりなく (第26日) and のいかんにかかわらず (第38日) would say good collaborators don't matter, contradicting the sentence. をものともせずに (第17日) is "braving (a difficulty)", but a good collaborator isn't an obstacle.

Q7

どうしてもと言われれば、やらないでもありませんが、（＿＿＿）。Doushite mo to iwarereba, yaranai de mo arimasen ga, (___).

EN: If you absolutely insist, I'm not saying I won't do it — but it will cost you.
HI: अगर आप ज़िद ही करें, तो ऐसा नहीं कि मैं नहीं करूँगा, लेकिन ख़र्च तो आएगा।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1コストはかかりますよkosuto wa kakarimasu yoit will cost you, thoughख़र्च तो आएगाખર્ચ તો થશે
2きっとうまくいくでしょうkitto umaku iku deshouit will surely go wellज़रूर ठीक होगाજરૂર સારું થશે

Q8

この連続ドラマは、最初はつまらないと思ったが、見ているうちに（＿＿＿）。Kono renzoku dorama wa, saisho wa tsumaranai to omotta ga, mite iru uchi ni (___).

Full sentence: この連続ドラマは、最初はつまらないと思ったが、見ているうちにおもしろくなってきた。Kono renzoku dorama wa, saisho wa tsumaranai to omotta ga, mite iru uchi ni omoshiroku natte kita.
EN: I thought this TV series was boring at first, but as I kept watching it became interesting.
HI: पहले मुझे यह धारावाहिक उबाऊ लगा, पर देखते-देखते यह दिलचस्प लगने लगा।
GU: પહેલાં મને આ ધારાવાહિક કંટાળાજનક લાગી, પણ જોતાં જોતાં તે રસપ્રદ લાગવા માંડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おもしろいとは思えなかったomoshiroi to wa omoenakattaI couldn't find it interestingदिलचस्प नहीं लगाરસપ્રદ ન લાગી
2おもしろくなってきたomoshiroku natte kitait became interestingदिलचस्प लगने लगाરસપ્રદ લાગવા માંડી

Why: 〜ているうちに must be followed by a change that happens unnoticed during the action (第13日) — 〜くなってきた. 最初は…が also announces a change. Options 1 and 4 deny any change; option 3 is a plain present opinion with no sense of developing over time.

Q9

Full sentence: だが、年をとるにつれて人は経験や知識を蓄えて環境や生活に慣れてくる。Daga, toshi o toru ni tsurete hito wa keiken ya chishiki o takuwaete kankyou ya seikatsu ni narete kuru.
EN: But as people grow older, they build up experience and knowledge and gradually get used to their surroundings and way of life.
HI: लेकिन उम्र बढ़ने के साथ-साथ इंसान अनुभव और ज्ञान जमा करता है और धीरे-धीरे अपने माहौल और ज़िंदगी का आदी होता जाता है।
GU: પણ ઉંમર વધવાની સાથે માણસ અનુભવ અને જ્ઞાન ભેગું કરે છે અને ધીમે ધીમે પોતાના વાતાવરણ અને જીવનથી ટેવાતો જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1慣れているnarete iruare used toआदी हैंટેવાયેલા છે
2慣れにくいnare nikuihard to get used toआदी होना मुश्किलટેવાવું મુશ્કેલ
3慣れてくるnarete kurugradually get used toआदी होते जाते हैंટેવાતા જાય છે
4慣れようとするnareyou to surutry to get used toआदी होने की कोशिशટેવાવાનો પ્રયત્ન

Why: 〜につれて (第3日) needs a gradual change in the result clause → 慣れてくる "come to be used to". 慣れている is a fixed state, not a change; 慣れにくい says the opposite; 慣れようとする makes it a conscious effort, while the passage describes something that just happens with age.

Q18

通常、記憶は発生後から時間がたつほど薄れるが、条件によっては時間がたった方が記憶が鮮明で［18］現象が起きる。Tsuujou, kioku wa hassei-go kara jikan ga tatsu hodo usureru ga, jouken ni yotte wa jikan ga tatta hou ga kioku ga senmei de [18] genshou ga okiru.

Full sentence: 通常、記憶は発生後から時間がたつほど薄れるが、条件によっては時間がたった方が記憶が鮮明で思い出しやすくなる現象が起きる。Tsuujou, kioku wa hassei-go kara jikan ga tatsu hodo usureru ga, jouken ni yotte wa jikan ga tatta hou ga kioku ga senmei de omoidashi yasuku naru genshou ga okiru.
EN: Normally memories fade the more time passes after an event, but under some conditions a phenomenon occurs in which, after time has passed, the memory is clearer and becomes easier to recall.
HI: आम तौर पर घटना के बाद जितना समय बीतता है, याद उतनी धुँधली होती है, लेकिन कुछ हालात में ऐसा होता है कि समय बीतने पर याद और साफ़ होती है और याद करना आसान हो जाता है।
GU: સામાન્ય રીતે ઘટના પછી જેટલો સમય વીતે તેટલી યાદ ઝાંખી થાય છે, પણ કેટલીક પરિસ્થિતિમાં એવું બને છે કે સમય વીત્યા પછી યાદ વધુ સ્પષ્ટ થાય છે અને યાદ કરવું સહેલું બને છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1思い出せないomoidasenaican't recallयाद नहीं आताયાદ નથી આવતું
2思い出しやすくなるomoidashi yasuku narubecomes easier to recallयाद करना आसान हो जाता हैયાદ કરવું સહેલું બને છે
3思い出しそうもないomoidashi sou mo naiunlikely to recallयाद आने की संभावना नहींયાદ આવે એવું લાગતું નથી
4思い出せなくなるomoidasenaku narubecome unable to recallयाद नहीं कर पातेયાદ ન કરી શકાય

Why: 〜が marks a contrast with "memories normally fade", and 鮮明で ("clear, and…") must continue in the same positive direction → 思い出しやすくなる. Options 1, 3 and 4 all describe fading memories, which is the normal case the sentence is contrasting with.

Q19

ただ、こうした時間が速くなる流れを止める方法も［19］。Tada, koushita jikan ga hayaku naru nagare o tomeru houhou mo [19].

Full sentence: ただ、こうした時間が速くなる流れを止める方法もないわけではない。Tada, koushita jikan ga hayaku naru nagare o tomeru houhou mo nai wake de wa nai.
EN: However, it's not as if there's no way to stop this trend of time speeding up.
HI: हालाँकि, ऐसा भी नहीं कि समय के तेज़ होते जाने के इस सिलसिले को रोकने का कोई तरीक़ा ही न हो।
GU: જોકે, એવું પણ નથી કે સમય ઝડપી બનતો જાય એ પ્રવાહને રોકવાનો કોઈ ઉપાય જ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あるとは限らないaru to wa kagiranaithere isn't necessarilyज़रूरी नहीं कि होજરૂરી નથી કે હોય
2あるわけではないaru wake de wa naiit's not that there isऐसा नहीं कि हैએવું નથી કે છે
3ないに等しいnai ni hitoshiipractically noneन के बराबरન બરાબર
4ないわけではないnai wake de wa naiit's not that there's noneऐसा नहीं कि कोई नहींએવું નથી કે કોઈ નથી

Why: The next sentence gives an example method ("for example, going on an exciting trip"), so a method does exist → double negative 〜ないわけではない "it's not that there's none" (わけではない, 第14日). ただ "however" marks a turn from the bleak explanation to hope. Options 1–3 all say a method probably doesn't exist, contradicting the example that follows.

Q20

マンネリを打破し、いかに［20］生活をおくるか。Manneri o daha shi, ika ni [20] seikatsu o okuru ka.

Full sentence: マンネリを打破し、いかに刺激のある生活をおくるか。Manneri o daha shi, ika ni shigeki no aru seikatsu o okuru ka.
EN: How to break out of routine and lead a life full of stimulation — (that seems to be the key).
HI: ढर्रे को तोड़कर कैसे एक रोमांच भरी ज़िंदगी जी जाए — (यही कुंजी लगती है)।
GU: ઘરેડ તોડીને કેવી રીતે રોમાંચભર્યું જીવન જીવવું — (એ જ ચાવી લાગે છે).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1単調なtanchou namonotonousएकरसએકધારું
2若返ったwakagaettarejuvenatedफिर से जवानફરી યુવાન
3刺激のあるshigeki no arustimulatingरोमांच भरीરોમાંચભર્યું
4知識を蓄えるchishiki o takuwaerustoring knowledgeज्ञान जमा करने वालीજ્ઞાન ભેગું કરતું

Why: The passage said youth is full of stimulation (刺激) and age brings monotony; breaking routine (マンネリを打破し) means bringing stimulation back → 刺激のある生活. 単調な is what we're escaping. 知識を蓄える is what made life monotonous in the first place. 若返った describes the person, not the kind of life, and is the result rather than the method.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The option sets in this test keep pairing the same look-alikes. These are the ones to have cold:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜をよそに vs 〜を問わずo yoso ni / o towazu ignoring (people's feelings) / regardless of (categories) 心配・反対 + をよそに / 年齢・性別 + を問わず Disregarding concern vs not distinguishing types. Q1
〜なり…なり vs 〜やら…やら vs 〜といい…といいnari nari / yara yara / to ii to ii either … or (choose) / what with … / both … and (judge) N, V-dict + なり / N, adj + やら / N + といい Suggestion vs messy list vs evaluation. Q3
〜ないとも限らない vs 〜とは限らないnai to mo kagiranai / to wa kagiranai it might (happen) / not necessarily V-ない + とも限らない / plain + とは限らない Positive possibility vs partial denial. Q5, Q19
〜ないわけではない vs 〜ないに等しいnai wake de wa nai / nai ni hitoshii it's not that there's none / practically none plain negative + わけではない / ない + に等しい Double negative = "some" vs "almost zero". Q19
〜もさることながら vs 〜にかかわりなくmo saru koto nagara / ni kakawari naku A of course, and B too / regardless of N + もさることながら、N も / N + にかかわりなく Acknowledges A vs dismisses A. Q6
〜だけに vs 〜ばかりかdake ni / bakari ka precisely because (all the more) / not only plain + だけに / plain, N + ばかりか Intensified feeling from a cause vs adding a fact. Q10
〜ていれば…のに vs 〜としても-te ireba ... noni / to shite mo if only I had / even if V-ていれば + のに / plain + としても Regret about the unreal past vs concession. Q4
Exam trap 1 — negatives inside fixed chunks: 〜ないとも限らない (Q5) and 〜ないわけではない (Q19) are both positive in meaning ("it could happen", "there is some"). Count the negatives before deciding the direction.
Exam trap 2 — the form demands its partner: くらいなら → ほうがましだ (Q12); 一度として → ない (Q15); ているうちに → a change (Q8); ないでもないが → a condition (Q7); 〜もさることながら → 〜も (Q6). In ★ items, place the partner pair first.
Exam trap 3 — 問題3 pairs and opposites: 逆に (Q16) and ただ (Q19) mark a turn; check whether the blank must be the opposite of what came before (長く ↔ 短く) or a hopeful reversal (ないわけではない).
