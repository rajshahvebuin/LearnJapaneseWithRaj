JLPT N1 · Grammar · 試験に出る文法と表現 · 第6章 — 接続の言葉—その1 (Setsuzoku no kotoba — sono 1 — connecting expressions, part 1)

第22日 — 〜からには、〜以上、〜ともなると、〜にひきかえ、〜もさることながら、なくして (~kara ni wa, ~ijou, ~tomo naru to, ~ni hikikae, ~mo saru koto nagara, nakushite)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.70–71 (PDF pages 70–71)
Grammar points covered
〜からには／〜からは〜以上／〜上は〜ともなると／〜ともなれば〜にひきかえ〜もさることながら〜なくして(は)…ない
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑪ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.4 (PDF page 170), under 第6章 "22 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed: ① b ② なくして ③ もさることながら ④ ともなると ⑤ にひきかえ ⑥ b ⑦ b ⑧ a ⑨ a ⑩ a ⑪ a.
Notes
The book tags 〜ともなると／ともなれば, 〜にひきかえ, 〜もさることながら and 〜なくして(は)…ない as N1; 〜からには／からは and 〜以上／上は are N2-level. The book gives からには and 以上 the same gloss (〜という事情があるのだから…: strong will, claim, judgement, conclusion) — the trap is what may follow: only a speaker's will／duty／judgement, never a neutral result (練習問題 ① ⑥). 〜もさることながら adds a second, more important item of the same kind (⑧). No メモ box on these pages. The 練習問題 mix a／b(／c) choices (① ⑥–⑪) with open blanks (②–⑤).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜からには／〜からは

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kara ni wa / ~kara wa
Meaning (EN)"now that ~ / since ~ (it follows that …)" — "given this situation, …" followed by a strong will, claim, judgement or conclusion (book: 〜という事情があるのだから…（強い意志、主張、判断、断定）)
Meaning (HI)जब ... है ही, तो (ज़रूर) ... / ...किया है तो ... ही
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે ... છે જ, તો (જરૂર) ... / ...કર્યું છે તો ... જ
Connection (接続)V-plain (dictionary／た) + からには・からは  |  N／な-adj + である + からには
RegisterNeutral, slightly firm; からは is a little more old-fashioned／written than からには.
Typical useSecond half: 〜たい, 〜べきだ, 〜つもりだ, 〜なければならない, 〜はずだ／〜だろう (a confident inference).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary)V-dictionary + からには出るからにはderu kara ni wa
Verb (た)V-た + からは引き受けたからはhikiuketa kara wa
い-adjective(added, not in book) (rare) い-adj + からには—
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + である + からには自由であるからにはjiyuu de aru kara ni wa
Noun(added, not in book) N + である + からには学生であるからにはgakusei de aru kara ni wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
オリンピックに出るからには、メダルをとりたい。Orinpikku ni deru kara ni wa, medaru o toritai. Now that I'm going to the Olympics, I want to win a medal. जब ओलंपिक में जा ही रहा हूँ, तो मेडल लेना चाहता हूँ। જ્યારે ઓલિમ્પિકમાં જઈ જ રહ્યો છું, તો મેડલ લેવો છે.
引き受けたからは、最後まで責任をもってするべきだ。Hikiuketa kara wa, saigo made sekinin o motte suru beki da. Once you have taken it on, you should see it through responsibly to the end. जब ज़िम्मेदारी ले ही ली है, तो आख़िर तक ज़िम्मेदारी से करना चाहिए। જ્યારે જવાબદારી લીધી જ છે, તો છેલ્લે સુધી જવાબદારીથી કરવું જોઈએ.
医者があんなに勧めるからには、きっとよく効く薬なんだろう。Isha ga anna ni susumeru kara ni wa, kitto yoku kiku kusuri nan darou. Since the doctor recommends it so strongly, it must be a really effective medicine. जब डॉक्टर इतना ज़ोर देकर सलाह दे रहे हैं, तो ज़रूर यह असरदार दवा होगी। જ્યારે ડૉક્ટર આટલો આગ્રહ કરે છે, તો જરૂર આ અસરકારક દવા હશે.

Think "since X is the case, then of course …". The second half is never a plain event that just happens; it is what the speaker wants, insists on, or concludes (とりたい, するべきだ, 薬なんだろう). That is why ① a (私の貯金もなくなってしまう — a simple consequence) and ⑥ a (私は驚いた — a reaction) are wrong in the 練習問題.

【2】 〜以上／〜上は

N2-level · neutral → 硬 (上は)
Reading~ijou / ~ue wa
Meaning (EN)"since ~ / now that ~" — same meaning as からには (book: 〜という事情があるのだから…（強い意志、主張、判断、断定）)
Meaning (HI)जब ... है / किया है, तो ... (ही)
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે ... છે / કર્યું છે, તો ... (જ)
Connection (接続)V-plain + 以上  |  N／な-adj + である + 以上  |  V-dictionary／た + 上は
Register以上 neutral (common in rules and formal speech); 上は is 硬 and a little dramatic (こうなった上は).
Typical useDuties and resolutions: 〜以上、〜べきだ／〜てもらいたい; 〜上は、〜しかない／〜つもりだ.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + 以上宣言した以上sengen shita ijou
VerbV-dictionary／た + 上は決めた上はkimeta ue wa
NounN + である + 以上学生である以上gakusei de aru ijou
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + である + 以上健康である以上kenkou de aru ijou
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + 以上 (rare)難しい以上muzukashii ijou
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この学校の学生である以上、校則は守るべきだ。Kono gakkou no gakusei de aru ijou, kousoku wa mamoru beki da. As long as you are a student of this school, you should follow the school rules. जब तुम इस स्कूल के छात्र हो, तो स्कूल के नियम मानने चाहिए। જ્યારે તમે આ શાળાના વિદ્યાર્થી છો, તો શાળાના નિયમો પાળવા જોઈએ.
もう遅刻はしないと宣言した以上、実行してもらいたい。Mou chikoku wa shinai to sengen shita ijou, jikkou shite moraitai. Now that you've declared you won't be late any more, I want you to actually do it. जब तुमने एलान कर दिया है कि अब देर नहीं करोगे, तो उस पर अमल भी करो। જ્યારે તમે જાહેર કર્યું છે કે હવે મોડું નહીં કરો, તો તેનો અમલ પણ કરો.
試合に出ると決めた上は、全力を尽くすつもりだ。Shiai ni deru to kimeta ue wa, zenryoku o tsukusu tsumori da. Now that I've decided to compete, I intend to give it everything I've got. जब मैच में उतरने का फ़ैसला कर लिया है, तो पूरी ताक़त लगा दूँगा। જ્યારે મેચમાં ઊતરવાનો નિર્ણય કરી લીધો છે, તો પૂરી તાકાત લગાવી દઈશ.
ガンであと半年しか生きられない、と宣告された。こうなった上は、心を決めて、あと半年を精一杯生きるしかない。Gan de ato hantoshi shika ikirarenai, to senkoku sareta. Kou natta ue wa, kokoro o kimete, ato hantoshi o seiippai ikiru shika nai. I was told I have cancer and only six months to live. Now that it has come to this, all I can do is make up my mind and live those six months to the full. मुझे बताया गया कि कैंसर है और सिर्फ़ छह महीने बचे हैं। अब जब ऐसा हो ही गया है, तो दिल पक्का करके बचे छह महीने भरपूर जीने के सिवा कोई रास्ता नहीं। મને કહેવામાં આવ્યું કે કેન્સર છે અને ફક્ત છ મહિના બાકી છે. હવે જ્યારે આવું થઈ જ ગયું છે, તો મન મક્કમ કરીને બાકીના છ મહિના ભરપૂર જીવવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.

Treat 以上 as a slightly more formal からには. Nouns take である (学生である以上), a favourite of rules and notices. 上は is the stiff, resolute variant, typically こうなった上は "now that things have come to this" + しかない／つもりだ. In ⑩ the promise (約束した) is the fixed situation, so 以上 + わけにはいかない is the natural duty.

【3】 〜ともなると／〜ともなれば

N1 · neutral register
Reading~tomo naru to / ~tomo nareba
Meaning (EN)"when it comes to ~ / once you reach (the level／time of) ~" — when a special, higher-level situation is reached, things are different (book: 〜という特定の状況になると)
Meaning (HI)जब बात ... की हो / ... के स्तर पर पहुँचें, तो (स्वाभाविक रूप से) ...
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે વાત ... ની હોય / ... ના સ્તરે પહોંચીએ, તો (સ્વાભાવિક રીતે) ...
Connection (接続)N + ともなると・ともなれば  |  V-dictionary + ともなると (less common)
RegisterNeutral; ともなれば is a touch more formal.
Typical useLevels, roles, seasons, ages: 上級ともなると, 社長ともなれば, 年末ともなると — followed by a natural, expected change (難しい, 忙しい, いっぱいになる).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ともなると上級ともなるとjoukyuu tomo naru to
Noun (person)N + ともなると歌手ともなるとkashu tomo naru to
Noun (time)N + ともなればシーズンともなればshiizun tomo nareba
Verb(added, not in book) V-dictionary + ともなると結婚するともなるとkekkon suru tomo naru to
い／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
上級ともなると、ずいぶん難しい漢字を習うんですね。Joukyuu tomo naru to, zuibun muzukashii kanji o narau n desu ne. So once you get to the advanced level, you learn some really difficult kanji. एडवांस्ड लेवल पर पहुँचकर तो काफ़ी कठिन कांजी सीखनी पड़ती हैं। એડવાન્સ્ડ લેવલે પહોંચીએ ત્યારે તો ઘણા અઘરા કાન્જી શીખવા પડે છે.
CDを何百万枚も売る歌手ともなると、納める税金も大変な額だ。Shiidii o nanbyakumanmai mo uru kashu tomo naru to, osameru zeikin mo taihen na gaku da. When it comes to a singer who sells millions of CDs, the tax they pay is an enormous amount. लाखों सीडी बेचने वाले गायक की बात हो, तो उनका भरा जाने वाला टैक्स भी भारी-भरकम होता है। લાખો સીડી વેચનાર ગાયકની વાત હોય, તો તેમણે ભરવો પડતો ટેક્સ પણ જબરો હોય છે.
この町はふだんは静かですが、行楽シーズンともなれば観光客でいっぱいになり、とてもにぎやかになります。Kono machi wa fudan wa shizuka desu ga, kouraku shiizun tomo nareba kankoukyaku de ippai ni nari, totemo nigiyaka ni narimasu. This town is usually quiet, but once the holiday season comes, it fills up with tourists and gets very lively. यह शहर आम तौर पर शांत रहता है, लेकिन घूमने-फिरने का मौसम आते ही सैलानियों से भर जाता है और बहुत रौनक हो जाती है। આ શહેર સામાન્ય રીતે શાંત હોય છે, પણ ફરવાની મોસમ આવતાં જ પ્રવાસીઓથી ભરાઈ જાય છે અને ખૂબ ચહલપહલ થઈ જાય છે.

The noun before ともなると names a stage that is a step above the ordinary (上級, million-selling singer, peak season, 50 years of marriage). The second half describes what is naturally true at that stage — an objective description, not the speaker's emotion. That is why ⑦ b (和服姿の女性でいっぱいになる) is right and a (やはり…いいものだ, a personal evaluation) is not.

【4】 〜にひきかえ

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni hikikae
Meaning (EN)"in contrast to ~ / whereas ~" — sets two things side by side as opposites (book: 〜とは対照的に)
Meaning (HI)... के बिल्कुल उलट / ... की तुलना में (इसके विपरीत)
Meaning (GU)... થી સાવ ઊલટું / ... ની સરખામણીમાં (એનાથી વિપરીત)
Connection (接続)N + にひきかえ  |  Plain clause + の + にひきかえ (な-adj + な + の)  |  それにひきかえ
Register硬 — written or formal speech; often carries the speaker's evaluation (one side good, the other bad).
Typical useComparing siblings, places, spouses: 兄にひきかえ弟は…, 南側がにぎやかなのにひきかえ北側は….
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にひきかえ兄にひきかえani ni hikikae
な-adjectiveな-adj + な + の + にひきかえにぎやかなのにひきかえnigiyaka na no ni hikikae
VerbV-plain + の + にひきかえ外出するのにひきかえgaishutsu suru no ni hikikae
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + の + にひきかえ明るいのにひきかえakarui no ni hikikae
Pronounそれ + にひきかえ (sentence-initial)それにひきかえsore ni hikikae
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
勉強家の兄にひきかえ、弟の私は本も読まずに外で遊んでばかりいました。Benkyouka no ani ni hikikae, otouto no watashi wa hon mo yomazu ni soto de asonde bakari imashita. Unlike my studious older brother, I — the younger one — never read books and just played outside all the time. पढ़ाकू बड़े भाई के बिल्कुल उलट, मैं छोटा भाई किताबें पढ़े बिना बस बाहर खेलता ही रहता था। ભણેશરી મોટા ભાઈથી સાવ ઊલટું, હું નાનો ભાઈ ચોપડીઓ વાંચ્યા વગર બસ બહાર રમ્યા જ કરતો.
駅の南側は商店街が続き、にぎやかなのにひきかえ、北側は店が一軒もなくひっそりとしている。Eki no minamigawa wa shoutengai ga tsuzuki, nigiyaka na no ni hikikae, kitagawa wa mise ga ikken mo naku hissori to shite iru. The south side of the station has a long shopping street and is lively, whereas the north side has not a single shop and is completely quiet. स्टेशन की दक्षिण तरफ़ लंबा बाज़ार है और रौनक रहती है, जबकि उत्तर तरफ़ एक भी दुकान नहीं और सन्नाटा रहता है। સ્ટેશનની દક્ષિણ બાજુ લાંબી બજાર છે અને ચહલપહલ રહે છે, જ્યારે ઉત્તર બાજુ એક પણ દુકાન નથી અને સૂનકાર છે.
となりのご主人はどんどん出世する。それにひきかえ、私の夫はいつまでたっても平社員だ。Tonari no goshujin wa dondon shusse suru. Sore ni hikikae, watashi no otto wa itsu made tatte mo hirashain da. The man next door keeps getting promoted. My husband, by contrast, is still an ordinary employee after all these years. पड़ोस वाले साहब तेज़ी से तरक़्क़ी करते जा रहे हैं। इसके उलट मेरे पति इतने साल बाद भी मामूली कर्मचारी ही हैं। પડોશના ભાઈ ઝડપથી બઢતી મેળવતા જાય છે. એનાથી ઊલટું, મારા પતિ આટલાં વર્ષે પણ સામાન્ય કર્મચારી જ છે.

にひきかえ is not a neutral "and": the two sides are opposite, and the sentence usually praises one and criticises the other (studious brother vs lazy me). After a verb or adjective clause you need の (頑張ったのにひきかえ, 外出するのにひきかえ) — exactly the shape of 練習問題 ⑤ and ⑪.

【5】 〜もさることながら

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~mo saru koto nagara
Meaning (EN)"~ is of course (important／true), but … too (even more)" — admits the first item, then adds a second one of the same kind that the speaker stresses more (book: 〜もそうであるが、…（〜も）)
Meaning (HI)... तो है ही, पर ... भी (और भी ज़्यादा)
Meaning (GU)... તો છે જ, પણ ... પણ (એથીય વધુ)
Connection (接続)N + もさることながら
Register硬 — written, speeches, reviews.
Typical useSecond half usually contains も (or a "more important" judgement): デザインもさることながら性能も…, 努力もさることながら…協力によるところが大きい.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + もさることながら映像の美しさもさることながらeizou no utsukushisa mo saru koto nagara
NounN + もさることながら本人の努力もさることながらhonnin no doryoku mo saru koto nagara
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + の + もさることながら勝つのもさることながらkatsu no mo saru koto nagara
い／な-adjective(added, not in book) Nominalise first (美しさ, 便利さ).—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このアニメは、映像の美しさもさることながら、ストーリーもなかなか感動的だ。Kono anime wa, eizou no utsukushisa mo saru koto nagara, sutoorii mo nakanaka kandouteki da. This anime has beautiful visuals, of course, but the story is also really moving. इस एनीमे के दृश्यों की ख़ूबसूरती तो है ही, कहानी भी काफ़ी भावुक कर देने वाली है। આ એનિમેનાં દૃશ્યોની સુંદરતા તો છે જ, વાર્તા પણ ઘણી ભાવુક કરી દે તેવી છે.
彼が記録を達成できたのは、本人の努力もさることながら、周囲の人たちの協力によるところが大きい。Kare ga kiroku o tassei dekita no wa, honnin no doryoku mo saru koto nagara, shuui no hitotachi no kyouryoku ni yoru tokoro ga ookii. His own effort counted, of course, but the record he set owes a great deal to the help of the people around him. उसने जो रिकॉर्ड बनाया, उसमें उसकी अपनी मेहनत तो थी ही, पर आसपास के लोगों के सहयोग का भी बड़ा हाथ है। તેણે જે રેકોર્ડ બનાવ્યો, તેમાં તેની પોતાની મહેનત તો હતી જ, પણ આસપાસના લોકોના સહકારનો પણ મોટો ફાળો છે.

The pattern is "A もさることながら、B も…": A and B are two separate members of the same category (visuals／story, effort／help, environment／ethnic conflict), and B gets the emphasis. It is not "A, and especially part of A" — that is why ⑧ b (そのなかでもゴミ問題, a sub-item of environmental problems) is wrong.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's six forms fall into three groups: "given this, so (will／judgement)" (からには, 以上／上は), "at that special stage" (ともなると), and comparing two things (にひきかえ = opposites, もさることながら = both, the second more), plus the conditional "without" (なくして). The 練習問題 test exactly where they diverge:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜からには／〜からは~kara ni wa now that, since (so …) V-plain + からには Followed by will／duty／confident judgement only. 〜からには、〜たい／べきだ／んだろう (Q6).
〜以上／〜上は~ijou / ~ue wa since, now that V-plain／Nである + 以上; V-た + 上は Same as からには; 上は is stiffer and resolute. Rules, promises: 約束した以上 (Q1, Q10).
〜ともなると／ともなれば~tomo naru to when it comes to (a special stage) N + ともなると Second half = what naturally becomes true, not a personal opinion. 上級, 50年の夫婦, 成人式 (Q4, Q7).
〜にひきかえ~ni hikikae in contrast to N／Clause + の + にひきかえ Two opposite sides; often evaluative. 女子 vs 男子, 祖母 vs 祖父 (Q5, Q11).
〜もさることながら~mo saru koto nagara A of course, but B too N + もさることながら Two members of the same category, both true, B stressed; second half has も. デザイン…性能も (Q3, Q8).
〜なくして(は)…ない~nakushite wa ... nai without ~, not … N + なくして(は) Must end in a negative. 努力, 応援, 練習 (Q2, Q9).
Exam trap 1 — からには／以上 + what? The second half must be the speaker's will, duty, request or confident inference. A neutral consequence (貯金がなくなってしまう) or a past feeling (驚いた) is always the wrong choice (Q1, Q6).
Exam trap 2 — にひきかえ vs もさることながら: both line up two things, but にひきかえ = opposites (good ↔ bad), もさることながら = both true, the second more important. Check whether the second half says the same direction (性能も抜群) or the opposite (さんざんな成績).
Exam trap 3 — なくして needs a negative: 〜なくして(は) + ない／あり得ない／できない／なかった. If the option ends affirmatively, it is wrong (Q9 b).
Exam trap 4 — ともなると is objective: it describes what happens at that stage (いっぱいになる, 難しい漢字を習う), not how the speaker feels about it (Q7 a).
