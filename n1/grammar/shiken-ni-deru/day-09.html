第9日 — だけ、のみ (dake, nomi)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.32–33 (PDF pages 32–33)
Grammar points covered
〜だけ（できるだけ）〜だけあって／〜だけのことはある〜だけの〜だけに〜のみならずただ〜のみ
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑪ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.3 (PDF page 169), under 第2章 "9 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed. (The exercise header on p.33 says 「解答は別冊3ページ」.)
Notes
The book tags 〜のみならず and ただ〜のみ as N1; the four だけ patterns are N2-level. The book's メモ: のみ can always replace だけ but is more formal. The big trap is だけあって vs だけに — both mean "because (as you'd expect)", but だけに can also introduce an unexpected or negative result ("all the more because"). The key accepts both in ⑧.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜だけ（できるだけ）

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~dake (dekiru dake)
Meaning (EN)"as much as ~ / to the limit of ~" (book: 〜の限界まで); very often in the form できるだけ "as … as possible"
Meaning (HI)जितना ... सके, उतना / जितना हो सके
Meaning (GU)જેટલું ... શકાય, એટલું / બને એટલું
Connection (接続)V-potential + だけ + V  |  V-たい + だけ + V  |  できるだけ + adverb／V
RegisterNeutral.
Typical use持てるだけ持つ, 寝られるだけ寝る, 飲みたいだけ飲む, できるだけ早く.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential)V-potential + だけ + same V持てるだけ持つmoteru dake motsu
Verb (〜たい)V-たい + だけ + same V飲みたいだけ飲むnomitai dake nomu
Set phraseできるだけ + adverbできるだけ早くdekiru dake hayaku
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this sense.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this sense.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
荷物がたくさんあるから、みんなで持てるだけ持って行こう。Nimotsu ga takusan aru kara, minna de moteru dake motte ikou. There's a lot of luggage, so let's all carry as much as we can. सामान बहुत है, इसलिए सब मिलकर जितना उठा सकें, उतना ले चलें। સામાન ઘણો છે, એટલે બધા મળીને જેટલો ઊંચકી શકાય એટલો લઈ જઈએ.
毎日睡眠不足だ。休みになったら寝られるだけ寝たいと思う。Mainichi suimin busoku da. Yasumi ni nattara nerareru dake netai to omou. I'm short of sleep every day. When the holidays come I want to sleep as much as I possibly can. रोज़ नींद पूरी नहीं होती। छुट्टी होते ही जितना सो सकूँ, उतना सोना चाहता हूँ। રોજ ઊંઘ પૂરી થતી નથી. રજા પડે ત્યારે જેટલું ઊંઘી શકાય એટલું ઊંઘવું છે.
病気が治ったら、お酒を飲みたいだけ飲もう。Byouki ga naottara, osake o nomitai dake nomou. Once I'm over this illness, I'm going to drink as much as I want. बीमारी ठीक होते ही जितनी चाहूँ उतनी शराब पीऊँगा। બીમારી મટી જાય પછી જેટલો ઇચ્છું એટલો દારૂ પીશ.
会議の準備がありますから、明日、できるだけ早く来てください。Kaigi no junbi ga arimasu kara, ashita, dekiru dake hayaku kite kudasai. We have to prepare for the meeting, so please come as early as possible tomorrow. मीटिंग की तैयारी करनी है, इसलिए कल जितना हो सके जल्दी आइए। મીટિંગની તૈયારી કરવાની છે, એટલે કાલે બને એટલા વહેલા આવજો.

Here だけ doesn't mean "only" — it marks the upper limit: "to the full extent possible". The shape is a sandwich of the same verb: 持てるだけ持つ, 書けるだけ書く (練習問題 ⑤). できるだけ (= なるべく) is the fixed version you already know.

【2】 〜だけあって／〜だけのことはある

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~dake atte / ~dake no koto wa aru
Meaning (EN)"as you'd expect from ~ / no wonder, since ~" — the result matches what the status or effort leads you to expect (book: 〜だから、やはり期待どおりに…)
Meaning (HI)...है, तभी तो / ...से यही उम्मीद थी
Meaning (GU)...છે, એટલે જ તો / ...પાસેથી આ જ અપેક્ષા હતી
Connection (接続)V-plain + だけあって  |  N + だけあって  |  sentence-final: V-plain／N + だけのことはある (often with さすが)
RegisterNeutral; usually praise.
Typical usePositive evaluation: 10年住んでいただけあって英語が上手, さすが元新聞記者だけのことはある.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + だけあって練習しただけあってrenshuu shita dake atte
Verb (final)V-plain + だけのことはある自慢しているだけのことはあるjiman shite iru dake no koto wa aru
NounN + だけあって／だけのことはある新聞記者だけのことはあるshinbun kisha dake no koto wa aru
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + だけあって高いだけあってtakai dake atte
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + だけあって有名なだけあってyuumei na dake atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
10年もアメリカに住んでいただけあって、英語が上手ですね。Juunen mo Amerika ni sunde ita dake atte, eigo ga jouzu desu ne. No wonder your English is good — you lived in America for ten whole years. दस साल अमेरिका में रहे हैं, तभी तो इतनी अच्छी अंग्रेज़ी है। દસ વર્ષ અમેરિકામાં રહ્યા છો, એટલે જ તો આટલું સરસ અંગ્રેજી છે.
何回も練習しただけあって、さすがに上手なスピーチだった。Nankai mo renshuu shita dake atte, sasuga ni jouzu na supiichi datta. As you'd expect after practising so many times, it was a really good speech. इतनी बार अभ्यास किया था, तभी तो भाषण वाक़ई शानदार रहा। આટલી વાર અભ્યાસ કર્યો હતો, એટલે જ તો ભાષણ ખરેખર સરસ રહ્યું.
彼女の料理はとてもおいしかった。いつも自慢しているだけのことはある。Kanojo no ryouri wa totemo oishikatta. Itsumo jiman shite iru dake no koto wa aru. Her cooking was delicious. No wonder she's always boasting about it. उसका खाना बहुत स्वादिष्ट था। हमेशा जो शेख़ी बघारती है, वह बेवजह नहीं। એનું જમવાનું ખૂબ સ્વાદિષ્ટ હતું. હંમેશાં બડાઈ મારે છે એ અમસ્તું નથી.
彼は文章が上手だ。さすが、もと新聞記者だけのことはある。Kare wa bunshou ga jouzu da. Sasuga, moto shinbun kisha dake no koto wa aru. He writes well. That's a former newspaper reporter for you. वह बहुत अच्छा लिखता है। आख़िर पूर्व अख़बार संवाददाता जो ठहरा। તે બહુ સરસ લખે છે. આખરે ભૂતપૂર્વ અખબાર પત્રકાર ખરો ને.

Both forms say "the result lives up to the reason". だけあって sits mid-sentence (reason first, result after); だけのことはある comes at the end, after the result has been stated, and loves さすが. It is almost always used for positive evaluations — for a bad result, use だけに (point 4).

【3】 〜だけの

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~dake no
Meaning (EN)"enough ~ to … / the amount of ~ needed for …" (book: 〜に必要な範囲の)
Meaning (HI)...के लिए ज़रूरी जितना / इतना कि ... कर सके
Meaning (GU)...માટે જરૂરી જેટલું / એટલું કે ... કરી શકે
Connection (接続)V-plain + だけの + N
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useAbility, money or knowledge that is just sufficient: 困らないだけのイタリア語, 通訳をするだけの力.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + だけの + N困らないだけのイタリア語komaranai dake no Itaria-go
Verb (potential)V-potential + だけの + N食べていけるだけの生活費tabete ikeru dake no seikatsuhi
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not usual.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not usual.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
イタリアに出張することになったので、日常生活に困らないだけのイタリア語を身につけていこうと思っている。Itaria ni shucchou suru koto ni natta node, nichijou seikatsu ni komaranai dake no Itaria-go o mi ni tsukete ikou to omotte iru. I've been sent on a business trip to Italy, so I mean to learn enough Italian to get by in daily life. मुझे इटली के दौरे पर जाना है, इसलिए रोज़मर्रा की ज़िंदगी में दिक़्क़त न हो, इतनी इटालियन सीखकर जाने की सोच रहा हूँ। મારે ઇટાલીના પ્રવાસે જવાનું છે, એટલે રોજિંદા જીવનમાં તકલીફ ન પડે એટલી ઇટાલિયન શીખીને જવાનું વિચારું છું.
日本語がだいぶ上手になったが、まだ通訳をするだけの力はない。Nihongo ga daibu jouzu ni natta ga, mada tsuuyaku o suru dake no chikara wa nai. My Japanese has got much better, but I'm not yet good enough to work as an interpreter. मेरी जापानी काफ़ी अच्छी हो गई है, पर अभी दुभाषिया बनने लायक़ क़ाबिलियत नहीं है। મારું જાપાની ઘણું સારું થઈ ગયું છે, પણ હજી દુભાષિયા બનવા જેટલી ક્ષમતા નથી.

だけの + noun measures something by what it allows you to do: "Italian sufficient for daily life". Compare 第8日 ほどの (プロに入れるほどの力) — ほどの stresses a high degree; だけの stresses "just enough / the necessary amount".

【4】 〜だけに

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~dake ni
Meaning (EN)"(all the more) because ~ / precisely because ~, more than usual" (book: 〜という理由があるから、普通以上に…)
Meaning (HI)...है, इसीलिए और भी ज़्यादा ...
Meaning (GU)...છે, એટલે જ વધુ ...
Connection (接続)V-plain + だけに  |  い-adj + だけに  |  な-adj + な + だけに  |  N + だけに (also N + の + 前／後 + だけに)
RegisterNeutral; slightly written.
Typical useA reason that makes the result stronger than usual — positive or negative: 当選確実と言われていただけにショックは大きかった.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + だけに言われていただけにiwarete ita dake ni
い-adjectiveい-adj + だけに近くて安いだけにchikakute yasui dake ni
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + だけに大切なだけにtaisetsu na dake ni
NounN + だけに大切な試験の前だけにtaisetsu na shiken no mae dake ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今回の選挙で当選確実と言われていただけに、落選のショックは大きかった。Konkai no senkyo de tousen kakujitsu to iwarete ita dake ni, rakusen no shokku wa ookikatta. Because he had been called a certain winner in this election, the shock of losing was all the greater. इस चुनाव में उसकी जीत पक्की बताई जा रही थी, इसीलिए हार का झटका और भी बड़ा था। આ ચૂંટણીમાં તેની જીત નિશ્ચિત ગણાતી હતી, એટલે જ હારનો આઘાત વધુ મોટો હતો.
大切な試験の前だけに、健康には十分注意しなければならない。Taisetsu na shiken no mae dake ni, kenkou ni wa juubun chuui shinakereba naranai. Precisely because it's just before an important exam, you have to be extra careful about your health. अहम परीक्षा सिर पर है, इसीलिए सेहत का और भी ज़्यादा ध्यान रखना होगा। મહત્ત્વની પરીક્ષા નજીક છે, એટલે જ તબિયતનું વધુ ધ્યાન રાખવું પડશે.
このアパートは駅から近くて安いだけに、入りたいという人が多い。Kono apaato wa eki kara chikakute yasui dake ni, hairitai to iu hito ga ooi. Since this apartment is close to the station and cheap, lots of people want to move in. यह फ़्लैट स्टेशन के पास और सस्ता है, इसीलिए इसमें रहने के इच्छुक बहुत लोग हैं। આ ફ્લેટ સ્ટેશન પાસે અને સસ્તો છે, એટલે જ તેમાં રહેવા ઇચ્છનારા ઘણા લોકો છે.

Key idea: the reason intensifies the result ("普通以上に"). That result can be good (example 3) or bad (example 1: the expected win made the loss hurt more) — だけあって can only do the good, expected kind. So: disappointment, shock, sadness after a high expectation → だけに (練習問題 ③ ⑨).

【5】 〜のみならず

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~nomi narazu
Meaning (EN)"not only ~ (but also …)" (book: 〜だけでなく、単に〜だけでなく)
Meaning (HI)न सिर्फ़ ... बल्कि ... भी
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ... જ નહીં, ... પણ
Connection (接続)N + のみならず  |  N + として + のみならず  |  V／い-adj-plain + のみならず  |  ひとり + N + のみならず
Register硬 — formal, written. Spoken equivalent: 〜だけでなく.
Typical useSecond half usually has も／までも: 国内のみならず海外でも; ひとりわが国のみならず世界全体.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + のみならず国内のみならずkokunai nomi narazu
Noun + としてN + として + のみならず父としてのみならずchichi to shite nomi narazu
VerbV-plain + のみならず優れているのみならずsugurete iru nomi narazu
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + のみならず安いのみならずyasui nomi narazu
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + である + のみならず便利であるのみならずbenri de aru nomi narazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの事件は、国内のみならず海外でも大いに話題になった。Ano jiken wa, kokunai nomi narazu kaigai demo ooi ni wadai ni natta. That incident became a hot topic not only in Japan but overseas as well. वह घटना न सिर्फ़ देश में बल्कि विदेशों में भी ख़ूब चर्चा में रही। એ ઘટના ફક્ત દેશમાં જ નહીં, વિદેશમાં પણ ખૂબ ચર્ચામાં રહી.
私は自分の父親を、父としてのみならず、一人の人間としても尊敬している。Watashi wa jibun no chichioya o, chichi to shite nomi narazu, hitori no ningen to shite mo sonkei shite iru. I respect my father not only as a father but also as a human being. मैं अपने पिता का सम्मान न सिर्फ़ पिता के रूप में, बल्कि एक इंसान के रूप में भी करता हूँ। હું મારા પિતાનો આદર ફક્ત પિતા તરીકે જ નહીં, એક માણસ તરીકે પણ કરું છું.
学力が優れているのみならずスポーツマンでもある彼は、常にクラスの中心だった。Gakuryoku ga sugurete iru nomi narazu supootsuman de mo aru kare wa, tsune ni kurasu no chuushin datta. Not only academically excellent but also a sportsman, he was always the centre of the class. पढ़ाई में न सिर्फ़ होशियार बल्कि खिलाड़ी भी, वह हमेशा कक्षा का केंद्र रहा। ભણવામાં ફક્ત હોશિયાર જ નહીં, ખેલાડી પણ — તે હંમેશાં વર્ગનું કેન્દ્ર રહ્યો.
環境問題は、ひとりわが国のみならず、世界全体の問題である。Kankyou mondai wa, hitori waga kuni nomi narazu, sekai zentai no mondai de aru. Environmental problems are not just our country's problem but the whole world's. पर्यावरण की समस्या सिर्फ़ हमारे देश की नहीं, पूरी दुनिया की समस्या है। પર્યાવરણની સમસ્યા ફક્ત આપણા દેશની નથી, આખી દુનિયાની સમસ્યા છે.

Literally "not being only ~" (のみ + ならず = classical negative of なる). Treat it as written だけでなく: the second clause adds a wider scope, typically marked with も／でも. ひとり〜のみならず (example 4) is a stiff set phrase meaning "not merely ~ alone" — worth recognising at N1.

【6】 ただ〜のみ

N1 · 硬 formal/written

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

ただ in front strengthens the "only". The pattern 後はただ〜のみだ ("now there is nothing left but to ~") is the classic N1 use — 練習問題 ⑦ has its だけ version: 後はただ結果を待つだけだ／のみだ. のみ is just a formal だけ (see メモ).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

だけ changes meaning completely depending on what follows it — "only", "to the limit", "enough", "as expected", "all the more". Compare:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜だけ（limit）~dake as much as possible V-potential／V-たい + だけ + V Upper limit, not "only". 持てるだけ持つ, できるだけ.
〜だけあって~dake atte as expected because V／N + だけあって Result matches expectation; positive. Praise, often with さすが.
〜だけのことはある~dake no koto wa aru no wonder / lives up to V／N + だけのことはある (final) Same as だけあって, but closes the sentence. After the result: さすが〜だけのことはある.
〜だけに~dake ni all the more because Plain + だけに (な-adj + な) Reason intensifies the result — can be negative or unexpected. Shock, sadness, pressure, extra care.
〜だけの + N~dake no enough ~ to V + だけの + N Sufficient amount; modifies a noun. Ability/money/knowledge for a purpose.
〜のみならず~nomi narazu not only … but also N／plain + のみならず Formal だけでなく; widens the scope. Written: 国内のみならず海外でも.
ただ〜のみtada ~ nomi only, nothing but ただ + N／V + のみだ Formal ただ〜だけ. 後はただ〜のみだ.
Exam trap 1 — だけあって vs だけに: if the result is a disappointment or goes against expectation (落選のショック, なくして悲しい, あきらめていたのに合格), only だけに works. If it is praise that matches the background, both are OK (Q8 accepts both).
Exam trap 2 — のみ cannot always replace だけ: fixed phrases keep だけ — できるだけ, だけあって, だけのことはある, だけに. The book's メモ says のみ = formal だけ, but only in the plain "only" meaning.
Exam trap 3 — だけの vs ほどの: だけの = "enough for (a purpose)" (食べていけるだけの生活費); ほどの (第8日) = "to such a high degree" (プロに入れるほどの力). Before a noun, ask: "just sufficient" or "impressively great"?
