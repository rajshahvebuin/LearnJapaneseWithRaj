第8日 — くらい、ほど、まし (kurai, hodo, mashi)

Source

Answer key

Notes
No item today carries the book's N1 tag — all are N2-level. The whole day is a くらい vs ほど substitution test: the book marks, for every use, whether the other word can replace it (※「ほど」も使える／使えない). Memorise the four places where only one works: 同じぐらい (only くらい), "a trivial thing like ~" (only くらい), 〜ほど…ない and 〜ば…ほど (only ほど). The 練習問題 also include three 「正しい方を選びなさい」 brackets (③ ④ ⑨).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜くらい／〜ぐらい

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kurai / ~gurai
Meaning (EN)Two uses: (a) "about / approximately ~" (book: だいたい〜程度); (b) "at least / something as small as ~" — a trivial, easy thing (book: 〜のようなたいしたことのないもの／ことは)
Meaning (HI)(a) लगभग / करीब   (b) कम से कम ... तो / ...जैसी छोटी-सी चीज़
Meaning (GU)(a) આશરે / લગભગ   (b) ઓછામાં ઓછું ... તો / ...જેવી નાની વાત
Connection (接続)N／quantity + くらい  |  V-plain + くらい  |  同じ + くらい
RegisterNeutral; くらい and ぐらい are both correct (book: ※「くらい」も「ぐらい」も使える).
Typical use(a) prices, times, sizes; ほど is also possible but くらい is more common — except 同じぐらい, where ほど is impossible. (b) 〜ぐらいしてほしい, 〜ぐらいできる — ほど is impossible.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun / quantityN + くらい7万円ぐらいnanaman-en gurai
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + くらい電話するくらいdenwa suru kurai
い-adjectiveい-adj + くらい同じぐらいの大きさonaji gurai no ookisa
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + くらい簡単なくらいkantan na kurai

Use (a) — approximate amount (だいたい〜程度)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
東京のワンルームマンションの家賃は月7万円ぐらいだ。Toukyou no wanruumu manshon no yachin wa tsuki nanaman-en gurai da. Rent for a one-room flat in Tokyo is about 70,000 yen a month. टोक्यो में एक कमरे वाले फ़्लैट का किराया महीने का लगभग 70,000 येन है। ટોક્યોમાં એક રૂમવાળા ફ્લેટનું ભાડું મહિને આશરે 70,000 યેન છે.
東京から大阪まで、新幹線で3時間ぐらいかかる。Toukyou kara Oosaka made, shinkansen de sanjikan gurai kakaru. It takes about three hours from Tokyo to Osaka by shinkansen. टोक्यो से ओसाका तक शिंकानसेन से करीब तीन घंटे लगते हैं। ટોક્યોથી ઓસાકા સુધી શિંકાનસેનમાં આશરે ત્રણ કલાક લાગે છે.
このリンゴと桃は、だいたい同じぐらいの大きさだ。Kono ringo to momo wa, daitai onaji gurai no ookisa da. This apple and this peach are roughly the same size. यह सेब और यह आड़ू लगभग एक ही आकार के हैं। આ સફરજન અને આ પીચ લગભગ સરખા કદનાં છે.

Use (b) — something trivial (〜のようなたいしたことのないもの／ことは; ※「ほど」は使えない)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ひらがなぐらい、すぐ書けるようになりますよ。Hiragana gurai, sugu kakeru you ni narimasu yo. Something like hiragana — you'll be able to write it in no time. हिरागाना जैसी चीज़ तो तुम जल्दी ही लिखना सीख जाओगे। હિરાગાના જેવું તો તું જલદી લખતાં શીખી જઈશ.
待ち合わせに遅れるのなら、電話ぐらいしてほしい。Machiawase ni okureru no nara, denwa gurai shite hoshii. If you're going to be late for our meeting, I'd like you to at least phone. अगर मिलने में देर होने वाली है तो कम से कम एक फ़ोन तो कर देना चाहिए। જો મળવામાં મોડું થવાનું હોય તો ઓછામાં ઓછો એક ફોન તો કરવો જોઈએ.

Use (b) is the one N1 likes: くらい downplays the thing as "no big deal", usually to say "at least do that much" (電話ぐらいしてほしい) or "that much is easy". ほど can never do this, and it also can't follow 同じ. In every other "about" sentence, ほど is possible but slightly more formal.

【2】 〜くらい…だ／〜て…くらいだ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kurai ... da / ~te ... kurai da
Meaning (EN)"so ~ that …" — describes a degree by an extreme result (book: とても…なので〜だ)
Meaning (HI)इतना ... कि ...
Meaning (GU)એટલું ... કે ...
Connection (接続)V-plain + くらい + adj／V  |  adj-くて + V-plain + くらいだ
RegisterNeutral. ※「ほど」も使える.
Typical useVivid exaggeration of pain, tiredness, joy: 泣きたくなるくらい痛かった.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + くらい泣きたくなるくらいnakitaku naru kurai
Verb (negative)V-ない + くらい歩けなくなるぐらいarukenaku naru gurai
い-adjectiveい-adj-くて + … + くらいだ痛くて泣きたくなるくらいだったitakute nakitaku naru kurai datta
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-で + … + くらいだ静かで怖いくらいだshizuka de kowai kurai da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電車の中で足を踏まれて、泣きたくなるくらい痛かった。／痛くて泣きたくなるくらいだった。Densha no naka de ashi o fumarete, nakitaku naru kurai itakatta. / Itakute nakitaku naru kurai datta. Someone stepped on my foot on the train and it hurt so much I wanted to cry. / It hurt so much that I nearly cried. ट्रेन में किसी ने पैर कुचल दिया, इतना दर्द हुआ कि रोने का मन हुआ। ટ્રેનમાં કોઈએ પગ કચડ્યો, એટલું દુખ્યું કે રડવાનું મન થઈ ગયું.
歩き回って、もう一歩も歩けなくなるぐらい疲れた。Arukimawatte, mou ippo mo arukenaku naru gurai tsukareta. I walked around so much I got too tired to take another step. घूम-घूमकर इतना थक गया कि अब एक क़दम भी नहीं चल सकता। ફરી ફરીને એટલો થાકી ગયો કે હવે એક ડગલું પણ ચાલી શકું તેમ નથી.

Two word orders, same meaning (example 1 shows both): "[result] くらい + adjective" or "adjective-て + [result] くらいだ". The result is usually an exaggeration (泣きたくなる, 一歩も歩けない). ほど can replace くらい in every sentence here.

【3】 〜くらい…はない／いない

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kurai ... wa nai / inai
Meaning (EN)"there is nothing / no one more … than ~" = "~ is the most …" (book: 〜以上の…はない／いない、〜はとても…だ)
Meaning (HI)...जितना ... और कुछ / कोई नहीं
Meaning (GU)...જેટલું ... બીજું કંઈ / કોઈ નથી
Connection (接続)N + くらい + adj + N + はない（things）／いない（people, animals） |  〜時くらい…ことはない
RegisterNeutral. ※「ほど」も使える.
Typical useSuperlative judgements, often personal: 地震くらいこわいものはない.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + くらい … はない地震くらいこわいものはないjishin kurai kowai mono wa nai
Noun (time)N-時 + くらい … ことはない合格した時くらいうれしかったことはないgoukaku shita toki kurai ureshikatta koto wa nai
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + こと + くらい … はない旅をすることくらい楽しいことはないtabi o suru koto kurai tanoshii koto wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
地震くらいこわいものはない。Jishin kurai kowai mono wa nai. Nothing is as frightening as an earthquake. भूकंप जितनी डरावनी चीज़ और कोई नहीं। ભૂકંપ જેટલી ડરામણી વસ્તુ બીજી કોઈ નથી.
大学に合格した時くらい、うれしかったことはない。Daigaku ni goukaku shita toki kurai, ureshikatta koto wa nai. I have never been as happy as when I passed the university entrance exam. जब मैं यूनिवर्सिटी की परीक्षा में पास हुआ, उतनी ख़ुशी मुझे कभी नहीं हुई। યુનિવર્સિટીની પરીક્ષામાં પાસ થયો ત્યારે જેટલો આનંદ થયો, એટલો ક્યારેય થયો નથી.
スキーぐらいおもしろいスポーツはないと思う。Sukii gurai omoshiroi supootsu wa nai to omou. I think there's no sport as fun as skiing. मुझे लगता है कि स्कीइंग जितना मज़ेदार कोई खेल नहीं। મને લાગે છે કે સ્કીઇંગ જેટલી મજાની કોઈ રમત નથી.

Read it as a superlative: "nothing beats ~". The choice of はない vs いない follows the noun at the end: もの／こと／スポーツ → ない; 動物／人／鳥 → いない (練習問題 ③ tests exactly this). With 時, the closing noun is こと: 〜時くらい…ことはない.

【4】 〜ほど

N2-level · slightly formal
Reading~hodo
Meaning (EN)"about / approximately ~" (book: だいたい〜程度)
Meaning (HI)लगभग / करीब
Meaning (GU)આશરે / લગભગ
Connection (接続)Quantity + ほど
RegisterSlightly more formal than くらい. ※「くらい」も使える.
Typical useDistances, amounts, remaining time — common in directions and service language (あと10分ほどで).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun / quantityQuantity + ほど400メートルほどyonhyaku meetoru hodo
Verb(added, not in book) Not used in this sense (see point 5).—
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this sense.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used in this sense.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この道を400メートルほど行ったところに銀行がある。Kono michi o yonhyaku meetoru hodo itta tokoro ni ginkou ga aru. There's a bank about 400 metres along this road. इस सड़क पर लगभग 400 मीटर आगे जाने पर एक बैंक है। આ રસ્તે આશરે 400 મીટર આગળ જતાં એક બેંક છે.
あと10分ほどで料理ができあがります。Ato juppun hodo de ryouri ga dekiagarimasu. The food will be ready in about ten more minutes. करीब दस मिनट में खाना तैयार हो जाएगा। આશરે દસ મિનિટમાં જમવાનું તૈયાર થઈ જશે.

For a plain approximate quantity, ほど and くらい are interchangeable; ほど sounds a touch more polite, which is why waiters and staff use it. Remember the exception from point 1: ×同じほど — always 同じくらい.

【5】 〜ほど…だ／〜て…ほどだ／〜ほどの

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~hodo ... da / ~te ... hodo da / ~hodo no
Meaning (EN)"so ~ that …" — degree shown by an extreme result (book: とても…なので〜だ); 〜ほどの + N = "a N so great that …"
Meaning (HI)इतना ... कि ... / ...जितना (+ संज्ञा)
Meaning (GU)એટલું ... કે ... / ...જેટલું (+ નામ)
Connection (接続)V-plain + ほど + adj  |  adj-くて + V-plain + ほどだ  |  V-plain + ほどの + N
RegisterNeutral. ※「くらい」も使える.
Typical useTears of joy, voices too small to hear, ability good enough for the pros.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + ほど涙が出るほどnamida ga deru hodo
Verb + nounV-plain + ほどの + Nプロのチームに入れるほどの力puro no chiimu ni haireru hodo no chikara
い-adjectiveい-adj-くて + … + ほどだうれしくて涙が出るほどだったureshikute namida ga deru hodo datta
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-で + … + ほどだ静かで怖いほどだshizuka de kowai hodo da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
困っていた時に親切にしてもらって、涙が出るほどうれしかった。（うれしくて涙が出るほどだった）Komatte ita toki ni shinsetsu ni shite moratte, namida ga deru hodo ureshikatta. (Ureshikute namida ga deru hodo datta) When I was in trouble someone was kind to me, and I was so happy I could have cried. मुश्किल वक़्त में किसी ने मदद की, तो इतनी ख़ुशी हुई कि आँसू आ गए। મુશ્કેલીના સમયે કોઈએ મદદ કરી, તો એટલો આનંદ થયો કે આંસુ આવી ગયાં.
しかられて言い訳をする子供の声は小さくて、そばにいても聞こえないほどだった。（聞こえないほど小さい声だった）Shikararete iiwake o suru kodomo no koe wa chiisakute, soba ni ite mo kikoenai hodo datta. (Kikoenai hodo chiisai koe datta) The scolded child's voice making excuses was so small that you couldn't hear it even standing right next to him. डाँट खाकर सफ़ाई देते बच्चे की आवाज़ इतनी धीमी थी कि पास खड़े होकर भी सुनाई नहीं देती थी। ઠપકો ખાઈને બહાનાં કાઢતા બાળકનો અવાજ એટલો ધીમો હતો કે પાસે ઊભા હોઈએ તો પણ સંભળાતો નહોતો.
あの高校生は、野球選手としてプロのチームに入れるほどの力を持っている。Ano koukousei wa, yakyuu senshu to shite puro no chiimu ni haireru hodo no chikara o motte iru. That high-school student has enough ability as a baseball player to get onto a professional team. उस हाई-स्कूल छात्र में बेसबॉल खिलाड़ी के रूप में इतनी क़ाबिलियत है कि वह प्रोफ़ेशनल टीम में जा सकता है। એ હાઈસ્કૂલના વિદ્યાર્થીમાં બેઝબોલ ખેલાડી તરીકે એટલી ક્ષમતા છે કે તે પ્રોફેશનલ ટીમમાં જઈ શકે.

This is the ほど twin of point 2 — the book gives both word orders again in brackets. ほどの + noun turns the degree into a description (プロに入れるほどの力). In these "so … that" sentences ほど sounds a little more serious and written than くらい, but both are accepted (練習問題 ⑤ ⑦).

【6】 〜ほど…ない

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~hodo ... nai
Meaning (EN)"not as … as ~ / not to the degree of ~" (book: 〜の程度まではいかない)
Meaning (HI)...जितना ... नहीं
Meaning (GU)...જેટલું ... નથી
Connection (接続)N + ほど + adj-negative  |  V-plain + ほど + adj-negative  |  V-plain + ほどではない／ほどじゃない
RegisterNeutral; ほどじゃない is conversational. ※「くらい」は使えない.
Typical useComparisons that fall short: いつもの夏ほど暑くない, 思ったほど高くない, 休むほどじゃない.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ほど + negativeいつもの夏ほど暑くないitsumo no natsu hodo atsuku nai
VerbV-plain + ほど + negative思ったほど高くないomotta hodo takaku nai
Verb (alone)V-plain + ほどではない／ほどじゃない休むほどじゃなかったyasumu hodo ja nakatta
な-adjective(added, not in book) N + ほど + な-adj + ではない東京ほど便利ではないToukyou hodo benri de wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今年の夏は、いつもの夏ほど暑くない。Kotoshi no natsu wa, itsumo no natsu hodo atsuku nai. This summer isn't as hot as usual. इस साल की गर्मी हमेशा जितनी तेज़ नहीं है। આ વર્ષનો ઉનાળો હંમેશા જેટલો ગરમ નથી.
日本の物価は、交通費や家賃以外は思ったほど高くない。Nihon no bukka wa, koutsuuhi ya yachin igai wa omotta hodo takaku nai. Apart from transport and rent, prices in Japan aren't as high as I'd thought. जापान में आने-जाने के ख़र्च और किराए को छोड़कर बाक़ी चीज़ें उतनी महँगी नहीं जितना मैंने सोचा था। જાપાનમાં વાહનવ્યવહાર અને ભાડા સિવાય બાકીની વસ્તુઓ ધાર્યું હતું એટલી મોંઘી નથી.
かぜをひいたが、学校を休むほどじゃなかった。Kaze o hiita ga, gakkou o yasumu hodo ja nakatta. I caught a cold, but it wasn't bad enough to stay home from school. सर्दी तो लगी, पर इतनी नहीं कि स्कूल से छुट्टी लेनी पड़े। શરદી તો થઈ, પણ એટલી નહીં કે શાળાએથી રજા લેવી પડે.

The rule to remember: in "not as … as" comparisons, only ほど works — ×夏くらい暑くない. The negative can be far from ほど (納豆は…食べられないほどきらいではない, 練習問題 ②), so scan to the end of the sentence for ない.

【7】 〜ほど…はない／いない

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~hodo ... wa nai / inai
Meaning (EN)"there is nothing / no one as … as ~" = "~ is the most …" (book: 〜以上の…はない／いない、〜はとても…だ)
Meaning (HI)...जितना ... और कुछ / कोई नहीं
Meaning (GU)...જેટલું ... બીજું કંઈ / કોઈ નથી
Connection (接続)N + ほど + adj／V + N + はない（things）／いない（people, animals）
RegisterNeutral; slightly more formal than くらい. ※「くらい」も使える.
Typical useSuperlatives: 人間ほど何でも食べる動物はいない.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ほど … はない／いない人間ほど…動物はいないningen hodo … doubutsu wa inai

Full sentence: 私は自分の作った料理はおいしいと思っているが、母の料理ほどおいしくないことは認める。Watashi wa jibun no tsukutta ryouri wa oishii to omotte iru ga, haha no ryouri hodo oishikunai koto wa mitomeru.
EN: I think my own cooking is tasty, but I admit it isn't as tasty as my mother's.
HI: मुझे लगता है मेरा बनाया खाना स्वादिष्ट है, पर मानता हूँ कि माँ के खाने जितना स्वादिष्ट नहीं।
GU: મને લાગે છે કે મારું બનાવેલું જમવાનું સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે, પણ સ્વીકારું છું કે માના જમવાના જેટલું સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી.

aほど … おいしくないhodo … oishikunainot as tasty asजितना स्वादिष्ट नहींજેટલું સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી

Why: が … 認める ("but I admit") signals a concession that her cooking falls short — 〜ほど…ない. The key gives only ほど here, because くらい can't be used in "not as … as". くらいおいしい ("as tasty as mother's") would not be an admission.

Q10 (⑩)

日本語の文法は難しくて、考えれば＿＿＿わからなくなってしまう。Nihongo no bunpou wa muzukashikute, kangaereba ___ wakaranaku natte shimau.

Full sentence: 日本語の文法は難しくて、考えれば考えるほどわからなくなってしまう。Nihongo no bunpou wa muzukashikute, kangaereba kangaeru hodo wakaranaku natte shimau.
EN: Japanese grammar is so hard that the more I think about it, the less I understand.
HI: जापानी व्याकरण इतना कठिन है कि जितना सोचो, उतना ही समझ से बाहर होता जाता है।
GU: જાપાની વ્યાકરણ એટલું અઘરું છે કે જેટલું વિચારીએ, એટલું જ સમજાતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a考えるほどkangaeru hodothe more I thinkजितना सोचोજેટલું વિચારીએ
b考えほどkangae hodo(wrong form)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)

Why: V-ば + V-dictionary + ほど: 考えれば考えるほど. Neither the stem nor the て-form can precede ほど, and くらい is impossible in this proportional pattern.

Q11 (⑪)

自分の部屋＿＿＿自分で片づけなさいと、子供のころ母によく言われた。Jibun no heya ___ jibun de katazukenasai to, kodomo no koro haha ni yoku iwareta.

Full sentence: 自分の部屋くらい自分で片づけなさいと、子供のころ母によく言われた。Jibun no heya kurai jibun de katazukenasai to, kodomo no koro haha ni yoku iwareta.
EN: As a child, my mother often told me, "At least tidy your own room yourself!"
HI: बचपन में माँ अक्सर कहती थीं, "कम से कम अपना कमरा तो ख़ुद साफ़ करो।"

Q12 (⑫)

手術なんか絶対にしたくない。手術をするぐらいなら、死んだ方が＿＿＿だ。Shujutsu nanka zettai ni shitaku nai. Shujutsu o suru gurai nara, shinda hou ga ___ da.

Full sentence: 手術なんか絶対にしたくない。手術をするぐらいなら、死んだ方がましだ。Shujutsu nanka zettai ni shitaku nai. Shujutsu o suru gurai nara, shinda hou ga mashi da.

GU: મારે ઓપરેશન બિલકુલ નથી કરાવવું. ઓપરેશન કરાવવા કરતાં તો મરી જવું સારું.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The core question on every くらい／ほど item: "can the other word replace it?" The book's ※ notes answer it use by use:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜くらい (a)~kurai about (amount) Quantity + くらい ほど also OK (くらい more common) — but ×同じほど. Prices, times, 同じくらい.
〜くらい (b)~kurai at least / a trivial thing like N + くらい ほど impossible. Downplays the thing. 電話ぐらいして, ひらがなぐらい.
〜くらい／ほど…だ~kurai / hodo ... da so … that V-plain + くらい／ほど Both OK. Exaggerated results.
〜くらい／ほど…はない~kurai / hodo ... wa nai nothing is as … as N + くらい／ほど … はない／いない Both OK; ない for things, いない for living beings. Superlatives.
〜ほど…ない~hodo ... nai not as … as N／V + ほど + negative くらい impossible. Falling short of a comparison.
〜ば…ほど~ba ... hodo the more …, the more V-ば + V-dict + ほど くらい impossible. ば may be dropped. Proportional change.
〜ましだ~mashi da less bad A の方が ましだ / 〜ぐらいなら…ましだ Both options are bad — never praise. Lesser evil; strong refusal.
Exam trap 1 — "nai" in two places: 〜ほど…ない (not as … as; only ほど) vs 〜ほど／くらい…はない (nothing is as … as; both). Check what the ない negates: the adjective (夏ほど暑くない) or the existence of another item (地震くらいこわいものはない).
Exam trap 2 — くらい that downplays: 自分の部屋くらい片づけなさい, 電話ぐらいしてほしい, ひらがなぐらいすぐ書ける. When the noun is presented as easy or minimal, only くらい works; ほど sounds like you're measuring an amount.
Exam trap 3 — ぐらいなら vs ば…ほど: both have a conditional shape, but 〜ぐらいなら…ましだ means "rather than do (the hated thing)", while 〜ば…ほど is proportional. A sentence ending in 方がましだ always takes ぐらいなら.
