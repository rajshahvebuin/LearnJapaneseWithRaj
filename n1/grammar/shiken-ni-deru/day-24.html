JLPT N1 · Grammar · 試験に出る文法と表現 · 第7章 — 接続の言葉—その2 (Setsuzoku no kotoba — sono 2 — connecting expressions, part 2)

第24日 — 上、上で、〜た末、あげく、〜というと、〜といえば、〜といったら (ue, ue de, ~ta sue, ageku, ~to iu to, ~to ieba, ~to ittara)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.78–79 (PDF pages 78–79)
Grammar points covered
〜上(に)〜上(で)〜た末／〜の末〜あげく〜というと〜といえば〜といったら…ない／否定的表現
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑩ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.5 (PDF page 171), under 第7章 "24 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed: ① 末 ② といったら ③ 上(に) ④ 上で ⑤ というと ⑥ 上 ⑦ あげく／末(に) ⑧ といえば ⑨ a ⑩ b.
Notes
Only 〜といったら…ない／否定的表現 is tagged N1 in the book; the others are N2-level. Two families: 上 (上に "on top of", 上で "after doing／in the area of") and outcome after a long process (た末 neutral, あげく usually a bad or exhausted end); then three topic-raising forms that look alike — というと (asking back about the other person's word), といえば (jumping to an associated topic) and といったら (the degree is extreme, followed by a negative or critical expression). No メモ box on these pages. The 練習問題 are open blanks (①–⑧) plus two a／b items (⑨ ⑩).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜上(に)

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ue (ni)
Meaning (EN)"on top of ~ / not only ~ but also" — adds a second fact in the same direction (book: 〜に加えて)
Meaning (HI)... के ऊपर से / ... ही नहीं, ... भी
Meaning (GU)... ઉપરાંત / ... તો ખરું, ... પણ
Connection (接続)V-plain + 上(に)  |  い-adj + 上(に)  |  な-adj + な／である + 上(に)  |  N + の／である + 上(に)
RegisterNeutral; slightly written.
Typical useTwo good things (安い上に品質もいい) or two bad things (込んでいた上に止める所もなく); the second clause often has も.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (た)V-た + 上(に)お世話になった上osewa ni natta ue
い-adjectiveい-adj + 上に安い上にyasui ue ni
Verb (ている)V-ていた + 上に込んでいた上にkonde ita ue ni
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + 上に便利な上にbenri na ue ni
Noun(added, not in book) N + の／である + 上に学生である上にgakusei de aru ue ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お世話になった上、駅まで送っていただいて、申し訳ありません。Osewa ni natta ue, eki made okutte itadaite, moushiwake arimasen. You've looked after me so well, and on top of that you've driven me to the station — I'm very grateful (I'm sorry to trouble you). आपने इतनी देखभाल की, ऊपर से स्टेशन तक छोड़ने भी आए — माफ़ कीजिए, बहुत तकलीफ़ दी। તમે આટલી સંભાળ લીધી, ઉપરાંત સ્ટેશન સુધી મૂકવા પણ આવ્યા — માફ કરજો, બહુ તકલીફ આપી.
この製品は、値段が安い上に品質もいいので、消費者に好評だ。Kono seihin wa, nedan ga yasui ue ni hinshitsu mo ii node, shouhisha ni kouhyou da. This product is cheap and on top of that good quality, so it is popular with consumers. यह प्रोडक्ट सस्ता होने के साथ-साथ अच्छी क्वालिटी का भी है, इसलिए ग्राहकों में लोकप्रिय है। આ ઉત્પાદન સસ્તું હોવા ઉપરાંત સારી ગુણવત્તાનું પણ છે, એટલે ગ્રાહકોમાં લોકપ્રિય છે.
今日は車で出かけたんですが、道が込んでいた上に止める所もなくて大変でした。Kyou wa kuruma de dekaketa n desu ga, michi ga konde ita ue ni tomeru tokoro mo nakute taihen deshita. I went out by car today, but the roads were crowded and on top of that there was nowhere to park — it was awful. आज कार से निकला था, पर सड़कों पर भीड़ थी, ऊपर से गाड़ी खड़ी करने की जगह भी नहीं थी — बड़ी मुश्किल हुई। આજે કારમાં નીકળ્યો હતો, પણ રસ્તા પર ભીડ હતી, ઉપરાંત ગાડી ઊભી રાખવાની જગ્યા પણ નહોતી — બહુ મુશ્કેલી પડી.

Think of 上に as "piled on top": the two facts must point the same way (good + good, bad + bad). In 練習問題 ③ (給料が安い上に仕事も大変) both are complaints, and the も in the second half is the tell. For a contrast (cheap but bad) use のに／が, not 上に.

【2】 〜上(で)

N2-level · neutral → formal
Reading~ue (de)
Meaning (EN)(1) "after doing ~ (then …)" — book: 〜してから; (2) "in ~ / for the purpose of ~ / in terms of ~" — book: 〜に関連して. cf. N + 上 (じょう) "from the standpoint of" (法律上)
Meaning (HI)(1) ... करने के बाद ही  |  (2) ... करने में / ... के मामले में
Meaning (GU)(1) ... કર્યા પછી જ  |  (2) ... કરવામાં / ... ની બાબતમાં
Connection (接続)(1) V-た + 上(で)  |  N + の + 上(で) (ご記入の上)  |  (2) V-dictionary + 上で  |  N + の + 上で
Register(1) is formal — instructions, business (ご記入の上、ご返送ください). (2) neutral.
Typical use(1) 相談した上で返事する, 話し合った上の結論; (2) 生活する上で困らない, 開発する上で役立つ, 仕事の上での付き合い.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (た) — "after"V-た + 上で相談した上でsoudan shita ue de
Noun (する-noun) — "after"N + の + 上(で)ご記入の上gokinyuu no ue
Modifying a nounV-た + 上の + N話し合った上の結論hanashiatta ue no ketsuron
Verb (dictionary) — "in ~ing"V-dictionary + 上で生活する上でseikatsu suru ue de
Noun — "in terms of"N + の + 上での + N仕事の上での付き合いshigoto no ue de no tsukiai
cf. Noun + 上 (じょう)N + 上 (jou)法律上houritsujou
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
〔してから〕 手続きの書類は、必要事項をご記入の上、ご返送ください。Tetsuzuki no shorui wa, hitsuyou jikou o gokinyuu no ue, gohensou kudasai. 〔after〕 Please fill in the required items on the application papers and then send them back. 〔के बाद〕 आवेदन के काग़ज़ों में ज़रूरी जानकारी भरने के बाद उन्हें वापस भेजिए। 〔પછી〕 અરજીના કાગળોમાં જરૂરી વિગતો ભર્યા પછી તે પાછા મોકલો.
〔してから〕 その件につきましては、上司と相談した上でお返事いたします。Sono ken ni tsukimashite wa, joushi to soudan shita ue de ohenji itashimasu. 〔after〕 Regarding that matter, I will reply after consulting my superior. 〔के बाद〕 उस मामले में, मैं अपने वरिष्ठ से सलाह करने के बाद जवाब दूँगा। 〔પછી〕 એ બાબતે, હું મારા ઉપરી સાથે સલાહ કર્યા પછી જવાબ આપીશ.
〔してから〕 みんなで十分話し合った上の結論なのだから、全員従ってほしい。Minna de juubun hanashiatta ue no ketsuron na no da kara, zenin shitagatte hoshii. 〔after〕 It's a conclusion we reached after discussing it thoroughly together, so I want everyone to follow it. 〔के बाद〕 यह सबने मिलकर अच्छी तरह चर्चा करने के बाद निकाला गया नतीजा है, इसलिए सब इसका पालन करें। 〔પછી〕 આ બધાએ સાથે મળી સારી રીતે ચર્ચા કર્યા પછીનો નિર્ણય છે, એટલે બધા તેનું પાલન કરે.
〔に関連して〕 ここは交通は便利ではないが、店が多いので生活する上で特に困ることはない。Koko wa koutsuu wa benri de wa nai ga, mise ga ooi node seikatsu suru ue de toku ni komaru koto wa nai. 〔in ~ing〕 Transport isn't convenient here, but there are many shops, so there's no particular problem in day-to-day living. 〔... में〕 यहाँ आना-जाना सुविधाजनक नहीं, पर दुकानें बहुत हैं, इसलिए रहने में कोई ख़ास दिक़्क़त नहीं। 〔... માં〕 અહીં અવરજવર સગવડભરી નથી, પણ દુકાનો ઘણી છે, એટલે રહેવામાં કોઈ ખાસ તકલીફ નથી.
〔に関連して〕 彼とは仕事の上での付き合いだけなので、個人的なことは知らない。Kare to wa shigoto no ue de no tsukiai dake na node, kojinteki na koto wa shiranai. 〔in terms of〕 My relationship with him is only through work, so I don't know anything personal. 〔के मामले में〕 उससे मेरा सिर्फ़ काम का रिश्ता है, इसलिए निजी बातें नहीं जानता। 〔ની બાબતમાં〕 તેની સાથે મારો ફક્ત કામનો સંબંધ છે, એટલે અંગત વાતો નથી જાણતો.
〔に関連して〕 若者の意識調査は、新商品を開発する上でたいへん役立つ。Wakamono no ishiki chousa wa, shinshouhin o kaihatsu suru ue de taihen yakudatsu. 〔in ~ing〕 Surveys of young people's attitudes are very useful in developing new products. 〔... में〕 युवाओं की सोच पर सर्वे नए उत्पाद बनाने में बहुत काम आते हैं। 〔... માં〕 યુવાનોના અભિપ્રાયનાં સર્વેક્ષણ નવાં ઉત્પાદનો વિકસાવવામાં ખૂબ કામ આવે છે.
cf. 法律上は無罪になっても、道義的な責任は残る。Houritsujou wa muzai ni natte mo, dougiteki na sekinin wa nokoru. cf. Even if he is found not guilty legally, the moral responsibility remains. cf. क़ानूनी तौर पर बरी हो जाए तब भी नैतिक ज़िम्मेदारी बनी रहती है। cf. કાયદેસર રીતે નિર્દોષ ઠરે તો પણ નૈતિક જવાબદારી રહે છે.

Tell the two uses apart by the verb form: V-た／Nの + 上(で) = "first do ~, then" (a careful, formal sequence; ⑥ ご確認の上、ご来店ください); V-dictionary + 上で = "in the process of ~ / for ~" (⑩ 交渉をうまく進める上で, ④ 仕事の上でも). The second use is followed by a general judgement (大切だ, 役立つ, 困らない), not a past event. 法律上 (じょう) is a separate suffix: "from a legal standpoint".

【3】 〜た末／〜の末

N2-level · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ta sue / ~no sue
Meaning (EN)"after (a long process of) ~, finally …" (book: 〜して最後に)
Meaning (HI)लंबे समय तक ... करने के बाद आख़िरकार
Meaning (GU)લાંબા સમય સુધી ... કર્યા પછી આખરે
Connection (接続)V-た + 末(に)  |  N + の + 末(に)
Register硬 — writing, news; the outcome can be good or bad.
Typical use話し合った末, 長い戦いの末に, 大恋愛の末, 悩んだ末に — after effort, hesitation or struggle.

長時間にわたって話し合った末、新しい工場を建設することになった。Choujikan ni watatte hanashiatta sue, atarashii koujou o kensetsu suru koto ni natta. After hours of discussion, it was finally decided to build a new factory. घंटों चर्चा करने के बाद आख़िरकार नया कारख़ाना बनाने का फ़ैसला हुआ। કલાકો સુધી ચર્ચા કર્યા પછી આખરે નવું કારખાનું બાંધવાનું નક્કી થયું.
あの国は、本国との長い戦いの末に、とうとう独立を勝ち取った。Ano kuni wa, hongoku to no nagai tatakai no sue ni, toutou dokuritsu o kachitotta. After a long war with the mother country, that nation finally won its independence. उस देश ने मूल देश के साथ लंबी लड़ाई के बाद आख़िरकार आज़ादी हासिल की। એ દેશે મૂળ દેશ સાથેના લાંબા યુદ્ધ પછી આખરે આઝાદી મેળવી.
二人は大恋愛の末、結婚した。Futari wa dairen'ai no sue, kekkon shita. After a grand love affair, the two finally got married. ज़बरदस्त प्रेम-कहानी के बाद आख़िरकार दोनों ने शादी कर ली। જબરદસ્ત પ્રેમકહાણી પછી આખરે બંનેએ લગ્ન કર્યાં.

た末 is the neutral, written "in the end, after much ~": a result after effort or hesitation, good or bad. The past tense is natural in the second half. Note the noun connection: 航海の末に (練習問題 ①), not ×航海末.

【4】 〜あげく

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ageku
Meaning (EN)"after a lot of (troublesome) ~, in the end …" — after a long, difficult process, finally (often a bad or disappointing result) (book: ずっといろいろ大変なことをしてきて、最後に…)
Meaning (HI)बहुत ... करने के बाद आख़िर में (अक्सर बुरा नतीजा)
Meaning (GU)ઘણું ... કર્યા પછી છેવટે (ઘણી વાર ખરાબ પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)V-た + あげく(に)  |  N + の + あげく(に)
RegisterNeutral; carries the speaker's sense of trouble or exasperation.
Typical useさんざん迷ったあげく, 心配をかけたあげく, 困ったあげく — the result is often regrettable (家出, 捨てた).

Verb (た)V-た + あげく迷ったあげくmayotta ageku

どれを買おうかとさんざん迷ったあげく、｛何も買わずに店を出てきてしまった。／結局、最初に目についた物を選んだ。｝Dore o kaou ka to sanzan mayotta ageku, {nani mo kawazu ni mise o dete kite shimatta. / kekkyoku, saisho ni me ni tsuita mono o eranda.} After agonising over which one to buy, {I ended up leaving the shop without buying anything. ／ in the end I chose the first thing that caught my eye.} (the book prints both continuations) कौन-सा ख़रीदूँ, इस पर बहुत उलझने के बाद आख़िर में {कुछ भी ख़रीदे बिना दुकान से निकल आया। ／ जो चीज़ सबसे पहले नज़र आई थी, वही चुन ली।} કઈ ખરીદું એ વિશે ખૂબ મૂંઝાયા પછી છેવટે {કંઈ ખરીદ્યા વિના દુકાનમાંથી નીકળી ગયો. ／ જે વસ્તુ સૌથી પહેલાં નજરે પડી હતી, એ જ પસંદ કરી.}
親に心配をかけたあげく、家出をするとは何という親不孝だ。Oya ni shinpai o kaketa ageku, iede o suru to wa nan to iu oyafukou da. After causing your parents so much worry, to then run away from home — how ungrateful can you be! माँ-बाप को इतनी चिंता देने के बाद आख़िर में घर छोड़कर भाग जाना — कितनी नालायकी है! માતા-પિતાને આટલી ચિંતા કરાવ્યા પછી છેવટે ઘર છોડીને ભાગી જવું — કેટલું કપાતરપણું!
ペットショップで買ったワニが大きくなりすぎ、処置に困ったあげく、公園の池に捨てたらしい。Petto shoppu de katta wani ga ookiku narisugi, shochi ni komatta ageku, kouen no ike ni suteta rashii. Apparently the crocodile he bought at a pet shop grew too big, and after not knowing what to do with it, he finally dumped it in the park pond. सुना है पेट शॉप से ख़रीदा मगरमच्छ बहुत बड़ा हो गया, और उसका क्या करें यह न सूझने पर आख़िर में उसे पार्क के तालाब में छोड़ दिया। સાંભળ્યું છે કે પેટ શોપમાંથી ખરીદેલો મગર બહુ મોટો થઈ ગયો, અને તેનું શું કરવું એ ન સૂઝતાં છેવટે તેને બગીચાના તળાવમાં છોડી દીધો.

あげく is た末 with a sigh: the process was troublesome (迷う, 心配をかける, 困る) and the ending is usually unwelcome or blameworthy. The book's first example prints two endings to show that the result can still go either way. In ⑦ (さんざん悩んだ＿＿断りの手紙を書いた) both あげく and 末(に) are accepted.

【5】 〜というと

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~to iu to
Meaning (EN)"when you say ~, (do you mean …?)" — used to ask back about the other person's word (book: ※相手の言葉を問い返す時に使う); also "if it's ~ you mean, then …" (giving the typical example)
Meaning (HI)"... से आपका मतलब?" / ... की बात करें तो (उदाहरण के लिए) ...
Meaning (GU)"... એટલે તમારો મતલબ?" / ... ની વાત કરીએ તો (ઉદાહરણ તરીકે) ...

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's forms split into "add／sequence" (上に, 上で), "final outcome" (末, あげく) and three look-alike topic forms (というと, といえば, といったら). The 練習問題 test exactly where they diverge:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜上(に)~ue ni on top of Plain + 上に; N + の + 上に Adds a fact in the same direction; often も. Q3 — 安い上に…も大変.
〜た上で／Nの上~ta ue de after (first) doing V-た／Nの + 上(で) Careful sequence; formal instructions. Q6 — ご確認の上.
V-る上で／Nの上で~ru ue de in ~ing, in terms of V-dictionary／Nの + 上で Followed by a general evaluation. Q4, Q10.
〜た末(に)~ta sue after (a long process) V-た／Nの + 末 Neutral, written; good or bad end. Q1, Q7.
〜あげく~ageku after (trouble), in the end V-た／Nの + あげく Troubled process; often regrettable result. Q7.
〜というと~to iu to you mean ~? / if it's ~, then N + というと Same topic: asks back or gives the typical example. Q5, Q9.
〜といえば~to ieba speaking of ~ N + といえば Jumps to an associated, different topic. Q8.
〜といったら~to ittara the ~ is beyond words N(〜さ) + といったら Extreme degree; negative／critical ending. Q2.
Exam trap 1 — というと vs といえば: both repeat the other speaker's word. If the reply asks about／clarifies that word (〜のことですか, どんなことですか, 1955年ですね) → というと. If it brings up something new the word reminds you of → といえば (Q5, Q8, Q9).
Exam trap 2 — といったら needs an extreme ending: 〜ないほど, 言葉では言い表せない, ひどい, あきれる. If the sentence just continues the conversation, it is not といったら (Q2).
Exam trap 3 — the two 上で: V-た上で = "after"; V-る上で = "in doing". Then check the ending: a request／plan follows "after", a general judgement follows "in doing" (Q6 vs Q10).
Exam trap 4 — 末 vs あげく: both mean "in the end", but あげく implies a troubled process and usually an unwelcome result; for a celebrated achievement (独立を勝ち取った, たどり着いた) prefer 末 (Q1).
