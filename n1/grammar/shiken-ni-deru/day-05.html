JLPT N1 · Grammar · 試験に出る文法と表現 · 第1章 — 助詞の働きをする言葉 (Joshi no hataraki o suru kotoba — expressions that work like particles)

第5日 — 〜と相まって、〜をもって、〜を込めて、〜ときたら、〜はもちろん、〜はもとより、〜はおろか、〜というより、〜たりとも…ない (~to aimatte, ~o motte, ~o komete, ~to kitara, ~wa mochiron, ~wa moto yori, ~wa oroka, ~to iu yori, ~tari tomo ... nai)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.20–21 (PDF pages 20–21)
Grammar points covered
〜と相まって〜をもって／をもってすれば／をもってしても〜を込めて〜ときたら〜はもちろん〜はもとより〜はおろか〜というより〜たりとも…ない
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑬ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.2 (PDF page 168), under 第1章 "5 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Five items carry the book's N1 tag: と相まって, the をもって cell (をもって／をもってすれば／をもってしても — the tag sits at the foot of that shared cell), ときたら, はおろか and たりとも…ない. を込めて, はもちろん, はもとより and というより are N2-level. Big trap today: the "not only A but B" trio はもちろん／はもとより／はおろか — the key often accepts several, but はおろか is restricted to negative / surprising contexts. ときたら carries complaint; たりとも must end in a negative. Today has no メモ box.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜と相まって

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~to aimatte
Meaning (EN)"combined with ~ (to produce an effect)" — the book's gloss is （二つ以上のものが）いっしょになって、（ある効果をあげている） (two or more things coming together and producing some effect)
Meaning (HI)...के साथ मिलकर (असर पैदा करना)
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે ભળીને (અસર ઊભી કરવી)
Connection (接続)N₁ と N₂ が相まって  |  N₁ が N₂ と相まって  |  N と相まった + N
Register硬 — reviews, essays, analysis.
Typical useTalent + effort, old + exotic, taste + aroma, weather + location — two factors that reinforce each other.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (pair as subject)N₁ と N₂ が + 相まって才能と努力が相まってsainou to doryoku ga aimatte
Noun (one + another)N₁ が N₂ + と相まって異国的なものと相まってikokuteki na mono to aimatte
Noun (+ noun)N₁ と N₂ が + 相まった + N (shown in 練習問題 ④)味と香りが相まった料理aji to kaori ga aimatta ryouri
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Nominalise first (〜の／〜こと／〜さ).安さと相まってyasusa to aimatte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
天性の才能と本人の努力が相まって、彼は世界的な画家になった。Tensei no sainou to honnin no doryoku ga aimatte, kare wa sekaiteki na gaka ni natta. His natural talent combined with his own effort made him a world-class painter. जन्मजात प्रतिभा और अपनी मेहनत के मेल से वह विश्वस्तरीय चित्रकार बन गया। જન્મજાત પ્રતિભા અને પોતાની મહેનતના સંગમથી તે વિશ્વકક્ષાનો ચિત્રકાર બન્યો.
彼の作品は、日本古来のものが異国的なものと相まって、独特の雰囲気を作り上げていた。Kare no sakuhin wa, Nihon korai no mono ga ikokuteki na mono to aimatte, dokutoku no fun'iki o tsukuriagete ita. In his work, traditional Japanese elements blended with exotic ones to create a unique atmosphere. उसकी कृतियों में पारंपरिक जापानी तत्व विदेशी तत्वों के साथ मिलकर एक अनोखा माहौल रचते थे। તેની કૃતિઓમાં પરંપરાગત જાપાની તત્ત્વો વિદેશી તત્ત્વો સાથે ભળીને અનોખું વાતાવરણ સર્જતાં હતાં.

相まって = "two things meet and multiply". It always yields a result (became a world-class painter, created a unique atmosphere). Note the particle: either 「AとBが相まって」 (both named together) or 「AがBと相まって」. ④ fills in the past noun-modifying form: 肉本来の味と香辛料の香りが相まった独特の料理 (the key prints 相まっ(た) because た is already on the page).

【2】 〜をもって／〜をもってすれば／〜をもってしても

N1 (book tag on the cell) · 硬 formal
Reading~o motte / ~o motte sureba / ~o motte shite mo
Meaning (EN)"with / by means of ~" — the book's gloss is 〜で、〜を手段にして. Used for: a time point (本日をもって "as of today"), a criterion (280点をもって合格), a means (身をもって "first-hand"). をもってすれば = "if one uses ~ (it can be done)"; をもってしても = "even with ~ (it can't be done)".
Meaning (HI)...से / ...के द्वारा  |  すれば: ...के बल पर तो (हो जाएगा)  |  しても: ...के बावजूद भी (नहीं होगा)
Meaning (GU)...થી / ...વડે  |  すれば: ...ના બળે તો (થઈ જશે)  |  しても: ...છતાં પણ (નહીં થાય)
Connection (接続)N + をもって  |  N + をもってすれば  |  N + をもってしても
Register硬 — official notices, ceremonies, formal writing.
Typical useClosing/ending announcements (本日をもって閉店／終了), pass marks, "with your ability… / even with modern science…".
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time / criterion / means)N + をもって本日をもってhonjitsu o motte280点をもってnihyaku hachijutten o motte身をもってmi o motte
Noun (positive condition)N + をもってすれば皆の協力をもってすればmina no kyouryoku o motte sureba
Noun (even with)N + をもってしても彼の頭脳をもってしてもkare no zunou o motte shite mo
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — nouns only.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
をもって — time point / criterion / means
当店は、本日をもって閉店することになりました。Touten wa, honjitsu o motte heiten suru koto ni narimashita. This shop will close as of today. हमारी दुकान आज से बंद हो रही है। અમારી દુકાન આજથી બંધ થઈ રહી છે.
日本語能力試験1級は、280点をもって合格とする。Nihongo nouryoku shiken ikkyuu wa, nihyaku hachijutten o motte goukaku to suru. For Level 1 of the JLPT, 280 points will be the pass mark. जापानी भाषा प्रवीणता परीक्षा के स्तर 1 में 280 अंकों पर उत्तीर्ण माना जाएगा। જાપાની ભાષા પ્રાવીણ્ય પરીક્ષાના સ્તર 1માં 280 ગુણે ઉત્તીર્ણ ગણાશે.
戦時中の苦労は、身をもって体験した人にしかわからないだろう。Senjichuu no kurou wa, mi o motte taiken shita hito ni shika wakaranai darou. The hardships of wartime can probably only be understood by those who experienced them first-hand. युद्ध के समय की तकलीफ़ें शायद वही समझ सकते हैं जिन्होंने उन्हें ख़ुद झेला हो। યુદ્ધકાળની તકલીફો કદાચ એ જ સમજી શકે જેમણે તે જાતે વેઠી હોય.
をもってすれば — if one uses ~, (it can be done)
どんな困難も皆の協力をもってすれば、解決できないことはない。Donna konnan mo mina no kyouryoku o motte sureba, kaiketsu dekinai koto wa nai. With everyone's cooperation, there's no difficulty that can't be solved. सबके सहयोग से कोई भी मुश्किल ऐसी नहीं जो हल न हो सके। બધાના સહકારથી કોઈ પણ મુશ્કેલી એવી નથી જે ઉકેલી ન શકાય.
をもってしても — even with ~, (it can't be done)
こんな難問は、優秀な彼の頭脳をもってしても解決できないだろう。Konna nanmon wa, yuushuu na kare no zunou o motte shite mo kaiketsu dekinai darou. Even with his brilliant mind, a problem this hard probably can't be solved. इतनी कठिन समस्या उसके तेज़ दिमाग़ से भी शायद हल नहीं होगी। આટલી અઘરી સમસ્યા તેના તેજ દિમાગથી પણ કદાચ ઉકેલાશે નહીં.

Three exam uses of をもって: ① end point (本日をもって, 今回をもって — 練習問題 ③), ② criterion / means (優秀な成績をもって卒業 — ①), ③ "by the body" = first-hand (身をもって). Then the conditional pair: すれば → positive (can do it, ⑪ 実力をもってすれば合格は間違いない) and しても → negative (still can't, ⑩ 現代の科学をもってしても解明できない).

【3】 〜を込めて

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~o komete
Meaning (EN)"with (a feeling) put into it / full of ~" — the book's gloss is 〜という気持ちを十分に入れて (putting plenty of the feeling ~ into it)
Meaning (HI)...से भरकर / ...के साथ (भावना)
Meaning (GU)...થી ભરીને / ...ની સાથે (લાગણી)
Connection (接続)N (feeling) + を込めて  |  N + を込めた + N (added)
RegisterNeutral; warm, often in letters and gifts.
Typical useFeelings and wishes: 感謝の気持ち, 心, 愛情, 願い, 祈り, 思い.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を込めて心を込めてkokoro o komete愛情を込めてaijou o komete
Noun (+ noun)(added, not in book) N + を込めた + N心を込めた贈り物kokoro o kometa okurimono
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
旅行先でお世話になった方に、感謝の気持ちを込めて手紙を書いた。Ryokousaki de osewa ni natta kata ni, kansha no kimochi o komete tegami o kaita. I wrote a letter full of gratitude to the person who looked after me on my trip. यात्रा में जिन्होंने मेरी देखभाल की, उन्हें मैंने आभार से भरा पत्र लिखा। પ્રવાસમાં જેમણે મારી સંભાળ લીધી, તેમને મેં આભારની લાગણીથી ભરેલો પત્ર લખ્યો.
このセーターは、母が心を込めて編んでくれたものだ。Kono seetaa wa, haha ga kokoro o komete ande kureta mono da. This sweater is one my mother knitted for me with all her heart. यह स्वेटर माँ ने पूरे मन से मेरे लिए बुना था। આ સ્વેટર માએ પૂરા દિલથી મારા માટે ગૂંથ્યું હતું.
愛情を込めて、彼のために料理を作った。Aijou o komete, kare no tame ni ryouri o tsukutta. I cooked for him with love. मैंने प्यार से उसके लिए खाना बनाया। મેં પ્રેમથી તેના માટે રસોઈ બનાવી.

込める = "to load / pack in", so を込めて means the feeling is packed into the action (writing, knitting, cooking). The noun is always a feeling or wish — ⑧ 強く育つようにという願いを込めて五月人形を飾ります. Don't confuse it with をもって (a means or tool): ×心をもって編んだ.

【4】 〜ときたら

N1 · colloquial
Reading~to kitara
Meaning (EN)"as for ~ / talk about ~ (complaint)" — the book's gloss is 〜は（困ったものだという不満の気持ち） (topic は, with a feeling of exasperation)
Meaning (HI)...की तो बात ही मत करो / ...तो बस (शिकायत)
Meaning (GU)...ની તો વાત જ ન કરો / ...તો બસ (ફરિયાદ)
Connection (接続)N + ときたら
RegisterColloquial, emotional speech — not for formal writing.
Typical useComplaining about a person/thing close to you: 田中, うちの親, うちの主人, このコピー機.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ときたらうちの親ときたらuchi no oya to kitaraこのコピー機ときたらkono kopiiki to kitara
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used — nouns only.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中ときたら、大切な書類を電車の中に忘れてきちゃったんだって。まったくどうしようもないやつだなあ。Tanaka to kitara, taisetsu na shorui o densha no naka ni wasurete kichatta n datte. Mattaku dou shiyou mo nai yatsu da naa. That Tanaka — I hear he left important documents on the train. He's absolutely hopeless. इस तानाका का क्या कहें — सुना है ज़रूरी काग़ज़ ट्रेन में भूल आया। बिल्कुल निकम्मा है। આ તાનાકાનું શું કહેવું — સાંભળ્યું કે જરૂરી કાગળો ટ્રેનમાં ભૂલી આવ્યો. સાવ નકામો છે.
うちの親ときたら、二言目には勉強しなさいって、うるさくてしょうがない。Uchi no oya to kitara, futakotome ni wa benkyou shinasai tte, urusakute shou ga nai. My parents — every other word is "Study!" They're unbearably naggy. मेरे माँ-बाप तो बस, हर दूसरी बात पर "पढ़ाई करो" — बेहद परेशान करते हैं। મારાં માબાપ તો બસ, દરેક બીજી વાતે "ભણ" — ભારે કંટાળો આપે છે.
まったくこのコピー機ときたら、すぐ故障するんだから……。Mattaku kono kopiiki to kitara, sugu koshou suru n da kara...... Honestly, this copier — it breaks down at the drop of a hat... सच में, यह कॉपी मशीन तो बस — ज़रा-सी बात पर ख़राब हो जाती है... ખરેખર, આ કૉપી મશીન તો બસ — જરાકમાં બગડી જાય છે...

ときたら is a topic marker loaded with irritation; the sentence must go on to criticise. Words like まったく, どうしようもない, うるさくて, 〜んだから…… often come with it. ⑫ うちの主人ときたら、休みの日には寝てばかりいて、何にもしてくれないんですよ is the classic housewife's complaint. Never use it for praise.

【5】 〜はもちろん

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~wa mochiron
Meaning (EN)"not to mention ~ / ~ of course, and also …" — the book's gloss is 〜は当然 (~ goes without saying)
Meaning (HI)...तो है ही, ... भी
Meaning (GU)...તો છે જ, ... પણ
Connection (接続)N₁ + はもちろん + N₂ + も
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing.
Typical useA₁ is obvious; add A₂ with も. Works with positive and negative statements.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + はもちろん … も英語はもちろんフランス語もeigo wa mochiron furansugo mo
Verb(added, not in book) V-plain + の + はもちろん読むのはもちろん、書くこともできるyomu no wa mochiron, kaku koto mo dekiru
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Adj + の + はもちろん安いのはもちろんyasui no wa mochiron
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女はカナダに住んでいたそうで、英語はもちろんフランス語も話せる。Kanojo wa Kanada ni sunde ita sou de, eigo wa mochiron furansugo mo hanaseru. Apparently she lived in Canada, so she can speak French, not to mention English. सुना है वह कनाडा में रहती थी, इसलिए अंग्रेज़ी तो बोलती ही है, फ़्रेंच भी बोल लेती है। સાંભળ્યું છે કે તે કૅનેડામાં રહેતી હતી, એટલે અંગ્રેજી તો બોલે જ છે, ફ્રેન્ચ પણ બોલી શકે છે.
京都や奈良はもちろん、日本全国に古い寺が残っている。Kyouto ya Nara wa mochiron, Nihon zenkoku ni furui tera ga nokotte iru. Old temples survive all over Japan, not just in Kyoto and Nara. क्योतो और नारा में तो हैं ही, पूरे जापान में पुराने मंदिर बचे हुए हैं। ક્યોતો અને નારામાં તો છે જ, આખા જાપાનમાં જૂનાં મંદિરો બચેલાં છે.
忙しい時は、土曜日はもちろん日曜日も出勤することがあります。Isogashii toki wa, doyoubi wa mochiron nichiyoubi mo shukkin suru koto ga arimasu. When we're busy, I sometimes go to work on Sundays, let alone Saturdays. व्यस्त समय में शनिवार तो क्या, रविवार को भी काम पर जाना पड़ता है। વ્યસ્ત સમયે શનિવારે તો ખરું જ, રવિવારે પણ કામે જવું પડે છે.

はもちろん is the all-purpose "A goes without saying, and B too". It's the safest of today's three "not only" forms: it works in positive sentences (英語はもちろんフランス語も話せる), negative ones, and even with requests or will (はもちろん is fine before 〜てください). The second item usually takes も.

【6】 〜はもとより

N2-level · 硬 formal/written
Reading~wa moto yori
Meaning (EN)"not to mention ~ / let alone ~" — the book's gloss is 〜はもちろん、〜は当然
Meaning (HI)...तो है ही, ... भी (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)...તો છે જ, ... પણ (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)N₁ + はもとより + N₂ + も
Register硬 — the formal written version of はもちろん.
Typical useNews, reports: 国内はもとより海外からも, 周辺地域はもとより, 野党はもとより与党にも.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + はもとより … も国内はもとより海外からもkokunai wa moto yori kaigai kara mo
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) V／Adj + の + はもとより (rare).—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
阪神大震災の時は、国内はもとより海外からも多くの援助を受けた。Hanshin daishinsai no toki wa, kokunai wa moto yori kaigai kara mo ooku no enjo o uketa. At the time of the Great Hanshin Earthquake, a great deal of aid came from abroad, not to mention from within Japan. हानशिन महाभूकंप के समय देश के भीतर से तो मदद मिली ही, विदेशों से भी बहुत सहायता मिली। હાનશિન મહાભૂકંપ વખતે દેશમાંથી તો મદદ મળી જ, વિદેશમાંથી પણ ઘણી સહાય મળી.
原子力発電所の事故は、周辺地域はもとよりかなり広い地域に被害を及ぼした。Genshiryoku hatsudensho no jiko wa, shuuhen chiiki wa moto yori kanari hiroi chiiki ni higai o oyoboshita. はもとより means exactly the same as はもちろん — the key treats them as interchangeable in ②, ⑥, ⑨ and ⑬ — but it sounds more formal, so it fits news and reports. もとより literally means "from the start", i.e. "A is a given from the outset".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today mixes two families: the "not only A, B too" trio (Q2, Q6, Q9, Q13) and the "with ~" forms (をもって／を込めて／と相まって — Q1, Q4, Q8, Q10, Q11).

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜と相まって (N1)~to aimatte combined with AとBが相まって／AがBと相まって Two factors multiply into one effect. 才能と努力, 味と香り.
〜をもって (N1)~o motte with, by; as of N + をもって／をもってすれば／をもってしても Formal で: means, criterion, end point. 本日をもって, 実力をもってすれば.
〜を込めて~o komete with (feeling) put in N (feeling) + を込めて Only feelings/wishes. 感謝, 心, 愛情, 願い.
〜ときたら (N1)~to kitara as for (complaint) N + ときたら Topic + irritation; colloquial. Complaining about family, colleagues, machines.
〜はもちろん~wa mochiron not to mention A はもちろん B も Neutral; positive or negative; OK with requests. Anywhere.
〜はもとより~wa moto yori not to mention (formal) A はもとより B も Same as はもちろん, more formal. News, reports.
〜はおろか (N1)~wa oroka let alone A はおろか B も／さえ Surprise; mostly negative; no requests/will. 〜も書けない, 〜さえできない.
〜というより~to iu yori rather than (calling it) N／V／Adj + というより（むしろ） Rejects A as the wrong word. Correcting a description.
〜たりとも…ない (N1)~tari tomo ... nai not even one 一 + counter + たりとも + neg. Must be negative; smallest unit. 一瞬, 一円, 一日.
Exam trap 1 — はおろか needs a negative/shocked context: the key accepts はおろか only in ⑥ (平仮名も書けない) and ⑬ (廃止されてしまった). In ② and ⑨ (plain or positive facts) only はもちろん／はもとより are listed. If the sentence praises someone or makes a request, はおろか is out.
Exam trap 2 — をもってすれば vs をもってしても: すれば → positive result (できる, 間違いない); しても → negative result (できない, 解明できない). The printed すれば／しても in the question tells you which, and the blank is just をもって.
Exam trap 3 — ときたら is only for complaints: ×うちの主人ときたら、とても優しいんです. For neutral topics use は; for praise use なんといっても or は.
