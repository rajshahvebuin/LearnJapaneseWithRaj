Notes
This is an N2-level test, the second of the book's two N2 mock tests (the book covers both levels; it is kept here because the book sits in the N1 module). Format: 問題1 choose the word for ( ), 問題2 ★ sentence building, 問題3 文章の文法. The book's target: 15 / 22 or more. Several 問題1 items test the end of the sentence (決められない, 何の連絡もしてきません, 見たことがなかった) — the grammar word is given and you must supply the matching predicate. The 問題3 passage (a reflection on death) is not reproduced: only the sentence holding each blank is quoted, with our own short summary.

Mondai 1: Tsugi no bun no ( ) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — Choose the best option (1–4) for the ( ). (HI: ( ) में भरने के लिए 1–4 में से सबसे सही विकल्प चुनिए। GU: ( ) માં ભરવા માટે 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.)

Q1

消費者の需要に（＿＿＿）、生産量を決めます。Shouhisha no juyou ni (___), seisanryou o kimemasu.

Full sentence: 消費者の需要に応じて、生産量を決めます。Shouhisha no juyou ni oujite, seisanryou o kimemasu.
EN: We decide production volume according to consumer demand.
HI: हम उपभोक्ताओं की माँग के अनुसार उत्पादन की मात्रा तय करते हैं।
GU: અમે ગ્રાહકોની માંગ મુજબ ઉત્પાદનનું પ્રમાણ નક્કી કરીએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1とってtotte(にとって) for, to (someone)के लिएમાટે
2おいてoite(において) in, atमेंમાં
3応じてoujite(に応じて) according toके अनुसारમુજબ
4関してkanshite(に関して) concerningके बारे मेंના વિશે

Why: Production changes to match a varying factor (demand) → 〜に応じて (第2日). にとって (第2日) gives the viewpoint of a person ("for consumers"). において (第3日) marks a place/field. に関して (第1日) means "about", and "we decide production about demand" is not the meaning.

Q2

今度、海外旅行に行きます。海外旅行（＿＿＿）、たった三日間ですけど。Kondo, kaigai ryokou ni ikimasu. Kaigai ryokou (___), tatta mikkakan desu kedo.

Full sentence: 今度、海外旅行に行きます。海外旅行といっても、たった三日間ですけど。Kondo, kaigai ryokou ni ikimasu. Kaigai ryokou to itte mo, tatta mikkakan desu kedo.
EN: I'm going on a trip abroad soon. Well, I say "a trip abroad", but it's only three days.
HI: मैं जल्दी ही विदेश यात्रा पर जा रहा हूँ। कहने को विदेश यात्रा है, पर सिर्फ़ तीन दिन की।
GU: હું થોડા સમયમાં વિદેશ પ્રવાસે જાઉં છું. કહેવા માટે વિદેશ પ્રવાસ છે, પણ ફક્ત ત્રણ દિવસનો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1というとto iu tospeaking of / when you sayकी बात करें तोની વાત કરીએ તો
2といえばto iebaspeaking ofकी बात चली तोની વાત નીકળી તો
3といってもto itte moalthough I sayकहने को तोકહેવા માટે તો
4としてもto shite moeven ifअगर … भीજો … તો પણ

Why: The speaker scales down what was just said ("abroad", but only three days, with たった and けど) → N + といっても (第25日). というと／といえば (第24日) bring up an association ("speaking of trips abroad, …"). としても (第21日) is a hypothetical "even if", but the trip is real.

Q3

こんなにたくさんの食事、わたしたちだけではとても（＿＿＿）ません。Konna ni takusan no shokuji, watashitachi dake de wa totemo (___) masen.

Full sentence: こんなにたくさんの食事、わたしたちだけではとても食べきれません。Konna ni takusan no shokuji, watashitachi dake de wa totemo tabekiremasen.
EN: We could never finish this much food by ourselves.
HI: इतना सारा खाना हम अकेले कभी पूरा नहीं खा पाएँगे।
GU: આટલું બધું ખાવાનું અમે એકલા ક્યારેય પૂરું નહીં ખાઈ શકીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1食べかけtabekakehalf-eaten (かけ)आधा खायाઅડધું ખાધેલું
2食べきれtabekire(きれない) can't finish eatingपूरा न खा पानाપૂરું ન ખાઈ શકવું
3食べさせtabesase(causative) make eatखिलानाખવડાવવું
4食べかねtabekane(かねる) can't bring oneself toखाने में संकोचખાવામાં સંકોચ

Why: Too much food + とても…ません → "unable to do completely" = Vます + きれない (第34日), here 食べきれません. 食べかけ (第34日) is "half-done" and doesn't conjugate to 〜ません this way. 食べかねません would mean "could easily end up eating" (かねない), or 食べかねる "hesitate to eat" — neither fits the amount. 食べさせません "won't let (them) eat" changes the meaning.

Q4

（電話で）「鈴木部長、いらっしゃいますか。」「鈴木はただいま出かけて（＿＿＿）。」(Denwa de) "Suzuki buchou, irasshaimasu ka." "Suzuki wa tadaima dekakete (___)."

Full sentence: （電話で）「鈴木部長、いらっしゃいますか。」「鈴木はただいま出かけております。」(Denwa de) "Suzuki buchou, irasshaimasu ka." "Suzuki wa tadaima dekakete orimasu."
EN: (On the phone) "Is Mr Suzuki, the department head, in?" "Suzuki is out at the moment."
HI: (फ़ोन पर) "क्या सुज़ुकी विभाग-प्रमुख जी हैं?" "सुज़ुकी इस समय बाहर गए हुए हैं।"
GU: (ફોન પર) "શું સુઝુકી વિભાગ-વડા સાહેબ છે?" "સુઝુકી અત્યારે બહાર ગયા છે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いられますiraremasu(honorific-passive of いる — raises Suzuki)(आदरसूचक)(આદરસૂચક)
2おられますoraremasu(honorific — raises Suzuki)(आदरसूचक)(આદરસૂચક)
3いらっしゃいますirasshaimasu(honorific — raises Suzuki)(आदरसूचक)(આદરસૂચક)
4おりますorimasu(humble いる — own company)(विनम्र — अपनी कंपनी के लिए)(નમ્ર — પોતાની કંપની માટે)

Why: Talking to an outside caller about your own company's member, you treat him as "us": no title (鈴木は, not 鈴木部長は) and the humble おります (第40日 敬語). Options 1–3 all raise Suzuki with respect, which is wrong toward an outsider — the classic 内・外 keigo trap.

Q5

教師になったばかりのころは、学生に教える（＿＿＿）教えられている、という感じでした。Kyoushi ni natta bakari no koro wa, gakusei ni oshieru (___) oshierarete iru, to iu kanji deshita.

Full sentence: 教師になったばかりのころは、学生に教えるというより教えられている、という感じでした。Kyoushi ni natta bakari no koro wa, gakusei ni oshieru to iu yori oshierarete iru, to iu kanji deshita.
EN: When I had just become a teacher, it felt less like I was teaching the students and more like I was being taught.
HI: जब मैं नया-नया शिक्षक बना था, तब ऐसा लगता था कि मैं छात्रों को पढ़ा कम रहा हूँ, उनसे सीख ज़्यादा रहा हूँ।
GU: હું નવો નવો શિક્ષક બન્યો ત્યારે એવું લાગતું કે હું વિદ્યાર્થીઓને ભણાવતો ઓછો અને તેમની પાસેથી શીખતો વધારે હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にしてはni shite wafor (unexpectedly)के हिसाब सेના હિસાબે
2というよりto iu yorirather thanके बजायકરતાં વધારે તો
3ところがtokoro gahoweverलेकिनપરંતુ
4かわりにkawari niinstead of / in returnके बदलेના બદલે

Why: Two descriptions of the same experience, the second being the more accurate one ("teaching" → really "being taught") → 〜というより (第5日). かわりに (第37日) would mean "in exchange for teaching, I'm taught", a trade rather than a re-description. にしては (第20日) needs an expectation gap. ところが is a sentence connector, not a link after a verb.

Q6

友達とレストランで食事を（＿＿＿）、そこに中村さんが入ってきました。Tomodachi to resutoran de shokuji o (___), soko ni Nakamura-san ga haitte kimashita.

Full sentence: 友達とレストランで食事をしていたら、そこに中村さんが入ってきました。Tomodachi to resutoran de shokuji o shite itara, soko ni Nakamura-san ga haitte kimashita.
EN: I was having a meal with a friend at a restaurant when Mr Nakamura walked in.
HI: मैं एक दोस्त के साथ रेस्तराँ में खाना खा रहा था, तभी वहाँ नाकामुरा जी आ गए।
GU: હું એક મિત્ર સાથે રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં જમતો હતો, ત્યાં જ નાકામુરા સાહેબ અંદર આવ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1していたらshite itarawhile I was doing (then…)कर रहा था, तभीકરતો હતો, ત્યાં જ
2していればshite irebaif I am doingअगर कर रहा हूँ तोજો કરતો હોઉં તો
3するならsuru naraif you're going toअगर करने वाले हो तोજો કરવાના હો તો
4するとsuru towhen I do (each time)जब करता हूँ तोજ્યારે કરું ત્યારે

Why: A past one-off event that happened in the middle of an ongoing action → 〜ていたら + past (the "discovery / unexpected event" use of たら; general grammar, added note — not a separate day in the book). ば and なら are hypothetical and don't go with a past fact (入ってきました). すると would need a dictionary-form momentary action, not the ongoing "having a meal".

Q7

くわしく調査をしてからでないと、どこに工場を建てるかは（＿＿＿）。Kuwashiku chousa o shite kara de nai to, doko ni koujou o tateru ka wa (___).

Full sentence: くわしく調査をしてからでないと、どこに工場を建てるかは決められない。Kuwashiku chousa o shite kara de nai to, doko ni koujou o tateru ka wa kimerarenai.
EN: We can't decide where to build the factory until we've done a detailed survey.
HI: विस्तार से जाँच-पड़ताल किए बिना यह तय नहीं किया जा सकता कि फ़ैक्टरी कहाँ बनाई जाए।
GU: વિગતવાર તપાસ કર્યા વિના ફૅક્ટરી ક્યાં બાંધવી તે નક્કી ન કરી શકાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1決めてくださいkimete kudasaiplease decideकृपया तय कीजिएકૃપા કરી નક્કી કરો
2決めてもいいでしょうkimete mo ii deshouit's fine to decideतय कर सकते हैंનક્કી કરી શકાય
3決めましょうkimemashoulet's decideचलिए तय करेंચાલો નક્કી કરીએ
4決められないkimerarenaican't decideतय नहीं कर सकतेનક્કી ન કરી શકાય

Why: 〜てからでないと must be followed by a negative / "impossible" ending: "unless A is done first, B can't happen" (第20日 〜てからでないと…ない). Only 決められない completes the frame; the three positive requests and suggestions break it.

Q8

彼女は、ちょっと出かけると言って出て行ったきり、（＿＿＿）。Kanojo wa, chotto dekakeru to itte dete itta kiri, (___).

Full sentence: 彼女は、ちょっと出かけると言って出て行ったきり、何の連絡もしてきません。Kanojo wa, chotto dekakeru to itte dete itta kiri, nan no renraku mo shite kimasen.
EN: She said she was just popping out and left, and we haven't heard a word from her since.
HI: वह "ज़रा बाहर जा रही हूँ" कहकर निकली, और तब से उसकी कोई ख़बर नहीं आई।
GU: તે "જરા બહાર જાઉં છું" કહીને નીકળી, અને ત્યારથી તેનો કોઈ સંપર્ક નથી થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1連絡してくるはずですrenraku shite kuru hazu desushe should get in touchउसे संपर्क करना चाहिएતેણે સંપર્ક કરવો જોઈએ
2何の連絡もしてきませんnan no renraku mo shite kimasenhasn't been in touch at allकोई ख़बर नहीं आईકોઈ સંપર્ક નથી થયો

Why: V-た + きり means "did ~ and then nothing after that" (第26日), so the follow-up must describe a situation that hasn't changed since — a negative such as 何の連絡もしてきません. A prediction (1), a quick contact (3) or a question (4) all ignore the "and never since" meaning.

Q9

学生時代に山に登った。その時の景色ほど美しいものは（＿＿＿）。Gakusei jidai ni yama ni nobotta. Sono toki no keshiki hodo utsukushii mono wa (___).

Full sentence: 学生時代に山に登った。その時の景色ほど美しいものは見たことがなかった。Gakusei jidai ni yama ni nobotta. Sono toki no keshiki hodo utsukushii mono wa mita koto ga nakatta.
EN: I climbed a mountain in my student days. I had never seen anything as beautiful as the view then.
HI: छात्र-जीवन में मैं एक पहाड़ पर चढ़ा था। उस समय के नज़ारे जितनी सुंदर चीज़ मैंने कभी नहीं देखी थी।
GU: વિદ્યાર્થીકાળમાં હું એક પહાડ પર ચઢ્યો હતો. એ વખતના દૃશ્ય જેટલી સુંદર વસ્તુ મેં ક્યારેય જોઈ નહોતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見ることがあったmiru koto ga attasometimes sawकभी-कभी देखता थाક્યારેક જોતો
2見たことがあったmita koto ga attahad seenदेखा थाજોયું હતું
3見るわけがなかったmiru wake ga nakattathere was no way I'd seeदेखने का सवाल ही नहींજોવાનો સવાલ જ નહોતો
4見たことがなかったmita koto ga nakattahad never seenकभी नहीं देखी थीક્યારેય જોઈ નહોતી

Why: N + ほど + adj + ものは…ない = "nothing is as … as N" (第8日 ほど…はない). The ending must be negative, so 1 and 2 are out. 見るわけがなかった (わけがない 第14日) means "it was impossible that I'd see", not "I had never seen" — experience needs 〜たことがない.

幸せな死に方だった、という人もいれば、まさかこんなところで、と無念だっただろうという人［18］。Shiawase na shinikata datta, to iu hito mo ireba, masaka konna tokoro de, to munen datta darou to iu hito [18].

Full sentence: 幸せな死に方だった、という人もいれば、まさかこんなところで、と無念だっただろうという人もいる。Shiawase na shinikata datta, to iu hito mo ireba, masaka konna tokoro de, to munen datta darou to iu hito mo iru.
EN: Some people say it was a happy way to die, and others say he must have felt bitter, thinking "surely not here, of all places".
HI: कुछ लोग कहते हैं कि यह सुख भरी मौत थी, तो कुछ कहते हैं कि "भला यहाँ?" सोचकर उसे बड़ा अफ़सोस हुआ होगा।
GU: કેટલાક કહે છે કે આ સુખભર્યું મૃત્યુ હતું, તો કેટલાક કહે છે કે "અરે, અહીં?" વિચારીને તેને બહુ અફસોસ થયો હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はいないと思うwa inai to omouI think there's nobody whoमुझे लगता है कोई नहींમને લાગે છે કોઈ નથી
2もいるmo iruthere are also those whoकुछ ऐसे भी हैंકેટલાક એવા પણ છે
3がいるだろうかga iru darou kawould there be anyone who?क्या कोई होगा?શું કોઈ હશે?
4さえいるsae iruthere are even those whoऐसे भी हैं (हैरानी से)એવા પણ છે (નવાઈથી)

Why: 〜もいれば is the first half of the pair frame 〜も…ば…も "some …, others …" (第28日), so the second half must also be 〜もいる. はいないと思う and がいるだろうか break the pair and deny what was just reported; さえいる (第7日) treats the second opinion as an extreme, surprising case, which isn't the balanced "some say / others say" tone.

Q19

私たちは、どんな死に方が一番いいか、［19］。Watashitachi wa, donna shinikata ga ichiban ii ka, [19].

Full sentence: 私たちは、どんな死に方が一番いいか、考えることがある。Watashitachi wa, donna shinikata ga ichiban ii ka, kangaeru koto ga aru.
EN: We sometimes think about what kind of death would be best.
HI: हम कभी-कभी सोचते हैं कि किस तरह की मौत सबसे अच्छी होगी।
GU: આપણે ક્યારેક વિચારીએ છીએ કે કેવું મૃત્યુ સૌથી સારું હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1考えることがあるkangaeru koto ga arusometimes thinkकभी-कभी सोचते हैंક્યારેક વિચારીએ છીએ
2よく考えたこともないyoku kangaeta koto mo naihave never really thoughtकभी ठीक से सोचा भी नहींક્યારેય બરાબર વિચાર્યું પણ નથી
3考えたくないkangaetaku naidon't want to thinkसोचना नहीं चाहतेવિચારવા નથી માગતા

Why: The passage goes straight on to list what various people say about their ideal death, so the writer is opening that topic: "we sometimes think about it" → V-dict + ことがある. Options 2–4 would close the topic (never thought / don't want to / better not), contradicting the examples that follow.

Q20

踊っている時が［20］……。Odotte iru toki ga [20]......

Full sentence: 踊っている時がいちばん幸せだから……。Odotte iru toki ga ichiban shiawase da kara......
EN: Because the time when (that friend is) dancing is when (they're) happiest…

GU: કારણ કે નાચતી વખતે જ (તે) સૌથી વધુ ખુશ (હોય) છે…

1自分の仕事だからjibun no shigoto da karabecause it's my jobक्योंकि यह मेरा काम हैકારણ કે એ મારું કામ છે

Why: Just before, a friend says they want to die while dancing (see summary). The blank explains why, and the whole passage is about wishing the last moment to be happy (see Q21) → いちばん幸せだから. Q21

［21］、人生の終わりという大切な瞬間が、幸せに満ちたものでありたいと願うのであろう。[21], jinsei no owari to iu taisetsu na shunkan ga, shiawase ni michita mono de aritai to negau no de arou.

Q22

最後に、いい人生だったと言えるなんて、［22］ではないだろうか。Saigo ni, ii jinsei datta to ieru nante, [22] de wa nai darou ka.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The option sets in this test keep pairing the same look-alikes. These are the ones to have cold:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜といっても vs 〜というと／〜といえばto itte mo / to iu to / to ieba although I say / speaking of N, plain form + といっても / N + というと・といえば といっても scales the word down; というと／といえば start an association. Q2, Q13
〜にもかかわらず vs 〜にかかわりなくni mo kakawarazu / ni kakawari naku in spite of (a fact) / regardless of (any case) plain form, N + にもかかわらず / N (range, A か B か) + にかかわりなく One real fact vs a set of possibilities. Q12
〜きれない vs 〜かねる vs 〜かけkirenai / kaneru / kake can't finish / can't bring oneself to / half-done all Vます + … Amount vs reluctance vs incomplete state. Q3
おります vs いらっしゃいますorimasu / irasshaimasu humble いる / honorific いる 〜ております / 〜ていらっしゃいます Own company to an outsider = humble. Q4
〜というより vs 〜かわりにto iu yori / kawari ni rather than (re-describe) / instead, in exchange plain form + というより / V-dict + かわりに Same event, better word vs two different things traded. Q5
（文頭）というのは vs ということはto iu no wa / to iu koto wa the reason is / that means というのは…からだ / ということは…ということだ Looks back to a cause vs forward to a conclusion. Q10
要するに vs したがってyousuru ni / shitagatte in short / therefore sentence connectors Summary of examples vs logical result. Q21
Exam trap 1 — forms that force the ending: 〜てからでないと → negative (Q7); 〜たきり → "nothing since" (Q8); 〜ほど…は → ない (Q9); 〜ようにも → potential + ない (Q14); 〜もいれば → 〜もいる (Q18). When the grammar word is already in the stem, choose the predicate it demands.
Exam trap 2 — keigo inside vs outside: on the phone to an outsider, your own boss loses his title and gets humble verbs (鈴木はおります). Three of the four options in Q4 are respectful forms; all of them are wrong for the same reason.
Exam trap 3 — 問題3 connectors: decide first whether the blank continues, contrasts, concludes or summarises. A list of examples followed by a general statement nearly always takes 要するに／つまり.
