JLPT N1 · Grammar · 試験に出る文法と表現 · 第1章 — 助詞の働きをする言葉 (Joshi no hataraki o suru kotoba — expressions that work like particles)

第3日 — 〜において、〜にあたって、〜に基づいて、〜に即して、〜にしたがって、〜につれて、〜に伴って、〜に伴う、〜とともに (~ni oite, ~ni atatte, ~ni motozuite, ~ni sokushite, ~ni shitagatte, ~ni tsurete, ~ni tomonatte, ~ni tomonau, ~to tomo ni)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.16–17 (PDF pages 16–17)
Grammar points covered
〜において／における〜にあたって〜に基づいて〜に即して〜にしたがって〜につれて〜に伴って〜に伴う〜とともに
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑭ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.2 (PDF page 168), under 第1章 "3 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Only 〜に即して carries the book's N1 tag; the other eight are N2-level. The core cluster is the "as A changes, B changes" group — にしたがって／につれて／に伴って／とともに — which the key often accepts interchangeably (③⑤ accept につれて／にしたがって; ④ accepts に伴う／とともに). Items ⑬⑭ are the book's "a／b" choices and test the main-clause restrictions of につれて (needs a change) and にあたって (needs a preparatory action). Today has no メモ box.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜において／〜における

N2-level · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni oite / ~ni okeru
Meaning (EN)"in / at ~" — a formal で. The book: 〜で ※場所・時・機会・状況・領域を表す言葉に付く (attaches to words for place, time, occasion, situation, field)
Meaning (HI)...में / ...पर (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)...માં / ...ખાતે (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)N + において（は／も）  |  N + における + N
Register硬 — announcements, academic and official writing.
Typical useVenues (会議室), fields (分野, 業界), eras (20世紀, 現代), situations (緊急時).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + において／における N第一会議室においてdaiichi kaigishitsu ni oite20世紀における発明nijusseiki ni okeru hatsumei
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
本日の会議は、第一会議室において行います。Honjitsu no kaigi wa, daiichi kaigishitsu ni oite okonaimasu. Today's meeting will be held in Conference Room 1. आज की बैठक कॉन्फ़्रेंस रूम नंबर 1 में होगी। આજની બેઠક કૉન્ફરન્સ રૂમ નંબર 1માં યોજાશે.
近年、遺伝子の分野においては、画期的な発見が相次いでいる。Kinnen, idenshi no bun'ya ni oite wa, kakkiteki na hakken ga aitsuide iru. In recent years there has been one groundbreaking discovery after another in the field of genetics. हाल के वर्षों में जीन के क्षेत्र में एक के बाद एक क्रांतिकारी खोजें हो रही हैं। તાજેતરનાં વર્ષોમાં જનીનના ક્ષેત્રમાં એક પછી એક ક્રાંતિકારી શોધો થઈ રહી છે.
20世紀における最大の発明は、コンピューターだろう。Nijusseiki ni okeru saidai no hatsumei wa, konpyuutaa darou. The greatest invention of the 20th century is probably the computer. 20वीं सदी का सबसे बड़ा आविष्कार शायद कंप्यूटर है। 20મી સદીની સૌથી મોટી શોધ કદાચ કમ્પ્યુટર છે.

において is simply a dressed-up で for place/time/field — it cannot replace で for tools or materials (×ペンにおいて書く). Before a noun it becomes における (練習問題 ⑨: 現代における…問題). It sounds stiff in casual speech: 部屋で会う, not 部屋において会う.

【2】 〜にあたって／〜にあたり

N2-level · 硬 formal
Reading~ni atatte / ~ni atari
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of / when about to ~" — the book's gloss is （これから行われる）〜を考えて (with an upcoming ~ in mind)
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / ...से पहले (तैयारी के रूप में)
Meaning (GU)...ના પ્રસંગે / ...પહેલાં (તૈયારી રૂપે)
Connection (接続)N + にあたって／にあたり  |  V-dictionary + にあたって
RegisterFormal — speeches, business, official notices.
Typical useBefore a significant start (開会, 出発, 入学, 海外進出); the main clause is a preparation or a formal act (あいさつ, 調査, 確認).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にあたって開会にあたってkaikai ni atatte
VerbV-dictionary + にあたって／にあたり生活を始めるにあたってseikatsu o hajimeru ni atatte海外進出をするにあたりkaigai shinshutsu o suru ni atari
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
開会にあたって、一言ごあいさつを申し上げます。Kaikai ni atatte, hitokoto goaisatsu o moushiagemasu. On the opening of this meeting, allow me to say a few words of greeting. सभा के उद्घाटन के अवसर पर मैं दो शब्द कहना चाहूँगा। સભાના ઉદ્ઘાટન પ્રસંગે હું બે શબ્દ કહેવા માગું છું.
日本での生活を始めるにあたって、まず部屋を探した。Nihon de no seikatsu o hajimeru ni atatte, mazu heya o sagashita. When starting my life in Japan, the first thing I did was look for a room. जापान में जीवन शुरू करते समय मैंने सबसे पहले कमरा ढूँढा। જાપાનમાં જીવન શરૂ કરતી વખતે મેં સૌથી પહેલાં રૂમ શોધ્યો.
海外進出をするにあたり、まず、現地の市場調査のためのスタッフを送り出した。Kaigai shinshutsu o suru ni atari, mazu, genchi no shijou chousa no tame no sutaffu o okuridashita. Before expanding overseas, the company first sent out staff to research the local market. विदेश में कारोबार फैलाने से पहले कंपनी ने सबसे पहले स्थानीय बाज़ार के सर्वे के लिए कर्मचारी भेजे। વિદેશમાં વેપાર વિસ્તારતાં પહેલાં કંપનીએ સૌપ્રથમ સ્થાનિક બજારના સર્વે માટે કર્મચારીઓ મોકલ્યા.

Note the まず in examples 2 and 3: にあたって is followed by something you do in preparation for the event. That's why ⑭ answer is a (まず付近の道路を整備した) — b (有名店が入る予定だ) is just a fact about the centre, not a preparatory act. Use the dictionary form of the verb (始める, する), even for past events.

【3】 〜に基づいて／〜に基づき／〜に基づいた

N2-level · slightly formal
Reading~ni motozuite / ~ni motozuki / ~ni motozuita
Meaning (EN)"based on ~" — the book's gloss is 〜を基礎として、〜を基盤にして (with ~ as the foundation / basis)
Meaning (HI)...के आधार पर
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે
Connection (接続)N + に基づいて／に基づき  |  N + に基づいた／に基づく + N
RegisterSlightly formal; に基づき is written.
Typical useFacts, data, rules, experience, ideas as the foundation: 事実, 事件, 資料, 校則, 経験, 精神.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に基づいて／に基づき／に基づいた N事件に基づいてjiken ni motozuite経験に基づいた方法keiken ni motozuita houhou
Noun (+ noun, alt.)(added, not in book) N + に基づく + Nデータに基づく判断deeta ni motozuku handan
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このドラマは実際にあった事件に基づいて作られたものです。Kono dorama wa jissai ni atta jiken ni motozuite tsukurareta mono desu. This drama was made based on an incident that really happened. यह नाटक एक सच्ची घटना के आधार पर बनाया गया है। આ નાટક એક સાચી ઘટનાના આધારે બનાવાયું છે.
違反した生徒は、校則に基づき、1週間の停学処分になった。Ihan shita seito wa, kousoku ni motozuki, isshuukan no teigaku shobun ni natta. Under the school rules, the student who broke them was suspended for a week. नियम तोड़ने वाले छात्र को स्कूल के नियमों के आधार पर एक हफ़्ते के लिए निलंबित कर दिया गया। નિયમ તોડનાર વિદ્યાર્થીને શાળાના નિયમોના આધારે એક અઠવાડિયા માટે સસ્પેન્ડ કરાયો.
この治療法は、彼の長年の経験に基づいた効果的な方法だ。Kono chiryouhou wa, kare no naganen no keiken ni motozuita kouka teki na houhou da. This treatment is an effective method grounded in his many years of experience. यह इलाज उसके बरसों के अनुभव पर आधारित एक कारगर तरीका है। આ સારવાર તેના વર્ષોના અનુભવ પર આધારિત એક અસરકારક પદ્ધતિ છે.

基 = foundation: something is built on top of X. Contrast に沿って (第2日, following a line already drawn) and に即して (point 4, matching the actual situation). Before a noun use に基づいた or に基づく — the key for ⑪ accepts both に基づいた (+教育) and に基づいて (+教育が行われている).

【4】 〜に即して／〜に即した

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni sokushite / ~ni sokushita
Meaning (EN)"in line with / in keeping with ~" (the actual facts or situation) — the book's gloss is 〜に合わせて
Meaning (HI)...के अनुरूप / ...के अनुसार (वास्तविक स्थिति)
Meaning (GU)...ને અનુરૂપ / ...ને અનુસરીને (વાસ્તવિક સ્થિતિ)
Connection (接続)N + に即して  |  N + に即した + N
Register硬 — policy, management, academic writing.
Typical useReality-type nouns: 現状, 現実, 実態, 状況, 事実, 状態, 実情.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に即して／に即した N現状に即してgenjou ni sokushite状態に即したアドバイスjoutai ni sokushita adobaisu
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
規則というものは、現状に即して改めていく必要がある。Kisoku to iu mono wa, genjou ni sokushite aratamete iku hitsuyou ga aru. Rules need to be revised in line with the current situation. नियमों को मौजूदा हालात के अनुरूप बदलते रहना ज़रूरी है। નિયમોને વર્તમાન પરિસ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ બદલતા રહેવું જરૂરી છે.
その時の経済状況に即した対応ができないと、経営は成功しない。Sono toki no keizai joukyou ni sokushita taiou ga dekinai to, keiei wa seikou shinai. If you can't respond in a way that fits the economic situation of the time, the business won't succeed. अगर उस समय की आर्थिक स्थिति के अनुरूप क़दम न उठाए जाएँ, तो व्यापार सफल नहीं होता। જો તે સમયની આર્થિક પરિસ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ પગલાં ન લેવાય, તો વેપાર સફળ થતો નથી.
あのカウンセラーは相談者の状態に即したアドバイスをしてくれる。Ano kaunseraa wa soudansha no joutai ni sokushita adobaisu o shite kureru. That counsellor gives advice suited to the client's condition. वह परामर्शदाता सलाह लेने वाले की हालत के अनुरूप सलाह देता है। એ સલાહકાર સલાહ લેનારની સ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ સલાહ આપે છે.

即 means "close to / right on": stick closely to reality. Its typical partner nouns are all about the actual state of things (現状, 現実, 実態). 練習問題 ⑧ (理想ばかり追わずに、もっと現実に即して考えた方がいい) contrasts 理想 (ideals) with 現実 — a perfect に即して signal.

【5】 〜にしたがって／〜にしたがい

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni shitagatte / ~ni shitagai
Meaning (EN)"as ~ (changes), …" — the book's gloss is 〜の変化や程度に合わせて…（〜も同時に変化する）※動詞に付くことが多い (matching the change/degree of ~, … also changes; usually attaches to verbs)
Meaning (HI)जैसे-जैसे ... वैसे-वैसे
Meaning (GU)જેમ જેમ ... તેમ તેમ
Connection (接続)V-dictionary + にしたがって／にしたがい  |  N (change noun) + にしたがって
RegisterNeutral; にしたがい is written.
Typical useGradual change verbs (年を取る, 進む, 離れる, 増える) → main clause also a gradual change (〜てきた, 〜ていく, 〜なる).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dictionary + にしたがって／にしたがい年を取るにしたがってtoshi o toru ni shitagatte離れるにしたがいhanareru ni shitagai
Noun(added, not in book) N (change) + にしたがって経済の発展にしたがってkeizai no hatten ni shitagatte
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly (use 〜くなる／〜になる).—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
年を取るにしたがって、足腰が弱くなってきた。Toshi o toru ni shitagatte, ashikoshi ga yowaku natte kita. As I've grown older, my legs and back have grown weaker. जैसे-जैसे उम्र बढ़ी, वैसे-वैसे पैर और कमर कमज़ोर होते गए। જેમ જેમ ઉંમર વધી, તેમ તેમ પગ અને કમર નબળાં પડતાં ગયાં.
宅地造成が進むにしたがって、自然破壊も急速に広がっている。Takuchi zousei ga susumu ni shitagatte, shizen hakai mo kyuusoku ni hirogatte iru. As housing development advances, the destruction of nature is spreading rapidly too. जैसे-जैसे आवासीय ज़मीन का विकास बढ़ रहा है, प्रकृति का विनाश भी तेज़ी से फैल रहा है। જેમ જેમ રહેણાંક જમીનનો વિકાસ વધે છે, તેમ તેમ પ્રકૃતિનો વિનાશ પણ ઝડપથી ફેલાય છે.
電車が都心から離れるにしたがい、緑が多くなってきた。Densha ga toshin kara hanareru ni shitagai, midori ga ooku natte kita. The further the train got from the city centre, the greener it became. ट्रेन जैसे-जैसे शहर के केंद्र से दूर होती गई, हरियाली बढ़ती गई। ટ્રેન જેમ જેમ શહેરના કેન્દ્રથી દૂર જતી ગઈ, તેમ તેમ હરિયાળી વધતી ગઈ.

The book gives にしたがって and につれて the identical gloss, and the key accepts both for ③ and ⑤. Both need change on both sides: ×年を取るにしたがって、病院に行った (one-off action). にしたがって also has a second meaning not on this page — "following (instructions)" (指示にしたがって) — which につれて can't have.

【6】 〜につれて／〜につれ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni tsurete / ~ni tsure
Meaning (EN)"as ~ (changes), …" — same gloss as にしたがって: 〜の変化や程度に合わせて…（〜も同時に変化する）※動詞に付くことが多い
Meaning (HI)जैसे-जैसे ... वैसे-वैसे
Meaning (GU)જેમ જેમ ... તેમ તેમ
Connection (接続)V-dictionary + につれて／につれ  |  N (change noun) + につれて
RegisterNeutral; につれ is written.
Typical useNatural, gradual, proportional change — main clause 〜てきた／〜ていく／〜なる.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dictionary + につれて／につれ工業化が進むにつれてkougyouka ga susumu ni tsurete慣れるにつれnareru ni tsure
Noun(added, not in book) N (change) + につれて時代の変化につれてjidai no henka ni tsurete
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly (use 〜くなる／〜になる).悪くなるにつれてwaruku naru ni tsurete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
工業化が進むにつれて、手づくりの技術を持つ人が減ってきた。Kougyouka ga susumu ni tsurete, tezukuri no gijutsu o motsu hito ga hette kita. As industrialisation has advanced, the number of people with handcraft skills has dropped. जैसे-जैसे औद्योगीकरण बढ़ा, हाथ के हुनर वाले लोग कम होते गए। જેમ જેમ ઔદ્યોગિકીકરણ વધ્યું, તેમ તેમ હસ્તકલાનું કૌશલ ધરાવતા લોકો ઘટતા ગયા.
景気が悪くなるにつれて、失業者が増えてきた。Keiki ga waruku naru ni tsurete, shitsugyousha ga fuete kita. As the economy has worsened, unemployment has risen. जैसे-जैसे अर्थव्यवस्था बिगड़ी, बेरोज़गार बढ़ते गए। જેમ જેમ અર્થતંત્ર બગડ્યું, તેમ તેમ બેરોજગારો વધતા ગયા.
一人暮らしに慣れるにつれ、寂しいとも思わなくなってきた。Hitorigurashi ni nareru ni tsure, sabishii to mo omowanaku natte kita. As I got used to living alone, I stopped even feeling lonely. जैसे-जैसे अकेले रहने की आदत पड़ी, अकेलापन महसूस होना भी बंद हो गया। જેમ જેમ એકલા રહેવાની ટેવ પડી, તેમ તેમ એકલતા લાગવાની પણ બંધ થઈ ગઈ.

⑬ is the textbook test: 慣れるにつれて + a 友達がたくさんいる (a static state) ✗ vs b 友達が増えてきた (a change) ✓. If the main clause has no change verb, につれて／にしたがって are wrong. につれて also dislikes the speaker's will in the main clause (×慣れるにつれて、友達を作ろう).

【7】 〜に伴って／〜に伴い

N2-level · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ni tomonatte / ~ni tomonai
Meaning (EN)"along with / as a result of ~ (a change occurs)" — the book's gloss is 〜と同時に…（変化が起こる）
Meaning (HI)...के साथ-साथ / ...के चलते (बदलाव)
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે સાથે / ...ને લીધે (ફેરફાર)

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q8 (⑧)

理想ばかり追わずに、もっと現実＿＿＿考えた方がいい。Risou bakari owazu ni, motto genjitsu ___ kangaeta hou ga ii.

Full sentence: 理想ばかり追わずに、もっと現実に即して考えた方がいい。Risou bakari owazu ni, motto genjitsu ni sokushite kangaeta hou ga ii.
EN: Rather than chasing ideals all the time, you'd better think more in line with reality.
HI: सिर्फ़ आदर्शों के पीछे भागने के बजाय, वास्तविकता के अनुरूप ज़्यादा सोचना बेहतर है।
GU: ફક્ત આદર્શો પાછળ દોડવાને બદલે, વાસ્તવિકતાને અનુરૂપ વધુ વિચારવું સારું.

Why: 現実 is the reality-type noun that に即して (N1) pairs with, set against 理想. に基づいて ("based on reality") is grammatical but the book's key chooses に即して — the nuance is "stay close to how things actually are". において would mean "think while inside reality"; とともに "think together with reality" makes no sense.

Q9 (⑨)

現代＿＿＿最も深刻な問題の一つに、環境問題があげられる。Gendai ___ mottomo shinkoku na mondai no hitotsu ni, kankyou mondai ga agerareru.

Full sentence: 現代における最も深刻な問題の一つに、環境問題があげられる。Gendai ni okeru mottomo shinkoku na mondai no hitotsu ni, kankyou mondai ga agerareru.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにおけるni okeruin (+ noun)का / मेंનું / માં

Q10 (⑩)

出発＿＿＿、もう一度持ち物のチェックをした。Shuppatsu ___, mou ichido mochimono no chekku o shita.

Full sentence: 出発にあたって、もう一度持ち物のチェックをした。Shuppatsu ni atatte, mou ichido mochimono no chekku o shita.
EN: Before setting off, I checked my belongings once more.
HI: रवाना होने से पहले मैंने एक बार फिर अपना सामान जाँचा।
GU: નીકળતાં પહેલાં મેં ફરી એક વાર મારો સામાન તપાસ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにあたってni atattewhen about to (prepare)से पहलेપહેલાં
bとともにto tomo niat the same time asके साथ हीની સાથે જ
cに伴ってni tomonattealong withके साथ-साथની સાથે સાથે

Why: Departure is an upcoming event and checking belongings is a preparation for it — にあたって. 出発とともに would mean "at the moment of departure"; に伴って needs a change; において treats 出発 as a place.

Q11 (⑪)

このミッションスクールでは、キリスト教の精神＿＿＿教育が行われている。Kono misshon sukuuru de wa, Kirisutokyou no seishin ___ kyouiku ga okonawarete iru.

Full sentence: このミッションスクールでは、キリスト教の精神に基づいた（に基づいて）教育が行われている。Kono misshon sukuuru de wa, Kirisutokyou no seishin ni motozuita (ni motozuite) kyouiku ga okonawarete iru.
EN: At this mission school, education based on the Christian spirit is provided.

GU: આ મિશન સ્કૂલમાં ખ્રિસ્તી ધર્મની ભાવના પર આધારિત શિક્ષણ અપાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: The Christian spirit is the foundation of the education. The key accepts both readings: に基づいた modifies 教育 ("education based on…"), に基づいて links to 行われている ("is carried out on the basis of…"). における would make 精神 a place; に伴う would make education a side effect of the spirit.

北国では、雪解け＿＿＿春がやってくる。Kitaguni de wa, yukidoke ___ haru ga yatte kuru.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today has two clusters: the "basis / fit" trio (に基づいて／に即して, plus 第2日's に沿って) and the "A changes → B changes" quartet (にしたがって／につれて／に伴って／とともに).

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜において~ni oite in, at (formal で) N + において／における N Place/time/field only — not tools. Venues, eras, fields.
〜にあたって~ni atatte on the occasion of N／V-dict + にあたって Upcoming event + preparation in the main clause. 開会, 出発, 建設する.
〜に基づいて~ni motozuite based on N + に基づいて／に基づいた N X is the foundation something is built on. 事実, 史実, 資料, 経験, 精神.
〜に即して (N1)~ni sokushite in keeping with N + に即して／に即した N Stay close to the actual situation. 現状, 現実, 実態, 状況.
〜にしたがって~ni shitagatte as A, B V-dict + にしたがって Proportional change; also "following instructions". Gradual change both sides.
〜につれて~ni tsurete as A, B V-dict + につれて Natural proportional change; no "follow" meaning. Gradual change both sides.
〜に伴って~ni tomonatte along with (a change) N + に伴って／に伴い Formal; mostly change nouns; result can be one-off. 〜の増加, 〜化, 〜の発展.
〜に伴う~ni tomonau accompanying N + に伴う + N The side issue X brings. 危険, 副作用, 就職難.
〜とともに~to tomo ni together with / at the same time as N／V-dict／N・な-adj + である + とともに Widest: person, two facts, change, or a moment. 皆さんとともに, 夜明けとともに.
Exam trap 1 — the main clause must change: にしたがって／につれて／に伴って all need a change on the right side (〜てきた, 〜ていく, 〜なる, 増える, 発展する). A static state (たくさんいる) or a single volitional act fails — that is the whole of Q13.
Exam trap 2 — にあたって needs preparation: the main clause is what you do before / for the event (確認する, あいさつする, 準備する), often with まず. A description of the event's result (有名店が入る予定だ) fails — Q14.
Exam trap 3 — noun shapes: における, に基づいた／に基づく, に即した, に伴う — Q4, Q9 and Q11 all sit directly before a noun. When the key accepts both 〜て and the noun form (⑪), it is because the sentence can be read either way.
