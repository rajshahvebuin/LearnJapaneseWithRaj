第7日 — さえ、すら、こそ (sae, sura, koso)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.28–29 (PDF pages 28–29)
Grammar points covered
〜さえ／〜でさえ〜すら／〜ですら〜さえ…ば〜こそ／〜てこそ〜からこそ〜ばこそ
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑫ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.2 (PDF page 168), under 第2章 "7 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
The book tags 〜すら／〜ですら and 〜ばこそ as N1; the other four are N2-level. Two families today: さえ／すら (an extreme example — "even X") and こそ (spotlight — "X, and no other"). The classic trap is that さえ has a second, completely different use: 〜さえ…ば "as long as / if only". The book's メモ adds a からこそ trap: it is unnatural for a plain, simple reason (see §1 メモ and §3).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜さえ／〜でさえ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~sae / ~de sae
Meaning (EN)"even ~" — gives an extreme example to imply that everything else is obviously the same (book: 〜も ※極端な例示)
Meaning (HI)...तक / ...भी (चरम उदाहरण)
Meaning (GU)...પણ / ...સુધ્ધાં (અતિશય ઉદાહરણ)
Connection (接続)N + さえ  |  N + でさえ (subject)  |  N + に／で／と + さえ  |  V-ること + さえ
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing.
Typical useUsually with a negative or surprising predicate: 〜さえできない, 〜さえ知らない, 〜でさえ知っている.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + さえ／N + でさえ時間さえないjikan sae nai
VerbV-dictionary + こと + さえ水を飲むことさえできないmizu o nomu koto sae dekinai
Verb (stem)(added, not in book) V-stem + さえ + する／しない挨拶さえしないaisatsu sae shinai
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly — nominalise (〜さ／〜こと).—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
母は病気が重く、水を飲むことさえできない。Haha wa byouki ga omoku, mizu o nomu koto sae dekinai. My mother is so seriously ill that she can't even drink water. माँ की बीमारी इतनी गंभीर है कि वह पानी तक नहीं पी सकतीं। માની બીમારી એટલી ગંભીર છે કે તે પાણી પણ પી શકતી નથી.
たばこが体に悪いことは、小学校の子供でさえ知っている。Tabako ga karada ni warui koto wa, shougakkou no kodomo de sae shitte iru. Even elementary-school children know that cigarettes are bad for your health. सिगरेट सेहत के लिए बुरी है, यह तो प्राइमरी स्कूल के बच्चे तक जानते हैं। સિગારેટ તબિયત માટે ખરાબ છે એ તો પ્રાથમિક શાળાનાં બાળકો પણ જાણે છે.
このごろ忙しくて、食事をする時間さえない。Konogoro isogashikute, shokuji o suru jikan sae nai. I'm so busy these days that I don't even have time to eat. आजकल इतना व्यस्त हूँ कि खाना खाने तक का समय नहीं है। આજકાલ એટલો વ્યસ્ત છું કે જમવાનો પણ સમય નથી.

さえ picks the easiest / most obvious case and says even that fails (or holds) — so every harder case is implied. Use でさえ when the extreme example is the subject (子供でさえ知っている = "even children know"). With verbs, nominalise first: 飲むことさえできない.

【2】 〜すら／〜ですら

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~sura / ~de sura
Meaning (EN)"even ~" — same meaning as さえ, but a more formal / literary word (book: ※「さえ」より改まった言葉)
Meaning (HI)...तक / ...भी (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)...પણ / ...સુધ્ધાં (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)N + すら  |  N + ですら (subject)  |  N + に + すら  |  V-ること + すら
Register硬 — writing, speeches, news commentary. In casual speech さえ is more natural.
Typical useMostly in negative or critical sentences: 〜ですら解けない, 〜にすら知らせない.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + すら／N + ですら教師ですらkyoushi de sura
Noun + particleN + に + すら両親にすらryoushin ni sura
Verb(added, not in book) V-dictionary + こと + すら立つことすらできないtatsu koto sura dekinai
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
教師ですら解けない問題が、入試に出されることがある。Kyoushi de sura tokenai mondai ga, nyuushi ni dasareru koto ga aru. Sometimes questions that even teachers can't solve appear in entrance exams. कभी-कभी प्रवेश परीक्षा में ऐसे सवाल आ जाते हैं जिन्हें शिक्षक तक हल नहीं कर पाते। ક્યારેક પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષામાં એવા પ્રશ્નો આવે છે જે શિક્ષકો પણ ઉકેલી શકતા નથી.
両親にすら知らせないで結婚する人も、最近では少なくない。Ryoushin ni sura shirasenaide kekkon suru hito mo, saikin de wa sukunaku nai. These days, quite a few people get married without even telling their parents. आजकल ऐसे लोग भी कम नहीं हैं जो माता-पिता तक को बताए बिना शादी कर लेते हैं। આજકાલ માતાપિતાને પણ જાણ કર્યા વગર લગ્ન કરનારા લોકો ઓછા નથી.

すら is a stiffer さえ: on the exam they are usually interchangeable, and the 別冊 key accepts both in ① ⑥ ⑦. The difference is that すら does not have the conditional "〜さえ…ば" use — ×カードすら持っていれば is wrong. Like でさえ, ですら marks an extreme subject.

【3】 〜さえ…ば

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~sae ... ba
Meaning (EN)"as long as ~ / if only ~" — that one condition is enough (book: 〜だけで十分だ)
Meaning (HI)बस ... हो तो / अगर सिर्फ़ ... हो जाए तो
Meaning (GU)બસ ... હોય તો / જો ફક્ત ... થાય તો
Connection (接続)N + さえ + V-ば  |  V-stem + さえ + すれば (行きさえすれば, 食べさえすれば, しさえすれば)  |  V-て + さえ + いれば
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useStating the single minimum condition; with のだが／のに it expresses regret ("if only …").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + さえ + V-ばカードさえ持っていればkaado sae motte ireba
VerbV-stem + さえ + すればやめさえすればyame sae sureba
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj (〜く) + さえ + あれば安くさえあればyasuku sae areba
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj (〜で) + さえ + あれば健康でさえあればkenkou de sae areba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
現金がなくてもカードさえ持っていれば、買い物も食事もできる。Genkin ga nakute mo kaado sae motte ireba, kaimono mo shokuji mo dekiru. Even without cash, as long as you have a card you can shop and eat. नक़द न हो तब भी, बस कार्ड पास हो तो ख़रीदारी और खाना दोनों हो सकते हैं। રોકડા ન હોય તો પણ, બસ કાર્ડ પાસે હોય તો ખરીદી અને જમવાનું બંને થઈ શકે.
あなたさえいれば、何もいらない。Anata sae ireba, nani mo iranai. As long as I have you, I need nothing else. बस तुम हो, तो मुझे और कुछ नहीं चाहिए। બસ તું હોય, તો મારે બીજું કંઈ જોઈતું નથી.
お酒をやめさえすれば、健康になれるのだが……。Osake o yame sae sureba, kenkou ni nareru no da ga… If only he would quit drinking, he could get healthy, but… अगर बस शराब छोड़ दे तो सेहत ठीक हो सकती है, पर…… જો બસ દારૂ છોડી દે તો તબિયત સારી થઈ શકે, પણ……

Same word, different job: here さえ means "only this is needed", not "even". The verb pattern is the one learners get wrong: put さえ between the stem and すれば — 起きさえすれば, 売れさえすれば (練習問題 ③ ⑧). Ending with 〜のだが…… turns it into regret about a condition that isn't being met.

【4】 〜こそ／〜てこそ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~koso / ~te koso
Meaning (EN)"this ~ (and no other) / precisely ~" — strong emphasis on the word before it (book: ※"〜"の言葉の強調); 〜てこそ = "only by doing ~ (does something become true)" (book: ※"〜てこそ"は動詞に付く時の形)
Meaning (HI)यही / ठीक यही ... (ज़ोर)  |  〜てこそ: ...करके ही
Meaning (GU)આ જ / ખરેખર આ જ ... (ભાર)  |  〜てこそ: ...કરીને જ
Connection (接続)N + こそ  |  V-て + こそ
RegisterNeutral; 〜てこそ…のだ sounds a little emphatic/persuasive.
Typical use今年こそ (this year for sure), 〜こそ必要だ (it's X that really needs it), 努力してこそ成功する.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + こそ今年こそkotoshi koso
VerbV-て + こそ努力してこそdoryoku shite koso
Verb (nominalised)V-dictionary + こと + こそ考えることこそkangaeru koto koso
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + の + こそ (rare)大切なのこそtaisetsu na no koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今まで2回も入試に失敗した。今年こそ絶対合格するぞ。Ima made nikai mo nyuushi ni shippai shita. Kotoshi koso zettai goukaku suru zo. I've failed the entrance exam twice already. This year I'm definitely going to pass! अब तक दो बार प्रवेश परीक्षा में फ़ेल हो चुका हूँ। इस साल तो पक्का पास होकर रहूँगा। અત્યાર સુધી બે વાર પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષામાં નાપાસ થયો છું. આ વર્ષે તો ચોક્કસ પાસ થઈશ.
医者は患者に、健康に気をつけろとよく言うが、医者こそ健康管理が必要だ。Isha wa kanja ni, kenkou ni ki o tsukero to yoku iu ga, isha koso kenkou kanri ga hitsuyou da. Doctors often tell patients to look after their health, but it's doctors themselves who really need to manage their health. डॉक्टर मरीज़ों से अक्सर सेहत का ध्यान रखने को कहते हैं, पर असल में सेहत की देखभाल की ज़रूरत डॉक्टरों को ही है। ડૉક્ટરો દર્દીઓને ઘણી વાર તબિયતનું ધ્યાન રાખવા કહે છે, પણ ખરેખર તબિયતની સંભાળની જરૂર ડૉક્ટરોને જ છે.
才能だけではうまくいかない。努力してこそ成功するのだ。Sainou dake de wa umaku ikanai. Doryoku shite koso seikou suru no da. Talent alone won't get you there. It's only by working hard that you succeed. सिर्फ़ प्रतिभा से काम नहीं बनता। मेहनत करके ही सफलता मिलती है। ફક્ત પ્રતિભાથી કામ નથી ચાલતું. મહેનત કરીને જ સફળતા મળે છે.

こそ puts a spotlight on one item and implicitly excludes the rest ("not the patients — the doctors"). With verbs it attaches to the て-form, and the meaning becomes "only when you actually do ~": 実際に使ってこそ (④), 遊んでこそ成長する (⑪). こそ is positive emphasis — it does not pair naturally with complaints the way さえ does.

【5】 〜からこそ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kara koso
Meaning (EN)"precisely because ~" — から gives a reason and こそ emphasises it (book: ※"〜から"は理由を表し、"こそ"を付けて理由を強調する)
Meaning (HI)ठीक इसीलिए कि... / ...है, इसीलिए तो
Meaning (GU)બરાબર એટલા માટે કે... / ...છે, એટલે જ તો
Connection (接続)Plain form + からこそ  |  な-adj／N + だ + からこそ
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useReasons that are surprising, paradoxical or "not simple" (book: 単純な理由ではない文で、よく使われる) — "because I love you, I leave", "because she's strict, she's liked".
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + からこそ愛しているからこそaishite iru kara koso
い-adjectiveい-adj + からこそ厳しいからこそkibishii kara koso
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + だ + からこそ大変だからこそtaihen da kara koso
NounN + だ + からこそ不景気だからこそfukeiki da kara koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
愛しているからこそ別れる、ということもある。Aishite iru kara koso wakareru, to iu koto mo aru. Sometimes people part precisely because they love each other. कभी-कभी ऐसा भी होता है कि प्यार करने की वजह से ही लोग अलग हो जाते हैं। ક્યારેક એવું પણ બને છે કે પ્રેમ કરે છે એટલે જ લોકો છૂટા પડે છે.
あの先生は、学生に人気がある。やさしいからではない。厳しいからこそ、好かれるのだ。Ano sensei wa, gakusei ni ninki ga aru. Yasashii kara de wa nai. Kibishii kara koso, sukareru no da. That teacher is popular with students. Not because she's kind — it's precisely because she's strict that she's liked. वह शिक्षक छात्रों में लोकप्रिय हैं। इसलिए नहीं कि वे नरम हैं — बल्कि सख़्त हैं, इसीलिए पसंद किए जाते हैं। એ શિક્ષક વિદ્યાર્થીઓમાં લોકપ્રિય છે. નરમ છે એટલે નહીં — કડક છે એટલે જ ગમે છે.
経済の状況が悪い時代に、弱気になってはいけない。不景気だからこそ、積極的な経営が必要だ。Keizai no joukyou ga warui jidai ni, yowaki ni natte wa ikenai. Fukeiki da kara koso, sekkyokuteki na keiei ga hitsuyou da. You mustn't lose heart when the economy is bad. It's precisely because times are hard that aggressive management is needed. आर्थिक हालात ख़राब हों तो हिम्मत नहीं हारनी चाहिए। मंदी है, इसीलिए तो सक्रिय प्रबंधन ज़रूरी है। આર્થિક સ્થિતિ ખરાબ હોય ત્યારે હિંમત હારવી ન જોઈએ. મંદી છે એટલે જ સક્રિય સંચાલન જરૂરી છે.

からこそ is for reasons the listener might not expect — often the "obvious" reason is rejected first (やさしいからではない). Because of that, it sounds odd for an everyday cause-and-effect answer (see メモ). Nouns and な-adjectives need だ: 不景気だからこそ.

【6】 〜ばこそ

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~ba koso
Meaning (EN)"precisely because ~" — same meaning as 〜からこそ (book gloss: 〜からこそ), in a more literary shape
Meaning (HI)ठीक इसीलिए कि... (साहित्यिक)
Meaning (GU)બરાબર એટલા માટે કે... (સાહિત્યિક)
Connection (接続)V-ば + こそ  |  い-adj-ければ + こそ  |  N／な-adj + であれば + こそ; usually closes with 〜ばこそだ or 〜ばこそ、…
Register硬 — formal, written; rare in conversation.
Typical useExplaining someone's real motive: 将来を考えればこそだ, 家庭があればこそ…がまんできる.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ば + こそ考えればこそkangaereba koso
Verb (ある／いる)あれば／いれば + こそ発達していればこそhattatsu shite ireba koso
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-ければ + こそ厳しければこそkibishikereba koso
な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book) な-adj／N + であれば + こそ親であればこそoya de areba koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
危険を承知で新事業を進めたのも、会社の将来を考えればこそだ。Kiken o shouchi de shinjigyou o susumeta no mo, kaisha no shourai o kangaereba koso da. Pushing ahead with the new business despite knowing the risks was precisely because we were thinking of the company's future. जोखिम जानते हुए भी नया व्यवसाय आगे बढ़ाया, तो वह भी कंपनी के भविष्य के बारे में सोचकर ही। જોખમ જાણતાં છતાં નવો વ્યવસાય આગળ વધાર્યો એ પણ કંપનીના ભવિષ્યનો વિચાર કરીને જ.
「女の人が家庭を持って仕事をするのは大変でしょうね。」「そうですね。でも、温かい家庭があればこそ、つらい仕事もがまんできる、ということもありますよ。」"Onna no hito ga katei o motte shigoto o suru no wa taihen deshou ne." "Sou desu ne. Demo, atatakai katei ga areba koso, tsurai shigoto mo gaman dekiru, to iu koto mo arimasu yo." "It must be hard for a woman to have a family and work too." "It is. But sometimes it's precisely because you have a warm family that you can put up with a tough job." "औरत के लिए घर-परिवार सँभालते हुए नौकरी करना मुश्किल होता होगा।" "हाँ। पर कभी-कभी प्यार भरा परिवार होने की वजह से ही मुश्किल काम भी सहन हो जाता है।" "સ્ત્રી માટે ઘર-પરિવાર સંભાળીને નોકરી કરવી અઘરી હશે, નહીં?" "હા. પણ ક્યારેક હૂંફાળો પરિવાર હોય એટલે જ અઘરું કામ પણ સહન થઈ જાય છે."
マスコミの弊害を言う人は少なくない。しかし、世界のニュースを知ることができるのは、マスコミが発達していればこそだと思う。Masukomi no heigai o iu hito wa sukunaku nai. Shikashi, sekai no nyuusu o shiru koto ga dekiru no wa, masukomi ga hattatsu shite ireba koso da to omou. Plenty of people talk about the harm done by the mass media. Don't read the ば as "if": 〜ばこそ is a reason, not a condition — exactly 〜からこそ. Its favourite shape is the sentence-final 〜のは…ばこそだ ("the reason X happened is precisely Y"), as in examples 1 and 3 and 練習問題 ⑤ (考えればこそ).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's six forms split into "even" (さえ／すら), "only this condition" (さえ…ば) and "spotlight / real reason" (こそ, からこそ, ばこそ). The 練習問題 test exactly where they diverge:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜さえ~sae even (extreme example) N + さえ / V-ること + さえ Implies "so of course everything else too"; mostly negative contexts. できない, ない, 知らない with a minimal example.
〜でさえ~de sae even (subject) N + でさえ Subject version of さえ. 子供でさえ知っている.
〜すら／〜ですら~sura / ~de sura even (formal) N + (で)すら Same as さえ but stiffer; has no 〜ば use. Written or formal criticism.
〜さえ…ば~sae ... ba as long as, if only N + さえ + V-ば / V-stem + さえすれば Minimum sufficient condition — not "even". One condition is enough; regret with のだが.
〜こそ~koso precisely this (one) N + こそ Positive spotlight, excludes alternatives. 今年こそ, 〜こそ重要だ.
〜てこそ~te koso only by doing V-て + こそ Action as the real precondition. 使ってこそ意味がある.
〜からこそ~kara koso precisely because Plain + からこそ (N／な + だ) Emphasised, often paradoxical reason; odd for simple causes. Rejecting an expected reason.
〜ばこそ~ba koso precisely because (literary) V-ば + こそ Same as からこそ; ば is NOT "if". 〜のは…ばこそだ.
Exam trap 1 — さえ has two meanings: 子供さえ知っている ("even") vs カードさえあれば ("as long as"). Look for ば／すれば later in the clause. If it is there, すら and こそ are automatically wrong (Q3, Q8, Q9).
Exam trap 2 — what the blank follows: て-form → こそ (てこそ); plain clause → からこそ; ば-form → こそ (ばこそ); stem → さえ (さえすれば); noun as subject → でさえ／ですら. The connection alone often decides the item.
Exam trap 3 — からこそ vs から: per the book's メモ, からこそ is unnatural for a simple, factual cause (電車が遅れたから). N1 distractors use からこそ in such ordinary "why?" answers — reject them.
