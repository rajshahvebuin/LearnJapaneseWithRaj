第2日 — 〜にとって、〜として、〜に応じて、〜にこたえて、〜に沿って、〜に代わって、〜に比べて、〜に加えて、〜に反して (~ni totte, ~to shite, ~ni oujite, ~ni kotaete, ~ni sotte, ~ni kawatte, ~ni kurabete, ~ni kuwaete, ~ni hanshite)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.14–15 (PDF pages 14–15)
Grammar points covered
〜にとって〜として〜に応じて〜にこたえて〜に沿って〜に代わって〜に比べて〜に加えて〜に反して
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑩ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.2 (PDF page 168), under 第1章 "2 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
None of today's nine items carries the book's N1 tag, so all are N2-level. Every one of them is a 「に + verb-て」 chunk, and almost all change shape before a noun (に応じた, に沿った, に代わる, に反する — see the メモ box). The big traps: にとって vs として (viewpoint vs role), に応じて vs にこたえて (adjust to vs respond to someone's wish), and the two meanings of に反して (contrary to vs in violation of).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜にとって

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni totte
Meaning (EN)"for ~ / from the standpoint of ~" — the book's gloss is 〜を基準に考えれば (judging with ~ as the yardstick)
Meaning (HI)...के लिए / ...की दृष्टि से
Meaning (GU)...માટે / ...ની દૃષ્ટિએ
Connection (接続)N + にとって（は／も）  |  N + にとっての + N
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing.
Typical useFollowed by an evaluation: 大変だ, 難しい, 大切だ, 珍しい, 興味がある, 問題だ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にとって現代人にとってgendaijin ni totteだれにとってもdare ni totte mo
Noun (+ noun)(added, not in book) N + にとっての + N私にとっての幸せwatashi ni totte no shiawase
Verb(added, not in book) Not used directly — the noun is a person/group/organisation.—
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
漢字を使わない国の人にとって、漢字を覚えるのは大変なことだ。Kanji o tsukawanai kuni no hito ni totte, kanji o oboeru no wa taihen na koto da. For people from countries that don't use kanji, learning kanji is a tough job. जिन देशों में कांजी का प्रयोग नहीं होता, वहाँ के लोगों के लिए कांजी याद करना बहुत मुश्किल काम है। જે દેશોમાં કાંજી વપરાતા નથી, ત્યાંના લોકો માટે કાંજી યાદ રાખવા ખૂબ અઘરું કામ છે.
海外旅行は、現代人にとっては珍しいことではなくなった。Kaigai ryokou wa, gendaijin ni totte wa mezurashii koto de wa naku natta. For people today, travelling abroad is no longer anything unusual. आज के लोगों के लिए विदेश यात्रा कोई अनोखी बात नहीं रही। આજના લોકો માટે વિદેશ પ્રવાસ કોઈ નવાઈની વાત રહી નથી.
彼のスピーチのテーマは、だれにとっても興味のあるものだった。Kare no supiichi no teema wa, dare ni totte mo kyoumi no aru mono datta. The theme of his speech was one that interested everyone. उसके भाषण का विषय ऐसा था जिसमें हर किसी की रुचि थी। તેના ભાષણનો વિષય એવો હતો કે જેમાં દરેકને રસ પડે.

にとって sets the viewpoint from which a judgement is made: "for X, it is hard / important / unusual". So the main clause is almost always an evaluation word, not an action. ×私にとって賛成です is wrong — opinions and stances use として or は (私としては賛成です).

【2】 〜として

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~to shite
Meaning (EN)"as ~ / in the capacity of ~" — the book's gloss is 〜の立場・資格で (in the position / qualification of ~)
Meaning (HI)...के रूप में / ...की हैसियत से
Meaning (GU)...તરીકે / ...ના નાતે
Connection (接続)N + として（は／も）  |  N + としての + N
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useRoles, jobs, status, purposes: 観光客として来る, 親として, 歌手として有名だ, 記念として.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + として（も）観光客としてkankoukyaku to shite親としてもoya to shite mo
Noun (+ noun)(added, not in book) N + としての + N教師としての責任kyoushi to shite no sekinin
Verb(added, not in book) Not this meaning (V + として is the different "try to / even if" pattern).—
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は、以前に一度観光客として日本に来たことがある。Kare wa, izen ni ichido kankoukyaku to shite Nihon ni kita koto ga aru. He has been to Japan once before, as a tourist. वह पहले एक बार पर्यटक के रूप में जापान आ चुका है। તે અગાઉ એક વાર પ્રવાસી તરીકે જાપાન આવી ચૂક્યો છે.
優秀な教師が、親としても立派とは限らない。Yuushuu na kyoushi ga, oya to shite mo rippa to wa kagiranai. An excellent teacher isn't necessarily an admirable parent too. ज़रूरी नहीं कि एक बेहतरीन शिक्षक माता-पिता के रूप में भी उतना ही अच्छा हो। જરૂરી નથી કે ઉત્તમ શિક્ષક માતાપિતા તરીકે પણ એટલા જ સારા હોય.
彼は俳優だが、歌手としても人気がある。Kare wa haiyuu da ga, kashu to shite mo ninki ga aru. He's an actor, but he is popular as a singer too. वह अभिनेता है, लेकिन गायक के रूप में भी लोकप्रिय है। તે અભિનેતા છે, પણ ગાયક તરીકે પણ લોકપ્રિય છે.

として names the hat you are wearing — tourist, parent, singer. Compare with point 1: 親にとって子供は宝だ ("for parents, children are a treasure" — the parents' viewpoint) vs 親として責任がある ("as a parent, I have a responsibility" — the role). Before a noun it becomes としての (教師としての責任).

【3】 〜に応じて／〜に応じた

N2-level · slightly formal
Reading~ni oujite / ~ni oujita
Meaning (EN)"in accordance with / according to ~ / suited to ~" — the book's gloss is 〜に合わせて、〜にふさわしく (matching ~, appropriate to ~)
Meaning (HI)...के अनुसार / ...के हिसाब से
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે / ...ને અનુરૂપ
Connection (接続)N + に応じて  |  N + に応じた + N
RegisterSlightly formal — rules, pay systems, services.
Typical useWith nouns that vary: 能力, 年齢, 収入, 必要, 体力, 状況, 予算 — the result changes to match them.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に応じて／に応じた N能力に応じてnouryoku ni oujite体力に応じた運動tairyoku ni oujita undou
Verb(added, not in book) Not used directly (use 〜か + に応じて).どれだけ働くかに応じてdore dake hataraku ka ni oujite
い-／な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この会社の給料は、経験年数と能力に応じて決められる。Kono kaisha no kyuuryou wa, keiken nensuu to nouryoku ni oujite kimerareru. Salaries at this company are set according to years of experience and ability. इस कंपनी में वेतन अनुभव के वर्षों और क्षमता के अनुसार तय किया जाता है। આ કંપનીમાં પગાર અનુભવનાં વર્ષો અને ક્ષમતાના આધારે નક્કી થાય છે.
調査レポートには、必要に応じてグラフや図を入れるつもりだ。Chousa repooto ni wa, hitsuyou ni oujite gurafu ya zu o ireru tsumori da. I intend to put graphs and diagrams into the research report as needed. मैं शोध रिपोर्ट में ज़रूरत के हिसाब से ग्राफ़ और चित्र डालने वाला हूँ। હું સંશોધન અહેવાલમાં જરૂર પ્રમાણે ગ્રાફ અને આકૃતિઓ મૂકવાનો છું.
体力に応じた運動ならいいが、やりすぎはかえって体に良くない。Tairyoku ni oujita undou nara ii ga, yarisugi wa kaette karada ni yokunai. Exercise suited to your stamina is fine, but overdoing it is actually bad for your body. अपनी शारीरिक क्षमता के अनुसार व्यायाम ठीक है, लेकिन ज़्यादा करना उल्टा शरीर के लिए नुक़सानदेह है। શારીરિક ક્ષમતાને અનુરૂપ કસરત બરાબર છે, પણ વધુ પડતી કસરત ઊલટી શરીર માટે સારી નથી.

に応じて means "adjusting to match a variable": as X changes, Y changes with it (収入に応じて税金が決まる). The noun before it is usually something that differs from person to person or case to case. Set phrase: 必要に応じて "as necessary". Before a noun use the past form に応じた (体力に応じた運動).

【4】 〜にこたえて／〜にこたえる

N2-level · slightly formal
Reading~ni kotaete / ~ni kotaeru (humble: ~ni okotae shite)
Meaning (EN)"in response to ~" — the book's gloss is 相手の〜に含まれた気持ちを実現するように (so as to fulfil the wishes contained in the other party's ~)
Meaning (HI)...के जवाब में / ...को पूरा करने के लिए
Meaning (GU)...ના જવાબમાં / ...ને પૂરી કરવા
Connection (接続)N + にこたえて  |  N + にこたえる + N  |  humble: N + におこたえして
RegisterSlightly formal; におこたえして is humble business Japanese (shops, broadcasters).
Typical useWith nouns expressing what someone wants: 期待, 要望, 希望, リクエスト, 声援, 拍手, 要求.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にこたえて／にこたえる N拍手にこたえてhakushu ni kotaete期待にこたえるためkitai ni kotaeru tame
Noun (humble)N + におこたえしてご要望におこたえしてgoyoubou ni okotae shite
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
観客の拍手にこたえて、ピアニストはアンコール曲を弾き始めた。Kankyaku no hakushu ni kotaete, pianisuto wa ankooru kyoku o hikihajimeta. In response to the audience's applause, the pianist began to play an encore. दर्शकों की तालियों के जवाब में पियानोवादक ने एक और धुन बजानी शुरू कर दी। પ્રેક્ષકોની તાળીઓના જવાબમાં પિયાનોવાદકે એન્કોર ધૂન વગાડવાની શરૂ કરી.
この大学では、学生たちの期待にこたえるため、いくつかの改革案を打ち出した。Kono daigaku de wa, gakuseitachi no kitai ni kotaeru tame, ikutsu ka no kaikakuan o uchidashita. To live up to the students' expectations, this university put forward several reform proposals. छात्रों की अपेक्षाओं पर खरा उतरने के लिए इस विश्वविद्यालय ने कई सुधार प्रस्ताव पेश किए। વિદ્યાર્થીઓની અપેક્ષાઓ પૂરી કરવા આ યુનિવર્સિટીએ કેટલાક સુધારા પ્રસ્તાવ રજૂ કર્યા.
皆様のご要望におこたえして、輸入品特別セールを行います。Minasama no goyoubou ni okotae shite, yunyuuhin tokubetsu seeru o okonaimasu. In response to your requests, we are holding a special sale on imported goods. आप सबकी माँग पर हम आयातित सामान की विशेष सेल लगा रहे हैं। આપ સૌની માગણીને માન આપીને અમે આયાતી માલનું ખાસ સેલ યોજી રહ્યા છીએ.

にこたえて is written in kana or as 応えて — not 答えて. It answers a wish (applause = "we want more", 期待 = "we hope you will…"), and the main clause is the action that fulfils it. That's the difference from に応じて: に応じて adjusts to a varying condition (能力, 収入), にこたえて responds to someone's desire.

【5】 〜に沿って／〜に沿った

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni sotte / ~ni sotta
Meaning (EN)"along ~ / following ~ / in line with ~" — the book's gloss is （すでに存在しているものや示されていること）に合わせて (matching something that already exists or has been laid out)
Meaning (HI)...के किनारे-किनारे / ...के मुताबिक़ / ...का पालन करते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ને કિનારે-કિનારે / ...ને અનુસરીને
Connection (接続)N + に沿って  |  N + に沿った + N
RegisterNeutral.
Typical usePhysical lines (川, 道, 線路) and plans/guidelines already set out (計画, プログラム, 方針, マニュアル, 意向).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に沿って／に沿った N川に沿ってkawa ni sotte意向に沿った番組ikou ni sotta bangumi
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この辺は、川に沿って土産物屋が並んでいる。Kono hen wa, kawa ni sotte miyagemonoya ga narande iru. Around here, souvenir shops line the river. इस इलाक़े में नदी के किनारे-किनारे स्मृति-चिह्न की दुकानें लगी हैं। આ વિસ્તારમાં નદીને કિનારે-કિનારે સંભારણાંની દુકાનો હારબંધ છે.
式典は、プログラムに沿ってすすめていきます。Shikiten wa, puroguramu ni sotte susumete ikimasu. The ceremony will proceed according to the programme. समारोह कार्यक्रम-सूची के मुताबिक़ आगे बढ़ेगा। સમારંભ કાર્યક્રમ મુજબ આગળ વધશે.
スポンサーの意向に沿った番組が、視聴者に喜ばれるとは限らない。Suponsaa no ikou ni sotta bangumi ga, shichousha ni yorokobareru to wa kagiranai. A programme made in line with the sponsor's wishes isn't necessarily one viewers enjoy. प्रायोजक की इच्छा के अनुसार बना कार्यक्रम दर्शकों को पसंद आए, यह ज़रूरी नहीं। પ્રાયોજકની ઇચ્છા મુજબ બનેલો કાર્યક્રમ દર્શકોને ગમે જ એવું જરૂરી નથી.

沿う means "to run alongside". Literally you walk along a river; figuratively you follow a plan or someone's wishes that are already laid down (the book stresses すでに存在している). That's how it differs from に応じて (which adjusts to a changing variable) and from に基づいて (第3日 — "based on" a foundation).

【6】 〜に代わって／〜に代わり／〜に代わる

N2-level · に代わり leans written
Reading~ni kawatte / ~ni kawari / ~ni kawaru
Meaning (EN)"in place of ~ / on behalf of ~" — the book's gloss is 〜の代理で (as ~'s proxy). The book cross-refers to p.114 「〜代わりに」.
Meaning (HI)...की जगह / ...की ओर से
Meaning (GU)...ની જગ્યાએ / ...વતી
Connection (接続)N + に代わって／に代わり  |  N + に代わる + N
RegisterNeutral; に代わり is the written 連用中止 form.
Typical useA person standing in for another (課長, 教授, 社長, 夫), or one thing replacing another (石油に代わるエネルギー).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に代わって／に代わり／に代わる N課長に代わってkachou ni kawatte石油に代わるエネルギーsekiyu ni kawaru enerugii
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) Not used directly (V + 代わりに is the separate p.114 pattern).—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
入院中の課長に代わって、このプロジェクトは私が担当します。Nyuuinchuu no kachou ni kawatte, kono purojekuto wa watashi ga tantou shimasu. I'll be in charge of this project in place of the section chief, who is in hospital. अस्पताल में भर्ती सेक्शन चीफ़ की जगह इस प्रोजेक्ट की ज़िम्मेदारी मैं संभालूँगा। હૉસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ વિભાગ પ્રમુખની જગ્યાએ આ પ્રોજેક્ટની જવાબદારી હું સંભાળીશ.
留学中の教授に代わり、助手が講義をしている。Ryuugakuchuu no kyouju ni kawari, joshu ga kougi o shite iru. An assistant is giving the lectures in place of the professor, who is studying abroad. विदेश में अध्ययन कर रहे प्रोफ़ेसर की जगह सहायक व्याख्यान दे रहा है। વિદેશમાં અભ્યાસ કરી રહેલા પ્રોફેસરની જગ્યાએ મદદનીશ વ્યાખ્યાન આપે છે.
夫が忙しい時は、夫に代わって有権者とのコミュニケーションを図るのも政治家の妻の仕事だ。Otto ga isogashii toki wa, otto ni kawatte yuukensha to no komyunikeeshon o hakaru no mo seijika no tsuma no shigoto da. When her husband is busy, keeping in touch with voters on his behalf is also part of a politician's wife's job. जब पति व्यस्त हों, तो उनकी ओर से मतदाताओं से संपर्क बनाए रखना भी नेता की पत्नी का काम है। પતિ વ્યસ્ત હોય ત્યારે તેમના વતી મતદારો સાથે સંપર્ક રાખવો એ પણ રાજકારણીની પત્નીનું કામ છે.

The noun before に代わって is the one who normally would do it; the subject of the main clause is the substitute. Don't mix this up with に加えて (point 8): 代わって = "instead of" (one replaces the other), 加えて = "in addition to" (both). Before a noun it is に代わる (石油に代わるエネルギー "an energy to replace oil").

【7】 〜に比べて／〜に比べ

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni kurabete / ~ni kurabe

Meaning (HI)...की तुलना में
Meaning (GU)...ની સરખામણીમાં

The book's メモ box shows how four of today's forms change before a noun. Note the two groups: に応じて／に沿って become past 〜た, while に代わって／に反して become dictionary 〜る. Reproduced in full:

aに比べてni kurabetecompared withकी तुलना मेंની સરખામણીમાં
bに代わってni kawattein place ofकी जगहની જગ્યાએ
cに加えてni kuwaetein addition toके अलावाઉપરાંત
dにとってni tottefor (viewpoint)के लिएમાટે

Why: Two universities are measured against each other on one quality (整っている) — に比べて. に加えて would need も (B大学も); に代わって would mean B replaces A; A大学にとって would mean "from A's viewpoint, B is well equipped", which is not the point.

Q7 (⑦)

100年前の人たち＿＿＿飛行機で外国に行くことは夢のような話だっただろう。Hyaku-nen mae no hitotachi ___ hikouki de gaikoku ni iku koto wa yume no you na hanashi datta darou.

Full sentence: 100年前の人たちにとって飛行機で外国に行くことは夢のような話だっただろう。Hyaku-nen mae no hitotachi ni totte hikouki de gaikoku ni iku koto wa yume no you na hanashi datta darou.
EN: For people 100 years ago, flying abroad must have sounded like a dream.
HI: 100 साल पहले के लोगों के लिए हवाई जहाज़ से विदेश जाना किसी सपने जैसी बात रही होगी।
GU: 100 વર્ષ પહેલાંના લોકો માટે વિમાનમાં વિદેશ જવું એ સપના જેવી વાત રહી હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにとってni tottefor (viewpoint)के लिएમાટે
bとしてto shiteas (a role)के रूप मेंતરીકે
cに比べてni kurabetecompared withकी तुलना मेंની સરખામણીમાં
dに代わってni kawattein place ofकी जगहની જગ્યાએ

Why: 夢のような話だった is an evaluation judged from those people's viewpoint — にとって. として would make "people of 100 years ago" a role; に比べて would need a comparison; に代わって makes no sense.

Q8 (⑧)

税金は、収入＿＿＿決められる。Zeikin wa, shuunyuu ___ kimerareru.

Full sentence: 税金は、収入に応じて決められる。Zeikin wa, shuunyuu ni oujite kimerareru.
EN: Taxes are set according to income.
HI: कर आमदनी के अनुसार तय किए जाते हैं।
GU: કર આવકના આધારે નક્કી થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aに応じてni oujiteaccording to (a variable)के अनुसारના આધારે
bにこたえてni kotaetein response to (a wish)के जवाब मेंના જવાબમાં
cに沿ってni sottefollowing (a set plan)के मुताबिक़મુજબ
dに比べてni kurabetecompared withकी तुलना मेंની સરખામણીમાં

Why: Income is a variable that differs person to person, and the tax changes to match — に応じて (cf. book example 1: 能力に応じて決められる). Income is not a wish (にこたえて) nor a laid-down plan (に沿って); に比べて would need a comparative.

Q9 (⑨)

社員の期待＿＿＿、ボーナスは昨年度を下回った。Shain no kitai ___, boonasu wa sakunendo o shitamawatta.

Full sentence: 社員の期待に反して、ボーナスは昨年度を下回った。Shain no kitai ni hanshite, boonasu wa sakunendo o shitamawatta.
EN: Contrary to the employees' expectations, the bonus fell below last year's.
HI: कर्मचारियों की उम्मीदों के विपरीत, बोनस पिछले साल से भी कम रहा।
GU: કર્મચારીઓની અપેક્ષાથી વિપરીત, બોનસ ગયા વર્ષ કરતાં પણ ઓછું રહ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aに反してni hanshitecontrary toके विपरीतથી વિપરીત
bにこたえてni kotaetein response toकी पूर्ति मेंને પૂરી કરવા
cに応じてni oujitein accordance withके अनुसारને અનુરૂપ
dに沿ってni sottein line withके मुताबिक़મુજબ

Why: The bonus fell (下回った) — the opposite of what the staff hoped, so に反して (use a). 期待にこたえて is a very common collocation, which is the trap: it would mean the company fulfilled the expectation, contradicting 下回った. に応じて／に沿って likewise imply the result matched the hope.

Q10 (⑩)

視聴者のリクエスト＿＿＿、あの番組を再放送することにした。Shichousha no rikuesuto ___, ano bangumi o saihousou suru koto ni shita.

Full sentence: 視聴者のリクエストにこたえて、あの番組を再放送することにした。Shichousha no rikuesuto ni kotaete, ano bangumi o saihousou suru koto ni shita.
EN: In response to viewers' requests, we decided to rebroadcast that programme.
HI: दर्शकों की फ़रमाइश पर हमने उस कार्यक्रम को दोबारा प्रसारित करने का फ़ैसला किया।
GU: દર્શકોની વિનંતીના જવાબમાં અમે એ કાર્યક્રમ ફરી પ્રસારિત કરવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにこたえてni kotaetein response to (a wish)की फ़रमाइश परના જવાબમાં
bに応じてni oujitein accordance withके अनुसारના આધારે
cに反してni hanshitecontrary toके विपरीतથી વિપરીત
dに代わってni kawatteon behalf ofकी ओर सेવતી

Why: A リクエスト is a wish, and rebroadcasting fulfils it — にこたえて. (リクエストに応じて also exists in real Japanese, "complying with a request", but the key's answer is にこたえて because the book teaches に応じて as "matching a variable".) に反して would mean ignoring the request; に代わって would make the viewers the ones normally broadcasting.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's nine forms fall into four look-alike families: viewpoint vs role, three kinds of "according to", replace vs add, and compare vs contradict.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にとって~ni totte for, from ~'s viewpoint N + にとって（は） Sets the viewpoint for an evaluation. 大変だ, 大切だ, 珍しい, 夢のようだ.
〜として~to shite as (role, status) N + として／としての N Names the role or capacity; main clause can be any action. 観光客として来る, 歌手として有名だ.
〜に応じて~ni oujite in accordance with N + に応じて／に応じた N Result changes to match a variable. 能力, 収入, 年齢, 必要.
〜にこたえて~ni kotaete in response to N + にこたえて／にこたえる N Fulfils someone's wish. 期待, 要望, リクエスト, 拍手, 声援.
〜に沿って~ni sotte along, following N + に沿って／に沿った N Follows a line or plan that already exists. 川, 道, 計画, プログラム, 方針, 意向.
〜に代わって~ni kawatte in place of, on behalf of N + に代わって／に代わる N One replaces the other. Proxies, substitutes, replacement energy.
〜に加えて~ni kuwaete in addition to N + に加えて／に加え Both are present; main clause usually has も. Stacking two causes / successes.
〜に比べて~ni kurabete compared with N + に比べて／に比べ Main clause must be comparative. more/less, higher/lower, better.
〜に反して~ni hanshite contrary to / in breach of N + に反して／に反する N Result goes against an expectation or a rule. 予想, 期待 / 校則, 規則, 法律.
Exam trap 1 — 期待にこたえて vs 期待に反して: both collocate with 期待, so the noun alone won't tell you. Read the result: if the expectation was met (成績が上がった, 優勝した) → にこたえて; if it failed (下回った, 負けた) → に反して. Q9 is built on this.
Exam trap 2 — にとって vs として: にとって + evaluation (難しい, 大切だ); として + role (親として, 代表として). A quick test: can you replace it with "as a…"? → として. "For / in the eyes of…"? → にとって. 私としては賛成です (my position) ≠ 私にとって大切です (my evaluation).
Exam trap 3 — the shape before a noun: に応じた／に沿った (past) vs に代わる／に反する (dictionary) vs としての／にとっての (+の). Q3 (法律＿＿＿行為) gives the right meaning in the wrong shape (に反して) as a distractor.
