Notes
A mixed review of 第12日 (「〜たらすぐ」 words) and 第13日 (うちに, 際, 最中, 以来 …). Every item gives four printed options (1–4), JLPT 問題1 style. Half the items are connection tests — the options are the same verb in four forms ((2) (4) (9)), so you must know that 次第 takes the ます-stem, なり the dictionary form, and か…ないかのうちに a repeated verb. The other half test the main clause each form allows: 以来 needs a continuing state (13), ているうちに a gradual change (14). Levels: the forms mix the chapter's N1-tagged items (が早いか, なり, や否や, てからというもの, そばから) with N2-level ones.

まとめの問題 (1)–(14) from p.46. Each item has four printed options; the option numbers 1–4 are the book's. Correct answers from 別冊 解答 p.3 (PDF page 169).

まとめの問題 — choose the best option (1・2・3・4)

(1)

試合の＿＿＿突然雷が鳴って、大粒の雨が降り出した。Shiai no ___ totsuzen kaminari ga natte, ootsubu no ame ga furidashita.

Full sentence: 試合の最中に突然雷が鳴って、大粒の雨が降り出した。Shiai no saichuu ni totsuzen kaminari ga natte, ootsubu no ame ga furidashita.
EN: Right in the middle of the match, thunder suddenly rumbled and big drops of rain started to fall.
HI: मैच के ठीक बीच में अचानक बादल गरजे और बड़ी-बड़ी बूँदों वाली बारिश शुरू हो गई।
GU: મૅચની બરાબર વચ્ચે અચાનક વીજળી ગાજી અને મોટાં ટીપાંનો વરસાદ શરૂ થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1とたんtotanthe moment (after V-た)...ते ही...તાં જ
2先立ってsakidatteprior to (needs に)से पहलेપહેલાં
3最中にsaichuu niright in the middle ofठीक बीच मेंબરાબર વચ્ચે
4際してsaishiteon the occasion (needs に)के अवसर परના પ્રસંગે

Why: The blank follows N + の (試合の). Only 最中 attaches as N の + 最中に, and an interruption (thunder, sudden rain) breaking into the ongoing match is its typical use. とたん needs V-た; 先立って and 際して need に (試合に先立って／に際して), not の.

(2)

調査の結果が＿＿＿次第、連絡いたします。Chousa no kekka ga ___ shidai, renraku itashimasu.

Full sentence: 調査の結果がわかり次第、連絡いたします。Chousa no kekka ga wakari shidai, renraku itashimasu.
EN: We will contact you as soon as the results of the investigation are known.
HI: जाँच के नतीजे पता चलते ही हम आपसे संपर्क करेंगे।
GU: તપાસનાં પરિણામ જાણવા મળે કે તરત અમે આપનો સંપર્ક કરીશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1わかってwakatteて-formて-रूपて-રૂપ
2わかりwakariます-stemます-स्टेमます-સ્ટેમ
3わかるwakarudictionary formशब्दकोश रूपશબ્દકોશ રૂપ
4わかったwakattaた-formた-रूपた-રૂપ

Why: 次第 ("as soon as") attaches to the ます-stem: わかり次第. The main clause 連絡いたします is a future intention — exactly the clause type 次第 is for. The other three forms are wrong connections.

(3)

新曲が発売されることになり、それ＿＿＿プロモーションビデオが公開された。Shinkyoku ga hatsubai sareru koto ni nari, sore ___ puromooshon bideo ga koukai sareta.

Full sentence: 新曲が発売されることになり、それに先立ちプロモーションビデオが公開された。Shinkyoku ga hatsubai sareru koto ni nari, sore ni sakidachi puromooshon bideo ga koukai sareta.
EN: A new song is going to be released, and ahead of that a promotional video was made public.
HI: नया गाना रिलीज़ होने वाला है, और उससे पहले उसका प्रमोशनल वीडियो जारी किया गया।
GU: નવું ગીત રિલીઝ થવાનું છે, અને તેની પહેલાં પ્રમોશનલ વીડિયો જાહેર કરવામાં આવ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1に先立ちni sakidachiprior to...से पहले...પહેલાં
2次第shidaias soon as (stem)होते हीથાય કે તરત
3なりnarias soon as (V-dict)...ते ही...તાં જ
4が早いかga hayai kano sooner (V-dict)...ते ही फ़ौरन...તાં જ તરત

Why: The blank follows a noun (それ = the release). Only に先立ち attaches to a noun; 次第 needs a ます-stem, なり／が早いか a dictionary-form verb. Meaning fits too: the video came out before the release.

(4)

子どもは学校から＿＿＿なり、ゲームをやりはじめた。Kodomo wa gakkou kara ___ nari, geemu o yarihajimeta.

Full sentence: 子どもは学校から帰ってくるなり、ゲームをやりはじめた。Kodomo wa gakkou kara kaette kuru nari, geemu o yarihajimeta.
EN: The moment the kid got home from school, he started playing video games.
HI: बच्चा स्कूल से लौटते ही गेम खेलने लगा।
GU: બાળક શાળાએથી પાછું આવતાં જ ગેમ રમવા લાગ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1帰ってきkaette kiます-stemます-स्टेमます-સ્ટેમ
2帰ってきてkaette kiteて-formて-रूपて-રૂપ
3帰ってくるkaette kurudictionary formशब्दकोश रूपશબ્દકોશ રૂપ
4帰ってきたkaette kitaた-formた-रूपた-રૂપ

Why: "As soon as" なり attaches to the dictionary form: 帰ってくるなり. Same subject (the child) in both halves ✓. Stem + なり doesn't exist in this meaning, and V-た + なり is a different pattern ("did … and then nothing more", e.g. 出かけたなり戻らない), so 1, 2, 4 are wrong.

(5)

彼はよほど急いでいたらしく、エレベーターのドアが開く＿＿＿外に飛び出した。Kare wa yohodo isoide ita rashiku, erebeetaa no doa ga aku ___ soto ni tobidashita.

Full sentence: 彼はよほど急いでいたらしく、エレベーターのドアが開くや否や外に飛び出した。Kare wa yohodo isoide ita rashiku, erebeetaa no doa ga aku ya ina ya soto ni tobidashita.
EN: He must have been in a real hurry — the instant the elevator door opened, he dashed out.
HI: लगता है वह बहुत जल्दी में था, लिफ़्ट का दरवाज़ा खुलते ही बाहर दौड़ पड़ा।
GU: લાગે છે કે તે ખૂબ ઉતાવળમાં હતો, લિફ્ટનો દરવાજો ખૂલતાં જ બહાર દોડી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かと思うとka to omou tono sooner (after V-た)...हुआ ही था कि...થયું જ હતું કે
2うちにuchi niwhile...रहते...ત્યાં સુધીમાં
3とたんtotanthe moment (after V-た)...ते ही...તાં જ
4や否やya ina yathe instant...ते ही...તાં જ

Why: 開く is dictionary form. かと思うと and とたん need V-た (開いたとたん); 開くうちに ("while it opens") makes no sense with a sudden dash. や否や + V-dict, followed by an immediate past action ✓.

(6)

今の会社に就職＿＿＿、自分の時間がほとんどなくなってしまった。Ima no kaisha ni shuushoku ___, jibun no jikan ga hotondo nakunatte shimatta.

Full sentence: 今の会社に就職してからというもの、自分の時間がほとんどなくなってしまった。Ima no kaisha ni shuushoku shite kara to iu mono, jibun no jikan ga hotondo nakunatte shimatta.
EN: Ever since I joined my current company, I've had almost no time for myself.
HI: जब से इस कंपनी में नौकरी लगी है, अपने लिए लगभग कोई समय ही नहीं बचा।
GU: જ્યારથી આ કંપનીમાં નોકરી લાગી છે, ત્યારથી પોતાના માટે લગભગ સમય જ નથી રહ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1するが早いかsuru ga hayai kano sooner had I joined thanनौकरी लगते ही फ़ौरनનોકરી લાગતાં જ તરત
2し次第shi shidaias soon as I join (I will …)नौकरी लगते ही (करूँगा)નોકરી લાગે કે તરત (કરીશ)
3しているうちにshite iru uchi niwhile being employedनौकरी करते-करतेનોકરી કરતાં-કરતાં
4してからというものshite kara to iu monoever since I joinedजब से नौकरी लगी हैજ્યારથી નોકરી લાગી છે

Why: Joining the company is a turning point, followed by a big lasting change (no more free time) — てからというもの. が早いか needs a quick next action, 次第 a request/intention. 就職しているうちに is odd: 就職 is a one-time event, not an ongoing activity.

HI: खड़े होते ही चक्कर आया और मैं गिर पड़ा।
GU: ઊભા થતાં જ ચક્કર આવ્યાં અને હું પડી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1とたんtotanthe moment...ते ही...તાં જ
2や否やya ina yathe instant (V-dict)...ते ही...તાં જ
3なりnarias soon as (V-dict)...ते ही...તાં જ

(8)

彼はポテトフライが好きで、揚げる＿＿＿どんどん食べてしまうんです。Kare wa poteto furai ga suki de, ageru ___ dondon tabete shimau n desu.

Full sentence: 彼はポテトフライが好きで、揚げるそばからどんどん食べてしまうんです。Kare wa poteto furai ga suki de, ageru soba kara dondon tabete shimau n desu.
EN: He loves French fries — he eats them up one after another as fast as I can fry them.
HI: उसे फ़्रेंच फ़्राइज़ बहुत पसंद हैं, जैसे-जैसे तलती हूँ, वह फटाफट खाता जाता है।
GU: તેને ફ્રેન્ચ ફ્રાઇઝ બહુ ભાવે છે, જેમ જેમ તળું તેમ તેમ તે ફટાફટ ખાતો જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1うちにuchi niwhile...रहते...ત્યાં સુધીમાં
2に先立ってni sakidatteprior to...से पहले...પહેલાં
3そばからsoba karaas soon as, one after anotherजैसे-जैसे … वैसे-वैसेજેમ જેમ … તેમ તેમ
4かと思うとka to omou tono sooner (after V-た)...हुआ ही था कि...થયું જ હતું કે

Why: A repeated pattern where each batch is undone immediately (どんどん食べてしまう) — the hallmark of そばから. うちに would need a "before it changes" sense, に先立って is for formal events, and かと思うと needs V-た and a single observed change.

(9)

彼は高校を卒業＿＿＿うちに自分の会社を作ったという青年実業家だ。Kare wa koukou o sotsugyou ___ uchi ni jibun no kaisha o tsukutta to iu seinen jitsugyouka da.

Full sentence: 彼は高校を卒業するかしないかのうちに自分の会社を作ったという青年実業家だ。Kare wa koukou o sotsugyou suru ka shinai ka no uchi ni jibun no kaisha o tsukutta to iu seinen jitsugyouka da.

(10)

ご来場有難うございました。お帰りの＿＿＿受付にて粗品をお受け取りください。Goraijou arigatou gozaimashita. Okaeri no ___ uketsuke nite soshina o ouketori kudasai.

(11)

赤ちゃんは、今泣いていた＿＿＿もう笑っている。Akachan wa, ima naite ita ___ mou waratte iru.

GU: બાળક હમણાં જ રડતું હતું, અને હવે તો હસી રહ્યું છે.

(12)

GU: આ ફિલ્મ ગમે તેટલી વાર જોઉં, દરેક વખતે દિલને સ્પર્શી જાય છે.

Why: 何度見ても signals repetition, and the same emotional reaction occurs each time — V-dict + たびに. 際に is formal and for one occasion; 最中に needs 〜ている; 見るうちに would need a gradual change.

(13)

彼は5年前に事故を起こして以来、＿＿＿。Kare wa gonen mae ni jiko o okoshite irai, ___.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The option sets in this review keep pairing the same look-alikes. These are the ones to have cold:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たとたん vs 〜や否や／〜なりta totan / ya ina ya / nari the moment ~ V-た + とたん / V-dict + や否や／なり Same meaning; the verb form decides. (7) vs (4) (5)
〜次第 vs the "fact" formsshidai as soon as (future) V-stem + 次第 Only 次第 leads into intention/request. (2) 連絡いたします
〜最中に vs 〜際にsaichuu ni / sai ni mid-way (interrupted) / on the occasion N の + 最中に / N の + 際に Both take N の; 最中 = interruption, 際 = formal instruction. (1) vs (10)
〜たびに vs 〜際にtabi ni / sai ni every time / at the time V-dict + たびに / V-dict + 際に Repetition vs one occasion. (12) vs (10)
〜て以来 vs 〜てからというものte irai / te kara to iu mono ever since V-て + 以来 / V-て + からというもの Both need a continuing state; というもの stresses a big change. (13) vs (6)
〜ているうちに vs 〜か…ないかのうちにte iru uchi ni / ka ... nai ka no uchi ni while doing (change) / barely ~ V-ている + うちに / V-dict か V-ない かのうちに Gradual change vs near-simultaneity. (14) vs (9)
〜そばから vs 〜かと思うとsoba kara / ka to omou to each time, immediately / no sooner (once) V-dict + そばから / V-た + かと思うと Repeated cancelling pattern vs one observed switch. (8) vs (11)
Exam trap 1 — four forms of one verb: when the options are the same verb conjugated four ways, the item is pure connection: 次第 ← ます-stem (2); なり ← dictionary form (4); か…ないかのうちに ← V-dict + か + V-ない + かの (9).
Exam trap 2 — what comes before the blank: N + の → 最中に／際に／たびに (1) (10); bare noun or それ → に先立ち (3); V-た → とたん／かと思ったら (7) (11); V-dict → や否や／なり／そばから (4) (5) (8).
Exam trap 3 — choose the main clause: 以来 → a continuing state such as 〜ていない (13); ているうちに → a gradual change 〜てきた (14). Future guesses (だろう, つもり) and one-off past events don't fit either.
