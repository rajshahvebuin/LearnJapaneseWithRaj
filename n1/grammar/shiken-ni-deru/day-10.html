第10日 — まで、〜どころ (made, ~dokoro)

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.34–35 (PDF pages 34–35)
Grammar points covered
〜までだ（までのことだ）〜までもない（言うまでもない）〜ないまでも〜ばそれまでだ〜までして〜てまで〜どころか〜どころではない
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑪ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.3 (PDF page 169), under 第2章 "10 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Four of today's forms carry the book's N1 tag: 〜までだ, 〜までもない, 〜ないまでも, 〜ばそれまでだ. The others (〜までして, 〜てまで, 〜どころか, 〜どころではない) are N2-level. Watch the near-twins: までもない ("no need to") vs ないまでも ("even if not"), and どころか ("far from ~, actually the opposite / even more") vs どころではない ("in no situation for ~"). ② and ⑤ include 「正しい方を選びなさい」 brackets that test exactly the verb form before these patterns.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜までだ／〜までのことだ

N1 · slightly formal
Reading~made da / ~made no koto da
Meaning (EN)"I only/merely did ~ (nothing special beyond that)" (book: 〜する／しただけだ（それ以外に特別なこと／ものはない）); with a dictionary-form verb it also means "if so, I'll simply ~" (やめるまでのことだ)
Meaning (HI)बस ... ही किया / ... कर देंगे, बस (और कुछ नहीं)
Meaning (GU)બસ ... જ કર્યું / ... કરી દઈશું, બસ (બીજું કંઈ નહીં)
Connection (接続)V-た + までだ（です） |  V-dictionary + までだ／までのことだ
RegisterSlightly formal; often defensive or matter-of-fact.
Typical useExcuses and explanations ("I just did what I was told"), and resigned resolve ("if it fails, I'll just quit").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)V-た + までだ置いたまでですoita made desu
Verb (dictionary)V-dictionary + までのことだやめるまでのことだyameru made no koto da
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
Noun(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ここに置けと言われたから置いたまでです。なぜだかわかりません。Koko ni oke to iwareta kara oita made desu. Naze da ka wakarimasen. I was told to put it here, so I simply did. I don't know why. मुझसे यहाँ रखने को कहा गया, इसलिए बस रख दिया। क्यों, यह मुझे नहीं पता। મને અહીં મૂકવાનું કહેવાયું, એટલે બસ મૂકી દીધું. કેમ, એ ખબર નથી.
「上司に強制されてやったまでだ」という言い訳をよく聞く。"Joushi ni kyousei sarete yatta made da" to iu iiwake o yoku kiku. You often hear the excuse, "I only did it because my boss forced me to." "मैंने तो बस बॉस के दबाव में किया" — यह बहाना अक्सर सुनने को मिलता है। "મેં તો બસ બૉસના દબાણથી કર્યું" — આ બહાનું ઘણી વાર સાંભળવા મળે છે.
とにかくやってみよう。だめだったらやめるまでのことだ。Tonikaku yatte miyou. Dame dattara yameru made no koto da. Let's just try it. If it doesn't work, we'll simply stop — that's all. चलो, एक बार करके देखते हैं। नहीं बना तो छोड़ देंगे, बस। ચાલો, એક વાર કરી જોઈએ. નહીં ફાવે તો છોડી દઈશું, બસ.

まで here marks the end-point: "that's as far as it goes". After V-た it minimises one's own action (just obeyed orders — 練習問題 ⑧); after V-dictionary + までのことだ it shows calm resolve: "worst case, I simply do X". Think of it as a firmer だけだ.

【2】 〜までもない（言うまでもない）

N1 · neutral register
Reading~made mo nai (iu made mo nai)
Meaning (EN)"there's no need to ~" — you get the expected result without doing it (book: 〜をしなくても期待する結果が得られる（〜必要はない）); 言うまでもない = "it goes without saying" (book: もちろん)
Meaning (HI)...करने की ज़रूरत नहीं / कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं (ज़ाहिर है)
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની જરૂર નથી / કહેવાની જરૂર નથી (સ્વાભાવિક છે)
Connection (接続)V-dictionary + までもない  |  V-dictionary + までもなく、…  |  言うまでもない／言うまでもなく
RegisterNeutral; 言うまでもなく is common in formal writing.
Typical useTelling someone an action is unnecessary (タクシーに乗るまでもない), or stating the obvious (英語が有利なことは言うまでもない).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dictionary + までもない乗るまでもないnoru made mo nai
Verb (adverbial)V-dictionary + までもなく聞くまでもなくkiku made mo naku
Set phrase言うまでもない／言うまでもなく言うまでもなくiu made mo naku
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not used.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
駅までそれほど遠くないから、タクシーに乗るまでもない。Eki made sore hodo tooku nai kara, takushii ni noru made mo nai. The station isn't that far, so there's no need to take a taxi. स्टेशन इतना दूर नहीं है, इसलिए टैक्सी लेने की ज़रूरत नहीं। સ્ટેશન એટલું દૂર નથી, એટલે ટૅક્સી લેવાની જરૂર નથી.
図書館で借りればいいのだから、わざわざ買うまでもない。Toshokan de karireba ii no da kara, wazawaza kau made mo nai. You can just borrow it from the library, so there's no need to go to the trouble of buying it. लाइब्रेरी से ले सकते हो, तो ख़ास तौर पर ख़रीदने की ज़रूरत नहीं। લાઇબ્રેરીમાંથી લઈ શકાય છે, તો ખાસ ખરીદવાની જરૂર નથી.
先生から話を聞くまでもなく、学生はこの事件をよく知っていた。Sensei kara hanashi o kiku made mo naku, gakusei wa kono jiken o yoku shitte ita. The students knew all about this incident without even needing to hear about it from the teacher. शिक्षक से सुनने की ज़रूरत ही नहीं पड़ी, छात्र इस घटना को अच्छी तरह जानते थे। શિક્ષક પાસેથી સાંભળવાની જરૂર જ ન પડી, વિદ્યાર્થીઓ આ ઘટના સારી રીતે જાણતા હતા.

cf. 「言うまでもない」（もちろん）

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
就職する時、英語が話せれば有利なことは言うまでもない。Shuushoku suru toki, eigo ga hanasereba yuuri na koto wa iu made mo nai. It goes without saying that being able to speak English is an advantage when job-hunting. कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं कि नौकरी ढूँढते वक़्त अंग्रेज़ी बोल पाना फ़ायदेमंद है। કહેવાની જરૂર નથી કે નોકરી શોધતી વખતે અંગ્રેજી બોલી શકવું ફાયદાકારક છે.
都心に近い所は言うまでもなく、郊外でも土地を買うのは大変だ。Toshin ni chikai tokoro wa iu made mo naku, kougai demo tochi o kau no wa taihen da. Buying land is hard even in the suburbs, let alone close to the city centre. शहर के बीचोंबीच की तो बात ही छोड़िए, उपनगरों में भी ज़मीन ख़रीदना मुश्किल है। શહેરના કેન્દ્ર પાસેની તો વાત જ જવા દો, પરાંમાં પણ જમીન ખરીદવી અઘરી છે.

Literally "it doesn't (need to) go as far as ~". It always follows the dictionary form (わざわざ着替えるまでもない, 練習問題 ①; 海外に行くまでもない, ⑨). 〜は言うまでもなく、…も works like "not to mention ~" — compare 第5日 はもちろん／はもとより.

【3】 〜ないまでも

N1 · neutral register
Reading~nai made mo
Meaning (EN)"even if not (as much as) ~, at least …" — the ideal is impossible, so you aim for something close (book: 〜は無理でも（それに近いことを要求したり望んだりする）)
Meaning (HI)भले ही ... न हो, कम से कम ... तो
Meaning (GU)ભલે ... ન થાય, ઓછામાં ઓછું ... તો
Connection (接続)V-ない + までも、（せめて）…  |  〜とは言わないまでも
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useSecond half often has せめて, 〜たい, 〜よう, 〜ぐらいは: 優勝はできないまでも、せめて3位には入りたい.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない + までもできないまでもdekinai made mo
Verb (potential)V-potential-ない + までも行けないまでもikenai made mo
QuoteN + とは言わない + までも5時とは言わないまでもgoji to wa iwanai made mo
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-くない + までも高くないまでもtakaku nai made mo
な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book) N／な-adj + ではない + までも満点ではないまでもmanten de wa nai made mo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
優勝はできないまでも、せめて3位ぐらいには入りたい。Yuushou wa dekinai made mo, semete san'i gurai ni wa hairitai. Even if we can't win the championship, I'd at least like to make it into the top three. भले ही जीत न पाएँ, कम से कम तीसरे स्थान तक तो पहुँचना चाहते हैं। ભલે ચેમ્પિયન ન બનીએ, ઓછામાં ઓછું ત્રીજા સ્થાને તો પહોંચવું છે.
親友が入院しているのだから、病院には行けないまでもお見舞いのはがきぐらいは書こう。Shin'yuu ga nyuuin shite iru no da kara, byouin ni wa ikenai made mo omimai no hagaki gurai wa kakou. My best friend is in hospital, so even if I can't visit, I'll at least write a get-well card. मेरा जिगरी दोस्त अस्पताल में है, अस्पताल भले न जा सकूँ, कम से कम हालचाल पूछने वाला कार्ड तो लिखूँगा। મારો જિગરી મિત્ર હૉસ્પિટલમાં છે, ભલે હૉસ્પિટલ ન જઈ શકું, ઓછામાં ઓછું ખબરઅંતર પૂછતું કાર્ડ તો લખીશ.
5時とは言わないまでも、せめて6時までには仕事を終えたい。Goji to wa iwanai made mo, semete rokuji made ni wa shigoto o oetai. I won't say by five, but I'd like to finish work by six at the latest. पाँच बजे तक तो नहीं कहता, पर कम से कम छह बजे तक काम ख़त्म करना चाहता हूँ। પાંચ વાગ્યા સુધી તો નથી કહેતો, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછું છ વાગ્યા સુધીમાં કામ પૂરું કરવું છે.

A step-down pattern: "ideal X is out of reach → settle for close-to-X". The verb before まで is negative (〜ない). 〜とは言わないまでも ("I won't go so far as to say ~") is a high-frequency N1 chunk — 練習問題 ② asks you to pick 言わない over 言う.

【4】 〜ばそれまでだ

N1 · neutral register
Reading~ba sore made da
Meaning (EN)"if ~, that's the end of it / it's all over (everything is wasted)" (book: 〜という場合には、もうそれで終わりだ)
Meaning (HI)अगर ... तो सब ख़त्म / फिर कोई फ़ायदा नहीं
Meaning (GU)જો ... તો બધું પૂરું / પછી કંઈ કામનું નહીં
Connection (接続)V-ば + それまでだ  |  V-てしまえば + それまでだ  |  〜なければ + それまでだ
RegisterNeutral; often resigned or philosophical.
Typical useAll effort becomes meaningless under one condition: 死んでしまえばそれまでだ.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ば + それまでだ死んでしまえばそれまでだshinde shimaeba sore made da
Verb (negative)V-なければ + それまでだ入っていなければそれまでだhaitte inakereba sore made da
Verb (passive)V-passive-ば + それまでだ反対されればそれまでだhantai sarereba sore made da
い-adjective(added, not in book) Not usual.—
な-adjective(added, not in book) Not usual.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
立派な金庫があっても、中身が入っていなければそれまでだ。Rippa na kinko ga atte mo, nakami ga haitte inakereba sore made da. Even a splendid safe is pointless if there's nothing inside it. शानदार तिजोरी हो तो भी, अंदर कुछ न हो तो किस काम की। શાનદાર તિજોરી હોય તો પણ, અંદર કંઈ ન હોય તો શા કામની.
こんなにお金をためても、死んでしまえばそれまでだ。Konna ni okane o tamete mo, shinde shimaeba sore made da. However much money you save, once you die, that's the end of it. कितना भी पैसा जोड़ लो, मर गए तो सब ख़त्म। ગમે તેટલા પૈસા ભેગા કરો, મરી ગયા તો બધું પૂરું.
どうしてこんなに成績が悪いんだろう。頭が悪いと言ってしまえばそれまでだが。Doushite konna ni seiseki ga warui n darou. Atama ga warui to itte shimaeba sore made da ga. Why are my grades so bad? Of course, if you just say "I'm not clever", that ends the discussion, but… मेरे नंबर इतने ख़राब क्यों आते हैं? "दिमाग़ ही कमज़ोर है" कह दो तो बात वहीं ख़त्म, पर…… મારા ગુણ આટલા ખરાબ કેમ આવે છે? "મગજ જ નબળું છે" કહી દો તો વાત ત્યાં જ પૂરી, પણ……

それ = "that point", まで = "up to": once the condition is met, nothing more can be done. The condition is often V-てしまえば (irreversible) or a passive (社長に反対されればそれまでだ, 練習問題 ④). Don't confuse it with point 1 〜までだ — that one has no ば and means "I simply ~".

【5】 〜までして

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~made shite
Meaning (EN)"going so far as to ~ / even doing something as extreme as ~" (book: 〜のような普通以上のこともして)

Meaning (GU)...સુધ્ધાં કરીને

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
以前は正月のために、主婦が徹夜までして料理を作っていた。Izen wa shougatsu no tame ni, shufu ga tetsuya made shite ryouri o tsukutte ita. In the past, housewives would even stay up all night cooking for New Year. पहले नए साल के लिए गृहिणियाँ रात भर जागकर तक खाना बनाती थीं। પહેલાં નવા વર્ષ માટે ગૃહિણીઓ આખી રાત જાગીને પણ રસોઈ બનાવતી.
留学までして身につけた英語だったが、使わないので忘れてしまった。Ryuugaku made shite mi ni tsuketa eigo datta ga, tsukawanai node wasurete shimatta. I went as far as studying abroad to learn English, but I've forgotten it because I don't use it. अंग्रेज़ी सीखने के लिए विदेश जाकर तक पढ़ाई की, पर इस्तेमाल न करने से भूल गया। અંગ્રેજી શીખવા વિદેશ જઈને પણ ભણ્યો, પણ ઉપયોગ ન કરવાથી ભૂલી ગયો.

までして = まで ("even, as far as") + して. The noun is an unusually big effort. It is neutral or slightly regretful (example 2), while 〜てまで (point 6) more often carries criticism or surprise.

試験は大切だけど、徹夜してまで勉強しようとは思わない。Shiken wa taisetsu da kedo, tetsuya shite made benkyou shiyou to wa omowanai. Exams matter, but I don't intend to go so far as staying up all night to study. परीक्षा ज़रूरी है, लेकिन रात भर जागकर पढ़ने तक की मेरी कोई इच्छा नहीं। પરીક્ષા મહત્ત્વની છે, પણ આખી રાત જાગીને ભણવા સુધીનો મારો વિચાર નથી.

Q9 (⑨)

Q10 (⑩)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today pairs up very similar-looking strings — read the kana around まで／どころ carefully:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜までだ~made da merely did ~ / will simply ~ V-た／V-dict + までだ Minimises the action; no ば. Excuses; calm resolve.
〜ばそれまでだ~ba sore made da if ~, it's all over V-ば + それまでだ Condition that wipes out everything. Resigned remarks.
〜までもない~made mo nai no need to ~ V-dict + までもない Affirmative verb + までもない. Unnecessary actions; 言うまでもない.
〜ないまでも~nai made mo even if not ~, at least V-ない + までも Negative verb + までも; step down to a lesser goal. With せめて／〜たい.
〜までして／〜てまで~made shite / ~te made going as far as ~ N + までして / V-て + まで N vs V connection; てまで often critical/surprised. Extreme effort.
〜どころか~dokoro ka far from ~; even more / the opposite N／plain + どころか Followed by a more extreme or opposite fact. Correcting someone's assumption.
〜どころではない~dokoro de wa nai in no situation for ~ N／V-dict + どころではない The situation is too serious for it. Turning down leisure.
Exam trap 1 — までもない vs ないまでも: same three kana, opposite logic. Affirmative verb + までもない = "no need" (着替えるまでもない). Negative verb + までも = "even if not, at least" (言わないまでも、3割引き). The verb's polarity tells you which one you're looking at (Q1, Q2).
Exam trap 2 — どころか vs どころではない: どころか must be followed by a clause that goes further or the opposite way (歩けるどころかジョギング). どころではない ends the thought: "no time for that". If the blank is followed by じゃない／ではなくなった, the answer is どころ／どころでは (Q6, Q7).
Exam trap 3 — までだ vs ばそれまでだ: 〜たまでだ is an excuse ("I merely did it"); 〜ばそれまでだ is a dead end ("if that happens, it's over"). Look for ば right before the blank.
