第23日 — 「限り」のつく言葉 (“Kagiri” no tsuku kotoba — expressions built on kagiri (limit))

Source
試験に出る文法と表現 N1・N2 (Shiken ni Deru Bunpou to Hyougen, 桐原書店) — printed p.72–73 (PDF pages 72–73)
Grammar points covered
〜限りで〜を限りに〜限り／〜ない限り〜限りでは〜に限って…ない〜に限って〜に限り〜に限らず
Answer key
練習問題 ①–⑫ answers are printed in the 別冊 解答, p.4 (PDF page 170), under 第6章 "23 練習問題" — confirmed, not guessed: ① 限り ② 限りに ③ に限って ④ している限り ⑤ 限りで ⑥ に限らず ⑦ に限って ⑧ に限り ⑨ やめない ⑩ に限って ⑪ に限り ⑫ 限りでは.
Notes
Eight expressions built on 限り "limit". Only 〜を限りに is tagged N1 in the book; the rest are N2-level but are tested at N1 as a look-alike set. Three groups: end point (限りで, を限りに — "with ~ as the last"), scope／condition (限り, ない限り, 限りでは), and に限… forms (に限って "of all times／people", に限り "only", に限らず "not only"). The classic trap is に限って (bad luck happens just then; or "X of all people would never…") vs に限り (a neutral "only ~" in notices). No メモ box on these pages.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜限りで

N2-level · neutral → formal
Reading~kagiri de
Meaning (EN)"as of ~ (and no longer) / with ~ as the last" — marks the end point of something (book: 〜を最後にして)
Meaning (HI)... तक ही (उसके बाद नहीं) / ... को आख़िरी मानकर
Meaning (GU)... સુધી જ (પછી નહીં) / ... ને છેલ્લું ગણીને
Connection (接続)Time N (今日, 今週, 今月, 今回) + 限りで
RegisterNeutral to formal — announcements, notices, polite explanations.
Typical useEnding services, stays, support: 今週限りで終わる, 今月限りで帰国する, 今回限りで終わり.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)N + 限りで今週限りでkonshuu kagiri de
Noun (occasion)N + 限りで今回限りでkonkai kagiri de
Verb／Adjective(added, not in book) Not used — attaches to time nouns.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
半額サービスは、今週限りで終わらせていただきます。Hangaku saabisu wa, konshuu kagiri de owarasete itadakimasu. The half-price offer will end as of this week. आधे दाम वाली सर्विस इसी हफ़्ते तक ही चलेगी, उसके बाद बंद कर दी जाएगी। અડધા ભાવની સર્વિસ આ અઠવાડિયા સુધી જ ચાલશે, પછી બંધ કરવામાં આવશે.
家庭の事情で、今月限りで帰国することになりました。Katei no jijou de, kongetsu kagiri de kikoku suru koto ni narimashita. Due to family circumstances, I will be returning to my country at the end of this month. घरेलू कारणों से, इस महीने के बाद मैं अपने देश लौट रहा हूँ। ઘરના કારણોસર, આ મહિનાના અંતે હું મારા દેશ પાછો જઈ રહ્યો છું.

The noun is always a time span or occasion, and the verb that follows is about stopping or leaving (終わる, 帰国する, やめる). Don't confuse it with 〜限りでは (scope, "as far as …") — the で／では difference changes the meaning completely (⑤ vs ⑫).

【2】 〜を限りに

N1 · 硬 formal/written
Reading~o kagiri ni
Meaning (EN)"with ~ as the last／final (day)" — same as 限りで, but more formal (book: 〜を最後にして). cf. 声を限りに "at the top of one's voice" (book: 出せる限界まで声を出して)
Meaning (HI)... को आख़िरी दिन मानकर / ... से (हमेशा के लिए)  |  声を限りに: पूरा गला फाड़कर
Meaning (GU)... ને છેલ્લો દિવસ ગણીને / ... થી (કાયમ માટે)  |  声を限りに: ગળું ફાડીને
Connection (接続)Time N + を限りに  |  fixed: 声を限りに
Register硬 — formal announcements (retirement, closures), speeches.
Typical use本日を限りに退職する, 今年度を限りに閉鎖する, 今月を限りに引退する.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)N + を限りに本日を限りにhonjitsu o kagiri ni
Noun (time)N + を限りに今年度を限りにkonnendo o kagiri ni
Fixed phrase声 + を限りに声を限りにkoe o kagiri ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
本日を限りに退職することになりました。Honjitsu o kagiri ni taishoku suru koto ni narimashita. Today will be my last day — I am retiring as of today. आज के दिन के साथ ही मैं नौकरी से सेवानिवृत्त हो रहा हूँ। આજના દિવસ સાથે જ હું નોકરીમાંથી નિવૃત્ત થઈ રહ્યો છું.
今年度を限りに、当工場は閉鎖することになりました。Konnendo o kagiri ni, tou koujou wa heisa suru koto ni narimashita. This factory will close at the end of this fiscal year. इस वित्तीय वर्ष के साथ ही यह कारख़ाना बंद हो जाएगा। આ નાણાકીય વર્ષ સાથે જ આ કારખાનું બંધ થઈ જશે.
自国のチームを声を限りに応援した。Jikoku no chiimu o koe o kagiri ni ouen shita. I cheered for my country's team at the top of my voice. मैंने अपने देश की टीम का पूरा गला फाड़कर हौसला बढ़ाया। મેં મારા દેશની ટીમને ગળું ફાડીને પ્રોત્સાહન આપ્યું.

Watch the particle: を限りに vs 限りで. Both mean "with ~ as the last", but they don't mix: ×今月を限りで, ×今月限りに. In ② the printed を before the bracket decides it: 今月を限りに. 声を限りに is a separate idiom ("as loud as one can").

【3】 〜限り／〜ない限り

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kagiri / ~nai kagiri
Meaning (EN)"as long as ~ / while ~ continues" (〜限り) and "unless ~" (〜ない限り) — the result holds while the state continues (book: 〜という状態が続く中では)
Meaning (HI)जब तक ... (रहे), तब तक ...  |  ない限り: जब तक ... न करें
Meaning (GU)જ્યાં સુધી ... (રહે), ત્યાં સુધી ...  |  ない限り: જ્યાં સુધી ... ન કરીએ
Connection (接続)V-dictionary／V-ている + 限り  |  V-ない + 限り  |  N + である + 限り
RegisterNeutral.
Typical useConditions that keep a state going: 部屋にいる限り安全だ; 雨が降らない限り解消されない; 〜をしている限り体はよくならない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary)V-dictionary + 限りいる限りiru kagiri
Verb (ている)V-ている + 限りしている限りshite iru kagiri
Verb (negative)V-ない + 限り歩かない限りarukanai kagiri
Noun／な-adjective(added, not in book) N／な-adj + である + 限り学生である限りgakusei de aru kagiri
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + 限り若い限りwakai kagiri
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
外は嵐でも、この部屋にいる限り安全だ。Soto wa arashi demo, kono heya ni iru kagiri anzen da. Even if there's a storm outside, you're safe as long as you stay in this room. बाहर तूफ़ान हो तब भी, जब तक इस कमरे में हो, तब तक सुरक्षित हो। બહાર તોફાન હોય તો પણ, જ્યાં સુધી આ ઓરડામાં છો, ત્યાં સુધી સલામત છો.
そんな不規則な生活をしている限り、体はよくならないよ。Sonna fukisoku na seikatsu o shite iru kagiri, karada wa yoku naranai yo. As long as you keep living such an irregular life, your health won't get better. जब तक ऐसी बेतरतीब ज़िंदगी जीते रहोगे, तब तक सेहत ठीक नहीं होगी। જ્યાં સુધી આવું અનિયમિત જીવન જીવશો, ત્યાં સુધી તબિયત સારી નહીં થાય.
その町は治安が悪いと言われているが、夜一人で歩かない限り大丈夫だそうだ。Sono machi wa chian ga warui to iwarete iru ga, yoru hitori de arukanai kagiri daijoubu da sou da. That town is said to be unsafe, but apparently it's fine as long as you don't walk alone at night. कहा जाता है कि उस शहर में क़ानून-व्यवस्था ख़राब है, पर सुना है कि जब तक रात में अकेले न घूमो, कोई दिक़्क़त नहीं। કહેવાય છે કે એ શહેરમાં કાયદો-વ્યવસ્થા ખરાબ છે, પણ સાંભળ્યું છે કે જ્યાં સુધી રાત્રે એકલા ન ફરો, ત્યાં સુધી વાંધો નથી.
雨が降らない限り、水不足は解消されない。Ame ga furanai kagiri, mizubusoku wa kaishou sarenai. Unless it rains, the water shortage won't be solved. जब तक बारिश नहीं होती, पानी की कमी दूर नहीं होगी। જ્યાં સુધી વરસાદ નહીં પડે, ત્યાં સુધી પાણીની તંગી દૂર નહીં થાય.

Read 限り as "for as long as this state lasts". With a negative verb (ない限り) it becomes "unless". The 練習問題 test which polarity makes sense: drinking won't stop → illness won't improve (① やめない限り), the boss doesn't quit → nothing changes (⑨), playing games every day → money runs out (④ している限り).

【4】 〜限りでは

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~kagiri de wa
Meaning (EN)"as far as ~ (I know／can see)" — limits a judgement to the range of one's information (book: 〜範囲では)
Meaning (HI)जहाँ तक ... (मुझे पता है / दिखता है)
Meaning (GU)જ્યાં સુધી ... (મને ખબર છે / દેખાય છે)
Connection (接続)Verbs of knowing／seeing／hearing: 知る・見る・聞く・調べる (V-dictionary／V-た／V-ている) + 限りでは
RegisterNeutral; common in reports (調査の結果を見る限りでは).
Typical useHedged judgements: 私の知る限りでは, グラフで見る限りでは, 聞いた限りでは.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (knowing)V-dictionary + 限りでは知る限りではshiru kagiri de wa
Verb (seeing)V-dictionary + 限りでは見る限りではmiru kagiri de wa
Verb (た)(added, not in book) V-た + 限りでは調べた限りではshirabeta kagiri de wa
Noun(added, not in book) N + の + 限りでは (rare: 私の記憶の限りでは)記憶の限りではkioku no kagiri de wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私の知る限りでは、彼はまじめな青年だ。Watashi no shiru kagiri de wa, kare wa majime na seinen da. As far as I know, he is a serious, hard-working young man. जहाँ तक मुझे पता है, वह एक गंभीर और मेहनती नौजवान है। જ્યાં સુધી મને ખબર છે, તે એક ગંભીર અને મહેનતુ યુવાન છે.
グラフで見る限りでは、わが社の売上げは伸びている。Gurafu de miru kagiri de wa, waga sha no uriage wa nobite iru. Judging from the graph, our company's sales are growing. ग्राफ़ से जहाँ तक दिखता है, हमारी कंपनी की बिक्री बढ़ रही है। ગ્રાફ પરથી જ્યાં સુધી દેખાય છે, અમારી કંપનીનું વેચાણ વધી રહ્યું છે.

The verb before 限りでは is almost always one of knowing, seeing, hearing or investigating, and the sentence ends in a judgement (〜だ, 〜と思われる). That is the shape of ⑫ (調査の結果を見る限りでは…と思われる). It is a hedge: "within what I know — there may be more".

【5】 〜に限って…ない

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni kagitte ... nai
Meaning (EN)"~ of all people／things would never …" — speaking about one specific person or thing, the speaker is sure nothing bad could happen (book: 〜について（私の考えを）述べるなら、…（のような悪いこと）はない)
Meaning (HI)और कोई भले ही करे, पर ... तो कभी ... नहीं (करेगा)
Meaning (GU)બીજું કોઈ ભલે કરે, પણ ... તો ક્યારેય ... નહીં (કરે)
Connection (接続)N + に限って + negative (〜はずはない, 〜ことはない, 〜ないと思う)
RegisterNeutral; expresses trust in a person or product.
Typical useあの学生に限って…はずはない, うちの子に限って, わが社の製品に限って….
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (person)N + に限って…ないあの学生に限ってano gakusei ni kagitte
Noun (thing)N + に限って…ない製品に限ってseihin ni kagitte
Verb／Adjective(added, not in book) Not used — attaches to nouns.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの学生に限って、カンニングなどするはずはない。Ano gakusei ni kagitte, kanningu nado suru hazu wa nai. That student, of all people, would never cheat on a test. और कोई भले करे, पर वह छात्र तो नक़ल कभी नहीं कर सकता। બીજું કોઈ ભલે કરે, પણ એ વિદ્યાર્થી તો ક્યારેય ચોરી ન કરે.
わが社の製品に限って、そんなに簡単に壊れることはないと思います。Waga sha no seihin ni kagitte, sonna ni kantan ni kowareru koto wa nai to omoimasu. Our company's products, of all things, would never break so easily, I believe. मुझे लगता है कि हमारी कंपनी के प्रोडक्ट तो इतनी आसानी से कभी नहीं टूट सकते। મને લાગે છે કે અમારી કંપનીનાં ઉત્પાદનો તો આટલી સહેલાઈથી ક્યારેય ન તૂટે.

This use always ends in a negative and expresses the speaker's trust: "anyone else maybe, but not him". ③ (彼女に限って浮気などしない) and ⑩ (良心的なあの店に限ってこんな悪い品物は売らないはず) are this pattern. Don't mistake it for を限りに ("as the last") just because both contain 限.

【6】 〜に限って

N2-level · neutral register
Reading~ni kagitte
Meaning (EN)"just when ~ / of all times, ~" — something inconvenient happens as if timed to that moment (book: 〜に合わせるように…（不都合なことが起きる）)
Meaning (HI)ठीक उसी वक़्त जब ... (तभी कोई परेशानी) / ... के वक़्त ही
Meaning (GU)બરાબર ત્યારે જ જ્યારે ... (ત્યારે જ કોઈ મુશ્કેલી) / ... વખતે જ
Connection (接続)N (〜とき, 〜日, 〜人) + に限って
RegisterNeutral; a complaint about bad luck／irony.
Typical use留守のときに限って, 急いでいるときに限って, 気をつけている人に限って….
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)〜とき + に限って留守のときに限ってrusu no toki ni kagitte
Verb + ときV-ている + とき + に限って急いでいるときに限ってisoide iru toki ni kagitte
Verb + 人V-ている + 人 + に限って気をつけている人に限ってki o tsukete iru hito ni kagitte
Noun (day)(added, not in book) N + に限って大事な日に限ってdaiji na hi ni kagitte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
留守のときに限って、荷物が届く。Rusu no toki ni kagitte, nimotsu ga todoku. Packages always arrive just when I'm out. जब मैं घर पर नहीं होता, ठीक तभी पार्सल आ जाता है। જ્યારે હું ઘરે ન હોઉં, બરાબર ત્યારે જ પાર્સલ આવે છે.
急いでいるときに限って、タクシーがつかまらない。Isoide iru toki ni kagitte, takushii ga tsukamaranai. Just when you're in a hurry, you can never get a taxi. जब जल्दी होती है, ठीक तभी टैक्सी नहीं मिलती। જ્યારે ઉતાવળ હોય, બરાબર ત્યારે જ ટેક્સી નથી મળતી.
健康に気をつけている人に限って、病気になったりするものだ。Kenkou ni ki o tsukete iru hito ni kagitte, byouki ni nattari suru mono da. Ironically, it's the very people who take care of their health who tend to get sick. अक्सर ऐसा होता है कि जो लोग सेहत का सबसे ज़्यादा ध्यान रखते हैं, वही बीमार पड़ जाते हैं। ઘણી વાર એવું બને છે કે જે લોકો તબિયતની સૌથી વધુ કાળજી રાખે છે, એ જ બીમાર પડે છે.

The second half is always something unwelcome (a missed delivery, no taxi, illness), and the tone is "why does it always happen then?". That is why ⑦ (朝寝坊したときに限って…電車が遅れる) takes に限って, not the neutral に限り. Compare 【5】: same form, but there the speaker says something bad would not happen.

【7】 〜に限り

N2-level · formal (notices)

Meaning (EN)"only ~ / limited to ~" — a neutral restriction (book: 〜だけ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Eight look-alikes, one kanji. Sort them by what comes before (time noun, verb, person) and what comes after (stopping, judgement, trust, bad luck, benefit, も):

〜に限らず~ni kagirazu not only N + に限らず Extends beyond; second half has も／でも. Q6.
Exam trap 1 — に限って vs に限り: に限って carries emotion (bad luck, or "he would never"); に限り is a dry "only" for rules and offers. If the sentence grants a benefit or exception (割引, 免除, 認めます, プレゼント), it is に限り (Q8, Q11); if something annoying happens at the worst time, に限って (Q7).
Exam trap 2 — the particle decides 限りで vs を限りに: 今月を限りに, 今月限りで — never mixed (Q2, Q5).
Exam trap 3 — 限り vs 限りでは: 限り = a continuing condition ("as long as"), 限りでは = scope of knowledge ("as far as"). If the verb is 知る／見る／聞く／調べる and the sentence ends in a judgement, choose 限りでは (Q1 vs Q12).
Exam trap 4 — polarity with 限り: work out the logic: している限り (keeps causing it) vs しない限り (unless). The wrong polarity always produces nonsense (Q4, Q9).
