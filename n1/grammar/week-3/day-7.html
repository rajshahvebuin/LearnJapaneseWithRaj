問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

（　）店を続ける気はないです。(___) mise o tsuzukeru ki wa nai desu.

Full sentence: 損をしてまでも店を続ける気はないです。Son o shite made mo mise o tsuzukeru ki wa nai desu.
EN: I have no intention of continuing to run the store even at the cost of taking a loss.
HI: नुक़सान उठाकर भी दुकान चलाने का कोई इरादा नहीं है।
GU: નુકસાન વેઠીને પણ દુકાન ચલાવવાનો કોઈ ઇરાદો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1損とはいえson towa iealthough it's a loss (wrong — doesn't match the "willing to go this far" sense)
2損といえどもson to ie domoeven if it's a loss (wrong register/fit for this structure)
3損をするまでもson o suru made mo(wrong — までも needs the て-form, not する directly)
4損をしてまでもson o shite made moeven to the point of taking a loss

Why: Week 3 Day 1's てまでも needs the て-form (して) before までも, describing the drastic length (a financial loss) one is unwilling to go to.

Q2

普段着はシンプルで（　）。Fudangi wa shinpuru de (___).

Full sentence: 普段着はシンプルで丈夫なのに限る。Fudangi wa shinpuru de joubu na no ni kagiru.
EN: For everyday clothes, simple and durable is best.
HI: रोज़मर्रा के कपड़े सादे और मज़बूत हों तो सबसे अच्छा है।
GU: રોજિંદા કપડાં સાદા અને મજબૂત હોય તો સૌથી સારું છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1丈夫なのに限るjoubu na no ni kagirunothing beats being durable
2丈夫な限りだjoubu na kagiri da(wrong — 限りだ is for emotional adjectives, not "durable")
3丈夫に限らないjoubu ni kagiranainot limited to being durable (wrong sense entirely)
4丈夫に限ったことだjoubu ni kagitta koto da(wrong — this isn't a "not unique to X" claim)

Why: Week 3 Day 2's に限る recommends durability as simply the best quality for everyday clothes.

Q3

病気（　）、いつまでも仕事を休んではいられない。Byouki (___), itsu made mo shigoto o yasunde wa irarenai.

Full sentence: 病気とはいえ、いつまでも仕事を休んではいられない。Byouki towa ie, itsu made mo shigoto o yasunde wa irarenai.
EN: Even though I'm sick, I can't keep taking time off work forever.
HI: बीमार होने के बावजूद, हमेशा के लिए काम से छुट्टी नहीं ले सकता/सकती।
GU: બીમાર હોવા છતાં, હંમેશા માટે કામમાંથી રજા લઈ ન શકાય.
#JapaneseEnglish
1といえずto iezu(not a valid form)
2というまでもなくto iu made mo nakuneedless to say (wrong — doesn't fit as a concession)
3と思いきやto omoikiyathought that... (wrong — no reversed expectation here)
4とはいえtowa iealthough, even so

Why: Week 3 Day 3's とはいえ concedes being sick while still asserting the obligation to work holds.

Q4

その商品がヒットしたの（　）、次々と類似品が発売された。Sono shouhin ga hitto shita no (___), tsugitsugi to ruijihin ga hatsubai sareta.

Full sentence: その商品がヒットしたのを皮切りに、次々と類似品が発売された。Sono shouhin ga hitto shita no o kawakiri ni, tsugitsugi to ruijihin ga hatsubai sareta.
EN: Starting with that product's hit, similar products were released one after another.
HI: उस उत्पाद के हिट होने के बाद, एक के बाद एक जैसे उत्पाद बाज़ार में आ गए।
GU: તે પ્રોડક્ટ હિટ થયા પછી, એક પછી એક એવી પ્રોડક્ટ બજારમાં આવી ગઈ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1が最後ga saigoonce ~, inevitably (wrong — no unstoppable habit here)
2を皮切りにo kawakiri nistarting with (then a series expands)
3や否やya ina yathe moment (wrong — this needs a plain verb form, not a noun-marked event)
4を思いきやo omoikiya(not a valid form)

Why: A hit product triggering a growing series of imitators is exactly Week 3 Day 4's を皮切りに.

Q5

近くまで来ましたので、ご挨拶（　）お伺いしました。Chikaku made kimashita node, go-aisatsu (___) oukagai shimashita.

Full sentence: 近くまで来ましたので、ご挨拶かたがたお伺いしました。Chikaku made kimashita node, go-aisatsu katagata oukagai shimashita.
EN: Since I was nearby, I came to visit, combined with paying my respects.
HI: पास ही आया/आई था/थी, तो अभिवादन के साथ आपसे मिलने आया/आई।
GU: નજીકમાં જ આવ્યો/આવી હતો/હતી, તો અભિવાદનની સાથે તમને મળવા આવ્યો/આવી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かたがたkatagatacombined with (formal social visit)
2かたわらkatawarawhile also (wrong — no two ongoing life-roles)
3ながらnagarawhile (wrong — too casual/simultaneous for this greeting)
4ながらもnagara mo(wrong — an unrelated concessive pattern)

Why: Week 3 Day 6's かたがた is the standard formal-visit phrase combining a greeting with the act of dropping by.

Q6

明日（　）、退職いたします。Ashita (___), taishoku itashimasu.

Full sentence: 明日をもって、退職いたします。Ashita o motte, taishoku itashimasu.
EN: As of tomorrow, I will retire.
HI: कल से, मैं इस्तीफ़ा दे रहा/रही हूँ।
GU: આવતીકાલથી, હું રાજીનામું આપી રહ્યો/રહી છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1をもってo motteas of (formal deadline marker)
2というものto iu monofor the entire duration of (wrong — this is a single cutoff point, not a duration)
3が最後ga saigoonce ~, inevitably (wrong sense)
4に限りni kagirirestricted to (wrong — unrelated meaning)

Why: Week 3 Day 4's をもって② formally marks tomorrow as the cutoff date for retirement — the same function as を限りに.

Q7

デパートが開店する（　）、主婦たちが特売場に押し寄せた。Depaato ga kaiten suru (___), shufutachi ga tokubaijou ni oshiyoseta.

Full sentence: デパートが開店するが早いか、主婦たちが特売場に押し寄せた。Depaato ga kaiten suru ga hayai ka, shufutachi ga tokubaijou ni oshiyoseta.
EN: No sooner had the department store opened than housewives rushed to the sale floor.
HI: डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर खुलते ही, गृहणियाँ सेल वाले हिस्से की ओर दौड़ पड़ीं।
GU: ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર ખુલતાં જ, ગૃહિણીઓ સેલ વિભાગ તરફ દોડી ગઈ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かと思いきやka to omoikiyathought that... (wrong — no reversed expectation)
2までもなくmade mo nakuwithout even needing to (wrong sense)
3が早いかga hayai kano sooner than
4そばからsoba karaimmediately undone (wrong — opening isn't "undone," just followed by a rush)

Why: A quick, almost simultaneous reaction (housewives rushing in) to a trigger (the store opening) is Week 3 Day 5's が早いか.

Q8

（　）と思っていたことだったが、記録しておいてよかった。(___) to omotte ita koto datta ga, kiroku shite oite yokatta.

Full sentence: 書くまでもないと思っていたことだったが、記録しておいてよかった。Kaku made mo nai to omotte ita koto datta ga, kiroku shite oite yokatta.
EN: I had thought there was no need to even write it down, but it was good that I recorded it.
HI: लगा था कि लिखने की ज़रूरत भी नहीं, लेकिन लिख लिया वह अच्छा हुआ।
GU: લાગ્યું હતું કે લખવાની જરૂર પણ નથી, પણ લખી લીધું તે સારું થયું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1書くまでのことだkaku made no koto da(wrong — downplays something already done, not a future need)
2書くまでもないkaku made mo naino need to even write
3書かないまでだkakanai made da(not a valid combination)
4書かないまでもないkakanai made mo nai(not a valid combination)

Why: Week 3 Day 1's までもない dismisses writing as unnecessary — until the outcome (glad it was recorded) proved that assumption wrong.

Q9

語彙を勉強しているが、覚えたと思った（　）忘れてしまう。Goi o benkyou shite iru ga, oboeta to omotta (___) wasurete shimau.

Full sentence: 語彙を勉強しているが、覚えたと思ったそばから忘れてしまう。Goi o benkyou shite iru ga, oboeta to omotta soba kara wasurete shimau.
EN: I'm studying vocabulary, but no sooner do I think I've memorized it than I forget it.
HI: शब्दावली पढ़ रहा/रही हूँ, लेकिन याद हुआ लगते ही भूल जाता/जाती हूँ।
GU: શબ્દભંડોળ ભણી રહ્યો/રહી છું, પણ યાદ થયું લાગે કે તરત ભૂલી જાઉં છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かたがたkatagatacombined with (wrong — not a formal visit)
2かたわらkatawarawhile also (wrong — not two ongoing roles)
3そばからsoba karano sooner than (immediately undone)
4がてらgaterawhile doing (wrong — not a two-purpose trip)

Why: Memorization being instantly undone by forgetting is exactly Week 3 Day 6's そばから.

Q10

この3週間（　）、ろくに寝ていない。Kono san-shuukan (___), roku ni nete inai.

Full sentence: この3週間というもの、ろくに寝ていない。Kono san-shuukan to iu mono, roku ni nete inai.
EN: For these entire three weeks, I haven't slept properly.
HI: इन तीन हफ़्तों में, ठीक से सोया/सोई ही नहीं।
GU: આ ત્રણ અઠવાડિયામાં, બરાબર ઊંઘ્યો/ઊંઘી જ નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1の限りno kagiri(not a valid combination)
2といえどもto ie domoeven ~ (wrong — a concession, not a duration marker)
3かと思いきやka to omoikiyathought that... (wrong — no reversed expectation)
4というものto iu monofor the entire duration of

Why: An unbroken continuous state (poor sleep) across a named period is Week 3 Day 4's というもの.

Q11

とにかくやってみよう。できなければ、あきらめる（　）。Tonikaku yatte miyou. Dekinakereba, akirameru (___).

Full sentence: とにかくやってみよう。できなければ、あきらめるまでのことだ。Tonikaku yatte miyou. Dekinakereba, akirameru made no koto da.
EN: Anyway, let's try it. If it doesn't work, that just means giving up, nothing more.
HI: बहरहाल, कोशिश करते हैं। अगर न हो पाए, तो बस छोड़ देना ही काफ़ी है।
GU: ગમે તે થાય, પ્રયત્ન કરીએ. જો ન થાય, તો બસ છોડી દેવું જ પૂરતું છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1わけだwake da(unrelated pattern, doesn't fit here)
2わけにはいかないwake ni wa ikanaicannot possibly (wrong sense entirely)
3までもないmade mo naino need to even (wrong — giving up isn't being dismissed as unnecessary)
4までのことだmade no koto dathat's all there is to it

Why: Week 3 Day 1's まで（のこと）だ downplays giving up as simply the natural, unremarkable consequence if the attempt fails.

Q12

彼が美人で若いお嫁さんをもらったとは、うらやましい（　）。Kare ga bijin de wakai oyomesan o moratta towa, urayamashii (___).

Full sentence: 彼が美人で若いお嫁さんをもらったとは、うらやましい限りだ。Kare ga bijin de wakai oyomesan o moratta towa, urayamashii kagiri da.
EN: The fact that he got a beautiful young wife is envious in the extreme.
HI: उसे इतनी सुंदर और जवान पत्नी मिली, यह बेहद ईर्ष्या की बात है।
GU: તેને આટલી સુંદર અને યુવાન પત્ની મળી, આ ખૂબ જ ઈર્ષ્યાની વાત છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1限りだkagiri daextremely (envious)
2限りではないkagiri de wa nai(wrong — negates the intensity, opposite of intended meaning)
3に限るni kagiruis the best (wrong — not a recommendation)
4に限らないni kagiranainot limited to (wrong sense entirely)

Why: Week 3 Day 2's 限りだ intensifies envy to its peak, matching とは's exclamatory opening.

Q13

遊びがてら（　）。Asobi gatera (___).

Full sentence: 遊びがてらぜひいらっしゃってください。Asobi gatera zehi irasshatte kudasai.
EN: Please do come by whenever you're out and about.
HI: घूमते-फिरते ज़रूर आ जाइए।
GU: ફરતાં ફરતાં જરૂર આવજો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1よく学んでくださいyoku manande kudasaiplease study well (wrong tone — doesn't match a casual "drop by" invitation)
2看護師の勉強をしていますkangoshi no benkyou o shite imasu(unrelated statement, not an invitation)
3ぜひいらっしゃってくださいzehi irasshatte kudasaiplease do come by
4お詫びに伺いますowabi ni ukagaimasu(unrelated — this would need かたがた, not がてら)

Why: 遊びがてら sets up a casual invitation (matching the book's own example, 遊びがてらお立ち寄りください) — only option 3 continues naturally as an invitation to visit.

Q14

彼女は子どもを育てるかたわら、（　）。Kanojo wa kodomo o sodateru katawara, (___).

Full sentence: 彼女は子どもを育てるかたわら、家で料理教室も開いている。Kanojo wa kodomo o sodateru katawara, ie de ryouri kyoushitsu mo hiraite iru.
EN: While raising her children, she also runs a cooking class at home.
HI: बच्चों को पालने के साथ-साथ, वह घर पर खाना पकाने की कक्षा भी चलाती है।
GU: બાળકોને ઉછેરવાની સાથે સાથે, તે ઘરે રસોઈનો ક્લાસ પણ ચલાવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1時間が足りなくて悩んでいるjikan ga tarinakute nayande iru(a complaint, not a second ongoing role)
2家で料理教室も開いているie de ryouri kyoushitsu mo hiraite irualso runs a cooking class at home
3家事が得意で何をするのも早いkaji ga tokui de nani o suru no mo hayai(a personality trait, not a second role)
4将来は教師の資格を生かしたいそうだshourai wa kyoushi no shikaku o ikashitai sou da(a future hope, not a current ongoing activity)

Why: かたわら needs a genuinely separate ongoing secondary activity, matching the pattern's own example structure (running a shop alongside farming) — only option 2 names a real second occupation happening now.

Q15

今日の試合に負けたといえども、（　）。Kyou no shiai ni maketa to ie domo, (___).

Full sentence: 今日の試合に負けたといえども、相手チームとの力の差はほとんどなかった。Kyou no shiai ni maketa to ie domo, aite chiimu to no chikara no sa wa hotondo nakatta.
EN: Even though we lost today's match, there was almost no gap in strength between us and the other team.
HI: आज का मैच हारने के बावजूद, विरोधी टीम से ताक़त का फ़र्क़ लगभग नहीं था।
GU: આજની મેચ હાર્યા હોવા છતાં, વિરોધી ટીમ સાથે તાકાતનો ફરક લગભગ નહોતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1勝つわけがないと思っていたkatsu wake ga nai to omotte ita(contradicts といえども's concessive redeeming point)
2勝てなかったのは仕方がなかったkatenakatta no wa shikata ga nakatta(plausible, but doesn't redeem/qualify the loss as strongly)
3相手チームとの力の差はほとんどなかったaite chiimu to no chikara no sa wa hotondo nakattathe gap in strength was almost nonexistent
4相手チームほどの実力はなかったaite chiimu hodo no jitsuryoku wa nakatta(contradicts the redeeming point — admits being weaker)

Why: といえども concedes the loss while still asserting something that softens/redeems it — option 3 asserts the teams were nearly evenly matched despite the loss, the classic "even though X, still Y holds" shape.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

息子は頑固でいったん＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 耳を傾けない。Musuko wa ganko de ittan ___ ★ ___ mimi o katamukenai.

Tiles: 1 だれの言うこと (dare no iu koto)　2 最後 (saigo)　3 にも (ni mo)　4 言い出したら (ii dashitara)

4 言い出したら→2 最後→★ 1 だれの言うこと→3 にも

息子は頑固でいったん言い出したら最後、だれの言うことにも耳を傾けない。Musuko wa ganko de ittan ii dashitara saigo, dare no iu koto ni mo mimi o katamukenai.

EN: My son is stubborn — once he says something, that's it, he won't listen to anyone.
HI: मेरा बेटा ज़िद्दी है — एक बार कुछ कह दे तो, फिर किसी की भी नहीं सुनता।
GU: મારો દીકરો હઠીલો છે — એક વાર કંઈક કહી દે તો, પછી કોઈની પણ સાંભળતો નથી.

Why: Built on Week 3 Day 5's たら最後 — once he states his opinion, there's no changing his mind, an unstoppable trait.

Q17

友人が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ とは、夢にも思わなかった。Yuujin ga ___ ★ ___ towa, yume ni mo omowanakatta.

Tiles: 1 連絡を受けたが (renraku o uketa ga)　2 との (to no)　3 そんなに悪かった (sonna ni warukatta)　4 入院した (nyuuin shita)

4 入院した→2 との→★ 1 連絡を受けたが→3 そんなに悪かった

友人が入院したとの連絡を受けたが、そんなに悪かったとは、夢にも思わなかった。Yuujin ga nyuuin shita to no renraku o uketa ga, sonna ni warukatta towa, yume ni mo omowanakatta.

EN: I received word that my friend was hospitalized, but I never dreamed it was that serious.
HI: दोस्त के भर्ती होने की ख़बर मिली, लेकिन इतना गंभीर होगा, यह सपने में भी नहीं सोचा था।
GU: મિત્રના દવાખાનામાં દાખલ થવાની ખબર મળી, પણ આટલું ગંભીર હશે, એ સ્વપ્નમાં પણ નહોતું વિચાર્યું.

Why: Built on Week 3 Day 3's とのN (a report/message) followed by とは's exclamatory shock at how serious it turned out to be.

Q18

彼は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 乗り換えの電車に間に合わなかったようだ。Kare wa ___ ★ ___ norikae no densha ni maniawanakatta you da.

Tiles: 1 やいなや (ya ina ya)　2 電車を降りる (densha o oriru)　3 どうやら (douyara)　4 走り出したが (hashiridashita ga)

2 電車を降りる→1 やいなや→★ 4 走り出したが→3 どうやら
