4日目 — 努力をもって (Doryoku o motte)

Source

Grammar points covered
〜を皮切りに（して）／〜を皮切りとして〜をもって（①手段 ②期限）（この/ここ）N というもの／〜てからというもの
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.55 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
〜をもって does double duty this day (a means/method marker, and — reused from Day 2's 〜を限りに — a formal deadline marker), both tagged 硬 formal/written. Keep its two senses straight; see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜を皮切りに（して）／〜を皮切りとして

neutral register
Reading~o kawakiri ni (shite) / ~o kawakiri to shite
Meaning (EN)"starting with ~ (and then expanding/continuing further)" — marks the starting point of a series that grows or spreads afterward
Meaning (HI)...से शुरुआत करके (और फिर आगे बढ़ते हुए)
Meaning (GU)...થી શરૂઆત કરીને (અને પછી આગળ વધીને)
Connection (接続)N + を皮切りに（して）／を皮切りとして
RegisterNeutral
Typical useNaming the first location/event in a series that then expands (a tour's first city, a business's first branch).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今度の演奏会は、東京を皮切りに、全国10ヵ所で行われる。Kondo no ensoukai wa, Toukyou o kawakiri ni, zenkoku jukka-sho de okonawareru. This round of concerts will take place in ten different locations, starting in Tokyo. इस बार का संगीत समारोह टोक्यो से शुरू होकर, पूरे देश में 10 जगहों पर होगा। આ વખતનો સંગીત સમારોહ ટોક્યોથી શરૂ થઈને, દેશભરમાં 10 સ્થળે થશે.
彼の店は大阪で成功したのを皮切りに、各地に次々と出店していずれも成功を収めた。Kare no mise wa Oosaka de seikou shita no o kawakiri ni, kakuchi ni tsugitsugi to shutten shite izure mo seikou o osameta. His store first succeeded in Osaka and later in other parts of the country. उसकी दुकान ने ओसाका में सफलता पाकर, धीरे-धीरे हर जगह शाखाएँ खोली और सभी में सफलता मिली। તેની દુકાને ઓસાકામાં સફળતા મેળવીને, ધીમે ધીમે બધે શાખાઓ ખોલી અને બધીમાં સફળતા મળી.

皮切り literally means "the first cut" (as in lancing a boil) — a vivid image for "the very first instance that then opens the floodgates" for everything that follows.

【2】 〜をもって

硬 formal/written
Reading~o motte
Meaning (EN)"① by means of ~ (N = a method/means) / ② as of ~ (N = a deadline/time, = を限りに)" — two related uses under one form
Meaning (HI)① ...के ज़रिए/माध्यम से (N=साधन) / ② ...से (एक निश्चित समय-सीमा, तय)
Meaning (GU)① ...ના માધ્યમથી (N=સાધન) / ② ...થી (એક નિશ્ચિત સમય-મર્યાદા, નક્કી)
Connection (接続)① N（手段／方法） + をもって　② N〈期限〉 + をもって（もちまして）
Register硬 — formal, mostly written/spoken formally
Typical use① Naming the tool/means used to accomplish something. ② Announcing a formal cutoff — speeches, sales, resignations.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
熱意と努力をもって、仕事をしてください。Netsui to doryoku o motte, shigoto o shite kudasai. You are expected to work hard and enthusiastically. उत्साह और मेहनत के साथ काम करें। ઉત્સાહ અને મહેનતથી કામ કરો.
彼の実力をもってすれば、成功するだろう。Kare no jitsuryoku o motte sureba, seikou suru darou. He is sure to succeed because he is very capable. उसकी क़ाबिलियत को देखते हुए, वह ज़रूर सफल होगा। તેની ક્ષમતાને જોતાં, તે ચોક્કસ સફળ થશે.
地震の恐ろしさを身をもって経験した。Jishin no osoroshisa o mi o motte keiken shita. I experienced the fear of being in an earthquake firsthand. भूकंप की भयावहता को ख़ुद अपने अनुभव से जाना। ધરતીકંપની ભયાનકતાનો પોતાના અનુભવથી અનુભવ કર્યો.
本日をもって、セールは終了となります。Honjitsu o motte, seeru wa shuuryou to narimasu. The sale will finish today. आज से यह सेल समाप्त हो जाएगी। આજથી આ સેલ સમાપ્ત થશે.
10月31日をもちまして、退職いたしました。Juugatsu sanjuuichi-nichi o mochimashite, taishoku itashimashita. I resigned as of October 31. 31 अक्टूबर से मैंने इस्तीफ़ा दे दिया। 31 ઓક્ટોબરથી મેં રાજીનામું આપ્યું.

「これをもって、始まりのあいさつとさせていただきます」("This brings my opening speech to an end") is a fixed formal-ceremony phrase worth memorizing whole. もちまして is the extra-polite/humble variant of もって, common in formal announcements (10月31日をもちまして).

【3】 （この/ここ）N というもの／〜てからというもの

neutral register
Reading(kono/koko) N to iu mono / ~te kara to iu mono
Meaning (EN)"for the entire duration of ~ (emphasizing how long/continuously something has held true)" — stresses the full length of a period during which a state has persisted without exception
Meaning (HI)पूरे...के दौरान (लगातार, बिना रुके, एक हालत बनी रहना)
Meaning (GU)સંપૂર્ણ...દરમિયાન (લગાતાર, વિરામ વગર, એક સ્થિતિ રહેવી)
Connection (接続)（この／ここ） N（期間） + というもの; Vてから + というもの
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizing that a state has held true continuously across an entire named period, no exceptions.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この1ヵ月というもの、シャワーを浴びるだけでお風呂に入っていない。Kono ikkagetsu to iu mono, shawaa o abiru dake de ofuro ni haitte inai. For the last month, I have only had showers, and no baths. इस पूरे एक महीने में, सिर्फ़ शावर लिया है, कभी नहाने के टब में नहीं गया। આ સંપૂર્ણ એક મહિનામાં, ફક્ત શાવર લીધો છે, ક્યારેય નહાવાના ટબમાં નથી ગયો.
結婚してからというもの、映画館で映画を見ていない。Kekkon shite kara to iu mono, eigakan de eiga o mite inai. I haven't gone to a movie theater since I got married. शादी के बाद से, कभी सिनेमा हॉल में फ़िल्म नहीं देखी। લગ્ન પછીથી, ક્યારેય સિનેમા હોલમાં ફિલ્મ નથી જોઈ.

Notes (れい): ここ3日間というもの, ここしばらくというもの. Always about an unbroken continuous state over the named period — not a one-off event.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.53. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.55.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

あの先生は僕が何を尋ねても、いつも誠意（　）答えてくれる。Ano sensei wa boku ga nani o tazunete mo, itsumo seii (___) kotaete kureru.

Full sentence: あの先生は僕が何を尋ねても、いつも誠意をもって答えてくれる。Ano sensei wa boku ga nani o tazunete mo, itsumo seii o motte kotaete kureru.
EN: Whatever I ask that teacher, they always answer sincerely.
HI: मैं जो भी पूछूँ, वह शिक्षक हमेशा सच्चाई से जवाब देते हैं।
GU: હું જે પણ પૂછું, તે શિક્ષક હંમેશા સચ્ચાઈથી જવાબ આપે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aをもってo motteby means of, withके साथ, सेસાથે, થી
bをもちましてo mochimashite(too formal — an over-elevated register for everyday teacher-student description)(यहाँ अनुपयुक्त औपचारिकता)(અહીં અયોગ્ય ઔપચારિકતા)

Why: 誠意をもって ("with sincerity," Point 2①) describes a manner/means of answering; もちまして is the extra-humble variant reserved for formal ceremony/announcement contexts, mismatched here.

Q2

私の父は定年退職（　）、一日中テレビばかり見ている。Watashi no chichi wa teinen taishoku (___), ichinichijuu terebi bakari mite iru.

Full sentence: 私の父は定年退職してからというもの、一日中テレビばかり見ている。Watashi no chichi wa teinen taishoku shite kara to iu mono, ichinichijuu terebi bakari mite iru.
EN: Ever since my father retired, all he does all day is watch TV.
HI: मेरे पापा के रिटायर होने के बाद से, वे दिन भर सिर्फ़ टीवी देखते रहते हैं।
GU: મારા પપ્પા રિટાયર થયા પછીથી, તેઓ દિવસભર માત્ર ટીવી જુએ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aを皮切りにo kawakiri nistarting with (wrong — retirement doesn't "expand," it's a single continuous state after)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bしてからというものshite kara to iu monoever since (continuous state)के बाद से (लगातार स्थिति)પછીથી (સતત સ્થિતિ)

Why: A continuous unbroken habit since retirement is Point 3's てからというもの; を皮切りに is for a starting point that then expands into a growing series, which doesn't fit "watching TV all day" as a static continuous state.

Q3

当選者の発表は、商品の発送（　）代えさせていただきます。Tousensha no happyou wa, shouhin no hassou (___) kaesasete itadakimasu.

Full sentence: 当選者の発表は、商品の発送をもって代えさせていただきます。Tousensha no happyou wa, shouhin no hassou o motte kaesasete itadakimasu.
EN: The announcement of winners will be replaced by the shipping of the product (i.e., only winners will receive a shipment; that itself serves as notification).
HI: विजेताओं की घोषणा, उत्पाद भेजने के ज़रिए ही की जाएगी।
GU: વિજેતાઓની ઘોષણા, પ્રોડક્ટ મોકલવા દ્વારા જ કરવામાં આવશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aをもってo motteby means of (product shipment serves as the notification)के ज़रिएદ્વારા
bを皮切りにo kawakiri nistarting with (wrong — no expanding series here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This is Point 2①'s "means" sense — shipping the product itself substitutes for a formal announcement; を皮切りに would need a growing series of events, not a one-time substitution.

Q4

一人暮らしを始めてから（　）、まともな食事をしたことがない。Hitorigurashi o hajimete kara (___), matomo na shokuji o shita koto ga nai.

Full sentence: 一人暮らしを始めてからというもの、まともな食事をしたことがない。Hitorigurashi o hajimete kara to iu mono, matomo na shokuji o shita koto ga nai.
EN: Ever since I started living alone, I haven't had a single proper meal.
HI: अकेले रहना शुरू करने के बाद से, कभी ठीक से खाना नहीं खाया।
GU: એકલા રહેવાનું શરૂ કર્યા પછીથી, ક્યારેય બરાબર ખાવાનું નથી ખાધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aというものto iu monoever since (continuous)के बाद से (लगातार)પછીથી (સતત)
bといえどもto ie domoeven ~ (wrong — a concession, not a duration marker)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A continuous state since a starting point is Point 3's てからというもの; といえども (Day 3's pattern) is an unrelated concession.

Q5

昨日の会議では、彼の発言（　）反対意見が次々と出た。Kinou no kaigi de wa, kare no hatsugen (___) hantai iken ga tsugitsugi to deta.

Full sentence: 昨日の会議では、彼の発言を皮切りに反対意見が次々と出た。Kinou no kaigi de wa, kare no hatsugen o kawakiri ni hantai iken ga tsugitsugi to deta.
EN: At yesterday's meeting, starting with his remark, objections came pouring in one after another.
HI: कल की मीटिंग में, उसकी टिप्पणी से शुरू होकर, विरोध के विचार एक के बाद एक आते गए।
GU: ગઈકાલની મીટિંગમાં, તેની ટિપ્પણીથી શરૂ થઈને, વિરોધના વિચારો એક પછી એક આવ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aを皮切りにo kawakiri nistarting with (and then a series follows)से शुरू होकरથી શરૂ થઈને
bというものto iu monoever since (wrong — this is a single event triggering a series, not a continuous duration)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: One remark triggering a growing series of objections is exactly Point 1's を皮切りに; というもの describes an unbroken continuous state, not a triggering event.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

あの歌手は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ いるようだ。Ano kashu wa ___ iru you da.

Tiles: 1 人気が出て (ninki ga dete)　2 大きくなって (ookiku natte)　3 からというもの (kara to iu mono)　4 態度が (taido ga)

1 人気が出て→3 からというもの→4 態度が→2 大きくなって

あの歌手は人気が出てからというもの態度が大きくなっているようだ。Ano kashu wa ninki ga dete kara to iu mono taido ga ookiku natte iru you da.

EN: That singer's attitude seems to have gotten arrogant ever since they became popular.
HI: वह गायक मशहूर होने के बाद से, लगता है घमंडी हो गया है।
GU: તે ગાયક પ્રખ્યાત થયા પછીથી, લાગે છે અભિમાની થઈ ગયો છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's てからというもの — a continuous change in attitude that has persisted ever since gaining popularity.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ も難しくないだろう。___ mo muzukashiku nai darou.

Tiles: 1 その開発 (sono kaihatsu)　2 最新技術 (saishin gijutsu)　3 をもって (o motte)　4 すれば (sureba)

2 最新技術→3 をもって→4 すれば→1 その開発

最新技術をもってすればその開発も難しくないだろう。Saishin gijutsu o motte sureba sono kaihatsu mo muzukashiku nai darou.

EN: With the latest technology, that development wouldn't be difficult either.
HI: नवीनतम तकनीक के साथ, वह विकास भी मुश्किल नहीं होगा।
GU: નવીનતમ ટેકનોલોજી સાથે, તે વિકાસ પણ મુશ્કેલ નહીં હોય.

Why: Built on Point 2①'s をもってすれば — the latest technology is named as the means by which the development becomes feasible.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

をもって's two senses are easy to conflate with を皮切りに and this week's Day 2 を限りに — compare all three:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜を皮切りに~o kawakiri ni Starting with ~ (then expanding) N + を皮切りに A single starting event that then grows into a series. "Starting in Tokyo, concerts spread nationwide."
〜をもって①~o motte (means) By means of ~ N（手段） + をもって Names the tool/method — no time element at all. "With enthusiasm," "with the latest tech."
〜をもって②~o motte (deadline) As of ~ (= を限りに) N〈期限〉 + をもって A formal deadline marker, functionally identical to Day 2's を限りに. Formal announcements: sales ending, resignations.
〜というもの~to iu mono For the entire duration of ~ （この/ここ）N + というもの An unbroken continuous state across a named period — no single trigger event. "Haven't had a bath in a month," continuously.
Exam trap: を皮切りに and というもの can both follow a time-flavored expression, but ask: does what follows describe a GROWING series of separate events (→ を皮切りに, Q5) or a single UNBROKEN state that hasn't changed (→ というもの, Q2/Q4)? "Objections poured in one after another" is a growing series; "hasn't had a proper meal" is one unbroken state.
