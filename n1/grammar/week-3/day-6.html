6日目 — 散歩がてら (Sanpo gatera)

Source

Grammar points covered
〜そばから〜のかたわら／〜るかたわら〜がてら〜かたがた
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.60 (7日目's last page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four patterns combine two activities, but in different ways — instantly undone (そばから), an ongoing secondary role (かたわら, tagged 硬 formal/written), a two-purpose trip (がてら), or a formal occasion-combining phrase (かたがた, also 硬).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜そばから

neutral register
Reading~soba kara
Meaning (EN)"no sooner than ~ (immediately undone or repeated)" — describes an action being immediately undone/countered by another, often frustratingly
Meaning (HI)...करते ही (तुरंत पलट जाना/दोहराना, अक्सर निराशाजनक ढंग से)
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ (તરત ઊલટું થવું/પુનરાવર્તન થવું, ઘણી વાર નિરાશાજનક રીતે)
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた／Vている + そばから　◆動作の繰り返しを表すことが多い
RegisterNeutral
Typical useFrustration at an action being undone almost as fast as it's performed — teaching someone who immediately forgets, tidying that's immediately messed up again.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
祖母は、携帯電話の使い方を教えても、教えるそばから忘れてしまう。Sobo wa, keitai denwa no tsukaikata o oshiete mo, oshieru soba kara wasurete shimau. My grandmother forgets how to use a cell phone as soon as I teach her. दादी को मोबाइल इस्तेमाल करना सिखाओ तो सिखाते ही भूल जाती हैं। દાદીને મોબાઈલ વાપરવાનું શીખવો તો શીખવતાં જ ભૂલી જાય છે.
小さい子どもは、片付けているそばから部屋を散らかす。Chiisai kodomo wa, katazukete iru soba kara heya o chirakasu. Young kids mess up the room as soon as you tidy it. छोटे बच्चे कमरा साफ़ करते ही उसे फिर से बिखेर देते हैं। નાનાં બાળકો રૂમ સાફ કરતાં જ તેને ફરી વિખેરી નાખે છે.

The frustration built into this pattern comes from the gap between effort and result being nearly zero — whatever you just did gets undone almost instantly, ◆often describing a repeated action (動作の繰り返し).

【2】 〜のかたわら／〜るかたわら

硬 formal/written
Reading~no katawara / ~ru katawara
Meaning (EN)"while also (doing something else as a secondary, ongoing activity)" — describes maintaining a secondary occupation/activity alongside a main one
Meaning (HI)...के साथ-साथ (एक मुख्य काम के अलावा, दूसरा भी लगातार करना)
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે સાથે (એક મુખ્ય કામ ઉપરાંત, બીજું પણ ચાલુ રાખવું)
Connection (接続)[aかたわらb] a=主になること; N + のかたわら; Vる + かたわら　◆同時に行うことには使えない
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useDescribing someone with two roles/occupations, one primary and one secondary but still substantial.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
父は農業のかたわら、小さい店を経営している。Chichi wa nougyou no katawara, chiisai mise o keiei shite iru. My father is a farmer and at the same time runs a small shop. पापा खेती के साथ-साथ, एक छोटी दुकान भी चलाते हैं। પપ્પા ખેતીની સાથે સાથે, એક નાની દુકાન પણ ચલાવે છે.
彼女は女優の仕事をするかたわら、家事もきちんとやっている。Kanojo wa joyuu no shigoto o suru katawara, kaji mo kichinto yatte iru. She is an actress but she's also a good homemaker. वह अभिनय के काम के साथ-साथ, घर के काम भी बख़ूबी करती है। તે અભિનયના કામની સાથે સાથે, ઘરકામ પણ સારી રીતે કરે છે.

◆同時に行うことには使えない (cannot be used for actions done at the exact same moment) — the book explicitly marks テレビを見るかたわら勉強する as ダメ (wrong; watching TV and studying at the literal same instant should be テレビを見ながら勉強する instead). かたわら is for two separate, ongoing life-roles, not simultaneous micro-actions.

【3】 〜がてら

neutral register
Reading~gatera
Meaning (EN)"while doing ~ (combining two purposes in one trip/action)" — the main activity is done specifically to also accomplish a secondary purpose along the way
Meaning (HI)...करते हुए (एक ही काम में दो मक़सद पूरे करना, जैसे रास्ते में)
Meaning (GU)...કરતી વખતે (એક જ કામમાં બે હેતુ પૂરા કરવા, જેમ કે રસ્તામાં)
Connection (接続)N（するN） + がてら; V（ますstem） + がてら　◆スル動詞は名詞として使われることが多い
RegisterNeutral
Typical useCasually combining an errand with an incidental activity — a walk that also drops by a shop, viewing something while walking somewhere.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
散歩がてら、本屋に行ってくるよ。Sanpo gatera, honya ni itte kuru yo. I'm going for a walk and will drop by a bookstore. सैर के बहाने, किताबों की दुकान भी हो आता/आती हूँ। ફરવાના બહાને, પુસ્તકની દુકાને પણ જઈ આવીશ.
桜を見がてら隣の駅まで歩いた。Sakura o migatera tonari no eki made aruita. I walked to the next station while enjoying the cherry blossoms. चेरी के फूल देखते हुए, अगले स्टेशन तक पैदल गया/गई। ચેરીનાં ફૂલો જોતાં જોતાં, બાજુના સ્ટેશન સુધી ચાલીને ગયો/ગઈ.
遊びがてらお立ち寄りください。Asobi gatera otachiyori kudasai. Please drop by at your leisure. घूमते-फिरते कभी भी आ जाइए। ફરતાં ફરતાં ક્યારેક આવી જજો.

◆スル動詞は名詞として使われることが多い — verbs that are normally する-compounds (like 散歩する) drop する and are used as a bare noun before がてら (散歩がてら, not 散歩するがてら).

【4】 〜かたがた

硬 formal/written
Reading~katagata
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of ~ / combining with ~" — a formal way to say an action is done together with/on the occasion of another
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / ...के साथ-साथ (औपचारिक भाषा, अभिवादन में)
Meaning (GU)...ના અવસર પર / ...ની સાથે સાથે (ઔપચારિક ભાષા, અભિવાદનમાં)
Connection (接続)N + かたがた
Register硬 — formal, mostly written; common in greetings/formal correspondence
Typical useFormally combining a visit/action with its social purpose — thanks, apology, a report.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ご挨拶かたがた、一言お礼を述べさせていただきます。Go-aisatsu katagata, hitokoto orei o nobesasete itadakimasu. I would like to say a word to convey my appreciation. अभिवादन के साथ, एक शब्द में धन्यवाद अर्पित करना चाहता/चाहती हूँ। અભિવાદનની સાથે, એક શબ્દમાં આભાર વ્યક્ત કરવા માંગું છું.
本日はお礼かたがたお伺いしました。Honjitsu wa orei katagata oukagai shimashita. I came to show my gratitude to you today. आज मैं धन्यवाद देने के साथ, आपसे मिलने आया/आई हूँ। આજે હું આભાર વ્યક્ત કરવાની સાથે, તમને મળવા આવ્યો/આવી છું.

Notes (れい): お見舞いかたがた (combined with a hospital visit), お詫びかたがた (combined with an apology), ご報告かたがた (combined with a report), 散歩かたがた (combined with a walk — same construction reused casually).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.57. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.60.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

食べる（　）次の料理がどんどん運ばれて、ゆっくり味わえなかった。Taberu (___) tsugi no ryouri ga dondon hakobarete, yukkuri ajiwaenakatta.

Full sentence: 食べるそばから次の料理がどんどん運ばれて、ゆっくり味わえなかった。Taberu soba kara tsugi no ryouri ga dondon hakobarete, yukkuri ajiwaenakatta.
EN: The next dish kept arriving the moment I finished eating, so I couldn't savor anything slowly.
HI: खाते ही अगला व्यंजन आ जाता, इसलिए आराम से स्वाद नहीं ले पाया/पाई।
GU: ખાતાં જ પછીની વસ્તુ આવી જતી, એટલે આરામથી સ્વાદ લઈ ન શક્યો/શકી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそばからsoba karano sooner than (immediately followed)खाते हीખાતાં જ
bかたわらkatawarawhile also (wrong — this isn't two ongoing life-roles)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Dishes arriving the instant one finishes eating each course is Point 1's そばから; かたわら describes two parallel ongoing occupations, not a rapid-fire dining sequence.

Q2

今日は先日のご報告（　）、部下の紹介に参りました。Kyou wa senjitsu no go-houkoku (___), buka no shoukai ni mairimashita.

Full sentence: 今日は先日のご報告かたがた、部下の紹介に参りました。Kyou wa senjitsu no go-houkoku katagata, buka no shoukai ni mairimashita.
EN: Today I've come both to report on the other day's matter and to introduce my subordinate.
HI: आज मैं पिछले दिन की रिपोर्ट देने के साथ, अपने अधीनस्थ को भी पेश करने आया/आई हूँ।
GU: આજે હું છેલ્લા દિવસનો રિપોર્ટ આપવાની સાથે, મારા ગૌણ કર્મચારીને પણ રજૂ કરવા આવ્યો/આવી છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかたわらkatawarawhile also (wrong — no two ongoing roles here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bかたがたkatagatacombined with (this formal visit)के साथ (यह औपचारिक मुलाक़ात)સાથે (આ ઔપચારિક મુલાકાત)

Why: A formal visit combining a report with an introduction is Point 4's かたがた; かたわら is for two parallel ongoing occupations, not a single combined formal visit.

Q3

散歩（　）立ち寄った美術館は、とてもすいていた。Sanpo (___) tachiyotta bijutsukan wa, totemo suite ita.

Full sentence: 散歩がてら立ち寄った美術館は、とてもすいていた。Sanpo gatera tachiyotta bijutsukan wa, totemo suite ita.
EN: The art museum I dropped by while out for a walk was very uncrowded.
HI: सैर करते हुए जो कला संग्रहालय देखा, वह बहुत कम भीड़ वाला था।
GU: ફરવા જતાં જે આર્ટ મ્યુઝિયમમાં ગયો/ગઈ, ત્યાં ખૂબ ઓછી ભીડ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aがてらgaterawhile doing, combining two purposesकरते हुए, दोनों मक़सदકરતી વખતે, બંને હેતુ
bのそばからno soba kara(not a valid combination — そばから attaches to verbs, not nouns this way)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Combining a walk with a museum visit is exactly Point 3's がてら; のそばから isn't a valid grammatical combination here.

Q4

その小説家は執筆（　）、趣味でピアノを弾いている。Sono shousetsuka wa shippitsu (___), shumi de piano o hiite iru.

Full sentence: その小説家は執筆のかたわら、趣味でピアノを弾いている。Sono shousetsuka wa shippitsu no katawara, shumi de piano o hiite iru.
EN: That novelist plays the piano as a hobby alongside their writing.
HI: वह उपन्यासकार लेखन के साथ-साथ, शौक़ के तौर पर पियानो भी बजाता है।
GU: તે નવલકથાકાર લેખનની સાથે સાથે, શોખ તરીકે પિયાનો પણ વગાડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのかたわらno katawarawhile also (an ongoing secondary role)के साथ-साथ (लगातार)ની સાથે સાથે (સતત)
bがてらgaterawhile doing (wrong — piano isn't incidental to one trip/errand)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Writing and piano-playing are two ongoing life activities, not a single combined errand — Point 2's かたわら; がてら is for a one-off trip combining two purposes.

HI: वह सामान शेल्फ़ पर रखते ही, बिजली की तरह बिक जाता था।
GU: તે સામાન શેલ્ફ પર મૂકતાં જ, વીજળીની જેમ વેચાઈ જતો હતો.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

忘年会の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ は、あまりよくなかった。Bounenkai no ___ wa, amari yokunakatta.

Tiles: 1 飲みに行った (nomi ni itta)　2 下見 (kekken/shitami)　3 居酒屋 (izakaya)　4 がてら (gatera)

2 下見→4 がてら→1 飲みに行った→3 居酒屋

忘年会の下見がてら飲みに行った居酒屋は、あまりよくなかった。Bounenkai no shitami gatera nomi ni itta izakaya wa, amari yokunakatta.

EN: The izakaya we went to drink at, while also scouting it out for the year-end party, wasn't very good.
HI: साल के अंत की पार्टी की जगह देखने के बहाने जिस इज़ाकाया में पीने गए, वह ज़्यादा अच्छी नहीं थी।
GU: વર્ષના અંતની પાર્ટીની જગ્યા જોવાના બહાને જે ઇઝાકાયામાં પીવા ગયા, તે વધુ સારી નહોતી.

Why: Built on Point 3's がてら — scouting the venue and drinking there combine into one trip.

Q7

本日は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ した次第です。Honjitsu wa ___ shita shidai desu.

Tiles: 1 かたがた (katagata)　2 先日の (senjitsu no)　3 お詫び (owabi)　4 お伺い (oukagai)

2 先日の→3 お詫び→1 かたがた→4 お伺い

EN: Today I've come to visit, combined with an apology for the other day.
HI: आज मैं पिछले दिन की माफ़ी के साथ, आपसे मिलने आया/आई हूँ।
GU: આજે હું છેલ્લા દિવસની માફીની સાથે, તમને મળવા આવ્યો/આવી છું.

Why: Built on Point 4's かたがた — the visit formally combines an apology with the act of visiting itself.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns combine two things, but the relationship between them differs sharply:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜そばから~soba kara No sooner than ~ (undone/repeated) Vる／Vた／Vている + そばから One action is immediately undone/countered by another — a frustrating cycle. "Forgets as soon as taught," "messes up as soon as tidied."
〜のかたわら~no katawara While also ~ (secondary ongoing role) N + のかたわら; Vる + かたわら [硬] Two separate, ongoing life-roles — never simultaneous micro-actions. "Farmer who also runs a shop," "actress who's also a homemaker."
〜がてら~gatera While doing ~ (two purposes, one trip) N（するN） + がてら; V（ますstem） + がてら A single errand/trip that incidentally serves a second purpose. "Walk + bookstore," "viewing cherry blossoms while walking."
〜かたがた~katagata On the occasion of ~ [硬] N + かたがた Formal social-visit language — combining a visit with its stated social purpose. Formal greetings, apologies, reports in person.
Exam trap: かたわら and がてら both mean roughly "while also doing X," but scale is the difference: かたわら is for substantial, ongoing dual roles (a career + a second career), while がてら is for a single casual trip with an incidental secondary purpose (a walk + a bookstore visit). If it's a lifelong or long-term arrangement, use かたわら (Q4); if it's a one-time errand, use がてら (Q3).
