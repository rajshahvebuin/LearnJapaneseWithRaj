2日目 — 今日を限りに (Kyou o kagiri ni)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.48–49 (PDF pages 49–50)
Grammar points covered
〜限りだ〜を限りに／〜限りで〜に限る〜に限ったことで（は/も）ない
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.51 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four patterns today share 限る／限り, but split into an emotional superlative (限りだ), a definitive time marker (を限りに), a recommendation (に限る), and a formal "not unique to" disclaimer (に限ったことではない, tagged 硬 formal/written). Watch the exam trap in §3 — this book explicitly marks 声限り (without を) as ungrammatical.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜限りだ

neutral register
Reading~kagiri da
Meaning (EN)"extremely ~ (an emotion at its peak)" — intensifies an emotional adjective to its maximum degree
Meaning (HI)बेहद..., हद से ज़्यादा... (भावना की चरम सीमा)
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત..., હદ કરતાં વધુ... (લાગણીની ટોચ)
Connection (接続)Aい + 限りだ; naな + 限りだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useExpressing an emotion (envy, loneliness, relief, gratitude) at its most intense — always about the speaker's own feeling, not a description of someone/something else.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
いとこの結婚式に出られないとは、残念な限りだ。Itoko no kekkonshiki ni derarenai towa, zannen na kagiri da. It's a shame I can't attend my cousin's wedding. चचेरे भाई/बहन की शादी में न जा पाना, बेहद अफ़सोस की बात है। પિતરાઈ ભાઈ/બહેનના લગ્નમાં ન જઈ શકવું, ખૂબ જ અફસોસની વાત છે.
年を取って、お金もなく、家族もいないのは、心細い限りです。Toshi o totte, okane mo naku, kazoku mo inai no wa, kokorobosoi kagiri desu. I feel helpless being old, and not having money or a family. बूढ़ा होकर, पैसे भी न हों, परिवार भी न हो — यह बेहद असहाय महसूस कराता है। ઘરડા થઈને, પૈસા પણ ન હોય, પરિવાર પણ ન હોય — આ ખૂબ જ અસહાય અનુભવ કરાવે છે.

The book's own れい list of other common members: うらやましい限り (extremely envious), うれしい限り (extremely happy), 寂しい限り (extremely lonely), 心強い限り (extremely reassuring), 頼もしい限り (extremely reliable-feeling). Always a first-person emotional reaction, never a neutral description.

【2】 〜を限りに／〜限りで

neutral register
Reading~o kagiri ni / ~kagiri de
Meaning (EN)"as of ~ (a definitive time marker), marking an end or a fresh start" — declares a specific time as the cutoff point
Meaning (HI)...से (एक निश्चित समय-सीमा), उसके बाद ख़त्म/शुरू
Meaning (GU)...થી (એક ચોક્કસ સમય-મર્યાદા), તે પછી પૂરું/શરૂ
Connection (接続)N（主に時を表す語） + を限りに／限りで
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAnnouncing a definite cutoff — a habit ending, a service discontinuing, a role concluding.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日を限りに甘いものをやめることにした。Kyou o kagiri ni amai mono o yameru koto ni shita. I've decided to quit eating sweets from today. आज से मीठा खाना छोड़ने का फ़ैसला किया। આજથી ગળ્યું ખાવાનું છોડવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
3月限りでこのクラスはなくなります。San-gatsu kagiri de kono kurasu wa naku narimasu. This course will not be offered from April onwards. मार्च के बाद यह क्लास बंद हो जाएगी। માર્ચ પછી આ ક્લાસ બંધ થઈ જશે.
彼は声を限りに助けを求めた。Kare wa koe o kagiri ni tasuke o motometa. He screamed at the top of his lungs for help. उसने पूरी आवाज़ से मदद के लिए पुकारा। તેણે પૂરા અવાજથી મદદ માટે બૂમ પાડી.

◆Nは主に時を表す語 (N is mainly a time-related word: 本日を限りに, 今回を限りに). 声を限りに ("at the top of one's lungs") is a fixed idiomatic exception using a non-time noun — but the book explicitly marks the shortened 声限り (dropping を) as ダメ (wrong): unlike 3月限りで, you cannot drop を here. See §3 for this exact trap.

【3】 〜に限る

neutral register
Reading~ni kagiru
Meaning (EN)"nothing beats ~ / ~ is the best (option)" — recommends one option as clearly superior to any alternative
Meaning (HI)...से बेहतर कुछ नहीं / ...ही सबसे अच्छा है
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં વધુ સારું કંઈ નથી / ...જ સૌથી સારું છે
Connection (接続)Vる／Vない + に限る; N + に限る
RegisterNeutral
Typical useGiving a strong personal recommendation — "if you ask me, X is simply the best choice."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
風邪を引いたときは、暖かくして寝るに限る。Kaze o hiita toki wa, atatakaku shite neru ni kagiru. It is best to keep warm and rest when you catch a cold. ज़ुकाम हो जाए तो गरम रहकर सोना ही सबसे अच्छा है। શરદી થાય તો ગરમ રહીને સૂવું જ સૌથી સારું છે.
日本語学校を選ぶなら、この学校に限る。Nihongo gakkou o erabu nara, kono gakkou ni kagiru. When it comes to a Japanese language school, this one is the best. जापानी भाषा स्कूल चुनना हो तो यही स्कूल सबसे अच्छा है। જાપાનીઝ ભાષા શાળા પસંદ કરવી હોય તો આ જ શાળા સૌથી સારી છે.

Note (れい): 〜を買うなら安いのに限る ("if buying ~, the cheap one is best"). Unlike Point 2's 限り, this に限る always expresses a personal recommendation/preference, not a time cutoff.

【4】 〜に限ったことで（は/も）ない

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni kagitta koto de (wa/mo) nai
Meaning (EN)"not limited to ~ (applies more broadly)" — a formal way of saying a problem/phenomenon isn't unique to the named case
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़...तक सीमित नहीं (यह व्यापक रूप से लागू होता है)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત...પૂરતું સીમિત નથી (આ વ્યાપક રીતે લાગુ પડે છે)
Connection (接続)N + に限ったことで（は/も）ない　◆「Nに限らない」の硬い表現
Register硬 — a formal version of the simpler Nに限らない
Typical usePushing back on the idea that a problem is unique to one country/year/group — it happens elsewhere too.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
若者の言葉遣いが悪いのは、我が国に限ったことではない。Wakamono no kotobazukai ga warui no wa, wagakuni ni kagitta koto de wa nai. It is not only in this country that young people do not know how to properly use the language. युवाओं की भाषा ख़राब होना, सिर्फ़ हमारे देश तक सीमित नहीं है। યુવાનોની ભાષા ખરાબ હોવી, ફક્ત આપણા દેશ પૂરતી સીમિત નથી.
夏にインフルエンザがはやったのは、今年に限ったことではなく、去年も同様だった。Natsu ni infuruenza ga hayatta no wa, kotoshi ni kagitta koto de wa naku, kyonen mo douyou datta. A flu broke out not only this summer but last summer as well. गर्मियों में फ़्लू फैलना सिर्फ़ इस साल की बात नहीं है, पिछले साल भी वैसा ही था। ઉનાળામાં ફ્લૂ ફેલાવો ફક્ત આ વર્ષની વાત નથી, ગયા વર્ષે પણ એવું જ હતું.

This is essentially に限らない dressed up formally for essays/reports — reach for the plain に限らない in conversation, and this ことではない version in writing.

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.49. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.51.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

応援していたチームが試合に負けてしまって残念（　）。Ouen shite ita chiimu ga shiai ni makete shimatte zannen (___).

Full sentence: 応援していたチームが試合に負けてしまって残念な限りだ。Ouen shite ita chiimu ga shiai ni makete shimatte zannen na kagiri da.
EN: I'm so disappointed that the team I was cheering for lost the match.
HI: जिस टीम का समर्थन कर रहा था वह मैच हार गई, बहुत अफ़सोस है।
GU: જે ટીમને ટેકો આપી રહ્યો હતો તે મેચ હારી ગઈ, ખૂબ અફસોસ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aな限りだna kagiri daextremely (disappointed)बहुत ज़्यादाખૂબ જ
bに限るni kagiruis the best (wrong — not a recommendation here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 残念な限りだ intensifies the emotion (disappointment) to its peak — Point 1; に限る is for recommending a best option, unrelated to expressing feelings.

Q2

新築の家を買った。今月（　）このマンションともお別れだ。Shinchiku no ie o katta. Kongetsu (___) kono manshon tomo owakare da.

Full sentence: 新築の家を買った。今月を限りにこのマンションともお別れだ。Shinchiku no ie o katta. Kongetsu o kagiri ni kono manshon tomo owakare da.
EN: I bought a newly-built house. As of this month, I'll be saying goodbye to this apartment too.
HI: नया घर ख़रीद लिया। इस महीने से इस फ़्लैट को भी अलविदा कहना है।
GU: નવું ઘર ખરીદી લીધું. આ મહિનાથી આ ફ્લેટને પણ આલવિદા કહેવાનું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aを限りにo kagiri nias of, marking the cutoffसे (यह अंतिम बिंदु है)થી (આ અંતિમ બિંદુ છે)
bに限りni kagiri(wrong — this means "restricted to," an unrelated pattern)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 2's を限りに marks "this month" as the definite ending point of living in the apartment; に限り (note: different from 限りで) means "restricted only to," a different, unrelated meaning.

Q3

風邪をひいたときは、薬など飲むよりゆっくり寝る（　）。Kaze o hiita toki wa, kusuri nado nomu yori yukkuri neru (___).

Full sentence: 風邪をひいたときは、薬など飲むよりゆっくり寝るに限る。Kaze o hiita toki wa, kusuri nado nomu yori yukkuri neru ni kagiru.
EN: When you catch a cold, sleeping well is better than taking medicine — nothing beats it.
HI: ज़ुकाम हो जाए तो दवा लेने से बेहतर है आराम से सो जाना — यही सबसे अच्छा है।
GU: શરદી થાય તો દવા લેવા કરતાં આરામથી ઊંઘી જવું સારું — આ જ સૌથી શ્રેષ્ઠ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aに限るni kagirunothing beats it, is the bestयही सबसे अच्छा हैઆ જ સૌથી શ્રેષ્ઠ છે
b限りだkagiri da(wrong — 限りだ needs an emotional adjective, not a verb)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This is a recommendation ("sleeping is best") — Point 3's に限る; 限りだ attaches to emotional adjectives (Point 1), not to a verb like 寝る here.

Q4

Full sentence: 朝の電車が混んでいるのは、今日に限ったことではない。Asa no densha ga konde iru no wa, kyou ni kagitta koto de wa nai.
EN: The morning train being crowded isn't limited to today.
HI: सुबह की ट्रेन में भीड़ होना सिर्फ़ आज की बात नहीं है।
GU: સવારની ટ્રેનમાં ભીડ હોવી ફક્ત આજની વાત નથી.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

そのドラマは、視聴率が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 打ち切られることになった。Sono dorama wa, shichouritsu ga ___ uchikirareru koto ni natta.

Tiles: 1 を限り (o kagiri)　2 に (ni)　3 伸びず (nobizu)　4 10回目 (jukkai-me)

3 伸びず→4 10回目→1 を限り→2 に

そのドラマは、視聴率が伸びず10回目を限りに打ち切られることになった。Sono dorama wa, shichouritsu ga nobizu jukkai-me o kagiri ni uchikirareru koto ni natta.

EN: That drama's ratings didn't grow, and it ended up being cancelled as of the 10th episode.
HI: उस ड्रामा की टीआरपी नहीं बढ़ी, तो 10वें एपिसोड के बाद उसे बंद कर दिया गया।
GU: તે ડ્રામાની ટીઆરપી ન વધી, તો 10મા એપિસોડ પછી તેને બંધ કરી દેવામાં આવ્યો.

Why: Built on Point 2's を限りに — the 10th episode is fixed as the definitive cutoff point for the show.

Q7

漢字が書けなくなったのは、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ なってからずっとだ。Kanji ga kakenaku natta no wa, ___ natte kara zutto da.

Tiles: 1 コンピューターを使うように (konpyuutaa o tsukau you ni)　2 最近 (saikin)　3 ではなく (de wa naku)　4 に限ったこと (ni kagitta koto)

2 最近→4 に限ったこと→3 ではなく→1 コンピューターを使うように

漢字が書けなくなったのは、最近に限ったことではなくコンピューターを使うようになってからずっとだ。Kanji ga kakenaku natta no wa, saikin ni kagitta koto de wa naku konpyuutaa o tsukau you ni natte kara zutto da.

EN: Not being able to write kanji isn't limited to recently — it's been the case ever since I started using computers.
HI: कांजी न लिख पाना सिर्फ़ हाल की बात नहीं है, कंप्यूटर इस्तेमाल करने के बाद से यह हमेशा से है।
GU: કાંજી ન લખી શકવું ફક્ત હાલની વાત નથી, કમ્પ્યુટર વાપરવાનું શરૂ કર્યા પછીથી આ હંમેશાં છે.

Why: Built on Point 4's に限ったことではない — the problem isn't recent, it extends back to when computer use began.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns share 限る／限り, but each answers a different question — how intense, how definite the cutoff, how good the choice, or how widespread the issue:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜限りだ~kagiri da Extremely ~ (emotion) Aい／naな + 限りだ Intensifies the speaker's own emotional adjective to its peak. "How envious/lonely/relieved I am!"
〜を限りに／〜限りで~o kagiri ni/kagiri de As of ~ (definitive cutoff) N（時） + を限りに／限りで Marks a specific time as an ending/starting point. "Quitting sweets from today," "the course ends in March."
〜に限る~ni kagiru Nothing beats ~ Vる／Vない／N + に限る A personal recommendation — this option is simply the best. "Rest is best for a cold," "this school is the best."
〜に限ったことではない~ni kagitta koto de wa nai Not limited to ~ N + に限ったことで（は/も）ない [硬] Disclaims that a problem is unique to the named case — it's broader. Formal writing pushing back on a narrow assumption.
Exam trap: Point 2's を限りに usually attaches to a time word and CAN drop を in casual speech (3月限りで), but the fixed idiom 声を限りに ("at the top of one's lungs") is the opposite — the book explicitly marks the shortened 声限り as ダメ (ungrammatical). Don't over-generalize "を can be dropped" from the time-word cases to this idiom.
