1日目 — 毎日はしないまでも (Mainichi wa shinai made mo)

Source

Grammar points covered
〜まで（のこと）だ／それ・これまでだ〜ないまでも〜てまでも〜までもない
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.49 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Every pattern today is built on まで, but each does a different job — declaring a limit ("that's all there is"), lowering a bar ("even if not fully"), raising the stakes ("even to the point of"), or dismissing a need ("no need to even"). See §3 for the full contrast.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜まで（のこと）だ／それ・これまでだ

neutral register
Reading~made (no koto) da / sore/kore made da
Meaning (EN)"that's all there is to it / merely ~, nothing more" — downplays an action as insignificant, or declares a situation as having reached its definitive end
Meaning (HI)बस इतना ही / यही अंत है, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं
Meaning (GU)બસ આટલું જ / આ જ અંત છે, એથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた + まで（のこと）だ; それ／これ + までだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useShrugging off an action as trivial ("I only just..."), or declaring that once a condition fails, there's simply no more to be done.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ちょっと聞いてみたまでです。Chotto kiite mita made desu. I just wanted to ask. बस थोड़ा पूछ लिया, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं। ફક્ત થોડું પૂછી લીધું, એથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં.
妻が反対だと言えば、あきらめるまでだ。Tsuma ga hantai da to ieba, akirameru made da. I'll have to give it up if my wife says no. अगर पत्नी मना करे, तो बस छोड़ ही देना पड़ेगा। જો પત્ની ના પાડે, તો બસ છોડી જ દેવું પડશે.
給料が上がらないなら、会社をやめるまでだ。Kyuuryou ga agaranai nara, kaisha o yameru made da. I'll have to quit this job if I don't get a salary raise. अगर तनख़्वाह नहीं बढ़ी, तो नौकरी छोड़नी ही पड़ेगी। જો પગાર નહીં વધે, તો નોકરી છોડવી જ પડશે.
いくら高いコンピューターを買っても、使わなければそれまでだ。Ikura takai konpyuutaa o katte mo, tsukawanakereba sore made da. It's a waste to buy an expensive computer if you don't use it. चाहे कितना भी महँगा कंप्यूटर ख़रीदो, इस्तेमाल न करो तो बस वहीं तक बात ख़त्म। ભલે ગમે તેટલું મોંઘું કમ્પ્યુટર ખરીદો, વાપરો નહીં તો બસ ત્યાં જ વાત પૂરી.

それまでだ／これまでだ is the fixed idiom form — "that's the end of it, nothing can be done." The plain Vる／Vたまでだ version instead downplays an action's significance ("I merely did X").

【2】 〜ないまでも

neutral register
Reading~nai made mo
Meaning (EN)"even if not (fully) doing ~, at least..." — concedes a higher bar can't be met, then sets a more modest one
Meaning (HI)भले ही पूरी तरह...न करें, कम से कम...
Meaning (GU)ભલે સંપૂર્ણ રીતે...ન કરો, ઓછામાં ઓછું...
Connection (接続)Vない + までも
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSoftening an expectation — "you don't have to go all the way, but do at least this much."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
毎日はしないまでも、週に1回くらいは部屋の掃除をしよう。Mainichi wa shinai made mo, shuu ni ikkai kurai wa heya no souji o shiyou. I won't clean my room every day but I will at least once a week. रोज़ नहीं तो, कम से कम हफ़्ते में एक बार कमरा साफ़ कर लेते हैं। રોજ નહીં તો, ઓછામાં ઓછું અઠવાડિયામાં એક વાર રૂમ સાફ કરી લઈએ.
手紙を書かないまでも電話ぐらいしろ。Tegami o kakanai made mo denwa gurai shiro. If you can't be bothered to write to me, at least call. चिट्ठी न लिखो तो कम से कम फ़ोन तो कर दो। પત્ર ન લખો તો ઓછામાં ઓછો ફોન તો કરો.

This softens a demand into two tiers: the ideal (writing/cleaning daily) and the bare minimum acceptable substitute — always introduced with ぐらい／くらい for that "at least this much" feel.

【3】 〜てまでも

neutral register
Reading~te made mo
Meaning (EN)"even to the extent of doing ~ (a drastic or sacrificial length)" — describes going so far as to do something costly to achieve a goal
Meaning (HI)यहाँ तक कि...करके भी (एक बड़ी क़ीमत चुकाकर)
Meaning (GU)ત્યાં સુધી કે...કરીને પણ (મોટી કિંમત ચૂકવીને)
Connection (接続)Vて + までも
RegisterNeutral
Typical useHighlighting the extreme lengths (health, safety, money) someone goes to for a goal.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
母は体を壊してまでも、朝から晩まで働き続けた。Haha wa karada o kowashite made mo, asa kara ban made hataraki tsuzuketa. My mother worked day and night and sacrificed her health. माँ ने अपनी सेहत ख़राब कर लेने तक, सुबह से शाम तक काम करना जारी रखा। માએ પોતાનું સ્વાસ્થ્ય બગાડી નાખવા સુધી, સવારથી સાંજ સુધી કામ કરવાનું ચાલુ રાખ્યું.
そのジャーナリストは危険を冒してまでも戦場に行こうとしている。Sono jaanarisuto wa kiken o okashite made mo senjou ni ikou to shite iru. Despite it being risky, the journalist is going to the war zone. वह पत्रकार ख़तरा उठाकर भी युद्धक्षेत्र में जाने की कोशिश कर रहा है। તે પત્રકાર જોખમ લઈને પણ યુદ્ધક્ષેત્રમાં જવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરી રહ્યો છે.

Compare directly with Point 2: ないまでも lowers the bar (doing less than expected is still okay), while てまでも raises it dramatically (doing far more than reasonable, at a real cost).

【4】 〜までもない

neutral register
Reading~made mo nai
Meaning (EN)"no need to even ~ / it's obvious without doing ~" — declares an action unnecessary because the conclusion is already self-evident
Meaning (HI)...करने की भी ज़रूरत नहीं (बात इतनी स्पष्ट है)
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની પણ જરૂર નથી (વાત એટલી સ્પષ્ટ છે)
Connection (接続)Vる + までもない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDismissing an action (asking permission, checking, testing) as redundant given how obvious the outcome already is.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
日本が狭いということは、言うまでもない。Nihon ga semai to iu koto wa, iu made mo nai. It goes without saying that Japan is a small country. यह कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं कि जापान एक छोटा देश है। એ કહેવાની જરૂર નથી કે જાપાન નાનો દેશ છે.
その件については、社長に許可を取るまでもない。Sono ken ni tsuite wa, shachou ni kyoka o toru made mo nai. You don't have to get the president's approval on this matter. इस मामले पर अध्यक्ष से मंज़ूरी लेने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। આ બાબતે અધ્યક્ષની મંજૂરી લેવાની જરૂર નથી.
アルコール検査をするまでもなく、彼が酔っ払っていることは明白だ。Arukooru kensa o suru made mo naku, kare ga yopparatte iru koto wa meihaku da. He is definitely drunk. I don't think we even need to give him a Breathalyzer. अल्कोहल जाँच करने की भी ज़रूरत नहीं, वह नशे में है यह साफ़ है। આલ્કોહોલ ટેસ્ટ કરવાની પણ જરૂર નથી, તે નશામાં છે એ સ્પષ્ટ છે.

言うまでもない ("needless to say") is the single most common fixed phrase built on this pattern — worth memorizing as a chunk on its own.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.47. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.49.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

来たくなければ来なくてもいい。ただ君が損を（　）までだ。Kitakunakereba konakute mo ii. Tada kimi ga son o (___) made da.

Full sentence: 来たくなければ来なくてもいい。ただ君が損をするまでだ。Kitakunakereba konakute mo ii. Tada kimi ga son o suru made da.
EN: You don't have to come if you don't want to. It's just that you'll be the one who loses out, nothing more.
HI: आना नहीं चाहते तो मत आओ। बस नुक़सान तुम्हारा ही होगा, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं।
GU: આવવું ન હોય તો ન આવો. બસ નુકસાન તારું જ થશે, એથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aするsuruplain non-past (correct form before までだ)सही रूपસાચું સ્વરૂપ
bしないshinai(wrong — contradicts the meaning, "you'll lose out")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 1's まで（のこと）だ needs 損をする ("to lose out"), stating that's simply the consequence — 損をしない would reverse the meaning entirely.

Q2

親が出る（　）、子どもたちだけでその問題を解決した。Oya ga deru (___), kodomotachi dake de sono mondai o kaiketsu shita.

Full sentence: 親が出るまでもなく、子どもたちだけでその問題を解決した。Oya ga deru made mo naku, kodomotachi dake de sono mondai o kaiketsu shita.
EN: Without even needing the parents to step in, the kids solved the problem themselves.
HI: माँ-बाप के दख़ल की भी ज़रूरत नहीं पड़ी, बच्चों ने ख़ुद ही समस्या सुलझा ली।
GU: માતાપિતાની દરમિયાનગીરીની પણ જરૂર ન પડી, બાળકોએ જાતે જ સમસ્યા ઉકેલી લીધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまでもなくmade mo nakuwithout even needing toज़रूरत भी नहीं पड़ीજરૂર પણ ન પડી
bまでもmade mo(wrong — incomplete/different pattern)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 4's までもない (here in its て-form, までもなく, linking into the next clause) — the kids didn't even need their parents to intervene.

あの人とは話はしない（　）、挨拶ぐらいはしますよ。Ano hito to wa hanashi wa shinai (___), aisatsu gurai wa shimasu yo.

HI: उससे बात तो नहीं करता, लेकिन कम से कम नमस्ते तो कर लेता हूँ।
GU: તેની સાથે વાત તો નથી કરતો, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછું નમસ્તે તો કરી લઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまでもなくmade mo nakuwithout even needing to (wrong — doesn't lower a bar)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 2's ないまでも sets a modest minimum (a greeting) after conceding the fuller action (talking) won't happen.

Q4

いくらお金を稼いでも死んでしまえば（　）。Ikura okane o kaseide mo shinde shimaeba (___).

Full sentence: いくらお金を稼いでも死んでしまえば、それまでだ。Ikura okane o kaseide mo shinde shimaeba, sore made da.
EN: No matter how much money you earn, once you die, that's the end of it.
HI: चाहे कितना भी पैसा कमाओ, मरने के बाद तो सब ख़त्म ही है।
GU: ભલે ગમે તેટલા પૈસા કમાઓ, મર્યા પછી તો બધું પૂરું જ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそれまでだsore made dathat's the end of itबस वहीं ख़त्मબસ ત્યાં જ પૂરું
bそれまでもないsore made mo nai(not a valid combination of these two patterns)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

Full sentence: これくらいの故障、修理を頼むまでもない。僕が直してあげるよ。Kore kurai no koshou, shuuri o tanomu made mo nai. Boku ga naoshite ageru yo.

HI: इतनी छोटी ख़राबी के लिए मरम्मत बुलाने की ज़रूरत नहीं। मैं ठीक कर दूँगा।
GU: આટલી નાની ખરાબી માટે રિપેર બોલાવવાની જરૂર નથી. હું ઠીક કરી દઈશ.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

上手になりたければ、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ のことだ。Jouzu ni naritakereba, ___ no koto da.

Tiles: 1 練習 (renshuu)　2 ひたすら (hitasura)　3 まで (made)　4 する (suru)

2 ひたすら→1 練習→4 する→3 まで

上手になりたければ、ひたすら練習するまでのことだ。Jouzu ni naritakereba, hitasura renshuu suru made no koto da.

EN: If you want to get good at it, all there is to it is to practice relentlessly.
HI: अगर माहिर बनना है, तो बस लगातार अभ्यास करना ही काफ़ी है।
GU: જો માહિર બનવું છે, તો બસ સતત અભ્યાસ કરવો જ પૂરતો છે.

Why: Built on Point 1's まで（のこと）だ — relentless practice is presented as the whole, simple answer, nothing more complicated.

Q7

彼の行為は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ と言えるだろう。Kare no koui wa, ___ to ieru darou.

Tiles: 1 犯罪 (hanzai)　2 まで (made)　3 法律を持ち出す (houritsu o mochidasu)　4 もなく (mo naku)

3 法律を持ち出す→2 まで→4 もなく→1 犯罪

彼の行為は、法律を持ち出すまでもなく犯罪と言えるだろう。Kare no koui wa, houritsu o mochidasu made mo naku hanzai to ieru darou.

EN: His conduct could be called criminal without even needing to invoke the law.
HI: उसके व्यवहार को क़ानून का हवाला दिए बिना ही अपराध कहा जा सकता है।
GU: તેના વર્તનને કાયદાનો ઉલ્લેખ કર્યા વગર જ ગુનો કહી શકાય.

Why: Built on Point 4's までもなく — the conduct is so obviously wrong that citing the law formally isn't even necessary.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns share まで, but each does a completely different rhetorical job:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜まで（のこと）だ~made (no koto) da That's all there is to it Vる／Vた + まで（のこと）だ Downplays an action as trivial, or declares a definitive limit reached. "I merely did X" / "If Y fails, that's the end."
〜ないまでも~nai made mo Even if not (fully), at least Vない + までも Lowers a bar — concedes the ideal won't happen, sets a modest minimum. "Can't write, but at least call."
〜てまでも~te made mo Even to the extent of ~ Vて + までも Raises the stakes — going to drastic/costly lengths for a goal. "Sacrificed her health to keep working."
〜までもない~made mo nai No need to even ~ Vる + までもない Dismisses an action as redundant — the outcome is already obvious. "Goes without saying," "no need to check."
Exam trap: ないまでも and までもない both contain a negative element but point in opposite directions: ないまでも negates the ACTION inside the pattern itself ("even if [not doing this]"), while までもない negates the NEED for the action ("there's no need to do this"). Check what's being negated — the verb before までも, or the whole ことmade explicit by もない at the end.
