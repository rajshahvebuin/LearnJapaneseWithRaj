3日目 — 兄弟といえども (Kyoudai to ie domo)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.50–51 (PDF pages 51–52)
Grammar points covered
〜とは〜とはいえ〜といえども[文]とのN／[文]とのことだ
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.53 (4日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Three concessive-flavored patterns (とは, とはいえ, といえども) escalate in register and force, plus one unrelated hearsay pattern (とのN／とのことだ). といえども is tagged 硬 formal/written, as is the hearsay pattern — both lean toward written/formal contexts.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とは

neutral register
Reading~towa
Meaning (EN)"the fact that ~ is (surprising/regrettable)!" — emphasizes shock, surprise, or strong emotion about a fact just stated
Meaning (HI)...होना तो हैरानी की बात है! (गहरी भावना पर ज़ोर)
Meaning (GU)...થવું તો નવાઈની વાત છે! (ગહન લાગણી પર ભાર)
Connection (接続)V／A／na／N（plain） + とは; N／na + とは
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆強調／驚き (emphasis/surprise) — a fact is so unexpected the speaker practically exclaims it.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
東京は家賃が高いと聞いていたが、こんなに高いとは驚きだ。Toukyou wa yachin ga takai to kiite ita ga, konna ni takai towa odoroki da. I heard that the rent in Tokyo was high but I never imagined that it would be this high. टोक्यो में किराया महँगा है यह सुना था, लेकिन इतना महँगा होना हैरानी की बात है। ટોક્યોમાં ભાડું મોંઘું છે એવું સાંભળ્યું હતું, પણ આટલું મોંઘું હોવું નવાઈની વાત છે.
才能のある彼が亡くなるとは、残念でならない。Sainou no aru kare ga naku naru towa, zannen de naranai. It is so sad that such a talented person like him has died. इतने प्रतिभाशाली व्यक्ति का चले जाना, बहुत दुखद है। આટલા પ્રતિભાશાળી વ્યક્તિનું જવું, ખૂબ જ દુઃખદ છે.

The book's own れい list: こんなに暇（だ）とは, そのような人（だ）とは, あんな場所だったとは — always followed by an exclamation of surprise, disappointment, or admiration.

【2】 〜とはいえ

neutral register
Reading~towa ie
Meaning (EN)"although ~, even so" — concedes a fact while still asserting the main point holds
Meaning (HI)भले ही...हो, फिर भी
Meaning (GU)ભલે...હોય, છતાં
Connection (接続)V／A／na／N（plain） + とはいえ; N／na + とはいえ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA mild, everyday concession — "yes, that's true, but it doesn't change the bigger point."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
教師とはいえ、答えられないこともあります。Kyoushi towa ie, kotaerarenai koto mo arimasu. There are questions even teachers cannot answer. टीचर होने पर भी, कुछ सवालों के जवाब नहीं दिए जा सकते। શિક્ષક હોવા છતાં, કેટલાક પ્રશ્નોના જવાબ ન આપી શકાય.
不景気で客が少ないとはいえ、一人も来ないことは今までになかったことだ。Fukeiki de kyaku ga sukunai towa ie, hitori mo konai koto wa ima made ni nakatta koto da. Although we have fewer customers due to the poor economy, this is the first time our store has had no customers. मंदी के कारण ग्राहक कम हैं, फिर भी एक भी न आना पहली बार हुआ है। મંદીના કારણે ગ્રાહકો ઓછા છે, છતાં એક પણ ન આવવું પહેલી વાર થયું છે.

This is the everyday, conversational concession — reach for it in normal speech and writing; save といえども for a heavier, more formal punch.

【3】 〜といえども

硬 formal/written
Reading~to ie domo
Meaning (EN)"even ~, no matter how much" — a stronger, more formal concession than とはいえ, often paired with たとえ or いくら
Meaning (HI)चाहे...ही क्यों न हो, फिर भी (एक ज़्यादा ज़ोरदार, औपचारिक रियायत)
Meaning (GU)ભલે...જ કેમ ન હોય, છતાં (વધુ ભારપૂર્વક, ઔપચારિક છૂટ)
Connection (接続)V／A／na／N（plain） + といえども; N／na + といえども
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useA heavier, more emphatic concession — often introduced by たとえ／いくら for extra weight.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
たとえ兄弟といえども、憎しみ合うこともある。Tatoe kyoudai to ie domo, nikushimiau koto mo aru. Even siblings sometimes hate each other. भाई-बहन हों, फिर भी कभी-कभी नफ़रत भी हो जाती है। ભાઈ-બહેન હોય, છતાં ક્યારેક નફરત પણ થઈ જાય છે.
広い土地があるといえども、田舎なので不動産の価値はない。Hiroi tochi ga aru to ie domo, inaka na node fudousan no kachi wa nai. I have a big chunk of land but it doesn't have much value because it's in the countryside. बड़ी ज़मीन है, फिर भी गाँव में होने की वजह से उसकी कीमत नहीं है। મોટી જમીન છે, છતાં ગામડામાં હોવાથી તેની કિંમત નથી.

Notes (れい): たとえ〜といえども, いくら〜といえども. Fixed idiom: 当たらずといえども遠からず ("not quite right, but not far off either" — "that's pretty close").

【4】 [文]とのN／[文]とのことだ

硬 formal/written
Reading~to no N / ~to no koto da
Meaning (EN)"a report/message that ~" / "I hear that ~" — reports secondhand information (hearsay)
Meaning (HI)ऐसी ख़बर/संदेश मिला कि... / सुना है कि...
Meaning (GU)એવો સંદેશ/ખબર મળ્યા કે... / સાંભળ્યું છે કે...
Connection (接続)[文] + とのN; [文] + とのことだ　◆伝聞 ◆N=話、依頼、提案など
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useRelaying a message, request, or proposal secondhand — N is typically 話・依頼・提案 (a message/request/proposal).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中さんから10分ほど遅れるとの連絡がありました。Tanaka-san kara juppun hodo okureru to no renraku ga arimashita. Tanaka-san left a message that he would be 10 minutes late. तनाका-सान से ख़बर मिली कि वे लगभग 10 मिनट देर से आएँगे। તનાકા-સાન તરફથી ખબર મળી કે તેઓ લગભગ 10 મિનિટ મોડા આવશે.
社長の話によると、来月から給料を1割カットするとのことだ。Shachou no hanashi ni yoru to, raigetsu kara kyuuryou o ichi-wari katto suru to no koto da. According to what the president said, our salaries will be reduced by 10% from next month. अध्यक्ष के अनुसार, अगले महीने से वेतन में 10% की कटौती होगी। અધ્યક્ષ મુજબ, આવતા મહિનાથી પગારમાં 10% ઘટાડો થશે.

Unlike Points 1–3 (all concessive), this is a hearsay pattern — grouped into this day simply because it shares と, not because it's related in meaning. Don't try to fit it into the concession family.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.51. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.53.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

近所の人の話では、昨夜あの家でだれかが亡くなった（　）。Kinjo no hito no hanashi de wa, sakuya ano ie de dareka ga naku natta (___).

Full sentence: 近所の人の話では、昨夜あの家でだれかが亡くなったとのことだ。Kinjo no hito no hanashi de wa, sakuya ano ie de dareka ga naku natta to no koto da.
EN: According to a neighbor, someone in that house passed away last night.
HI: पड़ोसी के अनुसार, कल रात उस घर में किसी की मृत्यु हो गई।
GU: પડોશીના કહેવા મુજબ, ગઈકાલે રાત્રે તે ઘરમાં કોઈનું મૃત્યુ થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとのことだto no koto daI hear that (hearsay)ऐसा सुना हैએવું સાંભળ્યું છે
bというらしいto iu rashii(not a valid combination — らしい already implies hearsay, redundant with という)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Relaying a neighbor's secondhand report needs Point 4's とのことだ; というらしい double-stacks two hearsay markers incorrectly.

Q2

入社したばかり（　）、もう少しまともな挨拶ができないのだろうか。Nyuusha shita bakari (___), mou sukoshi matomo na aisatsu ga dekinai no darou ka.

Full sentence: 入社したばかりとはいえ、もう少しまともな挨拶ができないのだろうか。Nyuusha shita bakari towa ie, mou sukoshi matomo na aisatsu ga dekinai no darou ka.
EN: Even though he just joined the company, couldn't he manage a slightly more proper greeting?
HI: भले ही अभी-अभी कंपनी जॉइन की हो, थोड़ा ठीक तरह से अभिवादन नहीं कर सकता क्या?
GU: ભલે હમણાં જ કંપની જોઈન કરી હોય, થોડું યોગ્ય રીતે અભિવાદન ન કરી શકે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとはtowathe fact that... (surprise, wrong fit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとはいえtowa iealthough, even soभले ही, फिर भीભલે, છતાં

Why: Conceding he's new while still expecting basic manners is Point 2's とはいえ; とは alone would express surprise, not a concession leading to a criticism.

Q3

親（　）、子どもをしかるのに暴力はいけない。Oya (___), kodomo o shikaru no ni bouryoku wa ikenai.

Full sentence: 親といえども、子どもをしかるのに暴力はいけない。Oya to ie domo, kodomo o shikaru no ni bouryoku wa ikenai.
EN: Even parents shouldn't use violence to scold their children.
HI: माँ-बाप हों तब भी, बच्चों को डाँटने के लिए हिंसा का सहारा नहीं लेना चाहिए।
GU: માતાપિતા હોય તો પણ, બાળકોને ધમકાવવા માટે હિંસાનો સહારો ન લેવો જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aといえどもto ie domoeven ~ (strong concession)चाहे...ही हो, फिर भीભલે...જ હોય, છતાં
bに限りni kagirirestricted to (wrong — unrelated pattern)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A strong, formal moral assertion ("even parents shouldn't...") calls for Point 3's といえども; に限り is Day 2's "restricted to" pattern, unrelated here.

Q4

鈴木さんから、今日は風邪で休む（　）電話がありました。Suzuki-san kara, kyou wa kaze de yasumu (___) denwa ga arimashita.

Full sentence: 鈴木さんから、今日は風邪で休むとの電話がありました。Suzuki-san kara, kyou wa kaze de yasumu to no denwa ga arimashita.
EN: Suzuki-san called to say he'll be off today with a cold.
HI: सुज़ुकी-सान का फ़ोन आया कि वे आज ज़ुकाम की वजह से छुट्टी पर हैं।
GU: સુઝુકી-સાનનો ફોન આવ્યો કે તેઓ આજે શરદીના કારણે રજા પર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとのto noa call that... (attaches to a noun, 電話)...का फ़ोन...નો ફોન
bとはいうtowa iu(not a valid form here)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: との directly modifies the noun 電話 ("a call that...") — Point 4's [文]とのN; とはいう isn't a grammatical construction.

Q5

あんなに女らしく美しく見える人が、実は男だった（　）……。Anna ni onna rashiku utsukushiku mieru hito ga, jitsu wa otoko datta (___)...

Full sentence: あんなに女らしく美しく見える人が、実は男だったとは……。Anna ni onna rashiku utsukushiku mieru hito ga, jitsu wa otoko datta towa...
EN: Someone who looks so feminine and beautiful actually being a man — what a surprise...
HI: इतनी सुंदर और स्त्रीसुलभ दिखने वाला इंसान असल में मर्द निकला... कितनी हैरानी की बात है।
GU: આટલી સુંદર અને સ્ત્રી જેવી દેખાતી વ્યક્તિ ખરેખર પુરુષ નીકળી... કેટલી નવાઈની વાત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとはtowathe fact that... (surprise, trailing off)...होना तो! (हैरानी)...હોવું તો! (નવાઈ)
bとはいえtowa iealthough (wrong — no concession, just shock)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The trailing "……" after a shocking revelation is the classic Point 1 とは exclamation of surprise; とはいえ would need a following clause that concedes and continues, not just trails off in shock.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ をしてしまい、大変申し訳ありませんでした。___ o shite shimai, taihen moushiwake arimasen deshita.

Tiles: 1 知らなかったこと (shiranakatta koto)　2 失礼なこと (shitsurei na koto)　3 いえ (ie)　4 とは (towa)

1 知らなかったこと→4 とは→3 いえ→2 失礼なこと

知らなかったこととはいえ、失礼なことをしてしまい、大変申し訳ありませんでした。Shiranakatta koto towa ie, shitsurei na koto o shite shimai, taihen moushiwake arimasen deshita.

EN: Even though I didn't know, I did something rude, and I'm very sorry.
HI: भले ही मुझे पता नहीं था, फिर भी मुझसे अभद्रता हुई, बहुत माफ़ी चाहता/चाहती हूँ।
GU: ભલે મને ખબર નહોતી, છતાં મારાથી અસભ્યતા થઈ, ખૂબ માફ કરશો.

Why: Built on Point 2's とはいえ — not knowing is conceded as a reason, but it doesn't excuse the rudeness that followed.

Q7

生活＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ に働かなくてはならない。Seikatsu ___ ni hatarakanakute wa naranai.

Tiles: 1 といえども (to ie domo)　2 のため (no tame)　3 休まず (yasumazu)　4 休日 (kyuujitsu)

2 のため→4 休日→1 といえども→3 休まず

生活のため休日といえども休まずに働かなくてはならない。Seikatsu no tame kyuujitsu to ie domo yasumazu ni hatarakanakute wa naranai.

EN: For the sake of livelihood, even on holidays, one must work without rest.
HI: गुज़ारे के लिए, छुट्टी के दिन भी, बिना आराम किए काम करना ही पड़ता है।
GU: ગુજરાન માટે, રજાના દિવસે પણ, આરામ કર્યા વગર કામ કરવું જ પડે છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's といえども — even the strong exception of a holiday doesn't excuse one from working, for the sake of livelihood.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

とは, とはいえ, and といえども all involve と, but only two are genuinely interchangeable in force — と compare them:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とは~towa The fact that ~ is (surprising)! V／A／na／N（plain） + とは Pure emotional exclamation — no concession, often trails off. Reacting with shock/disappointment/admiration.
〜とはいえ~towa ie Although ~, even so V／A／na／N（plain） + とはいえ A mild, everyday concession — the main point still holds. Everyday conversation and writing.
〜といえども~to ie domo Even ~, no matter how much V／A／na／N（plain） + といえども A stronger, more formal concession — often with たとえ／いくら. Formal writing, moral/legal assertions.
[文]とのN／とのことだ~to no N/koto da A report/message that ~ / I hear that ~ [文] + とのN／とのことだ [硬] Not a concession at all — relays secondhand information (hearsay). Reporting a message, request, or proposal.
Exam trap: とは and とはいえ look nearly identical but differ in whether a continuation follows: とは typically ends the thought right there (often with just "……" or 驚きだ／残念だ), while とはいえ always continues into a second clause that concedes-but-still-asserts. If the sentence trails off in pure reaction, it's とは (Q5 above); if it continues with a "but still..." clause, it's とはいえ (Q2 above).
