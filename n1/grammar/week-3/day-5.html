5日目 — 休めるかと思いきや (Yasumeru ka to omoikiya)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.54–55 (PDF pages 55–56)
Grammar points covered
〜や（否や）〜が早いか〜たが最後／〜たら最後〜（か）と思いきや
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.57 (6日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Three near-instantaneous "the moment X happened" patterns (や否や, が早いか, たが最後) plus one "but then, surprisingly not" pattern (と思いきや). や否や and と思いきや are tagged 硬 formal/written.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜や（否や）

硬 formal/written
Reading~ya (ina ya)
Meaning (EN)"the moment ~ / no sooner than ~" — one action immediately, almost simultaneously, followed by another
Meaning (HI)...करते ही, तुरंत
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ, તરત
Connection (接続)Vる + や（否や）
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useEmphasizing that a second, often abrupt, action followed a first almost instantly.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その男の人は部屋に入るや否や、いきなり大声で怒鳴り始めた。Sono otoko no hito wa heya ni hairu ya ina ya, ikinari oogoe de donari hajimeta. The man started yelling right after he entered the room. वह आदमी कमरे में घुसते ही, अचानक ज़ोर से चिल्लाने लगा। તે માણસ રૂમમાં દાખલ થતાં જ, અચાનક મોટા અવાજે બરાડવા લાગ્યો.
彼は、それを口に入れるや、吐き出した。Kare wa, sore o kuchi ni ireru ya, hakidashita. He spat it out as soon as he put it in his mouth. उसने उसे मुँह में डालते ही, थूक दिया। તેણે તેને મોંમાં મૂકતાં જ, થૂંકી નાખ્યું.
その政治家は、形勢が不利と見るや、態度を一変した。Sono seijika wa, keisei ga furi to miru ya, taido o ippen shita. The politician changed his attitude as soon as he sensed that he was in the minority. वह राजनेता, हालात ख़िलाफ़ देखते ही, अपना रुख़ पूरी तरह बदल गया। તે રાજકારણી, પરિસ્થિતિ વિરુદ્ધ જોતાં જ, પોતાનું વલણ સંપૂર્ણપણે બદલી નાખ્યું.

否や can be dropped (入るや alone works) — both versions mean the same thing, just with slightly different rhythm/weight in writing.

【2】 〜が早いか

neutral register
Reading~ga hayai ka
Meaning (EN)"no sooner than ~ / the instant ~" — near-identical to や（否や）, emphasizing an almost-simultaneous second action
Meaning (HI)...होते ही, उसी पल
Meaning (GU)...થતાં જ, તે જ પળે
Connection (接続)Vる + が早いか
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSame "instant trigger" meaning as Point 1, in a slightly less formal register.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その学生は、授業の終わりのベルが鳴るが早いか、教室を出て行った。Sono gakusei wa, jugyou no owari no beru ga naru ga hayai ka, kyoushitsu o dete itta. The student left the classroom as soon as the bell rang at the end of the class. वह छात्र, क्लास ख़त्म होने की घंटी बजते ही, कक्षा से बाहर निकल गया। તે વિદ્યાર્થી, ક્લાસ પૂરો થવાનો બેલ વાગતાં જ, વર્ગમાંથી બહાર નીકળી ગયો.
そのタクシーは、信号の色が変わるが早いか、車を発進させた。Sono takushii wa, shingou no iro ga kawaru ga hayai ka, kuruma o hasshin saseta. The taxi took off as soon as the traffic light changed. वह टैक्सी, सिग्नल का रंग बदलते ही, चल पड़ी। તે ટેક્સી, સિગ્નલનો રંગ બદલાતાં જ, ચાલવા લાગી.

Functionally interchangeable with Point 1 in most contexts — が早いか leans slightly more neutral/spoken, や否や slightly more literary. Both always attach to the plain non-past verb.

【3】 〜たが最後／〜たら最後

neutral register
Reading~ta ga saigo / ~tara saigo
Meaning (EN)"once ~ happens, then inevitably (an unstoppable consequence follows)" — describes a point of no return
Meaning (HI)एक बार...हो जाए, तो फिर हमेशा के लिए (कोई रोक नहीं)
Meaning (GU)એક વાર...થઈ જાય, તો પછી હંમેશા માટે (કોઈ રોકાણ નહીં)
Connection (接続)Vた + が最後; Vたら + 最後
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing a habit or trait where crossing one threshold guarantees an unstoppable, usually undesirable, chain of events.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼はパソコンでゲームを始めたが最後、いつも朝までやり続ける。Kare wa pasokon de geemu o hajimeta ga saigo, itsumo asa made yari tsuzukeru. Once he starts playing a computer game, he plays all night. वह एक बार कंप्यूटर गेम शुरू कर दे, तो सुबह तक खेलता ही रहता है। તે એક વાર કમ્પ્યુટર ગેમ શરૂ કરે, તો સવાર સુધી રમતો જ રહે છે.
父は、いったん寝たら最後、朝まで起きない。Chichi wa, ittan netara saigo, asa made okinai. Once in bed, my father never wakes up until morning. पापा एक बार सो जाएँ, तो सुबह तक नहीं उठते। પપ્પા એક વાર સૂઈ જાય, તો સવાર સુધી ઊઠતા નથી.

最後 here doesn't literally mean "the end/last" in a temporal sense — it emphasizes "and that's it, no going back," describing a character trait (someone who, once they start X, can't stop).

【4】 〜（か）と思いきや

硬 formal/written
Reading~(ka) to omoikiya
Meaning (EN)"thought that ~, but contrary to expectations..." — sets up an expectation that is then unexpectedly overturned
Meaning (HI)सोचा था कि..., लेकिन उम्मीद के ख़िलाफ़...
Meaning (GU)વિચાર્યું હતું કે..., પણ અપેક્ષાની વિરુદ્ધ...
Connection (接続)V／N／[文]（plain） + （か）と思いきや
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useA wry, often self-deprecating "I thought X, but actually Y" twist.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
やっとテストが終わって休めると思いきや、宿題をたくさん出された。Yatto tesuto ga owatte yasumeru to omoikiya, shukudai o takusan dasareta. I thought I could have a break when the test period was over, but I've got tons of homework. सोचा था कि टेस्ट ख़त्म होकर आराम मिलेगा, लेकिन ढेर सारा होमवर्क दे दिया। વિચાર્યું હતું કે ટેસ્ટ પૂરી થઈને આરામ મળશે, પણ ઘણું બધું હોમવર્ક આપી દીધું.
宝くじに当たったかと思いきや、番号を見間違えていた。Takarakuji ni atatta ka to omoikiya, bangou o mimachigaete ita. I thought I won the lottery, but I got the number wrong. सोचा था लॉटरी लग गई, लेकिन नंबर ग़लत पढ़ लिया था। વિચાર્યું હતું લોટરી લાગી ગઈ, પણ નંબર ખોટો વાંચ્યો હતો.

Unlike Points 1–3 (all about instant timing), と思いきや is about a reversal of EXPECTATION, not speed — the twist, often ironic, is the whole point.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.55. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.57.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

あの人はいつも電車に乗り込む（　）、席を確保しようとする。Ano hito wa itsumo densha ni norikomu (___), seki o kakuho shiyou to suru.

Full sentence: あの人はいつも電車に乗り込むが早いか、席を確保しようとする。Ano hito wa itsumo densha ni norikomu ga hayai ka, seki o kakuho shiyou to suru.
EN: That person always tries to grab a seat the instant they board the train.
HI: वह इंसान हमेशा ट्रेन में चढ़ते ही, तुरंत सीट पकड़ने की कोशिश करता है।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિ હંમેશા ટ્રેનમાં ચઢતાં જ, તરત સીટ પકડવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aが最後ga saigoonce ~, inevitably (wrong — no unstoppable chain reaction here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bが早いかga hayai kathe instant, no sooner thanहोते ही, तुरंतથતાં જ, તરત

Why: Boarding immediately followed by grabbing a seat is a simple instant-timing pattern — Point 2's が早いか; が最後 requires an unstoppable ongoing consequence (like playing games all night), not a quick one-off reaction.

Q2

彼女は家に（　）や、いきなりトイレに駆け込んだ。Kanojo wa ie ni (___) ya, ikinari toire ni kakekonda.

Full sentence: 彼女は家に入るや、いきなりトイレに駆け込んだ。Kanojo wa ie ni hairu ya, ikinari toire ni kakekonda.
EN: The moment she entered the house, she rushed straight to the bathroom.
HI: वह घर में घुसते ही, सीधे बाथरूम की तरफ़ दौड़ी।
GU: તે ઘરમાં દાખલ થતાં જ, સીધી બાથરૂમ તરફ દોડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a入るhairuplain non-past (correct form before や)सही रूपસાચું સ્વરૂપ
b入ったhaitta(wrong — や needs the plain non-past, not the past)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 1's や（否や） always attaches to the plain non-past verb form, exactly like なり from Week 2 Day 1.

Q3

やっと富士山の頂上に着いた（　）、まだ8合目だった。Yatto Fujisan no choujou ni tsuita (___), mada hachigou-me datta.

Full sentence: やっと富士山の頂上に着いたと思いきや、まだ8合目だった。Yatto Fujisan no choujou ni tsuita to omoikiya, mada hachigou-me datta.
EN: I thought I'd finally reached the summit of Mt. Fuji, but it turned out I was only at the 8th station.
HI: सोचा था आख़िर फ़ूजी पहाड़ की चोटी पर पहुँच गया, लेकिन असल में अभी 8वाँ पड़ाव ही था।
GU: વિચાર્યું હતું આખરે ફૂજી પર્વતના શિખર પર પહોંચી ગયો, પણ ખરેખર હજુ 8મો પડાવ જ હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aと思いきやto omoikiyathought that..., but contrary to expectationsसोचा था..., लेकिन असल मेंવિચાર્યું હતું..., પણ ખરેખર
bが早いかga hayai kathe instant (wrong — no second immediate action, just a reversed expectation)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A mistaken expectation (thought summit, was actually only 8th station) is Point 4's と思いきや; が早いか needs a fast follow-up action, not a corrected belief.

Q4

119番の通報を受ける（　）、救急車は出動した。Hyaku-juu-kyuu-ban no tsuuhou o ukeru (___), kyuukyuusha wa shutsudou shita.

Full sentence: 119番の通報を受けるが早いか、救急車は出動した。Hyaku-juu-kyuu-ban no tsuuhou o ukeru ga hayai ka, kyuukyuusha wa shutsudou shita.
EN: No sooner had the emergency call come in than the ambulance was dispatched.
HI: 119 नंबर पर कॉल आते ही, एम्बुलेंस निकल पड़ी।
GU: 119 નંબર પર કૉલ આવતાં જ, એમ્બ્યુલન્સ નીકળી પડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aが最後ga saigoonce ~, inevitably (wrong — no ongoing unstoppable chain)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

彼は普段はとてもおとなしいが、ひとたび（　）暴れて手がつけられなくなる。Kare wa fudan wa totemo otonashii ga, hitotabi (___) abarete te ga tsukerarenaku naru.

Full sentence: 彼は普段はとてもおとなしいが、ひとたび怒ったら最後、暴れて手がつけられなくなる。Kare wa fudan wa totemo otonashii ga, hitotabi okottara saigo, abarete te ga tsukerarenaku naru.
EN: He's usually very calm, but once he gets angry, there's no stopping him — he goes wild.
HI: वह आम तौर पर बहुत शांत रहता है, लेकिन एक बार गुस्सा आ जाए तो, फिर उसे रोकना नामुमकिन हो जाता है।
GU: તે સામાન્ય રીતે ખૂબ શાંત રહે છે, પણ એક વાર ગુસ્સો આવે તો, પછી તેને રોકવો અશક્ય બની જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a怒ったら最後okottara saigoonce he gets angry, inevitably (unstoppable)एक बार गुस्सा आए तो फिरએક વાર ગુસ્સો આવે તો પછી
b怒るが早いかokoru ga hayai kathe instant he gets angry (wrong — misses the ongoing unstoppable consequence)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

年賀状の最後の1枚を＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、20枚も残っていた。Nengajou no saigo no ichi-mai o ___ , nijuu-mai mo nokotte ita.

Tiles: 1 かと (ka to)　2 書き終わった (kakiowatta)　3 まだ (mada)　4 思いきや (omoikiya)

2 書き終わった→1 かと→4 思いきや→3 まだ

年賀状の最後の1枚を書き終わったかと思いきやまだ20枚も残っていた。Nengajou no saigo no ichi-mai o kakiowatta ka to omoikiya mada nijuu-mai mo nokotte ita.

EN: I thought I'd finished writing the last New Year's card, but there were still 20 left.
HI: सोचा था नए साल का आख़िरी कार्ड लिख लिया, लेकिन अभी भी 20 बचे हुए थे।
GU: વિચાર્યું હતું નવા વર્ષનું છેલ્લું કાર્ડ લખાઈ ગયું, પણ હજુ 20 બાકી હતા.

Why: Built on Point 4's （か）と思いきや — a mistaken belief the task was finished, immediately corrected.

Q7

彼のブログは、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ が相次ぐ。Kare no burogu wa, ___ ga aitsugu.

Tiles: 1 更新 (koushin)　2 反響 (hankyou)　3 する (suru)　4 や (ya)

1 更新→3 する→4 や→2 反響

彼のブログは、更新するや反響が相次ぐ。Kare no burogu wa, koushin suru ya hankyou ga aitsugu.

EN: As soon as he updates his blog, reactions come pouring in one after another.
HI: उसका ब्लॉग अपडेट होते ही, प्रतिक्रियाएँ लगातार आने लगती हैं।
GU: તેનો બ્લોગ અપડેટ થતાં જ, પ્રતિભાવો સતત આવવા લાગે છે.

Why: Built on Point 1's や（否や） — the blog update is followed almost instantly by a flood of reactions.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three "instant timing" patterns plus one "reversed expectation" pattern — the key is whether a fast reaction, an unstoppable habit, or a corrected belief is being described:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜や（否や）~ya (ina ya) The moment ~ Vる（plain non-past） + や（否や） [硬] Formal, literary instant-trigger timing. Written narratives, news-style writing.
〜が早いか~ga hayai ka No sooner than ~ Vる（plain non-past） + が早いか Same instant-trigger meaning, slightly more neutral register. Either speech or writing.
〜たが最後~ta ga saigo Once ~, inevitably (no return) Vた + が最後; Vたら + 最後 Not about speed — about an unstoppable ongoing consequence/habit. Describing a character trait or addictive pattern.
〜（か）と思いきや~(ka) to omoikiya Thought ~, but contrary to expectations V／N／[文]（plain） + （か）と思いきや [硬] Not about timing at all — a reversed/corrected expectation. An ironic or surprising twist on a belief.
Exam trap: や（否や）／が早いか vs たが最後 both look like "the moment X" but differ in what follows: や／が早いか is a single, quick reaction (Q1–Q4 above), while たが最後 describes an ongoing, unstoppable habit that continues indefinitely (Q5). Ask: does the sentence describe a brief one-time reaction, or an open-ended chain that "never stops until morning"?
