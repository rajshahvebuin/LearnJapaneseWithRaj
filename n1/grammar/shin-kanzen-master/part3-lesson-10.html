Notes
Japanese texts drop anything the reader can recover — above all a repeated topic 〜は. This lesson teaches when you may drop it (same topic, clearly identifiable) and when you must not (the actor changes, the referent is far away or ambiguous, the word now means something slightly different, the sentence says what the subject is, or the topic returns after a digression). 練習1 is an unusual format: put （　） round each underlined word that can be omitted; the 別冊 writes 省略できない for those that can't. 練習2 and まとめ are essay/memoir extracts, so per the site rule no passage is reproduced: only the sentence (or clause) containing each blank is quoted, with a 2–3 sentence summary in my own words.
Book introduction (p.156): 省略とは、文章の流れの中で、何を指しているかはっきりわかっている語を後に続く文では言わないことです。Shouryaku to wa, bunshou no nagare no naka de, nani o sashite iru ka hakkiri wakatte iru go o ato ni tsuzuku bun de wa iwanai koto desu.
EN: Omission (省略) means not saying again, in the following sentences, a word whose referent is already clear from the flow of the text. It removes wasted words and makes the text hang together. Re-expressing a word from the previous sentence with a related word is another way of creating cohesion.
HI: लोप (省略) का अर्थ है — जिस शब्द का संदर्भ लेख के प्रवाह से पहले ही साफ़ है, उसे आगे के वाक्यों में दोबारा न कहना। इससे बेकार शब्द हटते हैं और लेख एकजुट बनता है। पिछले वाक्य के शब्द को किसी संबंधित शब्द से दोबारा व्यक्त करना भी एकजुटता का एक तरीका है।
GU: લોપ (省略) એટલે — જે શબ્દનો સંદર્ભ લખાણના પ્રવાહથી પહેલેથી સ્પષ્ટ છે, તેને પછીનાં વાક્યોમાં ફરી ન બોલવો. તેનાથી નકામા શબ્દો દૂર થાય છે અને લખાણ એકસૂત્ર બને છે. આગલા વાક્યના શબ્દને સંબંધિત શબ્દથી ફરી વ્યક્ત કરવો એ પણ એકસૂત્રતાનો એક રસ્તો છે.

1 Grammar Points

One block per explanation section (A-1 … C-2). These are rules about whether to say a word again, not grammar forms, so there is no formation table. In the 例, the first mention (solid underline in the book) is bold; the omitted/repeated word (dotted underline) is shown in （　） or underlined.

【A-1】 前の文に出てきて、後の文で繰り返しになるとき — omit a repeated, identifiable word

omit
SectionA 省略されやすい場合 — 1.Shouryaku sare yasui baai
Rule (book)前の文に出てきて、後の文で繰り返しになるとき（省略された語が特定できる場合）Mae no bun ni dete kite, ato no bun de kurikaeshi ni naru toki (shouryaku sareta go ga tokutei dekiru baai)
Rule (EN)When a word from the previous sentence would be repeated in the next one, and the reader can identify it, it is usually dropped — whether it is the object (〜を) or the topic (〜は).
Rule (HI)जब पिछले वाक्य का शब्द अगले वाक्य में दोहराया जाना हो और पाठक उसे पहचान सके, तो उसे आमतौर पर छोड़ दिया जाता है — चाहे वह कर्म (〜を) हो या विषय (〜は)।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે આગલા વાક્યનો શબ્દ પછીના વાક્યમાં ફરી આવવાનો હોય અને વાચક તેને ઓળખી શકે, ત્યારે તે સામાન્ય રીતે છોડી દેવાય છે — ભલે તે કર્મ (〜を) હોય કે વિષય (〜は).
Typical useEssays where one keyword runs through several sentences.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
少子高齢化が取り上げられるようになって久しい。政策を立てる際にも（少子高齢化を）無視できない。（少子高齢化は）今や時代のキーワードなのである。Shoushi koureika ga toriagerareru you ni natte hisashii. Seisaku o tateru sai ni mo (shoushi koureika o) mushi dekinai. (Shoushi koureika wa) ima ya jidai no kiiwaado na no de aru. It's been a long time since the falling birthrate and ageing population became a talking point. It can't be ignored when making policy either. It is now the keyword of our age. घटती जन्म-दर और बढ़ती बुज़ुर्ग आबादी को चर्चा में आए काफ़ी समय हो गया। नीतियाँ बनाते समय भी (इसे) नज़रअंदाज़ नहीं किया जा सकता। (यह) अब इस युग का मुख्य शब्द है। ઘટતો જન્મદર અને વધતી વૃદ્ધ વસ્તી ચર્ચામાં આવ્યાને ઘણો સમય થયો. નીતિ ઘડતી વખતે પણ (તેને) અવગણી શકાતું નથી. (તે) હવે આ યુગનો મુખ્ય શબ્દ છે.

English needs "it" each time; Japanese simply leaves the slot empty. Hindi/Gujarati can also drop यह／તે, which makes this rule fairly natural for you.

【A-2】 前の文と後の文の主題が同じとき — same topic → omit 〜は

omit
SectionA-2.Mae no bun to ato no bun no shudai ga onaji toki
Rule (book)前の文と後の文の主題（「〜は」などで表される）が同じときMae no bun to ato no bun no shudai ("~wa" nado de arawasareru) ga onaji toki
Rule (EN)If the topic (marked by 〜は etc.) stays the same in the next sentence, it is left out.
Rule (HI)अगर अगले वाक्य में भी विषय (〜は आदि से चिह्नित) वही रहे, तो उसे छोड़ दिया जाता है।
Rule (GU)જો પછીના વાક્યમાં પણ વિષય (〜は વગેરેથી દર્શાવેલો) એ જ રહે, તો તેને છોડી દેવાય છે.
Typical useDescribing one city, person, company over several sentences.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この市はバスが住民の主な移動手段である。さらに（この市は）近隣の町や村からのバス路線も充実している。Kono shi wa basu ga juumin no omo na idou shudan de aru. Sara ni (kono shi wa) kinrin no machi ya mura kara no basu rosen mo juujitsu shite iru. In this city, buses are residents' main means of transport. What's more, it has plenty of bus routes from neighbouring towns and villages. इस शहर में बसें निवासियों का मुख्य परिवहन साधन हैं। साथ ही, (यहाँ) आसपास के क़स्बों-गाँवों से बस मार्ग भी भरपूर हैं। આ શહેરમાં બસ રહેવાસીઓનું મુખ્ય વાહનવ્યવહાર સાધન છે. વળી, (અહીં) આસપાસનાં નગરો-ગામોમાંથી બસ માર્ગ પણ ભરપૂર છે.
うちの子はM社に珍しい図鑑を送ってもらった。（うちの子は）自分で注文して楽しみに待っていたようだ。Uchi no ko wa M-sha ni mezurashii zukan o okutte moratta. (Uchi no ko wa) jibun de chuumon shite tanoshimi ni matte ita you da. My child had a rare illustrated guide sent by Company M. It seems he'd ordered it himself and was looking forward to it. मेरे बच्चे को M कंपनी से एक दुर्लभ सचित्र पुस्तक मिली। लगता है (उसने) ख़ुद ऑर्डर की थी और बेसब्री से इंतज़ार कर रहा था। મારા બાળકને M કંપની પાસેથી એક દુર્લભ સચિત્ર પુસ્તક મળ્યું. લાગે છે કે (તેણે) જાતે ઑર્ડર કર્યું હતું અને આતુરતાથી રાહ જોતો હતો.

Note the second example: because the first sentence uses 〜てもらった, うちの子 stays the topic and viewpoint, so it can be dropped. Compare B-1, where the same facts are written with M社が…くれた.

【A-3】 前の文に出てきた言葉を「は」で受けるとき：主題化 — topicalising a word from the previous sentence

omit
SectionA-3. 主題化Shudaika
Rule (book)前の文に出てきた言葉を、「は」で受けるとき：主題化Mae no bun ni dete kita kotoba o, "wa" de ukeru toki: shudaika
Rule (EN)When a non-topic word in the previous sentence (here 1945年) becomes the topic of the next one ("1945 was…"), that 〜は can be omitted.
Rule (HI)जब पिछले वाक्य का कोई शब्द (यहाँ 1945) अगले वाक्य का विषय बनता है, तो वह 〜は छोड़ा जा सकता है।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે આગલા વાક્યનો કોઈ શબ્દ (અહીં 1945) પછીના વાક્યનો વિષય બને, ત્યારે તે 〜は છોડી શકાય છે.
Typical useAdding an explanation about a date, place or term just mentioned.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中氏は1945年の生まれである。（1945年は）日本で戦争が終結した年である。Tanaka-shi wa sen kyuuhyaku yonjuugo-nen no umare de aru. (Sen kyuuhyaku yonjuugo-nen wa) Nihon de sensou ga shuuketsu shita toshi de aru. Mr Tanaka was born in 1945 — the year the war ended in Japan. तानाका जी का जन्म 1945 में हुआ। (वह) वर्ष जापान में युद्ध समाप्त होने का साल है। તાનાકાજીનો જન્મ 1945માં થયો. (એ) વર્ષ જાપાનમાં યુદ્ધ પૂરું થયાનું વર્ષ છે.

The omitted topic is not 田中氏 here — the reader works out from 年である that the second sentence is about the year. That's why the topic still counts as "identifiable".

【B-1】 注目する行為の主体が前の文とは違うとき — the actor changes → keep it

do not omit
SectionB 省略されない場合 — 1.Shouryaku sarenai baai
Rule (book)注目する行為の主体が前の文とは違うときChuumoku suru koui no shutai ga mae no bun to wa chigau toki
Rule (EN)If the person doing the action we focus on is different from the previous sentence's subject, it must be stated.
Rule (HI)अगर जिस क्रिया पर ध्यान है उसका कर्ता पिछले वाक्य के कर्ता से अलग है, तो उसे बताना ज़रूरी है।
Rule (GU)જે ક્રિયા પર ધ્યાન છે તેનો કર્તા આગલા વાક્યના કર્તાથી જુદો હોય, તો તેને જણાવવો જરૂરી છે.
Typical useSentence 1 has subject X acting on Y; sentence 2 is about Y's reaction.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
M社がうちの子に珍しい図鑑を送ってくれた。うちの子は飛び上がって喜んだ。M-sha ga uchi no ko ni mezurashii zukan o okutte kureta. Uchi no ko wa tobiagatte yorokonda. Company M sent my child a rare illustrated guide. My child jumped for joy. M कंपनी ने मेरे बच्चे को एक दुर्लभ सचित्र पुस्तक भेजी। मेरा बच्चा ख़ुशी से उछल पड़ा। M કંપનીએ મારા બાળકને એક દુર્લભ સચિત્ર પુસ્તક મોકલ્યું. મારું બાળક ખુશીથી ઊછળી પડ્યું.

The first subject is M社; the person who jumped is a different actor, so うちの子は is needed (without it, M社 would seem to jump). Compare A-2.

【B-2】 少し離れたときや、どの言葉の省略かわかりにくいとき — far away or ambiguous → keep it

do not omit
SectionB-2.Sugu mae no bun ni dete kuru no de wa naku, sukoshi hanareta toki ya, aida ni fukusuu no kotoba ga atte, dono kotoba no shouryaku na no ka wakarinikui toki
Rule (book)すぐ前の文に出てくるのではなく、少し離れたときや、間に複数の言葉があって、どの言葉の省略なのかわかりにくいとき(see above)
Rule (EN)If the word appeared not in the sentence just before but further back, or there are several candidates in between so it's unclear which one was dropped, say it.
Rule (HI)अगर शब्द ठीक पिछले वाक्य में नहीं बल्कि थोड़ा पहले आया हो, या बीच में कई शब्द हों जिससे साफ़ न हो कि कौन-सा छोड़ा गया, तो उसे बोलना चाहिए।
Rule (GU)જો શબ્દ તરત આગલા વાક્યમાં નહીં પણ થોડો પહેલાં આવ્યો હોય, અથવા વચ્ચે ઘણા શબ્દો હોય જેથી સ્પષ્ટ ન થાય કે કયો છોડાયો, તો તે બોલવો જોઈએ.
Typical useTwo companies, two people in one sentence.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
A社がB社に製品の部品を注文した。B社は（または、A社は）納入期日に間に合うかどうか心配だった。A-sha ga B-sha ni seihin no buhin o chuumon shita. B-sha wa (mata wa, A-sha wa) nounyuu kijitsu ni maniau ka dou ka shinpai datta. Company A ordered product parts from Company B. Company B (or Company A) was worried whether the delivery date would be met. A कंपनी ने B कंपनी से उत्पाद के पुर्ज़े मँगवाए। B कंपनी (या A कंपनी) को चिंता थी कि डिलीवरी की तारीख़ पर पहुँचेगा या नहीं। A કંપનીએ B કંપની પાસેથી ઉત્પાદનના ભાગ મંગાવ્યા. B કંપનીને (અથવા A કંપનીને) ચિંતા હતી કે ડિલિવરીની તારીખ સુધી પહોંચશે કે નહીં.

Either company could be worried, so the topic must be named — the book shows both readings are possible.

【B-3】 後の文で主題になり、省略すると特定がしにくいとき — new topic that can't be identified if dropped

do not omit
SectionB-3.Mae no bun ni dete kita kotoba de, ato no bun de shudai ni nari, shouryaku suru to tokutei ga shinikui toki
Rule (book)前の文に出てきた言葉で、後の文で主題になり、省略すると特定がしにくいとき(see above)
Rule (EN)When a word from the previous sentence becomes the topic of the next, but dropping it would leave the reader unsure what the topic is, keep it.
Rule (HI)जब पिछले वाक्य का कोई शब्द अगले वाक्य का विषय बने, पर उसे छोड़ने से पाठक को पता न चले कि विषय क्या है, तो उसे रखें।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે આગલા વાક્યનો કોઈ શબ્દ પછીના વાક્યનો વિષય બને, પણ તેને છોડવાથી વાચકને ખબર ન પડે કે વિષય શું છે, તો તેને રાખો.
Typical useShifting from "this shop" to "Italian restaurants in general" and back.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ここは「ミラノ」という店である。（ここは）昨年オープンしたイタリアンレストランである。イタリアンレストランはこの町に4店あるが、ここが一番雰囲気がいい。Koko wa "Mirano" to iu mise de aru. (Koko wa) sakunen oopun shita itarian resutoran de aru. Itarian resutoran wa kono machi ni yon-ten aru ga, koko ga ichiban fun'iki ga ii. This is a shop called "Milano". It's an Italian restaurant that opened last year. There are four Italian restaurants in this town, but this one has the best atmosphere. यह "मिलानो" नाम की दुकान है। (यह) पिछले साल खुला इतालवी रेस्टोरेंट है। इस शहर में चार इतालवी रेस्टोरेंट हैं, पर माहौल सबसे अच्छा यहीं का है। આ "મિલાનો" નામની દુકાન છે. (આ) ગયા વર્ષે ખુલેલું ઇટાલિયન રેસ્ટોરન્ટ છે. આ શહેરમાં ચાર ઇટાલિયન રેસ્ટોરન્ટ છે, પણ વાતાવરણ સૌથી સારું અહીંનું છે.

The second (ここは) can go (same topic, A-2), but イタリアンレストランは introduces a new, wider topic and ここが picks out one member of it — both must stay.

【B-4】 同じ言葉だが、指しているものが微妙に違うとき — same word, slightly different referent

do not omit
SectionB-4.Mae no bunmyaku de dete kita kotoba to onaji kotoba da ga, sashite iru mono ga bimyou ni chigau toki
Rule (book)前の文脈で出てきた言葉と同じ言葉だが、指しているものが微妙に違うとき(see above)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The decision this lesson trains: drop it, repeat it, or reword it? Ask (1) is the referent still unique and nearby? (2) did the actor or the meaning change? (3) is this a "what X is" sentence after an action sentence?

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
（X は）omittedX wa — shouryaku same topic continues A-1・A-2・A-3 Only one candidate, mentioned just before (老人, この論文, ストックホルム, 魚). Descriptions of one person/thing over consecutive sentences.
X は kept (actor change)X wa — kept new actor B-1 Previous subject ≠ this subject (M社がくれた → うちの子は喜んだ; 母にはスリッパ → 子どもは靴下). Reactions, contrasts between people.
X は kept (ambiguity)X wa — kept two or more candidates B-2・B-3 A社／B社, 渡辺さん／山田さん, 秋田／山梨; or X was only a modifier before (不景気のため). Whenever "who?" could have two answers.
X は kept (referent shift)X wa — kept same word, different thing B-4 桜 = Somei-Yoshino → 桜 = cherry trees in general; 精神的ケア (so far) ≠ 今回のケア. "X existed before, but this X…"
action → identity vs identity → actionB-5 order matters 〜を行った。X は〜である。 Action first, then 〜である: repeat X. Identity first, then action: X may be dropped. News/profile writing (佐藤氏, 山田氏).
繰り返し vs 言い換えkurikaeshi vs iikae repeat vs reword C-1・C-2 Repeat the word or part of it (TAK研究所 → 研究所); or use a synonym/broader word (グラウンドゴルフ → このスポーツ; お辞儀 = 頭を下げる; 看護 ⊂ 家庭看護). Tracking keywords in a passage cloze.
Exam trap 1 — "who is the subject?" blanks: when the options are 母は／子どもは／みんなは／自分たちは, look for an actor change (B-1). The word right before the blank (here 母には…が) usually tells you the new subject must be someone else.
Exam trap 2 — rewording tracks the answer: passages often name a thing once and later refer to it with a related noun (演説, 念願, このスポーツ). When an option repeats such a noun (父の演説), check whether the passage introduced it earlier.
Exam trap 3 — 変わる vs 変化する (added, not in book): for changing schools/jobs/addresses use 変わる (学校が変わる, 住所が変わる). 変化する is for gradual changes in quality or state (気温が変化する).
