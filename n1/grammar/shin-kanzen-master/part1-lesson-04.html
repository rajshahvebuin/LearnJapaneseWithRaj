Notes
Four paired listing forms, all taking examples from the same meaning group. The exam tests the second clause: suggestion (なり), general rule (であれ), evaluation (といい) or widespread state (といわず), each with its own ban list. None is tagged 硬い言い方 in the book. 〜であれ is cross-referenced to 10課【3】. The book's drills use 3 options (a–c) instead of the exam's 4.
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.20): the everyday ways to list examples ("…and… and such", "whether you call it… or…", "whether it's… or…"). Today's four N1 pairs are all X〜 Y〜 listing forms too — the exam tests what kind of sentence may follow each.

Not to be confused with 1課's 〜なり ("as soon as"). Here なり…なり means "X or Y — whichever", and it is a suggestion tool: every example ends in advice, a request or an invitation. That's the whole of drill [1]: eliminate past tense (整理した, 行きました) and options that demand a specific choice (どちらで知らせますか). With verbs, finish with する: 〜なり〜なりする.

【2】 〜であれ…であれ・〜であろうと…であろうと

neutral register (written feel)
Reading~de are … de are / ~de arou to … de arou to
Meaning (EN)"whether it's ~ or …, (the same applies)" — book: 〜でも…でも関係なく、同じ種類のものにはみんな同じことが言える (regardless of ~ or …, the same can be said of everything of that kind). Cross-ref → 10課【3】
Meaning (HI)चाहे ... हो या ..., (सब पर एक ही बात लागू)
Meaning (GU)ભલે ... હોય કે ..., (બધાં પર એક જ વાત લાગુ)
Connection (接続)名 ＋であれ
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; であれ／であろうと come from the written copula である and sound formal.
Typical useLists examples from the same meaning group — not unrelated, scattered examples (関係ないばらばらの例ではなく). The second clause says the same holds for all of them.

地震であれ火事であれ、緊急の場合に冷静になれる人は少ないだろう。Jishin de are kaji de are, kinkyuu no baai ni reisei ni nareru hito wa sukunai darou. Whether it's an earthquake or a fire, few people can stay calm in an emergency. चाहे भूकंप हो या आग, आपात स्थिति में शांत रह पाने वाले लोग कम ही होंगे। ભલે ભૂકંપ હોય કે આગ, કટોકટીમાં શાંત રહી શકે એવા લોકો ઓછા જ હશે.
文学であれ音楽であれ、芸術には才能が必要なのだ。努力だけではだめなのだ。Bungaku de are ongaku de are, geijutsu ni wa sainou ga hitsuyou na no da. Doryoku dake de wa dame na no da. Be it literature or music, art requires talent. Effort alone won't do. चाहे साहित्य हो या संगीत, कला के लिए प्रतिभा ज़रूरी है। सिर्फ़ मेहनत से काम नहीं चलता। ભલે સાહિત્ય હોય કે સંગીત, કળા માટે પ્રતિભા જરૂરી છે. ફક્ત મહેનતથી કામ ન ચાલે.
禁煙であれ禁酒であれ、周りの人の協力が大切だと思う。Kin'en de are kinshu de are, mawari no hito no kyouryoku ga taisetsu da to omou. Whether quitting smoking or quitting drinking, I think the support of those around you is important. चाहे सिगरेट छोड़नी हो या शराब, मुझे लगता है आसपास के लोगों का साथ ज़रूरी है। ભલે સિગારેટ છોડવી હોય કે દારૂ, મને લાગે છે કે આસપાસના લોકોનો સાથ જરૂરી છે.
ビールであろうとワインであろうと、酒は酒だ。運転前に絶対飲んではいけない。Biiru de arou to wain de arou to, sake wa sake da. Unten mae ni zettai nonde wa ikenai. Beer or wine, alcohol is alcohol. You must never drink before driving. बियर हो या वाइन, शराब तो शराब है। गाड़ी चलाने से पहले बिल्कुल नहीं पीनी चाहिए। બિયર હોય કે વાઇન, દારૂ એટલે દારૂ. ગાડી ચલાવતાં પહેલાં બિલકુલ ન પીવો જોઈએ.

The two nouns are members of one group — often its two ends (社長 / 平社員; 与党 / 野党) — and the main clause is a general truth that holds no matter which. Unlike といい and といわず, it can end with a command, prohibition or wish (④ 飲んではいけない; drill [2] Q3 頑張ってほしい). What it can't do is ask "which one?" or describe a choice — that contradicts "it doesn't matter which".

この虫は色といい形といい、木の葉にそっくりだ。Kono mushi wa iro to ii katachi to ii, ko no ha ni sokkuri da. In both colour and shape, this insect looks exactly like a leaf. रंग हो या आकार, यह कीड़ा बिल्कुल पेड़ के पत्ते जैसा है। રંગ હોય કે આકાર, આ જંતુ બિલકુલ ઝાડના પાંદડા જેવું છે.

といい is for judging something by looking at it from a couple of angles, then giving your verdict (最高だ, 面白い, そっくりだ). Since it states a verdict, the sentence can't turn into a request or plan, and it can't be a one-off event (テレビで紹介された). It attaches to nouns — so with adjectives use the noun form (美しさ, 素晴らしさ).

Mr. Shimada, a salesman, is out working without a moment's rest — weekdays and weekends alike. सेल्समैन शिमादा जी, चाहे हफ़्ते के दिन हों या सप्ताहांत, बिना आराम किए दफ़्तर से बाहर काम करते रहते हैं। સેલ્સમૅન શિમાદાજી, અઠવાડિયાના દિવસો હોય કે શનિ-રવિ, આરામ કર્યા વિના ઑફિસની બહાર કામ કરતા રહે છે.

といわず literally means "without saying (it's) ~" — i.e. without distinguishing. The two nouns are neighbours in space or time (壁/天井, 昼/夜, 春/秋) and together mean "everywhere / all the time" (全身, 家中, 一年中). Compared with といい it's not an evaluation but a description of how widespread something is, and it can take a plain verb sentence (捕まえたがる, 訪れる). Ban list: negatives (×車は通れない) and requests/commands (×行ってみてください).

あの部屋はかなり寒いから、長い時間いるなら上着なり（　）なりが必要だろう。Ano heya wa kanari samui kara, nagai jikan iru nara uwagi nari (___) nari ga hitsuyou darou.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms are "X〜 Y〜" pairs that list examples from one group, and the mixed set [1〜4] puts the same two nouns into each form. The deciding factor is always what kind of sentence follows:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜なり…なり~nari … nari ~ or … (anything will do) N(+助詞)／V-辞書形 + なり Avoids choosing; must end in advice / wish / request / invitation. No past tense. Suggesting options: 電話なりメールなり連絡してください.
〜であれ…であれ・〜であろうと…であろうと~de are … de are / ~de arou to … de arou to Whether ~ or …, the same applies N + であれ／であろうと General truth regardless of which; members of one group (often its two ends). Can end in a command or wish. Principles: 社長であろうと平社員であろうと…; 与党であれ野党であれ頑張ってほしい.
〜といい…といい~to ii … to ii Both ~ and … (verdict) N + といい Looks at two aspects → evaluation (adjective sentence). No requests/wishes/intentions. Praising or criticising: 味といい盛り付けといい素晴らしい.
〜といわず…といわず~to iwazu … to iwazu ~ and … alike — everywhere / always N + といわず Space/time neighbours (昼/夜, 壁/天井); describes how widespread; often negative; verb sentences OK. No negatives, no requests. "All over / all the time": 手といわず足といわず砂だらけ.
〜やら…やら (N2)~yara … yara ~ and … and such (messy list) N／V + やら Plain N2 listing, often of a chaotic mix. Describing clutter or mixed feelings.
〜にしても…にしても (N2)~ni shite mo … ni shite mo Whether ~ or … N／V + にしても Everyday equivalent of であれ…であれ. Conversation.
Exam trap 1 — the sentence end decides: advice/request → なり…なり; general rule (even a command) → であれ…であれ; evaluation adjective → といい…といい; "everywhere/always" state or habitual verb → といわず…といわず.
Exam trap 2 — ban lists: なり…なり: no past tense. といい: no wish/intention/request. といわず: no negative sentence, no request. A request at the end eliminates といい and といわず at once ([3] Q2, [4] Q2, [1〜4] Q2).
Exam trap 3 — 2 × なり: 4課 〜なり…なり ("X or Y") is not 1課 〜なり ("as soon as"). The paired shape and noun connection give it away.
