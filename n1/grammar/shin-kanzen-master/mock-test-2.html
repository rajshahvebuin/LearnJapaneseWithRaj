2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — 文法形式の判断 (items 1–10)

次の文の（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no (___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — "Choose the best option (1–4) for the blank." p.174–175. Answers confirmed from 別冊 p.7.

Q1

理由が（　）、いったん納入された会費は返却いたしかねます。Riyuu ga (___), ittan nounyuu sareta kaihi wa henkyaku itashikanemasu.

Full sentence: 理由が何であれ、いったん納入された会費は返却いたしかねます。Riyuu ga nan de are, ittan nounyuu sareta kaihi wa henkyaku itashikanemasu.
EN: Whatever the reason, we regret that membership fees cannot be refunded once they have been paid.
HI: कारण चाहे जो भी हो, एक बार जमा किया गया सदस्यता शुल्क हम वापस नहीं कर सकते।
GU: કારણ ગમે તે હોય, એક વાર ભરાયેલી સભ્યપદ ફી અમે પાછી આપી શકતા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1何であれnan de arewhatever it isचाहे जो भी होગમે તે હોય
2何でありnan de ari(broken) being what, and(ग़लत) क्या होकर(ખોટું) શું થઈને
3何であろうnan de arouwhat could it be (wondering)क्या हो सकता हैશું હોઈ શકે
4何であったかnan de atta kawhat it wasक्या थाશું હતું

Why: Question word + 〜であれ (10課【3】) = "no matter what ~ is", and the main clause stays the same (返却いたしかねます). 3) 何であろう alone is a musing question ("what might it be?"); the concessive form needs と: 何であろうと. 2) 〜であり is a written "is, and…", with no concessive meaning. 4) 何であったか is an embedded question ("what it was") and needs a verb like 知らない after it.

Q2

気に入っていた服だからと捨てるに（　）長い間しまっておいたら、かびが生えてしまった。Ki ni itte ita fuku da kara to suteru ni (___) nagai aida shimatte oitara, kabi ga haete shimatta.

Full sentence: 気に入っていた服だからと捨てるに忍びず長い間しまっておいたら、かびが生えてしまった。Ki ni itte ita fuku da kara to suteru ni shinobizu nagai aida shimatte oitara, kabi ga haete shimatta.
EN: They were clothes I'd loved, so I couldn't bear to throw them away and kept them stored for a long time — and they went mouldy.
HI: पसंदीदा कपड़े थे, इसलिए फेंकने का दिल नहीं किया और लंबे समय तक सँभालकर रखे, तो उनमें फफूँद लग गई।

1はばからずhabakarazuwithout hesitation / shamelesslyबेझिझकખચકાટ વગર
2忍びずshinobizuunable to bear toदिल न मानने सेમન ન માનતાં

自分の子どもを願いどおりの子にしたり、恋人の心を自分の意のままにしたりはできないし、そもそも［19-a］の心さえ［19-b］のままにならない。Jibun no kodomo o negai doori no ko ni shitari, koibito no kokoro o jibun no i no mama ni shitari wa dekinai shi, somosomo [19-a] no kokoro sae [19-b] no mama ni naranai.

Full sentence: …そもそも自分の心さえ自分のままにならない。… somosomo jibun no kokoro sae jibun no mama ni naranai.
EN: You can't make your child into the child you wished for, or bend your lover's heart to your will — in the first place, not even your own heart does as you want.

Q20

［20］、人間の願望には、自分を超えたものや力の存在を信じたい、ということも生じてくる。[20], ningen no ganbou ni wa, jibun o koeta mono ya chikara no sonzai o shinjitai, to iu koto mo shoujite kuru.

Full sentence: そんなわけで、人間の願望には、自分を超えたものや力の存在を信じたい、ということも生じてくる。Sonna wake de, ningen no ganbou ni wa, jibun o koeta mono ya chikara no sonzai o shinjitai, to iu koto mo shoujite kuru.
EN: And so, among human desires there also arises the wish to believe in the existence of things and powers beyond oneself.
HI: इसी वजह से इंसान की इच्छाओं में यह इच्छा भी पैदा होती है कि वह अपने से परे किसी चीज़ या शक्ति के अस्तित्व में विश्वास करे।
GU: આ કારણે માણસની ઇચ્છાઓમાં એવી ઇચ્છા પણ જન્મે છે કે પોતાનાથી પરે કોઈ વસ્તુ કે શક્તિના અસ્તિત્વમાં વિશ્વાસ કરે.

〜てくれる vs 〜てあげる (passage)~te kureru / ~te ageru someone does for us vs we do for someone V-て + くれる／あげる Direction of the favour: gods → us = くれる. 神様はそのとおりにしてくれるか怪しい (Q18).
そんなわけで vs そうだとしても (passage)sonna wake de / sou da to shite mo and so (result) vs even so (concession) sentence-initial Ask whether the new sentence follows from or goes against the previous one. そんなわけで、…信じたいということも生じてくる (Q20).
Exam trap 1 — positive/negative twins: 〜をもってしても／〜をもってすれば, なり得ない／ならないものでもない, 通えない／通える + ものでもない. These pairs differ only in whether the result is possible. Read the sentence end (変えられない, だろう) before you choose (Q6, Q7, Q10).
Exam trap 2 — ★ items built on 12課 and 10課: 〜とあって needs a plain form before it and a visible result after it (Q13); 〜ば〜で repeats the same adjective (Q14). Placing the grammar tile first and the repeated word next to it settles the order quickly.
Exam trap 3 — さえ and escalation: in a passage, そもそも…さえ always marks the extreme case of a list. If the list is "child, lover…", the extreme is oneself (自分の心さえ自分のままにならない, Q19). Choices that just repeat earlier items can't carry さえ.
