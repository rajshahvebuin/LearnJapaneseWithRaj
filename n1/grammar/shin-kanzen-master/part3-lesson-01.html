Notes
第3部 is passage grammar: no new forms, but rules for keeping a whole text consistent. This lesson's message: present form ≠ present time and past form ≠ past time. The tense of a noun-modifying clause is decided by its relation to the main clause, or is fixed present for nouns like 予定・心配・可能性. Scan quirk: in 練習2 passage 1 the blank (選ぶ→　)ば is printed without a circled number, but the 別冊 counts it as ③ — so that passage has ①–⑥, not ①–⑤. 練習 items are 2-option (a・b) or write-ins; まとめ is the exam's 4-option passage cloze.
Book introduction (p.120): 文章としてのまとまりを持たせるためには、時間の流れに矛盾がないように文を続ける必要があります。…現在形・過去形が必ずしも現在のこと・過去のことを表すわけではありません。Bunshou to shite no matomari o motaseru tame ni wa, jikan no nagare ni mujun ga nai you ni bun o tsuzukeru hitsuyou ga arimasu. … Genzaikei・kakokei ga kanarazushimo genzai no koto・kako no koto o arawasu wake de wa arimasen.
EN: To make a text hang together, sentences must follow on without contradicting the flow of time; you must also make clear whether you mean an event at one point in time or a state over a span of time. The present form does not always mean the present, nor the past form the past.
HI: किसी लेख को एकजुट बनाने के लिए, वाक्यों को इस तरह आगे बढ़ाना ज़रूरी है कि समय के प्रवाह में कोई विरोधाभास न हो; यह भी साफ़ होना चाहिए कि आप किसी एक क्षण की घटना की बात कर रहे हैं या किसी समय-अवधि की स्थिति की। वर्तमान रूप हमेशा वर्तमान और भूत रूप हमेशा भूतकाल नहीं दर्शाता।
GU: લખાણને એકસૂત્ર બનાવવા માટે, વાક્યો એવી રીતે આગળ વધારવાં જરૂરી છે કે સમયના પ્રવાહમાં કોઈ વિરોધાભાસ ન રહે; એ પણ સ્પષ્ટ કરવું જોઈએ કે તમે કોઈ એક ક્ષણની ઘટનાની વાત કરો છો કે કોઈ સમયગાળાની સ્થિતિની. વર્તમાન રૂપ હંમેશાં વર્તમાન અને ભૂત રૂપ હંમેશાં ભૂતકાળ દર્શાવતું નથી.

1 Grammar Points

One block per explanation section of the book (A1–D3). These sections describe uses of tense, not new grammar forms, so there is no formation table — the forms are ordinary る／た／ている／ていた. The underlined/boxed parts of each 例 are shown in bold.

【A-1】 歴史的な記録 — present form for historical records

written (年表・history texts)
SectionA 現在形の文の特別な用法 — 1. 歴史的な記録Genzaikei no bun no tokubetsu na youhou — rekishiteki na kiroku
Rule (book)過去の事実でも歴史的な記録の場合は現在形を使うことがあります。Kako no jijitsu demo rekishiteki na kiroku no baai wa genzaikei o tsukau koto ga arimasu.
Rule (EN)Even for past facts, a historical record may use the present (dictionary) form — the "historical present".
Rule (HI)भूतकाल के तथ्य होने पर भी, ऐतिहासिक विवरण में कभी-कभी वर्तमान रूप का प्रयोग होता है।
Rule (GU)ભૂતકાળની હકીકત હોવા છતાં, ઐતિહાસિક નોંધમાં ક્યારેક વર્તમાન રૂપ વપરાય છે.
Typical useChronologies, textbooks, encyclopedia-style statements with a year or century attached.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1868年、明治時代が始まる。Sen happyaku rokujuuhachi-nen, Meiji jidai ga hajimaru. 1868: the Meiji era begins. 1868 में, मेइजी काल शुरू होता है। 1868માં, મેઇજી યુગ શરૂ થાય છે.
16〜18世紀のヨーロッパ諸国では、国王が専制政治を行う絶対君主制が確立する。Juuroku kara juuhasseiki no Yooroppa shokoku de wa, kokuou ga sensei seiji o okonau zettai kunshusei ga kakuritsu suru. In 16th–18th-century European states, absolute monarchy, in which the king rules as a despot, becomes established. 16वीं–18वीं सदी के यूरोपीय देशों में, राजा द्वारा निरंकुश शासन चलाने वाली पूर्ण राजतंत्र व्यवस्था स्थापित होती है। 16મી–18મી સદીના યુરોપિયન દેશોમાં, રાજા દ્વારા આપખુદ શાસન ચલાવતી સંપૂર્ણ રાજાશાહી સ્થાપિત થાય છે.

English does the same in timelines ("1868 — Meiji era begins"). In a normal narrative you would write 始まった; the present form signals "this is a record", not storytelling.

【A-2】 その場にいるような感じを出す効果 — the vivid present in narratives

literary (novels, essays)
SectionA-2. その場にいるような感じを出す効果Sono ba ni iru you na kanji o dasu kouka
Rule (book)小説などの描写文（過去形の文章）で、現在形の文を使ってその場にいるような感じを出すことがあります。視点が過去のその場面に移動します。Shousetsu nado no byoushabun (kakokei no bunshou) de, genzaikei no bun o tsukatte sono ba ni iru you na kanji o dasu koto ga arimasu. Shiten ga kako no sono bamen ni idou shimasu.
Rule (EN)In a descriptive passage written in the past (novels etc.), present-form sentences can be mixed in to make the reader feel present at the scene; the viewpoint moves into that past moment.
Rule (HI)उपन्यास जैसे भूतकाल में लिखे वर्णन में, बीच में वर्तमान रूप के वाक्य डालकर पाठक को ऐसा अनुभव कराया जाता है जैसे वह उसी जगह मौजूद हो; दृष्टिकोण उस भूतकालीन दृश्य में पहुँच जाता है।
Rule (GU)નવલકથા જેવા ભૂતકાળમાં લખાયેલા વર્ણનમાં, વચ્ચે વર્તમાન રૂપનાં વાક્યો મૂકીને વાચકને એવો અનુભવ કરાવાય છે કે જાણે તે ત્યાં જ હાજર હોય; દૃષ્ટિબિંદુ તે ભૂતકાળના દૃશ્યમાં પહોંચી જાય છે.
Typical usePast-tense frame sentences (見た・入ってきた・ついた) with present-tense "camera" sentences inside.

マキは外を見た。雪が降っている。間もなく日が暮れる。今日は家にいようと思い直した。Maki wa soto o mita. Yuki ga futte iru. Mamonaku hi ga kureru. Kyou wa ie ni iyou to omoinaoshita. Maki looked outside. Snow is falling. Soon the sun will set. She changed her mind and decided to stay home today. माकी ने बाहर देखा। बर्फ़ गिर रही है। जल्द ही सूरज ढल जाएगा। उसने फिर सोचा और आज घर पर ही रहने का तय किया। માકીએ બહાર જોયું. બરફ પડી રહ્યો છે. થોડી વારમાં સૂરજ આથમી જશે. તેણે ફરી વિચાર્યું અને આજે ઘરે જ રહેવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
9月になって転校生が入ってきた。名を次郎と言った。黙々と本を読む。弁当を食べる。授業が終わるとさっさと帰る。そのうち「黙りん次郎」というあだ名がついた。Kugatsu ni natte tenkousei ga haitte kita. Na o Jirou to itta. Mokumoku to hon o yomu. Bentou o taberu. Jugyou ga owaru to sassa to kaeru. Sono uchi "Damarin Jirou" to iu adana ga tsuita. In September a transfer student joined us. His name was Jiro. He reads his book in silence. He eats his lunch. When class ends, he heads straight home. Before long he got the nickname "Silent Jiro". सितंबर में एक नया छात्र (दूसरे स्कूल से) आया। उसका नाम जिरो था। चुपचाप किताब पढ़ता है। टिफ़िन खाता है। क्लास ख़त्म होते ही फटाफट घर चला जाता है। कुछ समय बाद उसका उपनाम "चुप्पा जिरो" पड़ गया। સપ્ટેમ્બરમાં એક નવો વિદ્યાર્થી (બીજી શાળામાંથી) આવ્યો. તેનું નામ જિરો હતું. ચૂપચાપ પુસ્તક વાંચે છે. ટિફિન ખાય છે. વર્ગ પૂરો થતાં જ ફટાફટ ઘરે જતો રહે છે. થોડા સમયમાં તેનું ઉપનામ "મૂંગો જિરો" પડી ગયું.

The frame (見た／入ってきた／ついた) keeps the story in the past; the present-form sentences are the narrator "standing inside" the scene. The exam won't ask you to produce this, but it explains why a past-tense passage can suddenly contain る-forms that are not errors.

【B-1】 記憶の確認 — past form to confirm a memory (〜でしたか・〜んだった)

conversational
SectionB 過去形の文の特別な用法 — 1. 記憶の確認Kakokei no bun no tokubetsu na youhou — kioku no kakunin
Rule (book)かつて一度記憶したことを確認するときに過去形が使われます。その場で思い出したときに「の（ん）だった」がよく使われます。Katsute ichido kioku shita koto o kakunin suru toki ni kakokei ga tsukawaremasu. Sono ba de omoidashita toki ni "no (n) datta" ga yoku tsukawaremasu.
Rule (EN)The past form is used to double-check something you once knew (even if it is about the future). When you suddenly remember on the spot, 「の（ん）だった」 is common.
Rule (HI)जो बात पहले कभी याद की थी उसकी पुष्टि करते समय भूत रूप का प्रयोग होता है (चाहे बात भविष्य की हो)। अचानक याद आने पर 「の（ん）だった」 अक्सर बोला जाता है।
Rule (GU)અગાઉ ક્યારેક યાદ રાખેલી વાતની ખાતરી કરતી વખતે ભૂત રૂપ વપરાય છે (ભલે વાત ભવિષ્યની હોય). અચાનક યાદ આવે ત્યારે 「の（ん）だった」 ઘણી વાર બોલાય છે.
Typical use〜でしたか／〜だったっけ (checking), あ、〜んだった (sudden recall).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
会議はあしたでしたか。あさってだと思っていましたが……。Kaigi wa ashita deshita ka. Asatte da to omotte imashita ga……. The meeting is tomorrow, was it? I thought it was the day after… मीटिंग कल थी क्या? मुझे लगा था परसों है…… મીટિંગ કાલે હતી? મને તો લાગ્યું હતું પરમ દિવસે છે……
あ、いけない。赤ちゃんが寝ているんだった。静かにしなくては……。A, ikenai. Akachan ga nete iru n datta. Shizuka ni shinakute wa……. Oh no — the baby's asleep, I forgot. I must be quiet… अरे, गड़बड़। बच्चा तो सो रहा है (याद आया)। चुप रहना होगा…… અરે, ભૂલ થઈ. બાળક તો સૂતું છે (યાદ આવ્યું). શાંત રહેવું પડશે……

The meeting is in the future and the baby is sleeping now, yet the past form appears: it is the past of "I knew this", not of the event. Hindi/Gujarati "था／હતું" in "मीटिंग कल थी क्या?" works the same way.

【B-2】 事実に反すること① — 〜のだった・〜べきだった・〜はずだった・〜ところだった

neutral
SectionB-2. 事実に反すること①Jijitsu ni hansuru koto (1)
Rule (book)「〜のだった・〜べきだった・〜はずだった・〜ところだった」などの形で、実現しなかったことを表します。"~no datta・~beki datta・~hazu datta・~tokoro datta" nado no katachi de, jitsugen shinakatta koto o arawashimasu.
Rule (EN)With endings like 〜のだった／〜べきだった／〜はずだった／〜ところだった, the past form expresses something that did not happen (regret, unfulfilled expectation, a near miss).
Rule (HI)〜のだった／〜べきだった／〜はずだった／〜ところだった जैसे रूपों से वह बात व्यक्त होती है जो असल में नहीं हुई (पछतावा, अधूरी उम्मीद, बाल-बाल बचना)।
Rule (GU)〜のだった／〜べきだった／〜はずだった／〜ところだった જેવાં રૂપોથી એ વાત વ્યક્ત થાય છે જે ખરેખર ન બની (અફસોસ, અધૂરી અપેક્ષા, માંડ બચ્યા).
Typical use〜んだった／〜べきだった = "I should have ~" (regret); 〜はずだった = "it was supposed to ~ (but didn't)"; 〜ところだった = "I nearly ~".
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今では後悔している。若いとき留学するんだった。Ima de wa koukai shite iru. Wakai toki ryuugaku suru n datta. Now I regret it. I should have studied abroad when I was young. अब मुझे पछतावा है। जवानी में विदेश पढ़ने जाना चाहिए था। હવે મને અફસોસ છે. યુવાનીમાં વિદેશ ભણવા જવું જોઈતું હતું.
警察はもっとよく調査するべきだった。Keisatsu wa motto yoku chousa suru beki datta. The police should have investigated more thoroughly. पुलिस को और अच्छी तरह जाँच करनी चाहिए थी। પોલીસે વધુ સારી રીતે તપાસ કરવી જોઈતી હતી.

Note the verb before んだった／べきだった stays in the dictionary form (留学する, 調査する) — only the ending is past. 練習2 passage 2 ③ (するべきだ → するべきだった) tests exactly this.

【B-3】 事実に反すること② — hypothetical past result (〜た(だろう)のに・〜たんだけど)

neutral
SectionB-3. 事実に反すること②Jijitsu ni hansuru koto (2)
Rule (book)事実に反することを仮定して、それが実現していれば、後のことが起こったはずだ（しかし、実際は起こらなかった）という意味を表します。文末に「〜のに・〜んだけどなど」をつけることがあります。→第3部2課Jijitsu ni hansuru koto o katei shite, sore ga jitsugen shite ireba, ato no koto ga okotta hazu da (shikashi, jissai wa okoranakatta) to iu imi o arawashimasu. Bunmatsu ni "~noni・~n da kedo nado" o tsukeru koto ga arimasu.
Rule (EN)Suppose something contrary to fact: "if that had happened, the result would have followed (but in reality it didn't)". The result clause is in the past; the sentence often ends with 〜のに or 〜んだけど. (Cross-reference: 第3部 2課 条件.)
Rule (HI)तथ्य के विपरीत बात मानकर: "अगर वह हुआ होता तो यह परिणाम होता (पर असल में नहीं हुआ)"। परिणाम वाला हिस्सा भूत रूप में होता है; वाक्य के अंत में अक्सर 〜のに या 〜んだけど आता है।
Rule (GU)હકીકતથી વિરુદ્ધ વાત ધારીને: "જો તે બન્યું હોત તો આ પરિણામ આવત (પણ ખરેખર ન આવ્યું)". પરિણામવાળો ભાગ ભૂત રૂપમાં હોય છે; વાક્યના અંતે ઘણી વાર 〜のに કે 〜んだけど આવે છે.
Typical use〜ば／〜たら ……〜た（のに／んだけど）. The 〜のに shows regret or complaint.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
温度管理をする余裕があれば、いい花が咲いたんだけど……。Ondo kanri o suru yoyuu ga areba, ii hana ga saita n da kedo……. If I'd had the time to control the temperature, nice flowers would have bloomed, but… अगर तापमान नियंत्रित करने की फ़ुर्सत होती, तो अच्छे फूल खिलते, लेकिन…… જો તાપમાન નિયંત્રિત કરવાની ફુરસદ હોત, તો સારાં ફૂલ ખીલત, પણ……
あの日急用がなかったら、わたしもパーティーに参加できたのに。Ano hi kyuuyou ga nakattara, watashi mo paatii ni sanka dekita noni. If I hadn't had urgent business that day, I could have joined the party too. अगर उस दिन अचानक ज़रूरी काम न आया होता, तो मैं भी पार्टी में शामिल हो पाता। જો તે દિવસે અચાનક જરૂરી કામ ન આવ્યું હોત, તો હું પણ પાર્ટીમાં જોડાઈ શક્યો હોત.

The present form here (いい花が咲くんだけど) would mean a general/future possibility, not regret about a past that didn't happen. 練習1 ⑩ (できたかもしれない) and 練習2 passage 1 ④ (なかった) test this.

【C-1】 事実に反すること — 〜ていたら、〜ていた(だろう)

3つくれるわけではないtsukureru wake de wa naithat doesn't mean it can be builtइसका मतलब यह नहीं कि बन सकता हैતેનો અર્થ એ નથી કે બની શકે
4つくれなかったわけであるtsukurenakatta wake de aruthat's why it couldn't be builtइसीलिए नहीं बन सकाએટલે જ ન બની શક્યો

Why: たとえ〜としても ("even if") needs a denial of the expected consequence, and the next sentence says せいぜい…できるだけ ("at most you can only…") → つくれるわけではない (present: a general claim about what is possible). 2 is a double negative = "it can be built", the opposite. 1 and 4 are past-tense conclusions (〜わけである) that don't fit after たとえ〜としても.

Why: 〜に満ちた before a noun describes a state ("full of"), the same as 〜に満ちている — not a finished event. 2 (〜ていた) would push the state into a closed past, odd for "modern" science that is still going on. 3 and 4 need a person who feels; a 過程 (process) can't 感じる.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The pairs this lesson's exercises force you to choose between. Rule of thumb: decide tense by event order and reality (did it actually happen?), never by translating from Hindi/Gujarati/English.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
V-る + N／とき~ru + N / toki Action not yet done at the main event noun-modifying clause Happens after the main clause (食べる弁当を買った), or the noun is not yet realised (再発する可能性, 行く予定), or no specific moment (つまずく事故). Purpose, plan, worry, type of event (D-1/2/3).
V-た + N／とき~ta + N / toki Action completed before the main event noun-modifying clause Happens before the main clause, even in the future (来月行ったとき…よう; 改善したときはもう遅い). Specific earlier event (いじめられた子供, 頼まれた仕事).
〜ば／たら … V-た（のに）~ba / tara … ~ta (noni) Would have ~ (but didn't) condition + past result Counterfactual past; のに／んだけど add regret (B-3). Regret about a missed outcome.
〜ていたら／ていれば … 〜ていた(だろう)~te itara / te ireba … ~te ita (darou) Had ~, would (now) be ~ V-ていた Makes the counterfactual clearer than plain た; result may describe the imagined present (今はバリバリやっていた) (C-1). Strong "if only" statements.
〜んだった・〜べきだった~n datta / ~beki datta Should have ~ V-dict + んだった／べきだった Verb stays dictionary form; the regret is in the ending (B-2). Own regret (んだった) or criticism (べきだった).
〜んだった (recall) vs 〜んだった (regret)~n datta "Oh right, ~" vs "I should have ~" same form Recall: about a current fact you just remembered (寝ているんだった, B-1). Regret: about an action not taken (留学するんだった, B-2). Decide from context: is it now-true or never-done?
〜ている vs plain past~te iru vs ~ta State over a span vs event at a point V-ている／V-た 隠れていて (was hidden for a while) vs 出てきた (came out at one moment); まだ出ていない (not yet) vs ×まだ出ない. Check whether the passage describes duration or a single event.
Exam trap 1 — "future" with た: 来月日本に行ったとき… is correct if the action happens after arriving. Don't change it to 行くとき just because it's next month. Ask: does the main action happen before or after the modifier? (D-1, 練習1 ⑨).
Exam trap 2 — nouns that lock the present: 予定・計画・目的・可能性・恐れ・心配 are always preceded by the dictionary form, even inside a past sentence (行く予定をキャンセルした) (D-3, 練習2 passage 2 ①②).
Exam trap 3 — counterfactual signals: もし…なかったなら, あのとき…ば, 〜のに at the end → the result must be past (〜なかった／〜できた／〜ていた). In まとめ [2], 生まれない would be grammatical in isolation but breaks the "if it hadn't been for scientists" logic.
