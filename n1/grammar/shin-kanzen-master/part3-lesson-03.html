Notes
First of four 視点 (viewpoint) lessons (3課–6課). Japanese prefers to tell a story from the speaker's side and to keep the same subject through a passage, so the choice between 与える／受ける, 預ける／預かる, a passive, 〜合う, or an intransitive vs transitive verb is often decided by who the sentence is about, not by the facts. Passives get their own lesson in 5課; てくる／ていく in 4課. This lesson has no 練習2.
Book introduction (p.128): 文章としてのまとまりを持たせるために、書き手はふつう、いつも同じ視点から物事を述べます。視点を動かさないようにするために、いろいろな手段が使われます。視点＝話者が物事を見ている位置。日本語では、視点を話者側に置くのが自然です。Bunshou to shite no matomari o motaseru tame ni, kakite wa futsuu, itsumo onaji shiten kara monogoto o nobemasu. Shiten o ugokasanai you ni suru tame ni, iroiro na shudan ga tsukawaremasu. Shiten = washa ga monogoto o mite iru ichi. Nihongo de wa, shiten o washa-gawa ni oku no ga shizen desu.
EN: To make a text cohere, a writer normally describes things from one fixed viewpoint, and uses various devices to keep it from shifting. Viewpoint = the position from which the speaker sees things. In Japanese it is natural to place the viewpoint on the speaker's side.
HI: लेख को एकजुट रखने के लिए, लेखक आमतौर पर हमेशा एक ही दृष्टिकोण से बातें कहता है, और उसे हिलने न देने के लिए कई तरीक़े अपनाता है। दृष्टिकोण = वह स्थान जहाँ से वक्ता चीज़ों को देखता है। जापानी में दृष्टिकोण को वक्ता की तरफ़ रखना स्वाभाविक है।
GU: લખાણને એકસૂત્ર રાખવા માટે, લેખક સામાન્ય રીતે હંમેશાં એક જ દૃષ્ટિબિંદુથી વાતો કહે છે, અને તેને ખસવા ન દેવા માટે ઘણી રીતો વાપરે છે. દૃષ્ટિબિંદુ = એ સ્થાન જ્યાંથી વક્તા વસ્તુઓને જુએ છે. જાપાનીઝમાં દૃષ્ટિબિંદુને વક્તાની બાજુએ રાખવું સ્વાભાવિક છે.

【A-1】 話者を主語にする — direction verbs with the speaker as subject

viewpoint rule
SectionA 話者を主語にする場合Washa o shugo ni suru baai
Rule (book)次のような動詞を使った文で、動作主が話者のときは、話者を主語にして話者の視点から述べるのが普通です。Tsugi no you na doushi o tsukatta bun de, dousashu ga washa no toki wa, washa o shugo ni shite washa no shiten kara noberu no ga futsuu desu.
Rule (EN)With the verbs below, when the speaker is the one doing the action, it is normal to make the speaker the subject and narrate from the speaker's viewpoint.
Rule (HI)नीचे दी गई क्रियाओं वाले वाक्यों में, जब काम करने वाला वक्ता ख़ुद हो, तो वक्ता को कर्ता बनाकर उसी के दृष्टिकोण से कहना सामान्य है।
Rule (GU)નીચેની ક્રિયાઓવાળાં વાક્યોમાં, જ્યારે કામ કરનાર વક્તા પોતે હોય, ત્યારે વક્તાને કર્તા બનાવીને તેના જ દૃષ્ટિબિંદુથી કહેવું સામાન્ય છે.
Typical usePick the verb whose subject is "I": わたしが預ける (I leave it with you) vs わたしが預かる (I look after it for you).

【A-2】 受身で話者を主語に — passive when the action comes toward the speaker →第3部 5課

viewpoint rule
Rule (book)動作主が他者で、動作や感情が他者から話者に向かうことを表すとき、話者を主語にして受身の形で述べることが多いです。Dousashu ga tasha de, dousa ya kanjou ga tasha kara washa ni mukau koto o arawasu toki, washa o shugo ni shite ukemi no katachi de noberu koto ga ooi desu.
Rule (EN)When someone else does the action and it (or their feeling) is directed at the speaker, Japanese usually keeps the speaker as subject by using the passive.
Rule (HI)जब काम कोई और करे और वह (या उसकी भावना) वक्ता की ओर हो, तो जापानी में अक्सर कर्मवाच्य (受身) से वक्ता को ही कर्ता बनाए रखते हैं।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે કામ બીજું કોઈ કરે અને તે (કે તેની લાગણી) વક્તા તરફ હોય, ત્યારે જાપાનીઝમાં ઘણી વાર કર્મણિ (受身) વાપરીને વક્તાને જ કર્તા રાખે છે.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
わたしは両親に期待されている。Watashi wa ryoushin ni kitai sarete iru. My parents have high hopes for me. (lit. I am expected of by my parents.) मेरे माता-पिता को मुझसे बहुत उम्मीदें हैं। મારાં માતા-પિતાને મારી પાસેથી ઘણી અપેક્ષાઓ છે.

両親はわたしに期待している is grammatical, but in a text about "me" the passive keeps わたし as the subject. 練習1 passage 7 ③ (守ってきた, active) shows the reverse: if the speaker does the action, no passive.

【A-3】 〜合う — doing the same thing to each other

viewpoint rule
Rule (book)話者と他者がお互いに相手に向けて同じ動作をすることを表すときは「〜合う」を使い、話者と他者の両方を主語にして述べることが多いです。Washa to tasha ga otagai ni aite ni mukete onaji dousa o suru koto o arawasu toki wa "~au" o tsukai, washa to tasha no ryouhou o shugo ni shite noberu koto ga ooi desu.
Rule (EN)When the speaker and someone else do the same action toward each other, use V-stem + 合う with both as the subject.
Rule (HI)जब वक्ता और कोई दूसरा एक-दूसरे के प्रति एक ही काम करें, तो क्रिया-मूल + 合う लगाते हैं और दोनों को कर्ता बनाते हैं।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે વક્તા અને બીજું કોઈ એકબીજા પ્રત્યે એક જ કામ કરે, ત્યારે ક્રિયા-મૂળ + 合う લગાડે છે અને બંનેને કર્તા બનાવે છે.
Connection (接続)動 ます形の語幹 + 合う（助け合う・いたわり合う・励まし合う）

わたしとカンさんは助け合って仕事をしている。Watashi to Kan-san wa tasukeatte shigoto o shite iru. Kang and I work together, helping each other. मैं और कान जी एक-दूसरे की मदद करते हुए काम करते हैं। હું અને કાનજી એકબીજાને મદદ કરતાં કામ કરીએ છીએ.
わたしたちはいたわり合って生きてきた。Watashitachi wa itawariatte ikite kita. We have lived our lives caring for one another. हमने एक-दूसरे का ख़याल रखते हुए जीवन बिताया है। અમે એકબીજાની કાળજી રાખતાં જીવન વિતાવ્યું છે.

Tested in 練習1 passage 7 ④⑤: a team of colleagues → 励まし合う, 頑張り合える. Without 合う, the sentence would say only one side encouraged the other.

【B-1】 他動詞 — when to use the transitive verb

exam favourite
SectionB 自動詞・他動詞の使い分け — 他動詞Jidoushi・tadoushi no tsukaiwake — tadoushi
Rule (EN)Use the transitive verb (〜を V) when you focus on (1) the action that causes a change, (2) a failure / responsibility — the subject did it, even unintentionally, or (3) a fixed idiom.
Rule (HI)सकर्मक क्रिया (〜を V) तब: (1) जब बदलाव लाने वाली क्रिया पर ध्यान हो, (2) जब ग़लती / ज़िम्मेदारी की बात हो — कर्ता ने किया, भले अनजाने में, (3) जब मुहावरा हो।
Rule (GU)સકર્મક ક્રિયા (〜を V) ત્યારે: (1) જ્યારે બદલાવ લાવતી ક્રિયા પર ધ્યાન હોય, (2) જ્યારે ભૂલ / જવાબદારીની વાત હોય — કર્તાએ કર્યું, ભલે અજાણતાં, (3) જ્યારે રૂઢિપ્રયોગ હોય.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
変化を起こす動作に注目：（電化が）文化水準を高めた。Henka o okosu dousa ni chuumoku: (denka ga) bunka suijun o takameta. Focus on the cause: (Electrification) raised the cultural standard. कारण पर ध्यान: (विद्युतीकरण ने) सांस्कृतिक स्तर ऊँचा किया। કારણ પર ધ્યાન: (વીજળીકરણે) સાંસ્કૃતિક સ્તર ઊંચું કર્યું.
失敗・責任：車をガードレールにぶつけた。Shippai・sekinin: kuruma o gaadoreeru ni butsuketa. Failure/responsibility: I hit the car against the guardrail. ग़लती/ज़िम्मेदारी: मैंने गाड़ी रेलिंग से टकरा दी। ભૂલ/જવાબદારી: મેં ગાડી રેલિંગ સાથે અથડાવી દીધી.
失敗・責任：チャンスを逃した。Chansu o nogashita. I let the chance slip. मैंने मौक़ा गँवा दिया। મેં તક ગુમાવી દીધી.
慣用的表現：心臓が脈を打っている。Kan'youteki hyougen: shinzou ga myaku o utte iru. Idiom: The heart is beating (lit. striking a pulse). मुहावरा: दिल धड़क रहा है। રૂઢિપ્રયોગ: હૃદય ધબકી રહ્યું છે.
慣用的表現：おなかを壊した。Onaka o kowashita. I got an upset stomach. मेरा पेट ख़राब हो गया। મારું પેટ બગડી ગયું.

Note the Hindi/Gujarati: "पेट ख़राब हो गया" is intransitive, but Japanese says おなかを壊す (transitive, idiom) — a classic L1 trap tested in 練習1 passage 2 ①.

【B-2】 自動詞 — when to use the intransitive verb

exam favourite
Rule (EN)Use the intransitive verb (〜が V) when you focus on (1) the movement or change of the thing itself, (2) possibility (載る = "will fit", 動かない = "won't work"), or (3) the result of an action (通った = "finally went through", でき上がった).
Rule (HI)अकर्मक क्रिया (〜が V) तब: (1) जब वस्तु की अपनी गति या बदलाव पर ध्यान हो, (2) संभावना (載る = "समा जाएगा", 動かない = "चलता नहीं"), (3) किसी क्रिया का परिणाम (通った = "आख़िर पिरो गया", でき上がった = "बनकर तैयार")।
Rule (GU)અકર્મક ક્રિયા (〜が V) ત્યારે: (1) જ્યારે વસ્તુની પોતાની ગતિ કે બદલાવ પર ધ્યાન હોય, (2) શક્યતા (載る = "સમાઈ જશે", 動かない = "ચાલતું નથી"), (3) કોઈ ક્રિયાનું પરિણામ (通った = "છેવટે પરોવાયો", でき上がった = "તૈયાર થઈ ગયું").

A useful test: if a person deliberately did it and that matters, use 他動詞; if you just report what happened to the thing (or that it could/couldn't happen), use 自動詞. Japanese reports many results with 自動詞 where English would name an agent ("the editor extended the deadline" is 延ばしてくれた because the editor's kindness matters; but "the issue came out" is 出た).

練習1 — どちらか適当な方を選びなさい (choose the better option)

Why: Fixed expression 役に立つ (自動詞, "be useful"), in plain dictionary form because it is a hoped-for, not-yet-realised event in a 〜のなら condition (the speaker is deciding whether to accept). 3 and 4 describe something already happening, which contradicts the hypothetical のなら; 2 is not a natural form of this idiom.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Almost every item in this lesson is a pair of verbs that describe the same event from opposite sides. First find the subject the passage is about, then choose the verb that keeps it.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
与える／受けるataeru / ukeru give / receive (influence, a prize) 〜に〜を与える／〜から〜を受ける Subject = source vs subject = receiver. 受ける when "we/I" are the receiver (練習1 1, 7-⑥).
預ける／預かるazukeru / azukaru leave in someone's care / take into one's care 〜に〜を預ける／〜から〜を預かる Owner is subject of 預ける; keeper is subject of 預かる. Check who is speaking (練習1 3-①②).
授ける／授かる・教える／教わるsazukeru / sazukaru・oshieru / osowaru bestow / be blessed with; teach / learn from 〜に〜を授ける／〜から〜を授かる The 〜かる／〜わる verb keeps the receiver as subject. Human viewpoint: 遺伝子は授かったもの; 友人に教わった.
他動詞 (〜を V)tadoushi someone does it 高める・延ばす・広める・伝える・決める Focus on the agent's deliberate action, a failure/responsibility, or an idiom (おなかを壊す). Activities, tasks, kindnesses (〜てくれる), decisions with 〜ようと.
自動詞 (〜が V)jidoushi it happens 高まる・進む・広まる・起こる・できる・出る Focus on the change itself, possibility, or result; no agent named. Natural events (災害が起きる), trends (次第に広まる), results (8月号が出た).
受身 vs activeukemi be V-ed / V V-られる Passive only when someone else acts on the speaker (期待されている). Speaker acts → active (守ってきた, 言い聞かせた).
V vs V-合う~au do / do to each other V-stem + 合う 合う makes both people the subject. Teams, couples, friends (励まし合う・頑張り合う).
Exam trap 1 — が or を before the blank: if the blank follows 〜が (水準が, アニメーションが), you almost always need a 自動詞; if it follows 〜を, a 他動詞. In まとめ [4], 広げていく is wrong simply because of アニメーションが.
Exam trap 2 — L1 interference with idioms: Hindi/Gujarati/English say the stomach "got upset" (intransitive), but Japanese says おなかを壊す, 脈を打つ, チャンスを逃す (transitive). Learn these as fixed phrases.
Exam trap 3 — 〜てくれる needs a person: ×締め切り日が延びてくれた. A favour verb requires a human giver, so use the 他動詞 with that person as subject (締め切り日を延ばしてくれた).
