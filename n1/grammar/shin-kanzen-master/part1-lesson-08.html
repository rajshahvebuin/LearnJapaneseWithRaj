Notes
This is the first lesson of section II (主観を含めて説明する — the speaker's feelings are built into the form). All five points mean "but / although", so the exam separates them by what kind of clause comes before and after: a polite apology/thanks (ところを), a counter-to-fact regret (ものを), an evaluation (とはいえ), an obligation/assertion (といえども), or a surprising result (と思いきや). 〜ところを and 〜といえども are tagged 硬 formal/written (硬い言い方) in the book. 1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ところを

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~tokoro o
Meaning (EN)"(sorry / thank you for …) when you are ~ / even though it is ~" — book: 〜の時なのに・〜という事情があるのに、迷惑をかけて恐縮だ (although it is such a time / there are such circumstances, I am sorry to trouble you)
Meaning (HI)(आप) ...में होते हुए भी (आपको तकलीफ़ दी, क्षमा / धन्यवाद)
Meaning (GU)(આપ) ...માં હોવા છતાં (આપને તકલીફ આપી, માફ કરશો / આભાર)
Connection (接続)普通形（ナ形 だ −な・名 だ −の） + ところを
Register硬 — a ceremonial, polite set phrase (business, formal speech, letters).
Typical useAttaches to words describing an ongoing situation (継続的な状況: お忙しい, お休み, お急ぎ). A courteous (儀礼的) way of showing concern that you are troubling the listener; what follows is usually an apology or thanks. As in ③, 〜べきところを ("when I ought to have ~") also occurs.

すぐにご報告しなければいけないところを遅くなってしまって申し訳ありません。Sugu ni gohoukoku shinakereba ikenai tokoro o osoku natte shimatte moushiwake arimasen. I should have reported to you right away, and I'm very sorry it has come so late. मुझे तुरंत सूचना देनी चाहिए थी, पर देर हो गई — इसके लिए क्षमा चाहता हूँ। મારે તરત જાણ કરવી જોઈતી હતી, પણ મોડું થઈ ગયું — તે બદલ માફી માગું છું.
お急ぎのところをすみません。ちょっと伺ってもよろしいでしょうか。Oisogi no tokoro o sumimasen. Chotto ukagatte mo yoroshii deshou ka. I'm sorry to stop you when you're in a hurry — may I ask you something? आप जल्दी में हैं, फिर भी रोकने के लिए माफ़ कीजिए। क्या मैं कुछ पूछ सकता हूँ? આપ ઉતાવળમાં છો છતાં રોકવા બદલ માફ કરશો. શું હું કંઈક પૂછી શકું?
こちらからお願いに伺うべきところを先方からおいでいただき、恐縮した。Kochira kara onegai ni ukagau beki tokoro o senpou kara oide itadaki, kyoushuku shita. It was we who should have gone to them with our request, yet they came to us — I felt very embarrassed. अनुरोध लेकर हमें ही उनके पास जाना चाहिए था, पर वे ख़ुद आ गए — हमें बड़ा संकोच हुआ। વિનંતી લઈને અમારે જ તેમની પાસે જવું જોઈતું હતું, પણ તેઓ જાતે આવ્યા — અમને બહુ સંકોચ થયો.

Think "at a time when you are ~ (and yet I…)". The ところ is the listener's current situation, so the word before it is a continuing state — お忙しい, お休み, お急ぎ — not a one-off action. The sentence almost always ends in すみません／申し訳ありません or ありがとうございました ([1] Q3). Watch the connection: noun + の ([1] Q1: お休みのところを).

【2】 〜ものを

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book)
Reading~mono o
Meaning (EN)"if only ~ (it would have been fine), but…" — book: 〜が順当に成立していれば良かったのに、実際はそうではなかった (it would have been good if ~ had come about as it should, but in reality it didn't)
Meaning (HI)...होता तो अच्छा था, पर (ऐसा नहीं हुआ) — काश...
Meaning (GU)...થયું હોત તો સારું હતું, પણ (એવું ન થયું) — કાશ...
Connection (接続)動・形 普通形（ナ形 だ −な） + ものを
RegisterNeutral; carries a clearly emotional, reproachful tone.
Typical useThe whole sentence expresses the speaker's dissatisfaction or regret (不満・残念な気持ち). It attaches to a clause that supposes something contrary to fact (〜ば…のに-type hypothesis); what follows explains the actual fact. As in ④, the following clause can be omitted (sentence ends in 〜ものを).

安静にしていれば治るものを、田中さんはすぐに働き始めて、また病気を悪化させてしまった。Ansei ni shite ireba naoru mono o, Tanaka-san wa sugu ni hatarakihajimete, mata byouki o akka sasete shimatta. If he had just rested he would have recovered, but Mr. Tanaka went straight back to work and made his illness worse again. आराम करते तो ठीक हो जाते, पर तनाका जी तुरंत काम पर लौट आए और बीमारी फिर से बिगाड़ ली। આરામ કર્યો હોત તો સાજા થઈ જાત, પણ તનાકાજી તરત કામે લાગી ગયા અને બીમારી ફરી વકરાવી.
もっと慎重にやれば誤解されないものを、彼の強引なやり方がいつも誤解を招く。Motto shinchou ni yareba gokai sarenai mono o, kare no gouin na yarikata ga itsumo gokai o maneku. If he went about things more carefully he wouldn't be misunderstood, but his pushy approach always invites misunderstanding. अगर वह ज़्यादा सावधानी से काम करे तो ग़लतफ़हमी न हो, पर उसका ज़बरदस्ती वाला तरीक़ा हमेशा ग़लतफ़हमी पैदा करता है। જો તે વધુ સાવચેતીથી કામ કરે તો ગેરસમજ ન થાય, પણ તેની જબરદસ્તીવાળી રીત હંમેશાં ગેરસમજ ઊભી કરે છે.
よせばいいものを、彼は社長に大声で文句を言った。そのため会社を首になった。Yoseba ii mono o, kare wa shachou ni oogoe de monku o itta. Sono tame kaisha o kubi ni natta. He should have known better, but he complained loudly to the company president — and got fired for it. उसे ऐसा नहीं करना चाहिए था, पर उसने कंपनी के अध्यक्ष से ज़ोर-ज़ोर से शिकायत की, और इसी वजह से नौकरी से निकाल दिया गया। તેણે એવું ન કરવું જોઈતું હતું, પણ તેણે પ્રમુખ સામે જોરજોરથી ફરિયાદ કરી, અને એટલે જ નોકરીમાંથી કાઢી મુકાયો.
一言声をかけてくれれば手伝ったものを。Hitokoto koe o kakete kurereba tetsudatta mono o. If you'd only said a word, I'd have helped you (but you didn't). एक बार कह देते तो मैं मदद कर देता (पर तुमने कहा ही नहीं)। એક વાર કહ્યું હોત તો હું મદદ કરત (પણ તેં કહ્યું જ નહીં).

Pattern to memorise: 〜ば／〜たら … (good result) ものを, (but the bad reality). It is close to 〜のに, but heavier with regret or reproach. Three things the drills test: the first half must be a counter-to-fact supposition (〜ていれば, not 〜ていると／〜ていて — [2] Q1); what follows must be the actual, opposite fact ([2] Q2: どうして会わないの); and the supposed outcome must be the good one ([2] Q3–4).

【3】 〜とはいえ

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~to wa ie
Meaning (EN)"although it's true that ~, still…; granted ~, but…" — book: 〜というのは事実かもしれないが、それでもやはり状況は同じだ (~ may be true, but even so the situation is still the same)
Meaning (HI)माना कि ..., फिर भी...
Meaning (GU)માન્યું કે ..., છતાં પણ...
Connection (接続)名・普通形 + とはいえ
RegisterNot tagged 硬, but more written than 〜といっても／〜けれど.
Typical useAttaches to a fact, or what the speaker regards as fact. What follows is not a mere fact but the speaker's evaluation (話者の評価) that runs against what ~ would suggest.

ダイエット中とはいえ、出されたごちそうに手をつけないなんて失礼だと思う。Daietto chuu to wa ie, dasareta gochisou ni te o tsukenai nante shitsurei da to omou. Granted you're on a diet, but not touching a feast that's been served to you is rude, I think. माना कि तुम डाइटिंग पर हो, फिर भी परोसे गए पकवानों को हाथ तक न लगाना मुझे अशिष्टता लगती है। માન્યું કે તું ડાયેટિંગ પર છે, છતાં પીરસેલી વાનગીઓને હાથ પણ ન લગાડવો એ મને અવિવેક લાગે છે.
まだ締め切りまで時間があるとはいえ、早めに完成させておいたほうがいい。Mada shimekiri made jikan ga aru to wa ie, hayame ni kansei sasete oita hou ga ii. True, there's still time before the deadline, but it's better to finish early. माना कि अभी डेडलाइन में समय है, फिर भी जल्दी पूरा कर लेना बेहतर है। માન્યું કે હજી ડેડલાઇનમાં સમય છે, છતાં વહેલું પૂરું કરી લેવું સારું.
12月に入ったとはいえ、まだ年末という気がしない。Juunigatsu ni haitta to wa ie, mada nenmatsu to iu ki ga shinai. It may be December already, but it still doesn't feel like the end of the year. दिसंबर तो शुरू हो गया है, फिर भी अभी साल के अंत जैसा नहीं लगता। ડિસેમ્બર તો શરૂ થઈ ગયો છે, છતાં હજી વર્ષના અંત જેવું લાગતું નથી.
あの時は仕方がなかったとはいえ、ご迷惑をおかけしました。Ano toki wa shikata ga nakatta to wa ie, gomeiwaku o okake shimashita. Although it couldn't be helped at the time, I'm sorry for the trouble I caused you. माना कि उस समय कोई चारा नहीं था, फिर भी मैंने आपको परेशानी दी — क्षमा करें। માન્યું કે ત્યારે કોઈ ઉપાય નહોતો, છતાં મેં આપને તકલીફ આપી — માફ કરશો.

The first half is a real fact ("I admit X"); the second half is my judgment that X doesn't change things — 失礼だと思う, 〜ほうがいい, 気がしない, ものだ, だろう. That is the whole test in drill [3]: options that are plain events (不合格だった, 買った, 壊れた) are wrong because they aren't evaluations, and options that simply follow from X (予想通り → ショックではなかった) are wrong because they don't run against it.

【4】 〜といえども

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~to iedomo
Meaning (EN)"even though ~ / even if ~ / even a ~" — book: 〜は事実ではあるが・事実であっても・〜の立場の人であっても、実際はそこから普通に予想されることとは違う (though ~ is a fact / even if it were / even for a person in the position of ~, reality differs from what you would normally expect from that)

人間は自然災害に対して無力だといえども、国を挙げての対策を強化する必要がある。Ningen wa shizen saigai ni taishite muryoku da to iedomo, kuni o agete no taisaku o kyouka suru hitsuyou ga aru. どんな大富豪といえども、この有名な絵を買うことはできない。Donna daifugou to iedomo, kono yuumei na e o kau koto wa dekinai. No matter how rich a tycoon may be, no one can buy this famous painting. कोई कितना भी बड़ा धनकुबेर क्यों न हो, यह मशहूर चित्र नहीं ख़रीद सकता। કોઈ ગમે તેટલો મોટો ધનકુબેર કેમ ન હોય, આ પ્રખ્યાત ચિત્ર ખરીદી શકે નહીં.

The X in "X といえども" sets up a natural expectation (a minor → may be let off; an easy test → good score; a tycoon → can buy anything), and the second half denies it with a rule, duty or firm judgment — 〜てはならない, 〜必要がある, 〜できない, 〜だろう. So in drill [4] the noun must be one that creates the expectation (簡単な試験), and the ending must be an obligation/assertion (滞納してはいけない, 調べてみる必要がある), not a narrated event or a request.

Since the result has happened, the sentence is normally in the past.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Five N1 "but / although" forms plus the three N2 forms from the 〔復習〕 box. They all join a "but", so tell them apart by what the first half is (fact / supposition / expectation / listener's situation) and what the second half must be:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ところを~tokoro o When you are ~ (sorry / thanks) 普通形（ナ形な・名の） 硬. First half = the listener's ongoing situation; second half = apology or thanks. Formal courtesy: お忙しいところを, 遠いところを, お休みのところを.
〜ものを~mono o If only ~, but… (regret) 動・形 普通形（ナ形な） First half = counter-to-fact supposition (〜ば…); second half = the disappointing reality, often omitted. Reproach or regret: 言ってくれれば手伝ったものを。
〜とはいえ~to wa ie Granted ~, still… 名・普通形 First half = fact; second half = speaker's evaluation against it (not a bare fact). Conceding a point but judging otherwise: 12月とはいえ暖かい。
〜といえども~to iedomo Even (a) ~ / even though ~ 名・普通形 硬. First half = fact or hypothesis, often with たとえ／いかに／どんな; second half = obligation / resolve / assertion. Rules and firm statements: 子供といえども許されない。
〜と思いきや~to omoiki ya I thought ~, but (surprise!) 普通形 (+か) First half = expectation (not a fact); second half = an actual contrary result. Telling a story with a twist: 勝つと思いきや負けた。
〜ものの (N2)~mono no Although ~ (did), … 普通形（ナ形な・名である） Two plain facts; what should follow from the first didn't. No evaluation or assertion required. Narration: 買ったものの使っていない ([1〜5] Q7).
〜にもかかわらず／〜ながら (N2)~ni mo kakawarazu / ~nagara Despite ~ / although ~ 名・普通形 ／ 名・ナ形・動ます形 General-purpose N2 contrast; fewer restrictions on either half. When none of the N1 nuances (courtesy, regret, evaluation, assertion, surprise) is needed.
Exam trap 1 — ものを vs ものの: one kana apart. 〜ものを needs a counter-to-fact "if" (〜ば…た／〜ばいい) and expresses regret; 〜ものの joins two real facts. 言ったもののやれなかった (it was said) vs 言えば手伝ったものを (it wasn't said) ([1〜5] Q4, Q7).
Exam trap 2 — fact or expectation? If the first half is something that turned out false (晴れる, 負ける, 静かな人だ), only と思いきや works. とはいえ and といえども need it to be true ([1〜5] Q1–2, [5]).
Exam trap 3 — check the ending: とはいえ → evaluation (〜と思う, 〜ほうがいい, 〜ものだ, 〜だろう); といえども → duty/assertion (〜てはならない, 〜必要がある, 〜できない); ところを → すみません／ありがとう; と思いきや → past result (〜た). Options that are just "and then X happened" usually fail for the first two ([3], [4]).
