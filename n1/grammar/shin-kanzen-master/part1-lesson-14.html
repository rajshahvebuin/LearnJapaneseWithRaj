Notes
Every point here picks out a topic (a person, a role, a stage) and then judges it against a standard. So the exam tests the second clause: ときたら → a complaint; ともなると → the natural state at that stage; ともあろう → shock at unfitting behaviour; たるもの → how one should be; なりに → doing one's best within a limit. 〜ときたら is tagged 話し言葉 (spoken) in the book; none of the five is tagged 硬. Drill sets [1] and [2] have 4 items each here (not 3).
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.62): three N2 ways of taking up a topic and evaluating it. Today's N1 forms are stronger and more restricted about what the second clause may say.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ときたら

話し言葉 spoken/colloquial (book tag)
Reading~to kitara
Meaning (EN)"when it comes to ~ (ugh)…; talk about ~…" — book: 〜は良くない (~ is no good)
Meaning (HI)...की तो बात ही मत करो (शिकायत) — ...तो बिल्कुल...
Meaning (GU)...ની તો વાત જ ન કરો (ફરિયાદ) — ...તો સાવ...
Connection (接続)名 + ときたら
Register話し言葉 — tagged spoken language in the book; casual complaining tone.
Typical usePicks up a familiar person or topic (身近な人物や話題) to grumble about. The second clause is a negative evaluation — dissatisfaction or criticism (不満・非難). It cannot be followed by the speaker's wish/intention or by a request/invitation (希望・意向・働きかけ).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ときたらうちの父ときたらuchi no chichi to kitara
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns (a person or thing) only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近の若い人ときたら、言葉の使い方を知らない。Saikin no wakai hito to kitara, kotoba no tsukaikata o shiranai. Young people these days — they don't know how to use language properly. आजकल के नौजवानों की तो बात ही मत करो, उन्हें शब्दों का सही इस्तेमाल ही नहीं आता। આજકાલના જુવાનિયાઓની તો વાત જ ન કરો, તેમને શબ્દોનો સાચો ઉપયોગ જ નથી આવડતો.
うちの父ときたら、何でも母にやってもらっていて、だらしがない。Uchi no chichi to kitara, nan demo haha ni yatte moratte ite, darashi ga nai. My father — he has Mum do everything for him. He's hopeless. मेरे पिताजी तो बस — सब कुछ माँ से करवाते हैं, बिल्कुल लापरवाह हैं। મારા પિતાજી તો બસ — બધું જ મા પાસે કરાવે છે, સાવ બેદરકાર છે.
隣のうちの犬ときたら、いつもほえてばかりで、うるさくてしかたがない。Tonari no uchi no inu to kitara, itsumo hoete bakari de, urusakute shikata ga nai. The dog next door — it does nothing but bark, and the noise is unbearable. पड़ोसी के कुत्ते की तो बात ही क्या, हमेशा भौंकता ही रहता है, इतना शोर कि सहा नहीं जाता। પડોશીના કૂતરાની તો વાત જ શું, હંમેશાં ભસ્યા જ કરે છે, એટલો ઘોંઘાટ કે સહન નથી થતો.
あの店の料理ときたら、高いだけで全然おいしくない。Ano mise no ryouri to kitara, takai dake de zenzen oishiku nai. The food at that restaurant — it's just expensive and not tasty at all. उस दुकान के खाने का तो कहना ही क्या — बस महँगा है, ज़रा भी स्वादिष्ट नहीं। તે દુકાનના ખાવાનું તો કહેવું જ શું — બસ મોંઘું છે, જરાય સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી.

Think of an exasperated sigh: "Honestly, my husband…". All four examples are close-to-home topics (my father, the neighbour's dog, that shop) followed by a complaint. Positive or neutral facts (初めて会ったのは10年前だ) and wishes (連絡してほしい) don't fit — that is exactly what drill [1] tests. The N2 form 〜といったら can praise or criticise; 〜ときたら is almost always critical.

【2】 〜ともなると・〜ともなれば

neutral register
Reading~tomo naru to / ~tomo nareba
Meaning (EN)"once you get to (the level of) ~, naturally…" — book: 〜くらい立場・程度が高くなると、そのような状態になる (when one's position/degree rises as high as ~, that kind of state results)
Meaning (HI)...बन जाने पर / ...तक पहुँचने पर तो (स्वाभाविक रूप से)...
Meaning (GU)...બની જતાં / ...સુધી પહોંચતાં તો (સ્વાભાવિક રીતે)...
Connection (接続)名 + ともなると・ともなれば
RegisterNeutral — fine in speech and writing.
Typical useAttaches to words of an advanced stage (中学生・部長) or a special occasion (祭り・正月). The subject is often marked with emotional 「も」 (①④: 子供も…, この町も…). The second clause says what state naturally follows once that stage is reached. No wish/intention or request/invitation after it.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (stage/role)N + ともなると部長ともなるとbuchou tomo naru to
Noun (occasion/age)N + ともなれば祭りともなればmatsuri tomo nareba
Verb (辞書形)(added, not in book) occasionally V-辞書形 + ともなると, but the book gives nouns only結婚するともなるとkekkon suru tomo naru to
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—

子供も小さいときは素直だが、中学生ともなると、親の言うことを聞かなくなる。Kodomo mo chiisai toki wa sunao da ga, chuugakusei tomo naru to, oya no iu koto o kikanaku naru. Children are obedient when they're little, but once they're in junior high, they stop listening to their parents. बच्चे छोटे होते हैं तो कहना मानते हैं, पर मिडिल स्कूल में पहुँचते ही माँ-बाप की बात सुनना बंद कर देते हैं। બાળકો નાનાં હોય ત્યારે કહ્યું માને છે, પણ માધ્યમિક શાળામાં પહોંચતાં જ મા-બાપની વાત સાંભળવાનું બંધ કરી દે છે.
新入社員のころは自分のことだけで精いっぱいだったが、部長ともなると、部下を育てることを考えなければならない。Shinnyuu shain no koro wa jibun no koto dake de seiippai datta ga, buchou tomo naru to, buka o sodateru koto o kangaenakereba naranai. As a new employee I had my hands full just with my own work, but once you're a department head, you have to think about developing your staff. नया कर्मचारी था तब अपने काम में ही उलझा रहता था, पर विभाग प्रमुख बनने पर तो मातहतों को तैयार करने के बारे में सोचना पड़ता है। નવો કર્મચારી હતો ત્યારે પોતાના કામમાં જ વ્યસ્ત રહેતો, પણ વિભાગના વડા બનતાં તો હાથ નીચેના લોકોને તૈયાર કરવા વિશે વિચારવું પડે છે.
人間50歳ともなれば、親の介護や自分の老後のことを考え始める。Ningen gojussai tomo nareba, oya no kaigo ya jibun no rougo no koto o kangaehajimeru. Once a person reaches fifty, they start thinking about caring for their parents and about their own old age. इंसान पचास साल का हो जाए तो माँ-बाप की देखभाल और अपने बुढ़ापे के बारे में सोचने लगता है। માણસ પચાસ વર્ષનો થાય એટલે મા-બાપની સંભાળ અને પોતાના ઘડપણ વિશે વિચારવા લાગે છે.
普段は静かなこの町も、祭りともなれば、多くの観光客でにぎわう。Fudan wa shizuka na kono machi mo, matsuri tomo nareba, ooku no kankoukyaku de nigiwau. This town, usually quiet, bustles with crowds of tourists when festival time comes. आमतौर पर शांत रहने वाला यह शहर भी, त्योहार आने पर ढेरों पर्यटकों से गुलज़ार हो जाता है। સામાન્ય રીતે શાંત રહેતું આ શહેર પણ, તહેવાર આવતાં ઘણા પ્રવાસીઓથી ધમધમી ઊઠે છે.

The noun must be a high or special point on a scale: 3人の子の親, 10年目, 年末, 日本語能力試験N1. Words for a low point (不合格, 勉強不足) don't work ([2] Q4). After it comes a general, natural consequence — みんな忙しそうだ, 修理が必要なところも出てくる — not the speaker's plan (温泉に行きましょう, 修理してきれいにしたい).

【3】 〜ともあろう

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written/formal feel)
Reading~tomo arou
Meaning (EN)"someone of the standing of ~ (should not…)" — book: 立派な能力や責任がある〜なのに、それにふさわしくないことをする (though one is a ~ with fine ability or responsibility, one does something unworthy of it)
Meaning (HI)...जैसे (ऊँचे पद वाले) व्यक्ति होकर भी...
Meaning (GU)...જેવા (ઊંચા હોદ્દાવાળા) વ્યક્તિ થઈને પણ...
Connection (接続)名 + ともあろう + 名
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but a stiff, critical expression (news, editorials, scolding).
Typical useExpresses surprise or dissatisfaction that someone did what common sense says they should not. Or (④) the speaker's feeling that the person should act in a way fitting their position. Attaches to a noun the speaker rates highly (国会議員, 学長, 先生). A noun follows: 人・方・者.

Noun + NounN(role) + ともあろう + 人／方／者国会議員ともあろう人kokkai giin tomo arou hito
Noun + Noun (polite)N + ともあろう + お方大学の学長ともあろうお方daigaku no gakuchou tomo arou okata
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
国会議員ともあろう人が、差別的な発言をするなんて信じられない。Kokkai giin tomo arou hito ga, sabetsuteki na hatsugen o suru nante shinjirarenai. I can't believe that someone who is actually a member of the Diet would make discriminatory remarks. संसद सदस्य जैसा व्यक्ति भेदभावपूर्ण टिप्पणी करे, यक़ीन नहीं होता। સંસદ સભ્ય જેવી વ્યક્તિ ભેદભાવભરી ટિપ્પણી કરે, માન્યામાં નથી આવતું.
大学の学長ともあろうお方が、そのような無責任なことを言ってどうするんですか。Daigaku no gakuchou tomo arou okata ga, sono you na musekinin na koto o itte dou suru n desu ka. What good does it do for someone of your standing, a university president, to say something so irresponsible? विश्वविद्यालय के कुलपति जैसे व्यक्ति होकर आप ऐसी ग़ैर-ज़िम्मेदाराना बात कहें, यह कैसे चलेगा? યુનિવર્સિટીના કુલપતિ જેવી વ્યક્તિ થઈને આપ આવી બેજવાબદાર વાત કરો, એ કેમ ચાલે?
山田さんともあろう人が、こんな単純なミスをするなんて。どうしたのでしょう。Yamada-san tomo arou hito ga, konna tanjun na misu o suru nante. Dou shita no deshou. For someone like Mr. Yamada to make such a simple mistake! What's happened to him? यामादा जी जैसे व्यक्ति ऐसी मामूली ग़लती करें! क्या हो गया उन्हें? યામાદાજી જેવી વ્યક્તિ આવી સામાન્ય ભૂલ કરે! શું થયું હશે તેમને?
学会の会長ともあろう人は、最新の研究テーマについてしっかり把握しておくべきだ。Gakkai no kaichou tomo arou hito wa, saishin no kenkyuu teema ni tsuite shikkari haaku shite oku beki da. Someone who is the president of an academic society ought to have a firm grasp of the latest research topics. किसी विद्वत् परिषद के अध्यक्ष जैसे व्यक्ति को नवीनतम शोध विषयों की पक्की जानकारी रखनी चाहिए। કોઈ વિદ્વત્ પરિષદના પ્રમુખ જેવી વ્યક્તિએ નવીનતમ સંશોધન વિષયોની પાકી જાણકારી રાખવી જોઈએ.

The pattern is "X ともあろう人 + unworthy act + なんて／とは (shock)". Two traps: the noun must be someone respected — not わたし, not 夫 ([3] Q3) — and the act must be unfitting: winning a prize or buying a flat is not shocking ([3] Q2), and a plain request (レポートを見ていただけませんか) has no criticism at all ([3] Q1). Example ④ is the "should" use, where it overlaps with 〜たるもの.

【4】 〜たるもの(は)

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; old-style phrase)
Reading~taru mono (wa)
Meaning (EN)"anyone who is a ~ (should…)" — book: 〜のような責任のある立場・優れた立場には、そのようなことがふさわしい (for a responsible or eminent position like ~, such conduct is fitting)
Meaning (HI)जो ... है, उसे (ऐसा) होना ही चाहिए
Meaning (GU)જે ... છે, તેણે (આવું) હોવું જ જોઈએ
Connection (接続)名 + たるもの
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but たる is classical (= である) and the tone is preachy and formal.
Typical useAttaches to nouns of a responsible or eminent position. The second clause states the ideal, how one ought to be (あるべき姿) for that status — typically 〜なければならない／〜べきだ／〜てはならない／〜て当然だ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + たるもの(は)経営者たるものはkeieisha taru mono wa
Noun (other form)(added, not in book) N1 + たる + N2 ("an N2 worthy of being N1"), e.g. 教師たる者教師たる者kyoushi taru mono
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
経営者たるものは、一般的な法律や年金制度について知っておかなければならない。Keieisha taru mono wa, ippanteki na houritsu ya nenkin seido ni tsuite shitte okanakereba naranai. Anyone who runs a business must know about general laws and the pension system. जो व्यवसाय चलाता है, उसे सामान्य क़ानूनों और पेंशन व्यवस्था की जानकारी होनी ही चाहिए। જે વ્યવસાય ચલાવે છે, તેને સામાન્ય કાયદા અને પેન્શન વ્યવસ્થાની જાણકારી હોવી જ જોઈએ.
社会人たるもの、あいさつや時間を守ることなど、できて当然だろう。Shakaijin taru mono, aisatsu ya jikan o mamoru koto nado, dekite touzen darou. A working adult should naturally be able to greet people properly, be punctual and so on. जो कामकाजी वयस्क है, उसे अभिवादन करना और समय का पालन करना तो आना ही चाहिए। જે કામકાજી પુખ્ત વ્યક્તિ છે, તેને અભિવાદન કરવું અને સમયનું પાલન કરવું તો આવડવું જ જોઈએ.
紳士たるもの、強く優しくなければならない。Shinshi taru mono, tsuyoku yasashiku nakereba naranai. A gentleman must be strong and kind. जो सज्जन है, उसे मज़बूत और दयालु होना ही चाहिए। જે સજ્જન છે, તેણે મજબૂત અને દયાળુ હોવું જ જોઈએ.

Where 〜ともあろう reacts to something that did happen, 〜たるもの states a general rule about a role. The noun has to be a role with responsibility (一国のリーダー, 教師) — not a system (危機管理システム), not "you", and an ordinary 学生／国民 is too weak ([4] Q1–2). The second clause must be the ideal, so a statement of fact (失言が多い, また失言してしまった) is wrong ([4] Q3).

【5】 〜なりに

neutral register
Reading~nari ni

The word before なりに is a modest limit, never a boast: ×上手なりに, ○上手でないなりに ([5] Q2). A common N1 shape is "X ないなら ないなりに" ("if there's none, then make do accordingly", [5] Q3), and the second clause is a positive, fitting response — not just a complaint (大変困っている). Don't confuse it with 〜なり ("as soon as", 1課) or 〜なり〜なり ("either…or", 4課).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All five take a noun (a person, role or stage) and then evaluate it. Telling them apart is about what kind of evaluation follows:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ときたら~to kitara When it comes to ~ (ugh) N Spoken. Familiar topic + complaint. No wish/request after it. Grumbling about family, neighbours, a shop (うちの夫ときたら…).
〜ともなると・〜ともなれば~tomo naru to / ~tomo nareba Once it gets to (the level of) ~ N (advanced stage / special occasion) Higher stage → the state that naturally follows. General statement, no wish/request. Ages, ranks, seasons: 中学生ともなると, 年末ともなると.
〜ともあろう~tomo arou Someone of the standing of ~ (doing that!) N + ともあろう + 人／方 Respected person + unfitting act → shock/criticism (or: should act fittingly). Reacting to a real lapse: 先生ともあろう方が…なんて.
〜たるもの(は)~taru mono (wa) Anyone who is a ~ (must…) N (responsible/eminent role) General rule / ideal (〜べきだ, 〜なければならない, 〜てはならない). Not for facts. Preaching about how a role should behave: 紳士たるもの….
〜なりに~nari ni In one's own (limited) way N／V・い-adj plain／な-adj stem Word of a low degree + doing one's best within it. Never for a high role. Modest or sympathetic: わたしなりに, 子供なりに, ないならないなりに.
〜といったら／〜にしては／〜だけあって (N2)~to ittara / ~ni shite wa / ~dake atte As for ~ / for a ~ / as expected of ~ N (and others) From the 復習 box: といったら can praise or criticise; にしては = "contrary to expectation"; だけあって = "as expected" (positive). When the evaluation is neutral or positive and you don't need the N1 forms' restrictions.
Exam trap 1 — ともあろう (one event) vs. たるもの (general rule): both attach to respected roles. If the sentence reacts to something that happened (〜なんて, 〜とは, どうして…) → ともあろう. If it states what a role must always do (〜べきだ, 〜てはならない) → たるもの. ともあろう can also do the "should" job ([1〜5] Q2), but たるもの can never react to a real event ([4] Q3).
Exam trap 2 — the banned second clause: after 〜ときたら and 〜ともなると／ともなれば you cannot put the speaker's wish, intention or invitation (〜たい, 〜てほしい, 〜ましょう). Eliminate those options first ([1] Q1, [2] Q2–3, [1〜5] Q4).
Exam trap 3 — high vs. low noun: ともなると, ともあろう and たるもの need a high rank or stage; なりに needs a low or modest one. If the noun is わたし, 子供 or 下手 → なりに; if it's 学長, 党首, 家長 → one of the other three ([1〜5] Q3–5).
