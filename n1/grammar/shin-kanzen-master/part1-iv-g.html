IV G — 文法的性質の整理 (Bunpouteki seishitsu no seiri — Sorting out grammatical properties)

Source

Grammar points covered

Answer key
All 14 answers of 練習1 confirmed from the official 別冊 解答 p.5 (PDF page 197). The 別冊 gives letters only; explanations are mine.
Notes
G introduces no new forms (there are no ＊ entries): it re-sorts forms from 1課–20課 by the restrictions they place on the rest of the sentence — whether a wish/request may follow, whether the subject is 1st or 3rd person, and whether the result is positive or negative. These restrictions are exactly what the 3-option drills in 第1部 tested. 練習1 has 14 two-way choices (a／b); items 9 and 11 compare two whole sentences.

1 Grammar Points

Book's introduction (p.108): N1の文法形式は、それぞれに文法的性質を持っていて、文を作るときの制約になります。以下のような文法的性質に気をつけながら学習しましょう。N1 no bunpou keishiki wa, sorezore ni bunpouteki seishitsu o motte ite, bun o tsukuru toki no seiyaku ni narimasu. Ika no you na bunpouteki seishitsu ni ki o tsukenagara gakushuu shimashou. EN: Each N1 form has its own grammatical properties, which act as constraints when you build a sentence. Study them while paying attention to the properties below. · HI: हर N1 व्याकरण रूप के अपने व्याकरणिक गुण होते हैं, जो वाक्य बनाते समय सीमाएँ तय करते हैं। नीचे दिए गुणों पर ध्यान देते हुए पढ़ें। · GU: દરેક N1 વ્યાકરણ રૂપના પોતાના વ્યાકરણીય ગુણો હોય છે, જે વાક્ય બનાવતી વખતે મર્યાદા બને છે. નીચેના ગુણો પર ધ્યાન આપીને ભણો.

【1】 事実か気持ちが入っているか (Jijitsu ka kimochi ga haitte iru ka)

property group

EN: Fact, or does it contain feelings? · HI: तथ्य है या भावना शामिल है? · GU: હકીકત છે કે લાગણી સમાયેલી છે?

a) 後に話者の希望・意向を表す文や働きかけの文が来る。Ato ni washa no kibou・ikou o arawasu bun ya hatarakikake no bun ga kuru.EN: A sentence of the speaker's wish/intention or a request/command can follow. · HI: बाद में वक्ता की इच्छा/इरादा या अनुरोध वाला वाक्य आ सकता है। · GU: પછી વક્તાની ઇચ્છા/ઇરાદો કે વિનંતીવાળું વાક્ય આવી શકે.

働きかけ = a sentence that acts on the listener (request, command, invitation: 〜てください, 〜なさい, 〜ましょう). 希望・意向 = the speaker's wish or intention (〜たい, 〜(よ)う). Group c bans both; group b bans only 働きかけ. 〜ようでは is the only form that requires a prediction (〜しまいますよ, 〜だろう).

【2】 自分か他者か (Jibun ka tasha ka)

property group

EN: Oneself, or someone else? · HI: ख़ुद या कोई और? · GU: પોતે કે બીજું કોઈ?

【3】 プラスイメージかマイナスイメージか (Purasu imeeji ka mainasu imeeji ka)

property group

EN: Positive image or negative image? · HI: सकारात्मक छवि या नकारात्मक? · GU: હકારાત્મક છબી કે નકારાત્મક?

a) 後には主にマイナスイメージの文が来る。または全体としてマイナスイメージの文になる。Ato ni wa omo ni mainasu imeeji no bun ga kuru. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The three property tests in one table — check them in this order when choosing an ending:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜なり〜なり vs 〜といい〜といい~nari ~nari / ~to ii ~to ii either…or / both…and V-dict／N + なり; N + といい なり〜なり allows a request after it ([1]a); といい〜といい does not ([1]c). Q1 vs Q3.
〜べく vs 〜が早いか~beku / ~ga hayai ka in order to / no sooner than V-dict + べく; V-dict／た + が早いか べく bans only requests ([1]b) — a statement of intention is possible; が早いか bans wishes and requests ([1]c). Q6 vs Q2.
〜ようでは~you de wa if you go on like that V-plain + ようでは The only form here that needs a prediction after it ([1]d). Q8.
〜て(は)いられない／〜を禁じ得ない vs 〜とあって／〜をよそに~te wa irarenai / ~o kinjienai vs ~to atte / ~o yoso ni speaker's feeling vs others' behaviour — [2]a = first-person subject; [2]b = third-person subject. Q9–Q12.
〜ときたら／〜たら最後 vs 〜ならでは／〜を皮切りに~to kitara / ~tara saigo vs ~nara de wa / ~o kawakiri ni negative vs positive image — [3]a forms lead to criticism or bad results; [3]b forms to praise or success. Q13, Q14.
Exam trap 1 — the banned ending: in 3-option items, an answer ending in 〜てください／〜ましょう／〜たい after が早いか, や否や, べく, といい〜といい, ともなると, とはいえ is almost always wrong (Q2, Q3, Q4, Q6, Q7).
Exam trap 2 — whose feeling? 〜てやまない, 〜に堪えない, 〜を禁じ得ない, 〜て(は)いられない describe my feelings; with 母は／彼は they need らしい／ようだ. Conversely なり, をよそに, とあって, きらいがある describe others (Q9–Q12).
