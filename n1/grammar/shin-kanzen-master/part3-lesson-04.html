Notes
Second 視点 lesson (after 3課). 〜てくる = toward the speaker's position; 〜ていく = away from it. That "position" can be the speaker's real place, their psychological place (home, team, own country) or their point in time (now). 練習1 comes in minimal pairs (1-a / 1-b …) that differ only in where the speaker stands. Scan note: the A rule on p.132 prints 「〜ている・〜てくる」; from the heading and examples it is clearly meant to be 「〜ていく・〜てくる」 (likely a misprint).
Book diagram (p.132): 〜てくる ——→ 話者がいる実際の位置／話者の心理的位置／話者がいる時点 ——→ 〜ていく~te kuru → washa ga iru jissai no ichi / washa no shinriteki ichi / washa ga iru jiten → ~te iku
EN: 〜てくる points into the speaker's actual position, psychological position, or present moment; 〜ていく points out of it.
HI: 〜てくる वक्ता की असली जगह, मानसिक जगह या वर्तमान समय की ओर आने को दर्शाता है; 〜ていく उससे दूर जाने को।
GU: 〜てくる વક્તાની ખરી જગ્યા, માનસિક જગ્યા કે વર્તમાન સમય તરફ આવવાનું દર્શાવે છે; 〜ていく તેનાથી દૂર જવાનું.

1 Grammar Points

【A】 「〜てくる・〜ていく」をつける場合 — when you must add them

viewpoint rule
Rule (book)動作の方向や話者のいる位置をはっきりさせるために、移動の動詞や方向のある行為を表す動詞には「〜ている・〜てくる」〔sic: 〜ていく〕をつけたほうがいい場合があります。Dousa no houkou ya washa no iru ichi o hakkiri saseru tame ni, idou no doushi ya houkou no aru koui o arawasu doushi ni wa "~te iku・~te kuru" o tsuketa hou ga ii baai ga arimasu.
Rule (EN)To make clear the direction of an action and where the speaker is, verbs of movement and verbs of directed action (visiting, complaining, phoning…) often need 〜てくる／〜ていく.
Rule (HI)क्रिया की दिशा और वक्ता की स्थिति साफ़ करने के लिए, गति की क्रियाओं और किसी की ओर किए गए कामों (मिलने आना, शिकायत करना…) में अक्सर 〜てくる／〜ていく लगाना चाहिए।
Rule (GU)ક્રિયાની દિશા અને વક્તાનું સ્થાન સ્પષ્ટ કરવા માટે, ગતિની ક્રિયાઓ અને કોઈ તરફ કરાતાં કામો (મળવા આવવું, ફરિયાદ કરવી…)માં ઘણી વાર 〜てくる／〜ていく લગાડવું જોઈએ.

English and Hindi/Gujarati mark this with "me / मुझसे / મને"; Japanese prefers to drop わたしに and let 〜てくる show that the action arrives at the speaker.

【B-1】 視点の固定 — a fixed (psychological) viewpoint

viewpoint rule
SectionB 視点の位置 — 視点の固定Shiten no ichi — shiten no kotei
Rule (book)現在話者が実際にその位置にいなくても、心理的に話者のいる位置に視点を置いて述べることができます。Genzai washa ga jissai ni sono ichi ni inakute mo, shinriteki ni washa no iru ichi ni shiten o oite noberu koto ga dekimasu.
Rule (EN)Even if the speaker is not physically there now, they can place the viewpoint where they feel they belong (their psychological position).
Rule (HI)भले वक्ता अभी असल में उस जगह न हो, वह मन से जिस जगह को अपना मानता है, वहीं दृष्टिकोण रखकर बात कह सकता है।
Rule (GU)ભલે વક્તા અત્યારે ખરેખર એ જગ્યાએ ન હોય, તે મનથી જે જગ્યાને પોતાની માને છે, ત્યાં જ દૃષ્ટિબિંદુ રાખીને વાત કહી શકે છે.

(મનની જગ્યા ≠ વતનનું ઘર)

Same event, different verb: if you think of the family home as "your place" (you're going too), the cousins bring their kids (くる); if you're detached from it, they take them there (いく).

【B-2】 視点の移動 — the viewpoint moves with the speaker

viewpoint rule
Rule (book)複文、または文章の中で話者の心理的位置が変われば、視点も変わります。Fukubun, mata wa bunshou no naka de washa no shinriteki ichi ga kawareba, shiten mo kawarimasu.
Rule (EN)Within a complex sentence or a passage, if the speaker's (psychological) position changes, the viewpoint changes with it.
Rule (HI)किसी मिश्र वाक्य या लेख के भीतर, अगर वक्ता की (मानसिक) जगह बदलती है, तो दृष्टिकोण भी उसके साथ बदल जाता है।
Rule (GU)કોઈ મિશ્ર વાક્ય કે લખાણની અંદર, જો વક્તાની (માનસિક) જગ્યા બદલાય, તો દૃષ્ટિબિંદુ પણ તેની સાથે બદલાય છે.

In each sentence the first half is seen from the starting point (いく = leaving it) and the second half from the destination, where the speaker now is (くる = arriving there).

【B-3】 登場人物の視点 — narrating from a character's position

literary
Rule (book)小説や情景の描写などでは、書き手は感情移入している登場人物の視点に立って書くことが多いです。Shousetsu ya joukei no byousha nado de wa, kakite wa kanjou inyuu shite iru toujou jinbutsu no shiten ni tatte kaku koto ga ooi desu.
Rule (EN)In novels and scene descriptions, the writer usually stands in the position of the character they identify with, and てくる／ていく are chosen from that character's side.
Rule (HI)उपन्यासों और दृश्य-वर्णनों में लेखक अक्सर उस पात्र की जगह खड़ा होकर लिखता है जिससे वह भावनात्मक रूप से जुड़ा है, और てくる／ていく उसी पात्र के हिसाब से चुने जाते हैं।
Rule (GU)નવલકથાઓ અને દૃશ્ય-વર્ણનોમાં લેખક ઘણી વાર એ પાત્રની જગ્યાએ ઊભો રહીને લખે છે જેની સાથે તે લાગણીથી જોડાયેલો છે, અને てくる／ていく એ જ પાત્રના હિસાબે પસંદ થાય છે.

Why: どうすれば…ことができるか looks to the future from now → continuing an action from the present onward → 〜ていく. 4 is past; 3 is just a present state; 〜てくる (1) would look back toward now, which can't follow どうすれば.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every item is てくる vs ていく. Decide in this order: (1) where is the speaker — physically or psychologically? (2) is the movement toward or away from that point? (3) for time: is it up to now or from now on?

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜てくる (space)~te kuru come doing / toward the speaker V-て + くる Movement or action arrives at the speaker's place (訪ねてくる, 文句を言ってくる, 送られてくる). Guests, parcels, phone calls, complaints coming to "me / my place".
〜ていく (space)~te iku go doing / away from the speaker V-て + いく Movement leaves the speaker's place (持っていく, 出ていく, 運ばれていく). Taking things along, people leaving, goods shipped away.
〜てきた (time)~te kita has been ~ing up to now これまで・昔から・この1か月 + 〜てきた Span ends at the present. Looking back over a career, a tradition, a trend (暮らしてきた, 撮り続けてきた).
〜ていく (time)~te iku will go on ~ing from now これから・残りの人生 + 〜ていく Span starts at the present. Resolutions and forecasts (発言をしていく, 守っていく, 変わっていく).
〜てくる (appearance)~te kuru come into view / come about 生える・出る・見える + てくる Something emerges into the observer's world. Weeds coming up (生えてくる), problems surfacing.
視点の固定 vs 視点の移動shiten no kotei / idou fixed vs moving viewpoint — Fixed: anchor at "home" even when away (連れてくる). Moving: the anchor travels with the speaker (持っていく → 抱えてきた). Ask where the speaker feels they are at that point in the passage.
Exam trap 1 — the speaker isn't always where they are: a mother phoning from outside still treats home as her base (帰ってきたら, 持っていった) (練習2 passage 4). A writer in a forest essay treats Japan as "here" (外国から入ってきた) (まとめ [4]).
Exam trap 2 — time words decide: これまで／昔から → 〜てきた; これから／残りの人生／どうすれば…か → 〜ていく. Underline the time word before looking at the options (練習2 passage 5, まとめ [1][5]).
Exam trap 3 — who is in front? In 練習2 passage 3 the same speaker uses 歩いていく (leading from the front) and ついていっているか (watching from the back). The anchor moves with the speaker within one passage (B-2).
