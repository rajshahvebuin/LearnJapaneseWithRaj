16課 — 結末・最終の状態 (Ketsumatsu・saishuu no joutai — Outcomes and final states)

Source

Grammar points covered
〜に至って・〜に至っても〜に至っては〜始末だ〜っぱなしだ
Answer key
All 16 drill answers are confirmed from the official 別冊 解答 booklet, p.3 (PDF page 195), which is bound into the end of this scan — confirmed, not guessed. The 別冊 gives letters only; the "Why" explanations below are mine.
Notes
Three of the four points use 至る ("to reach"), and the exam loves to set them against each other: に至って (things got that far, and only then…), に至っても (even when they got that far, still not…), に至っては (the most extreme example is…). 〜始末だ is a bad final outcome after a bad process; 〜っぱなしだ is a state left as it is or going on and on (usually a bad one). None is tagged 硬 in the book, though the 至る forms sound written. The lower half of p.71 and p.73 is blank in the book.
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.70): three N2 forms for how things finally turned out. Today's N1 forms add a stronger sense of "it went that far" or of a bad end.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に至って・〜に至っても

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni itatte / ~ni itatte mo
Meaning (EN)に至って: "only when things reached the point of ~ (did…)"; に至っても: "even when things reached the point of ~ (still not…)" — book: 事態が〜まで進んでやっとある状態になった・事態が〜まで進んでもある状態にならない (the situation went as far as ~ and only then reached a certain state / even going that far it did not reach that state)
Meaning (HI)...की नौबत आने पर ही (आख़िरकार) / ...की नौबत आने पर भी (नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...ની નોબત આવ્યા પછી જ (આખરે) / ...ની નોબત આવ્યા પછી પણ (નહીં)
Connection (接続)名・動 辞書形 + に至って・に至っても
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but formal — typical of news and essays.
Typical useAttaches to words for a state normally unthinkable (普通では考えられない状態), e.g. deaths, mass culling. After 〜に至って comes what finally happened once things went that far (やっと・初めて・ようやく); after 〜に至っても comes what still didn't happen even then.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (辞書形)V-dictionary + に至って死者が出るに至ってshisha ga deru ni itatte
Verb (辞書形)V-dictionary + に至っても症状が出るに至ってもshoujou ga deru ni itatte mo
NounN + に至っても事態に至ってもjitai ni itatte mo
Verb (た形) / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — ×見たに至って—

死者が出るに至って、国は初めて病気の感染拡大の深刻さに気がついたのだ。Shisha ga deru ni itatte, kuni wa hajimete byouki no kansen kakudai no shinkokusa ni ki ga tsuita no da. Only when people started dying did the government finally realise how serious the spread of the disease was. मौतें होने की नौबत आने पर ही सरकार को पहली बार बीमारी के फैलाव की गंभीरता का एहसास हुआ। મોત થવાની નોબત આવી ત્યારે જ સરકારને પહેલી વાર રોગના ફેલાવાની ગંભીરતાનો ખ્યાલ આવ્યો.
病気の牛20万頭が処分されるに至って、経済的混乱がマスコミに取り上げられるようになった。Byouki no ushi nijuuman-tou ga shobun sareru ni itatte, keizaiteki konran ga masukomi ni toriagerareru you ni natta. It was only when 200,000 sick cattle had been culled that the media began covering the economic chaos. दो लाख बीमार गायों को मारे जाने की नौबत आने पर ही मीडिया ने आर्थिक उथल-पुथल की ख़बरें देनी शुरू कीं। બે લાખ બીમાર ગાયોનો નિકાલ કરવાની નોબત આવી ત્યારે જ મીડિયાએ આર્થિક અંધાધૂંધીના સમાચાર આપવા માંડ્યા.
ひどい症状が出るに至っても、彼は病院へ行こうとしなかった。Hidoi shoujou ga deru ni itatte mo, kare wa byouin e ikou to shinakatta. Even when severe symptoms appeared, he still refused to go to the hospital. गंभीर लक्षण दिखने की नौबत आने पर भी, वह अस्पताल जाने को तैयार नहीं हुआ। ગંભીર લક્ષણો દેખાવાની નોબત આવી તો પણ, તે હૉસ્પિટલ જવા તૈયાર ન થયો.
大切な情報がインターネットで流れるという事態に至っても、ことの重大さを認識できない人たちがいる。Taisetsu na jouhou ga intaanetto de nagareru to iu jitai ni itatte mo, koto no juudaisa o ninshiki dekinai hitotachi ga iru. Even now that it has come to important information leaking onto the internet, some people still can't grasp how serious the matter is. महत्वपूर्ण जानकारी इंटरनेट पर फैल जाने की नौबत आने पर भी, कुछ लोग मामले की गंभीरता नहीं समझ पाते। મહત્ત્વની માહિતી ઇન્ટરનેટ પર ફેલાઈ જવાની નોબત આવી તો પણ, કેટલાક લોકો બાબતની ગંભીરતા સમજી શકતા નથી.

Think of a crisis that keeps getting worse. 〜に至って marks the point at which someone finally reacts — often with 初めて, やっと, ようやく — and carries the criticism "too late!". 〜に至っても marks a point at which they still don't react. The thing before it must be a serious, extreme development (3度も交渉に失敗する, not 交渉を始める — [1] Q4), and the verb is always 辞書形 even for past events ([1] Q1).

【2】 〜に至っては

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni itatte wa
Meaning (EN)"as for ~ (the most extreme case), it is even…" — book: 〜という極端な例では、ある状態だ (in the extreme example of ~, the state is such-and-such)
Meaning (HI)...का तो हाल यह है कि... (सबसे बुरा उदाहरण)
Meaning (GU)...ની તો હાલત એ છે કે... (સૌથી ખરાબ ઉદાહરણ)
Connection (接続)名 + に至っては
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; formal-sounding, common in reports and complaints.
Typical useExpresses the evaluation that the situation as a whole is shocking/terrible (あきれた、ひどい). Attaches to the most extreme example within that whole, and the second clause says what state that example is in.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に至っては長崎県に至ってはNagasaki-ken ni itatte wa
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns (the extreme example) only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
デパートの閉店が相次いでいる。Aデパートに至ってはすでに三つの支店が閉店した。Depaato no heiten ga aitsuide iru. Ee depaato ni itatte wa sude ni mittsu no shiten ga heiten shita. Department stores are closing one after another. Store A, worst of all, has already shut three branches. डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर एक के बाद एक बंद हो रहे हैं। A स्टोर का तो हाल यह है कि उसकी तीन शाखाएँ पहले ही बंद हो चुकी हैं। ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર એક પછી એક બંધ થઈ રહ્યા છે. A સ્ટોરની તો હાલત એ છે કે તેની ત્રણ શાખા પહેલેથી બંધ થઈ ગઈ છે.
毎年この地方は洪水の被害を受ける。長崎県に至っては今年もう3回目だ。Maitoshi kono chihou wa kouzui no higai o ukeru. Nagasaki-ken ni itatte wa kotoshi mou sankaime da. This region suffers flood damage every year. Nagasaki Prefecture has already been hit three times this year. यह इलाक़ा हर साल बाढ़ की मार झेलता है। नागासाकी प्रांत का तो हाल यह है कि इस साल यह तीसरी बार है। આ પ્રદેશ દર વર્ષે પૂરનો માર ખમે છે. નાગાસાકી પ્રાંતની તો હાલત એ છે કે આ વર્ષે આ ત્રીજી વાર છે.
わたしは理数系の科目が不得意だった。物理に至っては全く理解できなかった。Watashi wa risuukei no kamoku ga futokui datta. Butsuri ni itatte wa mattaku rikai dekinakatta. I was weak at science and maths subjects. Physics, worst of all, I couldn't understand at all. मैं विज्ञान-गणित के विषयों में कमज़ोर था। भौतिकी का तो यह हाल था कि ज़रा भी समझ नहीं आती थी। હું વિજ્ઞાન-ગણિતના વિષયોમાં નબળો હતો. ભૌતિકશાસ્ત્રની તો એ હાલત હતી કે જરાય સમજાતું નહોતું.

The structure is always two-step: first a general bad situation (閉店が相次いでいる, 洪水の被害, 理数系が不得意), then "X に至っては" + the worst case. So the second clause must be more extreme than the general trend: "only two-thirds of last year's job offers" fits, "slightly lower than last year" does not ([2] Q2). Don't mix it up with に至って (time: "only when it came to that").

【3】 〜始末だ

neutral register
Reading~shimatsu da
Meaning (EN)"and in the end it came to (the bad state of) ~; ended up ~ing" — book: 悪い状態が経過し、とうとう〜という悪い結末になった (a bad situation went on and finally led to the bad outcome ~)
Meaning (HI)(हालात बिगड़ते-बिगड़ते) आख़िर ...तक की नौबत आ गई
Meaning (GU)(સ્થિતિ બગડતાં બગડતાં) આખરે ...સુધીની નોબત આવી
Connection (接続)動 辞書形 + 始末だ
RegisterNeutral; carries the speaker's exasperation or disapproval.
Typical useAttaches to a sentence describing a bad outcome. Usually the preceding part explains that a bad state had been continuing. Words that stress the outcome — ついに・とうとう・〜まで — often appear with it.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (辞書形)V-dictionary + 始末だ辞めてしまう始末だyamete shimau shimatsu da
Verb (辞書形), pastV-dictionary + 始末だった泣き出す始末だったnakidasu shimatsu datta

おいは遊んでばかりいてまじめに働きもせず、とうとう会社を辞めてしまう始末だ。Oi wa asonde bakari ite majime ni hataraki mo sezu, toutou kaisha o yamete shimau shimatsu da. My nephew did nothing but play and never worked seriously, and in the end he went and quit his company. मेरा भतीजा बस मौज करता रहा, मन लगाकर काम नहीं किया, और आख़िर में कंपनी ही छोड़ बैठा। મારો ભત્રીજો બસ મોજ કરતો રહ્યો, મન લગાવીને કામ ન કર્યું, અને આખરે કંપની જ છોડી બેઠો.
田中さんはお酒を飲んで大きな声でしゃべり続けた後で、ついに泣き出す始末だった。Tanaka-san wa osake o nonde ookina koe de shaberitsuzuketa ato de, tsui ni nakidasu shimatsu datta. Mr. Tanaka got drunk and kept talking loudly, and finally ended up bursting into tears. तनाका जी शराब पीकर ज़ोर-ज़ोर से बोलते रहे, और आख़िरकार रोने तक की नौबत आ गई। તનાકાજી દારૂ પીને જોરજોરથી બોલતા રહ્યા, અને આખરે રડવા સુધીની નોબત આવી.
兄は借金を返すためと言って家にあるものをお金に換え、父の時計まで売ってしまう始末だ。Ani wa shakkin o kaesu tame to itte ie ni aru mono o okane ni kae, chichi no tokei made utte shimau shimatsu da. Saying it was to repay his debts, my brother turned things in the house into cash — he even went as far as selling our father's watch. क़र्ज़ चुकाने के नाम पर भाई ने घर की चीज़ें बेच-बेचकर पैसे बनाए, यहाँ तक कि पिताजी की घड़ी तक बेच डाली। દેવું ચૂકવવાના નામે ભાઈએ ઘરની વસ્તુઓ વેચી વેચીને પૈસા કર્યા, ત્યાં સુધી કે પિતાજીની ઘડિયાળ પણ વેચી નાખી.

始末 literally means "how a matter ends up". The pattern is "bad process → worst result + 始末だ", and that result is described by someone fed up with it. A neutral or good result (もう知っている, 感謝される) can't end in 始末だ ([3] Q2), and neither can a step that is actually an improvement (アルバイトを探し始める, [3] Q3). The verb is always 辞書形 ([3] Q1).

【4】 〜っぱなしだ

neutral register (conversational feel)
Reading~ppanashi da
Meaning (EN)"(left) ~ing; ~ all the time / non-stop" — book: 普通はないような〜という状態が続いている (an unusual state of ~ is continuing)
Meaning (HI)...ही छोड़ देना / लगातार ...करते रहना
Meaning (GU)...જ છોડી દેવું / સતત ...કર્યા કરવું
Connection (接続)動 ます形（−ます） + っぱなしだ
RegisterNeutral; very common in everyday speech.
Typical useMainly for undesirable states (主に良くない状態). Often expresses dissatisfaction or criticism. Two shades: (a) something done and left as is (つけっぱなし, 借りっぱなし, 出しっぱなし); (b) an action that goes on non-stop (立ちっぱなし, 言われっぱなし, しゃべりっぱなし). Used as 〜っぱなしで／〜っぱなしの N／〜っぱなしにする・なる.

昨日は電気をつけっぱなしで寝てしまった。Kinou wa denki o tsukeppanashi de nete shimatta. Last night I fell asleep with the lights left on. कल रात मैं बत्ती जलती छोड़कर ही सो गया। ગઈ રાતે હું લાઇટ ચાલુ જ રાખીને સૂઈ ગયો.
友達に半年も本を借りっぱなしになっている。Tomodachi ni hantoshi mo hon o karippanashi ni natte iru. I've had a book borrowed from a friend for a whole six months without returning it. दोस्त से ली हुई किताब छह महीने से मेरे पास ही पड़ी है, लौटाई नहीं। મિત્ર પાસેથી લીધેલું પુસ્તક છ મહિનાથી મારી પાસે જ પડ્યું છે, પાછું નથી આપ્યું.
一日立ちっぱなしの仕事なので、足が疲れる。Ichinichi tachippanashi no shigoto na node, ashi ga tsukareru. It's a job where I'm on my feet all day, so my legs get tired. यह दिन भर लगातार खड़े रहने वाला काम है, इसलिए पैर थक जाते हैं। આ આખો દિવસ સતત ઊભા રહેવાનું કામ છે, એટલે પગ થાકી જાય છે.
相手も悪いのに、わたしだけ言われっぱなしで何も言い返せなかった。Aite mo warui no ni, watashi dake iwareppanashi de nani mo iikaesenakatta. The other person was in the wrong too, yet I just took it all and couldn't say a thing back. ग़लती सामने वाले की भी थी, फिर भी सिर्फ़ मुझे ही सुनाया जाता रहा और मैं कुछ जवाब न दे सका। ભૂલ સામેવાળાની પણ હતી, છતાં ફક્ત મને જ સંભળાવાતું રહ્યું અને હું કંઈ સામો જવાબ ન આપી શક્યો.

The "left as is" shade fits verbs that should normally be followed by an undoing action: 出す → put away, つける → turn off, 借りる → return. So a book lying around is 出しっぱなし, not 片付けっぱなし (tidying isn't a state you "forget to undo", [4] Q1). Because it's usually negative, a good result (冷えておいしくなった) after 〜っぱなしにして is unnatural ([4] Q2). The "non-stop" shade is where しゃべりっぱなし (talked all night, [4] Q4) belongs.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The three 至る forms differ by one particle, and the exam tests exactly that particle. 始末だ and っぱなし are told apart by end result vs. continuing state:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に至って~ni itatte Only when it came to ~ (did…) N／V-辞書形 Turning point → something finally happens (やっと・初めて・ようやく). Implies "too late". Crisis reports: 死者が出るに至って初めて….
〜に至っても~ni itatte mo Even when it came to ~ (still not…) N／V-辞書形 Turning point → something still doesn't happen. Second clause negative in meaning. Criticising inaction: 3度失敗するに至っても気づかない.
〜に至っては~ni itatte wa As for ~ (the extreme case) N only Not a time sequence: picks the worst example out of a bad general trend. "Everyone is bad; X is the worst": 20代の若者に至っては….
〜始末だ~shimatsu da Ended up (badly) ~ V-辞書形 Bad process → bad final result; often with ついに・とうとう・〜まで. Exasperated story endings: 会社を辞めてしまう始末だ.
〜っぱなしだ~ppanashi da Left ~ing / ~ non-stop V-ます stem A continuing state, usually undesirable; not an end point. Lights left on, standing all day, phone ringing all day.
〜末／〜ずじまい／〜たきり (N2)~sue / ~zujimai / ~ta kiri After ~ / ended up never ~ / since ~, no more V-た + 末; V-ない stem + ずじまい; V-た + きり From the 復習 box: 末 = result after long effort (neutral); ずじまい = a chance missed; たきり = nothing since. When the result is not especially "unthinkable" or exasperating.
Exam trap 1 — て／ても／ては: read the second clause first. Something finally changes (ようやく, 初めて) → 至って. Something still doesn't change (〜ない, 〜ようとしなかった) → 至っても. A general trend followed by the worst example → 至っては ([1] Q3, [2] Q1, [1〜4] Q3).
Exam trap 2 — 辞書形 only: 〜に至って(も) and 〜始末だ both take the dictionary form, even in past-tense stories (見るに至って, 命令する始末だ). た形 options are distractors ([1] Q1, [3] Q1).
Exam trap 3 — 始末だ vs. っぱなし: 一日中, 朝まで, ずっと → っぱなし (a continuing state). とうとう, ついに, 〜まで, さえ → 始末だ (a bad end point) ([1〜4] Q1–2).
