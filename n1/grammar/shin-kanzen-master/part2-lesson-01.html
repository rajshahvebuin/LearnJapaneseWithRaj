Answer key
The 別冊 解答 p.5 (PDF page 197) gives only the number of the tile in the ★ slot for each of the 12 items. All 12 ★ tiles below are confirmed from it; the full orders are my own reasoning (each one checked to put the official tile at ★). Explanations are mine.
Notes
p.112 is a rule page: forms that must be followed by a negative, forms that attach to a question word, and forms that attach to a number, each cross-referenced to the 第1部 lesson where it was taught. p.113 has 12 ★ items in the official JLPT 問題2 format (four blanks, ★ always in the 3rd slot here). Strategy: find the fixed partner first (何＋にもまして, 1匹＋たりとも, 〜ようにも＋〜ない), then fit the rest around it.

1 Grammar Points

The book's rule (p.112): 文を組み立てるときは、組み立てのルールに従わなければなりません。 — When you build a sentence you must follow the building rules. These three groups are the ones worth memorising, because each form "pulls" a specific partner into the sentence.

〜にしたところで~ni shita tokoro deわたしにしたところでいい案を持っているわけではない。Watashi ni shita tokoro de ii an o motte iru wake de wa nai.Even I, for my part, don't exactly have a good idea.मेरे पास भी कोई अच्छा उपाय हो, ऐसा नहीं है।મારી પાસે પણ કોઈ સારો ઉપાય છે એવું નથી.第1部 IV D

Every example ends in ない／なかった. In ★ items this means the negative verb is usually already printed after the blanks, so the tile with the form (をおいて, たりとも, にしたところで) goes near the end, right before the negative chunk. 〜に〜ない and 〜(よ)うにも〜ない also need the same verb twice (出るに出られない, 起き上がろうにも起き上がれない).

When a tile set contains 何／だれ／どこ／どんな, look for its partner among the other tiles: 何＋にもまして ("more than anything"), どこから＋ともなく, どんな N＋であれ, だれが何を〜た＋ところで. These two tiles are almost always adjacent or one chunk apart.

neutral
FormBook exampleEnglishHindiGujaratiTaught in
〜といったところだ~to itta tokoro daわたしの家から駅まで、7、8分といったところです。Watashi no ie kara eki made, nana, happun to itta tokoro desu.From my house to the station it's about 7 or 8 minutes, at most.मेरे घर से स्टेशन तक बस 7-8 मिनट का रास्ता है।મારા ઘરથી સ્ટેશન સુધી બસ 7-8 મિનિટનો રસ્તો છે.第1部 2課

〜からある~kara aru母は15キロからある荷物も軽々と運ぶ。Haha wa juugo kiro kara aru nimotsu mo karugaru to hakobu.My mother carries even loads of a good 15 kilos with ease.मेरी माँ 15 किलो तक के भारी सामान को भी आसानी से उठा लेती हैं।મારી મા 15 કિલો જેટલો ભારે સામાન પણ સહેલાઈથી ઊંચકી લે છે.第1部 17課
〜というもの(は)~to iu mono (wa)この10年というもの、仕事に追われて趣味を楽しむ余裕がなかった。Kono juunen to iu mono, shigoto ni owarete shumi o tanoshimu yoyuu ga nakatta.For these past ten years I've been so swamped with work that I had no time to enjoy hobbies.इन 10 सालों में काम में इतना व्यस्त रहा कि शौक के लिए समय ही नहीं मिला।આ 10 વર્ષોમાં કામમાં એટલો વ્યસ્ત રહ્યો કે શોખ માટે સમય જ ન મળ્યો.第1部 IV D

The number decides the form: small numbers (せいぜい 7、8分) → といったところだ; 1 + counter (1分, 1匹, 一瞬) → たりとも + negative; a large, impressive quantity (15キロ, 100ページ) → からある／からする／からの + noun; a period (この10年, 3か月) → というもの. So 1匹 and たりとも, 100ページ and からある are tile pairs.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

12 ★ items from p.113 (次の文の★に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4の中から一つ選びなさい). In every item the ★ is the third of the four blanks. The correct row in each options table is the tile that goes in the ★ slot (confirmed from 別冊 p.5); the order chain and assembled sentence are my reasoning.

Q1

この旅行で ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ でも気軽に話せたことだ。Kono ryokou de ___ ___ ★ ___ demo kigaru ni hanaseta koto da.

Tiles: 1 何 (nani)　2 だれと (dare to)　3 にもまして (ni mo mashite)　4 良かったことは (yokatta koto wa)

1 何→3 にもまして→4 良かったことは→2 だれと

この旅行で何にもまして良かったことは、だれとでも気軽に話せたことだ。Kono ryokou de nani ni mo mashite yokatta koto wa, dare to demo kigaru ni hanaseta koto da.

EN: What was better than anything else on this trip was that I could talk casually with anyone.
HI: इस यात्रा में सबसे बढ़कर अच्छी बात यह थी कि मैं किसी से भी आराम से बात कर सका।
GU: આ પ્રવાસમાં સૌથી વધુ સારી વાત એ હતી કે હું કોઈની પણ સાથે સહેલાઈથી વાત કરી શક્યો.

1何naniwhatक्याશું
2だれとdare towith whomकिसके साथકોની સાથે
3にもましてni mo mashitemore thanसे भी बढ़करથી પણ વધુ
4良かったことはyokatta koto wathe good thing wasअच्छी बात यह थीસારી વાત એ હતી

Q6

彼女の ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ あるのだろうと思って、何も聞かなかった。Kanojo no ___ ___ ★ ___ aru no darou to omotte, nani mo kikanakatta.

Tiles: 1 話せない (hanasenai)　2 話すに (hanasu ni)　3 退職には (taishoku ni wa)　4 事情が (jijou ga)

3 退職には→2 話すに→1 話せない→4 事情が

彼女の退職には話すに話せない事情があるのだろうと思って、何も聞かなかった。Kanojo no taishoku ni wa hanasu ni hanasenai jijou ga aru no darou to omotte, nani mo kikanakatta.

Q8

Q9

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The ★ items reward knowing which partner each form demands. These are the pairs most easily mixed up:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たりとも~tari tomo not even one ~ 1 + counter (一瞬, 1分, 1匹) + たりとも … ない Minimum quantity; must end in a negative or prohibition. Stressing "not a single one".
〜からある~kara aru a good (large amount) or more large number + からある + N Maximum / impressive quantity; modifies a following noun; no negative needed. Emphasising how big/heavy/long something is.
〜といったところだ~to itta tokoro da at most ~, about ~ small number + といったところだ Sentence-final; plays the amount down. Saying "only about X".
〜というもの(は)~to iu mono (wa) for a whole (period) period (この10年, 1か月) + というもの Time length + continuing state afterwards. Looking back on a long stretch.
〜にもまして~ni mo mashite more than ~ 何／だれ＋にもまして; 以前にもまして With a question word = "more than anything / anyone". Ranking something top.
〜たところで vs 〜にしたところで~ta tokoro de / ~ni shita tokoro de even if (one) does ~ / even for N V-た + ところで; N + にしたところで Both need a negative/futile result; the first follows a verb (often with どんなに／だれが何を), the second a noun (often a person). Futility vs. "N is no exception".
〜に〜ない vs 〜(よ)うにも〜ない~ni ~nai / ~(y)ou ni mo ~nai want to but can't V-dict + に + V-potential-neg; V-vol + にも + V-potential-neg Same verb twice in both; the first takes the dictionary form, the second the volitional. Circumstances block an action.
Exam trap 1 — the negative is already printed: in ★ items with をおいて／なしには／たりとも／にしたところで, the negative (ない, できない, するな) is usually printed after the last blank, so the form goes in the 2nd or 3rd slot, not the 4th (Q4, Q9, Q12).
Exam trap 2 — question word ↔ partner: if a tile is 何／だれ／どんな, find its partner tile (にもまして, ともなく, であれ, ところで) and place them as a unit before anything else (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7).
Exam trap 3 — doubled verb: 話すに話せない and 直そうにも直せない — the second verb may be printed outside the blanks (Q11), so the first half must be the last tile.
