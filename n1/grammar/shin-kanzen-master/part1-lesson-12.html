Notes
Five "because" forms, each with its own emotional colour and its own rule for what follows: ばこそ (a surprising reason, + のだ), とあって (a special situation → its natural result, never about yourself), ではあるまいし (a scolding "you're not a ~", + judgement/advice), 手前 (saving face → "I have to / I can't"), ゆえ(に) (formal letters/official writing). 〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.52): three N2 reason forms — an excuse (〜ものだから), an obligation that follows from a decision (〜からには) and a small cause with a bad result (〜ばかりに). Today's N1 forms add similar nuances.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ばこそ

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; emphatic, rather literary)
Reading~ba koso
Meaning (EN)"precisely because ~; it is just because ~ that…" — book: まさに〜からそうなる・〜からあえてそうする (it turns out so exactly because of ~ / one deliberately does so because of ~)
Meaning (HI)ठीक इसीलिए कि... / ...है, तभी तो...
Meaning (GU)બરાબર એટલા માટે કે... / ...છે, ત્યારે જ તો...
Connection (接続)動 ば形・イ形 −ければ・ナ形 −であれば・名 −であれば ＋こそ

今苦しければこそ、後で本当の喜びがある。Ima kurushikereba koso, ato de hontou no yorokobi ga aru. It's because things are hard now that real joy will come later. अभी कठिनाई है, इसीलिए बाद में सच्ची ख़ुशी मिलती है। અત્યારે મુશ્કેલી છે, એટલે જ પછી સાચો આનંદ મળે છે.

Think "A, therefore — surprisingly — B". If the result is the obvious consequence (busy → no time to eat; low income → can't live), ばこそ is wrong; it needs a twist (busy → you learn to manage time well). Notice that the second clause in ① and the drills is often the speaker's explanation with 〜のだ, and the connection is always the ば-form (なら/たら are not used). Drill [1] tests both the form (Q1) and the twist (Q2–Q5).

Reading~to atte
Meaning (EN)"because it is (such a special occasion as) ~, naturally…" — book: 〜という特別な状況だから、当然結果も特別だ (because the situation is special, the result is naturally special too)
Meaning (HI)...होने के कारण (ख़ास मौक़ा है, तो स्वाभाविक रूप से)...

Since the newspaper ran a story about the owner's paintings, every customer who comes in stops to look at the paintings on the walls. अख़बार में दुकान-मालिक की पेंटिंग्स की ख़बर छपी है, इसलिए यहाँ आने वाला हर ग्राहक दुकान में सजी पेंटिंग्स को निहारकर जाता है। છાપામાં દુકાન-માલિકનાં ચિત્રોના સમાચાર છપાયા છે, એટલે અહીં આવતો દરેક ગ્રાહક દુકાનમાં સજાવેલાં ચિત્રો નિહાળીને જાય છે.

A reporter's form: "special circumstance → what you'd expect to see". Three tests in drill [2]: the situation must be out of the ordinary (梅雨の晴れ間, not 平日 — Q1); the result must be a fact, not a wish or invitation (Q2); and the speaker cannot describe himself (×わたしも勉強を始めた — Q3). Don't confuse it with 9課's 〜とあれば ("if it's for ~, I'll do anything"), which is a condition and is followed by the speaker's will.

【3】 〜ではあるまいし

neutral register (not marked 硬; book tag 話し言葉 = spoken language)
Reading~de wa aru mai shi (colloquial: ~ja aru mai shi)
Meaning (EN)"it's not as if you were ~, so…; you're not a ~, after all" — book: 〜ならそのようなこともあるかもしれないが、〜ではないのだから (that might happen if it were ~, but since it isn't ~…)
Meaning (HI)(तुम) कोई ...तो हो नहीं, फिर...
Meaning (GU)(તમે) કંઈ ...તો છો નહીં, પછી...
Connection (接続)名 ＋ではあるまいし　(also 普通形 + わけではあるまいし・のではあるまいし)
Register話し言葉 (spoken language). じゃあるまいし is even more casual (②).
Typical useThe second clause is the speaker's judgement, claim or advice that rejects the current state (現状を否定する): "so don't ~ / so there's no need to ~ / so why are you ~?". With the plain form + 〜わけではあるまいし／〜のではあるまいし it can follow non-nouns (④).

You're not a child — you shouldn't pull such a sulky face just because you're sleepy or hungry. कोई बच्चे तो हो नहीं, सिर्फ़ नींद या भूख की वजह से इतना मुँह फुलाना ठीक नहीं। કંઈ બાળક તો છો નહીં, માત્ર ઊંઘ કે ભૂખને કારણે આટલું મોં ચડાવવું યોગ્ય નથી.

It's not your first interview — why are you so nervous this time? पहला इंटरव्यू तो है नहीं, इस बार इतने घबराए हुए क्यों हो? પહેલો ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ તો છે નહીં, આ વખતે આટલા ગભરાયેલા કેમ છો?
あなたが悪かったわけではあるまいし、そんなに自分を責めることはないよ。Anata ga warukatta wake de wa aru mai shi, sonna ni jibun o semeru koto wa nai yo. It's not as if it was your fault — there's no need to blame yourself so much. ग़लती तुम्हारी तो थी नहीं, ख़ुद को इतना दोष देने की ज़रूरत नहीं। ભૂલ તમારી તો હતી નહીં, પોતાને આટલો દોષ દેવાની જરૂર નથી.

まい is the negative-conjecture auxiliary ("surely isn't"), so the literal sense is "surely you aren't a ~, and…". The noun is something obviously not true of the person (子供, 神様, コンピューター), and the sentence then scolds, reassures or refuses. Because it's spoken, it pairs naturally with 〜な, 〜ことはない, 〜なさい, 〜の？. A neutral fact or a compliment after it is wrong ([3] Q4 a).

子供たちの手前、父親がこんな酔っ払った姿で帰宅しては体裁が悪い。Kodomotachi no temae, chichioya ga konna yopparatta sugata de kitaku shite wa teisai ga warui. In front of the children, it looks bad for a father to come home drunk like this. बच्चों के सामने, पिता का इस तरह नशे में घर लौटना अच्छा नहीं लगता। બાળકોની સામે, પિતાનું આ રીતે નશામાં ઘરે આવવું સારું નથી લાગતું.
ご近所の手前、家に警察官が来たことは知られたくない。Gokinjo no temae, ie ni keisatsukan ga kita koto wa shiraretakunai. Because of the neighbours, I don't want anyone to know a police officer came to our house. पड़ोसियों की वजह से, मैं नहीं चाहता कि किसी को पता चले कि घर पर पुलिस आई थी। પાડોશીઓને લીધે, હું નથી ઇચ્છતો કે કોઈને ખબર પડે કે ઘરે પોલીસ આવી હતી.

手前 literally means "this side / in front of" — you are acting in front of someone's eyes, or in front of your own words. So it is about face (面目, 体裁), and what follows limits your own freedom ("I can't not…", "I have to…"). An instruction to someone else (×あなたも参加してください) or a mere result (×払えなくなった) is wrong — see drill [4].

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All five forms (plus the three 〔復習〕 forms) mean "because", so the exam never tests the meaning alone. It tests what kind of reason and what may follow:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ばこそ~ba koso Precisely because ~ V-ば／い-ければ／な・N −であれば + こそ An unexpected reason stressed as the true one; ends in 〜のだ. Not for the obvious result. Heartfelt explanations (愛していればこそ別れる).
〜とあって~to atte Because it's (special) ~, naturally… N・普通形 + とあって Special situation → its natural, observed result. Fact only; not about the speaker. News-style scene reports (連休とあって人でいっぱい).
〜とあれば (9課)~to areba If it's for ~ / if it's the case that ~ N・普通形 + とあれば Same look as とあって, but a condition; followed by the speaker's wish/intention/judgement. 子供のためとあれば何でもする.
〜ではあるまいし~de wa aru mai shi You're not a ~, so… N + ではあるまいし (普通形 + わけ／の) 話し言葉. Something obviously untrue → scolding, reassurance, refusal. 子供じゃあるまいし、泣くな.
〜手前~temae Having said/done ~ (in front of others)… N の／V-辞書形・た形・ている形 + 手前 About face; second clause restricts the speaker (〜わけにはいかない／〜なければならない／〜ざるをえない). 約束した手前、やるしかない.
〜ゆえ(に)~yue (ni) Owing to ~ N (の)／普通形 (な-adj な・である, N である) 書き言葉; letters and official contexts only. Everything around it must be formal. 慣れないことゆえ、お許しください.
〜ものだから (N2 〔復習〕)~mono da kara It's just that ~ (excuse) 普通形 + ものだから Justifies something, usually a mishap; spoken. 残業が続いたものだから疲れた.
〜からには (N2 〔復習〕)~kara ni wa Now that ~ 普通形 + からには Decision/fact → obligation or resolve; close to 手前 but without the "others' eyes" nuance. 買ったからには活用しなければ.
〜ばかりに (N2 〔復習〕)~bakari ni Just because ~ (bad result) 普通形 + ばかりに A small cause → regretted bad result. 1字間違えたばかりに届かなかった.
Exam trap 1 — ばこそ is not a plain から: if the result is the obvious one (忙しい→時間がない, 収入が少ない→暮らせない), ばこそ is wrong. Look for the twist + 〜のだ ([1] Q2, Q3, Q5). And its connection is always the ば-form (高度であればこそ, never ×だったらばこそ — [1] Q1).
Exam trap 2 — とあって bans the speaker: 〜とあって, わたしも… is wrong; so are wishes and invitations after it. The result must be what other people did or what happened ([2] Q2–Q4). Contrast 〜手前, which is always about the speaker's own constraint ([4] Q2).
Exam trap 3 — ではあるまいし after a verb: it needs a bridge: 行くわけではあるまいし／行くのではあるまいし. ×行くではあるまいし and ×行くことではあるまいし are wrong ([3] Q2).
