7課 — 付随行動 (Fuzui koudou — Accompanying actions)

Source
新完全マスター文法 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Bunpou, スリーエーネットワーク) — printed p.34–35 (PDF pages 45–46). One of the book's two short (2-page) lessons.
Grammar points covered
〜がてら〜かたがた〜かたわら
Answer key
All 12 drill answers are confirmed from the official 別冊 解答 booklet, p.2 (PDF page 194), which is bound into the end of this scan — confirmed, not guessed. The 別冊 gives letters only; the "Why" explanations below are mine.
Notes
All three mean "doing B along with A", and all three are tested on what kind of A they take: がてら — an activity involving movement (散歩・買い物), casual; かたがた — a polite purpose (あいさつ・お礼・報告・見舞い) followed by a movement verb (行く・訪ねる・伺う), formal; かたわら — one's main occupation, followed by a separate, long-term social activity. None is tagged 硬 in the book, but かたがた belongs to polite/business Japanese. Drills are 3-option (a–c).
〔復習〕 Review (N2 form the book reminds you of first, p.34): ついでに — "while you're at it". All three 7課 forms are more specialised versions of it.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
スーパーへ買い物に行くついでに、クリーニング屋にも寄った。Suupaa e kaimono ni iku tsuide ni, kuriiningu-ya ni mo yotta. While I was going to the supermarket, I also dropped by the dry cleaner's. सुपरमार्केट जाते हुए, साथ ही ड्राई-क्लीनर के यहाँ भी हो आया। સુપરમાર્કેટ જતાં-જતાં, સાથે ડ્રાય-ક્લીનરને ત્યાં પણ જઈ આવ્યો.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜がてら

neutral register (casual–neutral)
Reading~gatera
Meaning (EN)"while ~ing, (also do…); combining ~ with…" — book: 〜のついでに、その機会を利用してあることをする (taking the chance while doing ~, one also does something)
Meaning (HI)~ करते-करते साथ में (मौक़े का फ़ायदा उठाकर) ... भी
Meaning (GU)~ કરતાં-કરતાં સાથે (મોકાનો લાભ લઈને) ... પણ
Connection (接続)名 する・動 ます + がてら
RegisterNeutral, fairly casual — fine in conversation.
Typical useAttaches mainly to nouns for actions that involve going somewhere / moving (散歩・買い物, etc.).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する-noun)N(する) + がてら散歩がてらsanpo gatera

い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — the pattern needs an action—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
散歩がてらちょっとパンを買いに行ってきます。Sanpo gatera chotto pan o kai ni itte kimasu. I'll take a walk and pick up some bread on the way. टहलते-टहलते ज़रा ब्रेड ख़रीद लाता हूँ। ફરતાં-ફરતાં જરા બ્રેડ ખરીદી લાવું છું.
花火の見物がてら一度うちへもおいでください。Hanabi no kenbutsu gatera ichido uchi e mo oide kudasai. When you come to see the fireworks, please drop by our house too. आतिशबाज़ी देखने आएँ तो साथ में एक बार हमारे घर भी ज़रूर आइए। આતશબાજી જોવા આવો ત્યારે સાથે એક વાર અમારે ઘરે પણ જરૂર આવજો.
友達を駅まで送りがてらDVDを返してきた。Tomodachi o eki made okuri gatera diibuidii o kaeshite kita. While seeing my friend off to the station, I returned the DVD. दोस्त को स्टेशन तक छोड़ने गया तो साथ में DVD भी लौटा आया। મિત્રને સ્ટેશન સુધી મૂકવા ગયો ત્યારે સાથે DVD પણ પાછી આપી આવ્યો.

A is the "outing" (walk, shopping, seeing someone off) and B is an extra errand squeezed into it. B must be a separate action from A: "exercise → running 3 km" or "shopping → buying" are the same thing, not an extra (drill [1] Q1–2). The A verb also needs to be a deliberate, movement-type activity — 急ぐ or 見つける don't work (Q3).

【2】 〜かたがた

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; polite/business)
Reading~katagata
Meaning (EN)"(go/visit) partly to ~, also as a ~" — book: 〜という別の目的も持って、あることをする (doing something while also having ~ as another purpose)
Meaning (HI)~ के उद्देश्य से भी (साथ ही) ... (जाना/मिलना)
Meaning (GU)~ ના હેતુથી પણ (સાથે) ... (જવું/મળવું)
Connection (接続)名 する + かたがた
RegisterNot tagged 硬, but it is polite/formal — letters, business visits, greetings.
Typical useFollowed very often by verbs of movement (行く・訪ねる, etc.). The noun is typically a social purpose: お礼・あいさつ・報告・見舞い・おわび.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する-noun)N(する) + かたがたご報告かたがたgohoukoku katagata
NounN + かたがたお礼かたがたorei katagata
Verb(added, not in book) not used — nouns only (×報告しかたがた)—
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—

部長のお宅へお礼かたがたごあいさつに行こうと思っています。Buchou no otaku e orei katagata goaisatsu ni ikou to omotte imasu. I'm thinking of visiting the department head's home to pay my respects and to thank him as well. मैं सोच रहा हूँ कि विभाग-प्रमुख के घर धन्यवाद देने और साथ ही मिलने-जुलने जाऊँ। હું વિચારું છું કે વિભાગ-પ્રમુખને ઘરે આભાર માનવા અને સાથે મળવા જાઉં.

見学かたがた祖父が入所している老人ホームを訪ねた。Kengaku katagata sofu ga nyuusho shite iru roujin hoomu o tazuneta. I visited the nursing home where my grandfather lives, partly to have a look around it. दादाजी जिस वृद्धाश्रम में रहते हैं, उसे देखने के इरादे से भी मैं वहाँ गया। દાદાજી જે વૃદ્ધાશ્રમમાં રહે છે, તે જોવાના ઇરાદાથી પણ હું ત્યાં ગયો.

The noun before かたがた is a second purpose for a visit — not the visit itself. So 訪問かたがた訪ねる or おじゃまかたがた伺う just repeat the main action (drill [2] Q1–2). The main verb should be a movement verb: 見舞いかたがた手伝いに行った ✓, but 見舞いかたがた手伝った／果物を贈った ✗ (Q3). For an apology to a business partner, use the polite かたがた, not casual がてら ([1〜3] Q2).

【3】 〜かたわら

neutral register (written feel)
Reading~katawara
Meaning (EN)"besides (one's main work) ~, also…; alongside ~" — book: 〜という本業をしながら、別の活動もしている (while doing ~ as one's main work, one also does another activity)
Meaning (HI)(मुख्य काम) ~ के साथ-साथ ... भी करते हैं
Meaning (GU)(મુખ્ય કામ) ~ ની સાથે-સાથે ... પણ કરે છે
Connection (接続)名-の・動 辞書形 + かたわら
RegisterNeutral; common in profiles, articles and introductions.
Typical useAttaches to words for the person's main job / occupation (本業). What follows is a separate social activity (社会的活動) done alongside it — volunteering, writing, coaching, a second career.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (辞書形)V-辞書形 + かたわら教師の仕事をするかたわらkyoushi no shigoto o suru katawara
Noun + のN + の + かたわら会社勤務のかたわらkaisha kinmu no katawara
Verb (た形)(added, not in book) not used — ×仕事をしたかたわら—
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は教師の仕事をするかたわら小説を書いている。Kare wa kyoushi no shigoto o suru katawara shousetsu o kaite iru. Besides working as a teacher, he writes novels. वह शिक्षक का काम करने के साथ-साथ उपन्यास भी लिखता है। તે શિક્ષકનું કામ કરવાની સાથે-સાથે નવલકથાઓ પણ લખે છે.
わたしは会社勤務のかたわら子供たちにサッカーを教えています。Watashi wa kaisha kinmu no katawara kodomotachi ni sakkaa o oshiete imasu. Alongside my office job, I teach football to children. मैं दफ़्तर की नौकरी के साथ-साथ बच्चों को फ़ुटबॉल सिखाता हूँ। હું ઑફિસની નોકરીની સાથે-સાથે બાળકોને ફૂટબૉલ શીખવું છું.
母は主婦としての仕事のかたわら日本語を教えるボランティアをしている。Haha wa shufu to shite no shigoto no katawara Nihongo o oshieru borantia o shite iru. Alongside her work as a homemaker, my mother volunteers teaching Japanese. माँ गृहिणी के काम के साथ-साथ जापानी सिखाने का स्वयंसेवी काम भी करती हैं। મા ગૃહિણીના કામની સાથે-સાથે જાપાનીઝ શીખવવાનું સ્વયંસેવી કામ પણ કરે છે.

かたわら (傍ら) = "at the side": the second activity runs beside the main job over a long period — not at the same moment. That's the difference from 〜ながら (two actions at once): gardening while listening to music is ながら, not かたわら ([1〜3] Q1). The A part must be a real occupation (創作活動, 数学を教える, 市役所に勤める), and B a social activity (NPO, translation work, singing career) — not private matters like living alone, hobbies, cooking dinner or caring for one's parents (drill [3]).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three "doing B along with A" forms, plus the two everyday forms they're contrasted with. The trick is always what A is:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜がてら~gatera While out ~ing, also… N(する)／V-ます stem A = casual outing / movement (散歩・買い物・花見). Casual tone. Squeezing an errand into a walk or trip.
〜かたがた~katagata Partly for the purpose of ~ N(する) only A = polite purpose (あいさつ・お礼・報告・見舞い・おわび); B = movement verb (行く・伺う・訪ねる). Formal. Business or polite visits, letters.
〜かたわら~katawara Alongside (main job) ~, also… N-の／V-辞書形 A = main occupation; B = separate social activity. Long-term, not simultaneous. Profiles: 教師のかたわら小説を書く.
〜ついでに (N2)~tsuide ni While you're at it V-辞書形／た形, N-の General "taking the opportunity"; no restriction on A. Everyday speech, any errand.
〜ながら (N4)~nagara While ~ing (at the same time) V-ます stem Two actions simultaneously, by the same person. 庭仕事をしながら音楽を聞く.
Exam trap 1 — polite visit vs casual outing: おわび／あいさつ／お礼／報告 + visit → かたがた; 散歩／買い物／花見 + side errand → がてら. The same noun choice decides [1〜3] Q2 and Q3.
Exam trap 2 — "beside" is not "while": かたわら means two roles running in parallel over time (a job and a sideline). If the two actions happen at the same moment (gardening + music), the answer is ながら ([1〜3] Q1).
Exam trap 3 — A must be different from B: 運動がてら走る, 本を借りがてら図書館, 訪問かたがた訪ねる all fail because A and B are the same activity. Look for a real "second thing".
