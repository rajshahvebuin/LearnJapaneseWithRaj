Q1

行方不明の子が見つかったという知らせが（　）、家族は泣き出した。Yukue fumei no ko ga mitsukatta to iu shirase ga (___), kazoku wa nakidashita.

Full sentence: 行方不明の子が見つかったという知らせが入るや、家族は泣き出した。Yukue fumei no ko ga mitsukatta to iu shirase ga hairu ya, kazoku wa nakidashita.
EN: The moment the news came in that the missing child had been found, the family burst into tears.
HI: लापता बच्चे के मिल जाने की ख़बर आते ही परिवार रो पड़ा।
GU: ગુમ થયેલું બાળક મળી ગયાના સમાચાર આવતાં જ પરિવાર રડી પડ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1入るやhairu yathe moment it came inआते हीઆવતાં જ
2入ったばかりでhaitta bakari dehaving only just come in, (so)अभी-अभी आई है, इसलिएહમણાં જ આવ્યા હોવાથી
3入って以来haitte iraiever since it came inआने के बाद सेઆવ્યા ત્યારથી
4 (printed "3")入ってからはhaitte kara wafrom the time it came in (onward)आने के बाद से (आगे)આવ્યા પછીથી (આગળ)

Why: A single trigger (the news) followed almost simultaneously by a reaction of a different subject (家族) → 〜や (1課). 2) 〜たばかりで gives a reason ("because it's only just come in"), which doesn't lead to crying. 3) and 4) mean "ever since" and need a continuing state afterwards (泣いてばかりいる), but 泣き出した is one instant event. This exact sentence also appears in 1課 [1〜6] Q6.

Q2

本年（　）本社の通信販売は終了させていただきます。長い間ありがとうございました。Honnen (___) honsha no tsuushin hanbai wa shuuryou sasete itadakimasu. Nagai aida arigatou gozaimashita.

Full sentence: 本年をもって本社の通信販売は終了させていただきます。長い間ありがとうございました。Honnen o motte honsha no tsuushin hanbai wa shuuryou sasete itadakimasu. Nagai aida arigatou gozaimashita.
EN: With the end of this year, our company's mail-order service will close. Thank you for your custom over the years.
HI: इस वर्ष के साथ हमारी कंपनी की मेल-ऑर्डर सेवा बंद की जा रही है। इतने लंबे समय तक साथ देने के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: આ વર્ષ સાથે અમારી કંપનીની મેલ-ઑર્ડર સેવા બંધ કરવામાં આવે છે. આટલા લાંબા સમય સુધી સાથ આપવા બદલ આભાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1をもってo motteas of / with (this point, it ends)...के साथ (समाप्त)...સાથે (સમાપ્ત)
2をおいてo oiteapart from / except (+ negative)...के सिवा (कोई नहीं)...સિવાય (કોઈ નહીં)
3限りではkagiri de waas far as (I know)जहाँ तक (मुझे पता है)જ્યાં સુધી (મને ખબર છે)
4に限りni kagirionly for / limited toकेवल ...के लिएમાત્ર ...માટે

Why: 〜をもって (2課) marks the point in time at which something ends — the classic formal-notice phrase (本日をもって閉店いたします). 2) 〜をおいて (3課) means "nobody/nothing except ~" and needs a negative (〜をおいてほかにいない). 3) 〜限りでは needs a verb of knowing (わたしの知る限りでは) and can't follow a time noun. 4) 本年に限り would mean "this year only", which contradicts 終了 (ending for good).

Q3

どんな困難な状況（　）、彼は希望を捨てなかった。Donna konnan na joukyou (___), kare wa kibou o sutenakatta.

Full sentence: どんな困難な状況にあっても、彼は希望を捨てなかった。Donna konnan na joukyou ni atte mo, kare wa kibou o sutenakatta.
EN: No matter how difficult the situation he was in, he never gave up hope.
HI: चाहे कितनी भी कठिन परिस्थिति में हो, उसने उम्मीद नहीं छोड़ी।
GU: ગમે તેટલી મુશ્કેલ પરિસ્થિતિમાં હોય, તેણે આશા છોડી નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1といってもto itte moalthough I say ~ (it's not much)कहने को तो ~, परકહેવા પૂરતું ~, પણ
2にとってもni totte moeven for (a person)...के लिए भी...માટે પણ
3にあってもni atte moeven (being) in (a situation)(स्थिति) में होते हुए भी(સ્થિતિ)માં હોવા છતાં
4としてもto shite moeven if / even asभले ही / ...के रूप में भीભલે / ...તરીકે પણ

Why: 状況 is exactly the kind of noun 〜にあって attaches to, and 〜にあっても (1課) introduces a result contrary to that situation — hard times, yet he kept hope. 1) といっても plays down what was just said ("it's a 'situation', but…") — no sense here. 2) にとっても needs a person/group as the one affected. 4) としても ("even supposing") would need a hypothetical, e.g. 困難な状況だとしても.

Q4

うちの子は自分の好きなお菓子を（　）、さっとつかんで口の中に入れた。Uchi no ko wa jibun no suki na okashi o (___), satto tsukande kuchi no naka ni ireta.

Full sentence: うちの子は自分の好きなお菓子を見つけるが早いか、さっとつかんで口の中に入れた。Uchi no ko wa jibun no suki na okashi o mitsukeru ga hayai ka, satto tsukande kuchi no naka ni ireta.
EN: No sooner had my child spotted his favourite sweets than he grabbed them and popped them into his mouth.
HI: मेरे बच्चे ने अपनी पसंदीदा मिठाई देखते ही झट से उठाकर मुँह में डाल ली।
GU: મારા બાળકે પોતાની મનપસંદ મીઠાઈ જોતાં જ ઝટથી ઉપાડીને મોંમાં મૂકી દીધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見つけ次第mitsuke shidaias soon as (you) find (do ~)मिलते ही (कर देना)મળતાં જ (કરી દેવું)
2見つけるが早いかmitsukeru ga hayai kano sooner had he found it thanदेखते ही (तुरंत)જોતાં જ (તરત)
3見つけた際にmitsuketa sai nion the occasion of findingमिलने के अवसर परમળવાના પ્રસંગે
4見つけた上でmitsuketa ue deafter (first) finding, then deliberatelyढूँढ़ लेने के बाद (सोच-समझकर)શોધી લીધા પછી (સમજીને)

Why: A past fact, instant follow-up, a touch of surprise (さっと) → 〜が早いか (1課). 1) 〜次第 (N2) is for future plans/requests (見つけ次第連絡します) and can't narrate a past event. 3) 〜際に is a formal "on the occasion of" with no sense of speed. 4) 〜た上で means "after doing A properly, then B" — the opposite of a reflex grab.

Q5

今世紀に（　）、地球の各地で大規模災害が相次いで起きている。Konseiki ni (___), chikyuu no kakuchi de daikibo saigai ga aitsuide okite iru.

Full sentence: 今世紀に入ってからというもの、地球の各地で大規模災害が相次いで起きている。Konseiki ni haitte kara to iu mono, chikyuu no kakuchi de daikibo saigai ga aitsuide okite iru.
EN: Ever since we entered this century, large-scale disasters have been happening one after another all over the globe.
HI: इस सदी में प्रवेश करने के बाद से, दुनिया भर में एक के बाद एक बड़ी आपदाएँ आ रही हैं।
GU: આ સદીમાં પ્રવેશ્યા પછીથી, દુનિયાભરમાં એક પછી એક મોટી આપત્તિઓ આવી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1入りつつhairitsutsuwhile enteringप्रवेश करते हुएપ્રવેશતાં-પ્રવેશતાં
2入り次第hairi shidaias soon as (we) enterप्रवेश करते हीપ્રવેશતાં જ
3入ってはじめてhaitte hajimeteonly after entering (did ~ for the first time)प्रवेश करने के बाद ही (पहली बार)પ્રવેશ્યા પછી જ (પહેલી વાર)
4入ってからというものhaitte kara to iu monoever since enteringप्रवेश करने के बाद सेપ્રવેશ્યા પછીથી

Why: A turning point (the new century) followed by a state that has continued ever since (相次いで起きている) → 〜てからというもの (1課). 1) 〜つつ ("while") needs two simultaneous actions by one subject. 2) 〜次第 is for future intentions/requests. 3) 〜てはじめて means "only after ~ did one realise/do for the first time", which needs a realisation (分かった, 気づいた), not a continuing series.

Q6

今日は1件（　）次の仕事を頼まれて、一日中本当に忙しかった。Kyou wa ikken (___) tsugi no shigoto o tanomarete, ichinichijuu hontou ni isogashikatta.

Full sentence: 今日は1件処理したそばから次の仕事を頼まれて、一日中本当に忙しかった。Kyou wa ikken shori shita soba kara tsugi no shigoto o tanomarete, ichinichijuu hontou ni isogashikatta.
EN: Today, no sooner had I dealt with one task than I was asked to do the next — I was really busy all day.
HI: आज एक काम निपटाते ही अगला काम सौंप दिया जाता था, पूरे दिन सचमुच बहुत व्यस्त रहा।
GU: આજે એક કામ પતાવતાં જ બીજું કામ સોંપી દેવાતું, આખો દિવસ ખરેખર ખૂબ વ્યસ્ત રહ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1処理するなりshori suru narithe moment (he) dealt with it, (he did)निपटाते ही (उसने तुरंत किया)પતાવતાં જ (તેણે તરત કર્યું)
2処理しつつshori shitsutsuwhile dealing with itनिपटाते हुएપતાવતાં-પતાવતાં
3処理してこのかたshori shite kono kataever since dealing with itनिपटाने के बाद सेપતાવ્યા પછીથી
4処理したそばからshori shita soba karano sooner had I dealt with it than (again)निपटाते ही फिर सेપતાવતાં જ ફરીથી

Why: A frustrating, repeating cycle — finish one, get another, all day long → 〜そばから (1課; た形 is allowed). 1) 〜なり needs a surprising volitional action by the same (usually third-person) subject; being asked (頼まれる) is neither. 2) 〜つつ is "while doing", not "right after". 3) 〜てこのかた ("ever since", formal) is for a long period from a past turning point, not task-by-task repetition within one day.

Q7

父は1月の（　）、次々に各地の大会に出場している。Chichi wa ichigatsu no (___), tsugitsugi ni kakuchi no taikai ni shutsujou shite iru.

Full sentence: 父は1月のゴルフ大会を皮切りに、次々に各地の大会に出場している。Chichi wa ichigatsu no gorufu taikai o kawakiri ni, tsugitsugi ni kakuchi no taikai ni shutsujou shite iru.
EN: Starting with a golf tournament in January, my father has been entering tournaments all over the country one after another.
HI: जनवरी के गोल्फ़ टूर्नामेंट से शुरुआत करके, पिताजी एक के बाद एक जगह-जगह की प्रतियोगिताओं में भाग ले रहे हैं।
GU: જાન્યુઆરીની ગોલ્ફ ટુર્નામેન્ટથી શરૂઆત કરીને, પિતાજી એક પછી એક જુદી જુદી જગ્યાની સ્પર્ધાઓમાં ભાગ લઈ રહ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ゴルフ大会からしてgorufu taikai kara shiteto begin with the golf tournament (judging from)गोल्फ़ टूर्नामेंट से ही (देखें तो)ગોલ્ફ ટુર્નામેન્ટથી જ (જોઈએ તો)
2ゴルフ大会をもってgorufu taikai o mottewith the golf tournament (as the end)गोल्फ़ टूर्नामेंट के साथ (समाप्त)ગોલ્ફ ટુર્નામેન્ટ સાથે (સમાપ્ત)
3ゴルフ大会を皮切りにgorufu taikai o kawakiri nistarting with the golf tournamentगोल्फ़ टूर्नामेंट से शुरुआत करकेગોલ્ફ ટુર્નામેન્ટથી શરૂઆત કરીને
4ゴルフ大会に出てはじめてgorufu taikai ni dete hajimeteonly after entering the golf tournamentगोल्फ़ टूर्नामेंट में भाग लेने के बाद हीગોલ્ફ ટુર્નામેન્ટમાં ભાગ લીધા પછી જ

Why: 次々に (one after another) signals a series that started with one event → 〜を皮切りに (2課). 1) 〜からして ("even ~, to start with") is used for judging/criticising (名前からして怪しい). 2) 〜をもって marks an end point, the opposite direction. 4) 〜てはじめて needs a first-time realisation, not a chain of entries.

Q8

旅行先が国外（　）国内（　）、健康には十分注意したほうがいい。Ryokousaki ga kokugai (___) kokunai (___), kenkou ni wa juubun chuui shita hou ga ii.

Full sentence: 旅行先が国外であれ国内であれ、健康には十分注意したほうがいい。Ryokousaki ga kokugai de are kokunai de are, kenkou ni wa juubun chuui shita hou ga ii.
EN: Whether your destination is abroad or within the country, you should take good care of your health.
HI: यात्रा का गंतव्य चाहे विदेश हो या देश के भीतर, सेहत का पूरा ध्यान रखना चाहिए।
GU: પ્રવાસનું સ્થળ ભલે વિદેશ હોય કે દેશની અંદર, તબિયતનું પૂરું ધ્યાન રાખવું જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1といわず・といわずto iwazu・to iwazuA, B — everywhere / all overA हो या B — हर जगहA હોય કે B — બધે
2とか・とかtoka・tokathings like A and BA या B जैसेA કે B જેવા
3なり・なりnari・nariA or B (pick one and do it)A या B (कोई एक चुनकर)A કે B (કોઈ એક પસંદ કરીને)
4であれ・であれde are・de arewhether A or Bचाहे A हो या Bભલે A હોય કે B

Why: "Whichever it is, the conclusion is the same" → 〜であれ…であれ (4課). 1) 〜といわず…といわず describes something spread all over concrete parts/places (手といわず足といわず泥だらけ) — not a choice between conditions. 2) とか・とか just lists examples; it can't create a "whether…or" condition. 3) 〜なり…なり offers options to choose for an action (電話なりメールなりで連絡して) — here the travel destination isn't being chosen.

Q9

このばら園は種類の多さ（　）、1年中美しいばらを咲かせていると評判だ。Kono baraen wa shurui no oosa (___), ichinenjuu utsukushii bara o sakasete iru to hyouban da.

Full sentence: このばら園は種類の多さもさることながら、1年中美しいばらを咲かせていると評判だ。Kono baraen wa shurui no oosa mo saru koto nagara, ichinenjuu utsukushii bara o sakasete iru to hyouban da.
EN: This rose garden is famous not only for its wide variety, but also for keeping beautiful roses in bloom all year round.
HI: यह गुलाब-बाग़ किस्मों की बहुतायत के लिए तो मशहूर है ही, साथ ही साल भर सुंदर गुलाब खिलाने के लिए भी जाना जाता है।
GU: આ ગુલાબ-બાગ જાતોની વિપુલતા માટે તો જાણીતો છે જ, સાથે આખું વર્ષ સુંદર ગુલાબ ખીલવવા માટે પણ પ્રખ્યાત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3にとどまらずni todomarazunot stopping at / spreading beyond...तक सीमित न रहकर...સુધી મર્યાદિત ન રહેતાં
4もさることながらmo saru koto nagaraA, of course — but also (even more) BA तो है ही, साथ ही B भीA તો છે જ, સાથે B પણ

Why: Two positive evaluations: "A is admirable, and B too" → 〜もさることながら (3課). 1) 〜はおろか is for "not even the easy thing, let alone…" and normally ends in a negative or a surprising extreme (漢字はおろかひらがなも書けない). 2) 〜のこととなると introduces a person's changed behaviour about a topic (子供のこととなると夢中になる). 3) 〜にとどまらず describes something spreading beyond a range (area, group) — 種類の多さ isn't a range that spreads.

Q10

その男は警官の姿を（　）、逃げていった。Sono otoko wa keikan no sugata o (___), nigete itta.

Full sentence: その男は警官の姿を見るなり、逃げていった。Sono otoko wa keikan no sugata o miru nari, nigete itta.
EN: The moment that man saw a police officer, he ran off.
HI: वह आदमी पुलिसवाले को देखते ही भाग गया।
GU: તે માણસ પોલીસને જોતાં જ ભાગી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見たきりmita kirihaving seen (once), and since then nothingएक बार देखने के बाद (फिर कभी नहीं)એક વાર જોયા પછી (ફરી ક્યારેય નહીં)
2見ているうちにmite iru uchi niwhile watching (gradually)देखते-देखतेજોતાં-જોતાં
3見るなりmiru narithe moment he sawदेखते हीજોતાં જ

Why: Third-person subject (その男), the same subject for both actions, a sudden volitional reaction (逃げた) → 〜なり (1課). 1) 〜たきり ("and then never again") needs a following negative (見たきり会っていない). 2) 〜ているうちに describes a gradual change during an ongoing action. 4) 〜ないうちに ("before ~ happens") clashes with fleeing because he saw the officer.

Q11

掃除（　）洗い物（　）、あなたができることを何か手伝ってよ。Souji (___) araimono (___), anata ga dekiru koto o nanika tetsudatte yo.

Full sentence: 掃除なり洗い物なり、あなたができることを何か手伝ってよ。Souji nari araimono nari, anata ga dekiru koto o nanika tetsudatte yo.
EN: Cleaning, washing up, whatever — help with something you can do!
HI: सफ़ाई हो या बर्तन धोना, जो भी तुम कर सकते हो, कुछ तो मदद करो!
GU: સફાઈ હોય કે વાસણ ધોવાં, તું જે કરી શકે તે, કંઈક તો મદદ કર!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1といい・といいto ii・to iiboth A and B (evaluation)A भी, B भी (मूल्यांकन)A પણ, B પણ (મૂલ્યાંકન)
2というか・というかto iu ka・to iu kashould I say A, or BA कहें या BA કહીએ કે B
3なり・なりnari・nariA or B (choose one)A या B (कोई एक)A કે B (કોઈ એક)
4やら・やらyara・yarawhat with A and B (and so on)A और B वगैरह (परेशानी)A અને B વગેરે (મુશ્કેલી)

Why: Giving the listener options to choose for a request (手伝ってよ) → 〜なり…なり (4課), which is typically followed by a request, suggestion or intention. 1) 〜といい…といい lists examples to back up an evaluation (色といい形といい素晴らしい). 2) 〜というか…というか searches for the right description. 4) 〜やら…やら lists things that piled up (often a complaint) and can't be followed by a request.

Q12

わたしは勉強といっても家で1、2時間教科書を読む（　）、たいしたことはしていないんです。Watashi wa benkyou to itte mo ie de ichi, ni jikan kyoukasho o yomu (___), taishita koto wa shite inai n desu.

Full sentence: わたしは勉強といっても家で1、2時間教科書を読むといったところで、たいしたことはしていないんです。Watashi wa benkyou to itte mo ie de ichi, ni jikan kyoukasho o yomu to itta tokoro de, taishita koto wa shite inai n desu.
EN: When I say I study, it's at most reading the textbook at home for an hour or two — I'm not doing anything much.
HI: पढ़ाई कहने को तो है, पर बस घर पर एक-दो घंटे पाठ्यपुस्तक पढ़ लेता हूँ, इतना ही — कुछ ख़ास नहीं करता।
GU: અભ્યાસ કહેવા પૂરતો છે, બસ ઘરે એક-બે કલાક પાઠ્યપુસ્તક વાંચી લઉં, એટલું જ — કંઈ ખાસ કરતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1というわけでto iu wake deand so / for that reasonइसलिएએટલે
2ということでto iu koto deon the basis that / it's been decided thatयह तय हुआ कि / इस आधार परએવું નક્કી થયું કે / એ આધારે
3といったものでto itta mono de(unnatural here) — something like(यहाँ अस्वाभाविक) कुछ ऐसा(અહીં અસ્વાભાવિક) કંઈક એવું
4といったところでto itta tokoro deit's at most about ~, andबस इतना ही ~, औरબસ એટલું જ ~, અને

Why: 〜といったところだ (2課) says "it's about this much, no more" — playing down the level. Here it appears in its connective form 〜といったところで, followed by たいしたことはしていない, which repeats the "not much" idea. (Don't confuse it with 〜たところで "even if" (10課) — that attaches to た形, but here 読む is dictionary form + といったところ.) 1) というわけで gives a conclusion from earlier reasons. 2) ということで reports a decision/basis. 3) といったもので is not a set expression in this slot.

Q13

田中さんの仕事ぶりにはいつも感心させられる。大きな仕事を安心して任せられる人は田中さん（　）いない。Tanaka-san no shigotoburi ni wa itsumo kanshin saserareru. Ookina shigoto o anshin shite makaserareru hito wa Tanaka-san (___) inai.

Full sentence: 田中さんの仕事ぶりにはいつも感心させられる。大きな仕事を安心して任せられる人は田中さんをおいてほかにいない。Tanaka-san no shigotoburi ni wa itsumo kanshin saserareru. Ookina shigoto o anshin shite makaserareru hito wa Tanaka-san o oite hoka ni inai.

GU: તનાકાજીની કામ કરવાની રીતથી હું હંમેશાં પ્રભાવિત થાઉં છું. મોટું કામ નિશ્ચિંત થઈને સોંપી શકાય એવું તનાકાજી સિવાય કોઈ નથી.

1ばかりかほかにbakari ka hoka ninot only ~ but others tooन केवल ~, बल्कि और भीમાત્ર ~ જ નહીં, બીજા પણ
2をおいてほかにo oite hoka niapart from ~, (no one) else...के सिवा और (कोई नहीं)...સિવાય બીજું (કોઈ નહીં)
3に限ってだれもni kagitte dare moof all people ~, nobodyख़ासकर ~ ही, कोई नहींખાસ કરીને ~ જ, કોઈ નહીં

Why: 〜をおいて(ほかに)〜ない (3課) = "there is no one/nothing other than ~", used for high praise of the one choice — fits the admiration in the first sentence. 1) 〜ばかりか means "not only…but also", which contradicts いない. 3) 〜に限って means "of all people ~ (wouldn't)" (うちの子に限ってそんなことはしない). 4) 〜ならでは must be followed by の＋noun or できない (田中さんならではの仕事), not だれもいない.

Q14

彼女はよほど花が好きらしい。家の（　）花でいっぱいだ。Kanojo wa yohodo hana ga suki rashii. Ie no (___) hana de ippai da.

Full sentence: 彼女はよほど花が好きらしい。家の中といわず外といわず花でいっぱいだ。Kanojo wa yohodo hana ga suki rashii. Ie no naka to iwazu soto to iwazu hana de ippai da.

GU: લાગે છે કે તેને ફૂલ બહુ જ ગમે છે. ઘરની અંદર હોય કે બહાર, બધે ફૂલ જ ફૂલ છે.

1中といわず外といわずnaka to iwazu soto to iwazuinside and outside alike (everywhere)अंदर हो या बाहर — हर जगहઅંદર હોય કે બહાર — બધે

Why: 〜といわず…といわず (4課) takes two concrete places/parts and means "all over, without distinction" — exactly the "full of flowers everywhere" picture. 2) 〜といっても…といっても is not a set pair. 3) 〜はともかく ("setting ~ aside") would downplay the inside, which doesn't fit "full everywhere". 4) 〜もかまわず needs a noun of something one should care about (人目もかまわず), and 中外 is not a word used this way.

Q15

その年のインフルエンザは関東地方（　）、全国に及んだ。Sono toshi no infuruenza wa Kantou chihou (___), zenkoku ni oyonda.

Full sentence: その年のインフルエンザは関東地方にとどまらず、全国に及んだ。Sono toshi no infuruenza wa Kantou chihou ni todomarazu, zenkoku ni oyonda.
EN: That year's influenza did not stay within the Kanto region — it spread across the whole country.
HI: उस साल का इन्फ़्लुएंज़ा कांतो क्षेत्र तक सीमित न रहकर पूरे देश में फैल गया।
GU: તે વર્ષનો ઇન્ફ્લુએન્ઝા કાંતો પ્રદેશ સુધી મર્યાદિત ન રહેતાં આખા દેશમાં ફેલાઈ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にわたってni watattethroughout / across (a range)...भर में / ...तक फैला...ભરમાં / ...સુધી ફેલાયેલું
2には限らずni wa kagirazu(unnatural) not limited to(अस्वाभाविक) ...तक सीमित नहीं(અસ્વાભાવિક) ...સુધી મર્યાદિત નહીં

Why: Something that goes beyond a limited area and spreads wider (全国に及んだ) → 〜にとどまらず (3課). 1) 〜にわたって covers a whole range (関東地方全域にわたって) and wouldn't contrast Kanto with the nation. 2) The set form is 〜に限らず (no は), and it lists a category example ("not just Kanto, any region…"), not spreading. 3) The correct form is 〜はもとより (N + はもとより), never にはもとより.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The test pits each N1 form against an N2 look-alike or a sister form from the same lesson. These are the pairs the distractors were built on:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜が早いか (1課)~ga hayai ka No sooner ~ than… V-辞書形／た形 Narrates a fact (often past) with slight surprise. Story-telling: 見つけるが早いか口に入れた (Q4).
〜次第 (N2)~shidai As soon as ~ (I will…) V-ます stem Future plans, requests, promises — not past facts. Business talk: 分かり次第ご連絡します.
〜てからというもの (1課)~te kara to iu mono Ever since ~ (big change) V-て形 Emotional "ever since"; 2nd clause = continuing state. 今世紀に入ってからというもの…起きている (Q5).
〜て以来／〜てからは (N2)~te irai / ~te kara wa Since ~ V-て形 Neutral; also need a continuing state, so a one-off reaction (泣き出した) fails. Plain "since then" statements (Q1 distractors).
〜そばから (1課) vs 〜なり (1課)~soba kara / ~nari Repeated cycle vs. one sudden act V-辞書形／た形 vs V-辞書形 そばから = again and again, undone; なり = same third-person subject, one volitional reaction. 処理したそばから頼まれる (Q6) / 見るなり逃げた (Q10).
〜を皮切りに (2課) vs 〜をもって (2課)~o kawakiri ni / ~o motte Starting with vs. ending with N + を皮切りに／をもって 皮切り = first of a series (次々に…); をもって = the cut-off point (終了, 閉店). Q7 vs Q2.
〜であれ…であれ (4課)~de are … de are Whether A or B N + であれ Conditions that don't change the conclusion. 国外であれ国内であれ注意 (Q8).
〜なり…なり (4課)~nari … nari A or B — pick one N／V-辞書形 + なり Options for an action; followed by request/suggestion/intention. 掃除なり洗い物なり手伝って (Q11).
〜といわず…といわず (4課)~to iwazu … to iwazu A and B alike — everywhere N + といわず Concrete places/parts; "all over". 中といわず外といわず花でいっぱい (Q14).
〜といい…といい (4課)~to ii … to ii Both A and B (judging) N + といい Examples supporting an evaluation; no request after it. 色といい形といい最高だ.
〜もさることながら (3課) vs 〜はおろか (3課)~mo saru koto nagara / ~wa oroka A, and B even more vs. not even B, let alone A N + もさることながら／はおろか さることながら = positive add-on; はおろか = extreme/negative, usually 〜も〜ない. Q9.
〜にとどまらず (3課) vs 〜に限らず (N2)~ni todomarazu / ~ni kagirazu Spreading beyond vs. not just (any member) N + にとどまらず／に限らず とどまらず = expands past a boundary (地方→全国); 限らず = category example. Q15.
〜をおいて(ほかに)〜ない (3課)~o oite (hoka ni) ~nai None other than ~ N + をおいて Praise for the only choice; needs a negative. 田中さんをおいてほかにいない (Q13).
Exam trap 1 — tense of 次第: 〜次第 and 〜が早いか both translate as "as soon as", but 次第 can never narrate a past event. If the sentence ends in 〜た as a story, eliminate 次第 first (Q4, Q5).
Exam trap 2 — the "ever since" family needs a lasting state: 〜てからというもの／〜て以来／〜てからは all need 〜ている／〜なった／〜日はない after them. A one-shot reaction (泣き出した, 逃げた) takes 〜や／〜なり／〜が早いか instead (Q1, Q10).
Exam trap 3 — the four pair-forms of 4課: look at the end of the sentence. Request/suggestion → なり…なり; "the conclusion is the same" → であれ…であれ; "everywhere" with concrete places → といわず…といわず; an evaluation (素晴らしい, 最高だ) → といい…といい (Q8, Q11, Q14).
