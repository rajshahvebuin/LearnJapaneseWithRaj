Notes
E collects expressions that repeat the same word, a pair of opposites or words of the same kind — for giving examples (〜といい〜といい, 〜なり〜なり) or for repeated actions (〜つ〜つ, 〜ては〜ては). F collects particles with restricted uses and old particles, which sound formal. Forms marked ＊ are taught here for the first time; the rest link back to their 課. 〜とて and 〜にて are tagged 書き言葉, 〜より is tagged 硬 formal (硬い言い方). 1 Grammar Points

E 二つの言葉を組にする言い方 (Futatsu no kotoba o kumi ni suru iikata) — summary table (p.104)

Book's introduction (p.104): N1の文法形式には、同じ言葉、対の言葉、同類の言葉を2回重ねて言う表現があります。例を挙げるものや、繰り返しの動作を表すものなどです。N1 no bunpou keishiki ni wa, onaji kotoba, tsui no kotoba, dourui no kotoba o nikai kasanete iu hyougen ga arimasu. Rei o ageru mono ya, kurikaeshi no dousa o arawasu mono nado desu. EN: Some N1 forms say the same word, a pair of opposite words or words of the same kind twice — some give examples, others express repeated actions. · HI: N1 में कुछ रूप एक ही शब्द, विपरीत शब्दों या एक जैसे शब्दों को दो बार दोहराते हैं — कुछ उदाहरण देते हैं, कुछ दोहराए जाने वाले काम बताते हैं। · GU: N1માં કેટલાંક રૂપો એક જ શબ્દ, વિરોધી શબ્દો કે એક જેવા શબ્દોને બે વાર બોલે છે — કેટલાંક ઉદાહરણ આપે છે, કેટલાંક પુનરાવર્તિત ક્રિયા બતાવે છે.

Reading~tsu … ~tsu
Meaning (EN)"~ing and ~ing back and forth" — book: ⇒対の動作を繰り返す (a pair of opposite actions is repeated)

マラソンの選手たちは抜きつ抜かれつ、激しい争いを繰り広げた。Marason no senshutachi wa nukitsu nukaretsu, hageshii arasoi o kurihirogeta.The marathon runners overtook and were overtaken in turn, waging a fierce battle.मैराथन धावक एक-दूसरे को पछाड़ते-पिछड़ते ज़बरदस्त मुक़ाबला करते रहे।મેરેથોન દોડવીરો એકબીજાને પાછળ છોડતા-પાછળ પડતા જોરદાર મુકાબલો કરતા રહ્યા.

Always a pair of opposites (go/return, overtake/be overtaken, float/sink), followed by する or a comma. Learn the pairs as vocabulary — 練習1 Q4 (浮きつ沈みつ) and 練習2 Q5 d (×飲みつ休みつ — not opposites) test this.

【E＊】 〜ては…、〜ては…

neutral
Reading~te wa …, ~te wa …
Meaning (EN)"do ~ and then …, over and over" — book: ⇒一連の動作を繰り返す (a sequence of actions is repeated)
Meaning (HI)बार-बार ... करना और फिर ...
Meaning (GU)વારંવાર ... કરવું અને પછી ...

RegisterNeutral.
Typical useA whole sequence repeats (write → erase → write → erase). ⚠ It can also be used without repeating the expression twice, as in ③ (③のように表現を2度繰り返さない使い方もある).

F 助詞・複合助詞 (Joshi・fukugou joshi) — summary table (p.106)

Book's introduction (p.106): N1の文法形式には、用法が限られている助詞・助詞相当語や、古くから使われている助詞があります。一般的に古い言葉は改まった感じがします。整理しておきましょう。N1 no bunpou keishiki ni wa, youhou ga kagirarete iru joshi・joshi soutougo ya, furuku kara tsukawarete iru joshi ga arimasu. Ippanteki ni furui kotoba wa aratamatta kanji ga shimasu. Seiri shite okimashou. EN: Among N1 forms are particles and particle-like words with restricted uses, and particles used since old times. Old words generally sound formal. Let us sort them out. · HI: N1 में सीमित प्रयोग वाले कण (पार्टिकल) और पुराने समय से चले आ रहे कण हैं। पुराने शब्द आम तौर पर औपचारिक लगते हैं। · GU: N1માં મર્યાદિત ઉપયોગવાળા નિપાત અને જૂના સમયથી વપરાતા નિપાત છે. જૂના શબ્દો સામાન્ય રીતે ઔપચારિક લાગે છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

E's paired forms look alike but differ in what they pair and what may follow; F's particles are mostly formal stand-ins for everyday ones:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜なり〜なり~nari ~nari either ~ or ~ (pick one) V-dict／N + なり Suggestions; followed by a request or advice (してください, したほうがいい). E 練習1 Q1, 練習2 Q5.
〜といい〜といい~to ii ~to ii both ~ and ~ (examples) N + といい Nouns only; followed by an evaluation (最高だ, そっくりだ). E 練習1 Q6.
〜つ〜つ vs 〜ては〜ては~tsu ~tsu / ~te wa ~te wa back and forth / over and over V-stem + つ; V-て + は つ〜つ = fixed opposite pairs; ては = any repeated sequence, also used once. E 練習1 Q4.
〜かれ〜かれ~kare ~kare more or less / sooner or later い-adj stem + かれ (悪しかれ) Opposite い-adjectives; fixed phrases. E 練習1 Q5, 練習2 Q2.
〜に〜ない vs 〜(よ)うにも〜ない~ni ~nai / ~(y)ou ni mo ~nai want to but can't V-dict + に; V-vol + にも Mixing the connections (出そうに／帰ろうに) is the trap. E 練習2 Q3, Q4.
〜とて vs 〜すら／〜さえ~tote / ~sura even (N is no exception) / even (extreme case) N + とて; N(で) + すら とて = "N too, like everyone else" (often + negative); すら = an extreme example. F 練習1 Q3, 練習2 B Q2.
〜にて／〜より／〜をもって~nite / ~yori / ~o motte で / から / with, as of N + にて／より／をもって Formal versions of で, から and で／までで; used in letters, notices, business. F 練習2 A Q1, Q3; B Q1, Q3.
Exam trap 1 — what follows the pair: なり〜なり wants a request/advice, といい〜といい an evaluation, (よ)うが〜まいが an "it doesn't matter" statement. Read the end of the sentence first (E 練習2 Q1, Q5).
Exam trap 2 — formal letters and notices: in a 【手紙】 or shop notice, expect にて (=で), より (=から) and こととて. These rarely appear in conversation.
