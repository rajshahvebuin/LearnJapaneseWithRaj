Notes
〜をおいて and 〜ならでは both attach to something the speaker rates highly — so the exam tests what may follow them (をおいて → negative ending; ならでは → の+N／だ, always praise). None of the five points is tagged 硬い言い方 in this lesson, though をおいて and もさることながら are formal in feel. The book's drills use 3 options (a–c) instead of the exam's 4.
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.16): the everyday "only / as far as / not only… but also / on top of" forms. Today's N1 forms express the same limiting and adding ideas, but with strong evaluation built in.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜をおいて

neutral register
Reading~o oite
Meaning (EN)"apart from ~, there is no other (as good)" — book: 〜のほかに、同じぐらい高く評価できるものはいない・ない (besides ~, there is no one/nothing that can be rated as highly)
Meaning (HI)...के सिवा (उतना अच्छा) कोई / कुछ नहीं
Meaning (GU)...સિવાય (એટલું સારું) કોઈ / કંઈ નથી
Connection (接続)名 ＋をおいて
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; formal-sounding, used in speeches and writing.
Typical useAttaches to a word for something the speaker rates highly. Must be followed by いない／〜ない (typically ほかにいない／ほかにない).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (person)N ＋をおいてほかにいない彼をおいてほかにいないkare o oite hoka ni inai
Noun (place/thing)N ＋をおいてほかにないここをおいてほかにないkoko o oite hoka ni nai
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今、こんな素晴らしい色使いの染色ができる人は、彼をおいてほかにいない。Ima, konna subarashii irozukai no senshoku ga dekiru hito wa, kare o oite hoka ni inai. Right now there's no one but him who can dye cloth with such wonderful use of colour. आज ऐसे शानदार रंगों से रंगाई कर सकने वाला उसके सिवा कोई नहीं है। આજે આવા સુંદર રંગો સાથે રંગકામ કરી શકે તેવું તેના સિવાય કોઈ નથી.
日本で世界的な平和会議を行うなら、広島か長崎をおいてほかに候補地は考えられない。Nihon de sekaiteki na heiwa kaigi o okonau nara, Hiroshima ka Nagasaki o oite hoka ni kouhochi wa kangaerarenai. If an international peace conference is to be held in Japan, no candidate site other than Hiroshima or Nagasaki is conceivable. अगर जापान में विश्व-स्तरीय शांति सम्मेलन होना है, तो हिरोशिमा या नागासाकी के सिवा कोई और स्थान सोचा ही नहीं जा सकता। જો જાપાનમાં વિશ્વ-સ્તરનું શાંતિ સંમેલન યોજવું હોય, તો હિરોશિમા કે નાગાસાકી સિવાય બીજું કોઈ સ્થળ વિચારી જ ન શકાય.
毎年夏にはこのホテルに来ている。心からくつろげる場所はここをおいてほかにない。Maitoshi natsu ni wa kono hoteru ni kite iru. Kokoro kara kutsurogeru basho wa koko o oite hoka ni nai. I come to this hotel every summer. There's no other place where I can truly relax. मैं हर गर्मी में इस होटल में आता हूँ। दिल से आराम कर सकूँ, ऐसी जगह इसके सिवा कोई नहीं। હું દર ઉનાળે આ હોટલમાં આવું છું. દિલથી આરામ કરી શકું એવી જગ્યા આના સિવાય બીજી કોઈ નથી.

おく = "put aside": "put ~ aside, and there's no other". Two fixed requirements, both tested in drill [1]: the noun is a single, specific thing the speaker admires (not a category like 男性, not わたし), and the sentence ends in a negative — ほかにいない／ほかにない／考えられない／あるまい. Without a negative ending, をおいて is wrong.

【2】 〜ならでは

neutral register
Reading~nara de wa
Meaning (EN)"only ~ could (do/have such a fine thing); unique to ~" — book: 〜だけがそのような素晴らしいことを実現できる (only ~ can achieve such a wonderful thing)
Meaning (HI)केवल ... की ही ख़ासियत / सिर्फ़ ... ही ऐसा कर सकता है
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...ની જ ખાસિયત / ફક્ત ... જ આવું કરી શકે
Connection (接続)名 ＋ならでは
RegisterNeutral; frequent in advertising, reviews and praise.
Typical useAttaches to something the speaker rates highly; used to say "this could not be achieved except by ~" (〜でなければ実現できない). Usual shapes: 〜ならではの N だ and 〜ならではだ.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + の + NN ＋ならではの ＋ N一之助さんならではの演技Ichinosuke-san nara de wa no engi
Noun + だN ＋ならではだハワイならではですHawai nara de wa desu
Noun + negative verbN ＋ならでは ＋ V-ない (literary: "none but N could…")老舗ならでは出せないshinise nara de wa dasenai
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
さすが歌舞伎俳優の一之助さんならではの演技だ。ほれぼれするほどリアリティーがある。Sasuga kabuki haiyuu no Ichinosuke-san nara de wa no engi da. Horebore suru hodo riariteii ga aru. That's a performance only the kabuki actor Ichinosuke could give. It's so realistic it's enchanting. यह अभिनय सचमुच काबुकी अभिनेता इचिनोसुके जी जैसा ही कोई कर सकता है। इतना यथार्थ कि मन मोह ले। આ અભિનય ખરેખર કાબુકી અભિનેતા ઇચિનોસુકેજી જ કરી શકે. એટલો વાસ્તવિક કે મન મોહી લે.
ぜひ一度ヨットに乗ってみてはいかがですか。この体験はハワイならではですよ。Zehi ichido yotto ni notte mite wa ikaga desu ka. Kono taiken wa Hawai nara de wa desu yo. Why not try sailing on a yacht at least once? It's an experience you can only have in Hawaii. एक बार ज़रूर याट की सैर करके देखिए। यह अनुभव सिर्फ़ हवाई में ही मिलता है। એક વાર જરૂર યૉટની સફર કરી જુઓ. આ અનુભવ ફક્ત હવાઈમાં જ મળે છે.
この布製の袋にはぬくもりが感じられる。手作りならではだと思う。Kono nunosei no fukuro ni wa nukumori ga kanjirareru. Tezukuri nara de wa da to omou. You can feel warmth in this cloth bag. I think only something handmade has that. इस कपड़े के थैले में एक गर्माहट महसूस होती है। मुझे लगता है यह हाथ से बनी चीज़ की ही ख़ासियत है। આ કાપડની થેલીમાં એક હૂંફ અનુભવાય છે. મને લાગે છે કે આ હાથબનાવટની જ ખાસિયત છે.
100年続いた老舗ならでは出せないこの味の良さ！店主が変わっても全く味が落ちていない。Hyakunen tsuzuita shinise nara de wa dasenai kono aji no yosa! Tenshu ga kawatte mo mattaku aji ga ochite inai. This fine flavour that only a 100-year-old shop could produce! Even with a new owner, the taste hasn't dropped at all. ऐसा बढ़िया स्वाद सिर्फ़ 100 साल पुरानी दुकान ही दे सकती है! मालिक बदलने पर भी स्वाद ज़रा नहीं गिरा। આવો સરસ સ્વાદ ફક્ત 100 વર્ષ જૂની દુકાન જ આપી શકે! માલિક બદલાયા છતાં સ્વાદ જરાય ઘટ્યો નથી.

Literally "if it weren't ~, then not" (なら + では, with a hidden negative). Always praise: what follows must be something good (純真さ, 素晴らしい字, 感動した). Like をおいて, it attaches to something the speaker admires, so わたし or a neutral/negative quality doesn't work. Example ④ shows the older full form 〜ならでは〜ない, but on the exam you'll mostly meet 〜ならではの N.

【3】 〜にとどまらず

neutral register
Reading~ni todomarazu
Meaning (EN)"not limited to ~, (it extends) further" — book: 〜の範囲に収まらないで、もっと広く及ぶ (doesn't stay within the range of ~ but reaches more widely)
Meaning (HI)...तक सीमित न रहकर (और आगे तक)
Meaning (GU)...સુધી મર્યાદિત ન રહેતાં (વધુ આગળ સુધી)
Connection (接続)名・動 辞書形 ＋にとどまらず
RegisterNeutral to formal; common in writing and news.
Typical useAttaches to a word for a limited range or a phenomenon. The second clause names a wider range that includes it.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋にとどまらず一国にとどまらずikkoku ni todomarazu
Noun (time)N ＋にとどまらず直後にとどまらずchokugo ni todomarazu
Verb (辞書形)V-辞書形 ＋にとどまらず明るくするにとどまらずakaruku suru ni todomarazu
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
マスメディアによる情報というものは、今や一国にとどまらず、世界中に伝わる。Masumedia ni yoru jouhou to iu mono wa, ima ya ikkoku ni todomarazu, sekaijuu ni tsutawaru. Information carried by the mass media now spreads not just within one country but all over the world. मास मीडिया की सूचना अब एक देश तक सीमित न रहकर पूरी दुनिया में फैल जाती है। માસ મીડિયાની માહિતી હવે એક દેશ સુધી મર્યાદિત ન રહેતાં આખી દુનિયામાં ફેલાઈ જાય છે.
農作物は、台風に襲われた直後にとどまらず、一年中その影響を受ける。Nousakubutsu wa, taifuu ni osowareta chokugo ni todomarazu, ichinenjuu sono eikyou o ukeru. Crops are affected not only right after a typhoon strikes but all year round. फ़सलें तूफ़ान आने के तुरंत बाद ही नहीं, पूरे साल उसका असर झेलती हैं। પાક વાવાઝોડું આવ્યા પછી તરત જ નહીં, આખું વર્ષ તેની અસર ભોગવે છે.
一人の人間の明るさは、場を明るくするにとどまらず、周囲の人々に心身の活力をも与える。Hitori no ningen no akarusa wa, ba o akaruku suru ni todomarazu, shuui no hitobito ni shinshin no katsuryoku o mo ataeru. One person's cheerfulness doesn't just brighten the room — it even gives the people around them mental and physical energy. एक व्यक्ति की ख़ुशमिज़ाजी केवल माहौल को रौशन ही नहीं करती, बल्कि आसपास के लोगों को तन-मन की ऊर्जा भी देती है। એક વ્યક્તિનો આનંદી સ્વભાવ ફક્ત વાતાવરણને ખુશનુમા જ નથી બનાવતો, આસપાસના લોકોને તન-મનની ઊર્જા પણ આપે છે.

Think of a circle that grows: the first noun is a small circle, the second clause is a bigger circle that contains it (一国 → 世界中; 直後 → 一年中; 学会内 → 産業界). Drill [3] Q3 is the trap: 女性 → 男性 or わたし → 母 are just different groups, not a bigger one. With verbs, use the dictionary form directly (作るにとどまらず) — unlike 〜に限らず, which normally attaches to nouns.

【4】 〜はおろか

neutral register
Reading~wa oroka
Meaning (EN)"let alone ~; not to mention ~, even…" — book: 〜はもちろん、程度が違うほかのことにも同じことが言える (not only ~, but the same is true of something of a different degree)
Meaning (HI)...तो दूर की बात, ... भी (नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...તો દૂરની વાત, ... પણ (નહીં)
Connection (接続)名(＋助詞) ＋はおろか
RegisterNeutral, slightly literary; sounds emotional/critical.
Typical useTakes "~" as obvious and stresses the state at a more extreme level. The whole sentence tends to be negative in image. The second part usually has an emphasis word: も・さえ・まで.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋はおろか手間がかかる料理はおろかtema ga kakaru ryouri wa oroka
Noun + particleN ＋ 助詞 ＋はおろか外ではおろかsoto de wa oroka
Verb(added, not in book) V-辞書形 ＋の／こと ＋はおろか — nominalise first歩くことはおろかaruku koto wa oroka
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used directly—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
手間がかかる料理はおろか、日常の簡単な料理を作るのさえ面倒だ。Tema ga kakaru ryouri wa oroka, nichijou no kantan na ryouri o tsukuru no sae mendou da. Never mind elaborate dishes — even making simple everyday food is a bother. मेहनत वाले पकवान तो दूर, रोज़ का सादा खाना बनाना भी झंझट लगता है। મહેનતવાળી વાનગીઓ તો દૂરની વાત, રોજનું સાદું ખાવાનું બનાવવું પણ કંટાળાજનક લાગે છે.
小売店を取り巻く状況は厳しい。町の専門店はおろか、有名デパートの閉店も相次いでいる。Kouriten o torimaku joukyou wa kibishii. Machi no senmonten wa oroka, yuumei depaato no heiten mo aitsuide iru. Things are tough for retailers. Not just small local specialist shops — even famous department stores are closing one after another. खुदरा दुकानों की हालत ख़राब है। मोहल्ले की ख़ास दुकानें तो छोड़िए, मशहूर डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर भी एक के बाद एक बंद हो रहे हैं। છૂટક દુકાનોની હાલત ખરાબ છે. વિસ્તારની ખાસ દુકાનો તો છોડો, જાણીતા ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર પણ એક પછી એક બંધ થઈ રહ્યા છે.
わたしは花粉症がひどくて、外ではおろか、家の中でさえマスクがはずせない。Watashi wa kafunshou ga hidokute, soto de wa oroka, ie no naka de sae masuku ga hazusenai. My hay fever is so bad that I can't take my mask off even indoors, let alone outside. मेरी परागज-एलर्जी इतनी तेज़ है कि बाहर तो क्या, घर के अंदर भी मास्क नहीं उतार पाती। મારી પરાગરજની ઍલર્જી એટલી તીવ્ર છે કે બહાર તો શું, ઘરમાં પણ માસ્ક ઉતારી નથી શકતી.

Order matters: "X はおろか Y さえ/も…" — X is the obvious case, Y the extreme one. If you swap them the sentence collapses (drill [4] Q1–2). It's mostly used for things being lacking or bad (can't, closing, no money), and (added, not in book) it isn't followed by a request, wish or command (×遊ぶのはおろか勉強もしなさい) — use 〜はもちろん for that.

さる = "that (well-known) thing": "~ is as it is, but…". The first item is accepted (not denied), and the second is on the same side — two good points (①②) or two problems (③) — with the weight on the second. Contrast with 〜はおろか: はおろか climbs to a more extreme degree and leans negative; もさることながら simply adds a more important point (drill [1〜5] Q5).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two "only" forms of praise (をおいて, ならでは) and three "not only… but" forms (にとどまらず, はおろか, もさることながら). The N2 〔復習〕 forms are their plain relatives:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜をおいて~o oite Apart from ~, there's no other N + をおいて + ほかに〜ない Praise of a single thing; must end in a negative (ほかにない／いない／あるまい). Naming the one and only candidate (彼をおいてほかにいない).
〜ならでは~nara de wa Only ~ could; unique to ~ N + ならではの N／ならではだ Praise of a quality unique to the noun; followed by の+N or だ, not by ほかにない. Ads, reviews: ハワイならではの体験.
〜に限り (N2)~ni kagiri Only for ~ N + に限り Neutral restriction, no praise. Conditions: 70歳以上の方に限り無料.
〜にとどまらず~ni todomarazu Not limited to ~ (reaches wider) N／V-辞書形 + にとどまらず Small range → bigger range that includes it; neutral/positive. Spread, influence (一国にとどまらず世界中に).
〜はおろか~wa oroka Let alone ~; not to mention ~, even… N(+助詞) + はおろか … も／さえ／まで Obvious case → more extreme case; usually negative; no requests/wishes after it (added, not in book). "Can't even…" (会話はおろかあいさつもできない).
〜もさることながら~mo saru koto nagara ~ too, but even more… N + もさることながら Accepts the first item and adds a weightier one on the same side; no degree-climb needed. Formal evaluation (人柄もさることながら頭の良さ…).
〜ばかりか／〜上に (N2)~bakari ka / ~ue ni Not only… but also / on top of 普通形 + ばかりか; 名詞修飾形 + 上に Plain "adding" forms without the strong evaluation of the N1 forms. Everyday adding of facts.
Exam trap 1 — をおいて vs. ならでは: both praise something unique, but the ending decides: …ほかにない → をおいて; …の N or …だ → ならでは ([1〜5] Q2–3).
Exam trap 2 — direction of はおろか: "X はおろか Y も／さえ" — Y is the more extreme item. If the options swap the order (身の回りのことはおろか家の掃除さえ), the logic breaks ([4] Q1–2).
Exam trap 3 — にとどまらず needs a bigger, containing range: 一部の女性 → 多くの人 ✓, but 女性 → 男性 or わたし → 母 ✗. And with verbs, にとどまらず takes the dictionary form, whereas 〜に限らず attaches to nouns ([3] Q3, [1〜5] Q1).
