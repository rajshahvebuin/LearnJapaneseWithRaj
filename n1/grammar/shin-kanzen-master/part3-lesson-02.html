Notes
The forms in A were taught in 第1部 9課 (条件) and 第1部 10課 (逆接条件); here the point is reading them in a passage: the same form can state a hypothesis (仮定 — not yet real) or a condition that is already fact (確定), and only the context tells you which. Section B is the counterfactual (反実仮想), linked back to 第3部 1課 B-3/C-1. This lesson has no 練習2: 練習1 (4 short passages, 3 a/b choices each) and まとめ (passage cloze, 5 blanks, 4 options).
Book introduction (p.124): 文章としてのまとまりを持たせるために、ある条件をどう扱うかがポイントになることがあります。仮定か・確定かの判断とともに、後に来る文はどうつながるのかなどが文の流れを決める要素になります。Bunshou to shite no matomari o motaseru tame ni, aru jouken o dou atsukau ka ga pointo ni naru koto ga arimasu. Katei ka・kakutei ka no handan to tomo ni, ato ni kuru bun wa dou tsunagaru no ka nado ga bun no nagare o kimeru youso ni narimasu.
EN: How a condition is handled can be the key to making a text hang together. Deciding whether it is hypothetical or already settled, and how the following sentence connects to it, determines the flow.
HI: किसी शर्त को कैसे प्रस्तुत किया जाता है, यही अक्सर लेख को एकजुट बनाने की कुंजी होती है। वह काल्पनिक है या तय हो चुकी है — यह तय करना, और अगला वाक्य उससे कैसे जुड़ता है, ये बातें लेख का प्रवाह निर्धारित करती हैं।
GU: કોઈ શરતને કેવી રીતે રજૂ કરાય છે, એ જ ઘણી વાર લખાણને એકસૂત્ર બનાવવાની ચાવી હોય છે. તે કાલ્પનિક છે કે નક્કી થઈ ચૂકેલી — એ નક્કી કરવું, અને પછીનું વાક્ય તેની સાથે કેવી રીતે જોડાય છે, એ બાબતો લખાણનો પ્રવાહ નક્કી કરે છે.

1 Grammar Points

A 条件を表す文法形式（仮定か確定か） — the book's rule: 同じ文法形式でも、実現していないことを仮定して条件を言う場合（仮定）と、実現したことを条件として言う場合（確定）があります。文脈からそれらを判断します。Onaji bunpou keishiki demo, jitsugen shite inai koto o katei shite jouken o iu baai (katei) to, jitsugen shita koto o jouken to shite iu baai (kakutei) ga arimasu. Bunmyaku kara sorera o handan shimasu.
EN: The same form can state a condition by supposing something not yet real (仮定 katei) or by taking something that has already happened as the condition (確定 kakutei). You judge which from the context.
HI: एक ही व्याकरण-रूप से कभी ऐसी बात मानकर शर्त कही जाती है जो अभी हुई नहीं (仮定 — कल्पना), और कभी ऐसी बात को शर्त बनाया जाता है जो हो चुकी है (確定 — तय)। कौन-सा है, यह संदर्भ से तय होता है।
GU: એક જ વ્યાકરણ-રૂપથી ક્યારેક એવી વાત ધારીને શરત કહેવાય છે જે હજી બની નથી (仮定 — કલ્પના), અને ક્યારેક એવી વાતને શરત બનાવાય છે જે બની ચૂકી છે (確定 — નક્કી). કયું છે, તે સંદર્ભ પરથી નક્કી થાય છે.

Points A-1 to A-7 are forms already taught in 第1部, so there is no formation table here — the connection rules and full drills are on the 9課 and 10課 pages. Each block gives the book's paired examples, labelled (仮定) or (確定) exactly as the book does.

(નક્કી)

The main clause is the speaker's resolve (つもりだ・〜てもいい). In 練習1 passage 1 and 2 the distractor 〜とあれば fails because the following clause is a general negative fact, not a resolve.

neutral
Reading~tara saigo / ~ta ga saigo
Meaning (EN)"once ~ happens, that's it (a bad outcome is certain)"
Meaning (HI)एक बार ~ हो गया, तो बस (बुरा नतीजा तय)
Meaning (GU)એક વાર ~ થયું, તો પત્યું (ખરાબ પરિણામ નક્કી)
Connection (接続)動 た形 + ら最後／が最後
In contextSame sentence, two readings: as a warning (仮定 — trust not yet lost) or as a reflection after the fact (確定 — trust already lost).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
我々は社会的な信用を失わないようにしなければならない。信用を失ったら最後、取り戻すのは難しい。（仮定）Wareware wa shakaiteki na shin'you o ushinawanai you ni shinakereba naranai. Shin'you o ushinattara saigo, torimodosu no wa muzukashii. (katei) We must make sure we don't lose public trust. Once trust is lost, it's hard to win back. (hypothetical) हमें ध्यान रखना होगा कि सामाजिक भरोसा न खोएँ। एक बार भरोसा गया, तो उसे वापस पाना मुश्किल है। (कल्पना) આપણે ધ્યાન રાખવું પડશે કે સામાજિક વિશ્વાસ ન ગુમાવીએ. એક વાર વિશ્વાસ ગયો, તો તેને પાછો મેળવવો મુશ્કેલ છે. (કલ્પના)
今回の事故で我々は社会的な信用を失ってしまった。信用を失ったら最後、取り戻すのは難しいだろう。今後の方策を真剣に考えなければならない。（確定）Konkai no jiko de wareware wa shakaiteki na shin'you o ushinatte shimatta. Shin'you o ushinattara saigo, torimodosu no wa muzukashii darou. Kongo no housaku o shinken ni kangaenakereba naranai. (kakutei) Through this accident we have lost public trust. Once trust is lost, it will probably be hard to win back. We must think seriously about our future measures. (settled) इस दुर्घटना से हमने सामाजिक भरोसा खो दिया है। एक बार भरोसा गया, तो वापस पाना शायद मुश्किल होगा। हमें आगे के उपायों पर गंभीरता से सोचना होगा। (तय) આ અકસ્માતથી આપણે સામાજિક વિશ્વાસ ગુમાવી દીધો છે. એક વાર વિશ્વાસ ગયો, તો પાછો મેળવવો કદાચ મુશ્કેલ હશે. આપણે આગળના ઉપાયો પર ગંભીરતાથી વિચારવું પડશે. (નક્કી)

This pair is the clearest demonstration of the lesson: the second clause is word-for-word the same; only the sentence before (失わないように vs 失ってしまった) decides 仮定 or 確定.

【A-5】 〜くらいなら →第1部 9課

neutral
Reading~kurai nara
Meaning (EN)"rather than (go as far as) ~, it's better to …"
Meaning (HI)~ करने से तो बेहतर है कि…
Meaning (GU)~ કરવા કરતાં તો સારું કે…

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

(hypothetical) इससे ज़्यादा ज़ोर लगाया तो सेहत बिगड़ सकती है। सेहत बिगाड़ने से तो बेहतर है कि यह काम छोड़ दूँ। (कल्पना) આનાથી વધારે જોર કર્યું તો તબિયત બગડી શકે. તબિયત બગાડવા કરતાં તો સારું કે આ કામ છોડી દઉં. (કલ્પના)
無理をして体を壊してしまった。体を壊すくらいなら、初めから引き受けなければよかった。（確定）Muri o shite karada o kowashite shimatta. Karada o kowasu kurai nara, hajime kara hikiukenakereba yokatta. (kakutei) I overdid it and ruined my health. If it was going to come to that, I shouldn't have taken it on in the first place. (settled) ज़्यादा ज़ोर लगाकर सेहत बिगाड़ ली। अगर ऐसा ही होना था, तो शुरू से ही काम नहीं लेना चाहिए था। (तय) વધુ પડતું જોર કરીને તબિયત બગાડી. જો આવું જ થવાનું હતું, તો શરૂઆતથી જ કામ ન લેવું જોઈતું હતું. (નક્કી)

Notice how the main clause's ending switches with 仮定／確定: advice (ほうがいい) vs regret (ばよかった). That's the lesson's point in miniature.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The book's own distractors pit each condition form against a near neighbour. Ask two questions every time: is the condition real or imagined? and what kind of clause follows (resolve, advice, regret, negative judgement)?

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ようでは~you de wa If (you're) like that, (it's bad) V-dict／V-ない + ようでは Usually 確定: criticises a real habit or shortcoming; ends in a negative judgement. A superior or interviewer scolding (練習1 1-③, 4-②).
〜としたら／〜とすれば~to shitara / ~to sureba Supposing ~ 普通形 + としたら Pure 仮定; neutral, no criticism. Hypothesising, not judging a fact.
〜とあれば~to areba If it's for ~, (I'll even…) 普通形 (N/ナ だ drops) + とあれば Main clause = willingness / resolve to do something special. 子供のためとあれば私費も投じる. Not for general negatives (練習1 1-①, 2-③).
〜なしには／〜を抜きにしては／〜ないことには~nashi ni wa / ~o nuki ni shite wa / ~nai koto ni wa Without / unless ~, not … N + なしには; V-ない + ことには Must be followed by a negative or impossibility. Stating a necessary precondition.
〜たら最後~tara saigo Once ~, it's all over V-た + ら最後 Bad, irreversible result. Not for desirable actions (×結果を出したら最後). Warnings or rueful reflection.
〜ば／〜ていれば … 〜ばよかった・〜んだった・〜ものを~ba … ~ba yokatta / ~n datta / ~mono o I wish / should have ~ 反実仮想 endings Only about what did not happen (B). Regret about your own past (練習1 3-①②, 4-①).
〜ばいい・〜たほうがいい~ba ii / ~ta hou ga ii You should ~ / it's good to ~ advice About the future; the action is still possible. Advising others who still have time (練習1 4-③).
〜(よ)うと・〜であろうと vs 〜としても~(y)ou to / ~de arou to vs ~to shite mo No matter ~ / even if ~ 意向形 + と; 普通形 + としても (よ)うと = "whatever/however ~" (range of cases); としても = one supposition conceded. 難しい課題であろうと (any degree of difficulty) vs たとえいたとしても (even if such a person existed).
Exam trap 1 — the same sentence, two readings: 信用を失ったら最後… can be a warning (仮定) or a lament (確定). The form doesn't change — read the sentence before the blank to see whether the event has happened (A-2).
Exam trap 2 — regret vs advice: 〜ばよかった／〜んだった only about the past and only when it did not happen; 〜ばいい／〜たほうがいい only when it can still happen. Check who the sentence is addressed to and whether there's まだ時間がある or 後悔する nearby (練習1 3-①, 4-③).
Exam trap 3 — 前置き are not conditions: 思えば, 考えてみれば, 言ってみれば, はっきり言うと and わたしに言わせてもらえば are lead-ins. Don't look for a hypothesis or a following result clause (C).
