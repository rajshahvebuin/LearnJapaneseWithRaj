Notes
Third cumulative test: 15 items, 4 options (1・2・3・4), 問題1 format (文法形式の判断 — fill the blank). The weight is on the newest lessons: 9課 条件 (5 items), 10課 逆接条件 (3) and 12課 原因・理由 (3). No correct answer comes from 2課, 3課, 4課, 5課 or 7課, though some of their forms appear as distractors. Many distractors are look-alike endings on the same verb (ところに／ところが／ところを／ところで; とあれば／とあって／とすれば／として), so the skill is knowing what each ending must be followed by. Print quirk: in item 13 the book labels the options "1, 2, 2, 4" — the second "2" (批判されまいと) is a misprint for 3; I number it 3 below.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

次の文の（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no (___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — "Choose the best option (1–4) for the blank." 15 items, p.56–57. All answers confirmed from 別冊 p.3 (PDF page 195).

Q1

本日は大雨の（　）わざわざお越しいただきまして、恐縮でございます。Honjitsu wa ooame no (___) wazawaza okoshi itadakimashite, kyoushuku de gozaimasu.

Full sentence: 本日は大雨のところをわざわざお越しいただきまして、恐縮でございます。Honjitsu wa ooame no tokoro o wazawaza okoshi itadakimashite, kyoushuku de gozaimasu.
EN: We are truly grateful that you have taken the trouble to come today in spite of the heavy rain.
HI: आज इतनी तेज़ बारिश के बावजूद आप ख़ास तौर पर पधारे, इसके लिए हम आपके बहुत आभारी हैं।
GU: આજે આટલા ભારે વરસાદ છતાં આપ ખાસ પધાર્યા, એ બદલ અમે આપના ખૂબ આભારી છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ところにtokoro nijust when (something happened)ठीक उसी समय जबબરાબર ત્યારે જ્યારે
2ところがtokoro gahowever / but thenलेकिन / पर हुआ यह किપરંતુ / પણ થયું એવું કે
3ところをtokoro oin spite of (the situation) — with thanks/apology(ऐसी स्थिति) के बावजूद(આવી સ્થિતિ) છતાં
4ところでtokoro deby the way / even ifवैसे / भले हीબાય ધ વે / ભલે

Why: 〜ところを (8課) = "although you are in such a situation", typically followed by thanks or apology for troubling someone (お忙しいところを…, 大雨のところを…). 1) ところに marks the moment something else arrives/happens (出かけようとしたところに電話が来た). 2) ところが is a conjunction "however" and can't follow 大雨の. 4) ところで is "by the way", or 〜たところで "even if" (10課), which needs a た形.

Q2

期待の新人選手が初めて試合に出る（　）、観客席は満員だった。Kitai no shinjin senshu ga hajimete shiai ni deru (___), kankyakuseki wa man'in datta.

Full sentence: 期待の新人選手が初めて試合に出るとあって、観客席は満員だった。Kitai no shinjin senshu ga hajimete shiai ni deru to atte, kankyakuseki wa man'in datta.
EN: With the much-anticipated rookie playing in a match for the first time, the stands were full.
HI: बहुप्रतीक्षित नया खिलाड़ी पहली बार मैच खेल रहा था, इसलिए दर्शक-दीर्घा खचाखच भरी थी।
GU: બહુપ્રતીક્ષિત નવો ખેલાડી પહેલી વાર મૅચ રમી રહ્યો હતો, એટલે પ્રેક્ષકગૅલરી ખીચોખીચ ભરેલી હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1としてto shiteas (a role)...के रूप में...તરીકે
2ときてto kite(×) and then (on top of that)(×) और ऊपर से(×) અને ઉપરથી
3とあってto attebecause it's (the special occasion of)(ख़ास मौक़ा) होने के कारण(ખાસ પ્રસંગ) હોવાથી
4といってto ittesaying thatयह कहकरએમ કહીને

Why: A special situation (the star rookie's debut) → its natural, observed result (full stands), about third parties → 〜とあって (12課). 1) として marks a role/capacity and doesn't express a reason. 2) 〜ときて is used for listing (or ときたら "speaking of" with criticism), not for cause. 4) といって would mean "saying that he'd play", making the stands the speaker.

Q3

毎年年末に放送されるこの国民的な歌番組を（　）年が越せないという人も多い。Maitoshi nenmatsu ni housou sareru kono kokuminteki na uta bangumi o (___) toshi ga kosenai to iu hito mo ooi.

Full sentence: 毎年年末に放送されるこの国民的な歌番組を見ることなしに年が越せないという人も多い。Maitoshi nenmatsu ni housou sareru kono kokuminteki na uta bangumi o miru koto nashi ni toshi ga kosenai to iu hito mo ooi.
EN: Many people say they can't see the year out without watching this national song show broadcast every year-end.
HI: बहुत से लोग कहते हैं कि हर साल के अंत में आने वाला यह राष्ट्रीय गीत-कार्यक्रम देखे बिना उनका नया साल नहीं आता।
GU: ઘણા લોકો કહે છે કે દર વર્ષના અંતે આવતો આ રાષ્ટ્રીય ગીત-કાર્યક્રમ જોયા વિના તેમનું નવું વર્ષ નથી આવતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見るともなくmiru tomo nakuwithout really meaning to watchबिना ध्यान दिए यूँ ही देखते हुएધ્યાન આપ્યા વિના એમ જ જોતાં
2見ることなしにmiru koto nashi niwithout watchingदेखे बिनाજોયા વિના
3見るのをよそにmiru no o yoso ni(×) ignoring the watching(×) देखने की परवाह किए बिना(×) જોવાની પરવા કર્યા વિના
4見るのをおいてmiru no o oite(×) other than watching(×) देखने के सिवा(×) જોવા સિવાય

Why: "Without A, B can't happen" + a negative ending (年が越せない) → 動 辞書形 + こと + なしに(は) (9課). 1) 〜ともなく (6課) = doing something absent-mindedly — no condition. 3) 〜をよそに (5課) = ignoring others' worries/feelings and takes a noun (周囲の心配をよそに). 4) 〜をおいて (3課) = "no one but ~" with a person/thing noun, not 見るの.

Q4

（　）相手が偉い人物であろうと、わたしは記者として真実を新聞に書く。(___) aite ga erai jinbutsu de arou to, watashi wa kisha to shite shinjitsu o shinbun ni kaku.

Full sentence: たとえ相手が偉い人物であろうと、わたしは記者として真実を新聞に書く。Tatoe aite ga erai jinbutsu de arou to, watashi wa kisha to shite shinjitsu o shinbun ni kaku.
EN: Even if the other party is an important figure, as a journalist I will write the truth in the paper.
HI: सामने वाला चाहे कितना ही बड़ा आदमी क्यों न हो, पत्रकार होने के नाते मैं अख़बार में सच ही लिखूँगा।
GU: સામેવાળો ભલે ગમે તેટલો મોટો માણસ હોય, પત્રકાર તરીકે હું છાપામાં સત્ય જ લખીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1たとえtatoeeven ifचाहे / भले हीભલે / ગમે તે
2たとえばtatoebafor exampleउदाहरण के लिएઉદાહરણ તરીકે
3どんなdonnawhat kind ofकैसाકેવું
4なにもnani mo(not) anythingकुछ भी (नहीं)કશું (નહીં)

Why: 〜であろうと ("even if it is ~", 10課【1】) is often introduced by たとえ・いかに・どんなに, as the book's ⚠ note says → たとえ. 2) たとえば means "for example". 3) どんな must sit right before the noun it asks about (どんな相手であろうと), so ×どんな相手が偉い人物であろうと is broken. 4) なにも pairs with a negative.

Q5

生物というものは、子孫を残すため（　）、どんなことでもする。Seibutsu to iu mono wa, shison o nokosu tame (___), donna koto demo suru.

Full sentence: 生物というものは、子孫を残すためとあれば、どんなことでもする。Seibutsu to iu mono wa, shison o nokosu tame to areba, donna koto demo suru.

GU: જીવો પોતાનાં સંતાન છોડવા માટે હોય, તો કંઈ પણ કરી છૂટે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1とあればto arebaif it's for (such a special reason)अगर ...के लिए हो, तोજો ...માટે હોય, તો
2とあってto attebecause it's (a special occasion)(ख़ास मौक़ा) होने के कारण(ખાસ પ્રસંગ) હોવાથી
3とすればto surebasupposing thatमान लें किમાની લઈએ કે
4としてto shiteas (a role)...के रूप में...તરીકે

Why: "For a special condition like ~, (one will do anything)" → 〜ためとあれば (9課【1】: 子供のためとあれば…). 2) 〜とあって (12課) reports a fact caused by a special situation; it can't take a general "will do anything" and doesn't follow ため. 3) とすれば is a hypothetical "if we suppose" — no sense of a special condition. 4) ためとして is not a pattern.

Q6

祖父母は、人一倍の忍耐力（　）困難な時代を生き抜いた。Sofubo wa, hitoichibai no nintairyoku (___) konnan na jidai o ikinuita.

Full sentence: 祖父母は、人一倍の忍耐力をもって困難な時代を生き抜いた。Sofubo wa, hitoichibai no nintairyoku o motte konnan na jidai o ikinuita.
EN: My grandparents lived through hard times by means of patience far greater than most people's.
HI: मेरे दादा-दादी ने दूसरों से कहीं ज़्यादा धैर्य के बल पर कठिन दौर पार किया।
GU: મારાં દાદા-દાદીએ બીજાઓ કરતાં અનેકગણી ધીરજના બળે મુશ્કેલ સમય પાર કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1を通してo tooshitethrough (a medium / a whole period)...के माध्यम से / पूरे ...में...ના માધ્યમથી / આખા ...માં
2をもってo motteby means of...के बल पर...ના બળે
3にわたってni watatteover (a span of time/space)(पूरी अवधि) तक(આખા ગાળા) સુધી
4にかかわってni kakawattebeing involved in...से जुड़कर...સાથે સંકળાઈને

Why: An abstract, admirable means (人一倍の忍耐力) used to achieve something → 〜をもって (11課【3】; compare 11課 [3] Q2 人一倍の努力をもって). 1) を通して marks a medium/intermediary (友人を通して) or a whole period, not one's inner strength. 3) にわたって needs a span (3年にわたって). 4) にかかわって means "being involved in" — no means relation.

Q7

親が何を（　）、子供は親の言うとおりにはならないものだ。Oya ga nani o (___), kodomo wa oya no iu toori ni wa naranai mono da.

Why: 何を + 〜たところで + negative judgement = "whatever you do, it's useless" (10課【4】: どんなに説明したところで…). 1) といっても and 2) とはいえ (8課) concede a fact ("they did wish, but…") and don't combine with the question word 何を. 4) 〜まいと only works in the pair 〜(よ)うと〜まいと (10課【2】), and still not with 何を.

Q8

面接試験では、難しい質問に（　）、また別の質問が次々に出された。Mensetsu shiken de wa, muzukashii shitsumon ni (___), mata betsu no shitsumon ga tsugitsugi ni dasareta.

3理解がなければこそrikai ga nakereba kosoprecisely because there's no understandingसमझ नहीं है, इसीलिए तोસમજણ નથી, એટલે જ તો
4理解がなかったが最後rikai ga nakatta ga saigo(×) once there was no understanding, that's the end(×) एक बार समझ न रही तो ख़त्म(×) એક વાર સમજણ ન રહી તો ખતમ

Why: "If something considered absolutely necessary is missing, B can't happen" + a negative (できない) → N + なくしては (9課【4】). 1) 〜ものなら goes with a potential verb (できるものなら) for an unlikely wish. 3) ばこそ (12課) stresses an unexpected reason, not a condition. 4) 〜たが最後 (9課) attaches to an action verb (飲み始めたが最後), and ×なかったが最後 is unnatural.

Q10

あの大臣、あんなひどい発言を（　）辞職に追い込まれるかもしれない。Ano daijin, anna hidoi hatsugen o (___) jishoku ni oikomareru kamo shirenai.

Full sentence: あの大臣、あんなひどい発言を繰り返すようでは辞職に追い込まれるかもしれない。Ano daijin, anna hidoi hatsugen o kurikaesu you de wa jishoku ni oikomareru kamo shirenai.

Why: An undesirable state + a prediction of a bad outcome (〜かもしれない) → 〜ようでは (9課【3】). 1) とばかりに (6課) describes someone's manner "as if to say" — no condition. 2) くらいなら (9課) must be followed by "it would be better to…" (〜ほうがいい). 4) ものなら needs a potential verb or the 〜(よ)うものなら form.

Q11

こんなところで財布を（　）、絶対お金が入ったまま戻ってはこないと思う。Konna tokoro de saifu o (___), zettai okane ga haitta mama modotte wa konai to omou.

Full sentence: こんなところで財布を落としたら最後、絶対お金が入ったまま戻ってはこないと思う。Konna tokoro de saifu o otoshitara saigo, zettai okane ga haitta mama modotte wa konai to omou.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1落としたようではotoshita you de wa(×) if you're the sort who dropped it(×) अगर गिरा देने जैसी हालत हो(×) જો પાડી દેવા જેવી હાલત હોય

Why: "Once A happens, a terrible result is certain" (絶対…ない) → 〜たら最後 (9課【2】, also usable for hypotheticals). 1) 〜ようでは criticises a habit/state (すぐ落とすようでは) rather than one hypothetical act. 3) 〜たところで (10課) means the act is pointless — the opposite of "disaster". 4) 〜たら〜たで (10課) means "even then, there's another problem / it's fine" — it doesn't predict certain loss.

Q12

幼稚園（　）、仕事中はおしゃべりはやめてください。Youchien (___), shigotochuu wa oshaberi wa yamete kudasai.

Full sentence: 幼稚園ではあるまいし、仕事中はおしゃべりはやめてください。Youchien de wa aru mai shi, shigotochuu wa oshaberi wa yamete kudasai.
EN: This isn't kindergarten — please stop chattering while you're working.
HI: यह कोई किंडरगार्टन तो है नहीं, काम के समय गपशप बंद कीजिए।
GU: આ કંઈ બાલમંદિર તો છે નહીં, કામ વખતે ગપસપ બંધ કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ではあるまいしde wa aru mai shithis isn't a ~, soकोई ...तो है नहीं, फिरકંઈ ...તો છે નહીં, પછી
2のことだしno koto da shisince it's (typical of) ~चूँकि ...की बात हैકારણ કે ...ની વાત છે

Why: "We're obviously not in a kindergarten, so stop it" — something clearly untrue + a request that rejects the current behaviour → 〜ではあるまいし (12課【3】). 2) 〜のことだし ("knowing ~, …") needs a person and supports, not rejects, the conclusion. 3) and 4) are concessive "even if" forms (cf. 10課) that make the request illogical ("even if this isn't a kindergarten, stop chatting").

Q13

あの議員は、マスコミにどんなに（　）、平気な顔をしている。Ano giin wa, masukomi ni donna ni (___), heiki na kao o shite iru.

Full sentence: あの議員は、マスコミにどんなに批判されようと、平気な顔をしている。Ano giin wa, masukomi ni donna ni hihan sareyou to, heiki na kao o shite iru.
EN: However harshly that politician is criticised by the media, he keeps a nonchalant face.
HI: मीडिया उस सांसद की कितनी भी आलोचना करे, वह बेफ़िक्र चेहरा बनाए रहता है।
GU: મીડિયા તે સાંસદની ગમે તેટલી ટીકા કરે, તે બેફિકર ચહેરો રાખે છે.

1批判されようとされまいとhihan sareyou to saremai towhether criticised or notआलोचना हो या न होટીકા થાય કે ન થાય
2批判されればされたでhihan sarereba sareta deif criticised, well then (another issue)आलोचना हुई तो हुई (फिर अलग बात)ટીકા થઈ તો થઈ (પછી અલગ વાત)

Q14

Q15

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The distractors in this test pit look-alike endings against each other. The forms that were set side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ところを (8課) vs ところに／ところが／ところで~tokoro o / ni / ga / de In spite of (your situation) vs. just when / however / by the way N の／V + ところを ところを = "you were busy/it was raining, yet…" + thanks/apology; ところに = something arrives at that moment. お忙しいところをありがとうございます (Q1).
〜とあって (12課) vs 〜とあれば (9課)~to atte / ~to areba Because it's special → natural result vs. if it's for ~ → I'll do anything N・普通形 + とあって／とあれば とあって = reason + observed fact about others; とあれば = condition + will/judgement. 新人が出るとあって満員 (Q2) / 子孫を残すためとあれば何でもする (Q5).
〜ことなしに・〜なくしては (9課)~koto nashi ni / ~nakushite wa Without ~ (B is impossible) V-辞書形 + ことなしに／N + なくしては Followed by a negative (できない, 越せない). Verbs need こと; nouns attach directly. 見ることなしに年が越せない (Q3) / 理解なくしてはできない (Q9).
〜(よ)うと (10課) vs 〜(よ)うと〜まいと (10課)~(y)ou to / ~(y)ou to ~mai to No matter how ~ vs. whether or not ~ V-う・よう形 + と／+ と + V-辞書形 + まいと どんなに・たとえ・いかに go with the single 〜(よ)うと; the pair is for yes/no alternatives. どんなに批判されようと (Q13) / たとえ…であろうと (Q4).
〜たところで (10課) vs 〜たら最後 (9課)~ta tokoro de / ~tara saigo Even if you do ~, it's useless vs. once you do ~, disaster V-た + ところで／V-た + ら最後 ところで → "no point"; たら最後 → "certain bad end". Both are often hypothetical. 何を望んだところで (Q7) / 財布を落としたら最後 (Q11).
〜ようでは (9課) vs 〜くらいなら (9課)~you de wa / ~kurai nara If (such a bad state continues), bad result vs. rather than ~, better to… 普通形 + ようでは／V-辞書形 + くらいなら ようでは + negative prediction; くらいなら + 〜ほうがいい. ひどい発言を繰り返すようでは辞職 (Q10).
〜ではあるまいし (12課) vs 〜ではなかろうと~de wa aru mai shi / ~de wa nakarou to You're not a ~, so (stop it) vs. even if it isn't ~ N + ではあるまいし／ではなかろうと あるまいし = obvious untruth → rejection/advice; なかろうと = concession, result unaffected. 幼稚園ではあるまいし、やめて (Q12).
〜手前 (12課) vs 〜(よ)うと (10課)~temae / ~(y)ou to Because others are watching, I must/can't vs. even if ~, unaffected V-ている + 手前／V-よう + と 手前 restricts the speaker (〜わけにはいかない); ようと says nothing changes. みんなが見ている手前 (Q15).
〜んばかりに (6課) vs 〜んがために (11課)~n bakari ni / ~n ga tame ni As if about to ~ vs. in order to ~ V-ない stem + んばかりに／んがために Same ん-stem, different job: manner vs. purpose. ×んがゆえに is not a pattern. 「だめ」と言わんばかりに (Q14).
〜をもって (11課) vs を通して／にわたって~o motte / ~o tooshite / ~ni watatte By means of vs. through (a medium) / over (a span) N + をもって をもって = an abstract means (努力, 忍耐力, 書面); 通して = intermediary; わたって = duration. 人一倍の忍耐力をもって (Q6).
Exam trap 1 — same verb, different ending: when all four options share the same stem (ところ〜, と〜, 落とした〜), read the end of the sentence first: thanks/apology → ところを; "will do anything" → とあれば; observed result → とあって; "certain disaster" → たら最後; "pointless" → たところで (Q1, Q2, Q5, Q7, Q11).
Exam trap 2 — question words and adverbs pick the form: どんなに／たとえ → 〜(よ)うと (not the pair 〜うと〜まいと); 何を → 〜たところで; また／次々に → 〜そばから (Q4, Q7, Q8, Q13).
Exam trap 3 — "without" needs a negative: 〜ことなしに and 〜なくしては must be followed by できない／〜ない. If the conclusion is positive, they are wrong (Q3, Q9).
