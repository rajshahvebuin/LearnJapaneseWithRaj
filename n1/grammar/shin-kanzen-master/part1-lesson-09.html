Answer key

Notes
Five "if" forms, each with a built-in attitude: a special reason that justifies anything (とあれば), a trigger for disaster (たら最後), an undesirable state predicting a bad result (ようでは), an absolute requirement + negative (なしには), and "the worst case — I'd rather…" (くらいなら). The exam tests them on the second clause: judgment vs. question, terrible outcome vs. mild one, prediction vs. command, negative vs. affirmative, preferable alternative vs. anything else. None of the five is tagged 硬い言い方 in the book, though 〜なくして(は) is noticeably literary.
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.42): three N2 conditionals. 〜ないことには is the N2 cousin of 【4】〜なしには (both need a negative after them); 〜ものなら is a near relative of 【5】〜くらいなら.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とあれば

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~to areba
Meaning (EN)"if it is (for) ~, then (I will / it must be…)" — book: 〜という特別な条件なら、あることをする・ある状態だろう (given such a special condition, someone will do something / something will be the case)
Meaning (HI)अगर बात ... की हो, तो (ज़रूर...)
Meaning (GU)જો વાત ... ની હોય, તો (જરૂર...)
Connection (接続)名・普通形 + とあれば
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal/written.
Typical useWhat follows is mainly the speaker's wish, intention or judgment (希望・意向・判断). The condition is something special (for my child, for my family, if money is needed for hospital) that justifies the result.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (普通形)(added, not in book) V-plain + とあれば(added, not in book) 社長が来るとあればshachou ga kuru to areba
い-adjective(added, not in book) A-い + とあれば(added, not in book) 安いとあればyasui to areba
な-adjectiveNa + だ + とあればお金が必要だとあればokane ga hitsuyou da to areba
NounN + とあれば子供のためとあればkodomo no tame to areba ／ 一人暮らしとあればhitorigurashi to areba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子供のためとあれば、わたしはどんなことでも我慢しますよ。Kodomo no tame to areba, watashi wa donna koto de mo gaman shimasu yo. If it's for my children, I'll put up with anything. अगर बात बच्चों की भलाई की हो, तो मैं कुछ भी सह लूँगी। જો વાત બાળકોના ભલાની હોય, તો હું કંઈ પણ સહન કરી લઈશ.
小さい島での一人暮らしとあれば、不自由なことも多いだろう。Chiisai shima de no hitorigurashi to areba, fujiyuu na koto mo ooi darou. If you're living alone on a small island, there must be a lot of inconveniences. अगर छोटे-से द्वीप पर अकेले रहना हो, तो कई असुविधाएँ भी होंगी। જો નાના ટાપુ પર એકલા રહેવાનું હોય, તો ઘણી અગવડો પણ હશે.
入院のためにお金が必要だとあれば、なんとかしてお金を用意しなければならない。Nyuuin no tame ni okane ga hitsuyou da to areba, nan to ka shite okane o youi shinakereba naranai. If money is needed for the hospital stay, I'll have to find it somehow. अगर अस्पताल में भर्ती के लिए पैसे चाहिए, तो किसी भी तरह इंतज़ाम करना ही होगा। જો હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ થવા માટે પૈસા જોઈએ, તો ગમે તેમ કરીને વ્યવસ્થા કરવી જ પડશે.

とあれば = "if that is the situation" — a stiffer 〜なら that stresses how special the condition is. The second clause is the speaker's stance: 我慢します (intention), 多いだろう／するだろう (judgment), 〜なければならない. It is usually not followed by a plain question or a request to someone else — that's the trap in drill [1] (だれが払うんですか, どうか払ってください, どんなことをするんですか are all wrong). The condition also has to be weighty enough: あなたの頼み works, 日常の家事 doesn't ([1] Q2).

【2】 〜たら最後・〜たが最後

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; たが最後 is stiffer)
Reading~tara saigo / ~ta ga saigo
Meaning (EN)"once ~ happens, that's it — something terrible follows" — book: 〜たら、必ずひどいことになる (if ~, it will definitely end badly)
Meaning (HI)एक बार ... हो गया, तो बस (बुरा हाल होना तय है)
Meaning (GU)એક વાર ... થયું, તો બસ (ખરાબ હાલ થવાના જ)
Connection (接続)動 た形 + ら最後・が最後
RegisterNeutral; 〜たら最後 is common in speech, 〜たが最後 sounds more written.
Typical useCan attach to things known to actually happen (①②, habits) or to a hypothesis (③). What follows is a situation the speaker considers extremely bad (非常に悪いと考えている事態) — and that can't be undone.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (た形 + ら)V-た + ら最後飲み始めたら最後nomihajimetara saigo ／ 持たせたら最後motasetara saigo
Verb (た形 + が)V-た + が最後座ったが最後suwatta ga saigo
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — the trigger must be a verb (an event)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
兄は大酒飲みだから、飲み始めたら最後、酔いつぶれるまで飲んでしまう。Ani wa oozakenomi da kara, nomihajimetara saigo, yoitsubureru made nonde shimau. My brother is a heavy drinker — once he starts, he keeps going until he passes out. मेरा भाई बहुत पीता है, एक बार पीना शुरू किया तो बस, बेहोश होने तक पीता रहता है। મારો ભાઈ બહુ પીએ છે, એક વાર પીવાનું શરૂ કર્યું તો બસ, બેભાન થાય ત્યાં સુધી પીતો રહે છે.
うちの娘はパソコンの前に座ったが最後、声をかけても返事もしない。Uchi no musume wa pasokon no mae ni suwatta ga saigo, koe o kakete mo henji mo shinai. Once my daughter sits down at the computer, that's it — she won't even answer when I call her. मेरी बेटी एक बार कंप्यूटर के सामने बैठ गई तो बस, आवाज़ दो तो जवाब तक नहीं देती। મારી દીકરી એક વાર કમ્પ્યુટર સામે બેઠી તો બસ, બોલાવીએ તો જવાબ પણ નથી આપતી.
彼にお金を持たせたら最後、何に使われるかわからない。Kare ni okane o motasetara saigo, nani ni tsukawareru ka wakaranai. Let him get his hands on money and there's no telling what it'll be spent on. उसके हाथ में पैसे दे दिए तो बस, पता नहीं किस चीज़ में उड़ा दे। તેના હાથમાં પૈસા આપ્યા તો બસ, ખબર નહીં શેમાં ઉડાવી દે.

最後 = "the end": once the trigger happens, there's no going back. The form is fixed — た形 + ら or た形 + が; ×なると最後, ×なれば最後 ([2] Q1). The second clause must be genuinely bad and irreversible: "he never forgives you", "you can't stop by willpower", "a rash all over my face". A mild or neutral result (forgets by the next day, makes a scary face) or advice/a wish ([2] Q3–4) doesn't fit.

【3】 〜ようでは

neutral register
Reading~you de wa
Meaning (EN)"if (you're) in such a (bad) state as ~, then (it'll turn out badly)" — book: 〜のような良くない状態では、良くない結果になるだろう (in an undesirable state like ~, the result will probably be bad)
Meaning (HI)अगर ... जैसी हालत है, तो (नतीजा बुरा ही होगा)
Meaning (GU)જો ... જેવી હાલત છે, તો (પરિણામ ખરાબ જ આવશે)
Connection (接続)普通形（ナ形 だ −な／−である・名 だ −である） + ようでは
RegisterNeutral; often used by superiors when scolding or warning.
Typical useAttaches to a sentence describing an undesirable fact (望ましくない事実). What follows is a guess/prediction that something undesirable will result (望ましくないことになるだろうという推測) — e.g. 〜てはいけない, 〜られない, 〜が心配だ, 〜だろう.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (普通形)V-plain + ようでは気にするようではki ni suru you de wa ／ 忘れるようではwasureru you de wa
い-adjectiveA-い + ようではミスが多いようではmisu ga ooi you de wa
な-adjectiveNa-な／Na-である + ようでは面倒なようではmendou na you de wa (added, not in book — taken from drill [3] Q2)
NounN + である + ようでは(added, not in book) こんな成績であるようではkonna seiseki de aru you de wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
小さな失敗をいちいち気にするようでは、この会社ではやっていけないよ。Chiisa na shippai o ichiichi ki ni suru you de wa, kono kaisha de wa yatte ikenai yo. If you fret over every little mistake, you won't survive at this company. अगर हर छोटी-सी ग़लती पर परेशान होते रहोगे, तो इस कंपनी में टिक नहीं पाओगे। જો દરેક નાની ભૂલ પર ચિંતા કરતા રહેશો, તો આ કંપનીમાં ટકી નહીં શકો.
ああ、僕は忘れっぽくて困る。こんなにすぐ忘れるようでは、この先のことが心配だ。Aa, boku wa wasureppokute komaru. Konna ni sugu wasureru you de wa, kono saki no koto ga shinpai da. Ugh, I'm so forgetful. If I forget things this fast, I worry about what lies ahead. उफ़, मैं बहुत भुलक्कड़ हूँ। अगर इतनी जल्दी भूल जाता हूँ, तो आगे की फ़िक्र होती है। ઉફ, હું બહુ ભુલકણો છું. જો આટલું જલદી ભૂલી જાઉં છું, તો આગળની ચિંતા થાય છે.
報告書にこんなにミスが多いようでは、安心して仕事を任せられない。Houkokusho ni konna ni misu ga ooi you de wa, anshin shite shigoto o makaserarenai. If your reports have this many mistakes, I can't entrust work to you with peace of mind. अगर रिपोर्ट में इतनी ग़लतियाँ हैं, तो मैं निश्चिंत होकर तुम्हें काम नहीं सौंप सकता। જો રિપોર્ટમાં આટલી ભૂલો છે, તો હું નિશ્ચિંત થઈને તને કામ સોંપી ન શકું.

You (or someone) are already in a bad state, and the speaker predicts a bad result: "at this rate…". The second clause is a prediction or judgment (やっていけない, 心配だ, 任せられない, 体を壊しますよ), not a command or advice — so しっかり勉強しなさい and 日本人に聞いたほうがいい fail in [3] Q4. Positive results (うらやましい, 充実している, 専門家になれますよ) contradict the meaning. Connection traps: ×遅刻しよう／×遅刻していよう — it is plain form + よう(では) ([3] Q1), and な-adjectives take な ([3] Q2: 面倒な).

【4】 〜なしに(は)・〜なしでは・〜なくして(は)

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; なくして(は) is literary)
Reading~nashi ni (wa) / ~nashi de wa / ~nakushite (wa)
Meaning (EN)"without ~, (it) cannot happen" — book: もし〜がなかったら、あることが成立しない (if there were no ~, something would not come about)
Meaning (HI)... के बिना (... संभव नहीं)
Meaning (GU)... વિના (... શક્ય નથી)
Connection (接続)名・動 辞書形 + こと + なしに(は)・なしでは・なくして(は)
Registerなしには／なしでは are neutral-formal; なくして(は) is literary and appears in speeches and essays.
Typical useShows the speaker's view that ~ is absolutely necessary (絶対に必要) for something to happen. It attaches to words for what the speaker considers essential, and is followed by a negative expression (否定表現: できない, 暮らせない, 解決しない, あり得ない).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + なしに(は)涙なしにnamida nashi ni
NounN + なしでは助けなしではtasuke nashi de wa
NounN + なくして(は)話し合いなくしてはhanashiai nakushite wa ／ ご指導なくしてgoshidou nakushite
VerbV-dictionary + こと + なしに(は)／なくして(は)確保することなしにはkakuho suru koto nashi ni wa
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns or V+こと only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
資金を確保することなしにはどんな計画も実行できない。Shikin o kakuho suru koto nashi ni wa donna keikaku mo jikkou dekinai. Without securing funds, no plan of any kind can be carried out. पूँजी का इंतज़ाम किए बिना कोई भी योजना लागू नहीं की जा सकती। મૂડીની વ્યવસ્થા કર્યા વિના કોઈ પણ યોજના અમલમાં મૂકી શકાય નહીં.
あのころのことは涙なしに語ることはできない。Ano koro no koto wa namida nashi ni kataru koto wa dekinai. I can't talk about those days without tears. उन दिनों की बात मैं आँसुओं के बिना नहीं कर सकता। એ દિવસોની વાત હું આંસુ વિના કરી શકતો નથી.
祖母はもう高齢で、周りの人たちの助けなしでは暮らせない。Sobo wa mou kourei de, mawari no hitotachi no tasuke nashi de wa kurasenai. My grandmother is very old now and can't live without the help of the people around her. दादी अब बहुत बुज़ुर्ग हैं, आसपास के लोगों की मदद के बिना वे रह नहीं सकतीं। દાદી હવે બહુ વૃદ્ધ છે, આસપાસના લોકોની મદદ વિના તેઓ રહી શકતાં નથી.
十分な話し合いなくしてはダム建設の問題は解決しないだろう。Juubun na hanashiai nakushite wa damu kensetsu no mondai wa kaiketsu shinai darou. Without thorough discussion, the dam-construction problem probably won't be solved. पूरी बातचीत के बिना बाँध-निर्माण की समस्या शायद हल नहीं होगी। પૂરતી ચર્ચા વિના બંધ-બાંધકામની સમસ્યા કદાચ ઉકેલાશે નહીં.
先生方のご指導なくしてわたしの大学合格はあり得ませんでした。Senseigata no goshidou nakushite watashi no daigaku goukaku wa ariemasen deshita. Without my teachers' guidance, I could never have got into university. शिक्षकों के मार्गदर्शन के बिना मेरा विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िला संभव ही नहीं था। શિક્ષકોના માર્ગદર્શન વિના મારો યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ શક્ય જ નહોતો.

"X なしには Y ない" = "X is indispensable for Y". It is the formal N1 version of the 復習 form 〜ないことには, and like it, it demands a negative ending — affirmative results (不満が多くなる), volition (わたしが説得しよう, 寄付をしてもらおう) or questions (いい発表ができるんですか) are all wrong in drill [4]. Remember that verbs need こと: 準備することなくしては, never ×準備するなくしては.

Rather than throw them out, give them to me. I'll wear them. वे कपड़े फेंक रहे हो? फेंकने से तो अच्छा है मुझे दे दो। मैं पहनूँगी। એ કપડાં ફેંકી દો છો? ફેંકવા કરતાં તો મને આપી દો. હું પહેરીશ.
途中でやめるくらいなら、初めからやらなければいいのに。Tochuu de yameru kurai nara, hajime kara yaranakereba ii noni. If you're going to quit halfway, you'd be better off not starting at all. बीच में छोड़ना ही है, तो शुरू ही न करते तो अच्छा था। વચ્ચે છોડી જ દેવું હોય, તો શરૂ જ ન કર્યું હોત તો સારું.

The first half is the worst option in the speaker's eyes (packed bus, wasting clothes, bowing to that man); the second half is the lesser evil chosen instead. Check that the second half really is an alternative to the first: in [5] Q3 the alternative to the air-conditioner is "put up with the heat", not a consequence of using it (電気代がかさむ, 部屋が涼しすぎる). And the first half is the dictionary form, so ×自由がなくなったくらいなら ([5] Q1).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Five N1 conditionals plus the N2 forms from the 〔復習〕 box. The mixed set [1〜5] makes you choose between them, and the deciding factor is almost always the type of second clause:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とあれば~to areba If it's (for) ~ 名・普通形 A special condition; 2nd clause = speaker's wish, intention or judgment. "For my kids I'd do anything": 子供のためとあれば.
〜たら最後・〜たが最後~tara saigo / ~ta ga saigo Once ~, it's all over V-た + ら／が最後 Trigger → very bad, irreversible result. Works for habits and hypotheses. Warning about a point of no return: 飲み始めたら最後.
〜ようでは~you de wa If (you're) like this, … 普通形（ナ形な／である・名である） Current bad state → prediction of a bad result. Not a command or advice. Scolding or warning: こんなにミスが多いようでは.
〜なしに(は)／なしでは／なくして(は)~nashi ni (wa) / ~nashi de wa / ~nakushite (wa) Without ~, (can't) 名・V辞書形＋こと ~ is indispensable; 2nd clause must be negative. Stating a prerequisite, thanking for support: ご指導なくして.
〜くらいなら~kurai nara Rather than ~, (I'd…) V-辞書形 ~ = the worst option; 2nd clause = the lesser evil (ほうがいい／ましだ／〜よう). Refusing one option for another: 満員のバスに乗るくらいなら歩く.
〜ないことには (N2)~nai koto ni wa Unless ~ V-ない／A-く／N・Naで + ないことには Like なしには, needs a negative after it; everyday register, attaches to a negative verb. Everyday "can't decide until I see it": 見てみないことには.
〜としたら／〜ものなら (N2)~to shitara / ~mono nara Supposing ~ / if (by any chance) ~ could 普通形 ／ V可能形 + ものなら Neutral hypotheticals; ものなら often voices a wish that seems impossible. Daydreams and wishes: やり直せるものならやり直したい.
Exam trap 1 — the negative-ending trio: 〜なしには／〜ないことには／〜ようでは all tend to end negatively, but for different reasons. なしには = "X is required"; ないことには = "until X happens"; ようでは = "you're already in a bad state". If the sentence criticises a current habit (毎日遅刻する, 子育てが大変なんて言う), it's ようでは ([3] Q1, [1〜5] Q5).
Exam trap 2 — consequence vs. alternative: after くらいなら you need what the speaker would do instead, not what would happen if they did the first thing. 電気代がかさむ／彼はいい気分になる are consequences and are always wrong ([5] Q3–4).
Exam trap 3 — how bad is "bad"? 〜たら最後 needs a dire, irreversible outcome (never forgives, can't stop, rash all over). A mild or temporary reaction (怖い顔をする, 次の日には忘れている) is a distractor ([2] Q2).
