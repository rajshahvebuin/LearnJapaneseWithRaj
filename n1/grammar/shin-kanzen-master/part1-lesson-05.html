1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜いかんだ

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ikan da (〜いかんで／〜いかんでは)
Meaning (EN)"depends on ~; is decided by ~" — book: 〜がどうであるかによって事態が変わる・事態が決まる (the situation changes / is decided by what ~ is like)
Meaning (HI)...पर निर्भर है / ...के अनुसार तय होगा
Meaning (GU)...પર આધાર રાખે છે / ...પ્રમાણે નક્કી થશે
Connection (接続)名 + いかんだ
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but it is a stiff, written-style word (いかん = 如何, "how").
Typical useAttaches to nouns whose meaning has a range of possible values (考え方・成績・態度, etc.). What follows says the outcome may change / will be decided. After 〜いかんでは, the sentence names one possible outcome among several (〜かもしれない, 〜だろう).

Noun (connective)N + いかんで面接の結果いかんでmensetsu no kekka ikan de
Noun (one possibility)N + いかんでは支持率いかんではshijiritsu ikan de wa
Noun + の(added, not in book) N + のいかんによって — also common成績のいかんによってseiseki no ikan ni yotte
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used directly — nominalise first (〜かどうか) or use a noun—

Depending on its approval rating, the current administration probably won't last long either. समर्थन-दर के हिसाब से, मौजूदा सरकार भी शायद ज़्यादा दिन नहीं चलेगी। સમર્થન-દરને આધારે, હાલની સરકાર પણ કદાચ લાંબું નહીં ટકે.

Think of いかん as a dial: the noun before it can take many settings (high/low score, good/bad result), and the outcome follows the dial. That's why a yes/no noun like 承諾 (consent) or 有無 (presence/absence) sounds wrong — drill [1] Q1. And because the outcome is still open, a completed past fact (商品が売れた, 選挙に負けた) can't follow いかんで — drill [1] Q3–4. いかんでは picks out one (often bad) possibility: 中止しなければならないかもしれない.

【2】 〜いかんにかかわらず・〜いかんによらず・〜いかんを問わず

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~ikan ni kakawarazu / ~ikan ni yorazu / ~ikan o towazu
Meaning (EN)"regardless of ~; irrespective of ~" — book: あることの成立に〜は関係ない・影響はない (~ has no bearing on / no effect on whether something holds)
Meaning (HI)...चाहे जो हो / ...की परवाह किए बिना (इससे कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता)
Meaning (GU)...ગમે તે હોય / ...ને ધ્યાનમાં લીધા વિના (તેનાથી કોઈ ફરક નથી પડતો)
Connection (接続)名(-の) + いかんにかかわらず・いかんによらず
名-の + いかんを問わず
Register硬 — formal, written: notices, rules, company policies, terms and conditions.
Typical useAttaches to nouns with a range of possible values. What follows is a statement that is not affected by ~ — typically a rule, policy or obligation.

Whatever the result of tomorrow's match, it's already settled that we can't win the championship. कल के मैच का नतीजा चाहे जो हो, यह तय हो चुका है कि हम ख़िताब नहीं जीत सकते। કાલની મૅચનું પરિણામ ગમે તે હોય, એ નક્કી થઈ ગયું છે કે અમે ખિતાબ નહીં જીતી શકીએ.
当社は学歴・年齢・過去の実績のいかんを問わず、初任給は一律です。Tousha wa gakureki・nenrei・kako no jisseki no ikan o towazu, shoninkyuu wa ichiritsu desu. At our company, the starting salary is the same for everyone, regardless of education, age or past achievements. हमारी कंपनी में शिक्षा, उम्र या पिछली उपलब्धियाँ चाहे जो हों, शुरुआती वेतन सबके लिए एक समान है। અમારી કંપનીમાં શિક્ષણ, ઉંમર કે અગાઉની સિદ્ધિઓ ગમે તે હોય, શરૂઆતનો પગાર બધા માટે એકસરખો છે.

The negative twin of 【1】: いかんで = "the outcome follows the dial"; いかんにかかわらず = "the outcome ignores the dial". Connection trap: の is optional with にかかわらず／によらず but required with を問わず (〜のいかんを問わず). What follows must be a general rule or fixed outcome (全員に参加賞が与えられる, 早く返すべきだ) — a one-off observation like みんな上手だった doesn't show "not being affected" (drill [2] Q3–4). For yes/no nouns use plain 〜を問わず: 男女を問わず, 国内外を問わず.

Meaning (EN)"undaunted by ~; in defiance of ~" — book: 普通なら〜という障害に気持ちが負けてしまうのに、それを乗り越えて行動する (normally one's spirit would give in to the obstacle ~, but one overcomes it and acts)

Meaning (GU)...ની પરવા કર્યા વિના / ...થી હાર માન્યા વિના (હિંમતથી)

Typical useAttaches to words for a situation that would be a big obstacle (台風・病気・危険, etc.). The whole sentence praises someone's courage. Not used about the speaker themself.

Literally "not treating ~ as anything". Two checks: the noun must be a real obstacle (プレッシャー, 不況 — not 味方の選手, 商品), and the second half must show the person overcoming it through positive action (指導に励んでいる, 新しい仕事に取り組んでいる). "Refusing to go to hospital" or "putting up with it" is not overcoming — drill [3] Q3. Since it's praise, わたしは〜をものともせず sounds self-congratulatory and is avoided.

【4】 〜をよそに

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~o yoso ni
Meaning (EN)"paying no heed to ~; in disregard of ~" — book: 〜は自分とは無関係であるかのように、少しも気にしないで行動する (acting as if ~ had nothing to do with oneself, without minding it at all)
Meaning (HI)...को अनदेखा करके / ...की ज़रा भी परवाह किए बिना
Meaning (GU)...ને અવગણીને / ...ની જરા પણ પરવા કર્યા વિના
Connection (接続)名 + をよそに
RegisterNeutral, slightly literary.
Typical useAttaches to words describing the surrounding situation — usually other people's feelings or reactions (心配, 抗議, 不安, 期待). What follows is an action that ignores that situation. The subject is mainly a person, never the speaker. The sentence as a whole expresses exasperation (あきれた) or sometimes wonder (感嘆).

In disregard of the residents' protests, the dam construction plan is being pushed ahead. निवासियों के विरोध-प्रदर्शनों को अनदेखा करते हुए, बाँध निर्माण की योजना आगे बढ़ाई जा रही है। રહેવાસીઓના વિરોધ-પ્રદર્શનોને અવગણીને, બંધ બાંધવાની યોજના આગળ વધારાઈ રહી છે.
彼は周囲の人たちの不安をよそに、再び戦地の取材に出発していった。Kare wa shuui no hitotachi no fuan o yoso ni, futatabi senchi no shuzai ni shuppatsu shite itta. Ignoring the anxiety of those around him, he set off once again to report from the war zone. आसपास के लोगों की घबराहट की परवाह किए बिना, वह फिर से युद्ध-क्षेत्र की रिपोर्टिंग के लिए निकल पड़ा। આસપાસના લોકોની ચિંતાની પરવા કર્યા વિના, તે ફરીથી યુદ્ધ-ક્ષેત્રનું રિપોર્ટિંગ કરવા નીકળી પડ્યો.

よそ = "somewhere else, someone else's business": the subject treats the surrounding feeling as none of their concern. Compare 【3】: 足のけが is an obstacle (→ をものともせず), 周囲の反対／親の心配 is other people's reaction (→ をよそに) — drill [4] Q1–2. The action after it must actually go against the situation: 国民の期待をよそに "the new government just carried on with the old policies" (drill [4] Q3).

Structure: [extreme case A] ならいざしらず, [ordinary/opposite case B] + complaint. A and B must be the same kind of thing at opposite ends: 小説家 vs 普通の人, 5歳の子供 vs 大人, 大統領 vs 普通の人間, 世界一周 vs 日帰り (drill [5]). If both ends are on the same side (6歳の子, 3歳の子 vs 5歳の子) the contrast collapses. Verbs and adjectives need の before なら.

Why: 学者 (extreme case: people who research for a living) vs 普通の人 (the ordinary case) + なんてできない (dismissive) → ならいざしらず. a) and b) treat 学者 as a variable with a range of values, which it isn't.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two "relation" forms (does ~ matter or not?) and three "ignoring ~" forms. The 〔復習〕 N2 forms are included where they are near-synonyms:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜いかんだ・いかんで~ikan da / ikan de Depends on ~ N + いかん Stiff version of 〜によって／〜次第だ. Noun must have a range of values; outcome still open. Formal statements: 結果いかんで決まる.
〜いかんでは~ikan de wa Depending on ~, (possibly) X N + いかんでは Picks out one possible outcome (〜かもしれない). Often a bad one. Warning of a possibility: 人数いかんでは中止.
〜いかんにかかわらず／によらず／を問わず~ikan ni kakawarazu / ni yorazu / o towazu Regardless of ~ N(の) + にかかわらず／によらず; Nの + を問わず 硬. Followed by a fixed rule/outcome. を問わず needs の. Notices, policies: 理由のいかんによらず返金不可.
〜にかかわらず／〜を問わず (N2)~ni kakawarazu / ~o towazu Regardless of ~ N + にかかわらず／を問わず Plain versions; fine with yes/no pairs (男女を問わず, 天候にかかわらず). Everyday rules; when the noun is a two-way choice.
〜をものともせず(に)~o mono to mo sezu (ni) Undaunted by ~ N + をものともせず Noun = obstacle (illness, danger, pressure). Praise. Not about oneself. Admiring someone's courage.
〜をよそに~o yoso ni Paying no heed to ~ N + をよそに Noun = others' reaction/surroundings (心配, 抗議, 期待). Exasperation (sometimes wonder). Not about oneself. Criticising someone who ignores others' feelings.
〜もかまわず (N2)~mo kamawazu Without caring about ~ N／V-の + もかまわず Everyday cousin of をよそに; often about manners (人目もかまわず). Describing rude or careless behaviour.
〜ならいざしらず~nara iza shirazu ~ would be one thing, but… N／普通形(の) + ならいざしらず Extreme case A vs opposite ordinary case B + complaint. A and B must be the same kind of thing. Complaining that B doesn't deserve the treatment A would.
Exam trap 1 — the いかん noun test: ask "can this noun take many values?" 結果・成績・理由・学歴・内容・金額 → yes. 男女・有無・承諾・不当な理由 → no (either already fixed, or just two choices). Distractors in [1] Q1 and [2] Q1–2 are all built on this.
Exam trap 2 — praise or exasperation? Both をものともせず and をよそに can follow 周囲の反対. Read the second half: admirable action (実験を続けている, 指導に励んでいる) → をものともせず; behaviour the speaker disapproves of (ぶらぶらしている, また出かけた) → をよそに ([1〜5] Q2 vs Q3).
Exam trap 3 — no first person: をものともせず and をよそに both describe someone else; "わたしは〜をよそに…" sounds odd. With ならいざしらず, check the two ends really are opposites (5歳 vs 大人, not 5歳 vs 6歳).
