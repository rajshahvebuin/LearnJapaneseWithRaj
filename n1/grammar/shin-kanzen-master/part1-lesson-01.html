【1】 〜が早いか

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; narrative feel)
Reading~ga hayai ka
Meaning (EN)"no sooner had ~ than…; the instant ~" — book: 〜するとすぐ続いて次のことが起こる (right after ~, the next thing follows)
Meaning (HI)...करते ही (तुरंत) — ...हुआ ही था कि...
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ (તરત) — ...થયું જ હતું કે...
Connection (接続)動 辞書形／た形 + が早いか
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but it is a written/narrative pattern — rarely heard in casual talk.
Typical useAttaches to verbs of instantaneous action. The second clause states a fact with a slight sense of surprise (意外感). It cannot be followed by the speaker's wish/intention (〜たい・〜よう) or by a request/command (〜てください・〜なさい).

今朝、寝坊した夫は、朝ご飯を口に押し込むが早いか、玄関を出ていった。Kesa, nebou shita otto wa, asagohan o kuchi ni oshikomu ga hayai ka, genkan o dete itta. This morning my husband, who had overslept, crammed breakfast into his mouth and was out the door the very next instant. आज सुबह देर से उठे मेरे पति नाश्ता मुँह में ठूँसते ही दरवाज़े से बाहर निकल गए। આજે સવારે મોડા ઊઠેલા મારા પતિ નાસ્તો મોંમાં ઠૂંસતાં જ દરવાજેથી બહાર નીકળી ગયા.
話題のその本は、店頭に並べられたが早いか、飛ぶように売れていった。Wadai no sono hon wa, tentou ni naraberareta ga hayai ka, tobu you ni urete itta. That much-talked-about book sold like hotcakes the moment it was put on display in stores. वह चर्चित किताब दुकानों में रखी जाते ही हाथों-हाथ बिक गई। તે ચર્ચિત પુસ્તક દુકાનોમાં મુકાતાં જ હાથોહાથ વેચાઈ ગયું.

Literally "whether [doing X] is early" — the second action is so quick it races the first. Note that example ② is a habitual fact (いつも…てしまう) and still fine: what's banned is not the present tense but a wish, intention or request in the second clause. That restriction is exactly what drill [1] Q2 and Q3 test.

【2】 〜や・〜や否や

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ya / ~ya ina ya
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~; the moment ~" — book: 〜とほとんど同時に次のことが起こる (the next thing happens almost simultaneously with ~)
Meaning (HI)...होते ही / ...करते ही (लगभग एक ही समय पर)
Meaning (GU)...થતાં જ / ...કરતાં જ (લગભગ એક જ સમયે)
Connection (接続)動 辞書形 + や・や否や
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but stiff and literary (否 = "or not": "whether it happened or not…").
Typical useInstantaneous verbs only. The second clause is a fact; no wish/intention, no request/command — same restriction as 〜が早いか.

選挙戦が始まるや否や、あちこちからにぎやかな声が聞こえてきた。Senkyosen ga hajimaru ya ina ya, achikochi kara nigiyaka na koe ga kikoete kita. As soon as the election campaign kicked off, lively voices could be heard from all over. चुनाव प्रचार शुरू होते ही, चारों ओर से शोर-शराबे की आवाज़ें सुनाई देने लगीं। ચૂંટણી પ્રચાર શરૂ થતાં જ, ચારે બાજુથી ઘોંઘાટભર્યા અવાજો સંભળાવા લાગ્યા.
この病気の新しい治療法が発表されるや否や、全国の病院から問い合わせが殺到した。Kono byouki no atarashii chiryouhou ga happyou sareru ya ina ya, zenkoku no byouin kara toiawase ga sattou shita. The instant a new treatment for this disease was announced, inquiries poured in from hospitals across the country. इस बीमारी के नए इलाज की घोषणा होते ही, देश भर के अस्पतालों से पूछताछ की बाढ़ आ गई। આ રોગની નવી સારવારની જાહેરાત થતાં જ, દેશભરની હોસ્પિટલોમાંથી પૂછપરછનો ધસારો થયો.

や and や否や mean the same; や否や is slightly heavier. Unlike 〜が早いか (dictionary or た形), this one takes the dictionary form only, even when the whole sentence is in the past (始まるや否や…聞こえてきた). The book's note for や否や drops the "slight surprise" remark it gives for が早いか — the second clause is simply a fact, and the two clauses may have different subjects.

Think of it as "he did A and — whoa — immediately did B." Because it narrates someone else's surprising behaviour, わたし／わたしたち as subject sounds odd, and a sentence whose second clause has a different subject (物音がするなり…, だれかが叫ぶなり…) breaks the same-subject rule — drill [3] tests exactly this. Don't mix it up with 〜なり〜なり ("either…or", 4課) or Nなりに ("in one's own way", 14課).

Reading~soba kara
Meaning (EN)"no sooner do I ~ than (it's undone again) — over and over" — book: 〜をしても、すぐにそれに対抗するような動きがあり、それを何度も繰り返す (you do ~, something immediately counters it, and this repeats again and again)

月末になると、払ったそばからまた別の請求書が来る。Getsumatsu ni naru to, haratta soba kara mata betsu no seikyuusho ga kuru. At the end of the month, no sooner have I paid one bill than another arrives. महीने के आख़िर में, एक बिल चुकाते ही दूसरा बिल आ जाता है। મહિનાના અંતે, એક બિલ ભરતાં જ બીજું બિલ આવી જાય છે.

そば = "right beside": the counter-movement happens right next to your action. The key idea is repetition — every example is a cycle (次々に, また). A one-off event ("the moment the siren rang, the fire engines left") is wrong for そばから; use や否や／が早いか there. The first-clause verb is usually something done again and again (書く, 調べる, 払う, 片付ける).

【5】 〜てからというもの(は)

neutral register
Reading~te kara to iu mono (wa)
Meaning (EN)"ever since ~ (things have been different)" — book: 〜してからある変化が起こり、その後ずっと同じ状態が続いている (after ~ a change happened, and the same state has continued ever since)
Meaning (HI)...के बाद से (तब से लगातार...)
Meaning (GU)...પછીથી (ત્યારથી સતત...)
Connection (接続)動 て形 + からというもの(は)
RegisterNeutral, slightly emotional/reflective — stronger than plain 〜て以来.
Typical useNot used for a period starting in the very recent past. The second clause must describe the continuing state after the change (often 〜ている／〜なった／〜日はない) — not a single completed event.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Five "right after" forms plus 〜にあって. The mixed set [1〜6] is built to make you choose between them, so the differences are all about what the second clause is allowed to be:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜が早いか~ga hayai ka No sooner ~ than… V-辞書形／た形 One-off sequence; 2nd clause = fact with slight surprise. No wish/intention/request. Narrating something that happened (or habitually happens) the very instant after.
〜や・〜や否や~ya / ~ya ina ya The moment ~ V-辞書形 only Almost simultaneous; 2nd clause = fact. Subjects may differ (サイレンが鳴るや、消防車が…). Formal narrative, news-style writing; trigger + immediate result.
〜なり~nari As soon as (he) did ~, (he) did… V-辞書形 Same subject, usually third person; 2nd clause = surprising volitional action. Describing someone's abrupt behaviour (入ってくるなりどなった).
〜そばから~soba kara No sooner ~ than it's undone — again and again V-辞書形／た形 Repetition; the second event counters the first. Usually negative. Complaining about a never-ending cycle (片付けるそばから散らかす).
〜てからというもの(は)~te kara to iu mono (wa) Ever since ~ V-て形 (+ 期間N というもの) Change → long-lasting state (〜ている／〜なった). Not for the very recent past. Reflecting on how life has been different since a turning point.
〜にあって~ni atte In (such a special situation) N (situation/position/period) + にあって(も) 硬. Not a time-sequence form; noun must be a situation, not a person. 〜にあっても = contrary result. Formal writing about circumstances (病床にあって, 不況下にあっても).
〜たとたん／〜たかと思うと (N2)~ta totan / ~ta ka to omou to Just as ~ / no sooner ~ than V-た Everyday N2 equivalents from the 復習 box; less restricted, and both take た形. Conversation, or when you're unsure of the N1 forms' restrictions.
Exam trap 1 — the banned second clause: after 〜が早いか, 〜や(否や) and 〜なり you cannot put 〜たい／〜てほしい／〜よう／〜てください／〜なさい. If the answer choices include a wish, invitation or command, eliminate it first ([1] Q2–3, [2] Q2).
Exam trap 2 — subject check for なり: 〜なり needs the same (usually third-person) subject in both halves. When the two clauses have different subjects (物音がする／だれかが叫ぶ … みんなが逃げた), switch to 〜や否や or 〜が早いか ([3] Q1–2).
Exam trap 3 — one-off vs. repeated vs. lasting: one instant → が早いか／や否や／なり; a repeating cycle (また, 次々に, どんどん) → そばから; a lasting state (〜ている, 夢中になっている) → てからというもの. Read the end of the sentence before you look at the options ([1〜6] Q1, Q3, Q4).
