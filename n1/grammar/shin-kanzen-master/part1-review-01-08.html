Notes
Second cumulative test: 15 items, 4 options (1・2・3・4), 問題1 format (文法形式の判断 — fill the blank). The weight shifts to the newer lessons: 8課 (3 items + its 〔復習〕 form ものの), 5課 (4 items) and 6課 (3 items). No item tests 4課. Several distractors are non-existent near-forms (だけではの, どこからともいえず, ならいがある) or the right form with the wrong partner word (はずのところを vs べきところを) — knowing the exact fixed shape is the skill being tested.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

次の文の（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no (___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — "Choose the best option (1–4) for the blank." 15 items, p.40–41. All answers confirmed from 別冊 p.2 (PDF page 194).

Q1

駅には時間通りに着いた（　）、駅からの道がわからず、会場に到着するまで時間がかかってしまった。Eki ni wa jikan doori ni tsuita (___), eki kara no michi ga wakarazu, kaijou ni touchaku suru made jikan ga kakatte shimatta.

Full sentence: 駅には時間通りに着いたものの、駅からの道がわからず、会場に到着するまで時間がかかってしまった。Eki ni wa jikan doori ni tsuita mono no, eki kara no michi ga wakarazu, kaijou ni touchaku suru made jikan ga kakatte shimatta.
EN: I got to the station on time, but I couldn't find the way from there, so it took me a long time to reach the venue.
HI: स्टेशन तो समय पर पहुँच गया, पर वहाँ से रास्ता समझ नहीं आया, इसलिए कार्यक्रम-स्थल तक पहुँचने में काफ़ी समय लग गया।
GU: સ્ટેશને તો સમયસર પહોંચી ગયો, પણ ત્યાંથી રસ્તો સમજાયો નહીં, એટલે કાર્યક્રમના સ્થળે પહોંચવામાં ઘણો સમય લાગી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1もののmono noalthough (I did)...तो किया, पर...તો કર્યું, પણ
2ものをmono oif only ~ (regret)काश ... (अफ़सोस)કાશ ... (અફસોસ)
3ものでmono debecause (excuse)क्योंकि (सफ़ाई)કારણ કે (બહાનું)
4ものでもmono de moeven a thing (not a conjunction)कोई चीज़ भीકોઈ વસ્તુ પણ

Why: Two real facts in contrast — "I did arrive on time, but then…" — is 〜ものの, the N2 form in 8課's 〔復習〕 box. 2) 〜ものを (8課) needs a counter-to-fact supposition (〜ば…ものを); 着いた really happened. 3) 〜もので gives a reason/excuse ("because I arrived on time…"), which doesn't lead to getting lost. 4) ものでも doesn't work as a clause connector here.

Q2

この店にはスプーン（　）大型家具に至るまで、生活用品は何でもそろっている。Kono mise ni wa supuun (___) oogata kagu ni itaru made, seikatsu youhin wa nan de mo sorotte iru.

Full sentence: この店にはスプーンから大型家具に至るまで、生活用品は何でもそろっている。Kono mise ni wa supuun kara oogata kagu ni itaru made, seikatsu youhin wa nan de mo sorotte iru.
EN: This shop stocks every household item you can think of, from spoons right up to large furniture.
HI: इस दुकान में चम्मच से लेकर बड़े फ़र्नीचर तक, घर की हर ज़रूरी चीज़ मिलती है।
GU: આ દુકાનમાં ચમચીથી માંડીને મોટા ફર્નિચર સુધી, ઘરવપરાશની દરેક વસ્તુ મળે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1からkarafromसे (लेकर)થી (માંડીને)
2からしてkara shiteto begin with / even (judging from)...से ही (शुरुआत से ही)...થી જ (શરૂઆતથી જ)
3を皮切りにo kawakiri nistarting with (and then expanding)...से शुरुआत करके...થી શરૂઆત કરીને
4をはじめにo hajime nibeginning with (non-standard; usually をはじめ)...से शुरू करके...થી શરૂ કરીને

Why: 〜に至るまで (2課) marks the far end of a range, and the near end takes plain から: AからBに至るまで "from A all the way to B". 2) からして picks one example to criticise ("even the spoons are…") and isn't paired with に至るまで. 3) を皮切りに (2課) is for a sequence of events that starts somewhere and spreads (東京を皮切りに全国で公演), not for a range of items. 4) The set phrase is 〜をはじめ(として); をはじめに doesn't form a range with に至るまで.

Q3

仕事（　）、徹夜続きでは体に悪いですよ。Shigoto (___), tetsuya tsuzuki de wa karada ni warui desu yo.

Full sentence: 仕事とはいえ、徹夜続きでは体に悪いですよ。Shigoto to wa ie, tetsuya tsuzuki de wa karada ni warui desu yo.
EN: It may be for work, but staying up all night, night after night, is bad for your health.
HI: माना कि काम है, फिर भी लगातार रात-रात भर जागना सेहत के लिए ख़राब है।
GU: માન્યું કે કામ છે, છતાં સતત રાતોની રાત જાગવું તબિયત માટે ખરાબ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1といってto ittesaying ~ (and)...कहकर...કહીને
2とはいえto wa iegranted ~, stillमाना कि ..., फिर भीમાન્યું કે ..., છતાં
3といったらto ittarawhen it comes to ~ (emphatic)...की तो बात ही क्या...ની તો વાત જ શી
4といえばto iebaspeaking of ~...की बात करें तो...ની વાત કરીએ તો

Why: A conceded fact (it's work) + the speaker's evaluation against it (体に悪い) = 〜とはいえ (8課). 1) といって just reports speech ("saying 'it's work', …"). 3) といったら introduces a topic with strong feeling (仕事の大変さといったら…). 4) といえば changes the topic by association ("speaking of work…"). Neither 3 nor 4 creates the "but" this sentence needs.

Q4

山川さんは運転席に（　）、勢いよくエンジンをかけた。Yamakawa-san wa untenseki ni (___), ikioi yoku enjin o kaketa.

Full sentence: 山川さんは運転席に座るなり、勢いよくエンジンをかけた。Yamakawa-san wa untenseki ni suwaru nari, ikioi yoku enjin o kaketa.
EN: The moment Mr. Yamakawa sat in the driver's seat, he started the engine with a roar.
HI: यामाकावा जी ड्राइवर की सीट पर बैठते ही ज़ोर से इंजन चालू कर दिया।
GU: યામાકાવાજીએ ડ્રાઇવરની સીટ પર બેસતાં જ જોરથી એન્જિન ચાલુ કરી દીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1座った上でsuwatta ue deafter sitting (and on that basis)बैठने के बाद (उसके आधार पर)બેઠા પછી (તેના આધારે)
2座らんばかりにsuwaran bakari nias if about to sitमानो बैठने ही वाले होंજાણે બેસવાના જ હોય
3座りがてらsuwari gaterawhile sitting, also (incidental)बैठने के साथ-साथબેસવાની સાથે સાથે
4座るなりsuwaru narithe moment he satबैठते हीબેસતાં જ

Why: Third-person subject, same subject in both clauses, a sudden volitional action straight after → 〜なり (1課). 1) 〜た上で means "after doing X properly (and then deciding)", with no sense of immediacy — clashes with 勢いよく. 2) 〜んばかりに (6課) means "as if about to ~" and describes a manner, but he actually sat. 3) 〜がてら (7課) is "while doing X, also do Y", used with outings like 散歩がてら, not with sitting.

Q5

頼み方（　）相手の気持ちが変わると思う。Tanomikata (___) aite no kimochi ga kawaru to omou.

Full sentence: 頼み方いかんで相手の気持ちが変わると思う。Tanomikata ikan de aite no kimochi ga kawaru to omou.
EN: I think the other person's feelings will change depending on how you ask.
HI: मुझे लगता है कि आप किस ढंग से अनुरोध करते हैं, उसी पर सामने वाले की भावना बदलती है।
GU: મને લાગે છે કે તમે કઈ રીતે વિનંતી કરો છો, તેના પર જ સામેની વ્યક્તિની લાગણી બદલાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にしたがってni shitagatteas ~ (proportionally) / following...के साथ-साथ / ...के अनुसार...ની સાથે સાથે / ...પ્રમાણે
2に伴ってni tomonattealong with (a change)...के साथ (बदलाव)...ની સાથે (ફેરફાર)
3かぎりでkagiri deas far as / within the limit of...की सीमा तक...ની હદ સુધી
4いかんでikan dedepending on...पर निर्भर करके...પર આધાર રાખીને

Why: N + いかんで／いかんだ (5課) = "depending on (the content/quality of) N", the formal version of 〜次第で. 頼み方 (way of asking) is exactly the kind of variable noun it takes. 1) and 2) express a gradual change that runs in step with another change (年をとるにしたがって／に伴って), not "is decided by". 3) かぎりで doesn't attach to 頼み方 in this sense.

Q6

魚が豊富なこの地方（　）この料理。大いに楽しみたい。Sakana ga houfu na kono chihou (___) kono ryouri. Ooi ni tanoshimitai.

Full sentence: 魚が豊富なこの地方ならではのこの料理。大いに楽しみたい。Sakana ga houfu na kono chihou nara de wa no kono ryouri. Ooi ni tanoshimitai.
EN: This dish is something only this fish-rich region can offer. I want to enjoy it to the full.
HI: मछलियों से भरपूर इस इलाक़े की यह ख़ास डिश — जो सिर्फ़ यहीं मिलती है। मैं इसका भरपूर आनंद लेना चाहता हूँ।
GU: માછલીથી સમૃદ્ધ આ પ્રદેશની આ ખાસ વાનગી — જે ફક્ત અહીં જ મળે. મારે તેનો ભરપૂર આનંદ માણવો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1だけではのdake de wa no(non-existent form)(ऐसा रूप नहीं होता)(આવું રૂપ હોતું નથી)
2ならではのnara de wa nounique to / only (this place) can offerकेवल ...में ही मिलने वालाફક્ત ...માં જ મળતું
3ながらのnagara no(unchanged) as it has always beenपहले जैसा ही (बिना बदले)પહેલાં જેવું જ (બદલાયા વગર)
4に限ってのni kagitte nolimited toकेवल ...तक सीमितમાત્ર ...પૂરતું મર્યાદિત

Why: N + ならではの + N (3課) = "the N that only N could produce" — praise for something unique to a place or person. 1) is not a real form. 3) 〜ながらの attaches to a few fixed words (昔ながらの, 生まれながらの) meaning "unchanged since…", not to a region. 4) に限っての means "restricted to", which loses the praise and sounds odd before この料理.

Q7

山中さんは日本語の教師をする（　）小説を書いているそうだ。Yamanaka-san wa Nihongo no kyoushi o suru (___) shousetsu o kaite iru sou da.

Full sentence: 山中さんは日本語の教師をするかたわら小説を書いているそうだ。Yamanaka-san wa Nihongo no kyoushi o suru katawara shousetsu o kaite iru sou da.
EN: I hear that Ms. Yamanaka writes novels alongside her work as a Japanese teacher.
HI: सुना है कि यामानाका जी जापानी भाषा की शिक्षिका का काम करने के साथ-साथ उपन्यास भी लिखती हैं।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે યામાનાકાજી જાપાની ભાષાનાં શિક્ષિકાનું કામ કરવાની સાથે સાથે નવલકથાઓ પણ લખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かたわらkatawaraalongside (a main occupation)...के साथ-साथ (मुख्य काम के अलावा)...ની સાથે સાથે (મુખ્ય કામ ઉપરાંત)
2ついでにtsuide niwhile (one is at it)...के बहाने / साथ में...ના બહાને / સાથે સાથે
3がてらgaterawhile (going out), also...के साथ-साथ (घूमते हुए)...ની સાથે સાથે (ફરતાં ફરતાં)

Why: 〜かたわら (7課) = doing something else in parallel with one's main job/role over a long period — teacher by profession, novelist on the side. 2) ついでに and 3) がてら (7課) are for a one-off occasion ("while I'm out shopping, I'll…"), usually with movement; がてら also takes a noun or ます-stem, not 教師をする. 4) そばで is physical location ("beside teaching").

Q8

値段のいかん（　）、物をもらったら必ず感謝の気持ちを伝えるべきだ。Nedan no ikan (___), mono o morattara kanarazu kansha no kimochi o tsutaeru beki da.

Full sentence: 値段のいかんにかかわらず、物をもらったら必ず感謝の気持ちを伝えるべきだ。Nedan no ikan ni kakawarazu, mono o morattara kanarazu kansha no kimochi o tsutaeru beki da.
EN: Regardless of the price, when you receive something you should always express your gratitude.
HI: क़ीमत चाहे जो भी हो, कोई चीज़ मिलने पर हमेशा आभार ज़रूर जताना चाहिए।
GU: કિંમત ગમે તે હોય, કોઈ વસ્તુ મળે ત્યારે હંમેશાં આભાર જરૂર વ્યક્ત કરવો જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1もかまわずmo kamawazuwithout caring about...की परवाह किए बिना...ની પરવા કર્યા વગર
2はともかくwa tomokakusetting ~ aside...को छोड़ दें तो...ને બાજુએ રાખીએ તો
3にもかかわらずni mo kakawarazudespite...के बावजूद...હોવા છતાં
4にかかわらずni kakawarazuregardless of...चाहे जो हो...ગમે તે હોય

Why: The fixed set is N のいかんにかかわらず／いかんによらず／いかんを問わず (5課): "whatever N turns out to be". 3) にもかかわらず ("despite") needs a known fact (雨にもかかわらず); いかん is an open variable, so the extra も is the classic trap. 1) 〜もかまわず (人目もかまわず) means acting without minding something, not "regardless of the price". 2) はともかく ("price aside") doesn't combine with いかん.

Q9

長期入院中（　）、わたしにも選挙権があるのだ。Chouki nyuuinchuu (___), watashi ni mo senkyoken ga aru no da.

Full sentence: 長期入院中といえども、わたしにも選挙権があるのだ。Chouki nyuuinchuu to iedomo, watashi ni mo senkyoken ga aru no da.
EN: Even though I'm in hospital long-term, I too have the right to vote.
HI: भले ही मैं लंबे समय से अस्पताल में भर्ती हूँ, मुझे भी वोट देने का अधिकार है।
GU: ભલે હું લાંબા સમયથી હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ છું, મને પણ મત આપવાનો અધિકાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1だからといってda kara to ittejust because ~ (doesn't mean)सिर्फ़ इसलिए कि ... (इसका मतलब नहीं)ફક્ત એટલે કે ... (એનો અર્થ નથી)
2にかかわらずni kakawarazuregardless of...चाहे जो हो...ગમે તે હોય
3と思いきやto omoiki yaI thought ~, butसोचा था ..., परધાર્યું હતું ..., પણ

Why: A fact that suggests an expectation (a long-term patient → can't take part), overturned by the speaker's firm assertion (〜のだ) → 〜といえども (8課). 1) だからといって is followed by a negative conclusion (〜わけではない), not by a right being asserted. 2) にかかわらず needs a noun of variation or a pair (天候にかかわらず, 経験の有無にかかわらず). 3) と思いきや needs an expectation that proved wrong; 入院中 is a fact.

Q10

先生にはもっと早くお礼を（　）、今ごろになってしまって申し訳ありません。Sensei ni wa motto hayaku orei o (___), imagoro ni natte shimatte moushiwake arimasen.

Full sentence: 先生にはもっと早くお礼を言うべきところを、今ごろになってしまって申し訳ありません。Sensei ni wa motto hayaku orei o iu beki tokoro o, imagoro ni natte shimatte moushiwake arimasen.
EN: I ought to have thanked you much sooner, Professor — I'm very sorry it has taken me until now.
HI: गुरुजी, मुझे आपका धन्यवाद बहुत पहले करना चाहिए था, इतनी देर हो जाने के लिए क्षमा चाहता हूँ।
GU: ગુરુજી, મારે આપનો આભાર ઘણો વહેલો માનવો જોઈતો હતો, આટલું મોડું થઈ જવા બદલ માફી માગું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1言うはずのところをiu hazu no tokoro owhen I was supposed (expected) to sayजब कहना अपेक्षित थाજ્યારે કહેવું અપેક્ષિત હતું
2言うべきところをiu beki tokoro owhen I ought to have saidजब मुझे कहना चाहिए थाજ્યારે મારે કહેવું જોઈતું હતું
3言うはずのことでiu hazu no koto dewith the thing I was supposed to sayजो कहना था उस बात सेજે કહેવાનું હતું તે વાતથી
4言うべきものでiu beki mono debeing something one should sayकहने लायक़ चीज़ होने सेકહેવા લાયક વસ્તુ હોવાથી

Why: This is the book's 8課【1】③ pattern, 〜べきところを + apology: "at a time when I should have…". 1) はず expresses the speaker's expectation, not duty, so it doesn't express a polite self-reproach. 3) and 4) don't form the ところを courtesy pattern at all — ことで／もので just give a reason and don't connect to 今ごろになってしまって.

Q11

佐藤君は足のけがを（　）、20キロもの距離を最後まで走り抜いた。Satou-kun wa ashi no kega o (___), nijikkiro mo no kyori o saigo made hashirinuita.

Full sentence: 佐藤君は足のけがをものともせずに、20キロもの距離を最後まで走り抜いた。Satou-kun wa ashi no kega o mono to mo sezu ni, nijikkiro mo no kyori o saigo made hashirinuita.
EN: Undaunted by his leg injury, Sato ran the full 20 kilometres right to the finish.
HI: पैर की चोट की परवाह किए बिना, सातो ने पूरे 20 किलोमीटर अंत तक दौड़ पूरी की।
GU: પગની ઈજાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, સાતોએ આખા 20 કિલોમીટર છેક સુધી દોડ પૂરી કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1問わずtowazuregardless of...की परवाह न करके (चाहे जो हो)...ગમે તે હોય
2よそにyoso niignoring (others' concerns)(दूसरों की चिंता को) अनदेखा करके(બીજાની ચિંતાને) અવગણીને
3おいてoiteexcept / other than...के सिवा...સિવાય
4ものともせずにmono to mo sezu niundaunted by, defying...की परवाह किए बिना (डटकर)...ની પરવા કર્યા વગર (હિંમતથી)

Why: 〜をものともせず(に) (5課) praises someone for overcoming a hardship (injury, storm, opposition) through their own strength — perfect for running 20 km injured. 1) 〜を問わず needs a noun of range (年齢・性別を問わず). 2) 〜をよそに (5課) is "ignoring other people's worry/criticism" (親の心配をよそに) and has a negative tone — not for one's own injury. 3) 〜をおいて (3課) means "none other than" and needs a negative.

Q12

昔（　）、今は男女平等の時代なんですよ。同じ権利が与えられるべきです。Mukashi (___), ima wa danjo byoudou no jidai nan desu yo. Onaji kenri ga ataerareru beki desu.

Full sentence: 昔ならいざしらず、今は男女平等の時代なんですよ。同じ権利が与えられるべきです。Mukashi nara iza shirazu, ima wa danjo byoudou no jidai nan desu yo. Onaji kenri ga ataerareru beki desu.
EN: In the old days, perhaps — but this is an age of gender equality. Men and women should be given the same rights.
HI: पुराने ज़माने की बात और थी, पर अब तो स्त्री-पुरुष समानता का युग है। दोनों को बराबर अधिकार मिलने चाहिए।
GU: જૂના જમાનાની વાત જુદી હતી, પણ હવે તો સ્ત્રી-પુરુષ સમાનતાનો યુગ છે. બંનેને સરખા અધિકાર મળવા જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ならいざしらずnara iza shirazuit might be different for ~, but...की बात और है, पर...ની વાત જુદી છે, પણ
2もさることながらmo saru koto nagaranot only ~ but also...तो है ही, साथ में...તો છે જ, સાથે
3はさておきwa sateokisetting ~ aside (for now)...को फ़िलहाल छोड़कर...ને હાલ પૂરતું બાજુએ રાખીને
4はおろかwa orokalet alone ~...तो दूर की बात...તો દૂરની વાત

Why: 〜ならいざしらず (5課) = "X might be a special case, but (the present case) is different, so…" — contrasts an exceptional situation (the old days) with the current one and ends in a judgment (べきです). 2) もさることながら (3課) adds a second, stronger point ("not only the old days but…"). 3) はさておき just postpones a topic; it doesn't contrast past and present. 4) はおろか (3課) needs "not even the easier one" plus a negative (漢字はおろか、ひらがなも書けない).

Q13

風が強く、木々が今にも（　）揺れている。Kaze ga tsuyoku, kigi ga ima ni mo (___) yurete iru.

Full sentence: 風が強く、木々が今にも倒れんばかりに揺れている。Kaze ga tsuyoku, kigi ga ima ni mo taoren bakari ni yurete iru.
EN: The wind is so strong that the trees are swaying as if they might come down at any moment.
HI: हवा इतनी तेज़ है कि पेड़ ऐसे हिल रहे हैं मानो अभी गिर पड़ेंगे।
GU: પવન એટલો જોરદાર છે કે વૃક્ષો એવી રીતે હાલી રહ્યાં છે જાણે હમણાં જ પડી જશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1倒れるとばかりにtaoreru to bakari ni(acting) as if to say "I'll fall"मानो कह रहे हों "गिर जाऊँगा"જાણે કહેતાં હોય "પડી જઈશ"
2倒れんばかりにtaoren bakari nias if about to fallमानो अभी गिर पड़ेंगेજાણે હમણાં પડી જશે
3倒れるともなくtaoreru to mo nakufalling without meaning to (unnatural)बिना इरादे के गिरते हुएઇરાદા વગર પડતાં
4倒れつつtaoretsutsuwhile fallingगिरते हुएપડતાં પડતાં

Why: V-ない形 + んばかりに (6課) = "so much that it looks as if it will ~ (though it doesn't)", and 今にも is its typical partner. 1) 〜とばかりに (6課) describes a person's attitude that seems to say something (「帰れ」とばかりに), not trees. 3) 〜ともなく (6課) is for a person's unintentional action with volitional verbs (見るともなく見る). 4) 〜つつ would mean they actually are falling while swaying.

Q14

（　）昔聞いたことがある歌が聞こえてきた。(___) mukashi kiita koto ga aru uta ga kikoete kita.

Full sentence: どこからともなしに昔聞いたことがある歌が聞こえてきた。Doko kara to mo nashi ni mukashi kiita koto ga aru uta ga kikoete kita.
EN: From somewhere or other came the sound of a song I had heard long ago.
HI: न जाने कहाँ से, बहुत पहले सुना हुआ एक गीत सुनाई देने लगा।
GU: ખબર નહીં ક્યાંથી, વર્ષો પહેલાં સાંભળેલું એક ગીત સંભળાવા લાગ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1どこからともいえずdoko kara to mo iezu(non-standard) can't say from where(ग़लत रूप) कह नहीं सकते कहाँ से(ખોટું રૂપ) કહી ન શકાય ક્યાંથી
2どこともいえずdoko to mo iezu(non-standard) can't say where(ग़लत रूप) कह नहीं सकते कहाँ(ખોટું રૂપ) કહી ન શકાય ક્યાં
3どこからともなしにdoko kara to mo nashi nifrom somewhere or otherन जाने कहाँ सेખબર નહીં ક્યાંથી

Q15

川口先生はどうも授業に関係ない話をする（　）。でも、そこがまた面白い。Kawaguchi-sensei wa doumo jugyou ni kankei nai hanashi o suru (___). Demo, soko ga mata omoshiroi.

Full sentence: 川口先生はどうも授業に関係ない話をするきらいがある。でも、そこがまた面白い。Kawaguchi-sensei wa doumo jugyou ni kankei nai hanashi o suru kirai ga aru. Demo, soko ga mata omoshiroi.
EN: Professor Kawaguchi does tend to talk about things unrelated to the lesson. But that's also what makes him interesting.
HI: कावागुची सर की आदत-सी है कि वे क्लास से असंबंधित बातें करने लगते हैं। पर यही बात उन्हें दिलचस्प भी बनाती है।
GU: કાવાગુચી સરને ટેવ જેવું છે કે તેઓ વર્ગ સાથે અસંબંધિત વાતો કરવા લાગે છે. પણ એ જ વાત તેમને રસપ્રદ પણ બનાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1きらいがあるkirai ga aruhas a (bad) tendency to...की (बुरी) आदत-सी है...ની (ખરાબ) ટેવ જેવું છે
2ものがあるmono ga aruthere is something ~ about itइसमें कुछ ... बात हैતેમાં કંઈક ... વાત છે
3わけがあるwake ga aruthere's a reason (why)इसकी वजह हैતેનું કારણ છે
4ならいがあるnarai ga aru(non-standard) there is a custom(ग़लत रूप) रिवाज़ है(ખોટું રૂપ) રિવાજ છે

Why: 〜きらいがある (6課) = "has a tendency to ~", used for undesirable habits — wandering off-topic in class — and どうも is a natural partner. でも、そこがまた面白い then softens the criticism. 2) 〜ものがある expresses the speaker's emotional appraisal (心に響くものがある), not a habit. 3) 〜わけがある explains a reason. 4) ならい (custom) doesn't form this pattern.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The test pits each N1 form against an N2 look-alike, a sister form from the same lesson, or a fake "near-form". These are the pairs the distractors were built on:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ものの (N2, 8課〔復習〕)~mono no Although (it's true that) ~ 普通形 Two real facts in contrast. 着いたものの、道がわからなかった (Q1).
〜ものを (8課)~mono o If only ~, but… 普通形 (after a 〜ば supposition) Counter-to-fact supposition + regret. 言えば手伝ったものを — never for something that actually happened (Q1).
〜とはいえ (8課)~to wa ie Granted ~, still 名・普通形 Fact + speaker's evaluation. 仕事とはいえ、体に悪い (Q3).
〜といえども (8課)~to iedomo Even though / even a ~ 名・普通形 硬; fact or hypothesis + assertion / obligation. 入院中といえども選挙権がある (Q9).
〜いかんで (5課) vs 〜にしたがって／〜に伴って (N2)~ikan de / ~ni shitagatte / ~ni tomonatte Depending on ~ vs. as ~ changes N(の) + いかんで ／ N・V + にしたがって いかんで: the result is decided by N. にしたがって／に伴って: two changes move in parallel. 頼み方いかんで変わる (Q5).
〜いかんにかかわらず (5課) vs 〜にもかかわらず (N2)~ikan ni kakawarazu / ~ni mo kakawarazu Regardless of ~ vs. despite ~ Nのいかん + に ／ 名・普通形 + にも The extra も turns "whatever it is" into "although it is (a fact)". 値段のいかんにかかわらず (Q8); 雨にもかかわらず.
〜をものともせず(に) vs 〜をよそに (both 5課)~o mono to mo sezu (ni) / ~o yoso ni Undaunted by ~ vs. ignoring ~ N + を ものともせず: overcoming a hardship (praise). よそに: disregarding others' worries (criticism). けがをものともせずに走った (Q11); 親の心配をよそに.
〜ならいざしらず (5課) vs 〜はさておき／〜はおろか~nara iza shirazu / ~wa sateoki / ~wa oroka ~ may be different, but vs. setting aside vs. let alone N + ならいざしらず Contrasts an exceptional case with the present one, then judges. 昔ならいざしらず、今は… (Q12).
〜かたわら vs 〜がてら／〜ついでに (7課 / N2)~katawara / ~gatera / ~tsuide ni Alongside one's main work vs. while (going out) Nの・V辞書形 + かたわら ／ N・Vます + がてら かたわら: two long-term activities. がてら／ついでに: a one-off occasion, often with movement. 教師をするかたわら小説を書く (Q7).
〜んばかりに vs 〜とばかりに (both 6課)~n bakari ni / ~to bakari ni As if about to ~ vs. as if to say ~ Vない形 + んばかりに ／ quote + とばかりに んばかり: degree of a state (今にも倒れんばかり). とばかり: a person's attitude that "says" something. 木々が倒れんばかりに揺れる (Q13).
Exam trap 1 — one kana makes the form: ものの／ものを／もので (Q1), べきところを／はずのところを (Q10), にかかわらず／にもかかわらず (Q8), んばかりに／とばかりに (Q13). When two options look almost identical, the test is the exact fixed shape, so check the connection before the meaning.
Exam trap 2 — fake near-forms: だけではの, どこからともいえず, ならいがある and をはじめに are distractors built to look like real patterns. If an option is a form you have never seen in the book, it is usually wrong (Q2, Q6, Q14, Q15).
Exam trap 3 — praise vs. criticism: ならではの and をものともせず are positive; をよそに and きらいがある are negative. Read the tone of the whole sentence (大いに楽しみたい, 走り抜いた, どうも…) before choosing (Q6, Q11, Q15).
