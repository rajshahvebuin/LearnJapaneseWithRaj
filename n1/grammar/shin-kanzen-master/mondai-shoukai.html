What this page is: the book's front-matter 問題紹介 (Mondai shoukai — "introducing the question types", printed p.2–5). It explains the three kinds of grammar question on the JLPT N1 文法 section, with one or two 例題 (sample questions) each, and tells you which part of the book trains each type. There are no numbered grammar points here; the forms that appear (〜が最後, 〜てからというもの, 〜に伴う, 〜とあれば …) are taught properly in the lessons.

1 Grammar Points

On this page the "grammar points" are the three question types themselves (I, II, III), each with the book's own solving advice. Formation tables are skipped: these sections are about how to answer, not about one form.

ReadingBun no bunpou 1 (bunpou keishiki no handan)
What it is (EN)Choose the grammar form that fits the meaning of the sentence. Book: 文の意味を考え、それに合う文法形式を判断する問題です。 Two sub-types: choose the form that fits the sentence (例題1), or choose the continuation that uses the form correctly (例題2).

Instruction line次の文の（　）に入れるのに最も良いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ( ) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.
Book's adviceBesides each form's meaning/function (意味機能), its connection (接続の形) and grammatical properties (文法的性質), check what the sentence is doing: a future prediction, a one-time past event, or a continuing state? and is the speaker stating an opinion (主観) or describing a fact? Questions may also combine several forms at once.

今度の選挙に落選した（が最後）、二度と政界には戻れないだろう。Kondo no senkyo ni rakusen shita ga saigo, nido to seikai ni wa modorenai darou. Once he loses this election, he'll probably never get back into politics. इस बार चुनाव हार गया तो बस, वह शायद फिर कभी राजनीति में नहीं लौट पाएगा। આ વખતે ચૂંટણી હારી ગયો એટલે પત્યું, તે કદાચ ફરી ક્યારેય રાજકારણમાં પાછો નહીં ફરી શકે.
会社に入ってからというもの、（ゆっくり釣りに行く時間もない）。Kaisha ni haitte kara to iu mono, yukkuri tsuri ni iku jikan mo nai. Ever since I joined the company, I haven't even had time to go fishing at leisure. कंपनी में आने के बाद से, आराम से मछली पकड़ने जाने का भी समय नहीं मिलता। કંપનીમાં જોડાયા પછીથી, નિરાંતે માછલી પકડવા જવાનો પણ સમય નથી મળતો.

Both 例題 are answered by reading what surrounds the blank: in 例題1 the sentence end 〜だろう shows a future prediction and the blank sits after the た形 落選した; in 例題2 the form 〜てからというもの demands a continuing state after a change. That is the habit this book drills in 第1部: check the end of the sentence and the connection before you look at the meanings of the options.

【II】 文の文法2（文の組み立て）

exam question type 2 — 問題2 (★ sentence ordering)
ReadingBun no bunpou 2 (bun no kumitate)
What it is (EN)Rearrange several word-groups into a grammatical, meaningful sentence, then choose which of the four goes in the ★ slot. Book: 複数の語句を並べ替えて、文法的に正しく、意味の通る文を作る問題です。四つの選択肢のうち★の位置に当たるものがどれかを選びます。
What it is (HI)चार शब्द-समूहों को सही क्रम में लगाकर अर्थपूर्ण वाक्य बनाना, फिर बताना कि ★ वाली जगह पर कौन-सा आता है।
What it is (GU)ચાર શબ્દ-સમૂહોને સાચા ક્રમમાં ગોઠવીને અર્થપૂર્ણ વાક્ય બનાવવું, પછી કહેવું કે ★ વાળી જગ્યાએ કયો આવે છે.
Instruction line次の文の ＿★＿ に入る最も良いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no hoshi ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.
Book's adviceUse the grammar form among the tiles as your anchor (例題3: 〜に伴う; 例題4: 〜とあれば). Besides the meaning of the forms learnt in 第1部, you need to know what part of speech each form attaches to (品詞) and its connection form (接続の形).

There will be no change to departure times due to tomorrow's track inspection. कल के रेल-पटरी निरीक्षण के कारण प्रस्थान समय में कोई बदलाव नहीं होगा। આવતી કાલના પાટાના નિરીક્ષણને કારણે ઉપડવાના સમયમાં કોઈ ફેરફાર નથી.

If it's a request from you, who helped me when I was in trouble, I'd very much like to help. मुश्किल में मेरी मदद करने वाले आपका अनुरोध हो, तो मैं ज़रूर आपकी मदद करना चाहूँगा। મુશ્કેલીમાં મારી મદદ કરનાર તમારી વિનંતી હોય, તો હું જરૂર તમારી મદદ કરવા ઇચ્છીશ.

Only the ★ tile is scored, but you can only find it by building the whole sentence. Start from the tile that is a grammar form and ask: what must come right before it (noun? plain form?) and what can follow it? In 例題3, 〜に伴う takes a noun before it and modifies a noun after it, so it must sit between 線路の点検 and 発車時刻の…変更.

【III】 文章の文法

exam question type 3 — 問題3 (passage cloze)
ReadingBunshou no bunpou
What it is (EN)In a passage of some length, choose the grammar form etc. that fits the context. Book: まとまった長さの文章の中で、その文脈に合う文法形式などを選ぶ問題です。 Two angles: making each sentence grammatically correct inside the passage, and giving the passage cohesion (まとまり).
What it is (HI)एक लंबे अनुच्छेद में संदर्भ के अनुसार सही व्याकरण-रूप, जोड़ने वाले शब्द या वाक्य चुनना।
What it is (GU)એક લાંબા ફકરામાં સંદર્ભ મુજબ સાચું વ્યાકરણ-રૂપ, જોડનારા શબ્દો કે વાક્ય પસંદ કરવા.
Instruction line次の文章を読んで、文章全体の趣旨を踏まえて、［1］から［5］の中に入る最もよいものを1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bunshou o yonde, bunshou zentai no shushi o fumaete, ichi kara go no naka ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.
Book's adviceWhat these questions test (p.5): (1) which expressions go together (呼応); (2) which form fits the condition in context; (3) which form fits the writer's intention; (4) choosing the right viewpoint (視点); (5) judging the link between sentences (接続). See the examples below.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

The book's five 例題 (sample questions), p.2–4. 例題1–2 are fill-in (問題1 type), 例題3–4 are ★ ordering (問題2 type) and 例題5 is a passage cloze with five blanks (問題3 type). All answers, and the gist of each explanation, are the book's own (p.2–5); the extra wrong-option notes are mine.

I 文の文法1 — 例題1・2

例題1

今度の選挙に落選した（　）、二度と政界には戻れないだろう。Kondo no senkyo ni rakusen shita (___), nido to seikai ni wa modorenai darou.

Full sentence: 今度の選挙に落選したが最後、二度と政界には戻れないだろう。Kondo no senkyo ni rakusen shita ga saigo, nido to seikai ni wa modorenai darou.
EN: Once he loses this election, that's it — he'll probably never be able to return to politics.
HI: इस बार चुनाव हार गया तो बस, वह शायद फिर कभी राजनीति में नहीं लौट पाएगा।
GU: આ વખતે ચૂંટણી હારી ગયો એટલે પત્યું, તે કદાચ ફરી ક્યારેય રાજકારણમાં પાછો નહીં ફરી શકે.

1が最後ga saigoonce ~, that's the end (no way back)...किया तो बस (फिर वापसी नहीं)...કર્યું એટલે પત્યું (પછી પાછું નહીં)
2が早いかga hayai kano sooner ~ than...करते ही...કરતાં જ
3ものならmono naraif (one) could ~ / if one dares to ~अगर ...कर सकता तो / अगर ...करने की हिम्मत की तोજો ...કરી શકે તો / જો ...કરવાની હિંમત કરી તો

Why: Book: first look at how the parts before and after the blank relate (落選した → 政界に戻れない), then notice the sentence end 〜だろう — a future prediction. The form must also attach to the た形 落選した. 〜たが最後 = "once ~ happens, it ends badly / there's no going back" and fits all three. 2) が早いか is for two actual events in quick succession, not a prediction about consequences. 3) ものなら attaches to a potential form (戻れるものなら) or volitional form (〜ようものなら), never to 落選した. 4) とたんに narrates a sudden change that actually happened at that moment; it doesn't lead into a prediction like 〜だろう.

(This form is taught in 1課 【5】.)

III 文章の文法 — 例題5 (passage cloze)

Passage (吉本隆明『ひきこもれ　ひとりの時間をもつということ』大和書房 — quoted in the book):

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The four options of 例題1 are all "when/once ~" forms that attach after a verb. They differ in what the sentence that follows may be (the book: future prediction vs. one-time past event vs. continuing state):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たが最後~ta ga saigo Once ~, that's it (bad, irreversible result) V-た + が最後 Follow-up is a (usually bad) consequence that can't be undone — often a prediction (〜だろう) or a generalisation. Taught in 9課 条件 (p.42). "If you once do X, there's no going back."
〜が早いか~ga hayai ka No sooner ~ than… V-辞書形／た形 + が早いか Two actual events in quick succession; follow-up is a fact, not a wish/prediction (1課 【1】). Narrating that B happened the instant after A.
〜ものなら／〜ようものなら~mono nara / ~you mono nara If one could ~ / if one dared to ~ V-可能形 + ものなら; V-意向形 + ものなら Connection trap: never after a plain た形 like 落選した. 〜ようものなら warns of a terrible result. "If I could…, I would…" / "If you dare…, you'll regret it."
〜たとたん(に)~ta totan (ni) Just as ~, suddenly… V-た + とたん(に) A sudden, unexpected change at that exact moment; follow-up is a past fact, no prediction or will (N2). Describing a surprise that actually happened.
〜てからというもの~te kara to iu mono Ever since ~ V-て + からというもの Follow-up = a continuing state after a change (例題2; 1課 【5】). Looking back on how things have been since a turning point.
Exam trap — read the sentence end first: 〜だろう (prediction), 〜た (one past event), 〜ている／〜ない (continuing state), 〜たい／〜てほしい (wish). The book's advice for question type 1 is to classify the sentence this way before comparing the options (例題1, 例題2).
Exam trap — ★ ordering: anchor on the grammar-form tile (〜に伴う, 〜とあれば) and its connection rule; most wrong orders break a noun-vs-verb connection. Then check that modifiers (助けてくれた → あなたの頼み) and adverbs (ぜひ → 力になりたい) sit next to what they modify.
Exam trap — passage cloze: connectives are chosen by the relation to the previous sentence (contrast それに対して vs. restatement つまり), and summary sentences by は／が — which part is the topic and which is the new point (例題5 ［3］–［5］).
