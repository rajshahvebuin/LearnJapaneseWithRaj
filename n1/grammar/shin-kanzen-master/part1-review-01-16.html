Notes
15 items, 4 options (1・2・3・4), 問題1 format (文法形式の判断 — fill the blank). The test leans heavily on the second half of Part I: four items from 14課 (ときたら, ともなると, なりに, ともあろう) and three from 13課 (に〜ない, べくもない, てはいられない). Many distractors are look-alikes built on the same verb (至るまで／至って／至っては; 断るに断れない／断らないわけにはいかない／断らずにはいられない) — read every option to the end. Item 9's correct answer is the ordinary form 〜たまま, not an N1 pattern.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

次の文の（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no (___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai. — "Choose the best option (1–4) for the blank." 15 items, p.74–75. All answers confirmed from 別冊 p.3 (PDF page 195).

Q1

ノーベル賞受賞者（　）、その後の言動が世界中のマスコミに注目される。Nooberu-shou jushousha (___), sono go no gendou ga sekaijuu no masukomi ni chuumoku sareru.

Full sentence: ノーベル賞受賞者ともなると、その後の言動が世界中のマスコミに注目される。Nooberu-shou jushousha to mo naru to, sono go no gendou ga sekaijuu no masukomi ni chuumoku sareru.
EN: Once you become a Nobel Prize winner, everything you say and do afterwards is watched by the media around the world.
HI: नोबेल पुरस्कार विजेता बन जाने पर तो, उसके बाद की हर बात और हरकत पर दुनिया भर का मीडिया नज़र रखता है।
GU: નોબેલ પારિતોષિક વિજેતા બની જાઓ એટલે, ત્યાર પછીની દરેક વાત અને વર્તન પર દુનિયાભરનું મીડિયા નજર રાખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ともするとto mo suru tois apt to / tends to (adverb)अक्सर / ज़रा-सी बात परઘણી વાર / સહેજમાં
2としたらto shitaraif (supposing)अगर मान लें किજો માની લઈએ કે
3とすればto surebaif (supposing)यदि ऐसा हो तोજો એવું હોય તો
4ともなるとto mo naru toonce (someone) reaches the level of ~...के स्तर पर पहुँचने पर तो...ના સ્તરે પહોંચીએ એટલે તો

Why: 〜ともなると (14課) attaches to a noun of an advanced stage or high position (受賞者, 部長, 中学生) and states the state that naturally follows — here, constant media attention. 1) ともすると is an adverb ("is prone to") and can't follow a noun as a topic. 2) and 3) are plain hypotheticals ("if he were a winner"), but the sentence is a general truth about anyone who reaches that status, not a supposition.

Q2

うちの弟（　）、夜中にすごいボリュームで音楽を聞くんだから、たまらないよ。Uchi no otouto (___), yonaka ni sugoi boryuumu de ongaku o kiku n da kara, tamaranai yo.

Full sentence: うちの弟ときたら、夜中にすごいボリュームで音楽を聞くんだから、たまらないよ。Uchi no otouto to kitara, yonaka ni sugoi boryuumu de ongaku o kiku n da kara, tamaranai yo.
EN: As for my little brother — he listens to music at full blast in the middle of the night. I can't stand it!
HI: मेरे छोटे भाई की तो बात ही मत करो — आधी रात को पूरी आवाज़ में गाने सुनता है, बर्दाश्त नहीं होता!
GU: મારા નાના ભાઈની તો વાત જ ન કરો — અડધી રાતે ફુલ અવાજે ગીતો સાંભળે છે, સહન નથી થતું!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ときたらto kitaraas for ~ (complaint)...की तो (शिकायत)...ની તો (ફરિયાદ)
2といったらto ittaraspeaking of ~ (striking degree)...की बात करें तो...ની વાત કરીએ તો
3としたらto shitaraif (supposing)अगर मान लें किજો માની લઈએ કે
4となったらto nattarawhen it comes to (doing) ~जब ...की नौबत आएજ્યારે ...નો વારો આવે

Why: 〜ときたら (14課) picks out someone/something close to the speaker (うちの弟) and is followed by complaint or criticism — たまらないよ is exactly that; it's also 話し言葉, matching the casual よ. 2) 〜といったら introduces an extreme degree with surprise (e.g. 弟のうるささといったら…) and needs a quality noun, not a person. 3) としたら is hypothetical. 4) となったら means "when it actually comes to doing X" (いざ試験となったら) and doesn't introduce a topic of complaint.

Q3

初戦で勝てたの（　）、あの選手が点を入れたことがうれしい。Shosen de kateta no (___), ano senshu ga ten o ireta koto ga ureshii.

Full sentence: 初戦で勝てたのにもまして、あの選手が点を入れたことがうれしい。Shosen de kateta no ni mo mashite, ano senshu ga ten o ireta koto ga ureshii.
EN: Even more than winning the first match, I'm happy that that player scored.
HI: पहला मैच जीतने से भी ज़्यादा, मुझे इस बात की ख़ुशी है कि उस खिलाड़ी ने गोल किया।
GU: પહેલી મેચ જીત્યા કરતાં પણ વધારે, મને એ વાતનો આનંદ છે કે તે ખેલાડીએ ગોલ કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にひきかえni hikikaein (stark) contrast to...के बिल्कुल उलट...થી બિલકુલ ઊલટું
2にもましてni mo mashiteeven more than...से भी बढ़कर...થી પણ વધીને
3に伴いni tomonaialong with / as ~ changes...के साथ-साथ...ની સાથે સાથે
4に反してni hanshitecontrary to (expectations)...के विपरीत...થી વિપરીત

Why: Both halves are happy things, and the second is happier → 〜にもまして (15課), "B even more than A". 1) 〜にひきかえ contrasts a good thing with a bad one (subjective good/bad evaluation), but both here are good. 3) 〜に伴い (N2) needs a change that drags another change along. 4) 〜に反して needs a noun like 予想・期待 ("contrary to expectation").

Q4

高橋先生は、専門の経済論はもちろん、タレントのうわさ話に（　）、どんな話題でも楽しそうに話す。Takahashi-sensei wa, senmon no keizairon wa mochiron, tarento no uwasabanashi ni (___), donna wadai demo tanoshisou ni hanasu.

Full sentence: 高橋先生は、専門の経済論はもちろん、タレントのうわさ話に至るまで、どんな話題でも楽しそうに話す。Takahashi-sensei wa, senmon no keizairon wa mochiron, tarento no uwasabanashi ni itaru made, donna wadai demo tanoshisou ni hanasu.
EN: Professor Takahashi happily talks about any topic — from his specialty, economics, of course, all the way down to celebrity gossip.
HI: ताकाहाशी सर अपने विषय अर्थशास्त्र से लेकर फ़िल्मी सितारों की गपशप तक, किसी भी विषय पर मज़े से बात करते हैं।
GU: તાકાહાશી સર પોતાના વિષય અર્થશાસ્ત્રથી માંડીને ફિલ્મી સિતારાઓની ગપસપ સુધી, કોઈ પણ વિષય પર મજાથી વાત કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1至るまでitaru made(all the way) down to / even...तक (भी)...સુધી (પણ)
2至ってitattehaving reached (the point of) ~...की नौबत आने पर...ની હદે પહોંચીને
3至ってはitatte waas for (the extreme case)...की तो बात ही क्या...ની તો વાત જ શી
4至るまではitaru made wauntil reaching ~ (but not after)...तक पहुँचने से पहले...સુધી પહોંચતાં પહેલાં

Why: 〜に至るまで (2課) marks the far end of a range, stressing how wide it is: from serious economics "even to" gossip, then どんな話題でも sums it up. 2) 〜に至って (16課) = "only when things went as far as ~" — needs a turning-point event. 3) 〜に至っては (16課) singles out the worst example of a bad situation. 4) adding は makes it a contrast ("up to that point, but not beyond"), which breaks the "everything" meaning.

Q5

社長に反抗など（　）、会社を辞めさせられるだろう。Shachou ni hankou nado (___), kaisha o yamesaserareru darou.

Full sentence: 社長に反抗などしたら最後、会社を辞めさせられるだろう。Shachou ni hankou nado shitara saigo, kaisha o yamesaserareru darou.
EN: If you ever defy the company president, that'll be the end of you — you'll probably be forced to quit.
HI: अगर तुमने कभी अध्यक्ष का विरोध किया, तो बस, समझो ख़त्म — शायद तुम्हें नौकरी छोड़नी पड़ेगी।
GU: જો તમે ક્યારેય પ્રમુખનો વિરોધ કર્યો, તો બસ, ખતમ — કદાચ તમારે નોકરી છોડવી પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1した手前shita temaehaving done it (I can't back out now)कर चुकने के बाद (अब पीछे नहीं हट सकते)કરી ચૂક્યા પછી (હવે પાછા ન હટાય)
2するに至ってsuru ni itattewhen it went as far as doingकरने की नौबत आने परકરવાની હદે પહોંચતાં
3すればこそsureba kosoprecisely because one doesकरने के कारण हीકરવાને કારણે જ
4したら最後shitara saigoonce you do it, that's the endएक बार किया तो बस ख़त्मએક વાર કર્યું તો બસ ખતમ

Why: 〜たら最後 (9課) = "once you do X, something terrible is bound to follow"; it works with a hypothetical (反抗など…), and the result (辞めさせられる) is the bad outcome the speaker fears. 1) 〜手前 (12課) explains why one feels obliged ("having said it in front of others"), not a dire consequence. 2) 〜に至って (16課) narrates a turning point that already happened, clashing with だろう. 3) 〜ばこそ (12課) gives a surprising reason, usually with のだ, not a threat.

Q6

以上がわたしからの忠告だ。後は君（　）考えて、結論を出してほしい。Ijou ga watashi kara no chuukoku da. Ato wa kimi (___) kangaete, ketsuron o dashite hoshii.

Full sentence: 以上がわたしからの忠告だ。後は君なりに考えて、結論を出してほしい。Ijou ga watashi kara no chuukoku da. Ato wa kimi nari ni kangaete, ketsuron o dashite hoshii.
EN: That's my advice. The rest is up to you — think it over in your own way and reach a conclusion.
HI: यह रही मेरी सलाह। आगे तुम अपने ढंग से सोचकर कोई निष्कर्ष निकालो।
GU: આ મારી સલાહ છે. હવે તમે તમારી રીતે વિચારીને કોઈ નિષ્કર્ષ પર આવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ながらにnagara niwhile remaining (as is) / since birth...की स्थिति में ही...ની સ્થિતિમાં જ
2なりにnari niin one's own way (within one's limits)अपने ढंग से / अपनी हैसियत के मुताबिक़પોતાની રીતે / પોતાની મર્યાદામાં
3いかんでikan dedepending on...पर निर्भर करते हुए...ના આધારે
4次第でshidai dedepending on...पर निर्भर...પર આધાર રાખીને

Why: 〜なりに (14課) = "doing one's best within the limits of who/what one is" — 君なりに考えて ("think it over as best you can, your way"). 1) 〜ながらに (6課) only attaches to a few fixed words (生まれながらに, 涙ながらに), not a person. 3) 〜いかんで (5課) and 4) 〜次第で (N2) mean "depending on", which need a variable noun (結果, 努力) — "depending on you, think" makes no sense.

Q7

今日の講演会は満員と（　）、半分近くが空席だった。Kyou no kouenkai wa man'in to (___), hanbun chikaku ga kuuseki datta.

Full sentence: 今日の講演会は満員と思いきや、半分近くが空席だった。Kyou no kouenkai wa man'in to omoiki ya, hanbun chikaku ga kuuseki datta.
EN: I thought today's lecture would be packed, but nearly half the seats were empty.
HI: सोचा था आज का व्याख्यान खचाखच भरा होगा, पर लगभग आधी सीटें ख़ाली थीं।
GU: ધાર્યું હતું કે આજનું વ્યાખ્યાન ખીચોખીચ ભરેલું હશે, પણ લગભગ અડધી બેઠકો ખાલી હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1思いきやomoiki yaI thought ~, but (surprisingly)सोचा था ~, पर (अप्रत्याशित रूप से)ધાર્યું હતું ~, પણ (અણધાર્યું)
2思わずomowazuwithout thinking / involuntarilyअनजाने में / अचानकઅજાણતાં / અચાનક

Q8

前回優勝の林選手（　）が、あんなつまらないミスで負けるなんて。Zenkai yuushou no Hayashi senshu (___) ga, anna tsumaranai misu de makeru nante.

Full sentence: 前回優勝の林選手ともあろう人が、あんなつまらないミスで負けるなんて。Zenkai yuushou no Hayashi senshu to mo arou hito ga, anna tsumaranai misu de makeru nante.
EN: To think that someone like Hayashi, last time's champion, would lose over such a silly mistake!
HI: पिछली बार के विजेता हयाशी जैसे खिलाड़ी का ऐसी मामूली ग़लती से हार जाना — यक़ीन नहीं होता!
GU: ગયા વખતના વિજેતા હયાશી જેવા ખેલાડીનું આવી મામૂલી ભૂલથી હારી જવું — માન્યામાં નથી આવતું!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1たるものtaru monoanyone who is a (true) ~ (should…)जो सच्चा ~ है (उसे चाहिए…)જે સાચો ~ છે (તેણે જોઈએ…)
2ともあろう人to mo arou hitosomeone of such standing as ~~ जैसा (प्रतिष्ठित) व्यक्ति~ જેવી (પ્રતિષ્ઠિત) વ્યક્તિ

Why: 〜ともあろう + N (14課) expresses surprise/disapproval that a person of high standing did something unworthy of it — exactly 〜が…負けるなんて. 1) 〜たるもの (14課) is followed by what such a person ought to do (〜べきだ／なければならない), not by a specific lapse, and it isn't followed by が here. 3) ともなろう is a blend of ともなると and ともあろう that doesn't exist. 4) でもあるもの is not a fixed expression.

Q9

母は夕飯を（　）、電話で長話をしている。Haha wa yuuhan o (___), denwa de nagabanashi o shite iru.

Full sentence: 母は夕飯を作りかけたまま、電話で長話をしている。Haha wa yuuhan o tsukurikaketa mama, denwa de nagabanashi o shite iru.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The distractors are mostly forms built on the same word as the answer, or "opposite-meaning twins". These are the pairs the test was built on:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ともなると (14課) vs 〜としたら (N3)~to mo naru to / ~to shitara Once one reaches the level of ~ vs. if (supposing) ~ N + ともなると／普通形 + としたら ともなると = general truth about a high stage; としたら = a hypothesis. 受賞者ともなると注目される (Q1).
〜ときたら (14課) vs 〜といったら (N2)~to kitara / ~to ittara As for ~ (complaint) vs. speaking of ~ (striking degree) N + ときたら／N(〜さ) + といったら ときたら = close person/thing + complaint, no wish/request after it; といったら = surprise at a degree. うちの弟ときたら…たまらない (Q2).

〜てはいられない (13課) vs 〜ないではいられない (N2)~te wa irarenai / ~nai de wa irarenai Can't afford to keep ~ vs. can't help ~ V-て + はいられない／V-ない + ではいられない One ない flips the meaning: stop doing it vs. compelled to do it. 居眠りなどしてはいられない (Q15).
Exam trap 1 — opposite-meaning twins: Q13 and Q15 each offer two options that differ by one negative. Before choosing, translate the whole option: 断るに断れない (didn't refuse) ≠ 断らずにはいられない (couldn't help refusing); してはいられない (must stop) ≠ しないではいられない (can't stop). Decide from context which outcome the sentence describes.
Exam trap 2 — one verb, many forms: when all four options start with the same word (至る… in Q4 and Q10), look at what follows the blank. A range with どんな〜でも → に至るまで; a late reaction with 始めた／気がついた → に至って(はじめて); an extreme example in a list of bad cases → に至っては; "ever since" forms need a lasting state.
Exam trap 3 — 14課 forms by what follows: ときたら → complaint (no request); ともなると → the state that naturally follows a high stage; ともあろう人が → shock at an unworthy act (〜なんて); たるもの → what one should do (〜べきだ); なりに → effort within one's limits (Q1, Q2, Q6, Q8).
