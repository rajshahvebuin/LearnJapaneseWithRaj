Answer key
The 別冊 解答 p.5 (PDF page 197) gives only the ★-slot tile for each of the 12 items. All 12 ★ tiles are confirmed from it; the full orders are my own reasoning, each checked to put the official tile at ★. Explanations are mine.
Notes
p.114 covers (1) N1 forms that are always followed by a noun and (2) the ways a noun can be explained by what precedes it — including two patterns learners often miss: て形／たり／conditional + の + N (子供を入れての人数) and adverb + の + N (たびたびの入院). Half of the ★ items turn on 〜という + N (content of a speech or feeling). In every item the ★ is the 3rd blank.

1 Grammar Points

The book's intro (p.114): N1の文法形式には、後に必ず名詞が来るものがあります。また、名詞を説明する形式も、初級で学習したものに加えてさらにいろいろあります。 — Some N1 forms must be followed by a noun, and beyond the basic relative clause there are several more ways of explaining a noun.

All six end in a noun-modifying form (〜き, 〜ざる, 〜う, 〜の, 〜ある, 〜る), so in ★ items the tile right after them must be a noun (or adjective + noun, as in 将来にかかわる大切な問題). まじき／べからざる are stiff classical forms; ともあろう needs a person of high status before it and a "how could they?" judgement after.

The key split is c) vs d). When the noun names a fact or situation (状態, 役割, 事件, くせ), the plain form attaches directly. When the noun is a speech/thought/feeling word (話, 不安, 焦り, 満足感, 姿勢, 志) the content is quoted with という. Pattern a) is the surprising one: て形, 〜てこそ, 〜たり〜たり, and even 〜たら／〜ば can take の to modify a noun.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

12 ★ items from p.115 (次の文の★に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4の中から一つ選びなさい). The ★ is always the 3rd blank. The correct row in each options table is the ★ tile confirmed from 別冊 p.5; order chains and assembled sentences are my reasoning.

Q1

他人の功績を横取りする ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ することではない。Tanin no kouseki o yokodori suru ___ ___ ★ ___ suru koto de wa nai.

Tiles: 1 大学の学長 (daigaku no gakuchou)　2 人の (hito no)　3 ともあろう (tomo arou)　4 なんて (nante)

4 なんて→1 大学の学長→3 ともあろう→2 人の

他人の功績を横取りするなんて大学の学長ともあろう人のすることではない。Tanin no kouseki o yokodori suru nante daigaku no gakuchou tomo arou hito no suru koto de wa nai.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This lesson is about what sits between a clause and its noun. The traps:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
普通形 + Nfutsuukei + N clause directly modifies N V/Adj plain + 状態・役割・事件・登山… N is a fact/situation; no という. Describing what the thing is (Q7, Q10).
普通形 + という + Nfutsuukei + to iu + N N whose content is "…" plain/volitional/たい + という + 話・不安・焦り・姿勢・志・満足感 N is speech/thought/feeling; という is required. Quoting the content of an idea (Q4, Q8, Q11, Q12).
副詞 + の + Nfukushi + no + N adverb used as a noun modifier たびたびの入院, 全くの誤解, 今すぐの話 Adverbs normally modify verbs; with の they modify nouns. Q5 (今すぐの話).
〜まじき vs 〜べからざる~majiki / ~bekarazaru must not / indispensable V-dict + まじき + N (N にあるまじき); V-dict + べからざる + N Both are prenominal only. まじき = morally unacceptable for that person; べからざる = must not (欠くべからざる = indispensable). Formal condemnation (Q2, Q3).
〜ともあろう vs 〜あっての~tomo arou / ~atte no someone of the status of / only possible thanks to N(status) + ともあろう + 人; N + あっての + N ともあろう criticises a high-status person; あっての states a precondition. Q1 vs Q4.
Exam trap 1 — という or not? If the printed noun after the blanks is 話／不安／焦り／満足感／姿勢／志／事態, expect a という tile right before it. If it is 状態／役割／事件 or an activity noun, the clause attaches directly.
Exam trap 2 — prenominal forms go last-but-noun: まじき, べからざる, ともあろう, あっての, からある, にかかわる cannot end a sentence here; the tile after them must be a noun (or adjective + noun). If the noun is printed, the form is the 4th tile (Q3).
