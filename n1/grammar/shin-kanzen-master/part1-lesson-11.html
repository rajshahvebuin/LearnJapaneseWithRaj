Notes
Two "in order to" forms and one "by means of" form. 〜べく and 〜んがため(に) are both tagged 書き言葉 (written language) in the book and share the same three rules: volitional verb before, volitional action after, same subject — and no request/advice in the second half. 〜をもって is tagged 硬 formal/written (硬い言い方); it is the "means" use of the 〜をもって you met in 2課 (there it marked an end point: 本日をもって). Drill [1] has 4 items, not 3.
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.50): the everyday "in order to" (〜ため) and "by means of" (〜によって). Today's N1 forms are written-style replacements for them.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜べく

neutral register (not marked 硬; book tag 書き言葉 = written language)
Reading~beku
Meaning (EN)"in order to ~; with the aim of ~" — book: 〜ようと思ってある行為をする (do something intending to ~)
Meaning (HI)...करने के लिए / ...करने के इरादे से
Meaning (GU)...કરવા માટે / ...કરવાના ઇરાદાથી
Connection (接続)動 辞書形 + べく　＊例外 する→するべく・すべく
Register書き言葉 (written language) — reports, news, formal writing. Not tagged 硬い言い方, but sounds out of place in chat.
Typical useAttaches to volitional verbs (意志動詞). The second clause is also a volitional action. It cannot be followed by a sentence that works on the listener (働きかけ: request, advice, question). The subject of both clauses is the same.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (辞書形)V-dictionary + べくなるべくnaru beku
する-verb (exception)する → するべく・すべく購入するべく／開発すべくkounyuu suru beku / kaihatsu su beku
Potential / non-volitional verb(added, not in book) not used — ×参加できるべく (use 〜ように)—
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — needs a volitional verb—

新型の機械を購入するべく、社長はいろいろ調べている。Shingata no kikai o kounyuu suru beku, shachou wa iroiro shirabete iru. With a view to buying a new-model machine, the company president is researching all kinds of options. नए मॉडल की मशीन ख़रीदने के इरादे से, अध्यक्ष तरह-तरह की जाँच-पड़ताल कर रहे हैं। નવા મૉડેલનું મશીન ખરીદવાના ઇરાદાથી, પ્રમુખ જાતજાતની તપાસ કરી રહ્યા છે.
介護ロボットを開発すべく、わたしたちは今日も実験を続ける。Kaigo robotto o kaihatsu su beku, watashitachi wa kyou mo jikken o tsuzukeru. In order to develop a care robot, we carry on with our experiments today as well. देखभाल करने वाला रोबोट विकसित करने के लिए, हम आज भी प्रयोग जारी रखेंगे। સંભાળ રાખનાર રોબોટ વિકસાવવા માટે, અમે આજે પણ પ્રયોગ ચાલુ રાખીશું.

べく is the old continuative form of べし ("should / ought to"), so it carries a sense of purposeful resolve. Think of it as a written 〜ために with three locks on it: volitional verb in front, a deliberate action after, and one subject throughout. Drill [1] tests each lock — the form (Q1), the subject (Q2, Q4) and the ban on requests/advice (Q3).

【2】 〜んがため(に)

neutral register (not marked 硬; book tag 書き言葉 = written language)
Reading~n ga tame (ni)
Meaning (EN)"(determined) in order to ~; for the sake of ~" — book: 〜という目的を持ってある行為をする (do something with the purpose ~)
Meaning (HI)(हर हाल में) ...करने के लिए / ...के ख़ातिर
Meaning (GU)(કોઈ પણ ભોગે) ...કરવા માટે / ...ની ખાતર
Connection (接続)動 ない形（ない）+ んがため（に）　＊例外 する→せん
Register書き言葉 (written language), with a classical, dramatic feel.
Typical useAttaches to words that express a serious purpose (volitional verbs). The second half is a volitional action; no 働きかけ (request/advice). The subject of both clauses is the same. Not used in everyday situations. Before a noun it becomes 〜んがための + N (③).
Word typeFormationExample
Group 1 verbV-ない stem + んがため(に)生きんがために／得んがためiki n ga tame ni / e n ga tame
Group 1 verb (う-row)売る → 売ら + んがため売らんがためのura n ga tame no
する-verb (exception)する → せん + がため証明せんがためshoumei sen ga tame
Causativeさせる → させ + んがため実現させんがためjitsugen sase n ga tame
Before a noun〜んがための + N得んがための発言e n ga tame no hatsugen
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used — needs a volitional verb—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は歌手になりたいという夢を実現させんがため、上京した。Kanojo wa kashu ni naritai to iu yume o jitsugen sase n ga tame, joukyou shita. Determined to realise her dream of becoming a singer, she moved to Tokyo. गायिका बनने का अपना सपना पूरा करने के लिए, वह टोक्यो चली गई। ગાયિકા બનવાનું પોતાનું સપનું સાકાર કરવા માટે, તે ટોક્યો ચાલી ગઈ.
ライオンがしまうまを食べるのは残酷に見えるが、ライオンは生きんがために、そうするのである。Raion ga shimauma o taberu no wa zankoku ni mieru ga, raion wa iki n ga tame ni, sou suru no de aru. A lion eating a zebra looks cruel, but the lion does it in order to survive. शेर का ज़ेबरा खाना क्रूर लगता है, पर शेर ऐसा ज़िंदा रहने के लिए ही करता है। સિંહનું ઝીબ્રા ખાવું ક્રૂર લાગે છે, પણ સિંહ એવું જીવતા રહેવા માટે જ કરે છે.
自分の利益を得んがための発言では、人の心を動かせない。Jibun no rieki o e n ga tame no hatsugen de wa, hito no kokoro o ugokasenai. Remarks made purely to serve your own interests cannot move people's hearts. केवल अपना फ़ायदा उठाने के लिए दिए गए बयान लोगों का दिल नहीं छू सकते। માત્ર પોતાનો લાભ મેળવવા માટે કરેલાં નિવેદનો લોકોનાં દિલ જીતી શકતાં નથી.

ん is the classical volitional auxiliary む ("intend to"), so 〜んがため literally means "for the sake of intending to ~" — heavier and more dramatic than 〜べく. Watch the conjugation: it is the ない-stem, so 売る → 売らん, する → せん (not ×するんがため). 〜んがための + N often has a critical tone (売らんがための誇張 "exaggeration just to sell"), as in [1〜3] Q2.

【3】 〜をもって

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~o motte
Meaning (EN)"by means of ~; with ~" — book: 〜を手段としてある行為をする (do something using ~ as the means). Cross-reference in the book: →2課-4 (〜をもって = end point).
Meaning (HI)...के द्वारा / ...के माध्यम से / ...से
Meaning (GU)...દ્વારા / ...ના માધ્યમથી / ...થી
Connection (接続)名 + をもって
Register硬 — formal notices, business letters, speeches.
Typical useNot used for everyday, concrete tools or methods (×はしをもって食べる). The fixed phrase 〜をもってすれば (③) expresses high praise of the power of ~: "with ~, (anything is possible)".
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をもって書面をもってshomen o motte
Question word何 + をもって何をもってnani o motte
Fixed phrase (praise)N + をもってすれば医療技術をもってすればiryou gijutsu o motte sureba
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — nominalise first: 論文提出をもって—

本日の採用試験の結果は後日書面をもってご連絡いたします。Honjitsu no saiyou shiken no kekka wa gojitsu shomen o motte go-renraku itashimasu. We will inform you of the results of today's recruitment exam in writing at a later date. आज की भर्ती परीक्षा के परिणाम हम बाद में लिखित रूप में सूचित करेंगे। આજની ભરતી પરીક્ષાનાં પરિણામ અમે પછીથી લેખિતમાં જણાવીશું.
何をもって人の価値を評価するかは難しい問題だ。Nani o motte hito no kachi o hyouka suru ka wa muzukashii mondai da. By what measure to judge a person's worth is a difficult question. किस आधार पर किसी इंसान का मूल्य आँका जाए, यह कठिन सवाल है। કયા આધારે કોઈ માણસનું મૂલ્ય આંકવું, એ મુશ્કેલ પ્રશ્ન છે.
最新の医療技術をもってすれば、人はさらに寿命を延ばせるだろう。Saishin no iryou gijutsu o motte sureba, hito wa sara ni jumyou o nobaseru darou. With the latest medical technology, people will probably be able to extend their lifespan even further. नवीनतम चिकित्सा तकनीक के बल पर, इंसान अपनी उम्र और भी बढ़ा सकेगा। નવીનતમ તબીબી ટેક્નોલૉજીના બળે, માણસ પોતાનું આયુષ્ય હજી વધારી શકશે.

The same 〜をもって appears in 2課 with a different job — marking the moment something ends (本日をもって閉店). Here it marks the means, but only abstract or formal means: documents (書面), effort (努力), ability (実力), technology. 〜をもってすれば always leads to a positive conclusion ("with his ability, success is certain"), never to doubt — that is what [3] Q3 tests.

[1] Q1

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two purpose forms that look interchangeable, one means form, and their everyday N2 equivalents. The differences are all about connection, who acts and what may follow:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜べく~beku In order to ~ V-辞書形 (する→するべく／すべく) 書き言葉. Volitional verb → volitional action, same subject, no request/advice. Links clauses only (×べくの + N). Written reports of someone's purposeful action (選手になるべく練習する).
〜んがため(に)~n ga tame (ni) (Determined) in order to ~ V-ない stem + んがため (する→せん) Same rules as べく, but for a serious/grand purpose; not for daily matters. Can modify nouns: 〜んがための + N (often critical). Dramatic purposes (生きんがため, 無罪を証明せんがため); criticism (売らんがための).
〜をもって (means)~o motte By means of ~ N + をもって 硬. Abstract/formal means only (書面, 努力, 実力) — not everyday tools. 〜をもってすれば = praise + positive conclusion. Official notices and letters (書面をもってご連絡します).
〜をもって (end point, 2課)~o motte As of / with (this point, it ends) N (time) + をもって Same shape, different job: marks the moment something ends or starts officially. 本日をもって閉店いたします.
〜ため(に) (N2 〔復習〕)~tame (ni) In order to ~ V-辞書形／N の + ため Neutral, any register. Still needs a volitional verb and the same subject. Everyday speech and writing.
〜ように (added, not in book)~you ni So that ~ V-辞書形／ない形 (potential, non-volitional) The fallback when the verb is potential (できる) or the subjects differ — where べく/んがため fail. だれもが参加できるように ([1〜3] Q1).
〜によって (N2 〔復習〕)~ni yotte By means of ~ N + によって Neutral "means"; works for concrete methods too (通信手段によって). When 〜をもって would be too stiff or the means is concrete.
Exam trap 1 — the banned second clause: after 〜べく and 〜んがため(に) there is no request, advice or question (〜てください, 〜たほうがいい, 〜ですか), and no state (〜がある, 〜が必要だ). If an option switches the subject or describes a state, eliminate it ([1] Q2–Q4, [2] Q2–Q3).
Exam trap 2 — the conjugation of んがため: it uses the ない-stem: 売る→売らん, 保つ→保たん, 示す→示さん, する→せん. ×するんがため and ×しようがため are wrong ([2] Q1). べく, by contrast, takes the plain dictionary form (するべく／すべく).
Exam trap 3 — potential verbs: 〜できる, 〜られる, わかる, 見える are not volitional, so they cannot take 〜べく／〜んがため. The exam puts ×参加できるべく next to the correct 参加できるように ([1〜3] Q1).
