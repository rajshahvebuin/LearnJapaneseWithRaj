1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜を皮切りに(して)・〜を皮切りとして

neutral register
Reading~o kawakiri ni (shite) / ~o kawakiri to shite
Meaning (EN)"starting with ~ (and then one after another…)" — book: 〜から始まって次々に何かをする (beginning from ~, something is done one after another)
Meaning (HI)...से शुरू करके (फिर एक के बाद एक...)
Meaning (GU)...થી શરૂ કરીને (પછી એક પછી એક...)
Connection (接続)名 ＋を皮切りに(して)・を皮切りとして
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; neutral-to-formal, common in news and announcements.
Typical useThe second clause must say that similar actions or events happen one after another and develop/expand. Use it when the chain is clearly one continuous series (一続き). Rarely used for natural phenomena or bad things (自然現象や良くないこと).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋を皮切りに小説を皮切りにshousetsu o kawakiri ni
NounN ＋を皮切りにして東京公演を皮切りにしてToukyou kouen o kawakiri ni shite
NounN ＋を皮切りとして合併を皮切りとしてgappei o kawakiri to shite
Verb(added, not in book) V-辞書形／た形 ＋の ＋を皮切りに — nominalise first優勝したのを皮切りにyuushou shita no o kawakiri ni
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この作家は自分の父親のことを書いた小説を皮切りに、次々に話題作を発表している。Kono sakka wa jibun no chichioya no koto o kaita shousetsu o kawakiri ni, tsugitsugi ni wadaisaku o happyou shite iru. Starting with a novel about his own father, this author has been publishing one much-talked-about work after another. अपने पिता के बारे में लिखे उपन्यास से शुरू करके, यह लेखक एक के बाद एक चर्चित रचनाएँ प्रकाशित कर रहा है। પોતાના પિતા વિશે લખેલી નવલકથાથી શરૂ કરીને, આ લેખક એક પછી એક ચર્ચિત કૃતિઓ પ્રકાશિત કરી રહ્યા છે.
わたしたちのバンドは来月3日の東京公演を皮切りにして、全国ツアーを予定しています。Watashitachi no bando wa raigetsu mikka no Toukyou kouen o kawakiri ni shite, zenkoku tsuaa o yotei shite imasu. Our band is planning a nationwide tour, kicking off with a Tokyo show on the 3rd of next month. हमारा बैंड अगले महीने की 3 तारीख़ के टोक्यो शो से शुरू करके राष्ट्रव्यापी टूर की योजना बना रहा है। અમારું બૅન્ડ આવતા મહિનાની 3 તારીખના ટોક્યો શોથી શરૂ કરીને દેશવ્યાપી ટૂરનું આયોજન કરી રહ્યું છે.
K銀行とM銀行の合併を皮切りとして、ここ数年企業の合併・統合が相次いで行われている。K-ginkou to M-ginkou no gappei o kawakiri to shite, koko suunen kigyou no gappei・tougou ga aitsuide okonawarete iru. Beginning with the merger of K Bank and M Bank, corporate mergers and consolidations have followed one after another in recent years. K बैंक और M बैंक के विलय से शुरू होकर, पिछले कुछ सालों में कंपनियों के विलय-एकीकरण लगातार हो रहे हैं। K બૅન્ક અને M બૅન્કના વિલયથી શરૂ થઈને, છેલ્લાં થોડાં વર્ષોમાં કંપનીઓનાં વિલય-એકીકરણ એક પછી એક થઈ રહ્યાં છે.

皮切り = "the first cut" — the opening move of a series. The noun is the first item, and the rest of the sentence must be the series that follows (次々に, 相次いで, ツアー, 130か所で…). Drill [1] keeps testing this: a single follow-up event, a change of state, or an expectation is not a series. Compare the N2 〜をはじめ(として), which just lists a representative example ("X and others") without the time sequence.

【2】 〜に至るまで

neutral register
Reading~ni itaru made
Meaning (EN)"(right down) to ~; even ~" — book: 〜という意外なことにまで、あることの範囲が及ぶ (the scope of something extends even to the unexpected ~)
Meaning (HI)...तक (यहाँ तक कि ... भी)
Meaning (GU)...સુધી (એટલે સુધી કે ... પણ)
Connection (接続)名 ＋に至るまで
RegisterNeutral; a little formal.
Typical useAttaches to a word for something surprising that normally wouldn't be mentioned, stressing how wide the range is. Often paired with a likely example first (①〜はもちろん) or a starting point of a completely different kind (②〜から).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋に至るまでスカートの長さに至るまでsukaato no nagasa ni itaru made
Noun (range)N1 から N2 ＋に至るまで起床時間から食事開始時間に至るまでkishou jikan kara shokuji kaishi jikan ni itaru made
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used in this meaning — attaches to nouns—

わたしの学校は服装に厳しい。制服の着方はもちろん、ヘアスタイルやスカートの長さに至るまで注意される。Watashi no gakkou wa fukusou ni kibishii. Seifuku no kikata wa mochiron, heasutairu ya sukaato no nagasa ni itaru made chuui sareru. My school is strict about dress. They pick on not only how you wear your uniform but even your hairstyle and the length of your skirt. मेरा स्कूल पहनावे को लेकर सख़्त है। यूनिफ़ॉर्म पहनने के तरीक़े के अलावा, हेयरस्टाइल और स्कर्ट की लंबाई तक पर टोका जाता है। મારી શાળા પહેરવેશ બાબતે કડક છે. યુનિફૉર્મ પહેરવાની રીત તો ખરી જ, હેરસ્ટાઇલ અને સ્કર્ટની લંબાઈ સુધી ટોકવામાં આવે છે.
今度の旅行のスケジュール表は綿密だ。起床時間から飛行機内の食事開始時間に至るまで書いてある。Kondo no ryokou no sukejuuru-hyou wa menmitsu da. Kishou jikan kara hikouki-nai no shokuji kaishi jikan ni itaru made kaite aru. The schedule for this trip is meticulous. Everything is written down, from wake-up time right down to when the in-flight meal starts. इस बार की यात्रा का कार्यक्रम बहुत बारीक है। उठने के समय से लेकर विमान में खाना शुरू होने के समय तक सब लिखा है। આ વખતના પ્રવાસનું સમયપત્રક બહુ ઝીણવટભર્યું છે. ઊઠવાના સમયથી માંડીને વિમાનમાં જમવાનું શરૂ થવાના સમય સુધી બધું લખેલું છે.
父の趣味は料理です。食材も自家製でないと気が済まないらしく、みそ、豆腐に至るまで自分で作ります。Chichi no shumi wa ryouri desu. Shokuzai mo jikasei de nai to ki ga sumanai rashiku, miso, toufu ni itaru made jibun de tsukurimasu. My father's hobby is cooking. He apparently isn't satisfied unless even the ingredients are homemade — he makes everything himself, right down to the miso and tofu. पिताजी का शौक़ खाना बनाना है। लगता है सामग्री भी घर की बनी न हो तो उन्हें चैन नहीं — मिसो और टोफ़ू तक वे ख़ुद बनाते हैं। પિતાજીનો શોખ રસોઈ છે. લાગે છે કે સામગ્રી પણ ઘરની બનાવેલી ન હોય તો તેમને ચેન નથી પડતું — મિસો અને ટોફુ સુધી તેઓ જાતે બનાવે છે.

The noun after which に至るまで sits is the far end of the range — the thing you'd least expect to be included (skirt length, in-flight meal time, homemade tofu). So in drill [2] the answer is always the most surprising, most extreme item, and in a から〜に至るまで pair the two ends should be as far apart as possible (ベッドからスプーン, not 皿からカップ).

【3】 〜を限りに

neutral register
Reading~o kagiri ni
Meaning (EN)"as of ~ (and no longer); with ~ as the last" — book: 〜の時までで、それまで続いていたことを終わりにする、と宣言する (declares that something that had continued up to then ends at ~)
Meaning (HI)...को आख़िरी मानकर / ...से (आगे) बंद
Meaning (GU)...ને છેલ્લું ગણીને / ...થી (આગળ) બંધ
Connection (接続)名 ＋を限りに
RegisterNeutral — usable for personal resolutions as well as public notices.
Typical useUsually attaches to time words (今日・今回・本年度 など). The second clause states that something that had been continuing stops.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)N ＋を限りに本年度を限りにhonnendo o kagiri ni
Noun (これ)これ ＋を限りにこれを限りにkore o kagiri ni
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
本年度を限りにこの講座の受講生募集を行わないことになりました。Honnendo o kagiri ni kono kouza no jukousei boshuu o okonawanai koto ni narimashita. It has been decided that, after this fiscal year, this course will no longer recruit students. यह तय हुआ है कि इस वित्त-वर्ष के बाद इस कोर्स के लिए छात्रों की भर्ती नहीं होगी। એવું નક્કી થયું છે કે આ નાણાકીય વર્ષ પછી આ કોર્સ માટે વિદ્યાર્થીઓની ભરતી નહીં થાય.
今日を限りにたばこをやめるぞ！Kyou o kagiri ni tabako o yameru zo! As of today, I'm giving up smoking! आज से सिगरेट बंद — पक्का! આજથી સિગારેટ બંધ — પાકું!
これを限りにお前とは親子の縁を切る。以後親でもなく、子でもない。Kore o kagiri ni omae to wa oyako no en o kiru. Igo oya de mo naku, ko de mo nai. As of now I'm cutting all ties with you as parent and child. From here on, I'm not your parent and you're not my child. आज के बाद तुमसे माँ-बाप और बच्चे का रिश्ता ख़त्म। आगे से न मैं तुम्हारा पिता, न तुम मेरी संतान। આજ પછી તારી સાથે મા-બાપ અને સંતાનનો સંબંધ પૂરો. હવેથી ન હું તારો પિતા, ન તું મારું સંતાન.

The key word is 宣言 (declaration): someone announces that a continuing thing ends at that point. So the second clause must be an ending (やめる, 辞める, 閉店する, 募集しない) — not a beginning or an ongoing state. Don't add まで (×今月までを限りに): 限り already marks the end point. Compare N2 〜限りで (今年限りで辞めます), which is very close.

【4】 〜をもって

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~o motte
Meaning (EN)"as of ~; with ~ (we hereby end…)" — book: 〜の時までで、ある行事やそれまで続いていたことを終わりにする、と宣言する (declares that an event, or something that had continued, ends at ~). Cross-ref → 11課【3】 (〜をもって as a means).
Meaning (HI)...के साथ (समाप्त) / ...से (औपचारिक घोषणा)
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે (સમાપ્ત) / ...થી (ઔપચારિક જાહેરાત)

Register硬 — official, formal. Humble/polite variant: 〜をもちまして. Not used for everyday personal matters.
Typical useAttaches to words for a time or deadline (時・期日). Typical in ceremonies, notices and announcements: 終わります, 締め切ります, 閉店させていただきました.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time/date)N ＋をもって2月20日をもってnigatsu hatsuka o motte
Noun (polite)N ＋をもちまして9月末日をもちましてkugatsu matsujitsu o mochimashite

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
これをもって第35回卒業証書授与式を終わります。Kore o motte dai sanjuugo-kai sotsugyou shousho juyoshiki o owarimasu. With this, we conclude the 35th graduation ceremony. इसी के साथ 35वाँ दीक्षांत (प्रमाणपत्र वितरण) समारोह समाप्त होता है। આ સાથે 35મો પદવીદાન (પ્રમાણપત્ર વિતરણ) સમારંભ પૂરો થાય છે.
2月20日をもって願書受け付けを締め切ります。遅れないように出してください。Nigatsu hatsuka o motte gansho uketsuke o shimekirimasu. Okurenai you ni dashite kudasai. Applications close as of February 20. Please submit yours on time. 20 फ़रवरी को आवेदन स्वीकार करना बंद होगा। कृपया देर किए बिना जमा करें। 20 ફેબ્રુઆરીએ અરજીઓ સ્વીકારવાનું બંધ થશે. કૃપા કરી મોડું કર્યા વિના જમા કરો.
当店は9月末日をもちまして閉店させていただきました。長い間のご利用ありがとうございました。Touten wa kugatsu matsujitsu o mochimashite heiten sasete itadakimashita. Nagai aida no goriyou arigatou gozaimashita. This shop closed its doors as of the last day of September. Thank you for your patronage over the years. हमारी दुकान 30 सितंबर से बंद कर दी गई है। लंबे समय तक सेवा का अवसर देने के लिए धन्यवाद। અમારી દુકાન સપ્ટેમ્બરના છેલ્લા દિવસથી બંધ કરવામાં આવી છે. લાંબા સમય સુધી સેવાનો લાભ આપવા બદલ આભાર.

Same "end at this point" meaning as 〜を限りに, but stiffer and official: a chairperson closing a meeting, a shop's notice, an exam office. That's the deciding factor in the mixed set: a personal resolution (今日を限りに車は運転しない) is を限りに, a public announcement (ただ今をもって販売を打ち切らせていただきます) is をもって. Set phrase: 以上をもちまして… ("with that, we conclude…").

【5】 〜といったところだ

neutral register
Reading~to itta tokoro da
Meaning (EN)"at most about ~; ~ or so, that's all" — book: 程度は最高でも〜で、あまり高くない (the degree is ~ at the very most — not very high)

Even on the hottest days it's only about 26 or 27 degrees. यहाँ गर्मियों में भी ज़्यादा गर्मी नहीं होती। सबसे गर्म दिन में भी बस 26-27 डिग्री के आसपास रहता है। અહીં ઉનાળામાં પણ બહુ ગરમી નથી પડતી. સૌથી ગરમ દિવસે પણ બસ 26-27 ડિગ્રી આસપાસ રહે છે.

This hiking club goes hiking every month, but each time there are six or seven participants at most. यह पर्वतारोहण क्लब हर महीने पहाड़ों पर घूमने जाता है, पर हर बार ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा 6-7 लोग ही आते हैं। આ પર્વત-પ્રવાસ ક્લબ દર મહિને પહાડોમાં ફરવા જાય છે, પણ દર વખતે વધુમાં વધુ 6-7 લોકો જ આવે છે.
休みがあってもほとんど遠出はしません。せいぜい1泊で温泉に行くといったところでしょうか。Yasumi ga atte mo hotondo toode wa shimasen. Seizei ippaku de onsen ni iku to itta tokoro deshou ka. Even when I have time off, I hardly ever travel far. At most I might go to a hot spring for one night, I suppose. छुट्टी होने पर भी मैं शायद ही दूर कहीं जाता हूँ। ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा एक रात के लिए गर्म पानी के झरने (ओनसेन) तक जाना, बस। રજા હોય તો પણ હું ભાગ્યે જ દૂર ક્યાંય જાઉં છું. વધુમાં વધુ એક રાત માટે ગરમ પાણીના ઝરા (ઓનસેન) સુધી જવું, બસ.

The speaker is playing down an amount: "it's only about this much". So it pairs with せいぜい (at most) and clashes with emphasis on largeness (40分も), with せめて ("at least" — a wish), and with 最低でも ("at minimum"). The drill [1〜5] Q5 distractor 限りで is the 〔復習〕 N2 form ("until the end of a period") — a time limit, not a small amount.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three "boundary" ideas in one lesson: where a series starts (を皮切りに), how far a range reaches (に至るまで), and where something ends (を限りに・をもって) — plus a "not more than" form (といったところだ). The mixed set [1〜5] is built on these contrasts:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜を皮切りに(して)・〜を皮切りとして~o kawakiri ni (shite) / ~o kawakiri to shite Starting with ~ (then one after another) N + を皮切りに Start of a developing series of similar events. Not for nature or bad things. Tours, launches, a run of results (東京公演を皮切りに全国ツアー).
〜をはじめ(として) (N2)~o hajime (to shite) ~ and others; starting with ~ N + をはじめ Picks a representative example from a group; no time sequence needed. Listing: 桜をはじめいろいろな花.
〜に至るまで~ni itaru made Right down to ~ (surprising) N + に至るまで (often 〜から〜に至るまで／〜はもちろん〜に至るまで) Marks the far, unexpected end of a range to stress how wide it is. Showing thoroughness (スカートの長さに至るまで).
〜を限りに~o kagiri ni As of ~ (no longer) N(time) + を限りに Declares an end; neutral — fine for personal resolutions. 今日を限りにたばこをやめる.
〜をもって~o motte As of ~ (hereby ends) N(time/date) + をもって／をもちまして Same end-point meaning but 硬, official; not for daily personal matters. Ceremonies, notices: これをもって閉会, 3月末日をもって停止.
〜限りで (N2)~kagiri de Until (the end of) ~ N(time) + 限りで Everyday N2 relative of を限りに (今年限りで辞めます). Plain statements of a final period.
〜といったところだ~to itta tokoro da At most about ~ N／V-辞書形 + といったところだ Downplays an amount; pairs with せいぜい, clashes with 〜も, せめて, 最低でも. "Only about this much" (せいぜい6、7人といったところ).
Exam trap 1 — start vs. end: を皮切りに opens a series that is still unfolding (連日熱戦が繰り広げられている); を限りに／をもって close something. If the sentence end shows things continuing and spreading, it's を皮切りに ([1〜5] Q1).
Exam trap 2 — を限りに vs. をもって (register): both end things at a time point, but をもって is official/formal and not for everyday personal decisions. Casual 〜んです + personal resolution → を限りに; 〜させていただきます + public notice → をもって ([1〜5] Q2–3).
Exam trap 3 — big vs. small: に至るまで exaggerates scope (everything, even X); といったところだ shrinks it (only X at most). Look at the sentence end: ないものはない → に至るまで; あまり役に立たない → といったところで ([1〜5] Q4–5).
