Answer key
All answers (練習1 ×6, 練習2 ×25 particle write-ins, まとめ ×5) are confirmed from the official 別冊 解答 p.6 (PDF page 198). The 別冊 gives answers only; the "Why" explanations are mine.
Notes
Basic rule: が = new information / something the reader can't yet identify; は = already mentioned / identifiable. The exceptions are what N1 tests: は for famous or obviously known topics and for things related to an earlier topic (理由は, 監督は) or for contrast; が for a new turn in the story, inside a 「〜は〜が」 sentence, and in news-style reports. Per the site rule for passages, only the sentence containing each blank is quoted; the rest is summarised in my own words.
Book introduction (p.148), in my words: は and が have different basic jobs and different roles in a text; to make a text hang together you must choose between them correctly.
HI: は और が के मूल काम अलग हैं और लेख में उनकी भूमिका भी अलग है; लेख को सुसंगत बनाने के लिए दोनों में सही चुनाव ज़रूरी है।
GU: は અને が નાં મૂળ કામ અલગ છે અને લખાણમાં તેમની ભૂમિકા પણ અલગ છે; લખાણને સુસંગત બનાવવા બંને વચ્ચે સાચી પસંદગી જરૂરી છે.

1 Grammar Points

One block per section of the book. These are particle-choice rules, not conjugations, so there is no formation table. The underlined phrase in each 例 is shown in bold.

【基本】 「は」と「が」の基本的機能 — topic vs subject

neutral
Rule (book)「は」主題（何について話すか）を示す。「が」主格（動作・事態の主体）を示す。"Wa" shudai (nani ni tsuite hanasu ka) o shimesu. "Ga" shukaku (dousa・jitai no shutai) o shimesu.
Rule (EN)は marks the topic (what we're talking about); が marks the subject (who/what does the action or is in the state).
Rule (HI)は विषय (किस बारे में बात हो रही है) दिखाता है; が कर्ता (कौन काम करता है या किस स्थिति में है) दिखाता है।
Rule (GU)は વિષય (કોના વિશે વાત થાય છે) દર્શાવે છે; が કર્તા (કોણ કામ કરે છે કે કઈ સ્થિતિમાં છે) દર્શાવે છે.
Typical useOne sentence can have both: [topic]は [subject]が [predicate].
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この部屋は午後課長が使うことになっている。Kono heya wa gogo kachou ga tsukau koto ni natte iru. As for this room, the section chief is to use it in the afternoon. यह कमरा — दोपहर में सेक्शन चीफ़ इस्तेमाल करेंगे, ऐसा तय है। આ ઓરડો — બપોરે સેક્શન ચીફ વાપરશે, એવું નક્કી છે.

この部屋は = topic (what we're talking about); 課長が = the one who uses it.

【A】 文章の中での「は」と「が」の基本的用法 — new vs known

neutral
Rule (book)「が」初めて話題に出たもの、または読み手には特定できないと考えられるものを示す。「は」すでに話題に出たもの、または読み手に特定できると考えられるものを示す。"Ga" hajimete wadai ni deta mono, mata wa yomite ni wa tokutei dekinai to kangaerareru mono o shimesu. "Wa" sude ni wadai ni deta mono, mata wa yomite ni tokutei dekiru to kangaerareru mono o shimesu.
Rule (EN)が marks something mentioned for the first time, or something the reader presumably can't identify. は marks something already mentioned, or something the reader can presumably identify.
Rule (HI)が पहली बार ज़िक्र में आई चीज़ या ऐसी चीज़ दिखाता है जिसे पाठक पहचान न सके। は पहले से ज़िक्र में आई या पाठक द्वारा पहचानी जा सकने वाली चीज़ दिखाता है।
Rule (GU)が પહેલી વાર ઉલ્લેખમાં આવેલી વસ્તુ કે એવી વસ્તુ દર્શાવે છે જેને વાચક ઓળખી ન શકે. は અગાઉ ઉલ્લેખમાં આવેલી કે વાચક ઓળખી શકે એવી વસ્તુ દર્શાવે છે.
Typical use〜に〜がある／いる (introduction) → その〜は… (follow-up).

Book example, summarised (not the book's text): a short paragraph introduces a tourist spot in northern Kantō (Oze), the writer's mother who visits it every year, the woman who runs a mountain hut there, its five young staff, the many visitors, and people who ask to work there. 母 is new in the text but the reader knows whose mother (the writer's); 小屋の従業員 is new but identifiable because the hut was already mentioned. That's why both take は.

【B】 初めて話題に出たものであっても「は」を使う場合 — は even at first mention

neutral
Rule (book)1. 多くの人が注目していると思われる話題や、読み手も当然知っていると考えられる話題のとき　2. すでに話題に出たものに関係があるとき　3. 二つのことを対比させるとき1. ooku no hito ga chuumoku shite iru to omowareru wadai ya, yomite mo touzen shitte iru to kangaerareru wadai no toki　2. sude ni wadai ni deta mono ni kankei ga aru toki　3. futatsu no koto o taihi saseru toki
Rule (EN)Use は even for a first mention: (1) a topic many people are watching or the reader surely knows (首相, 富士山); (2) something related to what's already been mentioned (理由は = the reason for it); (3) when contrasting two things.
Rule (HI)पहली बार ज़िक्र होने पर भी は: (1) ऐसा विषय जिस पर सबकी नज़र हो या पाठक ज़रूर जानता हो (प्रधानमंत्री, फ़ूजी पर्वत); (2) पहले कही बात से जुड़ी चीज़ (理由は = उसका कारण); (3) दो बातों की तुलना करते समय।
Rule (GU)પહેલી વાર ઉલ્લેખ થાય તોય は: (1) એવો વિષય જેના પર સૌની નજર હોય કે વાચક જરૂર જાણતો હોય (વડાપ્રધાન, ફૂજી પર્વત); (2) અગાઉ કહેલી વાત સાથે જોડાયેલી વસ્તુ (理由は = તેનું કારણ); (3) બે વાતોની સરખામણી કરતી વખતે.
Typical useNewspaper leads (首相は…); 理由は／題名は／監督は after an event; AはXだ。だが、Bは…
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1. 首相は4日、台風による被害状況を視察するために現地を訪れた。（新聞記事）Shushou wa yokka, taifuu ni yoru higai joukyou o shisatsu suru tame ni genchi o otozureta. (shinbun kiji) On the 4th, the Prime Minister visited the area to inspect typhoon damage. (newspaper) प्रधानमंत्री 4 तारीख़ को तूफ़ान से हुए नुक़सान का जायज़ा लेने वहाँ पहुँचे। (अख़बार) વડાપ્રધાન 4 તારીખે વાવાઝોડાથી થયેલા નુકસાનની સમીક્ષા કરવા ત્યાં પહોંચ્યા. (અખબાર)
1. 多くの人に知られている名所ではあるが、世界遺産に登録されていないところがある。富士山はその一例である。Ooku no hito ni shirarete iru meisho de wa aru ga, sekai isan ni touroku sarete inai tokoro ga aru. Fujisan wa sono ichirei de aru. Some places are famous landmarks yet not registered as World Heritage. Mt Fuji is one example. कुछ जगहें मशहूर तो हैं, पर विश्व धरोहर में दर्ज नहीं हैं। फ़ूजी पर्वत उसका एक उदाहरण है। કેટલીક જગ્યાઓ જાણીતી તો છે, પણ વિશ્વ વારસામાં નોંધાયેલી નથી. ફૂજી પર્વત તેનું એક ઉદાહરણ છે.
2. 明日の三者会談は中止だそうだ。理由は、その問題についての調査が完了していないからとのことである。Ashita no sansha kaidan wa chuushi da sou da. Riyuu wa, sono mondai ni tsuite no chousa ga kanryou shite inai kara to no koto de aru. Tomorrow's three-way talks are reportedly cancelled. The reason, apparently, is that the investigation into the issue isn't finished. (理由 = the reason for the cancellation) सुना है कल की त्रिपक्षीय वार्ता रद्द हो गई। कारण यह बताया गया कि उस मुद्दे की जाँच पूरी नहीं हुई। સાંભળ્યું છે કે કાલની ત્રિપક્ષીય વાટાઘાટ રદ થઈ. કારણ એ જણાવાયું કે એ મુદ્દાની તપાસ પૂરી થઈ નથી.
2. 女優の芝陽子が久々に映画に出る。題名は「風」。監督は若手の新人だが、期待されている。Joyuu no Shiba Youko ga hisabisa ni eiga ni deru. Daimei wa "Kaze". Kantoku wa wakate no shinjin da ga, kitai sarete iru. Actress Shiba Yōko is in a film for the first time in ages. The title is "Kaze". The director is a young newcomer, but much is expected of him/her. अभिनेत्री शिबा योको बहुत दिनों बाद फ़िल्म में आ रही हैं। नाम है "कज़े"। निर्देशक एक नया युवा है, पर उससे बड़ी उम्मीदें हैं। અભિનેત્રી શિબા યોકો ઘણા સમય પછી ફિલ્મમાં આવે છે. નામ છે "કાઝે". દિગ્દર્શક એક નવો યુવાન છે, પણ તેની પાસેથી મોટી અપેક્ષાઓ છે.
3. 日本語には「もったいない」という言葉があるのだから、若い世代も節約の大切さは理解できると思う。しかし、節約の方法はまだ十分に考えられていないようだ。Nihongo ni wa "mottainai" to iu kotoba ga aru no da kara, wakai sedai mo setsuyaku no taisetsusa wa rikai dekiru to omou. Shikashi, setsuyaku no houhou wa mada juubun ni kangaerarete inai you da. Since Japanese has the word "mottainai", I think young people can understand the importance of saving. But ways of saving don't seem to have been thought through yet. जापानी में "मोत्ताइनाई" शब्द है, इसलिए मुझे लगता है युवा पीढ़ी भी बचत का महत्त्व समझ सकती है। पर बचत के तरीक़ों पर अभी पूरा विचार नहीं हुआ लगता। જાપાનીમાં "મોત્તાઇનાઇ" શબ્દ છે, એટલે મને લાગે છે કે યુવા પેઢી પણ બચતનું મહત્ત્વ સમજી શકે. પણ બચતની રીતો પર હજી પૂરો વિચાર થયો નથી લાગતો.
3. 林氏は政治理念は立派だ。だが、具体的な政策は不透明だ。Hayashi-shi wa seiji rinen wa rippa da. Daga, gutaiteki na seisaku wa futoumei da. Mr Hayashi's political ideals are admirable. But his concrete policies are unclear. हायाशी जी के राजनीतिक आदर्श तो बढ़िया हैं। पर ठोस नीतियाँ अस्पष्ट हैं। હાયાશીના રાજકીય આદર્શો તો ઉત્તમ છે. પણ નક્કર નીતિઓ અસ્પષ્ટ છે.

Type 2 is the most tested: after an event is introduced, its parts (理由・題名・監督・原因・結果) take は because the reader knows "the X of what we just mentioned".

【C】 すでに話題に出たものであっても「が」を使う場合 — が even when already mentioned

neutral
Rule (book)1. 出来事が新しく展開するとき・前の文章とは流れが変わるとき　2. 話題を「は」で取り上げた文の中で、その話題について述べるとき：「〜は〜が」文（話題が書き手と読み手の間で明らかなときは、省略されることもある。）　3. 出来事の報告をするとき・ニュース性がある話題を述べるとき1. dekigoto ga atarashiku tenkai suru toki・mae no bunshou to wa nagare ga kawaru toki　2. wadai o "wa" de toriageta bun no naka de, sono wadai ni tsuite noberu toki: "~wa ~ga" bun (wadai ga kakite to yomite no aida de akiraka na toki wa, shouryaku sareru koto mo aru.)　3. dekigoto no houkoku o suru toki・nyuususei ga aru wadai o noberu toki
Rule (EN)Use が even for something already mentioned: (1) when the story takes a new turn or the flow changes; (2) inside a 「〜は〜が」 sentence, to say something about the は-topic (the は part may be dropped when obvious); (3) when reporting an event or something newsworthy.
Rule (HI)पहले ज़िक्र होने पर भी が: (1) जब कहानी नया मोड़ ले या प्रवाह बदले; (2) 「〜は〜が」 वाक्य में, は वाले विषय के बारे में कुछ कहते समय (विषय साफ़ हो तो は वाला हिस्सा छूट भी सकता है); (3) किसी घटना की सूचना या ख़बर जैसी बात बताते समय।
Rule (GU)અગાઉ ઉલ્લેખ થયો હોય તોય が: (1) જ્યારે વાર્તા નવો વળાંક લે કે પ્રવાહ બદલાય; (2) 「〜は〜が」 વાક્યમાં, は વાળા વિષય વિશે કંઈ કહેતી વખતે (વિષય સ્પષ્ટ હોય તો は વાળો ભાગ છૂટી પણ શકે); (3) કોઈ ઘટનાની જાણ કે સમાચાર જેવી વાત કહેતી વખતે.
Typical useある年、夫が突然…; [topic]は 内容が／値段が／帰宅時間が…; 開花日が早くなっている.

1. ある年、夫が突然「旅館を閉じよう」と言い出した。Aru toshi, otto ga totsuzen "Ryokan o tojiyou" to iidashita.

(Today's session) was packed with content and finished after five. आज दोपहर से आदरसूचक भाषा के प्रयोग पर प्रशिक्षण हुआ। (आज के प्रशिक्षण में) विषय-वस्तु बहुत थी, और वह पाँच बजे के बाद ख़त्म हुआ। આજે બપોરથી માનવાચક ભાષાના ઉપયોગ પર તાલીમ થઈ. (આજની તાલીમમાં) સામગ્રી ભરપૂર હતી, અને તે પાંચ વાગ્યા પછી પૂરી થઈ.
2. 省エネ機能が優れている製品が次々に開発されている。（省エネ機能が優れている製品は）電気代が安くて済み、そのため大人気なのである。Shouene kinou ga sugurete iru seihin ga tsugitsugi ni kaihatsu sarete iru. (Shouene kinou ga sugurete iru seihin wa) denkidai ga yasukute sumi, sono tame daininki na no de aru. Products with excellent energy-saving features are being developed one after another. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The choices this lesson's exercises test. Rule of thumb: first check the clause type (inside a modifying or embedded clause → が), then ask new vs known, then look for the exceptions (famous topic, related part, contrast → は; new turn, news, 〜は〜が → が).

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
N が (new)ga first mention / unidentifiable 〜に N がある／いる Introduces something (図書館がある, 女性がいた, 大勢の人が). The reader meets it for the first time.
N は (known)wa already mentioned / identifiable N は + comment Follows up on something introduced (女性は白いコート姿で), or the reader knows it anyway (母は). Commenting on an established topic.
part-of-topic はriyuu wa / shomei wa "the X of it" 理由・題名・監督・震源地・書名 + は New word, but tied to the event just mentioned (B-2). Details of a news item, a film, a book.
famous/obvious はshushou wa / keisatsu wa topic everyone knows N は First mention but a public figure or obvious party (B-1). News leads, commentary.
「〜は〜が」~wa ~ga topic + property (T は) N が + adj／V The が part describes the topic; the は part can be omitted (写真がきれい, 後ろ姿がすてき) (C-2). Describing a feature of something.
が in subordinate clausesjuuzokusetsu no ga subject inside a clause 〜が V-る N／〜が V-たのは／〜が V-るか Modifying, nominalised and embedded-question clauses take が, never は (仕事が多い奥さん, 身体が対応したのは, 源が見つかるか). Always — check this first.
が for a new turntenkai no ga "and then…" ある年、N が… Already-known person/thing does something new (夫が突然…, その母が…, プロジェクトが進んでいない) (C-1). Story development, surprises.
Exam trap 1 — は inside a clause: options like 「〜は見つかるか」「身体は…のは」 look natural in English but break the rule that a subordinate clause's subject is が (まとめ ［3］［4］).
Exam trap 2 — "already mentioned" doesn't always mean は: 夫が突然言い出した, その母が毎朝…, それが脂肪のベルトだ — が marks a new development or the answer/punch line (C-1).
Exam trap 3 — 問題は／理由は＋identification: 「問題は〜場合である」「理由は〜からだ」 — B-2 は followed by a 〜だ／である definition. 問題が needs a verb like ある／起こる (まとめ ［2］).
