Notes
Two families: strong feelings (〜てやまない, 〜に堪えない, 〜を禁じ得ない — "I can't help feeling ~") and being forced / it is inevitable (〜ないではすまない, 〜ないではおかない, 〜を余儀なくされる／させる). Every point except the plain 〜ないではすまない is tagged 硬 formal/written (硬い言い方) in the book. The exam tests what attaches (limited verbs/nouns of emotion), who the subject is (first person vs. person vs. thing), and the ず-form of する (せず). Drills use 3 options (a–c).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜てやまない

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~te yamanai
Meaning (EN)"never cease to ~; ~ from the bottom of my heart" — book: 〜という強い気持ちをずっと持ち続けている (keeps holding the strong feeling ~ all along)
Meaning (HI)हमेशा/दिल से ...करता रहता हूँ (यह भावना कभी ख़त्म नहीं होती)
Meaning (GU)હંમેશાં/દિલથી ...કરતો રહું છું (આ લાગણી ક્યારેય ખૂટતી નથી)
Connection (接続)動 て形 + やまない
Register硬 — speeches, greetings in writing, formal messages (卒業式, 年賀状).
Typical useAttaches only to a limited set of verbs of mental state (願う・期待する・愛する etc.). Not used with verbs of a temporary feeling (失望する・腹を立てる etc.). The subject is usually first person but is often not stated.

やむ = "to stop", so literally "doesn't stop ~ing". The trick is the verb list: 愛する, 願う, 期待する, 尊敬する, 後悔する, 祈る… — feelings you hold. Anger, disappointment or a passing mood (怒る, 腹が立つ, 失望する, 思い出す) don't work — that is exactly what drill [1] tests.

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~ni taenai
Meaning (EN)"I am overwhelmed with ~; I can't contain my ~" — book: 〜という感情が抑えられないほど強い (the feeling ~ is so strong it cannot be held back)
Meaning (HI)...की भावना से अभिभूत हूँ / ...को रोक नहीं पा रहा
Meaning (GU)...ની લાગણીથી અભિભૂત છું / ...ને રોકી શકતો નથી

Bare noun + に堪えない — no する, no こと (drill [2] Q1: 感謝に堪えません, not ×感謝するに堪えません). Also the noun must be a feeling: 同情, 悲しみ, 喜び work; 気の毒 (a な-adjective), 驚き (a momentary reaction) and 涙 (a thing, not a feeling) do not.

【3】 〜ないではすまない・〜ずにはすまない

neutral (〜ないではすまない) · 〜ずにはすまない = 硬
Reading~nai de wa sumanai / ~zu ni wa sumanai
Meaning (EN)"(you) can't get away without ~; ~ is unavoidable" — book: その場の状況や社会的な常識を考えると、必ず〜することは避けられない (given the situation or social common sense, doing ~ cannot be avoided)
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना काम नहीं चलेगा / ...करना ही पड़ेगा (हालात/समाज के नियम के कारण)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે / ...કરવું જ પડશે (સંજોગો/સમાજના નિયમને કારણે)
Connection (接続)動 ない形 + ではすまない　／　動 ない + ずにはすまない　＊例外 する→せず
Register〜ないではすまない is neutral; the book marks only 〜ずにはすまない → 硬い言い方. Negative-conjecture form: 〜ずにはすむまい.
Typical useThe obligation comes from the situation or social norms. Hard to use when the speaker feels they must do it out of personal feeling (個人の感情からそうしなければならないと考える場合には使いにくい) — for that, use 〜ずにはいられない.

する (exception)する → せずにはすまない借金をせずにはすむまいshakkin o sezu ni wa sumumai

If you keep a dog in an apartment, even if you think you're doing it secretly, the neighbours are bound to find out. फ़्लैट में कुत्ता पालोगे तो चाहे चुपके से पाल रहे हो, पड़ोसियों को पता चले बिना नहीं रहेगा। ફ્લૅટમાં કૂતરો પાળશો તો ભલે છાનામાના પાળતા હો, પાડોશીઓને ખબર પડ્યા વગર નહીં રહે.

すむ = "to be settled / to get by", so "it won't be settled without ~". Two traps: (1) the clause must be what common sense demands (apologise, compensate, explain) — "not forgetting" or "not hiding responsibility" are already negative and don't fit drill [3] Q1; (2) the ず-form: 弁償する → 弁償せず, never ×弁償しず (drill [3] Q2).

【4】 〜ないではおかない・〜ずにはおかない

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~nai de wa okanai / ~zu ni wa okanai
Meaning (EN)(a) "I will definitely make sure ~"; (b) "it inevitably makes (one) ~" — book: 〜しないままでは許さない・自然に必ず〜する (won't let it stay un-done / naturally and inevitably does ~)
Meaning (HI)(क) ...करवाकर ही छोड़ूँगा; (ख) ...किए बिना नहीं रहता (स्वाभाविक रूप से ज़रूर)
Meaning (GU)(ક) ...કરાવીને જ છોડીશ; (ખ) ...કર્યા વગર રહેતું નથી (સ્વાભાવિક રીતે જરૂર)
Connection (接続)動 ない形 + ではおかない　／　動 ない + ずにはおかない　＊例外 する→せず
Register硬 — both forms.
Typical useExpresses either the speaker's strong determination to make sure ~ happens, or the inevitability that a situation will naturally come about. In determination sentences the subject is first person; in inevitability sentences the subject is an inanimate thing or someone other than the speaker. Often used with causatives (白状させない, 抱かせず, 揺さぶらず).

Hearing the culprit's statement, I couldn't suppress my anger at the selfishness of his motive. अपराधी का बयान सुनकर, उसके अपराध के स्वार्थी मक़सद पर मैं ग़ुस्सा रोक नहीं सका। ગુનેગારનું નિવેદન સાંભળીને, તેના ગુનાના સ્વાર્થી હેતુ પર હું ગુસ્સો રોકી ન શક્યો.

禁じ得ない = "cannot forbid (oneself)". Very close to 〜に堪えない, but there are two differences worth remembering: を禁じ得ない takes 涙 and 驚き (which に堪えない does not), and it is a reaction to something seen or heard, while に堪えない is often used in polite speeches for 感謝・感激. Always a bare noun: 同情を禁じ得ない, not ×同情するを (drill [5] Q1).

[1〜6] Q6

長年探し続けてきた肉親がついに見つかったことに（　）。Naganen sagashitsuzukete kita nikushin ga tsui ni mitsukatta koto ni (___).

Full sentence: 長年探し続けてきた肉親がついに見つかったことに喜びを禁じ得ない。Naganen sagashitsuzukete kita nikushin ga tsui ni mitsukatta koto ni yorokobi o kinjienai.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three "I can't help feeling" forms and three "it can't be avoided" forms. The mixed set [1〜6] makes you choose by checking (1) what attaches and (2) where the pressure comes from:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜てやまない~te yamanai Never cease to ~ (feel) V-て (愛する・願う・期待する…) A lasting feeling; temporary emotions (怒る, 失望する) ✗. Speeches, wishes for others: 幸せを願ってやみません.
〜に堪えない~ni taenai Overwhelmed with ~ N (感謝・感激・同情・喜び・悲しみ) Bare emotion noun; polite set phrases. V + に堪えない = 19課 "unbearable to ~". Thanks, condolences: 感謝に堪えません.
〜を禁じ得ない~o kinjienai Can't suppress ~ N (涙・怒り・驚き・同情) Feeling as a reaction to something seen/heard; takes 涙 and 驚き. Written reactions: 怒りを禁じ得なかった.
〜ないではすまない・〜ずにはすまない~nai de wa sumanai / ~zu ni wa sumanai Can't get away without ~ V-ない ＋ では／V-ない ＋ ずには (せず) Pressure from situation / social norms; not personal feelings. Apologies, compensation, unavoidable steps.
〜ないではおかない・〜ずにはおかない~nai de wa okanai / ~zu ni wa okanai Will surely ~ / inevitably makes ~ V-ない ＋ では／V-ない ＋ ずには Speaker's determination (1st person) or a thing's inevitable effect (often causative). Threats/resolve; "this film cannot fail to…".
〜を余儀なくされる／させる~o yogi naku sareru / saseru Be forced to ~ / force ~ N (action noun) Outside force; される = person subject, させる = matter subject. News: 中止・引退・移転を余儀なくされた.
〜ずにはいられない／〜ざるを得ない (N2)~zu ni wa irarenai / ~zaru o enai Can't help ~ / have no choice but to ~ V-ない ＋ ずには／ざるを得ない The 復習 forms: いられない = from inside (personal impulse); ざるを得ない = general "no choice". Everyday speech when the N1 forms feel too stiff.
Exam trap 1 — すまない vs おかない: ask who is pushing. Society/the situation → 〜ずにはすまない ([3], [1〜6] Q3). The speaker's resolve or a thing's natural effect → 〜ずにはおかない ([4], [1〜6] Q2). And for personal urges ("I couldn't help laughing") use the N2 〜ずにはいられない — すまない is awkward there.
Exam trap 2 — the noun after に堪えない／を禁じ得ない: both need a bare emotion noun — no する, no こと, no の ([2] Q1, [5] Q1). 涙・驚き go with を禁じ得ない; 感謝・感激 go with に堪えない; 同情・悲しみ・喜び work with both. A non-emotion noun (中止) fits neither ([1〜6] Q4).
Exam trap 3 — voice and the ず-form: 〜を余儀なくされる (person suffers) vs 〜を余儀なくさせる (cause forces); ×させられる never ([6] Q3). And する → せず: 弁償せずにはすまない, never ×しず ([3] Q2).
