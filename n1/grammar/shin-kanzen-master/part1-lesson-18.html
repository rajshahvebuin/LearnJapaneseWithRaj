Notes
Three of the five forms contain まで, and they are the core trap: までもない = "no need to go as far as ~"; までだ has two meanings decided by the verb form — 辞書形 + までだ = "if all else fails, I'll just ~" (resolve), た形 + までだ = "I only did ~, nothing more" (playing down); ばそれまでだ = "if ~, it's all over". 〜には当たらない tells someone their reaction is excessive, and only 〜でなくてなんだろう(か) is tagged 硬 formal/written (硬い言い方). The "まで" means "going as far as": "it's not worth going as far as doing ~". The verb must be a deliberate act you could choose to skip — 知らせる, 説明する, 聞く. That's why drill [1] Q3 picks 聞く (ask) over わかる (non-volitional) and 見つける (a result, not a choice). The thing must also be minor or obvious: "not important news, so no need to rush to tell everyone".

The verb form decides the meaning. 辞書形 → A, a fallback plan announced calmly (usually after 〜ば／〜なら／〜場合は): "I'll just do X, it's no big deal". た形 → B, modestly shrinking something you already did. In A the verb must be a volitional action of the speaker — "I'll be scolded by my parents" or "it'll cost my parents money" are things that happen to you, not a resolve (drill [2] Q1). In B the act must be small: ちょっと寄った fits, わざわざ顔を見に来た contradicts "nothing more" ([2] Q4).

The ば-clause names the one thing that would wreck everything, and "everything" is what the first half (〜ても) built up: studying all night → falling asleep in the exam; taking a video camera → it isn't charged; giving flowers → they wilt. A condition that is merely inconvenient (a heavy camera) or the normal use of the thing (displaying or looking at flowers) doesn't "ruin it all" — drill [3]. Don't confuse with 〜までだ (A): ばそれまでだ is about a loss, までだ about a fallback.

In his position, it's only natural to say that. मुझे नहीं लगता कि उसकी बात के लिए उसे दोष देना ठीक है। उसकी जगह पर ऐसा कहना स्वाभाविक है। મને નથી લાગતું કે તેની વાત માટે તેને દોષ દેવો યોગ્ય છે. તેની જગ્યાએ એવું કહેવું સ્વાભાવિક છે.
あのホテルのサービスは称賛には当たらない。ホテルならあのくらいは当然だ。Ano hoteru no saabisu wa shousan ni wa ataranai. Hoteru nara ano kurai wa touzen da. That hotel's service doesn't merit praise. For a hotel, that level is only to be expected. उस होटल की सेवा तारीफ़ के लायक़ नहीं। होटल में इतना तो होना ही चाहिए। તે હોટલની સેવા વખાણવા લાયક નથી. હોટલમાં આટલું તો હોવું જ જોઈએ.

Pattern: "X には当たらない. (Reason it's normal/not serious.)" The verb is a reaction: 驚く, 悲観する, 嘆く, 責める, 非難する, 感心する, 称賛する. Verbs like 忘れる, 思い出す, 録音する are not reactions to be restrained, so they fail (drill [4] Q1, Q3). Note it can push down positive evaluations too (感心する, 称賛) — not only negative ones. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Five ways to state a firm view, plus the three N2 forms from the 〔復習〕 box. The まで-forms look alike, so tell them apart by the verb form before them and whether the act is skipped, chosen, or already done:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜までもない~made mo nai No need to ~ V-辞書形 (volitional) The act is skipped because the matter is minor or obvious. 言うまでもなく / 聞くまでもない.
〜までだ (A)~made da (If all else fails) I'll just ~ (〜ば／なら) V-辞書形 + までだ The act is chosen as a calm fallback; present/future only. 失敗したら来年また挑戦するまでだ.
〜までだ (B)~ta made da I merely did ~ V-た + までだ／までです The act is already done and played down; speaker's own act. Modest replies: すべきことをしたまでです.
〜ばそれまでだ~ba sore made da If ~, it's all over V-ば + それまでだ (often after 〜ても) Names the one event that ruins everything. 練習しても本番で失敗すればそれまでだ.
〜には当たらない~ni wa ataranai Nothing to ~ about V-辞書形・Nする (emotion/evaluation) Restrains someone's reaction (positive or negative) as excessive. 驚くには当たらない / 称賛には当たらない.
〜でなくてなんだろう(か)~de nakute nan darou (ka) If not ~, what is it? N (grand word) + でなくてなんだろう 硬; rhetorical, emotional; nouns only. 奇跡でなくてなんだろう.
べきだ／というものだ／にほかならない (N2)beki da / to iu mono da / ni hoka naranai Should / that's what ~ is / nothing but V-辞書形 + べきだ; 普通形 + というものだ; N・から + にほかならない Plain assertions; にほかならない ≈ でなくてなんだろう without the rhetoric. Neutral writing and speech.
Exam trap 1 — までだ: 辞書形 or た形? Look for a past reason or an apology (聞き取れなかったので, 誤解しないで, 見えたので) → た形 + までです (B). Look for a condition (〜たら, 〜なら, 〜場合は) about the future → 辞書形 + までだ (A) ([2] Q3, [1〜5] Q2–3).
Exam trap 2 — までもない vs には当たらない: both say "there's no need", but までもない skips a deliberate action (知らせる, 説明する, 聞く), while には当たらない tones down a reaction (驚く, 悲観する, 非難する, 感心する). 驚くまでもない is wrong ([1〜5] Q1, Q4).
Exam trap 3 — でなくてなんだろう needs a big noun: eliminate verbs, adjectives and plain feelings (うれしさ, 難しいこと). The right noun is a dramatic label for the event — 奇跡, 天才, 天職, 運命的再会, 神への挑戦 ([5], [1〜5] Q5).
