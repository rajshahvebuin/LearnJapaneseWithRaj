1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に足る

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni taru
Meaning (EN)"worthy of ~; enough to ~" — book: あるもの・あることが十分〜できる (something can amply be ~ed; it deserves ~)
Meaning (HI)...के लायक़ / ...करने योग्य
Meaning (GU)...ને લાયક / ...કરવા યોગ્ય
Connection (接続)動 辞書形・名する + に足る
RegisterNot tagged 硬, but written/formal.
Typical useMostly used to modify a noun (〜に足る人物／もの). For the negative, you usually don't put 〜に足らない before the noun; instead you make the end of the sentence negative (② 信頼に足るものとは思えない). ③ 「取るに足らない」 is idiomatic: "not worth taking up; trivial".

次の首相は国民の代表と言うに足る人物であってほしい。Tsugi no shushou wa kokumin no daihyou to iu ni taru jinbutsu de atte hoshii. I hope the next prime minister is someone truly worthy of being called the people's representative. मैं चाहता हूँ कि अगला प्रधानमंत्री ऐसा व्यक्ति हो जिसे सचमुच जनता का प्रतिनिधि कहा जा सके। હું ઇચ્છું છું કે આગામી વડાપ્રધાન એવી વ્યક્તિ હોય જેને ખરેખર જનતાનો પ્રતિનિધિ કહી શકાય.
インターネットで得たその情報は、信頼に足るものとは思えない。Intaanetto de eta sono jouhou wa, shinrai ni taru mono to wa omoenai. I don't think that information you got off the internet is reliable enough to trust. इंटरनेट से मिली वह जानकारी मुझे भरोसे के लायक़ नहीं लगती। ઇન્ટરનેટ પરથી મળેલી તે માહિતી મને ભરોસાપાત્ર લાગતી નથી.
だれかが離婚したとかしないとかなど、取るに足らないニュースだ。Dareka ga rikon shita toka shinai toka nado, toru ni taranai nyuusu da. Whether somebody got divorced or not — that's trivial news. किसका तलाक़ हुआ या नहीं हुआ — ऐसी ख़बरें तो बेमतलब हैं। કોના છૂટાછેડા થયા કે ન થયા — આવા સમાચાર તો નજીવા છે.

The word before に足る names what the thing deserves: 信頼 (trust), 尊敬, 満足, 代表と言う. A plain attribute like 正確 or a counting noun like 調査人数 isn't something data "deserves", so it fails (drill [1] Q1). Verbs go in the plain dictionary form — 人生をかけるに足る, not ×かけられる／×かけている ([1] Q2). And remember the negation rule: ×足らない証拠 → 足る証拠だとは言えない ([1] Q3).

Meaning (EN)"worth ~ing / (so bad that one) can't bear to ~" — book: 〜するだけの価値がある／ひどい状態で〜が我慢できない・〜するだけの価値がない (it's worth ~ing / it's in such a bad state that ~ing is unbearable; not worth ~ing)

優れた児童文学は、大人の鑑賞にも堪えるものだ。Sugureta jidou bungaku wa, otona no kanshou ni mo taeru mono da. Excellent children's literature stands up to being appreciated by adults too. उत्कृष्ट बाल-साहित्य बड़ों के पढ़ने-सराहने लायक़ भी होता है। ઉત્તમ બાળસાહિત્ય મોટાઓ માટે પણ માણવા લાયક હોય છે.
この説はまだ証拠が少なく、詳細な議論に堪えるものではない。Kono setsu wa mada shouko ga sukunaku, shousai na giron ni taeru mono de wa nai. This theory still has little evidence; it can't stand up to detailed debate. इस सिद्धांत के पक्ष में अभी बहुत कम सबूत हैं, यह गहरी बहस के लायक़ नहीं है। આ સિદ્ધાંતના પક્ષમાં હજી બહુ ઓછા પુરાવા છે, તે ઊંડી ચર્ચાને લાયક નથી.
人の悪口は聞くに堪えない。Hito no waruguchi wa kiku ni taenai. I can't bear to listen to people bad-mouthing others. दूसरों की बुराई सुनी नहीं जाती। બીજાની નિંદા સાંભળી નથી જતી.
こういうスキャンダル記事は読むに堪えない。Kou iu sukyandaru kiji wa yomu ni taenai. Scandal articles like this aren't fit to read. ऐसे घोटाले वाले लेख पढ़े नहीं जाते। આવા કૌભાંડવાળા લેખ વાંચ્યા નથી જતા.

Positive 〜に堪える = "holds up under ~" (鑑賞, 批判, 議論, 使用): the thing is good enough to be judged seriously. Negative 〜に堪えない usually goes with 見る／聞く／読む and means something is too awful to look at or hear — so it pairs with ひどい, not 面白い or 易しい (drill [2] Q3). Use the plain dictionary form: 見るに堪えない, not ×見える／×見られる ([2] Q2).

【3】 〜といったらない

話し言葉 conversational
Reading~to ittara nai
Meaning (EN)"so ~ that words can't describe it; incredibly ~" — book: 言葉で言い表せないほど最高に〜だ (~ to the highest degree, beyond words)
Meaning (HI)इतना ~ कि कहा नहीं जा सकता / बेहद ~
Meaning (GU)એટલું ~ કે કહી ન શકાય / અત્યંત ~
Connection (接続)イ形 い・ナ形 (だ)・名 + といったらない
RegisterTagged 話し言葉 (spoken language) in the book.
Typical useExpresses surprise at an extreme degree (good or bad). With nouns, it's usually an adjective turned into a noun with 〜さ (素晴らしさ, 立派さ). As in ③, the 「ない」 can be dropped: 〜といったら…….

富士山の頂上から見た景色の素晴らしさといったら……。いつかきっとまた行きたい。Fujisan no choujou kara mita keshiki no subarashisa to ittara…. Itsuka kitto mata ikitai. The view from the summit of Mt. Fuji — how magnificent it was… I definitely want to go again someday. फ़ूजी पर्वत की चोटी से दिखा नज़ारा — क्या कहूँ, कितना शानदार था… कभी न कभी ज़रूर फिर जाना है। ફૂજી પર્વતની ટોચ પરથી દેખાયેલો નજારો — શું કહું, કેટલો ભવ્ય હતો… ક્યારેક ને ક્યારેક ચોક્કસ ફરી જવું છે.

The fixed ending is ない／なかった — never ×といったらあった (drill [3] Q1). The word before it must be an adjective-type word describing a degree (心細い, 不親切, 怖さ), not a verb phrase or a past-tense adjective (×困ってしまった, ×心配した, ×不安だった) — [3] Q2–3. The sentence that follows often illustrates how extreme it was (二度と乗りたくない, [3] Q4). Compare 14課's 〔復習〕 form 〜といったら ("speaking of ~") — without ない it just introduces a topic.

かぎり = "the limit": "as ~ as I can possibly feel". 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Six evaluation forms plus the three N2 forms from the 〔復習〕 box. The mixed set [1〜6] decides almost everything by what kind of word comes before the form:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に足る~ni taru Worthy of ~ V-辞書形・Nする + に足る + N Modifies a noun; negate at the end (〜に足るものではない). 信頼に足る, 満足に足る, 取るに足らない (idiom).
〜に堪える／堪えない~ni taeru / taenai Holds up to ~ / unbearable to ~ 見る・聞く・読む・鑑賞・批判 + に堪える Closed word list; 堪えない = too awful (ひどい). 鑑賞に堪える / 見るに堪えない.
〜といったらない~to ittara nai ~ beyond words A-い・NA(だ)・N(〜さ) + といったらない 話し言葉; good or bad; ない may drop (〜といったら……); never ×あった. Exclaiming about a degree: 怖さといったらない.
〜かぎりだ~kagiri da I feel extremely ~ A-い・NA-な + かぎりだ Speaker's own emotion adjectives only, not properties. うらやましい／恥ずかしい／寂しいかぎりだ.
〜極まる・〜極まりない~kiwamaru / kiwamarinai Utterly ~ NA + 極まる／極まりない; A-いこと + 極まりない 硬; same meaning (ない doesn't negate); negative 漢語 な-adj. 失礼極まりない, 危険極まる, 感極まる (idiom).
〜とは~to wa To think that ~! 普通形 (NA・N だ optional) + とは Attaches to a surprising fact (sentence), not a feeling word; not about oneself. うそをつくとは驚いた / 居眠りするとはひどい.
にほかならない／にすぎない／というものだ (N2)ni hoka naranai / ni suginai / to iu mono da Nothing but / merely / that's what ~ is N + にほかならない／にすぎない; 普通形 + というものだ Calm, plain evaluations — no emotional intensity. Neutral statements and essays.
Exam trap 1 — word class decides: before かぎりだ → an emotion adjective (ほほえましい, not ほほえむ／ほほえみ); before 極まる → a bare な-adjective stem (不便, not 不便な／不便だ); before とは → a full plain-form sentence; before に足る／に堪える → a dictionary-form verb or する-noun ([4] Q2–3, [5] Q2, [1〜6] Q3, Q5).
Exam trap 2 — whose feeling is it? かぎりだ = my emotion (うらやましい ✓, 仲がいい ✗). とは = surprise at someone else's act or a situation, not at my own feelings ([4] Q4, [1〜6] Q3).
Exam trap 3 — the "negative" that isn't: 極まりない and といったらない both end in ない but are positive intensifiers ("utterly", "beyond words"). Meanwhile に足る and に堪える are negated with 〜ものではない／〜とは言えない at the end, not with ×足らない＋N ([1] Q3, [3] Q1).
