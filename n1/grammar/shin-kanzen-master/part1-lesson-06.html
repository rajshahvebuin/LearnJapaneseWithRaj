【1】 〜んばかりだ

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written/literary feel)
Reading~n bakari da (〜んばかりに／〜んばかりの)
Meaning (EN)"as if about to ~; almost ~ing" — book: まるで〜しそうなほどの状態だ (a state so intense it looks as if ~ is about to happen)
Meaning (HI)मानो अभी ~ हो जाएगा / ~ करने को ही था (इतना ज़्यादा)
Meaning (GU)જાણે હમણાં ~ થઈ જશે / ~ કરવાની જ તૈયારીમાં (એટલું વધારે)
Connection (接続)動 ない + んばかりだ　＊例外 する→せん
RegisterNeutral but literary (ん is the old negative-volitional ぬ/む); common in writing and vivid narration.
Typical useAttaches to expressions of a state that doesn't actually go that far but comes close (頭を畳につける, 会場が割れる, etc.). The whole sentence shows the degree is out of the ordinary.

かごいっぱい、あふれんばかりのさくらんぼをいただいた。Kago ippai, afuren bakari no sakuranbo o itadaita. I was given a basketful of cherries, practically overflowing. मुझे टोकरी भरकर, लगभग छलकती हुई चेरी मिलीं। મને ટોપલી ભરીને, લગભગ છલકાતી ચેરી મળી.

Same meaning as 〜そうなほど, but much more vivid. Watch the connection: 言う → 言わん, 飛び上がる → 飛び上がらん, 泣き出す → 泣き出さん, する → せん (not しん). The verb is an exaggerated image that doesn't really happen (heads don't touch the floor, halls don't crack) — so a literal, everyday result like "everyone could hear it" (みんなに聞こえる) doesn't suit it (drill [1] Q4).

【2】 〜とばかり(に)

neutral register
Reading~to bakari (ni)
Meaning (EN)"as if to say ~" — book: 実際に声は出さないが、〜と言うような態度・行動をとる (without actually saying it aloud, someone acts in a way that says ~)
Meaning (HI)मानो कह रहा हो "~" (बिना बोले, हाव-भाव से)
Meaning (GU)જાણે કહેતો હોય "~" (બોલ્યા વિના, હાવભાવથી)
Connection (接続)発話文 + とばかり
RegisterNeutral; narrative.
Typical useAttaches directly to an utterance in its spoken form (「帰れ」・「だめだ」, etc.); the 「 」 may be left out. Used to describe other people's behaviour, not the speaker's. ③ この時とばかり is an idiom ("seizing the moment").

開発計画について意見交換会が行われた。住民たちはこの時とばかり、いろいろな意見を言った。Kaihatsu keikaku ni tsuite iken koukankai ga okonawareta. Juumintachi wa kono toki to bakari, iroiro na iken o itta. A meeting was held to exchange views on the development plan. The residents seized the moment and voiced all sorts of opinions. विकास योजना पर विचार-विमर्श सभा हुई। निवासियों ने मौक़ा देखकर तरह-तरह की राय रखी। વિકાસ યોજના પર વિચાર-વિમર્શ સભા યોજાઈ. રહેવાસીઓએ મોકો જોઈને જાતજાતના અભિપ્રાય રજૂ કર્યા.

Compare 【1】: both mean "as if", but 〜と言わんばかり (via 言う) and 〜とばかり (directly on the quote) are different connections. The key rule is that the words are not spoken — so "he said loudly" after とばかりに contradicts the pattern (drill [2] Q1). And the action must match the "unspoken words": 「入るな」 → locking the door, 「食べたくない」 → turning away.

neutral register
Reading~to mo naku / ~to mo nashi ni
Meaning (EN)A "without really meaning to ~; idly ~ing" — book: 自分で〜しようとはっきり意識しないまま、ある動作を行う (doing an action without being clearly conscious of trying to)
B "from somewhere / someone / sometime — can't say exactly" — book: はっきり〜と特定できない (can't pin down exactly ~)
Meaning (HI)A बिना किसी इरादे के, यूँ ही ~ करते हुए
B न जाने कहाँ से / कौन / कब से
Meaning (GU)A કોઈ ઇરાદા વિના, એમ જ ~ કરતાં
B ખબર નહીં ક્યાંથી / કોણ / ક્યારથી
Connection (接続)A: 動 辞書形 + ともなく・ともなしに

Meaning A only works with the dictionary form (not 見よう or 見ている — drill [3] Q1) and implies no aim: "listen to a piano piece" already names a purpose, while 何を聞くともなしに means "not listening to anything in particular" (Q2). Not the "while doing" ながら of N4–N3 (食べながら見る): this one means a state that stays the same. Learn it as vocabulary: 生まれながら(に／の), 居ながらにして, 昔ながら(の), 涙ながら(に). 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of these forms contain ばかり, and two contain ながら — the mixed set [1〜5] exploits exactly that. Sort them by connection first, meaning second:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜んばかりだ／に／の~n bakari da / ni / no As if about to ~ V-ない stem + ん (する→せん) Exaggerated image of a degree; it doesn't really happen. 割れんばかりの拍手, 泣き出さんばかりの顔.
〜と言わんばかり~to iwan bakari As if saying ~ quote + と言わんばかり The んばかり form of 言う — overlaps with とばかり in meaning. 家族だと言わんばかりの顔.
〜とばかり(に)~to bakari (ni) As if to say "~" utterance (often command) + とばかり Unspoken words acted out. Someone else's behaviour. 「入るな」とばかりにかぎをかけた.
〜たばかりに (N2)~ta bakari ni Just because ~ (bad result) V-た + ばかりに Cause → regrettable result. Not about appearance at all. 横を見たばかりに疑われた.
〜ともなく・〜ともなしに~to mo naku / ~to mo nashi ni A: idly ~ / B: who-knows-where/when/who V-辞書形; 疑問詞(+助詞) No purpose / can't be pinned down. Question word must be time, place or person. 見るともなく見る; どこからともなく.
〜ながらに(して)／〜ながらの~nagara ni (shite) / ~nagara no Remaining as ~ V-ます stem／N (fixed words only) Unchanged state; vocabulary items, not productive. 生まれながらの, 居ながらにして, 涙ながらに.
〜ながら (N4)~nagara While ~ing V-ます stem Two simultaneous actions — a different grammar point. 音楽を聞きながら勉強する.
〜きらいがある~kirai ga aru Has a (bad) tendency to ~ V-辞書形／ない形, N-の Mild criticism of someone else; softened with どうも・少し. 大げさに言うきらいがある.
〜気味 (N2)~gimi A touch of ~ V-ます stem／N + 気味 A slight current condition (風邪気味, 太り気味), not a personality trait. Describing how one feels now.
Exam trap 1 — which ばかり? ない-stem + ん → んばかり (degree); quote/command + と → とばかり (unspoken words); た形 → たばかりに (just because). Check the form immediately before ばかり ([1〜5] Q1, Q2, Q4).
Exam trap 2 — particle after ながら／んばかり: before a noun → の (割れんばかりの拍手, 昔ながらの家); before a verb → に (飛び上がらんばかりに驚いた, 涙ながらに語った). な and で are never right ([1] Q3, [4] Q2–3).
Exam trap 3 — said or not said? 〜とばかり means the words were not spoken. If the sentence says 大声で言った or つぶやいた, とばかり is wrong ([2] Q1, [1〜5] Q3).
