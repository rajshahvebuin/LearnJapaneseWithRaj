Notes
The point of this lesson: choose passive / causative / causative-passive so that the subject (the viewpoint) stays the same through a passage. Watch the fixed passive idioms (バスに揺られる・努力が報われる・必要に迫られる) and the "spontaneous" passive (悔やまれる・予想される), and causatives that are really just transitive verbs (声を震わせる・目をきらきらさせる). 練習1 asks you to finish a verb from its first kanji; 練習2 adds a particle blank before each verb. まとめ is a 4-option passage cloze; per the site rule, only the sentences with blanks are quoted and the rest is summarised. Scan quirk: the faint header bar on p.139 seems to carry the 6課 title, but the footer says 5課 and the content belongs here.
Lesson overview (added, not in book): p.136 starts straight with section A and has no introduction paragraph. In short: once a passage has chosen whose story it tells (usually わたし or the topic), keep that person as the subject — if something is done to them use the passive; if they make/let someone do something use the causative; if they are made to do something use the causative-passive.
HI: जब कोई लेख तय कर ले कि किसकी कहानी कह रहा है (अक्सर "मैं" या विषय), तो उसी को कर्ता बनाए रखिए — अगर उसके साथ कुछ किया जाए तो कर्मवाच्य (受身), अगर वह किसी से कुछ करवाए या करने दे तो प्रेरणार्थक (使役), और अगर उससे ज़बरदस्ती कुछ करवाया जाए तो प्रेरणार्थक-कर्मवाच्य (使役受身)।
GU: જ્યારે લખાણ નક્કી કરી લે કે કોની વાત કહે છે (મોટે ભાગે "હું" કે વિષય), ત્યારે તેને જ કર્તા તરીકે રાખો — જો તેની સાથે કંઈ કરવામાં આવે તો કર્મણિ (受身), જો તે કોઈ પાસે કંઈ કરાવે કે કરવા દે તો પ્રેરક (使役), અને જો તેની પાસે પરાણે કંઈ કરાવવામાં આવે તો પ્રેરક-કર્મણિ (使役受身).

【A-1】 迷惑・影響の受身 — the speaker is affected by someone else's action

neutral
SectionA 受身文を使う場合 — 1.Ukemibun o tsukau baai — 1.
Rule (book)話者が、第三者の行為または出来事の影響を直接的・間接的に受けたことを表すとき（主語はふつう話者、または動作主よりも心理的に話者に近い人）→第3部3課Washa ga, daisansha no koui mata wa dekigoto no eikyou o chokusetsuteki・kansetsuteki ni uketa koto o arawasu toki (shugo wa futsuu washa, mata wa dousashu yori mo shinriteki ni washa ni chikai hito) → dai-3-bu 3-ka
Rule (EN)Use the passive when the speaker was affected, directly or indirectly, by a third party's action or by an event. The subject is normally the speaker, or someone psychologically closer to the speaker than the doer is (see 第3部 3課).
Rule (HI)जब वक्ता पर किसी तीसरे व्यक्ति के काम या किसी घटना का सीधा या परोक्ष असर पड़ा हो, तब कर्मवाच्य लगाते हैं। कर्ता आमतौर पर वक्ता ख़ुद होता है, या वह व्यक्ति जो काम करने वाले से ज़्यादा वक्ता के मन के क़रीब हो।
Rule (GU)જ્યારે વક્તા પર કોઈ ત્રીજી વ્યક્તિના કામ કે કોઈ ઘટનાની સીધી કે આડકતરી અસર થઈ હોય, ત્યારે કર્મણિ વપરાય છે. કર્તા સામાન્ય રીતે વક્તા પોતે હોય છે, અથવા એવી વ્યક્તિ જે કામ કરનાર કરતાં વક્તાના મનની વધુ નજીક હોય.
Typical use"Suffering" passive (〜に〜を…られた): our team, my family, I — on the receiving end.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
残り1分で相手チームの選手にゴールを入れられ、逆転された。Nokori ippun de aite chiimu no senshu ni gooru o irerare, gyakuten sareta. With one minute left, a player on the other team scored on us and we were overtaken. आख़िरी एक मिनट में सामने वाली टीम के खिलाड़ी ने हम पर गोल कर दिया और हम पिछड़ गए। છેલ્લી એક મિનિટમાં સામેની ટીમના ખેલાડીએ અમારી સામે ગોલ કરી દીધો અને અમે પાછળ પડી ગયા.

The story is told from "our team's" side, so both verbs are passive and the subject never changes. Writing 相手チームの選手がゴールを入れ、逆転した would switch the viewpoint to the opponents mid-sentence.

【A-2】 動作主を言う必要がない受身 — topic-centred passive (〜によって)

written / neutral
SectionA-2.A-2.
Rule (book)主題についての情報を重視するため、動作主をはっきり言う必要がないとき（主題とともに動作主も大切な情報のときは「〜によって」を使って示す。）Shudai ni tsuite no jouhou o juushi suru tame, dousashu o hakkiri iu hitsuyou ga nai toki (shudai to tomo ni dousashu mo taisetsu na jouhou no toki wa "~ni yotte" o tsukatte shimesu.)
Rule (EN)Use the passive when the information about the topic matters and the doer need not be named. When the doer is also important information, mark it with 〜によって.
Rule (HI)जब विषय के बारे में जानकारी अहम हो और काम करने वाले का नाम बताना ज़रूरी न हो, तब कर्मवाच्य। अगर करने वाला भी अहम जानकारी हो, तो उसे 〜によって से दिखाते हैं।

Typical useDescriptions of works, events and institutions: 〜によって作曲された／建てられた／連載された.

In the first example who sent the letter doesn't matter; in the second, "by Beethoven" is part of the point, so によって (not に) is used. 練習2 passage 3 (連載された小説, 評価されて) is this type.

【A-4】 自発 — the "spontaneous" passive (a feeling arises by itself)

written

Why: B-5 他動詞化: 尖る is intransitive and has no transitive partner, so with the object 口を you need the causative 口を尖らせる ("pout"). 1 can't take 口を; 3 and 4 make no sense — nobody else is involved in pouting.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The voices this lesson's exercises force you to choose between. Rule of thumb: first decide who the subject (viewpoint) of the sentence is, then ask whether that person acts, is acted on, causes, or is forced.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
V-られる (受身)~rareru (ukemi) be done to / be affected by agent に／によって Subject is on the receiving end (A-1) or the topic of a description (A-2). 虫に食われる, 部長に叱られる, 漱石によって書かれた.
V-させる (使役)~saseru (shieki) make / let / cause causee を (intr.)／に (tr.) Subject is the cause: force (B-1), permission (B-2), emotional cause (B-3), neglect (B-4), or simply a transitive verb (B-5). 入院させる, 悲しませる, 死なせてしまう, 声を震わせる.
V-させられる／V-される (使役受身)~saserareru / ~sareru be made to forcer に Subject does the action unwillingly (C-1), or is inevitably moved to a feeling (C-2). 練習をさせられる, 考えさせられる, 心配させられる.
閉じこめられる vs 閉じこもるtojikomerareru vs tojikomoru be locked in vs shut oneself in tr. passive vs intr. Passive of a transitive = someone did it to you; the intransitive = you chose it. Check whether the passage has a "doer" (the adult) acting on the subject.
迫る vs 迫られるsemaru vs semarareru draw near vs be pressed by N が迫る／必要に迫られる Time/events approach (卒業が迫っている) — active; "be pressed by necessity" is the fixed passive idiom. Look at the noun: 卒業・締め切り → 迫る; 必要・決断 → 迫られる.
悔やむ vs 悔やまれる (自発)kuyamu vs kuyamareru regret vs regret wells up mental-activity verb + れる／られる The 自発 form has no agent; it says the feeling arises by itself — common in formal writing (予想される, 案じられる). Reports and essays where the writer avoids "I".
Exam trap 1 — keep the subject: when the same event is told twice (子どものじぶんを静かにさせて → 静かにさせられた子どものじぶん), the voice flips as the subject changes. In まとめ ［1］–［3］ every answer follows from "the child-self is the subject".
Exam trap 2 — causative ≠ always "make someone": 口を尖らせる, 目をきらきらさせる, 声を震わせる, 花を咲かせる, 車を走らせる are just transitive verbs (B-5). If there's no second person, don't look for a forcer.
Exam trap 3 — particles follow the voice: 部長に書き直しをさせられる (forcer に), 被雇用者を辞めさせる (intransitive causee を), ある人に仕事をさせる (transitive causee に + object を). 練習2 tests these in every passage.
