Notes
Six ways to stress something, and the exam tests their connection and collocation more than their meaning: たりとも needs 1 + a small counter and a negative; すら takes a noun (+particle) and has a ですら variant; だに sticks to a handful of verbs (想像する・考える・聞く) or fixed phrases (夢にだに, 微動だに); からある／からする／からの need a large number and choose by what is measured (weight/size, price, people). 〜すら and 〜だに are tagged 硬 formal/written (硬い言い方). The book cross-references 〜にして to IV F (助詞・複合助詞). Drills are 3-option (a–c).
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.76): こそ, (一人)として…ない and さえ — the everyday emphasis particles. Today's 〜たりとも, 〜すら and 〜だに are stiffer cousins of them.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜たりとも…ない

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written/formal feel)
Reading~tari tomo … nai
Meaning (EN)"not even one ~; not a single ~" — book: 1〜も…ない。全く…ない (not even one ~; absolutely not)
Meaning (HI)एक ~ भी नहीं / ज़रा भी नहीं
Meaning (GU)એક ~ પણ નહીં / જરાય નહીં
Connection (接続)1 + 助数詞 + たりとも + …ない
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book, but stiff — used in warnings, rules, firm statements.
Typical useTakes the smallest unit as an example and stresses that there is none at all. It attaches to "1" + counter, but not to units that are too big (×1年, ×1トン). 何人たりとも (example ④) is an idiomatic exception ("no one whatsoever").
Word typeFormationExample
1 + counter (time)1日／一瞬 + たりとも + V-ない1日たりとも忘れたことはありませんichinichi tari tomo wasureta koto wa arimasen
1 + counter (size)1ミリ + たりとも + ない1ミリたりとも間違いがないichimiri tari tomo machigai ga nai
Idiom何人 + たりとも + V-ない何人たりとも立ち入ることは許されていないnanbito tari tomo tachiiru koto wa yurusarete inai
Verb / い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used — attaches only to 1 + counter—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あなたのことは1日たりとも忘れたことはありません。Anata no koto wa ichinichi tari tomo wasureta koto wa arimasen. I have never forgotten you for even a single day. मैं तुम्हें एक दिन के लिए भी नहीं भूला। હું તમને એક દિવસ માટે પણ ભૂલ્યો નથી.
試合中は一瞬たりとも気を抜いてはいけない。Shiai-chuu wa isshun tari tomo ki o nuite wa ikenai. During the match, you must not relax your guard for even an instant. मैच के दौरान एक पल के लिए भी ढील नहीं देनी चाहिए। મેચ દરમિયાન એક ક્ષણ માટે પણ ઢીલ ન આપવી જોઈએ.
この部分の設計は1ミリたりとも間違いがないようにお願いします。Kono bubun no sekkei wa ichimiri tari tomo machigai ga nai you ni onegai shimasu. Please make sure the design of this part is off by not even a single millimetre. कृपया ध्यान रखें कि इस हिस्से के डिज़ाइन में एक मिलीमीटर की भी ग़लती न हो। કૃપા કરીને ધ્યાન રાખો કે આ ભાગની ડિઝાઇનમાં એક મિલીમીટરની પણ ભૂલ ન થાય.
何人たりともこの神聖な場所に立ち入ることは許されていない。Nanbito tari tomo kono shinsei na basho ni tachiiru koto wa yurusarete inai. No one whatsoever is permitted to enter this sacred place. किसी को भी इस पवित्र स्थान में प्रवेश की अनुमति नहीं है। કોઈને પણ આ પવિત્ર સ્થળમાં પ્રવેશની પરવાનગી નથી.

Think "not even the smallest possible amount". The size of the unit matters: 1円, 1分, 1日, 1ミリ, 1本 are tiny, so たりとも works; 1万円, 1週間, 1年, 1トン are too big to be "the minimum", so they fail (drill [1] Q1–2). The end must be negative — a prohibition, an impossibility, or a wish phrased negatively (〜ないように, 〜てほしくない). A positive ending like 大切にしたい or a question like 遅れたらどうしよう breaks the pattern ([1] Q3–4).

【2】 〜すら

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~sura
Meaning (EN)"even ~ (so of course the rest)" — book: 〜という極端な例もそうなのだから、当然その他も同様だ (since even the extreme example ~ is so, naturally the others are too)
Meaning (HI)...तक / ...भी (तो बाक़ी का क्या कहना)
Meaning (GU)...સુધ્ધાં / ...પણ (તો બાકીનું શું કહેવું)
Connection (接続)名（＋助詞）＋すら
Register硬 — the written equivalent of さえ.
Typical useGives an extreme example. As in ④, it can appear as ですら: this is mainly where the noun would be marked by the subject particle が, and it means 「であっても」 ("even being ~").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + すら悲しみすら覚えたkanashimi sura oboeta
Noun + particleN + に + すら仕事にすら自信が持てないshigoto ni sura jishin ga motenai
Noun (subject)N + ですら専門家ですら直すのが難しいsenmonka de sura naosu no ga muzukashii
Verb (nominalised)V + こと + すら待たされることすらあるmatasareru koto sura aru
Verb (bare)(added, not in book) ×V-dictionary + すら — nominalise with こと first—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼のうそには、怒りだけではなく悲しみすら覚えた。Kare no uso ni wa, ikari dake de wa naku kanashimi sura oboeta. His lies made me feel not just anger but even sadness. उसके झूठ पर मुझे सिर्फ़ ग़ुस्सा ही नहीं, बल्कि दुख तक महसूस हुआ। તેના જૂઠાણા પર મને ફક્ત ગુસ્સો જ નહીં, દુઃખ સુધ્ધાં થયું.
バスは雨の日などにはたびたび遅れる。30分も待たされることすらある。Basu wa ame no hi nado ni wa tabitabi okureru. Sanjuppun mo matasareru koto sura aru. The bus is often late on rainy days and such. Sometimes you're even kept waiting as long as 30 minutes. बारिश वाले दिनों में बस अक्सर देर से आती है। कभी-कभी तो 30 मिनट तक इंतज़ार करना पड़ता है। વરસાદના દિવસોમાં બસ ઘણી વાર મોડી આવે છે. ક્યારેક તો 30 મિનિટ સુધી રાહ જોવી પડે છે.
自分が好きで選んだ仕事にすら自信が持てなくなってしまった。Jibun ga suki de eranda shigoto ni sura jishin ga motenaku natte shimatta. I've lost confidence even in the work I chose because I loved it. जिस काम को मैंने शौक़ से चुना था, उस पर भी अब मुझे भरोसा नहीं रहा। જે કામ મેં શોખથી પસંદ કર્યું હતું, તેમાં પણ હવે મને વિશ્વાસ નથી રહ્યો.
これは専門家ですら直すのが難しい故障だ。素人のわたしには全く手がつけられない。Kore wa senmonka de sura naosu no ga muzukashii koshou da. Shirouto no watashi ni wa mattaku te ga tsukerarenai. This is a fault that even an expert would find hard to fix. An amateur like me can't even begin to touch it. यह ऐसी ख़राबी है जिसे ठीक करना विशेषज्ञ के लिए भी मुश्किल है। मुझ जैसा नौसिखिया तो इसे हाथ भी नहीं लगा सकता। આ એવી ખામી છે જેને સુધારવી નિષ્ણાત માટે પણ મુશ્કેલ છે. મારા જેવો શિખાઉ તો તેને હાથ પણ ન લગાડી શકે.

すら ≈ さえ in a stiffer register. Two things the drills test: (1) すら attaches to a noun, so a verb must first become 〜こと (息をすることすら, not ×息をするすら) — drill [2] Q3; (2) すら does not have さえ's conditional use 「〜さえ〜ば」 ("as long as ~"): 盛りさえすれば is fine, ×盛りすらすれば is not ([2] Q4). The extreme example should be something that is normally easy (getting up by yourself, knowing what you like) so the "even" lands.

【3】 〜だに

硬 formal/written (硬い言い方)
Reading~da ni
Meaning (EN)"just ~ing is enough to…; not even ~" — book: 〜だけでもそのような状況なのだから、実際は極端だ・(「〜だに…ない」の形で) 全く…ない (even just ~ produces that state, so the reality would be extreme; in 〜だに…ない, "not at all")
Meaning (HI)...करने भर से (ही) / ...तक नहीं
Meaning (GU)...કરવા માત્રથી (જ) / ...સુધ્ધાં નહીં
Connection (接続)名（＋助詞）・動 辞書形 + だに
Register硬 — literary; mostly frozen expressions.
Typical useMostly idiomatic, attaching only to a limited set of words: 想像(する)・考える・聞く etc. (想像するだに恐ろしい, 夢にだに思わない, 微動だにしない).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (辞書形)想像する／考える／聞く + だに想像するだに恐ろしいsouzou suru da ni osoroshii
Noun + particle夢 + に + だに + V-ない夢にだに思わなかったyume ni da ni omowanakatta
Noun微動／予想 + だに + しない微動だにしなかったbidou da ni shinakatta
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その病気が広まって100万人もの人が死ぬなど、想像するだに恐ろしい。Sono byouki ga hiromatte hyakuman-nin mo no hito ga shinu nado, souzou suru da ni osoroshii. The disease spreading and a million people dying — it's terrifying even to imagine. उस बीमारी के फैलने और दस लाख लोगों के मरने की कल्पना करना ही भयानक है। તે રોગ ફેલાઈને દસ લાખ લોકો મરી જાય — એની કલ્પના કરવી પણ ભયાનક છે.
まさかわたしが歌手としてステージに立つなんて、夢にだに思わなかった。Masaka watashi ga kashu to shite suteeji ni tatsu nante, yume ni da ni omowanakatta. Me standing on stage as a singer — I never dreamed of it, not even in my dreams. मैं गायिका बनकर मंच पर खड़ी होऊँगी, ऐसा तो मैंने सपने में भी नहीं सोचा था। હું ગાયિકા તરીકે મંચ પર ઊભી રહીશ, એવું તો મેં સપનામાં પણ નહોતું વિચાર્યું.
そのニュースを聞いても、彼女は表情を変えず、微動だにしなかった。Sono nyuusu o kiite mo, kanojo wa hyoujou o kaezu, bidou da ni shinakatta. Even on hearing that news, she didn't change expression — she didn't so much as flinch. वह ख़बर सुनकर भी उसके चेहरे का भाव नहीं बदला, वह ज़रा भी नहीं हिली। તે સમાચાર સાંભળીને પણ તેના ચહેરાનો ભાવ ન બદલાયો, તે જરાય ન હલી.

Treat 〜だに as a set of fixed phrases rather than a productive particle. Two shapes: 想像する／考える／聞く + だに + emotion adjective (恐ろしい, つらい) = "just thinking about it is…"; and N + だに + negative (夢にだに思わない, 予想だにしない, 微動だにしない) = "not even". Drill [3] tests both: 予想だにしなかった (Q1, ×思うのだに, ×考えてだに) and 聞くだに恐ろしい (Q2, ×経験するだに, ×死ぬだに).

【4】 〜にして

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni shite
Meaning (EN)"only at (the stage of) ~; precisely because one is ~ / even ~" — book: 〜という高い段階・特別な条件に合っている・合っていない (matching — or not matching — a high stage or special condition ~)
Meaning (HI)...पर जाकर (ही) / ...होकर ही / ...होते हुए भी
Meaning (GU)...એ જઈને (જ) / ...હોવાથી જ / ...હોવા છતાં
Connection (接続)名 + にして
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; written, somewhat old-fashioned. Cross-referenced to IV F (助詞・複合助詞).
Typical useAttaches to words showing that a high stage has been reached or that something is special. What follows says the result matches that level (③ 天才にしてはじめて書ける) or doesn't (② プロの職人にして失敗をする).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time/stage)N + にして + ようやく／はじめて8年目にしてようやく授かったhachinen-me ni shite youyaku sazukatta
Noun (person, contrary)N + にして + 失敗する etc.プロの職人にして失敗をするpuro no shokunin ni shite shippai o suru
Noun (person, matching)N + にしてはじめて + V-可能天才にしてはじめて書けるtensai ni shite hajimete kakeru
Idiomこの N1 にしてこの N2 ありこの父にしてこの息子ありkono chichi ni shite kono musuko ari
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — attaches to nouns only—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
結婚してすぐに子供が欲しかったが、8年目にしてようやく授かった。Kekkon shite sugu ni kodomo ga hoshikatta ga, hachinen-me ni shite youyaku sazukatta. We wanted a child right after getting married, but it was only in the eighth year that we were finally blessed with one. शादी के तुरंत बाद हमें बच्चा चाहिए था, पर आठवें साल में जाकर आख़िरकार संतान का सुख मिला। લગ્ન પછી તરત અમને બાળક જોઈતું હતું, પણ આઠમા વર્ષે જઈને આખરે સંતાનનું સુખ મળ્યું.
プロの職人にして失敗をするのだ。君がうまくいかなくてもしょうがないだろう。Puro no shokunin ni shite shippai o suru no da. Kimi ga umaku ikanakute mo shou ga nai darou. Even professional craftsmen make mistakes. It can't be helped if it doesn't go well for you. पेशेवर कारीगर भी ग़लती कर बैठते हैं। तुमसे ठीक न हो पाए तो कोई बात नहीं। વ્યાવસાયિક કારીગર પણ ભૂલ કરી બેસે છે. તમારાથી બરાબર ન થાય તો કંઈ વાંધો નહીં.
この曲はベートーベンのような天才にしてはじめて書ける作品だ。Kono kyoku wa Beetooben no you na tensai ni shite hajimete kakeru sakuhin da. This piece is a work that only a genius like Beethoven could write. यह धुन ऐसी रचना है जिसे बीथोवन जैसा प्रतिभाशाली ही लिख सकता है। આ ધૂન એવી રચના છે જે બીથોવન જેવો પ્રતિભાશાળી જ લખી શકે.
やれやれ、この父にしてこの息子あり。二人ともとてもよく食べる。Yare yare, kono chichi ni shite kono musuko ari. Futari tomo totemo yoku taberu. Good grief — like father, like son. They both eat an awful lot. हाय राम, जैसा बाप वैसा बेटा। दोनों ख़ूब खाते हैं। અરે રે, જેવો બાપ તેવો દીકરો. બંને ખૂબ ખાય છે.

The noun must sound like a high or special level: 3回目の挑戦, 60歳, オリンピック選手, 天才. Then the second half either confirms it (やっと成功した, 初めて出せるタイム) or contrasts with it (失敗をする). A plain, unremarkable noun (just 選手, 初出場の選手) or a second half that has nothing to do with the level (いつも成功だ, 退職した at 60 is normal) breaks it — drill [4]. Don't confuse with 〜にしては (N2, "for a ~, surprisingly…").

結婚は、相手あってのことだから、相手がいなければどうしようもない。Kekkon wa, aite atte no koto da kara, aite ga inakereba dou shiyou mo nai. Marriage needs a partner to happen at all, so without one there's nothing you can do. शादी तो जीवनसाथी के होने से ही होती है, अगर साथी ही न हो तो कुछ नहीं किया जा सकता। લગ્ન તો જીવનસાથી હોય તો જ થાય, સાથી જ ન હોય તો કંઈ થઈ ન શકે.
海あっての漁業なのだから、海を汚してはいけない。Umi atte no gyogyou na no da kara, umi o yogoshite wa ikenai. Fishing exists only because of the sea, so we must not pollute it. मछली पकड़ने का धंधा समुद्र के कारण ही है, इसलिए समुद्र को गंदा नहीं करना चाहिए। માછીમારી સમુદ્રને કારણે જ છે, તેથી સમુદ્રને ગંદો ન કરવો જોઈએ.
読者あっての雑誌なので、読者が読みたいと思うものを提供したい。Dokusha atte no zasshi na node, dokusha ga yomitai to omou mono o teikyou shitai. A magazine exists only thanks to its readers, so we want to offer what readers want to read. पत्रिका पाठकों के कारण ही चलती है, इसलिए हम वही देना चाहते हैं जो पाठक पढ़ना चाहें। સામયિક વાચકોને કારણે જ ચાલે છે, તેથી અમે એ જ આપવા માગીએ છીએ જે વાચકો વાંચવા ઇચ્છે.

"A あっての B" = "B is only possible because of A" — A is the precondition, B is what it makes possible. So the order matters: 過去の失敗あっての成功 (success built on past failure), not ×失敗あっての反省. The A noun should be something you can be grateful for (家族の協力, 愛, 読者) rather than something everyone has anyway (健康, 命 sounds too general for "thanks to whom I recovered") — drill [5]. Compare 12課 〜ばこそ ("precisely because"), which works on clauses rather than nouns.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Six emphasis forms plus the three N2 particles from the 〔復習〕 box. The mixed set [1〜6] makes you choose between たりとも／すら／だに／にして, so learn them by what they attach to:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たりとも…ない~tari tomo … nai Not even one ~ 1 + small counter Needs "1" + a minimum unit and a negative end. ×1年, ×1トン. Firm rules and resolutions: 1円たりとも無駄にしない.
〜すら／〜ですら~sura / ~de sura Even ~ (extreme example) N(+助詞) + すら; subject N + ですら 硬 version of さえ, but no 〜すら〜ば. Verbs need こと. Something normally easy turned hard: 起き上がることすら大変.
〜だに~da ni Just ~ing is enough to… / not even 想像する・考える・聞く + だに; 夢にだに, 予想だに, 微動だに 硬, frozen phrases only. Not productive with other nouns/verbs. 想像するだに恐ろしい / 予想だにしなかった.
〜にして~ni shite Only at the (high) stage ~ / even ~ N + にして(はじめて) Noun must be a high or special level; result matches it (or contrasts with it). 天才にしてはじめて書ける / 3回目にしてやっと.
〜あっての~atte no B exists only thanks to A N + あっての + N A = indispensable precondition (often with gratitude); both sides nouns. Speeches, thanks: 皆様あっての私です.
〜からある／からする／からの~kara aru / suru / no A whopping ~ or more Large 数詞 + からある／する／の + N ある = weight/size/distance; する = price; の = people (and quantity). No time, no small numbers. 2トンからある岩 / 10万円からする服 / 1万人からの人.
こそ／として…ない／さえ (N2)koso / to shite … nai / sae Precisely / not a single / even, as long as N + こそ; 一＋counter + として; N(+助詞) + さえ Everyday counterparts; さえ also has the conditional 〜さえ〜ば that すら lacks. Conversation, or 〜さえすれば ([2] Q4).
Exam trap 1 — the "1 + counter" family: 1歩, 1匹, 1円, 1分 + negative → たりとも. If the number is big and the sentence is affirmative and impressed (2トン, 10万円, 1万人) → からある／からする／からの. Do not mix them: ×1万人たりとも, ×1円からある ([1], [6], [1〜6] Q1, Q3, Q6).
Exam trap 2 — すら vs にして with people: ですら = "even X (the least likely one) can/can't"; にして = "only X (the most qualified one) can". 子供ですらできる (easy) vs 斉藤さんにしてできる (masterful). Ask whether the person is the lowest or the highest point on the scale ([1〜6] Q2, Q4).
Exam trap 3 — だに is a closed list: if the option is "random noun/verb + だに" (1歩だに, 子供だに, 経験するだに), eliminate it. The only safe ones are 想像する／考える／聞くだに + emotion and 夢にだに／予想だに／微動だに + negative ([3], [1〜6]).
