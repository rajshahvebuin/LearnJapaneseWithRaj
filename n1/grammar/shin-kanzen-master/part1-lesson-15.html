Notes
Three ways of setting two things side by side: 〜にひきかえ (A is good, B by contrast is bad — a subjective judgement, not a neutral fact), 〜にもまして (B is even more ~ than the usual/earlier A; with a question word = "more than anything"), and 〜ないまでも (not as far as the ideal, but at least one step below it). None of the three is tagged 硬 in the book. Only three grammar points this lesson, so the drills are 15 items. The second half of p.67 and p.69 is blank in the book (not a scan problem).
〔復習〕 Review (N2 forms the book reminds you of first, p.66): the neutral N2 contrast 〜に対して and the "rather than ~" form 〜というより. Today's 〜にひきかえ is the evaluative, emotional cousin of 〜に対して.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜にひきかえ

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni hikikae
Meaning (EN)"in (stark) contrast to ~; compared with ~ (which is good/bad), this is…" — book: 〜とは大きく違って・〜とは反対に、そのことはいい・悪い (very different from / opposite to ~, this thing is good / bad)
Meaning (HI)...के ठीक उलट / ...की तुलना में (यह अच्छा/बुरा है)
Meaning (GU)...થી તદ્દન ઊલટું / ...ની સરખામણીમાં (આ સારું/ખરાબ છે)
Connection (接続)名・普通形（ナ形 だ→な／−である・名 だ→な／−である）＋の ＋ にひきかえ
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; slightly formal, common in writing and in emotional complaints.
Typical useNot a comparison of plain facts — it compares the two sides by the speaker's subjective evaluation of good vs. bad (主観的評価). It is not used for comparing things that are only guessed/predicted (推量されることの比較には使わない).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にひきかえ前日までのひどい天気にひきかえzenjitsu made no hidoi tenki ni hikikae
Noun (clause)N なの／N であるの + にひきかえ母が穏やかであるのにひきかえhaha ga odayaka de aru no ni hikikae
Verb (普通形)V-plain + の + にひきかえ遊んでばかりいたのにひきかえasonde bakari ita no ni hikikae
い-adjectiveい-adj + の + にひきかえ新しくて広いのにひきかえatarashikute hiroi no ni hikikae
な-adjectiveな-adj + なの／であるの + にひきかえきれい好きなのにひきかえkireizuki na no ni hikikae
Pronounそれ + にひきかえ (sentence-initial)それにひきかえsore ni hikikae
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大変な時代の中でも一生懸命に生きているその主人公にひきかえ、今のわたしは何とだらしないのだろう。Taihen na jidai no naka de mo isshoukenmei ni ikite iru sono shujinkou ni hikikae, ima no watashi wa nan to darashinai no darou. Compared with that hero, who lives with all his might even in such hard times, how slovenly I am now! कठिन दौर में भी पूरी लगन से जीने वाले उस नायक के ठीक उलट, आज मैं कितना लापरवाह हूँ! કપરા સમયમાં પણ પૂરી લગનથી જીવતા તે નાયકથી તદ્દન ઊલટું, આજે હું કેટલો બેદરકાર છું!
姉がきれい好きなのにひきかえ、妹はいつも部屋を散らかしている。困ったものだ。Ane ga kireizuki na no ni hikikae, imouto wa itsumo heya o chirakashite iru. Komatta mono da. Whereas the older sister loves things neat, the younger one always leaves her room in a mess. What a nuisance. बड़ी बहन को सफ़ाई पसंद है, उसके ठीक उलट छोटी बहन हमेशा कमरा बिखेरे रहती है। बड़ी मुश्किल है। મોટી બહેનને ચોખ્ખાઈ ગમે છે, તેનાથી તદ્દન ઊલટું નાની બહેન હંમેશાં ઓરડો વેરવિખેર રાખે છે. મોટી તકલીફ છે.
前日までのひどい天気にひきかえ、運動会の当日は気持ちよく晴れて良かった。Zenjitsu made no hidoi tenki ni hikikae, undoukai no toujitsu wa kimochi yoku harete yokatta. In contrast to the awful weather right up to the day before, sports day itself was beautifully clear — what luck. एक दिन पहले तक के ख़राब मौसम के ठीक उलट, खेल-दिवस के दिन बढ़िया धूप खिली, अच्छा हुआ। આગલા દિવસ સુધીના ખરાબ હવામાનથી તદ્દન ઊલટું, રમતોત્સવના દિવસે સરસ તડકો નીકળ્યો, સારું થયું.
田中さんの住んでいるマンションは新しくて広い。それにひきかえ、わたしのところは古くて狭いし、駅からも遠い。Tanaka-san no sunde iru manshon wa atarashikute hiroi. Sore ni hikikae, watashi no tokoro wa furukute semai shi, eki kara mo tooi. Mr. Tanaka's apartment is new and spacious. Mine, by contrast, is old and cramped, and far from the station too. तनाका जी का फ़्लैट नया और बड़ा है। उसके ठीक उलट, मेरा पुराना और तंग है, और स्टेशन से भी दूर है। તનાકાજીનો ફ્લૅટ નવો અને મોટો છે. તેનાથી તદ્દન ઊલટું, મારો જૂનો અને સાંકડો છે, અને સ્ટેશનથી પણ દૂર છે.

One side is praised and the other criticised (or the reverse) — e.g. tidy sister vs. messy sister. A neutral difference like age (58歳・52歳) or birthplace (関西・東北) can't be used ([1] Q3): for that, use 〜に対して. Watch the connection: a な-adjective or noun clause needs なの／であるの before にひきかえ (頑固なのにひきかえ, [1] Q1). And both sides must be facts — "next year's income will probably fall" is speculation and is ruled out ([1] Q4).

【2】 〜にもまして

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book; written feel)
Reading~ni mo mashite
Meaning (EN)"even more than ~; more than (usual / before)" — book: 通常のこと・以前のことよりも、ほかのこと・現在のことの方が程度が上だ (something else / the present is of a higher degree than the usual / earlier thing)
Meaning (HI)...से भी बढ़कर / ...से भी ज़्यादा
Meaning (GU)...થી પણ વધુ / ...કરતાં પણ વધારે
Connection (接続)名・疑問詞 + にもまして
RegisterNot tagged 硬 in the book; a slightly formal, written expression.
Typical useAttaches to nouns meaning the usual or earlier thing (去年, 以前, 自分の仕事のこと). With a question word (④ いつにもまして, 何にもまして) it expresses a superlative — "more than ever / more than anything". No negative sentence follows (後には否定文は来ない).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (usual/earlier)N + にもまして去年にもましてkyonen ni mo mashite
Noun (こと clause)V-plain + こと + にもまして成績が下がったことにもましてseiseki ga sagatta koto ni mo mashite
Question wordいつ／何／だれ + にもましていつにもましてitsu ni mo mashite
Verb / い-adj / な-adj directly(added, not in book) not used — nominalise with こと／の first—

去年は猛暑で連日気温が33度を超えたが、今年は去年にもまして暑さが厳しい。Kyonen wa mousho de renjitsu kion ga sanjuusan-do o koeta ga, kotoshi wa kyonen ni mo mashite atsusa ga kibishii. Last year was a scorcher, with temperatures over 33°C day after day, but this year the heat is even harsher than last year. पिछले साल भीषण गर्मी में रोज़ तापमान 33 डिग्री पार करता था, पर इस साल गर्मी पिछले साल से भी ज़्यादा कड़ी है। ગયા વર્ષે ભયંકર ગરમીમાં રોજ તાપમાન 33 ડિગ્રી પાર કરતું હતું, પણ આ વર્ષે ગરમી ગયા વર્ષ કરતાં પણ વધુ આકરી છે.
自分の仕事のことにもまして気になるのは、父の病気のことだ。Jibun no shigoto no koto ni mo mashite ki ni naru no wa, chichi no byouki no koto da. What weighs on my mind even more than my own work is my father's illness. अपने काम से भी बढ़कर जो बात मुझे खटकती है, वह है पिताजी की बीमारी। મારા પોતાના કામ કરતાં પણ વધુ જે વાત મને સતાવે છે, તે છે પિતાજીની બીમારી.
もともと覚えるのは得意ではなかったが、最近は以前にもまして物覚えが悪くなった。Motomoto oboeru no wa tokui de wa nakatta ga, saikin wa izen ni mo mashite monooboe ga waruku natta. I was never good at memorising, but lately my memory has got even worse than before. याद करने में मैं शुरू से ही कमज़ोर था, पर आजकल याददाश्त पहले से भी ज़्यादा ख़राब हो गई है। યાદ રાખવામાં હું પહેલેથી જ નબળો હતો, પણ આજકાલ યાદશક્તિ પહેલાં કરતાં પણ વધુ ખરાબ થઈ ગઈ છે.
結婚式の日の彼女は、いつにもまして美しかった。Kekkonshiki no hi no kanojo wa, itsu ni mo mashite utsukushikatta. On her wedding day she was more beautiful than ever. शादी के दिन वह हमेशा से भी ज़्यादा सुंदर लग रही थी। લગ્નના દિવસે તે હંમેશાં કરતાં પણ વધુ સુંદર લાગતી હતી.

The shape is "A にもまして B" = "B, even more than A". A is the baseline (the usual year, the earlier state, the other worry), and B is what is higher in degree. Example ③ shows that "higher degree" can be of something bad (物覚えが悪くなった) — the restriction is on the sentence being negative (×…にもまして楽しみではない), not on bad news ([2] Q2). In [2] Q3 the order matters: いつもの年 (baseline) にもまして 今年 (higher).

【3】 〜ないまでも

neutral register
Reading~nai made mo
Meaning (EN)"even if not (as far as) ~, at least…" — book: 〜という程度までは至らないが、その少し下のレベルの状態だ (it doesn't reach the degree ~, but is at a level a little below it)
Meaning (HI)...भले न हो, पर कम से कम...
Meaning (GU)...ભલે ન હોય, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછું...
Connection (接続)動 ない形 + までも
RegisterNeutral — fine in speech and writing.
Typical useAttaches to words for an ideal or extreme state (プロになる, ぜいたく, 快晴). The second clause gives a level a little below the fully satisfying / final goal — often with せめて, wishes (〜たい, 〜てほしい) or intentions (〜つもりだ). As in ③④ it also attaches to nouns as 〜とはいかないまでも・〜とは言わないまでも.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ない形)V-ない + までもプロにはなれないまでもpuro ni wa narenai made mo
NounN + とはいかないまでも快晴とはいかないまでもkaisei to wa ikanai made mo
NounN + とは言わないまでも毎週とは言わないまでもmaishuu to wa iwanai made mo
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) usually via とは言わないまでも, e.g. 完璧とは言わないまでも完璧とは言わないまでもkanpeki to wa iwanai made mo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
プロにはなれないまでも、演劇はずっと続けていくつもりだ。Puro ni wa narenai made mo, engeki wa zutto tsuzukete iku tsumori da. Even if I can't become a professional, I intend to keep doing theatre for good. भले ही मैं पेशेवर न बन पाऊँ, पर नाटक करना हमेशा जारी रखने का इरादा है। ભલે હું વ્યાવસાયિક ન બની શકું, પણ નાટક કરવાનું હંમેશાં ચાલુ રાખવાનો ઇરાદો છે.
わたしたち夫婦は海外旅行などのぜいたくはできないまでも、不自由のない暮らしをしています。Watashitachi fuufu wa kaigai ryokou nado no zeitaku wa dekinai made mo, fujiyuu no nai kurashi o shite imasu. My wife and I can't afford luxuries like trips abroad, but at least we live without want. हम पति-पत्नी विदेश यात्रा जैसी विलासिता भले न कर पाएँ, पर किसी चीज़ की कमी के बिना जी रहे हैं। અમે પતિ-પત્ની વિદેશ પ્રવાસ જેવી મોજશોખ ભલે ન કરી શકીએ, પણ કોઈ વાતની અછત વગર જીવીએ છીએ.
あしたはハイキングだ。快晴とはいかないまでも、雨は降らないでほしい。Ashita wa haikingu da. Kaisei to wa ikanai made mo, ame wa furanaide hoshii. Tomorrow's the hike. It needn't be perfectly clear, but I hope it at least doesn't rain. कल पैदल सैर है। भले ही आसमान एकदम साफ़ न हो, पर बारिश तो न हो, यही चाहता हूँ। કાલે પગપાળા સહેલ છે. ભલે આકાશ એકદમ સ્વચ્છ ન હોય, પણ વરસાદ તો ન પડે, એ જ ઇચ્છું છું.
毎週とは言わないまでも、せめて月に1回は外食したい。Maishuu to wa iwanai made mo, semete tsuki ni ikkai wa gaishoku shitai. I won't say every week, but I'd like to eat out at least once a month. हर हफ़्ते न सही, पर कम से कम महीने में एक बार तो बाहर खाना चाहता हूँ। દર અઠવાડિયે ભલે નહીં, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછું મહિને એક વાર તો બહાર જમવું છે.

Picture a ladder: the 〜ない part is the top rung you can't reach (pro, ideal weather, every week, best 4), and the second clause is the rung just below (keep doing theatre, no rain, once a month, best 8). So the second clause must be lower but still on the same scale: "best 4 is out, so let's win the whole thing" is going up, not down ([3] Q4). The second clause usually carries a wish or intention (〜たい, 〜てほしい, 〜つもりだ), and せめて ("at least") is its typical partner ([3] Q3).

[1] Q1

林先生が（　）にひきかえ、山田先生は物わかりがいい。Hayashi-sensei ga (___) ni hikikae, Yamada-sensei wa monowakari ga ii.

Full sentence: 林先生が頑固なのにひきかえ、山田先生は物わかりがいい。Hayashi-sensei ga ganko na no ni hikikae, Yamada-sensei wa monowakari ga ii.
EN: In contrast to stubborn Professor Hayashi, Professor Yamada is very understanding.
HI: हायाशी सर ज़िद्दी हैं, उनके ठीक उलट यामादा सर बहुत समझदार हैं।
GU: હાયાશી સાહેબ જિદ્દી છે, તેમનાથી તદ્દન ઊલટું યામાદા સાહેબ ખૂબ સમજદાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a頑固gankostubborn (bare stem)ज़िद्दी (मूल रूप)જિદ્દી (મૂળ રૂપ)
b頑固なのganko na nobeing stubborn (な + の)ज़िद्दी होनाજિદ્દી હોવું

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All three put two things side by side, but each relates them in a different way — opposite, same but stronger, or one step lower:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にひきかえ~ni hikikae In contrast to ~ (good vs. bad) N／plain + の (な-adj・N: なの／であるの) Two different things, judged subjectively as good vs. bad. Facts only — no guesses. Tidy sister vs. messy sister; awful weather before vs. sunny on the day.
〜に対して (N2)~ni taishite Whereas ~ N／plain + の Neutral, objective contrast. Fine for ages, birthplaces, statistics. Reports, descriptions with no value judgement.
〜にもまして~ni mo mashite Even more than ~ N (usual/earlier)／疑問詞 Same scale, higher degree than a baseline. 疑問詞 → superlative. No negative sentence after. This year vs. last year; いつにもまして, 何にもまして.
〜ないまでも~nai made mo Even if not ~, at least… V-ない／N とはいかない・とは言わない + までも Ideal level not reached → a level just below. Often せめて + wish/intention. Not a pro, but keep acting; not best 4, but best 8.
〜というより (N2)~to iu yori Rather than ~ plain form／N Corrects the description of one thing ("not so much X as Y"); no second thing compared. Choosing the better word for something (飲まないというより飲めない).
Exam trap 1 — にひきかえ needs a value judgement: if the two sides differ only in neutral facts (age, place, day of the week), にひきかえ is wrong. If either side is a guess (〜だろう, 〜かもしれない), it is also wrong ([1] Q3–4).
Exam trap 2 — direction on the scale: にもまして goes up from a baseline (いつも → more than ever); ないまでも goes down from an ideal (best 4 → best 8). An option that goes the wrong way is always a distractor ([3] Q4, [1〜3] Q1).
Exam trap 3 — the connection of にひきかえ: after a clause or な-adjective, you need の: 頑固なのにひきかえ, 話すのにひきかえ, 穏やかであるのにひきかえ ([1] Q1, [1〜3] Q2). Nouns attach directly (去年にひきかえ).
