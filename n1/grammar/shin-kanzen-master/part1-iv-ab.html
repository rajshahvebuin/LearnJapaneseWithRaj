Meaning (EN)"combined with ~; together with ~ (the effect is heightened)" — book: 〜と関係し合って、さらに程度が進んだりいい効果が出たりする (interacting with ~, the degree increases further or a good effect results)

Set off by the beautiful greenery, that white building looks like a castle from a picture book. सुंदर हरियाली के साथ मिलकर वह सफ़ेद इमारत किसी चित्र-पुस्तक के महल जैसी लगती है। સુંદર હરિયાળી સાથે મળીને એ સફેદ ઇમારત ચિત્રપુસ્તકના મહેલ જેવી લાગે છે.

【A＊】 〜にかこつけて

neutral register (not marked 硬 in book)
Reading~ni kakotsukete
Meaning (EN)"using ~ as a pretext" — book: 〜を表向きの口実にして、別の目的を果たす (making ~ the public excuse, one achieves a different aim)
Meaning (HI)...का बहाना बनाकर (असल में कोई और मक़सद)
Meaning (GU)...નું બહાનું બનાવીને (ખરેખર બીજો હેતુ)
Connection (接続)名 + にかこつけて／にかこつけた + 名 (read off the book's examples)
RegisterNeutral; carries a critical nuance (the real motive is hidden).
Typical useWork, a trip, illness, a cause (エコ) is the official reason; the real aim is something else, often self-serving.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (adverbial)N + にかこつけて取材にかこつけてshuzai ni kakotsukete
Noun (modifying)N + にかこつけた + Nエコにかこつけた金もうけeko ni kakotsuketa kanemouke
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
取材にかこつけて、あちこち店を見て回った。Shuzai ni kakotsukete, achikochi mise o mite mawatta. Using my reporting assignment as an excuse, I went round looking at shops here and there. रिपोर्टिंग का बहाना बनाकर मैं इधर-उधर दुकानें देखता फिरा। રિપોર્ટિંગનું બહાનું બનાવીને હું અહીંતહીં દુકાનો જોતો ફર્યો.
エコ商品への買い換えをやたらに勧めるのは、エコにかこつけた金もうけだ。Eko shouhin e no kaikae o yatara ni susumeru no wa, eko ni kakotsuketa kanemouke da. Pushing people so hard to switch to eco-products is just money-making under the pretext of ecology. बिना वजह इको-उत्पाद ख़रीदने पर ज़ोर देना पर्यावरण के बहाने पैसा कमाना है। વગર કારણે ઇકો-ઉત્પાદનો ખરીદવા પર ભાર મૂકવો એ પર્યાવરણના બહાને પૈસા કમાવા છે.

Near-synonym of 〜を口実に. Don't confuse with 〜をかねて: かねて = two genuine purposes at once; かこつけて = one fake purpose hiding the real one. Particle: に.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

These verb-derived forms come in look-alike clusters. The drills test two things: which verb's meaning fits, and which particle (に／を／と／の) it takes.

〜に恥じない vs 〜てはばからない~ni hajinai / ~te habakaranai worthy of vs. brazenly ~ N + に恥じない／V-て + はばからない Opposites in attitude: living up to a standard vs. ignoring the restraint you ought to show. 名に恥じない / 天才だと言ってはばからない.
Exam trap 1 — the particle is part of the form: に: 即して・則って・照らして・かこつけて・かまけて・かかわる・まつわる・ひきかえ・至る・忍びない・恥じない／を: おして・かねて・ひかえて・踏まえて・経て・禁じ得ない／と: 相まって／の: 至り. Learn each form with its particle — 練習1 marks you wrong for the right verb with the wrong particle.
Exam trap 2 — ひかえる takes に when the event already has を: 入試をひかえて, but 帰国を明日にひかえて／結婚を間近にひかえた (練習1 B-5, B-7). The time word (明日・間近・目前) is what gets に.
Exam trap 3 — four "according to" forms: ask what the noun is. Reality/current conditions → に即して; tradition/rules/style → に則って; a yardstick for judging → に照らして; data or feedback for planning → を踏まえて.
