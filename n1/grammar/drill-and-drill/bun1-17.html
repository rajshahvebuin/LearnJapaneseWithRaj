【Q161】 〜まじき

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Reading~majiki
Meaning (EN)"(a N) that must not be ~ed / unforgivable, unacceptable". It is a strong moral judgment. The 別冊 gives only the ポイント here; for the same pattern in Q171 it gives 意味:「Aがしてはいけない」.
Meaning (HI)...नहीं किया जाना चाहिए ऐसा (N) — कभी माफ़/स्वीकार न होने वाला
Meaning (GU)...ન કરવું જોઈએ એવું (N) — ક્યારેય માફ/સ્વીકાર્ય ન હોય તેવું
Connection (接続)V-dict + まじき + N ／ する → す（る）まじき ／ N（にある）まじき + N
Register硬 — classical negative-obligation auxiliary まじ. Used in news, editorials and speeches.
Typical useCondemning behavior: 許すまじき行為, 許されるまじき犯罪, 教師にあるまじき言動. It always sits right before a noun (行為, 発言, 態度).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + まじき + N許すまじき行為yurusu majiki koui
Noun (role)N + にある + まじき + N (別冊 形, Q171)教師にあるまじき行為kyoushi ni aru majiki koui
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
集団で1人の生徒をいじめることは、許すまじき行為である。(book, Q161)Shuudan de hitori no seito o ijimeru koto wa, yurusu majiki koui de aru. Bullying one student as a group is an act that must never be tolerated. समूह बनाकर किसी एक छात्र को सताना ऐसा काम है जिसे कभी माफ़ नहीं किया जा सकता। ટોળું બનાવીને એક વિદ્યાર્થીને હેરાન કરવો એ એવું કૃત્ય છે જેને ક્યારેય માફ ન કરી શકાય.
それは政治家として言うまじき発言だった。(added, not in book)Sore wa seijika to shite iu majiki hatsugen datta. That was a remark a politician should never make. वह ऐसा बयान था जो एक नेता को कभी नहीं देना चाहिए। એ એવું નિવેદન હતું જે એક રાજકારણીએ ક્યારેય ન કરવું જોઈએ.

まじき is the attributive form of classical まじ (≈ べきではない), so it must be followed by a noun. Its sister form is 〜べからざる (Q171 distractor), which also comes before a noun. The 別冊 links this to Q【154】 (参照), and the same pattern is tested again right away in Q【171】 as 教師にあるまじき.

【Q162】 〜ながら（も）

neutral–written register
Reading~nagara (mo)
Meaning (EN)"although / even though ~". It is a concessive "but", not the "while doing" ながら. The 別冊 gives only the ポイント here.
Meaning (HI)...होते हुए भी / ...के बावजूद
Meaning (GU)...હોવા છતાં / ...છતાં પણ
Connection (接続)V-ます stem ／ い-adj ／ な-adj stem ／ N + ながら（も）
RegisterNeutral; も makes it a bit more written
Typical useState words: 貧しいながらも, 狭いながらも, 残念ながら, 子供ながら, 知っていながら.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj + ながら（も）貧しいながらもmazushii nagara mo
Verb(added, not in book) V-ます stem / V-ている stem + ながら（も）知っていながらshitte inagara
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj stem + ながら（も）不便ながらもfuben nagara mo
Noun(added, not in book) N + ながら（も）子供ながらkodomo nagara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この村の人たちは、貧しいながらも平和な日々を送っている。(book, Q162)Kono mura no hitotachi wa, mazushii nagara mo heiwa na hibi o okutte iru. The people of this village live peaceful days, even though they are poor. इस गाँव के लोग गरीब होते हुए भी शांति भरे दिन बिता रहे हैं। આ ગામના લોકો ગરીબ હોવા છતાં શાંતિભર્યા દિવસો વિતાવી રહ્યા છે.
狭いながらも、ここは私の大切な家だ。(added, not in book)Semai nagara mo, koko wa watashi no taisetsu na ie da. Small as it is, this is my precious home. छोटा होते हुए भी, यह मेरा प्यारा घर है। નાનું હોવા છતાં, આ મારું વહાલું ઘર છે.

The two halves are in tension: poor ↔ peaceful. That "contrary" relation is exactly what ながらも signals. Don't confuse it with にしては (deviation from an expected standard) or からといって (which needs a negative ending like わけではない). The 別冊 links this to Q【127】 (参照).

【Q163】 〜てございます

polite (丁寧語) — customer-service register
Reading~te gozaimasu
Meaning (EN)"(it) has been ~ed / is ~ed (for you)". It is the very polite form of 〜てあります. 別冊 意味:「Aてあります」(丁寧語)
Meaning (HI)...किया हुआ है (बहुत विनम्र रूप)
Meaning (GU)...કરેલું છે (ખૂબ નમ્ર રૂપ)
Connection (接続)〜は／が + V-て + ございます (別冊 形: [〜は／が A てございます] A＝動詞・て形)
Register丁寧語 — shops, hotels, reception desks, announcements
Typical useDescribing a prepared state with a transitive verb: 書いて／用意して／置いて ございます.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (transitive)V-て + ございます書いてございますkaite gozaimasu
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not this pattern. (Noun + でございます is a different, "です" form.)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
商品の詳しいご案内はカタログに書いてございます。(book, Q163)Shouhin no kuwashii go-annai wa katarogu ni kaite gozaimasu. Detailed information on our products is written in the catalogue. उत्पादों की विस्तृत जानकारी कैटलॉग में लिखी हुई है। ઉત્પાદનોની વિગતવાર માહિતી કેટલોગમાં લખેલી છે.
必要なものは全部用意してございます。(別冊 使い方)Hitsuyou na mono wa zenbu youi shite gozaimasu. Everything you need has been prepared. ज़रूरत की सारी चीज़ें तैयार रखी हुई हैं। જરૂરી બધી વસ્તુઓ તૈયાર રાખેલી છે.
受付に案内のパンフレットが置いてございますので、ご自由にお取りください。(別冊 使い方)Uketsuke ni annai no panfuretto ga oite gozaimasu node, go-jiyuu ni otori kudasai. Information leaflets have been placed at reception, so please help yourself. रिसेप्शन पर जानकारी की पुस्तिकाएँ रखी हुई हैं, कृपया बेझिझक ले लें। રિસેપ્શન પર માહિતીની પુસ્તિકાઓ રાખેલી છે, કૃપા કરીને મરજી મુજબ લઈ લેજો.

ございます is the polite form of ある, so 〜てございます = 〜てあります. The subject is a thing (ご案内, パンフレット), which is why the honorific verbs in the other options (いらっしゃる, なさる, ご覧になる), which need a respected person as subject, fail.

【Q164】 ご覧に入れる

humble (謙譲語) — very polite
Readinggoran ni ireru
Meaning (EN)"(I) show (you)" in very humble keigo. 別冊 意味:「見せる」(謙譲語)
Meaning (HI)(मैं आपको) दिखाता हूँ — अत्यंत विनम्र रूप
Meaning (GU)(હું આપને) બતાવું છું — અત્યંત નમ્ર રૂપ
Connection (接続)（私は）（あなたに）N を ご覧に入れる (別冊 形)
Register謙譲語 — more polite than お見せする (別冊 ⚠)
Typical usePerformers, guides and hosts: マジックをご覧に入れましょう, 資料をご覧に入れます.
Word typeFormationExample
Fixed verb phraseN を + ご覧に入れるマジックをご覧に入れましょうmajikku o goran ni iremashou
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used — it replaces the verb 見せる itself—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
おもしろいものがあるんですよ。今ご覧に入れましょう。(book, Q164)Omoshiroi mono ga aru n desu yo. Ima goran ni iremashou. I have something interesting. Let me show it to you now. एक दिलचस्प चीज़ है। मैं अभी आपको दिखाता हूँ। એક રસપ્રદ વસ્તુ છે. હું હમણાં આપને બતાવું.
さあ、これからみなさんにおもしろいマジックをご覧に入れましょう。(別冊 使い方)Saa, kore kara minasan ni omoshiroi majikku o goran ni iremashou. Now then, let me show you all an amusing magic trick. तो चलिए, अब मैं आप सबको एक मज़ेदार जादू दिखाता हूँ। તો ચાલો, હવે હું આપ સૌને એક મજેદાર જાદુ બતાવું.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): ご覧に入れる is an even more polite way of saying お見せする (also humble). Keep the keigo "seeing" family straight: ご覧になる = (you) see (尊敬), 拝見する = (I) see (謙譲), ご覧に入れる／お目にかける = (I) show (謙譲), お目にかかる = (I) meet (謙譲).

【Q165】 〜かぎりだ

neutral–written register
Reading~kagiri da
Meaning (EN)"(I feel) extremely ~ / nothing could be more ~". It states the speaker's own strong feeling. The 別冊 gives only the ポイント here.
Meaning (HI)बेहद / बहुत ही ... लग रहा है
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત / ખૂબ જ ... લાગે છે
Connection (接続)い-adj + かぎりだ ／ な-adj-な + かぎりだ ／ N-の + かぎりだ
RegisterSlightly formal; common in letters and speeches
Typical useEmotion adjectives only: 寂しい／うれしい／心細い／うらやましい／残念な かぎりだ.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj + かぎりだ寂しいかぎりだsabishii kagiri da
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-な + かぎりだ残念なかぎりだzannen na kagiri da
Noun(added, not in book) N-の + かぎりだ (rare: 喜びのかぎりだ)喜びのかぎりだyorokobi no kagiri da
Verb(added, not in book) not used in this meaning—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
町の住民に愛されてきた映画館が閉鎖されることになり、寂しいかぎりだ。(book, Q165)Machi no juumin ni aisarete kita eigakan ga heisa sareru koto ni nari, sabishii kagiri da. The cinema the townspeople have loved so long is going to close, and I couldn't feel sadder. शहर के लोगों का प्यारा सिनेमाघर बंद होने वाला है, यह बेहद उदास कर देने वाली बात है। નગરના લોકોનું પ્રિય સિનેમાઘર બંધ થવાનું છે, એ અત્યંત ઉદાસ કરી દે તેવી વાત છે.
遠くから応援に来てくれて、うれしいかぎりです。(added, not in book)Tooku kara ouen ni kite kurete, ureshii kagiri desu. I'm so happy you came all this way to support us. आप इतनी दूर से हौसला बढ़ाने आए, मुझे बेहद खुशी है। આપ આટલે દૂરથી પ્રોત્સાહન આપવા આવ્યા, મને અત્યંત આનંદ છે.

The blank comes right after an い-adjective of feeling (寂しい), and かぎりだ is the only option that attaches there. を禁じえない needs a noun (寂しさを禁じえない), and しまつだ needs a verb describing a bad outcome. The 別冊 links this to Q【25】 (参照).

【Q166】 NたるN

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
ReadingN taru N
Meaning (EN)"one who is (in the position of) N / as a (proper) N". It stresses that the role brings high standards. The 別冊 gives only the ポイント plus its own English: "As a prime minister, he needs to be very careful when telling a joke."
Meaning (HI)(जो) N के पद पर है / N होने के नाते
Meaning (GU)(જે) N ના પદ પર છે / N હોવાને નાતે
Connection (接続)N (role / status) + たる + N (者・もの・人)
Register硬 — classical copula たり, attributive たる
Typical useStatus nouns + a duty: 首相たる者, 教師たる者, 医者たるもの, 王たる者 + 〜べきだ／〜なければならない.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (status)N + たる + 者首相たる者shushou taru mono
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
首相たる者、冗談を言うにも十分な注意が必要だ。(book, Q166)Shushou taru mono, joudan o iu ni mo juubun na chuui ga hitsuyou da. Someone who is prime minister must take great care even when making a joke. प्रधानमंत्री के पद पर बैठे व्यक्ति को मज़ाक करते समय भी पूरी सावधानी बरतनी चाहिए। વડાપ્રધાનના પદ પર બેઠેલી વ્યક્તિએ મજાક કરતી વખતે પણ પૂરી સાવચેતી રાખવી જોઈએ.
教師たる者、生徒の手本でなければならない。(added, not in book)Kyoushi taru mono, seito no tehon de nakereba naranai. A teacher must be a model for the students. शिक्षक होने के नाते, उसे छात्रों के लिए आदर्श होना चाहिए। શિક્ષક હોવાને નાતે, તેણે વિદ્યાર્થીઓ માટે આદર્શ બનવું જોઈએ.

NたるN almost always pairs with a "should / must" ending in the second half, as in this sentence (注意が必要だ). The noun before たる is a respected role. The 別冊 links this to Q【36】 (参照).

【Q167】 〜が早いか

written / narrative register
Reading~ga hayai ka
Meaning (EN)"no sooner had (someone) ~ than…; the instant ~". The second action follows almost at the same moment. The 別冊 gives only the ポイント here.
Meaning (HI)...करते ही (तुरंत) / ...किया नहीं कि
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ (તરત) / ...કર્યું નહીં કે
Connection (接続)V-dict + が早いか
RegisterWritten, used in narratives about past events
Typical useA third person's quick actions: ベルが鳴るが早いか教室を飛び出した. The second half is a past-tense fact, not a wish or order.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + が早いか切るが早いかkiru ga hayai ka
Verb (た)(added, not in book) V-た + が早いか is also seen言ったが早いかitta ga hayai ka
Adjectives / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
記者たちは電話を切るが早いか、カメラをもって部屋を飛び出して行った。(book, Q167)Kishatachi wa denwa o kiru ga hayai ka, kamera o motte heya o tobidashite itta. The instant the reporters hung up the phone, they grabbed their cameras and rushed out of the room. रिपोर्टरों ने फ़ोन रखा नहीं कि कैमरा उठाकर कमरे से बाहर भाग गए। રિપોર્ટરોએ ફોન મૂક્યો નહીં કે કેમેરા લઈને ઓરડાની બહાર દોડી ગયા.
子供たちはチャイムが鳴るが早いか、校庭へ駆け出した。(added, not in book)Kodomotachi wa chaimu ga naru ga hayai ka, koutei e kakedashita. No sooner had the bell rung than the children dashed out to the playground. घंटी बजते ही बच्चे खेल के मैदान की ओर दौड़ पड़े। ઘંટ વાગતાં જ બાળકો રમતના મેદાન તરફ દોડી ગયાં.

Its near-twins are 〜や否や (〜やいなや) and 〜なり. All three mean "as soon as" and need a past-fact second half. The 別冊 links this to Q【152】 (参照).

【Q168】 〜たところで

This is the same pattern as Q5 in 第1回. The four options here are all 〜ところ + a particle, so the particle decides the meaning: で = "even if (useless)", を = "at the moment (caught / interrupted)", に = "just when (something else happened)", が = "but, unexpectedly". The どんなに … わけではない frame only fits で.

HI: चाहे कितना भी रो लो, दुख मना लो, मरा हुआ इंसान वापस नहीं आएगा।
GU: ગમે તેટલું રડો, દુઃખ મનાવો, મરેલો માણસ પાછો નહીં આવે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ところがtokoro gabut (unexpectedly)लेकिन (अचानक उल्टा)પણ (અણધાર્યું ઊલટું)
2ところにtokoro nijust when (something else came)ठीक उसी समय जबબરાબર એ જ વખતે જ્યારે
3ところをtokoro oat the moment (caught / interrupted)उसी पल (पकड़ा गया)એ જ પળે (પકડાયો)
4ところでtokoro deeven if (it's no use)...कर भी लो तो भी (बेकार)...કરી પણ લો તો પણ (નકામું)

Why: 別冊 p.24: ポイント〈ところで〉, 形 [Aところで] A＝た形, 意味 「Aしても（だめだろう）」. どんなに … わけではない is the typical "no matter how much, it won't…" frame. 〜たところが reports an unexpected result of one action ("when I tried…, it turned out…"), and it doesn't combine with どんなに. 〜たところに needs something arriving or happening at that moment (帰ったところに電話が来た). 〜たところを needs a verb like 見られる／助ける after it.

Q169

あなたはこの会社の最高責任者なのだから、この問題について（　）のですよ。Anata wa kono kaisha no saikou sekininsha na no da kara, kono mondai ni tsuite (___) no desu yo.

Full sentence: …この問題について知らないではすまないのですよ。...kono mondai ni tsuite shiranai de wa sumanai no desu yo.
EN: You are the top person in charge of this company, so "I didn't know" about this problem won't be accepted.
HI: आप इस कंपनी के सर्वोच्च ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति हैं, इसलिए इस समस्या के बारे में "मुझे पता नहीं था" कहने से काम नहीं चलेगा।
GU: આપ આ કંપનીના સર્વોચ્ચ જવાબદાર વ્યક્તિ છો, એટલે આ સમસ્યા વિશે "મને ખબર નહોતી" કહેવાથી નહીં ચાલે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1知らないではおかないshiranai de wa okanai(✗) will definitely not-know (nonsense)(अर्थहीन)(અર્થહીન)
2知らないでもないshiranai de mo naiI do know a littleथोड़ा-बहुत पता तो हैથોડુંઘણું ખબર તો છે
3知らないではすまないshiranai de wa sumanainot knowing won't be excused"पता नहीं था" कहने से काम नहीं चलेगा"ખબર નહોતી" કહેવાથી નહીં ચાલે
4知らないではいられないshiranai de wa irarenai(✗) can't help not knowing(अर्थहीन)(અર્થહીન)

Why: 別冊 p.24: ポイント〈ないではすまない〉 = 「Aないわけにはいかない／Aしなければならない」; ⚠ it is what you must do to settle a bad situation. "Because you are the top person (なのだから), ignorance is not acceptable" fits exactly. 〜ないではおかない expresses a strong will or an inevitable effect ("will surely make…"), and it doesn't fit 知る. 知らないでもない ("I'm not completely ignorant") is a weak admission, wrong after a なのだから reproach. 〜ないではいられない is about an uncontrollable urge, and "can't help not knowing" is meaningless.

Q170

当社では、幅広いニーズに（　）、さまざまなサービスを提供している。Tousha de wa, habahiroi niizu ni (___), samazama na saabisu o teikyou shite iru.

Full sentence: 当社では、幅広いニーズにこたえるべく、さまざまなサービスを提供している。...niizu ni kotaeru beku, ...
EN: Our company offers a variety of services in order to meet a wide range of needs.
HI: हमारी कंपनी तरह-तरह की ज़रूरतों को पूरा करने के लिए कई प्रकार की सेवाएँ देती है।
GU: અમારી કંપની વિવિધ જરૂરિયાતો પૂરી કરવા માટે અનેક પ્રકારની સેવાઓ આપે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1こたえがてらkotae gaterawhile (also) meeting… (as a side activity)...के साथ-साथ...ની સાથે સાથે
2こたえるべくkotaeru bekuin order to meetपूरा करने के लिएપૂરી કરવા માટે
3こたえるともなるとkotaeru tomo naru towhen it comes to meeting…जब पूरा करने की बात आती है तोજ્યારે પૂરી કરવાની વાત આવે ત્યારે
4こたえるとばかりにkotaeru to bakari nias if to say "(I'll) meet them"मानो कह रहे हों "पूरा करेंगे"જાણે કહેતા હોય "પૂરી કરીશું"

Why: 別冊 p.24: ポイント〈べく〉 = 「Aするために」, 硬い表現. Meeting needs is the purpose, and offering services is the deliberate action. がてら needs two actions where one is done "on the way" (散歩がてら), and it attaches to an action noun or ます-stem, not a purpose. ともなると introduces a higher level or special case (社長ともなると) and needs a noun. とばかりに describes someone's visible attitude, "as if to say ~", which doesn't fit a company's service policy.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a 文の文法1 question, the four options are usually a ready-made confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 ⚠ notes):

ご覧になるgoran ni naru (you) see 尊敬語 of 見る Raises the listener who sees どうぞご覧になってください

〜べく vs 〜がてら~beku / ~gatera in order to / while also V-dict + べく ／ N・V-stem + がてら べく = purpose (硬); がてら = one trip, two aims 解決すべく努力 ／ 散歩がてら買い物
Exam trap — the ない-family endings: Q169 offers ないではおかない / ないでもない / ないではすまない / ないではいられない on one verb. Decide by where the pressure comes from: duty from outside → すまない; inner urge → いられない; strong will or inevitable effect → おかない; "might, a little" → ないでもない. Here the clue is なのだから (a reproach based on social position), so it's duty: すまない.
Exam trap — keigo direction: In Q163–164 every option is "polite", so politeness alone won't decide. Ask two questions: who acts? and is the subject a person or a thing? A thing as subject → 丁寧語 (〜てございます). The speaker acts toward the listener → 謙譲語 (ご覧に入れる). The listener acts → 尊敬語 (ご覧になる, いらっしゃる). お〜いただく always means the other person does it.
