【Q211】 よもや〜（ない／まい）

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Readingyomoya ~ (nai / mai)
Meaning (EN)"surely not / by no means / it is unthinkable that ~". 別冊 意味:「まさか（考えられない）」. A strong, old-fashioned version of まさか.
Meaning (HI)हरगिज़ नहीं / ऐसा सोचा भी नहीं जा सकता कि…
Meaning (GU)હરગિજ નહીં / એવું વિચારી પણ ન શકાય કે…
Connection (接続)よもや + 否定文 (〜ない／〜まい／〜とは思わなかった)　(別冊 形: [よもや A（ない／まい）] A＝否定文)
Register硬 — written or very formal speech; まさか is the everyday equivalent
Typical useRejecting a possibility as unthinkable: よもや〜まい ("surely ~ won't be"), よもや〜とは思わなかった ("I never dreamed that ~").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (negative guess)よもや + V-dict + まいよもや負けるまいyomoya makeru mai
Noun / な-adjよもや + N／な-adj + ではあるまいよもや本気ではあるまいyomoya honki de wa arumai
Past thoughtよもや + 〜とは思わなかったよもや彼が犯人だとは思っていなかったyomoya kare ga hannin da to wa omotte inakatta
い-adjective(added, not in book) よもや + い-adj-く + はあるまいよもや難しくはあるまいyomoya muzukashiku wa arumai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人がそんなばかなことを言うはずがない。よもや本気ではあるまい。(book, Q211)Ano hito ga sonna baka na koto o iu hazu ga nai. Yomoya honki de wa arumai. That person would never say something so foolish. Surely he can't have been serious. वह आदमी ऐसी बेवकूफ़ी की बात कह ही नहीं सकता। वह हरगिज़ गंभीरता से नहीं कह रहा होगा। એ માણસ આવી મૂર્ખામીભરી વાત કહી જ ન શકે. એ હરગિજ ગંભીરતાથી નહીં કહેતો હોય.
私はよもや彼が犯人だとは思っていなかった。(別冊 使い方)Watashi wa yomoya kare ga hannin da to wa omotte inakatta. I never for a moment thought that he was the culprit. मैंने सपने में भी नहीं सोचा था कि वही अपराधी है। મેં સપનામાં પણ નહોતું વિચાર્યું કે એ જ ગુનેગાર છે.
それはよもや真実ではあるまい。(別冊 使い方)Sore wa yomoya shinjitsu de wa arumai. That surely cannot be the truth. (cf. 別冊's own English) वह हरगिज़ सच नहीं हो सकता। એ હરગિજ સાચું ન હોઈ શકે.

よもや always needs a negative after it, so the answer must be a negative guess (〜まい = 〜ないだろう). That alone removes options 2 and 3, which are positive statements. Option 4 (予想できなくもない, "it's not entirely unpredictable") is a weak double negative and doesn't carry the "surely not" meaning. The 別冊 gives no 参照 for this point.

【Q212】 〜にたえない

neutral–written register
Reading~ni taenai
Meaning (EN)① "too awful to (look at / listen to / read)". 別冊 意味①:「Aことができないほどひどい」(A＝見る・聞く・読む など) ② (with feeling nouns) "be overwhelmed with ~". 別冊 意味②:「とてもAしている」(A＝感謝 など)
Meaning (HI)① इतना बुरा कि देखा/सुना न जाए ② (भावना) …से अभिभूत
Meaning (GU)① એટલું ખરાબ કે જોઈ/સાંભળી ન શકાય ② (લાગણી) …થી ગદ્ગદ
Connection (接続)V-dict (見る・聞く・読む) + にたえない ／ N (感謝・感激・遺憾) + にたえない　(別冊 形: [Aにたえない] A＝動詞・辞書形、名詞)
RegisterWritten / formal; meaning ② appears in speeches and letters
Typical use① Describing a scene, words or writing that is painful to witness: 見るにたえない, 聞くにたえない, 読むにたえない. ② 感謝にたえない, 遺憾にたえない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dict.)V-dict + にたえない見るにたえないmiru ni taenai
Noun (feeling)N + にたえない感謝にたえませんkansha ni taemasen
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
飛行機が墜落した現場は、見るにたえないひどい状況だった。(book, Q212)Hikouki ga tsuiraku shita genba wa, miru ni taenai hidoi joukyou datta. The site where the plane crashed was in a terrible state, too awful to look at. जहाँ विमान गिरा था, वहाँ की हालत इतनी भयानक थी कि देखी न जाए। જ્યાં વિમાન તૂટી પડ્યું હતું, ત્યાંની હાલત એટલી ભયાનક હતી કે જોઈ ન શકાય.
2人の言い争いは聞くにたえないものだった。(別冊 使い方 ①)Futari no iiarasoi wa kiku ni taenai mono datta. The quarrel between the two was unbearable to listen to. दोनों का झगड़ा सुनने लायक नहीं था (इतना बुरा था)। બંનેનો ઝઘડો સાંભળી ન શકાય એવો હતો.
温かいご支援をいただき、感謝にたえません。(別冊 使い方 ②)Atatakai go-shien o itadaki, kansha ni taemasen. I am deeply grateful for your warm support. आपके स्नेहपूर्ण सहयोग के लिए मैं हृदय से आभारी हूँ। આપના હૂંફાળા સહકાર બદલ હું હૃદયથી આભારી છું.

Meaning ① is negative ("so bad you can't bear to ~"); meaning ② is positive and formal. Don't mix it up with 〜にたえる (Q227, "be fit for / stand up to") or with 〜にかたくない (Q211 option, "not hard to ~"). The four options here are all 〜に + negative endings, so learn each one's meaning (see §3).

【Q213】 〜ど（も）、〜ど（も）

literary / written register
Reading~do (mo), ~do (mo)
Meaning (EN)"no matter how much (I) ~, ~". 別冊 意味:「Aしても、Aしても／どんなにAしても」. Classical concessive ど／ども, repeated for emphasis.
Meaning (HI)चाहे कितना भी …करो, (फिर भी नहीं)
Meaning (GU)ગમે તેટલું …કરો, (તો પણ નહીં)
Connection (接続)V-ば-form without ば (え段) + ど（も）　働く→働けど(も), 行く→行けど(も), する→すれど(も)　(別冊 形)
RegisterLiterary; used in written Japanese, poems and set phrases
Typical useEffort that repeatedly fails: 働けど働けど, 待てど暮らせど, 行けども行けども. The second half is negative (〜ない／まだ〜).
Word typeFormationExample
Godan verbえ段 + ど(も)働けど働けどhatarakedo hatarakedo
する-verbすれ + ど(も)勉強すれど勉強すれどbenkyou suredo benkyou suredo
Ichidan verb(added, not in book) V-れ + ど(も)見れども見れどもmiredomo miredomo
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
働けど働けど暮らしが一向に良くならないという気の毒な人たちが増えている。(book, Q213)Hatarakedo hatarakedo kurashi ga ikkou ni yoku naranai to iu kinodoku na hitotachi ga fuete iru. There are more and more unfortunate people whose lives don't get any better no matter how hard they work. ऐसे बेचारे लोग बढ़ रहे हैं जिनकी ज़िंदगी चाहे जितना काम करें, ज़रा भी नहीं सुधरती। એવા બિચારા લોકો વધી રહ્યા છે જેમનું જીવન ગમે તેટલું કામ કરે, જરાય સુધરતું નથી.
勉強すれど勉強すれど、成績は上がらない。(別冊 使い方)Benkyou suredo benkyou suredo, seiseki wa agaranai. No matter how hard I study, my grades don't go up. चाहे कितना भी पढ़ूँ, नंबर नहीं बढ़ते। ગમે તેટલું ભણું, માર્ક્સ વધતા નથી.
登れども、登れども、山の頂上はまだ遠い。(別冊 使い方)Noboredomo, noboredomo, yama no choujou wa mada tooi. However far I climb, the summit is still far away. चाहे जितना चढ़ूँ, पहाड़ की चोटी अभी भी दूर है। ગમે તેટલું ચઢું, પહાડનું શિખર હજુ દૂર છે.

働けど働けど is a famous line from the poet 石川啄木 (はたらけど はたらけど猶わが生活楽にならざり), so it feels literary. Only ど／ども works as a repeated concessive. のに can't be repeated like this, とも ("even if") is hypothetical, and ながら has no "no matter how" meaning.

【Q214】 〜とあって

neutral–written register
Reading~to atte
Meaning (EN)"because (it is the special occasion) ~, naturally ~". 別冊 意味:「Aだから、やはり」(A is a natural reason, and the pattern stresses A)
Meaning (HI)चूँकि (ख़ास मौक़ा) …है, इसलिए स्वाभाविक रूप से…
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે (ખાસ પ્રસંગ) …છે, તેથી સ્વાભાવિક રીતે…
Connection (接続)V/Adj 普通形 + とあって ／ N + とあって　(別冊 形: [Aとあって] A＝動詞・普通形、名詞)
RegisterNeutral, common in news reports
Typical useA special situation and the expected result: 連休の最終日とあって渋滞, 夏祭りとあってにぎやか, 人気歌手が来るとあって大勢集まった.

NounN + とあって最終日とあってsaishuubi to atte
VerbV-普通形 + とあって台風が来るとあってtaifuu ga kuru to atte
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + とあって安いとあってyasui to atte
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj (だ) + とあって有名とあってyuumei to atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
3連休の最終日とあって、東京方面へ向かう高速道路は大渋滞となっている。(book, Q214)San-renkyuu no saishuubi to atte, Toukyou houmen e mukau kousoku douro wa daijuutai to natte iru. As it is the last day of the three-day weekend, the expressways heading toward Tokyo are badly jammed. तीन दिन की छुट्टी का आख़िरी दिन होने की वजह से टोक्यो की ओर जाने वाले हाईवे पर भारी जाम है। ત્રણ દિવસની રજાનો છેલ્લો દિવસ હોવાથી ટોક્યો તરફ જતા હાઈવે પર ભારે ટ્રાફિક જામ છે.
今週末は夏祭りとあって、町がにぎやかだ。(別冊 使い方)Konshuumatsu wa natsumatsuri to atte, machi ga nigiyaka da. Since it's the summer festival this weekend, the town is lively. इस हफ़्ते के अंत में ग्रीष्म-उत्सव है, इसलिए शहर में रौनक है। આ સપ્તાહાંતે ઉનાળુ ઉત્સવ હોવાથી શહેરમાં રોનક છે.
台風が来るとあって、町は人通りが少ない。(別冊 使い方)Taifuu ga kuru to atte, machi wa hitodoori ga sukunai. With a typhoon coming, there are few people on the streets. तूफ़ान आने वाला है, इसलिए शहर में लोगों की आवाजाही कम है। વાવાઝોડું આવવાનું હોવાથી શહેરમાં લોકોની અવરજવર ઓછી છે.

The result clause is a fact that anyone would expect, not the speaker's will or request. The other options are all "N + particle" reason/means words: をもって = "by means of / as of (a date)", こととて = apologetic reason (慣れぬこととて), とて = "even (if)" in old style. Only とあって gives "because it's that special day".

The 別冊 links this to Q【173】 (参照), where the pattern is explained. お休みのところ(を) is a fixed polite phrase: the を after ところ shows that you are "intruding on" that state. なか (〜のなか) works with weather (お足元の悪いなか) but not with お休み. さい needs an action (〜の際), and うえ means "on top of" or "after".

The 別冊 links this to Q【181】 (参照). The ending 思いもよらなかった ("never even crossed my mind") is the signal for とは. なんか is a casual "something like" and is too light, くせして needs a "despite (criticism)" contrast, and とはいえ means "although", which needs a contrasting second half.

【Q217】 〜ないではおかない

neutral–written register
Reading~nai de wa okanai

Q216

Full sentence: そのとき彼女が自殺まで考えていたとは、思いもよらなかった。Sono toki kanojo ga jisatsu made kangaete ita to wa, omoi mo yoranakatta.
EN: I never imagined that she had even been thinking of suicide at that time.
HI: मैंने सोचा भी नहीं था कि उस समय वह आत्महत्या तक के बारे में सोच रही थी।
GU: મેં વિચાર્યું પણ નહોતું કે એ વખતે એ આત્મહત્યા સુધીનું વિચારતી હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なんかnankasomething like (casual)जैसा कुछ (बोलचाल)જેવું કંઈક (બોલચાલ)
2とはto wathat ~ (surprise)कि … (आश्चर्य)કે … (આશ્ચર્ય)
3くせしてkuse shitedespite (critical)होने के बावजूद (आलोचना)હોવા છતાં (ટીકા)
4とはいえto wa iealthough / even soहालाँकिજોકે

Why: 別冊 p.29: ポイント〈とは〉, 参照【181】. 〜とは marks the surprising content, and 思いもよらなかった ("it never occurred to me") is the typical ending. なんか is casual and belittling, which is out of place in such a serious sentence. 〜くせして criticizes someone ("even though she…") and needs a contrasting complaint. 〜とはいえ ("although") needs a contrasting second clause, but 思いもよらなかった is the speaker's reaction, not a contrast.

Q217

ころころと変わる国の政策は、国民に不信感を与え（　）だろう。Korokoro to kawaru kuni no seisaku wa, kokumin ni fushinkan o atae (___) darou.

Full sentence: ころころと変わる国の政策は、国民に不信感を与えないではおかないだろう。Korokoro to kawaru kuni no seisaku wa, kokumin ni fushinkan o ataenai de wa okanai darou.
EN: Government policies that keep changing will inevitably make the people distrustful. (cf. 別冊's own English)
HI: बार-बार बदलने वाली सरकारी नीतियाँ जनता में अविश्वास पैदा किए बिना नहीं रहेंगी।
GU: વારંવાર બદલાતી સરકારી નીતિઓ પ્રજામાં અવિશ્વાસ જગાવ્યા વગર નહીં રહે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なくてはすまないnakute wa sumanai(not a set pattern; cf. ないではすまない = must ~)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય રૂપ)
2ないではおかないnai de wa okanaiwill inevitably cause ~…किए बिना नहीं रहेगा…કર્યા વગર નહીં રહે
3てすむことではないte sumu koto de wa naiit can't be settled just by ~ingसिर्फ़ …करने से बात नहीं बनेगीફક્ત …કરવાથી વાત નહીં પતે
4てやまないte yamanaisincerely / never stop ~ing (wish)दिल से …करते रहनाદિલથી …કરતા રહેવું

Why: 別冊 p.29: ポイント〈ないではおかない〉, 参照【88】. A thing (constantly changing policy) naturally and inevitably produces a reaction (distrust) in people. That is the "inevitable effect" use of ないではおかない／ずにはおかない. Option 1 is not a set expression; the "must" pattern is ないではすまない, and "policy must give distrust" would be illogical anyway. Option 3 would mean "giving distrust is not something that settles matters", which makes no sense. てやまない is for a person's heartfelt wish (願ってやまない), not for a policy's effect.

Q218

「お客さまはもう（　）か」「いいえ、まだいらっしゃいません」"Okyakusama wa mou (___) ka." "Iie, mada irasshaimasen."

Full sentence: 「お客さまはもう見えましたか」「いいえ、まだいらっしゃいません」"Okyakusama wa mou miemashita ka." "Iie, mada irasshaimasen."
EN: "Has our guest arrived yet?" "No, not yet."
HI: "क्या मेहमान पधार चुके हैं?" "नहीं, अभी तक नहीं पधारे।"
GU: "શું મહેમાન પધારી ગયા?" "ના, હજુ નથી પધાર્યા."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見えましたmiemashitahas (the guest) come (honorific)पधारे (आदरसूचक)પધાર્યા (આદરસૂચક)
2お見えしましたomie shimashita(wrong: humble お〜する pattern on 見える)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)
3お目にかかりましたome ni kakarimashitaI met (humble)मैं मिला (विनम्र)હું મળ્યો (નમ્ર)
4お目にかかられたome ni kakarareta(wrong: honorific られる on a humble verb)(ग़लत रूप)(ખોટું રૂપ)

Why: 別冊 p.29: ポイント〈見える〉, 形 [見えます／お見えになります／お見えです], 意味「来る」（尊敬語）. The reply いらっしゃいません ("hasn't come") shows the question is about the guest coming, so we need honorific "come": 見えましたか. お見えしました mixes the humble frame お〜する with the guest's action. お目にかかる is the speaker humbly meeting someone, so "did the guest meet (me)?" is wrong, and お目にかかられる stacks honorific られる on a humble verb, which is a classic keigo error.

Q219

試験の受験者は、450人から460人（　）です。Shiken no jukensha wa, yonhyaku gojuu-nin kara yonhyaku rokujuu-nin (___) desu.

Full sentence: 試験の受験者は、450人から460人といったところです。Shiken no jukensha wa, yonhyaku gojuu-nin kara yonhyaku rokujuu-nin to itta tokoro desu.
EN: The number of examinees is about 450 to 460.
HI: परीक्षा देने वाले लगभग 450 से 460 लोग हैं।
GU: પરીક્ષા આપનારા આશરે 450 થી 460 લોકો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1というものto iu mono(〜てからというもの = ever since; not for amounts)(मात्रा के लिए नहीं)(માત્રા માટે નહીં)
2といったところto itta tokoroabout / roughlyलगभगઆશરે
3といったことto itta kotothings such as…जैसी बातें…જેવી બાબતો
4といったわけto itta wake(not a pattern)(अमान्य)(અયોગ્ય)

Why: 別冊 p.29: ポイント〈といったところだ〉, 形 [Aといったところだ] A = 名詞, 意味「Aぐらいだ」 (A shows a degree or amount). A number range like 450人から460人 is exactly the kind of rough figure this pattern gives. というもの is used in time phrases (この3年というもの "these three years", 〜てからというもの), not approximations. といったこと lists examples ("things like…"), and といったわけ is not a set phrase.

Q220

この虫に刺されると、毒のために呼吸困難になる（　）ある。Kono mushi ni sasareru to, doku no tame ni kokyuu konnan ni naru (___) aru.

Full sentence: この虫に刺されると、毒のために呼吸困難になることすらある。Kono mushi ni sasareru to, doku no tame ni kokyuu konnan ni naru koto sura aru.
EN: If you are stung by this insect, its poison can even cause breathing difficulties.
HI: इस कीड़े के डंक मारने पर ज़हर की वजह से साँस लेने में तकलीफ़ तक हो सकती है।
GU: આ જીવડું ડંખ મારે તો ઝેરને કારણે શ્વાસ લેવામાં તકલીફ સુધ્ધાં થઈ શકે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1さえsaeeven (needs こと before ある here)…तक (यहाँ こと चाहिए)…સુધ્ધાં (અહીં こと જોઈએ)
2ことこそkoto kosoit is precisely ~ thatठीक यही कि…ચોક્કસ એ જ કે…
3ことすらkoto surait can even (happen that) ~…तक हो सकता है…સુધ્ધાં થઈ શકે
4だにdanieven (with 想像・夢; literary)तक (साहित्यिक)સુધ્ધાં (સાહિત્યિક)

Why: 別冊 p.29: ポイント〈すら〉, 参照【91】. The structure is V-dict + こと + すら + ある ("it can even happen that ~"), with breathing difficulty as the extreme example. さえ means the same, but なるさえある is ungrammatical because a dictionary-form verb needs こと before the particle. ことこそある would stress "precisely this happens", which has no point here. だに only goes with a few nouns or verbs (想像だにしない, 考えるだに恐ろしい).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a 文の文法1 question, the four options are usually a ready-made confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にたえない~ni taenai too awful to ~ / overwhelmed with 見る・聞く・読む／感謝 + にたえない Negative about the thing seen or heard (①), or a formal feeling (②) 見るにたえない光景
〜にたえる~ni taeru stand up to / be worth ~ N（鑑賞・実用・批判）+ にたえる Positive: "good enough for ~" (Q227) 鑑賞にたえる作品
〜にたりない／にたる~ni tarinai / ni taru not worth / worth ~ V-dict + にたりない Judges value or importance 取るにたりない問題
〜にかたくない~ni katakunai easy to (imagine) 想像・察する + にかたくない Only with 想像・察する・理解 苦労は想像にかたくない
〜にはあたらない~ni wa ataranai no need to ~ 驚く・非難する + にはあたらない Says a reaction is unnecessary 驚くにはあたらない
〜とあって~to atte because it is (special) N／普通形 + とあって A special situation → expected result (fact) 連休とあって混雑
〜こととて~koto tote because (apologetic) V-ない／N の + こととて An excuse before an apology 慣れぬこととて失礼しました
〜ないではおかない~nai de wa okanai will inevitably cause / will surely make V-ない + ではおかない Inevitable effect or strong will 人を感動させないではおかない
〜ないではすまない~nai de wa sumanai must ~ (or it won't be forgiven) V-ない + ではすまない Outside obligation (Q3, Q233) 謝らないではすまない
見える／お目にかかるmieru / ome ni kakaru come (honorific) / meet (humble) お客様が見える／私がお目にかかる Who is the subject? Guest → 尊敬語; me → 謙譲語 お客様が見えました
さえ／すら／だにsae / sura / dani even ~ N + さえ・すら ／ 想像・夢 + だに すら is more written; だに is literary and has few partners 名前すら知らない／夢にだに思わない
Exam trap — the に + negative family: Q212 lines up にはあたらない, にたえない, にたりない and にかたくない. They look alike but each has its own partners: 驚くにはあたらない, 見るにたえない, 取るにたりない, 想像にかたくない. Learn them as collocations, not as single words.
Exam trap — keigo direction (Q218): first decide whose action it is. The guest comes → honorific (見える／お見えになる／いらっしゃる). The speaker meets → humble (お目にかかる). Then reject mixed forms: お見えする (humble frame on an honorific verb) and お目にかかられる (honorific ending on a humble verb) are both wrong.
