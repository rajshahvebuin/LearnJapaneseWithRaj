【Q261】 あわや〜（という）ところだった

neutral register
Readingawaya ~ (to iu) tokoro datta
Meaning (EN)"very nearly / within a hair of (something bad), but it didn’t happen". 別冊 意味:「あやうくA」「もう少しのところでA」「Aが起こる直前に」
Meaning (HI)बस … होते-होते बचा
Meaning (GU)બસ … થતાં-થતાં રહી ગયું
Connection (接続)あわや + V-dict + ところ(だった) ／ あわや + N + というところ(だった) ／ あわやというとき・ところ　(別冊 形: [あわやA1ところ] [あわやA2というところ] [あわやというとき／あわやというところ] A1＝動詞・辞書形 A2＝名詞)
RegisterNeutral, a little literary; narrating a close call
Typical useAccidents, collisions and disasters that were narrowly avoided: あわや大事故, あわや衝突, あわや車にひかれる.
Word typeFormationExample
Verbあわや + V-dict + ところだったあわや車にひかれるところだったawaya kuruma ni hikareru tokoro datta
Nounあわや + N + というところあわや衝突というところでawaya shoutotsu to iu tokoro de
Set phraseあわやというとき／ところ(example added, not in book) あわやというところで助かったawaya to iu tokoro de tasukatta
い-adj / な-adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
高速道路でトラックが反対車線に飛び出し、あわや大事故というところだった。(book, Q261)Kousoku douro de torakku ga hantai shasen ni tobidashi, awaya daijiko to iu tokoro datta. A truck shot out into the opposite lane on the expressway, and it very nearly became a major accident. हाईवे पर एक ट्रक उलटी लेन में घुस गया, और बस बड़ा हादसा होते-होते बचा। હાઈવે પર એક ટ્રક સામેની લેનમાં ઘૂસી ગયો, અને બસ મોટો અકસ્માત થતાં-થતાં રહી ગયો.
道を横断中にあわや車にひかれるところだった。危なかった。(別冊 使い方)Michi o oudanchuu ni awaya kuruma ni hikareru tokoro datta. Abunakatta. While crossing the road I very nearly got hit by a car. That was close. सड़क पार करते हुए मैं गाड़ी से टकराते-टकराते बचा। बाल-बाल बचे। રસ્તો ઓળંગતાં હું ગાડી સાથે અથડાતાં-અથડાતાં બચ્યો. માંડ બચ્યા.
あわや衝突というところで車が止まって、事故にならなかった。(別冊 使い方)Awaya shoutotsu to iu tokoro de kuruma ga tomatte, jiko ni naranakatta. The car stopped just short of a collision, so there was no accident. टक्कर होने ही वाली थी कि गाड़ी रुक गई, और हादसा नहीं हुआ। ટક્કર થવાની જ હતી કે ગાડી અટકી ગઈ, અને અકસ્માત ન થયો.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): it is usually used together with ところ or とき. The key nuance is that the bad event did not happen. That is why options that say it had to happen (ないではすまない) or did happen (起きることになった) are wrong.

【Q262】 〜が最後

neutral register
Reading~ga saigo
Meaning (EN)"once ~ happens, (something bad and irreversible follows)". (別冊 gives only the ポイント; meaning ours.)
Meaning (HI)एक बार … हो गया तो (फिर सब ख़त्म)
Meaning (GU)એક વાર … થયું તો (પછી પત્યું)
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) V-た + が最後 ／ (colloquial) V-たら最後
RegisterNeutral; spoken and written
Typical use(added, not in book) One trigger leads to an unstoppable, usually unwelcome, result: 知られたが最後, 寝たが最後, 一度始めたら最後.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb(added, not in book) V-た + が最後知られたが最後shirareta ga saigo
Verb (colloquial)(added, not in book) V-たら + 最後寝たら最後netara saigo
Adjective / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私たちが付き合っていることを彼女に知られたが最後、あっという間にみんなに伝わってしまうだろう。(book, Q262)Watashitachi ga tsukiatte iru koto o kanojo ni shirareta ga saigo, atto iu ma ni minna ni tsutawatte shimau darou. Once she finds out we are going out, it will spread to everyone in no time. एक बार उसे पता चल गया कि हम साथ हैं, तो पलक झपकते ही सबको पता चल जाएगा। એક વાર એને ખબર પડી ગઈ કે અમે સાથે છીએ, તો પલકવારમાં બધાને ખબર પડી જશે.
彼は一度寝たが最後、何があっても起きない。(added, not in book)Kare wa ichido neta ga saigo, nani ga atte mo okinai. Once he falls asleep, nothing will wake him up. एक बार सो गया तो वह कुछ भी हो जाए, नहीं उठता। એક વાર સૂઈ ગયો પછી ગમે તે થાય, એ ઊઠતો નથી.

The 別冊 links this to Q【206】 (参照). The result clause is usually a strong prediction (〜てしまうだろう／〜ない), and it is something the speaker can’t control. Compare たところで (Q-distractor), which also takes V-た but means "even if, it’s useless".

【Q263】 お気に召す

honorific (尊敬語)
Readingo-ki ni mesu
Meaning (EN)"to like / be pleased with" (honorific of 気に入る). 別冊 意味:「気に入る」(尊敬語)
Meaning (HI)पसंद आना (आदरसूचक)
Meaning (GU)ગમવું (આદરસૂચક)
Connection (接続)人は(が) + N を + お気に召す ／ N が + 人の + お気に召す　(別冊 形: [人は(が)〜をお気に召す] [〜が人のお気に召す])
RegisterHonorific keigo; shops, service, polite speech about superiors
Typical useAsking or hoping whether a customer or superior likes something: お気に召しましたか, お気に召すとよろしいのですが.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (honorific)〜をお気に召すこの絵をお気に召したkono e o o-ki ni meshita
Verb (honorific)〜が人のお気に召す(example added, not in book) お客様のお気に召す品okyakusama no o-ki ni mesu shina
Plain equivalent(added, not in book) 気に入る気に入るki ni iru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
こちらがお客様のご注文の品です。お気に召すとよろしいのですが。(book, Q263)Kochira ga okyakusama no gochuumon no shina desu. O-ki ni mesu to yoroshii no desu ga. Here is the item you ordered. I hope you like it. यह रहा आपका ऑर्डर किया हुआ सामान। आशा है आपको पसंद आएगा। આ રહ્યો આપનો ઓર્ડર કરેલો સામાન. આશા છે આપને ગમશે.
これ、お気に召すかどうかわかりませんが、どうぞお使いください。(別冊 使い方)Kore, o-ki ni mesu ka dou ka wakarimasen ga, douzo o-tsukai kudasai. I’m not sure whether you’ll like this, but please do use it. पता नहीं यह आपको पसंद आएगा या नहीं, पर कृपया इस्तेमाल कीजिए। ખબર નથી આ આપને ગમશે કે નહીં, પણ કૃપા કરી વાપરજો.
社長はこの絵が大変お気に召したそうです。(別冊 使い方)Shachou wa kono e ga taihen o-ki ni meshita sou desu. I hear the president liked this painting very much. सुना है अध्यक्ष जी को यह चित्र बहुत पसंद आया। સાંભળ્યું છે પ્રમુખશ્રીને આ ચિત્ર ખૂબ ગમ્યું.

召す is itself an honorific verb (cf. 召し上がる, お召しになる for clothes/風邪). So お気に召す must not be wrapped again in お〜になる (お気にお召しになる) or お〜される. That would be a double honorific (二重敬語), a classic keigo trap. Compare Q299 (お年を召す).

新しいゴミ処理場の建設は、ひとり地域住民のみならず、近隣の市町村にもかかわる問題だ。(book, Q264)Atarashii gomi shorijou no kensetsu wa, hitori chiiki juumin nominarazu, kinrin no shichouson ni mo kakawaru mondai da. Building the new waste-disposal plant is a problem not just for the local residents but for the neighbouring cities, towns and villages too. 環境破壊は、ひとり日本のみならず、世界全体の問題である。(added, not in book)Kankyou hakai wa, hitori Nihon nominarazu, sekai zentai no mondai de aru. Environmental destruction is a problem not just for Japan but for the whole world. पर्यावरण का विनाश सिर्फ़ जापान का नहीं, पूरी दुनिया का मसला है। પર્યાવરણનો વિનાશ માત્ર જાપાનનો નહીં, આખી દુનિયાનો પ્રશ્ન છે.

The 別冊 links this to Q【222】 (参照). ひとり is not "one person" here. It is a written adverb meaning "only", and it emphasises のみならず (≈ ただ〜だけでなく). The second half usually has も (近隣の市町村にも).

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ふだんはやさしい部長だが、本気で怒ったときの怖さといったらない。(book, Q265)Fudan wa yasashii buchou da ga, honki de okotta toki no kowasa to ittara nai. Our department head is usually kind, but when he gets really angry he is scary beyond words. हमारे विभाग-प्रमुख आमतौर पर नरम हैं, पर जब सचमुच गुस्सा होते हैं तो उनका डर बयान से बाहर है। અમારા વિભાગ-વડા સામાન્ય રીતે નરમ છે, પણ સાચેસાચ ગુસ્સે થાય ત્યારે એમનો ડર વર્ણવી ન શકાય એવો હોય છે.
初めて孫を抱いたときのうれしさといったらなかった。(added, not in book)Hajimete mago o daita toki no ureshisa to ittara nakatta. The joy I felt when I first held my grandchild was beyond words. पहली बार पोते को गोद में लेने की ख़ुशी शब्दों में नहीं कही जा सकती थी। પહેલી વાર પૌત્રને ખોળામાં લેવાનો આનંદ શબ્દોમાં કહી શકાય એવો નહોતો.

The 別冊 links this to Q【111】 (参照). Don’t confuse it with といったところだ ("only about ~", a small degree), which is one of the distractors. かぎりだ also expresses a strong feeling, but it attaches to an adjective (怖いかぎりだ), not to a さ-noun.

Q266

アメリカの人気ロックバンドが東京を（　）6つの都市でコンサートを行う。Amerika no ninki rokku bando ga Toukyou o (___) muttsu no toshi de konsaato o okonau.

1限りにkagiri ni(を限りに) as the last ~(को) आख़िरी बार मानकर(ને) છેલ્લી વાર ગણીને
2おいてoite(をおいて) other than(को) छोड़कर(ને) છોડીને

Why: 別冊 p.35: ポイント〈を皮切りに〉, 参照【92】. N + を皮切りに = "with N as the first, (then a series follows)". Q267

この成功はスタッフ全員の協力によってもたらされたものだ（　）。Kono seikou wa sutaffu zenin no kyouryoku ni yotte motarasareta mono da (___).

HI: कहा जा सकता है कि यह सफलता पूरे स्टाफ़ के सहयोग से मिली है।
GU: કહી શકાય કે આ સફળતા આખા સ્ટાફના સહકારથી મળી છે.

1と言えようto ieyouit can (probably) be saidकहा जा सकता हैકહી શકાય

Q268

君が娘の将来について心配するのは（　）だが、彼女も子どもじゃないんだから、そっと見守るほうがいい。Kimi ga musume no shourai ni tsuite shinpai suru no wa (___) da ga, kanojo mo kodomo ja nai n da kara, sotto mimamoru hou ga ii.

Full sentence: 君が娘の将来について心配するのはもっともだが、彼女も子どもじゃないんだから、そっと見守るほうがいい。Kimi ga musume no shourai ni tsuite shinpai suru no wa mottomo da ga, kanojo mo kodomo ja nai n da kara, sotto mimamoru hou ga ii.

GU: તારું દીકરીના ભવિષ્યની ચિંતા કરવું સ્વાભાવિક છે, પણ એ હવે બાળક નથી, એટલે ચૂપચાપ એના પર નજર રાખવી સારી.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a 文の文法1 question, the four options are usually a ready-made confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
あわや〜ところだった／〜おそれがあるawaya ~ tokoro datta / ~osore ga aru very nearly (but didn’t) / there’s a risk あわや + V-dict／N という + ところ ／ V-dict・Nの + おそれがある あわや = a close call in the past; おそれ = a future risk あわや大事故というところだった
〜が最後／〜たところで~ga saigo / ~ta tokoro de once ~, (bad result) / even if ~, (no use) V-た + が最後 ／ V-た + ところで Unstoppable result vs useless effort 知られたが最後／謝ったところで
〜そばから／〜なり~soba kara / ~nari as soon as (repeatedly) / the moment (once) V-dict・た + そばから ／ V-dict + なり Habitual repetition vs one sudden reaction (Q277) 聞いたそばから忘れる
ひとり〜のみならず／〜はおろかhitori ~ nominarazu / ~wa oroka not only / let alone ひとり + N + のみならず ／ N + はおろか のみならず just widens the scope; はおろか jumps to an extreme, usually negative ひとり住民のみならず／漢字はおろか

〜にかかっている／〜にきまっている~ni kakatte iru / ~ni kimatte iru depends on / is sure to be N・〜か + にかかっている ／ 普通形 + にきまっている Dependence vs certainty 準備にかかっている
Exam trap — double honorifics (Q263): some verbs are honorific on their own: 召す (お気に召す, お年を召す Q299), 召し上がる, いらっしゃる, おっしゃる. Don’t add お〜になる／お〜される to them. お召し上がりになる is a common error, too. Keep the form simple: お気に召す, 召し上がる.
Exam trap — "almost happened" (Q261): あわや, あやうく and もう少しで all need a "but it didn’t happen" frame: 〜ところだった (あわや大事故というところだった, あやうく遅刻するところだった). If an option says the event happened or was inevitable, it can’t follow あわや.
