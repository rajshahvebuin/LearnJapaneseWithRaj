Added because the book is drill-only. There is one point per blank, in blank order. Lines marked "別冊" are taken from the book's answer booklet (p.57); everything else is our own explanation.

【［1］】 N（人）のことだから

neutral register
ReadingN no koto da kara
Meaning (EN)"knowing N (as we do), …" / "since it's N we're talking about, (surely) …". The speaker judges from what N is always like. 別冊 ◆「〜（人）のことだから」＝「〜（人）はいつも〜だから」
Meaning (HI)(वह) ठहरा ..., इसलिए (ज़रूर) ... — किसी के स्वभाव/आदत के आधार पर अनुमान
Meaning (GU)(એ) તો ... છે, એટલે (ચોક્કસ) ... — કોઈના સ્વભાવ/ટેવ પરથી અનુમાન
Connection (接続)N（人・組織）＋ の ＋ ことだから（＋ 推量：きっと／さぞ〜だろう・はずだ）
RegisterNeutral; common in speech and writing
Typical useA guess about a person based on their well-known character: まじめな彼女のことだから… The second half is usually a guess (〜だろう／〜はずだ／〜にちがいない).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (person / group)N ＋ の ＋ ことだから彼らのことだからkarera no koto da kara
Noun with modifier〜（な／い／V）N ＋ の ＋ ことだからまじめな彼女のことだからmajime na kanojo no koto da kara
Verb / い-adj / な-adj alone(added, not in book) not used — the pattern needs a noun (a person) before の—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
顧客への対応が成績を左右する仕事に携わる彼らのことだから、さぞ話が上手だろう。(book, ［1］)Kokyaku e no taiou ga seiseki o sayuu suru shigoto ni tazusawaru karera no koto da kara, sazo hanashi ga jouzu darou. They work in a job where how you deal with customers decides your results, so surely they must be very good talkers. वे ठहरे ऐसे काम से जुड़े लोग जिसमें ग्राहकों से व्यवहार ही नतीजा तय करता है, तो ज़रूर वे बहुत अच्छा बोलते होंगे। તેઓ તો એવા કામ સાથે જોડાયેલા છે જેમાં ગ્રાહકો સાથેનો વ્યવહાર જ પરિણામ નક્કી કરે છે, એટલે ચોક્કસ તેઓ બહુ સારું બોલતા હશે.
まじめな彼女のことだから、約束は破らないはずだ。(別冊 例文)Majime na kanojo no koto da kara, yakusoku wa yaburanai hazu da. She's so conscientious — she surely won't break her promise. वह इतनी ईमानदार है, वादा तो नहीं तोड़ेगी। એ તો એટલી નિષ્ઠાવાન છે, વચન તો નહીં તોડે.
時間にうるさい部長のことだから、もう会議室に来ているだろう。(added, not in book)Jikan ni urusai buchou no koto da kara, mou kaigishitsu ni kite iru darou. Knowing our manager, who is strict about time, he's probably already in the meeting room. समय के पाबंद हमारे मैनेजर ठहरे, वे शायद मीटिंग रूम में पहुँच भी चुके होंगे। સમયના પાબંદ અમારા મેનેજર તો, તેઓ કદાચ મીટિંગ રૂમમાં પહોંચી પણ ગયા હશે.

別冊 (translated): the blank sentence means "they are salesmen whose results depend on whether they can handle customers well, so they are surely very good talkers". のことだから is the natural way to say "judging from the kind of people they are". Watch the partner word さぞ ("surely, no doubt"): it calls for a guess ending (〜だろう), which matches this pattern perfectly.

【［2］】 ところが、意外なことに… ＋ AよりもB（〜上手）

neutral register — discourse logic
Readingtokoro ga, igai na koto ni … A yori mo B
Meaning (EN)"However, surprisingly, … B rather than A". ところが and 意外なことに both signal that what follows goes against what was just expected. 別冊: A「……話が上手だろう」→「ところが、意外なことに、B」: BにはAと反対のことが来る.
Meaning (HI)लेकिन, हैरानी की बात है कि ... (उम्मीद के उलट) — A से ज़्यादा B
Meaning (GU)પણ, નવાઈની વાત એ છે કે ... (અપેક્ષાથી ઊલટું) — A કરતાં વધુ B
Connection (接続)［予想・常識］。ところが、（意外なことに）［予想と反対の事実］／ V-ます-stem ＋ 上手・下手（話し上手・聞き上手・聞き下手）
RegisterNeutral; ところが is typical of narrative and explanatory writing
Typical useOverturning a common assumption. 話し上手 = good talker, 聞き上手 = good listener, 聞き下手 = poor listener, おだて上手 = good at flattering.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb stem + 上手V-ます-stem ＋ 上手（な N）聞き上手な人kiki-jouzu na hito
Verb stem + 下手V-ます-stem ＋ 下手聞き下手kiki-beta
Sentence connector文。ところが、文。…と思う。ところが、…...to omou. Tokoro ga, ...
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ところが、意外なことに、優れたセールスマンは話し上手よりも聞き上手な人が多いそうだ。(book, ［2］)Tokoro ga, igai na koto ni, sugureta seerusuman wa hanashi-jouzu yori mo kiki-jouzu na hito ga ooi sou da. Surprisingly, however, it seems many excellent salesmen are good listeners rather than good talkers. लेकिन हैरानी की बात है कि बेहतरीन सेल्समैनों में अच्छा बोलने वालों से ज़्यादा अच्छा सुनने वाले लोग होते हैं। પણ નવાઈની વાત એ છે કે ઉત્તમ સેલ્સમેનોમાં સારું બોલનારા કરતાં સારું સાંભળનારા લોકો વધુ હોય છે.
簡単な試験だと思っていた。ところが、半分も解けなかった。(added, not in book)Kantan na shiken da to omotte ita. Tokoro ga, hanbun mo tokenakatta. I thought it would be an easy exam. But I couldn't even solve half of it. मुझे लगा था कि परीक्षा आसान होगी। लेकिन मैं आधा भी हल नहीं कर पाया। મને લાગ્યું હતું કે પરીક્ષા સહેલી હશે. પણ હું અડધું પણ ઉકેલી ન શક્યો.

This blank is answered by logic, not by a grammar form. The first sentence says "everyone thinks they must be good talkers". ところが、意外なことに tells you that the next sentence says the opposite, so B must be 聞き上手 and A must be 話し上手. The second paragraph (they listen, nod, give あいづち and don't talk too much) confirms it.

【［3］】 〜ほど（程度）

neutral register
Reading~hodo
Meaning (EN)"to the extent that ~ / so … that ~". The ほど-clause is a vivid comparison that shows how high the degree is. 別冊 ◆「〜ほど」＝「非常に」: 「〜」は、程度が高いことを表す例え. 驚くほど多く = 非常にたくさん.
Meaning (HI)इतना ... कि ... / ... जितना (बहुत ज़्यादा)
Meaning (GU)એટલું ... કે ... / ... જેટલું (ખૂબ વધારે)
Connection (接続)V-dict／V-たい／い-adj ＋ ほど ＋ adj／V
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSet "degree" images: 驚くほど, 死ぬほど, 泣きたいほど, 涙が出るほど, 信じられないほど.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict ＋ ほど驚くほど多くodoroku hodo ooku
Verb (〜たい)V-たい ＋ ほど泣きたいほどうれしいnakitai hodo ureshii
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj ＋ ほど怖いほど静かだkowai hodo shizuka da
Noun(added, not in book) N ＋ ほど山ほどあるyama hodo aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
セールスマンは驚くほど多くの情報を顧客から得ることができる。(book, ［3］)Seerusuman wa odoroku hodo ooku no jouhou o kokyaku kara eru koto ga dekiru. The salesman can get a surprising amount of information from the customer. सेल्समैन ग्राहक से हैरान कर देने जितनी ज़्यादा जानकारी पा सकता है। સેલ્સમેન ગ્રાહક પાસેથી નવાઈ પમાડે એટલી વધુ માહિતી મેળવી શકે છે.
君は、死ぬほど辛い経験をしたことがありますか。(別冊 例文)Kimi wa, shinu hodo tsurai keiken o shita koto ga arimasu ka. Have you ever had an experience so painful you felt you could die? क्या तुमने कभी इतना दर्दनाक अनुभव किया है कि मर जाने जैसा लगे? શું તેં ક્યારેય એટલો દુઃખદ અનુભવ કર્યો છે કે મરી જવા જેવું લાગે?
合格通知をもらって、泣きたいほどうれしかった。(別冊 例文)Goukaku tsuuchi o moratte, nakitai hodo ureshikatta. When I got the notice that I'd passed, I was so happy I wanted to cry. पास होने की सूचना मिली तो मैं इतना ख़ुश हुआ कि रोने का मन करने लगा। પાસ થયાની સૂચના મળી ત્યારે હું એટલો ખુશ થયો કે રડવાનું મન થઈ ગયું.

The ほど-clause here is not literal: nobody is actually "surprised to death". It is an image (例え) that pushes the degree up. Note that ほど links directly to the adjective or adverb after it (驚くほど多く), with no の.

【［4］】 半ば 〜たようなものだ

neutral register
Readingnakaba ~ta you na mono da
Meaning (EN)"it's as good as done / you've practically ~ed". 別冊 ◆「〜（し）たようなものだ」＝「（まだしていないが）〜（し）たのに近い」. 半ば here means "more than half", not "exactly half" (別冊: 「ちょうど半分」ではなく「半分以上」).
Meaning (HI)(अभी हुआ नहीं, पर) लगभग हो ही गया समझो
Meaning (GU)(હજી થયું નથી, પણ) લગભગ થઈ જ ગયું સમજો
Connection (接続)V-た ＋ ようなものだ（＋ もう／半ば／同然 などと一緒に）
RegisterNeutral; common in speech and essays
Typical useWhen the hardest part is over, so the result is almost certain: 書き上がったようなものだ, 勝ったようなものだ.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た ＋ ようなものだ成功したようなものだseikou shita you na mono da
Noun(added, not in book) N ＋ のようなものだ (= "is a kind of N", a different use)家族のようなものだkazoku no you na mono da

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
そうなれば、商品のセールスは半ば成功したようなものだ。(book, ［4］)Sou nareba, shouhin no seerusu wa nakaba seikou shita you na mono da. Once that happens, the sale of the product is more than half won. ऐसा हो जाए तो समझो सामान की बिक्री आधे से ज़्यादा सफल हो ही गई। એવું થઈ જાય તો સમજો માલનું વેચાણ અડધાથી વધુ સફળ થઈ જ ગયું.
資料は集めてある。君が書いてくれた報告書も使えるし、レポートはもう書き上がったようなものだ。(別冊 例文)Shiryou wa atsumete aru. Kimi ga kaite kureta houkokusho mo tsukaeru shi, repooto wa mou kakiagatta you na mono da. I've collected the materials, and I can use the report you wrote too, so the paper is as good as finished. सामग्री इकट्ठा हो चुकी है, तुम्हारी लिखी रिपोर्ट भी काम आएगी, तो समझो पेपर लिखा ही जा चुका है। સામગ્રી ભેગી થઈ ગઈ છે, તેં લખેલો અહેવાલ પણ કામ આવશે, એટલે સમજો પેપર લખાઈ જ ગયું છે.

別冊 paraphrase (translated): "if you win the customer's goodwill and trust, the sale is close to success". The key word is 半ば: it tells you the sale has not fully happened yet, so a plain past (成功することができた) or a flat guess (成功するであろう) doesn't fit. 〜たようなものだ is exactly "not yet, but nearly".

【［5］】 つまり、〜こそ…だ

neutral–written register — conclusion marker
Readingtsumari, ~ koso … da
Meaning (EN)"In short, it is precisely ~ that …". つまり introduces the writer's conclusion (a restatement of the main point), and こそ spotlights the one thing that matters. 別冊:「つまり」の後、成功の秘訣が何かという結論を言う.
Meaning (HI)यानी, ... ही (असली) ... है
Meaning (GU)એટલે કે, ... જ (ખરું) ... છે
Connection (接続)つまり、N（〜こと）＋ こそ ＋ N だ
RegisterNeutral to written; typical last line of an essay
Typical useSumming up the whole text: the blank after つまり must contain the passage's main point, not a detail.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun / こと-clause〜こと ＋ こそ ＋ N だ聞き上手になることこそ秘訣だkiki-jouzu ni naru koto koso hiketsu da
Noun(added, not in book) N ＋ こそ努力こそ成功への近道だdoryoku koso seikou e no chikamichi da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
つまり、聞き上手になることこそセールス成功への秘訣だと言えるだろう。(book, ［5］)Tsumari, kiki-jouzu ni naru koto koso seerusu seikou e no hiketsu da to ieru darou. In short, we can say that becoming a good listener is the very secret of sales success. यानी, अच्छा श्रोता बनना ही बिक्री में सफलता का असली राज़ है, ऐसा कहा जा सकता है। એટલે કે, સારા શ્રોતા બનવું એ જ વેચાણમાં સફળતાનું ખરું રહસ્ય છે, એમ કહી શકાય.
毎日少しずつ続けること、つまり習慣こそが語学上達のかぎだ。(added, not in book)Mainichi sukoshi zutsu tsuzukeru koto, tsumari shuukan koso ga gogaku joutatsu no kagi da. Keeping at it a little every day — in other words, habit — is the very key to improving at languages. रोज़ थोड़ा-थोड़ा करते रहना, यानी आदत ही भाषा में सुधार की कुंजी है। રોજ થોડું થોડું કરતાં રહેવું, એટલે કે ટેવ જ ભાષામાં સુધારાની ચાવી છે.

別冊 (translated): after つまり the writer states the conclusion — what the secret of success is. The point of this whole text (= being a good listener) goes in the blank. Strategy: when a blank follows つまり／要するに／このように, look back at the main idea of the passage, which here was set up in blank ［2］.

次の文章を読んで、［1］から［5］の中に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。

Passage

さまざまな企業で働く営業社員の中でも群をぬいて好成績をあげる社員はトップセールスマンなどと呼ばれている。顧客への対応が成績を左右する仕事に携わる彼らの［1］、さぞ話が上手だろうとだれもが思うにちがいない。ところが、意外なことに、優れたセールスマンは［2a］よりも［2b］な人が多いそうだ。Samazama na kigyou de hataraku eigyou shain no naka de mo gun o nuite kouseiseki o ageru shain wa toppu seerusuman nado to yobarete iru. Kokyaku e no taiou ga seiseki o sayuu suru shigoto ni tazusawaru karera no [1], sazo hanashi ga jouzu darou to dare mo ga omou ni chigai nai. Tokoro ga, igai na koto ni, sugureta seerusuman wa [2a] yori mo [2b] na hito ga ooi sou da.

彼らは顧客のもとへ何度も足を運び、話をよく聞く。その際に決してしゃべりすぎず、うなずき、あいづちを打つ。すると顧客は相手から同意や共感を得られたことで気持ちがよくなり、さらに多くの話をするようになる。その結果、初めは多少の警戒心をもっていた顧客からも信頼されるようになり、セールスマンは驚く［3］多くの情報を顧客から得ることができる。このようにして相手の警戒心を解き、好意はもちろん信頼感さえ抱かせる。そうなれば、商品のセールスは半ば［4］。Karera wa kokyaku no moto e nando mo ashi o hakobi, hanashi o yoku kiku. Sono sai ni kesshite shaberisugizu, unazuki, aizuchi o utsu. Suru to kokyaku wa aite kara doui ya kyoukan o erareta koto de kimochi ga yoku nari, sara ni ooku no hanashi o suru you ni naru. Sono kekka, hajime wa tashou no keikaishin o motte ita kokyaku kara mo shinrai sareru you ni nari, seerusuman wa odoroku [3] ooku no jouhou o kokyaku kara eru koto ga dekiru. Kono you ni shite aite no keikaishin o toki, koui wa mochiron shinraikan sae idakaseru. Sou nareba, shouhin no seerusu wa nakaba [4].

人は自分に対して関心を持つ相手に好意を持つものである。だから、顧客に会うときは、まず相手の名前を覚える、それから相手の目を見て相手の話に熱心に耳を傾けること。これが顧客の好意と信頼を得る近道である。つまり、［5］こそセールス成功への秘訣だと言えるだろう。Hito wa jibun ni taishite kanshin o motsu aite ni koui o motsu mono de aru. Dakara, kokyaku ni au toki wa, mazu aite no namae o oboeru, sore kara aite no me o mite aite no hanashi ni nesshin ni mimi o katamukeru koto. Kore ga kokyaku no koui to shinrai o eru chikamichi de aru. Tsumari, [5] koso seerusu seikou e no hiketsu da to ieru darou.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The options for each blank form a small confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
NのことだからN no koto da kara knowing N, (surely) … N（人）＋ の ＋ ことだから Reason = the person's known character; the ending is a guess 彼のことだから、きっと遅れてくる
〜わけだから~wake da kara since it follows that … 普通形（N-な／N-である）＋ わけだから Reason = a logical fact, not a person's character 三年住んだわけだから、町には詳しい
〜ものなら~mono nara if (one) could / if one dared V-可能形／V-意向形 ＋ ものなら Hypothetical, often impossible; never after N + の できるものなら、やり直したい
〜ほど~hodo to the extent that V-dict ＋ ほど ＋ adj／adv Links straight to the adjective it intensifies 驚くほど多い, 死ぬほど疲れた
〜ばかりだ／〜ばかりの~bakari da / ~bakari no nothing but; (V-dict) keeps getting … V-dict ＋ ばかりだ／ばかりの N Needs だ or の after it, not a bare adjective 驚くばかりの量, 悪くなるばかりだ
〜なりに~nari ni in one's own (limited) way N ＋ なりに／なりの N "Not perfect, but suited to N" 子どもなりに考えている
〜たようなものだ~ta you na mono da as good as done V-た ＋ ようなものだ Not yet done, but the result is almost certain 半ば／もう勝ったようなものだ
〜たとはいえない~ta to wa ienai can't be called ~ V-た ＋ とはいえない Denies the result — the opposite direction 一度勝っただけでは成功したとはいえない

Exam trap — read the connectors, not just the blank: In 文章の文法, a blank is often decided by a signal word in the same or previous sentence. Here, さぞ〜だろう fixed ［1］ (a guess → のことだから), ところが、意外なことに fixed ［2］ (reverse the expectation), 半ば fixed ［4］ (almost, not yet → たようなものだ), and つまり fixed ［5］ (state the main point). Circle these signal words before looking at the options.
Exam trap — "true but not the point": In a つまり／要するに blank, wrong options are often true statements from the passage (相手の信頼を得ること, 相手に関心を持つこと). Pick the option that sums up the whole passage, not the one that repeats one detail.
