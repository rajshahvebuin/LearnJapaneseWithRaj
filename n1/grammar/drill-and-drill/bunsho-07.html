【［1］】 A。とはいっても、B

neutral register — discourse logic
ReadingA. to wa itte mo, B
Meaning (EN)"A. That said, / Even so, B." A is true, but B limits it: "it isn't as much as A would make you think". 別冊: AとBとは対立する関係 ⇒「A。とはいってもB」.
Meaning (HI)A सही है। फिर भी / हालाँकि, B (A से जितना लगता है, उतना नहीं)
Meaning (GU)A સાચું છે. છતાં / એમ કહીએ તો પણ, B (A પરથી લાગે એટલું નહીં)
Connection (接続)文A。とはいっても、文B ／ 普通形（N・な-adj は だ）＋ とはいっても
RegisterNeutral; common in explanatory writing
Typical useAdmitting a fact and then pulling it back: 春になった。とはいっても、まだ寒い.
Word typeFormationExample
Sentence connector文。とはいっても、文。CMが流れるようになった。とはいっても、…CM ga nagareru you ni natta. To wa itte mo, ...
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形 ＋ とはいっても料理ができるとはいっても、簡単なものだけだryouri ga dekiru to wa itte mo, kantan na mono dake da
Noun / な-adj(added, not in book) N／な-adj（だ）＋ とはいっても社長とはいっても、社員は3人だshachou to wa itte mo, shain wa sannin da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近はテレビでもCMが流れるようになった。とはいってもジェネリック医薬品はまだそれほど普及はしてないのが実情である。(book, ［1］)Saikin wa terebi de mo shiiemu ga nagareru you ni natta. To wa itte mo jenerikku iyakuhin wa mada sore hodo fukyuu wa shite nai no ga jitsujou de aru. Recently there are even TV commercials for them. Even so, the reality is that generic medicines have not spread very far yet. आजकल तो टीवी पर भी इनके विज्ञापन आने लगे हैं। फिर भी सच्चाई यह है कि जेनेरिक दवाएँ अभी उतनी प्रचलित नहीं हुई हैं। આજકાલ તો ટીવી પર પણ તેની જાહેરાતો આવવા લાગી છે. છતાં હકીકત એ છે કે જેનેરિક દવાઓ હજી એટલી પ્રચલિત થઈ નથી.
日本語が話せるとはいっても、あいさつ程度です。(added, not in book)Nihongo ga hanaseru to wa itte mo, aisatsu teido desu. I can speak Japanese, but only at the level of greetings. जापानी बोल तो लेता हूँ, पर बस नमस्ते-दुआ सलाम जितनी। જાપાની બોલી તો લઉં છું, પણ બસ નમસ્તે જેટલું જ.

別冊 (translated): A = "these days there are even TV commercials (for generic medicines)"; B = "generic medicines have not spread very much yet". A and B are in opposition, so the link is 「A。とはいってもB」. Unlike ところが (surprise), とはいっても says "A is true, but don't over-read it". It is the natural choice when B corrects the impression A gives.

【［2］】 とうてい〜ない

neutral–written register
Readingtoutei ~ nai
Meaning (EN)"cannot possibly ~ / there's no way ~". 別冊 ◆「とうてい〜ない」＝「〜するのは無理だ／〜できない」.
Meaning (HI)किसी भी हाल में ... नहीं हो सकता / बिल्कुल नामुमकिन
Meaning (GU)કોઈ પણ રીતે ... ન થઈ શકે / બિલકુલ અશક્ય
Connection (接続)とうてい ＋ V-可能形のない形（〜できない・〜られない）／ とうてい ＋ 無理だ
RegisterNeutral to slightly formal
Typical useStrong impossibility: とうてい間に合わない, とうてい納得できない, とうてい信じられない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential, negative)とうてい ＋ V-できない／V-られないとうてい納得できないtoutei nattoku dekinai
Verb (potential, negative)とうてい ＋ V-（ら）れないとうてい入れないtoutei hairenai
な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) とうてい ＋ 無理だ／不可能だとうてい無理だtoutei muri da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
中身が同じ、値段が安いというだけではとうてい納得できない。(book, ［2］)Nakami ga onaji, nedan ga yasui to iu dake de wa toutei nattoku dekinai. Just "same contents, lower price" is nowhere near enough to convince them. सिर्फ़ "अंदर की चीज़ वही है, दाम कम है" — इतने भर से वे किसी भी हाल में संतुष्ट नहीं हो सकते। ફક્ત "અંદરની વસ્તુ એ જ છે, ભાવ ઓછો છે" — એટલાથી તેઓ કોઈ પણ રીતે સંતુષ્ટ ન થઈ શકે.
私の実力では、一流大学にはとうてい入れない。(別冊 例文)Watashi no jitsuryoku de wa, ichiryuu daigaku ni wa toutei hairenai. With my ability, there's no way I can get into a top university. मेरी क़ाबिलियत से तो किसी टॉप यूनिवर्सिटी में दाख़िला मिलना नामुमकिन है। મારી ક્ષમતાથી તો કોઈ ટોચની યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ મળવો અશક્ય છે.

別冊 (translated): "Will he choose the generic? The answer is 'No'" — the patient does not choose it, because a low price alone can't give him peace of mind. So the blank must be negative. とうてい always goes with a negative or "impossible" ending; it never appears with a positive verb.

【［3］】 〜かというと、そうではない

neutral register
Reading~ ka to iu to, sou de wa nai
Meaning (EN)"If you ask whether ~, the answer is no / It's not that ~". The writer raises a possible assumption and rejects it. 別冊 ◆「〜かというと、そうではない」＝「〜ではない」.
Meaning (HI)क्या ... है? — ऐसा नहीं है
Meaning (GU)શું ... છે? — એવું નથી
Connection (接続)普通形（N・な-adj は だ／なの）＋ かというと、そうではない
RegisterNeutral; typical of essays and explanations
Typical useCorrecting a common misunderstanding, then giving the real situation in the next sentence.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ なのかというと、そうではないコピー商品なのかというと、そうではないkopii shouhin na no ka to iu to, sou de wa nai
VerbV-普通形 ＋ かというと、そうではないいくらでも食べられるかというと、そうではないikura de mo taberareru ka to iu to, sou de wa nai
い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj ＋ かというと、そうではない高ければいいかというと、そうではないtakakereba ii ka to iu to, sou de wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
事実、ジェネリック医薬品は先発医薬品の全く同じコピー商品なのかというとそうではない。同じなのは主成分だけである。(book, ［3］)Jijitsu, jenerikku iyakuhin wa senpatsu iyakuhin no mattaku onaji kopii shouhin na no ka to iu to sou de wa nai. Onaji na no wa shuseibun dake de aru. In fact, are generics exact copies of the original medicines? No. Only the main ingredient is the same. असल में, क्या जेनेरिक दवा मूल दवा की हूबहू नक़ल है? ऐसा नहीं है। सिर्फ़ मुख्य तत्व ही एक जैसा होता है। હકીકતમાં, શું જેનેરિક દવા મૂળ દવાની બરાબર નકલ છે? એવું નથી. ફક્ત મુખ્ય ઘટક જ સરખો હોય છે.
好きなものなら、いくらでも食べられるかというと、そうではない。食べられる量には限度がある。(別冊 例文)Suki na mono nara, ikura de mo taberareru ka to iu to, sou de wa nai. Taberareru ryou ni wa gendo ga aru. Can you eat as much as you like of something you love? No — there's a limit to how much you can eat. क्या पसंद की चीज़ हो तो कितना भी खा सकते हैं? ऐसा नहीं है। खाने की मात्रा की एक हद होती है। શું ગમતી વસ્તુ હોય તો ગમે તેટલું ખાઈ શકાય? એવું નથી. ખાવાના પ્રમાણની એક મર્યાદા હોય છે.

The next sentence 「同じなのは主成分だけである」 ("only the main ingredient is the same") is the proof: the writer is denying "exact copy". 〜かというと usually sets up a negative answer; the positive version (そのとおりだ) is rare in exam passages.

【［4］】 AどころかB

neutral register
ReadingA dokoro ka B
Meaning (EN)(1) "far from A, (actually) B" — the opposite happens. 別冊 ◆「AどころかB」＝「Aではなく、全く反対に、B」. (2) "let alone A / not to mention A" — 別冊 ＊ 別の用法:「〜はもちろん」.
Meaning (HI)(1) A तो दूर, उल्टा B (2) A तो छोड़ो, B भी नहीं
Meaning (GU)(1) A તો દૂર, ઊલટું B (2) A તો છોડો, B પણ નહીં
Connection (接続)N／な-adj（な）／い-adj／V-普通形 ＋ どころか
RegisterNeutral
Typical useOverturning an expectation: 晴れるどころか大雨, 治るどころか悪化した, 治療どころか害を与える.

い-adj(added, not in book) い-adj ＋ どころか安いどころか、かえって高いyasui dokoro ka, kaette takai

病気の適切な治療どころか逆に体に害を与えるおそれまであるのだ。(book, ［4］)Byouki no tekisetsu na chiryou dokoro ka gyaku ni karada ni gai o ataeru osore made aru no da. Far from being proper treatment for the illness, they may even harm the body. बीमारी का सही इलाज तो दूर, उल्टा शरीर को नुक़सान पहुँचने का ख़तरा तक है। બીમારીની યોગ્ય સારવાર તો દૂર, ઊલટું શરીરને નુકસાન પહોંચવાનો ભય સુધ્ધાં છે.
天気予報では晴れると言っていたのに、晴れるどころか、大雨になってしまった。(別冊 例文)Tenki yohou de wa hareru to itte ita no ni, hareru dokoro ka, ooame ni natte shimatta. The forecast said it would be sunny, but far from clearing up, it poured. मौसम विभाग ने धूप की बात कही थी, पर धूप तो दूर, ज़ोरदार बारिश हो गई। હવામાન આગાહીમાં તડકો કહ્યો હતો, પણ તડકો તો દૂર, ધોધમાર વરસાદ પડ્યો.
忙しくて、旅行するどころか、家でテレビを見るひまさえない。(別冊 例文 — the "let alone" use)Isogashikute, ryokou suru dokoro ka, ie de terebi o miru hima sae nai. I'm so busy — forget travelling, I don't even have time to watch TV at home. इतना व्यस्त हूँ कि घूमने जाना तो छोड़ो, घर पर टीवी देखने की भी फ़ुर्सत नहीं। એટલો વ્યસ્ત છું કે ફરવા જવું તો છોડો, ઘરે ટીવી જોવાનો પણ સમય નથી.

別冊 (translated): A = "proper treatment of the illness", B = "on the contrary, harming the body" — A and B say completely opposite things, so AどころかB. The word 逆に right after the blank is a strong hint for the "opposite" use of どころか.

Meaning (EN)"in exchange for A, B / at the cost of A". 別冊 ◆「Aと引き換えにB」＝「A（B）を得て、B（A）を失う」 — you gain one and lose (or give) the other.
Meaning (HI)A के बदले में B
Meaning (GU)A ના બદલામાં B

Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ と引き換えにメリットと引き換えにmeritto to hikikae ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形 ＋ のと引き換えに便利になったのと引き換えにbenri ni natta no to hikikae ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ジェネリック医薬品に変えるということは、安価というメリットと引き換えに、十分に有効性と安全性が確認されていない薬を使用するリスクを患者が負うことになるのだ。(book, ［5］)Jenerikku iyakuhin ni kaeru to iu koto wa, anka to iu meritto to hikikae ni, juubun ni yuukousei to anzensei ga kakunin sarete inai kusuri o shiyou suru risuku o kanja ga ou koto ni naru no da. Switching to generics means that, in exchange for the advantage of a low price, the patient takes on the risk of using a medicine whose effectiveness and safety have not been fully confirmed. जेनेरिक दवा पर जाने का मतलब है कि सस्तेपन के फ़ायदे के बदले में मरीज़ ऐसी दवा लेने का जोखिम उठाता है जिसकी असर और सुरक्षा पूरी तरह साबित नहीं हुई है। જેનેરિક દવા પર જવાનો અર્થ એ છે કે સસ્તાપણાના ફાયદાના બદલામાં દર્દી એવી દવા લેવાનું જોખમ ઉઠાવે છે જેની અસર અને સલામતી પૂરેપૂરી સાબિત થઈ નથી.
誘拐犯人は身代金と引き換えに子どもを返した。(別冊 例文)Yuukai hannin wa minoshirokin to hikikae ni kodomo o kaeshita. The kidnapper returned the child in exchange for the ransom. अपहरणकर्ता ने फिरौती के बदले में बच्चे को लौटा दिया। અપહરણકારે ખંડણીના બદલામાં બાળક પાછું આપ્યું.
犯人は子どもと引き換えに身代金を奪った。(別冊 例文)Hannin wa kodomo to hikikae ni minoshirokin o ubatta. The criminal took the ransom in exchange for the child. अपराधी ने बच्चे के बदले में फिरौती ले ली। ગુનેગારે બાળકના બદલામાં ખંડણી લઈ લીધી.

別冊 (translated): 「安価」 = a メリット (advantage); 「安全性が確認されていない薬を使用する」 = a リスク (danger). 引き換えに links something gained with something given up, so a must be the plus and b the minus. Notice also the verb 負う ("bear, shoulder"): you 負う a リスク／責任／義務, which narrows b. 身代金 is printed with furigana みのしろきん in the 別冊.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

文章の文法 第7回 (p.104–105). All answers are confirmed from 【別冊】正解・解説 p.60 (PDF 169): ［1］4 · ［2］1 · ［3］4 · ［4］2 · ［5］3. The 別冊 explains the correct answer only, so the analysis of each wrong option is ours.

次の文章を読んで、［1］から［5］の中に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。

Passage

「ジェネリック医薬品（後発医薬品）」という言葉を耳にするようになって久しい。最近はテレビでもCMが流れるようになった。［1］ジェネリック医薬品はまだそれほど普及はしてないのが実情である。では、なぜ日本では普及が遅れているのか。その大きな理由の一つが情報量の少なさであろう。"Jenerikku iyakuhin (kouhatsu iyakuhin)" to iu kotoba o mimi ni suru you ni natte hisashii. Saikin wa terebi de mo shiiemu ga nagareru you ni natta. [1] jenerikku iyakuhin wa mada sore hodo fukyuu wa shite nai no ga jitsujou de aru. De wa, naze Nihon de wa fukyuu ga okurete iru no ka. Sono ookina riyuu no hitotsu ga jouhouryou no sukunasa de arou.

例えば、持病で10年間同じ薬を飲み続けてきた患者が、突然医師に「中身は同じで値段が半分のジェネリックがあるが…」とすすめられた場合、この患者は迷わずジェネリックを選ぶだろうか。答えは「ノー」だ。中身が同じ、値段が安いというだけでは［2］。10年間親しんだ従来の薬のように自分の体に合っているのか確証を持つことができないからだ。Tatoeba, jibyou de juunenkan onaji kusuri o nomitsuzukete kita kanja ga, totsuzen ishi ni "nakami wa onaji de nedan ga hanbun no jenerikku ga aru ga..." to susumerareta baai, kono kanja wa mayowazu jenerikku o erabu darou ka. Kotae wa "noo" da. Nakami ga onaji, nedan ga yasui to iu dake de wa [2]. Juunenkan shitashinda juurai no kusuri no you ni jibun no karada ni atte iru no ka kakushou o motsu koto ga dekinai kara da.

NはともかくN wa tomokaku setting N aside N ＋ はともかく（として） Puts A aside; B is more important 味はともかく量は多い
Aと引き換えにBA to hikikae ni B in exchange for A, B N ＋ と引き換えに Gain one, give up the other 健康と引き換えに富を得た
Exam trap — look right after the blank: ［4］ is followed by 逆に ("on the contrary"), which almost guarantees どころか. ［3］ is followed by "only the main ingredient is the same", which proves the answer is a "no". In 文章の文法, the sentence after a blank often gives the answer away.
Exam trap — "same general meaning, wrong collocation": In ［5］ every pair is "good thing / bad thing". The verb 負う (bear a risk, a responsibility) and the content (a possible danger) pick リスク. Check which nouns go naturally with the verb in the sentence.
