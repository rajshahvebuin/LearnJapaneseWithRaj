【Q241】 AあってのB

neutral–written register
ReadingA atte no B
Meaning (EN)"B exists only because of A / no B without A". 別冊 意味:「Aが(い)なければBは成り立たない／Aがある(いる)からBが存在できる」(B benefits from A)
Meaning (HI)A है तभी B है / A के बिना B नहीं
Meaning (GU)A છે તો જ B છે / A વગર B નહીં
Connection (接続)N + あっての + N　(別冊 形: [AあってのB] A＝名詞 B＝名詞)
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal; speeches and business mottos
Typical useExpressing dependence or gratitude: 客あっての商売, 社員あっての会社, あなたあっての私.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + NounN + あっての + N社員あってのものshain atte no mono
Noun + NounN + あっての + Nあなたあっての私anata atte no watashi
Verb / adjective(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
経営者は、会社が社員あってのものであることを忘れてはならない。(book, Q241)Keieisha wa, kaisha ga shain atte no mono de aru koto o wasurete wa naranai. Managers must not forget that a company exists only because of its employees. (cf. 別冊's own English) प्रबंधकों को यह नहीं भूलना चाहिए कि कंपनी कर्मचारियों की बदौलत ही है। સંચાલકોએ એ ન ભૂલવું જોઈએ કે કંપની કર્મચારીઓને કારણે જ છે.
あなたあっての私です。あなたにはとても感謝しています。(別冊 使い方)Anata atte no watashi desu. Anata ni wa totemo kansha shite imasu. I am who I am only because of you. I'm very grateful to you. आप हैं तो मैं हूँ। मैं आपका बहुत आभारी हूँ। આપ છો તો હું છું. હું આપનો ખૂબ આભારી છું.

The stem 会社が社員（　）のもの has a noun on both sides of the blank, which is the AあってのB slot. 社員にあたって ("when facing employees"), 社員とあって ("because they are employees") and 社員にあって ("in the case of employees", formal) don't produce "the company exists thanks to its employees".

【Q242】 〜たりとも（〜ない）

formal/written (our label — not tagged 硬 in 別冊)
Reading~tari tomo (~nai)
Meaning (EN)"not even one ~". (別冊 gives only the ポイント; meaning ours.) It follows 1 + counter.
Meaning (HI)एक … भी नहीं
Meaning (GU)એક … પણ નહીં
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) 1 + counter (一秒・一円・一人・一日) + たりとも + 否定
RegisterFormal, emphatic; speeches and instructions
Typical use(added, not in book) Strict prohibitions or resolve: 一秒たりとも油断できない, 一円たりとも無駄にしない.
Word typeFormationExample
Number + counter(added, not in book) 一 + counter + たりとも一秒たりともichibyou tari tomo
Noun (other)(added, not in book) 何人(なんぴと)たりとも何人たりともnanpito tari tomo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
たとえ大きくリードしていたとしても、試合終了の合図があるまでは、一秒たりとも油断はできない。(book, Q242)Tatoe ookiku riido shite ita to shite mo, shiai shuuryou no aizu ga aru made wa, ichibyou tari tomo yudan wa dekinai. Even with a big lead, you can't let your guard down for a single second until the final whistle. भले ही आप बहुत आगे हों, मैच ख़त्म होने के संकेत तक एक पल के लिए भी लापरवाह नहीं हो सकते। ભલે તમે ઘણા આગળ હો, મેચ પૂરી થવાના સંકેત સુધી એક પળ માટે પણ બેદરકાર ન રહી શકો.
この部屋には、何人たりとも入ってはならない。(added, not in book)Kono heya ni wa, nanpito tari tomo haitte wa naranai. No one whatsoever may enter this room. इस कमरे में कोई भी व्यक्ति प्रवेश नहीं कर सकता। આ ઓરડામાં કોઈ પણ વ્યક્તિ પ્રવેશી શકે નહીં.

The 別冊 links this to Q【139】 (参照). たりとも is basically "even one", like 一秒も but stronger, and it needs a negative or a prohibition. ながらも ("although"), にもまして ("more than") and はおろか ("let alone") can't follow 一秒 in this way.

neutral register
Reading~nara de wa no

Connection (接続)(added, not in book) N + ならではの + N ／ N + ならでは + だ
RegisterNeutral; ads, tourism, food writing
Typical use(added, not in book) Praising something special: その土地ならではの料理, プロならではの技, 日本ならではの風景.

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
旅先でその土地ならではの郷土料理を食べるのは楽しいことだ。(book, Q243)Tabisaki de sono tochi nara de wa no kyoudo ryouri o taberu no wa tanoshii koto da. When travelling, it's fun to eat the local dishes you can only get in that place. यात्रा में उस जगह के ख़ास स्थानीय व्यंजन खाना मज़ेदार होता है। પ્રવાસમાં એ જગ્યાની ખાસ સ્થાનિક વાનગીઓ ખાવી મજાની વાત છે.
この繊細な味は、老舗ならではのものだ。(added, not in book)Kono sensai na aji wa, shinise nara de wa no mono da. This delicate flavour is something only a long-established shop can achieve. यह नाज़ुक स्वाद सिर्फ़ पुरानी, प्रतिष्ठित दुकान की ही ख़ासियत है। આ નાજુક સ્વાદ ફક્ત જૂની, પ્રતિષ્ઠિત દુકાનની જ ખાસિયત છે.

The 別冊 links this to Q【115】 (参照). The blank sits before a noun (郷土料理) and the meaning is "local food unique to that place". あっての (Q241) means "existing thanks to", which doesn't fit. ならいざしらず ("A aside") needs a contrasting B clause, and からこその is "precisely because", which needs a reason clause.

【Q244】 〜はおろか

neutral–written register
Reading~wa oroka
Meaning (EN)"let alone ~ / not to mention ~ (not even …)". (別冊 gives only the ポイント; meaning ours.)
Meaning (HI)… तो दूर की बात, … भी नहीं
Meaning (GU)… તો દૂરની વાત, … પણ નહીં
Connection (接続)(added, not in book) N + はおろか、N + も／さえ／すら + (否定)
RegisterNeutral to written; usually critical or surprised
Typical use(added, not in book) The bigger thing is out of the question, and even the smaller one fails: 就職はおろかアルバイトもしない, 漢字はおろかひらがなも書けない.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun(added, not in book) N + はおろか + N も〜ない就職はおろかアルバイトもせずshuushoku wa oroka arubaito mo sezu
Verb (nominalized)(added, not in book) V-dict + の + はおろか走るのはおろか歩くこともできないhashiru no wa oroka aruku koto mo dekinai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
息子は、就職はおろかアルバイトもせず、毎日遊んで暮らしている。(book, Q244)Musuko wa, shuushoku wa oroka arubaito mo sezu, mainichi asonde kurashite iru. My son doesn't even do part-time work, let alone get a proper job; he just idles every day. मेरा बेटा नौकरी तो दूर, पार्ट-टाइम काम भी नहीं करता, रोज़ बस मौज-मस्ती में दिन बिताता है। મારો દીકરો નોકરી તો દૂર, પાર્ટ-ટાઈમ કામ પણ નથી કરતો, રોજ બસ રખડીને દિવસ વિતાવે છે.
祖母は足をけがして、走るのはおろか、歩くこともできない。(added, not in book)Sobo wa ashi o kega shite, hashiru no wa oroka, aruku koto mo dekinai. My grandmother hurt her leg and can't even walk, let alone run. दादी के पैर में चोट है, दौड़ना तो दूर, वह चल भी नहीं सकतीं। દાદીને પગે ઈજા છે, દોડવું તો દૂર, એ ચાલી પણ શકતાં નથી.

The 別冊 links this to Q【199】 (参照). The pair 就職 (big) → アルバイトも (small) + せず (negative) is the textbook はおろか shape. はともかく ("setting A aside") lacks the "not even" escalation, にもまして ("even more than") is comparative, and をよそに ("ignoring") makes no sense with 就職.

【Q245】 お〜ていらっしゃる（形容詞の尊敬語）

honorific (尊敬語)
Readingo- ~kute irassharu / o- ~de irassharu
Meaning (EN)"(a respected person) is ~" (honorific form of an adjective). 別冊 意味:「Aだ」(尊敬語)
Meaning (HI)(आदरणीय व्यक्ति) … हैं (विशेषण का आदरसूचक रूप)
Meaning (GU)(આદરણીય વ્યક્તિ) … છે (વિશેષણનું આદરસૂચક રૂપ)
Connection (接続)お + い-adj-くて + いらっしゃる ／ お + な-adj-で + いらっしゃる　(別冊 形: [おAて(で)いらっしゃる] A＝い形容詞［〜くて］、な形容詞［〜で］)
RegisterHonorific keigo
Typical useDescribing a superior's state: お忙しくていらっしゃる, お若くていらっしゃる, お元気でいらっしゃる.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveお + い-adj-くて + いらっしゃるお忙しくていらっしゃるoisogashikute irassharu
な-adjectiveお + な-adj-で + いらっしゃるお元気でいらっしゃるogenki de irassharu
Noun(added, not in book) N + でいらっしゃる先生でいらっしゃいますかsensei de irasshaimasu ka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生はお忙しくていらっしゃるようですから、また後日あらためて参りましょう。(book, Q245)Sensei wa oisogashikute irassharu you desu kara, mata gojitsu aratamete mairimashou. The professor seems to be busy, so let's come back another day. लगता है गुरुजी व्यस्त हैं, इसलिए हम किसी और दिन फिर आएँगे। લાગે છે ગુરુજી વ્યસ્ત છે, તેથી આપણે બીજા કોઈ દિવસે ફરી આવીશું.
先生の奥様はお若くていらっしゃいますね。(別冊 使い方)Sensei no okusama wa owakakute irasshaimasu ne. Your wife looks so young, Professor. गुरुजी की पत्नी तो बहुत युवा दिखती हैं। ગુરુજીનાં પત્ની તો ખૂબ યુવાન દેખાય છે.
お母様はお元気でいらっしゃいますか。(別冊 使い方)Okaasama wa ogenki de irasshaimasu ka. Is your mother well? क्या आपकी माताजी स्वस्थ हैं? શું આપનાં માતુશ્રી સ્વસ્થ છે?

Adjectives can't take the お〜になる frame, so honorific adjectives use 〜くて／〜で + いらっしゃる. お忙しくておられる (option 1) uses おる, which is humble at heart; many speakers use おられる but it is not the standard answer. お忙しくございます is the polite form, not honorific, and it can't be used before ようです. お忙しくてされる is ungrammatical.

2月の北海道は、寒いのなんのって。冷凍人間になりそうだ。(book, Q246)Nigatsu no Hokkaidou wa, samui no nan no tte. Reitou ningen ni nari sou da. Hokkaido in February is unbelievably cold. 昨日の試験は難しかったのなんのって。きっと平均点は低いだろうね。(別冊 使い方)Kinou no shiken wa muzukashikatta no nan no tte. Kitto heikinten wa hikui darou ne. Yesterday's exam was incredibly hard. The average score must be low. कल की परीक्षा इतनी कठिन थी कि पूछो मत। औसत अंक ज़रूर कम होंगे। ગઈકાલની પરીક્ષા એટલી અઘરી હતી કે પૂછો નહીં. સરેરાશ માર્ક્સ જરૂર ઓછા હશે.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): 「寒いの寒くないのって」「寒いったらない」「寒いったらありゃあしない」 also mean "extremely cold". They are similar spoken emphasis expressions. Option 2 寒いとか寒くないとか ("saying whether it's cold or not") is a different structure (people's talk). にもほどがある ("there's a limit to ~") criticizes excess behaviour (冗談にもほどがある). の極みだ follows a noun (贅沢の極み), not an adjective.

Reading~o yogi naku sareru
Meaning (EN)"be forced to ~ (against one's will, by circumstances)". (別冊 gives only the ポイント; meaning ours.)
Meaning (HI)(हालात से) … करने के लिए मजबूर होना
Meaning (GU)(સંજોગોને કારણે) … કરવા મજબૂર થવું

Word typeFormationExample
Noun (action)(added, not in book) N + を余儀なくされる退学を余儀なくされたtaigaku o yogi naku sareta
Causative (thing as subject)(added, not in book) X が Y に N を余儀なくさせる台風が中止を余儀なくさせたtaifuu ga chuushi o yogi naku saseta
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は希望の大学に入学したものの、校則違反をしたために退学を余儀なくされた。(book, Q247)Kare wa kibou no daigaku ni nyuugaku shita mono no, kousoku ihan o shita tame ni taigaku o yogi naku sareta. Although he got into the university he wanted, he was forced to leave because he broke the school rules. (cf. 別冊's own English) वह मनचाहे विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िल तो हो गया, पर नियम तोड़ने की वजह से उसे पढ़ाई छोड़नी पड़ी। એ મનગમતી યુનિવર્સિટીમાં દાખલ તો થયો, પણ નિયમ તોડવાને કારણે એને અભ્યાસ છોડવો પડ્યો.
大雨のため、試合は中止を余儀なくされた。(added, not in book)Ooame no tame, shiai wa chuushi o yogi naku sareta. Because of the heavy rain, the match had to be cancelled. भारी बारिश की वजह से मैच रद्द करना पड़ा। ભારે વરસાદને કારણે મેચ રદ કરવી પડી.

The 別冊 links this to Q【74】 (参照). 余儀 = "other way", so 余儀なく = "with no other choice". 退学を禁じ得なかった would mean "couldn't suppress (feelings of) leaving school", but 禁じ得ない goes with feelings (涙を禁じ得ない). 退学せずにはおかなかった ("would surely make leave") has the wrong subject, and 退学をものともしなかった ("made light of expulsion") contradicts the story.

【Q248】 Nごときに

A casual exaggeration followed by a joke (冷凍人間) matches the spoken のなんのって. 寒いとか寒くないとか reports a debate. 〜にもほどがある criticizes someone's excess (ふざけるにもほどがある), not the weather. の極み needs a noun (疲労の極み), and 寒いの極み is ungrammatical.

彼は希望の大学に入学したものの、校則違反をしたために退学（　）。Kare wa kibou no daigaku ni nyuugaku shita mono no, kousoku ihan o shita tame ni taigaku (___).

Full sentence: 彼は希望の大学に入学したものの、校則違反をしたために退学を余儀なくされた。Kare wa kibou no daigaku ni nyuugaku shita mono no, kousoku ihan o shita tame ni taigaku o yogi naku sareta.
EN: Although he got into the university he wanted, he was forced to leave because he broke the school rules. (cf. 別冊's own English)
HI: वह मनचाहे विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िल तो हो गया, पर नियम तोड़ने की वजह से उसे पढ़ाई छोड़नी पड़ी।
GU: એ મનગમતી યુનિવર્સિટીમાં દાખલ તો થયો, પણ નિયમ તોડવાને કારણે એને અભ્યાસ છોડવો પડ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1を禁じ得なかったo kinjienakattacouldn't suppress (a feeling)(भावना) रोक न सका(લાગણી) રોકી ન શક્યો
2を余儀なくされたo yogi naku saretawas forced toमजबूर होना पड़ाમજબૂર થવું પડ્યું
3せずにはおかなかったsezu ni wa okanakattawould surely make (it) happenज़रूर करवाकर रहाજરૂર કરાવીને રહ્યો
4をものともしなかったo mono to mo shinakattamade light of / defiedकी परवाह नहीं कीની પરવા ન કરી

Why: 別冊 p.32: ポイント〈を余儀なくされる〉, 参照【74】, 硬い表現. Breaking the rules → he had no choice but to leave. 〜を禁じ得ない takes feeling nouns (同情を禁じ得ない). 退学せずにはおかない would mean he forced someone (or himself) to leave on purpose. 〜をものともしない means "undaunted by (difficulties)", which is the opposite of being expelled.

Q248

新入社員（　）に負けるものかと、ベテラン社員たちも営業成績を伸ばそうと必死になっている。Shinnyuu shain (___) ni makeru mono ka to, beteran shaintachi mo eigyou seiseki o nobasou to hisshi ni natte iru.

Full sentence: 新入社員ごときに負けるものかと、ベテラン社員たちも営業成績を伸ばそうと必死になっている。Shinnyuu shain gotoki ni makeru mono ka to, beteran shaintachi mo eigyou seiseki o nobasou to hisshi ni natte iru.
EN: Determined not to lose to mere new recruits, the veteran staff are also working desperately to raise their sales figures.
HI: "नए कर्मचारियों जैसों से हार मानेंगे क्या!" — यह सोचकर पुराने कर्मचारी भी बिक्री बढ़ाने में जी-जान से लगे हैं।
GU: "નવા કર્મચારીઓ જેવાથી હારીશું કે!" — એમ વિચારી જૂના કર્મચારીઓ પણ વેચાણ વધારવા જીવ લગાવીને મંડ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かのごときka no gotokias if (+ noun; needs a clause)मानो (वाक्यांश चाहिए)જાણે (વાક્યાંશ જોઈએ)
2ごとくgotokulike (adverbial)की तरह (क्रिया-विशेषण)ની જેમ (ક્રિયાવિશેષણ)
3ごとしgotoshiis like (sentence-final)…जैसा है (वाक्य-अंत)…જેવું છે (વાક્યના અંતે)
4ごときgotokithe likes of / a mere… जैसे मामूली… જેવા મામૂલી

Why: 別冊 p.33: ポイント〈ごときに〉, 形 [Aごときに] A = 名詞, 意味「Aなんかに」, ⚠ an expression that looks down on A, 硬い表現. The veterans belittle the newcomers: 新入社員ごときに負けるものか. かのごとき needs a clause before it (〜かのごとき態度) and a noun after it. ごとく and ごとし are the adverbial and sentence-final forms, and neither can be followed by に here.

Q249

子どもが（　）、家に帰るのが楽しくてしかたがない。Kodomo ga (___), ie ni kaeru no ga tanoshikute shikata ga nai.

Full sentence: 子どもが生まれてからというもの、家に帰るのが楽しくてしかたがない。Kodomo ga umarete kara to iu mono, ie ni kaeru no ga tanoshikute shikata ga nai.
EN: Ever since my child was born, I can't wait to get home.
HI: जब से बच्चा पैदा हुआ है, घर लौटने में बहुत ख़ुशी होती है।
GU: જ્યારથી બાળક જન્મ્યું છે, ઘરે પાછા ફરવાનો ખૂબ આનંદ થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1生まれたからにはumareta kara ni wanow that (it) is born (must…)जब पैदा हो ही गया है तो (करना होगा)જ્યારે જન્મ્યું જ છે તો (કરવું પડશે)
2生まれたとしたところでumareta to shita tokoro deeven supposing (it) is bornमान लो पैदा हो भी जाएમાનો કે જન્મે પણ
3生まれたにもかかわらずumareta ni mo kakawarazudespite (it) being bornपैदा होने के बावजूदજન્મ્યું હોવા છતાં

Why: 別冊 p.33: ポイント〈てからというもの〉, 参照【28】. A life event, then a lasting new feeling (楽しくてしかたがない) = "ever since". 〜たからには needs a duty or resolve (〜なければ), and 〜としたところで needs a "no use" result. 〜にもかかわらず needs a contrast ("despite the birth, …"), which doesn't fit a happy result.

Q250

彼は昨日の夜、酒に酔って駅の階段から落ちたそうだ。よくけがをしなかった（　）。Kare wa kinou no yoru, sake ni yotte eki no kaidan kara ochita sou da. Yoku kega o shinakatta (___).

Full sentence: 彼は昨日の夜、酒に酔って駅の階段から落ちたそうだ。よくけがをしなかったものだ。Kare wa kinou no yoru, sake ni yotte eki no kaidan kara ochita sou da. Yoku kega o shinakatta mono da.
EN: I hear he got drunk last night and fell down the station stairs. It's amazing he didn't get hurt.
HI: सुना है कल रात वह नशे में स्टेशन की सीढ़ियों से गिर गया। कमाल है कि उसे चोट नहीं लगी।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે ગઈ રાતે એ નશામાં સ્ટેશનની સીડી પરથી પડી ગયો. કમાલ છે કે એને ઈજા ન થઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はずだhazu dashould be (expectation)होना चाहिएહોવું જોઈએ
2ものだmono da(with よく) it's amazing that(よく के साथ) कमाल है कि(よく સાથે) કમાલ છે કે
3わけだwake dano wonder / that's whyतभी तोએટલે જ તો
4ところだtokoro daabout to / just (time)बस … ही वालाબસ … જ વાળું

Why: 別冊 p.33: ポイント〈よく〜ものだ〉, 形 [よくAものだ] A = 動詞・普通形, 意味「Aに驚いている／Aが信じられない」. The 別冊 ⚠ says よく strengthens the surprise use of ものだ. はずだ and わけだ draw logical conclusions ("he should / no wonder he didn't get hurt"), which contradict the surprise. ところだ would need a different structure (けがをするところだった = "almost got hurt").

Exam trap — の + "extreme" endings (Q246): のなんのって (casual, after adjectives or verbs), の極み (after a noun), にもほどがある (criticism), ったらない (casual). Check the word before the blank: 寒い is an adjective, so の極み is out.
Exam trap — fixed partners: this round has many collocation items: 一秒たりとも, その土地ならではの, 退学を余儀なくされた, よく〜ものだ. When you see 一 + counter, think たりとも. When you see よく + V-た at the end of a sentence, think ものだ.
