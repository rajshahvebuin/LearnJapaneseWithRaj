【Q281】 〜そばから

neutral register
Reading~soba kara
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~, (it is undone / happens again), over and over". 別冊 意味:「Aしてもすぐに」
Meaning (HI)जैसे ही … , तुरंत (बार-बार)
Meaning (GU)જેવું … , તરત (વારંવાર)
Connection (接続)V-dict／V-た + そばから　(別冊 形: [Aそばから] A＝動詞・辞書形／た形)
RegisterNeutral; often a mild complaint
Typical useEfforts that are cancelled out at once, repeatedly: 聞いたそばから忘れる, 片付けるそばから散らかす, 作るそばから売れる.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dict)V-dict + そばから作るそばからtsukuru soba kara
Verb (た)V-た + そばから聞いたそばからkiita soba kara
Adjective / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このパンは、作るそばから売れてしまう、店一番の人気商品だ。(book, Q281)Kono pan wa, tsukuru soba kara urete shimau, mise ichiban no ninki shouhin da. This bread is the shop’s most popular item: it sells out as fast as they can make it. यह ब्रेड दुकान की सबसे लोकप्रिय चीज़ है, बनते ही बिक जाती है। આ બ્રેડ દુકાનની સૌથી લોકપ્રિય વસ્તુ છે, બનતાંની સાથે જ વેચાઈ જાય છે.
年を取ると忘れっぽくなって、何でも聞いたそばから忘れてしまう。(別冊 使い方)Toshi o toru to wasureppoku natte, nan demo kiita soba kara wasurete shimau. As I get older I get forgetful: I forget everything as soon as I hear it. उम्र बढ़ने के साथ भुलक्कड़ हो गया हूँ, जो भी सुनता हूँ तुरंत भूल जाता हूँ। ઉંમર વધતાં ભૂલકણો થઈ ગયો છું, જે પણ સાંભળું તરત ભૂલી જાઉં છું.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): it describes a state where this happens not just once but many times. The 別冊 links it to Q【69】 (参照). It also appeared as a distractor in Q262 and Q277. Compare とたんに／なり, which describe one single moment.

【Q282】 〜にして（年齢）

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Reading~ni shite
Meaning (EN)"at (the age of) ~ (surprisingly early or late)". 別冊 意味:「〜歳で／〜歳のときに」
Meaning (HI)(… की उम्र) में (हैरानी के साथ)
Meaning (GU)(… ની ઉંમરે) (નવાઈ સાથે)
Connection (接続)N (age) + にして　(別冊 形: [Aにして] A＝年齢)
Register硬 — written, literary
Typical useSurprising age milestones: 80歳にして, 15歳にして, 四十にして惑わず.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (age)N (年齢) + にして80歳にしてhachijussai ni shite
Noun (age, classical)N + にして四十にして惑わずshijuu ni shite madowazu
Verb / adjective(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
祖父は、なんと80歳にしてスキーを始めた。(book, Q282)Sofu wa, nanto hachijussai ni shite sukii o hajimeta. Amazingly, my grandfather took up skiing at the age of 80. हैरानी की बात है, दादाजी ने 80 साल की उम्र में स्कीइंग शुरू की। નવાઈની વાત છે, દાદાએ 80 વર્ષની ઉંમરે સ્કીઇંગ શરૂ કર્યું.
「男子、四十にして惑わず」と孔子は言った。(別冊 使い方)"Danshi, shijuu ni shite madowazu" to Koushi wa itta. Confucius said, "At forty, a man is free from doubts." कन्फ़्यूशियस ने कहा था, "चालीस की उम्र में मनुष्य दुविधाओं से मुक्त हो जाता है।" કન્ફ્યૂશિયસે કહ્યું હતું, "ચાલીસની ઉંમરે માણસ દ્વિધાઓથી મુક્ત થઈ જાય છે."
王妃は15歳にして王子を生んだ。(別冊 使い方)Ouhi wa juugosai ni shite ouji o unda. The queen gave birth to a prince at just fifteen. रानी ने महज़ पंद्रह साल की उम्र में राजकुमार को जन्म दिया। રાણીએ માત્ર પંદર વર્ષની ઉંમરે રાજકુમારને જન્મ આપ્યો.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): it emphasises "at the age of ~", and it is often used in sentences that express surprise that something is "early" or "late". The 別冊 links it to Q【22】 (参照). にして has other N1 uses too (幸いにして Q271, 一瞬にして, 天才にして初めて可能), and the age use is just one of them.

【Q283】 〜にはあたらない

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Reading~ni wa ataranai
Meaning (EN)"there is no need to ~ / it is not worth ~ing". 別冊 意味:「Aするほどのことではない／Aする必要はない」
Meaning (HI)… करने की ज़रूरत नहीं / … लायक बात नहीं
Meaning (GU)… કરવાની જરૂર નથી / … જેવી વાત નથી
Connection (接続)V-dict + には(に)あたらない (驚く・感謝する・非難する・嘆く etc.)　(別冊 形: [Aにはあたらない] A＝動詞・辞書形)
Register硬 — written commentary
Typical usePlaying down a reaction: 驚くにはあたらない, 礼を言うにはあたらない, 非難するにはあたらない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + にはあたらない驚くにはあたらないodoroku ni wa ataranai
VerbV-dict + にはあたらない礼を言うにはあたらないrei o iu ni wa ataranai
Adjective / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
厳しい労働条件の下で働く労働者がストライキをしたからといって、驚くにはあたらない。(book, Q283)Kibishii roudou jouken no moto de hataraku roudousha ga sutoraiki o shita kara to itte, odoroku ni wa ataranai. Just because workers under harsh working conditions went on strike, there’s nothing to be surprised about. कठिन कामकाजी हालात में काम करने वाले मज़दूरों ने हड़ताल कर दी, तो इसमें हैरान होने की कोई बात नहीं। કઠોર કામકાજી સંજોગોમાં કામ કરતા મજૂરોએ હડતાળ કરી, તો એમાં નવાઈ પામવા જેવું કંઈ નથી.
大したことはしていないのだから、礼を言うにはあたらない。(別冊 使い方)Taishita koto wa shite inai no da kara, rei o iu ni wa ataranai. I didn’t do anything special, so there’s no need to thank me. मैंने कुछ ख़ास नहीं किया, इसलिए शुक्रिया कहने की ज़रूरत नहीं। મેં કંઈ ખાસ નથી કર્યું, એટલે આભાર માનવાની જરૂર નથી.

Typical frame: 〜からといって、〜にはあたらない ("just because X, there’s no reason to Y"). Compare Q270’s distractor あたらない. The other options are verb + pattern collocation traps: 〜てやまない (願う・愛する), 〜にかたくない (想像する・察する), N + といったらない.

【Q284】 皆目〜ない

neutral–written (vocabulary)
Readingkaimoku ~ nai
Meaning (EN)"not at all / utterly (cannot)". 別冊 意味:「ぜんぜんAない／できない」
Meaning (HI)बिल्कुल नहीं / ज़रा भी नहीं
Meaning (GU)બિલકુલ નહીં / જરા પણ નહીં
Connection (接続)皆目 + V-ない (わからない・見当がつかない・理解できない)　(別冊 形: [皆目Aない] A＝動詞・ない形)
RegisterNeutral to written; a bit old-fashioned
Typical useTotal lack of knowledge or understanding: 皆目わからない, 皆目見当がつかない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (negative)皆目 + V-ない皆目わからないkaimoku wakaranai
Verb (negative)皆目 + 見当がつかない皆目見当がつかないkaimoku kentou ga tsukanai
Positive verb(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人がいったい何を考えているのか、皆目理解できない。(book, Q284)Ano hito ga ittai nani o kangaete iru no ka, kaimoku rikai dekinai. I can’t understand at all what on earth that person is thinking. वह आदमी आख़िर क्या सोच रहा है, मुझे बिल्कुल समझ नहीं आता। એ માણસ આખરે શું વિચારે છે, મને બિલકુલ સમજાતું નથી.
逃げた犬がどこへ行ったか、皆目見当がつかない。(別冊 使い方)Nigeta inu ga doko e itta ka, kaimoku kentou ga tsukanai. I have no idea at all where the dog that ran away has gone. भागा हुआ कुत्ता कहाँ गया, इसका बिल्कुल अंदाज़ा नहीं। ભાગી ગયેલો કૂતરો ક્યાં ગયો, એનો બિલકુલ અંદાજ નથી.

The 別冊 notes that A is usually a verb meaning "can’t / don’t know". Like 何ら (Q271), 皆目 is a negative-polarity adverb, a vocabulary item tested as grammar. Similar adverbs: 一向に〜ない, さっぱり〜ない, まるで〜ない.

【Q285】 〜かというと

neutral register
Reading~ka to iu to
Meaning (EN)"if you ask whether ~, (actually not)". 別冊 意味:「Aかと思うが、でもB」(Aではないと Bで否定する)
Meaning (HI)अगर पूछो कि क्या … , तो (ऐसा नहीं)
Meaning (GU)જો પૂછો કે શું … , તો (એવું નથી)
Connection (接続)文 (普通形) + かというと、B (否定)　(別冊 形: [Aかというと、B] A、B＝文)
RegisterNeutral; argumentative writing and speech
Typical useRaising a common assumption only to deny it: 〜かというと、必ずしもそうではない／決してそうではない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-普通形 + かというと就職できるかというとshuushoku dekiru ka to iu to
AdjectiveAdj + かというと幸せかというとshiawase ka to iu to
Variant(added, not in book) 〜かといえば／〜かといったら安いかといえばyasui ka to ieba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
学歴が高ければ一流企業に就職できるかというと、決してそのようなことはない。(book, Q285)Gakureki ga takakereba ichiryuu kigyou ni shuushoku dekiru ka to iu to, kesshite sono you na koto wa nai. Does a good education mean you can get a job at a top company? Not at all. क्या ऊँची पढ़ाई हो तो बड़ी कंपनी में नौकरी मिल जाती है? बिल्कुल ऐसा नहीं है। શું ઊંચું ભણતર હોય તો મોટી કંપનીમાં નોકરી મળી જાય? બિલકુલ એવું નથી.
お金があれば幸せかというと、必ずしもそうではない。(別冊 使い方)Okane ga areba shiawase ka to iu to, kanarazushimo sou de wa nai. Does having money make you happy? Not necessarily. क्या पैसा हो तो इंसान ख़ुश रहता है? ज़रूरी नहीं। શું પૈસા હોય તો માણસ સુખી રહે? જરૂરી નથી.

The B clause usually contains a denial: 必ずしも〜ない, 決してそうではない, そうとは限らない. Don’t confuse it with the question-word version (なぜかというと = "the reason is…"), which explains rather than denies.

【Q286】 〜べくして〜

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Reading~beku shite ~
Meaning (EN)"(it happened) as it was bound to / naturally, as expected". 別冊 意味:「Aする／したのは当然だ」
Meaning (HI)होना ही था, सो हुआ
Meaning (GU)થવાનું જ હતું, તે થયું
Connection (接続)V-dict + べくして + same V-dict／V-た　(別冊 形: [A1べくしてA2] A1、A2＝動詞A・辞書形 A2＝動詞A・辞書形／た形)
Register硬 — written commentary
Typical useSaying an outcome was inevitable given the causes: 起こるべくして起こった事故, 勝つべくして勝った.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (repeated)V-dict + べくして + V-た成功するべくして成功したseikou suru beku shite seikou shita
Verb (repeated)V-dict + べくして + V-た起こるべくして起こったokoru beku shite okotta
Adjective / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
億万長者になった彼は運がよかったわけではない。誰よりも努力をして、成功するべくして成功したのだ。(book, Q286)Okuman chouja ni natta kare wa un ga yokatta wake de wa nai. Dare yori mo doryoku o shite, seikou suru beku shite seikou shita no da. He didn’t become a billionaire through luck. He worked harder than anyone, and he succeeded because he was bound to succeed. वह किस्मत से करोड़पति नहीं बना। उसने सबसे ज़्यादा मेहनत की, और उसकी सफलता तो होनी ही थी। એ નસીબથી કરોડપતિ નથી બન્યો. એણે સૌથી વધુ મહેનત કરી, અને એની સફળતા તો થવાની જ હતી.
この事件は起こるべくして起こった。(別冊 使い方)Kono jiken wa okoru beku shite okotta. This incident happened because it was bound to happen. यह घटना तो होनी ही थी, सो हो गई। આ ઘટના તો થવાની જ હતી, તે થઈ ગઈ.

The same verb appears on both sides of べくして. That is the fastest clue. Don’t confuse it with べく ("in order to", Q254) or すべく. Both come from classical べし, but べくして is about inevitability, not purpose.

【Q287】 〜ばこそ

neutral–written register
Reading~ba koso
Meaning (EN)"precisely because ~ (and for no other reason)". (別冊 gives only the ポイント; meaning ours.)
Meaning (HI)… होने की वजह से ही
Meaning (GU)… હોવાને કારણે જ

RegisterNeutral to written; emphatic
Typical use(added, not in book) Stressing a positive, often surprising, reason: 愛すればこそ厳しくする, 感激があればこそ続けられる.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb(added, not in book) V-ば + こそあればこそareba koso
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-ければ + こそ若ければこそwakakereba koso
な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) な-adj・N + であればこそ親であればこそoya de areba koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
登山はきついスポーツだが、登頂したときの感激があればこそ続けられるというものだ。(book, Q287)Tozan wa kitsui supootsu da ga, touchou shita toki no kangeki ga areba koso tsuzukerareru to iu mono da. Mountain climbing is a tough sport, but it is precisely because of the thrill of reaching the summit that you can keep doing it. पर्वतारोहण कठिन खेल है, पर चोटी पर पहुँचने का रोमांच ही है जिसकी वजह से इसे जारी रखा जा सकता है। પર્વતારોહણ અઘરી રમત છે, પણ શિખરે પહોંચવાનો રોમાંચ જ છે જેના કારણે એ ચાલુ રાખી શકાય છે.
子どもを愛すればこそ、厳しく叱るのだ。(added, not in book)Kodomo o aisureba koso, kibishiku shikaru no da. It is precisely because I love my child that I scold him strictly. बच्चे से प्यार है इसीलिए तो सख़्ती से डाँटता हूँ। બાળકને પ્રેમ કરું છું એટલે જ તો કડકાઈથી ઠપકો આપું છું.

The 別冊 links this to Q【229】 (参照). ばこそ is close to からこそ, but more literary. The sentence often ends with 〜のだ／〜というものだ. The concessive distractors (とはいえ, ながら) reverse the logic, so check whether the first clause is a reason or an obstacle.

HI: डॉक्टरी के काम के साथ-साथ वह उपन्यास लिखता और पत्रिकाओं में छपवाता आया है।
GU: ડૉક્ટરના કામની સાથે-સાથે એ નવલકથાઓ લખતો અને સામયિકોમાં છપાવતો આવ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かたがたkatagatawhile also (on the same occasion)साथ ही (उसी मौक़े पर)સાથે સાથે (એ જ પ્રસંગે)
2のかたわらno katawaraalongside (main work)के साथ-साथ (मुख्य काम)ની સાથે-સાથે (મુખ્ય કામ)
3にともなってni tomonattealong with (a change)के साथ-साथ (बदलाव)ની સાથે (ફેરફાર)
4がてらgaterawhile (on a single outing)… के बहाने (एक बार)… ના બહાને (એક વાર)

Why: 別冊 p.38: ポイント〈かたわら〉, 参照【98】. N の／V-dict + かたわら = "while doing A as one’s main job, also doing B over a long period". かたがた attaches directly to a noun like お礼／ご挨拶 (お礼かたがた) for a single visit. がてら is also a single occasion (散歩がてら). にともなって describes one change accompanying another, not two parallel activities.

Q289

祭りの会場へ向かう道は押す（　）押す（　）の大混雑だった。Matsuri no kaijou e mukau michi wa osu (___) osu (___) no daikonzatsu datta.

Full sentence: 祭りの会場へ向かう道は押すな押すなの大混雑だった。Matsuri no kaijou e mukau michi wa osu na osu na no daikonzatsu datta.
EN: The road to the festival grounds was jam-packed with people pushing and shoving.
HI: मेले के मैदान की ओर जाने वाली सड़क पर भारी धक्का-मुक्की वाली भीड़ थी।
GU: મેળાના મેદાન તરફ જતા રસ્તા પર ભારે ધક્કામુક્કીવાળી ભીડ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なり／なりnari / nari(〜なり〜なり) either ~ or ~(या … या …)(કાં … કાં …)
2は／はwa / wa(not a pattern)(कोई पैटर्न नहीं)(કોઈ પેટર્ન નથી)
3な／なna / na(押すな押すな) packed crowd(भारी धक्का-मुक्की)(ભારે ધક્કામુક્કી)
4と／とto / to(not a pattern)(कोई पैटर्न नहीं)(કોઈ પેટર્ન નથી)

Why: 別冊 p.38: ポイント〈押すな押すな〉, 形 [押すな押すなのA] A = 名詞, 意味「人が大勢いて、とても混んでいる」. The 別冊 ⚠ explains that 押すな押すな literally means 押さないでください、押さないでください ("don’t push, don’t push!"), which is what people shout in a crush. 〜なり〜なり ("either A or B": 電話するなりメールするなり) means a choice, not a crowd. は／は and と／と are not patterns.

Q290

家族の心配（　）、彼は戦場へ取材に出かけて行った。Kazoku no shinpai (___), kare wa senjou e shuzai ni dekakete itta.

Full sentence: 家族の心配をよそに、彼は戦場へ取材に出かけて行った。Kazoku no shinpai o yoso ni, kare wa senjou e shuzai ni dekakete itta.
EN: Ignoring his family’s worries, he set off to the battlefield to report.
HI: परिवार की चिंता की परवाह किए बिना वह रिपोर्टिंग के लिए युद्धभूमि चला गया।
GU: પરિવારની ચિંતાની પરવા કર્યા વગર એ રિપોર્ટિંગ માટે યુદ્ધભૂમિ પર ચાલ્યો ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なしにnashi niwithoutके बिनाવગર
2ゆえにyue nibecause ofकी वजह सेના કારણે
3をよそにo yoso niignoring / regardless ofकी परवाह किए बिनाની પરવા કર્યા વગર
4をおいてo oiteother than / exceptको छोड़करને છોડીને

Why: 別冊 p.38: ポイント〈をよそに〉, 参照【237】. N (others’ worry, criticism, opinion) + をよそに = "paying no attention to ~". The family did worry, and he went anyway. 心配なしに would mean "without (any) worry", as if no one worried. ゆえに would make the worry the reason he went, which is illogical. をおいて ("except for", 彼をおいてほかにいない) needs ほかに〜ない.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a 文の文法1 question, the four options are usually a ready-made confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜そばから／〜とたんに／〜なり~soba kara / ~totan ni / ~nari as soon as (repeatedly) / just as / the moment V-dict・た + そばから ／ V-た + とたんに ／ V-dict + なり Repeated vs single surprise vs single reaction 作るそばから売れる
〜にして（年齢）／〜にあって~ni shite / ~ni atte at (age) / amid (situation) 年齢 + にして ／ 状況 N + にあって Age/time point vs circumstance 80歳にして
〜にはあたらない／〜にかたくない／〜てやまない~ni wa ataranai / ~ni katakunai / ~te yamanai no need to / not hard to / never cease to 驚く・感謝する + にはあたらない ／ 想像・察する + にかたくない ／ 願う・愛する + てやまない Each collocates with specific verbs 驚くにはあたらない
皆目〜ない／何ら〜ないkaimoku ~ nai / nanra ~ nai not at all (know) / not at all (effect) 皆目 + わからない類 ／ 何ら + 否定 Both need a negative; 皆目 is about knowing 皆目見当がつかない
〜かというと／〜までもなく~ka to iu to / ~made mo naku if you ask whether (no) / needless to say 文 + かというと ／ V-dict + までもなく Deny vs take for granted お金があれば幸せかというと
〜べくして／〜べく~beku shite / ~beku as bound to / in order to V + べくして + same V ／ V-dict + べく Inevitability vs purpose (Q254) 起こるべくして起こった
〜ばこそ／〜とはいえ~ba koso / ~to wa ie precisely because / although V-ば + こそ ／ 普通形 + とはいえ Reason vs concession 感激があればこそ
〜かたわら／〜がてら／〜かたがた~katawara / ~gatera / ~katagata alongside (long-term) / while (one outing) / while also (formal visit) Nの・V-dict + かたわら ／ N・V-stem + がてら ／ N + かたがた Duration and formality 仕事のかたわら
〜をよそに／〜なしに~o yoso ni / ~nashi ni ignoring / without N + をよそに ／ N + なしに The worry exists but is ignored vs it doesn’t exist 家族の心配をよそに
Exam trap — verb + pattern collocations (Q283): some N1 endings only go with certain verbs. 〜にかたくない: 想像する／察する. 〜てやまない: 願う／祈る／愛する／期待する. 〜にはあたらない: 驚く／感謝する／非難する. 〜に堪えない: 見る／聞く (見るに堪えない) or 感謝・喜び (感謝に堪えない). If the verb in the options doesn’t match its partner, eliminate it.
Exam trap — repeated-word patterns (Q286, Q289): when the stem repeats a word around the blank, think of a fixed frame. Same verb twice → 〜べくして〜 (成功するべくして成功した). V-dict + な twice → 押すな押すな. Two different verbs → 〜なり〜なり ("either…or").
