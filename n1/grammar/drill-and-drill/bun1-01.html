【Q1】 ご〜させていただく

humble (謙譲語) — polite business register
Readinggo- ~ sasete itadaku
Meaning (EN)"(I/we) humbly do ~ (for you)". It is a very humble way to say "do ~" for an action done toward the listener. 別冊 意味:「Aする」(謙譲語)
Meaning (HI)(आपके लिए) ...करने की अनुमति लेकर करना — "करना" का अत्यंत विनम्र रूप
Meaning (GU)(આપના માટે) ...કરવાની રજા લઈને કરવું — "કરવું"નું અત્યંત નમ્ર રૂપ
Connection (接続)ご + N (action Sino-Japanese noun: 案内・連絡・通知・報告…) + させていただく
RegisterHumble keigo, used in shops, offices, announcements and business email
Typical useAn announcement or service desk describing what it does for the customer: ご案内させていただく, ご連絡させていただく. 別冊 形: [(私は)(あなたに) A させていただく].
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (action 漢語)ご + N + させていただくご案内させていただくgo-annai sasete itadaku
Verb (no ご)V-causative-て + いただく (book ⚠ note)欠席させていただきますkesseki sasete itadakimasu
い-adjective(added, not in book) not used—
な-adjective(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
こちらの窓口では、製品に関する重要なお知らせをご案内させていただいております。(book, Q1)Kochira no madoguchi de wa, seihin ni kansuru juuyou na oshirase o go-annai sasete itadaite orimasu. At this counter, we provide information on important notices about our products. इस काउंटर पर हम उत्पादों से जुड़ी ज़रूरी सूचनाओं की जानकारी देते हैं। આ કાઉન્ટર પર અમે ઉત્પાદનો સંબંધિત મહત્ત્વની સૂચનાઓની માહિતી આપીએ છીએ.
お会いしてご相談させていただきたいと思います。よろしくお願いします。(別冊 使い方)Oai shite go-soudan sasete itadakitai to omoimasu. Yoroshiku onegai shimasu. I would like to meet you and consult with you. Thank you in advance. मैं आपसे मिलकर सलाह-मशविरा करना चाहूँगा। कृपया सहयोग करें। હું આપને મળીને સલાહ-મસલત કરવા ઇચ્છું છું. કૃપા કરીને સહકાર આપજો.
残念ですが、送別会は欠席させていただきます。(別冊 ⚠ example)Zannen desu ga, soubetsukai wa kesseki sasete itadakimasu. I'm sorry, but I will (have to) miss the farewell party. अफ़सोस है, लेकिन मैं विदाई पार्टी में शामिल नहीं हो पाऊँगा। દુઃખ છે, પણ હું વિદાય સમારંભમાં હાજર રહી શકીશ નહીં.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): when you are humbling only your own action and it is not done "toward you", drop the ご and just say 〜させていただく (欠席させていただきます). The ご + 漢語 form is for actions that reach the listener, like guiding, contacting or reporting to them. The extra いております in Q1 (いただいて + おります) adds "this is something we do on an ongoing basis".

【Q2】 AごときB

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
ReadingA gotoki B
Meaning (EN)"a B like A". 別冊 意味:「AのようなB」(A is an example of B). When A is something looked down on, the phrase becomes scornful ("the likes of A"), or humble if A is the speaker.
Meaning (HI)A जैसा B; (नीचा दिखाते हुए) A जैसे मामूली लोग
Meaning (GU)A જેવો B; (તુચ્છતાથી) A જેવા સામાન્ય લોકો
Connection (接続)N（の）+ ごとき + N　(別冊 形: [AごときB] A＝名詞 B＝名詞)
Register硬 — classical simile form, used in writing and speeches
Typical useSelf-deprecation (我々のごとき庶民 "ordinary people like us") or contempt (おまえのごとき人間 "the likes of you"). It always comes right before a noun.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN（の）+ ごとき + N我々のごとき庶民wareware no gotoki shomin
Verb(added, not in book) V-dict/V-た + （が）ごとき + N見下すがごとき態度mikudasu ga gotoki taido
い-adjective(added, not in book) not used—
な-adjective(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
いくら良いマンションだと勧められても、家賃が月30万円もするのでは我々のごとき庶民に借りられるはずがない。(book, Q2)Ikura yoi manshon da to susumerarete mo, yachin ga tsuki sanjuuman-en mo suru no de wa wareware no gotoki shomin ni karirareru hazu ga nai. No matter how much they recommend it as a good apartment, at 300,000 yen a month there's no way ordinary folk like us could rent it. चाहे कितना भी अच्छा फ़्लैट बताकर सिफ़ारिश करें, महीने का किराया 3 लाख येन हो तो हम जैसे आम लोग उसे किराये पर ले ही नहीं सकते। ભલે ગમે તેટલો સારો ફ્લેટ કહીને ભલામણ કરે, મહિને 3 લાખ યેન ભાડું હોય તો અમારા જેવા સામાન્ય લોકો તે ભાડે લઈ જ ન શકે.
おまえのごとき勝手な人間に人の苦しみがわかるものか。(別冊 使い方)Omae no gotoki katte na ningen ni hito no kurushimi ga wakaru mono ka. How could a selfish person like you understand other people's suffering? तुम्हारे जैसा स्वार्थी इंसान दूसरों का दुख क्या समझेगा? તારા જેવો સ્વાર્થી માણસ બીજાનું દુઃખ શું સમજે?
花のごとき笑顔 (別冊 ⚠ example)hana no gotoki egao a flower-like smile फूल जैसी मुस्कान ફૂલ જેવું સ્મિત

別冊 ⚠ (translated): 「AごときB」 compares B to A, as in 花のごとき笑顔. But when A is something rated negatively, as in this question, the phrase belittles A. In Q2 the speaker belittles himself ("people like us"), so it sounds humble. ごとき is the attributive form of classical ごとし, so it must be followed by a noun. That is the whole point of the question (see §3).

【Q3】 〜ずにはすまない

neutral–formal register
Reading~zu ni wa sumanai
Meaning (EN)"won't get away without ~ing / will have to ~". Social or moral circumstances make the action unavoidable. 別冊 意味:「Aしなければいけない／Aしなければ許されない」
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना काम नहीं चलेगा / ...करना ही पड़ेगा (वरना माफ़ी नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે / ...કરવું જ પડશે (નહીંતર માફી નહીં)
Connection (接続)V-ない (drop ない) + ずにはすまない ／ する → せずにはすまない
RegisterSlightly formal. The casual equivalent is 〜ないではすまない.
Typical useAfter causing trouble or a loss: 謝らずには／責任を取らずには／弁償せずにはすまない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない-stem + ずにはすまない取らずにはすまないtorazu ni wa sumanai
する-verb(added, not in book) N + せずにはすまない弁償せずにはすまないbenshou sezu ni wa sumanai
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used. The pattern needs an action verb.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私がしたミスのために会社に損失を与えてしまった以上、責任を取らずにはすまないだろう。(book, Q3)Watashi ga shita misu no tame ni kaisha ni sonshitsu o ataete shimatta ijou, sekinin o torazu ni wa sumanai darou. Now that my mistake has caused the company a loss, I probably won't get away without taking responsibility. जब मेरी गलती से कंपनी को नुकसान हो ही गया है, तो मुझे ज़िम्मेदारी लेनी ही पड़ेगी। જ્યારે મારી ભૂલથી કંપનીને નુકસાન થઈ જ ગયું છે, ત્યારે મારે જવાબદારી લેવી જ પડશે.
失礼なことを言って友人を傷つけてしまった。謝らずにはすまないだろう。(別冊 使い方)Shitsurei na koto o itte yuujin o kizutsukete shimatta. Ayamarazu ni wa sumanai darou. I said something rude and hurt my friend. I'll have to apologize. मैंने बदतमीज़ी भरी बात कहकर दोस्त का दिल दुखा दिया। माफ़ी माँगे बिना बात नहीं बनेगी। મેં અસભ્ય વાત કહીને મિત્રનું દિલ દુભાવ્યું. માફી માગ્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે.

別冊 ⚠ (translated): don't confuse it with 「〜ずにはいられない」, which means "can't hold back the urge to ~ / can't help ~ing". That one is about your own feelings. ずにはすまない is about outside pressure: "it won't be settled/excused unless ~". The 別冊 links this to Q【233】 (参照), where the pattern is tested again.

【Q4】 〜にひきかえ

neutral–written register
Reading~ni hikikae
Meaning (EN)"in (stark) contrast to A, B…". 別冊 意味:「AとはちがってBは／Aとは反対にBは」. A and B are people or things being compared, and the pattern stresses that they are opposites.
Meaning (HI)A के बिल्कुल विपरीत, B…
Meaning (GU)A થી બિલકુલ ઊલટું, B…
Connection (接続)N + にひきかえ ／ [V・い-adj 普通形／な-adj-な] + の + にひきかえ
RegisterNeutral, slightly written. Often carries the speaker's judgment (one side good, the other bad).
Typical useTwo people or things placed side by side with opposite qualities: 兄にひきかえ私は…, 去年にひきかえ今年は….
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にひきかえ (別冊 [Aにひきかえ、Bは])兄にひきかえani ni hikikae
VerbV-普通形 + の + にひきかえ (別冊 A2)増えているのにひきかえfuete iru no ni hikikae
い-adjectiveい-adj + の + にひきかえ (別冊 A2)(example added, not in book) 明るいのにひきかえakarui no ni hikikae
な-adjectiveな-adj-な + の + にひきかえ (別冊 A2)(example added, not in book) まじめなのにひきかえmajime na no ni hikikae
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大学で数学を専攻している兄にひきかえ、私は数字に弱く、計算もまともにできない。(book, Q4)Daigaku de suugaku o senkou shite iru ani ni hikikae, watashi wa suuji ni yowaku, keisan mo matomo ni dekinai. Unlike my older brother, who majors in mathematics at university, I'm bad with numbers and can't even do sums properly. विश्वविद्यालय में गणित पढ़ने वाले मेरे बड़े भाई के बिल्कुल उलट, मैं अंकों में कमज़ोर हूँ और ठीक से हिसाब भी नहीं कर पाता। યુનિવર્સિટીમાં ગણિત ભણતા મારા મોટા ભાઈથી બિલકુલ ઊલટું, હું આંકડામાં નબળો છું અને બરાબર હિસાબ પણ નથી કરી શકતો.
人口が増え続けているM町にひきかえ、N町は人口が減る一方だ。(別冊 使い方)Jinkou ga fuetsuzukete iru M-machi ni hikikae, N-machi wa jinkou ga heru ippou da. In contrast to M Town, whose population keeps growing, N Town's population just keeps falling. लगातार बढ़ती आबादी वाले M कस्बे के विपरीत, N कस्बे की आबादी घटती ही जा रही है। સતત વધતી વસ્તીવાળા M નગરથી ઊલટું, N નગરની વસ્તી ઘટતી જ જાય છે.

The two sides must be opposite. If B simply goes further than A in the same direction, you need にもまして or もさることながら instead (see §3). In Q4 the relative clause 大学で数学を専攻している describes 兄, so grammatically this is still "N + にひきかえ". The 別冊 links this to Q【128】 (参照).

【Q5】 〜たところで

neutral register
Reading~ta tokoro de
Meaning (EN)"even if (you) ~, it's no use / it won't make a difference". 別冊 意味:「Aしても（だめだ）」
Meaning (HI)...कर भी लिया तो (कोई फ़ायदा नहीं / कुछ नहीं बदलेगा)
Meaning (GU)...કરી પણ લીધું તો (કોઈ ફાયદો નહીં / કંઈ નહીં બદલાય)
Connection (接続)V-た + ところで (別冊 形: Aた＝動詞・た形)
RegisterNeutral, common in speech
Typical useThe second half is always negative or pointless: 〜ない／無駄だ／わけがない. Often paired with いくら／どんなに／〜人が.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + ところで言ったところでitta tokoro de
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used in this meaning. Turn it into a verb phrase first (e.g. 安くなったところで).—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
君一人が反対意見を言ったところで、会社の決定が変わるわけがないよ。(book, Q5)Kimi hitori ga hantai iken o itta tokoro de, kaisha no kettei ga kawaru wake ga nai yo. Even if you alone voice an objection, there's no way the company's decision will change. अगर अकेले तुम विरोध भी कर दो, तो भी कंपनी का फ़ैसला बदलने वाला नहीं। જો એકલો તું વિરોધ કરી પણ દે, તો પણ કંપનીનો નિર્ણય બદલાવાનો નથી.
もう9時だ。今から行ったところで間に合わない。どうしよう。(別冊 使い方)Mou ku-ji da. Ima kara itta tokoro de ma ni awanai. Dou shiyou. It's already nine. Even if I go now, I won't make it in time. What should I do? नौ बज गए। अभी निकल भी जाऊँ तो भी समय पर नहीं पहुँचूँगा। क्या करूँ? નવ વાગી ગયા. હમણાં નીકળું તો પણ સમયસર નહીં પહોંચું. શું કરું?

Think of it as a very pessimistic 〜ても. The result is always "no effect". You can't use it when the effort pays off ("even if I go now, I'll make it" ✗). The 別冊 links this to Q【89】 (参照).

【Q6】 〜てやまない

硬 formal/written (別冊: 硬い表現)
Reading~te yamanai
Meaning (EN)"(I) sincerely / never stop ~ing". It expresses a deep, lasting feeling. 別冊 意味:「強くAする」
Meaning (HI)दिल से / लगातार ...करता रहता हूँ (गहरी भावना)
Meaning (GU)દિલથી / સતત ...કરતો રહું છું (ઊંડી લાગણી)
Connection (接続)V-て + やまない (別冊: A＝願う・祈る・尊敬する・愛する など)
Register硬 — speeches, letters, formal writing
Typical useOnly with a small set of feeling verbs: 願って／祈って／期待して／愛して／尊敬して／後悔して やまない. A first-person feeling in the present (or a third person described in a narrative).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (feeling)V-て + やまない願ってやまないnegatte yamanai
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
災害に見舞われた地域の、一日も早い復興を願ってやまない。(book, Q6)Saigai ni mimawareta chiiki no, ichinichi mo hayai fukkou o negatte yamanai. I sincerely pray for the earliest possible recovery of the disaster-hit region. (cf. 別冊's own English) मैं दिल से कामना करता हूँ कि आपदा से प्रभावित क्षेत्र जल्द से जल्द फिर से खड़ा हो जाए। હું દિલથી પ્રાર્થના કરું છું કે આપત્તિગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તાર વહેલામાં વહેલો ફરી બેઠો થાય.
山田社長は故郷を愛してやまなかった。(別冊 使い方)Yamada shachou wa kokyou o aishite yamanakatta. President Yamada loved his hometown deeply all his life. यामादा अध्यक्ष अपने जन्मस्थान से हमेशा गहरा प्रेम करते रहे। યામાદા પ્રમુખ પોતાના વતનને હંમેશાં ઊંડો પ્રેમ કરતા રહ્યા.

やまない comes from 止む ("to stop"), so the literal meaning is "doesn't stop ~ing". It is a fixed collocation. Don't attach it to ordinary actions (食べてやまない ✗). The 別冊 links this to Q【112】 (参照).

【Q7】 AはBいかんだ／BいかんでA

硬 formal/written (別冊 ⚠: 古い表現)
ReadingA wa B ikan da / B ikan de A
Meaning (EN)"A depends on B". 別冊 意味:「AはBによって決まる」
Meaning (HI)A, B पर निर्भर करता है / B के अनुसार A तय होगा
Meaning (GU)A, B પર આધાર રાખે છે / B મુજબ A નક્કી થશે
Connection (接続)B (N) + いかんだ／いかんで ／ A = N or 文 + か／かどうか (別冊 形)
RegisterFormal/written. The 別冊 calls いかん an old expression (古い表現) meaning 「どうか／どのような状況か」.

The brother (math major) and "I" (bad with numbers) are opposites, which is exactly what にひきかえ stresses. もさることながら and にもまして both need the two sides to point the same way ("A is good, B even better"). はおろか needs a negative "let alone the harder thing" structure (兄はおろか私も… = "not just my brother but even I…"), which contradicts the contrast here.

Q5

君一人が反対意見を（　）、会社の決定が変わるわけがないよ。Kimi hitori ga hantai iken o (___), kaisha no kettei ga kawaru wake ga nai yo.

Full sentence: 君一人が反対意見を言ったところで、会社の決定が変わるわけがないよ。...hantai iken o itta tokoro de, kaisha no kettei ga kawaru wake ga nai yo.
EN: Even if you alone voice an objection, there's no way the company's decision will change.
HI: अगर अकेले तुम विरोध भी कर दो, तो भी कंपनी का फ़ैसला बदलने वाला नहीं।
GU: જો એકલો તું વિરોધ કરી પણ દે, તો પણ કંપનીનો નિર્ણય બદલાવાનો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1言ったところをitta tokoro oat the very moment (someone) said / caught sayingकहते समय (पकड़ा गया)કહેતી વખતે (પકડાયો)
2言ったところでitta tokoro deeven if (you) say it — no useकह भी दो तो भी (कोई फ़ायदा नहीं)કહી પણ દે તો પણ (કોઈ ફાયદો નહીં)
3言ったからにはitta kara ni wanow that you've said it (you must…)जब कह ही दिया है तो (करना होगा)જ્યારે કહી જ દીધું છે તો (કરવું પડશે)
4言ったとあってitta to attebecause (someone special) said itचूँकि (ख़ास व्यक्ति ने) कहा हैકારણ કે (ખાસ વ્યક્તિએ) કહ્યું છે

Why: 別冊 p.3: ポイント〈たところで〉 = 「Aしても（だめだ）」. The ending わけがない ("no way") is the built-in negative result that 〜たところで needs. 〜たところを is used for being caught at a moment (〜ところを見られる／お忙しいところを) and needs a verb like 見る／助ける after it. 〜たからには calls for a resolution or duty after it (〜べきだ), not a hopeless outcome. 〜とあって gives a reason with a special situation, and its result is a factual consequence, not "it won't change".

Q6

災害に見舞われた地域の、一日も早い復興を願って（　）。Saigai ni mimawareta chiiki no, ichinichi mo hayai fukkou o negatte (___).

Full sentence: 災害に見舞われた地域の、一日も早い復興を願ってやまない。...fukkou o negatte yamanai.
EN: I sincerely pray for the earliest possible recovery of the disaster-hit region.
HI: मैं दिल से कामना करता हूँ कि आपदा से प्रभावित क्षेत्र जल्द से जल्द फिर से खड़ा हो जाए।
GU: હું દિલથી પ્રાર્થના કરું છું કે આપત્તિગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તાર વહેલામાં વહેલો ફરી બેઠો થાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おえないoenai(not a pattern after て here; cf. 手に負えない "unmanageable")(यहाँ अमान्य)(અહીં અયોગ્ય)
2すまないsumanai〜てすまない = "sorry for ~ing"...के लिए माफ़ी (ग़लत अर्थ)...માટે માફી (ખોટો અર્થ)
3やまないyamanainever stop ~ing / sincerely ~दिल से ...करता रहता हूँદિલથી ...કરતો રહું છું
4たまらないtamaranai〜てたまらない = unbearably (with feelings or sensations)बर्दाश्त से बाहर (भावना/अनुभूति)સહન બહાર (લાગણી/અનુભૂતિ)

Why: 別冊 p.3: ポイント〈てやまない〉 = 「強くAする」, with A = 願う・祈る・尊敬する・愛する, a formal expression. 願ってやまない is the set phrase for a sincere public wish. 〜てたまらない attaches to emotions or physical sensations (心配でたまらない, 会いたくてたまらない), and 願ってたまらない is unnatural. 〜てすまない means "I'm sorry that ~", and 〜ておえない is not a grammar pattern.

Q7

この店が存続できるかどうかは、今期の売上額（　）だ。Kono mise ga sonzoku dekiru ka dou ka wa, konki no uriagegaku (___) da.

Full sentence: この店が存続できるかどうかは、今期の売上額いかんだ。...konki no uriagegaku ikan da.
EN: Whether this shop can survive depends on this term's sales figures.
HI: यह दुकान चल पाएगी या नहीं, यह इस अवधि की बिक्री पर निर्भर है।
GU: આ દુકાન ટકી શકશે કે નહીં, તે આ ગાળાના વેચાણ પર આધાર રાખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1がてらgaterawhile (doing A, also B)...करते-करते साथ में...કરતાં કરતાં સાથે
2限りkagirilimit / as long asसीमा / जब तकમર્યાદા / જ્યાં સુધી
3ばかりbakarionly / nothing butसिर्फ़ફક્ત
4いかんikandepends on...पर निर्भर...પર આધાર

Why: 別冊 p.3–4: ポイント〈いかんだ〉, 形 [AはBいかんだ], 意味 「AはBによって決まる」. The topic 存続できるかどうかは ("whether or not…") is the A of the 形 line, and it needs a "depends on" predicate. がてら links two actions (散歩がてら) and can't end a sentence with だ. 売上額限りだ means "it's limited to the sales figure", which makes no sense here. 売上額ばかりだ means "it's only sales figures", which doesn't answer "whether or not".

Q8

机の上の理論より、実際の状況に（　）解決法を探るべきだ。Tsukue no ue no riron yori, jissai no joukyou ni (___) kaiketsuhou o saguru beki da.

Full sentence: …実際の状況に即した解決法を探るべきだ。...jissai no joukyou ni soku shita kaiketsuhou o saguru beki da.
EN: Rather than armchair theory, we should look for solutions suited to the actual situation.
HI: किताबी सिद्धांतों के बजाय, हमें असली हालात के अनुरूप हल खोजना चाहिए।
GU: પુસ્તકિયા સિદ્ધાંતોને બદલે, આપણે વાસ્તવિક પરિસ્થિતિને અનુરૂપ ઉકેલ શોધવો જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1即したsoku shitasuited to (the reality)के अनुरूपને અનુરૂપ
2対したtai shita(に対する is the normal attributive; 対した is unnatural)(अस्वाभाविक रूप)(અસ્વાભાવિક રૂપ)
3先立つsakidatsupreceding / prior toसे पहले होने वालाપહેલાં થનારું
4足るtaruworthy of (に足る)के योग्यને લાયક

Why: 別冊 p.4: ポイント〈に即した〉 = 「Aに合った／Aに合わせた」, where A is something real (実情, 現状). The contrast 机の上の理論 vs 実際の状況 is the giveaway. 状況に対した is not natural (we say 状況に対する／に対応した). 状況に先立つ解決法 ("a solution preceding the situation") is illogical. に足る needs an evaluative noun or verb (信頼するに足る), not 状況.

Q9

パーティーでは、最高級のご馳走が並び、まさに贅沢の（　）だった。Paatii de wa, saikoukyuu no gochisou ga narabi, masa ni zeitaku no (___) datta.

Full sentence: …まさに贅沢の極みだった。...masa ni zeitaku no kiwami datta.
EN: At the party, the finest dishes were lined up. It was truly the height of luxury.
HI: पार्टी में बेहतरीन पकवान सजे थे, सचमुच वह विलासिता की पराकाष्ठा थी।
GU: પાર્ટીમાં ઉત્તમ વાનગીઓ ગોઠવાયેલી હતી, ખરેખર એ વૈભવની પરાકાષ્ઠા હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1至りitarithe utmost (光栄の至り, 若気の至り)चरम (सम्मान आदि के लिए)ચરમ (સન્માન વગેરે માટે)
2極みkiwamithe height / pinnacleपराकाष्ठाપરાકાષ્ઠા
3最高saikouthe best (贅沢の最高 isn't a collocation)सबसे बढ़िया (यहाँ अस्वाभाविक)સૌથી સરસ (અહીં અસ્વાભાવિક)
4限りkagirilimit (贅沢の限りを尽くす, but not 〜の限りだ)सीमाમર્યાદા

Why: 別冊 p.4: ポイント〈の極み〉, where A is one of a few fixed words like 贅沢・感激・痛恨. 贅沢の極み is the set phrase. 〜の至り also means "the utmost", but it collocates with 光栄・恐縮・感激・若気, not 贅沢. 最高 doesn't follow N + の in this way. 限り works as 贅沢の限りを尽くす ("indulge in every luxury") or い-adj + 限りだ (うれしい限りだ), but 贅沢の限りだった is not a phrase.

Q10

彼は自分の夢を実現（　）、家族を犠牲にした。Kare wa jibun no yume o jitsugen (___), kazoku o gisei ni shita.

Full sentence: 彼は自分の夢を実現させんがために、家族を犠牲にした。...yume o jitsugen sasen ga tame ni, kazoku o gisei ni shita.
EN: He sacrificed his family in order to make his dream come true.
HI: अपने सपने को साकार करने के लिए उसने अपने परिवार की बलि चढ़ा दी।
GU: પોતાનું સપનું સાકાર કરવા માટે તેણે પોતાના પરિવારનો ભોગ આપી દીધો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1させることとてsaseru koto totebecause it is a matter of (classical reason)चूँकि यह ...की बात है (पुराना कारण-सूचक)કારણ કે એ ...ની વાત છે (જૂનું કારણ-સૂચક)
2させんがためにsasen ga tame niin order to (at all costs)(हर हाल में) ...करने के लिए(ગમે તે ભોગે) ...કરવા માટે
3させればこそsasereba kosoprecisely because (it) makes…ठीक इसी वजह से कि…ચોક્કસ એ જ કારણે કે…
4させたが最後saseta ga saigoonce (it) has done… (then it's hopeless)एक बार कर दिया तो (फिर कोई रास्ता नहीं)એક વાર કરી દીધું તો (પછી કોઈ રસ્તો નહીં)

Why: 別冊 p.4: ポイント〈んがため（に）〉 = 「Aするために」, 硬い表現. The dream is the purpose and the family is the sacrifice, which is the classic んがために shape. 〜こととて gives an apologetic reason (慣れぬこととて) and needs a "please forgive" type of ending. 〜ばこそ gives a positive reason ("precisely because I love you…"), which doesn't fit "he sacrificed his family". 〜たが最後 needs an irreversible bad consequence that follows automatically, not a deliberate act of sacrifice.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a 文の文法1 question, the four options are usually a ready-made confusion set. These are the contrasts from this round most worth learning (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 ⚠ notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ずにはすまない~zu ni wa sumanai must ~ (or it won't be forgiven) V-ない-stem + ずには… Pressure from outside: society, duty, the situation Apologizing, taking responsibility, paying for damage
〜ずにはいられない~zu ni wa irarenai can't help ~ing V-ない-stem + ずには… Pressure from inside: your own urge (別冊 ⚠) Laughing, crying or worrying despite yourself
〜ずにはおかない~zu ni wa okanai will definitely (make) ~ V-ない-stem + ずには… The speaker's strong will, or something that inevitably causes a reaction この映画は見る人を感動させずにはおかない
ごとき／ごとく／ごとしgotoki / gotoku / gotoshi like ~ Nの + ごとき N ／ ごとく V ／ ごとし。 Same word, three slots: before a noun / before a verb / end of sentence Look at what comes right after the blank
にひきかえni hikikae in contrast to N／〜の + にひきかえ The two sides are opposites 兄は優秀なのにひきかえ、弟は…
にもまして／もさることながらni mo mashite / mo saru koto nagara even more than / A of course, and B too N + にもまして ／ N + もさることながら The two sides point the same way, and B wins 味もさることながら、サービスも素晴らしい
〜たところで~ta tokoro de even if ~, it's no use V-た + ところで The result is always negative or futile Talking someone out of a pointless effort
〜たからには~ta kara ni wa now that ~, (must) V-た + からには The result is a resolve or duty (〜べきだ／〜つもりだ) やると言ったからには最後までやれ
Nの極みno kiwami the height of N 贅沢・痛恨・疲労・感激 + の極み Peak of a state, good or bad 贅沢の極み, 痛恨の極み
Nの至りno itari the utmost N 光栄・恐縮・感激・若気 + の至り Mostly formal emotion words in greetings and speeches. 感激 takes both 極み and 至り. お招きいただき光栄の至りです
〜てやまない~te yamanai sincerely, lastingly ~ 願う・祈る・愛する・尊敬する + てやまない A formal, lasting wish or feeling (fixed verbs) Speeches, letters: ご活躍を祈ってやまない
〜てたまらない~te tamaranai unbearably ~ feelings/sensations (〜たくて, 心配で, 暑くて) + たまらない A personal, immediate feeling; casual 会いたくてたまらない
に即した／に則したni soku shita suited to reality / following the rules 実情・状況 + に即した ／ 規則・法 + に則した Same sound, different kanji (別冊 ⚠) 即 = reality, 則 = rule
Exam trap — the ず-family: ずにはすまない, ずにはいられない and ずにはおかない all look alike, and N1 loves putting them in one question, often with a causative or passive twist, as in Q3. Ask where the pressure comes from. Outside duty → すまない. Inside urge → いられない. The speaker's will or an inevitable effect → おかない. Then check who does the action: 取らせず (causative) makes someone else the doer.
Exam trap — slot grammar: Q2 (ごとき before a noun) and Q8 (即した + noun) are answered by the word after the blank, not by meaning. When the four options are one word in different conjugations, check the next word first: a noun needs the attributive form, a verb needs the adverbial form, and 。 needs the sentence-final form.
