【Q41】 〜ばそれまでだ ＋ 〜てしまう

neutral register
Reading~ba sore made da
Meaning (EN)"if ~ happens, that is the end of it / it is all for nothing". Everything before becomes useless once the condition happens. 別冊 問題文の意味:「…期限が過ぎてしまったら、もう終わりだ。（当たった宝くじの価値はゼロになる）」
Meaning (HI)अगर ~ हो गया, तो बस, सब ख़त्म / सब बेकार
Meaning (GU)જો ~ થઈ ગયું, તો બસ, બધું ખતમ / બધું નકામું
Connection (接続)V-ば + それまでだ　(often V-て + しまえば + それまでだ)　(別冊 解き方 ①: 「それまでだ」の前に「〜ば」が来る　②: 「しまう」の前に「〜て」が来る)
RegisterNeutral; common in both speech and writing
Typical useWarnings and resigned comments: 死んでしまえばそれまでだ, 使わなければそれまでだ, 見つかってしまえばそれまでだ.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ば form)V-ば + それまでだ過ぎてしまえばそれまでだsugite shimaeba sore made da
Verb (たら form)(added, not in book) V-たら + それまでだ壊れたらそれまでだkowaretara sore made da
い-adj / な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) rarely used (the condition is normally an event)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
せっかく当たった宝くじも、交換の期限を過ぎてしまえばそれまでだ。 (book, Q41)Sekkaku atatta takarakuji mo, koukan no kigen o sugite shimaeba sore made da. Even a lottery ticket you were lucky enough to win is worth nothing once the deadline for cashing it in has passed. क़िस्मत से जीती हुई लॉटरी भी, अगर उसे भुनाने की आख़िरी तारीख़ निकल जाए, तो बस वहीं ख़त्म — उसका कोई मोल नहीं रहता। નસીબથી લાગેલી લોટરી પણ, જો તેને બદલાવવાની છેલ્લી તારીખ વીતી જાય, તો ત્યાં જ ખતમ — તેની કોઈ કિંમત રહેતી નથી.
どんなに高いカメラでも、使わなければそれまでだ。 (added, not in book)Donna ni takai kamera demo, tsukawanakereba sore made da. However expensive a camera is, if you don't use it, it's worth nothing. कैमरा कितना भी महँगा हो, अगर इस्तेमाल न करो तो सब बेकार। કૅમેરો ગમે તેટલો મોંઘો હોય, જો વાપરો નહીં તો બધું નકામું.

〜てしまう adds the feeling that the bad thing happens completely and cannot be undone, so 〜てしまえばそれまでだ is a very common chunk. Do not confuse it with V-dict + までだ ("I will simply ~", e.g. だめならあきらめるまでだ) or V-た + までだ ("I only did ~"). A close relative is 〜たら最後 ("once ~, there is no going back"); see §3.

【Q42】 〜べからず ＋ 〜につき

硬 formal/written (our judgment)
Reading~bekarazu / ~ni tsuki
Meaning (EN)べからず: "must not ~ / ~ is forbidden" — the classical negative of べし, used on signs and notices. 別冊 ⚠:「Aべからず」＝「Aできない／Aしてはいけない」. につき: "because of / owing to N" — a formal reason word typical of notices.
Meaning (HI)べからず: ... करना मना है / न करें। につき: ... के कारण
Meaning (GU)べからず: ... કરવાની મનાઈ છે / ન કરવું. につき: ... ને કારણે
Connection (接続)V-dict + べからず (する → するべからず／すべからず)　／　N + につき　(別冊 解き方 ①: 「べからず」の前に動詞・辞書形が来る　③: 「につき」の前に名詞が来る)
Register硬 — signs, notices, rules, old-style writing
Typical useProhibition signs: 芝生に入るべからず, 立ち入るべからず; notices with a reason: 工事中につき, 準備中につき, 故障につき.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + べからず使用するべからずshiyou suru bekarazu
する-verb (short form)(added, not in book) N + すべからず駐車すべからずchuusha subekarazu
Noun (reason)N + につき故障につきkoshou ni tsuki
Adjectives(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
水道管が破損して水が流れないので、トイレのドアに「故障につき使用するべからず」と書いた紙を張っておいた。 (book, Q42)Suidoukan ga hason shite mizu ga nagarenai node, toire no doa ni "koshou ni tsuki shiyou suru bekarazu" to kaita kami o hatte oita. The water pipe was damaged and the water wouldn't flow, so I put a sign on the toilet door saying "Out of order — do not use." पानी का पाइप टूट जाने से पानी नहीं बह रहा था, इसलिए मैंने टॉयलेट के दरवाज़े पर "ख़राबी के कारण उपयोग न करें" लिखा काग़ज़ चिपका दिया। પાણીની પાઇપ તૂટી જવાથી પાણી વહેતું ન હતું, તેથી મેં ટોઇલેટના દરવાજા પર "ખરાબીને કારણે ઉપયોગ કરવો નહીં" લખેલો કાગળ ચોંટાડી દીધો.
芝生に入るべからず。 (別冊 ✎)Shibafu ni hairu bekarazu. Keep off the grass. घास पर न चलें। ઘાસ પર ચાલવું નહીં.
本日は定休日につき、休業いたします。 (added, not in book)Honjitsu wa teikyuubi ni tsuki, kyuugyou itashimasu. As today is our regular holiday, we are closed. आज साप्ताहिक छुट्टी के कारण दुकान बंद रहेगी। આજે સાપ્તાહિક રજાને કારણે દુકાન બંધ રહેશે.

べからず belongs to the べし family: べき (should), べく (in order to, Q43), べからざる (that must not, e.g. 欠くべからざる "indispensable"). Watch the two meanings of につき: N + につき = "because of N" (notices), but number + につき = "per" (1人につき3枚). The ★ trick here is that べからず must close the quotation, so it can only go in the last blank.

【Q43】 〜べく

硬 formal/written (our judgment)
Reading~beku
Meaning (EN)"in order to ~ / with the aim of ~". A formal purpose expression (≈ 〜ために / 〜ようと). The 別冊 gives no ⚠ for this item.
Meaning (HI)... करने के लिए / ... करने के इरादे से
Meaning (GU)... કરવા માટે / ... કરવાના ઇરાદાથી
Connection (接続)V-dict + べく (する → するべく／すべく)　(別冊 解き方 ①: 「べく」の前に動詞・辞書形が来る)
Register硬 — news, reports, speeches
Typical usePurpose of a deliberate action: 〜を解決すべく, 〜を実現すべく, 〜に会うべく. The main clause is a fact (what someone did), not a request or command.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-dict + べく要求するべくyoukyuu suru beku
する-verb (short form)(added, not in book) N + すべく実現すべくjitsugen subeku
Adjectives / Noun(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
不当な労働条件の改善を要求するべく、社員が団結して立ち上がった。 (book, Q43)Futou na roudou jouken no kaizen o youkyuu suru beku, shain ga danketsu shite tachiagatta. To demand that their unfair working conditions be improved, the employees united and rose up. (cf. 別冊's own English) अनुचित कामकाजी हालात में सुधार की माँग करने के लिए, कर्मचारी एकजुट होकर उठ खड़े हुए। અન્યાયી કામકાજની પરિસ્થિતિમાં સુધારાની માંગ કરવા માટે કર્મચારીઓ એક થઈને ઊભા થયા.
夢を実現すべく、彼は毎日努力を続けている。 (added, not in book)Yume o jitsugen subeku, kare wa mainichi doryoku o tsuzukete iru. In order to make his dream come true, he keeps working hard every day. अपना सपना साकार करने के लिए वह हर दिन मेहनत करता रहता है। પોતાનું સપનું સાકાર કરવા માટે તે દરરોજ મહેનત કરતો રહે છે.

Exam trap: べく cannot be followed by a request, command or invitation (✗ 合格すべく、勉強しなさい); use ために there. Also keep it apart from 〜べき (should) and 〜べからず (must not, Q42). 別冊 解き方 order: lock 要求するべく first, then 労働条件の + noun, then 改善を + verb.

【Q44】 〜を皮切りとして（〜を皮切りに）

neutral–formal register
Reading~o kawakiri to shite / ~o kawakiri ni
Meaning (EN)"starting with A (and then B, B, B…)". A is the first of a series of similar events. 別冊 ⚠:「Aを皮切りとして、B」＝「Aが最初で、それに続いてB」
Meaning (HI)A से शुरुआत करके (और फिर एक के बाद एक B)
Meaning (GU)A થી શરૂઆત કરીને (અને પછી એક પછી એક B)
Connection (接続)N + を皮切りとして／を皮切りに(して)　(別冊 解き方 ①: 「皮切り」の後に「として」が来る　②: 「皮切り」の前に「〜を」が来る)
RegisterNeutral to formal; news, business, event announcements
Typical useTours, product lines, a chain of incidents: 東京公演を皮切りに, この事件を皮切りに. The second half shows things spreading or continuing.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を皮切りとしてゲーム機を皮切りとしてgeemu-ki o kawakiri to shite
Noun (short form)(added, not in book) N + を皮切りに東京公演を皮切りにToukyou kouen o kawakiri ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形 + の + を皮切りに入選したのを皮切りにnyuusen shita no o kawakiri ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
S社は、5年前に発売したゲーム機を皮切りとしていくつもの新商品を開発している。 (book, Q44)Esu-sha wa, go-nen mae ni hatsubai shita geemu-ki o kawakiri to shite ikutsu mo no shin-shouhin o kaihatsu shite iru. Starting with the game console it released five years ago, Company S has developed one new product after another. पाँच साल पहले लॉन्च किए गए गेम कंसोल से शुरुआत करके, कंपनी S ने कई नए उत्पाद विकसित किए हैं। પાંચ વર્ષ પહેલાં લૉન્ચ કરેલા ગેમ કન્સોલથી શરૂઆત કરીને, કંપની S એ ઘણાં નવાં ઉત્પાદનો વિકસાવ્યાં છે.
来月の東京公演を皮切りに、全国ツアーが始まる。 (added, not in book)Raigetsu no Toukyou kouen o kawakiri ni, zenkoku tsuaa ga hajimaru. Starting with next month's Tokyo concert, a nationwide tour will begin. अगले महीने टोक्यो के कार्यक्रम से शुरू होकर, देशभर का दौरा शुरू होगा। આવતા મહિને ટોક્યોના કાર્યક્રમથી શરૂ થઈને, દેશભરનો પ્રવાસ શરૂ થશે.

皮切り literally means "the first cut". Compare 〜をはじめ(として) "A and others, A being the main example" (no time sequence) and 〜を契機に "taking A as the turning point". Here the 別冊 ポイント is printed as 〈を 皮切りとして〉, so the を tile (ゲーム機を) must sit right before 皮切り.

【Q45】 〜を禁じえない ＋ 〜に対する

硬 formal/written (our judgment)
Reading~o kinjienai / ~ni taisuru
Meaning (EN)を禁じえない: "cannot help feeling ~ / cannot hold back ~" — a feeling rises in spite of oneself. に対する + N: "N toward / against ~". (No ⚠ line in the 別冊 for this item.)
Meaning (HI)を禁じえない: ... की भावना रोक नहीं पाना। に対する: ... के प्रति
Meaning (GU)を禁じえない: ... ની લાગણી રોકી ન શકવી. に対する: ... પ્રત્યે
Connection (接続)N (emotion) + を禁じえない　／　N + に対する + N　(別冊 解き方 ①: 「禁じえない」の前に「〜を」が来る　②: 「対する」の前に「〜に」が来る)
Register硬 — essays, news commentary, formal speech
Typical useEmotion nouns only: 怒り・憤り・同情・涙・驚き・失望 を禁じえない. に対する links a target to a noun: 大人に対する怒り, 政府に対する不満.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (emotion)N + を禁じえない憤りの念を禁じえないikidoori no nen o kinjienai
Noun (target) + NounN + に対する + N大人に対する憤りotona ni taisuru ikidoori
Verb / adjectives(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
児童虐待のニュースを耳にするたびに身勝手な大人に対する憤りの念を禁じえないのは、私だけではないはずだ。 (book, Q45)Jidou gyakutai no nyuusu o mimi ni suru tabi ni migatte na otona ni taisuru ikidoori no nen o kinjienai no wa, watashi dake de wa nai hazu da. Surely I am not the only one who cannot help feeling anger toward selfish adults every time I hear news of child abuse. (cf. 別冊's own English) बाल-शोषण की ख़बर सुनते हुए हर बार स्वार्थी बड़ों के प्रति आक्रोश को न रोक पाने वाला अकेला मैं नहीं हो सकता। બાળ-શોષણના સમાચાર સાંભળું ત્યારે દર વખતે સ્વાર્થી મોટેરાંઓ પ્રત્યે આક્રોશ રોકી ન શકનાર હું એકલો નહીં હોઉં.
被災者の話を聞いて、涙を禁じえなかった。 (added, not in book)Hisaisha no hanashi o kiite, namida o kinjienakatta. Hearing the disaster victims' stories, I could not hold back my tears. आपदा पीड़ितों की बातें सुनकर मैं अपने आँसू रोक नहीं पाया। આપત્તિગ્રસ્તોની વાતો સાંભળીને હું મારાં આંસુ રોકી ન શક્યો.

禁じえない = 禁じる (forbid, hold back) + える (can) in the negative: "cannot hold back". It is the written cousin of 〜ずにはいられない and 〜てならない. It is only for emotions, never for actions (✗ 笑うことを禁じえない is unnatural; use 笑いを禁じえない).

HI: क़िस्मत से जीती हुई लॉटरी भी, अगर उसे भुनाने की आख़िरी तारीख़ निकल जाए, तो बस वहीं ख़त्म — उसका कोई मोल नहीं रहता।

Why: ★ = 3rd slot = 3 過ぎて (別冊 正解 3; full order 2→4→3→1). 別冊 p.49 ポイント〈ばそれまでだ〉〈てしまう〉. ① それまでだ needs a 〜ば form before it → しまえばそれまでだ (blank 4). ② しまう needs a 〜て form before it → 過ぎてしまえばそれまでだ (blanks 3–4). ③ 交換の needs a noun after it → 交換の期限を. ④ 期限を needs a verb after it → 交換の期限を過ぎてしまえばそれまでだ fills all four blanks.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In a ★ question the tiles are pieces of one sentence, so the traps are about connection (what must come right before or after each tile) and about near-synonyms of the round's patterns. These are the contrasts worth learning from this round (our own analysis, building on the 別冊 ⚠ notes):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ばそれまでだ~ba sore made da if ~, it's all over V-ば + それまでだ Everything before becomes worthless 過ぎてしまえばそれまでだ
〜たら最後~tara saigo once ~, there's no going back V-たら + 最後 Stresses an irreversible bad chain that follows 寝たら最後、朝まで起きない
V-dict + までだ~made da I'll simply ~ (no other choice) V-dict + までだ Resolve, not loss だめならやり直すまでだ
〜べからず~bekarazu must not ~ V-dict + べからず Signs/notices; ends the sentence 入るべからず
〜べく~beku in order to ~ V-dict + べく Purpose; mid-sentence; no command after it 要求するべく立ち上がった
〜べきだ~beki da should ~ V-dict + べきだ Opinion/duty, everyday register もっと休むべきだ
N + につき (reason)ni tsuki because of N N + につき Formal notice 故障につき使用禁止
number + につき (rate)ni tsuki per ~ number + につき Same form, different meaning 1人につき2枚
〜を皮切りに(として)~o kawakiri ni starting with A, then many N + を皮切りに Time order: A is first of a series 東京を皮切りに全国で
〜をはじめ(として)~o hajime A and others (A as the main example) N + をはじめ No time order; just a list headed by A 社長をはじめ社員全員
〜を禁じえない~o kinjienai can't help feeling ~ N (emotion) + を禁じえない 硬; emotion nouns only 同情を禁じえない
〜ずにはいられない~zu ni wa irarenai can't help doing ~ V-ない-stem + ずにはいられない Takes a verb; everyday 笑わずにはいられない
★ strategy — find the sentence-closer: tiles like べからず, 禁じえない and それまでだ (in the stem) close a clause. Work backwards from them: べからず ← 使用する, 禁じえない ← 〜を, それまでだ ← 〜ば ← 〜て. Three of this round's five questions fall out that way, exactly as the 別冊 解き方 shows.
Exam trap — the べし family: べく (purpose), べき (should), べからず (must not), べからざる (that must not be ~). All take the dictionary form, and する can shorten to す (すべく, すべからず). N1 often puts two of them in the same option set, so learn them as a group.
