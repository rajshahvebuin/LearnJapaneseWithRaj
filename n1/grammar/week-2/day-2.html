2日目 — どんなに高かろうが (Donna ni takakarou ga)

Source

Grammar points covered
〜であれ〜であれ〜うと〜うと／〜うが〜うが疑問詞＋volitional＋が／と（も）
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.35 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Today's three patterns are all concessive "it doesn't matter which" constructions, escalating from a plain either/or (Point 1) through a volitional either/or (Point 2) to a full "no matter how/what/who" when paired with a question word (Point 3). Point 1 and Point 3 are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; Point 2 is left unmarked (neutral), though it reads naturally literary in practice.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜であれ〜であれ

硬 formal/written
Reading~de are ~de are
Meaning (EN)"whether ~ or ~, it makes no difference" — concedes any of the listed alternatives, all leading to the same conclusion
Meaning (HI)चाहे...हो या...हो, कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता
Meaning (GU)ભલે...હોય કે...હોય, કંઈ ફરક પડતો નથી
Connection (接続)[aであれbであれ] N／na + であれ　❶ だれであれ、どこであれ、何であれ
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useStating that a rule or judgment applies regardless of which named category someone/something falls into.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生であれ学生であれ、この規則には従わなければならない。Sensei de are gakusei de are, kono kisoku ni wa shitagawanakereba naranai. You must follow the rules regardless of whether you are a teacher or student. चाहे शिक्षक हो या छात्र, इस नियम का पालन करना ही होगा। ભલે શિક્ષક હોય કે વિદ્યાર્થી, આ નિયમનું પાલન કરવું જ પડશે.
彼がだれであれ、特別扱いするのはおかしい。Kare ga dare de are, tokubetsu atsukai suru no wa okashii. It is wrong to give him special treatment even if he is someone important. वह चाहे कोई भी हो, उसे विशेष तरज़ीह देना ग़लत है। તે ભલે કોઈ પણ હોય, તેને ખાસ વ્યવહાર આપવો ખોટું છે.

だれであれ／どこであれ／何であれ are common fixed combinations with question words, all meaning "no matter who/where/what" — worth memorizing as ready-made chunks.

【2】 〜うと〜うと／〜うが〜うが

neutral register
Reading~u to ~u to / ~u ga ~u ga
Meaning (EN)"whether ~ or ~, either way" — the volitional-form version of concessive alternation, naming two options that lead to the same outcome
Meaning (HI)चाहे...हो या...हो, दोनों ही सूरत में वही परिणाम
Meaning (GU)ભલે...હોય કે...હોય, બંને સ્થિતિમાં એ જ પરિણામ
Connection (接続)[aうとbうと] [aうがbうが] Vよう／Aかろう／na・Nだろう／na・Nであろう + が／と　❶ 〜だろうとなかろうと
RegisterNeutral (unmarked in the source, though it reads as literary/formal in practice)
Typical useListing two opposite possibilities (often weather, presence/absence, new/used) and declaring the outcome unaffected.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
雨が降ろうと雪が降ろうと明日の集まりには必ず行くよ。Ame ga furou to yuki ga furou to, ashita no atsumari ni wa kanarazu iku yo. Come rain or snow (or whatever), I'll definitely come to the gathering tomorrow. बारिश हो या बर्फ़, कल की सभा में मैं ज़रूर आऊँगा। વરસાદ પડે કે બરફ પડે, કાલની સભામાં હું ચોક્કસ આવીશ.
私は肉だろうが魚だろうが、なんでも食べます。Watashi wa niku darou ga sakana darou ga, nan demo tabemasu. I'll eat meat, fish, or anything. मैं मांस हो या मछली, कुछ भी खा लेता/लेती हूँ। હું માંસ હોય કે માછલી, બધું જ ખાઈ લઉં છું.
新品であろうと、中古であろうと、そんな型の古いパソコンは買うべきではないと思う。Shinpin de arou to, chuuko de arou to, sonna kata no furui pasokon wa kau beki de wa nai to omou. I don't think you should buy such an old model whether it's new or secondhand. नया हो या पुराना, मुझे लगता है इस तरह का पुराना मॉडल कंप्यूटर नहीं ख़रीदना चाहिए। નવું હોય કે જૂનું, મને લાગે છે આવું જૂનું મોડેલ કમ્પ્યુટર ન ખરીદવું જોઈએ.
明日、時間があろうとなかろうと、連絡だけは入れてください。Ashita, jikan ga arou to nakarou to, renraku dake wa irete kudasai. Please contact me tomorrow even if you are busy. कल समय हो या न हो, बस एक बार संपर्क कर दें। કાલે સમય હોય કે ન હોય, બસ એક વાર સંપર્ક કરી દેજો.

〜だろうとなかろうと (Example 4) is the fixed "whether or not" shape of this pattern — pairing an affirmative volitional with its own negative counterpart rather than two different options.

【3】 疑問詞＋volitional＋が／と（も）

硬 formal/written
Readingdonna ni takakarou ga, etc.
Meaning (EN)"no matter how/what/where/who ~" — pairs a question word with the volitional-concessive form to mean "regardless of X"
Meaning (HI)चाहे कितना भी/जो भी/जहाँ भी...हो, कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता
Meaning (GU)ભલે ગમે તેટલું/ગમે તે/ગમે ત્યાં...હોય, કંઈ ફરક પડતો નથી
Connection (接続)[疑問詞] + Vよう／Aかろう／na・Nだろう／na・Nであろう + が／と（も）
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useDeclaring total indifference to how extreme, what kind, or who is involved.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
必要だから、どんなに高かろうがそれを買わなくてはいけない。Hitsuyou dakara, donna ni takakarou ga sore o kawanakute wa ikenai. I must buy it no matter how expensive it is because I need it. ज़रूरत है, तो चाहे कितना भी महँगा हो, यह ख़रीदना ही होगा। જરૂર છે, તો ભલે ગમે તેટલું મોંઘું હોય, આ ખરીદવું જ પડશે.
あなたがどんなに謝ろうとも、今回は許さない。Anata ga donna ni ayamarou tomo, konkai wa yurusanai. I won't forgive you this time no matter how much you apologize. तुम चाहे कितनी भी माफ़ी माँगो, इस बार माफ़ नहीं करूँगा/करूँगी। તું ભલે ગમે તેટલી માફી માંગ, આ વખતે માફ નહીં કરું.
あなたが何歳であろうが関係ない。仕事ができればいい。Anata ga nansai de arou ga kankei nai. Shigoto ga dekireba ii. I don't care how old you are as long as you can do the job. तुम्हारी उम्र चाहे कितनी भी हो, फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता। काम आना चाहिए बस। તારી ઉંમર ભલે ગમે તેટલી હોય, ફરક નથી પડતો. કામ આવડવું જોઈએ બસ.
私が何をしようが勝手だ。あなたに言う必要はない。Watashi ga nani o shiyou ga katte da. Anata ni iu hitsuyou wa nai. It's not your business. I don't need to tell you what I am going to do. मैं जो भी करूँ, वह मेरी मर्ज़ी है। तुम्हें बताने की ज़रूरत नहीं। હું જે પણ કરું, તે મારી મરજી છે. તને કહેવાની જરૂર નથી.

The book's own れい list of other fixed combinations worth memorizing: いくら寒かろうと（関係ない）, どこへ行こうと（同じだ）, 何と言おうが（自由だ）, どうなろうが（どうでもいい）. Notice the question word always sits right before the volitional-concessive form.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.33. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.35.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼は、（　）かまわず電話をかけてくる。Kare wa, (___) kamawazu denwa o kakete kuru.

Full sentence: 彼は、朝だろうが夜中だろうがかまわず電話をかけてくる。Kare wa, asa darou ga yonaka darou ga kamawazu denwa o kakete kuru.
EN: He calls me regardless of whether it's morning or the middle of the night.
HI: वह सुबह हो या आधी रात, बिना फ़िक्र किए फ़ोन कर देता है।
GU: તે સવાર હોય કે મધરાત, ચિંતા વગર ફોન કરી દે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a朝だろうが夜中だろうがasa darou ga yonaka darou gawhether morning or midnightसुबह हो या आधी रातસવાર હોય કે મધરાત
b朝だろう夜中だろうasa darou yonaka darou(missing the が that makes this concessive)(अपूर्ण रूप)(અપૂર્ણ સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 2's pattern needs が (or と) after each だろう to form the concessive "whether...or..."; without it, the two clauses don't connect grammatically.

Q2

この部屋でどんなに（　）、外からは何も聞こえません。Kono heya de donna ni (___), soto kara wa nani mo kikoemasen.

Full sentence: この部屋でどんなに騒ごうとも、外からは何も聞こえません。Kono heya de donna ni sawagou tomo, soto kara wa nani mo kikoemasen.
EN: No matter how much noise you make in this room, nothing can be heard from outside.
HI: इस कमरे में चाहे कितना भी शोर करो, बाहर से कुछ भी सुनाई नहीं देता।
GU: આ રૂમમાં ભલે ગમે તેટલો અવાજ કરો, બહારથી કંઈ સંભળાતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a騒ごうともsawagou tomono matter how much one makes noiseचाहे कितना भी शोर करोભલે ગમે તેટલો અવાજ કરો
b騒ぐであれsawagu de are(wrong pattern — であれ needs a noun/na-adj, not this verb form)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: どんなに pairs with Point 3's volitional-concessive (騒ごうとも); であれ is Point 1's pattern, reserved for nouns/na-adjectives, not verbs like 騒ぐ.

Q3

たとえ（　）、明日の試合は予定どおり行います。Tatoe (___), ashita no shiai wa yotei doori okonaimasu.

Full sentence: たとえ雨であろうと雪であろうと、明日の試合は予定どおり行います。Tatoe ame de arou to yuki de arou to, ashita no shiai wa yotei doori okonaimasu.
EN: Whether it rains or snows, tomorrow's match will proceed as scheduled.
HI: बारिश हो या बर्फ़, कल का मैच जैसा तय है वैसे ही होगा।
GU: વરસાદ પડે કે બરફ પડે, કાલની મેચ નક્કી કર્યા મુજબ જ થશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a雨であろうと雪であろうとame de arou to yuki de arou towhether it's rain or snowबारिश हो या बर्फ़વરસાદ પડે કે બરફ પડે

Why: たとえ ("even if") signals Point 2's volitional-concessive であろうと; plain-form ～が does not create a concessive meaning here.

Q4

どの大学（　）、進学先が決まってほっとした。Dono daigaku (___), shingakusaki ga kimatte hotto shita.

Full sentence: どの大学であれ、進学先が決まってほっとした。Dono daigaku de are, shingakusaki ga kimatte hotto shita.
EN: Whichever university it is, I felt relieved once my next school was decided.
HI: कोई भी कॉलेज हो, आगे पढ़ने की जगह तय होने से चैन मिला।
GU: ગમે તે કૉલેજ હોય, આગળ ભણવાની જગ્યા નક્કી થવાથી નિરાંત થઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aであるとde aru to(not a concessive form)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
bであれde arewhichever it is/no matter whichकोई भी होગમે તે હોય

Why: どの大学であれ ("whichever university") is Point 1's だれ／どこ／何であれ family applied to どの大学; であると is just a plain conditional, not a concession.

Q5

（　）君の自由だが、他人に迷惑をかけることは許されない。(___) kimi no jiyuu da ga, tanin ni meiwaku o kakeru koto wa yurusarenai.

Full sentence: 何をしようと君の自由だが、他人に迷惑をかけることは許されない。Nani o shiyou to kimi no jiyuu da ga, tanin ni meiwaku o kakeru koto wa yurusarenai.
EN: Whatever you do is your own business, but you're not allowed to inconvenience others.
HI: तुम जो भी करो, वह तुम्हारी मर्ज़ी है, लेकिन दूसरों को परेशान करने की इजाज़त नहीं है।
GU: તું જે પણ કરે, તે તારી મરજી છે, પણ બીજાને હેરાન કરવાની છૂટ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a何だろうがnan darou gawhatever it is (wrong — needs an action verb here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b何をしようとnani o shiyou towhatever one doesजो भी करोજે પણ કરો

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

あの夫婦が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、子どもはかわいそうだと思う。Ano fuufu ga ___ , kodomo wa kawaisou da to omou.

Tiles: 1 知った (shitta)　2 なろうが (narou ga)　3 どう (dou)　4 ことではないが (koto de wa nai ga)

3 どう→2 なろうが→1 知った→4 ことではないが

あの夫婦がどうなろうが知ったことではないが、子どもはかわいそうだと思う。Ano fuufu ga dou narou ga shitta koto de wa nai ga, kodomo wa kawaisou da to omou.

EN: Whatever happens to that couple isn't my concern, but I do feel sorry for their child.
HI: उस जोड़े का जो भी हो, मुझे उससे कोई मतलब नहीं, लेकिन बच्चे पर तरस आता है।
GU: તે યુગલનું જે પણ થાય, મને તેની સાથે કંઈ સંબંધ નથી, પણ બાળક પર દયા આવે છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's どう＋volitional＋が — total indifference to the couple's fate, contrasted with genuine concern for the child.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ には変わりはない。___ ni wa kawari wa nai.

Tiles: 1 なかろうと (nakarou to)　2 人を傷つけたこと (hito o kizutsuketa koto)　3 故意 (koi)　4 であろうと (de arou to)

3 故意→4 であろうと→1 なかろうと→2 人を傷つけたこと

故意であろうとなかろうと、人を傷つけたことには変わりはない。Koi de arou to nakarou to, hito o kizutsuketa koto ni wa kawari wa nai.

EN: Whether it was intentional or not, the fact that you hurt someone doesn't change.
HI: जान-बूझकर हो या न हो, किसी को चोट पहुँचाने की बात नहीं बदलती।
GU: ઈરાદાપૂર્વક હોય કે ન હોય, કોઈને ઈજા પહોંચાડ્યાની વાત બદલાતી નથી.

Why: Built on Point 2's 〜であろうと〜なかろうと ("whether or not") — intentional or not, the fact of harming someone stands unchanged.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All three of today's patterns concede "it doesn't matter which" — the difference is what kind of thing is being conceded:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜であれ〜であれ~de are ~de are Whether ~ or ~ N／na + であれ Concedes a category/identity (a role, a place, a thing). "Teacher or student, the rule applies."
〜うと〜うと／〜うが〜うが~u to/~u ga Whether ~ or ~, either way Vよう／Aかろう／na・Nだろう + が／と Concedes two named alternative conditions/actions. "Rain or snow, meat or fish."
疑問詞＋volitional＋が／とquestion word + volitional No matter how/what/where/who ~ [疑問詞] + Vよう／Aかろう／na・Nだろう + が／と（も） Concedes an open-ended range, not just two named options. "No matter how expensive," "whatever I do."
Exam trap: であれ (Point 1) only works with nouns/na-adjectives — you cannot say 騒ぐであれ for a verb like 騒ぐ (Q2 above tests exactly this). If the concession is about a verb or an い-adjective's degree, you need Point 2/3's volitional-concessive form (Vよう／Aかろう) instead, never であれ.
