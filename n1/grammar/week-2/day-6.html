6日目 — いつまで続くのやら (Itsu made tsuzuku no yara)

Answer key

Notes
Today's theme is uncertainty and resignation — an inability to judge between two options (とも, のやら) and an acceptance that either outcome brings its own minor issue (たらたで). The final pattern turns that same uncertainty into an exasperated, wondering "I have no idea" tone.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とも〜とも

neutral register
Reading~to mo ~to mo
Meaning (EN)"cannot say ~ or ~" — a clear judgment cannot be made between two possibilities
Meaning (HI)न...कह सकते हैं, न...(कोई स्पष्ट फ़ैसला नहीं कर सकते)
Meaning (GU)ન...કહી શકાય, ન...(કોઈ સ્પષ્ટ નિર્ણય ન કરી શકાય)
Connection (接続)[aともbとも] N＋とも; V／A／na／N（plain） + とも　❶ うんともすんともいわない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useRefusing to commit to a judgment between two named possibilities, usually followed by 言えない／判断ができない.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近のゲームは、子どもにいいとも悪いとも言えない。あるものは脳の発達に効果があるらしい。Saikin no geemu wa, kodomo ni ii to mo warui to mo ienai. Aru mono wa nou no hattatsu ni kouka ga aru rashii. You can't say all the recent games are bad. Some seem to stimulate brain development. आज के गेम बच्चों के लिए अच्छे हैं या बुरे, यह कहना मुश्किल है। कुछ तो दिमाग़ी विकास में मदद करते दिखते हैं। આજના ગેમ બાળકો માટે સારા છે કે ખરાબ, એ કહેવું અઘરું છે. કેટલાક તો મગજના વિકાસમાં મદદરૂપ લાગે છે.
これは本物とも偽物とも判断ができない。Kore wa honmono to mo nisemono to mo handan ga dekinai. I can't tell whether it is real or fake. यह असली है या नकली, फ़ैसला नहीं हो पा रहा। આ સાચું છે કે નકલી, નિર્ણય થઈ શકતો નથી.

❶ うんともすんともいわない ("doesn't say a peep") is a fixed idiom built on this same とも — うん and すん here are meaningless placeholder sounds, so the phrase literally emphasizes "not even a single sound," used for total silence/no response.

【2】 〜たら〜たで

neutral register
Reading~tara ~ta de
Meaning (EN)"if ~ (then that comes with its own issue)" — acknowledges that even the alternative/opposite situation brings its own (usually minor) complication
Meaning (HI)अगर...हो तो, उसकी अपनी ही एक समस्या है
Meaning (GU)જો...હોય તો, તેની પોતાની જ એક સમસ્યા છે
Connection (接続)VたらVたで; AかったらAかったで／AかったらAいで; naならnaで; NならNで　◆否定形も使う
RegisterNeutral
Typical usePhilosophically accepting that both a situation and its opposite each bring a (usually small) trade-off.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
庭があったらあったで、草むしりが大変だ。Niwa ga attara atta de, kusamushiri ga taihen da. It's nice to have a garden but someone has to do the weeding. बगीचा हो तो होने की अपनी समस्या है — घास निकालना मुश्किल हो जाता है। બગીચો હોય તો હોવાની પોતાની સમસ્યા છે — ઘાસ કાઢવું અઘરું બની જાય છે.
便せんがなかったらないで、コピー用紙でもかまいません。Binsen ga nakattara nai de, kopii youshi demo kamaimasen. If there's no letter writing paper available, photocopy paper will do. पत्र लिखने का काग़ज़ न हो तो न हो, फ़ोटोकॉपी काग़ज़ से भी चल जाएगा। પત્ર લખવાનો કાગળ ન હોય તો ન હોય, ફોટોકોપી કાગળથી પણ ચાલશે.

◆否定形も使う (negative forms are also used) — the second example (なかったらないで) shows this working smoothly even with a negative condition, still meaning "if that's the case too, it's fine/manageable."

【3】 〜のやら〜のやら

neutral register
Reading~no yara ~no yara
Meaning (EN)"whether to ~ or ~ — can't tell (torn between two opposite reactions)" — expresses uncertainty between two contrasting reactions/options
Meaning (HI)...करूँ या...करूँ, समझ नहीं आता (दो विरोधी भावनाओं के बीच उलझन)
Meaning (GU)...કરું કે...કરું, સમજાતું નથી (બે વિરોધી લાગણી વચ્ચે મૂંઝવણ)
Connection (接続)[aのやらbのやら] V／A（plain） + のやら; naな + のやら　◆aとbは反対の意味
RegisterNeutral
Typical useExpressing being torn between two opposite emotional reactions to the same situation.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
喜んでいいのやら悲しんでいいのやら、最近仕事の依頼が多く、趣味の時間がまったくとれない。Yorokonde ii no yara kanashinde ii no yara, saikin shigoto no irai ga ooku, shumi no jikan ga mattaku torenai. It's good to have people send me so many jobs, but on the other hand, I've got so many that I have no time for my hobbies. ख़ुश हूँ या दुखी, समझ नहीं आता — काम की माँग तो बहुत है, पर शौक़ के लिए समय ही नहीं मिलता। ખુશ છું કે દુઃખી, સમજાતું નથી — કામની માંગ ખૂબ છે, પણ શોખ માટે સમય જ મળતો નથી.

The book's own れい list of other fixed combinations: いいのやら悪いのやら (good or bad, can't tell), あるのやらないのやら (existing or not, unclear), うそなのやら本当なのやら (a lie or the truth, unclear). ◆aとbは反対の意味 — a and b must be an opposite pair, same requirement as つ〜つ from yesterday.

【4】 疑問詞＋のやら／ものやら／ことやら

neutral–colloquial register
Readingitsu made tsuzuku no yara, etc.
Meaning (EN)"I wonder ~ (no idea how/when/what)" — pairs a question word with のやら／ものやら／ことやら to express uncertain wondering, often with a resigned or exasperated tone
Meaning (HI)पता नहीं...(कब/कैसे/क्या) — एक उलझन या हैरानी भरा लहज़ा
Meaning (GU)ખબર નથી...(ક્યારે/કેવી રીતે/શું) — એક મૂંઝવણ કે નવાઈભર્યો સ્વર
Connection (接続)[疑問詞] + V／A（plain） + のやら／ものやら; na + ことやら
RegisterNeutral, colloquial-leaning
Typical useA rhetorical, half-exasperated "I wonder..." with no real expectation of finding out the answer.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この不景気はいつまで続くのやら。Kono fukeiki wa itsu made tsuzuku no yara. I wonder how long this economic slump will continue. पता नहीं यह मंदी कब तक चलेगी। ખબર નથી આ મંદી ક્યાં સુધી ચાલશે.
言葉も話せないのに、来年1年間オーストラリアに行くことにした。どうなることやら。Kotoba mo hanasenai noni, rainen ichinenkan oosutoraria ni iku koto ni shita. Dou naru koto yara. I've decided to go to Australia next year for a year even though I don't speak the language, but I don't know what to expect. भाषा भी नहीं आती, फिर भी अगले साल एक साल के लिए ऑस्ट्रेलिया जाने का फ़ैसला किया। पता नहीं क्या होगा। ભાષા પણ નથી આવડતી, છતાં આવતા વર્ષે એક વર્ષ માટે ઓસ્ટ્રેલિયા જવાનું નક્કી કર્યું. ખબર નથી શું થશે.
奥さんを病気で亡くした彼に、どう声をかけたらいいものやら。Okusan o byouki de nakushita kare ni, dou koe o kaketara ii mono yara. I don't know what to say to him. He lost his wife to an illness. बीमारी से पत्नी खो देने वाले उससे क्या कहें, समझ नहीं आता। બીમારીથી પત્ની ગુમાવનાર તેને શું કહેવું, સમજાતું નથી.

All three variants (のやら／ものやら／ことやら) are interchangeable in meaning — pick whichever fits the preceding grammatical shape smoothly. This differs from Point 3 in having only one option, not two contrasting ones — it's pure open-ended wondering, not indecision between two named alternatives.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

仕事がないので毎日友達と遊んでいる。（　）。Shigoto ga nai node mainichi tomodachi to asonde iru. (___).

Full sentence: 仕事がないので毎日友達と遊んでいる。暇なのやら忙しいのやら。Shigoto ga nai node mainichi tomodachi to asonde iru. Hima na no yara isogashii no yara.
EN: I have no job so I hang out with friends every day. I honestly can't tell if I'm idle or busy.
HI: काम नहीं है तो हर दिन दोस्तों के साथ घूमता/घूमती हूँ। पता नहीं फ़ुर्सत में हूँ या व्यस्त।
GU: કામ નથી તો રોજ મિત્રો સાથે ફરું છું. ખબર નથી ફ્રી છું કે વ્યસ્ત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a暇なのやら忙しいのやらhima na no yara isogashii no yaracan't tell if idle or busyपता नहीं फ़ुर्सत में हूँ या व्यस्तખબર નથી ફ્રી છું કે વ્યસ્ત
b暇とも忙しいともhima to mo isogashii to mo(grammatically close, but のやら's wondering tone fits the self-deprecating joke better here)(यहाँ कम उपयुक्त)(અહીં ઓછું યોગ્ય)

Why: The speaker is being wryly self-deprecating about a day filled with "nothing to do" that nonetheless feels busy with friends — Point 3's のやら captures that half-joking, torn-between-two-readings tone that plain とも doesn't quite carry.

Q2

ペットは（　）大変だけれど、ペットのいない生活は考えられない。Petto wa (___) taihen dakeredo, petto no inai seikatsu wa kangaerarenai.

Full sentence: ペットはいたらいたで大変だけれど、ペットのいない生活は考えられない。Petto wa itara ita de taihen dakeredo, petto no inai seikatsu wa kangaerarenai.
EN: Pets come with their own hassles, but I can't imagine life without one.
HI: पालतू जानवर हो तो अपनी परेशानी है, फिर भी उनके बिना ज़िंदगी की कल्पना नहीं कर सकता/सकती।
GU: પાલતુ પ્રાણી હોય તો પોતાની તકલીફ છે, છતાં તેના વગર જીવનની કલ્પના કરી શકતો/શકતી નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいるともいないとも言えなくてiru to mo inai to mo ienakutecan't say whether there is one or not (wrong — doesn't fit; pets clearly exist here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bいたらいたでitara ita deif you have one, that comes with its own issueहो तो अपनी परेशानी हैહોય તો પોતાની તકલીફ છે

Why: Having a pet brings its own (minor) hassle, yet is still worthwhile — exactly Point 2's たらたで; とも is for judgment between two possibilities, which doesn't fit since having a pet is a given fact here.

Q3

会社が倒産しそうだ。（　）ことやら……。Kaisha ga tousan shisou da. (___) koto yara...

Full sentence: 会社が倒産しそうだ。どうなることやら……。Kaisha ga tousan shisou da. Dou naru koto yara...
EN: The company looks like it might go bankrupt. I have no idea what will happen...
HI: कंपनी दिवालिया होने वाली लग रही है। पता नहीं क्या होगा...
GU: કંપની ડૂબવાની અણી પર લાગે છે. ખબર નથી શું થશે...
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a何するnani suru(wrong question word for this context)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

彼は（　）言えるが、とにかく口数が少ない。Kare wa (___) ieru ga, tonikaku kuchikazu ga sukunai.

Full sentence: 彼はおとなしいとも消極的だとも言えるが、とにかく口数が少ない。Kare wa otonashii to mo shoukyokuteki da to mo ieru ga, tonikaku kuchikazu ga sukunai.
EN: You could call him quiet, or you could call him passive, but in any case, he doesn't talk much.
HI: उसे शांत भी कह सकते हैं या निष्क्रिय भी, लेकिन बहरहाल वह कम बोलता है।
GU: તેને શાંત પણ કહી શકાય કે નિષ્ક્રિય પણ, પણ ગમે તે કહો, તે ઓછું બોલે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおとなしいとも消極的だともotonashii to mo shoukyokuteki da to mocould call him quiet or passiveशांत भी कह सकते या निष्क्रिय भीશાંત પણ કહી શકાય કે નિષ્ક્રિય પણ

EN: If you don't want to, just say so clearly.
HI: अगर नापसंद है, तो साफ़-साफ़ कह दो।
GU: જો ના ગમે, તો સ્પષ્ટ કહી દો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q6

「ピザ屋のチラシ、どこにある？」「＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。」"Piza-ya no chirashi, doko ni aru?" "___ ."

4 捨てたら→1 捨てた→2 でいいの→3 だけど

「ピザ屋のチラシ、どこにある？」「捨てたら捨てたでいいのだけど……。」"Piza-ya no chirashi, doko ni aru?" "Sutetara suteta de ii no dakedo..."

EN: "Where's the pizza place flyer?" "If it got thrown out, that's fine too, but... (I'm not sure)."
HI: "पिज़्ज़ा वाले का पर्चा कहाँ है?" "फेंक दिया हो तो ठीक है, पर... (पता नहीं)।"

Q7

この仕事は、いつ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ もつかない。Kono shigoto wa, itsu ___ mo tsukanai.

Tiles: 1 終わる (owaru)　2 になったら (ni nattara)　3 見当 (kentou)　4 ことやら (koto yara)

2 になったら→1 終わる→4 ことやら→3 見当

EN: I have no idea at all when this work will ever end.
HI: यह काम कब ख़त्म होगा, इसका कोई अंदाज़ा ही नहीं है।
GU: આ કામ ક્યારે પૂરું થશે, તેનો કોઈ અંદાજ જ નથી.

Why: Built on Point 4's いつ＋ことやら — open-ended wondering about when the work will end, reinforced by 見当もつかない ("no idea at all").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns express some flavor of uncertainty, but they differ in exactly what's uncertain:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とも〜とも~to mo ~to mo Cannot say ~ or ~ V／A／na／N（plain） + とも A judgment can't be made between two possibilities (not necessarily opposites). "Can't tell if it's real or fake."
〜たら〜たで~tara ~ta de If ~, that has its own issue too Vたら／Aかったら／na・Nなら + Vた／Aかった／na・Nで Both a situation and its opposite are accepted, each with a minor trade-off. "Having a garden is nice, but weeding is a hassle."
〜のやら〜のやら~no yara ~no yara Whether to ~ or ~ — can't tell V／A（plain）／naな + のやら (a and b must be opposites) Torn between two opposite emotional reactions to the same event. "Don't know whether to be happy or sad about it."
疑問詞＋のやら／ものやら／ことやらquestion word + yara I wonder ~ (no idea) [疑問詞] + V／A（plain） + のやら／ものやら; na + ことやら Open-ended wondering about one unknown, not choosing between two named options. "I wonder how long this will continue."
Exam trap: とも (Point 1) and のやら (Point 3) both pattern as "Xとも/のやらYとも/のやら," but のやら specifically requires X and Y to be an opposite pair (real vs. fake isn't quite the same shape as happy vs. sad) — if the two options aren't true opposites, default to とも. If there's only one blank with a question word rather than two paired options, it's always Point 4.
