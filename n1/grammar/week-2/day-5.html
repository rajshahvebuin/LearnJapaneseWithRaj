5日目 — 行きつ戻りつ (Ikitsu modoritsu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.38–39 (PDF pages 39–40)
Grammar points covered
〜ては（条件）〜ては（繰り返し）〜ては〜て、〜ては〜て〜つ〜つ
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.41 (6日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This day is really one long theme — repetition — approached from three angles: a single ては repeating a cause→effect cycle (Points 1–2), a two-step ては〜て cycle repeated wholesale (Point 3), and two opposite actions alternating back and forth with つ〜つ (Point 4).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ては (conditional)

neutral register
Reading~te wa
Meaning (EN)"if ~ (then a problem arises)" — a conditional that highlights an undesirable consequence
Meaning (HI)अगर ऐसा हो, तो (कोई समस्या पैदा होती है)
Meaning (GU)જો એવું થાય, તો (કોઈ સમસ્યા સર્જાય છે)
Connection (接続)Vて + は; Aく + ては; N／na + では
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSetting up a hypothetical or ongoing condition that leads to something negative or limiting.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
こんなに雪が降っては、どこにも出かけられない。Konna ni yuki ga futte wa, doko ni mo dekakerarenai. I can't go out when it snows this much. इतनी बर्फ़ पड़े तो कहीं भी नहीं जाया जा सकता। આટલો બરફ પડે તો ક્યાંય જઈ ન શકાય.
今日は弟の誕生日だが、肝心の本人が病気ではパーティーは延期するしかない。Kyou wa otouto no tanjoubi da ga, kanjin no honnin ga byouki de wa paatii wa enki suru shika nai. Today's my younger brother's birthday, but since he is ill we have no other choice but to postpone the party. आज छोटे भाई का जन्मदिन है, लेकिन ख़ुद वही बीमार है तो पार्टी टालनी ही पड़ेगी। આજે નાના ભાઈનો જન્મદિવસ છે, પણ પોતે જ બીમાર છે તો પાર્ટી મુલતવી રાખવી જ પડશે.

This ては is close to もし〜たら but with a built-in sense of "and that's a problem" — the sentence almost always continues with a negative-leaning consequence (できない, しかない, etc.).

【2】 〜ては (repeated cause → effect)

neutral register
Reading~te wa
Meaning (EN)"every time one does ~, ~ happens" — describes a recurring cycle where V1 always triggers V2
Meaning (HI)जब भी...करता/करती है, तब...हो जाता है (बार-बार)
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે પણ...કરે છે, ત્યારે...થાય છે (વારંવાર)
Connection (接続)V1てはV2　◆V1→V2の流れが何度も繰り返されることを表す
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing a habitual reaction — one action reliably, repeatedly, triggers another.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女はニキビに悩んでいて、鏡を見てはため息をついている。Kanojo wa nikibi ni nayande ite, kagami o mite wa tameiki o tsuite iru. She is worried about her pimples and sighs every time she looks at herself in the mirror. वह मुँहासों से परेशान है और जब भी शीशा देखती है, तब आह भरती है। તે પિમ્પલ્સથી પરેશાન છે અને જ્યારે પણ અરીસો જુએ છે, ત્યારે નિસાસો નાખે છે.
子どものころ、弟とけんかしては、母にしかられたものだ。Kodomo no koro, otouto to kenka shite wa, haha ni shikarareta mono da. When I was a kid, I used to be scolded by my mother whenever I had a fight with my younger brother. बचपन में, जब भी छोटे भाई से लड़ाई होती, तब माँ से डाँट पड़ती थी। બાળપણમાં, જ્યારે પણ નાના ભાઈ સાથે ઝઘડો થતો, ત્યારે માથી ધમકાવવામાં આવતું.

Same surface form as Point 1, but the meaning shifts from a single hypothetical condition to an established habitual cycle — context (もの, 〜たものだ, repeated past-tense description) is what signals which reading applies.

【3】 〜ては〜て、〜ては〜て

neutral register
Reading~te wa ~te, ~te wa ~te
Meaning (EN)"just ~ and ~, over and over" — repeats a two-step cycle of actions again and again, often implying a bad habit or monotonous routine
Meaning (HI)बस...और...करते रहना, बार-बार (एक ही चक्र दोहराना)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત...અને...કરતાં રહેવું, વારંવાર (એ જ ચક્ર પુનરાવર્તન)
Connection (接続)V1てはV2て、(V1てはV2て); also V1てはV2まま、(V1てはV2まま)
RegisterNeutral, often mildly self-deprecating or critical
Typical useDescribing a monotonous or unproductive repeated routine.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
食べては寝て、食べては寝てという生活を続けていたら、この半年で10キロも太ってしまった。Tabete wa nete, tabete wa nete to iu seikatsu o tsuzukete itara, kono hantoshi de jukkiro mo futotte shimatta. I just ate and slept for half a year and gained 10 kg. खाओ और सोओ, खाओ और सोओ — यही ज़िंदगी छह महीने चली, तो 10 किलो बढ़ गया। ખાવું અને સૂવું, ખાવું અને સૂવું — આ જ જીવન છ મહિના ચાલ્યું, તો 10 કિલો વધી ગયું.
久しぶりに山登りをした。歩いては休み、していたので、頂上までたどりつくのにずいぶん長い時間かかった。Hisashiburi ni yamanobori o shita. Aruite wa yasumi, shite ita node, choujou made tadoritsuku no ni zuibun nagai jikan kakatta. I went to climb a mountain for the first time in a long while. It took me a long time to reach the summit as I stopped and rested frequently. बहुत दिनों बाद पहाड़ पर चढ़ा/चढ़ी। चलते और रुकते-रुकते, चोटी तक पहुँचने में बहुत समय लग गया। ઘણા સમય પછી પર્વત ચઢ્યો/ચઢી. ચાલતાં અને અટકતાં અટકતાં, શિખર સુધી પહોંચવામાં ઘણો સમય લાગ્યો.

The repetition of the whole てはVて chunk (食べては寝て、食べては寝て — said twice) is what makes this feel monotonous/tedious in a way a single 食べては寝て wouldn't.

【4】 〜つ〜つ

neutral–literary register
Reading~tsu ~tsu
Meaning (EN)"alternating between ~ and ~" — describes two opposite actions happening back and forth repeatedly
Meaning (HI)कभी...कभी... (बार-बार आगे-पीछे होना, विरोधी क्रियाएँ बदलती रहना)
Meaning (GU)ક્યારેક...ક્યારેક... (વારંવાર આગળ-પાછળ થવું, વિરોધી ક્રિયાઓ બદલાતી રહેવી)
Connection (接続)V1（ますstem）つV2（ますstem）つ　◆V1とV2は反対の意味
RegisterNeutral, somewhat literary
Typical useDescribing physical back-and-forth motion or a close, seesawing competition.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は花束を持って、彼女の家の前を行きつ戻りつした。Kare wa hanataba o motte, kanojo no ie no mae o ikitsu modoritsu shita. He walked back and forth in front of her house carrying a bouquet. वह फूलों का गुलदस्ता लेकर उसके घर के सामने आगे-पीछे टहलता रहा। તે ફૂલોનો બુકે લઈને તેના ઘર આગળ આગળ-પાછળ ફરતો રહ્યો.
昨日のマラソンでは、2人の選手が最後まで抜きつ抜かれつトップ争いをしていた。Kinou no marason de wa, futari no senshu ga saigo made nukitsu nukaretsu toppu arasoi o shite ita. Two runners were competing for the first position throughout yesterday's marathon. कल की मैराथन में, दो धावक आख़िर तक बारी-बारी आगे-पीछे होते हुए पहले स्थान की दौड़ में थे। ગઈકાલની મેરેથોનમાં, બે દોડવીરો છેલ્લે સુધી વારાફરતી આગળ-પાછળ થતાં પ્રથમ સ્થાન માટે હરીફાઈ કરતા હતા.

V1 and V2 must be a genuine opposite pair (行く／戻る, 抜く／抜かれる) — you cannot pick two unrelated verbs. The ます-stem (行き, 戻り, 抜き, 抜かれ) is what attaches directly to つ, not the plain or te-form.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

今朝から雪が（　）を繰り返している。Kesa kara yuki ga (___) o kurikaeshite iru.

Full sentence: 今朝から雪が降ってはやみ、降ってはやみを繰り返している。Kesa kara yuki ga futte wa yami, futte wa yami o kurikaeshite iru.
EN: Since this morning, the snow has repeatedly started and stopped, over and over.
HI: आज सुबह से बर्फ़ बार-बार गिरती है और रुक जाती है, यही चक्र चल रहा है।
GU: આજે સવારથી બરફ વારંવાર પડે છે અને અટકે છે, આ જ ચક્ર ચાલુ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a降るならやみ、降るならやみfuru nara yami, furu nara yami(not a valid pattern here)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
b降ってはやみ、降ってはやみfutte wa yami, futte wa yamistarts and stops, over and overगिरती है और रुकती है, बार-बारપડે છે અને અટકે છે, વારંવાર

Why: A repeated cycle (snow starting, then stopping, again and again) is exactly Point 3's てはVて repeated twice; ならやみ isn't a recognized grammatical combination.

Q2

彼と私は、学生時代、（　）成績を争ったものだ。Kare to watashi wa, gakusei jidai, (___) seiseki o arasotta mono da.

Full sentence: 彼と私は、学生時代、抜きつ抜かれつ成績を争ったものだ。Kare to watashi wa, gakusei jidai, nukitsu nukaretsu seiseki o arasotta mono da.
EN: Back in our school days, he and I used to compete for grades, trading the lead back and forth.
HI: स्कूल के दिनों में, वह और मैं बारी-बारी आगे-पीछे होते हुए अंकों में मुक़ाबला करते थे।
GU: શાળાના દિવસોમાં, તે અને હું વારાફરતી આગળ-પાછળ થતાં માર્ક્સમાં હરીફાઈ કરતા હતા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a抜こうが抜かれまいがnukou ga nukaremai gawhether overtaking or not (wrong — no such indifference here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b抜きつ抜かれつnukitsu nukaretsualternately overtaking and being overtakenबारी-बारी आगे-पीछे होते हुएવારાફરતી આગળ-પાછળ થતાં

Why: Competing neck-and-neck for grades is Point 4's つ〜つ back-and-forth pattern; the volitional-concessive がまいが (Week 2 Day 3's pattern) would describe indifference to an outcome, not an actual seesawing competition.

Q3

若いころは、お酒を（　）踊ったものだ。Wakai koro wa, osake o (___) odotta mono da.

Full sentence: 若いころは、お酒を飲んでは踊ったものだ。Wakai koro wa, osake o nonde wa odotta mono da.
EN: Back when I was young, I used to drink and dance, over and over.
HI: जवानी के दिनों में, शराब पीकर बार-बार नाचता/नाचती था/थी।
GU: યુવાનીના દિવસોમાં, દારૂ પીને વારંવાર નાચતો/નાચતી હતો/હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a飲んではnonde waevery time one drankपीकर हर बारપીને દર વખતે
b飲もうとしてnomou to shitetrying to drink (wrong — describes an attempt, not a habitual repeated act)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

学んだことを（　）を繰り返している。Mananda koto o (___) o kurikaeshite iru.

Full sentence: 学んだことを覚えては忘れ、覚えては忘れを繰り返している。Mananda koto o oboete wa wasure, oboete wa wasure o kurikaeshite iru.

HI: जो सीखा उसे याद रखता हूँ फिर भूल जाता हूँ, यही चक्र चल रहा है।
GU: જે શીખ્યું તે યાદ રાખું છું ને પછી ભૂલી જાઉં છું, આ જ ચક્ર ચાલુ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a覚えるし忘れ、覚えるし忘れoboeru shi wasure, oboeru shi wasure(not a valid combination)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

遅刻したと言っても、電車の事故（　）仕方がない。Chikoku shita to itte mo, densha no jiko (___) shikata ga nai.

Full sentence: 遅刻したと言っても、電車の事故では仕方がない。Chikoku shita to itte mo, densha no jiko de wa shikata ga nai.
EN: Even though you were late, if it was because of a train accident, it can't be helped.
HI: भले ही देर हो गई, लेकिन ट्रेन दुर्घटना की वजह से हो तो कोई चारा नहीं।
GU: ભલે મોડું થયું, પણ ટ્રેન અકસ્માતના કારણે હોય તો કોઈ ઈલાજ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aではde waif it's the case that (conditional)अगर हो तोજો હોય તો
bならではnara de wa(wrong — this is an unrelated fixed phrase meaning "uniquely characteristic of")(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

この作文、ひどいね。間違いが＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 直してもよくならないよ。Kono sakubun, hidoi ne. Machigai ga ___ naoshite mo yoku naranai yo.

Tiles: 1 多く (ooku)　2 少しぐらい (sukoshi gurai)　3 ては (te wa)　4 こんなに (konna ni)

4 こんなに→1 多く→3 ては→2 少しぐらい

この作文、ひどいね。間違いがこんなに多くては少しぐらい直してもよくならないよ。Kono sakubun, hidoi ne. Machigai ga konna ni ooku te wa sukoshi gurai naoshite mo yoku naranai yo.

EN: This essay is terrible. With this many mistakes, fixing just a little won't make it better.
HI: यह निबंध बहुत ख़राब है। इतनी सारी ग़लतियाँ हैं तो थोड़ा सुधारने से कुछ नहीं होगा।
GU: આ નિબંધ ખૂબ ખરાબ છે. આટલી બધી ભૂલો હોય તો થોડું સુધારવાથી કંઈ નહીં થાય.

Why: Built on Point 1's conditional ては — having this many mistakes is the problematic condition that makes a small fix insufficient.

Q7

バーゲン会場は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 息苦しくなるほどだった。Baagen kaijou wa, ___ ikigurushiku naru hodo datta.

Tiles: 1 押しつ押されつ (oshitsu osaretsu)　2 で (de)　3 の (no)　4 大混雑 (daikonzatsu)

1 押しつ押されつ→3 の→4 大混雑→2 で

バーゲン会場は、押しつ押されつの大混雑で息苦しくなるほどだった。Baagen kaijou wa, oshitsu osaretsu no daikonzatsu de ikigurushiku naru hodo datta.

EN: The sale venue was so crowded with people pushing back and forth that it was almost suffocating.
HI: सेल वाली जगह पर इतनी धक्का-मुक्की और भीड़ थी कि साँस लेना मुश्किल हो रहा था।
GU: સેલની જગ્યાએ એટલી ધક્કામુક્કી અને ભીડ હતી કે શ્વાસ લેવો મુશ્કેલ થઈ ગયો હતો.

Why: Built on Point 4's つ〜つ — 押しつ押されつ (pushing and being pushed, back and forth) describes the crowd's constant jostling.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 1 and 2 share the exact same surface form ては — only context tells you which is meant. Compare all four:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ては (conditional)~te wa If ~ (problem arises) Vて／Aく／N・naで + は A single hypothetical/ongoing condition with a negative consequence. "If it snows this much, I can't go out."
〜ては (repeated)~te wa Every time ~, ~ happens V1てはV2 A habitual, repeated cause→effect cycle, not a single hypothetical. "Every time she looks in the mirror, she sighs."
〜ては〜て、〜ては〜て~te wa ~te... Just ~ and ~, over and over V1てはV2て (repeated twice) A whole two-step cycle repeated wholesale, often implying monotony. "Ate and slept, ate and slept, for months."
〜つ〜つ~tsu ~tsu Alternating between ~ and ~ V1（ますstem）つV2（ますstem）つ Two opposite actions alternating back and forth (not cause→effect). "Walked back and forth," "traded the lead back and forth."
Exam trap: Point 1 and Point 2 are identical in form, so ask: does the sentence describe one hypothetical situation (→ Point 1, conditional) or an established repeated habit, often flagged with 〜たものだ (→ Point 2)? Separately, don't confuse つ〜つ with ては〜て — つ〜つ needs two genuinely opposite verbs (行く／戻る), while ては〜て just needs any two sequential actions, opposite or not (食べる／寝る aren't opposites, just sequential).
