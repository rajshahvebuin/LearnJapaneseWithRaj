1日目 — 私なりに (Watashi nari ni)

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.33 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
The single kanji なり does two unrelated jobs this week — listing loose alternatives (Points 1–2) and marking "the instant something happens" (Point 3). Don't let the shared word fool you into merging them; see §3 for the full contrast.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜なり〜なり

neutral register
Reading~nari ~nari
Meaning (EN)"~ or ~ (or whatever), do either" — loosely lists a couple of options as acceptable, implying other unlisted options would work too
Meaning (HI)...या...(या जो भी उपयुक्त हो), दोनों में से कोई भी
Meaning (GU)...અથવા...(અથવા જે યોગ્ય લાગે), બંનેમાંથી કોઈ પણ
Connection (接続)N1なりN2なり／V1るなり（V2るなり）する　❶ なんなりと(=なんでも), 〜なりなんなり
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSuggesting a couple of concrete options while signaling the listener has freedom to pick anything similar.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN1なりN2なり電話なりメールなりdenwa nari meeru nari
VerbV1るなり（V2るなり）するあげるなり捨てるなりするageru nari suteru nari suru
い-adjective(added, not in book — this pattern lists actions/things, not descriptive states)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電話なりメールなりで、彼に連絡してください。Denwa nari meeru nari de, kare ni renraku shite kudasai. Please get in touch with him by phone or e-mail (or whatever). फ़ोन करें या ईमेल करें, उससे संपर्क कर लें। ફોન કરો કે ઈમેલ કરો, તેનો સંપર્ક કરી લો.
もう使わないなら、人にあげるなり捨てるなりしたほうがいいと思う。Mou tsukawanai nara, hito ni ageru nari suteru nari shita hou ga ii to omou. I think it's better to give it away or throw it out (or do whatever) if you are no longer using it. अगर अब इस्तेमाल नहीं करते, तो किसी को दे दो या फेंक दो, यही बेहतर होगा। જો હવે વાપરવાનું નથી, તો કોઈને આપી દો કે ફેંકી દો, એ જ સારું રહેશે.
会社が倒産した。家のローンもあるし、朝も夜もバイトするなり（なんなりして）、なんとかこの危機を乗り越えなくてはいけない。Kaisha ga tousan shita. Ie no roon mo aru shi, asa mo yoru mo baito suru nari (nan nari shite), nantoka kono kiki o norikoenakute wa ikenai. My company has gone bankrupt. Since I have a mortgage, I'll have to find a full-time job or a bunch of part-time jobs (or whatever) and work night and day. कंपनी दिवालिया हो गई। घर की किस्त भी है, इसलिए सुबह-शाम पार्ट-टाइम काम करके या जो भी हो, इस मुश्किल से निकलना ही होगा। કંપની ડૂબી ગઈ. ઘરની લોન પણ છે, એટલે સવાર-સાંજ પાર્ટ-ટાઇમ કામ કરીને કે જે પણ થાય, આ મુશ્કેલીમાંથી બહાર આવવું જ પડશે.

なんなり (lit. "whatever kind") often tags along after the listed options as a catch-all — 電話なりなんなり means "phone or whatever [similar method]." This softens the list into "these or anything like them."

【2】 〜なら〜なりに

neutral register
Reading~nara ~nari ni
Meaning (EN)"to the extent one can, in one's own way/capacity" — accepts a limiting condition and describes acting appropriately within it
Meaning (HI)अपनी सामर्थ्य/स्थिति के मुताबिक, जितना हो सके उतना
Meaning (GU)પોતાની ક્ષમતા/સ્થિતિ મુજબ, જેટલું બને એટલું
Connection (接続)（Vるなら）Vる／（Aいなら）Aい／（naなら）na／（Nなら）N + なりに／なりのN
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing effort or a result scaled appropriately to one's own means, without overreaching.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
できないならできないなりに、無理をしないで少しずつやりましょう。Dekinai nara dekinai nari ni, muri o shinaide sukoshizutsu yarimashou. If you can't do it well at this pace, don't rush and do it at your own pace. अगर नहीं हो पा रहा, तो जितना हो सके उतना, बिना दबाव के, थोड़ा-थोड़ा करें। જો ન થાય તો, જેટલું બને એટલું, દબાણ વગર, ધીમે ધીમે કરો.
貧しいなら貧しいなりの生活をしなければならない。Mazushii nara mazushii nari no seikatsu o shinakereba naranai. If you are poor, you must live within your means. अगर ग़रीब हो, तो अपनी हैसियत के मुताबिक जीना चाहिए। જો ગરીબ હો, તો પોતાની ક્ષમતા મુજબ જીવવું જોઈએ.
結婚式に出席するなら、それなりの服装をするべきだ。Kekkonshiki ni shusseki suru nara, sore nari no fukusou o suru beki da. If you attend the wedding, you should dress up for the occasion. अगर शादी में जाना है, तो उसके मुताबिक कपड़े पहनने चाहिए। જો લગ્નમાં જવાનું છે, તો તેને અનુરૂપ કપડાં પહેરવાં જોઈએ.
あなたが会社を辞めた理由は私なりに理解しています。Anata ga kaisha o yameta riyuu wa watashi nari ni rikai shite imasu. I think I understand the reason why you quit the job. तुमने नौकरी क्यों छोड़ी, इसे मैं अपने तरीके से समझता/समझती हूँ। તમે નોકરી કેમ છોડી, તે હું મારી રીતે સમજું છું.

それなりの (as in Example 3) is a fixed, extremely common combination meaning "appropriate/fitting" — a good one to memorize as its own chunk. Note that なりに attaches to a verb/adjective/noun clause, while なりの sits directly before a following noun.

【3】 〜なり (as soon as ~)

neutral register
Reading~nari
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~ / the moment ~" — a one-off event immediately followed by another, often surprising, action
Meaning (HI)...करते ही, ठीक उसी पल
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં જ, બરાબર તે જ પળે
Connection (接続)Vる + なり (plain non-past form only)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes how immediately the second action followed the first, often something unexpected.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中さんは私に会うなり泣き出した。Tanaka-san wa watashi ni au nari nakidashita. Tanaka-san started crying as soon as she saw me. तनाका-सान मुझे देखते ही रोने लगी। તનાકા-સાન મને જોતાં જ રડવા લાગી.
部長は電話を切るなり、部屋を出て行った。Buchou wa denwa o kiru nari, heya o dete itta. The manager left the room as soon as he hung up the phone. मैनेजर ने फ़ोन रखते ही कमरे से बाहर चले गए। મેનેજરે ફોન મૂકતાં જ રૂમમાંથી બહાર જતા રહ્યા.

This なり always attaches to the plain non-past verb form (Vる), never the past — it describes the first action as the trigger point, with the second following essentially instantaneously. Semantically it's close to 〜たとたんに／〜と同時に, both of which the book uses to gloss it.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼ったら、家に（　）なり、パソコンの前に座るんだから……。Kare ttara, ie ni (___) nari, pasokon no mae ni suwaru n dakara...

Full sentence: 彼ったら、家に帰るなり、パソコンの前に座るんだから……。Kare ttara, ie ni kaeru nari, pasokon no mae ni suwaru n dakara...
EN: Honestly, that guy — the moment he gets home, he sits down in front of the computer...
HI: उसकी तो बात ही मत करो — घर पहुँचते ही कंप्यूटर के आगे बैठ जाता है...
GU: તેની તો વાત જ ના કરો — ઘરે પહોંચતાં જ કમ્પ્યુટર આગળ બેસી જાય છે...
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a帰ったkaettapast tense (wrong form before なり)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b帰るkaeruplain non-past (correct form)सही रूपસાચું સ્વરૂપ

Why: Point 3's なり ("as soon as") always takes the plain non-past verb form, never the past — 帰るなり, not 帰ったなり.

Q2

私は素人だが、この詩を自分（　）解釈してみた。Watashi wa shirouto da ga, kono shi o jibun (___) kaishaku shite mita.

Full sentence: 私は素人だが、この詩を自分なりに解釈してみた。Watashi wa shirouto da ga, kono shi o jibun nari ni kaishaku shite mita.
EN: I'm an amateur, but I tried interpreting this poem in my own way.
HI: मैं एक शौकिया हूँ, लेकिन मैंने इस कविता को अपने तरीके से समझने की कोशिश की।
GU: હું શોખીન છું, પણ મેં આ કવિતાને મારી રીતે સમજવાનો પ્રયત્ન કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなりにnari niin one's own way/capacityअपने तरीके सेપોતાની રીતે
bなりしてnari shite(not a valid combination)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 自分なりに ("in my own way," scaled to one's own ability) is Point 2's fixed pattern; なりして isn't a recognized form.

顔色が悪いですよ。（　）、楽にしてください。Kaoiro ga warui desu yo. (___), raku ni shite kudasai.

GU: તમારો રંગ ઊડી ગયો છે. બેસી જાઓ કે સૂઈ જાઓ, આરામ કરી લો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a座るなり横になるなりsuwaru nari yoko ni naru narisit down or lie down (or whatever)बैठ जाएँ या लेट जाएँબેસી જાઓ કે સૂઈ જાઓ

Full sentence: 窓を開けたとたん、変な虫が入ってきた。Mado o aketa totan, hen na mushi ga haitte kita.
EN: The moment I opened the window, a strange bug came in.
HI: खिड़की खोलते ही, एक अजीब कीड़ा भीतर आ गया।
GU: બારી ખોલતાં જ, એક અજીબ જીવડું અંદર આવી ગયું.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

高得点を＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ の準備が必要だ。Koutokuten o ___ no junbi ga hitsuyou da.

Tiles: 1 狙う (nerau)　2 なり (nari)　3 それ (sore)　4 なら (nara)

1 狙う→4 なら→3 それ→2 なり

高得点を狙うならそれなりの準備が必要だ。Koutokuten o nerau nara sore nari no junbi ga hitsuyou da.

EN: If you're aiming for a high score, you need appropriate preparation for it.
HI: अगर ऊँचे अंक चाहिए, तो उसके मुताबिक तैयारी भी ज़रूरी है।
GU: જો ઊંચા માર્ક્સ જોઈએ છે, તો તેને અનુરૂપ તૈયારી પણ જરૂરી છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's それなりの — the preparation must be scaled appropriately to the ambitious goal.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ならお申し付けください。___ nara omoushitsuke kudasai.

Tiles: 1 なんなり (nan nari)　2 雑用 (zatsuyou)　3 必要 (hitsuyou)　4 なり (nari)

2 雑用→4 なり→1 なんなり→3 必要

雑用なりなんなり必要ならお申し付けください。Zatsuyou nari nan nari hitsuyou nara omoushitsuke kudasai.

EN: If you need any chores done, or anything at all, please just let me know.
HI: कोई छोटा-मोटा काम या कुछ भी ज़रूरत हो, तो बताइए।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All three patterns share the word なり, but it means three unrelated things — this is the day's built-in trap (see Q1/Q4 above).

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜なり〜なり~nari ~nari ~ or ~ (or whatever) N1なりN2なり／V1るなりV2るなりする Lists loose alternatives, implying more unlisted options exist too. Suggesting a couple of acceptable options.
〜なら〜なりに~nara ~nari ni To the extent one can, in one's own way （Vるなら）Vる／（Aいなら）Aい／（naなら）na／（Nなら）N + なりに Accepts a condition/limitation and scales the action to fit it. "Do what you can within your means/situation."
〜なり~nari (as soon as) The instant ~ Vる（plain non-past only） + なり Marks a single trigger moment immediately followed by another action. "As soon as X happened, Y followed."
Exam trap: Point 1's listing なりなり and Point 3's "as soon as" なり look identical but never appear with the same structure — the listing version always needs a second なり (or なんなり) later in the sentence, while the "as soon as" version is a single なり attached directly to one plain non-past verb, immediately followed by a different clause. If there's only one なり and the sentence describes a surprising quick reaction, it's Point 3.
