Notes
Today introduces まい, the classical negative-volitional ("will probably not / let's not") that N1 revives for concessive patterns. Points 2 and 3 are tagged 硬 formal/written and share tricky irregular negative-volitional forms — see the formation table under Point 2.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜というか〜というか

neutral register
Reading~to iu ka ~to iu ka
Meaning (EN)"you could call it ~, or ~ — hard to say exactly" — hedges between two descriptions when neither alone fully captures a mixed feeling or judgment
Meaning (HI)चाहे...कहें या...कहें, ठीक-ठीक कहना मुश्किल है (मिली-जुली भावना)
Meaning (GU)ભલે...કહો કે...કહો, ચોક્કસ કહેવું અઘરું છે (મિશ્ર લાગણી)
Connection (接続)[aというかbというか] Aい／na／N + というか
RegisterNeutral
Typical useVoicing mixed or hard-to-pin-down feelings/judgments about something.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は合格したが、親友は不合格だった。うれしいというか、残念というか、複雑な気持ちだ。Watashi wa goukaku shita ga, shinyuu wa fugoukaku datta. Ureshii to iu ka, zannen to iu ka, fukuzatsu na kimochi da. I passed the entrance exam but my best friend did not. I'm not sure if I am happy or not — I have mixed feelings. मैं पास हो गया/गई, लेकिन मेरा सबसे अच्छा दोस्त फ़ेल हो गया। ख़ुशी कहूँ या अफ़सोस, मन उलझा हुआ है। હું પાસ થયો/થઈ, પણ મારો સૌથી સારો મિત્ર નાપાસ થયો. ખુશી કહું કે અફસોસ, મન મૂંઝવણમાં છે.
そんなことをするとは、無茶というか、無知というか、彼のすることは理解できない。Sonna koto o suru towa, mucha to iu ka, muchi to iu ka, kare no suru koto wa rikai dekinai. He did that? Is he reckless or just ignorant? I don't understand what he does. ऐसा करना, बेतुका कहें या नासमझी, उसकी हरकतें समझ से बाहर हैं। એવું કરવું, બેજવાબદાર કહો કે અણસમજ, તેની હરકતો સમજની બહાર છે.

This というか is purely about description/labeling — it never concedes an action or a hypothetical, unlike the other three patterns today. It signals "I could describe this as A, or maybe B — either way, it's complicated."

【2】 〜ようと〜まいと／〜うが〜まいが

硬 formal/written
Reading~you to ~mai to / ~u ga ~mai ga
Meaning (EN)"whether one does ~ or not — it makes no difference" — the "do or don't" version of concessive alternation
Meaning (HI)करें या न करें, कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता
Meaning (GU)કરો કે ન કરો, કંઈ ફરક પડતો નથી
Connection (接続)Vようと + Vるまいと／Vようが + Vるまいが
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useDeclaring an outcome unaffected by whether an action happens or not.
Verb groupまい formationExample
る-verbs (ru-V)Vる + まい／V（stem） + まい降りまい／降るまいfurimai / furumai
う-verbs (u-V)Vる + まい降るまいfurumai
くる (irregular)こまい／くるまいこまい／くるまいkomai / kurumai
する (irregular)しまい／すまい／するまいしまい／すまい／するまいshimai / sumai / surumai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
雨が降ろうと降るまいと試合は行われます。Ame ga furou to furumai to shiai wa okonawaremasu. There will be a game regardless of the weather. बारिश हो या न हो, मैच होकर रहेगा। વરસાદ પડે કે ન પડે, મેચ થશે જ.
私がいようがいまいが関係なく、彼らはけんかを始めた。Watashi ga iyou ga imai ga kankei naku, karera wa kenka o hajimeta. In spite of me being there, they started fighting. मैं वहाँ रहूँ या न रहूँ, इससे बेपरवाह होकर वे झगड़ने लगे। હું ત્યાં હોઉં કે ન હોઉં, તેની બેપરવાહ થઈને તેઓ ઝઘડવા લાગ્યા.

まい functions here as the negative-volitional counterpart to よう — pairing them (するまい, 降るまい) creates a fossilized "or not" that modern learners otherwise only see in classical/literary contexts. Memorize the irregular くる／する forms specifically, since they don't follow the regular u-verb rule.

【3】 〜ようか〜まいか

硬 formal/written
Reading~you ka ~mai ka
Meaning (EN)"whether to ~ or not — torn/undecided" — expresses genuine indecision between doing something and not doing it
Meaning (HI)करूँ या न करूँ — इसी उलझन में
Meaning (GU)કરું કે ન કરું — એ જ મૂંઝવણમાં
Connection (接続)Vようか + Vるまいか　(同じ V1／V2 の否定・肯定を対にする)
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useExplicitly describing indecision — almost always paired with 迷う／悩む ("to be torn/troubled") in the same sentence.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
台風のような雨だ。予定通り美術館に行こうか、行くまいか。Taifuu no you na ame da. Yotei doori bijutsukan ni ikou ka, ikumai ka. It's raining hard like in a typhoon. Should I go to the museum as planned or not? तूफ़ान जैसी बारिश हो रही है। तय समय पर संग्रहालय जाऊँ या न जाऊँ? વાવાઝોડા જેવો વરસાદ છે. નક્કી કર્યા મુજબ મ્યુઝિયમ જવું કે ન જવું?
彼と結婚しようかするまいか悩むくらいなら、やめたほうがいい。Kare to kekkon shiyou ka surumai ka nayamu kurai nara, yameta hou ga ii. If you're having a hard time deciding to marry him, you'd better forget about it. उससे शादी करूँ या न करूँ, इसी उलझन में रहने से तो बेहतर है कि यह इरादा छोड़ ही दिया जाए। તેની સાથે લગ્ન કરવા કે ન કરવા, એ મૂંઝવણમાં રહેવા કરતાં તો આ વિચાર છોડી દેવો સારો.

The V1 and V2 here are always the same verb (行こうか行くまいか, not a mix of two different verbs) — this pattern is specifically "do this one thing, or don't," never a choice between two different actions.

【4】 〜にせよ〜にせよ／〜にしろ〜にしろ

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni seyo ~ni seyo / ~ni shiro ~ni shiro
Meaning (EN)"whether ~ or ~, in any case" — concedes any of the listed cases lead to the same conclusion; also forms fixed phrases with question words meaning "in any case"
Meaning (HI)चाहे...हो या...हो, किसी भी सूरत में
Meaning (GU)ભલે...હોય કે...હોય, કોઈ પણ સ્થિતિમાં
Connection (接続)[aにせよ(bにせよ)] [aにしろ(bにしろ)] V／A／な／N（plain） + に + せよ／しろ　❶ na・Nだ→な／だ に
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useConceding two named cases (実は/にせよ) or, with a question word, declaring universal indifference: 何にせよ／だれにせよ／いつにせよ／どこにせよ (all "in any case, regardless").
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
来るにせよ来ないにせよ、必ず連絡を入れてください。Kuru ni seyo konai ni seyo, kanarazu renraku o irete kudasai. Please make sure you inform us as to whether you will come or not. आओ या न आओ, ज़रूर सूचित कर दें। આવો કે ન આવો, ચોક્કસ જણાવી દેજો.
熱が下がったにせよ、しばらく安静が必要です。Netsu ga sagatta ni seyo, shibaraku ansei ga hitsuyou desu. You'll need to rest quietly in bed even though your temperature has come down. बुख़ार उतर गया हो, फिर भी कुछ समय आराम ज़रूरी है। તાવ ઉતરી ગયો હોય, તો પણ થોડો સમય આરામ જરૂરી છે.
事故で車は前のほうがめちゃくちゃになったが、何にしろ、誰もけがをしなくてよかった。Jiko de kuruma wa mae no hou ga mechakucha ni natta ga, nani ni shiro, dare mo kega o shinakute yokatta. The front of my car got smashed up in the accident but, in any case, I was relieved that no one was hurt. हादसे में गाड़ी का अगला हिस्सा तबाह हो गया, लेकिन जो भी हो, किसी को चोट न आना अच्छी बात रही। અકસ્માતમાં ગાડીનો આગળનો ભાગ ખરાબ થઈ ગયો, પણ જે પણ હોય, કોઈને ઈજા ન થવી એ સારી વાત રહી.

せよ and しろ are interchangeable — both are archaic imperative forms of する fossilized into this concessive pattern. どちらか一方 (either one) works equally in formal writing.

EN: Whether you object or not, I intend to go alone.
HI: तुम विरोध करो या न करो, मैं तो अकेला/अकेली जाने का इरादा रखता/रखती हूँ।
GU: તું વિરોધ કરે કે ન કરે, હું તો એકલા જવાનો ઇરાદો રાખું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aしようとしまいとshiyou to shimai towhether one does or notकरो या न करोકરે કે ન કરે

EN: Since you're already a working adult, whatever you do, you must take responsibility for your own actions.
HI: अब तुम कामकाजी व्यस्क हो, तो जो भी करो, अपने कामों की ज़िम्मेदारी लेनी होगी।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 2 and 3 both pair a volitional with まい, and it's easy to mix them up — the difference is decision vs. indecision:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜というか〜というか~to iu ka You could call it ~, or ~ Aい／na／N + というか Hedges a description/label — no concession of an action at all. Mixed feelings or hard-to-pin-down judgments.
〜ようと〜まいと~you to ~mai to Whether ~ or not (either way) Vようと + Vるまいと The speaker has already decided; the action's occurrence doesn't affect the outcome. "I'll go whether it rains or not" — indifference to the condition.
〜ようか〜まいか~you ka ~mai ka Whether to ~ or not (torn) Vようか + Vるまいか The speaker (or subject) is genuinely undecided — usually paired with 迷う／悩む. "Should I go or not?" — real indecision.
〜にせよ〜にせよ~ni seyo Whether ~ or ~, in any case V／A／な／N（plain） + にせよ／にしろ Concedes two named cases, or forms fixed phrases with question words (何にせよ). Formal writing, broader than just an action's occurrence.
Exam trap: と vs か after the volitional/まい pair is the whole ballgame here (see Q1/Q2 above): と means "it doesn't matter, I've already decided" (indifference), while か means "I genuinely don't know which to choose" (indecision). Look for 悩んだ／迷った nearby — their presence signals か, their absence usually signals と.
