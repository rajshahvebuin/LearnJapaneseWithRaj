2日目 — 思い出すだに (Omoidasu dani)

Grammar points covered

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.115 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Points 3 (〜だに) and 4 (〜こととて) are both tagged 硬 formal/written in the source, while Points 1 (〜にして) and 2 (〜もしないで) are register-neutral. Watch the classical negative trap: ない becomes ぬ (慣れない → 慣れぬ) right before こととて — this is the day's key exam trap, see §3 below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜にして(初めて)

neutral register
Reading~ni shite (hajimete)
Meaning (EN)"only at/as N (an exceptional stage, age, or person) — for the first time" — emphasizes that reaching this particular stage or being this particular person was the necessary condition for something happening
Meaning (HI)...होकर ही (पहली बार) / सिर्फ़ ...की वजह से ही (मुमकिन हुआ)
Meaning (GU)...થઈને જ (પ્રથમ વાર) / ફક્ત ...ના કારણે જ (શક્ય બન્યું)
Connection (接続)N + にして(初めて)
RegisterNeutral, with an emphatic (◆強調) nuance in writing and speech
Typical useHighlights that a particular age, career stage, status, or person was the essential condition without which something could not have happened — often paired with 初めて for extra emphasis.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にして(初めて)40歳にして(初めて)yonjussai ni shite (hajimete)
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — にして attaches to a noun marking a stage, age, or qualification, not to a verb or descriptive adjective)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
40歳にして(初めて)車の免許を取った。Yonjussai ni shite (hajimete) kuruma no menkyo o totta. I got my first driver's license at the age of 40. 40 साल की उम्र में होकर ही मैंने पहली बार ड्राइविंग लाइसेंस लिया। 40 વર્ષની ઉંમરે થઈને જ મેં પહેલી વાર ડ્રાઇવિંગ લાઇસન્સ લીધું.
この改革は、あの政治家にして初めてできたことだ。Kono kaikaku wa, ano seijika ni shite hajimete dekita koto da. That politician is responsible for spearheading this reform (lit. "only that politician could have achieved this"). यह सुधार सिर्फ़ उस नेता की वजह से ही मुमकिन हो पाया। આ સુધારો ફક્ત તે નેતાના કારણે જ શક્ય બન્યો.

にして marks an exceptional condition or point — a specific age (40歳にして) or a specific, irreplaceable person (あの政治家にして) — as the one and only circumstance under which the result could occur. Don't confuse this "only at/because of this exceptional point" nuance with Point 3's だに, which marks a minimal trigger rather than an exceptional condition (see §3 below).

【2】 〜もしないで

neutral register
Reading~mo shinaide
Meaning (EN)"without even doing ~" — criticizes someone for acting (or speaking) without first doing a minimal, expected step
Meaning (HI)बिना ...किए ही (जबकि करना ज़रूरी/उचित था)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર જ (જ્યારે કરવું જરૂરી/યોગ્ય હતું)
Connection (接続)V-ます stem + も + しないで
RegisterNeutral — common in speech, usually carries a critical/reproachful tone
Typical useScolds or criticizes someone for skipping an expected minimal action (apologizing, trying, looking) before doing something else — the added も intensifies "not even".
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます stem + もしないで  book examples: 謝りもしないで、見向きもしないで知りもしないでshiri mo shinaide
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern requires an action verb whose omission can be criticized)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
知りもしないで偉そうなことを言うな。Shiri mo shinaide erasou na koto o iu na. Don't act like you're an expert when you don't know much about it! ठीक से जाने बिना ही बड़ी-बड़ी बातें मत करो! બરાબર જાણ્યા વગર જ મોટી મોટી વાતો ના કરો!
彼は、いつもやりもしないで、できないと言う。Kare wa, itsumo yari mo shinaide, dekinai to iu. He always gives up before he tries. वह हमेशा बिना कोशिश किए ही कहता है कि यह नहीं हो सकता। તે હંમેશા પ્રયત્ન કર્યા વગર જ કહે છે કે આ થઈ શકે નહીં.

The book's own sidebar lists 謝りもしないで ("without even apologizing") and 見向きもしないで ("without even glancing") as model examples — both criticize skipping a small, expected courtesy. The も is what adds the "not even" emphasis; without it (謝らないで alone) the sentence loses that critical edge.

【3】 〜だに

硬 formal/written
Reading~dani
Meaning (EN)"even just ~ing (is enough to cause a strong reaction)" — a minimal action, thought, or mention alone already triggers a strong (usually negative) feeling
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...करना (या सोचना) भर ही काफ़ी है / ...तक नहीं (किया/सोचा)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...કરવું (અથવા વિચારવું) એટલું જ પૂરતું છે / ...પણ (કર્યું/વિચાર્યું) નહીં
Connection (接続)V-る (辞書形) + だに ／ N + (に)だに
Register硬 — formal, literary; common in essays and reflective writing
Typical useEmphasizes that merely doing, imagining, or recalling something (often painful, frightening, or unthinkable) is already sufficient to provoke a strong emotional predicate — frequently used with a negative verb (思わない) or an emotional adjective (恐ろしい、腹が立つ) for extra force.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + だに思い出すだにomoidasu dani
NounN + (に)だに夢（に）だにyume (ni) dani
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — だに attaches to an action/image verb or a noun like 夢, not to a descriptive adjective)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この間の彼女の態度は、思い出すだに腹が立つ。Kono aida no kanojo no taido wa, omoidasu dani hara ga tatsu. Thinking about her attitude the other day still upsets me. उसके पिछले दिनों के व्यवहार को याद करना भर ही मुझे गुस्सा दिला देता है। તેના તાજેતરના વર્તનને યાદ કરવું એટલું જ મને ગુસ્સો અપાવે છે.
地下鉄に乗っているときに大地震が起きたらどうなるだろう。想像するだに恐ろしい。Chikatetsu ni notte iru toki ni daijishin ga okitara dou naru darou. Souzou suru dani osoroshii. What will happen if an earthquake hits when I am using the subway? The thought is so scary. अगर सबवे में सफ़र करते वक़्त बड़ा भूकंप आ जाए तो क्या होगा? सोचना भर ही डरावना है। જો સબવેમાં મુસાફરી કરતી વખતે મોટો ધરતીકંપ આવે તો શું થાય? ફક્ત વિચારવું જ ડરામણું છે.
そんなこと、夢（に）だに思わない。Sonna koto, yume (ni) dani omowanai. I can't even imagine that. ऐसी बात, सपने में भी नहीं सोच सकता। આવી વાત, સપનામાં પણ વિચારી શકતો નથી.

だに is an intensifier: it says the bar for triggering the reaction is as low as possible — "even just doing/thinking X" is already enough. 夢（に）だに思わない ("not even in a dream did I think") is a fixed, very common exam phrase — memorize it as a set unit. Don't confuse this "minimal trigger" nuance with Point 1's にして, which marks an exceptional condition rather than a minimal one (see §3 below).

【4】 〜こととて

硬 formal/written
Reading~koto tote
Meaning (EN)"given that it's ~ / being that ~" — a formal, often self-deprecating or apologetic reason clause
Meaning (HI)चूँकि ...है (इसलिए कृपया क्षमा करें) — विनम्र, माफ़ी भरा कारण
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે ...છે (એટલે કૃપા કરી માફ કરશો) — નમ્ર, માફી સાથેનું કારણ
Connection (接続)V(plain, incl. classical negative ぬ)／い-adj(plain)／な-adj + な／N + の + こととて
Register硬 — formal, written; typical of apologetic announcements, greetings, and business letters
Typical usePolitely explains a shortcoming or excuses a lapse ("being new," "being on holiday," "being unaccustomed") before asking for forgiveness or understanding — very frequently uses the classical negative ぬ in place of ない.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain, incl. classical neg.)V-ぬ（＝V-ない） + こととて  ない→ぬ慣れぬこととてnarenu kototote
NounN + の + こととて休業中のこととてkyuugyouchuu no kototote
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + こととて不慣れなこととてfunare na kototote
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj (plain) + こととて忙しいこととてisogashii kototote
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
新入社員で慣れぬこととて、失礼があればお許しください。Shinnyuu shain de narenu kototote, shitsurei ga areba oyurushi kudasai. Please forgive us should any of our new staff not be as well-trained as they should be. नए कर्मचारी होने के कारण अभी अभ्यस्त न होने की वजह से, अगर कोई असभ्यता हो जाए तो कृपया क्षमा करें। નવા કર્મચારી હોવાથી હજુ ટેવાયેલા ન હોવાના કારણે, જો કોઈ અસભ્યતા થાય તો કૃપા કરી માફ કરશો.
年末年始は休業中のこととて、この時期の依頼にはすぐに対応ができない。Nenmatsu nenshi wa kyuugyouchuu no kototote, kono jiki no irai ni wa sugu ni taiou ga dekinai. We can't deal with such requests promptly during this long New Year holiday. साल के अंत और नए साल में छुट्टी होने के कारण, इस समय की माँगों का तुरंत जवाब नहीं दिया जा सकता। વર્ષના અંતે અને નવા વર્ષે રજા હોવાના કારણે, આ સમયની વિનંતીઓનો તાત્કાલિક જવાબ આપી શકાતો નથી.

The single biggest trap here: the negative ない classically becomes ぬ right before こととて — 慣れない → 慣れぬ (never 慣れないこととて). For nouns, insert の before こととて (休業中の＋こととて); for な-adjectives, insert な (added example: 不慣れな＋こととて). This whole pattern is used to excuse a shortcoming politely, almost always followed by a request for forgiveness or understanding.

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.113. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.115.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

まだ検討中の（　）、はっきりとしたお返事はできません。Mada kentouchuu no (___), hakkiri to shita ohenji wa dekimasen.

Full sentence: まだ検討中のこととて、はっきりとしたお返事はできません。Mada kentouchuu no kototote, hakkiri to shita ohenji wa dekimasen.
EN: Being that it's still under consideration, we can't give you a definite answer yet.
HI: अभी विचाराधीन होने के कारण, हम स्पष्ट जवाब नहीं दे सकते।
GU: હજુ વિચારણા હેઠળ હોવાના કારણે, અમે સ્પષ્ટ જવાબ આપી શકતા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこととてkotototebeing that it's (still under consideration)...होने के कारण...હોવાના કારણે
bだにdani(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 検討中 (a noun phrase, "under consideration") is being offered as a polite, apologetic reason for the delay — that's exactly N+の+こととて (Point 4). だに (Point 3) marks a minimal trigger for a strong emotional reaction, not a reason clause, so it cannot fit here.

Q2

あんな大きい会社の倒産は、誰もが想像（　）しなかった。Anna ookii kaisha no tousan wa, dare mo ga souzou (___) shinakatta.

Full sentence: あんな大きい会社の倒産は、誰もが想像だにしなかった。Dare mo ga souzou dani shinakatta.
EN: No one had even imagined that such a large company would go bankrupt.
HI: उतनी बड़ी कंपनी के दिवालिया होने की तो किसी ने कल्पना तक नहीं की थी।
GU: એટલી મોટી કંપનીના નાદારીની તો કોઈએ કલ્પના પણ કરી નહોતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだにdaninot even (imagine)कल्पना तक नहींકલ્પના પણ નહીં
bにしてni shite(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 想像だにしなかった ("didn't even imagine") is a classic だに (Point 3) construction — the minimal act of imagining didn't even happen. にして (Point 1) marks an exceptional stage/person as a necessary condition, which has no role in this "no one imagined it" sentence.

Q3

これほどの安売りは、現金取引（　）初めて可能になることだ。Kore hodo no yasuuri wa, genkin torihiki (___) hajimete kanou ni naru koto da.

Full sentence: これほどの安売りは、現金取引にして初めて可能になることだ。Genkin torihiki ni shite hajimete kanou ni naru koto da.
EN: A discount this steep is only possible because it's a cash transaction.
HI: इतनी बड़ी छूट सिर्फ़ नक़द लेन-देन होने की वजह से ही मुमकिन हो पाती है।
GU: આટલું મોટું ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ ફક્ત રોકડ વ્યવહાર હોવાના કારણે જ શક્ય બને છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにしてni shiteonly because it's (a cash transaction)...होने की वजह से ही...હોવાના કારણે જ
bだにdani(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: にして初めて (Point 1) says that only this exceptional condition (a cash transaction) makes the discount possible — exactly the meaning intended. だに (Point 3) has no exceptional-condition sense and doesn't attach naturally to 現金取引 in this way.

Q4

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aはしないでwa shinaide(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

その女優さんは＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 行ってしまった。Sono joyuu-san wa ___ itte shimatta.

Tiles: 1 声をかけた (koe o kaketa)　2 もしないで (mo shinaide)　3 見向き (mimuki)　4 ファンに (fan ni)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

1 声をかけた→4 ファンに→3 見向き→2 もしないで

その女優さんは声をかけたファンに見向きもしないで行ってしまった。Sono joyuu-san wa koe o kaketa fan ni mimuki mo shinaide itte shimatta.

EN: The actress walked off without even glancing at the fan who had called out to her.
HI: वह अभिनेत्री, उसे आवाज़ देने वाले फ़ैन की ओर देखे बिना भी वहाँ से चली गई।
GU: તે અભિનેત્રી, તેને અવાજ આપનાર ફેનની સામે જોયા વગર જ ત્યાંથી ચાલી ગઈ.

Why: Built on Point 2 (Vますもしないで) — 見向きもしないで ("without even glancing") criticizes the absence of even a minimal courteous gesture. 声をかけたファンに ("at the fan who had called out to her") sets up the target that gesture was withheld from.

Q7

彼女はまだ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ やってください。Kanojo wa mada ___ yatte kudasai.

Tiles: 1 初心者 (shoshinsha)　2 多少の失敗は (tashou no shippai wa)　3 大目に見て (oome ni mite)　4 のこととて (no kototote)

1 初心者→4 のこととて→2 多少の失敗は→3 大目に見て

彼女はまだ初心者のこととて、多少の失敗は大目に見てやってください。Kanojo wa mada shoshinsha no kototote, tashou no shippai wa oome ni mite yatte kudasai.

EN: Since she's still a beginner, please overlook her minor mistakes.
HI: चूँकि वह अभी भी शुरुआती है, कृपया उसकी छोटी-मोटी ग़लतियों को नज़रअंदाज़ कर दीजिए।
GU: કારણ કે તે હજુ શરૂઆત કરનાર છે, કૃપા કરી તેની નાની-મોટી ભૂલોને નજરઅંદાજ કરો.

Why: Built on Point 4 (N+の+こととて) — 初心者のこととて politely excuses her ("since she's a beginner"), and 多少の失敗は大目に見て delivers the request for leniency toward her minor mistakes.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today mixes two "even" sounding patterns (にして and だに) with a formal reason-clause (こととて) and a critical "not even doing" pattern (もしないで) — N1 loves to test all four against each other. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にして(初めて)~ni shite (hajimete) Only at/because of this exceptional point N + にして(初めて) Marks an exceptional condition, stage, age, or person as the necessary cause of a result. You want to say "only X could have made this happen" or "only at this stage."
〜もしないで~mo shinaide Without even doing ~ V-ます stem + もしないで Criticizes skipping a small, expected action before doing something else — carries a reproachful tone. You're scolding someone for not even trying/apologizing/looking, etc.
〜だに~dani (硬) Even just doing/thinking X (is enough) Vる + だに ／ N + (に)だに Marks a minimal trigger — the smallest possible action or thought already causes a strong reaction. You want to emphasize how little it takes to provoke a strong feeling (fear, anger, disbelief).
〜こととて~kototote (硬) Given that it's ~ (apologetic reason) V-ぬ／い-adj／な-adj+な／N+の + こととて A formal, self-deprecating reason clause — explains a shortcoming before asking for forgiveness. Uses classical ぬ, not ない. You're politely excusing a lapse (being new, being unaccustomed, being on holiday).
Exam trap: にして and だに can both loosely translate with an "even" flavor in English, but they attach completely differently and mean different things — にして marks an exceptional condition ("only at 40," "only that politician"), while だに marks a minimal trigger ("even just thinking about it is enough"). Separately, don't confuse こととて (硬, a formal reason-giving clause, often apologetic) with だに (硬, an intensifier on a minimal action) just because both are 硬 and both show up late in the same sentence pattern list — こととて explains why, だに emphasizes how little it takes. Finally, memorize the classical negative trap directly: ない → ぬ before こととて (慣れない → 慣れぬこととて, never 慣れないこととて) — this exact substitution is a favorite N1 fill-in-the-blank trap.
