2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

彼女は、夫の暴力に（　）いたが、ついに家を出て行く決心をした。Kanojo wa, otto no bouryoku ni (___) ita ga, tsui ni ie o dete iku kesshin o shita.

Full sentence: 彼女は、夫の暴力に我慢に我慢を重ねていたが、ついに家を出て行く決心をした。...gaman ni gaman o kasanete ita ga, tsui ni ie o dete iku kesshin o shita.
EN: She endured her husband's violence again and again, but finally decided to leave home.
HI: वह पति की हिंसा को बार-बार सहती रही, लेकिन आख़िरकार घर छोड़ने का फ़ैसला किया।
GU: તે પતિની હિંસાને વારંવાર સહન કરતી રહી, પણ છેવટે ઘર છોડવાનો નિર્ણય કર્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1我慢するも我慢しないでgaman suru mo gaman shinaide(not a natural/valid combination)
2我慢に我慢を重ねてgaman ni gaman o kasaneteenduring again and again
3我慢という我慢を組んでgaman to iu gaman o kunde(not a valid combination)
4我慢で我慢を合わせてgaman de gaman o awasete(not a valid combination)

Why: Day 1's N1にN2を重ねる (考えに考えて family) — repeated, piled-up endurance, echoing the week's own title 努力に努力を重ねている.

Q2

（　）「やります」と言った彼の責任は重い。(___) "yarimasu" to itta kare no sekinin wa omoi.

Full sentence: できもしないで「やります」と言った彼の責任は重い。Dekimoshinaide "yarimasu" to itta kare no sekinin wa omoi.
EN: His responsibility is heavy for having said "I'll do it" without even being able to.
HI: कर भी न सकते हुए "मैं करूँगा" कहने वाले उसकी ज़िम्मेदारी भारी है।
GU: કરી પણ ન શકવા છતાં "હું કરીશ" કહેનાર તેની જવાબદારી ભારે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1できるにたりてdekiru ni tarite(not a valid form)
2できないにしてdekinai ni shite(not a natural fit)
3できもしないでdekimoshinaidewithout even being able to
4できなくはなくてdekinaku wa nakute(not a valid/natural form here)

Why: Day 2's 〜もしないで — claiming ability one doesn't actually have, without even trying.

Q3

近所の梅の木も色づき、春（　）季節になりました。Kinjo no ume no ki mo irozuki, haru (___) kisetsu ni narimashita.

Full sentence: 近所の梅の木も色づき、春めく季節になりました。...haru meku kisetsu ni narimashita.
EN: The plum trees nearby have taken on their color, and it has become a season that really feels like spring.
HI: आस-पास के बेर के पेड़ भी रंगीन हो गए हैं, और अब बसंत जैसा मौसम हो गया है।
GU: આજુબાજુના પ્લમ વૃક્ષો પણ રંગીન થઈ ગયા છે, અને હવે વસંત જેવી ઋતુ થઈ ગઈ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1びる-biru(wrong suffix — びる pairs with different bases, e.g. 大人びる)
2ぶる-buru(wrong — ぶる means "to act/pretend," not "to feel like a season")
3まく-maku(not a valid suffix here)
4めく-mekutakes on the air of, feels like

Why: Day 3's Nめく — 春めく is the standard fixed form for "starting to feel like spring."

Q4

昨日見た映画は残酷なシーンばかりで、とても（　）ものだった。Kinou mita eiga wa zankoku na shiin bakari de, totemo (___) mono datta.

Full sentence: 昨日見た映画は残酷なシーンばかりで、とても見るにたえないものだった。...totemo miru ni taenai mono datta.
EN: The movie I saw yesterday was nothing but cruel scenes, and it was something unbearable to watch.
HI: कल जो फ़िल्म देखी वह बस क्रूर दृश्यों से भरी थी, और देखा ही नहीं जा रहा था।
GU: ગઈકાલે જોયેલી ફિલ્મ ફક્ત ક્રૂર દ્રશ્યોથી ભરેલી હતી, અને જોવાય જ નહીં તેવી હતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1見るにたえないmiru ni taenaiunbearable to watch
2見ざるを得ないmizaru o enaihave no choice but to watch (wrong sense)
3見るだに恐ろしいmiru dani osoroshiifrightening just to look at (close, but だに implies a milder "just doing X is enough," less natural than にたえない for graphic cruelty)
4見るには及ばないmiru ni wa oyobanaino need to watch (wrong sense)

Why: Day 4's Vるにたえない — the cruel scenes are unbearable to watch, matching 使用に耐える/聞くに堪えない's family of "can't stand it" meanings.

Q5

だれでも（　）、死にたいと思ったことがあるのではないだろうか。Dare demo (___), shinitai to omotta koto ga aru no de wa nai darou ka.

Full sentence: だれでも一度や二度、死にたいと思ったことがあるのではないだろうか。Dare demo ichido ya nido, shinitai to omotta koto ga aru no de wa nai darou ka.
EN: Hasn't everyone, at least once or twice, thought they wanted to die?
HI: क्या हर किसी ने, कम से कम एक-दो बार, मरने का मन नहीं किया होगा?
GU: શું દરેકને, ઓછામાં ઓછું એક-બે વાર, મરવાનું મન નથી થયું?
#JapaneseEnglish
1十回に一、二回jukkai ni ichi, nikai(an odd, overly specific ratio — not idiomatic)
2今度という今度kondo to iu kondothis time of all times (wrong pattern/meaning)
3一度や二度ichido ya nidoonce or twice, at least
4何回にして何回もnankai ni shite nankai mo(not a valid/natural form)

Why: Day 1's N1やN2 — 一人や二人 family, giving a rough minimum count ("at least once or twice").

Q6

彼が自分の犯行であることを（　）証拠が出てきた。Kare ga jibun no hankou de aru koto o (___) shouko ga dete kita.

Full sentence: 彼が自分の犯行であることを認めざるを得ない証拠が出てきた。...mitomezaru o enai shouko ga dete kita.
EN: Evidence came out that left him no choice but to admit it was his own crime.
HI: ऐसे सबूत सामने आए कि उसे यह मानना ही पड़ा कि यह उसका ही अपराध है।
GU: એવા પુરાવા સામે આવ્યા કે તેને એ સ્વીકારવું જ પડ્યું કે તે તેનો જ ગુનો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1認め足りないmitome tarinai(not a valid combination)
2認めるに耐えないmitomeru ni taenaiunbearable to admit (wrong sense)
3認めるに及ばないmitomeru ni oyobanaino need to admit (wrong sense)
4認めざるを得ないmitomezaru o enaihave no choice but to admit

Why: Day 6's ざるを得ない — the evidence is so strong there's no avoiding admission (認める is a regular verb, so ざる attaches directly, unlike する's irregular せざる).

Q7

このあたりは、（　）塀に落書きがされていて、地域の大きな問題になっている。Kono atari wa, (___) hei ni rakugaki ga sarete ite, chiiki no ookina mondai ni natte iru.

Full sentence: このあたりは、塀という塀に落書きがされていて、地域の大きな問題になっている。...hei to iu hei ni rakugaki ga sarete ite...
EN: Around here, every single fence has graffiti on it, and it has become a big local problem.
HI: इस इलाक़े में, हर दीवार-बाड़े पर भित्तिचित्र बना दिए गए हैं, जो एक बड़ी स्थानीय समस्या बन गई है।
GU: આ વિસ્તારમાં, દરેક દીવાલ-વાડ પર ચિતરામણ થઈ ગયું છે, જે એક મોટી સ્થાનિક સમસ્યા બની ગઈ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1塀やhei yafences and (such) (wrong — too weak, not "every single one")
2塀にhei ni(plain, no emphatic "every" meaning)
3塀としたhei to shita(not a valid form)
4塀というhei to iuevery single fence, without exception

Why: Day 1's NというN — 壁という壁 family, emphasizing "every last fence, no exceptions."

Q8

新入社員が入ってくると、急に先輩（　）口のきき方をする人が多い。Shinnyuu shain ga haitte kuru to, kyuu ni senpai (___) kuchi no kikikata o suru hito ga ooi.

Full sentence: 新入社員が入ってくると、急に先輩ぶった口のきき方をする人が多い。...senpai butta kuchi no kikikata o suru hito ga ooi.
EN: When new employees join, a lot of people suddenly start talking as if putting on senior airs.
HI: जब नए कर्मचारी आते हैं, तो अचानक कई लोग सीनियर होने का दिखावा करते हुए बोलने लगते हैं।
GU: જ્યારે નવા કર્મચારીઓ આવે છે, ત્યારે અચાનક ઘણા લોકો સિનિયર હોવાનો ડોળ કરીને બોલવા લાગે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1のふりでno furi depretending to be (a plausible near-synonym, but not the idiomatic verb-suffix form tested here)
2ぶったbuttaacted/put on airs of being
3っぷりの-ppuri no(wrong — っぷり describes manner/style, e.g. 食べっぷり, not "acting like")
4ぶりの-buri no(wrong — this is Point 4's "manner of," e.g. 仕事ぶり, not "pretending to be")

Why: Day 3's Nぶる (金持ちぶる family) — 先輩ぶった describes putting on the airs of being a senior.

Q9

病院で寝たきりの母の姿を思い浮かべる（　）、涙が出てくる。Byouin de netakiri no haha no sugata o omoiukaberu (___), namida ga dete kuru.

Full sentence: 病院で寝たきりの母の姿を思い浮かべるだに、涙が出てくる。...omoiukaberu dani, namida ga dete kuru.
EN: Just imagining my bedridden mother's condition in the hospital brings tears to my eyes.
HI: अस्पताल में बिस्तर पर पड़ी माँ की हालत की कल्पना करते ही, आँसू आ जाते हैं।
GU: હોસ્પિટલમાં પથારીવશ માતાની હાલતની કલ્પના કરતાં જ, આંસુ આવી જાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1めく-meku(wrong — めく means "feels like," not "just doing X")
2ぶり-buri(wrong — describes manner, not "merely imagining")
3こととてkoto totte(wrong — こととて gives an apologetic reason, not "just doing X")
4だにdanijust doing X is enough to...

Why: Day 2's 〜だに — merely picturing the scene is enough to bring tears, without anything more happening.

Q10

なにぶん不慣れ（　）、ご迷惑をかけるかもしれませんが、よろしくお願いいたします。Nanibun funare (___), gomeiwaku o kakeru kamo shiremasen ga, yoroshiku onegai itashimasu.

Full sentence: なにぶん不慣れなこととて、ご迷惑をかけるかもしれませんが、よろしくお願いいたします。Nanibun funare na kototote, gomeiwaku o kakeru kamo shiremasen ga, yoroshiku onegai itashimasu.
EN: Since I'm quite unaccustomed to this, I may cause you some trouble, but I appreciate your understanding.
HI: चूँकि मुझे अभी ज़्यादा अभ्यास नहीं है, हो सकता है कुछ परेशानी हो, फिर भी कृपया साथ दें।
GU: કારણ કે મને હજુ વધારે અનુભવ નથી, કદાચ થોડી તકલીફ પડે, છતાં કૃપા કરી સાથ આપો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1なこととてna kotototesince it's the case that (I'm unaccustomed)
2だにdani(wrong — だに doesn't attach this way to state a reason)
3ぶってbutte(wrong — ぶる means pretending, not stating a genuine reason)
4に足るものでni taru mono de(wrong — 足る means "worthy of," not a reason marker)

Why: Day 2's 〜こととて — a formal, apologetic reason ("since it's the case that I'm unaccustomed"), typical in polite self-introductions.

Q11

「何の苦労もない君なんかにぼくの気持ちは（　）さ。」"Nan no kurou mo nai kimi nanka ni boku no kimochi wa (___) sa."

Full sentence: 「何の苦労もない君なんかにぼくの気持ちはわかりゃしないさ。」"...boku no kimochi wa wakaryashinai sa."
EN: "Someone like you, who's never struggled, could never understand my feelings."
HI: "तुझे, जिसने कभी कोई तकलीफ़ नहीं झेली, मेरा दर्द समझ ही नहीं आएगा।"
GU: "તને, જેણે ક્યારેય કોઈ તકલીફ નથી વેઠી, મારી લાગણી સમજાશે જ નહીં."
#JapaneseEnglish
1わからないったらないwakaranai ttara nai(wrong — this idiom means "extremely/hopelessly X," doesn't fit here)
2わかるったらないwakaru ttara nai(wrong — same issue, and wrong polarity)
3わかりゃないwakarya nai(close, but not the standard contracted form)
4わかりゃしないwakaryashinaithere's just no way (you'd) understand

Why: Day 5's 〜やしない／ゃしない — a colloquial, emphatic negative meaning "definitely not."

Q12

その病気は現在、薬の開発のおかげで、昔ほど（　）そうだ。Sono byouki wa genzai, kusuri no kaihatsu no okage de, mukashi hodo (___) sou da.

Full sentence: その病気は現在、薬の開発のおかげで、昔ほど恐れるに足りないそうだ。...mukashi hodo osoreru ni tarinai sou da.
EN: Thanks to drug development, that disease is apparently no longer as much something to be feared as it once was.
HI: दवाओं के विकास की बदौलत, अब वह बीमारी पहले जितनी डरावनी नहीं मानी जाती।
GU: દવાઓના વિકાસના કારણે, હવે તે બીમારી પહેલા જેટલી ડરામણી ગણાતી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1恐れるに耐えるosoreru ni taeru(not a valid/natural combination)
2恐れざるものだosorezaru mono da(not a valid/natural form)
3恐れざるを得ないosorezaru o enaican't help but fear (wrong sense — opposite meaning)
4恐れるに足りないosoreru ni tarinainot worth fearing

Why: Day 4's 恐れるに足りない — the disease is no longer worth fearing as much, thanks to medical progress.

Q13

ご心配（　）。必ず期限までに完成させることをお約束いたします。Go-shinpai (___). Kanarazu kigen made ni kansei saseru koto o oyakusoku itashimasu.

Full sentence: ご心配には及びません。必ず期限までに完成させることをお約束いたします。Go-shinpai ni wa oyobimasen. ...
EN: There's no need to worry. I promise it will be completed by the deadline without fail.
HI: चिंता करने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। मैं वादा करता/करती हूँ कि यह समय सीमा तक ज़रूर पूरा हो जाएगा।
GU: ચિંતા કરવાની જરૂર નથી. હું વચન આપું છું કે તે સમયમર્યાદામાં ચોક્કસ પૂરું થશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1には足りませんni wa tarimasen(wrong — 足りない means "insufficient," not "unnecessary")
2には及びませんni wa oyobimasenthere's no need for (worry)
3かけるにたえませんkakeru ni taemasen(not a valid/natural combination)
4するもなんともありませんsuru mo nan to mo arimasen(not a valid form here)

Why: Day 6's Nには及ばない — a polite, formal way of reassuring someone that worry isn't necessary.

Q14

「僕と結婚してください。あなたを必ず幸せに（　）。」"Boku to kekkon shite kudasai. Anata o kanarazu shiawase ni (___)."

Full sentence: 「僕と結婚してください。あなたを必ず幸せにしてみせます。」"...anata o kanarazu shiawase ni shite misemasu."
EN: "Please marry me. I will definitely make you happy — I promise you'll see it."
HI: "मुझसे शादी कर लो। मैं तुम्हें ज़रूर ख़ुश करके दिखाऊँगा।"
GU: "મારી સાથે લગ્ન કરો. હું તમને ચોક્કસ ખુશ કરીને બતાવીશ."
#JapaneseEnglish
1してみせますshite misemasuwill definitely do it, I'll prove it
2してなりますshite narimasu(not a valid/natural combination)
3せざるを得ませんsezaru o emasenhave no choice but to (wrong tone — this is a vow, not a reluctant obligation)
4するにおよびますsuru ni oyobimasu(not a valid form — に及ぶ is negative-oriented, "no need to")

Why: Day 5's 〜てみせる — a determined promise/vow, "I will make it happen, just watch."

Q15

彼女が男性にもてるのは、うらやましくもなんともない。（　）。Kanojo ga dansei ni moteru no wa, urayamashiku mo nan to mo nai. (___).

Full sentence: 彼女が男性にもてるのは、うらやましくもなんともない。私には素敵な彼がいるから。...Watashi ni wa suteki na kare ga iru kara.
EN: The fact that she's popular with men is nothing for me to envy at all. Because I have a wonderful boyfriend.
HI: उसका मर्दों में लोकप्रिय होना मुझे बिल्कुल भी ईर्ष्या नहीं दिलाता। क्योंकि मेरे पास एक शानदार प्रेमी है।
GU: તેનું પુરુષોમાં લોકપ્રિય હોવું મને બિલકુલ ઈર્ષ્યા નથી કરાવતું. કારણ કે મારી પાસે એક શાનદાર બોયફ્રેન્ડ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1私も彼女のように異性に注目されたいwatashi mo kanojo no you ni isei ni chuumoku saretaiI too want attention like her (contradicts "not envious")
2彼女のような女性は男性がほっておかないだろうkanojo no you na josei wa dansei ga hotte okanai daroumen won't leave a woman like her alone (explains her popularity, not the speaker's lack of envy)
3私には素敵な彼がいるからwatashi ni wa suteki na kare ga iru karabecause I have a wonderful boyfriend
4彼女の彼はだれよりも素敵なんだからkanojo no kare wa dare yori mo suteki nan dakarabecause her boyfriend is more wonderful than anyone (off-topic — about her boyfriend, not the speaker's own reason)

Why: Day 5's 〜くもなんともない ("not X at all") needs a follow-up reason for the lack of envy — only option 3 gives the speaker's own reason for feeling unbothered.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ なかった。___ ★ ___ nakatta.

Tiles: 1 結婚10年目にして (kekkon juu-nen-me ni shite)　2 やっと妊娠したときの (yatto ninshin shita toki no)　3 夫の喜びよう (otto no yorokobi you)　4 といったら (to ittara)

1 結婚10年目にして→2 やっと妊娠したときの→★ 3 夫の喜びよう→4 といったら

問題3 — reading passage, five blanks (1問4点)
