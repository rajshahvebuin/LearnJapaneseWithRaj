Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.123 (7日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Watch three traps today: the classical negative ざる that modifies a following noun (知られざる真実, 見えざる敵) vs. the completely different fixed expression ざるを得ない ("must do") built on the same ざる morpheme; する's irregular classical negative is せざる, never しざる; and に(は)及ばない ("no need to do X") vs. には及ばない ("doesn't measure up to X") — same 及ぶ base, one about necessity, the other about comparison.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 知られざる真実 (shirarezaru shinjitsu)

neutral register (literary flavor)
ReadingV-nai stem + ざる + N
Meaning (EN)"un-~-ed / not-~-able N" — a classical negative form (ざる) directly modifying a following noun
Meaning (HI)न ~ हुआ/होने वाला (संज्ञा) — शास्त्रीय निषेधात्मक रूप जो सीधे संज्ञा से पहले आता है
Meaning (GU)ન ~ થયેલું/થઈ શકે તેવું (નામ) — શાસ્ત્રીય નકારાત્મક રૂપ જે સીધું નામ પહેલાં આવે છે
Connection (接続)Vないざる + N (V-nai stem + zaru + N)
RegisterNeutral in the source, but the classical ざる gives it a distinctly literary/written flavor — rare in casual speech
Typical useA bookish way to attach a negative meaning directly onto a noun, as if the negative form itself were an adjective (compare modern V-ない + N, which sounds much more plain/spoken).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない stem + ざる + N  exception: する→せざる (see Point 2)知られざる真実shirarezaru shinjitsu
Verb (alt.)絶えざる不安taezaru fuan絶えざる不安には夜も眠れないTaezaru fuan ni wa yoru mo nemurenai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ざる is a classical verb-negative suffix and cannot attach to adjectives or nouns directly)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
政治の世界には、知られざる真実がたくさんある。Seiji no sekai ni wa, shirarezaru shinjitsu ga takusan aru. There are a lot of unrevealed truths in the world of politics. राजनीति की दुनिया में बहुत-सी अनजानी सच्चाइयाँ हैं। રાજકારણની દુનિયામાં ઘણાં અજાણ્યાં સત્યો છે.
絶えざる不安に夜も眠れない。Taezaru fuan ni yoru mo nemurenai. I can't sleep at night because I worry incessantly. लगातार बनी रहने वाली चिंता की वजह से रात को नींद भी नहीं आती। સતત રહેતી ચિંતાને કારણે રાત્રે ઊંઘ પણ નથી આવતી.

ざる here is the classical (bungo) negative form of the auxiliary ず, used the way ない is used in modern Japanese, but only right before a noun. 知られざる真実 = 知られていない真実 ("truths that are not known"), 絶えざる不安 = 絶えない不安／ずっと続く不安 ("unceasing anxiety"). Do not confuse this noun-modifying ざる with the completely different expression ざるを得ない in Point 2 — same historical root, very different job. See §3 for the full contrast.

【2】 せざるを得ない (sezaru o enai)

硬 formal/written
ReadingV-nai stem + ざるを得ない
Meaning (EN)"cannot avoid doing ~ / have no choice but to ~" — a strong sense of forced obligation
Meaning (HI)...करना ही पड़ता है / ...किए बिना कोई चारा नहीं
Meaning (GU)...કરવું જ પડે છે / ...કર્યા વગર છૂટકો નથી
Connection (接続)Vないざるを得ない (V-nai stem + zaru o enai)  irregular: しない→せざる (never しざる)
Register硬 — formal, mostly written (news, essays, formal speech)
Typical useStates that despite reluctance, circumstances leave no alternative but to act — often used for admissions, apologies, or reluctant decisions.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない stem + ざるを得ない言わざるを得ないiwazaru o enai
Verb (する, irregular)する → せざるを得ない  never しざるそうせざるを得ないsou sezaru o enai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ざるを得ない attaches only to verbs)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
不本意だがそうせざるを得ない。Fuhon'i da ga sou sezaru o enai. Although I don't really want to do it, I have to. मन तो नहीं है, लेकिन ऐसा करना ही पड़ेगा। મન નથી થતું, પણ એવું કરવું જ પડશે.
今回の失敗は君の責任と言わざるを得ない。Konkai no shippai wa kimi no sekinin to iwazaru o enai. I must say that the fact that we failed is your responsibility. मुझे यह कहना ही पड़ेगा कि इस बार की नाकामी की ज़िम्मेदारी तुम्हारी है। મારે એ કહેવું જ પડશે કે આ વખતની નિષ્ફળતાની જવાબદારી તારી છે.

得ない (えない) literally means "cannot obtain/cannot manage," so ざるを得ない reads as "cannot manage to not-do it" → "cannot avoid doing it." The single most important memorization trap: する's classical negative is the irregular せざる, never しざる (see Quiz Q5). Note also that this ざるを得ない is a fixed, complete predicate meaning "must do" — it does not modify a following noun the way Point 1's ざる does.

【3】 わざわざ行くには及ばない (wazawaza iku ni wa oyobanai)

neutral register
ReadingV-る／N する + に(は)及ばない
Meaning (EN)"there's no need to do ~" — reassures the listener that an action isn't necessary
Meaning (HI)...करने की ज़रूरत नहीं है
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની જરૂર નથી
Connection (接続)「Vる／Nする」に(は)及ばない (V-plain/N-suru + ni (wa) oyobanai)
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical useTells someone they don't need to trouble themselves; frequent set phrases include 心配には及ばない ("no need to worry") and それには及ばない ("that's not necessary").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain, dictionary form)V-る + に(は)及ばないわざわざ行くには及ばないwazawaza iku ni wa oyobanai
Noun + するN する + に(は)及ばない郵便で送るには及ばないyuubin de okuru ni wa oyobanai
Noun (fixed idiom)(added, not in book) 心配 + には及ばない心配には及ばないshinpai ni wa oyobanai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電話で済むことだから、わざわざ行くには及ばない。Denwa de sumu koto dakara, wazawaza iku ni wa oyobanai. You won't need to trouble yourself to go since you can do it over the phone. फ़ोन से काम चल जाएगा, इसलिए ख़ास तौर पर जाने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। ફોનથી કામ થઈ જશે, એટલે ખાસ જવાની જરૂર નથી.
ファックスを使えばいい。郵便で送るには及ばない。Fakkusu o tsukaeba ii. Yuubin de okuru ni wa oyobanai. You can use a fax machine. You don't need to send it by post. फ़ैक्स का इस्तेमाल कर लो। डाक से भेजने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। ફેક્સનો ઉપયોગ કરી લો. ટપાલથી મોકલવાની જરૂર નથી.
もっと！ 若者は言うに及ばず、主婦やサラリーマンまで、彼のコンサートに行っている。Wakamono wa iu ni oyobazu, shufu ya sarariiman made, kare no konsaato ni itte iru. Not only are young people going to his concert (needless to say), so are housewives and salaried workers. युवाओं की तो बात ही छोड़िए, गृहिणियाँ और नौकरीपेशा लोग भी उसके कॉन्सर्ट में जा रहे हैं। યુવાનોની તો વાત જ છોડો, ગૃહિણીઓ અને નોકરિયાત લોકો પણ તેના કોન્સર્ટમાં જઈ રહ્યા છે.

及ぶ (およぶ) means "to reach/extend to," so 〜には及ばない literally reads "it doesn't reach the point of needing to ~," i.e. "there's no need." Bonus idiom: 言うに及ばず ("needless to say / not to mention") is a fixed, frozen extension of this same pattern using the negative -ず form instead of -ない — memorize it as a set phrase rather than trying to freely conjugate it.

【4】 彼には及ばない (kare ni wa oyobanai)

neutral register
ReadingN + には及ばない
Meaning (EN)"doesn't measure up to / isn't as good as N" — hasn't reached that level or standard
Meaning (HI)...के स्तर तक नहीं पहुँचता / ...की बराबरी नहीं कर सकता
Meaning (GU)...ના સ્તર સુધી પહોંચતું નથી / ...ની બરાબરી કરી શકતું નથી
Connection (接続)N + には及ばない (N + ni wa oyobanai)
RegisterNeutral — common in comparisons in both speech and writing
Typical useMeans "hasn't reached that level" (そのレベルには達していない); the idiom 足元にも及ばない ("isn't even close / doesn't hold a candle to," lit. "doesn't even reach [someone's] feet") intensifies this further, implying the other party is far superior.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + には及ばない彼には及ばないkare ni wa oyobanai
Noun (intensified idiom)N + の足元にも及ばない(added, not in book) 彼の足元にも及ばないkare no ashimoto ni mo oyobanai
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this comparison pattern targets nouns being compared, not verbs or descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
どんなに頑張っても、彼の成績には及ばない。Donna ni ganbatte mo, kare no seiseki ni wa oyobanai. No matter how hard I study, I can't compete with him. मैं कितनी भी मेहनत कर लूँ, उसके नतीजों की बराबरी नहीं कर सकता। હું ગમે તેટલી મહેનત કરું, તેના પરિણામોની બરાબરી કરી શકતો નથી.
そのオリンピック選手は優勝したが、世界記録には及ばなかった。Sono orinpikku senshu wa yuushou shita ga, sekai kiroku ni wa oyobanakatta. He won the gold medal at the Olympics but he could not break the world record. उस ओलंपिक खिलाड़ी ने स्वर्ण पदक जीता, लेकिन विश्व रिकॉर्ड तक नहीं पहुँच सका। તે ઓલિમ્પિક ખેલાડીએ સુવર્ણ ચંદ્રક જીત્યો, પણ વિશ્વ વિક્રમ સુધી પહોંચી ન શક્યો.

This に(は)及ばない is a different job for the same verb 及ぶ as in Point 3 — here it compares two things and says the level hasn't been reached, rather than saying an action is unnecessary. Don't let the shared 及ばない fool you into treating them as the same pattern; see §3 for the direct contrast.

Q1

面接の結果は電話で連絡しますので、こちらに（　）には及びません。Mensetsu no kekka wa denwa de renraku shimasu node, kochira ni (___) ni wa oyobimasen.

EN: We'll contact you by phone with the interview results, so there's no need for you to come here.
HI: इंटरव्यू के नतीजे हम फ़ोन पर बता देंगे, इसलिए यहाँ आने की ज़रूरत नहीं है।
GU: ઇન્ટરવ્યુના પરિણામો અમે ફોન પર જણાવીશું, એટલે અહીં આવવાની જરૂર નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a来るkuruto comeआनाઆવવું
b来ないkonai(wrong form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q2

この交通事故に関しては歩行者側の不注意と（　）えない。Kono koutsuu jiko ni kanshite wa hokousha-gawa no fuchuui to (___) enai.

Q3

その映画では、主人公が（　）敵と戦う姿が印象的だった。Sono eiga de wa, shujinkou ga (___) teki to tatakau sugata ga inshouteki datta.

Q6

レオナルド・ダ・ビンチは、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 分野においても後世に多くの影響を残した。Reonarudo da Binchi wa, ___ bun'ya ni oite mo kousei ni ooku no eikyou o nokoshita.

Tiles: 1 哲学や思想、 (tetsugaku ya shisou,)　2 言うにおよばず (iu ni oyobazu)　3 自然科学の (shizen kagaku no)　4 絵画や彫刻は (kaiga ya choukoku wa)

レオナルド・ダ・ビンチは、絵画や彫刻は言うにおよばず、哲学や思想、自然科学の分野においても後世に多くの影響を残した。Reonarudo da Binchi wa, kaiga ya choukoku wa iu ni oyobazu, tetsugaku ya shisou, shizen kagaku no bun'ya ni oite mo kousei ni ooku no eikyou o nokoshita.

EN: Leonardo da Vinci left a great influence on later generations not only in painting and sculpture (needless to say), but also in the fields of philosophy, thought, and natural science.
HI: लियोनार्दो दा विंची ने चित्रकला और मूर्तिकला की तो बात ही छोड़िए, दर्शनशास्त्र, विचारधारा और प्राकृतिक विज्ञान के क्षेत्रों में भी आने वाली पीढ़ियों पर गहरा असर छोड़ा।
GU: લિયોનાર્દો દા વિન્ચીએ ચિત્રકલા અને શિલ્પકલાની તો વાત જ છોડો, ફિલસૂફી, વિચારધારા અને પ્રાકૃતિક વિજ્ઞાનના ક્ષેત્રોમાં પણ પછીની પેઢીઓ પર ઊંડો પ્રભાવ છોડ્યો.

Why: Built on Point 3's bonus idiom 言うに及ばず ("needless to say"). 絵画や彫刻は言うにおよばず sets up "not to mention painting and sculpture," then 哲学や思想、自然科学の分野 lists the additional fields the influence extended to.

Q7

私は、自分の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ を得なかった。Watashi wa, jibun no ___ o enakatta.

Tiles: 1 ながらも (nagara mo)　2 納得せざる (nattoku sezaru)　3 決定したことに (kettei shita koto ni)　4 意に反し (i ni hanshi)

4 意に反し→1 ながらも→3 決定したことに→2 納得せざる

私は、自分の意に反しながらも決定したことに納得せざるを得なかった。Watashi wa, jibun no i ni hanshi nagara mo kettei shita koto ni nattoku sezaru o enakatta.

EN: Even though it went against my own wishes, I had no choice but to accept the decision that had been made.
HI: मेरी अपनी इच्छा के ख़िलाफ़ होने के बावजूद, मुझे लिए गए फ़ैसले को मानना ही पड़ा।
GU: મારી પોતાની ઇચ્છા વિરુદ્ધ હોવા છતાં, મારે લેવાયેલા નિર્ણયનો સ્વીકાર કરવો જ પડ્યો.

Why: Built on Point 2 (せざるを得ない, past tense 得なかった). 意に反しながらも ("even though it goes against my wishes") sets up the concession, 決定したことに納得 ("accept the decision that was made") is the thing being accepted, and せざるを得なかった delivers "had no choice but to."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split into two look-alike pairs that N1 loves to cross-test (see Q3 and Q1/Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
知られざる真実shirarezaru shinjitsu Unrevealed truths (unknown N) Vないざる + N Classical negative ざる directly modifying a following noun — a bookish substitute for V-ない + N. You want a literary way to say "an un-X-ed N" (見えざる敵, 絶えざる不安).
せざるを得ないsezaru o enai (硬) Have no choice but to do ~ Vないざるを得ない A complete fixed predicate ("must do"), not a noun-modifier — built on the same classical ざる but with an entirely different grammatical job. You want to say a forced action cannot be avoided.
Vるに(は)及ばない~ ni (wa) oyobanai No need to do ~ V-plain／N する + に(は)及ばない About necessity — the action is unnecessary. 及ぶ's negative applied to a verb/action. You're reassuring someone they don't need to bother doing X.
Nには及ばないN ni wa oyobanai Doesn't measure up to N N + には及ばない About comparison/rank — a level hasn't been reached. 及ぶ's negative applied to a noun being compared. You're saying something/someone falls short of a standard (彼には及ばない, 世界記録には及ばない).
Exam trap: The single biggest trap today is confusing Point 1's noun-modifying ざる (知られざる真実, 見えざる敵 — "an un-X-ed N") with Point 2's ざるを得ない ("cannot avoid doing X"). Both are built on the same classical negative ざる, but ざる alone sits in front of a noun while ざるを得ない is a complete "must do" predicate on its own — this is exactly the trap in Quiz Q3 (見えざる敵 vs. 見ざるをえない). Separately, memorize する's irregular classical negative as せざる, never しざる (Q5) — there is no rule to derive this from, it must simply be memorized. Finally, don't conflate に(は)及ばない ("no need to do X," about necessity, Point 3) with には及ばない ("doesn't measure up to X," about comparison, Point 4) — both come from 及ぶ, but one judges whether an action is needed and the other judges whether a standard has been reached.
