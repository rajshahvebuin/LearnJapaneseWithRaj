Notes
Today's biggest trap is 耐える／堪える (enduring/withstanding something unpleasant or demanding) vs. 足る／足りない (meeting a standard of sufficiency, worth, or trust) — see §3 below. None of today's four points are marked 硬 in the source, though in practice all of them read as somewhat literary/written.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 使用に耐える (Shiyou ni taeru)

neutral register
Reading~ni taeru／~ni tairu
Meaning (EN)"can withstand ~ / is good enough to be worth ~ing" — something is strong or high-quality enough to endure a process (use, viewing, reading)
Meaning (HI)...सहन करने में सक्षम / ...करने लायक़ (जैसे उपयोग, देखने, पढ़ने के लायक़)
Meaning (GU)...સહન કરવા સક્ષમ / ...કરવા લાયક (જેમ કે ઉપયોગ, જોવા, વાંચવા લાયક)
Connection (接続)Vる（辞書形）＋に耐える／堪える　／　N（する動詞の名詞）＋に耐える／堪える
RegisterNeutral — not tagged 硬 in the source, though it reads as somewhat literary/written in practice
Typical use耐える and 堪える are two different kanji for the exact same word たえる and are used interchangeably in this pattern — "to endure/withstand." With verbs of perception (見る, 読む, 聞く, 鑑賞する) it means "worth ~ing"; with durability nouns (使用, 洗濯) it means "can hold up under ~."
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)Vる（辞書形）＋に＋耐える／堪える読むに堪えるyomu ni taeru
Noun (＋する動詞)N（＋する、しばしば省略）＋に＋耐える／堪える使用に耐えるshiyou ni taeru
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets processes/actions that can be endured or judged worthwhile, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
これは50年間の使用に耐える丈夫な製品です。Kore wa gojuu-nenkan no shiyou ni taeru joubu na seihin desu. This is a very durable product which will last for 50 years [lit. "can withstand 50 years of use"]. यह एक मज़बूत उत्पाद है जो 50 साल के उपयोग को सहन कर सकता है। આ એક મજબૂત ઉત્પાદન છે જે 50 વર્ષના ઉપયોગને સહન કરી શકે છે.
この展示は鑑賞に堪える作品が少ない。Kono tenji wa kanshou ni taeru sakuhin ga sukunai. There are not many works of art which are worth looking at in this exhibition. इस प्रदर्शनी में देखने लायक़ कलाकृतियाँ बहुत कम हैं। આ પ્રદર્શનમાં જોવા લાયક કલાકૃતિઓ બહુ ઓછી છે.

耐える／堪える literally mean "to endure/withstand" — 使用に耐える literally reads "withstands (50 years of) use." Sidebar set-phrases to memorize: 議論に堪える (withstands scrutiny/debate), 読むに堪える (worth reading), 見るに堪える (worth watching), 聞くに堪える (worth listening to). Watch this same verb flip meaning entirely once negated in Point 2.

【2】 聞くに堪えない (Kiku ni taenai)

neutral register
Reading~ni taenai
Meaning (EN)"not even worth ~ing / too bad to bear ~ing" — the quality is so poor that one cannot bear to watch, listen to, or read it
Meaning (HI)...करने लायक़ भी नहीं / इतना ख़राब कि सहन नहीं किया जा सकता
Meaning (GU)...કરવા લાયક પણ નથી / એટલું ખરાબ કે સહન કરી શકાય નહીં
Connection (接続)Vる（辞書形）＋に堪えない　／　N（する動詞の名詞）＋に堪えない
RegisterNeutral — not tagged 硬 in the source, though it reads as somewhat literary/written in practice
Typical useThe exact negative of Point 1 — used when quality is so bad (a poor violin performance, cruel film scenes) that the speaker cannot bear to keep watching/listening/reading.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)Vる（辞書形）＋に＋堪えない聞くに堪えないkiku ni taenai
Noun (＋する動詞)N（＋する、しばしば省略）＋に＋堪えない鑑賞に堪えないkanshou ni taenai
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets processes/actions judged unbearable, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女のバイオリンは、あまりに下手で聞くに堪えない。Kanojo no baiorin wa, amari ni heta de kiku ni taenai. She plays the violin so poorly that we can hardly listen to her. उसकी वायलिन इतनी ख़राब है कि सुनी ही नहीं जाती। તેની વાયોલિન એટલી ખરાબ છે કે સાંભળી જ શકાતી નથી.
昨日の映画は残酷で見るに堪えなかった。Kinou no eiga wa zankoku de miru ni taenakatta. The movie I saw yesterday had many cruel scenes so it wasn't worth watching. कल की फ़िल्म इतनी क्रूर थी कि देखी ही नहीं जा सकती थी। ગઈકાલની ફિલ્મ એટલી ક્રૂર હતી કે જોઈ જ શકાય તેમ નહોતી.

Compare directly with Point 1: 堪えない is simply the negative of 堪える, so while 〜に堪える says something is worth enduring/watching, 〜に堪えない says the opposite — it's unbearable. Same verb, opposite polarity, opposite meaning: read the polarity carefully before answering.

【3】 信頼するに足る (Shinrai suru ni taru)

neutral register
Reading~ni taru
Meaning (EN)"sufficient/worthy enough to ~ / good enough to be relied upon" — a person or thing meets the standard needed for an action such as trust, respect, or reporting
Meaning (HI)...करने योग्य / ...के लायक़ (भरोसा करने, सम्मान करने आदि के लिए पर्याप्त)
Meaning (GU)...કરવા યોગ્ય / ...ને લાયક (વિશ્વાસ કરવા, આદર કરવા માટે પૂરતું)
Connection (接続)Vる（辞書形）＋に足る＋N　／　N（する動詞の名詞）＋に足る＋N
RegisterNeutral — not tagged 硬 in the source, though it reads as somewhat literary/written in practice
Typical use足る is a literary equivalent of 足りる ("to be sufficient/enough"). Usually modifies a following noun (信頼に足る人物 "a person worthy of trust") — this is a sufficiency/adequacy judgment, unlike Points 1–2's endurance/tolerance meaning.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)Vる（辞書形）＋に足る＋N尊敬するに足る（人物）sonkei suru ni taru (jinbutsu)
Noun (＋する動詞)N（＋する、しばしば省略）＋に足る＋N信頼に足る（人物）shinrai ni taru (jinbutsu)
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets actions/qualities judged sufficiently trustworthy, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は、信頼に足る人物だ。何でも相談するといい。Kare wa, shinrai ni taru jinbutsu da. Nandemo soudan suru to ii. He is very trustworthy. You can ask him for advice on many things. वह पूरी तरह भरोसे लायक़ इंसान है। उससे किसी भी बात पर सलाह ली जा सकती है। તે સંપૂર્ણપણે વિશ્વાસપાત્ર વ્યક્તિ છે. તેની સાથે કોઈ પણ બાબતે સલાહ લઈ શકાય છે.
彼女は、その仕事を任せるに足る人です。Kanojo wa, sono shigoto o makaseru ni taru hito desu. You can trust her to do the job well. वह ऐसी व्यक्ति है जिसे यह काम सौंपा जा सकता है। તે એવી વ્યક્તિ છે જેને આ કામ સોંપી શકાય છે.

Sidebar set-phrases: 尊敬するに足る (worthy of respect), 報告するに足る (worth reporting), 調査するに足る (worth investigating). The follow-up noun is often dropped in casual paraphrase but the pattern is fundamentally "Vるに足る＋N" — a sufficiency/worthiness judgment, the opposite semantic family from 耐える／堪える in Points 1–2.

【4】 恐れるに足りない (Osoreru ni tarinai)

neutral register
Reading~ni tarinai
Meaning (EN)"not worth ~ing / no need to ~" — insufficient grounds to warrant an action or feeling (fear, proof, attention)
Meaning (HI)...करने लायक़ नहीं / ...करने की ज़रूरत नहीं (डरने, साबित करने के लिए पर्याप्त नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...કરવા લાયક નથી / ...કરવાની જરૂર નથી (ડરવા, સાબિત કરવા માટે પૂરતું નથી)
Connection (接続)Vる（辞書形）＋に足りない　／　N（する動詞の名詞）＋に足りない
RegisterNeutral — not tagged 硬 in the source, though it reads as somewhat literary/written in practice
Typical useThe exact negative of Point 3 — says something does not meet the bar needed to justify an action or reaction (worrying, proving, mentioning something).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)Vる（辞書形）＋に＋足りない恐れるに足りないosoreru ni tarinai
Noun (＋する動詞)N（＋する、しばしば省略）＋に＋足りない証明するに足りないshoumei suru ni tarinai
Classical variant恐るる（文語連体形）＋に＋足らず（文語否定形）恐るるに足らずosoruru ni tarazu
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets actions/qualities judged insufficient to justify a reaction, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
就職難など、能力がある学生にとっては、恐れるに足りない。Shuushoku-nan nado, nouryoku ga aru gakusei ni totte wa, osoreru ni tarinai. Capable students need not worry about the job shortage. नौकरी की कमी जैसी समस्याएँ, योग्यता रखने वाले छात्रों के लिए डरने लायक़ नहीं हैं। નોકરીની અછત જેવી સમસ્યાઓ, ક્ષમતા ધરાવતા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ માટે ડરવા લાયક નથી.
この証拠だけでは彼が無罪であることを証明するに足りない。Kono shouko dake de wa kare ga muzai de aru koto o shoumei suru ni tarinai. This evidence alone is not good enough to prove his innocence. सिर्फ़ यह सबूत उसके निर्दोष होने को साबित करने के लिए पर्याप्त नहीं है। ફક્ત આ પુરાવો તેની નિર્દોષતા સાબિત કરવા માટે પૂરતો નથી.
取るに足りないうわさToru ni tarinai uwasa a worthless rumor (fixed expression) एक बेकार/महत्वहीन अफ़वाह એક નકામી/મહત્વહીન અફવા

恐るるに足らず is the classical/literary equivalent of 恐れるに足りない, built on the old rentaikei 恐るる and the old negative 足らず — still seen in formal writing and set phrases. Also memorize the very common fixed expression 取るに足りない (＝取り上げるほどの価値がない, "not worth mentioning/bringing up"), which behaves like a な-adjective phrase modifying a following noun (取るに足りない人／こと／うわさ).

Q1

電車の中で酔っ払いが聞く（　）言葉を大声で言っていた。Densha no naka de yopparai ga kiku (___) kotoba o oogoe de itte ita.

Full sentence: 電車の中で酔っ払いが聞くにたえない言葉を大声で言っていた。Kiku ni taenai kotoba o oogoe de itte ita.
EN: A drunk person on the train was loudly saying things that were unbearable to listen to.
HI: ट्रेन में एक शराबी ज़ोर-ज़ोर से ऐसी बातें कह रहा था जो सुनी ही नहीं जा सकती थीं।
GU: ટ્રેનમાં એક દારૂડિયો મોટેથી એવી વાતો બોલી રહ્યો હતો જે સાંભળી જ શકાય તેમ નહોતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにたえないni taenaiunbearable to listen toसुनी ही नहीं जातीસાંભળી જ શકાતી નથી
bにたりないni tarinai(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The drunk person's words are so unpleasant that they cannot be borne — this is Point 2's にたえない (enduring/tolerating something bad). にたりない (Point 4) is about whether something meets a sufficiency standard, which doesn't fit "unbearable words."

Q2

学生生活のいい思い出は、信頼する（　）教師に出会えたということだ。Gakusei seikatsu no ii omoide wa, shinrai suru (___) kyoushi ni deaeta to iu koto da.

Full sentence: 学生生活のいい思い出は、信頼するにたる教師に出会えたということだ。Shinrai suru ni taru kyoushi ni deaeta.

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ はっきりしたことは言えない。___ hakkiri shita koto wa ienai.

Tiles: 1 信ずるに (shinzuru ni)　2 なくして (nakushite)　3 足る (taru)　4 情報 (jouhou)
信ずる is the literary form of 信じる ("to believe/trust") — both mean the same thing.

1 信ずるに→3 足る→4 情報→2 なくして

信ずるに足る情報なくして、はっきりしたことは言えない。Shinzuru ni taru jouhou nakushite, hakkiri shita koto wa ienai.

EN: Without information reliable enough to trust, nothing definite can be said.
HI: भरोसे लायक़ जानकारी के बिना कुछ भी स्पष्ट नहीं कहा जा सकता।
GU: વિશ્વાસપાત્ર માહિતી વગર કંઈ પણ સ્પષ્ટ કહી શકાય નહીં.

Why: Built on Point 3 (Vるに足る＋N) — 信ずるに足る modifies 情報 ("information worthy of trust"), and なくして ("without ~") negates its existence, leading into the conclusion that nothing definite can be said.

Q7

彼女の演技は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ものだった。Kanojo no engi wa ___ mono datta.

Tiles: 1 失敗続きの (shippai tsuzuki no)　2 とても (totemo)　3 たえない (taenai)　4 見るに (miru ni)

1 失敗続きの→2 とても→4 見るに→3 たえない

失敗続きのとても見るにたえないものだった。Shippai tsuzuki no totemo miru ni taenai mono datta.

EN: Her performance was full of continuous mistakes, something truly unbearable to watch.
HI: उसका प्रदर्शन लगातार ग़लतियों से भरा था, बिल्कुल देखा ही नहीं जा सकता था।
GU: તેનું પ્રદર્શન સતત ભૂલોથી ભરેલું હતું, બિલકુલ જોઈ શકાય તેમ નહોતું.

Why: Built on Point 2 (Vるに堪えない) — 見るにたえない ("unbearable to watch") is intensified by とても ("truly/extremely"), describing a performance riddled with continuous mistakes (失敗続きの).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms attach with に after a verb/noun, so N1 loves to mix them up (see Q1, Q3, Q4 above especially). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Vるに耐える／堪える~ni taeru Can withstand ~ / worth ~ing Vる／N＋に＋耐える／堪える 耐える family — about ENDURING something demanding or unpleasant. Positive: bearable, worth it. You want to say something is good/strong enough to hold up under a process (use, viewing, reading).
Vるに堪えない~ni taenai Unbearable / not worth ~ing Vる／N＋に＋堪えない Negative of the row above — the exact opposite meaning. Quality is too poor to bear. You want to say something is so bad it can't be endured (a poor performance, cruel scenes).
Vるに足る~ni taru Sufficient/worthy enough to ~ Vる／N＋に足る＋N 足る family — about MEETING A STANDARD of adequacy, worth, or trust. Positive: good enough. You want to say a person/thing is trustworthy or meets the bar for an action (trust, respect, report).
Vるに足りない~ni tarinai Not worth ~ / no need to ~ Vる／N＋に＋足りない Negative of the row above — insufficient grounds to justify the action/feeling (worry, proof). You want to dismiss something as not meeting the bar (nothing to fear, not enough proof).
Exam trap: 耐える／堪える and 足る／足りない come from two completely different source verbs — 耐える／堪える ("to endure") and 足る ("to be sufficient") — yet both get used in a "Vるに〜" construction, and both can superficially translate into English as "worth ~ing," which is exactly why N1 mixes them up. Before choosing, ask: is this about tolerance for something unpleasant (耐える／堪える — can you bear to watch/listen/use it?) or about meeting a standard of adequacy/trust (足る／足りない — is it good/reliable/worthy enough)? Separately, watch the polarity flip within each family: 堪える (can bear it) vs. 堪えない (can't bear it), and 足る (good enough) vs. 足りない (not good enough) — opposite meanings from the same base word, easy to misread quickly under time pressure.
