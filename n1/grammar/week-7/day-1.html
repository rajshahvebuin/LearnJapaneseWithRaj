Notes
Today's four points are all about emphasis or approximation through repetition/pairing patterns (N1にN2, Vますに Vて, N1やN2, NというN) — none are marked neutral register as 硬 (formal); all are common in everyday speech and writing alike.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 ごはんにお味噌汁 (gohan ni omisoshiru) — N1にN2

neutral register
ReadingN1 ni N2
Meaning (EN)N1 and N2 form a fixed, typical pairing or combination — "N1 with N2" as a set
Meaning (HI)N1 और N2 की एक तय जोड़ी/संयोजन — जैसे कोई सामान्य "सेट"
Meaning (GU)N1 અને N2 ની એક નિશ્ચિત જોડ/સંયોજન — જાણે એક ચોક્કસ "સેટ"
Connection (接続)N1 + に + N2
RegisterNeutral — common in everyday conversation describing typical combos
Typical useDescribing a fixed, habitual pairing of two items (a meal, an outfit) that naturally "go together" as a set — not a random pair, but the expected combination.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + NounN1 + に + N2ごはんにお味噌汁gohan ni omisoshiru
Noun + Noun (alt.)N1 + に + N2ジーンズにTシャツjiinzu ni Tshatsu
い-adj／な-adj／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern requires two paired nouns describing a fixed combination, not a descriptive state or action)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
日本の朝食と言えば、ごはんにお味噌汁でしょう。Nihon no choushoku to ieba, gohan ni omisoshiru deshou. When you talk about Japanese breakfast, rice and miso soup should come into the picture. जापानी नाश्ते की बात करें तो, चावल के साथ मिसो सूप ज़रूर होगा। જાપાની નાસ્તાની વાત કરીએ તો, ભાત સાથે મિસો સૂપ હોવો જ જોઈએ.
私は、家ではたいていジーンズにTシャツを着ています。Watashi wa, ie de wa taitei jiinzu ni Tshatsu o kite imasu. I wear mostly jeans and a T-shirt when I am at home. मैं घर पर ज़्यादातर जींस के साथ टी-शर्ट पहनता हूँ। હું ઘરે મોટે ભાગે જીન્સ સાથે ટી-શર્ટ પહેરું છું.

に here doesn't mark location or time — it links two nouns that are understood as a fixed, expected combination (=の組み合わせ, "the combination of"). Keep this に distinct from Point 3's や, which looks similar in a sentence but signals a rough quantity, not a fixed pair — see the Confusion Pairs table in §3.

【2】 考えに考えて (kangae ni kangaete) — Vますに Vて／Vますに Vた

neutral register
ReadingV-masu ni V-te ／ V-masu ni V-ta
Meaning (EN)Repeating the same verb (ます-stem + に + て／た form) for emphasis — "V-ed and V-ed", doing something intensively or repeatedly
Meaning (HI)एक ही क्रिया को बार-बार दोहराकर ज़ोर देना — "लगातार/बहुत ज़्यादा करते रहे" जैसा भाव
Meaning (GU)એક જ ક્રિયાને વારંવાર દોહરાવીને ભાર મૂકવો — "સતત/ખૂબ જ કર્યું" જેવો ભાવ
Connection (接続)V-ますstem + に + V-て（進行）／ V-ますstem + に + V-た（完了） ◆強調 (same verb repeated)
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useThe same verb is repeated on both sides of に to stress that the action was done intensely, thoroughly, or continuously — commonly with 考える, 売れる, 困る, 悩む, etc.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem + に + て)V-ますstem + に + V-て考えに考えてkangae ni kangaete
Verb (ます-stem + に + ている)V-ますstem + に + V-ている売れに売れているure ni urete iru
Noun (related pattern 〜に〜を重ねて)N + に + N + を重ねて努力に努力を重ねてdoryoku ni doryoku o kasanete
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this repetition pattern targets actions being intensified, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
考えに考えて進学を断念した。Kangae ni kangaete shingaku o dannen shita. I decided not to go to a higher-level school after thinking carefully. बहुत सोच-विचार करने के बाद, मैंने आगे की पढ़ाई न करने का फ़ैसला किया। ખૂબ વિચાર્યા પછી, મેં આગળ ભણવાનું માંડી વાળવાનો નિર્ણય લીધો.
不景気だというのに、高級時計が売れに売れているという。Fukeiki da to iu noni, koukyuu tokei ga ure ni urete iru to iu. Despite the bad economy, I hear expensive watches are selling like hotcakes. मंदी के बावजूद, सुना है कि महंगी घड़ियाँ धड़ल्ले से बिक रही हैं। મંદી હોવા છતાં, સાંભળ્યું છે કે મોંઘી ઘડિયાળો ધૂમ વેચાઈ રહી છે.
彼女は、嘘に嘘を重ねて男からお金を引き出していた。Kanojo wa, uso ni uso o kasanete otoko kara okane o hikidashite ita. She used numerous lies to pressure him to give her money. (もっと！ bonus example) वह लगातार झूठ पर झूठ बोलकर उस आदमी से पैसे ऐंठ रही थी। તે સતત જૂઠ પર જૂઠ બોલીને તે માણસ પાસેથી પૈસા પડાવી રહી હતી.

Related box note: the book also flags a noun-based sibling of this pattern, N に N を重ねて (e.g. 研究に研究を重ねて "piling research upon research", 我慢に我慢を重ねて "piling patience upon patience", and this day's own 嘘に嘘を重ねて above). It's the same "repeat-the-same-word-twice-for-emphasis" idea as Vますに Vて, just built on a noun + 重ねる instead of a verb repeated directly — worth flagging now since 練習Ⅰ Q3 and 練習Ⅱ Q6 below both test this noun-based variant, and it directly echoes this week's own title 努力に努力を重ねている.

【3】 一人や二人 (hitori ya futari) — N1やN2

neutral register
ReadingN1 ya N2
Meaning (EN)"one or two / X or Y" — a rough, approximate quantity expressed with two consecutive numbers
Meaning (HI)"एक-दो / X या Y" — दो लगातार संख्याओं से एक अनुमानित मात्रा बताना
Meaning (GU)"એક-બે / X કે Y" — બે ક્રમિક સંખ્યાઓ વડે અંદાજિત જથ્થો દર્શાવવો
Connection (接続)quantity-N1 + や + quantity-N2 ◆Nは数量を表す言葉 (N is a quantity word; +の when modifying a following noun)
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useAttaches to a quantity word (people, years, amounts, counters) to say "just one or two ~" — almost always followed by a claim that this small/rough amount is unremarkable, common, or not enough.
Word typeFormationExample
Quantity-N + や + Quantity-N (as subject)N1 + や + N2一人や二人hitori ya futari
Quantity-N + や + Quantity-N + の + N (modifying)N1 + や + N2 + の + N1万や2万のお金ichiman ya niman no okane
い-adj／な-adj／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern requires a quantity-expressing noun/counter, not a descriptive state or action)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
どこの職場にも、意地の悪い先輩が一人や二人いる。Doko no shokuba ni mo, iji no warui senpai ga hitori ya futari iru. No matter where you work, you'll have to deal with one or two mean senior staff members. कहीं भी काम करो, एक-दो बदमिज़ाज सीनियर तो मिल ही जाते हैं। ગમે ત્યાં કામ કરો, એક-બે ખરાબ સ્વભાવના સિનિયર તો મળી જ જાય છે.
一年や二年働いても、たいして貯金はできない。Ichinen ya ninen hataraite mo, taishite chokin wa dekinai. You won't be able to save much money even if you work for one or two years. एक-दो साल काम करने से भी ज़्यादा बचत नहीं हो पाती। એક-બે વર્ષ કામ કરવાથી પણ ખાસ બચત થતી નથી.

The sidebar box also gives 3つや4つの子ども (mittsu ya yottsu no kodomo, "3 or 4 children") as another modifying-の example alongside 1万や2万のお金. Don't confuse this や with the fixed-pairing に of Point 1 — や here signals "roughly this many," not "these two specific things go together." See the Confusion Pairs table in §3.

【4】 壁という壁 (kabe to iu kabe) — NというN

neutral register
ReadingN to iu N
Meaning (EN)Repeating the same noun with という for emphasis — "every single N, without exception"
Meaning (HI)एक ही संज्ञा को というके साथ दोहराकर ज़ोर देना — "हर एक N, बिना किसी अपवाद के"
Meaning (GU)એક જ સંજ્ઞાને というસાથે દોહરાવીને ભાર મૂકવો — "દરેકેદરેક N, કોઈ અપવાદ વગર"
Connection (接続)N + という + N ◆強調 (same noun repeated) ⊙今度という今度 (=今度は絶対, "this time, for sure")
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useAttaches to a countable/enumerable noun to stress totality — "every single N" — often reinforced with すべて／全部 right after for extra emphasis (see 練習Ⅱ Q7 below).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + という + N壁という壁kabe to iu kabe
Noun (fixed idiom)N + という + N (fixed set phrase)今度という今度kondo to iu kondo
い-adj／な-adj／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern requires a countable/enumerable noun to stress totality across many instances, not a descriptive state or action)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
息子の部屋は壁という壁に車のポスターが貼ってある。Musuko no heya wa kabe to iu kabe ni kuruma no posutaa ga hatte aru. The walls of my son's room are covered with car posters. बेटे के कमरे की हर एक दीवार पर कार के पोस्टर चिपके हुए हैं। દીકરાના રૂમની દરેકેદરેક દીવાલ પર કારના પોસ્ટર ચોંટાડેલા છે.
チラシをこの地域の家という家に配って歩いた。Chirashi o kono chiiki no ie to iu ie ni kubatte aruita. I delivered flyers to every single house in this area. मैं इस इलाक़े के हर एक घर में पर्चे बाँटता हुआ घूमा। હું આ વિસ્તારના દરેકેદરેક ઘરે પત્રિકાઓ વહેંચતો ફર્યો.
今日という今日は、この仕事をやり遂げなければならない。Kyou to iu kyou wa, kono shigoto o yaritogenakereba naranai. I must finish the work today! आज तो आज ही, मुझे यह काम हर हाल में पूरा करना ही है! આજે તો આજે જ, મારે આ કામ ગમે તે ભોગે પૂરું કરવું જ છે!

今日という今日 is a fixed idiomatic use where the repeated noun isn't about physical totality (there's only one "today") but about resolve — "today, for certain, without fail," parallel to 今度という今度 (=今度は絶対). Compare this noun-repetition emphasis directly against Point 2's verb-repetition emphasis in the Confusion Pairs table below.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにni(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bというto iuevery single (window)हर एक (खिड़की)દરેકેદરેક (બારી)

Q2

（　）練習でギターは弾けるようにならないよ。(___) renshuu de gitaa wa hikeru you ni naranai yo.

GU: ફક્ત એક-બે વાર પ્રેક્ટિસ કરવાથી ગિટાર વગાડતા નહીં આવડે.

Q3

彼は（　）を重ねて、念願の弁護士になった。Kare wa (___) o kasanete, gannen no bengoshi ni natta.

Full sentence: 彼は努力に努力を重ねて、念願の弁護士になった。Kare wa doryoku ni doryoku o kasanete, gannen no bengoshi ni natta.
EN: He piled effort upon effort and became the lawyer he had long dreamed of being.
HI: उसने लगातार कड़ी मेहनत करते हुए, आख़िरकार वह वकील बन गया जिसका सपना उसने बरसों से देखा था।
GU: તેણે સતત મહેનત પર મહેનત કરીને, છેવટે તે વકીલ બની ગયો જેનું સપનું તેણે વર્ષોથી જોયું હતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a努力に努力doryoku ni doryokueffort upon effortमेहनत पर मेहनतમહેનત પર મહેનત
b努力するに努力doryoku suru ni doryoku(wrong form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This is Point 2's noun-based sibling pattern N に N を重ねて — it needs the bare noun repeated (努力に努力), not a conjugated verb form. Inserting する (努力するに努力) breaks the fixed N+に+N shape. Note the direct callback: this exact phrase 努力に努力を重ねて mirrors this week's own title, 努力に努力を重ねている.

Q4

EN: My daughter seemed to be hungry, and ate and ate.
HI: बेटी को शायद बहुत भूख लगी थी, वह खाती ही चली गई।
GU: દીકરીને કદાચ ખૂબ ભૂખ લાગી હતી, તે ખાધે જ રાખતી હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a食べるtaberu(wrong form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

HI: मेरा नाश्ता हमेशा कॉफ़ी के साथ ब्रेड होता है।
GU: મારો નાસ્તો હંમેશા કૉફી સાથે બ્રેડ હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aでde(wrong particle here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q6

彼は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ピアニストになった。Kare wa ___ pianisuto ni natta.

Tiles: 1 練習を (renshuu o)　2 ついに (tsui ni)　3 練習に (renshuu ni)　4 重ねて (kasanete)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores/numbers rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format — same convention as other weeks.)

3 練習に→1 練習を→4 重ねて→2 ついに

彼は練習に練習を重ねて、ついにピアニストになった。Kare wa renshuu ni renshuu o kasanete, tsui ni pianisuto ni natta.

EN: He practiced and practiced, and finally became a pianist.
HI: उसने लगातार अभ्यास पर अभ्यास किया, और आख़िरकार पियानोवादक बन गया।
GU: તેણે સતત પ્રેક્ટિસ પર પ્રેક્ટિસ કરી, અને છેવટે પિયાનોવાદક બની ગયો.

Why: Built on Point 2's noun-based sibling pattern N に N を重ねて (see Q3 above and this week's own title) — 練習に練習を重ねて emphasizes repeated, intense practice before ついに ("finally") delivers the result.

Q7

4 学校の規則をよそに→1 爪という→3 爪→2 すべて

彼女は学校の規則をよそに、爪という爪すべてを真っ赤に塗っていた。Kanojo wa gakkou no kisoku o yoso ni, tsume to iu tsume subete o makka ni nutte ita.

EN: Ignoring school rules, she painted every single nail bright red.
HI: वह स्कूल के नियमों की परवाह किए बिना, अपने हर एक नाखून को गहरे लाल रंग से रंग रही थी।
GU: તે શાળાના નિયમોની પરવા કર્યા વગર, પોતાના દરેકેદરેક નખને ઘાટા લાલ રંગે રંગી રહી હતી.

Why: Built on Point 4 (NというN) — 爪という爪 means "every single nail," and すべて ("all") right after doubles down on the same totality emphasis, showing how the two devices stack together.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's patterns lean on repetition or pairing to make a point, which is exactly why N1 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q2, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
N1にN2N1 ni N2 A fixed, typical pairing of two items N1 + に + N2 に links two different nouns as an expected combination/set (rice+miso soup, jeans+T-shirt) — not location/time, not a quantity. Describing a habitual pairing that naturally "goes together."
Vますに Vて／たV-masu ni V-te/ta Repeat the same verb for emphasis ("thought and thought") V-ますstem + に + V-て／た (same verb) Emphasizes intensity/repetition of a single action. Its noun-based sibling, N に N を重ねて, sits between this and NというN below. Stressing an action was done intensely or continuously (考えに考えて, 売れに売れる).
N1やN2N1 ya N2 An approximate/rough count ("one or two") quantity-N1 + や + quantity-N2 (+の if modifying a noun) や signals imprecision about a quantity using two consecutive numbers — not a fixed pairing of two different items, despite occupying a similar slot to Point 1's に. Giving a rough, small estimate of an amount (一人や二人, 1万や2万).
NというNN to iu N Repeat the same noun for emphasis ("every single N") N + という + N (same noun) Emphasizes totality/exhaustiveness across a set of objects via noun repetition — the noun-repeating sibling of Vますに Vて's verb repetition. Stressing "all of them, no exceptions" (壁という壁, 窓という窓).
Exam trap: N1にN2 (a fixed pairing, e.g. ごはんにお味噌汁) and N1やN2 (a rough quantity, e.g. 一人や二人) can look superficially similar — both drop a particle between two short noun-like phrases — but に marks a set/pairing while や marks an approximate count; check whether what follows the particle is a different item (Point 1) or the next number in a count (Point 3) before picking one (see Q5 vs Q2 above). Separately, Vますに Vて (Point 2) and NというN (Point 4) share the same emphatic-repetition idea, but one repeats a verb and the other repeats a noun — and N に N を重ねて (seen in Point 2's bonus example and tested directly in Q3/Q6) sits in between as a noun-based version of the verb-repetition family, not the NというN family. Don't mix up "repeat a noun to mean every one of them" (NというN) with "repeat a noun with を重ねて to mean piled up more and more" (NにNを重ねて) — they look alike but emphasize totality vs. accumulation respectively.
