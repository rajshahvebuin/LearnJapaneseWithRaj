Notes
Today is unusually colloquial/spoken-register-heavy for N1 grammar — 3 of the 4 points (Points 2–4) are casual speech patterns, marked with a speech-bubble icon in the book indicating spoken/colloquial register. The day's key exam trap is the negation-vs-intensification confusion between 〜くもなんともない (flatly negates a quality — "not X at all") and 〜（とい）ったらない (intensifies a quality to an extreme — "indescribably X"); see §3 below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜てみせる

neutral register
Reading~te miseru
Meaning (EN)"I'll do ~ and show you / do ~ so you can see" — either a strong resolve to demonstrably achieve something, or literally performing an action for someone to watch
Meaning (HI)...करके दिखाऊँगा (दृढ़ संकल्प) / ...करके दिखाना (सामने करके दिखाना)
Meaning (GU)...કરીને બતાવીશ (દૃઢ સંકલ્પ) / ...કરીને બતાવવું (સામે કરી બતાવવું)
Connection (接続)V-て form + みせる
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useSense 1: a strong promise/resolve — "I'll do X, just watch me prove it" (合格してみせる). Sense 2: literally carrying out an action so another person can see/hear the result (弾いてみせて — "play it for me to hear").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-て form + みせる合格してみせるgoukaku shite miseru
Verb (alt.)V-て form + みせて (request to perform)弾いてみせてhiite misete
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — てみせる requires a performable action, not a descriptive state)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今度こそ合格してみせるよ。Kondo koso goukaku shite miseru yo. This time I'll definitely pass the exam — just watch, I'll prove it. इस बार तो मैं ज़रूर परीक्षा पास करके दिखाऊँगा। આ વખતે તો હું ચોક્કસ પરીક્ષા પાસ કરીને બતાવીશ.
ピアノ、うまくなったね。今の曲、もう一回弾いてみせて。Piano, umaku natta ne. Ima no kyoku, mou ikkai hiite misete. You play the piano so much better now. Can you play the piece you just played again for me? पियानो में तो तुम बहुत अच्छे हो गए हो। अभी वाला गाना एक बार फिर बजाकर दिखाओ। પિયાનોમાં તો તું ઘણો સારો થઈ ગયો છે. હમણાંનું ગીત ફરી એકવાર વગાડીને બતાવ.

Don't collapse 〜てみせる into a single meaning — check which sense fits. In 合格してみせる, みせる is figurative: there's no literal audience, it's a declaration of resolve ("I will make this happen, you'll see"). In 弾いてみせて, みせる is literal: the speaker wants to actually watch/hear the piano being played. This point stands apart from the rest of today's set — Points 2–4 are all negation/intensification patterns, while Vてみせる is a positive resolve/demonstration pattern.

【2】 〜くもなんともない

colloquial/spoken
Reading~ku mo nan to mo nai / ~de mo nan de mo nai
Meaning (EN)"not ~ at all / not even slightly ~" — an emphatic, flat denial that a quality applies
Meaning (HI)बिल्कुल भी ...नहीं / ज़रा भी ...नहीं
Meaning (GU)જરાય ...નથી / બિલકુલ ...નથી
Connection (接続)い-adj stem + くも + なんともない ／ な-adj／N + でも + なんでもない
RegisterDistinctly casual/spoken — this is everyday conversational Japanese, common in daily speech but not something you'd use in formal writing.
Typical useEmphatically shooting down an assumption or claim someone else made about a quality/feeling — "you think X, but actually not X at all."
Word typeFormationExample
Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — the pattern negates adjectival states via くも／でも, not verb stems directly)—
い-adjectiveい-adj stem + くも + なんともない痛くもなんともないitaku mo nan to mo nai
な-adjectiveな-adj + でも + なんでもない好きでもなんでもないsuki de mo nan de mo nai
NounN + でも + なんでもない苦労でもなんでもないkurou de mo nan de mo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
歯医者「虫歯がありますよ。」患者「そうですか。まだ痛くもなんともないんですが……。」Haisha: "Mushiba ga arimasu yo." Kanja: "Sou desu ka. Mada itaku mo nan to mo nai n desu ga..." Dentist: "You've got a cavity." Patient: "Oh, yes? I don't feel it at all, though..." दंत चिकित्सक: "आपके दांत में कीड़ा लगा है।" मरीज़: "अच्छा? पर अभी तो बिल्कुल भी दर्द नहीं हो रहा..." દંત ચિકિત્સક: "તમારા દાંતમાં સડો છે." દર્દી: "અરે? પણ હજુ તો જરાય દુખાવો નથી થતો..."
彼のこと、好きでもなんでもないけれど、なぜか気にかかる。Kare no koto, suki de mo nan de mo nai keredo, nazeka ki ni kakaru. I don't really like him but there is something about him I can't ignore — I just don't know why. मुझे उससे कोई ख़ास लगाव नहीं है, फिर भी न जाने क्यों मेरा ध्यान उसकी तरफ़ चला जाता है। મને તેના પ્રત્યે કંઈ ખાસ લગાવ નથી, છતાં ના જાણે કેમ મારું ધ્યાન તેના તરફ જાય છે.

なんとも strengthens the negation the way "at all" does in English — 痛くもなんともない is stronger than plain 痛くない. Note the sidebar's ◆強調 (emphasis) marking: ほしくもなんともない ("don't want it at all") and 苦労でもなんでもない ("no trouble at all") follow the exact same pattern. This point flattens/negates a quality — the opposite job of Point 3 below, which intensifies a quality. Don't confuse the two.

【3】 〜（とい）ったらない

colloquial/spoken
Reading~(to it)tara nai / ~arihashinai / ~aryashinai
Meaning (EN)"indescribably ~ / ~ beyond words" — intensifies a quality or state to an extreme, whether positive or negative
Meaning (HI)बेहद ...जो बयान नहीं किया जा सकता / अत्यंत ...
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત ...જે શબ્દોમાં કહી ના શકાય
Connection (接続)い-adj／な-adj／V／N + (と) + いったらない／ありはしない／ありゃしない
RegisterDistinctly casual/spoken — common in daily conversation and exclamatory complaints, rare in formal writing.
Typical usePushes an adjective, verb-derived state, or noun to an extreme degree — frequently used for complaints (boring, exhausting, infuriating) but also for extreme praise (beautiful).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-plain + (と)いったらない腹が立つ（とい）ったらないhara ga tatsu (to it)tara nai
い-adjectiveい-adj + (と)いったらない／ありゃしないつまらないといったらないtsumaranai to ittara nai
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + (と)いったらない不便といったらないfuben to ittara nai
NounN + (と)いったらない美しさ（とい）ったらないutsukushisa (to it)tara nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
昨日見た映画は、つまらないといったらなかった。Kinou mita eiga wa, tsumaranai to ittara nakatta. The movie I saw yesterday was just too boring, beyond words. कल जो फ़िल्म देखी थी, वह बेहद उबाऊ थी, बयान नहीं कर सकता। ગઈકાલે જોયેલી ફિલ્મ એટલી કંટાળાજનક હતી કે શબ્દોમાં ના કહી શકાય.
今日は12時間も仕事をした。疲れたといったらありゃしない。Kyou wa juu-ni-jikan mo shigoto o shita. Tsukareta to ittara aryashinai. I worked for 12 hours today. I'm just so exhausted, words can't describe it. आज बारह घंटे काम किया। इतना थक गया हूँ कि बयान नहीं कर सकता। આજે બાર કલાક કામ કર્યું. એટલો થાકી ગયો છું કે શબ્દોમાં કહી શકાય નહીં.

The sidebar's ◆VやNでも使えるものがある ("some usages also work with verbs and nouns") is key — this pattern is not limited to い-adjectives: 腹が立つ(とい)ったらない ("indescribably infuriating"), 喉が渇く(とい)ったらない ("indescribably thirsty"), and 美しさ(とい)ったらない ("indescribably beautiful") all follow it. Unlike Point 2, this pattern amplifies a quality rather than negating it — and it can go in either direction, negative (つまらない, 疲れた) or positive (美しさ).

【4】 〜やしない／ゃしない

colloquial/spoken
Reading~ya shinai / ~ya shinai (contracted)
Meaning (EN)"definitely doesn't/won't ~ at all" — a colloquial contraction of 〜はしない, a strong negation of an action
Meaning (HI)बिल्कुल भी नहीं ...करता / ...होगा ही नहीं
Meaning (GU)જરાય નથી ...કરતું
Connection (接続)V-ます stem + や／ゃ + しない  ◆「〜はしない」の口語形
RegisterDistinctly casual/spoken — a colloquial contraction of 〜はしない, natural in daily conversation but not used in formal writing.
Typical useEmphatically states that an action definitely will not/does not happen — stronger and more casual than plain 〜ない.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます stem + や + しない聞きやしないkikiyashinai
Verb (r-stem contraction)V-ます stem (r-sound) + ゃ + しないわかりゃしないwakaryashinai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern negates verb actions, not adjectival states; compare Point 2 for that job)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近、息子は私の言うことなど、聞きやしない。Saikin, musuko wa watashi no iu koto nado, kikiyashinai. Recently my son never listens to what I say at all. आजकल, मेरा बेटा मेरी बात बिल्कुल भी नहीं सुनता। આજકાલ, મારો દીકરો મારી વાત જરાય નથી સાંભળતો.
ドッグフードを変えたら、おいしくないのか、食べやしない。Doggu fuudo o kaetara, oishikunai no ka, tabeyashinai. I've changed my dog's food but he hasn't been eating it at all — maybe it doesn't taste good. डॉग फ़ूड बदला तो, शायद स्वादिष्ट नहीं लगा, बिल्कुल नहीं खा रहा। ડોગ ફૂડ બદલ્યું તો, કદાચ સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી લાગતું, જરાય નથી ખાતો.
「ああ、スカートにシミがついちゃった……。」「大丈夫だよ。誰もわかりゃしないよ。」"Aa, sukaato ni shimi ga tsuichatta..." "Daijoubu da yo. Daremo wakaryashinai yo." "Oh, I got a stain on my skirt..." "Don't worry! Nobody will notice at all!" "अरे, स्कर्ट पर दाग लग गया..." "कोई बात नहीं! किसी को पता ही नहीं चलेगा!" "અરે, સ્કર્ટ પર ડાઘ પડી ગયો..." "કંઈ વાંધો નહીં! કોઈને ખબર જ નહીં પડે!"

〜やしない is the everyday contraction of 〜はしない (聞きはしない → 聞きやしない). After stems ending in an "r" mora such as わかり, it further contracts to 〜ゃしない (わかりはしない → わかりゃしない). Both contractions are purely spoken-style — never use them in formal writing.

Q3

Why — this is the day's classic trap: The context (a parent horrified their own child committed a crime) calls for Point 3's intensifier といったらない — "indescribably pathetic." Always check whether the sentence wants a quality amplified (Point 3) or flatly denied (Point 2).

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

それを知って相当＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ といったらなかった。Sore o shitte soutou ___ to ittara nakatta.

Tiles: 1 らしく (rashiku)　2 彼の (kare no)　3 ショックだった (shokku datta)　4 あわてよう (awateyou)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores/numbers rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

2 彼の→4 あわてよう→1 らしく→3 ショックだった

それを知って相当彼のあわてようらしく、ショックだったといったらなかった。Sore o shitte soutou kare no awateyou rashiku, shokku datta to ittara nakatta.

EN: Judging by how flustered he was, he seemed to be shocked beyond words upon learning that.
HI: यह जानकर वह जिस तरह घबरा गया, उससे लगा कि उसे बेहद सदमा लगा था, बयान नहीं कर सकता।
GU: આ જાણીને તે જે રીતે ગભરાઈ ગયો, તેના પરથી લાગ્યું કે તેને અત્યંત આઘાત લાગ્યો હતો, શબ્દોમાં કહી શકાય નહીં.

Why: 彼のあわてよう uses the "manner noun" 〜よう form of the verb 慌てる (あわてる, "to panic") — 彼のあわてよう literally means "the way/manner he panicked." らしく here means "in a manner that seems/fits" (not the hearsay らしい). The whole thing feeds into Point 3's といったらない, intensifying ショックだった ("was shocked") to an extreme, indescribable degree.

Q7

先日の試合は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 点に開きがあった。Senjitsu no shiai wa, ___ ten ni hiraki ga atta.

Tiles: 1 なんともない (nan to mo nai)　2 惜しくも (oshiku mo)　3 負けても (makete mo)　4 くらい (kurai)
(Note: again marked with plain underscores/numbers rather than a single ★ blank.)

3 負けても→2 惜しくも→1 なんともない→4 くらい

先日の試合は、負けても惜しくもなんともないくらい点に開きがあった。Senjitsu no shiai wa, makete mo oshiku mo nan to mo nai kurai ten ni hiraki ga atta.

EN: In the recent match, the score gap was so wide that even losing wouldn't have been the least bit regrettable.
HI: पिछले मैच में स्कोर का अंतर इतना ज़्यादा था कि हार जाने पर भी ज़रा भी अफ़सोस नहीं होता।
GU: ગયા મેચમાં સ્કોરનો તફાવત એટલો મોટો હતો કે હારી જવા છતાં જરાય અફસોસ ના થાય.

Why: 惜しくもなんともない is Point 2's い-adjective pattern (惜しい "regrettable" + くもなんともない) — flatly denying any regret at all, even if they lost (負けても). くらい then expresses degree ("to the point that..."), describing just how lopsided the score gap was.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split into two families that look superficially alike but do opposite jobs, plus one point that stands entirely apart. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜てみせる~te miseru Do ~ and show/prove it V-て + みせる Positive resolve or literal demonstration. Stands apart from the negation/intensification family below. You want to declare a promise ("I'll do X, watch me") or ask someone to perform something visibly.
〜くもなんともない~ku mo nan to mo nai Not ~ at all い-adj stem/な-adj/N + くも／でも + なん（と／で）もない Flatly negates/downplays a quality — the opposite job of といったらない. You want to emphatically deny a quality someone assumed applies.
〜（とい）ったらない~(to it)tara nai Indescribably ~ い-adj／な-adj／V／N + (と)いったらない／ありゃしない Intensifies a quality to an extreme (positive or negative) — the opposite job of くもなんともない. You want to say a quality is so extreme it can't be put into words.
〜やしない／ゃしない~ya shinai Definitely doesn't/won't ~ Vます stem + や／ゃ + しない Colloquial contraction of 〜はしない — negates a verb action, not an adjectival state (compare くもなんともない, which negates states). You want a strong, casual negation of something someone does or will do.
Exam trap: 〜くもなんともない and 〜（とい）ったらない look alike — both are casual, both attach to adjectives/na-adjectives/nouns, both feel emphatic — but they do opposite jobs: くもなんともない flattens/negates a quality ("not X at all"), while （とい）ったらない amplifies a quality to an extreme ("indescribably X"). Q3 above is the clearest illustration: 情けないといったらない ("indescribably pathetic") is correct for a parent horrified by their child's crime, while 情けなくもなんともない ("not pathetic at all") would flatly contradict the context. Always ask first: is this sentence trying to deny a quality, or push it to an extreme? Separately, don't confuse 〜やしない (negates verb actions) with 〜くもなんともない (negates adjective/noun states) — check what's being negated, an action or a state, before choosing. And keep 〜てみせる filed apart from all three — it's the day's only positive/resolve pattern.
