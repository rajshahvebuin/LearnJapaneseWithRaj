3日目 — 仕事ぶり (Shigoto-buri)

Grammar points covered
秋めく大人びる金持ちぶる仕事ぶり
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.117 (4日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
めく／びる／ぶる look deceptively similar and all attach to a noun/adjective stem, but they sit on a spectrum: めく = genuinely starting to take on a quality, びる = taking on an appearance (often through age/growth), ぶる = deliberately faking a persona. This three-way distinction is the day's central trap — see §3. None of today's four points are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; all are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 秋めく

neutral register
Readingakimeku
Meaning (EN)"genuinely starts to feel/seem like ~ / takes on the air of ~" — a real, gradual change (a season arriving, an impression forming)
Meaning (HI)धीरे-धीरे सचमुच ...जैसा महसूस होने लगना / ...का एहसास होने लगना
Meaning (GU)ધીમે ધીમે ખરેખર ...જેવું લાગવા લાગવું / ...નો અહેસાસ થવા લાગવો
Connection (接続)N + めく／めいて／めいた + N
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useDescribes a genuine, gradual change — most often a season "arriving" (秋めく), or the sense that something really carries a certain impression (mysterious, sarcastic, secretive), usually as めいた + noun.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (dictionary form)N + めく秋めくakimeku
Noun (て-form)N + めいて秋めいてきましたねakimeite kimashita ne
Noun (attributive)N + めいた + N謎めいた女性nazo meita josei、皮肉めいた言葉hiniku meita kotoba、秘密めいた手紙himitsu meita tegami
い-adj／な-adj／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — めく attaches only to nouns describing a quality, mood, or season)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
涼しくなって、ずいぶん秋めいてきましたね。Suzushiku natte, zuibun akimeite kimashita ne. It's getting cool and the weather has become very autumnal. ठंडक बढ़ गई है और मौसम अब काफ़ी पतझड़ जैसा लगने लगा है। ઠંડક વધી ગઈ છે અને હવામાન હવે ખૂબ પાનખર જેવું લાગવા લાગ્યું છે.
別れた恋人から、脅迫めいたメールが届いて、怖くなった。Wakareta koibito kara, kyouhaku meita meeru ga todoite, kowaku natta. I was scared when my ex-boyfriend texted me and it seemed to try to blackmail me. पूर्व प्रेमी से एक धमकी जैसा लगने वाला मेल आया, तो मुझे डर लगने लगा। ભૂતપૂર્વ પ્રેમી તરફથી ધમકી જેવો લાગતો મેલ આવ્યો, એટલે મને ડર લાગવા લાગ્યો.

めく is unique among today's points because it describes something genuinely, gradually taking on a real quality — a season actually arriving, a feeling that's really there — with no pretense involved. Contrast this with びる (a natural appearance) and ぶる (a deliberate act) below; see §3 for the full three-way comparison.

【2】 大人びる

neutral register
Readingotonabiru
Meaning (EN)"takes on the look/appearance of ~ over time" — used especially for people appearing more grown/mature, or things appearing aged/worn
Meaning (HI)समय के साथ ...जैसा दिखने/लगने लगना — ख़ासकर उम्र या परिपक्वता के हिसाब से
Meaning (GU)સમય જતાં ...જેવું દેખાવા લાગવું — ખાસ કરીને ઉંમર કે પરિપક્વતાની દૃષ્ટિએ
Connection (接続)N + びる／びて〜／びた + N  irregular: 悪びる→悪びれる
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribes the visible/felt appearance a person or thing has taken on through a natural process — growing up (大人びる) or aging/wear (古びる). No pretense implied.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (dictionary form)N + びる大人びるotonabiru
Noun (て-form)N + びて〜大人びてきましたねotonabite kimashita ne
Noun (attributive た)N + びた + N古びたコインfurubita koin
Irregular exception悪びる conjugates irregularly as 悪びれる, never 悪びた悪びれる様子もなくwarubireru yousu mo naku
い-adj／な-adj／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — びる attaches only to nouns describing age, maturity, or appearance)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
和子ちゃん、大きくなってずいぶん大人びてきましたね。Kazuko-chan, ookiku natte zuibun otonabite kimashita ne. Kazuko! You've grown so much and look so grown-up. काज़ुको, तुम कितनी बड़ी हो गई हो, अब तो बिल्कुल बड़ों जैसी दिखने लगी हो। કાઝુકો, તું કેટલી મોટી થઈ ગઈ છે, હવે તો સાવ મોટા માણસ જેવી દેખાવા લાગી છે.
男は、悪びれる様子もなく、取り調べに応じている。Otoko wa, warubireru yousu mo naku, torishirabe ni oujite iru. もっと! bonus The police are questioning him but he is showing no remorse for what he did. पुलिस उससे पूछताछ कर रही है, लेकिन वह ज़रा भी पछतावा दिखाए बिना जवाब दे रहा है। પોલીસ તેની પૂછપરછ કરી રહી છે, પણ તે જરાય પસ્તાવો બતાવ્યા વગર જવાબ આપી રહ્યો છે.

びる describes how something appears after a natural process (growing up, aging) — it never implies pretense, unlike ぶる in Point 3. Watch the irregular verb 悪びる, which conjugates as 悪びれる (never 悪びた) — a trap in its own right.

【3】 金持ちぶる

neutral register
Readingkanemochi buru
Meaning (EN)"acts like / puts on the airs of ~" — deliberately, often falsely, presenting oneself as a certain type of person
Meaning (HI)जान-बूझकर ...होने का दिखावा करना / ...जैसा बनने का स्वांग रचना
Meaning (GU)જાણી જોઈને ...હોવાનો ડોળ કરવો / ...જેવા બનવાનો દેખાડો કરવો
Connection (接続)な-adj／N + ぶる／ぶって／ぶった + N
RegisterNeutral
Typical useCriticizing or describing someone consciously performing a persona (acting rich, acting kind, acting like a scholar/senior) that may not be genuine.
Word typeFormationExample
な-adjective／Noun (dictionary)な/N + ぶる金持ちぶるkanemochi buru
な-adjective／Noun (て-form)な/N + ぶって親切ぶってshinsetsu butte
な-adjective／Noun (attributive た)な/N + ぶった + Nかわい子ぶった口のきき方kawaiko butta kuchi no kikikata
Additional book examplesな/N + ぶる学者ぶるgakusha buru、先輩ぶるsenpai buru
Verb／い-adjective(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ぶる attaches to な-adjective/noun stems describing a type of person or quality, not verbs or い-adjectives)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は金持ちぶっているが、本当は借金がたくさんある。Kare wa kanemochi butte iru ga, hontou wa shakkin ga takusan aru. Though he pretends to be rich, in reality he is in serious debt. वह अमीर होने का दिखावा करता है, लेकिन असल में उस पर बहुत क़र्ज़ है। તે અમીર હોવાનો દેખાડો કરે છે, પણ ખરેખર તેના પર ઘણું દેવું છે.
彼女はお酒に酔うと、かわい子ぶった口のきき方をする。Kanojo wa osake ni you to, kawaiko butta kuchi no kikikata o suru. She talks like a little girl once she gets drunk. वह जब शराब पीकर नशे में होती है, तो जान-बूझकर मासूम बच्ची जैसी बातें करने लगती है। તે દારૂ પીને નશામાં હોય ત્યારે જાણી જોઈને ભોળી છોકરી જેવી વાતો કરવા લાગે છે.
息子は悪ぶっているが、実は気の弱い優しい子です。Musuko wa waru butte iru ga, jitsu wa ki no yowai yasashii ko desu. My son acts tough, but he is actually quite weak and gentle. मेरा बेटा ऊपर से बदमाश बनने का दिखावा करता है, लेकिन असल में वह बहुत कमज़ोर दिल का और नरम स्वभाव का बच्चा है। મારો દીકરો ઉપરથી બદમાશ હોવાનો દેખાડો કરે છે, પણ ખરેખર તે ખૂબ નબળા મનનો અને નમ્ર સ્વભાવનો છોકરો છે.

ぶる always implies a conscious, often false performance — the person is not simply becoming or looking like something (that's めく／びる), they are actively acting the part. This is the day's central three-way exam trap; see §3.

【4】 仕事ぶり

neutral register
Readingshigoto buri
Meaning (EN)"the manner/way in which ~ is done" — describes the style, condition, or degree of how someone performs an action
Meaning (HI)किसी काम को करने का ढंग/तरीक़ा
Meaning (GU)કોઈ કામ કરવાની રીત/ઢબ
Connection (接続)N + ぶり／Vます-stem + っぷり っぷり = casual/spoken variant
Registerぶり is neutral/written; っぷり is its casual, spoken counterpart — identical meaning
Typical useDescribing how someone works, speaks, eats, drinks, etc. — ぶり fits reports and writing, っぷり fits casual conversation.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ぶり仕事ぶりshigoto buri
Noun (book examples)N + ぶり活躍ぶりkatsuyaku buri、慌てぶりawate buri
Verb (ます-stem, casual)Vます-stem + っぷり食べっぷりtabeppuri、走りっぷりhashiripuri casual/spoken
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ぶり／っぷり attaches to nouns or verb stems describing an action/manner, not adjectives)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先輩の仕事ぶりを見ながら要領を覚えよう。Senpai no shigoto buri o minagara youryou o oboeyou. You should learn the ropes by observing how your more experienced colleagues are working. वरिष्ठ साथियों के काम करने के तरीक़े को देखते हुए काम की बारीकियाँ सीख लो। વરિષ્ઠ સાથીદારોની કામ કરવાની રીત જોઈને કામની બારીકાઈઓ શીખી લો.
社長の話しぶりでは、今年のボーナスはないようだ。Shachou no hanashi buri de wa, kotoshi no boonasu wa nai you da. According to what the president told us, there may not be a bonus this year. अध्यक्ष के बोलने के अंदाज़ से लगता है कि इस साल बोनस नहीं मिलेगा। પ્રમુખના બોલવાની રીત પરથી લાગે છે કે આ વર્ષે બોનસ નહીં મળે.
彼女、飲みっぷりがいいね。相当、酒に強いんだろう。Kanojo, nomippuri ga ii ne. Soutou, sake ni tsuyoi ndarou. She's drinking quickly. She must be able to drink a lot. वह कितनी शान से पी रही है! ज़रूर शराब पीने में माहिर होगी। તે કેટલી છટાથી પી રહી છે! ચોક્કસ દારૂ પીવામાં માહેર હશે.

ぶり and っぷり share the exact same meaning ("the way/manner of doing ~") and differ only in register — ぶり is neutral/written, っぷり is casual/spoken. Don't confuse this ぶり with Point 3's ぶる (act like/pretend) — they look alike but are unrelated in meaning.

Q1

彼は中学生になったとたん、大人（　）ことを言うようになった。Kare wa chuugakusei ni natta totan, otona (___) koto o iu you ni natta.

Full sentence: 彼は中学生になったとたん、大人びたことを言うようになった。Otona bita koto o iu you ni natta.
EN: The moment he became a junior-high student, he started saying grown-up-sounding things.
HI: वह जैसे ही मिडिल स्कूल का छात्र बना, बड़ों जैसी बातें करने लगा।
GU: તે જેવો મિડલ સ્કૂલનો વિદ્યાર્થી બન્યો, કે તરત મોટા માણસો જેવી વાતો કરવા લાગ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aめいたmeita(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bびたbitasounding/looking grown-upबड़ों जैसा लगने वालाમોટા માણસ જેવું લાગતું

Why: 大人びる is the fixed idiom for a person increasingly looking or sounding grown-up as they mature (Point 2). めく／めいた (Point 1) is reserved for something taking on an abstract impression (mysterious, sarcastic, secretive) — it doesn't naturally combine with 大人 to describe a person's own growth.

Q6

彼は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ をしていたが、実は一度も行ったことがなかったらしい。Kare wa ___ o shite ita ga, jitsu wa ichido mo itta koto ga nakatta rashii.

Tiles: 1 ぶって (butte)　2 常連 (jouren)　3 の話 (no hanashi)　4 その店 (sono mise)

2 常連→1 ぶって→4 その店→3 の話

彼は常連ぶってその店の話をしていたが、実は一度も行ったことがなかったらしい。Kare wa jouren butte sono mise no hanashi o shite ita ga, jitsu wa ichido mo itta koto ga nakatta rashii.

EN: He talked about that shop acting like a regular customer, but apparently he had never actually been there.
HI: वह उस दुकान का नियमित ग्राहक होने का दिखावा करके उसके बारे में बातें कर रहा था, लेकिन असल में वह वहाँ कभी गया ही नहीं था।
GU: તે તે દુકાનનો નિયમિત ગ્રાહક હોવાનો દેખાડો કરીને તેના વિશે વાતો કરી રહ્યો હતો, પણ ખરેખર તે ત્યાં ક્યારેય ગયો જ નહોતો.

Why: Built on Point 3 (ぶる) — 常連ぶって = pretending/acting like a regular at that shop while talking as if he really knew it (その店の話をしていた), despite never having actually gone (実は一度も行ったことがなかった).

Q7

その記事に＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ が大量に出版社に届いた。Sono kiji ni ___ ga tairyou ni shuppansha ni todoita.

Tiles: 1 メール (meeru)　2 対する (taisuru)　3 反論 (hanron)　4 めいた (meita)

2 対する→3 反論→4 めいた→1 メール

その記事に対する反論めいたメールが大量に出版社に届いた。Sono kiji ni taisuru hanron meita meeru ga tairyou ni shuppansha ni todoita.

EN: A huge number of emails that seemed like rebuttals to the article poured into the publisher.
HI: उस लेख के जवाब में विरोध जैसा लगने वाला ईमेल बड़ी संख्या में प्रकाशक के पास पहुँचा।
GU: તે લેખના જવાબમાં વિરોધ જેવો લાગતો ઈમેલ મોટી સંખ્યામાં પ્રકાશકને મળ્યો.

Why: Built on Point 1 (めく／めいた) — 反論めいた = "seeming like a rebuttal/objection," genuinely carrying that impression; 対する connects 反論めいたメール back to その記事 ("an email that seemed like a rebuttal to the article").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of today's four forms (めく／びる／ぶる) all attach to a noun/adjective stem and superficially resemble each other, so N1 loves testing them against each other (see Q1–Q4 above). Compare all four side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
めく／めいたmeku / meita Genuinely starts to take on a quality/season N + めく／めいて／めいたN Real, gradual change — the quality is authentically emerging, no pretense. You want to say something is truly becoming/feeling like X (a season, a mood, an impression).
びる／びたbiru / bita Takes on the appearance of ~ (esp. maturity/age) N + びる／びて／びたN Describes a natural, honest change in appearance — not fake. You want to say a person/thing genuinely looks more grown-up, aged, or worn.
ぶる／ぶったburu / butta Acts like / pretends to be ~ な/N + ぶる／ぶって／ぶったN Deliberate, often false performance of a persona. You want to say someone is consciously faking a quality or role.
ぶり／っぷりburi / ppuri The manner/way something is done N + ぶり／Vます-stem + っぷり Pure register difference (neutral/written vs. casual/spoken); meaning identical. Use ぶり in writing/formal speech, っぷり in casual spoken contexts.
Exam trap: めく, びる, and ぶる all attach to a similar-looking noun/adjective stem and superficially resemble each other, but they sit on a spectrum from "genuinely becoming" (めく) → "looking like, through a real process" (びる) → "deliberately faking" (ぶる). N1 loves testing exactly this three-way distinction head-on (see Q1–Q4 above) — always ask whether the change is real (めく/びる) or a conscious performance (ぶる), and if real, whether it's about an abstract impression (めく) or a person/thing's own physical/mature appearance (びる). Separately, don't confuse Point 4's ぶり (the way something is done) with Point 3's ぶる (to act like) — they look alike but are unrelated in meaning; ぶり/っぷり is purely a register choice (written vs. spoken), not a meaning choice.
