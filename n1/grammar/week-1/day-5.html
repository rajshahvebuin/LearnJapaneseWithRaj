5日目 — 難しいとみると… (Muzukashii to miru to...)

Answer key

Notes
All four patterns today use 見る／見える in some form, but they split into three separate jobs: inferring from evidence (とみえて／とみられる), reacting the instant something is noticed (とみると), and building a pure hypothetical (とすれば). See §3 for how N1 likes to test the boundaries between them.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とみえて／とみえる

neutral register
Reading~to miete / ~to mieru
Meaning (EN)"it seems that ~, judging by the evidence" — an inference the speaker draws from something observed (a person's behavior, a symptom)
Meaning (HI)ऐसा लगता है कि... (सबूत/लक्षण देखकर अनुमान)
Meaning (GU)એવું લાગે છે કે... (પુરાવા/લક્ષણો પરથી અનુમાન)
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain） + とみえて／とみえる
RegisterNeutral
Typical useReporting an inference from observed evidence — often mid-sentence (とみえて, leading into a further comment) or sentence-final (とみえる).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる／Vた + とみえる驚いたとみえるodoroita to mieru
い-adjectiveAい + とみえて忙しいとみえてisogashii to miete
な-adjective(added, not in book) Naだ + とみえる元気だとみえるgenki da to mieru
Noun(added, not in book) N（だ） + とみえる留守だとみえるrusu da to mieru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中さんは忙しいとみえて、このごろ電話もしてこない。Tanaka-san wa isogashii to miete, kono goro denwa mo shite konai. Tanaka-san seems to be very busy — that's maybe why he hasn't called lately. तनाका-सान शायद व्यस्त हैं, इसीलिए आजकल फ़ोन भी नहीं करते। તનાકા-સાન કદાચ વ્યસ્ત છે, એટલે જ હમણાં ફોન પણ નથી કરતા.
それを聞いて、彼はしばらく声を出さなかった。かなり驚いたとみえる。Sore o kiite, kare wa shibaraku koe o dasanakatta. Kanari odoroita to mieru. He didn't say a word after hearing that. He must have been very surprised. यह सुनकर, वह कुछ देर तक कुछ नहीं बोला। लगता है वह काफ़ी चौंक गया था। આ સાંભળીને, તે થોડીવાર કંઈ ન બોલ્યો. લાગે છે તે ખૂબ ચોંકી ગયો હતો.

とみえて／とみえる is a personal, on-the-spot inference — "from what I can see, it seems..." Compare with Point 2 below, which is a more formal, often third-party prediction.

【2】 〜とみられる

neutral–formal register
Reading~to mirareru
Meaning (EN)"it is expected/predicted that ~" — a forecast or expert judgment based on evidence, more formal and impersonal than とみえる
Meaning (HI)ऐसा अनुमान लगाया जाता है / ऐसी संभावना जताई जाती है
Meaning (GU)એવું અનુમાન લગાવાય છે / એવી શક્યતા વ્યક્ત કરાય છે
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain） + とみられる
RegisterNeutral leaning formal — common in news forecasts and expert commentary
Typical useThird-party predictions: weather, the economy, an investigation's outcome.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + とみられる来るとみられるkuru to mirareru
い-adjectiveAい + とみられる難しいとみられるmuzukashii to mirareru
な-adjective(added, not in book) Naだ + とみられる深刻だとみられるshinkoku da to mirareru
Noun(added, not in book) N（だ） + とみられる原因だとみられるgen'in da to mirareru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この地域では、今後も大きい地震が来るとみられている。Kono chiiki de wa, kongo mo ookii jishin ga kuru to mirarete iru. This area is expected to have a major earthquake again in the future. इस क्षेत्र में आगे भी बड़े भूकंप आने की आशंका जताई जा रही है। આ વિસ્તારમાં ભવિષ્યમાં પણ મોટા ધરતીકંપ આવવાની શક્યતા વ્યક્ત કરાઈ છે.
その会社の再建は難しいとみられる。Sono kaisha no saiken wa muzukashii to mirareru. It looks very difficult to rebuild the company. उस कंपनी का पुनर्निर्माण मुश्किल माना जा रहा है। તે કંપનીનું પુનર્નિર્માણ મુશ્કેલ મનાય છે.

とみられる swaps out the personal "it looks to me like" of とみえる for an impersonal, expert-toned forecast — a hallmark of news headlines ("〜とみられる" appears constantly in Japanese reporting).

【3】 〜とみると

neutral register
Reading~to miru to
Meaning (EN)"as soon as one notices/perceives that ~" — an immediate reaction or habitual behavior triggered the instant something is perceived
Meaning (HI)जैसे ही पता चलता/दिखता है कि..., वैसे ही...
Meaning (GU)જેવું ખબર પડે/દેખાય કે..., તરત જ...
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain） + とみると
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribes a pattern of behavior — someone consistently reacts a certain way the moment they perceive X.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は問題がちょっと難しいとみると、自分で考えないで人に聞く。Kare wa mondai ga chotto muzukashii to miru to, jibun de kangaenaide hito ni kiku. As soon as he finds a question a little difficult, he asks others for help without trying to solve it himself. जैसे ही उसे प्रश्न थोड़ा मुश्किल लगता है, वह ख़ुद सोचे बिना दूसरों से पूछ लेता है। જેવો પ્રશ્ન થોડો અઘરો લાગે, તે પોતે વિચાર્યા વગર બીજાને પૂછી લે છે.
彼は、店員が韓国人だとみると、必ず韓国語で話しかける。Kare wa, ten'in ga kankokujin da to miru to, kanarazu kankokugo de hanashikakeru. As soon as he notices that the sales clerk is Korean, he switches to Korean. जैसे ही उसे पता चलता है कि दुकानदार कोरियाई है, वह ज़रूर कोरियाई भाषा में बात करने लगता है। જેવી તેને ખબર પડે કે દુકાનદાર કોરિયન છે, તે ચોક્કસ કોરિયન ભાષામાં વાત કરવા લાગે છે.
彼女は、男の人にお金がないとみると、急にその人に興味がなくなるようだ。Kanojo wa, otoko no hito ni okane ga nai to miru to, kyuu ni sono hito ni kyoumi ga naku naru you da. As soon as she notices that a man has no money, she loses interest. जैसे ही उसे पता चलता है कि किसी आदमी के पास पैसे नहीं हैं, उसकी दिलचस्पी अचानक ख़त्म हो जाती है। જેવી તેને ખબર પડે કે કોઈ પુરુષ પાસે પૈસા નથી, તેનામાં તેની રુચિ અચાનક ખતમ થઈ જાય છે.

とみると always describes an immediate, near-automatic reaction — the main clause is typically a habitual or characteristic behavior, not a one-off event.

【4】 〜とすれば／とすると

neutral register
Reading~to sureba / ~to suru to
Meaning (EN)"if we assume ~ / were it the case that ~" — sets up a pure hypothetical premise (equivalent to 〜としたら)
Meaning (HI)अगर मान लें कि... / यदि ऐसा हो कि...
Meaning (GU)જો માની લઈએ કે... / જો એવું હોય કે...
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain） + とすれば／とすると (= 〜としたら)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useReasoning through a hypothetical scenario — "if X were true/the case, then Y follows."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
そこまで歩いて行くとすれば、30分くらいかかるだろう。Soko made aruite iku to sureba, sanjuppun kurai kakaru darou. It'll take about half an hour if you walk there. अगर वहाँ तक पैदल जाया जाए, तो लगभग 30 मिनट लगेंगे। જો ત્યાં સુધી ચાલીને જવાય, તો લગભગ 30 મિનિટ લાગશે.
その話が本当だとすると、大変なことだ。Sono hanashi ga hontou da to suru to, taihen na koto da. If that is true, we are in trouble. अगर वह बात सच है, तो यह बड़ी मुसीबत की बात है। જો તે વાત સાચી હોય, તો તે મોટી મુસીબતની વાત છે.

Don't confuse とすると with Point 3's とみると: とすると builds a pure logical hypothetical ("if we assume..."), while とみると describes a perception-triggered habitual reaction ("the moment X is noticed..."). They look almost identical but answer completely different questions — see §3.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

もし車を買い換える（　）、次はドイツの車がほしい。Moshi kuruma o kaikaeru (___), tsugi wa doitsu no kuruma ga hoshii.

Full sentence: もし車を買い換えるとすれば、次はドイツの車がほしい。Moshi kuruma o kaikaeru to sureba, tsugi wa doitsu no kuruma ga hoshii.
EN: If I were to replace my car, I'd want a German car next.
HI: अगर मैं गाड़ी बदलूँ, तो अगली बार जर्मन गाड़ी चाहूँगा/चाहूँगी।
GU: જો હું ગાડી બદલું, તો પછીની વખતે જર્મન ગાડી જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとすればto surebaif we assume, hypotheticallyअगर मान लें किજો માની લઈએ કે
bとみるとto miru toas soon as noticing (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: もし ("if") signals a pure hypothetical, which is とすれば's job; とみると would need an actual perceived trigger, not a hypothetical もし-clause.

Q2

彼女は苦労した（　）、実際の年齢より老けて見える。Kanojo wa kurou shita (___), jissai no nenrei yori fukete mieru.

Full sentence: 彼女は苦労したとみえて、実際の年齢より老けて見える。Kanojo wa kurou shita to miete, jissai no nenrei yori fukete mieru.
EN: She seems to have had a hard life, as she looks older than her actual age.
HI: लगता है उसने बहुत मुश्किलें झेली हैं, क्योंकि वह अपनी असल उम्र से बड़ी दिखती है।
GU: લાગે છે તેણે ઘણી મુશ્કેલીઓ સહી છે, કારણ કે તે પોતાની ખરી ઉંમર કરતાં મોટી દેખાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとしてto shiteas, in the capacity of (wrong pattern)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとみえてto mieteit seems, judging by appearanceऐसा लगता हैએવું લાગે છે

Why: "Looking older than her age" is the evidence the speaker uses to infer she's had a hard life — exactly とみえて's job; として just means "as/in the role of," which doesn't fit an inference at all.

Q3

彼女の普段の成績から、合格は間違いないと（　）。Kanojo no fudan no seiseki kara, goukaku wa machigainai to (___).

Full sentence: 彼女の普段の成績から、合格は間違いないとみられている。Kanojo no fudan no seiseki kara, goukaku wa machigainai to mirarete iru.
EN: Judging from her usual grades, she is expected to pass without question.
HI: उसके सामान्य अंकों को देखते हुए, यह अनुमान है कि वह ज़रूर पास होगी।
GU: તેના સામાન્ય માર્ક્સ પરથી, એવું અનુમાન છે કે તે ચોક્કસ પાસ થશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aみられているmirarete iruis expected/predictedअनुमान लगाया जा रहा हैઅનુમાન લગાવાયું છે
bみえているmiete iru(not a standard collocation here)(अमान्य प्रयोग)(અયોગ્ય ઉપયોગ)

Why: "Based on her grades, she's expected to pass" is an impersonal, evidence-based forecast — Point 2's とみられる; とみえている isn't how this pattern is used (とみえる doesn't take みえている as a fixed form here).

Q4

彼は、女性が地方出身だ（　）すぐに声をかける。Kare wa, josei ga chihou shusshin da (___) sugu ni koe o kakeru.

GU: જેવી તેને ખબર પડે કે કોઈ સ્ત્રી ગ્રામ્ય વિસ્તારની છે, તે તરત તેની સાથે વાત કરવા જાય છે.

aとみるとto miru toas soon as noticingजैसे ही पता चलता हैજેવી ખબર પડે

Q5

彼が犯人（　）、犯罪を犯した動機は何だろう。Kare ga hannin (___), hanzai o okashita douki wa nan darou.

Full sentence: 彼が犯人だとすると、犯罪を犯した動機は何だろう。Kare ga hannin da to suru to, hanzai o okashita douki wa nan darou.
EN: If he is the culprit, what would be the motive for the crime?
HI: अगर वह अपराधी है, तो अपराध करने की वजह क्या होगी?
GU: જો તે ગુનેગાર હોય, તો ગુનો કરવાનું કારણ શું હશે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだとするとda to suru toif we assume he isअगर मान लें कि वह हैજો માની લઈએ કે તે છે
bだとみえてda to mieteit seems (wrong — no evidence being cited)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

景気は回復しつつ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 人は少ないと思う。Keiki wa kaifuku shitsutsu ___ hito wa sukunai to omou.

Tiles: 1 が (ga)　2 実感している (jikkan shite iru)　3 ある (aru)　4 とみられている (to mirarete iru)

3 ある→4 とみられている→1 が→2 実感している

景気は回復しつつあるとみられているが、実感している人は少ないと思う。Keiki wa kaifuku shitsutsu aru to mirarete iru ga, jikkan shite iru hito wa sukunai to omou.

EN: The economy is believed to be recovering, but I think few people actually feel it.
HI: माना जा रहा है कि अर्थव्यवस्था में सुधार हो रहा है, लेकिन मुझे लगता है कि इसे महसूस करने वाले लोग कम हैं।
GU: મનાય છે કે અર્થવ્યવસ્થામાં સુધારો થઈ રહ્યો છે, પણ મને લાગે છે કે તેને અનુભવનારા લોકો ઓછા છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜とみられる) — an impersonal expert forecast about the economy, contrasted with the personal, felt experience (実感している) of ordinary people.

Q7

インフルエンザが＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 場合があるので、しばらくは薬を続けてください。Infuruenza ga ___ baai ga aru node, shibaraku wa kusuri o tsuzukete kudasai.

Tiles: 1 ウイルスは (uirusu wa)　2 治った (naotta)　3 まだ残っている (mada nokotte iru)　4 とみえても (to mietemo)

2 治った→4 とみえても→1 ウイルスは→3 まだ残っている

インフルエンザが治ったとみえても、ウイルスはまだ残っている場合があるので、しばらくは薬を続けてください。Infuruenza ga naotta to mietemo, uirusu wa mada nokotte iru baai ga aru node, shibaraku wa kusuri o tsuzukete kudasai.

EN: Even though the flu may seem to have healed, the virus may still remain, so please continue the medication for a while.
HI: भले ही फ़्लू ठीक हुआ लगे, कभी-कभी वायरस अभी भी रह सकता है, इसलिए कुछ समय और दवा लेते रहें।
GU: ભલે ફ્લૂ સાજું થયું લાગે, ક્યારેક વાયરસ હજુ પણ રહી શકે છે, એટલે થોડો સમય દવા ચાલુ રાખો.

Why: とみえても combines Point 1's とみえる with the concessive も ("even if it seems...") — an appearance-based inference that the following clause then qualifies/contradicts.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four forms all contain 見る/見える, but answer four different questions — is this an inference, a forecast, a triggered habit, or a hypothesis? Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
〜とみえて／とみえる~to miete/mieru It seems that ~ A personal, on-the-spot inference from something observed. "He seems busy — he hasn't called."
〜とみられる~to mirareru It is expected/predicted that ~ An impersonal, often expert/media forecast. News-style predictions: earthquakes, the economy, an investigation.
〜とみると~to miru to As soon as one notices that ~ An immediate, habitual reaction triggered by a real perception. Describing someone's consistent behavior pattern.
〜とすれば／とすると~to sureba/suru to If we assume ~ A pure hypothetical premise — no perception or evidence required. "If X were true, then Y follows" reasoning.
Exam trap: とみると and とすると are near-homophones in casual speech and both can follow a plain-form clause, but they test opposite things: とみると requires a real, perceived trigger and describes habitual behavior ("every time he notices X, he does Y"); とすると requires no real perception at all — it's a logical "if" (もし often appears alongside it, as in Q1 above). If もし is present or implied, it's とすると; if the sentence describes someone's repeated real-world reaction, it's とみると.
