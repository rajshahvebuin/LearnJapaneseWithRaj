1日目 — 感謝こそすれ (Kansha koso sure)

Notes
All four points share the emphatic particle こそ, so this day is really one confusion-pair cluster — see §3 below. Two of the four forms (〜こそあれ, 〜こそすれ) are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other two are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜てこそ

neutral register
Reading~te koso
Meaning (EN)"only after / by doing ~ (does something become true/possible)" — marks a necessary precondition
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...करने पर ही (…होता है) — पहले वह काम करना ज़रूरी है
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...કર્યા પછી જ (...થાય છે)
Connection (接続)V-て + こそ
RegisterNeutral — used in both writing and speech
Typical useEmphasizing that a result only holds once a prior action is done. Cannot be used for a completed past event — the clause after こそ must stay in present/plain form, even if the whole sentence is a general truth.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-て + こそ親になってこそoya ni natte koso
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj-く + てこそ高くてこそtakakute koso
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + でこそ静かでこそshizuka de koso
Noun(added, not in book) N + でこそ学生でこそgakusei de koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
親になってこそ、親の苦労や気持ちがわかるものだ。Oya ni natte koso, oya no kurou ya kimochi ga wakaru mono da. Only after you become a parent can you understand a parent's hardships and feelings. माता-पिता बनने के बाद ही माता-पिता की मुश्किलें और भावनाएँ समझ में आती हैं। માતાપિતા બન્યા પછી જ માતાપિતાની મુશ્કેલીઓ અને લાગણીઓ સમજાય છે.
生活費を自分で稼いでこそ、自立していると言える。Seikatsuhi o jibun de kaseide koso, jiritsu shite iru to ieru. Only once you earn your own living expenses can you truly call yourself independent. अपना खर्च खुद कमाने पर ही आप खुद को आत्मनिर्भर कह सकते हैं। પોતાનો ખર્ચ જાતે કમાયા પછી જ તમે તમારી જાતને સ્વતંત્ર કહી શકો.

Literally "はじめて" (Vて + はじめて) with more emphasis. The negative example the book gives is important: 親になってこそ母の苦労がわかった is marked ダメ (wrong) because わかった is past tense; the corrected version 親になってこそ母の苦労がわかる uses the plain present form. Treat this as a hard rule for N1: the clause after 〜てこそ describes a general/timeless truth, not a one-off past event.

【2】 〜こそあれ

硬 formal/written
Reading~koso are
Meaning (EN)"although there is/are ~..." — concedes a shortcoming or quality while the main point still stands
Meaning (HI)यद्यपि ...है (फिर भी...) / ...है ज़रूर, लेकिन...
Meaning (GU)...છે ખરું, પરંતુ...
Connection (接続)N + こそ + あれ  |  な-adj + でこそ + あれ／あるが
Register硬 — formal, mostly written (essays, speeches)
Typical useConcessive emphasis: "it's true X exists/qualifies, but the bigger point holds anyway."
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + こそあれ苦労こそあれkurou koso are
な-adjectiveな-adj + でこそあれ／あるが不便でこそあれfuben de koso are
い-adjective(added, not in book; rare) い-adj + こそあれ難しくこそあれmuzukashiku koso are
Verb(added, not in book — not a typical use of this pattern)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
苦労こそあれ、介護の仕事はやりがいがある。Kurou koso are, kaigo no shigoto wa yarigai ga aru. Nursing care work is hard, but it is worth doing. मेहनत ज़रूर है, लेकिन देखभाल का काम करने लायक है। મહેનત ખરી છે, પણ સંભાળ રાખવાનું કામ કરવા જેવું છે.
彼の日本語は小さい間違いこそあれ、ほとんど完璧だ。Kare no nihongo wa chiisai machigai koso are, hotondo kanpeki da. Although he makes small mistakes, his Japanese is almost perfect. उसकी जापानी में छोटी-छोटी गलतियाँ हैं ज़रूर, लेकिन वह लगभग परफेक्ट है। તેની જાપાનીઝમાં નાની ભૂલો છે ખરી, પણ તે લગભગ સંપૂર્ણ છે.

あれ here is the classical realis (已然形) of ある, left over from the old 係り結び (kakari-musubi) construction "こそ...已然形" — that's why it looks like an odd conjugation rather than modern ある/あって. Treat 〜こそあれ as a fixed formal chunk meaning "though there is X".

【3】 〜こそすれ

硬 formal/written
Reading~koso sure
Meaning (EN)"if anything, X is (definitely) the case — certainly not the opposite" — concedes/asserts a positive fact and firmly denies its opposite
Meaning (HI)...ज़रूर होता/करता है, लेकिन बिल्कुल भी उल्टा नहीं
Meaning (GU)...ચોક્કસ થાય છે, પણ ઊલટું બિલકુલ નહીં
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem + こそすれ  |  N + こそすれ
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useThe clause that follows almost always denies the opposite outright, typically with 「絶対に〜ない」/「〜ものではない」 — that denial is the whole point of the pattern.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます-stem + こそすれ増えこそすれfue koso sure
NounN + こそすれ感謝こそすれkansha koso sure
い-adjective(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets actions/facts, not descriptive states)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; not a natural fit)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あなたには感謝こそすれ、恨んでなどいません。Anata ni wa kansha koso sure, urande nado imasen. I'm nothing but grateful to you — I have no resentment whatsoever. तुम्हारे प्रति मेरे मन में सिर्फ़ आभार है, कोई नाराज़गी बिल्कुल नहीं। તમારા પ્રત્યે મારા મનમાં ફક્ત આભાર છે, કોઈ નારાજગી બિલકુલ નથી.
白髪はふつう増えこそすれ、減ることはない。Shiraga wa futsuu fue koso sure, heru koto wa nai. Gray hair normally only increases — it never decreases. सफ़ेद बाल आमतौर पर बढ़ते ही हैं, कभी कम नहीं होते। સફેદ વાળ સામાન્ય રીતે વધે જ છે, ક્યારેય ઘટતા નથી.

すれ is the classical realis (已然形) of する, mirroring the あれ in 〜こそあれ — same kakari-musubi fossil, different verb. The meaning split is the key thing to memorize: こそあれ concedes an existing quality ("there is X, but…"); こそすれ asserts a fact and denies its opposite ("if anything X, definitely not Y").

【4】 Nこそ〜が／〜けれど

neutral register
ReadingN koso ~ga / ~keredo
Meaning (EN)"it's true that N is ~, but..." — a plain concessive contrast that singles out the one point being conceded
Meaning (HI)यह सच है कि ...है, लेकिन...
Meaning (GU)એ સાચું છે કે ...છે, પણ...
Connection (接続)N + こそ + [predicate in its normal form] + が／けれど
RegisterNeutral — the only one of today's four forms usable comfortably in conversation
Typical useこそ attaches to the noun/topic being conceded; whatever comes after keeps its ordinary form (plain adjective/verb + が or けれど). It just adds emphasis to "that one part, yes — but…".
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + こそ + predicate + が／けれど見た目こそ悪いがmitame koso warui ga
い-adjective / な-adjective / VerbNot a separate attachment point — こそ always marks the noun/topic here; the adjective or verb that follows simply keeps its own plain form (e.g. きれいだが, 悪いが) unaffected by こそ.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
父の料理は見た目こそ悪いが、とてもいい味をしている。Chichi no ryouri wa mitame koso warui ga, totemo ii aji o shite iru. My father's cooking may not look great, but it tastes very good. पिताजी के खाने की शक्ल भले अच्छी न हो, पर स्वाद बहुत बढ़िया होता है। પિતાજીની રસોઈ દેખાવમાં ભલે સારી ન હોય, પણ સ્વાદ ખૂબ સરસ હોય છે.
このキノコは色と形こそきれいだが、毒があって食べることはできない。Kono kinoko wa iro to katachi koso kirei da ga, doku ga atte taberu koto wa dekinai. This mushroom's color and shape are pretty, but it's poisonous and can't be eaten. इस मशरूम का रंग और आकार सुंदर है ज़रूर, लेकिन इसमें ज़हर है इसलिए इसे खाया नहीं जा सकता। આ મશરૂમનો રંગ અને આકાર સુંદર છે ખરો, પણ તેમાં ઝેર છે એટલે તે ખાઈ શકાય નહીં.

This is the mildest, most everyday form of today's four — reach for this one in conversation, and save 〜こそあれ／〜こそすれ for formal writing. Don't confuse the が here (a plain conjunction meaning "but") with the が inside a formation label — it's just "however" contrast.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.15. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.17.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

その国で生活（　）文化がわかるというものだ。Sono kuni de seikatsu (___) bunka ga wakaru to iu mono da.

Full sentence: その国で生活してこそ、文化がわかるというものだ。Sono kuni de seikatsu shite koso, bunka ga wakaru to iu mono da.
EN: It is only by living in that country that you come to understand its culture.
HI: उस देश में रहकर ही वहाँ की संस्कृति समझ में आती है।
GU: તે દેશમાં રહીને જ ત્યાંની સંસ્કૃતિ સમજાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aしてこそshite kosoby doing / and only thenकरने पर हीકર્યા પછી જ
bしたこそshita koso(not a valid form here)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 〜てこそ always attaches to the te-form, never the ta-form — したこそ simply isn't a grammatical option in this slot.

Q2

程度の違いこそ（　）、悪いことをしたのは皆同じだ。Teido no chigai koso (___), warui koto o shita no wa mina onaji da.

Full sentence: 程度の違いこそあれ、悪いことをしたのは皆同じだ。Teido no chigai koso are, warui koto o shita no wa mina onaji da.
EN: Although there's a difference in degree, everyone did something bad all the same.
HI: मात्रा में फ़र्क है ज़रूर, लेकिन बुरा काम तो सबने किया है।
GU: માત્રામાં ફરક છે ખરો, પણ ખરાબ કામ તો બધાએ કર્યું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあれareexistential concession ("though there is...")है ज़रूर (फिर भी)છે ખરું (પણ)
bすれsure(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 違い is a noun describing an existing state, which pairs with こそあれ. こそすれ is reserved for asserting a fact while denying its opposite — there's no such denial here, so it doesn't fit.

Q3

Full sentence: 彼は字こそ汚いが、いい文章を書く。Kare wa ji koso kitanai ga, ii bunshou o kaku.
EN: His handwriting is admittedly messy, but he writes good essays.
HI: उसकी लिखावट भले ही गंदी है, लेकिन वह अच्छे लेख लिखता है।
GU: તેનું અક્ષર ભલે ગંદું છે, પણ તે સારું લખાણ લખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこそkosoplain concessive marker (point 4 pattern)भले हीભલે

ここは、（　）こそあれ、緑が多くていいところだ。Koko wa, (___) koso are, midori ga ookute ii tokoro da.

GU: આ જગ્યા અસુવિધાજનક ખરી, પણ અહીં હરિયાળી ઘણી છે અને એ સારી જગ્યા છે.

GU: લખાણનો અર્થ ત્યારે જ છે જ્યારે તે સમજાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

君のやったことは、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ではない。Kimi no yatta koto wa, ___ de wa nai.

Tiles: 1 非難される (hinan sareru)　2 すれ (sure)　3 ほめられこそ (homerare koso)　4 もの (mono)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

3 ほめられこそ→2 すれ→1 非難される→4 もの

君のやったことは、ほめられこそすれ非難されるものではない。Kimi no yatta koto wa, homerare koso sure hinan sareru mono de wa nai.

EN: What you did deserves praise, not criticism.
HI: तुमने जो किया, उसकी तारीफ़ होनी चाहिए, आलोचना नहीं।
GU: તમે જે કર્યું તેની પ્રશંસા થવી જોઈએ, ટીકા નહીં.

Why: Built on point 3 (〜こそすれ) — ほめられ (passive ます-stem of ほめる) + こそすれ asserts "if anything, it should be praised," then flatly denies the opposite (非難されるものではない).

Q7

その2つの ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 中身に大きな違いはない。Sono futatsu no ___ nakami ni ookina chigai wa nai.

Tiles: 1 製品は (seihin wa)　2 名前 (namae)　3 違うが (chigau ga)　4 こそ (koso)

1 製品は→2 名前→4 こそ→3 違うが

その2つの製品は名前こそ違うが、中身に大きな違いはない。Sono futatsu no seihin wa namae koso chigau ga, nakami ni ookina chigai wa nai.

EN: Those two products differ in name, but there's no big difference in their content.
HI: उन दोनों उत्पादों के नाम भले अलग हों, लेकिन उनके अंदर के सामान में कोई बड़ा फर्क नहीं है।
GU: તે બંને ઉત્પાદનોના નામ ભલે અલગ હોય, પણ તેમની અંદરની વસ્તુમાં કોઈ મોટો ફરક નથી.

Why: Built on point 4 (Nこそ〜が) — 名前(N) + こそ + 違うが concedes the one point (the name) while the main clause asserts the products are basically the same.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today share こそ, which is exactly why N1 likes to test them against each other (see Q2 and Q3 above, which are built to trip up あれ vs すれ vs plain こそ). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜てこそ~te koso Only after/by doing ~ V-て + こそ Marks a precondition; following clause must be present/plain, never past. You want to say "X only becomes true once Y happens."
〜こそあれ~koso are Although there is/are ~ N／な-adj-で + こそあれ あれ = classical realis of ある — concedes an existing quality/state. Formal writing; conceding a shortcoming before making your main point.
〜こそすれ~koso sure If anything X — certainly not the opposite V-ます-stem／N + こそすれ すれ = classical realis of する — always paired with an explicit denial of the opposite. Formal writing; you're about to flatly deny the reverse of what you just said.
Nこそ〜が／けれどN koso ~ga/~keredo It's true N is ~, but... N + こそ + plain predicate + が／けれど こそ attaches to the noun/topic only; predicate keeps its ordinary form. Everyday conversation — the mildest of the four, no special register.
Exam trap: あれ and すれ look almost interchangeable at a glance (both formal, both follow こそ), but they attach to different things and mean different things — あれ concedes a state that already exists ("there is X, but…"), すれ asserts an action/fact and denies its opposite ("if anything X — definitely not Y"). When a question gives you an あれ/すれ choice, first check whether the second clause denies an opposite (→ すれ) or simply continues past the concession (→ あれ).
