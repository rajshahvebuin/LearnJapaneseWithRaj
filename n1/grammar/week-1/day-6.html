Notes
The book itself labels 〜たところで…ない as "(一)" — a first installment, implying a "(二)" continuation appears in a later week; flagged here transparently since only part (一) is covered this day. Watch the shared kanji 所/ところ across three of today's four patterns — they mean three different things (a critical moment, a rough estimate, and a "no matter how much" concession).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ところを

neutral register
Reading~tokoro o
Meaning (EN)"right at the moment/situation when ~" — marks the exact critical moment or state something happened in; often used to thank or apologize for an interruption during that moment
Meaning (HI)ठीक उस पल/स्थिति में जब... (एक नाज़ुक क्षण)
Meaning (GU)બરાબર તે પળ/સ્થિતિમાં જ્યારે... (એક નાજુક ક્ષણ)
Connection (接続)V／A（plain）／な＋な／N＋の + ところを
RegisterNeutral; common in set polite phrases (お忙しいところを…)
Typical useThanking/apologizing for someone's time or state at an inconvenient or critical moment.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + ところを危ないところをabunai tokoro o
VerbVない + ところをしなければいけないところをshinakereba ikenai tokoro o
な-adjectiveNaな + ところをお忙しい(い-adj example shown; な-adj added, not in book) 不便なところをfuben na tokoro o
Noun(added, not in book) Nの + ところを休みのところをyasumi no tokoro o
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
交通事故で命が危ないところを、その医者に助けてもらった。Koutsuu jiko de inochi ga abunai tokoro o, sono isha ni tasukete moratta. I was saved by the doctor when I was in critical condition due to a traffic accident. सड़क दुर्घटना में जान ख़तरे में होने के उस पल में, उस डॉक्टर ने मेरी जान बचाई। રોડ અકસ્માતમાં જીવ જોખમમાં હતો તે જ પળે, તે ડૉક્ટરે મારો જીવ બચાવ્યો.
すぐにお礼の電話をしなければいけないところを今になってしまい、申し訳ありません。Sugu ni orei no denwa o shinakereba ikenai tokoro o ima ni natte shimai, moushiwake arimasen. I apologize for not having given you a thank-you call sooner. तुरंत धन्यवाद का फ़ोन करना चाहिए था, फिर भी अब तक देर हो गई, इसके लिए क्षमा करें। તરત આભારનો ફોન કરવો જોઈતો હતો, છતાં હવે મોડું થઈ ગયું, તે બદલ માફ કરશો.

This ところを freezes a single moment in time and hands it to the rest of the sentence as the backdrop — "in that exact situation, X happened/was done." It's the same ところを behind polite openers like お忙しいところをすみません (sorry to bother you while you're busy).

【2】 〜というところだ／といったところだ

neutral register
Reading~to iu tokoro da / ~to itta tokoro da
Meaning (EN)"it's about ~ / roughly ~" — gives a rough estimate, most often of a quantity or a state of progress
Meaning (HI)लगभग/करीब...है
Meaning (GU)લગભગ...છે
Connection (接続)N／[文] + というところだ／といったところだ　◆Nは数量が入ることが多い
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAnswering "how much/how far along?" questions with a rough, hedged estimate.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
勉強時間は2、3時間というところだ。Benkyou jikan wa ni, san jikan to iu tokoro da. I study about 2 or 3 hours (every day). पढ़ने का समय लगभग 2-3 घंटे है। અભ્યાસનો સમય લગભગ 2-3 કલાક છે.
「例の書類はできましたか。」「はい、あとはもう一度見直せばいいといったところです。」"Rei no shorui wa dekimashita ka." "Hai, ato wa mou ichido minaoseba ii to itta tokoro desu." "Is the document we talked about ready?" "Yes, almost. I just have to review it again." "वह दस्तावेज़ तैयार हो गया?" "हाँ, बस एक बार और जाँच लेना बाकी है, लगभग इतना ही।" "તે દસ્તાવેજ તૈયાર થયો?" "હા, બસ એક વાર ફરી ચકાસવાનું બાકી છે, લગભગ એટલું જ."

というところだ／といったところだ are interchangeable hedges — reach for them whenever you want to give a number or a status update without committing to exact precision.

【3】 〜たところで…ない （一）

neutral register
Reading~ta tokoro de ... nai
Meaning (EN)"no matter how much one does ~, (the negative result still holds)" — doing V doesn't change an already-bad outcome
Meaning (HI)कितनी भी बार/जितना भी ...करें, फिर भी ...नहीं (नकारात्मक परिणाम बना ही रहता है)
Meaning (GU)ભલે ગમે તેટલી વાર/જેટલું ...કરો, છતાં ...નહીં (નકારાત્મક પરિણામ રહે જ છે)
Connection (接続)Vた + ところで…ない　れい いくらVたところで／どんなにVたところで
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆悪い結果・状態が続くことが多い (a bad result or state usually continues regardless).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
何回読んだところで、意味は全くわからない。Nankai yonda tokoro de, imi wa mattaku wakaranai. I can't understand it no matter how many times I read it. कितनी भी बार पढ़ लूँ, मतलब बिल्कुल समझ नहीं आता। ભલે ગમે તેટલી વાર વાંચું, અર્થ બિલકુલ સમજાતો નથી.
そんな多額の借金は、家を売ったところで、到底返せない。Sonna tagaku no shakkin wa, ie o utta tokoro de, toutei kaesenai. Even if we sell this house, we won't be able to repay such a huge debt. इतना बड़ा क़र्ज़, घर बेच देने पर भी, किसी तरह नहीं चुकाया जा सकता। આટલું મોટું દેવું, ઘર વેચી દેવા છતાં, કોઈ પણ રીતે ચૂકવી શકાય નહીં.

Note the past-tense-looking Vた before ところで — despite the ta-form, the meaning is not "having done V" but "even if one were to do V." This concessive-ta pattern always sets up a negative or disappointing continuation.

【4】 〜としたところで／としたって

neutral register
Reading~to shita tokoro de / ~to shitatte
Meaning (EN)"even if one tries to ~, (it won't help/matter)" — a concessive: attempting V doesn't change the outcome
Meaning (HI)...करने की कोशिश करने पर भी (कुछ फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता)
Meaning (GU)...કરવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવા છતાં (કંઈ ફરક પડતો નથી)
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain） + としたところで／としたって💬 としたって is more casual/spoken
RegisterNeutral; としたって leans casual/spoken
Typical useDismissing an attempt as futile — "even trying won't be enough."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
これは忘れようとしたところで、忘れられない出来事だ。Kore wa wasureyou to shita tokoro de, wasurerarenai dekigoto da. This is an incident I will never forget even if I try hard. यह ऐसी घटना है जिसे भूलने की कोशिश करने पर भी नहीं भुलाया जा सकता। આ એવો બનાવ છે જેને ભૂલવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવા છતાં ભૂલી શકાય નહીં.
今から急いだとしたって間に合わないだろう。Ima kara isoida to shitatte ma ni awanai darou. We won't make it even if we hurry now. अभी से जल्दी करने पर भी शायद समय पर नहीं पहुँच पाएँगे। હવેથી ઉતાવળ કરવા છતાં કદાચ સમયસર નહીં પહોંચી શકાય.

としたって is simply the contracted, conversational version of としたところで — same meaning, lighter register. Both mean roughly the same as としても, but carry a slightly more resigned, "it's futile anyway" tone.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.25. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.28 (7日目's third page).

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

どんなに（　）、絶対に間に合わないだろう。Donna ni (___), zettai ni ma ni awanai darou.

Full sentence: どんなに走ったところで、絶対に間に合わないだろう。Donna ni hashitta tokoro de, zettai ni ma ni awanai darou.
EN: No matter how much you run, you definitely won't make it in time.
HI: कितनी भी दौड़ लो, यक़ीनन समय पर नहीं पहुँच पाओगे।
GU: ભલે ગમે તેટલું દોડો, ચોક્કસ સમયસર નહીં પહોંચી શકાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a走るところをhashiru tokoro othe moment of running (wrong pattern)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b走ったところでhashitta tokoro deno matter how much one runsकितनी भी दौड़ लोભલે ગમે તેટલું દોડો

Why: どんなに ("no matter how") is the classic signal for Point 3's 〜たところで…ない; ところを would just mark a moment in time, not a futile effort.

Q2

お忙しい（　）お集まりいただき、ありがとうございます。Oisogashii (___) oatsumari itadaki, arigatou gozaimasu.

Full sentence: お忙しいところをお集まりいただき、ありがとうございます。Oisogashii tokoro o oatsumari itadaki, arigatou gozaimasu.
EN: Thank you for gathering here despite being busy.
HI: व्यस्त होते हुए भी आने के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: વ્યસ્ત હોવા છતાં આવવા બદલ આભાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aところをtokoro oat a moment when (thanking for the state)उस स्थिति मेंતે સ્થિતિમાં
bところでtokoro deeven if (wrong sense — no concession here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This is the classic polite set phrase from Point 1 — thanking someone for their state (being busy) at the moment they came; ところで would turn it into a "no matter how busy" concession, which isn't a natural thank-you.

Q3

「CDは月にどのくらい買うの。」「だいたい5枚（　）ね。」"CD wa tsuki ni dono kurai kau no." "Daitai go-mai (___) ne."

Full sentence: 「CDは月にどのくらい買うの。」「だいたい5枚というところだね。」"CD wa tsuki ni dono kurai kau no." "Daitai go-mai to iu tokoro da ne."
EN: "About how many CDs do you buy a month?" "About 5, I'd say."
HI: "महीने में कितने सीडी ख़रीदते हो?" "लगभग 5, मैं कहूँगा।"
GU: "મહિનામાં કેટલી સીડી ખરીદો છો?" "લગભગ 5, હું કહીશ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aというところだto iu tokoro daabout, roughlyलगभगલગભગ
bところだtokoro da(missing the という that makes this an estimate)(अपूर्ण रूप)(અપૂર્ણ સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A rough quantity estimate needs Point 2's full 〜というところだ; bare ところだ (without という) is a different, unrelated pattern meaning "just about to."

Q4

彼の借金は、休みなく毎日働いた（　）、返せるような額ではない。Kare no shakkin wa, yasumi naku mainichi hataraita (___), kaeseru you na gaku de wa nai.

Full sentence: 彼の借金は、休みなく毎日働いたところで、返せるような額ではない。Kare no shakkin wa, yasumi naku mainichi hataraita tokoro de, kaeseru you na gaku de wa nai.
EN: His debt is not an amount that could be repaid even if he worked every day without rest.
HI: उसका क़र्ज़ इतना है कि बिना छुट्टी लगातार काम करने पर भी नहीं चुकाया जा सकता।
GU: તેનું દેવું એટલું છે કે વગર રજા રોજ કામ કરવા છતાં ચૂકવી શકાય નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aところをtokoro oat the moment of (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bところでtokoro deno matter how muchकरने पर भीકરવા છતાં

Q5

あの頑固な父にタバコをやめさせよう（　）、無駄だよ。Ano ganko na chichi ni tabako o yamesaseyou (___), muda da yo.

Full sentence: あの頑固な父にタバコをやめさせようとしたって、無駄だよ。Ano ganko na chichi ni tabako o yamesaseyou to shitatte, muda da yo.
EN: Even if you try to make that stubborn father of mine quit smoking, it's pointless.
HI: उस ज़िद्दी पापा को सिगरेट छुड़वाने की कोशिश करने पर भी, कोई फ़ायदा नहीं।
GU: તે હઠીલા પપ્પાને સિગારેટ છોડાવવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવા છતાં, કંઈ ફાયદો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにしたってni shitatte(wrong particle before したって)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとしたってto shitatteeven if one tries toकोशिश करने पर भीપ્રયત્ન કરવા છતાં

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

どんなにやった＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 変えることはできない。Donna ni yatta ___ kaeru koto wa dekinai.

Tiles: 1 ところで (tokoro de)　2 後悔した (koukai shita)　3 ことを (koto o)　4 過去を (kako o)

3 ことを→2 後悔した→1 ところで→4 過去を

どんなにやったことを後悔したところで、過去を変えることはできない。Donna ni yatta koto o koukai shita tokoro de, kako o kaeru koto wa dekinai.

EN: No matter how much you regret what you did, you cannot change the past.
HI: जो किया उसका कितना भी पश्चाताप करो, अतीत नहीं बदला जा सकता।
GU: જે કર્યું તેનો ભલે ગમે તેટલો પસ્તાવો કરો, ભૂતકાળ બદલી શકાય નહીં.

Why: Built on Point 3 (〜たところで…ない) — however much regret follows, the negative fact (can't change the past) still holds.

Q7

トイレはもちろんキッチンもついていて、この車は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ です。Toire wa mochiron kitchin mo tsuite ite, kono kuruma wa ___ desu.

Tiles: 1 といった (to itta)　2 まさに (masani)　3 走る家 (hashiru ie)　4 ところ (tokoro)

2 まさに→3 走る家→1 といった→4 ところ

トイレはもちろんキッチンもついていて、この車はまさに走る家といったところです。Toire wa mochiron kitchin mo tsuite ite, kono kuruma wa masani hashiru ie to itta tokoro desu.

EN: It has a kitchen as well as a toilet, of course — this car is precisely what you'd call "a house on wheels."
HI: इसमें टॉयलेट के साथ किचन भी है — यह गाड़ी बिल्कुल "चलता-फिरता घर" कहलाने लायक है।
GU: તેમાં ટોઇલેટ સાથે કિચન પણ છે — આ ગાડી ખરેખર "ચાલતું ઘર" કહી શકાય એવી છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜といったところだ) — まさに ("precisely") reinforces the estimate, likening the car to a "house on wheels" as an apt, roughly-accurate description.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns share 所／ところ, but ところを, ところで, and というところだ do three unrelated jobs. Compare them:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ところを~tokoro o Right at the moment when ~ V／A（plain）／なな／Nの + ところを Freezes a single moment/state as the backdrop for something else. Thanking/apologizing for someone's state at an inconvenient time.
〜というところだ~to iu tokoro da It's about ~ N／[文] + というところだ A rough estimate of quantity or progress. Answering "how much/how far along?"
〜たところで…ない~ta tokoro de...nai No matter how much ~ Vた + ところで…ない A concessive: the effort doesn't fix an already-bad outcome. "Even doing X repeatedly, Y (bad) still holds."
〜としたところで／としたって~to shita tokoro de/shitatte Even if one tries to ~ V／A／な／N（plain） + としたところで／としたって A concessive on the attempt itself (volitional/hypothetical), not a completed action. "Even trying to do X won't help."
Exam trap: 〜たところで and 〜としたところで look almost identical (both end …ところで) but attach to different things: 〜たところで takes a plain past verb describing something actually done (Q4's 働いた), while 〜としたところで takes と + a volitional/hypothetical verb describing a mere attempt (Q5's やめさせようと). If the verb before ところで is in plain past form with no と, it's Point 3; if there's a と right before ところで/したって, it's Point 4.
