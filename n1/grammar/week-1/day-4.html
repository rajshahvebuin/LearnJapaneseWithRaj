4日目 — 私に言わせれば (Watashi ni iwasereba)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.20–21 (PDF pages 21–22)
Grammar points covered
〜とされる自発の受身形（〜られる）〜させられる（自発の使役受身形）Nに言わせれば
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.23 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Three of today's four points are all passive-shaped (とされる／られる／させられる) but describe three different things: a social consensus, an involuntary feeling, and an emotion someone else's action triggers in you. Register warning: 自発の受身形 is tagged 硬 formal/written and works with only a small, fixed set of perception/emotion verbs — don't try to make new ones freely.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とされる

neutral register
Reading~to sareru
Meaning (EN)"it is considered/held to be ~" — states a widely-accepted view, classification, or belief as if it were established fact
Meaning (HI)...माना जाता है / ...समझा जाता है
Meaning (GU)...મનાય છે / ...ગણાય છે
Connection (接続)V／A（plain）／な／N + とされる
RegisterNeutral, but leans formal/objective — common in news reports and academic writing
Typical useReporting a consensus, belief, or classification without committing the speaker to it personally.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + とされる促進するとされるsokushin suru to sareru
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい + とされる危険だとされるkiken da to sareru
な-adjectiveNaだ／na + とされる一番の問題（だ）とされているichiban no mondai (da) to sarete iru
NounN（だ） + とされる通説（だ）とされるtsuusetsu (da) to sareru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この国には、よい指導者がいないのが一番の問題（だ）とされている。Kono kuni ni wa, yoi shidousha ga inai no ga ichiban no mondai (da) to sarete iru. The biggest problem in this country is considered to be that there are no good leaders. इस देश में सबसे बड़ी समस्या यह मानी जाती है कि अच्छे नेता नहीं हैं। આ દેશમાં સૌથી મોટી સમસ્યા એ મનાય છે કે સારા નેતાઓ નથી.
この物質は植物の生長を促進するとされる。Kono busshitsu wa shokubutsu no seichou o sokushin suru to sareru. This substance is believed to promote plant growth. माना जाता है कि यह पदार्थ पौधों की वृद्धि को बढ़ावा देता है। મનાય છે કે આ પદાર્થ છોડની વૃદ્ધિને પ્રોત્સાહન આપે છે.

とされる is the "it is said/believed that" of objective reporting — it distances the speaker from personally vouching for the claim, which is exactly why it's everywhere in news and research writing.

【2】 自発の受身形 〜（ら）れる

硬 formal/written
Readingjihatsu no ukemikei (spontaneous-passive form)
Meaning (EN)"~ spontaneously happens/wells up" — describes an involuntary thought or feeling that arises on its own, independent of the subject's will
Meaning (HI)अपने आप ...महसूस होना/याद आना (इच्छा के बिना)
Meaning (GU)આપમેળે ...અનુભવાય/યાદ આવે (ઇચ્છા વગર)
Connection (接続)（〜が）V（ら）れる — a small fixed set of perception/emotion verbs
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical use◆意志と関係なく、ひとりでにその状態になるという意味 (means becoming that state by itself, unrelated to one's will).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この曲を聴くと、学生時代のことが思い出される。Kono kyoku o kiku to, gakusei jidai no koto ga omoidasareru. This song reminds me of my student days. यह गाना सुनते ही अपने आप छात्र जीवन की यादें ताज़ा हो जाती हैं। આ ગીત સાંભળતાં જ આપમેળે વિદ્યાર્થી જીવનની યાદો તાજી થાય છે.
彼にひどいことを言ってしまったことが悔やまれる。Kare ni hidoi koto o itte shimatta koto ga kuyamareru. I regret saying such a terrible thing to him. उससे इतनी बुरी बात कह देने का अफ़सोस अपने आप होता है। તેને આટલી ખરાબ વાત કહી દેવાનો અફસોસ આપોઆપ થાય છે.

This is not the ordinary passive (someone did something to someone) — it's the same られる ending used for a different job: marking that a thought or feeling arose by itself. The book's own れい list of other fixed members worth memorizing: 〜と考えられる (it is naturally concluded that), 懐かしく思われる (feels nostalgic, unbidden), 完成が待たれる (its completion is eagerly awaited), 息子の将来が案じられる (one can't help worrying about a son's future). Only a limited set of verbs work this way — don't extend the pattern to arbitrary verbs.

【3】 〜させられる（自発の使役受身形）

neutral–formal register
Reading~saserareru (jihatsu no shieki-ukemikei — spontaneous causative-passive)
Meaning (EN)"cannot help but feel/do ~" — something external triggers an emotion or reaction in you involuntarily (literally "is made to feel ~")
Meaning (HI)...महसूस किए बिना नहीं रह पाना (अनायास ऐसा महसूस होना)
Meaning (GU)...અનુભવ્યા વગર રહી ન શકાય (આપોઆપ એવું અનુભવાય)
Connection (接続)V（causative-passive stem） + させられる
RegisterNeutral to formal
Typical useSomething someone else does (their skill, their noise, their attitude) provokes an emotional reaction in the speaker that they couldn't suppress.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-causative-passive + させられる感心させられるkanshin saserareru
い-adjective(added, not in book — not a natural fit; this pattern targets verbs of feeling/reacting)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; same reason)—
Noun(added, not in book; same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼の仕事ぶりには感心させられる。Kare no shigotoburi ni wa kanshin saserareru. I am impressed with his work. उसके काम करने के तरीक़े से मैं प्रभावित हुए बिना नहीं रह पाता/पाती। તેની કામ કરવાની રીતથી હું પ્રભાવિત થયા વગર રહી શકતો/શકતી નથી.
隣の家の騒音に悩まされている。Tonari no ie no souon ni nayamasarete iru. I am troubled by the noise next door. पड़ोस के घर के शोर से परेशान हूँ। પડોશના ઘરના અવાજથી પરેશાન છું.

The book's own れい list of other common members: 考えさせられる (made to reflect), 感じさせられる (made to feel). Compare this closely with Point 2 — both describe involuntary reactions, but here there's always an external trigger (someone/something causing it), whereas Point 2's られる can arise with no clear external agent at all (a memory just surfaces).

【4】 Nに言わせれば

neutral register
ReadingN ni iwasereba
Meaning (EN)"in N's opinion / if you ask N" — introduces a personal viewpoint, often one that contrasts with a more common or expected view
Meaning (HI)N की राय में / N से पूछा जाए तो
Meaning (GU)N ના મત મુજબ / N ને પૂછો તો
Connection (接続)N（人） + に言わせれば／から言わせれば　◆N=人
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSets up the speaker's (or a named person's) opinion, typically right after describing what "everyone else" or "common wisdom" says.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は天才と言われているが、私に言わせれば、単なる努力家だ。Kare wa tensai to iwarete iru ga, watashi ni iwasereba, tan naru doryokuka da. Some people think he's a genius, but I would say he is just a hard worker. लोग उसे प्रतिभाशाली मानते हैं, लेकिन मेरी राय में वह बस एक मेहनती इंसान है। લોકો તેને પ્રતિભાશાળી માને છે, પણ મારા મત મુજબ તે ફક્ત મહેનતુ વ્યક્તિ છે.
「もう私も60歳を過ぎて、年をとりましたよ。」「いえ、うちの親に言わせれば、まだまだ若いそうですよ。」"Mou watashi mo rokujussai o sugite, toshi o torimashita yo." "Ie, uchi no oya ni iwasereba, madamada wakai sou desu yo." "I am already over 60 years old. I am old." "Not at all. My parents think you're still young." "मैं भी अब 60 साल पार कर चुका/चुकी हूँ, बूढ़ा/बूढ़ी हो गया/गई हूँ।" "नहीं, मेरे माता-पिता की राय में आप अभी भी जवान हैं।" "હું પણ હવે 60 વર્ષથી વધુનો/ની થઈ ગયો/ગઈ." "ના, મારા માતાપિતાના મતે તમે હજુ પણ યુવાન છો."

Nに言わせれば and Nから言わせれば are interchangeable — both mean "from N's point of view." This is the day's title pattern and pairs naturally with a preceding が-clause stating the opposing/common view.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.21. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.23.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

新しいロケットの完成が（　）。Atarashii roketto no kansei ga (___).

Full sentence: 新しいロケットの完成が待たれます。Atarashii roketto no kansei ga mataremasu.
EN: The completion of the new rocket is eagerly awaited.
HI: नए रॉकेट के पूरा होने का बेसब्री से इंतज़ार किया जा रहा है।
GU: નવા રોકેટના પૂર્ણ થવાની આતુરતાથી રાહ જોવાઈ રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a待たされますmatasaremasuis made to wait (ordinary causative-passive, wrong subject)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b待たれますmatearemasuis eagerly awaited (自発の受身形)बेसब्री से प्रतीक्षित हैઆતુરતાથી અપેક્ષિત છે

Why: Point 2's られる describes the completion being (spontaneously/naturally) awaited; 待たされます would mean a person is being made to wait by someone — the wrong picture entirely.

Q2

彼は社会人として必要（　）知識に欠ける。Kare wa shakaijin toshite hitsuyou (___) chishiki ni kakeru.

Full sentence: 彼は社会人として必要とされている知識に欠ける。Kare wa shakaijin toshite hitsuyou to sarete iru chishiki ni kakeru.
EN: He lacks the knowledge considered necessary for a working member of society.
HI: उसमें एक ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति के लिए ज़रूरी माने जाने वाले ज्ञान की कमी है।
GU: તેનામાં સમાજના જવાબદાર સભ્ય માટે જરૂરી ગણાતા જ્ઞાનની ખામી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとされているto sarete iruconsidered/regarded asमाना जाता हैગણાય છે
bにしているni shite iru(wrong pattern; means "makes it X")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: "Knowledge considered necessary" is a social consensus, exactly Point 1's とされる; にしている would mean someone actively makes something into X, which doesn't fit describing widely-held knowledge standards.

Q3

我々の世代の人間に（　）、彼の行動は普通ではない。Wareware no sedai no ningen ni (___), kare no koudou wa futsuu de wa nai.

Full sentence: 我々の世代の人間に言わせれば、彼の行動は普通ではない。Wareware no sedai no ningen ni iwasereba, kare no koudou wa futsuu de wa nai.
EN: As far as people of our generation are concerned, his behavior isn't normal.
HI: हमारी पीढ़ी के लोगों की राय में, उसका व्यवहार सामान्य नहीं है।
GU: અમારી પેઢીના લોકોના મતે, તેનું વર્તન સામાન્ય નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a言わせればiwaserebaif you ask, in the opinion ofकी राय मेंના મતે
b言われればiwarerebaif (something) is said (ordinary passive conditional, wrong fit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 4's Nに言わせれば introduces "in N's opinion"; 言われれば is just the ordinary passive conditional of 言う ("if it's said") and doesn't attach the intended "opinion-holder" meaning to 我々の世代の人間に.

Q4

それは、死について真剣に（　）映画だった。Sore wa, shi ni tsuite shinken ni (___) eiga datta.

Full sentence: それは、死について真剣に考えさせられる映画だった。Sore wa, shi ni tsuite shinken ni kangaesaserareru eiga datta.
EN: It was a movie that made me seriously think about death.
HI: वह एक ऐसी फ़िल्म थी जिसने मुझे मौत के बारे में गंभीरता से सोचने पर मजबूर कर दिया।
GU: તે એવી ફિલ્મ હતી જેણે મને મૃત્યુ વિશે ગંભીરતાથી વિચારવા મજબૂર કર્યો/કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a考えされるkangaesareru(not a valid conjugation)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
b考えさせられるkangaesaserarerumade to think, cannot help but thinkसोचने पर मजबूर होनाવિચારવા મજબૂર થવું

Why: This is Point 3's causative-passive 考えさせられる — the movie (external trigger) provokes reflection the viewer couldn't help; 考えされる simply isn't a correctly formed verb.

Q5

この新薬は、効果はあるが、副作用が強い（　）。Kono shinyaku wa, kouka wa aru ga, fukusayou ga tsuyoi (___).

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

若者の海外移住が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ そうだ。Wakamono no kaigai ijuu ga ___ sou da.

Tiles: 1 ことで (koto de)　2 その国の将来が (sono kuni no shourai ga)　3 増加している (zouka shite iru)　4 案じられている (anjirarete iru)

3 増加している→1 ことで→2 その国の将来が→4 案じられている

若者の海外移住が増加していることで、その国の将来が案じられているそうだ。Wakamono no kaigai ijuu ga zouka shite iru koto de, sono kuni no shourai ga anjirarete iru sou da.

EN: I hear that because the emigration of young people is increasing, that country's future is a matter of concern.
HI: कहते हैं कि युवाओं के विदेश जाकर बसने की संख्या बढ़ने से उस देश के भविष्य को लेकर चिंता है।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે યુવાનોના વિદેશ સ્થળાંતરમાં વધારો થવાથી તે દેશના ભવિષ્ય અંગે ચિંતા છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's 自発の受身形 — 案じられている describes worry about the country's future arising spontaneously/naturally among observers, triggered by the rising emigration.

Q7

彼の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ そんなのはただの甘えだ。Kare no ___ sonna no wa tada no amae da.

Tiles: 1 私に言わせれば (watashi ni iwasereba)　2 やる気がないように (yaruki ga nai you ni)　3 言葉からは (kotoba kara wa)　4 感じられたが (kanjirareta ga)

3 言葉からは→2 やる気がないように→4 感じられたが→1 私に言わせれば

彼の言葉からはやる気がないように感じられたが、私に言わせれば、そんなのはただの甘えだ。Kare no kotoba kara wa yaruki ga nai you ni kanjirareta ga, watashi ni iwasereba, sonna no wa tada no amae da.

EN: From his words it felt like he lacked motivation, but in my opinion, that's just him making excuses.
HI: उसकी बातों से लग रहा था कि उसमें उत्साह की कमी है, लेकिन मेरी राय में यह बस बहानेबाज़ी है।
GU: તેની વાતો પરથી લાગતું હતું કે તેનામાં ઉત્સાહની ખામી છે, પણ મારા મતે આ ફક્ત બહાનાબાજી છે.

Why: 感じられた (Point 2's spontaneous-passive) reports the surface impression his words gave; 私に言わせれば (Point 4) then pivots to the speaker's own, contrasting judgment.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of today's patterns end in 〜れる／られる, tempting learners to treat them as one thing. They aren't:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とされる~to sareru Considered/said to be ~ V／A／な／N + とされる A social consensus or classification — no involuntary feeling involved. Reporting a widely-held belief objectively.
自発の受身形jihatsu no ukemikei ~ spontaneously arises （〜が）V（ら）れる — fixed verb set An involuntary thought/feeling with no clear external trigger. "A memory just comes back," "completion is awaited."
〜させられる~saserareru Cannot help but feel/do ~ V-causative-passive + させられる An involuntary reaction with a clear external trigger (someone/something causes it). "His skill impresses me," "the noise troubles me."
Nに言わせればN ni iwasereba In N's opinion N（人） + に／から言わせれば Not a passive at all — introduces a stated personal viewpoint. Contrasting your own opinion against a common assumption.
Exam trap: 自発の受身形 and 〜させられる both describe feelings you "can't help" having, but ask: is there an external cause? 隣の家の騒音に悩まされている (troubled by the neighbor's noise) has a clear trigger → させられる/される family. 学生時代のことが思い出される (a memory just surfaces) has no named trigger, just an internal, spontaneous state → plain 自発の受身形. Day 1's exam-trap habit applies here too: check what's doing the causing before picking the form.
