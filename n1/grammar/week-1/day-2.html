2日目 — できるものとして (Dekiru mono to shite)

Answer key

Notes
Two related but distinct patterns share the word もの today: 〜ものとして (treating something as a given premise) and 〜ものとする (formally stipulating a rule, tagged 硬 formal/written — common in contracts). Don't let the shared もの fool you into thinking they're interchangeable.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜くらいなら／ぐらいなら

neutral register
Reading~kurai nara / ~gurai nara
Meaning (EN)"rather than do ~ (I'd prefer anything else)" — marks the preceding action as so undesirable that even an extreme alternative is preferable
Meaning (HI)...करने से तो बेहतर है कि... — X करना इतना नापसंद है कि कोई भी विकल्प उससे बेहतर लगता है
Meaning (GU)...કરવા કરતાં તો... — X કરવું એટલું અણગમતું છે કે ગમે તે વિકલ્પ વધુ સારો લાગે
Connection (接続)Vる + くらいなら／ぐらいなら
RegisterNeutral, very common in spoken Japanese
Typical use◆とてもいやだという気持ち (expresses "I really don't want to do that"). Often paired with 〜ほうがましだ／〜ほうがいい／〜なさい in the following clause.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + くらいなら死ぬくらいならshinu kurai nara
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい + くらいならまずいくらいならmazui kurai nara
な-adjective(added, not in book) Naな + くらいなら不便くらいならfuben kurai nara
Noun(added, not in book) N + くらいなら借金くらいならshakkin kurai nara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
そんなことをするくらいなら、死んだほうがましだ。Sonna koto o suru kurai nara, shinda hou ga mashi da. I would rather die than do that. ऐसा करने से तो मरना बेहतर है। એવું કરવા કરતાં તો મરી જવું સારું.
友達を傷つけるくらいなら、自分が我慢したほうがいい。Tomodachi o kizutsukeru kurai nara, jibun ga gaman shita hou ga ii. I would rather tolerate it than hurt my friend's feelings. दोस्त को दुख पहुँचाने से तो बेहतर है ख़ुद सह लेना। મિત્રને દુઃખ પહોંચાડવા કરતાં પોતે સહન કરી લેવું સારું.

This is the most casual/spoken of today's four patterns — reach for it when venting strong reluctance in conversation. The clause after くらいなら almost always names a preferred alternative, however extreme (even "dying").

【2】 〜くらい／ぐらいのものだ

neutral register
Reading~kurai no mono da / ~gurai no mono da
Meaning (EN)"it's only/no more than N" — limits the scope to exactly one person/thing, implying nothing/no one else qualifies
Meaning (HI)बस/सिर्फ़ N ही है (इसके अलावा कोई/कुछ नहीं)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત/બસ N જ છે (એના સિવાય કોઈ/કંઈ નહીં)
Connection (接続)(〜のは) N + くらい／ぐらい + のものだ／なものだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAlmost always follows a "〜のは" clause identifying who/what is being singled out as the sole qualifier.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + くらい／ぐらいのものだ君ぐらいのものだkimi gurai no mono da
い-adjective(added, not in book — this pattern targets people/things, not descriptive states)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; rare) Naな + ぐらいなものだ静かぐらいなものだshizuka gurai na mono da
Verb(added, not in book — not a typical use of this pattern)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼が仕事を辞めないように説得できるのは、君ぐらいのものだ。Kare ga shigoto o yamenai you ni settoku dekiru no wa, kimi gurai no mono da. You are the only one who can talk him out of quitting work. उसे नौकरी न छोड़ने के लिए मनाने वाला बस तुम ही हो। તેને નોકરી ન છોડવા મનાવી શકે એવો ફક્ત તું જ છે.
君が受かるのは、このランクの大学ぐらいのものだ。Kimi ga ukaru no wa, kono ranku no daigaku gurai no mono da. You can only aim for the universities at this level. तुम बस इसी दर्जे के कॉलेजों में ही पास हो सकते हो। તું ફક્ત આ સ્તરની કૉલેજોમાં જ પાસ થઈ શકે એમ છે.

Don't confuse this with くらい used for rough quantity ("about N") — here くらい／ぐらい is doing limiting work ("only," "no more than"), which is why のものだ always follows.

【3】 〜ものとして

neutral register
Reading~mono to shite
Meaning (EN)"treating/regarding it as ~, on the assumption that ~" — takes a premise as given and acts on that basis
Meaning (HI)...मानकर, ...माने हुए (ऐसा मानते हुए कि...)
Meaning (GU)...માનીને, ...ગણીને (એવું ધારીને કે...)
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain form） + ものとして　❶ naだ→である／な, Nだ→である
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSets up an assumption the rest of the sentence builds on — "assuming X is the case, (I did/we'll do) Y."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる／Vない + ものとしてできるものとしてdekiru mono to shite
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい + ものとして難しいものとしてmuzukashii mono to shite
な-adjectiveNaである／な + ものとして簡単であるものとしてkantan de aru mono to shite
NounNである + ものとして既定路線であるものとしてkitei rosen de aru mono to shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
中級漢字はできるものとして、上級漢字のクラスを取った。Chuukyuu kanji wa dekiru mono to shite, jyoukyuu kanji no kurasu o totta. I took the advanced kanji class assuming that I was beyond the intermediate level. यह मानते हुए कि मुझे मध्यम स्तर के कांजी आते हैं, मैंने उन्नत कांजी की क्लास ली। મધ્યમ સ્તરના કાંજી આવડે છે એમ માનીને, મેં ઉચ્ચ સ્તરના કાંજીનો ક્લાસ લીધો.
田中さんはもう来ないものとして、始めましょう。Tanaka-san wa mou konai mono to shite, hajimemashou. Let's presume Tanaka-san is not coming and start without him. यह मानकर कि तनाका-सान अब नहीं आएँगे, चलिए शुरू करते हैं। તનાકા-સાન હવે નહીં આવે એમ માનીને, ચાલો શરૂ કરીએ.

Think of ものとして as "treat X as the working premise." It's a private/personal assumption the speaker adopts — contrast this with Point 4 below, where ものとする fixes a premise as an official rule for everyone.

【4】 〜ものとする

硬 formal/written
Reading~mono to suru
Meaning (EN)"it is stipulated/agreed/decided that ~ shall be done" — formally fixes a rule, procedure, or agreement
Meaning (HI)यह तय/निर्धारित किया जाता है कि... (नियम/अनुबंध की भाषा)
Meaning (GU)એવું નક્કી/નિર્ધારિત કરવામાં આવે છે કે... (નિયમ/કરારની ભાષા)
Connection (接続)Vる + ものとする
Register硬 — formal, near-exclusively written
Typical use◆契約書などに多く出てくる表現 (an expression that appears often in contracts and official rules).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ものとする行うものとするokonau mono to suru
い-adjective(added, not in book — not a natural fit; this pattern fixes actions/rules, not descriptive states)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; same reason)—
Noun(added, not in book; same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
採用試験は、毎年10月に行うものとする。Saiyou shiken wa, maitoshi juu-gatsu ni okonau mono to suru. We administer the employment examination every October. भर्ती परीक्षा हर साल अक्टूबर में आयोजित की जाएगी, ऐसा तय है। ભરતી પરીક્ષા દર વર્ષે ઓક્ટોબરમાં લેવામાં આવશે, એમ નક્કી છે.
契約の延長は、双方の同意によるものとする。Keiyaku no enchou wa, souhou no doui ni yoru mono to suru. The extension of the contract must be agreed by both parties. अनुबंध का विस्तार दोनों पक्षों की सहमति पर निर्भर होगा, ऐसा तय है। કરારનું વિસ્તરણ બંને પક્ષોની સંમતિ પર આધારિત રહેશે, એમ નક્કી છે.

Whenever you see ものとする in a JLPT passage, expect a legalistic/regulatory tone — it functions like "shall" in English contract English. This is the pattern's single biggest exam signal.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

たぶん行けないと思うので、私はいない（　）話を進めてください。Tabun ikenai to omou node, watashi wa inai (___) hanashi o susumete kudasai.

Full sentence: たぶん行けないと思うので、私はいないものとして話を進めてください。Tabun ikenai to omou node, watashi wa inai mono to shite hanashi o susumete kudasai.
EN: I probably won't be able to go, so please proceed treating me as absent.
HI: शायद मैं नहीं जा पाऊँगा, तो कृपया यह मानकर आगे बढ़ें कि मैं वहाँ नहीं हूँ।
GU: કદાચ હું નહીં જઈ શકું, તો કૃપા કરીને એમ માનીને આગળ વધો કે હું ત્યાં નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aものとしてmono to shitetreating as, assumingमानकरમાનીને
b人としてhito to shiteas a person (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ものとして treats a premise ("I'm not there") as given; 人として means "as a person/in one's capacity as a human being," which doesn't fit "assume I'm absent."

Q2

愛する人が病気で苦しむのを見るくらいなら、（　）。Aisuru hito ga byouki de kurushimu no o miru kurai nara, (___).

Full sentence: 愛する人が病気で苦しむのを見るくらいなら、自分が病気になったほうがいい。Aisuru hito ga byouki de kurushimu no o miru kurai nara, jibun ga byouki ni natta hou ga ii.
EN: Rather than watch someone I love suffer from illness, I'd rather get sick myself.
HI: जिसे मैं प्यार करता/करती हूँ उसे बीमारी से तड़पते देखने से तो बेहतर है कि मैं ख़ुद बीमार पड़ जाऊँ।
GU: જેને હું પ્રેમ કરું છું તેને બીમારીથી તડપતું જોવા કરતાં તો હું પોતે બીમાર પડી જાઉં એ સારું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見ないほうがましだminai hou ga mashi dabetter not to look (doesn't resolve the comparison)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b自分が病気になったほうがいいjibun ga byouki ni natta hou ga iibetter to get sick myselfख़ुद बीमार पड़ना बेहतरપોતે બીમાર પડવું સારું

Why: くらいなら needs a genuine alternative action in the following clause; "not looking" doesn't actually replace the situation, while "getting sick myself" is a real (if extreme) alternative — exactly the shape this pattern needs.

Q3

転勤でそんな田舎に行かされる（　）、会社を辞めたほうがいい。Tenkin de sonna inaka ni ikasareru (___), kaisha o yameta hou ga ii.

Full sentence: 転勤でそんな田舎に行かされるくらいなら、会社を辞めたほうがいい。Tenkin de sonna inaka ni ikasareru kurai nara, kaisha o yameta hou ga ii.

HI: तबादले से ऐसे गाँव में भेजे जाने से तो बेहतर है नौकरी ही छोड़ देना।
GU: બદલીથી એવા ગામડામાં મોકલવામાં આવવા કરતાં તો નોકરી છોડી દેવી સારી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aくらいならkurai nararather than...से तो बेहतर...કરતાં તો સારું

HI: मैं जो घर ख़रीद सकता हूँ, वह बस इतना ही है।

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

昨日授業で教えた＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 次に進みます。Kinou jugyou de oshieta ___ tsugi ni susumimasu.

Tiles: 1 もの (mono)　2 ことは (koto wa)　3 として (to shite)　4 わかった (wakatta)

2 ことは→4 わかった→1 もの→3 として

昨日授業で教えたことはわかったものとして、次に進みます。Kinou jugyou de oshieta koto wa wakatta mono to shite, tsugi ni susumimasu.

EN: Assuming that what was taught in yesterday's class is understood, let's move on to the next part.
HI: यह मानते हुए कि कल की क्लास में जो पढ़ाया गया वह समझ आ गया है, अब आगे बढ़ते हैं।
GU: ગઈકાલના વર્ગમાં જે શીખવ્યું હતું તે સમજાઈ ગયું છે એમ માનીને, હવે આગળ વધીએ.

Why: Built on Point 3 (〜ものとして) — "ことはわかった" (the content is understood) becomes the assumed premise the class proceeds on.

Q7

そんなことを＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ほうがましだ。Sonna koto o ___ hou ga mashi da.

Tiles: 1 させられる (saserareru)　2 参加しない (sanka shinai)　3 なら (nara)　4 くらい (kurai)

1 させられる→4 くらい→3 なら→2 参加しない

そんなことをさせられるくらいなら、参加しないほうがましだ。Sonna koto o saserareru kurai nara, sanka shinai hou ga mashi da.

EN: Rather than being forced to do that, it's better not to participate.
HI: ऐसा करने पर मजबूर किए जाने से तो बेहतर है भाग ही न लेना।
GU: એવું કરવા મજબૂર થવા કરતાં તો ભાગ જ ન લેવો સારો.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜くらいなら) — させられる (being forced to do it, a causative-passive) is the rejected option, and 参加しないほうがましだ is the preferred alternative.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four patterns split cleanly into two confusable pairs — もの-as-comparison vs もの-as-premise. Compare them:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜くらいなら~kurai nara Rather than do ~ Vる + くらいなら Rejects one action in favor of a (possibly extreme) alternative. Venting that an action is so undesirable you'd take any alternative.
〜くらい／ぐらいのものだ~kurai/gurai no mono da It's only/no more than N N + くらい／ぐらいのものだ Limits scope to one person/thing — not a comparison, a restriction. Singling out the one qualifier, usually after a 〜のは clause.
〜ものとして~mono to shite Treating/assuming as ~ V／A／な／N（plain） + ものとして A personal/private assumption the speaker adopts for the moment. "Assuming X, I did/we'll do Y" — informal reasoning.
〜ものとする~mono to suru It is stipulated that ~ Vる + ものとする Formally fixes a rule for everyone — contract/regulation language. Legal or official documents (rules, contracts, procedures).
Exam trap: 〜ものとして and 〜ものとする look like simple conjugation variants of the same word (として vs とする) but serve very different jobs — として treats something as a private premise for reasoning ("let's proceed as if..."), while とする locks in an official rule ("it shall be done as..."). If the sentence sounds like a contract or regulation, it's とする; if it sounds like someone reasoning through a personal assumption, it's として.
