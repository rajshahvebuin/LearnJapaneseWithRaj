3日目 — 彼のことだから (Kare no koto da kara)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.18–19 (PDF pages 19–20)
Grammar points covered
〜ことのないよう（に）〜ことなしに（は）Nのことだから〜ことだから／ことだし〜ことにする／ことになる
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.21 (4日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Everything today shares the word こと, but splits into two very different jobs: precaution/condition patterns (〜ことのないように, 〜ことなしに) vs. reason/premise patterns (Nのことだから, 〜ことだから) vs. fiction/pretense patterns (〜ことにする／になる). Don't let the shared kanji fool you — see §3 for the full breakdown.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ことのないよう（に）

neutral register
Reading~koto no nai you (ni)
Meaning (EN)"so as not to ~" / "taking care that ~ doesn't happen" — a precaution taken to prevent an undesired action or state
Meaning (HI)...न हो, इसके लिए (सावधानी बरतते हुए)
Meaning (GU)...ન થાય એ માટે (સાવચેતી રાખીને)
Connection (接続)Vる + ことのないよう（に）
RegisterNeutral
Typical usePrecautionary advice, reminders, and warnings about avoiding a repeat mistake.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ことのないように慌てることのないようにawateru koto no nai you ni
い-adjective(added, not in book — this pattern targets avoidable actions, not descriptive states)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; same reason)—
Noun(added, not in book; same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
災害が来ても慌てることのないよう、日頃から準備しておこう。Saigai ga kite mo awateru koto no nai you, higoro kara junbi shite okou. We should be prepared for a disaster so that we can deal with it calmly when it comes. आपदा आने पर भी घबराए बिना सामना कर सकें, इसके लिए रोज़मर्रा में तैयारी रखें। આપત્તિ આવે તો પણ ગભરાયા વગર સામનો કરી શકીએ, એ માટે રોજિંદી તૈયારી રાખીએ.
今後、このような犯罪と関わることのないように注意してください。Kongo, kono you na hanzai to kakawaru koto no nai you ni chuui shite kudasai. Please make sure you do not get involved in this kind of crime again. आगे से ऐसे अपराध में शामिल न हों, इसका ध्यान रखें। હવેથી આવા ગુનામાં સંડોવાઈ ન જાઓ, તેનું ધ્યાન રાખો.

This reads almost like a softer, more deliberate cousin of 〜ないように (a form learners already know) — the extra こと nominalizes the whole avoided action, giving it slightly more formal weight.

【2】 〜ことなしに（は）

硬 formal/written
Reading~koto nashi ni (wa)
Meaning (EN)"without doing ~" — states that an outcome is impossible unless a prior action takes place
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना (औपचारिक भाषा में)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર (ઔપચારિક ભાષામાં)
Connection (接続)Vる + ことなしに（は）
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useAlmost always followed by a negative outcome (〜できない／〜見えてこない) — "you can't get Y without first doing X."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ことなしに知ることなしにshiru koto nashi ni
い-adjective(added, not in book — not a natural fit for this pattern)—
な-adjective(added, not in book; same reason)—
Noun(added, not in book; same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今の状況を知ることなしに、未来を予測することはできない。Ima no joukyou o shiru koto nashi ni, mirai o yosoku suru koto wa dekinai. It isn't possible to predict the future unless we understand the present situation. वर्तमान स्थिति को समझे बिना भविष्य का अनुमान लगाना संभव नहीं है। વર્તમાન સ્થિતિ સમજ્યા વગર ભવિષ્યની આગાહી કરવી શક્ય નથી.
過去の事例を見ることなしに、解決方法は見えてこないだろう。Kako no jirei o miru koto nashi ni, kaiketsu houhou wa miete konai darou. It will not be possible to find a solution without reviewing the past cases. पिछले उदाहरणों को देखे बिना समाधान का रास्ता नहीं दिखेगा। ભૂતકાળના ઉદાહરણો જોયા વગર ઉકેલનો રસ્તો નહીં દેખાય.

Functionally close to 〜ずに／〜ないで, but ことなしに carries a drier, written register — expect it in essays and formal writing, not casual speech.

【3】 Nのことだから

neutral register
ReadingN no koto dakara
Meaning (EN)"since it's [typical of] N, (I'm confident that)..." — bases a prediction on the subject's well-known character or habits
Meaning (HI)चूँकि यह N का जाना-पहचाना स्वभाव/आदत है, इसलिए यक़ीनन...
Meaning (GU)કેમ કે આ N નો જાણીતો સ્વભાવ/આદત છે, એટલે ચોક્કસ...
Connection (接続)N + のことだから
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆推量が続く (a guess/inference about the future or an unseen fact follows).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
よくできる彼のことだから、合格は間違いないでしょう。Yoku dekiru kare no koto dakara, goukaku wa machigai nai deshou. He will surely pass the exam because he is so bright. वह होशियार है, इसलिए यक़ीनन पास हो जाएगा। તે હોશિયાર છે, એટલે ચોક્કસ પાસ થઈ જશે.

The N here is always a person (or something personified) whose character the speaker already knows well — the whole sentence reads as "knowing them, obviously..."

【4】 〜ことだから／ことだし

neutral register
Reading~koto dakara / ~koto dashi
Meaning (EN)"given that ~ (is the case)" — a softer, more general reason marker than から alone, often leading into a decision
Meaning (HI)चूँकि ...है, तो (एक हल्का कारण-सूचक रूप)
Meaning (GU)કેમ કે ...છે, તો (હળવું કારણ દર્શાવતું સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（plain form） + ことだから／ことだし　❶ naだ→な／である, Nだ→である
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSets up a shared, observable fact as the basis for a suggestion or decision that follows.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
全員そろったことだから、時間前だけれど始めましょうか。Zen'in sorotta koto dakara, jikan mae dakeredo hajimemashou ka. It is not time yet but since everyone is here, let's start. सब लोग आ गए हैं, तो चलिए समय से पहले ही शुरू करते हैं। બધા આવી ગયા છે, તો ચાલો સમય પહેલાં જ શરૂ કરીએ.
もう誰も来ないことだし、店を閉めよう。Mou daremo konai koto dashi, mise o shimeyou. We'll have no customers so let's close the store. अब कोई नहीं आएगा, तो दुकान बंद कर देते हैं। હવે કોઈ નહીં આવે, તો દુકાન બંધ કરી દઈએ.

Unlike Point 3's Nのことだから (which is always about a person's character), this ことだから／ことだし attaches to any observable fact or situation, not just a person's known habits — compare them side by side in §3.

【5】 〜ことにする／ことになる

neutral register
Reading~koto ni suru / ~koto ni naru
Meaning (EN)"to treat/decide as if ~" (ことにする, a deliberate choice) / "to be established/treated as if ~" (ことになる, a resulting state) — ◆事実とは反対に扱うという意味 (means treating something contrary to the actual fact)
Meaning (HI)...ऐसा मान लेना/मान लिया जाना (भले ही असल में वैसा न हो)
Meaning (GU)...એવું માની લેવું/મનાવું (ભલે ખરેખર એવું ન હોય)
Connection (接続)Vた／Aかった／na（だ）／N（だ） + （という）ことにする／ことになる
RegisterNeutral
Typical usePretending, by mutual convention, that something happened (or didn't) contrary to what actually occurred.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この話は聞かなかったことにしてください。Kono hanashi wa kikanakatta koto ni shite kudasai. Please pretend that you did not hear that. यह बात मत सुनी हुई मान लीजिए, जैसे सुनी ही न हो। આ વાત ન સાંભળી હોય એમ માની લો.
彼は、大学を卒業したことになっているけれど、実際は中退したらしい。Kare wa, daigaku o sotsugyou shita koto ni natte iru keredo, jissai wa chuutai shita rashii. He tells everyone that he graduated from university but I gather he dropped out. वह कहता है कि उसने विश्वविद्यालय से स्नातक किया है, लेकिन असल में लगता है कि उसने पढ़ाई बीच में छोड़ दी। તે કહે છે કે તેણે યુનિવર્સિટીમાંથી સ્નાતક કર્યું છે, પણ ખરેખર લાગે છે કે તેણે ભણતર વચ્ચે જ છોડી દીધું.

ことにする is an active choice to treat something as X (usually by agreement — "let's just say..."); ことになっている describes a fiction that has already become the accepted story, which is why the second example pairs it with けれど実際は (but actually...).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.19. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.21.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

お互いが助け合うこと（　）、人間は生きていけない。Otagai ga tasukeau koto (___), ningen wa ikite ikenai.

Full sentence: お互いが助け合うことなしに、人間は生きていけない。Otagai ga tasukeau koto nashi ni, ningen wa ikite ikenai.
EN: Humans cannot survive without helping one another.
HI: एक-दूसरे की मदद किए बिना इंसान जी नहीं सकता।
GU: એકબીજાની મદદ કર્યા વગર માણસ જીવી શકતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなしにnashi niwithout...किए बिना...કર્યા વગર
bだからdakarabecause (wrong logic here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence needs "without helping each other, survival is impossible" — a condition, not a reason; ことなしに supplies exactly that, while ことだから would just restate helping each other as a cause.

Q2

明るい彼女のこと（　）、どこへ行ってもすぐ友達ができるだろう。Akarui kanojo no koto (___), doko e itte mo sugu tomodachi ga dekiru darou.

Full sentence: 明るい彼女のことだから、どこへ行ってもすぐ友達ができるだろう。Akarui kanojo no koto dakara, doko e itte mo sugu tomodachi ga dekiru darou.
EN: Since she's such a cheerful person, she'll surely make friends quickly wherever she goes.
HI: वह इतनी ख़ुशमिज़ाज है, तो कहीं भी जाए, जल्दी दोस्त बना लेगी।
GU: તે એટલી ખુશમિજાજ છે, તો ગમે ત્યાં જાય, જલદી મિત્રો બનાવી લેશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにしてni shite(not a valid form here)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
bだからdakarasince it's typical of herचूँकि यह उसका स्वभाव हैકેમ કે આ તેનો સ્વભાવ છે

Why: This is Point 3's Nのことだから — basing a confident prediction ("she'll make friends easily") on her known cheerful personality.

Q3

兄が書いたレポートを、自分が（　）提出した。Ani ga kaita repooto o, jibun ga (___) teishutsu shita.

Full sentence: 兄が書いたレポートを、自分が書いたことにして提出した。Ani ga kaita repooto o, jibun ga kaita koto ni shite teishutsu shita.

HI: बड़े भाई की लिखी रिपोर्ट को ख़ुद की लिखी हुई मानकर जमा कर दिया।
GU: મોટા ભાઈએ લખેલો રિપોર્ટ પોતે લખ્યો હોય એમ માનીને સુપરત કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a書いたことにしてkaita koto ni shitepretending to have written itख़ुद की लिखी मानकरપોતે લખ્યું હોય એમ માનીને

Q4

道に（　）、前もって地図で場所を確かめておいた。Michi ni (___), maemotte chizu de basho o tashikamete oita.

Full sentence: 道に迷うことのないように、前もって地図で場所を確かめておいた。Michi ni mayou koto no nai you ni, maemotte chizu de basho o tashikamete oita.
EN: So as not to get lost, I checked the location on a map beforehand.
HI: रास्ता न भटकूँ, इसलिए पहले ही नक़्शे में जगह देख ली।
GU: રસ્તો ન ભૂલાય એ માટે, પહેલેથી જ નકશામાં જગ્યા જોઈ લીધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a迷うことのないようにmayou koto no nai you niso as not to get lostभटकूँ नहीं इसलिएભૂલા ન પડાય એ માટે

Q5

Full sentence: 彼女は今まで苦労することなしに生きてきた。Kanojo wa ima made kurou suru koto nashi ni ikite kita.
EN: She has lived without ever experiencing hardship.
HI: वह अब तक बिना किसी तकलीफ़ के जी रही है।
GU: તે અત્યાર સુધી કોઈ પણ તકલીફ વગર જીવી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aするsuruplain non-past (correct form before ことなしに)सही रूपસાચું સ્વરૂપ
bしたshitapast tense (wrong form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

ケチで有名な彼＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 貸してくれないだろう。Kechi de yuumei na kare ___ kashite kurenai darou.

Tiles: 1 だって (datte)　2 だから (dakara)　3 のこと (no koto)　4 百円 (hyaku-en)

3 のこと→2 だから→4 百円→1 だって

ケチで有名な彼のことだから、百円だって貸してくれないだろう。Kechi de yuumei na kare no koto dakara, hyaku-en datte kashite kurenai darou.

EN: Since he's famous for being stingy, he probably won't even lend 100 yen.
HI: वह कंजूसी के लिए मशहूर है, तो सौ येन भी उधार नहीं देगा।
GU: તે કંજૂસાઈ માટે જાણીતો છે, તો સો યેન પણ ઉધાર નહીં આપે.

Why: Built on Point 3 (Nのことだから) — his well-known stinginess is the basis for confidently predicting he won't lend even a trivial amount (だって emphasizes "even").

Q7

その2つの関連性を＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ のは困難だ。Sono futatsu no kanrensei o ___ no wa konnan da.

Tiles: 1 こと (koto)　2 証明する (shoumei suru)　3 なしに (nashi ni)　4 解決する (kaiketsu suru)

2 証明する→1 こと→3 なしに→4 解決する

その2つの関連性を証明することなしに、解決するのは困難だ。Sono futatsu no kanrensei o shoumei suru koto nashi ni, kaiketsu suru no wa konnan da.

EN: Without proving the connection between the two, solving it is difficult.
HI: उन दोनों के बीच का संबंध साबित किए बिना, इसे सुलझाना मुश्किल है।
GU: બંને વચ્ચેનો સંબંધ સાબિત કર્યા વગર, તેને ઉકેલવું મુશ્કેલ છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜ことなしに) — proving the connection is the missing precondition for solving the problem.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every pattern today is built on こと, but they fall into three very different jobs — precaution, reasoning, and pretense. Compare them:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ことのないように~koto no nai you ni So as not to ~ Vる + ことのないように A precaution against a future mistake/accident. Giving advice or reminders ("be careful not to...").
〜ことなしに~koto nashi ni Without ~ [硬] Vる + ことなしに States a strict precondition — usually followed by a negative outcome. Formal writing: "Y is impossible without X first."
NのことだからN no koto dakara Since it's typical of N N + のことだから Reason based specifically on a person's known character. Predicting someone's behavior from their reputation.
〜ことだから／ことだし~koto dakara/dashi Given that ~ V／A／な／N + ことだから／ことだし Reason based on any observable fact, not just a person's character. Softly justifying a decision ("since that's the situation, let's...").
〜ことにする／になる~koto ni suru/naru To treat/be treated as if ~ Vた／Aかった／na（だ）／N（だ） + ことにする／になる Deliberately (にする) or by established convention (になる), treats something as contrary to fact. Pretending, or describing an accepted fiction.
Exam trap: Nのことだから and 〜ことだから look identical at a glance (both end in ことだから) but differ in what comes before: a bare person-noun + の (Nのことだから) always leans on that person's known character, while a full clause in plain form (〜ことだから) can justify a decision from any shared fact, not just personality. If the blank is immediately preceded by "〜の" attached to a person, it's the character-based reading.
