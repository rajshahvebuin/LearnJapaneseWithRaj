Notes
This day's "もっと！" box extends 用心するに越したことはない into a reduplicated intensifier (Vるなら(ば)Vる／Aければ Aい／早ければ早い), matching the day's own title. The book again crosses out an inserted する between a bare noun and に越したことはない — the same trap as Day 2's 想像に難くない, now a running theme this week: don't insert する after a bare noun before these に-idioms.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 明日でも差し支えない (ashita demo sashitsukaenai)

neutral register
ReadingV-ても／い-adj-くても／な-adj-でも／N-でも + 差し支えない
Meaning (EN)"it's fine even if ~ / no problem even with ~" — reassures that something poses no obstacle
Meaning (HI)...हो तो भी कोई हर्ज़ नहीं / ...से कोई परेशानी नहीं
Meaning (GU)...હોય તો પણ કોઈ વાંધો નથી / ...થી કોઈ તકલીફ નથી
Connection (接続)V-ても／Aくても／naでも／Nでも + 差し支えない (...temo/kutemo/de mo + sashitsukaenai)
RegisterNeutral, slightly polite — common in requests and official announcements
Typical useReassures someone that a later time, a combination, or a condition creates no problem; the negative form works too: 〜しなくても差し支えない ("it's fine even if you don't ~").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + でも + 差し支えない明日でも差し支えありませんashita demo sashitsukae arimasen
Verb (て-form)V-ても + 差し支えない併用しても差し支えないheiyou shite mo sashitsukaenai
Verb (negative)〜しなくても + 差し支えない(added, not in book) 来なくても差し支えないkonakute mo sashitsukaenai
い-adj／な-adjAくても／naでも + 差し支えない安くても差し支えないyasukute mo sashitsukaenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お返事は明日でも差し支えありません。Ohenji wa ashita demo sashitsukae arimasen. It will be fine if you give me your answer tomorrow. जवाब कल भी दें तो कोई हर्ज़ नहीं है। જવાબ કાલે પણ આપો તો કોઈ વાંધો નથી.
この薬はいつも飲んでいるビタミン剤と併用しても差し支えない。Kono kusuri wa itsumo nonde iru bitamin-zai to heiyou shite mo sashitsukaenai. There's no problem taking this medicine together with the vitamins you always take. यह दवा आप जो विटामिन हमेशा लेते हैं उसके साथ भी लेने में कोई परेशानी नहीं है। આ દવા તમે હંમેશા લેતા વિટામિન સાથે પણ લેવામાં કોઈ તકલીફ નથી.
もっと！ もし差し支えないようでしたら、お名前を伺えますか。Moshi sashitsukaenai you deshitara, onamae o ukagaemasu ka. If it's alright with you, may I ask your name? अगर कोई परेशानी न हो, तो क्या आपका नाम पूछ सकता/सकती हूँ? જો કોઈ તકલીફ ન હોય, તો શું તમારું નામ પૂછી શકું?

差し支える (さしつかえる) means "to hinder/cause a problem," so 差し支えない is its negative — "nothing gets in the way." もし差し支えないようでしたら ("if it's alright with you") is a very common polite cushion phrase before asking a personal question. Note the polite form is 差し支えありません (差し支え as a noun + ありません), not a directly-conjugated 差し支えません — see Quiz Q4.

【2】 用心するに越したことはない (youjin suru ni koshita koto wa nai)

neutral register
ReadingV-る／V-ない／Aい／Aくない／N(である) + に越したことはない
Meaning (EN)"it's best to ~ / nothing beats ~ing" — states the obviously superior, safest option
Meaning (HI)...करना ही सबसे अच्छा है / ...से बेहतर कुछ नहीं
Meaning (GU)...કરવું જ શ્રેષ્ઠ છે / ...કરતાં સારું બીજું કંઈ નથી
Connection (接続)Vる／Vないに越したことはない (V-ru/V-nai)；Aい／Aくないに越したことはない；N(である)に越したことはない trap: no する inserted after bare N
RegisterNeutral — very common in everyday advice-giving
Typical useDeclares that a certain choice (being cautious, having money, low price) is obviously the best one, almost too obvious to need saying.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + に越したことはない用心（する）に越したことはないyoujin (suru) ni koshita koto wa nai
い-adjectiveAい + に越したことはない安いに越したことはないyasui ni koshita koto wa nai
NounN + に越したことはない  trap: no する insertedお金はあるに越したことはないokane wa aru ni koshita koto wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
用心（する）に越したことはない。傘を持っていこう。Youjin (suru) ni koshita koto wa nai. Kasa o motte ikou. It's better to be prepared. Let's take umbrellas. सावधान रहना ही सबसे अच्छा है। छाता साथ ले चलते हैं। સાવચેત રહેવું જ શ્રેષ્ઠ છે. છત્રી સાથે લઈ જઈએ.
お金はあるに越したことはない。Okane wa aru ni koshita koto wa nai. It's better to have money (than not). पैसा हो तो बेहतर ही है। પૈસા હોય તો સારું જ છે.
安いに越したことはないが、品質が悪いものは買いたくない。Yasui ni koshita koto wa nai ga, hinshitsu ga warui mono wa kaitakunai. Cheaper is naturally better, but I don't want to buy something of poor quality. सस्ता होना तो अच्छा है, लेकिन घटिया क्वालिटी की चीज़ नहीं ख़रीदना चाहता/चाहती। સસ્તું હોવું તો સારું છે, પણ ખરાબ ગુણવત્તાની વસ્તુ ખરીદવી નથી.

越す (こす, "to exceed/surpass") negated in 越したことはない literally reads "there's nothing that surpasses ~ing" — i.e., nothing beats this option. Note the sentence stem before に越したことはない is always the plain/dictionary form (用心する, ある, 安い), never with a topic-marking は inserted mid-phrase. See the もっと！ box below for this pattern's reduplicated "the more X, the better" cousin.

【3】 早ければ早いに越したことはない (hayakereba hayai ni koshita koto wa nai) （もっと！ — extends Point 2）

neutral register
ReadingV-るなら(ば)V-る／Aければ Aい／naなら(ば)na(である)／Nなら(ば)N(である)／〜ないなら(ば) + に越したことはない
Meaning (EN)"the more/earlier/sooner ~, the better" — a reduplicated intensifier built on Point 2's pattern
Meaning (HI)जितना जल्दी/ज़्यादा ~ हो, उतना ही अच्छा
Meaning (GU)જેટલું વહેલું/વધારે ~ હોય, એટલું સારું
Connection (接続)同じ語を「なら(ば)」／「ければ」で繰り返して + に越したことはない (repeat the same word with nara(ba)/kereba, then ni koshita koto wa nai)
RegisterNeutral — a natural intensifying extension of Point 2
Typical useRepeats the same adjective/verb/noun in a conditional-then-plain pair to say the degree matters: the earlier/cheaper/more of it, the better — this day's own title, 早ければ早いに越したことはない, is a textbook example.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAければ + Aい + に越したことはない早ければ早いに越したことはないhayakereba hayai ni koshita koto wa nai
い-adjectiveAければ + Aい + に越したことはない安ければ安いに越したことはないyasukereba yasui ni koshita koto wa nai
Verb／な-adj／N(added, not in book) Vるなら(ば)Vる／naなら(ば)na(である)／Nなら(ば)N(である) — same reduplication logic, e.g. 多ければ多いに越したことはない多ければ多いに越したことはないookereba ooi ni koshita koto wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
レポートの提出は、早ければ早いに越したことはない。Repooto no teishutsu wa, hayakereba hayai ni koshita koto wa nai. It would be better if you could submit your paper earlier — the earlier the better. रिपोर्ट जमा करना जितना जल्दी हो सके उतना ही अच्छा है। રિપોર્ટ સબમિટ કરવો જેટલો વહેલો થાય એટલો સારો.
同じ商品だったら、安ければ安いに越したことはない。Onaji shouhin dattara, yasukereba yasui ni koshita koto wa nai. If it's the same product, the cheaper the better. अगर वही सामान है, तो जितना सस्ता हो उतना ही अच्छा। જો એ જ વસ્તુ હોય, તો જેટલી સસ્તી હોય એટલી સારી.

This is the same に越したことはない from Point 2, but with the same word repeated once in a conditional form (ければ/なら) and once plain, to emphasize that degree matters — "the more X, the better X." It's a very natural, frequently-heard intensifying pattern in spoken and written Japanese alike.

【4】 数えればきりがない (kazoereba kiri ga nai)

neutral register
ReadingV-ば／V-たら／V-ると + きりがない
Meaning (EN)"there's no end to it if you start ~ing" — used for endless or limitless things
Meaning (HI)अगर ~ करना शुरू करें तो कोई अंत नहीं
Meaning (GU)જો ~ કરવાનું શરૂ કરીએ તો કોઈ અંત નથી
Connection (接続)V-ば／V-たら／V-ると + きりがない (V-ba/V-tara/V-ru to + kiri ga nai)
RegisterNeutral — common in everyday speech
Typical useSays that once you start a certain action (counting flaws, complaining, wanting more), there's no natural stopping point — often used to talk oneself (or someone else) out of endless nitpicking.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ば-form)V-ば + きりがない数えればきりがないkazoereba kiri ga nai
Verb (ば-form)V-ば + きりがない欲を言えばきりがないyoku o ieba kiri ga nai
Verb (たら／と-form)(added, not in book) V-たら／V-ると + きりがない上を見たらきりがないue o mitara kiri ga nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼の欠点を数えればきりがない。Kare no ketten o kazoereba kiri ga nai. There's no end to his shortcomings if you start counting them. अगर उसकी कमियाँ गिनने बैठें तो कोई अंत ही नहीं है। જો તેની ખામીઓ ગણવા બેસીએ તો કોઈ અંત જ નથી.
「もっといい時計がほしい。」「このくらいで我慢しないと、上を見たらきりがないよ。」"Motto ii tokei ga hoshii." "Kono kurai de gaman shinai to, ue o mitara kiri ga nai yo." "I want a better watch." "You should make do with this one — if you keep looking for something better, there's no end to it." "मुझे एक बेहतर घड़ी चाहिए।" "इतने में ही संतोष कर लो, वरना और अच्छे की तलाश का कोई अंत नहीं है।" "મારે વધુ સારી ઘડિયાળ જોઈએ." "આટલામાં જ સંતોષ માનો, નહીં તો વધુ સારાની શોધનો કોઈ અંત નથી."
もっと！ きりがないから、この辺で終わりましょう。Kiri ga nai kara, kono hen de owarimashou. Let's stop here, since there's no natural end to it. इसका कोई अंत नहीं है, इसलिए यहीं रोक देते हैं। તેનો કોઈ અંત નથી, એટલે અહીં જ અટકીએ.

きり (a limit/cutoff point) + がない ("doesn't exist") literally means "there's no cutoff point" — i.e., the activity could continue forever. It pairs naturally with things that invite endless escalation: counting flaws (数えれば), wanting more (欲を言えば), or comparing upward (上を見たら).

Q6

資格を持っていると就職に有利だ。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ なら取っておこう。Shikaku o motte iru to shuushoku ni yuuri da. ___ nara totte okou.

Tiles: 1 持っている (motte iru)　2 持っていれば (motte ireba)　3 取れる (toreru)　4 に越したことはないから (ni koshita koto wa nai kara)

2 持っていれば→1 持っている→4 に越したことはないから→3 取れる

資格を持っていると就職に有利だ。持っていれば持っているに越したことはないから、取れるなら取っておこう。Shikaku o motte iru to shuushoku ni yuuri da. Motte ireba motte iru ni koshita koto wa nai kara, toreru nara totte okou.

EN: Having a qualification helps with job hunting. Having one is simply better than not, so if you can get it, get it while you can.
HI: योग्यता होना नौकरी पाने में मददगार है। होना न होने से बेहतर ही है, इसलिए हासिल कर सको तो कर ही लो।
GU: લાયકાત હોવી નોકરી મેળવવામાં મદદરૂપ છે. હોવું ન હોવા કરતાં સારું જ છે, એટલે મેળવી શકાય તો મેળવી લો.

Why: Built on Point 3's reduplicated 持っていれば持っているに越したことはない ("having it is simply better than not") — the same "Vば V に越したことはない" pattern as the day's title, 早ければ早いに越したことはない.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ところ、ほかの応募者は全員経験者だった。___ tokoro, hoka no oubosha wa zen'in keikensha datta.

Tiles: 1 応募した (oubo shita)　2 経験がなくても (keiken ga nakute mo)　3 というので (to iu node)　4 差し支えない (sashitsukaenai)

2 経験がなくても→4 差し支えない→3 というので→1 応募した

経験がなくても差し支えないというので応募したところ、ほかの応募者は全員経験者だった。Keiken ga nakute mo sashitsukaenai to iu node oubo shita tokoro, hoka no oubosha wa zen'in keikensha datta.

EN: Since it said no experience was fine, I applied — but it turned out all the other applicants were experienced.
HI: चूँकि लिखा था कि बिना अनुभव के भी कोई परेशानी नहीं, इसलिए आवेदन किया — लेकिन बाक़ी सब आवेदक अनुभवी निकले।
GU: કારણ કે લખ્યું હતું કે અનુભવ વગર પણ કોઈ વાંધો નથી, એટલે અરજી કરી — પણ બાકીના બધા અરજદારો અનુભવી નીકળ્યા.

Why: Built on Point 1's V てもさしつかえない — the job posting said lack of experience was fine, which is why the speaker applied.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All three of today's core idioms end in a negative (ない) after a fixed noun, but they judge very different things:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ても差し支えない~temo sashitsukaenai No problem/obstacle even if ~ V-ても／Aくても／Nでも + 差し支えない Grants permission or reassurance — nothing gets in the way of doing X. Reassuring someone a condition or timing is fine (明日でも差し支えない).
〜に越したことはない~ni koshita koto wa nai Best to ~ / nothing beats ~ V-る／Aい／N(である) + に越したことはない Ranks one option as obviously superior to any alternative — a value judgment, not permission. Recommending the clearly wiser or safer choice (用心するに越したことはない).
〜ばきりがない~ba kiri ga nai No end to it if you start ~ V-ば／V-たら／V-ると + きりがない Warns that an activity has no natural stopping point — about endlessness, not about permission or superiority. Talking yourself or someone else out of endless nitpicking (数えればきりがない).
Exam trap: These three superficially resemble each other (all end in a fixed ない idiom attached to a verb/adjective/noun stem) but answer three different questions — 差し支えない asks "does this cause a problem?", に越したことはない asks "is this the best choice?", and きりがない asks "does this activity ever end?" Separately, remember the running trap this week: neither に難くない (Day 2) nor に越したことはない (Day 3) takes an inserted する after a bare noun (想像に難くない, お金はあるに越したことはない — not 想像するに, あるするに). Finally, Point 3's reduplicated Vば V に越したことはない (早ければ早いに越したことはない) is not a separate grammar point to memorize in isolation — it's Point 2's own construction with the same word repeated for emphasis.
