Notes
This day has five grammar points instead of the usual four. Two pairs are easy to conflate: 遅かれ早かれ's bonus 良かれ悪しかれ and Point 2's 良きにつけ悪しきにつけ are near-synonyms for "for better or worse" (one casual/fixed, one more literary); and じゃあるまいし (dismissing something as obviously not the case) vs にあるまじき (behavior unbecoming of a role) share あるまい/あるまじき but serve very different jobs — see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 遅かれ早かれ (osokare hayakare)

neutral register
Readingい-adj stem1+かれ + antonym stem2+かれ
Meaning (EN)"sooner or later / to a greater or lesser extent" — covers both extremes without specifying which applies
Meaning (HI)देर-सवेर / कम या ज़्यादा (चाहे जो भी हो)
Meaning (GU)વહેલું-મોડું / ઓછું-વધારે (ભલે ગમે તે હોય)
Connection (接続)A₁かれA₂かれ (antonym adjective stems + kare, paired)
RegisterNeutral — a small, fixed set of reduplicated idioms
Typical usePairs two opposite adjective stems with かれ to say the outcome is inevitable either way — 遅かれ早かれ ("sooner or later"), 多かれ少なかれ ("more or less"), and the bonus idiom 良かれ悪しかれ ("for better or worse").
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (fixed pair)遅い／早い stems + かれ遅かれ早かれosokare hayakare
い-adjective (fixed pair)多い／少ない stems + かれ多かれ少なかれookare sukunakare
い-adjective (bonus fixed pair)良い／悪い stems + かれ良かれ悪しかれyokare ashikare
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この番組も、遅かれ早かれ視聴者に飽きられるだろう。Kono bangumi mo, osokare hayakare shichousha ni akirareru darou. People will sooner or later grow tired of this program too. यह शो भी, देर-सवेर, दर्शकों के लिए उबाऊ हो जाएगा। આ શો પણ, વહેલા-મોડા, દર્શકો માટે કંટાળાજનક બની જશે.
若い女性なら、多かれ少なかれセクハラを経験したことがあるだろう。Wakai josei nara, ookare sukunakare sekuhara o keiken shita koto ga aru darou. Any young woman has probably experienced sexual harassment to some degree, more or less. कोई भी युवा महिला ने कम या ज़्यादा, यौन उत्पीड़न ज़रूर झेला होगा। કોઈપણ યુવાન સ્ત્રીએ ઓછું-વધારે, જાતીય સતામણી ચોક્કસ અનુભવી હશે.

かれ here is a classical concessive form (from 〜かろう), so pairing two opposite adjective stems with it says "whether it turns out to be A₁ or A₂, either way..." These are frozen, memorize-as-a-set idioms — you can't freely invent new かれ pairs beyond this small handful.

【2】 良きにつけ悪しきにつけ (yoki ni tsuke ashiki ni tsuke)

neutral register
ReadingA₁(classical)につけ(A₂(classical)につけ)
Meaning (EN)"for better or worse / whether good or bad" — a near-synonym of Point 1's bonus 良かれ悪しかれ
Meaning (HI)चाहे अच्छा हो या बुरा
Meaning (GU)ભલે સારું હોય કે ખરાબ
Connection (接続)良き(classical)につけ悪しき(classical)につけ (yoki ni tsuke ashiki ni tsuke) ； OK modern equiv. いいにつけ、悪いにつけ
RegisterNeutral, with a slightly literary flavor from the classical adjective forms 良き／悪しき
Typical useSays that regardless of whether the outcome is good or bad, the following statement holds — most often used when describing an unavoidable influence, like a parent's effect on a child.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (classical)良き／悪しき + につけ良きにつけ悪しきにつけyoki ni tsuke ashiki ni tsuke
い-adjective (modern, OK)いい／悪い + につけ(added, not in book) いいにつけ、悪いにつけii ni tsuke, warui ni tsuke
Noun／Verb(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this fixed idiom pairs only the classical/modern good–bad adjective forms)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
良きにつけ悪しきにつけ、子どもは親の影響を受ける。Yoki ni tsuke ashiki ni tsuke, kodomo wa oya no eikyou o ukeru. For better or worse, children are influenced by their parents. अच्छे या बुरे, हर तरह से, बच्चे माता-पिता से प्रभावित होते हैं। સારા કે ખરાબ, દરેક રીતે, બાળકો માતાપિતાથી પ્રભાવિત થાય છે.

良き and 悪しき are classical (bungo) forms of 良い and 悪い, so this idiom has a slightly more formal, set-phrase feel than Point 1's 良かれ悪しかれ, even though both translate to "for better or worse." See §3 for exactly how these two near-identical expressions relate.

【3】 周囲の反対をものともせずに (shuui no hantai o mono to mo sezu ni)

neutral register
ReadingN + をものともせずに
Meaning (EN)"undaunted by N / without being deterred by N" — pushing forward despite an obstacle
Meaning (HI)N की परवाह किए बिना / N से बेपरवाह होकर
Meaning (GU)N ની પરવા કર્યા વગર / N થી બેપરવા થઈને
Connection (接続)N + をものともせずに (N + o mono to mo sezu ni)
RegisterNeutral — common in narratives about courage or determination
Typical useDescribes not letting a problem or difficulty (問題や困難に負けないで) stand in the way — 敵の攻撃をものともせずに ("undaunted by the enemy's attack"), 嵐をものともせずに ("undeterred by the storm").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をものともせずに周囲の反対をものともせずにshuui no hantai o mono to mo sezu ni
NounN + をものともせずに危険をものともせずにkiken o mono to mo sezu ni
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — をものともせずに attaches to a noun naming the obstacle being defied, not a verb or descriptive state)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
周囲の反対をものともせずに、二人は結婚した。Shuui no hantai o mono to mo sezu ni, futari wa kekkon shita. Ignoring the objections of those around them, the two got married. आस-पास के लोगों के विरोध की परवाह किए बिना, दोनों ने शादी कर ली। આજુબાજુના લોકોના વિરોધની પરવા કર્યા વગર, બંનેએ લગ્ન કરી લીધા.
消防士たちは、危険をものともせずに、火の中に飛び込んで行った。Shouboushi-tachi wa, kiken o mono to mo sezu ni, hi no naka ni tobikonde itta. The firefighters bravely went into the fire, undeterred by the danger. दमकलकर्मी, ख़तरे की परवाह किए बिना, आग में कूद पड़े। ફાયર ફાઇટરો, જોખમની પરવા કર્યા વગર, આગમાં કૂદી પડ્યા.

ものともせずに literally breaks down as "not even treating N as a thing (worth minding)" — a vivid way of saying an obstacle simply didn't register as a deterrent. It always carries an admiring tone toward the courage or resolve of the subject.

【4】 子どもじゃあるまいし (kodomo ja arumai shi)

casual-leaning
ReadingN + ではあるまいし ／ N + じゃあるまいし
Meaning (EN)"it's not like N (so ~) / this obviously isn't N" — dismisses a possibility before drawing a conclusion
Meaning (HI)यह तो N नहीं है (तो...) / भला N कैसे हो सकता है
Meaning (GU)આ તો N નથી (તો...) / ભલા N કેમ હોઈ શકે
Connection (接続)N + ではあるまいし (N + de wa arumai shi) ／ N + じゃあるまいし (casual contraction, spoken)
RegisterCasual-leaning — じゃ is the spoken contraction of では, common in everyday scolding or exasperated remarks
Typical usePoints out that the listener clearly isn't N (a child, summer, a horse), so their behavior doesn't make sense — often used to scold or tease.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (formal)N + ではあるまいし子どもではあるまいしkodomo de wa arumai shi
Noun (casual, spoken)N + じゃあるまいし夏じゃあるまいしnatsu ja arumai shi
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — あるまいし negates a noun's applicability, not a verb or descriptive state)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子どもではあるまいし、泣くのはやめなさい。Kodomo de wa arumai shi, naku no wa yamenasai. Stop crying! You're not a child. रोना बंद करो! तुम बच्चे नहीं हो। રડવાનું બંધ કરો! તમે બાળક નથી.
夏じゃあるまいし、この冬にTシャツ1枚で外出するなんて、頭がどうかしているよ。Natsu ja arumai shi, kono fuyu ni T-shatsu ichi-mai de gaishutsu suru nante, atama ga dou ka shite iru yo. It's not summer — going out in just a T-shirt this winter means you must be out of your mind. यह गर्मी का मौसम तो नहीं, फिर इस सर्दी में सिर्फ़ टी-शर्ट पहनकर बाहर निकलना, दिमाग़ ठीक नहीं लगता। આ ઉનાળો તો નથી, છતાં આ શિયાળામાં ફક્ત ટી-શર્ટ પહેરીને બહાર નીકળવું, દિમાગ ઠીક નથી લાગતું.

あるまい is a classical negative-presumptive form of ある ("probably isn't"), so N ではあるまいし literally reads "since it's probably not N..." It's almost always used to point out an obvious mismatch — you're not a child, it's not summer — before delivering a piece of advice or criticism.

【5】 教師にあるまじき行為 (kyoushi ni arumajiki koui)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN₁ + にあるまじき + N₂
Meaning (EN)"conduct unbecoming of N₁ / something that should never come from N₁"
Meaning (HI)N₁ के तौर पर बिल्कुल अनुचित / N₁ से ऐसा होना ही नहीं चाहिए
Meaning (GU)N₁ તરીકે તદ્દન અયોગ્ય / N₁ તરફથી આવું ન જ થવું જોઈએ
Connection (接続)N₁ + にあるまじき + N₂ (N1 + ni arumajiki + N2)  N2 = こと／発言／行動／態度
Register硬 — formal, written, used to formally condemn behavior that violates someone's role or position
Typical useNames a role (N₁: teacher, doctor, parent) and declares that a specific act, remark, or attitude (N₂) is utterly incompatible with that role.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (role) + NounN₁ + にあるまじき + N₂教師にあるまじき行為kyoushi ni arumajiki koui
Noun (role) + NounN₁ + にあるまじき + N₂(added, not in book) 人間にあるまじき行為ningen ni arumajiki koui
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — あるまじき is a classical attributive negative that must directly precede a noun like 行為/発言/態度)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
学生を恐喝するとは、教師にあるまじき行為だ。Gakusei o kyoukatsu suru to wa, kyoushi ni arumajiki koui da. Blackmailing students is conduct unbecoming of a teacher. छात्रों को धमकाना, यह एक शिक्षक के तौर पर बिल्कुल अनुचित हरकत है। વિદ્યાર્થીઓને ધમકાવવું, આ એક શિક્ષક તરીકે તદ્દન અયોગ્ય કૃત્ય છે.

あるまじき is the classical attributive form of あるまい ("should not be"), so N₁にあるまじきN₂ reads "N₂ that should not exist for/from N₁." Unlike Point 4's あるまいし (which dismisses a possibility as a lead-in to a separate statement), あるまじき directly modifies the following noun (こと/発言/行動/態度) — see §3 for the full contrast.

Q6

それを食べた＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、吐き出すなんて大げさすぎるよ。Sore o tabeta ___ , hakidasu nante oogesa sugiru yo.

Tiles: 1 から (kara)　2 死ぬ (shinu)　3 といって (to itte)　4 わけじゃあるまいし (wake ja arumai shi)

EN: It's not like you're going to die just because you ate that — spitting it out is way too dramatic.
HI: वह खा लेने से मरने वाले तो नहीं हो, थूक देना तो बहुत ही नाटकीय है।

Why: Built on Point 4's Nわけじゃあるまいし — dismisses the possibility of dying from the food as a reason the reaction is overblown.

Q7

子どもはいいに＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ながら成長している。Kodomo wa ii ni ___ nagara seichou shite iru.

Tiles: 1 につけ (ni tsuke)　2 大人を見習い (otona o minarai)　3 悪い (warui)　4 つけ (tsuke)

4 つけ→3 悪い→1 につけ→2 大人を見習い

子どもはいいにつけ悪いにつけ大人を見習いながら成長している。Kodomo wa ii ni tsuke warui ni tsuke otona o minarai nagara seichou shite iru.

EN: Children, for better or worse, grow up modeling themselves after adults.
HI: बच्चे, चाहे अच्छे के लिए हो या बुरे के लिए, बड़ों को देखकर सीखते हुए बड़े होते हैं।
GU: બાળકો, ભલે સારા માટે હોય કે ખરાબ માટે, મોટાઓને જોઈને શીખતાં મોટા થાય છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's modern-form いいにつけ悪いにつけ — children inevitably learn from the adults around them, for good or ill.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two near-synonym pairs collide today — "for better or worse" has two versions, and あるまい/あるまじき share a root but do very different jobs:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
良かれ悪しかれyokare ashikare For better or worse 良い／悪い stems + かれ (Point 1 bonus) One of a small fixed set of かれ-paired idioms (遅かれ早かれ, 多かれ少なかれ, 良かれ悪しかれ). Grouping with other かれ idioms about an unavoidable outcome either way.
良きにつけ悪しきにつけyoki ni tsuke ashiki ni tsuke For better or worse (same meaning) 良き／悪しき (classical) + につけ A separate construction (Aにつけ) using classical adjective forms — same meaning as 良かれ悪しかれ but a different grammatical shape. Slightly more literary contexts, or when you want the につけ pattern specifically.
Nじゃ／ではあるまいし~ja/de wa arumai shi It's not like N (so ~) N + じゃ／ではあるまいし Dismisses N as obviously not the case, as a lead-in to a separate following statement — does not modify a noun. Pointing out an obvious mismatch before giving advice or criticism (子どもじゃあるまいし).
N₁にあるまじきN₂~ni arumajiki (硬) N₂ unbecoming of N₁ N₁ + にあるまじき + N₂ An attributive form that directly modifies the following noun (こと/発言/行動/態度) — a formal condemnation, not a dismissal-then-comment. Formally condemning behavior that violates a role's expectations (教師にあるまじき行為).
Exam trap: 良かれ悪しかれ and 良きにつけ悪しきにつけ mean the same thing but are built completely differently (かれ-pairing vs. classical-adjective-につけ) — don't mix their shapes when producing them. Separately, あるまいし and あるまじき share the classical negative-presumptive あるまい as their root, but あるまいし trails off into a separate clause ("it's not like N, so...") while あるまじき sits directly in front of a noun it modifies ("N₂ that shouldn't exist for N₁") — see Q1/Q4 vs Q2 above for exactly this contrast.
