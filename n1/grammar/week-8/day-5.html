Notes
All four points today are marked 硬 (formal/written) — this whole day is about maxed-out intensity expressions. 極まる／極まりない (attach to な-adjectives) and の極み／の至り (attach to nouns) look similar but take different word types. The book's own box flags an overlap trap: 感激 can pair with either の至り (○感激の至り) or の極み (感激の極み, Point 3's own title), but 疲労 only pairs with の極み — ×疲労の至り is explicitly marked wrong in the book.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 失礼極まる (shitsurei kiwamaru)

硬 formal/written
Readingな-adjective (stem) + 極まる
Meaning (EN)"extremely ~ / ~ in the extreme" — nothing could be more so
Meaning (HI)बेहद/हद से ज़्यादा ~ (इससे ज़्यादा नहीं हो सकता)
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત/હદ બહાર ~ (આનાથી વધારે ન હોઈ શકે)
Connection (接続)な-adjective stem + 極まる (na-adjective stem + kiwamaru)
Register硬 — a fixed, idiomatic expression (慣用句的表現), formal and written
Typical useAttaches to na-adjectives describing a negative quality to intensify it to the maximum — 不愉快極まる ("extremely unpleasant"), 退屈極まる ("utterly boring"), 卑劣極まる ("thoroughly despicable").
Word typeFormationExample
な-adjectiveな-adj stem + 極まる失礼極まるshitsurei kiwamaru
な-adjectiveな-adj stem + 極まる平凡極まる内容heibon kiwamaru naiyou
Fixed idiom (bonus)(added, not in book) 感 + 極まって感極まって泣き出したkan kiwamatte nakidashita
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼の態度は失礼極まる。Kare no taido wa shitsurei kiwamaru. His attitude is rude in the extreme. उसका रवैया बेहद बदतमीज़ है। તેનું વલણ અત્યંત અસભ્ય છે.
昨日見た映画は、平凡極まる内容だった。Kinou mita eiga wa, heibon kiwamaru naiyou datta. The movie I watched yesterday had extremely mundane content. कल जो फ़िल्म देखी उसकी कहानी बेहद सामान्य थी। ગઈકાલે જોયેલી ફિલ્મની વાર્તા અત્યંત સામાન્ય હતી.
結婚式で両親の言葉を聞いて、彼女は感極まって泣き出した。Kekkonshiki de ryoushin no kotoba o kiite, kanojo wa kan kiwamatte nakidashita. Listening to her parents' words at the wedding, she was overcome with emotion and burst into tears. शादी में माता-पिता की बातें सुनकर, वह भावुक होकर रो पड़ी। લગ્નમાં માતાપિતાના શબ્દો સાંભળીને, તે ભાવુક થઈને રડી પડી.

極まる (きわまる) means "to reach an extreme," so na-adj + 極まる says the quality has hit its ceiling. Note the bonus fixed idiom 感極まって ("overcome with emotion") — here 感 (emotion) itself takes 極まる directly, a frozen exception outside the regular na-adjective pattern, always used in its て-form before a resulting action like crying.

【2】 無責任極まりない (musekinin kiwamarinai)

硬 formal/written
Readingな-adjective (なこと) ／い-adjective (こと) + 極まりない
Meaning (EN)"extremely ~ / ~ in the extreme" — the same meaning as Point 1's 極まる, in its more commonly used form
Meaning (HI)बेहद/हद से ज़्यादा ~ (और इससे ज़्यादा नहीं हो सकता)
Meaning (GU)અત્યંત/હદ બહાર ~ (આનાથી વધારે ન હોઈ શકે)
Connection (接続)な(なこと)／Aいこと + 極まりない (na(na koto)/Ai koto + kiwamarinai)
Register硬 — same meaning as 極まる; 極まりない is the more frequently used of the two (「極まる」と同じ意味。「極まりない」のほうがよく使われる)
Typical useIntensifies a na-adjective (or an い-adjective + こと) describing a negative quality to its absolute maximum.
Word typeFormationExample
な-adjectiveな-adj stem + 極まりない無責任極まりないmusekinin kiwamarinai
な-adjective (with こと)な-adj + なこと + 極まりない軽率なこと極まりないkeisotsu na koto kiwamarinai
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい + こと + 極まりない見苦しいこと極まりないmigurushii koto kiwamarinai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人は社長として、無責任極まりない。Ano hito wa shachou toshite, musekinin kiwamarinai. As president, he is totally irresponsible. वह अध्यक्ष के तौर पर बेहद ग़ैर-ज़िम्मेदार है। તે પ્રમુખ તરીકે અત્યંત બેજવાબદાર છે.
あの発言は一国の首相として、軽率なこと極まりない。Ano hatsugen wa ikkoku no shushou toshite, keisotsu na koto kiwamarinai. That statement, coming from the prime minister of a country, was totally inappropriate. उस बयान को एक देश के प्रधानमंत्री की ओर से देखते हुए, वह बेहद लापरवाह था। તે નિવેદન એક દેશના વડાપ્રધાન તરફથી જોતાં, તે અત્યંત બેદરકાર હતું.

Despite the ない, 極まりない is not a true negative — it's a fixed intensifying idiom with exactly the same emphatic meaning as 極まる, just more common in everyday formal use. Don't be tempted to read it as "not extreme."

【3】 感激の極み (kangeki no kiwami)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN + の極み
Meaning (EN)"the height/peak of N" — the degree of N has reached its absolute maximum
Meaning (HI)N की पराकाष्ठा / N चरम पर
Meaning (GU)N ની પરાકાષ્ઠા / N ચરમસીમાએ
Connection (接続)N + の極み (N + no kiwami)
Register硬 — formal, used for both positive states (delight) and negative ones (exhaustion, regret)
Typical useStates that N (an emotion or state) has reached its peak — 贅沢の極み ("the height of luxury"), 残念の極み ("utterly regrettable"), 無知の極み ("the height of ignorance").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + の極み感激の極みですkangeki no kiwami desu
NounN + の極みに達する疲労の極みに達していたhirou no kiwami ni tasshite ita
Noun (added)N + の極み贅沢の極みzeitaku no kiwami
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
優勝できて、感激の極みです。Yuushou dekite, kangeki no kiwami desu. I can't tell you how delighted I am that we've won the championship. चैंपियनशिप जीत पाना, यह ख़ुशी की पराकाष्ठा है। ચેમ્પિયનશિપ જીતવી, આ ખુશીની પરાકાષ્ઠા છે.
長時間の試合で、選手たちは疲労の極みに達していた。Choujikan no shiai de, senshu-tachi wa hirou no kiwami ni tasshite ita. After the long match, the players had reached the height of exhaustion. लंबे मैच के बाद, खिलाड़ी थकान की पराकाष्ठा पर पहुँच गए थे। લાંબી મેચ પછી, ખેલાડીઓ થાકની પરાકાષ્ઠાએ પહોંચી ગયા હતા.

極み (きわみ) is a noun meaning "the utmost point," so N の極み reads "the utmost point of N." Unlike Points 1–2 (which attach to adjectives), の極み attaches directly to a noun and works for both good extremes (delight, luxury) and bad ones (exhaustion, regret) — see §3 for how it compares with Point 4's の至り.

【4】 光栄の至り (kouei no itari)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN + の至り
Meaning (EN)"the height of N" — an idiomatic, emotionally-loaded way of expressing a feeling (esp. honor, gratitude, embarrassment)
Meaning (HI)N की पराकाष्ठा (भावनाएँ व्यक्त करने का मुहावरेदार तरीक़ा)
Meaning (GU)N ની પરાકાષ્ઠા (લાગણી વ્યક્ત કરવાની રૂઢિપ્રયોગાત્મક રીત)
Connection (接続)N + の至り (N + no itari)  only pairs with a limited set of feeling-nouns
Register硬 — used idiomatically to express feelings (慣用句的に使う。気持ちを表す), especially in formal/humble speeches of gratitude
Typical useAlmost always about honor, gratitude, or (in the fixed idiom 若気の至り) youthful rashness — it does NOT freely combine with any noun the way の極み does; 疲労 (fatigue) is explicitly marked wrong (×疲労の至り) in the book.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (honor/gratitude)N + の至り光栄の至りですkouei no itari desu
Noun (also valid with 極み)感激 + の至り○感激の至りkangeki no itari (also 感激の極み, Point 3)
Noun (fixed idiom)若気 + の至り若気の至りでwakage no itari de
Noun (invalid pairing)×疲労 + の至り×疲労の至り wrong — use 疲労の極み instead
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
こんなすばらしい賞をいただいて、光栄の至りです。Konna subarashii shou o itadaite, kouei no itari desu. I am sincerely honored to receive such a wonderful award. ऐसा शानदार पुरस्कार पाकर, यह मेरे लिए परम सम्मान की बात है। આવો શાનદાર પુરસ્કાર મેળવીને, આ મારા માટે પરમ સન્માનની વાત છે.
若気の至りで、せっかく入った大学をやめてしまった。Wakage no itari de, sekkaku haitta daigaku o yamete shimatta. Out of youthful rashness, I ended up quitting the university I'd worked so hard to get into. जवानी की नादानी में, इतनी मेहनत से मिले दाख़िले वाली यूनिवर्सिटी को छोड़ दिया। યુવાનીની અણસમજમાં, એટલી મહેનતથી મળેલા યુનિવર્સિટીના પ્રવેશને છોડી દીધો.

至り (いたり) means "the utmost result/consequence," and の至り is reserved for a narrow set of feeling-nouns tied to honor, gratitude, or regret at one's own past behavior (若気の至り is a frozen idiom, "the folly of youth"). Unlike の極み, it cannot freely attach to any intensifiable noun — 疲労 needs の極み, not の至り. Where a noun like 感激 can take either, the choice is largely idiomatic memorization rather than a rule.

GU: મારા સંશોધનને આવું સન્માન મળવું, આ ઊંડા આનંદની વાત છે.

Why: Built on Point 4's 感激の至り — receiving recognition for one's research is framed with the same idiom the book validates for 感激 (○感激の至り).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points intensify to "the max," but split cleanly by word type and by which nouns they can pair with:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
な-adj + 極まる~kiwamaru (硬) Extremely ~ (in the extreme) na-adj stem + 極まる Attaches to a na-adjective; can modify a following noun directly (残酷極まるシーン). Intensifying a na-adjective, especially right before a noun it describes.
な/い-adj + 極まりない~kiwamarinai (硬) Extremely ~ (same meaning, more common) na(なこと)／Aいこと + 極まりない Same meaning as 極まる despite the ない — just the more frequently used variant, and works with い-adjectives via こと. Intensifying either a na- or い-adjective as a predicate (無責任極まりない).
N + の極み~no kiwami (硬) The height/peak of N N + の極み Attaches to a bare noun; freely usable for both positive (delight, luxury) and negative (exhaustion, regret) extremes. Saying an emotion or state has hit its absolute peak (疲労の極み, 贅沢の極み).
N + の至り~no itari (硬) The height of N (idiomatic feeling expression) N + の至り Only pairs with a narrow set of feeling-nouns (honor, gratitude, youthful folly) — cannot freely substitute for の極み. Formally expressing honor, gratitude, or embarrassment at one's own past behavior (光栄の至り, 若気の至り).
Exam trap: 極まる/極まりない attach to adjectives (na- or い-), while の極み/の至り attach to bare nouns — mixing up word types is the most common mistake (Q1–Q3 test exactly this). Separately, の極み and の至り overlap only for a handful of words like 感激 (both ○感激の極み and ○感激の至り are valid) — but の至り is otherwise reserved for honor/gratitude/youthful-folly nouns, and the book explicitly rules out ×疲労の至り (fatigue must use 疲労の極み). When in doubt about a noun you haven't memorized as a set phrase, の極み is the safer general-purpose choice.
