Q1

一見平和に見えるこの国だが、（　）ほどの問題を抱えている。Ikken heiwa ni mieru kono kuni da ga, (___) hodo no mondai o kakaete iru.

Full sentence: 一見平和に見えるこの国だが、数えればきりがないほどの問題を抱えている。...kazoereba kiri ga nai hodo no mondai o kakaete iru.
EN: This country may look peaceful at first glance, but it has more problems than can be counted.
HI: यह देश देखने में शांत लगता है, लेकिन इसकी समस्याएँ गिनी न जा सकें इतनी हैं।
GU: આ દેશ જોવામાં શાંત લાગે છે, પણ તેની સમસ્યાઓ ગણી ન શકાય એટલી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1数えるにこしたことがないkazoeru ni koshita koto ga nai(wrong — に越したことはない means "best to," not "endless")
2数えればきりがないkazoereba kiri ga naitoo many to count, no end to them
3数えるにはあたらないkazoeru ni wa ataranai(wrong sense — "not worth counting")
4数えるにかたくないkazoeru ni katakunai(wrong — "not hard to count," opposite of the intended meaning)

Why: Day 3's V ばきりがない — the country's problems have no natural stopping point if you tried to count them.

Q2

小学生（　）、大学生にもなってこんな簡単な算数がわからないとは情けない。Shougakusei (___), daigakusei ni mo natte konna kantan na sansuu ga wakaranai to wa nasakenai.

Full sentence: 小学生ならいざ知らず、大学生にもなってこんな簡単な算数がわからないとは情けない。Shougakusei nara iza shirazu, daigakusei ni mo natte...
EN: I don't know about elementary schoolers, but for a university student not to understand such simple arithmetic is pathetic.
HI: प्राइमरी के बच्चों की तो बात छोड़ो, यूनिवर्सिटी में पढ़कर भी इतना आसान गणित न समझना शर्मनाक है।
GU: પ્રાઇમરીના બાળકોની તો વાત છોડો, યુનિવર્સિટીમાં ભણીને પણ આટલું સહેલું ગણિત ન સમજવું શરમજનક છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ならいざ知らずnara iza shirazuI don't know about (elementary schoolers), but
2はおろかwa oroka(wrong — needs a second, more extreme noun escalation, not a contrast with the present)
3に差し支えずni sashitsukaezu(wrong sense — not about permission)
4をものともせずにo mono to mo sezu ni(wrong — no obstacle being overcome here)

Why: Day 1's N ならいざ知らず — elementary schoolers are set aside as a separate, forgivable case before judging university students harshly.

Q3

私の隣に座っていた人は、コンサートの開始に遅れただけではなく、居眠り（　）。Watashi no tonari ni suwatte ita hito wa, konsaato no kaishi ni okureta dake de wa naku, inemuri (___).

Full sentence: 私の隣に座っていた人は、コンサートの開始に遅れただけではなく、居眠りをする始末だった。...inemuri o suru shimatsu datta.
EN: The person sitting next to me wasn't only late for the concert's start — it got to the point where they even dozed off.
HI: मेरे बगल में बैठा व्यक्ति न सिर्फ़ कॉन्सर्ट की शुरुआत में देर से आया, बल्कि झपकी तक ले बैठा।
GU: મારી બાજુમાં બેઠેલી વ્યક્તિ ફક્ત કોન્સર્ટની શરૂઆતમાં મોડી નહોતી આવી, પણ ઝોકું પણ ખાઈ બેઠી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1の至りだったno itari datta(wrong — の至り pairs with a narrow set of feeling-nouns, not a described bad habit)
2を禁じえなかったo kinjienakatta(wrong — this is the speaker's own suppressed emotion, not someone else's dozing off)
3をする始末だったo suru shimatsu dattait got to the point of even (dozing off)
4を余儀なくされたo yoginakusareta(wrong — dozing off isn't an externally forced necessity)

Why: Day 4's V る始末だ — being late escalates to the worse, final low point of falling asleep.

Q4

「酔っていたとはいえ、失礼（　）態度を取ってしまい、誠に申し訳ありませんでした。」"Yotte ita to wa ie, shitsurei (___) taido o totte shimai, makoto ni moushiwake arimasen deshita."

Full sentence: 「酔っていたとはいえ、失礼極まりない態度を取ってしまい、誠に申し訳ありませんでした。」"...shitsurei kiwamarinai taido o totte shimai..."
EN: "Even though I was drunk, I ended up behaving extremely rudely, and I am truly sorry."
HI: "भले ही मैं नशे में था/थी, लेकिन बेहद बदतमीज़ी से पेश आया/आई, इसके लिए सच में माफ़ी चाहता/चाहती हूँ।"
GU: "ભલે હું નશામાં હતો/હતી, પણ અત્યંત અસભ્યતાથી વર્ત્યો/વર્તી, તે માટે ખરેખર માફી માંગું છું."
#JapaneseEnglish
1きりがないkiri ga nai(wrong — no endlessness meaning fits here)
2極まりないkiwamarinaiextremely (rude)
3あるまじきarumajiki(wrong — needs a role noun + にあるまじき, no role is named here)
4の至りのno itari no(wrong — の至り doesn't modify an adjective like 失礼 this way)

Why: Day 5's な-adj + 極まりない — 失礼 is a na-adjective, intensified to its extreme.

Q5

仲間が海で遭難した。彼らの無事を（　）。Nakama ga umi de sounan shita. Karera no buji o (___).

Full sentence: 仲間が海で遭難した。彼らの無事を願ってやまない。...karera no buji o negatte yamanai.
EN: My companions were shipwrecked at sea. I sincerely and constantly pray for their safety.
HI: मेरे साथी समुद्र में फँस गए। मैं लगातार उनकी सलामती की दुआ करता/करती हूँ।
GU: મારા સાથીઓ સમુદ્રમાં ફસાયા. હું સતત તેમની સલામતી માટે પ્રાર્થના કરું છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1願ってやまないnegatte yamanainever stop wishing/praying
2願い極まるnegai kiwamaru(not a valid/natural combination)
3願う始末だnegau shimatsu da(wrong — 始末だ marks a bad final outcome, not a sincere ongoing wish)
4願うにかたくないnegau ni katakunai(wrong — not hard to wish is not the intended meaning)

Why: Day 2's V てやまない — an intense, unceasing hope for the missing companions' safety.

Q6

首相が辞任したのは、筋書き通りで（　）。Shushou ga jinin shita no wa, sujigaki doori de (___).

Full sentence: 首相が辞任したのは、筋書き通りで驚くにはあたらなかった。...odoroku ni wa ataranakatta.
EN: The prime minister's resignation went exactly according to the script, so it was nothing to be surprised about.
HI: प्रधानमंत्री का इस्तीफ़ा बिल्कुल तय पटकथा के अनुसार हुआ, इसलिए हैरान होने की कोई बात नहीं थी।
GU: વડાપ્રધાનનું રાજીનામું બિલકુલ નક્કી પટકથા મુજબ થયું, એટલે નવાઈ પામવા જેવું કંઈ નહોતું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1驚くことにこしたことはなかったodoroku koto ni koshita koto wa nakatta(not a valid/natural combination)
2驚きにかたくなかったodoroki ni katakunakatta(wrong — に難くない pairs with imagining/inferring, not with 驚き itself as a bare reaction)
3驚きを禁じ得なかったodoroki o kinjienakatta(wrong sense — this would mean the surprise WAS overwhelming, opposite of "expected")
4驚くにはあたらなかったodoroku ni wa ataranakattawas nothing to be surprised about

Why: Day 2's V るにはあたらない — since it went exactly as scripted, surprise would be disproportionate.

Q7

A社は昨今の不景気（　）、業績を伸ばしている。Eishisha wa sakkon no fukeiki (___), gyouseki o nobashite iru.

Full sentence: A社は昨今の不景気をものともせず、業績を伸ばしている。A-sha wa sakkon no fukeiki o mono to mo sezu, gyouseki o nobashite iru.
EN: Company A, undeterred by the recent recession, is growing its business results.
HI: कंपनी A, हाल की मंदी की परवाह किए बिना, अपना कारोबार बढ़ा रही है।
GU: કંપની A, તાજેતરની મંદીની પરવા કર્યા વગર, પોતાનો કારોબાર વધારી રહી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1はおろかwa oroka(wrong — needs a second, more extreme noun, not a single obstacle being defied)
2をいざ知らずo iza shirazu(wrong particle/pattern — いざ知らず takes なら／は, not を)
3はさておきwa sateoki(wrong — this shelves a topic, it doesn't describe overcoming an obstacle)
4をものともせずo mono to mo sezuundeterred by

Why: Day 6's N をものともせずに — the recession doesn't stop the company's growth.

Q8

馬（　）、こんなにたくさんのニンジンは食べられませんよ。Uma (___), konna ni takusan no ninjin wa taberaremasen yo.

Full sentence: 馬じゃあるまいし、こんなにたくさんのニンジンは食べられませんよ。Uma ja arumai shi, konna ni takusan no ninjin wa taberaremasen yo.
EN: I'm not a horse — I can't eat this many carrots!
HI: मैं घोड़ा तो नहीं हूँ, इतनी सारी गाजरें नहीं खा सकता/सकती!
GU: હું ઘોડો તો નથી, આટલી બધી ગાજર ખાઈ ન શકું!
#JapaneseEnglish
1は何であれwa nan de are(wrong sense — no "no matter what" meaning fits here)
2じゃあるまいしja arumai shiit's not like (a horse)
3はおろかwa oroka(wrong — no escalating comparison between two nouns here)
4にあるまじきでni arumajiki de(wrong — にあるまじき needs a role + a following noun like 行為, not this shape)

Why: Day 6's N じゃあるまいし — dismisses being a horse as obviously false, before the complaint about quantity.

Q9

特に秘密にしているわけではないので、（　）。Toku ni himitsu ni shite iru wake de wa nai node, (___).

Full sentence: 特に秘密にしているわけではないので、誰に話しても差し支えありません。...dare ni hanashite mo sashitsukae arimasen.
EN: It's not something I'm keeping especially secret, so there's no problem telling anyone.
HI: यह ख़ास तौर पर छिपाई गई बात नहीं है, इसलिए किसी को भी बताने में कोई हर्ज़ नहीं है।
GU: આ ખાસ છુપાવેલી વાત નથી, એટલે કોઈને પણ કહેવામાં કોઈ વાંધો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1誰に話しても極まりませんdare ni hanashite mo kiwamarimasen(not a valid/natural combination)
2誰かに話すにはあたりませんdareka ni hanasu ni wa atarimasen(wrong sense — にはあたらない judges a disproportionate reaction, not permission to speak)
3誰かに話すにこしたことはありませんdareka ni hanasu ni koshita koto wa arimasen(wrong sense — this claims telling someone is "best," not merely "fine")
4誰に話しても差し支えありませんdare ni hanashite mo sashitsukae arimasenno problem at all telling anyone

Why: Day 3's 〜ても差し支えない — since it isn't a secret, telling anyone poses no obstacle.

Q10

経営の危機に面して、社長はリストラの決断をしたが、彼の決断はいつも（　）。Keiei no kiki ni menshite, shachou wa risutora no ketsudan o shita ga, kare no ketsudan wa itsumo (___).

Full sentence: 経営の危機に面して、社長はリストラの決断をしたが、彼の決断はいつも遅すぎるきらいがある。...kare no ketsudan wa itsumo ososugiru kirai ga aru.
EN: Facing a management crisis, the president decided on restructuring, but his decisions always tend to come too late.
HI: प्रबंधन संकट का सामना करते हुए, अध्यक्ष ने पुनर्गठन का फ़ैसला किया, लेकिन उनके फ़ैसले हमेशा देर से आने की प्रवृत्ति रखते हैं।
GU: સંચાલન કટોકટીનો સામનો કરતાં, પ્રમુખે પુનર્ગઠનનો નિર્ણય લીધો, પણ તેમના નિર્ણયો હંમેશા મોડા આવવાનું વલણ ધરાવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1遅すぎるきらいがあるososugiru kirai ga arutends to be too late
2遅く極まりないosoku kiwamarinai(wrong form — 極まりない attaches to na-adjective stems, not く-adverb forms)
3遅いにこしたことはないosoi ni koshita koto wa nai(wrong sense — this would mean "being late is best," the opposite of the criticism intended)
4遅くても差し支えないosokute mo sashitsukaenai(wrong sense — "it's fine to be late" contradicts the critical tone)

Why: Day 4's V るすぎる嫌いがある — a recurring, criticized tendency for the president's decisions to be late.

Q11

偽造した硬貨を自動販売機などで使用する事件が相次いだため、その硬貨のデザインや材質の（　）。Gizou shita kouka o jidou hanbaiki nado de shiyou suru jiken ga aitsuida tame, sono kouka no dezain ya zairyou no (___).

Full sentence: 偽造した硬貨を自動販売機などで使用する事件が相次いだため、その硬貨のデザインや材質の変更を余儀なくされた。...henkou o yoginakusareta.
EN: Because incidents of using counterfeit coins in vending machines and the like occurred one after another, the coin's design and material had to be changed.
HI: नक़ली सिक्कों को वेंडिंग मशीनों में इस्तेमाल करने की घटनाएँ लगातार होने की वजह से, उस सिक्के के डिज़ाइन और सामग्री में बदलाव करना ही पड़ा।
GU: નકલી સિક્કાઓને વેન્ડિંગ મશીનોમાં વાપરવાની ઘટનાઓ સતત બનવાના કારણે, તે સિક્કાની ડિઝાઇન અને સામગ્રીમાં ફેરફાર કરવો જ પડ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1変更を禁じえなかったhenkou o kinjienakatta(wrong — this is an uncontrollable feeling, not an official forced action)
2変更するにあたったhenkou suru ni atatta(not a valid/natural combination in this sense)
3変更を余儀なくされたhenkou o yoginakusaretawas forced into a change
4変更をものともしなかったhenkou o mono to mo shinakatta(wrong sense — this would mean the change was defied/ignored, not that it happened)

Why: Day 4's N を余儀なくされる — repeated counterfeiting incidents left no choice but to change the coin's design.

Q12

若いときならいざ知らず、（　）。Wakai toki nara iza shirazu, (___).

Full sentence: 若いときならいざ知らず、今はそんなきつい運動はできません。...ima wa sonna kitsui undou wa dekimasen.
EN: I don't know about when I was young, but I can't do such intense exercise now.
HI: जवानी के दिनों की तो बात छोड़िए, अब ऐसी कठोर कसरत नहीं हो पाती।
GU: યુવાનીના દિવસોની તો વાત છોડો, હવે એવી સખત કસરત નથી થઈ શકતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1年を取るのは避けられませんtoshi o toru no wa sakeraremasen(too generic — doesn't follow from the "back then vs. now" contrast set up)
2年を取りたくないものですtoshi o toritakunai mono desu(off-topic — expresses a wish, not a concrete present-day contrast)
3今はそんなきつい運動はできませんima wa sonna kitsui undou wa dekimasennow I can't do such intense exercise
4今は運動がきつくても少しずつやっていますima wa undou ga kitsukute mo sukoshizutsu yatte imasu(contradicts ならいざ知らず's setup — implies still managing it, not the expected decline)

Why: Day 1's N ならいざ知らず — youth is set aside as a different, unknown-relevance case before stating the present limitation plainly.

Q13

彼は自分の店はおろか、（　）。Kare wa jibun no mise wa oroka, (___).

Full sentence: 彼は自分の店はおろか、自宅まで失ってしまった。...jitaku made ushinatte shimatta.
EN: Not only did he lose his own shop, he even lost his home.
HI: उसने अपनी दुकान तो खोई ही, यहाँ तक कि घर भी खो दिया।
GU: તેણે પોતાની દુકાન તો ગુમાવી જ, ત્યાં સુધી કે ઘર પણ ગુમાવ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1自宅まで失ってしまったjitaku made ushinatte shimattaeven lost his home
2自宅まで自分で建てたjitaku made jibun de tateta(wrong — a positive achievement, but はおろか needs an escalating negative)
3友人の店まで手伝っているyuujin no mise made tetsudatte iru(wrong — a positive/neutral action, not an escalating loss)
4友人の店まで有名にしたyuujin no mise made yuumei ni shita(wrong — a positive achievement, contradicts the negative escalation はおろか requires)

Why: Day 1's N1はおろかN2 — losing the shop is bad, but the escalating, worse loss (his home) is what the pattern demands.

Q14

自分の子どもを虐待するなど、（　）。Jibun no kodomo o gyakutai suru nado, (___).

Full sentence: 自分の子どもを虐待するなど、親としてあるまじき行為だ。...oya toshite arumajiki koui da.
EN: Abusing one's own child is conduct unbecoming of a parent.
HI: अपने ही बच्चे को प्रताड़ित करना, यह एक माता-पिता के तौर पर बिल्कुल अनुचित है।
GU: પોતાના જ બાળકને પ્રતાડિત કરવું, આ એક માતાપિતા તરીકે તદ્દન અયોગ્ય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1親にとって想像にあたらないoya ni totte souzou ni ataranai(not a valid/natural combination — 想像 pairs with 難くない, not あたらない)
2親になれば想像に難くないoya ni nareba souzou ni katakunai(wrong tone — this would mean it's "easy to imagine," far too mild for child abuse)
3親として失礼極まる行為だoya toshite shitsurei kiwamaru koui da(wrong word — 失礼 means "rude," far too mild for describing abuse)
4親としてあるまじき行為だoya toshite arumajiki koui daconduct unbecoming of a parent

Why: Day 6's N1にあるまじきN2 — child abuse is fundamentally incompatible with the role of a parent.

Q15

このような不当な判決には、怒りを禁じ得ません。（　）。Kono you na futou na hanketsu ni wa, ikari o kinjiemasen. (___).

1それどころかsore dokoro ka(wrong — this signals an escalating contrast, not a causal continuation)
2ならびにnarabi ni(wrong — a listing conjunction "as well as," not a causal link)
3およびoyobi(wrong — also a listing conjunction, not causal)
4それゆえにsore yue nitherefore, for that reason

Why: The mother's strong pride in not being lumped with divorced single mothers (previous paragraph) is the reason for her determination in raising the children — a causal link that それゆえに supplies.

Q23

私も弟もグレこそしなかったが、節目節目には多かれ少なかれ人並みに（　）。

#JapaneseEnglish
1反抗期というものを迎えたhankouki to iu mono o mukaetawent through what's called a rebellious phase
2迎えた反抗期に気付かなかったmukaeta hankouki ni kizukanakatta(wrong — contradicts the mother's clearly noticing and reacting to it)
3母に反抗できずにすんだhaha ni hankou dekizu ni sunda(wrong — contradicts the mother's own exasperated reaction, which implies they DID rebel)
4母の反抗をものともしなかったhaha no hankou o mono to mo shinakatta(wrong — reverses who is rebelling against whom)

Why: The mother's despondent quote right after ("a mother alone isn't enough... we need a father to give a good hard knock") only makes sense if the children went through a normal rebellious phase she felt unequipped to handle alone.

Q24

そうは言っても、女親としては母はかなり厳しいほうだったと私は思っている。（　）母は、本気で私たちを叱るときは容赦なく平手でパチンとやった。

#JapaneseEnglish
1小さなことはおろかchiisana koto wa oroka(wrong — needs a second, more extreme escalation noun, not this standalone use)
2小さいことじゃあるまいしchiisai koto ja arumai shi(wrong — dismisses "small matters" as untrue, contradicting the sentence's own point that even small matters counted)
3どんな小さいことにつきdonna chiisai koto ni tsuki(wrong — につき means "regarding/due to," not "no matter how small")
4どんな小さなことであれdonna chiisana koto de areno matter how small the matter

Why: Day 1's どんな〜であれ — however minor the issue, the mother's strict scolding applied unconditionally.

Q25

「テレビのほうばっかり、よそ見しているからでしょ！」とゴツンとやられ、（　）パチッとテレビを消された。

#JapaneseEnglish
1かつkatsuand, at the same time
2ないしはnaishi wa(wrong — means "or," presenting alternatives, but both things happened together)
3もしくはmoshiku wa(wrong — also means "or," same issue)
4だがdaga(wrong — "but" implies a contrast, but turning off the TV reinforces the scolding, it doesn't contradict it)

Why: かつ links the knock on the head and switching off the television as two things that happened together, not as alternatives or a contrast.
