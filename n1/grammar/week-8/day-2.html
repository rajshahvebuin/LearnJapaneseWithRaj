Notes
The book's own box flags a formation trap on 想像に難くない: don't insert する between a noun and に難くない (a crossed-out "N・するに難くない" in the margin warns against this) — a bare noun takes に難くない directly (理解に難くない), while suru-verbs like 察する simply use their own dictionary form (察するに難くない). Also watch にはあたらない vs. Week 7 Day 6's には及ばない — both mean "no need to," but あたらない specifically judges whether a reaction (surprise, sympathy, thanks) is warranted, while 及ばない is about the necessity of an action in general.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 嘆くにはあたらない (nageku ni wa ataranai)

neutral register
ReadingV-る + に(は)あたらない
Meaning (EN)"not worth ~ing / no need to ~" — the situation doesn't rise to the level of deserving this reaction
Meaning (HI)...करने लायक़ नहीं / ...करने की ज़रूरत नहीं (यह इतनी बड़ी बात नहीं है)
Meaning (GU)...કરવા લાયક નથી / ...કરવાની જરૂર નથી (આ એટલી મોટી વાત નથી)
Connection (接続)V-る + に(は)あたらない (V-ru + ni (wa) ataranai)
RegisterNeutral — common with reaction-verbs in both speech and writing
Typical useAlmost always paired with reaction verbs — 心配するにはあたらない ("no need to worry"), 非難するにはあたらない ("no grounds to criticize"), 驚くにはあたらない ("nothing to be surprised about") — telling someone their emotional reaction is disproportionate to the situation.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + にはあたらない嘆くにはあたらないnageku ni wa ataranai
Verb (passive)V-られる + にはあたらない感謝されるにはあたらないkansha sareru ni wa ataranai
Noun／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — にはあたらない judges a reaction/action, so it attaches to verbs, not bare nouns or adjectives)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
試験に落ちたからといって、嘆くにはあたらない。Shiken ni ochita kara to itte, nageku ni wa ataranai. It's not worth lamenting over failing the exam. परीक्षा में फ़ेल होने की वजह से इतना दुखी होने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। પરીક્ષામાં નાપાસ થવાના કારણે આટલા દુઃખી થવાની જરૂર નથી.
10円貸したくらいで、感謝されるにはあたらない。Juu-en kashita kurai de, kansha sareru ni wa ataranai. It's not worth being thanked just for lending someone 10 yen. सिर्फ़ 10 येन उधार देने भर से धन्यवाद पाने लायक़ बात नहीं है। ફક્ત 10 યેન ઉધાર આપવાથી આભાર માનવા જેવી વાત નથી.

あたる here means "to correspond to/be equivalent to," so V るにはあたらない literally reads "(this) doesn't correspond to a case where V-ing is warranted." It's almost always used to reassure or gently correct someone whose reaction (worry, surprise, gratitude, blame) seems too big for the actual situation. Compare with Point 2: あたらない judges whether a reaction is proportionate, while 難くない (next point) judges how easy or hard something is to figure out mentally.

【2】 想像に難くない (souzou ni katakunai)

neutral register
ReadingN + に難くない ／ V-る + に難くない
Meaning (EN)"not hard to ~ / easy to imagine/guess ~"
Meaning (HI)...करना मुश्किल नहीं / आसानी से अंदाज़ा लगाया जा सकता है
Meaning (GU)...કરવું મુશ્કેલ નથી / સહેલાઈથી અંદાજ લગાવી શકાય
Connection (接続)N + に難くない (N + ni katakunai, no する in between) ／ V-る + に難くない (V-ru + ni katakunai)
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal-leaning — common in writing about others' likely feelings or effort
Typical useStates that something (usually another person's feelings, effort, or a conclusion) is easy to picture or infer from context, without needing to be told directly.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に難くない  trap: no する in between想像に難くないsouzou ni katakunai (not 想像するに難くない)
Verb (dictionary form, incl. suru-verbs)V-る + に難くない察するに難くないsassuru ni katakunai
Noun (added)N + に難くない理解に難くないrikai ni katakunai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼らが離婚したことは想像に難くない。Karera ga rikon shita koto wa souzou ni katakunai. I'm not surprised to hear that they got divorced. उनका तलाक़ हो गया, यह सुनकर हैरानी नहीं होती। તેમનું છૂટાછેડા થયું, એ સાંભળીને નવાઈ નથી લાગતી.
この作品を作るのに、どんなに時間がかかったかは察するに難くない。Kono sakuhin o tsukuru no ni, donna ni jikan ga kakatta ka wa sassuru ni katakunai. It's not hard to imagine how much time it took to make this work. यह कृति बनाने में कितना समय लगा होगा, यह अंदाज़ा लगाना मुश्किल नहीं है। આ કૃતિ બનાવવામાં કેટલો સમય લાગ્યો હશે, તેનો અંદાજ લગાવવો મુશ્કેલ નથી.

難くない is the negative of 難い (かたい, "difficult"), so N/Vるに難くない literally means "not difficult to N/V." The book's own margin note crosses out a "する" between noun and に難くない — a bare noun goes straight to に難くない (想像に難くない, 理解に難くない), it never takes an inserted する. Suru-verbs like 察する are fine because する is already baked into the verb's dictionary form, not inserted separately.

【3】 願ってやまない (negatte yamanai)

neutral register
ReadingV-て + やまない
Meaning (EN)"never stop ~ing / ~ constantly and sincerely" — an intense, ongoing feeling
Meaning (HI)लगातार/दिल से ~ करते रहना, बिना रुके ~
Meaning (GU)સતત/દિલથી ~ કરતા રહેવું, અટક્યા વગર ~
Connection (接続)V-て + やまない (V-te + yamanai)
RegisterNeutral but emotionally elevated — appears with a small closed set of feeling-verbs
Typical useAttaches to verbs of hoping, wishing, believing, respecting, or loving to say the feeling never lets up — 祈ってやまない ("praying constantly"), 信じてやまない ("firmly, unwaveringly believing"), 尊敬してやまない ("endlessly respecting").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + やまない願ってやまないnegatte yamanai
Verb (て-form, する-verb)Vして + やまない愛してやまないaishite yamanai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — やまない is the negative of やむ "to stop," so it needs a verb describing an ongoing feeling or action, not a state noun/adjective)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
僕は君たちの合格を願ってやまない。Boku wa kimitachi no goukaku o negatte yamanai. I sincerely and constantly wish for your success (in the exam). मैं दिल से चाहता हूँ कि तुम सब पास हो जाओ। હું દિલથી ઇચ્છું છું કે તમે બધા પાસ થાઓ.
私が愛してやまない国は、タイです。Watashi ga aishite yamanai kuni wa, Tai desu. The country I dearly, endlessly love is Thailand. जिस देश से मैं बेइंतहा प्यार करता/करती हूँ, वह थाईलैंड है। જે દેશને હું અપાર પ્રેમ કરું છું, તે થાઈલેન્ડ છે.

やまない comes from やむ ("to stop/cease"), so V てやまない literally means "doing V without it ever stopping" — an emotion so strong and sustained it never lets up. It only pairs naturally with a small set of feeling/wishing verbs (願う, 祈る, 信じる, 尊敬する, 愛する); it would sound odd attached to a plain action verb like 食べる or 歩く.

【4】 愛でなくてなんだろう (ai de nakute nan darou)

neutral register
ReadingN + でなくてなんだろう
Meaning (EN)"if this isn't N, then what is it? / this must surely be N" — a rhetorical, emotionally charged assertion
Meaning (HI)अगर यह N नहीं है, तो और क्या है? / यह पक्का N ही है
Meaning (GU)જો આ N ન હોય, તો બીજું શું છે? / આ ચોક્કસ N જ છે
Connection (接続)N + でなくてなんだろう (N + de nakute nan darou)
RegisterNeutral, but an emotionally-loaded way of speaking (感情がこもった言い方) — mostly written/rhetorical
Typical useA rhetorical question that isn't really asking anything — it forcefully asserts that a fact or feeling absolutely IS N, precisely by pretending to wonder what else it could be.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + でなくてなんだろう愛でなくてなんだろうai de nakute nan darou
NounN + でなくてなんだろう汚職でなくてなんだろうoshoku de nakute nan darou
Verb／い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this rhetorical pattern names a noun/label for the situation, not a verb or descriptive state)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女に対するこの気持ちは、愛でなくてなんだろう。Kanojo ni taisuru kono kimochi wa, ai de nakute nan darou. This feeling of mine for her must be love. उसके प्रति मेरी यह भावना, प्यार के अलावा और क्या हो सकती है। તેના પ્રત્યેની આ મારી લાગણી, પ્રેમ સિવાય બીજું શું હોઈ શકે.
あの政治家のやったことは汚職でなくてなんだろう。Ano seijika no yatta koto wa oshoku de nakute nan darou. What that politician did was definitely corruption. उस नेता ने जो किया, वह भ्रष्टाचार नहीं तो और क्या है। તે નેતાએ જે કર્યું, તે ભ્રષ્ટાચાર નહીં તો બીજું શું છે.

Literally "if it's not N, what would it be?" — but no answer is actually invited; the speaker is emphatically declaring N to be exactly the right label, often for a strong emotion (love) or a strong judgment (corruption). This pairs naturally with Point 3's てやまない in tone — both are used for heightened, sincere feeling — but でなくてなんだろう asserts a label/identity, while てやまない describes an unceasing action/feeling.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two look-alike "not worth ~" patterns and two emotionally-charged patterns — compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Vるに(は)あたらない~ni (wa) ataranai Not worth ~ing / no need to ~ V-る + に(は)あたらない Judges whether a reaction (surprise, worry, blame, thanks) is proportionate to the situation. Reassuring someone their emotional response is bigger than the situation warrants (驚くにはあたらない).
N／Vるに難くない~ni katakunai Not hard to ~ / easy to guess N/V-る + に難くない (no する inserted) Judges how mentally easy something is to picture or infer — no emotional-proportionality judgment involved. Saying a conclusion or someone's feelings are easy to infer (想像に難くない).
Vてやまない~te yamanai Never stop ~ing, ~ constantly and sincerely V-て + やまない Describes an ongoing emotion/action that never lets up — attaches only to a small set of feeling-verbs. Expressing a deep, unceasing wish, belief, or love (願ってやまない).
NでなくてなんだろうN de nakute nan darou If not N, then what is it? (rhetorically, it must be N) N + でなくてなんだろう Asserts an identity/label for a fact or feeling through a rhetorical question — not describing an ongoing action. Forcefully declaring that something is exactly what it looks like (愛でなくてなんだろう).
Exam trap: あたらない and 難くない both follow a verb and both mean roughly "isn't much of a deal," but they judge completely different things: あたらない asks "is this reaction (surprise/worry/blame) proportionate?" while 難くない asks "is this easy to figure out mentally?" (Q1/Q2 vs Q5 above test exactly this). Separately, remember 難くない never takes an inserted する after a bare noun (想像に難くない, not 想像するに難くない) — suru-verbs like 察する are already complete verbs on their own. Finally, てやまない (an ongoing feeling) and でなくてなんだろう (a rhetorical identity claim) both carry heightened emotion but are grammatically unrelated — one is a verb pattern, the other a noun pattern.
