Notes
All four points today share the classical auxiliary べし, so — like Day 3's ごとし family — the whole day is one big lesson in how a single classical root fans out into four different modern forms. All four are tagged 硬 formal/written.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜べく

硬 formal/written
Reading~beku
Meaning (EN)"in order to ~ / with the intention of ~" — a purposeful, determined goal behind an action
Meaning (HI)...करने के लिए / ...करने के इरादे से
Meaning (GU)...કરવા માટે / ...કરવાના ઇરાદાથી
Connection (接続)V-る + べく  exception: する→するべく／すべく
Register硬 — formal, written; often in narration about determined effort
Typical useSimilar to 〜ために but more formal/literary — describes a deliberate goal driving sustained effort.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + べく応えるべくkotaeru beku
する-verb (irregular)する → するべく／すべく復帰すべくfukki subeku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
親の期待に応えるべく、努力して医者の道に進んだ。Oya no kitai ni kotaeru beku, doryoku shite isha no michi ni susunda. In order to live up to his parents' expectations, he worked hard and became a doctor. माता-पिता की उम्मीदों पर खरा उतरने के लिए, उसने मेहनत करके डॉक्टर बनने की राह चुनी। માતાપિતાની અપેક્ષાઓ પર ખરા ઉતરવા માટે, તેણે મહેનત કરીને ડોક્ટર બનવાનો રસ્તો પસંદ કર્યો.
彼はよい席を手に入れるべく、2時間も前から並んでいた。Kare wa yoi seki o te ni ireru beku, ni-jikan mo mae kara narande ita. In order to get a good seat, he had been lining up since two hours before. अच्छी सीट पाने के लिए, वह दो घंटे पहले से ही लाइन में लगा हुआ था। સારી બેઠક મેળવવા માટે, તે બે કલાક પહેલાંથી જ લાઇનમાં ઊભો હતો.

べく comes from the classical auxiliary べし ("should/must/will surely"), here in its 連用形 (adverbial) use meaning "intending to." Unlike 〜ために, which is neutral, 〜べく carries a literary, determined tone — reach for it in formal writing about someone pursuing a clear goal.

【2】 〜べくもない

硬 formal/written
Reading~beku mo nai
Meaning (EN)"cannot possibly ~ / not even worth ~ing" — flatly denies that an action is even conceivable
Meaning (HI)...करने की गुंजाइश ही नहीं / ...करना नामुमकिन ही है
Meaning (GU)...કરવાનો અવકાશ જ નથી / ...કરવું અશક્ય જ છે
Connection (接続)V-る + べくもない  exception: する→するべく／すべく
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useA stronger, more literary version of 〜はずがない／〜わけがない — says even hoping/trying is pointless given the circumstances.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + べくもない望むべくもないnozomu beku mo nai
Verb (comparison)比べる + べくもない比べるべくもないkuraberu beku mo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
祖父の病状は悪くなるばかりだ。回復はもう望むべくもない。Sofu no byoujou wa waruku naru bakari da. Kaifuku wa mou nozomu beku mo nai. My grandfather's condition just keeps getting worse. Recovery is no longer even something we can hope for. दादाजी की हालत बस बिगड़ती ही जा रही है। अब ठीक होने की उम्मीद करना भी बेकार है। દાદાની તબિયત ફક્ત બગડતી જ જાય છે. હવે સાજા થવાની આશા રાખવી પણ નકામી છે.
ビートルズとは比べるべくもないが、彼らは日本ではかなり有名なバンドです。Biitoruzu to wa kuraberu beku mo nai ga, karera wa Nihon de wa kanari yuumei na bando desu. They're not even comparable to the Beatles, but they're a fairly famous band in Japan. बीटल्स से उनकी तुलना करना ही ग़लत होगा, लेकिन जापान में वे काफ़ी मशहूर बैंड हैं। બીટલ્સ સાથે તેમની સરખામણી કરવી જ યોગ્ય નથી, પણ જાપાનમાં તેઓ ખાસ્સું પ્રખ્યાત બેન્ડ છે.

べくもない literally combines Point 1's べく ("intending to/trying to") with もない ("there isn't even") — "there isn't even room to try to ~." Use it when a hope or comparison is so far out of reach that even attempting it doesn't make sense.

【3】 〜べからず

硬 formal/written
Reading~bekarazu
Meaning (EN)"must not ~ / ~ is forbidden" — a blunt, formal prohibition, typically written rather than spoken
Meaning (HI)...करना मना है / ...न करें
Meaning (GU)...કરવું નહીં / ...કરવાની મનાઈ છે
Connection (接続)V-る + べからず
Register硬 — formal, almost exclusively written
Typical useUsed on signs and posted notices to state a prohibition (no parking, no smoking, keep off the grass) — never used in ordinary spoken conversation.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + べからず駐車するべからずchuusha suru bekarazu
Verb (classical alt.)(added, not in book) classical verb form + べからず忘るべからずwasuru bekarazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ここに駐車するべからず。Koko ni chuusha suru bekarazu. No parking here! यहाँ गाड़ी खड़ी करना मना है। અહીં ગાડી પાર્ક કરવી નહીં.
芝生に入るべからず。Shibafu ni hairu bekarazu. Keep off the grass! घास पर चलना मना है। ઘાસ પર ચાલવું નહીં.
ビル内でたばこを吸うべからず。Biru nai de tabako o suu bekarazu. No smoking inside the building! इमारत के अंदर धूम्रपान वर्जित है। ઇમારતની અંદર ધૂમ્રપાન નિષેધ છે.
初心、忘るべからず。Shoshin, wasuru bekarazu. Never forget your original resolve/beginner's spirit. शुरुआती संकल्प को कभी मत भूलो। શરૂઆતના સંકલ્પને ક્યારેય ભૂલશો નહીં.

忘る is the old classical form of 忘れる — 初心、忘るべからず is a fixed proverb, so memorize it as a whole chunk rather than trying to conjugate 忘る yourself elsewhere. べからず always ends the sentence as a blunt command; it can't be softened or embedded mid-clause.

【4】 〜べからざる

硬 formal/written
Reading~bekarazaru
Meaning (EN)"unpardonable ~ / ~ that must not (be done/allowed)" — the attributive form of べからず, describing a noun
Meaning (HI)अक्षम्य... / जिसे...नहीं किया जा सकता
Meaning (GU)અક્ષમ્ય... / જેને...ન કરી શકાય એવું
Connection (接続)V-る + べからざる + N
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useDirectly modifies a following noun to say that noun (an act, a person, a phenomenon) is beyond the pale — unforgivable or indispensable, depending on the verb.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + べからざる + N許すべからざる行為yurusu bekarazaru koui
Verb (negative meaning flips positive)欠く + べからざる + N欠くべからざる選手kaku bekarazaru senshu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
学生を脅迫してお金をゆするとは、教師として許すべからざる行為である。Gakusei o kyouhaku shite okane o yusuru to wa, kyoushi to shite yurusu bekarazaru koui de aru. Threatening students to extort money is an unpardonable act for a teacher. छात्रों को धमकाकर पैसे ऐंठना एक शिक्षक के तौर पर अक्षम्य कृत्य है। વિદ્યાર્થીઓને ધમકાવીને પૈસા પડાવવા એ શિક્ષક તરીકે અક્ષમ્ય કૃત્ય છે.
彼は、わがチームには欠くべからざる選手である。Kare wa, waga chiimu ni wa kaku bekarazaru senshu de aru. He is an indispensable player for our team. वह हमारी टीम के लिए एक अपरिहार्य खिलाड़ी है। તે અમારી ટીમ માટે અનિવાર્ય ખેલાડી છે.

べからざる is simply べからず's attributive (連体形) form, used so it can sit directly in front of a noun — compare 許すべからず (ends the sentence, "must not forgive") with 許すべからざる行為 (modifies 行為, "an act that must not be forgiven"). Watch how 欠く ("to lack") flips the feel positive: 欠くべからざる = "must not be lacked" = "indispensable."

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

妻は職場に復帰（　）、1歳になる子どもの預け先を探している。Tsuma wa shokuba ni fukki (___), issai ni naru kodomo no azukesaki o sagashite iru.

Full sentence: 妻は職場に復帰すべく、1歳になる子どもの預け先を探している。Tsuma wa shokuba ni fukki subeku, issai ni naru kodomo no azukesaki o sagashite iru.
EN: My wife, in order to return to work, is looking for somewhere to leave our one-year-old child.
HI: मेरी पत्नी काम पर लौटने के लिए, अपने एक साल के बच्चे के लिए देखभाल की जगह ढूँढ रही है।
GU: મારી પત્ની કામ પર પાછા ફરવા માટે, પોતાના એક વર્ષના બાળક માટે સંભાળની જગ્યા શોધી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aすべくsubekuin order to (return)लौटने के लिएપાછા ફરવા માટે
bすべからずsubekarazu(wrong pattern — a prohibition)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: She has a clear goal (returning to work) driving her search for childcare — Point 1's べく. べからず is a prohibition ("must not"), which makes no sense attached to her own intended action.

Q2

人殺しの道具を作るなど許す（　）ことだ。Hitogoroshi no dougu o tsukuru nado yurusu (___) koto da.

Full sentence: 人殺しの道具を作るなど許すべからざることだ。Yurusu bekarazaru koto da.
EN: Making tools for killing people is an unforgivable thing to do.
HI: इंसानों को मारने के औज़ार बनाना जैसा काम अक्षम्य है।
GU: માણસોને મારવાનાં સાધનો બનાવવાં જેવું કામ અક્ષમ્ય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aべからずbekarazu(wrong form — needs a following noun)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

「芝生に入る（　）。」という立て札が立ててあった。"Shibafu ni hairu (___)." to iu tatefuda ga tatete atta.

Full sentence: 「芝生に入るべからず。」という立て札が立ててあった。"Shibafu ni hairu bekarazu." to iu tatefuda ga tatete atta.
EN: There was a sign posted that said "Keep off the grass."
HI: वहाँ एक तख़्ती लगी थी जिस पर लिखा था "घास पर चलना मना है।"
GU: ત્યાં એક પાટિયું લગાવેલું હતું જેના પર લખ્યું હતું "ઘાસ પર ચાલવું નહીં."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aべきだbeki da(wrong nuance — recommendation, not prohibition)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A posted sign prohibiting entry is exactly Point 3's use case. べきだ means "should," the opposite instruction (it would tell people TO step on the grass), so it can't be what a "keep off" sign says.

Q4

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

今、夏なのに雪が降るといった＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ある。Ima, natsu na noni yuki ga furu to itta ___ aru.

Tiles: 1 あるべからざる (aru bekarazaru)　2 起こっている (okotte iru)　3 現象が (genshou ga)　4 地域が (chiiki ga)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

1 あるべからざる→3 現象が→2 起こっている→4 地域が

今、夏なのに雪が降るといったあるべからざる現象が起こっている地域がある。Ima, natsu na noni yuki ga furu to itta aru bekarazaru genshou ga okotteiru chiiki ga aru.

EN: Right now, there are regions where an unthinkable phenomenon — snow falling in summer — is occurring.
HI: अभी, ऐसे इलाक़े हैं जहाँ गर्मियों में बर्फ़ गिरने जैसी असंभावित घटना हो रही है।
GU: અત્યારે, એવા વિસ્તારો છે જ્યાં ઉનાળામાં બરફ પડવા જેવી અકલ્પ્ય ઘટના બની રહી છે.

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜べからざる) — あるべからざる現象 ("a phenomenon that should not exist") directly modifies 現象, describing something so abnormal it "shouldn't be happening."

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、試験に合格した。___, shiken ni goukaku shita.

Tiles: 1 懸命に続けた (kenmei ni tsuzuketa)　2 努力が実り (doryoku ga minori)　3 弁護士 (bengoshi)　4 になるべく (ni naru beku)

3 弁護士→4 になるべく→1 懸命に続けた→2 努力が実り

弁護士になるべく懸命に続けた努力が実り、試験に合格した。Bengoshi ni naru beku kenmei ni tsuzuketa doryoku ga minori, shiken ni goukaku shita.

EN: The effort I kept up desperately in order to become a lawyer paid off, and I passed the exam.
HI: वकील बनने के लिए जो मेहनत लगातार जारी रखी, वह रंग लाई और मैं परीक्षा पास कर गया।
GU: વકીલ બનવા માટે સતત ચાલુ રાખેલી મહેનત ફળી અને હું પરીક્ષા પાસ કરી ગયો.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜べく) — 弁護士になるべく ("in order to become a lawyer") states the goal behind the relentless effort (懸命に続けた努力) that ultimately succeeded (実り).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points share the classical root べし, so N1 loves testing whether you can tell them apart by their function (purpose, impossibility, prohibition, or attributive prohibition) — see Q1–Q5 above. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜べく~beku In order to ~ Vる + べく States a purposeful goal driving an action — positive, forward-looking. You're describing deliberate effort toward a real goal.
〜べくもない~beku mo nai Cannot possibly ~ Vる + べくもない Denies that even attempting/hoping makes sense — the goal is out of reach. You're saying something is beyond hope or comparison.
〜べからず~bekarazu Must not ~ (sentence-final) Vる + べからず A blunt written prohibition that ends the sentence — signage register. You're writing a rule/sign telling people not to do something.
〜べからざる~bekarazaru Unpardonable ~ / must-not-N Vる + べからざる + N The attributive form of べからず — must be followed directly by a noun. You're describing a noun (act, event, person) as beyond the pale or indispensable.
Exam trap: べからず and べからざる are the same word in two different forms — べからず ends a sentence/clause (signage: "Vるべからず。"), while べからざる must be glued directly to a following noun ("Vるべからざる+N"). If a noun comes right after the blank, it's べからざる; if the sentence ends there, it's べからず. Separately, べく (purpose, achievable) and べくもない (denial, unachievable) look almost identical but land on opposite outcomes — check whether the goal is being pursued or dismissed as impossible.
