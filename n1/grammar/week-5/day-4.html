Notes
All four points are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source — today is a fully formal-register day, common in news reports, official notices, and essays. いかん (如何) itself is a classical-flavored noun meaning "how/what" — it never changes form.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に至る

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni itaru
Meaning (EN)"to reach the point of ~ / it has come to ~" — a process finally arrives at a (often significant) end state
Meaning (HI)...की स्थिति तक पहुँच जाना / ...तक जा पहुँचना
Meaning (GU)...ની સ્થિતિ સુધી પહોંચવું
Connection (接続)N + に至る  |  V-る + に至る  |  〜に至って（は／も）  |  〜に至らず
Register硬 — formal, written (reports, narration of events)
Typical useMarks the final stage of a long or gradual process — often something serious (success, bankruptcy, a decision) after much buildup.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + に至る成功するに至ったseikou suru ni itatta
NounN + に至る手術に至らずshujutsu ni itarazu
Variant (topic/emphasis)〜に至って（は／も）今に至ってはima ni itatte wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
多くの人の協力を得て、この実験が成功するに至りました。Ooku no hito no kyouryoku o ete, kono jikken ga seikou suru ni itarimashita. With the cooperation of many people, this experiment finally reached success. बहुत लोगों के सहयोग से, यह प्रयोग आख़िरकार सफल होने की स्थिति तक पहुँच गया। ઘણા લોકોના સહકારથી, આ પ્રયોગ છેવટે સફળ થવાની સ્થિતિ સુધી પહોંચ્યો.
今に至ってはもう何もできません。Ima ni itatte wa mou nani mo dekimasen. At this late stage, there's nothing more I can do. अब इस मुक़ाम पर आकर कुछ भी करना मुमकिन नहीं है। હવે આ તબક્કે આવીને કંઈ પણ કરવું શક્ય નથી.
手術に至らず、薬だけで治った。Shujutsu ni itarazu, kusuri dake de naotta. It didn't get to the point of needing surgery — medication alone cured it. ऑपरेशन तक नौबत नहीं आई, सिर्फ़ दवाओं से ठीक हो गया। ઓપરેશન સુધીની નોબત ન આવી, ફક્ત દવાઓથી સારું થઈ ગયું.

至る literally means "to arrive/reach" — 〜に至る frames whatever precedes it as the finish line of a process. に至って(は) shifts focus onto "now that we've reached this stage," often to comment that it's too late to change course. に至らず flips it to "without reaching (that point)," implying a milder outcome than expected.

【2】 Nに至るまで

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni itaru made
Meaning (EN)"all the way up to N / even down to N" — extends a scope to an extreme, often surprising endpoint
Meaning (HI)...तक भी / यहाँ तक कि ...तक भी
Meaning (GU)...સુધી પણ / ત્યાં સુધી કે ...સુધી પણ
Connection (接続)（〜から）+ N + に至るまで
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useOften paired with から to show a full range ("from A to as far as B"); can carry a nuance of "even that much" surprise at how far the scope extends.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun（〜から）N + に至るまで人間から微生物に至るまでningen kara biseibutsu ni itaru made
い-adj／な-adj／V(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets nouns marking a scope's endpoint)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
会社の面接で、貯金の額に至るまで、いろいろ聞かれた。Kaisha no mensetsu de, chokin no gaku ni itaru made, iroiro kikareta. At the job interview, I was asked about all sorts of things, even down to the amount of my savings. कंपनी के इंटरव्यू में, मुझसे कई तरह के सवाल पूछे गए, यहाँ तक कि बचत की रक़म तक भी। કંપનીના ઇન્ટરવ્યુમાં, મને ઘણી બધી બાબતો પૂછવામાં આવી, ત્યાં સુધી કે બચતની રકમ સુધી પણ.
ここでは人間から微生物に至るまで、あらゆる生物の研究が行われている。Koko de wa ningen kara biseibutsu ni itaru made, arayuru seibutsu no kenkyuu ga okonawareteiru. Here, research is conducted on all kinds of living things, from humans all the way down to microbes. यहाँ, इंसानों से लेकर सूक्ष्मजीवों तक, हर तरह के जीवों पर शोध किया जाता है। અહીં, માનવીથી લઈને સૂક્ષ્મજીવો સુધી, દરેક પ્રકારના જીવો પર સંશોધન થાય છે.

Don't confuse this with Point 1's plain 〜に至る — here まで is doing the heavy lifting, marking N as the outer boundary of a range. The nuance note from the book is worth remembering: it can carry a "even something like that?!" feeling of surprise at how extreme the scope is (貯金の額に至るまで聞かれた implies "they even asked about my savings — that far!").

【3】 N（の）いかんでは

硬 formal/written
ReadingN (no) ikan de wa
Meaning (EN)"depending on N / according to how N turns out" — the outcome hinges on some as-yet-unknown factor
Meaning (HI)...के अनुसार / ...पर निर्भर करते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે / ...પર આધાર રાખીને
Connection (接続)N（の）+ いかんだ／いかんで（は）／いかんによって（は）
Register硬 — formal, written (notices, official language)
Typical useSays a future decision/result depends entirely on how a particular noun (results, performance, circumstances) plays out.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN（の）+ いかんでは検査の結果いかんではkensa no kekka ikan de wa
Noun (alt.)N（の）+ いかんによっては業績いかんによってはgyouseki ikan ni yotte wa
い-adj／な-adj／V(added, not in book; not a natural fit — いかん always attaches to a noun)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
検査の結果いかんでは、入院もありえます。Kensa no kekka ikan de wa, nyuuin mo ariemasu. Depending on the results of the exam, hospitalization is possible. जाँच के नतीजों के अनुसार, अस्पताल में भर्ती होना भी संभव है। તપાસના પરિણામોના આધારે, હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ થવું પણ શક્ય છે.
来月の業績いかんによっては、閉店することをも考えている。Raigetsu no gyouseki ikan ni yotte wa, heiten suru koto o mo kangaeteiru. Depending on next month's performance, we're even considering closing the shop. अगले महीने के प्रदर्शन के अनुसार, दुकान बंद करने पर भी विचार किया जा रहा है। આવતા મહિનાના પ્રદર્શનના આધારે, દુકાન બંધ કરવાનું પણ વિચારાઈ રહ્યું છે.

いかん (如何) is a formal, invariant noun meaning "how (it turns out)" — think of N+いかん as "however N turns out." では/によっては add "depending on that" to the mix, always leaving the actual outcome undetermined until N is known.

【4】 N（の）いかんにかかわらず

硬 formal/written
ReadingN (no) ikan ni kakawarazu
Meaning (EN)"regardless of N / no matter how N turns out" — the outcome/rule holds no matter what N is
Meaning (HI)...चाहे जो भी हो, उससे बेपरवाह / ...की परवाह किए बिना
Meaning (GU)...ગમે તે હોય, તેની પરવા કર્યા વગર
Connection (接続)N（の）+ いかんにかかわらず ／ いかんによらず
Register硬 — formal, written (rules, notices, legal-style language)
Typical useStates a rule or fact that applies unconditionally, no matter how the noun's value turns out — stronger and more absolute than Point 3.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN（の）+ いかんにかかわらず理由のいかんにかかわらずriyuu no ikan ni kakawarazu
Noun (alt.)N（の）+ いかんによらず判断力いかんによらずhandanryoku ikan ni yorazu
い-adj／な-adj／V(added, not in book; not a natural fit — いかん always attaches to a noun)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
理由のいかんにかかわらず、ここに駐車をしてはいけない。Riyuu no ikan ni kakawarazu, koko ni chuusha o shite wa ikenai. Regardless of the reason, you must not park here. कारण चाहे जो भी हो, यहाँ गाड़ी खड़ी करना मना है। કારણ ગમે તે હોય, અહીં ગાડી પાર્ક કરવી નહીં.
この講義は専攻のいかんにかかわらず、全員受けてください。Kono kougi wa senkou no ikan ni kakawarazu, zen'in ukete kudasai. Regardless of your major, everyone must take this lecture. विषय चाहे जो भी हो, यह व्याख्यान सभी को लेना ज़रूरी है। વિષય ગમે તે હોય, આ પ્રવચન બધાએ લેવું જરૂરી છે.
判断力いかんによらず、二十歳以上であれば選挙権がある。ただし認知症の場合などは問題があるだろう。Handanryoku ikan ni yorazu, hatachi ijou de areba senkyoken ga aru. Tadashi ninchishou no baai nado wa mondai ga aru darou. Regardless of one's judgment ability, anyone 20 or over has the right to vote. However, cases like dementia may pose an issue. निर्णय क्षमता चाहे जैसी भी हो, बीस साल से ऊपर हर किसी को मतदान का अधिकार है। हालाँकि, मनोभ्रंश जैसे मामलों में समस्या हो सकती है। નિર્ણયશક્તિ ગમે તેવી હોય, વીસ વર્ષથી ઉપરના દરેકને મતદાનનો અધિકાર છે. જોકે, ડિમેન્શિયા જેવા કિસ્સાઓમાં સમસ્યા હોઈ શકે.

かかわらず (from 関わる, "to be related to") literally means "without being affected by" — combined with いかん, "いかんにかかわらず" reads as "unaffected by however N turns out," i.e. completely regardless. によらず (from 拠る, "to depend on") gives the same "regardless" meaning via a different literal route: "not depending on how N is."

HI: निवासियों के विरोध के कारण, बहुमंज़िला इमारत का निर्माण आज तक योजना के चरण में ही अटका हुआ है।

Q7

EN: The lawyer explained the circumstances of how Company A, despite its best efforts, ended up going bankrupt.
HI: वकील ने बताया कि किस तरह पूरी कोशिशों के बावजूद कंपनी A दिवालिया होने की स्थिति तक पहुँच गई।
GU: વકીલે સમજાવ્યું કે કેવી રીતે તમામ પ્રયત્નો છતાં કંપની A નાદારીની સ્થિતિ સુધી પહોંચી ગઈ.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜に至った) — 倒産に至った ("reached the point of bankruptcy"), preceded by 努力のかいなく ("despite efforts being fruitless," a set phrase using かい "avail/use").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today splits cleanly into two families — 至る (reaching an endpoint) and いかん (an unknown-value noun) — and N1 tests both the family-internal variants and the two families against each other (see Q1–Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に至る~ni itaru Reach the point of ~ N／Vる + に至る Marks the endpoint of a process itself (success, bankruptcy, a decision). You're narrating that something finally happened after a long buildup.
Nに至るまでN ni itaru made All the way up to N （〜から）N + に至るまで Marks the outer edge of a range/scope, not a process's outcome. You're emphasizing how far a range extends ("even down to X").
N（の）いかんではN (no) ikan de wa Depending on N N（の）+ いかんで（は） The outcome is conditional — it genuinely varies with N. You want to say the result isn't decided yet; it hinges on N.
N（の）いかんにかかわらずN (no) ikan ni kakawarazu Regardless of N N（の）+ いかんにかかわらず／によらず The outcome/rule is unconditional — N's value makes no difference. You want to state a rule or fact that holds no matter what.
Exam trap: いかんでは (Point 3) and いかんにかかわらず (Point 4) look similar and both attach to N, but they're opposites — では says the result depends on N, while にかかわらず／によらず says N makes no difference at all. Always check what follows: a conditional/possible outcome (かもしれない、ありえる) signals いかんでは; an absolute rule or prohibition (てはいけない、してください) signals いかんにかかわらず. Separately, に至る (an endpoint) and に至るまで (a range boundary) share 至る but answer different questions — "did it get there?" vs. "how far does it go?"
