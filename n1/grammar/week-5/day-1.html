Answer key

Notes
All four points today are built on the verb 済む (すむ, "to be settled/resolved") but flip meaning depending on polarity and particle — this is the single biggest trap of the day, see §3 below. 〜ずにはおかない is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other three are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ずにすむ

neutral register
Reading~zu ni sumu
Meaning (EN)"manage without doing ~ / it turned out ~ wasn't needed" — a troublesome action ends up unnecessary
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना ही काम चल गया / ...करने की ज़रूरत नहीं पड़ी
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર જ કામ ચાલી ગયું / ...કરવાની જરૂર ન પડી
Connection (接続)V-ない stem + ずに + すむ
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useExpressing relief that something costly, painful, or troublesome didn't have to happen — very often followed by よかった ("I'm glad").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない stem + ずに + すむ  exception: する→せずに抜かずにすんだnukazu ni sunda
Verb (alt.)(added, not in book) V-ないで + すむ ／ V-なくて + すむ買わないですんだkawanaide sunda
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets avoidable actions, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
かなりひどい虫歯だったが、抜かずにすんでよかった。Kanari hidoi mushiba datta ga, nukazu ni sunde yokatta. Although the cavity was pretty bad, I'm glad I didn't have to get the tooth pulled. दांत में काफ़ी बुरी सड़न थी, लेकिन दांत निकलवाना नहीं पड़ा, यह अच्छी बात थी। દાંતમાં ઘણો ખરાબ સડો હતો, પણ દાંત કઢાવવો ન પડ્યો એ સારું થયું.
図書館で借りたので、本を買わずにすんだ。Toshokan de karita node, hon o kawazu ni sunda. I borrowed the book from the library, so I didn't have to buy it. लाइब्रेरी से किताब उधार ले ली, इसलिए किताब ख़रीदनी नहीं पड़ी। લાઇબ્રેરીમાંથી પુસ્તક ઉછીનું લીધું, એટલે પુસ્તક ખરીદવું ન પડ્યું.

済む (すむ) literally means "to be settled/resolved" — 抜かずにすんだ literally reads "it got resolved without pulling (the tooth)". Remember the irregular exception: する becomes せずに (never しずに). This point sets up the whole day: watch how the very same すむ flips meaning once it turns negative in Point 2.

【2】 〜ずにはすまない

neutral register
Reading~zu niwa sumanai
Meaning (EN)"cannot get away without doing ~ / must do ~" — social or moral obligation makes the action unavoidable
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना बात नहीं बनेगी / ...करना ही पड़ेगा
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે / ...કરવું જ પડશે
Connection (接続)V-ない stem + ずには + すまない
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal in writing
Typical useSays that skipping the action would be socially/morally unacceptable — a strong "there's no way around it, I/he/she must do this".
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない stem + ずには + すまない  exception: する→せずに払わずにはすまないharawazu ni wa sumanai
Verb (alt.)(added, not in book) V-ないでは + すまない払わないではすまないharawanaide wa sumanai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets obligatory actions, not states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中さんには本当にお世話になった。なにかお礼をせずにはすまない。Tanaka-san ni wa hontou ni osewa ni natta. Nanika orei o sezu ni wa sumanai. I owe Tanaka-san so much. I can't let this go without showing my gratitude somehow. तानाका जी का मुझ पर बहुत एहसान है। किसी तरह धन्यवाद दिए बिना बात नहीं बनेगी। તાનાકા સાનનો મારા પર ઘણો ઉપકાર છે. કોઈ રીતે આભાર માન્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે.
汚職が発覚した以上、彼は議員を辞職せずにはすまないだろう。Oshoku ga hakkaku shita ijou, kare wa giin o jishoku sezu ni wa sumanai darou. Now that his corruption has come to light, he will surely have no choice but to resign as an assemblyman. भ्रष्टाचार उजागर हो जाने के बाद, वह विधायक पद से इस्तीफ़ा दिए बिना नहीं रह पाएगा। ભ્રષ્ટાચાર છતો થયા પછી, તે ધારાસભ્ય પદેથી રાજીનામું આપ્યા વગર નહીં રહી શકે.

Compare directly with Point 1: 済まない is the negative of 済む, so while 〜ずにすむ says the action became unnecessary, 〜ずにはすまない says the exact opposite — the action is unavoidable. Same verb, opposite polarity, opposite meaning — this is the day's classic exam trap.

【3】 〜だけではすまない

neutral register
Reading~dake dewa sumanai
Meaning (EN)"just doing/being ~ alone won't settle the matter" — a minimal response is not enough given the situation
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...से बात नहीं बनेगी / मामला सिर्फ़ ...से नहीं सुलझेगा
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...થી વાત નહીં પતે
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj／N (plain form) + では／じゃ／だけでは + すまない
RegisterNeutral — じゃ is the casual spoken contraction of では
Typical useWarns that a minimal excuse or action (just apologizing, just being sorry) is insufficient — real, bigger consequences are implied.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)V-plain + だけでは + すまない謝るだけではすまないayamaru dake de wa sumanai
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + だけでは + すまない安いだけではすまないyasui dake de wa sumanai
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + なだけでは + すまない便利なだけではすまないbenri na dake de wa sumanai
Noun(added, not in book) N + だけでは + すまない子供だけではすまないkodomo dake de wa sumanai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
多大な損害を与えたのだから、謝るだけではすまない。Tadai na songai o ataeta no dakara, ayamaru dake de wa sumanai. You caused so much damage that you can't get off the hook by just apologizing. इतना बड़ा नुक़सान पहुँचाया है, तो सिर्फ़ माफ़ी माँगने से बात नहीं बनेगी। આટલું મોટું નુકસાન કર્યું છે, તો ફક્ત માફી માંગવાથી વાત નહીં પતે.
これ以上彼をからかうのはやめなさい。冗談じゃすまなくなるよ。Kore ijou kare o karakau no wa yamenasai. Joudan ja sumanaku naru yo. Stop making fun of him any more. It won't stay "just a joke" (things could turn serious). उसे और मत छेड़ो। यह सिर्फ़ मज़ाक नहीं रह जाएगा। તેને હવે વધુ ના છેડો. આ ફક્ત મજાક નહીં રહે.

だけ can drop out entirely (では／じゃすまない alone carries the same idea). じゃ is the everyday spoken contraction of では — expect じゃ in casual dialogue and では in writing/formal speech.

【4】 〜ずにはおかない

硬 formal/written
Reading~zu niwa okanai
Meaning (EN)"is bound to ~ / will inevitably cause ~" — a strong stimulus guarantees an effect on someone/something else
Meaning (HI)ज़रूर ...कर के ही रहेगा / असर डाले बिना नहीं रहेगा
Meaning (GU)ચોક્કસ ...કરીને જ રહેશે / અસર કર્યા વગર નહીં રહે
Connection (接続)V-ない stem + ずには + おかない  exception: する→せず
Register硬 — formal, mostly written (news, essays, literary description)
Typical useAlmost always follows a causative verb (させる／せる) or an effect-producing verb — the subject is typically inanimate (a performance, a slump, an incident) and forces a reaction onto others.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (often causative)V-ない stem + ずには + おかない感動させずにはおかないkandou sasezu ni wa okanai
Verb (alt.)(added, not in book) V-ないでは + おかない苦しめないではおかないkurushimenaide wa okanai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — pattern requires an action verb)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女の演技は見る人を感動させずにはおかない。Kanojo no engi wa miru hito o kandou sasezu ni wa okanai. Her performance never fails to move the audience. उसका अभिनय देखने वालों को ज़रूर भावुक कर देता है। તેની અભિનયકળા જોનારને ચોક્કસ ભાવુક કરી દે છે.
長引く不況は国民を苦しめずにはおかない。Nagabiku fukyou wa kokumin o kurushimezu ni wa okanai. A long-lasting economic slump is bound to cause the people suffering. लंबे समय तक चलने वाली मंदी लोगों को ज़रूर परेशान करेगी। લાંબા સમય સુધી ચાલતી મંદી લોકોને ચોક્કસ હેરાન કરશે.
この事件は、政治に影響を与えずにはおかなかった。Kono jiken wa, seiji ni eikyou o ataezu ni wa okanakatta. This incident was bound to affect the political world — and it did. यह घटना राजनीति पर असर डाले बिना नहीं रह सकी। આ ઘટના રાજકારણ પર અસર કર્યા વગર રહી ન શકી.

おかない comes from 「（そのままにして）おく」 = "to leave (something) as is" — so 〜ずにはおかない literally means "will not leave it without doing ~", i.e. is guaranteed to make it happen. The giveaway is a causative or effect verb with an inanimate/impersonal subject; contrast this with Point 2's すまない, which is about the subject's own unavoidable obligation, not a force acting on someone else.

Q1

帰りが遅くなってしまった。父にしかられないでは（　）だろう。Kaeri ga osoku natte shimatta. Chichi ni shikararenai de wa (___) darou.

Full sentence: 帰りが遅くなってしまった。父にしかられないではすまないだろう。Chichi ni shikararenai de wa sumanai darou.
EN: I got home late. There's no way I won't get scolded by my father.
HI: घर लौटने में देर हो गई। पिताजी की डांट खाए बिना बात नहीं बनेगी।
GU: ઘરે પાછા ફરવામાં મોડું થઈ ગયું. પિતાજીનો ઠપકો ખાધા વગર નહીં ચાલે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおかないokanai(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bすまないsumanaiwon't be settled without (being scolded)डांट खाए बिना नहीं चलेगाઠપકો ખાધા વગર નહીં ચાલે

Why: Being scolded is an inevitable natural consequence for the speaker, not something an outside force is causing to someone else — that's すまない's territory (Point 2). おかない (Point 4) needs a causative/effect verb where the subject forces an outcome onto another party, which doesn't fit here.

GU: તે નાટક એટલું અદભુત હતું કે જોનાર દરેકને ચોક્કસ ભાવુક કરી દે.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

知らなかった＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ すまないだろう。Shiranakatta ___ sumanai darou.

Tiles: 1 謝らずに (ayamarazu ni)　2 迷惑をかけた (meiwaku o kaketa)　3 とはいえ (to wa ie)　4 のだから (no dakara)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

3 とはいえ→2 迷惑をかけた→4 のだから→1 謝らずに

知らなかったとはいえ、迷惑をかけたのだから、謝らずにすまないだろう。Shiranakatta to wa ie, meiwaku o kaketa no dakara, ayamarazu ni sumanai darou.

EN: Even though you say you didn't know, since you caused trouble, there's no way you can avoid apologizing.
HI: भले ही तुम्हें पता नहीं था, लेकिन चूँकि तुमने परेशानी खड़ी की है, माफ़ी माँगे बिना बात नहीं बनेगी।
GU: ભલે તને ખબર નહોતી, પણ તેં તકલીફ ઊભી કરી છે, એટલે માફી માંગ્યા વગર નહીં ચાલે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜ずにはすまない, は dropped here — 〜ずにすまない is an accepted shorter variant). とはいえ ("even though") sets up the concession, のだから ("since/because") gives the reason, and 謝らずに + すまない delivers the "must apologize" conclusion.

Q7

学校側は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ だろう。Gakkou-gawa wa ___ darou.

Tiles: 1 おかない (okanai)　2 バイク通学を (baiku tsuugaku o)　3 禁止せずには (kinshi sezu ni wa)　4 危険性が高い (kikensei ga takai)

4 危険性が高い→2 バイク通学を→3 禁止せずには→1 おかない

学校側は危険性が高いバイク通学を禁止せずにはおかないだろう。Gakkou-gawa wa kikensei ga takai baiku tsuugaku o kinshi sezu ni wa okanai darou.

EN: The school is bound to ban commuting by motorbike, since it's so dangerous.
HI: स्कूल प्रशासन ज़रूर बाइक से आने-जाने पर रोक लगाएगा, क्योंकि यह बहुत ख़तरनाक है।
GU: શાળા તંત્ર ચોક્કસ બાઇક પર અવરજવર પર પ્રતિબંધ મૂકશે, કારણ કે તે ખૂબ જોખમી છે.

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜ずにはおかない) — the school (an institution, acting as a force) is guaranteed to impose a ban given the high danger; 禁止せずにはおかない literally "won't leave it without banning."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms are built from 済む (すむ), so N1 loves to test them against each other (see Q1–Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ずにすむ~zu ni sumu Manage without doing ~ Vない-stem + ずに + すむ Positive すむ — the action becomes unnecessary. Relief. You want to say "thankfully I didn't have to do X."
〜ずにはすまない~zu niwa sumanai Can't get away without doing ~ Vない-stem + ずには + すまない Negative すまない — the exact opposite of Point 1. The action is unavoidable for the subject. You want to say "I really must do X, there's no way around it."
〜だけではすまない~dake dewa sumanai Just doing X alone won't settle it V/A/na/N-plain + だけでは + すまない Judges a minimal action/excuse as insufficient — implies bigger consequences. You're warning that X alone isn't enough given the situation.
〜ずにはおかない~zu niwa okanai (硬) Is bound to make ~ happen Vない-stem + ずには + おかない An external force/stimulus guarantees an effect on someone else, usually with a causative verb. You're asserting something will definitely provoke a reaction (a performance, a slump, an incident).
Exam trap: すむ and すまない look like a simple plain/negative pair, but they land on opposite meanings (unnecessary vs. unavoidable) — always check the polarity first. Separately, すまない (Point 2) and おかない (Point 4) both mean "can't avoid X," but すまない is about the subject's own unavoidable duty (pay taxes, apologize, resign), while おかない needs a causative/effect verb where the subject forces an outcome onto someone/something else (move an audience, cause suffering, affect politics). Check who the action lands on before picking one.
