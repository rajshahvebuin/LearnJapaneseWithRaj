1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nによらず

neutral register
ReadingN ni yorazu
Meaning (EN)"regardless of N / not dependent on N" — N has no bearing on the statement being made
Meaning (HI)...की परवाह किए बिना / ...से कोई फ़र्क नहीं पड़ता
Meaning (GU)...ની પરવા કર્યા વગર / ...થી કોઈ ફરક પડતો નથી
Connection (接続)N + によらず
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useSays a statement holds true no matter what N is — often 見かけ (appearance), 職種 (job type), レベル (level).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + によらず見かけによらずmikake ni yorazu
い-adj／な-adj／V(added, not in book; not a natural fit — によらず always attaches to a noun)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「君は見かけによらずよく食べるね。」"Kimi wa mikake ni yorazu yoku taberu ne." "You eat a lot more than your appearance would suggest." "तुम अपनी शक्ल से लगने से कहीं ज़्यादा खाते हो।" "તું તારા દેખાવ કરતાં ઘણું વધારે ખાય છે."
職種によらず、賃金によらず、仕事があれば何でもやります。Shokushu ni yorazu, chingin ni yorazu, shigoto ga areba nan demo yarimasu. Regardless of job type or wage, I'll do any work available. काम के प्रकार या वेतन की परवाह किए बिना, अगर काम मिले तो मैं कुछ भी करूँगा। કામના પ્રકાર કે પગારની પરવા કર્યા વગર, જો કામ મળે તો હું ગમે તે કરીશ.
この学習書は、レベルによらず、役に立つので買ったほうがいいでしょう。Kono gakushuusho wa, reberu ni yorazu, yaku ni tatsu node katta hou ga ii deshou. This study book is useful regardless of level, so you'd better buy it. यह पढ़ाई की किताब स्तर की परवाह किए बिना उपयोगी है, इसलिए इसे ख़रीद लेना बेहतर होगा। આ અભ્યાસ પુસ્તક સ્તરની પરવા કર્યા વગર ઉપયોગી છે, એટલે તે ખરીદવું સારું રહેશે.

よる comes from 拠る/因る ("to be based on/depend on") — によらず literally says "not depending on N." This is the same によらず seen in Week 4/Day4's いかんによらず, just without いかん in front; here it attaches directly to an ordinary noun rather than to "however N turns out."

【2】 Nにとどまらず

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni todomarazu
Meaning (EN)"not limited to N (~ also) / not stopping at N" — the scope of something extends beyond N to something bigger
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...तक ही सीमित नहीं, बल्कि...भी
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...પૂરતું મર્યાદિત નથી, પણ...પણ
Connection (接続)N + にとどまらず（〜も）  |  V-る + にとどまらず（〜も）
Register硬 — formal, written (news, reports)
Typical useSays an effect/problem/action isn't confined to the first (smaller/expected) thing mentioned — a bigger, often worse, consequence also applies.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にとどまらず（〜も）輸入業者にとどまらずyunyuu gyousha ni todomarazu
Verb (dictionary form)V-る + にとどまらず（〜も）文句を言うにとどまらずmonku o iu ni todomarazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
円高の影響は、輸入業者にとどまらず、一般家庭にも及んでいる。Endaka no eikyou wa, yunyuu gyousha ni todomarazu, ippan katei ni mo oyondeiru. The effects of the strong yen aren't limited to importers — they're reaching ordinary households too. येन की मज़बूती का असर सिर्फ़ आयातकों तक सीमित नहीं है, बल्कि आम घरों तक भी पहुँच रहा है। યેનની મજબૂતીની અસર ફક્ત આયાતકારો પૂરતી મર્યાદિત નથી, પણ સામાન્ય ઘરો સુધી પણ પહોંચી રહી છે.
A社は、材料の偽装にとどまらず、食品の消費期限の改ざんも行っていた。A-sha wa, zairyou no gisou ni todomarazu, shokuhin no shouhi kigen no kaizan mo okonatteita. Company A wasn't just faking ingredients — it was also tampering with food expiration dates. A कंपनी सिर्फ़ सामग्री में हेराफेरी तक ही सीमित नहीं थी, बल्कि खाद्य पदार्थों की समाप्ति तिथि में भी छेड़छाड़ करती थी। A કંપની ફક્ત સામગ્રીની બનાવટ પૂરતી મર્યાદિત નહોતી, પણ ખાદ્ય પદાર્થોની સમાપ્તિ તારીખમાં પણ ચેડાં કરતી હતી.
彼はそのレストランで、文句を言うにとどまらず、テーブルをひっくり返してしまった。Kare wa sono resutoran de, monku o iu ni todomarazu, teeburu o hikkurikaeshite shimatta. At that restaurant, he didn't stop at just complaining — he flipped the table over. उस रेस्तराँ में, वह सिर्फ़ शिकायत करने तक ही सीमित नहीं रहा, बल्कि मेज़ ही पलट दी। તે રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં, તે ફક્ત ફરિયાદ કરવા પૂરતો મર્યાદિત ન રહ્યો, પણ ટેબલ જ ઊંધું વાળી દીધું.

とどまる means "to stay/stop (at a point)," so にとどまらず literally reads "not stopping at N" — always paired (explicitly or implicitly) with a bigger も-marked consequence that follows. This is functionally close to だけでなく／のみならず, but with a more formal, escalating "and it gets worse" flavor.

【3】 Nと相まって

硬 formal/written
ReadingN to aimatte
Meaning (EN)"combined with N / coupled with N" — two or more factors join together to produce an effect
Meaning (HI)...के साथ मिलकर / ...के साथ जुड़कर
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે ભળીને / ...ની સાથે જોડાઈને
Connection (接続)N + と相まって  |  N + も相まって
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useExplains a result as the joint product of two factors working together — often used in descriptive/analytical writing (weather + scenery, effort + luck, design + performance).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + と相まって空の青と相まってsora no ao to aimatte
Noun (alt., "also")N + も相まってデザインのよさも相まってdezain no yosa mo aimatte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
空の青と相まって、紅葉がより美しく見える。Sora no ao to aimatte, kouyou ga yori utsukushiku mieru. Combined with the blue sky, the autumn leaves look even more beautiful. आसमान के नीलेपन के साथ मिलकर, पतझड़ के पत्ते और भी सुंदर दिखते हैं। આકાશના ભૂરા રંગ સાથે ભળીને, પાનખરના પાંદડાં વધુ સુંદર દેખાય છે.
努力と運が相まって、合格することができた。Doryoku to un ga aimatte, goukaku suru koto ga dekita. Effort combined with luck let me pass the exam. मेहनत और क़िस्मत के मेल से, मैं परीक्षा पास कर पाया। મહેનત અને નસીબના મેળથી, હું પરીક્ષા પાસ કરી શક્યો.
この冷蔵庫はデザインのよさも相まってよく売れている。Kono reizouko wa dezain no yosa mo aimatte yoku urete iru. This fridge is selling well, helped along by its good design as well. यह फ़्रिज अच्छे डिज़ाइन के साथ मिलकर ख़ूब बिक रहा है। આ ફ્રિજ સારી ડિઝાઇન સાથે ભળીને સારું વેચાઈ રહ્યું છે.

相まって comes from 相まる ("to combine/join with each other"), always describing two (or more) forces acting together toward one outcome — unlike にとどまらず (Point 2), which is about scope escalating from small to big, と相まって is about separate factors merging into a single combined effect.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

その歌手の人気は日本だけ（　）、アジアの国々にも広まった。Sono kashu no ninki wa Nihon dake (___), Ajia no kuniguni ni mo hiromatta.

Full sentence: その歌手の人気は日本だけにとどまらず、アジアの国々にも広まった。Nihon dake ni todomarazu, Ajia no kuniguni ni mo hiromatta.
EN: That singer's popularity wasn't limited to Japan alone — it spread to Asian countries too.
HI: उस गायक की लोकप्रियता सिर्फ़ जापान तक सीमित नहीं रही, बल्कि एशियाई देशों तक भी फैल गई।
GU: તે ગાયકની લોકપ્રિયતા ફક્ત જાપાન પૂરતી મર્યાદિત ન રહી, પણ એશિયાના દેશો સુધી પણ ફેલાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにとどまらずni todomarazunot limited toतक सीमित नहींપૂરતું મર્યાદિત નથી
bによらずにni yorazu ni(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The も that follows ("アジアの国々にも") signals an escalating scope beyond Japan — exactly Point 2's にとどまらず. によらず would mean "regardless of Japan," which doesn't fit a sentence about geographic spread.

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ の回復は困難だと言われている。___ no kaifuku wa konnan da to iwareteiru.

Tiles: 1 個人消費 (kojin shouhi)　2 落ち込んだ (ochikonda)　3 不景気が相まって (fukeiki ga aimatte)　4 消費税のアップと (shouhizei no appu to)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

4 消費税のアップと→3 不景気が相まって→2 落ち込んだ→1 個人消費

消費税のアップと不景気が相まって落ち込んだ個人消費の回復は困難だと言われている。Shouhizei no appu to fukeiki ga aimatte ochikonda kojin shouhi no kaifuku wa konnan da to iwareteiru.

EN: It's said that the recovery of personal consumption — which fell due to the combination of the consumption-tax hike and the recession — will be difficult.
HI: कहा जाता है कि उपभोग कर की बढ़ोतरी और मंदी के संयुक्त असर से गिरी हुई निजी खपत को फिर से उठाना मुश्किल होगा।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે વપરાશ કરવેરાના વધારા અને મંદીના સંયુક્ત અસરથી ઘટેલા વ્યક્તિગત વપરાશને ફરી વધારવો મુશ્કેલ રહેશે.

Why: Built on Point 3 (と相まって) — the tax hike combined with the recession (消費税のアップと不景気が相まって) caused personal consumption to fall (落ち込んだ個人消費).

Q7

Tiles: 1 給料 (kyuuryou)　2 ボーナス (boonasu)　3 がなしになる (ga nashi ni naru)　4 にとどまらず (ni todomarazu)

ボーナスがなしになるにとどまらず、給料までカットされることになり、ショックだ。Boonasu ga nashi ni naru ni todomarazu, kyuuryou made katto sareru koto ni nari, shokku da.

EN: Not only is there going to be no bonus — my salary is even getting cut. It's a shock.
HI: सिर्फ़ बोनस बंद होने तक ही बात सीमित नहीं रही, वेतन में भी कटौती होने वाली है, यह झटका है।
GU: ફક્ત બોનસ બંધ થવા પૂરતી વાત મર્યાદિત ન રહી, પગારમાં પણ કાપ મુકાવાનો છે, આ આઘાતજનક છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜にとどまらず) — the bonus being cancelled isn't the end of it (ボーナスがなしになるにとどまらず); the escalation continues all the way to a salary cut (給料までカット).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All three points describe relationships between a noun and an outcome, but the logical relationship is different in each — exactly what N1 tests (see Q1–Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
NによらずN ni yorazu Regardless of N N + によらず N has no bearing at all on the statement. You want to say something is true no matter what N is.
NにとどまらずN ni todomarazu (硬) Not limited to N (~ also) N／Vる + にとどまらず（〜も） Scope escalates from N to something bigger/worse. You want to say "it's not just X — Y too" (usually getting more serious).
Nと相まってN to aimatte (硬) Combined with N N + と／も相まって Two (or more) separate factors merge to jointly cause one effect. You're explaining a result as the product of multiple combined causes.
Exam trap: にとどまらず and と相まって can both appear in sentences about "more than one factor," but they describe different relationships — にとどまらず is about a scope growing bigger (X, and even Y too), while と相まって is about two separate things joining forces to create a single result (X combined with Y produces Z). Ask: is one thing simply extending beyond another (→ にとどまらず), or are two things merging together (→ と相まって)? によらず is the simplest of the three: no scope, no merging — just "N doesn't matter here."
