2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

大学に合格したことを知らせたときの両親の（　）は、想像以上のものだった。Daigaku ni goukaku shita koto o shiraseta toki no ryoushin no (___) wa, souzou ijou no mono datta.

Full sentence: 両親の喜びようは、想像以上のものだった。Ryoushin no yorokobiyou wa, souzou ijou no mono datta.
EN: The way my parents rejoiced when I told them I'd passed the university entrance exam was beyond anything I imagined.
HI: जब मैंने बताया कि मैं यूनिवर्सिटी में पास हो गया हूँ, तो मेरे माता-पिता जिस तरह ख़ुश हुए, वह मेरी सोच से भी ज़्यादा था।
GU: જ્યારે મેં જણાવ્યું કે હું યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પાસ થયો છું, ત્યારે મારા માતાપિતા જે રીતે ખુશ થયા, તે મારી કલ્પના કરતાં પણ વધારે હતું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1喜びそうyorokobisouseems happy (wrong grammatical role)
2喜びようyorokobiyouthe manner of being happy
3喜びいかんyorokobi ikan(not a valid combination)
4喜びなりyorokobinari(not a valid form)

Why: Day 2's manner-noun 〜よう (ます-stem + よう) turns 喜び into "the way [they] rejoiced" — exactly what's needed as the subject of は here.

Q2

このホテルのバイキングは、すし、ステーキからラーメン（　）、あらゆる料理がそろっている。Kono hoteru no baikingu wa, sushi, suteeki kara raamen (___), arayuru ryouri ga sorotte iru.

Full sentence: すし、ステーキからラーメンに至るまで、あらゆる料理がそろっている。Sushi, suteeki kara raamen ni itaru made, arayuru ryouri ga sorotte iru.
EN: This hotel's buffet has every kind of dish, from sushi and steak all the way to ramen.
HI: इस होटल के बुफ़े में सुशी, स्टेक से लेकर रामेन तक, हर तरह का खाना मौजूद है।
GU: આ હોટેલના બુફેમાં સુશી, સ્ટીકથી લઈને રામેન સુધી, દરેક પ્રકારનું ભોજન છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1と相まってto aimattecombined with (wrong fit)
2ごとくまでgotoku made(not a valid form)
3に至るまでni itaru madeall the way up to
4に至らずni itarazuwithout reaching (wrong polarity)

Why: Day 4's Nに至るまで, paired with から, marks the full range "from sushi to as far as ramen."

Q3

その記事を読んだとき、なんともたとえ（　）恐ろしさに襲われた。Sono kiji o yonda toki, nantomo tatoe (___) osoroshisa ni osowareta.

Full sentence: なんともたとえようのない恐ろしさに襲われた。Nantomo tatoeyou no nai osoroshisa ni osowareta.
EN: When I read that article, I was struck by a terror beyond all description.
HI: वह लेख पढ़ते ही, मुझ पर एक ऐसा डर छा गया जिसे बयां करना नामुमकिन था।
GU: તે લેખ વાંચતાં જ, મારા પર એવો ડર છવાઈ ગયો જેને વર્ણવવો અશક્ય હતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1と相まったto aimatta(wrong fit)
2に至るようなni itaru you na(wrong fit)
3ようのないyou no naiindescribable, beyond comparison
4そうもないsou mo naidoesn't look likely (wrong sense)

Why: Day 2's 〜ようのない (before a noun) says no method exists to describe the terror — a fixed pairing with たとえ, just like 富士山のたとえようのない美しさ from Day 2.

Q4

彼女は美人の上にお金持ち（　）ので、とてもうらやましい。Kanojo wa bijin no ue ni okanemochi (___) node, totemo urayamashii.

Full sentence: 彼女は美人の上にお金持ちときているので、とてもうらやましい。Kanojo wa bijin no ue ni okanemochi to kite iru node, totemo urayamashii.
EN: She's beautiful, and on top of that rich, so I'm terribly envious of her.
HI: वह ख़ूबसूरत है और साथ में अमीर भी है, इसलिए मुझे उससे बहुत ईर्ष्या होती है।
GU: તે સુંદર છે અને સાથે અમીર પણ છે, એટલે મને તેની ખૂબ ઈર્ષ્યા થાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1といっているto itte iru(wrong pattern)
2となっているto natte iruhas become (wrong nuance)
3としているto shite iru(wrong pattern)
4ときているto kite irubeing that (as well)

Why: Day 3's 〜ときている stacks two enviable qualities (beautiful, and on top of that rich) to explain why envy naturally follows.

Q5

うちの息子は、見かけ（　）繊細な神経をしています。Uchi no musuko wa, mikake (___) sensai na shinkei o shite imasu.

Full sentence: うちの息子は、見かけによらず繊細な神経をしています。Uchi no musuko wa, mikake ni yorazu sensai na shinkei o shite imasu.
EN: My son, despite his looks, has a very delicate/sensitive temperament.
HI: मेरा बेटा, अपनी शक्ल-सूरत के उलट, बहुत नाज़ुक स्वभाव का है।
GU: મારો દીકરો, પોતાના દેખાવથી ઊલટું, ખૂબ સંવેદનશીલ સ્વભાવનો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1によらずni yorazuregardless of, despite
2ともなくto mo naku(wrong pattern)
3いかんでikan dedepending on (wrong nuance)
4ときたらto kitaraspeaking of (wrong fit, no complaint here)

Why: Day 5's Nによらず — his delicate nature has nothing to do with his appearance, the classic "looks can be deceiving" use.

Q6

それは（　）忘れられない出来事だった。Sore wa (___) wasurerarenai dekigoto datta.

Full sentence: それは忘れようにも忘れられない出来事だった。Sore wa wasureyou ni mo wasurerarenai dekigoto datta.
EN: It was an event that I couldn't forget no matter how hard I tried.
HI: वह ऐसी घटना थी जिसे भुलाना चाहूँ तो भी भुला नहीं पाता।
GU: તે એવી ઘટના હતી જેને ભૂલવા ઈચ્છું તોપણ ભૂલી શકતો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1忘れるごとくwasureru gotokuas if forgetting (wrong fit)
2忘れるべくwasureru bekuin order to forget (wrong fit)
3忘れようにもwasureyou ni moeven trying to forget
4忘れそうにもwasuresou ni mo(wrong shape — missing the volitional)

Why: Day 2's 〜（よ）うにも〜ない (same verb twice) — 忘れようにも忘れられない, "even trying to forget, I can't."

Q7

友人が掃除機を譲ってくれたので、（　）よかった。Yuujin ga soujiki o yuzutte kureta node, (___) yokatta.

Full sentence: 友人が掃除機を譲ってくれたので、買わずにすんでよかった。Kawazu ni sunde yokatta.
EN: A friend gave me their vacuum cleaner, so I'm glad I didn't have to buy one.
HI: दोस्त ने अपना वैक्यूम क्लीनर दे दिया, तो अच्छा हुआ मुझे नया ख़रीदना नहीं पड़ा।
GU: મિત્રએ પોતાનું વેક્યુમ ક્લીનર આપી દીધું, તો સારું થયું મારે નવું ખરીદવું ન પડ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1買わずに至ってkawazu ni itatte(not a valid combination)
2買わずにすんでkawazu ni sundeturned out unnecessary to buy
3買うに至らずkau ni itarazu(wrong fit)
4買うにとどまらずkau ni todomarazu(wrong fit)

Why: Day 1's 〜ずにすむ — the friend's gift made buying one unnecessary, textbook relief-pattern.

Q8

先生なんだから、「わからない」（　）でしょう。Sensei nan da kara, "wakaranai" (___) deshou.

Full sentence: 先生なんだから、「わからない」ではすまないでしょう。"Wakaranai" de wa sumanai deshou.
EN: Since you're the teacher, "I don't know" won't cut it as an answer.
HI: आप शिक्षक हैं, तो "मुझे नहीं पता" कहने से काम नहीं चलेगा।
GU: તમે શિક્ષક છો, તો "મને ખબર નથી" કહેવાથી નહીં ચાલે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ではすまないde wa sumanaiwon't be accepted as an excuse
2ではおかないde wa okanai(wrong fit)
3にはすまないni wa sumanai(wrong particle — attaches after ず, not a quoted phrase)
4にはおかないni wa okanai(wrong fit)

Why: Day 1's 〜ではすまない judges "I don't know" as an insufficient excuse for a teacher — the だけでは／ではすまない family.

Q9

税金は（　）ものだ。Zeikin wa (___) mono da.

Full sentence: 税金は払わずにはすまないものだ。Zeikin wa harawazu ni wa sumanai mono da.
EN: Taxes are something you simply can't avoid paying.
HI: टैक्स ऐसी चीज़ है जिसे दिए बिना कोई चारा नहीं है।
GU: ટેક્સ એવી વસ્તુ છે જેને ભર્યા વગર છૂટકો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1払わずにおかないharawazu ni okanai(wrong fit — needs an effect on someone else)
2払わずわけにはいかないharawazu wake ni wa ikanai(garbled form)
3払わずにはすまないharawazu ni wa sumanaicannot get away without paying
4払わないにはいかないharawanai ni wa ikanai(garbled form)

Why: Day 1's 〜ずにはすまない — this week's title phrase itself (努力せずにはすまない) uses the exact same pattern for an unavoidable obligation.

Q10

リフォームをしただけだが、（　）見違えるような家になった。Rifoomu o shita dake da ga, (___) michigaeru you na ie ni natta.

Full sentence: リフォームをしただけだが、建て直したかのごとく見違えるような家になった。Tatenaoshita ka no gotoku michigaeru you na ie ni natta.
EN: We only renovated it, but the house turned out looking as if it had been completely rebuilt.
HI: बस मरम्मत ही करवाई थी, लेकिन घर ऐसा हो गया मानो पूरा नया बना दिया गया हो।
GU: ફક્ત નવીનીકરણ જ કરાવ્યું હતું, પણ ઘર એવું બની ગયું જાણે સંપૂર્ણ નવેસરથી બાંધ્યું હોય.
#JapaneseEnglish
1建て直すべからずtatenaosu bekarazumust not rebuild (wrong fit)
2建て直しようがなくtatenaoshiyou ga nakuno way to rebuild (wrong fit)
3建て直したときてtatenaoshita to kite(not a valid form)
4建て直したかのごとくtatenaoshita ka no gotokuas if it had been rebuilt

Why: Day 3's 〜かのごとく — the house looks "as if" fully rebuilt, though only renovated, the classic "as if" comparison.

Q11

危険！この橋は渡る（　）！Kiken! Kono hashi wa wataru (___)!

Full sentence: 危険！この橋は渡るべからず！Kiken! Kono hashi wa wataru bekarazu!
EN: Danger! Do not cross this bridge!
HI: ख़तरा! इस पुल को पार करना मना है!
GU: ભય! આ પુલ પાર કરવો નહીં!
#JapaneseEnglish
1べからずbekarazumust not (sentence-final prohibition)
2べからざるbekarazaru(wrong form — needs a following noun)
3べくもなしbeku mo nashi(wrong pattern)
4べくもせずbeku mo sezu(not a valid form)

Why: Day 6's 〜べからず — a blunt written warning sign, exactly like ここに駐車するべからず from Day 6 itself.

Q12

その学生のスピーチは聞く者に感動を（　）。Sono gakusei no supiichi wa kiku mono ni kandou o (___).

Full sentence: その学生のスピーチは聞く者に感動を与えずにはおかなかった。Kandou o ataezu ni wa okanakatta.
EN: The student's speech never failed to move everyone who heard it.
HI: उस छात्र के भाषण ने सुनने वाले हर किसी को ज़रूर भावुक कर दिया।
GU: તે વિદ્યાર્થીના ભાષણે સાંભળનાર દરેકને ચોક્કસ ભાવુક કરી દીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1与えようにも与えなかったataeyou ni mo ataenakatta(garbled — wrong pattern combination)
2与えずにすんだataezu ni sundaturned out unnecessary to give (wrong sense)
3与えるべくもなかったataeru beku mo nakattawasn't even conceivable to give (wrong sense)
4与えずにはおかなかったataezu ni wa okanakattawas bound to give (an effect)

Why: Day 1's 〜ずにはおかない — the speech (an external stimulus) is guaranteed to move its listeners, the causative/effect-verb pattern.

Q13

彼が日本を代表する作家であることは（　）。Kare ga Nihon o daihyou suru sakka de aru koto wa (___).

Full sentence: 彼が日本を代表する作家であることは疑うべくもない。Utagau beku mo nai.
EN: That he is a writer who represents Japan is beyond doubt.
HI: वह जापान का प्रतिनिधित्व करने वाला लेखक है, इसमें कोई शक़ ही नहीं है।
GU: તે જાપાનનું પ્રતિનિધિત્વ કરતો લેખક છે, તેમાં કોઈ શંકા જ નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1疑うべくもないutagau beku mo nainot even worth doubting
2疑うにおかないutagau ni okanai(not a valid form)
3疑うようもないutagau you mo nai(not a standard fixed form)
4疑わないまでだutagawanai made da(wrong pattern)

Why: Day 6's 〜べくもない — his status is so obvious that even doubting it doesn't make sense, the "not even conceivable" pattern.

Q14

晴天と相まって、この休みは（　）。Seiten to aimatte, kono yasumi wa (___).

Full sentence: 晴天と相まって、この休みは花見をする人が多かった。Seiten to aimatte, kono yasumi wa hanami o suru hito ga ookatta.
EN: Combined with the sunny weather, a lot of people went cherry-blossom viewing this holiday.
HI: धूप के मौसम के साथ मिलकर, इस छुट्टी में बहुत लोग चेरी ब्लॉसम देखने गए।
GU: તડકાવાળા હવામાન સાથે ભળીને, આ રજામાં ઘણા લોકો ચેરી બ્લોસમ જોવા ગયા.
#JapaneseEnglish
1映画館はすいているに違いないeigakan wa suite iru ni chigainaithe cinema must be empty (unrelated consequence)
2花見をする人が多かったhanami o suru hito ga ookattamany people went flower-viewing
3秋は紅葉に限るaki wa kouyou ni kagiruautumn is best for foliage (off-topic, wrong season link)
4花火をおいてほかないhanabi o oite hoka nainothing beats fireworks (unrelated)

Why: Day 5's 〜と相まって needs a result that plausibly follows from "sunny weather + holiday" combining — more people going out for hanami fits that combined cause directly; the other options don't follow logically from the stated combination.

Q15

結果のいかんにかかわらず、（　）。Kekka no ikan ni kakawarazu, (___).

Full sentence: 結果のいかんにかかわらず、ベストを尽くしたことは評価すべきだ。Besuto o tsukushita koto wa hyouka subeki da.
EN: Regardless of the result, the fact that you gave it your best should be recognized.
HI: नतीजा चाहे जो भी हो, पूरी कोशिश करने की बात को सराहा जाना चाहिए।
GU: પરિણામ ગમે તે હોય, પૂરો પ્રયત્ન કરવાની વાતને બિરદાવવી જોઈએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1努力のしようにもできないことだろうdoryoku no shiyou ni mo dekinai koto darou(contradicts an unconditional statement)
2努力することに至るだろうdoryoku suru koto ni itaru darou(not a natural fit)
3ありえないくらい評価が悪かったarienai kurai hyouka ga warukatta(contradicts "regardless of result")
4ベストを尽くしたことは評価すべきだbesuto o tsukushita koto wa hyouka subeki dathe effort itself should be valued

Why: Day 4's 〜いかんにかかわらず sets up an unconditional statement — only option 4 states something that holds true no matter the result; the others secretly depend on what the result actually was, contradicting "regardless."

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

近頃の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ が多い。Chikagoro no ___ ★ ___ ga ooi.

Tiles: 1 ろくに (roku ni)　2 ときたら (to kitara)　3 若い連中 (wakai renchuu)　4 挨拶もできない者 (aisatsu mo dekinai mono)

3 若い連中→2 ときたら→★ 1 ろくに→4 挨拶もできない者

近頃の若い連中ときたら、ろくに挨拶もできない者が多い。Chikagoro no wakai renchuu to kitara, roku ni aisatsu mo dekinai mono ga ooi.

EN: Honestly, when it comes to young people these days, so many of them can't even greet people properly.
HI: आजकल के नौजवानों की तो बात ही मत करो, बहुत से लोग ठीक से नमस्ते तक नहीं कर पाते।
GU: આજકાલના યુવાનોની તો વાત જ ના કરો, ઘણા બરાબર નમસ્તે પણ કરી શકતા નથી.

Why: Built on Day 3's Nときたら — 若い連中ときたら introduces a complaint, followed by ろくに〜もできない ("can't even properly ~"), a common negative-emphasis collocation with ろくに.

Q17

保険金の支給に＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 流れをきちんと把握しておきましょう。Hokenkin no shikyuu ni ___ ★ ___ nagare o kichinto haaku shite okimashou.

Tiles: 1 様々な手続きがあるので (samazama na tetsuzuki ga aru node)　2 までは (made wa)　3 至る (itaru)　4 その (sono)

3 至る→2 までは→★ 1 様々な手続きがあるので→4 その

保険金の支給に至るまでは様々な手続きがあるので、その流れをきちんと把握しておきましょう。Hokenkin no shikyuu ni itaru made wa samazama na tetsuzuki ga aru node, sono nagare o kichinto haaku shite okimashou.

EN: There are various procedures before the insurance payout is finally made, so let's make sure to understand that process properly.
HI: बीमे की रक़म मिलने तक कई तरह की प्रक्रियाएँ होती हैं, इसलिए उस प्रक्रिया को अच्छी तरह समझ लेते हैं।
GU: વીમાની રકમ મળે ત્યાં સુધીમાં અનેક પ્રકારની પ્રક્રિયાઓ હોય છે, એટલે તે પ્રક્રિયાને બરાબર સમજી લઈએ.

Why: Built on Day 4's 〜に至るまでは — everything leading up to the payout involves procedures (様々な手続きがあるので), and その flows into 流れ ("that process") which sits just outside the blanks.

Q18
