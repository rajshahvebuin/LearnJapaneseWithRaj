Notes
Two of today's four points (〜ごとく／かのごとく and Nごとき) come from the same classical adjective ごとし, so watch the adverbial-vs-attributive split closely (see §3). ごとく／ごとき are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; ときたら／ときている are register-neutral, spoken-friendly forms.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nときたら

neutral register
ReadingN to kitara
Meaning (EN)"speaking of N... / when it comes to N..." — introduces a topic to complain or gripe about
Meaning (HI)...की तो बात ही मत करो / ...के बारे में कहूँ तो
Meaning (GU)...ની તો વાત જ ના કરો / ...વિશે કહું તો
Connection (接続)N + ときたら
RegisterNeutral, conversational — a stronger, more emotional cousin of は
Typical useSingles out a person/thing as a topic, almost always followed by a complaint or exasperated comment about them.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ときたらうちの息子ときたらuchi no musuko to kitara
V／A／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ときたら attaches to a noun topic, not a predicate)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
うちの娘ときたら、もうすぐ試験なのに遊んでばかりいるんですよ。Uchi no musume to kitara, mou sugu shiken na noni asonde bakari iru n desu yo. Speaking of my daughter, her exam is coming up soon and yet she does nothing but play. मेरी बेटी की तो बात ही मत करो, परीक्षा सिर पर है फिर भी बस खेलती रहती है। મારી દીકરીની તો વાત જ ના કરો, પરીક્ષા માથે છે છતાં ફક્ત રમ્યા કરે છે.
最近はやっている歌ときたら、心に残らないものばかりだ。Saikin hayatte iru uta to kitara, kokoro ni nokoranai mono bakari da. As for the songs popular these days, they're all forgettable. आजकल के लोकप्रिय गानों की बात करूँ तो, सब भूल जाने लायक़ हैं। આજકાલના લોકપ્રિય ગીતોની વાત કરું તો, બધાં ભુલાઈ જાય એવાં જ છે.

Think of ときたら as は with an attitude — it always signals the speaker is about to vent about the noun it marks. It's almost never used for praise.

【2】 〜ときている

neutral register
Reading~toki de iru
Meaning (EN)"being that ~ / given that ~ (as well)" — states a quality/fact that naturally explains a following result
Meaning (HI)...होने के कारण (स्वाभाविक रूप से) / ...है, तो ज़ाहिर है
Meaning (GU)...હોવાથી (સ્વાભાવિક રીતે) / ...છે, તો દેખીતું છે કે
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj／N (plain form) + ときている
RegisterNeutral
Typical useLists one or more qualities (often positive) that make a following outcome unsurprising — "being X (and Y), no wonder Z."
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (plain, chained)い-adj-くて + い-adj + ときている安くておいしいときているyasukute oishii toki de iru
NounN + ときているお金持ちときているokanemochi to kite iru
Verb／な-adjective(added, not in book) V／な-adj-plain + ときている親切ときているshinsetsu to kite iru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その店は安くておいしいときているから、いつも客でいっぱいだ。Sono mise wa yasukute oishii toki de iru kara, itsumo kyaku de ippai da. That restaurant is cheap and delicious, so naturally it's always full of customers. वह दुकान सस्ती और स्वादिष्ट है, तो ज़ाहिर है हमेशा ग्राहकों से भरी रहती है। તે દુકાન સસ્તી અને સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે, તો દેખીતું છે કે હંમેશાં ગ્રાહકોથી ભરેલી રહે છે.
田中さんは明るくて優秀ときているので、就職には困らないでしょう。Tanaka-san wa akarukute yuushuu to kite iru node, shuushoku ni wa komaranai deshou. Tanaka-san is cheerful and excellent, so she won't have trouble finding a job. तानाका जी उत्साही और होनहार हैं, तो नौकरी ढूँढने में परेशानी नहीं होगी। તાનાકા સાન ઉત્સાહી અને હોશિયાર છે, તો નોકરી શોધવામાં તકલીફ નહીં પડે.
彼女は美人の上にお金持ちときているから、みんなにうらやましがられるのは当然だ。Kanojo wa bijin no ue ni okanemochi to kite iru kara, minna ni urayamashigarareru no wa touzen da. She's beautiful, and on top of that rich, so of course everyone is envious of her. वह ख़ूबसूरत है और साथ में अमीर भी है, तो सबकी ईर्ष्या पाना स्वाभाविक ही है। તે સુંદર છે અને સાથે અમીર પણ છે, તો બધાની ઈર્ષ્યા પામવી સ્વાભાવિક જ છે.

〜ときている almost always stacks two or more qualities (often joined with の上に "on top of that") before delivering an unsurprising conclusion. Don't confuse this with Point 1's ときたら — both start with とき, but ている here is the plain verb "to be" (来ている), giving a completely different, neutral-to-positive meaning.

【3】 〜ごとく／〜かのごとく

硬 formal/written
Reading~gotoku / ~ka no gotoku
Meaning (EN)"as ~ / like ~ / as if ~" — the classical/literary equivalent of 〜のように
Meaning (HI)...जैसा / मानो ...जैसा (हो)
Meaning (GU)...જેવું / જાણે ...જેવું (હોય)
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた(が) + ごとく  |  N + の + ごとく  |  V/A/な/N (plain, N・な-adjは「である」) + かのごとく
Register硬 — formal, literary/written (essays, formal speeches, narration)
Typical useA bookish substitute for 〜ように／〜みたいに; かのごとく in particular describes something behaving "as if" it were something else.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain/た)Vる／Vた(が) + ごとくすでに述べた（が）ごとくsude ni nobeta (ga) gotoku
NounN + の + ごとく湯水のごとくyumizu no gotoku
Verb (plain, "as if")V-plain + かのごとく生きているかのごとくikite iru ka no gotoku
Noun／な-adj ("as if", exception)(added, not in book) N／な-adj + である + かのごとく天才であるかのごとくtensai de aru ka no gotoku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
すでに述べた（が）ごとく、この調査方法にはいくつかの問題点がある。Sude ni nobeta (ga) gotoku, kono chousa houhou ni wa ikutsu ka no mondaiten ga aru. As I already stated, this research method has several problems. जैसा पहले बताया जा चुका है, इस शोध पद्धति में कई समस्याएँ हैं। જેમ પહેલાં જણાવ્યું છે, આ સંશોધન પદ્ધતિમાં અનેક સમસ્યાઓ છે.
そのろう人形は、まるで生きているかのごとく、我々を見ていた。Sono rou ningyou wa, marude ikite iru ka no gotoku, wareware o mite ita. The wax figure was looking at us as if it were alive. वह मोम की मूर्ति हमें ऐसे देख रही थी मानो वह ज़िंदा हो। તે મીણની પ્રતિમા અમારી તરફ એવી રીતે જોઈ રહી હતી જાણે તે જીવંત હોય.
グラフに示したごとく、わが社の営業成績は伸びています。Gurafu ni shimeshita gotoku, wagasha no eigyou seiseki wa nobiteimasu. As shown in the graph, our company's sales performance is growing. ग्राफ़ में दिखाए अनुसार, हमारी कंपनी का बिक्री प्रदर्शन बढ़ रहा है। ગ્રાફમાં દર્શાવ્યા મુજબ, અમારી કંપનીનું વેચાણ પ્રદર્શન વધી રહ્યું છે.

Fixed idiom worth memorizing: 湯水のごとく (= お湯や水のように, literally "like hot water and water") — a metaphor for spending something (usually money) wastefully/freely, as in 湯水のごとくお金を使う. Remember the exception for かのごとく: nouns and な-adjectives insert である before it (天才であるかのごとく), rather than attaching directly.

【4】 Nごとき／N₁のごときN₂

硬 formal/written
ReadingN gotoki / N1 no gotoki N2
Meaning (EN)"a mere N / something as lowly as N / N-like N2" — belittles or dismisses the noun it attaches to
Meaning (HI)...जैसा (तुच्छ) / मामूली...
Meaning (GU)...જેવું (તુચ્છ) / સામાન્ય...
Connection (接続)N + ごとき  |  N₁ + の + ごとき + N₂
Register硬 — formal, and often deliberately condescending
Typical useDismisses the noun as too trivial/lowly to matter, or (with N₁のごときN₂) compares N₂ to N₁ in a belittling way.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (standalone)N + ごとき子どものけんかごときkodomo no kenka gotoki
Noun (comparative)N₁ + の + ごとき + N₂彼のごとき人物kare no gotoki jinbutsu
V／A／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — ごとき belittles a noun, it doesn't attach to a predicate)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼のごとき人物は忘れられて当然だ。Kare no gotoki jinbutsu wa wasurerarete touzen da. It's only natural for a person like him to be forgotten. उस जैसे तुच्छ इंसान का भुला दिया जाना स्वाभाविक ही है। તેના જેવા તુચ્છ માણસને ભુલાવી દેવો સ્વાભાવિક જ છે.
そのような莫大な金額は、我々のごとき庶民には払えるはずがない。Sono you na bakudai na kingaku wa, wareware no gotoki shomin ni wa haraeru hazu ga nai. Common folk like us could never afford to pay such an enormous amount of money. इतनी बड़ी रक़म हम जैसे आम लोग तो चुका ही नहीं सकते। આટલી મોટી રકમ અમારા જેવા સામાન્ય લોકો ચૂકવી જ ન શકે.

ごとき almost always carries a put-down tone — either belittling the noun itself (子どものけんかごとき, "a mere kids' quarrel") or, self-deprecatingly, lowering the speaker's own group (我々のごとき庶民, "common folk like us"). This is the attributive (rentaikei) form of ごとし, so it must be followed directly by a noun — never by a comma and a separate clause (that job belongs to ごとく, Point 3).

Q1

最近のテレビ番組（　）コマーシャルが多すぎる。Saikin no terebi bangumi (___) komaasharu ga oosugiru.

Full sentence: 最近のテレビ番組ときたら、コマーシャルが多すぎる。Saikin no terebi bangumi to kitara, komaasharu ga oosugiru.
EN: Speaking of TV shows these days, there are way too many commercials.
HI: आजकल के टीवी कार्यक्रमों की तो बात ही मत करो, विज्ञापन बहुत ज़्यादा होते हैं।
GU: આજકાલના ટીવી કાર્યક્રમોની તો વાત જ ના કરો, જાહેરાતો બહુ વધારે હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aときたらto kitaraspeaking of (with complaint)की तो बात ही मत करोની તો વાત જ ના કરો
bとしたらto shitara(wrong pattern — hypothetical "if")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence is a complaint about a topic (too many commercials) — exactly Point 1's job. としたら means "if we suppose/assume X," a hypothetical unrelated to griping about a topic.

Q2

グラフに示した（　）、わが社の営業成績は伸びています。Gurafu ni shimeshita (___), wagasha no eigyou seiseki wa nobiteimasu.

Full sentence: グラフに示したごとく、わが社の営業成績は伸びています。Gurafu ni shimeshita gotoku, wagasha no eigyou seiseki wa nobiteimasu.
EN: As shown in the graph, our company's sales performance is growing.
HI: ग्राफ़ में दिखाए अनुसार, हमारी कंपनी का बिक्री प्रदर्शन बढ़ रहा है।
GU: ગ્રાફમાં દર્શાવ્યા મુજબ, અમારી કંપનીનું વેચાણ પ્રદર્શન વધી રહ્યું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aごときgotoki(wrong form — needs a following noun)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

Full sentence: 子どものけんかごときに警察を呼ぶとは、大げさだ。Kodomo no kenka gotoki ni keisatsu o yobu to wa, oogesa da.
EN: Calling the police over a mere kids' quarrel is way overboard.
HI: बच्चों की मामूली-सी लड़ाई के लिए पुलिस बुलाना तो ज़्यादती है।
GU: બાળકોના સામાન્ય ઝઘડા માટે પોલીસ બોલાવવી એ અતિરેક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aごときgotokia mere (kids' quarrel)मामूली-सीસામાન્ય

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

その大統領夫人は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ お金を使ったそうだ。Sono daitouryou fujin wa, ___ okane o tsukatta sou da.

Tiles: 1 ごとく (gotoku)　2 派手 (hade)　3 好きで (suki de)　4 湯水の (yumizu no)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

2 派手→3 好きで→4 湯水の→1 ごとく

その大統領夫人は、派手好きで湯水のごとくお金を使ったそうだ。Sono daitouryou fujin wa, hade zuki de yumizu no gotoku okane o tsukatta sou da.

EN: I heard that first lady loved extravagance and spent money like water.
HI: सुना है वह राष्ट्रपति-पत्नी दिखावे की शौक़ीन थीं और पानी की तरह पैसे बहाती थीं।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે તે રાષ્ટ્રપતિ-પત્ની દેખાડાની શોખીન હતાં અને પાણીની જેમ પૈસા ખર્ચતાં હતાં.

Why: Built on Point 3's fixed idiom 湯水のごとく ("like water," i.e. wastefully) describing how she spent money, preceded by 派手好きで ("being fond of extravagance, and...").

Q7

彼女は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ うらやましがられるのは当然だろう。Kanojo wa ___ urayamashigarareru no wa touzen darou.

Tiles: 1 美人の上に (bijin no ue ni)　2 お金持ちと (okanemochi to)　3 みんなに (minna ni)　4 きているから (kite iru kara)

1 美人の上に→2 お金持ちと→4 きているから→3 みんなに

彼女は美人の上にお金持ちときているから、みんなにうらやましがられるのは当然だろう。Kanojo wa bijin no ue ni okanemochi to kite iru kara, minna ni urayamashigarareru no wa touzen darou.

EN: She's beautiful, and on top of that rich, so of course everyone envies her.
HI: वह ख़ूबसूरत है और साथ ही अमीर भी है, तो सबकी ईर्ष्या पाना स्वाभाविक ही है।
GU: તે સુંદર છે અને સાથે અમીર પણ છે, તો બધાની ઈર્ષ્યા પામવી સ્વાભાવિક જ છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜ときている) — 美人の上にお金持ちときている stacks two enviable qualities, making the following result (everyone's envy) unsurprising.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today mixes two unrelated とき-based forms with two forms from the same classical root ごとし — N1 loves testing all four against each other (see Q1–Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
NときたらN to kitara Speaking of N... (complaint) N + ときたら Introduces a topic purely to complain about it. You're about to vent about someone/something.
〜ときている~toki de iru Being that ~ (explains a result) V/A/na/N-plain + ときている Stacks qualities that make a following outcome unsurprising — usually neutral or positive. You're explaining why an outcome makes sense.
〜ごとく／かのごとく~gotoku / ka no gotoku (硬) As ~ / as if ~ Vる/Vた(が)ごとく; N+のごとく; plain+かのごとく Adverbial/continuative (連用形) — must be followed by a comma + clause, never directly by a noun. You're making a formal "as/like" comparison mid-sentence.
NごときN gotoki (硬) A mere N / N-like N + ごとき; N₁のごときN₂ Attributive (連体形) — must be followed directly by a noun (or a particle like に), never a comma. You're belittling or dismissing the noun as too trivial to matter.
Exam trap: ごとく and ごとき come from the same classical word ごとし but occupy different grammatical slots — ごとく is the connective form (comma + clause follows, like Point 3's Q2), while ごとき is the attributive form (a noun or に follows immediately, like Point 4's Q3/Q5). If you see a comma right after the blank, it's ごとく; if a noun/に follows directly, it's ごとき. Separately, don't let とき in ときたら and ときている trick you into thinking they're related — ときたら is about complaining, ときている is about an explanatory stack of qualities.
