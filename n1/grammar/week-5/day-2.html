2日目 — 間に合いそうもない (Ma ni ai sou mo nai)

Grammar points covered
〜そうもない〜ようがない〜よう (manner noun)〜（よ）うにも〜ない
Answer key

Notes
All four points repurpose the volitional/ます-stem of a verb in a different grammaticalized role — spotting which "よう/そう" shell is in play (probability, method, manner-noun, or an attempted-but-blocked action) is the whole game today.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜そうもない／〜そうにない

neutral register
Reading~sou mo nai / ~sou ni nai
Meaning (EN)"doesn't look like it will ~ / seems unlikely to ~" — a stronger, more confident negative conjecture than plain 〜なさそう
Meaning (HI)...होता हुआ नहीं लगता / ...होने की संभावना कम लगती है
Meaning (GU)...થતું હોય તેવું લાગતું નથી / ...થવાની શક્યતા ઓછી લાગે છે
Connection (接続)V-ます stem + そう + もない ／ にない
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useMaking a confident negative prediction — "at this rate, X clearly won't happen." もない and にない are interchangeable variants.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます stem + そう + もない／にない間に合いそうもないmaniai sou mo nai
Verb (potential)V-可能形-ます stem + そうにないわかり合えそうにないwakariae sou ni nai
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets predicted events/actions, not states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この分では、閉め切りの期限に間に合いそうもない。Kono bun de wa, shimekiri no kigen ni maniai sou mo nai. At this rate, it doesn't look like we'll make the deadline. इस रफ़्तार से देखें तो, आख़िरी तारीख़ तक पहुँच पाना मुश्किल लगता है। આ ગતિએ જોતાં, છેલ્લી તારીખ સુધીમાં પહોંચવું મુશ્કેલ લાગે છે.
できそうもないことを、簡単に引き受けるな。Dekisou mo nai koto o, kantan ni hikiukeru na. Don't casually take on tasks that don't look doable. जो काम होता हुआ नहीं लगता, उसे आसानी से मत स्वीकारो। જે કામ થઈ શકે તેમ ન લાગે, તેને સહેલાઈથી સ્વીકારશો નહીં.

Note the alternate reading 〜そうにない (as in わかり合えそうにない, from today's quiz) — もない and にない are freely interchangeable, so don't be thrown by seeing either. Don't confuse this fixed compound with 〜そうがない, which isn't a real pattern (がない belongs to Point 2's 〜ようがない instead).

【2】 〜ようがない

neutral register
Reading~you ga nai
Meaning (EN)"there's no way to ~ / it's impossible to ~" — no method or means exists to carry out the action
Meaning (HI)...करने का कोई तरीक़ा नहीं / ...करना नामुमकिन है
Meaning (GU)...કરવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી / ...કરવું અશક્ય છે
Connection (接続)V-ます stem + よう + がない ／ もない ／ のないN
RegisterNeutral — common in speech and writing
Typical useSays no method/means exists for the action — often "there's no way to describe/escape/explain X."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます stem + よう + がない逃げようがないnigeyou ga nai
Verb + の (before N)V-ます stem + よう + のないNたとえようのない美しさtatoeyou no nai utsukushisa
する-verb (nominal)(added, not in book) N + の + しようがない説明のしようがないsetsumei no shiyou ga nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先日見た富士山はたとえようがない美しさだった。Senjitsu mita Fujisan wa tatoeyou ga nai utsukushisa datta. The Mt. Fuji I saw the other day was beautiful beyond description. कुछ दिन पहले देखा हुआ फ़ूजी पर्वत ऐसी सुंदरता लिए था जिसकी कोई तुलना नहीं की जा सकती। થોડા દિવસ પહેલાં જોયેલો ફુજી પર્વત એવો સુંદર હતો કે તેની કોઈ સરખામણી થઈ શકે નહીં.
こんなところで地震が起きたら、逃げようがない。Konna tokoro de jishin ga okitara, nigeyou ga nai. If an earthquake happened in a place like this, there'd be no way to escape. अगर ऐसी जगह पर भूकंप आ जाए, तो भागने का कोई रास्ता नहीं होगा। આવી જગ્યાએ ધરતીકંપ આવે તો, ભાગવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નહીં હોય.

Fixed idiom to memorize as a whole chunk: どうしようもない (= どうする方法もない, "there's nothing that can be done about it") — extremely common in daily speech, built on this same よう+がない/もない skeleton. Also watch for the の-linking form before a noun (たとえようのない美しさ) versus the standalone predicate form (たとえようがない).

【3】 〜よう（manner-noun）

neutral register
Reading~you (noun)
Meaning (EN)"the way/manner of ~ing" — turns a verb's ます-stem into a noun describing how something is done
Meaning (HI)...करने का तरीक़ा / ...होने का ढंग
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની રીત / ...થવાની ઢબ
Connection (接続)V-ます stem + よう (used as a noun — can take に／は／だ)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing the manner/degree of a change or behavior, often with surprise or criticism — "the way X changed/behaved was striking."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます stem + よう + に (adverbial)変わりように驚いたkawariyou ni odoroita
VerbV-ます stem + よう + は (topic)かわいがりようは異常だkawaigariyou wa ijou da
Verb(added, not in book) V-ます stem + よう + だ (predicate)ものは考えようだmono wa kangaeyou da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
20年ぶりの故郷の変わりように驚いた。Nijuu-nen buri no kokyou no kawariyou ni odoroita. I was surprised at how much my hometown had changed after 20 years. बीस साल बाद अपने गृहनगर में आए बदलाव को देखकर हैरान रह गया। વીસ વર્ષ પછી પોતાના વતનમાં આવેલા બદલાવને જોઈને આશ્ચર્ય થયું.
彼女の子どものかわいがりようは、少し異常なくらいだ。Kanojo no kodomo no kawaigariyou wa, sukoshi ijou na kurai da. The way she dotes on her child is almost a bit excessive. वह अपने बच्चे को जिस तरह दुलारती है, वह कुछ हद तक असामान्य लगता है। તે પોતાના બાળકને જે રીતે લાડ લડાવે છે, તે થોડું અસામાન્ય લાગે છે.

This is a different grammatical animal from Points 1 and 2 — here よう isn't attaching a meaning of its own, it's simply converting the verb stem into an ordinary noun ("the manner of ~ing"), which can then take any particle (に／は／も) or act as a predicate with だ. Fixed idiom worth memorizing: ものは考えようだ ("it's all in how you look at it").

【4】 〜（よ）うにも〜ない

neutral register
Reading~(y)ou ni mo ~nai
Meaning (EN)"even if I try to ~, I can't" — an outside circumstance blocks an action the speaker wants to take
Meaning (HI)...करना चाहूँ तो भी नहीं कर सकता
Meaning (GU)...કરવા ઈચ્છું તોપણ કરી શકતો નથી
Connection (接続)V-volitional（よ）う + にも + V-potential-ない ／ V-辞書形 + にも + [理由となる節]
RegisterNeutral
Typical useUsed when a specific reason (money, a physical block, a missing counterpart) makes an otherwise-desired action impossible.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (same verb repeated)V-よう + にも + V-potential-ない行こうにも行けないikou ni mo ikenai
Verb (+ reason clause)V-よう／V-る + にも + [reason: いない／わからない, etc.]結婚しようにも相手がいないkekkon shiyou ni mo aite ga inai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電車が止まっているので、会社に行こうにも行けない。Densha ga tomatte iru node, kaisha ni ikou ni mo ikenai. The trains have stopped, so even though I want to go to the office, I can't. ट्रेनें रुकी हुई हैं, इसलिए दफ़्तर जाना चाहूँ तो भी नहीं जा सकता। ટ્રેનો બંધ છે, એટલે ઓફિસ જવા ઈચ્છું તોપણ જઈ શકતો નથી.
警察官なのに振り込め詐欺にあって、泣くにも泣けない。Keisatsukan na noni furikome sagi ni atte, naku ni mo nakenai. Even though I'm a police officer, I fell for a wire-transfer scam — I can't even properly cry about it. पुलिस अधिकारी होते हुए भी बैंक ट्रांसफ़र धोखाधड़ी का शिकार हो गया, रोना चाहूँ तो भी रो नहीं सकता। પોલીસ અધિકારી હોવા છતાં બેંક ટ્રાન્સફર છેતરપિંડીનો ભોગ બન્યો, રડવું હોય તોય રડી શકતો નથી.
結婚しようにも相手がいない。Kekkon shiyou ni mo aite ga inai. I'd like to get married, but there's no one to marry. शादी करना चाहूँ तो भी कोई साथी नहीं है। લગ્ન કરવા ઈચ્છું તોપણ કોઈ સાથી નથી.
天ぷらを作るにも作り方がわからない。Tenpura o tsukuru ni mo tsukurikata ga wakaranai. I want to make tempura, but I don't even know how, so I can't try. टेम्पुरा बनाना चाहूँ तो भी बनाने का तरीक़ा नहीं आता। ટેમ્પુરા બનાવવા ઈચ્છું તોપણ બનાવવાની રીત આવડતી નથી.

Two flavors, same skeleton: ① same verb twice (volitional + potential-negative), used when a concrete external reason blocks the action — "何か理由があってできないときに使う" (used when some specific reason prevents the action); ② volitional/plain form + にも, followed by a separate clause stating why the action can't happen (no partner, don't know how) — that following clause is literally "the reason V can't be done."

Q1

最終電車が行ってしまって、タクシーに（　）お金が足りなかった。Saishuu densha ga itte shimatte, takushii ni (___) okane ga tarinakatta.

Full sentence: 最終電車が行ってしまって、タクシーに乗ろうにもお金が足りなかった。Takushii ni norou ni mo okane ga tarinakatta.
EN: The last train had already left, and even though I wanted to take a taxi, I didn't have enough money.
HI: आख़िरी ट्रेन जा चुकी थी, टैक्सी लेना चाहा तो भी पैसे कम पड़ गए।
GU: છેલ્લી ટ્રેન જતી રહી હતી, ટેક્સી લેવા ઈચ્છ્યું તોપણ પૈસા ઓછા પડ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a乗ろうにもnorou ni moeven if (I) tried to takeलेना चाहा तो भीલેવા ઈચ્છ્યું તોપણ
b乗りようがなくnoriyou ga naku(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The following clause (お金が足りなかった) states the concrete reason the taxi couldn't be taken — exactly Point 4's "うにも + reason clause" structure. ようがない (Point 2) would need to mean "there's no method to board a taxi," which doesn't match a money-shortage reason.

Q2

できる限りやさしく言葉の意味を教えた。もうこれ以上、説明（　）ない。Dekiru kagiri yasashiku kotoba no imi o oshieta. Mou kore ijou, setsumei (___) nai.

Full sentence: もうこれ以上、説明のしようがない。Mou kore ijou, setsumei no shiyou ga nai.
EN: I explained the word's meaning as simply as I could. There's no more way to explain it than that.
HI: जितना सरल हो सका, उतना शब्द का अर्थ समझाया। अब इससे ज़्यादा समझाने का कोई तरीक़ा नहीं है।
GU: બની શકે એટલું સરળ રીતે શબ્દનો અર્થ સમજાવ્યો. હવે આનાથી વધુ સમજાવવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのしようがno shiyou gano way/method to doकरने का कोई तरीक़ा नहींકરવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી
bしそうもshisou mo(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: "There's no method left to explain further" is Point 2 (〜ようがない), attached here via の to the noun 説明. そうもない (Point 1) would mean "doesn't look like it will happen," which doesn't fit "no method remains."

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

あの人とは、いくら＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ と思う。Ano hito to wa, ikura ___ to omou.

Tiles: 1 そうにない (sou ni nai)　2 としても (to shite mo)　3 わかり合え (wakariae)　4 話し合った (hanashiatta)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

4 話し合った→2 としても→3 わかり合え→1 そうにない

あの人とは、いくら話し合ったとしても、わかり合えそうにないと思う。Ano hito to wa, ikura hanashiatta to shite mo, wakariae sou ni nai to omou.

EN: No matter how much we talked with that person, I don't think we could ever come to understand each other.
HI: उस इंसान से चाहे कितनी भी बात कर लें, मुझे नहीं लगता हम एक-दूसरे को समझ पाएँगे।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિ સાથે ગમે તેટલી વાત કરીએ, મને નથી લાગતું કે અમે એકબીજાને સમજી શકીશું.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜そうにない, the にない variant) stacked onto the potential verb わかり合え (from わかり合える) — "even having talked (however much), it doesn't seem we'll manage to understand each other."

Q7

彼女のこの＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ではないから、救急車を呼んだほうがいいよ。Kanojo no kono ___ de wa nai kara, kyuukyuusha o yonda hou ga ii yo.

Tiles: 1 普通 (futsuu)　2 ようは (you wa)　3 異常な (ijou na)　4 痛がり (itagari)

3 異常な→4 痛がり→2 ようは→1 普通

彼女のこの異常な痛がりようは普通ではないから、救急車を呼んだほうがいいよ。Kanojo no kono ijou na itagariyou wa futsuu de wa nai kara, kyuukyuusha o yonda hou ga ii yo.

EN: The way she's in such abnormal pain isn't normal — we should call an ambulance.
HI: जिस तरह वह असामान्य रूप से तड़प रही है, वह सामान्य नहीं है, इसलिए एम्बुलेंस बुलाना बेहतर होगा।
GU: તે જે રીતે અસામાન્ય પીડામાં છે, તે સામાન્ય નથી, એટલે એમ્બ્યુલન્સ બોલાવવી સારી રહેશે.

Why: Built on Point 3 (〜よう as manner-noun) — 痛がりよう ("the way [she's] in pain") takes は as topic marker, describing an abnormal manner that itself becomes the subject of judgment (普通ではない).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points recycle the ます-stem + そう／よう shell in different grammatical roles, which is exactly what N1 likes to test (see Q1–Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜そうもない~sou mo nai Doesn't look like it will happen Vます-stem + そう + もない／にない A confident negative prediction about a future event. You're forecasting that something clearly won't happen.
〜ようがない~you ga nai There's no way/method to do it Vます-stem + よう + がない／もない States that no method or means exists — not a prediction, a fact about possibility. You mean "it simply cannot be done," regardless of the future.
〜よう（名詞）~you (noun) The way/manner of ~ing Vます-stem + よう (as a plain noun) Not a fixed meaning at all — just nominalizes the verb into "the manner of X." You're describing or judging how something was done.
〜（よ）うにも〜ない~(y)ou ni mo ~nai Even trying to ~, can't V-volitional + にも + V-potential-ない／[reason clause] Requires a specific external reason blocking a desired action. You want to explain why an attempted action is impossible.
Exam trap: そう and よう are easy to mix up because both attach directly to the ます-stem, but がない only ever pairs with よう (ようがない), never with そう (there is no such thing as そうがない — see Q3). When in doubt, ask: is this a prediction about the future (→ そうもない) or a statement that no method exists (→ ようがない)? Also don't confuse Point 3's よう (a plain nominalizer with no built-in negativity) with Points 1/2/4, which are all fixed negative idioms — よう alone, followed by は/に/だ, is neutral and can even appear in a positive sentence like ものは考えようだ.
