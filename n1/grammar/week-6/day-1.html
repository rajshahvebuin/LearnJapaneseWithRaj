1日目 — 現状に即して (Genjou ni sokushite)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.94–95 (PDF pages 95–96)
Grammar points covered
Nに先駆けてNにもましてN1にひきかえN2はNに即してV／Nに即したN'
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.97 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Point 4 is tagged 硬 formal/written. Today's four patterns all involve comparison or timing relative to a standard — being first (先駆けて), exceeding a baseline (にもまして), contrasting two things (にひきかえ), or matching a standard exactly (に即して).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nに先駆けて

neutral register
ReadingN ni sakigakete
Meaning (EN)"ahead of ~ / being the first to ~" — describes doing something before others, pioneering
Meaning (HI)...से पहले / सबसे आगे बढ़कर (...करने वाला पहला)
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં પહેલાં / સૌથી આગળ વધીને (...કરનાર પ્રથમ)
Connection (接続)N + に先駆けて
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAnnouncing a first-mover achievement — a world premiere, a market-leading product launch.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その映画は、世界に先駆けて、日本での上映が決まった。Sono eiga wa, sekai ni sakigakete, nihon de no jouei ga kimatta. It's been decided that the world premiere for the movie will be in Japan. उस फ़िल्म का दुनिया भर से पहले जापान में प्रदर्शन तय हुआ। તે ફિલ્મનું દુનિયાભરથી પહેલાં જાપાનમાં પ્રદર્શન નક્કી થયું.
その会社は、他社に先駆けて、低燃費の車を開発した。Sono kaisha wa, tasha ni sakigakete, teinenpi no kuruma o kaihatsu shita. The company has been at the forefront of developing fuel-efficient cars. उस कंपनी ने दूसरी कंपनियों से पहले, कम ईंधन खपत वाली गाड़ी बनाई। તે કંપનીએ બીજી કંપનીઓ કરતાં પહેલાં, ઓછું ઈંધણ વાપરતી ગાડી બનાવી.

先駆け literally means "forerunner/pioneer" — this pattern always frames the subject as the first among a named group (the world, other companies) to do something.

【2】 Nにもまして

neutral register
ReadingN ni mo mashite
Meaning (EN)"even more than ~" — compares a current state to a normally-already-high baseline, saying it's exceeded even that
Meaning (HI)...से भी कहीं ज़्यादा (हमेशा से ज़्यादा होने वाली बात को भी पार कर जाना)
Meaning (GU)...કરતાં પણ ક્યાંય વધારે (હંમેશા વધારે હોય એના કરતાં પણ પાર)
Connection (接続)N + にもまして　(kanji note: 《増して》)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizing that something has surpassed its own usual high mark.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
久しぶりに会った彼女は、以前にもまして美しかった。Hisashiburi ni atta kanojo wa, izen ni mo mashite utsukushikatta. I saw her for the first time after a long time. She looked more beautiful than ever. बहुत दिनों बाद मिली, तो वह पहले से भी कहीं ज़्यादा ख़ूबसूरत लग रही थी। ઘણા સમય પછી મળી, તો તે પહેલાં કરતાં પણ વધુ સુંદર લાગતી હતી.
今日は平日なのに、いつにもましてお客さんが多い。Kyou wa heijitsu na noni, itsu ni mo mashite okyakusan ga ooi. There are more customers today even though it is a weekday. आज कामकाजी दिन होने के बावजूद, हमेशा से भी ज़्यादा ग्राहक हैं। આજે કામકાજનો દિવસ હોવા છતાં, હંમેશા કરતાં પણ વધુ ગ્રાહકો છે.

Notes (れい): だれにもまして (more than anyone), 何にもまして (more than anything), どこにもまして (more than anywhere) — all fixed combinations with question words.

【3】 N1にひきかえN2は

neutral register
ReadingN1 ni hikikae N2 wa
Meaning (EN)"in contrast to ~, ~..." — sets up a strong contrast between two things/people
Meaning (HI)N1 के मुक़ाबले, N2... (एक स्पष्ट विरोधाभास)
Meaning (GU)N1 ની સરખામણીમાં, N2... (એક સ્પષ્ટ વિરોધાભાસ)
Connection (接続)N1 + にひきかえ + N2は
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDrawing a sharp before/after or this-vs-that contrast, often between siblings, neighbors, or time periods.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
おとなしく引っ込み思案な姉にひきかえ、妹のほうは社交的で友達も多い。Otonashiku hikkomijian na ane ni hikikae, imouto no hou wa shakouteki de tomodachi mo ooi. In contrast to her quiet and reserved elder sister, the younger one is more social and has more friends. शांत और संकोची बड़ी बहन के मुक़ाबले, छोटी बहन मिलनसार है और उसके दोस्त भी ज़्यादा हैं। શાંત અને સંકોચશીલ મોટી બહેનની સરખામણીમાં, નાની બહેન મળતાવડી છે અને તેના મિત્રો પણ વધુ છે.
隣の立派な家にひきかえ、うちの家は古くてみすぼらしい。Tonari no rippa na ie ni hikikae, uchi no ie wa furukute misuborashii. Our house looks old and shabby in comparison to our neighbor's nice house. पड़ोस के शानदार घर के मुक़ाबले, हमारा घर पुराना और फीका है। પડોશના ભવ્ય ઘરની સરખામણીમાં, અમારું ઘર જૂનું અને ફિક્કું છે.

ひきかえ literally means "in exchange for/swapped" — the pattern always frames N2 as notably different from (often worse or better than) N1.

【4】 Nに即してV／Nに即したN'

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni sokushite V / N ni sokushita N'
Meaning (EN)"in line with ~ / according to ~" — matching something exactly to a standard/reality
Meaning (HI)...के अनुसार/मुताबिक (ठीक उसी के अनुरूप)
Meaning (GU)...ના અનુસાર/મુજબ (બરાબર તેને અનુરૂપ)
Connection (接続)N + に即してV; N + に即したN'
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useInsisting a decision/plan match a real standard (current circumstances, facts, the law) rather than an ideal or outdated one.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
現状に即して物事を考えよう。Genjou ni sokushite monogoto o kangaeyou. Let's consider things in line with the current circumstances. मौजूदा हालात के मुताबिक चीज़ों पर विचार करते हैं। વર્તમાન સ્થિતિ મુજબ બાબતો પર વિચાર કરીએ.
銀行も、時代に即した新しいサービスを提供しなければならない。Ginkou mo, jidai ni sokushita atarashii saabisu o teikyou shinakereba naranai. Banks must offer new services to meet the demands of the times. बैंकों को भी, समय के मुताबिक नई सेवाएँ देनी होंगी। બેંકોએ પણ, સમય મુજબ નવી સેવાઓ આપવી પડશે.

Notes (れい): 現実に即して, 実態に即して, 事実に即して, 法律に即して — all pairing に即して with something objective/factual, never a subjective wish.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.95. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.97.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

去年の夏も雨が少なかったけれど、今年の水不足は去年（　）深刻だ。Kyonen no natsu mo ame ga sukunakatta keredo, kotoshi no mizubusoku wa kyonen (___) shinkoku da.

Full sentence: 去年の夏も雨が少なかったけれど、今年の水不足は去年にもまして深刻だ。...kyonen ni mo mashite shinkoku da.
EN: Last summer had little rain too, but this year's water shortage is even more severe than last year's.
HI: पिछली गर्मियों में भी बारिश कम थी, लेकिन इस साल की पानी की कमी पिछले साल से भी ज़्यादा गंभीर है।
GU: ગયા ઉનાળામાં પણ વરસાદ ઓછો હતો, પણ આ વર્ષની પાણીની અછત ગયા વર્ષ કરતાં પણ વધુ ગંભીર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにもましてni mo mashiteeven more thanसे भी ज़्यादाકરતાં પણ વધુ
bに即してni sokushitein line with (wrong — not a comparison here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Comparing this year's shortage to an already-bad baseline (last year) and saying it's even worse is Point 2's にもまして.

Q2

CDの発売に（　）、そのアーチストのコンサートが開かれた。CD no hatsubai ni (___), sono aachisuto no konsaato ga hirakareta.

Full sentence: CDの発売に先駆けて、そのアーチストのコンサートが開かれた。CD no hatsubai ni sakigakete, sono aachisuto no konsaato ga hirakareta.
EN: Ahead of the CD's release, that artist's concert was held.
HI: सीडी की रिलीज़ से पहले, उस कलाकार का संगीत समारोह आयोजित हुआ।
GU: સીડીના રિલીઝ પહેલાં, તે કલાકારનો સંગીત સમારોહ યોજાયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a先駆けてsakigaketeahead ofसे पहलेપહેલાં
b即してsokushitein line with (wrong — no matching-a-standard sense here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The concert happening before the CD's release is Point 1's 先駆けて.

Q3

音楽祭でのあの女性歌手の衣装は前回（　）派手になっていた。Ongakusai de no ano josei kashu no ishou wa zenkai (___) hade ni natte ita.

Full sentence: 音楽祭でのあの女性歌手の衣装は前回にもまして派手になっていた。...zenkai ni mo mashite hade ni natte ita.
EN: That female singer's outfit at the music festival was even flashier than last time.
HI: संगीत समारोह में उस गायिका की पोशाक पिछली बार से भी ज़्यादा भड़कीली थी।
GU: સંગીત સમારોહમાં તે ગાયિકાનો પોશાક છેલ્લી વખત કરતાં પણ વધુ ભપકાદાર હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aに先駆けてni sakigaketeahead of (wrong — not about being first)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

友人の息子の優秀なの（　）、なんとうちの子の出来の悪いことか。Yuujin no musuko no yuushuu na no (___), nanto uchi no ko no deki no warui koto ka.

Full sentence: 友人の息子の優秀なのにひきかえ、なんとうちの子の出来の悪いことか。...yuushuu na no ni hikikae, nanto uchi no ko no deki no warui koto ka.

HI: दोस्त के बेटे की क़ाबिलियत के मुक़ाबले, मेरे बच्चे का प्रदर्शन कितना कमज़ोर है!
GU: મિત્રના દીકરાની કુશળતાની સરખામણીમાં, મારા બાળકનું પ્રદર્શન કેટલું નબળું છે!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにひきかえni hikikaein contrast toके मुक़ाबलेની સરખામણીમાં

Q5

時代（　）考え方をしないと若者には受け入れられない。Jidai (___) kangaekata o shinai to wakamono ni wa ukeirerarenai.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

これは＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ でお届けする商品です。Kore wa ___ de otodoke suru shouhin desu.

Tiles: 1 に先駆けて (ni sakigakete)　2 当店のみの (touten nomi no)　3 先行発売 (senkou hatsubai)　4 全国発売 (zenkoku hatsubai)

4 全国発売→1 に先駆けて→2 当店のみの→3 先行発売

これは全国発売に先駆けて当店のみの先行発売でお届けする商品です。Kore wa zenkoku hatsubai ni sakigakete touten nomi no senkou hatsubai de otodoke suru shouhin desu.

EN: This is a product we're delivering as an exclusive pre-release only at our store, ahead of the nationwide release.
HI: यह उत्पाद देशभर में जारी होने से पहले, सिर्फ़ हमारी दुकान में अग्रिम रूप से उपलब्ध कराया जा रहा है।
GU: આ પ્રોડક્ટ દેશભરમાં રિલીઝ થતાં પહેલાં, ફક્ત અમારી દુકાનમાં અગાઉથી ઉપલબ્ધ કરાવાઈ રહી છે.

Why: Built on Point 1's に先駆けて — the store's exclusive pre-release comes before the nationwide launch.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 困る。___ komaru.

Tiles: 1 いいかげんで (iikagen de)　2 ひきかえ (hikikae)　3 弟のほうは (otouto no hou wa)　4 しっかり者の兄に (shikkarimono no ani ni)

4 しっかり者の兄に→2 ひきかえ→3 弟のほうは→1 いいかげんで

しっかり者の兄にひきかえ弟のほうはいいかげんで困る。Shikkarimono no ani ni hikikae otouto no hou wa iikagen de komaru.

EN: In contrast to the dependable older brother, the younger brother is irresponsible, which is a problem.
HI: भरोसेमंद बड़े भाई के मुक़ाबले, छोटा भाई लापरवाह है, जो चिंता की बात है।
GU: ભરોસાપાત્ર મોટા ભાઈની સરખામણીમાં, નાનો ભાઈ બેજવાબદાર છે, જે ચિંતાની વાત છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's にひきかえ — contrasting the dependable older brother with the irresponsible younger one.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns involve some kind of comparison, but the type of comparison differs sharply:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nに先駆けてN ni sakigakete Ahead of ~ N + に先駆けて Being first among a group — a timing/order comparison. "The world premiere is in Japan first."
NにもましてN ni mo mashite Even more than ~ N + にもまして Exceeding an already-high baseline — a degree comparison. "More beautiful than ever before."
N1にひきかえN2N1 ni hikikae N2 In contrast to N1, N2... N1 + にひきかえN2は A direct this-vs-that contrast between two distinct things. "Unlike her sister, she's social."
Nに即してN ni sokushite In line with ~ [硬] N + に即してV／に即したN' Matching a standard exactly — not a comparison of degree at all. "Thinking based on current circumstances."
Exam trap: にもまして and にひきかえ both involve two things, but にもまして compares the SAME thing at two different times/degrees ("more beautiful than before" — still her), while にひきかえ contrasts two DIFFERENT things/people (her vs. her sister). If both sides of the comparison are literally the same subject, it's にもまして; if they're two separate entities, it's にひきかえ.
