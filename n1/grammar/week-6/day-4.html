1 Grammar Points

【1】 貧しさゆえに (Mazushisa yue ni)

硬 formal/written
ReadingV／A／な／N（普通形）+ (が)ゆえ(に)
Meaning (EN)"because of ~ / for the reason that ~" — a formal, literary way of stating a cause
Meaning (HI)...की वजह से / ...के कारण (औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)...ના કારણે / ...ને લીધે (ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V／A／な／N（普通形）+ (が)ゆえ(に) ; N/な-adj + である(が)ゆえの + N'
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useStates a reason in a bookish, weighty tone — common in essays, news, and formal narration rather than casual speech.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + (が)ゆえに貧しい（が）ゆえにmazushii (ga) yue ni
VerbV（普通形）+ (が)ゆえに知らなかった（が）ゆえにshiranakatta (ga) yue ni
Noun／な-adj (attributive)N／なである + (が)ゆえの + N'有名人である（が）ゆえの悩みyuumeijin de aru (ga) yue no nayami
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
貧しさゆえに、彼は盗みを働いた。Mazushisa yue ni, kare wa nusumi o hataraita. He stole things only because he was so poor. ग़रीबी की वजह से, उसने चोरी की। ગરીબીના કારણે, તેણે ચોરી કરી.
日本語を知らなかったがゆえに、誤解された。Nihongo o shiranakatta ga yue ni, gokai sareta. I was misunderstood because I couldn't speak Japanese. जापानी भाषा नहीं आती थी, इसी वजह से ग़लतफ़हमी हुई। જાપાનીઝ ભાષા નહોતી આવડતી, એ જ કારણે ગેરસમજ થઈ.
有名人ゆえの悩みがある。Yuumeijin yue no nayami ga aru. Due to their fame, well-known people have their own share of troubles. मशहूर होने की वजह से भी अपनी परेशानियाँ होती हैं। પ્રખ્યાત હોવાના કારણે પણ પોતાની મુશ્કેલીઓ હોય છે.

ゆえに is a more formal synonym for だから／ので — reach for it in writing, essays, or news-style narration where ので would sound too casual. ゆえの (attaching to a following noun) works like a formal version of ため の.

【2】 お金をもうけんがために (Okane o moukenga tame ni)

硬 formal/written
ReadingVない（stem）+ んがために
Meaning (EN)"in order to ~" — a formal, strong-willed version of ために, expressing determined purpose
Meaning (HI)...करने के इरादे से / ...करने के लिए (दृढ़ इच्छा के साथ)
Meaning (GU)...કરવાના ઇરાદાથી / ...કરવા માટે (દૃઢ ઇચ્છા સાથે)
Connection (接続)Vない（stem）+ んがために ／ んがための + N'  ❶ しない→せんが
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical use◆強い意志を表す (expresses a strong will/determination) — the action is done with deliberate, driven purpose.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVない stem + んがためにもうけんがためにmouke n ga tame ni
VerbVない stem + んがために救わんがためにsukuwa n ga tame ni
Verb (する, irregular)する → せんがために  never しんがために(added, not in book) そうせんがために
い-adj／な-adj／N(added, not in book; not a natural fit — んがために attaches only to verbs)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼はお金をもうけんがために、ずいぶんひどいことをやってきた。Kare wa okane o mouke n ga tame ni, zuibun hidoi koto o yatte kita. He has done a lot of terrible things in order to make money. उसने पैसे कमाने के लिए, बहुत बुरे काम किए हैं। તેણે પૈસા કમાવવા માટે, ઘણાં ખરાબ કામ કર્યાં છે.
一人の青年がおぼれている子どもを救わんがために、川に飛び込んだ。Hitori no seinen ga oborete iru kodomo o sukuwa n ga tame ni, kawa ni tobikonda. A young man jumped into the river in order to save a drowning child. एक युवक डूबते हुए बच्चे को बचाने के लिए, नदी में कूद पड़ा। એક યુવાન ડૂબતા બાળકને બચાવવા માટે, નદીમાં કૂદી પડ્યો.

The ん here comes from the same classical negative-stem family as ざる/ず (しない→せん, just like しない→せず in Week 6 Day 2) — but んがために is entirely about PURPOSE, not negation. Read it as "in order to," carrying an extra sense of urgency or resolve compared to plain ために.

【3】 あふれんばかりになっている (Afuren bakari ni natte iru)

neutral register
ReadingVない（stem）+ んばかりに／んばかりの
Meaning (EN)"as if about to ~ / all but ~" — describes a state right on the verge of happening, though it hasn't quite happened yet
Meaning (HI)मानो अभी ...होने ही वाला हो (लगभग वैसी हालत में)
Meaning (GU)જાણે હમણાં જ ...થવાનું હોય (લગભગ એ સ્થિતિમાં)
Connection (接続)Vない（stem）+ んばかりに／んばかりの + N'  ❶ しない→せん
RegisterNeutral, vivid/descriptive
Typical usePaints a dramatic picture of something on the brink — a river about to overflow, applause about to break something, a face about to burst into tears.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVない stem + んばかりにあふれんばかりにafuren bakari ni
Verb (attributive)Vない stem + んばかりの + N'泣き出さんばかりの顔nakidasan bakari no kao
Verb (added)(added, not in book) 割れんばかりの拍手waren bakari no hakushuthunderous applause (lit. "applause about to shatter (the walls)")
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
川の水があふれんばかりになっている。Kawa no mizu ga afuren bakari ni natte iru. The river is about to flood. नदी का पानी अभी उफ़नकर बाहर आने ही वाला है। નદીનું પાણી હમણાં જ ઊભરાઈને બહાર આવવાનું જ છે.
彼女は泣き出さんばかりの顔をして、私に助けを求めてきた。Kanojo wa nakidasan bakari no kao o shite, watashi ni tasuke o motomete kita. She came to me for help with a face that looked ready to burst into tears. वह रोने ही वाला चेहरा बनाकर, मुझसे मदद माँगने आई। તે રડવાની જ તૈયારીવાળો ચહેરો બનાવીને, મારી પાસે મદદ માંગવા આવી.

Common fixed images worth memorizing: 割れんばかりの拍手 (thunderous/wall-shaking applause), あふれんばかりの笑顔 (a smile that's about to overflow, i.e. beaming), 胸がはりさけんばかりに (as if one's chest were about to burst, from overwhelming emotion). The action never actually crosses the line — that's what keeps it vivid rather than literal.

【4】 うれしいとばかりに (Ureshii to bakari ni)

neutral register
Reading[文] + とばかりに／とばかりのN
Meaning (EN)"as if to say ~ / as though saying ~" — describes body language/manner that clearly conveys an unspoken message
Meaning (HI)मानो ...कह रहे हों (शब्दों में नहीं, पर हाव-भाव से)
Meaning (GU)જાણે ...કહી રહ્યા હોય (શબ્દોમાં નહીં, પણ હાવભાવથી)
Connection (接続)[文（普通形）] + とばかりに ／ とばかりの + N（表情・様子・調子・態度・口調・顔・目つきなど）
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆言葉では言わないがそのような様子で (not said in words, but the manner/expression clearly conveys it).
Word typeFormationExample
Sentence (plain)[文] + とばかりにうれしいとばかりにureshii to bakari ni
Sentence (plain)[文] + とばかりに「いやだ」と言わんばかりに"iyada" to iwan bakari ni
Fixed idiomここぞ + とばかりにここぞとばかりにkoko zo to bakari ni (seizing the moment)
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その子はうれしいとばかりに、飛び上がった。Sono ko wa ureshii to bakari ni, tobiagatta. The child jumped up and down as if overjoyed. वह बच्चा मानो बहुत ख़ुश होकर उछल पड़ा। તે બાળક જાણે ખૂબ ખુશ થઈને કૂદી પડ્યું.
彼は試験中、あきらめたとばかりに鉛筆を投げ出した。Kare wa shiken-chuu, akirameta to bakari ni enpitsu o nagedashita. He threw his pencil down during the exam as if he had given up. वह परीक्षा के दौरान, मानो हार मान चुका हो, इस तरह पेंसिल फेंक दी। તે પરીક્ષા દરમિયાન, જાણે હાર માની ગયો હોય, એ રીતે પેન્સિલ ફેંકી દીધી.
彼女は「いやだ」と言わんばかりに、首を振った。Kanojo wa "iyada" to iwan bakari ni, kubi o futta. She shook her head as if to say "No." वह मानो "नहीं" कह रही हो, इस तरह सिर हिला दिया। તે જાણે "ના" કહી રહી હોય, એ રીતે માથું હલાવ્યું.

とばかりに reports body language, not spoken words — the message is obvious from the way someone acts, even though nothing was actually said. ここぞとばかりに ("as if to say 'now's the moment'") is a common fixed idiom for seizing a rare opportunity.

Q6

その国の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 接し、私の人生観は変わった。Sono kuni no ___ sesshi, watashi no jinseikan wa kawatta.

EN: Coming into contact with that country's children, who were brimming with smiles, changed my whole outlook on life.
HI: उस देश के लबालब मुस्कान से भरे बच्चों से मिलकर, मेरी ज़िंदगी को देखने का नज़रिया बदल गया।

Why: Built on Point 3's んばかりの — smiles so full they seem about to overflow.

Q7

彼は重い罪を犯したが、事件当時、判断力が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、社会に復帰することができた。Kare wa omoi tsumi o okashita ga, jiken touji, handanryoku ga ___, shakai ni fukki suru koto ga dekita.

Tiles: 1 未熟 (mijuku)　2 がゆえ (ga yue)　3 とされる (to sareru)　4 未成年者であった (miseinensha de atta)

1 未熟→3 とされる→4 未成年者であった→2 がゆえ

彼は重い罪を犯したが、事件当時、判断力が未熟とされる未成年者であったがゆえ、社会に復帰することができた。Kare wa omoi tsumi o okashita ga, jiken touji, handanryoku ga mijuku to sareru miseinensha de atta ga yue, shakai ni fukki suru koto ga dekita.

EN: He committed a serious crime, but because at the time he was a minor considered to have immature judgment, he was able to return to society.
HI: उसने गंभीर अपराध किया, लेकिन घटना के समय, उसे अपरिपक्व निर्णय-क्षमता वाला नाबालिग माना गया, इसी वजह से वह समाज में फिर से लौट सका।
GU: તેણે ગંભીર ગુનો કર્યો, પણ ઘટના સમયે, તેને અપરિપક્વ નિર્ણયશક્તિ ધરાવતો સગીર ગણવામાં આવ્યો, એ જ કારણે તે સમાજમાં પાછો ફરી શક્યો.

Why: Built on Point 1's (が)ゆえ — being legally a minor with "immature judgement" is given as the formal reason for his return to society.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 2 and 3 share the same irregular ん-stem (from V-ない stem), but attach completely different endings with different jobs:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
(が)ゆえに(ga) yue ni [硬] Because of ~ V／A／な／N（普通形）+ (が)ゆえ(に) States a real, formal cause — like a bookish だから／ので. "He stole because he was poor."
んがためにn ga tame ni [硬] In order to ~ (with strong resolve) Vないstem + んがために States a deliberate PURPOSE, with a sense of determination — not a cause. "He jumped in the river to save the child."
んばかりに／のn bakari ni / no As if about to ~ / all but ~ Vないstem + んばかりに／の Describes a vivid state on the verge of happening — nothing was actually said or done yet. "The river looked about to flood."
とばかりにto bakari ni As if to say ~ [文（普通形）] + とばかりに Reports unspoken body language that clearly conveys a message. "She shook her head as if to say 'no'."
Exam trap: んがために and んばかりに/の both use the same irregular ん-stem (V-ない stem, with する→せん), which tempts learners into mixing them up — but がために is entirely about deliberate PURPOSE ("in order to"), while ばかりに/の is about a vivid, unfulfilled STATE ("on the verge of," "as if about to"). Separately, don't confuse ばかりに (Point 3, attaches to the ん-stem: あふれんばかりに) with とばかりに (Point 4, attaches to a whole plain-form clause: うれしいとばかりに) — the と is doing real grammatical work marking quoted "as if" content, not decoration.
