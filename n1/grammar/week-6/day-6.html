Notes
Five points today (unusual — most days have four). Point 1 is tagged 硬 formal/written. Points 3 and 5 (を境に／を機に) both mark a turning point and are near-synonyms; Point 4 (の折に) is a softer, more polite way to say "on the occasion of" than plain とき. This is the last content day before Week 6's 実戦問題 test — see §3 for a full recap table across all five points.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 結果を踏まえて (Kekka o fumaete)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN + を踏まえて
Meaning (EN)"based on N / taking N into account" — uses existing facts, results, or opinions as the foundation for what follows
Meaning (HI)...के आधार पर / ...को ध्यान में रखते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે / ...ને ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને
Connection (接続)N + を踏まえて
Register硬 — formal, common in business/academic writing
Typical useSignals that a decision, explanation, or improvement is grounded in specific evidence (survey results, facts, opinions, discussion) rather than made arbitrarily.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を踏まえてアンケート結果を踏まえてankeeto kekka o fumaete
NounN + を踏まえて事実を踏まえてjijitsu o fumaete
V／A／な(added, not in book; not a natural fit — 踏まえて needs a concrete noun as its evidentiary basis)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
アンケート結果を踏まえて、業務を改善したいと思います。Ankeeto kekka o fumaete, gyoumu o kaizen shitai to omoimasu. I would like to improve our operations based on the survey results. सर्वेक्षण के नतीजों के आधार पर, कामकाज में सुधार करना चाहता/चाहती हूँ। સર્વેના પરિણામોના આધારે, કામકાજમાં સુધારો કરવા માંગુ છું.
我が国の問題点を、事実を踏まえて説明しなさい。Waga kuni no mondaiten o, jijitsu o fumaete setsumei shinasai. Explain our country's problems, taking the facts into account. हमारे देश की समस्याओं को, तथ्यों के आधार पर समझाइए। આપણા દેશની સમસ્યાઓને, હકીકતોના આધારે સમજાવો.

踏まえる (ふまえる) literally means "to step on/stand on" — figuratively, "to stand on N as a foundation." Common collocations: 意見を踏まえて, 経験を踏まえて, 議論を踏まえて, 状況を踏まえて, ○○の話を踏まえて.

【2】 結婚を前提として (Kekkon o zentei toshite)

neutral register
ReadingN + を前提として／に（して）／前提で
Meaning (EN)"with N as the premise / on the assumption of N" — an underlying condition or assumption that everything else is built on
Meaning (HI)...को आधार/शर्त मानकर
Meaning (GU)...ને આધાર/શરત માનીને
Connection (接続)N + を前提として ; N + を前提に（して） ; N + 前提で
RegisterNeutral
Typical useStates the underlying assumption or condition behind a plan, relationship, or design.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を前提として結婚を前提としてkekkon o zentei toshite
Noun (clause + こと)[文]こと + を前提に事故が起こることを前提にjiko ga okoru koto o zentei ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼とは結婚を前提として交際しています。Kare to wa kekkon o zentei toshite kousai shite imasu. I am going out with him with marriage in view. मैं उसके साथ शादी को ध्यान में रखते हुए डेटिंग कर रही हूँ। હું તેની સાથે લગ્નને ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને ડેટિંગ કરી રહી છું.
事故対策は、事故が起こることを前提に練られている。Jiko taisaku wa, jiko ga okoru koto o zentei ni nerarete iru. Accident preventive measures are developed by anticipating that accidents will happen. दुर्घटना से बचाव के उपाय, यह मानकर तैयार किए जाते हैं कि दुर्घटना हो सकती है। અકસ્માત નિવારણના પગલાં, એમ માનીને તૈયાર કરાય છે કે અકસ્માત થઈ શકે છે.

前提 (ぜんてい) means "premise." を前提として/に describes what's assumed as a given from the start — everything downstream is built on that assumption holding true.

【3】 オリンピックを境に (Orinpikku o sakai ni)

neutral register
ReadingN + を境に（して）
Meaning (EN)"with N as the turning point / since N" — marks a clear before-and-after boundary in time
Meaning (HI)...को मोड़ या सीमा-रेखा मानकर (उसके बाद से)
Meaning (GU)...ને વળાંક કે સીમારેખા માનીને (તેના પછીથી)
Connection (接続)N + を境に（して）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useMarks a specific event or date as a dividing line, after which things noticeably changed.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (event)N + を境にオリンピックを境にorinpikku o sakai ni
Noun (date)N + を境にして4月2日を境にしてshigatsu futsuka o sakai ni shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
我が国はオリンピックを境に、急速に発展した。Waga kuni wa orinpikku o sakai ni, kyuusoku ni hatten shita. Our country rapidly developed after the Olympics. ओलंपिक के बाद से, हमारा देश तेज़ी से विकसित हुआ। ઓલિમ્પિક પછીથી, આપણો દેશ ઝડપથી વિકસ્યો.
日本では就学年齢を4月2日を境にして決めている。Nihon de wa shuugaku nenrei o shigatsu futsuka o sakai ni shite kimete iru. In Japan, the cut-off date for determining one's school age is April 2. जापान में स्कूल जाने की उम्र 2 अप्रैल को सीमा-रेखा मानकर तय की जाती है। જાપાનમાં શાળામાં પ્રવેશની ઉંમર 2 એપ્રિલને સીમારેખા માનીને નક્કી કરવામાં આવે છે.

境 (さかい) means "boundary." を境に treats an event/date as the exact line where a before-state ends and an after-state begins — it's neutral about whether the change is for better or worse.

【4】 来日の折に (Rainichi no ori ni)

neutral register
ReadingV／A／な／N（普通形）+ 折に（は）
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of ~ / at the time when ~" — a softer, more polite way of saying とき
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / ...के समय (とき का शालीन रूप)
Meaning (GU)...ના પ્રસંગે / ...ના સમયે (とき નું નમ્ર સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V／A（普通形）+ 折に（は） ; な + な + 折に ; N + の + 折に
RegisterNeutral, but noticeably more polite/formal-sounding than とき
Typical useCommon in polite invitations and gentle reminiscence — softer and more courteous than とき in the same slot.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + の + 折には来日の折にはrainichi no ori ni wa
NounN + の + 折に散歩の折にsanpo no ori ni
Verb／い-adj(added, not in book) 訪れる折にotozureru ori nion the occasion of visiting
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
来日の折には、私がご案内します。Rainichi no ori ni wa, watashi ga go-annai shimasu. I will show you around when you come to Japan. जब आप जापान आएँ, तो मैं आपको घुमाऊँगा/घुमाऊँगी। જ્યારે તમે જાપાન આવો, ત્યારે હું તમને ફેરવીશ.
私は、早朝の散歩の折に、花のスケッチをしています。Watashi wa, souchou no sanpo no ori ni, hana no suketchi o shite imasu. I stop to sketch flowers on my early morning walks. मैं सुबह-सुबह की सैर के दौरान, फूलों का स्केच बनाता/बनाती हूँ। હું વહેલી સવારના ફરવા દરમિયાન, ફૂલોનું સ્કેચ કરું છું.

折 (おり) literally means "occasion/juncture." Swap it into any place you'd use とき when you want a more polished, courteous tone — very common in formal letters and polite invitations (来日の折には, ぜひ我が家へお越しください).

【5】 引っ越しを機に (Hikkoshi o ki ni)

neutral register
ReadingN + を機に（して）
Meaning (EN)"taking N as an opportunity / triggered by N" — uses an event as the springboard for starting or stopping something
Meaning (HI)...को अवसर/मौक़ा बनाकर
Meaning (GU)...ને તક/મોકો બનાવીને
Connection (接続)N + を機に（して）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe trigger event (moving house, quitting a job, an illness) becomes the reason a new habit starts or an old one stops.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を機に引っ越しを機にhikkoshi o ki ni
Noun (clause + の)[文]の + を機に会社を辞めたのを機にkaisha o yameta no o ki ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
引っ越しを機に、不用品を処分しよう。Hikkoshi o ki ni, fuyouhin o shobun shiyou. Let's use this move as an opportunity to get rid of unnecessary things. घर बदलने को मौक़ा बनाकर, बेकार सामान निकाल दें। ઘર બદલવાને તક બનાવીને, નકામી વસ્તુઓ કાઢી નાખીએ.
会社を辞めたのを機に、水泳を始めた。Kaisha o yameta no o ki ni, suiei o hajimeta. Quitting my job gave me the opportunity to start swimming. नौकरी छोड़ने को मौक़ा बनाकर, तैराकी शुरू की। નોકરી છોડવાને તક બનાવીને, તરવાનું શરૂ કર્યું.

機 (き) means "opportunity/trigger." を機に is almost always about a NEW beginning or a decisive stop, prompted by the event — very close to を境に, but を機に leans toward a deliberate decision taken because of the event, while を境に is more neutrally descriptive of a before/after split. See §3.

Q1

その大地震（　）、各地で地震対策が見直されている。Sono oo-jishin (___), kakuchi de jishin taisaku ga minaosarete iru.

Full sentence: その大地震を機に、各地で地震対策が見直されている。Sono oo-jishin o ki ni, kakuchi de jishin taisaku ga minaosarete iru.

HI: उस बड़े भूकंप को अवसर मानकर, हर जगह भूकंप से बचाव के उपायों की समीक्षा हो रही है।
GU: તે મોટા ધરતીકંપને તક માનીને, દરેક જગ્યાએ ધરતીકંપ સામેના પગલાંની સમીક્ષા થઈ રહી છે.

Q6

彼は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 改め、まじめに働くようになった。Kare wa, ___ aratame, majime ni hataraku you ni natta.

Tiles: 1 生活態度を (seikatsu taido o)　2 境に (sakai ni)　3 これまでの (kore made no)　4 子どもが生まれたのを (kodomo ga umareta no o)

HI: बच्चे के जन्म को एक मोड़ मानते हुए, उसने अपनी पुरानी जीवनशैली बदल दी और मेहनत से काम करने लगा।

Q7

この自転車は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 作られています。Kono jitensha wa ___ tsukurarete imasu.

Tiles: 1 使用を (shiyou o)　2 身長 (shinchou)　3 前提として (zentei toshite)　4 160センチ以上の人の (hyaku-rokujus-senchi ijou no hito no)

EN: This bicycle is made on the assumption it will be used by people 160cm or taller.
HI: यह साइकिल 160 सेंटीमीटर या उससे ज़्यादा क़द के लोगों के इस्तेमाल को ध्यान में रखकर बनाई गई है।
GU: આ સાયકલ 160 સેન્ટિમીટર કે તેથી વધુ ઊંચાઈ ધરાવતા લોકોના ઉપયોગને ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને બનાવવામાં આવી છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's を前提として — the bicycle's design assumes a minimum-height user as its underlying premise.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A five-way recap — the key is asking what job the noun before the particle is doing:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nを踏まえてN o fumaete [硬] Based on N N + を踏まえて N is EVIDENCE used as a foundation for a judgment. "Improve based on survey results."
Nを前提としてN o zentei toshite With N as the premise N + を前提として／に N is an ASSUMED CONDITION everything else depends on. "Dating with marriage in mind."
Nを境にN o sakai ni With N as the dividing line N + を境に（して） N is a neutral BEFORE/AFTER boundary in time. "Development sped up after the Olympics."
Nの折にN no ori ni On the occasion of N N + の折に（は） A polite substitute for とき — no cause/effect implied at all. "I'll show you around when you visit Japan."
Nを機にN o ki ni Taking N as the opportunity N + を機に（して） N is a TRIGGER for a deliberate new start or stop. "Quit smoking after the move."
Exam trap: を境に and を機に are near-twins (both mark "after N, things changed") but differ in intent: を境に is a neutral, almost statistical before/after split (age 40, the Olympics) with no implication anyone chose it, while を機に implies a deliberate decision was made because of N (quitting a job because of a move). の折に is easy to mis-set for either, since it also references a point in time — but 折に carries NO cause/effect meaning at all; it's simply polite for とき. If the sentence describes a decision or action prompted BY the event, reach for を機に; if it just marks WHEN, reach for の折に; if it marks a statistical/objective divide, reach for を境に.
