3日目 — 狭いながらも (Semai nagara mo)

Answer key

Notes
Points 1 and 2 are both tagged 硬 formal/written and share the same ながら root, but point in different directions — see the confusion-pairs callout below. Point 4's 手前 is a face-saving cousin of Week 6 Day 2's つもりで — both are about the gap between a stated position and reality, but 手前 is about what others would think, not a mistaken self-image.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 涙ながらに (Namida nagara ni)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN／な／Aい + ながらに ; N + ながらの
Meaning (EN)"in the (unchanging) state of ~; while remaining ~" — describes an action or manner carried out exactly in that state, without it changing
Meaning (HI)...की उसी अवस्था में (बिना बदले, उसी हालत में)
Meaning (GU)...ની એ જ અવસ્થામાં (બદલ્યા વગર, એ જ હાલતમાં)
Connection (接続)N／な／Aい + ながらに ; N + ながらの（＋N'） ; Vます + ながら(にして)
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useDescribes tears/state/manner accompanying an action (涙ながらに = "in tears"), or a state unchanged since long ago (昔ながらの = "as of old, traditional").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + ながらに涙ながらに（訴えた）namida nagara ni
Verb (ます-stem)Vます + ながら(にして)家にいながら(にして)ie ni i-nagara (ni shite)
Noun (attributive)N + ながらの + N'昔ながらの製法mukashi nagara no seihou
Fixed idiom生まれ + ながらにして生まれながらにしてumare nagara ni shite (= 生まれたときから)
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は涙ながらに真実を訴えた。Kanojo wa namida nagara ni shinjitsu o uttaeta. She explained in tears what had actually happened. उसने आँसुओं के साथ सच्चाई बयान की। તેણે આંસુ સાથે સાચી વાત કહી.
コンピューターのおかげで、家にいながら（にして）世界の情報を知ることができる。Konpyuutaa no okage de, ie ni inagara (ni shite) sekai no jouhou o shiru koto ga dekiru. Thanks to computers, we can get all the information in the world while staying at home. कंप्यूटर की बदौलत, घर पर रहते हुए भी दुनिया भर की जानकारी पा सकते हैं। કમ્પ્યુટરના કારણે, ઘરે રહીને પણ દુનિયાભરની માહિતી મેળવી શકાય છે.
その店は、昔ながらの製法で豆腐を作っている。Sono mise wa, mukashi nagara no seihou de toufu o tsukutte iru. The shop makes tofu using traditional methods, unchanged since the old days. वह दुकान पुराने ज़माने की पुरानी विधि से टोफू बनाती है। તે દુકાન જૂના જમાનાની પરંપરાગત રીતથી ટોફુ બનાવે છે.

涙ながらに (tears) and 昔ながらの (the old method) both describe a state held exactly as-is while the main action continues — the tears or the method don't change. Don't confuse this state-describing ながらに with Point 2's concessive ながらも; see §3 for the direct contrast.

【2】 狭いながらも (Semai nagara mo)

硬 formal/written
ReadingN／な／Aい／Vます + ながら(も)
Meaning (EN)"even though ~ (is true), nonetheless ~" — concessive: admits one fact while asserting a contrasting one
Meaning (HI)भले ही ...हो, फिर भी...
Meaning (GU)ભલે ...હોય, છતાં પણ...
Connection (接続)N／な／Aい／Vます + ながら(も)
Register硬 — formal, mostly written, though many fixed forms are common in speech too
Typical useAdmits a limiting or unexpected circumstance (a small house, being a student) while asserting the situation is fine, or contrasts, regardless.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + ながらも狭いながらもsemai nagara mo
Noun (via である)Nであり + ながら学生の身分でありながらgakusei no mibun de ari nagara
Fixed idiom (adverb)残念 + ながら残念ながらzannen nagara (regrettably)
Fixed idiom (adverb)恥ずかし + ながら恥ずかしながらhazukashi nagara (I'm ashamed to say)
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
自分の家は狭いながらも落ち着く。Jibun no ie wa semai nagara mo ochitsuku. I find my home comfortable although it is small. अपना घर भले ही छोटा हो, पर उसमें सुकून मिलता है। પોતાનું ઘર ભલે નાનું હોય, પણ તેમાં શાંતિ મળે છે.
彼は学生の身分でありながら、高級車を乗り回している。Kare wa gakusei no mibun de ari nagara, koukyuusha o norimawashite iru. Although he is a student, he drives around in an expensive car. वह छात्र होते हुए भी, महँगी गाड़ी चलाता है। તે વિદ્યાર્થી હોવા છતાં, મોંઘી ગાડી ચલાવે છે.

A long list of ながら(も) forms are frozen idioms best memorized whole: 残念ながら (regrettably), 今さらながら (even at this late stage), 恥ずかしながら (I'm ashamed to say), 及ばずながら (for what little it's worth), 敵ながらあっぱれ (though an enemy, admirable), 勝手ながら〜させていただきます (though it may be forward of me, I will ~). Don't try to freely reconjugate these — learn them as fixed phrases.

【3】 外見もさることながら (Gaiken mo saru koto nagara)

neutral register
ReadingN1もさることながらN2も
Meaning (EN)"not to mention N1, N2 as well" — N1 is impressive/relevant enough on its own, but N2 also deserves equal (or more) attention
Meaning (HI)N1 तो है ही, N2 भी...
Meaning (GU)N1 તો છે જ, N2 પણ...
Connection (接続)N1もさることながら、N2も
RegisterNeutral, common in written reviews/descriptions
Typical usePraises or highlights two things together, one after the other, implying the first is already notable but the second stands out just as much.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN1もさることながらN2も外見もさることながら性格もgaiken mo saru koto nagara seikaku mo
NounN1もさることながらN2も疲労もさることながら、ストレスもhirou mo saru koto nagara, sutoresu mo
い-adj／な-adj／V(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern compares two nouns, not verbs or descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は外見もさることながら性格もとてもいい。Kanojo wa gaiken mo saru koto nagara seikaku mo totemo ii. She looks very nice, and her personality is wonderful as well. वह दिखने में तो सुंदर है ही, उसका स्वभाव भी बहुत अच्छा है। તે દેખાવમાં તો સુંદર છે જ, તેનો સ્વભાવ પણ ખૂબ સારો છે.
最近、忙しすぎて、疲労もさることながら、ストレスもピークに達している。Saikin, isogashi sugite, hirou mo saru koto nagara, sutoresu mo piiku ni tasshite iru. I've been so busy lately that, not to mention my fatigue, my stress has also peaked. आजकल इतना व्यस्त हूँ कि थकान तो है ही, तनाव भी चरम पर पहुँच गया है। આજકાલ એટલો વ્યસ્ત છું કે થાક તો છે જ, તણાવ પણ ચરમસીમાએ પહોંચી ગયો છે.

もさることながら doesn't dismiss N1 — it treats N1 as a given (obviously true) so the sentence can move on to spotlight N2 as equally worth noting. Think "N1, of course — but N2 too."

【4】 生徒たちの手前 (Seito-tachi no temae)

neutral register
ReadingN + の手前 ; V-plain + 手前
Meaning (EN)"given my position in front of N, I can't ~" — describes acting a certain way to save face / maintain consistency before a specific audience
Meaning (HI)...के सामने अपनी इज़्ज़त/स्थिति की वजह से (ऐसा नहीं कर सकते)
Meaning (GU)...ની સામે પોતાની આબરૂ/સ્થિતિને કારણે (એવું ન કરી શકાય)
Connection (接続)N + の手前 ; V-た（普通形） + 手前
RegisterNeutral
Typical useJustifies why the speaker must maintain consistency or can't back down, because of how it would look to a particular person or group.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + の手前生徒たちの手前seito-tachi no temae
Verb (plain, past)V-た + 手前必ず行くと言った手前kanarazu iku to itta temae
い-adj／な-adj(added, not in book; not a natural fit — 手前 needs a noun or a completed action as its "face-saving" basis)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
生徒たちの手前、この言葉を知らないとは言えない。Seito-tachi no temae, kono kotoba o shiranai to wa ienai. I can't admit that I don't know the word in front of my students. छात्रों के सामने, यह नहीं कह सकता कि मुझे यह शब्द नहीं आता। વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સામે, એવું ન કહી શકાય કે મને આ શબ્દ ખબર નથી.
必ず行くと言った手前、休むわけにはいかない。Kanarazu iku to itta temae, yasumu wake ni wa ikanai. I can't afford not to go since I already told them I would go. जब यह कह ही दिया कि ज़रूर जाऊँगा, तो अब आराम नहीं कर सकता। જ્યારે કહી જ દીધું કે ચોક્કસ જઈશ, તો હવે આરામ ન કરી શકાય.

手前 literally means "in front of / this side of" — here it's the social "front" the speaker must maintain. Compare with Week 6 Day 2's つもりで: つもりで is about a gap between one's own feeling and reality, while 手前 is about a gap between what one said/who's watching and what one would otherwise prefer to do.

Q1

人は外見より中身が重要であると（　）、つい外見のよさに目を奪われてしまう。Hito wa gaiken yori nakami ga juuyou de aru to (___), tsui gaiken no yosa ni me o ubawarete shimau.

Full sentence: 人は外見より中身が重要であると思いながらも、つい外見のよさに目を奪われてしまう。...omoi nagara mo, tsui gaiken no yosa ni me o ubawarete shimau.
EN: Even though people think character matters more than appearance, they inevitably end up captivated by good looks anyway.
HI: भले ही लोग सोचते हों कि दिखावे से ज़्यादा अंदर की बात मायने रखती है, फिर भी अनजाने में अच्छे दिखावे की तरफ़ आकर्षित हो ही जाते हैं।
GU: ભલે લોકો વિચારે કે દેખાવ કરતાં અંદરની વાત મહત્વની છે, છતાં અજાણતાં સારા દેખાવ તરફ ખેંચાઈ જ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a思いながらもomoi nagara moeven though one thinksभले ही सोचेंભલે વિચારે
b思いながらにomoi nagara ni(wrong — this needs a concessive contrast, not a state description)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence sets up a contradiction (believing X, yet doing Y anyway) — that's Point 2's concessive ながらも, not Point 1's state-describing ながらに.

GU: આ ફિલ્મની વાર્તા તો સારી છે જ, તેના દ્રશ્યોની સુંદરતા પણ ખૂબ વખણાય છે.

Q6

日本チームは＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 手に入れることができた。Nihon chiimu wa ___ te ni ireru koto ga dekita.

Tiles: 1 かろうじて (karoujite)　2 しながらも (shi nagara mo)　3 銅メダルを (dou medaru o)　4 苦戦 (kusen)

4 苦戦→2 しながらも→1 かろうじて→3 銅メダルを

日本チームは苦戦しながらも、かろうじて銅メダルを手に入れることができた。Nihon chiimu wa kusen shi nagara mo, karoujite dou medaru o te ni ireru koto ga dekita.

EN: The Japan team struggled, but they managed to barely secure the bronze medal.
HI: जापान की टीम को मुश्किलें आईं, फिर भी वे बड़ी मुश्किल से कांस्य पदक हासिल कर पाए।
GU: જાપાનની ટીમને મુશ્કેલીઓ પડી, છતાં તેઓ મહામુસીબતે કાંસ્ય ચંદ્રક મેળવી શક્યા.

Why: Built on Point 2's ながらも — struggling (苦戦し), yet still managing to get the medal.

Q7

彼のスピーチは、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ おかなかった。Kare no supiichi wa, ___ okanakatta.

Tiles: 1 表現力や迫力があり (hyougenryoku ya hakuryoku ga ari)　2 聞く者を (kiku mono o)　3 内容もさることながら (naiyou mo saru koto nagara)　4 感動させずには (kandou sasezu ni wa)

3 内容もさることながら→1 表現力や迫力があり→2 聞く者を→4 感動させずには

彼のスピーチは、内容もさることながら表現力や迫力があり、聞く者を感動させずにはおかなかった。Kare no supiichi wa, naiyou mo saru koto nagara hyougenryoku ya hakuryoku ga ari, kiku mono o kandou sasezu ni wa okanakatta.

EN: His speech, not to mention its content, was expressive and powerful, and it was bound to move everyone who heard it.
HI: उसका भाषण, विषय-वस्तु तो अच्छी थी ही, प्रभावशाली और दमदार भी था, और सुनने वालों को भावुक किए बिना नहीं रहा।
GU: તેનું ભાષણ, વિષયવસ્તુ તો સારી હતી જ, પ્રભાવશાળી અને જોરદાર પણ હતું, અને સાંભળનારાઓને ભાવુક કર્યા વગર ન રહ્યું.

Why: Combines Point 3's もさることながら with the ずにはおかない pattern (Week 5 Day 1) — the speech was bound to move its listeners.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points share a family resemblance (ながら appears in three of them), but each does a different job:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ながらに／ながらの~nagara ni / nagara no [硬] In the (unchanged) state of ~ N／な／Aい + ながらに ; N + ながらの Describes a state carried through unchanged — no contradiction implied. "She appealed in tears." / "Made the traditional way."
〜ながらも~nagara mo [硬] Even though ~, nonetheless ~ N／な／Aい／Vます + ながら(も) Concessive — admits a fact, then contradicts the expected outcome. "Small, but still cozy." / "A student, yet drives a luxury car."
N1もさることながらN2もN1 mo saru koto nagara N2 mo Not to mention N1, N2 as well N1もさることながら、N2も Lists two praiseworthy things together, not a contradiction. "Looks great, and personality too."
Nの手前N no temae Given my position in front of N, I can't ~ N + の手前 ; V-た + 手前 About face/reputation before a specific audience, not about facts or praise. "Can't back out — I already told them I'd go."
Exam trap: ながらに and ながらも share the same root but point in opposite directions: ながらに/ながらの describes a state carried through UNCHANGED (涙ながらに, 昔ながらの), while ながらも is concessive, admitting a fact and then contradicting the expectation it creates (狭いながらも, 学生でありながら). Watch out for 子どもながらに (Quiz Q5) — grammatically it's Point 1's state-form, yet it reads with a faint concessive flavor ("despite being just a child"), showing the two meanings can blur at the edges. When unsure, ask: is the sentence simply describing HOW something was done (→ ながらに), or is it setting up a fact that contradicts what comes next (→ ながらも)?
