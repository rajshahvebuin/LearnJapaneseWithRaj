問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

彼のコンサートの模様が10年の月日（　）ついに映像化された。Kare no konsaato no moyou ga juu-nen no tsukihi (___) tsui ni eizouka sareta.

Full sentence: 彼のコンサートの模様が10年の月日を経て、ついに映像化された。...juu-nen no tsukihi o hete, tsui ni eizouka sareta.
EN: After ten years passed, the footage of his concert was finally made into a video.
HI: उसके कॉन्सर्ट के दृश्य, 10 साल बीतने के बाद, आख़िरकार वीडियो में ढाले गए।
GU: તેના કોન્સર્ટના દ્રશ્યો, 10 વર્ષ વીત્યા પછી, છેવટે વીડિયોમાં બનાવાયા.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を経てo heteafter passing through (10 years)
2を踏まえてo fumaetebased on (wrong — years aren't evidence)
3を境にo sakai niwith... as the dividing line (wrong fit)
4を機にo ki nitaking... as the occasion (wrong fit)

Why: Day 5's を経て — ten years had to elapse before the footage was released.

Q2

容疑者は警官に（　）パトカーに乗った。Yougisha wa keikan ni (___) patokaa ni notta.

Full sentence: 容疑者は警官に促されるままパトカーに乗った。...unagasareru mama patokaa ni notta.
EN: The suspect got into the patrol car, simply as urged by the police officer.
HI: संदिग्ध, पुलिस अफ़सर के कहने पर, बिना विरोध किए गाड़ी में बैठ गया।
GU: શંકાસ્પદ, પોલીસ અધિકારીના કહેવા પર, વિરોધ કર્યા વગર ગાડીમાં બેસી ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1促されながらunagasare nagara(wrong shape for this passive-following meaning)
2促されたままunagasareta mama(wrong tense — needs the plain form まま, not た)
3促されるままunagasareru mamajust as urged, passively
4促されながらもunagasare nagara moeven while being urged (wrong — implies resistance, but he complied)

Why: Week 6 Day 2's Vる（が）まま — following the officer's direction with no resistance at all.

Q3

父親は、子どもを（　）、火事で燃えている家に飛び込んでいった。Chichioya wa, kodomo o (___), kaji de moete iru ie ni tobikonde itta.

Full sentence: 父親は、子どもを助けんがために、火事で燃えている家に飛び込んでいった。...tasuke n ga tame ni, kaji de moete iru ie ni tobikonde itta.
EN: The father jumped into the burning house in order to save his child.
HI: पिता अपने बच्चे को बचाने के इरादे से, आग में जलते घर में कूद पड़ा।
GU: પિતાએ પોતાના બાળકને બચાવવાના ઇરાદાથી, આગમાં સળગતા ઘરમાં કૂદકો માર્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1助けんがためにtasuke n ga tame niin order to save
2助けずともtasukezu tomoeven without saving (wrong sense)
3助けずじまいでtasukezujimai deended up never saving (wrong — he did act)
4助けんがままにtasuke n ga mama ni(not a valid combination)

Why: Day 4's んがために — jumping into a fire is a deliberate, resolved purpose to save the child.

Q4

さあ、最後の練習だ。本番（　）やってみよう。Saa, saigo no renshuu da. Honban (___) yatte miyou.

Full sentence: さあ、最後の練習だ。本番のつもりでやってみよう。...honban no tsumori de yatte miyou.
EN: All right, this is the last practice run. Let's try it as if it were the real thing.
HI: चलो, आख़िरी अभ्यास है। असली प्रदर्शन समझकर कोशिश करते हैं।
GU: ચાલો, છેલ્લી પ્રેક્ટિસ છે. અસલી પ્રદર્શન સમજીને પ્રયત્ન કરીએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1のおりにno ori nion the occasion of (wrong fit)
2ゆえにyue nibecause of (wrong — no cause is stated)
3にもましてni mo mashitemore than (wrong fit)
4のつもりでno tsumori dewith the mindset of (the real thing)

Why: Week 6 Day 2's つもりで — adopting the mindset/assumption of the real performance while still practicing.

Q5

生徒たち（　）、どうしていいかわからないとは言えない。Seito-tachi (___), dou shite ii ka wakaranai to wa ienai.

Full sentence: 生徒たちの手前、どうしていいかわからないとは言えない。Seito-tachi no temae, dou shite ii ka wakaranai to wa ienai.
EN: Given my position in front of my students, I can't say I don't know what to do.
HI: छात्रों के सामने, यह नहीं कह सकता कि मुझे समझ नहीं आ रहा क्या करूँ।
GU: વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સામે, એવું ન કહી શકાય કે મને સમજાતું નથી શું કરવું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を前提としてo zentei toshitewith... as the premise (wrong fit)
2の手前no temaegiven my position in front of
3をよそにo yoso niignoring (wrong fit)
4を経てo heteafter passing through (wrong fit)

Why: Day 3's の手前 — maintaining face in front of the students who look up to the speaker.

Q6

父は、うるさいと言わん（　）テレビの電源を切ってしまった。Chichi wa, urusai to iwan (___) terebi no dengen o kitte shimatta.

Full sentence: 父は、うるさいと言わんばかりにテレビの電源を切ってしまった。...urusai to iwan bakari ni terebi no dengen o kitte shimatta.
EN: My father turned off the TV as if to say "this is too noisy" (without actually saying it).
HI: पिताजी ने मानो "शोर बहुत है" कहते हुए, टीवी बंद कर दिया।
GU: પિતાજીએ જાણે "બહુ ઘોંઘાટ છે" કહેતાં, ટીવી બંધ કરી દીધું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1がためにga tame niin order to (wrong — no deliberate goal, this is body language)
2ばかりにbakari nias if to say
3ながらもnagara moeven though (wrong fit)
4つもりでtsumori dewith the mindset of (wrong fit)

Why: Day 4's とばかりに (here as 言わんばかりに) — silently but clearly conveying "too noisy" through action.

Q7

その事件（　）、やっと古い法律が改正された。Sono jiken (___), yatto furui houritsu ga kaisei sareta.

Full sentence: その事件を機に、やっと古い法律が改正された。Sono jiken o ki ni, yatto furui houritsu ga kaisei sareta.
EN: Taking that incident as the trigger, the old law was finally revised.
HI: उस घटना को मौक़ा बनाकर, आख़िरकार पुराना क़ानून बदला गया।
GU: તે ઘટનાને તક બનાવીને, છેવટે જૂનો કાયદો બદલવામાં આવ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を前提としてo zentei toshitewith... as the premise (wrong fit)
2の折にno ori nion the occasion of (too weak — no trigger sense)
3を機にo ki nitaking... as the trigger
4ゆえにyue nibecause of (grammatically odd with 事件 here)

Why: Day 6's を機に — the incident is the deliberate prompt for finally revising the law.

Q8

今にも（　）桜のつぼみがふくらんでいる。Ima ni mo (___) sakura no tsubomi ga fukurande iru.

Full sentence: 今にも咲き出さんばかりに桜のつぼみがふくらんでいる。...sakidasan bakari ni sakura no tsubomi ga fukurande iru.
EN: The cherry blossom buds are swelling, as if about to bloom at any moment.
HI: मानो अभी खिलने ही वाली हों, सकुरा की कलियाँ फूल रही हैं।
GU: જાણે હમણાં જ ખીલવાની હોય, સાકુરાની કળીઓ ફૂલી રહી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1咲き出しながらにsakidashi nagara ni(not a natural fit)
2咲き出さずともsakidasazu tomoeven without blooming (wrong sense)
3咲き出すつもりでsakidasu tsumori dewith the intention of blooming (wrong — flowers have no intention)
4咲き出さんばかりにsakidasan bakari nias if about to bloom

Why: Day 4's んばかりに — the buds are vividly on the verge of blooming, though not blooming yet.

Q9

私の家は裕福ではないけれど、まさに「（　）楽しい我が家」です。Watashi no ie wa yuufuku de wa nai keredo, masani "(___) tanoshii wagaya" desu.

Full sentence: 私の家は裕福ではないけれど、まさに「貧しいながらも楽しい我が家」です。...mazushii nagara mo tanoshii wagaya" desu.
EN: My family isn't wealthy, but it's truly "a poor yet happy home."
HI: मेरा घर अमीर तो नहीं है, लेकिन सचमुच "ग़रीब पर ख़ुशहाल घर" है।
GU: મારું ઘર શ્રીમંત નથી, પણ ખરેખર "ગરીબ પણ ખુશહાલ ઘર" છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1貧しいままでmazushii mama deremaining poor (wrong — no concession)
2貧しいながらもmazushii nagara mopoor, but nonetheless
3貧しいつもりでmazushii tsumori de(wrong — つもり doesn't attach to a factual state like this)
4貧しいがためにmazushii ga tame nibecause of being poor (wrong — no purpose/cause reading fits)

Why: Week 6 Day 3's ながらも — admitting poverty while asserting happiness regardless.

Q10

あなたに出会った日（　）、私の人生がばら色に変わりました。Anata ni deatta hi (___), watashi no jinsei ga barairo ni kawarimashita.

Full sentence: あなたに出会った日を境にして、私の人生がばら色に変わりました。...hi o sakai ni shite, watashi no jinsei ga barairo ni kawarimashita.
EN: Since the day I met you, my life has turned rosy.
HI: जिस दिन से तुमसे मिला/मिली, उस दिन से मेरी ज़िंदगी ख़ुशहाल हो गई।
GU: જે દિવસથી તને મળ્યો/મળી, તે દિવસથી મારું જીવન સુખી બની ગયું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を踏まえてo fumaetebased on (wrong fit)
2にひきかえてni hikikaetein contrast to (wrong — no contrast implied)
3を境にしてo sakai ni shitewith... as the dividing line
4に先駆けてni sakigaketeahead of (wrong fit)

Why: Day 6's を境にして — that day marks a clean before/after divide in the speaker's life.

Q11

天気がいいので運動（　）ハイキングに行きました。Tenki ga ii node undou (___) haikingu ni ikimashita.

Full sentence: 天気がいいので運動をかねてハイキングに行きました。...undou o kanete haikingu ni ikimashita.
EN: Since the weather was nice, I went hiking, combining it with exercise.
HI: मौसम अच्छा था, इसलिए व्यायाम को भी मिलाकर हाइकिंग पर चला/चली गया/गई।
GU: હવામાન સારું હતું, એટલે કસરતને પણ ભેગું કરીને હાઇકિંગ પર ગયો/ગઈ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を経てo heteafter passing through (wrong fit)
2をよそにo yoso niignoring (wrong fit)
3をかねてo kanetecombining with (exercise)
4もさることながらmo saru koto nagaranot to mention (wrong fit)

Why: Day 5's を兼ねて — the hike serves both exercise and leisure at once.

Q12

貧困（　）教育が受けられない子どもたちを支援しよう。Hinkon (___) kyouiku ga ukerarenai kodomo-tachi o shien shiyou.

Full sentence: 貧困ゆえに教育が受けられない子どもたちを支援しよう。Hinkon yue ni kyouiku ga ukerarenai kodomo-tachi o shien shiyou.
EN: Let's support children who can't receive an education because of poverty.
HI: ग़रीबी की वजह से शिक्षा न पा सकने वाले बच्चों की मदद करें।
GU: ગરીબીના કારણે શિક્ષણ ન મેળવી શકતાં બાળકોને મદદ કરીએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ゆえにyue nibecause of (poverty)
2ままにmama nijust as (wrong fit)
3ながらにnagara ni(wrong — no unchanged state being described)
4ばかりにbakari nias if about to (wrong fit)

Why: Day 4's ゆえに — poverty is stated as the formal cause for missing an education.

Q13

時代（　）制度の改革が求められている。Jidai (___) seido no kaikaku ga motomerarete iru.

Full sentence: 時代に即した制度の改革が求められている。Jidai ni sokushita seido no kaikaku ga motomerarete iru.
EN: Reform of the system in keeping with the times is called for.
HI: समय के अनुरूप व्यवस्था में सुधार की माँग की जा रही है।
GU: સમય અનુસાર વ્યવસ્થામાં સુધારાની માંગ થઈ રહી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を境にしたo sakai ni shita(wrong fit)
2を経るo heru(wrong fit)
3においたni oita(not a valid pattern here)
4に即したni sokushitain keeping with (the times)

Why: Week 6 Day 1's Nに即した — reform must fit exactly with the current era.

Q14

日々の散歩の折に、（　）。Hibi no sanpo no ori ni, (___).

Full sentence: 日々の散歩の折に、季節の移り変わりを楽しんでいます。...kisetsu no utsurikawari o tanoshinde imasu.
EN: On my daily walks, I enjoy the changing of the seasons.
HI: रोज़ की सैर के दौरान, मौसम के बदलाव का आनंद लेता/लेती हूँ।
GU: રોજની ફરવાની ટેવ દરમિયાન, ઋતુઓના બદલાવનો આનંદ માણું છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1季節の移り変わりを楽しんでいますkisetsu no utsurikawari o tanoshinde imasuenjoying the changing seasons
2ついでに郵便局に寄っていますtsuide ni yuubinkyoku ni yotte imasustopping by the post office along the way (plausible, but less natural as the "折に" headline continuation)
3隣人との挨拶が苦手ですrinjin to no aisatsu ga nigate desubad at greeting neighbors (a general trait, not tied to the walk itself)
4犬にほえられながらも歩いていますinu ni hoerare nagara mo aruite imasuwalking despite being barked at by dogs (an odd, negative image for a gentle 折に opener)

Why: Day 6's の折に sets a gentle, polite tone — enjoying the seasons is the most natural, positive continuation of a description of daily walks.

Q15

主人は、私が腹痛で苦しんでいるのをよそに、（　）。Shujin wa, watashi ga fukutsuu de kurushinde iru no o yoso ni, (___).

Full sentence: 主人は、私が腹痛で苦しんでいるのをよそに、ぐうぐういびきをかいて寝ている。...guuguu ibiki o kaite nete iru.
EN: My husband, ignoring the fact that I'm in agony with a stomachache, is fast asleep snoring away.
HI: मेरे पति, मेरे पेट दर्द से तड़पने की परवाह किए बिना, ख़र्राटे भरते हुए सो रहे हैं।
GU: મારા પતિ, મારા પેટના દુખાવાથી તડપવાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, નસકોરાં બોલાવતાં ઊંઘી રહ્યા છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1心配そうに私の顔をのぞいているshinpaisou ni watashi no kao o nozoite irupeering at my face with worry (wrong — contradicts をよそに)
2病院に連れて行こうと言い出せないbyouin ni tsurete ikou to iidasenaican't bring himself to suggest the hospital (shows some concern, weaker fit)
3家にある薬を探し回っているie ni aru kusuri o sagashimawatte irusearching the house for medicine (shows concern, weaker fit)
4ぐうぐういびきをかいて寝ているguuguu ibiki o kaite nete irufast asleep, snoring away

Why: Week 6 Day 5's をよそに means total disregard — only sleeping through her pain (option 4) is genuinely indifferent; the others all show some degree of care.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 評判はなかなかいい。___ ★ ___ hyouban wa nakanaka ii.

Tiles: 1 予備校の有名講師の授業が受けられる (yobikou no yuumei koushi no jugyou ga ukerareru)　2 自宅に居ながら (jitaku ni inagara)　3 そのシステムの (sono shisutemu no)　4 にして (ni shite)

2 自宅に居ながら→4 にして→★ 1 予備校の有名講師の授業が受けられる→3 そのシステムの

自宅に居ながらにして、予備校の有名講師の授業が受けられる、そのシステムの評判はなかなかいい。Jitaku ni inagara ni shite, yobikou no yuumei koushi no jugyou ga ukerareru, sono shisutemu no hyouban wa nakanaka ii.

EN: The system that lets you take classes from famous cram-school instructors while staying right at home has quite a good reputation.
HI: घर पर रहते हुए भी प्रसिद्ध कोचिंग शिक्षकों की क्लास ले सकने वाले उस सिस्टम की काफ़ी अच्छी प्रतिष्ठा है।
GU: ઘરે રહીને પણ પ્રખ્યાત કોચિંગ શિક્ષકોના ક્લાસ લઈ શકાય તેવી તે સિસ્ટમની ખૂબ સારી પ્રતિષ્ઠા છે.

Why: Built on Week 6 Day 3's ながら(に)して — "while remaining at home" — describing a service accessible without leaving the house.

Q17

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 季節が素晴らしい。___ ★ ___ kisetsu ga subarashii.

Tiles: 1 それにもまして (sore ni mo mashite)　2 紅葉の (kouyou no)　3 京都もいいが (Kyouto mo ii ga)　4 春の (haru no)

4 春の→3 京都もいいが→★ 1 それにもまして→2 紅葉の

春の京都もいいが、それにもまして、紅葉の季節が素晴らしい。Haru no Kyouto mo ii ga, sore ni mo mashite, kouyou no kisetsu ga subarashii.

EN: Kyoto in spring is nice too, but even more than that, the autumn-foliage season is wonderful.
HI: बसंत का क्योतो भी अच्छा है, लेकिन उससे भी बढ़कर, पतझड़ के रंगों का मौसम शानदार है।
GU: વસંતનું ક્યોટો પણ સારું છે, પણ તેના કરતાં પણ વધારે, પાનખરના રંગોની ઋતુ અદ્ભુત છે.

Why: Built on Week 6 Day 1's Nにもまして — the autumn season is praised even more than the already-good spring.

Q18

昨日が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 暖かかった。Kinou ga ___ ★ ___ atatakakatta.

Tiles: 1 コートもいらないくらい (kooto mo iranai kurai)　2 のにひきかえ (no ni hikikae)　3 今日は (kyou wa)　4 この冬一番の寒さだった (kono fuyu ichiban no samusa datta)
