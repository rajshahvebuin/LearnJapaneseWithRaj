5日目 — 趣味と実益を兼ねて (Shumi to jitsueki o kanete)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.102–103 (PDF pages 104–105)
Grammar points covered
彼女をおいてほかにない親の心配をよそに20年の時を経て趣味と実益を兼ねて
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.105 (6日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
None of today's four points are tagged 硬 — all are usable in ordinary written/spoken Japanese. Points 3 and 4 (を経て／を兼ねて) both attach directly to a noun with を, and both describe "passing through/combining with X," which makes them an easy pair to confuse — see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 彼女をおいてほかにない (Kanojo o oite hoka ni nai)

neutral register
ReadingN + をおいて（ほかに）+ V-ない
Meaning (EN)"other than N, there's no ~ / no one but N can ~" — singles out N as the one exception to an otherwise negative statement
Meaning (HI)...के अलावा कोई और नहीं / ...को छोड़कर कोई नहीं
Meaning (GU)...સિવાય બીજું કોઈ નહીં / ...ને છોડીને કોઈ નહીં
Connection (接続)N + をおいて（ほかに）+ V-ない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useStrongly praises/singles out N as uniquely qualified or irreplaceable for something.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をおいて（ほかに）+ V-ない彼女をおいてほかに（できる人はいない）kanojo o oite hoka ni
NounN + をおいて（ほかに）+ V-ないA大学をおいてほかに（考えられない）Ee daigaku o oite hoka ni
V／A／な(added, not in book; not a natural fit — をおいて singles out a noun, not an action or state)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女をおいてほかにその仕事ができる人はいない。Kanojo o oite hoka ni sono shigoto ga dekiru hito wa inai. She is the only one who can do the job. उसके अलावा वह काम करने वाला कोई और नहीं है। તેના સિવાય તે કામ કરી શકે તેવું બીજું કોઈ નથી.
進学するなら、A大学をおいてほかに考えられない。Shingaku suru nara, Ee daigaku o oite hoka ni kangaerarenai. If I go on to further study, University A is the only one I would consider. आगे पढ़ाई करनी है तो, A यूनिवर्सिटी के अलावा कुछ सोचा ही नहीं जा सकता। આગળ ભણવું હોય તો, A યુનિવર્સિટી સિવાય બીજું કંઈ વિચારી જ ન શકાય.

をおいて almost always pairs with a negative predicate (いない, 考えられない, ない) — think of it as "set N aside, and there's nothing/no one left." This makes N sound irreplaceable.

【2】 親の心配をよそに (Oya no shinpai o yoso ni)

neutral register
ReadingN + をよそに
Meaning (EN)"ignoring N / indifferent to N" — proceeds with an action while disregarding someone's worry, opposition, or expectation
Meaning (HI)...की परवाह किए बिना / ...को नज़रअंदाज़ करते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ની પરવા કર્યા વગર / ...ને અવગણીને
Connection (接続)N + をよそに
RegisterNeutral
Typical useN is almost always someone's emotional state (worry, opposition, unease, expectation) that the subject proceeds without regard for.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をよそに親の心配をよそにoya no shinpai o yoso ni
Noun (clause + の)[文] + のをよそに不景気なのをよそにfukeiki na no o yoso ni
Noun (added)(added, not in book) 住民の不安をよそにjuumin no fuan o yoso niignoring residents' unease
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
親の心配をよそに、彼女は一人旅に出かけた。Oya no shinpai o yoso ni, kanojo wa hitori-tabi ni dekaketa. She went on a trip alone, not caring how worried her parents were. माता-पिता की चिंता की परवाह किए बिना, वह अकेले यात्रा पर निकल गई। માતાપિતાની ચિંતાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, તે એકલી પ્રવાસે નીકળી ગઈ.
外食産業が不景気なのをよそに、そのレストランはいつもにぎわっている。Gaishoku sangyou ga fukeiki na no o yoso ni, sono resutoran wa itsumo nigiwatte iru. The restaurant industry is always packed there, despite the slump in the dining-out sector. बाहर खाने के कारोबार में मंदी होने के बावजूद, वह रेस्तराँ हमेशा गुलज़ार रहता है। બહાર જમવાના ધંધામાં મંદી હોવા છતાં, તે રેસ્ટોરન્ટ હંમેશા ધમધમતું રહે છે.

Common collocations: 親の反対をよそに, 住民の不安をよそに, 周囲の期待をよそに, 反対の声をよそに, ○○が苦しんでいるのをよそに. In every case, someone's feeling exists and is simply not being taken into account.

【3】 20年の時を経て (Nijuu-nen no toki o hete)

neutral register
ReadingN + を経て
Meaning (EN)"after passing through/experiencing N" — something happens only after N (a period of time, a place, a process, an experience) has elapsed or been gone through
Meaning (HI)...से गुज़रकर / ...के बाद (एक प्रक्रिया या समय बीतने के बाद)
Meaning (GU)...પસાર થઈને / ...પછી (એક પ્રક્રિયા કે સમય વીત્યા પછી)
Connection (接続)N（時、場所、過程、経験など）+ を経て
RegisterNeutral
Typical useMarks a necessary interval, process, or experience that had to elapse before the main event could occur.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)N（時）+ を経て20年の時を経てnijuu-nen no toki o hete
Noun (experience)N（経験）+ を経てオリンピックを経てorinpikku o hete
Noun (process)N（過程）+ を経て複雑な手続きを経てfukuzatsu na tetsuzuki o hete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
20年の時を経て、伝説のバンドが復活する。Nijuu-nen no toki o hete, densetsu no bando ga fukkatsu suru. The legendary music group is coming back after twenty years' absence. 20 साल के अंतराल के बाद, वह मशहूर बैंड फिर से लौट रहा है। 20 વર્ષના ગાળા પછી, તે પ્રખ્યાત બેન્ડ ફરી પાછું આવી રહ્યું છે.
その選手は、オリンピックを経て、急激に力をつけてきた。Sono senshu wa, orinpikku o hete, kyuugeki ni chikara o tsukete kita. This athlete has improved rapidly after competing in the Olympics. वह खिलाड़ी, ओलंपिक से गुज़रने के बाद, तेज़ी से मज़बूत हो गया है। તે ખેલાડી, ઓલિમ્પિકમાંથી પસાર થયા પછી, ઝડપથી મજબૂત બની ગયો છે.
複雑な手続きを経て、証明書が発行された。Fukuzatsu na tetsuzuki o hete, shoumeisho ga hakkou sareta. A certificate was issued following a very complicated process. जटिल प्रक्रिया से गुज़रने के बाद, प्रमाणपत्र जारी किया गया। જટિલ પ્રક્રિયામાંથી પસાર થયા પછી, પ્રમાણપત્ર જારી કરવામાં આવ્યું.

経る (へる) literally means "to pass through/elapse" — を経て is used for time, places, processes, or experiences that had to occur/elapse first. It always implies the main event happens AFTER and BECAUSE OF that interval passing.

【4】 趣味と実益を兼ねて (Shumi to jitsueki o kanete)

neutral register
Reading（N'と）N + を兼ねて
Meaning (EN)"serving both as N' and N / combining N with something else" — one action or thing fulfills two purposes at once
Meaning (HI)...और...दोनों के मक़सद से (एक ही काम से दो उद्देश्य पूरे करते हुए)
Meaning (GU)...અને...બંનેના હેતુથી (એક જ કામથી બે ઉદ્દેશ પૂરા કરીને)
Connection (接続)（N'と）N + を兼ねて ; N + も兼ねて
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribes doing one thing that conveniently serves a second purpose too — combining hobby with profit, travel with study, etc.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (N'と N を兼ねて)趣味と実益を兼ねて趣味と実益を兼ねてイラストを描くshumi to jitsueki o kanete irasuto o egaku
Noun (N も兼ねて)英語の勉強も兼ねて英語の勉強も兼ねて旅行するeigo no benkyou mo kanete ryokou suru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は趣味と実益を兼ねてイラストを描いています。Watashi wa shumi to jitsueki o kanete irasuto o egaite imasu. I draw illustrations, combining hobby with practical profit. मैं शौक़ और फ़ायदे दोनों को मिलाकर चित्र बनाता/बनाती हूँ। હું શોખ અને ફાયદો બંનેને ભેગું કરીને ચિત્રો દોરું છું.
英語の勉強も兼ねて、1ヵ月ほどアメリカを旅行してきます。Eigo no benkyou mo kanete, ikkagetsu hodo Amerika o ryokou shite kimasu. I will travel around the USA for about a month, combining it with studying English. अंग्रेज़ी की पढ़ाई को भी शामिल करते हुए, लगभग एक महीने अमेरिका घूमकर आऊँगा/आऊँगी। અંગ્રેજીના અભ્યાસને પણ ભેગું કરીને, લગભગ એક મહિનો અમેરિકા ફરીને આવીશ.

兼ねる (かねる) means "to combine/serve double duty as." 趣味と実益を兼ねて is a common set phrase meaning "combining hobby and profit" — the activity is enjoyable AND useful at the same time.

Q1

この仕事（　）、彼にふさわしい仕事はほかにないでしょう。Kono shigoto (___), kare ni fusawashii shigoto wa hoka ni nai deshou.

Full sentence: この仕事をおいて、彼にふさわしい仕事はほかにないでしょう。Kono shigoto o oite, kare ni fusawashii shigoto wa hoka ni nai deshou.
EN: There's probably no job other than this one that suits him so well.
HI: इस काम के अलावा, उसके लिए इतना मुफ़ीद कोई और काम नहीं होगा।
GU: આ કામ સિવાય, તેને આટલું અનુકૂળ બીજું કોઈ કામ નહીં હોય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aをおいてo oiteother than (this job)इसके अलावाઆ સિવાય

GU: પોતાની જ ભૂલોને અવગણીને, તે માત્ર બીજાની ટીકા કરતી રહે છે.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

その企業は不景気＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、売り上げを伸ばしている。Sono kigyou wa fukeiki ___, uriage o nobashite iru.

Tiles: 1 をよそに (o yoso ni)　2 による (ni yoru)　3 業績の低迷 (gyouseki no teimei)　4 他社の (tasha no)

2 による→4 他社の→3 業績の低迷→1 をよそに

その企業は不景気による他社の業績の低迷をよそに、売り上げを伸ばしている。Sono kigyou wa fukeiki ni yoru tasha no gyouseki no teimei o yoso ni, uriage o nobashite iru.

EN: That company is growing its sales, unaffected by other companies' sales slump caused by the recession.
HI: वह कंपनी, मंदी की वजह से दूसरी कंपनियों की गिरती बिक्री की परवाह किए बिना, अपनी बिक्री बढ़ा रही है।
GU: તે કંપની, મંદીના કારણે બીજી કંપનીઓના ઘટતા વેચાણની પરવા કર્યા વગર, પોતાનું વેચાણ વધારી રહી છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's をよそに — the company's growth continues regardless of the industry-wide slump affecting others.

Q7

今年の年賀状は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 、例年より多い数になった。Kotoshi no nengajou wa, ___, reinen yori ooi kazu ni natta.

Tiles: 1 報告も (houkoku mo)　2 ゆえに (yue ni)　3 兼ねていた (kanete ita)　4 引っ越しの (hikkoshi no)

今年の年賀状は、引っ越しの報告も兼ねていたゆえに、例年より多い数になった。Kotoshi no nengajou wa, hikkoshi no houkoku mo kanete ita yue ni, reinen yori ooi kazu ni natta.

EN: This year's New Year's cards, because they also served to announce our move, ended up being more numerous than usual.
HI: इस साल के नववर्ष कार्ड, चूँकि वे घर बदलने की सूचना भी दे रहे थे, इसलिए हर साल से ज़्यादा संख्या में हो गए।
GU: આ વર્ષના નવવર્ષના કાર્ડ, કારણ કે તે ઘર બદલવાની જાણ પણ કરતા હતા, એટલે દર વર્ષ કરતાં વધુ સંખ્યામાં થયા.

Why: Built on Point 4's も兼ねて (serving a double purpose) plus Week 6 Day 4's ゆえに (formal cause) — the cards did double duty, so more were sent than usual.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points attach to a noun with を, which makes them easy to mix up at a glance — but each answers a different question:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nをおいて（ほかに）N o oite (hoka ni) No one/nothing but N ~ N + をおいて（ほかに）+ V-ない Singles N out as the sole exception to a negative statement. "No one but her can do this job."
NをよそにN o yoso ni Ignoring/despite N N + をよそに Proceeds while disregarding someone's feeling (worry, opposition, expectation). "She traveled alone, ignoring her parents' worry."
Nを経てN o hete After passing through N N（時／場所／過程／経験）+ を経て A required interval, process, or experience the main event happens AFTER. "After 20 years, the band reunites."
Nを兼ねてN o kanete Combining with N / serving double duty as N （N'と）N + を兼ねて Two purposes fulfilled AT ONCE by the same action — not sequential, not ignored. "Drawing as both hobby and income."
Exam trap: を経て and を兼ねて are the pair N1 most likes to test together, since both attach directly to a noun with を and both can loosely translate as "through X." The test: does the noun describe something that happened BEFORE and led up to the main event in sequence (→ を経て — "経験, 過程, 時"), or something happening AT THE SAME TIME serving two purposes together (→ を兼ねて — "趣味と実益," "観光")? Quiz Q3 (観光をかねて) vs Q5 (結婚、出産を経て) tests exactly this contrast.
