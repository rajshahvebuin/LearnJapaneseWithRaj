1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜つもりで／つもりだ

neutral register
Reading~tsumori de / tsumori da
Meaning (EN)"with the (mistaken) intention/feeling of ~" — describes acting under an assumption/self-image that doesn't match reality
Meaning (HI)...(ग़लत) समझकर/मानकर (असलियत से अलग एक धारणा के साथ)
Meaning (GU)...(ખોટું) સમજીને/માનીને (વાસ્તવિકતાથી અલગ એક ધારણા સાથે)
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた／Aい／naな／Nの + つもりで／つもりだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆「事実は違う」という意味 (means "the fact is different [from this feeling/assumption]").
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
冗談のつもりで言ったのに、本気にされてしまった。Joudan no tsumori de itta noni, honki ni sarete shimatta. I was only joking but what I said was taken very seriously. मज़ाक़ समझकर कहा था, लेकिन गंभीरता से ले लिया गया। મજાક સમજીને કહ્યું હતું, પણ ગંભીરતાથી લેવાયું.
カラオケでは、いつも歌手になったつもりで歌っている。Karaoke de wa, itsumo kashu ni natta tsumori de utatte iru. I feel like a professional singer whenever I sing at a karaoke bar. कराओके में, हमेशा गायक बन जाने के अंदाज़ में गाता हूँ। કરાઓકેમાં, હંમેશા ગાયક બની ગયાના અંદાજમાં ગાઉં છું.
自分では若いつもりだが、年には勝てません。Jibun de wa wakai tsumori da ga, toshi ni wa katemasen. I think I'm young at heart but I really can't fight my age. ख़ुद को जवान समझता हूँ, लेकिन उम्र से जीत नहीं पाता। પોતાને યુવાન સમજું છું, પણ ઉંમર સામે જીતી શકતો નથી.

Every example here has a hidden "but actually..." — 冗談のつもり (was only joking) vs. it being taken seriously; 若いつもり (feels young) vs. actual age catching up. This gap between self-image and reality is the whole point.

【2】 Vる（が）まま（に）

neutral register
ReadingV ru (ga) mama (ni)
Meaning (EN)"as (someone else) directs / just as told" — acting entirely passively, following another's will without one's own input
Meaning (HI)जैसा कहा जाए वैसे ही (दूसरे की मर्ज़ी के मुताबिक, बिना अपनी राय के)
Meaning (GU)જેમ કહેવાય તેમ જ (બીજાની ઇચ્છા મુજબ, પોતાનો અભિપ્રાય આપ્યા વગર)
Connection (接続)Vる（が）まま（に）
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆「Vられるが（まま）」はほかの人の意志のとおりという意味 (the passive-form Vられるが(まま) means "exactly as another person's will dictates").
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
契約のとき、言われるままにハンコを押している人が多い。Keiyaku no toki, iwareru mama ni hanko o oshite iru hito ga ooi. Lots of people put their seals on contracts without reviewing them well. अनुबंध करते समय, कहे जाने पर ही मुहर लगा देने वाले लोग बहुत हैं। કરાર કરતી વખતે, કહેવાય એટલું જ સ્ટેમ્પ મારી દેનારા લોકો ઘણા છે.
ゆうべは同僚に誘われるままに飲みに行った。Yuube wa douryou ni sasowareru mama ni nomi ni itta. Persuaded by my colleagues, I went drinking with them. कल रात सहकर्मी के बुलाने पर, बिना मना किए पीने चला गया। ગઈકાલે રાત્રે સહકર્મીના બોલાવવા પર, ના પાડ્યા વગર પીવા ગયો.
あなたが思うまま、話してください。Anata ga omou mama, hanashite kudasai. Please say what you think. जो सोचते हैं वही बोल दें। જે વિચારો છો તે જ કહી દો.

言われるまま (as told), 誘われるまま (as invited) — both use the passive form to emphasize the subject had no active role; 思うまま (as one thinks) is the one exception where the subject's OWN will is being followed without restraint.

【3】 Vずとも

硬 formal/written
ReadingV zutomo
Meaning (EN)"even without doing ~ / even if one doesn't ~" — a formal way of saying "even without V"
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना भी (एक औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર પણ (એક ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)Vない（stem） + ずとも　❶ しない→せず
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useStating that something is self-evident, even without being explicitly stated.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼があなたのことを好きだということは、言わずともわかる。Kare ga anata no koto o suki da to iu koto wa, iwazu tomo wakaru. It is obvious that he is in love with you. वह तुमसे प्यार करता है, यह बिना कहे भी पता चल जाता है। તે તારા પર પ્રેમ કરે છે, એ કહ્યા વગર પણ ખબર પડી જાય છે.

ず is the classical negative form of ない (irregular: しない becomes せず, not しず) — ずとも then adds a concessive "even if." This is the same ず that appears in the fixed idiom 良かれと思ってやったことが良かれ悪しかれ, so getting comfortable with it pays off elsewhere too.

【4】 Vずじまい

neutral register
ReadingV zujimai
Meaning (EN)"ended up never doing ~" — describes an intended action that never got done, with regret
Meaning (HI)आख़िर तक...नहीं कर पाया/पाई (अफ़सोस के साथ)
Meaning (GU)છેવટ સુધી...ન કરી શક્યો/શકી (અફસોસ સાથે)
Connection (接続)Vない（stem） + ずじまい　❶ しない→せず
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆残念な気持ち (a feeling of regret) — the action was planned or hoped for, but circumstances got in the way.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
英語の本をたくさん買ったが、ほとんど読まずじまいだ。Eigo no hon o takusan katta ga, hotondo yomazujimai da. I bought many English books but I haven't touched most of them. अंग्रेज़ी की ढेर सारी किताबें ख़रीदी, लेकिन ज़्यादातर पढ़ी ही नहीं गईं। અંગ્રેજીના ઘણાં પુસ્તકો ખરીદ્યાં, પણ મોટા ભાગનાં વાંચ્યાં જ નહીં.
5着も試着したが、どれも気に入らず、買わずじまいで店を出た。Go-chaku mo shichaku shita ga, dore mo ki ni irazu, kawazujimai de mise o deta. I came out of the shop without buying anything because, even though I tried on five different dresses, nothing pleased me. पाँच कपड़े पहन कर देखे, लेकिन कोई पसंद नहीं आया, और बिना ख़रीदे ही दुकान से निकल गया/गई। પાંચ કપડાં પહેરીને જોયાં, પણ કોઈ ગમ્યું નહીં, અને ખરીદ્યા વગર જ દુકાનમાંથી નીકળી ગયો/ગઈ.

Notes (れい): 会わずじまい (never got to meet), 行かずじまい (never got to go), せずじまい (never got to do it), 買えずじまい (never got to buy it — note this variant uses the potential-negative stem 買え, not plain 買わ, showing the pattern is flexible about which negative stem it attaches to).

Q1

忙しかったので、あとでお昼を食べようと思っていたが、結局、食べず（　）で、夕食の時間になった。Isogashikatta node, ato de ohiru o tabeyou to omotte ita ga, kekkyoku, tabezu (___) de, yuushoku no jikan ni natta.

Full sentence: 忙しかったので、あとでお昼を食べようと思っていたが、結局、食べずじまいで、夕食の時間になった。...tabezujimai de, yuushoku no jikan ni natta.
EN: I was busy, so I thought I'd have lunch later, but in the end I never got around to eating it, and dinner time arrived.
HI: व्यस्त था/थी, तो सोचा बाद में लंच कर लूँगा/लूँगी, लेकिन आख़िर तक खा ही नहीं पाया/पाई, और डिनर का समय आ गया।
GU: વ્યસ્ત હતો/હતી, તો વિચાર્યું પછી લંચ કરી લઈશ, પણ છેવટ સુધી ખાઈ જ ન શક્યો/શકી, અને ડિનરનો સમય આવી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aままmama(wrong — this needs じまい's "ended up never doing" flavor, not まま's passive-following sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bじまいjimaiended up never doingआख़िर तक नहीं कर पायाછેવટ સુધી ન કરી શક્યો

Q2

感想を思う（　）アンケートに書いてください。Kansou o omou (___) ankeeto ni kaite kudasai.

Full sentence: 感想を思うままにアンケートに書いてください。Kansou o omou mama ni ankeeto ni kaite kudasai.
EN: Please write your impressions in the questionnaire just as you think them.
HI: जो सोचते हैं, वही सर्वेक्षण में लिख दें।
GU: જે વિચારો છો, તે જ સર્વેમાં લખી દો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aままにmama nijust as (one thinks)जैसा सोचते हैं वैसे हीજેમ વિચારો તેમ જ
bつもりでtsumori dewith the intention of (wrong — no mistaken self-image here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: તે જોઈ ન શકતા હોવા છતાં, એવો ઉત્તમ પ્રદર્શન કર્યો કે દર્શકો ભાવુક થઈ ગયા.

Q6

検査の結果を医師にもう少し詳しく＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 診察室を後にした。Kensa no kekka o ishi ni mou sukoshi kuwashiku ___ shinsatsushitsu o ato ni shita.

Tiles: 1 早く帰るつもりで (hayaku kaeru tsumori de)　2 いたのに (ita noni)　3 誘われるまま (sasowareru mama)　4 今日こそは (kyou koso wa)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Four patterns about intention, passivity, and unfinished action — the difference is whose will is in play, and whether the action happened at all:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜つもりで／つもりだ~tsumori de/da With the (mistaken) feeling of ~ Vる／Vた／Aい／naな／Nの + つもりで／つもりだ A gap between self-image/intention and actual reality. "Meant it as a joke, but it wasn't taken that way."
Vる（が）まま（に）~ru (ga) mama (ni) Just as (someone) directs Vる（が）まま（に） Fully passive — following another's (or one's own) will exactly, no resistance. "Signed the contract without reviewing it, as told."
Vずとも~zutomo Even without ~ [硬] Vない（stem） + ずとも A concessive — the action happens/is true despite a missing condition. "Obvious even without being said."
Vずじまい~zujimai Ended up never doing ~ Vない（stem） + ずじまい An intended action that never happened at all — regret. "Bought books but never read them."
Exam trap: ずとも and ずじまい share the ず-stem but point in opposite directions: ずとも says something IS true/happens even without a condition (a positive outcome despite absence), while ずじまい says something did NOT happen at all, with regret (a negative outcome from absence). Check whether the sentence ends positively (an achievement despite lacking something) or negatively (a missed opportunity).
