Grammar points covered

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.65 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This week's title itself ("努力なくして合格はない") uses today's very first pattern. Point 1 is tagged 硬 formal/written; the other three are neutral. ものを (Point 4) is a distinctively regretful, almost sighing form — keep its emotional flavor in mind.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nなくして（は）…ない

硬 formal/written
ReadingN nakushite (wa)...nai
Meaning (EN)"without ~, there is no ~" — asserts that something is an absolutely essential precondition
Meaning (HI)...के बिना, ...संभव ही नहीं (एक अनिवार्य शर्त)
Meaning (GU)...વગર, ...શક્ય જ નથી (એક અનિવાર્ય શરત)
Connection (接続)N + なくして（は）…ない　れい Vることなくして〜
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useAsserting a strong, almost moralistic necessity — this week's own title uses this exact pattern.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
努力なくして（は）成功はありえない。Doryoku nakushite (wa) seikou wa arienai. Success cannot be obtained without effort. मेहनत के बिना सफलता मिलना नामुमकिन है। મહેનત વગર સફળતા મળવી અશક્ય છે.
二国間の相互理解なくして両国の友好関係を保つことはできない。Nikoku-kan no sougo rikai nakushite ryoukoku no yuukou kankei o tamotsu koto wa dekinai. Mutual understanding is essential to the friendship between two countries. दो देशों के बीच आपसी समझ के बिना, दोनों देशों के दोस्ताना रिश्ते बरक़रार नहीं रखे जा सकते। બે દેશો વચ્ચે પરસ્પર સમજણ વગર, બંને દેશોના મૈત્રીપૂર્ણ સંબંધો જાળવી શકાય નહીં.

This week's title, 努力なくして合格はない ("there's no passing without effort"), is built directly on this pattern — a fitting reminder for JLPT study itself.

【2】 Nなしに（は）…ない／Nなしでは…ない

neutral register
ReadingN nashi ni (wa)...nai / N nashi de wa...nai
Meaning (EN)"without ~, cannot ~" — states something can't happen/be done without a required condition
Meaning (HI)...के बिना, ...नहीं हो सकता
Meaning (GU)...વગર, ...થઈ શકતું નથી
Connection (接続)N + なしに（は）…ない; N + なしでは…ない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA softer, everyday version of Point 1 — still essential, but without the moralistic weight.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生の許可なしに、それは使えません。Sensei no kyoka nashi ni, sore wa tsukaemasen. You cannot use it without the teacher's permission. शिक्षक की अनुमति के बिना, उसे इस्तेमाल नहीं किया जा सकता। શિક્ષકની પરવાનગી વગર, તેનો ઉપયોગ કરી શકાય નહીં.
あなたなしでは生きていけない。Anata nashi de wa ikite ikenai. I cannot live without you. तुम्हारे बिना जीना मुमकिन नहीं है। તારા વગર જીવવું શક્ય નથી.
みなさんの協力なしには、できません。Minasan no kyouryoku nashi ni wa, dekimasen. We cannot do it without your support. आप सभी के सहयोग के बिना, यह नहीं हो सकता। તમારા બધાના સહયોગ વગર, આ થઈ શકે નહીં.

なしに and なしでは are largely interchangeable — なしでは pairs especially naturally with a following いけない／生きていけない type of clause.

【3】 Vるともなく V

neutral register
ReadingV ru to mo naku V
Meaning (EN)"doing ~ without any particular intention to" — describes an action done half-mindedly/absentmindedly, without clear purpose
Meaning (HI)बिना किसी ख़ास इरादे के ...करना (उदासीनता से/बिना ध्यान के)
Meaning (GU)કોઈ ખાસ ઇરાદા વગર ...કરવું (બેધ્યાનપણે)
Connection (接続)Vる + ともなく V; Vる + ともなしに V
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA passive, unfocused activity — the same verb repeats, once with ともなく attached.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
テレビを見るともなく見ていたら、私の学校が映っていた。Terebi o miru to mo naku mite itara, watashi no gakkou ga utsutte ita. The TV was on and I suddenly noticed that my school was on the screen. टीवी बिना ख़ास ध्यान से देखते हुए, अचानक मेरा स्कूल स्क्रीन पर दिखा। ટીવી ખાસ ધ્યાન વગર જોતાં જોતાં, અચાનક મારી શાળા સ્ક્રીન પર દેખાઈ.
休みの日にはどこへ行くともなしにドライブすることが多い。Yasumi no hi ni wa doko e iku to mo nashi ni doraibu suru koto ga ooi. On my holidays, I often go out driving without any particular destination in mind. छुट्टियों में अक्सर बिना किसी ख़ास मंज़िल के ड्राइव करने चला जाता हूँ। રજાઓમાં ઘણી વાર કોઈ ખાસ મંજિલ વગર ડ્રાઇવ કરવા જાઉં છું.

Notes (れい): 聞くともなしに聞く (listening without really trying to), 何をするともなく、ただ座っている (sitting doing nothing in particular). The same idea also extends to question words: いつからともなく (from who-knows-when), どこへともなく (to who-knows-where).

【4】 〜ば V/A/na/N ものを

neutral register
Reading~ba V/A/na/N mono o
Meaning (EN)"if only ~ (but instead...) — how regrettable" — expresses regret/frustration that a better outcome was possible but didn't happen
Meaning (HI)अगर...होता, तो (लेकिन नहीं हुआ) — कितना अफ़सोस है
Meaning (GU)જો...હોત, તો (પણ ન થયું) — કેટલો અફસોસ છે
Connection (接続)〜ば + V／A／na／N（plain） + ものを; naだな + ものを; Nだであるものを　◆残念な気持ちを表す
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA sighing "if only..." — often trails off with "……" for unspoken frustration.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
やればできるものを、どうしてやらないんですか。Yareba dekiru mono o, doushite yaranai n desu ka. You know you can do it. Why don't you? कर सको तो हो जाएगा, फिर क्यों नहीं करते? કરો તો થઈ જશે, તો પછી કેમ નથી કરતા?
もう少し頑張れば1位になれたものを、惜しかったですね。Mou sukoshi ganbareba ichi-i ni nareta mono o, oshikatta desu ne. It was a shame you couldn't get the first prize. You were very close. थोड़ी और कोशिश करते तो पहला स्थान मिल जाता, अफ़सोस की बात है। થોડો વધુ પ્રયત્ન કરત તો પ્રથમ સ્થાન મળી જાત, અફસોસની વાત છે.
ちょっと知らせてくれたらいいものを……。Chotto shirasete kuretara ii mono o... Why didn't they tell me that? बता देते तो अच्छा होता... જણાવી દીધું હોત તો સારું થાત...

ものを always looks backward at a missed opportunity — the ば-clause names the better path that wasn't taken, and the frustration is directed at that gap between what could have been and what actually happened.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

何を買う（　）、デパートの中をぶらぶらしていたら、友人に会った。Nani o kau (___), depaato no naka o burabura shite itara, yuujin ni atta.

Full sentence: 何を買うともなく、デパートの中をぶらぶらしていたら、友人に会った。Nani o kau to mo naku, depaato no naka o burabura shite itara, yuujin ni atta.
EN: I was wandering around the department store without any particular thing to buy in mind, and I ran into a friend.
HI: बिना कुछ ख़ास ख़रीदने के इरादे के डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर में घूम रहा था, तभी एक दोस्त से मुलाक़ात हो गई।
GU: કંઈ ખાસ ખરીદવાના ઇરાદા વગર ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરમાં ફરી રહ્યો હતો, ત્યારે એક મિત્રને મળી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aともなくto mo nakuwithout particular intentionबिना ख़ास इरादे केખાસ ઇરાદા વગર
bことなしにkoto nashi niwithout doing (wrong — needs a condition, not aimless browsing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Wandering aimlessly with no clear shopping purpose is Point 3's ともなく; ことなしに states a strict precondition, which doesn't fit "browsing without purpose."

Q2

黙っていればいい（　）、つい余計なことを言ってしまった。Damatte ireba ii (___), tsui yokei na koto o itte shimatta.

Full sentence: 黙っていればいいものを、つい余計なことを言ってしまった。Damatte ireba ii mono o, tsui yokei na koto o itte shimatta.
EN: If only I'd stayed quiet, but I ended up saying something unnecessary.
HI: चुप रह जाता तो अच्छा था, लेकिन ग़लती से कुछ ज़्यादा बोल दिया।
GU: મૌન રહ્યો હોત તો સારું હતું, પણ ભૂલથી કંઈક વધારે બોલી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aものをmono oif only... (regret)...होता तो अच्छा था...હોત તો સારું હતું
bことをkoto o(not a valid combination here)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Regretting not staying silent is exactly Point 4's ものを.

Q3

健康な体（　）どんなにお金があっても幸せとは言えない。Kenkou na karada (___) donna ni okane ga atte mo shiawase towa ienai.

Full sentence: 健康な体なくしては、どんなにお金があっても幸せとは言えない。Kenkou na karada nakushite wa, donna ni okane ga atte mo shiawase towa ienai.
EN: Without a healthy body, no matter how much money you have, you can't call yourself happy.
HI: स्वस्थ शरीर के बिना, चाहे कितना भी पैसा हो, ख़ुशी नहीं कही जा सकती।
GU: તંદુરસ્ત શરીર વગર, ભલે ગમે તેટલા પૈસા હોય, ખુશી ન કહી શકાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなくしてにnakushite ni(not a valid combination)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
bなくしてはnakushite wawithout ~, there is noके बिनाવગર

Why: Point 1's なくして takes the optional は for extra emphasis; なくしてに isn't a valid grammatical form.

Q4

この小説は涙（　）読むことができない。Kono shousetsu wa namida (___) yomu koto ga dekinai.

Full sentence: この小説は涙なしに読むことができない。Kono shousetsu wa namida nashi ni yomu koto ga dekinai.
EN: I cannot read this novel without crying.
HI: यह उपन्यास बिना आँसू बहाए पढ़ा नहीं जा सकता।
GU: આ નવલકથા આંસુ વગર વાંચી શકાય નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなしにnashi niwithoutबिनाવગર
bなくでnaku de(not a valid form)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

駅を降りたら、どこから（　）おいしそうないい匂いがしてきた。Eki o oritara, doko kara (___) oishisou na ii nioi ga shite kita.

Full sentence: 駅を降りたら、どこからともなくおいしそうないい匂いがしてきた。Eki o oritara, doko kara to mo naku oishisou na ii nioi ga shite kita.
EN: When I got off at the station, a delicious smell started coming from who-knows-where.
HI: स्टेशन पर उतरते ही, न जाने कहाँ से एक स्वादिष्ट-सी ख़ुशबू आने लगी।
GU: સ્ટેશન પર ઉતરતાં જ, ક્યાંકથી એક સ્વાદિષ્ટ સુગંધ આવવા લાગી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなくしてnakushitewithout (wrong — this describes a source, not a missing precondition)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 勉強に集中できない。___ benkyou ni shuuchuu dekinai.

Tiles: 1 聞こえてくる (kikoete kuru)　2 ピアノの音が気になって (piano no oto ga ki ni natte)　3 ともなく (to mo naku)　4 どこから (doko kara)

4 どこから→3 ともなく→1 聞こえてくる→2 ピアノの音が気になって

どこからともなく聞こえてくるピアノの音が気になって勉強に集中できない。Doko kara to mo naku kikoete kuru piano no oto ga ki ni natte benkyou ni shuuchuu dekinai.

EN: The sound of a piano coming from who-knows-where distracts me, and I can't concentrate on studying.
HI: न जाने कहाँ से आ रही पियानो की आवाज़ ध्यान भटका देती है, पढ़ाई में मन नहीं लगता।
GU: ક્યાંકથી આવતો પિયાનોનો અવાજ ધ્યાન ભટકાવે છે, ભણવામાં મન લાગતું નથી.

Why: Built on Point 3's どこからともなく — the source of the sound is unknown, which is exactly the distraction.

Q7

五分早く＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 電車に乗り遅れてしまった。Go-fun hayaku ___ densha ni noriokurete shimatta.

Tiles: 1 のんびりしていて (nonbiri shite ite)　2 よかった (yokatta)　3 ものを (mono o)　4 家を出れば (ie o dereba)

4 家を出れば→2 よかった→3 ものを→1 のんびりしていて

五分早く家を出ればよかったものを、のんびりしていて電車に乗り遅れてしまった。Go-fun hayaku ie o dereba yokatta mono o, nonbiri shite ite densha ni noriokurete shimatta.

EN: If only I'd left the house five minutes earlier, but I dawdled and missed the train.
HI: पाँच मिनट पहले घर से निकल जाता तो अच्छा था, लेकिन आराम करते हुए ट्रेन छूट गई।
GU: પાંચ મિનિટ પહેલાં ઘરેથી નીકળ્યો હોત તો સારું હતું, પણ આરામ કરતાં ટ્રેન છૂટી ગઈ.

Why: Built on Point 4's ものを — regretting a missed chance to leave early, then dawdling and missing the train as a result.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 1 and 2 both mean "without ~, cannot ~," differing mainly in register; Points 3 and 4 do very different jobs entirely:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nなくして（は）N nakushite (wa) Without ~, there is no ~ [硬] N + なくして（は）…ない Formal, near-moralistic necessity. Essays, speeches on essential preconditions.
Nなしに（は）／なしではN nashi ni/de wa Without ~, cannot ~ N + なしに（は）／なしでは…ない Same meaning, but neutral/everyday register. Conversation and general writing.
Vるともなく~ru to mo naku Doing ~ without particular intent Vる + ともなく V Describes an unfocused, half-mindful action — no regret involved. "Watching TV absentmindedly."
〜ば〜ものを~ba~mono o If only ~ (regret) 〜ば + V/A/na/N + ものを Expresses regret over a missed better outcome. Sighing about a mistake or missed chance.
Exam trap: なくして and なしに are close to interchangeable in meaning, so the register clue (硬 vs neutral) is what the exam actually tests — a formal essay context favors なくして, a casual sentence favors なしに/なしでは. Meanwhile, don't confuse ともなく (Point 3, no emotional charge) with ものを (Point 4, always regretful) just because both can follow a verb — check whether the sentence sighs about a missed opportunity.
