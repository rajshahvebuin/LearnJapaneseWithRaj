Notes
Point 3 is tagged 硬 formal/written. Points 1–2 both emphasize extremes (a large quantity, a tiny quantity denied), while Points 3–4 both concede a small amount is still meaningful or unique.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 N〈重量・寸法・数〉からある／N〈値段〉からする／N〈費用・数〉からの

neutral register
ReadingN kara aru / N kara suru / N kara no
Meaning (EN)"as much as/costing at least ~ (emphasizing the quantity is large)" — emphasizes that a quantity/price is substantial, "at minimum this much (or more)"
Meaning (HI)कम से कम...(इतनी बड़ी मात्रा/कीमत पर ज़ोर)
Meaning (GU)ઓછામાં ઓછું...(આટલી મોટી માત્રા/કિંમત પર ભાર)
Connection (接続)N〈重量・寸法・数〉 + からある; N〈値段〉 + からする; N〈費用・数〉 + からの
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆その数量が多いということを表す (expresses that the quantity is large) — weight/size/count uses からある, price uses からする, and cost/count as a noun modifier uses からの.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は、20万からするバッグをいくつも持っている。Kanojo wa, nijuu-man kara suru baggu o ikutsu mo motte iru. She has many purses that are each worth about 200,000 yen (or more). उसके पास कई बैग हैं, जिनमें से हर एक की क़ीमत कम से कम 2 लाख येन है। તેની પાસે અનેક બેગ છે, જેમાંથી દરેકની કિંમત ઓછામાં ઓછી 2 લાખ યેન છે.
7キロからある道を歩いて帰った。Nana-kiro kara aru michi o aruite kaetta. I walked about 7 km (or more) to get home. कम से कम 7 किलोमीटर का रास्ता पैदल तय करके घर लौटा/लौटी। ઓછામાં ઓછા 7 કિલોમીટરનો રસ્તો ચાલીને ઘરે પાછો/પાછી આવ્યો/આવી.
この店を改装するには500万円からの資金が必要である。Kono mise o kaisou suru ni wa gohyaku-man-en kara no shikin ga hitsuyou de aru. You will need about 5 million yen (or more) to remodel the shop. इस दुकान का नवीनीकरण करने के लिए कम से कम 50 लाख येन की पूँजी चाहिए। આ દુકાનનું નવીનીકરણ કરવા માટે ઓછામાં ઓછા 50 લાખ યેનનું ફંડ જોઈએ.

The から here means "starting from" — the true amount could be higher, but is guaranteed to be at least this much, which is why the whole construction feels impressively large.

【2】 Nたりとも〜ない

neutral register
ReadingN taritomo...nai
Meaning (EN)"not even ~ (the smallest amount)" — emphatically denies even the tiniest quantity
Meaning (HI)एक भी...नहीं (सबसे छोटी मात्रा भी नहीं)
Meaning (GU)એક પણ...નહીં (સૌથી નાની માત્રા પણ નહીં)
Connection (接続)N〈数量〉 + たりとも〜ない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAn absolute, uncompromising denial — always paired with a unit-of-one noun (a second, a yen, a drop).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
試験では一秒たりとも時間をむだに使ってはいけません。Shiken de wa ichi-byou taritomo jikan o muda ni tsukatte wa ikemasen. You should not waste even one second during the exam. परीक्षा में एक सेकंड भी बर्बाद नहीं करना चाहिए। પરીક્ષામાં એક સેકન્ડ પણ બગાડવી ન જોઈએ.
その店は、一円たりともまけてくれませんよ。Sono mise wa, ichi-en taritomo makete kuremasen yo. The shop will not knock off even one yen. वह दुकान एक येन भी कम नहीं करेगी। તે દુકાન એક યેન પણ ઓછો નહીં કરે.

Notes (れい): 一日たりとも, 一滴たりとも, 一粒たりとも, 一瞬たりとも, 一度たりとも. The construction always uses "一" plus a counter — it's specifically about denying even the singular minimal unit.

【3】 Nなりとも

硬 formal/written
ReadingN naritomo
Meaning (EN)"even just a little ~ / at least somewhat ~" — a formal way of conceding a minimal amount is still meaningful
Meaning (HI)कम से कम कुछ...(थोड़ा-सा भी काफ़ी है)
Meaning (GU)ઓછામાં ઓછું થોડું...(થોડું પણ પૂરતું છે)
Connection (接続)N + なりとも
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useA polite, humble request or admission — "even a little would help/matter."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
歌舞伎については、多少なりとも知っています。Kabuki ni tsuite wa, tashou naritomo shitte imasu. I know a few things about kabuki. काबुकी के बारे में मुझे कुछ-कुछ पता है। કાબુકી વિશે મને થોડું ઘણું ખબર છે.
お嬢さんに一目なりとも会わせていただけませんでしょうか。Ojousan ni hitome naritomo awasete itadakemasen deshou ka. Could I not see your daughter just for a second? क्या आपकी बेटी से एक झलक भी नहीं मिल सकती? શું તમારી દીકરીની એક ઝલક પણ ન મળી શકે?

Notes (れい): わずかなりとも, どこへなりとも. なりとも conveys the opposite emotional tone from Point 2's たりとも — Point 2 fiercely denies even a minimum, while なりとも humbly welcomes even a minimum.

【4】 Nならでは

neutral register
ReadingN nara de wa
Meaning (EN)"unique to ~ / only possible because of ~ (praise)" — praises something as distinctively possible only because of the named source
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़...की ही ख़ासियत (एक प्रशंसा भरा तरीक़ा)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત...ની જ ખાસિયત (એક પ્રશંસાત્મક રીત)
Connection (接続)N + ならでは　◆よい内容を表すことが多い
RegisterNeutral
Typical useMarketing/praise language — highlighting an exclusive strength tied to a specific source.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あなたならではの発想で、この企画を考えてください。Anata nara de wa no hassou de, kono kikaku o kangaete kudasai. Please come up with your own unique plan. अपने ख़ास अंदाज़ में, इस योजना पर सोचें। તમારા ખાસ અંદાજમાં, આ યોજના પર વિચારો.
これは当店ならではの特別価格です。Kore wa touten nara de wa no tokubetsu kakaku desu. This is a special price which is being offered only at this store. यह सिर्फ़ हमारी दुकान की ख़ास क़ीमत है। આ ફક્ત અમારી દુકાનની ખાસ કિંમત છે.
このような習慣があるのは、この地方ならではです。Kono you na shuukan ga aru no wa, kono chihou nara de wa desu. This kind of custom is unique to this area. ऐसी रस्म होना, सिर्फ़ इस इलाक़े की ख़ासियत है। આવો રિવાજ હોવો, ફક્ત આ વિસ્તારની ખાસિયત છે.

Notes (れい): 女性ならでは, 主婦ならでは, 手作りならでは, 映画ならでは, 田舎ならでは. ◆よい内容を表すことが多い — almost always positive/complimentary, unlike neutral comparison patterns.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

「鈴木」なんていう名前、この町に千から（　）んだよ。どうやって彼を探すんだよ。"Suzuki" nante iu namae, kono machi ni sen kara (___) n da yo. Dou yatte kare o sagasu n da yo.

Full sentence: 「鈴木」なんていう名前、この町に千からあるんだよ。どうやって彼を探すんだよ。"Suzuki" nante iu namae, kono machi ni sen kara aru n da yo. Dou yatte kare o sagasu n da yo.
EN: There are as many as a thousand people named "Suzuki" in this town. How am I supposed to find him?
HI: "सुज़ुकी" नाम वाले इस शहर में कम से कम एक हज़ार लोग हैं। उसे कैसे ढूँढूँ?
GU: "સુઝુકી" નામવાળા આ શહેરમાં ઓછામાં ઓછા હજાર લોકો છે. તેને કેવી રીતે શોધું?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあるaruas many as (exist)कम से कम इतने हैंઓછામાં ઓછા આટલા છે
bないnai(wrong — contradicts the "so many" complaint)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 千からある ("as many as a thousand") is Point 1's count emphasis — the whole point is that there are too many people with that name.

Q2

300枚（　）CDの置き場に困っている。Sanbyaku-mai (___) CD no okiba ni komatte iru.

Full sentence: 300枚からあるCDの置き場に困っている。Sanbyaku-mai kara aru CD no okiba ni komatte iru.
EN: I'm having trouble finding storage space for as many as 300 (or more) CDs.
HI: कम से कम 300 सीडी रखने की जगह ढूँढना मुश्किल हो रहा है।
GU: ઓછામાં ઓછી 300 સીડી રાખવાની જગ્યા શોધવી મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aからするkara surucosting at least (wrong — 枚 counts items, not price)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bからあるkara aruas many as (a count)कम से कम इतनेઓછામાં ઓછા આટલા

Why: 枚 (a counter for flat objects like CDs) is a count/number, which takes からある, not からする (reserved for prices).

Q3

お米一粒（　）粗末にするなと、よく祖母から言われた。Okome hito-tsubu (___) somatsu ni suru na to, yoku sobo kara iwareta.

Full sentence: お米一粒たりとも粗末にするなと、よく祖母から言われた。Okome hito-tsubu taritomo somatsu ni suru na to, yoku sobo kara iwareta.
EN: My grandmother often told me not to waste even a single grain of rice.
HI: दादी अक्सर कहती थीं कि चावल का एक भी दाना बर्बाद मत करो।
GU: દાદી ઘણી વાર કહેતાં કે ચોખાનો એક પણ દાણો બગાડ ન કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aたりともtaritomonot even (the smallest unit)एक भी नहींએક પણ નહીં
bからあるkara aruas many as (wrong — this emphasizes a large amount, not denies a tiny one)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: "Not even a single grain" is Point 2's たりとも, denying the smallest possible unit; からある would (wrongly) emphasize a large quantity instead.

Q4

桜の木の下で酒を飲んで歌を歌う。日本（　）お花見の風景だ。Sakura no ki no shita de sake o nonde uta o utau. Nihon (___) ohanami no fuukei da.

Full sentence: 桜の木の下で酒を飲んで歌を歌う。日本ならではのお花見の風景だ。Sakura no ki no shita de sake o nonde uta o utau. Nihon nara de wa no ohanami no fuukei da.
EN: Drinking and singing under the cherry blossoms — a scene of flower-viewing unique to Japan.
HI: चेरी के पेड़ों के नीचे शराब पीना और गाना गाना — यह जापान की ही ख़ास फूल देखने की परंपरा है।
GU: ચેરીના વૃક્ષો નીચે દારૂ પીવો અને ગીતો ગાવાં — આ જાપાનની જ ખાસ ફૂલ જોવાની પરંપરા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aならではのnara de wa nounique toकी ही ख़ासियतની જ ખાસિયત
bならでものnara de mo no(not a valid form)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

その若者と話してみて、多少（　）共感できることがあった。Sono wakamono to hanashite mite, tashou (___) kyoukan dekiru koto ga atta.

Full sentence: その若者と話してみて、多少なりとも共感できることがあった。Sono wakamono to hanashite mite, tashou naritomo kyoukan dekiru koto ga atta.
EN: After talking with that young person, there were things I could relate to, at least to some extent.
HI: उस युवा से बात करने पर, कुछ बातों से थोड़ा-बहुत जुड़ाव महसूस हुआ।
GU: તે યુવાન સાથે વાત કરીને, કેટલીક બાબતોમાં થોડું ઘણું જોડાણ અનુભવાયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだけともdake tomo(not a valid combination)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)
bなりともnaritomoeven just a littleथोड़ा-बहुत भीથોડું ઘણું પણ

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

新作の映画の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ のある映像です。Shinsaku no eiga no ___ no aru eizou desu.

Tiles: 1 見どころは (midokoro wa)　2 ならではの (nara de wa no)　3 コンピューターグラフィック (konpyuutaa gurafikku)　4 迫力 (hakuryoku)

1 見どころは→3 コンピューターグラフィック→2 ならではの→4 迫力

新作の映画の見どころはコンピューターグラフィックならではの迫力のある映像です。Shinsaku no eiga no midokoro wa konpyuutaa gurafikku nara de wa no hakuryoku no aru eizou desu.

EN: The highlight of the new movie is the powerful visuals unique to computer graphics.
HI: नई फ़िल्म की सबसे ख़ास बात कंप्यूटर ग्राफ़िक्स की ख़ासियत वाला दमदार दृश्य है।
GU: નવી ફિલ્મની સૌથી ખાસ વાત કમ્પ્યુટર ગ્રાફિક્સની ખાસિયતવાળું જોરદાર દૃશ્ય છે.

Why: Built on Point 4's ならでは — the powerful visuals are specifically attributed to what CG uniquely makes possible.

Q7

その犬は飼い主の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ついていくそうだ。Sono inu wa kainushi no ___ tsuite iku sou da.

Tiles: 1 どこへ (doko e)　2 ところは (tokoro wa)　3 なりとも (naritomo)　4 行く (iku)

4 行く→2 ところは→1 どこへ→3 なりとも

その犬は飼い主の行くところはどこへなりともついていくそうだ。Sono inu wa kainushi no iku tokoro wa doko e naritomo tsuite iku sou da.

EN: I hear that dog follows its owner wherever they go.
HI: कहते हैं कि वह कुत्ता मालिक जहाँ भी जाए, वहाँ पीछे-पीछे चला जाता है।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે તે કૂતરો માલિક જ્યાં પણ જાય, ત્યાં પાછળ પાછળ જાય છે.

Why: どこへなりとも ("wherever, without exception") extends Point 3's なりとも with a question word, matching the note's どこへなりとも example.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 1–2 emphasize extremes of quantity (large vs. tiny-and-denied); Points 3–4 both concede something small or specific is still meaningful:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nからある／からする／からのN kara aru/suru/no As much as/costing at least ~ N〈重量・寸法・数〉+からある; N〈値段〉+からする; N〈費用・数〉+からの Emphasizes a LARGE quantity or price. "A bag costing 200,000 yen or more."
Nたりとも〜ないN taritomo...nai Not even ~ (the smallest amount) N〈数量〉+たりとも〜ない Fiercely DENIES even the tiniest quantity. "Not even one second wasted."
NなりともN naritomo Even just a little ~ [硬] N + なりとも Humbly WELCOMES a small amount as meaningful. "Even just a little would help."
NならではN nara de wa Unique to ~ N + ならでは Praises something as exclusively possible because of a source. "A dish unique to this restaurant."
Exam trap: たりとも and なりとも share the とも ending but pull in opposite directions — たりとも always pairs with ない to deny even a minimum ("not even one"), while なりとも affirms that even a minimum is acceptable/welcome ("even just one would do"). Check whether the sentence ends negatively (→ たりとも) or positively (→ なりとも).
