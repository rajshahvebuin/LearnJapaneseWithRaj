JLPT N1 · Grammar · Week 4 — 努力なくして合格はない (Doryoku nakushite goukaku wa nai)

4日目 — この状況にあって (Kono joukyou ni atte)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.68–69 (PDF pages 69–70)
Grammar points covered
〜とあって〜とあればNにあって（は）N1あってのN2
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.71 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four patterns today share あって／あれば／あった, but split into an explanatory circumstance (とあって), a strong hypothetical (とあれば), a formal "under these circumstances" marker (にあって, tagged 硬 formal/written), and a "this exists only because of that" priority statement (あっての).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とあって

neutral register
Reading~to atte
Meaning (EN)"given that it's ~ (a special circumstance, explains the following)" — explains a notable state of affairs, rarely used for describing oneself
Meaning (HI)चूँकि यह...है (एक ख़ास मौक़ा, आगे की बात को समझाता है)
Meaning (GU)કેમ કે આ...છે (ખાસ મોકો, પછીની વાતને સમજાવે છે)
Connection (接続)V／A／na／N（plain） + とあって; na／N + とあって
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆自分のことにはあまり使わない (rarely used about oneself) — usually a third-party observation about a notable event/situation.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日は休日とあって、車の混雑が激しい。Kyou wa kyuujitsu to atte, kuruma no konzatsu ga hageshii. Today is a holiday so the traffic is terribly congested. आज छुट्टी का दिन है, इसलिए ट्रैफ़िक बहुत ज़्यादा है। આજે રજાનો દિવસ છે, એટલે ટ્રાફિક ખૂબ છે.
久しぶりの再会とあって、彼らは何時間も話していた。Hisashiburi no saikai to atte, karera wa nanjikan mo hanashite ita. They talked for many hours because they had not seen each other for a long time. लंबे समय बाद मिले, तो वे घंटों बातें करते रहे। લાંબા સમય પછી મળ્યા, તો તેઓ કલાકો સુધી વાતો કરતા રહ્યા.

とあって reports an observed circumstance from the outside — since it's rarely used for the speaker's own situation, avoid sentences like "私は忙しいとあって" (awkward); it fits third-party description much better.

【2】 〜とあれば

neutral register
Reading~to areba
Meaning (EN)"if it's the case that ~ (then anything is possible/worth doing)" — a strong hypothetical emphasizing willingness to do whatever's needed
Meaning (HI)अगर...है/हो तो (तो कुछ भी कर सकता/सकती हूँ)
Meaning (GU)જો...છે/હોય તો (તો કંઈ પણ કરી શકું છું)
Connection (接続)V／A／na／N（plain） + とあれば; na／N + とあれば
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆強調 (emphasis) — expresses that the condition alone is enough to justify extreme effort.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
娘のためとあれば、どんなことでもします。Musume no tame to areba, donna koto de mo shimasu. I'll do anything for my daughter. अगर बेटी के लिए है, तो कुछ भी कर दूँगा/दूँगी। જો દીકરી માટે છે, તો કંઈ પણ કરી દઈશ.
いいものを安く買えるとあれば、遠くの店でも喜んで行きます。Ii mono o yasuku kaeru to areba, tooku no mise de mo yorokonde ikimasu. I'd be happy to go to a far-away shop if I could get nice things cheaply. अगर अच्छी चीज़ सस्ती मिल जाए, तो दूर की दुकान तक भी ख़ुशी से जाऊँगा/जाऊँगी। જો સારી ચીજ સસ્તી મળે, તો દૂરની દુકાન સુધી પણ ખુશીથી જઈશ.

Unlike とあって (a neutral explanation), とあれば carries a devoted, "I'll go to any length" tone — the classic use is a parent's love for a child or intense enthusiasm for a bargain.

【3】 Nにあって（は）

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni atte (wa)
Meaning (EN)"in/under the circumstances of ~" — describes a state of affairs one finds oneself in
Meaning (HI)...की स्थिति/हालात में
Meaning (GU)...ની સ્થિતિ/હાલતમાં
Connection (接続)N + にあって（は）
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useSetting the scene for a formal observation — a business situation, an era, a role at home vs. at work.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この状況にあって、利益を伸ばすのは困難だ。Kono joukyou ni atte, rieki o nobasu no wa konnan da. It is difficult to make more profit under the present circumstances. इस स्थिति में, मुनाफ़ा बढ़ाना मुश्किल है। આ સ્થિતિમાં, નફો વધારવો મુશ્કેલ છે.
父は大学教授だが、家庭にあってはふつうの父親です。Chichi wa daigaku kyouju da ga, katei ni atte wa futsuu no chichioya desu. My father is a university professor but he is an ordinary father at home. पापा विश्वविद्यालय के प्रोफ़ेसर हैं, लेकिन घर में एक सामान्य पिता हैं। પપ્પા યુનિવર્સિટીના પ્રોફેસર છે, પણ ઘરમાં એક સામાન્ય પિતા છે.

にあって is close to において in meaning but carries a more formal, essay-like weight — it also often implies the circumstance is challenging or notable, not neutral.

【4】 N1あってのN2

neutral register
ReadingN1 atte no N2
Meaning (EN)"N2 that only exists because of N1 / N1 comes first, then N2" — asserts that N1 is the essential precondition for N2 to exist/matter
Meaning (HI)N1 हो तो ही N2 (N1 के बिना N2 का कोई मतलब नहीं)
Meaning (GU)N1 હોય તો જ N2 (N1 વગર N2 નો કોઈ અર્થ નથી)
Connection (接続)N1 + あっての + N2
RegisterNeutral
Typical useReminding that something taken for granted (customers, health) is actually the foundation everything else depends on.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お客様あっての仕事だから、言葉遣いに気を付けてください。Okyakusama atte no shigoto dakara, kotobazukai ni ki o tsukete kudasai. Please pay attention to the language you use with clients since we cannot afford to lose any of them. यह काम ग्राहकों की वजह से ही है, इसलिए बोलचाल पर ध्यान दें। આ કામ ગ્રાહકોના કારણે જ છે, એટલે બોલચાલ પર ધ્યાન આપો.
健康あっての人生だ。病気にならないよう気をつけよう。Kenkou atte no jinsei da. Byouki ni naranai you ki o tsukeyou. You don't have much if you don't have your health. Let's stay healthy. ज़िंदगी सेहत की वजह से ही है। बीमार न पड़ने का ध्यान रखें। જીવન સ્વાસ્થ્યના કારણે જ છે. બીમાર ન પડવાની કાળજી રાખો.

The logic is always "N2 wouldn't exist/matter without N1" — お客様あっての仕事 means the job only exists because customers exist; lose them, and there's no job at all.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.69. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.71.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

僕の幸せは家族（　）ものだ。みんな病気をせず元気でいてほしい。Boku no shiawase wa kazoku (___) mono da. Minna byouki o sezu genki de ite hoshii.

Full sentence: 僕の幸せは家族あってのものだ。みんな病気をせず元気でいてほしい。Boku no shiawase wa kazoku atte no mono da. Minna byouki o sezu genki de ite hoshii.
EN: My happiness only exists because of my family. I want everyone to stay healthy and well.
HI: मेरी ख़ुशी परिवार की वजह से ही है। चाहता हूँ सब बीमार न पड़ें और स्वस्थ रहें।
GU: મારી ખુશી પરિવારના કારણે જ છે. ઈચ્છું છું બધા બીમાર ન પડે અને સ્વસ્થ રહે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあってのatte noonly existing because ofके कारण हीના કારણે જ
bにあってのni atte no(wrong — this inserts an unnecessary に)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 4's あっての attaches directly to the noun (家族あっての); にあっての isn't a valid combination.

Q2

父は、たとえ日曜であっても、仕事（　）どこへでも行く。Chichi wa, tatoe nichiyou de atte mo, shigoto (___) doko e demo iku.

Full sentence: 父は、たとえ日曜であっても、仕事とあればどこへでも行く。Chichi wa, tatoe nichiyou de atte mo, shigoto to areba doko e demo iku.
EN: My father will go anywhere if it's for work, even on a Sunday.
HI: पापा, इतवार भी हो, तो भी काम की बात हो तो कहीं भी चले जाते हैं।
GU: પપ્પા, રવિવાર પણ હોય, તો પણ કામની વાત હોય તો ક્યાંય પણ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとあればto arebaif it's the case that (then anything)अगर बात हो तोજો વાત હોય તો
bにあってni atteunder the circumstances of (wrong — this sets a scene, not a devoted willingness)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Willingness to go anywhere for work is Point 2's とあれば; にあって would just describe a circumstance, not devotion/willingness.

Q3

事業の成功は、皆さんの協力（　）ものと感謝しています。Jigyou no seikou wa, minasan no kyouryoku (___) mono to kansha shite imasu.

Full sentence: 事業の成功は、皆さんの協力あってのものと感謝しています。Jigyou no seikou wa, minasan no kyouryoku atte no mono to kansha shite imasu.
EN: I'm grateful that the success of this venture only exists thanks to everyone's cooperation.
HI: इस व्यवसाय की सफलता सबके सहयोग की वजह से ही है, इसके लिए आभारी हूँ।
GU: આ ધંધાની સફળતા બધાના સહયોગના કારણે જ છે, તે માટે આભારી છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあってのatte noonly existing because ofके कारण हीના કારણે જ
bとあってのto atte no(not a valid combination)(अमान्य रूप)(અયોગ્ય સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Success existing only because of everyone's cooperation is Point 4's あっての; とあっての incorrectly combines と with this pattern.

Q4

決勝戦（　）、その試合の入場券はすぐに売り切れた。Kesshousen (___), sono shiai no nyuujouken wa sugu ni urikireta.

Full sentence: 決勝戦とあって、その試合の入場券はすぐに売り切れた。Kesshousen to atte, sono shiai no nyuujouken wa sugu ni urikireta.
EN: Since it was the final match, the tickets sold out right away.
HI: फ़ाइनल मैच होने की वजह से, उसके टिकट फ़ौरन बिक गए।
GU: ફાઇનલ મેચ હોવાના કારણે, તેની ટિકિટ તરત વેચાઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとあってto attegiven that it's (explains the following)होने की वजह सेહોવાના કારણે
bにあってni atteunder the circumstances of (wrong — this sets a scene, not a causal explanation)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Explaining why the tickets sold out (because it's the final) is Point 1's とあって.

Q5

情報が氾濫する時代（　）、必要なものを選択するのは難しい。Jouhou ga hanran suru jidai (___), hitsuyou na mono o sentaku suru no wa muzukashii.

Full sentence: 情報が氾濫する時代にあって、必要なものを選択するのは難しい。Jouhou ga hanran suru jidai ni atte, hitsuyou na mono o sentaku suru no wa muzukashii.
EN: In an era overflowing with information, it's difficult to choose what's necessary.
HI: जानकारी की भरमार वाले इस दौर में, ज़रूरी चीज़ें चुनना मुश्किल है।
GU: માહિતીના ભરાવાવાળા આ યુગમાં, જરૂરી ચીજો પસંદ કરવી મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあってatte(incomplete — needs に before it for this formal "under the circumstances" sense)(अपूर्ण रूप)(અપૂર્ણ સ્વરૂપ)
bにあってni atteunder the circumstances of...के हालात में...ના હાલાતમાં

Why: Setting the scene of a formal "in this era..." observation is Point 3's にあって; bare あって without に isn't the correct fixed form here.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

我が子の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ も我慢できるものだ。Wagako no ___ mo gaman dekiru mono da.

Tiles: 1 ため (tame)　2 どんな (donna)　3 苦労 (kurou)　4 とあれば (to areba)

1 ため→4 とあれば→2 どんな→3 苦労

我が子のためとあれば、どんな苦労も我慢できるものだ。Wagako no tame to areba, donna kurou mo gaman dekiru mono da.

EN: If it's for one's own child, one can endure any hardship.
HI: अगर अपने बच्चे के लिए है, तो कोई भी तकलीफ़ सहन की जा सकती है।
GU: જો પોતાના બાળક માટે છે, તો કોઈ પણ મુશ્કેલી સહન કરી શકાય છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's とあれば — a parent's devotion means any hardship becomes bearable if it's for their child.

Q7

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 彼には頭が下がる。___ kare ni wa atama ga sagaru.

Tiles: 1 あきらめないで (akiramenaide)　2 困難な状況に (konnan na joukyou ni)　3 努力をする (doryoku o suru)　4 あっても (atte mo)

2 困難な状況に→4 あっても→1 あきらめないで→3 努力をする

困難な状況にあってもあきらめないで努力をする彼には頭が下がる。Konnan na joukyou ni atte mo akiramenaide doryoku o suru kare ni wa atama ga sagaru.

EN: I have great respect for him, who doesn't give up and keeps trying even in difficult circumstances.
HI: मुश्किल हालात में भी हार न मानकर मेहनत करने वाले उसके सामने सिर झुक जाता है।
GU: મુશ્કેલ સ્થિતિમાં પણ હાર ન માનીને મહેનત કરનાર તેની આગળ માથું ઝૂકી જાય છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's にあっても (にあって + concessive も) — "even under difficult circumstances" describes his admirable persistence.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns are built on あって／あれば, but each does a completely different rhetorical job:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とあって~to atte Given that it's ~ V／A／na／N（plain） + とあって A neutral, third-party explanation of a circumstance. "Since it was a holiday, traffic was bad."
〜とあれば~to areba If it's the case that ~ V／A／na／N（plain） + とあれば A devoted hypothetical — "given this condition, I'll do anything." "For my daughter, I'll do anything."
Nにあって（は）N ni atte (wa) Under the circumstances of ~ [硬] N + にあって（は） Formally sets a scene/context, often a challenging one. Essays describing an era, a role, or a difficult situation.
N1あってのN2N1 atte no N2 N2 exists only because of N1 N1 + あっての + N2 Asserts a foundational priority — N1 must come first. "Customers come first," "health comes first."
Exam trap: とあって and にあって look similar (both use あって) but attach differently and mean different things: とあって follows a full clause/adjective and gives a causal explanation ("because it's X"), while にあって attaches directly to a bare noun and sets a formal backdrop ("in the context of X") — it doesn't explain a cause. If removing the blank and inserting "だから" still makes sense, it's とあって (Q4); if it reads more like "in/under X," it's にあって (Q5).
