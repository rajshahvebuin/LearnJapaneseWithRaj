2日目 — 大学教授ですら (Daigaku kyouju de sura)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Bunpou) — printed p.64–65 (PDF pages 65–66)
Grammar points covered
N（で/に）すらNにしてN1ともあろうN2／N1たるN2Nともなると／なれば
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.67 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Points 2 and 3 are tagged 硬 formal/written. Three of today's four patterns (すら, にして, ともあろう) all cite someone/something impressive to make a point — either praising by extreme example or criticizing for falling short of expectations.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 N（で/に）すら

neutral register
ReadingN (de/ni) sura
Meaning (EN)"even ~ (an extreme example, emphasizing the point applies more strongly elsewhere)" — cites an extreme/unexpected example
Meaning (HI)...तक भी (एक चरम उदाहरण से ज़ोर देना)
Meaning (GU)...પણ (એક ચરમ ઉદાહરણથી ભાર મૂકવો)
Connection (接続)N（で）すら; N（に）すら
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆強調 (emphasis) — nearly identical in force to さえ.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は、大学教授（で）すら気が付かなかった問題点を指摘した。Kare wa, daigaku kyouju (de) sura ki ga tsukanakatta mondaiten o shiteki shita. He pointed out problems that even university professors had not noticed. उसने वे समस्याएँ बताई जिन्हें विश्वविद्यालय के प्रोफ़ेसर भी नहीं देख पाए थे। તેણે તે સમસ્યાઓ બતાવી જે યુનિવર્સિટીના પ્રોફેસરો પણ જોઈ શક્યા નહોતા.
この悩みは親友にすら言えない。Kono nayami wa shinyuu ni sura ienai. I can't talk about this problem even with my best friend. यह परेशानी मैं अपने सबसे अच्छे दोस्त को भी नहीं बता सकता/सकती। આ મુશ્કેલી હું મારા સૌથી સારા મિત્રને પણ કહી શકતો/શકતી નથી.

すら and さえ are near-perfect synonyms — すら leans slightly more written/formal, but both cite an extreme example to imply "and therefore, obviously, everything less extreme too."

【2】 Nにして

硬 formal/written
ReadingN ni shite
Meaning (EN)"even N (someone/something so accomplished/authoritative)" / "at the same time, both N and..." — an emphatic citation, or a "both...and" construction
Meaning (HI)...होते हुए भी (इतना क़ाबिल होकर भी); या "...होते हुए साथ ही"
Meaning (GU)...હોવા છતાં (આટલા સક્ષમ હોવા છતાં); અથવા "...હોવાની સાથે જ"
Connection (接続)N + にして
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical useEmphasizing that even an authoritative figure fell short, or that a change happened in a flash, or naming dual roles.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
先生にして間違えるのだから、できないのは当然である。Sensei ni shite machigaeru no dakara, dekinai no wa touzen de aru. It is only natural that you couldn't solve it because your teacher couldn't either. जब शिक्षक ही ग़लती कर सकते हैं, तो न कर पाना स्वाभाविक ही है। જ્યારે શિક્ષક જ ભૂલ કરી શકે છે, તો ન કરી શકવું સ્વાભાવિક જ છે.
竜巻が一瞬にして家を吹き飛ばした。Tatsumaki ga isshun ni shite ie o fukitobashita. A tornado blew my house away in a blink. बवंडर ने पल भर में घर उड़ा दिया। વાવાઝોડાએ પળભરમાં ઘર ઉડાવી દીધું.
彼は、医者にして、画家でもある。Kare wa, isha ni shite, gaka de mo aru. He is both a doctor and a painter. वह डॉक्टर होने के साथ-साथ चित्रकार भी है। તે ડોક્ટર હોવાની સાથે ચિત્રકાર પણ છે.

Notice the three distinct flavors in the examples: citing authority (Example 1, "even the teacher"), instantaneous speed (Example 2, 一瞬にして = "in an instant"), and dual identity (Example 3, "both X and Y"). Context determines which reading applies.

【3】 N1ともあろうN2／N1たるN2

硬 formal/written
ReadingN1 to mo arou N2 / N1 taru N2
Meaning (EN)"someone/something of the standing of N1 (should know better)" — criticizes someone for failing to live up to the expectations of their status/position
Meaning (HI)N1 जैसे रुतबे वाले को (ऐसा नहीं करना चाहिए था) — पद के मुताबिक अपेक्षा पूरी न करने पर आलोचना
Meaning (GU)N1 જેવા દરજ્જાવાળાએ (આવું ન કરવું જોઈતું હતું) — હોદ્દા મુજબ અપેક્ષા પૂરી ન કરવા બદલ ટીકા
Connection (接続)N1 + ともあろう + N2; N1 + たる + N2
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical use◆批判が続くことが多い (usually followed by criticism) — a status-based expectation that was disappointingly unmet.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大学生ともあろう者が、その漢字を読めないのは恥ずかしい。Daigakusei to mo arou mono ga, sono kanji o yomenai no wa hazukashii. University students should feel ashamed of not being able to read this kanji. एक विश्वविद्यालय के छात्र होकर भी यह कांजी न पढ़ पाना शर्मनाक है। યુનિવર્સિટીના વિદ્યાર્થી હોવા છતાં આ કાંજી ન વાંચી શકવું શરમજનક છે.
一国の首相たる者が、このような発言をしてはいけない。Ikkoku no shushou taru mono ga, kono you na hatsugen o shite wa ikenai. The Prime Minister of a country should never say anything like this. एक देश के प्रधानमंत्री होकर ऐसा बयान नहीं देना चाहिए। એક દેશના વડાપ્રધાન હોવા છતાં આવું નિવેદન ન કરવું જોઈએ.

Notes (れい): 社長ともあろう人 (someone of the standing of a company president), A社ともあろう企業 (a company of A社's standing), 教師たる者 (someone of the standing of a teacher). Both ともあろう and たる carry the same "should live up to their status" criticism.

【4】 Nともなると／なれば

neutral register
ReadingN to mo naru to / nareba
Meaning (EN)"once it becomes ~ (a significant threshold), things change accordingly" — describes a change once reaching a notable status or moment
Meaning (HI)जब...हो जाए (एक अहम पड़ाव), तो हालात बदल जाते हैं
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે...થઈ જાય (એક મહત્વનો પડાવ), તો પરિસ્થિતિ બદલાઈ જાય છે
Connection (接続)N + ともなると／なれば; N／V（plain） + と + なると／なれば／なったら　💬 なったら is more casual/spoken
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing a shift in freedom, responsibility, or feeling once a certain threshold (a role, a moment, a decision) is reached.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大臣ともなると、自由に行動できない。Daijin to mo naru to, jiyuu ni koudou dekinai. Once you become a minister in a cabinet, you lose your freedom. मंत्री बन जाने पर, आज़ादी से काम करना मुश्किल हो जाता है। મંત્રી બની ગયા પછી, સ્વતંત્રતાથી કામ કરવું મુશ્કેલ થઈ જાય છે.
いざ出発となると、不安になってきた。Iza shuppatsu to naru to, fuan ni natte kita. When the departure time actually came, I started feeling worried. जब सचमुच जाने का वक़्त आया, तो घबराहट होने लगी। જ્યારે ખરેખર જવાનો સમય આવ્યો, ત્યારે ગભરાટ થવા લાગ્યો.
銀行でお金を借りるとなったら、手続きが大変だ。Ginkou de okane o kariru to nattara, tetsuzuki ga taihen da. You have to deal with a complicated process once you decide to borrow money from a bank. बैंक से पैसे उधार लेने का फ़ैसला करते ही, काग़ज़ी काम बहुत मुश्किल हो जाता है। બેંકમાંથી પૈસા ઉછીના લેવાનું નક્કી કરતાં જ, કાગળિયાં ખૂબ મુશ્કેલ થઈ જાય છે.

Fixed idiom: いざとなったら (=重大な状況に直面したら) "when the time comes / if push comes to shove" — worth memorizing whole, since いざ often pairs with this pattern for extra emphasis on the pivotal moment.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼はとてもいい人だ。でも結婚（　）、ちょっと頼りない気がする。Kare wa totemo ii hito da. Demo kekkon (___), chotto tayorinai ki ga suru.

Full sentence: 彼はとてもいい人だ。でも結婚となると、ちょっと頼りない気がする。Kare wa totemo ii hito da. Demo kekkon to naru to, chotto tayorinai ki ga suru.
EN: He's a very nice person. But when it comes to marriage, I feel he's a bit unreliable.
HI: वह बहुत अच्छा इंसान है। लेकिन शादी की बात आते ही, थोड़ा भरोसे लायक़ नहीं लगता।
GU: તે ખૂબ સારો વ્યક્તિ છે. પણ લગ્નની વાત આવે ત્યારે, થોડો ભરોસાપાત્ર નથી લાગતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aともなくto mo nakuwithout particular intent (wrong pattern entirely)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとなるとto naru toonce it comes toबात आते हीવાત આવે ત્યારે

Why: "When it comes to marriage specifically" is Point 4's ともなると／となると, describing a shift in impression once a specific topic/threshold is reached.

Q2

親（　）者は、子どもが悪いことをしたときに、きちんとしかるべきだ。Oya (___) mono wa, kodomo ga warui koto o shita toki ni, kichinto shikaru beki da.

Full sentence: 親たる者は、子どもが悪いことをしたときに、きちんとしかるべきだ。Oya taru mono wa, kodomo ga warui koto o shita toki ni, kichinto shikaru beki da.
EN: Someone in the position of a parent should properly scold their child when they do something wrong.
HI: माँ-बाप की ज़िम्मेदारी है कि बच्चे के ग़लत काम करने पर उसे ठीक से डाँटा जाए।
GU: માતાપિતાની જવાબદારી છે કે બાળક ખોટું કામ કરે તો તેને બરાબર ધમકાવવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aたるtaruof the standing ofकी हैसियत/ज़िम्मेदारी वालाની હેસિયત/જવાબદારીવાળો
bにたるni taru(wrong — this attaches directly, no に needed)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: たる attaches directly to the noun (親たる者) — Point 3's N1たるN2 form; にたる inserts an unnecessary に.

Q3

あのような一流レストラン（　）と、男性はネクタイが必要だ。Ano you na ichiryuu resutoran (___) to, dansei wa nekutai ga hitsuyou da.

Full sentence: あのような一流レストランともなると、男性はネクタイが必要だ。Ano you na ichiryuu resutoran to mo naru to, dansei wa nekutai ga hitsuyou da.
EN: When it comes to a first-class restaurant like that, men need to wear a tie.
HI: उस तरह के प्रथम श्रेणी के रेस्तराँ की बात आते ही, पुरुषों को टाई पहननी ज़रूरी हो जाती है।
GU: તે પ્રકારની પ્રથમ કક્ષાની રેસ્ટોરન્ટની વાત આવે ત્યારે, પુરુષોએ ટાઈ પહેરવી જરૂરી બને છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aともなるto mo naruonce it becomes (+ と outside completes ともなると)बात आते हीવાત આવે ત્યારે
bともあろうto mo arouof the standing of (wrong — this criticizes a person's status, not a venue threshold)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Reaching the threshold of "a first-class restaurant" changing dress expectations is Point 4's ともなると; ともあろう (Point 3) is reserved for criticizing a person/entity for not living up to their status, not describing a venue's dress code.

Q4

結婚なんてとんでもない。私は男の人と話したこと（　）ないのです。Kekkon nante tondemonai. Watashi wa otoko no hito to hanashita koto (___) nai no desu.

Full sentence: 結婚なんてとんでもない。私は男の人と話したことすらないのです。Kekkon nante tondemonai. Watashi wa otoko no hito to hanashita koto sura nai no desu.
EN: Marriage is out of the question. I haven't even ever talked to a man.
HI: शादी की तो बात ही मत करो। मैंने तो किसी लड़के से बात भी नहीं की है।
GU: લગ્નની તો વાત જ ના કરો. મેં તો કોઈ છોકરા સાથે વાત પણ નથી કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aすらsuraevenतक भीપણ
bにすらni sura(wrong — こと doesn't need に before すら here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ にはこの暑さは厳しいだろう。___ ni wa kono atsusa wa kibishii darou.

Tiles: 1 老人 (roujin)　2 ですら (de sura)　3 つらいのだから (tsurai no dakara)　4 若い私たち (wakai watashitachi)

4 若い私たち→2 ですら→3 つらいのだから→1 老人

若い私たちですらつらいのだから老人にはこの暑さは厳しいだろう。Wakai watashitachi de sura tsurai no dakara roujin ni wa kono atsusa wa kibishii darou.

EN: Since even we young people find it tough, this heat must be severe for the elderly.
HI: हम जवान लोगों को ही मुश्किल हो रही है, तो बुज़ुर्गों के लिए यह गर्मी सचमुच कठिन होगी।
GU: અમને યુવાનોને જ મુશ્કેલ પડે છે, તો વૃદ્ધો માટે આ ગરમી ખરેખર કઠિન હશે.

Why: Built on Point 1's ですら — young people finding it tough is the extreme baseline that implies it's even worse for the elderly.

Q7

内科の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ が、栄養のことを気にかけないのは理解できない。Naika no ___ ga, eiyou no koto o ki ni kakenai no wa rikai dekinai.

Tiles: 1 者 (mono)　2 医者 (isha)　3 あろう (arou)　4 とも (tomo)

2 医者→4 とも→3 あろう→1 者

内科の医者ともあろう者が、栄養のことを気にかけないのは理解できない。Naika no isha to mo arou mono ga, eiyou no koto o ki ni kakenai no wa rikai dekinai.

EN: It's hard to understand how someone of the standing of an internal medicine doctor wouldn't care about nutrition.
HI: एक फिजिशियन होकर पोषण की परवाह न करना समझ से बाहर है।
GU: એક ફિઝિશિયન હોવા છતાં પોષણની કાળજી ન લેવી સમજની બહાર છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's N1ともあろうN2 — a doctor's professional status makes their indifference to nutrition especially hard to understand.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four patterns invoke a notable example or status, but for different rhetorical purposes:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
N（で/に）すらN (de/ni) sura Even ~ (extreme example) N（で）すら; N（に）すら Cites an extreme case to emphasize a broader point applies. "Even a professor didn't notice."
NにしてN ni shite Even N / at the same time [硬] N + にして Citing authority, instantaneous speed, or dual identity. Formal writing on an authority figure, sudden events, or dual roles.
N1ともあろうN2N1 to mo arou N2 Someone of N1's standing (should know better) [硬] N1 + ともあろう／たる + N2 Criticizes someone for failing to meet status-based expectations. "A university student should be ashamed of..."
NともなるとN to mo naru to Once it becomes ~ N + ともなると／なれば Describes a change once a threshold/status is reached — no criticism implied. "Once you're a minister, you lose your freedom."
Exam trap: N1ともあろうN2 and Nともなると both contain ともある／ともなる and look superficially similar, but ともあろう always sets up criticism ("should know better"), while ともなると is neutral, describing any change of circumstance once a threshold is reached — no disapproval implied (Q3 above tests exactly this distinction).
