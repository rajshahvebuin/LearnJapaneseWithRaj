Notes
Points 2 and 4 are tagged 硬 formal/written. Three of today's four patterns are built on double or soft negatives that end up meaning something closer to a hedged "yes" or a wish — a classic N1 trap for learners who read them too literally as plain negatives.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜くなく／でなく／なく／られなく＋はない／もない

neutral register
Reading~ku naku / de naku / naku / rarenaku + wa nai / mo nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not that ~ isn't the case (a soft, roundabout affirmation)" — a double-negative used to affirm something mildly/indirectly
Meaning (HI)ऐसा नहीं है कि...न हो (एक हल्की, घुमावदार पुष्टि)
Meaning (GU)એવું નથી કે...ન હોય (એક હળવી, ગોળ ગોળ પુષ્ટિ)
Connection (接続)Aく／naで／V／Vられ + なく + はない／もない; Nが + なくはない／なくもない
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆消極的に肯定する言い方 (a passive/indirect way of affirming something) — softens a "yes" by routing it through a double negative.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は会社を辞めたいらしい。その気持ちはわからなくはない。Kare wa kaisha o yametai rashii. Sono kimochi wa wakaranaku wa nai. I gather he wants to quit his job. I can understand him to some extent. वह नौकरी छोड़ना चाहता है ऐसा लग रहा है। उसकी भावना समझी जा सकती है। તે નોકરી છોડવા માંગે છે એવું લાગે છે. તેની લાગણી સમજી શકાય એવી છે.
できなくはないが、自信がないからやりたくない。Dekinaku wa nai ga, jishin ga nai kara yaritakunai. I may be able to do it but I don't want to do it because I don't have enough confidence. कर सकता/सकती हूँ, लेकिन आत्मविश्वास न होने से करना नहीं चाहता/चाहती। કરી શકું છું, પણ આત્મવિશ્વાસ ન હોવાથી કરવા માંગતો/માંગતી નથી.
それくらいのマンションなら、無理すれば買えなくもない。Sore kurai no manshon nara, muri sureba kaenaku mo nai. I may be able to buy such an apartment if I really put my mind to it. उस स्तर का अपार्टमेंट, अगर ज़ोर लगाया जाए तो ख़रीदा जा सकता है। તે સ્તરનું એપાર્ટમેન્ટ, જો પ્રયત્ન કરાય તો ખરીદી શકાય.

Read わからなくはない as "it's not [the case that I don't understand]" → "I do understand, to some degree" — the double negative is a hedge, not a true denial. This is a classic N1 reading-comprehension trap: don't mistake it for a negative statement.

【2】 Vない（もの）でもない

硬 formal/written
ReadingV nai (mono) de mo nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not that it's impossible to ~ (there's a real chance)" — leaves open a genuine possibility, more formal than Point 1
Meaning (HI)यह भी नहीं कि...नामुमकिन है (एक सच्ची संभावना बनी रहती है)
Meaning (GU)એ પણ નથી કે...અશક્ય છે (એક ખરી શક્યતા રહે છે)
Connection (接続)Vない（もの）でもない
Register硬 — formal, mostly written
Typical use◆可能性があるときに使う (used when there's a real possibility) — leaving the door open rather than firmly committing.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今すぐ会社を出れば、コンサートに間に合わないものでもない。Ima sugu kaisha o dereba, konsaato ni maniawanai mono de mo nai. You may be able to make it in time for the concert if you leave the office right now. अभी दफ़्तर से निकल जाओ, तो कॉन्सर्ट के लिए वक़्त पर पहुँचना नामुमकिन नहीं है। અત્યારે ઓફિસમાંથી નીકળી જાઓ, તો કોન્સર્ટ માટે સમયસર પહોંચવું અશક્ય નથી.
条件によっては、その仕事を引き受けないものでもない。Jouken ni yotte wa, sono shigoto o hikiukenai mono de mo nai. I may take the work depending on the conditions. शर्तों के हिसाब से, वह काम स्वीकार करना असंभव नहीं है। શરતો પ્રમાણે, તે કામ સ્વીકારવું અશક્ય નથી.

Compare directly with Point 1: なくはない／なくもない (Point 1) is a general, everyday hedge about facts or feelings, while ないものでもない (Point 2) is specifically about a real possibility for a future action, in a more formal register.

【3】 Nとは比べものにならない

neutral register
ReadingN to wa kurabemono ni naranai
Meaning (EN)"cannot be compared to ~ (the difference is too great)" — emphasizes an enormous gap/difference
Meaning (HI)...से इसकी तुलना ही नहीं हो सकती (फ़र्क़ बहुत बड़ा है)
Meaning (GU)...ની સાથે તેની સરખામણી જ ન થઈ શકે (ફરક ઘણો મોટો છે)
Connection (接続)N + とは比べものにならない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizing a dramatic, undeniable difference between two things.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今の車の性能は、昔とは比べものにならない。Ima no kuruma no seinou wa, mukashi to wa kurabemono ni naranai. Cars these days are much more fuel efficient than they were in the past. आज की गाड़ियों का प्रदर्शन, पुराने ज़माने से तुलना ही नहीं की जा सकती। આજની ગાડીઓનું પ્રદર્શન, જૂના જમાના સાથે સરખામણી જ ન થઈ શકે.
彼の成績は、私とは比べものにならないほどいい。Kare no seiseki wa, watashi to wa kurabemono ni naranai hodo ii. His marks are way better than mine. उसके अंक मुझसे तुलना ही न की जा सके, इतने अच्छे हैं। તેના માર્ક્સ મારી સાથે સરખામણી જ ન થઈ શકે, એટલા સારા છે.

This is a fixed idiom worth memorizing as a whole chunk — 比べものにならない always follows とは and asserts the gap is too extreme for a meaningful comparison at all.

【4】 （どうにか）（なんとか）（もう少し）Vないもの（だろう）か

硬 formal/written
Reading(dou ni ka) (nanto ka) (mou sukoshi) V nai mono (darou) ka
Meaning (EN)"isn't there some way to ~? / can't something be done about ~?" — a plea/wish for a solution to a frustrating situation
Meaning (HI)क्या कोई तरीक़ा नहीं है...(करने का)? — किसी हल की उम्मीद/गुज़ारिश
Meaning (GU)શું કોઈ રસ્તો નથી...(કરવાનો)? — કોઈ ઉકેલની આશા/વિનંતી
Connection (接続)（どうにか）（なんとか）（もう少し） + Vないもの（だろう）か／Vれないもの（だろう）か
Register硬 — formal, mostly written; also common as an exclamatory spoken wish
Typical useExpressing frustrated hope that a difficult problem might still have a solution.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このにおい、なんとかならないものか！Kono nioi, nanto ka naranai mono ka! Can't we do anything about this smell? इस बदबू का कुछ तो किया जाए! આ ગંધનું કંઈક તો કરો!
大事な時計が壊れてしまった。どうにか直せないものだろうか。Daiji na tokei ga kowarete shimatta. Dou ni ka naosenai mono darou ka. This precious clock is out of order. Can't we fix it one way or another? क़ीमती घड़ी टूट गई। क्या इसे किसी तरह ठीक नहीं किया जा सकता? કિંમતી ઘડિયાળ તૂટી ગઈ. શું તેને કોઈ રીતે ઠીક ન કરી શકાય?

The parenthetical どうにか／なんとか／もう少し are all optional intensifiers that can be added for extra emotional weight — the core grammar is simply Vないもの（だろう）か, a rhetorical wish/plea.

Q1

お金と時間をかければ、私にだってそれはできない（　）。Okane to jikan o kakereba, watashi ni datte sore wa dekinai (___).

Full sentence: お金と時間をかければ、私にだってそれはできないものでもない。Okane to jikan o kakereba, watashi ni datte sore wa dekinai mono de mo nai.
EN: If I put in enough money and time, it's not impossible for me to do that either.
HI: पैसा और समय लगाया जाए, तो मेरे लिए भी यह करना नामुमकिन नहीं है।
GU: પૈસા અને સમય લગાવવામાં આવે, તો મારા માટે પણ આ કરવું અશક્ય નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aものかmono ka(wrong — this would express strong refusal/rhetorical denial, not a hedge)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bものでもないmono de mo naiit's not impossible thatनामुमकिन नहीं हैઅશક્ય નથી

Why: Leaving open a real possibility given enough resources is Point 2's ないものでもない; ものか is an entirely different, much stronger rhetorical-denial pattern (not covered this week).

Q2

字がきれいに書ける方法は（　）だろうか。Ji ga kirei ni kakeru houhou wa (___) darou ka.

Full sentence: 字がきれいに書ける方法はないものだろうか。Ji ga kirei ni kakeru houhou wa nai mono darou ka.
EN: Isn't there some way to write neatly?
HI: क्या साफ़-सुथरी लिखावट सीखने का कोई तरीक़ा नहीं है?
GU: શું સ્વચ્છ અક્ષર લખવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aないものnai monoisn't there some wayकोई तरीक़ा नहीं है क्याકોઈ રસ્તો નથી શું

Why: Wishing for a method to exist is Point 4's ないものだろうか; the fixed pattern always uses もの, never こと.

Q3

Full sentence: 彼女が腹を立てるのもわからなくはないが、そんなに怒らなくてもいいだろう。Kanojo ga hara o tateru no mo wakaranaku wa nai ga, sonna ni okoranakute mo ii darou.
EN: I can understand her getting angry to some extent, but she doesn't need to be that mad.
HI: उसका गुस्सा होना समझ में आता है, लेकिन इतना ग़ुस्सा करने की ज़रूरत नहीं थी।
GU: તેનો ગુસ્સો સમજી શકાય એવો છે, પણ આટલો ગુસ્સો કરવાની જરૂર નહોતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

bわからないものだwakaranai mono da(wrong — this would just mean "it's incomprehensible")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

彼女の声の大きいのはもう少しどうにか（　）ものだろうか。Kanojo no koe no ookii no wa mou sukoshi dou ni ka (___) mono darou ka.

GU: તેના મોટા અવાજને થોડો ઓછો કરવાનો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી શું?

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

このパソコンは高いが性能がいいので、値引き率＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ない。Kono pasokon wa takai ga seinou ga ii node, nebikiritsu ___ nai.

Tiles: 1 買わない (kawanai)　2 によって (ni yotte)　3 は (wa)　4 ものでも (mono de mo)

2 によって→3 は→1 買わない→4 ものでも

このパソコンは高いが性能がいいので、値引き率によっては買わないものでもない。Kono pasokon wa takai ga seinou ga ii node, nebikiritsu ni yotte wa kawanai mono de mo nai.

EN: This computer is expensive but has good performance, so depending on the discount rate, buying it isn't out of the question.
HI: यह कंप्यूटर महँगा है लेकिन अच्छा है, तो छूट के हिसाब से इसे ख़रीदना नामुमकिन नहीं है।
GU: આ કમ્પ્યુટર મોંઘું છે પણ સારું છે, તો છૂટના આધારે તેને ખરીદવું અશક્ય નથી.

Why: Built on Point 2's ないものでもない — buying remains a real possibility depending on the discount.

Q7

これは、ほかの＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ がいいですよ。Kore wa, hoka no ___ ga ii desu yo.

Tiles: 1 比べもの (kurabemono)　2 品質 (hinshitsu)　3 にならないほど (ni naranai hodo)　4 とは (towa)

4 とは→1 比べもの→3 にならないほど→2 品質

これは、ほかのとは比べものにならないほど品質がいいですよ。Kore wa, hoka no towa kurabemono ni naranai hodo hinshitsu ga ii desu yo.

EN: This is of far better quality than any comparison with others — incomparably good.
HI: इसकी क्वालिटी बाक़ी चीज़ों से तुलना से भी परे, बहुत अच्छी है।
GU: આની ક્વોલિટી બીજી ચીજો સાથે સરખામણીથી પણ પર, ખૂબ સારી છે.

Why: Built on Point 3's 比べものにならない — the quality gap is so large it defies comparison.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Points 1–2 both use double/soft negatives to affirm something roundaboutly, while Points 3–4 make no attempt at hedging at all:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜なくはない／なくもない~naku wa nai/naku mo nai It's not that ~ isn't the case Aく／naで／V／Vられ+なく+はない／もない A general, everyday hedge about a fact/feeling. "I do understand, to some extent."
Vないものでもない~nai mono de mo nai It's not impossible that ~ [硬] Vない（もの）でもない Specifically leaves open a real possibility for a future action. "I might take the job, depending on conditions."
Nとは比べものにならないN to wa kurabemono ni naranai Cannot be compared to ~ N + とは比べものにならない A direct, unhedged claim about an extreme difference. "Today's cars vs. old ones — no comparison."
Vないもの（だろう）か~nai mono (darou) ka Isn't there some way to ~? [硬] （どうにか）（なんとか） + Vないもの（だろう）か A wish/plea for a solution, not an affirmation at all. "Can't something be done about this smell?"
Exam trap: Points 1 and 2 both look like plain negatives at first glance but actually lean positive/possible — a common N1 reading trap is translating わからなくはない or 買わないものでもない too literally as "don't understand" or "won't buy." Always double-check: does the sentence structure end in a double negative (なく+はない/もない, ない+でもない)? If so, flip your first instinct — it's a hedge toward "yes," not a "no."
