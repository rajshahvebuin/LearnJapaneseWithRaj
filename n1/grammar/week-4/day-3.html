Notes
A day of four noun-suffixes, none formally tagged 硬, but each with its own fixed-idiom trap the book flags directly: 黒だらけ and ほこりずくめ are both explicitly marked ダメ (wrong) in the source — see the notes under Points 1–2 and the confusion table in §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nずくめ

neutral register
ReadingN zukume
Meaning (EN)"entirely/all ~ (nothing but)" — describes something as consisting entirely of one thing, often overwhelming
Meaning (HI)बिल्कुल...से भरा हुआ (सिर्फ़ वही, कुछ और नहीं)
Meaning (GU)સંપૂર્ણપણે...થી ભરેલું (ફક્ત એ જ, બીજું કંઈ નહીં)
Connection (接続)N + ずくめ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing a stretch of time or a look as consisting exclusively of one quality — often something notable (all black, all rules, all bad luck).
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
黒ずくめの服装をする。Kuro zukume no fukusou o suru. I dress all in black. मैं पूरी तरह काले रंग के कपड़े पहनता/पहनती हूँ। હું સંપૂર્ણપણે કાળા રંગનાં કપડાં પહેરું છું.
規則ずくめのこの学校をやめたい。Kisoku zukume no kono gakkou o yametai. I want to quit my school because there are too many rules. नियमों से भरे इस स्कूल को छोड़ना चाहता/चाहती हूँ। નિયમોથી ભરેલી આ સ્કૂલ છોડવા માંગું છું.
去年は悪いことずくめの年だった。今年はいい年でありますように。Kyonen wa warui koto zukume no toshi datta. Kotoshi wa ii toshi de arimasu you ni. There were a lot of disasters last year. Let's hope this year will be a good one. पिछला साल सिर्फ़ बुरी घटनाओं से भरा था। इस साल अच्छा साल हो, यही उम्मीद है। ગયું વર્ષ ફક્ત ખરાબ ઘટનાઓથી ભરેલું હતું. આ વર્ષ સારું રહે, એવી આશા છે.

Notes (れい): 白ずくめ, 記録ずくめ, いいことずくめ, ごちそうずくめ, 異例ずくめ. The book explicitly flags two traps here: 黒だらけ is marked ダメ (wrong) for Example 1 — だらけ implies messy scattering, not a deliberate uniform look, so ずくめ is required instead; and ほこりずくめ is marked ダメ, correcting to ほこりだらけ (dust doesn't come in a "uniform," it's scattered mess). See §3 for the full ずくめ vs だらけ vs まみれ contrast.

【2】 Nまみれ

neutral register
ReadingN mamire
Meaning (EN)"covered all over in ~" — describes being thoroughly covered by something dirty/unpleasant
Meaning (HI)...से पूरी तरह लथपथ/ढका हुआ (गंदी चीज़)
Meaning (GU)...થી સંપૂર્ણપણે લપેટાયેલું/ઢંકાયેલું (ગંદી ચીજ)
Connection (接続)N + まみれ
RegisterNeutral
Typical use◆汚いものがたくさん付いているという状態を表す (describes a state where a lot of dirty things are stuck to something) — always a liquid, dust, or debt-like coating.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大変だ！女性が血まみれになって倒れている。Taihen da! Josei ga chi mamire ni natte taorete iru. My goodness! There is a woman lying on the ground covered with blood. अरे बाप रे! एक महिला ख़ून से लथपथ ज़मीन पर पड़ी है। અરે! એક સ્ત્રી લોહીથી લથબથ જમીન પર પડી છે.
彼は汗まみれになって、一生懸命サッカーの練習をしている。Kare wa ase mamire ni natte, isshoukenmei sakkaa no renshuu o shite iru. He is practicing soccer very hard so he is covered with sweat. वह पसीने से तरबतर होकर, बहुत मेहनत से फ़ुटबॉल की प्रैक्टिस कर रहा है। તે પરસેવાથી લથબથ થઈને, ખૂબ મહેનતથી ફૂટબોલની પ્રેક્ટિસ કરી રહ્યો છે.

Notes (れい): 泥まみれ, ほこりまみれ, 借金まみれ (buried in debt — a figurative extension of the "covered in something unpleasant" image).

【3】 Nぐるみ

neutral register
ReadingN gurumi
Meaning (EN)"the whole of ~ together / involving all of ~" — describes an activity/relationship involving an entire group, not just individuals
Meaning (HI)पूरे...समुदाय के साथ (सिर्फ़ व्यक्ति नहीं, सभी सहित)
Meaning (GU)સંપૂર્ણ...સમુદાય સાથે (ફક્ત વ્યક્તિ નહીં, બધા સહિત)
Connection (接続)N + ぐるみ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizing collective involvement — a whole family, town, or organization acting together, sometimes even complicit together.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼とは長年家族ぐるみの付き合いをしている。Kare to wa naganen kazoku gurumi no tsukiai o shite iru. He and I have been good friends for a long time and so have our families. उससे बरसों से पूरे परिवार के साथ दोस्ती चली आ रही है। તેની સાથે વર્ષોથી સંપૂર્ણ પરિવાર સાથે મિત્રતા ચાલી આવે છે.
子どもたちの非行を減らすために、町ぐるみで活動をしている。Kodomotachi no hikou o herasu tame ni, machi gurumi de katsudou o shite iru. We use different strategies in order to reduce crime among the young people in our town. बच्चों की बदसलूकी कम करने के लिए, पूरा शहर मिलकर काम कर रहा है। બાળકોની ગેરવર્તણૂક ઘટાડવા માટે, સંપૂર્ણ શહેર ભેગું થઈને કામ કરી રહ્યું છે.

Notes (れい): 地域ぐるみ, 組織ぐるみ. Fixed idiom: 身ぐるみはがされる (=身に着けているものも全部とられる) "to be stripped of everything one has, right down to one's clothes."

【4】 N並み

neutral register
ReadingN nami
Meaning (EN)"on par with ~ / at the same level as ~" — compares something favorably to a standard/benchmark
Meaning (HI)...के बराबर / ...जैसे स्तर का
Meaning (GU)...ની બરાબર / ...જેવા સ્તરનું
Connection (接続)N + 並み
RegisterNeutral
Typical useComparing skill, speed, price, or weather to a well-known benchmark.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
社長は将棋が趣味で、腕前はプロ並みだということだ。Shachou wa shougi ga shumi de, udemae wa puro nami da to iu koto da. The president's hobby is shogi and I hear he plays at the professional level. अध्यक्ष का शौक़ शोगी है, और सुना है उनका खेल पेशेवर स्तर का है। અધ્યક્ષનો શોખ શોગી છે, અને સાંભળ્યું છે તેમની રમત વ્યાવસાયિક સ્તરની છે.
裕福ではありませんが、世間並みの生活をしています。Yuufuku de wa arimasen ga, seken nami no seikatsu o shite imasu. I am not rich but I make a decent living. अमीर नहीं हूँ, लेकिन औसत लोगों जैसी ज़िंदगी जी रहा/रही हूँ। અમીર નથી, પણ સામાન્ય લોકો જેવું જીવન જીવી રહ્યો/રહી છું.

Notes (れい): 新幹線並みのスピード, 日本人並みに日本語を話す, 休日並みの混雑, 平年並みの気温. Fixed idiom: 十人並み (=平均的) "average-looking/ordinary" — literally "on par with [any] ten [ordinary] people."

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

旅行をしている間ずっと、ごちそう（　）で、少し太ってしまいました。Ryokou o shite iru aida zutto, gochisou (___) de, sukoshi futotte shimaimashita.

Full sentence: 旅行をしている間ずっと、ごちそうずくめで、少し太ってしまいました。Ryokou o shite iru aida zutto, gochisou zukume de, sukoshi futotte shimaimashita.
EN: The whole trip was nothing but feasting, so I gained a bit of weight.
HI: पूरी यात्रा में सिर्फ़ शानदार खाना ही खाना मिला, तो थोड़ा वज़न बढ़ गया।
GU: સંપૂર્ણ સફરમાં ફક્ત ભોજન જ ભોજન હતું, તો થોડું વજન વધી ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまみれmamirecovered in (wrong — feasting isn't a dirty coating)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bずくめzukumenothing butसिर्फ़ वहीફક્ત એ જ

Why: A trip consisting entirely of feasting is Point 1's ずくめ; まみれ is reserved for being physically covered in something unpleasant, which doesn't fit "feasting."

Q2

初めてのスキーでは転んでばかりで、雪（　）になってしまった。Hajimete no sukii de wa koronde bakari de, yuki (___) ni natte shimatta.

GU: પહેલી વાર સ્કીઇંગ કરતાં વારંવાર પડ્યો અને સંપૂર્ણપણે બરફથી ઢંકાઈ ગયો.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

彼は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ してしまった。Kare wa ___ shite shimatta.

Tiles: 1 まみれに (mamire ni)　2 なったあげく (natta ageku)　3 破産 (hasan)　4 借金 (shakkin)

4 借金→1 まみれに→2 なったあげく→3 破産

彼は借金まみれになったあげく破産してしまった。Kare wa shakkin mamire ni natta ageku hasan shite shimatta.

EN: He ended up buried in debt and eventually went bankrupt.
HI: वह क़र्ज़ में डूबता चला गया और आख़िर में दिवालिया हो गया।
GU: તે દેવામાં ડૂબતો ગયો અને છેવટે નાદાર થઈ ગયો.

Why: Built on Point 2's まみれ — 借金まみれ ("buried in debt") is a common figurative extension of the "covered in something unpleasant" image.

Q7

彼女は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ をしている。Kanojo wa ___ o shite iru.

Tiles: 1 としては (to shite wa)　2 女優 (joyuu)　3 十人並みの (juuninnami no)　4 容姿 (youshi)

2 女優→1 としては→3 十人並みの→4 容姿

彼女は女優としては十人並みの容姿をしている。Kanojo wa joyuu to shite wa juuninnami no youshi o shite iru.

EN: As an actress, she has a fairly average appearance.
HI: एक अभिनेत्री के तौर पर, उसका रूप-रंग औसत ही है।
GU: એક અભિનેત્રી તરીકે, તેનો દેખાવ સામાન્ય જ છે.

Why: Built on Point 4's 並み — 十人並み ("average-looking") is the fixed idiom form.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

ずくめ and まみれ both describe being thoroughly covered/consisted of something, but the book itself flags exactly where they cannot substitute for だらけ (a related but distinct pattern from outside this week):

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
NずくめN zukume Entirely/all ~ N + ずくめ A deliberate, uniform quality throughout (all black, all rules) — not messy scattering. "Dressed all in black," "a year of nothing but bad news."
NまみれN mamire Covered all over in ~ N + まみれ A dirty liquid/substance (or figurative debt) thoroughly coating something. "Covered in blood/sweat/sand," "buried in debt."
NぐるみN gurumi The whole of ~ together N + ぐるみ Collective involvement of an entire group, sometimes complicit. "The whole family/town/company."
N並みN nami On par with ~ N + 並み A comparison to a benchmark standard — no coating or collectivity implied. "Professional-level," "average-looking."
Exam trap: The book flags two explicit ダメ examples worth memorizing directly: 黒だらけ is wrong for "dressed all in black" (だらけ implies messy scattering of separate things, like litter or mistakes — a deliberate all-black outfit needs ずくめ); ほこりずくめ is wrong for "covered in dust" (dust scatters messily, so it needs だらけ or まみれ, not the "uniform" feel of ずくめ). The underlying rule: ずくめ = a deliberate uniform quality; だらけ/まみれ = messy scattered coverage.
