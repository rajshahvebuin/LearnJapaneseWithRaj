問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

医者（　）者が金もうけばかり考えてはいけない。Isha (___) mono ga kanemouke bakari kangaete wa ikenai.

Full sentence: 医者たる者が金もうけばかり考えてはいけない。Isha taru mono ga kanemouke bakari kangaete wa ikenai.
EN: Those of the standing of a doctor shouldn't only think about making money.
HI: डॉक्टर होने की हैसियत वालों को सिर्फ़ पैसा कमाने के बारे में नहीं सोचना चाहिए।
GU: ડોક્ટર હોવાની હેસિયતવાળાએ ફક્ત પૈસા કમાવવા વિશે જ ન વિચારવું જોઈએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1すらsuraeven (wrong — no extreme example being cited)
2にしたni shita(not a valid form here)
3であろうde arou(not a valid combination)
4たるtaruof the standing of

Why: Week 4 Day 2's N1たるN2 criticizes someone for not living up to their professional status.

Q2

高校生（　）、ファッションに敏感になるものだ。Koukousei (___), fasshon ni binkan ni naru mono da.

Full sentence: 高校生ともなると、ファッションに敏感になるものだ。Koukousei to mo naru to, fasshon ni binkan ni naru mono da.
EN: Once one becomes a high schooler, one naturally becomes sensitive to fashion.
HI: हाई स्कूल की उम्र आते ही, फ़ैशन को लेकर सजग हो जाते हैं।
GU: હાઈસ્કૂલની ઉંમર આવતાં જ, ફેશન અંગે સજાગ થઈ જવાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にしてni shiteeven/at the same time (wrong — no authority or dual role being cited)
2ともなるとto mo naru toonce it becomes
3にあってni atteunder the circumstances of (wrong — no formal scene-setting here)
4ならではnara de waunique to (wrong — not describing an exclusive quality)

Why: Reaching the threshold of "high schooler" naturally changes one's fashion sensitivity — Week 4 Day 2's ともなると.

Q3

家で楽しく運動できる（　）、そのゲームの人気が高いのもうなずける。Ie de tanoshiku undou dekiru (___), sono geemu no ninki ga takai no mo unazukeru.

Full sentence: 家で楽しく運動できるとあれば、そのゲームの人気が高いのもうなずける。Ie de tanoshiku undou dekiru to areba, sono geemu no ninki ga takai no mo unazukeru.
EN: Given that one can exercise enjoyably at home, it's understandable that the game is so popular.
HI: अगर घर पर मज़े से कसरत हो सकती है, तो उस गेम का लोकप्रिय होना समझ में आता है।
GU: જો ઘરે મજાથી કસરત થઈ શકે, તો તે ગેમનું લોકપ્રિય હોવું સમજી શકાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とあればto arebaif it's the case that
2とあろうとto arou to(not a valid form)
3となってto natte(incomplete/wrong fit)
4となろうとto narou to(not a valid fit here)

Why: Week 4 Day 4's とあれば sets up the condition that makes the game's popularity understandable.

Q4

魚（　）漁業なのに、最近は魚が少なくなってしまった。Sakana (___) gyogyou na noni, saikin wa sakana ga sukunaku natte shimatta.

Full sentence: 魚あっての漁業なのに、最近は魚が少なくなってしまった。Sakana atte no gyogyou na noni, saikin wa sakana ga sukunaku natte shimatta.
EN: It's a fishing industry that exists only because of fish, yet recently fish have become scarce.
HI: यह मछली की वजह से ही मौजूद मछली-पालन उद्योग है, फिर भी हाल में मछलियाँ कम हो गई हैं।
GU: આ માછલીના કારણે જ અસ્તિત્વમાં માછીમારી ઉદ્યોગ છે, છતાં હાલમાં માછલીઓ ઓછી થઈ ગઈ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1あってのatte noonly existing because of
2なくしてはnakushite wawithout ~, there is no (wrong grammatical fit here)
3ぐるみのgurumi nothe whole of ~ together (wrong sense)
4からするkara surucosting at least (wrong — 漁業 isn't a price)

Why: Week 4 Day 4's N1あってのN2 — the fishing industry only exists because fish exist.

Q5

海外に転勤になった。（　）が、楽しみでもある。Kaigai ni tenkin ni natta. (___) ga, tanoshimi de mo aru.

Full sentence: 海外に転勤になった。不安もなくはないが、楽しみでもある。Kaigai ni tenkin ni natta. Fuan mo naku wa nai ga, tanoshimi de mo aru.
EN: I got transferred abroad. It's not that there's no anxiety at all, but it's also something to look forward to.
HI: विदेश तबादला हो गया। चिंता बिल्कुल नहीं है ऐसा नहीं है, लेकिन यह ख़ुशी की बात भी है।
GU: વિદેશ બદલી થઈ ગઈ. ચિંતા બિલકુલ નથી એવું નથી, પણ આ ખુશીની વાત પણ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1不安がないものだfuan ga nai mono da(states flatly there's no anxiety — contradicts the hedge needed)
2不安なくしてはないfuan nakushite wa nai(wrong pattern/meaning)
3不安すらないfuan sura nai(states flatly there's not even anxiety — too strong)
4不安もなくはないfuan mo naku wa naiit's not that there's no anxiety at all

Why: Week 4 Day 6's double-negative なくはない softly admits some anxiety exists, fitting the contrast with "but it's also exciting."

Q6

私の本が、多少（　）若い人たちにいい影響を与えているとしたらうれしい限りです。Watashi no hon ga, tashou (___) wakai hitotachi ni ii eikyou o ataete iru to shitara ureshii kagiri desu.

Full sentence: 私の本が、多少なりとも若い人たちにいい影響を与えているとしたらうれしい限りです。...tashou naritomo wakai hitotachi ni...
EN: If my book is having even a slight good influence on young people, I'd be extremely happy.
HI: अगर मेरी किताब का युवाओं पर थोड़ा-सा भी अच्छा असर हो रहा हो, तो मुझे बहुत ख़ुशी होगी।
GU: જો મારા પુસ્તકની યુવાનો પર થોડી પણ સારી અસર થઈ રહી હોય, તો મને ખૂબ ખુશી થશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とあればto arebaif it's the case that (wrong — doesn't fit 多少's meaning)
2とあってto attegiven that it's (wrong fit)
3なりともnaritomoeven just a little
4なろうともnarou tomo(not a valid fit here)

Why: Week 4 Day 5's 多少なりとも is the fixed collocation meaning "even just a little."

Q7

彼女の悪口など一言（　）言ったことはありませんよ。Kanojo no waruguchi nado hitokoto (___) itta koto wa arimasen yo.

Full sentence: 彼女の悪口など一言たりとも言ったことはありませんよ。Kanojo no waruguchi nado hitokoto taritomo itta koto wa arimasen yo.
EN: I've never said even a single word of criticism about her.
HI: मैंने उसके बारे में एक शब्द भी बुरा नहीं कहा है।
GU: મેં તેના વિશે એક શબ્દ પણ ખરાબ કહ્યો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とあってはto atte wa(not a valid fit here)
2からありkara ari(not a valid combination)
3からともkara tomo(not a valid combination)
4たりともtaritomonot even (the smallest amount)

Why: Week 4 Day 5's たりとも denies even the smallest unit — one word.

Q8

事件を起こしたのは彼だが、私に責任が（　）。Jiken o okoshita no wa kare da ga, watashi ni sekinin ga (___).

Full sentence: 事件を起こしたのは彼だが、私に責任がないものでもない。Jiken o okoshita no wa kare da ga, watashi ni sekinin ga nai mono de mo nai.
EN: He's the one who caused the incident, but it's not that I bear no responsibility at all.
HI: घटना उसने की, लेकिन यह भी नहीं कि मेरी कोई ज़िम्मेदारी नहीं है।
GU: ઘટના તેણે કરી, પણ એ પણ નથી કે મારી કોઈ જવાબદારી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1なしにはないnashi ni wa nai(not a valid combination)
2なくしてはないnakushite wa nai(wrong — この文脈に合わない)
3ないものでもないnai mono de mo naiit's not that there's none at all
4なくとはないnaku towa nai(not a valid combination)

Why: Week 4 Day 6's ないものでもない softly admits some responsibility might exist.

Q9

住民の協力（　）、ゴミの削減はできない。Juumin no kyouryoku (___), gomi no sakugen wa dekinai.

Full sentence: 住民の協力なくして、ゴミの削減はできない。Juumin no kyouryoku nakushite, gomi no sakugen wa dekinai.
EN: Without residents' cooperation, waste reduction is impossible.
HI: निवासियों के सहयोग के बिना, कचरा कम करना संभव नहीं है।
GU: રહેવાસીઓના સહયોગ વગર, કચરો ઘટાડવો શક્ય નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にしてni shiteeven/at the same time (wrong fit)
2ならではnara de waunique to (wrong sense)
3なくしてnakushitewithout ~, there is no
4ないものをnai mono o(wrong — this expresses regret, not a necessary precondition)

Why: Week 4 Day 1's なくして asserts residents' cooperation as an essential precondition.

Q10

A社の今回の人事は異例（　）だった。A-sha no konkai no jinji wa irei (___) datta.

Full sentence: A社の今回の人事は異例ずくめだった。A-sha no konkai no jinji wa irei zukume datta.
EN: This personnel decision by Company A was full of irregularities.
HI: कंपनी A का यह मानव संसाधन फ़ैसला पूरी तरह असाधारण था।
GU: કંપની A નો આ કર્મચારી નિર્ણય સંપૂર્ણપણે અસાધારણ હતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ずくめzukumeentirely/all
2まみれmamirecovered all over in (wrong — 異例 isn't a dirty coating)
3ならではnara de waunique to (wrong sense)
4ぐるみgurumithe whole of ~ together (wrong — no group involvement here)

Why: Week 4 Day 3's ずくめ describes a decision consisting entirely of irregularities.

Q11

国際化の時代（　）、我が社の保守的なやり方は世界に通用しないのではないだろうか。Kokusaika no jidai (___), wagasha no hoshuteki na yarikata wa sekai ni tsuuyou shinai no de wa nai darou ka.

Full sentence: 国際化の時代にあって、我が社の保守的なやり方は世界に通用しないのではないだろうか。...jidai ni atte, wagasha no...
EN: In this era of globalization, isn't it the case that our company's conservative approach won't work internationally?
HI: इस वैश्वीकरण के दौर में, हमारी कंपनी का पुराना तरीक़ा दुनिया में नहीं चलेगा क्या?
GU: આ વૈશ્વિકરણના યુગમાં, અમારી કંપનીની જૂની પદ્ધતિ દુનિયામાં નહીં ચાલે શું?
#JapaneseEnglish
1ですらde suraeven (wrong — no extreme example)
2にあってni atteunder the circumstances of
3なみにnami nion par with (wrong sense)
4なしにnashi niwithout (wrong sense)

Why: Week 4 Day 4's にあって formally sets the scene of "this era of globalization."

Q12

試合中に大雨が降って泥（　）になった。Shiaichuu ni ooame ga futte doro (___) ni natta.

Full sentence: 試合中に大雨が降って泥まみれになった。Shiaichuu ni ooame ga futte doro mamire ni natta.
EN: Heavy rain fell during the match and everyone got covered in mud.
HI: मैच के दौरान भारी बारिश हुई और सब मिट्टी से लथपथ हो गए।
GU: મેચ દરમિયાન ભારે વરસાદ પડ્યો અને બધા માટીથી લથબથ થઈ ગયા.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ずくめzukumeentirely/all (wrong — mud isn't a deliberate uniform look)
2まみれmamirecovered all over in
3ぐるみgurumithe whole of ~ together (wrong sense)
4のだらけno darake(wrong grammatical form — だらけ doesn't take の here)

Q13

大企業（　）、賃金が減らされるような状況だから、ボーナスのカットは避けられないだろう。Daikigyou (___), chingin ga herasareru you na joukyou dakara, boonasu no katto wa sakerarenai darou.

Full sentence: 大企業ともなると、賃金が減らされるような状況だから、ボーナスのカットは避けられないだろう。Daikigyou to mo naru to, chingin ga...
EN: Even when it comes to a major corporation, given that wages are being cut, bonus cuts are probably unavoidable.
HI: एक बड़ी कंपनी की बात हो, तब भी, जब वेतन में कटौती हो रही हो, तो बोनस में कटौती से बचना मुश्किल होगा।
GU: એક મોટી કંપનીની વાત હોય, તો પણ, જ્યારે પગારમાં ઘટાડો થઈ રહ્યો હોય, તો બોનસમાં ઘટાડો ટાળવો મુશ્કેલ હશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とあればto arebaif it's the case that (wrong fit — no devoted willingness here)
2ともなるとto mo naru toonce it comes to
3なりともnaritomoeven just a little (wrong — 大企業 isn't a small quantity)
4にしてni shiteeven/at the same time (wrong fit)

Why: Week 4 Day 2's ともなると describes a shift in circumstance once the scale of "a major corporation" is reached — even they face wage cuts.

Q14

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

相談してくれれば＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ と言われても困る。Soudan shite kurereba ___ ★ ___ to iwarete mo komaru.

Tiles: 1 今頃 (imagoro)　2 何とかした (nanto ka shita)　3 何とかしてくれ (nanto ka shite kure)　4 ものを (mono o)

2 何とかした→4 ものを→★ 1 今頃→3 何とかしてくれ

相談してくれれば何とかしたものを、今頃何とかしてくれと言われても困る。Soudan shite kurereba nanto ka shita mono o, imagoro nanto ka shite kure to iwarete mo komaru.

EN: If you'd consulted me, I could have done something about it, but being told now to "do something about it" is a problem.
HI: पहले बताते तो कुछ हल निकल जाता, अब आकर कुछ करने को कहना मुसीबत है।
GU: પહેલાં જણાવ્યું હોત તો કંઈક ઉકેલ મળી જાત, હવે આવીને કંઈક કરવાનું કહેવું મુસીબત છે.

Why: Built on Week 4 Day 1's ものを — a missed chance for the speaker to have fixed it earlier, now regretted.

Q17

君の気持ちはわからなく＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ ことは認めたほうがいいだろう。Kimi no kimochi wa wakaranaku ___ ★ ___ koto wa mitometa hou ga ii darou.

Tiles: 1 言いすぎた (iisugita)　2 ないが (nai ga)　3 この状況 (kono joukyou)　4 にあって (ni atte)

2 ないが→3 この状況→★ 4 にあって→1 言いすぎた

君の気持ちはわからなくないが、この状況にあって言いすぎたことは認めたほうがいいだろう。Kimi no kimochi wa wakaranaku nai ga, kono joukyou ni atte iisugita koto wa mitometa hou ga ii darou.

EN: I don't fail to understand your feelings, but given the circumstances, you should admit that you said too much.
HI: तुम्हारी भावना समझी जा सकती है, लेकिन इस स्थिति में इतना ज़्यादा कहना मान लेना चाहिए।
GU: તારી લાગણી સમજી શકાય એવી છે, પણ આ સ્થિતિમાં આટલું વધારે કહેવું સ્વીકારવું જોઈએ.

Why: Built on Week 4 Day 6's なくない (a hedge) plus Day 4's にあって (setting the formal scene).

Q18

その映画は、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ ものだった。Sono eiga wa, ___ ★ ___ mono datta.

Tiles: 1 涙なしには (namida nashi ni wa)　2 見られない (mirarenai)　3 とあって (to atte)　4 アカデミー賞候補 (akademii-shou kouho)

4 アカデミー賞候補→3 とあって→★ 1 涙なしには→2 見られない

その映画は、アカデミー賞候補とあって涙なしには見られないものだった。Sono eiga wa, akademii-shou kouho to atte namida nashi ni wa mirarenai mono datta.

EN: That movie, being an Academy Award nominee, was one you couldn't watch without tears.
HI: वह फ़िल्म, ऑस्कर की नामांकित होने की वजह से, बिना आँसू के देखी नहीं जा सकती थी।
GU: તે ફિલ્મ, ઓસ્કારની નામાંકિત હોવાના કારણે, આંસુ વગર જોઈ શકાય એવી નહોતી.

Why: Built on Week 4 Day 4's とあって (explaining the circumstance) plus Week 4 Day 1's なしに (an essential precondition — tears).

Q19
