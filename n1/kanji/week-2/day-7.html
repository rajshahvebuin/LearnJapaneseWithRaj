2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — choose the correct reading of the underlined word (1問4点)

Q1

これは縮尺1/25000の地図です。Kore wa shukushaku 1/25000 no chizu desu.

EN: This is a map at a scale of 1/25,000.
HI: यह 1/25000 के पैमाने का नक़्शा है।
GU: આ 1/25000ના સ્કેલનો નકશો છે.
#ReadingNote
1しゅくしゃくshukushakucorrect — 尺 reads シャク (Week 2 Day 3's 尺 cluster)
2しゅくやくshukuyakunot a real reading of 尺 here
3しゅくたくshukutakuconfuses 尺 with its タク-reading relatives (択・沢)
4しゅくちゃくshukuchakunot a real reading of 尺 here

Why: 縮尺 is しゅくしゃく — 尺 keeps its シャク reading from Day 3's 尺 cluster (縮尺, 尺度, 解釈).

Q2

最先端の技術を駆使した映像がすばらしい。Saisentan no gijutsu o kushi shita eizou ga subarashii.

EN: The footage making full use of cutting-edge technology is wonderful.
HI: अत्याधुनिक तकनीक का पूरा इस्तेमाल करने वाला यह फ़ुटेज शानदार है।
GU: અત્યાધુનિક ટેકનોલોજીનો સંપૂર્ણ ઉપયોગ કરતું આ ફૂટેજ અદ્ભુત છે.
#ReadingNote
1おうしoushiconfuses 駆 with 欧 family (オウ)
2すうしsuushiconfuses 駆 with 枢 family (スウ)
3くしkushicorrect — 駆 reads ク (Day 3's 区 cluster)
4こうしkoushinot a real reading here

Why: 駆使 is くし, reusing Day 3's 区・駆 pair (both ク) — 区画 and 駆使 share the same reading.

Q3

男は殺人未遂で捕まった。Otoko wa satsujin misui de tsukamatta.

EN: The man was caught for attempted murder.
HI: उस आदमी को हत्या के प्रयास के आरोप में पकड़ा गया।
GU: તે માણસને હત્યાના પ્રયાસ બદલ પકડવામાં આવ્યો.
#ReadingNote
1みたいmitainot a real reading here
2みついmitsuinot a real reading here
3みすいmisuicorrect — 遂 reads スイ (Day 4's 隊・墜・遂 cluster)
4みずいmizuinot a real reading here

Why: 未遂 is みすい, reusing Day 4's 遂 (スイ, as in 遂行) — "attempted but not completed."

Q4

虫や動物の中には脱皮して成長するものがある。Mushi ya doubutsu no naka ni wa dappi shite seichou suru mono ga aru.

EN: Some insects and animals grow by shedding their skin.
HI: कुछ कीड़े और जानवर केंचुली उतारकर बड़े होते हैं।
GU: કેટલાક જીવડાં અને પ્રાણીઓ કાંચળી ઉતારીને મોટા થાય છે.
#ReadingNote
1だっぱdappanot a real reading here
2だつひdatsuhimissing the small っ sound shift
3だつはdatsuhanot a real reading here
4だっぴdappicorrect — 皮 reads ヒ, shifting to ぴ after the small っ (Day 1's 皮 cluster)

Why: 脱皮 is だっぴ — 皮 (ヒ, from Day 1's 皮 cluster: 皮革製品, 疲労, 披露) shifts to ぴ after the small っ, the same sound-change pattern Day 1 warned about with 電波 (でんぱ).

Q5

今年の新入部員は根性がありそうだ。Kotoshi no shin'nyuu buin wa konjou ga arisou da.

EN: This year's new club members seem to have grit.
HI: इस साल के नए सदस्यों में जीवट दिखता है।
GU: આ વર્ષના નવા સભ્યોમાં જુસ્સો દેખાય છે.
#ReadingNote
1こんせいkonseiapplies 性's more common セイ reading, but wrong here
2こんじょうkonjoucorrect — 性 reads ショウ in this fixed compound (Day 5's 生・性 cluster)
3こせいkoseinot a real reading here
4ねしょうneshounot a real reading here

Why: 根性 is こんじょう — 性 defaults to セイ (性格) but flips to ショウ here, exactly Day 5's "one kanji, two readings" lesson.

Q6

この飲み物は疲労回復に効きます。Kono nomimono wa hirou kaifuku ni kikimasu.

EN: This drink is effective for recovering from fatigue.
HI: यह पेय थकान दूर करने में असरदार है।
GU: આ પીણું થાક દૂર કરવામાં અસરકારક છે.
#ReadingNote
1はろうharouconfuses 疲 with 波 family (Day 1)
2ひろうhiroucorrect — 疲 reads ヒ (Day 1's 皮 cluster: 疲労, homophone of 披露)
3ふろうfurounot a real reading here
4へろうherounot a real reading here

Why: 疲労 is ひろう, the exact Day 1 homophone pair with 披露 — both read the same way but mean completely different things.

Q7

1級の試験に挑戦しよう。Ikkyuu no shiken ni chousen shiyou.

EN: Let's take on the challenge of the level-1 exam.
HI: चलो लेवल-1 परीक्षा की चुनौती स्वीकार करें।
GU: ચાલો લેવલ-1 પરીક્ષાનો પડકાર સ્વીકારીએ.
#ReadingNote
1ちょうせんchousencorrect — 挑 reads チョウ (Day 1's 兆・挑・眺 cluster)
2とうせんtousenconfuses 挑 with its トウ-reading relatives (逃・桃)
3ちゅうせんchuusena real word (抽選, drawing lots) but wrong kanji here
4ふんせんfunsennot a real reading here

Why: 挑戦 is ちょうせん — 挑 keeps チョウ from Day 1's 兆 family, unlike 逃・桃 (トウ) built on the very same 兆 shape.

Q8

その美しい渓谷は観光名所となっている。Sono utsukushii keikoku wa kankou meisho to natte iru.

EN: That beautiful ravine has become a tourist attraction.
HI: वह ख़ूबसूरत घाटी एक पर्यटन स्थल बन गई है।
GU: તે સુંદર ખીણ પ્રવાસન સ્થળ બની ગઈ છે.
#ReadingNote
1けいゆうkeiyuuconfuses 谷 with its ユウ-reading relative (裕)
2けいよくkeiyokuconfuses 谷 with its ヨク-reading relative (欲)
3けいぞくkeizokuconfuses 谷 with its ゾク-reading relative (俗)
4けいこくkeikokucorrect — 谷 reads コク (Day 4's 谷・欲・俗・裕 cluster)

Why: 渓谷 is けいこく — this question directly tests Day 4's four-way split of 谷 (コク／ヨク／ゾク／ユウ) by offering each wrong reading as a distractor.

Q9

それに関する根拠のないうわさが広まっている。Sore ni kansuru konkyo no nai uwasa ga hiromatte iru.

EN: A baseless rumor about it is spreading.
HI: उसके बारे में एक निराधार अफ़वाह फैल रही है।
GU: તેના વિશે એક પાયાવિહોણી અફવા ફેલાઈ રહી છે.
#ReadingNote
1こんきょkonkyocorrect — 拠 reads キョ in 根拠 (Day 4's 処・拠 cluster)
2こんしょkonshoconfuses 拠 with 処 (ショ)
3こんこkonkowrongly applies 拠's コ reading from 証拠 — but that's a fixed exception, not the default
4こんちょkonchonot a real reading here

Why: 根拠 is こんきょ — 拠's default reading is キョ (根拠, 占拠); it only becomes コ in the one fixed exception 証拠, which Day 4 flagged with △.

Q10

あの店は安いし、雰囲気もいい。Ano mise wa yasui shi, fun'iki mo ii.

EN: That shop is cheap, and the atmosphere is nice too.
HI: वह दुकान सस्ती भी है और माहौल भी अच्छा है।
GU: તે દુકાન સસ્તી પણ છે અને વાતાવરણ પણ સારું છે.
#ReadingNote
1ぶいきbuikinot a real reading here
2ふんいきfun'ikicorrect — 雰 reads フン (Day 6's 分・粉・紛・雰・盆 cluster)
3ふいきfuikidrops the ん sound incorrectly
4ぶんいきbun'ikiwrongly applies 分's more common ブン reading

Why: 雰囲気 is ふんいき — 雰 keeps フン from Day 6's 分 family, even though the more common member 分析 reads ブン.

問題2 — choose the correct kanji for the underlined word (1問4点)

Q11

昔の知り合いとぐうぜん町で出会った。Mukashi no shiriai to guuzen machi de deatta.

EN: I happened to run into an old acquaintance in town.
HI: मैं शहर में एक पुराने परिचित से इत्तेफ़ाक़न मिला/मिली।
GU: હું શહેરમાં એક જૂના ઓળખીતાને સંજોગવશાત્ મળ્યો/મળી.
#KanjiNote
1隅然guuzenwrong kanji — 隅 means "a corner" (一隅), not "chance"
2偶然guuzencorrect — 偶 is "chance/coincidence" (Day 2's 偶・隅・遇 cluster)
3遇然guuzenwrong kanji — 遇 means "treatment" (待遇), not "chance"
4愚然guuzenwrong kanji — 愚 means "foolish" (愚痴), reads グ not グウ

Why: Day 2's 偶・隅・遇 cluster shares グウ, but only 偶 (chance) fits "coincidentally running into someone" — 隅 and 遇 are unrelated meanings sharing the same shape.

Q12

事故でいしき不明になる。Jiko de ishiki fumei ni naru.

EN: To lose consciousness in an accident.
HI: दुर्घटना में बेहोश हो जाना।
GU: અકસ્માતમાં બેભાન થઈ જવું.
#KanjiNote
1意織ishikiwrong kanji — 織 is "to weave/organize" (組織), unrelated
2意職ishikiwrong kanji — 職 is "occupation" (職業), unrelated
3意識ishikicorrect — 意識 is "consciousness" (Day 2's 億・憶・臆・意 cluster)
4意熾ishikinot a standard word

Why: 織・職 both read シキ／ショク and share a component with 識 (Day 2's 識・織・職 cluster), which is exactly why they're tempting distractors for 意識 here.

Q13

富士山のちょうじょうに立つ。Fujisan no choujou ni tatsu.

EN: To stand at the summit of Mt. Fuji.
HI: फ़ूजी पर्वत की चोटी पर खड़े होना।
GU: ફૂજી પર્વતના શિખર પર ઊભા રહેવું.
#KanjiNote
1停上teijouwrong kanji — 停 is "stop" (停留所), unrelated
2訂上teijouwrong kanji — 訂 is "correction" (訂正), unrelated
3庁上choujouwrong kanji — 庁 is "government office" (省庁), unrelated
4頂上choujoucorrect — 頂 is "summit/top" (Day 3's 寺 family relative 頂)

Why: 停・訂・庁・頂 all share 丁 and could plausibly sound alike to an unsure reader, but only 頂 (a peak/summit) fits "the top of Mt. Fuji."

Q14

初めて太郎に会ったとき、きょうれつな印象を受けた。Hajimete Tarou ni atta toki, kyouretsu na inshou o uketa.

EN: When I first met Tarou, I got an intense impression.
HI: पहली बार तारो से मिलने पर मुझे एक तीव्र प्रभाव मिला।
GU: પહેલી વાર તારોને મળ્યો ત્યારે મને તીવ્ર છાપ પડી.
#KanjiNote
1強列kyouretsuwrong kanji — 列 is "a row/queue" (行列), unrelated
2強例kyoureiwrong kanji — 例 is "an example" (慣例), and also breaks the レツ pattern
3強烈kyouretsucorrect — 烈 is "severe/intense" (Day 2's 列・烈・裂 cluster)
4強裂kyouretsuwrong kanji — 裂 is "to split/tear" (分裂), unrelated

Why: 列・烈・裂 all share 列 and read レツ (Day 2), but only 烈 (intense) fits describing a strong personal impression — 裂 means splitting, and 列 means a queue.

Q15

その国の首相は軍事クーデターでしっきゃくした。Sono kuni no shushou wa gunji kuudetaa de shikkyaku shita.

EN: That country's prime minister fell from power in a military coup.
HI: उस देश के प्रधानमंत्री सैन्य तख़्तापलट में सत्ता से बेदख़ल हो गए।
GU: તે દેશના વડાપ્રધાન લશ્કરી બળવામાં સત્તા પરથી ઊથલી પડ્યા.
#KanjiNote
1失脚shikkyakucorrect — 失 is "to lose" (Day 3's 失 cluster)
2秩脚shikkyakuwrong kanji — 秩 reads チツ (秩序), not シツ
3鉄脚tekkyakuwrong kanji — 鉄 reads テツ (製鉄), not シツ
4迭脚tekkyakuwrong kanji — 迭 reads テツ (更迭), not シツ

Why: 秩・鉄・迭 all share the 失 shape but read チツ／テツ (Day 3's split-reading family), while 失 itself keeps シツ — 失脚 ("downfall from power") needs 失, not its lookalikes.

問題3 — choose the best kanji to fill the blank (1問4点)

Q16

お昼ごはんは（　）自でご用意ください。Ohiru gohan wa (___) ji de goyoui kudasai.

Full sentence: お昼ごはんは各自でご用意ください。Ohiru gohan wa kakuji de goyoui kudasai.
EN: Please prepare your own lunch individually.
HI: कृपया अपना दोपहर का खाना ख़ुद अलग से तैयार करें।
GU: કૃપા કરી પોતાનું બપોરનું ભોજન જાતે તૈયાર કરો.
#KanjiNote
1個ko個自 is not a word
2毎mai毎自 is not a word
3各kakucorrect — 各自 (kakuji, "each person individually")
4全zen全自 is not a word

Why: 各自 (Day 1's 各・客・格 cluster) is the fixed word for "each person on their own."

Q17

彼は年上ですが、職場では後（　）になります。Kare wa toshiue desu ga, shokuba de wa kou(___) ni narimasu.

Full sentence: 彼は年上ですが、職場では後輩になります。Kare wa toshiue desu ga, shokuba de wa kouhai ni narimasu.
EN: He's older than me, but at work he's my junior.
HI: वह उम्र में बड़ा है, लेकिन कार्यस्थल पर वह मुझसे जूनियर है।
GU: તે ઉંમરમાં મોટો છે, પણ કાર્યસ્થળે તે મારો જુનિયર છે.
#KanjiNote
1生sei後生 is a different, unrelated word (afterlife)
2方hou後方 means "the rear," not a person's rank
3君kun後君 is not a standard word
4輩haicorrect — 後輩 (kouhai, "a junior")

Why: 後輩 pairs with 先輩 (Day 1's 練習Ⅱ Q8) — 輩 is the fixed kanji for relative seniority in a group, unrelated to age.

Q18

2（　）3日の旅行に行く。Ni-(___) mikka no ryokou ni iku.

Full sentence: 2泊3日の旅行に行く。Nihaku mikka no ryokou ni iku.
EN: I'm going on a 3-day, 2-night trip.
HI: मैं 3 दिन और 2 रात की यात्रा पर जा रहा/रही हूँ।
GU: હું 3 દિવસ અને 2 રાતની સફર પર જાઉં છું.
#KanjiNote
1百hyakunot a counter for nights
2拍haku拍 counts musical beats (1拍・2拍), not nights
3宿shuku宿 alone isn't used as a night-counter here
4泊hakucorrect — 2泊3日 (nihaku mikka, "3 days/2 nights")

Why: Day 6's 白 cluster showed both 1拍・2拍 (beats) and 1泊・2泊 (nights) — 泊 is the counter for overnight stays, not 拍.

Q19

EN: Tofu made only from high-quality beans tastes different.
HI: केवल उम्दा सोयाबीन से बना टोफ़ू स्वाद में अलग होता है।
GU: ફક્ત ઉચ્ચ ગુણવત્તાવાળા સોયાબીનમાંથી બનેલું ટોફુ સ્વાદમાં અલગ હોય છે.
#KanjiNote
1生sei生質 is not a standard word
2良ryoucorrect — 良質 (ryoushitsu, "high quality")
3正sei正質 is not a standard word
4直choku直質 is not a standard word

Why: 良質 (Day 2's 郎・朗・浪・廊・良 cluster, where 良 breaks from ロウ to リョウ) is the fixed word for "good quality," here read as an adjectival prefix.

Q20

Full sentence: 学生証を見せてください。Gakuseishou o misete kudasai.

HI: कृपया अपना स्टूडेंट आईडी दिखाएँ।

#KanjiNote
1証shoucorrect — 学生証 (gakuseishou, "student ID card")

問題4 — fill each blank from the word bank (1問4点)

［21］や動物を切り開いてその［22］や細胞を観察する［23］学に性能を［24］する顕微鏡が発明されたのは17［25］ごろだとされている。

Bank: そしき　しょくぶつ　はっき　ちしき　かいぼう　せいき　かつやく (7 words for 5 blanks — 2 are distractors)
