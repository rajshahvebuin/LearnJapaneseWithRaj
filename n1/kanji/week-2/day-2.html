Notes
Three of today's four title kanji (違・偉・緯) share 韋 and read イ; the fourth (衛) shares the same 韋 but reads エイ instead — the book's own warning cat calls this out with 行列 (ぎょうれつ, not ぎょうれい/こうれつ): 列-family kanji mostly read レツ, and only 例 among them reads レイ. Watch for that "one exception in the family" shape throughout today.
❶ = a bonus/supplementary entry the book marks as lower-frequency (inferred from the book's own legend on p.9 — not a core word to prioritize)
◆ = an extended compound built from the headword shown just above it

Q1

細胞（　）を顕微鏡で観察する。Saibou (___) o kenbikyou de kansatsu suru.

Full sentence: 細胞分裂を顕微鏡で観察する。Saibou bunretsu o kenbikyou de kansatsu suru.
EN: I observe cell division under a microscope.
HI: मैं माइक्रोस्कोप से कोशिका विभाजन को देखता/देखती हूँ।
GU: હું માઇક્રોસ્કોપ વડે કોષ વિભાજન નિહાળું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a行列gyouretsua procession, queue (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b分裂bunretsua division, splitविभाजनવિભાજન

Why: 細胞分裂 ("cell division") is the fixed biology term; 行列 (a procession/queue) doesn't apply to cells.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps come in two shapes: same-reading pairs from one cluster that mean unrelated things, and same-component families that quietly split into two different on-yomi. Both are pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
相違soui A difference Names a discrepancy between two things being compared. "A difference of opinion," "discrepancies in testimony."
経緯keii Details, circumstances Names the background/timeline leading up to a situation — same イ family, unrelated meaning. "Explaining how an incident unfolded."
疑問gimon A doubt, question An unresolved uncertainty in someone's mind. "I have doubts about this plan."
模擬mogi Mock, simulated An artificial stand-in for the real thing — same ギ reading, unrelated meaning. "A mock exam," "a simulation drill."
憶測okusoku Conjecture, guessing Forming a belief without solid evidence. "Media speculation," "an unfounded guess."
臆病okubyou Cowardly, timid A personality trait of fearfulness — near-identical kanji to 憶, unrelated meaning. "A timid child," "too scared to try."
掲示keiji A notice, posting (read ケイ) The one member of the 曷 family that breaks from カツ. "Post a notice on the board."
恐喝・渇水・褐色kyoukatsu / kassui / kasshoku Threat / water shortage / brown (all read カツ) Same 曷 component as 掲, but three different words all sharing カツ instead. Any 曷-based kanji that isn't 掲.
Exam trap: 相違 (a difference) and 経緯 (background/circumstances) both come from today's title cluster (違・偉・緯, all イ) and are easy to reach for interchangeably in a "explain what happened" sentence — but only 経緯 fits a narrative timeline, and only 相違 fits a comparison. Q4 above is built entirely around this distinction.
