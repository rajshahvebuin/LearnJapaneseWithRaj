Notes
Unlike Week 1 Day 1 (where all three kanji shared one reading, ゴ), today's title mixes two readings: 放・訪 are ホウ, while 房・防 are ボウ — the same 方 component splits into two sound families depending on the added radical. Watch that split in every cluster below.

Q1

防犯カメラで店内を（　）する。Bouhan kamera de tennai o (___) suru.

Full sentence: 防犯カメラで店内を監視する。Bouhan kamera de tennai o kanshi suru.
EN: The inside of the store is monitored with security cameras.
HI: सुरक्षा कैमरों से दुकान के अंदर की निगरानी की जाती है।
GU: સુરક્ષા કેમેરાથી દુકાનની અંદરની દેખરેખ રાખવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a監視kanshisurveillance, watchingनिगरानीદેખરેખ
b鑑賞kanshouart appreciation (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Security cameras 監視する (keep watch over) a store for wrongdoing; 鑑賞 is specifically for appreciating art or scenery, not surveillance.

GU: મને રસ હોય તેવા કેસની સુનાવણીમાં હું હાજર રહું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a防衛boueidefence (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 裁判を傍聴する is the fixed collocation for attending a trial as an observer; 防衛 (defence) doesn't pair with 裁判 in this sense.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a逃亡toubouescape, flight (wrong collocation)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 非行に走る ("to fall into delinquency") is a fixed idiom for young people going astray; 逃亡 means fleeing/escaping and doesn't fit "走らない" in this idiomatic sense.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's clusters produce two kinds of traps: same-reading pairs that sound identical but mean different things, and same-component pairs that look identical but are read differently. Both are pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
疲労hirou Fatigue A physical/mental state of tiredness — same reading as 披露, unrelated meaning. "Fatigue recovery," "accumulated tiredness."
披露hirou An announcement, unveiling Publicly presenting or announcing something (a skill, a wedding). "Wedding reception (披露宴)," "unveiling a talent."
監視kanshi Surveillance, watching for wrongdoing Watching to guard against a problem — vigilant, suspicious watching. Security cameras, monitoring a process.
鑑賞kanshou Appreciation, enjoyment Watching/experiencing something for pleasure — art, film, scenery. "Art appreciation," "enjoying a film."
防衛bouei Defence Protecting something from attack or loss. National defence, defending a title.
妨害bougai Obstruction, disturbance Actively blocking or interfering with something. "Signal jamming," "obstruction of business."
郡gun A district (administrative unit) A geographic/administrative division — used in place names. "○○郡" as part of an address.
群gun A crowd, group A mass of people or things — not a fixed administrative unit. "群衆" (a crowd), "群れ" (a herd/flock).
兆・挑・眺chou Trillion / challenge / view Share the 兆 component and the reading チョウ. Any word built on 兆 that isn't 逃 or 桃.
逃・桃tou Flee / peach Share the very same 兆 component but read トウ instead — the exception to the チョウ pattern above. 逃亡 (escape), 白桃 (white peach).
Exam trap: 疲労 and 披露 are a perfect same-reading, zero-relation pair (ひろう read the same way, meaning totally different things) — the single most testable trap in this cluster, and it shows up again in 練習Ⅱ Q7. Whenever a sentence gives you ひろう, check whether it's about tiredness (→疲労) or a public announcement/unveiling (→披露) before picking kanji.
