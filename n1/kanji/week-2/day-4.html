4日目 — 欲・俗・裕 (Yoku, Zoku, Yuu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.34–35 (PDF pages 34–35; PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
14 kanji clusters that share a component but split into two or three different readings — the opposite trap from Days 1–3
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
The book's title for today's page is literally "同じ部分があっても読みが違うもの" — kanji that share a component but are read differently. Days 1–3 taught you to expect a shared reading from a shared shape; today teaches you the opposite: 曜・濯・躍 all contain 翟 but read ヨウ／タク／ヤク respectively (the opening quiz: 活躍 is かつやく, not かつよう or かつたく). Every cluster below is a warning list, not a shortcut.
❶ = a bonus/supplementary entry the book marks as lower-frequency (inferred from the book's own legend on p.9 — not a core word to prioritize)
◆ = an extended compound built from the headword shown just above it
* = a footnote about an alternate reading or usage
△ = the book's own marker for today's tables, meaning "just memorize this one fixed compound's reading — don't generalize it to other words with the same kanji"

Q1

（　）不十分で不起訴になる。(___) fujuubun de fukiso ni naru.

Full sentence: 証拠不十分で不起訴になる。Shouko fujuubun de fukiso ni naru.
EN: The case ends in non-prosecution due to insufficient evidence.
HI: पर्याप्त सबूत न होने के कारण मुक़दमा दायर नहीं होता।
GU: પૂરતા પુરાવાના અભાવે કેસ દાખલ થતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a処分shobundisposal, punitive action (wrong fixed phrase)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b証拠shoukoevidenceसबूतપુરાવો

Why: 証拠不十分 ("insufficient evidence") is a fixed legal phrase leading to non-prosecution; 処分 (disposal/punishment) is not what's described as "insufficient" here.

Q2

待望の赤ちゃんが（　）する。Taibou no akachan ga (___) suru.

Full sentence: 待望の赤ちゃんが誕生する。Taibou no akachan ga tanjou suru.
EN: The long-awaited baby is born.
HI: बहुप्रतीक्षित बच्चा जन्म लेता है।
GU: લાંબા સમયથી પ્રતીક્ષિત બાળકનો જન્મ થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生態seitaiecology (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b誕生tanjoua birthजन्मજન્મ

Why: 赤ちゃんが誕生する ("a baby is born") is the natural collocation; 生態 (ecology) describes how organisms live, not the event of birth.

HI: जाँच पास होने पर क्रेडिट कार्ड जारी किया जाता है।
GU: ચકાસણી પાસ થતાં ક્રેડિટ કાર્ડ જારી કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a審査shinsaan examination, screeningजाँच, समीक्षाચકાસણી
b吟味gimiclose examination (wrong register)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 審査が通る ("to pass a screening/review") is the fixed term for a formal approval process like a credit application; 吟味 means scrutinizing something carefully (words, ingredients) but isn't used for institutional approval processes.

Today's trap isn't "same reading, different kanji" — it's the reverse: one shared shape hiding two or three completely different readings. Pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

Shared shapeSplits intoKey differenceWatch for
翟(taku) 曜 ヨウ / 濯 タク / 躍 ヤク Three unrelated words (weekday, laundry, activity) from one shape. 活躍 is かつやく, never かつよう or かつたく.
谷(koku) 谷 コク / 欲 ヨク / 俗 ゾク / 裕 ユウ Four unrelated words (ravine, desire, custom, wealth) — the widest split this week. Never assume a 谷-based kanji reads like 谷 itself.
軍(gun) 軍 グン / 運 ウン / 揮・輝 キ Military, transport/fate, and command/shine all share one shape. 指揮 is しき, not ぐんき or うんき.
拠 alone(kyo/ko) 根拠・占拠 キョ / 証拠 コ The same single kanji reads differently depending on the compound — no second kanji involved. 証拠 is しょうこ, never しょうきょ.
骨(kotsu) 骨 コツ / 滑 カツ A bone-related word and a smoothness-related word from the same shape. 骨折 (bone fracture) vs 円滑 (smooth) — unrelated meanings entirely.
Exam trap: Today flips the whole week's logic: instead of "same reading → check the meaning," it's "same shape → check the reading first." 谷・欲・俗・裕 is the most extreme case — four kanji, four readings, one shape — and it's exactly the kind of item the 漢字読み section tests by swapping in a plausible-but-wrong reading from a neighbour in the same family.
