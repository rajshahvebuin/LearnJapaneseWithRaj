Notes
Today's trap lives inside single kanji rather than across a family: 生 itself is セイ in 生存 but ショウ in 一生; 登 is トウ in 登録 but ト in 登山 (the day's opening quiz: とざん, not とうざん). The book marks these narrower, fixed-compound-only readings with △ — memorize the specific word, not a general rule.
❶ = a bonus/supplementary entry the book marks as lower-frequency (inferred from the book's own legend on p.9 — not a core word to prioritize)
◆ = an extended compound built from the headword shown just above it
* = a footnote about an alternate reading or usage

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

書類のミスを（　）する。Shorui no misu o (___) suru.

Full sentence: 書類のミスを訂正する。Shorui no misu o teisei suru.
EN: I correct a mistake in the document.
HI: दस्तावेज़ की ग़लती को ठीक किया जाता है।
GU: દસ્તાવેજની ભૂલ સુધારવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a訂正teiseia correction (of an error)त्रुटि सुधारભૂલ સુધારો
b是正zeseia correction (of a systemic disparity, wrong register)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 訂正 is used for correcting a specific error, like a typo in a document; 是正 is reserved for correcting a systemic issue such as a wage gap (格差の是正), not a simple document mistake.

Q2

困難に（　）してもあきらめないで進む。Konnan ni (___) shite mo akiramenaide susumu.

Full sentence: 困難に直面してもあきらめないで進む。Konnan ni chokumen shite mo akiramenaide susumu.
EN: Even when facing difficulty, I press on without giving up.
HI: मुश्किलों का सामना होने पर भी हार माने बिना आगे बढ़ना।
GU: મુશ્કેલીનો સામનો કરવા છતાં હાર માન્યા વગર આગળ વધવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a直面chokumento face (a difficulty)सामना करनाસામનો કરવો
b進路shinroa course, route (not a verb-taking noun here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 困難に直面する ("to face difficulty") is the standard collocation; 進路する is not a natural verb phrase — 進路 is normally just a noun ("one's path/course").

Q3

商売が（　）する。Shoubai ga (___) suru.

Full sentence: 商売が繁盛する。Shoubai ga hanjou suru.
EN: The business thrives.
HI: कारोबार फल-फूल रहा है।
GU: ધંધો ધમધમે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a繁盛hanjouprosperity, thrivingसमृद्धि, फलना-फूलनाસમૃદ્ધિ
b盛大seidaigrand, large-scale (describes events, not business as a verb)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 商売が繁盛する is the fixed phrase for "business thrives"; 盛大 is an adjective describing the scale of an event (a grand ceremony), and doesn't pair naturally with する in this sense.

Q4

EN: About 300 people became victims of this earthquake.
HI: इस बार के भूकंप में लगभग 300 लोग इसकी चपेट में आकर मारे गए।
GU: આ ધરતીકંપમાં આશરે 300 લોકો ભોગ બન્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a悲惨hisanmiserable (adjective, doesn't fit "になった")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's trap lives inside single kanji: the same character reads one way in a common word and a completely different way in one specific fixed compound (marked △ throughout). Pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

KanjiCommon readingFixed-compound exception (△)Watch for
登 トウ — 登録 (touroku, registration) ト — 登山 (tozan, mountain climbing) The book's opening quiz: とざん, never とうざん.
生 セイ — 生存 (seizon, survival) ショウ — 一生 (isshou, one's whole life) 一生 is いっしょう, never いっせい.
成 セイ — 成熟 (seijuku, maturity) ジョウ — 成就 (jouju, accomplishment) 成就 is じょうじゅ, never せいじゅ.
除 ジョ — 削除 (sakujo, deletion) ジ — 掃除 (souji, cleaning) 掃除 is そうじ, never そうじょ.
暴 ボウ — 暴風 (boufuu, windstorm) バク — 暴露 (bakuro, disclosure) 暴露 is ばくろ, never ぼうろ.
露 ロ — 暴露 (bakuro, disclosure) ロウ — 披露 (hirou, unveiling) 披露 is ひろう, never ひろ — and homophone of Day 1's 疲労.
直 チョク — 直面 (chokumen, facing) ジキ — 正直な (shoujiki na, honest) 正直 is しょうじき, never しょうちょく.
Exam trap: 登山 (とざん) is the single most testable item today — every other N1 word built on 登 reads とう (登録, 登場, 登校), so と alone in 登山 is a pure memorization exception with no phonetic warning sign. When you see 登 in an unfamiliar compound, default to とう and only drop the う if the word is 登山 itself.
