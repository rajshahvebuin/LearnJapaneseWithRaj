Notes
The book's opening quiz asks how to read 解釈 (an interpretation) against three tempting wrong answers — かいやく (that would be 解約, a cancellation) and かいけつ (that would be 解決, a resolution) are real words built from different kanji entirely, not alternate readings of 釈. The correct reading is かいしゃく. Today's clusters are full of this same trap: a kanji that looks like it should share a reading with its neighbours but actually belongs to a different word altogether.

Q1

二十歳のとき、人生の（　）を迫られた。Hatachi no toki, jinsei no (___) o semarareta.

Full sentence: 二十歳のとき、人生の選択を迫られた。Hatachi no toki, jinsei no sentaku o semarareta.
EN: At twenty, I was forced to make a major life choice.
HI: बीस साल की उम्र में मुझे ज़िंदगी का बड़ा फ़ैसला लेने पर मजबूर होना पड़ा।
GU: વીસ વર્ષની ઉંમરે મારે જીવનનો મોટો નિર્ણય લેવાની ફરજ પડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a選択sentakua (personal) choiceफ़ैसला, विकल्प चुननाપસંદગી, નિર્ણય
b採択saitakuformal adoption (wrong register)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

EN: Please make an effort to maintain your current level of ability.
HI: कृपया अपनी वर्तमान क्षमता को बनाए रखने की कोशिश करें।
GU: કૃપા કરીને તમારી હાલની ક્ષમતા જાળવવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a支持shijisupport, backing (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's clusters again produce two kinds of traps: same-reading pairs from one cluster that mean unrelated things, and same-shape families that quietly split into two or three different on-yomi. Pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
選択sentaku A (personal) choice An individual deciding between options. "I had to make a choice," "multiple choice."
採択saitaku Adoption (of a proposal) A formal body officially adopting something — same タク reading, unrelated register. "The committee adopted the resolution."
支持shiji Support, backing Endorsing a person, party, or opinion. "Public support for a policy."
維持iji Maintenance Keeping an existing state or condition going — same ジ reading, unrelated meaning. "Maintaining one's health/skills."
凍結touketsu Freezing A physical or figurative freeze (roads, assets, negotiations). "The road froze over," "frozen assets."
A棟・B棟A-tou, B-tou Building A, Building B Counter/suffix for a building or wing — same トウ reading as 凍, unrelated meaning. Naming buildings on a campus or hospital.
区画kukaku A division, plot A section of land or space. "Land divided into plots."
駆使kushi Free/masterful use Wielding a tool, language, or skill fluently — same ク reading, unrelated meaning. "Fluently using multiple languages."
欧米oubei Europe and America, the West A geographic/cultural region. "Western countries," "European and American markets."
殴打ouda A beating, assault Physically striking someone — same オウ reading, unrelated meaning. "Assault and battery."
Exam trap: 支持 (support) and 維持 (maintenance) are both read with ジ and both come from this day's very own 寺 cluster, so they're easy to swap when a sentence is about "keeping something going" — only 維持 fits an abstract state like ability or health, while 支持 needs a person, opinion, or cause as its object. Q2 above is built entirely around telling them apart.
