6日目 — 由・油・抽 (Yu/Yuu/Yui, Yu, Chuu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.38–39 (PDF pages 38–39; PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
6 kanji clusters filed under 例外に注意したいもの ("watch out for the exceptions") — single kanji hiding two or three on-yomi each
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
The book's opening quiz asks which of 白夜・拍手・宿泊 does not read ハク — the answer is 白夜 (びゃくや), the exception, while 拍手 (はくしゅ) and 宿泊 (しゅくはく) both keep ハク. Today's title kanji 由 is the day's most extreme case: it carries three separate on-yomi (ユ, ユウ, ユイ) depending entirely on which word it's in.
❶ = a bonus/supplementary entry the book marks as lower-frequency (inferred from the book's own legend on p.9 — not a core word to prioritize)
◆ = an extended compound built from the headword shown just above it
* = a footnote about an alternate reading or usage
△ = the book's own marker meaning "just memorize this one fixed compound's reading — don't generalize it to other words with the same kanji"

Q1

パスポートの（　）をする。Pasupooto no (___) o suru.

Full sentence: パスポートの申請をする。Pasupooto no shinsei o suru.
EN: I apply for a passport.
HI: पासपोर्ट के लिए आवेदन करना।
GU: પાસપોર્ટ માટે અરજી કરવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a申告shinkokua declaration (wrong sense, e.g. tax/customs)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b申請shinseian applicationआवेदनઅરજી

Why: パスポートの申請をする ("to apply for a passport") is the fixed term; 申告 is reserved for declarations like customs or tax filings.

Q2

地名の（　）を調べる。Chimei no (___) o shiraberu.

Full sentence: 地名の由来を調べる。Chimei no yurai o shiraberu.
EN: I look up the origin of a place name.
HI: जगह के नाम की उत्पत्ति के बारे में जानना।
GU: સ્થળના નામનું મૂળ તપાસવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a経由keiyuvia, by way of (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b由来yuraithe origin, sourceउत्पत्ति, मूलઉદ્ભવ

Why: 地名の由来 ("the origin of a place name") is the standard phrase; 経由 describes a route/waypoint, not an origin story.

データを（　）する。Deeta o (___) suru.

GU: ડેટાનું વિશ્લેષણ કરવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: データを分析する ("to analyze data") is the standard collocation; 分別 usually means either personal discretion or sorting physical items (like waste), not statistical analysis.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's whole lesson is exceptions: single kanji that read one way almost everywhere, then flip to a completely different reading in one or two fixed compounds. Pulled straight from the groups and exercises above:

KanjiDefault readingFixed-compound exception(s)Watch for
白 ハク — 告白 (kokuhaku, confession) ビャク — 白夜 (byakuya, midnight-sun night) The book's opening quiz: びゃくや, never はくや.
拍 ハク — 拍手 (hakushu, applause) ヒョウ — 拍子 (hyoushi, rhythm/chance) 拍子 is ひょうし, never はくし.
由 ユウ — 自由 (jiyuu, freedom) ユ — 経由 (keiyu, via); ユイ — 由緒/由来 (yuisho, yurai) Three readings from one kanji — the day's biggest trap.
神 シン — 精神 (seishin, mind/spirit) ジン — 神社/神宮 (jinja, jinguu, shrine names) 神社 is じんじゃ, never しんしゃ.
経 ケイ — 経費 (keihi, expenses) キョウ — 経典/お経 (kyouten, okyou, Buddhist scripture) お経 is おきょう, never おけい.
分 ブン — 分析 (bunseki, analysis) フン — 5分間 (go-fun kan, five minutes); ブ — 五分五分 (gobugobu, fifty-fifty) Three readings from one kanji, echoing 由 above.
Exam trap: 由 and 分 are today's two "triple-reading" kanji — each carries three separate on-yomi depending entirely on the specific word. There's no shortcut for these: memorize 経由(ケイユ)／自由(ジユウ)／由緒・由来(ユイ) and 分析(ブン)／5分間(フン)／五分五分(ブ) as whole words, not as "由 reads X" or "分 reads Y" rules.
