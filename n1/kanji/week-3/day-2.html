2日目 — 平たい・平らな (Hiratai, Taira na)

Source

Theme
"2つ以上訓読みがあるもの②" — set 2 of kanji with multiple kun'yomi. Almost every entry today pairs neatly with another entry that shares its kanji (平たい／平らな, 訪れる／訪ねる, 負かす／負う, 老いる／老ける, 交わる／交える／交わす, 染みる／染まる, 潜る／潜む, 焦げる／焦る, 弾く／弾む／弾, 滑る／滑らかな, 覆う／覆す, 盛んな／盛り／盛る, 怠ける／怠る, 跳ぶ／跳ねる) — the day's organizing principle is literally "one kanji, several kun'yomi," grouped that way below.

Notes
The day's title itself pairs 平たい (hiratai, "flat" — describing a physical container) with 平らな (taira na, "flat" — describing a place/surface) — same kanji 平, same rough meaning, different grammatical shape (い-adjective vs な-adjective) and slightly different nuance.
Cross-reference column = a different, everyday kun'yomi (or compound) of the same kanji shown on the left.

Q1

締め切りが近付いて気持ちばかり（　）。Shimekiri ga chikazuite kimochi bakari (___).

Full sentence: 締め切りが近付いて気持ちばかり焦る。...kimochi bakari aseru.
EN: As the deadline approaches, I just feel more and more impatient/anxious.
HI: डेडलाइन नज़दीक आते ही मन ही मन बेचैनी बढ़ती जाती है।
GU: ડેડલાઇન નજીક આવતાં મન અધીરું બનતું જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a焦げるkogeruto burn (a pot) — wrong, feelings don't burn(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b焦るaseruto feel impatient/anxiousबेचैन होनाઅધીરા થવું

Why: 気持ちが焦る is the set way to describe inner impatience; 焦げる only applies to something physically scorching/burning.

Q2

住宅の売買契約を（　）。Juutaku no baibai keiyaku o (___).

Full sentence: 住宅の売買契約を交わす。Juutaku no baibai keiyaku o kawasu.
EN: To sign/exchange a house sale-and-purchase contract.
HI: घर की खरीद-बिक्री का अनुबंध करना/हस्ताक्षर करना।
GU: ઘરની ખરીદ-વેચાણનો કરાર કરવો/હસ્તાક્ષર કરવા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a交えるmajieruto mix in/exchange views (wrong object — contracts aren't "mixed in")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b交わすkawasuto exchange/sign (a contract, words)अनुबंध/बातचीत का आदान-प्रदान करनाકરાર/વાતચીતની આપ-લે કરવી

Why: 契約を交わす is the fixed collocation for signing a contract between two parties; 交える is for mixing in an element (like opinions) within a larger activity.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Almost the entire day is same-kanji, different-reading pairs. The 練習Ⅰ questions above test exactly these:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
盛んなsakan na Thriving, popular, active Describes an ongoing state (an industry, activity) being vigorous. "Fishing is a thriving industry on this island."
盛りsakari The peak, height (of a period) Names a specific peak moment, not an ongoing trait. "The height of summer."
盛るmoru To serve/heap up (food, a scale) A physical act of putting something into/onto a container. "Serve rice into a bowl."
染みるshimiru To seep/sting into a specific point Small-scale, localized penetration (often with a sting). "Smoke stings the eyes."
染まるsomaru To be dyed/take on a color A wide area or whole object taking on a color/influence. "The whole park turns pink."
覆うoou To cover (physically) A concrete act of covering something with hands/cloth. "Cover one's face with both hands."
覆すkutsugaesu To overturn, radically reverse An abstract reversal of a situation, prediction, or verdict. "Overturn everyone's predictions."
Exam trap: 盛 is this day's richest kanji, with 盛んな／盛り／盛る all sharing one kanji but meaning "thriving," "a peak," and "to serve" respectively — plus the cross-referenced 目盛り and 大盛り. When a 漢字読み question gives you もり/さかん/さかり, first decide whether the sentence is describing an ongoing state, a specific moment, or a physical action, then pick the reading.
