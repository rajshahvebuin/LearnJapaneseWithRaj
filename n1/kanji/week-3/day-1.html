1日目 — 生かす・生やす (Ikasu, Hayasu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.44–45 (PDF pages 44–45; PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
Week 3 shifts from same-component/on-yomi clusters to kun'yomi readings: 28 words whose kanji has 2 or more common kun'yomi. Each entry shows one reading in the main list; the right-hand "cross-reference" column shows another everyday reading/compound of that same kanji, echoing the book's own "やさしい読みを確認しよう" (check the easier reading) instruction.
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key — flagged transparently, same policy as every other day in this module.
Notes
The day's title "生かす・生やす" is itself the lesson: both words use 生, read two different ways (いかす／はやす), and the book's own cat mascots joke about guessing 生える wrong as いえる／うえる／はえる before landing on the right reading. Every group below works the same way — one kanji, multiple kun'yomi, told apart by the word it forms.
Cross-reference column = a different, everyday kun'yomi (or compound) of the same kanji shown on the left — not a new kanji, just the "easy" reading you may already know for it.
◆ = an extended compound built from the headword shown just above it
* = a footnote about an alternate reading or usage

Q1

偶然が（　）、すばらしい商品が開発された。Guuzen ga (___), subarashii shouhin ga kaihatsu sareta.

Full sentence: 偶然が重なって、すばらしい商品が開発された。Guuzen ga kasanatte, subarashii shouhin ga kaihatsu sareta.
EN: A string of coincidences overlapped, and a wonderful product was developed.
HI: कई संयोग एक साथ जुड़े, और एक शानदार उत्पाद बना।
GU: ઘણા સંજોગો ભેગા થયા, અને એક શાનદાર ઉત્પાદન બન્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a重なってkasanatteoverlapping, piling up (of events)एक साथ जुड़ना/घटित होनाભેગા થવું
b連なってtsuranatteforming a row/range (wrong — for physical lines, not events)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 重なる describes events/coincidences stacking on top of each other; 連なる describes physical things (mountains, a line of people) forming a continuous row — it can't take an abstract subject like 偶然.

Q2

彼は何か家庭に問題を（　）いるようだ。Kare wa nanika katei ni mondai o (___) iru you da.

Full sentence: 彼は何か家庭に問題を抱えているようだ。Kare wa nanika katei ni mondai o kakaete iru you da.
EN: He seems to be carrying some kind of family problem.
HI: लगता है उसके परिवार में कोई समस्या चल रही है, जिसे वह झेल रहा है।
GU: લાગે છે તેના પરિવારમાં કોઈ સમસ્યા છે, જે તે સહન કરી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a抱えてkakaetecarrying, bearing (a problem)बोझ उठाना, झेलनाબોજ ઊંચકવો
b強いてshiiteforcing (someone to do something) — wrong, needs an object being forced(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 問題を抱える is the fixed way to say "to be burdened with/carrying a problem"; 強いる needs a person being forced to do something, which doesn't fit "問題".

Q3

秋の青空に赤い木の実が（　）美しい。Aki no aozora ni akai ki no mi ga (___) utsukushii.

Full sentence: 秋の青空に赤い木の実が映えて美しい。Aki no aozora ni akai ki no mi ga haete utsukushii.
EN: The red berries stand out beautifully against the blue autumn sky.
HI: पतझड़ के नीले आसमान में लाल जामुन ख़ूबसूरती से उभरकर दिखते हैं।
GU: પાનખરના ભૂરા આકાશમાં લાલ બોર સુંદર રીતે ઊપસી આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生えてhaeteto grow (biologically) — wrong sense here(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b映えてhaetestanding out/looking brilliant against a backgroundउभरकर ख़ूबसूरत दिखनाસુંદર રીતે ઊપસવું

Why: 生える and 映える are true homophones (both はえる). The berries aren't sprouting — they're contrasting beautifully with the sky, which is exactly what 映える describes.

Q4

これを（　）と、もうチャンスはないだろう。Kore o (___) to, mou chansu wa nai darou.

Full sentence: これを逃すと、もうチャンスはないだろう。Kore o nogasu to, mou chansu wa nai darou.
EN: If you let this slip, there probably won't be another chance.
HI: अगर यह मौक़ा हाथ से निकल गया, तो शायद दोबारा मौक़ा न मिले।
GU: જો આ તક હાથમાંથી નીકળી ગઈ, તો કદાચ ફરી તક ન મળે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a外すhazusuremove, take off (wrong object — chances aren't "removed")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b逃すnogasumiss, let slip (an opportunity)मौक़ा गँवानाતક ગુમાવવી

Why: チャンスを逃す ("miss a chance") is the fixed collocation; 外す is for physically removing something (buttons, a seat) and doesn't pair with チャンス.

Q5

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a閉ざしてtozashiteshutting off, closing (one's heart)मन/दिल बंद कर लेनाદિલ બંધ કરી દેવું

Why: 心を閉ざす is the set phrase for emotionally shutting oneself off; 触れる means "to touch," which doesn't combine naturally with 心を…しまう here.

Q6

大学院の専門を（　）職に就く。Daigakuin no senmon o (___) shoku ni tsuku.

Full sentence: 大学院の専門を生かした職に就く。Daigakuin no senmon o ikashita shoku ni tsuku.
EN: To take a job that makes use of one's graduate-school specialty.
HI: ग्रेजुएट स्कूल की विशेषज्ञता का उपयोग करने वाली नौकरी पाना।
GU: ગ્રેજ્યુએટ સ્કૂલની વિશેષતાનો ઉપયોગ થાય તેવી નોકરી મેળવવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生かしたikashitamade use of (one's specialty)उपयोग किया हुआઉપયોગ કરેલું

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's confusion pairs are almost all same-kanji, different-reading traps — every 練習Ⅰ question above is really testing one of these:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
生えるhaeru To grow (biologically) True homophone of 映える — completely different kanji and meaning. Grass, hair, teeth sprouting/growing.
映えるhaeru To stand out, look brilliant (against a background) Describes visual contrast/beauty, not biological growth. A red flower against a blue sky; a photo that "映える" on social media.
汚す（よごす）yogosu To physically dirty/stain Object is a physical thing (clothes, hands). "He stained his clothes."
汚す（けがす）kegasu To disgrace, sully (honor/name) Object is intangible — reputation, name, honor. "She disgraced the family name."
逃がすnigasu To set free, let escape Transitive act on a living thing you release. "He let the fish go."
逃すnogasu To miss, let slip Object is an abstract opportunity/chance. "She missed her chance."
重なるkasanaru To overlap, coincide, pile up Subject is events/misfortunes/objects stacking. "Bad luck kept piling up."
連なるtsuranaru To form a row/range, be strung together Subject is physical things forming a continuous line. "The mountains form a range."
Exam trap: 生える and 映える are a true same-reading (はえる), zero-relation pair — the single most testable trap in this day's list. Whenever a sentence gives you はえる, check whether it's about something physically growing (→生える) or something visually standing out against a background (→映える) before picking kanji.
