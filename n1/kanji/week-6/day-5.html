練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

「た」はにごっていない音、つまり（　）です。"Ta" wa nigotte inai oto, tsumari (___) desu.

Full sentence: 「た」はにごっていない音、つまり清音です。"Ta" wa nigotte inai oto, tsumari seion desu.
EN: "Ta" is an unvoiced sound — in other words, a 清音.
HI: "ता" एक बिना-घोष (अनवॉइस्ड) ध्वनि है, यानी 清音 है।
GU: "તા" એ અઘોષ (અનવોઇસ્ડ) ધ્વનિ છે, એટલે કે 清音 છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a濁音dakuona voiced sound (opposite of "not voiced")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b清音seiona voiceless soundअघोष ध्वनिઅઘોષ ધ્વનિ

Why: The sentence describes a sound that is "not voiced" (にごっていない) — that is precisely the definition of 清音; 濁音 is the voiced counterpart, the opposite of what's described.

Q2

（　）なのはいいことだが、たまには大胆になってみてはどうか。(___) na no wa ii koto da ga, tama ni wa daitan ni natte mite wa dou ka.

Full sentence: 慎重なのはいいことだが、たまには大胆になってみてはどうか。Shinchou na no wa ii koto da ga, tama ni wa daitan ni natte mite wa dou ka.
EN: Being careful is good, but why not try being bold once in a while?
HI: सतर्क रहना अच्छी बात है, लेकिन कभी-कभी निडर बनकर भी देखो न?
GU: સાવધ રહેવું સારી વાત છે, પણ ક્યારેક નિડર બનીને પણ જુઓ ને?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a慎重shinchoucareful, cautiousसतर्कસાવધ
b貴重kichouprecious, valuable (not the antonym of 大胆)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence sets up a contrast with 大胆 "bold" — its true antonym in this list is 慎重 "careful"; 貴重 "precious" is an unrelated word that merely sounds similar.

Q3

この道路は（　）的に渋滞している。Kono douro wa (___) teki ni juutai shite iru.

Full sentence: この道路は慢性的に渋滞している。Kono douro wa mansei teki ni juutai shite iru.
EN: This road is chronically congested.
HI: यह सड़क लगातार/पुराने ढंग से जाम रहती है।
GU: આ રસ્તો સતત ટ્રાફિકજામ રહે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a慢性manseichronic (long-lasting)दीर्घकालिकલાંબા ગાળાનું
b悠長yuuchouleisurely (describes a manner, not a recurring state)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 慢性的に "chronically" describes a persistent, recurring state like traffic congestion; 悠長 describes an unhurried manner or pace, not a state that keeps recurring — a cross-family trap since both can sound plausible before 的に.

Q4

先頭を走るランナーとの距離を（　）追いついた。Sentou o hashiru rannaa to no kyori o (___) oitsuita.

Full sentence: 先頭を走るランナーとの距離を縮めて追いついた。Sentou o hashiru rannaa to no kyori o chijimete oitsuita.
EN: Closing the gap with the lead runner, he caught up.
HI: आगे दौड़ रहे धावक से दूरी कम करते हुए वह उसके बराबर आ गया।
GU: આગળ દોડતા દોડવીર સાથેનું અંતર ઘટાડીને તે તેની બરાબર આવી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a縮めてchijimeteshortening (the distance)दूरी कम करनाઅંતર ઘટાડવું
b伸ばしてnobashitelengthening (wrong direction to catch up)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Catching up means shrinking the gap, so 縮める "shorten" is correct; 伸ばす "lengthen/stretch" would mean widening the gap, the opposite of catching up.

Q5

信念は頑固に曲げないが、表現の仕方には（　）さがある。Shinnen wa ganko ni magenai ga, hyougen no shikata ni wa (___) sa ga aru.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a膨張bouchouexpansion (wrong sense — not about attitude)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Since the whole day is built from antonym pairs, the main "confusion" risk is crossing two different families — e.g. reaching for 悠長 (the antonym of 迅速) when the sentence actually needs 慢性 (the antonym of 急性), as in 練習Ⅰ Q3. Compare the pairs actually tested:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
清音seion A voiceless sound Kana pronounced without vocal-cord vibration (か・た・さ…). Explaining basic Japanese phonetics.
濁音dakuon A voiced consonant The "゛" (ten-ten) marked versions (が・だ・ざ…). Explaining voiced kana.
大胆daitan Bold, fearless Willing to take risks in action. "He made a bold decision."
慎重shinchou Careful, cautious Weighing risks carefully before acting. "She proceeded cautiously."
慢性mansei Chronic A condition or problem that persists/recurs over time. "Chronic traffic congestion," "a chronic illness."
悠長yuuchou Leisurely, unhurried Describes an unhurried manner or pace of action. "He spoke in an unhurried way" — not a recurring state.
縮めるchijimeru To shorten Actively reduce a distance, length, or time. "Close the gap with the leader."
伸ばすnobasu To lengthen, stretch Actively increase a distance, length, or time. "Extend the deadline," "stretch one's arm."
頑固ganko Stubborn Unwilling to change one's beliefs or ways. "He stubbornly refuses to change his mind."

Exam trap: 慢性 vs 悠長 is the day's sharpest trap (練習Ⅰ Q3) — both can precede 的に and both feel "slow/ongoing," but 慢性 describes a recurring state or condition while 悠長 describes an unhurried manner. Ask "is this a condition, or a way of acting?" before choosing.
