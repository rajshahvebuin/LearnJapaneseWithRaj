Theme

Notes
Today continues Day 2's "usage discrimination" approach, but most clusters here share a kanji cleanly (分, 覧, 没, 急, 点, 脈); two clusters (了解 group, 交渉 group) are meaning-based since the shared kanji only partially overlaps.

⇔ = an opposite form shown by the book next to a headword

Q1

人身事故のためダイヤが乱れています。予めご（　）ください。Jinshin jiko no tame daiya ga midarete imasu. Arakajime go-(___) kudasai.

Full sentence: 人身事故のためダイヤが乱れています。予めご了承ください。Jinshin jiko no tame daiya ga midarete imasu. Arakajime go-ryoushou kudasai.
EN: Train schedules are disrupted due to a person-involved accident. Please understand this in advance.
HI: दुर्घटना के कारण ट्रेन का समय-चक्र बिगड़ा हुआ है। कृपया पहले से इसे स्वीकार करें।
GU: અકસ્માતના કારણે ટ્રેનનું સમયપત્રક ખોરવાયું છે. કૃપા કરી અગાઉથી સ્વીકારો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a承諾shoudakuconsent (to a request — wrong register for an announcement)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b了承ryoushouplease understand/acceptकृपया स्वीकार करेंકૃપા કરી સ્વીકારો

Why: ご了承ください is the fixed polite phrase for asking the public to accept an inconvenience in advance; 承諾 is for consenting to a specific request/demand between two parties, not a public announcement.

GU: ઘડિયાળના ભાગ છૂટા પાડીને સાફ કરવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a分解bunkaidisassembleपुर्जे अलग करनाભાગ છૂટા પાડવા

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above pits two words sharing a kanji or a meaning-field against each other. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
了承ryoushou Please understand/accept A polite request for the public to accept something in advance. Announcements, apologies for inconvenience.
承諾shoudaku Consent Formally agreeing to a specific request or demand. Accepting a business proposal or demand.
分解bunkai Disassembly Taking a physical object apart into its pieces. "Take apart a clock/machine."
分析bunseki Analysis Examining data, results, or information. "Analyze the experiment results."
閲覧etsuran Perusal, browsing Reading/viewing a document, book, or website. "Browse a website," "a library reading room."
展覧tenran An exhibition Publicly displaying physical works (art, etc.). "An art exhibition."
沈没chinbotsu Sinking A vessel physically sinks in water. "The ship sank."
没落botsuraku Downfall, decline A family, class, or fortune declines over time. "The noble family fell into decline."
人脈jinmyaku A network of contacts A person's web of social/professional connections. "He has connections in politics."
文脈bunmyaku Context The surrounding context of a piece of text. "Meaning changes depending on context."
交渉koushou Negotiation A mutual, formal discussion toward an agreement. "Negotiate a price/partnership."
干渉kanshou Interference Unwanted meddling in someone else's affairs. "A parent interferes in a child's life."
Exam trap: 交渉 vs 干渉 is the day's sharpest trap — both involve two parties and share 渉, but 交渉 is a cooperative negotiation while 干渉 is one-sided, unwanted meddling. Check whether both sides want the conversation before picking.
