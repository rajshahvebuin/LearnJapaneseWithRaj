2日目 — 返済・返却・返還 (Hensai, Henkyaku, Henkan)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.94–95 (PDF pages 95–96; PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
12 word-families ("使い分けで覚える①" — learn by telling near-synonyms apart), each sharing one kanji
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
Unlike Day 1's plain everyday-word categories, today's words are near-synonyms that share one kanji (e.g. 復旧・回復・復帰・復活・復興 all contain 復) but attach to very different objects — a road, a patient, an employee, an old custom, a country. The skill being tested is knowing which noun each verb collocates with.
word family = this book's own "使い分け" (usage-discrimination) cluster: several words sharing one kanji, distinguished by which noun they attach to
◆ = the book's own example sentence for that word

Q1

育児休暇を終えて、仕事に（　）する。Ikuji kyuuka o oete, shigoto ni (___) suru.

Full sentence: 育児休暇を終えて、仕事に復帰する。Ikuji kyuuka o oete, shigoto ni fukki suru.
EN: After finishing childcare leave, she returns to work.
HI: बाल-देखभाल अवकाश खत्म करके, वह काम पर वापस लौटती है।
GU: બાળ-સંભાળ રજા પૂરી કરીને, તે કામ પર પરત ફરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a復活fukkatsurevival (of a custom/event, wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b復帰fukkireturn, comeback (to a job)वापसी (काम पर)પરત ફરવું (કામ પર)

Why: 仕事に復帰する is the fixed collocation for a person returning to their job; 復活 describes an event or custom being revived, not a person's own return.

Q2

（　）の用紙に必要事項を記入してください。(___) no youshi ni hitsuyou jikou o kinyuu shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 所定の用紙に必要事項を記入してください。Shotei no youshi ni hitsuyou jikou o kinyuu shite kudasai.
EN: Please fill in the required information on the prescribed form.
HI: कृपया निर्धारित फ़ॉर्म पर आवश्यक जानकारी भरें।
GU: કૃપા કરી નિર્ધારિત ફોર્મમાં જરૂરી માહિતી ભરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a所定shoteiprescribed, designatedनिर्धारितનિર્ધારિત
b規定kiteia regulation (pairs with fees/rules, not forms)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 所定の用紙 "the prescribed form" is the set phrase for an officially designated document; 規定 collocates with fees/rules (規定の料金), not with a physical form.

Q3

ネクタイやスーツは（　）だ。もっと気楽な服装で出勤したい。Nekutai ya suutsu wa (___) da. Motto kiraku na fukusou de shukkin shitai.

GU: ટાઈ અને સૂટ સાંકડાં/અસહજ લાગે છે. હું વધુ આરામદાયક કપડાંમાં ઑફિસ જવા માંગું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a窮屈kyuukutsucramped, restrictive (physically confining)तंग, असहजસાંકડું, અસહજ
b退屈taikutsuboring (wrong sense — clothes aren't "boring")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 窮屈 describes something physically confining or restrictive, which fits stiff formal clothing; 退屈 means "boring" and applies to time or activities, not garments.

HI: आपदा-ग्रस्त क्षेत्र के पुनर्निर्माण में सहायता करना।
GU: આપત્તિગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તારના પુનર્નિર્માણમાં સહાય કરવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a回復kaifukurecovery (of health/weather, wrong scale)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q6

腰痛がひどくなり、日常生活に（　）を来すようになった。Youtsuu ga hidoku nari, nichijou seikatsu ni (___) o kitasu you ni natta.

GU: કમરનો દુખાવો એટલો વધ્યો કે રોજિંદા જીવનમાં અડચણ આવવા લાગી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a支障sishouhindrance, trouble (to daily life)रुकावटઅડચણ

Why: 支障を来す "cause hindrance" is the fixed phrase for something interfering with one's own life/work; 障害 more commonly describes a technical fault (signal, server) or a disability.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above is really a same-kanji-family discrimination — the words share a root meaning but attach to different-scale objects. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
復帰fukki A return, comeback A person returns to a role (job, post) they had before. "She returned to work after leave."
復活fukkatsu A revival A custom, event, or trend comes back to life. "The old festival was revived."
所定shotei Prescribed, designated Describes a specific document/place set aside for a purpose. "The prescribed form/location."
規定kitei A regulation Describes a rule or fee fixed by a system. "The regulated fee/rule."
窮屈kyuukutsu Cramped, restrictive Physically or socially confining. "A tight space," "stiff/formal clothing."
退屈taikutsu Boring A lack of stimulation in time or activity. "A boring day," "a dull lecture."
回復kaifuku Recovery (small-scale) An individual's health or the weather improving. "Recovering from illness," "the weather clears up."
復興fukkou Reconstruction (large-scale) A region, nation, or economy being rebuilt. "Post-disaster reconstruction."
怠慢taiman Neglectful, negligent A moral/professional fault — failing a duty. "Dereliction of duty" (can cause punishment).
緩慢kanman Slow, sluggish A description of physical speed, no fault implied. "Slow movement/traffic."
支障sishou A hindrance, trouble Something interfering with one's own life or work. "It's causing trouble in my daily life."
障害shougai A fault, barrier A technical malfunction, or a disability/obstacle in general. "Server trouble," "a physical barrier."
Exam trap: 回復 vs 復興 is the single most testable pair — both mean "recovery," but 回復 is for a person/weather getting better, while 復興 is reserved for rebuilding something on the scale of a region or economy. Check the size of what's recovering before choosing.
