練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

（　）を繰り返して、ついに成功した。(___) o kurikaeshite, tsui ni seikou shita.

Full sentence: 試行錯誤を繰り返して、ついに成功した。Shikou sakugo o kurikaeshite, tsui ni seikou shita.
EN: After repeated trial and error, I finally succeeded.
HI: बार-बार आज़माइश और ग़लती करते हुए, आख़िरकार मुझे सफलता मिली।
GU: વારંવાર અજમાયશ અને ભૂલ કરીને, છેવટે મને સફળતા મળી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a試行錯誤shikou sakugotrial and errorआज़माइश और ग़लतीઅજમાયશ અને ભૂલ
b一喜一憂ikki ichiyuualternating joy and worry (no link to eventual success)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 試行錯誤 describes a repeated process of attempting and failing that eventually leads to success; 一喜一憂 describes being emotionally swayed by each individual outcome, not a path toward success.

Q2

マニュアル通りではなく、（　）に対応してほしい。Manyuaru doori de wa naku, (___) ni taiou shite hoshii.

Full sentence: マニュアル通りではなく、臨機応変に対応してほしい。Manyuaru doori de wa naku, rinki ouhen ni taiou shite hoshii.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Four-character idioms often sound superficially similar to each other despite meaning very different things. Compare the six pairs 練習Ⅰ tested:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
試行錯誤shikou sakugo Trial and error A repeated process that eventually leads somewhere (often success). "Through trial and error, I finally succeeded."
一喜一憂ikki ichiyuu Alternating joy and worry Being emotionally swayed by each individual outcome, no progress implied. "Don't get swayed by every little result."
臨機応変rinki ouhen Adapting to circumstances A positive, flexible response to changing situations. "Respond flexibly, not just by the manual."
支離滅裂shiri metsuretsu Incoherent A negative description of disorganized, contradictory speech/logic. "His argument was incoherent."
自暴自棄jibou jiki Desperation, self-abandonment Giving up on oneself and acting recklessly out of despair. "He turned to crime out of desperation."
暗中模索anchuu mosaku Groping in the dark Searching blindly for a solution — not reckless, just uncertain. "We're groping for a solution with no clear leads."
単刀直入tantou chokunyuu Straightforward, blunt Describes a direct, plain manner of speaking. "Please just tell me straight."
一網打尽ichimou dajin Round up all at once Describes catching/eliminating an entire group in one sweep. "The police rounded up the whole gang at once."
感慨無量kangai muryou Filled with deep emotion An emotional reaction to a meaningful personal moment. "I'm overcome with emotion at this achievement."
新陳代謝shinchin taisha Metabolism A biological/scientific term, no emotional content. "Exercise boosts your metabolism."
右往左往uousaou Scrambling about in confusion Aimless, unproductive movement — a negative description. "Everyone just panicked and ran around."
創意工夫souikufuu Ingenuity A positive trait — creative, resourceful problem-solving. "She showed real ingenuity in solving it."
Exam trap: 自暴自棄 vs 暗中模索 is the sharpest trap — both describe someone struggling without a clear path, but 自暴自棄 is a reckless, self-destructive spiral while 暗中模索 is a calm (if uncertain) search for answers. Check whether the person is giving up or still trying before choosing.
