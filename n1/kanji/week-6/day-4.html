Q1

（　）どの子どもにも愛情を注ぐ。(___) dono kodomo ni mo aijou o sosogu.

Full sentence: 分け隔てなくどの子どもにも愛情を注ぐ。Wakehedate naku dono kodomo ni mo aijou o sosogu.
EN: She pours affection on every child equally, without favoritism.
HI: वह बिना भेदभाव के हर बच्चे पर स्नेह लुटाती है।
GU: તે ભેદભાવ વગર દરેક બાળક પર સ્નેહ વરસાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a懲りなくkorinakuwithout learning a lesson (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b分け隔てなくwakehedate nakuequally, without discriminationबिना भेदभाव केભેદભાવ વગર

Why: 分け隔てなく means "without discrimination between people," which fits loving every child equally; 懲りない means "not learning a lesson from a bad experience," an unrelated idea.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q3

Full sentence: 大臣は時代錯誤な発言をした。Daijin wa jidai sakugo na hatsugen o shita.
EN: The minister made an anachronistic (out-of-touch) remark.
HI: मंत्री ने पुराने ख़यालों वाली/समय से पीछे की टिप्पणी की।
GU: મંત્રીએ જૂનવાણી નિવેદન કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a錯誤sakugoerror, anachronismपुरानापन, विसंगतिજૂનવાણીપણું
b錯覚sakkakuan illusion (only pairs with 目の, not 時代)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Because today's words are whole fixed expressions, the "confusion" is between two idioms that sound like they could fit the same slot. Compare the six pairs 練習Ⅰ tested:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
分け隔てなくwakehedate naku Equally, without discrimination Treating multiple people the same way, with no favoritism. "Love every child equally."
懲りないkorinai Not learning one's lesson Repeating a mistake despite a past bad experience. "A child who never learns even after being scolded."
陣中見舞いjinchuu mimai A morale visit to someone hard at work Visiting/supporting someone mid-effort (exam, project). Cheering on a friend writing a thesis.
謹賀新年kinga shinnen "Happy New Year" (written) A fixed seasonal greeting, only used at New Year. New Year's postcards (年賀状).
時代錯誤jidai sakugo An anachronism An idea/remark out of step with modern times. Criticizing an outdated opinion.
目の錯覚me no sakkaku An optical illusion A visual perception mistake, unrelated to social attitudes. "It looks longer than it is — an optical illusion."
感情の起伏kanjou no kifuku Emotional ups and downs Swings in one's own mood. "She has intense mood swings."
妥協dakyou A compromise A concession reached between two negotiating parties. "They reached a compromise plan."
添付書類tenpu shorui Attached documents Files sent along with an email/message. "Please check the attached file."
扶養家族fuyou kazoku Dependents Family members one is financially responsible for. Tax forms, insurance paperwork.
愚痴をこぼすguchi o kobosu To grumble, complain Voicing dissatisfaction verbally. "She rarely complains."
炊事・洗濯suiji, sentaku Cooking and laundry Plain nouns for housework tasks, no idiom attached. "He can do all the housework himself."
Exam trap: Fixed-expression days like this one punish guessing from kanji meaning alone — 錯誤 and 錯覚 both mean roughly "mistake," but only one collocates with 時代 and only the other with 目の. Memorize each idiom as a whole chunk, not kanji-by-kanji.
