絶える describes something ending completely (contact, a bloodline); 耐える is to endure a hardship and come through it; 堪える (almost always negated: 〜に堪えない) says something is too awful to bear watching/hearing. All three share the reading たえる.

練習Ⅰ — read a/b, then choose the correct option

Q1

優秀な成績を（a.修める　b.努める）。Yuushuu na seiseki o (a. osameru b. tsutomeru).

EN: To achieve excellent grades.
HI: उत्कृष्ट अंक/परिणाम हासिल करना।
GU: ઉત્કૃષ્ટ ગુણ/પરિણામ મેળવવા.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a修めるosameruおさめるto achieve/complete (grades, studies)
b努めるtsutomeruつとめるto strive/make an effort (wrong verb for a result)

Why: 成績を修める is the set collocation for "achieving/earning grades" as a completed result; 努める describes the ongoing effort itself, not the achieved outcome.

Q2

ご報告があって、筆を（a.執りました　b.討ちました）。Go-houkoku ga atte, fude o (a. torimashita b. uchimashita).

EN: Having something to report, I took up my pen (to write it).
HI: कुछ रिपोर्ट करना था, इसलिए मैंने कलम उठाई।
GU: કંઈક જણાવવાનું હતું, એટલે મેં કલમ ઉપાડી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a執りましたtorimashitaとりましたtook up (the pen, formally)
b討ちましたuchimashitaうちましたattacked/defeated (an enemy — wrong verb entirely)

Why: 筆を執る〈執筆〉is the fixed phrase for "taking up the pen to write"; 討つ means to attack or defeat an enemy and has no connection to writing.

Q3

秋の美しい空を（a.仰ぐ　b.澄む）。Aki no utsukushii sora o (a. aogu b. sumu).

EN: To look up at the beautiful autumn sky.
HI: पतझड़ के ख़ूबसूरत आसमान की ओर देखना।
GU: પાનખરના સુંદર આકાશ તરફ જોવું.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a仰ぐaoguあおぐto look up at
b澄むsumuすむto become clear (intransitive, wrong grammar/verb)

Why: 空を仰ぐ (looking up at the sky) needs 仰ぐ, a transitive "to gaze upward at" verb; 澄む describes the sky/water becoming clear on its own and can't take 空を as its direct object here.

Q4

ドラマの主役を（a.納める　b.務める）。Dorama no shuyaku o (a. osameru b. tsutomeru).

EN: To play the leading role in a drama.
HI: धारावाहिक में मुख्य भूमिका निभाना।
GU: નાટકમાં મુખ્ય ભૂમિકા ભજવવી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a納めるosameruおさめるto pay/supply (wrong verb for a role)
b務めるtsutomeruつとめるto serve in/play (a role)

Why: 主役を務める is the set phrase for "playing a leading role"; 納める is used for paying taxes or supplying goods, not for a theatrical role.

Q5

駅までの道を（a.謝る　b.尋ねる）。Eki made no michi o (a. ayamaru b. tazuneru).

EN: To ask the way to the station.
HI: स्टेशन जाने का रास्ता पूछना।
GU: સ્ટેશન સુધીનો રસ્તો પૂછવો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a謝るayamaruあやまるto apologize (wrong verb entirely)
b尋ねるtazuneruたずねるto ask (for directions/information)

Why: 道を尋ねる is the standard phrase for "asking the way"; 謝る (to apologize) doesn't fit at all — a common trap purely because 謝る and 尋ねる look similar in kana-stripped test formatting.

Q6

選択を（a.耐えた　b.誤った）。Sentaku o (a. taeta b. ayamatta).

EN: Made a wrong choice.
HI: ग़लत चुनाव किया।
GU: ખોટી પસંદગી કરી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a耐えたtaetaたえたendured (wrong verb — a choice isn't "endured")

Why: 選択を誤る is the set phrase for "making a wrong choice"; 耐える means to endure hardship and cannot take 選択 (a choice) as its object.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is built around same-reading, easily-confused kanji — every 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ question above is really testing one of these pairs. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
修めるosameru To achieve/complete (studies, results) Names a completed accomplishment. "Earned excellent grades," "completed one's studies."
努めるtsutomeru To strive, make an effort Names the ongoing effort, not a guaranteed result. "Strive to solve the problem."
取る／捕る／採る／撮る／執るtoru (×5) General take / catch / gather / photograph / take up (pen) Five unrelated kanji sharing one reading — each locked to its own narrow context. Context alone (camera? live animal? blood/staff? formal writing?) decides the kanji.
避けるsakeru To avoid A deliberate act of steering clear of something. "Avoid a crowd/collision."
裂けるsakeru To tear (intransitive) A physical material ripping on its own. "The bag/fabric tore."
堪えないtaenai Too awful to bear (hearing/watching) Fixed negative pattern 〜に堪えない. "An unbearable story," "can't bear to watch."
絶えないtaenai Never ceases Something keeps happening without end. "Quarrels never stop."
済むsumu To be finished/settled A task, meal, or matter reaches completion. "The errand/meal is done."
住むsumu To reside Living somewhere. "Live in Japan/with parents."
Exam trap: とる is the single most overloaded reading in this book so far — 取る・捕る・採る・撮る・執る are five completely different kanji, all read とる, each locked to a distinct real-world context (general handling, catching a live creature, gathering/selecting, photography, formal writing). A 漢字書き question that gives you とる in isolation is unanswerable without checking exactly what is being "taken."
