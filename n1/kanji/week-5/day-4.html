4日目 — 招待・正体 (Shoutai, Shoutai)

Source

Theme
11 on-yomi (音読み) clusters — the book switches strategy from kun-yomi words to grouping two-kanji compounds that are perfect homophones (identical on-yomi reading, unrelated meaning and kanji), e.g. たいしょう = 対象／対称／対照

練習Ⅰ — read a/b, then choose the correct option

Q1

（a.細菌　b.最近）を培養して研究する。(a. Saikin b. Saikin) o baiyou shite kenkyuu suru.

EN: Culture bacteria and study them.
HI: जीवाणुओं का संवर्धन (कल्चर) करके उन पर शोध करना।
GU: બેક્ટેરિયાનું સંવર્ધન કરીને તેના પર સંશોધન કરવું.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a細菌saikinさいきんbacteria/germs
b最近saikinさいきんrecently (an adverb, not a lab specimen)

Why: Only 細菌 (bacteria) is something you can 培養する (culture) in a lab; 最近 means "recently" and cannot be the object of "culture and study."

Q2

社会（a.補償　b.保障）制度の見直しをする。Shakai (a. hoshou b. hoshou) seido no minaoshi o suru.

EN: Review the social security system.
HI: सामाजिक सुरक्षा प्रणाली की समीक्षा करना।
GU: સામાજિક સુરક્ષા વ્યવસ્થાની સમીક્ષા કરવી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a補償hoshouほしょうmonetary compensation (wrong word)
b保障hoshouほしょうsystemic security/welfare

Why: 社会保障 (social security) is the fixed institutional term; 補償 refers to paying compensation for a specific loss or damage, not a welfare system.

Q3

携帯電話の使用が会議の（a.進行　b.振興）を妨げた。Keitai denwa no shiyou ga kaigi no (a. shinkou b. shinkou) o samatageta.

EN: Cellphone use hindered the meeting's progress.
HI: मोबाइल फ़ोन के इस्तेमाल ने बैठक की प्रगति में बाधा डाली।
GU: મોબાઈલ ફોનના ઉપયોગે બેઠકની પ્રગતિમાં અડચણ ઊભી કરી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a進行shinkouしんこうthe proceeding/progress (of a meeting)
b振興shinkouしんこうpromoting development (wrong word)

Why: 会議の進行 (the meeting's proceeding) is what phones would disrupt; 振興 means promoting the development of something (a town, an industry), unrelated to running a meeting.

Q4

若手議員は政界の世代（a.交代　b.新興）の必要性を訴えた。Wakate giin wa seikai no sedai (a. koutai b. shinkou) no hitsuyousei o uttaeta.

EN: The young lawmaker argued for the need for generational change in politics.
HI: युवा सांसद ने राजनीति में पीढ़ी परिवर्तन की ज़रूरत पर ज़ोर दिया।
GU: યુવાન સાંસદે રાજકારણમાં પેઢી પરિવર્તનની જરૂરિયાત પર ભાર મૂક્યો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a交代koutaiこうたいchangeover, replacement
b新興shinkouしんこうnewly-arisen (wrong word/reading entirely)

Why: 世代交代 (generational turnover/changeover) is the fixed phrase; 新興 is an adjective for something newly established (a religion, a neighborhood) and doesn't pair with 世代.

Q5

彼らは双子だが、性格は（a.対照　b.対称）的だ。Karera wa futago da ga, seikaku wa (a. taishou b. taishou) teki da.

EN: They're twins, but their personalities are a study in contrast.
HI: वे जुड़वां हैं, पर उनके स्वभाव में ज़मीन-आसमान का फ़र्क़ है।
GU: તેઓ જોડિયા છે, પણ તેમના સ્વભાવમાં સાવ વિરોધાભાસ છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a対照的taishoutekiたいしょうてきcontrasting
b対称的taishoutekiたいしょうてきsymmetrical (wrong word)

Why: 対照的 describes two things being different/opposite (fits "contrasting personalities"); 対称 describes mirror-image symmetry (shapes, designs), not personality traits.

Q6

人質（a.解放　b.開放）のニュースが伝えられた。Hitojichi (a. kaihou b. kaihou) no nyuusu ga tsutaerareta.

EN: News of the hostage's release was reported.
HI: बंधक की रिहाई की ख़बर सामने आई।
GU: બંધકની મુક્તિના સમાચાર આવ્યા.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a解放kaihouかいほうrelease/liberation (of a person)
b開放kaihouかいほうopening (a place to the public — wrong word)

Why: 人質を解放する is the fixed phrase for releasing a hostage; 開放 is for opening up a place or facility (a school, a border), not freeing a person from captivity.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every cluster in §1 today is already a same-reading confusion set — this section highlights the four biggest traps and pulls the exact pairs tested in the exercises above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
家庭katei A household/family The people and home life itself. "A home tutor," "a family-oriented person."
過程katei A process The stages something goes through toward a result. "The production process," "process and result."
仮定katei An assumption A hypothetical premise, not yet confirmed. "Based on an assumption," "let's assume that..."
課程katei A course (of study) A formal academic program. "A doctoral course," "the curriculum."
保証hoshou A guarantee A personal/product promise (a guarantor, a warranty). "A guarantor," "a product warranty."
保障hoshou Systemic security Institutional protection provided by a system. "Social security," "national security."
補償hoshou Compensation Money paid to make up for a specific loss. "Damage/disaster compensation."
不振fushin A slump/underperformance Weaker-than-normal results or condition. "A business slump," "poor appetite."
不信fushin Distrust Lack of trust in people or institutions. "Distrust of politics/people."
不審fushin Suspicious Behavior/objects that raise suspicion. "Suspicious behavior/object."
Exam trap: かてい is the day's deepest trap — 家庭 (household), 過程 (process), 仮定 (assumption), and 課程 (course of study) are four unrelated words sharing one reading. しんこう (進行・振興・新興・信仰) is a close second. Whenever either appears in isolation on a 漢字書き question, the surrounding sentence — not the sound — is the only way to pick the right kanji.
