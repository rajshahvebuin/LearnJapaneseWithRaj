練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items, 2 blanks each), both from p.87. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

練習Ⅰ — read a/b, then choose the correct option

Q1

事件の（a.契機　b.核心）に迫る情報はなかった。Jiken no (a. keiki b. kakushin) ni semaru jouhou wa nakatta.

EN: There was no information that got to the heart of the case.
HI: मामले की तह तक पहुँचने वाली कोई जानकारी नहीं थी।
GU: કેસના મૂળ સુધી પહોંચે તેવી કોઈ માહિતી ન હતી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a契機keikiけいきa triggering opportunity (wrong word)
b核心kakushinかくしんthe heart/core of a matter

Why: 核心に迫る is the fixed phrase for "getting to the heart of" a case; 契機 means an opportunity/trigger that starts something, unrelated to what a piece of information "reaches."

Q2

新市長は（a.抱負　b.負債）を述べた。Shin-shichou wa (a. houfu b. fusai) o nobeta.

EN: The new mayor stated his aspirations.
HI: नए महापौर ने अपनी महत्वाकांक्षाएं बताईं।
GU: નવા મેયરે પોતાની મહત્વાકાંક્ષાઓ જણાવી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a抱負houfuほうふone's hopes/aspirations
b負債fusaiふさいdebt (wrong word entirely)

Why: 抱負を述べる is the set phrase for stating one's aspirations/resolutions; 負債 (debt) is a financial term with no place in an inaugural speech about hopes.

Q3

転倒した選手は（a.閉口　b.棄権）せず、最後まで滑りきった。Tentou shita senshu wa (a. heikou b. kiken) sezu, saigo made suberikitta.

EN: The skater who fell didn't withdraw, and skated to the very end.
HI: गिरने वाली/वाला खिलाड़ी ने हार नहीं मानी और आख़िर तक स्केटिंग पूरी की।
GU: પડી ગયેલા ખેલાડીએ ખસી જવાને બદલે અંત સુધી સ્કેટિંગ પૂરું કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a閉口heikouへいこうbe exasperated/speechless (wrong word)
b棄権kikenきけんto withdraw/abstain (from a competition)

Why: 棄権する is the verb for withdrawing from a competition; 閉口する describes being at a loss/annoyed by something (like a rude question), unrelated to competing.

Q4

製品に（a.危険　b.欠陥）が見つかった。Seihin ni (a. kiken b. kekkan) ga mitsukatta.

EN: A defect was found in the product.
HI: उत्पाद में एक ख़राबी पाई गई।
GU: ઉત્પાદનમાં ખામી મળી આવી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a危険kikenきけんdanger (an adjective/noun, wrong fit)
b欠陥kekkanけっかんa defect/flaw

Why: 欠陥が見つかる is the set phrase for "a defect is found" (in a product); 危険 describes a state of danger, not a discoverable flaw itself.

Q5

今回の最優秀賞には（a.該当　b.確信）者がなかった。Konkai no saiyuushuushou ni wa (a. gaitou b. kakushin) sha ga nakatta.

EN: There was no eligible recipient for this round's top award.
HI: इस बार के सर्वश्रेष्ठ पुरस्कार के लिए कोई पात्र व्यक्ति नहीं मिला।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Nearly every cluster in §1 today is already a same-reading confusion set. Here are the pairs directly tested in the exercises above, plus the day's headline four-way trap:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
契機keiki An opportunity/trigger An event that prompts a change or review. "Using an accident as an opportunity to review safety."
景気keiki The economy Macroeconomic conditions. "The economy recovers."
計器keiki A measuring instrument A physical device/gauge. "Introduce/repair a measuring instrument."
刑期keiki A prison term The length of a criminal sentence. "Complete one's prison term."
核心kakushin The heart/core of a matter The essential point of an issue. "Get to the heart of the case."
確信kakushin Conviction, certainty A personal state of being sure. "Be convinced of/uncertain about."
革新kakushin Innovation, progressivism Change/advancement in technology or politics. "Technical innovation," "a progressive party."
棄権kiken To abstain/withdraw Voluntarily opting out of voting or competing. "Abstain from voting," "withdraw from a race."
危険kiken Danger A state or quality of being unsafe. "A dangerous job."
Exam trap: けいき is the single busiest reading of the entire week — 契機・景気・計器・刑期 are four unrelated words, all read けいき, spanning everyday news vocabulary (the economy), technical vocabulary (instruments), and legal vocabulary (prison terms). Isolated from context, けいき is unanswerable on a 漢字書き question.
