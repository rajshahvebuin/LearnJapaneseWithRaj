練習Ⅰ — read a/b, then choose the correct option

Q1

（a.空き巣　b.鳥の巣）が入らないように戸締まりに注意する。(a. Akisu b. Tori no su) ga hairanai you ni tojimari ni chuui suru.

EN: Be careful to lock up so that a sneak thief doesn't get in.
HI: ध्यान रखें कि सेंधमार अंदर न आ सके, इसलिए दरवाज़े-खिड़कियाँ बंद कर लें।
GU: ઘરફોડ ચોર અંદર ન આવે તે માટે બારીબારણાં બંધ રાખવાનું ધ્યાન રાખો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a空き巣akisuあきすsneak thief
b鳥の巣tori no suとりの すbird nest (doesn't "get in" through locked doors)

Why: 戸締まり (locking up doors/windows) is a home-security context, which matches 空き巣 (a burglar); 鳥の巣 is a literal bird's nest and has nothing to do with locking a house.

Q2

暗かったが、確かに（a.物陰　b.人影）を見た。Kurakatta ga, tashika ni (a. monokage b. hitokage) o mita.

EN: It was dark, but I definitely saw a human silhouette.
HI: अंधेरा था, पर मैंने वाक़ई किसी इंसान की परछाईं देखी।
GU: અંધારું હતું, પણ મેં ચોક્કસ કોઈ વ્યક્તિનો પડછાયો જોયો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a物陰monokageものかげa hiding place (behind an object)
b人影hitokageひとかげa human silhouette/figure

Why: The verb 見た (saw) needs an object you can visually see — 人影 (the shape of a person) fits; 物陰 names a location (behind something), not a thing you "see" directly.

Q3

彼女は（a.口が堅い　b.頭が固い）から何でも相談できる。Kanojo wa (a. kuchi ga katai b. atama ga katai) kara nandemo soudan dekiru.

EN: She keeps secrets, so I can consult her about anything.
HI: वह राज़दार है, इसलिए मैं उससे कुछ भी सलाह ले सकता/सकती हूँ।
GU: તે રહસ્ય જાળવે છે, એટલે હું તેની સાથે કંઈ પણ સલાહ-મસલત કરી શકું છું.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a口が堅いkuchi ga kataiくちが かたいtight-lipped, trustworthy with secrets
b頭が固いatama ga kataiあたまが かたいstubborn, inflexible thinker

Why: Being consultable about "anything" depends on trusting her not to leak secrets — 口が堅い (tight-lipped); 頭が固い describes a stubborn, closed-minded person, which would make her a worse confidant, not a better one.

Q4

秘書の仕事は（a.気が荒い　b.気が利く）人でなければできないだろう。Hisho no shigoto wa (a. ki ga arai b. ki ga kiku) hito de nakereba dekinai darou.

EN: A secretary's job probably can't be done except by someone who is smart and considerate.
HI: सचिव का काम शायद केवल वही कर सकता है जो समझदार और ख़याल रखने वाला हो।
GU: સેક્રેટરીનું કામ કદાચ ફક્ત તે જ કરી શકે જે સમજદાર અને ખ્યાલ રાખનારું હોય.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a気が荒いki ga araiきが あらいhas a violent/rough temper
b気が利くki ga kikuきが きくsmart and considerate

Why: A good secretary needs tact and quick thinking — 気が利く; 気が荒い (a rough temper) is the opposite of a quality you'd want in that role.

Q5

もう来ないかと思ったころに、やっと彼は（a.姿を現した　b.正体を現した）。Mou konai ka to omotta koro ni, yatto kare wa (a. sugata o arawashita b. shoutai o arawashita).

EN: Just when we thought he wasn't coming, he finally showed up.
HI: जब हमें लगने लगा था कि वह अब नहीं आएगा, तभी आख़िरकार वह सामने आ गया।
GU: જ્યારે લાગવા લાગ્યું કે તે નહીં આવે, ત્યારે છેવટે તે પ્રગટ થયો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a姿を現したsugata o arawashitaすがたを あらわしたappeared, showed up
b正体を現したshoutai o arawashitaしょうたいを あらわしたrevealed his true (hidden) identity

Why: Someone simply arriving late is 姿を現す (to appear/show up); 正体を現す is reserved for unmasking a hidden or disguised true identity — a much more dramatic meaning than "finally arrived."

Q6

（a.効き目　b.網の目）が粗いと、小さい魚は捕れない。(a. Kikime b. Ami no me) ga arai to, chiisai sakana wa torenai.

EN: If the mesh of the net is coarse, you can't catch small fish.
HI: अगर जाल का जाला मोटा (बड़े छेद वाला) हो, तो छोटी मछलियाँ नहीं पकड़ी जा सकतीं।
GU: જો જાળના કાણાં મોટા હોય, તો નાની માછલીઓ પકડી શકાતી નથી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a効き目kikimeききめeffectiveness (of medicine etc.)
b網の目ami no meあみの めthe mesh of a net

Why: 粗い (coarse) modifying a physical mesh size only makes sense with 網の目 (the net's mesh); 効き目 (effectiveness) isn't something that can be physically "coarse."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is built around same-reading, easily-confused kanji — every 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ question above is really testing one of these pairs. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
空き巣akisu A sneak/house thief People — a burglar who enters an empty house. Home-security contexts, locking doors.
鳥の巣tori no su A bird's nest A literal nest built by a bird. Nature/wildlife contexts.
人影hitokage A human silhouette/figure Something you visually perceive — the shape of a person. "I saw a figure in the dark."
物陰monokage A hiding place A location behind/shielded by an object. "He hid behind the tree."
口が堅いkuchi ga katai Tight-lipped, trustworthy with secrets About discretion — won't leak what they're told. Praising a trustworthy confidant.
頭が固いatama ga katai Stubborn, inflexible About closed-mindedness, not secrecy. Criticizing someone set in their ways.
探すsagasu To look for (a thing/goal) Object is something you want to find or obtain. A job, a house, a lost item you own.
捜すsagasu To search for (a person) Object is a missing person or a suspect. Police searching for a criminal or a missing child.
姿を現すsugata o arawasu To appear, show up Ordinary arrival of someone expected. "He finally showed up to the meeting."
正体を現すshoutai o arawasu To unmask, reveal true identity A hidden/disguised true nature is exposed. A villain's true identity is revealed.
墨・隅・炭sumi / sumi / sumi Ink / a corner / charcoal Three unrelated kanji, one identical kun-yomi. Context alone decides which word is meant.
Exam trap: すみ is read by three completely unrelated kanji in this day's own list — 墨 (ink), 隅 (corner), 炭 (charcoal) — with zero shared meaning. Whenever a 漢字読み question gives you すみ in isolation, you must lean entirely on the surrounding context (writing? a room? a barbecue?) to choose the right kanji.
