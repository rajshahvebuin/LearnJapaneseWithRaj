練習Ⅰ — read a/b, then choose the correct option

Q1

交通費は会社から（a.支給　b.支持）される。Koutsuuhi wa kaisha kara (a. shikyuu b. shiji) sareru.

EN: Transportation costs are paid by the company.
HI: यातायात का ख़र्च कंपनी द्वारा दिया जाता है।
GU: મુસાફરીનો ખર્ચ કંપની દ્વારા ચૂકવવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a支給shikyuuしきゅうto pay out/provide (an allowance)
b支持shijiしじto support (an idea/party — wrong word)

Why: 支給 is for a company/organization paying out money or goods; 支持 is for supporting a person, party, or opinion, not for issuing an allowance.

Q2

野外音楽会の開催を（a.企画　b.避難）する。Yagai ongakkai no kaisai o (a. kikaku b. hinan) suru.

EN: Plan the holding of an outdoor concert.
HI: खुले मैदान में संगीत समारोह आयोजित करने की योजना बनाना।
GU: ખુલ્લા મેદાનમાં સંગીત કાર્યક્રમ યોજવાનું આયોજન કરવું.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a企画kikakuきかくto plan (an event)
b避難hinanひなんto evacuate (wrong word entirely)

Why: 企画する is the verb for planning/organizing an event; 避難する means to evacuate to safety and has no connection to concert planning.

Q3

ハンドル（a.指示　b.操作）を誤って事故を起こす。Handoru (a. shiji b. sousa) o ayamatte jiko o okosu.

EN: Cause an accident through a steering error.
HI: स्टीयरिंग चलाने में ग़लती से दुर्घटना हो जाना।
GU: સ્ટિયરિંગ ચલાવવામાં ભૂલથી અકસ્માત થવો.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a指示shijiしじan instruction (wrong word)
b操作sousaそうさhandling/operating (a control)

Why: ハンドル操作 (steering operation) is the set phrase for physically handling a wheel/control; 指示 refers to giving/receiving verbal instructions, not physically operating machinery.

Q4

経済状況は（a.以前　b.依然）として厳しい。Keizai joukyou wa (a. izen b. izen) toshite kibishii.

EN: The economic situation remains severe.
HI: आर्थिक स्थिति अब भी कठिन बनी हुई है।
GU: આર્થિક પરિસ્થિતિ હજુ પણ કપરી છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a以前izenいぜんformerly, previously (wrong word)
b依然izenいぜんstill, remaining unchanged

Why: 依然として is the fixed adverbial phrase for "remaining unchanged"; 以前 simply means "before/formerly" as a point in time and can't take としてin this sense.

Q5

（a.形成　b.形勢）が逆転し、チームは優勝した。(a. Keisei b. Keisei) ga gyakuten shi, chiimu wa yuushou shita.

EN: The situation reversed, and the team won the championship.
HI: परिस्थिति पलट गई, और टीम चैंपियन बनी।
GU: પરિસ્થિતિ પલટાઈ, અને ટીમ ચેમ્પિયન બની.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a形成keiseiけいせいformation/development (wrong word)
b形勢keiseiけいせいthe state of a competitive situation

Why: 形勢逆転 (a reversal of the game situation) is the book's own listed compound; 形成 refers to the formation/development of something (like personality), which doesn't "reverse" in a match.

Q6

台風のため、電車が一時（a.不通　b.非難）になった。Taifuu no tame, densha ga ichiji (a. futsuu b. hinan) ni natta.

EN: Because of the typhoon, trains were temporarily suspended.
HI: तूफ़ान की वजह से, ट्रेनें कुछ समय के लिए बंद हो गईं।
GU: વાવાઝોડાને કારણે, ટ્રેનો થોડા સમય માટે બંધ થઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a不通futsuuふつうout of service/suspended
b非難hinanひなんcriticism (wrong word entirely)

Why: 電車が不通になる is the set phrase for train service being suspended; 非難 means to criticize/blame someone and has nothing to do with service disruptions.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Nearly every cluster in §1 today is already a same-reading confusion set. Here are the pairs directly tested in the exercises above, plus the day's two biggest three-way traps:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
普通futsuu Ordinary, a local train Normal/unremarkable. "An ordinary day," "a local (all-stops) train."
不通futsuu Out of service, cut off A line or contact has stopped functioning. "Trains suspended," "no contact for months."
指示shiji An instruction Telling someone what to do. "Follow the staff's instructions."
支持shiji Support (of an idea/party) Backing a cause, person, or opinion. "Support a political party," "approval rating."
意義igi Significance, worth How meaningful/valuable something is. "A meaningful job."
異議igi An objection A formal disagreement raised. "Raise an objection," "no objection."
異義igi A different meaning Used almost only inside 同音異義語 (homonym). Describing words that sound alike but mean differently.
公演kouen A staged performance Theater, opera, concerts. "A Tokyo performance."
講演kouen A lecture/talk An academic or informational speech. "Ask a professor to lecture."
後援kouen Backing/sponsorship Organizational support behind an event. "A support association's office."
Exam trap: こうえん is shared by three unrelated words — 公演 (a performance), 講演 (a lecture), 後援 (sponsorship) — and いぎ by another three — 意義, 異議, 異義. Both are classic N1 漢字読み traps precisely because news and academic contexts constantly use all of them in similar-sounding sentences.
