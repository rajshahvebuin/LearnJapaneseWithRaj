Q1

磁石の針が北の方向を（a.指して　b.計って）いる。Jishaku no hari ga kita no houkou o (a. sashite b. hakatte) iru.

EN: The compass needle is pointing north.
HI: कम्पास की सुई उत्तर दिशा दिखा रही है।
GU: હોકાયંત્રની સોય ઉત્તર દિશા બતાવી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a指してsashiteさしてpointing (toward a direction)
b計ってhakatteはかってmeasuring (a number/time — wrong verb)

Why: 北を指す is the fixed phrase for a needle/arrow "pointing" at a direction; 計る is for measuring counts or time, not indicating direction.

Q2

自ら命を（a.絶つ　b.犯す）若者が増えている。Mizukara inochi o (a. tatsu b. okasu) wakamono ga fuete iru.

EN: The number of young people who take their own lives is increasing.
HI: अपनी जान लेने वाले युवाओं की संख्या बढ़ रही है।
GU: પોતાનો જીવ લેનારા યુવાનોની સંખ્યા વધી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a絶つtatsuたつto end/cut off (one's life)
b犯すokasuおかすto commit (a crime — wrong verb)

Why: 命を絶つ is the set phrase for ending one's own life; 犯す pairs with 罪 (a crime) or 法 (a law), not with 命 (a life).

Q3

「反対」と答えた人が過半数を（a.締めて　b.占めて）いる。"Hantai" to kotaeta hito ga kahansuu o (a. shimete b. shimete) iru.

EN: Those who answered "opposed" make up a majority.
HI: "विरोध में" जवाब देने वाले लोग बहुमत में हैं।
GU: "વિરોધ" જવાબ આપનારા લોકો બહુમતીમાં છે.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a締めてshimeteしめてfastening/tightening (wrong verb)
b占めてshimeteしめてoccupying (a share/majority)

Why: 過半数を占める is the fixed phrase for "constitute/make up a majority"; 締める is for physically fastening (a belt, a lid), not for statistical proportions — both share the reading しめる.

Q4

金を渡して便宜を（a.図る　b.謀る）よう指示した。Kane o watashite bengi o (a. hakaru b. hakaru) you shiji shita.

EN: He instructed [them] to hand over money and arrange favorable treatment.
HI: उसने पैसे देकर सुविधा दिलवाने का निर्देश दिया।
GU: તેણે પૈસા આપીને સગવડ કરાવવાની સૂચના આપી.
OptionJapaneseReadingEnglish
a図るhakaruはかるarrange/plan for (convenience, a favor)
b謀るhakaruはかるplot/conspire (wrong collocation)

Why: 便宜を図る is the book's own fixed phrase for "arranging convenience/favorable treatment"; 謀る is reserved for plotting something like an assassination or coup, and doesn't collocate with 便宜.

仕事が忙しすぎて家庭を（a.省みる　b.顧みる）余裕がない。Shigoto ga isogashi-sugite katei o (a. kaerimiru b. kaerimiru) yoyuu ga nai.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is built around same-reading, easily-confused kanji — every 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ question above is really testing one of these pairs. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
犯すokasu To commit (a crime, mistake) Breaking a law, rule, or moral code. "Commit a crime," "violate the law."
冒すokasu To brave/risk Knowingly proceeding despite danger. "Take risks," "brave the elements."
侵すokasu To invade/encroach on Intruding into territory or rights that belong to someone/somewhere else. "Invade territory," "violate privacy," "afflicted by disease."
図るhakaru To arrange/plan for A (often benign) plan toward convenience or an attempt at something. "Arrange a favor," "attempt suicide."
謀るhakaru To plot/conspire A scheme, usually illicit or violent. "Plot an assassination."
顧みるkaerimiru To look back on/attend to Directed outward — history, one's family. "Review the past," "neglect one's family."
省みるkaerimiru To reflect on oneself Directed inward — one's own conduct. "Reflect on/repent one's own actions."
望むnozomu To hope for, wish A desire for a future outcome. "Hope for an improvement."
臨むnozomu To face, confront Formally standing before an event or place. "Face an exam," "a room facing the sea."
Exam trap: おかす is shared by three unrelated kanji: 犯す (break a law/rule), 冒す (brave a known danger), 侵す (encroach on territory, rights, or one's health). All three surface constantly in N1 reading passages about crime, risk, and rights — a 漢字書き question testing おかす in isolation is unanswerable without checking exactly what is being "wronged" (a law? a risk? someone else's territory?).
