問題1 — choose the reading of the underlined word (1問4点)

Q1

炭には脱臭、つまり臭いをとる効果がある。Sumi ni wa dasshuu, tsumari nioi o toru kouka ga aru.

EN: Charcoal has a deodorizing effect — that is, it removes odors.
HI: कोयले में गंध सोखने का असर होता है, यानी वह बदबू दूर करता है।
GU: કોલસામાં ગંધ દૂર કરવાની અસર હોય છે, એટલે કે તે વાસ દૂર કરે છે.
#Reading
1あぶら
2たん
3ほのお
4すみ

Why: Day 1's すみ group — 炭 (charcoal) shares the kun-yomi すみ with 墨 (ink) and 隅 (corner).

1かいしん
2かわしん
3かくしん
4こうしん

Why: Day 6's かくしん group — 革新 (innovation) shares this reading with 核心 (the core) and 確信 (conviction).

問題2 — write the underlined word in kanji (1問4点)

Q11

りんごのかわをむく。Ringo no kawa o muku.

EN: Peel an apple.
HI: सेब का छिलका उतारना।
GU: સફરજનની છાલ ઉતારવી.
#Kanji
1果
2革
3実
4皮

Why: Day 1's かわ group — 皮 is the kanji for a natural peel/skin (fruit, animal), distinct from 革 (tanned leather).

Q12

言葉では言いあらわせない。Kotoba de wa ii-arawasenai.

EN: It cannot be expressed in words.
HI: इसे शब्दों में बयान नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: તેને શબ્દોમાં વ્યક્ત ન કરી શકાય.
#Kanji
1荒わせない
2現せない
3表せない
4粗わせない

Why: Day 1's あらわす group — 言葉に表す ("put into words") uses 表す; 現す is for appearing/unmasking, not expressing in language.

Q13

問題の解決につとめる。Mondai no kaiketsu ni tsutomeru.

HI: समस्या सुलझाने का प्रयास करना।
GU: સમસ્યા ઉકેલવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવો.
#Kanji
1努める
2勤める
3務める
4修める

Why: Day 2's つとめる group — 問題の解決に努める (striving to solve a problem) is the book's own listed phrase for 努める.

Q14

受付はしめきりました。Uketsuke wa shimekirimashita.

EN: Reception has closed (the deadline has passed).
HI: पंजीकरण की अंतिम तिथि निकल चुकी है।
GU: નોંધણીની અંતિમ તારીખ પૂરી થઈ ગઈ છે.
#Kanji
1絞め切り
2締め切り
3占め切り
4閉め切り

Why: Day 3's しめる group lists 締め切る specifically as "to close by a deadline"; 閉める is for physically closing doors/lids, 絞める is to strangle, 占める is to occupy a share.

Q15

合格するまでチョコレートをたちます。Goukaku suru made chokoreeto o tachimasu.

EN: I will give up chocolate until I pass.
HI: पास होने तक चॉकलेट छोड़ दूँगा/दूँगी।
GU: પાસ થાઉં ત્યાં સુધી ચોકલેટ છોડી દઈશ.
#Kanji
1建ちます
2絶ちます
3裁ちます
4断ちます

Why: Day 3's たつ group lists both 酒を断つ (voluntarily giving up a habit/vice) and 食事を絶つ／断つ (fasting) — reasoned here as 断つ, since giving up an indulgence like chocolate as an act of willpower parallels 酒を断つ more closely than 絶つ's harsher "sever completely" sense (as in 命を絶つ). Flagged as the one genuinely ambiguous answer this week — the book's own entries show overlap between 絶つ and 断つ for "fasting/quitting," and without the official 別冊 key this is a reasoned best guess, not a certainty.

Test-day strategy: Nearly every trap this week was a same-reading, different-kanji homophone — not a shared radical like earlier weeks.
