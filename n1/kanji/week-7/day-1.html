JLPT N1 · Kanji · Week 7 — いろいろな覚え方をしよう (Iroiro na oboekata o shiyou)

1日目 — 困る・原因・囚人 (Komaru, Gen'in, Shuujin)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.108–109 (PDF pages 109–110). Note: this section of the scan runs one page ahead of the printed footer numbers (PDF index = printed page + 1, confirmed against the 第7週 section-title page and this day's own "1日目" heading) — different from Week 1, where PDF index matched the printed page exactly.
Theme
13 clusters of kanji grouped by visual resemblance ("見た目が似ているもの①" — things that look alike ①) rather than by shared reading — this week's whole point is trying out a different memorization angle each day.
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key — flagged transparently, same policy as Week 1.
Notes
Instead of grouping kanji by a shared component + on-yomi (Week 1's approach), this day groups kanji that simply *look* alike on the page — a stroke or two apart — so you learn to tell them apart by sight, then anchor each one with a distinct example word.

Q1

神社で試験の合格を（　）。Jinja de shiken no goukaku o (___).

Full sentence: 神社で試験の合格を祈る。Jinja de shiken no goukaku o inoru.
EN: I pray at the shrine for success on the exam.
HI: मंदिर में परीक्षा में सफलता के लिए प्रार्थना करता/करती हूँ।
GU: મંદિરમાં પરીક્ષામાં સફળતા માટે પ્રાર્થના કરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a折るorufold/break (wrong verb entirely)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b祈るinoruto prayप्रार्थना करनाપ્રાર્થના કરવી

Why: 合格を祈る ("pray for success") is the fixed collocation; 折る means to physically fold or break something, which cannot take 合格 as its object.

Q2

結婚式で（　）の愛を誓う。Kekkonshiki de (___) no ai o chikau.

Full sentence: 結婚式で永遠の愛を誓う。Kekkonshiki de eien no ai o chikau.
EN: At the wedding, they vow eternal love.
HI: शादी में वे हमेशा के प्रेम की क़सम खाते हैं।
GU: લગ્નમાં તેઓ કાયમી પ્રેમના શપથ લે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a永遠eieneternityअनंतताઅનંતતા
b因縁innenkarmic bond, fate (wrong register)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 永遠の愛 ("eternal love") is the standard wedding-vow phrase; 因縁 describes a fated/karmic connection (often ominous), not a romantic vow.

Q3

踏み切りで立ち（　）していた車に電車が突っ込んだ。Fumikiri de tachi (___) shite ita kuruma ni densha ga tsukkonda.

Full sentence: 踏み切りで立ち往生していた車に電車が突っ込んだ。Fumikiri de tachi oujou shite ita kuruma ni densha ga tsukkonda.
EN: A train crashed into a car that was stranded at the railway crossing.
HI: रेलवे क्रॉसिंग पर फँसी कार से ट्रेन टकरा गई।
GU: રેલવે ક્રોસિંગ પર ફસાયેલી કારને ટ્રેન અથડાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a往生oujoubeing stranded (立ち往生)फँस जानाફસાઈ જવું

Why: 立ち往生 is the fixed idiom for a vehicle stuck/stranded in place; 困惑 describes a person's mental bewilderment, not a stalled car.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Because this day groups kanji purely by shape, nearly every pair below is a "which stroke changes the meaning" trap pulled straight from the exercises above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
祈るinoru To pray 礻 (altar/ritual radical) — a spiritual act. Praying for exam success, a wish, a blessing.
折るoru To fold, to break 扌 (hand radical) — a physical action. Folding paper, breaking a bone or a branch.
立ち往生tachi oujou Being stranded, stuck in place Fixed idiom for a vehicle/person unable to move. "The car stalled at the crossing."
困惑konwaku Bewilderment, perplexity A person's mental/emotional state, not physical immobility. "I was confused by the sudden change."
暮れるkureru / 暮らしkurashi Dusk falls / livelihood, daily life Bottom radical 日 (sun) — time-of-day and day-to-day life. "Evening falls," "our daily life."
墓haka A grave Bottom radical 土 (earth) — literally buried in soil. "Visiting a grave," "a graveyard."
緑midori Green (colour) 糸 (thread) radical + 录 — originally a dyed-thread colour name. "Green," "fresh greenery."
録roku A record 釒 (metal) radical + 录 — engraved/inscribed record. "A world record," "registration."
老いるoiru To grow old Standalone kanji for "old" — no phonetic addition. Describing a person or thing aging.
秀でるhiideru To excel, be outstanding 禾 (grain) + 乃 — grain standing tall above the rest. "She excels at writing."
Exam trap: Whenever a 漢字読み/書き question shows you 墓・暮・募・慕・幕, cover the top (they're all 莫) and look only at the bottom radical — 土・日・力・心・巾 — that one stroke set is the entire difference between "grave," "dusk," "recruit," "adore," and "curtain."
