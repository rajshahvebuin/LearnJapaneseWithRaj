4日目 — 選挙権・著作権 (Senkyoken, Chosakuken)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.114–115 (PDF pages 115–116; same +1 PDF-to-printed offset confirmed on Days 1–3).
Theme
Day 3's mirror image: 語彙の後ろにつく漢字 (kanji that attach to the BACK/end of a vocabulary word), again by learner level — 初級 (8 kanji), 中級 (12 kanji), 上級 (8 kanji).
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
Where Day 3's kanji sat in front of a word (再～), today's kanji are suffix-like: they close out a word and name what kind of thing it is — a person (～員, ～師, ～士), a place (～室, ～場, ～街), a right (～権), a machine (～機), and so on.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

祖母は耳が遠いので（　）が必要だ。Sobo wa mimi ga tooi node (___) ga hitsuyou da.

Full sentence: 祖母は耳が遠いので補聴器が必要だ。Sobo wa mimi ga tooi node hochouki ga hitsuyou da.
EN: My grandmother is hard of hearing, so she needs a hearing aid.
HI: मेरी दादी/नानी को कम सुनाई देता है, इसलिए उन्हें श्रवण यंत्र की ज़रूरत है।
GU: મારી દાદી/નાનીને ઓછું સંભળાય છે, તેથી તેમને સાંભળવાના સાધનની જરૂર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a聴診器choushinkia stethoscope (wrong device)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b補聴器hochoukia hearing aidश्रवण यंत्रસાંભળવાનું સાધન

Why: 補聴器 ("hearing aid") is worn to help hearing; 聴診器 is the doctor's stethoscope, a completely different device that just happens to share the reading しんき/ちょうき pattern.

Q2

習ったことはないので、（　）で花を生けます。Naratta koto wa nai node, (___) de hana o ikemasu.

Full sentence: 習ったことはないので、自己流で花を生けます。Naratta koto wa nai node, jikoryuu de hana o ikemasu.
EN: I've never taken lessons, so I arrange flowers my own self-taught way.
HI: मैंने कभी सीखा नहीं है, इसलिए अपने ही तरीक़े से फूल सजाता/सजाती हूँ।
GU: મેં ક્યારેય શીખ્યું નથી, તેથી હું મારી પોતાની રીતે ફૂલો ગોઠવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a自己流jikoryuuone's own (self-taught) wayअपना ख़ुद का तरीक़ाપોતાની આગવી રીત
b日本流nihonryuuthe Japanese way (contradicts "never learned")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: "Never took lessons" implies no formal style was learned, so it must be 自己流 ("my own way"); 日本流 names a specific, learnable national style, which contradicts the sentence.

Q3

伝統芸能の後継者不足に（　）を抱く。Dentou geinou no koukeisha busoku ni (___) o idaku.

Full sentence: 伝統芸能の後継者不足に危機感を抱く。Dentou geinou no koukeisha busoku ni kikikan o idaku.
EN: He feels a sense of crisis about the shortage of successors in traditional arts.
HI: पारंपरिक कलाओं में उत्तराधिकारियों की कमी को लेकर संकट का एहसास होता है।
GU: પરંપરાગત કળાઓમાં અનુગામીઓની અછત અંગે સંકટની લાગણી થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a危機感kikikansense of crisisसंकट का एहसासસંકટની લાગણી
b充実感juujitsukana feeling of fulfillment (wrong emotion)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A shortage of successors is worrying, matching 危機感 ("sense of crisis"); 充実感 describes a positive feeling of fulfillment, the opposite emotional direction.

Q4

土地の（　）をめぐって争う。Tochi no (___) o megutte arasou.

Full sentence: 土地の所有権をめぐって争う。Tochi no shoyuuken o megutte arasou.
EN: They fight over the ownership of the land.
HI: ज़मीन के स्वामित्व को लेकर विवाद होता है।
GU: જમીનની માલિકી અંગે વિવાદ થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a所有権shoyuukenownershipस्वामित्वમાલિકી હક
b著作権chosakukencopyright (wrong domain — creative works, not land)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 所有権 applies to physical property like land; 著作権 specifically protects creative/intellectual works, not real estate.

Q5

今日は（　）に欠け、作業が進まない。Kyou wa (___) ni kake, sagyou ga susumanai.

Full sentence: 今日は集中力に欠け、作業が進まない。Kyou wa shuuchuuryoku ni kake, sagyou ga susumanai.
EN: Today I'm lacking in concentration, and the work isn't progressing.
HI: आज एकाग्रता की कमी है, और काम आगे नहीं बढ़ रहा।
GU: આજે એકાગ્રતાની ઉણપ છે, અને કામ આગળ વધતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a疲労感hiroukana feeling of fatigue (doesn't fit ～に欠け)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b集中力shuuchuuryokuconcentrationएकाग्रताએકાગ્રતા

Why: ～に欠ける ("to lack ~") pairs naturally with an ability noun like 集中力; 疲労感 is a feeling you "have," not an ability you "lack."

EN: This shutter is electrically operated.
HI: यह शटर बिजली से चलता है।
GU: આ શટર ઇલેક્ટ્રિકથી ચાલે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a計算機keisankia calculator (wrong noun category)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Same pattern as Day 3, mirrored to the end of the word — several back-attaching kanji share a reading or a rough sense but only one fits each headword:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
補聴器hochouki A hearing aid 器 for a general tool/instrument worn or used by a person. Hearing aids, stethoscopes — instruments.
洗濯機sentakuki A washing machine 機 for a powered machine/appliance. Washing machines, calculators, fans — machinery.
著作権chosakuken Copyright (over creative work) 権 marking a legal right or authority. Voting rights, copyright, veto power.
所有権shoyuuken Ownership (of property) Also 権, but the domain is physical property, not creative work. Land disputes, property titles.
危機感kikikan A sense of crisis 感 for a subjective feeling/emotional state. "I feel a sense of crisis about X."
集中力shuuchuuryoku The ability to concentrate 力 for a measurable ability/capacity. "I lack concentration/understanding."
自己流jikoryuu One's own (self-taught) way 流 for a personal style or method, no formal school. Doing something without formal training.
電動式dendoushiki Electrically operated 式 for a mode/format of operation or style. "This device runs electrically," "Japanese-style."
Exam trap: 機・器・式・師・士 all describe "the thing/person that does X," but each has a strict lane: 機 = powered machine, 器 = hand tool/instrument, 式 = style/mode, 師 = a licensed professional (看護師), 士 = a qualified specialist (保育士). Swapping any two produces a non-word, as tested throughout 練習Ⅱ above.
