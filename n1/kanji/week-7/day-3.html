JLPT N1 · Kanji · Week 7 — いろいろな覚え方をしよう (Iroiro na oboekata o shiyou)

3日目 — 再検査・再編成 (Saikensa, Saihensei)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.112–113 (PDF pages 113–114; same +1 PDF-to-printed offset confirmed on Days 1–2).
Theme
27 kanji grouped by a new memorization angle: 語彙の前につく漢字 (kanji that attach to the FRONT of a vocabulary word), organized by learner level — 初級 (elementary, 10 kanji), 中級 (intermediate, 12 kanji), 上級 (advanced, 7 kanji).
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
Instead of grouping by shape (Days 1–2), this day groups kanji by grammatical role: each one is a prefix-like kanji that can be bolted onto the front of many different words to add a consistent shade of meaning (再～ = "again," 諸～ = "various," 超～ = "ultra-/beyond normal," etc.).

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

新人のアナウンサーが原稿を（　）読みしている。Shinjin no anaunsaa ga genkou o (___) yomi shite iru.

Full sentence: 新人のアナウンサーが原稿を棒読みしている。Shinjin no anaunsaa ga genkou o bouyomi shite iru.
EN: The rookie announcer is reading the script in a flat monotone.
HI: नया उद्घोषक स्क्रिप्ट को बेजान लहज़े में पढ़ रहा है।
GU: નવો ઉદ્ઘોષક સ્ક્રિપ્ટ એકધારા સૂરમાં વાંચી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a棒boumonotone (棒読み)बेजान लहज़ाએકધારો સૂર
b誤gomistaken (誤読み isn't a standard word here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 棒読み is the fixed word for reading in a flat, expressionless tone, exactly describing a rookie reading from a script; 誤読 exists as "misreading" but not as "誤読み" in this construction.

Q2

ほぼ準備はできたので、あとは各自で（　）調整してください。Hobo junbi wa dekita node, ato wa kakuji de (___) chousei shite kudasai.

Full sentence: ほぼ準備はできたので、あとは各自で微調整してください。Hobo junbi wa dekita node, ato wa kakuji de bichousei shite kudasai.
EN: Preparations are mostly done, so please make your own small adjustments from here.
HI: तैयारी लगभग पूरी हो गई है, इसलिए बाक़ी बारीक़ समायोजन ख़ुद कर लें।
GU: તૈયારી લગભગ પૂરી થઈ ગઈ છે, તેથી બાકીના નાના ફેરફારો જાતે કરી લેજો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a豆mametiny (豆調整 isn't a real word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b微bifine, subtle (微調整)सूक्ष्म समायोजनસૂક્ષ્મ ગોઠવણ

Why: 微調整 ("fine-tuning") is the standard word for small adjustments; 豆 attaches to nouns like 知識 or 電球, not to 調整.

GU: આ દવા સારી અસર કરે છે, પણ તેની આડઅસર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a副fukuside (副作用)दुष्प्रभावઆડઅસર
b助joassistant (助作用 isn't a word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 副作用 ("side effect") is the fixed medical term; 助 attaches to roles like 監督 or 動詞, not to 作用.

Q4

（　）事情により、この度会社を辞めることになりました。(___) jijou ni yori, kono tabi kaisha o yameru koto ni narimashita.

Full sentence: 諸事情により、この度会社を辞めることになりました。Shojijou ni yori, kono tabi kaisha o yameru koto ni narimashita.

HI: विभिन्न परिस्थितियों के कारण, मैंने कंपनी छोड़ने का निर्णय लिया है।
GU: વિવિધ સંજોગોને કારણે, મેં કંપની છોડવાનો નિર્ણય લીધો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a諸shovarious (諸事情)विभिन्न परिस्थितियाँવિવિધ સંજોગો
b急kyuusudden (急事情 isn't a set phrase)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 諸事情 is the standard euphemism for "various (unspecified) reasons," commonly used when resigning; 急 would need a different noun, like 急用.

Q5

GU: તેનામાં એવી આવડત છે, એ મારા માટે તદ્દન નવી વાત છે.

Q6

そうしていただけるなら、私にとっては（　）都合です。Sou shite itadakeru nara, watashi ni totte wa (___) tsugou desu.

Full sentence: そうしていただけるなら、私にとっては好都合です。Sou shite itadakeru nara, watashi ni totte wa kou tsugou desu.
EN: If you could do that, it would be convenient for me.
HI: अगर आप ऐसा कर सकें, तो यह मेरे लिए फ़ायदेमंद होगा।
GU: જો તમે એમ કરી શકો, તો તે મારા માટે ફાયદાકારક રહેશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a高kouhigh (高都合 isn't a word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 好都合 ("convenient/favorable") is the fixed word; 高 attaches to measurable things (高性能, 高学歴), not to an abstract 都合. Both share the same reading こう, a common trap.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's traps are less about kanji shape and more about which front-attaching kanji actually collocates with the noun that follows — the same reading (こう, きゅう) hides very different prefixes:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
高（都合ではなく）性能kouseinou High (performance/qualifications) 高 attaches to measurable, gradable nouns. 高性能, 高学歴 — things with a scale.
好都合koutsugou Favorable, convenient 好 attaches to abstract nouns describing a state as "good/bad." 好都合, 好青年 — a state or type of person.
副作用fukusayou A side effect 副 marks something secondary/attached to a main thing. 副作用, 副都心 — a "second" version.
助動詞jodoushi An auxiliary verb 助 marks something that assists a main role. 助動詞, 助監督 — an assistant/helper role.
初耳hatsumimi News to me, first time hearing 初 marks the very first occurrence. 初耳, 初公開 — a debut/first instance.
諸事情shojijou Various (unspecified) circumstances 諸 marks an unspecified plural — "various." 諸問題, 諸事情 — deliberately vague plurals.
急停車kyuuteisha A sudden stop 急 marks abruptness/suddenness of an action. 急停車, 急発進 — something happening abruptly.
再検査saikensa A reexamination 再 marks repetition — "once again." 再検査, 再編成, 再入国 — doing something a second time.
Exam trap: 高 and 好 share the reading こう and both can precede many nouns, but only one collocates with any given word — 高性能 (a measurable spec) vs 好都合 (a favorable state). Q6 above tests exactly this: 高都合 is not a word, only 好都合 is.
