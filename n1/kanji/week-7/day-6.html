JLPT N1 · Kanji · Week 7 — いろいろな覚え方をしよう (Iroiro na oboekata o shiyou)

6日目 — 再会する＝再び会う (Saikai suru = Futatabi au)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.118–119 (PDF pages 119–120; same +1 PDF-to-printed offset confirmed on Days 1–5).
Theme
The week's final new angle: 音訓いっしょに覚える (memorize on-yomi and kun-yomi together) — 29 stiff, bookish on-yomi words (mostly ～する verbs or ～な adjectives), each paired with a plain-Japanese kun-yomi paraphrase that means the same thing.
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
The book's own caveat applies here: on-yomi compounds can be used as-is in a sentence, but their kun-yomi paraphrase sometimes sounds unnatural if used directly in formal writing — the paraphrase is a memorization aid for meaning, not necessarily a drop-in substitute.

Q1

私は（負傷した→　　　）子犬を拾ってきた。Watashi wa (fushou shita → ___) koinu o hirotte kita.

Full sentence: 私は傷を負った子犬を拾ってきた。Watashi wa kizu o otta koinu o hirotte kita.
EN: I picked up a puppy that had been injured.
HI: मैं एक घायल पिल्ला उठा लाया/लाई।
GU: હું એક ઘાયલ ગલૂડિયું ઉપાડી લાવ્યો/લાવી.
On-yomiKun-yomi answerEnglishHindiGujarati
負傷したfushou shita傷を負ったkizu o ottahad been injuredघायल हुआ थाઘાયલ થયેલું હતું

Why: 負傷する decomposes literally to 傷を負う ("to bear/receive a wound"), following the day's title pattern (再会する＝再び会う).

GU: આ પાણીમાં ડૂબકી મારતી વખતે પહેરવાનો સૂટ છે.
On-yomiKun-yomi answerEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's "confusion" isn't between two kanji but between the formal on-yomi register and the everyday kun-yomi register for the same meaning — mixing up which register fits a sentence is the actual N1 trap:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
再会するsaikai suru To reunite Formal, written/spoken register — a single dense word. News reports, formal writing, self-introductions.
再び会うfutatabi au To meet again Plain, everyday register — spells the meaning out. Casual conversation, explaining the on-yomi word's meaning.
負傷するfushou suru To be injured Clinical/news register (police reports, medical charts). "Three people were injured in the accident."
傷を負うkizu o ou To sustain a wound Plain, descriptive register. Everyday storytelling, explaining what happened.
悲惨なhisan na Disastrous, tragic A single adjective covering the whole idea at once. Formal descriptions of tragedy or hardship.
悲しくて惨めなkanashikute mijime na Sad and miserable Splits the same idea into two plain adjectives. Everyday description, spoken Japanese.
Exam trap: The book's own caveat is the real trap here — an on-yomi word like 就職する can be used as-is in any sentence, but its kun-yomi gloss 職に就く sounds natural in some contexts and stilted in others (especially since 職 itself has no everyday kun-yomi reading). Treat every kun-yomi paraphrase in this list as a meaning aid first, not an automatic substitute.
