Source

Theme
29 kanji continuing "見た目が似ているもの②" (things that look alike ②) — same memorization approach as Day 1, a fresh batch of visually confusable kanji.
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes

Q1

東大を出て、官僚になり、（　）なエリートコースを歩む。Todai o dete, kanryou ni nari, (___) na eriito koosu o ayumu.

Full sentence: 東大を出て、官僚になり、典型的なエリートコースを歩む。Todai o dete, kanryou ni nari, tenkeiteki na eriito koosu o ayumu.
EN: Graduating from Tokyo University and becoming a bureaucrat, he walks the typical elite career path.
HI: टोक्यो विश्वविद्यालय से स्नातक होकर, नौकरशाह बनकर, वह एक विशिष्ट कुलीन करियर पथ पर चलता है।
GU: ટોક્યો યુનિવર્સિટીમાંથી સ્નાતક થઈ, અધિકારી બનીને, તે એક લાક્ષણિક ચુનંદા કારકિર્દી માર્ગ પર ચાલે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a典型的tenkeitekitypical, ideal exampleआदर्श, विशिष्टલાક્ષણિક
b象徴的shouchoutekisymbolic (wrong nuance)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 典型的なエリートコース ("the typical/textbook elite path") describes a well-known pattern; 象徴的 means "symbolic," which doesn't fit describing a career route.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's look-alike clusters are, again, the confusion pairs — pulled straight from the 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ items above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
遺言yuigon A will, last testament 遺 = leave behind/bequeath. Writing or reading a will.
派遣haken Dispatch (of staff) 遣 = send/dispatch. A temp agency sending workers.
侮るanadoru To belittle, look down on イ (person) radical — directed at someone else. "Don't underestimate a weaker opponent."
悔やむkuyamu To regret 忄 (heart) radical — directed at oneself. "I regret not studying harder."
殻kara A hard shell/husk 殳 radical — an empty hard covering. Eggshell, seashell, cigarette butt.
穀物kokumotsu Grain, cereals 禾 (grain) radical — the food itself. Rice, wheat, and other grains.
存zon (依存) To hold as belief/rely on Abstract — mental reliance or awareness. "Dependent on medication."
在zai (不在) To physically exist/be present Concrete — physical presence somewhere. "Absent from the office."
Exam trap: 遺 vs 遣 is the single most testable pair this week — both are common in N1 kanji-reading questions, and mixing them up (遺言 vs 遣言) changes "a will" into a non-word. Whenever you see ゆいごん, check that it's the "leave behind" 遺, not the "dispatch" 遣.
