問題4 — fill each blank with a kanji from the bank, each used only once (1問4点)

人の職業を表す漢字は医［21］や歌［22］、小説［23］などいろいろあるが、社会の変化とともに呼び方が変わったものもある。女性の仕事だった看護婦さんや保母さんは、男性もするようになったことから、それぞれ看護［24］、保育［25］となった。 Hito no shokugyou o arawasu kanji wa i [21] ya uta [22], shousetsu [23] nado iroiro aru ga, shakai no henka to tomo ni yobikata ga kawatta mono mo aru. Josei no shigoto datta kangofu-san ya hobo-san wa, dansei mo suru you ni natta koto kara, sorezore kango [24], hoiku [25] to natta.

Bank (each used once): 者　家　師　手　士

EN: There are various kanji for expressing a person's profession, such as 医者 (doctor), 歌手 (singer), and 小説家 (novelist), but some of these have changed as society has changed. Nursing and childcare, once considered women's jobs, are now also done by men, so 看護婦 became 看護師 (nurse) and 保母 became 保育士 (childcare worker).
HI: किसी व्यक्ति के पेशे को दर्शाने वाले कांजी कई तरह के होते हैं, जैसे 医者 (डॉक्टर), 歌手 (गायक), और 小説家 (उपन्यासकार), लेकिन समाज के बदलाव के साथ कुछ के नाम बदल गए हैं। पहले महिलाओं का काम मानी जाने वाली नर्सिंग और बाल-देखभाल अब पुरुष भी करने लगे हैं, इसलिए 看護婦 अब 看護師 (नर्स) और 保母 अब 保育士 (बाल देखभाल कर्मी) कहलाने लगे।
GU: વ્યક્તિના વ્યવસાયને દર્શાવતા કાંજી અનેક પ્રકારના હોય છે, જેમ કે 医者 (ડોક્ટર), 歌手 (ગાયક), અને 小説家 (નવલકથાકાર), પણ સમાજના બદલાવ સાથે કેટલાકનાં નામ બદલાયાં છે. પહેલાં સ્ત્રીઓનું કામ ગણાતી નર્સિંગ અને બાળ-સંભાળ હવે પુરુષો પણ કરવા લાગ્યા છે, તેથી 看護婦 હવે 看護師 (નર્સ) અને 保母 હવે 保育士 (બાળ સંભાળ કર્મચારી) કહેવાય છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A week-wide cheat sheet — one line per day's memorization angle, to drill right before the test:

DayApproachKey trap to remember
1見た目が似ているもの① — look-alike shapes困・因・囚 (囗+木/大/人); 墓・暮・募・慕・幕 (all built on 莫, differ only by bottom radical)
2見た目が似ているもの② — more look-alike shapes遺 (leave behind) vs 遣 (dispatch) — the week's single most testable pair
3語彙の前につく漢字 — kanji attaching to the FRONT of a word高 (measurable spec) vs 好 (favorable state) — same こう reading, different collocation
4語彙の後ろにつく漢字 — kanji attaching to the END of a word機 (machine) vs 器 (hand tool) vs 式 (style) vs 師/士 (licensed professional vs qualified specialist)
5助数詞 — counters/classifiers軒 (counts houses) vs 件 (counts abstract matters) — true homophones, different categories
6音訓いっしょに覚える — on-yomi word ↔ kun-yomi paraphrase再会する＝再び会う — the paraphrase explains the meaning but isn't always a natural substitute
Test-day strategy: This test draws on every day's angle at once — a look-alike shape trap (Q11–Q14), a front-attaching prefix (Q16–Q20), a back-attaching suffix (Q21–Q25), and straightforward readings mixing kanji from every day (Q1–Q10). When stuck on a reading, first ask which of the six angles the word belongs to, then recall that day's specific trap rather than guessing from the reading alone.
