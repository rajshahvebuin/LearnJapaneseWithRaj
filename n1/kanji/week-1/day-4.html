Q1

労働に対して（　）が与えられる。Roudou ni taishite (___) ga ataerareru.

Full sentence: 労働に対して報酬が与えられる。Roudou ni taishite houshuu ga ataerareru.
EN: A reward/compensation is given for labor.
HI: श्रम के बदले पारिश्रमिक दिया जाता है।
GU: શ્રમના બદલામાં મહેનતાણું આપવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a応酬oushuua retort, exchange (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b報酬houshuua reward, compensationपारिश्रमिक, इनामમહેનતાણું

Why: 報酬 is what you receive in exchange for labor; 応酬 describes a back-and-forth exchange of words (an argument/retort), not payment.

Q2

携帯電話が（　）する。Keitai denwa ga (___) suru.

Full sentence: 携帯電話が普及する。Keitai denwa ga fukyuu suru.
EN: Mobile phones become widespread.
HI: मोबाइल फ़ोन का प्रसार होता है।
GU: મોબાઇલ ફોનનો પ્રસાર થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a普遍fuhenuniversal (a philosophical state, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b普及fukyuuspread, diffusionप्रसारપ્રસાર

Why: 普及する describes something (a product/technology) becoming widely used; 普遍 describes a timeless, universal truth or quality — it doesn't describe a product spreading through a population.

Q3

突然の出来事に頭が（　）する。Totsuzen no dekigoto ni atama ga (___) suru.

Full sentence: 突然の出来事に頭が混乱する。Totsuzen no dekigoto ni atama ga konran suru.
EN: One's mind becomes confused by the sudden event.
HI: अचानक हुई घटना से दिमाग़ उलझ जाता है।
GU: અચાનક બનેલી ઘટનાથી મન મૂંઝાઈ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a混乱konranconfusion, disorder (of the mind/situation)उलझन, अफरा-तफरीમૂંઝવણ, અંધાધૂંધી
b混雑konzatsucongestion (of people/traffic, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 頭が混乱する ("my head is in confusion") describes mental disorder; 混雑 describes physical crowding of people or traffic — a head can't be "congested" in that sense.

Q4

疲労が（　）する。Hirou ga (___) suru.

Full sentence: 疲労が蓄積する。Hirou ga chikuseki suru.
EN: Fatigue accumulates/builds up.
HI: थकान जमा होती जाती है।
GU: થાક સંચિત થતો જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a蓄積chikusekiaccumulationसंचय, जमावસંચય
b消耗shoumouconsumption, being used up (wrong direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 蓄積する means something builds up/accumulates over time — fatigue piling up fits; 消耗する means something is used up/depleted (usually stamina or supplies), the opposite direction of the sentence.

HI: पड़ोसी देश के साथ समझौता करना।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above pairs two candidates that a test-taker could plausibly confuse. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
報酬houshuu A reward, compensation Payment received for work. "Compensation for labor."
応酬oushuu A retort, exchange A back-and-forth verbal exchange, often heated. "An exchange of insults."
普及fukyuu Spread, diffusion A product/technology becoming widely adopted over time. "Smartphones became widespread."
普遍fuhen Universal A quality/truth that holds everywhere, timelessly — not about adoption. "A universal value."
混乱konran Confusion, disorder Mental or situational chaos — a mind, plan, or system in disarray. "My mind went blank in confusion."
混雑konzatsu Congestion Physical crowding of people, traffic, or space. "The train station is congested."
蓄積chikuseki Accumulation Something builds up/piles up over time. "Fatigue/stress builds up."
消耗shoumou Consumption, exhaustion Something is used up/depleted — the opposite direction of accumulation. "Physical stamina is used up."
出張shutchou A business trip Traveling for work — a physical trip, not a claim. "He's away on a business trip."
主張shuchou An assertion, claim Voicing/insisting on an opinion or right. "To assert one's rights."
協定kyoutei An agreement, pact A formal agreement you conclude/sign. "Sign a pact with a neighboring country."
妥協dakyou A compromise Yielding ground in a dispute — an attitude, not a document. "He wouldn't compromise on price."
Exam trap: 混乱 (mental/situational confusion) vs 混雑 (physical crowding) is the single most testable pair today — both are extremely common N1 vocabulary that look almost identical, but one describes a mind or system in disarray and the other describes a crowded place. Check whether the sentence's subject is a person's head/a process (→混乱) or a physical space full of people (→混雑) before picking kanji.
