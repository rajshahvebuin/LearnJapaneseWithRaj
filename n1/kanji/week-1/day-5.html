5日目 — 麻・磨・摩・魔 (Ma, Ma, Ma, Ma)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.20–21 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
11 kanji clusters under a new sub-theme「違う部分に注意！①」(watch the different part! ①) — today's clusters are kanji that look almost identical and often share a reading, differing in just one small part.
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key.
Notes
The day's title "麻・磨・摩・魔" are four different kanji that all read マ — the book's opening question (治療 vs 治寮 vs 治僚) shows exactly why this day's "watch the different part" framing matters: get one stroke wrong and you've written a different word entirely.

Q1

手術の前に（　）をする。Shujutsu no mae ni (___) o suru.

Full sentence: 手術の前に麻酔をする。Shujutsu no mae ni masui o suru.
EN: Anesthesia is administered before surgery.
HI: सर्जरी से पहले बेहोश करने की दवा दी जाती है।
GU: સર્જરી પહેલાં બેભાન કરવાની દવા આપવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a摩擦masatsufriction (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b麻酔masuianesthesiaबेहोशी की दवाબેભાન કરવાની દવા

Why: 麻酔 is what's administered before surgery to numb pain; 摩擦 describes physical rubbing/friction between surfaces — the two share マ but are unrelated to medicine.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above pairs two visually or phonetically similar candidates. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
麻酔masui Anesthesia A medical procedure to numb pain/consciousness. "Anesthesia before surgery."
摩擦masatsu Friction Physical rubbing between two surfaces (also figurative: "friction" between people). "Friction between two countries."
自慢jiman Pride, boasting Taking/showing pride in something, often boastfully. "He boasted about his new car."
我慢gaman Patience, endurance Enduring/putting up with something difficult. "I'll put up with the inconvenience."
観察kansatsu Observation Watching something closely and systematically. "Observe plant growth."
勧誘kan'yuu Soliciting, an invitation Actively persuading someone to join/buy/participate. "A sales rep solicited new customers."
譲渡jouto A transfer (of ownership) Formally handing over property/rights to someone else. "Transfer land to an heir."
譲歩jouho A concession Yielding ground in a negotiation or argument. "Neither side would concede."
組織soshiki An organization A structured group of people (a company, a body). "Company reorganization."
編集henshuu Editing Revising/assembling text, film, or media. "Editing a magazine."
Exam trap: 譲渡 and 譲歩 are the single most testable pair today — both start with 譲 and read じょう, but 譲渡 hands over property/rights while 譲歩 gives ground in an argument. When you see 譲 in a sentence, check whether the object is something owned (→譲渡) or a position in a dispute (→譲歩) before choosing the second kanji.
