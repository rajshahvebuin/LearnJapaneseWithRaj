Q1

運転免許を（　）するために自動車学校に通う。Unten menkyo o (___) suru tame ni jidousha gakkou ni kayou.

Full sentence: 運転免許を取得するために自動車学校に通う。Unten menkyo o shutoku suru tame ni jidousha gakkou ni kayou.
EN: I go to driving school in order to obtain a driver's license.
HI: ड्राइविंग लाइसेंस हासिल करने के लिए मैं ड्राइविंग स्कूल जाता/जाती हूँ।
GU: ડ્રાઇવિંગ લાયસન્સ મેળવવા માટે હું ડ્રાઇવિંગ સ્કૂલ જાઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a取得shutokuacquisition, obtainingप्राप्ति, हासिल करनाપ્રાપ્તિ, મેળવવું
b取材shuzainews-gathering (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 免許 (a license) is something you 取得する (acquire); 取材 is specifically about gathering material/information for a news story, not obtaining a document.

Q2

来年の3月まで日本に（　）する予定だ。Rainen no san-gatsu made nihon ni (___) suru yotei da.

Full sentence: 来年の3月まで日本に滞在する予定だ。Rainen no san-gatsu made nihon ni taizai suru yotei da.
EN: I plan to stay in Japan until March next year.
HI: मैं अगले साल मार्च तक जापान में रहने की योजना बना रहा/रही हूँ।
GU: હું આવતા વર્ષે માર્ચ સુધી જાપાનમાં રોકાવાની યોજના ધરાવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a世帯setaia household (noun, can't take する here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b滞在taizaito stayठहरना, प्रवासરોકાણ, નિવાસ

Why: 滞在する is the verb "to stay/reside temporarily"; 世帯 is a counting noun for a household unit and doesn't collocate with する in this sense.

Q3

テレビの受信（　）が発生している地区がある。Terebi no jushin (___) ga hassei shite iru chiku ga aru.

Full sentence: テレビの受信障害が発生している地区がある。Terebi no jushin shougai ga hassei shite iru chiku ga aru.
EN: There are areas experiencing TV reception interference.
HI: कुछ इलाकों में टीवी के रिसेप्शन में गड़बड़ी (हस्तक्षेप) हो रही है।
GU: અમુક વિસ્તારોમાં ટીવી રિસેપ્શનમાં અડચણ (દખલ) થઈ રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a故障koshoua device breaking down (wrong scope)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b障害shougaiinterference, obstructionबाधा, गड़बड़ीઅડચણ, દખલ

Why: 受信障害 is the fixed collocation for signal/reception interference across an area; 故障 describes a specific device malfunctioning, which doesn't fit "地区" (a whole district).

Q4

宇宙には（　）の生物が生存する可能性がある。Uchuu ni wa (___) no seibutsu ga seizon suru kanousei ga aru.

Full sentence: 宇宙には未知の生物が生存する可能性がある。Uchuu ni wa michi no seibutsu ga seizon suru kanousei ga aru.
EN: There is a possibility that unknown life-forms exist in space.
HI: अंतरिक्ष में अज्ञात जीवों के अस्तित्व की संभावना है।
GU: અવકાશમાં અજ્ઞાત જીવસૃષ્ટિ અસ્તિત્વ ધરાવવાની શક્યતા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a未定miteiundecided (for plans, not life-forms)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b未知michiunknownअज्ञातઅજ્ઞાત

Why: 未知 describes something not yet known/discovered (fits "life-forms"); 未定 describes a plan or schedule not yet decided — the wrong kind of "not yet."

Q5

その記事に対する（　）は大きく、手紙やメールが500件以上も寄せられた。Sono kiji ni taisuru (___) wa ookiku, tegami ya meeru ga gohyaku-ken ijou mo yoserareta.

Full sentence: その記事に対する反響は大きく、手紙やメールが500件以上も寄せられた。Sono kiji ni taisuru hankyou wa ookiku, tegami ya meeru ga gohyaku-ken ijou mo yoserareta.
EN: The response to that article was huge — over 500 letters and emails poured in.
HI: उस लेख पर मिली प्रतिक्रिया बहुत बड़ी थी — 500 से ज़्यादा चिट्ठियाँ और ईमेल आए।
GU: તે લેખ પર મળેલો પ્રતિભાવ ખૂબ મોટો હતો — 500થી વધુ પત્રો અને ઈમેલ આવ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a反響hankyouresponse, reaction from an audienceप्रतिक्रिया, गूँजપ્રતિભાવ, પડઘો
b影響eikyouinfluence, effect (too broad/causal)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 反響 names the reaction/feedback an audience sends back (matching "500+ letters and emails"); 影響 is a general cause-and-effect influence, not specifically "replies received."

Q6

日本人は日本（　）の文化をもっと大切にするべきではないだろうか。Nihonjin wa nihon (___) no bunka o motto taisetsu ni suru beki de wa nai darou ka.

Full sentence: 日本人は日本固有の文化をもっと大切にするべきではないだろうか。Nihonjin wa nihon koyuu no bunka o motto taisetsu ni suru beki de wa nai darou ka.

GU: શું જાપાનીઓએ પોતાની વિશિષ્ટ (મૂળ) સંસ્કૃતિને વધુ મહત્વ ન આપવું જોઈએ?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a固有koyuupeculiar to, native toविशिष्ट, अपना ख़ासખાસ, વિશિષ્ટ
b固定koteifixed, fastened (physical, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 日本固有の文化 = "culture native/unique to Japan" — a set meaning for cultural identity; 固定 means physically fixed/fastened in place, which doesn't fit "culture."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is built around same-sounding, easily-confused kanji — every 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ question above is really testing one of these pairs. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
成功seikou Success Describes an outcome/result — an attempt that worked. A plan, launch, or effort achieves its goal.
精巧seikou Elaborate, finely made Describes an object's craftsmanship — same reading as 成功, completely unrelated meaning. Praising how intricately something (a machine, a model) is built.
取得shutoku Acquisition (of a license, qualification, right) Object is a document/status you formally obtain. Getting a license, degree, or citizenship.
取材shuzai News-gathering, coverage Object is information/material for a report — a journalist's activity. A reporter interviews someone or researches a story.
故障koshou A breakdown, malfunction A specific machine/device stops working correctly. "The car broke down" / "the printer is malfunctioning."
障害shougai An obstacle, interference A broader hindrance or interference affecting a process, signal, or plan. "Reception interference," "an obstacle to progress."
未知michi Unknown Something not yet discovered/understood. Unknown species, unknown territory, unknown facts.
未定mitei Undecided A plan, date, or decision not yet fixed. "The schedule is still undecided."
反響hankyou Response, repercussion Feedback/reaction that comes back from an audience. Public reaction to an article, speech, or event.
影響eikyou Influence, effect A general causal effect one thing has on another. "Climate change affects agriculture."
固有koyuu Peculiar to, native to Describes an intangible trait/identity unique to a group. "Culture native to Japan," "a species endemic to an island."
固定kotei Fixed, fastened Describes something physically or structurally fixed in place. "Bolt the shelf to the wall," "a fixed exchange rate."
Exam trap: 成功 and 精巧 are a perfect same-reading, zero-relation pair (せいこう read two totally different ways in meaning) — the single most testable trap in this cluster. Whenever a 漢字読み question gives you せいこう, check whether the sentence is about an outcome (→成功) or an object's craftsmanship (→精巧) before picking kanji.
