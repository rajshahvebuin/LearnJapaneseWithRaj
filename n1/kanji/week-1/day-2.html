Q1

係員の（　）に従ってください。Kakariin no (___) ni shitagatte kudasai.

Full sentence: 係員の指示に従ってください。Kakariin no shiji ni shitagatte kudasai.
EN: Please follow the staff member's instructions.
HI: कृपया कर्मचारी के निर्देशों का पालन करें।
GU: કૃપા કરી કર્મચારીની સૂચનાઓનું પાલન કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a指示shijiinstruction, directionनिर्देशસૂચના
b指摘shitekipointing out (an error, wrong here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 指示 is an order/direction that staff give you to follow; 指摘 is calling attention to a mistake or fact — you don't "follow" a 指摘.

Q2

日本語のクラスは初級、中級、（　）の3つに分かれています。Nihongo no kurasu wa shokyuu, chuukyuu, (___) no mittsu ni wakarete imasu.

Full sentence: 日本語のクラスは初級、中級、上級の3つに分かれています。Nihongo no kurasu wa shokyuu, chuukyuu, joukyuu no mittsu ni wakarete imasu.

HI: जापानी भाषा की कक्षाएँ तीन स्तरों में बँटी हैं: शुरुआती, मध्यम और उन्नत।
GU: જાપાનીઝ ક્લાસ ત્રણ સ્તરોમાં વહેંચાયેલા છે: પ્રારંભિક, મધ્યમ અને અદ્યતન.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a上級joukyuuadvanced levelउन्नत स्तरઅદ્યતન કક્ષા
b高級koukyuuhigh-class, luxury (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

彼は論理的というより（　）っぽい。Kare wa ronriteki to iu yori (___) ppoi.

Full sentence: 彼は論理的というより理屈っぽい。Kare wa ronriteki to iu yori rikutsuppoi.
EN: He's less logical than he is argumentative/a hair-splitter.
HI: वह तार्किक होने से ज़्यादा दलीलबाज़ है।
GU: તે તાર્કિક હોવા કરતાં વધુ દલીલબાજ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a理屈rikutsulogic, argumentativenessतर्क, दलीलતર્ક, દલીલ
b屈折kussetsurefraction (a physics term, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 理屈っぽい is a set phrase for someone who over-argues/rationalizes everything; 屈折 is strictly a physical-science term (light bending), never used for a personality trait.

Q4

他国の内政には（　）するべきではないとA氏は述べた。Takoku no naisei ni wa (___) suru beki de wa nai to Ei-shi wa nobeta.

Full sentence: 他国の内政には干渉するべきではないとA氏は述べた。Takoku no naisei ni wa kanshou suru beki de wa nai to Ei-shi wa nobeta.

HI: श्री ए ने कहा कि किसी अन्य देश के आंतरिक मामलों में हस्तक्षेप नहीं करना चाहिए।
GU: શ્રી એ એ જણાવ્યું કે અન્ય દેશની આંતરિક બાબતોમાં દખલગીરી ન કરવી જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a追及tsuikyuupursuit, pressing for (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b干渉kanshouinterferenceहस्तक्षेपદખલગીરી

Why: 干渉する is the fixed collocation for interfering in another party's affairs (internal politics, someone's private life); 追及 means pressing/pursuing someone for an answer or responsibility — a different action entirely.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above pairs two candidates that a test-taker could plausibly confuse. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
指示shiji Instruction, direction An order given by someone with authority, meant to be followed. "Follow the staff's instructions."
指摘shiteki Pointing out Drawing attention to a fact, error, or problem — not an order. "The teacher pointed out my mistake."
上級joukyuu Advanced level A rank in a skill/proficiency scale (beginner → intermediate → advanced). Course levels, belt ranks, exam grades.
高級koukyuu High-class, luxury Describes premium quality or price, not skill. "A high-class restaurant," "luxury goods."
理屈rikutsu Logic, argumentativeness Reasoning/argument, often used about a person who over-rationalizes. "He's a hair-splitter" (理屈っぽい).
屈折kussetsu Refraction A physical-science term for light/waves bending — never a personality trait. "Light refraction through a prism."
追及tsuikyuu Pursuit (of blame/truth) Pressing a person for an answer or responsibility. "The reporters pressed the minister for an answer."
干渉kanshou Interference Meddling in someone else's affairs uninvited. "Don't interfere in another country's politics."
拒否kyohi Rejection, refusal An outward act — refusing an external request or offer. "He refused the proposal."
嫌悪ken'o Hatred, disgust An inward feeling of revulsion, often self-directed (自己嫌悪). "She fell into self-loathing."
大胆daitan Bold, daring A trait describing courage/audacity — an adjective, not a financial verb. "A bold plan," "a daring design."
負担futan A burden, to bear (a cost) Shouldering a cost, duty, or responsibility. "The company covers half the cost."
Exam trap: 上級 (an advanced skill level) and 高級 (luxury/high-class) are the single most testable pair today — both translate loosely as "high ___" in English, but one is about proficiency and the other about price/prestige. When you see じょうきゅう or こうきゅう, check whether the sentence is grading a skill or a price tag before picking kanji.
