Q1

あの人は（　）で、無駄遣いはしません。Ano hito wa (___) de, mudazukai wa shimasen.

Full sentence: あの人は倹約家で、無駄遣いはしません。Ano hito wa ken'yakuka de, mudazukai wa shimasen.
EN: That person is thrifty and doesn't waste money.
HI: वह व्यक्ति मितव्ययी है और फ़िज़ूलख़र्ची नहीं करता।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિ કરકસરિયું છે અને બિનજરૂરી ખર્ચ કરતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a倹約家ken'yakukaa thrifty personमितव्ययी व्यक्तिકરકસરિયું
b菜食主義saishoku shugivegetarianism (unrelated to spending)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 倹約家 describes someone who is careful with money; 菜食主義 is about diet, not spending habits — unrelated to "not wasting money."

Q2

薬を長く飲み続けるのは（　）場合があります。Kusuri o nagaku nomitsuzukeru no wa (___) baai ga arimasu.

Full sentence: 薬を長く飲み続けるのは危険な場合があります。Kusuri o nagaku nomitsuzukeru no wa kiken na baai ga arimasu.
EN: Continuing to take medicine for a long time can sometimes be dangerous.
HI: दवा लंबे समय तक लेते रहना कभी-कभी ख़तरनाक हो सकता है।
GU: લાંબા સમય સુધી દવા લેવાનું ચાલુ રાખવું ક્યારેક ખતરનાક બની શકે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a危険なkiken nadangerousख़तरनाकખતરનાક
b真剣なshinken naserious (an attitude, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 危険な describes a hazard/risk to health; 真剣な describes a serious attitude or intent, which doesn't fit "a risk to your health."

Q3

今日は学長の（　）があります。Kyou wa gakuchou no (___) ga arimasu.

Full sentence: 今日は学長の講義があります。Kyou wa gakuchou no kougi ga arimasu.
EN: Today there is a lecture by the university president.
HI: आज विश्वविद्यालय के अध्यक्ष का व्याख्यान है।
GU: આજે યુનિવર્સિટીના પ્રમુખનું વ્યાખ્યાન છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a購入kounyuua purchase (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b講義kougia lectureव्याख्यानવ્યાખ્યાન

Why: 講義 is what a professor/president delivers; 購入 means "to purchase" and has nothing to do with a person giving a talk — the two look alike (both コウ, both built on 冓) but are not interchangeable.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習 question above pairs two candidates that look or sound alike. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
危険なkiken na Dangerous Describes a hazard or risk to safety/health. "Long-term use can be dangerous."
真剣なshinken na Serious Describes a serious attitude or mindset, not a physical risk. "He's dating her seriously."
講義kougi A lecture Something a teacher/professor delivers verbally. "There's a lecture today."
購入kounyuu A purchase Buying goods — shares the 冓 component and コウ reading with 講義, unrelated meaning. "Purchase teaching materials."
銅dou Bronze/copper (the metal) A true homophone of 胴 — refers to the metal, as in medals. "A bronze medal" (銅メダル).
胴dou The trunk/torso of the body Refers to body anatomy — nothing to do with metal. "Armor covering the torso" (胴体).
懸命なkenmei na Trying hard, desperate Describes effort/exertion. "He tried his hardest" (一生懸命).
愉快なyukai na Pleasant, amusing Describes a fun, likeable personality or mood. "He's an amusing person."
西暦seireki Common Era (A.D.) A calendar-era term — shares レキ with 履歴, unrelated meaning. "The year 2000 CE."
履歴rireki Personal history, resume A record of someone's life/work events. "Write your resume" (履歴書).
Exam trap: 銅 and 胴 are the single most testable pair today — both read exactly どう with no pitch or spelling cue to tell them apart in speech, yet one is a metal and the other is a body part. Whenever どう appears in a listening or reading-comprehension question, check whether the context is "prize/material" (→銅) or "body/anatomy" (→胴) before choosing.
