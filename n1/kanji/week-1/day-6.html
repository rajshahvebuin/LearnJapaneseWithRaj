6日目 — 壮・荘 (Sou, Sou)

Theme

Notes

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

（　）な計画を立てる。(___) na keikaku o tateru.

Full sentence: 壮大な計画を立てる。Soudai na keikaku o tateru.
EN: To make a grand/magnificent plan.
HI: भव्य योजना बनाना।
GU: ભવ્ય યોજના બનાવવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a壮大なsoudai namagnificent, grandभव्य, विशालભવ્ય
b娯楽gorakuamusement (a noun, wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 壮大な describes something grand in scale/ambition, exactly fitting "a plan"; 娯楽 means amusement/entertainment and can't modify 計画 the way 壮大な can.

Q2

前言を（　）する。Zengen o (___) suru.

Full sentence: 前言を撤回する。Zengen o tekkai suru.
EN: To retract a previous statement.
HI: पहले कहे गए बयान को वापस लेना।
GU: અગાઉના નિવેદનને પાછું ખેંચવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a撤回tekkaia withdrawal (of a statement)वापस लेनाપાછું ખેંચવું
b徹底tetteithoroughness (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 前言を撤回する ("to retract one's previous words") is a fixed collocation; 徹底する means to carry something out thoroughly and cannot take 前言 as its object.

Q3

（　）と供給のバランスを保つ。(___) to kyoukyuu no baransu o tamotsu.

Full sentence: 需要と供給のバランスを保つ。Juyou to kyoukyuu no baransu o tamotsu.
EN: To maintain the balance between supply and demand.
HI: माँग और आपूर्ति के बीच संतुलन बनाए रखना।
GU: માંગ અને પુરવઠા વચ્ચે સંતુલન જાળવવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a補充hojuureplenishment (wrong pairing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b需要juyoua demandमाँगમાંગ

Why: 需要と供給 ("demand and supply") is a fixed economic pairing; 補充 means refilling/replenishing a depleted stock, a different concept from market demand.

Q5

Full sentence: 組織の中で孤立する。Soshiki no naka de koritsu suru.
EN: To become isolated within an organization.
HI: संगठन के भीतर अलग-थलग पड़ जाना।
GU: સંગઠનની અંદર એકલા પડી જવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a孤独kodokulonesome (a felt state, not "する")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q6

時間は短くても（　）できればいい。Jikan wa mijikakute mo (___) dekireba ii.

Full sentence: 時間は短くても熟睡できればいい。Jikan wa mijikakute mo jukusui dekireba ii.
EN: Even if it's short, it's fine as long as I can sleep soundly.
HI: समय भले ही कम हो, गहरी नींद आ जाए तो काफ़ी है।
GU: સમય ઓછો હોય તોપણ, ઘસઘસાટ ઊંઘ આવે તો સારું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a熟睡jukusuia sound sleepगहरी नींदઘસઘસાટ ઊંઘ
b徹夜tetsuyastaying up all night (opposite meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 熟睡できれば ("as long as I can sleep deeply") fits a short-but-quality sleep; 徹夜 means staying awake the whole night — the opposite of sleeping at all.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every 練習Ⅰ question above pairs two visually or phonetically similar candidates. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
撤回tekkai A withdrawal (of words) Taking back a statement or proposal. "He retracted his earlier remark."
徹底tettei Thoroughness Carrying something out completely/rigorously. "Thoroughly check every detail."
需要juyou Demand Market/economic demand for goods or services. "Supply and demand."
補充hojuu Replenishment Refilling a stock that has been depleted. "Restock the shelves."
沿岸engan The coast Land alongside the sea. "Coastal areas."
沿線ensen Along a railway line Land alongside a train route. "An apartment along the train line."
孤独kodoku Lonesome, solitary The felt emotion of being alone — an adjective describing a state of mind. "He felt lonely."
孤立koritsu Isolation An objective/structural state of being cut off from others. "She became isolated within the group."
熟睡jukusui A sound sleep Sleeping deeply and well, regardless of duration. "I slept soundly, even if briefly."
徹夜tetsuya Staying up all night Not sleeping at all through the night. "He pulled an all-nighter to study."
Exam trap: 熟睡 (sleeping soundly) and 徹夜 (staying awake all night) are the single most testable pair today because they describe opposite physical states, yet both are common answers offered for "sleep"-themed questions. Before picking either, check whether the sentence is praising rest quality (→熟睡) or describing a lack of sleep entirely (→徹夜).
