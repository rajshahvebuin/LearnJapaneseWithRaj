1 Newspaper Headlines — politics

These are the four sample headlines/leads printed on p.124, plus the book's own usage note (spoken by its cat mascot). Reading real headline grammar (noun-stacking, dropped particles) is the point of this week.

Headline 1

中田氏、衆院選に出馬Nakata-shi, shuuin-sen ni shutsuba
財政改革に意欲Zaisei kaikaku ni iyoku

EN: Mr. Nakata to run in the House of Representatives election — shows enthusiasm for fiscal reform
HI: श्री नाकाता प्रतिनिधि सभा के चुनाव में उतरेंगे — वित्तीय सुधार के प्रति उत्साह दिखाया
GU: શ્રી નાકાટા પ્રતિનિધિ સભાની ચૂંટણીમાં ઉતરશે — નાણાકીય સુધારા માટે ઉત્સાહ દર્શાવ્યો
Headline 2

○○内閣総辞職へ○○ Naikaku soujishoku e
発足から1年で退陣Hossoku kara ichi-nen de taijin

EN: The ○○ Cabinet heads toward mass resignation — stepping down just one year after taking office
HI: ○○ मंत्रिमंडल सामूहिक इस्तीफ़े की ओर — गठन के महज़ एक साल बाद पद छोड़ रहा है
GU: ○○ મંત્રીમંડળ સામૂહિક રાજીનામા તરફ — રચનાના માત્ર એક વર્ષ પછી પદ છોડી રહ્યું છે
Headline 3

A国大統領来日A-koku daitouryou rainichi
首脳会談で両国の同盟関係を強調Shunou kaidan de ryoukoku no doumei kankei o kyouchou

EN: President of Country A visits Japan — summit talks emphasize the two countries' alliance
HI: देश A के राष्ट्रपति जापान आए — शिखर वार्ता में दोनों देशों के गठबंधन पर ज़ोर
GU: દેશ Aના રાષ્ટ્રપતિ જાપાનની મુલાકાતે — શિખર મંત્રણામાં બંને દેશોના જોડાણ પર ભાર
Headline 4

田中財務大臣更迭Tanaka Zaimu Daijin koutetsu (後任に山中総務相 kounin ni Yamanaka Soumu-shou)
収賄疑惑で ― 金銭の授受は否定Shuuwai giwaku de — kinsen no juju wa hitei

EN: Finance Minister Tanaka replaced (successor: Minister of Public Management Yamanaka) — amid bribery allegations, denies any exchange of money
HI: वित्त मंत्री तानाका की जगह बदली गई (उत्तराधिकारी: लोक-प्रबंधन मंत्री यामानाका) — घूसखोरी के आरोपों के बीच, पैसों के लेन-देन से इनकार
GU: નાણાં મંત્રી તાનાકાની જગ્યાએ ફેરબદલી (અનુગામી: જાહેર-વ્યવસ્થાપન મંત્રી યામાનાકા) — લાંચના આરોપો વચ્ચે, નાણાંની આપ-લેનો ઇનકાર
Usage Note

「出馬」は「出馬する」、「意欲」は「意欲を見せる」という意味。"Shutsuba" wa "shutsuba suru", "iyoku" wa "iyoku o miseru" to iu imi.

EN: "出馬" is used as "出馬する" (to run/enter a race); "意欲" is used as "意欲を見せる" (to show enthusiasm) — the book's own cat-mascot note on Headline 1's grammar.
HI: "出馬" का प्रयोग "出馬する" (चुनाव में उतरना) के रूप में होता है; "意欲" का प्रयोग "意欲を見せる" (उत्साह दिखाना) के रूप में होता है।
GU: "出馬" નો ઉપયોગ "出馬する" (ચૂંટણીમાં ઉતરવું) તરીકે થાય છે; "意欲" નો ઉપયોગ "意欲を見せる" (ઉત્સાહ બતાવવો) તરીકે થાય છે.

Q1

市長は市立博物館の館長を（　）して組織を一新したいと述べた。Shichou wa shiritsu hakubutsukan no kanchou o (___) shite soshiki o isshin shitai to nobeta.

Full sentence: 市長は市立博物館の館長を更迭して組織を一新したいと述べた。Shichou wa shiritsu hakubutsukan no kanchou o koutetsu shite soshiki o isshin shitai to nobeta.
EN: The mayor said he wants to replace the city museum's director and renew the organization.
HI: मेयर ने कहा कि वे शहर के संग्रहालय के निदेशक को बदलकर संगठन का कायाकल्प करना चाहते हैं।
GU: મેયરે કહ્યું કે તેઓ શહેરના મ્યુઝિયમના ડિરેક્ટરને બદલીને સંસ્થાનું નવીનીકરણ કરવા માંગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a更迭koutetsureplace (a person in a post)पद पर बदलाव करनाહોદ્દા પર ફેરબદલી કરવી
b統合tougouintegration (merging things, wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 更迭 targets a person in a post being replaced — exactly "館長を更迭する"; 統合 means merging/integrating organizations or systems, not swapping out one person.

Q2

新（　）が発足してから1年が経過した。Shin (___) ga hossoku shite kara ichi-nen ga keika shita.

Full sentence: 新内閣が発足してから1年が経過した。Shin naikaku ga hossoku shite kara ichi-nen ga keika shita.
EN: A year has passed since the new cabinet was formed.
HI: नए मंत्रिमंडल के गठन को एक साल बीत चुका है।
GU: નવા મંત્રીમંડળની રચનાને એક વર્ષ થઈ ગયું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a閣僚kakuryoucabinet ministers (individuals, wrong scope)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b内閣naikakuthe cabinet (as a body)मंत्रिमंडलમંત્રીમંડળ

Why: 内閣発足 (the cabinet's formation/launch) is the book's own fixed collocation; 閣僚 refers to individual ministers, who are appointed, not "launched" as a group.

Q3

公共事業をめぐる（　）事件で町長が起訴された。Koukyou jigyou o meguru (___) jiken de choushou ga kiso sareta.

Full sentence: 公共事業をめぐる贈収賄事件で町長が起訴された。Koukyou jigyou o meguru zoushuuwai jiken de choushou ga kiso sareta.
EN: The town mayor was indicted in a bribery scandal involving public works.
HI: सार्वजनिक निर्माण-कार्यों से जुड़े घूसखोरी के मामले में नगर प्रमुख पर मुक़दमा चलाया गया।
GU: જાહેર બાંધકામ પ્રોજેક્ટ સાથે સંકળાયેલા લાંચરુશવતના કેસમાં નગરના મુખ્યાધિકારી પર કામ ચલાવવામાં આવ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a罷免himendismissal (an action, not a type of scandal)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b贈収賄zoushuuwaibriberyघूसखोरीલાંચરુશવત

Why: 贈収賄事件 (bribery scandal/case) is the standard news term for a giving-and-receiving-bribes case; 罷免 is the separate act of formally dismissing someone from a post, not a kind of "事件."

Q4

（　）改正について考える会が開かれた。(___) kaisei ni tsuite kangaeru kai ga hirakareta.

Full sentence: 憲法改正について考える会が開かれた。Kenpou kaisei ni tsuite kangaeru kai ga hirakareta.
EN: A meeting was held to think about constitutional reform.
HI: संविधान में संशोधन पर विचार करने के लिए एक बैठक आयोजित हुई।
GU: બંધારણમાં સુધારા વિશે વિચારવા માટે એક સભા યોજાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a憲法kenpouconstitutionसंविधानબંધારણ
b違憲ikenunconstitutional (a state, not a document to amend)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 憲法改正 (constitutional amendment) is the fixed phrase — you amend the 憲法 itself; 違憲 describes something being unconstitutional, and "違憲を改正する" is not meaningful.

Q5

選挙に出馬というのは（　）するという意味だ。Senkyo ni shutsuba to iu no wa (___) suru to iu imi da.

Full sentence: 選挙に出馬というのは立候補するという意味だ。Senkyo ni shutsuba to iu no wa rikkouho suru to iu imi da.
EN: "Running (出馬) in an election" means to stand as a candidate.
HI: चुनाव में "出馬" का मतलब है उम्मीदवार के रूप में खड़ा होना।
GU: ચૂંટણીમાં "出馬" નો અર્થ છે ઉમેદવાર તરીકે ઊભા રહેવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a立候補rikkouhocandidacy, standing as a candidateउम्मीदवारीઉમેદવારી
b総辞職soujishokumass resignation (opposite direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 出馬 and 立候補 are near-synonyms for entering a race as a candidate; 総辞職 is a cabinet or group stepping down together — the opposite kind of event.

Q6

来月中旬に日米（　）会談が開かれる予定だ。Raigetsu chuujun ni Nichi-Bei (___) kaidan ga hirakareru yotei da.

Full sentence: 来月中旬に日米首脳会談が開かれる予定だ。Raigetsu chuujun ni Nichi-Bei shunou kaidan ga hirakareru yotei da.
EN: A Japan-US summit is scheduled to be held in the middle of next month.
HI: अगले महीने के मध्य में जापान-अमेरिका शिखर वार्ता होने वाली है।
GU: આવતા મહિનાના મધ્યમાં જાપાન-અમેરિકા શિખર મંત્રણા યોજાવાની છે.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Pulled straight from today's 練習Ⅰ — each pair is a real a/b choice from the exercise above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
更迭koutetsu Replacing a person in a post Targets a specific individual being swapped out. "The museum director was replaced."
統合tougou Integration, merging Targets organizations, systems, or departments, not a single person. "Two departments were merged."
罷免himen Formal dismissal The specific legal/administrative act of removing someone from office. "The governor dismissed the official."
贈収賄zoushuuwai Bribery (giving + receiving) Names the whole scandal/crime, not a single act of removal. "A bribery scandal broke out over the public project."
憲法kenpou The constitution itself The document being discussed, amended, or debated. "Constitutional reform," "the constitution."
違憲iken Unconstitutional (a judgment) A state/verdict about something else — cannot itself be "amended." "The law was ruled unconstitutional."
立候補rikkouho Standing as a candidate Entering a race — near-synonym of 出馬. "She announced her candidacy."
総辞職soujishoku Mass resignation A cabinet or group all stepping down together — the opposite direction from running for office. "The cabinet resigned en masse."
同盟doumei An alliance (the relationship) Names the ongoing bond between two countries/parties. "The US-Japan alliance."
首脳shunou A head of state/leader Names the people meeting — pairs with 会談 to mean "summit." "A summit between leaders."
Exam trap: 更迭 vs 罷免 vs 総辞職 are three different "someone leaves a post" words with three different scopes — 更迭 replaces one person, 罷免 is a formal dismissal of one person, and 総辞職 is an entire cabinet/group resigning together. News articles pick precisely, so match the scope, not just the vague meaning of "stepping down."
