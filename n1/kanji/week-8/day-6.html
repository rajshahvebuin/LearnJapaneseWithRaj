1 Newspaper Headlines — society

Seven sample headlines/leads from p.134, covering the Imperial family, sports, drug testing, demographics, and pollution.

Headline 1

迎賓館の前庭が一般公開Geihinkan no maeniwa ga ippan koukai

EN: Commemorating 20 years of the Emperor's reign — the State Guest House's front garden opens to the public

Headline 5

Headline 6

GU: બાળ-ત્રાસ વારંવાર સામે આવે છે — બાળકો સંબંધિત સલાહ-વ્યવસ્થા મજબૂત કરાઈ
Headline 7

EN: Air pollution, water pollution, noise, ground subsidence… Photos tell the story of City B's transformation — aiming to build a pollution-free town
HI: वायु प्रदूषण, जल प्रदूषण, शोर, भूमि-धंसाव... तस्वीरें बताती हैं शहर B के बदलाव की कहानी — प्रदूषण-मुक्त शहर बनाने का लक्ष्य
GU: હવા પ્રદૂષણ, જળ પ્રદૂષણ, અવાજ, જમીન બેસી જવી... તસવીરો કહે છે શહેર Bના પરિવર્તનની વાર્તા — પ્રદૂષણ-મુક્ત શહેર બનાવવાનું લક્ષ્ય

練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items), both from p.135. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊 p.14] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

Q1

違法薬物取り締まり強化で、抜き打ち（　）検査を実施する。Ihou yakubutsu torishimari kyouka de, nukiuchi (___) kensa o jisshi suru.

Full sentence: 違法薬物取り締まり強化で、抜き打ち尿検査を実施する。Ihou yakubutsu torishimari kyouka de, nukiuchi nyou kensa o jisshi suru.
EN: With stronger crackdowns on illegal drugs, surprise urine tests are carried out.
HI: अवैध नशीले पदार्थों पर सख़्ती के तहत अचानक मूत्र-जाँच की जाती है।
GU: ગેરકાયદે નશીલા પદાર્થો પર કડકાઈ સાથે અચાનક પેશાબ પરીક્ષણ કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a腸chouintestines (unrelated to drug testing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b尿nyouurineमूत्रપેશાબ

Why: 尿検査 (urine test) is the book's own listed collocation and the real-world method for drug testing; 腸 (intestines) has no such testing use.

Q2

高度成長から経済大国への（　）を振り返る。Koudo seichou kara keizai taikoku e no (___) o furikaeru.

Full sentence: 高度成長から経済大国への変遷を振り返る。Koudo seichou kara keizai taikoku e no hensen o furikaeru.
EN: Looking back at the transition from a period of rapid growth to becoming an economic power.
HI: तीव्र आर्थिक विकास से आर्थिक महाशक्ति बनने तक के बदलाव पर एक नज़र।
GU: ઝડપી આર્થિક વિકાસથી આર્થિક મહાસત્તા બનવા સુધીના પરિવર્તન પર નજર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a変遷hensenchanges/transition (over time)समय के साथ परिवर्तनસમય જતાં થયેલા ફેરફારો
b返還henkanreturn/restoration (of territory/objects, wrong context)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 変遷 (historical transition/changes) fits a decades-long economic narrative; 返還 refers to returning territory or objects, unrelated to describing gradual change.

Q3

空港の拡張で（　）被害が懸念される。Kuukou no kakuchou de (___) higai ga kenen sareru.

Full sentence: 空港の拡張で騒音被害が懸念される。Kuukou no kakuchou de souon higai ga kenen sareru.
EN: With the airport's expansion, damage from noise is a concern.
HI: हवाई अड्डे के विस्तार से शोर-जनित नुक़सान की चिंता है।
GU: એરપોર્ટના વિસ્તરણથી અવાજને લીધે થતા નુકસાનની ચિંતા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a騒音souonnoiseशोरઅવાજ, ઘોંઘાટ
b騒動soudoua disturbance/riot (a commotion, not ambient sound)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 騒音 (noise, esp. from aircraft) is the correct term for the kind of pollution airport expansion causes; 騒動 describes an uproar or social disturbance, not ambient sound.

Q4

被害者に（　）にメールを送っていた男が捜査線上に浮かび上がった。Higaisha ni (___) ni meeru o okutte ita otoko ga sousa-senjou ni ukabiagatta.

Full sentence: 被害者に頻繁にメールを送っていた男が捜査線上に浮かび上がった。Higaisha ni hinpan ni meeru o okutte ita otoko ga sousa-senjou ni ukabiagatta.
EN: A man who had frequently sent emails to the victim emerged as a person of interest in the investigation.
HI: पीड़िता को बार-बार ई-मेल भेजने वाला एक व्यक्ति जाँच में संदिग्ध के रूप में सामने आया।
GU: પીડિતાને વારંવાર ઈમેલ મોકલનાર એક વ્યક્તિ તપાસમાં શંકાસ્પદ તરીકે સામે આવ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a頻繁hinpanfrequently (adverbial に)बार-बारવારંવાર
b繁栄han'eiprosperity (a noun, doesn't work adverbially here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 頻繁に (frequently) is the adverbial form used before a verb like メールを送る; 繁栄 (prosperity) is a noun about economic/social flourishing, not usable this way.

HI: श्री A ने टीम B के नए कोच का पद संभाला।
GU: શ્રી Aએ ટીમ Bના નવા કોચનું પદ સંભાળ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a来賓raihina guest (an attendee, not a post one takes up)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q6

（　）化の進む村で、地域の振興と活性化を図るイベントが行われた。(___)-ka no susumu mura de, chiiki no shinkou to kasseika o hakaru ibento ga okonawareta.

Full sentence: 過疎化の進む村で、地域の振興と活性化を図るイベントが行われた。Kaso-ka no susumu mura de, chiiki no shinkou to kasseika o hakaru ibento ga okonawareta.
EN: In a village experiencing advancing depopulation, an event was held aiming at regional promotion and revitalization.
HI: जनसंख्या-ह्रास से जूझ रहे एक गाँव में क्षेत्रीय पुनरुद्धार हेतु आयोजन किया गया।
GU: વસ્તી ઘટાડાનો સામનો કરી રહેલા એક ગામમાં પ્રાદેશિક પુનરુત્થાન માટે કાર્યક્રમ યોજાયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a過疎kasodepopulationग्रामीण जनसंख्या-ह्रासગ્રામ્ય વસ્તી ઘટાડો
b過密kamitsuovercrowding (happens in cities, not shrinking villages)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 過疎化 (depopulation) fits a village needing "regional revitalization"; 過密 describes overcrowding, the opposite demographic problem seen in large cities.

Pulled straight from today's 練習Ⅰ — each pair is a real a/b choice from the exercise above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when

過疎kaso Depopulation Rural areas losing people. "A depopulating village."
過密kamitsu Overcrowding Urban areas gaining too many people. "An overcrowded city."
Exam trap: 過疎 vs 過密 are a direct pair describing opposite ends of Japan's urban-rural population shift — 過疎 (rural depopulation) and 過密 (urban overcrowding) are always used about different kinds of places, so check whether the sentence is about a shrinking village or a growing city before picking.
