EN: Overcome an illness and return to society.
HI: बीमारी पर विजय पाकर समाज में वापस लौटना।
GU: બીમારી પર વિજય મેળવીને સમાજમાં પાછા ફરવું.
#ReadingNote
1かっぷくkappukunot a reading of 克服
2こくふくkokufukucorrect — 克服 (overcoming, Day 6's 克 entry)
3こうふくkoufukuthat would be 幸福 (happiness), wrong kanji
4かいふくkaifukuthat would be 回復 (recovery), wrong kanji

Why: Day 6's 克 entry gives 克服 (こくふく) directly, matching the "○○監督" headline about overcoming illness.
