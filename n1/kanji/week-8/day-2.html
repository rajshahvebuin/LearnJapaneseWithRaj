1 Newspaper Headlines — world & international affairs

Eight sample headlines/leads from p.126, covering famine, natural disaster, conflict, and non-proliferation news.

Headline 1

世界的食糧危機への懸念 高まるSekaiteki shokuryou kiki e no kenen takamaru

EN: Concern over a global food crisis is growing
HI: वैश्विक खाद्य संकट को लेकर चिंता बढ़ रही है
GU: વૈશ્વિક ખાદ્ય કટોકટી અંગે ચિંતા વધી રહી છે
Headline 2

A国の飢餓、ここ10年で最悪A-koku no kiga, koko juu-nen de saiaku
人権擁護団体が報告Jinken yougo dantai ga houkoku

EN: Hunger in Country A is at its worst in the past 10 years — a human-rights group reports
HI: देश A में भुखमरी पिछले 10 वर्षों में सबसे भीषण — मानवाधिकार संगठन की रिपोर्ट
GU: દેશ Aમાં ભૂખમરો છેલ્લા 10 વર્ષમાં સૌથી ખરાબ — માનવ અધિકાર સંગઠનનો અહેવાલ
Headline 3

D国 豪雨で洪水、106人死亡D-koku gou-u de kouzui, hyaku-roku-nin shibou
浸水や農作物の被害も多数Shinsui ya nousakubutsu no higai mo tasuu

EN: Country D: floods from torrential rain kill 106 — widespread flooding and crop damage too
HI: देश D: भारी बारिश से बाढ़, 106 लोगों की मौत — जलभराव और फ़सल-नुक़सान भी बड़े पैमाने पर
GU: દેશ D: ભારે વરસાદથી પૂર, 106 લોકોનાં મોત — પાણી ભરાવાનું અને પાક નુકસાન પણ મોટા પાયે
Headline 4

B国 M8地震 津波死者7000人超B-koku M-hachi jishin, tsunami shisha nanasen-nin chou
A国政府 被災者支援に救助隊の派遣を要請A-koku seifu, hisaisha shien ni kyuujotai no haken o yousei

EN: Country B: M8 earthquake, tsunami deaths exceed 7,000 — Country A's government requests a rescue team be sent to support the disaster victims
HI: देश B: तीव्रता-8 भूकंप, सुनामी में 7000 से अधिक मौतें — देश A की सरकार ने पीड़ितों की सहायता हेतु बचाव-दल भेजने का अनुरोध किया
GU: દેશ B: M8 ધરતીકંપ, સુનામીમાં 7000થી વધુ મૃત્યુ — દેશ Aની સરકારે પીડિતોની મદદ માટે બચાવ ટુકડી મોકલવાની વિનંતી કરી
Headline 5

C国紛争激化 (宗教間の対立再び shuukyou-kan no tairitsu futatabi)C-koku funsou gekika
政府は邦人の安全確保に全力Seifu wa houjin no anzen kakuho ni zenryoku

EN: Conflict intensifies in Country C (religious strife flares up again) — the government puts all effort into securing the safety of Japanese nationals
HI: देश C में संघर्ष तेज़ (धार्मिक तनाव फिर भड़का) — सरकार जापानी नागरिकों की सुरक्षा सुनिश्चित करने में पूरी ताक़त झोंक रही है
GU: દેશ Cમાં સંઘર્ષ તીવ્ર (ધાર્મિક તણાવ ફરી ભડક્યો) — સરકાર જાપાની નાગરિકોની સલામતી સુનિશ્ચિત કરવામાં પૂરી તાકાત લગાવી રહી છે
Headline 6

核不拡散、核軍縮に関する決議を採択Kaku fukakusan, kaku gunshuku ni kansuru ketsugi o saitaku

EN: Resolution on nuclear non-proliferation and disarmament adopted
HI: परमाणु अप्रसार और परमाणु निरस्त्रीकरण संबंधी प्रस्ताव पारित
GU: પરમાણુ અપ્રસાર અને પરમાણુ નિઃશસ્ત્રીકરણ અંગેનો ઠરાવ પસાર
Headline 7

A国ミサイル開発 経済制裁措置を発動A-koku misairu kaihatsu, keizai seisai sochi o hatsudou

EN: Country A's missile development: economic sanction measures invoked
HI: देश A के मिसाइल विकास पर — आर्थिक प्रतिबंध लागू
GU: દેશ Aના મિસાઇલ વિકાસ પર — આર્થિક પ્રતિબંધનાં પગલાં લાગુ
Headline 8

森林伐採による砂漠化を防ぐため…Shinrin bassai ni yoru sabakuka o fusegu tame…
森林保護、温暖化対策支援に基金を設立Shinrin hogo, ondanka taisaku shien ni kikin o setsuritsu

EN: To prevent desertification caused by deforestation… a fund is established to support forest protection and climate-change countermeasures
HI: वनों की कटाई से होने वाले मरुस्थलीकरण को रोकने के लिए... वन-संरक्षण व जलवायु-परिवर्तन उपायों हेतु एक कोष स्थापित
GU: વનનાબૂદીથી થતા રણીકરણને રોકવા માટે... વન સંરક્ષણ અને આબોહવા પરિવર્તન પગલાં માટે એક ફંડ સ્થાપ્યું

3 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items), both from p.127. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊 p.13] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

各地で大雨による（　）警報が出される。Kakuchi de oo-ame ni yoru (___) keihou ga dasareru.

Full sentence: 各地で大雨による洪水警報が出される。Kakuchi de oo-ame ni yoru kouzui keihou ga dasareru.
EN: Flood warnings are issued in various places due to heavy rain.
HI: भारी बारिश के कारण कई जगहों पर बाढ़ की चेतावनी जारी की जाती है।
GU: ભારે વરસાદને કારણે અનેક સ્થળોએ પૂરની ચેતવણી જારી કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a津波tsunamitsunami (caused by earthquakes, not rain)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b洪水kouzuia floodबाढ़પૂર

Why: 洪水警報 is the fixed term for a flood warning triggered by heavy rain; 津波 (tsunami) is caused by seismic activity, not rainfall.

Q2

核実験を続ける国に対し、経済（　）措置をとる。Kaku jikken o tsuzukeru kuni ni taishi, keizai (___) sochi o toru.

Full sentence: 核実験を続ける国に対し、経済制裁措置をとる。Kaku jikken o tsuzukeru kuni ni taishi, keizai seisai sochi o toru.
EN: Economic sanction measures are taken against a country that continues nuclear testing.
HI: परमाणु परीक्षण जारी रखने वाले देश के ख़िलाफ़ आर्थिक प्रतिबंध लगाए जाते हैं।
GU: પરમાણુ પરીક્ષણ ચાલુ રાખતા દેશ સામે આર્થિક પ્રતિબંધનાં પગલાં લેવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a制裁seisaia sanctionप्रतिबंधપ્રતિબંધ
b救援kyuuenrelief, rescue (wrong direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 経済制裁措置 (economic sanction measures) is the book's own listed collocation for punishing a state; 救援 is aid given to victims, the opposite kind of action.

Q3

森林（　）の現状について環境省が報告をした。Shinrin (___) no genjou ni tsuite Kankyoushou ga houkoku o shita.

Full sentence: 森林伐採の現状について環境省が報告をした。Shinrin bassai no genjou ni tsuite Kankyoushou ga houkoku o shita.
EN: The Ministry of the Environment reported on the current state of deforestation.
HI: पर्यावरण मंत्रालय ने वनों की कटाई की मौजूदा स्थिति पर रिपोर्ट दी।
GU: પર્યાવરણ મંત્રાલયે વનનાબૂદીની હાલની સ્થિતિ પર અહેવાલ આપ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a災害saigaia disaster (too broad, no specific act)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b伐採bassaitree fellingपेड़ों की कटाईવૃક્ષોનું કટાણ

Why: 森林伐採 (deforestation/logging) is today's headline term and the book's fixed collocation; 災害 is a general disaster, not the specific act of cutting down forests.

Q4

世界的な（　）危機、穀物の不足などが心配されている。Sekaiteki na (___) kiki, kokumotsu no fusoku nado ga shinpai sarete iru.

GU: વૈશ્વિક ખાદ્ય કટોકટી અને અનાજની અછત જેવી ચિંતાઓ વ્યક્ત કરવામાં આવી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a食糧shokuryoufood, provisionsखाद्यान्नઅનાજ, ખોરાક

Why: 食糧危機 (food crisis) is the book's own listed fixed phrase; 飢餓 names the resulting state of starvation rather than the crisis itself.

Q5

Q6

（　）上の理由から、空港での検査を拒否した人がいる。(___) jou no riyuu kara, kuukou de no kensa o kyohi shita hito ga iru.

GU: ધાર્મિક કારણોસર એરપોર્ટ પર તપાસનો ઇનકાર કરનારા લોકો પણ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a宗教shuukyoureligionधर्मધર્મ
b弁護bengolegal defense (unrelated to airport screening)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 宗教上の理由 (for religious reasons) is the book's own listed fixed phrase; 弁護 relates to legal defense, not personal-belief reasons for refusing an inspection.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Pulled straight from today's 練習Ⅰ — each pair is a real a/b choice from the exercise above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
洪水kouzui A flood Caused by rain/rivers overflowing. "Flood warning" from heavy rain.
津波tsunami A tsunami Caused by an earthquake or undersea disturbance. "Tsunami warning" after a quake.
制裁seisai A sanction Punitive measures taken against a state or actor. "Economic sanctions on a country."
救援kyuuen Relief, rescue Aid given to help victims — the opposite intent from a sanction. "Relief activities for disaster victims."
伐採bassai Tree felling / logging The specific act of cutting down trees. "Deforestation," "logging report."
災害saigai A disaster Any general calamity — too broad to name a specific act. "Natural disaster," "disaster relief."
食糧shokuryou Food, provisions The resource itself, as in the fixed phrase 食糧危機. "Food crisis," "food supplies."
飢餓kiga Hunger, starvation The resulting human condition, not the resource shortage itself. "Starvation," "famine victims."
救援kyuuen Relief/rescue activity The act of providing help on the ground. "Relief activities" (救援活動).
要請yousei A request The act of asking for help — precedes the relief itself. "Request for a rescue team."
宗教shuukyou Religion Personal belief as a reason for an action. "For religious reasons."
弁護bengo Legal defense A courtroom/legal act, unrelated to belief-based refusal. "A lawyer's defense of a client."
Exam trap: 洪水 vs 津波 are the day's most testable pair — both are "big water disasters" in English but the cause (rain vs. earthquake) decides which kanji a Japanese news report uses. Always check what triggered the event before picking the word.
