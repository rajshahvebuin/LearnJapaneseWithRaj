3 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items), both from p.133. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊 p.14] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

登山ブームの中で（　）件数も増加している。Tozan buumu no naka de (___) kensuu mo zouka shite iru.

Full sentence: 登山ブームの中で遭難件数も増加している。Tozan buumu no naka de sounan kensuu mo zouka shite iru.
EN: Amid the mountain-climbing boom, the number of mountaineering accidents is also increasing.
HI: पर्वतारोहण की लोकप्रियता के बीच, दुर्घटनाओं की संख्या भी बढ़ रही है।
GU: પર્વતારોહણની લોકપ્રિયતા વચ્ચે, અકસ્માતોની સંખ્યા પણ વધી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a侵略shinryakuinvasion (military, unrelated to hiking)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b遭難sounanan accident/disasterदुर्घटनाઅકસ્માત

Why: 遭難 is the standard term for a mountaineering/maritime mishap; 侵略 refers to a military invasion and has nothing to do with hikers.

Q2

現場に残された（　）が容疑者のものと一致した。Genba ni nokosareta (___) ga yougisha no mono to icchi shita.

Full sentence: 現場に残された指紋が容疑者のものと一致した。Genba ni nokosareta shimon ga yougisha no mono to icchi shita.
EN: The fingerprints left at the scene matched the suspect's.
HI: घटनास्थल पर मिले उँगलियों के निशान संदिग्ध के निशानों से मेल खाए।
GU: ઘટનાસ્થળે મળેલી આંગળીની છાપ શંકાસ્પદ વ્યક્તિની છાપ સાથે મળતી આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a指紋shimona fingerprintउँगलियों के निशानઆંગળીની છાપ
b身柄migaraa person's custody/identity (a person, not something left behind)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 指紋が一致する (fingerprints match) is the exact phrase from today's headline; 身柄 refers to a person's physical custody, not physical evidence that can "match."

Q3

（　）されていた会社社長は無事保護された。(___) sarete ita kaisha shachou wa buji hogo sareta.

Full sentence: 誘拐されていた会社社長は無事保護された。Yuukai sarete ita kaisha shachou wa buji hogo sareta.
EN: The company president, who had been kidnapped, was safely taken into protective custody.
HI: अपहृत कंपनी अध्यक्ष को सुरक्षित रूप से बचा लिया गया।
GU: અપહૃત કંપની પ્રમુખને સુરક્ષિત રીતે બચાવી લેવાયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a誘拐yuukaikidnappingअपहरणઅપહરણ
b解放kaihourelease (would make "was then safely protected" redundant)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence needs the state the president was in *before* being rescued — kidnapped (誘拐された); 解放 (already released) would make the following "was safely protected" nonsensical.

Q4

行政に失望し、選挙を（　）した。Gyousei ni shitsubou shi, senkyo o (___) shita.

Full sentence: 行政に失望し、選挙を棄権した。Gyousei ni shitsubou shi, senkyo o kiken shita.
EN: Disillusioned with the administration, he/she abstained from voting in the election.
HI: प्रशासन से निराश होकर, उसने चुनाव में मतदान से परहेज़ किया।
GU: વહીવટીતંત્રથી નિરાશ થઈને, તેમણે ચૂંટણીમાં મતદાનથી દૂર રહેવાનું પસંદ કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a黙秘mokuhistaying silent (during interrogation, wrong context)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b棄権kikenabstentionमतदान से परहेज़મતદાનથી દૂર રહેવું

Why: 選挙を棄権する (abstaining from an election) is the book's own listed collocation; 黙秘 is specifically a suspect's right to remain silent under questioning, unrelated to voting.

Q5

男は住居侵入の容疑で（　）された。Otoko wa juukyo shinnyuu no yougi de (___) sareta.

Full sentence: 男は住居侵入の容疑で逮捕された。Otoko wa juukyo shinnyuu no yougi de taiho sareta.
EN: The man was arrested on suspicion of trespassing.
HI: घर में अवैध प्रवेश के संदेह में उस व्यक्ति को गिरफ़्तार किया गया।
GU: ઘરમાં ગેરકાયદે પ્રવેશની શંકામાં તે વ્યક્તિની ધરપકડ કરવામાં આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a逮捕taihoarrestगिरफ़्तारीધરપકડ
b釈放shakuhourelease (the opposite direction from "on suspicion of")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 容疑で逮捕される (arrested on suspicion of) is the standard news pattern; 容疑で釈放される (released on suspicion of) would be contradictory, since release resolves a suspicion rather than acting on it.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Pulled straight from today's 練習Ⅰ — each pair is a real a/b choice from the exercise above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
遭難sounan An accident/disaster Typically mountaineering or maritime mishaps. "Climbing accident," "shipwreck."
侵略shinryaku Invasion A military act by one state against another. "A war of invasion."
指紋shimon A fingerprint Physical evidence that can be compared/matched. "Fingerprints match the suspect."
身柄migara A person (in custody) Refers to the physical person/identity, not left-behind evidence. "Taken into protective custody."
誘拐yuukai Kidnapping Describes what happened *to* the victim beforehand. "He/she had been kidnapped."
解放kaihou Release, liberation The resolution — being set free. "The hostage was released."
棄権kiken Abstention Giving up a right (to vote, to compete). "Abstained from the election/race."
黙秘mokuhi Remaining silent A suspect's right to not answer questions. "Exercised the right to remain silent."
逮捕taiho Arrest Taking a suspect into custody on suspicion. "Arrested on suspicion of theft."
釈放shakuhou Release Letting someone go — resolves a suspicion, doesn't act on one. "Released after questioning."
名称meishou An official name The real, formal designation of a thing/institution. "The museum's official name."
自称jishou Self-styled A person's unverified claim about themselves. "A self-proclaimed expert."
Exam trap: 逮捕 vs 釈放 are opposite ends of the same process — a suspect is 逮捕される (arrested) *on* suspicion, then later either 起訴される (indicted) or 釈放される (released). Reading which direction a sentence points (into custody vs. out of it) is the key to picking correctly.
