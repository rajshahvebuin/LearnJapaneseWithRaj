3 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items), both from p.129. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊 p.14] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

食材の相次ぐ値上がりに庶民の食卓は（　）を受けている。Shokuzai no aitsugu neagari ni shomin no shokutaku wa (___) o ukete iru.

Full sentence: 食材の相次ぐ値上がりに庶民の食卓は打撃を受けている。Shokuzai no aitsugu neagari ni shomin no shokutaku wa dageki o ukete iru.
EN: Ordinary people's dinner tables are taking a hit from successive food-price hikes.
HI: लगातार बढ़ती खाद्य क़ीमतों से आम जनता की रसोई पर आघात पड़ रहा है।
GU: સતત વધતા ખાદ્ય ભાવોથી સામાન્ય લોકોના રસોડા પર ફટકો પડી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a打撃dagekia blow, a hitआघातફટકો
b攻撃kougekian attack (too aggressive for a "dinner table")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 打撃を受ける (to take a hit/blow) is the natural collocation for something suffering economic harm; 攻撃 implies an aggressive act by an agent, which doesn't fit "食卓" (a dinner table).

Q2

円高差益を（　）して値下げをする。Endaka saeki o (___) shite nesage o suru.

Full sentence: 円高差益を還元して値下げをする。Endaka saeki o kangen shite nesage o suru.
EN: Prices are cut by returning the profit margin from yen appreciation.
HI: येन की मज़बूती से हुए मुनाफ़े को वापस लौटाकर क़ीमतें घटाई जाती हैं।
GU: યેનની મજબૂતીથી થયેલો નફો પાછો આપીને ભાવ ઘટાડવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a還元kangenreturn (profits)(मुनाफ़े को) वापस लौटाना(નફો) પાછો આપવો
b返還henkanreturn/restoration (of territory/objects, wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 利益を還元する (return a profit to customers) is the book's own listed collocation; 返還 is used for returning territory or physical objects, not abstract profit margins.

Q3

A社が子会社2社を吸収（　）することになった。A-sha ga kogaisha ni-sha o kyuushuu (___) suru koto ni natta.

Full sentence: A社が子会社2社を吸収合併することになった。A-sha ga kogaisha ni-sha o kyuushuu gappei suru koto ni natta.
EN: Company A is set to absorb and merge with its two subsidiaries.
HI: कंपनी A अपनी दो सहायक कंपनियों का अधिग्रहण-विलय करेगी।
GU: કંપની A તેની બે પેટાકંપનીઓનું શોષણ-વિલિનીકરણ કરશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a倒産tousanbankruptcy (doesn't collocate with "absorb")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b合併gappeia mergerविलयવિલિનીકરણ

Why: 吸収合併 (absorption merger) is the book's own fixed phrase; 倒産 (bankruptcy) is something that happens to a company, not an action one company does to another.

Q4

解雇等で職を失った人を対象に（　）する国の制度がある。Kaiko tou de shoku o ushinatta hito o taishou ni (___) suru kuni no seido ga aru.

Full sentence: 解雇等で職を失った人を対象に融資する国の制度がある。Kaiko tou de shoku o ushinatta hito o taishou ni yuushi suru kuni no seido ga aru.
EN: There is a national system that provides loans targeting people who lost their jobs through layoffs, etc.
HI: छँटनी आदि से नौकरी खो चुके लोगों के लिए एक राष्ट्रीय ऋण-योजना है।
GU: છટણી વગેરેથી નોકરી ગુમાવેલા લોકો માટે એક રાષ્ટ્રીય ધિરાણ યોજના છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a金融kin'yuufinance (the field, doesn't take する this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b融資yuushifinancing, a loanऋण, वित्तपोषणધિરાણ

Why: 融資する (to lend/finance someone) is a verb describing an act of lending; 金融 names the general field of finance and doesn't naturally take する to mean "lend to a person."

Q5

男性に人気のファストフード店が女性や子どもの需要（　）を検討している。Dansei ni ninki no fasuto fuudo-ten ga josei ya kodomo no juyou (___) o kentou shite iru.

Full sentence: 男性に人気のファストフード店が女性や子どもの需要開拓を検討している。Dansei ni ninki no fasuto fuudo-ten ga josei ya kodomo no juyou kaitaku o kentou shite iru.
EN: A fast-food chain popular with men is considering tapping into demand from women and children.
HI: पुरुषों में लोकप्रिय एक फ़ास्ट-फ़ूड चेन महिलाओं और बच्चों की माँग को नया बाज़ार बनाने पर विचार कर रही है।
GU: પુરુષોમાં લોકપ્રિય એક ફાસ્ટ-ફૂડ ચેઇન મહિલાઓ અને બાળકોની માંગને નવું બજાર બનાવવા વિચારી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a利益riekia profit (a noun, not something you "検討" as an action on demand)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b開拓kaitakucultivation, tapping into (new demand)नए क्षेत्र का विकासનવા ક્ષેત્રનો વિકાસ

Q6

職業訓練と就職先（　）の雇用支援制度を設ける。Shokugyou kunren to shuushoku-saki (___) no koyou shien seido o mookeru.

Full sentence: 職業訓練と就職先斡旋の雇用支援制度を設ける。Shokugyou kunren to shuushoku-saki assen no koyou shien seido o mookeru.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Pulled straight from today's 練習Ⅰ — each pair is a real a/b choice from the exercise above.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
打撃dageki A blow, an economic hit Something (a market, a household) suffers harm. "Price hikes dealt a blow to households."
攻撃kougeki An attack An aggressive act with a clear attacker. "A military/verbal attack."
還元kangen Returning (profits) Giving back an abstract benefit, like profit, to customers. "Return profits by cutting prices."
返還henkan Return, restoration Giving back territory or a physical/legal object. "Return of Okinawa," "return a deposit."
合併gappei A merger One company actively absorbs/joins with another. "Company A merges with Company B."
倒産tousan Bankruptcy Something that happens to a failing company, not an action it takes. "7,000 bankruptcies this year."
融資yuushi Financing, a loan The specific act of lending money to someone. "The bank financed the local business."
金融kin'yuu Finance (the field) The broad industry/sector, not a single lending action. "The financial markets," "monetary policy."
開拓kaitaku Cultivating, opening up Actively developing new demand, land, or a field. "Tap into a new market."
利益rieki A profit The resulting gain, not the act of pursuing it. "The company posted a profit."
斡旋assen Mediation, job placement An institution arranges a match between two parties. "Job-placement assistance."
推薦suisen Recommendation An individual/party endorses one specific candidate. "Recommended by the party."
Exam trap: 合併 vs 倒産 both describe a company's fate in a downturn but point opposite directions — 合併 is an active, often strategic joining of two companies, while 倒産 is a company failing outright. News headlines about "20 sectors" or "7,000 firms" almost always need 倒産 for the failure count and 合併 for the deal.
