JLPT N1 · Kanji · Week 4 — 難しい読みを覚えよう (Muzukashii yomi o oboeyou)

6日目 — 大家・大家 (Ooya, Taika)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.70–71 (PDF pages 71–72)
Theme
14 written words whose meaning changes entirely depending on how they're read (読み方で意味が変わるもの) — the title itself, 大家, is the example: おおや (a landlord) and たいか (a master) are the same two kanji
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key — flagged transparently, same policy as Days 1–5.
Notes
The board-question gag on p.70 asks how to read 舞台の上手 — the answer is かみて (a stage-direction term), not じょうず (good at) or うわて (superior) — proof that context alone decides which reading applies.

Q1

チャンピオンも強かったが、さらに上手の挑戦者がいた。Chanpion mo tsuyokatta ga, sara ni joute no chousensha ga ita.

EN: The champion was strong too, but there was an even more skilled challenger.
HI: चैंपियन भी मज़बूत था, पर एक और भी बेहतर चुनौती देने वाला मौजूद था।
GU: ચેમ્પિયન પણ મજબૂત હતો, પણ એક વધુ કુશળ પડકારનાર હાજર હતો.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
aうわてa superior, more skilled personअधिक निपुण व्यक्तिવધુ નિપુણ વ્યક્તિ
bかみてthe stage-direction term (wrong context)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Comparing skill levels between two competitors calls for うわて ("someone better/superior"); かみて only applies to describing stage position, which is irrelevant here.

Q2

その件については目下検討中です。Sono ken ni tsuite wa mokka kentouchuu desu.

EN: That matter is currently under consideration.
HI: उस मामले पर फ़िलहाल विचार किया जा रहा है।
GU: તે બાબત પર હાલમાં વિચારણા ચાલી રહી છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
aめしたa subordinate (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bもっかat presentफ़िलहाल, अभीહાલમાં, અત્યારે

Why: 目下検討中 ("currently under review") uses もっか ("at present"); めした describes a person's subordinate rank and cannot modify "under consideration."

Q3

分別のある人なら、そんなことはしないはずだ。(___) no aru hito nara, sonna koto wa shinai hazu da.

EN: Someone with good sense/discretion wouldn't do such a thing.
HI: जिसमें विवेक होगा, वह ऐसा नहीं करेगा।
GU: જેનામાં વિવેક હોય તે આવું ન કરે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
aふんべつdiscretion, good senseविवेकવિવેક
bぶんべつsorting/separation (wrong sense, describes objects)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 分別のある人 ("a person of discretion/good judgment") uses ふんべつ; ぶんべつ describes sorting physical items (like garbage) and cannot describe a person's character.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every pair below is literally the same written word — the entire day is one long confusion-pairs lesson. Pulled straight from 練習Ⅰ above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
上手（うわて）joute Someone more skilled Compares two people's ability directly. "There's someone even better than the champion."
上手（かみて）kamite Stage-direction term Refers to physical position on a stage. "The actor entered from stage right (as seen by the audience)."
目下（もっか）mokka At present A time adverb — describes an ongoing state now. "Currently under review."
目下（めした）meshita A subordinate Describes a person's rank relative to another. "Speaking to one's junior/subordinate."
分別（ふんべつ）funbetsu Discretion, good sense Describes a person's judgment/character. "A person of good sense wouldn't do that."
分別（ぶんべつ）bunbetsu Sorting, separation Describes sorting physical items. "Sort the garbage into categories."
生物（なまもの）namamono Perishable food A food-safety/storage term. "Refrigerate — perishable."
生物（せいぶつ）seibutsu Living things (biology) A scientific/academic term. "The study of biology."
Exam trap: 大家, 上手, and 生物 are the day's most testable words because their two readings don't just differ in nuance — they mean genuinely unrelated things. On a 漢字読み question, never guess a reading from the kanji alone; find the context clue (a stage, a craftsman, a food label) that tells you which meaning — and therefore which reading — applies.
