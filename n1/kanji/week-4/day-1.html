1日目 — 相互・首相・相性 (Sougo, Shushou, Aishou)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N1 Kanji) — printed p.60–61 (PDF pages 61–62; this scan runs one page ahead of the printed number for Week 4 — verified against the printed folio at the bottom of each page)
Theme
9 kanji, each with several very different readings (on-yomi and/or kun-yomi) that change depending on the word — the opposite problem from Weeks 1–2, where different kanji shared one reading
Answer key
This book's answers/explanations live entirely in a separate [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, not included in the uploaded scan. 練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers below are worked out from the book's own definitions on this spread, not copied from an official key — flagged transparently, same policy as Week 1–3.
Notes
The board-question gag on p.60 asks how to read 利口 (りこう, clever) — the point being 口 alone can be コウ, ク, or くち depending on the word. Every kanji below works the same way: don't memorize "one kanji = one sound," memorize the reading word by word.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

その女は（　）電話をかけて嫌がらせをした。Sono onna wa (___) denwa o kakete iyagarase o shita.

Full sentence: その女は無言電話をかけて嫌がらせをした。Sono onna wa mugon denwa o kakete iyagarase o shita.
EN: That woman harassed people by making silent phone calls.
HI: उस औरत ने ख़ामोश फ़ोन कॉल करके परेशान किया।
GU: તે સ્ત્રીએ મૌન ફોન કોલ કરીને હેરાન કર્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a無口mukuchireticent (a personality trait, not a call)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b無言mugonsilent, wordlessमौन, ख़ामोशમૌન

Why: 無言電話 is the fixed term for a "silent call" (the caller says nothing) — a known form of harassment. 無口 describes a quiet person's character, not the nature of a phone call.

Q2

（　）は聞きたくない。今後注意するように。(___) wa kikitakunai. Kongo chuui suru you ni.

Full sentence: 言い訳は聞きたくない。今後注意するように。Ii wake wa kikitakunai. Kongo chuui suru you ni.
EN: I don't want to hear excuses. Be more careful from now on.
HI: मुझे बहाने नहीं सुनने। आगे से सावधान रहना।
GU: મારે બહાનાં નથી સાંભળવાં. હવેથી ધ્યાન રાખજો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a言い付けii tsukean order/tattling (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b言い訳ii wakean excuseबहानाબહાનું

Why: "I don't want to hear ___, be careful from now on" is a scolding — you refuse to hear someone's 言い訳 (excuse). 言い付け means an order given to someone, or tattling on someone — it isn't something a scolded person offers.

Q3

電力消費の無駄を（　）。Denryoku shouhi no muda o (___).

Full sentence: 電力消費の無駄を省く。Denryoku shouhi no muda o habuku.
EN: Eliminate the waste in power consumption.
HI: बिजली की खपत में फ़िज़ूलख़र्ची को हटाओ।
GU: વીજ વપરાશમાં બિનજરૂરી બગાડ દૂર કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a省くhabukuto eliminate, cut outहटाना, छाँटनाદૂર કરવું
b省みるkaerimiruto reflect on oneself (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 無駄を省く ("eliminate waste") is a fixed collocation; 省みる is reflexive self-examination (自らを省みる) and cannot take "無駄" as its object.

Q4

中途（　）な改革ではなく、やるなら徹底的にやってほしい。Chuuto (___) na kaikaku de wa naku, yaru nara tettei-teki ni yatte hoshii.

Full sentence: 中途半端な改革ではなく、やるなら徹底的にやってほしい。Chuuto hanpa na kaikaku de wa naku, yaru nara tettei-teki ni yatte hoshii.
EN: I want a thorough reform, not a half-hearted one, if we're doing it at all.
HI: मैं चाहता/चाहती हूँ कि अधूरा सुधार नहीं, बल्कि पूरी तरह से किया जाए।
GU: અધૂરો સુધારો નહીં, પણ સંપૂર્ણ સુધારો જોઈએ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a極端kyokutanextreme (中途極端 is not a word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b半端hanpahalfway, incompleteअधूराઅધૂરું

Why: 中途半端 is a fixed four-character-style phrase meaning "halfway, indecisive." 中途極端 does not exist as a word — 極端 (extreme) pairs with other phrasing, not 中途.

Q5

皇室関係の仕事をする行政機関を（　）庁という。Koushitsu kankei no shigoto o suru gyousei kikan o (___) chou to iu.

Full sentence: 皇室関係の仕事をする行政機関を宮内庁という。Koushitsu kankei no shigoto o suru gyousei kikan o kunaichou to iu.
EN: The administrative body that handles Imperial Household affairs is called the Imperial Household Agency.
HI: राजपरिवार से जुड़े काम करने वाली प्रशासनिक संस्था को "कुनाइचो" कहते हैं।
GU: શાહી પરિવારને લગતું કામ કરતી વહીવટી સંસ્થાને "કુનાઇચો" કહે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a宮殿kyuudena palace (宮殿庁 is not a word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b宮内kunaiwithin the Imperial Householdराजगृह-संबंधीશાહી ગૃહને લગતું

Why: 宮内庁 (くないちょう) is the actual, fixed name of Japan's Imperial Household Agency. 宮殿 means "palace" (the building) and never combines with 庁.

Q6

勝った試合のインタビューは監督の（　）も滑らかだった。Katta shiai no intabyuu wa kantoku no (___) mo nameraka datta.

Full sentence: 勝った試合のインタビューは監督の口調も滑らかだった。Katta shiai no intabyuu wa kantoku no kuchou mo nameraka datta.
EN: In the post-victory interview, the manager's tone was smooth too.
HI: जीत के बाद के साक्षात्कार में कोच का लहजा भी सहज था।
GU: જીત પછીના ઈન્ટરવ્યુમાં કોચનો બોલવાનો લહેજો પણ સહજ હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a利口rikouclever (describes a person, not a tone)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b口調kuchoua tone of voice, manner of speechबोलने का लहजाબોલવાનો લહેજો

Why: 口調が滑らかだ ("the tone/delivery was smooth") describes how someone spoke. 利口 describes intelligence/cleverness as a trait, and doesn't collocate with 滑らか (smooth).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's "confusion" isn't between two different kanji — it's between two different readings of the same kanji that get mixed up because they look alike on the page. Every 練習Ⅰ question above tests exactly this:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
無言mugon Silent, wordless Describes an act (a silent call, silent protest) — 言 read ゴン. "A silent phone call," "he left without a word."
無口mukuchi Reticent, taciturn Describes a person's personality trait — 口 read くち. "He's a quiet, reticent person by nature."
言い訳ii wake An excuse What the person being scolded offers. "Stop making excuses."
言い付けii tsuke An order; tattling What a superior gives, or what a tattletale reports. "Follow my orders." / "She told on him."
省くhabuku To cut out, eliminate Takes an external object (waste, a step, trouble). "Eliminate the wasted steps."
省みるkaerimiru To reflect on oneself Reflexive — the object is always oneself (自ら). "Reflect on your own conduct."
中途半端chuuto hanpa Halfway, incomplete A fixed four-part phrase — 端 read は here. "A half-hearted, unfinished effort."
極端kyokutan Extreme A separate word — never combines with 中途. "An extreme case/example."
宮内庁kunaichou The Imperial Household Agency The real, fixed name of the government body — 宮 read ク here. Referring to Japan's Imperial administrative agency.
宮殿kyuuden A palace The physical building — 宮 read キュウ here. "The Emperor's palace."
口調kuchou A tone of voice Describes how something is said. "His tone was smooth/harsh."
利口rikou Clever Describes intelligence, not delivery. "A clever child/animal."
Exam trap: 相 is the single most testable kanji this week — read そう in 相互/相談/相違/真相, ショウ in 首相/外相, and あい in 相性/相変わらず. On a 漢字読み question, never assume 相 has one sound; check whether the word is about mutuality (そう), government rank (ショウ), or a personal match (あい) before choosing.
