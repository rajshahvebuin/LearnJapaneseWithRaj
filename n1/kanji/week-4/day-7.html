問題1 — choose the correct reading (Q1–10, 1問4点)

Q1

社員募集。経験の有無は問いません。Shain boshuu. Keiken no umu wa toimasen.

EN: Now hiring. Whether or not you have experience doesn't matter.
HI: कर्मचारी भर्ती। अनुभव है या नहीं, इससे फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता।
GU: કર્મચારી ભરતી. અનુભવ છે કે નહીં, તેનાથી ફરક પડતો નથી.
#ReadingNote
1ゆうぶnot a valid reading
2うぶa different word (naive) — wrong kanji match
3ゆうむnot a valid reading
4うむpresence or absence — Day 2, Set D

Why: 有無 always reads うむ; the other options aren't valid readings of this compound.

Q2

吹雪の後、雪崩が起きた。Fubuki no ato, nadare ga okita.

EN: After the snowstorm, an avalanche occurred.
HI: बर्फ़ीले तूफ़ान के बाद, हिमस्खलन हुआ।
GU: બરફના તોફાન પછી, હિમપ્રપાત થયો.
#ReadingNote
1すゆきnot a valid reading
2ふゆきnot a valid reading
3すぶきnot a valid reading
4ふぶきa snowstorm — Day 4, Set C

Why: 吹雪 reads ふぶき; none of the other choices are valid readings.

Q3

差し支えなければ、ご住所を教えていただけませんか。Sashitsukae nakereba, go-juusho o oshiete itadakemasen ka.

EN: If it's no trouble, could you tell me your address?
HI: अगर कोई परेशानी न हो, तो क्या आप अपना पता बता सकते हैं?
GU: જો કોઈ તકલીફ ન હોય, તો શું તમે તમારું સરનામું જણાવી શકશો?
#ReadingNote
1さしつかえto interfere/be a problem — Day 4, Set A
2さしささえnot a valid reading
3さしひかえnot a valid reading
4さしかかえnot a valid reading

Why: 差し支える reads さしつかえる; no other option is a real reading of this word.

Q4

江戸時代の情緒が残る町並みを歩く。Edo jidai no jouchou ga nokoru machinami o aruku.

EN: To walk through a townscape that retains the atmosphere of the Edo period.
HI: एदो काल का माहौल बरक़रार रखने वाली गलियों में चलना।
GU: એદો કાળનું વાતાવરણ જાળવી રાખતી શેરીઓમાં ચાલવું.
#ReadingNote
1ぜいしょnot a valid reading
2せいちょnot a valid reading
3じょうちょan emotion/atmosphere — Day 2, Set A
4ふぜいa real word (風情, tasteful appearance) but the wrong kanji

Why: 情緒 reads じょうちょ; ふぜい is a genuine Japanese word but belongs to a different kanji (風情), not this one.

Q5

世間体を気にしないで思い通りに生きる。Sekentei o ki ni shinai de omoidoori ni ikiru.

EN: To live as one pleases without worrying about appearances.
HI: लोगों की परवाह किए बिना अपनी मर्ज़ी से जीना।
GU: લોકોની પરવા કર્યા વગર પોતાની મરજી પ્રમાણે જીવવું.
#ReadingNote
1せけんていdecency, appearances — Day 2, Set C
2せげんていnot a valid reading
3せけんたいnot a valid reading
4せまんたいnot a valid reading

Why: 世間体 reads せけんてい; the other options aren't valid readings.

Q6

私は日本の相撲に興味があります。Watashi wa nihon no sumou ni kyoumi ga arimasu.

EN: I'm interested in Japanese sumo.
HI: मुझे जापानी सूमो में दिलचस्पी है।
GU: મને જાપાની સૂમોમાં રસ છે.
#ReadingNote
1こうみnot a valid reading
2きゅうみnot a valid reading
3しゅみa real word (趣味, a hobby) but the wrong kanji
4きょうみinterest — Day 5, Group 1

Why: 興味 reads きょうみ; しゅみ is a real word ("a hobby") but is written 趣味, a different kanji.

Q7

もう少し効率のいいやり方はないのだろうか。Mou sukoshi kouritsu no ii yarikata wa nai no darou ka.

EN: Isn't there a somewhat more efficient way of doing this?
HI: क्या इससे थोड़ा और बेहतर, दक्ष तरीक़ा नहीं है?
GU: શું આના કરતાં થોડી વધુ કાર્યક્ષમ રીત નથી?
#ReadingNote
1こりつnot a valid reading
2こうりつefficiency — Day 5, Group 7
3きょうりつnot a valid reading
4こうそつnot a valid reading (率 read ソツ pairs with 軽率, not 効率)

Why: 効率 reads こうりつ; こうそつ mixes up 率's two readings — ソツ belongs to 軽率/統率, not 効率.

Q8

首相は発言の事実を否定した。Shushou wa hatsugen no jijitsu o hitei shita.

EN: The prime minister denied having made the remark.
HI: प्रधानमंत्री ने अपने बयान के तथ्य से इनकार किया।
GU: વડાપ્રધાને પોતાના નિવેદનની હકીકતનો ઇનકાર કર્યો.
#ReadingNote
1はつげんa remark — Day 1, Group 3
2ほつげんnot a valid reading (発 reads ホツ only in specific words like 発作)
3はつごんnot a valid reading
4ほつごんnot a valid reading

Why: 発言 reads はつげん; 発's irregular ホツ reading is specific to words like 発作/発足/発起 (Day 2), not 発言.

#ReadingNote
1せんだんnot a valid reading
2せんたんthe cutting edge — Day 1, Group 6
3さきばたnot a valid reading
4さきはしnot a valid reading

Why: 先端 reads せんたん; the other options aren't valid readings.

問題2 — choose the correct kanji (Q11–15, 1問4点)

Q11

名前のかしらもじを書く。Namae no kashiramoji o kaku.

EN: To write the initials of one's name.
HI: नाम के आद्याक्षर लिखना।
GU: નામના આદ્યાક્ષર લખવા.
#JapaneseNote
1首文字not a real word
2顔文字a real word (kaomoji, an emoticon) — wrong word
3絵文字a real word (emoji) — wrong word
4頭文字initials — Day 3

Why: かしらもじ is written 頭文字; 顔文字/絵文字 are unrelated real words that merely look similar.

Q12

いっこだてに住む。Ikkodate ni sumu.

EN: To live in a detached house.
HI: स्वतंत्र मकान में रहना।
GU: સ્વતંત્ર મકાનમાં રહેવું.
#JapaneseNote
1一戸建てa detached house — Day 2, Set F
2一個建てnot a real word (wrong counter kanji)
3一戸立てnot a real word (wrong verb kanji)
4一個立てnot a real word

Why: いっこだて is written 一戸建て — 戸 (a household/door), not 個 (a generic counter), and 建て (built), not 立て (stood up).

Q13

かいがを見に行く。Kaiga o mi ni iku.

EN: To go see a painting/art exhibition.
HI: चित्रकला देखने जाना।
GU: ચિત્રકળા જોવા જવું.
#JapaneseNote
1階下reads かいか (downstairs) — wrong word
2海外reads かいがい (overseas) — wrong word
3絵画a painting — Day 3
4開花reads かいか (blooming) — wrong word

Why: かいが is written 絵画; 階下/開花 both read かいか (one syllable short) and 海外 reads かいがい (one syllable long) — close but different words.

Q14

店の前に長いぎょうれつができた。Mise no mae ni nagai gyouretsu ga dekita.

EN: A long line formed in front of the store.
HI: दुकान के सामने लंबी कतार लग गई।
GU: દુકાનની આગળ લાંબી કતાર લાગી ગઈ.
#JapaneseNote
1系列reads けいれつ (a corporate group/series) — wrong word
2業列not a real word
3形列not a real word
4行列a procession/queue — Day 2, Set C

Why: ぎょうれつ is written 行列; 系列 is a real but unrelated word (a corporate affiliation group) read けいれつ.

Q15

傘のえに名前を書いた。Kasa no e ni namae o kaita.

EN: I wrote my name on the umbrella's handle.
HI: मैंने छाते की मूठ पर अपना नाम लिखा।
GU: મેં છત્રીના હાથા પર મારું નામ લખ્યું.
#JapaneseNote
1枝a branch — wrong word
2柄a handle — Day 1, Group 8
3得gain/profit — wrong word
4江an inlet — wrong word

Why: An umbrella's handle (傘の柄) is written 柄, read え in this word — the same kanji whose other readings (へい/がら) appeared on Day 1.

問題3 — choose the correct kanji for the blank (Q16–20, 1問4点)

Q16

家族連れが海（　）で楽しそうに遊んでいる。Kazokuzure ga umi(___) de tanoshisou ni asonde iru.

Full sentence: 家族連れが海辺で楽しそうに遊んでいる。Kazokuzure ga umibe de tanoshisou ni asonde iru.
EN: Families are happily playing at the seaside.
HI: परिवार समुद्रतट पर ख़ुशी-ख़ुशी खेल रहे हैं।
GU: પરિવારો દરિયાકિનારે ખુશીથી રમી રહ્યા છે.
#JapaneseNote
1周海周 isn't a word
2端海端 isn't a standard word
3辺海辺, the seaside
4近海近 isn't a word

Why: 海辺 (umibe, "the seaside") was named directly on Day 3 as a related form of 浜辺 (hamabe, "a beach").

Q17

この野菜には（　）毒作用がある。Kono yasai ni wa (___)doku sayou ga aru.

Full sentence: この野菜には解毒作用がある。Kono yasai ni wa gedoku sayou ga aru.
EN: This vegetable has a detoxifying effect.
HI: इस सब्ज़ी में विषहरण करने का गुण है।
GU: આ શાકભાજીમાં વિષહરણ કરવાનો ગુણ છે.
#JapaneseNote
1下not a real combination here
2解解毒, detoxification — Day 5, Group 4
3省not a real combination here
4上not a real combination here

Why: 解毒 (げどく, "detoxification") is Day 5's own word; none of the other kanji forms a real "___毒" compound with this meaning.

Q18

このところ不調だった投手が今日は好投し、（　）名を返上した。Kono tokoro fuchou datta toushu ga kyou wa koutou shi, (___)mei o henjou shita.

Full sentence: ...汚名を返上した。...omei o henjou shita.
EN: The pitcher, who had been struggling lately, pitched well today and cleared his tarnished name.
HI: हाल ही में ख़राब फ़ॉर्म में रहे गेंदबाज़ ने आज अच्छी गेंदबाज़ी करके अपनी बदनामी मिटाई।
GU: તાજેતરમાં નબળા ફોર્મમાં રહેલા બોલરે આજે સારો બોલિંગ કરીને પોતાની બદનામી દૂર કરી.
#JapaneseNote
1悪悪名 exists but doesn't pair with 返上 as a fixed idiom
2汚汚名返上, clearing a tarnished name — Day 2, Set F
3醜not a real combination here
4卑not a real combination here

Why: 汚名返上 (おめいへんじょう, "clearing one's tarnished name") is the fixed four-character phrase introduced on Day 2.

Q19

EN: For an interview suit, colors like navy, gray, or black are probably a safe choice.
HI: इंटरव्यू के सूट के लिए, नेवी, ग्रे या काला रंग शायद सुरक्षित विकल्प होंगे।
GU: ઇન્ટરવ્યુના સૂટ માટે, નેવી, ગ્રે અથવા કાળો રંગ કદાચ સલામત વિકલ્પ હશે.

3否not a real combination here

1札立て札, a signboard — Day 6, Group 8

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A week-wide cheat sheet — the one pattern each day was built around, to drill right before the test:

PatternWhat it meansExampleDay
One kanji, several unrelated readings相 alone is そう (相互), ショウ (首相), or あい (相性)相互・首相・相性1
A flat glossary of irregular on-yomiEach kanji hides one unexpected sound: 弟＝デ, not テイ弟子deshi2
The same kanji shifts kun-yomi mid-list小 is お in 小川 but こ in 小包小川／小包3
熟字訓 (jukujikun) — whole-word readingsThe reading belongs to the whole word, not each kanji行方yukue／迷子maigo4
Two on-yomi per kanji, worth pairing省 is ショウ (省略) and セイ (反省); 率 is リツ and ソツ省略／反省5
Same word, different reading, different meaning大家 is おおや (landlord) or たいか (a master)大家6
Test-day strategy: Every question on this page hinges on one habit: never guess a reading from the kanji shapes alone. Find the context — a stage direction, a food label, a clinic's night hours, a suit color — and let it tell you which of the week's several possible readings actually applies.
