3日目 — 浜辺・雨具・家主 (Hamabe, Amagu, Yanushi)

Source

Theme
Two lists: 21 words with difficult kun-yomi readings (難しい訓読み), then 12 words whose readings are easy to mix up or guess wrong (とまどいやすい読み) — again a flat vocabulary sheet rather than component clusters
Answer key

Notes
The board-question gag on p.64 asks how to read 頭文字 (かしらもじ, initials) — 頭 here reads かしら, not あたま or トウ. Kun-yomi words like this resist any single memorization rule; each one has to be learned as its own word.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items) and 練習Ⅱ (4 items, one shared kanji per item), both from p.65. This book's answer key is in a separate [別冊] booklet not included in the scan — answers below are worked out from the meanings on this same spread, flagged as reasoned rather than officially confirmed.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

ベテランの店員は（　）よく商品を包装した。Beteran no ten'in wa (___) yoku shouhin o housou shita.

Full sentence: ベテランの店員は手際よく商品を包装した。Beteran no ten'in wa tegiwa yoku shouhin o housou shita.
EN: The veteran clerk wrapped the product deftly/efficiently.
HI: अनुभवी कर्मचारी ने कुशलतापूर्वक सामान की पैकिंग की।
GU: અનુભવી કર્મચારીએ કુશળતાપૂર્વક વસ્તુ પેક કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手際tegiwaskill, efficiencyदक्षता, कुशलताકુશળતા
b巧みtakumiskillful (wrong adverb form: 巧みに, not 巧みよく)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 手際よく is the fixed adverbial phrase meaning "deftly, efficiently" (手際がいい/よい + よく). 巧み takes a different adverb form, 巧みに — "巧みよく" is not natural Japanese.

Q2

（　）のたった、炊きたてのご飯。(___) no tatta, takitate no gohan.

Full sentence: 湯気のたった、炊きたてのご飯。Yuge no tatta, takitate no gohan.
EN: Freshly cooked rice with steam rising off it.
HI: भाप उठती हुई, ताज़ी पकी हुई चावल।
GU: વરાળ ઊઠતા, તાજા બનેલા ભાત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生地kijifabric/dough (wrong noun for rice)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b湯気yugesteamभापવરાળ

Why: 湯気が立つ ("steam rises") is the fixed collocation describing hot food/drink; 生地 refers to fabric or unbaked dough, not vapor.

Q3

（　）と建前を使い分ける。(___) to tatemae o tsukaiwakeru.

Full sentence: 本音と建前を使い分ける。Honne to tatemae o tsukaiwakeru.
EN: To switch between one's real feelings and one's public stance as needed.
HI: असली मंशा और दिखावटी रुख़ को हालात के हिसाब से बदलना।
GU: સાચી લાગણી અને દેખાડાનું વલણ પરિસ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે બદલવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a音色neiroa tone (of an instrument, wrong pairing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b本音honnea real intentionअसली मंशाસાચો ઈરાદો

Why: 本音と建前 ("real feelings vs. public façade") is one of the most famous fixed pairs in Japanese social vocabulary. 音色 describes a musical tone and has no relation to 建前.

Q4

（　）の時報とともに、休憩する。(___) no jihou to tomo ni, kyuukei suru.

Full sentence: 正午の時報とともに、休憩する。Shougo no jihou to tomo ni, kyuukei suru.
EN: (We/They) take a break at the noon time signal.
HI: दोपहर के समय-संकेत के साथ ही विश्राम करते हैं।
GU: બપોરના સમય-સંકેત સાથે જ આરામ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a正午shougonoonदोपहर, बारह बजेબપોરે બાર વાગ્યે
b夜明けyoakedawn (no time-signal custom)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 正午の時報 (the noon siren/time signal) is a real daily custom in Japan; 夜明け (dawn) has no equivalent fixed "time signal" phrase.

Q5

夫が見るテレビ番組は、（　）ニュースです。Otto ga miru terebi bangumi wa, (___) nyuusu desu.

Full sentence: 夫が見るテレビ番組は、専らニュースです。Otto ga miru terebi bangumi wa, moppara nyuusu desu.
EN: The TV programs my husband watches are exclusively the news.
HI: मेरे पति जो टीवी कार्यक्रम देखते हैं, वे सिर्फ़ समाचार होते हैं।
GU: મારા પતિ જે ટીવી કાર્યક્રમો જુએ છે તે ફક્ત સમાચાર જ હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a自らmizukaraoneself, personally (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's confusions come from words that sound like they should pair together but don't — pulled straight from 練習Ⅰ above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
手際（よく）tegiwa (yoku) Skillfully, efficiently Adverb form is 手際よく — pairs with よく, not に. "She wrapped it deftly/efficiently."
巧み（に）takumi (ni) Skillful, clever Adverb form is 巧みに — pairs with に, not よく. "He spoke with clever wordplay (言葉巧みに)."
本音honne One's real intention/feelings Pairs with 建前 as a famous fixed contrast. "Say what you really think, not just the polite version."
音色neiro A tone (of an instrument/voice) Describes acoustic quality, unrelated to 建前. "The violin's tone was warm."
正午shougo Noon Has a real fixed custom: 正午の時報 (the noon signal). "Take a break at the noon siren."
夜明けyoake Dawn No equivalent "time signal" custom attached to it. "They departed at dawn."
屋外／野外okugai / yagai Outdoors, in the open air Near-synonyms; both describe being outside. "Outdoor parking," "an open-air concert."
屋内okunai Indoors The direct opposite of 屋外. "An indoor pool."
Exam trap: 小 and 子 are today's most testable kanji — each keeps changing sound between words that look almost identical in structure (小川 お・小包 こ; 双子 ご・扇子 す). Never assume a kun-yomi kanji keeps the same sound in its next compound — check the whole word.
