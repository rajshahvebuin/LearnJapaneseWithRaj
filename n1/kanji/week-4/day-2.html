Q1

子どもの教育費を（　）するために昼夜を問わず働く。Kodomo no kyouikuhi o (___) suru tame ni chuuya o towazu hataraku.

Full sentence: 子どもの教育費を工面するために昼夜を問わず働く。Kodomo no kyouikuhi o kumen suru tame ni chuuya o towazu hataraku.
EN: (He/She) works day and night to scrape together the children's education expenses.
HI: बच्चों की पढ़ाई के ख़र्च के लिए पैसों का जुगाड़ करने को दिन-रात काम करता/करती है।
GU: બાળકોના શિક્ષણ ખર્ચ માટે પૈસાની વ્યવસ્થા કરવા દિવસ-રાત કામ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a工夫kufuua devised method (wrong object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b工面kumento manage to get moneyपैसों का जुगाड़પૈસાની વ્યવસ્થા

Why: 教育費を工面する ("scrape together money for education costs") is the fixed collocation for raising a specific sum; 工夫する means to devise a clever method or idea and doesn't pair naturally with a sum of money.

GU: અભૂતપૂર્વ પૂરની આપત્તિનો સામનો કરવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a未曾有mizouunprecedentedअभूतपूर्वઅભૂતપૂર્વ
b有頂天uchoutenecstasy (wrong sense entirely)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 未曾有 describes a disaster of a scale never before seen; 有頂天 describes personal elation/joy, which cannot describe a flood.

Q3

トキという鳥は（　）記念物に指定されている。Toki to iu tori wa (___) kinenbutsu ni shitei sarete iru.

Full sentence: トキという鳥は天然記念物に指定されている。Toki to iu tori wa tennen kinenbutsu ni shitei sarete iru.
EN: The bird called the Japanese crested ibis is designated a natural monument.
HI: टोकी नाम की चिड़िया को प्राकृतिक स्मारक घोषित किया गया है।
GU: ટોકી નામનું પક્ષી કુદરતી સ્મારક તરીકે જાહેર થયેલું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a天然tennennaturalप्राकृतिकકુદરતી
b黄金ougongold (黄金記念物 isn't a word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 天然記念物 is the fixed legal term for a protected natural monument (species or site); 黄金 (gold) doesn't combine with 記念物 in this sense.

Q4

必要な時、その（　）発行できる1回限りのクレジットカードがあるらしい。Hitsuyou na toki, sono (___) hakkou dekiru ikkai kagiri no kurejitto kaado ga aru rashii.

GU: લાગે છે કે જરૂર પડે ત્યારે દર વખતે ઈશ્યુ કરી શકાય એવું વન-ટાઇમ ક્રેડિટ કાર્ડ છે.

Q5

この商品の（　）品にご注意ください。Kono shouhin no (___)-hin ni go-chuui kudasai.

Full sentence: この商品の類似品にご注意ください。Kono shouhin no ruiji-hin ni go-chuui kudasai.
EN: Please beware of imitation/lookalike products of this item.
HI: कृपया इस उत्पाद के नक़ली/मिलते-जुलते उत्पादों से सावधान रहें।
GU: કૃપા કરી આ ઉત્પાદનની મળતી (નકલી) વસ્તુઓથી સાવધ રહો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a類似ruijiresemblance, similarसमानता, मिलता-जुलताસામ્યતા, મળતું
b疑似gijiquasi-/pseudo- (wrong term for counterfeits)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 類似品 is the standard warning phrase for "imitation/counterfeit goods"; 疑似 means "quasi-/pseudo-" (as in 疑似体験, a simulated experience) and isn't used for counterfeit merchandise.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's confusions are between look-alike words built from the same starting kanji but meaning something completely different — pulled straight from 練習Ⅰ above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
工夫kufuu A devised method, an idea The object is a plan, method, or approach. "Come up with a clever way to do X."
工面kumen Managing to get money together The object is always a sum of money. "Scrape together money for tuition."
未曾有mizou Unprecedented Describes a scale of event never seen before. "An unprecedented disaster/crisis."
有頂天uchouten Ecstasy, being overjoyed Describes a person's emotional high. "He was over the moon about the win."
都度tsudo Every time, each occasion Marks repetition across occasions. "Issued each and every time it's needed."
都合tsugou Convenience, circumstances Describes whether something suits someone. "If it's convenient for you."
類似ruiji Resemblance, similarity Used for lookalike/counterfeit products. "Beware of imitation products."
疑似giji Quasi-, pseudo-, simulated Used for simulations/near-equivalents, not fakes sold as real. "A simulated experience," "pseudo-science."
人質hitojichi A hostage Someone held against their will for leverage. "The hijacker took hostages."
弟子deshi An apprentice, disciple Someone who studies under a master voluntarily. "A martial arts master's apprentice."
Exam trap: 工 is the day's most testable kanji — read ク in 工夫/工面/大工, none of which mean the same thing. Whenever a 漢字読み question gives you a 工-compound, check whether it's about a method (工夫), money (工面), or a profession (大工) before assuming the meaning.
