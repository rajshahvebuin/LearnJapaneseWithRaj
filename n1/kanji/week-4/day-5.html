練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

金メダルが取れた（　）で、ゆうべは眠れませんでした。Kin medaru ga toreta (___) de, yuube wa nemuremasen deshita.

Full sentence: 金メダルが取れた興奮で、ゆうべは眠れませんでした。Kin medaru ga toreta koufun de, yuube wa nemuremasen deshita.
EN: I couldn't sleep last night from the excitement of winning the gold medal.
HI: स्वर्ण पदक जीतने की उत्तेजना के कारण मैं कल रात सो नहीं पाया/पाई।
GU: ગોલ્ડ મેડલ જીતવાની ઉત્તેજનાને કારણે ગઈકાલે રાત્રે ઊંઘ ન આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a興奮koufunexcitementउत्तेजना, जोशઉત્તેજના
b余興yokyouentertainment/a sideshow act (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 興奮で眠れない ("too excited to sleep") is a natural cause-and-effect; 余興 refers to a supplementary performance/entertainment at an event and has no "keeping you awake" sense.

Q2

（　）お時間をいただきまして、ありがとうございます。(___) o-jikan o itadakimashite, arigatou gozaimasu.

Full sentence: 貴重なお時間をいただきまして、ありがとうございます。Kichou na o-jikan o itadakimashite, arigatou gozaimasu.
EN: Thank you for your valuable time.
HI: आपके क़ीमती समय के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: તમારા મૂલ્યવાન સમય માટે આભાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a尊重なsonchou narespect (尊重 doesn't take な this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b貴重なkichou navaluableबहुमूल्यમૂલ્યવાન

Why: 貴重なお時間 ("your valuable time") is the standard polite fixed phrase. 尊重 normally appears as 尊重する (to respect) or 尊重されるべき, not as a plain 尊重な adjective before a noun like 時間.

Q3

（　）する会社との争いが激しくなってきた。(___) suru kaisha to no arasoi ga hageshiku natte kita.

Full sentence: 競合する会社との争いが激しくなってきた。Kyougou suru kaisha to no arasoi ga hageshiku natte kita.
EN: The rivalry with competing companies has intensified.
HI: प्रतिस्पर्धी कंपनियों के साथ होड़ तेज़ होती जा रही है।
GU: સ્પર્ધક કંપનીઓ સાથેની હરીફાઈ તીવ્ર બની રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a貿易bouekitrade (doesn't itself imply rivalry)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b競合kyougourivalry, competitionप्रतिस्पर्धाસ્પર્ધા

Why: 競合する会社 ("a rival/competing company") directly matches "争い" (conflict/rivalry). 貿易する会社 just means "a company that trades" and carries no built-in sense of rivalry.

車にはいろいろな安全（　）がついています。Kuruma ni wa iroiro na anzen (___) ga tsuite imasu.

EN: The car comes with various safety devices.
HI: कार में कई तरह के सुरक्षा उपकरण लगे हुए हैं।
GU: કારમાં અનેક પ્રકારનાં સલામતી ઉપકરણો લગાડેલાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a装置souchia deviceउपकरण, यंत्रસાધન, ઉપકરણ

Q5

文字や記号の表記を（　）する。Moji ya kigou no hyouki o (___) suru.

Full sentence: 文字や記号の表記を統一する。Moji ya kigou no hyouki o touitsu suru.
EN: To standardize the notation of characters and symbols.
HI: अक्षरों और चिह्नों की लेखन-शैली को एक समान बनाना।
GU: અક્ષરો અને ચિહ્નોની લેખન-શૈલીને એકસમાન બનાવવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a統一touitsuunification, standardizationएकीकरणએકીકરણ
b同一douitsuidentical (a state, not a verb of standardizing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 表記を統一する ("unify/standardize the notation") is the natural collocation for making a style consistent. 同一 describes two things already being identical/the same — it doesn't function as "to make consistent" in this construction.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's confusions come from words that share a kanji but land in very different registers — pulled straight from 練習Ⅰ above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
興奮koufun Excitement A personal emotional state. "Too excited to sleep."
余興yokyou Entertainment, a sideshow act A performance/activity at an event. "A magic show as the evening's entertainment."
貴重なkichou na Valuable The standard polite adjective for someone's time/input. "Thank you for your valuable time."
尊重（する）sonchou (suru) To respect Almost always a verb (尊重する), not a plain な-adjective. "Respect someone's opinion."
競合kyougou Rivalry, competition Implies conflict/contention between parties. "A competing/rival company."
貿易boueki Trade Neutral — describes commerce, not rivalry. "A trading company."
統一touitsu Unification, standardization An action — making things consistent. "Standardize the notation style."
同一douitsu Identical A state — two things already being the same. "The same person" (同一人物)."
Exam trap: 省 and 率 both carry two unrelated on-yomi (省: ショウ/セイ; 率: リツ/ソツ) that Week 4 tests repeatedly — 省 already appeared on Day 1 with the same two readings. Whenever a 漢字読み question gives you one of these two kanji, check whether the compound is about "omitting/administration" (ショウ) vs. "reflecting/returning home" (セイ) for 省, or "a ratio" (リツ) vs. "leading/hasty" (ソツ) for 率.
