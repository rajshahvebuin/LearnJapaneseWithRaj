新完全マスター N1 漢字

Following 新完全マスター漢字 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Kanji, スリーエーネットワーク) lesson by lesson — 第1部 kun-yomi (11 回 by word class: verbs A/B/C level, adjectives, nouns by theme), 第2部 on-yomi and special readings (20 回), four 広がる広げる漢字の知識 columns, and 第3部 力試し (5 mock tests + 2 challenge sections). Each page gives the book's word list with readings and examples, every exercise and test item with answers from the book's own key (page-foot answers and the bound-in 別冊), and confusion pairs — in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
