Notes
"動詞Cレベル" = both the word and its kanji are N1 — so 諮る・賜る・奉る・免れる may be new to you as words, not just as kanji. From this 回 each study page also has a 練習 II (read the sentence aloud = give the reading of the bold verb). Traps: three or more verbs sharing one reading — 堪える/耐える (たえる), 諮る/計る/図る (はかる), 障る/触る (さわる), 傷める/痛める (いためる), 駆ける/掛ける (かける) — and the spelling まぬかれる (also まぬがれる).
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
(＝…) = an accepted alternative reading the book gives
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–42)

Same-sound traps on this page: 障る (さわる, "be harmful / offend", 阝 radical) vs everyday 触る (touch); 諮る (はかる, "consult a committee", 言 radical) vs 計る/測る/量る (measure) and 図る (plan, attempt). 偽る (いつわる) is a godan verb — 偽り is its noun, tested in テスト I⑤. 賜る and 奉る are humble/ceremonial words you meet in speeches and religious contexts.
