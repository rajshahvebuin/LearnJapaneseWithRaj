Notes
A 第2部 lesson: the list gives each kanji's on-yomi and the N1 words built from it, most of which become verbs with する (反抗する, 把握する, 削減する…). The traps are kanji that share a reading — ふしん 不振/不審, ていこう 抵抗 vs てい 低・定・底, しんどう 振動/震動 — and readings that change in compounds: 悪化 (あっか), 徹する (てっする), 勘定 (かんじょう, not かんてい).
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–30); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every pair comes from this 回's list or its テスト (不審/不振 is Test II①, 抵/低/定/底 is Test II②, the three こうぎ are Test I⑦). Same sound, different kanji — the meaning of the sentence picks the kanji.
