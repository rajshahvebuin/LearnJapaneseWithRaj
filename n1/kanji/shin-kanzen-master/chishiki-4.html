Notes
This column is the key to the whole of 第2部: once you know that 青 sounds セイ, you can guess 清・晴・精・請 (all セイ). The book says about 80% of kanji are 形声文字. The traps are the exceptions — kanji that share the sound part but not the sound (特 is とく, not じ; 住 is じゅう, not ちゅう).
［形］ (けい) = the part that shows the meaning (often the radical, e.g. 木 tree, 言 words, 貝 money)
［声］ (せい) = the part that shows the sound (on-yomi), e.g. 冓 (こう) in 構・講・購

1 The Rule — 形声文字

形 + 声 — how a 形声文字 is built (the column's opening explanation)

p.88
Column

漢字の8割を占める形声文字は、意味を表す部分［形］と音を表す部分［声］からできています。Kanji no hachiwari o shimeru keisei moji wa, imi o arawasu bubun [kei] to oto o arawasu bubun [sei] kara dekite imasu.

EN: Phono-semantic characters, which make up 80% of kanji, consist of a part that shows the meaning [形] and a part that shows the sound [声].
HI: कांजी का 80% हिस्सा बनाने वाले 形声文字 (ध्वनि-अर्थ अक्षर) दो भागों से बने होते हैं — अर्थ बताने वाला भाग [形] और ध्वनि बताने वाला भाग [声]।
GU: કાંજીનો 80% ભાગ બનાવતા 形声文字 (ધ્વનિ-અર્થ અક્ષરો) બે ભાગના બનેલા હોય છે — અર્થ દર્શાવતો ભાગ [形] અને અવાજ દર્શાવતો ભાગ [声].

Tip

よく使われる漢字の音の読み方を覚えると、知らない漢字でも読み方の予想がつきます。Yoku tsukawareru kanji no on no yomikata o oboeru to, shiranai kanji demo yomikata no yosou ga tsukimasu.

EN: If you learn the on-yomi of common kanji, you can guess the reading even of kanji you don't know.
HI: आम कांजी के ओन-योमि याद कर लें, तो अनजान कांजी का उच्चारण भी अंदाज़ा लगाया जा सकता है।
GU: સામાન્ય કાંજીના ઓન-યોમિ યાદ રાખો, તો અજાણી કાંજીનું વાંચન પણ અંદાજી શકાય છે.

Three kinds of exception (the box under クイズ1)

p.88
TypeBook's wordingBook's exampleEnglishHindiGujarati
(a)違う発音をする漢字が少ないもの。Chigau hatsuon o suru kanji ga sukunai mono.青・清・晴・精・請（せい）／ 情（じょう）sei / jouOnly a few kanji in the family break the pattern.परिवार में बहुत कम कांजी नियम तोड़ती हैं।પરિવારમાં બહુ ઓછી કાંજી નિયમ તોડે છે.
(b)複数の読み方に分かれるもの。Fukusuu no yomikata ni wakareru mono.皮・疲・被（ひ）／ 波・破（は）hi / haThe family splits into two (or more) readings.परिवार दो (या ज़्यादा) उच्चारणों में बँट जाता है।પરિવાર બે (કે વધુ) વાંચનમાં વહેંચાઈ જાય છે.
(c)読み方が一つ一つ異なるもの。Yomikata ga hitotsu hitotsu kotonaru mono.根（こん）／ 銀（ぎん）／ 眼（がん）kon / gin / ganEvery kanji in the family is read differently.परिवार की हर कांजी अलग पढ़ी जाती है।પરિવારની દરેક કાંજી જુદી રીતે વંચાય છે.

Even in (c) the sound part 艮 still gives a "family resemblance": こん・ぎん・がん all end in ‑ん (our observation, added, not in book). The book's footnote (*) lists the other kinds of kanji: 象形文字 (しょうけいもじ, pictographs — 川・木・山・日・鳥), 指事文字 (しじもじ, signs for a situation — 上・下・本・天) and 会意文字 (かいいもじ, combinations of those — 林・明・休・鳴). Only 形声文字 carry a sound hint.

Exam trap: do not over-apply the rule. 形声文字 tell you the likely on-yomi, and the exam picks the exceptions: 特 (とく), 住 (じゅう), 返 (へん), 防 (ぼう), 超 (ちょう), 較 (かく), 情 (じょう). When a kanji is in a "family", check whether it is the odd one out before you trust the family reading.
