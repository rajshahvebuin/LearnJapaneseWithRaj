Notes
"動詞Cレベル" = both the word and its kanji are N1 — the hardest verb group. C-level study pages have only 練習 I (picture) and II (read aloud) — no matching task — and this テスト is out of 35点, not 40 (7 + 11 + 6 + 11). The big trap: 脅 has two verbs here — 脅す (おどす, threaten with violence) and 脅かす (おびやかす, endanger) — and in 第1回 the same 脅かす was read おどかす (startle).
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–37)
