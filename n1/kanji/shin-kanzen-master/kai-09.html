Notes
The テスト here is a single page worth 20 points. Nouns are mostly one kanji read with its kun-yomi, so the traps are look-alike kanji with the same radical: 網/綱/縄 (all 糸, all rope-ish), 鐘/鈴/鎖/鏡 (all 金), 姿/婆/茨/次, 器/品. Note also 柄, listed twice: え (handle, No. 11) and がら (pattern, No. 30).
/ = a second example, or related words the book adds (釣り鐘, 戸棚 / 本棚, 傷つく / 傷つける)
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–52); the book rules lines between related sub-groups (e.g. 1–3 things that ring, 4–7 ropes and chains)

Three ropes, one radical: 網 (あみ, net — 糸 + 罔), 綱 (つな, thick rope — 糸 + 岡), 縄 (なわ, straw rope — 糸 + 黽). 練習 I uses all three. And four 金 kanji: 鐘 (かね, big bell), 鈴 (すず, small bell), 鎖 (くさり, chain), 鏡 (かがみ, mirror). 杯 is さかずき as a noun but ハイ as a counter (一杯).
