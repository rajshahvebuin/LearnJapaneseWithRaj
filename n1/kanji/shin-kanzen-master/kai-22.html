Notes
Measuring and dividing words: shapes (直径・半径・側面・斜面・垂直), maps (緯度・経度), time (上旬・中旬・下旬・隔週・瞬間・西暦), lists (項目・箇条書き・括弧・選択肢・欄). Readings to watch: 有無 is うむ (有 = ウ, not ユウ); 対 is ツイ in 一対; 括弧 is かっこ and 一括 いっかつ (small ッ); 直径 ちょっけい; 寸法 すんぽう (ホ → ポ).
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–37); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level
