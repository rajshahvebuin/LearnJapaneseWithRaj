Notes
Hospital and body vocabulary. Many kanji here have a second, rarer on-yomi that is the whole point of the entry: 解熱 (げねつ), 小児科 (しょうにか), 寿命 (じゅみょう), 発作 (ほっさ), 下痢 (げり). Several words need two consecutive list kanji (脂肪, 妊娠, 睡眠, 麻酔), so the book prints the word once across both rows.
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–38); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level

The pairs come from this 回's list and テスト: the same kanji with its usual and its rare on-yomi (児・命・発・解), and same-sound words that differ only in kanji (腸/聴, 中傷/抽象).

Exam trap: Test II asks "which kanji has the same reading", so you must know which words use the rare reading. Learn them as a set: 発 = ほっ in 発作・発足 (and 発端), 下 = げ in 下痢・下車・下旬, 児 = に only in 小児科, 解 = げ in 解熱・解毒.
