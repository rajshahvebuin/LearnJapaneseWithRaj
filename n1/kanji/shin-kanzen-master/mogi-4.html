2 Quiz / Exercise Section

All 15 items from p.152, in book order, one point each. Answers are the official ones from 別冊 p.7; every option is translated so you can see why the distractors fail.

問題1 ＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から1つ選びなさい。(1点×13)

___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.
EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. · HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही उच्चारण 1–4 में से चुनिए। · GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4 માંથી પસંદ કરો.

Q1

時代がやがて彼の行為を【裁く】だろう。Jidai ga yagate kare no koui o [sabaku] darou.

Full sentence: 時代がやがて彼の行為を裁くだろう。Jidai ga yagate kare no koui o sabaku darou.
EN: In time, history will pass judgement on his actions.
HI: समय आख़िरकार उसके कर्मों का फ़ैसला करेगा।
GU: સમય આખરે તેનાં કર્મોનો ચુકાદો આપશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はばくhabaku(not a word)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)
2さばくsabakuto judgeफ़ैसला करनाચુકાદો આપવો
3あばくabaku(= 暴く, expose)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)
4たばくtabaku(not a word)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)

Why: 裁く = さばく (第5回); on-yomi サイ (裁判, 第13回). Don't confuse 裁 with 栽 (栽培) and 載 (掲載) — same right-hand part, same サイ.

Q2

一瞬の判断を【誤った】。Isshun no handan o [ayamatta].

Full sentence: 一瞬の判断を誤った。Isshun no handan o ayamatta.
EN: I made a wrong split-second decision.
HI: एक पल के फ़ैसले में मुझसे ग़लती हो गई।
GU: એક પળના નિર્ણયમાં મારી ભૂલ થઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おちいったochiitta(= 陥った, fell into)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)
2あやまったayamattagot it wrongग़लती कीભૂલ કરી
3まちがったmachigatta(= 間違った — written differently)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)
4あらたまったaratamatta(= 改まった, formal/renewed)(ग़लत)(ખોટું)

Why: 誤る = あやまる (第4回). 謝る (apologise, 第2回) is also あやまる — a same-sound pair. Option 3 has the right meaning but is 間違う.

Q14

就職が決まったといっても、3か月は（　）採用の身なんです。Shuushoku ga kimatta to itte mo, sankagetsu wa ( ) saiyou no mi nan desu.

Full sentence: 就職が決まったといっても、3か月は仮採用の身なんです。Shuushoku ga kimatta to itte mo, sankagetsu wa kari saiyou no mi nan desu.
EN: I may have got the job, but for three months I'm only on probation.
HI: नौकरी तो मिल गई, पर तीन महीने मैं परिवीक्षा पर हूँ।
GU: નોકરી તો મળી, પણ ત્રણ મહિના હું પ્રોબેશન પર છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1未minot yet (未採用 = not yet hired)अभी नहींહજી નહીં
2副fukuvice-, sub- (not with 採用)उप-ઉપ-
3仮kariprovisional (仮採用)अस्थायीકામચલાઉ
4本honreal, permanent (本採用 = after probation)स्थायीકાયમી

Why: 仮〜 (かり) = provisional: 仮採用, 仮免許, 仮の住まい. 本採用 is the opposite — the full hire after probation; it doesn't fit "3か月は〜の身".

Q15

仕事を通して（　）実現を図りたい。Shigoto o tooshite ( ) jitsugen o hakaritai.

Full sentence: 仕事を通して自己実現を図りたい。Shigoto o tooshite jiko jitsugen o hakaritai.
EN: I want to achieve self-fulfilment through my work.
HI: मैं अपने काम के ज़रिए आत्म-विकास करना चाहता हूँ।
GU: હું મારા કામ દ્વારા સ્વ-વિકાસ સાધવા માગું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1自分jibunoneself (everyday)ख़ुदપોતે
2自身jishinoneself (emphatic)स्वयंસ્વયં
3自立jiritsuindependenceआत्मनिर्भरताસ્વાવલંબન
