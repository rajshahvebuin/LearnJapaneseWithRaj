知識① — 音の濁り (Oto no nigori — sound voicing in compounds)

Source
新完全マスター漢字 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Kanji, スリーエーネットワーク) — printed p.50 (PDF page 53): a one-page column (dialogue + ルール box + クイズ1)
Points covered
ルール: 2nd word voiced in compoundsException: verb + verbクイズ1: 15 words
Answer key
クイズ answers: the 答え box printed upside-down at the foot of p.50 — official, including the book's ★ note on which words do not voice. The "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Japanese linguists call this 連濁（れんだく, "sequential voicing") — that term is not used in the book (added, not in book). It explains why 本＋棚 is ほんだな and 勉強＋部屋 is べんきょうべや. N1 漢字読み items often hinge on whether a compound voices or not.
＝ under a kana (shown here as bold) = the book's mark for "the sound changed here"
゛ (濁点, dakuten) = the voicing mark: か→が, さ→ざ, た→だ, は→ば

1 The Rule — 音の濁り

Q & A from the column (the column's opening dialogue)

p.50
Question

「本棚」を「ほんたな」と読まないのはなぜですか。"Hondana" o "hontana" to yomanai no wa naze desu ka.

EN: Why isn't 本棚 (bookshelf) read "hontana"?
HI: 本棚 (किताबों की शेल्फ़) को "होनताना" क्यों नहीं पढ़ते?
GU: 本棚 (પુસ્તકોની છાજલી)ને "હોનતાના" કેમ નથી વાંચતા?
Answer

それは、「本」と「棚」のような独立した言葉どうしがつながってできた複合語の場合、後の言葉に「゛」がついて音が濁ることがあるからです。Sore wa, "hon" to "tana" no you na dokuritsu shita kotoba doushi ga tsunagatte dekita fukugougo no baai, ato no kotoba ni "dakuten" ga tsuite oto ga nigoru koto ga aru kara desu.

EN: Because in a compound made by joining two independent words, like 本 (book) and 棚 (shelf), the second word can take a "゛" and its sound becomes voiced.
HI: क्योंकि जब 本 (किताब) और 棚 (शेल्फ़) जैसे दो स्वतंत्र शब्द जुड़कर समास बनाते हैं, तो दूसरे शब्द पर "゛" लगकर उसकी ध्वनि सघोष (濁音) हो सकती है।
GU: કારણ કે 本 (પુસ્તક) અને 棚 (છાજલી) જેવા બે સ્વતંત્ર શબ્દો જોડાઈને સમાસ બને ત્યારે બીજા શબ્દ પર "゛" લાગીને તેનો અવાજ ઘોષ (濁音) બની શકે છે.

ルール (ruuru — the rule box)

p.50
No.Rule (book)RomajiEnglishHindiGujarati
1複合語の場合、２番目の語の始めの音が濁ることがある。Fukugougo no baai, niban-me no go no hajime no oto ga nigoru koto ga aru.In a compound, the first sound of the second word may become voiced.समास में दूसरे शब्द की पहली ध्वनि सघोष हो सकती है।સમાસમાં બીજા શબ્દનો પ્રથમ અવાજ ઘોષ બની શકે છે.
2ただし、「訓読みの動詞＋訓読みの動詞→複合動詞」の場合は濁りにくい。Tadashi, "kun'yomi no doushi + kun'yomi no doushi → fukugou doushi" no baai wa nigorinikui.However, when two kun-yomi verbs join into a compound verb, voicing rarely happens.लेकिन जब दो कुन-योमि क्रियाएँ जुड़कर संयुक्त क्रिया बनती हैं, तब ध्वनि प्रायः सघोष नहीं होती।પરંતુ બે કુન-યોમિ ક્રિયાપદો જોડાઈને સંયુક્ત ક્રિયાપદ બને ત્યારે અવાજ ભાગ્યે જ ઘોષ બને છે.
ExampleWordPartsReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
column本棚本（ほん）＋棚（たな）ほんだなhondanabookshelfकिताबों की शेल्फ़પુસ્તકોની છાજલી

The book says 濁ることがある — "may" voice. It is a tendency, not a law: you still have to learn each word. A well-known extra limit (added, not in book): if the second word already contains a voiced sound, it usually does not voice again — e.g. 一人旅 is ひとりたび, not ひとりだび (Lyman's law).

★ Book's note

「役立つ、長引く、裏返す、区切る」は（名詞または形容詞＋動詞）なので濁る。「追い越す、引き返す、打ち消す」は（動詞＋動詞）なので濁らない。"Yakudatsu, nagabiku, uragaesu, kugiru" wa (meishi mata wa keiyoushi + doushi) na node nigoru. "Oikosu, hikikaesu, uchikesu" wa (doushi + doushi) na node nigoranai.

EN: 役立つ, 長引く, 裏返す and 区切る are (noun or adjective + verb), so they voice. 追い越す, 引き返す and 打ち消す are (verb + verb), so they do not.
HI: 役立つ, 長引く, 裏返す, 区切る (संज्ञा या विशेषण + क्रिया) हैं, इसलिए सघोष होते हैं। 追い越す, 引き返す, 打ち消す (क्रिया + क्रिया) हैं, इसलिए नहीं होते।
GU: 役立つ, 長引く, 裏返す, 区切る (નામ અથવા વિશેષણ + ક્રિયાપદ) છે, તેથી ઘોષ બને છે. 追い越す, 引き返す, 打ち消す (ક્રિયાપદ + ક્રિયાપદ) છે, તેથી નથી બનતા.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The pairs below come straight from クイズ1 and the ★ note: the same second element (返す, 引く, 切る) voices after a noun or adjective but not after a verb.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
裏返すuragaesuturn over裏 (noun) + 返す → voiced がシャツを裏返す
引き返すhikikaesuturn back引く (verb) + 返す → no voicing途中で引き返す
長引くnagabikudrag on長 (adj. stem) + 引く → voiced び会議が長引く
区切るkugirudivide, mark off区 (noun) + 切る → voiced ぎ文を区切る
打ち消すuchikesudeny打つ (verb) + 消す → no voicingうわさを打ち消す
本棚hondanabookshelfnoun + noun → voiced だ本棚に並べる
棚tanashelf (on its own)no voicing when it stands alone (第9回 No. 15)棚の上

The usage examples in the last column are ours (added, not in book).

Exam trap: look at the first element. If it is a verb stem (引き〜, 追い〜, 打ち〜), the second verb keeps its plain sound: ひきかえす, おいこす. If it is a noun or adjective (裏, 長, 区, 役), expect voicing: うらがえす, ながびく.
