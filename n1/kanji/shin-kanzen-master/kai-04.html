Notes
"動詞Bレベル" = N1-level word written with a kanji you already know. This 回 is full of second readings of easy kanji: 強いる (しいる), 生ける (いける), 老いる/老ける (おいる/ふける), 勝る (まさる), 交わる (まじわる), 映える (はえる). Two different ふける (更ける/老ける) sit next to each other, and Test IV pairs same-kanji verbs: 訪れる/訪ねる, 試み/試す, 勝る/勝つ, 老いる/老ける, 交わる/交わす, 映える/映る.
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–40)
