Notes
All 56 words are nouns read with kun-yomi (native Japanese readings) — 峠, 岬, 滝, 霧, 稲, 溝 and so on are everyday Japanese words, but their kanji are N1. The traps are on-yomi guesses that look reasonable but are wrong (津波 is つなみ, not しんぱ; 粗筋 is あらすじ) and near-synonyms with different kanji: 沼/池/湖, 溝/堀, 縁/枠, 稲/穂/種, 汁/液. This 回's テスト is the short kind: 20 points, sections I–II.
/ = a second example, or a related word with the same reading shown with its furigana (e.g. 浜辺 / 浜, 梅 / 梅干し)
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–56)

Weather words come in pairs worth separating: 霜 (しも, frost — ice on the ground) vs 露 (つゆ, dew — water drops), 雷 (かみなり, thunder/lightning in general) vs 稲光 (いなびかり, the flash itself). 滴 (しずく) is a single drop. 潮 (しお, tide) sounds like 塩 (しお, salt) (added, not in book). 津波 is a kun reading (つ + なみ) — the テスト offers しんぱ/しんは as traps.
