尊い and 貴い are both とうとい (also たっとい): 尊い leans to sacred, worthy of respect (尊い命, 尊い教え), 貴い to of high value (貴い資料, 貴い体験) — in practice they overlap. 堅い (firm in character, 口が堅い) is not 固い (hard, fixed) or 硬い (hard material). 危うい adverb 危うく = "nearly" is tested in テスト IV①.
