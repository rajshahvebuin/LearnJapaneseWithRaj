Notes
Many kanji here are N2 kanji (marked *) whose on-yomi is new: 雨 → 雨天 (うてん), 夏 → 夏季 (かき), 石 → 磁石 (じしゃく), 冬 → 冬眠 (とうみん), 腹 → 山腹 (さんぷく), 川 → 河川 (かせん). Watch the special reading 梅雨 (ばいう, also つゆ) and look-alikes in the テスト: 胞 / 抱 / 砲, 漠 / 莫 / 爆 / 暴, 衛星 / 衛生.
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–47); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets)
Blank compound cell = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level
