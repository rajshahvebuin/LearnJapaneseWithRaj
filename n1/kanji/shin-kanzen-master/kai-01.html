Notes
"動詞Aレベル" = the word is everyday (N2-or-below) vocabulary but its kanji is N1 — so you already know 酔う・脱ぐ・踏む by sound; the job is to read and pick the right kanji. The exam traps here are homophones with different kanji: 撃つ/討つ (うつ), 吐く/履く (はく), 遭う/会う (あう), 渇く/乾く (かわく), 挿す/指す (さす), 澄む/済む/住む (すむ).
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–45)

遭う (あう) is "meet with" something bad — an accident, theft, rain, a disaster. Meeting a person is 会う. Test III① tests exactly this. Also note 従う (したがう) vs 伴う (ともなう): 従う = you follow someone/obey; 伴う = you take someone along, or a thing "comes with" (danger, pain).

Two はく on one page: 吐く (vomit / spit out words, 口 radical = mouth) and 履く (put on shoes/trousers, 尸 radical). And 渇く (のどが渇く — thirst, 氵 water radical) is not 乾く (洗濯物が乾く — things drying). 聴く is "listen attentively" (music, lectures), narrower than 聞く.
