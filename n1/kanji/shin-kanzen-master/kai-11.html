Notes
The last kun-yomi noun lesson. Many words here are kanji you know with an unexpected native reading: 上 = かみ, 下 = しも, 音 = ね, 市 = いち, 公 = おおやけ, 的 = まと, 値 = あたい, 問屋 = とんや.
