Notes
The words of money and business: 賃金, 預金, 融資, 負債, 需要, 倹約. The traps are readings that change at the join — 月賦 (げっぷ), 合併 (がっぺい), 合致 (がっち), 欠陥 (けっかん), 融通 (ゆうずう, not ゆうつう), 控除 (こうじょ) — and look-alike kanji: 倹 / 険 / 検 / 剣, 歴 / 暦, 盤 / 板, 蓄 / 畜.
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–29); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets)
Blank compound cell = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level
