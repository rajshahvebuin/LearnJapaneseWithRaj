Notes
Computer words (添付, 挿入, 媒体, 履歴) and the arts (演奏, 楽譜, 彫刻, 戯曲, 漫画). The traps: 情緒 (じょうちょ, also じょうしょ), 雰囲気 (ふんいき, not ふいんき), 力士 (りきし), 色彩 (しきさい), 添付 (てんぷ), 拍手 (はくしゅ), and look-alikes 監 / 鑑, 督 / 得, 媒 / 謀, 脚 / 却. Note: 容量, 圧縮 and 削除 in 練習 I are not in this 回's list — the book uses them as context words.
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–30); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets)
Blank compound cell = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level
