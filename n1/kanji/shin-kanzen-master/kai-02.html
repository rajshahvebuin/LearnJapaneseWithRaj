Notes
"動詞Aレベル" = the word is everyday (N2-or-below) vocabulary but its kanji is N1. Every verb in this 回 ends in 〜る. The traps are homophones with different kanji: 掘る/彫る (ほる), 撮る/取る (とる), 殖える/増える (ふえる), 載せる/乗せる (のせる), 診る/見る (みる), 謝る/誤る (あやまる), 攻める/責める (せめる), 尋ねる/訪ねる (たずねる) — and look-alike 偏/遍/編 in Test I⑦.
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–56)

Two ほる on one page: 掘る (dig a hole, 扌 hand radical — you dig with your hands/tools) and 彫る (carve or engrave, 彡 "decoration strokes" radical). 撮る is only for photos and filming; 取る is the general "take". 刈る (cut grass/hair) is not 狩る (hunt) (added, not in book).
