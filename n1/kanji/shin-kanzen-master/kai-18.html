Notes
Two lists: kanji that mostly make な-adjectives (平凡な, 頻繁な, 悲惨な) and kanji that mostly make adverbs (至急, 一斉に, 徐々に, 悠々と). The traps are unexpected readings — 肝心 (かんじん), 繁盛 (はんじょう, not はんせい), 懸念 (けねん, not けんねん), 一切 (いっさい), 若干 (じゃっかん) — and pairs of look-alike or same-sound kanji: 粗 / 祖, 徐 / 除, 剰 / 乗, and 滑 / 活 / 渇 (all カツ).
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–43); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level
