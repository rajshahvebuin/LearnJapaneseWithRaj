Notes
These are 熟字訓 and other special readings: the whole word has a fixed reading that cannot be built from the on- or kun-yomi of its kanji (今日 = きょう, not こんにち/いまひ). The only way is to memorise each word. The テスト is a single page worth 15 points; note that the book prints "1点×8" for section I although it has 9 items, and "1点×7" for section II although it has 6 — the total of 15 holds either way.
No. = the book's running number for this 回's word list (1–21); the book rules lines between related groups (1–5, 6–8, 9–11, 12, 13–14, 15–20, 21) — the group names on this page are ours
/ = two spellings with the same reading (叔父／伯父), as the book prints them
Readings are for the whole word — do not split them kanji by kanji

特別な読み方とは (Tokubetsu na yomikata to wa — what is a special reading?)

p.144 · intro box

漢字の言葉の中には、30回までで勉強した読み方ではなく、特別な読み方をするものがあります。漢字一つ一つの「音」や「訓」ではなく、言葉全体で決まった読み方をします。Kanji no kotoba no naka ni wa, sanjikkai made de benkyou shita yomikata de wa naku, tokubetsu na yomikata o suru mono ga arimasu. Kanji hitotsu hitotsu no "on" ya "kun" de wa naku, kotoba zentai de kimatta yomikata o shimasu.

EN: Some kanji words are not read with the readings studied up to lesson 30 but with a special reading. The reading is fixed for the word as a whole, not taken from each kanji's on- or kun-yomi.
HI: कुछ कांजी शब्द पाठ 30 तक पढ़े उच्चारणों से नहीं, बल्कि विशेष उच्चारण से पढ़े जाते हैं। यह उच्चारण हर कांजी के ओन या कुन से नहीं, पूरे शब्द के लिए तय होता है।
GU: કેટલાક કાંજી શબ્દો પાઠ 30 સુધી ભણેલાં વાંચનથી નહીં, પણ વિશેષ વાંચનથી વંચાય છે. આ વાંચન દરેક કાંજીના ઓન કે કુનથી નહીં, આખા શબ્દ માટે નક્કી હોય છે.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

Two practice sets — 練習 I on p.144 (7 readings + matching) and 練習 II on p.145 (match 5 words to pictures, with readings) — then the one-page 15-point テスト on p.146 (sections I–II). The book's illustrations are not reproduced; each is described in one line in our own words. Practice answers are from the key printed upside-down at the foot of p.144; test answers from 別冊 p.6–7.
