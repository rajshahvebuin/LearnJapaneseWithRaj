Notes
Two themes: 産業 (farming and resources — 収穫・耕地・穀物・酪農・牧畜・埋蔵・採掘) and 交通 (transport — 新幹線・街道・軌道・船舶・運搬・操縦・墜落). Readings to watch: 街 is ガイ (市街・街頭) but カイ in 街道; 旅客 is りょかく (りょきゃく also accepted); 運搬 うんぱん; 船舶 せんぱく; 出荷 しゅっか; 採掘 さいくつ. The book numbers 街 and 客 twice (two on-yomi rows) — kept as one entry each here.
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–37); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level

練習 I (p.124) — グラフを見て、2人が話しています。＿＿の部分の漢字の読み方を書きなさい。

EN: Two people are talking while looking at graphs. Write the readings of the underlined kanji. · HI: दो लोग ग्राफ़ देखकर बात कर रहे हैं। रेखांकित कांजी का उच्चारण लिखिए। · GU: બે જણ ગ્રાફ જોઈને વાત કરે છે. રેખાંકિત કાંજીનું વાંચન લખો.

Key: ①まいぞうりょう ②さいくつ ③こくもつ ④じきゅうりつ ⑤しゅうかく ⑥こうち ⑦ぼくちく.
