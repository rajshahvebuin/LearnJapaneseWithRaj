Notes
First lesson of 第2部: now the list gives each kanji with its on-yomi and the N1 compounds built from it, mostly nouns that become verbs with する (援助する, 撮影する, 分析する). Watch the kanji with two on-yomi — 献 ケン/コン (文献/献立), 納 ノウ/ナッ (納入/納得) — and the small っ/ゃ details the テスト loves: 克服 こくふく (not こっぷく), 活躍 かつやく, 屈折 くっせつ, 恐縮 きょうしゅく.
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book adds when you met it in 第1部 or N2 (the bold part in the book is okurigana)
* N2 = the book marks this kanji with ＊ as N2-level (here only 迎); all others are N1 kanji
No. = the book's item number (1–31). 歓迎 and 矛盾 are printed once across two rows (3–4, 29–30); we repeat them on both

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 I (matching, 4 items), 練習 II (word grid, 5 items), the ちょっと一休み quiz (p.62) and the 30-point テスト (p.64–65: sections I–V). All answers are from the book's keys (foot of p.63, the 一休み box, and 別冊 p.4).

練習 I (p.62) — ①〜④の読み方を書きなさい。また、右の＿＿部分と意味が最も近いものを選んで、線で結びなさい。

1–4 no yomikata o kakinasai. Mata, migi no ___ bubun to imi ga mottomo chikai mono o erande, sen de musubinasai.
EN: Write the readings of ①–④, then join each to the right-hand phrase whose underlined part is closest in meaning. · HI: ①–④ के उच्चारण लिखिए, फिर हर एक को दाईं ओर के उस वाक्यांश से जोड़िए जिसका रेखांकित भाग अर्थ में सबसे निकट है। · GU: ①–④ નાં વાંચન લખો, પછી દરેકને જમણી બાજુના એ વાક્યાંશ સાથે જોડો જેનો રેખાંકિત ભાગ અર્થમાં સૌથી નજીક છે.
The words come from a notice board, みんなの掲示板 ("everyone's notice board"): 7月1日より循環バスの時間が変更になります (the loop-bus timetable changes from 1 July) · 市民税の納入は6月30日までに (pay city tax by 30 June) · 写真撮影会 A公園で 6月20日 (a photo session in Park A on 20 June). Right side: a 税金をおさめる · b いい景色を写真にとる · c お知らせを皆に見える所に書いてしめす · d このバスは市内をまわる · e 予定をかえる. Book's example: 変更する（へんこう）— e.

From here the book tests on-yomi: same-reading kanji that must be told apart, and kanji with two readings. Every pair below comes from the list or the テスト (成功, 交互, 一致, 治安, 貢献, 体験 and こっぷく are the テスト's own options).

Exam trap: small っ. 屈折 くっせつ and 一致 いっち double the consonant, but 克服 こくふく and 活躍 かつやく do not — the テスト offers こっぷく and かっやく as wrong answers. Rule of thumb: く/つ before a k-, s-, t- or p- sound often becomes っ; before ふ the く usually stays, and っ is never written before や/ゆ/よ.
