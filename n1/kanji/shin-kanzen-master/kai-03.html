Notes
"動詞Bレベル" = the word itself is N1 vocabulary but its kanji is one you met by N2 (負・競・説・記・保・産・歩…). So the kanji look easy — the trap is the unexpected kun-yomi: 説く (とく, not せつ), 著す (あらわす), 記す (しるす), 生かす (いかす) vs 生やす (はやす) vs 生 (なま). One kanji, several verbs: 負う/負ける, 危ない/危ぶむ, 歩く/歩む, 逃げる/逃す, 断つ/断る — Test IV is built on exactly these.
(自) = the paired intransitive verb (自動詞); (他) = the paired transitive verb (他動詞) — the book lists the partner after the example
/ = a second example showing another use of the same word
No. = the book's running item number for this 回 (1–40)
