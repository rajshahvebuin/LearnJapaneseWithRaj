Notes
The 練習 pair each formal する-noun with the everyday verb that shares its kanji — 兼用する = かねる, 譲歩する = ゆずる, 燃焼する = もえる, 沈黙する = だまる, 勧誘する = さそう/すすめる. That kun-yomi link is the fastest way to remember the meaning. Watch the sound changes: 錯覚 (さっかく), 実践 (じっせん), 没収 (ぼっしゅう), and the long/short traps 誇張 (こちょう, not こうちょう) and 寄贈 (きぞう).
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–26); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level

練習 I (p.76) — ＿＿の部分と意味が最も近い言葉を右から選んで、線で結びなさい。＿＿の部分の読み方も書きなさい。
