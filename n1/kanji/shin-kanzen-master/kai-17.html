Notes
Character words: 練習 I is "plus-image" adjectives (優秀・謙虚・誠実・慎重・勇敢・賢明), 練習 II "minus-image" ones (頑固・陰気・鈍感・冷淡・怠慢・貧乏). Readings to watch: 貧 is ヒン in 貧血・貧困・貧弱 but ビン in 貧乏; 丁 is チョウ (包丁) or テイ (丁寧); 吉 is キツ in 不吉; 厄介 is やっかい (small ッ).
No. = the book's running kanji number for this 回 (1–33); * after a kanji = the book marks it as an N2-level kanji (its new N1 reading/word is what is taught here)
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book lists (okurigana in brackets, e.g. にぎ(る))
Blank row = the book leaves the word cell empty because that reading was studied earlier; — = a reading the book does not teach at this level

貧 has two on-yomi: ヒン (貧血, 貧困, 貧弱) and ビン (only in 貧乏). Test IV① puts 貧乏 and 貧弱 in the same sentence. 冷淡 (れいたん) and 冷酷 (れいこく) are both "cold" — 冷淡 = indifferent, 冷酷 = cruel. 頑丈 (がんじょう, sturdy) vs 丈夫 (じょうぶ, healthy/sturdy) share 丈.
