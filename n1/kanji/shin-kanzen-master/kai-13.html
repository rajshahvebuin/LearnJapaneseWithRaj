Notes
These 28 kanji each build a whole family of compounds (視 alone gives 視覚・視察・視点・視野・近視・重視・無視). Learn the family, then watch the kanji with two on-yomi: 興 コウ/キョウ (興奮/余興), 拠 キョ/コ (根拠/証拠), 素 ソ/ス (素材/素晴らしい), 模 モ/ボ (模型/規模). The テスト (30 points, I–V) also tests the small っ (密接 みっせつ) and same-sound kanji (貴重→尊重, 良識→善良).
On / kun: katakana = on-yomi; "kun:" = the kun-yomi the book adds when you met it in 第1部 or N2
* N2 = the book marks this kanji with ＊ as N2-level (根, 守, 良); all others are N1 kanji
No. = the book's item number (1–28); when a kanji has two on-yomi, each gets its own block of rows

AnswerReadingCompleted sentenceEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A kanji that makes many words often has two on-yomi, and the テスト puts both readings in one sentence (Test V② 興奮/余興, V③ 根拠/証拠, V④ 素材/素晴らしい). The pairs below come from the list and the テスト.

拠（キョ）/ 拠（コ）kyo / kogrounds / evidenceコ only in 証拠根拠がない, 証拠を出す

Exam trap: one kanji, two readings in one sentence. When the same kanji appears twice in a reading question, do not copy the first reading: 根拠 こんきょ but 証拠 しょうこ; 興奮 こうふん but 余興 よきょう; 素材 そざい but 素晴らしい すばらしい; 模型 もけい but 規模 きぼ.
