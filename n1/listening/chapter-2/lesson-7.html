1 Test Overview

Key idea

This lesson is the chapter's own review test — a shortened mock exam covering all five 聴解 question types taught in Lessons 1–6, in the same five-block structure (問題I〜V) and scoring weights as the real N1 exam:

BlockQuestion typeItemsPoints
問題I即時応答 (Lesson 1)55点×5 = 25
問題II課題理解 (Lesson 2)25点×2 = 10
問題IIIポイント理解 (Lesson 3)25点×2 = 10
問題IV概要理解 (Lesson 4)210点×2 = 20
問題V統合理解 (Lessons 5–6)1番 + 2番(2質問)15 + 10点×2 = 35

Total: 100点, printed time limit 16分. Try to work through all five blocks in one sitting, exactly as you'll face them on exam day.

2 問題I — 即時応答 (5点×5、答え p.98)

Nothing is printed on the sheet — listen to the line, then to the three replies, and choose 1〜3.

1番 CD1, Track 41

お祝いをこんなにいただいて…恐れ入ります。Oiwai o konna ni itadaite... osoreirimasu.

EN: "To receive such a wonderful gift for the occasion... I'm much obliged."   HI: इस अवसर के लिए इतना बड़ा उपहार पाकर... मैं अभिभूत हूँ।   GU: આ પ્રસંગ માટે આટલી મોટી ભેટ મળવાથી... હું આભારી છું.

#ReplyMeaning
1いいえー、お気を使わせてしまいました。Iie, o-ki o tsukawasete shimaimashita."No no, I'm sorry to have made you go to the trouble of worrying about it."
2いいえー、ありがたくいただいておきます。Iie, arigataku itadaite okimasu."No no, I'll gratefully accept it." (duplicates the function the first speaker already performed)
3いいえー、遅くなりまして申し訳ありませんでした。Iie, osoku narimashite moushiwake arimasen deshita."No no, I'm sorry it ended up being late." — the giver's humble deflection, acknowledging the gift arrived later than it should have

Why: The over-the-top thanks in the opening line implies there's something to apologize for beyond the gift itself — the correct reply owns up to it being late, deflecting the profuse gratitude with an apology rather than simply re-accepting the gift a second time.

2番 CD1, Track 42

悪いけど、手、貸してくれる？Warui kedo, te, kashite kureru?

EN: "Sorry, but could you lend me a hand?"   HI: माफ़ करना, पर क्या तुम मेरी मदद कर सकते हो?   GU: માફ કરજો, પણ શું તમે મને મદદ કરી શકો?

#ReplyMeaning
1いや、都合悪ければ、だめだと言うよ。Iya, tsugou warukereba, dame da to iu yo."No, if it's inconvenient I'll say so." (doesn't actually answer the request)
2いいけど、今すぐでないとだめ？Ii kedo, ima sugu de nai to dame?"Sure, but does it have to be right now?" — accepts, while naturally clarifying the timing
3えっと、できないとは言ってないけど。Etto, dekinai to wa itte nai kedo."Um, I didn't say I couldn't." (evasive — never actually agrees or asks anything)

Why: A direct request calls for a direct (even if conditional) acceptance. Option 2 both agrees and negotiates timing, which is exactly how this kind of casual request is normally answered.

3番 CD1, Track 43

目上の者に向かって、そんな言葉遣いするもんじゃない。Meue no mono ni mukatte, sonna kotobazukai suru mon ja nai.

EN: "You shouldn't speak that way to someone senior to you."   HI: बड़ों से इस तरह बात नहीं करनी चाहिए।   GU: વડીલો સાથે આ રીતે વાત ન કરવી જોઈએ.

#ReplyMeaning
1はい、今後気をつけるようにします。Hai, kongo ki o tsukeru you ni shimasu."Yes, I'll be careful from now on." — the expected, appropriately humble response to being corrected
2意味がわからないものもありますが。Imi ga wakaranai mono mo arimasu ga."There are some things whose meaning I don't understand, though." (non sequitur)
3え？そんなふうに思ってるんですか。E? Sonna fuu ni omotteru n desu ka."Huh? Is that how you see it?" (argues back rather than accepting the correction)

Why: Being reprimanded for poor speech habits toward a superior calls for a humble acknowledgment, not a rebuttal — 1 fits; 3 sounds combative, and 2 doesn't respond to the reprimand at all.

4番 CD1, Track 44

こんなところにゴミ捨てるなんて、いったい、どういうつもりだろう。Konna tokoro ni gomi suteru nante, ittai, dou iu tsumori darou.

EN: "Dumping trash in a place like this — what on earth are they thinking?"   HI: इस जगह पर कूड़ा फेंकना... आख़िर वे क्या सोच रहे थे?   GU: આવી જગ્યાએ કચરો ફેંકવો... છેવટે તેઓ શું વિચારતા હતા?

#ReplyMeaning
1どうせわかってもらえないよね。Douse wakatte moraenai yo ne."They wouldn't understand anyway." (misdirected — not about the litterer's mindset)
2まったく！拾ってくれればいいのにね。Mattaku! Hirotte kurereba ii noni ne."Honestly! If only they'd pick it up." (talks about picking up litter, not littering itself)
3人のことなんか、なんにも考えてないんでしょ。Hito no koto nanka, nanni mo kangaetenai n desho."They're probably not thinking about other people at all, are they." — directly agrees with and elaborates on the complaint

Why: The opening line is a rhetorical complaint about a litterer's inconsiderate mindset — option 3 is the only reply that actually engages with that same judgment about the litterer's thoughtlessness.

5番 CD1, Track 45

お手伝いできればよかったのですが、申し訳ありませんでした。Otetsudai dekireba yokatta no desu ga, moushiwake arimasen deshita.

EN: "I wish I could have helped — I'm terribly sorry."   HI: काश मैं मदद कर पाता... मुझे बहुत खेद है।   GU: કાશ હું મદદ કરી શકત... મને ખૂબ દુઃખ છે.

#ReplyMeaning
1いえいえ、お忙しかったでしょうから。Ie ie, oisogashikatta deshou kara."No no, you must have been busy." — a gracious reply that lets the apologizer off the hook
2えーっと、お願いしてもいいですか。Eetto, onegai shite mo ii desu ka."Um, could I ask you a favor?" (ignores the apology entirely)
3あー、そんなー大丈夫でしょう。Aa, sonnaa daijoubu deshou."Ah, no need, it's probably fine." (too vague/dismissive to be the best fit)

Why: An apology for not being able to help calls for a gracious reassurance — option 1 explicitly excuses them ("you were busy"), the most natural and complete reply.

#OptionMeaning
17千円Nana-sen enIgnores the weekend surcharge entirely
28千円Has-sen enThe room rental alone (4 hrs × 2000円), forgetting the piano fee

2番 CD1, Track 47

電話で、女の人が電気店の人と話しています。女の人はどうすることにしますか。Denwa de, onna no hito ga denkiten no hito to hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa dou suru koto ni shimasu ka.

EN: A woman is talking to an appliance store employee over the phone. 1番 CD1, Track 48

評論家が、ある画家について話しています。評論家は、この画家の作品の最も優れているところはどこだと言っていますか。Hyouronka ga, aru gaka ni tsuite hanashite imasu. Hyouronka wa, kono gaka no sakuhin no mottomo sugurete iru tokoro wa doko da to itte imasu ka.

EN: A critic is talking about a painter. Where does the critic say this painter's work most excels?   HI: एक समीक्षक एक चित्रकार के बारे में बात कर रहा है। समीक्षक के अनुसार इस चित्रकार का काम सबसे बेहतर कहाँ है?   GU: એક વિવેચક એક ચિત્રકાર વિશે વાત કરી રહ્યા છે. વિવેચકના મતે આ ચિત્રકારનું કામ સૌથી શ્રેષ્ઠ ક્યાં છે?

女: 彼の作品は、構図と色使いが特徴的です。構図には、数学的に計算された比率を使うなど、大変高度なテクニックを使っています。色は、特に青色の使い方が独創的です。しかしなんと言っても他に類を見ないのは、光の表現方法です。陽に当たって影のできたような何気ない景色にも、細かいこだわりを持って、その陰影を表現しています。この表現力によって、また青色がとても鮮やかに映えています。Kare no sakuhin wa, kouzu to irozukai ga tokuchouteki desu. Kouzu ni wa, suugakuteki ni keisan sareta hiritsu o tsukau nado, taihen koudo na tekunikku o tsukatte imasu. Iro wa, toku ni aoiro no tsukaikata ga dokusouteki desu. EN translation: "His work is marked by its composition and color use — highly advanced techniques like mathematically calculated ratios in the composition, and a highly original use of blue. 2番 CD1, Track 49

1番 CD1, Track 50

ラジオで、女の人が話しています。Rajio de, onna no hito ga hanashite imasu.

EN: A woman is speaking on the radio.   HI: रेडियो पर एक महिला बात कर रही है।   GU: રેડિયો પર એક સ્ત્રી વાત કરી રહી છે.

7 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes — Chapter 2 Recap

This test drew on all five question types taught in this chapter. Here's the one-line reminder for each, worth reviewing before moving on to Chapter 3:

TypeCore skill#1 trap to avoid
即時応答
Sokuji outou		Judge a reply's function, instantly, from one line.Don't be swayed by a reply that recycles a keyword from the prompt.
課題理解
Kadai rikai		Track what's already done vs. what's still to do.Don't pick a real action that's scheduled for later or already finished.
ポイント理解
Pointo rikai		Predict from the question stem, then filter for one fact.Don't answer with a true-but-irrelevant detail the stem didn't ask for.
概要理解
Gaiyou rikai		Judge the whole monologue's throughline; no re-listen.Don't mistake a supporting example/statistic for the overall theme.
統合理解
Tougou rikai		Follow multi-way comparisons or multi-speaker talks to their final line.Don't lock in an answer before the conversation actually resolves.
Exam trap, chapter-wide: Every single wrong answer across all 14 items in this test was a real word, fact, or opinion that actually appeared somewhere in the audio — never an invented distractor. N1 聴解 tests whether you can tell which true thing answers the question, not whether you can spot something false.
