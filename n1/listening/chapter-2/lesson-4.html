1 Question Type: 概要理解 — Overview Comprehension

Key idea

キーワードを見つけよう！Kiiwaado o mitsukeyou! — "Find the keyword!"

Nothing at all is printed on the question sheet this time — not even the options. You hear a single person talk for roughly 30–40 seconds (a TV commentator, a teacher, a doctor, an announcer), and only after the monologue ends do you hear the question and the four spoken options. There's no second listen. You have to judge the overall topic/theme from the whole talk, so listen for the words that get repeated or that the speaker circles back to — that repetition is usually the real keyword.

  1. ① 聞説明 — Listen to the setup (who's speaking, where).
  2. ② 聞一段話 — Listen to the entire monologue, once, all the way through.
  3. ③ 聞提問 — Only now do you hear the question.
  4. ④ 選択答案 — Choose from the spoken options.

Judge the whole talk's content as a single unit. The question is heard only once, and it's almost always a single speaker.

2 Worked Example — 例をやってみよう

例 CD1, Track 32

テレビで女の人が話しています。Terebi de onna no hito ga hanashite imasu.

EN: A woman is speaking on TV. (Listen to the whole talk before the question is even asked — pay attention to the second half especially.)   HI: टीवी पर एक महिला बात कर रही है। (सवाल पूछे जाने से पहले पूरी बात सुनें — खासकर दूसरे भाग पर ध्यान दें।)   GU: ટીવી પર એક સ્ત્રી વાત કરી રહી છે. (પ્રશ્ન પુછાય તે પહેલાં આખી વાત સાંભળો — ખાસ કરીને બીજા ભાગ પર ધ્યાન આપો.)

女: ロシアなどでは、ウォッカなどの強いお酒が好んで飲まれます。これは、寒い地方で体を温めるために飲むということもありますが、実は他にも理由があるようです。あまり強くないお酒だと、寒い戸外に置いておくと凍ってしまうのです。アルコールは度数が高くなると、冷凍しようとしても凍りません。その性質を活かして、車の不凍液などにもアルコールが入っていたりするんですが、ロシアなどでは、戸外の温度が冷凍庫より低いということもあります。そのせいで、凍らない強いお酒が普及したと言えるのではないでしょうか。Roshia nado dewa, wokka nado no tsuyoi osake ga kononde nomaremasu. Kore wa, samui chihou de karada o atatameru tame ni nomu to iu koto mo arimasu ga, jitsu wa hoka ni mo riyuu ga aru you desu. Amari tsuyokunai osake da to, samui kogai ni oite oku to kootte shimau no desu. Arukooru wa dosuu ga takaku naru to, reitou shiyou to shite mo koorimasen. Sono seishitsu o ikashite, kuruma no futoueki nado ni mo arukooru ga haitte itari suru n desu ga, Roshia nado dewa, kogai no ondo ga reitouko yori hikui to iu koto mo arimasu. Sono sei de, koranai tsuyoi osake ga fukyuu shita to ieru no de wa nai deshou ka.

EN translation: "In places like Russia, people favor strong liquors like vodka. This is partly to warm the body in cold regions, but it seems there's actually another reason. A weaker drink will freeze if left outdoors in the cold — but once alcohol's proof gets high enough, it won't freeze even if you try. That property is even used in car antifreeze, and in Russia the outdoor temperature can get lower than inside a freezer. That's arguably why strong, non-freezing liquor became so widespread there."

女の人は何について話していますか。Onna no hito wa nani ni tsuite hanashite imasu ka.

#OptionMeaning
1ロシア人が酒に強いことについてRoshiajin ga sake ni tsuyoi koto ni tsuiteAbout Russians being able to hold their liquor — not what she said (about the drink's chemistry/climate, not people's tolerance)
2ロシアで強い酒が飲まれる理由についてRoshia de tsuyoi sake ga nomareru riyuu ni tsuiteAbout the reason strong liquor is drunk in Russia — matches the whole talk's throughline
3アルコールの凍り方についてArukooru no kōrikata ni tsuiteAbout how alcohol freezes — only one supporting detail, not the overall topic
4アルコールと気温の関係についてArukooru to kion no kankei ni tsuiteAbout the relationship between alcohol and temperature — also just a supporting detail

Why: Options 3 and 4 each grab one true supporting fact from the middle of the talk, but the talk opens and closes on the same throughline: why strong liquor is common in Russia. Keywords to track: 「ロシア」「酒」「凍る」「アルコール」— all four appear, but only option 2 ties them into the actual overall reason being explained.

Glossary: 戸外 kogai — "outdoors"; アルコールの度数 arukooru no dosuu — "alcohol content/proof"; 車の不凍液 kuruma no futoueki — "car anti-freeze."

1番 CD1, Track 33

テレビで医者が話しています。Terebi de isha ga hanashite imasu.

EN: A doctor is speaking on TV.   HI: टीवी पर एक डॉक्टर बात कर रहा है।   GU: ટીવી પર એક ડોક્ટર વાત કરી રહ્યા છે.

男: 以前は私も、風邪をひいて熱のある患者さんには、必ず解熱剤を処方していました。熱が下がると体のだるさも取れ、一見治ったような気がしますが、実は薬が切れると、かえって前より熱が高くなったり、場合によっては、命に関わることもあるのです。最近の研究では、人は体温が39度になると免疫細胞が通常の20倍になり、また40度になると、ウイルスと闘うインターフェロンの働きが3倍になるという報告もあります。熱が出ても安易に薬で下げないで、自己免疫力で回復させるようにすることが大切なんですよね。Izen wa watashi mo, kaze o hiite netsu no aru kanja-san ni wa, kanarazu genetsuzai o shohou shite imashita. Netsu ga sagaru to karada no darusa mo tore, ikken naotta you na ki ga shimasu ga, jitsu wa kusuri ga kireru to, kaette mae yori netsu ga takaku nattari, baai ni yotte wa, inochi ni kakawaru koto mo aru no desu. Saikin no kenkyuu de wa, hito wa taion ga sanjuukyuu-do ni naru to men'eki saibou ga tsuujou no nijuu-bai ni nari, mata yonjuu-do ni naru to, uirusu to tatakau intaafuron no hataraki ga san-bai ni naru to iu houkoku mo arimasu. Netsu ga dete mo an'i ni kusuri de sagenaide, jiko men'ekiryoku de kaifuku saseru you ni suru koto ga taisetsu nan desu yo ne.

EN translation: "I used to always prescribe fever-reducers to feverish cold patients. Once the fever drops, the sluggishness goes too, so it looks like they've recovered — but actually, once the medicine wears off, the fever often rebounds even higher, and in some cases it can even become life-threatening. Recent research reports that at 39°C body temperature, immune cells rise to 20 times normal, and at 40°C, the virus-fighting interferon's activity triples. So even with a fever, rather than casually bringing it down with medicine, it's important to let your own immune power bring about recovery."

医者はどのようなテーマで話をしていますか。Isha wa dono you na teema de hanashi o shite imasu ka.

#OptionMeaning
1解熱剤の即効性Genetsuzai no sokkouseiThe immediate effectiveness of fever-reducers — a fact mentioned, not the overall theme
2インターフェロンの働きIntaafuron no hatarakiThe function of interferon — one supporting statistic, not the theme
3薬に頼らずに治すことの重要性Kusuri ni tayorazu ni naosu koto no juuyouseiThe importance of healing without relying on medicine — his closing conclusion, and the throughline of the whole talk
4免疫細胞の数Men'eki saibou no kazuThe number of immune cells — a supporting statistic only

Why: The immune-cell and interferon statistics (options 2, 4) are evidence he cites, not his overall point. His talk opens by saying fever-reducers can backfire, and closes with the explicit conclusion "自己免疫力で回復させるようにすることが大切" — that's the theme, matching 3.

2番 CD1, Track 34

大学の授業で先生が話しています。Daigaku no jugyou de sensei ga hanashite imasu.

EN: A professor is speaking during a university class.   HI: विश्वविद्यालय की कक्षा में एक प्रोफेसर बात कर रहे हैं।   GU: યુનિવર્સિટીના વર્ગમાં એક પ્રોફેસર વાત કરી રહ્યા છે.

女: えー、今日は、授業の初日ということなので、今学期の授業内容について、簡単に説明します。この授業では、地域活性化について考えていきます。過去における成功例、失敗例を参考にしながら、地域活性化のために新しく考えられることについて、意見を出し合ってもらいます。地域を活性化させるには、その地域の経済活性化が必要ですが、それは行政に任せるとして、ここでは、地域の人々の心が豊かになるようなアイデアを中心として、活発な議論を展開していきたいと思っています。Ee, kyou wa, jugyou no shonichi to iu koto na node, kongakki no jugyou naiyou ni tsuite, kantan ni setsumei shimasu. Kono jugyou dewa, chiiki kasseika ni tsuite kangaete ikimasu. Kako ni okeru seikourei, shippairei o sankou ni shinagara, chiiki kasseika no tame ni atarashiku kangaerareru koto ni tsuite, iken o dashiatte moraimasu. Chiiki o kasseika saseru ni wa, sono chiiki no keizai kasseika ga hitsuyou desu ga, sore wa gyousei ni makaseru to shite, koko dewa, chiiki no hitobito no kokoro ga yutaka ni naru you na aidea o chuushin to shite, kappatsu na giron o tenkai shite ikitai to omotte imasu.

EN translation: "Well, since today is the first day of class, let me briefly explain this term's content. In this course, we'll think about regional revitalization. Drawing on past successes and failures, I'll have you all share ideas about new approaches to revitalizing a region. Revitalizing a region requires economic revitalization too, but I'll leave that part to the administration — here, I'd like to build a lively discussion centered on ideas that enrich the hearts of the people who live there."

今学期の授業のテーマはどのようなことですか。Kongakki no jugyou no teema wa dono you na koto desu ka.

#OptionMeaning
1地域活性化についての問題点Chiiki kasseika ni tsuite no mondaitenProblems with regional revitalization — not stated as the focus
2地域活性化と行政の関係Chiiki kasseika to gyousei no kankeiThe relationship between revitalization and administration — explicitly set aside ("それは行政に任せる")
3地域活性化と経済の関係Chiiki kasseika to keizai no kankeiThe relationship between revitalization and the economy — mentioned only as background, then set aside
4地域活性化についての新しいアイデアChiiki kasseika ni tsuite no atarashii aideaNew ideas for regional revitalization — matches "新しく考えられることについて、意見を出し合ってもらいます"

Why: The professor explicitly hands off the economic/administrative angle ("それは行政に任せるとして") and states the course will center on generating new ideas — matching option 4. Options 2 and 3 both name things she says she is deliberately not focusing on.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

概要理解 talks are given by a recognizable type of speaker in a recognizable setting — the book's own 「注意しよう」 box shows how the setup line alone predicts the likely theme:

Setting / speakerLikely theme
会社の入社式でkaisha no nyuushashiki deMindset/cautions for joining the company (心構えや注意など)
大学の授業でdaigaku no jugyou deAn outline/summary of the course (授業の概略)
医者ishaIllness or health (病気や健康について)
スポーツ選手supootsu senshuReflections and future challenges (反省点や今後の課題など)

Most questions ask for the overall theme, but some instead ask for the speaker's purpose or opinion — listen for whether they agree or disagree with a stated plan/common view, since that judgment (賛成か反対か) can itself be the answer.

Exam trap: In both practice items, every wrong option was a real fact mentioned in the talk — the trap is mistaking a supporting detail (a statistic, a caveat) for the actual overall theme, which is usually stated in the speaker's opening or closing sentence.
