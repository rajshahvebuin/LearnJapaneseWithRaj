1 Question Type: 課題理解 — Task Comprehension

Key idea

まだしていないことを見つけよう！Mada shite inai koto o mitsukeyou! — "Find the thing that hasn't been done yet!"

Unlike 即時応答, this question type does print options on the question sheet. Two people (usually) discuss a task with several steps, and you must identify what the person still has to do next — not what they've already finished, and not the task's ultimate goal. Distractor options are almost always steps that are already done, optional, or scheduled for later.

  1. ① 聞説明和提問 — Listen to the setup and the question first.
  2. ② 聞対話 — Listen to the dialogue.
  3. ③ 再聞一遍提問 — Hear the question repeated.
  4. ④ 選択答案 — Choose the answer from the printed options.

The question almost always asks "what will you/he/she do next?" (これから何をするか) — while listening, actively sort each mentioned action into "already done," "still to do," and "not needed after all."

2 Worked Example — 例をやってみよう

例 CD1, Track 25

植物園で、男の人が窓口の人と話しています。男の人は植物園のパスポートを買うために、まず最初に何をしなければなりませんか。Shokubutsuen de, otoko no hito ga madoguchi no hito to hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito wa shokubutsuen no pasupooto o kau tame ni, mazu saisho ni nani o shinakereba narimasen ka.

EN: A man is talking to a clerk at a botanical garden's counter. In order to buy the garden's pass, what must he do first of all?   HI: एक आदमी वनस्पति उद्यान की खिड़की पर कर्मचारी से बात कर रहा है। पास खरीदने के लिए उसे सबसे पहले क्या करना होगा?   GU: એક માણસ વનસ્પતિ ઉદ્યાનના કાઉન્ટર પર કર્મચારી સાથે વાત કરે છે. પાસ ખરીદવા માટે તેણે સૌ પ્રથમ શું કરવું પડશે?

男: すみません。こちらの植物園、何度でも入園できるパスポートっていうのがあるって聞いたんですけど…。Sumimasen. Kochira no shokubutsuen, nando demo nyuuen dekiru pasupooto tte iu no ga aru tte kiitan desu kedo...

女: ありますよ。2000円で販売しています。ただ、この植物園の「若草の会」への入会が条件でして…入会金は200円です。Arimasu yo. Ni-sen en de hanbai shite imasu. Tada, kono shokubutsuen no "Wakakusa no Kai" e no nyuukai ga jouken deshite... nyuukaikin wa ni-hyaku en desu.

男: あ、そう。じゃ、お願いします。A, sou. Ja, onegaishimasu.

女: それでは、この申込書に必要事項を記入して…あと写真が1枚必要ですが。Sore dewa, kono moushikomisho ni hitsuyou jikou o kinyuu shite... ato shashin ga ichimai hitsuyou desu ga.

男: 写真かあ…今持ってないなあ…。Shashin kaa... ima motte nai naa...

女: その先に機械がありますが。Sono saki ni kikai ga arimasu ga.

男: いや、うちにあるので。近くなんで、あとでまた来ます。とりあえず、これもらっていきます。Iya, uchi ni aru node. Chikaku nan de, ato de mata kimasu. Toriaezu, kore moratte ikimasu.

#OptionMeaning
12000円払うNi-sen en harauPay 2000 yen — this happens later, when he actually buys the pass itself, not first
2「若草の会」に入る"Wakakusa no Kai" ni hairuJoin the "Wakakusa Club" — a prerequisite for buying the pass at all, so it must come first
3入会用紙を手に入れるNyuukai youshi o te ni ireruGet hold of the membership application form — he already takes this form away with him; it's the very next physical step, but the underlying task he must complete first is joining the club
4写真を撮るShashin o toruTake a photo — he says he'll do this later at home, so it's explicitly deferred

Why: Membership in the 若草の会 is the stated condition (条件) for buying the pass, so joining comes conceptually first — the 2000円 payment for the pass itself and the photo both happen afterward. This is why the book marks 2 as correct even though, procedurally, he takes the application form (3) in hand before anything else — 3 is a means to joining, not a separate prior task.

3 Listening Practice — れんしゅう (answers confirmed p.88)

2 items from p.27. First listen to the question, then to the story, and choose the best of 1〜4 (printed on the sheet). Play the referenced CD track alongside this page, or read the script/options below as a pure recall drill.

1番 CD1, Track 26

大学で、男の学生と女の学生が話しています。男の学生は明日、どの電車に乗りますか。Daigaku de, otoko no gakusei to onna no gakusei ga hanashite imasu. Otoko no gakusei wa ashita, dono densha ni norimasu ka.

EN: A male and female student are talking at university. Which train will the male student take tomorrow?   HI: विश्वविद्यालय में एक छात्र और एक छात्रा बात कर रहे हैं। कल छात्र कौन-सी ट्रेन लेगा?   GU: યુનિવર્સિટીમાં એક વિદ્યાર્થી અને વિદ્યાર્થિની વાત કરી રહ્યા છે. કાલે વિદ્યાર્થી કઈ ટ્રેન લેશે?

男: いや〜今日の電車の混みようったらなかったよ。まいった。もう一本早い急行にしてみようかなあ。Iyaa, kyou no densha no komiyou ttara nakatta yo. Maitta. Mou ippon hayai kyuukou ni shite miyou ka naa.

女: もう一本くらい早くたって同じよ。いっそのこと、各駅にしてみれば？Mou ippon kurai hayakutatte onaji yo. Isso no koto, kakueki ni shite mireba?

男: そうだね。ちょっと携帯で調べてみる。…えっと、8時5分前の各駅がある。あー、これだと間に合わないな。えーっと、その前だと…7時45分…それだと大丈夫だけど、今より15分も早いのはなあ…。やっぱりいつものに乗るしかないっか。あれ？すぐあとに快速っていうのがある。あ、これでも間に合うなあ。Sou da ne. Chotto keitai de shirabete miru. ...Etto, hachi-ji go-fun mae no kakueki ga aru. Aa, kore da to ma ni awanai na. Eetto, sono mae da to... shichi-ji yonjuugo-fun... sore da to daijoubu dakedo, ima yori juugo-fun mo hayai no wa naa... Yappari itsumo no ni noru shika nai kka. Are? Sugu ato ni kaisoku tte iu no ga aru. A, kore demo ma ni au naa.

女: 急行のすぐあとの？じゃ、そんなに混まないかもよ。Kyuukou no sugu ato no? Ja, sonna ni komanai kamo yo.

男: そうだよね。明日はそれにしてみるよ。Sou da yo ne. Ashita wa sore ni shite miru yo.

#OptionMeaning
1いつもより5分早い各駅停車Itsumo yori go-fun hayai kakuekiteishaA local train 5 min earlier than usual (not one of the times he actually finds)
2いつもより15分早い各駅停車Itsumo yori juugo-fun hayai kakuekiteishaThe 7:45 local, 15 min earlier than usual — makes it in time, but he rejects it as too early
3いつもより遅い急行Itsumo yori osoi kyuukouA later express — not discussed; he only considered an earlier express at the start
4いつもより遅い快速Itsumo yori osoi kaisokuA rapid-service train right after the express, slightly later than his usual train and likely less crowded

Why: He first rejects an earlier express (too crowded either way) and an earlier local (7:45 — makes it, but 15 minutes earlier than he'd like). He starts resigning himself to his usual train, then discovers a 快速 running right after it, which still makes it to class and should be less crowded — that's his final decision, option 4. The trap options (1, 2, 3) are each a train he mentions and discards along the way.

2番 CD1, Track 27

会社で、男の人と女の人が話しています。女の人は、このあとまず何をしますか。Kaisha de, otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa, kono ato mazu nani o shimasu ka.

EN: A man and a woman are talking at the office. What will the woman do first, right after this?   HI: कंपनी में एक पुरुष और एक महिला बात कर रहे हैं। महिला अब सबसे पहले क्या करेगी?   GU: ઓફિસમાં એક પુરુષ અને એક સ્ત્રી વાત કરી રહ્યા છે. સ્ત્રી હવે સૌથી પહેલા શું કરશે?

男: この書類、大至急10部ずつコピーして、会議室に持っていってくれる？あれ？どうかしたの。Kono shorui, daishikyuu juubu-zutsu kopii shite, kaigishitsu ni motte itte kureru? Are? Dou ka shita no?

女: ちょっと歯が痛くて…もうすぐ痛み止めが効くと思うんですが…。Chotto ha ga itakute... mou sugu itamidome ga kiku to omou n desu ga...

男: そう、でも早めに歯医者さんに行ったほうがいいよ。Sou, demo hayame ni haisha-san ni itta hou ga ii yo.

女: はい、帰りに行こうと思っています。Hai, kaeri ni ikou to omotte imasu.

男: そうだね。あ、悪いけど、とにかく、これ、急いで。会議2時からだから。Sou da ne. A, warui kedo, tonikaku, kore, isoide. Kaigi ni-ji kara dakara.

女: はい、わかりました。Hai, wakarimashita.

男: そうそう、例のアンケート、手が空いたら集計しておいてね。Sou sou, rei no ankeeto, te ga aitara shuukei shite oite ne.

#OptionMeaning
1書類をコピーするShorui o kopii suruCopy the documents — urgent, needed before the 2pm meeting, and she just agreed to it
2痛み止めを飲むItamidome o nomuTake a painkiller — she says it should already be kicking in soon on its own, not a task to do
3歯医者に行くHaisha ni ikuGo to the dentist — explicitly planned for "on the way home," i.e. later
4アンケートをまとめるAnkeeto o matomeruTally up the survey — explicitly "whenever she has free time," i.e. not urgent

Why: Three tasks are mentioned, but only one is urgent and immediate: copying the documents before the 2pm meeting. The dentist visit is deferred to "on the way home," and the survey tally is deferred to "whenever she's free" — both are classic 課題理解 distractors (real tasks, wrong timing).

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

課題理解 also likes to layer in a numeric sub-question (time or cost) on top of the sequencing trap. The book's own 「注意しよう」 box gives the standard question shapes and the sequencing vocabulary that signals "this comes before/after that":

CategorySet question / wordWhat to listen for
Time questions どのくらいかかりますか。
何時に家を出なければなりませんか。		 "How long does it take?" / "What time must you leave by?" — expect a duration or clock-time answer buried inside the dialogue, not stated up front.
Cost questions 入園料は全部でいくらですか。
ホテルの宿泊料はいくらですか。
チケットのキャンセル料はいくらですか。		 "How much is the entrance fee in total?" / hotel rate / cancellation fee — these often require adding two mentioned amounts together (base fee + optional extra), as in this lesson's worked example (2000円 pass + 200円 membership).
Sequencing words 至急（＝すぐに）・大至急、それより、先に、その前に、とりあえず "Urgently / ASAP," "before that," "first," "prior to that," "for now" — these words are the actual signal for which task is next; options that ignore them are traps.
Exam trap: In both practice items above, the wrong options were real actions the speakers actually do mention — just at the wrong time (already rejected, or explicitly scheduled for later). 課題理解 is a sequencing test disguised as a comprehension test: always ask "when," not just "what."
