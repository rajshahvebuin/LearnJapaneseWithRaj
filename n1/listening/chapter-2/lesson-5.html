1 Question Type: 統合理解① — Integrated Comprehension

Key idea

情報や意見の違いに注意しよう！Jouhou ya iken no chigai ni chuui shiyou! — "Pay attention to the differences between pieces of information or opinions!"

統合理解 is the longest, most information-dense question type: a longer, harder conversation, several options laid out (four products, four plans, four rooms), and a decision that gets made only at the very end after back-and-forth. There are two sub-shapes: (a) choosing between four distinct items/categories, or (b) following two or more speakers as they negotiate and settle on one option. Either way, the final answer is usually only clear from the last one or two lines.

  1. ① 聞説明 — Listen to the setup.
  2. ② 聞対話 — Listen to a longer, somewhat harder conversation.
  3. ③ 聞提問 — Listen to the question.
  4. ④ 選択答案 — Choose from the spoken options.

The question is only played once, so track each option's pros/cons as they come up rather than waiting to reconstruct everything from memory at the end.

2 Worked Example — 例をやってみよう

例 CD1, Track 35

女の人がワインを選んでいます。Onna no hito ga wain o erande imasu.

EN: A woman is choosing a wine. (The question here is asked only once, right after the dialogue — no second listen.)   HI: एक महिला वाइन चुन रही है। (यहाँ सवाल संवाद के तुरंत बाद सिर्फ एक बार पूछा जाता है।)   GU: એક સ્ત્રી વાઇન પસંદ કરી રહી છે. (અહીં પ્રશ્ન સંવાદ પછી ફક્ત એક જ વાર પુછાય છે.)

男: 今、こちらの棚のワインがキャンペーン中になっております。この2つは特にお勧めです。左のほうはドイツの少し甘口のもので、右のほうはイタリアのかなりドライなワインです。辛口好きの人には大変好まれています。Ima, kochira no tana no wain ga kyanpeen-chuu ni natte orimasu. Kono futatsu wa toku ni osusume desu. Hidari no hou wa Doitsu no sukoshi amakuchi no mono de, migi no hou wa Itaria no kanari dorai na wain desu. Karakuchi-zuki no hito ni wa taihen konomarete imasu.

女: きれいなビンねえ。でも、どっちも白なのね…。Kirei na bin nee. Demo, dotchi mo shiro na no ne...

男: 赤がよろしければ、これなどはいかがでしょうか？フランスのもので、マイルドな口当たりです。それから、こちらのチリのワインも大変人気がありますよ。Aka ga yoroshikereba, kore nado wa ikaga deshou ka? Furansu no mono de, mairudo na kuchiatari desu. Sore kara, kochira no Chiri no wain mo taihen ninki ga arimasu yo.

女: チリのワインね、この間飲んだのもチリだったけど、すごくおいしかったです。うーん、お友達の家に持っていくのですが、赤か白よりビンがおしゃれなほうがいいかな。えーっと、あんまりドライなものじゃないほうがいいので、これお願いします。Chiri no wain ne, kono aida nonda no mo Chiri datta kedo, sugoku oishikatta desu. Uun, otomodachi no ie ni motte iku no desu ga, aka ka shiro yori bin ga oshare na hou ga ii ka na. Eetto, anmari dorai na mono ja nai hou ga ii node, kore onegaishimasu.

EN translation: Clerk: "These two are especially recommended — the left is a slightly sweet German wine, the right a fairly dry Italian wine popular with dry-wine lovers." Woman: "Pretty bottles — but they're both white." Clerk: "If you'd prefer red, how about this mild French one? This Chilean one is also very popular." Woman: "Chilean, huh — the one I had recently was Chilean too and was delicious. Well, I'm bringing it to a friend's, so a stylish bottle might matter more than the color. I'd rather it not be too dry, though — I'll take this one, please."

女の人はどのワインを選びましたか。Onna no hito wa dono wain o erabimashita ka.

#OptionMeaning
1ドイツの白ワインDoitsu no shiro wainGerman white wine — the only wine explicitly described as not-dry (甘口, "slightly sweet")
2イタリアの白ワインItaria no shiro wainItalian white wine — explicitly the driest option, which she rules out
3フランスの赤ワインFuransu no aka wainFrench red wine — "mild," but she never picks a red over a white in the end
4チリの赤ワインChiri no aka wainChilean wine — she reminisces fondly about it, a classic distractor, but never actually orders it

Why: Whether the bottle is red or white turns out not to matter to her ("赤か白よりビンがおしゃれなほうがいいかな"). Her one hard requirement is stated last: not too dry. Of the four wines, only the German one was explicitly called sweet rather than dry — so despite fondly recalling the Chilean wine, her final "これお願いします" points back to the German bottle.

3 Listening Practice — れんしゅう (answers confirmed p.94)

2 items from p.33. Listen to the (longer) conversation first, then to the question and spoken options, and choose 1〜4. Play the referenced CD track alongside this page, or read the script/options below as a pure recall drill.

1番 CD1, Track 36

男の人と女の人がレストランの料理について話しています。Otoko no hito to onna no hito ga resutoran no ryouri ni tsuite hanashite imasu.

EN: A man and a woman are talking about a restaurant's food.   HI: एक पुरुष और एक महिला रेस्टोरेंट के खाने के बारे में बात कर रहे हैं।   GU: એક પુરુષ અને એક સ્ત્રી રેસ્ટોરન્ટના ખોરાક વિશે વાત કરી રહ્યા છે.

女: 今日の夕食、さっき、例の和食屋さんを予約したよ。Kyou no yuushoku, sakki, rei no washokuya-san o yoyaku shita yo.

男: あ、そう。コース料理にした？A, sou. Koosu ryouri ni shita?

女: コースにしたかったんだけど、コース料理は前日までに頼まないといけないんだって。Koosu ni shitakatta n dakedo, koosu ryouri wa zenjitsu made ni tanomanai to ikenai n datte.

男: え、じゃ、向こうへ行ってから頼むってことになるの？E, ja, mukou e itte kara tanomu tte koto ni naru no?

女: ううん、おまかせ料理というのがあって、一応それを頼んだんだけど…。Uun, omakase ryouri to iu no ga atte, ichiou sore o tanonda n dakedo...

男: おまかせってどういうのが出るのかな。おふくろ、好き嫌いが激しいからなー。アラカルトで頼めばいいんじゃない？Omakase tte dou iu no ga deru no ka na. Ofukuro, sukikirai ga hageshii kara naa. Arakaruto de tanomeba ii n ja nai?

女: 行ったことがないところだし、アラカルトで頼むったって、迷って時間がかかると思うけど…。変更しとく？それか、別のとこにする？キャンセルもまだできるよ。Itta koto ga nai tokoro da shi, arakaruto de tanomu tta tte, mayotte jikan ga kakaru to omou kedo... Henkou shitoku? Sore ka, betsu no toko ni suru? Kyanseru mo mada dekiru yo.

男: そうだねえ、別のところといってもねえ。ま、いっか、このままで。Sou da nee, betsu no tokoro to itte mo nee. Ma, ikka, kono mama de.

EN translation: She's booked a Japanese restaurant, but the set course had to be ordered a day ahead, so she instead booked the "omakase" (chef's choice) meal. He worries his fussy mother won't like whatever comes, and floats ordering à la carte instead — but she points out that would mean dithering over an unfamiliar menu for ages. She offers to change it or try somewhere else, or cancel — he decides it's fine to just leave it as is.

2人はどうすることにしましたか。Futari wa dou suru koto ni shimashita ka.

#OptionMeaning
1コース料理を頼むKoosu ryouri o tanomuOrder the set course — ruled out early (needed a day's notice)
2アラカルトで頼むArakaruto de tanomuOrder à la carte — floated by him, rejected by her as too time-consuming
3行ってから決めるItte kara kimeruDecide once they arrive — this was the original worry before learning "omakase" already existed; never chosen
4おまかせ料理を頼むOmakase ryouri o tanomuKeep the already-booked omakase (chef's choice) meal — "このままで" (leave it as is)

Why: His final line, 「いっか、このままで」("fine, let's just leave it as is"), refers back to what she'd already booked at the start of the conversation — the omakase meal. Every other option was raised and then talked back out of.

2番 CD1, Track 37

古いテレビをどうするかについて、家族3人が話しています。Furui terebi o dou suru ka ni tsuite, kazoku san-nin ga hanashite imasu.

EN: Three family members are discussing what to do with an old TV.   HI: तीन परिवार के सदस्य पुराने टीवी का क्या करें, इस पर बात कर रहे हैं।   GU: ત્રણ કુટુંબીજનો જૂના ટીવીનું શું કરવું તે વિશે વાત કરી રહ્યા છે.

息子: ね、俺の部屋のテレビ、処分しないと。もう映らないし。Ne, ore no heya no terebi, shobun shinai to. Mou utsuranai shi.

母: 処分するのに、粗大ごみでは出せないし、リサイクル法とかなんとかで、けっこうかかるんじゃないの？取りに来てもらうのも大変だし、新しいのを買えば、その電気屋で引き取ってくれるでしょ。Shobun suru noni, sodai gomi de wa dasenai shi, risaikuru hou toka nanka de, kekkou kakaru n ja nai no? Tori ni kite morau no mo taihen da shi, atarashii no o kaeba, sono denkiya de hikitotte kureru desho.

息子: え？買ってくれるの？E? Katte kureru no?

父: 不用品回収車に持っていってもらえばいいじゃないか。パソコンでもテレビ、見られるんだろ？Fuyouhin kaishuusha ni motte itte moraeba ii ja nai ka. Pasokon demo terebi, mirareru n daro?

母: え？パソコンで見られるの？じゃ、必要ないじゃない。E? Pasokon de mirareru no? Ja, hitsuyou nai ja nai.

息子: えー、いや、映画なんか、パソコンの小さい画面だと雰囲気出ないよー。Ee, iya, eiga nanka, pasokon no chiisai gamen da to fun'iki denai yoo.

父: 何言ってんだ。ぜいたくも甚だしいよ。自分でリサイクルショップに持っていけ。Nani itten da. Zeitaku mo hanahadashii yo. Jibun de risaikuru shoppu ni motte ike.

母: 回収車、いつも土曜日に回ってるから、明日ね。健太、あとで玄関先まで運んどきなさい。Kaishuusha, itsumo doyoubi ni mawatteru kara, ashita ne. Kenta, ato de genkansaki made hakondokinasai.

息子: あ〜あ、仕方ないか。Aa, shikata nai ka.

EN translation: The son wants to get rid of his broken TV. Mom suggests buying a new one so the electronics store takes the old one; Dad suggests the junk-removal truck instead, since you can watch TV on a computer anyway. Mom then says a new TV isn't even necessary if that's true; the son objects that movies feel wrong on a small computer screen, and Dad scolds him for being extravagant, telling him to take it to a recycle shop himself. Mom settles it: the collection truck comes Saturdays (tomorrow), so Kenta should carry it to the entrance later.

家族は古いテレビをどうすることにしますか。Kazoku wa furui terebi o dou suru koto ni shimasu ka.

#OptionMeaning
1電気屋に引き取ってもらうDenkiya ni hikitotte morauHave the electronics store take it — Mom's first idea, dropped once buying a new TV is ruled unnecessary
2不用品回収車に持っていってもらうFuyouhin kaishuusha ni motte itte morauHave the junk-removal truck take it — Dad's suggestion, which Mom's final line confirms ("回収車、いつも土曜日に回ってる")
3リサイクルショップに持っていくRisaikuru shoppu ni motte ikuTake it to a recycle shop — Dad's angry aside to the son about the computer comment, not the family's actual plan
4粗大ごみとして玄関先に出しておくSodai gomi toshite genkansaki ni dashite okuPut it out as oversized garbage — explicitly ruled out at the very start (too expensive/inconvenient)

Why: Mom's closing line locks in Dad's junk-removal-truck idea — she only tells the son to carry the TV to the entrance for the truck's usual Saturday run. Option 3 is a red herring: Dad only mentions the recycle shop while scolding his son, not as the family's actual decision.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

統合理解① dialogues almost always involve 2–3 speakers negotiating, and the book's own 「注意しよう」 box gives the shape to expect:

PatternDetail
Speaker countUsually 2 or more (often around 3) people, mostly exchanging opinions or negotiating a decision together.
Opening scene-setter家族3人がペットについて話しています。／学生3人が友人にあげるプレゼントについて相談しています。— listen carefully to this first line; it tells you how many speakers and what they're deciding.
Question stemsどうすることに決めましたか。どのコースを選びますか。どの〜を買うでしょうか。— "what did they decide to do," "which course do they choose," "which ~ will they buy."
Typical arcOne opinion is raised, then a counter-opinion or alternative appears, and the group's opinion converges only near the very end — the second half of the conversation carries the real answer far more often than the first half.
Exam trap: In both items above, an option that was raised, discussed, and even sounded appealing for a moment (アラカルト, リサイクルショップ) was ultimately dropped. 統合理解 rewards patience — never lock in an answer before the final line.
