1 Strategy & Key Words — the first step and the combined answer

The book's advice (summarised, p.33) + tips for this set
  • Listen only for what has to be done. The question is heard before and after the talk; note the key points and pick from the printed options. Shinakereba naranai koto dake o kikitorimashou.
    HI: सिर्फ़ वही पकड़ें जो करना ज़रूरी है; ज़रूरी बातें नोट करें। GU: ફક્ત જે કરવું જરૂરી છે તે પકડો; જરૂરી મુદ્દા નોંધો.
  • Two-part options (added, not in book): each option here joins two choices (voucher yes/no + which service; which company + bring/collect). Decide each half separately and cross out options as soon as one half is wrong. For 一番先に, look for words of priority and timing (最優先, 朝一, その前に). ichiban saki ni, saiyuusen, asaichi, sono mae ni
    HI: हर विकल्प में दो हिस्से हैं; दोनों को अलग-अलग तय करें और एक भी ग़लत हो तो विकल्प काट दें। "सबसे पहले" के लिए 最優先, 朝一, その前に जैसे शब्द पकड़ें। GU: દરેક વિકલ્પમાં બે ભાગ છે; બંને અલગ નક્કી કરો અને એક પણ ખોટો હોય તો વિકલ્પ કાઢી નાખો. "સૌથી પહેલાં" માટે 最優先, 朝一, その前に જેવા શબ્દ પકડો.

2 Every question — 12番〜14番

4ソミー電器に電話をし、資料を取りに行くSomii Denki ni denwa o shi, shiryou o tori ni ikuphone Somii and go to collect the documentsसोमी को फ़ोन करके काग़ज़ लेने जानाસોમીને ફોન કરીને કાગળ લેવા જવું

Answer 3 (book 正解, p.72). Why (added, not in book): two halves again. Which company? Shibata has already been handled by the woman (私がお詫びとお礼を言っておいた), so 1 and 2 are out. Bring or collect? The documents are already back in the office (送り返してきてくれた), so there is nothing to collect (4); he takes them to Somii (持って行く), as she agrees. The book's note on ただ〜送ればいいってもんじゃない (= sending alone doesn't settle it) points to the apology call.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Where this unit's wrong options come from (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey differenceSeen in
〜はいい vs 〜はお願いします… wa ii / … wa onegai shimasu no thanks vs yes please いい after an offer usually means "no need". HI: いい = नहीं चाहिए · GU: いい = નથી જોઈતું 12番
今週は使えない vs 来週から使えるkonshuu wa tsukaenai / raishuu kara tsukaeru can't use it this week vs from next week The voucher is valid in general but not now, so "no voucher" today. HI: अभी नहीं ≠ कभी नहीं · GU: હમણાં નહીં ≠ ક્યારેય નહીં 12番
〜が最優先だから、〜しといて… ga saiyuusen da kara, … shitoite since ~ is top priority, do ~ The priority (仕込み) is the reason; the instruction after から is the task. HI: कारण ≠ काम · GU: કારણ ≠ કામ 13番
朝一で vs 最後にasaichi de / saigo ni first thing vs last Order words are the whole question in 一番先に items. HI: सुबह सबसे पहले ≠ आख़िर में · GU: સવારે સૌથી પહેલાં ≠ છેલ્લે 13番
持って行く vs 取りに行くmotte iku / tori ni iku take something there vs go to fetch it Direction of the object: you already have it vs you don't. HI: ले जाना ≠ लेने जाना · GU: લઈ જવું ≠ લેવા જવું 14番
〜ばいいってもんじゃない… ba ii tte mon ja nai it's not enough just to ~ Signals a missing earlier step (here the apology). HI: सिर्फ़ ~ से काम नहीं चलेगा · GU: ફક્ત ~થી કામ નહીં ચાલે 14番
Exam trap: when every option joins two choices, the distractors are built by mixing a right half with a wrong one. Settle each half from a separate line of the talk (voucher / service; company / bring-or-collect) and only then pick the option that has both right. (added, not in book)
