1 問題Ⅰ — 課題理解 (10点×3問)

まず質問を聞いてください。それから話を聞いて、1～4の中から最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mazu shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Sore kara hanashi o kiite, 1~4 no naka kara mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. — "First listen to the question. Then listen to the talk, and choose the best answer from 1–4."

1番 CD2, Track 26

女の人と男の人が話しています。女の人は、このあとまず何をしますか。Onna no hito to otoko no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa, kono ato mazu nani o shimasu ka.

EN: A woman and a man are talking. What will the woman do first after this?   HI: एक महिला और पुरुष बात कर रहे हैं। महिला अब सबसे पहले क्या करेगी?   GU: એક સ્ત્રી અને પુરુષ વાત કરે છે. સ્ત્રી હવે સૌથી પહેલાં શું કરશે?

女：ああ、このパソコン、イライラしちゃう。なんにもやってないのに、急に文字が大きくなったり小さくなったり…インターネットを立ち上げるのも、時々できなくなるし…。Onna: Aa, kono pasokon, iraira shichau. Nan ni mo yattenai noni, kyuu ni moji ga ookiku nattari chiisaku nattari… intaanetto o tachiageru no mo, tokidoki dekinaku naru shi….
男：何もやってないってことないよ。知らないうちに操作してるんだよ。Otoko: Nani mo yattenai tte koto nai yo. Shiranai uchi ni sousa shiterun da yo.
女：何もやってないよ。再起動させると直るんだけど、こう何度もしなきゃいけないと、作業だってなかなか進まないし…。Onna: Nani mo yattenai yo. Saikidou saseru to naorun da kedo, kou nando mo shinakya ikenai to, sagyou datte nakanaka susumanai shi….
男：トラブルの原因をちゃんと追究したほうがいいよ。電話するとか、説明書を読み直すとか…インターネットで、調べてみたら？そんなとき、どうすればいいかって、たいてい誰かが質問してるよ。Otoko: Toraburu no gen'in o chanto tsuikyuu shita hou ga ii yo. Denwa suru toka, setsumeisho o yominaosu toka… intaanetto de, shirabete mitara? Sonna toki, dou sureba ii ka tte, taitei dareka ga shitsumon shiteru yo.
女：あ、ネットでね…今、調べてみる。…あー、また立ち上がらない。接続が悪いのかなあ。またやり直しか。Onna: A, netto de ne… ima, shirabete miru. …Aa, mata tachiagaranai. Setsuzoku ga warui no ka naa. Mata yarinaoshi ka.

EN translation: W: "Ugh, this computer is so frustrating. Even though I haven't done anything, the text suddenly gets bigger or smaller... and sometimes I can't even start the internet..." M: "There's no way you haven't done anything — you're operating it without realizing." W: "I really haven't. Restarting fixes it, but having to do that over and over, my work barely progresses..." M: "You should properly get to the bottom of what's causing the trouble — call support, re-read the manual, or look it up online? Usually someone's already asked what to do in that situation." W: "Ah, online... let me look it up now. ...Ugh, it won't start up again. Maybe the connection's bad. Guess I have to redo it again."

#OptionEnglish
1パソコンの説明書を読み直すPasokon no setsumeisho o yominaosu"Re-read the computer's manual" (one of the man's suggestions, but not what she does next)
2電話でトラブルについて相談するDenwa de toraburu ni tsuite soudan suru"Consult about the trouble by phone" (also just a suggestion she doesn't take)
3インターネットで質問してみるIntaanetto de shitsumon shite miru"Try asking a question online" (she starts to look it up, but the internet won't load)
4パソコンを再起動させるPasokon o saikidou saseru"Restart the computer"

Why: She tries to search online (option 3) but the internet won't come up (「また立ち上がらない」), forcing her to conclude 「またやり直しか」("guess I have to redo it again") — i.e. restart the computer once more before she can do anything else. The other two suggestions were raised but never acted on.

2番 CD2, Track 27

大学で、男の学生と女の学生がインターンシップについて話しています。男の学生は、まず何をしなければなりませんか。Daigaku de, otoko no gakusei to onna no gakusei ga intaanshippu ni tsuite hanashite imasu. Otoko no gakusei wa, mazu nani o shinakereba narimasen ka.

EN: At university, a male and female student are talking about internships. What must the male student do first?   HI: विश्वविद्यालय में एक छात्र और छात्रा इंटर्नशिप के बारे में बात कर रहे हैं। छात्र को सबसे पहले क्या करना होगा?   GU: યુનિવર્સિટીમાં એક વિદ્યાર્થી અને વિદ્યાર્થિની ઇન્ટર્નશિપ વિશે વાત કરે છે. વિદ્યાર્થીએ સૌથી પહેલાં શું કરવું પડશે?

女：加藤君、インターンシップ、決まった？Onna: Katou-kun, intaanshippu, kimatta?
男：いや、まだ。やっぱりやったほうがいいのかなあ、インターンシップって。Otoko: Iya, mada. Yappari yatta hou ga ii no ka naa, intaanshippu tte.
女：うん、ゼミの先生も、そう言ってたじゃない。Onna: Un, zemi no sensei mo, sou itteta ja nai.
男：けど、僕、まだどういう職種が自分に合ってるかわかんないし…。何より、就職課に行って書類をもらっておかないと。エミちゃんはもう決まったの？Otoko: Kedo, boku, mada dou iu shokushu ga jibun ni atteru ka wakannai shi…. Nani yori, shuushokuka ni itte shorui o moratte okanai to. Emi-chan wa mou kimatta no?
女：ううん、まだ、リサーチ中。ね、よさそうな会社があるんだけど、あさって、一緒にセミナーへ行ってみる？Onna: Uun, mada, risaachi chuu. Ne, yosasou na kaisha ga arun da kedo, asatte, issho ni seminaa e itte miru?
男：あさってねえ…。バイトのスケジュール、調整してみようかなあ。Otoko: Asatte nee…. Baito no sukejuuru, chousei shite miyou ka naa.

EN translation: W: "Katou, have you decided on an internship?" M: "No, not yet. I wonder if I really should do one..." W: "Yeah, didn't our seminar professor say so too?" M: "But I still don't know what job type suits me... More than anything, I need to go to the career center and get the documents. Has Emi decided already?" W: "No, still researching. There's a company that looks good — want to go to their seminar together the day after tomorrow?" M: "The day after tomorrow, huh... I might try adjusting my part-time work schedule."

#OptionEnglish
1就職課で書類をもらうShuushokuka de shorui o morau"Get documents at the career center"
2インターンシップ先をリサーチするIntaanshippu saki o risaachi suru"Research internship placements" (that's Emi's task, not his)
3会社のセミナーを受けるKaisha no seminaa o ukeru"Attend the company's seminar" (still undecided — he's only considering adjusting his schedule)
4セミナーのスケジュールを調整するSeminaa no sukejuuru o chousei suru"Adjust the seminar schedule" (he's adjusting his part-time work schedule, not the seminar's)

Why: He states his own most urgent to-do directly: 「何より、就職課に行って書類をもらっておかないと」("more than anything, I need to get documents from the career center"). Researching companies (2) is what Emi is doing; the seminar (3, 4) is still just a maybe.

3番 CD2, Track 28

店長とアルバイトの学生が話しています。アルバイトの学生は、来週は何日間、店で働きますか。Tenchou to arubaito no gakusei ga hanashite imasu. Arubaito no gakusei wa, raishuu wa nan-nichikan, mise de hatarakimasu ka.

EN: A store manager and a part-time-worker student are talking. How many days will the student work at the store next week?   HI: स्टोर मैनेजर और पार्ट-टाइम काम करने वाला छात्र बात कर रहे हैं। अगले हफ्ते छात्र कितने दिन दुकान पर काम करेगा?   GU: સ્ટોર મેનેજર અને પાર્ટ-ટાઇમ કામ કરતો વિદ્યાર્થી વાત કરે છે. આવતા અઠવાડિયે વિદ્યાર્થી કેટલા દિવસ દુકાનમાં કામ કરશે?

男：君は今、月曜日と木曜日だけだけど、他の曜日は来られないの？Otoko: Kimi wa ima, getsuyoubi to mokuyoubi dake da kedo, hoka no youbi wa korarenai no?
女：いえ、時間によっては大丈夫な曜日もあります。Onna: Ie, jikan ni yotte wa daijoubu na youbi mo arimasu.
男：そう、土曜日はだめだって言ってたよね。金曜日は？Otoko: Sou, doyoubi wa dame datte itteta yo ne. Kin'youbi wa?
女：はい、土曜日は無理なんですが、金曜日は、都合をつけようと思えばつけられます。何時からでしょうか。Onna: Hai, doyoubi wa muri nan desu ga, kin'youbi wa, tsugou o tsukeyou to omoeba tsukeraremasu. Nanji kara deshou ka.
男：午後からでいいんだけど、大丈夫かな。1時から6時まで。Otoko: Gogo kara de ii n da kedo, daijoubu ka na. Ichiji kara rokuji made.
女：それなら、大丈夫です。Onna: Sore nara, daijoubu desu.
男：じゃ、来月から金曜日も来てくれるかな。Otoko: Ja, raigetsu kara kin'youbi mo kite kureru ka na.
女：来月からって言うと、もう来週ですよね。わかりました。Onna: Raigetsu kara tte iu to, mou raishuu desu yo ne. Wakarimashita.
男：あ、来週は、木曜日から3日間、旅行で店を休むこと、伝えてたよね。だから、金曜日に来てもらうのは再来週からということで。Otoko: A, raishuu wa, mokuyoubi kara mikkakan, ryokou de mise o yasumu koto, tsutaeteta yo ne. Dakara, kin'youbi ni kite morau no wa saraishuu kara to iu koto de.
女：はい、わかりました。Onna: Hai, wakarimashita.

EN translation: M: "Right now you only come Monday and Thursday — can't you come other days?" W: "There are days that could work depending on the time." M: "Right, you said Saturday's out. What about Friday?" W: "Saturday's impossible, but for Friday I could manage it if I try. What time would it start?" M: "Afternoon is fine — 1pm to 6pm, would that work?" W: "That would be fine." M: "Then can you start coming Fridays too from next month?" W: "Starting next month means it's already next week, right? Understood." M: "Oh — I did tell you the store is closed for 3 days starting next Thursday because of my trip, right? So Fridays will actually start from the week after next." W: "Yes, understood."

#OptionEnglish
1来週は働かないRaishuu wa hatarakanai"Doesn't work next week at all"
21日だけ働くIchinichi dake hataraku"Works only 1 day"
32日間働くFutsukakan hataraku"Works 2 days"
43日間働くMikkakan hataraku"Works 3 days"

Why: Her normal schedule is Monday + Thursday. But next week the store is closed for 3 days starting Thursday (for the manager's trip), cancelling her Thursday shift — and her new Friday shift doesn't start until the week after next. So next week she only works her usual Monday: 1 day.

2 問題Ⅱ — 概要理解 (10点×3問)

まず質問を聞いてください。そのあと、選択肢を読んでください。読む時間があります。それから話を聞いて、1～4の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mazu shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Sono ato, sentakushi o yonde kudasai. Yomu jikan ga arimasu. Sore kara hanashi o kiite, 1~4 no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. — "First listen to the question. Then read the options — you'll have time to read. Then listen to the talk, and choose the best answer from 1–4."

ラジオの電話相談のコーナーで、女の人が話しています。女の人はどういうことで悩んでいますか。Rajio no denwa soudan no koonaa de, onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa dou iu koto de nayande imasu ka.

EN: On a radio call-in advice segment, a woman is speaking. What is troubling her?   HI: रेडियो के फ़ोन-इन सलाह कार्यक्रम में एक महिला बात कर रही है। वह किस बात से परेशान है?   GU: રેડિયોના ફોન-ઇન સલાહ કાર્યક્રમમાં એક સ્ત્રી બોલે છે. તે શેનાથી પરેશાન છે?

男：では、次の方ですが、社会人になって3年目だそうですね。何でお悩みなのでしょうか。Otoko: Dewa, tsugi no kata desu ga, shakaijin ni natte san-nenme da sou desu ne. Nan de onayami na no deshou ka.
女：新卒で入社した周りの友達が、最近次々と会社を辞めて、新しい会社に転職し始めたんです。みんな口をそろえてキャリアアップとか、市場価値を高めたいとか言って…。でも、前の職種を活かすことができずに、新しい会社で新入社員として働いていたり、給料も下がったっていう話もよく聞きますし…。私は、今の仕事の中で、キャリアアップを目指したり、自分のやりたいことをやればいいのではないかと思っているんですが、みんなが転職、転職と、転職しないとキャリアアップできないようなことを言っているのを聞くと、私だけこのままでいいのだろうかと、不安になってしまうんです。Onna: Shinsotsu de nyuusha shita mawari no tomodachi ga, saikin tsugitsugi to kaisha o yamete, atarashii kaisha ni tenshoku shihajimetan desu. Minna kuchi o soroete kyaria appu toka, shijou kachi o takametai toka itte…. Demo, mae no shokushu o ikasu koto ga dekizu ni, atarashii kaisha de shin'nyuu shain toshite hataraiteitari, kyuuryou mo sagatta tte iu hanashi mo yoku kikimasu shi…. Watashi wa, ima no shigoto no naka de, kyaria appu o mezashitari, jibun no yaritai koto o yareba ii no de wa nai ka to omotteirun desu ga, minna ga tenshoku, tenshoku to, tenshoku shinai to kyaria appu dekinai you na koto o itte iru no o kiku to, watashi dake kono mama de ii no darou ka to, fuan ni natte shimaun desu.

EN translation: M: "Next caller — it's been 3 years since you became a working adult, I hear. What's troubling you?" W: "My friends who joined as new grads with me have been quitting and switching companies one after another lately. They all say the same thing — wanting career advancement, wanting to raise their market value. But I also hear that many end up as new hires again at the new company, unable to use their old job type, or that their pay dropped. I feel like I could aim for career growth within my current job instead, but hearing everyone talk about changing jobs as if you can't advance without it makes me anxious about whether it's okay for just me to stay as I am."

#OptionEnglish
1転職する会社の給料が思ったよりよくないことTenshoku suru kaisha no kyuuryou ga omotta yori yokunai koto"The salary at a company she'd switch to isn't as good as expected" (that's other people's experience, not her own worry)
2友人が次々と転職し、取り残されたように感じることYuujin ga tsugitsugi to tenshoku shi, torinokosareta you ni kanjiru koto"Her friends keep switching jobs and she feels left behind"
3今の仕事がキャリアアップにつながらないことIma no shigoto ga kyaria appu ni tsunagaranai koto"Her current job doesn't lead to career advancement" (she actually believes it could)
4今の経験を活かした転職ができないことIma no keiken o ikashita tenshoku ga dekinai koto"She can't change jobs in a way that uses her current experience" (she isn't trying to change jobs at all)

Why: Her closing line names the real anxiety directly: 「私だけこのままでいいのだろうか」("is it okay for just me to stay as I am") — worry about being left behind while friends job-hop, not about salary, her own career prospects, or her own job search.

女1：ねえ、貯金してる？私は全然。Onna1: Nee, chokin shiteru? Watashi wa zenzen.
女2：私は、将来に備えて一応ね。でももっと貯めないと。Onna2: Watashi wa, shourai ni sonaete ichiou ne. Demo motto tamenai to.
女1：お金持ちのいい人、現れてくれないかなあ。Onna1: Okanemochi no ii hito, arawarete kurenai ka naa.
女2：あなたもまだ、結婚願望ある？Onna2: Anata mo mada, kekkon ganbou aru?

Demo, watashitachi ki o tsukenai to. Ano ne, watashi no koukou jidai no tomodachi, kono aida kekkon sagi ni atta no yo. Kanojo, mou isshou hitori de ikite iku tte itteta.
女1：あら、かわいそう。私たちの年で独り者だと聞けば、お金を持ってるだろうと思って、変な男が狙ってくるのね。ほんと、警戒しなきゃ。Onna1: Ara, kawaisou. Watashitachi no toshi de hitorimono da to kikeba, okane o motteru darou to omotte, hen na otoko ga neratte kuru no ne. Honto, keikai shinakya.

EN translation: W1: "Hey, are you saving money? I'm not at all." W2: "I do, sort of, for the future — but I need to save more." W1: "I wish a rich, nice person would show up." W2: "Do you still want to get married?" W1: "I do. I wonder where my prince is dawdling. Hurry up~ hehe." W2: "I wonder what my prince is doing too — I've been waiting so long. Haha. But we need to be careful. You know, my friend from high school got scammed by a marriage fraud recently — she said she's decided to live alone for good now." W1: "Oh, poor thing. At our age, if men hear you're single they assume you must have money, and weird men target you. We really have to be on our guard."

#OptionEnglish
1一生独身のまま過ごすつもりだIsshou dokushin no mama sugosu tsumori da"They intend to stay single for life" (that's the friend's decision, not theirs — they both still want to marry)
2結婚しても幸せになれるかどうかわからないKekkon shitemo shiawase ni nareru ka dou ka wakaranai"They don't know if marriage would make them happy" (not discussed)
3結婚に備えて、お金を貯めておくべきだKekkon ni sonaete, okane o tamete oku beki da"They should save money in preparation for marriage" (only one of them saves, and not framed as advice to both)
4結婚したいと近づいてくる男には注意したほうがいいKekkon shitai to chikazuite kuru otoko ni wa chuui shita hou ga ii"They should be wary of men who approach wanting to marry them"

Why: The conversation pivots on the friend's marriage-fraud story, and both women land on the same conclusion together: 「ほんと、警戒しなきゃ」("we really have to be on guard") against men who target single women assumed to have money — a shared warning, not a shared life plan or savings habit.

女：このカッター、どうやってもうまく切れないんだけど…もう刃がダメになってるのかも。Onna: Kono kattaa, dou yattemo umaku kirenain da kedo… mou ha ga dame ni natteru no kamo.
男：貸してごらん。これ使うの、コツがあるんだよ。こんなふうに一気にやらないとダメなんだよ。昔から「バカとはさみは使いよう」って言うだろ。Otoko: Kashite goran. Kore tsukau no, kotsu ga arun da yo. Konna fuu ni ikki ni yaranai to dame nan da yo. Mukashi kara "baka to hasami wa tsukaiyou" tte iu daro.
女：何、それ、どういう意味？私がバカだって言いたいの？Onna: Nani, sore, dou iu imi? Watashi ga baka da tte iitai no?
男：いや、そういうつもりで言ったわけじゃないよ。コツをつかめば君も切れるよという意味で言ったんだよ。変なふうに取るなよ。とにかく、うまく切れたんだから、いいだろ。Otoko: Iya, sou iu tsumori de itta wake ja nai yo. Kotsu o tsukameba kimi mo kireru yo to iu imi de ittan da yo. Hen na fuu ni toru na yo. Tonikaku, umaku kiretan da kara, ii daro.
女：よかないわよ。だいたいね、あなたは自分だけが偉いと思っているから、そういうことを言うのよ。人を見下すのもいいかげんにしてよ。Onna: Yokanai wa yo. Daitai ne, anata wa jibun dake ga erai to omotte iru kara, sou iu koto o iu no yo. Hito o mikudasu no mo ii kagen ni shite yo.
男：だからー、深い意味で言ったんじゃないって、言ってんじゃないか。いいかげんにしてほしいのはこっちだよ。Otoko: Dakaraa, fukai imi de ittan ja nai tte, itten ja nai ka. Ii kagen ni shite hoshii no wa kocchi da yo.

EN translation: W: "No matter how I use this cutter, it won't cut well... maybe the blade's done for." M: "Let me try. There's a trick to using this — you have to do it all at once like this. They've always said, 'even a fool can use scissors well if you know how.'" W: "What's that supposed to mean? Are you calling me a fool?" M: "No, I didn't mean it that way — I meant if you get the hang of it, you can cut well too. Don't take it the wrong way. Anyway, it cut fine, so it's fine, right?" W: "It's not fine. Honestly, you say things like that because you think you're the only one who's great. Quit looking down on people already." M: "That's exactly what I'm saying — I told you I didn't mean anything deep by it! I'm the one who wants this to stop."

#OptionEnglish
1女の人に誤解されてあきれているOnna no hito ni gokai sarete akireteiru"He's exasperated at being misunderstood by the woman"
2女の人に悪いことを言ったと思っているOnna no hito ni warui koto o itta to omotteiru"He thinks he said something bad to her" (he maintains he meant nothing bad — the opposite stance)
3女の人がコツを覚えないのでイライラしているOnna no hito ga kotsu o oboenai node iraira shiteiru"He's irritated she won't learn the trick" (not what the argument is about at all)
4女の人に感謝されるべきだと思っているOnna no hito ni kansha sareru beki da to omotteiru"He thinks he should be thanked by her" (too strong — he just wants the misunderstanding to end)

Why: His last line, 「深い意味で言ったんじゃないって、言ってんじゃないか。いいかげんにしてほしいのはこっちだよ」("I keep telling you I didn't mean anything by it — I'm the one who wants this to stop"), is pure exasperation at being repeatedly misread, not guilt, irritation at her skill, or a wish for gratitude.

3 問題Ⅲ — 概要理解（長め）(20点×2問)

この問題は、全体としてどんな内容かを聞く問題です。話の前に質問はありません。まず話を聞いてください。それから質問と選択肢を聞いて、1～4の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Kono mondai wa, zentai to shite donna naiyou ka o kiku mondai desu. Hanashi no mae ni shitsumon wa arimasen. Mazu hanashi o kiite kudasai. Sore kara shitsumon to sentakushi o kiite, 1~4 no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. — "This question asks about the overall content. There's no question before the talk — listen to the talk first, then the question and options, and choose the best answer."

1番 CD2, Track 32

テレビで、消防士が話しています。Terebi de, shouboushi ga hanashite imasu.

消防士：皆さん、119番に通報してから救急車が到着するまでに、どのくらいの時間を要すると思いますか。もちろん、いろいろなケースがありますが、平均6分から7分かかります。この救急車が来るまでの間に応急手当を行うことができれば、命を助けたり、その後の治療の経過にいい影響を与えたりすることができます。緊急な事態は予告なく突然やってきます。そのいざというときに適切な処置を実施するには、日頃から、応急手当に関する知識と技術を身につけておくことが大切です。Shouboushi: Minasan, hyaku juu kyuu-ban ni tsuuhou shite kara kyuukyuusha ga touchaku suru made ni, dono kurai no jikan o yousuru to omoimasu ka. Mochiron, iroiro na keesu ga arimasu ga, heikin roppun kara nanafun kakarimasu. Kono kyuukyuusha ga kuru made no aida ni oukyuu teate o okonau koto ga dekireba, inochi o tasuketari, sono ato no chiryou no keika ni ii eikyou o ataetari suru koto ga dekimasu. Kinkyuu na jitai wa yokoku naku totsuzen yatte kimasu. Sono iza to iu toki ni tekisetsu na shochi o jisshi suru ni wa, higoro kara, oukyuu teate ni kansuru chishiki to gijutsu o mi ni tsukete oku koto ga taisetsu desu.

EN translation: "Everyone, how long do you think it takes from calling 119 until an ambulance arrives? Of course cases vary, but it averages 6 to 7 minutes. If you can administer first aid during that wait, you can help save a life and positively affect the course of later treatment. Emergencies arrive suddenly, without warning. To carry out the right measures in that critical moment, it's important to build first-aid knowledge and skill day to day, in advance."

消防士は、どういうテーマで話していますか。Shouboushi wa, dou iu teema de hanashite imasu ka. — "What theme is the firefighter talking about?"

#OptionEnglish
1救急車通報の問題点Kyuukyuusha tsuuhou no mondaiten"Problems with calling for an ambulance" (the 6–7 min figure is just context, not a criticism of the system)
2適切な処置の方法Tekisetsu na shochi no houhou"The method for proper treatment" (no specific technique is actually taught here)
3緊急事態の予測Kinkyuu jitai no yosoku"Predicting emergencies" (he says the opposite — they arrive 予告なく, without warning)
4応急手当の重要性Oukyuu teate no juuyousei"The importance of first aid"

Why: Every sentence builds toward one closing claim: build first-aid knowledge and skill in advance, because it can save a life in the gap before the ambulance arrives — that's a statement about why first aid matters, not a how-to guide, a system critique, or a forecasting method.

2番 CD2, Track 33

講演会で、男の人が話しています。Kouenkai de, otoko no hito ga hanashite imasu.

男：プレッシャーに弱い部下をもったときに、その上司は、どう対処すればいいかということに悩むと思います。よい信頼関係を築いていれば、部下に自信を持たせることは、そんなに難しいことではありません。信頼している相手の前で失敗することは、そんなにプレッシャーにはならないのです。上司は部下の長所を認め、悪いところを心より心配し、指摘したり、また仕事の話だけではなく、プライベートなことにも触れて、趣味や家族のことを聞いたりすることも重要です。部下が上司を信頼できると感じ、安心して仕事をする環境を作れば、結果、部下のプレッシャーも克服されていくものです。Otoko: Puresshaa ni yowai buka o motta toki ni, sono joushi wa, dou taisho sureba ii ka to iu koto ni nayamu to omoimasu. Yoi shinrai kankei o kizuiteireba, buka ni jishin o motaseru koto wa, sonna ni muzukashii koto de wa arimasen. Shinrai shiteiru aite no mae de shippai suru koto wa, sonna ni puresshaa ni wa naranai no desu. Joushi wa buka no chousho o mitome, warui tokoro o kokoro yori shinpai shi, shiteki shitari, mata shigoto no hanashi dake de wa naku, puraibeeto na koto ni mo furete, shumi ya kazoku no koto o kiitari suru koto mo juuyou desu. Buka ga joushi o shinrai dekiru to kanji, anshin shite shigoto o suru kankyou o tsukureba, kekka, buka no puresshaa mo kokufuku sareteiku mono desu.

EN translation: "When you have a subordinate who's weak under pressure, I think the boss struggles with how to handle it. If you've built a good relationship of trust, giving that subordinate confidence isn't so difficult — failing in front of someone you trust doesn't create that much pressure to begin with. It's important for the boss to recognize the subordinate's strengths, genuinely care about and point out their weak points, and touch on private matters too — not just work — like hobbies and family. If you create an environment where the subordinate feels they can trust their boss and can work with peace of mind, their pressure will, as a result, naturally be overcome."

男の人は、何について話していますか。Otoko no hito wa, nani ni tsuite hanashite imasu ka. — "What is the man talking about?"

#OptionEnglish
1部下のプレッシャーの原因を突き止める方法Buka no puresshaa no gen'in o tsukitomeru houhou"How to pinpoint the cause of a subordinate's pressure" (the cause — fear of failing in front of an untrusted boss — is stated, not something to investigate)
2プレッシャーに弱い部下を信頼するコツPuresshaa ni yowai buka o shinrai suru kotsu"The trick to trusting a subordinate weak under pressure" (inverts it — it's the boss who must be trusted, not the other way round)
3部下にプレッシャーを克服させるためのアドバイスBuka ni puresshaa o kokufuku saseru tame no adobaisu"Advice for helping a subordinate overcome pressure"
4プレッシャーを感じない部下への対応Puresshaa o kanjinai buka e no taiou"How to deal with a subordinate who feels no pressure" (the whole talk assumes the opposite — a subordinate who IS weak under pressure)

Why: The talk is framed from the start as guidance for a boss (上司) on how to help a pressure-sensitive subordinate (部下), closing with 「部下のプレッシャーも克服されていくものです」("the subordinate's pressure will be overcome") — practical advice aimed at the boss's actions, using this chapter's ネック／克服する-style vocabulary.
