1 Vocabulary List — 会社や学校で使われるカタカナ語

会社や学校で使われるカタカナ語を覚えよう！Kaisha ya gakkou de tsukawareru katakana-go o oboeyou! — "Let's learn the katakana words used at work and school!" Chapter 4 shifts from listening strategy (Chapters 1–3) to listening vocabulary — words so common on the N1 聴解 audio that not knowing them costs you the question even if you understood the grammar perfectly.

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

4 exercises from p.53, drilling the words above in context (即時応答／課題理解 style). Play the referenced CD track alongside this page (see note in the header), or use these as pure read-and-recall drills if you don't have the audio to hand. Answers confirmed from p.122–123.

1番 CD2, Track 10

クライアントから、価格がなんとかならないかと言われてるんですが…。Kuraianto kara, kakaku ga nantoka naranai ka to iwareterun desu ga…

EN: "The client is asking if we can't do something about the price…"   HI: ग्राहक कह रहा है कि क्या कीमत के बारे में कुछ नहीं किया जा सकता…   GU: ક્લાયન્ટ કહી રહ્યા છે કે કિંમત વિશે કંઈક કરી શકાય કે નહીં…

#ResponseEnglish
1ボーダーラインまで、あと少しですね。Boodaarain made, ato sukoshi desu ne."We're almost at the cutoff line." (wrong topic — no borderline was mentioned)
2アピールが足りないということですか。Apiiru ga tarinai to iu koto desu ka."Do you mean we're not promoting it enough?" (mishears "price" as a promotion problem)
3これ以上、コストダウンできません。Kore ijou, kosuto daun dekimasen."We can't cut costs any further than this."

Why: 価格がなんとかならないか ("can't something be done about the price") is a request to lower the price, i.e. to コストダウンする. Only option 3 directly answers a price complaint; 1 and 2 answer different, unasked questions (deadline/borderline and promotion effort).

2番 CD2, Track 11

キャンペーンの効果があんまりないようですね…。Kyanpeen no kouka ga anmari nai you desu ne…

EN: "It seems the campaign isn't having much effect…"   HI: लगता है कैंपेन का ज़्यादा असर नहीं हो रहा…   GU: લાગે છે કે ઝુંબેશની ખાસ અસર થઈ નથી…

#ResponseEnglish
1新しいのをうったほうがいいんじゃないでしょうか。Atarashii no o utta hou ga ii n ja nai deshou ka."Wouldn't it be better to launch a new one?"
2それはアイデアになかったことですが。Sore wa aidea ni nakatta koto desu ga."That wasn't part of the idea, but…" (non sequitur)
3予想が外れるんじゃないでしょうか。Yosou ga hazureru n ja nai deshou ka."Wouldn't the forecast turn out to be wrong?" (doesn't respond to the complaint)

Why: A complaint that the current campaign isn't working invites a constructive next step — replacing it with a new campaign (option 1). 2 and 3 don't propose any solution to the stated problem.

3番 CD2, Track 12

会社で、男の人と女の人が話しています。男の人は、新商品の売り上げが悪い原因は何だと言っていますか。Kaisha de, otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito wa, shinshouhin no uriage ga warui gen'in wa nan da to itte imasu ka.

EN: A man and woman are talking at a company. What does the man say is the reason the new product's sales are poor?   HI: कंपनी में एक पुरुष और महिला बात कर रहे हैं। पुरुष के अनुसार नए उत्पाद की बिक्री खराब होने का कारण क्या है?   GU: કંપનીમાં એક પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી વાત કરે છે. પુરુષના મતે નવા ઉત્પાદનનું વેચાણ ખરાબ હોવાનું કારણ શું છે?

男：売り上げ…これほど伸びないとは思っていませんでした…。Otoko: Uriage… kore hodo nobinai to wa omotte imasen deshita…
女：他社では真似ができないいい企画だと思ったんだけどねえ。うーん、デザインの奇抜さには問題ないよね。Onna: Tasha de wa mane ga dekinai ii kikaku da to omottan da kedo nee. Uun, dezain no kibatsusa ni wa mondai nai yo ne.
男：はい、かなり変わったデザインですが、アンケートによると、若者には特にそこが支持されてますので。あのー、宣伝方法をもう少し考えたほうがよかったように思います。キャンペーンをもう少し早くからしたほうがよかったのかもしれませんが、それよりも、もっと性能をわかりやすく提示すべきだったと思います。Otoko: Hai, kanari kawatta dezain desu ga, ankeeto ni yoru to, wakamono ni wa toku ni soko ga shiji saretemasu node. Anoo, senden houhou o mou sukoshi kangaeta hou ga yokatta you ni omoimasu. Kyanpeen o mou sukoshi hayaku kara shita hou ga yokatta no kamo shiremasen ga, sore yori mo, motto seinou o wakariyasuku teiji subeki datta to omoimasu.
女：そう、じゃ、広告を見直さないと…。Onna: Sou, ja, koukoku o minaosanai to…

EN translation: M: "Sales… I didn't think they'd grow this little…" W: "I thought it was a great plan other companies couldn't copy. Hmm, the design's unconventionalness isn't the problem, right?" M: "Right, it's quite an unusual design, and the survey shows young people especially support that. Um, I think we should've thought a bit more about the promotion method. Starting the campaign a bit earlier might have helped, but more than that, I think we should have presented the performance more clearly." W: "I see — then we need to rethink the advertising…"

#OptionEnglish
1商品のデザインがよくなかったShouhin no dezain ga yokunakatta"The product's design wasn't good" (explicitly ruled out — no problem with the design)
2商品の性能がよくなかったShouhin no seinou ga yokunakatta"The product's performance wasn't good" (performance itself is fine — it just wasn't explained clearly)
3キャンペーンの時期が早すぎたKyanpeen no jiki ga hayasugita"The campaign timing was too early" (reverses what was said — starting earlier was the missed opportunity, not the mistake)
4商品のアピールに問題があったShouhin no apiiru ni mondai ga atta"There was a problem with how the product was promoted/appealed"

Why: The man repeatedly points to 宣伝方法 (promotion method) and 性能をわかりやすく提示すべきだった (should have presented the performance more clearly/appealingly) — both are about アピール (promotion), not the design or the performance itself. Option 4 is the only one matching both specifics he raises.

4番 CD2, Track 13

就職ガイダンスで、女の人が話しています。Shuushoku gaidansu de, onna no hito ga hanashite imasu.

女：日本語の履歴書とは違い、英文で書くレジメには、決まった様式というのはありません。そのため、自分で書きやすいようなフォーマットを考えて作成することができます。キーポイントは、書面を通して、どのように自己アピールするかということです。基本的なガイドラインですが、通常は1枚にまとめ、多くとも2枚までにします。字の大きさやレイアウトにも気を配るようにします。家族構成などは、個人情報になりますので書きません。日本語の履歴書の用紙には、趣味などを書く欄もありますが、仕事に関係のない情報は必要ありません。スペルや文法の間違いを犯さないのは、言うまでもありません。Onna: Nihongo no rirekisho to wa chigai, eibun de kaku rejume ni wa, kimatta youshiki to iu no wa arimasen. Sono tame, jibun de kakiyasui you na foomatto o kangaete sakusei suru koto ga dekimasu. Kii pointo wa, shomen o tooshite, dono you ni jiko apiiru suru ka to iu koto desu. Kihonteki na gaidorain desu ga, tsuujou wa ichi mai ni matome, ooku tomo ni mai made ni shimasu. Ji no ookisa ya reiauto ni mo ki o kubaru you ni shimasu. Kazoku kousei nado wa, kojin jouhou ni narimasu node kakimasen. Nihongo no rirekisho no youshi ni wa, shumi nado o kaku ran mo arimasu ga, shigoto ni kankei no nai jouhou wa hitsuyou arimasen. Superu ya bunpou no machigai o okasanai no wa, iu made mo arimasen.

EN translation: "Unlike a Japanese résumé, there's no fixed format for an English-language resume, so you can design a format that's easy for you to write. The key point is how you present yourself through the written page. As a basic guideline, keep it to one page, at most two. Also pay attention to font size and layout. Things like family composition are personal information, so don't write them. Japanese résumé forms have a space for hobbies, but information unrelated to the job isn't necessary. Needless to say, don't make spelling or grammar mistakes."

女の人は、何について話していますか。Onna no hito wa, nani ni tsuite hanashite imasu ka. — "What is the woman talking about?"

#OptionEnglish
1日本語でレジメを書く場合の注意点Nihongo de rejume o kaku baai no chuuiten"Points to note when writing a resume in Japanese" (the talk is about English, not Japanese, résumés)
2英文で履歴書を書く場合のガイドラインEibun de rirekisho o kaku baai no gaidorain"Guidelines for writing a résumé in English"
3履歴書での自己アピールの仕方Rirekisho de no jiko apiiru no shikata"How to promote yourself in a résumé" (only one part of the talk, not the whole topic)
4履歴書に書くべき情報Rirekisho ni kaku beki jouhou"Information that should be written in a résumé" (also only part of the talk)

Why: The talk opens by contrasting 日本語の履歴書 with 英文で書くレジメ and covers format, length, layout, and content rules specifically for the English-language version — option 2 is the only one broad enough to cover the whole talk (format + length + layout + what to omit), while 3 and 4 each capture only one sub-point.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

These katakana words often get confused with each other because they overlap in English — but each has a distinct role in Japanese business/school speech:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
アピールapiiru to promote, to highlight About drawing attention to a product/quality — a communication act. 商品をアピールする — presenting a product's strengths to an audience.
プレゼンテーションpurezenteeshon a formal presentation A specific event/format (slides, a meeting) — the vehicle, not the act of promoting. プレゼンで緊張した — describing the event itself, not the persuading.
コンセプトkonseputo the underlying idea/concept Abstract — the idea behind a design or plan, decided before execution. デザインのコンセプト — what a design is trying to express.
キャンペーンkyanpeen a promotional campaign Concrete — the actual promotional push/event that puts the コンセプト into action. キャンペーンをうつ — launching the concrete promotional activity.
カンファレンス／コンファレンスkanfarensu / konfarensu a conference A formal multi-person meeting/event — larger scale than 打ち合わせ (a working meeting). Describing an industry-wide or large-scale meeting.
ゼミ（ナール）zemi(naaru) a university seminar course An ongoing academic course under one professor, taken over a term. 田中先生のゼミをとる — enrolling in an ongoing academic seminar.
セミナーseminaa a seminar / workshop event A one-off or short event (often for professionals), not a term-long course. セミナーを開催する — holding a single workshop-style event.
Exam trap: リストラ sounds like it should mean "restart" or "restructuring" broadly, but in everyday Japanese it has narrowed to specifically mean being laid off / downsized (リストラにあう) — don't confuse it with 再起動する (to restart a machine) just because both come from "re-" in English.
