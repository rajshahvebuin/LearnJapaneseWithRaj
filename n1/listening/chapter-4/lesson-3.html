1 Vocabulary List — 言い換え

選択肢では言い換えられている場合が多いので注意しよう！Sentakushi de wa iikaerarete iru baai ga ooi node chuui shiyou! — "Watch out — the answer options often paraphrase what was actually said!" N1 聴解 rarely repeats the exact words from the audio in its answer options — instead the correct option restates the same idea with a different word. This lesson pairs each katakana verb/word with its native-Japanese paraphrase so you can recognize both directions instantly. ＊一辺大声朗読一辺記 Ichihen ooogoe rouoku ippen ki — "read aloud as you memorize."

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

4 exercises from p.57, drilling the paraphrase pairs above. Play the referenced CD track alongside this page (see note in the header), or use these as pure read-and-recall drills if you don't have the audio to hand. Answers confirmed from p.126–127.

1番 CD2, Track 18

少しお酒を控えたほうがいいんじゃありませんか。Sukoshi osake o hikaeta hou ga ii n ja arimasen ka.

EN: "Wouldn't it be better to cut back on drinking a bit?"   HI: क्या थोड़ी शराब कम करना बेहतर नहीं होगा?   GU: શું થોડો દારૂ ઓછો કરવો સારું ન રહે?

#ResponseEnglish
1飲む機会は、あることはあるんですが…。Nomu kikai wa, aru koto wa arun desu ga…"Well, I do have occasions to drink, but…" (dodges the question — doesn't say what he actually does)
2飲まないというわけではありませんが…。Nomanai to iu wake de wa arimasen ga…"It's not that I don't drink, but…" (also incomplete/evasive)
3できるだけ飲まないようにしているんですが…。Dekiru dake nomanai you ni shite irun desu ga…"I'm trying not to drink as much as possible, but…"

Why: This is the whole lesson's point in miniature: 控える means できるだけ〜しないようにする, and only option 3 uses that exact paraphrase to confirm he's already doing what was suggested. 1 and 2 talk around the issue without confirming or denying it.

2番 CD2, Track 19

今日、どういうわけかお客が殺到して大変だったよ。Kyou, dou iu wake ka okyaku ga sattou shite taihen datta yo.

EN: "Today, for some reason customers flooded in and it was rough."   HI: आज पता नहीं क्यों ग्राहक अचानक बहुत ज़्यादा आ गए, बहुत मुश्किल हो गई।   GU: આજે કોણ જાણે કેમ ગ્રાહકો અચાનક ધસી આવ્યા, ખૂબ મુશ્કેલ થઈ ગયું.

#ResponseEnglish
1忙しいのはいいことじゃない？Isogashii no wa ii koto ja nai?"Isn't being busy a good thing (for business)?"
2どういうつもりなんだろうね。Dou iu tsumori nan darou ne."I wonder what they meant by that." (treats it as an intentional act, which it wasn't)
3え？それでお客さん、どうなったの？E? Sore de okyakusan, dou natta no?"Huh? So what happened to the customer(s)?" (misreads 殺到 as a problem needing resolution, e.g. an accident)

Why: 殺到する just means a sudden rush/flood of people — a busy-but-good problem for a shop, not a crisis. Only option 1 reframes it positively, matching what "customers flooding in" naturally implies for a business.

3番 CD2, Track 20

男の人が、国際運転免許証の申請について窓口の人と話しています。男の人はこれからまず何をしなければなりませんか。Otoko no hito ga, kokusai unten menkyoshou no shinsei ni tsuite madoguchi no hito to hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito wa kore kara mazu nani o shinakereba narimasen ka.

EN: A man is talking to a counter clerk about applying for an international driving permit. What must he do first from here?   HI: एक पुरुष अंतरराष्ट्रीय ड्राइविंग परमिट के आवेदन के बारे में काउंटर पर बात कर रहा है। उसे अब सबसे पहले क्या करना होगा?   GU: એક પુરુષ આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય ડ્રાઇવિંગ પરમિટની અરજી વિશે કાઉન્ટર પર વાત કરે છે. તેણે હવે સૌથી પહેલાં શું કરવું પડશે?

男：あのー、国際運転免許証の申請をしたいんですが…。Otoko: Anoo, kokusai unten menkyoshou no shinsei o shitain desu ga…
女：では、この用紙に必要事項を記入してください。免許証とパスポートと写真が必要ですが、お持ちですか。Onna: Dewa, kono youshi ni hitsuyou jikou o kinyuu shite kudasai. Menkyoshou to pasupooto to shashin ga hitsuyou desu ga, omochi desu ka.
男：はい。あ、これは、前にもらって書き込んできたものがあります。Otoko: Hai. A, kore wa, mae ni moratte kakikonde kita mono ga arimasu.
女：ちょっと写真を見せてください。ああ、ちょっと大きいですね。あちらに器具がありますから、カットしてください。それから、3000円の証紙を購入して、全部まとめて3番の窓口へ持っていってください。写真はこちらで貼付しますので、そのままお出しください。Onna: Chotto shashin o misete kudasai. Aa, chotto ookii desu ne. Achira ni kigu ga arimasu kara, katto shite kudasai. Sore kara, sanzen'en no shoushi o kounyuu shite, zenbu matomete sanban no madoguchi e motte itte kudasai. Shashin wa kochira de choufu shimasu node, sono mama o-dashi kudasai.
男：わかりました。Otoko: Wakarimashita.

EN translation: M: "Um, I'd like to apply for an international driving permit..." W: "Then please fill in the required items on this form. You'll need your license, passport, and a photo — do you have them?" M: "Yes. Oh, I already have this filled in from before." W: "Let me see the photo... ah, it's a bit too big. There's a device over there — please cut it. Then buy a 3000-yen certificate stamp, and take everything together to window 3. We'll affix the photo here, so just hand it over as is." M: "Understood."

#OptionEnglish
1用紙に必要事項を書くYoushi ni hitsuyou jikou o kaku"Write the required items on the form" (already done — 前にもらって書き込んできた)
2写真を切るShashin o kiru"Cut the photo (down to size)"
33番の窓口へ行くSanban no madoguchi e iku"Go to window 3" (comes after buying the certificate stamp, not first)
4写真を貼るShashin o haru"Paste the photo" (the clerk says she'll do this — 貼付する — not the man)

Why: The form is already filled in, so 1 is done. The clerk's very next instruction is カットしてください ("please cut it") because the photo is too big — that's the immediate next action for the man. Buying the stamp and going to window 3 come after; affixing the photo (4) is the clerk's job, using this lesson's 貼付する＝貼る pair.

4番 CD2, Track 21

テレビで、サッカーの選手がインタビューを受けています。Terebi de, sakkaa no senshu ga intabyuu o ukete imasu.

女：イギリスでは、いろいろと苦労なさったということを聞きましたが…。Onna: Igirisu de wa, iroiro to kurou nasatta to iu koto o kikimashita ga…
男：体格的に違うというのは初めからわかっていたことですし、それほど苦に思ったことはないです。小さいころからそのプレーをすることができましたし、僕は、かえって、この小さいことがメリットだと思っています。ただ、体力の維持には常に細心の注意を払っていました。トレーニングはつらくなかったといえばうそになりますが、それより何より、言葉がネックで…。大変でしたね。チームのメンバーがいろいろと手助けしてくれましたが、わからないことだらけで、自己嫌悪に陥ることも度々でした。それについては結局、克服できずに帰ってきましたが…ハハハ。Otoko: Taikakuteki ni chigau to iu no wa hajime kara wakatteita koto desu shi, sore hodo ku ni omotta koto wa nai desu. Chiisai koro kara sono puree o suru koto ga dekimashita shi, boku wa, kaette, kono chiisai koto ga meritto da to omotte imasu. Tada, tairyoku no iji ni wa tsune ni saishin no chuui o haratte imashita. Toreeningu wa tsurakunakatta to ieba uso ni narimasu ga, sore yori nani yori, kotoba ga nekku de…. Taihen deshita ne. Chiimu no menbaa ga iroiro to tetsudatte kuremashita ga, wakaranai koto darake de, jiko ken'o ni ochiiru koto mo tabitabi deshita. Sore ni tsuite wa kekkyoku, kokufuku dekizu ni kaette kimashita ga… hahaha.

EN translation: W: "I heard you went through a lot of hardship in England..." M: "The physical difference was something I knew from the start, so it wasn't that hard for me. I've been able to play this way since I was small, and I actually think being small is an advantage. That said, I always paid the utmost attention to maintaining my fitness. It would be a lie to say training wasn't tough, but more than anything, language was the obstacle... It was rough — my teammates helped me a lot, but I was constantly confused, and I often fell into self-loathing over it. In the end, I never managed to overcome that before coming home... haha."

この選手はどういうことを言っていますか。Kono senshu wa dou iu koto o itte imasu ka. — "What is this player saying?"

#OptionEnglish
1体格的に劣っていることが悩みだったTaikakuteki ni ototte iru koto ga nayami datta"Being physically inferior was his worry" (explicitly denied — he calls his size a merit)
2体力を維持するのは大変ではなかったTairyoku o iji suru no wa taihen de wa nakatta"Maintaining stamina wasn't hard" (reverses 常に細心の注意を払っていた — it took constant careful effort)
3言葉ができないことが、大きい問題だったKotoba ga dekinai koto ga, ookii mondai datta"Not being able to speak the language was a big problem"
4チームのメンバーが不親切だったChiimu no menbaa ga fushinsetsu datta"His teammates were unkind" (reverses it — they helped him a lot)

Why: He explicitly says 「言葉がネックで」("language was the obstacle") — this lesson's ネック＝障害 pair stated directly — and describes the resulting confusion and self-loathing as something he never overcame. Options 1, 2, and 4 each invert something he actually said.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A few of this lesson's paraphrase pairs are easy to mix up with near-neighbors — worth isolating on their own:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
控えるhikaeru to cut back on, refrain from Voluntary self-restraint on something you'd otherwise do (drinking, salt) お酒を控える, 塩分を控える — moderating one's own habit
回避するkaihi suru to avoid, evade Actively steering away from a danger/responsibility, not just moderating a habit リスクを回避する, 責任を回避する — dodging something external
妨げるsamatageru to hinder, obstruct (something else) The subject gets in the way of someone/something else's progress 進行を妨げる, 安眠を妨げられる — an external obstruction imposed on someone
ネックになるnekku ni naru to become a bottleneck/obstacle A single specific factor that blocks progress on something else — narrower and more personal than 妨げる's general "interference" 学歴のないことがネックになる, 言葉がネックだった — naming the one root blocker
添付するtenpu suru to attach (a file/document) Adding a separate item alongside a main document — digital/paperwork sense 領収書を添付する — attaching a receipt to a report or email
貼付するchoufu suru to affix, stick on Physically pasting something onto a surface — a physical, not digital, action 写真を貼付する — physically gluing a photo onto a form
Exam trap: This whole lesson's premise is the trap: N1 聴解 options are written to paraphrase the audio, not quote it. If you're listening for the exact katakana word you memorized and it never comes up in the options, that's normal — check whether a native-Japanese synonym from this list is doing the same job instead.
