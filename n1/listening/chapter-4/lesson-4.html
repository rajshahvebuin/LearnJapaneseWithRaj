1 Vocabulary List — 会話によく使われる副詞や表現

会話によく使われる副詞や特別な表現を覚えよう！Kaiwa ni yoku tsukawareru fukushi ya tokubetsu na hyougen o oboeyou! — "Let's learn adverbs and special expressions often used in conversation!" These mood/manner adverbs and fixed expressions carry a scene's emotional tone in a single word — miss one and you'll misjudge whether a speaker is relieved, furious, or exhausted.

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

4 exercises from p.59, drilling the mood adverbs and expressions above. Play the referenced CD track alongside this page (see note in the header), or use these as pure read-and-recall drills if you don't have the audio to hand. Answers confirmed from p.128–129.

1番 CD2, Track 22

雨に降られるわ、電車に乗り遅れるわで、今日はついてなかったよ。Ame ni furareru wa, densha ni noriokureru wa de, kyou wa tsuiteinakatta yo.

EN: "Between getting rained on and missing the train, today was just unlucky."   HI: बारिश में भीगना और ट्रेन छूटना — आज तो किस्मत ही ख़राब थी।   GU: વરસાદમાં પલળી ગયો અને ટ્રેન ચૂકી ગયો — આજે તો કિસ્મત જ ખરાબ હતી.

#ResponseEnglish
1それは、さんざんだったね。Sore wa, sanzan datta ne."That sounds like an awful day."
2それは、かっとなるね。Sore wa, katto naru ne."That would make you flare up in anger." (nothing in the story is about anger)
3それは、はらはらするね。Sore wa, harahara suru ne."That would make you nervous/on edge." (harahara is for suspense, not a string of bad luck)

Why: A string of unrelated bad luck (rain, missed train) is exactly what さんざん describes (a generally awful outcome/experience). かっと is specifically anger, and はらはら is specifically nervous suspense — neither matches "a bad day" in general.

2番 CD2, Track 23

あー、まだこんなにやらないといけないんですか。もうへとへとですよ。Aa, mada konna ni yaranai to ikenain desu ka. Mou hetoheto desu yo.

EN: "Ugh, do I still have to do this much? I'm already exhausted."   HI: उफ़, क्या अभी इतना और करना बाकी है? मैं तो पहले ही थक चुका हूँ।   GU: ઓહ, હજી આટલું બધું કરવાનું બાકી છે? હું તો પહેલેથી જ થાકી ગયો છું.

#ResponseEnglish
1いざとなったら、お願いします。Iza to nattara, onegaishimasu."If it comes to it, I'll ask you for help." (doesn't address the exhaustion)
2きりのいいところで休んでください。Kiri no ii tokoro de yasunde kudasai."Please take a break once you reach a good stopping point."
3くよくよしないでください。Kuyokuyo shinaide kudasai."Please don't fret about it." (くよくよ is for anxious rumination, not physical exhaustion)

Why: The complaint is physical tiredness (へとへと), so the natural response offers rest — きりのいいところで休んでください directly addresses that. 1 answers a different, hypothetical situation; 3 responds to worry, not fatigue.

3番 CD2, Track 24

会社で、男の人と女の人が話しています。女の人はこれから何をしなければなりませんか。Kaisha de, otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa kore kara nani o shinakereba narimasen ka.

EN: A man and woman are talking at a company. What must the woman do from now?   HI: कंपनी में एक पुरुष और महिला बात कर रहे हैं। महिला को अब आगे क्या करना होगा?   GU: કંપનીમાં એક પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી વાત કરે છે. સ્ત્રીએ હવે શું કરવું પડશે?

女：来月発売の新商品のポスターの見本ですが、ご覧いただけましたでしょうか。Onna: Raigetsu hatsubai no shinshouhin no posutaa no mihon desu ga, goran itadakemashita deshou ka.
男：あ、これね。ちょっと商品の写真が小さいんじゃないかと思うんだけど、紹介文の文字数が多いから、仕方ないね。Otoko: A, kore ne. Chotto shouhin no shashin ga chiisain ja nai ka to omou n da kedo, shoukaibun no mojisuu ga ooi kara, shikata nai ne.
女：では、これで入稿してよろしいでしょうか。Onna: Dewa, kore de nyuukou shite yoroshii deshou ka.
男：写真の色がちょっと暗いなあ。ま、欲を言えばきりがないから、これでいっか。あれ？これ、間違ってないか。この販売予定日。Otoko: Shashin no iro ga chotto kurai naa. Ma, yoku o ieba kiri ga nai kara, kore de ikka. Are? Kore, machigattenai ka. Kono hanbai yotei bi.
女：いえ、3月1日で間違いないですが…あー、曜日が違ってます。申し訳ありません。Onna: Ie, sangatsu tsuitachi de machigai nai desu ga… aa, youbi ga chigattemasu. Moushiwake arimasen.
男：じゃ、訂正ついでに、写真の色合いだけど、やっぱり、もう少し明るいのにして。Otoko: Ja, teisei tsuide ni, shashin no iroai dake do, yappari, mou sukoshi akarui no ni shite.
女：はい、それは印刷所で調整してもらえますので、忘れずに伝えておきます。Onna: Hai, sore wa insatsujo de chousei shite moraemasu node, wasurezu ni tsutaete okimasu.
男：じゃ、よろしく。Otoko: Ja, yoroshiku.

EN translation: W: "This is the sample poster for next month's new product launch — have you had a look?" M: "Ah, this one. The product photo might be a bit small, but since the description text is long, it can't be helped." W: "Then shall I send this to print as is?" M: "The photo color is a bit dark. Well, there's no end to wanting more (きりがない), so let's go with it. Huh, wait — isn't this wrong? This scheduled release date." W: "No, March 1st is correct, but... ah, the day of the week is wrong. I'm sorry." M: "Then while you're correcting it, let's make the photo's coloring a bit brighter after all." W: "Yes, the print shop can adjust that — I'll be sure to tell them." M: "Alright, thanks."

#OptionEnglish
1写真を拡大するShashin o kakudai suru"Enlarge the photo" (the small photo was accepted as unavoidable — 仕方ない)
2文字数を変更するMojisuu o henkou suru"Change the character count" (also accepted as-is)
3販売予定日を訂正するHanbai yotei bi o teisei suru"Correct the scheduled release date"
4写真の色を修正するShashin no iro o shuusei suru"Fix the photo's color" (she says the print shop — 印刷所 — will do this, not her)

Why: The man accepts the small photo and long text as きりがない ("no end to perfectionism") but catches a real error: the day-of-week on the release date is wrong, which the woman herself apologizes for and must fix. The color adjustment (4) is explicitly delegated to 印刷所 (the print shop), not done by her.

4番 CD2, Track 25

女の人が息子について話しています。Onna no hito ga musuko ni tsuite hanashite imasu.

女：小さいときは、周りの者をよく笑わせる明るい子どもでしたが、いじめにあってから、自分の殻に閉じこもるようになり…学校もよく休むようになりました。3年生になってからは完全な登校拒否になり、家に引きこもるようになりました。家では、私の体調を気遣ってくれたり、妹の宿題を手伝ったりする、心優しい子です。主人にどんなに怒鳴られても、じっと耐えています。主人には、私が甘やかして育てたからだと責められるし、本当にどうしていいかわからなくて、毎日悩んでいます。Onna: Chiisai toki wa, mawari no mono o yoku warawaseru akarui kodomo deshita ga, ijime ni atte kara, jibun no kara ni tojikomoru you ni nari… gakkou mo yoku yasumu you ni narimashita. San-nensei ni natte kara wa kanzen na toukoukyohi ni nari, ie ni hikikomoru you ni narimashita. Ie de wa, watashi no taichou o kizukatte kuretari, imouto no shukudai o tetsudattari suru, kokoro yasashii ko desu. Shujin ni donna ni donararetemo, jitto taete imasu. Shujin ni wa, watashi ga amayakashite sodateta kara da to semerarerushi, hontou ni dou shite ii ka wakaranakute, mainichi nayande imasu.

EN translation: "When he was little, he was a cheerful child who often made those around him laugh, but after being bullied he started shutting himself in his own shell... and began missing school often too. Once he became a third-year student he completely refused to go to school and became a homebound recluse. At home, he's a kind-hearted child who worries about my health and helps his little sister with homework. No matter how much his father yells at him, he just quietly endures it. My husband blames me, saying it's because I spoiled him — I really don't know what to do, and I worry about it every day."

女の人の息子はどういう人ですか。Onna no hito no musuko wa dou iu hito desu ka. — "What kind of person is the woman's son?"

#OptionEnglish
1周りの空気が読めない人Mawari no kuuki ga yomenai hito"Someone who can't read the room" (nothing in the story suggests this — he's described as considerate)
2わがままで我慢が足りない人Wagamama de gaman ga tarinai hito"Selfish and lacking patience" (reverses it — he endures his father's yelling じっと耐えている)
3怒りっぽく暗い人Okorippoku kurai hito"Quick to anger and gloomy" (not supported — he's described as kind and considerate at home)
4社会生活がうまく送れない人Shakai seikatsu ga umaku okurenai hito"Someone who can't function well in social life"

Why: The core facts are 登校拒否 (school refusal) and 引きこもる (withdrawing/becoming a recluse) — both about failing to function in the outside/social world, while at home he remains gentle and considerate. Only option 4 captures that split between social withdrawal and a kind home personality; 1, 2, and 3 all contradict details explicitly given.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several of today's mood adverbs describe negative emotional states and are easy to swap for each other — compare them directly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
さんざんsanzan terribly, an awful experience overall Sums up a whole bad experience/outcome after the fact — general and retrospective Describing a string of bad luck or a rough day as a whole
はらはらharahara nervous, on edge (suspense) A specific worried anticipation while something risky is happening — not retrospective Watching someone do something dangerous/uncertain in real time
かっとkatto flaring up suddenly in anger A sudden, momentary anger spike — an event, not a lasting state Describing a quick temper (性格) or a single outburst
かんかんkankan being furious (sustained state) A lasting, intense state of anger someone is currently "in" — often with になっている Describing someone who is currently, visibly furious
くよくよkuyokuyo fretting, dwelling on something uselessly About unproductive worry over something already unchangeable Telling someone to stop dwelling on a past mistake
へとへとhetoheto physically exhausted Bodily fatigue from exertion — not an emotional state at all After physical labor or a long task
Exam trap: 空気を読む (Exercise 4番's distractor option) is one of the most commonly tested "personality" set expressions in N1 聴解 — but here it's a trap precisely because the audio gives no evidence for or against it. When an option introduces a trait never actually discussed, that absence itself is the tell that it's wrong, however plausible-sounding the phrase is on its own.
