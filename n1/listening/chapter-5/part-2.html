1 問題Ⅲ — 概要理解 (Gaiyou rikai — "gist comprehension")

20点×2問 + 5点×4問 (this test prints it as 5点×4問, 20 points total). この問題は、全体としてどんな内容かを聞く問題です。話の前に質問はありません。まず話を聞いてください。それから、質問と選択肢を聞いて、1～4の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Kono mondai wa, zentai to shite donna naiyou ka o kiku mondai desu. Hanashi no mae ni shitsumon wa arimasen. Mazu hanashi o kiite kudasai. Sore kara, shitsumon to sentakushi o kiite, ichi kara yon no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: No question is given beforehand — you hear the whole talk first, then the question and options are read aloud (nothing is printed on the page for this section). You're listening for the speaker's overall point or stance. HI: पहले पूरा भाषण सुनें, प्रश्न पहले से नहीं बताया जाता — फिर प्रश्न और विकल्प सुनकर उत्तर चुनें। GU: પહેલાં આખું ભાષણ સાંભળો, પ્રશ્ન અગાઉથી અપાતો નથી — પછી પ્રશ્ન અને વિકલ્પો સાંભળીને જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

男：新入社員が会社訪問を始める時期ですが、御社では、どのような人材を求めますか。Shinnyuu shain ga kaisha houmon o hajimeru jiki desu ga, onsha de wa, dono you na jinzai o motomemasu ka.

女：そうですね…今の子たちは問題に答えることにずいぶん慣れていますよね。お勉強もそういったものが多いんじゃないでしょうか。よくお勉強して、テストの点もいいかもしれませんが、問題が出されるのを待っているんですよね。早く出して、答えるからって。それじゃ困るんです。問題は自分で見つけないとね。で、それから、その解決法も考える、そういうことができる人を求めています。Sou desu ne... ima no kotachi wa mondai ni kotaeru koto ni zuibun narete imasu yo ne. Obenkyou mo sou itta mono ga ooi n ja nai deshou ka. Yoku obenkyou shite, tesuto no ten mo ii kamo shiremasen ga, mondai ga dasareru no o matte iru n desu yo ne. Hayaku dashite, kotaeru kara tte. Sore ja komaru n desu. Mondai wa jibun de mitsukenai to ne. De, sore kara, sono kaiketsuhou mo kangaeru, sou iu koto ga dekiru hito o motomete imasu.

Q: 女の人は新入社員に何を求めていますか。Onna no hito wa shinnyuu shain ni nani o motomete imasu ka. — EN: What does the woman look for in new employees?

#ChoiceMeaning
1問題に答えることに慣れていることMondai ni kotaeru koto ni narete iru kotobeing used to answering questions that are given to them
2自分で問題点や解決法を見出せる力Jibun de mondaiten ya kaiketsuhou o miidaseru chikarathe ability to find problems and their solutions on their own
3どのような問題にも答えられる知識Dono you na mondai ni mo kotaerareru chishikiknowledge broad enough to answer any question
4できるだけ早く問題に答えられる力Dekiru dake hayaku mondai ni kotaerareru chikarathe ability to answer questions as fast as possible

Why: She's actually criticizing option 1 (today's students are too used to being handed a ready-made question) as the wrong skill. Her real requirement is 「問題は自分で見つけないとね…その解決法も考える」— finding the problem yourself and thinking through a solution, i.e. option 2.

Q: 男の人はどんな考えを持っていますか。Otoko no hito wa donna kangae o motte imasu ka. — EN: What view does the man hold?

#ChoiceMeaning
1もう男女の区別をするべきではないMou danjo no kubetsu o suru beki de wa naimen and women shouldn't be distinguished anymore
2男には男の、女には女の役割があるOtoko ni wa otoko no, onna ni wa onna no yakuwari ga arumen have a man's role, women have a woman's role
3男と女は逆転していくのかもしれないOtoko to onna wa gyakuten shite iku no kamo shirenaimen's and women's roles may be reversing
4女は男のすることに口出しすべきではないOnna wa otoko no suru koto ni kuchidashi subeki de wa naiwomen shouldn't meddle in what men do

Why: His closing line is the thesis: 「それぞれのやり方とか役割があって生きているっていうのがさ、いいなあ」— he admires each sex quietly keeping to its own role (women silently knowing everything, men silently doing their duty), which is option 2. He's not arguing gender roles should vanish (1) or reverse (3), and the "women knew but stayed silent" detail is admiration for restraint, not a claim that women shouldn't comment (4).

男：おもちゃ作ってるって言うとね、みんな、木のおもちゃですかって聞くんだよね。木のおもちゃだって、色が塗ってあるものなんかは、石油使ってるわけだし、たくさんあっても、古くからあるものをただ真似してるものも結構あるんですよね。そういうのは、オリジナリティに欠けると思うし。まあ、だからって、アイデアいっぱいならいいか、ってことになるわけで。僕はね、ほら、子どものときって何かに夢中になって遊ぶじゃないですか、経験ないですか？ そういうね、夢中になれるのが、いいおもちゃだと思う。Omocha tsukutteru tte iu to ne, minna, ki no omocha desu ka tte kiku n da yo ne. Ki no omocha datte, iro ga nutte aru mono nanka wa, sekiyu tsukatteru wake da shi, takusan atte mo, furuku kara aru mono o tada mane shiteru mono mo kekkou aru n desu yo ne. Sou iu no wa, orijinariti ni kakeru to omou shi. Maa, dakara tte, aidea ippai nara ii ka, tte koto ni naru wake de. Boku wa ne, hora, kodomo no toki tte nanika ni muchuu ni natte asobu ja nai desu ka, keiken nai desu ka? Sou iu ne, muchuu ni nareru no ga, ii omocha da to omou.

Q: 男の人にとって、いいおもちゃとはどういうものですか。Otoko no hito ni totte, ii omocha to wa dou iu mono desu ka. — EN: For this man, what makes a good toy?

#ChoiceMeaning
1すべてが木でできたおもちゃSubete ga ki de dekita omochaa toy made entirely of wood
2プラスティックのおもちゃPurasutikku no omochaa plastic toy
3子どもが夢中になれるおもちゃKodomo ga muchuu ni nareru omochaa toy a child can get completely absorbed in
4新しいアイデアのあるおもちゃAtarashii aidea no aru omochaa toy with a fresh, original idea

Why: He deflates the "wooden = good" assumption (wooden toys can use petroleum-based paint and copy old designs too, undermining option 1), and treats "full of ideas" as a consolation prize rather than the real answer ("まあ、だからって…ってことになるわけで" — a resigned "I guess so" tone, not endorsement, so it undercuts option 4). His actual conclusion is 「夢中になれるのが、いいおもちゃだと思う」— option 3.

Q: 女の人は「エコ」についてどう思っていますか。Onna no hito wa "eko" ni tsuite dou omotte imasu ka. — EN: What does the woman think about the word "eco"?

#ChoiceMeaning
1エコは地球に優しくないと思うEko wa chikyuu ni yasashikunai to omoushe thinks "eco" isn't actually good for the planet
2もっとエコを進めるべきだMotto eko o susumeru beki daeco efforts should be pushed further

4エコという言葉のあいまいさが気になるEko to iu kotoba no aimaisa ga ki ni narushe's bothered by how vague/loosely-used the word "eco" is

Why: When the man assumes she's against eco cars (option 3, in reverse — she's not against them, so this is a trap either way), she corrects him directly: 「そうじゃなくてね、言葉の定義があいまいっていうか、使い方がね」— it's not opposition, it's that the word's definition and usage feel loose/inconsistent. That's option 4; she never claims eco is bad for the planet (1) or that it should be pushed harder (2).

2 問題Ⅳ — 即時応答 (Sokuji outou — "quick response")

3点×7問 (21 points). まず文を聞いてください。それからその返事を聞いて、1～3の中から最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mazu bun o kiite kudasai. Sore kara sono henji o kiite, ichi kara san no naka kara mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: Listen to a short line, then three possible replies (only 3 choices in this section, not 4) — pick the one that responds naturally. Nothing is printed for the replies either; the text below each item is reconstructed from the appendix script. HI: एक छोटा वाक्य सुनें, फिर तीन संभावित उत्तर सुनकर सबसे उपयुक्त उत्तर चुनें। GU: એક ટૂંકું વાક્ય સાંભળો, પછી ત્રણ સંભવિત જવાબોમાંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

1番 CD2, Track 47

例の仕事、伊藤さん、しぶしぶだけど引き受けてくれたよ。Rei no shigoto, Itou-san, shibushibu da kedo hikiukete kureta yo.

EN: "That job — Mr. Itou agreed to take it on, reluctantly."   HI: "वह काम — इतो-सान ने अनिच्छा से ही सही, स्वीकार कर लिया।"   GU: "તે કામ — ઇતો-સાને અનિચ્છાએ પણ સ્વીકારી લીધું."

#ReplyMeaning
1ふーん、初めからできないって言えばいいのに。Fu-n, hajime kara dekinai tte ieba ii noni.Hm, he should've just said no from the start.
2やりたがっていたから、よかったんじゃない？Yaritagatte ita kara, yokatta n ja nai?He wanted to do it, so isn't that good?
3無理やり頼んだんじゃないの？Muriyari tanonda n ja nai no?Didn't you force him into it?

Why: 「しぶしぶ」("reluctantly") means Itou didn't want to do it — so option 2 ("he wanted to") directly contradicts the premise. The natural reaction to hearing someone accepted reluctantly is to ask whether he was pressured into it, option 3.

2番 CD2, Track 48

明日、暇だったら、エミちゃんが働いているレストラン、行ってみない？Ashita, hima dattara, Emi-chan ga hataraite iru resutoran, itte minai?

EN: "If you're free tomorrow, want to check out the restaurant where Emi works?"   HI: "अगर कल खाली हो, तो एमी जिस रेस्तरां में काम करती है वहाँ चलें?"   GU: "જો કાલે ફ્રી હોય, તો એમી જે રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં કામ કરે છે ત્યાં જઈએ?"

5番 CD2, Track 51

石田さんのプレゼンだけど、あいまいな言葉が多くて、いまいちだったと思わない？Ishida-san no purezen da kedo, aimai na kotoba ga ookute, imaichi datta to omowanai?

EN: "About Mr. Ishida's presentation — don't you think it was a bit lacking, with all that vague wording?"   HI: "इशिदा-सान की प्रेजेंटेशन में अस्पष्ट शब्द बहुत थे, नहीं लगता कि कुछ कमी थी?"   GU: "ઇશિદા-સાનનું પ્રેઝન્ટેશન — અસ્પષ્ટ શબ્દો ઘણા હતા, નથી લાગતું કે થોડું ઓછું હતું?"

#ReplyMeaning
1そんなことないでしょ。私にはわけがわからなかった。Sonna koto nai desho. Watashi ni wa wake ga wakaranakatta.That's not true. I couldn't make sense of it at all.

3うん、私もピンとこなかった。Un, watashi mo pin to konakatta.Yeah, it didn't click for me either.

6番 CD2, Track 52

渋滞、ひどいねー。全然動かないよ。目的地に着いても遊ぶ時間ないかも。Juutai, hidoi nee. Zenzen ugokanai yo. Mokutekichi ni tsuite mo asobu jikan nai kamo.
