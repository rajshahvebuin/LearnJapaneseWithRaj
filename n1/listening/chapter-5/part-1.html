0 About This Mock Test

Key idea

これは本番の N1 聴解を模した総合模擬試験です。Kore wa honban no N1 choukai o moshita sougou mogi shiken desu. — "This is a comprehensive mock exam modeled on the real N1 listening test."

Chapter 5 is the book's one comprehensive test (30 minutes, 100 points) rather than a series of teaching lessons — it walks through all 5 question-type sections from Chapter 2 back-to-back, exactly as they'd appear on test day: 問題1 課題理解 (task comprehension), 問題2 ポイント理解 (point comprehension), 問題3 概要理解 (gist comprehension), 問題4 即時応答 (quick response), 問題5 統合理解 (integrated comprehension). This site splits the 8-printed-page test into 3 parts so each stays a manageable read — Part 1 covers 問題I–II, Part 2 covers 問題III–IV, and Part 3 covers 問題V plus a closing test-day traps summary.

1 問題Ⅰ — 課題理解 (Kadai rikai — "task comprehension")

4点×4問 (16 points). まず質問を聞いてください。それから話を聞いて、1～4の中から最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mazu shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Sore kara hanashi o kiite, ichi kara yon no naka kara mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: Listen to the question first, then the conversation, and choose the best option — you're listening for what someone needs to do next. HI: पहले प्रश्न सुनें, फिर बातचीत सुनकर सबसे उपयुक्त विकल्प चुनें। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન સાંભળો, પછી વાતચીત સાંભળીને સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.

Why: The man rules out his usual advice (force-quit + restart) since this is a new symptom, then rules out virus removal since she didn't visit anything suspicious. The backup question is just a side-check ("did you already do it?") that she'd already handled, not the thing he's teaching her now.

2 問題Ⅱ — ポイント理解 (Pointo rikai — "point comprehension")

5点×5問 (25 points). まず質問を聞いてください。そのあと、選択肢を読んでください。読む時間があります。それから話を聞いて、1～4の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mazu shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Sono ato, sentakushi o yonde kudasai. Yomu jikan ga arimasu. Sore kara hanashi o kiite, ichi kara yon no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: Hear the question first, then you get time to read the printed choices, then listen to the conversation and pick the best answer — you're listening for one specific point, not the whole gist. HI: पहले प्रश्न सुनें, फिर विकल्प पढ़ने का समय मिलेगा, उसके बाद बातचीत सुनकर उत्तर चुनें। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન સાંભળો, પછી વિકલ્પો વાંચવાનો સમય મળશે, પછી વાતચીત સાંભળીને જવાબ પસંદ કરો.
