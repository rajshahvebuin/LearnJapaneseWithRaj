1 Grammar Patterns to Watch For

Key idea

したのか、しなかったのか、するのか、しないのかに注意しよう！Shita no ka, shinakatta no ka, suru no ka, shinai no ka ni chuui shiyou! — "Pay attention to whether it was done, wasn't done, will be done, or won't be done!"

N1 聴解 constantly hides the real fact inside a grammar pattern rather than stating it directly — "したらよかった" (I wish I had) actually tells you the person did not do it, while "しなくてよかった" (I'm glad I didn't) tells you they also did not do it, but feel relief instead of regret. Confusing these reverses the entire meaning of a question. This lesson drills the fourteen most common shapes.

2a. 反実仮想・後悔・満足 — counterfactuals, regret, and satisfaction

Each row's 「表現」(expression you'll hear) maps to a hidden 「事実」(the real, unstated fact) — read the 「例」(example) and check you can recover the fact without being told it directly.

表現事実例
していたらshite itara しなかったshinakatta あの電車に乗ってたら、事故に遭ってたよね。Ano densha ni notte tara, jiko ni atteta yo ne.
(＝乗らなかったので、事故に遭わなかったnoranakatta node, jiko ni awanakatta) — "If I'd gotten on that train, I bet I'd have been in the accident, huh." (i.e., I didn't get on — and wasn't in it)
していなかったらshite inakattara したshita あのとき気がついてなかったら、火事になってたよ。Ano toki ki ga tsuite nakattara, kaji ni natteta yo.
(＝気がついたから、火事にならなかったki ga tsuita kara, kaji ni naranakatta) — "If I hadn't noticed right then, it would've turned into a fire." (i.e., I did notice — so there was no fire)
したらよかったshitara yokatta しなかったことを後悔shinakatta koto o koukai (regrets not doing it) 早く準備をしておいたらよかったのに。Hayaku junbi o shite oitara yokatta noni.
— "I really should have gotten ready earlier." (didn't get ready — and regrets it)
↔しなくてよかったshinakute yokatta しなかったことに満足shinakatta koto ni manzoku (satisfied about not doing it) 不良品を買わされなくてよかった。Furyouhin o kawasarenakute yokatta.
— "Good thing I wasn't talked into buying the defective one." (didn't buy it — and is relieved)
しなければよかったshinakereba yokatta したことを後悔shita koto o koukai (regrets doing it) 無理な約束をしなきゃよかった。Muri na yakusoku o shinakya yokatta.
— "I shouldn't have made such an unreasonable promise." (did make it — and regrets it)
↔してよかったshite yokatta したことに満足shita koto ni manzoku (satisfied about doing it) はっきり断ってよかった。Hakkiri kotowatte yokatta.
— "Glad I turned it down clearly." (did refuse — and is relieved)
しないといけなかったshinai to ikenakatta するのを忘れたsuru no o wasureta (forgot to do it) 事前に許可を取らないといけなかったんだ。Jizen ni kyoka o toranai to ikenakattanda.
— "I was supposed to get permission beforehand." (implies: and I forgot to)
↔しなくてよかったんだshinakute yokattanda したが、する必要がなかったことを知るshita ga, suru hitsuyou ga nakatta koto o shiru (did it, then learns it wasn't necessary) 予約しなくてよかったのか…。Yoyaku shinakute yokatta no ka….
— "Oh, so I didn't need to reserve after all…" (already reserved — now finds out it was unnecessary)
すればいいんだがsureba iinda ga しない、したほうがいいのはわかっているが、難しいshinai, shita hou ga ii no wa wakatte iru ga, muzukashii (knows they should, but it's hard) 毎日少しずつでも勉強すれば上達するんだろうけど、なかなかねえ。Mainichi sukoshi zutsu demo benkyou sureba joutatsu surun darou kedo, nakanaka nee.
(＝しないから上達しないshinai kara joutatsu shinai) — "I know I'd improve if I studied a little every day, but it's just not easy." (not studying — so not improving)
しなければいいんだがshinakereba iinda ga するsuru (does it anyway) 嫌われるようなことしなければいいのにね。Kirawareru you na koto shinakereba ii noni ne.
(＝するから嫌われるsuru kara kirawareru) — "If only he wouldn't do things that get him disliked." (he does — and gets disliked)
するつもりはないsuru tsumori wa nai しない *強い意志tsuyoi ishi (strong resolve not to) 何度来ていただいても、買うつもりはありませんから。Nando kite itadaite mo, kau tsumori wa arimasen kara.
— "No matter how many times you come, I have no intention of buying."
するつもりだったんですがsuru tsumori dattandesu ga したかったが、できなかったshitakatta ga, dekinakatta (wanted to, but couldn't) 昨日のうちに仕上げるつもりだったんですが…Kinou no uchi ni shiageru tsumori dattandesu ga…
(＝できなかったdekinakatta) — "I meant to finish it by yesterday, but…" (didn't manage to)
するつもりはなかったんですがsuru tsumori wa nakattandesu ga したくなかったが、そうなったshitakunakatta ga, sou natta (didn't want to, but it happened anyway) ご面倒をおかけするつもりはなかったんですが…Gomendou o okake suru tsumori wa nakattandesu ga…
(＝そうなったsou natta) — "I never meant to trouble you, but…" (it ended up happening)
するところだったsuru tokoro datta しそうになったが、しなかった、ならなかったshisou ni natta ga, shinakatta, naranakatta (almost happened, but didn't) 危うく、だまされるところだった。Ayauku, damasareru tokoro datta.
(＝だまされなかったdamasarenakatta) — "I nearly got tricked." (almost — but didn't)
しなかったことにするshinakatta koto ni suru したshita (actually did it — pretending otherwise) その発言は聞かなかったことにしよう。Sono hatsugen wa kikanakatta koto ni shiyou.
— "Let's pretend I never heard that remark." (did hear it — choosing to act as if not)
したことにするshita koto ni suru しなかったshinakatta (actually didn't do it — pretending otherwise) 昨日、君と会ってたことにしてくれない？Kinou, kimi to atteta koto ni shite kurenai?
— "Can you just say we met up yesterday?" (didn't actually meet — asking to fake it)

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

1番 CD1, Track 5

内容の正しいほうを選んでください。Naiyou no tadashii hou o erande kudasai.

EN: Choose the option that correctly matches what you heard.   HI: जो सुना उससे सही मेल खाने वाला विकल्प चुनें।   GU: જે સાંભળ્યું તેની સાથે સાચી રીતે મેળ ખાતો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.

①車にひかれてしまった。Kuruma ni hikarete shimatta.車にひかれなかった。Kuruma ni hikarenakatta.2
②申し込んだ。Moushikonda.申し込まなかった。Moushikomanakatta.2
③困らせた。Komaraseta.困らせなかった。Komarasenakatta.1
④気がついた。Ki ga tsuita.気がつかなかった。Ki ga tsukanakatta.1

Why: ① もう少しで車にひかれるところだった (nearly got hit — table row 14: しそうになったが、しなかった) means it did not happen. ② 昨日までに申し込まなきゃいけなかったんだ (should've applied by yesterday — row 7) tells you the deadline was missed, i.e. they did not apply. ③ 困らせるつもりはなかったんです (row 13: didn't mean to, but it happened anyway) means they did trouble the listener. ④ 早く気がついてよかった (row 6: relief about doing it) means they did notice. ⑤ あんなに強がらなくてもいいのに ("he doesn't need to act so tough") is a comment about behavior actually happening — he is acting tough (強がる = to put on a brave/tough front).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This lesson is built entirely around three mirror-image (↔) pairs from the reference table — they're the easiest to swap by accident under exam time pressure, so treat them as this lesson's confusion-pairs summary:

PairWhat confuses learnersHow to catch itSeen in
したらよかった
↔ しなくてよかった		 Both describe something that did not happen — but one is regret (should have) and the other is relief (glad I didn't) Check whether よかった follows した (regret at not acting) or しなくて (relief at not acting) — the fact is identical, only the emotion flips Table row 3–4
しなければよかった
↔ してよかった		 Both describe something that did happen — one regrets it, the other is glad of it しなければよかった always regrets an action taken; してよかった is always satisfied with an action taken Table row 5–6
しないといけなかった
↔ しなくてよかったんだ		 Both look like they're about an obligation, but one means "I forgot to do a required thing" and the other means "I did something, then learned it wasn't required" しないといけなかった = forgot a duty; しなくてよかったんだ = relief on discovering an action was unnecessary after already doing it or not Table row 7–8
Exam trap: N1 聴解 rarely asks "what happened?" directly — it asks you to infer the fact from an emotional reaction (regret, relief, resignation) wrapped around a hypothetical. Decode the grammar shape first (did it / didn't do it), then let the emotion tell you how the speaker feels about that fact — never the other way around.
