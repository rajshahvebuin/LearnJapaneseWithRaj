Key idea

This lesson is a graded test combining every trap from Chapter 1: 問題Ⅰ retests pronunciation minimal pairs (Lesson 1), 問題Ⅱ retests counterfactual grammar (1番–2番, Lesson 2) and keigo/conversational trailing-off (3番–4番, Lessons 3–4), and 問題Ⅲ combines all of it inside three longer two- or three-person dialogues. Work through it like a real test, then check against the answer key below.

1 問題Ⅰ — Pronunciation (5点×5問)

問題Ⅰ CD1, Track 11

MP3を聞いてください。発音したのはどれですか。1～4の中から正しいものを一つ選んでください。MP3 o kiite kudasai. Hatsuon shita no wa dore desu ka. 1~4 no naka kara tadashii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

#1234Correct
①ぎゃこうがっこうgakkou "school"きゃっこうkyakkou "spotlight"ぎゃっこう（逆光）gyakkou "backlighting"4
②ちゅうかい（仲介）chuukai "mediation"しょうかいshoukai "introduction"ちょうかいchoukai "listening comp. (聴解 — this very skill!)"しゅうかいshuukai "assembly"1
③ボレボールバレーボールbareebooru "volleyball"ボレーバールバーレボル2
④そうかいかんsoukaikan "refreshed feeling (爽快感)"そがいかんsogaikan "sense of alienation (疎外感)"そんざいかん（存在感）sonzaikan "sense of presence"そうざいかん3

Why: Every item stacks Lesson 1's two traps — a geminate っ that may or may not belong (③ バレーボール has none; ①④ do) and a long/short vowel choice (② ちゅうかい 仲介 "mediation" vs. ちょうかい 聴解 "listening comprehension" vs. しょうかい "introduction" vs. しゅうかい "assembly" — four words that differ only in vowel length and initial consonant). Note the pointed irony of ②'s distractor ちょうかい being 聴解 itself — the very skill this book teaches.

2 問題Ⅱ — Grammar & Expressions (3点×20問)

1番 CD1, Track 12

どちらの意味ですか。Dochira no imi desu ka. — "Which meaning is it?" (Lesson 2 grammar: stated pattern vs. hidden fact)

#Option 1Option 2Correct
①席を取っておいてください。Seki o totte oite kudasai.席を取っておいてあげます。Seki o totte oite agemasu.2
②押してだめなら引いてみたらいい。Oshite dame nara hiite mitara ii.押しても引いてもだめみたい。Oshite mo hiite mo dame mitai.1
③遅れるといっても限度がある。Okureru to itte mo gendo ga aru.遅れるといっても大したことはない。Okureru to itte mo taishita koto wa nai.1
④引き受ければよかった。Hikiukereba yokatta.引き受けなければよかった。Hikiukenakereba yokatta.2
⑤言わなければ伝わらない。Iwanakereba tsutawaranai.言わなくても伝わる。Iwanakute mo tsutawaru.1

Why: ① 席、取っといてあげるね ("I'll save you a seat") = option 2. ② 押してだめなら、引いてみりゃいい ("if pushing fails, try pulling") = option 1. ③ 遅れるってったって、ほどがある ("there's a limit to how late is acceptable") = option 1. ④ よしゃいいのに、引き受けちゃった ("should've just declined, but took it on anyway" — Lesson 2's しなければよかった, regret at having acted) = option 2. ⑤ 言わなきゃ伝わんないよ ("it won't get across unless you say it") = option 1.

2番 CD1, Track 13

事実はどちらですか。Jijitsu wa dochira desu ka. — "Which is the actual fact?" (Lesson 2 grammar: what really happened, hidden inside the sentence)

#Option 1Option 2Correct
①12時までに帰ってきた。Juuniji made ni kaette kita.12時までに帰ってこられなかった。Juuniji made ni kaette korarenakatta.2
②伝えるのを忘れた。Tsutaeru no o wasureta.伝えるのを忘れなかった。Tsutaeru no o wasurenakatta.2
③つまらないことを言った。Tsumaranai koto o itta.つまらないことを言わなかった。Tsumaranai koto o iwanakatta.1
④あの道を通った。Ano michi o tootta.あの道を通らなかった。Ano michi o tooranakatta.1
⑤もう返した。Mou kaeshita.まだ返していない。Mada kaeshite inai.2

Why: ① 12時までに帰ってくるつもりだったのに ("I meant to be back by 12, but…") implies they weren't = option 2. ② お伝えするのを忘れるところでした ("almost forgot to tell you" — Lesson 2 row 14, しそうになったが、しなかった) means they did NOT actually forget = option 2. ③ つまらないことを言わなきゃよかった ("shouldn't have said that" — regret at having acted) means they DID say it = option 1. ④ あの道を通らなければ、事故に遭っていなかった ("if I hadn't gone that way, I wouldn't have had the accident") means they DID go that way = option 1. ⑤ これ、返さなきゃいけなかったんだ ("I was supposed to return this") implies it's still not returned = option 2.

④女:16日必着でお願いします。男:承りました。Onna: Juurokunichi hicchaku de onegai shimasu. Otoko: Uketamawarimashita.かしこまりました。Kashikomarimashita.お断りします。O-kotowari shimasu.1
⑤女:もういいかしら。男:もう少し、時間をおいたほうがよろしいかと存じます。Onna: Mou ii kashira. Otoko: Mou sukoshi, jikan o oita hou ga yoroshii ka to zonjimasu.わかりかねます。Wakarikanemasu.もう少しあとのほうがいいと思います。Mou sukoshi ato no hou ga ii to omoimasu.2

Why: ① だからって (Lesson 4a) rejects her excuse for being late → "you kept me waiting too long." ② 似合うもんか (Lesson 4a, …もんか = strong denial) → "it would NOT suit you, better not wear it." ③ 恐れ入ります (Lesson 3b) here is simply a polite acknowledgment/thanks, i.e. "yes, there's fine." ④ 承りました (Lesson 3b) = a polite, formal "understood/accepted" = かしこまりました. ⑤ …ほうがよろしいかと存じます (Lesson 3a, humble/soft recommendation) = a polite way of saying "a bit later would be better."

Why: ① …たところで (4b) → pointless, "no use" = 無駄だ. ② まさか (4c) → disbelief that it happened = "there's no way I'd win." ③ これといって…ない (4b) → nothing in particular = "nowhere in particular I want to go." ④ せっかくだから (4b) → accepting despite it being unnecessary = "I'll take it, thanks." ⑤ 君のことだから、てっきり…(4c) — "君のことだから" ("knowing you") means he's about to name an assumption based on her known character (stubbornly pushing through), so despite her looking so unwell yesterday, he figured she'd show up anyway: てっきり来ると思った ("I was sure you'd come regardless").

3 問題Ⅲ — Combined Dialogues (5点×3問)

MP3を聞いてください。内容と合うものはどれですか。1～3の中から正しいものを一つ選んでください。MP3 o kiite kudasai. Naiyou to au mono wa dore desu ka. 1~3 no naka kara tadashii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. — EN: Listen to the MP3 and choose the option matching its content.   HI: MP3 सुनें और सामग्री से मेल खाने वाला विकल्प चुनें।   GU: MP3 સાંભળો અને સામગ્રી સાથે મેળ ખાતો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.

男：山本先生の絵を、どうお思いになりますか。
女：あら、絵もお描きになるんですか、存じませんでした。俳句はすばらしいと思いますが。
男：絵も、味がありますよ。Otoko: Yamamoto-sensei o go-zonji desu ka. / Onna: Ee, zonjiagete orimasu. / Otoko: Yamamoto-sensei no e o, dou o-omoi ni narimasu ka. / Onna: Ara, e mo o-kaki ni narun desu ka, zonjimasen deshita. Haiku wa subarashii to omoimasu ga. / Otoko: E mo, aji ga arimasu yo.

#OptionCorrect
1女の人は山本先生を知らない。Onna no hito wa Yamamoto-sensei o shiranai.
2女の人は山本先生の絵を見たことがある。Onna no hito wa Yamamoto-sensei no e o mita koto ga aru.
3男の人は山本先生の絵や俳句について知っている。Otoko no hito wa Yamamoto-sensei no e ya haiku ni tsuite shitte iru.✓

Why: 存じ上げております (Lesson 3a, humble "I know him") shows she DOES know Mr. Yamamoto, ruling out option 1. 絵もお描きになるんですか、存じませんでした ("he paints too? I didn't know that") shows she has NOT seen his paintings, ruling out option 2. The man is the one who brings up both the paintings and knows enough to comment "絵も、味がありますよ" ("the paintings have character too"), and the woman already knew about his haiku — between the two, only the man clearly demonstrates knowledge of both his paintings and his haiku, matching option 3.

#OptionCorrect
1男の人は今日診察してもらう。Otoko no hito wa kyou shinsatsu shite morau.✓
2男の人は診察を断られた。Otoko no hito wa shinsatsu o kotowarareta.

Why: This is a dental clinic scene (詰め物がとれた = "a filling came out"). The receptionist is fully booked but agrees to fit him in today, after scheduled patients, since he explains he leaves for a business trip tomorrow — he thanks her (助かりました, "that's a huge help"), confirming he WILL be seen today (option 1), not turned away (option 2). He isn't rescheduling an existing reservation (option 3) — he never had one; he's asking for a same-day walk-in slot.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

As a chapter-recap test, this lesson's own structure IS the confusion-pairs summary — each 問題 maps straight back to one of the earlier lessons' trap types:

SectionTrap typeHow to catch itTraces back to
問題Ⅰ Geminate っ / long vs. short vowels in near-identical words (ちゅうかい／しょうかい／ちょうかい／しゅうかい) Isolate the exact mora count and listen for a held consonant or extra vowel length before matching to a word Lesson 1
問題Ⅱ 1番・2番 Counterfactual/regret grammar hiding whether an action actually happened Decode the grammar shape (したら／しなければ／するところ, etc.) before trying to guess the fact Lesson 2
問題Ⅱ 3番 Fixed keigo phrases (恐れ入ります、承りました、〜と存じます) whose literal reading misleads Treat each phrase as a memorized block tied to its function, not its individual kanji Lesson 3
問題Ⅱ 4番 Trailing adverbs/connectors (まさか、てっきり、せっかく…、これといって…) whose ending is implied, not spoken Learn each adverb's one fixed partner ending so you supply it automatically Lesson 4
問題Ⅲ All of the above stacked inside a longer, multi-turn dialogue with 3-way distractors Track who says what and whether it's fact, opinion, or politeness — don't let the longest option "sound most complete" bias your choice Lessons 1–4
Exam trap: A "まとめ問題" like this is exactly what real N1 聴解 sections feel like — several unrelated trap types back to back with no warning of which one is coming next. Practicing the switch between "listen for a fact," "listen for an emotion," and "listen for a polite formula" in the same sitting is the actual point of this lesson, more than any single item.
