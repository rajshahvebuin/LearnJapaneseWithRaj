1 Conversational Expressions to Watch For

Key idea

いろいろな表現を覚えよう！Iroiro na hyougen o oboeyou! — "Let's learn all kinds of expressions!"

Natural spoken Japanese constantly leaves sentences unfinished — the speaker trails off with 「…」 and trusts the listener to fill in the rest from context and tone. N1 聴解 tests exactly this: can you supply the missing ending, or correctly read what a half-said sentence implies?

4a. いろいろな会話表現 — everyday discourse markers

MarkerFunctionExample
でde （＝それでsore de）*話の続きを促すhanashi no tsuzuki o unagasu — prompts the speaker to continue 「車ぶつけた。」「えー！で、どうしたの？」"Kuruma butsuketa." "Ee! De, dou shita no?"
— "I crashed the car." "What?! So, then what happened?"
だからってdakaratte （＝からといってkara to itte）*その理由は納得できないsono riyuu wa nattoku dekinai — "that's still no excuse" 「約束忘れてごめん。忙しくて…」「だからって…。」"Yakusoku wasurete gomen. Isogashikute…" "Dakaratte…"
— "Sorry I forgot our promise, I was busy…" "That's still no excuse…"
…ったらない…ttara nai （＝とても…totemo…）extreme emphasis, usually negative 「お隣の犬、うるさいったらない。」"O-tonari no inu, urusai ttara nai."
— "The neighbor's dog is unbelievably noisy."
…とか？…toka? *提案や推測teian ya suisoku — a tentative suggestion or guess 「どうしよう、使い方わかんないよ。」「先輩に聞くとか？」"Dou shiyou, tsukaikata wakannai yo." "Senpai ni kiku toka?"
— "What do I do, I don't get how to use it." "Maybe ask a senpai?"
w「どうしたの疲れた顔して。徹夜したとか？」"Dou shita no tsukareta kao shite. Tetsuya shita toka?" — "Why the tired face? Pulled an all-nighter or something?"
だったりしてdattari shite *理由や状況の推測riyuu ya joukyou no suisoku — a playful guess at the reason/situation 「田中君欠席？　ずる休みだったりして」"Tanaka-kun kesseki? Zuruyasumi dattari shite."
— "Tanaka's absent? Maybe he's just skipping."
…もんですか…mon desu ka *強い否定tsuyoi hitei — strong denial/rejection of the premise 「上手だね。」「上手なもんですか。」"Jouzu da ne." "Jouzu na mon desu ka."
— "You're good at this." "As if I'm any good!"

4b. 会話の省略 — trailing off mid-sentence

ExchangePattern / meaning
A：走ったところで…Hashitta tokoro de…
B：もう間に合いませんか？Mou maniaimasen ka?		 …たところで～ない…ta tokoro de ~nai
*…ても無駄だという諦めの気持ち…temo muda da to iu akirame no kimochi — resignation that it'd be pointless even if done
A：条件はこれといって…Jouken wa kore to itte…
B：特にありませんか？Toku ni arimasen ka?		 …は、これといってない…wa, kore to itte nai（＝特にないtoku ni nai）
"nothing in particular"
A：せっかく料理作ったのに…Sekkaku ryouri tsukutta noni…
B：食べてくれなかったの？Tabete kurenakatta no?		 せっかくだがsekkaku da ga *ありがたいが、断るarigatai ga, kotowaru — grateful, but declining
せっかくだからsekkaku dakara *不要だが、受け入れるfuyou da ga, ukeireru — unnecessary, but accepting anyway
せっかく…のにsekkaku…noni *不満fuman — dissatisfaction that the effort went to waste

4c. あとに続くことばが決まっている副詞 — adverbs with a fixed unspoken ending

AdverbFixed continuation
きっぱりkippari→ やめる・断るyameru / kotowaru — to quit/refuse cleanly and decisively
てっきりtekkiri→ …と思った…to omotta *誤解gokai — "I was (wrongly) sure that…" — a mistaken assumption
まさかmasaka→ …とは思わない…to wa omowanai — "there's no way…" / disbelief. Book's own gloss: まさか！ = "No way! I can't believe it!" 怎么可能！설마！
おちおちochiochi→ …ない…nai *安心して…ないanshin shite…nai — "can't even calmly/leisurely do…" (always negative)
かろうじてkaroujite / どうにかdou ni ka / なんとかnantoka→ …できた…dekita — "just barely managed to…"

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

2 exercises from p.19, testing whether you can decode a trailing-off line or a fixed conversational expression. Play the referenced CD track alongside this page (see note in the header), or use these as pure read-and-recall drills if you don't have the audio to hand.

1番 CD1, Track 9

どちらの意味ですか。正しいほうを選んでください。Dochira no imi desu ka. Tadashii hou o erande kudasai.

EN: Which meaning is it? Choose the correct one.   HI: कौन सा अर्थ सही है? सही विकल्प चुनें।   GU: કયો અર્થ સાચો છે? સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.

#Option 1Option 2Correct
①じゃ、しょうがない。Ja, shouganai.それは理由にはならない。Sore wa riyuu ni wa naranai.2
②かなりおもしろい。Kanari omoshiroi.全然おもしろくない。Zenzen omoshirokunai.2
③信じられない。Shinjirarenai.すごいね。Sugoi ne.1
④ちょっと都合が悪い。Chotto tsugou ga warui.是非ご一緒したい。Zehi go-issho shitai.1
⑤ずうずうしい人だ。Zuuzuushii hito da.気の置けない人だ。Ki no okenai hito da.1

Why: ① 男:お客さんと一緒だったから、電話できなかったんだよ。女:だからって…。 (4a) — だからって always rejects the reason offered, so "that's no excuse." ② 男:部長が怒っちゃってさー、おもしろいだろ?女:おもしろいもんですか。(4a) — …もんですか is strong denial, so "not funny at all." ③ 女:まさか！(4c) — disbelief, "I can't believe it." ④ 女:せっかくですが…。(4b) — せっかくだが politely declines, i.e. "it's a bit inconvenient" (masking the real reason, a refusal). ⑤ 女:厚かましいったらないんだから。(4a, …ったらない = extremely) — 厚かましい means shameless/pushy, matching option 1. ⑥ 女:ああいうことをする人の気が知れないわ。— 気が知れない = "I can't fathom/understand," matching option 2.

2番 CD1, Track 10

男の人と女の人が話しています。男の人のあとに女の人が言うのはどちらですか。正しいほうを選んでください。Otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito no ato ni onna no hito ga iu no wa dochira desu ka. Tadashii hou o erande kudasai.

①タバコは、もうきっぱり…Tabako wa, mou kippari…やめましたか。Yamemashita ka.吸えなくなりましたね。Suenaku narimashita ne.1
②雨が降るとは言ってたけど、まさか…Ame ga furu to wa itteta kedo, masaka…こんなにひどいとはねえ。Konna ni hidoi to wa nee.天気予報が外れるなんてねえ。Tenki yohou ga hazureru nante nee.1
③今の職場には、これといって不満は…Ima no shokuba ni wa, kore to itte fuman wa…ないわけないですよね。Nai wake nai desu yo ne.ないんですか、うらやましい。Nain desu ka, urayamashii.2
④会社に希望を出したところで…Kaisha ni kibou o dashita tokoro de…なんとかなりそうですね。Nantoka narisou desu ne.やっぱり無理でしょうか。Yappari muri deshou ka.2
⑤あれ？本当に病気だったの。君のことだから、てっきり…Are? Hontou ni byouki datta no. Kimi no koto dakara, tekkiri…仮病だと思った？Kebyou da to omotta?心配かけてごめん。Shinpai kakete gomen.1

Why: ① きっぱり (4c) fixes to やめる, so "quit completely" → 1. ② まさか (4c) signals disbelief at how bad it got, matching "I can't believe it was this bad" → 1. ③ これといって…ない (4b) means "nothing in particular" — the reply teases "lucky you, no complaints?" → 2. ④ …たところで (4b) signals futility — "even if I applied, it'd be pointless," so the natural follow-up is "so is it hopeless after all?" → 2. ⑤ てっきり…と思った (4c) is a mistaken assumption — he assumed she was faking illness (仮病), so she calls that out directly → 1.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This lesson's whole content is a confusion-pairs cluster in disguise — every entry below sounds like an incomplete thought, but each has one fixed, learnable resolution. Treat this table as the lesson's confusion-pairs summary:

Trap typeWhat confuses learnersHow to catch itSeen in
せっかく… family せっかくだが／だから／…のに look almost identical but resolve to opposite actions (decline / reluctantly accept / complain) だが = declining; だから = accepting anyway; …のに = venting about wasted effort — the connector after せっかく decides everything 4b
…たところで／これといって…ない Both trail off before the negative conclusion, so learners wait for a "no" that never comes explicitly …たところで always implies "~ても無駄" (pointless); これといって…ない always implies "nothing in particular" — supply the ない yourself 4b
てっきり／まさか／おちおち／きっぱり Each adverb "locks in" a specific verb ending that's often left unsaid — guessing the wrong ending flips the sentence's meaning Memorize each adverb's fixed partner (てっきり→と思った, まさか→とは思わない, おちおち→…ない, きっぱり→やめる・断る) as a single chunk 4c, 1番 & 2番
Exam trap: When a script line ends in 「…」 or a clearly unfinished clause, N1 聴解 is testing whether you know the fixed continuation, not whether you can improvise one — treat every trailing adverb/connector in 4a–4c as a lookup, not a guess.
