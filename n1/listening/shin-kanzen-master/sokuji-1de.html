1 Points — intonation and set expressions

Key idea

EN: 1-D: the same form can change meaning with intonation. 1-E: watch for expressions common in conversation, and when someone gives an opinion or impression, also listen for the feeling they want to get across — surprise, dissatisfaction and so on (book p.21, 23, paraphrased).   HI: 1-D: एक ही रूप स्वर-लहर से अलग अर्थ दे सकता है। 1-E: बोलचाल के आम भाव-वाक्यांशों पर ध्यान दें, और राय या भाव के समय वक्ता की भावना — आश्चर्य, असंतोष आदि — भी पकड़ें।   GU: 1-D: એક જ રૂપ સ્વર-લહેરથી અલગ અર્થ આપી શકે. 1-E: બોલચાલના સામાન્ય શબ્દપ્રયોગો પર ધ્યાન આપો, અને મત કે લાગણી વખતે બોલનારની ભાવના — આશ્ચર્ય, અસંતોષ વગેરે — પણ પકડો.

例題1-D · (1)(2) CD1 · Track 15

文を聞いてください。どちらですか。　(1)(2)（意見・禁止）Bun o kiite kudasai. Dochira desu ka. (iken / kinshi)

EN: Listen to the sentence. Which is it — opinion or prohibition?
HI: वाक्य सुनें। कौन-सा है — राय या मनाही?
GU: વાક્ય સાંભળો. કયું છે — મત કે મનાઈ?
The lines the answer hinges on

(1) 9時ごろ来るんじゃない？Kuji goro kuru n ja nai?

EN: (Honda) will probably come around 9, I think. HI: (होंडा जी) शायद 9 बजे के आसपास आएँगे। GU: (હોન્ડાજી) કદાચ 9 વાગ્યાની આસપાસ આવશે.

(2) 危ないから、こっちへ来るんじゃない！Abunai kara, kocchi e kuru n ja nai!

EN: It's dangerous — don't come over here! HI: ख़तरनाक है, इधर मत आओ! GU: જોખમી છે, આ બાજુ ન આવો!

#AnswerEnglishHindiGujarati
(1)意見ikenopinionरायમત
(2)禁止kinshiprohibitionमनाहीમનાઈ

Answers: (1) 意見, (2) 禁止 (book key, p.22). Why: the book explains that (1) raises the sentence end and gives the opinion "〜と思う". (2) lowers the end and stresses 「ない」; the reason 「危ないから」 also shows it is a prohibition.

3 Confusion pairs — same form, different job

The unit's own contrast set (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
〜ないって。 vs 〜ないって！~nai tte. / ~nai tte! (he) says not vs I'm telling you, not 練習1-D (1)(2): flat = 報告 (hearsay); emphatic = 主張. HI: सपाट = सूचना, ज़ोरदार = ज़ोर देना। GU: સપાટ = સૂચના, ભારપૂર્વક = દાવો.
〜んじゃない！ vs 〜んじゃない？~n ja nai! / ~n ja nai? don't! vs I think… 例題1-D, 練習1-D (7)(10): sharp fall = 禁止; rise = 意見. HI: तीखी गिरावट = मनाही, चढ़ाव = राय। GU: તીખો ઘટાડો = મનાઈ, ચઢાવ = મત.
〜んじゃなかった。 vs 〜んじゃなかった？~n ja nakatta. / ~n ja nakatta? I shouldn't have vs weren't you supposed to…? 練習1-D (3)(6) vs (4): falling = 後悔; rising = 確認. HI: उतरती = पछतावा, चढ़ती = पुष्टि। GU: ઊતરતી = પસ્તાવો, ચડતી = ખાતરી.
〜ったら (不満) vs 〜たら (提案)~ttara / ~tara that X! (complaint) vs why not…? 田中ったら = topic + annoyance; こっちにしたら？ = suggestion. HI: 田中ったら = झुँझलाहट; したら？ = सुझाव। GU: 田中ったら = ચીડ; したら？ = સૂચન.
〜のなんのって vs 〜なんてもんじゃない~no nan no tte / ~nante mon ja nai extremely ~ (both) Both are strong emphasis, never denial: 寒いのなんのって = very cold. HI: दोनों ज़ोर हैं, नकार नहीं। GU: બંને ભાર છે, નકાર નહીં.
Exam tip: in 即時応答, first name the function of the line — complaint, suggestion, reproach, report, insisting — then pick the reply that answers that function: agree with a complaint, accept or decline a suggestion, apologise for a reproach. (added, not in book)
