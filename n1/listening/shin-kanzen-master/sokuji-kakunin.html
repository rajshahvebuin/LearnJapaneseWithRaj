Notes
Four 即時応答 items in exam format: the page shows only 1 2 3. For each, the first line (the line the answer hinges on) and the correct reply are quoted from the key; the two wrong replies are given as a gist in our own words （要旨）, not the book's text. §1 recaps which II skill each item tests; §3 lists the traps. The audio is copyrighted and is not published here: play the matching track from your own copy. Hindi, Gujarati and anything marked "(added, not in book)" are ours.

1 Points — which II skill each item checks

Key idea

EN: The review test mixes the skills of section II: who acts (1-A), what the speaker thinks (1-B), did it happen (1-C), intonation (1-D), conversational expressions (1-E), and direct vs indirect replies (II-2). Before the replies start, decide what kind of line you heard and what a natural reply must do. (added, not in book)   HI: यह परीक्षण भाग II के सभी कौशल मिलाता है: काम कौन करेगा, वक्ता क्या सोचता है, घटना हुई या नहीं, स्वर-लहर, बोलचाल के भाव, सीधा या घुमाकर जवाब। जवाब शुरू होने से पहले तय करें कि वाक्य किस तरह का है और स्वाभाविक जवाब को क्या करना चाहिए।   GU: આ કસોટી ભાગ II નાં બધાં કૌશલ્ય ભેળવે છે: કામ કોણ કરશે, બોલનાર શું વિચારે છે, ઘટના બની કે નહીં, સ્વર-લહેર, બોલચાલના પ્રયોગ, સીધો કે આડકતરો જવાબ. જવાબ શરૂ થાય તે પહેલાં નક્કી કરો કે વાક્ય કેવું છે અને સ્વાભાવિક જવાબે શું કરવું જોઈએ.

HI: पहले वाक्य सुनें। फिर उसके जवाब सुनकर 1–3 में से सबसे सही चुनें। GU: પહેલાં વાક્ય સાંભળો. પછી તેના જવાબ સાંભળી 1–3માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય પસંદ કરો.

確認問題 (1) CD1 · Track 20
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A woman apologises to a man: she believed she had already told him something by email. He has to reply.

HI: एक औरत आदमी से माफ़ी माँगती है: उसे लगा था कि उसने ईमेल से बात बता दी थी। आदमी को जवाब देना है।

GU: એક સ્ત્રી પુરુષની માફી માગે છે: તેને લાગ્યું હતું કે તેણે ઈમેલથી વાત જણાવી દીધી હતી. પુરુષે જવાબ આપવાનો છે.

The line the answer hinges on

申し訳ありません、メールでお伝えしたと思っていたんですが……。Moushiwake arimasen, meeru de otsutae shita to omotte ita n desu ga…

EN: I'm very sorry — I thought I had told you by email… HI: माफ़ कीजिए, मुझे लगा था कि मैंने ईमेल से बता दिया था… GU: માફ કરશો, મને લાગ્યું હતું કે મેં ઈમેલથી જણાવી દીધું હતું…

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1（要旨）伝えたはずだと同意する(youshi) tsutaeta hazu da to doui surugist: agrees she should have told himसार: सहमत होता है कि बताया होगाસાર: સંમત થાય છે કે જણાવ્યું હશે
2おかしいですね。届いてなかったようですが。Okashii desu ne. Todoite nakatta you desu ga.That's odd. It doesn't seem to have arrived.अजीब बात है। लगता है पहुँचा नहीं।અજુગતું છે. લાગે છે પહોંચ્યું નથી.
3（要旨）大丈夫、自分もそう思うと言う(youshi) daijoubu, jibun mo sou omou to iugist: says it's fine and he thinks so tooसार: कहता है कोई बात नहीं, मैं भी यही सोचता हूँસાર: કહે છે વાંધો નહીં, હું પણ એવું જ માનું છું

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.6). (added, not in book) Why: 〜たと思っていた is a 1-C form: she thought she had sent it, so in fact he did not get it, and she is apologising. Reply 2 confirms that it never arrived. 1 and 3 treat the message as received, which contradicts both the apology and the 思っていた.

確認問題 (2) CD1 · Track 20
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A man notices a bill the woman has and reminds her that it looks overdue and must be paid quickly. She has to reply.

HI: आदमी औरत के पास एक बिल देखता है और याद दिलाता है कि उसकी अंतिम तिथि शायद निकल गई है, जल्दी भरना होगा। औरत को जवाब देना है।

GU: પુરુષ સ્ત્રી પાસે એક બિલ જુએ છે અને યાદ અપાવે છે કે તેની છેલ્લી તારીખ કદાચ વીતી ગઈ છે, જલદી ભરવું પડશે. સ્ત્રીએ જવાબ આપવાનો છે.

The line the answer hinges on

あ、それ、もう期限、過ぎてるんじゃなかった？早く払わないと。A, sore, mou kigen, sugiteru n ja nakatta? Hayaku harawanai to.

EN: Oh, isn't that already past the due date? You'd better pay it quickly. HI: अरे, इसकी अंतिम तिथि निकल नहीं गई? जल्दी भरना होगा। GU: અરે, આની છેલ્લી તારીખ વીતી નથી ગઈ? જલદી ભરવું પડશે.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1（要旨）払うのではないかと推測する(youshi) harau no de wa nai ka to suisoku surugist: guesses that (someone) will payसार: अनुमान लगाती है कि (कोई) भर देगाસાર: અનુમાન કરે છે કે (કોઈ) ભરી દેશે
2（要旨）「いいえ」と言いながら期限は昨日だったと認める(youshi) "iie" to iinagara kigen wa kinou datta to mitomerugist: says "no" but then confirms the deadline was yesterdayसार: "नहीं" कहती है, फिर मानती है कि तिथि कल थीસાર: "ના" કહે છે, પછી સ્વીકારે છે કે તારીખ ગઈ કાલે હતી
3あ、忘れてた。すぐ行ってくる。A, wasureteta. Sugu itte kuru.Oh, I forgot! I'll go (and pay) right away.अरे, भूल गई थी! अभी जाकर आती हूँ।અરે, ભૂલી ગઈ હતી! હમણાં જઈ આવું.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.6). (added, not in book) Why: 〜んじゃなかった？ with a rising tone is 確認 (1-D): "it's past the deadline, isn't it?" The natural reply admits it and acts. 2 starts with ううん (denying) but then agrees the deadline has passed, so it contradicts itself. 1 answers as if he had asked for an opinion about who will pay.

確認問題 (3) CD1 · Track 20
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A man complains that unexpected visitors turned up today, so he got no work done. The woman has to respond.

HI: आदमी शिकायत करता है कि आज अचानक मेहमान आ गए, इसलिए कोई काम नहीं हो पाया। औरत को जवाब देना है।

GU: પુરુષ ફરિયાદ કરે છે કે આજે અચાનક મહેમાન આવી ગયા, એટલે કોઈ કામ ન થઈ શક્યું. સ્ત્રીએ જવાબ આપવાનો છે.

The line the answer hinges on

今日は急な来客があって、仕事にならなかったよ。Kyou wa kyuu na raikyaku ga atte, shigoto ni naranakatta yo.

EN: Some visitors turned up out of the blue today, and I couldn't get anything done. HI: आज अचानक मेहमान आ गए, काम ही नहीं हो पाया। GU: આજે અચાનક મહેમાન આવી ગયા, કામ જ ન થઈ શક્યું.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1ふーん、大変だったね。Fuun, taihen datta ne.Hmm, that must have been hard.अच्छा, बड़ी मुश्किल रही होगी।અચ્છા, બહુ મુશ્કેલી પડી હશે.
2（要旨）よかったと喜ぶ(youshi) yokatta to yorokobugist: says "good for you"सार: कहती है "अच्छा हुआ"સાર: કહે છે "સારું થયું"
3（要旨）休んだらと提案する(youshi) yasundara to teian surugist: suggests he take a restसार: आराम करने की सलाह देती हैસાર: આરામ કરવાની સલાહ આપે છે

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.6). (added, not in book) Why: 〜にならない is a 1-E grumbling expression (話にならない type): 仕事にならなかった = "the day was wasted". The reply should sympathise, as 1 does. 2 takes it as good news. 3 would fit "I'm exhausted", but he did no work, so there is nothing to rest from.

確認問題 (4) CD1 · Track 20
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: They are packing things away, and the woman says flatly that everything will never fit into a container this size. The man has to reply.

HI: वे सामान रख रहे हैं, और औरत साफ़ कहती है कि इस आकार में सब कुछ कभी नहीं समाएगा। आदमी को जवाब देना है।

GU: તેઓ સામાન મૂકી રહ્યા છે, અને સ્ત્રી સ્પષ્ટ કહે છે કે આ માપમાં બધું ક્યારેય નહીં સમાય. પુરુષે જવાબ આપવાનો છે.

The line the answer hinges on

このサイズじゃ、全部入りっこないよ。Kono saizu ja, zenbu hairikko nai yo.

EN: At this size, there's no way it'll all go in. HI: इस आकार में सब कुछ समाने का सवाल ही नहीं। GU: આ માપમાં બધું સમાવાનો સવાલ જ નથી.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1あ、これは小さいか。A, kore wa chiisai ka.Ah, this one's too small, then.अच्छा, यह छोटा है।અચ્છા, આ નાનું છે.
2（要旨）「来っこない」と聞き違えて「来るだろう」と言う(youshi) "kikko nai" to kikichigaete "kuru darou" to iugist: answers "no, (they) will come", as if she had said someone won't comeसार: "नहीं, आएँगे" कहता है, मानो उसने कहा हो कि कोई नहीं आएगाસાર: "ના, આવશે" કહે છે, જાણે તેણે કહ્યું હોય કે કોઈ નહીં આવે
3（要旨）同意しつつ入れてみようと言う(youshi) doui shitsutsu irete miyou to iugist: says "yes" and suggests trying to put it all inसार: "हाँ" कहकर सब डालकर देखने को कहता हैસાર: "હા" કહીને બધું નાખી જોવાનું કહે છે

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.6). (added, not in book) Why: 〜っこない (1-E) = できるわけがない, a strong "impossible". Reply 1 accepts her point: the container is too small. 3 says うん (agreeing) and then suggests the very thing she called impossible. 2 answers as if she had said someone 来っこない ("won't possibly come").

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong replies

Where the wrong replies come from (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
〜たと思っていた vs 〜た~ta to omotte ita / ~ta I thought I had (but hadn't) vs I did (1): 思っていた turns the past into a mistaken belief. HI: 思っていた = ग़लतफ़हमी थी। GU: 思っていた = ગેરસમજ હતી.
うん／ううん + contradictionun / uun yes / no, then the opposite (2) option 2, (4) option 3: the first word and the rest of the reply don't match. HI: पहला शब्द और बाक़ी जवाब मेल नहीं खाते। GU: પહેલો શબ્દ અને બાકીનો જવાબ મેળ ખાતા નથી.
仕事にならなかった vs 仕事が終わったshigoto ni naranakatta / shigoto ga owatta couldn't work at all vs finished the work (3): 〜にならない = it didn't work out, a complaint, not a report of a finished job. HI: 〜にならない = शिकायत। GU: 〜にならない = ફરિયાદ.
入りっこない vs 入るかもhairikko nai / hairu kamo can't possibly fit vs might fit (4): 〜っこない leaves no room for "let's try". HI: 〜っこない में "कोशिश करें" की जगह नहीं। GU: 〜っこない માં "પ્રયત્ન કરીએ" ની જગ્યા નથી.
Exam tip: in 即時応答 a wrong reply often starts correctly (うん, ええ) and then goes wrong. Listen to each reply to the end, and check that its first word and its content agree with each other and with the first line. (added, not in book)
