1 Listening Strategy

Key idea

説明を聞くための語彙や表現を覚えよう！Setsumei o kiku tame no goi ya hyougen o oboeyou! — "Learn the vocabulary and expressions needed to follow explanations!"

"Explanation" listening items describe a graph/trend, a proportion, an introduction to something (a place, a species, a work of art, a product, a person), or the cause of a malfunction. None of these require creative interpretation — they're delivered through a small, fixed vocabulary of direction words (up/down/flat), degree words (sharp/gradual), and domain nouns. Learn the templates below and you can track the shape of a graph or the root of a problem purely by ear.

2 Listening Practice — れんしゅう

3 exercises from p.45. Full script, options and answers below are reproduced/confirmed from the answer appendix at p.112–113 (「答えは p.112」 is printed directly on the exercise page). Play the referenced CD track alongside this page, or use these as read-and-recall drills.

1番 CD1, Track 61

テレビで女の人がある島について説明しています。この島で雨が多いのはどの地域ですか。Terebi de onna no hito ga aru shima ni tsuite setsumei shite imasu. Kono shima de ame ga ooi no wa dono chiiki desu ka.

EN: On TV, a woman is explaining about an island. Which area of this island gets a lot of rain?   HI: टीवी पर एक महिला एक द्वीप के बारे में बता रही है। इस द्वीप में सबसे ज़्यादा बारिश किस क्षेत्र में होती है?   GU: ટીવી પર એક સ્ત્રી એક ટાપુ વિશે સમજાવી રહી છે. આ ટાપુ પર સૌથી વધુ વરસાદ કયા વિસ્તારમાં પડે છે?

Map: a compass-oriented island outline with four labeled regions — A (a mountain peak, north-center), B (east side, next to A), C (south), D (west).

Script: 雨量は場所によって異なりますが、平野部より山間部に多く降ります。島の北部に高い山がありますが、そのあたりが最も雨量が多く、山頂付近の平均雨量は、海岸地域の10倍もあります。海岸部では、東海岸、特に東北地域で雨が多く、植物がよく育ち、産業の中心になっています。Uryou wa basho ni yotte kotonarimasu ga, heiyabu yori sankanbu ni ooku furimasu. Shima no hokubu ni takai yama ga arimasu ga, sono atari ga mottomo uryou ga ooku, sanchou fukin no heikin uryou wa, kaigan chiiki no juubai mo arimasu. Kaiganbu de wa, higashi-kaigan, toku ni touhoku chiiki de ame ga ooku, shokubutsu ga yoku sodachi, sangyou no chuushin ni natte imasu.
#OptionCorrect?
1AとBA to BCorrect
2AとCA to CNo
3BとCB to CNo
4CとDC to DNo

Why: Two separate rainy zones are named: the mountain peak in the north (高い山, most rain of all — that's A, the peak marked on the map) and the east coast, especially its northeastern part (東海岸、特に東北地域 — that's B, drawn directly east of the peak). C (south) and D (west) are never mentioned as high-rainfall areas, so any option pairing them with A or with each other is wrong.

2番 CD1, Track 62

会社で、男の人と女の人が、ある商品の売り上げについて話しています。この商品の今月の売り上げは、先月と比べてどうですか。Kaisha de, otoko no hito to onna no hito ga, aru shouhin no uriage ni tsuite hanashite imasu. Kono shouhin no kongetsu no uriage wa, sengetsu to kurabete dou desu ka.

EN: At a company, a man and woman are discussing a product's sales. How do this month's sales for this product compare to last month?   HI: कंपनी में, एक पुरुष और महिला किसी उत्पाद की बिक्री पर चर्चा कर रहे हैं। इस महीने की बिक्री पिछले महीने की तुलना में कैसी है?   GU: કંપનીમાં, એક પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી કોઈ ઉત્પાદનના વેચાણ વિશે વાત કરી રહ્યાં છે. આ મહિનાનું વેચાણ ગયા મહિનાની સરખામણીમાં કેવું છે?

Script:
男: 今月の売り上げはどうだ？Otoko: Kongetsu no uriage wa dou da?
女: この分だと、先月よりほんのわずか上回る程度です。Onna: Kono bun da to, sengetsu yori honno wazuka uwamawaru teido desu.
男: 雑誌に広告を出して急激に伸びたけれど、それも一時的だったということだね。Otoko: Zasshi ni koukoku o dashite kyuugeki ni nobita keredo, sore mo ichijiteki datta to iu koto da ne.
女: はい、そのあと落ち込んでからは、ずっと横ばいで…。Onna: Hai, sono ato ochikonde kara wa, zutto yokobai de…
男: やっぱり、宣伝しないとだめということか。しかし、広告を出し続けるのは、厳しいものがあるね…別の方法も検討してみるか。Otoko: Yappari, senden shinai to dame to iu koto ka. Shikashi, koukoku o dashitsuzukeru no wa, kibishii mono ga aru ne … betsu no houhou mo kentou shite miru ka.
#OptionCorrect?
1大幅に上回るOohaba ni uwamawaru — substantially exceedsNo
2わずかに下回るWazuka ni shitamawaru — slightly falls belowNo
3落ち込みが激しいOchikomi ga hageshii — the drop is severeNo
4ほとんど変わらないHotondo kawaranai — barely changedCorrect

Why: The woman says this month is only ほんのわずか上回る程度 ("only just barely above" last month), and later confirms the trend has been 横ばい (flat) since the initial ad-driven spike faded. "Barely above" + "flat" together mean effectively unchanged — option 4. Option 1 (a big rise) mistakes the earlier temporary ad spike for the current month; option 2 has the direction backwards (it's slightly above, not below); option 3 confuses the post-spike dip with the current month's actual (flat) performance.

3番 CD1, Track 63

男の人が話しています。トイレの不具合の原因は何でしたか。Otoko no hito ga hanashite imasu. Toire no fuguai no gen'in wa nan deshita ka.

EN: A man is speaking. What was the cause of the toilet's malfunction?   HI: एक पुरुष बोल रहा है। शौचालय की खराबी का कारण क्या था?   GU: એક પુરુષ બોલી રહ્યો છે. શૌચાલયની ખરાબીનું કારણ શું હતું?

Script: もうトイレは使えるようになりました。水も流れて、詰まりもありません。原因は紙が詰まったとか、水道に問題があったわけではなく、大もとの排水溝にあったので、恐らく、お風呂の排水にも何らかの影響が出ていたはずです。庭にある大もとの排水溝に、庭木の根が伸びて、いっぱいに広がって、水の流れを邪魔していました。根を切って取り除きましたので問題はなくなりましたが、排水溝の近くの庭木を別の場所に移すか、あるいは根が伸びていないか、年に一度くらい点検して、伸びていたら切って取り除くようにするなどの対策が必要です。Mou toire wa tsukaeru you ni narimashita. Mizu mo nagarete, tsumari mo arimasen. Gen'in wa kami ga tsumatta toka, suidou ni mondai ga atta wake dewa naku, oomoto no haisuikou ni atta node, osoraku, ofuro no haisui ni mo nanraka no eikyou ga dete ita hazu desu. Niwa ni aru oomoto no haisuikou ni, niwaki no ne ga nobite, ippai ni hirogatte, mizu no nagare o jama shite imashita. Ne o kitte torinozokimashita node mondai wa nakunarimashita ga, haisuikou no chikaku no niwaki o betsu no basho ni utsusu ka, arui wa ne ga nobite inai ka, nen ni ichido kurai tenken shite, nobite itara kitte torinozoku you ni suru nado no taisaku ga hitsuyou desu.
#OptionCorrect?
1トイレに紙が詰まっていたToire ni kami ga tsumatte itaNo
2水道管に不具合があったSuidoukan ni fuguai ga attaNo
3木の根が排水溝に詰まっていたKi no ne ga haisuikou ni tsumatte itaCorrect
4庭木が枝を伸ばしていたNiwaki ga eda o nobashite itaNo — the problem was roots, not branches

Why: The speaker explicitly rules out both distractor causes first — "not clogged paper, not a water-pipe problem" (紙が詰まったとか、水道に問題があったわけではなく) — before naming the real cause: a garden tree's roots grew into the main drainage channel and blocked the water flow (庭木の根が伸びて…水の流れを邪魔していました). Option 4 is a near-miss trap: it was the tree's roots (根), not its branches (枝), that caused the blockage.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Explanation-type items reward tracking direction and degree words precisely, and distinguishing a stated root cause from the surface symptoms or ruled-out possibilities mentioned along the way:

PairWhat confuses learnersHow to catch itSeen in
急激に伸びる vs 徐々に伸びる Both mean "increase," but one is a spike and one is a steady climb — mixing them up flips the shape of the described graph 急激・急速 = sudden/rapid (often temporary, e.g. after an ad campaign); 徐々に = gradual, sustained 1a, 2番
上回る vs 下回る Near-mirror-image verbs — one means "exceed," the other "fall below" 上 = above/up, 下 = below/down — match the kanji to the direction, don't rely on sound alone 1a, 2番
Root cause vs. ruled-out cause vs. symptom A speaker may name two or three candidate causes before revealing the real one, and options are built from each candidate Listen for explicit rejection phrases (～わけではなく, "it's not that...") — whatever is rejected is a trap option, and the real cause usually follows immediately after 3番
根 (root) vs 枝 (branch) Both are parts of a tree and appear in the same sentence shape ("the tree's ___ did X") 根 grows underground/into pipes and drains; 枝 grows above ground — a drainage-clog question can only be about 根 3番
Exam trap: When a graph/trend description mentions a temporary spike (一時的, "temporary") before settling into a flat trend, N1 listening loves to offer an option describing the spike itself as if it were the current, ongoing state (as in 2番's 大幅に上回る). Always identify which part of the timeline the question is actually asking about.
