1 Test Structure

Key idea

この章で覚えた表現を、実際の問題形式で確認しよう！Kono shou de oboeta hyougen o, jissai no mondai keishiki de kakunin shiyou! — "Confirm what you memorized in this chapter, in the actual question format!"

This lesson doesn't introduce new vocabulary — it's a cumulative test drawing on all four skills from Lessons 1–4 (information, instructions, explanation, theme/opinion), delivered in three different N1 聴解 question shapes:

  • 問題I (10点×3) — question asked first, then the talk, then pick the best of 4 printed options (課題理解-style: "what will this person do next").
  • 問題II (10点×3) — question asked first, then you're given time to read the 4 printed options, then the talk plays (ポイント理解-style: focus on one specific point).
  • 問題III (20点×2) — no question or options beforehand; you hear the whole talk cold, and only then hear the question and options read aloud (概要理解-style: grasp the overall gist).

1番 CD2, Track 01

電話で、男の人と女の人が話しています。男の人はこれから何をしますか。Denwa de, otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito wa kore kara nani o shimasu ka.

EN: A man and woman are talking on the phone. What will the man do next?   HI: फ़ोन पर एक पुरुष और महिला बात कर रहे हैं। पुरुष अब क्या करेगा?   GU: ફોન પર એક પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી વાત કરી રહ્યાં છે. પુરુષ હવે શું કરશે?

Script:
男: もしもし、そちらの製品をダウンロードしたいんですが、銀行振込みの場合は、領収書ってもらえるのでしょうか。Otoko: Moshimoshi, sochira no seihin o daunroodo shitain desu ga, ginkou furikomi no baai wa, ryoushuusho tte moraeru no deshou ka.

Hensousaki o kinyuu shita teikei no fuutou, kochira wa hachijuu-en kitte o o-hari kudasai. Sono fuutou ni ginkou furikomi no hikae to o-kyakusama bangou, uketsuke bangou, ryoushuusho no atena o kaita memo o irete itadaite…
男: へえー、面倒なんですねえ。Otoko: Heee, mendou nan desu nee.
女: あのー、インターネットから銀行振込みする場合も、振込みの事実が確認できる画面を印刷すれば、それも領収書の代わりとして利用できますが…。Onna: Anoo, intaanetto kara ginkou furikomi suru baai mo, furikomi no jijitsu ga kakunin dekiru gamen o insatsu sureba, sore mo ryoushuusho no kawari to shite riyou dekimasu ga…
男: あ、そうなの。じゃそうします。Otoko: A, sou na no. Ja sou shimasu.
#OptionCorrect?
1銀行口座に振込みをするGinkou kouza ni furikomi o suru — make the bank transferCorrect
2振込みの控えを印刷するFurikomi no hikae o insatsu suru — print the transfer receiptNo — this is the step after, not the immediate next action
3返送用の封筒を用意するHensouyou no fuutou o youi suru — prepare a return envelopeNo — that was the more troublesome paper-mail option he didn't choose
4メモに必要事項を書くMemo ni hitsuyou jikou o kaku — write the required details on a noteNo — same reason as option 3

Why: The woman offers a simpler path: if he transfers via internet banking, printing the confirmation screen can substitute for a receipt. He accepts with そうします ("I'll do that"). Since he hasn't transferred the money yet, his very next action must be to make the bank transfer itself (option 1) — printing the confirmation (option 2) necessarily comes after that, and the envelope/memo (options 3–4) belong to the paper-mail process he's avoiding.

#OptionCorrect?
1顔Kao — faceNo — only an indirect "resembles his uncle" comment
2体型Taikei — body type/buildCorrect
3体質Taishitsu — physical constitutionNo — only a speculative future risk, not a stated resemblance

Why: He explicitly rules out personality (似ても似つきません — "doesn't resemble him at all," takes after his mother instead) and only vaguely connects the face (via a comment that he looks like his father's brother, not the father himself). Body type gets the most direct, doubly-confirmed statement: 体型もおやじ似で、がっしりしています ("build takes after Dad too, sturdy") plus others noting his back and gait are そっくり (just alike) — the clearest, most direct match, and the only one about constitution (体質) is pure speculation about future cancer risk, not an actual observed resemblance.

The single highest-value habit from this whole chapter is to track when and how certainly each detail was said, not just whether it was said.
