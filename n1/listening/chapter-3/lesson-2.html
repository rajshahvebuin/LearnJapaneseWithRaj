1 Listening Strategy

Key idea

指示を聞くための語彙や表現を覚えよう！Shiji o kiku tame no goi ya hyougen o oboeyou! — "Learn the vocabulary and expressions needed to follow instructions!"

"Instruction" listening items (a doctor telling a patient how to take medicine, a conductor's cabin announcement, a boss directing a subordinate) live or die on domain vocabulary — if you don't know the noun for the thing being managed, you can't track which instruction applies to it. Below are the four settings this lesson drills: hospital/pharmacy, train-cabin manners, school/university, and company/workplace.

1番 CD1, Track 58

医者と患者が話しています。医者の指示と合わないものはどれですか。合わないものです。Isha to kanja ga hanashite imasu. Isha no shiji to awanai mono wa dore desu ka. Awanai mono desu.

EN: A doctor and patient are talking. Which option does NOT match the doctor's instructions?   HI: डॉक्टर और मरीज़ बात कर रहे हैं। कौन-सा विकल्प डॉक्टर के निर्देशों से मेल नहीं खाता?   GU: ડૉક્ટર અને દર્દી વાત કરી રહ્યાં છે. કયો વિકલ્પ ડૉક્ટરની સૂચનાઓ સાથે મેળ ખાતો નથી?

Script:
医者: 喉に異常はないので、安心してください。ただ、症状が改善されるように薬を出しておきましょう。一般的なトローチを出しますが、服用の仕方に注意してください。いつ舐めてもかまわないのですが、服用したあと30分は飲食をしないほうが効果があります。それと、寝るときにマスクをするといいでしょう。Isha: Nodo ni ijou wa nai node, anshin shite kudasai. Tada, shoujou ga kaizen sareru you ni kusuri o dashite okimashou. Ippanteki na torouchi o dashimasu ga, fukuyou no shikata ni chuui shite kudasai. Itsu namete mo kamawanai no desu ga, fukuyou shita ato sanjuppun wa inshoku o shinai hou ga kouka ga arimasu. Sore to, neru toki ni masuku o suru to ii deshou.
患者: マスクは使ってるんですが、寝ている間に自分で取っちゃうんですよね。Kanja: Masuku wa tsukatterun desu ga, nete iru aida ni jibun de tocchaun desu yo ne.
医者: 鼻と口を覆ってしまうと、煩わしくて外してしまうのでしょう。口だけを覆うようにしてみてください。あとは、喉に刺激のある食べ物や飲み物は控えてください。Isha: Hana to kuchi o ootte shimau to, wazurawashikute hazushite shimau no deshou. Kuchi dake o oou you ni shite mite kudasai. Ato wa, nodo ni shigeki no aru tabemono ya nomimono wa hikaete kudasai.
患者: 僕は、お酒もタバコもやらないんですが…。Kanja: Boku wa, osake mo tabako mo yaranain desu ga…
医者: そうですか、それはいいですね。あとは…香辛料や辛いもの、果物などでも、桃とかメロンは、喉に刺激を与えますね。Isha: Sou desu ka, sore wa ii desu ne. Ato wa … koushinryou ya karai mono, kudamono nado demo, momo toka meron wa, nodo ni shigeki o ataemasu ne.
#StatementMatches instructions?
1トローチの服用後30分は飲食しないTorouchi no fukuyou-go sanjuppun wa inshoku shinaiYes — matches
2マスクを就寝時に着用するMasuku o shuushin-ji ni chakuyou suruYes — matches
3酒はできるだけ控えるSake wa dekiru dake hikaeruYes — matches (patient already doesn't drink, but this is still consistent advice)
4果物は何でも積極的に摂取するKudamono wa nan demo sekkyokuteki ni sesshu suruNo — does NOT match (doctor names peach/melon as throat irritants)

Why: The doctor specifically warns that peach and melon (桃とかメロン) irritate the throat, so "actively eat any fruit" directly contradicts the instructions — option 4 is the mismatch. Options 1–3 are all stated or implied directly: 30-minute wait after the throat lozenge, wearing the mask at night (mouth only), and avoiding alcohol/tobacco (which the patient already doesn't do, so it's trivially consistent).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This lesson's vocabulary comes in built-in opposite pairs — mixing them up is the exact trap N1 listening exploits:

PairWhat confuses learnersHow to catch itSeen in
内服薬 vs 外用薬 Both are "medicine," but one is swallowed and the other is applied/patched/dropped externally 内服 contains 服 (used with 服用する, "to take internally"); 外用 explicitly marks "for external use" 1a, 1番
下書き vs 清書 Sound like near-synonyms for "written document," but one is a draft and one is the polished final version 清 ("clean/pure") signals the finished, submittable version; 下 ("under/rough") signals the throwaway draft 1c
配布資料 vs 読み原稿 Both are papers a presenter prepares, but only one gets handed out to the audience 配布 ("distribute") is the audience's copy (formal だ・である style); 読み原稿 is what the speaker reads aloud from (です・ます style) — never distributed 1c
「合うもの」 vs 「合わないもの」 Two question stems that look almost identical but ask for opposite answers 合う = "matches," 合わない = "doesn't match" — always re-read the question stem before scanning options, since this lesson uses both types back to back (1番 asks 合わない, 2番 asks 合う) 1番, 2番
Exam trap: In 3番, the boss's speech is full of market-research vocabulary, tempting you to answer with whichever option repeats the most words you recognized (市場調査, 分析, 割引 all appear in the distractor options). The real instruction is buried in the very last sentence — always weight the final directive most heavily when a speaker rambles before giving it.
