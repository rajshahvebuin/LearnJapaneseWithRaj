1 Listening Strategy

Key idea

表現に注意して趣旨を聞き取ろう！Hyougen ni chuui shite shushi o kikitorou! — "Pay attention to the expressions and catch the gist!"

Real speech (unlike a written essay) rarely lays out its reasoning in tidy, logical order — a speaker rambles, lists several points, then drops the one that actually matters. This lesson trains you to recognize the grammar patterns that flag "this is my real point" (as opposed to a point being conceded or dismissed) and "this is my conclusion" (as opposed to a rejected option), so you can find the speaker's actual opinion inside a messy monologue.

1b. 結論 (Ketsuron) — "I will do A" vs. "I will not do A"

口語的表現は必ずしも因果関係を順序立てて示すとは限らないので、結論と理由の表現に注意しよう。Kougo-teki hyougen wa kanarazushimo ingakankei o junjodatete shimesu to wa kagiranai node, ketsuron to riyuu no hyougen ni chuui shiyou. — "Spoken language doesn't always lay out cause-and-effect in tidy order, so pay close attention to the conclusion- and reason-marking expressions."

1c. 要約の例 (Youyaku no rei) — vocabulary clusters for summarizing a topic

1番 CD1, Track 64

男の人と女の人が話しています。女の人の意見はどれですか。Otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito no iken wa dore desu ka.

EN: A man and woman are talking. What is the woman's opinion?   HI: एक पुरुष और महिला बात कर रहे हैं। महिला की राय क्या है?   GU: એક પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી વાત કરી રહ્યાં છે. સ્ત્રીનો અભિપ્રાય શું છે?

Script:
女: ねえねえ、今さあ、ブレーキついてない自転車が流行ってるんだって！最初はね、ついてるらしいんだけど、買ってから取っちゃうんだって。そのほうが、かっこいいんだとか言っちゃって。Onna: Nee nee, ima saa, bureeki tsuitenai jitensha ga hayatterun datte! Saisho wa ne, tsuiteru rashiin da kedo, katte kara tocchaun datte. Sono hou ga, kakkoiin da to ka icchatte.
男: 知ってる。で、止まれなくて無謀運転して注意されると、だってブレーキないんだもーんって言うんだろ。ひどいもんだね。Otoko: Shitteru. De, tomarenakute mubou unten shite chuui sareru to, datte bureeki nain damoon tte iun daro. Hidoi mon da ne.
女: 自転車も免許制にすべきだと思うな。罰則がないからやってんでしょ、危ないこと。一人で勝手にやってんならいいけど、絶対、歩行者や車も巻き込むでしょ？自賠責保険にも入らせるしかないでしょ。Onna: Jitensha mo menkyosei ni subeki da to omou na. Bassoku ga nai kara yatteru n desho, abunai koto. Hitori de katte ni yatteru nara ii kedo, zettai, hokousha ya kuruma mo makikomu desho? Jibaiseki hoken ni mo hairaseru shika nai desho.
男: そうだよ。俺なんか、朝、駅まで歩くのも命がけだよ。いつ自転車がすっ飛んでくるか、油断も隙もあったもんじゃない。Otoko: Sou da yo. Ore nanka, asa, eki made aruku no mo inochigake da yo. Itsu jitensha ga suttonde kuru ka, yudan mo suki mo atta mon ja nai.
#OptionCorrect?
1自転車専用の道路をつくるJitensha senyou no douro o tsukuru — build bicycle-only roadsNo
2無謀運転の車に対する罰則を強化するMubou unten no kuruma ni taisuru bassoku o kyouka suru — strengthen penalties for reckless-driving carsNo — she's talking about bicycles, not cars
3自転車も免許制にして自賠責保険に加入させるJitensha mo menkyosei ni shite jibaiseki hoken ni kanyuu saseru — license bicycles too, and require mandatory insuranceCorrect
4ブレーキのない自転車を売る業者を取り締まるBureeki no nai jitensha o uru gyousha o torishimaru — crack down on sellers of brakeless bicyclesNo

Why: The woman states her opinion in a direct 結論 pattern: 自転車も免許制にすべきだと思う ("I think bicycles too should be brought under a licensing system") and follows it with 自賠責保険にも入らせるしかない ("there's no choice but to make them get mandatory insurance too") — both patterns from §1b. Option 3 is a verbatim match of these two demands together; the others describe things neither speaker proposed.

3番 CD1, Track 66

先生と生徒の母親が話しています。先生の言いたいことは何ですか。Sensei to seito no hahaoya ga hanashite imasu. Sensei no iitai koto wa nan desu ka.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every item in this lesson hides its answer behind a concession-then-pivot shape — catching the pivot word is the whole game:

PatternWhat confuses learnersHow to catch itSeen in
Bはいいが／もさることながら…A The conceded point (B) is often stated first, longer, and more vividly than the real point (A) — learners latch onto B Whatever comes after が／けど／のに／のは… is the speaker's real point; the clause before it is being granted, not asserted 2番, 3番
Aするべきだ vs Aするつもりはない Both are firm-sounding conclusions, but one commits to action and the other explicitly refuses it べきだ／しかない／ざるを得ない = will act; つもりはない／見送る = will not act — check which side of §1b's table the final verb lands on 1a, 1番
要約カテゴリー vs 実際の発言 An abstract topic-label option (e.g. 効率化の利点と欠点) never appears word-for-word in the speech — you must map concrete details onto the label List the concrete points made (benefit, then drawback) and ask which one-line label covers both — don't search for the label's literal characters in the script 1c, 2番
Exam trap: In 3番, the mother's remark ("at home he barely talks") is real and true, but it describes a different context (home) than the question being asked (the teacher's opinion about class). N1 listening routinely plants a true-but-irrelevant statement from a second speaker as a distractor — always re-anchor to whose opinion, and which setting, the question actually asks about.
