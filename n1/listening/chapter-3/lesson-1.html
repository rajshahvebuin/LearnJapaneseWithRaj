1 Listening Strategy

Key idea

表現を覚えて予測しよう！Hyougen o oboete yosoku shiyou! — "Memorize the set expressions and predict what's coming!"

"Informational" broadcasts (weather reports, traffic updates, disaster alerts) are built almost entirely out of fixed template phrases. You will never need to guess their meaning in real time — instead, memorize the templates below so that the moment you hear the first few words, you already know the shape of the sentence and can listen for just the one changing detail (a place name, a number, a direction).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every "information" broadcast in this lesson runs on the same reflex: a template phrase tells you instantly whether the news is reassuring or alarming, before you've even parsed the details. Learn to sort every phrase into one of these two buckets on first hearing:

DomainReassuring / improvingAlarming / worseningSeen in
Weather 天気は回復に向かっています・雨は上がる 天気は下り坂です・天気は崩れる・にわか雨 1a, 3番
Traffic 順調に流れています・ダイヤ通り 流れが悪くなっています・欠航・見通しは立っておりません 1b, 2番
Earthquake 津波の恐れはありません 津波警報が出ました・高台に避難してください 1c, 1番
Exam trap: N1 聴解's "which one doesn't match the broadcast" format (放送の内容と合わないものはどれですか, as in 1番) always hides the mismatch in a single number or word buried inside an otherwise faithful summary — here, 3m instead of the broadcast's 1m. Don't just check whether an option sounds plausible; check every number against what was actually said.
