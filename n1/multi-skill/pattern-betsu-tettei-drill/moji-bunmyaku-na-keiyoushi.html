2 Every question — 問題 (p.31)

問題　（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

犬は、人間が感じることのできない（　）においをかぐことができる。Inu wa, ningen ga kanjiru koto no dekinai (___) nioi o kagu koto ga dekiru.

Full sentence: 犬は、人間が感じることのできないかすかなにおいをかぐことができる。Inu wa, ningen ga kanjiru koto no dekinai kasuka na nioi o kagu koto ga dekiru.
EN: Dogs can pick up faint smells that humans cannot sense.
HI: कुत्ते वह हल्की-सी गंध सूँघ सकते हैं जिसे इंसान महसूस नहीं कर पाते।
GU: કૂતરાં એવી આછી ગંધ સૂંઘી શકે છે જે માણસ અનુભવી શકતો નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1かすかなkasuka nafaintहल्काઆછું
2のどかなnodoka napeaceful, tranquilशांत, सुकून भराશાંત, નિરાંતવાળું
3じゅうなんなjuunan naflexible (柔軟な)लचीलाલવચીક
4せつじつなsetsujitsu naearnest, pressing (切実な)गंभीर, तीव्रગંભીર, તીવ્ર

Answer 1 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): かすかな means "very small, weak"; here わずかな would also work.

(2)

このアパートは、日当たりが（　）で、駅からも近いです。Kono apaato wa, hiatari ga (___) de, eki kara mo chikai desu.

Full sentence: このアパートは、日当たりが良好で、駅からも近いです。Kono apaato wa, hiatari ga ryoukou de, eki kara mo chikai desu.
EN: This apartment gets plenty of sunlight and is close to the station too.
HI: इस अपार्टमेंट में धूप अच्छी आती है और यह स्टेशन के भी पास है।
GU: આ અપાર્ટમેન્ટમાં તડકો સારો આવે છે અને તે સ્ટેશનથી પણ નજીક છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1視野shiyafield of view, outlook (noun)दृष्टि-क्षेत्रદૃષ્ટિક્ષેત્ર
2新鮮shinsenfreshताज़ाતાજું
3風景fuukeiscenery (noun)दृश्यદૃશ્ય
4良好ryoukougood, favourableअच्छाસારું

Answer 4 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): 日当たりが良好 means 日当たりがよい (gets good sunlight). (added, not in book) 新鮮 is a な-adjective too, but it is for food, air or impressions, not sunlight.

(3)

このマンションなら、サラリーマンでも（　）価格で購入できる。Kono manshon nara, sarariiman demo (___) kakaku de kounyuu dekiru.

Full sentence: このマンションなら、サラリーマンでもてごろな価格で購入できる。Kono manshon nara, sarariiman demo tegoro na kakaku de kounyuu dekiru.
EN: With this condominium, even an ordinary salaried worker can buy at an affordable price.
HI: इस फ़्लैट को तो एक आम नौकरीपेशा व्यक्ति भी वाजिब दाम में खरीद सकता है।
GU: આ ફ્લેટ તો સામાન્ય નોકરિયાત પણ પોસાય તેવી કિંમતે ખરીદી શકે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1明確なmeikaku naclear, definiteस्पष्टસ્પષ્ટ
2対等なtaitou naequal, on equal termsबराबरी काસમોવડિયું
3てごろなtegoro nareasonable, affordableवाजिब, किफ़ायतीપોસાય તેવું
4ぞんざいなzonzai nacareless, sloppyलापरवाहબેદરકાર

Answer 3 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): てごろな means "just right (for doing something)".

(4)

犯人逮捕の（　）てがかりが見つからないまま、1カ月が過ぎてしまった。Hannin taiho no (___) tegakari ga mitsukaranai mama, ikkagetsu ga sugite shimatta.

Full sentence: 犯人逮捕の有力なてがかりが見つからないまま、1カ月が過ぎてしまった。Hannin taiho no yuuryoku na tegakari ga mitsukaranai mama, ikkagetsu ga sugite shimatta.
EN: A month has gone by without any strong lead toward arresting the culprit.
HI: अपराधी को पकड़ने का कोई ठोस सुराग़ मिले बिना एक महीना बीत गया।
GU: ગુનેગારને પકડવા માટે કોઈ મજબૂત કડી મળ્યા વિના એક મહિનો વીતી ગયો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1気軽なkigaru nacasual, easy-goingबेझिझक, सहजસહજ, હળવું
2知的なchiteki naintellectualबौद्धिकબૌદ્ધિક
3有力なyuuryoku nastrong, promisingठोस, प्रबलમજબૂત, પ્રબળ
4むじゃきなmujaki nainnocent, naiveमासूमનિર્દોષ, ભોળું

Answer 3 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): 有力な expresses having strong power to make something happen (here: to lead to an arrest).

(5)

あれこれ言われる前に自分から社長の職を辞めたのは（　）判断だった。Arekore iwareru mae ni jibun kara shachou no shoku o yameta no wa (___) handan datta.

Full sentence: あれこれ言われる前に自分から社長の職を辞めたのは賢明な判断だった。Arekore iwareru mae ni jibun kara shachou no shoku o yameta no wa kenmei na handan datta.
EN: Stepping down as president on his own before people started saying things was a wise decision.
HI: लोगों की बातें शुरू होने से पहले ही खुद अध्यक्ष पद छोड़ देना समझदारी भरा फ़ैसला था।
GU: લોકો કંઈ કહે તે પહેલાં જાતે જ પ્રમુખપદ છોડી દેવું એ ડહાપણભર્યો નિર્ણય હતો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1素朴なsoboku nasimple, unsophisticatedसीधा-सादाસીધુંસાદું
2賢明なkenmei nawiseसमझदारડહાપણભર્યું
3なめらかなnameraka nasmoothचिकना, सहजલીસું, સરળ
4ひそかなhisoka nasecret, privateगुप्तગુપ્ત

Answer 2 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): 賢明な means "wise and appropriate"; it is mostly used as 賢明な判断 (decision) or 賢明な選択 (choice).

(6)

（　）リズムに乗って、楽しく踊りましょう。(___) rizumu ni notte, tanoshiku odorimashou.

Full sentence: 軽快なリズムに乗って、楽しく踊りましょう。Keikai na rizumu ni notte, tanoshiku odorimashou.
EN: Let's move to the light, lively rhythm and dance and have fun.
HI: हल्की-फुल्की, उछलती लय पर मज़े से नाचें।
GU: હળવી, ઉમંગભરી લય પર મજાથી નાચીએ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1軽快なkeikai nalight and livelyहल्का-फुल्का, जोशीलाહળવું, ઉમંગભર્યું
2軽率なkeisotsu narash, carelessजल्दबाज़, बिना सोचेઉતાવળિયું, વિચાર્યા વિના
3気軽なkigaru nacasual, easy-goingबेझिझकસહજ
4手軽なtegaru naeasy, handyआसान, सुविधाजनकસરળ, સહેલું

Answer 1 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): 軽快な describes something that makes you feel pleasantly light. It can also describe light, quick-looking movements. (added, not in book) All four share 軽 — the "same kanji" trap. 軽率な is negative (thoughtless), 気軽な/手軽な are about ease.

(7)

一つ問題が解決したと思ったら、（　）問題が発生した。Hitotsu mondai ga kaiketsu shita to omottara, (___) mondai ga hassei shita.

Full sentence: 一つ問題が解決したと思ったら、あらたな問題が発生した。Hitotsu mondai ga kaiketsu shita to omottara, arata na mondai ga hassei shita.
EN: Just when I thought one problem had been solved, a new one came up.
HI: जैसे ही लगा कि एक समस्या सुलझ गई, एक नई समस्या खड़ी हो गई।
GU: એક સમસ્યા ઉકલી એવું લાગ્યું ત્યાં જ નવી સમસ્યા ઊભી થઈ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1しとやかなshitoyaka nagraceful, ladylikeशालीन, सुशीलશાલીન, સૌમ્ય
2きよらかなkiyoraka napure, clearनिर्मल, पवित्रનિર્મળ, પવિત્ર
3あらたなarata nanewनयाનવું
4がんこなganko nastubbornज़िद्दीજિદ્દી

Answer 3 (book key, p.32). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): Written 新たな; it means the same as 新しい.

(8)

大きな失敗をすると、だれでも（　）気持ちになるものだ。Ookina shippai o suru to, dare demo (___) kimochi ni naru mono da.

Full sentence: 大きな失敗をすると、だれでもゆううつな気持ちになるものだ。Ookina shippai o suru to, dare demo yuuutsu na kimochi ni naru mono da.
EN: When you make a big mistake, anyone ends up feeling gloomy.
HI: बड़ी ग़लती करने पर कोई भी उदास हो ही जाता है।
GU: મોટી ભૂલ થાય ત્યારે કોઈ પણ ઉદાસ થઈ જ જાય છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1そうだいなsoudai nagrand, magnificent (壮大な)भव्य, विशालભવ્ય, વિશાળ
2ぞんざいなzonzai nacareless, sloppyलापरवाहબેદરકાર
3めんどうなmendou natroublesomeझंझट भराકડાકૂટભર્યું
4ゆううつなyuuutsu nagloomy, depressedउदासઉદાસ

(10)

あなたの発想は、ほかの人にはないとても（　）ものですね。Anata no hassou wa, hoka no hito ni wa nai totemo (___) mono desu ne.

Full sentence: あなたの発想は、ほかの人にはないとてもユニークなものですね。Anata no hassou wa, hoka no hito ni wa nai totemo yuniiku na mono desu ne.
EN: Your way of thinking is really unique — nobody else has ideas like that.
HI: तुम्हारी सोच बहुत अनोखी है, जो किसी और में नहीं।
GU: તમારી વિચારસરણી ખૂબ અનોખી છે, જે બીજા કોઈમાં નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1トーンなtoon na(トーン "tone" is a noun; not used with な)(संज्ञा है, な के साथ नहीं)(નામ છે, な સાથે નહીં)
2ニュアンスなnyuansu na(ニュアンス "nuance" is a noun; not used with な)(संज्ञा है, な के साथ नहीं)(નામ છે, な સાથે નહીં)
3ユニークなyuniiku nauniqueअनोखाઅનોખું

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Built from this drill's distractors (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
かすかなkasuka na vs のどかなnodoka na faint vs peaceful かすかなにおい・音・希望; のどかな田舎・春の日.
良好ryoukou vs 新鮮shinsen good (condition) vs fresh 日当たり・経過・関係が良好; 野菜・空気が新鮮.
てごろなtegoro na vs ぞんざいなzonzai na affordable / handy vs careless てごろな価格・大きさ; ぞんざいな言葉遣い・扱い.
有力なyuuryoku na vs 知的なchiteki na strong, promising vs intellectual 有力な手がかり・候補; 知的な会話・女性.
賢明なkenmei na vs 素朴なsoboku na wise vs simple, unsophisticated 賢明な判断; 素朴な疑問・人柄.
軽快なkeikai na vs 軽率なkeisotsu na light and lively vs rash 軽快なリズム・足取り; 軽率な発言 (thoughtless remark).
気軽なkigaru na vs 手軽なtegaru na casual (feeling) vs easy (effort) 気軽に相談する (no hesitation); 手軽な料理 (little effort).
ゆううつなyuuutsu na vs めんどうなmendou na gloomy (mood) vs troublesome (task) 気持ちがゆううつ; 手続きがめんどう.
シックなshikku na vs ドライなdorai na chic vs unsentimental シックな服; ドライな性格 / ドライな関係.
ユニークなyuniiku na vs ユーモアのあるyuumoa no aru unique vs humorous ユーモア is a noun — ユーモアのある人, never ユーモアな.
Exam trap: item (10) tests whether a katakana word can take な at all — トーン, ニュアンス and ユーモア are nouns, so only ユニークな is grammatical. Item (6) is the "same kanji" trap: four 軽〜 words, only one of which describes a rhythm.
