1 Grammar Points

The book has no grammar table for 文章の文法. The points below are the forms the correct answers use, explained for this site — all (added, not in book); the only book sentences are the blank sentences themselves.

文章の文法 — how to approach passage cloze (from p.134, summarised; not the book's text)

EN: In 文章の文法 you read a passage with five numbered blanks and pick particles, conjunctions, adverbs or sentence endings that fit the flow of the whole text. The book's advice is to practise reading while analysing how each conjunction links the sentences, what each sentence ending expresses, and how the paragraphs relate. Its 解説 for these drills always argues from the context: what comes just before and just after the blank.

HI: 文章の文法 में पाँच क्रमांकित रिक्त स्थानों वाला लेख पढ़कर, पूरे लेख के प्रवाह के अनुसार कण, संयोजक, क्रियाविशेषण या वाक्यांत चुनने होते हैं। किताब की सलाह: पढ़ते समय देखें कि संयोजक वाक्यों को कैसे जोड़ते हैं, वाक्यांत क्या दिखाते हैं और अनुच्छेदों का आपसी संबंध क्या है। इसका 解説 हमेशा रिक्त स्थान से ठीक पहले और बाद के संदर्भ से तर्क देता है।

GU: 文章の文法 માં પાંચ ક્રમાંકિત ખાલી જગ્યાવાળું લખાણ વાંચીને, આખા લખાણના પ્રવાહ મુજબ કણ, સંયોજક, ક્રિયાવિશેષણ કે વાક્યાંત પસંદ કરવાના હોય છે. પુસ્તકની સલાહ: વાંચતી વખતે જુઓ કે સંયોજકો વાક્યોને કેવી રીતે જોડે છે, વાક્યાંત શું દર્શાવે છે અને ફકરાઓ વચ્ચે શો સંબંધ છે. તેનું 解説 હંમેશાં ખાલી જગ્યાની બરાબર પહેલાં અને પછીના સંદર્ભથી દલીલ કરે છે.

【1】 〜ものだから

neutral register
Reading~ mono da kara
Meaning in Japanese (added, not in book)〜ので（理由・言い訳）
Meaning (EN)because (explaining a reason, often an excuse or natural feeling)
Meaning (HI)क्योंकि (कारण या सफ़ाई देते हुए)
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે (કારણ કે ખુલાસો આપતાં)
Connection (接続)普通形（な-adj／N は 〜な）＋ ものだから
RegisterSpoken-neutral (casual: もんだから)
Typical useExplains why someone did something, often with a sense of "it couldn't be helped". The result is a fact, not a command or request.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV(普通形) ＋ ものだから甘えてみたいものだからamaete mitai mono da kara
な-adj / Noun(added, not in book) 〜な ＋ ものだから静かなものだからshizuka na mono da kara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [1]) 老人の方もやはり誰かに甘えてみたいものだから、何やかや要求する。Roujin no hou mo yahari dare ka ni amaete mitai mono da kara, naniyakaya youkyuu suru. The elderly people, too, want to lean on someone after all, so they ask for all sorts of things. बुज़ुर्ग भी आख़िर किसी पर निर्भर होना चाहते हैं, इसलिए तरह-तरह की माँगें करते हैं। વડીલો પણ આખરે કોઈના પર આધાર રાખવા માંગે છે, એટલે જાતજાતની માગણીઓ કરે છે.
(added, not in book) 道が混んでいたものだから、遅れてしまいました。Michi ga konde ita mono da kara, okurete shimaimashita. The roads were crowded, so I ended up being late. सड़कों पर भीड़ थी, इसलिए देर हो गई। રસ્તા પર ભીડ હતી, એટલે મોડું થઈ ગયું.

Point added for this site — the 文章の文法 drills have no この文法をマスターしよう table. 甘える = "to depend on / be pampered by someone"; 〜てみたい softens it ("would like to try").

【2】 しかも

neutral register
Readingshikamo
Meaning in Japanese (added, not in book)その上（さらに付け加える）
Meaning (EN)moreover, what's more, and on top of that
Meaning (HI)इतना ही नहीं, ऊपर से
Meaning (GU)એટલું જ નહીં, ઉપરથી
Connection (接続)文。しかも、文 ／ 〜、しかも〜
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAdds a further point in the same direction, often stronger. The book's 解説 glosses it as その程度に留まらず ("not stopping at that level").
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [2]) これは確かに素晴らしいことである。しかも、このような行為を、この人は無償でやっているのだから、ますますそれは「善行」と言うべきである。Kore wa tashika ni subarashii koto de aru. Shikamo, kono you na koui o, kono hito wa mushou de yatte iru no da kara, masumasu sore wa "zenkou" to iu beki de aru. This is certainly wonderful. What's more, this person does it for nothing, so all the more it should be called a "good deed". यह सचमुच अद्भुत है। इतना ही नहीं, यह व्यक्ति यह सब मुफ़्त में करता है, इसलिए इसे और भी ज़्यादा "नेक काम" कहना चाहिए। આ ખરેખર અદ્ભુત છે. એટલું જ નહીં, આ વ્યક્તિ આ બધું મફતમાં કરે છે, એટલે તેને વધુ "સત્કર્મ" કહેવું જોઈએ.

Point added for this site. したがって (therefore) gives a result, ところで (by the way) changes topic, なぜなら (because) needs 〜からだ at the end.

Meaning in Japanese (added, not in book)（結果として）〜という事態になる

Meaning (HI)नतीजा यह होगा कि ~

Typical useStates a consequence that follows from a situation (here: the residents learn to depend, so the staff will have a hard time).

VerbV-る ＋ ことになる苦労することになるkurou suru koto ni naru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [3]) しかし、こんな人が時たま来てくれると、施設の人たちが後で苦労することになる。Shikashi, konna hito ga tokitama kite kureru to, shisetsu no hitotachi ga ato de kurou suru koto ni naru. But when such a person comes now and then, the staff of the home end up having a hard time afterwards. पर जब ऐसा व्यक्ति कभी-कभार आता है, तो बाद में संस्था के कर्मचारियों को परेशानी झेलनी पड़ती है। પણ જ્યારે આવી વ્યક્તિ ક્યારેક આવે છે, ત્યારે પછીથી સંસ્થાના કર્મચારીઓને તકલીફ વેઠવી પડે છે.

Point added for this site. はずだ (苦労させるはずだ) is the wrong voice and a guess; 〜てもいい (permission) and だろうか (doubt) do not state a consequence.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題　次の文章を読んで、（1）から（5）の中に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bunshou o yonde, (1) kara (5) no naka ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Read the passage and choose the best option for each blank (1)–(5). HI: लेख पढ़कर (1) से (5) तक हर रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: લખાણ વાંચીને (1) થી (5) સુધીની દરેક ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. — Book key: 1, 2, 3, 1, 4.

Passage

Source credit printed in the book: 河合隼雄『こころの処方箋』新潮社 (Kawai Hayao, Kokoro no shohousen, Shinchōsha). The passage is not reproduced here.

Passage summary (not the book's text) — EN: The writer says some people enjoy doing "good deeds", but because they are sure they are doing good, they hardly notice the trouble they cause others. His example is a volunteer at an old people's home who is extremely kind to the residents and grants every request, for free. The residents are delighted — but afterwards they stop doing even what they used to do for themselves and lean on the staff, who cannot respond to every demand and want the residents to stay as independent as possible. A resident may even praise the volunteer and call the staff cold, which the staff naturally resent.
HI: लेखक कहता है कि कुछ लोग "नेक काम" करना पसंद करते हैं, पर क्योंकि उन्हें यक़ीन होता है कि वे अच्छा कर रहे हैं, वे दूसरों को होने वाली परेशानी पर ध्यान नहीं देते। उदाहरण: वृद्धाश्रम का एक स्वयंसेवक जो बुज़ुर्गों पर बहुत मेहरबान है और मुफ़्त में उनकी हर माँग पूरी करता है। बुज़ुर्ग ख़ुश होते हैं — पर बाद में वे अपने काम भी ख़ुद करना छोड़कर कर्मचारियों पर निर्भर हो जाते हैं, जबकि कर्मचारी हर माँग पूरी नहीं कर सकते और चाहते हैं कि बुज़ुर्ग जितना हो सके आत्मनिर्भर रहें। कोई बुज़ुर्ग स्वयंसेवक की तारीफ़ और कर्मचारियों को रूखा कह दे, तो कर्मचारियों को बुरा लगता है।
GU: લેખક કહે છે કે કેટલાક લોકોને "સત્કર્મ" કરવું ગમે છે, પણ પોતે સારું કરે છે એવી ખાતરીને કારણે તેઓ બીજાને પડતી તકલીફ પર ધ્યાન નથી આપતા. ઉદાહરણ: વૃદ્ધાશ્રમનો એક સ્વયંસેવક જે વડીલો પર ખૂબ માયાળુ છે અને મફતમાં તેમની દરેક માગણી પૂરી કરે છે. વડીલો ખુશ થાય છે — પણ પછી તેઓ પોતાનાં કામ પણ જાતે કરવાનું છોડીને કર્મચારીઓ પર આધાર રાખવા લાગે છે, જ્યારે કર્મચારીઓ દરેક માગણી પૂરી કરી શકતા નથી અને ઇચ્છે છે કે વડીલો શક્ય તેટલા સ્વાવલંબી રહે. કોઈ વડીલ સ્વયંસેવકનાં વખાણ અને કર્મચારીઓને રૂક્ષ કહે, તો કર્મચારીઓને ખરાબ લાગે છે.
［1］

老人の方もやはり誰かに（　1　）、何やかや要求する。Roujin no hou mo yahari dare ka ni ( 1 ), naniyakaya youkyuu suru.

Full sentence: 老人の方もやはり誰かに甘えてみたいものだから、何やかや要求する。Roujin no hou mo yahari dare ka ni amaete mitai mono da kara, naniyakaya youkyuu suru.
EN: The elderly people, too, want to lean on someone after all, so they ask for all sorts of things.
HI: बुज़ुर्ग भी आख़िर किसी पर निर्भर होना चाहते हैं, इसलिए तरह-तरह की माँगें करते हैं।
GU: વડીલો પણ આખરે કોઈના પર આધાર રાખવા માંગે છે, એટલે જાતજાતની માગણીઓ કરે છે.

Answer 1 (book key, p.176). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): the volunteer is very kind → the residents, wanting to lean on someone, → make all kinds of demands. A reason clause is needed, so 〜たいものだから. Conditions (2, 4) don't fit a general fact, and 3 (past "tried") doesn't explain a habitual demand.

［2］

HI: यह सचमुच अद्भुत है। इतना ही नहीं, यह व्यक्ति यह सब मुफ़्त में करता है, इसलिए इसे और भी ज़्यादा "नेक काम" कहना चाहिए।
GU: આ ખરેખર અદ્ભુત છે. એટલું જ નહીં, આ વ્યક્તિ આ બધું મફતમાં કરે છે, એટલે તેને વધુ "સત્કર્મ" કહેવું જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1したがってshitagattethereforeइसलिएતેથી
2しかもshikamowhat's moreइतना ही नहींએટલું જ નહીં
3ところでtokoro deby the wayवैसेબાય ધ વે
4なぜならnaze narabecauseक्योंकिકારણ કે

Answer 2 (book key). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): the residents become happy and do things they normally can't → not stopping at that (= しかも) → it is done for free. ますます ("all the more") confirms an added point. したがって gives a result, ところで changes topic, なぜなら would need 〜からだ.

［3］

しかし、こんな人が時たま来てくれると、施設の人たちが後で（　3　）。Shikashi, konna hito ga tokitama kite kureru to, shisetsu no hitotachi ga ato de ( 3 ).

Full sentence: しかし、こんな人が時たま来てくれると、施設の人たちが後で苦労することになる。Shikashi, konna hito ga tokitama kite kureru to, shisetsu no hitotachi ga ato de kurou suru koto ni naru.
EN: But when such a person comes now and then, the staff of the home end up having a hard time afterwards.
HI: पर जब ऐसा व्यक्ति कभी-कभार आता है, तो बाद में संस्था के कर्मचारियों को परेशानी झेलनी पड़ती है।
GU: પણ જ્યારે આવી વ્યક્તિ ક્યારેક આવે છે, ત્યારે પછીથી સંસ્થાના કર્મચારીઓને તકલીફ વેઠવી પડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1苦労させるはずだkurou saseru hazu dashould make (others) sufferदूसरों को परेशानी देनी चाहिएબીજાને તકલીફ આપવી જોઈએ
2苦労してもいいkurou shite mo iimay have a hard timeपरेशानी झेलें तो कोई बात नहींતકલીફ વેઠે તો વાંધો નહીં
3苦労することになるkurou suru koto ni naruend up having a hard timeपरेशानी झेलनी पड़ती हैતકલીફ વેઠવી પડે છે
4苦労するだろうかkurou suru darou kawill they have a hard time?क्या परेशानी होगी?શું તકલીફ થશે?

Answer 3 (book key). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): the next sentence says residents who have tasted being pampered stop doing things themselves and depend on others — as a result the staff end up struggling (〜ことになる). 1 is causative and the staff are not the cause; 2 (permission) and 4 (doubt) don't state the consequence.

［4］

施設にいる人たちは、それにいちいち応じて居られないし、やはり老人と言っても出来る限りは自立的に（　4　）。Shisetsu ni iru hitotachi wa, sore ni ichiichi oujite irarenai shi, yahari roujin to itte mo dekiru kagiri wa jiritsuteki ni ( 4 ).

Full sentence: 施設にいる人たちは、それにいちいち応じて居られないし、やはり老人と言っても出来る限りは自立的に生きて欲しいものだ。Shisetsu ni iru hitotachi wa, sore ni ichiichi oujite irarenai shi, yahari roujin to itte mo dekiru kagiri wa jiritsuteki ni ikite hoshii mono da.
EN: The staff can't respond to every single request, and besides, even if they are old, one would like them to live as independently as they can.
HI: कर्मचारी हर माँग पूरी नहीं कर सकते, और भले वे बुज़ुर्ग हों, चाहते हैं कि वे जहाँ तक हो सके आत्मनिर्भर होकर जिएँ।
GU: કર્મચારીઓ દરેક માગણી પૂરી કરી શકતા નથી, અને ભલે તેઓ વડીલ હોય, ઇચ્છીએ કે તેઓ શક્ય હોય ત્યાં સુધી સ્વાવલંબી રીતે જીવે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1生きて欲しいものだikite hoshii mono daone would like them to liveचाहते हैं कि वे जिएँઇચ્છીએ કે તેઓ જીવે
2生きてしまっているikite shimatte iruhave ended up livingजी चुके हैं (अनचाहे)જીવી ચૂક્યા છે (અનિચ્છાએ)
3生きてみたらいいikite mitara iithey should try livingजीकर देखें तो अच्छाજીવી જુએ તો સારું
4生きているだろうikite iru darouare probably livingशायद जी रहे होंगेકદાચ જીવતા હશે

Answer 1 (book key). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): 老人と言っても = "it's true they are old, but" → a wish from the staff's side follows: as far as possible they would like them to live independently (〜て欲しいものだ). 2 and 4 describe facts, 3 is casual advice — none expresses the staff's hope.

［5］

時には、この老人が、ボランティアの誰それさんは（　5　）、ここの施設の人は冷たい人ばかりだ、などと言いだすかもしれない。Toki ni wa, kono roujin ga, borantia no dare sore-san wa ( 5 ), koko no shisetsu no hito wa tsumetai hito bakari da, nado to iidasu kamo shirenai.

Full sentence: 時には、この老人が、ボランティアの誰それさんは優しい人だけど、ここの施設の人は冷たい人ばかりだ、などと言いだすかもしれない。Toki ni wa, kono roujin ga, borantia no dare sore-san wa yasashii hito da kedo, koko no shisetsu no hito wa tsumetai hito bakari da, nado to iidasu kamo shirenai.
EN: Sometimes the resident might even start saying, "Volunteer so-and-so is a kind person, but the staff here are all cold."
HI: कभी-कभी वह बुज़ुर्ग कहने लग सकता है कि "फलाँ स्वयंसेवक तो दयालु है, पर यहाँ के कर्मचारी सब रूखे हैं।"
GU: ક્યારેક એ વડીલ કહેવા લાગે કે "ફલાણા સ્વયંસેવક તો દયાળુ છે, પણ અહીંના કર્મચારીઓ બધા રૂક્ષ છે."

1優しければyasashikerebaif (he) is kindअगर दयालु हो तोજો દયાળુ હોય તો
2冷たいしtsumetai shiis cold, andरूखा है, औरરૂક્ષ છે, અને

Answer 4 (book key). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): the next sentence says this makes the staff unhappy, so the remark must praise the volunteer and criticise the staff — 優しい人だけど sets up exactly that contrast. 1 and 3 are conditions; 2 would call the volunteer cold too.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Built from the options the book set against each blank, plus close relatives (added, not in book).

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ものだからmono da kara because (excuse/feeling) 普通形 ＋ ものだから Reason; result is a fact. Explaining behaviour.
〜からkara because 普通形 ＋ から Neutral reason; can precede requests. Everyday reasons.
しかもshikamo what's more 文。しかも、文 Adds a stronger point. Building an argument.
したがってshitagatte therefore 文。したがって、文 Logical result. Formal conclusions.
なぜならnaze nara because (reason follows) なぜなら〜からだ Needs 〜からだ at the end. Giving a reason after a claim.
〜ことになるkoto ni naru it ends up that V-る ＋ ことになる Consequence of a situation. Results, decisions.
〜はずだhazu da should be (expectation) 普通形 ＋ はずだ Speaker's logical guess. Expectations.
〜て欲しいものだte hoshii mono da I wish (they) would V-て ＋ 欲しいものだ Heartfelt wish about others. Hopes, mild criticism.
〜けどkedo but 普通形 ＋ けど Contrast inside a quote. Comparisons, complaints.
Passage cloze tip: every answer here is decided by the sentence just after the blank — the residents stop doing things themselves (→ [3] 苦労することになる), the staff find it unpleasant (→ [5] 優しい人だけど). Read one sentence past each blank before choosing.
Conjunction blanks: ［2］ is followed by ますます ("all the more"), a sign that a further point is being added → しかも. したがって would need a conclusion, なぜなら a 〜からだ ending.
