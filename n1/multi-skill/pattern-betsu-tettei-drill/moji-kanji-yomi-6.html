Readings in katakana = on-yomi, hiragana = kun-yomi (the ・ marks where okurigana starts, as in ただよ・う). 津 is listed only with its kun-yomi つ; 津波 = つ + なみ (kun + kun). The drill tested 径 inside 直径 (ちょっけい) and 衆 inside 大衆 (たいしゅう), and 廃 inside 廃止 (はいし).

2 Every question — 問題 (p.19)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

問1　相撲の土俵の直径は、4メートル55センチだそうだ。Sumou no dohyou no chokkei wa, yon meetoru gojuugo senchi da sou da.

Underlined word: 土俵dohyou — sumo ring
EN: I hear the diameter of a sumo ring is 4 metres 55 centimetres.
HI: सुना है कि सूमो के अखाड़े का व्यास 4 मीटर 55 सेंटीमीटर होता है।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે સુમોના અખાડાનો વ્યાસ 4 મીટર 55 સેન્ટિમીટર હોય છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1とひょうtohyounot the reading of 土俵(土俵 का पठन नहीं)(土俵નું વાંચન નથી)
2とびょうtobyounot the reading of 土俵(土俵 का पठन नहीं)(土俵નું વાંચન નથી)
3どひょうdohyousumo ringसूमो का अखाड़ाસુમોનો અખાડો

Answer 3 (book key, p.20). Why (added, not in book — the key gives numbers only): 土 = ド here (土地 とち uses ト, but 土俵・土木 use ド), 俵 = ヒョウ (unvoiced; the kun-yomi たわら = straw bag — the ring is made of straw bales). The four options are every voiced / unvoiced combination. 土俵 also appeared in 漢字読み① (横綱の土俵入り).

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Every wrong option above is one of the four trap types from the tips page (see 漢字読み①). These are the ones worth remembering (added, not in book):

土俵dohyou vs 土地tochi sumo ring vs land 土 = ド in 土俵・土木, ト in 土地. 俵 stays ヒョウ.
直径chokkei vs 経験keiken diameter vs experience 径 and 経 share 圣; both ケイ, but only 経 also has キョウ (お経).
津波tsunami vs 電波denpa tsunami vs radio wave 波 = なみ (kun) in 津波; ハ／パ (on) in 電波・波長.

Exam trap: 雰囲気 (ふんいき) is a word many native speakers mispronounce as ふいんき — and that is precisely the option offered. The exam rewards the dictionary reading built kanji by kanji: 雰 フン + 囲 イ + 気 キ. When in doubt, split the word and read each kanji.
