問題を解くコツ — 文法 (summary of p.134, not the book's text)

EN: The N1 grammar section has three question types: 文の文法1 (文法形式の判断, choose the grammar form for a blank in one sentence, 10 questions), 文の文法2 (文の組み立て, put four pieces in order and choose the one at ★, 5 questions) and 文章の文法 (choose particles, conjunctions, adverbs or sentence endings that fit the flow of a passage, 5 questions). The book's advice: (1) for 文の文法1, many particles and connective forms are fixed to particular verbs or expressions, so learn them as combinations — e.g. 31日を限りに, 文句を言った (た-form) あげく; (2) for 文の文法2, practise writing short sentences with each grammar expression; (3) for 文章の文法, practise reading while analysing how each conjunction links the sentences, what each sentence ending expresses and how paragraphs relate.

HI: N1 व्याकरण भाग में तीन प्रकार के प्रश्न हैं: 文の文法1 (एक वाक्य के रिक्त स्थान के लिए सही व्याकरण रूप चुनना, 10 प्रश्न), 文の文法2 (चार टुकड़ों को सही क्रम में रखकर ★ वाला टुकड़ा चुनना, 5 प्रश्न) और 文章の文法 (पूरे लेख के प्रवाह के अनुसार कण, संयोजक, क्रियाविशेषण या वाक्यांत चुनना, 5 प्रश्न)। सलाह: (1) कण और जोड़ने वाले रूप अक्सर ख़ास क्रियाओं/अभिव्यक्तियों के साथ तय होते हैं, इसलिए उन्हें जोड़ी में याद करें; (2) हर व्याकरण अभिव्यक्ति से छोटे वाक्य लिखने का अभ्यास करें; (3) लेख पढ़ते समय देखें कि संयोजक वाक्यों को कैसे जोड़ते हैं, वाक्यांत क्या दिखाते हैं और अनुच्छेदों का आपसी संबंध क्या है।

【1】 Aと(言わん)ばかりにB

neutral register
ReadingA to (iwan) bakari ni B
Book's meaning (意味)まるでAと言うようにBする
Meaning (EN)"as if to say A", B — an action or look that all but says the words A
Meaning (HI)मानो कह रहा हो कि "A", (वैसे) B करना
Meaning (GU)જાણે કહેતો હોય કે "A", (એ રીતે) B કરવું
Connection (接続)命令表現（〜しろ・〜するな・〜しなさい など）＋ と（言わん）ばかりに
RegisterWritten / narrative; slightly literary (言わん = old negative-volitional form of 言う)
Typical useA is usually a command or a short quoted thought; B describes how someone behaves (にらむ, 鳴く, たたく). 言わん can be dropped: 「AとばかりにB」 means the same.
Word typeFormationExample

Short form(added, not in book) A ＋ とばかりにどうだとばかりにdou da to bakari ni
Noun (adverbial)(added, not in book) A と言わんばかりの ＋ N帰れと言わんばかりの顔kaere to iwan bakari no kao
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 静かにしろと（言わん）ばかりに、頭をたたかれた。Shizuka ni shiro to (iwan) bakari ni, atama o tatakareta. I was hit on the head as if to say "Be quiet!" मानो कह रहे हों "चुप रहो!", मेरे सिर पर मारा गया। જાણે કહેતા હોય "ચૂપ રહો!", મારા માથા પર માર્યું.
(book: 解説) 公衆電話を使っていたら、後ろの人に早くしろと（言わん）ばかりににらまれた。Koushuu denwa o tsukatte itara, ushiro no hito ni hayaku shiro to (iwan) bakari ni niramareta. While I was using the public phone, the person behind me glared at me as if to say "Hurry up!" सार्वजनिक फ़ोन इस्तेमाल करते समय पीछे वाले ने मुझे ऐसे घूरा मानो कह रहा हो "जल्दी करो!" જાહેર ફોન વાપરતી વખતે પાછળની વ્યક્તિએ મને એવી રીતે ઘૂરીને જોયું જાણે કહેતી હોય "જલ્દી કરો!"
(book: 問題 (1)) ペットの猫が、そこは俺の座る場所だと言わんばかりに大声で鳴いている。Petto no neko ga, soko wa ore no suwaru basho da to iwan bakari ni oogoe de naite iru. Our pet cat is meowing loudly as if to say "That is my seat!" हमारी पालतू बिल्ली ज़ोर-ज़ोर से म्याऊँ कर रही है, मानो कह रही हो "वह मेरी बैठने की जगह है!" અમારી પાળેલી બિલાડી જોરથી મ્યાઉં કરે છે, જાણે કહેતી હોય "એ મારી બેસવાની જગ્યા છે!"

The person (or animal) does not actually say A — their behaviour makes it obvious. 言わん is the classical negative-volitional ん (= 言おう), so 言う／言った／言って cannot replace it. Compare plain 〜ばかりに ("just because ~, something bad happened"), which is a different pattern.

【2】 Aんがために、B

硬 formal/written
ReadingA n ga tame ni, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aするために、Bする
Meaning (EN)in order to A, (do) B — a stiff, emphatic "for the purpose of"
Meaning (HI)A करने के लिए ही, B करना (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)A કરવા માટે જ, B કરવું (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)動詞の「ない形」＋ んがために　★「する」→「せんがために」
Register硬 — formal, written; slightly stiffer than 〜ために
Typical useShows a strong goal behind a big effort or a questionable act. Also seen as 〜んがための＋N.

Verb (godan)ない形 ＋ んがために得んがために／選ばれんがためにen ga tame ni / erabaren ga tame ni
するせんがために出世せんがためにshusse sen ga tame ni
Noun-modifying(added, not in book) ない形 ＋ んがための ＋ N勝たんがための作戦katan ga tame no sakusen
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 出世せんがために、休日を返上して働いた。Shusse sen ga tame ni, kyuujitsu o henjou shite hataraita. In order to get ahead, I gave up my days off and worked. तरक्की पाने के लिए मैंने छुट्टियाँ छोड़कर काम किया। બઢતી મેળવવા માટે મેં રજાઓ છોડીને કામ કર્યું.
(book: 解説) 次の大会の選手に選ばれんがために、努力を重ねている。Tsugi no taikai no senshu ni erabaren ga tame ni, doryoku o kasanete iru. I keep working hard in order to be chosen as a player for the next tournament. अगली प्रतियोगिता के खिलाड़ी के रूप में चुने जाने के लिए मैं लगातार मेहनत कर रहा हूँ। આગામી સ્પર્ધાના ખેલાડી તરીકે પસંદ થવા માટે હું સતત મહેનત કરું છું.
(book: 問題 (2)) 自分が利益を得んがために、多くの人を犠牲にするなんて許されない。Jibun ga rieki o en ga tame ni, ooku no hito o gisei ni suru nante yurusarenai. Sacrificing many people just so that you yourself can profit is unforgivable. सिर्फ़ ख़ुद को फ़ायदा पहुँचाने के लिए बहुत लोगों की बलि चढ़ाना माफ़ नहीं किया जा सकता। ફક્ત પોતાને ફાયદો મળે એ માટે ઘણા લોકોનો ભોગ આપવો માફ કરી શકાય નહીં.

The trap is the stem: 得る (ichidan) → ない形 得(ない) → 得んがために; する → せん. Forms like 得よう／得る／得ない before がために are wrong. The book notes it is a little harder (more formal) than 〜ために.

【3】 Aを限りに、B

neutral register
ReadingA o kagiri ni, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aを最後に、Bする
Meaning (EN)with A as the last time; from A on (something ends)
Meaning (HI)A को आख़िरी बार मानकर / A के बाद से (कुछ समाप्त)
Meaning (GU)A ને છેલ્લી વાર ગણીને / A પછીથી (કંઈક સમાપ્ત)

Verb / adj(added, not in book) not used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 来年の3月を限りに、教師生活にピリオドを打つ。Rainen no sangatsu o kagiri ni, kyoushi seikatsu ni piriodo o utsu. With next March as the last, I will put a full stop to my life as a teacher. अगले साल मार्च के बाद मैं अपने शिक्षक जीवन पर विराम लगा दूँगा। આવતા વર્ષના માર્ચ પછી હું મારા શિક્ષક જીવન પર પૂર્ણવિરામ મૂકીશ.
(book: 解説 例1) 私の好きな野球選手が今期を限りに、引退してしまう。Watashi no suki na yakyuu senshu ga konki o kagiri ni, intai shite shimau. My favourite baseball player is retiring at the end of this season. मेरा पसंदीदा बेसबॉल खिलाड़ी इस सीज़न के बाद संन्यास ले रहा है। મારો મનપસંદ બેઝબોલ ખેલાડી આ સીઝન પછી નિવૃત્ત થઈ રહ્યો છે.
(book: 解説 例2) 声を限りに応援したが、私のチームは負けてしまった。Koe o kagiri ni ouen shita ga, watashi no chiimu wa makete shimatta. I cheered at the top of my voice, but my team lost. मैंने पूरी आवाज़ से चिल्लाकर हौसला बढ़ाया, फिर भी मेरी टीम हार गई। મેં પૂરા અવાજે ચીસો પાડી પ્રોત્સાહન આપ્યું, છતાં મારી ટીમ હારી ગઈ.
(book: 問題 (3)) 長年続けてきたバレーボールだが、次の大会を限りに、引退するつもりだ。Naganen tsuzukete kita bareebooru da ga, tsugi no taikai o kagiri ni, intai suru tsumori da. I have played volleyball for many years, but I plan to retire after the next tournament. मैं कई सालों से वॉलीबॉल खेल रहा हूँ, पर अगली प्रतियोगिता के बाद संन्यास लेने का इरादा है। હું ઘણાં વર્ષોથી વૉલીબૉલ રમું છું, પણ આગામી સ્પર્ધા પછી નિવૃત્ત થવાનો વિચાર છે.

Think "this is the final one". The book contrasts three N2 forms that also follow a noun: だけに (precisely because A, all the more B), あげく (after doing A, in the end B — usually bad; needs た-form), にあたって (on the occasion of A, when the time for A comes).

硬 formal/written
ReadingA o yogi naku sareru

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Built from the distractors the book set against each correct answer, plus the related forms its 解説 mentions.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜と言わんばかりにto iwan bakari ni as if to say ~ 命令・引用 ＋ と言わんばかりに Behaviour that "says" A without words. Describing a look, gesture or tone.
〜たばかりにta bakari ni just because ~ (bad result) V-た ＋ ばかりに Cause → regretted result; no と. (added, not in book) Blaming one small cause.
〜んがためにn ga tame ni in order to (stiff) V-ない形 ＋ んがために (する→せん) Same purpose as ために but formal and emphatic. Strong goal behind a big effort.
〜ためにtame ni in order to / because V-る ＋ ために／N ＋ の ＋ ために Neutral; attaches to dictionary form, not ない形. (added, not in book) Everyday purpose / reason.
〜を限りにo kagiri ni with ~ as the last 時の表現 ＋ を限りに Marks the final occasion; B = ending (引退・閉店). Announcing that something stops.
〜にあたってni atatte on the occasion of N／V-る ＋ にあたって A starting point / special occasion, not an end. Speeches before an event.
〜を余儀なくされるo yogi naku sareru be forced to ~ N ＋ を余儀なくされる Already happened, against one's will. Reporting forced change.
〜恐れがあるosore ga aru there is a risk of ~ V-る／N の ＋ 恐れがある Only a possibility (future); needs の after a noun. Warnings, forecasts.
〜ゆえのyue no B caused by A N1 ＋ ゆえの ＋ N2 Links two nouns as cause → result. Formal writing.
〜しだいshidai depends on ~ AはBしだいだ Decision depends on B; not a cause. 結果は努力しだいだ.
〜ものをmono o would have ~, but (regret) Vば ＋ V-た ＋ ものを Counterfactual regret, ≈ のに. Sentence-final complaint.
〜ものかmono ka never! / as if! V-る ＋ ものか Strong refusal, not regret. 二度と行くものか.
〜をよそにo yoso ni ignoring ~ N ＋ をよそに A = others' feelings; B = own action. Criticising someone's indifference.
〜をおいて〜ないo oite ~ nai no one but ~ N ＋ をおいて ＋ 否定 Needs a negative ending. Praising the only candidate.
Exam trap 1 — the old ん form: 言わんばかりに (Q1) and 得んがために (Q2) both use the ない-stem + ん. Choose the ない-stem (言わ・得・せ), never the dictionary, volitional or た-form.
Exam trap 2 — "what follows" questions: Q7 and Q8 put the blank after the grammar point. Check what the pattern demands of the rest of the sentence: をよそに needs the subject's own disregarding action; をおいて needs a negative (〜ない／いないだろう).
