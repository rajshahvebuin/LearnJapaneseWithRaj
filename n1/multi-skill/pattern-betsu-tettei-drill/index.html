パターン別徹底ドリル N1

Following パターン別徹底ドリル 日本語能力試験N1 (Pattern-betsu Tettei Drill JLPT N1) drill by drill — the book is organised by JLPT question pattern: 文字・語彙 (kanji reading, word in context, paraphrase, usage), 聴解 (the five listening patterns), 文法 (grammar form, ★ sentence building, passage grammar) and 読解 (six reading question types). Every page gives the points the drill teaches, every question with the book's own in-scan answer key, and confusion pairs, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
