2 Every question — 問題 (p.29)

問題　（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

現在、インターネットには（　）数のブログがある。Genzai, intaanetto ni wa (___) kazu no burogu ga aru.

Full sentence: 現在、インターネットにはおびただしい数のブログがある。Genzai, intaanetto ni wa obitadashii kazu no burogu ga aru.
EN: There is now an enormous number of blogs on the internet.
HI: आज इंटरनेट पर बेहिसाब ब्लॉग हैं।
GU: આજે ઇન્ટરનેટ પર અસંખ્ય બ્લૉગ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1なだかいnadakaifamous (名高い)प्रसिद्धપ્રખ્યાત
2きびしいkibishiistrict, severeसख़्तકડક
3あさましいasamashiishameful, baseशर्मनाक, नीचશરમજનક, હલકું
4おびただしいobitadashiivast in numberबेहिसाबઅસંખ્ય

Answer 4 (book key, p.30). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): おびただしい数の〜 means "an extremely large number of …".

(2)

3人の中で、会長に（　）のはだれだと思いますか。Sannin no naka de, kaichou ni (___) no wa dare da to omoimasu ka.

Full sentence: 3人の中で、会長にふさわしいのはだれだと思いますか。Sannin no naka de, kaichou ni fusawashii no wa dare da to omoimasu ka.
EN: Of the three, who do you think is fit to be chairperson?

GU: ત્રણમાંથી અધ્યક્ષ માટે કોણ યોગ્ય છે, તમને શું લાગે છે?
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1にくらしいnikurashiihateful, detestableघृणितનફરતપાત્ર
2ふさわしいfusawashiisuitable, befittingउपयुक्तયોગ્ય
3みすぼらしいmisuborashiishabby, wretched-lookingफटेहाल, दयनीयફાટેલું-તૂટેલું, દયનીય
4たまらないtamaranaiunbearable; irresistibleअसहनीय; बेहदઅસહ્ય; ખૂબ જ

(3)

彼女に何を質問しても、「うん」という（　）返事しかしない。Kanojo ni nani o shitsumon shite mo, "un" to iu (___) henji shika shinai.

Full sentence: 彼女に何を質問しても、「うん」というそっけない返事しかしない。Kanojo ni nani o shitsumon shite mo, "un" to iu sokkenai henji shika shinai.
EN: Whatever you ask her, all you get is a curt "Yeah."
HI: उससे कुछ भी पूछो, वह बस रूखा-सा "हूँ" कह देती है।
GU: તેને કંઈ પણ પૂછો, તે બસ રૂખું "હં" કહી દે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1こころよいkokoroyoipleasant; willingसुखद; ख़ुशी सेસુખદ; રાજીખુશીથી
2やすっぽいyasuppoicheap-lookingसस्ता-साસસ્તું દેખાતું
3そっけないsokkenaicurt, coldरूखाરૂખું
4なれなれしいnarenareshiiover-familiarज़रूरत से ज़्यादा घुलने वालाવધુ પડતું હળીમળી જતું

Answer 3 (book key, p.30). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): A word showing an unfriendly, uninterested manner; finishing a reply with the single word "un" is そっけない. (added, not in book) こころよい返事 would be the opposite — a willing, friendly answer (快く引き受ける).

(4)

面接のときは、厳しい質問をされても（　）顔をしないほうがいい。Mensetsu no toki wa, kibishii shitsumon o sarete mo (___) kao o shinai hou ga ii.

Full sentence: 面接のときは、厳しい質問をされてもしぶい顔をしないほうがいい。Mensetsu no toki wa, kibishii shitsumon o sarete mo shibui kao o shinai hou ga ii.
EN: In an interview, it is best not to pull a sour face even when you get a tough question.
HI: इंटरव्यू में कठिन सवाल पूछे जाएँ तब भी मुँह नहीं बनाना चाहिए।
GU: ઇન્ટરવ્યૂમાં અઘરા પ્રશ્નો પુછાય તો પણ મોં બગાડવું ન જોઈએ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1しぶいshibuisour, displeasedनाख़ुशનારાજ
2しぶといshibutoitenacious, stubbornज़िद्दी, हार न मानने वालाજિદ્દી, હાર ન માનનાર
3いちじるしいichijirushiiremarkableउल्लेखनीयનોંધપાત્ર
4たくましいtakumashiisturdy, robustहट्टा-कट्टाમજબૂત

Answer 1 (book key, p.30). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): しぶい顔 expresses dissatisfaction or perplexity. (added, not in book) しぶとい differs by one kana — the "partly the same form" trap. It describes a person who never gives up, not a face.

(5)

たき火をすると（　）目が開けられなくなることがある。Takibi o suru to (___) me ga akerarenaku naru koto ga aru.

Full sentence: たき火をするとけむたくて目が開けられなくなることがある。Takibi o suru to kemutakute me ga akerarenaku naru koto ga aru.
EN: When you have a bonfire, it can get so smoky that you can't keep your eyes open.
HI: अलाव जलाने पर कभी-कभी इतना धुआँ होता है कि आँखें खोलना मुश्किल हो जाता है।
GU: તાપણું કરીએ ત્યારે ક્યારેક એટલો ધુમાડો થાય છે કે આંખો ખોલી શકાતી નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1まぶしくてmabushikutedazzling, too brightचकाचौंधઆંજી નાખે તેવું
2けむたくてkemutakutesmokyधुएँ से भराધુમાડાવાળું
3はなばなしくてhanabanashikutebrilliant, spectacularशानदार, भव्यભવ્ય, ઝળહળતું
4けがらわしくてkegarawashikutefilthy, disgustingघिनौनाગંદું, ધૃણાસ્પદ

Answer 2 (book key, p.30). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): It describes coughing or watering eyes because of smoke. It also has the meaning "hard to approach, awkward to be around". (added, not in book) まぶしい also stops you opening your eyes, but it is about strong light; たき火 points to smoke.

(6)

彼の運転は、急な発進や停止が多く、とても（　）。Kare no unten wa, kyuu na hasshin ya teishi ga ooku, totemo (___).

GU: તેના ડ્રાઇવિંગમાં અચાનક ચાલવું-અટકવું બહુ થાય છે, ખૂબ રફ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1めざましいmezamashiiremarkable, strikingउल्लेखनीयનોંધપાત્ર
2なまぬるいnamanuruilukewarm; half-heartedगुनगुना; ढीला-ढालाહૂંફાળું; ઢીલું
3荒っぽいarappoiroughरूखा, उग्रઉગ્ર, રફ

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Built from this drill's distractors (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
おびただしいobitadashii vs なだかいnadakai vast in number vs famous おびただしい数・量 (amount); なだかい観光地 (reputation).
ふさわしいfusawashii vs のぞましいnozomashii suited to vs desirable 〜にふさわしい (fits a role); 〜ことがのぞましい (is the preferred choice).
そっけないsokkenai vs なれなれしいnarenareshii cold, curt vs over-familiar Opposite ends: too distant vs too close.
しぶいshibui vs しぶといshibutoi sour (face) vs tenacious One kana apart. しぶい顔 / しぶとい相手.
けむたいkemutai vs まぶしいmabushii smoky vs dazzling Both make you shut your eyes: smoke → けむたい, light → まぶしい.
荒っぽいarappoi vs 頼もしいtanomoshii rough vs reliable 運転・言葉が荒っぽい; 頼もしい先輩.
わずらわしいwazurawashii vs きまりわるいkimariwarui bothersome vs embarrassed 手続きがわずらわしい; 失敗してきまりわるい.
いちじるしいichijirushii vs めざましいmezamashii striking vs remarkable Close synonyms: いちじるしい is for any clearly visible change (good or bad); めざましい praises progress.
まんまるいmanmarui vs まちどおしいmachidooshii perfectly round vs eagerly awaited 月 → shape; 夏休みが待ち遠しい → feeling.
Exam trap: the same adjectives turn up as wrong options in several items (ふさわしい, なだかい, みすぼらしい, なれなれしい, まちどおしい). When you review, make sure you can say what each distractor does mean — next time it may be the answer.
