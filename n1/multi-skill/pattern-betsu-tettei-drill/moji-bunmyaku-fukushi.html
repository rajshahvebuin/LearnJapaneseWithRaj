2 Every question — 問題 (p.33)

問題　（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

練習中は、走って走って、（　）走ります。Renshuuchuu wa, hashitte hashitte, (___) hashirimasu.

Full sentence: 練習中は、走って走って、ひたすら走ります。Renshuuchuu wa, hashitte hashitte, hitasura hashirimasu.
EN: In practice we run and run — we just keep on running.
HI: अभ्यास के दौरान हम दौड़ते हैं, दौड़ते हैं, बस लगातार दौड़ते ही रहते हैं।
GU: પ્રેક્ટિસ દરમિયાન અમે દોડીએ, દોડીએ, બસ સતત દોડતા જ રહીએ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ひたすらhitasurasingle-mindedlyपूरी लगन सेપૂરી લગનથી
2おおかたookatamostlyज़्यादातरમોટે ભાગે
3ふんだんにfundan niabundantly, lavishlyभरपूरભરપૂર

Answer 1 (book key, p.34). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): It describes continuing the same thing, or repeating only that one thing.

(3)

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati

(4)

物価上昇による運賃改定で、バスも電車も（　）値上がりした。Bukka joushou ni yoru unchin kaitei de, basu mo densha mo (___) neagari shita.

1ちっともchittomo(not) at all(बिलकुल) नहीं(જરાય) નહીં

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Built from this drill's distractors (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
ひたすらhitasura vs ひたむきにhitamuki ni single-mindedly (just keep doing) vs earnestly (sincere attitude) ひたすら走る / 謝る (nothing but); ひたむきに努力する (wholehearted). Close, but ひたすら stresses repetition.
ことごとくkotogotoku vs のきなみnokinami every single one vs all alike ことごとく断られる (each one, often negative outcomes); AもBものきなみ値上がり (across a row of things).
なおさらnaosara vs ましてmashite all the more vs let alone They work together: まして〜はなおさらだ.
やけにyakeni vs ちっともchittomo awfully vs (not) at all やけに + positive statement; ちっとも + negative only.
がっくりとgakkuri to vs しっかりとshikkari to dejectedly vs firmly がっくりと肩を落とす; しっかりと持つ.
つくづくtsukuzuku vs はらはらharahara keenly feel vs be on edge つくづく思う・感じる; はらはらしながら見る.
ぼつぼつbotsubotsu vs そろそろsorosoro little by little / about time vs about time ぼつぼつ出かけましょうか = そろそろ出かけましょうか.
おおかたookata vs くっきりkukkiri mostly vs clearly visible おおかたできた; 山がくっきり見える.
もはやmohaya vs しいてshiite by now vs if pressed もはや手遅れだ; しいて言えば (if I had to say).
Exam trap: four distractors in this drill need a negative or a request (ちっとも, さっぱり, てんで + 〜ない; なにとぞ + お願いします). In an affirmative sentence you can cross them out at once — exactly the "know how it is used" point from the tips page.
