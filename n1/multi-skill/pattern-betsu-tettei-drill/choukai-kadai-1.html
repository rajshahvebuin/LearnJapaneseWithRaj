1 Points — how 聴解 questions work + expressions from this unit

問題を解くコツ — 聴解 (summary of p.60, not the book's text)

EN: The book opens the listening unit by pointing out that the current JLPT no longer tests only whether you understood what you heard: it also asks what you would do next based on it, and how you would reply to someone. So practise listening together with speaking. N1 聴解 has five question types, and the book gives the order of steps for each — learn the order, because it tells you what to listen for.

HI: किताब बताती है कि नया JLPT सिर्फ़ यह नहीं जाँचता कि आपने सुना हुआ समझा या नहीं — यह भी पूछता है कि सुनकर आप आगे क्या करेंगे और सामने वाले को क्या जवाब देंगे। इसलिए सुनने के साथ बोलने का अभ्यास भी ज़रूरी है। N1 聴解 में पाँच तरह के प्रश्न हैं, और हर एक के लिए चरणों का क्रम दिया गया है।

GU: પુસ્તક કહે છે કે નવું JLPT ફક્ત એ નથી તપાસતું કે તમે સાંભળેલું સમજ્યા કે નહીં — એ પણ પૂછે છે કે સાંભળ્યા પછી તમે શું કરશો અને સામેવાળાને શું જવાબ આપશો. તેથી સાંભળવાની સાથે બોલવાનો અભ્યાસ પણ જરૂરી છે. N1 聴解માં પાંચ પ્રકારના પ્રશ્નો છે અને દરેક માટે પગલાંનો ક્રમ આપેલો છે.

TypeWhat it testsOrder of steps
1. 課題理解kadai rikaiCatch the information needed to solve a concrete task and choose the right next action.situation + question → talk → question again → choose from the printed options 1–4
2. ポイント理解pointo rikaiNarrow down and catch the one point (reason, feeling, fact) the question asks for.situation + question → read the printed options (time given) → talk → question again → choose
3. 概要理解gaiyou rikaiGrasp the speaker's overall message or opinion.situation → long talk (news, explanation) → question → spoken options 1–4
4. 即時応答sokuji outouJudge instantly and give the right reply.short utterance (question, greeting) → three spoken replies → choose
5. 統合理解tougou rikaiCombine a lot of information (three-person talks, people discussing news).問題1: situation → long talk → question → spoken options. 問題2: situation → long talk → question → printed options.

How to use this for 課題理解 (added, not in book): the question comes before the talk, so you know whose action you need. Speakers often change their minds or add a correction at the end (「あ、そうそう」), so wait for the final decision before marking an option.

Answer 4 (book key, p.64). Why (added, not in book): three position words decide it — 背にする (the big building is behind you), 道を挟んで (the other side of the road) and 斜め向かい (diagonally, not straight across). The other map points would be the big building itself, straight across, or the same side of the road.

3 Confusion pairs — position words and afterthoughts

Traps built into this lesson's options (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
斜め向かいnaname mukai diagonally opposite Not 真向かい (directly opposite) and not 隣 (next door) — the classic map-question trap.
道を挟んでmichi o hasande on the other side of the road 挟む = to have something in between; the road lies between you and the place.
〜を背にする~ o se ni suru with ~ behind you Fixes which way you face — every left/right after it depends on this.
横書き vs 縦書きyokogaki / tategaki horizontal vs vertical writing Changes where 'left / right end / bottom' are on the page — compare the 名刺 options.
あ、そうそうa, sou sou oh, that's right (afterthought) Signals an added or corrected instruction — often the detail that separates two options.
Exam trap: in 課題理解 the last thing said often changes the answer. Here the teacher adds the student number after describing the name position. Keep listening until the question is repeated.
