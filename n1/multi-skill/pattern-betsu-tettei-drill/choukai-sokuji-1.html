1 Points — how to answer 即時応答 + expressions to catch in this unit

問題を解くコツ — 即時応答 (summary of p.60, not the book's text)

EN: 即時応答 checks whether you can judge instantly what the other person means and give a fitting reply. You hear one short utterance (a question, a request, a greeting, a complaint…), then three spoken replies 1–3, and choose the best. Nothing is printed.

HI: 即時応答 यह जाँचता है कि आप सामने वाले की बात का मतलब तुरंत समझकर सही जवाब दे पाते हैं या नहीं। आप एक छोटा वाक्य सुनते हैं (प्रश्न, अनुरोध, अभिवादन, शिकायत…), फिर तीन बोले गए जवाब 1–3, और सबसे सही चुनते हैं। कुछ भी छपा नहीं होता।

GU: 即時応答 તપાસે છે કે તમે સામેની વ્યક્તિની વાતનો અર્થ તરત સમજીને યોગ્ય જવાબ આપી શકો છો કે નહીં. તમે એક ટૂંકું વાક્ય સાંભળો છો (પ્રશ્ન, વિનંતી, અભિવાદન, ફરિયાદ…), પછી ત્રણ બોલાયેલા જવાબ 1–3, અને સૌથી યોગ્ય પસંદ કરો છો. કશું છપાયેલું નથી.

How to listen (added, not in book): decide at once who is doing what (is the speaker asking, offering, apologising, scolding?) and whether the tone is polite or casual. A wrong reply often repeats a word from the utterance, or answers a different speech act (e.g. thanks for an offer that was really a complaint).

基本問題 (p.109) — CD2 Tracks 9–11

問題　まず文を聞いてください。それから、それに対する返事を聞いて、1から3の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mondai: Mazu bun o kiite kudasai. Sorekara, sore ni taisuru henji o kiite, 1 kara 3 no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: First listen to the sentence. Then listen to the replies to it and choose the best one from 1–3. HI: पहले वाक्य सुनें। फिर उसके जवाब सुनकर 1–3 में से सबसे सही जवाब चुनें। GU: પહેલાં વાક્ય સાંભળો. પછી તેના જવાબો સાંભળીને 1–3માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

基本問題 1番 CD2 · Track 9
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A receptionist tells a caller that Mr Tanaka is not at his desk; the caller has to reply.

HI: रिसेप्शनिस्ट फ़ोन करने वाले को बताता है कि तनाका जी अपनी सीट पर नहीं हैं; फ़ोन करने वाले को जवाब देना है।

GU: રિસેપ્શનિસ્ટ ફોન કરનારને કહે છે કે તનાકાજી પોતાની જગ્યા પર નથી; ફોન કરનારે જવાબ આપવાનો છે.

The line the answer hinges on

田中はあいにく席をはずしておりますが。Tanaka wa ainiku seki o hazushite orimasu ga.

EN: I'm afraid Tanaka is away from his desk at the moment. HI: माफ़ कीजिए, तनाका अभी अपनी सीट पर नहीं हैं। GU: માફ કરશો, તનાકા અત્યારે પોતાની જગ્યા પર નથી.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1（要旨）相手に少し待つように頼む(youshi) aite ni sukoshi matsu you ni tanomugist: asks the other person to wait a momentसार: सामने वाले से थोड़ा रुकने को कहता हैસાર: સામેનાને થોડી વાર રાહ જોવા કહે છે
2（要旨）いない田中さんに代わってほしいと頼む(youshi) inai Tanaka-san ni kawatte hoshii to tanomugist: asks to be put through to Tanaka anywayसार: फिर भी तनाका जी से बात कराने को कहता हैસાર: છતાં તનાકાજી સાથે વાત કરાવવા કહે છે
3では、後ほど改めてお電話します。Dewa, nochihodo aratamete odenwa shimasu.Then I'll call again later.तो मैं बाद में फिर फ़ोन करूँगा।તો હું પછીથી ફરી ફોન કરીશ.

Answer 3 (book key, p.110). Why (added, not in book): 席をはずす = not at one's desk. The caller's natural reply is to call back later. "Please wait" is the receptionist's own line (role mix-up), and asking for Tanaka ignores that he is out.

基本問題 2番 CD2 · Track 10
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: Someone greets Ms Ito after not seeing her for a long time.

HI: कोई बहुत दिनों बाद इतो जी से मिलकर अभिवादन करता है।

GU: કોઈ ઘણા સમય પછી ઇતોજીને મળીને અભિવાદન કરે છે.

The line the answer hinges on

伊藤さん、ご無沙汰しております。Itou-san, gobusata shite orimasu.

EN: Ms Ito, it's been a long time. HI: इतो जी, बहुत दिनों बाद मिल रहे हैं। GU: ઇતોજી, ઘણા સમય પછી મળીએ છીએ.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1お久しぶりですね。お元気ですか。Ohisashiburi desu ne. Ogenki desu ka.It's been a while! How are you?बहुत दिन हो गए! कैसे हैं?ઘણા દિવસ થયા! કેમ છો?
2（要旨）悪い知らせを聞いたように謝る(youshi) warui shirase o kiita you ni ayamarugist: apologises as if hearing bad newsसार: जैसे बुरी ख़बर सुनी हो, माफ़ी माँगती हैસાર: જાણે ખરાબ સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા હોય તેમ માફી માગે છે
3（要旨）「昨日」のお礼を言う(youshi) "kinou" no orei o iugist: thanks him for yesterdayसार: "कल" के लिए धन्यवाद देती हैસાર: "ગઈ કાલ" માટે આભાર માને છે

Answer 1 (book key, p.110). Why (added, not in book): ご無沙汰しております is a greeting after a long gap, so the matching reply is お久しぶりですね. Thanking for "yesterday" contradicts the long gap; an apology does not answer a greeting.

基本問題 3番 CD2 · Track 11
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A company employee tells a caller that the person in charge will call back.

HI: कंपनी का कर्मचारी फ़ोन करने वाले से कहता है कि ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति वापस फ़ोन करेगा।

GU: કંપનીનો કર્મચારી ફોન કરનારને કહે છે કે જવાબદાર વ્યક્તિ પાછો ફોન કરશે.

The line the answer hinges on

すみません。担当者に折り返しお電話をかけさせます。Sumimasen. Tantousha ni orikaeshi odenwa o kakesasemasu.

EN: Sorry. I'll have the person in charge call you back. HI: माफ़ कीजिए। मैं ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति से आपको वापस फ़ोन करवाऊँगा। GU: માફ કરશો. હું જવાબદાર વ્યક્તિ પાસે તમને પાછો ફોન કરાવીશ.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1（要旨）担当者に今すぐ代わってほしいと頼む(youshi) tantousha ni ima sugu kawatte hoshii to tanomugist: asks to be put through to the person in charge nowसार: अभी ज़िम्मेदार व्यक्ति से बात कराने को कहती हैસાર: હમણાં જ જવાબદાર વ્યક્તિ સાથે વાત કરાવવા કહે છે
2そうですか。ではお待ちしております。Sou desu ka. Dewa omachi shite orimasu.I see. Then I'll be waiting (for the call).अच्छा। तो मैं इंतज़ार करूँगी।સારું. તો હું રાહ જોઈશ.
3（要旨）何時ごろ自分から電話すればいいか聞く(youshi) nanji goro jibun kara denwa sureba ii ka kikugist: asks what time she should callसार: पूछती है कि वह ख़ुद कितने बजे फ़ोन करेસાર: પૂછે છે કે પોતે કેટલા વાગ્યે ફોન કરે

Answer 2 (book key, p.110). Why (added, not in book): 折り返し電話をかけさせます = they will call you back, so the caller just waits. Asking when to call herself reverses who calls; asking to be transferred ignores that the person isn't available.

応用問題 1番 CD2 · Track 12
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A woman asks her section chief to look over a document.

HI: एक महिला अपने सेक्शन चीफ़ से एक दस्तावेज़ देखने को कहती है।

応用問題 2番 CD2 · Track 13
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: A man thanks a woman for taking the trouble to visit him.

HI: एक आदमी एक महिला को कष्ट करके आने के लिए धन्यवाद देता है।

GU: એક પુરુષ એક સ્ત્રીને તકલીફ લઈને આવવા બદલ આભાર માને છે.

The line the answer hinges on

わざわざこんなところまで足を運んでくださって、どうもありがとうございました。Wazawaza konna tokoro made ashi o hakonde kudasatte, doumo arigatou gozaimashita.

EN: Thank you so much for taking the trouble to come all the way here. HI: इतनी दूर यहाँ तक आने का कष्ट करने के लिए बहुत धन्यवाद। GU: આટલે દૂર અહીં સુધી આવવાની તકલીફ લેવા બદલ ખૂબ આભાર.

OptionReplyEnglishHindiGujarati
1（要旨）「運ぶ」のは大したことではないと言う(youshi) "hakobu" no wa taishita koto de wa nai to iugist: says carrying it was no big dealसार: कहती है कि ढोना कोई बड़ी बात नहीं थीસાર: કહે છે કે ઊંચકવું કોઈ મોટી વાત નહોતી
2いえいえ。一度こちらへうかがってみたいと思っておりましたし。Ie ie. Ichido kochira e ukagatte mitai to omotte orimashita shi.Not at all. I had been wanting to visit you here anyway.अरे नहीं। मैं वैसे भी एक बार यहाँ आना चाहती थी।અરે ના. હું આમેય એક વાર અહીં આવવા માગતી હતી.
3（要旨）荷物は「重くない」と言う(youshi) nimotsu wa "omoku nai" to iugist: says it isn't heavyसार: कहती है कि सामान भारी नहीं हैસાર: કહે છે કે સામાન ભારે નથી

応用問題 3番 CD2 · Track 14

EN: A business partner says his company will consider the contract positively.

HI: एक व्यावसायिक साझेदार कहता है कि उनकी कंपनी अनुबंध पर सकारात्मक विचार करेगी।

Answer 3 (book key, p.110). Why (added, not in book): 前向きに検討する = will consider it favourably — hopeful, but not yet decided. So the reply thanks him and asks for his favour. Thanking for the contract itself (2) jumps ahead; asking him to reconsider (1) treats it as a refusal.

3 Confusion pairs — why the other replies are wrong

Where the wrong replies come from (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
席をはずす vs 少々お待ちくださいseki o hazusu / shoushou omachi kudasai is away vs please hold Two halves of a phone call — the receptionist says both; the caller says neither.
ご無沙汰 vs 昨日はどうもgobusata / kinou wa doumo long time no see vs thanks for yesterday The first means you haven't met for ages; the second assumes you met yesterday.
折り返し電話をかけさせるorikaeshi denwa o kakesaseru have someone call back The other side calls — you wait.
目を通す vs 目薬・目の疲れme o toosu / megusuri / me no tsukare look over vs eye drops / tired eyes Idiom with 目 ≠ eyes. Distractors echo the body part.
足を運ぶ vs 荷物を運ぶashi o hakobu / nimotsu o hakobu come / visit vs carry luggage Idiom vs literal 運ぶ.
前向きに検討 vs 契約決定maemuki ni kentou / keiyaku kettei positive consideration vs signed contract Business Japanese softens — 検討 is not yet a yes.
Exam trap: 即時応答 loves idioms built on body parts (目を通す, 足を運ぶ, 手を貸す). A reply that talks about real eyes, feet or hands is almost always wrong.
