1 Grammar Points

The book has no grammar table for 文章の文法. The points below are the words and forms the correct answers use; where the book's 解説 gives the meaning it is labelled so, everything else is (added, not in book).

【1】 「石」のような（比喩）

neutral register
Reading"ishi" no you na
Meaning in Japanese (added, not in book)石のように硬い（たとえ）
Meaning (EN)like a "stone" — a metaphor for being stiff and rigid
Meaning (HI)"पत्थर" जैसा — सख़्त/कठोर होने का रूपक
Meaning (GU)"પથ્થર" જેવું — સખત/કઠોર હોવાનું રૂપક
Connection (接続)「N」のような感じで
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe book's 解説: the next words 態度も口調も硬い人 call up the image of something hard → 石. (水 = fluid, 花 = showy/soft, 月 = distant/beautiful.)
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [1]) 場がかなりほぐれてきたのに、相変わらず「石」のような感じで態度も口調も硬い人、これなど場の空気が読めない人の典型である。Ba ga kanari hogurete kita no ni, aikawarazu "ishi" no you na kanji de taido mo kuchou mo katai hito, kore nado ba no kuuki ga yomenai hito no tenkei de aru. Even though the mood has relaxed quite a bit, someone who stays stiff as a "stone" in attitude and tone — that is a typical person who can't read the room. माहौल काफ़ी सहज हो जाने के बावजूद जो व्यक्ति "पत्थर" की तरह रवैये और लहजे में सख़्त रहता है, वह माहौल न समझ पाने वाले का आम उदाहरण है। વાતાવરણ ઘણું હળવું થઈ જવા છતાં જે વ્યક્તિ "પથ્થર" જેવી વર્તણૂક અને લહેજામાં સખત રહે છે, તે વાતાવરણ ન સમજી શકનારનું સામાન્ય ઉદાહરણ છે.

Point added for this site. 場の空気が読めない = "can't read the room / the mood of the gathering" (also KY in slang).

【2】 それどころか

neutral register
Readingsore dokoro ka
Book's 解説 (meaning)Aという程度ではなく、それよりもっと程度が（上／下）のB
Meaning (EN)far from that / on the contrary, even (worse / more)
Meaning (HI)इतना ही नहीं, उलटा उससे भी बढ़कर (बुरा)
Meaning (GU)એટલું જ નહીં, ઊલટું તેનાથી પણ વધુ (ખરાબ)
Connection (接続)A。それどころか、B
RegisterNeutral
Typical useFrom the book's 解説: "not just A, but B, which is further along the same scale". Here: not only can't you get closer → the conversation dies and everyone falls silent.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [2]) これでは、相手との距離を縮めることなどとうていできない。それどころか、話題がなかなか広がらないので場が盛り上がらず、しまいには誰もが無口になってしまう。Kore de wa, aite to no kyori o chijimeru koto nado toutei dekinai. Sore dokoro ka, wadai ga nakanaka hirogaranai node ba ga moriagarazu, shimai ni wa daremo ga mukuchi ni natte shimau. That way you can never close the distance with the other person. Worse than that, the topics don't spread, the gathering never livens up, and in the end everyone falls silent. इस तरह सामने वाले से दूरी कभी कम नहीं होती। इतना ही नहीं, विषय आगे नहीं बढ़ते, माहौल नहीं जमता, और आख़िर में सब चुप हो जाते हैं। આ રીતે સામેની વ્યક્તિ સાથેનું અંતર ક્યારેય ઘટતું નથી. એટલું જ નહીં, વિષયો આગળ વધતા નથી, વાતાવરણ જામતું નથી, અને આખરે બધા ચૂપ થઈ જાય છે.

Point from the book's 解説. それにもかかわらず (despite that) needs a contrast; それにつれて (along with that) needs gradual change; それとも (or) is for questions.

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [4]) 相手の話を聞かずにひとりでしゃべりまくっているような人も空気を読めない人と言える。Aite no hanashi o kikazu ni hitori de shaberimakutte iru you na hito mo kuuki o yomenai hito to ieru. Someone who talks on and on alone without listening to others can also be called a person who can't read the room. जो दूसरों की सुने बिना अकेले बोलता ही रहता है, उसे भी माहौल न समझने वाला कहा जा सकता है। જે બીજાની વાત સાંભળ્યા વગર એકલો બોલ્યા જ કરે છે, તેને પણ વાતાવરણ ન સમજનાર કહી શકાય.

Point from the book's 解説. は would contrast with the earlier examples; が marks a new subject without "also"; に doesn't fit before 〜と言える.

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題 [5-a]) 話のつまらない人というのは、「場の空気を感じる感性が鈍い人」と言い換えてもいい。Hanashi no tsumaranai hito to iu no wa, "ba no kuuki o kanjiru kansei ga nibui hito" to iikaete mo ii. (book: 問題 [5-b]) 感性の鋭い人なら、場の空気がちょっと白けたと感じたら、自分が三枚目になって場がなごむことを言ってみるだろう。Kansei no surudoi hito nara, ba no kuuki ga chotto shiraketa to kanjitara, jibun ga sanmaime ni natte ba ga nagomu koto o itte miru darou. A person with sharp sensitivity, sensing the mood has gone a little flat, would play the clown and say something to lighten things up. तेज़ संवेदनशीलता वाला व्यक्ति, माहौल थोड़ा फीका लगते ही, ख़ुद मसख़रा बनकर कुछ ऐसा कहेगा जिससे माहौल हल्का हो जाए। તીક્ષ્ણ સંવેદનશીલતાવાળી વ્યક્તિ, વાતાવરણ થોડું ફિક્કું લાગતાં જ, પોતે વિદૂષક બનીને કંઈક એવું કહેશે જેથી વાતાવરણ હળવું થાય.

Point from the book's 解説. 三枚目 = the comic role (from kabuki billing); 白ける = to go flat / become awkward.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題　次の文章を読んで、（1）から（5）の中に入る最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bunshou o yonde, (1) kara (5) no naka ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Read the passage and choose the best option for each blank (1)–(5). HI: लेख पढ़कर (1) से (5) तक हर रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: લખાણ વાંચીને (1) થી (5) સુધીની દરેક ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. — Book key: 2, 1, 3, 4, 2.

Passage

Source credit printed in the book: 高嶋秀武『話のおもしろい人、つまらない人』PHP研究所 (Takashima Hidetake, Hanashi no omoshiroi hito, tsumaranai hito, PHP Institute). The passage is not reproduced here.

Passage summary (not the book's text) — EN: The writer says the most important thing when talking with people is "the atmosphere of the place" (場の空気). At a first meeting, once introductions are over and people relax, someone who stays stiff and keeps asking formulaic questions cannot close the distance; the conversation fails to spread, the mood dies and everyone goes quiet. Equally, when the talk is lively, a person who talks non-stop without listening also can't read the room. Boring talkers are people whose sense for the mood is dull; a sensitive person would lighten a flat mood by playing the clown, and hold back and watch when it gets overheated.
HI: लेखक कहता है कि लोगों से बात करते समय सबसे ज़रूरी चीज़ "माहौल" (場の空気) है। पहली मुलाक़ात में परिचय के बाद जब सब सहज हो जाएँ, तब भी जो व्यक्ति सख़्त बना रहे और रटे-रटाए सवाल पूछता रहे, वह दूरी कम नहीं कर पाता; बातचीत आगे नहीं बढ़ती, माहौल ठंडा पड़ जाता है और सब चुप हो जाते हैं। इसी तरह, जब बातचीत जीवंत हो, तब दूसरों की सुने बिना लगातार बोलने वाला भी माहौल नहीं समझता। बोरिंग लोग वे हैं जिनकी माहौल भाँपने की संवेदनशीलता कम है; संवेदनशील व्यक्ति फीके माहौल को मसख़रा बनकर हल्का करेगा, और ज़्यादा गरम माहौल में थोड़ा पीछे रहकर देखेगा।
GU: લેખક કહે છે કે લોકો સાથે વાત કરતી વખતે સૌથી મહત્ત્વની વસ્તુ "વાતાવરણ" (場の空気) છે. પહેલી મુલાકાતમાં પરિચય પછી બધા હળવા થઈ જાય ત્યારે પણ જે વ્યક્તિ સખત રહે અને ગોખેલા પ્રશ્નો પૂછ્યા કરે, તે અંતર ઘટાડી શકતી નથી; વાતચીત આગળ વધતી નથી, વાતાવરણ ઠંડું પડે છે અને બધા ચૂપ થઈ જાય છે. એ જ રીતે, વાતચીત જીવંત હોય ત્યારે બીજાનું સાંભળ્યા વગર સતત બોલનાર પણ વાતાવરણ સમજતો નથી. કંટાળાજનક લોકો એ છે જેમની વાતાવરણ પારખવાની સંવેદનશીલતા ઓછી છે; સંવેદનશીલ વ્યક્તિ ફિક્કા વાતાવરણને વિદૂષક બનીને હળવું કરશે, અને વધુ પડતા ગરમ વાતાવરણમાં થોડી પાછળ રહીને જોશે.

Answer 1 (book key). Why (book's 解説, paraphrased): 「A。それどころか、B」 = "not just A, but B, further along the scale". A = you can't get closer; B = the talk dies and everyone goes silent — a worse result. それにもかかわらず needs a contradiction; それにつれて needs gradual parallel change; それとも offers an alternative in a question.

［3］

［4］

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Built from the options the book set against each blank, plus close relatives (added, not in book).

それどころかsore dokoro ka far from that / worse still A。それどころか、B B goes further on the same scale. Escalation.
それにもかかわらずsore ni mo kakawarazu despite that A。それにもかかわらず、B B contradicts expectations from A. Unexpected results.

あえてaete dare to, deliberately あえて ＋ V Deliberate choice. Opinions, decisions.

は (contrast)wa as for (in contrast) N ＋ は Sets apart from others. Contrasts.
感性が鈍い／鋭いkansei ga nibui / surudoi dull / sharp sensitivity 感性が ＋ adj Opposites; read the description that follows. Describing people.
Passage cloze tip: ［4］ is a single particle, decided by the structure of the whole passage — the writer is listing several kinds of people who can't read the room, so the later one takes も. Keep track of what the passage has already said.
Paired blanks (5-a / 5-b): decide each blank from its own description — "boring talker" → dull; "lightens the mood, holds back when needed" → sharp. Then pick the option with that pair.
