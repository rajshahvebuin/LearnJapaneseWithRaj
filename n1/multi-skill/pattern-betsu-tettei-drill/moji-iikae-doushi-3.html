2 Every question — 問題 (p.39)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

最近、何をやってもしくじってばかりで、元気が出ない。Saikin, nani o yatte mo shikujitte bakari de, genki ga denai.

Underlined word: しくじるshikujiru — to mess up, fail
Sentence with the answer: 最近、何をやっても失敗してばかりで、元気が出ない。Saikin, nani o yatte mo shippai shite bakari de, genki ga denai.
EN: Lately I keep messing up whatever I do, and I can't cheer up.
HI: आजकल मैं जो भी करूँ, बस गड़बड़ ही हो जाती है, इसलिए मन उदास रहता है।
GU: આજકાલ હું જે કંઈ કરું તેમાં ગડબડ જ થાય છે, તેથી ઉત્સાહ નથી આવતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1外れてhazuretemissing, coming off(निशाना) चूककर, निकलकर(નિશાન) ચૂકીને, નીકળી જઈને
2迷ってmayottebeing lost, undecidedभटककर, उलझन में पड़करભટકીને, મૂંઝાઈને
3後悔してkoukai shiteregrettingपछताकरપસ્તાઈને
4失敗してshippai shitefailingनाकाम होकर, ग़लती करकेનિષ્ફળ જઈને

Answer 4 (book key, p.40). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): しくじる means the same as 失敗する. Other options (added, not in book): 後悔する (regret) is what you might feel after failing, not the failing itself.

(2)

会社の命令にそむいて、自分だけの判断で行動した。Kaisha no meirei ni somuite, jibun dake no handan de koudou shita.

Underlined word: そむく（背く）somuku — to go against, disobey
Sentence with the answer: 会社の命令に反して、自分だけの判断で行動した。Kaisha no meirei ni hanshite, jibun dake no handan de koudou shita.
EN: I went against the company's orders and acted on my own judgement.
HI: मैंने कंपनी के आदेश के ख़िलाफ़ जाकर सिर्फ़ अपने फ़ैसले से काम किया।
GU: મેં કંપનીના આદેશની વિરુદ્ધ જઈને ફક્ત મારા પોતાના નિર્ણયથી કામ કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ためらってtamerattehesitatingहिचकिचाकरઅચકાઈને
2断ってkotowatterefusing, decliningमना करकेના પાડીને
3違ってchigattedifferingअलग होकरઅલગ હોઈને
4反してhanshitegoing againstके विरुद्ध जाकरવિરુદ્ધ જઈને

Answer 4 (book key, p.40). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): as the kanji 背く shows, it originally meant "to turn one's back on", and from that it came to mean 裏切る (betray), 反する (go against) and 逆らう (defy). Other options (added, not in book): 断る is refusing a request; it does not take 命令に and does not mean acting against an order.

GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે આજકાલ ખેતી સાથે જોડાતા લોકો વધી રહ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1疲れるtsukareruto get tiredथकनाથાકવું
2慣れるnareruto get used toआदी होनाટેવાવું

GU: ઇન્ટરનેટ પર ઘણો સમય વિતાવનારા લોકો ઘણા વધારે છે.

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

The wrong options here are mostly verbs that live next to the right idea. Separate them like this (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
しくじるshikujiru vs 外れるhazureru fail vs miss / come off しくじる = make a mistake in something you do; 外れる = miss a target or prediction (予想が外れる), or come loose (ボタンが外れる).
そむくsomuku / 逆らうsakarau / 断るkotowaru go against / defy / refuse そむく and 逆らう take に (命令にそむく・親に逆らう); 断る takes を and is a refusal of a request or invitation (誘いを断る).
たずさわるtazusawaru vs 従事するjuuji suru be engaged in vs work in Both take に and a field of work (農業・教育); 従事する is the more formal written word.
たまわるtamawaru vs いただくitadaku receive (very formal) vs receive (humble) Both humble forms of もらう; たまわる is for ceremonies and formal letters (ご指導を賜りますよう…).
費やすtsuiyasu vs 消費するshouhi suru spend vs consume 費やす = put money/time/effort into something; 消費する = use up goods or energy (電力を消費する).
ひやかすhiyakasu / からかうkarakau / ふざけるfuzakeru tease / tease / fool around ひやかす and からかう take a person as object; ひやかす is typical for teasing couples, and also means browsing a shop without buying. ふざける has no object.
もよおすmoyoosu: 開催 vs 吐き気 hold an event vs feel (sick, sleepy) パーティーを催す = 開催する; 吐き気・眠気を催す = come to feel it.
Exam trap: たまわる and 有する are formal written words, and the right options (いただく, 持っている) are their everyday versions. In 言い換え類義 the underlined word is often the "stiff" version of a common word — ask yourself "how would I say this in conversation?"
