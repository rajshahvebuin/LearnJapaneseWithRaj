2 Every question — 問題 (p.37)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

Underlined word: かえりみるkaerimiru — to reflect on (省みる) / look back on (顧みる)
Sentence with the answer: 自分を成長させるには、自分を反省する時間を持つことが大事だ。Jibun o seichou saseru ni wa, jibun o hansei suru jikan o motsu koto ga daiji da.
EN: To grow as a person, it is important to have time to reflect on yourself.
HI: ख़ुद को आगे बढ़ाने के लिए अपने बारे में आत्मचिंतन करने का समय रखना ज़रूरी है।
GU: પોતાનો વિકાસ કરવા માટે આત્મચિંતન કરવાનો સમય રાખવો જરૂરી છે.

Answer 1 (book key, p.38). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): かえりみる can be written 省みる or 顧みる. Written 省みる it means the same as 反省する; written 顧みる it means the same as (昔のことを)振り返る, "look back on the past". Other options (added, not in book): with 自分を and 成長, the 省みる meaning (反省) is the one that fits.

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

Several of these verbs have a second meaning or a look-alike that the exam can use against you (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
省みるkaerimiru vs 顧みるkaerimiru reflect on oneself vs look back on Same sound. 省みる = 反省する (自分を省みる); 顧みる = 振り返る (過去を顧みる), also "pay attention to" (家庭を顧みない).
かさむkasamu vs 積もるtsumoru costs mount vs snow/dust piles up かさむ is for amounts and money (費用・出費・荷物); 積もる is for physical layers (雪・ほこり) or 積もる話.
夢がかなうyume ga kanau vs 夢をかなえるyume o kanaeru a dream comes true vs make a dream come true かなう is intransitive (が); かなえる is transitive (を). 条件にかなう = meets the conditions.
覆すkutsugaesu vs 覆るkutsugaeru overturn vs be overturned Transitive/intransitive pair: 判決を覆す / 判決が覆る. Often used with 常識・定説・判決.
けなすkenasu ⇔ ほめるhomeru run down ⇔ praise The book gives ほめる as the antonym. けなす criticises a person or thing; 打ち消す denies a statement or rumour (うわさを打ち消す).
こじれるkojireru vs もつれるmotsureru go wrong vs get tangled Both describe talks/relationships going wrong; こじれる also fits an illness that drags on (風邪がこじれる).
料理にこるryouri ni koru vs 肩がこるkata ga koru be into cooking vs have stiff shoulders Same verb 凝る: 〜にこる = be absorbed in; 〜がこる = (muscles) stiffen.
Exam trap: several underlined verbs here are usually written in hiragana (かえりみる, かさむ, かなう, けなす, こじれる, こる). If you can recall their kanji — 省みる, 嵩む, 叶う, 貶す, 拗れる, 凝る — the meaning is easier to pin down, and for かえりみる the kanji decides which meaning is meant.
