2 Every question — 問題 (p.45)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

面接は、意外とあっさり終わった。Mensetsu wa, igai to assari owatta.

Underlined word: あっさりassari — easily, quickly and simply
Sentence with the answer: 面接は、意外と簡単に終わった。Mensetsu wa, igai to kantan ni owatta.
EN: The interview was over surprisingly quickly and easily.
HI: इंटरव्यू उम्मीद से कहीं आसानी से ख़त्म हो गया।
GU: ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ ધાર્યા કરતાં સહેલાઈથી પૂરો થઈ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1簡単にkantan nisimply, easilyआसानी सेસહેલાઈથી
2にわかにniwaka nisuddenlyअचानकઅચાનક
3しつこくshitsukokupersistentlyपीछा न छोड़ते हुए, बार-बारવારંવાર, પીછો ન છોડતાં
4ひとまずhitomazufor the time beingफ़िलहालહાલ પૂરતું

Answer 1 (book key, p.46). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): besides 簡単に, あっさり also means "light and fresh, not rich or heavy" (of a taste). Other options (added, not in book): しつこい is close to the opposite of あっさり (persistent; heavy taste).

(2)

ドライブをするときには、あらかじめルートを決めておくとよい。Doraibu o suru toki ni wa, arakajime ruuto o kimete oku to yoi.

Underlined word: あらかじめ（予め）arakajime — in advance
Sentence with the answer: ドライブをするときには、前もってルートを決めておくとよい。Doraibu o suru toki ni wa, maemotte ruuto o kimete oku to yoi.
EN: When you go for a drive, it's a good idea to decide your route in advance.
HI: ड्राइव पर जाते समय रास्ता पहले से तय कर लेना अच्छा रहता है।
GU: ડ્રાઇવ પર જતી વખતે રસ્તો અગાઉથી નક્કી કરી લેવો સારો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おのずからonozukaranaturally, of itselfअपने आपઆપોઆપ
2前もってmaemottebeforehandपहले सेઅગાઉથી

Answer 2 (book key, p.46). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): written 予め in kanji; 予 carries the meaning 前もって, as in 予習 (preparing lessons) and 予報 (forecast).

(3)

GU: મેં ધાર્યું હતું કે આ પરીક્ષામાં આવશે, અને ધાર્યા પ્રમાણે જ થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いっきょにikkyo niall at onceएक ही झटके मेंએક જ ઝાટકે

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しいてshiiteif forced to, by forceज़बरदस्ती, अगर कहना ही पड़ेજબરદસ્તીથી, કહેવું જ પડે તો

1まさしくmasashikusurely, exactlyनिश्चय ही, बिल्कुलચોક્કસ, બરાબર

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

Adverbs that sound alike are the classic trap in this drill. Keep these apart (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
あっさりassari ⇔ しつこいshitsukoi easily / light ⇔ persistent / heavy あっさり負けを認める (give up readily); あっさりした味 (light taste). しつこく is the opposite in both senses.
あらかじめarakajime vs おのずからonozukara in advance vs naturally, by itself 予め = before something happens (前もって); 自ずから = happens on its own (おのずから明らかになる).
案の定an no jou vs てっきりtekkiri as expected (and right) vs I was sure (but wrong) 案の定 = the guess came true; てっきり〜と思った = the belief turned out wrong.
依然としてizen to shite vs 以前izen still, unchanged vs before Same sound いぜん: 依然として = 相変わらず; 以前 = previously (≈ かつて).
かつてkatsute vs もはやmohaya once (in the past) vs already, no longer かつて looks back (かつて栄えた); もはや says the time for something has passed (もはや遅い). かつてない = unprecedented.
くっきりkukkiri / はっきりhakkiri / てっきりtekkiri sharp outline / clearly / mistakenly sure くっきり is visual (outline, letters, shadows); はっきり is wider (はっきり言う); てっきり is unrelated.
まるっきりmarukkiri / めっきりmekkiri / すっかりsukkari not at all / noticeably / completely まるっきり + negative (= 全然); めっきり + a change (めっきり減った); すっかり + completion (すっかり治った).
目下mokka vs 目下meshita at present vs one's junior Same kanji, two readings: もっか = 現在; めした = 部下・年下 (⇔ 目上 めうえ).
Exam trap: まるっきり, めっきり, すっかり, てっきり, くっきり, はっきり all end in the same sound, and the book put three of them in this drill as wrong options. For each adverb, learn one fixed partner — まるっきり〜ない, めっきり寒くなる, すっかり忘れる, てっきり〜と思った, くっきり見える — so the sound does not decide for you.
