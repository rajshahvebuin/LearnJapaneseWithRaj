2 Every question — 問題 (p.25)

問題　（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

子どもたちに、2度とガラスを割らないよう（　）で注意した。Kodomotachi ni, nido to garasu o waranai you (___) de chuui shita.

Full sentence: 子どもたちに、2度とガラスを割らないよう口頭で注意した。Kodomotachi ni, nido to garasu o waranai you koutou de chuui shita.
EN: I warned the children verbally never to break the glass again.
HI: मैंने बच्चों को मौखिक रूप से चेतावनी दी कि दोबारा काँच न तोड़ें।
GU: મેં બાળકોને મૌખિક રીતે ચેતવણી આપી કે ફરી કાચ ન તોડે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1口頭koutouoral, verbalमौखिकમૌખિક
2根底konteiroot, foundationजड़, आधारમૂળ, પાયો
3実質jisshitsusubstance, in effectसार, वास्तविकताસાર, વાસ્તવિકતા
4厳重genjuustrict, rigorousसख़्तકડક

Answer 1 (book key, p.26). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): The で in で注意した is the hint: the question asks how the warning was given, so choose 1. (added, not in book) 厳重 also goes with 注意, but as 厳重に注意する, not 厳重で.

(2)

彼の病気は今、大変危険な状態にあり、面会（　）である。Kare no byouki wa ima, taihen kiken na joutai ni ari, menkai (___) de aru.

Full sentence: 彼の病気は今、大変危険な状態にあり、面会謝絶である。Kare no byouki wa ima, taihen kiken na joutai ni ari, menkai shazetsu de aru.
EN: His illness is now at a very dangerous stage, and no visitors are allowed.
HI: उसकी बीमारी अभी बहुत गंभीर हालत में है, इसलिए किसी को मिलने की अनुमति नहीं है।
GU: તેની બીમારી હાલ ખૂબ ગંભીર સ્થિતિમાં છે, તેથી મુલાકાતની મનાઈ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1不意fuisudden, unexpectedअचानकઅચાનક
2脱退dattaiwithdrawal (from a group)(संगठन से) अलग होना(સંગઠનમાંથી) છૂટા પડવું
3静止seishistandstill, restस्थिरता, ठहरावસ્થિરતા

Answer 4 (book key, p.26). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): Learn it as the set expression 面会謝絶 (visitors refused).

(3)

彼は明るく活発で仕事ができるので、すべての社員の（　）となるような存在である。Kare wa akaruku kappatsu de shigoto ga dekiru node, subete no shain no (___) to naru you na sonzai de aru.

Full sentence: 彼は明るく活発で仕事ができるので、すべての社員の模範となるような存在である。Kare wa akaruku kappatsu de shigoto ga dekiru node, subete no shain no mohan to naru you na sonzai de aru.
EN: He is cheerful, energetic and good at his job, so he is a model for every employee.
HI: वह खुशमिज़ाज, फुर्तीला और काम में कुशल है, इसलिए सभी कर्मचारियों के लिए आदर्श है।
GU: તે ખુશમિજાજ, ચપળ અને કામમાં કુશળ છે, તેથી બધા કર્મચારીઓ માટે આદર્શ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1模範mohanmodel, exampleआदर्शઆદર્શ
2模倣mohouimitation, copyingनक़लનકલ
3模型mokei(scale) modelमॉडल, प्रतिकृतिમૉડલ, પ્રતિકૃતિ
4模索mosakugroping for, searchingटटोलना, खोजनाફંફોસવું, શોધવું

Answer 1 (book key, p.26). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): Same meaning as 手本 (an example to follow). (added, not in book) All four share 模 — exactly the "same kanji" distractor type from the tips page. 模倣 is the act of copying, not the person you copy.

この公園は丘の上にあるので、とても（　）がいい。Kono kouen wa oka no ue ni aru node, totemo (___) ga ii.

EN: This park is on a hill, so it has a wonderful view.

GU: આ બગીચો ટેકરી પર છે, તેથી અહીંથી દૃશ્ય ખૂબ સારું દેખાય છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1見晴らしmiharashiviewनज़ाराદૃશ્ય
2見おろしmioroshilooking down (from above)ऊपर से नीचे देखनाઉપરથી નીચે જોવું

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Built from this drill's distractors (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
口頭koutou vs 厳重genjuu oral vs strict 口頭で注意する (how) vs 厳重に注意する (how strongly) — the particle decides.
模範mohan vs 模倣mohou model vs imitation 模範になる = be the example; 模倣する = copy someone.
見晴らしmiharashi vs 景色keshiki view (from a place) vs scenery 見晴らしがいい (the place has a view) / 景色がいい (the scenery is nice). 色景 is not a word.
ファイルfairu vs フィルターfirutaa file vs filter 書類をファイルにとじる; コーヒーのフィルター.
コーナーkoonaa vs コースkoosu section of a place vs course / route 検索コーナー・喫煙コーナー; マラソンのコース.
ライバルraibaru vs ラベルraberu rival vs label Similar katakana shape; 競争 → ライバル.
遭難sounan vs 災害saigai getting into disaster (a person) vs the disaster itself 山で遭難する (person); 自然災害 (event).
対応taiou vs 待機taiki support / deal with vs stand by OSに対応する, 客に対応する; 自宅で待機する.
提示teiji vs 指示shiji show vs instruct 免許証を提示する; 部下に指示する.
Exam trap: when all four options share a kanji (模範・模倣・模型・模索) or look alike in katakana (ライバル・ラベル), read the clue word in the sentence — 社員の〜となる, 競争 — and choose by meaning, not by the familiar shape.
