1 Points — how to answer 漢字読み + the kanji to master

問題を解くコツ — kanji reading (summary of p.8, not the book's text)

EN: The book lists four kinds of reading traps. (a) Missing or extra dakuten, small っ and long vowels — 配分 はいぶん, 母国 ぼこく, 窒息 ちっそく, 至急 しきゅう (not しっきゅう), 待遇 たいぐう, 拒絶 きょぜつ (not きょうぜつ). (b) Small ゃ・ゅ・ょ sounds — 結晶 けっしょう, 返却 へんきゃく. (c) Being misled by a part shared with another kanji — 円滑 えんかつ (not えんこつ), 中枢 ちゅうすう (not ちゅうく). (d) Kanji with several on- or kun-readings — 包丁 ほうちょう / 丁寧 ていねい, 衣装 いしょう / 装備 そうび, 危ぶむ あやぶむ / 危ない あぶない, 解ける ほどける / 解く とく. Learn such kanji inside whole words, not as single readings.

HI: किताब चार तरह के जाल बताती है: (a) दाकुतेन, छोटा っ और लंबे स्वर का होना या न होना; (b) छोटे ゃ・ゅ・ょ वाली ध्वनियाँ; (c) किसी दूसरे कांजी से मिलते-जुलते हिस्से से भ्रमित होना; (d) कई ऑन/कुन पठन वाले कांजी। ऐसे कांजी को अकेले नहीं, पूरे शब्दों के साथ याद करें।

GU: પુસ્તક ચાર પ્રકારની જાળ બતાવે છે: (a) દાકુતેન, નાનો っ અને લાંબા સ્વરની હાજરી કે ગેરહાજરી; (b) નાના ゃ・ゅ・ょ વાળા અવાજો; (c) બીજા કાંજી સાથેના સમાન ભાગથી ભ્રમમાં પડવું; (d) અનેક ઓન/કુન વાંચન ધરાવતા કાંજી. આવા કાંજીને એકલા નહીં, આખા શબ્દો સાથે યાદ કરો.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.9)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

問1　欠陥は、即座に解決しなくてはならない。Kekkan wa, sokuza ni kaiketsu shinakute wa naranai.

Underlined word: 欠陥kekkan — defect, flaw
EN: The defect must be fixed immediately.
HI: इस खराबी (दोष) को तुरंत दूर करना ज़रूरी है।
GU: આ ખામી તરત જ દૂર કરવી જરૂરી છે.

1けつかんketsukannot the reading of 欠陥(欠陥 का पठन नहीं)(欠陥નું વાંચન નથી)
2けっかんkekkandefect, flawखराबी, दोषખામી, દોષ

Answer 2 (book key, p.10). Why (added, not in book — the key gives numbers only): 欠 (ケツ) + 陥 (カン): when ケツ is followed by a k-sound it shrinks to a small っ — けっかん. 1 forgets the 促音 (sokuon); 3/4 give 陥 a しょう reading it never has.

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Every wrong option above is one of the four trap types from the tips page. These are the ones worth remembering (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
欠陥kekkan けっかん ✓ / けつかん ✗ ケツ + k-sound → けっ (促音). Same rule: 結果 けっか, 決定 けってい.
苗nae vs 縄nawa seedling vs rope Both two-kana kun-yomi starting with な — know the kanji, not just the sound.
栽培saibai vs 裁判saiban growing plants vs a court trial 栽 (木 inside) is for plants; 裁 (衣 inside) is for cutting/judging. 培 = バイ, 判 = バン.
依然izen vs 以前izen still / as ever vs before / formerly Same sound. 依然として = "still (unchanged)"; 以前 = "previously".
譲歩jouho vs 丈夫joubu concession vs sturdy / healthy 歩 = ホ here (徒歩 とほ) or ポ after ん (進歩 しんぽ); ブ only in words like 歩合 ぶあい.
綱tsuna vs 網ami rope vs net The two kanji differ only in the right side; 綱 becomes づな in 横綱 by rendaku.
威力iryoku vs 力士rikishi power vs sumo wrestler 力 = リョク in most compounds (能力・威力); リキ in 力士・自力・馬力.
腸chou / 場jou / 陽you intestine / place / sun Same right side 昜, three different readings — look at the left-hand radical (月 = body part).
良好ryoukou りょうこう ✓ / りょうこ ✗ 好 = コウ (long vowel): 友好 ゆうこう, 格好 かっこう.
請求seikyuu vs 遡及sokyuu charge / claim vs retroactive 請 = セイ (申請・要請); 遡及 is a different word that only sounds similar.
Exam trap: two of the wrong options were the meaning or a different real word instead of the reading — すぐに for 即座に, and そきゅう (遡及) for 請求. In 漢字読み, an option that looks familiar is not necessarily the reading: sound out each kanji's on-yomi and put them together.
