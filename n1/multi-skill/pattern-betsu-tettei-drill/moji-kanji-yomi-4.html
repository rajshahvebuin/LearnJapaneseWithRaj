Readings in katakana = on-yomi, hiragana = kun-yomi (the ・ marks where okurigana starts, as in さず・ける). 扱 has no on-yomi in common use — only あつか・う. The drill tested 盆 inside 盆地 (ぼんち) and 公 inside 主人公 (しゅじんこう).

2 Every question — 問題 (p.15)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

問1　この町には、我が国が誇る遺跡があります。Kono machi ni wa, waga kuni ga hokoru iseki ga arimasu.

Underlined word: 誇るhokoru — to take pride in, boast of
EN: This town has ruins that our country is proud of.
HI: इस शहर में ऐसे प्राचीन अवशेष हैं जिन पर हमारे देश को गर्व है।
GU: આ નગરમાં એવા પ્રાચીન અવશેષો છે જેના પર આપણા દેશને ગર્વ છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1おごるogorunot the reading of 誇る(誇る का पठन नहीं)(誇るનું વાંચન નથી)
2ほこるhokoruto take pride inगर्व करनाગર્વ લેવો
3またがるmatagarunot the reading of 誇る(誇る का पठन नहीं)(誇るનું વાંચન નથી)
4むらがるmuragarunot the reading of 誇る(誇る का पठन नहीं)(誇るનું વાંચન નથી)

Answer 2 (book key, p.16). Why (added, not in book — the key gives numbers only): 誇 = ほこ・る (誇り). おごる is 驕る (to be arrogant) / 奢る (to treat) — close in feeling to "boasting", which makes it the main trap. またがる is 跨る — it shares the right side 夸 with 誇; むらがる is 群がる.

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Every wrong option above is one of the four trap types from the tips page (see 漢字読み①). These are the ones worth remembering (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
誇るhokoru vs 驕るogoru to be proud of (positive) vs to be arrogant (negative) Different kanji; 誇る takes an object you are proud of (〜を誇る).
遺跡iseki vs 遺言yuigon ruins vs a will 遺 = イ almost everywhere; ユイ only in 遺言.
異議igi / 会議kaigi / 協議kyougi objection / meeting / discussion All end in 議 ギ. 異議を唱える = to object.
唱えるtonaeru vs 訴えるuttaeru to advocate, chant vs to complain, sue Both take 〜を and fit "objection"; only the kanji tells you which.
購入kounyuu vs 挿入sounyuu purchase vs insertion 購 = コウ (long vowel).
盆bon vs 分bun tray vs part 盆 contains 分 but is ボン.
貫くtsuranuku / 赴くomomuku / 基づくmotozuku to pierce / to proceed to / to be based on Three N1 〜く verbs — learn each with its kanji.
主人公shujinkou しゅじんこう ✓ / しゅじんごう ✗ 公 stays コウ — no rendaku in on-yomi compounds like this.
授けるsazukeru / 受けるukeru / 預けるazukeru to grant / to receive / to entrust 授 = 扌 + 受: the giver's hand. 授けられる = to be given.
樹立juritsu vs 自立jiritsu establishing vs independence 樹 = ジュ (拗音), 自 = ジ.
阻むhabamu vs 挑むidomu to block vs to challenge Opposite directions: 阻む stops someone; 挑む takes someone on.
Exam trap: with kun-yomi verbs, all four options usually share the same okurigana (〜えた, 〜って, 〜く, 〜けられた), and several are real verbs that fit the sentence's meaning (訴えた, 預けられた, 挑もう). Don't choose by meaning — choose the reading that belongs to that kanji.
