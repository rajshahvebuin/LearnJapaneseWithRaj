The 問題を解くコツ (tips) for 漢字読み are summarised on 漢字読み①. This drill is heavy on 濁点 (dakuten) traps — 修行, 打撃, 独創的, 滞納, 詐欺 — and includes one special reading, 雪崩 (なだれ). §1 = the kanji table the book gives after the drill; §2 = every question; §3 = the reading traps hidden in the wrong options.

Readings in katakana = on-yomi, hiragana = kun-yomi (the ・ marks where okurigana starts, as in とどこお・る). 修 has two on-yomi: シュウ (必修・修理) and the shorter シュ in 修行 しゅぎょう — exactly what (2) tests. 雪崩 (なだれ) in (3) is a special reading (熟字訓) that is not in the table: learn it as a whole word.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.17)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Every wrong option above is one of the four trap types from the tips page (see 漢字読み①). These are the ones worth remembering (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
鍛えるkitaeru / 称えるtataeru / 伝えるtsutaeru to train / to praise / to convey All 〜たえる; only the first syllable tells them apart.
修行shugyou vs 修理shuuri training vs repair 修 = シュ (short) in 修行 only; シュウ elsewhere (必修・修正).
行gyou / kou 修行 しゅぎょう / 旅行 りょこう ギョウ in Buddhist / old words (修行・行事), コウ in most others.
雪崩nadare avalanche (熟字訓) Not built from 雪 + 崩 readings — learn the whole word.
避難hinan vs 非難hinan evacuation vs criticism Same sound — choose by meaning when writing.
打撃dageki だげき ✓ / たげき ✗ 打 = ダ in compounds (打者・打開); う・つ alone.
素朴soboku vs 素直sunao simple vs honest, obedient 素 = ソ in 素朴・素材, ス in 素直・素顔.
独創的dokusouteki vs 特徴的tokuchouteki original vs characteristic 独 = ドク (voiced), 特 = トク (unvoiced).
滞納tainou vs 納屋naya arrears vs barn 納 = ノウ in money words (納税・滞納); ナ only in a few old words.
促すunagasu vs 促進sokushin to urge vs promotion Same kanji: kun-yomi うながす, on-yomi ソク.
詐欺sagi vs 作業sagyou fraud vs work 詐 and 作 share 乍 — 詐 = サ, 作 = サ／サク.
大筋oosuji vs 大事daiji outline vs important 大 = おお before a kun-yomi (大筋・大声), usually ダイ before an on-yomi (大事・大会).
Exam trap: five of these twelve items (修行, 打撃, 独創的, 滞納, 詐欺) turn on 濁点 alone — the options differ only by voiced vs unvoiced sounds. When you study a compound, say it out loud and notice which syllables are voiced; that is what this question type checks.
