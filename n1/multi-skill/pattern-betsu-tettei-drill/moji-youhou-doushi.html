1 Points — how to answer 用法 + the verbs to master

問題を解くコツ — 用法 (summary of p.8 ④ and ②, not the book's text)

EN: In 用法 (usage) questions one word is given and you pick the one sentence of four that uses it correctly. The book's advice: look for a hint word in the sentence that the target word naturally goes with. Its example is 潤う (うるおう, "to become moist, take in water"): since it means getting wet with water, check whether the sentence has something that needs moisture — the throat, the ground. And, as for 文脈規定, learn verbs, adjectives and nouns as whole phrases (the book's examples: 会社を営む "to run a company", 発展・変化がいちじるしい "development / change is remarkable") instead of single-word meanings; then wrong options will mislead you much less. For adverbs, what counts is knowing how the word is actually used.

HI: 用法 (प्रयोग) प्रश्नों में एक शब्द दिया जाता है और चार वाक्यों में से वह एक वाक्य चुनना होता है जिसमें उसका सही प्रयोग हो। किताब की सलाह: वाक्य में ऐसा संकेत-शब्द ढूँढें जिसके साथ लक्ष्य शब्द स्वाभाविक रूप से आता है। उदाहरण 潤う (うるおう, "नमी पाना, पानी सोखना") — इसका अर्थ पानी से भीगना है, इसलिए देखें कि वाक्य में नमी चाहने वाली चीज़ है या नहीं, जैसे गला या ज़मीन। और क्रिया, विशेषण, संज्ञा को अकेले अर्थ से नहीं, पूरे वाक्यांश के रूप में याद करें (会社を営む "कंपनी चलाना", 発展・変化がいちじるしい "विकास/बदलाव उल्लेखनीय है"); तब ग़लत विकल्प कम भटकाएँगे। क्रियाविशेषण के लिए उसका वास्तविक प्रयोग जानना ही मुख्य बात है।

GU: 用法 (ઉપયોગ) પ્રશ્નોમાં એક શબ્દ આપેલો હોય છે અને ચાર વાક્યોમાંથી એ એક વાક્ય પસંદ કરવાનું હોય છે જેમાં તેનો સાચો ઉપયોગ થયો હોય. પુસ્તકની સલાહ: વાક્યમાં એવો સંકેત-શબ્દ શોધો જેની સાથે લક્ષ્ય શબ્દ સ્વાભાવિક રીતે આવે. ઉદાહરણ 潤う (うるおう, "ભેજ મળવો, પાણી શોષવું") — તેનો અર્થ પાણીથી ભીંજાવું છે, એટલે જુઓ કે વાક્યમાં ભેજની જરૂર હોય એવી વસ્તુ છે કે નહીં, જેમ કે ગળું કે જમીન. ક્રિયાપદ, વિશેષણ, નામને એકલા અર્થથી નહીં, આખા શબ્દસમૂહ તરીકે યાદ રાખો (会社を営む "કંપની ચલાવવી", 発展・変化がいちじるしい "વિકાસ/ફેરફાર નોંધપાત્ર છે"); તો ખોટા વિકલ્પો ઓછા ભટકાવશે. ક્રિયાવિશેષણ માટે તેનો વાસ્તવિક ઉપયોગ જાણવો એ જ મુખ્ય વાત છે.

How the wrong options are built (added, not in book): in every 用法 drill on these pages the three wrong sentences fall into the same few types — (a) a sound-alike word was swapped in (つつしむ for つつむ, アクセル for アクセス, ぞんざい for そんざい); (b) the word's meaning is roughly right but it does not collocate with the noun (めど "aim" ≠ 基準 "criterion"); (c) the part of speech is wrong (an adverb used as a predicate: とりわけだった, はなはだです); (d) the sentence contradicts itself (だるいので気分がいい). Name the type for each wrong option and the right one is what is left.

(1)　ほこるhokoru

Word: ほこるhokoru — to boast of, take pride in
Correct sentence (1): 彼女は社内営業成績トップの実力をほこっている。Kanojo wa shanai eigyou seiseki toppu no jitsuryoku o hokotte iru.
EN: She boasts the ability to be top in sales within the company.
HI: वह कंपनी में बिक्री में सबसे आगे रहने की क़ाबिलियत पर गर्व करती है।
GU: તે કંપનીમાં વેચાણમાં ટોચ પર રહેવાની ક્ષમતા પર ગર્વ કરે છે.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1彼女は社内営業成績トップの実力をほこっている。Kanojo wa shanai eigyou seiseki toppu no jitsuryoku o hokotte iru.She boasts the ability to be top in sales within the company.वह कंपनी में बिक्री में सबसे आगे रहने की क़ाबिलियत पर गर्व करती है।તે કંપનીમાં વેચાણમાં ટોચ પર રહેવાની ક્ષમતા પર ગર્વ કરે છે.
2ほこったじゃがいもをつぶして、しょうゆとバターをつけて食べます。Hokotta jagaimo o tsubushite, shouyu to bataa o tsukete tabemasu.✗ Means "mash steamed potatoes and eat them with soy sauce and butter" — needs ふかした (fukashita).✗ अर्थ: "उबले/भाप में पके आलू मसलकर…" — यहाँ ふかした चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "બાફેલા બટાકા છૂંદીને…" — અહીં ふかした જોઈએ.
3完ぺきな彼にも少しずつ欠点がほこり始めた。Kanpeki na kare ni mo sukoshi zutsu ketten ga hokori hajimeta.✗ Means "flaws began to show even in perfect him" — needs 見え (mie) or ほころび (hokorobi, to come apart).✗ अर्थ: "उसकी कमियाँ दिखने लगीं" — 見え या ほころび चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "તેની ખામીઓ દેખાવા લાગી" — 見え કે ほころび જોઈએ.
4部屋を掃除しないと、すぐにほこってしまう。Heya o souji shinai to, sugu ni hokotte shimau.✗ Means "the room soon gets dusty" — needs ほこりがたまって (hokori ga tamatte); ほこり "dust" is a noun, not this verb.✗ अर्थ: "कमरे में जल्दी धूल जम जाती है" — ほこりがたまって चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "રૂમમાં જલદી ધૂળ જામી જાય છે" — ほこりがたまって જોઈએ.

Answer 1 (book key, p.48). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): ほこる expresses that someone or something has strength or force (here: real ability as the top seller). Why the others are wrong (added, not in book): 2 and 4 play on sound: ほこり (埃, dust) is a noun that looks like the verb's stem; 3 confuses it with ほころびる (綻びる, a seam coming apart → flaws showing). ほこる always takes something good as its object: 実力・技術・伝統をほこる.

2もう少しで水泳がそこなうところだった。Mou sukoshi de suiei ga sokonau tokoro datta.✗ Means "I almost drowned while swimming" — needs おぼれる (oboreru); そこなう needs an object, or a verb before it (〜しそこなう).✗ अर्थ: "तैरते समय लगभग डूब गया" — おぼれる चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "તરતી વખતે લગભગ ડૂબી ગયો" — おぼれる જોઈએ.
3景気が悪くなると、そこなうまでは消費活動は停滞する。Keiki ga waruku naru to, sokonau made wa shouhi katsudou wa teitai suru.✗ Means "once the economy worsens, spending stalls until it recovers" — needs 回復する (kaifuku suru).✗ अर्थ: "…जब तक (अर्थव्यवस्था) सुधरती नहीं" — 回復する चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "…જ્યાં સુધી (અર્થતંત્ર) સુધરે નહીં" — 回復する જોઈએ.
4花見のときは、大勢の見物客でそこなうことが多い。Hanami no toki wa, oozei no kenbutsukyaku de sokonau koto ga ooi.✗ Means "at cherry-blossom time it is often crowded with visitors" — needs 混雑する / にぎわう (konzatsu suru / nigiwau).✗ अर्थ: "भीड़ बहुत होती है" — 混雑する/にぎわう चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "ભીડ બહુ થાય છે" — 混雑する/にぎわう જોઈએ.

Answer 1 (book key, p.48). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): そこなう means "to hurt", "to fail", "to lose". As 〜しそこなう it also means "to come close to doing" or "to fail to do". Why the others are wrong (added, not in book): そこなう is transitive: something good (関係・健康・信用・機嫌) is the object with を. 2–4 use it with no object and with the meanings drown / recover / be crowded.

(4)

(4)　つつしむtsutsushimu

Word: つつしむtsutsushimu — to do respectfully; to refrain from
Correct sentence (3): お子さまの誕生を、つつしんでお喜び申し上げます。Okosama no tanjou o, tsutsushinde oyorokobi moushiagemasu.
EN: I respectfully offer my congratulations on the birth of your child.
HI: आपके बच्चे के जन्म पर मैं आदरपूर्वक बधाई देता/देती हूँ।
GU: આપના બાળકના જન્મ પર હું આદરપૂર્વક અભિનંદન પાઠવું છું.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1明日から、試験の勉強を一生懸命につつしもうと思う。Ashita kara, shiken no benkyou o isshoukenmei ni tsutsushimou to omou.✗ Means "from tomorrow I'll work hard at studying for the exam" — needs 取り組もう / 励もう (torikumou / hagemou).✗ अर्थ: "कल से मेहनत से पढ़ाई में जुटूँगा" — 取り組もう चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "કાલથી મહેનતથી ભણવામાં લાગી જઈશ" — 取り組もう જોઈએ.
2住民の反対のために、工事の計画がなかなかつつしまない。Juumin no hantai no tame ni, kouji no keikaku ga nakanaka tsutsushimanai.✗ Means "because residents object, the construction plan does not move forward" — needs 進まない (susumanai).✗ अर्थ: "योजना आगे नहीं बढ़ रही" — 進まない चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "યોજના આગળ વધતી નથી" — 進まない જોઈએ.
3お子さまの誕生を、つつしんでお喜び申し上げます。Okosama no tanjou o, tsutsushinde oyorokobi moushiagemasu.I respectfully offer my congratulations on the birth of your child.आपके बच्चे के जन्म पर मैं आदरपूर्वक बधाई देता/देती हूँ।આપના બાળકના જન્મ પર હું આદરપૂર્વક અભિનંદન પાઠવું છું.
4割れそうな皿は、新聞紙でつつしんだほうがよい。Waresou na sara wa, shinbunshi de tsutsushinda hou ga yoi.✗ Means "better wrap fragile plates in newspaper" — needs つつんだ (tsutsunda, 包む).✗ अर्थ: "नाज़ुक प्लेटें अख़बार में लपेटें" — つつんだ (包む) चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "નાજુક પ્લેટો છાપામાં લપેટો" — つつんだ (包む) જોઈએ.

Answer 3 (book key, p.48). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): Written 謹んで, meaning "with a composed, respectful feeling". つつしむ can also be written 慎む; then it means "to try not to do something, to hold back from doing it". Why the others are wrong (added, not in book): 4 is the sound trap: 包む (つつむ) "wrap" vs つつしむ. 1 and 2 have nothing to do with respect or restraint. Remember the fixed formal phrase つつしんで〜申し上げます.

(5)

(5)　おしよせるoshiyoseru

Word: おしよせるoshiyoseru — to surge, rush in like a wave
Correct sentence (2): 開店を待てない人々がデパートの入り口におしよせた。Kaiten o matenai hitobito ga depaato no iriguchi ni oshiyoseta.
EN: People who could not wait for opening time surged to the department-store entrance.
HI: दुकान खुलने का इंतज़ार न कर पाने वाले लोग डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर के दरवाज़े पर उमड़ पड़े।
GU: દુકાન ખૂલવાની રાહ ન જોઈ શકનારા લોકો ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરના દરવાજે ઊમટી પડ્યા.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1彼が、背中が痛いというので、背中をおしよせてみた。Kare ga, senaka ga itai to iu node, senaka o oshiyosete mita.✗ Means "he said his back hurt, so I tried pressing / massaging it" — needs 押して / もんで (oshite / monde).✗ अर्थ: "पीठ दबाकर/मालिश करके देखी" — 押して/もんで चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "પીઠ દબાવી/માલિશ કરી જોઈ" — 押して/もんで જોઈએ.
2開店を待てない人々がデパートの入り口におしよせた。Kaiten o matenai hitobito ga depaato no iriguchi ni oshiyoseta.People who could not wait for opening time surged to the department-store entrance.दुकान खुलने का इंतज़ार न कर पाने वाले लोग डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर के दरवाज़े पर उमड़ पड़े।દુકાન ખૂલવાની રાહ ન જોઈ શકનારા લોકો ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરના દરવાજે ઊમટી પડ્યા.
3これは多くの人からおしよせられた募金です。Kore wa ooku no hito kara oshiyoserareta bokin desu.✗ Means "these are donations sent in by many people" — needs 寄せられた (yoserareta).✗ अर्थ: "बहुत लोगों से आया चंदा" — 寄せられた चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "ઘણા લોકો તરફથી આવેલો ફાળો" — 寄せられた જોઈએ.
4彼は多くの人の信用をおしよせられている。Kare wa ooku no hito no shin'you o oshiyoserarete iru.✗ Means "he has the trust of many people" — needs 得て (ete) or 信頼を寄せられて (yoserarete).✗ अर्थ: "उसे बहुत लोगों का भरोसा मिला है" — 得て चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "તેને ઘણા લોકોનો વિશ્વાસ મળ્યો છે" — 得て જોઈએ.

Answer 2 (book key, p.48). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): Written 押し寄せる. Used like 波が押し寄せる (waves surge in); people can be likened to a wave, as in sentence 2. Why the others are wrong (added, not in book): おしよせる is intransitive — a crowd or a wave is the subject and the destination takes に. 1 uses it transitively (背中を); 3 and 4 need plain 寄せる ("send in, give"), which only shares the second half.

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Words that the wrong sentences really needed, set against the drilled word (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
ほこるhokoru vs ほこりhokori to take pride in vs dust (also 誇り "pride") ほこり is both 埃 (dust) and 誇り (pride, the noun of ほこる). Dust is ほこりがたまる; there is no verb "ほこる" for getting dusty.
ほこるhokoru vs ほころびるhokorobiru take pride in vs come apart at the seams ほころびる (綻びる) = a seam splits, a flower starts to open, a weakness begins to show.
たずさわるtazusawaru vs さわるsawaru vs たずねるtazuneru be involved in vs touch vs visit / ask 仕事にたずさわる / 熱い物にさわる / 故郷をたずねる. Only たずさわる is about taking part in work.
そこなうsokonau vs 〜そこなう-sokonau to damage vs to fail to do 健康をそこなう (harm health) ／ 電車に乗りそこなう (miss the train) ／ 死にそこなう (nearly die).
つつしむtsutsushimu vs つつむtsutsumu respectfully / refrain vs wrap 謹んでお祝い申し上げます・言動を慎む vs 皿を新聞紙で包む.
おしよせるoshiyoseru vs よせるyoseru surge in vs send in, place (trust) 人が入り口におしよせる vs 募金・信頼が寄せられる. 押し adds the wave-like force.
Exam trap: in this drill three of the four questions hid a look-alike word among the wrong sentences — ほこり (dust), さわる (touch), つつむ (wrap), よせる (send in). When a 用法 sentence feels "almost right", read the word aloud and ask whether a shorter or similar word was meant instead.
