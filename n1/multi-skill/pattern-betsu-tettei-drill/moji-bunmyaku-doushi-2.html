2 Every question — 問題 (p.23)

問題　（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

GU: જો નિયમનો ભંગ કરો તો સજા ભોગવવી પડે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Built from this drill's distractors (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
そむくsomuku vs むかうmukau disobey vs head toward Both take に: 法にそむく vs 駅に向かう. A rule plus 罰 points to そむく.
ひっかくhikkaku vs ひきずるhikizuru scratch hard vs drag Both start ひ〜; かゆい → ひっかく. ひきずる is for feet, luggage or a lingering feeling (過去を引きずる).
ねだるnedaru vs ゆさぶるyusaburu beg for vs shake 親に物をねだる; 木・心をゆさぶる (shake a tree / move someone).
にぎわうnigiwau vs さわぐsawagu be bustling vs make noise A place にぎわう (町が人でにぎわう); people さわぐ (子どもがさわぐ).
いどむidomu vs うちこむuchikomu challenge vs devote oneself 記録・試験にいどむ = try to beat it; 研究に打ち込む = put all your energy into it.
かくkaku vs いじるijiru (恥・汗を)かく vs fiddle with 恥をかく / 汗をかく / いびきをかく are fixed; いじる = touch and fiddle (髪をいじる).
ゆすぐyusugu vs そそぐsosogu rinse vs pour 口・洗濯物をゆすぐ; 水を注ぐ / 力を注ぐ.
なつくnatsuku vs にげるnigeru warm up to vs run away 私になつく (become attached to me) — the natural complaint about a new pet.
すますsumasu vs まにあうmaniau make do / finish vs be in time 〜を〜ですます = make do with; 電車に間に合う = be in time.
ぼやくboyaku vs さまようsamayou grumble vs wander 「…」とぼやく is speech; さまよう is movement (街をさまよう).
Exam trap: several items here are fixed phrases you simply have to know — 恥をかく, 口をゆすぐ, 記録にいどむ, 〜で済ます. When two options both fit the grammar (both take に, both are transitive), the noun right before the blank decides.
