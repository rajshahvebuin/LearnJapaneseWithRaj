2 Every question — 問題 (p.13)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

問1　次回の首相会談が円滑に行われるように十分な準備をする必要がある。Jikai no shushou kaidan ga enkatsu ni okonawareru you ni juubun na junbi o suru hitsuyou ga aru.

Underlined word: 会談kaidan — talks, conference
EN: Thorough preparation is needed so that the next prime-ministerial talks go smoothly.
HI: अगली प्रधानमंत्री-स्तरीय वार्ता सुचारु रूप से हो, इसके लिए पूरी तैयारी करना ज़रूरी है।
GU: આગામી વડાપ્રધાન-સ્તરની વાટાઘાટો સરળતાથી થાય તે માટે પૂરતી તૈયારી કરવી જરૂરી છે.

3 Confusion pairs — the traps in the wrong options

Every wrong option above is one of the four trap types from the tips page (see 漢字読み①). These are the ones worth remembering (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
円滑enkatsu vs 骨kotsu smooth vs bone 滑 contains 骨, but is read カツ (円滑) / すべる (滑る).
勢力seiryoku vs 大勢oozei strength vs a crowd 勢 = セイ at the start; ゼイ only after another element (大勢・多勢).
放棄houki vs 破棄haki giving up (a right) vs cancelling (a contract) Same 棄 = キ; 放 ホウ vs 破 ハ.
免除menjo vs 免許menkyo exemption vs licence Same 免 = メン; 除 ジョ vs 許 キョ.
危ないabunai / 危ういayaui / 危ぶむayabumu dangerous / precarious / to fear for あぶ- only in 危ない; あや- in 危うい and 危ぶむ.
指摘shiteki vs 視点shiten remark vs viewpoint Both in the same sentence — 摘 = テキ, 点 = テン.
欠如ketsujo vs 欠陥kekkan lack vs defect 欠 becomes けっ only before k/s/t/h sounds: けっかん, but けつじょ.
介護kaigo vs 会合kaigou nursing care vs meeting 護 = ゴ (short); 合 = ゴウ (long). Check the long vowel.
