【1】 Aかぎりだ

neutral register
ReadingA kagiri da
Book's meaning (意味)本当にAだと感じる
Meaning (EN)"I feel truly / extremely A" — the speaker's strong feeling
Meaning (HI)सचमुच बहुत A लगता है (वक्ता की भावना)
Meaning (GU)ખરેખર ખૂબ A લાગે છે (વક્તાની લાગણી)
Connection (接続)感情のイ形容詞「〜い」＋ かぎりだ（さびしい・うらやましい・心細い・うれしい など）
RegisterNeutral-formal
Typical useOnly for the speaker's own emotions; adjectives of feeling: さびしい, うらやましい, 心細い, うれしい, 恥ずかしい. な-adj: 残念なかぎりだ.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj ＋ かぎりだ心細いかぎりだkokorobosoi kagiri da
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj ＋ な ＋ かぎりだ残念なかぎりだzannen na kagiri da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 友人が二人も帰国してしまい、さびしいかぎりだ。Yuujin ga futari mo kikoku shite shimai, sabishii kagiri da. Two of my friends have gone back to their countries, and I feel terribly lonely. मेरे दो-दो दोस्त अपने देश लौट गए, बहुत अकेलापन लग रहा है। મારા બબ્બે મિત્રો સ્વદેશ પાછા ગયા, ખૂબ એકલું લાગે છે.
(book: 解説) 海外旅行を年に何度もしているなんて、うらやましいかぎりです。Kaigai ryokou o nen ni nando mo shite iru nante, urayamashii kagiri desu. Travelling abroad several times a year — I'm truly envious. साल में कई बार विदेश यात्रा करते हो, सचमुच बहुत ईर्ष्या होती है। વર્ષમાં ઘણી વાર વિદેશ પ્રવાસ કરો છો, ખરેખર ખૂબ ઈર્ષ્યા થાય છે.
(book: 問題 (1)) 頼りにしていた先輩が次々と部をやめてしまい、心細いかぎりだ。Tayori ni shite ita senpai ga tsugitsugi to bu o yamete shimai, kokorobosoi kagiri da. The seniors I relied on have quit the club one after another, and I feel very insecure. जिन सीनियर्स पर भरोसा था वे एक-एक करके क्लब छोड़ गए, बहुत असहाय महसूस हो रहा है। જે સિનિયરો પર ભરોસો હતો તેઓ એક પછી એક ક્લબ છોડી ગયા, ખૂબ અસહાય લાગે છે.

The 解説 rules out 向きだ: 「N1はN2向きだ」 = "N1 suits N2" (子ども向きの本). 以上だ ("that's all") and べきだ (should) don't express a feeling.

【2】 A（に／として）あるまじきB

硬 formal/written
ReadingA (ni / to shite) aru majiki B
Book's meaning (意味)AがしてはならないB
Meaning (EN)B (conduct) that a person in the position A must never do — "unbecoming of A"
Meaning (HI)A (पद वाले) के लिए अशोभनीय B
Meaning (GU)A (પદ ધરાવનાર) માટે અશોભનીય B
Connection (接続)立場の名詞 ＋（に／として）あるまじき ＋ 行為などの名詞
Register硬 — news, formal criticism
Typical useA = 教師, 政治家, 医者, 教員 …; B = 行為, 言動, 姿, 発言.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ にあるまじき ＋ N教員にあるまじき行為kyouin ni aru majiki koui
NounN ＋ としてあるまじき ＋ N医者としてあるまじき行為isha to shite aru majiki koui
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 汚職をするなんて、政治家にあるまじき行為だ。Oshoku o suru nante, seijika ni aru majiki koui da. Being corrupt is conduct unbecoming of a politician. भ्रष्टाचार करना एक राजनेता के लिए अशोभनीय काम है। ભ્રષ્ટાચાર કરવો એ રાજનેતા માટે અશોભનીય કૃત્ય છે.
(book: 解説) 麻薬に手を出すなんて、医者（に／として）あるまじき行為だ。Mayaku ni te o dasu nante, isha (ni / to shite) aru majiki koui da. Getting involved with drugs is conduct unbecoming of a doctor. नशीली दवाओं में हाथ डालना डॉक्टर के लिए अशोभनीय काम है। નશીલી દવાઓમાં હાથ નાખવો એ ડૉક્ટર માટે અશોભનીય કૃત્ય છે.
(book: 問題 (2)) 学生によって差別するなんて、教員にあるまじき行為だ。Gakusei ni yotte sabetsu suru nante, kyouin ni aru majiki koui da. Treating students differently depending on who they are is conduct unbecoming of a teacher. छात्र देखकर भेदभाव करना शिक्षक के लिए अशोभनीय काम है। વિદ્યાર્થી જોઈને ભેદભાવ કરવો એ શિક્ષક માટે અશોભનીય કૃત્ય છે.

あるまじき always modifies a noun. In Q2 the blank sits between 教員に and 行為, so only あるまじき fits; すぎない would need 〜にすぎない + a judgement, not 行為.

【3】 Aとあって、B

neutral register
ReadingA to atte, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aので、B
Meaning (EN)because (of the special circumstance) A, B — "since it is A, (naturally) B"
Meaning (HI)क्योंकि (ख़ास स्थिति) A है, इसलिए B
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે (ખાસ સ્થિતિ) A છે, એટલે B
Connection (接続)動詞・イ形容詞の「普通形」、名詞 ＋ とあって
RegisterFormal / news style
Typical useThe 解説 notes it is used to describe things objectively, not the speaker's personal topic. B is a natural result: 混雑している, にぎわっている, 大勢集まった.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ とあってお祭りとあってomatsuri to atte
Verb(added, not in book) 普通形 ＋ とあって人気歌手が来るとあってninki kashu ga kuru to atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 花見の季節とあって、公園へ続く道は混雑している。Hanami no kisetsu to atte, kouen e tsuzuku michi wa konzatsu shite iru. Since it's cherry-blossom season, the road to the park is crowded. चेरी-ब्लॉसम का मौसम है, इसलिए पार्क की ओर जाने वाली सड़क पर भीड़ है। ચેરી-બ્લૉસમની મોસમ છે, એટલે બગીચા તરફના રસ્તે ભીડ છે.
(book: 解説) 初孫とあって、父は私の息子に毎日のように会いにやってくる。Hatsumago to atte, chichi wa watashi no musuko ni mainichi no you ni ai ni yatte kuru. Since he is his first grandchild, my father comes to see my son almost every day. पहला पोता होने के कारण पिताजी मेरे बेटे से मिलने लगभग रोज़ आते हैं। પહેલો પૌત્ર હોવાથી પિતાજી મારા દીકરાને મળવા લગભગ રોજ આવે છે.
(book: 問題 (3)) 今日はこの地区最大のお祭りとあって、大勢の観光客でにぎわっている。Kyou wa kono chiku saidai no omatsuri to atte, oozei no kankoukyaku de nigiwatte iru. Since today is the biggest festival in this district, it is bustling with crowds of tourists. आज इस इलाक़े का सबसे बड़ा त्योहार है, इसलिए बहुत से पर्यटकों से रौनक है। આજે આ વિસ્તારનો સૌથી મોટો તહેવાર છે, એટલે ઘણા પ્રવાસીઓથી ધમધમે છે.

Distractors from the 解説: をおいて = 「AをおいてBない」 (no one but A) and どころか = "not only A but even B (higher/lower)". のせいで blames a cause for a bad result — crowds of tourists are not presented as bad here.

【4】 Aべく、B

硬 formal/written
ReadingA beku, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aするために、Bする
Meaning (EN)in order to A, B (stiff written purpose)
Meaning (HI)A करने के उद्देश्य से B करना
Meaning (GU)A કરવાના હેતુથી B કરવું
Connection (接続)動詞の「辞書形」＋ べく　★「する」→「するべく／すべく」
Register硬 — more formal than ために
Typical useB is the speaker's / subject's deliberate action; not for requests or commands. Negative form 〜べからず is different (prohibition).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-る ＋ べくなるべく（医者に）naru beku
するするべく／すべく美しくすべくutsukushiku su beku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 富士山を美しくすべく、ゴミ収拾が続けられている。Fujisan o utsukushiku su beku, gomi shuushuu ga tsuzukerarete iru. To keep Mt Fuji beautiful, rubbish collection continues. फ़ूजी पर्वत को सुंदर बनाए रखने के लिए कचरा सफ़ाई जारी है। ફુજી પર્વતને સુંદર રાખવા માટે કચરો એકઠો કરવાનું ચાલુ છે.
(book: 解説) 恵まれない子どもたちに手を差し伸べるべく、募金活動に取り組んだ。Megumarenai kodomotachi ni te o sashinoberu beku, bokin katsudou ni torikunda. In order to reach out to underprivileged children, we worked on a fund-raising campaign. वंचित बच्चों की मदद के लिए हमने चंदा अभियान चलाया। વંચિત બાળકોને મદદ કરવા માટે અમે ફાળો એકઠો કરવાનું અભિયાન ચલાવ્યું.
(book: 問題 (4)) 将来医者になるべく、日々努力を重ねている。Shourai isha ni naru beku, hibi doryoku o kasanete iru. I am working hard every day in order to become a doctor in the future. भविष्य में डॉक्टर बनने के लिए मैं रोज़ मेहनत कर रहा हूँ। ભવિષ્યમાં ડૉક્ટર બનવા માટે હું રોજ મહેનત કરું છું.

The 解説 rules out ところで (A-た ところで = "even if A, (bad) B") and までもなく ("without needing to A"). うちに ("while") gives no purpose.

【5】 Aや否や、B

硬 formal/written
ReadingA ya ina ya, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aすると同時にBが起こった
Meaning (EN)as soon as A, B (happened) / no sooner A than B
Meaning (HI)जैसे ही A हुआ, तुरंत B हुआ
Meaning (GU)જેવું A થયું, તરત B થયું
Connection (接続)動詞の「辞書形」＋ や否や
Register硬 — written, narrative
Typical useB is a past event that happened right away (often unexpected). Shorter form: 〜や.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-る ＋ や否や終わるや否やowaru ya ina ya
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 話題の俳優が現れるや否や、記者が彼を取り囲んだ。Wadai no haiyuu ga arawareru ya ina ya, kisha ga kare o torikakonda. The moment the much-talked-about actor appeared, reporters surrounded him. जैसे ही चर्चित अभिनेता आया, पत्रकारों ने उसे घेर लिया। જેવો ચર્ચિત અભિનેતા આવ્યો, પત્રકારોએ તેને ઘેરી લીધો.
(book: 解説) 人気歌手の新曲が発売されるや否や、店頭のCDが完売した。Ninki kashu no shinkyoku ga hatsubai sareru ya ina ya, tentou no shii-dii ga kanbai shita. As soon as the popular singer's new song was released, the CDs in the shops sold out. जैसे ही लोकप्रिय गायक का नया गाना रिलीज़ हुआ, दुकानों की CD बिक गईं। જેવું લોકપ્રિય ગાયકનું નવું ગીત રિલીઝ થયું, દુકાનોની CD વેચાઈ ગઈ.
(book: 問題 (5)) 生徒たちは授業が終わるや否や、グラウンドに向かっていった。Seitotachi wa jugyou ga owaru ya ina ya, guraundo ni mukatte itta. The moment class ended, the students headed for the playground. जैसे ही कक्षा ख़त्म हुई, छात्र मैदान की ओर चल दिए। જેવો વર્ગ પૂરો થયો, વિદ્યાર્થીઓ મેદાન તરફ ચાલ્યા ગયા.

Unlike とたん (V-た とたん), や否や takes the dictionary form even for past events — the main trap in Q5 (終わった is wrong).

【6】 AともなくB

neutral register
ReadingA tomo naku B
Book's meaning (意味)特にAしようと思わずに、Bして（いる／いた）
Meaning (EN)doing B without really meaning to A — idly / absent-mindedly
Meaning (HI)बिना ख़ास इरादे के, यूँ ही B करना
Meaning (GU)ખાસ ઇરાદા વગર, એમ જ B કરવું
Connection (接続)動詞の「辞書形」＋ ともなく（同じ動詞をくり返す）　★「AともなしにB」もほぼ同じ意味
RegisterWritten / literary
Typical useSame verb repeated: 見るともなく見る, 聞くともなく聞く, 読むともなく読む. Then something is noticed by chance.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (same verb ×2)V-る ＋ ともなく ＋ V見るともなく見ていたmiru tomo naku mite ita
Question word(added, not in book) どこ／だれ ＋ からともなくどこからともなくdoko kara tomo naku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 聞くともなく聞いていたラジオから校歌が流れた。Kiku tomo naku kiite ita rajio kara kouka ga nagareta. From the radio I was half-listening to, my school song came on. मैं जो रेडियो यूँ ही सुन रहा था, उस पर मेरे स्कूल का गीत बजा। હું જે રેડિયો એમ જ સાંભળતો હતો, તેના પર મારી શાળાનું ગીત વાગ્યું.
(book: 解説) 読むともなく読んでいた週刊誌に、小学生のときの同級生の記事があった。Yomu tomo naku yonde ita shuukanshi ni, shougakusei no toki no doukyuusei no kiji ga atta. In the weekly magazine I was idly flipping through, there was an article about a classmate from primary school. जो साप्ताहिक पत्रिका मैं यूँ ही पढ़ रहा था, उसमें प्राइमरी स्कूल के एक सहपाठी पर लेख था। જે સાપ્તાહિક હું એમ જ વાંચતો હતો, તેમાં પ્રાથમિક શાળાના એક સહપાઠી વિશે લેખ હતો.
(book: 問題 (6)) 見るともなく見ていたインターネットに、母校のニュースが出ていた。Miru tomo naku mite ita intaanetto ni, bokou no nyuusu ga dete ita. On the internet I was idly browsing, there was news about my old school. जो इंटरनेट मैं यूँ ही देख रहा था, उस पर मेरे पुराने स्कूल की ख़बर थी। જે ઇન્ટરનેટ હું એમ જ જોતો હતો, તેના પર મારી જૂની શાળાના સમાચાર હતા.

The 解説: なしに = "without A" (with a noun, N2); そばから = 「A（辞書形／た形）そばから、B」 "no sooner A than B (again and again)". ばかりに gives a bad cause.

【7】 Aたるもの、B

硬 formal/written
ReadingA taru mono, B
Book's meaning (意味)Aという立場の者は、B
Meaning (EN)anyone who is (in the position of) A should / must B
Meaning (HI)जो A (पद पर) है, उसे B करना ही चाहिए
Meaning (GU)જે A (પદ પર) છે, તેણે B કરવું જ જોઈએ
Connection (接続)「教師」「社長」など立場を表す名詞 ＋ たるもの　★Bは「はず／べき／てはならない」などの表現
Register硬 — formal, moralising
Typical useStates what the role requires, so B must be べきだ, なければならない, てはならない — not a description of a fact.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ たるもの社長たるものshachou taru mono
Noun-modifier(added, not in book) N ＋ たる ＋ N教師たる者kyoushi taru mono
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: この文法をマスター) 大学生たるもの、何よりも学業を優先するべきだ。Daigakusei taru mono, nani yori mo gakugyou o yuusen suru beki da. A university student should put studies before anything else. विश्वविद्यालय के छात्र को सबसे पहले पढ़ाई को प्राथमिकता देनी चाहिए। યુનિવર્સિટીના વિદ્યાર્થીએ સૌથી પહેલાં અભ્યાસને પ્રાથમિકતા આપવી જોઈએ.
(book: 解説) 弁護士たるもの、日頃から人の模範となるよう努力すべきだ。Bengoshi taru mono, higoro kara hito no mohan to naru you doryoku su beki da. A lawyer should always strive to be a model for others. वकील को हमेशा दूसरों के लिए आदर्श बनने का प्रयास करना चाहिए। વકીલે હંમેશાં બીજાઓ માટે આદર્શ બનવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવો જોઈએ.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Built from the distractors the book set against each correct answer, plus the related forms its 解説 mentions.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜かぎりだkagiri da I feel so ~ 感情い-adj ＋ かぎりだ Speaker's own feeling only. さびしい／うらやましいかぎりだ.
〜向きだmuki da suited to ~ N1はN2向きだ Suitability, not emotion. 子ども向きの本.
〜にあるまじきni aru majiki unbecoming of ~ 立場N ＋ にあるまじき ＋ N Moral criticism of a role. Formal condemnation.
〜とあってto atte because (special situation) N／普通形 ＋ とあって Objective, special occasion → natural result. News-style description.
〜どころかdokoro ka far from ~ / not only N／普通形 ＋ どころか Contradicts or escalates. 休むどころか働いた.
〜べくbeku in order to V-る ＋ べく (する→すべく) Formal purpose; own deliberate action. Written reports.
〜や否やya ina ya as soon as V-る ＋ や否や Dictionary form even for past. Narrating instant reactions.
〜たとたんta totan the moment ~ V-た ＋ とたん Uses た-form; sudden change. (added, not in book) Everyday narration.
〜ともなくtomo naku idly, without intending V-る ＋ ともなく ＋ same V Unintentional action → chance discovery. 見るともなく見る.
〜そばからsoba kara no sooner ~ than (again) V-る／V-た ＋ そばから Repeated undoing of an action. 覚えるそばから忘れる.
〜たるものtaru mono anyone who is ~ (should) 立場N ＋ たるもの B must be べき／はず／てはならない. Moral rules for a role.
〜てからというものte kara to iu mono ever since ~ V-て ＋ からというもの B = lasting change, not one event. Life-changing turning points.
Exam trap 1 — tense before や否や: 終わるや否や (Q5), not 終わったや否や. Past tense goes only at the end of the sentence.
Exam trap 2 — "ending" questions: Q7 and Q8 test what the pattern requires after it. たるもの → べき／なければならない; てからというもの → a lasting changed state (〜なくなった／〜ようになった).
