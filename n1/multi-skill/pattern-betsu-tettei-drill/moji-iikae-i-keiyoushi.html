2 Every question — 問題 (p.41)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

The wrong options in this drill were mostly look-alike or sound-alike words. These are worth keeping apart (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
はなばなしいhanabanashii vs はなはだしいhanahadashii brilliant vs extreme 華々しい (デビュー・活躍) is praise; 甚だしい (誤解・被害) is "extreme", usually negative. One kana apart.
はなやかhanayaka vs はれやかhareyaka gorgeous vs bright (mood) 華やか: showy and glamorous (華やかな舞台); 晴れやか: a clear, happy face or mood (晴れやかな顔).
乏しいtoboshii vs 少ないsukunai lacking vs few 乏しい is formal and means something needed is in short supply (資源・経験・才能が乏しい); 少ない is neutral.
紛らわしいmagirawashii vs おぼえにくいoboenikui confusing vs hard to remember The book's point: confusion is the meaning; being hard to remember is only a possible result, so it is not a paraphrase.
見苦しいmigurushii / みっともないmittomonai / 醜いminikui unsightly / disgraceful / ugly 見苦しい and みっともない are about behaviour or appearance that is shameful to watch; 醜い is also physical ugliness.
名高いnadakai vs 名誉なmeiyo na famous vs honourable 名高い = widely known (有名・著名); 名誉 = honour, prestige (名誉な賞).
わずらわしいwazurawashii vs つまらないtsumaranai troublesome vs boring わずらわしい = complicated and bothersome (手続き・人間関係); つまらない = dull or worthless.
おっかないokkanai vs こわいkowai scary (casual) vs scary Same meaning; おっかない is colloquial (originally eastern Japan) and is not used in formal writing.
Exam trap: in this drill the answer could be a different part of speech: ふさわしい → ぴったりの (noun + の), 名高い → 有名な (な-adjective), and even a whole phrase: 紛らわしい → まちがえやすい (verb + やすい). Do not reject an option just because its form does not match the underlined word — test it in the sentence.
