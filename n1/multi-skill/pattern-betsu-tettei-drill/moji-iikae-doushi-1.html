1 Points — how to answer 言い換え類義 + the verbs to master

問題を解くコツ — 同じ意味の言葉を選ぶもの (summary of p.8 ③, not the book's text)

EN: For the "choose the word with the same meaning" questions, the book's advice is simple: whenever you learn a word, learn its near-synonyms with it. Watch out because the pair may be the same part of speech — 営む = 経営する (both verbs, "to run a business") — or a different one — ややこしい = 複雑だ (an い-adjective matched with a な-adjective, "complicated"). The same tips page also says to learn verbs and adjectives inside short phrases (会社を営む), not as bare words.

HI: "समान अर्थ वाला शब्द चुनें" वाले प्रश्नों के लिए किताब की सलाह है: कोई भी शब्द सीखते समय उसके मिलते-जुलते अर्थ वाले शब्द भी साथ में याद करें। ध्यान दें कि जोड़ी एक ही शब्द-भेद की हो सकती है — 営む = 経営する (दोनों क्रिया, "व्यवसाय चलाना") — या अलग शब्द-भेद की — ややこしい = 複雑だ (い-विशेषण और な-विशेषण, "पेचीदा")। शब्दों को अकेले नहीं, छोटे वाक्यांशों में याद करें (会社を営む)।

GU: "સમાન અર્થવાળો શબ્દ પસંદ કરો" પ્રકારના પ્રશ્નો માટે પુસ્તકની સલાહ છે: કોઈ પણ શબ્દ શીખો ત્યારે તેના સમાનાર્થી શબ્દો પણ સાથે યાદ કરો. ધ્યાન રાખો કે જોડી એક જ શબ્દભેદની હોઈ શકે — 営む = 経営する (બંને ક્રિયાપદ, "વ્યવસાય ચલાવવો") — અથવા જુદા શબ્દભેદની — ややこしい = 複雑だ (い-વિશેષણ અને な-વિશેષણ, "જટિલ"). શબ્દોને એકલા નહીં, નાના વાક્યાંશોમાં યાદ કરો (会社を営む).

How this drill works (added, not in book): the underlined word is usually a harder or more formal word (案じる, 赴く, 害する), and the right option is a plain everyday word or a 漢語 compound (心配する, 行く, 損なう). The book's 解説 keeps pointing to shared kanji (怠る→怠ける, 訪れる→訪ねる, 衰える→衰弱, 赴く→赴任) — use the kanji as a clue. The other 言い換え類義 pages (動詞②③, イ形容詞, ナ形容詞, 副詞) follow the same tips.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.35)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

タバコを吸うことは健康を害する行為である。Tabako o suu koto wa kenkou o gai suru koui de aru.

Underlined word: 害するgai suru — to harm
Sentence with the answer: タバコを吸うことは健康を損なう行為である。Tabako o suu koto wa kenkou o sokonau koui de aru.

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

The wrong options are mostly real verbs that sound or look close to the right idea. These are worth separating (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
怠るokotaru vs 怠けるnamakeru to neglect (a duty) vs to be lazy Same kanji, different readings. 怠る takes a specific duty as object (準備を怠る, 注意を怠る); 怠ける describes slacking in general (仕事を怠ける).
訪れるotozureru / 訪ねるtazuneru / 尋ねるtazuneru to visit / to visit / to ask 訪れる is more formal and can take seasons as subject (春が訪れる); 訪ねる = visit a person or place; 尋ねる (same sound) = ask a question.
いたわるitawaru vs やしなうyashinau treat gently vs support, feed いたわる is about kindness and care (患者をいたわる); やしなう is supporting someone with money or food (家族を養う), or building up (体力を養う).
打ち明けるuchiakeru vs 説明するsetsumei suru confide vs explain 打ち明ける = reveal what you were hiding (秘密・気持ち); 説明する = make facts clear to someone.
あせるaseru vs あわてるawateru be anxious/impatient vs be flustered 焦る: inner anxiety from time pressure (時間がなくて焦る); 慌てる: losing composure at something sudden (地震で慌てる).
衰えるotoroeru vs 薄くなるusuku naru grow weak vs grow thin/faint 衰える = strength, power or popularity declines (体力・勢力・人気); 薄くなる = thickness or colour (髪・色).
赴くomomuku → 赴任funin go to → go to a new post 赴 = "go toward". 赴任 is going to work at a new place, not 任せる (entrust).
害するgai suru vs 損なうsokonau harm vs damage Near-perfect synonyms with 健康・気分 (健康を害する ＝ 健康を損なう). 損なう also forms 〜損なう "fail to ~" (言い損なう).
Exam trap: in 言い換え類義, the book's 解説 keeps pointing to a shared kanji: 怠る→怠ける, 訪れる→訪ねる, 衰える→衰弱, 赴く→赴任, 害する→損害. If you know the kanji of the underlined verb, think of a compound that uses it — that often leads you straight to the answer. But a shared kanji can also be a trap (赴任 → 任せる ✗).
