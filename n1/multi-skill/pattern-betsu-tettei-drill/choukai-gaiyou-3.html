2 Every question — 基本問題 + 応用問題

基本問題 (p.101) — CD2 Tracks 1–2

問題　まず話を聞いてください。それから質問と選択肢を聞いて、1から4の中から、最もよいものを一つ選んでください。Mondai: Mazu hanashi o kiite kudasai. Sorekara shitsumon to sentakushi o kiite, 1 kara 4 no naka kara, mottomo yoi mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: First listen to the talk. Then listen to the question and the options, and choose the best answer from 1–4. HI: पहले बात सुनें। फिर प्रश्न और विकल्प सुनकर 1–4 में से सबसे सही उत्तर चुनें। GU: પહેલાં વાત સાંભળો. પછી પ્રશ્ન અને વિકલ્પો સાંભળીને 1–4માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

基本問題 1番 CD2 · Track 1

男の人が休みの日の過ごし方について話しています。
（質問）男の人が休みの日によくするのはどれですか。Otoko no hito ga yasumi no hi no sugoshikata ni tsuite hanashite imasu. (Shitsumon) Otoko no hito ga yasumi no hi ni yoku suru no wa dore desu ka.

EN: A man is talking about how he spends his days off. (Question) Which does the man often do on his days off?
HI: एक आदमी अपनी छुट्टी का दिन बिताने के बारे में बता रहा है। (प्रश्न) आदमी छुट्टी के दिन अक्सर क्या करता है?
GU: એક પુરુષ પોતાની રજાનો દિવસ કેવી રીતે વિતાવે છે તે વિશે વાત કરે છે. (પ્રશ્ન) પુરુષ રજાના દિવસે ઘણીવાર શું કરે છે?

In 概要理解 the question and the four options are spoken only — the book prints just 1 2 3 4. The question and options below are taken from the book's script page (p.102); they are short and quoted so the item can stand on its own.

Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: He feels lazing around at home is a waste, but he isn't the type to throw himself into a sports club either. Growing all sorts of things in a field suits him better: his wife is happy that they taste better than shop-bought, and having grown up in the countryside, the smell of earth makes him feel at ease.

HI: उसे लगता है कि घर पर आलस में पड़े रहना बेकार है, पर वह स्पोर्ट्स क्लब में जी-जान लगाने वाला भी नहीं है। खेत में तरह-तरह की चीज़ें उगाना उसे ज़्यादा जमता है: पत्नी ख़ुश होती है कि ये दुकान वाली से ज़्यादा स्वादिष्ट हैं, और गाँव में पले होने से मिट्टी की ख़ुशबू उसे सुकून देती है।

GU: તેને લાગે છે કે ઘરે આળસમાં પડ્યા રહેવું નકામું છે, પણ તે સ્પોર્ટ્સ ક્લબમાં જીવ રેડે એવો પણ નથી. ખેતરમાં જાતજાતની વસ્તુઓ ઉગાડવી તેને વધુ ફાવે છે: પત્ની ખુશ થાય છે કે એ દુકાન કરતાં વધુ સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે, અને ગામડામાં ઊછર્યો હોવાથી માટીની સુગંધથી તેને હાશ થાય છે.

The line the answer hinges on

畑でいろいろなものを育てるほうが合っているんですよ。Hatake de iroiro na mono o sodateru hou ga atte iru n desu yo.

EN: Growing all sorts of things in a field suits me better. HI: खेत में तरह-तरह की चीज़ें उगाना मुझे ज़्यादा जमता है। GU: ખેતરમાં જાતજાતની વસ્તુઓ ઉગાડવી મને વધુ ફાવે છે.

男の人が母親について話しています。
（質問）男の人は母親をどんな人だと言っていますか。Otoko no hito ga hahaoya ni tsuite hanashite imasu. (Shitsumon) Otoko no hito wa hahaoya o donna hito da to itte imasu ka.

GU: એક પુરુષ પોતાની માતા વિશે વાત કરે છે. (પ્રશ્ન) તે પોતાની માતાને કેવી વ્યક્તિ કહે છે?

In 概要理解 the question and the four options are spoken only — the book prints just 1 2 3 4. The question and options below are taken from the book's script page (p.102); they are short and quoted so the item can stand on its own.

Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: His mother has sharp intuition and sees through everything. When his boss scolded him at work, she guessed something had happened although he had said nothing at home; when his shy elder brother got a girlfriend, she casually asked if something nice had happened, even though the brother kept a poker face. Nobody can beat her.

HI: उसकी माँ का अंदाज़ा बहुत तेज़ है और वह सब भाँप लेती है। जब ऑफ़िस में बॉस ने उसे डाँटा, घर पर कुछ न बताने पर भी माँ ने अंदाज़ा लगा लिया; जब शर्मीले बड़े भाई की गर्लफ़्रेंड बनी, भाई के चेहरे पर कुछ न दिखने पर भी माँ ने सहज पूछ लिया कि कुछ अच्छा हुआ क्या। माँ से कोई नहीं जीत सकता।

GU: તેની માતાનો અંદાજ બહુ તેજ છે અને તે બધું પારખી લે છે. ઑફિસમાં બોસે તેને ઠપકો આપ્યો ત્યારે ઘરે કશું ન કહ્યું છતાં માતાએ અંદાજ લગાવી લીધો; શરમાળ મોટા ભાઈની ગર્લફ્રેન્ડ બની ત્યારે ભાઈના ચહેરા પર કંઈ ન દેખાયું છતાં માતાએ સહજતાથી પૂછ્યું કે કંઈ સારું થયું? માતા સામે કોઈ ન જીતે.

EN: In her rebellious years she paid no attention to her parents: when they told her off for huge phone bills or staying out late, she just thought it was none of their business. Only after living alone and learning things the hard way did she realise how lucky it is to have someone who nags you — and she feels that deeply now.

The line the answer hinges on

口うるさく言ってくれる人がいるっていうのはありがたいことなんだなって。今しみじみ思います。Kuchiurusaku itte kureru hito ga iru tte iu no wa arigatai koto nan da na tte. Ima shimijimi omoimasu.

EN: That having someone who nags you is something to be grateful for — I feel that deeply now. HI: कि टोकने वाला कोई होना कितनी क़द्र की बात है — अब मैं यह दिल से महसूस करती हूँ। GU: કે ટોકનાર કોઈ હોવું એ કેટલી કદરની વાત છે — હવે હું આ દિલથી અનુભવું છું.

Answer 2 (book key, p.104). Why (added, not in book): before: 全然気にも留めない・余計なお世話 (= うっとうしい); after: ありがたい・しみじみ思う (= ありがたみを実感). Option 3 reverses the direction of the change; 1 is the opposite of her rebellious past; 4 picks up 世話 from 余計なお世話 but she never talks about caring for her parents.

（質問）今どきの若者はどんな傾向があると言っていますか。Terebi bangumi de nyuusu kaisetsusha ga hanashite imasu. (Shitsumon) Imadoki no wakamono wa donna keikou ga aru to itte imasu ka.

In 概要理解 the question and the four options are spoken only — the book prints just 1 2 3 4. The question and options below are taken from the book's script page (p.104); they are short and quoted so the item can stand on its own.

Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: Young people used to spend their part-time pay on trips abroad and cars and party all night. Lately, though, a saving mindset has spread and more of them enjoy a simple life — partly because incomes are unstable in the economic slump, partly from eco-consciousness. They rarely buy clothes or eat out, yet do not seem dissatisfied; and they are not frugal about everything, spending even a little lavishly on hobbies and enjoying life cleverly.

HI: पहले युवा पार्ट-टाइम की कमाई विदेश यात्रा और कार पर ख़र्च करते और रात भर मौज करते थे। पर आजकल बचत की सोच फैल गई है और ज़्यादा युवा सादा जीवन पसंद करते हैं — कुछ आर्थिक मंदी में अस्थिर आमदनी के कारण, कुछ पर्यावरण-चेतना के कारण। वे कम कपड़े ख़रीदते और कम बाहर खाते हैं, पर असंतुष्ट नहीं लगते; और हर चीज़ में कंजूसी नहीं करते — शौक़ पर थोड़ा ज़्यादा भी ख़र्च करते हैं और समझदारी से आनंद लेते हैं।

GU: પહેલાં યુવાનો પાર્ટ-ટાઇમની કમાણી વિદેશ પ્રવાસ અને કાર પર ખર્ચતા અને આખી રાત મોજ કરતા. પણ હમણાંથી બચતની વિચારસરણી ફેલાઈ છે અને વધુ યુવાનો સાદું જીવન પસંદ કરે છે — થોડું મંદીમાં અસ્થિર આવકને કારણે, થોડું પર્યાવરણ-જાગૃતિને કારણે. તેઓ ઓછાં કપડાં ખરીદે છે અને ઓછું બહાર જમે છે, છતાં અસંતુષ્ટ નથી લાગતા; અને બધામાં કરકસર નથી કરતા — શોખ પર થોડો વધુ ખર્ચ પણ કરે છે અને સમજદારીથી આનંદ માણે છે.

The line the answer hinges on

趣味などには、少々ぜいたくでも使うところには使っていて、上手に賢く楽しんでいるようです。Shumi nado ni wa, shoushou zeitaku demo tsukau tokoro ni wa tsukatte ite, jouzu ni kashikoku tanoshinde iru you desu.

EN: On hobbies and the like they spend where it counts, even if it is a little extravagant, and seem to enjoy themselves skilfully and wisely. Answer 4 (book key, p.104). Why (added, not in book): 1 describes young people in the past (〜ものでした). 2 is denied: 不満に思っているわけでもなさそう. 3 is denied by 〜かと思いきや: they are not frugal in everything (すべてにおいて) — they spend on hobbies. That leaves 4: simple life + spending where it counts.

3 Confusion pairs — why the other options are wrong

Where the wrong options come from (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
もったいない vs 〜が合っているmottainai / ~ ga atte iru a waste vs suits me Activities called もったいない or ruled out with 〜タイプでもない are distractors.
勘が鋭い vs 知りたがるkan ga surudoi / shiritagaru intuitive vs nosy The mother doesn't ask — she already knows. 怖い = "uncanny", not strict.
うっとうしい → ありがたいuttoushii → arigatai annoying → grateful Check the direction of a change; option 3 of 応用 1番 swaps before and after.
〜ものでした~ mono deshita used to be (as a rule) Marks the past habit the speaker contrasts with now — option 1 of 応用 2番.
〜かと思いきや~ ka to omoikiya contrary to what you might think Cancels "frugal in everything" (option 3).
〜わけでもなさそう~ wake de mo nasasou doesn't seem to be the case either Denies dissatisfaction (option 2).
Exam trap: in 概要理解 the wrong options are often things the speaker raises in order to deny (〜わけではない, 〜かと思いきや, 〜タイプでもない). Note each denial with a quick ×.
