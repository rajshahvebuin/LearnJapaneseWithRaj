2 Every question — 問題 (p.43)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब वाला विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

この地図は左右があべこべだ。Kono chizu wa sayuu ga abekobe da.

Underlined word: あべこべだabekobe da — is reversed, the wrong way round
Sentence with the answer: この地図は左右が反対だ。Kono chizu wa sayuu ga hantai da.
EN: Left and right on this map are the wrong way round.
HI: इस नक़्शे में बायाँ और दायाँ उल्टा है।
GU: આ નકશામાં ડાબું અને જમણું ઊલટું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1周辺だshuuhen da(is) the surroundingsआसपास का इलाक़ा हैઆસપાસનો વિસ્તાર છે
2競争だkyousou da(is) a competitionप्रतियोगिता हैસ્પર્ધા છે
3変更だhenkou da(is) a changeबदलाव हैફેરફાર છે

Answer 4 (book key, p.44). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): あべこべな is used about order or position.

(2)

かなり昔の映画なので、どんな内容だったかあやふやだ。Kanari mukashi no eiga na node, donna naiyou datta ka ayafuya da.

Underlined word: あやふやだayafuya da — is vague, hazy
Sentence with the answer: かなり昔の映画なので、どんな内容だったかあいまいだ。Kanari mukashi no eiga na node, donna naiyou datta ka aimai da.

GU: આ ઘણી જૂની ફિલ્મ છે, એટલે તેની વાર્તા શું હતી તે મને ઝાંખું યાદ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

GU: મેં 思う અને 考える વચ્ચેનો તફાવત આશરે (સ્થૂળ રીતે) સમજાવ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1丁寧にteinei nicarefully, politelyध्यान से, विनम्रता सेકાળજીપૂર્વક
2素直にsunao nifrankly, obedientlyसीधे-सादे ढंग सेનિખાલસપણે
3わずかにwazuka nislightlyथोड़ा-साસહેજ

Answer 4 (book key, p.44). Book's 解説 (paraphrased): おおざっぱ describes grasping the overall picture quickly without considering the details. Unlike おおまかな, it can also be used for personality. Other options (added, not in book): 丁寧に is the opposite idea (with care for detail).

(4)

Underlined word: こっけいに（滑稽に）kokkei ni — comical
Sentence with the answer: 財布を忘れて買い物にきた自分自身が、おかしく思えた。Saifu o wasurete kaimono ni kita jibun jishin ga, okashiku omoeta.
EN: Coming shopping without my wallet, I felt ridiculous.
HI: पर्स भूलकर ख़रीदारी करने आए अपने आप पर मुझे हँसी आई।
GU: પાકીટ ભૂલીને ખરીદી કરવા આવેલો હું મને પોતાને જ રમુજી લાગ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おかしくokashikufunnyमज़ेदार, हास्यास्पदરમુજી

3 Confusion pairs — near-synonyms and look-alikes from this drill

Several of these な-adjectives have look-alike partners or a second use. Compare (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
あやふやayafuya / あいまいaimai / やっかいyakkai vague / vague / troublesome あやふや and あいまい are near-synonyms (unclear memory, answer, attitude); やっかい is "a nuisance" (やっかいな問題).
おおざっぱoozappa vs おおまかoomaka rough vs broad Both "not detailed" (おおざっぱ／おおまかに説明する). The book: only おおざっぱ also describes a person (おおざっぱな性格 = careless about details).
こっけいkokkei vs おかしいokashii comical vs funny / strange こっけい = laughable. おかしい has two meanings — "funny" (= こっけい) and "strange" (様子がおかしい); only the first is a paraphrase.
誠実seijitsu vs 熱心nesshin sincere vs eager 誠実 = honest and faithful to people; 熱心 = putting energy into something (熱心に勉強する).
頻繁にhinpan ni ⇔ めったに〜ないmettani ~nai frequently ⇔ rarely 頻繁に = しょっちゅう; めったに needs a negative verb.
不審fushin vs 不信fushin suspicious vs distrust Same sound. 不審な人物 = a suspicious person; 不信感 = a feeling of distrust (政治不信).
あべこべabekobe vs 反対hantai back to front vs opposite あべこべ is only about order/position being reversed (左右・上下・順番); 反対 also means "opposition" (計画に反対する).
Exam trap: the tips page (p.8) gave あやふやな／あいまいな as its own example of words with close meanings, and the pair turned up as question (2) here. Learn such pairs together — the exam usually underlines the less common word and hides the everyday word among the options.
