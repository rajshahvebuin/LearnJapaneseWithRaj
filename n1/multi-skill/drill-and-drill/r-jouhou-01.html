1 Strategy & Key Words

Key idea

お知らせや案内などの実用文を読んで、必要な情報を見つけます。Oshirase ya annai nado no jitsuyoubun o yonde, hitsuyou na jouhou o mitsukemasu.

EN: Read practical documents such as notices and guides, and find the information you need.   HI: सूचना, गाइड जैसे व्यावहारिक दस्तावेज़ पढ़कर ज़रूरी जानकारी खोजें।   GU: સૂચના, માર્ગદર્શિકા જેવા વ્યવહારુ દસ્તાવેજ વાંચીને જરૂરી માહિતી શોધો.

  • Read as if you really needed it. The book's 前書き (PDF 5) warns that without enough kanji and vocabulary these questions are hard to answer. Its tip: read as though you yourself were looking for that information right now. You'll concentrate better and find the answer more easily.
    HI: कांजी और शब्दावली कम हो तो ये प्रश्न कठिन लगते हैं। ऐसे पढ़ें जैसे आपको सच में अभी यह जानकारी चाहिए, इससे ध्यान लगेगा और उत्तर जल्दी मिलेगा। GU: કાંજી અને શબ્દભંડોળ ઓછાં હોય તો આ પ્રશ્નો અઘરા લાગે છે. એ રીતે વાંચો જાણે તમને ખરેખર હમણાં આ માહિતી જોઈએ છે, એનાથી ધ્યાન રહેશે અને જવાબ જલદી મળશે.
  • Questions first, then scan. Read the 問 and note the conditions (date, day of week, age, person's situation). Then scan the document only for those items: tables, ※ notes and small print hold most answers. (added, not in book)
    HI: पहले प्रश्न पढ़ें और शर्तें (तारीख़, दिन, उम्र, स्थिति) नोट करें। फिर दस्तावेज़ में सिर्फ़ वही खोजें: तालिकाएँ, ※ नोट और बारीक़ अक्षर ज़्यादातर उत्तर रखते हैं। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન વાંચો અને શરતો (તારીખ, વાર, ઉંમર, સ્થિતિ) નોંધો. પછી દસ્તાવેજમાં ફક્ત એ જ શોધો: કોઠા, ※ નોંધ અને ઝીણા અક્ષરોમાં મોટા ભાગના જવાબ હોય છે.
  • Watch the exceptions. 「ただし」「※」「〜のみ」「〜いたしかねます」 and "holidays excepted" lines are where the traps live. Do the arithmetic yourself before choosing. (added, not in book)
    HI: अपवादों पर ध्यान दें: 「ただし」「※」「〜のみ」「〜いたしかねます」 वाली पंक्तियों में जाल होते हैं। चुनने से पहले हिसाब ख़ुद लगाएँ। GU: અપવાદો પર ધ્યાન આપો: 「ただし」「※」「〜のみ」「〜いたしかねます」 વાળી લીટીઓમાં જાળ હોય છે. પસંદ કરતાં પહેલાં ગણતરી જાતે કરો.

The payment method is 口座振替, and the note asks for the deposit 「支払期限の前日まで」, which it rewrites as 「支払期限の前日までに自分の口座に入金しなければならない」. ① The deadline is 4 October, so the day before is 3 October. ② Tarō's account is at はなまる銀行. ① + ② → deposit into his own account by 3 October. 1 and 2 use the deadline itself and a transfer method (振り込み) the statement doesn't use. 4 has the right date, but nothing is posted: the money is taken from his account. Correct. 3: goods are chosen from the catalogue and ordered by phone, internet or post, so he can't see them in person. 4: delivery is to the registered home address only (〜のみ), so another address isn't possible.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Business-document Japanese packs its conditions into a few set phrases:

FormPlain meaningTrap it setsSeen in

〜のみ ／ 〜いたしかねます… nomi / … itashikanemasu ＝ だけ ／ できない= dake / dekinai Polite forms that close off options (other address, returns). HI: केवल / नहीं कर सकते · GU: ફક્ત / કરી શકતા નથી 問2
Exam trap: Information-retrieval options often copy a number from the document but change its unit or date (6416 points → ¥6416; deadline 10/4 → pay on 10/4). Re-check every number in an option against the exact line it comes from. (added, not in book)
