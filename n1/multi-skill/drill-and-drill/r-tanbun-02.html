1 Strategy & Key Words

Key idea

次の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bunshou o yonde, ato no toi ni taisuru kotae to shite mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Read the passage and choose the best answer to the question from 1–4.   HI: नीचे दिया पाठ पढ़ें और प्रश्न का सबसे अच्छा उत्तर 1–4 में से चुनें।   GU: નીચેનું લખાણ વાંચો અને પ્રશ્નનો સૌથી સારો જવાબ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

  • Read fast and grab the main point. The book's 前書き (PDF 5) says 短文 questions use short texts, and practical ones such as letters, e-mails and notices may appear. Practise reading quickly and catching the gist straight away.
    HI: जल्दी पढ़ें और मुख्य बात तुरंत पकड़ें। छोटे पाठ आते हैं, जिनमें चिट्ठी, ई-मेल, सूचना जैसे व्यावहारिक पाठ भी हो सकते हैं। GU: ઝડપથી વાંચો અને મુખ્ય વાત તરત પકડો. ટૂંકાં લખાણ આવે છે, જેમાં પત્ર, ઈ-મેલ, સૂચના જેવાં વ્યવહારુ લખાણ પણ હોઈ શકે.
  • Use the 解き方のヒント box. Under every passage the book prints a fill-in box that retells the passage step by step. Fill it in after answering: if you can't fill a blank, that's the part of the text you misread. The 別冊 gives the answers. (added, not in book)
    HI: उत्तर देने के बाद 解き方のヒント भरें: जो खाली जगह न भर पाएँ, वही हिस्सा आपने ग़लत पढ़ा है। GU: જવાબ આપ્યા પછી 解き方のヒント ભરો: જે ખાલી જગ્યા ન ભરી શકો, તે જ ભાગ તમે ખોટો વાંચ્યો છે.
  • Check the "size" of each option. In 「内容と合っているもの」 questions, the wrong options in this round change some into all (〜人もいる → 〜人は…), may into must (〜場合もある → 義務がある), or hoped into done (期待されている → 解決された). Underline those words in the text and in the option. (added, not in book)
    HI: हर विकल्प का "दायरा" जाँचें: "कुछ" को "सब", "हो सकता है" को "ज़रूरी है", "उम्मीद है" को "हो गया" बनाना आम जाल है। GU: દરેક વિકલ્પનો "વ્યાપ" તપાસો: "કેટલાક"ને "બધા", "થઈ શકે"ને "ફરજિયાત", "આશા છે"ને "થઈ ગયું" બનાવવું સામાન્ય જાળ છે.

2 Reading Exercises — 6番〜10番

Five short passages from p.63–67, one question each (the numbering runs on from 第1回). Read the passage in your copy of the book and answer first. Then use the summary, the key sentence and the explanation to check yourself. The passages are summarised, not transcribed.

6番

Passage — an essay on intelligence and communication (p.63)

Passage summary (not the book's text): We tend to assume that famous people and company presidents who succeed internationally must be highly intelligent, but that isn't always so. Some are, but others, without especially high intelligence, still achieve a lot because they are good at reading others' feelings, taking in others' ideas and putting their own across so that people are moved. People think and communicate in words, so the writer concludes that such people are skilled users of words with strong communication ability.
HI: हम मान लेते हैं कि दुनिया भर में सफल प्रसिद्ध लोग और बड़ी कंपनियों के अध्यक्ष बहुत बुद्धिमान होंगे, पर ऐसा हमेशा नहीं। कुछ होते हैं, पर कुछ बहुत ऊँची बुद्धि के बिना भी बड़ी उपलब्धियाँ पाते हैं, क्योंकि वे दूसरों की भावनाएँ भाँपने, उनके विचार अपनाने और अपनी बात इस तरह कहने में माहिर हैं कि लोग प्रभावित हों। इंसान शब्दों में सोचता और संवाद करता है, इसलिए लेखक कहता है कि ऐसे लोग शब्दों के कुशल प्रयोगकर्ता और श्रेष्ठ संवाद-क्षमता वाले हैं।
GU: આપણે માની લઈએ છીએ કે દુનિયામાં સફળ પ્રખ્યાત લોકો અને મોટી કંપનીઓના પ્રમુખો ખૂબ બુદ્ધિશાળી હશે, પણ હંમેશાં એવું નથી. કેટલાક હોય છે, પણ કેટલાક બહુ ઊંચી બુદ્ધિ વિના પણ મોટી સિદ્ધિઓ મેળવે છે, કારણ કે તેઓ બીજાની લાગણી પારખવામાં, તેમના વિચાર અપનાવવામાં અને પોતાની વાત એ રીતે કહેવામાં પારંગત છે કે લોકો પ્રભાવિત થાય. માણસ શબ્દોમાં વિચારે અને સંવાદ કરે છે, તેથી લેખક કહે છે કે આવા લોકો શબ્દોના કુશળ વાપરનાર અને ઉત્તમ સંવાદશક્તિ ધરાવનાર છે.

Key sentence (quoted from 別冊 ポイント)

彼らは言葉で人の心を動かすことができる、優れた言葉の使い手であり、優れたコミュニケーション力の持ち主だと言えよう。Karera wa kotoba de hito no kokoro o ugokasu koto ga dekiru, sugureta kotoba no tsukaite de ari, sugureta komyunikeeshon-ryoku no mochinushi da to ieyou.

EN: "One could say they are excellent users of words who can move people's hearts with language, and people with excellent communication skills."   HI: "कहा जा सकता है कि वे शब्दों से लोगों का दिल जीत सकने वाले, शब्दों के कुशल प्रयोगकर्ता और श्रेष्ठ संवाद-क्षमता के धनी हैं।"   GU: "કહી શકાય કે તેઓ શબ્દોથી લોકોનાં દિલ જીતી શકનારા, શબ્દોના કુશળ વાપરનાર અને ઉત્તમ સંવાદશક્તિના ધણી છે."

問　筆者がここでもっとも言いたいことは何か。Toi: Hissha ga koko de mottomo iitai koto wa nani ka.

EN: What does the writer most want to say here?   HI: लेखक यहाँ सबसे ज़्यादा क्या कहना चाहता है?   GU: લેખક અહીં સૌથી વધુ શું કહેવા માગે છે?

解き方のヒント — the blanks (別冊 p.77): ① 高い · 高くない   ② 高くなく · 業績   ③ 心／気持ち／心理 · 受け入れる · 人を動かす   ④ 考え／思考し · 言葉 · コミュニケーション   ⑤ 言葉 · コミュニケーション

Why 2 (別冊 p.77): The 別冊 key is the last sentence, the writer's conclusion about 後者 (the successful people without especially high intelligence): they are 優れた言葉の使い手. The text says some successful people are like this (〜人もいる), and option 2 keeps that "some" (〜人がいる). 1 says they all lack intelligence and credits their colleagues, which the text never says (仲間や同僚に恵まれ is only one of the possibilities listed). 3 is never claimed; the passage doesn't say intelligent people lack communication skills. 4 turns "some people without high intelligence" into a rule about all such people. 7番

Passage — a library notice on borrowing audio-visual materials (p.64)

Passage summary (not the book's text): A city library lists its rules for borrowed videotapes, CDs and DVDs. Don't use or keep them in hot places (direct sun, a closed car) or where there is dust, humidity or strong magnetism. The library isn't responsible for damage to your own player, but if something goes wrong, such as a tape getting stuck, you must contact the library at once. 解き方のヒント — the blanks (別冊 p.78): ① 競売／公売   ② 財政／財政難   ③ 税金 · 滞納 · 税金 · 物／物品   ④ だれでも · 国 · 市町村   ⑤ 国 · 市町村 · 多い · 返し

Why 4 (別冊 p.78): The 別冊 key is the surplus rule: when the winning bid is higher than the unpaid tax, the extra goes back to the defaulter, so he can come out with money (利益を得る), and 〜こともある keeps it to "sometimes". 1 confuses buying and selling: anyone can bid (だれでも簡単に入札できる), but the sellers are the state and municipalities (hint ④). 2 turns a hope (救うことになるのではと期待されている) into a result. 3 adds 価値の高い: the text says a wide range of goods is traded, and it actually raises problems over things whose value is hard to judge. Passage — an explanatory text on disposable contact lenses (p.67)

Passage summary (not the book's text): Many people choose disposable contact lenses because they feel less like a foreign body and are always clean, but even a good lens is still a foreign body in the eye. Because they are thin and comfortable, warning symptoms such as pain are hard to notice when something goes wrong. They also hold more water than conventional lenses, so bacteria multiply more easily, and a worsening inflammation can even cause blindness. They are classed as highly controlled medical devices to be used under an eye specialist's supervision, with correct use and regular check-ups essential.
HI: कई लोग डिस्पोज़ेबल कॉन्टैक्ट लेंस चुनते हैं क्योंकि वे आँख में कम खटकते हैं और हमेशा साफ़ रहते हैं, पर अच्छा लेंस भी आँख के लिए बाहरी चीज़ ही है। पतले और आरामदायक होने से, गड़बड़ होने पर दर्द जैसे लक्षण महसूस करना कठिन होता है। इनमें पारंपरिक लेंस से ज़्यादा पानी होता है, इसलिए जीवाणु आसानी से पनपते हैं, और सूजन बढ़े तो अंधापन तक हो सकता है। इन्हें उच्च-नियंत्रित चिकित्सा उपकरण माना गया है जिन्हें नेत्र-विशेषज्ञ की निगरानी में इस्तेमाल करना है; सही उपयोग और नियमित जाँच ज़रूरी है।
