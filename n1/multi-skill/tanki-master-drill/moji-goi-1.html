問題1 漢字読み — choose the reading of the underlined word

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Choose the best reading for the underlined word from 1–4.   HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही उच्चारण (पढ़ने का तरीका) 1–4 में से चुनें।   GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય ઉચ્ચારણ (વાંચવાની રીત) 1–4 માંથી પસંદ કરો.

問題1 Q1

犯人は客の一人を人質に取って立てこもっている。Hannin wa kyaku no hitori o hitojichi ni totte tatekomotte iru.

EN: The criminal has taken one of the customers hostage and has barricaded himself inside.
HI: अपराधी ने ग्राहकों में से एक को बंधक बना लिया है और अंदर मोर्चा बाँधकर बैठा है।
GU: ગુનેગારે ગ્રાહકોમાંથી એકને બંધક બનાવી લીધો છે અને અંદર ભરાઈને બેઠો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1じんしつjinshitsunot a real wordवास्तविक शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી
2ひとしつhitoshitsunot a real wordवास्तविक शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી
3ひとじちhitojichihostage ✓बंधकબંધક
4じんしちjinshichinot a real wordवास्तविक शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી

Why: 人質 uses the native (訓) reading ひと for 人. 質 is read しち (as in 質屋 shichiya, "pawnshop"), and here it becomes voiced: じち. The other options mix in the on-reading じん, or keep 質 as the common しつ (質問). They look possible, but they are not real words. Set phrase: 人質に取る "take as a hostage".

問題1 Q2

知人が仲介してくれたおかげで、問題が早く解決した。Chijin ga chuukai shite kureta okage de, mondai ga hayaku kaiketsu shita.

EN: Thanks to an acquaintance acting as a go-between, the problem was solved quickly.

1なかがいnakagaibrokerage (a different word, written 仲買)दलाली (अलग शब्द, 仲買)દલાલી (અલગ શબ્દ, 仲買)

各ページの右下にイラストを挿入することにした。Kaku peeji no migishita ni irasuto o sounyuu suru koto ni shita.

EN: We decided to insert an illustration at the bottom right of each page.

2ちゅうにゅうchuunyuuinjection, pouring in (a different word, 注入)इंजेक्शन/भरना (注入)ઇન્જેક્શન/ભરવું (注入)

The other three are real words, but each one uses a different first kanji (転・注・投). For this question type, picture the kanji of each option. Do not choose just because a reading "sounds familiar".

問題1 Q4

最近、運動不足のため体力が衰えてきたようだ。Saikin, undou busoku no tame tairyoku ga otoroete kita you da.

EN: Lately my physical strength seems to have been declining because I don't get enough exercise.
HI: लगता है कि आजकल व्यायाम की कमी से मेरी शारीरिक ताक़त घटने लगी है।
GU: લાગે છે કે હમણાં કસરતના અભાવે મારી શારીરિક શક્તિ ઘટવા લાગી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おとろえてotoroetedeclining ✓घटते हुएઘટતું
2なえてnaetegoing limp, wilting (萎えて)मुरझाकर, ढीला पड़कर (萎えて)કરમાઈને, ઢીલું પડીને (萎えて)
3きたえてkitaetetraining, strengthening (鍛えて)मज़बूत बनाकर (鍛えて)મજબૂત બનાવીને (鍛えて)
4うれえてureetegrieving, worrying (憂えて)चिंता करके (憂えて)ચિંતા કરીને (憂えて)

Why: 衰 = おとろ-える, or すい in 衰退 (decline) and 老衰 (old age). 萎える also means "to weaken", so the meaning alone could mislead you. But it is a different kanji. 鍛える is the opposite idea (体力を鍛える "build up strength"). Look-alike warning: 衰 vs 哀 (あわ-れ, "pity") vs 喪 (も, "mourning").

問題1 Q5

同級生の姿を見つけたが、人込みに紛れて見失ってしまった。Doukyuusei no sugata o mitsuketa ga, hitogomi ni magirete miushinatte shimatta.

GU: મેં મારા સહપાઠીને જોયો, પણ તે ભીડમાં ભળી ગયો અને મારી નજરથી ઓઝલ થઈ ગયો.

1まぎれてmagiretegetting lost in (a crowd) ✓(भीड़ में) खोकर(ભીડમાં) ખોવાઈને
2もまれてmomaretebeing jostled (揉まれて)धक्के खाकर (揉まれて)ધક્કા ખાઈને (揉まれて)
3かすれてkasuretebecoming faint/hoarse (掠れて)धुँधला/भर्राया होकर (掠れて)ઝાંખું/ઘોઘરું થઈને (掠れて)

Why: 紛 = まぎ-れる, or ふん in 紛失 (loss) and 紛争 (dispute). 人込みに紛れる "vanish into a crowd" is a set phrase. The trap is もまれて: 人込みにもまれる ("get jostled in a crowd") is also natural Japanese, but it is written 揉まれる. The kanji tells you the answer, not the collocation.

問題1 Q7

この罪は、一生かけても償えるものではない。Kono tsumi wa, isshou kakete mo tsugunaeru mono de wa nai.

EN: This crime is not something you can atone for, even if you spent your whole life trying.
HI: यह अपराध ऐसा नहीं है जिसका प्रायश्चित पूरी ज़िंदगी लगाकर भी किया जा सके।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1つぐなえるtsugunaerucan atone for ✓प्रायश्चित कर सकनाપ્રાયશ્ચિત કરી શકવું

Note that 償 has the 賞 part on the right, so the on-reading しょう is easy to guess.

Exam trap: in 漢字読み, the wrong options are often real words written with different kanji: なかがい (仲買), てんにゅう (転入), はばむ (阻む), もまれる (揉まれる). If an option "sounds like a real word", that does not make it the answer. Check that its kanji is the one underlined. The other big trap is voicing: 人質 = ひとじち (voiced), but 仲介 = ちゅうかい (not voiced).
