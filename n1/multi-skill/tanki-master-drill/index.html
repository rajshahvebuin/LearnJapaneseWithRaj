短期マスター N1

Following 短期マスター 日本語能力試験ドリル N1 (Tanki Master JLPT Drill N1, 凡人社) section by section. It is a short drill book that covers every JLPT section in one volume: 文字・語彙, 文法, 読解 and 聴解. The 練習問題 (practice questions) come first, then a half-length まとめのテスト (review test). Every page gives the tested words or patterns, every question with the answer from the book's 別冊 key, and confusion pairs. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • The book's own advice (p.iii): work through the 練習問題 first, then take the まとめのテスト in about half the real exam time.
  • If time is short, take the まとめのテスト first, then go back to the 練習問題 for the sections where you made the most mistakes.
  • Reading and listening pages give a short summary of each passage or script in our own words, not the book's text, and quote only the lines each question needs.
