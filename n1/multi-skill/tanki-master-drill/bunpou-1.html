【Q1】 Nゆえ（に）

硬 formal/written
ReadingN yue (ni)
Meaning (EN)"because of N, owing to N" — a cause, in a stiff written style
Meaning (HI)N के कारण, N की वजह से (औपचारिक लिखित शैली)
Meaning (GU)N ના કારણે, N ને લીધે (ઔપચારિક લેખિત શૈલી)
Connection (接続)N（の）＋ゆえ（に） ／ V・い-adj 普通形＋（が）ゆえに ／ な-adj-である＋ゆえに
Register硬 — essays, news, formal speech
Typical useExplains a cause, often a social or moral one. Also used as a noun modifier: N＋ゆえの＋N (若さゆえの過ち "a mistake of youth").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN（の）＋ゆえに親の無関心ゆえにoya no mukanshin yue ni
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形＋（が）ゆえに愛するがゆえにaisuru ga yue ni
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj＋（が）ゆえに貧しいがゆえにmazushii ga yue ni
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-である＋ゆえに未熟であるゆえにmijuku de aru yue ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q1) 親の無関心ゆえに、子どもが非行に走る場合がある。Oya no mukanshin yue ni, kodomo ga hikou ni hashiru baai ga aru. Sometimes, because of parents' lack of interest, children turn to delinquency. कभी-कभी माता-पिता की उदासीनता के कारण बच्चे ग़लत रास्ते पर चले जाते हैं। ક્યારેક માતા-પિતાની ઉદાસીનતાને કારણે બાળકો ખોટા રસ્તે ચડી જાય છે.
(added, not in book) 若さゆえの過ちは誰にでもある。Wakasa yue no ayamachi wa dare ni demo aru. Everyone makes mistakes because they are young. जवानी की वजह से होने वाली ग़लतियाँ हर किसी से होती हैं। યુવાનીને લીધે થતી ભૂલો દરેકથી થાય છે.

ゆえ is the written cousin of ため／せいで. It sounds neutral-to-serious, so it fits 非行に走る ("turn to delinquency") in a social comment. In the question, both せいで and ゆえ mean "because of", but the blank is followed by に, and せいでに is not Japanese. That particle is what decides it.

【Q2】 Nといえども

硬 formal/written
ReadingN to iedomo
Meaning (EN)"even though (one is) N, even N" — the expected status does not guarantee the result
Meaning (HI)भले ही N हो, फिर भी / N होने के बावजूद
Meaning (GU)ભલે N હોય, છતાં / N હોવા છતાં
Connection (接続)N／普通形＋といえども
Register硬 — formal speech, writing
Typical useThe first part names someone or something with high status or ability (教師, 専門家, 子ども). The second part says that even they cannot do or avoid something: 〜わけではない, 〜てはいけない, 〜ものだ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN＋といえども教師といえどもkyoushi to iedomo
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形＋といえども知っているといえどもshitte iru to iedomo
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj＋といえども若いといえどもwakai to iedomo
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj（だ）＋といえども有名（だ）といえどもyuumei (da) to iedomo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q2) 教師といえども、すべてを把握しているわけではない。Kyoushi to iedomo, subete o haaku shite iru wake de wa nai. Even a teacher does not know everything. भले ही कोई शिक्षक हो, उसे सब कुछ पता हो, ऐसा नहीं है। ભલે કોઈ શિક્ષક હોય, તેને બધું ખબર હોય એવું નથી.
(added, not in book) 子どもといえども、ルールは守らなければならない。Kodomo to iedomo, ruuru wa mamoranakereba naranai. Even children have to follow the rules. बच्चे हों तो भी, नियमों का पालन करना ही होगा। બાળકો હોય તો પણ, નિયમો પાળવા જ પડે.

Often seen with たとえ／いかに (たとえ専門家といえども). It is a formal way to say 〜であっても. The other options are near-misses built on いう: いうなら ("if I had to say"), いったから ("because I said"), いうなり ("as soon as one said").

【Q3】 〜とすれば

neutral register
Reading~ to sureba
Meaning (EN)"if we suppose ~, if ~ is the case (then I estimate)" — a supposition followed by the speaker's guess
Meaning (HI)अगर मान लें कि ~, तो (अनुमान)
Meaning (GU)જો ધારીએ કે ~, તો (અનુમાન)
Connection (接続)普通形（N／な-adj だ）＋とすれば
RegisterNeutral — speech and writing
Typical useThe second part is a guess or judgement: 〜だろう, 〜はずだ, 〜かもしれない. Same family as 〜としたら and 〜とすると.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-普通形＋とすれば出発するとすればshuppatsu suru to sureba
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj＋とすれば高いとすればtakai to sureba
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-だ＋とすれば本当だとすればhontou da to sureba
Noun(added, not in book) N-だ＋とすれば事実だとすればjijitsu da to sureba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q3) 今夜ここを出発するとすれば、あさっての朝には着くだろう。Kon'ya koko o shuppatsu suru to sureba, asatte no asa ni wa tsuku darou. If we leave here tonight, we should arrive by the morning of the day after tomorrow. अगर हम आज रात यहाँ से निकलें, तो परसों सुबह तक पहुँच जाएँगे। જો આપણે આજે રાત્રે અહીંથી નીકળીએ, તો પરમ દિવસે સવાર સુધીમાં પહોંચી જઈશું.
(added, not in book) その話が本当だとすれば、大変なことだ。Sono hanashi ga hontou da to sureba, taihen na koto da. If that story is true, it's a serious matter. अगर वह बात सच है, तो यह बहुत गंभीर बात है। જો તે વાત સાચી હોય, તો આ ગંભીર બાબત છે.

ともなると／ともなれば look similar but mean "when it comes to (a higher level)": 大学生ともなれば ("once you are a university student"). They attach to nouns of rank or time, not to 出発する. とくれば ("speaking of X, next comes Y") is a different pattern again.

【Q4】 NのみならずNも

硬 formal/written
ReadingN nominarazu N mo
Meaning (EN)"not only N but also…"
Meaning (HI)न केवल N, बल्कि ... भी
Meaning (GU)માત્ર N જ નહીં, ... પણ
Connection (接続)N／普通形（な-adj・N である）＋のみならず
Register硬 — written; spoken equivalent ばかりでなく／だけでなく
Typical useThe second part usually has も: AのみならずBも. B is often bigger or more surprising than A.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN＋のみならず味のみならずaji nominarazu
Verb(added, not in book) V-普通形＋のみならず読むのみならずyomu nominarazu
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj＋のみならず高いのみならずtakai nominarazu
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj-である＋のみならず不便であるのみならずfuben de aru nominarazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q4) この料理は味のみならず見た目も今一つだ。Kono ryouri wa aji nominarazu mitame mo imahitotsu da. This dish is not great — not only the taste but the look too. यह व्यंजन न सिर्फ़ स्वाद में, बल्कि देखने में भी कुछ ख़ास नहीं है। આ વાનગી માત્ર સ્વાદમાં જ નહીં, દેખાવમાં પણ ખાસ નથી.
(added, not in book) この問題は日本のみならず世界中で起きている。Kono mondai wa Nihon nominarazu sekaijuu de okite iru. This problem is happening not only in Japan but all over the world. यह समस्या न केवल जापान में, बल्कि पूरी दुनिया में हो रही है। આ સમસ્યા માત્ર જાપાનમાં જ નહીં, આખી દુનિયામાં થઈ રહી છે.

The も after 見た目 is the clue: only のみならず links "A … B も" here. のみで ("only with") and だけで ("just by") need a different ending. でなく would need a full noun phrase before it (味だけでなく); 味でなく alone means "not the taste, but…", which clashes with も.

【Q5】 Vかたわら

neutral register
ReadingV katawara
Meaning (EN)"while doing (one's main job), also do (something else) on the side"
Meaning (HI)(मुख्य काम) करने के साथ-साथ (दूसरा काम भी)
Meaning (GU)(મુખ્ય કામ) કરવાની સાથે સાથે (બીજું કામ પણ)
Connection (接続)V-辞書形＋かたわら ／ N の＋かたわら
RegisterNeutral to slightly written
Typical useA: a long-term main activity (job, study). B: a long-term second activity. Not used for two short actions at the same moment (that is ながら).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-辞書形＋かたわら教えるかたわらoshieru katawara
Noun(added, not in book) N の＋かたわら仕事のかたわらshigoto no katawara
い-adjective / な-adjectivenot used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題1 Q5) 私は大学で英語を教えるかたわら小説を書いている。Watashi wa daigaku de eigo o oshieru katawara shousetsu o kaite iru. While teaching English at a university, I also write novels. मैं विश्वविद्यालय में अंग्रेज़ी पढ़ाने के साथ-साथ उपन्यास भी लिखता हूँ। હું યુનિવર્સિટીમાં અંગ્રેજી ભણાવવાની સાથે સાથે નવલકથાઓ પણ લખું છું.
(added, not in book) 彼は会社勤めのかたわら、ボランティア活動をしている。Kare wa kaisha-zutome no katawara, borantia katsudou o shite iru. Alongside his office job, he does volunteer work. वह ऑफ़िस की नौकरी के साथ-साथ स्वयंसेवा का काम भी करता है। તે ઑફિસની નોકરીની સાથે સાથે સ્વયંસેવાનું કામ પણ કરે છે.

がてら is the classic trap: it also joins two actions, but it means "while I'm at it, on the way" for a short one-time trip (散歩がてら買い物する). It attaches to nouns or V-stem, not V-辞書形. くわえて ("in addition") and ひきかえ ("in contrast") do not mean "on the side".

Compare the three suffixes in the options: まみれ = something sticky covers the surface. ずくめ = "nothing but" (黒ずくめ "all in black", 規則ずくめ). づくし = "a full range of" (料理づくし, usually positive). よごれ is a noun ("dirt"), not a suffix that takes 泥.

Typical usePattern AこそXが、Y: X is a minus that the speaker accepts, Y is the real point. Very common in reviews and evaluations.

NounN＋こそ〜がデザインこそ古いがdezain koso furui ga
Verb (stem)(added, not in book) V-ます stem＋こそしたが謝りこそしたがayamari koso shita ga

(book: 問題1 Q7) この服はデザインこそ古いが、着やすくてもう10年も着ている。Kono fuku wa dezain koso furui ga, kiyasukute mou juu-nen mo kite iru. This outfit's design may be old, but it's comfortable, and I've already worn it for ten years. इस कपड़े का डिज़ाइन भले पुराना है, पर पहनने में आरामदायक है, और मैं इसे दस साल से पहन रहा हूँ। આ કપડાંની ડિઝાઇન ભલે જૂની છે, પણ પહેરવામાં આરામદાયક છે, અને હું તેને દસ વર્ષથી પહેરું છું.
(added, not in book) この店は値段こそ高いが、味は確かだ。Kono mise wa nedan koso takai ga, aji wa tashika da. This restaurant's prices may be high, but the food is reliably good. इस दुकान के दाम भले ज़्यादा हैं, पर स्वाद पक्का अच्छा है। આ દુકાનના ભાવ ભલે વધારે છે, પણ સ્વાદ ચોક્કસ સારો છે.

Most learners know こそ as "precisely, exactly" (今度こそ). In 〜こそ〜が it works as a concession. だけ ("only the design is old") stresses a limit rather than conceding a weak point, and the test targets the こそ〜が frame, which the book key confirms. すら ("even") and ゆえ ("because") do not fit the contrast with 着やすくて.

This question tests keigo vocabulary, not a grammar pattern. The object メール decides it: you receive an email (拝受). 拝借 = borrow, 拝顔 = meet (see someone's face), 拝聴 = listen. All four are humble, so only the meaning tells them apart.

問題1 Q6

公園で子どもたちは全身泥（　　）になって遊んでいる。Kouen de kodomotachi wa zenshin doro (___) ni natte asonde iru.

GU: પાર્કમાં બાળકો માથાથી પગ સુધી કાદવથી ખરડાઈને રમી રહ્યાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

問題1 Q7

この服はデザイン（　　）古いが、着やすくてもう10年も着ている。Kono fuku wa dezain (___) furui ga, kiyasukute mou juu-nen mo kite iru.

Full sentence: この服はデザインこそ古いが、着やすくてもう10年も着ている。Kono fuku wa dezain koso furui ga, kiyasukute mou juu-nen mo kite iru.
EN: This outfit's design may be old, but it's comfortable, and I've already worn it for ten years.
HI: इस कपड़े का डिज़ाइन भले पुराना है, पर पहनने में आरामदायक है, और मैं इसे दस साल से पहन रहा हूँ।
GU: આ કપડાંની ડિઝાઇન ભલે જૂની છે, પણ પહેરવામાં આરામદાયક છે, અને હું તેને દસ વર્ષથી પહેરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1こそkoso(N) may be ~, but ✓भले ही ... हो, परભલે ... હોય, પણ
2だけdakeonlyकेवलફક્ત
3すらsuraevenतक, भीપણ, સુધ્ધાં
4ゆえyuebecause ofके कारणના કારણે

Why: The sentence admits one weak point (old design) and then praises the clothes (easy to wear, ten years). AこそXが、Y is exactly this concession frame. すら would be "even the design is old", stressing something extreme, not a concession. ゆえ ("because of the design, it is old") makes no sense.

問題1 Q8

昨日、メールを（　　）しました。Kinou, meeru o (___) shimashita.

EN: I received your email yesterday.
HI: मुझे कल आपका ईमेल प्राप्त हुआ।
GU: મને ગઈકાલે તમારો ઈમેલ મળ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1拝借haishakuborrowing (humble)उधार लेना (विनम्र)ઉધાર લેવું (નમ્ર)
2拝顔haiganmeeting someone (humble)मिलना (विनम्र)મળવું (નમ્ર)

問題1 Q9

貴社のご返答の（　　）では、弊社は契約解除を求めざるをえません。Kisha no go-hentou no (___) de wa, heisha wa keiyaku kaijo o motomezaru o emasen.

Full sentence: 貴社のご返答のいかんでは、弊社は契約解除を求めざるをえません。Kisha no go-hentou no ikan de wa, heisha wa keiyaku kaijo o motomezaru o emasen.
EN: Depending on your company's reply, we may have no choice but to request cancellation of the contract.
HI: आपकी कंपनी के जवाब पर निर्भर करते हुए, हमें अनुबंध रद्द करने की माँग करनी पड़ सकती है।
GU: આપની કંપનીના જવાબ પર આધાર રાખીને, અમારે કરાર રદ કરવાની માગણી કરવી પડી શકે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いかんikandepending on ✓... पर निर्भर... પર આધાર
2いよいよiyoiyoat last, finallyआख़िरकारઆખરે
3いずれizuresooner or laterकभी न कभीવહેલું-મોડું
4いかんせんikansenunfortunately, nothing can be doneअफ़सोस, कोई चारा नहींકમનસીબે, કોઈ ઉપાય નથી

Why: The frame N の（　）では needs a noun, and the meaning is "depending on your reply": いかん. いよいよ and いずれ are adverbs and cannot sit between の and では. いかんせん is also an adverb ("regrettably") that starts a clause.

問題1 Q10

結婚後、弟は以前（　　）、仕事に精を出している。Kekkon go, otouto wa izen (___), shigoto ni sei o dashite iru.

Full sentence: 結婚後、弟は以前にもまして、仕事に精を出している。Kekkon go, otouto wa izen ni mo mashite, shigoto ni sei o dashite iru.
EN: Since getting married, my younger brother has been working even harder than before.
HI: शादी के बाद मेरा छोटा भाई पहले से भी ज़्यादा मेहनत से काम कर रहा है।
GU: લગ્ન પછી મારો નાનો ભાઈ પહેલાં કરતાં પણ વધુ મહેનતથી કામ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1からずっとkara zuttoall along since... से लगातार... થી સતત
2にもましてni mo mashiteeven more than ✓... से भी ज़्यादा... કરતાં પણ વધુ
3であるもののde aru mono noalthough it isहालाँकि ... हैજોકે ... છે
4をよそにo yoso niignoring, regardless of... की परवाह किए बिना... ની પરવા કર્યા વગર

Why: 結婚後 contrasts "after marriage" with "before": he works harder than before. 以前にもまして is the set phrase. 以前からずっと would mean he has always worked hard, losing the change that 結婚後 points to. をよそに needs something to ignore (親の心配をよそに). であるものの is a concession and leaves 以前 hanging.

問題1 Q11

部長のご指導（　　）、今回の昇進はありえませんでした。Buchou no go-shidou (___), konkai no shoushin wa ariemasen deshita.

Full sentence: 部長のご指導なくしては、今回の昇進はありえませんでした。Buchou no go-shidou nakushite wa, konkai no shoushin wa ariemasen deshita.
EN: Without your guidance, sir, this promotion would never have happened.
HI: आपके मार्गदर्शन के बिना, यह पदोन्नति संभव नहीं होती।
GU: આપના માર્ગદર્શન વિના, આ બઢતી શક્ય ન બનત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なくしてはnakushite wawithout ✓... के बिना... વિના
2ないもののnai mono noalthough there is notहालाँकि ... नहीं हैજોકે ... નથી
3ないのでnai nodebecause there is notक्योंकि ... नहीं हैકારણ કે ... નથી

Why: The ending ありえませんでした ("was impossible") pairs with Nなくしては: "without N, Y could not have happened". The other three would need a verb or adjective before ない (ご指導がないので), and none gives the thank-you logic of the sentence.

問題1 Q12

私の部屋は散らかし（　　）なので、いつも母に怒られる。Watashi no heya wa chirakashi (___) na node, itsumo haha ni okorareru.

Full sentence: 私の部屋は散らかしっぱなしなので、いつも母に怒られる。Watashi no heya wa chirakashippanashi na node, itsumo haha ni okorareru.
EN: I always leave my room in a mess, so my mother is always scolding me.
HI: मैं अपना कमरा हमेशा बिखरा हुआ छोड़ देता हूँ, इसलिए माँ से डाँट पड़ती रहती है।
GU: હું મારો રૂમ હંમેશાં અસ્તવ્યસ્ત છોડી દઉં છું, એટલે મમ્મી હંમેશાં ગુસ્સે થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1っぺppe(dialect sentence ending)(बोली का प्रत्यय)(બોલીનો પ્રત્યય)
2っぷりppurimanner, way of doingकरने का ढंगકરવાની ઢબ
3っぱなしppanashileft (undone) as it is ✓वैसे ही छोड़ा हुआએમ જ છોડેલું
4っぽいppoi-ish, tends to... जैसा, ... की आदत... જેવું, ... ની ટેવ

Why: 散らかしたまま片づけない: "messed up and left that way". That is V-stem＋っぱなし, and it takes な before ので (〜っぱなしなので). っぽい is an い-adjective, so 散らかしっぽいので would need no な, and the meaning "tends to scatter" is odd. っぷり (飲みっぷり) describes a style of doing something. っぺ is not a suffix here.

問題1 Q13

日本の夏（　　）蒸し暑くてたまらない。Nihon no natsu (___) mushiatsukute tamaranai.

Full sentence: 日本の夏ときたら蒸し暑くてたまらない。Nihon no natsu to kitara mushiatsukute tamaranai.
EN: Japanese summers are just unbearably hot and humid.
HI: जापान की गर्मी की तो बात ही मत करो, इतनी उमस है कि सहन नहीं होती।
GU: જાપાનના ઉનાળાની તો વાત જ ન કરો, એટલો બફારો છે કે સહન નથી થતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にすればni surebafrom the viewpoint of... की नज़र से... ની નજરે
2によるとni yoru toaccording to... के अनुसार... મુજબ
3とすればto surebaif we supposeअगर मान लेंજો ધારીએ
4ときたらto kitaraas for (complaint) ✓... की तो बात ही मत करो... ની તો વાત જ ન કરો

Why: 〜てたまらない is a complaint, and ときたら introduces a topic the speaker wants to complain about. によると needs a source (天気予報によると). にすれば needs a person whose view it is (子どもにすれば). とすれば needs a clause or だ (夏だとすれば).

問題1 Q14

Full sentence: 携帯電話で話しながら自転車をこぐなんて危険きわまりない行為だ。Keitai denwa de hanashinagara jitensha o kogu nante kiken kiwamari nai koui da.
EN: Riding a bicycle while talking on a mobile phone is extremely dangerous.
HI: मोबाइल पर बात करते हुए साइकिल चलाना बेहद ख़तरनाक काम है।
GU: મોબાઇલ પર વાત કરતાં કરતાં સાઇકલ ચલાવવી અત્યંત જોખમી કામ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1きわだつkiwadatsuto stand outअलग दिखनाઅલગ તરી આવવું
2きわまりないkiwamari naiextremely ✓बेहदઅત્યંત
3きわどいkiwadoirisky, close (call)बाल-बाल, जोखिम भराજોખમી, માંડ માંડ
4きわまらないkiwamaranai(not used)प्रयोग नहीं होताવપરાતું નથી
