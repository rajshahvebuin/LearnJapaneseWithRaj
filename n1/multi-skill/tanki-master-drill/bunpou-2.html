【2-Q1】 Nずくめ

ReadingN zukume
Meaning (EN)"nothing but N, full of N" (everything around is N)

Meaning (GU)ચારે બાજુ બસ N જ N

RegisterNeutral
Typical useFixed partners: いいことずくめ, 黒ずくめ, 規則ずくめ, ごちそうずくめ. Can be positive or negative.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN＋ずくめいいことずくめii koto zukume
Verb / adjectivesnot used—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q1) 夏休みの旅行は嫌なことが一つもなく、いいことずくめだった。Natsuyasumi no ryokou wa iya na koto ga hitotsu mo naku, ii koto zukume datta. The summer trip had nothing bad at all — it was nothing but good things. गर्मी की छुट्टियों की यात्रा में एक भी बुरी बात नहीं हुई, सब कुछ अच्छा ही अच्छा था। ઉનાળાની રજાની મુસાફરીમાં એક પણ ખરાબ વાત ન થઈ, બધું સારું જ સારું હતું.
(added, not in book) 彼はいつも黒ずくめの服を着ている。Kare wa itsumo kurozukume no fuku o kite iru. He always dresses all in black. वह हमेशा सिर से पाँव तक काले कपड़े पहनता है। તે હંમેશાં માથાથી પગ સુધી કાળાં કપડાં પહેરે છે.

Also tested in 問題1 Q6 as a distractor (ずくめ vs まみれ). ずくめ does not mean "covered with dirt"; it means the whole of something is one kind of thing.

【2-Q2】 AはおろかBも

硬 formal/written
ReadingA wa oroka B mo
Meaning (EN)"not to mention A, (not even) B" — A is the easier thing, B is the more basic or extreme one
Meaning (HI)A तो दूर की बात, B भी (नहीं)
Meaning (GU)A તો દૂરની વાત, B પણ (નહીં)
Connection (接続)N＋はおろか＋N＋も／さえ／まで
RegisterSlightly formal; usually with a negative or critical tone
Typical useUsually negative: 漢字はおろか、ひらがなも書けない. B is the item you would least expect to be missing.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN＋はおろか予習はおろかyoshuu wa oroka
Verb(added, not in book) V-辞書形＋こと＋はおろか歩くことはおろかaruku koto wa oroka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q2) 予習はおろか宿題もしていないとは、授業に出席する資格がない。Yoshuu wa oroka shukudai mo shite inai to wa, jugyou ni shusseki suru shikaku ga nai. Not even doing your homework, let alone preparing for class — you have no right to attend the lesson. पहले से तैयारी तो दूर, होमवर्क तक नहीं किया — तुम्हें कक्षा में बैठने का हक़ नहीं है। પહેલેથી તૈયારી તો દૂર, હોમવર્ક સુધ્ધાં નથી કર્યું — તને વર્ગમાં બેસવાનો હક નથી.
(added, not in book) 足をけがして、走ることはおろか歩くこともできない。Ashi o kega shite, hashiru koto wa oroka aruku koto mo dekinai. I hurt my leg, so I can't even walk, never mind run. पैर में चोट लगी है, दौड़ना तो दूर, चल भी नहीं सकता। પગમાં ઈજા થઈ છે, દોડવું તો દૂર, ચાલી પણ નથી શકતો.

The order is "big thing はおろか, basic thing も". Homework is more basic than preparation, so 宿題も is B. The とは at the end ("to think that…!") shows the speaker's shock.

【2-Q3】 V(よ)うがVまいが

neutral register
ReadingV-(y)ou ga V-mai ga
Meaning (EN)"whether (someone) does V or not, (it makes no difference)"
Meaning (HI)चाहे V करे या न करे
Meaning (GU)ભલે V કરે કે ન કરે
Connection (接続)V-意向形＋が＋V-辞書形（する→する／し）＋まいが
RegisterNeutral to slightly formal
Typical useThe second clause states the speaker's firm decision or an unchanging fact. Same as 〜ても〜なくても. Variant: 〜(よ)うと〜まいと.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (する)しようが＋するまいが賛成しようがするまいがsansei shiyou ga suru mai ga
Verb (group 1)(added, not in book) V-意向形＋が＋V-辞書形＋まいが行こうが行くまいがikou ga iku mai ga
Verb (group 2)(added, not in book) V-意向形＋が＋V-stem／辞書形＋まいが食べようが食べまいがtabeyou ga tabemai ga
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q3) 両親が賛成しようがするまいが、私は進学することに決めた。Ryoushin ga sansei shiyou ga suru mai ga, watashi wa shingaku suru koto ni kimeta. Whether my parents agree or not, I have decided to go on to further study. माता-पिता सहमत हों या न हों, मैंने आगे पढ़ने का फ़ैसला कर लिया है। માતા-પિતા સહમત થાય કે ન થાય, મેં આગળ ભણવાનું નક્કી કરી લીધું છે.
(added, not in book) 雨が降ろうが降るまいが、試合は行われる。Ame ga furou ga furu mai ga, shiai wa okonawareru. The match will be held whether it rains or not. बारिश हो या न हो, मैच होगा। વરસાદ પડે કે ન પડે, મેચ થશે.

The noun 賛成 must come right before しようが (賛成しようが), and the pair しようが／するまいが always stays together in that order: positive first, then negative.

neutral register
Readingkono ue nai
Meaning (EN)"the greatest possible, nothing could be more ~"
Meaning (HI)इससे बढ़कर कुछ नहीं, सबसे ज़्यादा

RegisterFormal; speeches and thank-you messages
Typical useこの上ない幸せ／喜び／光栄. As an adverb: この上なく美しい.
Word typeFormationExample
Before a nounこの上ない＋Nこの上ない幸せkono ue nai shiawase
Adverb(added, not in book) この上なく＋adj／Vこの上なくうれしいkono ue naku ureshii
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q4) 皆様のおかげで、留学生活を無事に終えられたことはこの上ない幸せでした。Minasama no okage de, ryuugaku seikatsu o buji ni oerareta koto wa kono ue nai shiawase deshita. Thanks to all of you, being able to finish my study abroad safely was the greatest happiness. आप सबकी बदौलत विदेश में पढ़ाई सकुशल पूरी कर पाना मेरे लिए सबसे बड़ी ख़ुशी थी। આપ સૌના કારણે વિદેશમાં અભ્યાસ સહીસલામત પૂરો કરી શક્યો, એ મારા માટે સૌથી મોટી ખુશી હતી.
(added, not in book) このような賞をいただき、この上ない光栄です。Kono you na shou o itadaki, kono ue nai kouei desu. Receiving such an award is the greatest honour. ऐसा पुरस्कार पाना मेरे लिए सबसे बड़ा सम्मान है। આવો પુરસ્કાર મળવો એ મારા માટે સૌથી મોટું સન્માન છે.

Related N1 forms with the same meaning: 〜極まりない (often negative), 〜の極み (光栄の極み), 〜といったらない. この上ない is mostly positive.

【2-Q5】 Nのごとく

硬 formal/written
ReadingN no gotoku
Meaning (EN)"like N, as if N" (written form of のように)
Meaning (HI)N की तरह, मानो N हो
Meaning (GU)N ની જેમ, જાણે N હોય
Connection (接続)N の＋ごとく ／ V-る・V-た＋（が）ごとく ／ N の＋ごとき＋N
Register硬 — written, literary
Typical useごとく is adverbial (〜のごとく暑い), ごとき modifies a noun (夢のごとき日々), ごとし ends a sentence.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN の＋ごとく夏のごとくnatsu no gotoku
Verb(added, not in book) V-る／V-た＋（が）ごとく見たごとくmita gotoku
Before a noun(added, not in book) N の＋ごとき＋N夢のごとき日々yume no gotoki hibi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q5) 異常気象のせいか、春なのに夏のごとく暑い日々が続いている。Ijou kishou no sei ka, haru na no ni natsu no gotoku atsui hibi ga tsuzuite iru. Perhaps because of the strange weather, it has been hot like summer day after day, even though it is spring. शायद असामान्य मौसम की वजह से, वसंत होते हुए भी गर्मियों जैसे गरम दिन चल रहे हैं। કદાચ અસામાન્ય હવામાનને કારણે, વસંત હોવા છતાં ઉનાળા જેવા ગરમ દિવસો ચાલે છે.
(added, not in book) 時は矢のごとく過ぎていく。Toki wa ya no gotoku sugite iku. Time flies like an arrow. समय तीर की तरह बीत जाता है। સમય તીરની જેમ વીતી જાય છે.

The tile 夏 has no の, but のごとく includes の, so 夏＋のごとく is complete. なのに must follow a noun (春なのに), so 春 comes first.

neutral register
Readingtada ~ tame no N
Meaning (EN)"(an act) only for the purpose of ~" — ただ limits the purpose to just one thing
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ~ के लिए (किया जाने वाला)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ~ માટે (કરાતું)
Connection (接続)ただ＋V-辞書形＋ため（の N）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useただ (= 単に, "merely") goes at the front of the phrase it limits. Often in a critical or sad comment: ただ金を稼ぐための仕事.
Word typeFormationExample
Verbただ＋V-辞書形＋ためただ稼ぐためtada kasegu tame
Noun(added, not in book) ただ＋N の＋ためただ金のためtada kane no tame
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q6) 田中さんにとって仕事とは、ただ生活費を稼ぐための行為であった。Tanaka-san ni totte shigoto to wa, tada seikatsuhi o kasegu tame no koui de atta. For Mr Tanaka, work was simply an act for earning his living costs. तनाका-सान के लिए काम बस रोज़ी-रोटी कमाने का ज़रिया था। તનાકા-સાન માટે કામ ફક્ત રોજીરોટી કમાવાનું સાધન હતું.
(added, not in book) 彼はただ人を笑わせるために生きているようだ。Kare wa tada hito o warawaseru tame ni ikite iru you da. He seems to live just to make people laugh. लगता है वह बस लोगों को हँसाने के लिए जीता है। લાગે છે કે તે બસ લોકોને હસાવવા માટે જીવે છે.

Remember the frame N にとって N とは〜である ("for X, Y is…"), a common definition pattern at N1. ただ comes before the object 生活費を, not right before ため.

【2-Q7】 Nを記念して

neutral register
ReadingN o kinen shite
Meaning (EN)"to commemorate N, in honour of N"
Meaning (HI)N की याद में, N के उपलक्ष्य में
Meaning (GU)N ની યાદમાં, N ના ઉપલક્ષ્યમાં
Connection (接続)N を＋記念して（＋V-た N）
RegisterNeutral, official
Typical useUsed for events or objects made for an anniversary: 〜周年を記念して, 創立を記念して. Before a noun: 〜を記念した N.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN を＋記念して100周年を記念してhyaku shuunen o kinen shite
Before a noun(added, not in book) N を＋記念した＋N創立を記念した式典souritsu o kinen shita shikiten
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題2 Q7) これは創立100周年を記念して建てられた銅像です。Kore wa souritsu hyaku shuunen o kinen shite taterareta douzou desu. This is a bronze statue built to mark the 100th anniversary of the founding. यह स्थापना की 100वीं वर्षगाँठ के उपलक्ष्य में बनाई गई काँसे की मूर्ति है। આ સ્થાપનાની 100મી વર્ષગાંઠના ઉપલક્ષ્યમાં બનાવાયેલી કાંસાની પ્રતિમા છે.
(added, not in book) 結婚10周年を記念して旅行に行った。Kekkon juu shuunen o kinen shite ryokou ni itta. We went on a trip to celebrate our 10th wedding anniversary. शादी की 10वीं सालगिरह मनाने के लिए हम यात्रा पर गए। લગ્નની 10મી વર્ષગાંઠ ઊજવવા અમે પ્રવાસે ગયા.

This is a noun-modifying chain: [創立100周年を記念して建てられた] 銅像. The verb right before 銅像 must be the passive 建てられた ("was built").

【3-[2]】 〜のである（説明・強調）

neutral register
Reading~ no de aru
Meaning (EN)"it is that ~, the fact is ~" — explains or stresses a point, written style of 〜のだ／〜んです
Meaning (HI)बात यह है कि ~ (समझाना / ज़ोर देना)
Meaning (GU)વાત એમ છે કે ~ (સમજાવવું / ભાર મૂકવો)
Connection (接続)普通形（な-adj・N な）＋のである
RegisterWritten (essays); spoken: 〜んだ
Typical useEssay writers use it to give the conclusion or the real explanation of something already described. In a general statement (habit, truth) it takes the dictionary form: 後悔するのである.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-普通形＋のである後悔するのであるkoukai suru no de aru
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj＋のである遅いのであるosoi no de aru
な-adjective / nounな-adj／N＋なのである傑作なのであるkessaku na no de aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題3 [2]) 「しまった。もっと年相応に振る舞うべきだった」と、後悔するのである。"Shimatta. Motto toshisouou ni furumau beki datta" to, koukai suru no de aru. People regret it, thinking "Oh no — I should have acted more my age." लोग पछताते हैं: "अरे! मुझे अपनी उम्र के हिसाब से बर्ताव करना चाहिए था।" લોકો પસ્તાય છે: "અરે! મારે મારી ઉંમર પ્રમાણે વર્તવું જોઈતું હતું."
(added, not in book) 彼が怒ったのは、約束を破られたからなのである。Kare ga okotta no wa, yakusoku o yaburareta kara na no de aru. The reason he got angry is that his promise was broken. वह इसलिए नाराज़ हुआ क्योंकि उससे किया वादा तोड़ा गया। તે એટલા માટે ગુસ્સે થયો કારણ કે તેને આપેલું વચન તોડાયું.

The passage talks about what always happens to people (いつでも…), so the verb is non-past: 後悔するのである, not 後悔したのである. べきである and つもりである would give advice or an intention, not a description.

neutral register
Readingnaze ~ no darou ka

Meaning (HI)ऐसा क्यों होता है कि ~?
Meaning (GU)આવું કેમ થાય છે કે ~?
Connection (接続)なぜ＋普通形＋のだろうか
RegisterWritten; essays and explanations
Typical useA question to the reader; the answer follows with 〜からだ. In the passage: なぜ…のだろうか → …からだ。…からだ。

Verbなぜ＋V-普通形＋のだろうかなぜ遅れてしまうのだろうかnaze okurete shimau no darou ka
Adjective(added, not in book) なぜ＋adj＋のだろうかなぜ難しいのだろうかnaze muzukashii no darou ka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
(book: 問題3 [3]) なぜ、年相応に振る舞うのが遅れてしまうのだろうか。Naze, toshisouou ni furumau no ga okurete shimau no darou ka. Why is it that we are late in acting our age? ऐसा क्यों होता है कि उम्र के हिसाब से बर्ताव करने में हम पीछे रह जाते हैं? આવું કેમ થાય છે કે ઉંમર પ્રમાણે વર્તવામાં આપણે મોડા પડીએ છીએ?

When a blank is at the start of a sentence ending in 〜のだろうか, look for a question word. けれども, また and さらに join statements; they cannot open a question.

Key word: 年相応 (toshisouou) — "suitable for one's age" (उम्र के अनुरूप / ઉંમરને અનુરૂપ). 傑作 (kessaku) is used ironically: "comical, quite a joke".

問題3 ［1］

しかし、現実には年相応の振る舞いは、かならず［１］から傑作なのである。Shikashi, genjitsu ni wa toshisouou no furumai wa, kanarazu [1] kara kessaku na no de aru.

EN: But in reality, behaviour that fits one's age always comes late, which is why it is so comical.
HI: लेकिन असल में उम्र के हिसाब का बर्ताव हमेशा देर से आता है, इसलिए यह मज़ेदार (हास्यास्पद) है।
GU: પણ વાસ્તવમાં ઉંમર પ્રમાણેનું વર્તન હંમેશાં મોડું આવે છે, એટલે તે મજેદાર (હાસ્યાસ્પદ) છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1人それぞれ異なるhito sorezore kotonarudiffers from person to personहर व्यक्ति में अलग होता हैદરેક વ્યક્તિમાં અલગ હોય છે
2同時期に来るdoujiki ni kurucomes at the same timeएक ही समय पर आता हैએક જ સમયે આવે છે
3早くなってしまうhayaku natte shimauends up coming earlyजल्दी आ जाता हैવહેલું આવી જાય છે
4遅れてしまうokurete shimauends up coming late ✓देर से आता हैમોડું આવે છે

Why: The next paragraph says 40-year-old behaviour is never seen at 35 and people regret it only years after 40, and the passage later repeats 年相応の振る舞いが遅れてしまう. So the blank is 遅れてしまう. かならず ("always") rules out 1 (it does not vary by person). 3 is the opposite. 傑作 here is ironic: "comical, a joke".

問題3 ［2］

いつでも四十歳を過ぎて何年かしてから、「しまった。もっと年相応に振る舞うべきだった」と、［２］。Itsu demo yonjussai o sugite nannen ka shite kara, "Shimatta. Motto toshisouou ni furumau beki datta" to, [2].

EN: It is always several years after turning forty that people regret it: "Oh no, I should have acted my age."
HI: हमेशा चालीस पार करने के कुछ साल बाद ही इंसान पछताता है: "अरे! मुझे उम्र के हिसाब से बर्ताव करना चाहिए था।"
GU: હંમેશાં ચાળીસ વટાવ્યા પછી થોડાં વર્ષે જ માણસ પસ્તાય છે: "અરે! મારે ઉંમર પ્રમાણે વર્તવું જોઈતું હતું."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1後悔したのであるkoukai shita no de aru(they) regrettedपछताया थाપસ્તાયો હતો
2後悔するべきであるkoukai suru beki de aru(they) should regretपछताना चाहिएપસ્તાવું જોઈએ
3後悔するのであるkoukai suru no de aru(they) regret (always) ✓पछताता हैપસ્તાય છે
4後悔するつもりであるkoukai suru tsumori de aru(they) intend to regretपछताने का इरादा हैપસ્તાવાનો ઇરાદો છે

Why: いつでも ("always") shows a general truth, so the verb is non-past with explanatory のである: 後悔するのである. 1 is past, for one event. 2 gives advice, which the writer is not doing here. 4 (intend to regret) is illogical.

問題3 ［3］

［３］、年相応に振る舞うのが遅れてしまうのだろうか。[3], toshisouou ni furumau no ga okurete shimau no darou ka.

EN: Why is it that we are late in acting our age?
HI: ऐसा क्यों होता है कि उम्र के हिसाब से बर्ताव करने में हम देर कर देते हैं?
GU: આવું કેમ થાય છે કે ઉંમર પ્રમાણે વર્તવામાં આપણે મોડા પડીએ છીએ?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1けれどもkeredomohoweverलेकिनપરંતુ
2またmataalso, againऔर भी, फिर सेવળી, ફરીથી
3なぜnazewhy ✓क्योंકેમ
4さらにsara nifurthermoreइसके अलावाવધુમાં

Why: The sentence ends with 〜のだろうか, a question, and the following sentences answer it with 〜からだ ("it is because…") twice. So the question word なぜ is needed. The other three are connectors for statements.

問題3 ［4］

人間は日々年々、自分は何歳であると自覚して年をとっていく［４］。Ningen wa hibi toshidoshi, jibun wa nansai de aru to jikaku shite toshi o totte iku [4].

EN: It is not the case that people grow older day by day, year by year, always aware of how old they are.
HI: ऐसा नहीं है कि इंसान हर दिन, हर साल यह जानते हुए बूढ़ा होता है कि वह कितने साल का है।
GU: એવું નથી કે માણસ દરરોજ, દર વર્ષે એ જાણીને મોટો થાય છે કે પોતે કેટલા વર્ષનો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1わけであるwake de aruthat is why, so it isइसीलिए तोએટલે જ તો
2わけではないwake de wa naiit is not the case that ✓ऐसा नहीं है किએવું નથી કે
3わけないことだwake nai koto dait is an easy thingयह आसान बात हैઆ સહેલી વાત છે
4わけがあるwake ga aruthere is a reasonइसकी वजह हैતેનું કારણ છે

Why: The next sentence says most people think of themselves as younger than they really are. That only follows if this sentence denies that people track their age: わけではない. わけである would agree with the idea. わけない means "easy", and わけがある means "there is a reason", neither of which fits.

問題3 ［5］

しかし、年をとり年齢にふさわしい行動や考えができないと、［５-a］、［５-b］を晒すことになる。Shikashi, toshi o tori nenrei ni fusawashii koudou ya kangae ga dekinai to, [5-a], [5-b] o sarasu koto ni naru.

EN: But if, as you grow older, you cannot act and think in a way that fits your age, you end up exposing an ugly figure and an ugly attitude.
HI: लेकिन अगर उम्र बढ़ने पर भी उसके अनुरूप व्यवहार और सोच न हो, तो इंसान अपना भद्दा रूप और भद्दा रवैया उजागर कर बैठता है।
GU: પણ જો ઉંમર વધવા છતાં તેને અનુરૂપ વર્તન અને વિચાર ન આવે, તો માણસ પોતાનું ભદ્દું રૂપ અને ભદ્દું વલણ ઉઘાડું પાડી બેસે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1a 美しい姿 ／ b 美しい態度a utsukushii sugata / b utsukushii taidoa beautiful figure / a beautiful attitudeसुंदर रूप / सुंदर रवैयाસુંદર રૂપ / સુંદર વલણ
2a 美しい姿 ／ b 醜い態度a utsukushii sugata / b minikui taidoa beautiful figure / an ugly attitudeसुंदर रूप / भद्दा रवैयाસુંદર રૂપ / ભદ્દું વલણ
3a 醜い姿 ／ b 醜い態度a minikui sugata / b minikui taidoan ugly figure / an ugly attitude ✓भद्दा रूप / भद्दा रवैयाભદ્દું રૂપ / ભદ્દું વલણ
4a 醜い姿 ／ b 美しい態度a minikui sugata / b utsukushii taidoan ugly figure / a beautiful attitudeभद्दा रूप / सुंदर रवैयाભદ્દું રૂપ / સુંદર વલણ

Why: The condition 〜ができないと is negative, so the result is negative too, and 晒す ("expose to view") usually takes something shameful: 恥を晒す, 醜態を晒す. Both a and b are listed side by side (、) as the same kind of thing, so both must be 醜い. Mixed pairs (2, 4) break the parallel; 1 is the opposite meaning.
