問題3 言い換え類義 — choose the word closest in meaning

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word from 1–4.   HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे क़रीब शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें।   GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો શબ્દ 1–4 માંથી પસંદ કરો.

問題3 Q1

そんなに焦っていては、うまくいくものもいかなくなってしまう。Sonna ni asette ite wa, umaku iku mono mo ikanaku natte shimau.

With the answer: そんなに慌てていては、うまくいくものもいかなくなってしまう。Sonna ni awatete ite wa, umaku iku mono mo ikanaku natte shimau.
EN: If you are in such a rush, even things that would go well will go wrong.
HI: अगर तुम इतनी हड़बड़ी में रहोगे, तो जो काम ठीक हो सकता था, वह भी बिगड़ जाएगा।
GU: જો તું આટલી ઉતાવળમાં રહીશ, તો જે કામ સારું થઈ શકે તે પણ બગડી જશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1悩んでnayandeworrying, troubledपरेशान होकरમૂંઝાઈને
2慌ててawatetein a hurry, flustered ✓हड़बड़ाकरઉતાવળમાં, ગભરાઈને
3苦しんでkurushindesufferingपीड़ा सहकरપીડાઈને
4怒ってokottegetting angryग़ुस्सा होकरગુસ્સે થઈને

Why: 焦る means you are impatient and rush because time is short or things are not going well. 慌てる is the closest: acting in a hurry and losing your calm. 悩む and 苦しむ are about inner worry or pain, not speed. 怒る is anger. The second half (うまくいくものもいかなくなる) is the classic result of rushing.

問題3 Q2

買うつもりはなかったが、子どもにせがまれて、つい買ってしまった。Kau tsumori wa nakatta ga, kodomo ni segamarete, tsui katte shimatta.

With the answer: 買うつもりはなかったが、子どもにねだられて、つい買ってしまった。Kau tsumori wa nakatta ga, kodomo ni nedararete, tsui katte shimatta.
EN: I had no plan to buy it, but my child kept begging, and I ended up buying it.
HI: ख़रीदने का इरादा नहीं था, पर बच्चे ने ज़िद करके माँगा, तो आख़िर ख़रीद ही लिया।
GU: ખરીદવાનો ઇરાદો નહોતો, પણ બાળકે જીદ કરીને માગ્યું, એટલે આખરે ખરીદી જ લીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1せかされてsekasaretebeing hurried (急かされて)जल्दी करने को कहे जाकरઉતાવળ કરાવાઈને
2もてなされてmotenasaretebeing entertained as a guestमेहमाननवाज़ी पाकरમહેમાનગતિ પામીને
3嘆かれてnagekaretebeing lamented overकिसी के दुखी होने परકોઈના દુઃખી થવાથી
4ねだられてnedararetebeing begged ✓ज़िद करके माँगे जाने परજીદ કરીને માગવામાં આવતાં

Why: せがむ and ねだる both mean asking again and again for something you want, typically a child to a parent. Both are used here in the passive (〜られて): "being begged by the child". せかす (急かす) also sounds similar and also pressures someone, but it means "make someone hurry", not "ask for a thing". もてなす (welcome a guest) and 嘆く (grieve) do not fit.

問題3 Q3

新しい駅ビルは、2年後に完成の見込みだ。Atarashii eki biru wa, ni-nen go ni kansei no mikomi da.

With the answer: 新しい駅ビルは、2年後に完成の見通しだ。Atarashii eki biru wa, ni-nen go ni kansei no mitooshi da.
EN: The new station building is expected to be completed in two years.
HI: नई स्टेशन बिल्डिंग के दो साल बाद पूरी होने की संभावना है।
GU: નવી સ્ટેશન બિલ્ડિંગ બે વર્ષ પછી પૂરી થવાની શક્યતા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見極めmikiwamecareful judgement, seeing throughपरख, ठीक से पहचाननाપારખ, બરાબર ઓળખવું
2見通しmitooshioutlook, prospect ✓अनुमान, संभावनाઅંદાજ, શક્યતા
3見積もりmitsumoriestimate (of cost)लागत का अनुमान (कोटेशन)ખર્ચનો અંદાજ (ક્વોટેશન)
4見晴らしmiharashiview (from a high place)नज़ाराદૃશ્ય

Why: 〜の見込みだ and 〜の見通しだ both mean "it is expected that ~", as in news reports about schedules. All four options start with 見, so you must know each one. 見積もり is a money estimate (見積書 "quotation"). 見極め is judging something carefully. 見晴らし is a scenic view (見晴らしがいい).

問題3 Q4

定年後は田舎に戻って、のどかな生活を送りたいと思っていた。Teinen go wa inaka ni modotte, nodoka na seikatsu o okuritai to omotte ita.

With the answer: 定年後は田舎に戻って、穏やかな生活を送りたいと思っていた。Teinen go wa inaka ni modotte, odayaka na seikatsu o okuritai to omotte ita.
EN: I was thinking that after retirement I would go back to the countryside and live a calm, peaceful life.
HI: मैं सोच रहा था कि रिटायरमेंट के बाद गाँव लौटकर शांत, सुकून-भरी ज़िंदगी बिताऊँगा।
GU: હું વિચારતો હતો કે નિવૃત્તિ પછી ગામડે પાછો જઈને શાંત, નિરાંતવાળું જીવન જીવીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1さわやかなsawayaka nafresh, refreshingताज़गी-भराતાજગીભર્યું
2健康的なkenkouteki nahealthyसेहतमंदતંદુરસ્ત
3穏やかなodayaka nacalm, gentle ✓शांत, सौम्यશાંત, સૌમ્ય
4自由なjiyuu nafreeआज़ादસ્વતંત્ર

Why: のどか describes a slow, quiet, peaceful scene or life with no stress, often in the countryside. 穏やか ("calm, without trouble") is the nearest. A country life may also be 健康的 or 自由, but those are different ideas, not paraphrases. さわやか is about a fresh feeling (weather, a smile).

問題3 Q5

フロントで働く人は、さまざまな問題にすぐ対応できる人物が好ましいと思う。Furonto de hataraku hito wa, samazama na mondai ni sugu taiou dekiru jinbutsu ga konomashii to omou.

With the answer: フロントで働く人は、さまざまな問題にすぐ対応できる人物が望ましいと思う。Furonto de hataraku hito wa, samazama na mondai ni sugu taiou dekiru jinbutsu ga nozomashii to omou.
EN: For people working at the front desk, I think someone who can deal with all kinds of problems at once is desirable.
HI: मुझे लगता है कि फ़्रंट डेस्क पर काम करने के लिए ऐसा व्यक्ति बेहतर है जो तरह-तरह की समस्याएँ तुरंत सँभाल सके।
GU: મને લાગે છે કે ફ્રન્ટ ડેસ્ક પર કામ કરવા માટે એવી વ્યક્તિ ઇચ્છનીય છે જે જાતજાતની સમસ્યાઓ તરત સંભાળી શકે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1喜ばしいyorokobashiijoyful, happy (news)ख़ुशी की बातઆનંદની વાત
2望ましいnozomashiidesirable ✓वांछनीयઇચ્છનીય
3素晴らしいsubarashiiwonderfulशानदारઅદ્ભુત
4目覚ましいmezamashiiremarkable, striking (progress)उल्लेखनीय, ज़बरदस्तનોંધપાત્ર, ઝળહળતું

Why: Here 好ましい does not mean "likeable". It means "the kind that should be chosen", which is exactly 望ましい. The sentence is a condition for a job, not praise of a real person, so 素晴らしい does not paraphrase it. 喜ばしい is for happy events (喜ばしい知らせ). 目覚ましい is for fast progress (目覚ましい発展).

問題3 Q6

式典会場の周りは、物々しい警戒態勢が敷かれている。Shikiten kaijou no mawari wa, monomonoshii keikai taisei ga shikarete iru.

With the answer: 式典会場の周りは、厳重な警戒態勢が敷かれている。Shikiten kaijou no mawari wa, genjuu na keikai taisei ga shikarete iru.
EN: Very tight security has been set up around the ceremony venue.
HI: समारोह स्थल के चारों ओर कड़ी सुरक्षा व्यवस्था लगाई गई है।
GU: સમારોહ સ્થળની આસપાસ કડક સુરક્ષા વ્યવસ્થા ગોઠવવામાં આવી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1勇ましいisamashiibrave, gallantबहादुरબહાદુર
2ずうずうしいzuuzuushiishameless, cheekyबेशर्म, ढीठનફ્ફટ, બેશરમ
3厳重なgenjuu nastrict, tight ✓कड़ा, सख़्तકડક, સખત
4物騒なbussou nadangerous, unsafe (area, times)ख़तरनाक, असुरक्षितજોખમી, અસલામત

Why: 物々しい describes security or equipment that looks heavy and serious, almost too much. With 警戒／警備, the paraphrase is 厳重な ("strict, tight"). The trap is 物騒な: it shares the 物 kanji and is about danger, but it means the place is unsafe, not that the guarding is strong. 勇ましい and ずうずうしい describe people.

問題3 Q7

休みの日は、もっぱら子どもの相手をして過ごしています。Yasumi no hi wa, moppara kodomo no aite o shite sugoshite imasu.

With the answer: 休みの日は、主に子どもの相手をして過ごしています。Yasumi no hi wa, omo ni kodomo no aite o shite sugoshite imasu.

GU: રજાના દિવસે હું મોટે ભાગે બાળકો સાથે સમય વિતાવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しきりにshikiri nirepeatedly, frequentlyबार-बारવારંવાર
2主にomo nimainly ✓मुख्य रूप सेમુખ્યત્વે

問題3 Q9

経済的な発展に伴い、国民の生活も徐々に豊かになった。Keizaiteki na hatten ni tomonai, kokumin no seikatsu mo jojo ni yutaka ni natta.

4祖父は懐かしそうにつくづくと昔の話を語った。Sofu wa natsukashisou ni tsukuzuku to mukashi no hanashi o katatta.Grandfather told old stories ~, full of nostalgia.दादाजी ने यादों में खोकर ~ पुरानी बातें सुनाईं।દાદાએ યાદોમાં ખોવાઈને ~ જૂની વાતો કહી.

Why: つくづく goes with verbs of feeling or realising: つくづく思う／感じる／嫌になる／感心する. It shows a feeling that comes from deep inside after thinking it over. 1 needs 次々と ("one after another"). 2 needs めきめき ("remarkably"): めきめき腕が上がる is a set phrase. 4 needs しみじみと ("with deep feeling"), which is close to つくづく but is the word used with 語る and 話す.

問題4 Q9

こってりkotteri — rich, heavy (taste); thoroughly, severely (scolded)

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1カンニングが見つかり、先生にこってりと油を絞られた。Kanningu ga mitsukari, sensei ni kotteri to abura o shiborareta.I was caught cheating and got a real telling-off from the teacher. ✓नकल करते पकड़ा गया, और टीचर से ख़ूब डाँट पड़ी।ચોરી કરતાં પકડાયો, અને શિક્ષકે ખૂબ ઠપકો આપ્યો.
2英才教育だと言い、子どもの頃からこってりピアノを練習させられた。Eisai kyouiku da to ii, kodomo no koro kara kotteri piano o renshuu saserareta.They called it gifted education and made me practise piano ~ from childhood.इसे ख़ास शिक्षा बताकर बचपन से मुझसे ~ पियानो का अभ्यास कराया गया।તેને ખાસ શિક્ષણ કહીને નાનપણથી મારી પાસે ~ પિયાનોનો અભ્યાસ કરાવાયો.
3この料理は脂肪が少なくこってりしていて、さわやかだ。Kono ryouri wa shibou ga sukunaku kotteri shite ite, sawayaka da.This dish is low in fat, ~ and refreshing.इस व्यंजन में चर्बी कम है, यह ~ और ताज़गी-भरा है।આ વાનગીમાં ચરબી ઓછી છે, તે ~ અને તાજગીભરી છે.
4彼は相手の弱みをこってりとついてくる。Kare wa aite no yowami o kotteri to tsuite kuru.He attacks the other person's weak points ~.वह सामने वाले की कमज़ोरी पर ~ वार करता है।તે સામેવાળાની નબળાઈ પર ~ ઘા કરે છે.

Why: こってり has two uses: a heavy, rich taste (こってりしたラーメン) and, figuratively, being scolded long and hard: こってり油を絞られる／しぼられる／叱られる. 1 is that idiom. 2 needs みっちり ("intensively, thoroughly", for training or practice). 3 is a contradiction: low fat and refreshing is あっさり, the opposite of こってり. 4 needs 容赦なく or しつこく ("mercilessly, persistently"); 弱みを突く does not take こってり.

Exam trap: in 用法, a wrong sentence often uses the headword in its literal or kanji meaning, when Japanese only uses it figuratively, or the reverse. ビジョン is not something you see with your eyes. 潔い is not "clean". 手堅い is not about hard hands. 脅かす read おびやかす is not "to scare". If a sentence translates neatly using the English gloss of the word, be suspicious, and check its typical partner words instead.
