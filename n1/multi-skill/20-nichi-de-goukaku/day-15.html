Q43

その中には、匿名性を［43］無責任な発信をしたものも多い。Sono naka ni wa, tokumeisei o [43] musekinin na hasshin o shita mono mo ooi.

Exam trap 1 — 果たして has two meanings (Q22): with a question, 果たして = "will it really…?" (果たして成功するだろうか). In a statement it means "just as expected" (果たして彼は来なかった), but then the sentence must describe a result that was predicted. It never means "certainly" or "on reflex".
Exam trap 2 — humble vs respectful in apologies (Q34): when you talk about what you didn't know, use humble 存じませんでした. Respectful なさる／ご存じ are for the other person. A wrong keigo direction is the most common N1 dialogue trap.
Exam trap 3 — causative wishes in editorials (Q45): X には〜を身につけさせたい = "we want X to acquire ~". When a sentence names someone with に and the writer is proposing something for them, look for 〜させたい, not plain 〜たい.
