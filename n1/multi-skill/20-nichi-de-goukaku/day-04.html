Q1

雨に煙って、裏山がかすんで見えた。Ame ni kemutte, urayama ga kasunde mieta.

EN: Veiled in rain, the hill behind the house looked hazy.
HI: बारिश में धुँधलाया पीछे का पहाड़ धुंधला दिख रहा था।
GU: વરસાદમાં ધૂંધળો બનેલો પાછળનો ડુંગર ઝાંખો દેખાતો હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しめってshimette湿って — getting dampनम होकरભીનું થઈને
2かかってkakatte掛かって — hanging overछाकरછવાઈને

EN: However, the smoking rate among adult men is still over 30 per cent, and restaurants and the like strongly resist, saying it's hard to ban all customers from smoking.

Q44

だが、成人人口の8割近くはたばこを吸わず、喫煙者の7、8割は禁煙を望んでいることにも目を［44］。Daga, seijin jinkou no hachiwari chikaku wa tabako o suwazu, kitsuensha no nana, hachiwari wa kin'en o nozonde iru koto ni mo me o [44].

Full sentence: …禁煙を望んでいることにも目を向けたい。… kin'en o nozonde iru koto ni mo me o muketai.
EN: But we should also turn our attention to the fact that nearly 80% of adults don't smoke and 70–80% of smokers want to quit.
HI: लेकिन इस बात पर भी ध्यान देना चाहिए कि लगभग 80% वयस्क धूम्रपान नहीं करते और 70–80% धूम्रपान करने वाले इसे छोड़ना चाहते हैं।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1開けたいaketaiwant to open (目を開ける = open one's eyes)आँखें खोलनाઆંખો ખોલવી

Why: 〜に目を向ける = to pay attention to ~; the editorial wants readers to look at the other side of the statistics. 目を奪う (be captivated) and 目を回す (get dizzy / be extremely busy) are real idioms with different meanings; 目を開ける is literal.

Q45

世界で［45］禁煙が進めば、煙たい日本は、禁煙が進む外国からの観光客に文字通り煙たがられる。Sekai de [45] kin'en ga susumeba, kemutai Nihon wa, kin'en ga susumu gaikoku kara no kankoukyaku ni mojidoori kemutagarareru.

Full sentence: 世界でこれだけ禁煙が進めば、煙たい日本は、…文字通り煙たがられる。Sekai de kore dake kin'en ga susumeba, kemutai Nihon wa, … mojidoori kemutagarareru.
EN: With smoking bans spreading this much around the world, smoky Japan will literally be found "smoky" (unwelcoming) by tourists from smoke-free countries.
HI: दुनिया में धूम्रपान-निषेध इतना फैल रहा है कि धुएँ से भरा जापान, धूम्रपान-मुक्त देशों के पर्यटकों को सचमुच "धुआँ-धुआँ" और अखरने वाला लगेगा।
GU: દુનિયામાં ધૂમ્રપાન-નિષેધ આટલો ફેલાય છે ત્યારે ધુમાડાભર્યું જાપાન, ધૂમ્રપાન-મુક્ત દેશોના પ્રવાસીઓને ખરેખર "ધુમાડિયું" અને ખટકતું લાગશે.

Exam trap 1 — irregular readings (Q6): 指図 is さしず (kun + on), not しず or しと. Other high-frequency mixed readings: 手本 (てほん), 場所 (ばしょ), 番組 (ばんぐみ), 見当 (けんとう). If all the "regular" options look wrong, suspect a mixed reading.
Exam trap 2 — keigo direction (Q33): when the action belongs to a superior, you need 尊敬語 (お帰りになる). お帰りする is 謙譲語 and お帰りいただく means "we have you leave" — both are classic distractors. Ask: whose action is it?
Exam trap 3 — 目を〜 idioms in 問題7 (Q44): 目を向ける (pay attention), 目を奪う (captivate), 目を回す (be dizzy / swamped), 目をつぶる (overlook), 目を通す (look through). Editorials asking the reader to notice a fact use 目を向けたい.
