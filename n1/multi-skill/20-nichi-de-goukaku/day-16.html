Exam trap 1 — the same meaning, a different kanji (Q2, Q3): 問題1 often offers a reading that fits the meaning of the sentence but belongs to another kanji (とだえる for 絶える, しずまる for 収まる on 第14日). Always ask "is this the reading of this kanji?", not "does this word fit?".
Exam trap 2 — 横着 and 寝返り look literal but aren't (Q19, Q25): 横着 has nothing to do with lying down, and the core collocation of 寝返り is 寝返りを打つ. Kanji-based guessing fails with these idiomatic words — learn the collocation.
Exam trap 3 — 一方 in news Japanese (Q41, Q42): 〜一方(で) links two contrasting actions or facts. When you see it in a cloze, the two halves must point in opposite directions (cancel ↔ extend, new era ↔ end of an era).
