Q45

日本は高度な技術を生かした多様な付加価値で勝負する知識集約型資本主義への脱皮が一段と［45］。Nihon wa koudo na gijutsu o ikashita tayou na fuka kachi de shoubu suru chishiki shuuyakugata shihon shugi e no dappi ga ichidan to [45].

Full sentence: …知識集約型資本主義への脱皮が一段と求められる。… chishiki shuuyakugata shihon shugi e no dappi ga ichidan to motomerareru.
EN: Japan is all the more required to transform itself into a knowledge-intensive capitalism that competes on diverse added value built on advanced technology.
HI: जापान से और भी ज़्यादा अपेक्षा है कि वह उन्नत तकनीक पर टिके विविध मूल्य-वर्धन से मुक़ाबला करने वाले ज्ञान-आधारित पूँजीवाद की ओर कायापलट करे।
GU: જાપાન પાસે વધુ અપેક્ષા છે કે તે અદ્યતન ટેક્નોલોજી પર આધારિત વિવિધ મૂલ્યવર્ધનથી સ્પર્ધા કરતા જ્ઞાન-આધારિત મૂડીવાદ તરફ કાયાપલટ કરે.

Exam trap 1 — the particle picks the noun (Q9, Q11): in 問題2 the word after the blank often decides the answer. 〜に＿＿する leaves only 対処 among 処置／処理／処分／対処; ＿＿心 leaves only 自負. Read the particle or suffix first, then think about meaning.
Exam trap 2 — voicing in 問題1 (Q4, Q5): the distractors often differ only by ゛ or ゜. 偽造 = ぎぞう (ぎそう is 偽装), 譲歩 = じょうほ (ぽ only after ん, as in 散歩; じょうぶ is 丈夫). Learn the compound, not just each kanji.
Exam trap 3 — 問題7 editorials end in passive/ongoing forms: newspaper writing likes 〜が求められる, 〜つつある, 〜とみていい. When the subject of the blank is an abstract noun (脱皮が, 分野が), an active or causative verb is almost never right.
