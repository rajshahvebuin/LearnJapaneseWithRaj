Q44

消費者が回収に応じやすい環境を［44］、増やせる。Shouhisha ga kaishuu ni oujiyasui kankyou o [44], fuyaseru.

Full sentence: 消費者が回収に応じやすい環境を整えれば、増やせる。Shouhisha ga kaishuu ni oujiyasui kankyou o totonoereba, fuyaseru.
EN: If we create conditions that make it easy for consumers to hand in their phones, the number can be increased.
HI: अगर उपभोक्ताओं के लिए फ़ोन लौटाना आसान बनाने वाला माहौल तैयार किया जाए, तो संख्या बढ़ाई जा सकती है।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: The result 増やせる (can increase) is possible on condition that the environment is prepared → 〜ば. 1) 〜ては leads to an undesirable result; 2) 〜ても is concessive ("even if") and contradicts the hopeful tone; 3) a plain sequence "prepare and increase" loses the condition that the potential form needs.

（途上国には使用済み家電などの「電子ゴミ」が流れ込み、ひどい公害を引き起こしている。）そうした問題の解決にも［45］。(Tojoukoku ni wa shiyouzumi kaden nado no "denshi gomi" ga nagarekomi, hidoi kougai o hikiokoshite iru.) Sou shita mondai no kaiketsu ni mo [45].

Full sentence: そうした問題の解決にも役立ちうる。Sou shita mondai no kaiketsu ni mo yakudachiuru.
EN: (E-waste such as used appliances flows into developing countries and causes serious pollution.) A recycling system could also help to solve such problems.

Exam trap 1 — one kanji, two readings, two meanings (Q5): 粋 (すい／いき), 生物 (せいぶつ／なまもの), 大家 (たいか／おおや). When the sentence is about "collecting the best of technology", only すい fits; read the context before choosing the reading you know best.
Exam trap 2 — 敬語 direction (Q33): A uses humble 伺う about his own visit; B must use honorific お越しください about A's coming. The exam often offers the humble verb from the previous line as a distractor.
Exam trap 3 — compound verbs split across tiles (Q40): a する-noun tile (日本で消費) and a bare する tile (されている) must sit side by side, because together they are one verb (消費されている). Find such pairs first and the rest of the order falls into place.
