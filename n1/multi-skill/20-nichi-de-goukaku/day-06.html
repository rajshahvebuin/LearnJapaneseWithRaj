Exam trap 1 — お〜いただく vs. お〜する (Q33): when the customer does the action, the shop says お待ちいただく／ご利用いただく. お待ちする and お待ち申し上げる are the shop's own humble actions ("we await you") — they can never be combined with いただく. Watch for extra する／される／申す inserted into the polite frame.
Exam trap 2 — who is the subject? (Q43, Q44): in the 問題7 article the care worker is the implied subject of every clause. Things the residents did to her become passive (顔をそむけられる, 怒られる); care she gives them, seen from the family, is 世話してもらう. Fix the subject first, then pick voice and giving/receiving verb.
Exam trap 3 — 4-item 問題4 katakana and set collocations (Q22, Q23): ファスナー only belongs on bags and clothes; 割く only takes a resource you share out (時間・人手・紙面). The wrong options usually hide the correct collocation: 便宜を図る, キャベツを刻む.
