3a 低くした ／ b 復活させられたa hikuku shita / b fukkatsu saseraretaa lowered / b was made to revivea घटाया / b फिर लागू करने को मजबूरa ઘટાડ્યો / b ફરી લાગુ કરવા મજબૂર
4a ゼロにした ／ b 復活させたa zero ni shita / b fukkatsu sasetaa cut to zero / b revived (it)a शून्य किया / b फिर लागू कियाa શૂન્ય કર્યો / b ફરી લાગુ કર્યો

Why: You can only "revive" something that was gone, so (a) must be ゼロにした (abolished in effect). The subject 現政権は is the agent that brings the tax back → active causative 復活させた. 復活された (passive) or 復活させられた (causative-passive) wrongly make the government the one affected.

Exam trap 1 — kana-written grammar in ★ tiles (Q37): についで is に次いで ("next after"), not について ("about"). Tiles often print grammar in kana; read the whole string and check which one makes the sentence work (ロシア、アメリカについで = after Russia and the US).
Exam trap 2 — request keigo (Q33): asking a customer to do something = お〜いただけますか / お〜ください. お聞きする is humble (your own action), so お聞きしてください is wrong when the customer is the one asking.
Exam trap 3 — voice in 問題7 (Q44, Q45): check who acts. The state suffers capital fleeing → 逃げ出される; the government brings a tax back → 復活させる. Passive and causative distractors look similar — decide the agent first.
