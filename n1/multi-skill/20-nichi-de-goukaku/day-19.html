Exam trap 1 — sound-alike verbs in 問題4 (Q21, Q25): the wrong sentences often hide a different verb that sounds similar: けなす vs けがをする, こらえる vs こたえる／こしらえる. If a sentence feels absurd, ask which similar-sounding word it really needs.
Exam trap 2 — humble verbs never take honorifics (Q33): 申す、参る、いたす are humble; adding いらっしゃる or なさる to them (申していらっしゃった, 申しなさった) is always wrong. For "as I said earlier" use 申し上げましたとおり／申しましたとおり.
Exam trap 3 — 〜づくり in 問題7 (Q42): 〜づくり means "building / creating (a system, a town, a body)": 態勢づくり, 町づくり, 体づくり. When a blank is followed by づくり, look for an abstract structure noun.
