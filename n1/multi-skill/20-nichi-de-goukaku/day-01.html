Q1

一度や二度の失敗で懲りるようではだめだ。Ichido ya nido no shippai de koriru you de wa dame da.

EN: If you are put off by failing once or twice, you won't get anywhere.
HI: एक-दो बार की असफलता से ही तौबा कर लो, तो काम नहीं चलेगा।
GU: એક-બે વારની નિષ્ફળતાથી જ તોબા કરી લો, તો નહીં ચાલે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おりるoriru降りる — get offउतरनाઊતરવું
2こりるkoriru懲りる — learn one's lesson, be put offसबक़ लेना, तौबा करनाબોધપાઠ લેવો
3かりるkariru借りる — borrowउधार लेनाઉધાર લેવું

（文科省は85年に米飯給食の実施の目標を「週3回程度」と決めた。）それが達成されたことから、昨年からは「週3回以上」［44］。(Monkashou wa hachijuugo-nen ni beihan kyuushoku no jisshi no mokuhyou o "shuu sankai teido" to kimeta.) Sore ga tassei sareta koto kara, sakunen kara wa "shuu sankai ijou" [44].

Exam trap 1 — 承る is humble, so check who is being humble to whom (Q25): it is for the speaker (or their company) receiving something from a customer or superior — 注文を承る, ご予約を承ります. Using it for an examiner listening to a student, for a newspaper, or toward your younger brother is always wrong.
Exam trap 2 — katakana in 問題4 (Q20, Q24): the wrong options use the katakana word where Japanese uses a different word: extra innings are 延長戦, not アンコール; an essay writer is an エッセイスト, not "エッセイ家". Check the collocation, not just the meaning.
Exam trap 3 — 問題6 here tests noun + particle links: においては, への + N, における + N, によって. Put the tile that must touch the fixed text first (a noun before を／が, a verb before のは or before a noun), then slot the particle phrases in. When two orders look grammatical (Q40), the key prefers the one with the "means" phrase first.
