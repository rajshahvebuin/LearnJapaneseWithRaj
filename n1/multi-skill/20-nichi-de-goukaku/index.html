20日で合格 N1 文字・語彙・文法

Following 日本語能力試験 20日で合格 N1 文字・語彙・文法［改訂版］ (20-nichi de Goukaku N1 Moji・Goi・Bunpou, 国書日本語学校 / 国書刊行会) day by day. Each of the 20 days is one 45-question set in real JLPT format: 漢字読み, 文脈規定, 言い換え類義, 用法, 文の文法 1 & 2 (★ ordering) and a newspaper-article 文章の文法. Every page gives the day's kanji readings, vocabulary and grammar, every question with the official answer, and confusion pairs, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
