Q1

子どもが投げたビーチボールが波間に漂っていた。Kodomo ga nageta biichi booru ga namima ni tadayotte ita.

EN: The beach ball the child had thrown was drifting among the waves.
HI: बच्चे की फेंकी हुई बीच-बॉल लहरों के बीच तैर रही थी।
GU: બાળકે ફેંકેલો બીચ-બૉલ મોજાં વચ્ચે તરતો હતો.

Exam trap 1 — same reading, different kanji (Q12): 子・小・個・固 are all こ. When every option has the same reading, the test is purely "which compound exists" — 小切手. Learn compounds as whole words.
Exam trap 2 — the 〜たところで double (Q40, and 第19日 Q36): V-たところで ("even if you do ~") is usually followed by a negative or rhetorical clause (意味がない, 何の不思議があるものか). Once you spot ところで among the tiles, put the た-verb just before it.
Exam trap 3 — humble ておる on the phone (Q33): describing your own colleague to an outside caller = 〜ております with the normal collocation (電話に出る). Don't force a special humble verb (申す, 参る, 致す) into a set phrase where it doesn't belong.
