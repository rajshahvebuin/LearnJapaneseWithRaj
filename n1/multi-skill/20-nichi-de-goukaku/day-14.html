【Q32】 〜ずにはおかない

硬 formal / written
Reading~zu ni wa okanai (〜ないではおかない)
Meaning (EN)(something) cannot fail to ~ / will certainly ~ — a thing naturally provokes a feeling or reaction; or a strong will to make something happen
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना नहीं रहता, ज़रूर ...करता है
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વિના રહેતું નથી, ચોક્કસ ...કરે છે
Connection (接続)V-ない形 + ずにはおかない (する → せずにはおかない)
Registerwritten; often with causative/emotion verbs: 驚かす, 感動させる, 考えさせる

Exam trap 1 — どうやら needs a guessing ending (Q24): どうやら, まるで, あたかも and まさか all "call" a specific ending. どうやら → 〜ようだ／らしい; あたかも → 〜かのように; まさか → 〜ないだろう. If the ending is missing, the sentence is wrong — check the end of each option first.
Exam trap 2 — give/receive verbs in dialogues (Q34): ask "who does the action, and who is affected?" When the listener's action causes the speaker trouble, it's 〜てもらっては困る. 〜てやる／〜てあげる make the speaker the doer, and 〜させる makes the speaker the one allowing — both reverse the roles.
Exam trap 3 — fixed politeness formula (Q35): to decline an offer of help, use それには及びません (no need for that). Don't let the potential form 及べません or the particle では tempt you — the set phrase never changes.
