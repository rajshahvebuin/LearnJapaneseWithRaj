【Q27】 〜べからざる

硬 formal / literary
Reading~bekarazaru (sentence-final: 〜べからず)
Meaning (EN)that must not be ~ / that cannot be ~ — a classical negative of べき, used before a noun
Meaning (HI)जिसे ...नहीं किया जाना चाहिए; अक्षम्य

Exam trap 1 — ベストセラー and セレモニー have narrow Japanese uses (Q20, Q24): loanwords often keep only one sense of the English word. ベストセラー is for books; セレモニー is the formal staged part of an event. Products are ヒット商品／売れ筋; a wedding without religion is 人前式.
Exam trap 2 — keigo for borrowing (Q34): "May I borrow?" = お借りしてもよろしいでしょうか (humble, your action). "Could you lend me?" = 貸していただけますか. Mixing お貸し with くれる／もらう produces forms that don't exist.
Exam trap 3 — parallel 〜たり (Q43): when one 〜たり already appears, the matching item must also be 〜たり, and the series closes with する／しない. Long sentences in 問題7 hide this pair far apart — scan back for the first たり.
