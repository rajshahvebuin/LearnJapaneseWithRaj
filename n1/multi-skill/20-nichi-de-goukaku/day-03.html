Full sentence: 電力使用が集中する昼間は避け、夜中に使うという手もある。Denryoku shiyou ga shuuchuu suru hiruma wa sake, yonaka ni tsukau to iu te mo aru.
EN: Another option is to avoid the daytime, when power use peaks, and use it at night.
HI: एक तरीक़ा यह भी है कि बिजली की सबसे ज़्यादा खपत वाले दिन के समय से बचें और इसे रात में चलाएँ।

Why: 〜という手もある = "one (more) way is to ~"; the reader is the one who uses the ice-maker, so the active 使う is right, and も adds this tip to the previous one. Passive (2), causative (3) and causative-passive (4) give the wrong agent; 手にある is not the idiom.

Q45

大石さんは①庫内の物を取り出しづらくなり、［45-a］ドアを開けている時間が［45-b］いないか、…に注意が必要と指摘。Ooishi-san wa (1) konai no mono o toridashizuraku nari, [45-a] doa o akete iru jikan ga [45-b] inai ka, … ni chuui ga hitsuyou to shiteki.

Full sentence: …庫内の物を取り出しづらくなり、かえってドアを開けている時間が長くなっていないか…… konai no mono o toridashizuraku nari, kaette doa o akete iru jikan ga nagaku natte inai ka …
EN: Ms Oishi points out that you need to check (1) whether things inside become harder to take out, so that the door actually ends up open for longer, …

Exam trap 1 — synonym distractors in 問題1 (Q2, Q3): いそしむ (勤しむ) and のぞむ (臨む) are real verbs with nearly the right meaning. The question asks for the reading of this kanji, so a word with a different kanji is wrong however well it fits the sentence.
Exam trap 2 — the connection decides (Q28, Q31): two options often mean the same thing but attach differently. 出し（ます-stem）+ かねない vs 出す + 恐れがある; 懐かしく + てたまらない vs V-て + やまない. Check what comes right before the blank.
Exam trap 3 — 問題7 split blanks (Q43, Q45): read both halves together. The a-part is usually a connector (例えば, かえって) that must match the logic of the b-part (まとめておけば, 長くなって). Eliminate any pair where one half contradicts the surrounding sentence.
