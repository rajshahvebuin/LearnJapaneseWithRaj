JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 8 — まとめて覚えよう② (Matomete oboeyou ②)

2日目 — 似ている言葉② (Niteiru kotoba ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.126–127 (PDF pages 116–117). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10, confirmed by reading the printed page numbers directly.
Theme
Two prefix/attachment families this time instead of loose near-synonyms: 24 compounds built on 一 (ichi — "one/whole/a single ~") covering everything from 一同 ("everyone present") to 一新する ("to completely renew"), followed by 7 compounds built on 見 (mi — "to look/see") such as 見逃す, 見落とす, and 見失う, three verbs English would all flatten into "miss."
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.129 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Format shift within the chapter: unlike Day 1's loose cluster of unrelated near-synonyms, today groups compounds sharing a literal kanji prefix — the same organizing principle Week 4 used for 手／分／同／心. The book's own English translations are kept; Hindi/Gujarati are added fresh for this site, replacing the book's Chinese/Korean.
→ = a related derived noun form, shown in the Note column with its own romaji + gloss
Warm-up

（　）に入るのは？(___) ni hairu no wa?
問題が（　）に解決した。Mondai ga (___) ni kaiketsu shita.
("What goes in the blank? — 'The problem was resolved [___].' Options shown: 一変, 一連, 一挙.")

一挙 (ikkyo) — "all at once" (問題が一挙に解決した — the problem was solved all at once, ＝一気に)

Q1

スリを追いかけたが、途中で（a.見失って b.見落として）しまった。Suri o oikaketa ga, tochuu de (a. miushinatte b. miotoshite) shimatta.

Full sentence: スリを追いかけたが、途中で見失ってしまった。Suri o oikaketa ga, tochuu de miushinatte shimatta.
EN: I chased the pickpocket, but lost sight of him partway through.
HI: मैं जेबकतरे का पीछा कर रहा था, पर बीच में उसे खो बैठा।
GU: હું ખિસ્સાકાતરુની પાછળ દોડ્યો, પણ વચ્ચે તેને ગુમાવી દીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a見失ってmiushinattelost sight/track of
b見落としてmiotoshiteoverlooked (wrong verb — for a stationary detail, not a fleeing person)

Why: 見失う describes losing track of something moving that one was following — exactly a pickpocket vanishing mid-chase; 見落とす means failing to notice something that was there in front of you the whole time, like an error on a page.

Q2

全社員に（a.一連 b.一斉）にメールを送った。Zen shain ni (a. ichiren b. issei) ni meeru o okutta.

Full sentence: 全社員に一斉にメールを送った。Zen shain ni issei ni meeru o okutta.
EN: An email was sent to all employees all at once.
HI: सभी कर्मचारियों को एक साथ ईमेल भेजा गया।
GU: બધા કર્મચારીઓને એકસાથે ઈમેલ મોકલવામાં આવ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a一連ichirena connected series (wrong meaning — for events, not simultaneity)
b一斉isseiall at once, simultaneously

Why: 一斉に describes many people/things acting or being acted on at the exact same moment — a mass email hitting every inbox together; 一連 describes a chain of connected events (a series of crimes), not simultaneous timing.

Q3

古い家がリフォームで（a.見違える b.見計らう）ようになった。Furui ie ga rifoomu de (a. michigaeru b. mihakarau) you ni natta.

Full sentence: 古い家がリフォームで見違えるようになった。Furui ie ga rifoomu de michigaeru you ni natta.
EN: The old house was renovated until it became unrecognizably better.
HI: पुराना घर मरम्मत से इतना बदल गया कि पहचान में नहीं आता।
GU: જૂનું ઘર સમારકામથી એટલું બદલાઈ ગયું કે ઓળખાય જ નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
a見違えるmichigaerutransformed beyond recognition
b見計らうmihakaraujudge the right timing (wrong verb — unrelated to physical transformation)

Why: 見違える describes something changing so dramatically for the better that it looks like a different thing entirely — the perfect fit for a renovated house; 見計らう is strictly about choosing the right moment to act.

Q4

ドラマの最終回を（a.見落として b.見逃して）しまった。Dorama no saishuukai o (a. miotoshite b. minogashite) shimatta.

Full sentence: ドラマの最終回を見逃してしまった。Dorama no saishuukai o minogashite shimatta.
EN: I ended up missing the final episode of the drama.
HI: मैं ड्रामा का आख़िरी एपिसोड देखने से चूक गया।
GU: હું ડ્રામાનો છેલ્લો એપિસોડ જોવાનું ચૂકી ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a見落としてmiotoshiteoverlooked (wrong verb — for a detail present in front of you)
b見逃してminogashitemissed (a time-limited chance to watch)

Why: 見逃す is the fixed verb for missing a one-time chance to see/catch something before it's gone, exactly a broadcast episode; 見落とす is about failing to notice a static detail, not a broadcast slipping by.

Q5

黒い煙が空（a.一様 b.一面）に広がっている。Kuroi kemuri ga sora (a. ichiyou b. ichimen) ni hirogatte iru.

Full sentence: 黒い煙が空一面に広がっている。Kuroi kemuri ga sora ichimen ni hirogatte iru.
EN: Black smoke is spreading across the entire sky.
HI: काला धुआँ पूरे आकाश में फैल रहा है।
GU: કાળો ધુમાડો આખા આકાશમાં ફેલાઈ રહ્યો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a一様ichiyouuniformly, everyone alike (wrong meaning — describes people's reactions, not a covered area)
b一面ichimenthe entire surface/area

Why: 空一面 ("the whole sky") is the fixed phrase for something covering an entire visible area; 一様に describes multiple people or things all behaving the same way, not smoke covering a surface.

Q6

ここからの眺めは（a.一目 b.一見）の価値がある。Koko kara no nagame wa (a. ichimoku b. ikken) no kachi ga aru.

Full sentence: ここからの眺めは一見の価値がある。Koko kara no nagame wa ikken no kachi ga aru.
EN: The view from here is worth seeing.
HI: यहाँ से दिखने वाला नज़ारा देखने लायक़ है।
GU: અહીંથી દેખાતું દૃશ્ય જોવા લાયક છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a一目ichimokua single glance (wrong word here — pairs with 置く or 瞭然, not 価値がある)
b一見ikkena look/glance

Why: 一見の価値がある is the fixed idiom for "worth a look"; 一目 is reserved for its own set phrases like 一目置く ("to hold in high regard") and 一目瞭然 ("obvious at a glance"), and doesn't combine with 価値がある.

Q7

この棚の商品は（　）3割引きになっています。Kono tana no shouhin wa (___) san-wari-biki ni natte imasu.

Full sentence: この棚の商品は一律3割引きになっています。Kono tana no shouhin wa ichiritsu san-wari-biki ni natte imasu.
EN: The items on this shelf are uniformly 30% off.
HI: इस शेल्फ़ के सामान पर एक समान 30% छूट है।
GU: આ છાજલીની ચીજવસ્તુઓ પર એકસમાન 30% છૂટ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1一同ichidoueveryone present (wrong meaning — a group of people, not a rate)
2一律ichiritsuuniformly, across the board
3一帯ittaia whole area (wrong meaning — land, not a discount)
4一連ichirena connected series (wrong meaning)

Why: 一律に is the fixed way to say a rate/rule applies equally to everything — a discount here, salaries in the vocabulary list above; 一同 refers to a group of people, not a percentage.

Q8

新型の車は以前のデザインを（　）した。Shingata no kuruma wa izen no dezain o (___) shita.

Full sentence: 新型の車は以前のデザインを一新した。Shingata no kuruma wa izen no dezain o isshin shita.
EN: The new car model completely renewed its previous design.
HI: नई कार मॉडल ने पुराने डिज़ाइन को पूरी तरह बदल दिया।
GU: નવા કાર મોડેલે જૂની ડિઝાઇનને સંપૂર્ણપણે બદલી નાખી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1一掃issousweep away entirely (wrong verb — for eliminating something unwanted, not updating a design)
2一心isshinwholehearted (wrong meaning)
3一新isshincompletely renew
4一見ikkenat a glance (wrong meaning)

Why: 一新する means "to refresh/renew something completely" — exactly a redesigned car; 一掃する means "to eliminate/sweep away" something undesirable (violence, debt), not to refresh a design.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's whole lesson is one shared kanji branching into many unrelated meanings — here are the two clusters most likely to blur together:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
一目ichimoku A single glance Only appears in its own fixed idioms — 一目置く, 一目瞭然. 彼の才能には社長も一目置いている。
一見ikken A look/glance (worth taking) Pairs with 価値がある ("worth it") — a different fixed set than 一目. ここからの眺めは一見の価値がある。
一様にichiyou ni Everyone/everything alike Describes multiple people reacting or behaving identically. 参加者は一様に驚いた。
一律にichiritsu ni Uniformly, across the board Describes a single rate/rule being applied equally to everyone. 給料を一律に上げる。
見逃すminogasu To miss a time-limited chance Something was there to catch/see, and the moment passed. チャンスを見逃す, ドラマを見逃す.
見落とすmiotosu To fail to notice something present A static detail was there the whole time and simply wasn't seen. 間違いを見落とす.
見失うmiushinau To lose sight/track of something Something was being actively followed/pursued and then got lost. 道を見失う, 目標を見失う.
One kanji, four unrelated grammatical jobs: 一 shows up today as a noun-forming prefix meaning "whole/entire" (一面, 一帯), as "a single instance of" (一目, 一見, 一瞬), as "in unison" (一斉に, 一様に), and inside native-Japanese counter-style words meaning "a little/one round of" (一休み, 一眠り, 一苦労). Don't assume any two 一〜 words share a nuance just because they share the kanji — check which of these four jobs it's doing in that specific compound.
