Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.8–9 (PDF pages 202–203), bound into this scan: 導入練習 ①–③ at the foot of p.8, everything else on p.9. The key adds a short note on 実践練習 4-1 and 4-2; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Technology words are built from affixes: 〜発電 (原子力・火力・水力・太陽光発電), 〜源 (エネルギー源, 発生源), 〜化 (多様化, デジタル化), 省〜 (省エネ, 省電力), 人工〜 (人工衛星, 人工知能). Fixed verb pairs: 特許を申請／取得する, 衛星を打ち上げる, 金属を加工／塗装する, 鉄がさびる, プロジェクトが発足する. Main exam traps: 携わる means being involved in a field of work (開発に携わる), not just being employed at a company. いまだに means "still, even now" when a change was expected, so it isn't "now" or "soon". 次々に ≈ 絶えず, not 次第に.
A—B = antonyms (出力する—入力する, 離陸する—着陸する, アナログ—デジタル) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (ハードウェア・ソフトウェア, 情報・データ) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (開発／試作する, 太陽電池／ソーラーパネル, 申請／取得／登録する)
A←B = A is the short form of B (家電←家庭用電気製品) · □ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. 金属を□ → 金属を加工する)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)

Sample answer (added, not in book): インドでは主に石炭を使った火力発電で電力を作っていますが、最近は太陽光発電や風力発電も増えて、エネルギー源が多様化しています。電力は以前より足りるようになりましたが、夏は消費が増えて停電することもあります。Indo de wa omo ni sekitan o tsukatta karyoku hatsuden de denryoku o tsukutte imasu ga, saikin wa taiyoukou hatsuden ya fuuryoku hatsuden mo fuete, enerugiigen ga tayouka shite imasu. Denryoku wa izen yori tariru you ni narimashita ga, natsu wa shouhi ga fuete teiden suru koto mo arimasu. — "India makes electricity mainly by coal-fired thermal power, but solar and wind power are growing and energy sources are diversifying. There's more electricity than before, but demand rises in summer and there are sometimes power cuts." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

❹ 例文 (Reibun — example sentences; learn the bold adverbs too)

例1 · ★次々

電気メーカーは次々に新製品を開発している。Denki meekaa wa tsugitsugi ni shinseihin o kaihatsu shite iru.

EN: Electronics makers are developing new products one after another.
HI: बिजली उपकरण बनाने वाली कंपनियाँ एक के बाद एक नए उत्पाद विकसित कर रही हैं।
GU: વીજ ઉપકરણ બનાવતી કંપનીઓ એક પછી એક નવાં ઉત્પાદનો વિકસાવી રહી છે.
次々(に／と) tsugitsugi (ni / to) = one after another, in succession · HI: एक के बाद एक · GU: એક પછી એક
例2 · ★未だに

山田さんは未だに携帯電話を持たない主義を貫いている。Yamada-san wa imada ni keitai denwa o motanai shugi o tsuranuite iru.

EN: Mr Yamada still sticks to his principle of not owning a mobile phone.
HI: यामादा जी आज तक मोबाइल फ़ोन न रखने के अपने उसूल पर क़ायम हैं।
GU: યામાદાજી આજ સુધી મોબાઇલ ફોન ન રાખવાના પોતાના સિદ્ધાંત પર અડગ છે.
未だに imada ni = still, even now (when a change was expected) · HI: आज तक, अब भी · GU: આજ સુધી, હજી પણ
例3 · ★果たして

そんなことが果たして可能だろうか。Sonna koto ga hatashite kanou darou ka.

EN: Is such a thing really possible?
HI: क्या ऐसा सचमुच मुमकिन है?
GU: શું એવું ખરેખર શક્ય છે?
果たして〜だろうか hatashite ~ darou ka = (I wonder) really / actually…? (doubt) · HI: सचमुच…? · GU: ખરેખર…?
例4 · ★すっかり

省エネ家電はすっかり家庭に浸透した。Shouene kaden wa sukkari katei ni shintou shita.

EN: Energy-saving appliances have completely spread into households.
HI: ऊर्जा-बचत उपकरण पूरी तरह घरों में फैल चुके हैं।

Exam trap: 携わる needs a field of activity as its 〜に target (開発・教育・研究・事業に携わる).
