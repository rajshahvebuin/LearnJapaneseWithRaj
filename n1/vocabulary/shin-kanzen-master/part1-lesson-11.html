Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.7–8 (PDF pages 201–202), bound into this scan: 導入練習 and 連語 (1) on p.7, the rest on p.8. The key adds a short note on 実践練習 4-1 and 4-2; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Statistics vocabulary is built from suffixes: 〜率 (出生率, 未婚率, 死亡率, 就業率), 〜層 (貧困層, 若年層), 〜化 (少子化, 高齢化), and each has its own verb: 出生率が低下する, 未婚率が上昇する, 平均寿命が延びる, 死亡率が激増する. Care words: 高齢者を介護する (elderly care) vs 患者を看護する (nursing the sick). Main exam traps: 老後 (one's life after retirement — not 老期 / 末期), 軽視 (make light of — not "light-sightedness"), てきぱき (brisk, efficient action — not a state), 促進 ≈ 推進, 急速 ≈ 急激.
A—B = antonyms (貧困層—富裕層, 受給する—支給する, 老けた—若返った, 減少／激減する—増加／激増する, 軽視する—重視する) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (介護する・ケアする) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (両立させる／こなす)
A→B = B is closely related to A (介護福祉士→介護士; here also ← for the full form of a short word: 産休←産前産後休暇, 育休←育児休暇) · □ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. □が急速に広がる → 格差が急速に広がる)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)

Sample answer (added, not in book): インドには年金制度や健康保険制度がありますが、加入していない人も多いです。インドはまだ若い人が多いので少子高齢化は深刻ではありませんが、都市部では出生率が低下しています。Indo ni wa nenkin seido ya kenkou hoken seido ga arimasu ga, kanyuu shite inai hito mo ooi desu. Indo wa mada wakai hito ga ooi node shoushi koureika wa shinkoku de wa arimasen ga, toshibu de wa shusshouritsu ga teika shite imasu. — "India has pension and health-insurance schemes, but many people aren't enrolled. India still has many young people, so the falling-birth-rate/ageing problem isn't serious, but in cities the birth rate is falling." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

Exam trap: N1 用法 items love reading kanji literally. 軽視 is not "light vision" (that would be 近視 or わき見): it means "treat as unimportant" (〜を軽視できない／軽視してはいけない).
