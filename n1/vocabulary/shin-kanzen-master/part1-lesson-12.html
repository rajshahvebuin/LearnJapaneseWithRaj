Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.8 (PDF page 202), bound into this scan. The key adds a short note on 実践練習 4-1 and 4-2; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Weather vocabulary is built from compound verbs: 吹き荒れる／吹き寄せる (wind), 打ち寄せる (waves), 立ち込める (fog), 降り積もる (snow), and from 〜降り (土砂降り = 大雨). This lesson also has ten sound-and-manner adverbs: ひっそり, きらきら／こうこうと, ぽっかり, どんより, ざあざあ, ごうごう, ぽつぽつ, じめじめ. Main exam traps: じめじめ describes damp, muggy air, not the rain itself. 斜面 is a sloping surface (山の斜面), not a "side view". 広大 is for land on a huge scale, so a living room is 広々としている.
A—B = antonyms (日の出—日の入り, 南半球—北半球) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (日の入り・日没) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (集中豪雨／土砂降りの雨, 注意報／警報, 傾斜／斜面, 惑星／衛星)
□ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. □が吹き荒れる → 強風が吹き荒れる)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

(1) あなたの住んでいる国や地域はどんな気候ですか。Anata no sunde iru kuni ya chiiki wa donna kikou desu ka.EN: What is the climate like in the country or region where you live? · HI: आप जिस देश या इलाक़े में रहते हैं, वहाँ की जलवायु कैसी है? · GU: તમે જે દેશ કે વિસ્તારમાં રહો છો ત્યાંની આબોહવા કેવી છે?

(2) あなたの国の地形について説明してください。Anata no kuni no chikei ni tsuite setsumei shite kudasai.EN: Please describe the landforms (geography) of your country. · HI: अपने देश की भू-आकृति (भूगोल) के बारे में बताइए। · GU: તમારા દેશની ભૂરચના (ભૂગોળ) વિશે સમજાવો.

Sample answer (added, not in book): インドの多くの地域は高温多湿で、夏はとても暑く、雨季には集中豪雨もあります。北にはヒマラヤ山脈があり、中央には広大な平野が広がっていて、南は三方を海に囲まれています。Indo no ooku no chiiki wa kouon tashitsu de, natsu wa totemo atsuku, uki ni wa shuuchuu gouu mo arimasu. Kita ni wa Himaraya sanmyaku ga ari, chuuou ni wa koudai na heiya ga hirogatte ite, minami wa sanpou o umi ni kakomarete imasu. — "Much of India is hot and humid; summers are very hot and the rainy season brings torrential downpours. The Himalayas are in the north, vast plains spread across the centre, and the south is surrounded by sea on three sides." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

例1 · ★ひっそり

その船は今も海底にひっそりと沈んでいるという。Sono fune wa ima mo kaitei ni hissori to shizunde iru to iu.

EN: They say that ship still lies quietly on the seabed.
HI: कहते हैं वह जहाज़ आज भी समुद्र तल पर चुपचाप डूबा पड़ा है।
GU: કહે છે કે તે જહાજ આજે પણ દરિયાના તળિયે ચૂપચાપ ડૂબેલું પડ્યું છે.
ひっそり(と) hissori (to) = quietly, unnoticed, still · HI: चुपचाप, सुनसान · GU: ચૂપચાપ, સૂમસામ
例2 · ★果てしなく

果てしなく広がる広大な砂漠を旅する。Hateshinaku hirogaru koudai na sabaku o tabi suru.

EN: I travel across a vast desert that stretches on endlessly.
HI: अंतहीन फैले विशाल रेगिस्तान में सफ़र करना।
GU: અનંત ફેલાયેલા વિશાળ રણમાં પ્રવાસ કરવો.
果てしなく hateshinaku = endlessly, without limit · HI: अंतहीन रूप से · GU: અનંત રીતે
例3 · ★きらきら／★こうこうと

夜空にきらきら／こうこうと星が輝いている。Yozora ni kirakira / koukou to hoshi ga kagayaite iru.

EN: Stars are twinkling / shining brightly in the night sky.
HI: रात के आसमान में तारे टिमटिमा / जगमगा रहे हैं।
GU: રાતના આકાશમાં તારા ટમટમી / ઝગમગી રહ્યા છે.
きらきら kirakira = twinkling, sparkling · こうこうと koukou to (煌々と) = brilliantly, brightly · HI: टिमटिमाते हुए · जगमग · GU: ટમટમતા · ઝગમગ
例4 · ★ぽっかり

満月がぽっかり浮かんでいる。Mangetsu ga pokkari ukande iru.

EN: A full moon is floating alone in the sky.
HI: पूरा चाँद आसमान में अकेला तैर रहा है।
GU: પૂનમનો ચંદ્ર આકાશમાં એકલો તરી રહ્યો છે.
ぽっかり pokkari = (floating) lightly and alone; (a hole) gaping · HI: अकेले हल्के से (तैरता) · GU: એકલું હળવેથી (તરતું)
例5 · ★どんより

昨日は空がどんより曇っていた。Kinou wa sora ga don'yori kumotte ita.

例6 · ★ざあざあ

4-1 じめじめ

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1最近は雪がじめじめと降っている。Saikin wa yuki ga jimejime to futte iru.Lately the snow has been falling "damply".आजकल बर्फ़ "सीलन से" गिर रही है।આજકાલ બરફ "ભેજથી" પડી રહ્યો છે.

3じめじめと激怒するのはよくない。Jimejime to gekido suru no wa yoku nai.It's not good to get furious "damply"."सीलन से" भड़कना ठीक नहीं।"ભેજથી" ભડકવું સારું નથી.

Why: The key's note: じめじめ means humid and unpleasant (e.g. 部屋の空気がじめじめ), and it isn't used for heavy rain itself, as in 2's 暴風雨. Our addition: 1 and 3 fail for the same reason, because じめじめ is a state of the air or a place, not a way of falling or of getting angry. Key: 4.

Exam trap: The 用法 items test what a word can describe. じめじめ is damp, unpleasant air or a damp place (梅雨時はじめじめする, じめじめした部屋), never the rain or snow itself, and never a person's anger. 斜面 is a physical sloping surface (山の斜面) — don't confuse it with 側面 (side), 斜に構える (cynical attitude) or 覆面 (mask).
