Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.5 (PDF page 199), bound into this scan. The key adds a short note on 実践練習 4-1 and 4-2; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
University words come in near-identical 講 compounds: 講義を受講する (take a course), ゼミを聴講する (audit), 文献を講読する (read closely in class). Research verbs to learn as pairs: 先行研究を参照する, 用語を定義する, 意味を類推する. Main exam traps: 志す (aim at a goal — not 望む / 試す), 厳格 (strict about rules — not 厳密 / 厳重 / 威厳), 権威 vs 権利 / 権力, 参照 vs 参考 / 参加.
A—B = antonyms (男子校—女子校, 保守的—革新的, 必修科目—選択科目) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (自由な・リベラルな, 学費・授業料) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (塾／予備校／専門学校, 学士／修士／博士)
A→B = B is closely related to A (here: the short form, 短期大学→短大) · □ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. □校風 → 厳格な校風)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

(1) あなたの国にはどんな学校がありますか。そこでは何が勉強できますか。Anata no kuni ni wa donna gakkou ga arimasu ka. Soko de wa nani ga benkyou dekimasu ka.EN: What kinds of schools are there in your country? What can you study there? · HI: आपके देश में किस तरह के स्कूल हैं? वहाँ क्या पढ़ा जा सकता है? · GU: તમારા દેશમાં કેવા પ્રકારની શાળાઓ છે? ત્યાં શું ભણી શકાય?

(2) あなたはこれまでどんな学校で何を学んできましたか。Anata wa kore made donna gakkou de nani o manande kimashita ka.EN: What kinds of schools have you attended so far, and what have you studied? · HI: आपने अब तक किन स्कूलों में क्या-क्या पढ़ा है? · GU: તમે અત્યાર સુધી કઈ શાળાઓમાં શું શું ભણ્યા છો?

Sample answer (added, not in book): インドの男女共学の高校を出て、四年制大学で工学を学びました。今は日本語学校にも通っています。Indo no danjo kyougaku no koukou o dete, yonensei daigaku de kougaku o manabimashita. Ima wa nihongo gakkou ni mo kayotte imasu. — "I finished a co-educational high school in India and studied engineering at a four-year university. Now I also go to a Japanese language school." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

例1 · ★いざ

発表の準備は十分でも、いざ本番となると緊張してしまう。Happyou no junbi wa juubun demo, iza honban to naru to kinchou shite shimau.

EN: Even when I've prepared my presentation thoroughly, I get nervous when the real moment comes.
HI: प्रस्तुति की तैयारी पूरी हो, फिर भी असली वक़्त आते ही घबराहट हो जाती है।
GU: પ્રસ્તુતિની તૈયારી પૂરી હોય તોય, ખરો સમય આવતાં જ ગભરાટ થઈ જાય છે.
いざ〜となると iza … to naru to = when it actually comes to ~ · HI: जब असल में ~ की बारी आती है · GU: જ્યારે ખરેખર ~ નો વારો આવે
例2 · ★てっきり

田村さんは若そうだから、てっきり大学生かと思い込んでいた。Tamura-san wa wakasou da kara, tekkiri daigakusei ka to omoikonde ita.

EN: Tamura looks young, so I'd simply assumed he was a university student.
HI: तामुरा जवान दिखते हैं, इसलिए मैंने पक्का मान लिया था कि वे कॉलेज के छात्र हैं।
GU: તામુરા યુવાન દેખાય છે, એટલે મેં પાકું માની લીધું હતું કે તેઓ કૉલેજના વિદ્યાર્થી છે.

"Key difference" and "Use when" examples are ours (added, not in book), except the 厳格/厳密/厳重 and 志す/望む/願う/試す points, which follow the 別冊 notes.

Exam trap: The 厳〜 family shares a kanji and the English word "strict", so 用法 questions mix them up. Remember them by their partners. 厳格 goes with people and rules (父, 校風, しつけ). 厳密 goes with accuracy (言えば, 定義, 区別). 厳重 goes with security (保管, 警戒, 鍵). Do the same with 参照, which is a する-verb (文献を参照する). 参考 needs にする (〜を参考にする), so the bare form 参考する in a cloze is always wrong.
