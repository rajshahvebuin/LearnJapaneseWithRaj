新完全マスター N1 語彙

Following 新完全マスター語彙 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Vocabulary, スリーエーネットワーク) lesson by lesson — 第1部 topic-based vocabulary (9 chapters, 15 lessons: people, daily life, arts, education, work, media, society, science, abstract ideas), 第2部 vocabulary by property (16 lessons: multi-meaning words, look-alike and sound-alike words, adverbs, onomatopoeia, idioms, compound verbs, affixes) and two 50-point mock tests. Each page gives the book's word map with meanings, word formation and example sentences, every exercise with answers from the book's own 別冊 key, and confusion pairs — in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
