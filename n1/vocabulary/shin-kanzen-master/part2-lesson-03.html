Notes
Each book line pairs a hard N1 word with everyday synonyms: ことごとく = すべて, ろくに = 十分に (with a negative), あえて = 無理に, つれない = 冷たい, あやふや = はっきりしない. The whole 実践練習 is one 25-item paraphrase drill. Main exam traps: うっとうしい (annoying) ≈ 煩わしい but not ややこしい; いやに (strangely, with a doubt) ≈ 妙に, not かなり; 不意に ≈ 突然, not とっさに; ぎりぎり ≈ かろうじて, not ようやく; 面倒 ≈ ややこしい; もっぱら ≈ いつも (only that).
（A・B・C） = the book prints near-synonyms together inside one sentence; any of them fits there
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)

"Key difference" and "Use when" examples are ours (added, not in book), except where they follow the 別冊 notes cited.

Exam trap: paraphrase questions put a near-synonym that is wrong for this sentence beside the right one: ややこしい for うっとうしい (1-1), かなり for いやに (1-6), とっさに for 不意に (1-12), ようやく for ぎりぎり (1-13), 主に for もっぱら (1-24). Read the whole sentence: the key's notes always turn on what the context adds (doubt, surprise, a minimum, "only").
