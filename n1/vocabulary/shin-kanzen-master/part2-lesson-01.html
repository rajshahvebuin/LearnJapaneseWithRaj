Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.10–11 (PDF pages 204–205), bound into this scan. The key adds notes to the warm-up and to 実践練習 3-1, 3-2 and 3-3; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Each noun has a concrete core sense and one or more abstract ones: ひび (a crack → a rift in a relationship), 溝 (a ditch → a gap between people), 筋 (a tendon → logic → aptitude → a source), 芯 (a core → the body's core → backbone). Learn the verb each sense takes: あてにする／あてが外れる, 見込みが立つ, 拍子を取る, 隙をつく／ねらう, 枠を超える, 溝が深まる／埋まる, 柄に合わない. Main exam traps: 見込み (outlook, prospect) vs あて (something you count on) vs 模様 (the "looks like" of a situation), and 縁 (ふち, a rim) vs 枠 (a frame, a limit).

Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

下の三つの文の中で使われている「模様」にはどんな意味があると思いますか。Shita no mittsu no bun no naka de tsukawarete iru "moyou" ni wa donna imi ga aru to omoimasu ka.EN: What do you think 模様 means in the three sentences below? · HI: नीचे के तीन वाक्यों में 模様 का क्या अर्थ है, आपके विचार से? · GU: નીચેનાં ત્રણ વાક્યોમાં 模様નો શો અર્થ છે, તમારા મતે?

試合の模様を放送する。／会議の模様を見てから決める。／事故の模様を伝える。Shiai no moyou o housou suru. / Kaigi no moyou o mite kara kimeru. / Jiko no moyou o tsutaeru.EN: Broadcast how the match is going. / Decide after seeing how the meeting goes. / Report the circumstances of the accident. · HI: मैच का हाल प्रसारित करना। / बैठक का हाल देखकर तय करना। / दुर्घटना का हाल बताना। · GU: મેચનો હાલ પ્રસારિત કરવો. / બેઠકનો હાલ જોઈને નક્કી કરવું. / અકસ્માતનો હાલ જણાવવો.

a 絵や形 e ya katachi (pictures, shapes) · b 結果 kekka (result) · c 様子 yousu (state, how things look) · d 事実 jijitsu (fact)

Key (別冊 p.10): c 様子. The key's note says that in all three sentences 模様 shares the sense of "what can be seen from outside" — the visible state of things. · HI: तीनों वाक्यों में 模様 = बाहर से दिखने वाली हालत। · GU: ત્રણે વાક્યમાં 模様 = બહારથી દેખાતી હાલત.

Exam trap: the verb gives the noun away. 見込み・目途 立つ, あてにする／あてが外れる, ひびが入る, 溝ができる・深まる・埋まる, 隙をつく・ねらう, 枠を超える, 筋が通る. In 用法 questions the key repeatedly swaps 見込み into sentences that wrongly use あて or 模様 — check whether the sentence means "prospect / chance" (見込み) or "something to rely on" (あて).
