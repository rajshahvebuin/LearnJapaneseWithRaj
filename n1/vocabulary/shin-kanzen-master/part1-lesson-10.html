Notes
This lesson is newspaper Japanese, organised by the three branches of government. ▶立法 (the Diet): 法案が審議される → 採択される・成立する, 法律を制定／改定／施行する. ▶行政 (the executive): 規制が緩和される, 措置を取る, 条約に調印する. ▶司法 (the courts): 訴訟を起こす, 賠償を求める, 判決が下る. Main exam traps: 賠償 (compensation for serious or legal damage — not 弁償 for a broken vase), 措置 (measures — not 処置 for treating a tooth), 分断 vs 切断 / 横断, 質疑 vs 問答 / 回答.
A—B = antonyms (申請する—発給する, 繁栄する—衰退する) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (採択される・成立する, 内戦・紛争) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (制定する／整備する, 万全の／異例の／寛大な, 権利／基本的人権, 死刑／懲役, 遺跡／住居跡)
A→B = B is closely related to A (here: 整備する→法整備) · □ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. □措置を取る → 万全の措置を取る)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

(1) 最近、あなたの国や世界の政治に関してどんなニュースを聞きましたか。Saikin, anata no kuni ya sekai no seiji ni kanshite donna nyuusu o kikimashita ka.EN: What news about politics in your country or the world have you heard recently? · HI: हाल में आपने अपने देश या दुनिया की राजनीति से जुड़ी कौन-सी ख़बरें सुनीं? · GU: તાજેતરમાં તમે તમારા દેશ કે દુનિયાના રાજકારણ વિશે કયા સમાચાર સાંભળ્યા?

(2) あなたの国の有名な歴史上の人物について教えてください。Anata no kuni no yuumei na rekishijou no jinbutsu ni tsuite oshiete kudasai.EN: Tell us about a famous historical figure from your country. · HI: अपने देश के किसी प्रसिद्ध ऐतिहासिक व्यक्ति के बारे में बताइए। · GU: તમારા દેશની કોઈ પ્રખ્યાત ઐતિહાસિક વ્યક્તિ વિશે જણાવો.

Sample answer (added, not in book): 最近、国会で新しい法案が審議されているというニュースを聞きました。歴史上の人物では、アショーカ王が有名です。戦争の後、仏教を広めて国を繁栄させました。Saikin, kokkai de atarashii houan ga shingi sarete iru to iu nyuusu o kikimashita. Rekishijou no jinbutsu de wa, Ashooka-ou ga yuumei desu. Sensou no ato, bukkyou o hiromete kuni o han'ei sasemashita. — "Recently I heard news that a new bill is being debated in parliament. As for historical figures, King Ashoka is famous: after a war he spread Buddhism and made his country prosper." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1すみません、花瓶を壊してしまったので賠償します。Sumimasen, kabin o kowashite shimatta node baishou shimasu.Sorry, I broke the vase, so I'll pay "damages".माफ़ कीजिए, फूलदान टूट गया, इसलिए "हर्जाना" दूँगा।માફ કરશો, ફૂલદાની તૂટી ગઈ, એટલે "નુકસાની" આપીશ.

Exam trap: This lesson's 用法 questions turn on look-alike kanji families. 〜置: 措置 (measures) ／ 処置 (treat a wound or tooth) ／ 放置 (leave alone) ／ 設置 (install). 〜償: 賠償 (legal compensation) ／ 弁償 (pay for a broken item).
