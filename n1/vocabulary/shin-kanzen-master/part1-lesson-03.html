Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.3 (PDF page 197), bound into this scan. The key adds a short note on 実践練習 4-1 and 4-2; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Cooking verbs are a precise set: 米を研ぐ, 水に浸す, 小麦粉を練る, フィルターでこす, うどんをすする. Housing nouns pair with fixed verbs: 店・住居を構える, 橋を改修する, 家を補強する／再建する. Main exam traps: 構える (set up a home or shop — not 盛り付ける / 改築する), 本場 (genuine home of something — not 現場 / 本番 / 基礎), こってり ≈ しつこい, 吟味する ≈ 調べる (not 味わう).
A—B = antonyms (あっさりした—こってりした, 甘口—辛口) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (名産・特産, なめる・しゃぶる) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (米／包丁を研ぐ)
□ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. □味 → 本場の味, □匂い → こうばしい匂い)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

(1) あなたの得意な料理は何ですか。その作り方を説明してください。Anata no tokui na ryouri wa nan desu ka. Sono tsukurikata o setsumei shite kudasai.EN: What dish are you good at cooking? Please explain how to make it. · HI: आप कौन-सा खाना अच्छा बनाते हैं? उसे बनाने का तरीका समझाइए। · GU: તમે કઈ વાનગી સારી બનાવો છો? તે બનાવવાની રીત સમજાવો.

(2) 今、どんなところに住んでいますか。周囲にはどんなものがありますか。Ima, donna tokoro ni sunde imasu ka. Shuui ni wa donna mono ga arimasu ka.EN: What kind of place do you live in now? What is there around it? · HI: आप अभी कैसी जगह रहते हैं? आसपास क्या-क्या है? · GU: તમે હમણાં કેવી જગ્યાએ રહો છો? આસપાસ શું શું છે?

Sample answer (added, not in book): カレーが得意です。玉ねぎをじっくり炒めて、肉と野菜を煮込みます。駅から徒歩五分の、治安のいい地域に住んでいます。Karee ga tokui desu. Tamanegi o jikkuri itamete, niku to yasai o nikomimasu. Eki kara toho gofun no, chian no ii chiiki ni sunde imasu. — "I'm good at curry: I fry the onions slowly, then simmer the meat and vegetables. I live in a safe area five minutes' walk from the station." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

❹ 例文 (Reibun — example sentences; learn the bold adverbs too)

例1 · ★てきぱき

母はてきぱき手際よく料理する。Haha wa tekipaki tegiwa yoku ryouri suru.

EN: My mother cooks briskly and efficiently.
HI: माँ फुर्ती से और सलीके से खाना बनाती हैं।
GU: મમ્મી ઝડપથી અને કુશળતાથી રસોઈ કરે છે.
てきぱき tekipaki = briskly, efficiently (no wasted movement) · HI: फुर्ती से · GU: ઝડપથી, ચપળતાથી
例2 · ★じっくり

肉が軟らかくなるまでじっくり煮込む。Niku ga yawarakaku naru made jikkuri nikomu.

EN: Simmer the meat slowly until it becomes tender.
HI: मांस नरम होने तक धीमी आँच पर आराम से पकाइए।
GU: માંસ નરમ થાય ત્યાં સુધી ધીમે તાપે નિરાંતે રાંધો.
じっくり jikkuri = slowly and thoroughly, taking one's time · HI: आराम से, ध्यान से · GU: નિરાંતે, ધ્યાનથી
例3 · ★ずるずる

私はうどんやそばをずるずるすする音があまり好きじゃない。Watashi wa udon ya soba o zuruzuru susuru oto ga amari suki ja nai.

EN: I don't really like the sound of people noisily slurping udon or soba.
HI: मुझे उडोन या सोबा को सुड़-सुड़ करके खाने की आवाज़ ख़ास पसंद नहीं।
GU: મને ઉદોન કે સોબાને સબડ-સબડ અવાજે ખાવાનો અવાજ ખાસ ગમતો નથી.
ずるずる zuruzuru = (slurping sound); also dragging on (ずるずる延びる) · HI: सुड़-सुड़ · GU: સબડ-સબડ
例4 · ★一切

このインスタント食品は着色料や保存料が一切使用されていない。Kono insutanto shokuhin wa chakushokuryou ya hozonryou ga issai shiyou sarete inai.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

These groups come from this lesson's word map and its 実践練習 distractors. In English each group collapses into one word ("repair", "rebuild", "set up", "skill", "rich"), but in Japanese they are tested apart.

Exam trap: 構える and 本場 look easy because their kanji are basic. The 用法 options swap them in for verbs or nouns with a similar feel: 盛り付ける (arrange on a plate), 改築する (remodel), こだわる (be particular) for 構える; 現場, 本番, 基礎 for 本場. A quick test: 構える needs a building as its object (店・家・事務所). 本場 needs a place of origin (本場のイタリア料理).
