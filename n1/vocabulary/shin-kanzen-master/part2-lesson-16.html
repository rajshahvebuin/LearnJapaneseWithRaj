Notes
Learn affixes with the words they actually attach to. Key traps: 〜視 makes a する-verb "regard as" (問題視する) while 〜観 is a noun "outlook" (結婚観 — the warm-up answer). 就職難 describes society, not one person. 新鮮味 is freshness of ideas, not of fish. 人並み = average, プロ並み = pro level, 月並み = trite. 私生活 = private life, not "my life". 欲 is 強い, not 高い.
〜 = where the stem goes (〜団 = suffix, 誤〜 = prefix) · ／ = the words after it use the affix with a different meaning (素足／素早い) · ★ = printed in bold in the book
