Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.22–23 (PDF pages 216–217): warm-up and 基本練習 ❶–❸ on p.22, ❹ and 実践練習 on p.23. The key adds notes on the three 実践練習 3. 用法 items; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
The second verb sets the pattern: 〜込む = into, 〜出す = out / start, 〜渡る = all over, 〜立てる = loudly / showily, 〜直る = back to normal. Key splits: 付け込む (exploit a weakness) vs 突っ込む (thrust in, poke one's nose in) vs 食い込む (dig in; eat into time) vs 割り込む (cut in a queue); 溶け込む (blend in) vs 巻き込む (drag an outsider in) vs 呑み込む (understand); 立ち直る (recover) vs 開き直る (turn defiant). 行き渡る means reach everyone, not "arrive".
〜 = the first verb of the compound (〜込む = another verb + 込む) · [ ] = the book's explanation (we give it in English) · ／ = a second meaning with its own example
Each Note cell gives the book's example sentence(s) (JP + romaji + EN/HI/GU).
All headwords in 第2部 are printed in bold, so there is no separate ★ marking.
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

電車に乗るとき、してはいけないのは？…… （ 突っ込み　忍び込み　駆け込み ）乗車Densha ni noru toki, shite wa ikenai no wa? … (tsukkomi / shinobikomi / kakekomi) joushaEN: What must you not do when boarding a train? "(…) boarding." · HI: ट्रेन में चढ़ते समय क्या नहीं करना चाहिए? "(…) चढ़ना।" · GU: ટ્રેનમાં ચડતી વખતે શું ન કરવું? "(…) ચડવું."

Key (別冊 p.22): 駆け込みkakekomi — 駆け込み乗車 = dashing onto the train as the doors close, which station announcements always ask you not to do. 忍び込み is sneaking in, 突っ込み is thrusting in (our explanation).
