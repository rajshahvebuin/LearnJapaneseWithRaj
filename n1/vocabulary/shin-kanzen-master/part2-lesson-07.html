Answer key
Every answer below is from the official 別冊 解答 p.16 (PDF page 210), bound into this scan. The key adds notes to the warm-up, to 基本練習 ❶ (1) and to all five 実践練習 3 (用法) items; all other "Why" notes are ours.
Notes
Many of these adverbs carry a built-in attitude: はなはだ and いささか lean negative, すこぶる positive; いやに and やけに add "strangely"; ごく emphasises smallness or ordinariness (ごくわずか, ごく平凡) and can't go with たくさん. Time words split by direction: 先ごろ・先だって point to a recent past event, 追って・ぼつぼつ to the near future. Main exam traps: 始終 (all the time) vs 終始 (from start to finish, unchanged); とっさに is a reflex, not "quickly on purpose"; やや can't modify 急増.
[ ] = the book's definition of each word (translated in the English column) · ／ in a definition = two separate senses · A・B = two forms with the same meaning (年中・年がら年中) · (に／と) = optional particle
Every headword in this lesson is printed in bold in the book (all are N1 study words).

2海外の大学に入学することになったので、追って日本から離れます。Kaigai no daigaku ni nyuugaku suru koto ni natta node, otte Nihon kara hanaremasu.I'm entering a university abroad, so I'll leave Japan "in due course".विदेशी विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िला हुआ है, "बाद में" जापान छोड़ूँगा।વિદેશી યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ મળ્યો, "પછીથી" જાપાન છોડીશ.
3前回お手紙を書いてから、追ってご無沙汰をしておりました。Zenkai otegami o kaite kara, otte gobusata o shite orimashita.Since my last letter I have "in due course" been out of touch.पिछले पत्र के बाद "बाद में" संपर्क नहीं रहा।છેલ્લા પત્ર પછી "પછીથી" સંપર્ક નથી રહ્યો.
4試験の合格者には、追って合格通知を郵送します。Shiken no goukakusha ni wa, otte goukaku tsuuchi o yuusou shimasu.Successful candidates will be mailed a notice of acceptance in due course.परीक्षा में सफल उम्मीदवारों को बाद में सूचना-पत्र डाक से भेजा जाएगा।પરીક્ષામાં પાસ થનારને પછીથી પરિણામ-પત્ર ટપાલથી મોકલાશે.

Why: The key's note: 1 should be 次に, 2 間もなく, and 3 しばらく. 追って is a formal "we'll follow up later" used in notices. Key: 4.

Exam trap: these adverbs restrict what follows them. ごく can't go with たくさん; とびきり can't modify 普通; やや can't modify 急増; かねて／かねがね need a continuing verb (思っていた, not 思った); 長らく is only an adverb, so "( )の経験" needs 長年. Also watch the look-alikes: しばし (for a moment) vs しばしば (often), 始終 (constantly) vs 終始 (throughout), とっくに (long ago) vs とっさに (reflexively).
