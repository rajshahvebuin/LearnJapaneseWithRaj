Answer key

Notes
The first verb only hints at the meaning; learn each compound as one word. Key splits: 見過ごす (let something pass without caring) vs 見逃す (miss / deliberately let off) vs 見落とす (overlook by carelessness) vs 見抜く (see through) — the warm-up asks which teacher is kind (見逃す). 取り返す vs 取り戻す: both "get back", but 取り戻す also covers a normal state (落ち着きを取り戻す). 追い込む (drive into a corner) vs 追い詰める (leave no escape).
〜 = the first verb of the compound (言い〜 = 言う + another verb) · [ ] = the book's explanation (we give it in English) · ／ = a second meaning with its own example
Each Note cell gives the book's example sentence(s) (JP + romaji + EN/HI/GU).
All headwords in 第2部 are printed in bold, so there is no separate ★ marking.
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

どの先生が優しい？…… いたずらを（ 見過ごす　見逃す　見抜く ）先生Dono sensei ga yasashii? … Itazura o (misugosu / minogasu / minuku) senseiEN: Which teacher is kind? "A teacher who (misugosu / minogasu / minuku) pranks." · HI: कौन-सा टीचर दयालु है? "शरारत को (…) वाला टीचर।" · GU: કયા શિક્ષક દયાળુ છે? "તોફાનને (…) વાળા શિક્ષક."

Key (別冊 p.21): 見逃すminogasu — a teacher who notices the prank but lets it go. 見過ごす is not caring enough to act, and 見抜く is seeing through it (our explanation).
