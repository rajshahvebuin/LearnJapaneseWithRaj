Notes
Same verb, different body word: 腹をくくる (resolve) — never ×懐をくくる; 胸が詰まる (choked with emotion) vs 息が詰まる (suffocating tension); 気が引ける (feel bad asking) vs 身を引く (step down); 泥をかぶる (take the blame) vs 猫をかぶる (feign innocence). Main exam trap: 気が置けない means you can relax with someone (a good thing), not "untrustworthy". 紛らわす takes feelings (気・不安・悲しみ), not 心 or 涙 (key note).
〜 = the body word (腹, 胸, 気 …) · [ ] = the book's explanation of the meaning (we give it in English) · A／B = paired forms (胸を貸す／借りる, 懐が暖かい／寒い)

Key (別冊 p.21): 気が（合う／置けない）友人のことです。「親友」とは、（腹を）割って話せます。Ki ga (au / okenai) yuujin no koto desu. "Shin'yuu" to wa, (hara o) watte hanasemasu. — "A friend you get on with / can be at ease with; with a best friend you can speak frankly (腹を割る)."
