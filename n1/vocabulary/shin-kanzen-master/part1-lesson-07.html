Notes
Work verbs come with fixed particles: 仕事を手掛ける, 仕事がはかどる, 仕事に取り掛かる／打ち込む. Job titles are built with suffixes that must be learnt word by word: 〜士 (弁護士, 介護福祉士, 消防士), 〜師 (医師, 助産師, 看護師), 〜官 (検事 is not 〜官, but 裁判官 is), 〜家 (実業家, 投資家). Main exam traps: おろそか (neglect a duty — not おろか / 大らか), 転任 (move post inside one organisation — not 転職 / 転業 / 転戦), 赴く (go to a place — not はかどる / たずさわる), 昇進 (promotion — not 向上 / 上昇 / 上達).
A—B = antonyms (常勤—非常勤, 正規—非正規, 雇用する—解雇する) · A・B = shared meaning/usage (はかどる・順調に進む, 打ち込む・全力で取り組む, 赴任する・赴く, 不景気・不況) · A／B = separate expressions with a different meaning or usage (主任／係長／課長／次長／部長, 企画／開発／広報／経理／営業)
A→B = B is closely related to A (here: the short form, 介護福祉士→介護士) · □ = collocation slot: the words under it fill the box (e.g. 仕事を□ → 仕事を手掛ける)
★ = printed in bold in the book = a word to learn specifically for N1 (本書をお使いになる方へ p.vii)
Warm-up · ❶ ウォーミングアップ

(1) あなたはどんな仕事をしたいですか。今、どんな仕事をしていますか。Anata wa donna shigoto o shitai desu ka. Ima, donna shigoto o shite imasu ka.EN: What kind of work do you want to do? What work are you doing now? · HI: आप किस तरह का काम करना चाहते हैं? अभी आप क्या काम कर रहे हैं? · GU: તમે કેવું કામ કરવા માગો છો? અત્યારે તમે શું કામ કરો છો?

(2) あなたの国ではどんな仕事が人気がありますか。それはどうしてですか。Anata no kuni de wa donna shigoto ga ninki ga arimasu ka. Sore wa doushite desu ka.EN: What jobs are popular in your country? Why? · HI: आपके देश में कौन-से काम लोकप्रिय हैं? ऐसा क्यों है? · GU: તમારા દેશમાં કયાં કામ લોકપ્રિય છે? એવું કેમ છે?

Sample answer (added, not in book): 今はIT企業の開発部で正社員として働いていますが、将来は独立してコンサルタントになりたいです。インドでは、収入が安定しているので公務員やエンジニアが人気です。Ima wa IT kigyou no kaihatsubu de seishain to shite hataraite imasu ga, shourai wa dokuritsu shite konsarutanto ni naritai desu. Indo de wa, shuunyuu ga antei shite iru node koumuin ya enjinia ga ninki desu. — "I now work as a full-time employee in the development department of an IT company, but in future I want to go independent and become a consultant. In India, civil servants and engineers are popular because the income is stable." The book's questions are personal, so the key gives no answer.

2会社を辞めて、田舎で農業に転任することにした。Kaisha o yamete, inaka de nougyou ni tennin suru koto ni shita.I decided to quit my company and "transfer" to farming in the countryside.मैंने कंपनी छोड़कर गाँव में खेती में "तबादला" करने का फ़ैसला किया।મેં કંપની છોડીને ગામમાં ખેતીમાં "બદલી" કરવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
3その野球チームは、大阪、神戸、広島と転任して、試合を続けた。Sono yakyuu chiimu wa, Oosaka, Koube, Hiroshima to tennin shite, shiai o tsuzuketa.The baseball team "transferred" from Osaka to Kobe to Hiroshima, playing games as it went.वह बेसबॉल टीम ओसाका, कोबे, हिरोशिमा में "तबादला" करते हुए मैच खेलती रही।એ બેઝબૉલ ટીમ ઓસાકા, કોબે, હિરોશિમામાં "બદલી" કરતી મૅચ રમતી રહી.
4営業部への転任をずっと希望していたが、それがようやく実現した。Eigyoubu e no tennin o zutto kibou shite ita ga, sore ga youyaku jitsugen shita.I had long hoped for a transfer to the sales department, and it finally happened.मैं लंबे समय से बिक्री विभाग में तबादला चाहता था, आख़िरकार वह हो गया।હું લાંબા સમયથી વેચાણ વિભાગમાં બદલી ઇચ્છતો હતો, આખરે તે થઈ ગઈ.

Why: The key's note: 転任 means your place of work or duties change within the same organisation. When you change the job itself, as in 1 and 2, use 転職 or 転業. When a team moves from place to place to fight, as in 3, use 転戦. Key: 4.

Exam trap: The 転〜 family all mean "change", so 用法 questions mix them. Ask what changes. A post inside the same organisation is 転任. A company or job is 転職. A trade is 転業. A battlefield is 転戦. Also watch the look-alike おろそか (neglect a duty: 勉強をおろそかにする) and おろか (foolish): only おろそか takes 〜にする with a task as its object.
