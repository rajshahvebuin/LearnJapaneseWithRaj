Source
新完全マスター語彙 日本語能力試験N1 (Shin Kanzen Master N1 Goi, スリーエーネットワーク) — printed p.168–169 (PDF pages 175–176): p.168 = 1. 文脈規定 (7 items) + 2. 言い換え類義 (6 items); p.169 = 3. 用法 (6 items). 50 points in all.
Question types
1. 文脈規定 (2点×7)2. 言い換え類義 (3点×6)3. 用法 (3点×6)
Answer key
All 19 answers are from the official 別冊 解答 p.23–24 (PDF pages 217–218). The key for this test is an answer grid only (no * notes), so every "Why" note on this page is our own explanation.
Notes
Traps in this test: 改築 (pull down and rebuild) vs 改修/修繕 (repair) vs 改装 (refit the inside); 支給 (hand out to staff) vs 供給/配給; the sound-alike distractors in 用法 — 要請 vs 養成, となえる vs 途絶える/整える/たとえる, 余地 vs 余儀, 緩和 vs 温和/緩慢; and ひときわ (stands out among others) vs ひとしお / 一段と / ひとまず.
1. 文脈規定 — choose the word that best fits ( ) · 2. 言い換え類義 — choose the word closest in meaning to the underlined word · 3. 用法 — choose the sentence that uses the word correctly
1-1, 2-3 … = section number + the book's boxed item number · 1–4 = the book's option numbers · the correct row is highlighted
In 用法 items, ✗ in a translation marks where the word is misused; the "should be" words in the Why notes are ours (the key gives no notes for this test).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

1. (p.168) —（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。 (2点×7問)

( ) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.
EN: Choose the best option for ( ). · HI: ( ) के लिए सबसे सही विकल्प चुनिए। · GU: ( ) માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો.

1-1

古い木造校舎を（　）して、鉄筋コンクリートの新校舎を建てた。Furui mokuzou kousha o ( ) shite, tekkin konkuriito no shin-kousha o tateta.

Answer: 4 改築
Full sentence: 古い木造校舎を改築して、…Furui mokuzou kousha o kaichiku shite, …
EN: They rebuilt the old wooden school building and put up a new one in reinforced concrete.
HI: पुरानी लकड़ी की स्कूल इमारत का पुनर्निर्माण करके, सरिया-कंक्रीट की नई इमारत बनाई गई।
GU: જૂની લાકડાની શાળાની ઇમારતનું પુનઃનિર્માણ કરીને, સળિયા-કૉંક્રીટની નવી ઇમારત બનાવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1修繕shuuzenrepairमरम्मतસમારકામ
2改修kaishuurenovation, repair workजीर्णोद्धारજીર્ણોદ્ધાર
3改装kaisourefurbishment (interior/look)साज-सज्जा बदलनाસજાવટ બદલવી
4改築kaichikurebuildingपुनर्निर्माणપુનઃનિર્માણ

Why: The old building is replaced by a new concrete one, so the structure itself is rebuilt — 改築. 修繕・改修 fix what is there, 改装 changes the inside or appearance. Key: 別冊 p.23.

Full sentence: この料理は当ホテルならではの名物料理です。Kono ryouri wa tou-hoteru nara de wa no meibutsu ryouri desu.
EN: This dish is a speciality you can only get at this hotel.
HI: यह व्यंजन केवल हमारे इस होटल की ख़ास पहचान है।
GU: આ વાનગી ફક્ત અમારી આ હોટેલની ખાસિયત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1自jiself- (自社 = own company)स्वयं काપોતાનું
2当touthis, our (当店, 当ホテル)यह, हमाराઆ, અમારું

2. (p.168) — ＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。 (3点×6問)
