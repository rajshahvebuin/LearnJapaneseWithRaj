Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions and an answer key, but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 目標解答時間 10分, 20 points — 問題1 漢字読み ×5 (1点), 問題2 言い換え類義 ×5 (1点), 問題3 文脈規定 ×6 (1点), 問題4 用法 ×2 (2点). The scan has a previous owner's handwritten circles and notes on it; they were ignored. Traps this round: 周知 is しゅうち, not しょうち (承知); 手際 is a 訓読み compound (て＋ぎわ), not さい; 発足 is used only for organisations.

問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

ヘアスタイルを変えたら、華やかな印象になった。Hea sutairu o kaetara, hanayaka na inshou ni natta.

EN: When I changed my hairstyle, I came across as more glamorous.
HI: हेयरस्टाइल बदलने पर (मेरा) रूप ज़्यादा शानदार लगने लगा।
GU: હેરસ્ટાઇલ બદલી તો (મારો) દેખાવ વધુ શાનદાર લાગવા લાગ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あざやか（鮮やか）azayakavivid, bright (colours); skilfulचटकीला, स्पष्टચટકદાર, સ્પષ્ટ
2あでやか（艶やか）adeyakabewitchingly beautiful (of a woman)मोहक, लुभावनाમોહક, લોભામણું
3はなやか（華やか）hanayakagorgeous, glamorous, showyशानदार, रौनकदारશાનદાર, રોનકદાર
4はれやか（晴れやか）hareyakabright, cheerful (face, mood, sky)प्रसन्न, खिला हुआપ્રસન્ન, ખીલેલું

Why: 華 here is 訓読み はな ("flower, splendour") → はなやか. All four options are real 〜やか adjectives, so the question is purely which kanji: 鮮 = あざ(やか), 艶 = あで(やか)／つや, 晴 = は(れやか). In 音読み 華 is か (豪華 gouka, 中華 chuuka).

2こんなにうるさい音楽が好きな人の気がしれない。Konna ni urusai ongaku ga suki na hito no ki ga shirenai.I can't understand people who like such noisy music.मेरी समझ में नहीं आता कि लोगों को इतना शोर वाला संगीत कैसे पसंद आता है।મને સમજાતું નથી કે લોકોને આટલું ઘોંઘાટવાળું સંગીત કેમ ગમે છે.
3サービスの悪い店には文句を言わないと気がしれない。Saabisu no warui mise ni wa monku o iwanai to ki ga shirenai.(Intended: I'm not satisfied until I complain to a shop with bad service.)(इरादा: ख़राब सेवा वाली दुकान से शिकायत किए बिना मुझे चैन नहीं आता।)(ઇરાદો: ખરાબ સેવાવાળી દુકાનને ફરિયાદ કર્યા વગર મને ચેન નથી પડતું.)
4誕生日のプレゼントを用意していないなんて、気がしれない人だね。Tanjoubi no purezento o youi shite inai nante, ki ga shirenai hito da ne.(Intended: Not even getting a birthday present ready — what a thoughtless person.)(इरादा: जन्मदिन का तोहफ़ा भी तैयार नहीं किया — कितना बेध्यान इंसान है।)(ઇરાદો: જન્મદિવસની ભેટ પણ તૈયાર નથી કરી — કેવો બેધ્યાન માણસ છે.)

Why: 〜の気がしれない = "I can't fathom what (that person) is thinking". It is always about someone else's mindset, usually critical, in the pattern 〜人の気がしれない. The 別冊 gives the expressions the other sentences need: 1 → 気が乗らない／気が進まない (don't feel like it), 3 → 気が済まない (not satisfied until…), 4 → 気が利かない (thoughtless, not attentive).

Exam trap 1 — 漢字読み distractors are real words: in Q1 all four 〜やか options exist, and in Q3 しょうち (承知) is a real word close in meaning to 周知. Don't choose a reading just because it sounds like a word you know. Check the kanji: 周 = しゅう (long vowel, and しゅ not しょ), 華 = はな.
Exam trap 2 — 訓読み compounds and rendaku: 手際 (て＋きわ → てぎわ) and 取り扱い are read with native readings, so 音読み options (さい, しゅ) are the trap. Words like 手際, 手抜き, 手落ち, 後回し come up again and again in this book. Learn them as whole words.
Exam trap 3 — 用法 tests the subject: in 問題4 the word is often grammatically fine in every sentence, but only one has the right kind of subject (発足 → an organisation; 気がしれない → another person's mind). Ask "who or what is doing this?" before you look at meaning.
