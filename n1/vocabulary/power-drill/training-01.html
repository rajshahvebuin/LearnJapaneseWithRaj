Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is noun + verb collocations: both verbs are common words, and only one is the fixed partner of the noun. A previous owner circled and crossed out options and wrote glosses on this page; all of it was ignored. Biggest traps: 勝負がつく and 利息がつく both take つく, but 期限が切れる, not なくなる.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

美術館はこの絵に3億円の保険を（ a いれた ／ b かけた ）そうだ。Bijutsukan wa kono e ni san-oku-en no hoken o (a ireta / b kaketa) sou da.

Full sentence: 美術館はこの絵に3億円の保険をかけたそうだ。Bijutsukan wa kono e ni san-oku-en no hoken o kaketa sou da.
EN: I hear the museum has insured this painting for 300 million yen.
HI: सुना है संग्रहालय ने इस चित्र का 30 करोड़ येन का बीमा करवाया है।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે સંગ્રહાલયે આ ચિત્રનો 30 કરોડ યેનનો વીમો ઉતરાવ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいれたiretaput in — not used with 保険をडाला — 保険を के साथ नहींનાખ્યું — 保険を સાથે નહીં
bかけたkaketainsured (保険をかける)बीमा करवायाવીમો ઉતરાવ્યો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): The fixed collocation is 〜に保険をかける "insure something". If you are the one joining a policy, you say 保険に入る (intransitive, with に). 保険をいれる mixes the two and is not used.

Q2

もうすぐパスポートの有効期限が（ a きれる ／ b なくなる ）。Mou sugu pasupooto no yuukou kigen ga (a kireru / b nakunaru).

Full sentence: もうすぐパスポートの有効期限が切れる。Mou sugu pasupooto no yuukou kigen ga kireru.
EN: My passport is about to expire.
HI: मेरे पासपोर्ट की वैधता जल्द ही ख़त्म होने वाली है।
GU: મારા પાસપોર્ટની મુદત ટૂંક સમયમાં પૂરી થવાની છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aきれる（切れる）kireruexpireअवधि ख़त्म होनाમુદત પૂરી થવી
bなくなるnakunarurun out, be lostख़त्म हो जाना, खो जानाખૂટી જવું, ખોવાઈ જવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A deadline or validity period "runs out" with 切れる: 期限／契約／賞味期限が切れる. なくなる is for things that get used up or lost (お金がなくなる), not for a date passing.

Q3

家に入る前に、靴の汚れを（ a おとした ／ b はずした ）。Ie ni hairu mae ni, kutsu no yogore o (a otoshita / b hazushita).

Full sentence: 家に入る前に、靴の汚れを落とした。Ie ni hairu mae ni, kutsu no yogore o otoshita.
EN: Before going into the house, I cleaned the dirt off my shoes.
HI: घर में घुसने से पहले मैंने जूतों की गंदगी साफ़ की।
GU: ઘરમાં પ્રવેશતાં પહેલાં મેં જોડાંનો મેલ સાફ કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおとした（落とした）otoshitaremoved (dirt)साफ़ कीસાફ કર્યો
bはずした（外した）hazushitatook off (something attached)उताराઉતાર્યું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Dirt, stains and make-up are "dropped" off: 汚れ／化粧／泥を落とす. 外す is for objects fastened on (眼鏡／ボタン／席を外す).

Q4

3時間にわたる熱戦の末、ようやく勝負が（ a きいた ／ b ついた ）。Sanjikan ni wataru nessen no sue, youyaku shoubu ga (a kiita / b tsuita).

Full sentence: 3時間にわたる熱戦の末、ようやく勝負がついた。Sanjikan ni wataru nessen no sue, youyaku shoubu ga tsuita.
EN: After a fierce three-hour battle, the match was finally decided.
HI: तीन घंटे चली कड़ी टक्कर के बाद आख़िरकार हार-जीत का फ़ैसला हुआ।
GU: ત્રણ કલાક ચાલેલી જોરદાર ટક્કર પછી આખરે હારજીતનો ફેંસલો થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aきいた（効いた）kiitatook effectअसर कियाઅસર કરી
bついたtsuitawas decided (勝負がつく)फ़ैसला हुआફેંસલો થયો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A contest being settled = 勝負／決着／けりがつく. きく ("take effect") goes with medicine or a strategy (作戦が効いた), not with the match itself.

Q5

金利が低いので、貯金をしてもほとんど利息が（ a きかない ／ b つかない ）。Kinri ga hikui node, chokin o shite mo hotondo risoku ga (a kikanai / b tsukanai).

Full sentence: 金利が低いので、貯金をしてもほとんど利息がつかない。Kinri ga hikui node, chokin o shite mo hotondo risoku ga tsukanai.
EN: Interest rates are low, so even if you save you earn almost no interest.
HI: ब्याज दरें कम हैं, इसलिए बचत करने पर भी लगभग कोई ब्याज नहीं मिलता।
GU: વ્યાજદર ઓછા છે, એટલે બચત કરવા છતાં લગભગ કોઈ વ્યાજ મળતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aきかないkikanaidoesn't work / take effectअसर नहीं करताઅસર કરતું નથી
bつかないtsukanaidoesn't accrue (利息がつく)(ब्याज) नहीं जुड़ता(વ્યાજ) ઉમેરાતું નથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Interest is "attached" to your savings: 利息／利子がつく. Same verb as Q4, but here つく means "be added", not "be settled".

Q6

水で冷やしたら、少し痛みが（ a とんで ／ b ひいて ）きた。Mizu de hiyashitara, sukoshi itami ga (a tonde / b hiite) kita.

Full sentence: 水で冷やしたら、少し痛みがひいてきた。Mizu de hiyashitara, sukoshi itami ga hiite kita.
EN: When I cooled it with water, the pain eased a little.
HI: पानी से ठंडा करने पर दर्द थोड़ा कम हो गया।
GU: પાણીથી ઠંડું કર્યું તો દુખાવો થોડો ઓછો થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとんで（飛んで）tondeflyingउड़करઊડીને
bひいて（引いて）hiitesubsiding (痛みが引く)कम होकरઓછું થઈને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Pain, fever, swelling and floodwater "draw back": 痛み／熱／腫れ／水が引く. 〜てきた adds "gradually". 飛ぶ appears only in the children's charm 痛いの痛いの飛んでいけ, not in normal description.

Q7

家庭を（ a つくる ／ b もつ ）と責任感がわいてくる。Katei o (a tsukuru / b motsu) to sekininkan ga waite kuru.

Full sentence: 家庭をもつと責任感がわいてくる。Katei o motsu to sekininkan ga waite kuru.
EN: Once you have a family of your own, a sense of responsibility develops.
HI: अपना घर-परिवार बसाने पर ज़िम्मेदारी का एहसास पैदा होता है।
GU: પોતાનો ઘરસંસાર માંડો એટલે જવાબદારીની ભાવના જાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aつくるtsukurumake, buildबनानाબનાવવું
bもつ（持つ）motsuhave (家庭を持つ = have one's own family)(घर-परिवार) बसाना(ઘરસંસાર) માંડવો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 家庭を持つ is the set phrase for marrying and having your own household; the condition 〜と (whenever / once) describes that life change. 家庭をつくる does exist ("create a [happy] home", e.g. 明るい家庭をつくる), but it describes building the atmosphere of a home, so b is the better fit for "once you have a family".

Q8

社長にお酒を（ a ついで ／ b つけて ）いただいて恐縮した。Shachou ni osake o (a tsuide / b tsukete) itadaite kyoushuku shita.

Full sentence: 社長にお酒をついでいただいて恐縮した。Shachou ni osake o tsuide itadaite kyoushuku shita.
EN: I felt very humbled when the company president poured sake for me.
HI: जब अध्यक्ष ने ख़ुद मेरे लिए साके डाली तो मैं बहुत संकोच में पड़ गया।
GU: પ્રમુખે જાતે મારા માટે સાકે રેડી ત્યારે મને ખૂબ સંકોચ થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aついで（注いで）tsuidepouring (つぐ)डालकरરેડીને
bつけてtsuketeattaching; warming (sake)लगाकर; गर्म करकेલગાવીને; ગરમ કરીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Pouring a drink into someone's glass = 注ぐ (つぐ), te-form ついで — easy to confuse with ついで "on the way" (次いで). お酒をつける means heating sake (熱燗をつける), not pouring it into your glass.

Q10

Full sentence: この薬は、食後しばらく時間をおいてから飲んでください。Kono kusuri wa, shokugo shibaraku jikan o oite kara nonde kudasai.
EN: Please take this medicine a little while after eating.
HI: यह दवा खाने के कुछ देर बाद लें।
GU: આ દવા જમ્યા પછી થોડી વાર રહીને લો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおいて（置いて）oiteleaving an intervalअंतर रखकरઅંતર રાખીને
bかけてkaketespending (time on something)(समय) लगाकर(સમય) આપીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 時間をおく／間をおく = let some time pass before doing the next thing. 時間をかける is spending a long time on an activity (時間をかけて読む) — nothing is being done here, you are just waiting. (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 a」 agrees.)

Exam trap 1 — translate the collocation, not the verb: English "expire", "decide", "ease" each need a different Japanese verb that you can't guess from the noun: 期限が切れる, 勝負がつく, 痛みが引く. Learn noun + verb as one block.
Exam trap 2 — same verb, different meaning: つく in Q4 and Q5, and 引く in Q6 and Q9, look like "the same answer twice". That is deliberate: a basic verb has many meanings, each tied to particular nouns.
