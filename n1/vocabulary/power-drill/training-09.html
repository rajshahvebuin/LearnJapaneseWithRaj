Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is 擬音語・擬態語 (3): every word on this page has the shape Xっと (かっと, きっと, ぐっと …). The book prints furigana over the title words 擬音語 (ぎおんご) and 擬態語 (ぎたいご).

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

つい（ a はっと ／ b かっと ）なって、机をたたいてしまった。Tsui (a hatto / b katto) natte, tsukue o tataite shimatta.

Full sentence: ついかっとなって、机をたたいてしまった。Tsui katto natte, tsukue o tataite shimatta.
EN: I lost my temper and banged the desk before I knew it.
HI: मैं अचानक भड़क गया और मेज़ पर हाथ पटक दिया।
GU: હું અચાનક ભડકી ગયો અને ટેબલ પર હાથ પછાડી દીધો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aはっとhattowith a start, suddenly realizingचौंककरચોંકીને
bかっとkattoflaring up in angerग़ुस्से से भड़ककरગુસ્સાથી ભડકીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): かっとなる = flare up in sudden anger — the reason you bang the desk. はっとする is a sudden realization or start (はっと気づく), and it takes する, not なる.

Q2

その子は怒ったような顔で（ a きっと ／ b ざっと ）私をにらんだ。Sono ko wa okotta you na kao de (a kitto / b zatto) watashi o niranda.

Full sentence: その子は怒ったような顔できっと私をにらんだ。Sono ko wa okotta you na kao de kitto watashi o niranda.
EN: The child glared at me sharply with an angry look.
HI: उस बच्चे ने ग़ुस्से भरे चेहरे से मुझे तीखी नज़र से घूरा।
GU: તે બાળકે ગુસ્સાભર્યા ચહેરે મને તીખી નજરે ઘૂર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aきっとkittosharply, sternly (look)तीखी नज़र सेતીખી નજરે
bざっとzattoroughly, brieflyमोटे तौर परઆશરે, ઉપરછલ્લું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Besides "surely", きっと describes a sharp, severe look: きっとにらむ／きっと見すえる. ざっと is a quick rough pass (ざっと目を通す, ざっと100人) — not a glare.

Q3

涙が出そうになるのを（ a ぐっと ／ b ぱっと ）こらえた。Namida ga desou ni naru no o (a gutto / b patto) koraeta.

Full sentence: 涙が出そうになるのをぐっとこらえた。Namida ga desou ni naru no o gutto koraeta.
EN: I held back the tears that were about to come.
HI: उमड़ते आँसुओं को मैंने कसकर रोक लिया।
GU: ઊભરાતાં આંસુને મેં મક્કમતાથી રોકી રાખ્યાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aぐっとguttofirmly, with effortकसकर, ज़ोर लगाकरમક્કમતાથી, જોર કરીને
bぱっとpattosuddenly, in a flashझट सेઝટ દઈને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ぐっとこらえる／我慢する = hold something back with inner effort. ぱっと is sudden and open (ぱっと明るくなる) — the opposite of holding in.

Q4

彼女は私が隣に座ると、（ a ほっと ／ b さっと ）席を立ってしまった。Kanojo wa watashi ga tonari ni suwaru to, (a hotto / b satto) seki o tatte shimatta.

Full sentence: 彼女は私が隣に座ると、さっと席を立ってしまった。Kanojo wa watashi ga tonari ni suwaru to, satto seki o tatte shimatta.
EN: When I sat down next to her, she quickly got up and left her seat.
HI: मैं उसके पास बैठा तो वह झट से उठकर चली गई।
GU: હું તેની બાજુમાં બેઠો તો તે ઝટ દઈને ઊઠી ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aほっとhottorelievedराहत सेરાહતથી
bさっとsattoquickly, swiftlyफुर्ती से, झट सेફટાફટ, ઝટ દઈને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): さっと = a quick, smooth movement (さっと立つ／さっと片付ける). ほっとする is relief and is a feeling, not a way of standing up.

Q5

セール初日とあって、開店と同時に客が（ a じっと ／ b どっと ）押し寄せた。Seeru shonichi to atte, kaiten to douji ni kyaku ga (a jitto / b dotto) oshiyoseta.

Full sentence: セール初日とあって、開店と同時に客がどっと押し寄せた。Seeru shonichi to atte, kaiten to douji ni kyaku ga dotto oshiyoseta.
EN: It being the first day of the sale, customers poured in the moment the shop opened.
HI: सेल का पहला दिन होने से दुकान खुलते ही ग्राहक एकदम उमड़ पड़े।
GU: સેલનો પહેલો દિવસ હોવાથી દુકાન ખૂલતાં જ ગ્રાહકો એકસાથે ઊમટી પડ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aじっとjittostill, motionless; fixedlyस्थिर, टकटकी लगाकरસ્થિર, તાકીને
bどっとdottoall at once, in a rush (crowds, laughter)एकदम, भरभराकरએકસાથે, ધસમસતું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): どっと = a large mass arriving at once: 客がどっと押し寄せる, どっと笑う, 疲れがどっと出る. じっと is the opposite — not moving at all. (押し寄せる was also tested in 第24回 問題3 Q5.)

Q6

その人は何も言わず（ a すっと ／ b わっと ）メモを差し出した。Sono hito wa nani mo iwazu (a sutto / b watto) memo o sashidashita.

Full sentence: その人は何も言わずすっとメモを差し出した。Sono hito wa nani mo iwazu sutto memo o sashidashita.
EN: Without a word, the person quietly held out a note.
HI: उस व्यक्ति ने कुछ कहे बिना चुपचाप एक पर्ची आगे बढ़ा दी।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિએ કંઈ બોલ્યા વગર ચૂપચાપ એક ચિઠ્ઠી આગળ ધરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aすっとsuttosmoothly, quietly; refreshedसहजता से, चुपचापસહજતાથી, ચૂપચાપ
bわっとwattowith a sudden burst (cry, cheer)एकदम ज़ोर से (रोना, शोर)એકદમ જોરથી (રડવું, બૂમ)

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): すっと = a smooth, silent movement (すっと手を出す／すっと消える) — matching 何も言わず. わっと is a loud sudden outburst (わっと泣き出す), which clashes with the silence.

Q7

一人で考えたいので（ a そっと ／ b ふっと ）しておいてください。Hitori de kangaetai node (a sotto / b futto) shite oite kudasai.

Full sentence: 一人で考えたいのでそっとしておいてください。Hitori de kangaetai node sotto shite oite kudasai.
EN: I want to think on my own, so please leave me alone.
HI: मैं अकेले सोचना चाहता हूँ, इसलिए कृपया मुझे अकेला छोड़ दीजिए।
GU: મારે એકલા વિચારવું છે, એટલે કૃપા કરીને મને એકલો રહેવા દો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそっとsottogently, quietly; leave aloneधीरे से; अकेला छोड़नाહળવેથી; એકલું રહેવા દેવું
bふっとfuttosuddenly (a thought), with a puffअचानक (ख़याल); फूँक सेઅચાનક (વિચાર); ફૂંકથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): そっとしておく is a fixed phrase: leave someone undisturbed. ふっと is a thought that suddenly comes (ふっと思い出す) or a puff of breath.

Q8

もし今、大地震が来たらと思うと、（ a ぞっと ／ b ぽっと ）する。Moshi ima, daijishin ga kitara to omou to, (a zotto / b potto) suru.

Full sentence: もし今、大地震が来たらと思うと、ぞっとする。Moshi ima, daijishin ga kitara to omou to, zotto suru.
EN: It sends a shiver down my spine to think what would happen if a big earthquake hit now.
HI: अगर अभी बड़ा भूकंप आ जाए, यह सोचकर ही रोंगटे खड़े हो जाते हैं।
GU: જો અત્યારે મોટો ભૂકંપ આવે તો, એ વિચારથી જ રૂંવાડાં ઊભાં થઈ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aぞっとzottoshuddering with fearसिहरकर, रोंगटे खड़े होनाકંપારી, રૂંવાડાં ઊભાં થવાં
bぽっとpottoblushing; (light) coming onशर्म से लाल होनाશરમથી લાલ થવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ぞっとする = shudder with fear or disgust — the natural reaction to imagining a big earthquake. ぽっと is a soft glow or blush (顔がぽっと赤くなる).

Q9

プレゼントを見た彼女は（ a ぱっと ／ b ぺっと ）表情を明るくした。Purezento o mita kanojo wa (a patto / b petto) hyoujou o akaruku shita.

Full sentence: プレゼントを見た彼女はぱっと表情を明るくした。Purezento o mita kanojo wa patto hyoujou o akaruku shita.
EN: When she saw the present, her face instantly lit up.
HI: तोहफ़ा देखते ही उसका चेहरा झट से खिल उठा।
GU: ભેટ જોતાં જ તેનો ચહેરો ઝટ દઈને ખીલી ઊઠ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aぱっとpattosuddenly, in a flash (brightening)झट से, एकदमઝટ દઈને, એકદમ
bぺっとpettospitting outथूकते हुएથૂંકતાં

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ぱっと = a sudden, bright change: ぱっと明るくなる／ぱっと顔が輝く. ぺっと is the sound of spitting (ぺっと吐き出す). (The book's page-foot answer fragment 「…9 a」 is readable and agrees.)

Q10

彼の冗談があまりにおかしくて、先生も（ a ぽっと ／ b ぷっと ）吹き出した。Kare no joudan ga amari ni okashikute, sensei mo (a potto / b putto) fukidashita.

Exam trap 1 — the Xっと family: every word on this page has the shape Xっと. Learn each one with its usual verb: かっとなる, ほっとする, ぞっとする, そっとしておく.
Exam trap 2 — きっと has two lives: the everyday "surely, definitely" (きっと来る) and the N1 mimetic "sharply, sternly" (きっとにらむ). When it sits before a verb of looking, it's the second.
Exam trap 3 — loud vs silent pairs: どっと (crowd rushing) vs じっと (motionless); わっと (outburst) vs すっと (silent). One word in the sentence — 押し寄せた, 何も言わず — tells you which half you need.
