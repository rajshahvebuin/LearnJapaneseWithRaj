Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is katakana loanwords for business, society and media. Several pairs differ by only one or two sounds (インフラ／インフレ, コンセプト／コンセント, プライド／ブランド), and others are near in meaning (リラックス／カジュアル, アピール／アプローチ), so read each one carefully. インフラ／インフレ also appeared in 第15回 問題3 Q6.

Q1

この地域は、水道・電気・ガスなどの（ a インフラ ／ b インフレ ）の整備が遅れている。Kono chiiki wa, suidou, denki, gasu nado no (a infura / b infure) no seibi ga okurete iru.

Full sentence: この地域は、水道・電気・ガスなどのインフラの整備が遅れている。Kono chiiki wa, suidou, denki, gasu nado no infura no seibi ga okurete iru.
EN: This area is behind in developing infrastructure such as water, electricity and gas.
HI: यह इलाक़ा पानी, बिजली, गैस जैसे बुनियादी ढाँचे के विकास में पिछड़ा हुआ है।
GU: આ વિસ્તાર પાણી, વીજળી, ગેસ જેવી માળખાકીય સુવિધાઓના વિકાસમાં પાછળ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aインフラinfurainfrastructureबुनियादी ढाँचाમાળખાકીય સુવિધા
bインフレinfureinflationमुद्रास्फीतिફુગાવો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Water, power and gas are インフラ (infrastructure), and インフラの整備 "building/maintaining infrastructure" is the set phrase. インフレ (inflation) is about prices and can't be 整備される.

Q2

結果は大切だが、結果までの（ a サクセス ／ b プロセス ）はもっと大切である。Kekka wa taisetsu da ga, kekka made no (a sakusesu / b purosesu) wa motto taisetsu de aru.

Full sentence: 結果は大切だが、結果までのプロセスはもっと大切である。Kekka wa taisetsu da ga, kekka made no purosesu wa motto taisetsu de aru.
EN: Results matter, but the process of getting there matters even more.
HI: परिणाम ज़रूरी है, पर परिणाम तक पहुँचने की प्रक्रिया उससे भी ज़्यादा ज़रूरी है।
GU: પરિણામ મહત્ત્વનું છે, પણ પરિણામ સુધી પહોંચવાની પ્રક્રિયા એથીય વધુ મહત્ત્વની છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aサクセスsakusesusuccessसफलताસફળતા
bプロセスpurosesuprocessप्रक्रियाપ્રક્રિયા

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 結果までの (up to the result) = the steps that lead to it = プロセス. サクセス is itself a kind of result, so "the success up to the result" makes no sense.

Q3

この仕事を進めるには、会社全体の（ a サポート ／ b サプライズ ）が必要だ。Kono shigoto o susumeru ni wa, kaisha zentai no (a sapooto / b sapuraizu) ga hitsuyou da.

Full sentence: この仕事を進めるには、会社全体のサポートが必要だ。Kono shigoto o susumeru ni wa, kaisha zentai no sapooto ga hitsuyou da.
EN: To move this work forward, we need the support of the whole company.
HI: इस काम को आगे बढ़ाने के लिए पूरी कंपनी का सहयोग ज़रूरी है।
GU: આ કામ આગળ વધારવા માટે આખી કંપનીનો સહયોગ જરૂરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aサポートsapootosupportसहयोगસહયોગ
bサプライズsapuraizusurpriseसरप्राइज़સરપ્રાઇઝ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): What you need from the whole company to get work done is サポート (help, backing). サプライズ is a pleasant surprise (サプライズプレゼント) and only shares the opening sounds.

Q4

私は自分の仕事に（ a プライド ／ b ブランド ）を持っている。Watashi wa jibun no shigoto ni (a puraido / b burando) o motte iru.

Full sentence: 私は自分の仕事にプライドを持っている。Watashi wa jibun no shigoto ni puraido o motte iru.
EN: I take pride in my work.
HI: मुझे अपने काम पर गर्व है।
GU: મને મારા કામ પર ગર્વ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aプライドpuraidoprideगर्वગર્વ
bブランドburandobrandब्रांडબ્રાન્ડ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 〜にプライドを持つ = take pride in something (same as 誇りを持つ). ブランド is a brand name or label (ブランドのバッグ); you don't "hold a brand in your work".

Q5

明日は気楽な集まりなので、（ a リラックス ／ b カジュアル ）な服装で来てください。Ashita wa kiraku na atsumari na node, (a rirakkusu / b kajuaru) na fukusou de kite kudasai.

Full sentence: 明日は気楽な集まりなので、カジュアルな服装で来てください。Ashita wa kiraku na atsumari na node, kajuaru na fukusou de kite kudasai.
EN: Tomorrow is an informal get-together, so please come in casual clothes.
HI: कल की बैठक अनौपचारिक है, इसलिए कृपया साधारण कपड़ों में आइए।
GU: કાલનું મિલન અનૌપચારિક છે, એટલે કૃપા કરીને સાદાં કપડાંમાં આવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aリラックスrirakkusurelaxationआरामઆરામ
bカジュアルkajuarucasualअनौपचारिकઅનૌપચારિક

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Clothes are described as カジュアルな服装 (⇔ フォーマルな服装). リラックス is a noun/する-verb — リラックスした雰囲気 is fine, but リラックスな服装 is not Japanese. The な after the blank decides it.

Q6

面接では、自分の長所をうまく（ a アピール ／ b アプローチ ）できなかった。Mensetsu de wa, jibun no chousho o umaku (a apiiru / b apuroochi) dekinakatta.

Full sentence: 面接では、自分の長所をうまくアピールできなかった。Mensetsu de wa, jibun no chousho o umaku apiiru dekinakatta.
EN: In the interview I couldn't get my strengths across well.
HI: इंटरव्यू में मैं अपनी ख़ूबियाँ ठीक से नहीं दिखा पाया।
GU: ઇન્ટરવ્યૂમાં હું મારી ખૂબીઓ બરાબર બતાવી ન શક્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aアピールapiiruappeal, present (oneself) wellअपनी ख़ूबी दिखानाપોતાની ખૂબી બતાવવી
bアプローチapuroochiapproachदृष्टिकोण; पहल करनाઅભિગમ; પહેલ કરવી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): In Japanese, アピールする means presenting your good points to others — the key word in job interviews (自己アピール). アプローチ is approaching a problem or a person (彼女にアプローチする); 長所をアプローチする doesn't work.

Q7

この新型車の開発（ a コンセプト ／ b コンセント ）は、「環境にやさしい」ということです。Kono shingatasha no kaihatsu (a konseputo / b konsento) wa, "kankyou ni yasashii" to iu koto desu.

Full sentence: この新型車の開発コンセプトは、「環境にやさしい」ということです。Kono shingatasha no kaihatsu konseputo wa, "kankyou ni yasashii" to iu koto desu.
EN: The development concept for this new car is "environmentally friendly".
HI: इस नई कार के विकास की अवधारणा है "पर्यावरण के अनुकूल"।
GU: આ નવી કારના વિકાસની સંકલ્પના છે "પર્યાવરણને અનુકૂળ".
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aコンセプトkonseputoconceptअवधारणाસંકલ્પના
bコンセントkonsentopower socketबिजली का सॉकेटવીજળીનું સોકેટ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): The guiding idea behind a product is its コンセプト (開発コンセプト). コンセント, one sound away, is Japanese for an electrical wall socket (not "consent").

Q8

太陽光や風力を利用した自然（ a エコロジー ／ b エネルギー ）の活用が注目を集めている。Taiyoukou ya fuuryoku o riyou shita shizen (a ekorojii / b enerugii) no katsuyou ga chuumoku o atsumete iru.

Full sentence: 太陽光や風力を利用した自然エネルギーの活用が注目を集めている。Taiyoukou ya fuuryoku o riyou shita shizen enerugii no katsuyou ga chuumoku o atsumete iru.
EN: The use of natural energy from sunlight and wind is attracting attention.
HI: सूरज की रोशनी और हवा से मिलने वाली प्राकृतिक ऊर्जा का उपयोग लोगों का ध्यान खींच रहा है।
GU: સૂર્યપ્રકાશ અને પવનથી મળતી કુદરતી ઊર્જાનો ઉપયોગ લોકોનું ધ્યાન ખેંચી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aエコロジーekorojiiecologyपारिस्थितिकीપરિસ્થિતિવિજ્ઞાન
bエネルギーenerugiienergyऊर्जाઊર્જા

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Sun and wind are sources of 自然エネルギー (renewable energy), which you can 活用 (put to use). エコロジー is the trap: it is on-theme, but it is a science or way of thinking, not something generated from sunlight.

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A programme that 追う (follows) real people over time is a ドキュメンタリー番組. アナウンサー is a person who reads the news, not a type of programme. (The readable page-foot fragment 「9 b」 agrees.)

Q10

昔は手紙・電話・電報のみだった通信（ a メディア ／ b アイディア ）が、IT技術の進歩とともに多様化してきた。Mukashi wa tegami, denwa, denpou nomi datta tsuushin (a media / b aidia) ga, ai-tii gijutsu no shinpo to tomo ni tayouka shite kita.

Full sentence: 昔は手紙・電話・電報のみだった通信メディアが、IT技術の進歩とともに多様化してきた。Mukashi wa tegami, denwa, denpou nomi datta tsuushin media ga, ai-tii gijutsu no shinpo to tomo ni tayouka shite kita.
EN: Means of communication, once limited to letters, phone and telegram, have diversified as IT has advanced.
HI: संचार के माध्यम, जो पहले सिर्फ़ चिट्ठी, फ़ोन और तार तक सीमित थे, आईटी की प्रगति के साथ विविध हो गए हैं।
GU: સંચારનાં માધ્યમો, જે પહેલાં માત્ર પત્ર, ફોન અને તાર સુધી સીમિત હતાં, આઇટીની પ્રગતિ સાથે વૈવિધ્યસભર બન્યાં છે.

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Letters, telephone and telegrams are all 通信メディア (means of communication). アイディア rhymes with メディア, which is the only reason it is there. (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 a」 agrees.)

Exam trap 1 — one sound changes everything: インフラ／インフレ, コンセプト／コンセント, プライド／ブランド. The pairs on this page are chosen to look the same at a glance. Read every kana.
