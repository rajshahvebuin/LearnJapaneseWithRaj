Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is 擬音語・擬態語 (onomatopoeia and mimetic words). In every item a and b differ only by voicing (゛) — e.g. かりかり／がりがり, こそこそ／ごそごそ. As a rule of thumb the voiced form is heavier, rougher or bigger, and the unvoiced form lighter or neater, but each word has its own fixed meaning, so learn them as pairs. The book prints furigana over 擬音語 (ぎおんご) and 擬態語 (ぎたいご) in the title.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

数年ぶりに会った彼女は（ a かりかり ／ b がりがり ）にやせていた。Suunenburi ni atta kanojo wa (a karikari / b garigari) ni yasete ita.

Full sentence: 数年ぶりに会った彼女はがりがりにやせていた。Suunenburi ni atta kanojo wa garigari ni yasete ita.
EN: When I saw her for the first time in years, she had become skin and bones.
HI: कई साल बाद मिली तो वह बिल्कुल हड्डियों का ढाँचा हो गई थी।
GU: ઘણાં વર્ષે મળી ત્યારે તે સાવ હાડપિંજર જેવી પાતળી થઈ ગઈ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかりかりkarikaricrispy; on edgeकुरकुरा; चिड़चिड़ाકરકરું; ચીડિયું
bがりがりgarigariscrawny, bonyबहुत दुबलाખૂબ પાતળું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): がりがりにやせる is the set phrase for being painfully thin. かりかり describes crisp food (かりかりのベーコン) or an irritable mood (試験前でかりかりしている), never body shape.

Q2

彼は（ a こそこそ ／ b ごそごそ ）逃げるように出ていった。Kare wa (a kosokoso / b gosogoso) nigeru you ni dete itta.

Full sentence: 彼はこそこそ逃げるように出ていった。Kare wa kosokoso nigeru you ni dete itta.
EN: He slipped out as if sneaking away.
HI: वह चोरी-छिपे भागने की तरह बाहर निकल गया।
GU: તે છાનુંમાનું ભાગતો હોય એમ બહાર નીકળી ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこそこそkosokososneakilyचोरी-छिपेછાનુંમાનું
bごそごそgosogosorustling, rummagingखड़खड़ाते हुएખડખડ કરતાં

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): こそこそ = doing something secretly so others won't notice — it goes naturally with 逃げる. ごそごそ is the sound of rummaging (引き出しをごそごそ探す) and has no sense of hiding.

Full sentence: あの人は言うことがころころ変わる。Ano hito wa iu koto ga korokoro kawaru.

HI: उस व्यक्ति की बातें बार-बार बदलती रहती हैं।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિની વાતો વારેઘડીએ બદલાતી રહે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aころころkorokoro(changing) easily, oftenबार-बारવારેઘડીએ
bごろごろgorogorolazing; rumblingआलस में; गड़गड़આળસમાં; ગડગડાટ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ころころ変わる = (opinions, plans, a story) change easily and often — from something light rolling about. ごろごろ is heavy: lazing about, thunder, or lots of big things lying around.

Q4

公演開始前の会場は（ a さわさわ ／ b ざわざわ ）していた。Kouen kaishi mae no kaijou wa (a sawasawa / b zawazawa) shite ita.

Full sentence: 公演開始前の会場はざわざわしていた。Kouen kaishi mae no kaijou wa zawazawa shite ita.
EN: Before the performance started, the hall was buzzing with voices.
HI: प्रदर्शन शुरू होने से पहले हॉल में शोरगुल था।
GU: કાર્યક્રમ શરૂ થતાં પહેલાં હૉલમાં કોલાહલ હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aさわさわsawasawalight rustle of leavesपत्तों की हल्की सरसराहटપાંદડાંનો હળવો સરસરાટ
bざわざわzawazawamurmur of a crowdभीड़ की भिनभिनाहटભીડનો ગણગણાટ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ざわざわする = many people talking at once, a restless crowd noise. さわさわ is the soft sound of wind through leaves — a quiet, pleasant sound, not a full hall.

Q5

雨が続いたせいか、部屋が（ a しとしと ／ b じとじと ）して気持ちが悪い。Ame ga tsuzuita sei ka, heya ga (a shitoshito / b jitojito) shite kimochi ga warui.

EN: Maybe because of all the rain, the room feels damp and unpleasant.
HI: शायद लगातार बारिश की वजह से कमरे में सीलन है और बुरा लगता है।
GU: કદાચ સતત વરસાદને કારણે ઓરડો ભેજવાળો છે અને ખરાબ લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aしとしとshitoshito(rain) softly falling(बारिश) धीमे-धीमे(વરસાદ) ધીમે-ધીમે

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): もごもご = speaking unclearly, as if with your mouth full — which is why you can't hear. もこもこ describes puffy, bulging things (もこもこのダウンジャケット), not speech.

Q10

今は成績がトップでも、（ a ほやほや ／ b ぼやぼや ）していると、すぐにみんなに追い抜かれますよ。Ima wa seiseki ga toppu demo, (a hoyahoya / b boyaboya) shite iru to, sugu ni minna ni oinukaremasu yo.

Full sentence: 今は成績がトップでも、ぼやぼやしていると、すぐにみんなに追い抜かれますよ。Ima wa seiseki ga toppu demo, boyaboya shite iru to, sugu ni minna ni oinukaremasu yo.
EN: Even if your grades are top now, if you slack off, everyone will soon overtake you.
HI: अभी तुम्हारे अंक सबसे ऊपर हैं, पर लापरवाही करोगे तो सब जल्दी आगे निकल जाएँगे।
GU: હાલ તમારા ગુણ સૌથી ઉપર છે, પણ બેદરકારી રાખશો તો બધા ઝડપથી આગળ નીકળી જશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aほやほやhoyahoyafresh, just madeताज़ा-ताज़ाતાજું-તાજું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ぼやぼやする = be careless and slow, not paying attention — the warning before 追い抜かれる. ほやほや means fresh and new (焼きたてのほやほや, 新婚ほやほや). (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 b」 agrees.)

Exam trap 1 — voiced ≠ just "louder": the general feel is that the voiced form is heavier or rougher (ごろごろ vs ころころ), but many pairs change meaning completely (ぼやぼや "careless" vs ほやほや "fresh"; へらへら "silly grin" vs ぺらぺら "fluent"). Learn each pair, not a rule.
Exam trap 2 — read the clue in the sentence: 大きすぎて → ぶかぶか, 気持ちが悪い → じとじと, 聞こえません → もごもご, 追い抜かれる → ぼやぼや. N1 mimetic questions almost always contain a word that fixes the meaning.
Exam trap 3 — the noun the word attaches to: rain しとしと but a room じとじと; leaves さわさわ but a hall ざわざわ. Check the subject before choosing.
