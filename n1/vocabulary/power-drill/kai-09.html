問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

これは架空の物語です。事実ではありません。Kore wa kakuu no monogatari desu. Jijitsu de wa arimasen.

EN: This is a fictional story. It is not fact.
HI: यह एक काल्पनिक कहानी है। यह सच नहीं है।
GU: આ એક કાલ્પનિક વાર્તા છે. આ હકીકત નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かくう（架空）kakuufictionalकाल्पनिकકાલ્પનિક
2けくうkekuu(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3かいくうkaikuu(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4けいくうkeikuu(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: 架 = か (架橋 kakyou "bridge-building", 担架 tanka "stretcher"); 訓読み か(ける) as in 橋を架ける. 空 = くう. 架空 literally means "built in the air", i.e. imaginary. Its reading is short か, not かい (which you might get from 介／会).

Exam trap 1 — same kanji, different reading in an idiom: 一目 is ひとめ in 一目惚れ but いちもく in 一目置く; 目 is ま in 目の当たり (第8回). When a 漢字読み item is a set phrase, the 別冊's ⊂⊃ mark is a hint that the reading belongs to the phrase.
Exam trap 2 — 用法 distractors built from a kanji's other compounds: 潔 also means "clean" in 清潔／簡潔, so sentences about clean hands and brief explanations look plausible. 潔い itself is only about honourable conduct.
Exam trap 3 — reduplicated words that differ by one sound: ちょくちょく (often) vs ちょこちょこ (little steps). N1 likes these pairs; learn what each one describes (frequency, movement, progress, secrecy) rather than an English gloss.
