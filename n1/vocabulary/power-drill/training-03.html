Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is body-part idioms (慣用句) with 目・耳・口: both choices are real idioms, so you must know what each one means. A previous owner wrote a score and highlighted some options on this page; all of it was ignored. Biggest traps: 耳を貸す (listen to someone's request) vs 耳をすます (strain to hear a sound), and 耳が痛い (it hurts to hear — it hits home) vs 耳が遠い (hard of hearing).

Q1

（ a 耳を貸す ／ b 耳をすます ）と、虫の声が聞こえる。もう秋だ。(a Mimi o kasu / b Mimi o sumasu) to, mushi no koe ga kikoeru. Mou aki da.

Full sentence: 耳をすますと、虫の声が聞こえる。もう秋だ。Mimi o sumasu to, mushi no koe ga kikoeru. Mou aki da.
EN: If you listen carefully, you can hear the insects singing. It's autumn already.
HI: कान लगाकर सुनो तो कीड़ों की आवाज़ सुनाई देती है। शरद ऋतु आ गई।
GU: કાન માંડીને સાંભળો તો જીવજંતુઓનો અવાજ સંભળાય છે. પાનખર આવી ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a耳を貸すmimi o kasulend an ear (to someone's words)किसी की बात पर कान देनाકોઈની વાત પર કાન આપવો
b耳をすますmimi o sumasulisten carefully, strain to hearकान लगाकर सुननाકાન માંડીને સાંભળવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 耳を澄ます is focusing your hearing on a faint sound — insects, footsteps, distant music. 耳を貸す is about paying attention to what a person says or asks (相談に耳を貸す), mostly in the negative (耳を貸さない), so it doesn't fit insects.

Q2

あの学生の授業中の態度は、（ a 目にあまる ／ b 目に浮かぶ ）ものがある。Ano gakusei no jugyouchuu no taido wa, (a me ni amaru / b me ni ukabu) mono ga aru.

Full sentence: あの学生の授業中の態度は、目にあまるものがある。Ano gakusei no jugyouchuu no taido wa, me ni amaru mono ga aru.
EN: That student's behaviour in class is beyond what can be tolerated.
HI: उस छात्र का कक्षा में रवैया हद से ज़्यादा ख़राब है।
GU: તે વિદ્યાર્થીનું વર્ગમાંનું વર્તન હદ બહારનું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a目にあまるme ni amarutoo much to overlook, intolerableबर्दाश्त से बाहरસહન ન થાય તેવું
b目に浮かぶme ni ukabuto picture vividlyआँखों के सामने तैरनाઆંખ સામે તરી આવવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 目に余る = more than the eye can take, so bad that you can't ignore it — used for behaviour; 〜は目に余るものがある is a standard pattern. 目に浮かぶ is imagining a scene (喜ぶ顔が目に浮かぶ) and doesn't evaluate behaviour.

Q3

初めて見たかわいい孫の姿に、祖父母は（ a 目を細めた ／ b 目をつぶった ）。Hajimete mita kawaii mago no sugata ni, sofubo wa (a me o hosometa / b me o tsubutta).

Full sentence: 初めて見たかわいい孫の姿に、祖父母は目を細めた。Hajimete mita kawaii mago no sugata ni, sofubo wa me o hosometa.
EN: Seeing their sweet grandchild for the first time, the grandparents beamed with delight.
HI: प्यारे पोते-पोती को पहली बार देखकर दादा-दादी ख़ुशी से मुस्कुरा उठे।
GU: વહાલા પૌત્ર-પૌત્રીને પહેલી વાર જોઈને દાદા-દાદી આનંદથી મલકાઈ ઊઠ્યાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a目を細めたme o hosometasmiled fondlyप्यार से मुस्कुराएપ્રેમથી મલકાયાં
b目をつぶったme o tsubuttaclosed their eyes; turned a blind eyeआँखें मूँद लीं; अनदेखा कियाઆંખો મીંચી; આંખ આડા કાન કર્યા

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 目を細める = to narrow your eyes in a smile of tenderness — the classic expression of grandparents with a grandchild. 目をつぶる means either literally closing your eyes or overlooking a fault, and neither fits this happy first meeting.

Q4

新大統領が誕生したA国の情勢から（ a 目が離せない ／ b 目も当てられない ）。Shin-daitouryou ga tanjou shita A-koku no jousei kara (a me ga hanasenai / b me mo aterarenai).

Full sentence: 新大統領が誕生したA国の情勢から目が離せない。Shin-daitouryou ga tanjou shita A-koku no jousei kara me ga hanasenai.
EN: I can't take my eyes off the situation in Country A now that it has a new president.
HI: नए राष्ट्रपति वाले देश A की स्थिति से नज़र हटाना मुश्किल है।
GU: નવા રાષ્ટ્રપ્રમુખવાળા દેશ A ની પરિસ્થિતિ પરથી નજર હટાવવી મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a目が離せないme ga hanasenaican't look away, must keep watchingनज़र नहीं हटा सकतेનજર હટાવી ન શકાય

Q5

最近友人が禁煙を始めたと聞いて、愛煙家の私は（ a 耳が遠い ／ b 耳が痛い ）。Saikin yuujin ga kin'en o hajimeta to kiite, aienka no watashi wa (a mimi ga tooi / b mimi ga itai).

Full sentence: 最近友人が禁煙を始めたと聞いて、愛煙家の私は耳が痛い。Saikin yuujin ga kin'en o hajimeta to kiite, aienka no watashi wa mimi ga itai.
EN: Hearing that my friend has given up smoking stings a bit, as I'm a keen smoker.
HI: दोस्त ने सिगरेट छोड़ दी, यह सुनकर मुझ जैसे सिगरेट-प्रेमी को बात चुभती है।
GU: મિત્રે સિગારેટ છોડી દીધી એ સાંભળીને મારા જેવા સિગારેટ-પ્રેમીને વાત ખૂંચે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a耳が遠いmimi ga tooihard of hearingऊँचा सुननाઓછું સંભળાવું
b耳が痛いmimi ga itaiit hurts to hear (it applies to me)बात चुभनाવાત ખૂંચવી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 耳が痛い = what you hear is uncomfortable because it points at your own weak spot. A heavy smoker hearing that a friend has quit feels exactly that. 耳が遠い is a physical hearing problem, which has nothing to do with being a smoker.

Q6

EN: Please pay back the money I lent you in full by the end of next month.
HI: जो पैसे उधार दिए हैं, वे अगले महीने के अंत तक पूरे के पूरे लौटा दीजिए।
GU: ઉછીના આપેલા પૈસા આવતા મહિનાના અંત સુધીમાં પૂરેપૂરા પાછા આપજો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a耳をそろえてmimi o soroete(pay) in fullपूरी रक़म एक साथપૂરી રકમ એકસાથે

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 耳をそろえて返す／払う = repay the whole amount without missing anything (耳 here is the edge of an old gold coin, and you "line up the edges" of a full stack). 耳を傾ける is attentive listening, which has nothing to do with paying.

Q7

（ a 長い目 ／ b 白い目 ）で見たら、借りるより買ったほうが得かもしれない。(a Nagai me / b Shiroi me) de mitara, kariru yori katta hou ga toku kamo shirenai.

Full sentence: 長い目で見たら、借りるより買ったほうが得かもしれない。Nagai me de mitara, kariru yori katta hou ga toku kamo shirenai.
EN: In the long run, buying might work out cheaper than renting.
HI: लंबे समय के हिसाब से देखें तो किराये से बेहतर ख़रीदना फ़ायदेमंद हो सकता है।
GU: લાંબા ગાળાની દૃષ્ટિએ જોઈએ તો ભાડે લેવા કરતાં ખરીદવું ફાયદાકારક હોઈ શકે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a長い目nagai methe long viewदूरगामी नज़रલાંબા ગાળાની નજર
b白い目shiroi mea cold, disapproving lookतिरस्कार भरी नज़रતિરસ્કારભરી નજર

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 長い目で見る = judge over a long period, not by the immediate cost — exactly the reasoning about buying vs renting. 白い目で見る is looking at a person with contempt, and its object is always a person.

Q8

吉田さんは甘いものに（ a 目がない ／ b 歯が立たない ）。Yoshida-san wa amai mono ni (a me ga nai / b ha ga tatanai).

Full sentence: 吉田さんは甘いものに目がない。Yoshida-san wa amai mono ni me ga nai.
EN: Ms Yoshida can't resist anything sweet.
HI: योशिदा-सान मीठे की बेहद शौक़ीन हैं।
GU: યોશિદા-સાન મીઠાઈનાં ખૂબ શોખીન છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a目がないme ga naihas a weakness forबहुत शौक़ीन हैખૂબ શોખીન છે
b歯が立たないha ga tatanaiis no match for, can't handleमुक़ाबला नहीं कर पातीમુકાબલો કરી શકતી નથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 〜に目がない = love something so much you lose your judgement over it — food, drink or hobbies. 歯が立たない is a trap because sweets and teeth seem to go together, but the idiom means "too strong / too hard for me" (an opponent, a problem).

Q9

三日前にけんかして以来、二人は（ a 口を挟んで ／ b 口をきいて ）いない。Mikka mae ni kenka shite irai, futari wa (a kuchi o hasande / b kuchi o kiite) inai.

Full sentence: 三日前にけんかして以来、二人は口をきいていない。Mikka mae ni kenka shite irai, futari wa kuchi o kiite inai.
EN: Since they quarrelled three days ago, the two haven't spoken to each other.
HI: तीन दिन पहले झगड़ा होने के बाद से दोनों में बोलचाल बंद है।
GU: ત્રણ દિવસ પહેલાં ઝઘડો થયો ત્યારથી બંને વચ્ચે બોલચાલ બંધ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a口を挟んでkuchi o hasandebutting inबीच में बोलकरવચ્ચે બોલીને
b口をきいてkuchi o kiitespeaking to (each other)बात करकेવાત કરીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 口をきく = talk to someone; 口をきかない／きいていない is the usual way to say "not on speaking terms" after a fight. 口を挟む is cutting into another person's conversation, which needs a third party and doesn't fit two people who have fallen out.

Q10

マンションの建設計画に対し、近隣の住民は（ a 腕を上げて ／ b 口をそろえて ）反対した。Manshon no kensetsu keikaku ni taishi, kinrin no juumin wa (a ude o agete / b kuchi o soroete) hantai shita.

Full sentence: マンションの建設計画に対し、近隣の住民は口をそろえて反対した。Manshon no kensetsu keikaku ni taishi, kinrin no juumin wa kuchi o soroete hantai shita.
EN: The local residents opposed the plan to build the apartment block with one voice.
HI: अपार्टमेंट बनाने की योजना का आसपास के निवासियों ने एक सुर में विरोध किया।
GU: એપાર્ટમેન્ટ બાંધવાની યોજનાનો આસપાસના રહેવાસીઓએ એક સૂરે વિરોધ કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a腕を上げてude o ageteimproving one's skillहुनर निखारकरહુન્નર નિખારીને
b口をそろえてkuchi o soroetewith one voiceएक सुर मेंએક સૂરે

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 口をそろえる = many people say the same thing together: 口をそろえて反対する／ほめる. 腕を上げる is a trap for learners who picture raising arms in protest, but the idiom means "get better at something" (料理の腕を上げた). (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 b」 agrees.)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This page tests idioms built on 目・耳・口. The groups below collect the ones that share a body part, so you can see how the verb changes the meaning.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when

Exam trap 1 — same body part, opposite feeling: 目を細める (fond delight) vs 目をつぶる (overlooking), 長い目 (patience) vs 白い目 (contempt). Don't guess from the body part alone — learn the verb or adjective that goes with it.
Exam trap 2 — literal pictures mislead: 腕を上げる is not raising your arms in protest, 歯が立たない has nothing to do with sweets, and 耳が遠い is not "far from hearing the news". Body-part idioms rarely mean what the picture suggests.
Exam trap 3 — the particle points to the idiom: 〜から目が離せない, 〜に目がない, 〜に耳を傾ける, 〜は目に余るものがある. The words around the blank often leave room for only one idiom.
