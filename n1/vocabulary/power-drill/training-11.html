Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is 大学生活で使うことば (words used in university life): courses, credits, clubs and events. No furigana are printed on this page.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

先生が学会参加のため、授業が（ a 休業 ／ b 休講 ）になった。Sensei ga gakkai sanka no tame, jugyou ga (a kyuugyou / b kyuukou) ni natta.

Full sentence: 先生が学会参加のため、授業が休講になった。Sensei ga gakkai sanka no tame, jugyou ga kyuukou ni natta.
EN: The class was cancelled because the professor is attending an academic conference.
HI: प्रोफ़ेसर के सम्मेलन में जाने के कारण कक्षा रद्द हो गई।
GU: પ્રોફેસર પરિષદમાં જવાના હોવાથી વર્ગ રદ થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a休業kyuugyouclosed for business (shop, company)कारोबार बंद (दुकान)વેપાર બંધ (દુકાન)
b休講kyuukoucancelled lectureव्याख्यान रद्दવ્યાખ્યાન રદ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 休講 (講 = lecture) is the university word for a cancelled class. 休業 is a shop or company closing (本日休業).

Q2

この大学では、2年生までに40単位とらないと、（ a 停学 ／ b 留年 ）してしまう。Kono daigaku de wa, ninensei made ni yonjuu tan'i toranai to, (a teigaku / b ryuunen) shite shimau.

Full sentence: この大学では、2年生までに40単位とらないと、留年してしまう。Kono daigaku de wa, ninensei made ni yonjuu tan'i toranai to, ryuunen shite shimau.
EN: At this university, if you don't earn 40 credits by the second year, you have to repeat the year.
HI: इस विश्वविद्यालय में दूसरे साल तक 40 क्रेडिट न लेने पर साल दोहराना पड़ता है।
GU: આ યુનિવર્સિટીમાં બીજા વર્ષ સુધી 40 ક્રેડિટ ન મેળવો તો વર્ષ ફરી કરવું પડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a停学teigakususpension (as punishment)निलंबन (सज़ा के तौर पर)સસ્પેન્શન (સજા રૂપે)
b留年ryuunenrepeating a yearसाल दोहरानाવર્ષ ફરી કરવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Too few 単位 (credits) means you can't move up → 留年. 停学 is a disciplinary suspension for misconduct, not for low credits.

Q3

学園祭も無事に終わり、みんなで（ a 打ち上げ ／ b 打ち合わせ ）をした。Gakuensai mo buji ni owari, minna de (a uchiage / b uchiawase) o shita.

Full sentence: 学園祭も無事に終わり、みんなで打ち上げをした。Gakuensai mo buji ni owari, minna de uchiage o shita.
EN: The school festival ended safely, so we all had a wrap party.
HI: कॉलेज उत्सव ठीक-ठाक निपट गया, तो सबने मिलकर समापन पार्टी की।
GU: કૉલેજ ઉત્સવ સારી રીતે પૂરો થયો, એટલે બધાએ મળીને સમાપન પાર્ટી કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a打ち上げuchiagewrap party; launch (rocket)समापन पार्टी; प्रक्षेपणસમાપન પાર્ટી; પ્રક્ષેપણ
b打ち合わせuchiawaseplanning meetingपूर्व-बैठक, तैयारी चर्चाપૂર્વ-બેઠક, તૈયારી ચર્ચા

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): After an event ends (終わり), you hold an 打ち上げ — a celebration party. 打ち合わせ is a meeting to plan before something.

Q4

4月下旬までに、どの講義を（ a 履修 ／ b 修得 ）するか、決めなければならない。Shigatsu gejun made ni, dono kougi o (a rishuu / b shuutoku) suru ka, kimenakereba naranai.

Full sentence: 4月下旬までに、どの講義を履修するか、決めなければならない。Shigatsu gejun made ni, dono kougi o rishuu suru ka, kimenakereba naranai.
EN: By late April, I have to decide which lectures to take.
HI: अप्रैल के आख़िर तक तय करना है कि कौन-से व्याख्यान लूँ।
GU: એપ્રિલના અંત સુધીમાં નક્કી કરવું પડશે કે કયાં વ્યાખ્યાન લેવાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a履修rishuutaking (a course)कोर्स लेना, पंजीकरणકોર્સ લેવો, નોંધણી
b修得shuutokuearning (credits), mastering(क्रेडिट) अर्जित करना(ક્રેડિટ) મેળવવી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 講義を履修する = register for and take a course (履修登録). 修得 is the result — 単位を修得する (earn credits). Choosing which lecture comes before earning anything.

Q5

経済学や経営学は、（ a 自然科学 ／ b 社会科学 ）と呼ばれている。Keizaigaku ya keieigaku wa, (a shizen kagaku / b shakai kagaku) to yobarete iru.

Full sentence: 経済学や経営学は、社会科学と呼ばれている。Keizaigaku ya keieigaku wa, shakai kagaku to yobarete iru.
EN: Economics and business administration are called social sciences.
HI: अर्थशास्त्र और प्रबंधन शास्त्र को सामाजिक विज्ञान कहा जाता है।
GU: અર્થશાસ્ત્ર અને વ્યવસ્થાપન શાસ્ત્રને સામાજિક વિજ્ઞાન કહેવાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a自然科学shizen kagakunatural sciencesप्राकृतिक विज्ञानપ્રાકૃતિક વિજ્ઞાન
b社会科学shakai kagakusocial sciencesसामाजिक विज्ञानસામાજિક વિજ્ઞાન

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Economics and management study society → 社会科学. 自然科学 covers physics, chemistry, biology. (The third group is 人文科学 — humanities.)

Q6

卒業論文を書くために、図書館で（ a 参考文献 ／ b 推薦図書 ）を探している。Sotsugyou ronbun o kaku tame ni, toshokan de (a sankou bunken / b suisen tosho) o sagashite iru.

Full sentence: 卒業論文を書くために、図書館で参考文献を探している。Sotsugyou ronbun o kaku tame ni, toshokan de sankou bunken o sagashite iru.
EN: I'm looking for reference materials in the library to write my graduation thesis.
HI: स्नातक शोध-प्रबंध लिखने के लिए पुस्तकालय में संदर्भ-ग्रंथ ढूँढ रहा हूँ।
GU: સ્નાતક મહાનિબંધ લખવા માટે પુસ્તકાલયમાં સંદર્ભગ્રંથો શોધું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a参考文献sankou bunkenreferences, bibliographyसंदर्भ-ग्रंथસંદર્ભગ્રંથ
b推薦図書suisen toshorecommended booksअनुशंसित पुस्तकेंભલામણ કરેલાં પુસ્તકો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A thesis needs 参考文献 — the sources you cite. 推薦図書 is a reading list someone recommends, usually for general reading, not research sources you search out.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Exam trap 1 — before vs after: 打ち合わせ (before — planning) vs 打ち上げ (after — celebrating); 履修 (choosing a course) vs 修得 (earning its credits). The sentence's timing word (終わり, 決めなければ) decides.
Exam trap 2 — ゼミ ≠ セミナー: a ゼミ is a group you belong to; a セミナー is an event you attend once. Likewise ガイダンス is a session, ガイド a person or book.
Exam trap 3 — long vowels in katakana: サークル (club) vs サイクル (cycle). One ー changes the word.
