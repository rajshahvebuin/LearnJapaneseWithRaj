Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. Like ①, this page tests noun + verb collocations. A previous owner circled options, and a stray pen line runs through item 5; the printed text is still readable and all handwriting was ignored. Biggest traps: 口座を開く (not あける, although both are written 開), 新聞をとる = subscribe, and 時間をつぶす = kill time.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

田中さんが車に（ a おされて ／ b ひかれて ）入院したそうだ。Tanaka-san ga kuruma ni (a osarete / b hikarete) nyuuin shita sou da.

Full sentence: 田中さんが車にひかれて入院したそうだ。Tanaka-san ga kuruma ni hikarete nyuuin shita sou da.
EN: I hear Mr Tanaka was hit by a car and is in hospital.
HI: सुना है तानाका जी गाड़ी की चपेट में आ गए और अस्पताल में भर्ती हैं।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે તાનાકા સાહેબ ગાડીની અડફેટે આવી ગયા અને હૉસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおされて（押されて）osaretebeing pushedधक्का खाकरધક્કો ખાઈને
bひかれて（轢かれて）hikaretebeing run overगाड़ी के नीचे आकरવાહન નીચે કચડાઈને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A vehicle hitting a person = 車にひかれる (轢かれる, passive of 轢く) — or 車にはねられる when thrown by the impact. 押される is being pushed by a person or crowd, not a car accident.

Q2

未成年でも銀行口座を（ a あける ／ b ひらく ）ことはできますか。Miseinen demo ginkou kouza o (a akeru / b hiraku) koto wa dekimasu ka.

Full sentence: 未成年でも銀行口座をひらくことはできますか。Miseinen demo ginkou kouza o hiraku koto wa dekimasu ka.
EN: Can a minor open a bank account?
HI: क्या नाबालिग भी बैंक खाता खोल सकता है?
GU: શું સગીર પણ બેંક ખાતું ખોલી શકે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあける（開ける）akeruopen (a door, lid)(दरवाज़ा) खोलना(બારણું) ખોલવું
bひらく（開く）hirakuopen, start (an account, a shop, a meeting)खोलना, शुरू करनाખોલવું, શરૂ કરવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Both verbs are written 開, but 開く (ひらく) is used for starting something new: 口座／店／会議／パーティーを開く. 開ける (あける) is for physically opening a door, window or box.

Q3

実力が違いすぎて、10点以上差が（ a あって ／ b ついて ）しまった。Jitsuryoku ga chigaisugite, juutten ijou sa ga (a atte / b tsuite) shimatta.

Full sentence: 実力が違いすぎて、10点以上差がついてしまった。Jitsuryoku ga chigaisugite, juutten ijou sa ga tsuite shimatta.
EN: Our abilities were so different that the gap ended up at more than 10 points.
HI: क़ाबिलियत में इतना फ़र्क़ था कि 10 से ज़्यादा अंकों का अंतर हो गया।
GU: ક્ષમતામાં એટલો ફરક હતો કે 10થી વધુ પૉઇન્ટનું અંતર પડી ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあってattethere being (a difference)(अंतर) होकर(અંતર) હોઈને
bついてtsuite(a gap) opening up (差がつく)अंतर बढ़करઅંતર પડીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A gap that opens during a game = 差がつく (transitive 差をつける "pull ahead"). 〜てしまった marks a change that happened, which suits つく; ある only states that a difference exists.

Q4

私はここ数年ずっとA新聞を（ a とっている ／ b もっている ）。Watashi wa koko suunen zutto Ee-shinbun o (a totte iru / b motte iru).

Full sentence: 私はここ数年ずっとA新聞をとっている。Watashi wa koko suunen zutto Ee-shinbun o totte iru.
EN: I've been taking Newspaper A for the last few years.
HI: मैं पिछले कुछ सालों से A अख़बार लगवाता हूँ।
GU: હું છેલ્લાં થોડાં વર્ષથી A છાપું બંધાવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとっているtotte irusubscribe toलगवाता हूँબંધાવું છું
bもっているmotte iruhold, haveपास रखता हूँપાસે રાખું છું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Having a newspaper delivered regularly = 新聞をとる (also 雑誌をとる). ここ数年ずっと points to a continuing subscription. 持っている would just mean holding a copy.

Q5

ワイシャツにアイロンを（ a かける ／ b すべる ）のはけっこう難しい。Waishatsu ni airon o (a kakeru / b suberu) no wa kekkou muzukashii.

Full sentence: ワイシャツにアイロンをかけるのはけっこう難しい。Waishatsu ni airon o kakeru no wa kekkou muzukashii.
EN: Ironing a dress shirt is quite difficult.
HI: शर्ट पर इस्त्री करना काफ़ी मुश्किल है।
GU: શર્ટ પર ઇસ્ત્રી કરવી ઘણી અઘરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかけるkakeruapply (an iron)(इस्त्री) करना(ઇસ્ત્રી) કરવી
bすべる（滑る）suberuslide (intransitive)फिसलनाલપસવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 〜にアイロンをかける is the fixed phrase (like 掃除機をかける "vacuum"). 滑る is intransitive, so it can't take アイロンを. The iron does slide over the shirt, which is what makes b tempting.

Q6

家族を（ a 育てる ／ b 養う ）ために、どんな仕事でもしようと思った。Kazoku o (a sodateru / b yashinau) tame ni, donna shigoto demo shiyou to omotta.

Full sentence: 家族を養うために、どんな仕事でもしようと思った。Kazoku o yashinau tame ni, donna shigoto demo shiyou to omotta.
EN: I decided I'd take any job to support my family.
HI: परिवार का पेट पालने के लिए मैंने कोई भी काम करने की ठानी।
GU: કુટુંબનું ભરણપોષણ કરવા મેં કોઈ પણ કામ કરવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a育てるsodateruraise, bring upपालना-पोसनाઉછેરવું
b養うyashinausupport financially, provide forभरण-पोषण करनाભરણપોષણ કરવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Earning money to keep a household going = 家族を養う; the どんな仕事でも ("any job") clause is about money. 育てる means bringing up children or plants, and you don't "raise" your whole family (including a spouse).

Q7

友達が来るまで、本屋で時間を（ a 倒した ／ b つぶした ）。Tomodachi ga kuru made, honya de jikan o (a taoshita / b tsubushita).

Full sentence: 友達が来るまで、本屋で時間をつぶした。Tomodachi ga kuru made, honya de jikan o tsubushita.
EN: I killed time in a bookshop until my friend arrived.
HI: दोस्त के आने तक मैंने किताबों की दुकान में समय काटा।
GU: મિત્ર આવે ત્યાં સુધી મેં પુસ્તકની દુકાનમાં સમય પસાર કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a倒したtaoshitaknocked downगिरा दियाપાડી દીધું
bつぶした（潰した）tsubushitakilled (time)(समय) काटा(સમય) પસાર કર્યો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 時間をつぶす "crush time" = kill time while waiting (noun 暇つぶし). 倒す (knock over, defeat) doesn't combine with 時間.

Q8

この問題について、早急に結論を（ a 出す ／ b つける ）べきだ。Kono mondai ni tsuite, soukyuu ni ketsuron o (a dasu / b tsukeru) beki da.

Full sentence: この問題について、早急に結論を出すべきだ。Kono mondai ni tsuite, soukyuu ni ketsuron o dasu beki da.
EN: We should reach a conclusion on this issue as soon as possible.
HI: इस मुद्दे पर जल्द से जल्द निष्कर्ष निकालना चाहिए।
GU: આ મુદ્દે બને તેટલી જલદી નિષ્કર્ષ કાઢવો જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a出すdasureach, produce (a conclusion)(निष्कर्ष) निकालना(નિષ્કર્ષ) કાઢવો
bつけるtsukeruattach; settle (決着をつける)लगाना; फ़ैसला करनाલગાવવું; ફેંસલો કરવો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 結論を出す／結論が出る is the standard pair (also 答えを出す). つける goes with 決着／けり／見当をつける — close in meaning, which is the trap, but 結論をつける is not the natural collocation.

Q9

飛行機が着陸する前に、時計を現地の時刻に（ a 合わせた ／ b そろえた ）。Hikouki ga chakuriku suru mae ni, tokei o genchi no jikoku ni (a awaseta / b soroeta).

Full sentence: 飛行機が着陸する前に、時計を現地の時刻に合わせた。Hikouki ga chakuriku suru mae ni, tokei o genchi no jikoku ni awaseta.
EN: Before the plane landed, I set my watch to local time.
HI: विमान उतरने से पहले मैंने घड़ी स्थानीय समय से मिला ली।
GU: વિમાન ઊતરે તે પહેલાં મેં ઘડિયાળ સ્થાનિક સમય મુજબ મેળવી લીધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a合わせたawasetaset, adjusted (to match)मिला लीમેળવી લીધી
bそろえた（揃えた）soroetalined up, made uniformएक जैसा कियाએકસરખું કર્યું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Adjusting one thing to match a standard = A を B に合わせる (時計を時刻に／予定を相手に合わせる). そろえる makes several things the same as each other (靴をそろえる, 長さをそろえる). (The readable page-foot fragment 「9 a」 agrees.)

Q10

みんなから「がんばって」「期待しているよ」と言われ、ますますプレッシャーが（ a かかった ／ b のった ）。Minna kara "ganbatte" "kitai shite iru yo" to iware, masumasu puresshaa ga (a kakatta / b notta).

Full sentence: みんなから「がんばって」「期待しているよ」と言われ、ますますプレッシャーがかかった。Minna kara "ganbatte" "kitai shite iru yo" to iware, masumasu puresshaa ga kakatta.
EN: Everyone told me "Good luck!" and "We're counting on you!", which put me under even more pressure.
HI: सबने "शुभकामनाएँ" और "तुमसे उम्मीद है" कहा, जिससे मुझ पर और भी दबाव पड़ गया।
GU: બધાએ "શુભેચ્છા" અને "તારી પાસે આશા છે" કહ્યું, એટલે મારા પર વધુ દબાણ આવ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかかったkakattawas put on (pressure)(दबाव) पड़ा(દબાણ) આવ્યું
bのった（乗った）nottarode, got onसवार हुआસવાર થયો

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Pressure "hangs" on you: プレッシャー／負担／圧力がかかる (transitive: 〜にプレッシャーをかける). のる is the trap because a weight "rests on" your shoulders in English, but のる doesn't go with プレッシャー. (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 a」 agrees.)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This page tests which verb a noun takes. The groups below collect the pairs from this drill that an English gloss would treat as interchangeable.

"Open": 開ける vs 開く (Q2)

開く（ひらく）hirakuOpen, startStart an account, business or event; open a book or flower.口座／店／会議を開く

"Match": 合わせる vs そろえる (Q9)

かける / かかる with abstract nouns (Q5, Q10)

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
アイロン／掃除機をかけるairon / soujiki o kakeruIron / vacuumApply a tool to a surface.シャツにアイロンをかける
プレッシャーがかかるpuresshaa ga kakaruBe under pressureIntransitive; the person feels it.試合前にプレッシャーがかかる
プレッシャーをかけるpuresshaa o kakeruPut pressure onTransitive; someone causes it.部下にプレッシャーをかける
Exam trap 1 — same kanji, different verb: 開ける／開く and 合わせる／合う look alike on paper. In 集中トレーニング the choices are usually written in kana, so you must know the sound and its collocation: 口座をひらく, not あける.
Exam trap 2 — "general" verbs with special meanings: とる (新聞をとる = subscribe), つぶす (時間をつぶす = kill time), かかる (プレッシャーがかかる). These basic verbs are the ones N1 likes to test in 文脈規定, because the English meaning doesn't help.
