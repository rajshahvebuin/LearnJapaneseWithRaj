問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

水道代は毎月実費を請求します。Suidoudai wa maitsuki jippi o seikyuu shimasu.

EN: For water, we charge you the actual cost every month.
HI: पानी का बिल हर महीने असल ख़र्च के हिसाब से लिया जाएगा।
GU: પાણીનું બિલ દર મહિને ખરા ખર્ચ પ્રમાણે લેવામાં આવશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1じひ（慈悲）jihimercy, compassionदया, करुणाદયા, કરુણા
2じぴjipi(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3じっひjihhi(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4じっぴ（実費）jippiactual costअसल ख़र्चખરો ખર્ચ

Why: 実 = じつ, and before a h-row sound the つ becomes small っ and h turns into p: じつ＋ひ → じっぴ (same as 実施 じっし, 出費 しゅっぴ, 発表 はっぴょう). Options 2 and 3 apply only half the sound change; 1 is a real word (慈悲) with different kanji.

Q2

怒っていたのか、彼はあいさつもせずに無言で帰ってしまった。Okotte ita no ka, kare wa aisatsu mo sezu ni mugon de kaette shimatta.

EN: Maybe he was angry — he went home without a word, not even saying goodbye.
HI: शायद वह नाराज़ था — बिना अलविदा कहे, चुपचाप घर चला गया।
GU: કદાચ તે નારાજ હતો — આવજો કહ્યા વગર, ચૂપચાપ ઘરે જતો રહ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1むげん（無限）mugeninfiniteअसीम, अनंतઅસીમ, અનંત
2むごん（無言）mugonsilent, wordlessमौन, चुपचापમૌન, ચૂપચાપ
3ぶげんbugen(not this word)(यह शब्द नहीं)(આ શબ્દ નથી)
4ぶごんbugon(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: 無 is む in most words (無理, 無料, 無言) and ぶ only in a handful (無事 buji, 無難 bunan, 無礼 burei). 言 is ごん here (伝言 dengon, 遺言 yuigon) — げん is its other 音読み (発言, 言語). むげん is a real word, 無限, which makes option 1 the trap.

何と言って友人を慰めたらいいのだろう。Nan to itte yuujin o nagusametara ii no darou.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ほめたら（褒めたら）hometaraif I praiseतारीफ़ करूँ तोવખાણ કરું તો

もっと高尚な文章を書きたいものだ。Motto koushou na bunshou o kakitai mono da.

1こしょう（胡椒／故障）koshoupepper; breakdownकाली मिर्च; ख़राबीકાળાં મરી; ખામી

問題2 — 言い換え類義: choose the word closest in meaning (1点×5)

Q1

彼に聞いても、いたって普通の意見しか出てこないだろう。Kare ni kiite mo, itatte futsuu no iken shika dete konai darou.

EN: Even if you ask him, all you'll get is an utterly ordinary opinion.
HI: उससे पूछोगे तो भी बिल्कुल साधारण राय ही मिलेगी।
GU: તેને પૂછશો તો પણ એકદમ સામાન્ય અભિપ્રાય જ મળશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いつもitsumoalwaysहमेशाહંમેશાં
2どうせdouseanyway (resigned)वैसे भीઆમેય
3きわめてkiwameteextremelyबेहदઅત્યંત
4比較的hikakutekirelativelyअपेक्षाकृतપ્રમાણમાં

Why: いたって (至って) is a degree adverb meaning "to the utmost, very" — the same as きわめて／非常に. どうせ is a trap because the sentence has a resigned tone, but どうせ expresses the speaker's attitude, not degree. 比較的 ("relatively") is the opposite of an extreme.

Exam trap 1 — sound changes in 音読み compounds: 実＋費 → じっぴ, 発＋表 → はっぴょう. When a つ/ち/く syllable meets an h-row sound, expect っ + p. Options that apply only one of the two changes (じぴ, じっひ) are always wrong.
Exam trap 2 — the "obvious" kanji reading is the trap: 目の当たり uses ま, not め; 無言 uses む and ごん. Fixed expressions often keep old readings — the 別冊 marks them ⊂⊃ for a reason.
Exam trap 3 — particles decide 文脈規定: Nにふさわしい (not たのもしい), 被害をもたらす (not 及ぶ, which needs が), 〜的 only after 圧倒. Check which particle or suffix the blank sits next to before you choose by meaning.
