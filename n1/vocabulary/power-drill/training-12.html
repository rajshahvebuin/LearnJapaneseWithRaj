Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is ビジネスで使うことば (words used in business): office kanji compounds and business katakana. This is the last unit in the book. The book prints furigana ほこ over 誇 (Q9). Scan note: in Q8 the particle after the blank is partly blurred; it reads as 「には」, which fits the sentence.

10 items — choose a or b for （　） (1点×10)

Q1

今度A社の坂本部長を（ a 応対 ／ b 接待 ）することになったんだが、どこかいい店を知らないか。Kondo A-sha no Sakamoto buchou o (a outai / b settai) suru koto ni natta n da ga, dokoka ii mise o shiranai ka.

Full sentence: 今度A社の坂本部長を接待することになったんだが、どこかいい店を知らないか。Kondo A-sha no Sakamoto buchou o settai suru koto ni natta n da ga, dokoka ii mise o shiranai ka.
EN: I've got to entertain Mr Sakamoto, the department head at Company A — do you know a good place?
HI: मुझे A कंपनी के विभाग प्रमुख साकामोतो जी की ख़ातिरदारी करनी है, कोई अच्छी जगह पता है?
GU: મારે A કંપનીના વિભાગ વડા સાકામોતોજીની મહેમાનગતિ કરવાની છે, કોઈ સારી જગ્યા ખબર છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a応対outaidealing with, receiving (callers)जवाब देना, सामना करना (आगंतुक)જવાબ આપવો, સંભાળવું (મુલાકાતી)
b接待settaibusiness entertainingव्यावसायिक ख़ातिरदारीવ્યાવસાયિક મહેમાનગતિ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Taking a client out to a restaurant (いい店) is 接待 — corporate wining and dining (接待ゴルフ). 応対 is handling visitors or calls at the office (電話応対).

Q2

B社は、ここ数年で急激に（ a 業績 ／ b 経営 ）を伸ばしている。B-sha wa, koko suunen de kyuugeki ni (a gyouseki / b keiei) o nobashite iru.

Full sentence: B社は、ここ数年で急激に業績を伸ばしている。B-sha wa, koko suunen de kyuugeki ni gyouseki o nobashite iru.
EN: Company B has rapidly improved its business results over the past few years.
HI: B कंपनी ने पिछले कुछ सालों में तेज़ी से अपने कारोबारी नतीजे बढ़ाए हैं।
GU: B કંપનીએ છેલ્લાં થોડાં વર્ષોમાં ઝડપથી પોતાનાં વેપારી પરિણામો વધાર્યાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a業績gyousekibusiness results, performanceकारोबारी प्रदर्शनવેપારી કામગીરી
b経営keieimanagement, running a businessप्रबंधन, संचालनસંચાલન, વ્યવસ્થાપન

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 業績を伸ばす = grow sales and profits (業績が上がる／悪化する). 経営 is the act of running a company; you don't 伸ばす it.

Q3

では、明日の2時に、（ a 弊社 ／ b 御社 ）にお伺いいたします。Dewa, ashita no niji ni, (a heisha / b onsha) ni oukagai itashimasu.

Full sentence: では、明日の2時に、御社にお伺いいたします。Dewa, ashita no niji ni, onsha ni oukagai itashimasu.
EN: Then I will call at your company at two o'clock tomorrow.
HI: तो कल दो बजे मैं आपकी कंपनी में हाज़िर होऊँगा।
GU: તો કાલે બે વાગ્યે હું આપની કંપનીમાં હાજર થઈશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a弊社heishaour company (humble)हमारी कंपनी (विनम्र)અમારી કંપની (નમ્ર)
b御社onshayour company (respectful, spoken)आपकी कंपनी (आदरसूचक)આપની કંપની (આદરસૂચક)

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): お伺いする is humble "visit" — you visit the other company → 御社. 弊社 is your own company; you don't humbly visit yourself. (In writing, 貴社 is used instead of 御社.)

Q4

発注先を決める前に、2、3社から（ a 見込み ／ b 見積もり ）書を取った。Hacchuusaki o kimeru mae ni, ni-sansha kara (a mikomi / b mitsumori) sho o totta.

Full sentence: 発注先を決める前に、2、3社から見積もり書を取った。Hacchuusaki o kimeru mae ni, ni-sansha kara mitsumorisho o totta.
EN: Before deciding which supplier to order from, I got quotations from two or three companies.
HI: किस कंपनी को ऑर्डर दें, यह तय करने से पहले दो-तीन कंपनियों से कोटेशन लिए।
GU: કઈ કંપનીને ઑર્ડર આપવો એ નક્કી કરતાં પહેલાં બે-ત્રણ કંપનીઓ પાસેથી ક્વોટેશન લીધાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見込みmikomiprospect, expectationसंभावना, अनुमानસંભાવના, અંદાજ
b見積もりmitsumoriestimate, quotationकोटेशन, लागत-अनुमानક્વોટેશન, ખર્ચનો અંદાજ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 見積もり書 (見積書) = a written quotation. 見込み is a forecast (合格の見込み) and doesn't form 〜書.

Q5

仕事で必要な（ a 経費 ／ b 実費 ）を支払ったときは、必ず領収書をもらっておいてください。Shigoto de hitsuyou na (a keihi / b jippi) o shiharatta toki wa, kanarazu ryoushuusho o moratte oite kudasai.

Full sentence: 仕事で必要な経費を支払ったときは、必ず領収書をもらっておいてください。Shigoto de hitsuyou na keihi o shiharatta toki wa, kanarazu ryoushuusho o moratte oite kudasai.
EN: Whenever you pay business expenses, be sure to get a receipt.
HI: काम के ज़रूरी ख़र्च चुकाते समय रसीद ज़रूर ले लीजिए।
GU: કામના જરૂરી ખર્ચ ચૂકવો ત્યારે રસીદ જરૂર લઈ લેજો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a経費keihi(business) expenses(व्यावसायिक) ख़र्च(વ્યાવસાયિક) ખર્ચ
b実費jippiactual cost (no markup)वास्तविक लागतવાસ્તવિક ખર્ચ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 仕事で必要な + 領収書 = expenses you claim back → 経費 (経費で落とす). 実費 is the bare cost of materials or transport, as in 交通費は実費支給 — it's about the amount, not the category.

Q6

会議の前に、企画を関係部署に説明し、（ a コンセプト ／ b コンセンサス ）を得ておいた。Kaigi no mae ni, kikaku o kankei busho ni setsumei shi, (a konseputo / b konsensasu) o ete oita.

Full sentence: 会議の前に、企画を関係部署に説明し、コンセンサスを得ておいた。Kaigi no mae ni, kikaku o kankei busho ni setsumei shi, konsensasu o ete oita.
EN: Before the meeting, I explained the plan to the departments concerned and got their consensus.
HI: बैठक से पहले मैंने योजना संबंधित विभागों को समझाकर उनकी सहमति ले ली।
GU: બેઠક પહેલાં મેં યોજના સંબંધિત વિભાગોને સમજાવીને તેમની સંમતિ લઈ લીધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aコンセプトkonseputoconceptअवधारणाખ્યાલ
bコンセンサスkonsensasuconsensus, agreementसहमतिસંમતિ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 〜を得る (obtain) from other departments = their agreement → コンセンサス (= 合意). コンセプト is the idea behind the plan itself — you have it, you don't get it from others.

Q7

彼女は入社後、係長、課長、部長と、順調に（ a スキル ／ b キャリア ）を重ねてきた。Kanojo wa nyuusha go, kakarichou, kachou, buchou to, junchou ni (a sukiru / b kyaria) o kasanete kita.

Full sentence: 彼女は入社後、係長、課長、部長と、順調にキャリアを重ねてきた。Kanojo wa nyuusha go, kakarichou, kachou, buchou to, junchou ni kyaria o kasanete kita.
EN: Since joining the company she has steadily built her career — section chief, manager, department head.
HI: कंपनी में आने के बाद वह अनुभाग प्रमुख, प्रबंधक, विभाग प्रमुख बनती गई और लगातार करियर बनाती रही।
GU: કંપનીમાં જોડાયા પછી તે વિભાગ-મુખ્ય, મૅનેજર, વિભાગ-વડા બનતી ગઈ અને સતત કારકિર્દી ઘડતી રહી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aスキルsukiruskillहुनरકૌશલ્ય
bキャリアkyariacareerकरियरકારકિર્દી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A chain of promotions (係長→課長→部長) is a career → キャリアを重ねる／積む. Skills are 身につける or 磨く. (キャリア ≈ 経歴 was tested in 第28回 問題2 Q3.)

Q8

お客様からの（ a クレーム ／ b ストレス ）には誠実な対応が求められる。Okyakusama kara no (a kureemu / b sutoresu) ni wa seijitsu na taiou ga motomerareru.

Full sentence: お客様からのクレームには誠実な対応が求められる。Okyakusama kara no kureemu ni wa seijitsu na taiou ga motomerareru.
EN: Complaints from customers must be handled sincerely.
HI: ग्राहकों की शिकायतों पर ईमानदारी से कार्रवाई ज़रूरी है।
GU: ગ્રાહકોની ફરિયાદો પર પ્રામાણિક કાર્યવાહી જરૂરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aクレームkureemucomplaintशिकायतફરિયાદ
bストレスsutoresustressतनावતણાવ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Japanese クレーム = a customer complaint (not an English "claim") — クレーム対応 is a standard business term. ストレス is not something customers send you (お客様からの). The particle after the blank (には) is partly blurred in the scan.

Exam trap 1 — 御社 / 弊社 follows the honorific verb: お伺いする (humble visit) points to the other side's company → 御社. With ご連絡ください (please contact) it would be 弊社.
Exam trap 2 — Japanese-English false friends: クレーム = complaint (not "claim"); シェア = market share; キャリア = career record. Read them as Japanese words.
Exam trap 3 — verb partners decide: コストがかかる, リスクが高い, コンセンサスを得る, キャリアを重ねる, 業績を伸ばす. Check which verb follows the blank.
