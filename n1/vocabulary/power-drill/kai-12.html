Notes

The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 目標解答時間 10分, 20 points — 問題1 漢字読み ×5 (1点), 問題2 言い換え類義 ×5 (1点), 問題3 文脈規定 ×6 (1点), 問題4 用法 ×2 (2点). The book prints furigana over 完璧 (かんぺき, 問題2 Q1 option 1), 叱る (しか), 手持ち無沙汰 (てもちぶさた) and 腐って (くさ). 問題1 Q5 is printed 「いつ発作が起きるわからないので」 — the か of 起きるかわからない is missing in the book; it is quoted as printed. A previous owner wrote a score and highlighted some items; all of it was ignored. Traps this round: 墓地 is ぼち (not はか); 発作 is ほっさ (発 = ほっ as in 発足, 作 = さ as in 動作); 一見 is いっけん, not いちみ／ひとみ.

問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

夜の墓地は怖いので、近くを通りたくない。Yoru no bochi wa kowai node, chikaku o toritakunai.

EN: Graveyards are scary at night, so I don't want to go near one.
HI: रात में क़ब्रिस्तान डरावना लगता है, इसलिए मैं उसके पास से गुज़रना नहीं चाहता।
GU: રાત્રે કબ્રસ્તાન ડરામણું લાગે છે, એટલે હું તેની પાસેથી પસાર થવા માંગતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ぼじboji(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2ぼち（墓地）bochigraveyardक़ब्रिस्तानકબ્રસ્તાન
3はかじhakaji(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4はかちhakachi(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: Both kanji take 音読み: 墓 = ぼ (墓参 bosan) + 地 = ち (as in 土地, 地図). 地 is じ in some words (地面, 地震), which is the trap of option 1. Options 3–4 use the 訓読み はか, which is only for 墓 on its own.

Q2

この企画は、我が社にとって初めての試みである。Kono kikaku wa, waga sha ni totte hajimete no kokoromi de aru.

EN: This project is a first attempt of its kind for our company.
HI: यह योजना हमारी कंपनी के लिए पहला प्रयोग है।
GU: આ યોજના અમારી કંપની માટે પહેલો પ્રયોગ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いどみ（挑み）idomichallenge (from 挑む)चुनौती देनाપડકાર આપવો
2のぞみ（望み）nozomihope, wishआशा, इच्छाઆશા, ઇચ્છા
3こころみ（試み）kokoromiattemptप्रयासપ્રયાસ

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おしむ（惜しむ）oshimuto regret (a loss)अफ़सोस करनाઅફસોસ કરવો

Q4

あの人は一見優しそうに見えるが、実はそうではない。Ano hito wa ikken yasashisou ni mieru ga, jitsu wa sou de wa nai.

EN: At first glance that person looks kind, but actually they aren't.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いちみ（一味）ichimigang, band (of conspirators)गिरोहટોળકી
2ひとみ（瞳）hitomipupil (of the eye)आँख की पुतलीઆંખની કીકી

Exam trap 1 — 発 = ほっ in a few words: 発作 (ほっさ), 発足 (ほっそく), 発端 (ほったん), 発起人 (ほっきにん). Everywhere else it is はつ／はっ, which is why はっさ looks right.
Exam trap 2 — 言い換え idioms are not literal: 肩を持つ is "take sides", not touching a shoulder; 手持ち無沙汰 is boredom; いたたまれない is wanting to leave. Choose the option that matches the feeling, not the picture.
Exam trap 3 — 用法 adverbs are about manner: やんわり shows how something unwelcome is said. Look for verbs like 断る／注意する／伝える; physical softness needs やわらか.
