問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

彼はついに世界記録を樹立した。Kare wa tsui ni sekai kiroku o juritsu shita.

EN: He finally set a world record.
HI: आख़िरकार उसने विश्व रिकॉर्ड बना दिया।
GU: આખરે તેણે વિશ્વ રેકોર્ડ બનાવી દીધો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1きたつkitatsu(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2きりつ（起立／規律）kiritsustanding up; discipline (not this kanji)खड़े होना; अनुशासन (यह कांजी नहीं)ઊભા થવું; શિસ્ત (આ કાંજી નહીં)
3じゅたつjutatsu(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4じゅりつ（樹立）juritsuestablishing, setting (a record)स्थापित करना, बनानाસ્થાપવું, બનાવવું

Why: 樹 = じゅ (樹木 jumoku "trees"), 立 = りつ (独立, 設立) — not たつ, which is its 訓読み. → じゅりつ. Option 2 きりつ is the trap: a real word with the same 立 = りつ.

無理なダイエットは健康を損なうので、気をつけましょう。Muri na daietto wa kenkou o sokonau node, ki o tsukemashou.

EN: Extreme dieting damages your health, so be careful.
HI: ज़बरदस्ती की डाइटिंग सेहत बिगाड़ती है, इसलिए सावधान रहें।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1になう（担う）ninauto shoulder, bear (responsibility)(ज़िम्मेदारी) उठाना(જવાબદારી) ઉપાડવી

あの頃は何もせず、怠惰な毎日を過ごしていた。Ano koro wa nani mo sezu, taida na mainichi o sugoshite ita.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q4

彼女は汚らわしいものを見るような目で私を見た。Kanojo wa kegarawashii mono o miru you na me de watashi o mita.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Exam trap 1 — one kanji, several 訓読み: 汚 = きたな(い)／よご(れる)／けが(らわしい). The okurigana tells you which: 〜らわしい → けが. The same idea helps with 損なう (そこ) vs 損 (そん).
Exam trap 2 — body-part idioms: 背中合わせ (danger always close), 顔合わせ (first meeting), 肩を並べる (be equal), 腹を割る (speak frankly). The 別冊 flags 背中合わせ as a ⊂⊃ chunk — learn it with its partner 危険と.
Exam trap 3 — kanji meaning vs usage: 恐れ入る contains 恐 "fear", and 指図 contains 図 "diagram", but neither means what the kanji suggest. In 用法 questions, the sentence that matches the kanji's literal meaning is often the wrong one.
