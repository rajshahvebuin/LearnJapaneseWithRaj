Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is everyday katakana loanwords (restaurants, shops, services). Both choices are real words, and several pairs sound alike (キャンパス／キャンペーン, モーター／モニター, カウンター／アカウント), so you must know which one Japanese actually uses in each context. Several are 和製英語-style usages whose Japanese meaning differs from English: ボリュームがある (a hearty portion), マニュアル (a staff rule book), モニター (a test user who gives feedback).

Q1

このレストランの肉料理はどれも（ a バランス ／ b ボリューム ）がある。Kono resutoran no niku ryouri wa dore mo (a baransu / b boryuumu) ga aru.

Full sentence: このレストランの肉料理はどれもボリュームがある。Kono resutoran no niku ryouri wa dore mo boryuumu ga aru.
EN: The meat dishes at this restaurant all come in generous portions.
HI: इस रेस्टोरेंट के सभी मांसाहारी व्यंजन भरपूर मात्रा में आते हैं।
GU: આ રેસ્ટોરાંની બધી માંસાહારી વાનગીઓ ભરપૂર માત્રામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aバランスbaransubalanceसंतुलनસંતુલન
bボリュームboryuumuvolume; hearty portionsभरपूर मात्राભરપૂર માત્રા

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Of food, ボリュームがある means "filling, generous in quantity" — a standard restaurant-review phrase. バランス goes with がいい／がとれている (栄養のバランスがいい), and どれもバランスがある is not natural Japanese.

Q2

日本料理は、魚や野菜を多く使っていて、（ a ヘルシー ／ b カロリー ）だと言われている。Nihon ryouri wa, sakana ya yasai o ooku tsukatte ite, (a herushii / b karorii) da to iwarete iru.

Full sentence: 日本料理は、魚や野菜を多く使っていて、ヘルシーだと言われている。Nihon ryouri wa, sakana ya yasai o ooku tsukatte ite, herushii da to iwarete iru.
EN: Japanese food uses a lot of fish and vegetables and is said to be healthy.
HI: जापानी भोजन में मछली और सब्ज़ियाँ ज़्यादा इस्तेमाल होती हैं, इसलिए उसे सेहतमंद माना जाता है।
GU: જાપાની ભોજનમાં માછલી અને શાકભાજી વધુ વપરાય છે, એટલે તેને આરોગ્યપ્રદ માનવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aヘルシーherushiihealthyसेहतमंदઆરોગ્યપ્રદ
bカロリーkaroriicalorie(s)कैलोरीકેલરી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): The blank is followed by だ, so it needs a word that describes the food: ヘルシーだ (a な-adjective). カロリー is a noun and needs an adjective: カロリーが低い. "Japanese food is calorie" doesn't make sense.

Q3

このレストランは、接客の仕方がすべて（ a メニュー ／ b マニュアル ）で決められている。Kono resutoran wa, sekkyaku no shikata ga subete (a menyuu / b manyuaru) de kimerarete iru.

Full sentence: このレストランは、接客の仕方がすべてマニュアルで決められている。Kono resutoran wa, sekkyaku no shikata ga subete manyuaru de kimerarete iru.
EN: At this restaurant, the way staff serve customers is all laid down in a manual.
HI: इस रेस्टोरेंट में ग्राहकों की सेवा का हर तरीक़ा नियमावली में तय है।
GU: આ રેસ્ટોરાંમાં ગ્રાહકોની સેવાની દરેક રીત નિયમાવલીમાં નક્કી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aメニューmenyuumenuमेन्यूમેનુ
bマニュアルmanyuarumanual (procedures)नियमावलीનિયમાવલી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 接客の仕方 (how to serve customers) is a procedure, and procedures are set out in a マニュアル. メニュー is the trap because the setting is a restaurant, but a menu lists dishes, not how staff behave.

Q4

お困りの際は、2階の相談（ a カウンター ／ b アカウント ）までお越しください。Okomari no sai wa, nikai no soudan (a kauntaa / b akaunto) made okoshi kudasai.

Full sentence: お困りの際は、2階の相談カウンターまでお越しください。Okomari no sai wa, nikai no soudan kauntaa made okoshi kudasai.
EN: If you need help, please come to the help desk on the 2nd floor.
HI: किसी परेशानी में हों तो कृपया दूसरी मंज़िल के सहायता काउंटर पर आइए।
GU: કોઈ મુશ્કેલી હોય તો કૃપા કરીને બીજા માળના સહાયતા કાઉન્ટર પર આવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aカウンターkauntaacounter, deskकाउंटरકાઉન્ટર
bアカウントakauntoaccountखाताખાતું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): You can physically go to (お越しください) a place on the 2nd floor, so it must be the 相談カウンター (help desk). アカウント is an online or bank account — not somewhere you can walk to. The two words share sounds, which is the trap.

Q9

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Air pollution and food cause アレルギー (from German Allergie, hence the spelling). ホームシック is missing home — not caused by pollution, and people with it aren't 患者 (patients).

Q10

新メニューの開発のため、100名の（ a モーター ／ b モニター ）に実際に食べてもらい、意見を聞いた。Shin-menyuu no kaihatsu no tame, hyakumei no (a mootaa / b monitaa) ni jissai ni tabete morai, iken o kiita.

Exam trap 1 — one-sound differences: キャンパス／キャンペーン, モーター／モニター, カウンター／アカウント. Say each word aloud and check the long vowels and ン; the exam relies on you reading katakana too quickly.
Exam trap 2 — Japanese meanings that English lacks: ボリュームがある (a big portion), モニター (a test user), マニュアル (staff procedures). Don't judge a katakana word only by its English meaning.
Exam trap 3 — grammar decides: ヘルシーだ needs an adjective (not カロリー); 画面をタッチする needs a する-verb (not スイッチ). Check what comes right after the blank.
