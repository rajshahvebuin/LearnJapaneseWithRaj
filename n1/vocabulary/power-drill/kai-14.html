問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

この学校は研究のための設備が整っています。Kono gakkou wa kenkyuu no tame no setsubi ga totonotte imasu.

EN: This school is well equipped with facilities for research.
HI: इस विद्यालय में शोध के लिए सभी सुविधाएँ मौजूद हैं।
GU: આ શાળામાં સંશોધન માટેની બધી સુવિધાઓ ઉપલબ્ધ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1せいび（整備）seibimaintenance, servicingरखरखाव, मरम्मतજાળવણી, મરામત
2せつび（設備）setsubiequipment, facilitiesउपकरण, सुविधाएँસાધનો, સુવિધાઓ
3そうび（装備）soubiequipment, gear (carried)साज़-सामान (साथ ले जाने का)સાધનસામગ્રી (સાથે લઈ જવાની)
4じゅんび（準備）junbipreparationतैयारीતૈયારી

Why: 設 = せつ (設立, 設計) + 備 = び → せつび. All four options are real 〜備 words, so this is a kanji test. 整備 (せいび) is the trap: 整 even appears in the same sentence (整っています).

Q2

外に出て、空に輝くきれいな星を見た。Soto ni dete, sora ni kagayaku kirei na hoshi o mita.

EN: I went outside and saw beautiful stars shining in the sky.
HI: बाहर जाकर मैंने आसमान में चमकते सुंदर तारे देखे।
GU: બહાર જઈને મેં આકાશમાં ચમકતા સુંદર તારા જોયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かがやく（輝く）kagayakuto shineचमकनाચમકવું
2きらめく（煌めく）kiramekuto glitter, sparkleझिलमिलानाઝગમગવું

Q3

このケーキは見た目もきれいで、食べるのが惜しい。Kono keeki wa mitame mo kirei de, taberu no ga oshii.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おしい（惜しい）oshiia pity, too good to wasteअफ़सोस की बातઅફસોસની વાત
2くやしい（悔しい）kuyashiifrustrating, mortifyingखीझ भरा, अफ़सोसनाकખીજવાળું, અફસોસજનક

Why: 惜 = お(しい) — same kanji as 惜しむ (第12回). 〜のが惜しい = "it seems a waste to ~". 悔しい is a different kind of regret (frustration at losing). The 〜しい distractors all exist.

頭も痛いし、寒気もする。風邪をひいたようだ。Atama mo itai shi, samuke mo suru. Kaze o hiita you da.

1かんき（寒気／換気）kankicold air mass; ventilationठंडी हवा; हवादारीઠંડી હવા; હવાઉજાસ

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q1

HI: इतने तंग जूतों में मैं चल नहीं सकता।
GU: આટલા તંગ જૂતામાં હું ચાલી શકતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1重いomoiheavyभारीભારે
2古いfuruioldपुरानाજૂનું

すぐにsugu niImmediatelyNeutral "right away".すぐに返事をする
Exam trap 1 — one spelling, two readings: 寒気 is さむけ (shivers) in everyday speech but かんき (cold air) in weather reports. Choose from context: 寒気がする = さむけ.
Exam trap 2 — ⊂⊃ body idioms: 角が立つ and 歯を食いしばる are both marked as set phrases by the 別冊. 角 here is かど (not つの), and 食いしばる only goes with 歯.
Exam trap 3 — とっさに needs a person reacting: the subject must be someone responding to a sudden event. If nature or a situation is the subject (rain, sales), use 急に／突然／たちまち instead.
