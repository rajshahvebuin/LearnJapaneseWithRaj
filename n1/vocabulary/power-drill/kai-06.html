問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

年齢相応の態度や発言が必要だ。Nenrei souou no taido ya hatsugen ga hitsuyou da.

EN: You need to behave and speak in a way that fits your age.
HI: उम्र के अनुरूप रवैया और बातचीत ज़रूरी है।
GU: ઉંમરને અનુરૂપ વર્તન અને વાતચીત જરૂરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あいおaio(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2そうおsouo(not a word — vowel too short)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3あいおうaiou(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4そうおう（相応）sououbefitting, suitableके अनुरूप, उचितને અનુરૂપ, યોગ્ય

Why: 相 is 音読み そう (相談 soudan, 相当 soutou) and 応 is おう (反応 hannou, 対応 taiou), so both syllables are long: そう＋おう. あい is the 訓読み of 相 (相手 aite), which options 1 and 3 borrow; options 1 and 2 also drop the long vowel of 応.

Q2

遠くから参加してくれる人への配慮を忘れないようにしたい。Tooku kara sanka shite kureru hito e no hairyo o wasurenai you ni shitai.

EN: I want to make sure we don't forget to be considerate towards people coming from far away.
HI: दूर से आने वाले लोगों का ख़याल रखना हम न भूलें, ऐसा चाहता हूँ।
GU: દૂરથી આવનારા લોકોનો ખ્યાલ રાખવાનું આપણે ન ભૂલીએ એવું ઇચ્છું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ぱいりょpairyo(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2はいりょ（配慮）hairyoconsiderationख़याल, लिहाज़ખ્યાલ, કાળજી
3ぱいりょうpairyou(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4はいりょうhairyou(not this word — long vowel is wrong)(यह शब्द नहीं)(આ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: 配 = はい (配達 haitatsu, 心配 shinpai). It only becomes ぱい after ん, as in 心配, never at the start of a word. 慮 = りょ, short (遠慮 enryo, 考慮 kouryo), so はいりょう with a long vowel is wrong. It's easy to mix it up with りょう (料・量).

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Exam trap 1 — long vs short vowels: 相応 is そうおう (both long), but 配慮 is はいりょ (short). N1 漢字読み often offers all four combinations of long/short, so learn the reading of each kanji: 応 = おう, 慮 = りょ, 料／量 = りょう.
Exam trap 2 — 発 = はっ or ほっ: 発揮 はっき, but 発足 ほっそく, 発作 ほっさ, 発起 ほっき. ほっ is the exception; learn those few words and read every other 発 as はつ／はっ.
Exam trap 3 — intransitive verbs can't be "willed": はかどる (捗る), like 経つ, describes how something turns out, so 〜をはかどる, はかどろう and はかどるつもり are all impossible. When a 用法 option puts an intransitive verb in a volitional form (〜よう, 〜つもり, 〜てください), it is almost always wrong.
