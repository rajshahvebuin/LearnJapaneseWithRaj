問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

この法律は来年の4月から施行されます。Kono houritsu wa rainen no shigatsu kara shikou saremasu.

EN: This law will come into force from April next year.
HI: यह क़ानून अगले साल अप्रैल से लागू होगा।
GU: આ કાયદો આવતા વર્ષના એપ્રિલથી અમલમાં આવશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しこう（施行）shikouenforcement (of a law)लागू करनाઅમલ
2りこう（利口）rikoucleverचतुरહોશિયાર
3しっこう（執行）shikkouexecution, carrying out (a sentence, duty)(सज़ा, कर्तव्य का) पालन/अमल(સજા, ફરજનો) અમલ
4りっこうrikkou(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: 施 = し (実施 jisshi, 施設 shisetsu) + 行 = こう → しこう. Option 3 is the trap: 執行 しっこう is a real legal word (刑の執行 "carrying out a sentence"), close in meaning, but its first kanji is 執. Lawyers sometimes say せこう to avoid confusion with 試行 (第10回), but しこう is the standard reading.

Q2

門には頑丈な鍵が付けられていた。Mon ni wa ganjou na kagi ga tsukerarete ita.

EN: A heavy-duty lock had been fitted to the gate.
HI: फाटक पर एक मज़बूत ताला लगा हुआ था।
GU: દરવાજા પર એક મજબૂત તાળું લગાવેલું હતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1げんこう（原稿）genkoumanuscriptपांडुलिपिહસ્તપ્રત
2げんごう（元号）gengouera name (Reiwa, Heisei…)युग का नामયુગનું નામ
3がんしょう（岩礁）ganshoureefचट्टानी रीफ़ખડકાળ રીફ
4がんじょう（頑丈）ganjousturdy, robustमज़बूतમજબૂત

Why: 頑 = がん (頑固 ganko, 頑張る ganbaru) and 丈 = じょう (大丈夫 daijoubu) — voiced, with a long vowel. 頑 is never げん, and the unvoiced しょう option is the classic voicing trap.

偽造のクレジットカードが大量に見つかった。Gizou no kurejitto kaado ga tairyou ni mitsukatta.

GU: મોટી સંખ્યામાં નકલી ક્રેડિટ કાર્ડ પકડાયાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いぞう（遺贈）izoubequest (rare word)वसीयत से देनाવસિયતથી આપવું
2ぎぞう（偽造）gizouforgery, counterfeitingजालसाज़ीબનાવટ

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あけましakemashi(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Q1

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1問題点mondaitenproblems, issuesसमस्याएँસમસ્યાઓ

Exam trap 1 — the "wrong half" of a kanji: 偽 looks as if it should read い (from 為), but it's ぎ; 施 is し, but its sibling 施工 is せこう. When the reading feels uncertain, recall a common word with the same kanji (偽善 ぎぜん, 実施 じっし) instead of guessing from the shape.
Exam trap 2 — 用法 options that borrow the kanji: in 問題4 Q1, sentences 3 and 4 are about "promoting" and "holding", which use 促 (促進) and 催 (開催). If the wrong options look like they were built from the word's own kanji, ask what the whole word means.
Exam trap 3 — ⊂⊃ set phrases: 顔負け only exists as N顔負け(の). Body-part idioms (腕, 顔, 口, 手) come up again in 集中トレーニング③④ — learn them as fixed chunks.
