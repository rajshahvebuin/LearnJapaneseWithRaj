Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is body-part idioms (慣用句) with 頭・足・腹・手・腰・首・胸; the book prints furigana for 丁寧 (ていねい) and 噂 (うわさ). A previous owner wrote a score and highlighted some options on this page; all of it was ignored. Biggest traps: four 手〜 idioms in a row (届かない／回らない, 負えない／つかない, 抜く／焼く, 貸す／組む), and 腹を割る (speak frankly) vs 腹をくくる (resolve yourself).

Q1

部長はとても（ a 頭が切れる ／ b 頭を冷やす ）人で、仕事が効率的だ。Buchou wa totemo (a atama ga kireru / b atama o hiyasu) hito de, shigoto ga kouritsuteki da.

Full sentence: 部長はとても頭が切れる人で、仕事が効率的だ。Buchou wa totemo atama ga kireru hito de, shigoto ga kouritsuteki da.
EN: Our department head is very sharp, and works efficiently.
HI: हमारे विभाग प्रमुख बहुत तेज़ दिमाग़ वाले हैं और उनका काम कुशल है।
GU: અમારા વિભાગના વડા ખૂબ તેજ દિમાગવાળા છે અને તેમનું કામ કાર્યક્ષમ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a頭が切れるatama ga kirerusharp, quick-wittedतेज़ दिमाग़ वालाતેજ દિમાગવાળું
b頭を冷やすatama o hiyasucool one's head, calm downदिमाग़ ठंडा करनाમગજ ઠંડું કરવું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): The sentence describes a permanent quality of a person (〜人で) that explains efficient work: 頭が切れる "razor-sharp mind". 頭を冷やす is an action you take once when you are angry or excited, so it can't describe what kind of person someone is.

Q2

入院中は私のために何度も（ a 足を運んで ／ b 足を伸ばして ）くださって、ありがとうございました。Nyuuinchuu wa watashi no tame ni nando mo (a ashi o hakonde / b ashi o nobashite) kudasatte, arigatou gozaimashita.

Full sentence: 入院中は私のために何度も足を運んでくださって、ありがとうございました。Nyuuinchuu wa watashi no tame ni nando mo ashi o hakonde kudasatte, arigatou gozaimashita.
EN: Thank you for coming to see me so many times while I was in hospital.
HI: अस्पताल में रहते समय मेरे लिए कई बार ख़ुद चलकर आने के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: હોસ્પિટલમાં હતો ત્યારે મારા માટે ઘણી વાર જાતે આવવા બદલ આભાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a足を運んでashi o hakondetaking the trouble to comeख़ुद चलकर आकरજાતે આવીને
b足を伸ばしてashi o nobashitegoing further afield; stretching legsआगे तक जाकर; पैर फैलाकरઆગળ સુધી જઈને; પગ લંબાવીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 足を運ぶ = to go somewhere on purpose, in person — and with 何度も and くださる it thanks someone for their effort in visiting. 足を伸ばす means extending a trip to a further place (ついでに隣町まで足を伸ばす), not visiting a sick person.

Q3

ここまで来たら、（ a 腹を割って ／ b 腹をくくって ）やるしかない。Koko made kitara, (a hara o watte / b hara o kukutte) yaru shika nai.

Full sentence: ここまで来たら、腹をくくってやるしかない。Koko made kitara, hara o kukutte yaru shika nai.
EN: Now that we've come this far, there's nothing to do but steel ourselves and go for it.
HI: यहाँ तक आ गए हैं तो अब मन पक्का करके करना ही होगा।
GU: અહીં સુધી આવી ગયા છીએ તો હવે મન મક્કમ કરીને કરવું જ પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a腹を割ってhara o wattefrankly, openlyदिल खोलकरદિલ ખોલીને
b腹をくくってhara o kukuttesteeling oneselfमन पक्का करकेમન મક્કમ કરીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ここまで来たら〜しかない expresses resolve — there's no turning back, so 腹をくくる "brace yourself, accept the risk". 腹を割る is about honest talk and almost always goes with 話す (腹を割って話す), not やる.

Q4

1台数千万円もする車は、私には（ a 手が届かない ／ b 手が回らない ）。Ichidai suusenman-en mo suru kuruma wa, watashi ni wa (a te ga todokanai / b te ga mawaranai).

Full sentence: 1台数千万円もする車は、私には手が届かない。Ichidai suusenman-en mo suru kuruma wa, watashi ni wa te ga todokanai.
EN: A car costing tens of millions of yen is out of my reach.
HI: करोड़ों येन की कार मेरी पहुँच से बाहर है।
GU: કરોડો યેનની કાર મારી પહોંચ બહાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手が届かないte ga todokanaiout of reachपहुँच से बाहरપહોંચ બહાર
b手が回らないte ga mawaranaitoo busy to deal withफ़ुर्सत नहींફુરસદ નથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): A price beyond your means = 手が届かない (高嶺の花). 手が回らない is about not having enough time or people to deal with everything (忙しくて〜まで手が回らない); it says nothing about money.

Q5

こんなに多くの資料の整理は、一人では（ a 手に負えない ／ b 手につかない ）。Konna ni ooku no shiryou no seiri wa, hitori de wa (a te ni oenai / b te ni tsukanai).

Full sentence: こんなに多くの資料の整理は、一人では手に負えない。Konna ni ooku no shiryou no seiri wa, hitori de wa te ni oenai.
EN: Sorting out this many documents is too much for one person to handle.
HI: इतने सारे दस्तावेज़ों को अकेले सँभालना बस के बाहर है।
GU: આટલા બધા દસ્તાવેજો એકલા સંભાળવા ગજા બહાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手に負えないte ni oenaitoo much to handleबस के बाहरગજા બહાર
b手につかないte ni tsukanaican't concentrate onमन नहीं लगताમન લાગતું નથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 一人では "alone" points to capacity: the job is beyond what one person can manage = 手に負えない. 手につかない needs an emotional cause (心配で／うれしくて仕事が手につかない), and the subject is the task you can't focus on.

Q6

子どもがちっとも言うことを聞かず、（ a 手を抜いている ／ b 手を焼いている ）。Kodomo ga chitto mo iu koto o kikazu, (a te o nuite iru / b te o yaite iru).

Full sentence: 子どもがちっとも言うことを聞かず、手を焼いている。Kodomo ga chitto mo iu koto o kikazu, te o yaite iru.
EN: My child won't listen to a word I say, and I'm at my wits' end.
HI: बच्चा मेरी एक नहीं सुनता, मैं परेशान हो गया हूँ।
GU: બાળક મારું જરાય સાંભળતું નથી, હું હેરાન થઈ ગયો છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手を抜いているte o nuite irucutting cornersलापरवाही कर रहा हूँકચાશ રાખું છું
b手を焼いているte o yaite iruhaving a hard time withपरेशान हूँહેરાન છું

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): When someone won't do as told and you don't know how to handle them, you 手を焼く (〜に手を焼く — children, a stubborn customer). 手を抜く would mean the parent is slacking, which doesn't follow from the child not listening.

Q7

自動車メーカー二社が（ a 手を貸して ／ b 手を組んで ）新車の開発を行うと発表した。Jidousha meekaa nisha ga (a te o kashite / b te o kunde) shinsha no kaihatsu o okonau to happyou shita.

Full sentence: 自動車メーカー二社が手を組んで新車の開発を行うと発表した。Jidousha meekaa nisha ga te o kunde shinsha no kaihatsu o okonau to happyou shita.
EN: Two carmakers announced that they will join forces to develop a new car.
HI: दो कार कंपनियों ने घोषणा की कि वे हाथ मिलाकर नई कार विकसित करेंगी।
GU: બે કાર કંપનીઓએ જાહેરાત કરી કે તેઓ હાથ મિલાવીને નવી કાર વિકસાવશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手を貸してte o kashitelending a handमदद करकेમદદ કરીને
b手を組んでte o kundejoining forcesहाथ मिलाकरહાથ મિલાવીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Two equal parties (二社が) working together = 手を組む (提携する). 手を貸す is one side helping another (友達の引っ越しに手を貸す), so it needs a helper and a helped, and a に-object.

Q8

小川課長は、部下に対しても丁寧に接する（ a 腰が重い ／ b 腰が低い ）人だ。Ogawa kachou wa, buka ni taishite mo teinei ni sessuru (a koshi ga omoi / b koshi ga hikui) hito da.

Full sentence: 小川課長は、部下に対しても丁寧に接する腰が低い人だ。Ogawa kachou wa, buka ni taishite mo teinei ni sessuru koshi ga hikui hito da.
EN: Section chief Ogawa is a humble person who is polite even to his subordinates.
HI: ओगावा सेक्शन चीफ़ विनम्र व्यक्ति हैं, जो अपने मातहतों से भी अदब से पेश आते हैं।
GU: ઓગાવા સેક્શન ચીફ નમ્ર વ્યક્તિ છે, જે પોતાના હાથ નીચેના લોકો સાથે પણ વિવેકથી વર્તે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a腰が重いkoshi ga omoislow to actसुस्तસુસ્ત
b腰が低いkoshi ga hikuihumble, modestविनम्रનમ્ર

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Being polite even to people below you = 腰が低い (bowing low → modest). 腰が重い means slow to start doing something (なかなか動かない), which is a criticism and doesn't match 丁寧に接する.

Q9

彼女は噂好きで、何にでも（ a 首をつっこんで ／ b 首をひねって ）くる。Kanojo wa uwasazuki de, nan ni demo (a kubi o tsukkonde / b kubi o hinette) kuru.

Full sentence: 彼女は噂好きで、何にでも首をつっこんでくる。Kanojo wa uwasazuki de, nan ni demo kubi o tsukkonde kuru.
EN: She loves gossip and pokes her nose into everything.
HI: उसे गपशप का शौक़ है और वह हर बात में टाँग अड़ाती है।
GU: તેને ગપસપનો શોખ છે અને તે દરેક વાતમાં માથું મારે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a首をつっこんでkubi o tsukkondepoking her nose inटाँग अड़ाकरમાથું મારીને
b首をひねってkubi o hinettepuzzling, doubtingसिर खुजाते हुएમૂંઝાઈને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): Someone who loves gossip gets involved in other people's business = 〜に首を突っ込む; the に of 何にでも fits it. 首をひねる is tilting your head because something is puzzling, and it isn't used with に or "everything". (The readable page-foot fragment 「9 a」 agrees.)

Q10

彼は、「任せてください」と（ a 顔を立てて ／ b 胸を張って ）答えた。Kare wa, "makasete kudasai" to (a kao o tatete / b mune o hatte) kotaeta.

Full sentence: 彼は、「任せてください」と胸を張って答えた。Kare wa, "makasete kudasai" to mune o hatte kotaeta.
EN: "Leave it to me," he answered confidently.
HI: उसने सीना तानकर जवाब दिया, "मुझ पर छोड़ दीजिए।"
GU: તેણે છાતી કાઢીને જવાબ આપ્યો, "મારા પર છોડી દો."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a顔を立ててkao o tatetesaving (someone's) face(किसी की) इज़्ज़त रखकर(કોઈની) આબરૂ સાચવીને
b胸を張ってmune o hatteconfidently, proudlyसीना तानकरછાતી કાઢીને

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): 「任せてください」 is a confident promise, said 胸を張って "chest out, with pride". 顔を立てる means acting to protect someone else's reputation (上司の顔を立てる) and needs a に／の-person — there is none here. (The readable page-foot fragment 「10 b」 agrees.)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This page tests idioms built on other body parts. The groups below collect the ones that share a body part, so you can see how the verb changes the meaning.

Exam trap 1 — four 手〜 idioms on one page: if you only remember "手 = hand", every option looks possible. Sort them by situation: price (届かない), time (回らない), capacity (負えない), feelings (つかない), a difficult person (焼く), laziness (抜く), partnership (組む), help (貸す).
Exam trap 2 — who is the idiom about?: 胸を張る is about yourself, 顔を立てる about someone else; 手を組む needs equals, 手を貸す a helper and a helped. Check the subject and the people in the sentence first.
Exam trap 3 — fixed partners: 腹を割って話す, 耳をそろえて返す (集中③), 〜に首をつっこむ. Many idioms only appear with one verb or particle, which narrows the choice before you think about meaning.
