Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 集中トレーニング (focused training), 目標解答時間 3分, 10 points (1点×10) — 「（　）の中のaとbのうち、文に合うほうを選びましょう」 — choose whichever of a / b fits the sentence. The theme is 擬音語・擬態語 (2): every word on this page has the shape XんXん (くんくん, ぱんぱん, かんかん …). Unlike 集中⑦, the two choices are usually completely different words, so the meaning, not the voicing, decides. The book prints furigana over 嘘 (うそ) in Q3 and 頬 (ほお) in Q7.

Q1

ミルクをやったら、子犬は（ a うんうん ／ b くんくん ）とにおいをかいだ。Miruku o yattara, koinu wa (a un'un / b kunkun) to nioi o kaida.

Full sentence: ミルクをやったら、子犬はくんくんとにおいをかいだ。Miruku o yattara, koinu wa kunkun to nioi o kaida.
EN: When I gave the puppy some milk, it sniffed at it.
HI: मैंने पिल्ले को दूध दिया तो उसने सूँ-सूँ करके सूँघा।
GU: મેં ગલૂડિયાને દૂધ આપ્યું તો તેણે સૂં-સૂં કરીને સૂંઘ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aうんうんun'unuh-huh; groaningहाँ-हाँ; कराहनाહા-હા; કણસવું
bくんくんkunkunsniffingसूँघने की आवाज़સૂંઘવાનો અવાજ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): くんくん is the sound/action of sniffing, and goes with においをかぐ. うんうん is nodding agreement (うんうんとうなずく) or groaning in pain.

Q2

焼肉を食べすぎて、お腹が（ a ぱんぱん ／ b ぽんぽん ）だ。Yakiniku o tabesugite, onaka ga (a panpan / b ponpon) da.

Full sentence: 焼肉を食べすぎて、お腹がぱんぱんだ。Yakiniku o tabesugite, onaka ga panpan da.
EN: I ate too much yakiniku — my stomach is stuffed.
HI: याकिनिकु ज़्यादा खा लिया, पेट फूलकर ठसाठस हो गया है।
GU: યાકિનિકુ વધુ ખાઈ લીધું, પેટ ઠાંસોઠાંસ ભરાઈ ગયું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aぱんぱんpanpanstuffed, burstingठसाठस भराઠાંસોઠાંસ ભરેલું
bぽんぽんponponone after another; (baby talk) tummyफटाफट; (बच्चों की भाषा) पेटફટાફટ; (બાળભાષા) પેટ

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): ぱんぱん = filled to bursting: お腹／かばんがぱんぱん. ぽんぽん is a baby word for "tummy" (お腹 itself), not its state, and also means "rapidly, bluntly" (ぽんぽん言う), so お腹がぽんぽんだ doesn't work.

Q3

私が嘘をつくと、いつもは優しい母が（ a かんかん ／ b たんたん ）に怒った。Watashi ga uso o tsuku to, itsumo wa yasashii haha ga (a kankan / b tantan) ni okotta.

Full sentence: 私が嘘をつくと、いつもは優しい母がかんかんに怒った。Watashi ga uso o tsuku to, itsumo wa yasashii haha ga kankan ni okotta.
EN: When I lied, my usually gentle mother was furious.
HI: जब मैंने झूठ बोला तो हमेशा नरम रहने वाली माँ आग-बबूला हो गईं।
GU: મેં જૂઠું બોલ્યું ત્યારે હંમેશાં નરમ રહેતી મા લાલચોળ ગુસ્સે થઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかんかんkankanfuriousआग-बबूलाલાલચોળ ગુસ્સે
bたんたんtantancalmly, coollyशांत भाव सेશાંતિથી

Why (worked out for this site — not from an official key): かんかんに怒る = be absolutely furious (the contrast with いつもは優しい). たんたん (淡々と) is the opposite — calm and unemotional — and takes と, not に.

EN: Since starting high school, my little brother has shot up in height.
HI: हाई स्कूल में आने के बाद मेरे छोटे भाई का क़द तेज़ी से बढ़ा।
GU: હાઈસ્કૂલમાં આવ્યા પછી મારા નાના ભાઈની ઊંચાઈ ઝડપથી વધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aごんごんgongon(dull banging sound)(भारी ठक-ठक)(ભારે ઠક-ઠક)
bぐんぐんgungunrapidly, by leaps and boundsतेज़ी सेઝડપથી

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q9

Exam trap 1 — opposites in one pair: かんかん (furious) vs たんたん (calm), さんさん (sunny summer) vs しんしん (silent snow). When the two choices are opposites, one word in the sentence (怒った, 夏の午後) settles it.
Exam trap 2 — fixed partners: においをかぐ → くんくん, 背が伸びる → ぐんぐん, 頭が痛い → がんがん, 泣く → わんわん. Learn each mimetic with the verb it usually goes with.
Exam trap 3 — と vs に: かんかんに怒る, ぱんぱんだ, but さんさんと輝く, じんじんと痛む. The particle after the blank is a quiet extra clue.
