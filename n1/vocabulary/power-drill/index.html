日本語パワードリル N1 文字・語彙

Nihongo Power Drill N1 Moji・Goi (アスク出版) — a pure drill book: 30 two-page rounds in the real JLPT N1 文字・語彙 format (問題1 漢字読み, 問題2 言い換え類義, 問題3 文脈規定, 問題4 用法 — 18 questions, 20 points, 10 minutes), interleaved with 12 one-page 集中トレーニング drills on verbs, idioms, katakana words, onomatopoeia, computer words, university words and business words. The book has no word lists or explanations, so every meaning, example and nuance note on these pages is added for this site. Round answers come from the book's 別冊 answer key; the scan has no key for the 集中トレーニング pages, so those answers are worked out and flagged.
