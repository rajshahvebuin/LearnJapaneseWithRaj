Notes
This is a drill-only book: it prints questions and an answer key, but no word lists or explanations. Every meaning, example sentence and nuance note on this page is added for this site. The only book sentences are the question sentences (labelled "book"); all other examples are marked (added, not in book). Format: 目標解答時間 10分, 20 points — 問題1 漢字読み ×5 (1点), 問題2 言い換え類義 ×5 (1点), 問題3 文脈規定 ×6 (1点), 問題4 用法 ×2 (2点). The book prints furigana over 重宝 (ちょうほう), 欺く (あざむ) and 胸騒ぎ (むなさわ) in 問題2; the scan's handwritten circles and notes were ignored. Traps this round: 試行 is しこう like 施行 (第7回), 経緯 is けいい, 快く is こころよく (not いさぎよく — 潔く), and 執着 means clinging to something you should let go of — it is not "being absorbed in" (熱中).

問題1 — 漢字読み: choose the reading of the underlined word (1点×5)

Q1

試行を重ねて、やっと商品化が実現した。Shikou o kasanete, yatto shouhinka ga jitsugen shita.

EN: After repeated trials, the product finally made it to market.
HI: बार-बार परीक्षण के बाद आख़िरकार उत्पाद बाज़ार में आ पाया।
GU: વારંવાર પરીક્ષણ પછી આખરે ઉત્પાદન બજારમાં આવી શક્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しこう（試行）shikoutrialपरीक्षणપરીક્ષણ
2せこう（施工）sekouconstruction workनिर्माण कार्यબાંધકામ
3しぎょう（始業）shigyoustart of work/schoolकाम/स्कूल की शुरुआतકામ/શાળાની શરૂઆત
4せぎょうsegyou(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Why: 試 = し (試験, 試合) + 行 = こう (旅行, 実行) → しこう. 行 is ぎょう only in a few words (行事, 修行, 行列), so しぎょう is the trap — it is a real word, 始業, with different kanji. Note that 試行, 施行 (第7回) and 思考 are all read しこう.

Q2

事件の経緯を説明してください。Jiken no keii o setsumei shite kudasai.

EN: Please explain how the incident came about.
HI: कृपया घटना का पूरा घटनाक्रम बताइए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને ઘટનાનો પૂરો ઘટનાક્રમ સમજાવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1けいい（経緯）keiicircumstances, sequence of eventsघटनाक्रमઘટનાક્રમ
2けいえい（経営）keieimanagement (of a business)व्यापार प्रबंधनધંધાનું સંચાલન
3けいせい（形成）keiseiformationनिर्माण, गठनરચના
4けいたい（携帯）keitaicarrying; mobile phoneसाथ रखना; मोबाइलસાથે રાખવું; મોબાઇલ

Why: 経 = けい + 緯 = い → けいい. 緯 means the weft threads in weaving (and "latitude", 緯度 ido), so 経緯 = warp and weft → the whole story of how things developed. In speech the same kanji are often read いきさつ. The distractors are all common けい〜 words.

Q5

一身上の都合により、今月末で退職することになりました。Isshinjou no tsugou ni yori, kongetsumatsu de taishoku suru koto ni narimashita.

EN: For personal reasons, I will be leaving the company at the end of this month.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いちみじょうichimijou(not a word)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

問題2 — 言い換え類義: choose the word closest in meaning (1点×5)

Q1

母にもらったペンはとても重宝していたのに、なくしてしまった。Haha ni moratta pen wa totemo chouhou shite ita no ni, nakushite shimatta.

EN: I'd been finding the pen my mother gave me so handy, and then I lost it.
HI: माँ से मिला पेन मेरे बहुत काम आता था, फिर भी वह खो गया।
GU: મા પાસેથી મળેલી પેન મારે બહુ કામની હતી, છતાં તે ખોવાઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いつも持っていたitsumo motte itaalways carriedहमेशा साथ रखता थाહંમેશાં સાથે રાખતો
2大事にしていたdaiji ni shite itatreasured, took good care ofसंभालकर रखता थाસાચવીને રાખતો
3便利に使っていたbenri ni tsukatte itafound useful, used handilyबड़े काम से इस्तेमाल करता थाઉપયોગી રીતે વાપરતો
4人に自慢していたhito ni jiman shite itashowed off to peopleलोगों को दिखाकर इतराता थाલોકોને બતાવીને ગર્વ કરતો

Exam trap 1 — しこう homophones: 試行 (trial, this round), 施行 (enforcement, 第7回), 思考 (thinking, 問題3 Q1 option) are all しこう. In 漢字読み the reading is the same, but in 文脈規定 you must pick by meaning.
Exam trap 2 — misleading kanji meanings: 重宝 isn't "treasure" but "handy"; 経緯 isn't "longitude and latitude" in everyday use but "how it happened". In 言い換え, a paraphrase that matches the literal kanji (大事にする) is often the trap.
Exam trap 3 — またしても is not a connective: it cannot mean "also" or "moreover". If the sentence lists two facts (〜であり、〜でもある／〜し、〜にも), the answer is 同時に／そのうえ, not またしても.
