Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: rare on-yomi that must simply be memorised (外科 げか, 強引 ごういん, 再来週 さらいしゅう, 作業 さぎょう), the e→a vowel change in 上着, and a かく drill in Ⅷ.

Ⅱ write the readings of two look-alike-sounding words
Ⅲ sort words into same-reading groups
Ⅳ write the 訓読み / mixed reading

Ⅵ pick the right word (sound look-alikes)
