試験に出る漢字と語彙 N1・N2

Shiken ni Deru Kanji to Goi (桐原書店, 50日完成) — the 文字・語彙 book of the 試験に出る series. 第1部 is 25 days of kanji (readings, look-alike shapes, look-alike sounds, same-sound kanji), 第2部 is 25 days of vocabulary (similar 漢語, verbs and nouns in use, adverbs, homonyms, correct usage, paraphrase), each part ending with N2 and N1 練習問題 in JLPT format, and 第3部 is one N2 and one N1 mock test. The book covers both N1 and N2; the N2 practice sets are kept here as N2-level warm-ups. Answers come from the book's own 別冊 (解答・試験に出るポイント解説).
