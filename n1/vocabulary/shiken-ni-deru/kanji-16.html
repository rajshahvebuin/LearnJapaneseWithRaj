Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 性 しょう/じょう again, ぎ/い and ど/のう sound pairs, and a たい drill (対・耐・待・退・帯) in Ⅷ. Scan note: in Ⅴ-⑥ (土地/士地) and Ⅴ-⑦ (月末/月未) the printed look-alikes differ only in stroke length and cannot be told apart reliably on this scan; the option order shown here is our best reading, and the answers (土地, 月末) are from the key.
