Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: one kanji, two readings — 然 (ねん/ぜん), 気 (け/き), 平 (びょう/へい), 頭 (ず/とう) — all flagged by the book; べん/びん in Ⅱ; せいしん/せいじん and けんとう/けんどう boxes in Ⅲ; and a よう drill (要・容・養・幼・用) in Ⅷ.

一層（　　）— 一斉（　　）(___) — (___) — write both readings

Answer: 一層 = いっそうissou — 一斉 = いっせいissei
EN: 一層: all the more; layer / 一斉: all at once
