Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes, the last 漢字 day: 夜 = や, 暴 = ばく, 便 = びん/べん and 発 = ほっ (all from the book's notes), くう/こう in Ⅱ, はんじょう/はんせい/はんしょう/はんしゃ and そうき/そうぎ/せいき boxes in Ⅲ, and a とう drill (当・投・統・討・倒) in Ⅷ where six sentences have two blanks. The key's ポイント解説 box runs from 別冊 p.16 onto p.17.
