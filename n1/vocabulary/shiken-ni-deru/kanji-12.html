Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 往 おう, 屋 おく, 下 げ, 会 え, a wall of かんせい／きかん words in Ⅲ, and a twelve-item か drill in Ⅷ.

Ⅳ write the 訓読み / mixed reading
