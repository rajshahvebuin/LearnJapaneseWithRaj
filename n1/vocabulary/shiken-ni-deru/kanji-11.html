Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: かい〜 words everywhere (Ⅱ, Ⅲ), 間柄 あいだがら, 外／外す, and a twelve-item えい drill in Ⅷ. Note: the book misprints Ⅱ's third item as a second ②; the key numbers it ③, and so do we.

Ⅱ write the readings of two look-alike-sounding words
Ⅲ sort words into same-reading groups
Ⅳ write the 訓読み / mixed reading

Ⅵ pick the right word (sound look-alikes)
Ⅶ pick the right kanji (meaning look-alikes)
Ⅷ fill in one of six same-reading kanji
