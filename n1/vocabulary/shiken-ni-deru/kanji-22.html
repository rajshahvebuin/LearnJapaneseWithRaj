Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: three readings the book sends back to earlier days (大 = たい Day 20, 域 = いき Day 14, 木 = もく Day 18), short vs long vowels in Ⅱ (と/とう, きょ/きょう, しき/そうしき), てんかい/てんか/でんか and ちょうかん/ちょうかい/そうかい boxes in Ⅲ, a run of ふ/ぶ/にん/ねん words in Ⅵ, and a へん drill (変・編・偏・辺・返) in Ⅷ. Ⅱ has seven pairs. The key starts on 別冊 p.14 and finishes on p.15.
