Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 是 = ぜ and 木 = ぼく (both flagged by the book), も/む and みょう/めい pairs in Ⅱ, a じたい/じだい/ちたい and a しょうめい/しょめい/せいめい sorting box in Ⅲ, and a はん drill (版・販・飯・伴・判) in Ⅷ, where item ⑤ has three blanks.

大賞（　　）— 代償（　　）(___) — (___) — write both readings

Answer: 大賞 = たいしょうtaishou — 代償 = だいしょうdaishou
EN: 大賞: grand prize / 代償: price, compensation
HI: 大賞: सर्वोच्च पुरस्कार / 代償: क़ीमत, हर्जाना

所有 ／ 余裕shoyuu / yoyuuしょ ／ よA different first syllable.
操作 ／ 捜索sousa / sousakuさ ／ さくAn extra syllable. Same for 丁重／添乗.
Exam trap: 自体・事態・辞退 are all じたい, and 生命・声明・姓名 are all せいめい: on the exam, only the context tells you which kanji is meant.

writing
