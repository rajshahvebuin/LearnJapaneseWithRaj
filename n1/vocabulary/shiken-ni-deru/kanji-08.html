Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 応 のう and 歩 ぽ after ん/っ, a wall of しん〜 words in Ⅲ, 辛い からい/つらい, and a ten-item しょう drill in Ⅷ. The day's answers start on 別冊 p.6 and its ポイント解説 is on p.7.
