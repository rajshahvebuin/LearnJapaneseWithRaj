Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 大 = たい again (Day 20 tip), 模 = ぼ/も, 体 = てい (rare), voicing pairs in Ⅱ, ようせい/ようしょく and はっしょう/はっしん/はっせい/はっそう sorting boxes in Ⅲ, a long ひ/び/ひょう/びょう set in Ⅵ, and a ぼう drill (防・冒・望・暴・乏) in Ⅷ. Ⅴ has only seven items on this day.
