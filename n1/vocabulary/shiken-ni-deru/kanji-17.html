Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). Today's themes: rare on-yomi that the book says to simply memorise (赴任 ふにん, 衣装 いしょう, 折衷 せっちゅう), voicing pairs in Ⅱ (ごう/こう, ぼう/ほう, じん/しん), a ほ/ほう/ほしゅ sorting box in Ⅲ, and a ちょう drill (張・調・超・徴・頂) in Ⅷ where four sentences have two blanks each.
