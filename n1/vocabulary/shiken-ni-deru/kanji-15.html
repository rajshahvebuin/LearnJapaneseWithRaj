Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: じょう readings (天井, 繁盛), voiced/unvoiced pairs, and a のう drill (脳・能・納・農・濃) in Ⅷ. The key starts at the bottom of 別冊 p.10 (Ⅰ–Ⅴ) and continues at the top of p.11 (Ⅵ–Ⅷ and the ポイント解説).

Ⅱ write the readings of two look-alike-sounding words

Ⅵ pick the right word (sound look-alikes)
