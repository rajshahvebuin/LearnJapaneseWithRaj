Q1 (Ⅰ(1)-①)

①職業で人を（　　）してはいけない。Shokugyou de hito o (___) shite wa ikenai.

Answer: 差別sabetsu
Full sentence: 職業で人を差別してはいけない。Shokugyou de hito o sabetsu shite wa ikenai.
EN: You must not discriminate against people because of their job.

GU: વ્યવસાયના આધારે લોકો સાથે ભેદભાવ ન કરવો જોઈએ.
