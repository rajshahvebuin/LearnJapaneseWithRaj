Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: unusual readings the book flags — 心 = じん (用心), 一 = いつ (唯一), 遺 = ゆい (遺言, Day 2) — a long run of short/long and voiced/unvoiced pairs in Ⅱ, たいしょう/だいしょう/たいしょく and ようじ/ようし/ようち boxes in Ⅲ, and a たん drill (担・単・探・嘆・端) in Ⅷ. Scan note: in Ⅴ-⑤ the two printed options (車輪／車輸) differ by one small component and are hard to tell apart on this scan; the answer 車輪 is from the key.
