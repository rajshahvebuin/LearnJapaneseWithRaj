Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 無 = ぶ/む and 有 = う (both spelled out by the book), 戸 = こ (Day 2), voicing and っ pairs in Ⅱ, でんとう/でんどう/てんとう and どうよう/とうよう/どよう/とうよ boxes in Ⅲ, and a てい drill (定・訂・提・停・程) in Ⅷ. The key starts on 別冊 p.15 and continues on p.16.
