Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: 悪 お, 為 い vs 偽 ぎ, a wall of い〜 and かい〜 words in Ⅲ, 汚い／汚れる, and a ten-item い drill in Ⅷ.
