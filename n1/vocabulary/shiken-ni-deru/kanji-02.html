Notes
Every 漢字 day has the same eight drills: Ⅰ–Ⅳ test readings (漢字読み, N1 & N2), Ⅴ–Ⅷ test choosing the kanji (表記 — N2 only on the real exam, but the look-alike and same-sound kanji still trip up N1 readers). The book does not tag items by level: treat everything as shared N2/N1. Today's themes: kanji whose reading changes with position (言 げん/ごん, 行 こう/ぎょう), long-vowel and しゅう/しゅつ traps, and a twelve-item えん drill in Ⅷ.

Ⅰ 難しい読み — 正しい読み方を選びなさい。 (Tadashii yomikata o erabinasai. — Choose the correct reading.)

Q1 (Ⅰ-①)

広大（　　）(___) — choose the reading

Answer: 広大 = こうだいkoudai
EN: vast, extensive
HI: विशाल, बहुत बड़ा
GU: વિશાળ, ખૂબ મોટું
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1こうだいkoudaicorrect readingसही उच्चारणસાચું ઉચ્ચારણ
2ほうだいhoudai広 is not ほうग़लत उच्चारणખોટું ઉચ્ચારણ
3ひょうだいhyoudai広 is not ひょうग़लत उच्चारणખોટું ઉચ્ચારણ

Why: 広 = こう, 大 = だい → こうだい. かくだい is 拡大 (enlargement) — the 広 inside 拡 is a tempting link, but the readings differ.

Q2 (Ⅰ-②)

伝言（　　）(___) — choose the reading

Answer: 伝言 = でんごんdengon
EN: message (passed on for someone)
HI: संदेश (किसी के लिए छोड़ा गया)
GU: સંદેશો (કોઈ માટે મૂકેલો)
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1でんげんdengen言 second → ごん, not げんग़लत उच्चारणખોટું ઉચ્ચારણ
2でんこんdenkon言 is voiced hereग़लत उच्चारणખોટું ઉચ્ચારણ
