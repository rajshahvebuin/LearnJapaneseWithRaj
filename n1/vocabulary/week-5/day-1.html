Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
（　）だけで葬式※を済ませた。(___) dake de soushiki o sumaseta.
("Which word fits the blank? 'We finished the funeral with just [___].' The cats hold up 身元, 身内, and 身の上 as candidates; the mascot's own hint: this is about 家族や親せき — family and relatives.")
※葬式 soushiki — a funeral

身内 (miuchi) — 身内だけで葬式を済ませた, "we held the funeral with just family/relatives present."

Q1

彼は、（a. 前借り　b. 前科）があるので、就職は難しいだろう。Kare wa, (a. maegari / b. zenka) ga aru node, shuushoku wa muzukashii darou.

Full sentence: 彼は、前科があるので、就職は難しいだろう。Kare wa, zenka ga aru node, shuushoku wa muzukashii darou.
EN: Since he has a criminal record, finding a job will probably be difficult.
HI: उसका आपराधिक रिकॉर्ड होने की वजह से, नौकरी मिलना मुश्किल होगा।
GU: તેનો ગુનાહિત રેકોર્ડ હોવાથી, નોકરી મળવી મુશ્કેલ હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a前借りmaegariborrowing in advance (wrong meaning — not a job barrier by itself)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b前科zenkaa criminal recordआपराधिक रिकॉर्डગુનાહિત રેકોર્ડ

Q2

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a正当seitoulegitimate, properउचितયોગ્ય

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This kanji-focused chapter's trap is the same as Week 4's: several 身-words and 取-words look similar but occupy very different domains.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
身の上mi no ue One's personal life circumstances An abstract life story/situation, not a physical thing. 身の上相談 — an advice column about someone's personal troubles.
身の回りmi no mawari One's personal belongings/everyday surroundings Physical items one keeps close, or everyday practical matters. 身の回りのものを持って逃げる — grabbing one's belongings to flee.
身元mimoto One's identity/background Who a person is — used for identification, references, backgrounds. 身元不明の遺体 — a body whose identity is unknown.
生身namami A living body of flesh and blood Emphasizes being alive/physically vulnerable, the opposite of身元's identity sense. 生身の体で立ち向かう — to face something with one's own bare body.
手取りtedori Take-home pay Money left after deductions — always about salary. 手取り20万円 — a take-home salary of 200,000 yen.
日取りhidori A set date for an event About scheduling a calendar date, unrelated to 取り meaning "take" here. 結婚式の日取りを決める — to set a wedding date.
下取りshitadori Trading in an old item An old item's value offset against a new purchase. 車を下取りに出す — to trade in a car.
表向きomotemuki Outwardly, on the surface Contrasts a stated/apparent reason with the real one underneath. 表向きの理由 — an official reason (that may not be the true one).
前向きmaemuki Positive, forward-looking About a person's attitude/outlook, not concealment. 前向きに考える — to think positively about something.
Idiom trap: 根回し literally suggests "working around the roots" (an arborist's term for preparing a tree's roots before transplanting it), but in everyday use it always means quietly lobbying people or laying groundwork before a decision — it cannot be swapped for a literal "preparation" word like 準備 or 手はず, which don't carry that behind-the-scenes, political nuance.
