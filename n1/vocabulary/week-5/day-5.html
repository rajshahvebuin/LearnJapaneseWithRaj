JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 5 — 中級漢字の語彙を覚えよう (Chuukyuu kanji no goi o oboeyou)

5日目 — 背く・経る・値する (Somuku / Heru / Ataisuru)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.84–85 (PDF pages 74–75). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
動詞②（グループⅠ・Ⅱ・Ⅲ） — continues Day 4's verb theme, now mixing Group I (く/ぐ-ending), Group II (～iru/～eru), and Group III (漢字一字＋する, and 漢字一字＋じる／ずる) verbs, again grouped by conjugation pattern rather than shared kanji.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.87 (this week's Day 6, second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
The book itself notes that for the 漢字一字＋じる（Ⅱ）group, じる can always be swapped for ずる（Ⅲ）with no change in meaning (e.g. 演じる／演ずる) — both are given as valid, and this page lists the more common じる form.
→ = a related verb printed alongside the headword (often the transitive/intransitive counterpart or an equivalent phrase), shown in the Note column with its own romaji + gloss
(＝〜) = an equivalent alternate verb form the book prints in parentheses right after the headword
Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
美しい音楽に耳を（　）。Utsukushii ongaku ni mimi o (___).
("Which word fits the blank? '[___] one's ear to beautiful music.' The cats hold up 傾けた, 整えた, and 試みた as candidates.")

傾けた (katamuketa) — 耳を傾ける, "to listen attentively to."

GU: સાંજની લાલાશથી રંગાયેલું આકાશ ખૂબ સુંદર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a暮れたkuretagrew dark (wrong meaning — doesn't take 赤みを as an object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This chapter's 漢字一字＋する／じる verbs are easy to mix up because they all share the pattern [kanji]+する/じる but carry unrelated meanings — exactly what Q3, Q6, and Q7 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
案じるanjiru To worry about (someone) Personal emotional concern for a person's wellbeing. 両親の健康を案じる — to worry about one's parents' health.
報じるhoujiru To report (news) Only for media/news coverage, never personal feelings. 新聞が事件を報じる — a newspaper reports on an incident.
重んじるomonjiru To value highly (a principle/quality) About abstract values one holds in high regard. 学歴を重んじる — to place importance on educational background.
興じるkyoujiru To enjoy, be absorbed in (an activity) About leisure/pastime activities one is having fun with. トランプに興じる — to enjoy playing cards.
準じるjunjiru To conform to, follow (a standard) Aligning with an external rule/benchmark. 待遇は年齢に準じる — treatment follows age as its standard.
背くsomuku To disobey, go against The direct opposite of 準じる — defying rather than following a standard. 命令に背く — to disobey an order.
絶えるtaeru To cease, be cut off (general) A broad word for contact/a line of something ending. 連絡が絶える — to lose touch with someone.
途絶えるtodaeru To be abruptly cut off (stronger) Emphasizes a sudden, complete discontinuation. 通信が途絶える — communications are suddenly cut off entirely.
Reading note: The book itself flags that every 漢字一字＋じる（Ⅱ）verb on this page can also be conjugated as 漢字一字＋ずる（Ⅲ）with the identical meaning (演じる＝演ずる, 報じる＝報じる, etc.) — both forms appear in real Japanese, so recognizing either ending matters more than memorizing one as "the" correct form.
