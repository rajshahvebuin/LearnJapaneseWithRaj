Warm-up

何という？Nan to iu?
自分の失敗などを正当化※するために事情を説明することJibun no shippai nado o seitouka suru tame ni jijou o setsumei suru koto
("What's this called? 'Explaining the circumstances in order to justify one's own mistakes, etc.' The cats hold up 口実, 説得, and 言い訳 as candidates.")

言い訳 (iiwake) — an excuse, exactly this act of explaining away one's own failure.

Q1

会議で新しい製品の必要性を（a. つかえた　b. といた）。Kaigi de atarashii seihin no hitsuyousei o (a. tsukaeta / b. toita).

Full sentence: 会議で新しい製品の必要性を説いた。Kaigi de atarashii seihin no hitsuyousei o toita.
EN: At the meeting, he explained persuasively why the new product is necessary.
HI: बैठक में उसने नए उत्पाद की ज़रूरत के बारे में समझाकर बताया।
GU: મીટિંગમાં તેણે નવા ઉત્પાદનની જરૂરિયાત વિશે સમજાવીને કહ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aつかえたtsukaetaserved (wrong meaning — for serving a lord/God)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bといたtoitaexplained persuasivelyसमझाकर बतायाસમજાવીને કહ્યું

Why: 説く means "to explain persuasively/preach a point" — exactly presenting a case for something's necessity at a meeting; 仕える only means "to serve" a person or deity.

Full sentence: これは本物を忠実に再現した模型です。Kore wa honmono o chuujitsu ni saigen shita mokei desu.
EN: This is a model that faithfully recreates the real thing.
HI: यह एक ऐसा मॉडल है जो असली चीज़ को हूबहू फिर से पेश करता है।
GU: આ એક એવું મોડેલ છે જે અસલી વસ્તુને હૂબહૂ ફરીથી રજૂ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a出現shutsugenappearing (wrong meaning — something appearing for the first time, not recreating an existing thing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 再現する ("to recreate/reproduce") fits a model faithfully reproducing a real object; 出現する describes something new appearing/emerging, not a faithful copy.

GU: માતા-પિતાએ સમજાવ્યા પછી મેં આગળ ભણવાનો વિચાર છોડી દીધો.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji-sharing traps are especially sharp — three different 訳-words share a kanji but mean wildly different things:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
直訳chokuyaku A literal translation Translating word-for-word between languages. 文章を直訳する — to translate a sentence literally.
内訳uchiwake An itemized breakdown Breaking a total sum down into its component parts, no translation involved. 請求書の内訳 — the line-item breakdown of a bill.
言い訳iiwake An excuse Verbally justifying one's own failure/lateness — no relation to translation or breakdowns. 遅刻の言い訳をする — to make an excuse for being late.
仕上げるshiageru To finish/complete a piece of work The end stage of creating something (art, a project). 作品を仕上げる — to finish a work of art.
仕入れるshiireru To stock/purchase goods The start of a business cycle — acquiring goods to sell. 商品を仕入れる — to stock up on merchandise.
仕切るshikiru To partition, or to run/control an event Either a literal physical divider, or figuratively being the one in charge. 宴会を仕切る — to be the one running a banquet.
間柄aidagara A relationship between people Always about the connection between two or more people. 親密な間柄 — a close relationship.
人柄hitogara An individual's character A single person's own personal quality/nature. 人柄の良い人物 — a person of good character.
Same-sound trap: 有益（な）"useful/beneficial" and 有力（な）"influential" both start with 有 and describe a positive quality, but 有益 is about a thing's usefulness (a book, a way of spending time) while 有力 is about a person or theory's power/influence — swapping them, as Q6 above tests, produces a sentence that doesn't parse.
