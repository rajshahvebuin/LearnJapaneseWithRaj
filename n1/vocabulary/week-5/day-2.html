Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
医者からガンの（　）を受けた。Isha kara gan no (___) o uketa.
("Which word fits the blank? 'I received [___] of cancer from the doctor.' The cats hold up 告知, 告白, and 予告 as candidates.")

告知 (kokuchi) — ガンの告知を受けた, "received notification that I had cancer."

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.79) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.79). Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.81.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

小さい輪がいくつも（a. 連ねた　b. 連なった）ネックレス。Chiisai wa ga ikutsu mo (a. tsuraneta / b. tsuranatta) nekkuresu.

Full sentence: 小さい輪がいくつも連なったネックレス。Chiisai wa ga ikutsu mo tsuranatta nekkuresu.
EN: A necklace made of many small rings strung together in a row.
HI: कई छोटे छल्ले एक क़तार में जुड़े हुए हार।
GU: અનેક નાની કડીઓ હાર બંધ જોડાયેલો હાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a連ねたtsuranetatransitive form (needs an agent doing the stringing, wrong here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b連なったtsuranattalined up, strung together (intransitive)क़तार में जुड़े हुएહાર બંધ જોડાયેલા

Why: The necklace itself is being described as a state (rings that "are lined up") — the intransitive 連なる fits a noun-modifying description; 連ねる is transitive and requires a subject actively doing the stringing.

Q2

犯人の（a. 予告　b. 口実）どおり、犯行が行われてしまった。Hannin no (a. yokoku / b. koujitsu) doori, hankou ga okonawarete shimatta.

Full sentence: 犯人の予告どおり、犯行が行われてしまった。Hannin no yokoku doori, hankou ga okonawarete shimatta.
EN: Just as the culprit had forewarned, the crime was carried out.
HI: अपराधी की पूर्व चेतावनी के मुताबिक़, अपराध हो ही गया।
GU: ગુનેગારની અગાઉથી આપેલી ચેતવણી મુજબ, ગુનો થઈ જ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a予告yokokuadvance noticeपूर्व चेतावनीઅગાઉથી ચેતવણી
b口実koujitsuan excuse (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 予告どおり ("just as forewarned") matches a criminal's advance warning being fulfilled; 口実 is an excuse for one's own behavior, not a warning about a future act.

Q3

もらった報酬を全員で（a. 公平に　b. 連帯で）分けた。Moratta houshuu o zen'in de (a. kouhei ni / b. rentai de) waketa.

Full sentence: もらった報酬を全員で公平に分けた。Moratta houshuu o zen'in de kouhei ni waketa.
EN: Everyone divided the reward they received fairly amongst themselves.
HI: मिले हुए इनाम को सबने आपस में बराबर बाँट लिया।
GU: મળેલા ઈનામને બધાએ સરખા ભાગે વહેંચી લીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a公平にkouhei nifairly, equallyबराबरસરખા ભાગે
b連帯でrentai dejointly (describes shared responsibility, not how a division is made)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 公平に分ける ("to divide fairly/equally") describes the manner of splitting something; 連帯 describes shared/joint liability, a different concept from splitting a reward evenly.

Q4

その店員は（a. 誠実に　b. 真実に）対応をしてくれた。Sono ten'in wa (a. seijitsu ni / b. shinjitsu ni) taiou o shite kureta.

Full sentence: その店員は誠実に対応をしてくれた。Sono ten'in wa seijitsu ni taiou o shite kureta.
EN: That store clerk responded to me sincerely/honestly.
HI: उस दुकान के कर्मचारी ने ईमानदारी से मेरी मदद की।
GU: એ દુકાનના કર્મચારીએ પ્રામાણિકતાથી મદદ કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a誠実にseijitsu nisincerelyईमानदारी सेપ્રામાણિકતાથી
b真実にshinjitsu nitruthfully (真実 is a noun for "the truth," not naturally used as an adverb describing conduct)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 誠実 describes a person's sincere, honest manner of behaving toward others; 真実 refers to objective truth/facts, not a person's attitude.

Q5

彼は母親に万引きをしてしまったことを（a. 告白　b. 告知）した。Kare wa hahaoya ni manbiki o shite shimatta koto o (a. kokuhaku / b. kokuchi) shita.

Full sentence: 彼は母親に万引きをしてしまったことを告白した。Kare wa hahaoya ni manbiki o shite shimatta koto o kokuhaku shita.
EN: He confessed to his mother that he had shoplifted.
HI: उसने अपनी माँ को क़बूल किया कि उसने दुकान से चोरी की थी।
GU: તેણે તેની માતાને કબૂલ કર્યું કે તેણે દુકાનમાંથી ચોરી કરી હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a告白kokuhakuto confessक़बूल करनाકબૂલ કરવું
b告知kokuchito notify (wrong direction — for delivering news to someone, not admitting one's own wrongdoing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 告白する is for confessing one's own secret/wrongdoing (or feelings); 告知する is for a third party (e.g. a doctor) delivering news to someone else.

Q6

我々は、自然と（a. 温和　b. 調和）のとれた街作りを目指している。Wareware wa, shizen to (a. onwa / b. chouwa) no toreta machizukuri o mezashite iru.

Full sentence: 我々は、自然と調和のとれた街作りを目指している。Wareware wa, shizen to chouwa no toreta machizukuri o mezashite iru.
EN: We are aiming to build a town that is in harmony with nature.
HI: हम प्रकृति के साथ सामंजस्य रखने वाला शहर बनाना चाहते हैं।
GU: અમે પ્રકૃતિ સાથે સંવાદિતા ધરાવતું શહેર બનાવવા માંગીએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a温和onwamild/gentle (describes a climate or personality, not a relationship between two things)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several words sharing a kanji look interchangeable in English but occupy different domains — exactly Q3, Q4, and Q6's traps above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
公正kousei Fair, just (dealings/processes) About a process or system being free from bias or corruption. 公正な取り引き — a fair (uncorrupted) transaction.
公平kouhei Fair, equal (a division/treatment) About treating individuals/portions equally, not a system's integrity. 公平に分ける — to divide something equally among people.
誠実seijitsu Sincere, honest (a person's conduct) Describes how someone behaves toward others. 誠実な対応 — a sincere, honest response to a customer.
真実shinjitsu The truth (a fact) An objective fact, not a manner of behaving — rarely used adverbially. 真実を語る — to speak the truth (state a fact).
温和onwa Mild, gentle (an inherent quality) A single thing's own gentle nature (a climate, a personality). 温和な気候 — a mild climate, on its own.
調和chouwa Harmony, balance (a relationship) How two or more things fit together well. 自然と調和のとれた街 — a town in harmony with nature.
告白kokuhaku To confess (one's own secret) The speaker reveals something about themselves. 罪を告白する — to confess to a wrongdoing.
告知kokuchi To notify (someone else of news) A third party delivers news to the listener. ガンの告知を受ける — to be notified of a cancer diagnosis.
Transitivity trap: 連なる (intransitive — things "are lined up") and 連ねる (transitive — someone "lines things up") share the same 連 kanji and a near-identical sound, but take opposite grammatical roles, exactly as Q1 above tests. Always check whether the sentence names an agent doing the lining-up (連ねる) or just describes the resulting state (連なる).
