Q1

新薬の効果をまず動物で（　）研究しています。Shinyaku no kouka o mazu doubutsu de (___) kenkyuu shite imasu.

Full sentence: 新薬の効果をまず動物で試して研究しています。...mazu doubutsu de tameshite kenkyuu shite imasu.
EN: We are researching the new drug's effects by first testing it on animals.
HI: हम नई दवा के असर की जाँच पहले जानवरों पर करके शोध कर रहे हैं।
GU: અમે નવી દવાની અસર પહેલા પ્રાણીઓ પર ચકાસીને સંશોધન કરી રહ્યા છીએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1慣らしてnarashitegetting accustomed to (wrong meaning)
2荒らしてarashiteravaging/damaging (wrong meaning)
3試してtameshitetrying out, testing
4志してkokorozashiteaspiring to (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 4's 試す ("to try out/test") is exactly what animal testing of a new drug's effects is.

Q2

兄弟でもめないように、ケーキを（　）に切り分けた。Kyoudai de momenai you ni, keeki o (___) ni kiriwaketa.

Full sentence: 兄弟でもめないように、ケーキを公平に切り分けた。...keeki o kouhei ni kiriwaketa.
EN: So the siblings wouldn't fight, the cake was cut into equal, fair portions.
HI: भाई-बहनों में झगड़ा न हो, इसलिए केक बराबर-बराबर बाँटा गया।
GU: ભાઈ-બહેનો વચ્ચે ઝઘડો ન થાય એટલે કેક સરખા ભાગે વહેંચી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1公正kouseifair (about dealings/processes, not literally dividing food)
2公私koushipublic and private (wrong meaning)
3公用kouyouofficial use (wrong meaning)
4公平kouheifair, equal (a division)

Why: Day 2's 公平に分ける is precisely "to divide equally/fairly" — matching cutting a cake into equal shares.

Q3

（　）だけでささやかな結婚式を挙げた。(___) dake de sasayaka na kekkonshiki o ageta.

Full sentence: 身内だけでささやかな結婚式を挙げた。Miuchi dake de sasayaka na kekkonshiki o ageta.
EN: We held a modest wedding with just family present.
HI: सिर्फ़ परिवार वालों के साथ एक छोटी-सी शादी की।
GU: ફક્ત કુટુંબીજનો સાથે એક નાનકડું લગ્ન કર્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1身近michikaclose/familiar (an adjective, not a group of people)
2身内miuchifamily/relatives
3身の上mi no ueone's life circumstances (wrong meaning)
4単身tanshinalone, by oneself (wrong meaning — a wedding needs at least two people)

Why: Day 1's 身内だけで祝う ("to celebrate with only family present") is the exact fixed phrase for a small family-only event.

Q4

隣人は変わっているが、このあたりは環境に（　）地域です。Rinjin wa kawatte iru ga, kono atari wa kankyou ni (___) chiiki desu.

Full sentence: 隣人は変わっているが、このあたりは環境に恵まれている地域です。...kono atari wa kankyou ni megumarete iru chiiki desu.
EN: The neighbor is a bit odd, but this area is a region blessed with a good environment.
HI: पड़ोसी थोड़ा अजीब है, पर यह इलाक़ा अच्छे माहौल से नवाज़ा हुआ है।
GU: પડોશી થોડો વિચિત્ર છે, પણ આ વિસ્તાર સારા વાતાવરણથી નવાજાયેલો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1恵まれているmegumarete irublessed with
2悩まされているnayamasarete irutroubled/plagued by (wrong meaning — the sentence is positive about the environment)
3和んでいるnagonde iruis put at ease (wrong meaning — for a feeling, not an environment being described as blessed)
4目覚めているmezamete iruis awake/awakened (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 4's 恵む, in its passive form 恵まれている, is the standard way to describe a place as "blessed with" (a good climate/environment).

Q5

この新しい装置は自動車産業にとって、（　）な発明である。Kono atarashii souchi wa jidousha sangyou ni totte, (___) na hatsumei de aru.

Full sentence: この新しい装置は自動車産業にとって、画期的な発明である。...jidousha sangyou ni totte, kakkiteki na hatsumei de aru.
EN: This new device is an epoch-making invention for the automobile industry.
HI: यह नया उपकरण ऑटोमोबाइल उद्योग के लिए एक युगांतरकारी आविष्कार है।
GU: આ નવું ઉપકરણ ઓટોમોબાઈલ ઉદ્યોગ માટે એક યુગપરિવર્તક શોધ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1客観的kyakkantekiobjective (wrong meaning)
2正当的seitoutekinot a standard word (正当 doesn't normally take 的)
3画期的kakkitekiepoch-making
4根本的konpontekifundamental (wrong meaning — describes a root cause, not a breakthrough)

Why: Day 6's 画期的な発明/発見 is the fixed phrase for praising a groundbreaking invention.

Q6

彼の推理は、はなはだしく（　）違いだ。Kare no suiri wa, hanahadashiku (___) chigai da.

Full sentence: 彼の推理は、はなはだしく見当違いだ。Kare no suiri wa, hanahadashiku kentou chigai da.
EN: His reasoning is way off the mark.
HI: उसका अनुमान बिल्कुल ग़लत दिशा में है।
GU: તેનું અનુમાન સાવ ખોટી દિશામાં છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1実態jittaiactual situation (wrong meaning — doesn't combine with 違い this way)
2見当kentoua guess (見当違い = "wide of the mark")
3根気konkiperseverance (wrong meaning)
4内訳uchiwakean itemized breakdown (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 1's 見当 combines with 違い as the fixed idiom 見当違い ("a mistaken guess, wide of the mark") — none of the other options form a real compound with 違い.

Q7

彼はまだ高校1年生だが、将来（　）な野球選手になるだろう。Kare wa mada koukou ichinensei da ga, shourai (___) na yakyuu senshu ni naru darou.

Full sentence: 彼はまだ高校1年生だが、将来有望な野球選手になるだろう。...shourai yuubou na yakyuu senshu ni naru darou.
EN: He's still only a high school first-year, but he'll probably become a promising baseball player.
HI: वह अभी हाई स्कूल के पहले साल में है, पर आगे चलकर एक होनहार बेसबॉल खिलाड़ी बनेगा।
GU: તે હજુ હાઈસ્કૂલના પહેલા વર્ષમાં છે, પણ ભવિષ્યમાં એક આશાસ્પદ બેઝબોલ ખેલાડી બનશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1有益yuuekiuseful/beneficial (wrong meaning — for things, not a person's future prospects)
2有力yuuryokuinfluential (wrong meaning — for someone with power now, not future promise)
3有望yuuboupromising
4画期的kakkitekiepoch-making (wrong meaning — for inventions, not a person's career)

Why: Day 3's 有望な研究者-type usage extends naturally to 有望な野球選手 ("a promising baseball player") — describing someone's future potential.

Q8

仕事をサボって、マージャンに（　）いたところを上司に見つかってしまった。Shigoto o sabotte, maajan ni (___) ita tokoro o joushi ni mitsukatte shimatta.

Full sentence: 仕事をサボって、マージャンに興じていたところを上司に見つかってしまった。...maajan ni kyoujite ita tokoro o joushi ni mitsukatte shimatta.
EN: I skipped work, and my boss caught me right in the middle of enjoying a game of mahjong.
HI: काम से बचकर माह-जोंग खेलने में मशग़ूल था, तभी बॉस ने पकड़ लिया।
GU: કામમાંથી છટકીને માહજોંગ રમવામાં મશગૂલ હતો, ત્યાં જ બોસે પકડી પાડ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1演じてenjiteperforming/playing a role (wrong meaning)
2興じてkyoujiteenjoying oneself with
3群がってmuragatteswarming/flocking (wrong meaning)
4病んでyandesuffering illness (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 5's ～に興じる ("to enjoy oneself doing ~") is the exact fit for being absorbed in a game of mahjong.

Q9

（　）通りのやり方ではだめだ。別の対策を考えなければならない。(___) doori no yarikata de wa dame da. Betsu no taisaku o kangaenakereba naranai.

Full sentence: 従来通りのやり方ではだめだ。別の対策を考えなければならない。Juurai doori no yarikata de wa dame da. Betsu no taisaku o kangaenakereba naranai.
EN: Doing it the same way as always won't work. We have to think of another approach.
HI: हमेशा की तरह करना अब काम नहीं करेगा। कोई और उपाय सोचना होगा।
GU: હંમેશની જેમ કરવાથી નહીં ચાલે. બીજો કોઈ ઉપાય વિચારવો પડશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1従来juuraiconventional, as always
2自己jikoself (wrong meaning)
3意欲iyokueagerness (wrong meaning)
4手際tegiwaskill/efficiency (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 6's 従来通り ("as has always been done") is the exact fixed phrase for the old, conventional way of doing something.

Q10

彼女の家がゴミだらけだという（　）を誰も知らない。Kanojo no ie ga gomi darake da to iu (___) o dare mo shiranai.

Full sentence: 彼女の家がゴミだらけだという実態を誰も知らない。...to iu jittai o dare mo shiranai.
EN: No one knows the actual reality that her house is full of garbage.
HI: यह सच्चाई कि उसका घर कचरे से भरा है, किसी को पता नहीं।
GU: તેનું ઘર કચરાથી ભરેલું છે એ ખરી હકીકત કોઈને ખબર નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1言い訳iiwakean excuse (wrong meaning)
2表向きomotemukioutward appearance (wrong meaning — this is a hidden reality, not a stated official reason)
3現行genkoucurrent, in effect (wrong meaning)
4実態jittaithe actual situation

Why: Day 2's 実態 ("the actual state of affairs") fits a hidden, unpleasant reality that "no one knows" — exactly this sentence's structure.

Q13

りっぱに見えたりして、目立つ。Rippa ni mietari shite, medatsu. ("To look impressive and stand out.")

#JapaneseEnglish
1栄えるsakaeruto prosper (wrong meaning — about a place/nation flourishing over time, not visually standing out)
2映えるhaeruto be set off attractively, stand out
3生えるhaeruto grow/sprout (wrong meaning — an unrelated homophone-like word for plants/hair)
4帯びるobiruto be tinged with (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 5's 映える (as in 夕日に映える) describes something being set off beautifully and standing out visually — exactly this definition; 栄える describes prosperity/flourishing over time, and 生える means "to grow" (plants, hair), an unrelated word that happens to share the reading はえる.

Q16

Q17

問題4 — which sentence uses the word correctly? (1問4点)

Q21

和むnagomu

#JapaneseEnglish
1朝から気持ちが悪かったが、少し寝たら和みました。Asa kara kimochi ga warukatta ga, sukoshi netara nagomimashita."I felt sick since morning, but after sleeping a bit I 和みました" — wrong; recovering from feeling ill/nauseated needs 治る/よくなる, not 和む
2日本の国旗が空に和んでいた。Nihon no kokki ga sora ni nagonde ita."The Japanese flag was 和んでいた in the sky" — wrong; a flag waving in the sky needs はためく, not 和む
3彼のジョークのおかげで、固かった雰囲気が一気に和んだ。Kare no jooku no okage de, katakatta fun'iki ga ikki ni nagonda.Thanks to his joke, the tense atmosphere relaxed all at once.
4彼とは古くから和んだ友達です。Kare to wa furuku kara nagonda tomodachi desu."He's a longtime 和んだ friend" — wrong; a longtime, easy-going friend needs 気心の知れた, not 和んだ as a noun modifier

Why: 和む describes an atmosphere/feeling being softened and put at ease — sentence 3 matches Day 2's own example (心が和む extended to 雰囲気が和む) exactly; 1, 2, and 4 each swap in a meaning (recovering from illness, a flag fluttering, an old friendship) that a different word actually covers.

Q22

勝手katte

#JapaneseEnglish
1勝手に決めないで、私にも相談してください。Katte ni kimenaide, watashi ni mo soudan shite kudasai.Don't decide on your own without asking — consult me too.
2秋になって気温が勝手に下がってきた。Aki ni natte kion ga katte ni sagatte kita."The temperature dropped 勝手に as autumn came" — wrong; a natural, automatic process needs 自然に/ひとりでに, not 勝手に which implies willful human agency
3あなたの意見を聞けないのは、私勝手です。Anata no iken o kikenai no wa, watashi katte desu.Grammatically malformed — the fixed phrase is 自分勝手／身勝手, not "私勝手"
4勝手の結果で、不合格になってしまった。Katte no kekka de, fugoukaku ni natte shimatta."As a result of 勝手, I failed" — wrong; doesn't make sense as a cause for failing an exam

Why: 勝手に決める ("to decide arbitrarily/selfishly, without consulting others") is the standard usage — sentence 1 matches Day 6's own example (勝手な行動をとる) exactly; 2–4 each misuse it for a natural process, a malformed compound, or an unrelated cause.

Q23

値しないataishinai

#JapaneseEnglish
1その製品は値しない評判がある。Sono seihin wa ataishinai hyouban ga aru."That product has a reputation that 値しない" — grammatically malformed; 値する needs a specific に-marked object (e.g. 信頼に値する), not a vague noun modifier
2彼女の行動は問題があるから、私に値しない人物だ。Kanojo no koudou wa mondai ga aru kara, watashi ni ataishinai jinbutsu da."Her behavior is problematic, so she's a person who 私に値しない" — wrong/malformed; 値する needs an abstract standard like 尊敬 (respect), not a personal pronoun
3この本は内容に値しないと言われている。Kono hon wa naiyou ni ataishinai to iwarete iru."This book is said to 内容に値しない" — wrong/unclear referent; doesn't parse against a standard the way the book's own example does
4社員を大事にしない会社は、信頼に値しない。Shain o daiji ni shinai kaisha wa, shinrai ni ataishinai.A company that doesn't value its employees isn't worthy of trust.

Why: ～に値する／値しない ("to be worth/not worth ~") always takes a specific abstract standard marked by に (信頼, 尊敬, 一読) — sentence 4 matches Day 5's own example (この本は一読に値する) exactly in structure; 1–3 each attach 値する to the wrong kind of object.

Q24

根回しnemawashi

#JapaneseEnglish
1今度は、根回さないようにしたから大丈夫です。Kondo wa, nemawasanai you ni shita kara daijoubu desu.Grammatically malformed — 根回し is a noun that pairs with する (根回しする), not a plain verb "根回す"
2選挙での根回しが失敗して、彼は落選したと言われている。Senkyo de no nemawashi ga shippai shite, kare wa rakusen shita to iwarete iru.It's said that his behind-the-scenes groundwork for the election failed, and he lost.
3大至急この書類を根回ししておいてください。Daishikyuu kono shorui o nemawashi shite oite kudasai."Please urgently 根回し this document" — wrong; 根回し targets people/support one lobbies, not a document itself
4根回しで会社をやめたけれど、後悔している。Nemawashi de kaisha o yameta keredo, koukai shite iru."I quit the company because of 根回し, but I regret it" — wrong; quitting isn't something 根回し (groundwork/lobbying) causes

Why: 根回し names the quiet, behind-the-scenes work of building support among the people who matter for a decision — sentence 2 matches Day 1's own example (関係者に根回しする) in spirit exactly, describing pre-election lobbying; 1, 3, and 4 each misuse the word's grammar or target.

Q25

実入りmiiri

#JapaneseEnglish
1今年は会社に三人の実入りが来た。Kotoshi wa kaisha ni sannin no miiri ga kita."This year, three people's 実入り came to the company" — wrong; should be 新入り (newcomers), an unrelated word
2今度の仕事はかなり実入りがよい。Kondo no shigoto wa kanari miiri ga yoi.This next job pays quite well.
3彼は若いが、とても実入りがよい。Kare wa wakai ga, totemo miiri ga yoi."He's young, but very 実入りがいい" — wrong register; 実入り describes a job/business's profitability, not a person's own trait out of context
4彼女は初心者ながら、立派に実入りを果たした。Kanojo wa shoshinsha nagara, rippa ni miiri o hatashita."Though a beginner, she splendidly 実入りを果たした" — wrong; not a real collocation, 役目を果たす ("fulfill a role") is the standard phrase with 果たす

Why: 実入りがいい ("to pay well, be profitable") describes a job or business venture — sentence 2 matches Day 2's own example (実入りのいい商売) exactly; 1, 3, and 4 each misapply the word to a person or force it into a non-existent collocation.
