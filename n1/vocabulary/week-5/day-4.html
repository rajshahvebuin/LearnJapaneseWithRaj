Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
私は足が悪いが、日常生活を（　）には問題がない。Watashi wa ashi ga warui ga, nichijou seikatsu o (___) ni wa mondai ga nai.
("Which word fits the blank? 'My leg isn't good, but there's no problem in [___] daily life.' The cats hold up 満たす, 営む, and 操る as candidates.")

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼女は歌手を（a. 志して　b. 試して）上京した。Kanojo wa kashu o (a. kokorozashite / b. tameshite) joukyou shita.

Full sentence: 彼女は歌手を志して上京した。Kanojo wa kashu o kokorozashite joukyou shita.
EN: She came to Tokyo aspiring to become a singer.
HI: वह गायिका बनने का सपना लेकर तोक्यो आई।
GU: તે ગાયિકા બનવાનું સપનું લઈને ટોક્યો આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a志してkokorozashiteaspiring toसपना लेकरસપનું લઈને
b試してtameshitetrying out (wrong meaning — for testing a thing, not pursuing a career goal)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 志す is specifically about aspiring toward a long-term goal/career; 試す means to try something out to see the result, a different sense entirely.

Full sentence: 年を取っても、彼の腕は鈍っていない。Toshi o totte mo, kare no ude wa nibutte inai.
EN: Even as he's aged, his skill hasn't dulled.
HI: उम्र बढ़ने के बावजूद, उसका हुनर कम नहीं हुआ है।
GU: ઉંમર વધવા છતાં, તેની આવડત ઓછી થઈ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a鈍ってnibuttedulled, lost its edgeकम हुआઓછું થયું
b乱してmidashitedisturbed/disordered (wrong meaning and wrong transitivity — needs a direct object, not a skill "going bad" on its own)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: કૃપા કરીને તમારી તબિયતને નુકસાન ન થાય તેનું ધ્યાન રાખો.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This verb-focused chapter's own trap is near-synonym verbs that look interchangeable in English but take different objects/registers — exactly Q3, Q4, and Q5 above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
損なうsokonau To damage/harm (health) Pairs with the abstract noun 健康 itself. 健康を損なう — to harm one's health (in general).
病むyamu To suffer from (a specific illness/organ) Pairs with a named illness or body part, not the abstract word "health." 肺を病む — to suffer from a lung disease.
満たすmitasu To fill / to meet (a requirement) The object is a container or a standard/request being satisfied. 基準を満たす — to meet a standard.
果たすhatasu To achieve / fulfill (a purpose or role) The object is a goal, duty, or role one carries out to completion. 目的を果たす — to achieve one's purpose; 責任を果たす — to fulfill a duty.
乱すmidasu To throw into disorder (transitive) Someone/something actively disturbs an abstract order. 秩序を乱す — to disturb public order.
荒らすarasu To physically damage/ravage Concrete physical damage, typically to a place or crop. イノシシが畑を荒らす — wild boars ravage a field.
Idiom trap: 気に病む ("to worry/fret over something," lit. "to fall ill in one's spirit") is a fixed idiom built on 病む, but it does not mean the person is literally sick — don't confuse it with the plain, literal 肺を病む ("to suffer from a lung disease"). The same verb, two very different registers depending on what follows 気に vs. a body part.
