6日目 — 先・道・種 (Saki / Michi / Tane)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.102–103 (PDF pages 92–93). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
先 (saki — tip/ahead/future/beforehand/priority — one word covering space, time, and precedence), 道 (michi — road/path/field of expertise/morality), and 種 (tane — seed/ingredient/cause/topic/trick). Two bonus headwords open the day: あたり (atari — vicinity/a guess/a "hit") and 筋 (suji — tendon/streak/plot/aptitude/logic).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.106 (further into this week's Day 7 test pages, since a pure-test day leaves no room right after Day 6's own practice) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes

Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
その小説の（　）を教えてください。Sono shousetsu no (___) o oshiete kudasai.
("What fits the blank? — 'Please tell me the [___] of that novel.'" The cat notes: この文は「先」を入れても意味が通じますね — "this sentence would also make sense with 先 in it," hinting at how easily this chapter's words overlap.)

Q1

首相はきちんと（a.筋の入った b.筋の通った）説明をした。Shushou wa kichinto (a. suji no haitta b. suji no tootta) setsumei o shita.

Full sentence: 首相はきちんと筋の通った説明をした。Shushou wa kichinto suji no tootta setsumei o shita.
EN: The prime minister gave a properly logical explanation.
HI: प्रधानमंत्री ने ठीक ढंग से तर्कसंगत स्पष्टीकरण दिया।
GU: વડાપ્રધાને બરાબર તાર્કિક ખુલાસો આપ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a筋の入ったsuji no haittahaving a streak/line in it (wrong meaning — a physical streak, not logic)
b筋の通ったsuji no toottalogical, coherent

Why: 筋が通る ("to be logical") is the fixed idiom for a coherent explanation.

Q2

工事はだいぶ（a.先へ b.道を）進んだ。Kouji wa daibu (a. saki e b. michi o) susunda.

Full sentence: 工事はだいぶ先へ進んだ。Kouji wa daibu saki e susunda.
EN: Construction has progressed quite far.
HI: निर्माण कार्य काफ़ी आगे बढ़ गया है।
GU: બાંધકામ ઘણું આગળ વધ્યું છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a先へsaki eforward, ahead
b道をmichi owrong particle/word — 道を進む needs no 先 competing here, and idiomatically "progress" uses 先へ進む

Why: 先へ進む ("to advance forward") is the fixed phrase for progress, matching today's own 目的地を目指して先へ進む example.

Q3

（a.話の先 b.話の種）に、新しくできたレストランに食べに行ってみた。(a. Hanashi no saki b. Hanashi no tane) ni, atarashiku dekita resutoran ni tabe ni itte mita.

Full sentence: 話の種に、新しくできたレストランに食べに行ってみた。Hanashi no tane ni, atarashiku dekita resutoran ni tabe ni itte mita.
EN: As something to talk about, I went to try the newly opened restaurant.
HI: बातचीत के लिए एक विषय बनाने की ख़ातिर, नए खुले रेस्तराँ में खाना खाने गया।
GU: વાતચીતનો વિષય બનાવવા, નવી ખૂલેલી રેસ્ટોરાંમાં જમવા ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a話の先hanashi no sakithe rest/continuation of a story (wrong meaning)
b話の種hanashi no taneconversation material

Why: 話の種に ("as conversation material/a topic to talk about") describes doing something specifically to have something to talk about later — see Confusion Pairs for how this differs from 話の先.

Q4

犯人の（a.筋 b.当たり）をつけて、捜査を開始した。Hannin no (a. suji b. atari) o tsukete, sousa o kaishi shita.

Full sentence: 犯人の当たりをつけて、捜査を開始した。Hannin no atari o tsukete, sousa o kaishi shita.
EN: They made a guess about the culprit and began the investigation.
HI: अपराधी का अंदाज़ा लगाकर जाँच शुरू की।
GU: ગુનેગારનો અંદાજ લગાવીને તપાસ શરૂ કરી.

Q5

君は（a.筋 b.人当たり）がいいから、すぐに試合に出ることができるだろう。Kimi wa (a. suji b. hitoatari) ga ii kara, sugu ni shiai ni deru koto ga dekiru darou.

Full sentence: 君は筋がいいから、すぐに試合に出ることができるだろう。Kimi wa suji ga ii kara, sugu ni shiai ni deru koto ga dekiru darou.
EN: You've got talent, so you'll probably be able to compete soon.
HI: तुम्हारे अंदर प्रतिभा है, इसलिए जल्द ही मुक़ाबले में उतर पाओगे।
GU: તારામાં આવડત છે, તેથી જલદી હરીફાઈમાં ભાગ લઈ શકીશ.
#JapaneseEnglish
a筋sujiaptitude, talent

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

先 alone spans space, time, and precedence within a single word — plus two "X がいい" and "話の X" traps from today's list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
先（空間）saki (space) Ahead, the tip/front A literal forward direction or physical endpoint. 指の先, 先へ進む.
先（時間）saki (time) Earlier, in advance, or in the future Same word, now describing a point in time — either before now or yet to come. 先にお知らせした, 先のことはわからない.
先（優先）saki (priority) Being first/ahead of others Competing to be earlier than other people, not a place or time per se. 先を争う.
話の先hanashi no saki The rest/continuation of a story already being told You're mid-story and want to hear what comes next. 話の先を聞かせてください.
話の種hanashi no tane Material/fodder for a future conversation You're gathering an experience specifically so you'll have something to talk about later. 話の種に新しい店に行く.
筋がいいsuji ga ii Naturally talented at a skill About raw aptitude for an activity (sport, art, craft). 君は筋がいいから上達が早い.
人当たりがいいhitoatari ga ii Pleasant, easy to get along with About social manner, unrelated to skill or talent. 人当たりのいい人.
Today's real lesson: as the warm-up itself hints, several of this day's words (先, 筋, 道, 種, 当たり) can plausibly fill the same blank in isolation — the JLPT trap is always the specific collocation (話の種 vs 話の先; 筋がいい vs 人当たりがいい), not the word's dictionary meaning alone.
