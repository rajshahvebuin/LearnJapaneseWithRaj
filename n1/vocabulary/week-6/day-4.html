4日目 — 甘い・かたい・重い (Amai / Katai / Omoi)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.98–99 (PDF pages 88–89). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
This chapter's first set of polysemous i-adjectives instead of verbs: 甘い (amai — sweet/lenient/naive/loose), かたい (katai — hard/firm/stubborn/reliable), and 重い (omoi — heavy/important/sluggish/serious). Two bonus adjectives ride along: まずい (mazui — bad-tasting/poorly done/awkward) and 明るい (akarui — bright/cheerful/knowledgeable).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.101 (this week's Day 5, second page) — confirmed, not guessed.

Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
このねじは（　）からすぐに外れてしまう。Kono neji wa (___) kara sugu ni hazurete shimau.

パソコンの動きが重い(＝遅い)。Pasokon no ugoki ga omoi (=osoi).This PC is slow.कंप्यूटर की रफ़्तार धीमी/भारी है।કમ્પ્યુટરની ગતિ ધીમી/ભારે છે.
その仕事を断るのは気が重い(＝憂うつだ)。Sono shigoto o kotowaru no wa ki ga omoi (=yuuutsu da).I am reluctant to turn down the job.उस काम को मना करने का मन भारी/उदास हो रहा है।તે કામની ના પાડવાનું મન ભારે/ઉદાસ થાય છે.
今日の会議の内容は重かった(＝深刻だった)。Kyou no kaigi no naiyou wa omokatta (=shinkoku datta).The contents of today's meeting were serious.आज की बैठक का विषय गंभीर था।આજની મીટિંગનો વિષય ગંભીર હતો.

Q1

これからのローンの返済を考えると気が（a.暗い b.重い）。Kore kara no roon no hensai o kangaeru to ki ga (a. kurai b. omoi).

Full sentence: これからのローンの返済を考えると気が重い。Kore kara no roon no hensai o kangaeru to ki ga omoi.
EN: Thinking about the loan repayments ahead makes me feel gloomy.
HI: आगे की लोन की किस्तों के बारे में सोचकर मन भारी हो जाता है।
GU: આગળની લોનની ચુકવણી વિચારીને મન ભારે થાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a暗いkuraidark (wrong word — 気が暗い isn't a standard phrase)
b重いomoiheavy-hearted, reluctant

Why: 気が重い is the fixed idiom for feeling gloomy/reluctant about something looming, matching today's own example about turning down a job.

Q2

この仕事を（a.まずく b.あまく）見ていると、あとで大変だよ。Kono shigoto o (a. mazuku b. amaku) mite iru to, ato de taihen da yo.

Full sentence: この仕事をあまく見ていると、あとで大変だよ。Kono shigoto o amaku mite iru to, ato de taihen da yo.
EN: If you take this job too lightly, you'll be in trouble later.
HI: इस काम को हल्के में लोगे तो बाद में मुश्किल होगी।
GU: આ કામને હલકામાં લેશો તો પછી મુશ્કેલી થશે.
#JapaneseEnglish

bあまくamakulightly/naively

Why: 甘く見る ("to underestimate, not take seriously") is the fixed collocation, matching today's own 仕事を甘く見る example.

Q6

Q8

EN: Our company takes this accident seriously and will work to prevent recurrence.

GU: અમારી કંપની આ અકસ્માતને ગંભીરતાથી લે છે અને પુનરાવર્તન અટકાવવાનો પ્રયાસ કરશે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's adjectives frequently combine with the same body-part nouns (頭, 気, 顔) but shift meaning completely depending on which adjective is used:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
頭がかたいatama ga katai Stubborn, inflexible (personality) Describes a fixed, unwilling-to-change way of thinking. 父は頭がかたい — my father won't budge on his opinions.
頭が重いatama ga omoi Foggy-headed, sluggish (physical state) Describes tiredness/grogginess, not stubbornness. 寝不足で頭が重い — my head feels heavy from lack of sleep.
気が重いki ga omoi Reluctant, gloomy about something An emotional weight about a specific looming task. その仕事を断るのは気が重い.
気が張るki ga haru Feeling tense/on edge From Day 3's 張る group — alertness/tension, not gloom. 初対面の人の前では気が張る.
甘いamai Naive, overly lenient/optimistic A judgment or plan underestimates a real difficulty. 考えが甘い, 見通しが甘い.
まずいmazui Badly done, inadvisable An action or situation is actively bad, not just naive. 対応がまずい, まずいことになった.
Same face, opposite words: 顔がまずい (from today's list, "not very handsome") and かたい表情 ("a stern look") both describe someone's face negatively, but for completely different reasons — one is about appearance, the other about a tense/unfriendly expression. Don't reach for the wrong "face" adjective on the test.
