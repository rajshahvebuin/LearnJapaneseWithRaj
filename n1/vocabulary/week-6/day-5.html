JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 6 — 意味が多い言葉を覚えよう (Imi ga ooi kotoba o oboeyou)

5日目 — 強い・ばか・あと (Tsuyoi / Baka / Ato)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.100–101 (PDF pages 90–91). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
強い (tsuyoi — strong, in body/will/expertise), ばか(な) (baka — foolish, but idiomatically "negligible/extreme" depending on what verb it pairs with), and あと (ato, incl. 跡 — after/the rest/traces). Two bonus headwords: 根 (ne — root, incl. figurative "true nature" and "holding a grudge") and 波 (nami — wave, incl. figurative "trend/mood swing").
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.103 (this week's Day 6, second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This day is a showcase for a single noun (バカ or 後) completely changing meaning depending on which verb it's paired with — see Confusion Pairs below.

Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
事故が（　）を絶たない。Jiko ga (___) o tatanai.
("What fits the blank? — 'Accidents keep happening one after another, without [___].'" The cat mutters: もう試験を受けるのはやめようかな…受験料もバカにならないし — "Maybe I should stop taking the exam... the exam fee isn't negligible either.")

後 (ato) — 後を絶たない, "there continue to be (accidents), one after another"

Q1

長い人生には、良いときと悪いときの（a.根 b.波）がある。Nagai jinsei ni wa, yoi toki to warui toki no (a. ne b. nami) ga aru.

Full sentence: 長い人生には、良いときと悪いときの波がある。Nagai jinsei ni wa, yoi toki to warui toki no nami ga aru.
EN: In a long life, there are ups and downs.
HI: लंबी ज़िंदगी में अच्छे और बुरे वक़्त की लहरें आती रहती हैं।
GU: લાંબા જીવનમાં સારા-ખરાબ સમયનાં મોજાં આવતાં રહે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a根neroot (wrong word)
b波namiwave, ups and downs

Why: 波がある ("to have waves/ups and downs") describes fluctuation over time, matching 感情に波がある in today's list.

Q2

今日の授業は（a.重く b.ばかに）難しかったね。Kyou no jugyou wa (a. omoku b. baka ni) muzukashikatta ne.

Full sentence: 今日の授業はばかに難しかったね。Kyou no jugyou wa baka ni muzukashikatta ne.
EN: Today's class was awfully difficult, wasn't it.
HI: आज की क्लास बेहद मुश्किल थी, है ना।
GU: આજનો વર્ગ ખૂબ જ અઘરો હતો, નહીં?
#JapaneseEnglish
a重くomokuheavily (wrong word here)
bばかにbaka niextremely, awfully

Why: ばかに + adjective means "extremely," matching today's own 今日はばかに寒い example.

Q3

ホテルを（a.後に回して b.後にして）駅へ向かった。Hoteru o (a. ato ni mawashite b. ato ni shite) eki e mukatta.

Full sentence: ホテルを後にして駅へ向かった。Hoteru o ato ni shite eki e mukatta.
EN: I left the hotel and headed for the station.
HI: होटल छोड़कर स्टेशन की ओर निकल पड़ा।
GU: હોટેલ છોડીને સ્ટેશન તરફ ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a後に回してato ni mawashitepostponing (wrong meaning — you can't "postpone" a hotel)
b後にしてato ni shiteleaving (a place) behind

Why: ～を後にする means "to leave (a place)," matching today's own 故郷を後にする example.

Q4

通うのはいいが、交通費がバカに（a.ならない b.なる）。Kayou no wa ii ga, koutsuuhi ga baka ni (a. naranai b. naru).

Full sentence: 通うのはいいが、交通費がバカにならない。Kayou no wa ii ga, koutsuuhi ga baka ni naranai.
EN: Commuting is fine, but the transportation cost isn't negligible.
HI: आना-जाना ठीक है, पर किराया कोई मामूली रक़म नहीं है।
GU: અવરજવર ઠીક છે, પણ ભાડું નજીવું નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
aならないnaranaiisn't negligible
bなるnarubecomes (wrong — this would flip the meaning to "the cost becomes negligible")

Why: バカにならない (negative form) is the fixed idiom for a cost/effect that turns out to be more significant than expected.

Q5

その病原菌の（a.ばか力 b.息の根）を止める新薬が発明された。Sono byougenkin no (a. baka-jikara b. iki no ne) o tomeru shinyaku ga hatsumei sareta.

Full sentence: その病原菌の息の根を止める新薬が発明された。Sono byougenkin no iki no ne o tomeru shinyaku ga hatsumei sareta.
EN: A new drug was invented that completely wipes out that pathogen.
HI: उस रोगाणु का पूरी तरह ख़ात्मा करने वाली नई दवा बनाई गई।
GU: તે રોગાણુનો સંપૂર્ણ નાશ કરતી નવી દવા શોધાઈ.
#JapaneseEnglish
aばか力baka-jikarasuperhuman strength (wrong meaning here)
b息の根iki no nethe root of breath — "to completely finish off"

Why: 息の根を止める ("to stop the root of one's breath") is the fixed idiom for completely destroying/eliminating something, extended here to wiping out a pathogen.

Q6

昔のことをいつまでも（a.根に持つ b.後を継ぐ）のはやめましょう。Mukashi no koto o itsu made mo (a. ne ni motsu b. ato o tsugu) no wa yamemashou.

Full sentence: 昔のことをいつまでも根に持つのはやめましょう。Mukashi no koto o itsu made mo ne ni motsu no wa yamemashou.

Q7

警官はその不審な男の（　）。Keikan wa sono fushin na otoko no (___).

Full sentence: 警官はその不審な男の後をつけた。Keikan wa sono fushin na otoko no ato o tsuketa.
EN: The police officer tailed the suspicious man.
HI: पुलिस अधिकारी ने उस संदिग्ध आदमी का पीछा किया।
GU: પોલીસ અધિકારીએ તે શંકાસ્પદ માણસનો પીછો કર્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1後ろに回したushiro ni mawashitaturned behind (wrong meaning)
2後を継いだato o tsuidasucceeded (wrong meaning)
3後をつけたato o tsuketatailed, followed secretly
4後にしたato ni shitaleft behind (wrong meaning)

Why: 後をつける ("to tail/secretly follow") is the fixed idiom for surveillance, matching today's own 彼の後をつける example.

Q8

正直者が（　）社会であるべきだ。Shoujiki-mono ga (___) shakai de aru beki da.

Full sentence: 正直者がバカをみない社会であるべきだ。Shoujiki-mono ga baka o minai shakai de aru beki da.
EN: Society should be one where honest people don't end up as the ones who lose out.
HI: समाज ऐसा होना चाहिए जहाँ ईमानदार लोगों को नुक़सान न उठाना पड़े।
GU: સમાજ એવો હોવો જોઈએ જ્યાં પ્રામાણિક લોકોને નુકસાન ન થાય.
#JapaneseEnglish
1バカになるbaka ni narubecomes useless/stripped (wrong meaning — that's for screws, not people)
2バカにならないbaka ni naranaiisn't negligible (wrong meaning here)
3バカをみるbaka o miruloses out (wrong polarity — the sentence needs the negative)
4バカをみないbaka o minaidoesn't end up losing out

Why: バカを見る ("to end up as the loser/sucker") negated as バカを見ない fits "a society where honest people AREN'T the ones who lose out" — matching today's own 自分だけがばかを見る example.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's real trap: the SAME noun (バカ or 後) means something totally different depending on which verb follows it:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
バカにならないbaka ni naranai Not negligible (a cost/effect) Describes an amount that turns out bigger than expected. 交通費がバカにならない.
バカになるbaka ni naru To wear out / stop working (a mechanism) A screw or fastener strips and loses its grip. ねじがばかになる.
バカを見るbaka o miru To end up as the loser/sucker A person suffers a loss because of others' actions. 自分だけがばかを見る.
後を継ぐato o tsugu To succeed/take over a position Inheriting a role or business. 社長の後を継ぐ.
後をつけるato o tsukeru To tail/secretly follow someone Surveillance, not succession. 警官が男の後をつける.
後に回すato ni mawasu To postpone a task Delaying something, not following or inheriting. 面倒なことを後に回す.
後を絶たないato o tatanai To keep happening, one after another Describes a recurring problem/incident, not a person's actions. 事故が後を絶たない.
Test strategy: When バカ or 後 shows up blanked out on the exam, don't just recall "foolish" or "after" — look at the verb immediately attached to it, since that verb alone determines which of several unrelated idioms is in play.
