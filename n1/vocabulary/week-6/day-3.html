JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 6 — 意味が多い言葉を覚えよう (Imi ga ooi kotoba o oboeyou)

3日目 — はる・送る・飛ぶ (Haru / Okuru / Tobu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.96–97 (PDF pages 86–87). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
張る／貼る (haru — to stretch/pitch/tense, or to paste flat things — two different kanji sharing one reading), 送る (okuru — to send/see someone off/spend time), and 飛ぶ (tobu — to fly/jump/vanish). Two bonus headwords ride along this same day: 解ける／溶ける (tokeru, plus the related ほどける reading — to come undone/dissolve/melt/be resolved) and 結ぶ (musubu — to tie/sign/bear fruit).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.99 (this week's Day 4, second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Today's list quietly hides a same-reading, different-kanji trap: 張る (haru — to stretch/pitch) vs 貼る (haru — to paste flat things like posters) both appear in the 張る group below, printed one after another with no explicit callout — flagged in Confusion Pairs.

Q1

彼とは固い友情で（a.結ばれて b.結んで）いる。Kare to wa katai yuujou de (a. musubarete b. musunde) iru.

Full sentence: 彼とは固い友情で結ばれている。Kare to wa katai yuujou de musubarete iru.
EN: We are bound by a strong friendship.
HI: उसके साथ मेरी गहरी दोस्ती का बंधन है।
GU: તેની સાથે મારે ગાઢ મિત્રતાનું બંધન છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a結ばれてmusubaretebound together (passive)
b結んでmusundetying (active — wrong voice for describing a mutual state)

Why: 結ばれている (the passive/stative form) describes two people being bound together by a relationship, matching today's own example 二人は信頼感で結ばれている.

Q2

結婚して数年間は幸せな日々を（a.はって b.送って）いた。Kekkon shite suunenkan wa shiawase na hibi o (a. hatte b. okutte) ita.

Full sentence: 結婚して数年間は幸せな日々を送っていた。Kekkon shite suunenkan wa shiawase na hibi o okutte ita.
EN: For the first few years of marriage, I lived happy days.
HI: शादी के बाद कुछ सालों तक मैं ख़ुशहाल दिन बिता रहा था।
GU: લગ્ન પછી થોડાં વર્ષો સુધી હું સુખી દિવસો પસાર કરી રહ્યો હતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
aはってhattestretching (wrong verb)
b送ってokuttespending (days)

Why: 日々を送る ("to spend one's days") is the fixed collocation for describing how one lives over a period of time.

Q3

努力が実を（a.結んで b.解けて）、実験に成功した。Doryoku ga mi o (a. musunde b. tokete), jikken ni seikou shita.

Full sentence: 努力が実を結んで、実験に成功した。Doryoku ga mi o musunde, jikken ni seikou shita.
EN: The effort bore fruit, and the experiment succeeded.
HI: मेहनत रंग लाई और प्रयोग सफल हुआ।
GU: મહેનત ફળી ને પ્રયોગ સફળ થયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a結んでmusundebearing (fruit)
b解けてtoketecoming undone/resolving (wrong verb)

Why: 実を結ぶ ("to bear fruit," figuratively "to pay off") is the fixed idiom for effort finally producing results.

Q4

私は、胸を（a.はって b.とんで）、この作品には自信があると言えます。Watashi wa, mune o (a. hatte b. tonde), kono sakuhin ni wa jishin ga aru to iemasu.

Full sentence: 私は、胸を張って、この作品には自信があると言えます。Watashi wa, mune o hatte, kono sakuhin ni wa jishin ga aru to iemasu.
EN: I can proudly say I'm confident about this work.
HI: मैं सीना तानकर कह सकता हूँ कि मुझे इस रचना पर भरोसा है।
GU: હું છાતી કાઢીને કહી શકું છું કે મને આ કૃતિ પર વિશ્વાસ છે.

Q5

パソコンがフリーズして一瞬でデータが（a.とんで b.とけて）しまった。Pasokon ga furiizu shite isshun de deeta ga (a. tonde b. tokete) shimatta.

Full sentence: パソコンがフリーズして一瞬でデータが飛んでしまった。Pasokon ga furiizu shite isshun de deeta ga tonde shimatta.
EN: The computer froze and the data vanished in an instant.
HI: कंप्यूटर हैंग हो गया और पल भर में डेटा उड़ गया।
GU: કમ્પ્યુટર ફ્રીઝ થયું ને એક ક્ષણમાં ડેટા ઊડી ગયો.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's list hides two same-reading, different-kanji traps, plus 飛ぶ's split between literal and figurative "vanishing":

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
張るharu To stretch, pitch, tense up Something becomes taut, spread out, or is put up by stretching (a tent, a web, one's chest). テントを張る, 気が張る, 胸を張る.
貼るharu To paste/stick a flat thing on a surface Same reading, different kanji — always a flat object affixed to something (a poster, a stamp, a sticker). ポスターを貼る, 切手を貼る.
解けるtokeru To be resolved / dissolve (figurative or chemical) An abstract problem/misunderstanding is untangled, or a solid dissolves in liquid. 誤解が解ける, 洗剤が水に溶ける.
解けるhodokeru To come untied (physical) Same kanji, different reading — specifically for something knotted/tied coming loose. 靴のひもが解ける.
飛ぶ (literal)tobu To fly, be airborne An actual physical object moves through the air. ヘリコプターが飛ぶ, 看板が飛んだ.
飛ぶ (figurative)tobu To vanish suddenly / rush off Same verb extended to money, data, or a topic disappearing/jumping abruptly — nothing physically flies. ボーナスが飛ぶ, 話があちこちに飛ぶ.
Reading trap: 靴のひもが解ける is read ほどける here (something physically coming untied), not とける — even though it's the same 解ける kanji the day's other examples (誤解が解ける, 難しくて解けない) read as とける. Context — is it a literal knot, or an abstract problem — decides the reading.
