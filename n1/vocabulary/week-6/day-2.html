JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 6 — 意味が多い言葉を覚えよう (Imi ga ooi kotoba o oboeyou)

2日目 — 通る・引く・回す (Tooru / Hiku / Mawasu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.94–95 (PDF pages 84–85). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
Three more polysemous verbs: 通る (tooru — to pass through/carry/be accepted), 引く (hiku — to pull/draw/subtract/inherit), and 回す (mawasu — to turn/rotate/pass around/refer). As on Day 1, the book also slips in a 4th, untitled bonus headword: 乗る (noru — to ride/go along with/be in the mood).

Q1

会議で反対意見が出ないよう、事前に手を（a.引いて b.回して）おいた。Kaigi de hantai iken ga denai you, jizen ni te o (a. hiite b. mawashite) oita.

Full sentence: 会議で反対意見が出ないよう、事前に手を回しておいた。...jizen ni te o mawashite oita.
EN: To prevent objections at the meeting, I arranged things behind the scenes beforehand.
HI: बैठक में विरोध न हो, इसलिए पहले से गुप्त रूप से व्यवस्था कर रखी।
GU: મીટિંગમાં વિરોધ ન થાય એ માટે અગાઉથી છૂપી ગોઠવણ કરી રાખી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a引いてhiitepulling (wrong verb)
b回してmawashitearranging in advance

Why: 手を回す ("to work behind the scenes") is the fixed idiom for quietly arranging things before a meeting.

Q2

こんなに遅い時間まで子どもを（a.連れ回す b.連れ引く）のは、非常識だ。Konna ni osoi jikan made kodomo o (a. tsuremawasu b. tsurehiku) no wa, hijoushiki da.

Full sentence: こんなに遅い時間まで子どもを連れ回すのは、非常識だ。...kodomo o tsuremawasu no wa, hijoushiki da.
EN: Dragging a child around this late is thoughtless.
HI: इतनी देर रात तक बच्चे को इधर-उधर घुमाना समझदारी नहीं है।
GU: આટલી મોડી રાત સુધી બાળકને આમતેમ ફેરવવું અવિવેકી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a連れ回すtsuremawasudrag around
b連れ引くtsurehikunot a real compound verb

Why: 連れ回す is the standard compound for dragging someone along from place to place.

Q3

桜の花びらが風に（a.通って b.乗って）運ばれてきた。Sakura no hanabira ga kaze ni (a. tootte b. notte) hakobarete kita.

Full sentence: 桜の花びらが風に乗って運ばれてきた。Sakura no hanabira ga kaze ni notte hakobarete kita.
EN: Cherry blossom petals were carried along by the wind.
HI: चेरी के फूलों की पंखुड़ियाँ हवा के सहारे उड़ती हुई आईं।
GU: ચેરીના ફૂલોની પાંખડીઓ પવનની સાથે ઊડીને આવી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a通ってtoottepassing through (wrong verb)
b乗ってnotteriding along (with the wind)

Why: 風に乗る ("to ride/be carried on the wind") is the exact phrase for something drifting along on air currents — the book's own example uses pollen the same way.

Q4

この肉はまだ火がじゅうぶんに（a.乗って b.通って）ないから、もう一度焼いてください。Kono niku wa mada hi ga juubun ni (a. notte b. tootte) nai kara, mou ichido yaite kudasai.

Full sentence: この肉はまだ火がじゅうぶんに通っていないから、もう一度焼いてください。...hi ga juubun ni tootte inai kara, mou ichido yaite kudasai.
EN: This meat isn't cooked through yet, so please grill it again.
HI: यह मांस अभी अच्छी तरह पका नहीं है, इसलिए इसे दोबारा भूनिए।
GU: આ માંસ હજુ બરાબર ચડ્યું નથી, તેથી ફરી શેકો.

Q5

彼女は泣いてみんなの注意を（a.引いた b.回した）。Kanojo wa naite minna no chuui o (a. hiita b. mawashita).

Full sentence: 彼女は泣いてみんなの注意を引いた。Kanojo wa naite minna no chuui o hiita.
EN: She cried and drew everyone's attention.
HI: वह रोकर सबका ध्यान खींच लाई।
GU: તે રડીને બધાનું ધ્યાન ખેંચી ગઈ.

Q6

彼はイギリスではかなり名の（a.乗った b.通った）作家です。Kare wa Igirisu de wa kanari na no (a. notta b. tootta) sakka desu.

Q7

EN: I don't feel like it, but when my boss invites me for drinks I can't refuse.
HI: मन नहीं है, फिर भी बॉस जब पीने के लिए बुलाते हैं तो मना नहीं कर पाता।
GU: મન નથી થતું, છતાં બોસ પીવા બોલાવે તો ના પાડી શકાતી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1引けないhikenaicannot withdraw (wrong verb for 気が)
2通らないtooranaidoesn't pass (wrong verb for 気が)

Q8

Full sentence: 彼の提案は筋が通っているが、実現するには難しいだろう。...suji ga tootte iru ga, jitsugen suru ni wa muzukashii darou.
EN: His proposal makes sense, but it'll probably be hard to actually carry out.
HI: उसका प्रस्ताव तर्कसंगत तो है, पर उसे लागू करना मुश्किल होगा।
GU: તેની દરખાસ્ત તાર્કિક છે, પણ અમલમાં મૂકવી અઘરી હશે.

1通っているtootte irumakes sense
2乗っているnotte iruriding (wrong verb)
3回しているmawashite iruturning (wrong verb)
4引いているhiite irupulling (wrong verb)

Why: 筋が通る ("to be logical, make sense") is the fixed collocation, matching today's own example 彼の言うことは筋が通っている.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Four verbs that can all describe something "going along/through," easy to blur together:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
通るtooru To pass through / be accepted / make sense Something moves through a space, or an idea "goes through" logically/officially. 火が通る, 議案が通る, 筋が通る.
引くhiku To pull / draw / recede / inherit A force pulls something, or something recedes/is inherited. 熱が引く, 注意を引く, 血を引く.
乗るnoru To ride along / go along with / be receptive Something rides along on a carrier (wind, rhythm), or a person is willing/in the mood. 風に乗る, 気が乗る, 誘いに乗る.
回すmawasu To turn / rotate / pass along / refer Something is physically rotated, or passed from person to person / department to department. ふたを回す, 電話を回す, 患者を回す.
Same body part, different verb: 気が乗らない ("not in the mood") and 気が引ける ("to feel awkward/hesitant about doing something") both start with 気が but take completely different verbs and mean different things — don't mix them up, as today's Q7 tests directly.
