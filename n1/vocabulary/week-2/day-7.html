問題1 — choose the best word for the blank (1問4点)

Q1

一度払い込んだ入学金は、（　）理由があっても返金しません。Ichido haraikonda nyuugakukin wa, (___) riyuu ga atte mo henkin shimasen.

Full sentence: 一度払い込んだ入学金は、いかなる理由があっても返金しません。...ikanaru riyuu ga atte mo henkin shimasen.
EN: Once paid, the entrance fee will not be refunded, whatever the reason.
HI: एक बार जमा की गई प्रवेश शुल्क राशि किसी भी कारण से वापस नहीं की जाएगी।
GU: એકવાર ભરેલી પ્રવેશ ફી કોઈપણ કારણસર પરત કરવામાં આવશે નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ものすごいmonosugoitremendous (wrong meaning — doesn't fit "reason")
2なにげないnanigenaicasual (wrong meaning)
3いかなるikanaruwhatever kind of
4ややこしいyayakoshiicomplicated (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 6's いかなる〜でも ("whatever ___ it may be") is the fixed pattern for "no matter the reason" — none of the others combine with 理由 this way.

Q2

母はいつも「お金がない、お金がない」と（　）いる。Haha wa itsumo "Okane ga nai, okane ga nai" to (___) iru.

Full sentence: 母はいつも「お金がない、お金がない」とぼやいている。...to boyaite iru.
EN: My mother always grumbles "no money, no money."
HI: मेरी माँ हमेशा "पैसे नहीं हैं, पैसे नहीं हैं" कहकर बड़बड़ाती रहती है।
GU: મારી મમ્મી હંમેશાં "પૈસા નથી, પૈસા નથી" કહીને બબડ્યા કરે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ぼやいてboyaitegrumbling
2とぼけてtoboketeplaying dumb (wrong meaning)
3ぼけてboketebecoming senile/blurry (wrong meaning)
4こりてkoritelearning a lesson (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 1's ぼやく (grumble) fits this casual, habitual complaint about money — the other three are unrelated verbs that only sound similar.

Q3

彼女はいま、ロックに（　）います。Kanojo wa ima, rokku ni (___) imasu.

Full sentence: 彼女はいま、ロックにかぶれています。...rokku ni kaburete imasu.
EN: She's really into rock music right now.
HI: वह इन दिनों रॉक संगीत में पूरी तरह डूबी हुई है।
GU: તે હાલમાં રોક સંગીતમાં સંપૂર્ણપણે ડૂબેલી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かたよってkatayottebeing biased (wrong meaning)
2意気込んでikigondebeing fired up (wrong grammar with に)
3かぶれてkaburetestrongly influenced by
4ばれてbaretebeing exposed (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 3's ロックにかぶれる is the word list's own example for being really into/influenced by a music genre.

Q4

凶悪犯は（　）逮捕されてしまった。Kyouakuhan wa (___) taiho sarete shimatta.

Full sentence: 凶悪犯はあっけなく逮捕されてしまった。...akkenaku taiho sarete shimatta.
EN: The vicious criminal was arrested with surprising ease.
HI: क्रूर अपराधी बड़ी आसानी से पकड़ा गया।
GU: ક્રૂર ગુનેગાર ખૂબ સહેલાઈથી પકડાઈ ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1なにげなくnanigenakucasually (wrong meaning)
2そっけなくsokkenakucurtly (wrong meaning — describes a manner of response, not an arrest's ease)
3みっともなくmittomonakuunseemly (wrong meaning)
4あっけなくakkenakuanticlimactically, with surprising ease

Why: Day 5's あっけない describes something ending faster/more easily than expected — a dangerous criminal being caught without a struggle is exactly this "anticlimactic" nuance.

Q5

このアイスクリームはとても（　）舌触りですね。Kono aisukuriimu wa totemo (___) shitazawari desu ne.

Full sentence: このアイスクリームはとてもなめらかな舌触りですね。...totemo nameraka na shitazawari desu ne.
EN: This ice cream has a very smooth texture, doesn't it.
HI: इस आइसक्रीम का टेक्सचर बहुत मुलायम है, है ना?
GU: આ આઇસક્રીમનું ટેક્સચર ખૂબ મુલાયમ છે, ખરું ને?
#JapaneseEnglish
1あざやかなazayaka navivid (wrong domain — colors)
2のどかなnodoka natranquil (wrong domain — scenery)
3すみやかなsumiyaka naprompt (wrong domain — speed)
4なめらかなnameraka nasmooth

Why: Day 4's 滑らか describes a smooth texture — exactly what "舌触り" (mouthfeel) calls for; the others describe color, scenery, and speed.

Q6

母の愛情の（　）手作りのマフラー。Haha no aijou no (___) tezukuri no mafuraa.

Full sentence: 母の愛情のこもった手作りのマフラー。...aijou no komotta tezukuri no mafuraa.
EN: A handmade scarf filled with mother's love.
HI: माँ के स्नेह से भरा हाथ से बना मफ़लर।
GU: મમ્મીના સ્નેહથી ભરેલો હાથે બનાવેલો મફલર.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からんだkarandatangled (wrong meaning)
2ばれたbaretaexposed (wrong meaning)
3つきたtsukitaran out (wrong meaning)
4こもったkomottafilled with (feeling)

Why: Day 1's こもる can mean a feeling is "packed into" something made with care — 愛情のこもった手作り (handmade, filled with love/affection) is a common set phrase.

Q7

地震で倒れたビルの下から（　）声が聞こえる。Jishin de taoreta biru no shita kara (___) koe ga kikoeru.

Full sentence: 地震で倒れたビルの下からかすかな声が聞こえる。...kasuka na koe ga kikoeru.
EN: A faint voice can be heard from under the building that collapsed in the earthquake.
HI: भूकंप से गिरी इमारत के नीचे से एक धीमी आवाज़ सुनाई देती है।
GU: ભૂકંપથી પડી ગયેલી ઇમારત નીચેથી એક ઝાંખો અવાજ સંભળાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1うつろなutsuro navacant (wrong domain — eyes/expression)
2かすかなkasuka nafaint
3あらゆるarayuruevery kind of (wrong meaning)
4はんぱなhanpa naincomplete (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 6's かすか describes something barely perceptible to the senses, exactly fitting a faint voice heard from under rubble.

Q8

津波はその地方に大きな被害を（　）。Tsunami wa sono chihou ni ookina higai o (___).

Full sentence: 津波はその地方に大きな被害をもたらした。...ookina higai o motarashita.
EN: The tsunami brought great damage to that region.
HI: सुनामी ने उस क्षेत्र में भारी नुक़सान पहुँचाया।
GU: સુનામીએ તે વિસ્તારમાં ભારે નુકસાન સર્જ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1もたらしたmotarashitabrought about
2はかどったhakadottaprogressed (wrong meaning)
3引きずったhikizuttadragged (wrong meaning)
4かさばったkasabattawas bulky (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 3's 被害をもたらす (bring about damage) is the fixed collocation for a disaster causing harm.

Q9

近所に雷が落ちて、（　）音がした。Kinjo ni kaminari ga ochite, (___) oto ga shita.

Full sentence: 近所に雷が落ちて、ものすごい音がした。...monosugoi oto ga shita.
EN: Lightning struck nearby, making a tremendous sound.
HI: पास में बिजली गिरी और ज़बरदस्त आवाज़ हुई।
GU: નજીકમાં વીજળી પડી અને જબરદસ્ત અવાજ થયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1はなはだしいhanahadashiiextreme (usu. negative traits, not a sound's intensity)
2ものすごいmonosugoitremendous
3すばしっこいsubashikkoiagile (wrong meaning)
4おびただしいobitadashiia great number of (wrong domain — for countable things, not sound intensity)

Why: Day 6's ものすごい directly intensifies things like sounds/wind (ものすごい風); おびただしい quantifies countable nouns (an insect swarm), not a single sound's loudness.

Q10

安かったから仕方がないかもしれないが、このソファーは作りがとても（　）です。Yasukatta kara shikata ga nai kamoshirenai ga, kono sofaa wa tsukuri ga totemo (___) desu.

Full sentence: 安かったから仕方がないかもしれないが、このソファーは作りがとても雑です。...tsukuri ga totemo zatsu desu.
EN: It might not be helped since it was cheap, but this sofa's construction is very sloppy.
HI: सस्ता था इसलिए शायद मजबूरी थी, पर इस सोफ़े की बनावट बहुत लापरवाही से की गई है।
GU: સસ્તું હતું એટલે કદાચ છૂટકો ન હતો, પણ આ સોફાની બનાવટ ખૂબ બેદરકારીથી કરેલી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1雑zatsusloppy
2露骨rokotsublunt/overt (wrong domain — for attitudes, not craftsmanship)
3巧妙koumyoucunning (wrong meaning — implies clever scheming, not shoddiness)
4不気味bukimieerie (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 5's 雑な仕事/作り describes sloppy, careless workmanship — exactly this cheap sofa's construction.

問題4 — which sentence uses the word correctly? (1問4点)

Q21

かさばるkasabaru

#JapaneseEnglish
1今月は食事代がかさばってしまった。Kongetsu wa shokujidai ga kasabatte shimatta.This month's food costs "took up space" — wrong; cost increases use かさむ, not かさばる
2雨が降ってきたのでかさばって歩いた。Ame ga futte kita node kasabatte aruita.Walked "bulkily" because it started raining — nonsensical
3袋が大きく、かさばって持ちにくい。Fukuro ga ookiku, kasabatte mochinikui.The bag is big, bulky, and hard to carry.
4住宅がたくさんかさばっている。Juutaku ga takusan kasabatte iru.Houses are "bulking up" a lot — doesn't fit; houses aren't objects one carries

Why: かさばる describes a physical object being awkwardly bulky to carry/store — sentence 3 matches Day 2's own word-list example almost verbatim; sentence 1 confuses it with かさむ (costs increasing), a classic same-root trap.

Q22

どもるdomoru

#JapaneseEnglish
1社長との面談で、緊張のあまりどもってしまった。Shachou to no mendan de, kinchou no amari domotte shimatta.In the meeting with the president, I stammered out of nervousness.
2家に明かりがどもっている。Ie ni akari ga domotte iru.Light in the house is "stammering" — wrong word, likely confusing with ともる (to be lit)
3年をとって頭がだんだんどもってきた。Toshi o totte atama ga dandan domotte kita.As I aged, my head gradually "stammered" — wrong; should be ぼける
4調子の悪かったパソコンがついにどもった。Choushi no warukatta pasokon ga tsuini domotta.The malfunctioning PC finally "stammered" — wrong; should be いかれる

Why: どもる only describes a person stammering while speaking — sentence 1 matches Day 1's own example (スピーチでどもる); the others misapply it to lights, aging minds, and machines, each of which has its own correct verb (ともる, ぼける, いかれる).

Q23

そっけないsokkenai

#JapaneseEnglish
1家の裏でそっけない音がする。Ie no ura de sokkenai oto ga suru.A "curt" sound is heard behind the house — nonsensical, wrong domain
2その事件はそっけなく解決した。Sono jiken wa sokkenaku kaiketsu shita.The incident was resolved "curtly" — wrong; should be あっけなく
3相当がんばってもそっけなく合格できないでしょう。Soutou ganbatte mo sokkenaku goukaku dekinai deshou.Even with great effort, you probably won't "curtly" pass — nonsensical combination
4怒った妻は「勝手にすれば？」とそっけない返事をした。Okotta tsuma wa "Katte ni sureba?" to sokkenai henji o shita.The angry wife gave a curt reply, "Do whatever you want?"

Why: 素っ気ない describes a cold, curt response or attitude toward a person — sentence 4 matches Day 5's own example (素っ気ない返事) exactly; sentences 2–3 confuse it with あっけない (anticlimactic/surprisingly easy), a sound-alike trap.

Q24

すたれるsutareru

#JapaneseEnglish
1長い間水をあげなかったから、植木がすたれてしまった。Nagai aida mizu o agenakatta kara, ueki ga sutarete shimatta.The plant "went out of fashion" from not being watered — wrong; should be 枯れる (wither)
2彼の考え方はすたれていて、年寄りはもちろん若者にも理解できない。Kare no kangaekata wa sutarete ite, toshiyori wa mochiron wakamono ni mo rikai dekinai.His thinking is "outdated," not understood even by the young, let alone the old — logically backwards (old-fashioned ideas are typically the elderly's, not something young people alone would fail to get)
3あんなにはやったゲームだが、あっという間にすたれてしまった。Anna ni hayatta geemu da ga, atto iu ma ni sutarete shimatta.That game was so popular, but it went out of fashion in an instant.
4「知らない」とすたれてもだめだよ。みんな知っているんだから。"Shiranai" to sutaretemo dame da yo. Minna shitte irun dakara.Saying "I don't know" and "going obsolete" doesn't work — wrong word entirely, likely confusing with とぼける

Why: 廃れる describes something that was popular/prevalent falling out of use — sentence 3 mirrors Day 3's own example (流行が廃れる) precisely; sentence 2 misuses it with contradictory logic, and 1/4 confuse it with 枯れる and とぼける respectively.

Q25

ひさしいhisashii

#JapaneseEnglish
1ひさしく素晴らしい映画を見た。Hisashiku subarashii eiga o mita.Watched a "long-time" wonderful movie — wrong; should be 久々に (for the first time in a while)
2そういえば、ひさしくすきやきを食べていないなあ。Souieba, hisashiku sukiyaki o tabete inai naa.Come to think of it, I haven't had sukiyaki in a long time.
3彼は自分の意見をひさしく述べた。Kare wa jibun no iken o hisashiku nobeta.He stated his opinion "for a long time" — wrong; should be 長々と (at length)
4ひさしくない関係の人には、メールアドレスを知らせていない。Hisashikunai kankei no hito niwa, meeru adoresu o shirasete inai.Haven't given my email to people who aren't "hisashii"-related — nonsensical, likely confusing with 親しい (close/intimate)

Why: 久しい pairs with a negative verb to mean "it's been a long time since ___" — sentence 2 matches Day 5's own example (彼に久しく会っていない) exactly; the others misapply it where 久々に, 長々と, or 親しい would be the correct word instead.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

A week-wide cheat sheet — the trickiest near-synonym clusters from Days 1–6, one line each, to drill right before the test:

ClusterCore distinctionDay
くたびれる／ばてるGeneral tiredness vs. physical exhaustion from sustained exertion1
とぼける／ごまかすFeign not knowing vs. actively distort facts/money1
戸惑う／まごつくInner bewilderment vs. visible flustered fumbling1
かさむ／かさばる／重なるCosts rise / physically bulky / schedules overlap — same かさ root, three meanings2
ぶれる／ぼやけるCamera-shake blur vs. general visual/mental indistinctness2
はげる／はぐSomething peels off by itself vs. someone strips it off (自動詞/他動詞)2

Test-day strategy: This test deliberately mixes look-alikes from every day this week — same-root triples that split into different meanings (かさむ／かさばる／重なる in Q21's family), sound-alike traps (しぶとい-style confusion appears as そっけない vs. あっけない in Q23, すたれる vs. 枯れる/とぼける in Q24, and ひさしい vs. 久々に/親しい in Q25), and the recurring 自動詞/他動詞 pattern (とどまる／とどめる, かなう／かなえる, 染める／染まる, 重なる／重ねる). When stuck, ask "does this word describe a person's trait, a physical process, or a fixed set phrase from one specific day?" — that question eliminates most wrong options above.
