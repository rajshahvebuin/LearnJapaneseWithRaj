Warm-up

Q. ___に入るのは？___ ni hairu no wa?
首相は___ところで歓迎された。Shushou wa ___ tokoro de kangei sareta.
("Which word fits the blank? 'The prime minister was welcomed ___.' Hint from the book: 「あらゆる」も入りますね — degree words often overlap in meaning, so learn them as set phrases.")

いたる（ところ） (itaru (tokoro)) — "everywhere" — 首相はいたるところで歓迎された ("the PM was welcomed everywhere")

Q1

（　）の人が、彼の意見に賛成した。(___) no hito ga, kare no iken ni sansei shita.

Full sentence: おおかたの人が、彼の意見に賛成した。Ookata no hito ga, kare no iken ni sansei shita.
EN: Most people agreed with his opinion.
HI: अधिकतर लोग उसकी राय से सहमत थे।
GU: મોટા ભાગના લોકો તેના અભિપ્રાય સાથે સંમત હતા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおおかたookatamost (of a group)अधिकतरમોટા ભાગના
bおおまかoomakarough/broad (describes manner, not a subgroup of people)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: おおかたの人 (most people) uses おおかた as "the greater part of" a group; おおまか describes doing something in a rough/broad manner, not a portion of people.

Q2

今回、価格を（　）値下げしました。Konkai, kakaku o (___) nesage shimashita.

Full sentence: 今回、価格を大幅に値下げしました。...kakaku o oohaba ni nesage shimashita.
EN: This time, we've cut prices substantially.
HI: इस बार हमने कीमतों में बड़ी कटौती की है।
GU: આ વખતે અમે ભાવમાં મોટો ઘટાડો કર્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a大幅にoohaba nisubstantially, drasticallyबड़े पैमाने परમોટા પાયે
b極めてkiwameteextremely (wrong grammar — modifies adjectives, not a verb like 値下げする directly this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 大幅に値下げする (cut prices drastically) is the standard collocation for a big price change; 極めて typically modifies an adjective (極めて重要) rather than directly quantifying a price cut.

Q5

このあたりは（　）ところにコンビニがあります。Kono atari wa (___) tokoro ni konbini ga arimasu.

Full sentence: このあたりはいたるところにコンビニがあります。Kono atari wa itaru tokoro ni konbini ga arimasu.
EN: There are convenience stores everywhere around here.
HI: यहाँ आस-पास हर जगह कन्वीनियंस स्टोर हैं।
GU: આ વિસ્તારમાં દરેક જગ્યાએ કન્વીનિયન્સ સ્ટોર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいたるitaruevery (place)हर (जगह)દરેક (જગ્યાએ)

HI: चीनी थोड़ी ज़्यादा डालने से स्वाद बेहतर होता है।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q7

1無限にmugen niinfinitely (too abstract/literal for a skill comparison)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

1いかなるikanaruwhatever kind ofचाहे जो भीભલે ગમે તે

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The book itself warns that today's "degree" words are close synonyms best learned as fixed phrases — here are the trickiest clusters:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
おおかた／だいたいookata / daitai Mostly, almost all Refers to a large proportion of a whole — "most of it/them." "Most people agreed," "I understood most of the lecture."
おおよそooyoso Roughly, approximately An imprecise estimate of a number or amount. "Make a rough estimate" (おおよその見当をつける).
おおむねoomune Generally, on the whole Describes overall agreement or a general tendency, often with an opinion/verdict. "We generally agree" (おおむね賛成である).
いかなるikanaru Whatever kind of Formal, used before a noun + でも/も to mean "no matter what kind." "Whatever the situation" (いかなる場合でも).
あらゆるarayuru Every, all kinds of Directly modifies a noun to mean "all/every" without needing でも. "Every opportunity" (あらゆる機会).
いたる（ところ）itaru (tokoro) Everywhere Only ever combines with ところ ("place") — describes spatial distribution, not "every kind." "Welcomed everywhere" (いたるところで歓迎される).
Study strategy: As the in-book comic itself points out, this day's words cluster tightly around "a lot/every/roughly" with overlapping English glosses — memorize each one bundled with the one fixed phrase it always appears in (いたるところ, いかなる〜でも, おおむね賛成) rather than trying to translate it standalone; that's the only reliable way to avoid mixing them up on the exam.
