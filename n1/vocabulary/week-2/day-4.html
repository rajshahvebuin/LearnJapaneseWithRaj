Warm-up

Q. どんな様子？Donna yousu?
物事を時間をかけずにすぐに行う様子Monogoto o jikan o kakezu ni sugu ni okonau yousu
("What state is this? Doing something right away, without spending time on it.")

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Many of today's words look like simple synonyms in English but belong to different domains:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
健全（な）kenzen na Sound, wholesome (abstract) Describes an abstract system, mind, or set of finances being in good, uncorrupted order. "A sound mind," "sound public finances."
健やか（な）sukoyaka na Healthy (physical growth) Describes a body — usually a child's — growing up physically healthy. "Grow up healthy" (健やかに育つ).
巧妙（な）koumyou na Cunning, shrewd Skillful in a scheming, often ethically shady way — commonly describes tricks/scams. A fraudster's "shrewd trick" (巧妙な手口).
巧み（な）takumi na Skillful Neutral-to-positive skill or dexterity, usually in a performance or craft. An athlete's or artist's skillful technique.
和やか（な）nagoyaka na Harmonious (social atmosphere) Describes the mood of an interaction between people — a meeting, a party, a conversation. A friendly, warm social gathering or discussion.
のどか（な）nodoka na Tranquil (place/pace) Describes a peaceful scene, landscape, or unhurried pace of life — not about people interacting. A quiet countryside view or a leisurely day.
Exam trap: 渋い covers three unrelated senses in this single word list — an astringent/bitter taste (このお茶は渋い), a grim facial expression (渋い顔をする), and a sober, muted color (渋い色). Using one sense's collocation in another's context is wrong: you can't call a shirt "渋い" to mean it tastes bitter — pick the sense from context, not from a single memorized translation.
