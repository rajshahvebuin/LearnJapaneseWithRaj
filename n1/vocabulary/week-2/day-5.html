Warm-up

Q. ___に入るのは？___ ni hairu no wa?
今日は、朝から___食べていない。Kyou wa, asa kara ___ tabete inai.
("Which word fits the blank? 'Today, I haven't eaten ___ since morning.'")

ろくに (roku ni) — "(haven't eaten) properly/decently" — used with a negative verb

Q1

ドライブの前に（　）車の点検をした。Doraibu no mae ni (___) kuruma no tenken o shita.

Full sentence: ドライブの前に念入りに車の点検をした。...nen'iri ni kuruma no tenken o shita.
EN: Before the drive, I did a thorough car inspection.
HI: ड्राइव से पहले मैंने गाड़ी की बारीकी से जाँच की।
GU: ડ્રાઇવ પહેલાં મેં ગાડીની ઝીણવટપૂર્વક તપાસ કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまともにmatomo niproperly/directly (wrong nuance — not about thoroughness)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b念入りにnen'iri nithoroughly, carefullyबारीकी सेઝીણવટપૂર્વક

Why: 念入りな点検 (a thorough inspection) is the fixed collocation for carefully checking something before a trip; まとも means "proper/decent," not "thorough."

Q2

一軒家といっても、二部屋だけの（　）家です。Ikkenya to itte mo, futaheya dake no (___) ie desu.

Full sentence: 一軒家といっても、二部屋だけのちっぽけな家です。...futaheya dake no chippoke na ie desu.
EN: Though it's called a house, it's just a tiny two-room place.
HI: घर तो कहते हैं, पर यह सिर्फ़ दो कमरों का बहुत छोटा-सा घर है।
GU: ઘર કહેવાય છે, પણ તે ફક્ત બે ઓરડાનું ખૂબ નાનું ઘર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aちっぽけなchippoke natinyबहुत छोटाખૂબ નાનું
bとぼしいtoboshiiscarce/lacking (wrong grammar — describes a lack of something, not a house's size)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Full sentence: 子育ては決してたやすいことではない。Kosodate wa kesshite tayasui koto dewa nai.
EN: Raising children is by no means an easy thing.
HI: बच्चों की परवरिश करना बिल्कुल भी आसान काम नहीं है।
GU: બાળઉછેર બિલકુલ સહેલું કામ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aおろそかなorosoka naneglectful (wrong meaning here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's list has several words for "bad/shabby/careless" that split into different domains:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
みっともないmittomonai Unseemly (about decorum) A breach of manners/decorum that embarrasses onlookers — about behavior, not looks. Doing your makeup in public, or other socially improper behavior.
みすぼらしいmisuborashii Shabby (about appearance) Poor, run-down physical appearance — a person's clothes or a building. A worn-out house or threadbare clothing.
ぞんざい（な）zonzai na Careless, rough manner Sloppy carelessness in how something is done or said — not maliciously intended. Rough speech or offhand treatment of someone.
あくどいakudoi Vicious, crooked Deliberate, scheming wrongdoing — a much stronger, malicious word than ぞんざい. A scammer's methods or shady business dealing.
ややこしいyayakoshii Complicated (structurally) Hard to follow because it has many interlocking parts — a relationship, a procedure. A tangled situation with many factors.
紛らわしいmagirawashii Confusing (easily mistaken) Liable to be mistaken for something else — about ambiguity, not structural complexity. Two similar-looking words or signs that get mixed up.
Exam trap: ろくな〜ない always appears with a negative predicate and means "not even a decent/proper ___" — 朝からろくに食べていない doesn't mean "I ate roughly since morning," it means "I haven't eaten properly since morning." Dropping the ない turns the meaning nonsensical; ろく is never used on its own in modern speech outside this fixed negative pattern.
