Notes

※ = 「が」can be swapped for 「の」, more common in writing

・ = a common example phrase using the headword, not a separate word
Warm-up

Q. ___に入るのは？___ ni hairu no wa?
勉強が___います。Benkyou ga ___ imasu.
("Which word fits the blank? 'Studying is ___.' — Hint: means things are going smoothly and efficiently.")

はかどって（いる） (hakadotte (iru)) — "(my) studying is making good progress"

Q1

最近、先の（　）靴がはやっているようだ。Saikin, saki no (___) kutsu ga hayatte iru you da.

Full sentence: 最近、先のとがった靴がはやっているようだ。...saki no togatta kutsu ga hayatte iru you da.
EN: Pointy-toed shoes seem to be trending recently.
HI: आजकल आगे से नुकीले जूते चलन में लग रहे हैं।
GU: હમણાં આગળથી અણીદાર બૂટ ચલણમાં હોય એવું લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそびえたsobietatowered (wrong scale — for buildings, not shoe tips)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとがったtogattapointed, sharpनुकीलाઅણીદાર

Why: A shoe's pointed toe is とがった (sharp/pointed) — そびえる describes something towering high, the wrong scale for a shoe tip.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aきしんでkishindecreakingचरचराकरકિચૂડ કિચૂડ કરીને

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several of today's words share roots or surface meanings but describe different things:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
かさむkasamu Costs increasing Money/expenses growing larger over time. Talking about rising bills or expenses.
かさばるkasabaru Physically bulky An object's physical size/volume making it awkward to carry. Describing luggage or packages that take up too much space.
重なるkasanaru Overlapping Two or more things (usually dates/schedules) coinciding. Plans or events landing on the same day.
ぶれるbureru Motion-blurred Blur specifically from camera shake during a photo. Describing a shaky, blurred photograph.
ぼやけるboyakeru Indistinct, blurry General loss of clarity — vision, text, memory — not tied to camera shake. Describing blurry eyesight, faded text, or a hazy memory.
はげるhageru Peels off by itself Something (paint, hair) comes off on its own — intransitive. Paint flaking off a wall, or a head going bald.
はぐhagu Someone strips it off An agent actively removes an outer layer from something — transitive. Skinning a fish, stripping a covering off deliberately.
Exam trap: The word list pairs 板が反る (a board warps, そる) with the related-sounding example ひげをそる (shave your beard) — but that そる is a completely different word, 剃る, that just happens to share the same reading. They are not the same verb and can't be substituted for each other; this is a same-reading trap, not a derived form like the →こ pairs elsewhere this week (かなう→かなえる, 重なる→重ねる).
