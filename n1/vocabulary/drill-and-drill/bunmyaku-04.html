2 Every question — 問題 (p.39)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【22】

では、次の章に行きましょう。ページを（　）ください。Dewa, tsugi no shou ni ikimashou. Peeji o (___) kudasai.

Full sentence: では、次の章に行きましょう。ページをめくってください。Dewa, tsugi no shou ni ikimashou. Peeji o mekutte kudasai.
EN: Now, let's move on to the next chapter. Please turn the page.
HI: तो चलिए, अगले अध्याय पर चलते हैं। कृपया पन्ना पलटिए।
GU: તો ચાલો, આગળના પ્રકરણ પર જઈએ. કૃપા કરીને પાનું ફેરવો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ねじってnejittetwist (ねじる)मरोड़नाમરડવું
2はかどってhakadottemake good progress (はかどる)काम तेज़ी से आगे बढ़नाકામ ઝડપથી આગળ વધવું
3ひねってhinettetwist, turn (a tap, a knob) (ひねる)घुमाना, ऐंठनाફેરવવું, આમળવું
4めくってmekutteturn over (a page) (めくる)पलटनाફેરવવું (પાનું)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.42–43, PDF 124–125). Book's notes (paraphrased): めくる = turn (pages). 選択肢の言葉: 2 はかどる = progress smoothly and quickly (例: in summer, studying early in the cool morning はかどる (hakadoru) more than working late at night). The 別冊 has no notes for 1 ねじる and 3 ひねる. (added, not in book) ねじる and ひねる both mean twisting (ねじる a wet towel, ひねる a tap / your ankle); a page is turned over → ページをめくる, カレンダーをめくる. はかどる is intransitive (仕事がはかどる), so it can't even take ページを.

【23】

天候が（　）であることから、野菜の価格の上昇が懸念される。Tenkou ga (___) de aru koto kara, yasai no kakaku no joushou ga kenen sareru.

Full sentence: 天候が不順であることから、野菜の価格の上昇が懸念される。Tenkou ga fujun de aru koto kara, yasai no kakaku no joushou ga kenen sareru.
EN: Because the weather has been unseasonable, there are fears that vegetable prices will rise.
HI: मौसम अनियमित होने के कारण सब्ज़ियों के दाम बढ़ने की आशंका है।
GU: હવામાન અનિયમિત હોવાથી શાકભાજીના ભાવ વધવાની આશંકા છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1不順fujununseasonable, irregularअनियमितઅનિયમિત
2不審fushinsuspiciousसंदिग्धશંકાસ્પદ
3不快fukaiunpleasantअप्रिय, बुरा लगने वालाઅણગમતું, અપ્રિય
4不振fushinslump, poor performanceमंदी, कमज़ोर प्रदर्शनમંદી, નબળો દેખાવ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.43, PDF 125). Book's notes (paraphrased): 不順(な) = not stable, not going as it should. 選択肢の言葉: 2 不審(な) = うたがわしい / あやしい / 変な (例: if you find 不審な物 (fushin na mono) in town, tell the police); 3 不快(な) = feeling bad, unpleasant (例: 不快なにおい (fukai na nioi) hung over the rubbish collection point); 4 不振(な) (例: a recession is when 経済の不振な状況 (keizai no fushin na joukyou) continues). (added, not in book) All four start with 不 — only the second kanji matters. 天候不順 is a set compound; 不振 is for business, appetite or a player's form (食欲不振, 成績不振).

【24】

商社マンとして海外での勤務が長かった父は、退職後、（　）を買われて大学の商学部で教えることになった。Shousha man to shite kaigai de no kinmu ga nagakatta chichi wa, taishokugo, (___) o kawarete daigaku no shougakubu de oshieru koto ni natta.

Full sentence: 商社マンとして海外での勤務が長かった父は、退職後、キャリアを買われて大学の商学部で教えることになった。Shousha man to shite kaigai de no kinmu ga nagakatta chichi wa, taishokugo, kyaria o kawarete daigaku no shougakubu de oshieru koto ni natta.
EN: My father worked abroad for many years for a trading company; after retiring, his experience was valued and he was invited to teach in a university commerce department.
HI: मेरे पिता ने ट्रेडिंग कंपनी में लंबे समय तक विदेश में काम किया था; रिटायर होने के बाद उनके अनुभव की क़द्र हुई और वे एक विश्वविद्यालय के वाणिज्य विभाग में पढ़ाने लगे।
GU: મારા પિતાએ ટ્રેડિંગ કંપનીમાં લાંબો સમય વિદેશમાં કામ કર્યું હતું; નિવૃત્તિ પછી તેમના અનુભવની કદર થઈ અને તેઓ યુનિવર્સિટીના વાણિજ્ય વિભાગમાં ભણાવવા લાગ્યા.

2レギュラーregyuraaregular (member of a team)नियमित सदस्य (टीम का)નિયમિત સભ્ય (ટીમનો)

Answer 1 (別冊 p.43, PDF 125). Book's notes (paraphrased): キャリア = career. 選択肢の言葉: 2 レギュラー = regular member (例: the football club has many members, but only about one in three レギュラーの選手になれる (regyuraa no senshu ni nareru)); 3 スタッフ = staff (例: about ten スタッフ (sutaffu) work on this job); 4 タレント = an entertainer who appears on TV or radio (例: that person is a タレント (tarento) often on TV). 【25】

晴れた日は、ここから富士山が（　）見える。Hareta hi wa, koko kara Fujisan ga (___) mieru.

HI: साफ़ दिनों में यहाँ से फ़ूजी पर्वत बिल्कुल साफ़ दिखाई देता है।
GU: ચોખ્ખા દિવસોમાં અહીંથી ફૂજી પર્વત એકદમ સ્પષ્ટ દેખાય છે.

1きっぱりkippariflatly, firmly (refuse, say)साफ़ शब्दों में, दृढ़ता सेસ્પષ્ટ શબ્દોમાં, દૃઢતાથી
2くっきりkukkiriclearly, sharply (outline)साफ़-साफ़ (आकार)સ્પષ્ટ (આકાર)

Answer 2 (別冊 p.43, PDF 125). Book's notes (paraphrased): くっきり = a shape is seen clearly. 選択肢の言葉: 1 きっぱり = はっきり (言う) (例: she きっぱり否定した (kippari hitei shita) the rumour that she would marry a film star); 3 きっかり = (時間が) ちょうど (例: he came 10時きっかり (juuji kikkari) as promised); 4 あっさり = すぐに / 簡単に (例: don't あっさりあきらめちゃいけない (assari akiramecha ikenai)). 【26】

ここは自然が美しい場所だが、虫が多いのに（　）。Koko wa shizen ga utsukushii basho da ga, mushi ga ooi no ni (___).

Full sentence: ここは自然が美しい場所だが、虫が多いのに閉口する。Koko wa shizen ga utsukushii basho da ga, mushi ga ooi no ni heikou suru.
EN: The scenery here is beautiful, but the number of insects drives me mad.
HI: यहाँ की प्रकृति सुंदर है, पर कीड़ों की भरमार से मैं तंग आ जाता/जाती हूँ।
GU: અહીંની પ્રકૃતિ સુંદર છે, પણ જીવજંતુઓની ભરમારથી હું ત્રાસી જાઉં છું.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1反発するhanpatsu suruto rebel against, resistविरोध करना, पलटकर जवाब देनाવિરોધ કરવો, સામો જવાબ આપવો
2閉口するheikou suruto be fed up withतंग आ जानाકંટાળી જવું
3動揺するdouyou suruto be shaken, upsetविचलित होना, घबरानाવિચલિત થવું, ગભરાવું
4降参するkousan suruto surrender, give inहार मान लेना, आत्मसमर्पण करनाહાર માની લેવી, શરણે થવું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.43, PDF 125). Book's notes (paraphrased): 閉口する = be bothered, be at a loss. 選択肢の言葉: 1 反発する (例: my son いちいち反発する (ichiichi hanpatsu suru) to everything I say — he was so obedient when little); 3 動揺する = be upset (例: the driver was so 動揺していて (douyou shite ite) he couldn't answer the police properly); 4 降参する = surrender (例: 敵はついに降参し (teki wa tsui ni kousan shi), our side won). (added, not in book) 〜に閉口する = "be fed up with something you can't escape" (暑さ・騒音・おしゃべりに閉口する). 降参 is tempting ("I give up on the bugs") but it is for losing a fight or a contest.

Book's notes (paraphrased): 求人 = wanted (staff); 参考: 求人広告 = want ad. 選択肢の言葉: 1 応募 = application, entry (例: T company is hiring — why not 応募してみたら (oubo shite mitara)?); 4 人材 = human resources, staff (例: 当社では有能な人材を求めている (tousha de wa yuunou na jinzai o motomete iru)). 【28】

Full sentence: これは内緒の話だから、だれにも言わないでね。Kore wa naisho no hanashi da kara, dare ni mo iwanaide ne.

HI: यह राज़ की बात है, इसलिए किसी को मत बताना, ठीक है?
GU: આ ખાનગી વાત છે, એટલે કોઈને કહેતા નહીં, હં?
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1内定naiteiinformal job offer, unofficial decisionअनौपचारिक नियुक्ति-पक्कीઅનૌપચારિક નિમણૂક નક્કી
2内部naibuthe inside, internalअंदरूनी हिस्साઅંદરનો ભાગ
3内緒naishosecretराज़ખાનગી વાત
4内心naishinone's inner feelingsमन के भीतर, असली भावनाમનની અંદર, સાચી લાગણી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.44, PDF 126). Book's notes (paraphrased): 内緒 = secret. 選択肢の言葉: 1 内定 = unofficially / tentatively decided (例: I had received 採用の内定 (saiyou no naitei) from company A, then a cancellation notice came — a big shock); 4 内心 = 心の中 / 本当の気持ち (例: many employees 内心では社長を信頼していない (naishin de wa shachou o shinrai shite inai)). 3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
めくるmekuru / ねじるnejiru / ひねるhineru turn over / twist / twist, turn めくる flips a flat thing (ページ, カード, 布団); ねじる twists something long (タオル, 体); ひねる turns with the fingers or hurts by twisting (蛇口をひねる, 足首をひねる).
不順fujun / 不振fushin / 不審fushin / 不快fukai irregular / slump / suspicious / unpleasant 不振 and 不審 are both ふしん! 天候不順・生理不順; 経営不振・食欲不振; 不審者・挙動不審; 不快な音.
キャリアkyaria vs タレントtarento vs スタッフsutaffu career vs TV personality vs staff Katakana false friends: タレント = TV entertainer (not "talent"); スタッフ = the people working on a project; キャリア = experience built up over years.
くっきりkukkiri / きっぱりkippari / きっかりkikkari / はっきりhakkiri sharp outline / firmly / exactly / clearly くっきり for shapes and contrasts you see; きっぱり for refusing or deciding; きっかり for exact time or amounts; はっきり covers all three loosely.
閉口するheikou suru vs 降参するkousan suru be fed up vs give up / surrender 閉口 = annoyed by something you must put up with; 降参 = admitting defeat (in a fight, a game, a debate).
求人kyuujin vs 応募oubo vs 人材jinzai recruiting vs applying vs capable people Company side: 求人 / 募集 / 人材を求める. Applicant side: 応募 / 就職.
内緒naisho / 内心naishin / 内定naitei / 内部naibu secret / inner feelings / informal offer / inside 内緒 = kept from others; 内心 = what you privately feel; 内定 = a decision not yet official (esp. job offers); 内部 = the inside of an organisation or thing.
Exam trap: in Q23 and Q28 all four options share the first kanji (不〜, 内〜). Cover the shared kanji with your finger and match the second kanji to the sentence: 天候 → 順 (the weather is out of its normal order), だれにも言わないで → 緒 (内緒 = a secret).
