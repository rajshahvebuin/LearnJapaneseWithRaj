【91】

輸入をめぐって両国間に摩擦が起きた。Yunyuu o megutte ryoukokukan ni masatsu ga okita.

Underlined word: 摩擦masatsu — friction, conflict
EN: Friction arose between the two countries over imports.
HI: आयात को लेकर दोनों देशों के बीच तनाव पैदा हो गया।
GU: આયાતના મુદ્દે બંને દેશો વચ્ચે ઘર્ષણ ઊભું થયું.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1まさつmasatsufriction (摩擦)घर्षण, तनावઘર્ષણ, તણાવ
2てさつtesatsunot a word(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3しゅさつshusatsunot a word (摩 read like 手 シュ)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4まっさつmassatsuobliteration, erasing (抹殺)मिटा देना, ख़त्म कर देनाભૂંસી નાખવું, ખતમ કરવું

Answer 1 (別冊 p.28, PDF 110). Book's notes (paraphrased): 摩擦 = まさつ (friction, conflict). 摩 = マ; 擦 = サツ (擦過傷), す(れる／る) (strike a match), こす(る) (rub). 選択肢の言葉: 4 抹殺. (added, not in book) 摩 is マ — a single mora — so there is no 促音; まっさつ is a different word (抹 マツ + 殺 サツ). 2 and 3 come from the 手 at the bottom of 摩. 貿易摩擦 (trade friction) is a common news phrase.

Book's notes (paraphrased): 素人 = しろうと (amateur). 素 = ソ (素朴, 素材, 元素, 酸素, 平素), ス (素直); 人 = ジン / ニン / ひと, with exceptional readings 玄人 (くろうと), 素人 (しろうと), 仲人, 若人, 大人, 一人, 二人. 選択肢の言葉: 2 玄人. (added, not in book) Trap type 6 — whole-word reading. The pair is a colour contrast: 素 ≈ 白 (しろ, "plain/white") and 玄 ≈ 黒 (くろ, "dark") — the amateur vs the seasoned pro. Both were in the exception list on 第11回.

【96】

選手の間の結束の固さがこのチームに勝利をもたらした。Senshu no aida no kessoku no katasa ga kono chiimu ni shouri o motarashita.

Underlined word: 結束kessoku — unity, solidarity
EN: The strong unity among the players brought this team victory.

GU: ખેલાડીઓ વચ્ચેની મજબૂત એકતાએ આ ટીમને જીત અપાવી.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.30, PDF 112). Book's notes (paraphrased): 結束 = けっそく (unity, cooperation). 結 = ケツ (結核, 結晶, 団結, 連結 …), むす(ぶ), ゆ(う／わえる); 束 = ソク (束縛, 拘束), たば, たば(ねる), つか (束の間). (added, not in book) Trap type 3: ケツ + ソク → けっそく — the same pattern as 結成 (けっせい), 結晶 (けっしょう). 3 drops the final ク of ソク. Literally "tie (結) into a bundle (束)" — a team bound together. 結束が固い = closely united.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
摩擦masatsu vs 抹殺massatsu friction vs obliteration 摩 = マ (no っ); 抹 = マツ → まっ. 貿易摩擦; 存在を抹殺する.

Exam trap: in a 促音 round, test each word on its own. Rule of thumb: an on-yomi ending in ク / キ / ツ / チ often becomes っ before a カ-, サ-, タ- or パ-row sound (若干, 結束), but a one-mora reading like 摩 (マ) never doubles (摩擦 ≠ まっさつ).
