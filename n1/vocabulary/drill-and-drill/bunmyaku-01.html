1 Points — how to answer 文脈規定 + the words tested in this round

N1「文字語彙」の勉強のポイント — 文脈規定 (summary of p.3–4, not the book's text)

EN: In 文脈規定 you work out what the sentence means and choose the word that fits it. The book calls it a standard vocabulary question, and finding the word for the （　） can feel like a quiz. The catch: the four options are always words that look alike in meaning, in sound or in kanji, so read the whole sentence before choosing and don't pick a word just because it looks familiar.

HI: 文脈規定 में वाक्य का अर्थ समझकर उसमें फ़िट होने वाला शब्द चुनना होता है। किताब इसे शब्दावली का सामान्य प्रश्न कहती है, और （　） में सही शब्द ढूँढना पहेली जैसा मज़ेदार लग सकता है। पर ध्यान रहे: चारों विकल्प हमेशा अर्थ, ध्वनि या कांजी में एक-दूसरे से मिलते-जुलते होते हैं, इसलिए पूरा वाक्य पढ़कर ही चुनें, केवल जाना-पहचाना दिखने से कोई शब्द न चुनें।

GU: 文脈規定માં વાક્યનો અર્થ સમજીને તેમાં બંધબેસતો શબ્દ પસંદ કરવાનો હોય છે. પુસ્તક તેને શબ્દભંડોળનો સામાન્ય પ્રશ્ન કહે છે, અને （　）માં યોગ્ય શબ્દ શોધવો કોયડા જેવું મજેદાર લાગી શકે. પણ ધ્યાન રાખો: ચારેય વિકલ્પો હંમેશાં અર્થ, અવાજ કે કાંજીમાં એકબીજાને મળતા આવે છે, તેથી આખું વાક્ય વાંચીને જ પસંદ કરો, ફક્ત ઓળખીતો લાગે એટલે કોઈ શબ્દ ન પસંદ કરો.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.36)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【1】

あの人の言うことは（　）ばかりだ。本音が聞きたい。Ano hito no iu koto wa (___) bakari da. Honne ga kikitai.

Full sentence: あの人の言うことは建前ばかりだ。本音が聞きたい。Ano hito no iu koto wa tatemae bakari da. Honne ga kikitai.
EN: Everything that person says is just the official line. I want to hear what they really think.
HI: वह व्यक्ति जो कुछ कहता है, सब दिखावटी बातें होती हैं। मैं उसके मन की असली बात सुनना चाहता/चाहती हूँ।
GU: એ વ્યક્તિ જે કંઈ કહે છે તે બધી દેખાડાની વાતો જ હોય છે. મારે એના મનની સાચી વાત સાંભળવી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1矛盾mujuncontradictionविरोधाभासવિરોધાભાસ
2建前tatemaeofficial stance, the proper lineदिखावटी/औपचारिक बातદેખાડાની/ઔપચારિક વાત
3侮辱bujokuinsultअपमानઅપમાન
4偏見henkenprejudiceपूर्वाग्रहપૂર્વગ્રહ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.38, PDF 120). Book's notes (paraphrased): 建前 = the principle / official position one states in public. 選択肢の言葉: 1 矛盾 = contradiction (例: what he says and what he does 矛盾している (mujun shite iru)); 3 侮辱 = insult (例: anyone who received ひどい侮辱 (hidoi bujoku) like that could not stay silent); 4 偏見 = prejudice (例: the common claim that women drive badly は偏見だ (wa henken da)). (added, not in book) The second sentence gives it away: 本音 (real feelings) is the classic opposite of 建前. 矛盾 is tempting, but it is about two statements clashing (言うことが矛盾している (iu koto ga mujun shite iru)), not about public words vs real feelings.

【2】

今度の私たちのコンサートの批評は（　）好評だ。Kondo no watashitachi no konsaato no hihyou wa (___) kouhyou da.

Full sentence: 今度の私たちのコンサートの批評はおしなべて好評だ。Kondo no watashitachi no konsaato no hihyou wa oshinabete kouhyou da.
EN: The reviews of our latest concert are favourable on the whole.
HI: हमारे इस बार के कॉन्सर्ट की समीक्षाएँ कुल मिलाकर अच्छी हैं।
GU: અમારા આ વખતના કોન્સર્ટની સમીક્ષાઓ એકંદરે સારી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1おしなべてoshinabeteon the whole, generallyकुल मिलाकरએકંદરે
2万一man'ichiin the unlikely event, if by any chanceअगर कहीं, भूले-भटकेજો કદાચ, ભૂલેચૂકે
3逐一chikuichione by one, in every detailएक-एक करके, हर बातએક એક કરીને, દરેક વાત

Book's notes (paraphrased): おしなべて = だいたい / おおむね / 概して (generally, on the whole). 選択肢の言葉: 3 逐一 = 一つ一つ (例: there is no need to report progress 逐一 (chikuichi) — just tell me the important points); 4 ひときわ = 一段と / 際立って (例: who is the beauty who ひときわ目立つ (hitokiwa medatsu) among all those women?). The 別冊 has no note for 2 万一. (added, not in book) 万一 needs a condition (万一雨なら (man'ichi ame nara)), and ひときわ singles one thing out rather than summing up. Only おしなべて sums up many reviews.

【3】

団体競技では（　）が何より重要だ。Dantai kyougi de wa (___) ga nani yori juuyou da.

Full sentence: 団体競技ではチームワークが何より重要だ。Dantai kyougi de wa chiimuwaaku ga nani yori juuyou da.
EN: In team sports, teamwork matters more than anything.
HI: टीम वाले खेलों में टीमवर्क सबसे ज़्यादा ज़रूरी है।
GU: ટીમ રમતોમાં ટીમવર્ક સૌથી વધુ મહત્ત્વનું છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.38, PDF 120). Book's notes (paraphrased): チームワーク = teamwork. 選択肢の言葉: 1 トーン (例: saying "this is a secret", she 声のトーンを落とした (koe no toon o otoshita)); 3 アナウンス (例: at stations, guidance アナウンスが流れている (anaunsu ga nagarete iru) all the time); 4 ネットワーク (例: the Internet is the best-known 通信ネットワーク (tsuushin nettowaaku)). 【4】

能力の高い人が会社で（　）とは限らない。Nouryoku no takai hito ga kaisha de (___) to wa kagiranai.

Full sentence: 能力の高い人が会社で出世するとは限らない。Nouryoku no takai hito ga kaisha de shusse suru to wa kagiranai.
EN: Talented people don't necessarily get ahead in a company.
HI: यह ज़रूरी नहीं कि ज़्यादा क़ाबिल लोग कंपनी में तरक़्क़ी करें।
GU: એવું જરૂરી નથી કે વધુ કાબેલ લોકો કંપનીમાં પ્રગતિ કરે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1進級するshinkyuu surumove up to the next grade/classअगली कक्षा में जानाઆગલા ધોરણમાં જવું
2進出するshinshutsu suruadvance into (a market, the finals)आगे बढ़कर प्रवेश करना (बाज़ार, फ़ाइनल)આગળ વધીને પ્રવેશ કરવો (બજાર, ફાઇનલ)
3出世するshusse suruget ahead, be promotedतरक़्क़ी करनाપ્રગતિ કરવી
4上昇するjoushou sururise, go up (numbers, prices)बढ़ना, ऊपर जाना (संख्या, दाम)વધવું, ઉપર જવું (આંકડા, ભાવ)

Answer 3 (別冊 p.38, PDF 120). Book's notes (paraphrased): 出世する = to be successful at work. 選択肢の言葉: 1 進級する = step up to a higher grade (例: students take a test when they 上のクラスに進級する (ue no kurasu ni shinkyuu suru)); 2 進出する = advance (例: the Japanese team kept winning and 決勝戦に進出した (kesshousen ni shinshutsu shita)); 4 上昇する = (数量が)上がる / 増える (例: last year 物価はわずかに上昇した (bukka wa wazuka ni joushou shita)). (added, not in book) 進級 is for school years, 進出 for moving into a new field or stage, 上昇 for quantities — only 出世 is about a person's position in a company.

【5】

電力不足は国民にとって（　）問題だ。Denryoku busoku wa kokumin ni totte (___) mondai da.

EN: The power shortage is a pressing problem for the people of the country.
HI: बिजली की कमी देश के लोगों के लिए एक गंभीर, सीधे असर डालने वाली समस्या है।
GU: વીજળીની અછત દેશના લોકો માટે એક ગંભીર, સીધી અસર કરતી સમસ્યા છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1惨めなmijime namiserable, wretchedदयनीय, बदहालદયનીય, બેહાલ
2得がたいegataihard to come by, preciousमुश्किल से मिलने वाला, क़ीमतीમુશ્કેલીથી મળતું, કીમતી
3切実なsetsujitsu napressing, keenly feltगंभीर, शिद्दत से महसूस होने वालाગંભીર, તીવ્રતાથી અનુભવાતું
4切ないsetsunaiheart-rending, painfully sadदिल दुखाने वाला, बहुत दुखदહૃદય દુભાવે એવું, ખૂબ દુઃખદ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.38, PDF 120). Book's notes (paraphrased): 切実(な) = closely bound up with you and serious. 選択肢の言葉: 2 得がたい = 貴重(な), precious (例: two years of study abroad became 得がたい経験 (egatai keiken)); 4 切ない = sad and painful at heart (例: losing a friend is as 切ない (setsunai) as losing family). The 別冊 has no note for 1 惨めな. (added, not in book) 切実な and 切ない share 切, but 切ない is an emotion (sadness), not a description of a problem. 切実な問題 (setsujitsu na mondai) / 切実な願い are set collocations.

【6】

駅から（　）5分以内という条件の家を探しています。Eki kara (___) go-fun inai to iu jouken no ie o sagashite imasu.

EN: I'm looking for a house within five minutes' walk of the station.

GU: હું એવું ઘર શોધું છું જે સ્ટેશનથી ચાલીને 5 મિનિટની અંદર હોય.

【7】

この果物は、（　）はよくないが、食べてみると甘くておいしい。Kono kudamono wa, (___) wa yoku nai ga, tabete miru to amakute oishii.

Full sentence: この果物は、見た目はよくないが、食べてみると甘くておいしい。Kono kudamono wa, mitame wa yoku nai ga, tabete miru to amakute oishii.
EN: This fruit doesn't look nice, but when you eat it, it is sweet and delicious.
HI: यह फल देखने में अच्छा नहीं है, पर खाकर देखो तो मीठा और स्वादिष्ट है।
GU: આ ફળ દેખાવમાં સારું નથી, પણ ખાઈને જુઓ તો મીઠું અને સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.39, PDF 121). Book's notes (paraphrased): 見た目 = appearance. 選択肢の言葉: 3 見所 = (in this question) a part worth seeing, a good point (例: sadly, the play I saw today 見所がほとんどなかった (midokoro ga hotondo nakatta)); 4 一見 = at a quick glance (例: this problem 一見難しそうに見える (ikken muzukashisou ni mieru) but is not hard at all). The 別冊 has no note for 1 見方. (added, not in book) 一見 is an adverb (一見〜そうだ), so it can't take は and よくない like a noun here. The contrast "looks bad / tastes good" needs the noun for outward appearance: 見た目.

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
建前tatemae vs 本音honne official stance vs real feelings A fixed pair: 建前ばかり → 本音が聞きたい. 矛盾 is about two statements clashing, not about public vs private.
おしなべてoshinabete vs 逐一chikuichi vs ひときわhitokiwa on the whole vs one by one vs conspicuously おしなべて sums up many things; 逐一 goes through every single one (逐一報告する); ひときわ makes one thing stand out from the rest (ひときわ目立つ).
チームワークchiimuwaaku vs ネットワークnettowaaku teamwork vs network Same 〜ワーク sound, different idea: people cooperating vs a linked system (通信ネットワーク).
出世shusse / 進級shinkyuu / 進出shinshutsu / 上昇joushou promotion / next school year / advance into / rise All "going up", but for different things: a career (会社で出世), a student (2年に進級), a team or company (決勝・海外に進出), a number (物価が上昇).
切実なsetsujitsu na vs 切ないsetsunai pressing vs heart-rending Same kanji 切. 切実な describes a problem or wish that affects you directly; 切ない describes a painful feeling (別れが切ない).
徒歩toho / 歩行hokou / 歩幅hohaba / 初歩shoho on foot / walking / stride / basics Four 歩 words: 徒歩 = means of travel (徒歩5分); 歩行 = the act of walking (歩行者); 歩幅 = step length; 初歩 = beginner level (初歩の英語).
見た目mitame / 見所midokoro / 一見ikken / 見方mikata looks / highlight / at first glance / viewpoint Four 見 words: 見た目 = outward appearance; 見所 = the part worth seeing; 一見 = adverb "at first sight"; 見方 = way of thinking about something.
Exam trap: in 文脈規定 the other half of the sentence usually holds the key — 本音 (Q1) points to 建前, 団体競技 (Q3) to チームワーク, 甘くておいしい (Q7) to a contrast with 見た目. When three options share a kanji (歩, 見), the kanji tells you nothing: decide from the collocation (徒歩5分, 見た目はよくない).
