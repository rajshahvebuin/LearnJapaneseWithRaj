2 Every question — 問題 (p.52)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【113】

上司が新入社員に対して職場での（　）を教えた。Joushi ga shinnyuu shain ni taishite shokuba de no (___) o oshieta.

Full sentence: 上司が新入社員に対して職場での心構えを教えた。Joushi ga shinnyuu shain ni taishite shokuba de no kokorogamae o oshieta.
EN: The boss taught the new employees the right attitude to have in the workplace.
HI: बॉस ने नए कर्मचारियों को दफ़्तर में रखने लायक़ सही रवैया सिखाया।
GU: બૉસે નવા કર્મચારીઓને કામના સ્થળે રાખવાનું યોગ્ય વલણ શીખવ્યું.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1やる気yarukimotivation, will to doकाम करने का जोशકામ કરવાનો ઉત્સાહ
2気力kiryokuwillpower, mental energyमनोबल, हिम्मतમનોબળ, હિંમત
3下心shitagokoroulterior motiveछिपा मक़सद, गुप्त इरादाછૂપો હેતુ
4心構えkokorogamaemental readiness, attitudeमानसिक तैयारी, रवैयाમાનસિક તૈયારી, વલણ

Answer 4 (別冊 p.65, PDF 147). Book's notes (paraphrased): 心構え = readiness. 選択肢の言葉: 1 やる気 = a strong feeling of wanting to do something (例: lately なんだかやる気が出なくて (nandaka yaruki ga denakute) I'm stuck; my grandfather is old but shows 若者顔負けのやる気 (wakamono kaomake no yaruki)); 2 気力 = mental strength / will to carry on even when it is hard (例: since my mother died my father seems to have lost 生きる気力 (ikiru kiryoku)); 3 下心 = a not-so-good intention you hide but would act on if you got the chance (例: my brother approached her という下心があって (to iu shitagokoro ga atte) — her father is a company president who might find him a job). (added, not in book) Something a boss can teach (〜を教えた) about the workplace → 心構え. やる気 / 気力 come from inside and cannot be taught.

【115】

パーティーのご案内状をお送りいたします。（　）5月10日までにお返事をいただきたく、よろしくお願い申し上げます。Paatii no go-annaijou o o-okuri itashimasu. (___) gogatsu tooka made ni o-henji o itadakitaku, yoroshiku onegai moushiagemasu.

Full sentence: パーティーのご案内状をお送りいたします。つきましては5月10日までにお返事をいただきたく、よろしくお願い申し上げます。Paatii no go-annaijou o o-okuri itashimasu. Tsukimashite wa gogatsu tooka made ni o-henji o itadakitaku, yoroshiku onegai moushiagemasu.
EN: Please find enclosed an invitation to the party. We would accordingly be grateful for your reply by 10 May.
HI: पार्टी का निमंत्रण-पत्र भेज रहे हैं। अतः 10 मई तक आपका उत्तर मिल जाए, ऐसा विनम्र अनुरोध है।
GU: પાર્ટીનું આમંત્રણ-પત્ર મોકલીએ છીએ. આથી 10 મે સુધીમાં આપનો જવાબ મળે એવી નમ્ર વિનંતી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1つきましてはtsukimashite waaccordingly (formal)अतःઆથી
2つつしんでtsutsushinderespectfully, humblyसादर, विनम्रतापूर्वकસાદર, નમ્રતાપૂર્વક
3はやばやとhayabaya toearly, earlier than usualजल्दी-जल्दी, समय से पहलेવહેલાસર, સમય પહેલાં
4つとめてtsutometemaking an effort to, as far as possibleभरसक कोशिश करकेબને ત્યાં સુધી પ્રયત્ન કરીને

Answer 1 (別冊 p.65–66, PDF 147–148). Book's notes (paraphrased): つきましては = ですから / それで; used in formal invitations and notices. 選択肢の言葉: 2 つつしんで (謹んで) = showing respect, formally (例: つつしんで新年のご挨拶を申し上げます (tsutsushinde shinnen no go-aisatsu o moushiagemasu)); 3 はやばやと (早々と) = earlier than usual (例: still the rainy season, yet people are はやばやと海水浴を楽しむ (hayabaya to kaisuiyoku o tanoshimu); the baker はやばやと店じまいをしている (hayabaya to misejimai o shite iru) — sold out?); 4 つとめて (努めて) = making as much effort as possible (例: for my health つとめて歩くようにしています (tsutomete aruku you ni shite imasu)). (added, not in book) The blank starts a new sentence and links "we are sending an invitation" → "so please reply" → a formal connective: つきましては.

【116】

田舎育ちの彼の服装は、いつもどこか（　）で、洗練されていない。Inakasodachi no kare no fukusou wa, itsumo dokoka (___) de, senren sarete inai.

GU: ગામડામાં ઊછરેલા તેનાં કપડાં હંમેશાં થોડાં ગામઠી હોય છે, તેમાં ઠાઠ નથી હોતો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1無茶muchaunreasonable, recklessबेतुका, बेहिसाबવાહિયાત, અવિચારી
2強硬kyoukoufirm, hard-lineकड़ा, अड़ियलકડક, જિદ્દી
3野暮yabounrefined, unfashionableगँवारू, बेढंगाગામઠી, અણઘડ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.66, PDF 148). Book's notes (paraphrased): 野暮(な) = not sophisticated. 選択肢の言葉: 1 無茶(な) = unreasonable (例: finishing this much work today — 無茶だ (mucha da)); 2 強硬(な) = strongly insisting on one's own opinion (例: 父は娘の結婚に強硬に反対した (chichi wa musume no kekkon ni kyoukou ni hantai shita)). The 別冊 has no note for 4 素敵. (added, not in book) 洗練されていない (not refined) restates the blank → 野暮. 素敵 is the opposite.

【117】

30分以上の遅刻は欠席と（　）。Sanjuppun ijou no chikoku wa kesseki to (___).

Full sentence: 30分以上の遅刻は欠席とみなします。Sanjuppun ijou no chikoku wa kesseki to minashimasu.
EN: Arriving more than 30 minutes late will be treated as an absence.
HI: 30 मिनट से ज़्यादा देर से आने को ग़ैरहाज़िरी माना जाएगा।
GU: 30 મિનિટથી વધુ મોડા આવવાને ગેરહાજરી ગણવામાં આવશે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1認めますmitomemasurecognise, admit, approve (認める)मानते हैं, स्वीकार करते हैंમાન્ય રાખીએ છીએ, સ્વીકારીએ છીએ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.66, PDF 148). Book's notes (paraphrased): みなす = regard. 選択肢の言葉: 3 見計らう = estimate the timing / choose something suitable (例: I 相手のひまな時間を見計らって (aite no hima na jikan o mihakaratte) rang him); 4 見積もる = roughly estimate an amount / calculate time or cost in advance (例: ざっと見積もっても、3億円以上かかる (zatto mitsumotte mo, san'oku-en ijou kakaru)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 認める. (added, not in book) A treated as B by rule → AをBとみなす. 認める usually takes 〜を認める (欠席を認める = allow the absence), which changes the meaning.

2取材shuzainews gathering, coverageरिपोर्टिंग, ख़बर जुटानाરિપોર્ટિંગ

【119】

さて、そろそろ仕事に（　）か。Sate, sorosoro shigoto ni (___) ka.

Full sentence: さて、そろそろ仕事に取り掛かるか。Sate, sorosoro shigoto ni torikakaru ka.
EN: Right, shall I get down to work now?
HI: चलो, अब काम शुरू किया जाए?
GU: ચાલો, હવે કામ શરૂ કરીએ?
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1働きかけるhatarakikakeruto approach, lobby, urgeआग्रह करना, पहल करनाઅનુરોધ કરવો, પહેલ કરવી
2仕上げるshiageruto finish, completeपूरा करनाપૂરું કરવું
3取り上げるtoriageruto take up (a topic); take awayउठाना (मुद्दा); छीन लेनाઉઠાવવું (મુદ્દો); છીનવી લેવું
4取り掛かるtorikakaruto start onशुरू करनाશરૂ કરવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.66–67, PDF 148–149). Book's notes (paraphrased): 取り掛かる = 始める. 選択肢の言葉: 1 働きかける = propose to or call on someone (例: citizens 市に働きかけて (shi ni hatarakikakete) started a campaign to keep the town's historic buildings); 2 仕上げる = 完成させる (例: この絵を仕上げるのに (kono e o shiageru no ni) took months); 3 取り上げる = ① take up (例: the problem NHKの報道番組で取り上げられた (NHK no houdou bangumi de toriagerareta)) ② take away from someone (例: the child was playing with a lighter, so I quickly 取り上げた (toriageta)). (added, not in book) そろそろ (it's about time) + 仕事に〜 → begin: 仕事に取り掛かる. 仕上げる would take を (仕事を仕上げる).

つきましてはtsukimashite wa / つつしんでtsutsushinde / つとめてtsutomete / はやばやとhayabaya to accordingly / respectfully / making an effort / early つきましては links two sentences in a formal letter; つつしんで〜申し上げます (greetings, condolences); つとめて明るく振る舞う; はやばやと準備する.
野暮yabo / 無茶mucha / 強硬kyoukou / 素敵suteki unrefined, tactless / unreasonable / hard-line / lovely 野暮な服・野暮な質問 (opposite: 粋 iki); 無茶な計画; 強硬に反対する; 素敵な服.
みなすminasu / 認めるmitomeru / 見計らうmihakarau / 見積もるmitsumoru deem / admit, approve / choose the right moment / estimate AをBとみなす; 失敗を認める; 頃合いを見計らう; 費用を見積もる (compare 見積もり in 第11回).
取材shuzai / 捜査sousa / 観察kansatsu / 審判shinpan reporting / police investigation / observation / judgement, referee Who does it? reporters 取材; police 捜査; scientists 観察; referees and courts 審判.
取り掛かるtorikakaru / 取り上げるtoriageru / 仕上げるshiageru / 働きかけるhatarakikakeru start / take up, take away / finish / lobby 仕事に取り掛かる (に); 問題を取り上げる; レポートを仕上げる; 政府に働きかける.
Exam trap: in formal letters, つきましては (therefore, in connection with this) is the standard bridge from an announcement to a request. Don't confuse it with 〜につきましては (= 〜については, "regarding ~"), as in 第16回 Q112 詳しい内容につきましては.
