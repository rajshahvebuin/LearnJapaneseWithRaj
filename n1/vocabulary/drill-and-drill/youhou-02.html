2 Every question — 問題 (printed pp.68–69)

次の言葉の使い方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no kotoba no tsukaikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the sentence that uses the word best. HI: दिए गए शब्द का सबसे सही प्रयोग वाला वाक्य चुनें। GU: આપેલા શબ્દનો સૌથી યોગ્ય ઉપયોગ ધરાવતું વાક્ય પસંદ કરો.

【7】

つくづくtsukuzuku

Word: deeply, keenly, thoroughly (feel) — 別冊: つくづく = 深く感じる様子 (the state of feeling something deeply)
HI: गहराई से, पूरी तरह (महसूस करना)
GU: ઊંડાણથી, સંપૂર્ણપણે (અનુભવવું)
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1いつも厳しい部長だが、今回の企画に関してはつくづくオーケーが出た。Itsumo kibishii buchou da ga, konkai no kikaku ni kanshite wa tsukuzuku ookee ga deta.The manager is always strict, but this plan got an easy OK.प्रबंधक हमेशा सख़्त रहते हैं, पर इस योजना को आसानी से मंज़ूरी मिल गई।મેનેજર હંમેશાં કડક છે, પણ આ યોજનાને સહેલાઈથી મંજૂરી મળી.
2自分勝手なふるまいをやめない彼がつくづく嫌になった。Jibun-katte na furumai o yamenai kare ga tsukuzuku iya ni natta.I have become thoroughly sick of him – he won't stop behaving selfishly.वह अपनी मनमानी नहीं छोड़ता, इसलिए मैं उससे पूरी तरह ऊब गई हूँ।તે પોતાની મનમાની છોડતો નથી, તેથી હું તેનાથી સાવ કંટાળી ગઈ છું.
3台風のせいで庭に植えたばかりの花がつくづく倒れてしまった。Taifuu no sei de niwa ni ueta bakari no hana ga tsukuzuku taorete shimatta.Because of the typhoon, the flowers I had just planted were all knocked down, roots and all.तूफ़ान के कारण बगीचे में अभी-अभी लगाए फूल जड़ समेत गिर गए।વાવાઝોડાને કારણે બગીચામાં હમણાં જ વાવેલાં ફૂલ મૂળ સાથે ઢળી પડ્યાં.
4どうしてこんな結果になったのか、いくら考えてもつくづくわからない。Doushite konna kekka ni natta no ka, ikura kangaete mo tsukuzuku wakaranai.However much I think about it, I have no idea at all why it turned out like this.कितना भी सोचूँ, बिल्कुल समझ नहीं आता कि ऐसा नतीजा क्यों निकला।ગમે તેટલું વિચારું, જરાય સમજાતું નથી કે આવું પરિણામ કેમ આવ્યું.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). Book's definition: つくづく = 深く感じる様子 (the state of feeling something deeply). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be あっさり (assari) "easily, without fuss"; 3 → should be 根こそぎ (nekosogi) "roots and all, completely"; 4 → should be さっぱり (sappari) "(not) at all". (added, not in book) つくづく goes with verbs of feeling or realising: つくづく感じる・思う・嫌になる・考えさせられる. It does not describe physical events (flowers falling) or "not at all" (that is さっぱり〜ない).

【8】

ニュアンスnyuansu

Word: nuance, subtle shade of meaning
HI: बारीक अर्थ-भेद
GU: સૂક્ષ્મ અર્થભેદ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1地震で被災して以来、国民の防災意識にニュアンスが生じている。Jishin de hisai shite irai, kokumin no bousai ishiki ni nyuansu ga shoujite iru.Since the earthquake disaster, there has been a change in people's awareness of disaster prevention.भूकंप की तबाही के बाद से लोगों की आपदा-सजगता में बदलाव आया है।ભૂકંપની આફત પછી લોકોની આપત્તિ-જાગૃતિમાં પરિવર્તન આવ્યું છે.
2彼は皆を楽しませるユーモアのニュアンスがある。Kare wa mina o tanoshimaseru yuumoa no nyuansu ga aru.He has a sense of humour that entertains everyone.उसमें सबको हँसाने वाली हास्य-समझ है।તેનામાં બધાને હસાવે તેવી રમૂજવૃત્તિ છે.
3この2つの言葉のニュアンスの違いが感じ取れますか。Kono futatsu no kotoba no nyuansu no chigai ga kanjitoremasu ka.Can you sense the difference in nuance between these two words?क्या तुम इन दो शब्दों के बारीक अर्थ-भेद को महसूस कर सकते हो?શું તમે આ બે શબ્દોના સૂક્ષ્મ અર્થભેદને અનુભવી શકો છો?
4いいニュアンスが思いつかないので、少し時間をください。Ii nyuansu ga omoitsukanai node, sukoshi jikan o kudasai.I can't think of a good idea, so please give me a little time.कोई अच्छा विचार नहीं सूझ रहा, थोड़ा समय दीजिए।કોઈ સારો વિચાર સૂઝતો નથી, થોડો સમય આપો.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 変化 (henka) "change"; 2 → should be センス (sensu) "sense, flair"; 4 → should be アイデア (aidea) "idea". (added, not in book) ニュアンス belongs to words, expressions, tone and colour (言葉のニュアンス・微妙なニュアンス). It is not a "change", a talent (センス) or an idea.

【9】

気がとがめるki ga togameru

Word: to feel guilty, have a guilty conscience — 別冊: 気がとがめる = 自分の行動について、良心に恥ずかしいと感じる (to feel ashamed before one's conscience about what one did)
HI: मन में अपराध-बोध होना
GU: મનમાં અપરાધભાવ થવો
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1地震の被災地にいる息子がどうしているかと気がとがめる。Jishin no hisaichi ni iru musuko ga dou shite iru ka to ki ga togameru.I'm anxious about how my son in the earthquake zone is doing.भूकंप-प्रभावित इलाक़े में मेरा बेटा कैसा है, इसकी मुझे बेचैनी है।ભૂકંપગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તારમાં મારો દીકરો કેમ છે તેની મને ચિંતા થાય છે.
2息子を叱った後で、ちょっときびしく言い過ぎたかと気がとがめた。Musuko o shikatta ato de, chotto kibishiku iisugita ka to ki ga togameta.After scolding my son, I felt guilty that I'd been a bit too harsh.बेटे को डाँटने के बाद मुझे अपराध-बोध हुआ कि शायद मैंने ज़्यादा सख़्ती से कहा।દીકરાને ઠપકો આપ્યા પછી મને અપરાધભાવ થયો કે કદાચ વધુ કડક બોલી ગઈ.
3気がとがめるときは、楽しい音楽を聴いて元気を取り戻す。Ki ga togameru toki wa, tanoshii ongaku o kiite genki o torimodosu.When I feel down, I listen to cheerful music to get my spirits back.जब मन उदास होता है, मैं ख़ुशनुमा संगीत सुनकर फिर से ताज़गी पाता हूँ।મન ઉદાસ હોય ત્યારે હું આનંદી સંગીત સાંભળીને ફરી તાજગી મેળવું છું.
4ライバルが目の前にいるのに、彼は平然と、まったく気がとがめない様子だった。Raibaru ga me no mae ni iru noni, kare wa heizen to, mattaku ki ga togamenai yousu datta.Even with his rival right in front of him, he looked calm and paid no attention at all.प्रतिद्वंद्वी सामने होने पर भी वह शांत था और उसे ज़रा भी परवाह नहीं थी।હરીફ સામે હોવા છતાં તે શાંત હતો અને તેને જરાય પરવા નહોતી.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). Book's definition: 気がとがめる = 自分の行動について、良心に恥ずかしいと感じる (to feel ashamed before one's conscience about what one did). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 気がもめる (ki ga momeru) "to feel anxious, fret"; 3 → should be 心が落ち込んだ (kokoro ga ochikonda) "felt down"; 4 → should be 気にとめない (ki ni tomenai) "pay no attention". (added, not in book) 気がとがめる is about your own wrongdoing — you feel your conscience pricking. Worry about someone else is 気がもめる; not minding is 気にとめない.

【10】

若手wakate

Word: the younger members (of a group or profession), up-and-coming — 別冊: 若手 = グループの中の若いほうの人たち (the younger people within a group)
HI: समूह/पेशे के युवा सदस्य
GU: જૂથ/વ્યવસાયના યુવાન સભ્યો
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1もう若手のころのようには、体が思うように動かない。Mou wakate no koro no you ni wa, karada ga omou you ni ugokanai.My body no longer moves the way I want, as it did when I was young.अब शरीर जवानी के दिनों की तरह मन मुताबिक़ नहीं चलता।હવે શરીર યુવાનીના દિવસોની જેમ મન મુજબ ચાલતું નથી.
2若手議員がこれからの日本を変えていくべきだ。Wakate giin ga korekara no Nihon o kaete iku beki da.Young members of parliament should be the ones to change Japan's future.युवा सांसदों को आने वाले जापान को बदलना चाहिए।યુવાન સાંસદોએ આવનારા જાપાનને બદલવું જોઈએ.
3彼女は若手が着るような派手な服を好む。Kanojo wa wakate ga kiru you na hade na fuku o konomu.She likes flashy clothes of the kind young people wear.उसे युवाओं जैसे भड़कीले कपड़े पसंद हैं।તેને યુવાનો પહેરે તેવાં ભડકીલાં કપડાં ગમે છે.
4私はまだまだ若手ですから、これからもどうぞご指導ください。Watashi wa madamada wakate desu kara, korekara mo douzo go-shidou kudasai.I am still very inexperienced, so please continue to guide me.मैं अभी बहुत कच्चा हूँ, कृपया आगे भी मार्गदर्शन करते रहिए।હું હજી ઘણો કાચો છું, કૃપા કરીને આગળ પણ માર્ગદર્શન આપતા રહો.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). Book's definition: 若手 = グループの中の若いほうの人たち (the younger people within a group). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 若い (wakai) "young"; 3 → should be 若者 (wakamono) "young people (in general)"; 4 → should be 未熟 (mijuku) "inexperienced, immature". (added, not in book) 若手 means the younger members of a particular group (若手議員・若手社員・若手作家). Young people in general are 若者; a period of one's life is 若いころ. The book rejects 4 because the speaker is stressing lack of skill — 未熟.

【11】

日向hinata

Word: a sunny place, (in) the sun — 別冊: 日向 = 日が当たる場所 (a place the sun shines on)
HI: धूप वाली जगह
GU: તડકાવાળી જગ્યા
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1今日は曇っていて日向が差さない。Kyou wa kumotte ite hinata ga sasanai.It's cloudy today and the sun isn't shining.आज बादल हैं और धूप नहीं निकल रही।આજે વાદળ છે અને તડકો નીકળતો નથી.
2この部屋は南向きでとても日向がいい。Kono heya wa minami-muki de totemo hinata ga ii.This room faces south and gets lots of sun.यह कमरा दक्षिणमुखी है और इसमें खूब धूप आती है।આ ઓરડો દક્ષિણમુખી છે અને તેમાં ખૂબ તડકો આવે છે.
3夏の日向を浴びて、植物がすくすくと成長する。Natsu no hinata o abite, shokubutsu ga sukusuku to seichou suru.Bathed in summer sunshine, the plants grow fast.गर्मियों की धूप में नहाकर पौधे तेज़ी से बढ़ते हैं।ઉનાળાના તડકામાં નહાઈને છોડ ઝડપથી વધે છે.
4天気のいい日は鉢植えの植物を日向に出してやる。Tenki no ii hi wa hachiue no shokubutsu o hinata ni dashite yaru.On fine days I put the potted plants out in the sun.अच्छे मौसम वाले दिन मैं गमले के पौधों को धूप में रख देता हूँ।સારા હવામાનના દિવસે હું કૂંડાના છોડને તડકામાં મૂકું છું.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). Book's definition: 日向 = 日が当たる場所 (a place the sun shines on). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 日 (hi) "the sun / sunlight (日が差す)"; 2 → should be 日当たり (hiatari) "exposure to sunlight (日当たりがいい)"; 3 → should be 日差し (hizashi) "sunshine, the sun's rays (日差しを浴びる)". (added, not in book) 日向 is a place, so it takes place particles: 日向に出す・日向で干す・日向ぼっこ. The sun's rays are 日差し, how much sun a room gets is 日当たり.

【12】

ざっくばらんzakkubaran

Word: frank, open, unreserved — 別冊: ざっくばらん = 心を隠さない様子・遠慮をしない様子 (not hiding one's feelings; not holding back)
HI: खुले दिल वाला, बेबाक
GU: ખુલ્લા દિલનું, નિખાલસ

1彼女はざっくばらんな性格で、裏表がなく、みんなに好かれている。Kanojo wa zakkubaran na seikaku de, uraomote ga naku, minna ni sukarete iru.She is frank by nature, never two-faced, and everyone likes her.वह बेबाक स्वभाव की है, दोमुँही नहीं, और सब उसे पसंद करते हैं।તે નિખાલસ સ્વભાવની છે, બેમોઢી નથી, અને બધાને ગમે છે.
2ざっくばらんな量の仕事を前にして、思わずため息が出た。Zakkubaran na ryou no shigoto o mae ni shite, omowazu tameiki ga deta.Faced with an enormous amount of work, I sighed in spite of myself.काम का विशाल ढेर सामने देखकर अनायास आह निकल गई।કામનો વિશાળ ઢગલો સામે જોઈને અનાયાસે નિસાસો નીકળી ગયો.
3毎日雨ばかりで、気分がざっくばらんになる。Mainichi ame bakari de, kibun ga zakkubaran ni naru.It rains every day and I feel gloomy.रोज़ बारिश से मन उदास हो जाता है।રોજ વરસાદથી મન ઉદાસ થઈ જાય છે.
4今回の企画のざっくばらんな予定を説明します。Konkai no kikaku no zakkubaran na yotei o setsumei shimasu.I'll explain the rough schedule for this project.मैं इस योजना का मोटा-मोटा कार्यक्रम बताता हूँ।હું આ યોજનાનો આશરે કાર્યક્રમ સમજાવું છું.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.86–87, PDF 168–169). Book's definition: ざっくばらん = 心を隠さない様子・遠慮をしない様子 (not hiding one's feelings; not holding back). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 膨大 (boudai) "enormous, vast"; 3 → should be ゆううつ (yuuutsu) "gloomy, depressed"; 4 → should be おおまか (oomaka) "rough, approximate". (added, not in book) ざっくばらん describes people and the way they talk (ざっくばらんな人・ざっくばらんに話す). Its sound may suggest "rough" or "big", but quantities are 膨大 and rough plans are おおまか.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
つくづくtsukuzuku / あっさりassari / 根こそぎnekosogi / さっぱりsappari deeply, keenly, thoroughly (feel) / easily, without fuss / roots and all, completely / (not) at all つくづく = deeply feel (つくづく嫌になる); あっさり = easily, simply (あっさり認める); 根こそぎ = uprooting everything; さっぱり〜ない = not … at all. All are adverbs; only つくづく is about inner feeling.
ニュアンスnyuansu / 変化henka / センスsensu / アイデアaidea nuance, subtle shade of meaning / change / sense, flair / idea ニュアンス = fine shade of meaning; センス = taste, flair (ユーモアのセンス); アイデア = an idea (アイデアが浮かぶ); 変化 = change. Three katakana words that English speakers might mix up.
気がとがめるki ga togameru / 気がもめるki ga momeru / 心が落ち込んだkokoro ga ochikonda / 気にとめないki ni tomenai to feel guilty, have a guilty conscience / to feel anxious, fret / felt down / pay no attention 気がとがめる = guilty conscience; 気がもめる = anxious, on edge (waiting for news); 気にとめない = not bother about; 落ち込む = feel low. All 気 idioms, all about the mind, all different.
若手wakate / 若いwakai / 若者wakamono / 未熟mijuku the younger members (of a group or profession), up-and-coming / young / young people (in general) / inexperienced, immature 若手 = younger members of a field (若手社員); 若者 = young people in society; 若い = young (adj.); 未熟 = not yet skilled. 若手 is about position in a group, not just age.
日向hinata / 日hi / 日当たりhiatari / 日差しhizashi a sunny place, (in) the sun / the sun / sunlight (日が差す) / exposure to sunlight (日当たりがいい) / sunshine, the sun's rays (日差しを浴びる) 日向 = a sunny spot (place); 日差し = sunlight falling on you; 日当たり = how sunny a room/site is; 日が差す = the sun shines. Opposite of 日向 = 日陰 (shade).
ざっくばらんzakkubaran / 膨大boudai / ゆううつyuuutsu / おおまかoomaka frank, open, unreserved / enormous, vast / gloomy, depressed / rough, approximate ざっくばらん = frank (people/speech); おおまか = rough, broad-brush (plan, estimate); 膨大 = huge in amount; ゆううつ = gloomy mood. Don't guess from the sound — ざっくり (roughly) is a different word.
Exam trap: several wrong sentences in this round would be fine in English: "I'm thoroughly confused" (つくづく), "a frank (=rough) schedule" (ざっくばらん), "the nuance changed". Translate the headword back into Japanese collocations, not English ones: つくづく感じる, ざっくばらんに話す, 言葉のニュアンス, 若手社員, 日向に出す.
