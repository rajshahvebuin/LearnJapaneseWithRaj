2 Every question — 問題 (p.38)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【15】

初めての職場で（　）いた私に、先輩がやさしく声をかけてくれた。Hajimete no shokuba de (___) ita watashi ni, senpai ga yasashiku koe o kakete kureta.

Full sentence: 初めての職場でまごついていた私に、先輩がやさしく声をかけてくれた。Hajimete no shokuba de magotsuite ita watashi ni, senpai ga yasashiku koe o kakete kureta.
EN: When I was floundering at my first workplace, a senior colleague kindly spoke to me.
HI: पहली नौकरी की जगह पर जब मैं घबराया/घबराई हुआ था/थी, तो एक सीनियर ने प्यार से मुझसे बात की।
GU: પહેલી નોકરીની જગ્યાએ હું ગભરાયેલો/ગભરાયેલી હતો/હતી ત્યારે એક સિનિયરે પ્રેમથી મારી સાથે વાત કરી.

1まぎれてmagiretebe lost among, blend in (紛れる)घुल-मिल जाना, छिप जानाભળી જવું, છુપાઈ જવું
2とぎれてtogiretebreak off, be interrupted (途切れる)टूट जाना, रुक जानाતૂટી જવું, અટકી જવું
3まごついてmagotsuitebe at a loss (まごつく)घबरा जानाગભરાઈ જવું

Answer 3 (別冊 p.40–41, PDF 122–123). Book's notes (paraphrased): まごつく = まごまごする, be at a loss. 選択肢の言葉: 1 まぎれる (紛れる) = ① become impossible to tell apart (例: our army approached the enemy 夜の闇に紛れて (yoru no yami ni magirete)); ② 気がまぎれる = bad feelings or tension fade because your attention turns elsewhere (例: I was down after failing the exam, but after a trip with a friend 気がまぎれた (ki ga magireta)); 2 とぎれる (途切れる) = something continuing is cut off (例: talking with her, the conversation never とぎれる (togireru)); 4 うろつく = うろうろする (例: a strange man 家の前をうろついている (ie no mae o urotsuite iru)). (added, not in book) A newcomer who gets a kind word from a senior was confused, not hiding or loitering: まごつく.

【16】

この機械の（　）は複雑だから、故障するとやっかいだ。Kono kikai no (___) wa fukuzatsu da kara, koshou suru to yakkai da.

Full sentence: この機械の仕組みは複雑だから、故障するとやっかいだ。Kono kikai no shikumi wa fukuzatsu da kara, koshou suru to yakkai da.
EN: This machine's mechanism is complicated, so it's a real nuisance when it breaks down.
HI: इस मशीन की बनावट जटिल है, इसलिए ख़राब होने पर बड़ी मुसीबत होती है।
GU: આ મશીનની રચના જટિલ છે, તેથી બગડે ત્યારે મોટી મુશ્કેલી થાય છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1用法youhouusage, how to useप्रयोग-विधिઉપયોગની રીત
2仕組みshikumimechanism, structureढाँचा, काम करने का तरीक़ाમાળખું, કામ કરવાની રીત
3構成kouseicomposition, make-upसंरचना, गठनરચના, ગઠન
4取り組みtorikumieffort, approach (to a problem)प्रयास, मुद्दे से निपटने की कोशिशપ્રયાસ, પ્રશ્ન ઉકેલવાની કોશિશ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.41, PDF 123). Book's notes (paraphrased): 仕組み = structure, how something is put together. 選択肢の言葉: 1 用法 = 使い方 (例: this dictionary explains not only meanings but 用法 (youhou)); 3 参考: you do not say 機械の構成 — you say 機械の構造 (kikai no kouzou); 4 取り組み = an effort to solve a problem (例: エネルギー問題への取り組み (enerugii mondai e no torikumi) is a task for the whole world). (added, not in book) 構成 is for things made of parts that are "arranged" — a text, a team, a family (家族構成) — not machinery.

【17】

大事な部分をもっと強調すれば、（　）のある、生き生きした文章になるでしょう。Daiji na bubun o motto kyouchou sureba, (___) no aru, ikiiki shita bunshou ni naru deshou.

Full sentence: 大事な部分をもっと強調すれば、めりはりのある、生き生きした文章になるでしょう。Daiji na bubun o motto kyouchou sureba, merihari no aru, ikiiki shita bunshou ni naru deshou.
EN: If you stress the important parts more, your writing will have more light and shade and feel more alive.
HI: अगर तुम ज़रूरी हिस्सों पर और ज़ोर दो, तो लेख में उतार-चढ़ाव आएगा और वह जीवंत लगेगा।
GU: જો તમે મહત્ત્વના ભાગો પર વધુ ભાર મૂકો, તો લખાણમાં ચઢાવ-ઉતાર આવશે અને તે જીવંત લાગશે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1けじめkejimea clear line, distinction (between right/wrong, public/private)सीमा-रेखा, स्पष्ट भेदસીમારેખા, સ્પષ્ટ ભેદ
2切れ目kiremea break, gap, pauseदरार, बीच का अंतरालતિરાડ, વચ્ચેનો ગાળો
3めりはりmeriharicontrast, light and shadeउतार-चढ़ावચઢાવ-ઉતાર
4区別kubetsudistinction, telling apartभेद, अंतर करनाભેદ, તફાવત પાડવો

Answer 3 (別冊 p.41, PDF 123). Book's notes (paraphrased): めりはり = accent, stress (ups and downs). 選択肢の言葉: 1 けじめ = drawing a line, distinction (例: for a politician, 公私のけじめをきちんとつける (koushi no kejime o kichinto tsukeru) is only natural); 2 切れ目 = 区切り, a break (例: cars pass 切れ目なく (kireme naku) on this road, so crossing is dangerous). The 別冊 has no note for 4 区別. (added, not in book) 強調 (emphasis) + 生き生き (lively) describe rhythm and contrast in style → めりはりのある文章. けじめ and 区別 are about separating things, not about making them lively.

【18】

（　）一年で一番忙しいこの時期に、旅行に行くから休みを取りたいなんて、何を言ってるんだ。とんでもないよ。(___) ichinen de ichiban isogashii kono jiki ni, ryokou ni iku kara yasumi o toritai nante, nani o itteru n da. Tondemonai yo.

Full sentence: よりによって一年で一番忙しいこの時期に、旅行に行くから休みを取りたいなんて、何を言ってるんだ。とんでもないよ。Yori ni yotte ichinen de ichiban isogashii kono jiki ni, ryokou ni iku kara yasumi o toritai nante, nani o itteru n da. Tondemonai yo.
EN: You want time off for a trip now, of all times — the busiest season of the year? What are you saying? Out of the question!
HI: साल के सबसे व्यस्त समय में ही — और कोई समय नहीं मिला? — घूमने जाने के लिए छुट्टी चाहिए? क्या कह रहे हो! बिल्कुल नहीं।
GU: વર્ષના સૌથી વ્યસ્ત સમયે જ — બીજો કોઈ સમય ન મળ્યો? — ફરવા જવા માટે રજા જોઈએ છે? શું બોલો છો! બિલકુલ નહીં.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1あいにくainikuunfortunately (as it happens)दुर्भाग्य सेકમનસીબે
2あしからずashikarazuplease don't take it badly (polite apology)कृपया बुरा न मानेंકૃપા કરીને ખોટું ન લગાડશો
3とりたててtoritatete(not) particularlyख़ास तौर पर (नहीं)ખાસ કરીને (નહીં)
4よりによってyori ni yotteof all times/peopleऔर कोई समय/व्यक्ति नहीं मिला?બીજો કોઈ સમય/વ્યક્તિ ન મળ્યું?

Answer 4 (別冊 p.41–42, PDF 123–124). Book's notes (paraphrased): よりによって = making a bad choice although better ones were available. 選択肢の言葉: 1 あいにく = 残念ながら (例: the wedding day あいにく雨になった (ainiku ame ni natta)); 2 あしからず (悪しからず) = I hope you won't think badly of it (例: I can't attend that day — あしからず、よろしくお願いします (ashikarazu, yoroshiku onegai shimasu)); 3 とりたてて (取り立てて) = 特に(〜ない) (例: that happens all the time, so we needn't とりたてて問題にしなくても (toritatete mondai ni shinakute mo)). (added, not in book) The speaker is angry about a bad choice of timing → よりによって. あいにく is regret about bad luck, with no one to blame.

【19】

報告書の原稿は（　）を3回もしたのに、まだ間違いがあった。Houkokusho no genkou wa (___) o sankai mo shita no ni, mada machigai ga atta.

GU: રિપોર્ટના મુસદ્દાને મેં ત્રણ વાર તપાસ્યો, છતાં તેમાં ભૂલો રહી ગઈ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1コメントkomentocommentटिप्पणीટિપ્પણી
2キャッチkyacchicatch, pick up (information)पकड़ना, (ख़बर) हासिल करनाપકડવું, (માહિતી) મેળવવી
3チェックchekkucheckजाँचતપાસ
4ドリルdorirudrill (repeated practice)अभ्यास (बार-बार)મહાવરો (વારંવાર)

Answer 3 (別冊 p.42, PDF 124). Book's notes (paraphrased): チェック(する) = check. 選択肢の言葉: 1 コメント = comment (例: Minister, コメントを一言お願いします (komento o hitokoto onegai shimasu) on this issue); 2 キャッチ = catch, hear (例: where did you キャッチした (kyacchi shita) that information?); 4 ドリル = practice repeated many times (例: ドリルが効果的だ (doriru ga koukateki da) for improving arithmetic). (added, not in book) The goal of reading a draft again is finding mistakes (まだ間違いがあった) → チェック.

【20】

だれかの（　）一言が、新しいアイデアのヒントになることもある。Dareka no (___) hitokoto ga, atarashii aidea no hinto ni naru koto mo aru.

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1おっかないokkanaiscaryडरावनाડરામણું

3何気ないnanigenaicasual, offhandसहज, बिना ख़ास इरादे केસહજ, ખાસ ઇરાદા વગર

(added, not in book) The point of the sentence is that a remark made without any intention can spark an idea → 何気ない一言 (a set phrase).

【21】

だれに言われたわけでもありません。私（　）の考えでやりました。Dare ni iwareta wake de mo arimasen. Watashi (___) no kangae de yarimashita.

Full sentence: だれに言われたわけでもありません。私独自の考えでやりました。Dare ni iwareta wake de mo arimasen. Watashi dokuji no kangae de yarimashita.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.42, PDF 124). Book's notes (paraphrased): 独自 = one's own, original. 選択肢の言葉: 1 独特 = a feature nobody else has (例: his paintings are famous for 独特の色使い (dokutoku no iro-zukai)); 2 固有 = something a thing has had from the start, by nature (例: protection campaigns so that 日本固有の動物 (Nihon koyuu no doubutsu) don't die out); 3 特有 = found only in that thing (例: soy sauce was originally 日本料理特有の調味料 (Nihon ryouri tokuyuu no choumiryou)). (added, not in book) "Nobody told me — it was my ___ idea" means independent of others → 独自. 独特 / 特有 describe a distinctive quality, not who decided.

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
まごつくmagotsuku vs うろつくurotsuku be at a loss vs loiter Both 〜つく verbs of "not settled". まごつく = mentally confused about what to do; うろつく = physically wandering about (often suspicious).
まぎれるmagireru vs とぎれるtogireru blend in / be distracted vs break off 闇に紛れる, 気がまぎれる vs 会話・連絡がとぎれる. One letter apart in sound.

めりはりmerihari / けじめkejime / 区別kubetsu / 切れ目kireme contrast / clear line / distinction / break めりはり = ups and downs that make speech or life lively; けじめ = a moral or social line you keep (公私のけじめ); 区別 = telling two things apart; 切れ目 = a gap (切れ目なく = non-stop).
よりによってyori ni yotte vs あいにくainiku of all times (bad choice) vs unfortunately (bad luck) よりによって blames a choice someone made; あいにく just regrets circumstances (あいにく雨だ).
何気ないnanigenai vs 冴えないsaenai casual vs dull / not looking well Both 〜ない adjectives that are not negatives of a verb. 何気ない一言 (offhand remark); 冴えない顔 (a gloomy face).
独自dokuji / 独特dokutoku / 特有tokuyuu / 固有koyuu one's own / unique / peculiar to / native to 独自 = done independently (独自の考え, 独自に開発); 独特 = a distinctive style (独特の味); 特有 = typical only of a group (日本料理特有); 固有 = belonging to it from the start (日本固有の動物).
Exam trap: 独自・独特・特有・固有 is one of the most-tested N1 sets. Ask "who made it so?" — if a person chose or created it, 独自; if it is simply a feature, 独特 / 特有; if it was there from the origin, 固有.
