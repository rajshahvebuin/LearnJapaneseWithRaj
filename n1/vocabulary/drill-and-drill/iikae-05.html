1 Points — the words tested in this round

The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.77–78)

2 Every question — 問題 (p.60)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【25】

事件解決の手掛かりは全くない。Jiken kaiketsu no tegakari wa mattaku nai.

Underlined word: 手掛かり = 解決の鍵となるもの (the starting point / key to solving something).
EN: There is absolutely no clue to solving the case.
HI: मामले को सुलझाने का कोई सुराग़ बिल्कुल नहीं है।
GU: કેસ ઉકેલવા માટે કોઈ સુરાગ બિલકુલ નથી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1正解seikaithe correct answerसही उत्तरસાચો જવાબ
2糸口itoguchia clue, the thread-end to pullसुराग़, सिराસુરાગ, દોરાનો છેડો
3手段shudanmeansसाधनસાધન
4方法houhoumethodतरीक़ाરીત

Answer 2 (別冊 p.77, PDF 159). Book's notes (paraphrased): 手掛かり = 糸口, the beginning of a solution, the thing that becomes the key to it. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 糸口 is literally "the end of the thread" you pull to unravel a knot — a starting point, not the whole 方法／手段 (how you proceed) and not the 正解 (the finished answer).

【26】

私にその仕事をこなすことができるだろうか。Watashi ni sono shigoto o konasu koto ga dekiru darou ka.

Underlined word: こなす = (仕事を)ちゃんと済ませる (to get a job properly done).
EN: I wonder whether I can handle that job.
HI: क्या मैं वह काम ठीक से निपटा पाऊँगा?
GU: શું હું એ કામ બરાબર પાર પાડી શકીશ?
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1把握するhaaku suruto grasp, understand fullyपूरी तरह समझनाપૂરેપૂરું સમજવું
2完了するkanryou suruto completeपूरा करनाપૂર્ણ કરવું
3継続するkeizoku suruto continueजारी रखनाચાલુ રાખવું
4処理するshori suruto handle, processनिपटानाપતાવવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.78, PDF 160). Book's notes (paraphrased): こなす = 処理する, to get (work) done properly. 選択肢の言葉: 1 把握する = to understand firmly (例: 私にはこの文章の意味が把握できない (watashi ni wa kono bunshou no imi ga haaku dekinai)). The 別冊 has no note for 2 or 3. (added, not in book) 2 完了する is the near-miss — it only marks the end point, whereas こなす／処理する covers dealing competently with the whole task (仕事をこなす・注文をさばく・案件を処理する).

【27】

宿泊したホテルのサービスは申し分なかった。Shukuhaku shita hoteru no saabisu wa moushibun nakatta.

Underlined word: 申し分ない = 不足なところや不満なところがない (nothing lacking, nothing to complain about).
EN: The service at the hotel we stayed in was impeccable.
HI: जिस होटल में हम ठहरे, उसकी सेवा में कोई कमी नहीं थी।
GU: અમે જે હોટલમાં રોકાયા તેની સેવામાં કોઈ ખામી નહોતી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1思いがけなかったomoigakenakattawas unexpectedअप्रत्याशित थाઅણધાર્યું હતું
2月並みだったtsukinami dattawas ordinary, run-of-the-millसाधारण थाસામાન્ય હતું
3もの足りなかったmonotarinakattawas unsatisfying, left something wantingकुछ कमी-सी थीકંઈક ખૂટતું હતું
4完璧だったkanpeki dattawas perfectपरिपूर्ण थाસંપૂર્ણ હતું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.78, PDF 160). Book's notes (paraphrased): 申し分ない = 完璧だ, nothing insufficient or unsatisfactory from any angle. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) The 〜ない ending makes 申し分ない look negative, so 2 and 3 (both mildly negative judgements) are the traps; it literally means "there is nothing one could say against it" — fully positive. 月並み is tested again in 第7回 Q42.

【28】

これだけの貯金があれば、当面は困らないだろう。Kore dake no chokin ga areba, toumen wa komaranai darou.

Underlined word: 当面 = (将来はわからないが)今のところ (for now, whatever the future holds).
EN: With this much saved, we won't be in trouble for the time being.
HI: इतनी बचत है तो फ़िलहाल कोई परेशानी नहीं होगी।
GU: આટલી બચત હોય તો હાલ પૂરતી કોઈ મુશ્કેલી નહીં પડે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1さしあたりsashiatarifor the presentफ़िलहालહાલ પૂરતું
2将来shouraiin the futureभविष्य मेंભવિષ્યમાં
3近頃chikagororecently, these daysआजकलહમણાં હમણાં
4直ちにtadachi niimmediatelyतुरंतતરત જ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.78, PDF 160). Book's notes (paraphrased): 当面 = さしあたり, for now (the future being unknown). The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) All four are time words, but 近頃 looks back (recent past), 直ちに is a point (right now, at once), and 将来 is the far side — 当面 is the stretch from now into the near future.

【29】

この辺でちょっと一服しようか。Kono hen de chotto ippuku shiyou ka.

Underlined word: 一服する = 休憩する (to take a break).
EN: Shall we take a short break about now?
HI: क्यों न यहीं थोड़ा सुस्ता लें?
GU: ચાલો, અહીં થોડો વિરામ લઈએ?
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1休養kyuuyourest and recuperation (longer, for health)आराम, स्वास्थ्य-लाभઆરામ, સ્વાસ્થ્ય માટે વિશ્રામ
2休憩kyuukeia (short) break(छोटा) विराम(ટૂંકો) વિરામ
3着席chakusekitaking one's seatबैठ जाना, आसन ग्रहणબેસી જવું, આસન લેવું
4外出gaishutsugoing outबाहर जानाબહાર જવું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.78, PDF 160). Book's notes (paraphrased): 一服する = 休憩する. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 1 休養 is the near-miss: it means days of rest to recover health (休養を取る), far too long for ちょっと一服. 一服 originally = one puff of tobacco or one cup of tea — hence a few minutes' pause.

【30】

これは日本酒をベースにした、さわやかで飲みやすいお酒です。Kore wa nihonshu o beesu ni shita, sawayaka de nomiyasui o-sake desu.

Underlined word: ベース = 基本になるもの／主要な材料 (the basis; the main ingredient).
EN: This is a refreshing, easy-to-drink liquor based on sake.
HI: यह जापानी साके पर आधारित, ताज़गी भरी और पीने में आसान शराब है।
GU: આ જાપાની સાકે પર આધારિત, તાજગીભર્યો અને પીવામાં સરળ દારૂ છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1材料zairyouingredient, materialसामग्रीસામગ્રી
2見本mihonsample, modelनमूनाનમૂનો
3基礎kisobasis, foundationआधारઆધાર, પાયો
4中心chuushincentreकेंद्रકેન્દ્ર

Answer 3 (別冊 p.78, PDF 160). Book's notes (paraphrased): ベース = 基礎, what something is built on / its main material. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 1 材料 is the trap: sake is an ingredient here, but 〜をベースにする means "take X as the foundation and build on it", which is 基礎, not just "one of the ingredients". Compare the 別冊's ✎ example, where ベース is a pay scale — no ingredients at all.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
手掛かりtegakari / 糸口itoguchi / 手段shudan / 方法houhou clue / clue, starting thread / means / method 手掛かり・糸口 = where you start (捜査の手掛かり, 解決の糸口); 手段・方法 = how you proceed. Having a clue is not yet having a method.
こなすkonasu / 処理するshori suru / 完了するkanryou suru / 把握するhaaku suru handle competently / process / complete / grasp 仕事をこなす・処理する = deal with it (competence); 完了する = reach the end (status); 把握する = understand it (状況を把握する) — no action on the task.
申し分ないmoushibun nai / 完璧kanpeki / もの足りないmonotarinai / 月並みtsukinami flawless / perfect / unsatisfying / ordinary Two 〜ない words, opposite polarity: 申し分ない = fully positive; もの足りない = "not enough". 月並み is lukewarm (nothing special). Don't read ない as "bad".
当面toumen / さしあたりsashiatari / 当座touza / 近頃chikagoro / 直ちにtadachi ni for the time being / for the present / for now / lately / at once 当面・さしあたり・当座 (第2回 Q10) all = "for now, until things change". 近頃 looks back; 直ちに is instantaneous. 当面の課題 = the immediate task.
一服ippuku / 休憩kyuukei / 休養kyuuyou / 休息kyuusoku short break / break / recuperation / rest 一服 ≈ 休憩 = minutes (ちょっと一服・10分休憩). 休養 = days off for health (十分な休養を取る). 休息 = rest in general.
ベースbeesu / 基礎kiso / 材料zairyou / 中心chuushin base / foundation / ingredient / centre 〜をベースにする = build on X as the foundation (給与をベースに, トマトをベースにしたソース). 材料 is any component; 中心 is the focal point, not the foundation.
Exam trap: 申し分ない (Q27) belongs to a family of positive words that end in ない: 申し分ない, 文句ない ("no complaints"), 間違いない ("certain"), とんでもない ("outrageous / not at all"), せわしない ("restless" — here ない is not even a negative). When an option list offers a negative-sounding paraphrase for a 〜ない word, pause and check its real meaning.
