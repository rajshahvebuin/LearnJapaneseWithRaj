2 Every question — 問題 (p.48)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【85】

（　）の業界は人気が高いので、就職の希望者が多く、競争率が高い。(___) no gyoukai wa ninki ga takai node, shuushoku no kibousha ga ooku, kyousouritsu ga takai.

Full sentence: マスコミの業界は人気が高いので、就職の希望者が多く、競争率が高い。Masukomi no gyoukai wa ninki ga takai node, shuushoku no kibousha ga ooku, kyousouritsu ga takai.
EN: The media industry is very popular, so many people want jobs there and competition is fierce.
HI: मीडिया उद्योग बहुत लोकप्रिय है, इसलिए वहाँ नौकरी चाहने वाले बहुत हैं और प्रतिस्पर्धा कड़ी है।
GU: મીડિયા ઉદ્યોગ બહુ લોકપ્રિય છે, તેથી ત્યાં નોકરી ઇચ્છનારા ઘણા છે અને હરીફાઈ સખત છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1マスコミmasukomithe mass mediaमास मीडियाસમૂહ માધ્યમો
2スタイルsutairustyle; figure (body shape)शैली; शरीर की बनावटશૈલી; શરીરનો બાંધો
3キャリアkyariacareer, experienceकरियर, अनुभवકારકિર્દી, અનુભવ
4コメントkomentocommentटिप्पणीટિપ્પણી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.57, PDF 139). Book's notes (paraphrased): マスコミ = mass communication, the media. 選択肢の言葉: 2 スタイル = style; figure (例: he is tall and モデルのようなスタイルをしている (moderu no you na sutairu o shite iru); she is sensitive to 流行のスタイル (ryuukou no sutairu) and studies fashion design); 3 キャリア = career (例: tutors キャリア豊かな (kyaria yutaka na) in exam coaching will get you through); 4 コメント = comment (例: the reporter asked the Prime Minister コメントを求めた (komento o motometa) on youth unemployment). (added, not in book) 〜の業界 (an industry) + 就職 → only マスコミ names a field of work.

【86】

図書館で参考書を（　）調べたら、疑問の答えがようやくわかった。Toshokan de sankousho o (___) shirabetara, gimon no kotae ga youyaku wakatta.

Full sentence: 図書館で参考書をかたっぱしから調べたら、疑問の答えがようやくわかった。Toshokan de sankousho o katappashi kara shirabetara, gimon no kotae ga youyaku wakatta.
EN: I went through the reference books in the library one after another and finally found the answer to my question.
HI: पुस्तकालय में एक-एक करके सारी संदर्भ-पुस्तकें छान मारीं, तब जाकर सवाल का जवाब मिला।
GU: પુસ્તકાલયમાં એક પછી એક બધાં સંદર્ભ-પુસ્તકો ફેંદી નાખ્યાં, ત્યારે માંડ પ્રશ્નનો જવાબ મળ્યો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ありのままにari no mama nijust as it is / wasजैसा है वैसा हीજેવું છે તેવું જ
2続けざまにtsuzukezama niin quick succession (things happen)लगातार एक के बाद एक (घटनाएँ)એક પછી એક સતત (ઘટનાઓ)
3ひき続きhikitsuzukicontinuing, next without a breakआगे जारी, बिना रुकेઆગળ ચાલુ, વગર અટક્યે
4かたっぱしからkatappashi karaone after another, all in turnएक-एक करके सबએક પછી એક બધું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.57–58, PDF 139–140). Book's notes (paraphrased): かたっぱしから (片っ端から) = taking many things one after another, at random (= 手当たり次第に). 選択肢の言葉: 1 ありのままに = exactly as it really was (例: 見たことをありのままに話してください (mita koto o ari no mama ni hanashite kudasai)); 2 続けざまに = the same thing happens repeatedly in a row (例: on the icy road one car slid and 数台続けざまにスリップした (suudai tsuzukezama ni surippu shita); recently earthquakes have struck in several places in a row); 3 ひき続き = continuing without a break (例: the performances are over; ひき続き審査に移ります (hikitsuzuki shinsa ni utsurimasu)). (added, not in book) 参考書を〜調べる — I act on many books, one by one → かたっぱしから. 続けざまに is about events piling up, not about working through a set.

【87】

私が生まれた農村では、今も（　）風景が見られる。Watashi ga umareta nouson de wa, ima mo (___) fuukei ga mirareru.

Full sentence: 私が生まれた農村では、今ものどかな風景が見られる。Watashi ga umareta nouson de wa, ima mo nodoka na fuukei ga mirareru.
EN: In the farming village where I was born, you can still see peaceful countryside scenes.
HI: जिस गाँव में मैं पैदा हुआ, वहाँ आज भी शांत, सुकून भरे नज़ारे दिखते हैं।
GU: જે ગામડામાં હું જન્મ્યો, ત્યાં આજે પણ શાંત, નિરાંતવાળાં દૃશ્યો જોવા મળે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ゆるやかなyuruyaka nagentle (slope, curve); lenientहल्का, धीमा (ढलान); ढीलाહળવું, ધીમું (ઢાળ); ઢીલું
2しなやかなshinayaka nasupple, flexibleलचीलाલચીલું
3ひたむきなhitamuki naearnest, single-mindedलगनशील, एकनिष्ठલગનવાળું, એકનિષ્ઠ
4のどかなnodoka napeaceful, tranquilशांत, सुकून भराશાંત, નિરાંતવાળું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.58, PDF 140). Book's notes (paraphrased): のどか(な) = quiet and relaxed. 選択肢の言葉: 1 ゆるやか(な) = ① a slope or bend is gentle (例: an old church stands ゆるやかな丘の上に (yuruyaka na oka no ue ni)) ② not strict (例: the road was repaired, so 交通規制も緩やかになった (koutsuu kisei mo yuruyaka ni natta)); 2 しなやか(な) = soft and springy (例: the building uses the way bamboo しなやかに曲がる (shinayaka ni magaru)); 3 ひたむき(な) = working hard at one thing without thinking of gain or loss (例: 長年のひたむきな努力 (naganen no hitamuki na doryoku) paid off and the work won first prize). (added, not in book) 農村の風景 (a farming village scene) → のどかな風景 (peaceful, unhurried) is the natural pairing.

【88】

しまった。重大な誤りをうっかり（　）いた。Shimatta. Juudai na ayamari o ukkari (___) ita.

Full sentence: しまった。重大な誤りをうっかり見落としていた。Shimatta. Juudai na ayamari o ukkari miotoshite ita.
EN: Oh no — I had carelessly overlooked a serious mistake.
HI: अरे! एक गंभीर ग़लती मुझसे लापरवाही में नज़रअंदाज़ हो गई थी।
GU: અરે! એક ગંભીર ભૂલ મારાથી બેધ્યાનપણે જોવામાં ચૂકી જવાઈ હતી.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.58, PDF 140). Book's notes (paraphrased): 見落とす = overlook, miss. 選択肢の言葉: 1 取り逃がす = fail to catch (例: the officer 犯人を取り逃がしてしまった (hannin o torinigashite shimatta) right in front of him); 3 すり抜ける = pass through (例: 人ごみをすり抜け (hitogomi o surinuke), I ran to the platform and just caught the train); 4 やり過ごす = wait for people or vehicles to pass (例: a tour group was there, so I 彼らをやり過ごしてから (karera o yarisugoshite kara) went through the ticket gate). (added, not in book) 誤りを〜 + うっかり (carelessly) → failing to notice an error: 見落とす.

【89】

彼女はモデルだけあって、スタイル（　）だ。Kanojo wa moderu dake atte, sutairu (___) da.

【90】

【91】

まだ若いのに、彼はよく礼儀を（　）いる。Mada wakai noni, kare wa yoku reigi o (___) iru.

Full sentence: まだ若いのに、彼はよく礼儀をわきまえている。Mada wakai noni, kare wa yoku reigi o wakimaete iru.
EN: He is still young, but he really knows his manners.
HI: अभी कम उम्र का है, फिर भी उसे शिष्टाचार की अच्छी समझ है।
GU: હજી નાની ઉંમરનો છે, છતાં તેને શિષ્ટાચારની સારી સમજ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1悟ってsatotterealising (悟る)समझ आकर, बोध होकरસમજાઈને, બોધ થઈને
2わきまえてwakimaeteknowing properly (わきまえる)समझ-बूझ रखकरસમજ રાખીને
3見極めてmikiwameteseeing through, judging (見極める)परखकरપારખીને
4踏まえてfumaetebased on (踏まえる)के आधार परના આધારે

Answer 2 (別冊 p.59, PDF 141). Book's notes (paraphrased): わきまえる = know well, discern. 選択肢の言葉: 1 悟る = become aware of the meaning or facts of something you didn't understand (例: 自分が悪かったということを悟り、謝罪した (jibun ga warukatta to iu koto o satori, shazai shita)); 3 見極める = look carefully to the end / see clearly the true nature of something (例: 芸術作品の本当の価値を見極める (geijutsu sakuhin no hontou no kachi o mikiwameru) is difficult); 4 踏まえる = base on (例: the government should make policy 現状を踏まえた (genjou o fumaeta)). (added, not in book) 礼儀をわきまえる (know and keep to proper manners) is a fixed collocation — compare 立場をわきまえる, 場所柄をわきまえる.

Exam trap: four 新〜 words (Q90) or four 見/取/やり compound verbs (Q88) — the shared kanji gives nothing away. Memorise each with the noun it typically goes with: 新米の社員, 新手の詐欺, 新規採用, 新興国; 誤りを見落とす, 犯人を取り逃がす.
