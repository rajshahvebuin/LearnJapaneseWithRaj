2 Every question — 問題 (p.54)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【127】

クレジットカードを（　）ので、すぐカード会社に連絡した。Kurejitto kaado o (___) node, sugu kaado-gaisha ni renraku shita.

Full sentence: クレジットカードを紛失したので、すぐカード会社に連絡した。Kurejitto kaado o funshitsu shita node, sugu kaado-gaisha ni renraku shita.
EN: I lost my credit card, so I contacted the card company straight away.
HI: मेरा क्रेडिट कार्ड खो गया, इसलिए मैंने तुरंत कार्ड कंपनी से संपर्क किया।
GU: મારું ક્રેડિટ કાર્ડ ખોવાઈ ગયું, તેથી મેં તરત કાર્ડ કંપનીનો સંપર્ક કર્યો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1紛失したfunshitsu shitalost (紛失する)खो दियाખોઈ નાખ્યું
2横取りしたyokodori shitasnatched (from someone) (横取りする)हड़प लियाપડાવી લીધું
3万引きしたmanbiki shitashoplifted (万引きする)दुकान से चोरी कीદુકાનમાંથી ચોરી કરી
4失念したshitsunen shitaforgot (失念する)भूल गयाભૂલી ગયો

Answer 1 (別冊 p.69, PDF 151). Book's notes (paraphrased): 紛失する = lose. 選択肢の言葉: 2 横取りする = take someone else's thing from the side (例: 兄が弟の財産を横取りした (ani ga otouto no zaisan o yokodori shita)); 3 万引きする = steal goods while pretending to shop (例: the store guard caught a woman 化粧品を万引きした (keshouhin o manbiki shita)); 4 失念する = carelessly forget (例: お約束していたことを失念し (o-yakusoku shite ita koto o shitsunen shi), I am truly sorry). (added, not in book) You call the card company after you lose your card → 紛失. 失念 is forgetting a matter, not losing an object.

【128】

Answer 2 (別冊 p.69, PDF 151). Book's notes (paraphrased): なんなら (何なら) = (in this question) もしよろしければ, if it's all right with you. 選択肢の言葉: 1 どのみち = in any case / in the end (例: wherever you run, どのみち捕まるだろう (donomichi tsukamaru darou) — better to give yourself up); 3 もしくは = あるいは (例: 品物は月曜日もしくは火曜日にお届けします (shinamono wa getsuyoubi moshiku wa kayoubi ni o-todoke shimasu)); 4 なんだか (何だか) = for no clear reason, somehow (例: なんだか寒いと思ったら (nandaka samui to omottara), the window was open). (added, not in book) An offer (〜ましょうか) introduced politely → なんなら (if it suits you).

【129】

これまでには見られなかった（　）デザインが評価され、彼女の作品が入賞した。Kore made ni wa mirarenakatta (___) dezain ga hyouka sare, kanojo no sakuhin ga nyuushou shita.

Full sentence: これまでには見られなかった斬新なデザインが評価され、彼女の作品が入賞した。Kore made ni wa mirarenakatta zanshin na dezain ga hyouka sare, kanojo no sakuhin ga nyuushou shita.
EN: Her work won a prize, praised for an original design never seen before.
HI: पहले कभी न देखा गया अनोखा डिज़ाइन सराहा गया और उसकी कृति को पुरस्कार मिला।

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1盛大なseidai nagrand, magnificent (event)भव्यભવ્ય
2貴重なkichou naprecious, valuableक़ीमती, बहुमूल्यકીમતી, મૂલ્યવાન
3斬新なzanshin nanovel, originalअनोखा, नयाઅનોખું, નવું
4過剰なkajou naexcessiveज़रूरत से ज़्यादाજરૂર કરતાં વધુ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.69, PDF 151). Book's notes (paraphrased): 斬新(な) = drastic, original, unconventional. 選択肢の言葉: 1 盛大(な) = ① full of energy (例: when the performance ended 盛大な拍手が起こった (seidai na hakushu ga okotta)) ② (a ceremony or party) held on a large scale (例: 20周年記念パーティーが盛大に行われた (nijisshuunen kinen paatii ga seidai ni okonawareta)); 4 過剰(な) = more than necessary (例: 彼は自信過剰で (kare wa jishin kajou de), thinks he can do anything). The 別冊 has no note for 2 貴重. (added, not in book) これまでには見られなかった (never seen before) = new → 斬新なデザイン.

35年の（　）を組んで、マンションを購入した。Sanjuugonen no (___) o kunde, manshon o kounyuu shita.

HI: 35 साल का लोन लेकर हमने फ़्लैट ख़रीदा।
GU: 35 વર્ષની લોન લઈને અમે ફ્લૅટ ખરીદ્યો.

2メディアmediamediaमीडियाમીડિયા

Answer 3 (別冊 p.69–70, PDF 151–152; the 正解 line is faint but "3" and "loan" are legible). Book's notes (paraphrased): ローン = loan. 選択肢の言葉: 1 チャージ = charge (electricity, money) (例: some travel cards now 自動的にチャージする (jidouteki ni chaaji suru) when the balance gets low); 2 メディア = media (例: メディアが発達したおかげで (media ga hattatsu shita okage de), the world has become smaller); 4 クレジット = credit (例: online you can pay クレジットカードを使う (kurejitto kaado o tsukau) or on delivery). 【131】

不況が続く中、国内よりも海外に（　）を見出す企業が増えている。Fukyou ga tsuzuku naka, kokunai yori mo kaigai ni (___) o miidasu kigyou ga fuete iru.

Full sentence: 不況が続く中、国内よりも海外に活路を見出す企業が増えている。Fukyou ga tsuzuku naka, kokunai yori mo kaigai ni katsuro o miidasu kigyou ga fuete iru.
EN: With the recession dragging on, more and more companies are finding their way forward overseas rather than at home.
HI: लगातार मंदी के बीच ऐसी कंपनियाँ बढ़ रही हैं जो देश के बजाय विदेश में आगे बढ़ने का रास्ता ढूँढ रही हैं।
GU: સતત મંદી વચ્ચે એવી કંપનીઓ વધી રહી છે જે દેશને બદલે વિદેશમાં આગળ વધવાનો માર્ગ શોધે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1活気kakkiliveliness, energyरौनक़, जोशરોનક, ઉત્સાહ
2進展shintenprogress, developmentप्रगतिપ્રગતિ
3進路shinrocourse, path (one's future direction)आगे का रास्ता (करियर आदि)આગળનો માર્ગ (કારકિર્દી વગેરે)
4活路katsuroa way out, means of survivalबचने का रास्ताબચવાનો રસ્તો

Answer 4 (別冊 p.70, PDF 152). Book's notes (paraphrased): 活路 = way out, means of survival. 選択肢の言葉: 1 活気 = energy, liveliness (例: this has long been a key point of traffic, many people come and go, and 町は活気に満ちている (machi wa kakki ni michite iru)); 2 進展 = development, progress (例: a year has passed but その問題には何の進展もない (sono mondai ni wa nan no shinten mo nai)). The 別冊 has no note for 3 進路. (added, not in book) 不況 (a crisis) + 〜を見出す (find) → a way to survive: 活路を見出す / 活路を開く. 進路 is a direction you choose (進路指導), not an escape from difficulty.

【133】

新しく開発されたLED電球は、（　）の電球よりも消費電力がはるかに少ない。Atarashiku kaihatsu sareta LED denkyuu wa, (___) no denkyuu yori mo shouhi denryoku ga haruka ni sukunai.

EN: The newly developed LED bulbs use far less electricity than conventional bulbs.
HI: नए विकसित LED बल्ब पारंपरिक बल्बों से कहीं कम बिजली खाते हैं।

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1元来ganraioriginally, by natureमूल रूप से, शुरू सेમૂળથી, શરૂઆતથી

Answer 2 (別冊 p.70–71, PDF 152–153). The 別冊 has no note for 4 本来. (added, not in book) 新しく開発された (newly developed) vs the existing ones → 従来の電球. 元来 / 本来 are about original nature, not "the old model".

活路katsuro / 進路shinro / 活気kakki / 進展shinten way out / course, career path / liveliness / progress 活路を見出す・開く; 卒業後の進路; 活気のある町; 交渉に進展がない.
盛り上がるmoriagaru / 飛び上がるtobiagaru / 浮き上がるukiagaru / でき上がるdekiagaru liven up / jump up / float up / be completed パーティーが盛り上がる; 飛び上がって喜ぶ; 水面に浮き上がる; 料理ができ上がる.
従来juurai / 元来ganrai / 本来honrai / 往来ourai up to now / originally / by right, originally / traffic 従来の方法 (the method used so far); 元来おとなしい性格; 本来なら私が行くべきだ; 車の往来が多い.
Exam trap: 〜来 words (Q133) look alike but point in different directions in time: 従来 = from the past up to now (contrasted with something new), 元来 / 本来 = the original or proper nature of something, 往来 is not about time at all (traffic). When the sentence says 新しく〜 / 新型, look for 従来.
