1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.21–23)

Two kanji with several readings (trap type 5): 無 ム / ブ / な(い) and 茶 チャ / サ. 斡旋 あっせん has a 促音 (trap type 3), and 弊害 へいがい a long vowel へい (trap type 1). Three questions are kun-yomi verbs and words (潜る, 嘆く, 人柄). See the six trap types on 第1回.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.25)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【67】

母親の飲酒はお腹の赤ちゃんの健康に弊害を及ぼすという。Hahaoya no inshu wa onaka no akachan no kenkou ni heigai o oyobosu to iu.

EN: It is said that a mother's drinking harms the health of the baby in her womb.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.21, PDF 103). Book's notes (paraphrased): 弊害 = へいがい (harm). 弊 = ヘイ as in 疲弊; 害 = ガイ as in 害悪, 害虫, 危害, 水害, 災害, 妨害. 選択肢の言葉: 1 被害, 2 気概 / 危害, 4 障害. (added, not in book) All four end in 〜がい, so only the first kanji decides. 弊 (ヘイ) looks a little like 幣 (ヘイ, as in 紙幣) — both are へい. Collocation: 弊害を及ぼす / 弊害が生じる; 被害 is the damage someone suffers (被害を受ける).

Answer 1 (別冊 p.21–22, PDF 103–104). Book's notes (paraphrased): 無茶 = むちゃ (extreme, unreasonable). 無 = ム (無意味, 無口, 無茶苦茶 …), ブ (無愛想, 無難, 無礼), な(い); 茶 = チャ (茶の間, 茶の湯, 紅茶) and サ (茶道, 喫茶). 【69】

Underlined word: 潜るmoguru — get under, dive into

Answer 2 (別冊 p.22, PDF 104). Book's notes (paraphrased): 潜る = もぐる (dive, go under). 潜 = セン (潜水, 潜入), もぐ(る), ひそ(む) (hide, lie hidden). 選択肢の言葉: 1 勝る, 3 隠れる, 4 嵌る. (added, not in book) かくれる is the meaning trap — taking cover under a desk feels like "hiding", but 隠れる is written 隠. 潜 has two kun-yomi: もぐる (go under something / underwater) and ひそむ (lurk, be hidden). The okurigana is the same 〜む / 〜る ending pattern, so read the whole word.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.22–23, PDF 104–105). Book's notes (paraphrased): 斡旋 = あっせん (introduce, help find a job). 斡 = アツ (only in 斡旋); 旋 = セン (旋回, 旋律, 凱旋). 選択肢の言葉: 1 感染 / 観戦 / 幹線 / 艦船 / 汗腺. (added, not in book) 斡 looks like 幹 (カン, trunk) — that's the かんせん trap. アツ + セン → あっせん: the ツ becomes a small っ (促音, trap type 3). 旋 must keep its ん, so 2 and 4 fail. Common uses: 就職の斡旋, 斡旋業者 (agency).

【72】

知り合ってからまだ日が浅いので、私は彼の人柄をよく知りません。Shiriatte kara mada hi ga asai node, watashi wa kare no hitogara o yoku shirimasen.

Underlined word: 人柄hitogara — personality, character
EN: It hasn't been long since we met, so I don't know his character well.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.23, PDF 105). Book's notes (paraphrased): 人柄 = ひとがら (personality, character). 人 = ジン / ニン / ひと + exceptional readings (玄人, 素人, 仲人 …); 柄 = ヘイ (横柄), え (handle), がら (pattern). 選択肢の言葉: 1 人格, 3 人相, 4 人並み / 人波. (added, not in book) 人格 じんかく is a real word with a very close meaning, which makes it the strongest trap — but the kanji is 格, not 柄. 柄 as the second part of a compound is usually がら (人柄, 家柄, 花柄, 間柄).

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
弊害heigai vs 被害higai harmful effect vs damage suffered 弊害 = the bad side-effect of something (制度の弊害); 被害 = damage you receive (台風の被害).
無茶mucha vs 無礼・無難burei · bunan ム vs ブ 無 is ム in most words (無茶, 無理, 無口); ブ only in a small set (無礼, 無難, 無事, 無愛想).
紅茶koucha vs 茶道・喫茶sadou · kissa チャ vs サ 茶 = チャ normally (無茶, 茶の間); サ in 茶道 and 喫茶 (also read ちゃどう sometimes).
潜るmoguru vs 潜むhisomu get under, dive vs lurk, lie hidden Same kanji, different kun-yomi. 机の下に潜る / 海に潜る; 森に潜む / 危険が潜む.
嘆くnageku vs 呟くtsubuyaku lament vs mutter Both have 口. 不幸を嘆く (grieve); 独り言を呟く (mutter to oneself).
斡旋assen vs 幹線kansen arranging a job vs trunk line 斡 (アツ) and 幹 (カン) look alike. 新幹線 has 幹; 仕事の斡旋 has 斡.
人柄hitogara vs 人格jinkaku (kun + kun) vs (on + on) Similar meaning, different kanji: 人柄がいい (pleasant personality); 人格者 (a person of fine character).
Exam trap: when the options are every combination of two readings (無茶 → むちゃ / むさ / ぶさ / ぶちゃ), the question tests whether you know which reading each kanji takes in this word. For 人柄 / 人格, the trap is a real word with the same meaning — check the second kanji, not just the meaning.
