【43】

手引きtebiki

Word: (inside) help, guiding someone (especially in a crime); also: a guide, manual — 別冊: 手引き = （正解文中の意味）（犯罪など悪いことの）案内、導き (meaning in the correct sentence: guidance or leading, in something bad such as a crime)
HI: (अपराध में) अंदर से मदद, राह दिखाना
GU: (ગુનામાં) અંદરથી મદદ, રસ્તો બતાવવો
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1傷が悪化しないように、病院で手引きを受けたほうがいい。Kizu ga akka shinai you ni, byouin de tebiki o uketa hou ga ii.You should get the wound treated at hospital so it doesn't get worse.घाव न बिगड़े, इसलिए अस्पताल में इलाज करा लेना चाहिए।ઘા વકરે નહીં માટે હૉસ્પિટલમાં સારવાર લેવી જોઈએ.
2社長は会社経営からの手引きを発表した。Shachou wa kaisha keiei kara no tebiki o happyou shita.The president announced his retirement from running the company.अध्यक्ष ने कंपनी संचालन से संन्यास की घोषणा की।પ્રમુખે કંપનીના સંચાલનમાંથી નિવૃત્તિની જાહેરાત કરી.
3今回の事件は内部から手引きをした者がいるようだ。Konkai no jiken wa naibu kara tebiki o shita mono ga iru you da.In this case, it seems someone on the inside helped the criminals.लगता है इस वारदात में किसी अंदर के आदमी ने मदद की।લાગે છે આ ગુનામાં કોઈ અંદરના માણસે મદદ કરી.
4学校長の手引きで、彼を採用することにした。Gakkouchou no tebiki de, kare o saiyou suru koto ni shita.On the head teacher's recommendation, we decided to hire him.प्रधानाचार्य की सिफ़ारिश पर हमने उसे रखने का फ़ैसला किया।આચાર્યની ભલામણથી અમે તેને નોકરીએ રાખવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). Book's definition: 手引き = （正解文中の意味）（犯罪など悪いことの）案内、導き (meaning in the correct sentence: guidance or leading, in something bad such as a crime). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 手当て / 治療 (teate / chiryou) "treatment"; 2 → should be 引退 (intai) "retirement"; 4 → should be 推薦 / 紹介 (suisen / shoukai) "recommendation / introduction". (added, not in book) In the exam sense 手引き is helping criminals get in or carry out a crime (内部の手引き). It also means a handbook (利用の手引き), but never medical treatment, retirement or a job reference.

【44】

ひたむき（な）hitamuki (na)

Word: single-minded, wholehearted — 別冊: ひたむき（な） = 1つのことに向かって一生懸命で、忍耐強い (working hard and patiently toward one thing)
HI: पूरी लगन से, एकनिष्ठ
GU: પૂરી લગનથી, એકનિષ્ઠ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1彼は目的の達成に向かって、ひたむきに努力している。Kare wa mokuteki no tassei ni mukatte, hitamuki ni doryoku shite iru.He is working single-mindedly toward achieving his goal.वह अपने लक्ष्य को पाने के लिए पूरी लगन से मेहनत कर रहा है।તે પોતાનું લક્ષ્ય મેળવવા પૂરી લગનથી મહેનત કરે છે.
2今日は店にひたむきに客が入って来る。Kyou wa mise ni hitamuki ni kyaku ga haitte kuru.Customers keep coming into the shop one after another today.आज दुकान में ग्राहक लगातार आ रहे हैं।આજે દુકાનમાં ગ્રાહકો સતત આવે છે.
3そんなに暗くならないで、ひたむきに考えよう。Sonna ni kuraku naranaide, hitamuki ni kangaeyou.Don't be so gloomy – let's think positively.इतने उदास मत हो, सकारात्मक सोचो।આટલા ઉદાસ ન થાઓ, હકારાત્મક વિચારો.
4国際空港では飛行機がひたむきに発着する。Kokusai kuukou de wa hikouki ga hitamuki ni hacchaku suru.At an international airport, planes take off and land frequently.अंतरराष्ट्रीय हवाई अड्डे पर विमान बार-बार उड़ते-उतरते हैं।આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય હવાઈ મથકે વિમાનો વારંવાર ઊડે-ઊતરે છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). Book's definition: ひたむき（な） = 1つのことに向かって一生懸命で、忍耐強い (working hard and patiently toward one thing). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be ひっきりなしに (hikkirinashi ni) "non-stop, one after another"; 3 → should be 前向きに (maemuki ni) "positively"; 4 → should be 頻繁に (hinpan ni) "frequently". (added, not in book) ひたむき describes a person's devoted attitude (ひたむきな努力・情熱・姿). It is not about frequency (頻繁・ひっきりなし) and not about optimism (前向き).

【45】

うんざりするunzari suru

Word: to be fed up, sick and tired
HI: ऊब जाना, तंग आ जाना
GU: કંટાળી જવું, ત્રાસી જવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1うんざりして電車を乗り間違えて、遅刻してしまった。Unzari shite densha o norimachigaete, chikoku shite shimatta.I carelessly got on the wrong train and was late.लापरवाही में ग़लत ट्रेन पकड़ ली और देर हो गई।બેધ્યાનપણે ખોટી ટ્રેન પકડી લીધી અને મોડું થયું.
2妻はうんざりした性格で、特にお金の使い方には厳しい。Tsuma wa unzari shita seikaku de, toku ni okane no tsukaikata ni wa kibishii.My wife is very precise by nature and especially strict about how money is spent.मेरी पत्नी बहुत सलीक़ेदार स्वभाव की है, ख़ासकर पैसे ख़र्च करने में सख़्त है।મારી પત્ની ખૂબ ચોક્કસ સ્વભાવની છે, ખાસ કરીને પૈસા વાપરવામાં કડક છે.
31つレポートを提出すると、またレポートの課題が出る。もううんざりする。Hitotsu repooto o teishutsu suru to, mata repooto no kadai ga deru. Mou unzari suru.As soon as I hand in one report, another one is set. I'm sick of it.एक रिपोर्ट जमा करते ही दूसरी रिपोर्ट का काम मिल जाता है। अब तो मैं तंग आ गया हूँ।એક રિપોર્ટ જમા કરતાં જ બીજો રિપોર્ટ સોંપાય છે. હવે તો હું કંટાળી ગયો છું.
4彼はラグビーの選手だっただけあって、体がうんざりしている。Kare wa ragubii no senshu datta dake atte, karada ga unzari shite iru.As you'd expect of a former rugby player, he is solidly built.रग्बी खिलाड़ी रह चुका है, इसलिए उसका शरीर गठीला है।રગ્બીનો ખેલાડી રહી ચૂક્યો છે, તેથી તેનું શરીર કસાયેલું છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be うっかりして (ukkari shite) "carelessly"; 2 → should be きっちりした (kitchiri shita) "precise, meticulous"; 4 → should be がっしり / がっちり (gasshiri / gatchiri) "sturdily built". (added, not in book) うんざり is the feeling when something unpleasant goes on too long or happens too often (もううんざりだ・〜にはうんざりする).

【46】

見せびらかすmisebirakasu

Word: to show off, flaunt — 別冊: 見せびらかす = 自慢そうにみんなに見せる (show to everyone with pride)
HI: दिखावा करना, इतराकर दिखाना
GU: દેખાડો કરવો, ઇતરાઈને બતાવવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1彼がわざわざ車で来たのは、新しい車を見せびらかすためだった。Kare ga wazawaza kuruma de kita no wa, atarashii kuruma o misebirakasu tame datta.He came by car on purpose – to show off his new car.वह जानबूझकर कार से आया ताकि अपनी नई कार का दिखावा कर सके।તે જાણી જોઈને કારમાં આવ્યો જેથી નવી કારનો દેખાડો કરી શકે.
2本屋の店先に今月の新刊書が見せびらかされている。Hon'ya no misesaki ni kongetsu no shinkansho ga misebirakasarete iru.This month's new books are laid out at the front of the bookshop.किताबों की दुकान के आगे इस महीने की नई किताबें सजाई गई हैं।પુસ્તકની દુકાનના આગળના ભાગમાં આ મહિનાનાં નવાં પુસ્તકો ગોઠવેલાં છે.
3彼は断然トップで優勝し、力の差を見せびらかした。Kare wa danzen toppu de yuushou shi, chikara no sa o misebirakashita.He won by a huge margin, showing everyone the gap in ability.वह बहुत आगे रहकर जीता और सबको अपनी श्रेष्ठता दिखा दी।તે ઘણો આગળ રહી જીત્યો અને બધાને પોતાની શ્રેષ્ઠતા બતાવી દીધી.
4父親は子どもに英語を覚えさせようと、教育番組を見せびらかした。Chichioya wa kodomo ni eigo o oboesaseyou to, kyouiku bangumi o misebirakashita.The father showed his child educational programmes so he would learn English.पिता ने बच्चे को अंग्रेज़ी सिखाने के लिए शैक्षिक कार्यक्रम दिखाए।પિતાએ બાળકને અંગ્રેજી શીખવવા શૈક્ષણિક કાર્યક્રમો બતાવ્યા.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). Book's definition: 見せびらかす = 自慢そうにみんなに見せる (show to everyone with pride). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 並べられて (naraberarete) "are lined up"; 3 → should be 見せ付けた (misetsuketa) "displayed (forcefully)"; 4 → should be 見せた (miseta) "showed". (added, not in book) 見せびらかす always includes boasting about something one owns (新しい車・指輪・写真). Showing superiority in a contest is 見せ付ける; neutral showing is just 見せる.

【47】

てんでtende

Word: (not) at all (casual) — 別冊: てんで = 全く（〜ない）（くだけた会話で使うことが多い） (not … at all; mostly in casual conversation)
HI: बिल्कुल (नहीं) (बोलचाल)
GU: જરાય (નહીં) (બોલચાલ)
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1僕は、英語はてんで分からないんだ。Boku wa, eigo wa tende wakaranai n da.I don't understand English at all.मुझे अंग्रेज़ी बिल्कुल समझ नहीं आती।મને અંગ્રેજી જરાય સમજાતું નથી.
2彼はてんでストレスが溜まっているのか、いつも疲れた顔をしている。Kare wa tende sutoresu ga tamatte iru no ka, itsumo tsukareta kao o shite iru.Maybe he's terribly stressed – he always looks tired.शायद उस पर बहुत ज़्यादा तनाव है, वह हमेशा थका दिखता है।કદાચ તેના પર ખૂબ તણાવ છે, તે હંમેશાં થાકેલો દેખાય છે.
3主人の帰宅が今日はてんで遅い。何かあったのだろうか。Shujin no kitaku ga kyou wa tende osoi. Nanika atta no darou ka.My husband is particularly late home today. I wonder if something happened.आज पति ख़ास तौर पर देर से घर आ रहे हैं। कुछ हुआ क्या?આજે પતિ ખાસ મોડા ઘરે આવે છે. કંઈ થયું હશે?
4当時はてんで食料が不足していて、苦しい生活を強いられていた。Touji wa tende shokuryou ga fusoku shite ite, kurushii seikatsu o shiirarete ita.In those days food was terribly scarce, and people were forced to live hard lives.उस समय भोजन की भारी कमी थी और लोगों को कठिन जीवन जीना पड़ता था।તે સમયે ખોરાકની ભારે અછત હતી અને લોકોને કઠણ જીવન જીવવું પડતું.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). Book's definition: てんで = 全く（〜ない）（くだけた会話で使うことが多い） (not … at all; mostly in casual conversation). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be すごく (sugoku) "terribly, very"; 3 → should be 特に (toku ni) "especially"; 4 → should be ひどく (hidoku) "severely". (added, not in book) てんで is a negative-polarity adverb: it needs a negative (てんで分からない・てんでだめだ・てんで話にならない). Options 2–4 have no negative, so it cannot work.

【48】

満場一致manjou itchi

Word: unanimous (agreement of everyone present)
HI: सर्वसम्मति

1週末はどのレストランも満場一致なので、事前に予約したほうがいい。Shuumatsu wa dono resutoran mo manjou itchi na node, jizen ni yoyaku shita hou ga ii.Every restaurant is full at weekends, so you'd better book in advance.सप्ताहांत में हर रेस्तराँ भरा रहता है, इसलिए पहले से बुकिंग कर लें।વીકએન્ડમાં દરેક રેસ્ટોરાં ભરેલું હોય છે, તેથી અગાઉથી બુકિંગ કરો.
2衆議院に提出された法案は満場一致で可決された。Shuugiin ni teishutsu sareta houan wa manjou itchi de kaketsu sareta.The bill submitted to the House of Representatives was passed unanimously.प्रतिनिधि सभा में पेश विधेयक सर्वसम्मति से पारित हुआ।પ્રતિનિધિ ગૃહમાં રજૂ થયેલો ખરડો સર્વસંમતિથી પસાર થયો.
3この公園は春になると桜が満場一致になる。Kono kouen wa haru ni naru to sakura ga manjou itchi ni naru.In spring the cherry trees in this park come into full bloom.वसंत में इस पार्क की चेरी पूरी तरह खिल उठती है।વસંતમાં આ બગીચાની ચેરી પૂરી ખીલી ઊઠે છે.
4Aさんの試験の点数は満場一致で、みんなを驚かせた。A-san no shiken no tensuu wa manjou itchi de, minna o odorokaseta.A got full marks in the exam, which surprised everyone.A को परीक्षा में पूरे अंक मिले, जिससे सब हैरान हो गए।Aને પરીક્ષામાં પૂરા ગુણ મળ્યા, જેથી બધા ચકિત થઈ ગયા.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.93–94, PDF 175–176). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 満員 (man'in) "full (of people)"; 3 → should be 満開 (mankai) "in full bloom"; 4 → should be 満点 (manten) "full marks". (added, not in book) 満場一致 is about a vote or decision: everyone in the hall (満場) agrees (一致). It typically appears as 満場一致で可決する・決まる.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
手引きtebiki / 手当て / 治療teate / chiryou / 引退intai / 推薦 / 紹介suisen / shoukai (inside) help, guiding someone (especially in a crime); also: a guide, manual / treatment / retirement / recommendation / introduction 手引き = (criminal) inside help; guide booklet; 手当て = first aid, treatment; 引退 = retirement; 推薦 = recommendation. 手引き and 引退 share 引; 手引き and 手当て share 手.
ひたむき（な）hitamuki (na) / ひっきりなしにhikkirinashi ni / 前向きにmaemuki ni / 頻繁にhinpan ni single-minded, wholehearted / non-stop, one after another / positively / frequently ひたむき = devoted, single-minded effort; ひっきりなし = without a break (things keep coming); 頻繁 = frequent; 前向き = positive outlook. ひたむき (direct one's whole self) vs 前向き (face forward): both contain 向き.
うんざりするunzari suru / うっかりしてukkari shite / きっちりしたkitchiri shita / がっしり / がっちりgasshiri / gatchiri to be fed up, sick and tired / carelessly / precise, meticulous / sturdily built うんざり = fed up; うっかり = carelessly; きっちり = precisely, exactly; がっしり・がっちり = sturdy. Four 〜り adverbs with similar sounds (う〜り, 〜っ〜り).
見せびらかすmisebirakasu / 並べられてnaraberarete / 見せ付けたmisetsuketa / 見せたmiseta to show off, flaunt / are lined up / displayed (forcefully) / showed 見せびらかす = flaunt one's possessions; 見せ付ける = make others see (power, affection) forcefully; 見せる = show; 並べる = line up. All 見せ〜 verbs: only 見せびらかす is about possessions and pride.
てんでtende / すごくsugoku / 特にtoku ni / ひどくhidoku (not) at all (casual) / terribly, very / especially / severely てんで〜ない = not at all (casual); すごく = very (casual, positive or negative); 特に = especially; ひどく = badly, severely. Check for 〜ない/だめ after てんで.
満場一致manjou itchi / 満員man'in / 満開mankai / 満点manten unanimous (agreement of everyone present) / full (of people) / in full bloom / full marks 満場一致 = unanimous; 満員 = full of people; 満開 = full bloom; 満点 = full marks. All 満〜 = "full" — the second kanji tells you full of what.
Exam trap: watch for negative-only adverbs like てんで (〜ない): if the sentence has no negative, it is wrong. And for 満〜 / 見せ〜 / 〜り sets, the shared part tells you nothing — read the other kanji or sound.
