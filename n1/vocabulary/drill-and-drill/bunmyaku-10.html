2 Every question — 問題 (p.45)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【64】

仕事があるからと嘘をついて約束を破った私を、彼女はひどく（　）。Shigoto ga aru kara to uso o tsuite yakusoku o yabutta watashi o, kanojo wa hidoku (___).

Full sentence: 仕事があるからと嘘をついて約束を破った私を、彼女はひどくなじった。Shigoto ga aru kara to uso o tsuite yakusoku o yabutta watashi o, kanojo wa hidoku najitta.
EN: I broke our date with a lie that I had work, and she tore into me for it.
HI: मैंने काम का झूठा बहाना बनाकर वादा तोड़ा, तो उसने मुझे बुरी तरह खरी-खोटी सुनाई।
GU: મેં કામનું ખોટું બહાનું કાઢીને વચન તોડ્યું, તો તેણે મને ખૂબ ઠપકો આપ્યો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1おだてたodatetaflattered (おだてる)चापलूसी की, चने के झाड़ पर चढ़ायाખુશામત કરી, ચણાના ઝાડ પર ચડાવ્યો
2もめたmometaquarrelled, had trouble (もめる)झगड़ा हुआ, विवाद हुआઝઘડો થયો, વિવાદ થયો
3懲りたkoritalearned one's lesson (懲りる)सबक़ मिला, तौबा कर लीપાઠ મળ્યો, તોબા કરી
4なじったnajittarebuked (なじる)खरी-खोटी सुनाईઠપકો આપ્યો

Answer 4 (別冊 p.52–53, PDF 134–135). Book's notes (paraphrased): なじる = criticise, blame. 選択肢の言葉: 1 おだてる = compliment, flatter (例: anyone feels good おだてられると (odaterareru to)); 2 もめる = be in trouble, quarrel (例: those two 離婚問題でもめているらしい (rikon mondai de momete iru rashii) — the poor children); 3 懲りる = learn the hard way (例: five countries in a week wore me out; 懲りたので (korita node) next time I'll travel at an easier pace). (added, not in book) 私を〜 needs a transitive verb aimed at me, and the context (a lie, a broken promise) is negative → なじる. もめる is intransitive (〜と / 〜で もめる), and 懲りる is what I would feel.

【65】

受験生なら、わきめもふらず勉強（　）に頑張るべきだ。Jukensei nara, wakime mo furazu benkyou (___) ni ganbaru beki da.

Full sentence: 受験生なら、わきめもふらず勉強一筋に頑張るべきだ。Jukensei nara, wakime mo furazu benkyou hitosuji ni ganbaru beki da.
EN: If you're preparing for entrance exams, you should focus on nothing but studying, without looking aside.
HI: अगर तुम प्रवेश-परीक्षा की तैयारी कर रहे हो, तो इधर-उधर ध्यान दिए बिना सिर्फ़ पढ़ाई में जुटे रहना चाहिए।
GU: જો તમે પ્રવેશ-પરીક્ષાની તૈયારી કરતા હો, તો આમતેમ ધ્યાન આપ્યા વગર ફક્ત ભણવામાં જ લાગ્યા રહેવું જોઈએ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1一律ichiritsuuniformly, across the boardसबके लिए एक समानબધા માટે એકસરખું
2一頃hitokoroat one time, once (in the past)एक समय, कभीએક સમયે, ક્યારેક
3一筋hitosujisingle-minded (devotion)एक ही काम में लगनએક જ કામમાં લગન
4一連ichirena series, chain (of events)सिलसिला, कड़ीશ્રેણી, કડી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.53, PDF 135). Book's notes (paraphrased): 一筋 = for only one purpose. 選択肢の言葉: 1 一律 = treating everything the same way (例: earthquake victims received 一律35万円の見舞金 (ichiritsu sanjuugoman-en no mimaikin)); 2 一頃 = a certain time in the past (例: 一頃は (hitokoro wa) shoes with soles over 10 cm thick were in fashion); 4 一連 = a chain of related things (例: the police think 一連の放火事件 (ichiren no houka jiken) of the past two months are by the same person). (added, not in book) わきめもふらず (without looking aside) + 勉強〜に頑張る → 〜一筋 (nothing but ~). Note the readings: 一筋 / 一頃 are ひと〜, 一律 / 一連 are いち〜.

【66】

テレビ局に勤める兄は、来年度の新しいテレビ番組の企画に（　）がない。Terebikyoku ni tsutomeru ani wa, rainendo no atarashii terebi bangumi no kikaku ni (___) ga nai.

Full sentence: テレビ局に勤める兄は、来年度の新しいテレビ番組の企画に余念がない。Terebikyoku ni tsutomeru ani wa, rainendo no atarashii terebi bangumi no kikaku ni yonen ga nai.
EN: My brother, who works at a TV station, is completely absorbed in planning new programmes for next year.
HI: टीवी चैनल में काम करने वाला मेरा भाई अगले साल के नए कार्यक्रमों की योजना बनाने में पूरी तरह जुटा हुआ है।
GU: ટીવી ચૅનલમાં કામ કરતો મારો ભાઈ આવતા વર્ષના નવા કાર્યક્રમોનું આયોજન કરવામાં પૂરેપૂરો ડૂબેલો છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1余念yonen(余念がない) be absorbed in(余念がない) पूरी तरह जुटे रहना(余念がない) પૂરેપૂરા ડૂબેલા રહેવું
2余裕yoyuuroom, leeway, spare time/moneyगुंजाइश, फ़ुर्सतગુંજાઇશ, ફુરસદ
3ゆとりyutorileeway, room to spareखुलापन, फ़ुर्सतમોકળાશ, ફુરસદ
4余分yobunextra, surplusफ़ालतू, अतिरिक्तવધારાનું, ફાજલ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.53, PDF 135). Book's notes (paraphrased): 余念がない = be occupied (with nothing else on one's mind). 選択肢の言葉: 3 ゆとり = 余裕 (例: I'd like a life with 時間とお金にゆとりのある (jikan to okane ni yutori no aru) — but when?); 4 余分 = extra, not necessary (例: this is all we need; あとは余分です (ato wa yobun desu)). The 別冊 has no note for 2 余裕. (added, not in book) 余裕がない / ゆとりがない ("no time to spare") also fit the grammar — that is the trap. But 〜に余裕がない would mean the planning itself has no slack; with a hard-working person and 企画に, the fixed idiom 〜に余念がない (absorbed in) is the answer. 余念 = "other thoughts", so 余念がない = "no other thoughts".

【67】

この団体は、豪雨で被害を受けた地域を支援するために（　）を行っている。Kono dantai wa, gouu de higai o uketa chiiki o shien suru tame ni (___) o okonatte iru.

GU: આ સંસ્થા ભારે વરસાદથી અસરગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તારોને મદદ કરવા ફાળો ઉઘરાવી રહી છે.

1募金bokinfund-raisingचंदा इकट्ठा करनाફાળો ઉઘરાવવો
2集金shuukincollecting money (owed: fees, bills)बकाया पैसा वसूलनाબાકી પૈસા ઉઘરાવવા

Answer 1 (別冊 p.53, PDF 135). Book's notes (paraphrased): 募金 = fund-raising. 選択肢の言葉: 3 資金 = money needed to carry something out (例: I want to open an Indian restaurant but 資金不足で困っている (shikin busoku de komatte iru)); 4 預金 = depositing money in a bank, or that money (例: keep your 銀行の預金通帳 (ginkou no yokin tsuuchou) safe). The 別冊 has no note for 2 集金. (added, not in book) 支援するために + asking the public for money → 募金 (募 = recruit, call for). 集金 is collecting money people already owe (新聞代の集金).

【68】

1さりげないsarigenaicasual, nonchalantसहज, बिना दिखावे केસહજ, દેખાડા વગર
2ふがいないfugainaifeeble, disappointing (shameful)नाकारा, शर्मनाकનકામું, શરમજનક

Answer 3 (別冊 p.53, PDF 135). 3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
なじるnajiru / おだてるodateru / もめるmomeru / 懲りるkoriru rebuke / flatter / quarrel / learn one's lesson なじる and おだてる are opposites (blame vs flatter someone). もめる = two sides in dispute (遺産でもめる). 懲りる = you won't repeat it after a bad experience (失敗に懲りる — compare こりごり in 第9回).
一筋hitosuji / 一律ichiritsu / 一頃hitokoro / 一連ichiren single-minded / uniform / once / a series 仕事一筋; 一律に支給する; 一頃流行った; 一連の事件. Two are ひと〜, two are いち〜.
余念がないyonen ga nai vs 余裕がないyoyuu ga nai wholly absorbed vs no time/money to spare Positive vs negative: 練習に余念がない (devoted); 忙しくて余裕がない (stretched too thin). ゆとり = 余裕.
募金bokin / 集金shuukin / 資金shikin / 預金yokin donations / collecting dues / funds / savings 募金を呼びかける (ask the public); 会費を集金する (collect what's owed); 開業資金 (capital); 銀行に預金する (deposit).
たやすいtayasui / さりげないsarigenai / ふがいないfugainai / あっけないakkenai easy / casual / pathetic / over too soon たやすい = not difficult; さりげない = natural, without showing off (さりげない優しさ); ふがいない = a disappointing performance; あっけない = ended so fast it's a let-down.
さてsate / よしyoshi / まあmaa / ついtsui now then / right! / well, I suppose / without meaning to さて moves on to the next thing; よし announces a decision; まあ softens an opinion; つい = you did it before you could stop yourself.
プレゼンテーションpurezenteeshon / コンディションkondishon presentation / physical condition Same 〜ション ending. プレゼンを行う (give a talk); コンディションがいい (in good shape, of athletes or the weather).
Exam trap: fixed idioms beat dictionary meaning. 余裕 and 余念 both contain 余, and both can be followed by がない, but only 〜に余念がない means "fully absorbed in". Learn such idioms with their particle: 〜に余念がない, 〜一筋に, 〜に閉口する (第4回).
