【31】

丸ごとmarugoto

Word: whole, in its entirety — 別冊: 丸ごと = 切ったり部分に分けたりしないで、そのまま全部 (all of it as it is, without cutting or dividing)
HI: पूरा का पूरा
GU: આખેઆખું
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1他人の書いたレポートを丸ごとコピーして提出する学生が増えている。Tanin no kaita repooto o marugoto kopii shite teishutsu suru gakusei ga fuete iru.More and more students copy someone else's report in its entirety and hand it in.दूसरों की रिपोर्ट पूरी की पूरी नक़ल करके जमा करने वाले छात्र बढ़ रहे हैं।બીજાનો રિપોર્ટ આખેઆખો નકલ કરીને જમા કરનારા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ વધી રહ્યા છે.
2当社の社員食堂では100人からの昼食をたった1人の調理師が一手に引き受けている。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Tousha no shain shokudou de wa hyakunin kara no chuushoku o tatta hitori no chourishi ga itte ni hikiukete iru.In our staff canteen, a single cook single-handedly takes care of lunch for as many as 100 people.हमारी कैंटीन में अकेला एक रसोइया 100 से ज़्यादा लोगों का दोपहर का खाना अकेले सँभालता है।અમારી કેન્ટીનમાં એકલો એક રસોઇયો 100થી વધુ લોકોનું બપોરનું ભોજન એકલે હાથે સંભાળે છે.
3困難が予想されたプロジェクトだったが、今のところ万事順調に進んでいる。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Konnan ga yosou sareta purojekuto datta ga, ima no tokoro banji junchou ni susunde iru.The project was expected to be difficult, but so far everything is going smoothly.परियोजना कठिन मानी जा रही थी, पर अब तक सब कुछ ठीक चल रहा है।પ્રોજેક્ટ અઘરો મનાતો હતો, પણ અત્યાર સુધી બધું બરાબર ચાલે છે.
4野原一面にれんげの花が咲いていて、ピンク色のじゅうたんのようだった。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Nohara ichimen ni renge no hana ga saite ite, pinku-iro no juutan no you datta.Milk-vetch flowers covered the whole field, like a pink carpet.पूरे मैदान में रेंगे के फूल खिले थे, जैसे गुलाबी क़ालीन बिछा हो।આખા મેદાનમાં રેંગેનાં ફૂલ ખીલ્યાં હતાં, જાણે ગુલાબી ગાલીચો પાથર્યો હોય.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). Book's definition: 丸ごと = 切ったり部分に分けたりしないで、そのまま全部 (all of it as it is, without cutting or dividing). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → the book's corrected sentence uses 一手に (itte ni) "single-handedly"; 3 → the book's corrected sentence uses 万事 (banji) "everything, all matters"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses 一面（に） (ichimen (ni)) "all over (a surface)". (added, not in book) 丸ごと = one object taken whole, uncut and undivided (丸ごと食べる・丸ごとコピーする). Covering a whole area is 一面; doing all the work alone is 一手に; "everything" in general is 万事.

【32】

浮き彫りukibori

Word: relief carving; (fig.) bringing something into sharp relief, making it stand out — 別冊: 浮き彫り = （正解文中の意味）状況がはっきり目立つこと (meaning in the correct sentence: a situation clearly standing out)
HI: उभरी नक़्क़ाशी; (लाक्षणिक) स्पष्ट रूप से उभरना
GU: ઉપસાવેલી કોતરણી; (લાક્ષણિક) સ્પષ્ટ રીતે ઊપસી આવવું
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1新規の参入もあり、また倒産もあり、この業界は浮き沈みが激しい。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Shinki no sannyuu mo ari, mata tousan mo ari, kono gyoukai wa ukishizumi ga hageshii.With new companies coming in and others going bankrupt, this industry has big ups and downs.नए प्रवेश भी होते हैं और दिवालियापन भी, इस उद्योग में उतार-चढ़ाव बहुत हैं।નવા પ્રવેશ પણ થાય અને નાદારી પણ, આ ઉદ્યોગમાં ચડાવ-ઉતાર ઘણા છે.
2公園の中央には、馬に乗った将軍の像／彫像が立っている。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Kouen no chuuou ni wa, uma ni notta shougun no zou / chouzou ga tatte iru.In the middle of the park stands a statue of a general on horseback.पार्क के बीच घोड़े पर सवार एक सेनापति की मूर्ति खड़ी है।બગીચાની વચ્ચે ઘોડા પર બેઠેલા સેનાપતિની મૂર્તિ ઊભી છે.
3犯人の自白から事件の真相が浮き彫りにされた。Hannin no jihaku kara jiken no shinsou ga ukibori ni sareta.The criminal's confession brought the truth of the case into sharp relief.अपराधी के इक़बालिया बयान से मामले की सच्चाई साफ़ उभरकर आई।ગુનેગારની કબૂલાતથી કેસનું સત્ય સ્પષ્ટ ઊપસી આવ્યું.
4この子は泳げないので、いつも浮袋／浮輪を持ってプールに行く。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Kono ko wa oyogenai node, itsumo ukibukuro / ukiwa o motte puuru ni iku.This child can't swim, so always takes a rubber ring to the pool.यह बच्चा तैर नहीं सकता, इसलिए हमेशा तैरने वाला छल्ला लेकर पूल जाता है।આ બાળક તરી શકતું નથી, તેથી હંમેશાં તરવાનું રિંગ લઈને પૂલ જાય છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). Book's definition: 浮き彫り = （正解文中の意味）状況がはっきり目立つこと (meaning in the correct sentence: a situation clearly standing out). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → the book's corrected sentence uses 浮き沈み (ukishizumi) "ups and downs"; 2 → the book's corrected sentence uses 像 / 彫像 (zou / chouzou) "statue"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses 浮袋 / 浮輪 (ukibukuro / ukiwa) "swim ring". (added, not in book) The 別冊 notes the set phrases 浮き彫りにする / 浮き彫りになる. In exam usage it is almost always figurative: a hidden problem or truth comes clearly into view (問題・実態・真相が浮き彫りになる).

オンラインonrain

Word: online
HI: ऑनलाइन
GU: ઑનલાઇન
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1駅前に新しいデパートがオープンするらしい。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Ekimae ni atarashii depaato ga oopun suru rashii.Apparently a new department store is opening in front of the station.सुना है स्टेशन के सामने नया डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर खुलने वाला है।સાંભળ્યું છે કે સ્ટેશન સામે નવો ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર ખૂલવાનો છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → the book's corrected sentence uses オープン (oopun) "opening (of a shop)"; 2 → the book's corrected sentence uses ホット (hotto) "hot, topical"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses アンダーライン (andaarain) "underline". HI: बची हुई निशानियाँ/छाप
GU: બચેલી નિશાનીઓ/છાપ
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1この地域の町並みには江戸時代の名残が見られる。Kono chiiki no machinami ni wa Edo jidai no nagori ga mirareru.Traces of the Edo period can be seen in the streets of this area.इस इलाक़े की गलियों में एदो काल की छाप दिखती है।આ વિસ્તારની શેરીઓમાં એદો યુગની છાપ દેખાય છે.

3この店では閉店直前に売れ残りの商品が半額で購入できる。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Kono mise de wa heiten chokuzen ni urenokori no shouhin ga hangaku de kounyuu dekiru.At this shop you can buy unsold goods at half price just before closing time.इस दुकान में बंद होने से ठीक पहले बचा हुआ माल आधे दाम पर मिलता है।આ દુકાનમાં બંધ થવા પહેલાં વધેલો માલ અડધી કિંમતે મળે છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). Book's definition: 名残 = （正解文中の意味）過ぎ去ったあとにまだ影響が残っていること (meaning in the correct sentence: an influence that still remains after something has passed). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → the book's corrected sentence uses 残業 (zangyou) "overtime"; 3 → the book's corrected sentence uses 売れ残り (urenokori) "unsold goods"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses 心残り (kokoronokori) "regret, something left undone". (added, not in book) 名残 is what remains of a past era or event (江戸の名残・戦乱の名残), or the wistful feeling at parting (名残惜しい). All the substitutes share the kanji 残.

【35】

ばれるbareru

Word: to be found out, come to light (a secret or wrongdoing) — 別冊: ばれる = 悪いことが表に出る (something bad comes out into the open)
HI: पोल खुल जाना, भेद खुलना
GU: પોલ ખૂલી જવી, ભેદ ખૂલવો
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1A氏は妻に浮気がばれて離婚した。A-shi wa tsuma ni uwaki ga barete rikon shita.Mr A's wife found out about his affair, and they divorced.श्री A की पत्नी को उनके अफ़ेयर का पता चल गया और तलाक़ हो गया।શ્રી Aની પત્નીને તેમના લફરાની ખબર પડી ગઈ અને છૂટાછેડા થયા.
2壁のペンキがはげたので、塗り直した。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Kabe no penki ga hageta node, nurinaoshita.The paint on the wall had peeled off, so I repainted it.दीवार का पेंट उखड़ गया था, इसलिए दोबारा पेंट किया।દીવાલનો રંગ ઊખડી ગયો હતો, તેથી ફરી રંગ્યું.
3新学期が始まり、新たな期待に心が弾んでいる。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Shingakki ga hajimari, arata na kitai ni kokoro ga hazunde iru.The new term has begun and my heart is bounding with fresh hopes.नया सत्र शुरू हुआ है और नई उम्मीदों से मन उछल रहा है।નવું સત્ર શરૂ થયું છે અને નવી આશાઓથી મન ઊછળે છે.
4家賃の支払いが滞って、家主から催促されている。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Yachin no shiharai ga todokootte, yanushi kara saisoku sarete iru.I've fallen behind with the rent and the landlord is pressing me to pay.किराया बकाया हो गया है और मकान-मालिक तक़ाज़ा कर रहा है।ભાડું બાકી પડ્યું છે અને મકાનમાલિક ઉઘરાણી કરે છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). Book's definition: ばれる = 悪いことが表に出る (something bad comes out into the open). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → the book's corrected sentence uses はげる (hageru) "to peel off"; 3 → the book's corrected sentence uses 弾む (hazumu) "to bounce; (heart) leap"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses 滞る (todokooru) "to fall behind, be overdue". (added, not in book) ばれる is casual and is only for secrets, lies or misdeeds (うそ・浮気・カンニングがばれる). It is not a general "come off" or "stop".

【36】

有意義（な）yuuigi (na)

Word: meaningful, worthwhile — 別冊: 有意義（な） = 意味が大きい (of great significance)
HI: सार्थक, उपयोगी
GU: સાર્થક, ઉપયોગી
OptionSentence (正解 / 別冊 corrected version)EnglishHindiGujarati
1何度も練習しただけあって、彼女のスピーチは見事なできばえだった。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Nando mo renshuu shita dake atte, kanojo no supiichi wa migoto na dekibae datta.As you'd expect after so much practice, her speech was superb.इतना अभ्यास करने का नतीजा — उसका भाषण शानदार रहा।આટલી પ્રૅક્ટિસનું પરિણામ — તેનું ભાષણ શાનદાર રહ્યું.
2大学では有意義な学生生活を送りたいものだ。Daigaku de wa yuuigi na gakusei seikatsu o okuritai mono da.I really want to have a meaningful student life at university.मैं विश्वविद्यालय में सार्थक छात्र-जीवन बिताना चाहता हूँ।હું યુનિવર્સિટીમાં સાર્થક વિદ્યાર્થીજીવન જીવવા માંગું છું.
3料理好きな彼女の台所には、便利な道具がそろっている。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Ryouri-zuki na kanojo no daidokoro ni wa, benri na dougu ga sorotte iru.Her kitchen – she loves cooking – is full of handy tools.खाना बनाने की शौक़ीन उसकी रसोई में सब काम के औज़ार हैं।રસોઈની શોખીન તેના રસોડામાં બધાં કામનાં સાધનો છે.
4この保証書は5年間有効です。 (別冊 正しい例文 — original sentence not in scan)Kono hoshousho wa gonenkan yuukou desu.This warranty is valid for five years.यह गारंटी-पत्र पाँच साल तक मान्य है।આ ગૅરંટી-પત્ર પાંચ વર્ષ માન્ય છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.91–92, PDF 173–174). Book's definition: 有意義（な） = 意味が大きい (of great significance). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → the book's corrected sentence uses 見事 (migoto) "splendid, superb"; 3 → the book's corrected sentence uses 便利 (benri) "convenient, handy"; 4 → the book's corrected sentence uses 有効 (yuukou) "valid, effective". (added, not in book) 有意義 describes time, experiences and activities that are worth doing (有意義な時間・議論・経験). It does not mean "excellent", "handy" or "valid".

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
丸ごとmarugoto / 一手にitte ni / 万事banji / 一面（に）ichimen (ni) whole, in its entirety / single-handedly / everything, all matters / all over (a surface) 丸ごと = the whole thing as it is; 一面 = the entire surface (野原一面); 一手に = all by oneself; 万事 = all matters (万事順調). All translate as "all/whole" in English.
浮き彫りukibori / 浮き沈みukishizumi / 像 / 彫像zou / chouzou / 浮袋 / 浮輪ukibukuro / ukiwa relief carving; (fig.) bringing something into sharp relief, making it stand out / ups and downs / statue / swim ring 浮き彫り = (truth/problem) stands out clearly; 浮き沈み = ups and downs; 彫像 = a statue; 浮輪・浮袋 = swim ring. 浮〜 words and 彫 (carve) make all four look related.
オンラインonrain / オープンoopun / ホットhotto / アンダーラインandaarain online / opening (of a shop) / hot, topical / underline オンライン = online; オープン = open (a shop); ホット = hot (topic, drink); アンダーライン = underline. Sound-alike katakana: 〜ライン in オンライン and アンダーライン.
名残nagori / 残業zangyou / 売れ残りurenokori / 心残りkokoronokori traces, remains (of something past); lingering feeling / overtime / unsold goods / regret, something left undone 名残 = lingering trace of the past; 心残り = regret about something you couldn't do; 売れ残り = leftover stock; 残業 = overtime. All contain 残 (remain) — the meaning of the other kanji decides.
ばれるbareru / はげるhageru / 弾むhazumu / 滞るtodokooru to be found out, come to light (a secret or wrongdoing) / to peel off / to bounce; (heart) leap / to fall behind, be overdue ばれる = (secret) is exposed; はげる = (paint) peels; 弾む = bounce, (heart) leaps; 滞る = (payment, work) stalls. Four intransitive verbs; only ばれる involves a secret.
有意義（な）yuuigi (na) / 見事migoto / 便利benri / 有効yuukou meaningful, worthwhile / splendid, superb / convenient, handy / valid, effective 有意義 = worthwhile; 有効 = valid, effective (有効期限); 見事 = splendid; 便利 = convenient. 有意義 vs 有効: both begin 有〜 — meaning vs validity.
