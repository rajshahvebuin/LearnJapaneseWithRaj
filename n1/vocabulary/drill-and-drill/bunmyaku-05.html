2 Every question — 問題 (p.40)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【29】

授業を欠席した場合は、必ず（　）を出すこと。Jugyou o kesseki shita baai wa, kanarazu (___) o dasu koto.

Full sentence: 授業を欠席した場合は、必ず届けを出すこと。Jugyou o kesseki shita baai wa, kanarazu todoke o dasu koto.
EN: If you miss a class, you must hand in an absence notice.
HI: अगर कक्षा में अनुपस्थित रहे, तो सूचना-पत्र ज़रूर जमा करें।
GU: જો વર્ગમાં ગેરહાજર રહો, તો સૂચના-પત્ર જરૂર જમા કરો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1願書ganshoapplication form (for admission, permission)आवेदन-पत्र (प्रवेश/अनुमति)અરજીપત્ર (પ્રવેશ/મંજૂરી)
2申告shinkokudeclaration (tax, customs)घोषणा (कर, सीमा-शुल्क)જાહેરાત (કર, કસ્ટમ)
3届けtodokenotice, reportसूचना-पत्रસૂચના-પત્ર
4申し出moushidean offer, proposal, requestप्रस्ताव, पेशकश, अनुरोधદરખાસ્ત, ઓફર, વિનંતી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.44, PDF 126). Book's notes (paraphrased): 届け = notice, report. 選択肢の言葉: 1 願書 = a document you submit to an office or school to get permission (例: 入学願書 (nyuugaku gansho) are accepted until 1 February); 2 申告 = stating facts yourself to an office, as the law requires (例: don't forget 税金の申告 (zeikin no shinkoku)); 4 申し出 = telling an office or organisation your own opinion or request (例: 援助の申し出 (enjo no moushide) came from all over the country to the disaster area). (added, not in book) An absence is simply reported, not applied for → 欠席届 / 届けを出す. 願書 asks for permission; 申告 is a legal declaration; 申し出 is an offer.

【30】

手が空いたところで、各自で（　）食事をしてください。Te ga aita tokoro de, kakuji de (___) shokuji o shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 手が空いたところで、各自で適宜食事をしてください。Te ga aita tokoro de, kakuji de tekigi shokuji o shite kudasai.
EN: When you have a free moment, please each take your meal as suits you.
HI: जब हाथ ख़ाली हो, तो हर कोई अपनी सुविधा से खाना खा ले।
GU: જ્યારે હાથ નવરા થાય, ત્યારે દરેક જણ પોતાની સગવડે જમી લે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1適宜tekigias appropriate, at one's discretionअपनी सुविधा/विवेक सेપોતાની સગવડ/વિવેક મુજબ
2時折tokiorioccasionally, from time to timeकभी-कभीક્યારેક ક્યારેક
3軒並みnokinamiacross the board, every one of themसब के सब, हर जगहબધે જ, દરેકેદરેક
4悠然とyuuzen tocalmly, composedlyइत्मीनान से, शांत भाव सेનિરાંતે, શાંત ભાવે

Answer 1 (別冊 p.44, PDF 126). Book's notes (paraphrased): 適宜(に) = (because nothing is fixed) according to the situation / judging for yourself / suitably. 選択肢の言葉: 2 時折 = ときどき (例: it is sunny, but 時折雲が太陽を隠す (tokiori kumo ga taiyou o kakusu)); 3 軒並み = どこも全部 (例: across Japan today temperatures were 軒並み30度を超える (nokinami sanjuu-do o koeru)); 4 悠然と = calmly, unhurriedly (例: however hard things get, she 悠然としている (yuuzen to shite iru) — a big personality). (added, not in book) 各自で (each person by themselves) + 手が空いたところで (when free) = no fixed time → 適宜. 時折 would mean "eat occasionally", which makes no sense as an instruction.

【31】

その小説家の新作は100万部を超える（　）となった。Sono shousetsuka no shinsaku wa hyakuman-bu o koeru (___) to natta.

Full sentence: その小説家の新作は100万部を超えるベストセラーとなった。Sono shousetsuka no shinsaku wa hyakuman-bu o koeru besutoseraa to natta.
EN: That novelist's new book became a best-seller with over a million copies.
HI: उस उपन्यासकार की नई किताब दस लाख से ज़्यादा प्रतियों वाली बेस्टसेलर बन गई।
GU: એ નવલકથાકારનું નવું પુસ્તક દસ લાખથી વધુ નકલોવાળું બેસ્ટસેલર બની ગયું.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ベストセラーbesutoseraabest-sellerबेस्टसेलरબેસ્ટસેલર
2流通ryuutsuucirculation, distributionप्रचलन, वितरणચલણ, વિતરણ
3スターsutaastar (celebrity)सितारा, मशहूर हस्तीસિતારો, પ્રખ્યાત વ્યક્તિ
4話題wadaitopic (of conversation)चर्चा का विषयચર્ચાનો વિષય

Answer 1 (別冊 p.44–45, PDF 126–127). Book's notes (paraphrased): ベストセラー = best-seller. 選択肢の言葉: 2 流通 = circulation, being in use (例: there are six kinds of 現在流通している硬貨 (genzai ryuutsuu shite iru kouka) in Japan); 3 スター = star (例: スター (sutaa) are always being asked for autographs); 4 話題になっている / 話題の = much talked about (例: this is 最近話題になっている小説 (saikin wadai ni natte iru shousetsu)). (added, not in book) 100万部 (copies sold) is a sales figure → ベストセラー. 話題 is the near miss, but you say 話題になる / 話題の本, not 100万部を超える話題.

【32】

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1本店hontenhead shop, main branchमुख्य दुकान/शाखाમુખ્ય દુકાન/શાખા
2古参kosanold hand, veteran (person)पुराना अनुभवी (व्यक्ति)જૂનો અનુભવી (વ્યક્તિ)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.45, PDF 127). Book's notes (paraphrased): 老舗 = an old, long-established shop. 選択肢の言葉: 2 古参 = old hand, veteran (例: he is 古参の営業マン (kosan no eigyou man) and trains the young staff); 3 年配 = an age above middle age (例: grey-haired and かなり年配の方 (kanari nenpai no kata)). 3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
届けtodoke / 願書gansho / 申告shinkoku / 申し出moushide notice / application / declaration / offer Four "papers or statements to an office": 届け reports a fact (欠席届, 婚姻届); 願書 asks permission (入学願書); 申告 is required by law (税金の申告); 申し出 offers help or makes a request (援助の申し出).
適宜tekigi vs 時折tokiori at your discretion vs now and then 適宜 = no fixed rule, do as fits; 時折 = occasionally. 適宜休憩を取る (rest when needed) ≠ 時折休憩を取る (rest now and then).
軒並みnokinami vs 悠然とyuuzen to all across the board vs calmly 軒並み = every house / every one (literally "row of eaves"): 軒並み値上がり. 悠然と = unhurried, composed: 悠然と構える.
老舗shinise / 古参kosan / 年配nenpai old shop / veteran / elderly Only 老舗 is a business. 古参 = someone who has been in a group a long time; 年配 = someone of mature age (ご年配の方).
衝動的shoudouteki / 積極的sekkyokuteki / 必然的hitsuzenteki / 圧倒的attouteki impulsive / proactive / inevitable / overwhelming 衝動的に買う (no thinking); 積極的に参加する (willingly); 必然的な結果 (bound to happen); 圧倒的な勝利 (by a huge margin).
粘るnebaru / 励むhagemu / 保つtamotsu hang on / work hard at / keep 粘る = refuse to give up (最後まで粘る, コーヒー1杯で粘る); 励む = put effort into an activity (練習に励む); 保つ = keep a state (健康を保つ).
宛先atesaki vs 差出人sashidashinin addressee / destination vs sender Front of the envelope = 宛先 (宛名 = the recipient's name); back = 差出人.
Exam trap: watch for words that only describe people (古参, 年配) offered for a blank that needs a thing (老舗の旅館). Checking "person or thing?" removes two options in Q32 at once.
