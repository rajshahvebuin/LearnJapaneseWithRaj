1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.26–28)

Three kun-yomi verbs (遂げる, 臨む, 弾む) whose options are other verbs with the same ending. 挫折 ざせつ tests 促音 (trap type 3 — there is no small っ here), and 人質 ひとじち is a kun-yomi + rendaku word (trap type 2: しち → じち). See the six trap types on 第1回.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.28)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.27, PDF 109). Book's notes (paraphrased): 臨む = のぞむ (look out on, face). 臨 = リン (臨時, 臨海, 君臨), のぞ(む); 〜に臨む also = attend / face an occasion (入社式に臨む). 選択肢の言葉: 1 挑んで, 3 阻んで, 4 挟んで. Answer 1 (別冊 p.27–28, PDF 109–110; the 正解 digit is faded in the scan, but the entry's reading 「ざせつ」 matches option 1). Book's notes (paraphrased): 挫折 = ざせつ (failure, collapse, breakdown). 挫 = ザ (only 挫折 here); 折 = セツ (折角, 骨折, 右折, 左折), お(れる／る), おり (occasion). 選択肢の言葉: 4 座席. Answer 2 (別冊 p.28, PDF 110). Book's notes (paraphrased): 人質 = ひとじち (hostage). 人 = ジン / ニン / ひと (+ exceptions); 質 = シチ (質 = pawn, 質屋 = pawnshop) and シツ (悪質, 気質, 品質, 本質 …). (added, not in book) 質 = シチ originally meant "something held as a pledge" — a hostage is a "person (ひと) held as a pledge (しち)", and しち voices to じち (trap type 2). Everywhere else 質 is usually シツ (品質, 性質). 人質 was listed in the 人 block on 第11回.
