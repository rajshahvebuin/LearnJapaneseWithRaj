2 Every question — 問題 (printed pp.74–75)

次の言葉の使い方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no kotoba no tsukaikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the sentence that uses the word best. HI: दिए गए शब्द का सबसे सही प्रयोग वाला वाक्य चुनें। GU: આપેલા શબ્દનો સૌથી યોગ્ય ઉપયોગ ધરાવતું વાક્ય પસંદ કરો.

【25】

むやみにmuyami ni

Word: recklessly, excessively, without thinking — 別冊: むやみに = よく考えないで、過度に（よくない意味で使う） (without thinking, to excess; used in a bad sense)
HI: बिना सोचे, अंधाधुंध
GU: વિચાર્યા વગર, આંધળુકિયું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1夫の死後、3人の子どもを抱えて、私はむやみに働くしかなかった。Otto no shigo, sannin no kodomo o kakaete, watashi wa muyami ni hataraku shika nakatta.After my husband died, with three children to raise, I had no choice but to work flat out.पति की मृत्यु के बाद तीन बच्चों के साथ मुझे जी-तोड़ मेहनत करनी पड़ी।પતિના મૃત્યુ પછી ત્રણ બાળકો સાથે મારે તનતોડ મહેનત કરવી પડી.
2欲しいものを見つけるたび、むやみに借金を繰り返す弟のことが心配だ。Hoshii mono o mitsukeru tabi, muyami ni shakkin o kurikaesu otouto no koto ga shinpai da.I worry about my brother, who recklessly borrows money every time he sees something he wants.मुझे भाई की चिंता है, जो हर पसंद की चीज़ देखते ही अंधाधुंध क़र्ज़ ले लेता है।મને ભાઈની ચિંતા છે, જે ગમતી વસ્તુ જોતાં જ આંધળુકિયું દેવું કરે છે.
3母の手術の成功を、家族みんなでむやみに祈った。Haha no shujutsu no seikou o, kazoku minna de muyami ni inotta.The whole family prayed earnestly for Mother's operation to succeed.पूरे परिवार ने माँ के ऑपरेशन की सफलता के लिए तन-मन से प्रार्थना की।આખા પરિવારે માના ઑપરેશનની સફળતા માટે તન-મનથી પ્રાર્થના કરી.
4事故現場の処理が進むにつれて、原因がむやみに明らかになってきた。Jiko genba no shori ga susumu ni tsurete, gen'in ga muyami ni akiraka ni natte kita.As work at the accident site went on, the cause gradually became clear.दुर्घटनास्थल का काम आगे बढ़ने के साथ कारण धीरे-धीरे साफ़ होता गया।અકસ્માતસ્થળનું કામ આગળ વધતાં કારણ ધીમે ધીમે સ્પષ્ટ થતું ગયું.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). Book's definition: むやみに = よく考えないで、過度に（よくない意味で使う） (without thinking, to excess; used in a bad sense). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be がむしゃらに (gamushara ni) "desperately, flat out"; 3 → should be ひたすら (hitasura) "earnestly, single-mindedly"; 4 → should be 徐々に / 次第に (jojo ni / shidai ni) "gradually". (added, not in book) むやみに criticises the action: it is done thoughtlessly and too much (むやみに借金する・叱る・薬を飲む). Hard work out of necessity (がむしゃら) or sincere prayer (ひたすら) is not criticised.

【26】

不審fushin

Word: suspicious, questionable — 別冊: 不審 = うたがわしい・あやしい (doubtful; suspicious)
HI: संदिग्ध
GU: શંકાસ્પદ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1彼女は不審なセンスの持ち主で、着る服もちょっと変わっている。Kanojo wa fushin na sensu no mochinushi de, kiru fuku mo chotto kawatte iru.She has a very individual sense of style, and her clothes are a little unusual.उसकी रुचि अनोखी है और उसके कपड़े भी थोड़े अलग होते हैं।તેની રુચિ અનોખી છે અને તેનાં કપડાં પણ થોડાં જુદાં હોય છે.
2彼の事件当日の行動には不審な点がある。Kare no jiken toujitsu no koudou ni wa fushin na ten ga aru.There is something suspicious about what he did on the day of the incident.घटना वाले दिन उसकी हरकतों में कुछ संदिग्ध बातें हैं।ઘટનાના દિવસે તેના વર્તનમાં કંઈક શંકાસ્પદ છે.
3その学生は入学試験で不審を行って不合格になった。Sono gakusei wa nyuugaku shiken de fushin o okonatte fugoukaku ni natta.That student cheated in the entrance exam and was failed.उस छात्र ने प्रवेश परीक्षा में धोखाधड़ी की और उसे अनुत्तीर्ण कर दिया गया।તે વિદ્યાર્થીએ પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષામાં ગેરરીતિ કરી અને તેને નાપાસ કરાયો.
4発見された遺体は年齢、性別が不審です。Hakken sareta itai wa nenrei, seibetsu ga fushin desu.The age and sex of the body that was found are unknown.मिले शव की उम्र और लिंग अज्ञात हैं।મળેલા મૃતદેહની ઉંમર અને જાતિ અજ્ઞાત છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). Book's definition: 不審 = うたがわしい・あやしい (doubtful; suspicious). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 個性的 (koseiteki) "distinctive, individual"; 3 → should be 不正 (fusei) "cheating, wrongdoing"; 4 → should be 不詳 (fushou) "unknown (details)". (added, not in book) 不審 means "arousing suspicion": 不審な人物・物・点, 挙動不審. Cheating itself is 不正 (不正を行う); unknown details are 不詳 (年齢不詳).

【27】

お詫びowabi

Word: an apology — 別冊: お詫び = 謝ること (apologising)
HI: माफ़ी, क्षमा-याचना
GU: માફી, ક્ષમાયાચના
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1お世話になった先生へのお詫びの品を買った。Osewa ni natta sensei e no owabi no shina o katta.I bought a thank-you gift for the teacher who had helped me.मदद करने वाले शिक्षक के लिए मैंने धन्यवाद का उपहार ख़रीदा।મદદ કરનાર શિક્ષક માટે મેં આભારની ભેટ ખરીદી.
2お詫びのこもった贈り物をいただいた。Owabi no komotta okurimono o itadaita.I received a heartfelt present.मुझे दिल से दिया गया उपहार मिला।મને દિલથી આપેલી ભેટ મળી.
3彼はとてもお詫び正しい好青年だ。Kare wa totemo owabi tadashii kouseinen da.He is a very polite, pleasant young man.वह बहुत शिष्ट और भला नौजवान है।તે ખૂબ વિનયી અને સારો યુવાન છે.
4この度は大変なご迷惑をおかけいたしました。深くお詫び申し上げます。Kono tabi wa taihen na gomeiwaku o okake itashimashita. Fukaku owabi moushiagemasu.We have caused you serious inconvenience on this occasion. We offer our sincere apologies.इस बार हमने आपको बड़ी असुविधा दी है। हम हृदय से क्षमा माँगते हैं।આ વખતે અમે આપને મોટી અસુવિધા આપી છે. અમે હૃદયથી માફી માગીએ છીએ.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). Book's definition: お詫び = 謝ること (apologising). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be お礼 (orei) "thanks, a thank-you gift"; 2 → should be 心 (kokoro) "heart, feeling (心のこもった)"; 3 → should be 礼儀 (reigi) "manners (礼儀正しい)". (added, not in book) お詫び is only for apologising (お詫び申し上げます・お詫びの品・お詫びに行く). Thanks are お礼; good manners are 礼儀.

【28】

とぼけるtobokeru

Word: to play dumb, feign ignorance — 別冊: とぼける = 知らない振りをする (pretend not to know)
HI: अनजान बनना, भोला बनना
GU: અજાણ્યા બનવું, ભોળા બનવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1この写真は少しとぼけているね。ピントが合っていない。Kono shashin wa sukoshi tobokete iru ne. Pinto ga atte inai.This photo is a bit blurred – it's out of focus.यह फ़ोटो थोड़ा धुंधला है, फ़ोकस ठीक नहीं है।આ ફોટો થોડો ઝાંખો છે, ફોકસ બરાબર નથી.
2目が悪くなったのだろうか。最近細かい字がとぼけてよく見えない。Me ga waruku natta no darou ka. Saikin komakai ji ga tobokete yoku mienai.Maybe my eyes have got worse – lately small print looks blurry and I can't read it well.शायद मेरी आँखें कमज़ोर हो गई हैं, आजकल छोटे अक्षर धुंधले दिखते हैं।કદાચ મારી આંખો નબળી પડી છે, આજકાલ ઝીણા અક્ષર ઝાંખા દેખાય છે.
3どっちへ行ったらいいのだろう。どうやら道にとぼけてしまったようだ。Dotchi e ittara ii no darou. Douyara michi ni tobokete shimatta you da.Which way should I go? It seems I've lost my way.किधर जाऊँ? लगता है मैं रास्ता भटक गया हूँ।કઈ બાજુ જાઉં? લાગે છે કે હું રસ્તો ભૂલી ગયો છું.
4父は、自分の立場が悪くなると、とぼける。Chichi wa, jibun no tachiba ga waruku naru to, tobokeru.Whenever things start going against him, my father plays dumb.जब पिताजी की स्थिति बिगड़ने लगती है, तो वे अनजान बन जाते हैं।પિતાજીની સ્થિતિ બગડવા લાગે ત્યારે તેઓ અજાણ્યા બની જાય છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). Book's definition: とぼける = 知らない振りをする (pretend not to know). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be ぼけている (bokete iru) "is blurred"; 2 → should be かすんで / ぼけて (kasunde / bokete) "look hazy / blurred"; 3 → should be 迷って (mayotte) "get lost". (added, not in book) とぼける is a deliberate act by a person. ぼける (one sound shorter) is what images and eyesight do: 写真がぼける, 字がかすむ.

【29】

見ごたえmigotae

Word: being worth seeing, impressive to watch
HI: देखने लायक़ होना
GU: જોવા જેવું હોવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1昨日のパーティーには、何人か見ごたえのある顔があった。Kinou no paatii ni wa, nannin ka migotae no aru kao ga atta.At yesterday's party there were a few faces I recognised.कल की पार्टी में कुछ जाने-पहचाने चेहरे थे।ગઈ કાલની પાર્ટીમાં થોડા જાણીતા ચહેરા હતા.
2店内の内装を変えて、白い壁にしたほうが見ごたえがいい。Tennai no naisou o kaete, shiroi kabe ni shita hou ga migotae ga ii.It would look better if we changed the interior and made the walls white.दुकान की भीतरी सजावट बदलकर दीवारें सफ़ेद करें तो ज़्यादा अच्छा दिखेगा।દુકાનની અંદરની સજાવટ બદલી દીવાલો સફેદ કરીએ તો વધુ સારું દેખાશે.
3これは自然の脅威を教えてくれる、見ごたえのある映画です。Kore wa shizen no kyoui o oshiete kureru, migotae no aru eiga desu.This is a film well worth watching that shows us the threat of nature.यह प्रकृति के ख़तरे को दिखाने वाली, देखने लायक़ फ़िल्म है।આ કુદરતના જોખમને બતાવતી, જોવા જેવી ફિલ્મ છે.
4今日のランチは洋子が作ってくれる。彼女の腕の見ごたえだ。Kyou no ranchi wa Youko ga tsukutte kureru. Kanojo no ude no migotae da.Yoko is making today's lunch – this is her chance to show what she can do.आज का लंच योको बनाएगी — यह उसका हुनर दिखाने का मौक़ा है।આજનું લંચ યોકો બનાવશે — આ તેનું કૌશલ્ય બતાવવાનો મોકો છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 見覚え (mioboe) "recognition (見覚えのある顔)"; 2 → should be 見栄え (mibae) "good appearance (見栄えがいい)"; 4 → should be 見せどころ (misedokoro) "the moment to show off (腕の見せどころ)". (added, not in book) 見ごたえ = 見る + 手応え: a film, game or exhibition gives you something solid to watch. 参考 in the 別冊: 見ごたえがある / ない.

【30】

ケースkeesu

Word: case, instance (= 場合) — 別冊: ケース = （正解文中の意味）場合 (meaning in the correct sentence: case, situation)
HI: मामला, स्थिति
GU: કિસ્સો, પરિસ્થિતિ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1新居の庭には、野菜が少し作れるぐらいのケースが欲しい。Shinkyo no niwa ni wa, yasai ga sukoshi tsukureru gurai no keesu ga hoshii.In the garden of our new home I'd like enough space to grow a few vegetables.नए घर के बगीचे में थोड़ी सब्ज़ियाँ उगाने लायक़ जगह चाहिए।નવા ઘરના બગીચામાં થોડી શાકભાજી ઉગાડી શકાય તેટલી જગ્યા જોઈએ.
2仕事でたまったケースの解消法はカラオケで歌うことです。Shigoto de tamatta keesu no kaishouhou wa karaoke de utau koto desu.My way of relieving the stress that builds up at work is singing karaoke.काम से जमा तनाव दूर करने का मेरा तरीक़ा कराओके गाना है।કામથી ભેગો થયેલો તણાવ દૂર કરવાની મારી રીત કરાઓકે ગાવાની છે.
3映画は、どんなケースのものが好きですか。Eiga wa, donna keesu no mono ga suki desu ka.What genre of film do you like?आपको किस शैली की फ़िल्में पसंद हैं?તમને કઈ શૈલીની ફિલ્મો ગમે છે?
4この薬を飲んでも、効果が出ないケースもあります。Kono kusuri o nonde mo, kouka ga denai keesu mo arimasu.There are also cases where this medicine has no effect.कुछ मामलों में यह दवा लेने पर भी असर नहीं होता।કેટલાક કિસ્સામાં આ દવા લેવા છતાં અસર થતી નથી.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.90–91, PDF 172–173). Book's definition: ケース = （正解文中の意味）場合 (meaning in the correct sentence: case, situation). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be スペース (supeesu) "space, room"; 2 → should be ストレス (sutoresu) "stress"; 3 → should be ジャンル (janru) "genre". (added, not in book) ケース has two meanings in Japanese: a box/container (めがねケース) and a case/instance (= 場合). Here the 別冊 tests the second. It never means "space" or "genre".

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
むやみにmuyami ni / がむしゃらにgamushara ni / ひたすらhitasura / 徐々に / 次第にjojo ni / shidai ni recklessly, excessively, without thinking / desperately, flat out / earnestly, single-mindedly / gradually むやみに = recklessly (negative); がむしゃらに = flat out, regardless of everything; ひたすら = earnestly, nothing but; 徐々に・次第に = gradually. Three "intensity" adverbs: only むやみに blames.
不審fushin / 個性的koseiteki / 不正fusei / 不詳fushou suspicious, questionable / distinctive, individual / cheating, wrongdoing / unknown (details) 不審 = suspicious; 不正 = dishonest act; 不詳 = not known (年齢不詳); 個性的 = individual, distinctive. 不〜 words: check the second kanji — 審 (examine), 正 (right), 詳 (detail).
お詫びowabi / お礼orei / 心kokoro / 礼儀reigi an apology / thanks, a thank-you gift / heart, feeling (心のこもった) / manners (礼儀正しい) お詫び = apology; お礼 = thanks; 礼儀 = manners, courtesy; 心のこもった = heartfelt. お詫び and お礼 are both gifts/visits in Japanese culture — the reason (sorry vs thank you) decides.
とぼけるtobokeru / ぼけているbokete iru / かすんで / ぼけてkasunde / bokete / 迷ってmayotte to play dumb, feign ignorance / is blurred / look hazy / blurred / get lost とぼける = pretend not to know; ぼける = be blurred / become senile; かすむ = look hazy; 迷う = lose one's way. A one-mora trap: と+ぼける.
見ごたえmigotae / 見覚えmioboe / 見栄えmibae / 見せどころmisedokoro being worth seeing, impressive to watch / recognition (見覚えのある顔) / good appearance (見栄えがいい) / the moment to show off (腕の見せどころ) 見ごたえ = worth watching; 見覚え = (I've) seen it before; 見栄え = looks attractive; 見せどころ = the highlight to show off (腕の見せどころ). Four 見〜 nouns — each with its own fixed phrase.
ケースkeesu / スペースsupeesu / ストレスsutoresu / ジャンルjanru case, instance (= 場合) / space, room / stress / genre ケース = case, instance (〜ケースもある); スペース = space; ストレス = stress (ストレスがたまる); ジャンル = genre. Katakana words ending in 〜ス look alike in the question.
Exam trap: two sound-alike traps here: とぼける (play dumb) vs ぼける (be blurred), and 見ごたえ vs 見覚え / 見栄え. Also remember the book's own note that katakana words keep their Japanese usage: ケース here = 場合 ("case"), not a container.
