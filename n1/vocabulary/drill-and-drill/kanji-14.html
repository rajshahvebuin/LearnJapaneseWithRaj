1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.25–26)

Four of the six questions are kun-yomi (兆し, 狙う, 粘る, 拭く), where the options are other real verbs/nouns with the same ending. 欠陥 けっかん tests the 促音 (trap type 3: ケツ + カン → けっかん), and 名残 なごり is an irregular reading (trap type 6). See the six trap types on 第1回.

【79】

入院中の妻の容体に回復の兆しが見えてきた。Nyuuinchuu no tsuma no youdai ni kaifuku no kizashi ga miete kita.

Underlined word: 兆しkizashi — sign, indication
EN: Signs of recovery have begun to appear in the condition of my wife, who is in hospital.
HI: अस्पताल में भर्ती मेरी पत्नी की हालत में सुधार के संकेत दिखने लगे हैं।
GU: હૉસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ મારી પત્નીની હાલતમાં સુધારાનાં એંધાણ દેખાવા લાગ્યાં છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1きざしkizashisign (兆し)संकेत, आसारએંધાણ, અણસાર
2ひざしhizashisunlight (日差し)धूपતડકો
3ほどこしhodokoshicharity, alms (施し)दानદાન
4まなざしmanazashigaze, look (眼差し)निगाह, दृष्टिનજર, દૃષ્ટિ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.25, PDF 107). Book's notes (paraphrased): 兆し = きざし (sign). 兆 = チョウ (兆候, 前兆, 予兆), きざ(し). 選択肢の言葉: 2 日差し, 3 施し, 4 眼差し. (added, not in book) All four end in 〜し, and three end in 〜ざし, so the ending tells you nothing. The on-yomi words help: 兆候 / 前兆 = sign, omen — the kun-yomi noun 兆し has the same meaning. Collocations: 回復の兆し, 春の兆し, 景気回復の兆しが見える.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.25, PDF 107). Book's notes (paraphrased): 狙う = ねらう (aim at, watch for). 狙 = ソ (狙撃), ねら(う). 選択肢の言葉: 1 計って / 測って / 図って / 量って / 諮って / 謀って, 2 誘って, 4 伴って. 【81】

コーヒー1杯で6時間も粘るなんて、ずうずうしい客だ。Koohii ippai de rokujikan mo nebaru nante, zuuzuushii kyaku da.

Underlined word: 粘るnebaru — stay on and on, hold out; be sticky
EN: Staying for six hours on one cup of coffee — what a cheeky customer.
HI: एक कप कॉफ़ी पर छह घंटे जमे रहना — कितना बेशर्म ग्राहक है!
GU: એક કપ કૉફી પર છ કલાક બેસી રહેવું — કેટલો નફ્ફટ ગ્રાહક!
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1ねばるnebarustick it out, linger (粘る)डटे रहना, जमे रहनाઅડગ રહેવું, બેસી રહેવું
2のこるnokoruremain (残る)बचे रहनाબાકી રહેવું

4とどまるtodomarustay, remain (留まる)ठहरनाરોકાવું

Answer 1 (別冊 p.25–26, PDF 107–108). Book's notes (paraphrased): 粘る = ねばる (stay a long time, stick, be sticky; persevere). 粘 = ネン (粘着, 粘液, 粘土, 粘膜), ねば(る); 粘り気 = stickiness. 選択肢の言葉: 2 残る, 3 湿る / 占める / 閉める / 締める, 4 留まる. (added, not in book) のこる and とどまる are meaning traps — "staying" fits the sentence — but 粘 is always ねば-. The core image is "sticky", extended to people who won't give up or won't leave: 最後まで粘る (hang in until the end).

【82】

Answer 4 (別冊 p.26, PDF 108). Book's notes (paraphrased): 名残 = なごり (remains, traces). 名 = メイ (名産, 名簿, 名誉), ミョウ (本名), な (宛名, あだ名); 残 = ザン (残金, 残酷), のこ(る／す). The 別冊 marks なごり as an exceptional reading (⚠). (added, not in book) Trap type 6: 残 is never ごり anywhere else, so it must be memorised as a whole word. Common phrases: 名残惜しい (sad to part), 夏の名残 (the last traces of summer). 1 めいざん comes from the normal on-yomi.
