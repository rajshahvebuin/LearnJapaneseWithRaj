Answer 2 (別冊 p.35–36, PDF 117–118). Book's notes (paraphrased): 合鍵 = あいかぎ (spare key, duplicate key). 合 = ゴウ (合意, 合成, 合流), ガッ (合唱, 合致), あ(う); 鍵 = かぎ (key). (added, not in book) All kun-yomi: 合う's stem あい + かぎ (compare 合図 あいず, 試合 しあい). 1 keeps the dictionary form あう, which never appears inside a compound. 3/4 use the on-yomi ゴウ and a made-up ケン for 鍵 (鍵 does have the on-yomi ケン, as in 鍵盤 けんばん, but not in this word).

Answer 4 (別冊 p.37, PDF 119). Book's notes (paraphrased): 脅かす = おびやかす (threaten, damage). 脅 = キョウ (脅迫, 脅威), おど(す／かす), おびや(かす). 選択肢の言葉: 1 散らかす, 2 驚かす, 3 冷やかす. (added, not in book) 脅かす can be read two ways: おどかす (scare, threaten a person) and おびやかす (endanger something abstract — 安全・生活・平和を脅かす). おどかす is not among the options, but おどろかす (surprise) is, as a near-miss. Here the object is 安全, so おびやかす.

【120】

彼女は抜群の記憶力の持ち主だ。Kanojo wa batsugun no kiokuryoku no mochinushi da.

Underlined word: 抜群batsugun — outstanding, exceptional
EN: She has an outstanding memory.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.37, PDF 119). Book's notes (paraphrased): 抜群 = ばつぐん (excellent, extraordinary). 抜 = バツ (抜歯, 抜粋, 海抜, 奇抜, 選抜), ぬ(ける／く／かす); 群 = グン (群集, 大群, 症候群), む(れ／れる), むら(がる). (added, not in book) Literally "pulled out (抜) of the crowd (群)". The 別冊 gives no notes on the wrong options; ゆう〜 and 〜よう are look-alike traps (群 resembles 羊 よう, 抜 resembles 友 ゆう). No 促音: バツ + グ stays ばつぐん, since グ is voiced. 抜群に上手い = exceptionally good.
