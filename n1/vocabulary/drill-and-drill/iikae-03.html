1 Points — the words tested in this round

The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.75–76)

2 Every question — 問題 (p.58)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【13】

彼は仕事の段取りがいい。Kare wa shigoto no dandori ga ii.

Underlined word: 段取り = 事を行う順序や方法を決めること (deciding the order and method of doing something).
EN: He is good at organising his work.
HI: वह काम की योजना अच्छी तरह बनाता है।
GU: તે કામની ગોઠવણ સારી રીતે કરે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1手順の整え方tejun no totonoekatathe way he sets up the stepsकाम के क्रम को व्यवस्थित करने का तरीक़ाકામના ક્રમને ગોઠવવાની રીત
2見通しのつけ方mitooshi no tsukekatathe way he forecasts / sizes things upअनुमान लगाने का तरीक़ाઅંદાજ બાંધવાની રીત
3成果の出し方seika no dashikatathe way he produces resultsनतीजे निकालने का तरीक़ाપરિણામ લાવવાની રીત
4手段の選び方shudan no erabikatathe way he chooses meansसाधन चुनने का तरीक़ाસાધન પસંદ કરવાની રીત

Answer 1 (別冊 p.75, PDF 157). Book's notes (paraphrased): 段取り = deciding the order or method (= 手順) of doing a job. 選択肢の言葉: 2 見通し = prospect, outlook (例: いつになったら好況が取り戻せるのだろうか。景気回復の見通しがつかない (itsu ni nattara koukyou ga torimodoseru no darou ka. Keiki kaifuku no mitooshi ga tsukanai)); 3 成果 = a good result (例: あの人は、会社で成果を出して出世することばかり考えているようだ (ano hito wa, kaisha de seika o dashite shusse suru koto bakari kangaete iru you da)). The 別冊 has no note for 4 手段. (added, not in book) 段 = step, 取り = arranging: 段取り is literally "arranging the steps" — 手順 is the 別冊's own synonym.

【14】

君にもらった地図は、古くて、てんで役に立たないよ。Kimi ni moratta chizu wa, furukute, tende yaku ni tatanai yo.

Underlined word: てんで(〜ない) = まったく〜ない (not at all; colloquial).
EN: The map you gave me is old and no use at all.
HI: तुमने जो नक़्शा दिया था वह पुराना है और बिल्कुल भी काम का नहीं।
GU: તેં આપેલો નકશો જૂનો છે અને જરાય કામનો નથી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ろくにroku ni(not) properly, (not) enoughठीक से (नहीं)બરાબર (નહીં)
2かすかにkasuka nifaintly, slightlyहल्का-सा, धुँधला-साઆછું, ઝાંખું
3てっきりtekkiri(I was) sure that… (but was wrong)पक्का (समझ बैठना) — पर ग़लतચોક્કસ (માની લેવું) — પણ ખોટું
4まるっきりmarukkiricompletely, utterly (not)बिल्कुल, पूरी तरह (नहीं)બિલકુલ, સાવ (નહીં)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.75–76, PDF 157–158). Book's notes (paraphrased): てんで〜ない = まるっきり〜ない, "not at all", common in casual speech. 選択肢の言葉: 1 ろくに〜ない = not sufficiently (例: サイズをろくに見ないでシャツを買ったら、子ども用だった (saizu o roku ni minaide shatsu o kattara, kodomo-you datta)); 2 かすかに = (feel) just a little (例: 水平線にかすかに島が見えるでしょう。あれが大島です (suiheisen ni kasuka ni shima ga mieru deshou. Are ga Ooshima desu)); 3 てっきり = (think) without doubt, though it is actually not so (例: だれもいないので、てっきりみんな先に帰ったと思った (dare mo inai no de, tekkiri minna saki ni kaetta to omotta)). (added, not in book) ろくに is also a negative-polarity adverb, but it means "not adequately" (partial), while てんで／まるっきり is total negation.

【15】

彼女はこのごろいつも私にそっけない態度を見せる。Kanojo wa kono goro itsumo watashi ni sokkenai taido o miseru.

Underlined word: そっけない = 興味も関心もない (showing neither interest nor concern).
EN: These days she always treats me coldly.
HI: आजकल वह मेरे साथ हमेशा रूखा व्यवहार करती है।
GU: હમણાં તે મારી સાથે હંમેશાં રુક્ષ વર્તન કરે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1冷淡なreitan nacold, indifferentरूखा, उदासीनઠંડું, ઉદાસીન
2あやふやなayafuya navague, uncertainअस्पष्ट, डाँवाँडोलઅસ્પષ્ટ, અચોક્કસ
3いいかげんなiikagen nasloppy, irresponsibleलापरवाह, गैर-ज़िम्मेदारબેદરકાર, બેજવાબદાર
4生意気なnamaiki nacheeky, impertinentगुस्ताख़, ढीठઉદ્ધત, બેઅદબ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.76, PDF 158). Book's notes (paraphrased): そっけない = 冷淡な, having no interest or concern. 選択肢の言葉: 2 あやふやな = unclear whether true and unreliable (例: 記憶があやふやで、はっきり思い出せない (kioku ga ayafuya de, hakkiri omoidasenai)); 3 いいかげんな = not certain and irresponsible (例: また計算が違っている。こんないいかげんな報告書を書いたのはだれだ (mata keisan ga chigatte iru. Konna iikagen na houkokusho o kaita no wa dare da)). The 別冊 has no note for 4 生意気な. (added, not in book) そっけない態度 is cold but not rude: 生意気 (cheeky) adds disrespect that the word does not carry.

【16】

今年は車の修理の費用がかさんだ。Kotoshi wa kuruma no shuuri no hiyou ga kasanda.

Underlined word: かさむ = (数量が)大きくなる／多くなる (to grow in amount).
EN: This year the cost of car repairs has mounted up.
HI: इस साल गाड़ी की मरम्मत का ख़र्च बढ़ गया।
GU: આ વર્ષે ગાડીના સમારકામનો ખર્ચ વધી ગયો.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1減ったhettadecreasedघट गयाઘટી ગયો
2節約できたsetsuyaku dekitacould be savedबचत हो सकीબચત થઈ શકી
3支払われたshiharawaretawas paidचुकाया गयाચૂકવાયો
4増えたfuetaincreasedबढ़ गयाવધી ગયો

Answer 4 (別冊 p.76, PDF 158). Book's notes (paraphrased): かさむ = (of a quantity) to get bigger, to increase. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) かさむ shares its root with かさばる (第1回 Q3): 嵩 = bulk/volume. かさばる is physical bulk; かさむ is for costs and debts piling up (費用・出費・借金がかさむ). 1 and 2 are the opposite direction; 3 says nothing about amount.

【17】

図書館で本を探すときは、まずジャンルで検索するのがよい。Toshokan de hon o sagasu toki wa, mazu janru de kensaku suru no ga yoi.

Underlined word: ジャンル = 種類 (category — especially of works classified by style).
EN: When looking for a book in the library, it is best to search by genre first.
HI: पुस्तकालय में किताब ढूँढ़ते समय पहले विधा (श्रेणी) से खोजना अच्छा रहता है।
GU: પુસ્તકાલયમાં પુસ્તક શોધતી વખતે પહેલાં પ્રકાર (શ્રેણી) પ્રમાણે શોધવું સારું.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1種類shuruikind, categoryप्रकार, श्रेणीપ્રકાર, શ્રેણી
2題名daimeititleशीर्षकશીર્ષક
3出版社shuppanshapublisherप्रकाशकપ્રકાશક
4著者choshaauthorलेखकલેખક

Answer 1 (別冊 p.76, PDF 158). Book's notes (paraphrased): ジャンル = 種類, in particular the categories into which literary and other works are divided by style. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 2–4 are all real library search fields — the trap is that they are plausible, not that they are near in meaning. Only 種類 is a classification.

【18】

彼女に対する私の思いは募るばかりだ。Kanojo ni taisuru watashi no omoi wa tsunoru bakari da.

Underlined word: 募る = (here) (感情などが)強くなる (of a feeling, to grow stronger).
EN: My feelings for her only keep growing stronger.
HI: उसके प्रति मेरी भावनाएँ बस तीव्र होती जा रही हैं।
GU: તેના પ્રત્યેની મારી લાગણીઓ બસ વધુ ને વધુ તીવ્ર થતી જાય છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1片寄るkatayoruto lean to one side, be biasedएक तरफ़ झुकना, पक्षपाती होनाએક બાજુ ઢળવું, પક્ષપાતી થવું
2強まるtsuyomaruto grow strongerतीव्र होना, बढ़नाતીવ્ર થવું, વધવું
3逆らうsakarauto go against, defyविरोध करना, उलटा चलनाવિરોધ કરવો, સામે થવું
4長引くnagabikuto drag onलंबा खिंचनाલંબાવું, ખેંચાવું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.76, PDF 158). Book's notes (paraphrased): 募る in this sentence = (a feeling) grows stronger. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 思いが募る・不安が募る・恋しさが募る are set phrases for intensifying emotion. 長引く is the near-miss — it is about duration (a long-lasting feeling), not intensity.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
段取りdandori / 手順tejun / 手段shudan / 見通しmitooshi planning the steps / procedure / means / outlook 段取り = planning the order of steps (段取りを決める); 手順 = the steps themselves; 手段 = the means chosen (手段を選ばない); 見通し = a forecast of how things will go (見通しが立たない, 第10回 Q55).
てんでtende / まるっきりmarukkiri / ろくにroku ni / てっきりtekkiri not at all / completely (not) / not properly / "I was sure" てんで・まるっきり・まったく = total negation; ろくに〜ない = insufficient (ろくに寝ていない); てっきり is not a negator at all — it marks a confident wrong assumption (てっきり〜と思った).
そっけないsokkenai / 冷淡なreitan na / あやふやなayafuya na / いいかげんなiikagen na curt / cold / vague / sloppy そっけない返事 = short and cold (冷淡); あやふやな返事 = evasive, non-committal; いいかげんな返事 = careless (also 第7回 Q38). All three can describe a reply — the attitude behind each differs.
かさむkasamu / かさばるkasabaru / 増えるfueru (costs) mount / (objects) are bulky / to increase Same root 嵩. 費用・借金がかさむ (money) vs 荷物がかさばる (things). 増える is the neutral paraphrase for both numbers and amounts.
ジャンルjanru / 種類shurui / 題名daimei genre / kind / title ジャンル is 種類 for creative works (音楽・映画・小説のジャンル); 題名 names one work (第1回 Q6).
募るtsunoru / 強まるtsuyomaru / 長引くnagabiku / 募集するboshuu suru (feeling) intensifies / strengthens / drags on / recruits Intransitive 募る (不安が募る) = 強まる. Transitive 募る (参加者を募る) = 募集する "recruit" — same kanji, different sense. 長引く is about time, not strength.
Exam trap: Q14 lines up four adverbs that all look like negative intensifiers. Only two take 〜ない: てんで／まるっきり (total) and ろくに (insufficient). かすかに is positive ("faintly visible"), and てっきり goes with 〜と思った. Learn each adverb together with the ending it requires.
