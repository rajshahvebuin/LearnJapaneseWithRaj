【37】

斡旋するassen suru

Word: to arrange, find (a job, lodging) for someone as a go-between
HI: बीच में पड़कर (नौकरी, घर) दिलवाना
GU: વચ્ચે રહી (નોકરી, રહેઠાણ) અપાવવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1私の姉は、留学生に住まいやホームステイ先などを斡旋する仕事をしている。Watashi no ane wa, ryuugakusei ni sumai ya hoomusutei-saki nado o assen suru shigoto o shite iru.My sister's job is finding housing and homestay families for international students.मेरी बहन का काम विदेशी छात्रों के लिए घर और होमस्टे की व्यवस्था कराना है।મારી બહેનનું કામ વિદેશી વિદ્યાર્થીઓ માટે રહેઠાણ અને હોમસ્ટેની વ્યવસ્થા કરાવવાનું છે.
2親友のAは私が斡旋した男性と付き合い始めたらしい。Shin'yuu no A wa watashi ga assen shita dansei to tsukiaihajimeta rashii.Apparently my best friend A has started going out with the man I introduced her to.लगता है मेरी पक्की सहेली A उस आदमी के साथ घूमने लगी है जिससे मैंने उसका परिचय कराया था।લાગે છે મારી ખાસ બહેનપણી A એ પુરુષ સાથે ફરવા લાગી છે જેનો મેં પરિચય કરાવ્યો હતો.
3あなたが斡旋してくれなかったら、この計画は実現しなかっただろう。Anata ga assen shite kurenakattara, kono keikaku wa jitsugen shinakatta darou.If you hadn't cooperated, this plan would never have come about.अगर तुमने सहयोग न किया होता, तो यह योजना साकार न होती।તમે સહકાર ન આપ્યો હોત તો આ યોજના સાકાર ન થાત.
4少しでも社会に斡旋したいと思い、ボランティア活動に参加した。Sukoshi demo shakai ni assen shitai to omoi, borantia katsudou ni sanka shita.Wanting to contribute to society even a little, I joined volunteer activities.समाज में थोड़ा भी योगदान देने की चाह से मैं स्वयंसेवा में शामिल हुआ।સમાજમાં થોડું પણ યોગદાન આપવાની ઇચ્છાથી હું સ્વયંસેવામાં જોડાયો.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 紹介した (shoukai shita) "introduced"; 3 → should be 協力して (kyouryoku shite) "cooperated"; 4 → should be 貢献したい (kouken shitai) "want to contribute". (added, not in book) 斡旋 is mediation that gets someone a concrete thing — a job, housing, a deal (就職の斡旋・住宅を斡旋する). Introducing two people is 紹介; helping is 協力; giving to society is 貢献.

【38】

まちまちmachimachi

Word: varied, differing from one to another — 別冊: まちまち = いろいろ (various)
HI: अलग-अलग, भिन्न-भिन्न
GU: જુદું-જુદું, ભિન્ન-ભિન્ન
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1厚いセーターにビーチサンダルなんて、彼のファッションはかなりまちまちだ。Atsui seetaa ni biichi sandaru nante, kare no fasshon wa kanari machimachi da.A thick sweater with beach sandals – his outfit is pretty mismatched.मोटा स्वेटर और बीच-चप्पल — उसका पहनावा काफ़ी बेमेल है।જાડું સ્વેટર અને બીચ-ચંપલ — તેનો પહેરવેશ ઘણો કઢંગો છે.
2珍しいファッションの若者を、お年寄りがまちまちと眺めている。Mezurashii fasshon no wakamono o, otoshiyori ga machimachi to nagamete iru.An elderly person is staring hard at a young man in unusual clothes.एक बुज़ुर्ग अनोखे कपड़ों वाले युवक को एकटक घूर रहे हैं।એક વડીલ અનોખાં કપડાંવાળા યુવાનને એકીટશે તાકી રહ્યા છે.
3この型のカメラの価格はピンからキリまでまちまちです。Kono kata no kamera no kakaku wa pin kara kiri made machimachi desu.Prices for this type of camera range all the way from the top to the bottom.इस प्रकार के कैमरे की क़ीमतें सबसे ऊँची से सबसे नीची तक तरह-तरह की हैं।આ પ્રકારના કૅમેરાની કિંમતો સૌથી ઊંચીથી સૌથી નીચી સુધી જાતજાતની છે.
4新型ウイルスの伝染性については医者によって見解がまちまちである。Shingata uirusu no densensei ni tsuite wa isha ni yotte kenkai ga machimachi de aru.Doctors' views on how contagious the new virus is differ from one to another.नए वायरस की संक्रामकता पर डॉक्टरों की राय अलग-अलग है।નવા વાયરસની ચેપીપણા વિશે ડૉક્ટરોના મત જુદા-જુદા છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). Book's definition: まちまち = いろいろ (various). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be ちぐはぐ (chiguhagu) "mismatched, inconsistent"; 2 → should be まじまじと (majimaji to) "staring intently"; 3 → should be さまざま (samazama) "various, all kinds". (added, not in book) まちまち = each one is different and there is no unity (意見・服装・時間がまちまち). It is predicative (〜がまちまちだ); after ピンからキリまで the book prefers さまざま. Things that don't go together are ちぐはぐ.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 姿勢 (shisei) "posture"; 2 → should be 態度 (taido) "manner, attitude (接客態度)"; 3 → should be 姿勢 (shisei) "attitude, stance". (added, not in book) ポーズ is a deliberate body position for being looked at (ポーズをとる・決める). Everyday posture and someone's attitude toward work are both 姿勢 in Japanese — 姿勢 covers body and mind.

【40】

よっぽど（＝よほど）yoppodo (= yohodo)

Word: must be really … (strong guess about degree) — 別冊: よっぽど（＝よほど） = きっととても (surely, very)
HI: ज़रूर बहुत ही
GU: જરૂર બહુ જ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1ここは一流レストランと言われるだけあって、料理はよっぽど最高だ。Koko wa ichiryuu resutoran to iwareru dake atte, ryouri wa yoppodo saikou da.Just as you'd expect of a top-class restaurant, the food here really is the best.प्रथम श्रेणी का रेस्तराँ कहलाने के अनुरूप यहाँ का खाना वाक़ई लाजवाब है।પ્રથમ કક્ષાનું રેસ્ટોરાં કહેવાય તે મુજબ અહીંનું ભોજન ખરેખર લાજવાબ છે.
2いつも厳しい部長が今日はよっぽどやさしい。何かあるのだろうか。Itsumo kibishii buchou ga kyou wa yoppodo yasashii. Nanika aru no darou ka.The usually strict manager is strangely kind today. I wonder if something's up.हमेशा सख़्त रहने वाले मैनेजर आज अजीब तरह से नरम हैं। कुछ बात है क्या?હંમેશાં કડક મેનેજર આજે વિચિત્ર રીતે નરમ છે. કંઈ વાત છે કે?
3帰ってきた息子はよっぽど疲れていたのか、すぐに寝てしまった。Kaette kita musuko wa yoppodo tsukarete ita no ka, sugu ni nete shimatta.My son must have been really tired – he went straight to sleep when he got home.बेटा ज़रूर बहुत थका होगा, घर आते ही सो गया।દીકરો જરૂર બહુ થાકેલો હશે, ઘરે આવતાં જ સૂઈ ગયો.
4彼女の指輪のダイヤがよっぽど大きかったので、びっくりした。Kanojo no yubiwa no daiya ga yoppodo ookikatta node, bikkuri shita.The diamond in her ring was so big that I was astonished.उसकी अँगूठी का हीरा इतना बड़ा था कि मैं हैरान रह गया।તેની વીંટીનો હીરો એટલો મોટો હતો કે હું ચકિત થઈ ગયો.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). Book's definition: よっぽど（＝よほど） = きっととても (surely, very). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be さすがに (sasuga ni) "as expected"; 2 → should be いやに / 妙に (iya ni / myou ni) "strangely, oddly"; 4 → should be とても (totemo) "very". (added, not in book) よっぽど is a guess from evidence: the speaker sees a result (fell asleep at once) and infers "he must have been very …". It pairs with 〜のだろう・〜のか・〜らしい. Plain "very" is とても.

【41】

着実（な）chakujitsu (na)

Word: steady, sure and safe — 別冊: 着実（な） = しっかりと落ち着いていて、危ない感じがない (solid and calm, with no sense of danger)
HI: स्थिर, लगातार और भरोसेमंद
GU: સ્થિર, સતત અને ભરોસાપાત્ર
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1A氏がこの選挙で当選するのは着実である。A-shi ga kono senkyo de tousen suru no wa chakujitsu de aru.It is certain that Mr A will win this election.तय है कि श्री A यह चुनाव जीतेंगे।નક્કી છે કે શ્રી A આ ચૂંટણી જીતશે.
2怒らないから本当のことを着実に言いなさい。Okoranai kara hontou no koto o chakujitsu ni iinasai.I won't get angry, so tell me the truth honestly.मैं नाराज़ नहीं होऊँगा, सच-सच बताओ।હું ગુસ્સે નહીં થાઉં, સાચેસાચું કહી દો.
3この会社は、多くはないが着実に利益をあげている。Kono kaisha wa, ooku wa nai ga chakujitsu ni rieki o agete iru.This company is making profits steadily, if not in large amounts.यह कंपनी बहुत नहीं, पर लगातार मुनाफ़ा कमा रही है।આ કંપની બહુ નહીં, પણ સતત નફો કમાય છે.
4彼が犯人だという着実な証拠がある。Kare ga hannin da to iu chakujitsu na shouko ga aru.There is firm evidence that he is the culprit.इसका पक्का सबूत है कि वही अपराधी है।તે જ ગુનેગાર છે તેનો પાકો પુરાવો છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). Book's definition: 着実（な） = しっかりと落ち着いていて、危ない感じがない (solid and calm, with no sense of danger). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 確実 (kakujitsu) "certain"; 2 → should be 正直（に） (shoujiki (ni)) "honestly"; 4 → should be 確か (tashika) "reliable, sure". (added, not in book) 着実 describes progress made step by step without risk (着実に進む・上がる・利益をあげる). Certainty of an outcome is 確実; reliable evidence is 確かな証拠.

1彼は人望が厚いので、リーダーにふさわしい。Kare wa jinbou ga atsui node, riidaa ni fusawashii.He enjoys people's deep trust and respect, so he is well suited to be leader.लोग उस पर गहरा भरोसा और आदर रखते हैं, इसलिए वह नेता बनने लायक़ है।લોકો તેના પર ઊંડો વિશ્વાસ અને આદર રાખે છે, તેથી તે નેતા બનવા યોગ્ય છે.
2私は医師の免許を持っていないので、治療を行う人望がない。Watashi wa ishi no menkyo o motte inai node, chiryou o okonau jinbou ga nai.I don't have a medical licence, so I'm not qualified to give treatment.मेरे पास डॉक्टरी लाइसेंस नहीं है, इसलिए मुझे इलाज करने की योग्यता नहीं।મારી પાસે ડૉક્ટરી લાઇસન્સ નથી, તેથી મને સારવાર કરવાની લાયકાત નથી.
3この作家の新しい作品は非常に人望がよい。Kono sakka no atarashii sakuhin wa hijou ni jinbou ga yoi.This author's new work has an excellent reputation.इस लेखक की नई रचना की बहुत अच्छी ख्याति है।આ લેખકની નવી કૃતિની ખૂબ સારી નામના છે.
4彼女は医者になりたいという人望を持っている。Kanojo wa isha ni naritai to iu jinbou o motte iru.She has the hope of becoming a doctor.उसकी डॉक्टर बनने की इच्छा है।તેની ડૉક્ટર બનવાની ઇચ્છા છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.92–93, PDF 174–175). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 資格 (shikaku) "qualification"; 3 → should be 評判 (hyouban) "reputation"; 4 → should be 望み (nozomi) "hope, wish". (added, not in book) The 別冊 lists the fixed partners: 人望が厚い / ある / ない, 人望を集める / 失う. 人望 belongs only to people (leaders, bosses); works and products get 評判.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
斡旋するassen suru / 紹介したshoukai shita / 協力してkyouryoku shite / 貢献したいkouken shitai to arrange, find (a job, lodging) for someone as a go-between / introduced / cooperated / want to contribute 斡旋する = act as an agent to obtain (job, room) for someone; 紹介する = introduce; 協力する = cooperate; 貢献する = contribute (社会に貢献). 斡旋 is formal and business-like.
まちまちmachimachi / ちぐはぐchiguhagu / まじまじとmajimaji to / さまざまsamazama varied, differing from one to another / mismatched, inconsistent / staring intently / various, all kinds まちまち = each different (no consensus); さまざま = various (a wide range); ちぐはぐ = badly matched; まじまじと = staring hard. まちまち vs まじまじ: one mora apart.
ポーズpoozu / 姿勢shisei / 態度taido / 姿勢shisei a pose (for a camera, painting) / posture / manner, attitude (接客態度) / attitude, stance ポーズ = pose (for camera/art); 姿勢 = posture; stance or attitude (仕事に対する姿勢); 態度 = manner, behaviour toward others (接客態度). English "attitude" splits into 姿勢 and 態度.
よっぽど（＝よほど）yoppodo (= yohodo) / さすがにsasuga ni / いやに / 妙にiya ni / myou ni / とてもtotemo must be really … (strong guess about degree) / as expected / strangely, oddly / very よっぽど = must have been very (inference); とても = very (fact); さすがに = as you'd expect; いやに・妙に = unusually, strangely. If the sentence just states a fact, よっぽど does not fit.
着実（な）chakujitsu (na) / 確実kakujitsu / 正直（に）shoujiki (ni) / 確かtashika steady, sure and safe / certain / honestly / reliable, sure 着実 = steady progress; 確実 = certain to happen; 確か = reliable, definite; 正直 = honest. 着実 vs 確実 differ by one kanji: 着 (arrive, step) vs 確 (sure).
人望jinbou / 資格shikaku / 評判hyouban / 望みnozomi popularity based on trust and respect / qualification / reputation / hope, wish 人望 = trust and respect people feel for a person; 評判 = reputation (of a person or thing); 資格 = qualification; 望み = hope. 人望 vs 望み share 望 (hope/look up to).
Exam trap: several English words split into two Japanese ones here: "attitude" = 姿勢 / 態度, "pose/posture" = ポーズ / 姿勢, "steady/certain" = 着実 / 確実, "reputation" = 人望 (people) / 評判 (anything). Decide what the word is describing before choosing.
