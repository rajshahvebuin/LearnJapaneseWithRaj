1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.17–19)

Most of this round is about homophones: many options are real words that sound almost the same (看護 / 介護, 幼児 / 要旨, 被害者 / 被災者, 習得 / 獲得). Knowing the exact kanji of each on-yomi is what scores here. Also trap type 2 (要旨 ようし, not ようじ) and a kun-yomi noun (舵 かじ). See the six trap types on 第1回.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.17–18, PDF 99–100). Book's notes (paraphrased): 介護 = かいご (looking after the daily life of elderly or sick people). 介 = カイ (介入, 介在, 介抱, 仲介); 護 = ゴ (護衛, 看護, 弁護, 保護) and まも(る). 選択肢の言葉: 1 看護, 3 感情 / 勘定 / 環状, 4 会場 / 海上 / 開場 / 階上. (added, not in book) かんご (看護) is the closest trap: 看護 = medical nursing (看護師 = nurse); 介護 = everyday care (介護士 = care worker). 護 is short ご — not ごう or じょう.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.19, PDF 101). Book's notes (paraphrased): 獲得 = かくとく (earn, obtain). 獲 = カク (捕獲) and え (獲物); 得 = トク (得点, 所得, 説得, 取得, 習得), え(る), う(る). 選択肢の言葉: 1 如く, 3 修得 / 拾得 / 習得. (added, not in book) Trap type 3 does not apply here: カク / ク shrinks to っ mainly before a k-sound (画期的 かっきてき, 学期 がっき); before t it normally stays く (獲得 かくとく, 確定 かくてい), so かっとく is wrong. しゅうとく words are real but mean "learn" or "pick up".

Answer 3 (別冊 p.19, PDF 101). Book's notes (paraphrased): 舵 = かじ (steering of a boat); on-yomi ダ as in 方向舵 (rudder). 例: a teacher's job is to "steer" (舵を取る) students so they learn efficiently. 選択肢の言葉: 2 and 4 = 蛇. (added, not in book) 舵 = 舟 (boat) + 它, and 蛇 = 虫 + 它 — same right-hand part, which is where じゃ / へび come from. Alone, 舵 is kun-yomi かじ.
