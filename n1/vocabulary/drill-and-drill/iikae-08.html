The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.80–81)

2 Every question — 問題 (p.63)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【43】

景気が回復しそうな兆しが少しずつ現れている。Keiki ga kaifuku shisou na kizashi ga sukoshi zutsu arawarete iru.

Underlined word: 兆し = 物事が起こる前に現れる印 (a sign that appears before something happens).
EN: Signs that the economy may recover are gradually appearing.
HI: अर्थव्यवस्था में सुधार के आसार धीरे-धीरे दिख रहे हैं।
GU: અર્થતંત્રમાં સુધારાનાં એંધાણ ધીમે ધીમે દેખાઈ રહ્યાં છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1予想yosouforecast, expectationअनुमानઅનુમાન
2希望kibouhopeआशाઆશા
3証明shoumeiproofप्रमाणપુરાવો
4前ぶれmaebureforewarning, heraldपूर्व-संकेतપૂર્વસંકેત

Answer 4 (別冊 p.80, PDF 162). Book's notes (paraphrased): 兆し = 前ぶれ, a sign that shows itself before something happens. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 予想 and 希望 are in people's heads; 兆し is something you can observe (現れる). 証明 is proof after the fact, not a forewarning.

【44】

新しいソフトを使ってみたところ、パソコンの作業がはかどった。Atarashii sofuto o tsukatte mita tokoro, pasokon no sagyou ga hakadotta.

Underlined word: はかどる = 調子よく、どんどん進む (to go ahead briskly and smoothly).
EN: When I tried the new software, my computer work went much faster.
HI: नया सॉफ़्टवेयर इस्तेमाल करके देखा तो कंप्यूटर का काम तेज़ी से आगे बढ़ा।
GU: નવું સૉફ્ટવેર વાપરી જોયું તો કમ્પ્યૂટરનું કામ ઝડપથી આગળ વધ્યું.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1手間取ったtemadottatook a lot of time / troubleबहुत समय लगाઘણો સમય લાગ્યો
2早く進んだhayaku susundawent ahead quicklyजल्दी आगे बढ़ाઝડપથી આગળ વધ્યું
3全部済んだzenbu sundawas all finishedसब पूरा हो गयाબધું પૂરું થયું
4成功したseikou shitasucceededसफल हुआસફળ થયું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): はかどる = (work or study) progresses smoothly and steadily. 選択肢の言葉: 1 手間取る = not going smoothly, taking time / はかどらない (例: 論文に取り掛かったのだが、資料集めに手間取っていて一向に進まない (ronbun ni torikakatta no da ga, shiryou atsume ni temadotte ite ikkou ni susumanai)). The 別冊 has no note for 3 or 4. (added, not in book) はかどる is about the pace of progress, not completion (3) or a good outcome (4).

【45】

お手元の資料にある表のデータをご覧ください。O-temoto no shiryou ni aru hyou no deeta o goran kudasai.

Underlined word: データ = 資料としての数値 (figures used as reference material).
EN: Please look at the data in the table in the handout in front of you.
HI: कृपया अपने सामने रखे दस्तावेज़ की तालिका के आँकड़े देखिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને આપની સામેના દસ્તાવેજના કોષ્ટકના આંકડા જુઓ.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1計算keisancalculationगणनाગણતરી
2予定yoteischedule, planकार्यक्रमકાર્યક્રમ
3企画kikakuproject, planयोजनाયોજના
4数値suuchinumerical values, figuresआँकड़े, संख्यात्मक मानઆંકડા, સંખ્યાત્મક મૂલ્ય

Answer 4 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): データ = 数値, numbers used as material. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 計算 is what you do with data; 予定・企画 are plans. A 表 (table) holds figures → 数値.

【46】

彼は私の目をまともに見ようとしなかった。Kare wa watashi no me o matomo ni miyou to shinakatta.

Underlined word: まともに = まっすぐに真正面から／普通に (straight from the front / in a normal way).
EN: He would not look me straight in the eye.
HI: उसने मेरी आँखों में सीधे देखने की कोशिश नहीं की।
GU: તેણે મારી આંખમાં સીધું જોવાનો પ્રયત્ન ન કર્યો.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1まっすぐにmassugu nistraightसीधेસીધું
2真面目にmajime niseriously, earnestlyगंभीरता सेગંભીરતાથી
3強くtsuyokustronglyज़ोर सेજોરથી
4やさしくyasashikugentlyप्यार सेપ્રેમથી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): まともに = straight from directly in front / in an ordinary way. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 真面目に (2) is the sound-and-meaning trap: まとも can mean "decent, proper" (まともな人), close to 真面目, but with 目を見る the sense is physical — straight on, so まっすぐに.

【47】

こんなありきたりな発想から生まれた商品は売れるはずがない。Konna arikitari na hassou kara umareta shouhin wa ureru hazu ga nai.

Underlined word: ありきたりな = 普通で、特に変わったところがない (ordinary, nothing special — negative "boring" sense).
EN: A product born of such an unoriginal idea can't possibly sell.
HI: ऐसे घिसे-पिटे विचार से बना उत्पाद बिक ही नहीं सकता।
GU: આવા ઘસાયેલા વિચારમાંથી બનેલું ઉત્પાદન વેચાય જ નહીં.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1特殊なtokushu naspecial, unusualविशेष, असाधारणવિશેષ, અસામાન્ય
2手軽なtegaru naeasy, handyआसान, सुविधाजनकસહેલું, સગવડભર્યું
3ありふれたarifuretacommonplaceआम, मामूलीસામાન્ય, સાધારણ
4一般的なippanteki nageneral, typicalसामान्यસામાન્ય, સર્વસાધારણ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): ありきたりな = ありふれた, ordinary with nothing different about it, with the negative sense "boring". 選択肢の言葉: 2 手軽な = simple, no trouble (例: このスーツケースは大きすぎないし軽いので、手軽に持ち運べて旅行に便利だ (kono suutsukeesu wa ookisuginai shi karui node, tegaru ni mochihakobete ryokou ni benri da)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 4. (added, not in book) 4 一般的な is neutral (widely applicable); ありきたり／ありふれた carry the criticism "seen it all before".

【48】

彼の生き方は行き当たりばったりだ。Kare no ikikata wa ikiatari battari da.

Underlined word: 行き当たりばったり = 前もってちゃんと準備をしたり考えておいたりしない様子 (not preparing or thinking ahead).
EN: He lives his life without any plan.
HI: उसकी ज़िंदगी बिना किसी योजना के, जैसे-तैसे चलती है।
GU: તેનું જીવન કોઈ આયોજન વિના, જેમતેમ ચાલે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1計画的keikakutekiplanned, systematicयोजनाबद्धઆયોજનબદ્ધ
2慎重shinchoucautiousसावधानસાવચેત
3無神経mushinkeiinsensitive, inconsiderateअसंवेदनशीलઅસંવેદનશીલ
4無計画mukeikakuunplannedबिना योजना काઆયોજન વિનાનું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): 行き当たりばったり = 無計画, not preparing or thinking things out in advance. 選択肢の言葉: 3 無神経(な) = not caring what others dislike or find a nuisance / inconsiderate (例: 医者の無神経な言葉が患者の心を傷つけた (isha no mushinkei na kotoba ga kanja no kokoro o kizutsuketa)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 1 and 2 are opposites; 3 shares the 無〜 prefix but is about feelings, not planning.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
兆しkizashi / 前ぶれmaebure / 予想yosou / 希望kibou sign / forewarning / forecast / hope 兆し・前ぶれ are observable signs (回復の兆しが見える, 地震の前ぶれ). 予想・希望 are mental. 兆し is usually for gradual changes, 前ぶれ often for events.
はかどるhakadoru / 手間取るtemadoru / 済むsumu progress well / take time / be finished Antonyms: 仕事がはかどる vs 手間取る. 済む is completion, not pace.
データdeeta / 数値suuchi / 資料shiryou data / figures / materials, documents データ = 数値 used as evidence; 資料 is the document that may contain it (お手元の資料).
まともにmatomo ni / まっすぐにmassugu ni / 真面目にmajime ni head-on; properly / straight / earnestly まともに見る・ぶつかる = head-on (physical). まともな人・まともに働く = decent/proper (close to 真面目). The verb tells which sense.
ありきたりarikitari / ありふれたarifureta / 月並みtsukinami / 一般的ippanteki run-of-the-mill / commonplace / hackneyed / general The first three criticise lack of originality (第7回 Q42 月並み); 一般的 is neutral ("typical, widespread").
行き当たりばったりikiatari battari / 無計画mukeikaku / 無神経mushinkei / 計画的keikakuteki haphazard / unplanned / insensitive / planned 行き当たりばったり ≈ 無計画 (colloquial vs formal). 無神経 is about others' feelings. Antonym: 計画的.
Exam trap: Q48 offers two 無〜 words. 無計画 (no plan) and 無神経 (no sensitivity) share the prefix but answer different questions. When options share a prefix or suffix, ignore it and compare the stems: 計画 vs 神経.
