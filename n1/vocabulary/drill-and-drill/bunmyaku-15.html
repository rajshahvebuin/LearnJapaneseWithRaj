2 Every question — 問題 (p.50)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【99】

初出演の映画がヒットして、彼は（　）人気スターの仲間入りをした。Hatsushutsuen no eiga ga hitto shite, kare wa (___) ninki sutaa no nakamairi o shita.

Full sentence: 初出演の映画がヒットして、彼はいちやく人気スターの仲間入りをした。Hatsushutsuen no eiga ga hitto shite, kare wa ichiyaku ninki sutaa no nakamairi o shita.
EN: His first film was a hit, and he joined the ranks of popular stars overnight.
HI: उसकी पहली फ़िल्म हिट हुई और वह रातों-रात लोकप्रिय सितारों में शामिल हो गया।
GU: તેની પહેલી ફિલ્મ હિટ થઈ અને તે રાતોરાત લોકપ્રિય સિતારાઓમાં સામેલ થઈ ગયો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1いちやくichiyakusuddenly (rising to fame)रातों-रातરાતોરાત
2いみじくもimijikumoaptly, fittingly (said)बिल्कुल ठीक ही (कहा)બરાબર જ (કહ્યું)
3かねがねkaneganefor a long time, previouslyबहुत पहले सेઘણા સમયથી
4まざまざとmazamaza tovividly, clearlyसाफ़-साफ़, स्पष्ट रूप सेસ્પષ્ટપણે, આબેહૂબ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.61, PDF 143). Book's notes (paraphrased): いちやく = skipping the middle stages and suddenly gaining high status or fame. 選択肢の言葉: 2 いみじくも = very aptly (例: "send your beloved child on a journey" — いみじくも言ったものだ (imijikumo itta mono da); my daughter came back from abroad quite grown up); 3 かねがね = from before (例: 先生のお名前はかねがねうかがっておりました (sensei no o-namae wa kanegane ukagatte orimashita)); 4 まざまざと = as it was, clearly (例: I まざまざと思い出した (mazamaza to omoidashita) the first time I met her). (added, not in book) One hit film → straight into the stars: いちやく (一躍) 人気者になる / 有名になる.

【100】

この村の、自然に恵まれた（　）生活が訪れた人々に安らぎを与える。Kono mura no, shizen ni megumareta (___) seikatsu ga otozureta hitobito ni yasuragi o ataeru.

Full sentence: この村の、自然に恵まれた素朴な生活が訪れた人々に安らぎを与える。Kono mura no, shizen ni megumareta soboku na seikatsu ga otozureta hitobito ni yasuragi o ataeru.
EN: The village's simple life, blessed with nature, brings peace of mind to those who visit.
HI: प्रकृति से भरपूर इस गाँव का सादा जीवन यहाँ आने वालों को सुकून देता है।
GU: કુદરતથી ભરપૂર આ ગામનું સાદું જીવન અહીં આવનારાઓને શાંતિ આપે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1細やかなkomayaka naconsiderate, attentive (to others' feelings)बारीक ख़याल रखने वालाઝીણવટથી કાળજી રાખતું
2素朴なsoboku nasimple, unaffectedसादाસાદું
3貧弱なhinjaku napoor, feeble, meagreकमज़ोर, दुबलाનબળું, પાતળું
4ぞんざいなzonzai nacareless, rough (manner)लापरवाह, रूखाબેદરકાર, તોછડું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.61–62, PDF 143–144). Book's notes (paraphrased): 素朴(な) = simple. 選択肢の言葉: 1 細やか(な) = thinking carefully about the other person's feelings (例: the hotel's service shows 客への細やかな配慮 (kyaku e no komayaka na hairyo)); 3 貧弱(な) = inadequate and weak (例: a fighter 貧弱な体格 (hinjaku na taikaku) needs technique to beat a big opponent); 4 ぞんざい(な) = (way of speaking or acting) careless and rough (例: I was angry at the reporter's ぞんざいな話し方 (zonzai na hanashikata) to the elderly). (added, not in book) 自然に恵まれた + 安らぎを与える (gives peace) → a positive, plain life: 素朴な生活. 貧弱 is negative.

【101】

その男が犯人だという決定的な（　）は一つもない。Sono otoko ga hannin da to iu ketteiteki na (___) wa hitotsu mo nai.

Full sentence: その男が犯人だという決定的な証拠は一つもない。Sono otoko ga hannin da to iu ketteiteki na shouko wa hitotsu mo nai.
EN: There is not a single piece of conclusive evidence that the man is the culprit.
HI: वह आदमी ही अपराधी है, इसका एक भी पक्का सबूत नहीं है।
GU: તે માણસ જ ગુનેગાર છે, એનો એક પણ નક્કર પુરાવો નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1真実shinjitsuthe truth (what really happened)सच्चाईસત્ય હકીકત
2証拠shoukoevidence, proofसबूतપુરાવો
3原理genriprincipleसिद्धांतસિદ્ધાંત
4真理shinritruth (universal)परम सत्यપરમ સત્ય

Answer 2 (別冊 p.62, PDF 144). Book's notes (paraphrased): 証拠 = proof. 選択肢の言葉: 1 真実 = what is really true (例: この新聞記事に書かれていることは真実ではない (kono shinbun kiji ni kakarete iru koto wa shinjitsu de wa nai)); 4 真理 = truth (例: I want a career 宇宙の真理を追求する (uchuu no shinri o tsuikyuu suru)). The 別冊 has no note for 3 原理. (added, not in book) 決定的な〜 + 一つもない (not a single one) → countable proof: 決定的な証拠. 真実 is not counted in pieces.

【102】

金曜日が祝日なので、今週末は三（　）になる。Kin'youbi ga shukujitsu na node, konshuumatsu wa san (___) ni naru.

Full sentence: 金曜日が祝日なので、今週末は三連休になる。Kin'youbi ga shukujitsu na node, konshuumatsu wa sanrenkyuu ni naru.
EN: Friday is a public holiday, so this weekend will be a three-day weekend.
HI: शुक्रवार को सार्वजनिक छुट्टी है, इसलिए इस सप्ताहांत लगातार तीन दिन की छुट्टी होगी।
GU: શુક્રવારે જાહેર રજા છે, તેથી આ સપ્તાહાંતે સળંગ ત્રણ દિવસની રજા થશે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1連日renjitsuday after dayरोज़-रोज़, लगातार कई दिनરોજેરોજ, સતત ઘણા દિવસ
2休暇kyuukaleave, vacationछुट्टी (अवकाश)રજા (વેકેશન)
3連休renkyuuconsecutive holidaysलगातार छुट्टियाँસળંગ રજાઓ
4休日kyuujitsuday off, holidayछुट्टी का दिनરજાનો દિવસ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.62, PDF 144). Book's notes (paraphrased): 連休 = two or more days off in a row. 選択肢の言葉: 1 連日 = every single day (例: this summer 連日35度以上の猛暑が続いた (renjitsu sanjuugo-do ijou no mousho ga tsuzuita)). The 別冊 has no note for 2 休暇 or 4 休日. (added, not in book) Friday holiday + Saturday + Sunday = three days off in a row → 三連休. The number 三 before the blank only combines with 連休 (三連休, 五連休).

【103】

荷物が間違いなく届くように（　）のが私の仕事です。Nimotsu ga machigai naku todoku you ni (___) no ga watashi no shigoto desu.

Full sentence: 荷物が間違いなく届くように手配するのが私の仕事です。Nimotsu ga machigai naku todoku you ni tehai suru no ga watashi no shigoto desu.
EN: My job is to arrange things so that parcels arrive without fail.
HI: सामान बिना ग़लती के पहुँचे, इसका इंतज़ाम करना मेरा काम है।
GU: સામાન ભૂલ વગર પહોંચે તેની વ્યવસ્થા કરવી એ મારું કામ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1手探りするtesaguri suruto grope, feel one's wayटटोलनाફંફોસવું
2手配するtehai suruto arrangeइंतज़ाम करनाવ્યવસ્થા કરવી
3手当てするteate suruto give (medical) treatmentइलाज / मरहम-पट्टी करनाસારવાર કરવી
4手加減するtekagen suruto go easy on, make allowancesढील देना, नरमी बरतनाઢીલ આપવી, નરમાશ રાખવી

Answer 2 (別冊 p.62, PDF 144). Book's notes (paraphrased): 手配する = prepare (e.g. by giving directions). 選択肢の言葉: 1 手探りする = search by hand / by feel rather than with the eyes (例: there was a power cut at night and I 部屋の中を手探りで (heya no naka o tesaguri de) found the torch); 3 手当てする = treat (例: in the ambulance 救急隊員がけが人を手当てし始めた (kyuukyuu taiin ga keganin o teate shihajimeta)); 4 手加減する = adjust to the other person's situation (例: he's only a child, 手加減してやりなさいよ (tekagen shite yarinasai yo)). (added, not in book) Making sure parcels arrive = organising transport → 手配する. All four start with 手 — the kanji gives no clue.

【104】

彼は今、脇目もふらずに新しい仕事に（　）。Kare wa ima, wakime mo furazu ni atarashii shigoto ni (___).

Full sentence: 彼は今、脇目もふらずに新しい仕事に打ち込んでいる。Kare wa ima, wakime mo furazu ni atarashii shigoto ni uchikonde iru.
EN: He is now throwing himself into his new job without looking left or right.
HI: वह इन दिनों इधर-उधर देखे बिना अपने नए काम में पूरी तरह जुटा हुआ है।
GU: તે અત્યારે આમતેમ જોયા વગર પોતાના નવા કામમાં પૂરેપૂરો લાગી ગયો છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1打ち込んでいるuchikonde iruis devoted to (打ち込む)पूरी तरह जुटा हैપૂરેપૂરો લાગ્યો છે
2たて込んでいるtatekonde iruis crowded / busy (たて込む)भीड़ है / व्यस्त हैભીડ છે / વ્યસ્ત છે
3取り込んでいるtorikonde iruis in a bustle; is taking in (取り込む)उलझा है; अंदर ले रहा हैગૂંચવાયો છે; અંદર લે છે
4持ち込んでいるmochikonde iruis bringing in (持ち込む)अंदर ला रहा हैઅંદર લાવે છે

Answer 1 (別冊 p.62–63, PDF 144–145). Book's notes (paraphrased): 打ち込む = devote oneself to something. 選択肢の言葉: 2 たて込む = ① many people gather in one place (例: we're having a sale, so 店内がたて込んでいます (tennai ga tatekonde imasu)) ② things to do pile up at one time (例: 今週は予定がたて込んでいて (konshuu wa yotei ga tatekonde ite), no free time) ③ buildings stand packed together (例: old houses たて込んでいる (tatekonde iru) here, so a fire would be very dangerous); 3 取り込む = ① take inside (例: it started raining, so I 洗濯物を取り込んだ (sentakumono o torikonda)) ② be in confusion (例: 今、ちょっと取り込んでいますので (ima, chotto torikonde imasu node), I'll call back later); 4 持ち込む = bring in (例: 飛行機の中に液体を持ち込まないでください (hikouki no naka ni ekitai o mochikomanaide kudasai); this place is popular with students because you can bring your own drinks). (added, not in book) 脇目もふらずに (single-mindedly) + 仕事に〜 → 打ち込む.

連休renkyuu / 連日renjitsu / 休日kyuujitsu / 休暇kyuuka holidays in a row / day after day / day off / leave 三連休・大型連休; 連日の猛暑; 休日出勤; 有給休暇を取る (you apply for 休暇).
手配tehai / 手当てteate / 手探りtesaguri / 手加減tekagen arrangement / treatment / groping / going easy チケットを手配する; けがの手当て (also 手当 = allowance); 手探りで進む (also "by trial and error"); 手加減する (hold back).
打ち込むuchikomu / たて込むtatekomu / 取り込むtorikomu / 持ち込むmochikomu devote oneself / be crowded, busy / take in; be in a bustle / bring in 研究に打ち込む; 予定がたて込む; 洗濯物を取り込む・取り込み中; 機内に持ち込む. Only 打ち込む takes 〜に with an activity.
インフォメーションinfomeeshon / コミュニケーションkomyunikeeshon / レクリエーションrekurieeshon information desk / communication / recreation Same 〜ション ending (like 第10回 Q70). Only インフォメーション names a place you can go to.
Exam trap: 〜込む compound verbs (Q104) change meaning with the first verb. Learn them as pairs with their objects: 仕事に打ち込む, 予定がたて込む, 洗濯物を取り込む, 液体を持ち込む. Also note 脇目もふらず again (see 第10回 Q65 and 第12回 Q82) — it signals total concentration.
