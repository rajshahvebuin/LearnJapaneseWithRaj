Answer 1 (別冊 p.11, PDF 93). Book's notes (paraphrased): 繁盛 = はんじょう (prosperity, a thriving business). 繁 = ハン (繁栄, 繁華街, 頻繁) and しげ(る); 盛 = セイ (盛大, 全盛), ジョウ (繁盛), も(る), さか(る／り／ん). 選択肢の言葉: 2 反省 / 半生. (added, not in book) はんせい is the trap: セイ is 盛's usual on-yomi, but in 繁盛 it is ジョウ (trap type 5 — a kanji with many readings). Compare 繁栄 はんえい, which means the same thing.

【32】

被災地では復興（ふっこう）に協力するボランティアを募っている。Hisaichi de wa fukkou ni kyouryoku suru borantia o tsunotte iru.

Underlined word: 募って (募る)tsunotte (tsunoru) — call for, recruit, collect
EN: In the disaster area they are calling for volunteers to help with the reconstruction.
HI: आपदा-प्रभावित क्षेत्र में पुनर्निर्माण में मदद के लिए स्वयंसेवकों को बुलाया जा रहा है।
GU: આપત્તિગ્રસ્ત વિસ્તારમાં પુનર્નિર્માણમાં મદદ માટે સ્વયંસેવકોને બોલાવવામાં આવે છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1つのってtsunottecall for, recruit (募る)आमंत्रित करना, भर्ती करनाઆમંત્રવું, ભરતી કરવી
2したってshitatteadore, look up to (慕う)चाहना, आदर करनाચાહવું, આદર કરવો
3ぼってbottewrong — on-yomi ボ + って(ग़लत — ऑन पठन ボ + って)(ખોટું — ઓન વાંચન ボ + って)
4くだってkudattego down (下る)नीचे उतरनाનીચે ઊતરવું

Answer 1 (別冊 p.11, PDF 93). Book's notes (paraphrased): 募る = つのる (call for, advertise for, recruit); on-yomi ボ as in 募金, 応募. 選択肢の言葉: 2 慕って (慕う), 4 下って (下る). (added, not in book) 募 and 慕 share the top part 莫 — that's why したって is offered. Remember the bottom: 力 in 募 ("gather with effort") vs ⺗ (a form of 心) in 慕 ("feel for"). 募る also means "grow stronger" for feelings: 不安が募る.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.12, PDF 94). Book's notes (paraphrased): 待遇 = たいぐう (treatment, working conditions). 待 = タイ (待望, 待機, 接待) and ま(つ) (待ち遠しい, 待ち合わせ); 遇 = グウ (遭遇, 優遇, 奇遇). (added, not in book) The 別冊 lists no wrong-option words here. 待 contains 寺 (ジ) and 遇 contains 禺 — options 2 and 4 play on these parts. 待遇 also means how you treat a guest: 手厚い待遇.

【36】

インターネットで電話料金の内訳を確認した。Intaanetto de denwa ryoukin no uchiwake o kakunin shita.

Underlined word: 内訳uchiwake — breakdown, itemisation (of costs)
EN: I checked the breakdown of my phone bill on the internet.
HI: मैंने इंटरनेट पर फ़ोन बिल का ब्योरा जाँचा।
GU: મેં ઇન્ટરનેટ પર ફોન બિલની વિગતો તપાસી.

2うちやくuchiyakunot a word (kun + on mixed)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.12–13, PDF 94–95). Book's notes (paraphrased): 内訳 = うちわけ (breakdown of expenses). 内 = ナイ (内閣, 内緒, 内臓) and うち (内側); 訳 = ヤク (翻訳, 英訳) and わけ (reason). (added, not in book) A kun + kun word: うち + わけ ("dividing up the inside"). ないやく is the on + on reading, which looks natural but is not this word. Other kun + kun money words: 割引 わりびき, 手当 てあて.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
繁盛hanjou vs 盛大・全盛seidai · zensei ジョウ vs セイ 盛 = ジョウ only in 繁盛; otherwise セイ. Kun-yomi: 盛る もる (dish up), 盛ん さかん, 盛り さかり.
募るtsunoru vs 慕うshitau call for vs adore Same top 莫. 募 (力 at the bottom) = gather; 慕 (⺗ at the bottom) = long for. 募金 ぼきん / 思慕 しぼ.
描写byousha vs 美容biyou びょう (small ょ) vs びよう (big よ) Count the beats: びょう = 2, びよう = 3. 描 = ビョウ (also 猫 ビョウ).
妬むnetamu vs 僻むhigamu envy vs feel resentful / hard done by 人の成功を妬む (envy someone); 自分だけ損だと僻む (sulk that only you lose out).
待遇taiguu vs 優遇yuuguu treatment vs favourable treatment Both グウ. 待遇がいい = good conditions; 経験者を優遇する = give preference to experienced people.
内訳uchiwake vs 翻訳hon'yaku kun わけ vs on ヤク 訳 = わけ (reason; 内訳, 言い訳) or ヤク (translation; 翻訳, 通訳).
Exam trap: watch the small ゃ/ゅ/ょ. Options like びようしゃ vs びょうしゃ are only one tiny kana apart, and the exam counts that as a completely different reading.
