1 Points — the words tested in this round

The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.81–83)

【49】

彼は寛大な人だから、きっと許してくれるだろう。Kare wa kandai na hito da kara, kitto yurushite kureru darou.

Underlined word: 寛大な = 他人のすることや考えを受け入れることができる様子 (able to accept what others do and think).
EN: He is a broad-minded man, so he will surely forgive you.
HI: वह उदार व्यक्ति है, इसलिए ज़रूर माफ़ कर देगा।
GU: તે ઉદાર માણસ છે, એટલે જરૂર માફ કરી દેશે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1心が広いkokoro ga hiroibig-hearted, broad-mindedबड़े दिल वालाમોટા મનવાળું
2世話好きなsewazuki nafond of looking after othersदूसरों की देखभाल का शौक़ीनબીજાની સંભાળનો શોખીન
3温厚なonkou namild-mannered, gentleसौम्य, शांत स्वभाव काસૌમ્ય, શાંત સ્વભાવનું
4情が深いjou ga fukaiwarm-hearted, deeply caringस्नेही, दयालुસ્નેહાળ, લાગણીશીલ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.81, PDF 163). Book's notes (paraphrased): 寛大な = 心が広い, able to accept others' actions and ideas. 選択肢の言葉: 2 世話好きな = likes taking care of others (例: 私の母は世話好きで、近所にいる一人暮らしの老人に毎日昼食を届けている (watashi no haha wa sewazuki de, kinjo ni iru hitorigurashi no roujin ni mainichi chuushoku o todokete iru)); 3 温厚な = calm and gentle by nature (例: あの先生はとても温厚な方だ。彼が大きな声で怒っているのを見たことがない (ano sensei wa totemo onkou na kata da. Kare ga ookina koe de okotte iru no o mita koto ga nai)); 4 情が深い = strong feelings for others (例: 社長は一見冷たくて、会社のことしか考えていないように見えるが、実は、情が深く、辞めた社員の世話までするそうだ (shachou wa ikken tsumetakute, kaisha no koto shika kangaete inai you ni mieru ga, jitsu wa, jou ga fukaku, yameta shain no sewa made suru sou da)). (added, not in book) All four are kind people; only 心が広い carries 寛大's core of tolerating and forgiving others' faults.

【50】

新商品の発売に向けて、目下準備が進められている。Shin-shouhin no hatsubai ni mukete, mokka junbi ga susumerarete iru.

Underlined word: 目下 = ただ今 (right now, currently).
EN: Preparations for the launch of the new product are currently under way.
HI: नए उत्पाद की बिक्री शुरू करने के लिए इस समय तैयारी चल रही है।
GU: નવા ઉત્પાદનના વેચાણ માટે હાલમાં તૈયારી ચાલી રહી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1直ちにtadachi niimmediatelyतुरंतતરત જ
2徐々にjojo nigraduallyधीरे-धीरेધીમે ધીમે
3ただ今tadaimaat present, right nowइस समयઅત્યારે
4すみやかにsumiyaka nipromptly, swiftlyशीघ्रता सेઝડપથી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.82, PDF 164). Book's notes (paraphrased): 目下 = ただ今, right now, currently. 選択肢の言葉: 4 すみやかに = quickly (例: 校舎が古くなって危険なので、すみやかに対策を立ててほしいと、保護者たちは学校に要望書を出した (kousha ga furuku natte kiken na node, sumiyaka ni taisaku o tatete hoshii to, hogosha-tachi wa gakkou ni youbousho o dashita)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 1, 2 and 4 describe how fast; 目下 says when — now. Careful: 目下 read めした means "one's junior" (目下の人) — a different word.

【51】

ここに高層ビルを建設する計画は見送られることになった。Koko ni kousou biru o kensetsu suru keikaku wa miokurareru koto ni natta.

Underlined word: 見送る = 行わないで、そのままにする (to not carry it out and leave things as they are).
EN: The plan to build a high-rise here has been shelved.
HI: यहाँ ऊँची इमारत बनाने की योजना टाल दी गई है।
GU: અહીં ઊંચી ઇમારત બાંધવાની યોજના મુલતવી રાખવામાં આવી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1行われないokonawarenaiwill not be carried outलागू नहीं होगीઅમલમાં નહીં આવે
2実施されるjisshi sareruwill be implementedलागू की जाएगीઅમલમાં મુકાશે
3変更されるhenkou sareruwill be changedबदली जाएगीબદલાશે
4修正されるshuusei sareruwill be revisedसंशोधित की जाएगीસુધારાશે

Answer 1 (別冊 p.82, PDF 164). Book's notes (paraphrased): 見送る = not to do it, leaving things as they are. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 2 is the opposite; 3 and 4 mean the plan goes ahead in altered form. 見送る suggests "not now, maybe later" (shelving rather than cancelling — 中止).

【52】

髪が伸びて、うっとうしくなってきた。Kami ga nobite, uttoushiku natte kita.

Underlined word: うっとうしい = じゃまになって、いやな感じ (getting in the way and unpleasant).
EN: My hair has grown and is getting annoying.
HI: बाल बढ़ गए हैं और अड़चन बनकर खीज होने लगी है।
GU: વાળ વધી ગયા છે અને નડવા લાગ્યા છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1長くnagakulongलंबाલાંબું
2厚くatsukuthickघना, मोटाજાડું
3ゆううつにyuuutsu nidepressed, gloomyउदासઉદાસ
4じゃまにjama niin the way, a nuisanceअड़चन, रुकावटનડતરરૂપ

Answer 4 (別冊 p.82–83, PDF 164–165). Book's notes (paraphrased): うっとうしい = in the way, giving an unpleasant feeling. 選択肢の言葉: 3 ゆううつ(な) = a heavy, unpleasant mood (例: ああ、せっかくの日曜日なのに社長の命令で接待ゴルフをしなければならないなんてゆううつだな (aa, sekkaku no nichiyoubi na no ni shachou no meirei de settai gorufu o shinakereba naranai nante yuuutsu da na)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) うっとうしい has two senses: "gloomy" (うっとうしい天気, ≈ ゆううつ) and "bothersome" (≈ じゃま). With hair, it is the second; 1 長く is the cause, not the meaning.

【53】

最近あの課の上司と部下の間には摩擦が生じているようだ。Saikin ano ka no joushi to buka no aida ni wa masatsu ga shoujite iru you da.

Underlined word: 摩擦 = (here) 仲が悪いこと／関係が良くないこと (not getting along).
EN: There seems to be friction lately between the boss and his staff in that section.
HI: लगता है आजकल उस विभाग के बॉस और मातहतों के बीच मनमुटाव हो गया है।
GU: લાગે છે કે હમણાં એ વિભાગના ઉપરી અને હાથ નીચેના લોકો વચ્ચે ઘર્ષણ થઈ રહ્યું છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1不和fuwadiscord, bad relationsअनबनઅણબનાવ
2好意kouigoodwill, likingसद्भाव, पसंदસદ્ભાવ, પસંદ
3合意gouiagreementसहमतिસહમતિ
4不信fushindistrustअविश्वासઅવિશ્વાસ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.83, PDF 165). Book's notes (paraphrased): 摩擦 here = 不和, being on bad terms. 選択肢の言葉: 2 好意 = the feeling of liking someone (例: 彼は彼女に好意を持っているみたいだけど、彼女のほうはどうだろうか (kare wa kanojo ni koui o motte iru mitai da kedo, kanojo no hou wa dou darou ka)); 3 合意 = views coinciding (例: 交渉の結果、2つの会社は合意し、近々契約を結ぶことになった (koushou no kekka, futatsu no kaisha wa goui shi, chikajika keiyaku o musubu koto ni natta)); 4 不信 = not trusting (例: 国民の間に内閣に対する不信が高まっている (kokumin no aida ni naikaku ni taisuru fushin ga takamatte iru)). (added, not in book) 4 不信 is the near-miss — distrust can cause friction, but 摩擦／不和 is the clash itself; 不信 is one-directional.

【54】

今度の仕事は、まったく新しいアプローチで行われる。Kondo no shigoto wa, mattaku atarashii apuroochi de okonawareru.

Underlined word: アプローチ = (here) 目的のものに近づくための方法 (a way of getting closer to the goal).
EN: The next job will be carried out with an entirely new approach.
HI: अगला काम बिल्कुल नए तरीक़े से किया जाएगा।
GU: આગળનું કામ સાવ નવા અભિગમથી કરવામાં આવશે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1目的mokutekipurpose, aimउद्देश्यહેતુ
2方法houhoumethodतरीक़ाરીત
3動機doukimotiveप्रेरणा, कारणપ્રેરણા, હેતુ
4内容naiyoucontentविषय-वस्तुવિષયવસ્તુ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.83, PDF 165). Book's notes (paraphrased): アプローチ here = the way of approaching one's goal, i.e. 方法. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 1 目的 appears in the 別冊's own definition — a trap: アプローチ is the way toward the goal, not the goal. 〜で行われる also calls for a means (方法で).

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
寛大kandai / 心が広いkokoro ga hiroi / 温厚onkou / 情が深いjou ga fukai / 世話好きsewazuki tolerant / broad-minded / mild / warm-hearted / likes helping 寛大・心が広い = forgives faults (寛大な処置); 温厚 = never angry; 情が深い = cares deeply; 世話好き = keen to help. All "nice", each a different trait.
目下mokka / ただ今tadaima / 目下meshita / すみやかにsumiyaka ni currently / right now / one's junior / promptly もっか = now (目下検討中); めした = lower in rank (目下の人, ⇔ 目上). すみやかに = speed, not time.
見送るmiokuru / 中止するchuushi suru / 延期するenki suru shelve; see off / cancel / postpone 見送る = decide not to do it (for now) (採用を見送る); 中止 = called off; 延期 = moved to a later date. Literal 見送る = see someone off (駅で友達を見送る).
うっとうしいuttoushii / じゃまjama / ゆううつyuuutsu annoying; gloomy / in the way / depressed うっとうしい前髪 ≈ じゃま; うっとうしい梅雨 ≈ ゆううつ. ゆううつ describes your mood; じゃま describes the object.
摩擦masatsu / 不和fuwa / 不信fushin / 合意goui / 好意koui friction / discord / distrust / agreement / goodwill 摩擦 (physics: rubbing) → 貿易摩擦, 人間関係の摩擦 ≈ 不和. 合意 (agreement) and 好意 (liking) sound alike — both are opposites of 摩擦.
アプローチapuroochi / 方法houhou / 目的mokuteki / 動機douki approach / method / aim / motive Method (how) vs aim (what for) vs motive (why). アプローチ is "how". It can also mean approaching a person (彼女にアプローチする).
Exam trap: 目下 (Q50) has two readings with unrelated meanings: もっか "at present" and めした "one's junior". The JLPT may test the reading in 漢字読み and the meaning here; in a sentence like 目下準備が進められている, only もっか makes sense.
