Answer 2 (別冊 p.10, PDF 92). Book's notes (paraphrased): 束縛 = そくばく. 束 = ソク (拘束, 結束), たば (bundle; 花束), たば(ねる), つか (束の間); 縛 = バク (呪縛) and しば(る) (tie, bind). (added, not in book) 1 and 3 use the kun-yomi たば; じゅう / せん come from kanji that look like 縛 (縦 じゅう, 繊 せん — both 糸-radical). 4 adds a 促音 that the word doesn't have.

【30】

彼はいつも見栄を張って部下に食事をおごっている。Kare wa itsumo mie o hatte buka ni shokuji o ogotte iru.

Underlined word: 見栄mie — show, vanity, keeping up appearances (見栄を張る = show off)
EN: To show off, he is always treating his subordinates to meals.
HI: दिखावे के लिए वह हमेशा अपने मातहतों को खाना खिलाता है।
GU: દેખાડો કરવા તે હંમેશાં પોતાના હાથ નીચેના લોકોને જમાડે છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.11, PDF 93). Book's notes (paraphrased): 見栄 = みえ (show, vanity, display). 見 = ケン (見識, 会見, 外見) and み(る／える／せる); 栄 = エイ (光栄, 虚栄, 繁栄) and さか(える). (added, not in book) This is trap type 6 (irregular reading): 栄 is just え here — the word comes from 見え ("how one looks") and the kanji was attached later. 虚栄 (きょえい, vanity) has a similar meaning but the normal reading.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
円滑enkatsu vs 滑稽kokkei カツ vs コツ 滑 is カツ in 円滑, 潤滑; コツ (こっ) only in 滑稽 "funny". Kun-yomi: 滑る すべる, 滑らか なめらか.
退くshirizoku vs 引退intai kun しりぞく vs on タイ Same meaning (retire). 第一線を退く = 引退する. 退く can also be のく (step aside: そこを退いて).
無造作muzousa vs 無礼・無難burei · bunan む vs ぶ 無 is ム in most words; ブ only in a short list (無礼, 無難, 無愛想, 無事 ぶじ). 作 is サ in 無造作, 作用, 発作.
隔てるhedateru vs おだてるodateru separate vs flatter Same 〜だてる ending. 川が町を隔てる; 人をおだてて働かせる.
束縛sokubaku vs 拘束kousoku restriction vs detention, restraint Both mean "restrain". 束縛 = limiting freedom (自由を束縛する); 拘束 = physically or legally holding someone (身柄を拘束する).
見栄mie vs 虚栄kyoei え vs エイ Same idea (vanity). 栄 = え only in 見栄; everywhere else エイ. 見栄を張る / 虚栄心.
Exam trap: kanji like 無 and 作 have a main reading (ム, サク) and a minority reading (ブ, サ). The exam loves the minority words — 無礼, 無難, 無造作, 作用, 発作 — so learn them as a list.
