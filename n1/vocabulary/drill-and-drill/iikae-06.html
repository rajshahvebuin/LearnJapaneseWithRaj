2 Every question — 問題 (p.61)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【31】

当店は、「どの店よりも安く」をモットーにしている。Touten wa, "dono mise yori mo yasuku" o mottoo ni shite iru.

Underlined word: モットー = 目標を表す語 (a phrase expressing one's aim).
EN: Our shop's motto is "cheaper than any other shop".
HI: हमारी दुकान का आदर्श-वाक्य है "हर दुकान से सस्ता"।
GU: અમારી દુકાનનું સૂત્ર છે "બીજી કોઈ પણ દુકાન કરતાં સસ્તું".
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1目標を表す語mokuhyou o arawasu goa phrase expressing one's goalलक्ष्य बताने वाला वाक्यલક્ષ્ય દર્શાવતું વાક્ય
2価格のレベルkakaku no reberuprice levelदाम का स्तरભાવનું સ્તર
3客の希望kyaku no kiboucustomers' wishesग्राहकों की इच्छाગ્રાહકોની ઇચ્છા
4販売の基準hanbai no kijunsales standardबिक्री का मानदंडવેચાણનું ધોરણ

Answer 1 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): モットー = a word or phrase expressing one's aim. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) The quoted slogan is about price, which makes 2 and 4 tempting, but モットー is the slogan itself — a short phrase stating what you aim for — not a price level or a rule.

【32】

この仕事のこつを教えてください。Kono shigoto no kotsu o oshiete kudasai.

Underlined word: こつ = 物事をうまくやるための方法 (a way of doing things well).
EN: Please teach me the knack of this job.
HI: कृपया इस काम का गुर सिखाइए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને આ કામની યુક્તિ શીખવો.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1意義igisignificance, meaningमहत्त्व, सार्थकताમહત્ત્વ, સાર્થકતા
2作法sahouetiquette, proper mannersशिष्टाचारશિષ્ટાચાર
3手順tejunprocedure, order of stepsक्रम, प्रक्रियाક્રમ, પ્રક્રિયા
4要領youryouthe knack, the essential point of how to do itगुर, तरीक़े का सारગુર, રીતનો સાર

Answer 4 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): こつ = 要領, a way of doing things well. 選択肢の言葉: 3 手順 = the order in which things are done (例: 機械を動かす手順は、この説明書に書いてあるから、その通りにやってください (kikai o ugokasu tejun wa, kono setsumeisho ni kaite aru kara, sono toori ni yatte kudasai)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 手順 is the near-miss: a manual can tell you the 手順, but こつ／要領 is the experienced person's trick for doing it well.

【33】

夏の暑さをしのぐためのよい方法はないでしょうか。Natsu no atsusa o shinogu tame no yoi houhou wa nai deshou ka.

Underlined word: しのぐ = (here) 苦しいことや辛いことに耐えて、なんとか乗り切る (to bear hardship and get through it somehow).
EN: Is there a good way to get through the summer heat?
HI: क्या गर्मी की तपिश झेलने का कोई अच्छा तरीक़ा है?
GU: શું ઉનાળાની ગરમી સહન કરીને પાર પાડવાની કોઈ સારી રીત છે?
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1冷やすhiyasuto coolठंडा करनाઠંડું કરવું
2忘れるwasureruto forgetभूल जानाભૂલી જવું
3耐えるtaeruto endure, bearसहनाસહન કરવું
4消し去るkeshisaruto erase, wipe outमिटा देनाભૂંસી નાખવું

Answer 3 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): しのぐ (凌ぐ) here = to bear something painful and somehow pull through. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 1 冷やす fits "summer heat" on the surface, but しのぐ does not remove the heat — you survive it (暑さ・寒さ・飢えをしのぐ). しのぐ also has a second sense, "to surpass" (前作をしのぐ), not tested here.

【34】

いいか。途中で投げ出しちゃだめだぞ。Ii ka. Tochuu de nagedashicha dame da zo.

Underlined word: 投げ出す = ぜんぶ終わらないうちに、あきらめてやめてしまう (to give up and stop before it's all done); 投げ出しちゃ = 投げ出しては.
EN: Listen — you mustn't give up halfway, OK?
HI: सुनो, बीच में हार मानकर छोड़ना मत, समझे?
GU: સાંભળ, અધવચ્ચે હાર માનીને છોડી ન દેતો, સમજ્યો?
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あきちゃakichaget bored (and)ऊब जानाકંટાળી જવું
2やめちゃyamechaquitछोड़ देनाછોડી દેવું
3失敗しちゃshippai shichafailअसफल होनाનિષ્ફળ જવું
4休んじゃyasunjatake a restआराम करनाઆરામ કરવો

Answer 2 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): 投げ出す = to give up and stop before everything is finished. 選択肢の言葉 (the 別冊 just expands each casual form): 1 あきちゃ = あきては; 2 やめちゃ = やめては; 3 失敗しちゃ = 失敗しては; 4 休んじゃ = 休んでは. (added, not in book) あきる (get bored) is often the reason people give up, but it is not the act of quitting; 投げ出す literally "throw out" = abandon, i.e. やめる.

【35】

猫の体の動きは非常にしなやかだ。Neko no karada no ugoki wa hijou ni shinayaka da.

Underlined word: しなやかな = 柔らかくてよく曲がる (soft and bending easily).
EN: A cat's movements are extremely supple.
HI: बिल्ली के शरीर की हरकतें बेहद लचीली होती हैं।
GU: બિલાડીના શરીરની હિલચાલ અત્યંત લચકદાર હોય છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1速いhayaifastतेज़ઝડપી
2力強いchikarazuyoipowerfulताक़तवरશક્તિશાળી
3柔らかいyawarakaisoft, flexibleलचीला, मुलायमનરમ, લવચીક
4軽いkaruilightहल्काહળવું

Answer 3 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): しなやかな = soft and bending well. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) All four could describe a cat. しなやか is specifically about flexibility (bending without breaking), so only 柔らかい matches; 軽い (軽やか) and 速い are different qualities.

【36】

妻を驚かせようと、ひそかに結婚記念日のプレゼントを用意した。Tsuma o odorokaseyou to, hisoka ni kekkon kinenbi no purezento o youi shita.

Underlined word: ひそかに = 他の人に知られないように (so that others don't find out).
EN: To surprise my wife, I secretly got a wedding-anniversary present ready.
HI: पत्नी को चौंकाने के लिए मैंने चुपके से शादी की सालगिरह का तोहफ़ा तैयार किया।
GU: પત્નીને ચોંકાવવા માટે મેં છાનુંમાનું લગ્નની વર્ષગાંઠની ભેટ તૈયાર કરી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1くっきりkukkiriclearly, sharply (visible)साफ़-साफ़ (दिखना)સ્પષ્ટ (દેખાવું)
2こっそりkossorisecretly, on the quietचुपके सेછાનુંમાનું
3ばっさりbassari(cut) off in one strokeएक झटके में (काटना)એક ઝાટકે (કાપવું)
4きっぱりkippariflatly, decisivelyसाफ़ तौर पर, दो टूकસ્પષ્ટપણે, ચોખ્ખું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): ひそかに = こっそり, so that others won't know. 選択肢の言葉: 1 くっきり = (a shape is seen) clearly (例: 今日は天気がよくて空気もきれいで、青い空を背景に雪で真っ白の富士山がくっきり見える (kyou wa tenki ga yokute kuuki mo kirei de, aoi sora o haikei ni yuki de masshiro no Fujisan ga kukkiri mieru)); 3 ばっさり = (cut) in one go (例: 彼女は長く伸ばしていた髪をばっさり切った (kanojo wa nagaku nobashite ita kami o bassari kitta)); 4 きっぱり = clearly, without hesitation (例: 今は無理だと思ったときはきっぱり断ることにしている (ima wa muri da to omotta toki wa kippari kotowaru koto ni shite iru)). (added, not in book) Four 〜っ〜り mimetic adverbs that look alike; only こっそり is about secrecy.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
モットーmottoo / 目標mokuhyou / 基準kijun motto / goal / standard モットー is the phrase you live or trade by (「安全第一」をモットーに); 目標 is the target itself; 基準 is a yardstick for judging.
こつkotsu / 要領youryou / 手順tejun / 作法sahou knack / knack, gist / procedure / etiquette こつ・要領 = how to do it well (こつをつかむ, 要領がいい); 手順 = what order to do it in; 作法 = the socially correct way (茶道の作法).
しのぐshinogu / 耐えるtaeru / 乗り切るnorikiru get through / endure / pull through 暑さをしのぐ ≈ 暑さに耐える (note particle: を vs に). 乗り切る adds "to the end" (危機を乗り切る). しのぐ also = surpass (予想をしのぐ).
投げ出すnagedasu / やめるyameru / あきるakiru / サボるsaboru abandon halfway / quit / get bored / skive 投げ出す = quit midway, often in frustration; あきる = lose interest (cause, not act); サボる (第2回 Q7) = skip it out of laziness without quitting.
しなやかshinayaka / 柔らかいyawarakai / 軽やかkaroyaka supple / soft, flexible / light, nimble しなやかな枝・動き = bends gracefully; 軽やかな足取り = light-footed. Supple ≠ light.
ひそかにhisoka ni / こっそりkossori / くっきりkukkiri / ばっさりbassari / きっぱりkippari secretly / on the quiet / sharply visible / in one cut / flatly ひそかに (written) ≈ こっそり (spoken). くっきり見える; ばっさり切る; きっぱり断る — each mimetic adverb has its typical verb.
Exam trap: Q36's options are all "っ＋り" adverbs that differ by one consonant (こっそり / くっきり / ばっさり / きっぱり). Learn them as fixed collocations — こっそり抜け出す, くっきり見える, ばっさり切る, きっぱり断る — so the verb in the sentence points to the right adverb.
