1 Points — the words tested in this round

The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.76–77)

ちやほやするchiyahoya suruto fawn over, pamper and flatterलाड़ लड़ाना, चापलूसी से सिर चढ़ानाલાડ લડાવવા, ખુશામત કરી માથે ચડાવવું= 人をほめたり、甘やかしたりして機嫌をとる hito o hometari, amayakashitari shite kigen o toru → 甘やかされて (for ちやほやされて) amayakasarete. ✎「スターはいつもファンの人たちからちやほやされている」Sutaa wa itsumo fan no hito-tachi kara chiyahoya sarete iru. — "Stars are always being fawned over by their fans."
カップルkappurua couple (man and woman)जोड़ा (स्त्री-पुरुष)યુગલ, જોડું (સ્ત્રી-પુરુષ)= 夫婦や恋人同士など男女2人の組 fuufu ya koibito doushi nado danjo futari no kumi → 男女のペア danjo no pea. ✎「ハワイには、いつも新婚のカップルがたくさん来ている」Hawai ni wa, itsumo shinkon no kappuru ga takusan kite iru. — "Hawaii always has lots of newly-wed couples visiting."
打ち込むuchikomuto devote oneself to, throw oneself intoजी-जान से लगना, समर्पित होनाજીવ રેડીને લાગવું, સમર્પિત થવું= 熱中する／専心する netchuu suru / senshin suru → 熱中している netchuu shite iru. ✎「彼女は看護の仕事に打ち込んでいる」Kanojo wa kango no shigoto ni uchikonde iru. — "She is devoted to her nursing work."
消息を絶つshousoku o tatsuto go silent, disappear without wordख़बर आना बंद हो जाना, लापता होनाખબર બંધ થઈ જવી, લાપતા થવું= 連絡が来なくなる。行方がわからなくなる renraku ga konaku naru. Yukue ga wakaranaku naru → 連絡 (for 消息) renraku. ✎「消息が途絶えていて、家を出た息子がどこにいるのかわからない」Shousoku ga todaete ite, ie o deta musuko ga doko ni iru no ka wakaranai. — "All word has stopped; I don't know where my son is since he left home."
ルーズなruuzu naloose, lax, slack (about time, money, lifestyle)ढीला, लापरवाहઢીલું, બેદરકાર= きちんとしていない kichin to shite inai → だらしない darashinai. ✎「ルーズな生活をしていると、体にもよくないし、精神的にも集中力がなくなるのではないか」Ruuzu na seikatsu o shite iru to, karada ni mo yoku nai shi, seishinteki ni mo shuuchuuryoku ga nakunaru no de wa nai ka. — "A slack lifestyle is bad for the body and probably robs you of mental focus too."
すんなりsunnarismoothly, without a hitchबिना किसी अड़चन के, सहज हीકોઈ અડચણ વિના, સહેલાઈથી= (この文では) 順調に junchou ni → 問題なく mondai naku. ✎「両親に留学したいと言ったら反対されるかと思っていたのに、すんなり許してくれて、びっくりした」Ryoushin ni ryuugaku shitai to ittara hantai sareru ka to omotte ita no ni, sunnari yurushite kurete, bikkuri shita. — "I thought my parents would object when I said I wanted to study abroad, but they agreed without a fuss — I was amazed."

Meanings follow the 別冊's Japanese definition, translated by us (not the book's English). The 別冊 marks すんなり as 問題文中の意味 (the sense in this sentence; すんなり can also mean "slender, supple" (added, not in book)).

2 Every question — 問題 (p.59)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【19】

彼女は周囲の人からちやほやされて育った。Kanojo wa shuui no hito kara chiyahoya sarete sodatta.

Underlined word: ちやほやする = 人をほめたり、甘やかしたりして機嫌をとる (to keep someone happy by praising and indulging them).
EN: She grew up being pampered by everyone around her.
HI: वह आस-पास के लोगों के लाड़-प्यार में पली-बढ़ी।
GU: તે આસપાસના લોકોના લાડપ્યારમાં ઊછરી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1きたえられてkitaeraretebeing toughened up, trained hardकड़ी तालीम पाकरકડક તાલીમ પામીને
2かわいがられてkawaigararetebeing loved, doted onप्यार पाकरવહાલ પામીને
3甘やかされてamayakasaretebeing spoilt, indulgedलाड़ में बिगाड़ा जाकरલાડમાં બગાડાઈને
4いじめられてijimeraretebeing bulliedसताया जाकरહેરાન કરાઈને

Answer 3 (別冊 p.76, PDF 158). Book's notes (paraphrased): ちやほやする = to please someone by flattering and indulging them — so the passive ちやほやされる = 甘やかされる. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 2 かわいがられて is the near-miss: it is plain affection with no negative shade, whereas ちやほや (like 甘やかす) implies the person was spoilt by it. 1 and 4 are opposites.

【20】

週末はいつもこの通りがカップルで賑わう。Shuumatsu wa itsumo kono toori ga kappuru de nigiwau.

Underlined word: カップル = 夫婦や恋人同士など男女2人の組 (a man-and-woman pair such as a married couple or lovers).
EN: At weekends this street is always bustling with couples.
HI: सप्ताहांत में यह सड़क हमेशा जोड़ों से गुलज़ार रहती है।
GU: સપ્તાહાંતે આ રસ્તો હંમેશાં યુગલોથી ધમધમતો હોય છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1家族連れkazokuzurefamilies (with children)परिवार सहित आए लोगપરિવાર સાથે આવેલા લોકો
2学生のグループgakusei no guruupugroups of studentsछात्रों के समूहવિદ્યાર્થીઓનાં જૂથ
3男女のペアdanjo no peaman-and-woman pairsस्त्री-पुरुष जोड़ेસ્ત્રી-પુરુષ જોડાં
4若者たちwakamono-tachiyoung peopleयुवा लोगયુવાનો

Answer 3 (別冊 p.76–77, PDF 158–159). Book's notes (paraphrased): カップル = a pair of one man and one woman, such as spouses or lovers. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 4 若者たち is the trap — couples on a weekend street are often young, but カップル says nothing about age, only about being a pair.

【21】

大きな試合を前に、選手たちは練習に打ち込んでいる。Ookina shiai o mae ni, senshu-tachi wa renshuu ni uchikonde iru.

Underlined word: 打ち込む = 熱中する／専心する (to be absorbed in, devote oneself to).
EN: With a big match ahead, the players are throwing themselves into practice.
HI: बड़े मुक़ाबले से पहले खिलाड़ी अभ्यास में जी-जान से जुटे हैं।
GU: મોટી મેચ પહેલાં ખેલાડીઓ પ્રૅક્ટિસમાં જીવ રેડીને લાગ્યા છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1緊張しているkinchou shite iruare tense, nervousतनाव में हैंતણાવમાં છે
2興奮しているkoufun shite iruare excitedउत्तेजित हैंઉત્તેજિત છે
3楽しんでいるtanoshinde iruare enjoyingआनंद ले रहे हैंઆનંદ માણી રહ્યા છે
4熱中しているnetchuu shite iruare absorbed, wholly devotedपूरी तरह लीन हैंપૂરેપૂરા લીન છે

Answer 4 (別冊 p.77, PDF 159). Book's notes (paraphrased): 打ち込む = 熱中する／専心する. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 〜に打ち込む (仕事・研究・練習に) is about concentrated effort; 緊張・興奮 are emotional states, and 楽しむ says nothing about effort. Note 打ち込む also means literally "to drive in (a nail)" or "to type in" — context decides.

【22】

その船が消息を絶って1週間になる。Sono fune ga shousoku o tatte isshuukan ni naru.

Underlined word: 消息を絶つ = 連絡が来なくなる (contact stops coming); 消息 ≈ 連絡.
EN: It has been a week since all contact with that ship was lost.
HI: उस जहाज़ से संपर्क टूटे एक हफ़्ता हो गया है।
GU: એ વહાણ સાથે સંપર્ક તૂટ્યે એક અઠવાડિયું થઈ ગયું છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1連絡renrakucontact, communicationसंपर्कસંપર્ક
2通報tsuuhoua report (to police etc.)(पुलिस को) सूचना(પોલીસને) જાણ
3航海koukaia voyageसमुद्री यात्राદરિયાઈ સફર
4運行unkouoperation, running (of a service)परिचालन, सेवा का चलनाસંચાલન, સેવા ચાલવી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.77, PDF 159). Book's notes (paraphrased): 消息を絶つ = contact stops arriving; the person's whereabouts become unknown. 選択肢の言葉: 2 通報 = informing the police or similar (例: 事故が起きたという通報を受けて、パトカーが現場に向かった (jiko ga okita to iu tsuuhou o ukete, patokaa ga genba ni mukatta)); 3 航海 = travelling over the sea by ship (例: タイタニック号の航海は悲劇的な結末に終わった (Taitanikku-gou no koukai wa higekiteki na ketsumatsu ni owatta)). The 別冊 has no note for 4 運行. (added, not in book) 航海・運行 fit the ship context but not the verb 絶つ; 通報 is a one-way report, whereas 消息 is news of someone — i.e. 連絡.

【23】

彼は時間にルーズな人だ。Kare wa jikan ni ruuzu na hito da.

Underlined word: ルーズな = きちんとしていない (not neat / not properly disciplined).
EN: He is slack about time.
HI: वह समय के मामले में ढीला है।
GU: તે સમયની બાબતમાં ઢીલો છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1細かいkomakaiparticular, fussyबारीक़ी से देखने वाला, नुक़्ताचींઝીણવટભર્યું, ચીકણું
2だらしないdarashinaislovenly, undisciplinedलापरवाह, बेतरतीबબેદરકાર, અસ્તવ્યસ્ત
3厳しいkibishiistrictसख़्तકડક
4鈍いnibuislow, dull (of senses)सुस्त, मंदમંદ, ધીમું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.77, PDF 159). Book's notes (paraphrased): ルーズな = だらしない, not kept properly in order. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 時間に細かい・時間に厳しい are the opposite (punctilious about time). 鈍い is a false friend from English "loose → slow"; ルーズ is about discipline, not speed.

【24】

私が出した案は会議ですんなりと決まった。Watashi ga dashita an wa kaigi de sunnari to kimatta.

Underlined word: すんなり = (here) 順調に (smoothly, with no hitch).
EN: The proposal I put forward was approved at the meeting without any fuss.
HI: मेरा दिया हुआ प्रस्ताव बैठक में बिना किसी अड़चन के पास हो गया।
GU: મેં મૂકેલો પ્રસ્તાવ બેઠકમાં કોઈ અડચણ વિના મંજૂર થઈ ગયો.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ようやくyouyakuat last, finallyआख़िरकारઆખરે
2問題なくmondai nakuwithout any problemबिना किसी समस्या केકોઈ સમસ્યા વિના
3かろうじてkaroujitebarely, only justबड़ी मुश्किल से, बाल-बालમાંડ માંડ
4なんとかnantokasomehow, with difficultyकिसी तरहકોઈક રીતે

Answer 2 (別冊 p.77, PDF 159). Book's notes (paraphrased): すんなり here = 問題なく, 順調に. 選択肢の言葉: 3 かろうじて = やっと／ぎりぎりで, barely (例: レポートの締め切りに遅れそうだったが、かろうじて間に合った (repooto no shimekiri ni okuresou datta ga, karoujite ma ni atta)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 4. (added, not in book) 1, 3 and 4 all imply difficulty before success (ようやく = after a long wait; かろうじて = by a hair; なんとか = somehow). すんなり is the reverse — no resistance at all.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
ちやほやするchiyahoya suru / 甘やかすamayakasu / かわいがるkawaigaru fawn over / spoil / dote on ちやほや and 甘やかす both carry a "spoilt" nuance (ちやほやされて育つ); かわいがる is neutral affection (孫をかわいがる). ちやほや also implies flattery from many people.
カップルkappuru / ペアpea / 夫婦fuufu couple (romantic) / pair (any two) / married couple ペア is any two (ペアのカップ, ダブルスのペア); カップル is a romantic man-woman pair, married or not; 夫婦 only married.
打ち込むuchikomu / 熱中するnetchuu suru / 興奮するkoufun suru / 緊張するkinchou suru devote oneself / be absorbed / get excited / get tense 〜に打ち込む・〜に熱中する = sustained absorption in an activity; 興奮・緊張 are momentary emotional states before/at an event.
消息shousoku / 連絡renraku / 通報tsuuhou news of someone / contact / report to authorities 消息 is literary ("tidings"); 連絡 is everyday contact; 通報 is a one-way alert to 警察・消防. Set phrases: 消息を絶つ・消息不明; 連絡を取る; 110番に通報する.
ルーズなruuzu na / だらしないdarashinai / 厳しいkibishii / 細かいkomakai lax / slovenly / strict / fussy 時間にルーズ・金にルーズ ≈ だらしない (negative). 時間に厳しい・細かい = the strict opposite. English "loose" never means "slow" (鈍い).
すんなりsunnari / ようやくyouyaku / かろうじてkaroujite / なんとかnantoka smoothly / at last / barely / somehow すんなり決まる = no resistance. ようやく = after long effort; かろうじて = just scraped through (かろうじて間に合う); なんとか = managed, with trouble. The last three share "difficulty"; すんなり excludes it.
Exam trap: katakana loanwords are tested on their Japanese range, not their English one. ルーズ (Q23) is only "lax/undisciplined" — never "not tight" as in loose clothes (that is ゆるい). カップル (Q20) is only a romantic pair, not "a couple of" = two or three. Learn the Japanese definition the 別冊 gives, not the English word's dictionary entry.
