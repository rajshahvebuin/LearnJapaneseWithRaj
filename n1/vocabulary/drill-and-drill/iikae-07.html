1 Points — the words tested in this round

2 Every question — 問題 (p.62)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【37】

彼はその仕事を、いやな顔などみじんも見せずに引き受けた。Kare wa sono shigoto o, iya na kao nado mijin mo misezu ni hikiuketa.

Underlined word: みじんも(〜ない) = ほんの少しも(〜ない) (not even the tiniest bit).
EN: He took on the job without showing the slightest sign of reluctance.
HI: उसने ज़रा भी नाराज़गी दिखाए बिना वह काम स्वीकार कर लिया।
GU: તેણે લેશમાત્ર નારાજગી બતાવ્યા વિના એ કામ સ્વીકારી લીધું.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あまりamari(not) very muchज़्यादा (नहीं)બહુ (નહીં)
2ほとんどhotondohardly, almost (not)लगभग (नहीं)લગભગ (નહીં)
3一向にikkou ni(not) at all — no change however long(समय बीतने पर भी) बिल्कुल (नहीं)(સમય વીતવા છતાં) જરાય (નહીં)
4少しもsukoshi mo(not) in the leastज़रा भी (नहीं)જરા પણ (નહીં)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.79, PDF 161). Book's notes (paraphrased): みじんも〜ない = ほんの少しも〜ない. 選択肢の言葉: 3 一向に〜ない = no change at all / nothing happens however long you wait (例: いくら体に悪いと言っても彼女は一向に禁煙しようとしない (ikura karada ni warui to itte mo kanojo wa ikkou ni kin'en shiyou to shinai)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) あまり・ほとんど are partial negation ("not much / barely"); 一向に is total but tied to "no change over time". みじん (微塵) = a speck of dust → "not one speck".

【38】

いい加減なことを言わないでください。Iikagen na koto o iwanaide kudasai.

Underlined word: いい加減な = おおざっぱで信頼できない (rough and not to be trusted).
EN: Please don't say irresponsible things.
HI: कृपया गैर-ज़िम्मेदाराना बातें मत कहिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને બેજવાબદાર વાતો ન કરો.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1不公平なfukouhei naunfairअन्यायपूर्णઅન્યાયી
2無責任なmusekinin nairresponsibleगैर-ज़िम्मेदारબેજવાબદાર
3無礼なburei narude, insolentबदतमीज़અસભ્ય, ઉદ્ધત
4不適当なfutekitou nainappropriate, unsuitableअनुचितઅયોગ્ય

Answer 2 (別冊 p.79–80, PDF 161–162). Book's notes (paraphrased): いい加減な = 無責任な, careless and unreliable. 選択肢の言葉: 1 不公平な = not fair; 3 無礼な = 失礼な (例: 知り合いでもないのに、『おばあちゃん』と声をかけるなんて、無礼なやつだ (shiriai de mo nai no ni, "obaachan" to koe o kakeru nante, burei na yatsu da)). The 別冊 has no note for 4. (added, not in book) 無礼 and 不適当 judge politeness and suitability; いい加減 judges reliability — saying things without caring whether they're true.

【39】

社会生活を営む上では、妥協することも必要だ。Shakai seikatsu o itonamu ue de wa, dakyou suru koto mo hitsuyou da.

Underlined word: 妥協する = 事をまとめるために譲り合う (to give way to each other to settle a matter).
EN: In living in society, you also need to compromise.
HI: सामाजिक जीवन जीने में समझौता करना भी ज़रूरी है।
GU: સામાજિક જીવન જીવવામાં સમાધાન કરવું પણ જરૂરી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1折り合うoriauto come to terms, meet halfwayआपस में समझौता करनाએકબીજા સાથે સમાધાન કરવું
2尊重し合うsonchou shiauto respect each otherएक-दूसरे का सम्मान करनाએકબીજાનો આદર કરવો
3助け合うtasukeauto help each otherएक-दूसरे की मदद करनाએકબીજાને મદદ કરવી
4伝え合うtsutaeauto communicate with each otherआपस में बात पहुँचानाએકબીજાને વાત પહોંચાડવી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.80, PDF 162). Book's notes (paraphrased): 妥協する = 折り合う, opposing parties give ground to each other to settle things. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) All options are 〜合う "mutual" verbs and all are social virtues; the key element of 妥協 is giving up part of your demand, which only 折り合う (折る = to bend) expresses.

【40】

企画課の社員は明日のプレゼンテーションの準備で忙しい。Kikakuka no shain wa ashita no purezenteeshon no junbi de isogashii.

Underlined word: プレゼンテーション = 発表 (a presentation).
EN: The planning department staff are busy preparing for tomorrow's presentation.
HI: योजना विभाग के कर्मचारी कल की प्रस्तुति की तैयारी में व्यस्त हैं।
GU: આયોજન વિભાગના કર્મચારીઓ આવતીકાલની પ્રસ્તુતિની તૈયારીમાં વ્યસ્ત છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1会議kaigimeetingबैठकબેઠક
2出張shutchoubusiness tripव्यावसायिक यात्राધંધાકીય પ્રવાસ
3発表happyoupresentation, announcementप्रस्तुति, घोषणाપ્રસ્તુતિ, જાહેરાત
4契約keiyakucontractअनुबंधકરાર

Answer 3 (別冊 p.80, PDF 162). Book's notes (paraphrased): プレゼンテーション = 発表. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) A presentation often happens at a 会議 or leads to a 契約, but the word itself is the act of presenting — 発表.

【41】

前途のある若者たちを教えるのは、やりがいのあることです。Zento no aru wakamono-tachi o oshieru no wa, yarigai no aru koto desu.

Underlined word: 前途 = 将来 (one's future).
EN: Teaching young people with a future ahead of them is rewarding work.
HI: उज्ज्वल भविष्य वाले युवाओं को पढ़ाना संतोषजनक काम है।
GU: ઉજ્જ્વળ ભવિષ્યવાળા યુવાનોને ભણાવવું સંતોષકારક કામ છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1才能sainoutalentप्रतिभाપ્રતિભા
2やる気yarukimotivation, driveलगन, उत्साहઉત્સાહ, ધગશ
3能力nouryokuabilityक्षमताક્ષમતા
4将来shouraifutureभविष्यભવિષ્ય

Answer 4 (別冊 p.80, PDF 162). Book's notes (paraphrased): 前途 = 将来. 選択肢の言葉: 2 やる気 = a strong will to do things actively (例: やる気さえあれば、難しい仕事もうまくいくはずだ (yaruki sae areba, muzukashii shigoto mo umaku iku hazu da)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 3. (added, not in book) 才能・やる気・能力 are all qualities a promising youth might "have" (〜のある若者), which is the trap; 前途 (前 = ahead, 途 = road) is the road ahead — 将来. 前途のある = 前途有望な "promising".

【42】

あの評論家が言っていることは月並みだ。Ano hyouronka ga itte iru koto wa tsukinami da.

Underlined word: 月並みだ = 普通で、優れたところも変わったところもない (ordinary, neither excellent nor original — with the negative sense "boring").
EN: What that critic says is commonplace.
HI: वह आलोचक जो कहता है, वह घिसा-पिटा है।
GU: એ વિવેચક જે કહે છે તે ઘસાયેલું છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1変わっているkawatte iruis unusual, oddअजीब हैવિચિત્ર છે
2平凡だheibon dais ordinary, mediocreसाधारण हैસામાન્ય છે
3常識的だjoushikiteki dais common-sense, sensibleव्यावहारिक, समझदारी भरा हैવ્યવહારુ, સમજદાર છે
4鋭いsurudoiis sharp, incisiveतीखा, पैना हैતીક્ષ્ણ છે

Answer 2 (別冊 p.80, PDF 162). Book's notes (paraphrased): 月並みだ = 平凡だ, ordinary with nothing excellent or different about it, and carrying the negative sense of "boring". The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 3 常識的だ is the near-miss: it is neutral-to-positive ("sensible"), while 月並み is a put-down. 1 and 4 are the opposite.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
みじんもmijin mo / 少しもsukoshi mo / 一向にikkou ni / ほとんどhotondo / あまりamari not a speck / not at all / no change at all / hardly / not much Total: みじんも・少しも・一向に (一向に＝"still not, despite time": 一向に良くならない). Partial: ほとんど・あまり.
いい加減なiikagen na / 無責任なmusekinin na / 無礼なburei na / 不適当なfutekitou na sloppy / irresponsible / rude / inappropriate いい加減なことを言う = talk carelessly (無責任). 無礼 = 失礼 (manners). Note いい加減にしなさい = "that's enough!" — a different use.
妥協するdakyou suru / 折り合うoriau / 譲るyuzuru / 尊重し合うsonchou shiau compromise / meet halfway / give way / respect each other 妥協・折り合う = both sides give something up (条件が折り合う). 譲る can be one-sided; 尊重 gives up nothing.
プレゼンテーションpurezenteeshon / 発表happyou / 会議kaigi presentation / presentation, announcement / meeting プレゼン = 発表 to an audience; 会議 is the gathering in which it may happen. 発表 also = public announcement (合格発表).
前途zento / 将来shourai / 前途有望zento yuubou / 才能sainou prospects / future / promising / talent 前途 is formal (前途多難 "a hard road ahead", 前途洋々 "a bright future"). 才能 is what you have now; 前途 is what lies ahead.
月並みtsukinami / 平凡heibon / ありきたりarikitari / 常識的joushikiteki hackneyed / ordinary / run-of-the-mill / sensible 月並み・ありきたり (第8回 Q47) = negative "nothing new"; 平凡 = plain, can be neutral (平凡な幸せ); 常識的 = reasonable, not a criticism. Don't confuse 月並み with 軒並み (第2回 Q11, "all across the board").
Exam trap: 月並み (Q42) and 軒並み (第2回 Q11) look alike — both 〜並み — but mean "hackneyed" and "across the board". Also in Q37, みじんも must be followed by a negative (見せずに = 見せないで): if an option is not a negative-polarity word, it cannot paraphrase みじんも.
