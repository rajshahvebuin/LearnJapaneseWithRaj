This round is full of trap type 5 (kanji with many readings): 万 マン / バン, 人 ジン / ニン / ひと + a list of exceptional readings, 風 フウ / フ / かぜ / かざ, 情 ジョウ / ゼイ / なさけ. 風情 ふぜい is a special reading (trap type 6). Also kun-yomi verbs (唱える, 賭ける, 陥る). See the six trap types on 第1回.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.20–21, PDF 102–103). Book's notes (paraphrased): 陥る = おちいる (fall into); 陥れる = おとしいれる (trap, frame someone). On-yomi カン as in 陥没, 欠陥. 選択肢の言葉: 1 落ちる, 3 劣る, 4 訪れる. (added, not in book) おちる is the trap: the meaning is related (落ちる = fall), but 陥る is used for falling into a state — 危機・混乱・困難に陥る. Count the kana: お・ち・い・る.

EN: That building is old, but somehow it has a certain charm.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.21, PDF 103). Book's notes (paraphrased): 風情 = ふぜい (charm, elegance, good taste). 風 = フウ (風習, 風土, 洋風), フ (only 風情), かぜ, かざ (風上); 情 = ジョウ (情勢, 情熱, 同情), ゼイ (only 風情), なさ(け). 選択肢の言葉: 1 婦女 / 扶助. (added, not in book) A double special reading (trap types 1 + 6): 風 shortens to ふ and 情 becomes ぜい only in this word. ふうじょ uses the everyday readings and ふうぜい gets only one of the two changes. 3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
唱えるtonaeru vs 称えるtataeru voice, advocate vs praise Both "say something out loud". 異議を唱える (object); 功績を称える (praise achievements).
賭けるkakeru vs 儲けるmoukeru bet vs make money Both about money and end in 〜ける. お金を賭ける (risk it); お金を儲ける (earn it).
万人bannin vs 一万人ichiman-nin everyone vs 10,000 people バン = "all" (万能, 万国, 万全); マン = the number (一万, 万一 まんいち).

承諾shoudaku vs 承認・承知shounin · shouchi consent vs official approval · knowing / agreeing 申し出を承諾する (accept an offer); 国会の承認 (approval by parliament); 承知しました (understood).
陥るochiiru vs 落ちるochiru fall into (a state) vs fall (physically) 危機に陥る; 木から落ちる. 陥れる (おとしいれる) = trap someone.
風情fuzei vs 風習・同情fuushuu · doujou ふ + ぜい vs ふう / じょう Both kanji take special readings only in 風情. Everywhere else: 風 = ふう, 情 = じょう.
Exam trap: words like 風情 (ふぜい), 出納 (すいとう) and 万人 (ばんにん) are chosen precisely because the "normal" readings produce a plausible-looking wrong answer (ふうじょう, しゅつのう, まんにん). Keep a list of such words and review it before the exam.
