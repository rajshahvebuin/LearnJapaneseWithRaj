1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.30–32)

The last four questions all test a rare reading of a common kanji (trap type 5): 指図 (図 = ず, not と), 唯一 (一 = いつ, not いち), 体裁 (体 = てい, not たい) and 規模 (模 = ぼ, not も). The first two are kun-yomi verbs (欺く, 鍛える). See the six trap types on 第1回.

【99】

だれからも指図されずにできる仕事がしたい。Dare kara mo sashizu sarezu ni dekiru shigoto ga shitai.

Underlined word: 指図sashizu — instructions, orders (being told what to do)
EN: I want a job I can do without being ordered around by anyone.
HI: मुझे ऐसा काम चाहिए जिसमें कोई मुझे हुक्म न दे।
GU: મારે એવું કામ જોઈએ જેમાં કોઈ મને હુકમ ન કરે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1しずshizunot a word (on + on)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2ゆびずyubizunot a word(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3さしずsashizuorders, directionsनिर्देश, हुक्मસૂચના, હુકમ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.30–31, PDF 112–113). Book's notes (paraphrased): 指図 = さしず (direction, order). 指 = シ (指揮), ゆび (親指, 小指), さ(す) (point); 図 = ズ (図式, 図解), ト (図書, 意図), はか(る) (try, plan; 便宜を図る). 選択肢の言葉: 4 使途. (added, not in book) A kun + on mix (湯桶読み): さし (from 指す) + ず. Literally "point (指す) at the plan (図)". 指図する = give orders; 人の指図は受けない = I won't take orders from anyone. Compare 指示 (しじ), the more neutral word for "instructions".

Answer 4 (別冊 p.32, PDF 114). Book's notes (paraphrased): 規模 = きぼ (scale, size). 規 = キ (規範, 定規); 模 = ボ (only 規模 here), モ (模擬, 模範, 模倣). 選択肢の言葉: 1 肝, 3 義母. (added, not in book) Trap type 5: 模 is usually モ (模型, 模様), so きも looks natural — but in 規模 it is ボ. The options are every mix of き/ぎ × も/ぼ (trap type 2 too). 大規模 (だいきぼ, large-scale), 小規模 (small-scale).

Exam trap: the 漢字読み section loves "minority readings" of common kanji — 一 = いつ, 体 = てい, 模 = ぼ, 図 = ず. When you learn a kanji, write down the one or two words where it breaks its usual reading; those are the words the exam picks.
