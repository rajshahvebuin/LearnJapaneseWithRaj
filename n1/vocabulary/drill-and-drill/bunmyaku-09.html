2 Every question — 問題 (p.44)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【57】

ちょうど雨が降り始めたときに（　）よくバスが来たので、ぬれずにすんだ。Choudo ame ga furihajimeta toki ni (___) yoku basu ga kita node, nurezu ni sunda.

Full sentence: ちょうど雨が降り始めたときにタイミングよくバスが来たので、ぬれずにすんだ。Choudo ame ga furihajimeta toki ni taimingu yoku basu ga kita node, nurezu ni sunda.
EN: The bus came at just the right moment, when it started to rain, so I didn't get wet.
HI: ठीक जब बारिश शुरू हुई, उसी वक़्त बस आ गई, इसलिए मैं भीगने से बच गया/गई।
GU: બરાબર જ્યારે વરસાદ શરૂ થયો ત્યારે જ બસ આવી ગઈ, એટલે હું ભીંજાતાં બચી ગયો/ગઈ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1タイムtaimutime (a recorded time, e.g. in a race)समय (रेस आदि में दर्ज)સમય (રેસ વગેરેમાં નોંધાયેલો)
2タイミングtaimingutimingसही वक़्त, तालमेलયોગ્ય સમય, મેળ
3チャンスchansuchance, opportunityमौक़ाતક
4センスsensusense, taste (in fashion etc.)समझ, रुचि (फ़ैशन आदि)સૂઝ, રુચિ (ફૅશન વગેરે)

Answer 2 (別冊 p.50, PDF 132). Book's notes (paraphrased): タイミング = timing; 参考: タイミングよく = ちょうどいい時に. 選択肢の言葉: 1 タイム = time (例: T's 100メートル走のタイム (hyaku-meetoru-sou no taimu) was 10.5 seconds); 3 チャンス = chance (例: in my second year at the company 外国へ出張するチャンス (gaikoku e shucchou suru chansu) came along); 4 センス = taste (例: she has おしゃれのセンス (oshare no sensu) and always looks great). (added, not in book) 〜よく is the clue: タイミングよく is a set adverb ("with good timing"). チャンスよく is not said.

【58】

この道は、車が（　）すれ違えるぐらいの幅しかない。Kono michi wa, kuruma ga (___) surechigaeru gurai no haba shika nai.

Full sentence: この道は、車がかろうじてすれ違えるぐらいの幅しかない。Kono michi wa, kuruma ga karoujite surechigaeru gurai no haba shika nai.
EN: This road is only just wide enough for two cars to pass each other.
HI: यह सड़क बस इतनी ही चौड़ी है कि दो गाड़ियाँ मुश्किल से एक-दूसरे के पास से निकल सकें।
GU: આ રસ્તો એટલો જ પહોળો છે કે બે ગાડીઓ માંડ માંડ એકબીજાની બાજુમાંથી નીકળી શકે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1間一髪でkan'ippatsu deby a hair's breadth (a narrow escape)बाल-बाल (बचना)વાળ જેટલા અંતરે (બચવું)
2のるかそるかnoru ka soru kasink or swim, all or nothingआर या पारઆર યા પાર
3かろうじてkaroujitebarely, justमुश्किल सेમાંડ માંડ
4あやうくayaukualmost (something bad happened)बाल-बाल, लगभग (कुछ बुरा होते-होते)લગભગ (કંઈ ખરાબ થતાં થતાં)

Answer 3 (別冊 p.50–51, PDF 132–133). Book's notes (paraphrased): かろうじて = difficult, but finally / somehow. 選択肢の言葉: 1 間一髪で = it was dangerous but (it was all right) (例: I ran to the station and 最終電車に間一髪で間に合った (saishuu densha ni kan'ippatsu de maniatta)); 2 のるかそるか = not knowing if you'll succeed or fail, but going for it / 一か八か (例: nothing to lose — のるかそるかやってみよう (noru ka soru ka yatte miyou)); 4 あやうく (危うく) = it was dangerous / very nearly (例: I tripped on a stone and あやうく転ぶところだった (ayauku korobu tokoro datta)). (added, not in book) The road is not a one-off escape from danger but a permanent "only just" → かろうじてすれ違える. あやうく needs 〜ところだった (it nearly happened).

【59】

山ほど抱えている仕事の1つが終わり、（　）の休息をとった。Yama hodo kakaete iru shigoto no hitotsu ga owari, (___) no kyuusoku o totta.

Full sentence: 山ほど抱えている仕事の1つが終わり、束の間の休息をとった。Yama hodo kakaete iru shigoto no hitotsu ga owari, tsuka no ma no kyuusoku o totta.
EN: One of the mountain of jobs I have was finished, so I took a short break.
HI: ढेर सारे कामों में से एक पूरा हुआ, तो मैंने थोड़ी देर आराम किया।
GU: ઢગલો કામમાંથી એક પૂરું થયું, એટલે મેં થોડી વાર આરામ કર્યો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1目先mesakiright before one's eyes, the immediate futureआँखों के सामने, तात्कालिकઆંખ સામે, તાત્કાલિક
2束の間tsuka no maa brief momentपल भरપળવાર
3日取りhidorithe date (fixed for an event)तारीख़ (कार्यक्रम की)તારીખ (કાર્યક્રમની)
4間取りmadorifloor plan, room layoutकमरों की बनावटઓરડાઓની રચના

Answer 2 (別冊 p.51, PDF 133). Book's notes (paraphrased): 束の間 = for a short time. 選択肢の言葉: 1 目先 = in front of your eyes / for now (the near future) (例: the president 目先のことしか見えない (mesaki no koto shika mienai), so I worry about the company's future); 3 日取り = 日程, schedule for a day (例: 結婚式の日取りが決まりました (kekkonshiki no hidori ga kimarimashita)); 4 間取り = the number and arrangement of rooms (例: the house is big but 間取りが悪い (madori ga warui), so we'll remodel). (added, not in book) 束の間の休息 / 束の間の幸せ is the set collocation for a short moment. 目先 is about short-sightedness, not duration.

【60】

田舎と比べて都会では近所の人との付き合いが（　）だと言われる。Inaka to kurabete tokai de wa kinjo no hito to no tsukiai ga (___) da to iwareru.

Full sentence: 田舎と比べて都会では近所の人との付き合いが希薄だと言われる。Inaka to kurabete tokai de wa kinjo no hito to no tsukiai ga kihaku da to iwareru.
EN: It's said that, compared with the countryside, ties with neighbours are weak in cities.
HI: कहा जाता है कि गाँव की तुलना में शहर में पड़ोसियों से मेल-जोल कमज़ोर होता है।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે ગામડાંની સરખામણીમાં શહેરમાં પડોશીઓ સાથેનો સંપર્ક ઓછો હોય છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1希薄kihakuthin, weakकमज़ोर, फीकाનબળું, ઓછું
2深遠shin'enprofoundगहन, गूढ़ગહન, ગૂઢ
3簡素kansosimple, plain (lifestyle)सादाસાદું
4簡潔kanketsuconcise, briefसंक्षिप्तસંક્ષિપ્ત

Answer 1 (別冊 p.51, PDF 133). Book's notes (paraphrased): 希薄(な) = thin, weak. 選択肢の言葉: 2 深遠(な) = profound (例: 彼の思想は深遠すぎて (kare no shisou wa shin'en sugite) I can't understand it at all); 3 簡素(な) = simple (例: more young people long for 山村の簡素な生活 (sanson no kanso na seikatsu)); 4 簡潔(な) = brief (例: please speak もう少し簡潔な言い方で (mou sukoshi kanketsu na iikata de), so it's easy to follow). (added, not in book) 付き合い・人間関係・責任感が希薄 is the standard way to say relationships or a sense of something are "thin". 簡素 sounds possible (a simple life) but describes lifestyle, not relationships.

【61】

親友なら、私の気持ちを態度から（　）くれるはずです。Shin'yuu nara, watashi no kimochi o taido kara (___) kureru hazu desu.

Full sentence: 親友なら、私の気持ちを態度から察してくれるはずです。Shin'yuu nara, watashi no kimochi o taido kara sasshite kureru hazu desu.
EN: A close friend should be able to read how I feel from my attitude.
HI: अगर वह सच्चा दोस्त है, तो मेरे व्यवहार से मेरी भावनाएँ भाँप लेगा।
GU: જો એ સાચો મિત્ર હોય, તો મારા વર્તન પરથી મારી લાગણી પારખી લેશે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1阻んでhabandeblock, hinder (阻む)रोकना, बाधा डालनाરોકવું, અડચણ નાખવી
2承ってuketamawattereceive, hear (humble) (承る)स्वीकार करना, सुनना (विनम्र)સ્વીકારવું, સાંભળવું (નમ્ર)
3救ってsukuttesave, rescue (救う)बचानाબચાવવું
4察してsasshitesense, understand (察する)भाँप लेनाપારખી લેવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.51, PDF 133). Book's notes (paraphrased): 察する = guess, sense, understand. 選択肢の言葉: 1 阻む = hamper (例: 貧困が国民の生活水準の向上を阻んでいる (hinkon ga kokumin no seikatsu suijun no koujou o habande iru)); 2 承る = receive what a superior has said and act on it (例: we will 承りますので (uketamawarimasu node), please ask for anything). The 別冊 has no note for 3 救う. (added, not in book) 気持ちを態度から〜 = reading feelings from outward signs → 察する (相手の気持ちを察する). 承る is a humble word for your action towards a customer, never a friend's.

【62】

8月に入ってから（　）35度以上の猛暑が続いている。Hachigatsu ni haitte kara (___) sanjuugo-do ijou no mousho ga tsuzuite iru.

EN: Since August began, we've had fierce heat over 35°C day after day.
HI: अगस्त शुरू होने के बाद से रोज़ 35 डिग्री से ऊपर की भीषण गर्मी पड़ रही है।
GU: ઑગસ્ટ શરૂ થયો ત્યારથી રોજેરોજ 35 ડિગ્રીથી વધુની કાળઝાળ ગરમી પડે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1終始shuushifrom start to finishशुरू से अंत तकશરૂઆતથી અંત સુધી
2従来juuraiup to now, conventionalअब तक का, पारंपरिकઅત્યાર સુધીનું, પરંપરાગત
3常時joujiat all times, constantlyहर समयહંમેશાં

Answer 4 (別冊 p.51–52, PDF 133–134). Book's notes (paraphrased): 連日 = 毎日. 選択肢の言葉: 1 終始 = from beginning to end (例: in the sales meeting the president's face was 終始厳しかった (shuushi kibishikatta)); 2 従来 = traditionally, up to now (例: natural energy may one day replace 従来のエネルギー (juurai no enerugii)); 3 常時 = いつも / 常に (例: we 常時携帯電話を持ち歩いている (jouji keitai denwa o mochiaruite iru)). (added, not in book) 猛暑 (a heat wave) is counted in days → 連日. 終始 covers one event (a meeting) from start to end; 常時 is "constantly" for habits or states.

【63】

「結婚なんて、もういや。（　）だわ」と離婚した友人が言っていた。"Kekkon nante, mou iya. (___) da wa" to rikon shita yuujin ga itte ita.

Full sentence: 「結婚なんて、もういや。こりごりだわ」と離婚した友人が言っていた。"Kekkon nante, mou iya. Korigori da wa" to rikon shita yuujin ga itte ita.
EN: "Marriage? Never again. I've had enough," said my divorced friend.
HI: "शादी? अब नहीं। तौबा!" — मेरी तलाक़शुदा दोस्त ने कहा।
GU: "લગ્ન? હવે નહીં. તોબા!" — મારી છૂટાછેડા લીધેલી મિત્રે કહ્યું.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1さんざんsanzanterribly, severely (a bad degree)बुरी तरहખરાબ રીતે
2さっぱりsapparirefreshed; (not) at allताज़गी भरा; बिल्कुल (नहीं)તાજગીભર્યું; બિલકુલ (નહીં)
3ひっそりhissoriquiet, stillसुनसान, चुपचापસૂમસામ, ચૂપચાપ

Answer 4 (別冊 p.52, PDF 134). Book's notes (paraphrased): こりごり = be fed up, not want any more. 選択肢の言葉: 1 さんざん = a very bad degree (例: さんざん待たせておいて (sanzan matasete oite) not even apologising — unforgivable); 3 ひっそり = very quiet / quiet and unnoticed (例: the classroom after the students left ひっそりとしている (hissori to shite iru); a famous painter ひっそりと暮らしている (hissori to kurashite iru) in a small seaside town). 束の間tsuka no ma / 目先mesaki brief moment / what is right in front of you 束の間の休息 (short in time); 目先の利益 (short-sighted, immediate).
日取りhidori vs 間取りmadori date of an event vs room layout Same 〜取り: 結婚式の日取り; 3LDKの間取り.
希薄kihaku / 簡素kanso / 簡潔kanketsu / 深遠shin'en thin / plain / concise / profound 人間関係が希薄; 簡素な生活 (simple lifestyle); 簡潔な説明 (brief wording); 深遠な思想 (deep thought).
察するsassuru vs 承るuketamawaru sense, understand vs receive (humble) 気持ちを察する (read someone's feelings); ご注文を承る (a shop politely takes your order).
連日renjitsu / 終始shuushi / 常時jouji / 従来juurai day after day / start to finish / at all times / up to now 連日の猛暑; 終始笑顔だった; 常時携帯する; 従来の方法.
こりごりkorigori vs さんざんsanzan never again vs terribly さんざんな目に遭った → だからもうこりごりだ. さんざん describes the bad experience; こりごり is your reaction to it.
Exam trap: Q58 and Q63 offer adverbs that all sound like "narrow escape" or "bad experience". Check what follows the blank: a potential verb (すれ違える) → かろうじて; 〜ところだった → あやうく; だ at the end → こりごり.
