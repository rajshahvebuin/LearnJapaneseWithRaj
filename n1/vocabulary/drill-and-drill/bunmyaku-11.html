2 Every question — 問題 (p.46)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【71】

前年に税金を払いすぎた場合、税務署に（　）をすれば、払いすぎた分は返してもらえる。Zennen ni zeikin o haraisugita baai, zeimusho ni (___) o sureba, haraisugita bun wa kaeshite moraeru.

Full sentence: 前年に税金を払いすぎた場合、税務署に申告をすれば、払いすぎた分は返してもらえる。Zennen ni zeikin o haraisugita baai, zeimusho ni shinkoku o sureba, haraisugita bun wa kaeshite moraeru.
EN: If you overpaid tax last year, you can get the excess back by filing a return with the tax office.
HI: अगर पिछले साल ज़्यादा टैक्स भर दिया हो, तो कर-कार्यालय में रिटर्न भरने पर ज़्यादा भरी रक़म वापस मिल जाती है।
GU: જો ગયા વર્ષે વધારે ટૅક્સ ભરાઈ ગયો હોય, તો કર-કચેરીમાં રિટર્ન ભરવાથી વધારાની રકમ પાછી મળે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1依頼irairequest (asking someone to do something)अनुरोध, निवेदनવિનંતી
2願望ganbouwish, longingइच्छा, अभिलाषाઇચ્છા, અભિલાષા
3希望kibouhope, wishआशा, इच्छाઆશા, ઇચ્છા
4申告shinkokudeclaration, filingघोषणा, रिटर्न भरनाજાહેરાત, રિટર્ન ભરવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.54, PDF 136). Book's notes (paraphrased): 申告(する) = filing, declaration. 選択肢の言葉: 2 願望 (例: 外国で仕事をしたいという願望 (gaikoku de shigoto o shitai to iu ganbou) — he has long wanted to work abroad). The 別冊 has no note for 1 依頼 or 3 希望. (added, not in book) 税務署 (tax office) + 〜をする → the official word for reporting income/tax is 申告 (確定申告 = the annual tax return). 依頼 is asking someone a favour; 願望 / 希望 are feelings, not procedures.

【72】

日本のテレビ番組では、約15分おきに様々な商品の（　）が入る。Nihon no terebi bangumi de wa, yaku juugofun oki ni samazama na shouhin no (___) ga hairu.

Full sentence: 日本のテレビ番組では、約15分おきに様々な商品のコマーシャルが入る。Nihon no terebi bangumi de wa, yaku juugofun oki ni samazama na shouhin no komaasharu ga hairu.
EN: On Japanese TV programmes, commercials for all kinds of products come on about every 15 minutes.
HI: जापानी टीवी कार्यक्रमों में लगभग हर 15 मिनट पर तरह-तरह के उत्पादों के विज्ञापन आते हैं।
GU: જાપાની ટીવી કાર્યક્રમોમાં લગભગ દર 15 મિનિટે જાતજાતનાં ઉત્પાદનોની જાહેરાતો આવે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1メッセージmesseejimessageसंदेशસંદેશ
2タイトルtaitorutitleशीर्षकશીર્ષક
3ポーズpoozuposeमुद्रा, पोज़મુદ્રા, પોઝ
4コマーシャルkomaasharucommercial (advert)विज्ञापनજાહેરાત

Answer 4 (別冊 p.54, PDF 136). Book's notes (paraphrased): コマーシャル = commercial. 選択肢の言葉: 1 メッセージ = message (例: Mr Yamada was out, so I メッセージを残した (messeeji o nokoshita) that I'd call again); 2 タイトル = title (例: I don't know 映画のタイトル (eiga no taitoru), but a 3D film for children is showing); 3 ポーズ = pose (例: he always このポーズをとる (kono poozu o toru) for photos). (added, not in book) 商品の〜 + 番組に入る (comes on during a programme) → advertisements, コマーシャル (CM).

【73】

祖父の病状は重く、完治する（　）はないと医者に宣告された。Sofu no byoujou wa omoku, kanchi suru (___) wa nai to isha ni senkoku sareta.

Full sentence: 祖父の病状は重く、完治する見込みはないと医者に宣告された。Sofu no byoujou wa omoku, kanchi suru mikomi wa nai to isha ni senkoku sareta.
EN: My grandfather's condition is serious, and the doctor told us there is no prospect of a full recovery.
HI: दादाजी की हालत गंभीर है, और डॉक्टर ने साफ़ कह दिया कि पूरी तरह ठीक होने की कोई संभावना नहीं है।
GU: દાદાની હાલત ગંભીર છે, અને ડૉક્ટરે જાહેર કરી દીધું કે સંપૂર્ણ સાજા થવાની કોઈ શક્યતા નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1見極めmikiwamejudging after careful observationअच्छी तरह परखकर निर्णयસારી રીતે પારખીને નિર્ણય
2見込みmikomiprospect, likelihoodसंभावना, उम्मीदશક્યતા, આશા
3見合いmiaiarranged marriage meetingरिश्ते के लिए मुलाक़ात (अरेंज्ड)લગ્ન માટે મુલાકાત (ગોઠવેલી)
4見積もりmitsumoriestimate, quotationअनुमानित लागत, कोटेशनઅંદાજિત ખર્ચ, ક્વોટેશન

Answer 2 (別冊 p.54–55, PDF 136–137). Book's notes (paraphrased): 見込み = expectation, hope. 選択肢の言葉: 1 見極め = looking carefully and judging what a situation really is (例: 原子力発電は安全性の見極めが最大の課題 (genshiryoku hatsuden wa anzensei no mikiwame ga saidai no kadai)); 3 見合い = meeting someone introduced to you as a possible marriage partner (例: 見合い結婚にも恋愛結婚にも (miai kekkon ni mo ren'ai kekkon ni mo) each has its merits); 4 見積もり = calculating in advance how much an amount or cost will be (例: we got 見積もり (mitsumori) from several firms for the renovation and chose the cheapest). (added, not in book) 完治する〜はない = "there is no prospect of a cure" → 見込み.

【74】

この携帯電話の使い方は（　）簡単。幼児にも使えますよ。Kono keitai denwa no tsukaikata wa (___) kantan. Youji ni mo tsukaemasu yo.

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1いたってitatteextremely, veryबेहदએકદમ
2すっきりsukkirineatly, refreshed (clear, uncluttered)साफ़-सुथरा, हल्का महसूसચોખ્ખું, હળવું લાગવું
3まっぴらmappiraabsolutely not (I refuse)हरगिज़ नहींહરગિજ નહીં
4そろってsorotteall together, all alikeसब मिलकर, सब एक जैसेબધા સાથે મળીને, બધા એકસરખા

Answer 1 (別冊 p.55, PDF 137). Book's notes (paraphrased): いたって = 非常に / きわめて. 選択肢の言葉: 3 まっぴら = absolutely not wanting to (例: こんな大変な仕事はもうまっぴらだ (konna taihen na shigoto wa mou mappira da)); 4 そろって = everyone gathered / everyone in the same way (例: 家族8人がそろって食事をする (kazoku hachinin ga sorotte shokuji o suru) is rare; スタッフは全員そろって赤いTシャツ (sutaffu wa zen'in sorotte akai T-shatsu)). The 別冊 has no note for 2 すっきり. (added, not in book) The blank modifies the adjective 簡単 → a degree adverb: いたって簡単 (very easy). すっきり describes a state (部屋がすっきりした), not degree.

【75】

メッセージmesseeji / タイトルtaitoru / ポーズpoozu / コマーシャルkomaasharu message / title / pose / commercial メッセージを残す; 映画のタイトル; ポーズをとる; コマーシャルが入る・流れる.
見込みmikomi / 見極めmikiwame / 見合いmiai / 見積もりmitsumori prospect / careful judgement / marriage meeting / estimate All begin with 見. 回復の見込みがない (no prospect); 安全性の見極め (judging); お見合いをする; 工事の見積もりを取る (get a quote).
いたってitatte / すっきりsukkiri / まっぴらmappira / そろってsorotte extremely / neat, refreshed / absolutely not / all together いたって + adjective (いたって元気); 頭がすっきりする; 〜はもうまっぴらだ (I've had enough of ~); 家族そろって.
裕福なyuufuku na vs 豊富なhoufu na wealthy vs abundant Both contain "rich", but 裕福 is about a person/family having money; 豊富 is about a large amount of something (資源・知識・経験が豊富).
録画rokuga / 録音rokuon / 記載kisai / 記憶kioku video recording / sound recording / entry in a document / memory 画 = picture → 録画; 音 = sound → 録音; 記載 = written in a document (書類に記載する); 記憶 = memory (記憶に残る).
照れるtereru / めげるmegeru / 冴えるsaeru / もめるmomeru feel bashful / lose heart / be sharp, clear / quarrel 照れる = shy because of attention or praise; めげる = discouraged by a hardship; 頭が冴える / 冴えた音; 遺産でもめる.
Exam trap: words that share a kanji or a sound are the usual distractors — 録画 / 録音, 裕福 / 豊富, and the four 見〜 nouns in Q73. Fix each one with a typical collocation (見込みがない, 見積もりを取る, 録画予約) rather than the dictionary gloss.
