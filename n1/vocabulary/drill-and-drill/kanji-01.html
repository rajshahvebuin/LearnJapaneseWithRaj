1 Points — how to answer 漢字読み + the kanji in this round

N1「文字語彙」の勉強のポイント — 漢字読み (summary of p.3, not the book's text)

EN: In 漢字読み you choose how a kanji word is written in hiragana, so you must know the exact kana spelling, not just roughly how it sounds. The book names six kinds of reading that are easy to get wrong: (1) long vs short vowels, (2) plain vs voiced sounds (清音 / 濁音), (3) a sound turning into a small っ (促音), (4) h-sounds turning into p-sounds (半濁音), (5) kanji with many readings, and (6) words with special, irregular readings. Learn each kanji inside whole words.

HI: 漢字読み में कांजी शब्द का हिरागाना में सही लिखित रूप चुनना होता है, इसलिए सिर्फ़ मोटा-मोटा उच्चारण नहीं, सटीक काना वर्तनी पता होनी चाहिए। किताब छह तरह की आम गलतियाँ बताती है: (1) लंबा या छोटा स्वर, (2) साधारण या सघोष ध्वनि (清音 / 濁音), (3) ध्वनि का छोटे っ में बदलना (促音), (4) h-ध्वनि का p-ध्वनि बनना (半濁音), (5) कई पठन वाले कांजी, (6) विशेष/अनियमित पठन वाले शब्द। हर कांजी को पूरे शब्दों के साथ याद करें।

GU: 漢字読みમાં કાંજી શબ્દનું હિરાગાનામાં સાચું લખાણ પસંદ કરવાનું હોય છે, તેથી માત્ર અંદાજે ઉચ્ચાર નહીં, ચોક્કસ કાના જોડણી આવડવી જોઈએ. પુસ્તક છ પ્રકારની સામાન્ય ભૂલો બતાવે છે: (1) લાંબો કે ટૂંકો સ્વર, (2) સાદો કે ઘોષ અવાજ (清音 / 濁音), (3) અવાજનું નાના っમાં બદલાવું (促音), (4) h-અવાજનું p-અવાજ બનવું (半濁音), (5) અનેક વાંચનવાળા કાંજી, (6) ખાસ/અનિયમિત વાંચનવાળા શબ્દો. દરેક કાંજીને આખા શબ્દો સાથે યાદ કરો.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.14)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(added, not in book) Option 4 is the trap: it uses each kanji's everyday reading, which is exactly what reading type 5 on p.3 warns about. 1 and 2 mix kun/on pieces that never occur.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
出納suitou vs 出世・納税shusse · nouzei すいとう ✓ / しゅつのう ✗ Inside 出納 only, 出 = スイ and 納 = トウ. Everywhere else 出 = シュツ (しゅっ before s/t/p), 納 = ノウ.
納得・納豆・納戸・納屋nattoku · nattou · nando · naya なっ / なん / な readings of 納 納 has five on-type readings (ノウ・トウ・ナッ・ナン・ナ) — learn them through these four words.
滞るtodokooru vs 漂うtadayou be held up vs drift Both kanji have 氵, both readings start with t-; 支払い・仕事・流れが滞る, but 香り・雲が漂う.
遡るsakanoboru vs 逆らうsakarau go back in time / upstream vs disobey Both begin with さか and are about "going against". 過去に遡る (go back to the past); 親に逆らう (oppose parents).
浸すhitasu vs 浸るhitaru dip (transitive) vs be soaked / immersed (intransitive) パンをスープに浸す; 喜びに浸る (be immersed in joy) — the 別冊 example.
告げるtsugeru vs 遂げるtogeru vs 焦げるkogeru tell vs achieve vs get burnt Same 〜げる ending, one kana apart. 別れを告げる / 目的を遂げる / パンが焦げる.
抜粋bassui vs 抜群batsugun ばっ before s vs ばつ before g バツ shrinks to ばっ before s/t/k/p sounds (抜粋, 抜歯 ばっし) but stays ばつ before g (抜群).
逆様sakasama vs 逆転gyakuten kun + kun vs on + on Readings normally pair up within one system: さか+さま, ギャク+テン. Mixed options (ぎゃくさま, さかよう) are almost always traps.
Exam trap: when all four options are pronounceable, check two things — does each half belong to the same reading system (on + on / kun + kun), and does a small っ appear where the sound rules need it? Then watch for "special-word" readings like 出納 (すいとう), which you can only know by having met the word.
