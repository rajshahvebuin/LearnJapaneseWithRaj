2 Every question — 問題 (p.55)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【134】

山の空気は実に（　）。Yama no kuuki wa jitsu ni (___).

Full sentence: 山の空気は実にすがすがしい。Yama no kuuki wa jitsu ni sugasugashii.
EN: The mountain air is truly refreshing.
HI: पहाड़ों की हवा सचमुच ताज़गी भरी है।
GU: પર્વતોની હવા ખરેખર તાજગીભરી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1せわしないsewashinairestless, bustling, hurriedहड़बड़ी भरा, बेचैनઉતાવળિયું, બેચેન
2おびただしいobitadashiihuge in number; extremeबेशुमार; बेहदઅઢળક; અત્યંત
3うっとうしいuttoushiigloomy, oppressive, annoyingउदास, भारी; खीज भराઉદાસ, ભારે; ત્રાસદાયક
4すがすがしいsugasugashiirefreshingताज़गी भराતાજગીભર્યું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.71, PDF 153). Book's notes (paraphrased): すがすがしい = clear, fresh and pleasant. 選択肢の言葉: 1 せわしない = せわしい, busy-looking and unsettled (例: something must have happened — 警備員がせわしなく行ったり来たりしている (keibiin ga sewashinaku ittari kitari shite iru)); 2 おびただしい = very many or very much / to an extreme degree (例: at the accident scene おびただしい血が流れていた (obitadashii chi ga nagarete ita)); 3 うっとうしい = gloomy / bothersome and unpleasant (例: the rainy season has started and うっとうしい天気が続いている (uttoushii tenki ga tsuzuite iru); 前髪が伸びてうっとうしい (maegami ga nobite uttoushii)). (added, not in book) 山の空気 (mountain air) → fresh and clean: すがすがしい. うっとうしい is the opposite feeling.

【135】

クラブに（　）人には会員証が送られてくる。Kurabu ni (___) hito ni wa kaiinshou ga okurarete kuru.

Full sentence: クラブに登録した人には会員証が送られてくる。Kurabu ni touroku shita hito ni wa kaiinshou ga okurarete kuru.
EN: People who register with the club are sent a membership card.
HI: जो लोग क्लब में पंजीकरण कराते हैं, उन्हें सदस्यता-कार्ड भेजा जाता है।
GU: જે લોકો ક્લબમાં નોંધણી કરાવે છે, તેમને સભ્યપદ-કાર્ડ મોકલવામાં આવે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1収録したshuuroku shitarecorded (for broadcast); included (収録する)रिकॉर्ड किया (प्रसारण हेतु)રેકૉર્ડ કર્યું (પ્રસારણ માટે)
2登録したtouroku shitaregistered (登録する)पंजीकरण करायाનોંધણી કરાવી
3記入したkinyuu shitafilled in (a form) (記入する)भरा (फ़ॉर्म)ભર્યું (ફૉર્મ)
4記録したkiroku shitarecorded, wrote down (記録する)दर्ज कियाનોંધ્યું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.71, PDF 153). Book's notes (paraphrased): 登録する = register. 選択肢の言葉: 1 収録する = record pictures or sound (例: after テレビ番組の収録 (terebi bangumi no shuuroku) was over, all the staff went for a meal). The 別冊 has no note for 3 記入 or 4 記録. (added, not in book) クラブに〜 + 会員証 (membership card) → you register as a member: クラブに登録する. 記入 takes a form as object (用紙に記入する), not a club.

【136】

お（　）にある資料をご覧ください。O-(___) ni aru shiryou o goran kudasai.

Full sentence: お手元にある資料をご覧ください。O-temoto ni aru shiryou o goran kudasai.
EN: Please look at the materials in front of you.
HI: कृपया अपने सामने रखे दस्तावेज़ देखिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને આપની સામે રાખેલા દસ્તાવેજો જુઓ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1手口teguchimodus operandi (of a crime)अपराध का तरीक़ाગુનાની રીત
2手元temotoat handहाथ में, पास मेंહાથમાં, પાસે
3手本tehonmodel, example to followआदर्श, नमूनाઆદર્શ, નમૂનો
4手前temaethis side, in front ofइस तरफ़, सामनेઆ તરફ, સામે

Answer 2 (別冊 p.71, PDF 153). Book's notes (paraphrased): 手元 = at hand, nearby. 選択肢の言葉: 1 手口 = how a crime is done (例: この3つの盗難事件の手口はとても似ている (kono mittsu no tounan jiken no teguchi wa totemo nite iru) — possibly the same thief; the thief stole ¥600 million 巧妙な手口で (koumyou na teguchi de)); 3 手本 = 模範, model (例: 子どもは親を手本にして学び (kodomo wa oya o tehon ni shite manabi), grows up). The 別冊 has no note for 4 手前. (added, not in book) お〜にある資料 at a meeting → the papers in front of each person: お手元の資料 (a fixed polite phrase).

【137】

夫婦で（　）働いても、贅沢ができるほどの収入には程遠い。Fuufu de (___) hataraite mo, zeitaku ga dekiru hodo no shuunyuu ni wa hodotooi.

Full sentence: 夫婦でめいっぱい働いても、贅沢ができるほどの収入には程遠い。Fuufu de meippai hataraite mo, zeitaku ga dekiru hodo no shuunyuu ni wa hodotooi.
EN: Even with both of us working as hard as we can, our income is far from enough for any luxury.
HI: पति-पत्नी दोनों जी-जान से काम करें, तब भी आमदनी ऐशो-आराम लायक़ होने से कोसों दूर है।
GU: પતિ-પત્ની બંને જીવ લગાવીને કામ કરે, તોય આવક મોજશોખ જેટલી થવાથી ઘણી દૂર છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1よりいっそうyori issoueven more (than before)और भी ज़्यादाહજી વધુ
2手いっぱいteippaihaving one's hands fullकाम में पूरी तरह फँसा, फ़ुर्सत नहींકામમાં ગળાડૂબ, ફુરસદ નહીં
3ふんだんにfundan niin abundance, lavishlyभरपूर, खुले हाथ सेભરપૂર, છૂટા હાથે
4めいっぱいmeippaito the utmostपूरी ताक़त सेપૂરી તાકાતથી

Answer 4 (別冊 p.71–72, PDF 153–154). Book's notes (paraphrased): めいっぱい = to the point where you cannot do more / 精一杯. 選択肢の言葉: 1 よりいっそう = more than before / even more than now (例: congratulations — once at university, よりいっそうがんばって勉強してください (yori issou ganbatte benkyou shite kudasai)); 2 手いっぱい = having no room to do anything else (例: sorry, 今、こっちも手いっぱいで (ima, kotchi mo teippai de), I can't help you); 3 ふんだんに = plenty, in large numbers or amounts (例: 昔この地方には石炭がふんだんにあった (mukashi kono chihou ni wa sekitan ga fundan ni atta)). (added, not in book) 〜働いても…程遠い (however hard we work, still far) → めいっぱい働く. 手いっぱい is a state (手いっぱいだ), not a way of working.

【138】

その時計、素敵ですね。なんという（　）のものですか。Sono tokei, suteki desu ne. Nan to iu (___) no mono desu ka.

Full sentence: その時計、素敵ですね。なんというブランドのものですか。Sono tokei, suteki desu ne. Nan to iu burando no mono desu ka.
EN: That watch is lovely. What brand is it?
HI: वह घड़ी बहुत सुंदर है। किस ब्रांड की है?
GU: એ ઘડિયાળ બહુ સુંદર છે. કઈ બ્રાન્ડની છે?
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ブランドburandobrandब्रांडબ્રાન્ડ
2ファッションfasshonfashionफ़ैशनફૅશન
3デザインdezaindesignडिज़ाइनડિઝાઇન
4モードmoodomode (input mode, method)मोड, तरीकाમોડ, રીત

Answer 1 (別冊 p.72, PDF 154). Book's notes (paraphrased): ブランド = brand. 選択肢の言葉: 2 ファッション = fashion (例: she is ファッションに関心があり (fasshon ni kanshin ga ari) and studies fashion business at a vocational school); 3 デザイン = design (例: 日本の伝統的なデザイン (nihon no dentouteki na dezain) is being re-evaluated; my brother works in car design); 4 モード = mode (例: even for Japanese, typing ローマ字モードで (roomaji moodo de) is easier than kana mode). (added, not in book) なんという〜のもの (made by which ~?) asks for a name → a brand name.

【139】

このマンションの平均的な（　）は3LDKです。Kono manshon no heikinteki na (___) wa san-eru-dii-kei desu.

Full sentence: このマンションの平均的な間取りは3LDKです。Kono manshon no heikinteki na madori wa san-eru-dii-kei desu.
EN: The typical layout of the flats in this building is 3LDK (three rooms plus a living-dining-kitchen).
HI: इस अपार्टमेंट बिल्डिंग के फ़्लैटों का आम नक़्शा 3LDK (तीन कमरे + बैठक-भोजन-रसोई) है।
GU: આ ઍપાર્ટમેન્ટ બિલ્ડિંગના ફ્લૅટનો સામાન્ય નકશો 3LDK (ત્રણ રૂમ + બેઠક-ભોજન-રસોડું) છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1屋内okunaiindoors, inside a buildingइमारत के अंदरઇમારતની અંદર
2室内shitsunaiinside a room, indoorकमरे के अंदरઓરડાની અંદર
3部屋割りheyawariroom allocation (who stays where)कमरों का बँटवाराઓરડાઓની ફાળવણી
4間取りmadorifloor plan, room layoutघर का नक़्शाઘરનો નકશો

Answer 4 (別冊 p.72, PDF 154). Book's notes (paraphrased): 間取り = the arrangement of rooms in a house. 選択肢の言葉: 1 屋内 = inside a building / a place with a roof (例: 屋内プールでは一年中泳ぐことができる (okunai puuru de wa ichinenjuu oyogu koto ga dekiru)); 3 部屋割り = assigning who stays in which room. The 別冊 has no note for 2 室内. (added, not in book) 3LDK describes the layout of a flat → 間取り. 部屋割り is for a trip or dormitory (who sleeps where).

【140】

政府は新たなエネルギー対策を（　）。Seifu wa arata na enerugii taisaku o (___).

Full sentence: 政府は新たなエネルギー対策を打ち出した。Seifu wa arata na enerugii taisaku o uchidashita.
EN: The government has put forward new energy measures.
HI: सरकार ने नई ऊर्जा नीति पेश की।
GU: સરકારે નવી ઊર્જા નીતિ રજૂ કરી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1打ち出したuchidashitaput forward (打ち出す)पेश कियाરજૂ કર્યું
2持ち出したmochidashitatook out; brought up (a topic) (持ち出す)बाहर ले गया; बात छेड़ीબહાર લઈ ગયો; વાત છેડી
3取り出したtoridashitatook out (from inside) (取り出す)निकालाકાઢ્યું
4切り出したkiridashitaquarried; broached (a subject) (切り出す)काटकर निकाला; बात शुरू कीકાપીને કાઢ્યું; વાત શરૂ કરી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.72, PDF 154). Book's notes (paraphrased): 打ち出す = come up with, put forward. 選択肢の言葉: 2 持ち出す = ① take something from inside to outside (例: let's gather 非常時に持ち出すもの (hijouji ni mochidasu mono)) ② raise a topic (例: at dinner my wife 家を買う話を持ち出した (ie o kau hanashi o mochidashita)); 4 切り出す = ① cut stone or timber from a mountain and carry it out (例: this line was used to carry 山から切り出した材木 (yama kara kiridashita zaimoku)) ② start talking about something formally (例: I was wondering when to 結婚の話を切り出したら (kekkon no hanashi o kiridashitara), and my mother asked if I had something to say). The 別冊 has no note for 3 取り出す. (added, not in book) 政府 + 対策・方針 (policy) → announce it clearly: 打ち出す.
