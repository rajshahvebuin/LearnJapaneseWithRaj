1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.23–25)

A round of very common kanji used in uncommon ways. 成 and 就 take their rarer on-yomi (ジョウ, ジュ) in 成就 — trap type 5 with a 長音 twist (trap type 1). 下 has eight readings in the 別冊 list, and 下火 したび mixes two kun-yomi with 濁音 (trap type 2). Three questions are kun-yomi verbs (濁る, 儲ける, 反る). See the six trap types on 第1回.

【73】

この数日、川の水が濁っている。Kono suujitsu, kawa no mizu ga nigotte iru.

Underlined word: 濁って (濁る)nigotte (nigoru) — become muddy, cloudy
EN: For the past few days the river water has been muddy.
HI: पिछले कुछ दिनों से नदी का पानी मटमैला है।
GU: છેલ્લા થોડા દિવસથી નદીનું પાણી ડહોળું છે.

1にごってnigottebe muddy (濁る)मटमैला होनाડહોળું થવું
2におってniottesmell (匂う / 臭う)महकना / बदबू आनाસુગંધ આવવી / દુર્ગંધ આવવી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.23, PDF 105). Book's notes (paraphrased): 濁る = にごる (become muddy/cloudy). 濁 = ダク (濁音, 濁流, 混濁), にご(る／す); 言葉を濁す = answer vaguely. 選択肢の言葉: 2 匂って / 臭って, 3 混ざって / 交ざって, 4 滞って. (added, not in book) 濁 has 氵 (water), as do 混 and 滞 — that's why まざって and とどこおって are offered. におって is the sound trap (に・お vs に・ご). 濁音 (dakuon) is the grammar term for が・ざ・だ・ば sounds — the same kanji.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.24, PDF 106). Book's notes (paraphrased): 反る = そる (bend, curve, warp). 反 = ハン (反響, 反射, 反応, 反乱), ホン (謀反), そ(る／らす). 選択肢の言葉: 1 張って / 貼って / 這って, 2 帰って / 返って, 3 曲がって. (added, not in book) Two traps: かえって looks right because 返 contains 反 (and 反って is sometimes used for かえって "on the contrary" — but here the meaning is clearly physical), and まがって has a close meaning. 反る = bend backwards / warp out of flat; 曲がる = bend in general. 反らす = arch (your back).

【78】

彼らの人気は一時に比べて下火になっている。Karera no ninki wa ichiji ni kurabete shitabi ni natte iru.

Underlined word: 下火shitabi — on the decline, waning
EN: Their popularity has waned compared with its peak.
HI: उनकी लोकप्रियता एक समय की तुलना में ढलान पर है।

Answer 3 (別冊 p.24–25, PDF 106–107). Book's notes (paraphrased): 下火 = したび (becoming weaker / smaller in scale). 下 = カ, ゲ, お(りる／ろす), くだ(る／す), さ(がる／げる), した, しも, もと; 火 = カ (火星, 火力, 発火), ひ. 選択肢の言葉: 1 外科, 2 可否. (added, not in book) Two kun-yomi, and ひ becomes び (rendaku, trap type 2). Literally "the fire dying down" — 人気・流行・ブームが下火になる. げか and かひ use on-yomi of the same kanji (下 = ゲ/カ, 火 = カ) but are different words.

濁るnigoru vs 匂うniou be muddy vs smell One kana apart (ご / お). 水が濁る; 花が匂う. 言葉を濁す = be evasive.

反るsoru vs 曲がるmagaru warp, curve backwards vs bend, turn 反る = bow out of flat/straight; 曲がる = any bend (角を曲がる, 腰が曲がる).

Exam trap: common kanji can be the hardest. 成, 就, 下 and 火 are N5–N3 kanji, but 成就 (じょうじゅ) and 下火 (したび) use readings you rarely meet. When the "obvious" reading gives a non-word (せいしゅう, しもひ), look for the rare reading.
