1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.6–7)

This round tests trap types 1 + 2 together (貧乏 びんぼう — voiced び and long ぼう), trap type 3 (折衷 せっちゅう — せつ becomes せっ), and kun-yomi words (練る, 果てしない, 慎む, 麓). See the six trap types on 第1回.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.16)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

EN: Even if you are poor, you can be happy if there is love.
HI: भले ही ग़रीबी हो, प्यार हो तो इंसान ख़ुश रहता है।
GU: ભલે ગરીબી હોય, પ્રેમ હોય તો માણસ સુખી છે.

1びんぼうbinboupoor, povertyग़रीब, ग़रीबीગરીબ, ગરીબી
2ひんほうhinhouwrong — both sounds unvoiced(ग़लत — दोनों ध्वनियाँ अघोष)(ખોટું — બંને અવાજ અઘોષ)

4ひんぽうhinpouwrong — ひん + ぽう(ग़लत — ひん + ぽう)(ખોટું — ひん + ぽう)

Answer 1 (別冊 p.6, PDF 90). Book's notes (paraphrased): 貧乏 = びんぼう. 貧 has ヒン (貧血, 貧困, 貧弱), ビン (only 貧乏) and まず(しい); 乏 has ボウ (窮乏, 欠乏) and とぼ(しい). (added, not in book) Three traps at once: voiced び (not ひ), voiced ぼ (not ほ / ぽ), and a long vowel ぼう. びんぼ is how people sometimes say it casually, but the dictionary reading is びんぼう.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.7, PDF 91). Book's notes (paraphrased): 麓 = ふもと; on-yomi ロク as in 山麓 (さんろく). 選択肢の言葉: 3 裾野, 4 鹿. (added, not in book) 麓 is built from 林 over 鹿 — that is why しか (鹿) is offered as a trap. すその is close in meaning, but it is written 裾野. On its own, 麓 is read with the kun-yomi ふもと.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
貧乏binbou vs 貧困hinkon びん vs ひん 貧 is voiced ビン only in 貧乏; in 貧困, 貧血, 貧弱 it is ヒン. 乏 is ボウ (欠乏 けつぼう).
練るneru vs 粘るnebaru work out (a plan) vs hold out, persist 計画・対策を練る; 最後まで粘る. Both start with ね-, different kanji.
果てしないhateshinai vs 果実kajitsu kun は vs on カ 果 is は in 果て / 果てる / 果たす; カ in compounds (効果, 成果).
折衷secchuu vs 折り合いoriai blending (on + on) vs getting along (kun) Similar meaning, different reading system. 和洋折衷 (a blend of Japanese and Western) is a set phrase.
慎むtsutsushimu vs 慈しむitsukushimu refrain from vs cherish Both end in 〜しむ. 発言を慎む (watch what you say); 子を慈しむ (love one's child).
麓fumoto vs 裾野susono foot of a mountain vs lower slopes spreading out 麓 = the base area; 裾野 = the wide skirt-like slopes (also "breadth" of a field: 産業の裾野).
Exam trap: a single kanji standing alone (麓) or a kanji with verb okurigana (慎む, 練る) almost always takes the kun-yomi. Options built from the on-yomi plus a kana ending (しんむ, ろく on its own) are traps.
