Answer 2 (別冊 p.4, PDF 88). Book's notes (paraphrased): 披露宴 = ひろうえん. 披 = ヒ (披露); 露 has three readings — ロ (露骨, 露出, 暴露 ばくろ), ロウ (only in 披露) and つゆ (dew); 宴 = エン (宴会, 祝宴). Answer 1 (別冊 p.5, PDF 89). Book's notes (paraphrased): 費やす = ついやす; on-yomi ヒ／ピ as in 出費 (しゅっぴ), 交通費, 学費. 選択肢の言葉: 2 冷やす, 3 耕す, 4 燃やす. (added, not in book) ひやす is the trap because 費's on-yomi is ヒ — but the verb 費やす uses the kun-yomi つい. All four options end in 〜やす / 〜す, so the kanji itself must be known.

【11】

彼は辛い仕事を忍耐強く続けている。Kare wa tsurai shigoto o nintai-zuyoku tsuzukete iru.

EN: He keeps doing his hard job with great patience.

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
披露hirou vs 暴露・露骨bakuro · rokotsu ろう (long) vs ろ (short) 露 is long ロウ only in 披露; in 暴露, 露骨, 露出, 露店 it is short ロ.
冴えるsaeru vs 冷えるhieru be clear / bright vs get cold Both kanji have 冫. 頭が冴える (mind is sharp), 顔色が冴えない (look off-colour); 体が冷える (body gets cold).
半端hanpa vs 端hashi / hata ぱ after ん vs kun-yomi はし / はた 端 has four readings: タン (極端), はし (edge), は／ぱ (半端), はた (池の端). In 半端 it is ぱ.
中途chuuto vs 一日中ichinichijuu ちゅう vs じゅう 中 = じゅう when it means "throughout" (一日中, 年中, 町中); otherwise ちゅう.
費やすtsuiyasu vs 冷やすhiyasu spend vs cool 費's on-yomi is ヒ (出費), which makes ひやす tempting — but the verb uses つい.
忍耐nintai vs 人体jintai patience vs human body Same ending たい; the first kanji differs (忍 ニン / 人 ジン). 耐 stays unvoiced たい.
手際tegiwa vs 主催shusai kun + kun vs on + on 手 = て, 際 = ぎわ (like 窓際). しゅさい is a real word, but written 主催 / 主宰.
Exam trap: the second kanji of a native-style word often becomes voiced (際 → ぎわ in 手際 / 窓際), while on-yomi compounds usually keep their plain sound (耐 → たい in 忍耐). Don't apply the voicing rule automatically — check whether the word is kun + kun or on + on.
