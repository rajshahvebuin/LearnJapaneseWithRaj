1 Points — the words tested in this round

How to tackle 用法 (the book's tips, 前書き p.4 — summarised in our words)

用法 (youhou) gives one word and four sentences that all contain it; you choose the sentence where the word is used appropriately. The book's points:

  • It is a vocabulary question, not a grammar question. Judge whether the word's meaning fits the sentence, not whether the grammar is correct. HI: यह व्याकरण का नहीं, अर्थ का प्रश्न है — देखें कि शब्द का अर्थ वाक्य में बैठता है या नहीं। GU: આ વ્યાકરણનો નહીં, અર્થનો પ્રશ્ન છે — જુઓ કે શબ્દનો અર્થ વાક્યમાં બેસે છે કે નહીં.
  • Knowing the meaning alone is not enough. You must know how the word is used inside a sentence. HI: केवल अर्थ जानना काफ़ी नहीं; शब्द वाक्य में कैसे प्रयोग होता है, यह जानना ज़रूरी है। GU: માત્ર અર્થ જાણવો પૂરતો નથી; શબ્દ વાક્યમાં કેવી રીતે વપરાય છે તે જાણવું જરૂરી છે.
  • So learn words in sentences, not as bare translations. Pick an easy-to-remember example and memorise the whole sentence — e.g. for 手間 (tema), learn 「この仕事は手間がかかる」 (Kono shigoto wa tema ga kakaru.) "This job takes a lot of time and effort." HI: आसान उदाहरण-वाक्य चुनकर पूरा वाक्य याद करें। GU: સહેલું ઉદાહરણ-વાક્ય પસંદ કરી આખું વાક્ય યાદ કરો.
  • Katakana words are tested too. Even for loanwords, you need to know how they are used as Japanese words (see ダイエット in Q3). HI: कटाकाना (विदेशी) शब्द भी पूछे जाते हैं; उनका जापानी प्रयोग जानना ज़रूरी है। GU: કાતાકાના (વિદેશી) શબ્દો પણ પુછાય છે; તેનો જાપાની ઉપયોગ જાણવો જરૂરી છે.

(added, not in book) A practical check: every wrong sentence in this book would be correct with a different word. Ask "which word really belongs here?" — the 別冊 names exactly that word (正しい例文), and it is shown in every "Why" box below.

【1】

接客sekkyaku

Word: attending to customers or visitors
HI: ग्राहकों/मेहमानों से मिलना और उनकी सेवा करना
GU: ગ્રાહકો/મહેમાનોને મળીને સેવા કરવી
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1部長はただ今、接客中です。Buchou wa tadaima, sekkyaku-chuu desu.The department manager is with a visitor right now.विभाग प्रमुख अभी एक आगंतुक से मिल रहे हैं।વિભાગના વડા અત્યારે એક મુલાકાતીને મળી રહ્યા છે.
2ラッシュ時の車内は通勤、通学の接客でいっぱいだ。Rasshu-ji no shanai wa tsuukin, tsuugaku no sekkyaku de ippai da.At rush hour the train is packed with passengers going to work and school.भीड़ के समय डिब्बा काम और स्कूल जाने वाले यात्रियों से भरा रहता है।ભીડના સમયે ડબ્બો કામે અને શાળાએ જતા મુસાફરોથી ભરેલો હોય છે.
3今夜はアメリカからの接客があり、一緒に食事をします。Kon'ya wa Amerika kara no sekkyaku ga ari, issho ni shokuji o shimasu.Tonight we have a visitor from America and will have dinner together.आज रात अमेरिका से एक मेहमान आ रहे हैं और हम साथ खाना खाएँगे।આજે રાત્રે અમેરિકાથી એક મહેમાન આવે છે અને અમે સાથે જમીશું.
4新しい接客を開拓するための企画が社内で進められている。Atarashii sekkyaku o kaitaku suru tame no kikaku ga shanai de susumerarete iru.A plan to win new customers is under way inside the company.नए ग्राहक बनाने की एक योजना कंपनी में चल रही है।નવા ગ્રાહકો મેળવવાની એક યોજના કંપનીમાં ચાલી રહી છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 乗客 (joukyaku) "passenger(s)"; 3 → should be 来客 (raikyaku) "a visitor / guest"; 4 → should be 顧客 (kokyaku) "customers, clientele". (added, not in book) 接客 is an activity (receiving and serving people: 接客中, 接客業, 接客態度). 乗客, 来客 and 顧客 are the people themselves, so wherever the sentence needs a person, 接客 fails.

【2】

まぎらわしいmagirawashii

Word: confusingly similar, easily mixed up — 別冊: まぎらわしい = まちがえやすい・見分けがつかない (easy to mistake; can't tell apart)
HI: आसानी से भ्रम में डालने वाला, मिलता-जुलता
GU: સહેલાઈથી ગૂંચવણ ઊભી કરે તેવું, મળતું આવતું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1梅雨の間はまぎらわしい天気が続く。Tsuyu no aida wa magirawashii tenki ga tsuzuku.During the rainy season, gloomy weather goes on and on.बरसात के मौसम में उदास, भारी मौसम बना रहता है।ચોમાસામાં ઉદાસ, ભારે હવામાન ચાલુ રહે છે.
2この線には似たような名前の駅があって、まぎらわしい。Kono sen ni wa nita you na namae no eki ga atte, magirawashii.This line has stations with similar names, which is confusing.इस लाइन पर मिलते-जुलते नामों वाले स्टेशन हैं, जिससे भ्रम होता है।આ લાઇન પર મળતાં નામવાળાં સ્ટેશન છે, તેથી ગૂંચવણ થાય છે.
3今日の夕飯は品数が少なくて、なんかまぎらわしいなあ。Kyou no yuuhan wa shinakazu ga sukunakute, nanka magirawashii naa.Tonight's dinner has only a few dishes – it feels a bit unsatisfying.आज रात के खाने में व्यंजन कम हैं, कुछ अधूरा-सा लगता है।આજના રાતના ભોજનમાં વાનગીઓ ઓછી છે, થોડું અધૂરું લાગે છે.
4休みの日は、まぎらわしい仕事のことを忘れてのんびりしたい。Yasumi no hi wa, magirawashii shigoto no koto o wasurete nonbiri shitai.On my days off I want to forget my tiresome work and relax.छुट्टी के दिन मैं झंझट भरे काम को भूलकर आराम करना चाहता हूँ।રજાના દિવસે હું કંટાળાજનક કામ ભૂલીને આરામ કરવા માંગું છું.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). Book's definition: まぎらわしい = まちがえやすい・見分けがつかない (easy to mistake; can't tell apart). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be うっとうしい (uttoushii) "gloomy, oppressive"; 3 → should be 物足りない (monotarinai) "unsatisfying, not quite enough"; 4 → should be わずらわしい (wazurawashii) "troublesome, bothersome". (added, not in book) まぎらわしい always involves two or more things that look or sound alike (names, stations, signs). If nothing is being confused with anything else, it cannot be used.

【3】

ダイエットdaietto

Word: dieting (to lose weight)
HI: वज़न घटाने के लिए डाइटिंग
GU: વજન ઘટાડવા ડાયટિંગ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1健康のために、食事の量をダイエットすることにした。Kenkou no tame ni, shokuji no ryou o daietto suru koto ni shita.For my health, I decided to cut down the amount I eat.सेहत के लिए मैंने खाने की मात्रा घटाने का फ़ैसला किया।તંદુરસ્તી માટે મેં ખોરાકનું પ્રમાણ ઘટાડવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
2週に3回ジムに通って、体力をつけるダイエットをやっている。Shuu ni sankai jimu ni kayotte, tairyoku o tsukeru daietto o yatte iru.I go to the gym three times a week and do training to build stamina.मैं हफ़्ते में तीन बार जिम जाकर ताक़त बढ़ाने की ट्रेनिंग करता हूँ।હું અઠવાડિયે ત્રણ વાર જિમ જઈને તાકાત વધારવાની તાલીમ કરું છું.
3ダイエットに成功して5キロやせた。Daietto ni seikou shite go-kiro yaseta.My diet worked and I lost 5 kg.मेरी डाइटिंग सफल रही और मेरा 5 किलो वज़न घटा।મારું ડાયટિંગ સફળ થયું અને 5 કિલો વજન ઘટ્યું.
4毎日の食事のダイエットを計算する。Mainichi no shokuji no daietto o keisan suru.I count the calories in my daily meals.मैं रोज़ के खाने की कैलोरी गिनता हूँ।હું રોજના ભોજનની કેલરી ગણું છું.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 減らす (herasu) "to reduce"; 2 → should be トレーニング (toreeningu) "training, exercise"; 4 → should be カロリー (karorii) "calories". (added, not in book) In Japanese ダイエット means dieting to lose weight (ダイエットする・ダイエット中). It is not a verb for "reducing" something, nor the general English sense "what you eat".

見極めるmikiwameru

Word: to see through, judge accurately, get to the bottom of
HI: गहराई से परखना, ठीक-ठीक समझना
GU: ઊંડાણથી પારખવું, બરાબર સમજવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1経済の動向を見極めるのは証券会社にとっても難しいことだ。Keizai no doukou o mikiwameru no wa shouken-gaisha ni totte mo muzukashii koto da.Judging which way the economy is heading is hard even for securities firms.अर्थव्यवस्था का रुख़ ठीक से परखना ब्रोकरेज कंपनियों के लिए भी कठिन है।અર્થતંત્રનું વલણ બરાબર પારખવું શેર-દલાલ કંપનીઓ માટે પણ અઘરું છે.
2あなたも少しは隣のご主人を見極めて、家事を手伝ってください。Anata mo sukoshi wa tonari no goshujin o mikiwamete, kaji o tetsudatte kudasai.Follow the example of the husband next door a little and help with the housework.तुम भी पड़ोस वाले पति से कुछ सीखो और घर के काम में हाथ बँटाओ।તમે પણ પડોશના પતિ પાસેથી થોડું શીખો અને ઘરકામમાં મદદ કરો.
3伝統工芸の技を見極めようと地元の人が立ち上がった。Dentou kougei no waza o mikiwameyou to jimoto no hito ga tachiagatta.Local people stood up to preserve the skills of traditional crafts.पारंपरिक शिल्प की कला को बचाने के लिए स्थानीय लोग आगे आए।પરંપરાગત હસ્તકળાની કળા બચાવવા સ્થાનિક લોકો આગળ આવ્યા.
4この問題についてはできるだけ早く解決策を見極めてほしい。Kono mondai ni tsuite wa dekiru dake hayaku kaiketsusaku o mikiwamete hoshii.I want you to find a solution to this problem as soon as possible.मैं चाहता हूँ कि तुम इस समस्या का हल जल्द से जल्द ढूँढो।હું ઈચ્છું છું કે તમે આ સમસ્યાનો ઉકેલ શક્ય તેટલો જલદી શોધો.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 見習って (minaratte) "follow the example of"; 3 → should be 残そう (nokosou) "(try to) preserve"; 4 → should be 見出して / 見つけて (miidashite / mitsukete) "find". (added, not in book) 見極める takes something uncertain or hidden as its object — 動向・本質・真偽・実力 — and means judging it correctly after careful observation. It is not "find" and not "copy".

【5】

とかくtokaku

Word: (people/things) tend to, are apt to (usually something undesirable) — 別冊: とかく = 一般的に（〜する傾向がある）。よくない傾向に使う (generally (tend to …); used for bad tendencies)
HI: अक्सर (बुरी प्रवृत्ति के लिए), आम तौर पर
GU: ઘણી વાર (ખરાબ વલણ માટે), સામાન્ય રીતે
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1いくら頑張っても、とかく結果は見えている。Ikura ganbatte mo, tokaku kekka wa miete iru.No matter how hard you try, the result is obvious anyway.चाहे कितनी भी कोशिश करो, नतीजा तो वैसे भी साफ़ है।ગમે તેટલો પ્રયત્ન કરો, પરિણામ તો આમેય દેખાય છે.
2病人の具合はとかくましになったが、まだ回復したとは言えない。Byounin no guai wa tokaku mashi ni natta ga, mada kaifuku shita to wa ienai.The patient is somewhat better, but can't yet be called recovered.मरीज़ की हालत कुछ बेहतर हुई है, पर अभी ठीक हुआ नहीं कहा जा सकता।દર્દીની હાલત થોડી સુધરી છે, પણ હજી સાજો થયો એમ ન કહી શકાય.
3やけどをしたら、とかく早く冷水で冷やすことが大事だ。Yakedo o shitara, tokaku hayaku reisui de hiyasu koto ga daiji da.If you get burned, the important thing, whatever else, is to cool it quickly with cold water.जल जाने पर सबसे ज़रूरी है कि जल्दी से ठंडे पानी से ठंडा करो।દાઝી જાઓ તો સૌથી અગત્યનું એ છે કે જલદી ઠંડા પાણીથી ઠંડું કરો.
4人は、とかく間違いをしやすいものだ。Hito wa, tokaku machigai o shiyasui mono da.People are apt to make mistakes.इंसान से अक्सर गलतियाँ हो ही जाती हैं।માણસથી ઘણી વાર ભૂલ થઈ જ જાય છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). Book's definition: とかく = 一般的に（〜する傾向がある）。よくない傾向に使う (generally (tend to …); used for bad tendencies). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be どうせ (douse) "anyway (resigned)"; 2 → should be 多少 / 幾分 (tashou / ikubun) "somewhat, a little"; 3 → should be とにかく (tonikaku) "at any rate, above all". (added, not in book) とかく usually pairs with tendency endings: 〜しがちだ・〜しやすい・〜ものだ. Option 4 has both (しやすい + ものだ).

【6】

決め手kimete

Word: the deciding factor, clincher — 別冊: 決め手 = 決めるときの重要なポイント (the key point when deciding something)
HI: निर्णायक बात/कारक
GU: નિર્ણાયક મુદ્દો/પરિબળ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1ここで入れる、この少しの砂糖が味の決め手です。Koko de ireru, kono sukoshi no satou ga aji no kimete desu.This little bit of sugar added here is what makes the flavour.यहाँ डाली जाने वाली यह थोड़ी-सी चीनी ही स्वाद की असली कुंजी है।અહીં નાખવામાં આવતી આ થોડી ખાંડ જ સ્વાદની ખરી ચાવી છે.
2会議での決め手に従い、新商品の開発スケジュールを見直した。Kaigi de no kimete ni shitagai, shin-shouhin no kaihatsu sukejuuru o minaoshita.Following the decision made at the meeting, we revised the new product's development schedule.बैठक के फ़ैसले के अनुसार नए उत्पाद की विकास-समय-सारणी दोबारा तय की गई।બેઠકના નિર્ણય મુજબ નવા ઉત્પાદનનું વિકાસ-સમયપત્રક ફરી ગોઠવાયું.
3世間が注目していた裁判の決め手は、無罪だった。Seken ga chuumoku shite ita saiban no kimete wa, muzai datta.The verdict in the trial everyone had been watching was "not guilty".जिस मुक़दमे पर सबकी नज़र थी, उसका फ़ैसला "निर्दोष" रहा।જે કેસ પર બધાની નજર હતી તેનો ચુકાદો "નિર્દોષ" આવ્યો.
4社長は、海外の市場から撤退するという決め手を下した。Shachou wa, kaigai no shijou kara tettai suru to iu kimete o kudashita.The president made the decision to withdraw from overseas markets.अध्यक्ष ने विदेशी बाज़ारों से हटने का निर्णय लिया।પ્રમુખે વિદેશી બજારોમાંથી ખસી જવાનો નિર્ણય લીધો.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.85–86, PDF 167–168). Book's definition: 決め手 = 決めるときの重要なポイント (the key point when deciding something). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 決定 (kettei) "decision"; 3 → should be 判決 (hanketsu) "verdict, judgment"; 4 → should be 決定 (kettei) "decision (決定を下す)". (added, not in book) 決め手 is not the decision itself but the thing that settles it (味の決め手, 逮捕の決め手, 決め手に欠ける). A decision someone makes is 決定 / 決断; a court's decision is 判決.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
接客sekkyaku / 乗客joukyaku / 来客raikyaku / 顧客kokyaku attending to customers or visitors / passenger(s) / a visitor / guest / customers, clientele 接客 = the act of serving people (接客中・接客業); 乗客 = people riding a vehicle; 来客 = someone who comes to visit you; 顧客 = a business's regular customers (顧客を開拓する).
まぎらわしいmagirawashii / うっとうしいuttoushii / 物足りないmonotarinai / わずらわしいwazurawashii confusingly similar, easily mixed up / gloomy, oppressive / unsatisfying, not quite enough / troublesome, bothersome まぎらわしい = hard to tell A from B; わずらわしい = a bother (手続き・人間関係); うっとうしい = gloomy/annoying (梅雨の天気・前髪); 物足りない = leaves you wanting more. まぎらわしい and わずらわしい look alike — see Q49, where the book swaps them the other way.
ダイエットdaietto / 減らすherasu / トレーニングtoreeningu / カロリーkarorii dieting (to lose weight) / to reduce / training, exercise / calories ダイエット = slimming by controlling food; トレーニング = physical training; カロリー = energy value of food; 減らす = to reduce (an amount). A katakana trap: English "diet" ≠ Japanese ダイエット.
見極めるmikiwameru / 見習ってminaratte / 残そうnokosou / 見出して / 見つけてmiidashite / mitsukete to see through, judge accurately, get to the bottom of / follow the example of / (try to) preserve / find 見極める = judge correctly what is uncertain (動向・本質); 見習う = learn by imitating a person; 残す = keep, preserve; 見出す・見つける = find (a solution).
とかくtokaku / どうせdouse / 多少 / 幾分tashou / ikubun / とにかくtonikaku (people/things) tend to, are apt to (usually something undesirable) / anyway (resigned) / somewhat, a little / at any rate, above all とかく = tend to (bad habits: とかく〜がちだ); とにかく = anyway / first of all (urging action); どうせ = it's no use anyway; 多少・幾分 = to some degree. とかく and とにかく look alike but do different jobs.
決め手kimete / 決定kettei / 判決hanketsu / 決定kettei the deciding factor, clincher / decision / verdict, judgment / decision (決定を下す) 決め手 = the factor that settles something; 決定 = a decision (決定を下す・決定に従う); 判決 = a court verdict (判決が下る). 決め手に欠ける = nothing decisive.
Exam trap: in 用法 the four sentences often share one theme word (決める, 客, 見る…). The wrong options are built from real near-neighbours — 決め手/決定/判決, 接客/乗客/来客/顧客 — so check whether the sentence needs the act, the person, the result or the deciding factor.
