【55】

廃れるsutareru

Word: to go out of use or fashion, decline — 別冊: 廃れる = （流行、習慣、言葉、考え方、制度などが）行われなくなる ((fashions, customs, words, ideas, systems) stop being practised)
HI: चलन से बाहर हो जाना
GU: ચલણમાંથી નીકળી જવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1子どものころに気に入っていた絵本は廃れて、色があせている。Kodomo no koro ni ki ni itte ita ehon wa sutarete, iro ga asete iru.The picture book I loved as a child has grown old and its colours have faded.बचपन की पसंदीदा चित्र-पुस्तक पुरानी होकर फीकी पड़ गई है।બાળપણની મનપસંદ ચિત્રપોથી જૂની થઈને ઝાંખી પડી ગઈ છે.
2流行語は廃れるのも早い。Ryuukougo wa sutareru no mo hayai.Buzzwords also go out of use quickly.चलताऊ शब्द जल्दी ही चलन से बाहर भी हो जाते हैं।ફૅશનના શબ્દો જલદી ચલણમાંથી નીકળી પણ જાય છે.
3毎日大量のごみが廃れている。Mainichi tairyou no gomi ga sutarete iru.Huge amounts of rubbish are thrown away every day.रोज़ भारी मात्रा में कचरा फेंका जाता है।રોજ મોટા પ્રમાણમાં કચરો ફેંકાય છે.
4事故を起こした発電所は廃れることになった。Jiko o okoshita hatsudensho wa sutareru koto ni natta.It has been decided that the power plant that had the accident will be shut down.दुर्घटना वाले बिजलीघर को बंद करने का फ़ैसला हुआ।અકસ્માતવાળું વીજમથક બંધ કરવાનો નિર્ણય લેવાયો.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: 廃れる = （流行、習慣、言葉、考え方、制度などが）行われなくなる ((fashions, customs, words, ideas, systems) stop being practised). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 古びて (furubite) "growing old (古びる)"; 3 → should be 捨てられて (suterarete) "being thrown away"; 4 → should be 廃止される (haishi sareru) "be abolished, shut down". (added, not in book) 廃れる is for intangible things that people stop using or doing: 流行・習慣・言葉・技術・制度. A physical object that gets old is 古びる; a facility closed by decision is 廃止される.

【56】

くちずさむkuchizusamu

Word: to hum, sing softly to oneself — 別冊: くちずさむ = 自分ひとりで小さい声で歌う (sing alone in a low voice)
HI: गुनगुनाना
GU: ગણગણવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1酔っ払い同士がけんかしていて、お互いをひどくくちずさんでいた。Yopparai doushi ga kenka shite ite, otagai o hidoku kuchizusande ita.Two drunks were fighting and calling each other terrible names.दो शराबी लड़ रहे थे और एक-दूसरे को बुरी तरह गालियाँ दे रहे थे।બે દારૂડિયા લડતા હતા અને એકબીજાને ખરાબ રીતે ગાળો દેતા હતા.
2早朝の公園を散歩すると、小鳥たちがくちずさむのが聞こえる。Souchou no kouen o sanpo suru to, kotoritachi ga kuchizusamu no ga kikoeru.If you walk in the park early in the morning, you can hear little birds singing.सुबह-सुबह पार्क में टहलो तो चिड़ियों का चहचहाना सुनाई देता है।વહેલી સવારે બગીચામાં ફરો તો પંખીઓનો કલરવ સંભળાય છે.
3気が付くといつもくちずさんでいる歌がある。Ki ga tsuku to itsumo kuchizusande iru uta ga aru.There's a song I always catch myself humming.एक गाना है जिसे मैं अनजाने में हमेशा गुनगुनाता रहता हूँ।એક ગીત છે જે હું અજાણતાં હંમેશાં ગણગણતો રહું છું.
4つまずいて、持っていた資料を床にくちずさんでしまった。Tsumazuite, motte ita shiryou o yuka ni kuchizusande shimatta.I tripped and scattered the papers I was carrying all over the floor.मैं ठोकर खा गया और हाथ के काग़ज़ फ़र्श पर बिखर गए।હું ઠોકર ખાઈ ગયો અને હાથના કાગળો ફરસ પર વેરાઈ ગયા.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: くちずさむ = 自分ひとりで小さい声で歌う (sing alone in a low voice). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be ののしって (nonoshitte) "hurling abuse (ののしる)"; 2 → should be さえずる (saezuru) "(birds) chirp, sing"; 4 → should be ばらまいて (baramaite) "scattering (ばらまく)". (added, not in book) くちずさむ (口ずさむ) is a person humming or singing quietly, often without noticing. Birds さえずる; angry people ののしる; papers are ばらまかれる.

【57】

前途zento

Word: one's future, prospects — 別冊: 前途 = これから先・将来 (from now on; the future)
HI: भविष्य, आगे का रास्ता
GU: ભવિષ્ય, આગળનો માર્ગ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1高校を卒業してからの前途を決めなければならない。Koukou o sotsugyou shite kara no zento o kimenakereba naranai.I have to decide on my path after I finish high school.मुझे तय करना है कि हाई स्कूल के बाद कौन-सा रास्ता चुनूँ।મારે નક્કી કરવું છે કે હાઈસ્કૂલ પછી કયો માર્ગ લઉં.
2その船の前途は依然として不明のままである。Sono fune no zento wa izen to shite fumei no mama de aru.The ship's whereabouts are still unknown.उस जहाज़ का अब तक कोई पता नहीं है।તે જહાજનો હજી કોઈ પત્તો નથી.
3彼は前途有望な若者である。Kare wa zento yuubou na wakamono de aru.He is a young man with a promising future.वह उज्ज्वल भविष्य वाला युवक है।તે ઉજ્જવળ ભવિષ્યવાળો યુવાન છે.
4プロジェクトの前途を上司に報告する。Purojekuto no zento o joushi ni houkoku suru.I report the progress of the project to my boss.मैं परियोजना की प्रगति बॉस को बताता हूँ।હું પ્રોજેક્ટની પ્રગતિ બૉસને જણાવું છું.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: 前途 = これから先・将来 (from now on; the future). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 進路 (shinro) "one's course (after school)"; 2 → should be 行方 (yukue) "whereabouts"; 4 → should be 途中経過 / 進捗状況 (tochuu keika / shinchoku joukyou) "interim progress / status". (added, not in book) 前途 is mostly met in set phrases: 前途有望 (promising), 前途多難 (difficult road ahead), 前途洋々 (bright future). A concrete choice of school or job is 進路.

【58】

カムバックするkamubakku suru

Word: to make a comeback — 別冊: カムバックする = 復帰する (to return to one's former position)
HI: वापसी करना
GU: પુનરાગમન કરવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1芸能界を一度引退して数年後にカムバックする人も多い。Geinoukai o ichido intai shite suunengo ni kamubakku suru hito mo ooi.Many people retire from show business and then make a comeback a few years later.बहुत लोग मनोरंजन जगत से एक बार संन्यास लेकर कुछ साल बाद वापसी करते हैं।ઘણા લોકો મનોરંજન જગતમાંથી એક વાર નિવૃત્ત થઈ થોડાં વર્ષ પછી પુનરાગમન કરે છે.
2この町を一度訪れた人は必ずカムバックしたくなるという。Kono machi o ichido otozureta hito wa kanarazu kamubakku shitaku naru to iu.They say anyone who visits this town once always wants to come back again.कहते हैं जो इस शहर में एक बार आता है, वह फिर से आना चाहता है।કહે છે કે જે આ શહેરમાં એક વાર આવે છે તે ફરી આવવા ઇચ્છે છે.
3電車は終点の駅からカムバックして運行します。Densha wa shuuten no eki kara kamubakku shite unkou shimasu.The train turns back at the terminal station and runs in the other direction.ट्रेन अंतिम स्टेशन से वापस मुड़कर चलती है।ટ્રેન છેલ્લા સ્ટેશનથી પાછી વળીને દોડે છે.
4脳は使わないと、日に日にカムバックしていく。Nou wa tsukawanai to, hi ni hi ni kamubakku shite iku.If you don't use your brain, it gets weaker day by day.दिमाग़ का इस्तेमाल न करो तो वह दिन-ब-दिन कमज़ोर होता जाता है।મગજ ન વાપરો તો તે દિવસે-દિવસે નબળું પડતું જાય છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: カムバックする = 復帰する (to return to one's former position). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 再訪したく (saihou shitaku) "want to visit again"; 3 → should be 折り返して (orikaeshite) "turning back"; 4 → should be 衰えて (otoroete) "declining". (added, not in book) カムバックする means returning to one's former activity or position after being away (芸能界・試合・政界にカムバック). It does not mean "come back" to a place or "turn back".

【59】

同然douzen

Word: as good as, virtually the same as — 別冊: 同然 = 同様 (the same)
HI: लगभग वैसा ही, के बराबर
GU: લગભગ એવું જ, બરાબર
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1社会人だったら、そんなことは知っていて同然だよ。Shakaijin dattara, sonna koto wa shitte ite douzen da yo.If you're a working adult, you ought to know that, naturally.कामकाजी वयस्क हो तो यह जानना स्वाभाविक है।કામકાજી પુખ્ત હો તો આ જાણવું સ્વાભાવિક છે.
2友人から譲ってもらった中古のピアノは新品同然だった。Yuujin kara yuzutte moratta chuuko no piano wa shinpin douzen datta.The second-hand piano my friend passed on to me was as good as new.दोस्त से मिला पुराना पियानो बिल्कुल नए जैसा था।મિત્ર પાસેથી મળેલો જૂનો પિયાનો બિલકુલ નવા જેવો હતો.
3彼女の行方は同然としてわからないままだ。Kanojo no yukue wa douzen to shite wakaranai mama da.Her whereabouts are still unknown.उसका अब भी कोई पता नहीं है।તેનો હજી પણ કોઈ પત્તો નથી.
4最年長の彼女が同然にグループのまとめ役になった。Sainenchou no kanojo ga douzen ni guruupu no matomeyaku ni natta.As the eldest, she naturally became the group's coordinator.सबसे बड़ी होने से वह स्वाभाविक रूप से समूह की संयोजक बन गई।સૌથી મોટી હોવાથી તે સ્વાભાવિક રીતે જૂથની સંયોજક બની.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: 同然 = 同様 (the same). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 当然 (touzen) "natural, of course"; 3 → should be 依然 (izen) "still (依然として)"; 4 → should be 自然（に） (shizen (ni)) "naturally, of its own accord". (added, not in book) 同然 follows a noun: X同然 = practically X (新品同然・ただ同然・家族同然). The other three are 〜然 words with different jobs; 依然 was tested in Q22.

【60】

漠然（と）bakuzen (to)

Word: vague(ly), hazy — 別冊: 漠然（と） = ぼんやりしていてはっきりしない (dim and not clear)
HI: धुंधला, अस्पष्ट रूप से
GU: ઝાંખું, અસ્પષ્ટ રીતે
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1彼は大勢の人の前でも漠然と話すことができる。Kare wa oozei no hito no mae de mo bakuzen to hanasu koto ga dekiru.He can speak with confidence even in front of a crowd.वह भीड़ के सामने भी आत्मविश्वास से बोल सकता है।તે ભીડ સામે પણ આત્મવિશ્વાસથી બોલી શકે છે.
2計画は予定通りに漠然と進んでいる。Keikaku wa yotei doori ni bakuzen to susunde iru.The plan is progressing steadily, on schedule.योजना तय समय के अनुसार लगातार आगे बढ़ रही है।યોજના નક્કી સમય મુજબ સતત આગળ વધે છે.
3友達に悩みを聞いてもらったら、気分が漠然とした。Tomodachi ni nayami o kiite morattara, kibun ga bakuzen to shita.After a friend listened to my worries, I felt refreshed.दोस्त ने मेरी परेशानी सुनी तो मन हल्का हो गया।મિત્રએ મારી મૂંઝવણ સાંભળી તો મન હળવું થઈ ગયું.
4この言葉の意味は漠然としていてわかりにくい。Kono kotoba no imi wa bakuzen to shite ite wakarinikui.The meaning of this word is vague and hard to grasp.इस शब्द का अर्थ अस्पष्ट है और समझना मुश्किल है।આ શબ્દનો અર્થ અસ્પષ્ટ છે અને સમજવો અઘરો છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.95–96, PDF 177–178). Book's definition: 漠然（と） = ぼんやりしていてはっきりしない (dim and not clear). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 堂々と (doudou to) "confidently, boldly"; 2 → should be 着々と (chakuchaku to) "steadily"; 3 → should be すっきり (sukkiri) "refreshed, clear". (added, not in book) 漠然 describes ideas, memories and plans that are unclear (漠然とした不安・漠然と覚えている). The wrong options swap in other 〜と adverbs of manner.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
廃れるsutareru / 古びてfurubite / 捨てられてsuterarete / 廃止されるhaishi sareru to go out of use or fashion, decline / growing old (古びる) / being thrown away / be abolished, shut down 廃れる = (custom, fashion) dies out naturally; 廃止する = abolish by decision; 古びる = (object) get old and worn; 捨てる = throw away. 廃れる and 廃止 share 廃 (abandon) — natural decline vs official decision.
くちずさむkuchizusamu / ののしってnonoshitte / さえずるsaezuru / ばらまいてbaramaite to hum, sing softly to oneself / hurling abuse (ののしる) / (birds) chirp, sing / scattering (ばらまく) くちずさむ = hum to oneself; さえずる = (birds) chirp; ののしる = swear at; ばらまく = scatter. All N1 verbs of sound or spreading; only くちずさむ is singing by a person.
前途zento / 進路shinro / 行方yukue / 途中経過 / 進捗状況tochuu keika / shinchoku joukyou one's future, prospects / one's course (after school) / whereabouts / interim progress / status 前途 = the future in general (前途有望); 進路 = the course you choose (進路を決める); 行方 = whereabouts; 進捗 = progress of work. 前途 and 途中 share 途 (road).
カムバックするkamubakku suru / 再訪したくsaihou shitaku / 折り返してorikaeshite / 衰えてotoroete to make a comeback / want to visit again / turning back / declining カムバック = comeback to former activity (= 復帰); 再訪 = visit again; 折り返す = turn back (train, reply); 衰える = decline. A katakana word with a narrower meaning than English "come back".
同然douzen / 当然touzen / 依然izen / 自然（に）shizen (ni) as good as, virtually the same as / natural, of course / still (依然として) / naturally, of its own accord 同然 = as good as (noun + 同然); 当然 = of course; 依然として = still; 自然に = naturally, spontaneously. Four 〜然 words — 同 (same), 当 (proper), 依 (rely, unchanged), 自 (self).
漠然（と）bakuzen (to) / 堂々とdoudou to / 着々とchakuchaku to / すっきりsukkiri vague(ly), hazy / confidently, boldly / steadily / refreshed, clear 漠然と = vaguely; 堂々と = confidently; 着々と = steadily (compare 着実, Q41); すっきり = refreshed, neat. 漠然 is negative-leaning (unclear); すっきり is the opposite feeling.
Exam trap: the last round is full of 〜然 words (同然・当然・依然・自然・漠然) and katakana with a narrower Japanese meaning (カムバック = 復帰, not "come back" to a place). For 〜然 words, learn the frame each one lives in: 新品同然, 当然だ, 依然として, 自然に, 漠然とした.
