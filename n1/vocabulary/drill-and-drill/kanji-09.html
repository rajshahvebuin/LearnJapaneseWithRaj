1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.16–17)

2 Every question — 問題 (p.22)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

GU: અમારી સતત જીતને અટકાવે એવો એક મજબૂત હરીફ આવ્યો છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.16, PDF 98). Book's notes (paraphrased): 阻む = はばむ (block, prevent); on-yomi ソ as in 阻止, 阻害. 選択肢の言葉: 1 拒む, 3 危ぶむ, 4 慎む. 3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
阻むhabamu vs 拒むkobamu block vs refuse 行く手・連勝を阻む (stand in the way); 要求・申し出を拒む (say no). Both 〜ばむ.
慕うshitau vs 募るtsunoru look up to vs call for Same top part 莫. 先輩を慕う; ボランティアを募る (第6回 Q32).
炊事suiji vs 家事kaji cooking vs housework 炊事 is one part of 家事. 炊 = 火 + 欠 (スイ, たく).
背くsomuku vs 背けるsomukeru disobey vs turn away 命令・期待に背く (go against); 目・顔を背ける (avert one's eyes / face).
暴露bakuro vs 暴力bouryoku バク vs ボウ 暴 = バク only in 暴露. Kun-yomi: 暴く あばく (expose), 暴れる あばれる (go wild).
闇yami vs 暗闇kurayami darkness vs pitch darkness 闇 also means "illegal / shady": 闇市 (black market), 闇取引 (shady deal).
Exam trap: when the kanji is built from parts that are kanji themselves (闇 = 門 + 音; 炊 = 火 + 欠; 背 = 北 + 月), the distractors are often the readings of those parts. Don't read a kanji by its pieces.
