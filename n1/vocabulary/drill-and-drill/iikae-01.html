1 Points — how to attack 言い換え類義, and the words of this round

The book's advice for this question type (前書き p.4, summarised in our words)

In 言い換え類義 (iikae ruigi) you pick the option whose meaning is the same as, or closest to, the underlined word in the sentence. You can only answer if you know both the underlined word and the four options — so this type tests breadth. The authors' study tip: most learners make word cards or a vocabulary notebook and write the meaning in their mother tongue; instead (or as well), write the meaning in other Japanese — a synonym or a short Japanese definition. That way one card teaches you two or three words at once, which is the most efficient way to grow your vocabulary for this question type.

(added, not in book) Practical reading order for the exam: read the whole sentence first, decide the underlined word's meaning in this context (several words in this section are polysemous — e.g. タイトル = title or job title), then test each option in the sentence. Options are often real words that share a kanji or a sound with the underlined word.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.56)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【1】

我が軍の資金はふんだんにある。Waga gun no shikin wa fundan ni aru.

Underlined word: ふんだんに = 使いきれないほどたくさんある様子 (so much you could not use it all).
EN: Our side has funds in abundance.
HI: हमारे पक्ष के पास धन भरपूर मात्रा में है।
GU: અમારા પક્ષ પાસે ભંડોળ ભરપૂર છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1若干jakkana few, some (not many)थोड़ा, कुछથોડું, કેટલુંક
2多少tashousomewhat, a littleकुछ-कुछ, थोड़ा-बहुतથોડુંઘણું
3おおまかにoomaka niroughly, in broad outlineमोटे तौर परસ્થૂળ રીતે, આશરે
4たっぷりtappuriplenty, amplyभरपूरભરપૂર

Answer 4 (別冊 p.73, PDF 155). Book's notes (paraphrased): ふんだんに = たっぷり, more than can be used up. 選択肢の言葉: 1 若干 = an amount you cannot state exactly but which is not large / a little (例: 募集人数、若干名 (boshuu ninzuu, jakkan-mei) "a few positions open"; 先日提出した報告書を若干修正します (senjitsu teishutsu shita houkokusho o jakkan shuusei shimasu)); 3 おおまかに = roughly, dealing only with the main points (例: 当社の業務内容をおおまかにお話しします (tousha no gyoumu naiyou o oomaka ni o-hanashi shimasu) — details are in the handout). The 別冊 has no note for 2 多少. (added, not in book) 若干 and 多少 both point to a small amount — the opposite of ふんだんに; おおまかに describes how you explain something, not how much money there is.

【2】

彼女のおせっかいにはうんざりだ。Kanojo no osekkai ni wa unzari da.

Underlined word: おせっかい = 他人のことに不必要に立ち入ること (poking into other people's business when not needed).
EN: I am fed up with her meddling.
HI: मैं उसकी दख़लंदाज़ी से तंग आ गया हूँ।
GU: હું તેની દખલગીરીથી કંટાળી ગયો છું.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1無作法busahoubad manners, rudenessबदतमीज़ी, अशिष्टताઅસભ્યતા, બેઅદબી
2干渉kanshouinterference, meddlingहस्तक्षेप, दख़लદખલ, હસ્તક્ષેપ
3説教sekkyousermon; lecturing, preaching at someoneउपदेश; भाषण पिलानाઉપદેશ; ભાષણ આપવું
4強情goujouobstinacy, stubbornnessहठ, ज़िदહઠ, જીદ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.73, PDF 155). Book's notes (paraphrased): おせっかい = 干渉, interfering in others' affairs unnecessarily. 選択肢の言葉: 1 無作法(な) = out of line with proper manners, impolite (例: あの人、無作法だから、好きになれないわ (ano hito, busahou da kara, suki ni narenai wa)); 3 説教／お説教 = ① a talk a religious leader gives to guide people (例: 日曜日は教会で牧師さんの説教を聞く (nichiyoubi wa kyoukai de bokushi-san no sekkyou o kiku)) ② a stiff moralising lecture (例: お父さんのお説教が始まったよ。今日もきっと長いよ (otousan no o-sekkyou ga hajimatta yo. Kyou mo kitto nagai yo)); 4 強情(な) = stubbornly refusing to change one's mind (例: うちの娘は強情だから、一度言い出したら、なかなか考えを変えないんですよ (uchi no musume wa goujou da kara, ichido iidashitara, nakanaka kangae o kaenai n desu yo)). (added, not in book) All four are unpleasant traits, but only 干渉 is about stepping into someone else's affairs, which is the core of おせっかい.

【3】

旅行のお土産はかさばらないものがいい。Ryokou no omiyage wa kasabaranai mono ga ii.

Underlined word: かさばる = 体積が大きくて場所を取る (large in volume, takes up space); かさばらない = the negative.
EN: For travel souvenirs, things that aren't bulky are best.
HI: यात्रा के उपहार ऐसे हों जो ज़्यादा जगह न घेरें, वही अच्छे हैं।
GU: પ્રવાસની ભેટસોગાદો એવી સારી જે વધુ જગ્યા ન રોકે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1場所を取らないbasho o toranaidoes not take up spaceजगह नहीं घेरताજગ્યા રોકતું નથી
2持ちやすいmochiyasuieasy to carry / holdउठाने में आसानઊંચકવામાં સહેલું
3壊れにくいkowarenikuihard to break, sturdyआसानी से नहीं टूटताસહેલાઈથી તૂટતું નથી
4重くないomokunainot heavyभारी नहींભારે નહીં

Answer 1 (別冊 p.73, PDF 155). Book's notes (paraphrased): かさばる = large in bulk so it takes space / too big to handle easily; so かさばらない = 場所を取らない. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) かさ (嵩) is volume, not weight — a big box of light biscuits かさばる but is 重くない (4 is a different property). 2 and 3 are about handling and durability, not size.

【4】

彼女は気まぐれな性格で、人に迷惑をかけることが多い。Kanojo wa kimagure na seikaku de, hito ni meiwaku o kakeru koto ga ooi.

Underlined word: 気まぐれな = 考えや行動がそのときそのときで変わる (ideas and actions change from moment to moment).
EN: She has a capricious nature and often causes trouble for others.
HI: वह मनमौजी स्वभाव की है और अक्सर दूसरों को परेशानी में डालती है।
GU: તે મનમોજી સ્વભાવની છે અને ઘણી વાર બીજાને તકલીફમાં મૂકે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1まじめ過ぎるmajime sugirutoo seriousज़रूरत से ज़्यादा गंभीरવધુ પડતું ગંભીર
2思いつきで行動するomoitsuki de koudou suruacts on impulse / on a whimमन में जो आए उस पर चल पड़नाમનમાં આવે તેમ વર્તવું
3怒りっぽいokorippoiquick-temperedगुस्सैलગુસ્સાવાળું, ચીડિયું
4落ち着きがないochitsuki ga nairestless, cannot settle downबेचैन, चंचलબેચેન, ચંચળ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.73, PDF 155–156). Book's notes (paraphrased): 気まぐれな = acting on whatever occurs to you at the time; one's thinking and behaviour keep changing. 選択肢の言葉: 4 落ち着きがない = cannot stay still, gives a feeling of unease (例: あの子はいつも落ち着きがなくて、授業中もじっとすわっていられない (ano ko wa itsumo ochitsuki ga nakute, jugyouchuu mo jitto suwatte irarenai); the opposite — 彼、就職したら落ち着きが出て、大人っぽくなったね (kare, shuushoku shitara ochitsuki ga dete, otonappoku natta ne)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 3. (added, not in book) 落ち着きがない is the near-miss: it is about physical/mental restlessness, whereas 気まぐれ is about changing one's mind and acting on sudden ideas.

【5】

2人は双方の家族にお互いを紹介し合った間柄だ。Futari wa souhou no kazoku ni otagai o shoukai shiatta aidagara da.

Underlined word: 間柄 = (人と人の)関係 (the relationship between people).
EN: The two are in a relationship where they have introduced each other to both families.
HI: दोनों का रिश्ता ऐसा है कि उन्होंने एक-दूसरे को अपने-अपने परिवारों से मिलवा दिया है।
GU: બંનેનો સંબંધ એવો છે કે તેમણે એકબીજાને પોતપોતાના પરિવાર સાથે ઓળખાણ કરાવી દીધી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1間隔kankakuinterval, spacingअंतराल, फ़ासलाઅંતર, ગાળો
2関連kanrenconnection, relatedness (between things)संबद्धता, जुड़ावસંબદ્ધતા, જોડાણ
3関係kankeirelationshipरिश्ता, संबंधસંબંધ
4縁ena special bond, fate that ties peopleविशेष बंधन, नाताખાસ બંધન, ઋણાનુબંધ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.74, PDF 156). Book's notes (paraphrased): 間柄 = the relationship between people. 選択肢の言葉: 4 縁 = a special, strong tie (例: あの2人は不思議な縁で知り合って、とうとう結婚することになったそうだ (ano futari wa fushigi na en de shiriatte, toutou kekkon suru koto ni natta sou da)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 間隔 shares the kanji 間 but means a gap in space or time (5分間隔); 関連 is for links between things or topics (関連記事); 縁 is a poetic "bond of fate", too strong for a neutral statement of what their relationship is.

【6】

この論文にはタイトルがない。Kono ronbun ni wa taitoru ga nai.

Underlined word: タイトル = 題名／肩書き (title of a work / a person's title).
EN: This paper has no title.
HI: इस शोध-पत्र का कोई शीर्षक नहीं है।
GU: આ શોધપત્રનું કોઈ શીર્ષક નથી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1主題shudaitheme, main subjectमुख्य विषय, थीमમુખ્ય વિષય, થીમ
2題名daimeititleशीर्षकશીર્ષક
3論旨ronshithe main point / line of argumentतर्क का सार, मुख्य बिंदुદલીલનો સાર, મુખ્ય મુદ્દો
4要旨youshigist, summary of contentsसारांशસારાંશ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.74, PDF 156). Book's notes (paraphrased): タイトル = 題名 (or 肩書き). 選択肢の言葉: 1 主題 = テーマ (例: 家族の絆がこの作品の主題です (kazoku no kizuna ga kono sakuhin no shudai desu)); 3 論旨 = the important points of a paper or argument (例: あなたのこの論文は論旨が明快ではありませんね。ポイントを絞って書き直してください (anata no kono ronbun wa ronshi ga meikai de wa arimasen ne. Pointo o shibotte kakinaoshite kudasai)); 4 要旨 = the general content (例: 長い文章の要旨を短くまとめた (nagai bunshou no youshi o mijikaku matometa)). (added, not in book) 1, 3 and 4 are all about what a paper says; only 題名 is the label written at the top — exactly what タイトル means here.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
ふんだんにfundan ni / たっぷりtappuri / 若干jakkan / 多少tashou abundantly / amply / a few / somewhat ふんだんに・たっぷり = a lot (資金・材料がふんだんにある; 時間はたっぷりある). 若干・多少 = a little (若干名; 多少高い). Opposite poles — don't let "quantity" lump them together.
おせっかいosekkai / 干渉kanshou / 無作法busahou / 説教sekkyou / 強情goujou meddling / interference / rudeness / lecturing / obstinacy おせっかい (everyday, often well-meant) ≈ 干渉 (formal; also 内政干渉 "interference in internal affairs"). 無作法 breaks etiquette; 説教 talks at you; 強情 won't budge — none of them enter your business.
かさばるkasabaru / 重いomoi bulky (volume) / heavy (weight) かさばる荷物 can be light; 重い荷物 can be small. The question's 場所を取らない targets volume only.
気まぐれkimagure / 落ち着きがないochitsuki ga nai / 怒りっぽいokorippoi capricious / restless / short-tempered 気まぐれ = decisions flip on a whim (気まぐれな天気・気まぐれで始めた); 落ち着きがない = fidgety, unsettled (授業中じっとしていない); 怒りっぽい = anger comes quickly.
間柄aidagara / 関係kankei / 関連kanren / 間隔kankaku / 縁en relationship (people) / relationship / relatedness (things) / interval / bond of fate 間柄 is only for people (親子の間柄); 関係 is for people or things; 関連 only things/topics; 間隔 = 間 + 隔 "gap" (10分間隔); 縁 = 縁がある・縁を切る, a destined tie.
タイトルtaitoru / 題名daimei / 主題shudai / 論旨ronshi / 要旨youshi title / title / theme / line of argument / gist 題名 is the name on the cover; 主題 is what it is about; 論旨 is the argument it makes; 要旨 is a summary of it. All share 題/旨 kanji — read the whole word.
Exam trap: the 別冊 glosses タイトル as 題名／肩書き — the same katakana word can mean a work's title or a person's job title (部長というタイトル). In Q6 the subject is 論文, so only 題名 fits; if the sentence had been about a business card, 肩書き would be the paraphrase. Always let the sentence choose the sense.
