1 Points — the kanji in this round

漢字 — the kanji of this round (from the 別冊 漢字 blocks, p.7; Q21–24 rows added by us)

This round tests kun-yomi verbs (催す, 漂う), trap type 2 (貧困 ひんこん — not voiced), trap type 3 (傑作 けっさく), and kanji with several kun-yomi (細 こま / ほそ; 良 よ / りょう; 悪 あ / わる / あく). See the six trap types on 第1回.

2 Every question — 問題 (p.17)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【19】

国際交流のイベントを催す計画が進んでいる。Kokusai kouryuu no ibento o moyoosu keikaku ga susunde iru.

Underlined word: 催すmoyoosu — hold (an event)
EN: Plans to hold an international exchange event are under way.
HI: अंतरराष्ट्रीय आदान-प्रदान का एक कार्यक्रम आयोजित करने की योजना आगे बढ़ रही है।
GU: આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય આદાનપ્રદાનનો કાર્યક્રમ યોજવાની યોજના આગળ વધી રહી છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1ほどこすhodokosugive, apply, carry out (施す)देना, लागू करनाઆપવું, લાગુ કરવું
2もよおすmoyoosuhold (an event) (催す)आयोजित करनाયોજવું
3さいすsaisuwrong — on-yomi サイ + す(ग़लत — ऑन पठन サイ + す)(ખોટું — ઓન વાંચન サイ + す)
4うながすunagasuurge, prompt (促す)प्रेरित करना, आग्रह करनाપ્રેરવું, આગ્રહ કરવો

Answer 2 (別冊 p.7, PDF 91). Book's notes (paraphrased): 催す = もよおす (hold an event); on-yomi サイ as in 催促, 開催, 主催; 催し物 = an event. 選択肢の言葉: 1 施す, 4 促す. (added, not in book) うながす is the trap: 催促 (さいそく) means "pressing someone", and 促 = うながす — but 促 and 催 are different kanji. 催す also means "feel (an urge)": 眠気を催す.

【20】

GU: આ બગીચામાં ફૂલોની સુગંધ ફેલાયેલી છે.

3とどこおってtodokoottebe held up, fall behind (滞る)रुक जाना, बकाया होनाઅટકી જવું, બાકી પડવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.7, PDF 91). Book's notes (paraphrased): 漂う = ただよう (float, drift, hang in the air); on-yomi ヒョウ as in 漂流. 選択肢の言葉: 1 逆らって (逆らう), 2 操って (操る), 3 滞って (滞る). (added, not in book) 漂う and 滞る both have 氵 and were already paired in 第1回 Q2 — the book deliberately reuses them as each other's distractors.

これは彼の作品の中で最高の傑作だ。Kore wa kare no sakuhin no naka de saikou no kessaku da.

Underlined word: 傑作kessaku — masterpiece
EN: This is the greatest masterpiece among his works.
HI: यह उसकी रचनाओं में सबसे बेहतरीन कृति है।
GU: આ તેની કૃતિઓમાં સૌથી ઉત્તમ કૃતિ છે.

2けさくkesakuwrong — けつ shortened without っ(ग़लत — っ ग़ायब)(ખોટું — っ ગાયબ)

Answer 3 — reasoned, official key page missing from the scan (別冊 pp.8–9 are not in the PDF; not from the official key). Trap type 3: ケツ + s-sound → けっ, so 傑作 = けっさく (like 傑出 けっしゅつ). 作 is サ only in some words (作業 さぎょう, 動作 どうさ), so けっさ (1) is wrong; 2 and 4 get the 促音 wrong.

【23】

彼女は細やかな心づかいのできる人だ。Kanojo wa komayaka na kokorozukai no dekiru hito da.

Underlined word: 細やかなkomayaka na — warm and attentive to small details
EN: She is someone who can show thoughtful, attentive consideration for others.
HI: वह छोटी-छोटी बातों का ध्यान रखकर दूसरों की परवाह करने वाली इंसान है।
GU: તે નાની નાની વાતોનું ધ્યાન રાખીને બીજાની કાળજી લેનારી વ્યક્તિ છે.

1こまやかなkomayaka nawarm and attentive, delicateबारीकी से ध्यान रखने वाला, स्नेहपूर्णઝીણવટથી ધ્યાન રાખનારું, પ્રેમાળ

Answer 1 — reasoned, official key page missing from the scan (別冊 pp.8–9 are not in the PDF; not from the official key). (added, not in book) 細 has two kun-yomi: こま(か／かい) "fine, detailed" and ほそ(い) "thin". 細やか is read こまやか and is used for feelings and care (細やかな心づかい / 愛情). ほそやか exists only as a rare word for "slender", and does not fit 心づかい. ひそやか is written 密やか; さいやか is not a word.

【24】

製品の良し悪しは見た目ではよくわからない。Seihin no yoshiashi wa mitame de wa yoku wakaranai.

Underlined word: 良し悪しyoshiashi — whether something is good or bad, quality
EN: You can't really tell whether a product is good or bad from how it looks.
HI: किसी उत्पाद का अच्छा या बुरा होना उसकी शक्ल देखकर ठीक से पता नहीं चलता।
GU: કોઈ ઉત્પાદન સારું છે કે ખરાબ તે દેખાવ પરથી બરાબર ખબર પડતી નથી.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1よしわしyoshiwashinot a word(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
2りょうしあくしryoushiakushinot a word (on-yomi + し)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
3りょうしわるしryoushiwarushinot a word (on + kun mixed)(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)
4よしあしyoshiashigood and bad, qualityअच्छाई-बुराईસારું-નરસું

Answer 4 — reasoned, official key page missing from the scan (別冊 pp.8–9 are not in the PDF; not from the official key). (added, not in book) 良し悪し uses the old classical adjective forms 良し (よし, "good") and 悪し (あし, "bad"), both kun-yomi. The trap is わる from 悪い: in this fixed word 悪 is あし, not わるし. Options 2 and 3 use the on-yomi りょう of 良, which never takes the ending し. (よしあし can also mean "having both good and bad sides": それは良し悪しだ.)

3 Confusion pairs — the reading traps in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 漢字 blocks (added, not in book):

FormMeaning / readingKey difference
催すmoyoosu vs 促すunagasu hold (an event) vs urge, prompt 催 (イ-radical) and 促 (イ-radical) look alike, and both appear in 催促. 会を催す / 注意を促す.
施すhodokosu vs 催すmoyoosu give, apply vs hold Both end in 〜す and are "do something for people": 治療を施す (give treatment), 式典を催す (hold a ceremony).
漂うtadayou vs 滞るtodokooru drift vs be held up Both have 氵 and start with t-: 香りが漂う; 支払いが滞る.
貧困hinkon vs 貧乏binbou ひん vs びん 貧乏 is the only common word with voiced びん. 困 (コン) vs 因 (イン) look alike.
傑作kessaku vs 作業sagyou さく vs さ 作 is サク in most words (作品, 傑作) and サ in a few (作業, 動作, 作用).
細やかkomayaka vs 細いhosoi attentive, fine vs thin Both kun-yomi of 細. こま- = fine/detailed (細かい, 細やか); ほそ- = thin/narrow (細い, 細長い).
良し悪しyoshiashi vs 悪いwarui あし vs わる Classical pair 良し + 悪し; the modern adjective is 悪い (わるい). Also 善し悪し with the same reading.
Exam trap: set phrases built from old adjective forms (良し悪し よしあし) keep their classical readings — don't "modernise" them to わるし. And when two kanji look alike (困／因, 催／促), slow down: the reading follows the exact kanji, not the shape you expect.
