【49】

わずらわしいwazurawashii

Word: troublesome, bothersome — 別冊: わずらわしい = 面倒でいやな (tiresome and unpleasant)
HI: झंझट भरा, परेशान करने वाला
GU: કડાકૂટ ભર્યું, ત્રાસદાયક
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1同じ名前の学生がクラスに2人いて、わずらわしい。Onaji namae no gakusei ga kurasu ni futari ite, wazurawashii.There are two students with the same name in the class, which is confusing.कक्षा में एक ही नाम के दो छात्र हैं, जिससे भ्रम होता है।વર્ગમાં એક જ નામના બે વિદ્યાર્થી છે, તેથી ગૂંચવણ થાય છે.
2午前中はいつも掃除や洗濯などでわずらわしく過ぎてしまう。Gozenchuu wa itsumo souji ya sentaku nado de wazurawashiku sugite shimau.My mornings always rush by in a flurry of cleaning, laundry and so on.मेरी सुबह हमेशा सफ़ाई, कपड़े धोने वग़ैरह की भागदौड़ में बीत जाती है।મારી સવાર હંમેશાં સફાઈ, કપડાં ધોવાં વગેરેની દોડધામમાં વીતી જાય છે.
3相続についてのわずらわしい手続きは、弁護士にまかせている。Souzoku ni tsuite no wazurawashii tetsuzuki wa, bengoshi ni makasete iru.I've left the tiresome inheritance procedures to my lawyer.विरासत से जुड़ी झंझट भरी कार्रवाई मैंने वकील पर छोड़ दी है।વારસા અંગેની કડાકૂટ ભરી કાર્યવાહી મેં વકીલને સોંપી છે.
4大勢の人が見ている前で失敗をして、とてもわずらわしかった。Oozei no hito ga mite iru mae de shippai o shite, totemo wazurawashikatta.I made a mistake in front of lots of people and was very embarrassed.बहुत लोगों के सामने गलती करके मुझे बड़ी शर्म आई।ઘણા લોકો સામે ભૂલ કરીને મને ખૂબ શરમ આવી.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). Book's definition: わずらわしい = 面倒でいやな (tiresome and unpleasant). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be ややこしい / まぎらわしい (yayakoshii / magirawashii) "complicated / confusingly similar"; 2 → should be あわただしく (awatadashiku) "in a hurry, hectic"; 4 → should be はずかしかった (hazukashikatta) "was embarrassed". (added, not in book) わずらわしい is for things that take effort and are unpleasant to deal with: 手続き・人間関係・雑事. Compare Q2: there the book replaced まぎらわしい with わずらわしい; here it replaces わずらわしい with まぎらわしい.

【50】

段取りdandori

Word: arrangements; planning the order of steps — 別冊: 段取り = 仕事などの手順を決めること (deciding the procedure for a job, etc.)
HI: काम का क्रम/व्यवस्था तय करना
GU: કામનો ક્રમ/વ્યવસ્થા નક્કી કરવી
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1段取りが悪かったせいで仕事の完成が遅れてしまった。Dandori ga warukatta sei de shigoto no kansei ga okurete shimatta.Because the work was badly planned, it was finished late.ख़राब योजना के कारण काम देर से पूरा हुआ।ખરાબ આયોજનને કારણે કામ મોડું પૂરું થયું.
2荷物の段取りができたら、出発しよう。Nimotsu no dandori ga dekitara, shuppatsu shiyou.Once the luggage is ready, let's set off.सामान तैयार हो जाए तो चल पड़ते हैं।સામાન તૈયાર થાય એટલે નીકળીએ.
3他人の文章を使用するには事前の段取りが必要だ。Tanin no bunshou o shiyou suru ni wa jizen no dandori ga hitsuyou da.You need permission in advance to use someone else's writing.दूसरे का लेख इस्तेमाल करने के लिए पहले से अनुमति चाहिए।બીજાનું લખાણ વાપરવા અગાઉથી પરવાનગી જોઈએ.
4来月のイベントで使用する会場の段取りを新入社員に頼んだ。Raigetsu no ibento de shiyou suru kaijou no dandori o shinnyuu shain ni tanonda.I asked a new employee to go and check out the venue for next month's event beforehand.मैंने नए कर्मचारी से अगले महीने के कार्यक्रम का हॉल पहले जाकर देख आने को कहा।મેં નવા કર્મચારીને આવતા મહિનાના કાર્યક્રમનો હૉલ અગાઉથી જોઈ આવવા કહ્યું.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). Book's definition: 段取り = 仕事などの手順を決めること (deciding the procedure for a job, etc.). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 支度 / 準備 (shitaku / junbi) "getting ready, preparation"; 3 → should be 許可 (kyoka) "permission"; 4 → should be 下見 (shitami) "inspecting in advance". (added, not in book) 段取り is the plan of steps for a job or event (段取りがいい/悪い・段取りをつける). Packing luggage is 支度/準備; going to look at a place beforehand is 下見.

【51】

あがるagaru

Word: to get nervous, have stage fright — 別冊: あがる = （正解文中の意味）緊張して落ち着きがなくなる (meaning in the correct sentence: become tense and lose one's composure)
HI: घबरा जाना (मंच-भय)
GU: ગભરાઈ જવું (મંચ-ભય)
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1今月は経費の出費があがって、赤字になってしまった。Kongetsu wa keihi no shuppi ga agatte, akaji ni natte shimatta.Our expenses piled up this month and we went into the red.इस महीने ख़र्च बढ़ता गया और घाटा हो गया।આ મહિને ખર્ચ વધતો ગયો અને ખોટ ગઈ.
2私は人前に立つとあがってしまい、顔が赤くなり声がふるえる。Watashi wa hitomae ni tatsu to agatte shimai, kao ga akaku nari koe ga furueru.When I stand in front of people, I get nervous: my face turns red and my voice shakes.लोगों के सामने खड़े होते ही मैं घबरा जाता हूँ, चेहरा लाल हो जाता है और आवाज़ काँपती है।લોકો સામે ઊભો રહું એટલે હું ગભરાઈ જાઉં છું, મોઢું લાલ થાય છે અને અવાજ ધ્રૂજે છે.
3濃いコーヒーを飲むと頭があがって仕事がはかどる。Koi koohii o nomu to atama ga agatte shigoto ga hakadoru.When I drink strong coffee my mind becomes clear and my work goes well.कड़क कॉफ़ी पीने से दिमाग़ साफ़ होता है और काम तेज़ी से होता है।કડક કૉફી પીવાથી મગજ સાફ થાય છે અને કામ ઝડપથી થાય છે.
4昨日の弁論大会の記事が新聞にあがっていた。Kinou no benron taikai no kiji ga shinbun ni agatte ita.An article about yesterday's speech contest was in the newspaper.कल की भाषण प्रतियोगिता की ख़बर अख़बार में छपी थी।ગઈ કાલની વક્તૃત્વ સ્પર્ધાનો લેખ છાપામાં છપાયો હતો.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). Book's definition: あがる = （正解文中の意味）緊張して落ち着きがなくなる (meaning in the correct sentence: become tense and lose one's composure). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be かさんで (kasande) "piling up (かさむ)"; 3 → should be 冴えて (saete) "being clear-headed (冴える)"; 4 → should be 載って (notte) "appearing in print (載る)". (added, not in book) あがる has many meanings (go up, rise, finish…). The exam tests the idiomatic one: nerves before an audience (人前であがる・本番であがる). Costs that pile up are かさむ, not あがる.

【52】

やけにyake ni

Word: awfully, unusually (casual) — 別冊: やけに = いつもとちがってひどく（くだけた会話で使うことが多い） (unusually intensely, unlike usual; mostly in casual speech)
HI: आज कुछ ज़्यादा ही, बेहद (बोलचाल)
GU: આજે કંઈક વધારે પડતું, ખૂબ (બોલચાલ)
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1彼女はいつも秘密を言いふらしてしまうので、今回はやけに話さないでおこう。Kanojo wa itsumo himitsu o iifurashite shimau node, konkai wa yake ni hanasanaide okou.She always spreads secrets around, so this time let's be sure not to tell her.वह हमेशा राज़ फैला देती है, इसलिए इस बार उसे हरगिज़ मत बताना।તે હંમેશાં રહસ્ય ફેલાવી દે છે, તેથી આ વખતે તેને હરગિજ ન કહેવું.
2テストに向けてやけに勉強しているが、毎回その努力は報われない。Tesuto ni mukete yake ni benkyou shite iru ga, maikai sono doryoku wa mukuwarenai.I study as hard as I can for tests, but my efforts never pay off.मैं परीक्षा के लिए जी-जान से पढ़ता हूँ, पर हर बार मेहनत बेकार जाती है।હું પરીક્ષા માટે જીવ લગાવીને ભણું છું, પણ દર વખતે મહેનત ફળતી નથી.
3今日はやけに蒸し暑いな。Kyou wa yake ni mushiatsui na.It's awfully humid today.आज कुछ ज़्यादा ही उमस है।આજે કંઈક વધારે જ બફારો છે.
4会場に入ったら、宴会はやけに始まっていたので、びっくりした。Kaijou ni haittara, enkai wa yake ni hajimatte ita node, bikkuri shita.When I walked into the hall, I was surprised – the party had started long before.हॉल में घुसा तो हैरान रह गया, क्योंकि दावत कब की शुरू हो चुकी थी।હૉલમાં ગયો તો ચકિત થયો, કારણ કે ભોજનસમારંભ ક્યારનો શરૂ થઈ ગયો હતો.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). Book's definition: やけに = いつもとちがってひどく（くだけた会話で使うことが多い） (unusually intensely, unlike usual; mostly in casual speech). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 絶対に (zettai ni) "absolutely, definitely"; 2 → should be 一生懸命に (isshoukenmei ni) "as hard as one can"; 4 → should be とっくに (tokku ni) "long ago, already". (added, not in book) やけに comments on a state that is strangely more intense than usual — often weather, body feelings or someone's behaviour (やけに暑い・やけに静かだ・やけに優しい). It is not for effort, certainty or time.

【53】

もめるmomeru

Word: to quarrel, be in dispute — 別冊: もめる = けんかをする (to quarrel)
HI: झगड़ा होना, विवाद होना
GU: ઝઘડો થવો, વિવાદ થવો
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1冷蔵庫に入れたまま忘れていた野菜がすっかりもめてしまった。Reizouko ni ireta mama wasurete ita yasai ga sukkari momete shimatta.The vegetables I forgot in the fridge have gone completely rotten.फ़्रिज में रखकर भूली सब्ज़ियाँ पूरी तरह सड़ गईं।ફ્રિજમાં મૂકીને ભૂલી ગયેલી શાકભાજી સાવ સડી ગઈ.
2初めて行った町で道にもめてしまった。Hajimete itta machi de michi ni momete shimatta.I got lost in a town I'd never been to before.पहली बार गए शहर में मैं रास्ता भटक गया।પહેલી વાર ગયેલા શહેરમાં હું રસ્તો ભૂલી ગયો.
3浮気問題でもめていた近所のご夫婦が、とうとう離婚したらしい。Uwaki mondai de momete ita kinjo no go-fuufu ga, toutou rikon shita rashii.I hear the couple next door, who had been fighting over an affair, have finally divorced.सुना है अफ़ेयर को लेकर झगड़ रहे पड़ोसी पति-पत्नी का आख़िर तलाक़ हो गया।સાંભળ્યું છે લફરાને લઈને ઝઘડતાં પડોશી પતિ-પત્ની આખરે છૂટાં થયાં.
4肩が凝っちゃった。ちょっともめてくれない？Kata ga kocchatta. Chotto momete kurenai?My shoulders are stiff. Could you give them a little massage?कंधे अकड़ गए हैं, थोड़ा दबा दोगे?ખભા જકડાઈ ગયા છે, થોડા દબાવી આપશો?

Answer 3 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). Book's definition: もめる = けんかをする (to quarrel). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 腐って (kusatte) "going rotten (腐る)"; 2 → should be 迷って (mayotte) "getting lost (迷う)"; 4 → should be もんで (monde) "massaging (もむ)". (added, not in book) もめる (intransitive) = people argue or a matter becomes a dispute (相続・離婚・会議がもめる). Its look-alike もむ (transitive) = to massage; て-form もんで, not もめて.

【54】

なじみnajimi

Word: familiarity, a close connection (with a place or people)
HI: जान-पहचान, अपनापन
GU: પરિચય, આત્મીયતા
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1なじみの研究が実を結び、ついに新薬が開発された。Najimi no kenkyuu ga mi o musubi, tsui ni shin'yaku ga kaihatsu sareta.Many years of research bore fruit, and a new drug was finally developed.बरसों का शोध रंग लाया और आख़िरकार नई दवा बनी।વર્ષોનું સંશોધન ફળ્યું અને આખરે નવી દવા બની.
2わが社は創立80年のなじみを持つ。Waga sha wa souritsu hachijuunen no najimi o motsu.Our company has an 80-year history.हमारी कंपनी का 80 साल का इतिहास है।અમારી કંપનીનો 80 વર્ષનો ઇતિહાસ છે.
3また同じクラスになるなんて、私たちなじみがあるね。Mata onaji kurasu ni naru nante, watashitachi najimi ga aru ne.In the same class again – we must be fated to be together!फिर से एक ही कक्षा में — लगता है हमारा कोई नाता है!ફરી એક જ વર્ગમાં — લાગે છે આપણો કોઈ નાતો છે!
4ここは以前私たちが住んでいたところで、なじみが深い土地です。Koko wa izen watashitachi ga sunde ita tokoro de, najimi ga fukai tochi desu.We used to live here, so it's a place we know very well.हम पहले यहाँ रहते थे, इसलिए यह जगह हमारी बहुत जानी-पहचानी है।અમે પહેલાં અહીં રહેતાં, તેથી આ જગ્યા અમારી ખૂબ જાણીતી છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.94–95, PDF 176–177). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 長年 (naganen) "many years"; 2 → should be 歴史 (rekishi) "history"; 3 → should be 縁 (en) "fate, karmic bond". (added, not in book) The 別冊 gives the set phrases なじみが深い / ある / ない. なじみ comes from 馴染む (to become familiar), so it is about long familiarity — not about time spent (長年) or destiny (縁).

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
わずらわしいwazurawashii / ややこしい / まぎらわしいyayakoshii / magirawashii / あわただしくawatadashiku / はずかしかったhazukashikatta troublesome, bothersome / complicated / confusingly similar / in a hurry, hectic / was embarrassed わずらわしい = a nuisance to deal with; まぎらわしい = easily confused (Q2); ややこしい = complicated; あわただしい = hectic; はずかしい = embarrassing. The わずらわしい / まぎらわしい pair is a favourite exam trap.
段取りdandori / 支度 / 準備shitaku / junbi / 許可kyoka / 下見shitami arrangements; planning the order of steps / getting ready, preparation / permission / inspecting in advance 段取り = planning the steps; 準備・支度 = getting things ready; 下見 = preliminary inspection; 許可 = permission. Compare 日取り (Q23) = the date of an event.
あがるagaru / かさんでkasande / 冴えてsaete / 載ってnotte to get nervous, have stage fright / piling up (かさむ) / being clear-headed (冴える) / appearing in print (載る) あがる = get stage fright; かさむ = (costs) mount up; 冴える = (mind) is sharp; 載る = appear in print. A one-verb, many-meanings word — read the situation (audience? money? newspaper?).
やけにyake ni / 絶対にzettai ni / 一生懸命にisshoukenmei ni / とっくにtokku ni awfully, unusually (casual) / absolutely, definitely / as hard as one can / long ago, already やけに = unusually (state); 一生懸命 = with all one's effort; 絶対に = definitely; とっくに = long since. Compare いやに / 妙に in Q40 — near-synonyms of やけに.
もめるmomeru / 腐ってkusatte / 迷ってmayotte / もんでmonde to quarrel, be in dispute / going rotten (腐る) / getting lost (迷う) / massaging (もむ) もめる = quarrel, be in dispute; もむ = massage (肩をもむ); 腐る = rot; 迷う = get lost. もめる / もむ is a verb-form trap.
なじみnajimi / 長年naganen / 歴史rekishi / 縁en familiarity, a close connection (with a place or people) / many years / history / fate, karmic bond なじみ = familiarity (なじみの店・幼なじみ); 縁 = fate, bond (縁がある); 長年 = many years; 歴史 = history. なじみがある vs 縁がある: familiarity you built vs a bond fate gave you.
Exam trap: this round reuses a trap from 第1回: わずらわしい vs まぎらわしい (Q2/Q49). Also note polysemous verbs like あがる — the exam picks one idiomatic sense (stage fright), so match the situation, not the dictionary's first meaning.
