2 Every question — 問題 (p.37)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【8】

（　）というのは、一年の最後の日のことだ。(___) to iu no wa, ichinen no saigo no hi no koto da.

Full sentence: 大晦日というのは、一年の最後の日のことだ。Oomisoka to iu no wa, ichinen no saigo no hi no koto da.
EN: "Oomisoka" means the last day of the year.
HI: "ओओमिसोका" का मतलब है साल का आख़िरी दिन।
GU: "ઓઓમિસોકા" એટલે વર્ષનો છેલ્લો દિવસ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1年末年始nenmatsu nenshithe year-end and New Year holidaysसाल के अंत और नए साल की छुट्टियाँવર્ષના અંત અને નવા વર્ષની રજાઓ
2元旦gantanNew Year's Day (1 January)नए साल का पहला दिन (1 जनवरी)નવા વર્ષનો પહેલો દિવસ (1 જાન્યુઆરી)
3元日ganjitsuNew Year's Day (1 January)नए साल का पहला दिन (1 जनवरी)નવા વર્ષનો પહેલો દિવસ (1 જાન્યુઆરી)
4大晦日oomisokaNew Year's Eve (31 December)साल का आख़िरी दिन (31 दिसंबर)વર્ષનો છેલ્લો દિવસ (31 ડિસેમ્બર)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.39, PDF 121). Book's notes (paraphrased): 大晦日 = 31 December. 選択肢の言葉: 2 元旦 = 3 元日 = 1 January. The 別冊 has no note for 1 年末年始. (added, not in book) 元旦 and 元日 are the opposite end of the year — and since the book treats them as the same day, neither can be the single answer. 年末年始 is a period of several days, not "the last day". 晦日 (みそか (misoka)) is the last day of a month; 大 makes it the last day of the year.

【9】

運転免許証を紛失してしまったので、（　）を申請した。Unten menkyoshou o funshitsu shite shimatta node, (___) o shinsei shita.

Full sentence: 運転免許証を紛失してしまったので、再交付を申請した。Unten menkyoshou o funshitsu shite shimatta node, saikoufu o shinsei shita.
EN: I lost my driving licence, so I applied for a replacement.
HI: मेरा ड्राइविंग लाइसेंस खो गया, इसलिए मैंने उसे दोबारा जारी करवाने का आवेदन दिया।
GU: મારું ડ્રાઇવિંગ લાઇસન્સ ખોવાઈ ગયું, એટલે મેં તેને ફરીથી જારી કરાવવા અરજી કરી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1再審査saishinsare-examination, reviewदोबारा जाँच, पुनर्विचारફરી તપાસ, પુનર્વિચાર
2再確認saikakuninreconfirmation, double-checkदोबारा पुष्टि करनाફરીથી ખાતરી કરવી
3再交付saikoufureissueदोबारा जारी करनाફરીથી જારી કરવું
4再認識saininshikifresh recognition, coming to see anewफिर से समझना, नए सिरे से एहसासફરીથી સમજવું, નવેસરથી અહેસાસ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.39, PDF 121). Book's notes (paraphrased): 再交付 = もう一度交付すること (issuing again); 参考: 再〜 = do 〜 once more; 交付 = issue (of documents). 選択肢の言葉: 1 再審査 = re-judgement, review; 4 再認識 = recognising again (例: because Japanese people make many mistakes in Japanese, we are made to 再認識させられる (saininshiki saserareru) how important studying the national language is). The 別冊 has no note for 2 再確認. (added, not in book) All four start with 再, so the second half decides: a lost licence must be issued again → 交付. Learn 免許証・パスポートの交付 / 再交付 as a set.

【10】

近年、警察は飲酒運転をいっそう厳しく（　）ようになった。Kinnen, keisatsu wa inshu unten o issou kibishiku (___) you ni natta.

Full sentence: 近年、警察は飲酒運転をいっそう厳しく取り締まるようになった。Kinnen, keisatsu wa inshu unten o issou kibishiku torishimaru you ni natta.
EN: In recent years the police have cracked down on drink-driving even more strictly.
HI: हाल के वर्षों में पुलिस शराब पीकर गाड़ी चलाने पर और भी सख़्ती से कार्रवाई करने लगी है।
GU: તાજેતરનાં વર્ષોમાં પોલીસ દારૂ પીને વાહન ચલાવવા પર વધુ કડકાઈથી પગલાં લેવા લાગી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1裁くsabakuto judge, try (in court)न्याय करना, मुक़दमे का फ़ैसला करनाન્યાય કરવો, મુકદ્દમાનો ચુકાદો આપવો
2監督するkantoku suruto supervise, directनिगरानी करना, निर्देशन करनाદેખરેખ રાખવી, દિગ્દર્શન કરવું
3判断するhandan suruto judge, decideनिर्णय करना, आँकनाનિર્ણય કરવો, આંકવું
4取り締まるtorishimaruto crack down on, controlसख़्ती से रोकना, नियंत्रण करनाસખતાઈથી રોકવું, નિયંત્રણ કરવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.39, PDF 121). Book's notes (paraphrased): 取り締まる = watch over and restrict (control, regulate). 選択肢の言葉: 1 裁く = judge (例: there are views for and against the "lay judge system" in which citizens 罪人を裁く (zainin o sabaku)). The 別冊 has no notes for 2 監督する and 3 判断する. (added, not in book) The subject is 警察 and the object 飲酒運転 (an offence): police crack down on offences → 取り締まる. Courts 裁く; coaches and managers 監督する.

【11】

失敗の原因について（　）としたが、だれも聞いてくれなかった。Shippai no gen'in ni tsuite (___) to shita ga, dare mo kiite kurenakatta.

Full sentence: 失敗の原因について弁解しようとしたが、だれも聞いてくれなかった。Shippai no gen'in ni tsuite benkai shiyou to shita ga, dare mo kiite kurenakatta.
EN: I tried to explain myself about what caused the failure, but nobody would listen.
HI: मैंने असफलता के कारण के बारे में सफ़ाई देने की कोशिश की, पर किसी ने नहीं सुना।
GU: મેં નિષ્ફળતાના કારણ વિશે સફાઈ આપવાનો પ્રયત્ન કર્યો, પણ કોઈએ સાંભળ્યું નહીં.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1説教しようsekkyou shiyoulecture, preach (説教する)उपदेश देना, डाँट-भरी नसीहत देनाઉપદેશ આપવો, ઠપકા સાથે શિખામણ આપવી
2弁解しようbenkai shiyoumake excuses, explain oneself (弁解する)सफ़ाई देनाસફાઈ આપવી
3申請しようshinsei shiyouapply for (申請する)आवेदन करनाઅરજી કરવી
4解釈しようkaishaku shiyouinterpret (解釈する)व्याख्या करनाઅર્થઘટન કરવું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.39, PDF 121). Book's notes (paraphrased): 弁解する = make an excuse. 選択肢の言葉: 1 説教する = ① talk to teach a religion; ② talk to someone to guide or scold them (例: when they found out I had skipped school, 先生と親に説教された (sensei to oya ni sekkyou sareta)). The 別冊 has no notes for 3 申請する or 4 解釈する. (added, not in book) The person who failed is talking about the cause of their own failure and nobody listens → an excuse. 説教 goes the other way: a teacher or parent lectures you.

【12】

オリンピックでの優勝をはじめとして、ここ数年の田中選手の活躍には（　）ものがある。Orinpikku de no yuushou o hajime to shite, koko suunen no Tanaka senshu no katsuyaku ni wa (___) mono ga aru.

Full sentence: オリンピックでの優勝をはじめとして、ここ数年の田中選手の活躍にはめざましいものがある。Orinpikku de no yuushou o hajime to shite, koko suunen no Tanaka senshu no katsuyaku ni wa mezamashii mono ga aru.

GU: ઓલિમ્પિકમાં જીતથી માંડીને, છેલ્લાં કેટલાંક વર્ષોમાં તનાકા ખેલાડીનો દેખાવ ખરેખર નોંધપાત્ર રહ્યો છે.

Book's notes (paraphrased): めざましい = remarkable. 選択肢の言葉: 1 あさましい = 情けない / みっともない / 心が卑しい (例: cheating old people out of money とは、あさましいことだ (to wa, asamashii koto da)); 3 たくましい = 力強い / タフな (例: it's a boy, so I want him to grow up たくましい子 (takumashii ko)); 4 おびただしい = 非常に多い (例: おびただしい数の人 (obitadashii kazu no hito) had gathered in the square). (added, not in book) All four end in 〜しい — a "similar sound" set. 活躍 (performance) + positive context (優勝) needs praise: めざましい活躍 / めざましい発展 are set phrases.

(added, not in book) ねほりはほり only goes with 聞く / 尋ねる / 調べる and sounds intrusive; 話し合う to clear a misunderstanding needs とことん.

【14】

EN: This accident is believed to have been caused by some kind of fault in the train's wheels.
HI: माना जा रहा है कि यह दुर्घटना ट्रेन के पहियों में आई किसी ख़राबी की वजह से हुई।
GU: એવું મનાય છે કે આ અકસ્માત ટ્રેનનાં પૈડાંમાં આવેલી કોઈ ખામીને કારણે થયો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1チェンジchenjichange, switchबदलाव, अदला-बदलीબદલાવ, અદલાબદલી
2トラブルtoraburutrouble, malfunctionगड़बड़ी, ख़राबीગરબડ, ખામી
3ブレーキbureekibrakeब्रेकબ્રેક
4マークmaakumark, symbolनिशान, चिह्नનિશાન, ચિહ્ન

Answer 2 (別冊 p.40, PDF 122). Book's notes (paraphrased): トラブル = trouble, problem, breakdown. 選択肢の言葉: 1 チェンジ = change (例: in football or volleyball the teams make a コートのチェンジ (kooto no chenji) during the match); 3 ブレーキ = brake (例: accidents from mixing up the accelerator and ブレーキ (bureeki) are increasing); 4 マーク = mark (例: the 若葉のマーク (wakaba no maaku) on a car shows the driver got the licence less than a year ago). (added, not in book) 何らかの〜が発生した (some ___ occurred) needs something that "happens" — a fault. A brake is a part of the train, not something that occurs; ブレーキ is the "related topic" distractor.

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
大晦日oomisoka vs 元旦・元日gantan · ganjitsu vs 年末年始nenmatsu nenshi 31 Dec vs 1 Jan vs the holiday period Two ends of the New Year: 大晦日 closes the year, 元日 / 元旦 opens it (strictly 元旦 is the morning of 1 Jan). 年末年始 covers both.
再交付saikoufu / 再確認saikakunin / 再審査saishinsa / 再認識saininshiki reissue / reconfirm / re-examine / realise anew 再 + noun: the second half must fit the object — documents are 交付 (issued), bookings are 確認, applications or verdicts are 審査, values or importance are 認識.
取り締まるtorishimaru vs 裁くsabaku vs 監督するkantoku suru crack down vs judge (court) vs supervise Police 取り締まる offences (飲酒運転を取り締まる); a court 裁く people (罪人を裁く); a coach / director 監督する a team or a film.
弁解するbenkai suru vs 説教するsekkyou suru make excuses vs lecture someone Direction: you 弁解する for your own mistake; someone above you 説教する you about yours.
めざましいmezamashii / あさましいasamashii / たくましいtakumashii / おびただしいobitadashii remarkable / shameful / robust / countless Same 〜しい rhythm, different meaning: めざましい活躍, あさましい行為, たくましい体, おびただしい数. Only めざましい is praise for progress.
とことんtokoton vs ねほりはほりnehori hahori thoroughly (to the end) vs prying into every detail Both mean "all the way", but ねほりはほり is about questions only and is negative (根掘り葉掘り聞かれた). とことん works with any effort: とことん話し合う / やる.
たかだかtakadaka vs かれこれkarekore at most vs roughly Both go with amounts: たかだか50万 = "no more than 500,000" (small); かれこれ10年 = "about ten years" (feels long).
Exam trap: when the four options share a prefix or ending (再〜 in Q9, 〜しい in Q12), the shared part is useless — read the other half of each word against the sentence. 免許証 → 交付, 活躍 → めざましい.
