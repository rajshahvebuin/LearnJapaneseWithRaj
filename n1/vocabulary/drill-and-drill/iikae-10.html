The 6 underlined words of this round (definitions from the 別冊 p.83–84)

2 Every question — 問題 (p.65)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【55】

工事がいつ終わるか目処が立たない。Kouji ga itsu owaru ka medo ga tatanai.

Underlined word: 目処が立たない = 予測できない (cannot be foreseen).
EN: There is no telling when the construction work will be finished.
HI: निर्माण-कार्य कब पूरा होगा, इसका कोई अंदाज़ा नहीं है।
GU: બાંધકામ ક્યારે પૂરું થશે તેનો કોઈ અંદાજ નથી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1見通しがつかないmitooshi ga tsukanaicannot foreseeअनुमान नहीं लगताઅંદાજ નથી આવતો
2決まっていないkimatte inaihas not been decidedतय नहीं हुआનક્કી નથી થયું
3発表されないhappyou sarenaiis not announcedघोषित नहीं होताજાહેર નથી થતું
4検討していないkentou shite inaiis not being consideredविचार नहीं किया जा रहाવિચારણા નથી થતી

Answer 1 (別冊 p.83, PDF 165). Book's notes (paraphrased): 目処が立たない = 見通しがつかない, impossible to predict. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 2 決まっていない is close but is about a decision; 目処 is about being able to foresee — even with no decision pending, nobody can estimate the end. 目処が立つ／つく = an end is in sight.

【56】

我々のチームのスタッフを紹介しましょう。Wareware no chiimu no sutaffu o shoukai shimashou.

Underlined word: スタッフ = 仕事をいっしょにする人 (people who work together on a job).
EN: Let me introduce the members of our team.
HI: आइए, अपनी टीम के सदस्यों का परिचय कराता हूँ।
GU: ચાલો, અમારી ટીમના સભ્યોનો પરિચય કરાવું.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1メンバーmenbaamembersसदस्यસભ્યો
2リーダーriidaaleaderनेताનેતા
3チーフchiifuchief, headप्रमुखવડા
4モニターmonitaamonitor (person who checks / tests)निगरानीकर्ताનિરીક્ષક

Answer 1 (別冊 p.83, PDF 165). Book's notes (paraphrased): スタッフ = メンバー, the people one works with. 選択肢の言葉 (glosses only): 2 リーダー = leader; 3 チーフ = chief/leader; 4 モニター = monitor. (added, not in book) 2 and 3 are single people at the top; スタッフ is everyone on the team. モニター in Japanese also means a consumer tester (商品モニター).

【57】

私は彼女にさんざん迷惑をかけている。Watashi wa kanojo ni sanzan meiwaku o kakete iru.

Underlined word: さんざん = いやになるほどひどく (so badly it becomes unbearable).
EN: I have caused her no end of trouble.
HI: मैंने उसे हद से ज़्यादा परेशान किया है।
GU: મેં તેને હદ બહાર તકલીફ આપી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1時折tokiorioccasionallyकभी-कभीક્યારેક
2通常tsuujouusuallyसामान्यतःસામાન્ય રીતે
3大変taihenvery much, terriblyबहुत ज़्यादाખૂબ જ
4一頃hitokoroat one time, onceएक समयએક સમયે

Answer 3 (別冊 p.83–84, PDF 165–166). Book's notes (paraphrased): さんざん = 大変, badly enough to be sickening. 選択肢の言葉: 1 時折 = ときどき (例: 明日は、午前は天気がよくても午後は時折小雨が降りますから、傘を持ってお出かけください (ashita wa, gozen wa tenki ga yokute mo gogo wa tokiori kosame ga furimasu kara, kasa o motte o-dekake kudasai)); 2 通常 = 普通は／いつもは (例: 重役会議は通常水曜日に開かれるが、今週は火曜日に変更された (juuyaku kaigi wa tsuujou suiyoubi ni hirakareru ga, konshuu wa kayoubi ni henkou sareta)); 4 一頃 = a certain time in the past (例: これ、一頃流行ったゲームだね。なつかしいなあ (kore, hitokoro hayatta geemu da ne. Natsukashii naa)). (added, not in book) 1, 2 and 4 are about when / how often; さんざん is about degree.

【58】

その手紙からは、彼女の人となりがよく伝わってきます。Sono tegami kara wa, kanojo no hitonari ga yoku tsutawatte kimasu.

Underlined word: 人となり = その人の特徴になっている性格 (the character that defines a person).
EN: That letter conveys her character very well.
HI: उस पत्र से उसका स्वभाव अच्छी तरह झलकता है।
GU: એ પત્રમાંથી તેનો સ્વભાવ સારી રીતે છતો થાય છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1人生jinseilife (course of a life)जीवनજીવન
2考え方kangaekataway of thinkingसोचने का ढंगવિચારવાની રીત
3生き方ikikataway of lifeजीने का ढंगજીવવાની રીત
4人柄hitogarapersonality, characterस्वभाव, व्यक्तित्वસ્વભાવ, વ્યક્તિત્વ

Answer 4 (別冊 p.84, PDF 166). Book's notes (paraphrased): 人となり = 人柄, the personality that characterises someone. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 人となり looks like 人 + なる, tempting 人生 or 生き方; it is fixed as "a person's nature", which is 人柄 (also a 人〜 word).

【59】

この部屋の家具はどれも洗練されている。Kono heya no kagu wa dore mo senren sarete iru.

Underlined word: 洗練されている = 上品だ (refined, elegant).
EN: Every piece of furniture in this room is refined.
HI: इस कमरे का हर फ़र्नीचर सुरुचिपूर्ण है।
GU: આ ઓરડાનું દરેક ફર્નિચર સુરુચિપૂર્ણ છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1豪華だgouka dais luxurious, gorgeousशानदार, आलीशान हैભવ્ય, વૈભવી છે
2質素だshisso dais plain, frugalसादा हैસાદું છે
3上品だjouhin dais elegant, tastefulसुरुचिपूर्ण, शालीन हैસુરુચિપૂર્ણ, શાલીન છે
4精巧だseikou dais finely crafted, exquisiteबारीक़ी से बना हैઝીણવટથી બનેલું છે

Answer 3 (別冊 p.84, PDF 166). Book's notes (paraphrased): 洗練されている = 上品だ. 選択肢の言葉: 2 質素(な) = having only what is necessary, not spending on extras, not living lavishly (例: A氏は今や大企業の社長だが、貧しかった昔と変わらない質素な生活をしているという (A-shi wa ima ya daikigyou no shachou da ga, mazushikatta mukashi to kawaranai shisso na seikatsu o shite iru to iu)); 4 精巧(な) = made accurately down to the finest detail (例: この人形は精巧に作られているので、まるで生きているように見える (kono ningyou wa seikou ni tsukurarete iru node, marude ikite iru you ni mieru)). The 別冊 has no note for 1. (added, not in book) 豪華 is about expense and splendour; 精巧 about craftsmanship; 洗練 (洗 wash + 練 polish) is about taste — 上品.

【60】

あの人がしくじるなんて、考えられない。Ano hito ga shikujiru nante, kangaerarenai.

Underlined word: しくじる = 失敗する (to fail, slip up).
EN: I can't imagine that person messing up.
HI: वह व्यक्ति गड़बड़ कर दे, यह सोचा भी नहीं जा सकता।
GU: એ માણસ ગડબડ કરે એવું વિચારી પણ ન શકાય.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1裏切るuragiruto betrayधोखा देनाદગો દેવો
2ごまかすgomakasuto cheat, fudgeहेराफेरी करनाછેતરપિંડી કરવી
3失敗するshippai suruto failअसफल होना, चूकनाનિષ્ફળ જવું, ચૂકવું
4なまけるnamakeruto be lazy, slack offआलस करनाઆળસ કરવી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.84, PDF 166). Book's notes (paraphrased): しくじる = 失敗する. 選択肢の言葉: 2 ごまかす = to do something dishonest so others won't notice (例: 子どものとき、母に頼まれて買い物に行った。そのとき、おつりをごまかして、母にひどく怒られた (kodomo no toki, haha ni tanomarete kaimono ni itta. Sono toki, otsuri o gomakashite, haha ni hidoku okorareta)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 4. (added, not in book) 1, 2 and 4 are all moral failings (betraying, cheating, slacking); しくじる is just a mistake, with no bad intent.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
目処が立つmedo ga tatsu / 見通しがつくmitooshi ga tsuku / 決まるkimaru end in sight / can foresee / be decided 目処・見通し = being able to estimate (完成の目処が立った). 決まる = a decision exists. Note the verbs: 目処が立つ, 見通しがつく／立つ.
スタッフsutaffu / メンバーmenbaa / チーフchiifu / リーダーriidaa staff / members / chief / leader スタッフ = people working on something (often behind the scenes: 撮影スタッフ); メンバー = members of any group; チーフ・リーダー = the head.
さんざんsanzan / 大変taihen / 時折tokiori / 一頃hitokoro / 通常tsuujou terribly / very / occasionally / at one time / usually さんざん = degree, usually unpleasant (さんざんな目にあう, さんざん待たされた). The others are frequency/time words.
人となりhitonari / 人柄hitogara / 人生jinsei / 生き方ikikata character / personality / life / way of living 人となり (formal, written) ≈ 人柄 (人柄がいい). 人生 is one's life story; 生き方 how one chooses to live.
洗練senren / 上品jouhin / 豪華gouka / 精巧seikou / 質素shisso refined / elegant / luxurious / finely crafted / plain Taste (洗練・上品) vs money (豪華 ⇔ 質素) vs workmanship (精巧). A 質素 room can still be 洗練 — the axes are independent.
しくじるshikujiru / 失敗するshippai suru / ごまかすgomakasu / なまけるnamakeru blunder / fail / cheat / be lazy しくじる (colloquial) = 失敗する, an honest mistake. ごまかす = deliberate deceit; なまける = not trying (cf. サボる, 第2回 Q7).
Exam trap: in Q60 three of the four options describe bad behaviour and only one describes a bad result. しくじる is about the result. A useful habit for 言い換え: classify the underlined word first (result? attitude? degree? time?), then discard options from the wrong class.
