1 Points — the words tested in this round

2 Every question — 問題 (p.57)

＿＿＿の言葉に意味が最も近いものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。___ no kotoba ni imi ga mottomo chikai mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the option closest in meaning to the underlined word. HI: रेखांकित शब्द के अर्थ के सबसे निकट विकल्प 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દના અર્થની સૌથી નજીકનો વિકલ્પ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【7】

彼女は最近よく仕事をサボる。Kanojo wa saikin yoku shigoto o saboru.

Underlined word: サボる = なまけて仕事や学校を休む (to skip work or school out of laziness).
EN: She has been skipping work a lot lately.
HI: वह आजकल अक्सर काम से जी चुराकर गैरहाज़िर रहती है।
GU: તે હમણાં ઘણી વાર કામમાંથી ગાપચી મારે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1早くさばくhayaku sabakuto deal with (work) quicklyजल्दी निपटानाઝડપથી પતાવવું
2ずる休みをするzuruyasumi o suruto skive, take time off dishonestlyबहाना बनाकर छुट्टी मारनाબહાનું કાઢી રજા પાડવી
3途中でやめるtochuu de yameruto quit halfwayबीच में छोड़ देनाઅધવચ્ચે છોડી દેવું
4うまく処理するumaku shori suruto handle skilfullyकुशलता से निपटानाકુશળતાથી પતાવવું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.74, PDF 156). Book's notes (paraphrased): サボる = ずる休みをする, staying away from work or school out of laziness. 選択肢の言葉: 1 (早く)さばく = to process (fast) (例: 彼女は有能な人だ。毎日山のような仕事を1人でさばいている (kanojo wa yuunou na hito da. Mainichi yama no you na shigoto o hitori de sabaite iru)). The 別冊 has no note for 3 or 4. (added, not in book) サボる (from French sabotage) sounds like さばく but means the opposite of getting work done; 3 途中でやめる is 投げ出す (see 第6回 Q34), not サボる.

【8】

あの子は引っ込み思案で、なかなか友だちができない。Ano ko wa hikkomijian de, nakanaka tomodachi ga dekinai.

Underlined word: 引っ込み思案 = 消極的で自分から行動しようとしない性格 (a passive nature that won't act on its own initiative).
EN: That child is shy and retiring, so finds it hard to make friends.
HI: वह बच्चा संकोची स्वभाव का है, इसलिए उसके दोस्त आसानी से नहीं बनते।
GU: એ બાળક સંકોચશીલ સ્વભાવનું છે, એટલે તેને મિત્રો સહેલાઈથી બનતા નથી.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いじわるijiwarumean, spitefulदुष्ट, चिढ़ाने वालाદુષ્ટ, ચીડવનારું
2乱暴ranbourough, violentउद्दंड, हिंसकઉદ્ધત, હિંસક
3消極的shoukyokutekipassive, unassertiveनिष्क्रिय, दब्बूનિષ્ક્રિય, પાછળ રહેનારું
4勝気kachikistrong-willed, hates to loseजीतने की ज़िद वाला, हार न मानने वालाહાર ન માનનારું, જીદ્દી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.74–75, PDF 156–157). Book's notes (paraphrased): 引っ込み思案 = 消極的, a personality that does not take the initiative. 選択肢の言葉: 4 勝気 = a very strong wish not to lose (例: 彼女は勝ち気で、ゲームに負けると、もう一度もう一度と、勝つまでやめない (kanojo wa kachiki de, geemu ni makeru to, mou ichido mou ichido to, katsu made yamenai)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 引っ込む = to withdraw; 思案 = thinking — "someone who thinks and hangs back". 勝気 is its mirror image (pushes forward); いじわる and 乱暴 are about how one treats others, not about shyness.

【9】

この絵は、色のコントラストが印象的だ。Kono e wa, iro no kontorasuto ga inshouteki da.

Underlined word: コントラスト = 対比／対照：対立する2つの違い (the difference between two opposed things).
EN: In this painting the contrast of colours is striking.
HI: इस चित्र में रंगों का विरोधाभास (कंट्रास्ट) प्रभावशाली है।
GU: આ ચિત્રમાં રંગોનો વિરોધાભાસ (કૉન્ટ્રાસ્ટ) પ્રભાવશાળી છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1使い方tsukaikatathe way (colours) are usedइस्तेमाल का तरीक़ाવાપરવાની રીત
2美しさutsukushisabeautyसुंदरताસુંદરતા
3工夫kufuuingenuity, clever devisingयुक्ति, सूझ-बूझયુક્તિ, કળા-કૌશલ
4対比taihicontrastविरोधाभास, तुलनाવિરોધાભાસ, તુલના

Answer 4 (別冊 p.75, PDF 157). Book's notes (paraphrased): コントラスト = 対比／対照, the difference between two contrasting things. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 1–3 are all things you might praise in a painting's colours, which is the trap: only 対比 carries the idea of two things set against each other, which is what "contrast" means.

【10】

うちの社長は目先の利益しか考えない人だ。Uchi no shachou wa mesaki no rieki shika kangaenai hito da.

Underlined word: 目先 = (here) 目の前のこと／その時のこと (what is immediately in front of one, the present moment).
EN: Our president is a man who thinks only of immediate profit.
HI: हमारे अध्यक्ष ऐसे व्यक्ति हैं जो सिर्फ़ तात्कालिक मुनाफ़े के बारे में सोचते हैं।
GU: અમારા પ્રમુખ એવા માણસ છે જે માત્ર તાત્કાલિક નફા વિશે જ વિચારે છે.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1将来shouraithe futureभविष्यભવિષ્ય
2自分jibunoneself (one's own profit)अपनाપોતાનું
3取引torihikibusiness dealings, transactionलेन-देन, सौदाલેવડદેવડ, સોદો
4当座touzafor the present, immediateतात्कालिक, फ़िलहाल काતાત્કાલિક, હાલ પૂરતું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.75, PDF 157). Book's notes (paraphrased): 目先 here = 当座, the thing right in front of you / of the moment. 選択肢の言葉: 3 取引 = commercial dealings (例: 我が社はA社と取引をするのをやめた (waga sha wa A-sha to torihiki o suru no o yameta)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 2. (added, not in book) 1 将来 is the exact opposite of 目先 (the 別冊's ✎ example sets 目先 against 長い目で将来を見通す). 2 自分の利益 "his own profit" is a plausible-sounding criticism but not what 目先 means.

【11】

冷夏の影響で秋野菜が軒並み値上がりした。Reika no eikyou de aki yasai ga nokinami neagari shita.

Underlined word: 軒並み = (here) どれもすべて (every one of them, without exception).
EN: Because of the cool summer, autumn vegetables have all gone up in price across the board.
HI: ठंडी गर्मी के असर से शरद ऋतु की सभी सब्ज़ियों के दाम एक साथ बढ़ गए।
GU: ઠંડા ઉનાળાની અસરથી પાનખરનાં બધાં જ શાકભાજીના ભાવ એકસાથે વધી ગયા.
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1急激にkyuugeki nisharply, suddenlyतेज़ी से, अचानकઝડપથી, અચાનક
2一様にichiyou niuniformly, all alikeएक समान रूप से, सब के सबએકસરખી રીતે, બધાં જ
3異常にijou niabnormallyअसामान्य रूप सेઅસામાન્ય રીતે
4大幅にoohaba niby a large margin, substantiallyबड़े पैमाने पर, काफ़ीમોટા પ્રમાણમાં, ઘણું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.75, PDF 157). Book's notes (paraphrased): 軒並み here = 一様に, every single one. 選択肢の言葉: 4 大幅に／大幅な = (changing) greatly (例: 不景気で給料が大幅にカットされた (fukeiki de kyuuryou ga oohaba ni katto sareta)). The 別冊 has no note for 1 or 3. (added, not in book) 軒並み literally = "a row of eaves", i.e. every house in the street → every item. The distractors describe how much / how fast prices rose; 軒並み says which items rose: all of them.

【12】

若さを保つ秘訣はどんなことですか。Wakasa o tamotsu hiketsu wa donna koto desu ka.

Underlined word: 秘訣 = 他人にはあまり知られていない効果的な方法 (an effective method that few others know).
EN: What is your secret for staying young?
HI: जवान बने रहने का आपका गुर क्या है?
GU: જુવાન રહેવાનું તમારું રહસ્ય શું છે?
OptionParaphraseEnglishHindiGujarati
1特別な価値tokubetsu na kachispecial valueविशेष मूल्यવિશેષ મૂલ્ય
2一番の問題点ichiban no mondaitenthe biggest problemसबसे बड़ी समस्याસૌથી મોટી સમસ્યા
3一番の長所ichiban no choushothe greatest strengthसबसे बड़ी ख़ूबीસૌથી મોટી ખૂબી
4特別な方法tokubetsu na houhoua special methodविशेष तरीक़ाવિશેષ રીત

Answer 4 (別冊 p.75, PDF 157). Book's notes (paraphrased): 秘訣 = an effective method that the person treasures and that others hardly know. The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) 訣 = "knack, secret method"; 秘 = "secret". So 秘訣 is a way of doing something (方法), never a value, a problem or a merit.

3 Confusion pairs — the underlined word vs. its near-miss options

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
サボるsaboru / さばくsabaku / 投げ出すnagedasu skive off / process (work) / abandon halfway Sound-alikes サボる vs さばく are opposites: 仕事をサボる (avoid it) vs 仕事をさばく (get through it). 途中で投げ出す = start, then quit.
引っ込み思案hikkomijian / 消極的shoukyokuteki / 勝気kachiki shy and retiring / passive / competitive, hates losing 引っ込み思案 ≈ 消極的 (both hang back); 勝気 (also 勝ち気) pushes forward and never gives in — the antonym.
コントラストkontorasuto / 対比taihi / 対照taishou / 工夫kufuu contrast / contrast / contrast; comparison / ingenuity 対比・対照 both paraphrase コントラスト (対照的な性格). 工夫 is the clever idea behind something (色使いの工夫), not the visual effect itself.
目先mesaki / 当座touza / 当面toumen / 将来shourai immediate / for the present / for the time being / future 目先の利益 ≈ 当座の利益 (short-term). 当面 (第5回 Q28) = "for now" with the future left open. 将来 is the long view — the antonym here.
軒並みnokinami / 一様にichiyou ni / 大幅にoohaba ni / 急激にkyuugeki ni across the board / uniformly / substantially / sharply 軒並み・一様に answer "which ones?" (all); 大幅に answers "how much?"; 急激に answers "how fast?". 軒並み値上がり vs 大幅に値上がり are different statements.
秘訣hiketsu / こつkotsu / 要領youryou / 長所chousho secret (method) / knack / the trick, efficiency / strong point 秘訣 is a special, little-known method (成功の秘訣); こつ・要領 (第6回 Q32) are the knack of doing a task well; 長所 is a merit, not a method.
Exam trap: two words in this round are glossed by the 別冊 as 問題文中の意味 — the meaning in this sentence. 目先 can also literally mean "before one's eyes" (目先が変わる = a change of scene), and 軒並み can literally mean "the row of houses". The JLPT tests the figurative sense; always paraphrase the word as it works in the sentence, not its dictionary headword.
