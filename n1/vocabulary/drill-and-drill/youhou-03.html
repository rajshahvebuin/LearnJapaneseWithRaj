2 Every question — 問題 (printed pp.70–71)

次の言葉の使い方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no kotoba no tsukaikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the sentence that uses the word best. HI: दिए गए शब्द का सबसे सही प्रयोग वाला वाक्य चुनें। GU: આપેલા શબ્દનો સૌથી યોગ્ય ઉપયોગ ધરાવતું વાક્ય પસંદ કરો.

【13】

情けないnasakenai

Word: pathetic, shameful, miserable — 別冊: 情けない = みじめ・恥ずべき・嘆かわしい (miserable; shameful; deplorable)
HI: शर्मनाक, दयनीय
GU: શરમજનક, દયનીય
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1うちの息子は気が情けなくて、人前に出るのがいやだと言う。Uchi no musuko wa ki ga nasakenakute, hitomae ni deru no ga iya da to iu.My son is timid and says he hates appearing in front of people.मेरा बेटा डरपोक है और कहता है कि उसे लोगों के सामने आना पसंद नहीं।મારો દીકરો બીકણ છે અને કહે છે કે તેને લોકો સામે આવવું ગમતું નથી.
2この国は失業者からも情けなく税金を取り立てる。Kono kuni wa shitsugyousha kara mo nasakenaku zeikin o toritateru.This country mercilessly collects tax even from the unemployed.यह देश बेरोज़गारों से भी बेरहमी से कर वसूलता है।આ દેશ બેરોજગારો પાસેથી પણ નિર્દયતાથી કર વસૂલે છે.
3何度も頼んでいるのに、彼女は情けない返事しかくれない。Nando mo tanonde iru noni, kanojo wa nasakenai henji shika kurenai.Even though I've asked many times, she only gives me curt replies.कई बार कहने पर भी वह सिर्फ़ रूखा जवाब देती है।ઘણી વાર કહ્યા છતાં તે માત્ર લૂખો જવાબ આપે છે.
4弱いと思っていた相手に2回続けて負けるなんて、情けない。Yowai to omotte ita aite ni nikai tsuzukete makeru nante, nasakenai.Losing twice in a row to an opponent I thought was weak – how pathetic.जिसे कमज़ोर समझा था उससे लगातार दो बार हारना — कितनी शर्म की बात है।જેને નબળો માન્યો હતો તેની સામે સતત બે વાર હારવું — કેટલું શરમજનક.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). Book's definition: 情けない = みじめ・恥ずべき・嘆かわしい (miserable; shameful; deplorable). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 小さくて (chiisakute) "timid (気が小さい)"; 2 → should be 情け容赦なく (nasake yousha naku) "mercilessly"; 3 → should be そっけない (sokkenai) "curt, cold". (added, not in book) Although it contains 情け (compassion), 情けない does not mean "without mercy" — that is 情け容赦なく. 情けない is the speaker's shame or dismay at a miserable situation (often one's own).

【14】

慎むtsutsushimu

Word: to be careful about, restrain (one's words or behaviour) — 別冊: 慎む = （正解文中の意味）気をつける (meaning in the correct sentence: be careful)
HI: संयम रखना, सावधान रहना
GU: સંયમ રાખવો, સાવધ રહેવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1どうぞ慎まないで、たくさん召し上がってください。Douzo tsutsushimanaide, takusan meshiagatte kudasai.Please don't hold back – eat as much as you like.कृपया संकोच मत कीजिए, जी भरकर खाइए।કૃપા કરીને સંકોચ ન કરો, મન ભરીને જમો.
2お客様と話すときは言葉を慎むこと。Okyakusama to hanasu toki wa kotoba o tsutsushimu koto.Be careful with your words when you speak to customers.ग्राहकों से बात करते समय शब्दों में संयम रखें।ગ્રાહકો સાથે વાત કરતી વખતે શબ્દોમાં સંયમ રાખો.
3社長の後ろには秘書が慎んでいた。Shachou no ushiro ni wa hisho ga tsutsushinde ita.The secretary was standing by behind the president.अध्यक्ष के पीछे सचिव तैयार खड़ा था।પ્રમુખની પાછળ સચિવ તૈયાર ઊભો હતો.
4彼女はまだ幼いながら、善いことと悪いことをきちんと慎んでいる。Kanojo wa mada osanai nagara, yoi koto to warui koto o kichinto tsutsushinde iru.Though still young, she clearly knows right from wrong.छोटी होते हुए भी वह अच्छे-बुरे का फ़र्क़ अच्छी तरह समझती है।નાની હોવા છતાં તે સારા-ખરાબનો ફરક બરાબર સમજે છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). Book's definition: 慎む = （正解文中の意味）気をつける (meaning in the correct sentence: be careful). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 遠慮しないで (enryo shinaide) "don't hold back"; 3 → should be 控えていた (hikaete ita) "was waiting in attendance"; 4 → should be わきまえている (wakimaete iru) "knows, discerns (right from wrong)". (added, not in book) 慎む takes 言葉・行動・酒・発言 as object: you hold yourself back so as not to cause trouble. Polite "don't hold back" to a guest is 遠慮しないで.

【15】

妥結daketsu

Word: settlement, agreement reached in negotiations
HI: बातचीत से समझौता होना
GU: વાટાઘાટોથી સમજૂતી થવી
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1賃上げ交渉の妥結まで、社員は一致団結して経営者と闘った。Chin'age koushou no daketsu made, shain wa itchi danketsu shite keieisha to tatakatta.Until the wage negotiations were settled, the employees stood united against management.वेतन-वार्ता का समझौता होने तक कर्मचारी एकजुट होकर प्रबंधन से लड़े।પગાર-વાટાઘાટોની સમજૂતી થાય ત્યાં સુધી કર્મચારીઓ એકજૂટ થઈ મેનેજમેન્ટ સામે લડ્યા.
2社会生活を営む上では、折り合いや妥結も時には必要になる。Shakai seikatsu o itonamu ue de wa, oriai ya daketsu mo toki ni wa hitsuyou ni naru.In social life, give-and-take and compromise are sometimes necessary.सामाजिक जीवन में कभी-कभी तालमेल और समझौता भी ज़रूरी होता है।સામાજિક જીવનમાં ક્યારેક તાલમેલ અને બાંધછોડ પણ જરૂરી હોય છે.
3指輪の交換が行われ、2人の婚約がめでたく妥結に至った。Yubiwa no koukan ga okonaware, futari no kon'yaku ga medetaku daketsu ni itatta.Rings were exchanged and the couple's engagement was happily made official.अँगूठियाँ बदली गईं और दोनों की सगाई ख़ुशी से पक्की हो गई।વીંટીઓની આપ-લે થઈ અને બંનેની સગાઈ આનંદથી પાકી થઈ.
4このチームは、選手の妥結が強い。Kono chiimu wa, senshu no daketsu ga tsuyoi.This team's players are strongly united.इस टीम के खिलाड़ियों में ज़बरदस्त एकजुटता है।આ ટીમના ખેલાડીઓમાં જબરદસ્ત એકતા છે.

Answer 1 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 2 → should be 妥協 (dakyou) "compromise"; 3 → should be 成立 (seiritsu) "coming into being, conclusion"; 4 → should be 結束 (kessoku) "unity, solidarity". (added, not in book) 妥結 is used for negotiations between opposing sides (労使交渉・賃金交渉が妥結する). The everyday "compromise" between people is 妥協.

【16】

ことごとくkotogotoku

Word: every single one, without exception — 別冊: ことごとく = どれも全部 (every one of them, all)
HI: एक-एक करके सब, बिना अपवाद
GU: એકે-એક, અપવાદ વગર
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1過密スケジュールでことごとく疲れている。Kamitsu sukejuuru de kotogotoku tsukarete iru.I'm worn out from my packed schedule.ठसाठस कार्यक्रम से मैं बुरी तरह थक गया हूँ।ભરચક કાર્યક્રમથી હું સાવ થાકી ગયો છું.
2今年は例年に比べて、ことごとく厳しい暑さが続いている。Kotoshi wa reinen ni kurabete, kotogotoku kibishii atsusa ga tsuzuite iru.This year, compared with usual, the heat has been especially severe.इस साल हर साल की तुलना में ख़ास तौर पर कड़ी गर्मी पड़ रही है।આ વર્ષે દર વર્ષની સરખામણીમાં ખાસ કરીને સખત ગરમી પડે છે.
3就職試験にことごとく失敗して、すっかり自信をなくしてしまった。Shuushoku shiken ni kotogotoku shippai shite, sukkari jishin o nakushite shimatta.I failed every single job exam and have lost all my confidence.नौकरी की हर एक परीक्षा में असफल होकर मेरा आत्मविश्वास पूरी तरह टूट गया।નોકરીની દરેક પરીક્ષામાં નાપાસ થઈ મારો આત્મવિશ્વાસ સાવ તૂટી ગયો.
4私は毎日朝刊にことごとく目を通してから、出社しています。Watashi wa mainichi choukan ni kotogotoku me o tooshite kara, shussha shite imasu.Every day I look over the morning paper before going to work.मैं रोज़ सुबह का अख़बार एक बार देखकर दफ़्तर जाता हूँ।હું રોજ સવારનું છાપું એક વાર જોઈને ઑફિસ જાઉં છું.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). Book's definition: ことごとく = どれも全部 (every one of them, all). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be へとへとに / くたくたに (hetoheto ni / kutakuta ni) "exhausted"; 2 → should be ことに (koto ni) "especially"; 4 → should be 一通り (hitotoori) "roughly, once through". (added, not in book) ことごとく needs a set of many things that all share the same fate (試験・計画・意見 … ことごとく失敗する・反対される). One person's tiredness or one season's heat is not a set.

【17】

一人前ichininmae

Word: fully fledged, a full adult/professional; also: one portion — 別冊: 一人前 = （正解文中の意味）大人や専門家としての能力があること (meaning in the correct sentence: having the ability of an adult or a professional)
HI: पूरी तरह योग्य/परिपक्व; एक व्यक्ति का हिस्सा
GU: સંપૂર્ણ લાયક/પરિપક્વ; એક વ્યક્તિનો ભાગ
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1サッカーの観戦チケットが一人前余っているんだけど、一緒にどうですか。Sakkaa no kansen chiketto ga ichininmae amatte iru n da kedo, issho ni dou desu ka.I have one spare ticket for the football match – would you like to come?मेरे पास फ़ुटबॉल मैच का एक टिकट बचा है, साथ चलोगे?મારી પાસે ફૂટબૉલ મૅચની એક ટિકિટ વધારાની છે, સાથે આવશો?
2彼は懸命に修行に励んでいるが、一人前の職人になるにはまだまだだ。Kare wa kenmei ni shugyou ni hagende iru ga, ichininmae no shokunin ni naru ni wa madamada da.He is training hard, but he still has a long way to go before becoming a fully fledged craftsman.वह मेहनत से सीख रहा है, पर पूरा कारीगर बनने में अभी बहुत समय है।તે મહેનતથી તાલીમ લે છે, પણ પૂરો કારીગર બનવામાં હજી ઘણી વાર છે.
34月から親元を離れて、一人前暮らしをすることにした。Shigatsu kara oyamoto o hanarete, ichininmae-gurashi o suru koto ni shita.From April I've decided to leave my parents' home and live on my own.अप्रैल से मैंने माता-पिता का घर छोड़कर अकेले रहने का फ़ैसला किया।એપ્રિલથી મેં માતા-પિતાનું ઘર છોડી એકલા રહેવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
4私はまだ一人前ですので、これからもご指導のほど、よろしくお願いいたします。Watashi wa mada ichininmae desu node, korekara mo go-shidou no hodo, yoroshiku onegai itashimasu.I am still only half-trained, so I ask for your continued guidance.मैं अभी अधकचरा हूँ, कृपया आगे भी मार्गदर्शन दीजिए।હું હજી અધકચરો છું, કૃપા કરીને આગળ પણ માર્ગદર્શન આપો.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). Book's definition: 一人前 = （正解文中の意味）大人や専門家としての能力があること (meaning in the correct sentence: having the ability of an adult or a professional). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be 一枚 (ichimai) "one (ticket)"; 3 → should be 一人暮らし (hitorigurashi) "living alone"; 4 → should be 半人前 (hanninmae) "half-trained, not yet competent". (added, not in book) 一人前 has two senses: a full portion for one person (すし一人前) and being a fully capable adult or professional (一人前の職人・一人前になる). Tickets are counted with 枚; "living alone" is 一人暮らし.

GU: ડરથી ધબકારા વધવા, ચિંતિત રહેવું
OptionSentenceEnglish (intended meaning)HindiGujarati
1人気歌手のコンサートが始まるのを、観客がはらはらしながら待っている。Ninki kashu no konsaato ga hajimaru no o, kankyaku ga harahara shinagara matte iru.The audience are waiting excitedly for the popular singer's concert to start.दर्शक लोकप्रिय गायक के कॉन्सर्ट के शुरू होने का उत्साह से इंतज़ार कर रहे हैं।પ્રેક્ષકો લોકપ્રિય ગાયકના કૉન્સર્ટ શરૂ થવાની ઉત્સાહથી રાહ જુએ છે.
2歩き始めたばかりの孫がけがをしないかと、私はいつもはらはらしている。Arukihajimeta bakari no mago ga kega o shinai ka to, watashi wa itsumo harahara shite iru.My grandchild has only just started walking, and I'm always on edge in case they get hurt.अभी-अभी चलना सीखा पोता कहीं चोट न खा ले, इस डर से मेरा दिल हमेशा धड़कता रहता है।હમણાં જ ચાલતાં શીખેલો પૌત્ર ક્યાંક વાગી ન બેસે, એ ડરથી મારું હૃદય ધબકતું રહે છે.
3両親が留守なのをいいことに、弟はすっかりはらはらしている。Ryoushin ga rusu na no o ii koto ni, otouto wa sukkari harahara shite iru.Taking advantage of our parents being away, my little brother is completely relaxed and carefree.माता-पिता के घर पर न होने का फ़ायदा उठाकर छोटा भाई पूरी मौज में है।માતા-પિતા ઘરે નથી તેનો લાભ લઈ નાનો ભાઈ સાવ મોજમાં છે.
4同窓会のためにわざわざ着物を買ってきた母は、はらはらしている。Dousoukai no tame ni wazawaza kimono o katte kita haha wa, harahara shite iru.My mother, who went out of her way to buy a kimono for the reunion, is in high spirits.पुराने सहपाठियों के मिलन के लिए ख़ास तौर पर किमोनो ख़रीद लाई माँ बहुत ख़ुश है।જૂના સહપાઠીઓના મિલન માટે ખાસ કિમોનો ખરીદી લાવેલી મા ખૂબ ઉત્સાહિત છે.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.87–88, PDF 169–170). Book's definition: はらはらする = feel nervous, worried or scared (the 別冊 gloss). 正しい例文 (the 別冊's fixes for the wrong options): 1 → should be わくわくしながら (wakuwaku shinagara) "excitedly"; 3 → should be のびのびしている (nobinobi shite iru) "is relaxed, carefree"; 4 → should be うきうきしている (ukiuki shite iru) "is cheerful, in high spirits". (added, not in book) はらはら is fear that something bad may happen, usually while watching someone else (a child, an acrobat, a risky driver). Happy anticipation is わくわく / うきうき.

3 Confusion pairs — the headword vs the words the book substitutes

Each row sets the headword against the words the 別冊 put into the wrong sentences (正しい例文). The "Key difference" column is ours (added, not in book).

FormMeaningKey difference
情けないnasakenai / 小さくてchiisakute / 情け容赦なくnasake yousha naku / そっけないsokkenai pathetic, shameful, miserable / timid (気が小さい) / mercilessly / curt, cold 情けない = pathetic, shameful; 情け容赦なく = without mercy; そっけない = curt, unfriendly (reply); 気が小さい = timid. A literal-kanji trap: 情け + ない ≠ "merciless".
慎むtsutsushimu / 遠慮しないでenryo shinaide / 控えていたhikaete ita / わきまえているwakimaete iru to be careful about, restrain (one's words or behaviour) / don't hold back / was waiting in attendance / knows, discerns (right from wrong) 慎む = restrain one's words/conduct; 遠慮する = hold back out of politeness; 控える = (1) refrain, (2) wait in attendance (後ろに控える); わきまえる = know and observe (善悪をわきまえる). 控える overlaps with 慎む in "refrain", which is why 3 is tempting.
妥結daketsu / 妥協dakyou / 成立seiritsu / 結束kessoku settlement, agreement reached in negotiations / compromise / coming into being, conclusion / unity, solidarity 妥結 = negotiations reach agreement; 妥協 = compromise, giving ground; 成立 = (a contract, engagement, bill) is concluded; 結束 = unity of a group. Same 妥 in 妥結/妥協; same 結 in 妥結/結束 — classic look-alike set.
ことごとくkotogotoku / へとへとに / くたくたにhetoheto ni / kutakuta ni / ことにkoto ni / 一通りhitotoori every single one, without exception / exhausted / especially / roughly, once through ことごとく = all of them, one after another (often negative results); ことに = especially; 一通り = going through once, roughly; へとへと・くたくた = dead tired. ことごとく vs ことに: both start こと〜 but mean "all" vs "especially".
一人前ichininmae / 一枚ichimai / 一人暮らしhitorigurashi / 半人前hanninmae fully fledged, a full adult/professional; also: one portion / one (ticket) / living alone / half-trained, not yet competent 一人前 = fully competent / one serving; 半人前 = not yet competent (its opposite); 一人暮らし = living by oneself; 一枚 = one flat item. 4 says the opposite of what the speaker means.
はらはらするharahara suru / わくわくしながらwakuwaku shinagara / のびのびしているnobinobi shite iru / うきうきしているukiuki shite iru to be on edge, anxious (watching something risky) / excitedly / is relaxed, carefree / is cheerful, in high spirits はらはら = anxious (danger); わくわく = excited (looking forward); うきうき = cheerful, buoyant; のびのび = free and relaxed. All onomatopoeia for feelings — only はらはら is negative.
Exam trap: watch for literal-kanji traps: 情けない is not "merciless" (情け容赦なく), 慎む is not "to wait" (控える), 一人前 is not "one ticket" (一枚). And in the 〜〜する feeling words (はらはら・わくわく・うきうき・のびのび), first decide whether the feeling is fear or joy.
