2 Every question — 問題 (p.42)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【43】

重役たちの反対にもかかわらず、社長は終始（　）方針を崩さない。Juuyakutachi no hantai ni mo kakawarazu, shachou wa shuushi (___) houshin o kuzusanai.

Full sentence: 重役たちの反対にもかかわらず、社長は終始一貫して方針を崩さない。Juuyakutachi no hantai ni mo kakawarazu, shachou wa shuushi ikkan shite houshin o kuzusanai.
EN: Despite the directors' opposition, the president consistently refuses to budge from his policy.
HI: निदेशकों के विरोध के बावजूद अध्यक्ष शुरू से आख़िर तक अपनी नीति पर अड़े हुए हैं।
GU: ડિરેક્ટરોના વિરોધ છતાં પ્રમુખ શરૂઆતથી અંત સુધી પોતાની નીતિ પર અડગ છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1貫通してkantsuu shitepass through, pierce (貫通する)आर-पार निकलनाઆરપાર નીકળવું
2通過してtsuuka shitepass (through/by) (通過する)से होकर गुज़रनाપસાર થવું
3開通してkaitsuu shiteopen to traffic (開通する)यातायात के लिए खुलनाવાહનવ્યવહાર માટે ખુલ્લું થવું
4一貫してikkan shiteconsistently (一貫する)लगातार एक-साસતત એકસરખું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.47, PDF 129). Book's notes (paraphrased): 一貫する = continue without changing one's ideas or methods to the end. 選択肢の言葉: 1 貫通する = (a thing) passes right through (例: when あの山を貫通するトンネル (ano yama o kantsuu suru tonneru) is finished, the trip to the next station will be much shorter); 3 開通する = (trains, roads) go into operation (例: 新しい新幹線が開通して (atarashii shinkansen ga kaitsuu shite), it's very convenient). The 別冊 has no note for 2 通過する. (added, not in book) 終始一貫 is a four-kanji idiom ("consistent from start to finish"); 終始 is your clue. The others are about tunnels, trains and roads.

【44】

日本には1月7日の朝に7種類の野菜が入った粥を食べる（　）がある。Nihon ni wa ichigatsu nanoka no asa ni nanashurui no yasai ga haitta kayu o taberu (___) ga aru.

Full sentence: 日本には1月7日の朝に7種類の野菜が入った粥を食べる風習がある。Nihon ni wa ichigatsu nanoka no asa ni nanashurui no yasai ga haitta kayu o taberu fuushuu ga aru.
EN: In Japan there is a custom of eating rice porridge with seven kinds of greens on the morning of 7 January.
HI: जापान में 7 जनवरी की सुबह सात तरह की सब्ज़ियों वाला दलिया (चावल का) खाने की परंपरा है।
GU: જાપાનમાં 7 જાન્યુઆરીની સવારે સાત પ્રકારનાં શાકવાળી ભાતની રાબ (કાંજી) ખાવાની પરંપરા છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1慣用kan'youcommon usage (of expressions)आम प्रयोग (मुहावरे)સામાન્ય પ્રયોગ (રૂઢિપ્રયોગ)
2趣向shukoua clever idea / plan (to make something enjoyable)मज़ेदार बनाने की युक्तिમજેદાર બનાવવાની યુક્તિ
3風俗fuuzokucustoms and manners (of an era or region)रहन-सहन, सामाजिक रीतिરહેણીકરણી, સામાજિક રીત
4風習fuushuucustom, traditionरिवाज, परंपराરિવાજ, પરંપરા

Answer 4 (別冊 p.47–48, PDF 129–130). Book's notes (paraphrased): 風習 = custom, tradition. 選択肢の言葉: 1 慣用 = something widely and often used (例: 食わず嫌い is a 慣用表現 (kan'you hyougen) meaning disliking something without trying it); 2 趣向 = an idea for enjoying something to the full (例: the next party will have 趣向をこらしたゲーム (shukou o korashita geemu)); 3 風俗 = the habits, rules and long-standing practices of a region or period (例: my brother 東北地方の風俗を研究している (Touhoku chihou no fuuzoku o kenkyuu shite iru)). (added, not in book) 風俗 is close, but it is the overall way of life studied as a whole; one specific practice (eating 七草粥 on 7 January) is a 風習. In everyday modern Japanese 風俗 alone can also refer to the adult-entertainment industry — another reason to prefer 風習 here.

【45】

彼は（　）自分の罪を認めた。Kare wa (___) jibun no tsumi o mitometa.

Full sentence: 彼はいさぎよく自分の罪を認めた。Kare wa isagiyoku jibun no tsumi o mitometa.
EN: He admitted his guilt honestly, without excuses.
HI: उसने बिना कोई बहाना बनाए साफ़ दिल से अपना अपराध मान लिया।
GU: તેણે કોઈ બહાનું કાઢ્યા વગર ચોખ્ખા દિલથી પોતાનો ગુનો કબૂલ કર્યો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1はてしなくhateshinakuendlessly (果てしない)अंतहीन रूप सेઅનંત રીતે
2いさぎよくisagiyokuhonourably, without excuses (潔い)बिना बहाने, साफ़ दिल सेબહાનાં વગર, ચોખ્ખા દિલથી
3うしろめたくushirometakuwith a guilty conscience (後ろめたい)मन में अपराध-बोध के साथમનમાં અપરાધભાવ સાથે
4ねばりづよくnebarizuyokutenaciously (粘り強い)दृढ़ता से, धैर्य सेદૃઢતાથી, ધીરજથી

Answer 2 (別冊 p.48, PDF 130). Book's notes (paraphrased): いさぎよく (潔い) = without regret or clinging. 選択肢の言葉: 1 はてしなく (果てしない) = never-ending; huge, wide, long (例: beyond このはてしない海 (kono hateshinai umi) lies America); 3 うしろめたく (後ろめたい) = feeling you did wrong; ashamed before your conscience (例: I keep troubling you, so asking again うしろめたいのですが (ushirometai no desu ga)…); 4 ねばりづよく (粘り強い) = able to persevere without giving up (例: ねばりづよい努力 (nebarizuyoi doryoku) completed this great project). (added, not in book) 罪を認める + an adverb of manner: いさぎよく認める / 謝る / 負けを認める is the set phrase. うしろめたい is a feeling, not a way of confessing.

【47】

工場長の指令に反発して、工員が仕事を（　）した。Koujouchou no shirei ni hanpatsu shite, kouin ga shigoto o (___) shita.

1ボイコットboikottoboycottबहिष्कारબહિષ્કાર

3キャンセルkyanserucancelरद्द करनाરદ કરવું
4カムバックkamubakkucomebackवापसीપુનરાગમન

Answer 1 (別冊 p.48, PDF 130). Book's notes (paraphrased): ボイコットする = boycott. 選択肢の言葉: 2 カット(する) = 削る, cut down (例: if wages カットされれば (katto sarereba), the workers soon can't make ends meet); 3 キャンセル(する) = cancel (例: a typhoon was coming, so I 旅行をキャンセルした (ryokou o kyanseru shita)); 4 カムバック(する) = come back (例: the actor who was off sick 舞台にカムバックした (butai ni kamubakku shita)). (added, not in book) 反発して (in protest) → refusing to work as a protest = ボイコット. キャンセル is cancelling a booking or plan, not a protest action.

【49】

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
一貫ikkan / 貫通kantsuu / 開通kaitsuu / 通過tsuuka consistent / pierce / open (to traffic) / pass through 一貫 is about attitude or policy (終始一貫); 貫通 = a tunnel or bullet goes right through; 開通 = a new line or road opens; 通過 = a train passes a station, a bill passes parliament.
風習fuushuu / 風俗fuuzoku / 慣用kan'you / 趣向shukou custom / manners of an era / common usage / clever idea 風習 = a specific traditional practice; 風俗 = the general way of life (also a euphemism for adult businesses); 慣用 = conventional use of words (慣用句); 趣向 = a creative touch (趣向をこらす).
いさぎよいisagiyoi vs うしろめたいushirometai honourable, no excuses vs guilty conscience Both appear around wrongdoing: いさぎよく罪を認める (admit like a decent person) vs うしろめたい気持ち (feeling guilty inside).
ねばりづよいnebarizuyoi vs 粘るnebaru (第5回) tenacious vs hang on Same root. ねばりづよい努力・交渉 (adjective); 最後まで粘る (verb).
さしあたりsashiatari / とおからずtookarazu / とっくにtokku ni / さぞかしsazokashi for now / before long / long ago / surely Time words with different directions: さしあたり = now, temporarily; とおからず = soon, in the future; とっくに = already, in the past. さぞかし is a guess about feelings (さぞかしお疲れでしょう).
ボイコットboikotto vs キャンセルkyanseru boycott vs cancel ボイコット = refuse to take part as a protest; キャンセル = call off something you had booked or planned.
的mato / ねらいnerai / 目標mokuhyou / 目印mejirushi target / aim / goal / landmark 的 = what is aimed at (非難の的, 的外れ); ねらい = the aim behind an action (この企画のねらい); 目標 = a goal to reach; 目印 = a mark to find something (駅の目印).
Exam trap: 的 has two readings. Alone it is まと (非難の的, 的外れ); as a suffix it is てき (衝動的, 積極的 — 第5回). If the blank stands as a noun after の, read it まと.
