2 Every question — 問題 (p.49)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【92】

模擬試験の成績はよかったが、安心はできない。（　）まであと1か月がんばろう。Mogi shiken no seiseki wa yokatta ga, anshin wa dekinai. (___) made ato ikkagetsu ganbarou.

Full sentence: 模擬試験の成績はよかったが、安心はできない。本番まであと1か月がんばろう。Mogi shiken no seiseki wa yokatta ga, anshin wa dekinai. Honban made ato ikkagetsu ganbarou.
EN: My mock exam results were good, but I can't relax. One more month of hard work until the real thing.
HI: मॉक टेस्ट में नंबर अच्छे आए, पर निश्चिंत नहीं हो सकते। असली परीक्षा तक एक महीना और मेहनत करते हैं।
GU: મૉક ટેસ્ટમાં માર્ક્સ સારા આવ્યા, પણ નિરાંતે બેસી શકાય નહીં. ખરી પરીક્ષા સુધી હજી એક મહિનો મહેનત કરીએ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1本場honbathe home / centre (of a product or activity)असली घर, मूल स्थानમૂળ સ્થાન, ઘર
2本番honbanthe real performance / examअसली मौक़ाખરો મોકો

4本音honneone's true feelingsमन की असली बातમનની સાચી વાત

Answer 2 (別冊 p.59, PDF 141). Book's notes (paraphrased): 本番 = the real test / real performance. 選択肢の言葉: 1 本場 = where a product comes from, or where an activity started or is thriving (例: I decided to study in Britain 本場の英語を学ぶために (honba no eigo o manabu tame ni); Kobe beef in Kobe — さすが本場だけあって (sasuga honba dake atte) delicious); 3 本体 = the central part of a thing (例: this PC's 本体価格が6万円 (hontai kakaku ga rokuman-en), under ¥100,000 even with accessories); 4 本音 = one's true feelings and thoughts (例: at the drinking party the alcohol みんなの本音が出た (minna no honne ga deta)). (added, not in book) 模擬試験 (mock exam) contrasts with the real exam → 本番.

【94】

ホームステイした家の人たちは、（　）彼女の面倒を見た。Hoomusutei shita ie no hitotachi wa, (___) kanojo no mendou o mita.

Full sentence: ホームステイした家の人たちは、なにくれとなく彼女の面倒を見た。Hoomusutei shita ie no hitotachi wa, nanikure to naku kanojo no mendou o mita.
EN: Her host family looked after her in all sorts of ways.
HI: जिस परिवार में वह होमस्टे पर रही, उन्होंने हर तरह से उसका ख़याल रखा।
GU: જે કુટુંબમાં તે હોમસ્ટે પર રહી, તેમણે દરેક રીતે તેની સંભાળ રાખી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1どこからともなくdoko kara tomo nakufrom somewhere or otherन जाने कहाँ सेખબર નહીં ક્યાંથી
2なにくれとなくnanikure to nakuin all sorts of waysहर तरह सेદરેક રીતે
3どことなくdoko to nakusomehow (can't say exactly why)कुछ-कुछ, न जाने क्योंકંઈક અંશે, ખબર નહીં કેમ
4なにはなくともnani wa naku tomoeven if there is nothing elseऔर कुछ हो न होબીજું કંઈ હોય કે ન હોય

Answer 2 (別冊 p.59–60, PDF 141–142). Book's notes (paraphrased): なにくれとなく = あれこれ / いろいろ. 選択肢の言葉: 1 どこからともなく = from somewhere, you can't tell where (例: walking along, どこからともなくいいにおいがしてきた (doko kara tomo naku ii nioi ga shite kita) — plum blossom somewhere); 3 どことなく = you can't say exactly where, but… (例: あの2人はどことなく似ている (ano futari wa doko to naku nite iru) — maybe brothers); 4 なにはなくとも = even without anything else (this comes first) (例: なにはなくとも健康が一番だ (nani wa naku tomo kenkou ga ichiban da)). (added, not in book) 面倒を見る (take care of) in many ways → なにくれとなく.

【95】

日本では、食事のときに器を持たないで食べるのは（　）だとされる。Nihon de wa, shokuji no toki ni utsuwa o motanaide taberu no wa (___) da to sareru.

Full sentence: 日本では、食事のときに器を持たないで食べるのは無作法だとされる。Nihon de wa, shokuji no toki ni utsuwa o motanaide taberu no wa busahou da to sareru.
EN: In Japan, eating without holding up your bowl is considered bad manners.
HI: जापान में खाते समय कटोरा हाथ में उठाए बिना खाना बदतमीज़ी माना जाता है।
GU: જાપાનમાં જમતી વખતે વાટકો હાથમાં લીધા વગર ખાવું અસભ્યતા ગણાય છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1不法fuhouillegal, unlawfulग़ैर-क़ानूनीગેરકાયદે
2無作法busahoubad mannersबदतमीज़ीઅસભ્યતા
3失礼shitsureirude, impolite (to someone)अशिष्ट (किसी के प्रति)અવિનયી (કોઈ પ્રત્યે)
4無礼bureiinsolent, disrespectfulगुस्ताख़, अपमानजनकઉદ્ધત, અપમાનજનક

Answer 2 (別冊 p.60, PDF 142). Book's notes (paraphrased): 無作法 (不作法)(な) = 行儀が悪い, ill-mannered. 選択肢の言葉: 1 不法(な) = against the law or reason (例: parking is banned on this narrow road, but 不法駐車をする人 (fuhou chuusha o suru hito) never stops; a friend who stayed after his visa expired was arrested for 不法滞在 (fuhou taizai)); 4 無礼(な) = breaking courtesy (例: I was angry at 謝らない若者の無礼な態度 (ayamaranai wakamono no burei na taido) after he bumped into me). The 別冊 has no note for 3 失礼. (added, not in book) Table manners (行儀) → 無作法. 失礼 and 無礼 are rudeness toward a person; holding your bowl is etiquette, 作法.

【96】

交通量の多い道路で子どもが遊んでいた。見ていて（　）した。Koutsuuryou no ooi douro de kodomo ga asonde ita. Mite ite (___) shita.

Full sentence: 交通量の多い道路で子どもが遊んでいた。見ていてはらはらした。Koutsuuryou no ooi douro de kodomo ga asonde ita. Mite ite harahara shita.
EN: A child was playing on a busy road. I was on tenterhooks just watching.
HI: भारी ट्रैफ़िक वाली सड़क पर एक बच्चा खेल रहा था। देखकर मेरा दिल धक-धक करने लगा।
GU: ભારે ટ્રાફિકવાળા રસ્તા પર એક બાળક રમતું હતું. જોઈને મારો જીવ અધ્ધર થઈ ગયો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1はらはらharaharaanxious, on edgeघबराहट, दिल धक-धकગભરાટ, જીવ અધ્ધર
2ばらばらbarabarascattered, all differentबिखरा हुआ, अलग-अलगવિખરાયેલું, અલગ અલગ
3こそこそkosokososneakily, secretlyचोरी-छिपेછાનુંમાનું
4ごそごそgosogosorustling (sound of things rubbing)खड़खड़ (चीज़ें टटोलने की आवाज़)ખડખડ (વસ્તુઓ ફંફોસવાનો અવાજ)

Answer 1 (別冊 p.60, PDF 142). Book's notes (paraphrased): はらはらする = feel nervous, worried whether something will be all right. 選択肢の言葉: 2 ばらばら = everyone different, not together (例: 出席者の意見はばらばらで (shussekisha no iken wa barabara de), no conclusion was reached); 3 こそこそ = hiding so as not to be noticed, because being found out would be a problem (例: there are junior-high pupils こそこそたばこを吸っている (kosokoso tabako o sutte iru) in a corner of the school yard); 4 ごそごそ = the sound of things rubbing together (例: "何、ごそごそさがしてるの？ (nani, gosogoso sagashiteru no?)" "My key — I thought I put it in my bag"). (added, not in book) Watching a child in danger → anxiety: はらはらする.

【97】

EN: Hit by the wave of global recession, our company too remains in a difficult financial situation.
HI: विश्वव्यापी मंदी की लहर के असर से हमारी कंपनी की हालत भी लगातार कठिन बनी हुई है।

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1不順fujunirregular, unseasonable (weather)अनियमित (मौसम)અનિયમિત (હવામાન)
2不調fuchouout of order, in poor conditionख़राब हालत, गड़बड़ખરાબ હાલત, ગડબડ
3不況fukyourecessionमंदीમંદી

Answer 3 (別冊 p.60–61, PDF 142–143). Book's notes (paraphrased): 不況 = bad economy, recession. 選択肢の言葉: 1 不順 = not going smoothly (例: at the change of seasons 天候が不順になりがちだ (tenkou ga fujun ni narigachi da)); 2 不調 = not in good condition (例: the plane returned to Haneda エンジンの不調で (enjin no fuchou de)); 4 不振 = lacking vigour; grades or business not as good as hoped (例: in summer the heat causes 食欲不振 (shokuyoku fushin); golfer A is 成績不振 (seiseki fushin) and his prize money is falling). (added, not in book) 世界的な〜の波 → an economic condition affecting the whole world: 不況. 不振 is about one company's or player's results (業績不振).

【98】

1盛況seikyougreat success, packed (event)भारी भीड़, ज़बरदस्त सफलताભારે ભીડ, જબરી સફળતા

3限定genteilimited (to)सीमितમર્યાદિત

Answer 4 (別冊 p.61, PDF 143). Book's notes (paraphrased): もちきり = filled with (one topic). 選択肢の言葉: 1 盛況 = thriving, well attended (例: the international trade fair 連日押すな押すなの盛況だ (renjitsu osu na osu na no seikyou da)); 2 もちこし (持ち越し) = carried over (例: first we will discuss 前回からの持ち越しの議案 (zenkai kara no mochikoshi no gian)); 3 限定 = limitation (例: this sale is お客様限定 (o-kyakusama gentei) — only for customers who bring this postcard). (added, not in book) 〜でもちきりだ = "everyone is talking about ~" is a set pattern; もちこし looks similar but means postponed.

本番honban / 本場honba / 本体hontai / 本音honne the real event / home of / main body / true feelings 本番に強い (good under real conditions); 本場の味; パソコン本体; 本音と建前. Note ほんば vs ほんばん.
ガイドブックgaidobukku / テキストtekisuto / ベストセラーbesutoseraa / ミスプリントmisupurinto guidebook / textbook / best-seller / misprint Four book-related loanwords; the context (旅先) decides. テキスト in Japanese usually = course textbook.
なにくれとなくnanikure to naku / どことなくdoko to naku / どこからともなくdoko kara tomo naku / なにはなくともnani wa naku tomo in all sorts of ways / somehow / from somewhere / above all else なにくれとなく世話をする; どことなく寂しい; どこからともなく聞こえる; なにはなくとも健康第一.
無作法busahou / 無礼burei / 失礼shitsurei / 不法fuhou bad manners / insolent / impolite / illegal 無作法 = breaking etiquette (食べ方が無作法); 無礼 = disrespect to a person (無礼な態度); 失礼 = everyday "rude/excuse me"; 不法 = against the law (不法駐車).
はらはらharahara / ばらばらbarabara / こそこそkosokoso / ごそごそgosogoso anxious / scattered / sneakily / rustling Voiced vs unvoiced pairs change the meaning completely: はらはら (worry) ↔ ばらばら (apart); こそこそ (secretly) ↔ ごそごそ (rummaging noise).
不況fukyou / 不振fushin / 不調fuchou / 不順fujun recession / slump / bad condition / unseasonable 世界的な不況 (economy as a whole); 業績不振・食欲不振; エンジン・体の不調; 天候不順.
もちきりmochikiri / もちこしmochikoshi / 盛況seikyou only topic / carried over / packed, thriving 〜の話でもちきり; 結論は次回にもちこし; 大盛況のうちに終わる.
Exam trap: four 不〜 words (Q97) all mean "not going well" — the difference is what is not going well: the economy (不況), results (不振), a machine or the body (不調), the weather (不順). Ask "what is the subject?" before choosing.
