2 Every question — 問題 (p.43)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【50】

チョコレートには脳を（　）して元気にする成分が含まれているらしい。Chokoreeto ni wa nou o (___) shite genki ni suru seibun ga fukumarete iru rashii.

Full sentence: チョコレートには脳を活性化して元気にする成分が含まれているらしい。Chokoreeto ni wa nou o kasseika shite genki ni suru seibun ga fukumarete iru rashii.
EN: Chocolate apparently contains substances that stimulate the brain and give you energy.
HI: कहते हैं कि चॉकलेट में ऐसे तत्व होते हैं जो दिमाग़ को सक्रिय करके ताज़गी देते हैं।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે ચૉકલેટમાં એવાં તત્ત્વો હોય છે જે મગજને સક્રિય કરીને તાજગી આપે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1活性化kasseikaactivation, revitalisingसक्रिय करनाસક્રિય કરવું
2高速化kousokukaspeeding upतेज़ करनाઝડપી બનાવવું
3健全化kenzenkaputting on a sound footingस्वस्थ/मज़बूत बनानाતંદુરસ્ત/મજબૂત બનાવવું
4正常化seijoukanormalisationसामान्य बनानाસામાન્ય બનાવવું

Answer 1 (別冊 p.49, PDF 131). Book's notes (paraphrased): 活性化する = activate. 選択肢の言葉: 3 健全化する = become healthy / sound (例: if 国の財政が健全化されれば (kuni no zaisei ga kenzenka sarereba), people's trust in the government should return). The 別冊 has no notes for 2 高速化 and 4 正常化. (added, not in book) 元気にする (make lively) matches 活性 (active nature). 健全化 is for finances and organisations, not the brain; all four share 〜化, so look at the first two kanji.

【51】

警備室では警備員が監視カメラの（　）を見て社内をチェックしている。Keibishitsu de wa keibiin ga kanshi kamera no (___) o mite shanai o chekku shite iru.

Full sentence: 警備室では警備員が監視カメラのモニターを見て社内をチェックしている。Keibishitsu de wa keibiin ga kanshi kamera no monitaa o mite shanai o chekku shite iru.
EN: In the security room, guards check the building by watching the surveillance camera monitors.
HI: सुरक्षा कक्ष में गार्ड सीसीटीवी कैमरे का मॉनिटर देखकर दफ़्तर के अंदर की निगरानी करते हैं।

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1センサーsensaasensorसेंसरસેન્સર
2フィルターfirutaafilterफ़िल्टर, छन्नीફિલ્ટર, ગળણી
3フィルムfirumufilm (camera)फ़िल्म (कैमरे की)ફિલ્મ (કૅમેરાની)
4モニターmonitaamonitor (screen)मॉनिटरમૉનિટર

Answer 4 (別冊 p.49, PDF 131). Book's notes (paraphrased): モニター = (in this question) a device with a screen for monitoring. 選択肢の言葉: 1 センサー = sensor (例: there are many kinds — some 温度を感じる (ondo o kanjiru), some light); 2 フィルター = filter (例: impurities in water are removed フィルターを使って (firutaa o tsukatte)); 3 フィルム = film (例: since digital cameras spread, フィルムの需要が減った (firumu no juyou ga hetta)). (added, not in book) 〜を見て (looking at it) → a screen: モニター. フィルター / フィルム sound alike and are the "similar sound" trap.

【52】

何度か会っているのに、その人の名前を（　）してしまった。Nando ka atte iru no ni, sono hito no namae o (___) shite shimatta.

Full sentence: 何度か会っているのに、その人の名前を度忘れしてしまった。Nando ka atte iru no ni, sono hito no namae o dowasure shite shimatta.
EN: I've met them several times, yet their name just slipped my mind.
HI: कई बार मिल चुका/चुकी हूँ, फिर भी उस व्यक्ति का नाम अचानक भूल गया/गई।
GU: ઘણી વાર મળી ચૂક્યો/ચૂકી છું, છતાં એ વ્યક્તિનું નામ અચાનક ભૂલી ગયો/ગઈ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1失礼shitsureirudeness, impolitenessअशिष्टताઅશિષ્ટતા
2度忘れdowasuremomentary forgettingअचानक भूल जानाઅચાનક ભૂલી જવું
3失敬shikkeirudeness; leaving (casual, male)अशिष्टता; चलता हूँ (पुरुष, अनौपचारिक)અશિષ્ટતા; હું જાઉં (પુરુષ, અનૌપચારિક)
4物忘れmonowasureforgetfulnessभुलक्कड़पनભુલકણાપણું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.49, PDF 131). Book's notes (paraphrased): 度忘れする = slip one's mind. 選択肢の言葉: 3 失敬する = ① be rude to someone (said by men to people close to them) (例: 夕べは飲み過ぎて失敬した (yuube wa nomisugite shikkei shita) — sorry); ② leave (same users) (例: お先に失敬するよ (osaki ni shikkei suru yo)); 4 物忘れする = forget things (例: grandfather 最近よく物忘れするようになった (saikin yoku monowasure suru you ni natta), so I'm worried). The 別冊 has no note for 1 失礼. (added, not in book) 物忘れ is a general tendency (often with age) and takes no object; a single known name forgotten for a moment, with 名前を, is 度忘れ.

【53】

毎日残業して準備した（　）があって、企画の発表は大成功だった。Mainichi zangyou shite junbi shita (___) ga atte, kikaku no happyou wa daiseikou datta.

EN: All the overtime I put in preparing every day paid off: the project presentation was a great success.
HI: रोज़ ओवरटाइम करके की गई तैयारी का फल मिला — प्रोजेक्ट की प्रस्तुति बेहद सफल रही।
GU: રોજ ઓવરટાઇમ કરીને કરેલી તૈયારીનું ફળ મળ્યું — પ્રોજેક્ટની રજૂઆત ખૂબ સફળ રહી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1犠牲giseisacrificeबलिदान, त्यागબલિદાન, ત્યાગ
2甲斐kaiworth, reward (of effort)मेहनत का फलમહેનતનું ફળ
3影響eikyouinfluence, effectअसर, प्रभावઅસર, પ્રભાવ
4経緯keiithe course of events, backgroundघटनाक्रम, पृष्ठभूमिઘટનાક્રમ, પૃષ્ઠભૂમિ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.49, PDF 131). Book's notes (paraphrased): 甲斐 = (in this question) the effect of an action, its result. 選択肢の言葉: 1 犠牲 = sacrifice (例: to make his dream come true he 家族を犠牲にしてしまった (kazoku o gisei ni shite shimatta)); 4 経緯 = details, circumstances (例: the police investigation made その事件の経緯 (sono jiken no keii) clear). The 別冊 has no note for 3 影響. (added, not in book) 〜した甲斐があって = "the effort was worth it" — a fixed pattern (努力の甲斐があって, 頑張った甲斐がある). 影響があって would be neutral or negative and doesn't express "paid off".

【54】

この事件には、ある政治家が（　）関わっていた。Kono jiken ni wa, aru seijika ga (___) kakawatte ita.

Full sentence: この事件には、ある政治家が密接に関わっていた。Kono jiken ni wa, aru seijika ga missetsu ni kakawatte ita.
EN: A certain politician was closely involved in this case.
HI: इस मामले से एक नेता गहराई से जुड़ा हुआ था।
GU: આ કેસ સાથે એક નેતા ગાઢ રીતે સંકળાયેલો હતો.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1軽薄にkeihaku nifrivolously, shallowlyछिछले ढंग सेછીછરી રીતે
2密接にmissetsu nicloselyघनिष्ठ रूप सेગાઢ રીતે
3手軽にtegaru nieasily, casually (without effort)आसानी से, झटपटસહેલાઈથી, ઝટપટ
4手近にtejika niclose at handहाथ के पासહાથવગું

Answer 2 (別冊 p.50, PDF 132). Book's notes (paraphrased): 密接に (密接な) = close(ly). 選択肢の言葉: 1 軽薄に (軽薄な) (例: that woman is empty-headed and 軽薄な (keihaku na)); 3 手軽に (手軽な) = casual, simple (例: hamburgers are popular as 手軽な食事 (tegaru na shokuji)); 4 手近に (手近な) (例: so I can look up words any time, I keep the dictionary 手近な所に (tejika na tokoro ni)). (added, not in book) 関わる / 関係 + "how deeply" → 密接に関わる, 密接な関係. 手近 is about physical distance, not involvement.

【55】

OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati

2めにみえてme ni mietevisibly, noticeably (目に見えて)साफ़ दिखाई देने लायक़સ્પષ્ટ દેખાય તેમ

(added, not in book) むしょうに goes with desires and feelings (〜たくなる, 眠くなる, 腹が立つ). 目に見えて needs a change others can observe.

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
活性化kasseika vs 健全化kenzenka vs 正常化seijouka revitalise vs make sound vs normalise All 〜化. 経済・脳・地域を活性化 (make lively); 財政を健全化 (fix finances); 関係・ダイヤを正常化 (return to normal).
モニターmonitaa / フィルターfirutaa / フィルムfirumu / センサーsensaa screen / filter / film / sensor You watch a モニター; water passes through a フィルター; old cameras use フィルム; a センサー detects heat or light automatically.
度忘れdowasure vs 物忘れmonowasure a momentary blank vs forgetfulness 度忘れ = suddenly can't recall something you know well (名前を度忘れする); 物忘れ = tending to forget things in general (物忘れがひどい).
失敬shikkei vs 失礼shitsurei rude / I'll be off (casual male) vs rude / excuse me Same meanings, different register: 失敬 is old-fashioned, mostly men to friends; 失礼 is neutral and polite.
甲斐kai / 犠牲gisei / 経緯keii reward / sacrifice / course of events 〜した甲斐がある (it paid off); 〜を犠牲にする (give something up); 事件の経緯 (how it happened). 経緯 = けいい (also いきさつ).
密接missetsu / 手近tejika / 手軽tegaru / 軽薄keihaku close (relation) / near at hand / easy / frivolous 密接な関係; 手近な所に置く; 手軽な料理; 軽薄な人. 手近 and 手軽 differ in one kanji: 近 (near) vs 軽 (light).
むしょうにmushou ni / 目に見えてme ni miete / 瞬く間にmatataku ma ni / もれなくmorenaku irresistibly / visibly / in an instant / without exception むしょうに = inner urge; 目に見えて = visible change; 瞬く間に = speed; もれなく = everyone included.
初対面shotaimen / 初心者shoshinsha / 初歩shoho / 初旬shojun first meeting / beginner / basics / early in the month All 初〜: 初対面の人, 初心者コース, 初歩から学ぶ, 3月初旬.
Exam trap: when the blank sits before して／する (Q50, Q52), every option is grammatical — the choice comes from the object: 脳を (活性化), 名前を (度忘れ). Ask which option naturally takes that object.
