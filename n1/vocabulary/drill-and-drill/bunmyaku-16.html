2 Every question — 問題 (p.51)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4. HI: खाली जगह के लिए सबसे सही शब्द 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

【106】

この地域では昔から行われてきた（　）が今も残っているという。Kono chiiki de wa mukashi kara okonawarete kita (___) ga ima mo nokotte iru to iu.

Full sentence: この地域では昔から行われてきたしきたりが今も残っているという。Kono chiiki de wa mukashi kara okonawarete kita shikitari ga ima mo nokotte iru to iu.
EN: They say customs practised since olden times still survive in this area.
HI: कहते हैं कि इस इलाक़े में पुराने ज़माने से चले आ रहे रीति-रिवाज आज भी बचे हुए हैं।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે આ વિસ્તારમાં જૂના જમાનાથી ચાલ્યા આવતા રીતરિવાજ આજે પણ ટકી રહ્યા છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1名物meibutsulocal speciality, famous productमशहूर चीज़ (स्थानीय)પ્રખ્યાત વસ્તુ (સ્થાનિક)
2しきたりshikitaricustom, conventionरीति-रिवाजરીતરિવાજ
3環境kankyouenvironmentपर्यावरण, माहौलપર્યાવરણ, વાતાવરણ
4風土fuudonatural features, climate (of a region)जलवायु और भू-प्रकृतिઆબોહવા અને ભૂપ્રકૃતિ

Answer 2 (別冊 p.63, PDF 145). Book's notes (paraphrased): しきたり = rules set by tradition. 選択肢の言葉: 4 風土 = the natural conditions special to a place, such as its climate. The 別冊 has no note for 1 名物 or 3 環境. (added, not in book) 昔から行われてきた (has been practised since long ago) needs something people do → しきたり. 風土 is nature, not a practice; 名物 is a product.

【107】

警察の調べが進むにつれ、事件の（　）が明らかになってきた。Keisatsu no shirabe ga susumu ni tsure, jiken no (___) ga akiraka ni natte kita.

Full sentence: 警察の調べが進むにつれ、事件の真相が明らかになってきた。Keisatsu no shirabe ga susumu ni tsure, jiken no shinsou ga akiraka ni natte kita.
EN: As the police investigation went on, the truth about the case gradually came to light.
HI: जैसे-जैसे पुलिस की जाँच आगे बढ़ी, मामले की असलियत सामने आने लगी।
GU: જેમ જેમ પોલીસની તપાસ આગળ વધી, તેમ તેમ બનાવની ખરી હકીકત બહાર આવવા લાગી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1真理shinritruth (universal)परम सत्यપરમ સત્ય
2実質jisshitsusubstance, in realityअसल में, सारહકીકતમાં, સાર
3真相shinsouthe truth of the matterमामले की असलियतબનાવની ખરી હકીકત
4実情jitsujouactual circumstancesवास्तविक स्थितिવાસ્તવિક સ્થિતિ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.63–64, PDF 145–146). Book's notes (paraphrased): 真相 = the true state of things. 選択肢の言葉: 1 真理 = truth (例: I want work that 宇宙の真理を追求する (uchuu no shinri o tsuikyuu suru)); 2 実質 = reality, substance (例: on paper it's a recommendation to retire, 実質はクビ切りなんです (jisshitsu wa kubikiri nan desu)); 4 実情 = the real circumstances (例: he knows この業界の実情 (kono gyoukai no jitsujou) well, so ask him). (added, not in book) 事件の〜が明らかになる → what really happened in a case: 真相 (真相を究明する).

【108】

去年のこの時期は大漁だったのに、今年はまだ1匹も釣れないのはどうしてだろうかと漁師たちは首を（　）。Kyonen no kono jiki wa tairyou datta noni, kotoshi wa mada ippiki mo tsurenai no wa doushite darou ka to ryoushitachi wa kubi o (___).

Full sentence: 去年のこの時期は大漁だったのに、今年はまだ1匹も釣れないのはどうしてだろうかと漁師たちは首をかしげている。Kyonen no kono jiki wa tairyou datta noni, kotoshi wa mada ippiki mo tsurenai no wa doushite darou ka to ryoushitachi wa kubi o kashigete iru.
EN: At this time last year there was a huge catch, but this year not a single fish yet — the fishermen are scratching their heads, wondering why.
HI: पिछले साल इस समय ढेर सारी मछलियाँ मिली थीं, पर इस साल अब तक एक भी नहीं — मछुआरे हैरान होकर सोच रहे हैं कि ऐसा क्यों।
GU: ગયા વર્ષે આ સમયે ઢગલાબંધ માછલી મળી હતી, પણ આ વર્ષે હજી એક પણ નહીં — માછીમારો મૂંઝાઈને વિચારે છે કે આવું કેમ.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1まわしているmawashite iruturning (まわす)घुमा रहे हैंફેરવે છે
2まげているmagete irubending (まげる)मोड़ रहे हैंવાળે છે
3うなずいているunazuite irunodding (うなずく)सिर हिलाकर हाँ कह रहे हैंમાથું હલાવી હા પાડે છે
4かしげているkashigete irutilting (かしげる)झुका रहे हैं (हैरानी में)નમાવે છે (મૂંઝવણમાં)

Answer 4 (別冊 p.64, PDF 146). Book's notes (paraphrased): かしげる = 傾ける (lean, tilt). The 別冊 gives no 選択肢の言葉 for this question. (added, not in book) どうしてだろうか (wondering why) → the gesture of doubt is 首をかしげる (tilt your head). 首をまわす is a stretch; うなずく is agreeing — the opposite of puzzled.

【109】

あんなに親しくしていた友人なのに、彼女は最近なぜか（　）態度を見せる。Anna ni shitashiku shite ita yuujin na noni, kanojo wa saikin nazeka (___) taido o miseru.

Full sentence: あんなに親しくしていた友人なのに、彼女は最近なぜかよそよそしい態度を見せる。Anna ni shitashiku shite ita yuujin na noni, kanojo wa saikin nazeka yosoyososhii taido o miseru.
EN: We used to be such close friends, but lately, for some reason, she has been acting distant.
HI: हम इतने गहरे दोस्त थे, फिर भी आजकल न जाने क्यों वह रूखा व्यवहार करती है।
GU: અમે આટલાં ગાઢ મિત્રો હતાં, છતાં આજકાલ ખબર નહીં કેમ તે અતડું વર્તન કરે છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1みずみずしいmizumizushiifresh, juicy, youthfulताज़ा, रसीलाતાજું, રસદાર
2たどたどしいtadotadoshiifaltering, halting (not yet fluent)अटक-अटककर, लड़खड़ाताઅટકી અટકીને, લથડતું
3よそよそしいyosoyososhiidistant, coldरूखा, बेगानाઅતડું, પારકું
4なれなれしいnarenareshiiover-familiarज़रूरत से ज़्यादा घुला-मिलाજરૂર કરતાં વધુ હળતુંભળતું

Answer 3 (別冊 p.64–65, PDF 146–147). Book's notes (paraphrased): よそよそしい = unfriendly. 選択肢の言葉: 1 みずみずしい = freshly picked and very fresh / young and lively (例: みずみずしい果物 (mizumizushii kudamono) on the table of a southern hotel; this cosmetic gives you 少女のようなみずみずしい肌 (shoujo no you na mizumizushii hada)); 2 たどたどしい = way of speaking or moving not yet smooth (例: someone asked me the way たどたどしい日本語で (tadotadoshii nihongo de), so I took them there); 4 なれなれしい = too familiar, without restraint (例: a man next to me on the train なれなれしく話しかけてくる (narenareshiku hanashikakete kuru)). (added, not in book) あんなに親しくしていた…のに (we were so close, but…) → the opposite now: よそよそしい. なれなれしい is the opposite extreme.

【110】

歩き始めた1歳の息子は、（　）じっとしていないので、目が離せない。Arukihajimeta issai no musuko wa, (___) jitto shite inai node, me ga hanasenai.

Full sentence: 歩き始めた1歳の息子は、かたときもじっとしていないので、目が離せない。Arukihajimeta issai no musuko wa, katatoki mo jitto shite inai node, me ga hanasenai.
EN: My one-year-old son, who has just started walking, never keeps still for a moment, so I can't take my eyes off him.
HI: मेरा एक साल का बेटा अभी चलना सीखा है और एक पल भी चैन से नहीं बैठता, इसलिए उस पर से नज़र नहीं हटा सकते।
GU: મારો એક વર્ષનો દીકરો હમણાં જ ચાલતાં શીખ્યો છે અને એક પળ પણ જંપીને બેસતો નથી, તેથી તેના પરથી નજર હટાવી શકાતી નથી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1見るからにmiru kara niat first glance, obviouslyदेखते ही (लगता है)જોતાં જ (લાગે છે)
2かたときもkatatoki mo(not) even for a momentएक पल भी (नहीं)એક પળ પણ (નહીં)
3つねひごろtsunehigoroalways, habituallyहमेशा, रोज़मर्रा मेंહંમેશાં, રોજબરોજ
4ふだんからfudan karaas a habit, routinelyआम तौर पर, आदतनસામાન્ય રીતે, ટેવથી

Answer 2 (別冊 p.65, PDF 147). Book's notes (paraphrased): かたときも = (not doing something) even for a moment. 選択肢の言葉: 1 見るからに = just one look gives that impression (例: the table was laid with 見るからにおいしそうな料理 (miru kara ni oishisou na ryouri)); 3 つねひごろ (常日頃) = ふだん / いつも (例: I つねひごろ (tsunehigoro) tell my children not to trouble others); 4 ふだんから = いつも (例: I ふだんから脂っこい物を食べないように (fudan kara aburakkoi mono o tabenai you ni) take care). (added, not in book) じっとしていない (negative) needs "not even for a moment" → かたときも〜ない.

【111】

近年、（　）が原因の病気が増えている。Kinnen, (___) ga gen'in no byouki ga fuete iru.

Full sentence: 近年、ストレスが原因の病気が増えている。Kinnen, sutoresu ga gen'in no byouki ga fuete iru.
EN: In recent years, illnesses caused by stress have been increasing.
HI: हाल के वर्षों में तनाव से होने वाली बीमारियाँ बढ़ रही हैं।
GU: તાજેતરનાં વર્ષોમાં તણાવથી થતી બીમારીઓ વધી રહી છે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1ストライキsutoraikistrike (labour)हड़तालહડતાળ
2ルールruururuleनियमનિયમ
3ストレスsutoresustressतनावતણાવ
4アクシデントakushidentoaccidentदुर्घटनाઅકસ્માત

Answer 3 (別冊 p.65, PDF 147). Book's notes (paraphrased): ストレス = stress. 選択肢の言葉: 1 ストライキ = strike (例: 鉄道会社のストライキで (tetsudou-gaisha no sutoraiki de) trains in the capital stopped and many people were affected); 2 ルール = 規則 (例: people who ルールを守らない (ruuru o mamoranai) aren't fit to be working adults); 4 アクシデント = 事故. (added, not in book) 〜が原因の病気 (illness caused by ~) → ストレス. ストライキ / ストレス share the スト〜 start.

【112】

詳しい内容につきましては（　）お知らせいたします。Kuwashii naiyou ni tsukimashite wa (___) o-shirase itashimasu.

Full sentence: 詳しい内容につきましては別途お知らせいたします。Kuwashii naiyou ni tsukimashite wa betto o-shirase itashimasu.
EN: We will inform you of the details separately.
HI: विस्तृत जानकारी आपको अलग से दी जाएगी।
GU: વિગતવાર માહિતી આપને અલગથી આપવામાં આવશે.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1別途bettoseparatelyअलग सेઅલગથી
2別々betsubetsueach separately, individuallyअलग-अलगઅલગ અલગ
3終始shuushifrom start to finishशुरू से आख़िर तकશરૂથી અંત સુધી
4途中tochuuon the way, midwayबीच में, रास्ते मेंવચ્ચે, રસ્તામાં

Answer 1 (別冊 p.65, PDF 147). Book's notes (paraphrased): 別途 = separately. 選択肢の言葉: 3 終始 = from beginning to end (例: at the sales meeting 社長の表情は終始厳しかった (shachou no hyoujou wa shuushi kibishikatta)). The 別冊 has no note for 2 別々 or 4 途中. (added, not in book) A formal notice: details will be sent by another channel / later → 別途お知らせします. 別々 means "each one apart" (別々に払う = pay separately, each person), not "by separate notice".

3 Confusion pairs — the look-alike options in this round

Built from this round's options and the 別冊 選択肢の言葉 (added, not in book):

FormMeaningKey difference
しきたりshikitari / 風土fuudo / 名物meibutsu / 環境kankyou custom / natural climate of a region / local speciality / environment しきたりに従う; 日本の風土に合う; 名物料理; 環境を守る.
真相shinsou / 真理shinri / 実情jitsujou / 実質jisshitsu truth of an incident / universal truth / real circumstances / in substance 事件の真相; 宇宙の真理; 現場の実情; 実質的な値上げ (a price rise in effect). See also 真実 in 第15回.
首をかしげるkubi o kashigeru / うなずくunazuku tilt one's head (doubt) / nod (agree) Body-language idioms: 首をかしげる = puzzled; 首を横に振る = no; うなずく = yes / I see.
よそよそしいyosoyososhii / なれなれしいnarenareshii / たどたどしいtadotadoshii / みずみずしいmizumizushii distant / over-familiar / halting / fresh All reduplicated 〜しい adjectives. よそよそしい ↔ なれなれしい are opposites; たどたどしい日本語; みずみずしい果物・肌.
かたときもkatatoki mo / つねひごろtsunehigoro / ふだんからfudan kara / 見るからにmiru kara ni not for a moment / always / habitually / at first sight かたときも needs a negative (片時も忘れない). つねひごろ・ふだんから are positive "always". 見るからに + そう/らしい (見るからに高そう).
ストレスsutoresu / ストライキsutoraiki stress / strike ストレスがたまる・解消する; ストライキ (スト) を行う.
別途betto / 別々betsubetsu / 終始shuushi separately (another time/channel) / each apart / throughout 送料は別途 (charged separately); 別々に住む; 終始笑顔だった.
Exam trap: with adverbs, look at the end of the sentence. かたときも, 一向に, ろくに only work with a negative verb (じっとしていない), while つねひごろ / ふだんから describe a habit. If the sentence ends in 〜ない, try the "not even" adverbs first.
