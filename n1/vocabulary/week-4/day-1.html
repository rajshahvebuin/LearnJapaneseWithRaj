1日目 — 手分け・同い年・心地 (Tewake / Onai-doshi / Kokochi)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.60–61 (PDF pages 51–52). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 9. Week 4's own divider page (PDF index 50, printed p.59) titled 初級漢字の語彙を覚えよう confirms the chapter theme: basic-level kanji, revisited through the intermediate/advanced compounds they form.
Theme
Compounds built from four everyday kanji: 手 (hand), 分 (divide/part), 同 (same), 心 (heart/mind) — grouped exactly as the book groups them, each headword shown with a usage example rather than a bare gloss.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.63 (this week's Day 2, second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Format shift from Weeks 1–3: this chapter is not a flat list of standalone words — it groups compounds under a shared base kanji, and every headword comes with its own example phrase/sentence printed by the book (folded into the Note column below, with romaji, following the same approach Week 3 used for adverb example sentences). The book's own English translations are kept as the English column; Hindi/Gujarati are natural translations added for this site, replacing the book's Chinese/Korean.
→ = a related compound printed alongside the headword (often built from the same kanji), shown in the Note column with its own romaji + gloss

Same kanji, different reading = this kanji-focused chapter's own trap: e.g. 分別 read ぶんべつ (sorting) vs. ふんべつ (good sense) — flagged in Confusion Pairs below

Q1

だんだん年をとって、物事の良し悪しの（　）がつくようになった。Dandan toshi o totte, monogoto no yoshi-ashi no (___) ga tsuku you ni natta.

Full sentence: だんだん年をとって、物事の良し悪しの分別がつくようになった。...monogoto no yoshi-ashi no funbetsu ga tsuku you ni natta.
EN: As I've gotten older, I've come to be able to judge right from wrong.
HI: उम्र बढ़ने के साथ-साथ मुझमें सही-ग़लत परखने की समझ आ गई है।
GU: ઉંમર વધવાની સાથે મારામાં સાચા-ખોટાનો ભેદ પારખવાની સમજ આવી ગઈ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a分別funbetsugood sense, discretionसमझदारीસમજદારી
b分野bun'yafield/area (unrelated to judgment)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 物事の良し悪しの分別 ("discretion about right and wrong") is the fixed collocation for good judgment; 分野 is a field of study/work, unrelated here.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: He looks unfazed, but inwardly he isn't calm.
HI: वह ऊपर से बेफ़िक्र दिखता है, पर मन ही मन शांत नहीं है।
GU: તે ઉપરથી બેફિકર દેખાય છે, પણ મનમાં શાંત નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q4

これはオークションで（　）した中古の冷蔵庫です。Kore wa ookushon de (___) shita chuuko no reizouko desu.

Full sentence: これはオークションで入手した中古の冷蔵庫です。...nyuushu shita chuuko no reizouko desu.
EN: This is a secondhand fridge I obtained at an auction.
HI: यह नीलामी में हासिल किया हुआ पुराना फ़्रिज है।
GU: આ હરાજીમાં મેળવેલું જૂનું ફ્રિજ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手中shuchuuin one's grasp (doesn't take で於て object like this)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b入手nyuushuto obtain, acquireहासिल करनाમેળવવું

Why: 入手する is the verb for actively acquiring an item (e.g. at an auction); 手中にする means "to have in one's grasp" and doesn't collocate with a place-marked acquisition like オークションで.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This kanji-focused chapter's own trap is the same kanji compound read two different ways with two unrelated meanings — today's list has two textbook examples of exactly that, plus a cluster of near-synonym "handy/convenient" words:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
分別bunbetsu Sorting, separating (e.g. waste) A physical/administrative act of categorizing items. ゴミの分別 — sorting garbage for recycling.
分別funbetsu Good sense, discretion Same kanji, different reading — a personality trait, not an action. 分別のある人 — a sensible, discreet person.
心中shinchuu One's inner feelings An abstract emotional state someone else tries to guess/sense. 心中を察する — to sense someone's true feelings.
心中shinjuu Double suicide (with a lover) Same kanji, completely different reading and meaning — a serious false-friend trap. 心中を図る — to attempt a double suicide.
手近tejika na Close at hand, familiar About physical/conceptual proximity — what's nearby or already available. 手近な材料で作る — cooking with what's already in the kitchen.
手ごろtegoro na Reasonable, just-right (size/price) About a size or price being appropriate — not about location. 手ごろな値段 — an affordable price.
手軽tegaru na Easy, convenient to do About the low effort/simplicity of an action itself. 手軽に持ち運べる — can be carried around with no hassle.
Reading trap: 同い年 ("the same age") is NOT read どういねん — it takes the irregular native-Japanese reading おないどし, exactly as the book's own ＊言い方に注意 note next to it warns. Similarly, don't let 塩分 (えんぶん, "salt content") and 水分 (すいぶん, "moisture") blur together just because they're both printed next to each other as "～分" nutrition/composition terms — they describe two different substances.
