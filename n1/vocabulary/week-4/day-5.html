5日目 — 世代・本場・作用 (Sedai / Honba / Sayou)

Source

Theme
The chapter's biggest day yet: ten kanji groups — 産 (produce), 不 (negative prefix), 特 (special), 代 (generation/substitute), 場 (place), 事 (matter), 主 (main), 生 (life/raw), 用 (use), 所 (place/point).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.71 (Day 6's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
33 words — the longest day this week — spanning 産/不/特/代/場/事/主 on p.68 and 生/用/所 on p.69 (主 splits across the page break). Same example-in-Note format as Days 1–4.
→ = a related compound printed alongside the headword
⇔ = antonym

Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
このビルの（　）者はだれですか。Kono biru no (___)-sha wa dare desu ka.
("Who is the [___] of this building?")

所有（する） (shoyuu (suru)) — "to own" — 所有者 (shoyuusha) = "the owner" (the book contrasts it with the look-alikes 生産 and 代理 shown alongside)

Q1

本日の講演の（　）はエコロジーです。Honjitsu no kouen no (___) wa ekorojii desu.

Full sentence: 本日の講演の主題はエコロジーです。...kouen no shudai wa ekorojii desu.
EN: Today's lecture topic is ecology.
HI: आज के व्याख्यान का विषय पारिस्थितिकी (इकोलॉजी) है।
GU: આજના પ્રવચનનો વિષય પર્યાવરણવિજ્ઞાન (ઇકોલોજી) છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a主体shutaithe main body/core (of a group, not a lecture's topic)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b主題shudaithe theme/topicविषय, थीमવિષય, થીમ

Why: 主題 is the word for a lecture's/work's theme or subject; 主体 describes the main constituent group of something (like who makes up a workforce), not a topic.

Q2

その国は世界有数の石油（　）国である。Sono kuni wa sekai yuusuu no sekiyu (___) koku de aru.

Full sentence: その国は世界有数の石油産出国である。...sekiyu sanshutsu koku de aru.
EN: That country is one of the world's leading oil-producing nations.
HI: वह देश दुनिया के प्रमुख तेल-उत्पादक देशों में से एक है।
GU: એ દેશ દુનિયાના મુખ્ય તેલ-ઉત્પાદક દેશોમાંનો એક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a所有shoyuuownership (doesn't compound with 国 this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b産出sanshutsuproducing, yieldingउत्पादन करनाઉત્પન્ન કરવું

Why: 石油産出国 ("an oil-producing nation") is the fixed compound; 所有 describes owning something, not a country's natural output.

Q3

父の（　）で市役所に介護保険の申請に行った。Chichi no (___) de shiyakusho ni kaigo hoken no shinsei ni itta.

Full sentence: 父の代理で市役所に介護保険の申請に行った。Chichi no dairi de...
EN: I went to city hall to apply for nursing-care insurance on my father's behalf.
HI: मैं अपने पिता की जगह नगरपालिका कार्यालय देखभाल बीमा के लिए आवेदन करने गया।
GU: હું મારા પિતાના વતી નગરપાલિકા ઓફિસ સંભાળ વીમા માટે અરજી કરવા ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a代用daiyousubstituting an object for another (wrong domain — for things, not acting for a person)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b代理dairiacting on someone's behalfकिसी की जगह काम करनाવતી કામ કરવું

Why: 父の代理で ("on my father's behalf") is the phrase for representing a person; 代用 is for substituting one object for another, not standing in for a person.

Q4

京都には伝統的な年中（　）が多くあります。Kyouto ni wa dentouteki na nenjuu (___) ga ooku arimasu.

Full sentence: 京都には伝統的な年中行事が多くあります。...nenjuu gyouji ga ooku arimasu.
EN: Kyoto has many traditional annual events.
HI: क्योतो में पारंपरिक वार्षिक आयोजन बहुत होते हैं।
GU: ક્યોટોમાં પરંપરાગત વાર્ષિક કાર્યક્રમો ઘણા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a行事gyoujian event/functionआयोजनકાર્યક્રમ
b人事jinjipersonnel/HR affairs (wrong domain)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 年中行事 ("annual events") is the fixed compound for recurring festivals/ceremonies; 人事 refers to personnel/HR matters, an unrelated domain.

Q5

彼とは大学を卒業して以来、（　）不通です。Kare to wa daigaku o sotsugyou shite irai, (___) futsuu desu.

Full sentence: 彼とは大学を卒業して以来、音信不通です。...irai, onshin futsuu desu.
EN: I've been out of touch with him since we graduated from university.
HI: यूनिवर्सिटी से निकलने के बाद उससे मेरा कोई संपर्क नहीं रहा।
GU: યુનિવર્સિટીમાંથી નીકળ્યા પછી તેની સાથે મારો કોઈ સંપર્ક રહ્યો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a音信onshinnews/word from someoneख़बर, संपर्कસમાચાર, સંપર્ક
b通信tsuushincommunication/telecommunications (wrong compound — 通信不通 isn't a phrase)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 音信不通 ("completely out of touch") is a fixed four-character idiom; 通信 means "communication" in general and doesn't combine with 不通 this way.

Q6

会社の繁栄のために、（　）交代は避けられないだろう。Kaisha no han'ei no tame ni, (___) koutai wa sakerarenai darou.

Full sentence: 会社の繁栄のために、世代交代は避けられないだろう。...sedai koutai wa sakerarenai darou.
EN: For the company's prosperity, generational change is probably unavoidable.
HI: कंपनी की तरक़्क़ी के लिए, पीढ़ी बदलाव शायद टाला नहीं जा सकता।
GU: કંપનીની સમૃદ્ધિ માટે, પેઢી પરિવર્તન કદાચ ટાળી ન શકાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a世代sedaigenerationपीढ़ीપેઢી

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1特有tokuyuupeculiar to (an adjective, doesn't take 物 this way)ख़ास तौर पर उससे जुड़ाખાસ કરીને તેને લગતું

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's clearest kanji trap is 市場, plus a cluster of near-synonym "production/specialty" words that this day's quiz drills directly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
市場shijou The (economic) market An abstract economic concept — supply, demand, trading. 金融市場 — the financial market.
市場ichiba A physical marketplace Same kanji, different reading — an actual place you walk into. 魚市場 — a fish market you can visit.
産出sanshutsu To yield/produce (esp. natural resources) What a country or land naturally yields. 石油産出国 — an oil-producing nation.
生産seisan To manufacture Industrial/factory output, a human-made process. 大量に生産する — mass production.
名産meisan ／ 特産tokusan A noted/special local product Near-synonyms the book itself glosses as equal (特産物＝名産) — either works for a region's signature product. この地方の名産／特産 — this region's specialty.
Exam trap: 代理 (acting on behalf of a person) and 代用 (substituting one object for another) share 代 but never swap: you say 父の代理で行く ("go on my father's behalf") but クッションを枕に代用する ("use a cushion in place of a pillow") — never the reverse pairing of subject and word.
