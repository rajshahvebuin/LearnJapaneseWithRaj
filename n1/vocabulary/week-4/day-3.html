3日目 — 目下・大家・万人 (Mokka / Taika / Bannin)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.64–65 (PDF pages 55–56). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 9.
Theme
The book itself subtitles today's list 人の動作を表す言葉 ("words describing a person's actions/state") — compounds from 目 (eye), 明 (bright/reveal), 家 (house/family), 人 (person), 進 (advance), 先 (ahead/prior).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.67 (Day 4's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
27 words across six kanji groups (目/明/家 on p.64, 人/進/先 on p.65). Same fold-example-into-Note format as Days 1–2. This day's own speech-bubble callout warns explicitly about a reading trap in the title word itself — see 目下 below and Confusion Pairs.

Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
（　）、失業中なので、家計が苦しい。(___), shitsugyouchuu na node, kakei ga kurushii.
("[___], I'm unemployed, so money is tight.")

目下 (mokka) — "at present, currently" (today's title word). The book's speech bubble flags this explicitly: 「現在」「今のところ」という意味です。読み方に注意してください。(“Genzai”, “ima no tokoro” to iu imi desu. Yomikata ni chuui shite kudasai.) — "It means 'at present/currently.' Watch the reading" (it's もっか here, not めした — see Confusion Pairs).

Q1

日本では少子高齢化が（　）している。Nihon de wa shoushi-koureika ga (___) shite iru.

Full sentence: 日本では少子高齢化が進行している。...shoushi-koureika ga shinkou shite iru.
EN: In Japan, the falling birthrate and aging population are progressing.
HI: जापान में घटती जन्म-दर और बढ़ती उम्रदराज़ आबादी की समस्या बढ़ती जा रही है।
GU: જાપાનમાં ઘટતો જન્મદર અને વધતી વૃદ્ધ વસ્તીની સમસ્યા આગળ વધી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a行進koushinmarching (a physical parade, wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b進行shinkouprogressingआगे बढ़नाઆગળ વધવું

Why: 進行する describes an abstract trend/process advancing (like demographic change); 行進する is literal marching, e.g. in a parade.

Full sentence: その件に関して目下検討しているところです。...mokka kentou shite iru tokoro desu.
EN: Regarding that matter, we are currently examining it.
HI: उस मामले पर हम फ़िलहाल विचार कर रहे हैं।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's list has two of this chapter's classic same-kanji-different-reading traps, both flagged by the book itself:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
目下mokka At present, currently An adverb of time — describes when something is happening. 目下検討している — "currently under review."
目下meshita A subordinate, someone junior to you Same kanji, different reading — a noun describing a person's rank, unrelated to time. 目下の人 — "a person below you in rank."
大家ooya A landlord Refers to the owner of a rental property. 貸家の大家 — the landlord of a rented house.
大家taika A great master (of an art/field) Same kanji, different reading and completely unrelated meaning — an authority figure in a craft. 絵の大家 — a great master of painting.
Exam trap: Both 目下 and 大家 are genuine JLPT reading traps — the kanji alone cannot tell you which reading applies, only the surrounding context can. If a sentence is talking about timing or the present moment, 目下 is もっか; if it's ranking people, it's めした. If a sentence names a role tied to a rental property, 大家 is おおや; if it's praising expertise in an art, it's たいか.
