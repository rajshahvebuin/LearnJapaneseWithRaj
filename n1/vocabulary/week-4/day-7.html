問題1 — choose the best word for the blank (1問4点)

Q1

このバッグは財布を入れるのに（　）な大きさだ。Kono baggu wa saifu o ireru no ni (___) na ookisa da.

Full sentence: このバッグは財布を入れるのに手ごろな大きさだ。...tegoro na ookisa da.
EN: This bag is just the right size for holding a wallet.
HI: यह बैग बटुआ रखने के लिए बिल्कुल सही आकार का है।
GU: આ બેગ પાકીટ રાખવા માટે એકદમ યોગ્ય કદની છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1心地kokochia feeling/sensation (wrong meaning)
2人気hitokea sign of human presence (wrong meaning)
3手近tejikaclose at hand (about proximity, not size)
4手ごろtegorojust the right size/price

Why: Day 1's 手ごろ specifically describes a size or price being "just right" — exactly this bag's fit for a wallet.

Q2

その会社は、この春、海外に（　）するらしい。Sono kaisha wa, kono haru, kaigai ni (___) suru rashii.

Full sentence: その会社は、この春、海外に進出するらしい。...kaigai ni shinshutsu suru rashii.
EN: That company is apparently expanding overseas this spring.
HI: ऐसा लगता है कि वह कंपनी इस वसंत विदेशों में विस्तार करने वाली है।
GU: એવું લાગે છે કે એ કંપની આ વસંતમાં વિદેશમાં વિસ્તરવાની છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1自立jiritsubecoming independent (wrong meaning)
2先着senchakuarriving first (wrong meaning)
3先行senkoupreceding, being ahead (wrong meaning — doesn't fit 海外に)
4進出shinshutsuadvancing/expanding into

Why: Day 3's 海外に進出する is the fixed collocation for a company expanding into overseas markets.

Q3

彼は（　）があって、大金持ちの彼女にアプローチした。Kare wa (___) ga atte, oogane mochi no kanojo ni apuroochi shita.

Full sentence: 彼は下心があって、大金持ちの彼女にアプローチした。...shitagokoro ga atte...
EN: He had an ulterior motive and approached the wealthy woman.
HI: उसके मन में कुछ और था, इसलिए वह उस अमीर महिला के पास गया।
GU: તેના મનમાં કંઈક છૂપો ઈરાદો હતો, એટલે તે એ પૈસાદાર સ્ત્રી પાસે ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1心づかいkokorozukaithoughtfulness (wrong meaning — a positive trait)
2気心kigokorodisposition/rapport (wrong meaning)
3内心naishininwardly (an adverb, not a motive one "has")
4下心shitagokoroan ulterior motive

Why: Day 1's 下心がある exactly means "to have an ulterior/hidden motive" — matching approaching someone for a hidden reason (here, her wealth).

Q4

警察は犯人逮捕の（　）をつかんだようだ。Keisatsu wa hannin taiho no (___) o tsukanda you da.

Full sentence: 警察は犯人逮捕の手がかりをつかんだようだ。...taiho no tegakari o tsukanda you da.
EN: The police seem to have grasped a clue toward the culprit's arrest.
HI: ऐसा लगता है कि पुलिस को अपराधी की गिरफ़्तारी का एक सुराग़ मिल गया है।
GU: એવું લાગે છે કે પોલીસને ગુનેગારની ધરપકડનો એક સુરાગ મળી ગયો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1心当たりkokoroataria guess/idea (a mental impression, not something physically "grasped")
2手がかりtegakaria clue, a lead
3手はずtehazuarrangements (wrong meaning)
4見通しmitoushia forecast/outlook (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 1's 手がかりをつかむ ("to grasp a clue/lead") is the standard idiom for investigative progress; 心当たり is a vaguer personal hunch, not literally "grasped."

Q5

彼は、親に言われたのではなく、（　）的に留学したいと言った。Kare wa, oya ni iwareta no de wa naku, (___)teki ni ryuugaku shitai to itta.

Full sentence: 彼は、親に言われたのではなく、自発的に留学したいと言った。...jihatsuteki ni ryuugaku shitai to itta.
EN: He said he wanted to study abroad on his own initiative, not because his parents told him to.
HI: उसने कहा कि वह माता-पिता के कहने पर नहीं, बल्कि अपनी मर्ज़ी से विदेश पढ़ाई करना चाहता है।
GU: તેણે કહ્યું કે તે માતા-પિતાના કહેવાથી નહીં, પણ પોતાની ઈચ્છાથી વિદેશમાં ભણવા માંગે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1意地ijistubbornness (wrong meaning, and 意地的 isn't a standard form)
2意思ishiintention (意思的 isn't standard usage)
3自発jihatsuof one's own accord
4自首jishuturning oneself in to police (unrelated meaning)

Why: Day 6's 自発的に is the fixed adverbial form for "on one's own initiative," directly contrasting with being told to do something by someone else.

Q6

この病気（　）の症状は、下痢と吐き気です。Kono byouki (___) no shoujou wa, geri to hakike desu.

Full sentence: この病気特有の症状は、下痢と吐き気です。Kono byouki tokuyuu no shoujou wa...
EN: The symptoms peculiar to this disease are diarrhea and nausea.
HI: इस बीमारी के ख़ास लक्षण हैं दस्त और जी मिचलाना।
GU: આ બીમારીના ખાસ લક્ષણો છે ઝાડા અને ઉબકા.
#JapaneseEnglish
1特色tokushokua distinctive characteristic (for things/places, not medical symptoms)
2特有tokuyuupeculiar to, characteristic of
3主体shutaithe main body/core (wrong meaning)
4作用sayouan effect/reaction (a different grammatical slot — 副作用 would fit, not 作用 alone here)

Why: Day 5's ～特有の症状 ("symptoms peculiar to ~") is the standard medical collocation; 特色 describes a place/thing's distinctive flavor, not a disease's symptoms.

Q7

長野県の（　）はりんごです。Nagano-ken no (___) wa ringo desu.

Full sentence: 長野県の名産はりんごです。Nagano-ken no meisan wa ringo desu.
EN: Nagano Prefecture's famous local product is apples.
HI: नागानो प्रान्त का प्रसिद्ध उत्पाद सेब है।
GU: નાગાનો પ્રાંતનું પ્રખ્યાત ઉત્પાદન સફરજન છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1産地sanchia producing region (Nagano IS a producing region, but "Nagano's 産地 is apples" is backwards phrasing)
2産出sanshutsuproducing/yielding (a verb, doesn't fit の...は...です)
3主食shushokua staple food (apples aren't a staple food)
4名産meisana noted/famous local product

Why: Day 5's ～の名産は…です ("~'s famous product is...") is the natural way to name a region's signature product; 産地 names the region itself as a producing area, the reverse direction of this sentence.

Q8

今度の展覧会には、この絵を（　）しよう。Kondo no tenrankai ni wa, kono e o (___) shiyou.

Full sentence: 今度の展覧会には、この絵を出品しよう。...kono e o shuppin shiyou.
EN: Let's submit this painting to the upcoming exhibition.
HI: आने वाली प्रदर्शनी में इस पेंटिंग को प्रस्तुत करते हैं।
GU: આવનારા પ્રદર્શનમાં આ ચિત્ર રજૂ કરીએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1出品shuppinto submit/exhibit works
2代理dairiacting on someone's behalf (wrong meaning)
3出生shusshoubirth (wrong meaning)
4用品youhinsupplies/goods (a noun, not a verb "to submit")

Why: Day 2's 展覧会に出品する ("to submit works to an exhibition") is the exact fixed collocation for this sentence.

Q9

リストラされた彼は、今はコンビニのバイトで（　）を立てている。Risutora sareta kare wa, ima wa konbini no baito de (___) o tatete iru.

Full sentence: リストラされた彼は、今はコンビニのバイトで生計を立てている。...konbini no baito de seikei o tatete iru.
EN: Having been laid off, he now makes a living with a convenience-store part-time job.
HI: नौकरी से निकाले जाने के बाद, वह अब कन्वीनियंस स्टोर की पार्ट-टाइम नौकरी से गुज़ारा कर रहा है।
GU: નોકરીમાંથી કાઢી મુકાયા પછી, તે હવે કન્વીનિયન્સ સ્ટોરની પાર્ટ-ટાઈમ નોકરીથી ગુજરાન ચલાવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1主体shutaithe main body/core (wrong meaning)
2生産seisanmanufacturing (wrong meaning — for goods, not a livelihood)
3自立jiritsuindependence (doesn't collocate with 立てる)
4生計seikeia livelihood

Why: Day 5's 生計を立てる ("to make a living") is the fixed idiom for earning enough to live on; 自立 (independence) is a related idea but doesn't take 立てる as its verb.

Q10

この地方では、正月に川で泳ぐという（　）がある。Kono chihou de wa, shougatsu ni kawa de oyogu to iu (___) ga aru.

Full sentence: この地方では、正月に川で泳ぐという風習がある。...to iu fuushuu ga aru.
EN: In this region, there's a custom of swimming in the river on New Year's Day.
HI: इस इलाक़े में नए साल के दिन नदी में तैरने की एक परंपरा है।
GU: આ વિસ્તારમાં નવા વર્ષના દિવસે નદીમાં તરવાનો એક રિવાજ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1風習fuushuua custom, tradition
2意図itoan intention (wrong meaning)
3野心yashinambition (wrong meaning)
4特産tokusana local specialty product (wrong meaning — for goods, not customs)

Why: Day 6's 風習がある ("there is a custom of...") is the standard way to describe a region's traditional practice.

Q11

Q13

公の機関に属さない人。Ooyake no kikan ni zokusanai hito. ("A person who doesn't belong to a public institution.")

Q16

Q18

Q21

下火shitabi

#JapaneseEnglish
1私は彼に好きだと言いたいが、下火がないのでなかなか言えない。Watashi wa kare ni suki da to iitai ga, shitabi ga nai node nakanaka ienai."I want to tell him I like him, but I have no 下火, so I can't say it" — wrong; should be 勇気 (courage), an unrelated word
2気持ちが下火になったので、落ち着いて試験を受けることができた。Kimochi ga shitabi ni natta node, ochitsuite shiken o ukeru koto ga dekita."My feelings became 下火, so I could calmly take the exam" — wrong register; personal nervousness "settling down" needs 落ち着く, not 下火 (reserved for trends/epidemics/booms)
3貯金の額が下火になったので、不安になってきた。Chokin no gaku ga shitabi ni natta node, fuan ni natte kita."The savings amount became 下火, so I got anxious" — wrong; a shrinking balance needs 減る, not 下火
4ペットブームが最近下火になってきた。Petto buumu ga saikin shitabi ni natte kita.The pet-ownership boom has recently died down.

Why: 下火になる describes a trend, fad, epidemic, or popularity dying down — sentence 4 matches Day 2's own example (インフルエンザの流行が下火になってきた) exactly; 1–3 each swap in an unrelated word that happens to fit the same sentence slot.

Q22

台無しdainashi

#JapaneseEnglish
1味付けを間違えてしまい、せっかくのシチューが台無しになってしまった。Ajitsuke o machigaete shimai, sekkaku no shichuu ga dainashi ni natte shimatta.I got the seasoning wrong, and the stew I'd worked hard on was ruined.
2先日買ったDVDプレーヤーは台無しの不良品だった。Senjitsu katta DVD pureeyaa wa dainashi no furyouhin datta."The DVD player I bought was a 台無し defective item" — wrong; 台無し isn't used as an adjective describing a product's condition when bought, just 不良品 alone
3俳優たちの演技のおかげで監督は台無しにならなかった。Haiyuu-tachi no engi no okage de kantoku wa dainashi ni naranakatta."Thanks to the actors' performances, the director didn't become 台無し" — wrong; 台無し describes an effort/thing being ruined, not a person
4一生懸命勉強した結果が台無しで困った。Isshoukenmei benkyou shita kekka ga dainashi de komatta."The result of studying hard was 台無し and I was troubled" — awkward; the natural phrasing ruins the effort (苦労が台無しになった), not "the result" itself

Why: 台無しになる describes an effort or thing being spoiled/ruined, typically by an external cause — sentence 1 mirrors Day 6's own example (雨で桜が台無しになる) exactly; 2–4 each misuse it as an adjective for a product, a person, or an abstract result.

Q23

生かすikasu

#JapaneseEnglish
1節約をするのは、生活を生かした方法だ。Setsuyaku o suru no wa, seikatsu o ikashita houhou da."Saving money is a method that 生かした one's life" — wrong; not a natural collocation
2自分の意見を生かしたので、みんなに嫌われてしまった。Jibun no iken o ikashita node, minna ni kirawarete shimatta."Because I 生かした my own opinion, I was disliked" — wrong; pushing one's opinion needs 押し通す, not 生かす
3この経験を生かして、今後の仕事に役立ててください。Kono keiken o ikashite, kongo no shigoto ni yakudatete kudasai.Please make use of this experience and put it toward your future work.
4事業を生かしたから、彼が首になるはずはない。Jigyou o ikashita kara, kare ga kubi ni naru hazu wa nai."Because he 生かした the business, there's no way he'd be fired" — wrong; not a natural collocation

Why: 生かす means "to make good use of" something valuable one already has (skill, experience) — sentence 3 mirrors Day 5's own example (経験を生かす) precisely; 1, 2, and 4 all force 生かす onto objects it doesn't naturally combine with.

Q24

目方mekata

#JapaneseEnglish
1彼の目方が不明なので、連絡することはできない。Kare no mekata ga fumei na node, renraku suru koto wa dekinai."His 目方 [weight] is unknown, so we can't contact him" — wrong; should be 行方 (whereabouts), an unrelated word
2人気歌手が人の目方を避けて裏口から帰っていった。Ninki kashu ga hito no mekata o sakete uraguchi kara kaette itta."The popular singer avoided people's 目方 [weight] and left by the back door" — wrong; should be 人目 (hitome, "the public eye"), this week's own Day 3 word
3今後の政局がどうなるか、目方が付かない状況だ。Kongo no seikyoku ga dou naru ka, mekata ga tsukanai joukyou da."It's a situation where 目方 [weight] can't be attached to how politics will go" — wrong; should be 見当がつかない ("no way to guess"), a fixed idiom unrelated to weight
4容器は商品の目方に含まれていません。Youki wa shouhin no mekata ni fukumarete imasen.The container's weight is not included in the product's weight.

Why: 目方 literally means "weight" — sentence 4 (net weight excluding packaging) is the only one using it in its real meaning; 1–3 each substitute it for an unrelated word (行方, 人目, 見当) that merely happens to fit the same sentence slot.

Q25

小売りkouri

#JapaneseEnglish
1これは原価は安いのに、小売り価格があまりに高い。Kore wa genka wa yasui noni, kouri kakaku ga amari ni takai.Even though the cost price is cheap, the retail price is far too high.
2コンサートの小売り券を購入した。Konsaato no kouriken o kounyuu shita."I bought a 小売り ticket for the concert" — wrong; should be 前売り券 (advance ticket), this week's own Day 4 word
3無理な小売りで、もうけがなくなった。Muri na kouri de, mouke ga nakunatta."Due to unreasonable 小売り, profit disappeared" — wrong; should be 安売り (discount selling), an unrelated word
4新築の小売り住宅を購入した。Shinchiku no kouri juutaku o kounyuu shita."I bought a newly built 小売り house" — wrong; should be 建売り住宅 (a pre-built house for sale), an unrelated word

Why: 小売り価格 ("retail price," contrasted with 原価/wholesale cost) is a completely standard compound, matching sentence 1 exactly; 2–4 each substitute a different, unrelated 売り-word that happens to fit the same sentence slot.

Test-day strategy: This chapter's traps run in both directions — the same kanji read two ways (分別, 心中, 人気, 目下, 大家, 市場) and the same reading written with different kanji (回答／解答, 意思／意志, 自主／自首). Since neither the kanji alone nor the sound alone is reliable on its own, always check what role the word plays in the sentence — a time adverb vs. a person's rank, a economic concept vs. a physical place, an emotion vs. a legal act.
