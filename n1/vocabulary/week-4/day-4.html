4日目 — 意図・終日・小売り (Ito / Shuujitsu / Kouri)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.66–67 (PDF pages 57–58). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 9.
Theme
Compounds from eight kanji: 体 (body/form), 意 (intent/mind), 見 (see), 着 (attach/arrive), 日 (day), 自 (self), 売 (sell), 間 (interval/space) — the widest kanji spread of any day so far.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.69 (Day 5's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
30 words across eight kanji groups (体/意/見/日 on p.66, 自/売/間 on p.67, plus 着 splitting across the page break). Same example-in-Note format as Days 1–3. Two same-reading-different-kanji pairs appear back to back today: 意思／意志 and 自主／自首.
→ = a related compound printed alongside the headword
⇔ = antonym
＊ = a free-text caution/clarification note the book prints next to a headword
Same reading, different kanji = another face of this chapter's trap, seen today in 意思／意志 and 自主／自首

Q1

嵐のため、電車のダイヤが（　）乱れた。Arashi no tame, densha no daiya ga (___) midareta.

Full sentence: 嵐のため、電車のダイヤが終日乱れた。...densha no daiya ga shuujitsu midareta.
EN: Because of the storm, train schedules were disrupted all day.
HI: तूफ़ान की वजह से ट्रेनों का समय पूरे दिन गड़बड़ रहा।
GU: તોફાનને કારણે ટ્રેનનું ટાઈમટેબલ આખો દિવસ ખોરવાયેલું રહ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a日々hibiday by day (a recurring pattern, wrong for a single day's event)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b終日shuujitsuall day longदिन भरઆખો દિવસ

Why: 終日 describes something continuing for the entire length of one day — exactly a storm-caused disruption; 日々 describes a recurring daily pattern across many days, not one day's duration.

EN: This is for wholesalers, so we don't sell it retail.
HI: यह थोक व्यापारियों के लिए है, इसलिए खुदरा नहीं बेचते।
GU: આ જથ્થાબંધ વેપારીઓ માટે છે, એટલે છૂટક વેચતા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a前売りmaeuriadvance sale (wrong meaning — about timing, not sales channel)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

Full sentence: こうなったら意地でも完成させてみせよう。...iji de mo kansei sasete miseyou.
EN: At this point, I'll finish it no matter what it takes.
HI: अब तो चाहे कुछ भी हो, मैं इसे पूरा करके दिखाऊँगा।
GU: હવે તો ગમે તે થાય, હું આ પૂરું કરીને જ બતાવીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a意地ijistubbornness, prideज़िद, हठજિદ્દ
b意思ishiintention (doesn't take でも in this idiom)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 意地でも～ is a fixed idiom meaning "out of sheer stubbornness/no matter what"; 意思 (a stated intention) doesn't appear in this construction.

GU: કામ વચ્ચેના સમયમાં હું મારી મનપસંદ કૉફી શોપ પર ગયો.

Full sentence: 大雪なので外出するのを見合わせた。...gaishutsu suru no o miawaseta.
EN: Because of the heavy snow, I decided to postpone going out.
HI: भारी बर्फ़बारी की वजह से बाहर जाना टाल दिया।
GU: ભારે બરફવર્ષાને કારણે બહાર જવાનું મુલતવી રાખ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today brings the reverse of Day 3's trap: two pairs read identically but written with different kanji and meaning different things:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
意思ishi An intention (as expressed to others) What you've communicated you plan to do. 意思表示 — formally expressing your intention.
意志ishi Willpower, resolve Same reading, different kanji (志 = "aspiration") — an inner trait, not a communicated plan. 意志が固い — "strong-willed, resolute."
自主jishu One's own initiative, autonomy Acting without being told — independence of action. 自主的にトレーニングする — training on one's own initiative.
自首jishu Turning oneself in (to police) Same reading, unrelated kanji/meaning — a legal-context word only. 警察に自首する — surrendering to the police.
見下すmikudasu To look down on someone (socially) A judgmental, disrespectful attitude toward a person. 人を見下す — belittling someone.
見下ろすmiorosu To look down from a height (literally) Purely physical/visual — a viewpoint from above, no judgment implied. 屋上から見下ろす — looking down from a rooftop.
Exam trap: Because 意思 and 意志 are perfect homophones, and 自主 and 自首 are too, N1 listening/reading questions can only be disambiguated by context — 意志 always pairs with adjectives of strength/weakness (固い／弱い), while 意思 pairs with expressing or confirming a plan (表示する／確認する). 自首 only ever appears with 警察 or a crime; 自主 appears with 的（に）or トレ, describing self-driven effort.
