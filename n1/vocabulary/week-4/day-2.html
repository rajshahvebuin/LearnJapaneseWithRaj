2日目 — 気長・下火・上の空 (Kinaga / Shitabi / Uwa no sora)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.62–63 (PDF pages 53–54). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 9.
Theme
Compounds built from five everyday kanji: 気 (spirit/feeling), 出 (go out/emerge), 下 (below/under), 上 (above/up), 回 (turn/times) — each headword shown with its own example, grouped by base kanji exactly as the book prints them.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.65 (Day 3's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
27 words across five kanji groups, spanning both printed pages (回 group starts on p.62 and finishes at the top of p.63, before 練習Ⅰ/Ⅱ). Same format as Day 1: headword + book's own example phrase (folded into the Note column with romaji) + the book's English kept as-is, with Hindi/Gujarati added.
→ = a related compound printed alongside the headword
⇔ = antonym
＊ = a free-text caution/clarification note the book prints next to a headword (varies each time)
Same kanji, different reading / same reading, different kanji = this chapter's recurring trap — see Confusion Pairs below
Warm-up

（　）の中に入るのは？(___) no naka ni hairu no wa?
インフルエンザは今月に入って少し（　）になった。Infuruenza wa kongetsu ni haitte sukoshi (___) ni natta.
("The flu has [___] a little since this month started.")

下火 (shitabi) — "died down, on the wane" (today's title word; the book contrasts it with the look-alikes 下回り and 下地 shown alongside)

Q1

彼は面倒なことはいつも（　）にする。Kare wa mendou na koto wa itsumo (___) ni suru.

Full sentence: 彼は面倒なことはいつも後回しにする。...itsumo atomawashi ni suru.
EN: He always puts off troublesome things.
HI: वह हमेशा झंझट वाले कामों को टाल देता है।
GU: તે હંમેશા મુશ્કેલ કામોને મુલતવી રાખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a回り道mawarimichia detour (a route, not a way of handling tasks)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b後回しatomawashiputting something offटालनाમુલતવી રાખવું

Why: 後回しにする is the fixed phrase for postponing a task; 回り道 describes a physical detour on a route, unrelated to task priority.

Q2

彼の（　）地は、青森です。Kare no (___) chi wa, Aomori desu.

EN: His birthplace is Aomori.
HI: उसका जन्मस्थान आओमोरी है।
GU: તેનું જન્મસ્થળ આઓમોરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a出生shusshoubirthजन्मજન્મ
b出産shussangiving birth (doesn't compound with 地 this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's list has both directions of this chapter's kanji trap: the same kanji read two ways (人気), and the same reading written with two different kanji (回答／解答):

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
人気hitoke A sign of human presence Whether anyone seems to be around at all — a near-empty-place word. 人気のない通り — a deserted street.
人気ninki Popularity (not in today's list, but the far more common reading) Same kanji — how well-liked something/someone is. 人気のある歌手 — a popular singer. Context (でとない vs である) usually disambiguates.
回答kaitou A reply/response (to an inquiry, survey) For responding to a question that was asked of you personally/administratively. アンケートに回答する — filling out a questionnaire.
解答kaitou An answer (to a problem/quiz) Same reading, different kanji (解 = "solve") — for solving a set problem, not replying to someone. 問題に解答する — answering a test question.
上回るuwamawaru ／ 下回るshitamawaru Exceed / fall short of A clean antonym pair for comparing a figure against an expectation. 予想を上回る／下回る — beating/missing a forecast.
Exam trap: 出勤（しゅっきん, "commute to work") and 出動（しゅつどう, "be dispatched/mobilized") look and sound similar and both involve "going somewhere for work," but 出動 is reserved for emergency services, troops, or vehicles being sent to a scene — a company employee always 出勤s, never 出動s.
