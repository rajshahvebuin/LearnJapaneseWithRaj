JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 4 — 初級漢字の語彙を覚えよう (Shokyuu kanji no goi o oboeyou)

6日目 — 新入り・本音・発足 (Shin'iri / Honne / Hossoku)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.70–71 (PDF pages 61–62). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 9.
Theme
Compounds from 国 (nation), 新 (new), 土 (earth), 業 (trade/work), 音 (sound), 発 (emit/start), 風 (wind/custom), 春 (spring), 台 (platform/stand), plus a final ungrouped その他 (other) cluster of six higher-register words the book doesn't file under a single kanji.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.74 (Day 7's third page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
32 words: 国/新/土/業/音/発/風/春 on p.70, 台 + その他 on p.71. Last content day before Day 7's 実戦問題 review test — the book's その他 grouping here is a new wrinkle: not every word in this chapter fits neatly under one shared kanji.
→ = a related compound printed alongside the headword
その他 = "miscellaneous" — the book's own catch-all group for words that don't share a kanji with anything else on the page
Warm-up

何という？Nanto iu?
組織や団体などが設立されて活動を始めること。Soshiki ya dantai nado ga setsuritsu sarete katsudou o hajimeru koto.
("The word for when an organization or group is established and begins its activities.")

発足（する） (hossoku (suru)) — "to be founded, launch" (today's title word; also read ほっそく)

Q1

次期社長は田中専務だろうという意見が（　）だ。Jiki shachou wa Tanaka senmu darou to iu iken ga (___) da.

Full sentence: 次期社長は田中専務だろうという意見が有力だ。...iken ga yuuryoku da.
EN: The prevailing view is that Managing Director Tanaka will be the next president.
HI: ऐसी प्रबल राय है कि अगला अध्यक्ष तनाका होंगे।
GU: એવો પ્રબળ મત છે કે આગામી પ્રમુખ તનાકા હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a正味shouminet, actual (a quantity word, wrong domain)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b有力yuuryokuinfluential, prevailingप्रबल, प्रभावशालीપ્રબળ, પ્રભાવશાળી

Why: 有力な意見 ("a prevailing/strong opinion") is the fixed collocation; 正味 describes a net quantity (like hours or weight), unrelated to how widely held an opinion is.

Q2

川の（　）に沿って道路がある。Kawa no (___) ni sotte douro ga aru.

Full sentence: 川の土手に沿って道路がある。Kawa no dote ni sotte douro ga aru.
EN: There's a road along the riverbank.
HI: नदी के किनारे के साथ-साथ एक सड़क है।
GU: નદીના કિનારે એક રસ્તો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a土手dotea riverbank/embankmentनदी का किनाराનદીકિનારો
b土台dodaia foundation (of a structure, not a riverside path)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 土手 specifically means the raised bank alongside a river; 土台 means the foundation of a building or an abstract basis, not a riverside feature.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a本音honneone's true feelingsअसली मंशाસાચી લાગણી

Q5

新しいプロジェクトを（　）させた。Atarashii purojekuto o (___) saseta.

Full sentence: 新しいプロジェクトを発足させた。...purojekuto o hossoku saseta.
EN: We launched a new project.
HI: हमने एक नई परियोजना शुरू की।
GU: અમે નવો પ્રોજેક્ટ શરૂ કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a発足hossokulaunching, being establishedशुरू करना, स्थापित करनाશરૂ કરવું, સ્થાપવું
b自発jihatsuspontaneous (a manner adjective, doesn't take させる as "launch X")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: એ ટૂરનું શેડ્યૂલ ખૂબ કપરું હતું — 2 અઠવાડિયામાં 10 જગ્યાએ ફરવાનું હતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's near-synonym cluster is all about "new," plus one word that looks like a compound but is actually a fixed idiom:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
新入りshin'iri A newcomer (casual/informal register) Everyday word for "the new person" in a group, club, or team. 新入りの会員 — a new club member.
新入shinnyuu Newly entered/enrolled (formal register) A more formal prefix used in institutional compounds. 新入生／新入社員 — a new student/employee.
新人shinjin A rookie, up-and-comer Emphasizes being new to a profession/skill, not just an organization. 新人歌手 — a debut/rookie singer.
Exam trap: 台無し (だいなし) looks like it should mean something about a 台 ("platform/stand"), but it's a fixed idiom meaning "ruined, spoiled" with no literal connection to a stand or platform — don't try to derive its meaning compositionally from 台 the way most other words in this chapter can be.
