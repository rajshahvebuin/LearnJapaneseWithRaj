問題1 — choose the best word for the blank (1問4点)

Q1

うそをついているのがばれるのではないかと、（　）した。Uso o tsuite iru no ga bareru no de wa nai ka to, (___) shita.

Full sentence: うそをついているのがばれるのではないかと、はらはらした。...harahara shita.
EN: I was on edge, worried that my lie might be found out.
HI: मुझे बेचैनी थी कि कहीं मेरा झूठ पकड़ा न जाए।
GU: મને ચિંતા હતી કે ક્યાંક મારું જૂઠ પકડાઈ ન જાય.
#JapaneseEnglish
1はらはらharaharaon edge, nervously anticipating an uncertain outcome
2おどおどodoodotimid in manner/presence (wrong nuance — about how one acts in front of others, not anticipatory anxiety)
3むかむかmukamukanauseous/irritated (wrong meaning)
4うんざりunzarifed up (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 2's はらはらする describes the nervous, on-edge feeling of watching a risky/uncertain situation — exactly the anxiety of maybe being caught in a lie.

Q2

父は（　）医者に忠告されているにもかかわらず、酒をやめようとしない。Chichi wa (___) isha ni chuukoku sarete iru ni mo kakawarazu, sake o yameyou to shinai.

Full sentence: 父は再三医者に忠告されているにもかかわらず、酒をやめようとしない。...saisan isha ni chuukoku sarete iru...
EN: Even though the doctor has warned my father over and over, he won't quit drinking.
HI: डॉक्टर के बार-बार समझाने के बावजूद पिताजी शराब छोड़ने को तैयार नहीं हैं।
GU: ડૉક્ટરે વારંવાર ચેતવણી આપી હોવા છતાં પપ્પા દારૂ છોડવા તૈયાર નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1不意にfui niunexpectedly (wrong meaning)
2とっさにtossa niinstantly (wrong meaning)
3片時もkatatoki monot even a moment (wrong — needs a negative verb, but 忠告されている is affirmative)
4再三saisanover and over again

Why: Day 4's 再三 fits repeated warnings over time; the others don't describe repetition of an action.

Q3

衝突事故を起こした車は（　）つぶれていた。Shoutotsu jiko o okoshita kuruma wa (___) tsuburete ita.

Full sentence: 衝突事故を起こした車はぐちゃぐちゃにつぶれていた。...kuruma wa guchagucha ni tsuburete ita.
EN: The car involved in the collision was crushed into a mangled wreck.
HI: टक्कर में शामिल कार बुरी तरह चकनाचूर हो गई थी।
GU: અથડામણમાં સામેલ કાર સંપૂર્ણપણે ભાંગીને ભુક્કો થઈ ગઈ હતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1さんざんsanzanseverely (wrong grammar — modifies an experience one undergoes, not the physical shape of an object)
2ぐちゃぐちゃにguchagucha niinto a mangled mess
3はなはだhanahadaextremely (wrong register — too formal/abstract for physical wreckage)
4ちょくちょくchokuchokuevery once in a while (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 1's ぐちゃぐちゃ describes something crushed into a disordered mess — exactly a wrecked car; the others don't fit physical destruction.

Q4

荷物は（　）そこに置いておいてください。Nimotsu wa (___) soko ni oite oite kudasai.

Full sentence: 荷物はとりあえずそこに置いておいてください。...toriaezu soko ni oite oite kudasai.
EN: For now, please just leave the luggage there.
HI: फ़िलहाल सामान वहीं रख दीजिए।
GU: હાલ પૂરતું સામાન ત્યાં જ મૂકી દો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とりあえずtoriaezufor now, first of all
2もっぱらmopparaexclusively (wrong meaning)
3ことごとくkotogotokuall, without exception (wrong meaning)
4しょっちゅうshocchuuall the time (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 6's とりあえず is the fixed word for a provisional, for-now instruction; the others don't express temporariness.

Q5

徹夜で作業して（　）期限に間に合わせることができた。Tetsuya de sagyou shite (___) kigen ni ma ni awaseru koto ga dekita.

Full sentence: 徹夜で作業してかろうじて期限に間に合わせることができた。...karoujite kigen ni ma ni awaseru koto ga dekita.
EN: I worked through the night and just barely made the deadline.
HI: रात भर काम करके मैंने बड़ी मुश्किल से समय-सीमा पूरी की।
GU: આખી રાત કામ કરીને માંડ માંડ સમયમર્યાદા પૂરી કરી શક્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1もしかしてmoshika shiteby any chance (wrong meaning)
2かろうじてkaroujitebarely
3かえってkaetteon the contrary (wrong meaning)
4わざわざwazawazagoing out of one's way (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 6's かろうじて describes an outcome achieved with the narrowest possible margin, matching "worked all night and just barely made it."

Q6

ブランド品と見れば何でも買う彼女には（　）愛想が尽きた。Burando-hin to mireba nan demo kau kanojo ni wa (___) aiso ga tsukita.

Full sentence: ブランド品と見れば何でも買う彼女にはつくづく愛想が尽きた。...kanojo ni wa tsukuzuku aiso ga tsukita.
EN: I've thoroughly lost all patience with her buying anything as long as it's a brand-name item.
HI: ब्रांड का सामान देखते ही कुछ भी ख़रीद लेने वाली उससे अब पूरी तरह मन उचट गया है।
GU: બ્રાન્ડની વસ્તુ જોતાં જ ગમે તે ખરીદી લેતી તેનાથી હવે સંપૂર્ણપણે કંટાળી ગયો છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1むかむかmukamukairritated (close, but describes a flare of anger, not a settled, deep disillusionment)
2ひたすらhitasurasingle-mindedly (wrong meaning — describes focused action, not an emotional state)
3やたらとyatara toexcessively (wrong meaning)
4つくづくtsukuzukuthoroughly, deeply (a feeling)

Why: Day 2's つくづく intensifies a deep, settled inner feeling (自分がつくづくいやになる) — fitting 愛想が尽きる (become thoroughly disillusioned/fed up), a slow-building realization rather than a sudden flare of anger.

Q7

赤ちゃんの肌は（　）している。Akachan no hada wa (___) shite iru.

Full sentence: 赤ちゃんの肌はつやつやしている。Akachan no hada wa tsuyatsuya shite iru.
EN: A baby's skin is glossy/silky smooth.
HI: बच्चे की त्वचा चमकदार और मुलायम होती है।
GU: બાળકની ત્વચા ચમકદાર અને મુલાયમ હોય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1まるまるmarumaruplump (a real trait of babies, but describes body shape, not skin texture)
2ごしごしgoshigoshiscrubbing vigorously (wrong meaning)
3つやつやtsuyatsuyaglossy, silky
4ぴんぴんpinpinfull of life/vigor (wrong domain — describes vitality, not skin texture)

Why: Day 1's つやつや describes glossy, silky texture (skin/hair) directly; まるまる and ぴんぴん describe unrelated qualities (build, vitality) that a baby also happens to have, but don't answer "what is the skin like."

Q8

注意しなくても（　）間違いに気が付くだろう。Chuui shinakute mo (___) machigai ni ki ga tsuku darou.

Full sentence: 注意しなくてもおのずと間違いに気が付くだろう。...onozuto machigai ni ki ga tsuku darou.
EN: Even without pointing it out, they'll naturally notice the mistake on their own.
HI: बताए बिना भी उसे अपनी ग़लती अपने आप पता चल जाएगी।
GU: કહ્યા વગર પણ તેને પોતાની ભૂલ પોતાની મેળે ખબર પડી જશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1案の定an no jouas expected (wrong meaning — a comment on an outcome matching a prediction, not "by itself")
2いかにもikanimotypically (wrong meaning)
3おのずとonozutonaturally, by itself
4あえてaetedare to (wrong meaning — implies deliberate effort, opposite of "without being told")

Why: Day 6's 自ずと describes something happening on its own, without external intervention (努力すれば自ずから道は開ける) — matching noticing a mistake without being told.

Q9

そろそろ失礼します。（　）とおじゃましました。Sorosoro shitsurei shimasu. (___) to ojama shimashita.

Full sentence: そろそろ失礼します。長々とおじゃましました。...Naganaga to ojama shimashita.
EN: I should get going soon. I'm sorry to have stayed and imposed for so long.
HI: अब मुझे चलना चाहिए। इतनी देर तक बैठे रहने के लिए माफ़ी चाहता हूँ।
GU: હવે મારે નીકળવું જોઈએ. આટલો લાંબો સમય રોકાવા બદલ માફ કરશો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1点々tentendotted here and there (wrong meaning)
2転々tentenmoving from place to place (wrong meaning)
3着々chakuchakusteadily making progress (wrong meaning)
4長々naganagafor a long time (overstaying)

Why: Day 2's own example is nearly identical — 長々とおじゃましました, the set phrase for apologizing after a long visit.

Q10

最近、昼食は（　）隣のラーメン屋で済ませています。Saikin, chuushoku wa (___) tonari no raamen-ya de sumasete imasu.

Full sentence: 最近、昼食はもっぱら隣のラーメン屋で済ませています。...moppara tonari no raamen-ya de sumasete imasu.
EN: Lately, I've been having lunch exclusively at the ramen shop next door.
HI: आजकल मैं दोपहर का खाना हमेशा बग़ल की रामेन दुकान पर ही खाता हूँ।
GU: આજકાલ હું બપોરનું ભોજન હંમેશા બાજુની રામેન દુકાનમાં જ કરું છું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ひたすらhitasurasingle-mindedly (wrong nuance — for effort toward a goal, not a repeated habitual choice)
2もろにmoro nisquarely, directly (wrong meaning)
3ひょっとしてhyotto shiteby any chance (wrong meaning)
4もっぱらmopparaexclusively, entirely

Why: Day 5's もっぱら describes exclusively devoting a routine activity to one thing (休日はもっぱらゴルフをしている), matching always eating lunch at the same place.

問題4 — which sentence uses the word correctly? (1問4点)

Q21

到底toutei

#JapaneseEnglish
1その計画は到底進展するでしょう。Sono keikaku wa toutei shinten suru deshou."That plan will到底progress" — wrong; 到底 needs a negative, not an affirmative prediction
2そんな自分勝手な要求は、到底受け入れられません。Sonna jibun katte na youkyuu wa, toutei ukeiraremasen.Such a selfish demand cannot possibly be accepted.
3今日は到底暑くないですね。Kyou wa toutei atsukunai desu ne."It's到底not hot today" — wrong register; a mild "not very hot" needs さほど/それほど, not the extreme-impossibility word 到底
4タバコはここでは到底吸わないでください。Tabako wa koko de wa toutei suwanaide kudasai."到底don't smoke here" — wrong; 到底 doesn't attach to a request/prohibition, only to statements of impossibility

Why: 到底〜ない describes something being flatly, absolutely impossible to do or accept — sentence 2 matches Day 4's own examples (到底間に合わない, 到底無理だ) exactly; 1 wrongly pairs it with an affirmative, 3 confuses it with the much milder さほど, and 4 wrongly attaches it to a prohibition/request.

Q22

ぐっしょりgusshori

#JapaneseEnglish
1傘が無かったので、かばんの中までぐっしょりぬれてしまった。Kasa ga nakatta node, kaban no naka made gusshori nurete shimatta.Since I had no umbrella, even the inside of my bag got completely soaked.
2川の水がぐっしょり流れている。Kawa no mizu ga gusshori nagarete iru."The river water is flowingぐっしょり" — wrong; describes flowing motion, not a soaked state
3今日は一日中雨がぐっしょり降っていた。Kyou wa ichinichijuu ame ga gusshori futte ita."It rainedぐっしょりall day" — wrong; should be ざあざあ, describing the rain itself falling, not a soaked result
4湿気が多くて部屋がぐっしょりしている。Shikke ga ookute heya ga gusshori shite iru."The room isぐっしょりfrom humidity" — wrong; should be じめじめ, describing ambient dampness, not actual liquid soaking

Why: ぐっしょり describes something already thoroughly soaked/drenched with liquid — sentence 1 matches Day 3's own example (汗でぐっしょりぬれる) exactly; 2–4 each confuse it with a different word for a related but distinct water-related state (flowing, falling, humid).

Q23

うんざりunzari

#JapaneseEnglish
1その件に関してはうんざり考えてからお返事いたします。Sono ken ni kanshite wa unzari kangaete kara ohenji itashimasu."I'll replyうんざりconsidering it" — wrong; should be じっくり (carefully), not "fed up"
2テレビは同じような番組ばかりでうんざりだ。Terebi wa onaji you na bangumi bakari de unzari da.I'm sick and tired of TV always having the same kind of programs.
3彼女は風邪で熱があるのか、力なくうんざりと寝ていた。Kanojo wa kaze de netsu ga aru no ka, chikara naku unzari to nete ita."She lay thereうんざり, weakly" — wrong; should be ぐったり (limply exhausted), not "fed up"
4あなたの言うことは、うんざりわかりません。Anata no iu koto wa, unzari wakarimasen."I don'tうんざりunderstand you" — wrong; should be さっぱり (not at all), an intensifier, not an emotion word

Why: うんざり describes being sick and tired of a recurring annoyance — sentence 2 matches Day 3's own example (その話はもううんざりだ) exactly; 1, 3, and 4 each substitute it for a different, unrelated word that happens to fit the same sentence slot.

Q24

ことごとくkotogotoku

#JapaneseEnglish
1新型ロケットの打ち上げはことごとく失敗してしまった。Shingata roketto no uchiage wa kotogotoku shippai shite shimatta.Every single launch of the new rocket model ended in failure.
2私はお酒ではことごとくワインが好きです。Watashi wa osake de wa kotogotoku wain ga suki desu."Among drinks Iことごとくlike wine" — wrong; should be とりわけ/もっぱら (particularly/exclusively), a preference word, not "all without exception"
3駅までことごとく走り続けて、電車に間に合った。Eki made kotogotoku hashiritsuzukete, densha ni ma ni atta."I kept runningことごとくto the station" — wrong; should be ひたすら (single-mindedly), describing manner of a continuous action, not totality of a set
4ことごとくできない問題を解いた。Kotogotoku dekinai mondai o toita.Nonsensical combination — should be something like かろうじて (barely) or やっと (finally), not "all without exception"

Why: ことごとく describes every single item in a set meeting the same fate, without exception — sentence 1 mirrors Day 6's own example (私の案はことごとく却下された) precisely; 2–4 each misuse it in place of a preference, manner, or completion word.

Q25

とりわけtoriwake

#JapaneseEnglish
1料理の前にとりわけビールを2本お願いします。Ryouri no mae ni toriwake biiru o nihon onegai shimasu."Before the food,とりわけtwo beers please" — wrong; should be とりあえず (for now), an unrelated word that merely sounds similar
2休みの日はとりわけ家でごろごろしています。Yasumi no hi wa toriwake ie de gorogoro shite imasu."On days off Iとりわけlaze around at home" — wrong; should be もっぱら (exclusively), describing an exclusive habit, not "especially"
3遠いところをとりわけお越しいただきどうもありがとうございます。Tooi tokoro o toriwake okoshi itadaki doumo arigatou gozaimasu."Thank you forとりわけcoming from so far" — wrong; should be わざわざ (going out of one's way), an unrelated word
4私は甘いものの中でもとりわけショートケーキが好きです。Watashi wa amai mono no naka demo toriwake shootokeeki ga suki desu.Among sweets, I particularly like shortcake.

Why: とりわけ singles one item out from an implied group — sentence 4 mirrors Day 5's own example (とりわけビールが好きだ) precisely, naming the group (甘いもの) explicitly; 1–3 each substitute it for とりあえず, もっぱら, and わざわざ, three unrelated words that happen to fit the same sentence slot.
