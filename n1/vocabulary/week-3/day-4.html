Q1

彼女はぐっすり寝ていて、（a. いっこうに　b. とうてい）目を覚ます気配がない。Kanojo wa gussuri nete ite, (a. ikkou ni / b. toutei) me o samasu kehai ga nai.

Full sentence: 彼女はぐっすり寝ていて、一向に目を覚ます気配がない。...ikkou ni me o samasu kehai ga nai.
EN: She's sound asleep, showing no sign whatsoever of waking up.
HI: वह गहरी नींद में सोई है, जागने का कोई संकेत ही नहीं दिख रहा।
GU: તે ગાઢ ઊંઘમાં છે, જાગવાનો કોઈ સંકેત જ દેખાતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a一向にikkou ninot at all (+ negative)बिल्कुल नहींબિલકુલ નહીં
b到底touteicannot possibly (wrong register — for flat impossibility of an action, not "no sign of")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 一向に…ない matches "not the slightest sign of" (一向に気配がない, echoing the book's own 一向に進まない); 到底…ない is for something being flatly impossible to achieve, a different shade of "not at all."

HI: मैं आज भी कैसेट टेप रिकॉर्डर इस्तेमाल करता हूँ।
GU: હું આજે પણ કેસેટ ટેપ રેકોર્ડર વાપરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいまにima nisomeday (wrong tense — about the future, not an ongoing present habit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: આ નવા મોડેલનું છે, પણ કિંમત એટલી બધી ઊંચી નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aさっぱりsapparinot at all (too strong — implies zero, not "not so much")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The book itself groups these five under "words a negative comes after" — but they're not interchangeable degrees of "not." Compare them directly, as Q1 and Q3 above test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
到底〜ないtoutei ~nai Cannot possibly Flat impossibility of achieving/accepting something — the strongest of the five. "Cannot possibly make it in time"; "absolutely impossible."
一向に〜ないikkou ni ~nai Not at all, no sign of Emphasizes total lack of progress or change over time. "The talks aren't progressing at all."
一切〜ないissai ~nai Not at all, none whatsoever A blanket denial covering every single instance/connection — often legal/formal register. "I have absolutely nothing to do with it."
さっぱり〜ないsappari ~nai Not at all (understanding/memory) Almost always about failing to understand or recall something — a narrower domain than the others. "I don't understand the lecture at all"; "can't remember a thing."
さほど〜ないsahodo ~nai Not that much, not so The mildest of the five — softens rather than flatly denies; means "less than you'd expect," not "zero." "It's not that cold today"; "the price isn't that high."
Exam trap: 一向に normally demands a following negative (一向に〜ない), but the word list itself flags an exception: 一向に平気だ ("I don't care at all/I'm completely fine") pairs it with a positive word, where 一向に instead means "entirely/completely." Don't assume 一向に always signals a negative is coming — check the word it's modifying first.
