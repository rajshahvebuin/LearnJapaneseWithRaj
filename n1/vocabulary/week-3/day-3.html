3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's ○っり family is full of near-identical-sounding pairs that mean completely different things — exactly what Q1 and Q6 above are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
あっさりassari Light/simple; easily, without resistance About something being done or accepted with no fuss or fight — taste, or the manner of losing/admitting. A light-tasting dish; someone giving in or confessing readily.
さっぱりsappari Frank personality; refreshed; (+ない) not at all Three senses in one word: a blunt/frank character trait, a physical feeling of being refreshed, and (only with a negative) a total-absence intensifier. Describing personality, describing a post-bath feeling, or "I don't understand [X] at all."
きっかりkikkari Exactly, precisely Measures an exact point — a time, an amount — with no margin. "Opens at exactly 10:00."
きっぱりkippari Flatly, resolutely Describes firmness of resolve or refusal — a decision, not a measurement. "Flatly refused"; "resolutely quit smoking/drinking."
がっしりgasshiri Solidly built (body) Describes a person's sturdy physical build specifically. Describing someone's muscular, sturdy physique.
がっちりgacchiri Solid (building); shrewd/firm (money) Interchangeable with がっしり for physical solidity, but also extends to being firm/calculating about money — a sense がっしり doesn't have. A sturdy building, or someone who saves money shrewdly.
Exam trap: てっきり always describes a confident assumption that turns out to be wrong (てっきり…と思っていた「けど、違った」) — it cannot be used for a correct guess. Don't confuse it with きっぱり (firmness of decision) or きっかり (exactness of measurement) just because all three start with き/て・っ sounds; none of the three overlap in meaning.
