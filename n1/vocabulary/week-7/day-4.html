JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えよう① (Matomete oboeyou ①)

4日目 — 組み合わせの言葉① (Kumiawase no kotoba ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.114–115 (PDF pages 104–105). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10. New sub-theme starting today: 組み合わせの言葉 ("combination words") — two-part compound verbs built from a base verb (取り〜, 引き〜, 打ち〜, 割り〜, 申し〜, 追い〜, 立ち〜, 読み〜) fused with a second verb.
Theme
28 compound verbs built on 取り〜 (torimost of today's list), 引き〜, 申し〜, 追い〜, 立ち〜, 打ち〜, 割り〜, and 読み〜 — many of the 取り〜 verbs share the same base but produce quite different meanings depending on the second half.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.117 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Many entries have a derived noun form the book marks with an arrow (e.g. 取り締まる → 取り締まり); these are shown in the Note column as "→ noun-form (romaji)."
→ = the derived noun form of the compound verb (marked (N) in the book)
⇔ = antonym
Warm-up

（　）に共通して入るのは？(___) ni kyoutsuu shite hairu no wa?
（　）次ぐ・（　）囲む・（　）寄せる(___) tsugu, (___) kakomu, (___) yoseru
("What word goes in all three blanks? 'to relay (a call)', 'to surround', 'to draw together' — signs shown: 引き, 取り, 付け.")

取り (tori) — 取り次ぐ (relay a message), 取り囲む (surround), 取り寄せる (order in) — all built on the base 取り〜

Q1

花火を（a.打ち明けた b.打ち上げた）。Hanabi o (a. uchiaketa b. uchiageta).

Full sentence: 花火を打ち上げた。Hanabi o uchiageta.
EN: We set off fireworks.
HI: हमने आतिशबाज़ी छोड़ी।
GU: અમે ફટાકડા છોડ્યા.
#JapaneseEnglish
a打ち明けたuchiaketaconfided (wrong verb — fireworks can't be "confided")
b打ち上げたuchiagetalaunched/set off

Why: 打ち上げる is the fixed verb for launching fireworks or a rocket into the sky; 打ち明ける is about revealing a secret.

Q2

借金を強引に（a.取り立てられた b.引き立てられた）。Shakkin o gouin ni (a. toritaterareta b. hikitaterareta).

Full sentence: 借金を強引に取り立てられた。Shakkin o gouin ni toritaterareta.
EN: The debt was forcibly collected from me.
HI: ज़बरदस्ती मुझसे क़र्ज़ वसूला गया।
GU: બળજબરીથી મારી પાસેથી દેવું વસૂલવામાં આવ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
a取り立てられたtoritateraretawas forcibly collected
b引き立てられたhikitateraretawas championed/promoted (wrong meaning)

Why: 取り立てる is the fixed verb for aggressively collecting a debt/tax; 引き立てる means to promote or make someone/something look good — unrelated to money collection.

Q3

昨日、その通りで酔っ払い運転の（a.追い出し b.取り締まり）をしていた。Kinou, sono toori de yopparai unten no (a. oidashi b. torishimari) o shite ita.

Full sentence: 昨日、その通りで酔っ払い運転の取り締まりをしていた。Kinou, sono toori de yopparai unten no torishimari o shite ita.
EN: Yesterday, they were cracking down on drunk driving on that street.
HI: कल उस सड़क पर शराब पीकर गाड़ी चलाने के ख़िलाफ़ कार्रवाई हो रही थी।
GU: ગઈકાલે તે રસ્તા પર દારૂ પીને ડ્રાઇવિંગ સામે કડક કાર્યવાહી ચાલી રહી હતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a追い出しoidashikicking out (wrong verb — not what police do to drivers on a street)
b取り締まりtorishimaria crackdown/policing operation

Why: 取り締まり is the fixed word for police enforcement operations like drunk-driving checkpoints.

Q4

北海道から評判のチョコレート菓子を（a.受けとめた b.取り寄せた）。Hokkaidou kara hyouban no chokoreeto kashi o (a. uketometa b. toriyoseta).

Full sentence: 北海道から評判のチョコレート菓子を取り寄せた。Hokkaidou kara hyouban no chokoreeto kashi o toriyoseta.
EN: I ordered some famous chocolate confections from Hokkaido.
HI: होक्काइदो से मशहूर चॉकलेट मिठाई मँगवाई।
GU: હોક્કાઇડોથી પ્રખ્યાત ચોકલેટ મીઠાઈ મંગાવી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a受けとめたuketometacaught/accepted (wrong verb)
b取り寄せたtoriyosetaordered in from afar

Why: 取り寄せる is the fixed verb for ordering a product from a distant place to be delivered; 受け止める is about catching or psychologically accepting something.

Q5

友人に悩みを（a.打ち明けた b.申し入れた）。Yuujin ni nayami o (a. uchiaketa b. moushiireta).

Full sentence: 友人に悩みを打ち明けた。Yuujin ni nayami o uchiaketa.
EN: I confided my worries to a friend.
HI: मैंने दोस्त को अपनी परेशानी दिल खोलकर बताई।
GU: મેં મિત્રને મારી ચિંતા ખુલ્લા મનથી કહી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a打ち明けたuchiaketaconfided
b申し入れたmoushiiretaformally proposed/lodged (wrong register — too formal for confiding in a friend)

Why: 打ち明ける is the natural verb for opening up about personal troubles to someone; 申し入れる is for formal proposals/protests, not personal confessions.

Q6

一人の中年女性が列に（a.割り当て b.割り込んで）きた。Hitori no chuunen josei ga retsu ni (a. wariate b. warikonde) kita.

Full sentence: 一人の中年女性が列に割り込んできた。Hitori no chuunen josei ga retsu ni warikonde kita.
EN: A middle-aged woman cut into the line.
HI: एक अधेड़ महिला लाइन में घुस आई।
GU: એક આધેડ સ્ત્રી લાઇનમાં ઘૂસી ગઈ.

a割り当てwariateassignment (a noun, not a verb form fitting here)

Q7

その選手は事件を起こして、引退に（　）。Sono senshu wa jiken o okoshite, intai ni (___).

Full sentence: その選手は事件を起こして、引退に追い込まれた。Sono senshu wa jiken o okoshite, intai ni oikomareta.
EN: That athlete caused an incident and was driven into retirement.
HI: उस खिलाड़ी ने एक विवाद खड़ा किया और उसे संन्यास लेने पर मजबूर कर दिया गया।
GU: તે ખેલાડીએ વિવાદ સર્જ્યો અને તેને નિવૃત્તિ લેવા મજબૂર કરવામાં આવ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1受け止められたuketomeraretawas accepted (wrong verb)
2打ち切られたuchikiraretawas discontinued (wrong subject — careers aren't "discontinued" this way)
3追い込まれたoikomaretawas driven/forced into
4取り調べられたtorishiraberaretawas interrogated (wrong verb — about being questioned, not being forced into retirement)

Why: 引退に追い込まれる ("be driven/forced into retirement") is the fixed pattern for someone being pressured out of their career by scandal.

Q8

ゴマ油がこの料理の味を（　）いる。Gomaabura ga kono ryouri no aji o (___) iru.

Full sentence: ゴマ油がこの料理の味を引き立てている。Gomaabura ga kono ryouri no aji o hikitatete iru.
EN: Sesame oil brings out the flavour of this dish.
HI: तिल का तेल इस व्यंजन के स्वाद को उभारता है।
GU: તલનું તેલ આ વાનગીના સ્વાદને ઉભારે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1引き上げてhikiageteraising (wrong verb — for prices/rates, not flavour)
2引きもどしてhikimodoshitepulling back (wrong verb)
3引き起こしてhikiokoshitecausing (wrong verb — for problems, not flavour)
4引き立ててhikitatetebringing out/enhancing

Why: 味を引き立てる is the fixed phrase for an ingredient enhancing a dish's flavour.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Many of today's compounds share a base (取り〜, 引き〜, 申し〜) but land on very different meanings — exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q2 and Q3 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
取り立てるtoritateru To forcibly collect (money) About extracting money someone owes — tax, debt. 税金を取り立てる, 借金を取り立てる.
引き立てるhikitateru To enhance/champion About making something/someone look better — a flavour, a junior colleague's reputation. 味を引き立てる, 後輩を引き立てる.
取り調べるtorishiraberu To interrogate Questioning one specific suspect in detail. 容疑者を取り調べる.
取り締まるtorishimaru To crack down on/police Enforcing a rule broadly against violations in general, not questioning one person. 交通違反を取り締まる, 酔っ払い運転を取り締まる.
申し入れるmoushiireru To formally propose/protest The speaker is asserting something toward the other party — often a demand or objection. 抗議を申し入れる — lodging a protest.
申し出るmoushideru To (voluntarily) offer The speaker is offering something FOR the other party's benefit. 援助を申し出る — offering to help.
Same verb, unrelated senses: 打ち上げる (uchiageru) literally means "to launch upward" — ロケットを打ち上げる ("launch a rocket"), 花火を打ち上げる ("set off fireworks"). But its noun form 打ち上げ has drifted into ordinary workplace slang for a celebratory party held after finishing a big job — 「打ち上げをしよう！」("Let's have a wrap party!") has nothing literally flying into the sky.
