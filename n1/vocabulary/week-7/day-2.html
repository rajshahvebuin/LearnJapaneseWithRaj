JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えよう① (Matomete oboeyou ①)

2日目 — カタカナ② (Katakana ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.110–111 (PDF pages 100–101). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
カタカナ語 (katakana loanwords), part 2 — mostly な-adjectives borrowed from English (ラフな, タイトな, シャープな, デリケートな…) plus a run of everyday katakana nouns (トラウマ, プレッシャー, ギャップ, バブル, ストーカー, ニート…), several of which have drifted from their source word's original English meaning.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.113 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Several headwords (ラフ, ソフト, ブレイク) carry two unrelated senses depending on collocation — both are listed as separate rows below with the same headword.
⇔ = antonym
→ = a related word/expression
Warm-up

（　）に入るのは？(___) ni hairu no wa?
これは、（　）な素材でできているので、扱いに注意してください。Kore wa, (___) na sozai de dekite iru node, atsukai ni chuui shite kudasai.
("What goes in the blank? — 'This is made of [___] material, so please handle it with care.' Options shown: タイト, ブランド, デリケート.")

デリケート (derikeeto) — "delicate" — a delicate material needs careful handling

Q1

彼女は顔と声にずいぶん（a.シャープ b.ギャップ）があるね。Kanojo wa kao to koe ni zuibun (a. shaapu b. gyappu) ga aru ne.

Full sentence: 彼女は顔と声にずいぶんギャップがあるね。Kanojo wa kao to koe ni zuibun gyappu ga aru ne.
EN: There's quite a gap between her face and her voice, isn't there.
HI: उसके चेहरे और आवाज़ में काफ़ी फ़र्क़ है, है ना।
GU: તેના ચહેરા અને અવાજ વચ્ચે ઘણો ફરક છે, ખરું ને.
#JapaneseEnglish
aシャープshaapusharp/clear (wrong meaning here)
bギャップgyappua gap, a mismatch

Why: ギャップ describes a mismatch between two impressions (here: face vs. voice); シャープ describes clarity/sharpness, unrelated to contrast.

Q2

（a.バブルがはじけて b.ブレイクして）、経済は落ち込んだ。(a. Baburu ga hajikete b. Bureiku shite), keizai wa ochikonda.

Full sentence: バブルがはじけて、経済は落ち込んだ。Baburu ga hajikete, keizai wa ochikonda.
EN: The bubble burst, and the economy declined.
HI: बुलबुला फूटा और अर्थव्यवस्था गिर गई।
GU: પરપોટો ફૂટ્યો અને અર્થતંત્ર કથળ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
aバブルがはじけてbaburu ga hajiketethe bubble burst
bブレイクしてbureiku shitehad a hit (wrong meaning — describes success, not a crash)

Why: バブルがはじける is the fixed idiom for an economic bubble collapsing; ブレイク describes a breakthrough hit, the opposite of decline.

Q3

（a.タイトな b.ラフな）格好で行ってもいいでしょうか。(a. Taito na b. Rafu na) kakkou de itte mo ii deshou ka.

Full sentence: ラフな格好で行ってもいいでしょうか。Rafu na kakkou de itte mo ii deshou ka.
EN: Would it be okay to go in casual clothes?
HI: क्या मैं कैज़ुअल कपड़ों में जा सकता/सकती हूँ?
GU: શું હું કેઝ્યુઅલ કપડાંમાં જઈ શકું?
#JapaneseEnglish
aタイトなtaito natight (wrong meaning here)
bラフなrafu nacasual

Why: ラフな格好 is the fixed phrase for casual attire; タイト describes fit/tightness, not formality level.

Q4

優勝のかかった最後の試合だから、かなりの（a.トラウマ b.プレッシャー）だ。Yuushou no kakatta saigo no shiai da kara, kanari no (a. torauma b. puresshaa) da.

Full sentence: 優勝のかかった最後の試合だから、かなりのプレッシャーだ。Yuushou no kakatta saigo no shiai da kara, kanari no puresshaa da.
EN: Since it's the final match with the championship on the line, it's quite a lot of pressure.
HI: चूँकि यह चैंपियनशिप तय करने वाला आख़िरी मुक़ाबला है, इसका दबाव काफ़ी ज़्यादा है।
GU: આ ચેમ્પિયનશિપ નક્કી કરતી છેલ્લી મેચ છે, એટલે તેનું દબાણ ઘણું છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
aトラウマtoraumatrauma (wrong meaning — describes a past wound, not present stress)
bプレッシャーpuresshaapressure

Why: プレッシャーがかかる describes the stress of a high-stakes situation happening now; トラウマ describes lasting psychological damage from a past event.

Q5

（a.セキュリティー b.アフターサービス）対策は十分にしてあります。(a. Sekyuritii b. Afutaa-saabisu) taisaku wa juubun ni shite arimasu.

Full sentence: セキュリティー対策は十分にしてあります。Sekyuritii taisaku wa juubun ni shite arimasu.
EN: Security measures are thoroughly in place.
HI: सुरक्षा के इंतज़ाम पूरी तरह से किए गए हैं।
GU: સુરક્ષાની વ્યવસ્થા સંપૂર્ણ રીતે કરવામાં આવી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
aセキュリティーsekyuritiisecurity
bアフターサービスafutaa-saabisuafter-sales service (wrong meaning here)

Why: セキュリティー対策 ("security measures") is the fixed collocation; アフターサービス is about post-purchase support, unrelated to protection/safety.

Q6

ふかふかで（a.アットホーム b.ソフト）な肌触りの毛布を買った。Fukafuka de (a. atto hoomu b. sofuto) na hadazawari no moufu o katta.

Full sentence: ふかふかでソフトな肌触りの毛布を買った。Fukafuka de sofuto na hadazawari no moufu o katta.
EN: I bought a fluffy, soft-textured blanket.
HI: मैंने एक मुलायम/नर्म बनावट वाला रोएँदार कंबल ख़रीदा।
GU: મેં એક નરમ સ્પર્શવાળું રુવાંટીદાર ધાબળો ખરીદ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
aアットホームatto hoomucozy/homey (wrong sense — describes atmosphere, not texture)
bソフトsofutosoft (texture)

Why: ソフトな肌触り describes a physically soft texture; アットホーム describes the feel of a place/atmosphere, not fabric.

Q7

新型の車両は、より（　）なデザインになった。Shingata no sharyou wa, yori (___) na dezain ni natta.

Full sentence: 新型の車両は、よりシャープなデザインになった。Shingata no sharyou wa, yori shaapu na dezain ni natta.
EN: The new model has a sharper design.
HI: नए मॉडल की डिज़ाइन ज़्यादा तीखी/शार्प हो गई है।
GU: નવા મોડેલની ડિઝાઇન વધુ શાર્પ બની છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1シャープshaapusharp, crisp
2エリートeriitoelite (describes people, not designs)
3タイトtaitotight (wrong sense for "design")
4ラフrafurough/casual (opposite feel)

Why: シャープなデザイン ("a sharp/crisp design") is the natural collocation for a sleek, refined product look.

Q8

（　）があまりにもきつかったので、営業の仕事をやめました。(___) ga amari ni mo kitsukatta node, eigyou no shigoto o yamemashita.

Full sentence: ノルマがあまりにもきつかったので、営業の仕事をやめました。Noruma ga amari ni mo kitsukatta node, eigyou no shigoto o yamemashita.
EN: The quota was too tough, so I quit my sales job.
HI: कोटा बहुत सख़्त था, इसलिए मैंने सेल्स की नौकरी छोड़ दी।
GU: ક્વોટા બહુ કડક હતો, એટલે મેં સેલ્સની નોકરી છોડી દીધી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1オファーofaaan offer (wrong meaning)
2ノルマnorumaa work quota
3ストーカーsutookaaa stalker (wrong meaning)
4トラウマtoraumatrauma (wrong meaning)

Why: ノルマがきつい ("the quota is tough") is the natural fit for a sales job someone quit; the others don't fit "quitting because of a demand placed on you at work."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several of today's borrowed adjectives carry two unrelated senses depending on what they describe — exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q2, Q3, and Q6 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
ラフ（服装）rafu (fukusou) Casual Describes clothing/manner that's relaxed and informal. ラフな格好, ラフな服装 — casual dress code.
ラフ（図面）rafu (zumen) Rough, unfinished Same word, describes a draft/sketch that isn't polished yet — nothing to do with clothing. ラフな図面, ラフなアイデア — an early, unpolished version of something.
ソフト（話し方）sofuto (hanashikata) Gentle, mild Describes manner/texture/tone. ソフトな話し方, ソフトな肌触り — gentleness of manner or touch.
ソフト（＝ソフトウェア）sofuto (=sofutowea) Software Same word, unrelated technical shortening of "software" — no connotation of gentleness at all. パソコンのソフト — computer programs.
ブレイク（コーヒー）bureiku (koohii) A short rest/pause From "break" in the sense of a pause in activity. コーヒーブレイク — a coffee break.
ブレイク（する）bureiku (suru) To become a big hit Same word, unrelated to resting — describes a sudden surge in popularity (a "breakthrough"). その曲は去年大ブレイクした — a song/artist suddenly become famous.
False friend: テンション (tenshon) does NOT mean English "tension" (stress, strain, awkwardness). In Japanese it means one's excitement/energy level — テンションが上がる means "to get pumped up/excited," and テンションが低い means "to be in a low-energy mood," not "to be tense."
