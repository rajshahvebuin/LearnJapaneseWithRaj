Q1

そのバンドはデビュー10年目で（　）した。Sono bando wa debyuu juu-nen-me de (___) shita.

Full sentence: そのバンドはデビュー10年目でブレイクした。Sono bando wa debyuu juu-nen-me de bureiku shita.
EN: That band had a monster hit in their 10th year since debut.
HI: उस बैंड को अपने डेब्यू के 10वें साल में ज़बरदस्त हिट मिली।
GU: તે બેન્ડને પોતાના ડેબ્યુના 10મા વર્ષે જબરદસ્ત હિટ મળી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1トラウマtoraumatrauma (wrong meaning)
2ブランドburandoa brand (wrong meaning)
3リストラrisutoralayoffs (wrong meaning — bands don't "restructure" this way)
4ブレイクbureikua breakthrough hit

Why: Day 2's その曲は去年大ブレイクした shows ブレイク's "become a sudden hit" sense, exactly matching a band's breakout after years of work.

Q2

Full sentence: 私は、ふだんお酒を飲むとテンションがあがるが、今日はまったく気分がよくならない。...fudan osake o nomu to tenshon ga agaru ga...
EN: Normally my mood/energy lifts when I drink, but today I don't feel good at all.
HI: आमतौर पर शराब पीने से मेरा उत्साह बढ़ जाता है, पर आज बिल्कुल अच्छा महसूस नहीं हो रहा।
GU: સામાન્ય રીતે દારૂ પીવાથી મારો ઉત્સાહ વધે છે, પણ આજે બિલકુલ સારું નથી લાગતું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1テンションtenshonexcitement/energy level
2プレッシャーpuresshaapressure (wrong meaning)
3ギャップgyappua gap (wrong meaning)
4グレードgureedograde/quality (wrong meaning)

Why: Day 2's テンションが上がる is exactly "one's excitement level rises" — the false-friend callout on that page warns it doesn't mean English "tension."

Q3

Q21

持ち込むmochikomu

#JapaneseEnglish
1それは日本に持ち込むことはできないんじゃないの。Sore wa Nihon ni mochikomu koto wa dekinai n ja nai no.Isn't it true that you can't bring that into Japan?
2パソコンに音符を持ち込んで作曲をした。Pasokon ni onpu o mochikonde sakkyoku o shita."I brought musical notes into the computer and composed" — wrong verb; inputting data needs 打ち込んで (Day 5), not 持ち込んで
3苦い薬を無理やり口に持ち込んだ。Nigai kusuri o muriyari kuchi ni mochikonda."Forcibly brought bitter medicine into my mouth" — wrong verb; a small, forceful action into the mouth needs 放り込んだ (Day 5), not 持ち込んだ
4バナナやミカンをバッグに持ち込んで、旅に出かけた。Banana ya mikan o baggu ni mochikonde, tabi ni dekaketa."Brought bananas and mikans into my bag and went on a trip" — odd; simply packing fruit in one's own bag just needs 入れて, since 持ち込む implies carrying something into a separate, often restricted, place

Why: 持ち込む describes bringing something from outside into a bounded place — a country, a venue, a hotel room; option 1's customs/import context (日本に持ち込む) is the textbook use. Options 2, 3, and 4 each substitute 持ち込む for a verb that actually fits better: 打ち込む, 放り込む, and simple 入れる.

Q22

トラウマtorauma

#JapaneseEnglish
1彼は上司のトラウマが原因で出世できなかった。Kare wa joushi no torauma ga gen'in de shusse dekinakatta."He couldn't get promoted because of his boss's trauma" — nonsensical; someone else's trauma doesn't explain your own career stagnation
2すぐに落ち込むのは、トラウマになるからだ。Sugu ni ochikomu no wa, torauma ni naru kara da."Getting depressed easily is because it becomes trauma" — backwards causality; trauma CAUSES lasting distress, it isn't caused BY easily feeling down
3私は、震災で被害を受けたことがトラウマになっている。Watashi wa, shinsai de higai o uketa koto ga torauma ni natte iru.Having suffered damage from the earthquake has become a trauma for me.
4小さいころのトラウマがいい状況になってきました。Chiisai koro no torauma ga ii joukyou ni natte kimashita."The trauma of my childhood has come into a good situation" — nonsensical; trauma doesn't "become good"

Why: Day 2's 子どもの頃の体験がトラウマになる is the exact pattern for a painful past event leaving lasting psychological damage; 1, 2, and 4 each misuse the causality or imply trauma can improve.

Q23

#JapaneseEnglish

2彼は体が不自由であるというハンデを乗り越えて、国立大学に合格した。Kare wa karada ga fujiyuu de aru to iu hande o norikoete, kokuritsu daigaku ni goukaku shita.He overcame the handicap of having a physical disability and got into a national university.
3プロの彼にはハンデをあげたほうがいいよ。Puro no kare ni wa hande o ageta hou ga ii yo."It's better to give a handicap to him since he's a pro" — backwards; a pro should GIVE a handicap to a weaker opponent, not receive one
4彼女の考え方はハンデがあるので、あまり参考にならない。Kanojo no kangaekata wa hande ga aru node, amari sankou ni naranai."Her way of thinking has a handicap" — odd collocation; a way of thinking doesn't "have" a handicap

Why: Day 1's ハンデを克服する is the natural collocation for overcoming a genuine disadvantage; 1, 3, and 4 each misuse ハンデ in contexts it doesn't fit, or reverse who should give/receive it.

Q24

取り戻すtorimodosu

#JapaneseEnglish
1住民たちは、災害を取り戻して、立ち直った。Juumin-tachi wa, saigai o torimodoshite, tachinaotta."The residents recovered the disaster and got back on their feet" — nonsensical; you cannot "regain" a disaster itself
2父が意識を取り戻してまた話ができることを願っています。Chichi ga ishiki o torimodoshite mata hanashi ga dekiru koto o negatte imasu.I hope my father regains consciousness and can talk again.
3事件の内容をじっくり取り戻してみた。Jiken no naiyou o jikkuri torimodoshite mita."I carefully retrieved the content of the incident" — odd verb choice; reviewing details needs 調べて/思い出して, not 取り戻して
4病気の症状がいくらか取り戻してきた。Byouki no shoujou ga ikuraka torimodoshite kita."The illness's symptoms have recovered somewhat" — backwards; a person's HEALTH recovers, symptoms themselves don't "get regained"

Why: Day 4's 遅れを取り戻す shows 取り戻す's "regain/recover" sense; 意識を取り戻す ("regain consciousness") is a standard fixed idiom, while 1, 3, and 4 each apply the verb to something that can't logically be "regained" this way.

Q25

ギャップgyappu

#JapaneseEnglish
1最近の不況でついにギャップができてしまった。Saikin no fukyou de tsui ni gyappu ga dekite shimatta."The recent recession finally created a gap" — vague/unnatural; a recession creates 格差 (disparity), not typically described as ギャップ forming
2年をとったせいか、最近の若者とのギャップを感じる。Toshi o totta sei ka, saikin no wakamono to no gyappu o kanjiru.Perhaps because I've gotten older, I feel a gap with today's youth.
3天候のギャップを気にしないで、日夜練習に励んだ。Tenkou no gyappu o ki ni shinaide, nichiya renshuu ni hagenda."Not minding the gap in weather" — nonsensical; weather doesn't have a "gap," it has 変化 (changes)
4あなたと私のギャップの違いに驚いてしまった。Anata to watashi no gyappu no chigai ni odoroite shimatta."Surprised by the difference of the gap between you and me" — redundant; "the gap's difference" doubles up on the same meaning

Why: Day 2's 世代間のギャップ extends naturally to feeling a gap with younger generations due to aging; 1, 3, and 4 each force ギャップ into a collocation it doesn't naturally take, or double up redundantly.
