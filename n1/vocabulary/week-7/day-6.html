Warm-up

（　）に入るのは？(___) ni hairu no wa?
明日の仕事に（　）ので、夜更かしはやめよう。Ashita no shigoto ni (___) node, yofukashi wa yameyou.
("What goes in the blank? — 'Since it [___] tomorrow's work, let's not stay up late.' Signs shown: たどり着く, 差し支える, 結びつく.")

差し支える (sashitsukaeru) — "affects/interferes with" — staying up late would interfere with the next day's work

Q1

彼は、絶対やってないと（a.言い込んだ b.言い張った）。Kare wa, zettai yattenai to (a. iikonda b. iihatta).

Full sentence: 彼は、絶対やってないと言い張った。Kare wa, zettai yattenai to iihatta.
EN: He insisted that he absolutely didn't do it.
HI: उसने ज़िद पकड़ ली कि उसने बिल्कुल नहीं किया।
GU: તેણે જીદ પકડી કે તેણે બિલકુલ કર્યું નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a言い込んだiikondanot a real compound verb
b言い張ったiihattainsisted stubbornly

Why: 言い張る is the fixed verb for stubbornly maintaining a claim despite doubt from others.

Q2

詐欺にあって、店を（a.乗っ取られた b.差し出された）。Sagi ni atte, mise o (a. nottorareta b. sashidasareta).

Full sentence: 詐欺にあって、店を乗っ取られた。Sagi ni atte, mise o nottorareta.
EN: I was defrauded and had my shop taken over.
HI: धोखाधड़ी का शिकार होकर मेरी दुकान छीन ली गई।
GU: છેતરપિંડીનો ભોગ બનીને મારી દુકાન પડાવી લેવાઈ.
#JapaneseEnglish
a乗っ取られたnottoraretawas taken over
b差し出されたsashidasaretawas handed over (wrong voice/nuance — implies the owner voluntarily gave it up)

Why: 乗っ取られる (passive of 乗っ取る) fits being the victim of a hostile takeover through fraud; 差し出す implies willingly handing something over, which contradicts "被害にあって" (being a fraud victim).

Q3

イルカが2匹、仲良く（a.寄せ集めて b.寄り添って）泳いでいる。Iruka ga nihiki, nakayoku (a. yoseatsumete b. yorisotte) oyoide iru.

Full sentence: イルカが2匹、仲良く寄り添って泳いでいる。Iruka ga nihiki, nakayoku yorisotte oyoide iru.
EN: Two dolphins are swimming closely side by side.
HI: दो डॉल्फ़िन आपस में सटकर तैर रही हैं।
GU: બે ડોલ્ફિન એકબીજાને અડીને તરી રહી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a寄せ集めてyoseatsumetegathering together (wrong verb — for collecting objects, not two animals swimming)
b寄り添ってyorisottestaying close together

Why: 寄り添う describes two living beings staying affectionately close together; 寄せ集める is for gathering scattered objects into one place.

Q4

開店したら、大勢のお客さんが（a.押し込んで b.押し寄せて）きた。Kaiten shitara, oozei no okyakusan ga (a. oshikonde b. oshiyosete) kita.

Full sentence: 開店したら、大勢のお客さんが押し寄せてきた。Kaiten shitara, oozei no okyakusan ga oshiyosete kita.
EN: As soon as the shop opened, a huge crowd of customers surged in.
HI: दुकान खुलते ही बड़ी संख्या में ग्राहक उमड़ पड़े।
GU: દુકાન ખૂલતાં જ મોટી સંખ્યામાં ગ્રાહકો ધસી આવ્યા.
#JapaneseEnglish
a押し込んでoshikondebeing pushed/crammed in (wrong voice — implies someone forcing them in, not customers arriving on their own)
b押し寄せてoshiyosetesurging/flocking in

Why: 押し寄せる describes a crowd surging toward/into a place on its own momentum (like waves); 押し込む implies someone actively cramming people/things into a space.

Q5

彼は医師の忠告を（a.押し切って b.言い張って）試合に出てしまった。Kare wa ishi no chuukoku o (a. oshikitte b. iihatte) shiai ni dete shimatta.

Full sentence: 彼は医師の忠告を押し切って試合に出てしまった。Kare wa ishi no chuukoku o oshikitte shiai ni dete shimatta.
EN: He went ahead and played in the match, disregarding the doctor's advice.
HI: उसने डॉक्टर की सलाह को नज़रअंदाज़ करते हुए मुक़ाबले में हिस्सा ले ही लिया।
GU: તેણે ડૉક્ટરની સલાહને અવગણીને મેચમાં ભાગ લઈ જ લીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a押し切ってoshikittepushing through/disregarding (advice)
b言い張ってiihatteinsisting verbally (wrong verb — needs an object you act against, not just assert)

Why: ～を押し切って ("pushing through ~ / overriding ~") is the fixed pattern for acting against someone else's advice or objection; 言い張る describes making a verbal claim, not overriding another's advice with action. See confusion notes below.

Q6

客がいなければ商売は（a.待ち望めない b.成り立たない）。Kyaku ga inakereba shoubai wa (a. machinozomenai b. naritatanai).

Full sentence: 客がいなければ商売は成り立たない。Kyaku ga inakereba shoubai wa naritatanai.
EN: Without customers, a business can't be sustained.
HI: ग्राहक न हों तो व्यवसाय टिक नहीं सकता।
GU: ગ્રાહકો ન હોય તો ધંધો ટકી શકતો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
a待ち望めないmachinozomenaicannot be longed for (wrong verb — grammatically odd here)
b成り立たないnaritatanaicannot be sustained/is unviable

Why: 商売が成り立つ／成り立たない is the fixed phrase for whether a business is financially viable; 待ち望む is about longing for something, unrelated to viability.

Q7

ついに問題集を丸々一冊（　）。Tsui ni mondaishuu o marumaru issatsu (___).

Full sentence: ついに問題集を丸々一冊やりとげた。Tsui ni mondaishuu o marumaru issatsu yaritogeta.
EN: I finally finished an entire practice workbook cover to cover.
HI: आख़िरकार पूरी अभ्यास पुस्तिका खत्म कर ही दी।
GU: આખરે આખી અભ્યાસ પુસ્તિકા પૂરી કરી જ દીધી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1寄せ集めたyoseatsumetagathered together (wrong verb — for objects, not finishing a book)
2たどり着いたtadoritsuitareached/arrived at (wrong verb — for a destination, not completing work)
3やりとげたyaritogetacompleted, saw through to the end
4盛り上がったmoriagattagot lively/excited (wrong verb)

Why: やり遂げる is the fixed verb for completing a demanding task all the way through, exactly fitting "finishing an entire workbook."

Q8

気持ちを（　）、さあ、また一から出直した。Kimochi o (___), saa, mata ichi kara denaoshita.

Full sentence: 気持ちを切り替えて、さあ、また一から出直した。Kimochi o kirikaete, saa, mata ichi kara denaoshita.
EN: Switching my mindset, I started all over again from scratch.
HI: मन बदलकर, अब फिर से शुरुआत की।
GU: મન બદલીને, ફરી શરૂઆતથી કર્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1使いこなしてtsukaikonashitemastering (the use of something — wrong object, doesn't fit 気持ち)
2切り替えてkirikaeteswitching (one's mindset)
3消し去ってkeshisatteerasing completely (too strong — you switch your mood, not erase it entirely)
4折り返してorikaeshiteturning back/calling back (wrong verb, unrelated to mindset)

Why: 気持ちを切り替える ("switch one's mindset/mood") is the fixed idiom for resetting one's emotional state before trying again.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two "pushing through opposition" verbs and two 差し〜 compounds are exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q5 and today's list test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
押し切るoshikiru To push through, override (an objection) Describes an ACTION taken despite someone else's objection/advice — takes を+objection as its object. 反対を押し切って結婚する, 忠告を押し切って試合に出る.
言い張るiiharu To insist stubbornly (verbally) Describes a verbal claim repeated stubbornly — takes と+claim, not an objection being overridden. 知らないと言い張る, やってないと言い張る.
差し出すsashidasu To hold out/hand over A physical/voluntary act of offering something to someone. 名刺を差し出す — extending a business card.
差し引くsashihiku To deduct/subtract Same 差し〜 base, unrelated meaning — a numerical/financial operation. 給料から税金を差し引く — deducting tax from pay.
One verb, three senses: 成り立つ (naritatsu) covers three distinct situations depending on its subject: 交渉が成り立つ (a negotiation successfully concludes), 経営が成り立たない (a business isn't financially sustainable), and この会は30人から成り立っている (a group is composed of 30 members). All three share the core idea of something "coming together into a working whole," but which English translation fits depends entirely on context.
