JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えよう① (Matomete oboeyou ①)

5日目 — 組み合わせの言葉② (Kumiawase no kotoba ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.116–117 (PDF pages 106–107). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
More compound verbs, this time mostly built on 〜込む ("into") and 〜出す ("out"): 取り込む, 溶け込む, 組み込む, 乗り込む, 踏み込む, 放り込む, 打ち込む, 買い込む, 引っ込む, 落ち込む, 持ち込む, 飲み込む (all "~込む"), plus 抜け出す, 投げ出す/放り出す, 逃げ出す ("~出す"), and a handful of other everyday combinations (突っ張る, 出っ張る, 振り返る, 折り返す, かき回す, 埋め立てる, かみ切る).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.119 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Today's warm-up highlights that 切る／張る／込む are the three most common second-halves for -る compound verbs in this chapter.
＝ = a near-equivalent phrase given by the book
≠ = a phrase that looks equivalent but is NOT (the book explicitly warns against it)
→ = the derived noun form of the compound verb
Warm-up

（　）に共通して入るのは？(___) ni kyoutsuu shite hairu no wa?
買い（　）・引っ（　）・持ち（　）Kai (___), hik (___), mochi (___)
("What goes in all three blanks? 'stock up on', 'withdraw/retreat', 'bring in' — signs shown: 切る, 張る, 込む.")

込む (komu) — 買い込む (stock up), 引っ込む (withdraw), 持ち込む (bring in) — all built on 〜込む ("into")

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, a/b choice, from p.117) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, 4-option, from p.117). Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.119.

練習Ⅰ — circle the correct option

Q1

首相は政権を途中で（a.抜け出した b.投げ出した）。Shushou wa seiken o tochuu de (a. nukedashita b. nagedashita).

Full sentence: 首相は政権を途中で投げ出した。Shushou wa seiken o tochuu de nagedashita.
EN: The prime minister abandoned power midway through his term.
HI: प्रधानमंत्री ने कार्यकाल बीच में ही छोड़ दिया।
GU: વડાપ્રધાને પોતાનો કાર્યકાળ અધવચ્ચે જ છોડી દીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a抜け出したnukedashitaslipped out (wrong nuance — implies sneaking away quietly, not abandoning a duty)
b投げ出したnagedashitaabandoned/gave up midway

Why: 投げ出す describes giving up a responsibility partway through; 抜け出す describes physically/quietly leaving a place, not relinquishing a duty.

Q2

（a.出っ張った b.突っ張った）釘で服が破けてしまった。(a. Deppatta b. Tsuppatta) kugi de fuku ga yaburete shimatta.

Full sentence: 出っ張った釘で服が破けてしまった。Deppatta kugi de fuku ga yaburete shimatta.
EN: My clothes got torn on a nail that was sticking out.
HI: बाहर निकली हुई कील से कपड़ा फट गया।
GU: બહાર નીકળેલી ખીલીથી કપડું ફાટી ગયું.
#JapaneseEnglish
a出っ張ったdeppattasticking out (a shape)
b突っ張ったtsuppattatight/tensed (wrong meaning — describes muscle tension, not a protruding object)

Why: 出っ張る describes something physically protruding/sticking out (like a nail); 突っ張る describes a muscle or surface becoming taut/stretched — see confusion notes below.

Q3

売り上げがどんどん（a.引っ込んで b.落ち込んで）いる。Uriage ga dondon (a. hikkonde b. ochikonde) iru.

Full sentence: 売り上げがどんどん落ち込んでいる。Uriage ga dondon ochikonde iru.
EN: Sales are steadily declining.
HI: बिक्री लगातार गिरती जा रही है।
GU: વેચાણ સતત ઘટી રહ્યું છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
a引っ込んでhikkondewithdrawing/retreating (wrong meaning — describes a person moving away, not numbers declining)
b落ち込んでochikondedeclining

Why: 落ち込む is the fixed verb for numbers/figures like sales or grades dropping; 引っ込む describes a person withdrawing to somewhere, unrelated to statistics.

Q4

名前を呼ばれて（a.折り返したら b.振り返ったら）、山田さんだった。Namae o yobarete (a. orikaeshitara b. furikaettara), Yamada-san datta.

Full sentence: 名前を呼ばれて振り返ったら、山田さんだった。Namae o yobarete furikaettara, Yamada-san datta.
EN: When I was called by name and turned around, it was Mr./Ms. Yamada.
HI: नाम पुकारे जाने पर मुड़कर देखा तो यामादा-सान थे।
GU: નામ બોલાવવાથી પાછળ ફરીને જોયું તો યામાડા-સાન હતા.
#JapaneseEnglish
a折り返したらorikaeshitaraturned back/called back (wrong verb for physically turning around)
b振り返ったらfurikaettaraturned around

Why: 振り返る is the verb for physically turning to look behind oneself when called; 折り返す is for turning back partway along a route, or calling someone back.

Q5

ガムを口に（a.持ち込んだ b.放り込んだ）。Gamu o kuchi ni (a. mochikonda b. hourikonda).

Full sentence: ガムを口に放り込んだ。Gamu o kuchi ni hourikonda.
EN: I tossed a piece of gum into my mouth.
HI: च्युइंगम मुँह में डाल दिया।
GU: ચ્યુઇંગમ મોઢામાં નાખી દીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a持ち込んだmochikondabrought in (wrong nuance — implies carrying something into a restricted place, too formal here)
b放り込んだhourikondatossed/popped in

Why: 放り込む fits the quick, casual motion of popping gum into one's mouth; 持ち込む implies deliberately carrying something into a place, an odd fit for a small casual action like this.

Q6

夜中にこっそり家を（a.抜け出して b.放り出して）、遊びに行った。Yonaka ni kossori ie o (a. nukedashite b. houridashite), asobi ni itta.

Full sentence: 夜中にこっそり家を抜け出して、遊びに行った。Yonaka ni kossori ie o nukedashite, asobi ni itta.
EN: I secretly slipped out of the house at midnight and went out to play.
HI: आधी रात को चुपके से घर से निकलकर घूमने चला गया।
GU: મધરાતે છાનેમાને ઘરેથી નીકળીને રખડવા ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglish
a抜け出してnukedashiteslipping out quietly
b放り出してhouridashiteabandoning (wrong meaning — you can't "abandon" a house this way)

Why: 抜け出す is exactly the verb for secretly slipping away from a place, matching こっそり ("stealthily"); 放り出す means to abandon a duty or throw something out, not to sneak out.

Q7

彼はギターの練習に（　）いる。Kare wa gitaa no renshuu ni (___) iru.

Full sentence: 彼はギターの練習に打ち込んでいる。Kare wa gitaa no renshuu ni uchikonde iru.
EN: He's devoting himself wholeheartedly to guitar practice.
HI: वह गिटार अभ्यास में पूरी लगन से जुटा है।
GU: તે ગિટાર પ્રેક્ટિસમાં પૂરા મનથી ડૂબેલો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1組み込んでkumikondeincorporating (wrong verb — for plans/budgets, not personal effort)
2取り込んでtorikondebringing in (wrong verb)
3溶け込んでtokekondeblending in (wrong verb — for fitting into a group, not devoting effort)
4打ち込んでuchikondedevoting oneself to

Why: ～に打ち込む is the fixed phrase for pouring one's heart and soul into an activity like guitar practice.

Q8

このあたりは、海を（　）作った土地だ。Kono atari wa, umi o (___) tsukutta tochi da.

Full sentence: このあたりは、海を埋め立てて作った土地だ。Kono atari wa, umi o umetatete tsukutta tochi da.
EN: This area is land created by reclaiming it from the sea.
HI: यह इलाक़ा समुद्र को पाटकर बनाई गई ज़मीन है।
GU: આ વિસ્તાર સમુદ્રને પૂરીને બનાવેલી જમીન છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1埋め立ててumetatetereclaiming (land)
2埋め入れてumeiretenot a real compound verb
3乗り込んでnorikondeboarding (wrong verb)
4乗り入れてnoriireteextending a rail line into (wrong verb)

Why: 海を埋め立てる is the fixed term for reclaiming land from the sea by filling it in.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Two look-alike pairs of "leaving" and "sticking out" verbs are exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q1 and Q2 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
抜け出すnukedasu To slip out quietly Physically/stealthily leaving a place — the focus is on the escape itself. 会社を抜け出す, 家を抜け出す.
投げ出す／放り出すnagedasu / houridasu To abandon/give up midway Focus is on relinquishing a responsibility or task before it's finished, not the physical act of leaving. 政権を投げ出す, 仕事を途中で放り出す.
出っ張るdepparu To stick out, protrude Describes a static shape jutting outward — a belly, a nail. 腹が出っ張る, 出っ張った釘.
突っ張るtsupparu To become taut/tight Describes a muscle, tendon, or surface becoming stretched and stiff — a sensation, not a shape. 筋肉が突っ張る, 肩が突っ張る.
Exam trap: The book explicitly flags that かき回す cannot always be swapped for its near-synonym かき混ぜる: 砂糖を入れてコーヒーをかき回す (stirring sugar into coffee) does equal かき混ぜる, but 会議がかき回された (a meeting thrown into chaos by someone's behavior) is a purely figurative "disrupted/stirred up" sense — you cannot say 会議がかき混ぜられた here.
