JLPT N1 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えよう① (Matomete oboeyou ①)

3日目 — カタカナ③ (Katakana ③)

Source
日本語総まとめ N1 語彙 — printed p.112–113 (PDF pages 102–103). Offset holds at printed page = PDF index + 10.
Theme
和製語 (wasei-go) — katakana words built in Japan out of foreign-word parts that don't exist in the original languages (オーダーメイド, ベッドタウン, Uターン, ペーパードライバー…), plus a set of verbified slang loanwords (ダブる, キレる, サボる) and the multi-purpose slang adjective ヤバい.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.115 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
"和製語" (wasei-go, "Japan-made words") means these look like English/foreign loanwords but were assembled in Japan and often aren't understood by native speakers of the source language — e.g. オーダーメイド ("order" + "made") isn't real English.
⇅ = antonym pair shown together (プラス／マイナス)
Warm-up

何と言う？Nanto iu?
大学の単位を落として、一年（　）しまった。Daigaku no tan'i o otoshite, ichinen (___) shimatta.
("What do you call it? — 'I failed a university course and ended up [___] a year.' Options shown: サボって, ダブって, キレて.")

ダブって (dabutte) — "repeating/doubling" — 一年ダブる (ichinen daburu) means to repeat a grade after failing a course

Q1

注意したら、彼は突然（a.キレた b.サボった）。Chuui shitara, kare wa totsuzen (a. kireta b. sabotta).

Full sentence: 注意したら、彼は突然キレた。Chuui shitara, kare wa totsuzen kireta.
EN: When I warned him, he suddenly snapped/lost his temper.
HI: जब मैंने उसे टोका, तो वह अचानक भड़क उठा।
GU: મેં તેને ટોક્યો ત્યારે તે અચાનક ભડકી ઊઠ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
aキレたkiretasnapped, lost his temper
bサボったsabottaskipped work/school (wrong meaning here)

Why: キレる describes a sudden emotional outburst — exactly what happens when someone reacts badly to being warned; サボる is about avoiding a duty, unrelated to anger.

Q2

ワープロのソフトを1.0から2.0に（a.グレードアップ b.バージョンアップ）した。Waapuro no sofuto o 1.0 kara 2.0 ni (a. gureedo appu b. baajon appu) shita.

Full sentence: ワープロのソフトを1.0から2.0にバージョンアップした。Waapuro no sofuto o 1.0 kara 2.0 ni baajon appu shita.
EN: I upgraded the word-processing software from version 1.0 to 2.0.
HI: मैंने वर्ड-प्रोसेसिंग सॉफ़्टवेयर को 1.0 से 2.0 में अपग्रेड किया।
GU: મેં વર્ડ-પ્રોસેસિંગ સોફ્ટવેરને 1.0 થી 2.0 માં અપગ્રેડ કર્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
aグレードアップgureedo appuupgrading to pricier items (wrong meaning for software versions)
bバージョンアップbaajon appuupgrading (software version)

Why: バージョンアップ specifically describes a software/version upgrade; グレードアップ is about moving up to a higher-grade/pricier item, not version numbers.

Q3

Full sentence: スリーサイズを計ってオーダーメイドのスーツを作った。Surii saizu o hakatte oodaa meido no suutsu o tsukutta.
EN: I had a custom-made suit made after measuring my three sizes.
HI: तीनों माप लेकर एक कस्टम-मेड सूट बनवाया।
GU: ત્રણેય માપ લઈને એક કસ્ટમ-મેડ સૂટ બનાવડાવ્યો.

aワンパターンwanpataanone-track/repetitive (wrong meaning — describes a personality trait, not tailoring)

Q4

私は不運な経験が多いせいか、物事を（a.プラス b.マイナス）に考えられなくなっています。Watashi wa fu'un na keiken ga ooi sei ka, monogoto o (a. purasu b. mainasu) ni kangaerarenaku natte imasu.

Full sentence: 私は不運な経験が多いせいか、物事をプラスに考えられなくなっています。Watashi wa fu'un na keiken ga ooi sei ka, monogoto o purasu ni kangaerarenaku natte imasu.
EN: Perhaps because I've had so many unlucky experiences, I've become unable to think of things positively.
HI: शायद इतने सारे बदक़िस्मत अनुभवों की वजह से, मैं चीज़ों को सकारात्मक ढंग से सोच नहीं पा रहा/रही।
GU: કદાચ ઘણા બધા કમનસીબ અનુભવોના કારણે, હું બાબતોને હકારાત્મક રીતે વિચારી શકતો/શકતી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
aプラスpurasupositively
bマイナスmainasunegatively (contradicts "unable to think ~ anymore")

Why: 考えられなくなっている ("have become unable to think ~") signals loss of a previous positive ability — so the blank names the ability lost: プラスに考える ("to think positively"), not マイナス.

Q5

雑誌に彼女が2歳年下の医師と来春（a.Uターン b.ゴールイン）するという記事が載っていた。Zasshi ni kanojo ga nisai toshishita no ishi to raishun (a. yuu taan b. gooru in) suru to iu kiji ga notte ita.

Full sentence: 雑誌に彼女が2歳年下の医師と来春ゴールインするという記事が載っていた。...raishun gooru in suru to iu kiji ga notte ita.
EN: A magazine ran an article saying she'll marry a doctor two years her junior next spring.
HI: पत्रिका में यह ख़बर छपी थी कि वह अगले वसंत में अपने से 2 साल छोटे डॉक्टर से शादी करेगी।
GU: મેગેઝિનમાં એવા સમાચાર છપાયા હતા કે તે આવતી વસંતમાં પોતાનાથી 2 વર્ષ નાના ડૉક્ટર સાથે લગ્ન કરશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
aUターンyuu taana U-turn/return to one's hometown (wrong meaning here)
bゴールインgooru inget married

Why: ゴールインする used with a romantic partner is the fixed figurative expression for getting married; Uターン is about geographic return, unrelated to marriage.

Q6

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Wasei-go words often smuggle in a second, unrelated sense of the same short word — and this day's "no variation" word overlaps with a Day 1 word that looks similar but isn't:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
ワンパターン(な)wanpataan (na) Having no variation (a trait) Describes a person/thing that inherently always does the same thing — a fixed character trait. ワンパターンな人間 — "a person with a one-track mind."
マンネリ（化する）manneri (-ka suru) Falling into a rut (a process) From Day 1 — describes something that USED to be fresh gradually losing its novelty through repetition. 人気番組もマンネリ化してくる — "even a popular show eventually gets stale."
ダブる（文字）daburu (moji) To overlap/look blurry Describes two images/things visually overlapping. 文字がダブって見える — "letters look doubled/blurry."
ダブる（留年）daburu (ryuunen) To repeat a school year Same word, unrelated meaning — failing a course and redoing the year. 単位を落として1年ダブる — "repeat a grade after failing a course."
ゴールイン（レース）gooru in (reesu) To cross the finish line Literal racing sense. 1着でゴールインする — "come in first place."
ゴールイン（結婚）gooru in (kekkon) To get married Same word, figurative sense — marriage treated as the "finish line" of a relationship. 来春ゴールインする — "will get married next spring."
Word history: サボる (saboru) is one of the oldest verbified loanwords in Japanese — it comes from French "sabotage" (via サボタージュ), imported in the early 20th century to describe labor strikes/slowdowns, and later shortened and turned into an ordinary -る verb meaning simply "to skip/skive off" school or work.
