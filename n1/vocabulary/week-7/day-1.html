Theme
カタカナ語 (katakana loanwords) — this first of three katakana days covers abbreviated/clipped loanwords (ハイテク, リストラ, ゼネコン…), katakana suffix words that combine with different nouns (マニア, ドリル, カンパ, ネック), and everyday wasei-eigo verbs/phrases (バトンタッチする, カンニングする).

Warm-up

（　）に入るのは？(___) ni hairu no wa?
父の（　）を使って就職した。Chichi no (___) o tsukatte shuushoku shita.
("What goes in the blank? — 'I got a job using my father's [___].' Options shown: ファザコン, ハンデ, コネ.")

Q1

彼が（a.カンニング b.マンネリ）したのは明白だ。Kare ga (a. kanningu b. manneri) shita no wa meihaku da.

Full sentence: 彼がカンニングしたのは明白だ。Kare ga kanningu shita no wa meihaku da.
EN: It's obvious that he cheated (on the test).
HI: यह साफ़ है कि उसने नक़ल की।
GU: એ સ્પષ્ટ છે કે તેણે ચોરી કરી.
#JapaneseEnglish
aカンニングkanningucheating (on a test)
bマンネリmanneria rut (unrelated to test-taking)

Why: カンニング is the fixed word for exam cheating; マンネリ describes something growing stale through repetition, not dishonest behavior.

Q2

会議でパソコンを使って（a.カンパ b.プレゼン）をした。Kaigi de pasokon o tsukatte (a. kanpa b. purezen) o shita.

Full sentence: 会議でパソコンを使ってプレゼンをした。Kaigi de pasokon o tsukatte purezen o shita.
EN: I gave a presentation using a computer at the meeting.
HI: मीटिंग में कंप्यूटर का इस्तेमाल करके प्रस्तुति दी।
GU: મીટિંગમાં કમ્પ્યુટરનો ઉપયોગ કરીને પ્રેઝન્ટેશન આપ્યું.
#JapaneseEnglish
aカンパkanpaa financial contribution (wrong meaning)
bプレゼンpurezena presentation

Why: プレゼン (presentation) is what one does with a computer in a meeting; カンパ is about collecting money, unrelated here.

Q3

これは（a.エコ b.バイオ）の技術を利用して作った害虫に強い野菜です。Kore wa (a. eko b. baio) no gijutsu o riyou shite tsukutta gaichuu ni tsuyoi yasai desu.

Full sentence: これはバイオの技術を利用して作った害虫に強い野菜です。Kore wa baio no gijutsu o riyou shite tsukutta gaichuu ni tsuyoi yasai desu.
EN: This is a pest-resistant vegetable made using bio-technology.
HI: यह जैव-तकनीक का उपयोग करके बनाई गई कीट-प्रतिरोधी सब्ज़ी है।
GU: આ બાયો-ટેકનોલોજીનો ઉપયોગ કરીને બનાવેલી જીવાત-પ્રતિરોધક શાકભાજી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
aエコekoeco-friendly (wrong field — about environment, not genetics)
bバイオbaiobio-technology

Why: Making a pest-resistant vegetable is a genetic/bio-engineering feat (バイオ), not simply an environmental one (エコ).

Q4

会社を（a.セクハラ b.リストラ）されたらどうしよう。Kaisha o (a. sekuhara b. risutora) saretara dou shiyou.

Full sentence: 会社をリストラされたらどうしよう。Kaisha o risutora saretara dou shiyou.
EN: What will I do if I get laid off from the company?
HI: अगर कंपनी से निकाल दिया गया तो क्या करूँगा?
GU: જો કંપનીમાંથી કાઢી મુકાય તો શું કરીશ?

aセクハラsekuharasexual harassment (wrong meaning)

Q5

彼はとても時間に（a.スムーズ b.ルーズ）だ。Kare wa totemo jikan ni (a. sumuuzu b. ruuzu) da.

Full sentence: 彼はとても時間にルーズだ。Kare wa totemo jikan ni ruuzu da.
EN: He's very unpunctual about time.
HI: वह समय के मामले में बहुत लापरवाह है।
GU: તે સમય બાબતે ખૂબ બેદરકાર છે.

Q6

エコカーには（a.ゼネコン b.ハイテク）技術がたくさん使われている。Ekokaa ni wa (a. zenekon b. haiteku) gijutsu ga takusan tsukawarete iru.

Full sentence: エコカーにはハイテク技術がたくさん使われている。Ekokaa ni wa haiteku gijutsu ga takusan tsukawarete iru.
EN: A lot of high-tech technology is used in eco-friendly cars.
HI: पर्यावरण-अनुकूल कारों में बहुत-सी उच्च-तकनीक का इस्तेमाल होता है।
GU: પર્યાવરણ-અનુકૂળ કારમાં ઘણી ઉચ્ચ-ટેકનોલોજીનો ઉપયોગ થાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
aゼネコンzenekona general contractor (wrong meaning — a company, not a technology)

Q7

いいマンションだが、交通の便の悪さが（　）になって、借り手がいない。Ii manshon da ga, koutsuu no ben no warusa ga (___) ni natte, karite ga inai.

Full sentence: いいマンションだが、交通の便の悪さがネックになって、借り手がいない。...koutsuu no ben no warusa ga nekku ni natte, karite ga inai.
EN: It's a nice apartment, but poor transport access is the sticking point, so there are no tenants.
HI: फ़्लैट अच्छा है, पर आने-जाने की असुविधा ही सबसे बड़ी रुकावट बनी हुई है, इसलिए कोई किराएदार नहीं मिल रहा।
GU: ફ્લેટ સારો છે, પણ અવરજવરની અગવડ જ સૌથી મોટો અવરોધ બની છે, એટલે કોઈ ભાડુઆત મળતું નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1タートルネックtaatoru nekkuturtleneck (wrong sense of ネック)
2シックshikkusophisticated (unrelated meaning)
3ネックnekkua bottleneck, sticking point
4ハンデhandea handicap (close in feel, but not the fixed word for "the one problem holding something back")

Why: ～がネックになる ("~ becomes the bottleneck") is the fixed idiom for the one factor blocking otherwise-good conditions.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Katakana loanwords in this chapter carry their own trap: several short words hide two unrelated senses, or drift from what their English/foreign source word actually means:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
ハンデ（克服する）hande (kokufuku suru) A handicap/disadvantage to overcome The person carrying it is at a disadvantage. 体が不自由であるというハンデを乗り越えて… ("overcoming the handicap of having a disability")
ハンデ（もらう）hande (morau) A head-start/advantage given Same word, opposite framing — a benefit given to the weaker side in a game. Casual games/sports where a weaker player is given an edge.
ネック（服）nekku (fuku) A shirt/sweater collar style Literal clothing sense — ハイネック, タートルネック. Describing a garment's neckline.
ネック（問題）nekku (mondai) A bottleneck, the sticking point Same katakana word, completely unrelated meaning — from "bottleneck", not the body part. 予算がネックになっている — naming the one obstacle blocking progress.
マニア（〜マニア）mania An enthusiast, a devoted fan As a Japanese suffix it's much milder than English "mania" — just means "really into ~", not obsessive/pathological. 切手マニア, 鉄道マニア — describing a hobbyist, not implying anything unhealthy.
False friend: カンニング (kanningu) looks like it should mean "cunning/clever," but in Japanese it specifically means cheating on a test — it comes from English "cunning" but the meaning narrowed to exam fraud along the way. Don't use it to compliment someone's cleverness.
