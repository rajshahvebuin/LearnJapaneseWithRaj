Theme

Answer key

Notes
30 main verb entries in one continuous list (no body-part or particle sub-groups this time, unlike Days 3–4). Several entries carry a related transitive/intransitive partner verb marked with → (e.g. 緩める→緩む, 生やす→生える, 逃す→逃れる) — this day leans heavily on that pattern, which the exercises test directly (Q1, Q8).
※ = 「が」can be swapped for 「の」 (e.g. 愛想が いい ＝ 愛想の いい), more common in writing
→ = related word (usually the verb form)
⇔ = antonym
・ = a common example phrase using the headword, not a separate word
Warm-up

＿＿＿に入るのは？___ ni hairu no wa?
これは、特別な工夫を＿＿＿作りました。Kore wa, tokubetsu na kufuu o ___ tsukurimashita.
("What goes in the blank? 'This was made by ___ special ingenuity.'" — candidates: こらして／そらして／さらして)

工夫をこらして (kufuu o korashite) — "by really applying/concentrating one's ingenuity"

Q1

風呂場にかびが（　）。Furoba ni kabi ga (___).

Full sentence: 風呂場にかびが生えた。Furoba ni kabi ga haeta.
EN: Mold grew in the bathroom.
HI: बाथरूम में फफूंद उग आई।
GU: બાથરૂમમાં ફૂગ ઊગી ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生けたiketaarranged (flowers) — wrong verb, wrong subject(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b生えたhaetagrew (of its own accord)उग आईઊગી ગઈ

Why: 生える is the intransitive verb for something growing on its own (mold, hair, plants) with が; 生ける means to deliberately arrange flowers, an unrelated action a person does to flowers, not something mold does.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The warm-up's こらす／そらす／さらす are near-rhyming verbs that sound alike but take completely different objects — exactly what Q3 and the warm-up test. 交える／交わす, sharing the kanji 交, form a second common trap:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
凝らすkorasu To concentrate/apply intensively Almost fixed to 工夫 (ingenuity/creativity) — "to really put your mind into devising something." 工夫を凝らす — devising something cleverly.
そらすsorasu To avert, deflect, turn away Takes 目 (eyes) or 話 (a topic) — turning something away from its current direction/focus. 目をそらす (avert your gaze), 話をそらす (change the subject).
さらすsarasu To expose Takes 日光 (sunlight) or 水 (water) — leaving something open/exposed to an external element. 日光にさらす (expose to sunlight), 水にさらす (soak in water).
交えるmajieru To mix in, include (a third party) One party brings another person/element into something already happening — an addition to a group. 先生を交えて話し合う — discuss with the teacher included.
交わすkawasu To exchange (mutually) Two parties mutually give/receive the same thing — greetings, promises, words — back and forth. あいさつ／約束を交わす — exchange greetings/a promise.
Exam trap: This day is full of transitive/intransitive verb pairs marked with → in the word list (緩める⇔緩む, 生やす⇔生える, 逃す⇔逃れる) — the transitive member takes を and a deliberate agent ("someone loosens/grows/lets-slip something"), while the intransitive member takes が and just describes the state changing on its own ("something becomes loose/grows/gets away"). Q1 above (かびが生えた, not 生けた) tests exactly this: mold growing is something that happens on its own, so it must take the intransitive verb, not a transitive one that describes a deliberate human action.
