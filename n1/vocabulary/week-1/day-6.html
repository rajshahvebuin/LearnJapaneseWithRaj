Warm-up

＿＿＿に入るのは？___ ni hairu no wa?
痛みを＿＿＿ために、薬を飲んだ。Itami o ___ tame ni, kusuri o nonda.
("What goes in the blank? 'I took medicine in order to ___ the pain.'" — candidates: 遠ざける／弱まる／和らげる. Hint: 自動詞・他動詞に注意 — watch for transitive vs. intransitive verbs.)

痛みを和らげる (itami o yawarageru) — "to ease/relieve the pain" (弱まる is intransitive and can't take を; 遠ざける is transitive but means "keep something at a distance," the wrong meaning for pain)

Q1

母が病院に通うのに（　）いけない。Haha ga byouin ni kayou no ni (___) ikenai.

Full sentence: 母が病院に通うのに付き添わなければいけない。Haha ga byouin ni kayou no ni tsukisowanakereba ikenai.
EN: I have to accompany my mother when she goes to the hospital.
HI: माँ को अस्पताल जाते समय मुझे उनके साथ जाना पड़ता है।
GU: માતાને દવાખાને જતી વખતે મારે તેમની સાથે જવું પડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a面会しなければmenkai shinakerebamust visit (wrong verb — for a one-off visit, not accompanying)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's own warm-up hint says it best — 自動詞・他動詞に注意 (watch the transitive/intransitive split). Four symptom-verbs form matched pairs, and mixing up which one takes を is the exam's favorite trap here:

Intransitive (が, happens by itself)Transitive (を, someone/something does it)Meaning
早まるhayamaru 早めるhayameru To speed up / to hasten (something)
強まるtsuyomaru 強めるtsuyomeru To intensify / to strengthen (something)
弱まるyowamaru 弱めるyowameru To weaken/lessen / to lessen (something)
和らぐyawaragu 和らげるyawarageru To soften/ease (by itself) / to ease (something)
Exam trap: The warm-up's 痛みを＿＿＿ needs a transitive verb because 痛み is marked with を (someone/something actively easing the pain) — 弱まる is intransitive and grammatically cannot take を at all, and 遠ざける, while transitive, means "push something away/keep it at a distance," the wrong meaning for pain. Only 和らげる is both transitive and semantically correct. The same logic runs through Q6 and Q8 above: check whether the particle is が or を before picking a member of one of these pairs.
