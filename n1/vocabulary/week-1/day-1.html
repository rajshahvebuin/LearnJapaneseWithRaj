Warm-up

何と言う？Nanto iu?
心がゆったりして、細かいことを気にしない人。Kokoro ga yuttari shite, komakai koto o ki ni shinai hito.
("What do you call a person whose heart is relaxed and who doesn't sweat the small stuff?")

おおらかな人 (oouraka na hito) — "an easygoing / magnanimous person"

Q1

私の夫はとても金づかいが（　）。Watashi no otto wa totemo kanezukai ga (___).

Full sentence: 私の夫はとても金づかいが荒い。Watashi no otto wa totemo kanezukai ga arai.
EN: My husband is a real spendthrift.
HI: मेरे पति बहुत फ़िज़ूलख़र्ची करते हैं।
GU: મારા પતિ ખૂબ ઉડાઉ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a悪いwaruibad (doesn't collocate here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b荒いarairough/wasteful (fixed idiom)फ़िज़ूलख़र्चઉડાઉ

Why: 金遣いが荒い is a fixed idiom for being wasteful with money — 悪い simply doesn't collocate with 金遣い this way.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several of today's "friendly/hardworking" words look interchangeable in English but aren't in Japanese — this is exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q3 and Q6 above are testing. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
愛想がいいaiso ga ii Friendly, amiable to others About surface social manner — how one behaves toward people (esp. customers/strangers). Describing a shopkeeper, host, or someone's manner "to everyone" (だれにでも).
気さくなkisaku na Approachable, easygoing About overall personality being easy to talk to — works even for someone famous/high-status. Contrasting someone's status/fame with how down-to-earth they are (なのに contrast).
気立てがいいkidate ga ii Good-natured, kind at heart About underlying character/temperament, not observable social behavior. Describing someone's fundamental disposition (e.g. as a marriage prospect).
まめなmame na Diligent about small tasks Effort shown in small, everyday details (chores, staying in touch, upkeep). Someone who keeps on top of small tasks without being asked.
勤勉なkinben na Industrious, hardworking General hard work, usually at study or a job — bigger scale than まめ. Describing work/study ethic generally.
誠実なseijitsu na Sincere, honest A moral/character trait about honesty in dealing with people — not about effort level at all. Describing trustworthiness, not work ethic.
でしゃばりなdeshabari na Pushy, intrusive About inserting oneself/one's opinion where not wanted — a presence/attention problem. Someone who butts in and draws attention to themselves.
おせっかいなosekkai na Meddlesome, a busybody About giving unsolicited help/advice in others' affairs — an interference problem, not attention-seeking. Someone who "helps" or advises when nobody asked them to.
Exam trap: 荒っぽい (rough/coarse manner in general) and 金遣いが荒い (spendthrift — a fixed idiom specifically about money) share the kanji 荒 but are not interchangeable: you cannot say 金遣いが荒っぽい. When 金遣い ("money usage") is the subject, the idiom always takes 荒い, not 荒っぽい.
