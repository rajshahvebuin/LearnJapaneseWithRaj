ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One word changes its meaning with the situation, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set. The 例題 (つよい) and the full 解き方 for this pattern are on I ①.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ स्थिति के अनुसार बदलता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके याद करें। इस पैटर्न का 例題 (つよい) और पूरा 解き方 I ① पर है।

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.79)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

(1)

つうじる……隣の町まで道がつうじて、便利になった。Tsuujiru…… Tonari no machi made michi ga tsuujite, benri ni natta.

Sense used: ① あるところまで届く — reach, connect
EN: A road now goes through to the next town, which is convenient.
HI: पड़ोसी शहर तक सड़क बन जाने से सुविधा हो गई।
GU: પડોશી શહેર સુધી રસ્તો બનતાં સગવડ થઈ.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1仏教は中国をつうじて日本に入ってきた。 → ⑥ viaBukkyou wa Chuugoku o tsuujite Nihon ni haitte kita.Buddhism came to Japan via China.बौद्ध धर्म चीन के रास्ते जापान आया।બૌદ્ધ ધર્મ ચીન મારફતે જાપાન આવ્યો.
2この地方は1年をつうじて温暖な気候だ。 → ⑤ throughoutKono chihou wa ichinen o tsuujite ondan na kikou da.This region is warm all year round.यह इलाक़ा साल भर गर्म रहता है।આ વિસ્તાર આખું વર્ષ હૂંફાળો રહે છે.
3日本に来たばかりのころは、言葉がつうじないので困った。 → ③ be understoodNihon ni kita bakari no koro wa, kotoba ga tsuujinai node komatta.When I'd just come to Japan, I had trouble because I couldn't make myself understood.जापान आते ही भाषा न समझ आने से परेशानी हुई।જાપાન આવ્યો ત્યારે ભાષા ન સમજાતાં તકલીફ પડી.
4ずっと話し中で、電話がつうじないんです。 → ② get through (connect)Zutto hanashichuu de, denwa ga tsuujinai n desu.It's engaged all the time and I can't get through on the phone.लगातार व्यस्त है, फ़ोन नहीं लग रहा।સતત વ્યસ્ત છે, ફોન લાગતો નથી.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A road reaching the next town and a phone line reaching the other person are both a physical connection getting through. The book's list puts the phone under ② 連絡できる, but of these four options it is the closest to ①. Options 1 and 2 are the grammar pattern 〜をつうじて (via, throughout), and option 3 is about being understood. (added, not in book)

OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1社長にはほめられるし、彼女はデートの約束をしてくれるし、ついているな。 → ⑨ luckyShachou ni wa homerareru shi, kanojo wa deeto no yakusoku o shite kureru shi, tsuite iru na.Praised by the boss and my girlfriend agreed to a date — I'm on a roll.बॉस ने तारीफ़ की, गर्लफ़्रेंड डेट पर राज़ी हो गई — आज क़िस्मत साथ है।બૉસે વખાણ કર્યા, ગર્લફ્રેન્ડ ડેટ માટે રાજી થઈ — આજે નસીબ સાથ આપે છે.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A stain on clothes and a smell in hair are both something that clings to a surface and is hard to remove (ケチャップ, インク, 匂い). Option 3 is the close trap: a scratch is damage, a mark left behind (⑤), not something stuck on. Option 1 is luck and option 4 staying beside someone.

3スカートが長すぎるので、3センチほどつめてください。 → ⑦ shortenSukaato ga nagasugiru node, sansenchi hodo tsumete kudasai.The skirt is too long, so please take it up about 3 cm.स्कर्ट बहुत लंबी है, लगभग 3 सेंटीमीटर छोटी कर दीजिए।સ્કર્ટ બહુ લાંબું છે, લગભગ 3 સેન્ટીમીટર ટૂંકું કરી આપો.
4父は記者なので、事件が起こったときは、新聞社につめることがよくある。 → ③ be on dutyChichi wa kisha na node, jiken ga okotta toki wa, shinbunsha ni tsumeru koto ga yoku aru.My father is a reporter, so when something happens he often stays at the newspaper office.पिताजी पत्रकार हैं, इसलिए घटना होने पर अक्सर अख़बार के दफ़्तर में डटे रहते हैं।પિતાજી પત્રકાર છે, એટલે ઘટના બને ત્યારે ઘણી વાર છાપાની ઑફિસે જ રહે છે.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Food into a lunch box and clothes into a suitcase are both packing a container full. Option 1 is something stuck in the throat, option 3 shortening a skirt, and option 4 staying on duty at a place.

(4)

でる……山で迷い、歩きまわっているうちに川原にでた。Deru…… Yama de mayoi, arukimawatte iru uchi ni kawara ni deta.

Sense used: ⑤ 行きつく — come out at
EN: I got lost in the mountains and, wandering about, came out at a riverbank.
HI: मैं पहाड़ों में भटक गया और घूमते-घूमते नदी किनारे जा निकला।
GU: હું પર્વતોમાં ભૂલો પડ્યો અને ફરતાં ફરતાં નદીકિનારે જઈ પહોંચ્યો.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1この道をまっすぐ行くと、駅前にでる。 → ⑤ come out atKono michi o massugu iku to, ekimae ni deru.Go straight along this road and you'll come out in front of the station.यह सड़क सीधी जाएँ तो स्टेशन के सामने पहुँचेंगे।આ રસ્તે સીધા જાઓ તો સ્ટેશનની સામે પહોંચશો.
2地震が起こったときは、すぐ外にでるのは危険だ。 → ① go outJishin ga okotta toki wa, sugu soto ni deru no wa kiken da.When an earthquake hits, it's dangerous to go straight outside.भूकंप आने पर तुरंत बाहर निकलना ख़तरनाक है।ભૂકંપ આવે ત્યારે તરત બહાર નીકળવું જોખમી છે.
3本社の会議にでるために、日帰りで東京へ行った。 → ⑥ attendHonsha no kaigi ni deru tame ni, higaeri de Toukyou e itta.I made a day trip to Tokyo to attend a meeting at head office.मुख्यालय की बैठक में जाने के लिए मैं एक दिन के लिए टोक्यो गया।મુખ્ય કચેરીની બેઠકમાં હાજર રહેવા હું એક દિવસ માટે ટોક્યો ગયો.

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Both describe walking along until you arrive at a place: 〜にでる after a route (歩きまわる, この道を行く). Option 2 is leaving a building, option 3 attending a meeting, and option 4 a train departing.

(5)

とばす……時間がなかったので、報告書をとばして読んだ。Tobasu…… Jikan ga nakatta node, houkokusho o tobashite yonda.

Sense used: ② 途中を抜かす — skip
EN: I had no time, so I skimmed the report, skipping parts.
HI: समय नहीं था, इसलिए मैंने रिपोर्ट बीच-बीच में छोड़कर पढ़ी।
GU: સમય નહોતો, એટલે મેં અહેવાલ વચ્ચે વચ્ચે છોડીને વાંચ્યો.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あの先生はいつもつばをとばしながら話すので、汚い。 → ⑥ sprayAno sensei wa itsumo tsuba o tobashinagara hanasu node, kitanai.That teacher always sprays spit as he talks — it's gross.वे शिक्षक हमेशा थूक उछालते हुए बोलते हैं, गंदा लगता है।એ શિક્ષક હંમેશાં થૂંક ઉડાડતાં બોલે છે, ગંદું લાગે છે.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Skimming a report and skipping lyrics both leave out part of something on the way through. The others are spraying spit, the intensifier 叱りとばす, and driving fast.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Verbs with very wide sense lists, sorted by the clue that picks the sense:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
つうじる ① / ② / ③tsuujiru reach / get through / be understood 道・電車がつうじる → ①; 電話・話がつうじる → ② (contact); 言葉・気持ちがつうじる → ③. Q1: 道 ≈ 電話 (connection)
〜をつうじて ⑤ / ⑥o tsuujite throughout / via Time + をつうじて → ⑤ (1年をつうじて); a person, country or medium + をつうじて → ⑥. Q1 options 1–2
つく ① / ⑤tsuku cling / leave a mark しみ・インク・匂いがつく (something stuck on) vs 傷がつく (damage). Q2: しみ = 匂い
つめる ① / ② / ⑦tsumeru pack / block / shorten Container + につめる → ①; のど・穴につめる → ②; 丈・袖をつめる → ⑦. Q3: 弁当箱 = カバン
でる ① / ⑤deru go out / come out at 外・庭へでる (leaving a space) → ①; route + 〜にでる (arrive somewhere) → ⑤. Q4: 川原にでた = 駅前にでる
とばす ② / ⑦tobasu skip / speed 読む・歌う + とばして → ②; 車・バイクをとばす → ⑦. Q5: 報告書 = 歌詞
Exam trap — the best answer, not a perfect match: in Q1 no option repeats sense ① exactly. The 別冊 key chooses the phone line (connection reaching the other end) as the closest. In this pattern, pick the option nearest to the opening sentence even if the book's list files it under a different number. (added, not in book)
Exam trap — damage vs residue: Q2 options 2 and 3 both use 〜がつく with something left on a surface. A smell or a stain is something added; a scratch is damage. Ask yourself whether you could wash it off.
