ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Look at the okurigana first; if it does not decide the reading, use the meaning of the sentence. The 例題 for this pattern are on I-2 ①.

HI: पहले ओकुरिगाना देखें; अगर उससे उच्चारण तय न हो, तो वाक्य के अर्थ से तय करें। इस पैटर्न के 例題 I-2 ① पर हैं।

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.27)

問題Ⅰ — choose the reading

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is each underlined word read? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द कैसे पढ़ा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે વંચાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

問1 — (1)

緊張が緩み、和やかなムードになった。Kinchou ga yurumi, nagoyaka na muudo ni natta.

EN: The tension eased and the mood became friendly.
HI: तनाव कम हुआ और माहौल सौहार्दपूर्ण हो गया।
GU: તણાવ ઓછો થયો અને વાતાવરણ સૌહાર્દપૂર્ણ બની ગયું.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1ゆるみyurumi緩み (緩む) = easingढीला पड़नाઢીલું પડવું
2なごみnagomi和み = relaxing (written 和, not 緩)सहज होना (和 से लिखा जाता है)હળવાશ (和થી લખાય)
3あたたかみatatakami温かみ = warmthगर्माहटહૂંફ
4よわみyowami弱み = weaknessकमज़ोरीનબળાઈ

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) 緊張が緩む is the book's example; here it is the 連用形 緩み used to join two clauses. なごみ fits the mood of the sentence but belongs to 和, which is the next item.

問1 — (2)

緊張が緩み、和やかなムードになった。Kinchou ga yurumi, nagoyaka na muudo ni natta.

問2 — (3)

苦い薬ほど病に効くといいます。Nigai kusuri hodo yamai ni kiku to iimasu.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In this pattern one kanji carries several readings. The okurigana usually tells you which one; where it cannot, meaning decides. These are the sets the questions test, plus the ones most likely to trip you up:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
苦い / 苦しい / 苦しむnigai / kurushii / kurushimu bitter / painful / suffer -い alone → にがい; -しい / -しむ → くるし-. 苦い薬 (Q3) · 息が苦しい · 病気に苦しむ
老いる / 老けるoiru / fukeru grow old / look old 老いる is ageing itself (formal, neutral). 老ける is looking older, often older than one's age. 老いた手 (Q7) · 年より老けて見える
触れる / 触るfureru / sawaru touch lightly / touch ふれる is a light or brief contact (and figurative: 話題に触れる). さわる is deliberate handling. 手にそっと触れた (Q8) · 触ると危険だ
抱く (だく / いだく) / 抱えるdaku / idaku / kakaeru hold / cherish / carry Same okurigana く: だく is physical (a baby), いだく is a feeling (hope, doubt). かかえる is holding with both arms, or "having" problems. 赤ん坊を抱く · 希望を抱く · 子どもを抱えた (Q13)
和む / 和やか / 和らげるnagomu / nagoyaka / yawarageru relax / friendly / soften なご- in 和む and 和やか; やわら- in 和らげる (and 和らぐ). 和やかなムード (Q2) · 気持ちを和らげる
緩む / 緩い / 緩やかyurumu / yurui / yuruyaka loosen / loose / gentle One stem ゆる- for all three; only the ending changes. 緊張が緩む (Q1) · 緩やかな坂道
覆う / 覆すoou / kutsugaesu cover / overturn -う → おおう; -す → くつがえす (覆る = くつがえる). 顔を覆う · 前言を覆す (Q6)
潜む / 潜るhisomu / moguru lurk / dive -む → ひそむ (hidden); -る → もぐる (go under water or under covers). 泥棒が潜む · 潜ることができない (Q10)
開く (あく / ひらく)aku / hiraku open Same okurigana: 〜が開く (あく) for things that open by themselves; 〜を開く (ひらく) when someone opens it, and for events (会を開く). ドアが開く · 口を開く
Exam trap — same sound, wrong kanji: in 問題Ⅱ the strongest distractor is often a real word with the same reading: 送れて for 遅れて (Q12), and in 問題Ⅰ the reading さわれた, a real form of 触る (Q8). Check that the meaning fits the sentence, not just that the word exists.
Exam trap — two real phrases, one kanji: 前言を翻す and 前言を覆す both mean "go back on one's word". The test shows you 覆, so the answer must be a reading of 覆 (くつがえす), even though ひるがえす is a real word that fits the sentence.
