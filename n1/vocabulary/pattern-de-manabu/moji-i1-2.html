ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Same sound, different kanji: learn each word inside its compound and example phrase, not as a single character. The 例題 for this pattern are on I-1 ①.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.11)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

市民税は地域によりかくさがある。Shiminzei wa chiiki ni yori kakusa ga aru.

Full sentence: 市民税は地域により格差がある。Shiminzei wa chiiki ni yori kakusa ga aru.
EN: Municipal tax differs from region to region.
HI: नगर-कर में इलाक़े के हिसाब से अंतर होता है।
GU: નગર-કરમાં વિસ્તાર પ્રમાણે તફાવત હોય છે.
OptionKanjiEnglishHindiGujarati
1格差kakusagap, disparityअसमानताઅસમાનતા
2革差(kakusa)not a real wordअसली शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી
3確差(kakusa)not a real wordअसली शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી
4較差kakusa / kousarange (max. minus min.)उतार-चढ़ाव का अंतरવધઘટનો તફાવત

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) A difference in level between groups (regions, generations, incomes) is 格差, as in 世代間の格差. 較差 is the spread between a highest and a lowest value of one thing, like temperature over a day.

(2)

最近はロボットにかんしんを持つ人が増えた。Saikin wa robotto ni kanshin o motsu hito ga fueta.

Full sentence: 最近はロボットに関心を持つ人が増えた。Saikin wa robotto ni kanshin o motsu hito ga fueta.
EN: Recently more people have become interested in robots.

GU: હમણાં રોબોટમાં રસ ધરાવતા લોકો વધ્યા છે.

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) 〜に関心を持つ = "be interested in ~" (スポーツへの関心が高い). 感心 is used as 〜に感心する ("be impressed"), not with 持つ, and 歓心 goes with 買う／得る.

(3)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above test, plus the classic exam pairs from the list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
解答 / 回答kaitou solution / reply 解答 solves a problem that has a correct answer. 回答 replies to a question, request or survey. 試験の解答 (Q8) · アンケートに回答する
鑑賞 / 観賞kanshou appreciating art / enjoying the sight 鑑賞 is for works people made (music, films, paintings). 観賞 is for looking at nature: fish, flowers, plants. 音楽鑑賞 · 熱帯魚を観賞する (Q6)
関心 / 感心 / 歓心kanshin interest / admiration / favour 関心を持つ・関心が高い (interest). 〜に感心する (be impressed). 歓心を買う・得る (curry favour). ロボットに関心を持つ (Q2) · 努力に感心する
過重 / 加重 / 荷重kajuu too heavy / add weight / load 過重 (な) = excessive. 加重 (する) = make heavier. 荷重 = the load a structure bears. 過重な労働 (Q3) · 刑が加重される · 荷重制限
強行 / 強硬 / 強攻 / 凶行kyoukou force through / hard-line / all-out attack / violent crime 強行 is an action (〜を強行する). 強硬 is an attitude (強硬な). 強攻 is sports/war tactics. 凶行 is a crime. 採決を強行 (Q4) · 強硬に反対する
加熱 / 過熱kanetsu heating / overheating 加熱 is applying heat on purpose. 過熱 is getting too hot, also figuratively (競争が過熱する). 加熱に弱い (Q5) · ブームが過熱する
格差 / 較差kakusa disparity / range 格差 compares levels between groups. 較差 is the spread between the highest and lowest values of one thing. 地域格差 (Q1) · 気温の較差
改定 / 改訂kaitei revise (rules) / revise (text) 改定 changes rules, prices and fees. 改訂 (訂 = correct) revises a book or document. 料金改定 · 改訂版
解放 / 開放kaihou release / open up 解放 frees a person from something (〜から解放される). 開放 opens a place or market to others. 労働から解放 (Q3) · 校庭を開放する
過程 / 課程katei process / curriculum 過程 is how something unfolds over time. 課程 is a set course of study you complete (修了する). 成長の過程 · 博士課程
Exam trap — fake and rare words: 問題Ⅰ mixes real homophones with invented compounds: 革差・確差 (Q1), 勘重 (Q3), 改答 (Q8). It also uses rare real words that you will not need: 甘心 (Q2), 隔心 (Q7), 快答 (Q8). Cross out what you have never seen, then choose between the common pair by collocation.
問題Ⅱ strategy: the distractors often share the underlined word's kanji but not its sound: 過去 (かこ) vs 過小, 関心 (かんしん) vs 関知, 警察 (けいさつ) vs 観察. A shared kanji is a warning sign, not a clue. Say the reading of each option in your head and compare whole sounds.
