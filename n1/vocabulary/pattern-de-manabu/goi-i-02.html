ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One word changes its meaning with the situation, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set. The 例題 (つよい) and the full 解き方 for this pattern are on I ①.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ स्थिति के अनुसार बदलता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके याद करें। इस पैटर्न का 例題 (つよい) और पूरा 解き方 I ① पर है।

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.63)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) 〜にあかるい (with に + a field) means "knows a lot about". 地理にあかるい and コンピュータにあかるい have the same structure. Options 3 and 4 both use the "hopeful" sense, and option 1 is about colour.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) くろいうわさ and 腹がくろい are both about wrongdoing or bad intentions, not colour. Option 1 is the colour black, option 2 skin darkened by the sun, and option 4 dirt.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Adjective pairs whose senses mirror each other, plus the senses an English gloss blurs:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
あかるい ⑤ / くらい ④akarui / kurai familiar with / unfamiliar with Both take に + a field: 地理にあかるい (knows the area) ⇔ 地理にくらい (doesn't). Q1: コンピュータにあかるい
くろい ③ / ④kuroi dirty / shady ③ is literal (cuffs, uniforms); ④ is moral (噂, 腹がくろい). Q2: くろい噂 = 腹がくろい
まずい ① / ② / ③mazui bad taste / poor skill / bad timing Look at the subject: food → ①; a song or a piece of work → ②; a day or a situation → ③. Q3: 日曜日はまずい = ③
わかい ② / ③wakai naive / youthful 考えがわかい usually criticises (green, naive); わかいですね said to an older person praises their energy. Q4: 90歳でマラソン = ③
かたい ① / ②katai hard / stuck ① the material resists pressure (meat, bed, leather); ② a moving part won't move (戸, 栓). Q5: 肉・ベッド = ①
うすい ② vs あかるい ②usui / akarui pale vs bright colour うすい色 = weak, faded colour; あかるい色 = light, cheerful colour. Not the same thing. うすい色 · あかるい色のセーター
Exam trap — same words, different sense: in Q4 option 4 repeats 考えがわかい from the opening sentence, but the senses differ. Matching words is not enough; check what the speaker means.
Exam trap — に + adjective: when あかるい or くらい follows a noun + に (〜にあかるい, 〜にくらい), it is almost always about knowledge, not light. (added, not in book)
