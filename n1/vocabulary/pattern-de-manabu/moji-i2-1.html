1 Points — the 例題 and the kun-reading list

例題 — the question type of this pattern (p.20)

例題：次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Reidai: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is each underlined word read? Choose the reading from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द कैसे पढ़ा जाता है? 1–4 में से उच्चारण चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે વંચાય? 1–4માંથી ઉચ્ચાર પસંદ કરો.

例題 ①

過ちを犯しても、時が過ぎれば忘れてしまうものだ。Ayamachi o okashite mo, toki ga sugireba wasurete shimau mono da.

EN: Even when you make a mistake, once time passes you tend to forget it.
HI: ग़लती हो भी जाए, तो समय बीतने पर इंसान उसे भूल ही जाता है।
GU: ભૂલ થઈ જાય તો પણ, સમય વીતે એટલે માણસ તેને ભૂલી જ જાય છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1すごちsugochinot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી
2あやちayachinot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી
3あやまちayamachimistake, faultग़लतीભૂલ
4すぎちsugichinot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી

Answer 3 (book's 解き方, p.20). Book's 解き方 (paraphrased): when 過 is followed by the okurigana ち, it is read あやまち. (added, not in book) 過ちを犯す ("commit a mistake") is a fixed collocation.

例題 ②

過ちを犯しても、時が過ぎれば忘れてしまうものだ。Ayamachi o okashite mo, toki ga sugireba wasurete shimau mono da.

EN: (same sentence) … once time passes you tend to forget it.
HI: (वही वाक्य) … समय बीतने पर इंसान भूल जाता है।
GU: (એ જ વાક્ય) … સમય વીતે એટલે માણસ ભૂલી જાય છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1あやぎayaginot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી
2すぎsugi(過ぎる) to passबीतनाવીતવું
3かぎkagikey (鍵) — not a reading of 過चाबी — 過 का उच्चारण नहींચાવી — 過નો ઉચ્ચાર નથી
4ずぎzuginot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી

Answer 2 (book's 解き方, p.20). Book's 解き方 (paraphrased): with the okurigana ぎる, 過 is read すぎる. (added, not in book) Here it appears in the conditional 過ぎれば.

ヒント — the book's tip (summary, not the book's text)

EN: When a kanji has many native (kun) readings, look at the okurigana (the kana written after it) first. If two readings share the same okurigana, or there is none, decide from the meaning of the sentence.

HI: जब किसी कांजी के कई कुन-उच्चारण हों, तो पहले उसके बाद लिखे काना (ओकुरिगाना) को देखें। अगर दो उच्चारणों का ओकुरिगाना एक जैसा हो या हो ही नहीं, तो वाक्य के अर्थ से तय करें।

GU: જ્યારે કોઈ કાંજીના ઘણા કુન-ઉચ્ચાર હોય, ત્યારે પહેલાં તેની પછી લખેલા કાના (ઓકુરિગાના) જુઓ. જો બે ઉચ્ચારનું ઓકુરિગાના સરખું હોય કે ન જ હોય, તો વાક્યના અર્થ પરથી નક્કી કરો.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.21)

問題Ⅰ — choose the reading

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is each underlined word read? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द कैसे पढ़ा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે વંચાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

問1 — (1)

10年ぶりに先生に会って懐かしかった。Juunen-buri ni sensei ni atte natsukashikatta.

EN: Meeting my teacher for the first time in ten years brought back fond memories.
HI: दस साल बाद अपने शिक्षक से मिलकर पुरानी यादें ताज़ा हो गईं।
GU: દસ વર્ષ પછી શિક્ષકને મળીને જૂની યાદો તાજી થઈ ગઈ.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1はずかしhazukashi恥ずかしい = embarrassedशर्मिंदाશરમિંદું
2なつかしnatsukashi懐かしい = nostalgicपुरानी याद वालाજૂની યાદવાળું
3いぶかしibukashi訝しい = suspicious, doubtfulसंदेहजनकશંકાસ્પદ
4かがやかしkagayakashi輝かしい = brilliantशानदारઝળહળતું

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) All four options are 〜かしい adjectives, so the okurigana alone does not decide. 懐 with 〜かしい is なつかしい (懐かしい友達 in the list), and seeing an old teacher after ten years fits that meaning.

問題Ⅱ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように書きますか。その書き方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni kakimasu ka. Sono kakikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In this pattern one kanji carries several readings. The okurigana usually tells you which one; where it cannot, meaning decides. These are the sets the questions test, plus the ones most likely to trip you up:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
陥る / 陥れるochiiru / otoshiireru fall into / trap someone 〜に陥る has no object: you fall into a state. 〜を陥れる has an object: you push someone else in. The longer okurigana (れる) goes with the longer reading. スランプに陥る (Q6) · 人を陥れる (Q5)
滑る / 滑らかsuberu / nameraka slide / smooth Verb with る = すべる. Adjective with らか = なめらか. 氷の上を滑る (Q4) · 滑らかに話す (Q2)
軽い / 軽やかkarui / karoyaka light / nimble The stem changes: かる + い, but かろ + やか. 軽いかばん · 軽やかなリズム (Q3)
偽る / 偽itsuwaru / nise falsify / fake With る it is the verb いつわる. Alone, before の or 物, it is にせ (偽物, 偽札). 本物だと偽る (Q7) · 偽の商品 (Q8)
汚れる (よごれる / けがれる)yogoreru / kegareru get dirty / be defiled Same kanji, same okurigana. よごれる is physical dirt (服). けがれる is moral or spiritual (心, 名誉). Only the context decides. 服が汚れる · 心が汚れる
下 (した / しも / もと)shita / shimo / moto under / lower part / under (rule) した is physical position. しも is the lower or downstream side (上下 かみしも). もと is "under someone's control or guidance" (〜の下で). 机の下 · 舞台の下 · 監視の下
外 (そと / ほか) · 外れるsoto / hoka / hazureru outside / other / miss そと is a place outside. ほか means "other" (外の人 = other people). With れる it is はずれる. 部屋の外 (Q11) · 宝くじが外れる
割る / 割くwaru / saku break / spare Okurigana decides: る → わる, く → さく. 割く is used with time, space or staff. 卵を割る · 時間を割く (Q9)
Exam trap — meaning-alike distractors: the wrong readings often match the meaning, not the kanji: うそ for 偽 (Q8), だまし for 偽る (Q7), こおった next to 氷 (Q4), 締 for むすぶ (Q13). Check that the option is a real reading of that kanji, not just a word that fits the sentence.
Exam trap — impossible verb forms: 問3 offers おちいれ, おちって, おとしって, おとしいって. Conjugate the real verbs yourself (おちいる → おちいって; おとしいれる → おとしいれよう) and the fake forms drop out.
