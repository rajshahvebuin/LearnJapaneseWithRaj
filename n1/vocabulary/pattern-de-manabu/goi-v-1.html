解き方 (summary, not the book's text) — EN: Look for the word that means "a false story". HI: वह शब्द खोजें जिसका अर्थ "झूठी कहानी" है। GU: એવો શબ્દ શોધો જેનો અર્થ "ખોટી વાર્તા" થાય.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.107)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿の部分に入れるのに最も適当なものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ no bubun ni ireru no ni mottomo tekitou na mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the most suitable word for the blank. HI: रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त शब्द चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

(1)

父は酒を飲んで、（　）で帰ってきた。Chichi wa sake o nonde, (　) de kaette kita.

Full sentence: 父は酒を飲んで、千鳥足で帰ってきた。Chichi wa sake o nonde, chidoriashi de kaette kita.
EN: My father had been drinking and came home staggering.
HI: पिताजी शराब पीकर लड़खड़ाते हुए घर लौटे।
GU: પિતાજી દારૂ પીને લથડતા પગે ઘરે આવ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1千鳥足chidoriashistaggering drunk gaitलड़खड़ाती चालલથડતી ચાલ
2蛇行足dakouashi(not a word; 蛇行 = meander)(शब्द नहीं)(શબ્દ નથી)
3無駄足mudaashiwasted tripबेकार चक्करનકામો ધક્કો
4勇み足isamiashioverstepping (from eagerness)जोश में ग़लतीઉત્સાહમાં ભૂલ

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 千鳥足: a plover (千鳥) zigzags as it walks, like a drunk. 勇み足 is a real word (jumping the gun) but not on the list and unrelated to drink.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
〜性 (しょう) / 〜性 (せい)-shou / -sei personal tendency / quality しょう attaches to everyday words to describe a person's habit (苦労性, 貧乏性, 心配性); せい makes abstract nouns (社交性, 可能性, 植物性). 母は苦労性 (Q7) · 社交性がある (Q4)
掛かりつけ / 行きつけkakaritsuke / ikitsuke regular (doctor) / regular (shop) 掛かる = be under a doctor's care; 行く = go somewhere often. 掛かりつけの医者 (Q9) · 行きつけの店
〜並み / 〜まがい-nami / -magai on a par with / imitation of 並み compares a level (世間並み, 小学生並み); まがい says it looks like X but isn't genuine — usually negative. 小学生並み (Q3) · 詐欺まがい (Q10)
〜割り / 〜増し-wari / -mashi divided by / extra 頭割り = split per head; 割り増し = surcharge. Both use 割, in reverse roles. 割り増し料金 (Q2) · 頭割りで計算
競争心 / 向上心 / 好奇心 / 平常心kyousoushin / koujoushin / koukishin / heijoushin competitiveness / ambition / curiosity / composure 競争心 is against others; 向上心 is against your past self; 平常心 is staying calm. 競争心をあおる (Q8)
無駄足 / 千鳥足mudaashi / chidoriashi wasted trip / staggering 無駄足 is the result (nobody home); 千鳥足 is the way you walk. 千鳥足で帰る (Q1)
Exam trap — fake compounds: 蛇行足, 増し割り, 乗り増し, 歩道帯, 努力性 and 掛けつけ are not words. They are built from a real first half plus the right suffix (or the right parts reversed). If you have never seen the whole word, be suspicious.
Exam trap — words not on the list: the answers to Q6 (情報網) and the distractors 勇み足, 将来性, 老婆心, 繁華街 are not on p.106. The test expects you to apply the suffix's meaning to new words.
