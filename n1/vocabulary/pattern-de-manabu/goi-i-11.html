2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.81)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

(1)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Verbs where the object noun, not the verb, tells you the sense:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
なげる ① / ②nageru throw / give up A physical object (石, ボール) → ①; 試合・仕事・勝負をなげる → ②. Q1: 試合 = 仕事
のびる ① / ⑨nobiru grow longer / lose springiness ひげ・つめ・髪がのびる → ①; ゴム・麺 (うどん, そば) がのびる → ⑨. Q2: ゴム = うどん
のる ① / ②noru get up on / be on top ① a person climbs up (action); ② a thing is resting there (state, 〜のっている). Q3: テーブル = ひざ
はいる ① / ③hairu enter a place / enter a period Place + にはいる → ①; a season, holiday or stage (夏休み, 梅雨, 交渉) + にはいる → ③. Q4: 夏休み = 交渉
ひく ④ / ⑭hiku attract / subside Transitive (〜をひく): 同情・人目・注意 → ④; intransitive (〜がひく): 熱・腫れ・潮 → ⑭/⑬. Q5: 同情 = 人目
のびる ⑤ vs のる ⑦nobiru / noru worn out vs in the mood Both describe a person's condition, with opposite feelings: 1日中のびていた (flat out) vs 気分がのっている (in the zone). (added, not in book) 二日酔いでのびる · 気分がのる
Exam trap — action vs state: Q3 options 3 and 4 both have 〜の上に / 〜に + のる. 棚の上にのっている (a doll resting) is a state; ひざにのる (a child climbing up) is an action, like the opening sentence.
Exam trap — transitive vs intransitive ひく: with を (同情をひく) the subject pulls something towards itself; with が (腫れがひく) the thing goes away. Check the particle first. (added, not in book)
