1 Points — the meaning table (p.96)

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: There are many words with similar meanings; learn them in groups by meaning. Within a group, check which nouns or verbs each word goes with. The 例題 is on III ①.

HI: मिलते-जुलते अर्थ वाले बहुत शब्द हैं; उन्हें अर्थ के अनुसार समूहों में याद करें। हर समूह में देखें कि कौन-सा शब्द किस संज्ञा या क्रिया के साथ आता है।

GU: સરખા અર્થવાળા ઘણા શબ્દો છે; તેમને અર્થ પ્રમાણે જૂથમાં યાદ રાખો. દરેક જૂથમાં જુઓ કે કયો શબ્દ કઈ સંજ્ઞા કે ક્રિયા સાથે આવે છે.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
とびきり / いたって / いともtobikiri / itatte / itomo all "very" とびきり = outstandingly (good things); いたって = quite (normal states: 元気, 健康, 平凡); いとも = only with 簡単に／たやすく. とびきりすてき (Q1) · いたって元気 (Q2) · いとも簡単に (Q3)
たっぷり / なみなみ / たんまり / どっさりtappuri / naminami / tanmari / dossari all "plenty" たっぷり = generous amount of anything; なみなみ = liquid to the brim; たんまり = money; どっさり = a heap of things. たっぷり入る (Q4) · なみなみと注ぐ (Q8)
ほのか / かすかhonoka / kasuka both "faint" ほのか is pleasant (scent, light, love); かすか is neutral, just barely perceptible (sound, memory). ほのかに香る (Q6) · かすかに聞こえる
ねばり強い / 我慢強い / 辛抱強いnebarizuyoi / gamanzuyoi / shinbouzuyoi all "persistent / patient" ねばり強い = keeps working at a goal; 我慢強い = endures pain or discomfort; 辛抱強い = waits patiently through hardship. ねばり強い交渉 (Q9) · 我慢強い子
照れくさい / 肩身が狭い / 合わせる顔がないterekusai / katami ga semai / awaseru kao ga nai all "embarrassed" 照れくさい = bashful at praise; 肩身が狭い = ashamed in society because of something; 合わせる顔がない = can't face someone you let down. ほめられて照れくさい (Q10)
すがすがしい / さわやか / うっとうしいsugasugashii / sawayaka / uttoushii refreshing / fresh / gloomy すがすがしい = clean, refreshed feeling (morning air, after resolving something); さわやか also describes people and taste; うっとうしい is the opposite. すがすがしい気分 (Q7)
Exam trap — group membership is not enough: in Q9 three options belong to the same 強い group as ねばり強い. You must know what each one is "strong" at. The same is true of the 恥ずかしい options in Q10.
Exam trap — grammar shape: in Q1 only とびきり is an adverb that can sit in front of an adjective; 絶妙な and うってつけの need a noun right after them. Checking the form often removes two options at once.
