(should be 腹いっぱい / 飽きるほど — to her heart's content)× … (यहाँ 腹いっぱい / 飽きるほど = जी भरकर चाहिए)× … (અહીં 腹いっぱい / 飽きるほど = ધરાઈને જોઈએ)

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.5). (added, not in book) 掃いて捨てるほどいる/ある is said of people or things that are so plentiful they are worthless (たとえ, pattern VI). It carries contempt, so it fits 1 only; for rubbish (2) it is oddly literal, and for dreams or cake (3, 4) the tone is wrong.
