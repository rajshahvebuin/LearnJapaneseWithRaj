1 Points — the same-reading word list

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Same sound, different kanji: learn each word inside its compound and example phrase, not as a single character. The 例題 for this pattern are on I-1 ①.

HI: एक ही उच्चारण, अलग कांजी: हर शब्द को उसके संयुक्त शब्द और उदाहरण-वाक्य के साथ याद करें। इस पैटर्न के 例題 I-1 ① पर हैं।

GU: એક જ ઉચ્ચારણ, જુદા કાંજી: દરેક શબ્દને તેના સંયુક્ત શબ્દ અને ઉદાહરણ-વાક્ય સાથે યાદ રાખો. આ પેટર્નના 例題 I-1 ① પર છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.19)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

この薬はそっこう性がある。Kono kusuri wa sokkousei ga aru.

Full sentence: この薬は即効性がある。Kono kusuri wa sokkousei ga aru.
EN: This medicine works immediately.
HI: यह दवा तुरंत असर करती है।
GU: આ દવા તરત અસર કરે છે.

2速攻sokkouswift attackतेज़ हमलाઝડપી હુમલો

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) For medicine the standard word is 即効 (即効薬, 即効性), as in the list. 速効 also exists (速効性の肥料) and dictionaries often treat it as a variant, but the book's key wants 即効 here: 効 = effect, so only 即効・速効 are possible, and 即 "instantly" is the normal choice for drugs.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above test, plus the classic exam pairs from the list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
保証 / 保障 / 補償hoshou guarantee / security / compensation 保証 vouches that something is good or will be paid (保証書, 保証人). 保障 protects rights or safety (社会保障). 補償 pays for a loss already caused. 家具の補償 (Q2) · 品質を保証 · 安全保障
追求 / 追究 / 追及tsuikyuu pursue / investigate / press 追求: a goal you want (利益, 幸福, 平和). 追究: truth or cause (真理, 原因). 追及: someone's responsibility (責任を追及). 平和を追求 (Q7) · 原因を追究 · 責任を追及
体勢 / 態勢 / 体制taisei posture / readiness / system 体勢 is your body position. 態勢 is being ready for something (受け入れ態勢). 体制 is a lasting system or organisation (政治体制). 変な体勢 (Q4) · 万全の態勢 · 社会体制
対象 / 対照 / 対称taishou target / contrast / symmetry 対象 is who or what something is aimed at. 対照 compares two things (対照的). 対称 is mirror symmetry (左右対称). 注射の対象 (Q6) · 対照的な性格
平衡 / 平行 / 並行heikou balance / parallel / side by side 平衡 is balance (平衡感覚). 平行 is geometric parallel lines (平行線). 並行 is running or doing things alongside (並行して進める). 平衡感覚 (Q5) · 話し合いは平行線
即効 / 速効sokkou immediate effect / quick-acting Very close; 即効 (即効薬, 即効性) is the usual form for medicine and in the exam. 速効 appears with fertilisers and some products. 即効性がある (Q1)
促成 / 速成sokusei force-grow / train quickly 促成 is for crops (促成栽培). 速成 is for people and courses (速成教育). 促成栽培 (Q8) · 速成講座
必死 / 必至hisshi desperate / inevitable 必死 describes effort (必死に走る). 必至 describes an outcome that will surely happen (倒産は必至). 必死に走った (Q11) · 値上げは必至
特長 / 特徴tokuchou strong point / characteristic 特長 is a feature that is an advantage. 特徴 is any distinctive feature, good or neutral. 新製品の特長 · 顔の特徴
野生 / 野性yasei wild (living) / wild nature 野生 describes animals or plants living in nature. 野性 is a wild, untamed character (野性的). 野生動物 · 野性的な魅力
Exam trap — near-synonym homophones: 即効・速効, 並行・併行 and 直感・直観 overlap so much that even dictionaries list them as variants. When two options both seem possible, choose the more common, fixed compound (即効性, 平衡感覚, 促成栽培). These questions test set expressions, not fine meaning.
問題Ⅱ strategy: watch the small っ and long う: ちょっかん vs ちょうかん (Q9), ひっし vs ひし (Q11), ちょうしゅう vs ちょうしゅ (Q12). Count the beats (mora) of each option against the underlined word.
