Exam trap — one kanji, two 訓読み: 脅かす (おどかす / おびやかす), 重ねる (かさねる) vs 重い (おもい), 若しくは (もしくは) vs 若い (わかい). The okurigana and the sentence decide the reading.
Exam trap — adjectives in 問題Ⅴ: 都合がまずい (inconvenient), 政治に明るい (well versed in politics) are not about taste or light. When the four options are all plain adjectives, look for the idiomatic sense (pattern 第2章 I 多義語).
Exam trap — 魔がさす vs 魔が入る: 魔がさす is a sudden evil impulse that makes a good person do something bad (Q65). It is never a decision you make or a mood that lets you behave nicely.
