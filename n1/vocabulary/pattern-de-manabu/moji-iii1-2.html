1 Points — the one-kanji word list (continued)

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Learn one-kanji words with their reading. Most take a kun reading, but some common ones are used alone with their on-reading (核, 芸, 票, 脈). The 例題 is on III-1 ①.

HI: एक-कांजी शब्दों को उनके पाठ के साथ याद करें। अधिकतर का कुन पाठ होता है, पर कुछ आम शब्द अकेले ओन पाठ से पढ़े जाते हैं (核, 芸, 票, 脈)। 例題 III-1 ① पर है।

GU: એક-કાંજી શબ્દોને તેમના વાંચન સાથે યાદ રાખો. મોટા ભાગનાનું કુન વાંચન હોય છે, પણ કેટલાક સામાન્ય શબ્દો એકલા ઓન વાંચનથી વંચાય છે (核, 芸, 票, 脈). 例題 III-1 ① પર છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.41)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is the underlined word read? Choose the reading from 1–4 for each. HI: रेखांकित शब्द कैसे पढ़ा जाता है? हर एक के लिए 1–4 में से पढ़ने का तरीका चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે વંચાય? દરેક માટે 1–4માંથી વાંચવાની રીત પસંદ કરો.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) 格が高い = "of high standing". 格 alone is かく; くらい means a similar thing but is written 位.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

On this page the trap is knowing whether a one-kanji word takes a kun or an on reading:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
源 / 元minamoto / motosource / origin, base源 (氵) is a source, literally of a river; 元 (もと, もともと) is the origin or base of something.源を発する (Q3)
技 / 枝waza / edaskill / branchSame right part 支: 扌 hand → skill, 木 tree → branch.技 (Q4)
枠 / 粋waku / suiframe / chic, essence枠 (木 + 卆) is a frame; 粋 (米) is すい / いき. They look alike in print.枠 (Q2)
格 / 位kaku / kuraistatus / rank格 is the class or standard of a thing (格が違う); 位 is a position in a ranking (位が高い).格が高い (Q10)
芸 / 技gei / wazaart, trick / technique芸 is a performance or trained trick (芸を仕込む); 技 is technical skill (技を磨く).芸 (Q8) · 技 (Q4)
Exam trap — on-reading words: 核 かく, 液 えき, 芸 げい, 尿 にょう, 票 ひょう, 格 かく have no everyday kun reading and are used alone with the on-reading. Do not force a kun reading on them.
Exam trap — small kana: Q7 offers にょ / によ / にょう / によう. Write the small ょ and the long う: にょう.
