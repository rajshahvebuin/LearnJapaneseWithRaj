2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.17)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

しゅうとく物はどんな小さなものでも警察に届けた方がよい。Shuutokubutsu wa donna chiisa na mono demo keisatsu ni todoketa hou ga yoi.

Full sentence: 拾得物はどんな小さなものでも警察に届けた方がよい。Shuutokubutsu wa donna chiisa na mono demo keisatsu ni todoketa hou ga yoi.
EN: You should hand in anything you find to the police, however small.
HI: मिली हुई चीज़ कितनी भी छोटी हो, पुलिस को सौंप देनी चाहिए।
GU: મળેલી વસ્તુ ગમે તેટલી નાની હોય, પોલીસને સોંપી દેવી જોઈએ.

GU: જે માતા-પિતાએ બાળક ગુમાવ્યું હોય, તેમના મન પર શું વીતે છે તેનો અંદાજ ન લગાવી શકાય.

問題Ⅱ — choose the word with the same reading

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above test, plus the classic exam pairs from the list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
受賞 / 授賞 / 受章 / 授章jushou win a prize / award a prize / receive a medal / confer a medal 受 = receive, 授 = give. 賞 = prize, 章 = decoration or medal (勲章). Decide each half separately. 最優秀賞を受賞 (Q3) · 文化勲章を受章
習得 / 修得 / 拾得 / 収得shuutoku master / earn / find / acquire 習得 is skills and languages. 修得 is course credits. 拾得 is picking up lost property. 収得 is acquiring assets. 拾得物 (Q1) · 技術を習得 · 単位を修得
心情 / 真情 / 信条 / 身上shinjou feelings / true feelings / principle / strong point 心情 is a state of mind others can imagine. 真情 is sincere feeling openly shown. 信条 is a belief you live by. 身上 is a merit. 親の心情 (Q2) · 真情を吐露
首席 / 主席shuseki top of the class / head of state 首席 is first place in a ranking. 主席 is a political or official chief. 首席で卒業 (Q6) · 国家主席
障害 / 傷害shougai obstacle / injury 障害 blocks or interferes (also disability). 傷害 is physical harm, often a crime (傷害事件). 電波障害 (Q8) · 傷害罪
真性 / 真正shinsei confirmed (disease) / authentic 真性 is a medical "true case". 真正 is genuine as opposed to forged or false. 真性のウイルス (Q5) · 真正な文書
進路 / 針路shinro course ahead / heading 進路 is the way forward for anything (typhoon, student's future). 針路 is a compass heading of a ship or plane. 台風の進路 (Q9) · 卒業後の進路 · 針路を北に
清算 / 精算 / 成算seisan settle up, end / exact settlement / chance of success 清算 closes accounts or relationships (過去を清算). 精算 adjusts an amount exactly (運賃を精算). 成算 is confidence of success. 成算がある (Q12) · 乗り越し精算
制作 / 製作seisaku create (art, media) / manufacture 制作 is creative work (films, programmes, art). 製作 is making physical products (furniture, machines). 番組を制作 · 家具を製作
成育 / 生育seiiku growth (animals) / growth (plants) 成育 is used for people and animals. 生育 is used mainly for plants. 子どもの成育 · イネの生育
Exam trap — two-part choices: in Q3 the four options are a 2×2 grid (受/授 × 賞/章). Ask two separate questions: is the person receiving or giving? Is it a prize or a medal? Each half removes two options.
問題Ⅱ strategy: this page's distractors mostly differ by vowel length or dakuten: しんろう vs しんろ (Q9), じょうせい vs じょせい (Q10), しんぎ vs しんき (Q11). Say every option aloud in your head, counting the beats.
