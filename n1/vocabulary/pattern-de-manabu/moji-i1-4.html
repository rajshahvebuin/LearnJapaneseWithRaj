ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Same sound, different kanji: learn each word inside its compound and example phrase, not as a single character. The 例題 for this pattern are on I-1 ①.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.15)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

彼は被害者側の人間だからこうせいな判断はできないだろう。Kare wa higaishagawa no ningen da kara kousei na handan wa dekinai darou.

Full sentence: 彼は被害者側の人間だから公正な判断はできないだろう。Kare wa higaishagawa no ningen da kara kousei na handan wa dekinai darou.
EN: He is on the victim's side, so he probably can't judge fairly.
HI: वह पीड़ित पक्ष का आदमी है, इसलिए शायद निष्पक्ष फ़ैसला नहीं कर पाएगा।
GU: તે પીડિત પક્ષનો માણસ છે, એટલે કદાચ નિષ્પક્ષ નિર્ણય નહીં લઈ શકે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above test, plus the classic exam pairs from the list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
公正 / 校正 / 更正 / 更生kousei fair / proofreading / correction / rehabilitation 公正 (な) = impartial. 校正 = checking printed text. 更正 = correcting figures or records. 更生 = a person starting over. 公正な判断 (Q1) · 原稿の校正 · 非行少年の更生
施行 / 試行 / 施工shikou enforce / try out / build 施行 puts a law or system into effect. 試行 is a trial run (試行錯誤). 施工 is carrying out construction. 法律が施行される (Q2) · 試行期間 · 施工業者
時機 / 時期 / 時季jiki opportunity / period / season 時機 is the right moment to act (時機を逃す, 時機を待つ). 時期 is a span of time. 時季 is a season of the year. 時機を逃す (Q3) · 試験の時期 · 時季はずれ
収集 / 収拾shuushuu collect / bring under control 収集 gathers things (ごみ, 切手, 情報). 収拾 restores order to a messy situation (収拾がつかない). ごみの収集 (Q4) · 事態を収拾する
辞典 / 事典 / 字典jiten word dictionary / encyclopedia / kanji dictionary 辞典 explains words. 事典 explains things and topics. 字典 explains characters. 国語辞典 · 百科事典 (Q6) · 漢字字典
自認 / 自任jinin admit / regard oneself as 自認 admits a fault or fact about yourself. 自任 claims a role or talent for yourself. 能力不足を自認 (Q7) · 天才を自任する
行程 / 工程koutei journey / work process 行程 is a route or distance to travel. 工程 is the sequence of steps in making something. 今日の行程 (Q8) · 製造工程
実態 / 実体jittai actual situation / substance 実態 is what is really going on (実態調査). 実体 is the real thing behind a name (実体のない会社). 実態は家族企業 (Q12) · 実体がない
最後 / 最期saigo last / death 最後 is the end of any sequence. 最期 is the end of a life only. 最後の挨拶 · 最期を迎える
誇示 / 固持 / 固辞koji show off / hold firm / firmly decline 誇示 displays power or talent proudly. 固持 refuses to give up an opinion. 固辞 refuses an offer or post. 才能を誇示 (Q13) · 自説を固持 · 就任を固辞
Exam trap — short vs long こ: こじ (誇示・固持・固辞) and こうじ (工事) differ only by vowel length. 問題Ⅱ uses exactly this in Q13. Pattern IV-3 practises long and short sounds in depth.
Exam trap — two readings: 施行 and 施工 are read しこう or せこう; legal and construction professionals often say せこう to keep them apart from 試行. In the exam, either reading may appear, so learn both.
