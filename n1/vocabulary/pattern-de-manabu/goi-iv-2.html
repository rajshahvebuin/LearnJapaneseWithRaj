1 Points — 〈前半に注目する〉 (p.102)

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Some idioms always use a fixed noun–verb combination. This page sorts them by the first half — mostly body parts — so you can compare, for example, 顔が広い (well connected) with 顔が利く (have influence). The 例題 is on IV ①.

HI: कुछ मुहावरों में संज्ञा-क्रिया की जोड़ी तय होती है। यह पृष्ठ उन्हें पहले हिस्से (ज़्यादातर शरीर के अंग) से छाँटता है, ताकि आप 顔が広い (जान-पहचान ज़्यादा) और 顔が利く (रसूख़ होना) जैसे जोड़ों की तुलना कर सकें।

GU: કેટલાક રૂઢિપ્રયોગોમાં સંજ્ઞા-ક્રિયાની જોડી નક્કી હોય છે. આ પાનું તેમને પહેલા ભાગ (મોટે ભાગે શરીરનાં અંગો) પ્રમાણે ગોઠવે છે, જેથી 顔が広い (ઓળખાણ ઘણી) અને 顔が利く (વગ હોવી) જેવી જોડીઓ સરખાવી શકાય.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.103)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿の部分に入れるのに最も適当なものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ no bubun ni ireru no ni mottomo tekitou na mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the most suitable word for the blank. HI: रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त शब्द चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

(1)

招待状といっても場所と時間しか書いていない味も（　）もないものだった。Shoutaijou to itte mo basho to jikan shika kaite inai aji mo (　) mo nai mono datta.

Full sentence: 招待状といっても場所と時間しか書いていない味も素っ気もないものだった。Shoutaijou to itte mo basho to jikan shika kaite inai aji mo sokke mo nai mono datta.
EN: It was called an invitation, but it was a dry thing with only the place and time on it.
HI: कहने को निमंत्रण-पत्र था, पर उसमें सिर्फ़ जगह और समय लिखा था — बिल्कुल रूखा-सूखा।
GU: કહેવા પૂરતું આમંત્રણપત્ર હતું, પણ એમાં ફક્ત સ્થળ અને સમય લખેલાં — સાવ લુખ્ખું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1素っ気sokke(味も素っ気もない) dull, dryरूखापनલુખ્ખાપણું
2色気irokecharm, sex appealआकर्षणઆકર્ષણ
3嫌気iyakedistasteऊबકંટાળો
4気持ちkimochifeelingभावनाલાગણી

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 味も素っ気もない is a fixed doubling: "neither taste nor flavour" = completely dry and plain. 色気もない is a separate phrase, not paired with 味も.

(2)

この食品は（　）が速いから冷蔵庫に入れておいてください。Kono shokuhin wa (　) ga hayai kara reizouko ni irete oite kudasai.

Full sentence: この食品は足が速いから冷蔵庫に入れておいてください。Kono shokuhin wa ashi ga hayai kara reizouko ni irete oite kudasai.
EN: This food goes off quickly, so please keep it in the fridge.
HI: यह खाना जल्दी ख़राब होता है, इसलिए फ़्रिज में रख दीजिए।
GU: આ ખોરાક જલદી બગડી જાય છે, એટલે ફ્રિજમાં મૂકી દેજો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1手tehandहाथહાથ
2足ashi(足が速い) spoils quickly(जल्दी ख़राब होना)(જલદી બગડવું)
3口kuchimouthमुँहમોં
4味ajitasteस्वादસ્વાદ

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) For food, 足が速い means "perishable". 手が速い (quick worker, or fast with the opposite sex) doesn't fit a fridge.

(3)

郵便局ならここから（　）の先だから歩いて行こう。Yuubinkyoku nara koko kara (　) no saki da kara aruite ikou.

Full sentence: 郵便局ならここから目と鼻の先だから歩いて行こう。Yuubinkyoku nara koko kara me to hana no saki da kara aruite ikou.
EN: The post office is just a stone's throw from here, so let's walk.
HI: डाकघर यहाँ से बिल्कुल पास है, पैदल चलते हैं।
GU: પોસ્ટ ઑફિસ અહીંથી સાવ નજીક છે, ચાલતા જઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1鼻と口hana to kuchinose and mouthनाक और मुँहનાક અને મોં
2目と耳me to mimieyes and earsआँख और कानઆંખ અને કાન
3目と鼻me to hana(目と鼻の先) very close(बिल्कुल पास)(સાવ નજીક)
4目と口me to kuchieyes and mouthआँख और मुँहઆંખ અને મોં

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 目と鼻の先 = as close as your eyes are to your nose. The other pairs are made up.

(4)

彼は顔が（　）から、彼に頼めば仕事を紹介してくれるだろう。Kare wa kao ga (　) kara, kare ni tanomeba shigoto o shoukai shite kureru darou.

Full sentence: 彼は顔が広いから、彼に頼めば仕事を紹介してくれるだろう。Kare wa kao ga hiroi kara, kare ni tanomeba shigoto o shoukai shite kureru darou.
EN: He knows a lot of people, so if you ask him he'll probably introduce you to a job.
HI: उसकी जान-पहचान बहुत है, उससे कहोगे तो शायद नौकरी दिलवा देगा।
GU: એની ઓળખાણ ઘણી છે, એને કહેશો તો કદાચ નોકરી અપાવી દેશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1長いnagailongलंबाલાંબું
2丸いmaruiroundगोलગોળ
3大きいookiibig (literal only)बड़ाમોટું
4広いhiroi(顔が広い) well connected(जान-पहचान ज़्यादा)(ઓળખાણ ઘણી)

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 顔が広い = know many people, which is why he can find you work. 顔が大きい is only literal (a big face); acting important is 大きな顔をする.

(5)

店主は私の（　）を見て、高い値段を吹っかけてきた。Tenshu wa watashi no (　) o mite, takai nedan o fukkakete kita.

Full sentence: 店主は私の足元を見て、高い値段を吹っかけてきた。Tenshu wa watashi no ashimoto o mite, takai nedan o fukkakete kita.
EN: The shopkeeper took advantage of my situation and demanded a high price.
HI: दुकानदार ने मेरी मजबूरी भाँपकर ऊँचा दाम माँग लिया।
GU: દુકાનદારે મારી મજબૂરી પારખીને ઊંચો ભાવ માગ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1手元temotoat handहाथ मेंહાથવગું
2口元kuchimotoaround the mouthहोंठों के पासહોઠ પાસે
3足元ashimoto(足元を見る) exploit weakness(मजबूरी का फ़ायदा)(મજબૂરીનો લાભ)
4目元memotoaround the eyesआँखों के पासઆંખો પાસે

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 足元を見る comes from porters who checked a traveller's tired feet before naming a price — so it means exploiting someone's weak position. 高い値段を吹っかける confirms it.

(6)

私は甘いものには（　）んですよ。Watashi wa amai mono ni wa (　) n desu yo.

Full sentence: 私は甘いものには目がないんですよ。Watashi wa amai mono ni wa me ga nai n desu yo.
EN: I just can't resist sweet things.
HI: मिठाइयों का तो मैं बेहद शौक़ीन हूँ।
GU: ગળી વસ્તુઓનો હું ખૂબ શોખીન છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1目じゃないme ja naiis no matchकुछ भी नहींવિસાતમાં નહીં
2目がないme ga nai(〜に目がない) can't resistबेहद शौक़ीनખૂબ શોખીન
3目にないme ni nai(not an idiom)(मुहावरा नहीं)(રૂઢિપ્રયોગ નહીં)
4目もないme mo nai(not an idiom)(मुहावरा नहीं)(રૂઢિપ્રયોગ નહીં)

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 〜に目がない = love something so much you lose judgement. 目じゃない (from the same list) means "no match for", e.g. 彼なんか目じゃない.

(7)

発見されたとき、彼女は（　）で、死にそうになっていたらしい。Hakken sareta toki, kanojo wa (　) de, shini sou ni natte ita rashii.

Full sentence: 発見されたとき、彼女は虫の息で、死にそうになっていたらしい。Hakken sareta toki, kanojo wa mushi no iki de, shini sou ni natte ita rashii.
EN: Apparently when she was found she was barely breathing and close to death.
HI: बताते हैं कि जब वह मिली तब आख़िरी साँसें ले रही थी, मरने के क़रीब थी।
GU: કહે છે કે એ મળી ત્યારે છેલ્લા શ્વાસ લેતી હતી, મરવાની અણી પર હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1虫の息mushi no ikifaint breath, near deathआख़िरी साँसेंછેલ્લા શ્વાસ
2魚の息sakana no iki(not an idiom)(मुहावरा नहीं)(રૂઢિપ્રયોગ નહીં)
3鳥の息tori no iki(not an idiom)(मुहावरा नहीं)(રૂઢિપ્રયોગ નહીં)
4犬の息inu no iki(not an idiom)(मुहावरा नहीं)(રૂઢિપ્રયોગ નહીં)

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 虫の息 = breath as faint as an insect's. Only 虫 forms the idiom; the other animals are invented distractors.

(8)

みんなが尻込みする中、彼は一人、給料を上げろと（　）を吐いていた。Minna ga shirigomi suru naka, kare wa hitori, kyuuryou o agero to (　) o haite ita.

Full sentence: みんなが尻込みする中、彼は一人、給料を上げろと気を吐いていた。Minna ga shirigomi suru naka, kare wa hitori, kyuuryou o agero to ki o haite ita.
EN: While everyone else hung back, he alone was spiritedly demanding a pay rise.
HI: जब सब पीछे हट रहे थे, वह अकेला जोश से वेतन बढ़ाने की माँग कर रहा था।
GU: બધા પાછા પડતા હતા ત્યારે એ એકલો જુસ્સાથી પગાર વધારવાની માગણી કરતો હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1血chiblood (血を吐く = cough up blood)ख़ूनલોહી
2口kuchimouthमुँहમોં
3息ikibreath (息を吐く = breathe out)साँसશ્વાસ
4気ki(気を吐く) show spiritजोशજુસ્સો

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 気を吐く = speak or act with conspicuous spirit, especially when others are timid. 息を吐く and 血を吐く are literal. Compare 弱音を吐く (whine) in IV ③.

(9)

怖そうに見えたが（　）を割って話をすると、いい人だということがわかった。Kowasou ni mieta ga (　) o watte hanashi o suru to, ii hito da to iu koto ga wakatta.

Full sentence: 怖そうに見えたが腹を割って話をすると、いい人だということがわかった。Kowasou ni mieta ga hara o watte hanashi o suru to, ii hito da to iu koto ga wakatta.
EN: He looked scary, but when we talked frankly I found he was a good person.
HI: वह डरावना लगता था, पर दिल खोलकर बात की तो पता चला कि अच्छा आदमी है।
GU: એ બિહામણો લાગતો હતો, પણ દિલ ખોલીને વાત કરી તો ખબર પડી કે સારો માણસ છે.

2顔kaofaceचेहराચહેરો

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 腹を割って話す = talk with complete honesty (the 腹 is where one's true intentions are kept; compare 腹が黒い, 腹を探る).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Same body part, opposite meanings — and different body parts that sound alike:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
顔が広い / 顔が利く / 顔が売れるkao ga hiroi / kao ga kiku / kao ga ureru well connected / influential / famous 広い = knows many people; 利く = their name opens doors; 売れる = many people know them. 顔が広いから紹介してくれる (Q4)
目がない / 目じゃないme ga nai / me ja nai love / no match 〜に目がない = can't resist; 〜なんか目じゃない = is nothing compared with me. 甘いものには目がない (Q6)
口が堅い / 口が軽い / 口が重いkuchi ga katai / karui / omoi discreet / loose-lipped / taciturn 堅い vs 軽い is about keeping secrets; 重い is about not talking much at all. 口が堅いから安心だ
耳をそろえる / 口をそろえるmimi o soroeru / kuchi o soroeru pay in full / say together Money vs speech, same verb. 耳をそろえて返す (Q10)
足元を見る / 足元にも及ばないashimoto o miru / ashimoto ni mo oyobanai exploit weakness / be no match The first is something done to you; the second compares ability. 足元を見て値段を吹っかける (Q5)
気を吐く / 弱音を吐く / 息を吐くki o haku / yowane o haku / iki o haku show spirit / whine / exhale 吐く takes opposite nouns: 気 = bold talk, 弱音 = complaints. 一人気を吐く (Q8)
腹を割る / 腹を探る / 腹が黒いhara o waru / saguru / ga kuroi be frank / sound out / scheming 腹 = one's real intentions: open them, probe them, or have dark ones. 腹を割って話す (Q9)
Exam trap — swapped body parts: five of the eleven questions (Q2, Q3, Q5, Q9, Q10) have four nouns with the same verb. Anchor each idiom to its picture: porters checking feet (足元を見る), coins with their edges lined up (耳をそろえる).
Exam trap — invented forms: 目にない, 目もない, 魚の息, 鳥の息 are not idioms. Changing one particle or animal is a favourite way to make distractors.
