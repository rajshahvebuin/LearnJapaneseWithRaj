1 Points — the word list, one row per sense

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One word changes its meaning with the situation, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set. The 例題 (つよい) and the full 解き方 for this pattern are on I ①.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ स्थिति के अनुसार बदलता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके याद करें। इस पैटर्न का 例題 (つよい) और पूरा 解き方 I ① पर है।

GU: એક જ શબ્દનો અર્થ પરિસ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે બદલાય છે, એટલે અર્થોને ગોઠવીને યાદ રાખો. આ પેટર્નનું 例題 (つよい) અને આખું 解き方 I ① પર છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.65)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

(1)

あたま……さあ、もういちどあたまから練習してみよう。Atama…… Saa, mou ichido atama kara renshuu shite miyou.

Sense used: ⑥ 一番最初 — the very beginning
EN: Right, let's practise once more from the top.
HI: चलो, एक बार फिर शुरू से अभ्यास करते हैं।
GU: ચાલો, ફરી એક વાર શરૂઆતથી અભ્યાસ કરીએ.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1鼻のあたまに汗をかいて、少女は一生懸命歩いていた。 → ③ top, tipHana no atama ni ase o kaite, shoujo wa isshoukenmei aruite ita.With sweat on the tip of her nose, the girl walked hard.नाक की नोक पर पसीना लिए लड़की पूरे ज़ोर से चल रही थी।નાકની ટોચે પરસેવો લઈને છોકરી પૂરા જોરથી ચાલતી હતી.
2今月は余分な出費があったので、赤字が出そうであたまが痛い。 → ① head (あたまが痛い = worry)Kongetsu wa yobun na shuppi ga atta node, akaji ga desou de atama ga itai.I had extra expenses this month and might go into the red — what a headache.इस महीने फ़ालतू ख़र्च हुआ, घाटा होने की आशंका से सिर-दर्द है।આ મહિને વધારાનો ખર્ચ થયો, ખોટ જવાની બીકે માથાનો દુખાવો છે.

4サッカーをしたいんだけど、あたま数がそろわないので、出てくれよ。 → ④ headcountSakkaa o shitai n da kedo, atamakazu ga sorowanai node, dete kure yo.We want to play football but we're short of players — join us.फ़ुटबॉल खेलना है पर लोग पूरे नहीं, तुम भी आ जाओ।ફૂટબૉલ રમવું છે પણ માણસો પૂરા નથી, તું પણ આવ.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) あたまから = "from the very start", and the opening of a song is the same idea. Option 1 is the physical top of the nose, option 2 the idiom あたまが痛い (worrying), and option 4 あたま数 (number of people).

4自動車教習所でおに教官にあたったので、なかなか合格できない。 → ④ strict (おに〜)Jidousha kyoushuujo de oni kyoukan ni atatta node, nakanaka goukaku dekinai.I got a strict instructor at driving school, so I can't pass.ड्राइविंग स्कूल में सख़्त प्रशिक्षक मिला, इसलिए पास नहीं हो पा रहा।ડ્રાઇવિંગ સ્કૂલમાં કડક શિક્ષક મળ્યા, એટલે પાસ નથી થવાતું.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) 〜のおに (仕事のおに, 練習のおに) praises someone who throws themselves into something. Option 4 おに教官 is close but means "strict towards others" (④). Option 1 is a real ogre, and option 2 is about being hard-hearted.

(4)

かお……彼女は我が社のかおです。Kao…… Kanojo wa wagasha no kao desu.

Sense used: ⑥ 代表 — the face, representative
EN: She is the face of our company.
HI: वह हमारी कंपनी का चेहरा है।
GU: તે અમારી કંપનીનો ચહેરો છે.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1親のかおを立てて、あいさつに行く。 → ③ honourOya no kao o tatete, aisatsu ni iku.To save my parents' face, I'll go and pay my respects.माता-पिता की इज़्ज़त रखने के लिए मैं मिलने जाऊँगा।માતાપિતાની આબરૂ રાખવા હું મળવા જઈશ.
2うちの祖父はこのあたりではかおだ。 → ⑤ well-known personUchi no sofu wa kono atari de wa kao da.My grandfather is a well-known figure around here.मेरे दादाजी इस इलाक़े में जाने-माने हैं।મારા દાદા આ વિસ્તારમાં જાણીતા છે.
3かおがそろったところで、会議を始めたいと思います。 → ④ membersKao ga sorotta tokoro de, kaigi o hajimetai to omoimasu.Now that everyone is here, I'd like to start the meeting.सब आ गए हैं, तो बैठक शुरू करना चाहूँगा।બધા આવી ગયા છે, તો બેઠક શરૂ કરવા માગું છું.
4今月号のかおとなる記事にクレームが付いた。 → ⑥ the face, representativeKongetsugou no kao to naru kiji ni kureemu ga tsuita.There was a complaint about the lead article of this month's issue.इस महीने के अंक के मुख्य लेख पर शिकायत आई।આ મહિનાના અંકના મુખ્ય લેખ પર ફરિયાદ આવી.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A person who represents a company and the lead article that represents a magazine issue are both its "face" (⑥). Option 2 is the closest trap: かおだ there means "well known locally" (⑤), not "representing" anything.

(5)

かげ……かげでこそこそうわさしていないで、堂々と言ったほうがいい。Kage…… Kage de kosokoso uwasa shite inaide, doudou to itta hou ga ii.

Sense used: ⑤ 目立たない場所 — behind the scenes, behind someone's back
EN: Don't gossip behind people's backs — say it openly.
HI: पीठ पीछे काना-फूसी करने के बजाय खुलकर कहना बेहतर है।
GU: પીઠ પાછળ ગુસપુસ કરવાને બદલે ખુલ્લેઆમ કહેવું સારું.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あの俳優には大女優だった母親のかげが見える。 → ⑨ trace, likenessAno haiyuu ni wa daijoyuu datta hahaoya no kage ga mieru.In that actor you can see something of his mother, a great actress.उस अभिनेता में उसकी महान अभिनेत्री माँ की झलक दिखती है।એ અભિનેતામાં તેની મહાન અભિનેત્રી માતાની ઝલક દેખાય છે.
2石油価格の上昇は、持ち直してきた景気にかげをさすものだ。 → ⑥ gloomSekiyu kakaku no joushou wa, mochinaoshite kita keiki ni kage o sasu mono da.Rising oil prices cast a shadow over the recovering economy.तेल के बढ़ते दाम सुधरती अर्थव्यवस्था पर छाया डालते हैं।તેલના વધતા ભાવ સુધરતા અર્થતંત્ર પર છાયા પાડે છે.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) かげで and かげながら both mean doing something where the person concerned doesn't see it: gossiping in one case, supporting in the other. Option 4 is a physical position behind a building, option 1 a likeness, and option 2 the idiom かげをさす "cast a shadow".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Nouns whose figurative senses English often renders with the same word ("face", "head", "shadow"):

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
あたま ③ / ⑥atama top, tip / beginning ③ is a position in space (鼻のあたま); ⑥ is a position in time or order (あたまから, 歌のあたま). Q1: あたまから = 歌のあたま
うち ④ / ⑤uchi our group / my home うちの + noun (会社, 生徒, 子) = the group I belong to; うちに来る / 帰る = the house. Q2: うちの会社 = うちの生徒
おに ③ / ④oni devoted / strict 〜のおに (after a noun) = devoted to it; おに〜 (before a noun: おにコーチ, おに教官) = harsh to others. Q3: 仕事のおに = 練習のおに
かお ③ / ⑤ / ⑥kao honour / well known / representative かおを立てる・つぶす (honour); かおがきく, 〜ではかおだ (known, influential); 〜のかお (representative). Q4: 我が社のかお = 今月号のかお
かげ ④ / ⑤ / ⑧kage shade / behind the scenes / behind ④ and ⑧ are physical places; ⑤ is figurative: out of sight of the person concerned (かげで, かげながら). Q5: かげで = かげながら
あな ③ / ⑤ana weak point / dark horse Both are figurative, with opposite feelings: あながある (a flaw) vs この馬はあなだ (an unnoticed good bet). 攻撃力にあながある · この馬はあなだ
Exam trap — a pattern of answers: the answer is 3 for items 1, 2, 3 and 5 here. Don't let a run of identical answers make you doubt yourself; each item is independent.
Exam trap — fixed idioms inside the options: あたまが痛い (worried), 心をおににする (harden one's heart), かおを立てる (save face) and かげをさす (cast a shadow) are idioms. Learn them as whole phrases so you can rule them out quickly. (added, not in book)
