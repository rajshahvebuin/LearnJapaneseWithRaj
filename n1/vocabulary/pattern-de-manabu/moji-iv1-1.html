Notes
First spread of IV-1. This page lists kanji ending in く that become っ before a か-row kanji; the つ group is on IV-1 ②. The 例題 uses 発 (つ) to explain the whole rule. The key gives answer numbers only, so every "Why" line is our explanation.

1 Points — 例題 and the く + か行 list

例題 — worked example (p.44)

この本は発行日と発売日がちがいます。Kono hon wa hakkoubi to hatsubaibi ga chigaimasu.

EN: This book's publication date and release date are different. HI: इस किताब की प्रकाशन तिथि और बिक्री तिथि अलग हैं। GU: આ પુસ્તકની પ્રકાશન તારીખ અને વેચાણ તારીખ જુદી છે.

Answer 1 (book's 解き方). The book explains: 発 is はつ, ending in つ. In [1] the next kanji 行 (こう) is in the か-row, so つ becomes っ; in [2] 売 (ばい) is in the ば-row, so it does not. The small っ is always the second sound. Answers: [1] 3, [2] 1.

ヒント (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Look at the sound at the end of the first kanji. If it ends in く and the next kanji starts with a か-row sound, or it ends in つ and the next kanji starts with a か・さ・た・ぱ-row sound, the く / つ turns into a small っ. The small っ is always the second sound of the word.

HI: पहले कांजी की अंतिम ध्वनि देखें। अगर वह く पर ख़त्म हो और अगला कांजी か-पंक्ति से शुरू हो, या वह つ पर ख़त्म हो और अगला कांजी か・さ・た・ぱ-पंक्ति से शुरू हो, तो く / つ छोटा っ बन जाता है। छोटा っ हमेशा शब्द की दूसरी ध्वनि होती है।

GU: પહેલા કાંજીનો છેલ્લો અવાજ જુઓ. જો તે く પર પૂરો થાય અને પછીનો કાંજી か-હરોળથી શરૂ થાય, અથવા તે つ પર પૂરો થાય અને પછીનો કાંજી か・さ・た・ぱ-હરોળથી શરૂ થાય, તો く / つ નાનો っ બની જાય છે. નાનો っ હંમેશાં શબ્દનો બીજો અવાજ હોય છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The same first kanji can be read with or without っ, depending on the next kanji:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
悪化 / 悪影響akka / akueikyouworsening / bad effectか-row after 悪 → っ; a vowel (え) after it → no change.Q1, Q2
作曲 / 作詞sakkyoku / sakushicomposing / lyricsく + か-row → っ; く + さ-row → no change (only つ doubles before さ-row).Q9, Q10
借金 / 借用shakkin / shakuyoudebt / borrowingき (か-row) → っ; よ (や-row) → no change.Q13, Q14
薬局 / 服用yakkyoku / fukuyoupharmacy / taking medicineSame logic: きょ → っ, よ → none.Q15, Q17
職権 / 食券shokken / shokkenauthority / meal ticketTwo different words with the same reading — both follow the rule.Q11
Exam trap — the っ is always the second sound: distractors like さこっん, こっこっう, しゃっきっん put the っ in the wrong place or twice. Only one っ, right after the first syllable.
Exam trap — く before さ-row: 作詞 さくし does not double. For く, only the か-row triggers っ (for つ, see IV-1 ②).
