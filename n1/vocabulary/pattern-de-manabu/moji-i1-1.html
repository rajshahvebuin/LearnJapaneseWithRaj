1 Points — the 例題 and the same-reading word list

例題 — the two question types of this pattern (p.8)

例題1：次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、ひらがなでどう書きますか。同じひらがなで書く言葉を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Reidai 1: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, hiragana de dou kakimasu ka. Onaji hiragana de kaku kotoba o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is the underlined word written in hiragana? Choose the word written with the same hiragana. HI: रेखांकित शब्द हिरागाना में कैसे लिखा जाता है? वही हिरागाना वाला शब्द चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ હિરાગાનામાં કેવી રીતે લખાય? એ જ હિરાગાનાવાળો શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

例題1

すぐに救急車が来るので、もう少し辛抱してください。Sugu ni kyuukyuusha ga kuru node, mou sukoshi shinbou shite kudasai.

EN: The ambulance will be here soon, so please hang on a little longer.
HI: एम्बुलेंस जल्दी आ रही है, इसलिए थोड़ा और धैर्य रखिए।
GU: એમ્બ્યુલન્સ જલદી આવે છે, એટલે થોડી વધુ ધીરજ રાખો.

Answer 2 (book's 解き方, p.8). Book's 解き方 (paraphrased): 辛抱 is read しんぼう, so the answer is 2. (added, not in book) 信奉 is しんぽう (a ぽ, not a ぼ), and 介抱 shares the kanji 抱 but is read かいほう. Matching the kanji does not help here; only the sound counts.

EN: Which kanji is the underlined word written with? Choose it from 1–4. HI: रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

例題2

点検の結果、いじょうが見つかった。Tenken no kekka, ijou ga mitsukatta.

Full sentence: 点検の結果、異状が見つかった。Tenken no kekka, ijou ga mitsukatta.
EN: The inspection turned up something wrong.
HI: जाँच में एक गड़बड़ी पाई गई।
GU: તપાસમાં એક ગરબડ મળી આવી.
OptionWordEnglishHindiGujarati
1以上ijoumore than; the aboveसे अधिक; उपर्युक्तથી વધુ; ઉપર્યુક્ત

Answer 4 (book's 解き方, p.8). Book's 解き方 (paraphrased): what an inspection "finds" is a state that is not normal, so the answer is 4. (added, not in book) All four options are read いじょう, so you choose by meaning. 異状 is a noun for a concrete fault you can find; compare 異常 in the list below.

ヒント — the book's tip (summary, not the book's text)

EN: When kanji look alike and share a reading, learn them inside compound words and example sentences, not as single characters.

HI: जब कांजी एक जैसे दिखें और उनका उच्चारण भी एक हो, तो उन्हें अकेले नहीं बल्कि संयुक्त शब्दों और उदाहरण-वाक्यों के साथ याद करें।

GU: જ્યારે કાંજી સરખા દેખાય અને તેમનું ઉચ્ચારણ પણ એક જ હોય, ત્યારે તેમને એકલા નહીં પણ સંયુક્ત શબ્દો અને ઉદાહરણ-વાક્યો સાથે યાદ રાખો.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.9)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

EN: I don't see the point of holding this meeting.
HI: मुझे यह बैठक करने का मतलब समझ नहीं आता।
GU: મને આ બેઠક કરવાનો અર્થ સમજાતો નથી.

(6)

(7)

Full sentence: あの女優の哀感の表情が人々の涙を誘っている。Ano joyuu no aikan no hyoujou ga hitobito no namida o sasotte iru.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) A face that brings tears shows sadness only, which is 哀感 (哀 "sorrow" + 感 "feeling"). 哀歓 is "joys and sorrows" shared over time (哀歓を共にする), so it cannot describe a single sad expression.

(8)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above actually test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
意志 / 意思 / 遺志ishi will / intention / wishes of the dead 意志 is resolve and can be 強い or 弱い. 意思 is simply what you mean or want (意思表示, 意思の疎通). 遺志 is a wish left behind by someone who has died. 意志が強い · 意思を伝える · 父の遺志を継ぐ (Q2)
異常 / 異状ijou abnormal / an abnormal condition 異常 is a general quality and works as a な-adjective (異常な音, 異常気象). 異状 is a noun for a specific fault that is found or reported (異状が見つかる, 異状なし). 異常な暑さ · 点検で異状が見つかった (例題2)
委譲 / 移譲ijou delegate / transfer 委譲 hands authority (権限) down to someone, who then acts for you. 移譲 moves ownership of rights or property to another party. 権限を委譲する (Q12) · 土地を移譲する
移動 / 異動 / 異同idou movement / personnel transfer / differences 移動 is a physical move. 異動 is a change of post inside an organisation. 異同 is a noun for points of difference. 車を移動する · 人事異動 (Q3) · 二者の異同
懐古 / 回顧kaiko nostalgia / looking back 懐古 is a general, emotional longing for "the good old days". 回顧 reviews something specific in your or someone's past (業績, 作品, 生涯). 懐古趣味 · 業績を回顧する (Q5)
英気 / 鋭気eiki energy / fighting spirit 英気 is reserve energy you build up by resting. 鋭気 is the sharp, aggressive spirit you bring against an opponent. 英気を養う (Q4) · 鋭気をくじく
哀感 / 哀歓aikan sadness / joys and sorrows 哀感 is sadness only (哀 + 感 "feeling"). 哀歓 is both joy (歓) and sorrow (哀), usually shared over a long time. 哀感の表情 (Q7) · 哀歓を共にする
会心 / 改心kaishin satisfaction / reform 会心 means "matches your heart", i.e. exactly what you wanted (会心の作). 改心 means "changes the heart", i.e. stops doing wrong. 会心の演技 (Q6) · 罪人が改心する
異議 / 異義 / 意義igi objection / different meaning / significance 異議 is an objection you raise (唱える, 申し立てる). 異義 hardly appears outside 同音異義語. 意義 is the point or value of something. 異議あり · 同音異義語 · 〜する意義がある (Q1)
Exam trap — fake words: in "choose the kanji" questions the book mixes real homophones with invented compounds: 移道 (Q3), 栄気・影気 (Q4), 懐顧 (Q5), 快心 (Q6, not the standard form), 愛感・愛環 (Q7), 開掃 (Q8). Cross out the options you have never seen as a word first. You are usually left with two real homophones, and you choose between them by collocation.
問題Ⅱ strategy: read the underlined word aloud in your head, then read each option. The distractors differ by exactly one sound: a different consonant (いっこん / いっけん vs いっかん), a missing dakuten (いきょう vs いぎょう), or a long vowel (いちりょう vs いちり). Do not choose by shared kanji: 偉業 → 遺業 shares 業, but so do 兼業 and 修業.
