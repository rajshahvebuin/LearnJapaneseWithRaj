ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One word changes its meaning with the situation, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set. The 例題 (つよい) and the full 解き方 for this pattern are on I ①.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ स्थिति के अनुसार बदलता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके याद करें। इस पैटर्न का 例題 (つよい) और पूरा 解き方 I ① पर है।

GU: એક જ શબ્દનો અર્થ પરિસ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે બદલાય છે, એટલે અર્થોને ગોઠવીને યાદ રાખો. આ પેટર્નનું 例題 (つよい) અને આખું 解き方 I ① પર છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.83)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

(1)

まわる……寿司がカウンターの上をまわっているのを初めて見た時はびっくりした。Mawaru…… Sushi ga kauntaa no ue o mawatte iru no o hajimete mita toki wa bikkuri shita.

Sense used: ② 周りを丸く動く — go around
EN: The first time I saw sushi going round on the counter, I was amazed.
HI: पहली बार काउंटर पर सुशी को चक्कर लगाते देखकर मैं हैरान रह गया।
GU: પહેલી વાર કાઉન્ટર પર સુશીને ગોળ ફરતી જોઈને હું નવાઈ પામ્યો.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1丘の上には風力発電の風車がまわっていた。 → ① spinOka no ue ni wa fuuryoku hatsuden no fuusha ga mawatte ita.Wind turbines were turning on top of the hill.पहाड़ी पर पवन-चक्कियाँ घूम रही थीं।ટેકરી પર પવનચક્કીઓ ફરતી હતી.
2新社長は、就任の挨拶にまわっている。 → ④ make the roundsShinshachou wa, shuunin no aisatsu ni mawatte iru.The new president is making the rounds to introduce himself.नए अध्यक्ष पद-ग्रहण की औपचारिक मुलाक़ातों पर घूम रहे हैं।નવા પ્રમુખ પદગ્રહણની ઔપચારિક મુલાકાતોએ ફરે છે.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Conveyor-belt sushi travels round the counter, and the earth travels round the sun; both move along a loop. Option 1 is the close trap: a windmill spins on its own axis (①) and does not travel. Options 2 and 3 are about going to places.

(2)

もつ……結婚した時、みんなから絶対にもたない、すぐ離婚すると言われた。Motsu…… Kekkon shita toki, minna kara zettai ni motanai, sugu rikon suru to iwareta.

Sense used: ⑦ その状態を保ち続ける — last
EN: When we married, everyone said it would never last and we'd soon divorce.
HI: शादी के समय सबने कहा था कि यह कभी नहीं टिकेगी, जल्दी तलाक़ होगा।

OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A marriage that won't last and a house that will last 50 years both use もつ with no object, meaning to keep going. Options 1–3 are transitive (〜をもつ): carry, hold, have.

3この地方では炭をやいて生計を立てている。 → ③ make by firingKono chihou de wa sumi o yaite seikei o tatete iru.In this region people make a living making charcoal.इस इलाक़े में लोग कोयला बनाकर रोज़ी चलाते हैं।આ વિસ્તારમાં લોકો કોલસો બનાવીને રોજી ચલાવે છે.
4海岸でオイルを塗って体をやいた。 → ④ tanKaigan de oiru o nutte karada o yaita.I put on oil and tanned on the beach.समुद्र-तट पर तेल लगाकर मैंने धूप सेंकी।દરિયાકિનારે તેલ લગાવીને મેં તડકો ખાધો.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Burning letters and burning rubbish both destroy something with fire. Option 3 also uses fire, but to produce charcoal (③), not to get rid of something. Option 1 is cooking and option 4 tanning.

(4)

よぶ……この池は「鏡池」とよばれている。Yobu…… Kono ike wa "Kagami-ike" to yobarete iru.

Sense used: ④ 名づける — call, name
EN: This pond is called "Mirror Pond".
HI: इस तालाब को "दर्पण-तालाब" कहा जाता है।
GU: આ તળાવને "દર્પણ-તળાવ" કહેવાય છે.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1名前をよんだら、大きい声で返事をして立って下さい。 → ① call outNamae o yondara, ookii koe de henji o shite tatte kudasai.When I call your name, answer loudly and stand up.नाम पुकारा जाए तो ज़ोर से जवाब देकर खड़े हो जाइए।નામ બોલાય ત્યારે મોટેથી જવાબ આપીને ઊભા થાઓ.
2父が倒れた時、初めて救急車をよんだ。 → ② send forChichi ga taoreta toki, hajimete kyuukyuusha o yonda.When my father collapsed, I called an ambulance for the first time.पिताजी गिर पड़े तो मैंने पहली बार एम्बुलेंस बुलाई।પિતાજી પડી ગયા ત્યારે મેં પહેલી વાર ઍમ્બ્યુલન્સ બોલાવી.
3彼女は何でも知っているので、みんなは「博士」とよんでいる。 → ④ call, nameKanojo wa nan demo shitte iru node, minna wa "Hakase" to yonde iru.She knows everything, so everyone calls her "the Professor".वह सब कुछ जानती है, इसलिए सब उसे "प्रोफ़ेसर" कहते हैं।તે બધું જાણે છે, એટલે બધા તેને "પ્રોફેસર" કહે છે.
4この商品はダイエットにきくと人気をよんでいる。 → ⑤ cause, drawKono shouhin wa daietto ni kiku to ninki o yonde iru.This product is popular because people say it helps with dieting.यह उत्पाद वज़न घटाने में असरदार माना जाता है, इसलिए लोकप्रिय है।આ ઉત્પાદન વજન ઘટાડવામાં અસરકારક મનાય છે, એટલે લોકપ્રિય છે.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) 「鏡池」とよぶ and 「博士」とよぶ are both the pattern 「name」とよぶ, giving something a name. Option 1 calls out a name that already exists, option 2 sends for an ambulance, and option 4 is 人気をよぶ (draw popularity).

(5)

わる……今月の売上は先月の半数をわった。Waru…… Kongetsu no uriage wa sengetsu no hansuu o watta.

Sense used: ⑦ ある基準より低くなる — fall below
EN: This month's sales fell below half of last month's.
HI: इस महीने की बिक्री पिछले महीने की आधी से भी कम रही।
GU: આ મહિનાનું વેચાણ ગયા મહિનાના અડધાથી પણ ઓછું રહ્યું.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1すみません。コップをわってしまいました。 → ① breakSumimasen. Koppu o watte shimaimashita.I'm sorry, I broke a glass.माफ़ कीजिए, मुझसे गिलास टूट गया।માફ કરજો, મારાથી ગ્લાસ તૂટી ગયો.
2卵をわって、味をつけて卵焼きを作る。 → ① crackTamago o watte, aji o tsukete tamagoyaki o tsukuru.Crack the eggs, season them, and make a rolled omelette.अंडे फोड़कर, मसाला डालकर ऑमलेट बनाना।ઈંડાં ફોડીને, મસાલો નાખીને ઑમલેટ બનાવવી.
3学生数は定員をわってしまった。 → ⑦ fall belowGakuseisuu wa teiin o watte shimatta.Student numbers fell below the quota.छात्रों की संख्या निर्धारित सीमा से नीचे चली गई।વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સંખ્યા નિર્ધારિત મર્યાદાથી નીચે ગઈ.
4ウィスキーをソーダでわって飲むのが好きだ。 → ④ diluteWisukii o sooda de watte nomu no ga suki da.I like drinking whisky mixed with soda.मुझे व्हिस्की सोडा मिलाकर पीना पसंद है।મને વ્હિસ્કી સોડા ભેળવીને પીવી ગમે છે.

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Sales below half and student numbers below the quota both mean a figure dropping under a standard (〜をわる + a number or limit). Options 1 and 2 are breaking things, and option 4 is mixing a drink.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Verbs where transitivity or the noun type decides the sense:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
まわる ① / ②mawaru spin / go around ① turns in place (風車, こま); ② travels along a loop round something (〜の周りを, カウンターの上を). Q1: 寿司 = 地球
もつ ①–⑥ / ⑦motsu have, hold / last 〜をもつ (with an object) = hold, carry, have; 〜がもつ or a length of time + もつ (no object) = last. Q2: 結婚がもたない = 家が50年もつ
やく ① / ③yaku burn up / make by firing Both use fire. ① destroys (手紙, ゴミ); ③ produces (炭, 陶器). Q3: 手紙 = ゴミ
よぶ ① / ④yobu call out / name 名前をよぶ (call out a name) vs 「X」とよぶ (call something X). Q4: 「鏡池」と = 「博士」と
わる ① / ⑦waru break / fall below Physical object (コップ, 卵) → ①; a number or standard (半数, 定員, 1万円) → ⑦. Q5: 半数 = 定員
よむ ④ / ⑤yomu read minds / read ahead 心・気持ちをよむ (guess feelings) vs 先・手をよむ (predict moves). (added, not in book) 人の心をよむ · 先をよむ
Exam trap — motion in place vs motion around: in Q1 a spinning windmill and conveyor-belt sushi both まわる, but only one travels along a path. The 別冊 key chooses the earth orbiting the sun.
Exam trap — the same tool, a different purpose: in Q3 three options involve fire (肉, ゴミ, 炭). Ask what the fire is for: cooking, destroying or producing. (added, not in book)
