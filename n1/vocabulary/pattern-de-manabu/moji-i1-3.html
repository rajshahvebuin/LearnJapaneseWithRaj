ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Same sound, different kanji: learn each word inside its compound and example phrase, not as a single character. The 例題 for this pattern are on I-1 ①.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.13)

問題Ⅰ — choose the kanji

次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字をそれぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Which kanji is each underlined word written with? Choose from 1–4. HI: हर रेखांकित शब्द किस कांजी से लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से चुनें। GU: દરેક રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કયા કાંજીથી લખાય? 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો.

(1)

有名な小説家のこうえんを聞きに行く。Yuumei na shousetsuka no kouen o kiki ni iku.

Full sentence: 有名な小説家の講演を聞きに行く。Yuumei na shousetsuka no kouen o kiki ni iku.
EN: I'm going to hear a lecture by a famous novelist.
HI: मैं एक प्रसिद्ध उपन्यासकार का व्याख्यान सुनने जा रहा हूँ।
GU: હું એક પ્રખ્યાત નવલકથાકારનું વ્યાખ્યાન સાંભળવા જાઉં છું.
OptionKanjiEnglishHindiGujarati
1講演kouenlecture, talkव्याख्यानવ્યાખ્યાન
2口演kouenoral performance (rakugo)मौखिक प्रस्तुतिમૌખિક પ્રસ્તુતિ

4好演kouena fine performance (by an actor)बढ़िया अभिनयસરસ અભિનય

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) A novelist gives a talk, and you "go to hear" (聞きに行く) a talk: 講演 (講 = lecture, as in 講義). 公演 is a play or concert you "see"; 口演 is a storyteller's performance.

Full sentence: このアパートの台所は共同で使用することになっている。Kono apaato no daidokoro wa kyoudou de shiyou suru koto ni natte iru.
EN: In this apartment building the kitchen is shared.
HI: इस अपार्टमेंट में रसोई सब मिलकर इस्तेमाल करते हैं।
GU: આ એપાર્ટમેન્ટમાં રસોડું બધા સાથે મળીને વાપરે છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every set in today's list sounds identical, so only meaning and collocation can tell them apart. These are the sets the questions above test, plus the classic exam pairs from the list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
講演 / 公演 / 口演kouen lecture / stage show / storytelling 講演 is a talk you listen to. 公演 is a play, concert or dance shown to the public. 口演 is a spoken performance such as rakugo. 小説家の講演 (Q1) · 劇団の公演
共同 / 協同 / 協働kyoudou shared / cooperative / collaboration 共同 = doing or owning something together (共同で使う, 共同生活). 協同 and 協働 (協 = cooperate) stress partners joining forces. 台所は共同 (Q2) · 産学協同
既成 / 既製kisei established / ready-made 既成 is for ideas, facts and groups (既成概念, 既成政党). 既製 is for manufactured goods (既製服). 既成の考え方 (Q7) · 既製品
決裁 / 決済kessai approval / payment 決裁 is a superior's decision to approve. 決済 settles a payment or transaction. 上司の決裁 (Q8) · カード決済
健診 / 検診 / 検針kenshin check-up / screening / meter reading 健診 = a general health check. 検診 = an examination for a specific disease. 検針 = reading a utility meter. 水道の検針 (Q5) · がん検診
極地 / 局地 / 極致kyokuchi polar region / local area / the height 極地 = the poles and other extreme places. 局地 = a limited area (局地的). 極致 = the highest degree (美の極致). 極地探検 (Q6) · 局地的な大雨
起点 / 基点kiten starting point / reference point 起点 is where a route or process begins. 基点 is the fixed point you measure distances from. 路線の起点 · ここを基点に (Q11)
好意 / 厚意koui liking / kindness 好意 is your warm feeling towards someone. 厚意 is the kindness someone shows you, often in polite thanks (ご厚意). 好意を抱く · ご厚意に感謝します
究明 / 糾明kyuumei get to the bottom of / investigate wrongdoing 究明 looks for the truth or cause (原因究明). 糾明 (糾 = question closely) exposes a crime or misconduct. 真相を究明する · 不正を糾明する
Exam trap — readings that break the pattern: 形相 (Q9) is ぎょうそう, not けいそう, even though 形 is けい in 形成 and 形勢. In the same way, 決 drops to けっ before s- and t- sounds (決戦, 決闘) but stays けつ in 決意 (Q13). A kanji you "know" can switch reading inside a fixed word. In 問題Ⅱ, check each option's actual reading, not the reading you expect from one kanji.
問題Ⅰ strategy: this page's questions are all-real-word sets (Q2–Q7), so cross-outs are rare. Look for the collocation in the sentence: 聞きに行く → 講演, 共同で使用 → 共同, 水道の → 検針, 探検 → 極地, 上司の〜を待つ → 決裁.
