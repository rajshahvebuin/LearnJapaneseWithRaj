1 Points — 〈その他の決まり言葉〉 (p.104)

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Fixed expressions must be learnt whole: the noun, the particle and the verb all belong together (舟をこぐ = nod off, not "row a boat"). For the repetition phrases in list Ⅱ, notice how the pair works: opposites (大なり小なり), the same word twice (みんながみんな), or a sound pair (ピンからキリまで). The 例題 is on IV ①.

HI: तय मुहावरों को पूरा याद करें: संज्ञा, कण और क्रिया साथ चलते हैं (舟をこぐ = ऊँघना, "नाव चलाना" नहीं)। सूची Ⅱ के दोहराव वाले वाक्यांशों में जोड़ी का तरीक़ा देखें: विपरीत (大なり小なり), वही शब्द दो बार (みんながみんな), या ध्वनि-जोड़ी (ピンからキリまで)।

GU: નક્કી રૂઢિપ્રયોગો આખા યાદ રાખો: સંજ્ઞા, કણ અને ક્રિયા સાથે જ ચાલે (舟をこぐ = ઝોકાં ખાવાં, "હોડી ચલાવવી" નહીં). સૂચિ Ⅱ ના પુનરાવર્તન-વાક્યાંશોમાં જોડીની રીત જુઓ: વિરોધી (大なり小なり), એ જ શબ્દ બે વાર (みんながみんな), કે અવાજ-જોડી (ピンからキリまで).

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ・Ⅱ (p.105)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿の部分に入れるのに最も適当なものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ no bubun ni ireru no ni mottomo tekitou na mono o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the most suitable word for the blank. HI: रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त शब्द चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

(1)

彼は人が（　）から、手伝いを頼まれると、すぐ引き受けてしまう。Kare wa hito ga (　) kara, tetsudai o tanomareru to, sugu hikiukete shimau.

Full sentence: 彼は人がいいから、手伝いを頼まれると、すぐ引き受けてしまう。Kare wa hito ga ii kara, tetsudai o tanomareru to, sugu hikiukete shimau.
EN: He's so good-natured that whenever someone asks for help, he says yes straight away.
HI: वह इतना भला है कि कोई मदद माँगे तो तुरंत हाँ कर देता है।
GU: એ એટલો ભલો છે કે કોઈ મદદ માગે તો તરત હા પાડી દે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いいii(人がいい) good-naturedभलाભલો
2悪いwarui(人が悪い) meanबुराખરાબ
3ばかbakafoolishमूर्खમૂરખ
4甘いamaisoft, lenientनरमનરમ

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.4). (added, not in book) 人がいい = kind and a little too trusting, so he can't refuse — the しまう ending hints at that weakness. 人がばか / 人が甘い are not set phrases.

(2)

午後からの授業では、（　）学生が多い。Gogo kara no jugyou de wa, (　) gakusei ga ooi.

Full sentence: 午後からの授業では、舟をこぐ学生が多い。Gogo kara no jugyou de wa, fune o kogu gakusei ga ooi.
EN: In afternoon classes, lots of students nod off.
HI: दोपहर की कक्षाओं में बहुत से छात्र ऊँघते हैं।

(6)

EN: Nothing to it; brushing aside a difficulty. HI: कुछ भी नहीं; मुश्किल को चुटकी में उड़ाना। GU: કંઈ જ નહીં; મુશ્કેલીને ચપટીમાં ઉડાડવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1漢字の読み方がわからず、先生に何のそのと聞いた。Kanji no yomikata ga wakarazu, sensei ni nan no sono to kiita.I didn't know the kanji reading, so I asked the teacher "nan no sono". 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
人がいい / 人が悪い / 人聞きが悪いhito ga ii / hito ga warui / hitogiki ga warui good-natured / mean / sounds bad 人がいい often hints "too nice, easily used"; 人が悪い = sly, teasing; 人聞きが悪い is about reputation ("don't say it like that!"). 人がいいからすぐ引き受ける (Q1)
どっちもどっち / 似たり寄ったり / 持ちつ持たれつdocchi mo docchi / nitari yottari / mochitsu motaretsu both to blame / much the same / mutual help どっちもどっち is a judgement of two people's faults; 似たり寄ったり compares things (usually mediocre); 持ちつ持たれつ is positive interdependence. 殴ったほうも悪い、どっちもどっちだ (Q5)
夜が更ける / 夜が明けるyo ga fukeru / yo ga akeru night grows late / day breaks Opposite ends of the night; both use が and a fixed verb. 夜も更けてまいりました (Q3)
猫をかぶる / 白を切る / さばを読むneko o kaburu / shira o kiru / saba o yomu hide one's nature / feign ignorance / fudge numbers Three kinds of pretending: about your character, about what you know, about figures (age, count). 知らない人の前では猫をかぶる (Q8)
めがねにかなう / 口に合うmegane ni kanau / kuchi ni au pass someone's judgement / suit one's taste めがね = an expert's eye (people, work); 口 = food. 監督のめがねにかなう (Q7)
Exam trap — literal readings: 舟をこぐ is not about boats, めがねにかなう is not about glasses, and 猫をかぶる is not about cats. 問題Ⅱ always includes options built on the literal picture.
Exam trap — parts of other phrases: Q4 offers ツー and カー, which come from ツーといえばカー in the same list. Learn each repetition phrase as one unit.
