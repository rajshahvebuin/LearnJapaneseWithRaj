Notes
Only spread of pattern II-2. Unlike II-1 (same reading), these kanji look alike but are read differently, so the question gives a word in hiragana and you choose the kanji that both matches the sound and makes sense. Several distractors are not real words (重投, 服製, 角漠, 真縁, 穏居, 祖織, 緩除, 俊速, 急隆, 悦利 …); they are marked below. The book's key gives answer numbers only, so every "Why" line is our explanation.

1 Points — 例題 and the look-alike kanji list

例題 — worked example (p.36)

この街の産業はすっかりすいたいしてしまった。Kono machi no sangyou wa sukkari suitai shite shimatta.

EN: This town's industry has completely declined. HI: इस शहर का उद्योग पूरी तरह से गिरावट में चला गया है। GU: આ શહેરનો ઉદ્યોગ સાવ પડી ભાંગ્યો છે.

OptionKanjiReading / meaning
1衷退(chuu-tai)衷 is チュウ (inner heart) — not a word (added, not in book)
2衰退suitaidecline, decay — 衰 スイ "weaken"
3哀退(ai-tai)哀 is アイ (sorrow) — not a word (added, not in book)
4喪退(sou-tai)喪 is ソウ (mourning, loss) — not a word (added, not in book)

Answer 2 (book's 解き方). The book says: すいたい means 「衰えてなくなること」 (to weaken and fade away), so think from the meaning of the character and the answer is 2.

ヒント (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Look-alike kanji are easy to confuse, so learn each one together with its meaning and inside a compound word (哀歓, 衰退, 喪失, 衷心).

HI: एक जैसे दिखने वाले कांजी में अक्सर गलती होती है, इसलिए हर कांजी को उसके अर्थ के साथ और किसी संयुक्त शब्द के अंदर याद करें।

GU: સરખા દેખાતા કાંજીમાં ઘણી વાર ભૂલ થાય છે, એટલે દરેક કાંજીને તેના અર્થ સાથે અને કોઈ સંયુક્ત શબ્દની અંદર યાદ રાખો.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.37)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのような漢字を書きますか。その漢字を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you na kanji o kakimasu ka. Sono kanji o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) Family 5 (縁・緑・録). Only 緑 is read リョク; also 新 (しん, new) fits the May scene better than 真 (true).

(5)

祖父はいんきょして、のんびり暮らしております。Sofu wa inkyo shite, nonbiri kurashite orimasu.

Full sentence: 祖父は隠居して、のんびり暮らしております。Sofu wa inkyo shite, nonbiri kurashite orimasu.
EN: My grandfather has retired and lives a relaxed life.
HI: मेरे दादा सेवानिवृत्त होकर आराम से रह रहे हैं।
GU: મારા દાદા નિવૃત્ત થઈને આરામથી રહે છે.
OptionKanjiReading → meaningHindiGujarati
1隠居inkyo隠 イン (hide) → retirement from active lifeसेवानिवृत्तिનિવૃત્તિ
2穏居(on-kyo)穏 is オン — not a wordशब्द नहीं हैશબ્દ નથી
3陰居(in-kyo)sound fits, but 陰 (shade) is wrong — not the wordशब्द नहीं हैશબ્દ નથી
4院居(in-kyo)院 (institution, not in the list) — not a wordशब्द नहीं हैશબ્દ નથી

Answer 1 (book key, 別冊 p.2). (added, not in book) 隠居 = "hidden dwelling" = withdrawing from work. 陰 and 院 are also read イン, so here the meaning decides: 隠 (hide) fits, 陰 (shade) and 院 (hall) do not.

(6)

Full sentence: 顕微鏡で植物標本の組織を調べる。Kenbikyou de shokubutsu hyouhon no soshiki o shiraberu.
EN: To examine the tissue of a plant specimen under a microscope.

(7)

(8)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In this pattern the shared part is only a shape, not a sound. The radical (left or bottom part) tells you the meaning, and the reading has to be learned per kanji. These are the families the ten questions test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
役 / 投 / 没 / 段yaku / tou / botsu / dan role / throw / sink / step Same 殳 on the right; left part 彳 (go) → duty, 扌 → throw, 氵 → sink, ⺁ → steps. Four different readings. 重役 (Q1) · 投手 · 水没 · 段階
装 / 製 / 裂 / 袋sou / sei / retsu / tai dress / make / tear / bag All have 衣 (cloth) underneath; the top part gives the sound: 壮 ソウ, 制 セイ, 列 レツ, 代 タイ. 服装 (Q2) · 製造 · 決裂 · 薬袋
膜 / 漠 / 模maku / baku / mo membrane / vague / model 月 flesh → body membrane; 氵 → vast, vague (砂漠); 木 → a model or pattern. 角膜 (Q3) · 漠然 · 模型
緑 / 縁 / 録ryoku / en / roku green / connection / record 緑 and 縁 both have 糸 but the right part differs slightly (彔 vs 彖); 録 has 金. 緑 is ロク in 緑青 only. 新緑 (Q4) · 血縁 · 記録
隠 / 陰 / 穏in / in / on hide / shade / calm 隠 and 陰 are both イン — distinguish by meaning (hide vs shadow). 穏 (禾) is オン: calm. 隠居 (Q5) · 陰影 · 穏当
織 / 識 / 職shoku, shiki / shiki / shoku weave / know / job 糸 → weave (ショク, but シキ in 組織); 言 → recognise; 耳 → occupation. 組織 (Q6) · 意識 · 職業
援 / 緩 / 暖en / kan / dan help / loose / warm 扌 → a helping hand; 糸 → slack thread (loosen, slow); 日 → warmth. 援助 (Q7) · 緩和 · 暖房
俊 / 唆 / 酸shun / sa / san excellent / instigate / acid 亻 → a talented person; 口 → whispering suggestions (示唆); 酉 → sour, acid. 俊足 (Q8) · 示唆 · 酸素
陵 / 隆 / 降ryou / ryuu / kou hill, tomb / rise / descend All 阝 (mound). リョウ vs リュウ is a long-vowel trap: 丘陵 きゅうりょう, 隆起 りゅうき. 丘陵 (Q9) · 隆盛 · 下降
鋭 / 悦 / 税 / 脱ei / etsu / zei / datsu sharp / joy / tax / take off 金 → sharp metal; 忄 → feeling of joy; 禾 → grain paid as tax; 月 → the body sheds. 鋭利 (Q10) · 満悦 · 税金 · 脱出
Exam trap — right sound, wrong kanji: in Q3 殻膜, Q5 陰居, Q6 組識 and Q8 俊速 the reading matches but the kanji does not. Checking the sound alone is not enough in II-2; confirm the meaning of each character (膜 = membrane, 隠 = hide, 織 = weave, 足 = foot).
Exam trap — one kanji, two on-readings: 織 is ショク in 染織 but シキ in 組織; 婦 is フ in 婦人 but プ in 新婦; 緑 is リョク in 新緑 but ロク in 緑青. The book lists these readings side by side on p.36 — learn the compound, not just the kanji.
