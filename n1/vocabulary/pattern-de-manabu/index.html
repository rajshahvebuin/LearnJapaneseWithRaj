パターンで学ぶ N1 文字・語彙

Following 増補版 パターンで学ぶ 日本語能力試験 N1文字・語彙問題集 (Pattern de Manabu N1 Moji・Goi, Jリサーチ出版) spread by spread. 第1章 sorts kanji by sound, shape and meaning (same-reading words, look-alike kanji, special readings, sound changes). 第2章 sorts vocabulary into six patterns (many meanings, look-alikes, near-synonyms, fixed collocations, compounds, figurative expressions). 第3章 has 3 review sets and 4 mock tests. Every page gives the book's word list, every exercise with answers from the official 別冊 key, and confusion pairs, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.
