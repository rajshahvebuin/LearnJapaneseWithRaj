1 Points — what this test covers

How the 模擬試験 is built (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Each 模擬試験 is a 25-item mock of the 文字・語彙 section of the real JLPT N1, in its four question types: 問題1 漢字読み (6 items), 問題2 文脈規定 (7), 問題3 言い換え類義 (6), 問題4 用法 (6). Give yourself about 15 minutes, then check the "Why" lines and revisit the pattern each item comes from.

問題1 漢字読み — items 1–6 (p.153)

(Kanji yomi — readings)

Meaning: EN: look the part, be presentable · HI: जँचने लगना · GU: શોભવા લાગવું
OptionOption sentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1あんなさまになった男とは、きっぱり別れたほうがいい。Anna sama ni natta otoko to wa, kippari wakareta hou ga ii.× You'd better break up firmly with a man who has さまになった like that. (should be ざまになった / 落ちぶれた)× … (यहाँ 落ちぶれた = पतित चाहिए)× … (અહીં 落ちぶれた = પડતી પામેલો જોઈએ)
2今年の桜は去年に比べて、さまになっている。Kotoshi no sakura wa kyonen ni kurabete, sama ni natte iru.× This year's cherry blossoms さまになっている compared with last year. (should be 見事だ)× … (यहाँ 見事だ = शानदार चाहिए)× … (અહીં 見事だ = શાનદાર જોઈએ)
3はじめは聞けたものではなかったが、練習したら、さまになってきた。Hajime wa kiketa mono de wa nakatta ga, renshuu shitara, sama ni natte kita.○ At first it was unbearable to listen to, but with practice it has started to sound respectable.○ शुरू में सुनने लायक़ नहीं था, पर अभ्यास से अब जँचने लगा है।○ શરૂઆતમાં સાંભળવા લાયક નહોતું, પણ અભ્યાસથી હવે શોભવા લાગ્યું છે.
4さすが先生、さまになった授業ですね。Sasuga sensei, sama ni natta jugyou desu ne.× As expected of a teacher — a さまになった lesson. (patronising for an expert; say 見事な授業)× … (विशेषज्ञ के लिए अटपटा; 見事な कहें)× … (નિષ્ણાત માટે અટપટું; 見事な કહો)

Answer 3 (book key, 別冊 p.8). (added, not in book) 様になる = something done by a learner now looks or sounds the part. It implies progress from a poor start, so it doesn't suit an expert's work.
