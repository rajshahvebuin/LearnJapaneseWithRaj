例題 — the book's worked example (p.60)

例題：次の＿＿の言葉の意味が、はじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Reidai: Tsugi no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: Choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the first sentence. HI: वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द पहले वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ પહેલા વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

例題

つよい……つよい酒を一気に飲んで、その場に倒れてしまった。Tsuyoi…… Tsuyoi sake o ikki ni nonde, sono ba ni taorete shimatta.

Sense used: ③ 働きや勢いが大きい — strong in effect (strong liquor)
EN: I downed strong liquor in one go and collapsed on the spot.
HI: मैंने तेज़ शराब एक साँस में पी ली और वहीं गिर पड़ा।
GU: મેં કડક દારૂ એક શ્વાસે પી લીધો અને ત્યાં જ ઢળી પડ્યો.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1腕相撲は力のつよい人が必ず勝つとはいえない。→ ① stronger than othersUdezumou wa chikara no tsuyoi hito ga kanarazu katsu to wa ienai.In arm-wrestling the physically stronger person does not always win.पंजा लड़ाने में ताक़तवर व्यक्ति हमेशा जीते, ऐसा नहीं।પંજા લડાવવામાં તાકાતવાળો માણસ હંમેશાં જીતે એવું નથી.
2彼女の香水は香りがつよすぎて、気分が悪くなる。→ ③ strong in effectKanojo no kousui wa kaori ga tsuyosugite, kibun ga waruku naru.Her perfume's scent is too strong; it makes me feel sick.उसके इत्र की ख़ुशबू इतनी तेज़ है कि तबीयत ख़राब हो जाती है।તેના અત્તરની સુગંધ એટલી તીવ્ર છે કે તબિયત બગડે છે.
3あのチームはこの町で一番サッカーがつよいチームだ。→ ① stronger than othersAno chiimu wa kono machi de ichiban sakkaa ga tsuyoi chiimu da.That team is the strongest football team in this town.वह टीम इस शहर की सबसे मज़बूत फ़ुटबॉल टीम है।એ ટીમ આ શહેરની સૌથી મજબૂત ફૂટબૉલ ટીમ છે.
4この紙はつよいので、水にぬれても破れません。→ ② durableKono kami wa tsuyoi node, mizu ni nurete mo yaburemasen.This paper is tough, so it doesn't tear even when wet.यह काग़ज़ मज़बूत है, इसलिए भीगने पर भी नहीं फटता।આ કાગળ મજબૂત છે, એટલે ભીંજાય તોય ફાટતો નથી.

Answer 2 (book's 解き方, p.60). The book: つよい has three senses — ① 他と比べ力が優れている (stronger than others), ② 耐える力が大きい (durable, resistant), ③ 働きや勢いが大きい (strong in effect or force). ① is used in options 1 and 3, ② in 4, ③ in 2. つよい酒 means the alcohol's effect is strong, so the answer is 2.

ヒント (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One and the same word changes its meaning with the situation it is used in, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ प्रयोग की स्थिति के अनुसार बदल जाता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके एक साथ याद करें।

GU: એક જ શબ્દનો અર્થ વપરાશની પરિસ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે બદલાય છે, એટલે અર્થોને ગોઠવીને સાથે યાદ રાખો.

解き方 — how to solve (summary, not the book's text)

EN: First decide which sense the headword has in the opening sentence, then test each option against that sense. In this pattern the four options are full sentences, and usually each uses a different sense from the list.

HI: पहले तय करें कि शुरुआती वाक्य में शब्द किस अर्थ में है, फिर हर विकल्प को उसी अर्थ से मिलाएँ। इस पैटर्न में चारों विकल्प पूरे वाक्य हैं और आमतौर पर हर एक सूची का अलग अर्थ दिखाता है।

GU: પહેલાં નક્કી કરો કે શરૂઆતના વાક્યમાં શબ્દ કયા અર્થમાં છે, પછી દરેક વિકલ્પને એ અર્થ સાથે સરખાવો. આ પેટર્નમાં ચારેય વિકલ્પો આખાં વાક્યો છે અને સામાન્ય રીતે દરેકમાં યાદીનો જુદો અર્થ વપરાય છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.61)

(1)

がらがら……ラッシュアワーが終わった時間帯の電車はがらがらだった。Garagara…… Rasshu awaa ga owatta jikantai no densha wa garagara datta.

GU: ધસારાનો સમય પૂરો થયા પછી ટ્રેનો લગભગ ખાલી હતી.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) The opening sentence and option 4 both describe a place with almost nobody in it (電車, 映画館). Options 1 and 2 use がらがら for the rattling, crashing sound of a heavy thing, and option 3 for a hoarse throat.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several of these adverbs can be translated "gently", "slowly" or "little by little" in English. In Japanese the sense depends on the scene. These are the contrasts the five questions test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
がらがら ① / がらがら ②garagara empty / rattling ① describes a place with no people (がらがらの電車); ② is the sound of something heavy moving or falling (戸, 石). Q1: 電車・映画館 = ①
さっぱり ① / ④sappari refreshed / not at all ① is affirmative (さっぱりした = I feel fresh); ④ only appears with a negative (さっぱり来ない, さっぱりわからない). Q2: 片付ける・髪を切る = ①
じりじり ② / ④jirijiri inching / impatient じりじりと + a movement verb = slowly, steadily; じりじりする (with する, describing a person) = fretting. Q3: 待つ = ④
ずるずる ② / ③zuruzuru slipping down / dragging on ② a physical or numerical slide (成績が下がる); ③ time or a habit continuing longer than it should (続ける, 延びる). Q4: 会議が延びる = ③
そっと ① / ② / ③ / ④sotto quietly / secretly / gently / leave be ① no sound; ② nobody notices; ③ careful handling; ④ only in そっとしておく. Q5: 泥棒・カンニング = ②
ころころ ③ vs さっぱり ③korokoro / sappari fickle vs easy-going Both describe character, with opposite feelings: ころころ変える is negative (keeps changing), さっぱりした性格 is positive (no grudges). 考えをころころ変える · さっぱりした性格
Exam trap — same headword in every option: in this pattern all four options contain the same word, so you cannot answer from the word alone. Name the sense of the opening sentence first (e.g. "empty"), then look for the option with the same kind of subject (a train or a cinema, not a door or a throat).
Exam trap — と or no と: many onomatopoeic adverbs change sense with the grammar around them: じりじりと近づく (movement) vs じりじりする (feeling); がらがらだ / がらがらの (state) vs がらがらと (sound). (added, not in book)
