1 Points — 例題 and the one-kanji word list

例題 — worked example (p.38)

あの山の頂から日が昇るのを見た。Ano yama no itadaki kara hi ga noboru no o mita.

EN: I watched the sun rise from the summit of that mountain. HI: मैंने उस पहाड़ की चोटी से सूरज उगते देखा। GU: મેં તે પર્વતના શિખર પરથી સૂરજ ઊગતો જોયો.

OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1いただきitadakisummit, peakशिखर, चोटीશિખર, ટોચ
2ちょうchouon-reading of 頂 (頂上) — not used alone頂 का ओन पाठ — अकेले नहीं頂નું ઓન વાંચન — એકલું નહીં
3てっぺんteppen"top" — a different word (天辺)"सबसे ऊपर" — अलग शब्द"ટોચ" — અલગ શબ્દ
4ていteinot a wordशब्द नहींશબ્દ નથી

Answer 1 (book's 解き方). The book says: when 頂 stands alone it is read いただき and means 頂上 (summit).

ヒント (summary, not the book's text)

EN: A single kanji used as a word on its own, without okurigana, usually takes a native (kun) reading. Learn the reading together with the word (頂 = いただき, the summit).

HI: जब कोई कांजी बिना ओकुरिगाना के अकेले एक शब्द बनता है, तो उसका आमतौर पर कुन (जापानी) पाठ होता है। शब्द को उसके पाठ के साथ ही याद करें (頂 = いただき, शिखर)।

GU: જ્યારે કોઈ કાંજી ઓકુરિગાના વગર એકલો શબ્દ બને, ત્યારે સામાન્ય રીતે તેનું કુન (જાપાની) વાંચન હોય છે. શબ્દને તેના વાંચન સાથે જ યાદ રાખો (頂 = いただき, શિખર).

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.39)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is the underlined word read? Choose the reading from 1–4 for each. HI: रेखांकित शब्द कैसे पढ़ा जाता है? हर एक के लिए 1–4 में से पढ़ने का तरीका चुनें। GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે વંચાય? દરેક માટે 1–4માંથી વાંચવાની રીત પસંદ કરો.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The distractors on p.39 are mostly near-synonyms with a different kanji. These are the pairs worth keeping apart:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
器 / 皿 / 入れ物utsuwa / sara / iremonovessel / plate / container器 is any serving vessel (also "calibre" of a person: 器が大きい); 皿 is a flat plate; 入れ物 is any container.白い器に盛る (Q1)
志 / 意志 / 心kokorozashi / ishi / kokoroaim / will / heart志 is a goal you set yourself; 意志 is will-power; 心 is the heart or mind in general.志を持つ (Q4) · 意志が強い
富 / 宝 / 財産tomi / takara / zaisanwealth / treasure / property富 is riches in general; 宝 is a precious object; 財産 is property owned (legal).富を貯える (Q9)
主 ぬし / 主 あるじnushi / arujiowner / masterぬし in 飼い主・持ち主・声の主 ("the one who …"); あるじ for the head of a house or shop.飼い主 (Q7) · 家の主
市 いち / 市 しichi / shimarket / cityAlone or in 朝市・野菜市 it is いち (a market); し is a city (市長, 市役所).野菜市 (Q3)
Exam trap — the on-reading as a distractor: almost every question offers the kanji's on-reading (き, じょう, にゅう, こう, れき, ぞう). When the kanji stands alone as a noun, the kun reading is almost always the answer.
Exam trap — voicing and length: たけ vs だけ (Q2), おおやけ vs おやけ (Q6), くら vs くらい (Q10). Check every kana of the reading, not just the first sound.
