2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.73)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

(1)

あがる……たくさんの観客がいたのであがってしまった。Agaru…… Takusan no kankyaku ga ita node agatte shimatta.

Sense used: ⑦ 頭に血がのぼる — get nervous
EN: There was a big audience, so I got stage fright.
HI: बहुत दर्शक थे, इसलिए मैं घबरा गया।
GU: ઘણા પ્રેક્ષકો હતા, એટલે હું ગભરાઈ ગયો.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1落ち着きましたらごあいさつにあがろうと思っています。 → ⑩ visit (humble)Ochitsukimashitara goaisatsu ni agarou to omotte imasu.Once things settle down, I'm thinking of coming by to pay my respects.सब ठीक हो जाने पर मैं आपसे मिलने आने की सोच रहा हूँ।બધું થાળે પડે પછી હું આપને મળવા આવવાનું વિચારું છું.
2あがらないように深呼吸してからスピーチをはじめた。 → ⑦ get nervousAgaranai you ni shinkokyuu shite kara supiichi o hajimeta.I took a deep breath so I wouldn't get nervous, then started my speech.घबराहट न हो, इसलिए गहरी साँस लेकर भाषण शुरू किया।ગભરાટ ન થાય એ માટે ઊંડો શ્વાસ લઈને ભાષણ શરૂ કર્યું.
3味方のチームがゴールしたので、観客から大きな声があがった。 → ⑥ (a shout) goes upMikata no chiimu ga gooru shita node, kankyaku kara ookina koe ga agatta.Our team scored, so a great shout went up from the crowd.हमारी टीम ने गोल किया तो दर्शकों से ज़ोरदार आवाज़ उठी।અમારી ટીમે ગોલ કર્યો તો પ્રેક્ષકોમાંથી મોટો અવાજ ઊઠ્યો.
4練習の成果があがっているので、いい記録が出ている。 → ⑧ produce resultsRenshuu no seika ga agatte iru node, ii kiroku ga dete iru.The practice is paying off, so good records are coming.अभ्यास का फल मिल रहा है, इसलिए अच्छे रिकॉर्ड बन रहे हैं।અભ્યાસનું ફળ મળે છે, એટલે સારા રેકૉર્ડ બને છે.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A big audience and a speech both cause nerves; あがる here means stage fright. Option 3 is the trap: it has 観客 too, but there a shout goes up (⑥). Option 1 is the humble "visit" and option 4 "get results".

OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Both sentences have a person + にあたる, with the anger pointed at someone who did nothing wrong (部下, 子どもたち). Option 1 is "corresponds to", option 3 sitting in the breeze, and option 4 "is right".

Sense used: ④ 確かめる、調べる — check
EN: Hand luggage is inspected at the airport departure gate.
HI: हवाई अड्डे के प्रस्थान-द्वार पर हाथ का सामान जाँचा जाता है।
GU: હવાઈ મથકના પ્રસ્થાન-દ્વારે હાથનો સામાન તપાસાય છે.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
14月から制服を新しいデザインにあらためることになった。 → ① changeShigatsu kara seifuku o atarashii dezain ni aratameru koto ni natta.From April the uniform will change to a new design.अप्रैल से वर्दी नए डिज़ाइन में बदली जाएगी।એપ્રિલથી યુનિફોર્મ નવી ડિઝાઇનમાં બદલાશે.
2お客様、乗車券をあらためさせていただきます。 → ④ checkOkyakusama, joushaken o aratamesasete itadakimasu.Excuse me, may I check your ticket?महोदय, कृपया अपना टिकट जाँचने दीजिए।સાહેબ, કૃપા કરી ટિકિટ તપાસવા દો.
3日をあらためて、またごあいさつにうかがいます。 → ② another timeHi o aratamete, mata goaisatsu ni ukagaimasu.I'll come again another day to pay my respects.किसी और दिन फिर मिलने आऊँगा।કોઈ બીજા દિવસે ફરી મળવા આવીશ.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) Checking luggage at the airport and checking tickets on a train are both official inspections. Options 1 and 4 mean change or improve, and option 3 is 日をあらためる "another day".

(4)

うつ……自分の体験をもとにした話が聴衆の胸をうった。Utsu…… Jibun no taiken o moto ni shita hanashi ga choushuu no mune o utta.

Sense used: ⑤ 感動を与える — move, touch
EN: The talk based on her own experience moved the audience.
HI: अपने अनुभव पर आधारित बात ने श्रोताओं का दिल छू लिया।
GU: પોતાના અનુભવ પર આધારિત વાતે શ્રોતાઓનું દિલ સ્પર્શી લીધું.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1一日中パソコンに向かって数字をうつ仕事なので肩がこる。 → ② typeIchinichijuu pasokon ni mukatte suuji o utsu shigoto na node kata ga koru.My job is typing numbers into a computer all day, so my shoulders get stiff.दिन भर कंप्यूटर पर अंक टाइप करने का काम है, इसलिए कंधे अकड़ जाते हैं।આખો દિવસ કમ્પ્યુટર પર આંકડા ટાઇપ કરવાનું કામ છે, એટલે ખભા જકડાઈ જાય છે.
2子どもたちだけで生き抜いたという話に強くうたれた。 → ⑤ move (passive: be moved)Kodomotachi dake de ikinuita to iu hanashi ni tsuyoku utareta.I was deeply moved by the story of the children who survived on their own.अकेले बच्चों के जीवित बच निकलने की कहानी ने मुझे गहरे तक छू लिया।એકલાં બાળકો જીવતાં બચી નીકળ્યાંની વાર્તાએ મને ઊંડે સુધી સ્પર્શી લીધો.
3注射をうたれるのは嫌だと子どもたちは逃げ回った。 → ③ injectChuusha o utareru no wa iya da to kodomotachi wa nigemawatta.The children ran about, saying they didn't want injections.बच्चे सुई न लगवाने के लिए इधर-उधर भागे।બાળકો ઇન્જેક્શન ન લેવા આમતેમ ભાગ્યાં.
4突然の大洪水に人々は何の手もうてなかった。 → ⑥ take measures (手をうつ)Totsuzen no daikouzui ni hitobito wa nan no te mo utenakatta.Faced with the sudden great flood, people could do nothing.अचानक आई बड़ी बाढ़ के सामने लोग कुछ न कर सके।અચાનક આવેલા મોટા પૂર સામે લોકો કંઈ કરી ન શક્યા.

Answer 2 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) 胸をうつ (active) and 話にうたれる (passive) are the same "move emotionally" sense. Option 3 also uses the passive (うたれる), but for an injection. Option 1 is typing and option 4 the idiom 手をうつ.

(5)

うつる……夫にプレゼントしたネクタイは新しいスーツによくうつっていた。Utsuru…… Otto ni purezento shita nekutai wa atarashii suutsu ni yoku utsutte ita.

Sense used: ④ 似合う — suit, go with
EN: The tie I gave my husband went really well with his new suit.
HI: पति को दी गई टाई नए सूट पर बहुत फब रही थी।
GU: પતિને આપેલી ટાઈ નવા સૂટ પર બહુ શોભતી હતી.
OptionOption sentence → senseEnglishHindiGujarati
1湖にうつる美しい山の姿に感動した。 → ② be reflectedMizuumi ni utsuru utsukushii yama no sugata ni kandou shita.I was moved by the beautiful mountain reflected in the lake.झील में दिखते पहाड़ के सुंदर प्रतिबिंब ने मन मोह लिया।તળાવમાં દેખાતા પર્વતના સુંદર પ્રતિબિંબે મન મોહી લીધું.
2彼の気持ちがほかの女性にうつってしまった。 → ⑤ shift, changeKare no kimochi ga hoka no josei ni utsutte shimatta.His feelings shifted to another woman.उसका मन किसी और औरत पर आ गया।તેનું મન બીજી સ્ત્રી પર લાગી ગયું.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) A tie with a suit and a sofa with a room are both about things going well together. Option 2 is about feelings moving (⑤). Look out for 引っ越した in option 4: it suggests 移る (move house), but the うつる there means "match".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Verbs where a short object or particle decides the sense:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
あがる ⑥ / ⑦agaru a shout goes up / get nervous 声・歓声があがる (the subject is a sound); a person + あがる with no object (人前で・観客がいてあがる) = stage fright. Q1: あがってしまった = あがらないように
あたる ② / ③ / ⑤ataru be right / take it out on / be exposed to 指摘・占いがあたる → ②; person + にあたる → ③; 日・風にあたる → ⑤. Q2: 部下にあたる = 子どもにあたる
あらためる ① / ② / ④aratameru change / another time / inspect Object 制服・性格・食事 → ①; 日をあらためる → ②; 荷物・切符・乗車券 → ④. Q3: 手荷物 = 乗車券
うつ ③ / ⑤utsu inject / move emotionally Both can be passive (うたれる); 注射をうたれる vs 話に胸をうたれる. Q4: 胸をうつ = 話にうたれる
うつる ② / ④ / ⑤utsuru be reflected / suit / shift 湖・ガラスにうつる → ② (映る); 〜によくうつる (clothes, colour) → ④; 気持ち・政権がうつる → ⑤ (移る). Q5: ネクタイ = ソファの色
Exam trap — the same noun in the trap option: Q1 option 3 repeats 観客, and Q4 option 3 repeats the passive form うたれる. The sense comes from the whole phrase, not one shared word.
Exam trap — different kanji behind one kana verb: うつる can be written 映る (reflect, suit), 移る (move, shift) or 写る (in a photo). In this pattern everything is in kana, so the kanji you picture can mislead you (Q5 option 4). (added, not in book)
