Notes
Second spread of IV-1. The 例題 and the full tip are on IV-1 ①. This page lists first kanji ending in つ; they become っ before か・さ・た・ぱ-row sounds (a following は-row kanji turns into ぱ-row — see IV-2). The key gives answer numbers only, so every "Why" line is our explanation.

1 Points — the つ + か・さ・た・ぱ行 list

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: Look at the sound at the end of the first kanji. If it ends in く and the next kanji starts with a か-row sound, or it ends in つ and the next kanji starts with a か・さ・た・ぱ-row sound, the く / つ turns into a small っ. The small っ is always the second sound of the word.

HI: पहले कांजी की अंतिम ध्वनि देखें। अगर वह く पर ख़त्म हो और अगला कांजी か-पंक्ति से शुरू हो, या वह つ पर ख़त्म हो और अगला कांजी か・さ・た・ぱ-पंक्ति से शुरू हो, तो く / つ छोटा っ बन जाता है। छोटा っ हमेशा शब्द की दूसरी ध्वनि होती है।

GU: પહેલા કાંજીનો છેલ્લો અવાજ જુઓ. જો તે く પર પૂરો થાય અને પછીનો કાંજી か-હરોળથી શરૂ થાય, અથવા તે つ પર પૂરો થાય અને પછીનો કાંજી か・さ・た・ぱ-હરોળથી શરૂ થાય, તો く / つ નાનો っ બની જાય છે. નાનો っ હંમેશાં શબ્દનો બીજો અવાજ હોય છે.

The 例題 is on IV-1 ①.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The questions pair one word that doubles with one that does not. The deciding factor is the first sound of the second kanji:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
血管 / 血圧kekkan / ketsuatsublood vessel / blood pressureか → っ; a vowel (あ) → no change.Q3, Q4
実感 / 実物jikkan / jitsubutsureal feeling / the real thingか → っ; voiced ぶ → no change.Q5, Q6
発表 / 発明happyou / hatsumeipresentation / inventionは-row → っ + ぱ-row; ま-row → no change.Q7, Q8
雑誌 / 雑然zasshi / zatsuzenmagazine / disorderlyunvoiced し → っ; voiced ぜ → no change.Q10, Q11
筆記 / 筆談hikki / hitsudanwriting / talk in writingunvoiced き → っ; voiced だ → no change.Q16, Q17
湿気 しっけ / 躾 しつけshikke / shitsukedamp / disciplineSame kana except っ; meanings totally different.Q14
Rule summary (added, not in book): after つ, double before unvoiced か・さ・た-row and before は-row (which also changes to ぱ-row). Voiced sounds (が・ざ・だ・ば), vowels, and な・ま・や・ら・わ rows never cause っ.
Exam trap — the exception 日: 日 is ニチ (ending in ち, not つ) but still becomes にっ in 日課 and 日食. The book flags this on p.46.
