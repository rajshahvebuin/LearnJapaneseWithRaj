Second spread of IV-2. The 例題 and the full tip are on IV-2 ①. This page finishes the ぱ-row list and adds two short lists: words where は-row becomes ば-row instead, and 配, which can become either. The key gives answer numbers only, so every "Why" line is our explanation.

1 Points — the は行 → ぱ行 list (continued)

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: In a two-kanji word, when the first kanji ends in ん and the second starts with a は-row sound (ハ・ヒ・フ・ヘ・ホ), the second kanji takes ゛ or ゜ (電波 でん + は → でんぱ).

HI: दो-कांजी वाले शब्द में जब पहला कांजी ん पर ख़त्म हो और दूसरा は-पंक्ति (ハ・ヒ・フ・ヘ・ホ) से शुरू हो, तो दूसरे कांजी पर ゛ या ゜ लग जाता है (電波 でん + は → でんぱ)।

GU: બે-કાંજીવાળા શબ્દમાં જ્યારે પહેલો કાંજી ん પર પૂરો થાય અને બીજો は-હરોળ (ハ・ヒ・フ・ヘ・ホ)થી શરૂ થાય, ત્યારે બીજા કાંજી પર ゛ કે ゜ લાગે છે (電波 でん + は → でんぱ).

The 例題 is on IV-2 ①.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.51)

問題Ⅰ　次の文の＿＿をつけた言葉は、どのように読みますか。その読み方を、それぞれの1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no bun no ＿＿ o tsuketa kotoba wa, dono you ni yomimasu ka. Sono yomikata o, sorezore no 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: How is the underlined word read? Choose the reading from 1–4 for each. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Same kanji, with and without the change — check for ん first:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
添付 てんぷ / 交付 こうふtenpu / koufuattachment / issuing付 after ん → ぷ; after a long vowel → ふ.Q3, Q7
近辺 きんぺん / 周辺 しゅうへんkinpen / shuuhenneighbourhood / vicinity辺 after ん → ぺん; after う → へん.Q5

心配 しんぱい / 案配 あんばいshinpai / anbaiworry / arrangementSame 配 after ん, but one takes ゜ and one ゛ — the book lists 配 as "either".list p.50
辛抱 しんぼう / 信奉 しんぽうshinbou / shinpoupatience / beliefException: 抱 becomes ぼう (voiced), while 奉 follows the normal rule → ぽう.list p.50
Exam trap — the ゛ exceptions: 辛抱 しんぼう and 探訪 たんぼう take ゛, not ゜. Learn these two (plus 案配 あんばい) as exceptions to the ぱ-row rule.
Exam trap — く + ほ: 確保 かくほ (Q9) offers かっぽ and かんぽ. く only becomes っ before the か-row, and there is no ん, so nothing changes.
