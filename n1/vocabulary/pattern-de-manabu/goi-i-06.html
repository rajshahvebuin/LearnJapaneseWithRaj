1 Points — the word list, one row per sense

ヒント — reminder (summary, not the book's text)

EN: One word changes its meaning with the situation, so sort the senses out and learn them as a set. The 例題 (つよい) and the full 解き方 for this pattern are on I ①.

HI: एक ही शब्द का अर्थ स्थिति के अनुसार बदलता है, इसलिए अर्थों को व्यवस्थित करके याद करें। इस पैटर्न का 例題 (つよい) और पूरा 解き方 I ① पर है।

GU: એક જ શબ્દનો અર્થ પરિસ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે બદલાય છે, એટલે અર્થોને ગોઠવીને યાદ રાખો. આ પેટર્નનું 例題 (つよい) અને આખું 解き方 I ① પર છે.

2 Every question — 問題Ⅰ (p.71)

問題Ⅰ　次の1から5の＿＿の言葉の意味が、それぞれのはじめの文と最もちかい意味で使われている文を、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai I: Tsugi no 1 kara 5 no ＿＿ no kotoba no imi ga, sorezore no hajime no bun to mottomo chikai imi de tsukawarete iru bun o, 1・2・3・4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. EN: For each of items 1–5, choose the sentence (1–4) in which the underlined word is used in the meaning closest to the opening sentence. HI: 1–5 में से हर एक के लिए वह वाक्य (1–4) चुनें जिसमें रेखांकित शब्द शुरुआती वाक्य के सबसे निकट अर्थ में प्रयुक्त है। GU: 1–5 દરેક માટે એ વાક્ય (1–4) પસંદ કરો જેમાં રેખાંકિત શબ્દ શરૂઆતના વાક્યના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થમાં વપરાયો હોય.

Answer 4 (book key, 別冊 p.3). (added, not in book) 駅まえ and 家のまえ are both the space in front of a building. Option 2 is the front of a moving vehicle (②), and options 1 and 3 are about time, not place.

(5)

やま……「今夜がやまだと思います」と医者は言った。Yama…… "Kon'ya ga yama da to omoimasu" to isha wa itta.

EN: "I think tonight will be the critical point," the doctor said.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Space-and-time nouns that switch between a place sense and a time or abstract sense:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
なか ① / ③ / ④naka inside / in the midst of / among Box, wall → ① (space); 雨の・忙しいなか → ③ (a situation going on); 〜のなかから選ぶ → ④ (a group). Q1: 雨のなか = 忙しいなか
ひとくち ① / ③hitokuchi one bite / in a word Both say ひとくちで; with food it's one mouthful, with 言う・表現する it's a brief way of putting it. Q2: ひとくちで表現 = ひとくちでは言えない
まえ ① / ② / ⑤mae in front of / front of a vehicle / before 駅まえ・家のまえ (a fixed building) → ①; 電車のまえ (a moving thing) → ②; 〜するまえ (a verb before まえ) → ⑤. Q3: 駅まえ = 家のまえ
みち ① / ② / ⑤michi road / route / means 広い・狭いみち (the road) → ①; みちを聞く・わからない (the route) → ②; 〜するみちはない (a way) → ⑤. Q4: みちを聞く = みちがわからない
やま ② / ④ / ⑤yama pile / gamble / crisis 〜のやま (a pile of) → ②; やまをかける・やまが当たる (exam guessing) → ④; やまを越す・今夜がやま (crisis) → ⑤. Q5: 今夜がやま = やまを越えた
とき ④ / ⑥toki the right moment / when ときを待つ = wait for your chance; 〜ときは = whenever, in the case of. ときを待つ · 眠いときは
Exam trap — space vs time: まえ, なか and みち each have both a place sense and a time or abstract sense. In Q3 two options are about time (まえに会った, 来るまえ) and are easy to rule out once you see the opening sentence is about place.
Exam trap — やま idioms: やまをかける (gamble on exam topics), やまが当たる (the gamble pays off) and やまを越す (get through the worst) are fixed expressions. (added, not in book)
