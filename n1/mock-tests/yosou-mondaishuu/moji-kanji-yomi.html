1 Points — how 漢字読み works + the kanji tested

The question type (summary of the 別冊 introduction, p.2 — not the book's text)

問題　＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Mondai: ___ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Choose the best reading of the underlined word from 1–4. The 別冊 explains that many kanji have two or more readings and that compounds built from such kanji are likely to come up, along with kun-readings of adverbs and verbs. Very often every option would make sense in the sentence, so you can't guess from the meaning — you have to really know the reading.

HI: रेखांकित शब्द का सबसे सही पठन 1–4 में से चुनें। 別冊 के अनुसार बहुत से कांजी के दो या अधिक पठन होते हैं, और ऐसे कांजी वाले संयुक्त शब्द, साथ ही क्रियाविशेषण और क्रियाओं के कुन-पठन, अक्सर पूछे जाते हैं। अक्सर हर विकल्प वाक्य में अर्थ के हिसाब से ठीक बैठता है, इसलिए अंदाज़ा काम नहीं आता — पठन पक्का याद होना चाहिए।

GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દનું સૌથી યોગ્ય વાંચન 1–4માંથી પસંદ કરો. 別冊 મુજબ ઘણા કાંજીનાં બે કે વધુ વાંચન હોય છે, અને આવા કાંજીવાળા સંયુક્ત શબ્દો, તેમ જ ક્રિયાવિશેષણ અને ક્રિયાપદોનાં કુન-વાંચન, વારંવાર પુછાય છે. ઘણી વાર દરેક વિકલ્પ વાક્યમાં અર્થની દૃષ્ટિએ બંધબેસે છે, એટલે અનુમાન કામ નથી આવતું — વાંચન પાકું યાદ હોવું જોઈએ.

The trap types in this set (added, not in book): look-alike kanji (慕／募, 待／持), long vs short vowels (しゅうし／しゅし), small っ (錯覚 さっかく vs 錯誤 さくご), false dakuten (気配 けはい, not けばい), on/kun mixes (街角 まちかど), a sound change at the start of a compound (雨具 あまぐ), and meaning traps where the wrong options are near-synonyms (崇拝 vs 尊敬・信仰・心酔).

3 Confusion Pairs / Reading Traps

The traps hidden in this set's wrong options, grouped by type. Examples in the "Use when" column come from the items above (item number in brackets).

FormMeaningReadingKey differenceUse when
慕う vs 募るshitau / tsunoru to adore vs to recruit; to grow stronger した・う ／ つの・る Look-alike kanji: 慕 has 心 (heart) at the bottom, 募 has 力 (strength). The 別冊 warns about this pair. 学生から慕われている (1) / 会員を募る.
待 vs 持 vs 侍 ・ 遇 vs 偶 vs 隅tai / ji / ji ・ guu / guu / guu wait vs hold vs samurai ・ treat vs chance vs corner タイ／ジ／ジ ・ グウ／グウ／グウ Same right-hand part (寺, 禺), different left-hand radical. 待 keeps タイ: 待遇 たいぐう, not じぐう. 会社によって待遇が違う (12).
錯誤 vs 錯覚sakugo / sakkaku error vs illusion さくご ／ さっかく 錯 = サク, but it shrinks to サッ before a k-sound (覚 カク). Before ゴ it stays サク. 事実認識に大きな錯誤 (4).
修士 vs 種子・趣旨shuushi / shushi master's degree vs seed / gist しゅうし ／ しゅし Long vowel or not: 修 = シュウ (修理, 修飾), but シュ only in a few words like 修羅場. 修士号取得を目指す (5).
気配 vs 気分・湿気kehai / kibun・shikke sign, presence vs mood ・ humidity けはい ／ きぶん・しっけ 気 = キ or ケ; in 気配 it is ケ, and 配 keeps ハイ with no dakuten. 人がいる気配を感じた (23).
雨具・雨戸 vs 雨amagu・amado / ame rain gear, storm shutter vs rain あま〜 ／ あめ 雨 becomes あま at the start of many compounds; likewise 酒屋 さかや, 白雪 しらゆき, 船出 ふなで (the 別冊's list). 雨具を忘れずに (21).
街角 vs がいかど／まちかくmachikado street corner まち（訓）＋かど（訓） Most compounds are on + on, but some everyday words are kun + kun. Mixed readings (がい＋かど) are almost always wrong. 街角に1人の老女が (22).
臭い (くさい) vs 臭い (におい)kusai / nioi smelly (adjective) vs a smell (noun) くさ・い ／ にお・い Same spelling; the grammar decides. Ending a sentence after は → adjective くさい; after が／の → noun におい. この部屋は臭い (3) / 変な臭いがする.
被る (こうむる) vs 被る (かぶる)koumuru / kaburu to suffer (loss) vs to put on (a hat) こうむ・る ／ かぶ・る Same kanji, two verbs: こうむる goes with 損害・被害・迷惑; かぶる with 帽子・布団 (added, not in book). 損害を被った (25) / 帽子を被る.
崇拝 vs 尊敬・信仰・心酔suuhai / sonkei・shinkou・shinsui worship vs respect ・ faith ・ devotion すうはい ／ そんけい・しんこう・しんすい Meaning trap: all four fit "she ___ a rock singer". Only the reading of 崇 (スウ) and 拝 (ハイ) decides. ロック歌手を崇拝している (18).
認識 vs 見識・学識・知識ninshiki / kenshiki・gakushiki・chishiki awareness vs insight ・ learning ・ knowledge にんしき ／ けんしき・がくしき・ちしき Same second kanji 識 (シキ); read the first one. 知識不足 also makes sense, so meaning won't save you. 法律違反になるとは、認識不足だった (15).
厳しい vs 激しい・険しい・乏しいkibishii / hageshii・kewashii・toboshii severe vs violent ・ steep ・ scarce きび・しい ／ はげ・しい・けわ・しい・とぼ・しい Four 〜しい adjectives; 激しい非難 is also natural. Know which kanji goes with which reading. 厳しい非難にさらされた (8).
翼 vs 羽tsubasa / hane wing(s) vs feather; wing つばさ ／ はね Close meanings, different kanji: 翼 = the whole wing (also a plane's), 羽 = feather or small wing. 翼を広げると2メートル (20).
Exam trap 1 — every option fits the sentence: 席を守る／取る／譲る (7), 激しい／厳しい非難 (8), 知識／認識不足 (15), 尊敬／崇拝している (18). In 漢字読み the meaning almost never tells you the answer. Ask "how is this kanji read?", not "which word makes sense?".
Exam trap 2 — sound changes: watch for small っ (錯覚 さっかく), long vowels (修士 しゅうし), dakuten that should or shouldn't be there (気配 けはい, 怠慢 たいまん not だいまん), and the あま／さか／しら／ふな changes at the start of compounds (雨具, 酒屋, 白雪, 船出).
Exam trap 3 — the option built from a look-alike kanji: つのわれて comes from 募る, not 慕う (1); じぐう from 持, not 待 (12). If an option looks like a reading of a similar kanji, check the radical before choosing it.
