1 Points — how 文脈規定 works + the words tested

The question type (summary of the 別冊 introduction, p.4 — not the book's text)

問題　（　　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、１・２・３・４から一つ選びなさい。Mondai: (　) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Choose the word that best fits the blank. The 別冊 says this type tests whether you can pick the right word for the context from options that are close in meaning or that share a kanji but mean different things. Kango compounds, katakana words, prefixes and suffixes, adverbs and onomatopoeia can all appear, so learn exactly how similar words are used differently.

HI: रिक्त स्थान के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त शब्द चुनें। 別冊 के अनुसार यह प्रश्न जाँचता है कि क्या आप मिलते-जुलते अर्थ वाले या एक ही कांजी वाले पर अलग अर्थ वाले विकल्पों में से संदर्भ के अनुसार सही शब्द चुन सकते हैं। कांगो, कताकाना शब्द, उपसर्ग-प्रत्यय, क्रियाविशेषण और ध्वन्यात्मक शब्द आ सकते हैं, इसलिए मिलते-जुलते शब्दों का सही प्रयोग याद रखें।

GU: ખાલી જગ્યા માટે સૌથી યોગ્ય શબ્દ પસંદ કરો. 別冊 મુજબ આ પ્રશ્ન ચકાસે છે કે તમે મળતા અર્થવાળા કે એક જ કાંજીવાળા પણ જુદા અર્થવાળા વિકલ્પોમાંથી સંદર્ભ પ્રમાણે સાચો શબ્દ પસંદ કરી શકો છો કે નહીં. કાંગો, કાતાકાના શબ્દો, ઉપસર્ગ-પ્રત્યય, ક્રિયાવિશેષણ અને ધ્વનિદર્શક શબ્દો આવી શકે, એટલે મળતા શબ્દોનો સાચો ઉપયોગ યાદ રાખો.

The trap types in this set (added, not in book): four kango sharing one kanji (推進／推理／推測／推移, 免職／免税／免許／免除), four kanji with the same reading (観／感／勘／関, 福／副／複／腹, 否／非／被／比), suffix choice (〜的／〜性／〜状), mimetic adverbs (じっくり, すんなり), and set phrases (微動だにしない, 究極の選択, 理屈ではなく).

2 Every question — 問題2 文脈規定 (p.12–14)

Cover the answers and try each item first. Answers are from the 別冊 【解答】 (p.4, PDF 127); each "Why" paraphrases that item's 別冊 解説 (p.4–6) and adds notes on the other options.

1

信じるに足る（　　）がないうちは、沈黙するしかない。Shinjiru ni taru (　) ga nai uchi wa, chinmoku suru shika nai.

EN: Until there is ( ) worth believing, all I can do is stay silent.
HI: जब तक भरोसा करने लायक ( ) न हो, चुप रहने के सिवा कोई चारा नहीं।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી વિશ્વાસ કરવા લાયક ( ) ન હોય, ત્યાં સુધી ચૂપ રહેવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
OptionJapanese / ReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1証拠shoukoevidence, proofसबूतપુરાવો
2承諾shoudakuconsentसहमतिસંમતિ
3基準kijunstandard, criterionमानदंडધોરણ
4信念shinnenbelief, convictionदृढ़ विश्वासદૃઢ માન્યતા

Answer 1 (別冊 p.4). Why: 「信じるに足る」 = 信じることができる (can be believed). What can be believed is 証拠 — something that makes it clear a fact is true. 参考 in the 別冊: 承諾 = accepting the other side's request; 基準 = the yardstick for judging; 信念 = strongly believing you are right. 信念 shares 信 with 信じる — a kanji trap (added, not in book).

EN: I can't reach a conclusion yet. I want to decide after watching how the discussion ( ).
HI: अभी मैं निष्कर्ष नहीं निकाल सकता। आगे की चर्चा का ( ) देखकर तय करना चाहता हूँ।

OptionJapanese / ReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1推進suishinpromotion, pushing forwardआगे बढ़ानाઆગળ ધપાવવું

Answer 4 (別冊 p.4). Why: The blank means 議論の行く先 (where the discussion goes). 推移 = 移り変わり、変化 (changes over time) fits. 参考: 推進 = making something go well; 推理 = working out the unknown from known facts; 推測 = guessing roughly from a fact. All four share 推, so only the second kanji decides.

4脅迫kyouhakuthreatधमकीધમકી

Answer 2 (別冊 p.6). Why: 「究極の選択」 is a set phrase, used when whichever you choose you may pay a big price. 参考: 救援 = helping people in danger; 窮屈 = cramped, unable to move freely; 脅迫 = threatening someone to make them do something.

3 Confusion Pairs / Traps

The traps hidden in this set's wrong options. Item numbers in brackets.

FormMeaningReadingKey differenceUse when
推進・推理・推測・推移suishin・suiri・suisoku・suii promote ・ deduce ・ guess ・ change over time すいしん・すいり・すいそく・すいい Same first kanji; the second decides. 移 = move → the course of events. 議論の推移を見る (2).
模範 vs 規範・師範mohan / kihan・shihan model vs norm ・ master もはん／きはん・しはん All 〜範; only 模範的 means 'a model to copy'. 師範的 is not said. 模範的なドライバー (3).
じっくり vs がっくり・がっちりjikkuri / gakkuri・gatchiri carefully vs dejectedly ・ firmly じっくり／がっくり・がっちり Mimetic words that differ by one sound; じっくり goes with 考える. じっくり考えて出した結論 (4).
観・感・勘・関kan view ・ feeling ・ intuition ・ relation かん Same reading. 〜観 = an outlook: 価値観, 人生観, 世界観 (the last two added). 異なった価値観 (7).
〜的 vs 〜性 vs 〜状-teki / -sei / -jou -ic (adjective) vs quality (noun) vs shape てき／せい／じょう 〜的 → な-adjective (知的な); 〜性 → noun you can lack (積極性に欠ける); 〜状 → shape (帯状). 知的好奇心 (8), 積極性 (15), 帯状 (16).
無難 vs 非難bunan / hinan safe vs criticism ぶなん／ひなん Both end in 難, but 無難 = 'no trouble' (and is read ぶ, not む). 無難な生き方 (11).
免除 vs 免税・免許・免職menjo / menzei・menkyo・menshoku exemption vs tax-free ・ licence ・ dismissal めんじょ／めんぜい・めんきょ・めんしょく 入会金 is a fee, not a tax: fees are 免除 (waived). 入会金は免除される (19).
紛失 vs 落下funshitsu / rakka losing vs falling ふんしつ／らっか 落とした tempts you to 落下, but a lost item is 紛失 (紛失届). 紛失届を書いた (20).
滞る vs 渋るtodokooru / shiburu be overdue vs be reluctant とどこおる／しぶる 支払いが滞る = payment is overdue; 支払いを渋る = reluctant to pay (has を, a person as subject; added, not in book). 家賃の支払いが滞っている (23).
副・福・複・腹fuku side ・ fortune ・ multiple ・ belly ふく Same reading; 副 = secondary → 副作用, 副社長. 副作用が強い (27).
Exam trap 1 — the shared-kanji set: when all four options start (推〜, 免〜) or end (〜識, 〜範) with the same kanji, ignore it and read the other kanji: 移 = move, 除 = remove, 織 = weave/organize, 模 = copy.
Exam trap 2 — same reading, different kanji: items 5, 7 and 27 give four kanji all read ひ／かん／ふく. Decide by what the kanji means and which compound it makes (被修飾語, 価値観, 副作用).
Exam trap 3 — grammar decides the suffix: 〜的 makes an adjective (知的な好奇心), 〜性 a noun (積極性に欠ける). Check what comes right after the blank (な? に欠ける? の?).
