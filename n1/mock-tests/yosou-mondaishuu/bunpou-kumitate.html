1 Points — how 文の組み立て works + the patterns tested

The question type (summary of the 別冊 introduction, p.14 — not the book's text)

問題　次の文の＿★＿に入る最もよいものを、１・２・３・４から一つ選びなさい。Mondai: tsugi no bun no _★_ ni hairu mottomo yoi mono o, ichi・ni・san・yon kara hitotsu erabinasai.

EN: Put four scattered pieces into the correct grammatical order and answer which piece lands on ★. The 別冊's advice: until you are used to it, don't try to juggle all four at once. Instead look for two pieces that clearly join, and for what links to the words right before and right after the blanks, using your vocabulary and grammar knowledge.

HI: चार बिखरे टुकड़ों को सही व्याकरणिक क्रम में लगाएँ और बताएँ कि ★ पर कौन-सा टुकड़ा आता है। 別冊 की सलाह: आदत होने तक चारों को एक साथ न सोचें, बल्कि दो टुकड़ों का साफ़ जोड़ ढूँढें और रिक्त स्थानों के ठीक पहले और ठीक बाद वाले शब्दों से क्या जुड़ता है, यह शब्दावली-व्याकरण से सोचें।

GU: ચાર છૂટા ટુકડાને સાચા વ્યાકરણક્રમમાં ગોઠવો અને ★ પર કયો ટુકડો આવે તે કહો. 別冊ની સલાહ: ટેવ પડે ત્યાં સુધી ચારેયને એકસાથે ન વિચારો; બે ટુકડાનું સ્પષ્ટ જોડાણ શોધો અને ખાલી જગ્યાની બરાબર પહેલાં અને બરાબર પછીના શબ્દો સાથે શું જોડાય તે શબ્દભંડોળ-વ્યાકરણથી વિચારો.

Method used in every item below (added, not in book): ① find the grammar pattern (おろか…さえ, に足る, きらいがある); ② lock the last slot from what follows (です → noun, 和む → 心が); ③ lock the first slot from what precedes (好き → きらいを); ④ ★ is usually slot 3 — but item 10 puts it in slot 2, so always check.

2 Every question — 問題6 文の組み立て (p.27–29)

Try ordering each set first. Answers (the ★ piece) are from the 別冊 【解答】 (p.14, PDF 137); the full order is the one the 別冊 prints in each 解説 (p.14–17), and each "Why" paraphrases that 解説.

OptionFragmentEnglishHindiGujarati
1ハムエッグhamu egguham and eggsहैम-अंडाહૅમ-ઈંડાં
2てんぷらはtenpura watempura (topic)टेम्पुरा तोટેમ્પુરા તો

EN: Just between us, my wife can't make even ham and eggs, let alone tempura.
HI: बात हमारे बीच की है — मेरी पत्नी टेम्पुरा तो दूर, हैम-अंडा तक नहीं बना पाती।

Answer 1 (別冊 p.14). Why: Spot the pattern 「AはおろかBさえ」 = A goes without saying; even B. A is usually the harder thing. So 2 てんぷらは is followed by 3 おろか, and 1 ハムエッグ comes before 4 さえ.

Full sentence: なぜ、彼がそんな愚かなことをしてしまったのかまるで見当がつかない。Naze, kare ga sonna oroka na koto o shite shimatta no ka marude kentou ga tsukanai.
EN: I have absolutely no idea why he did something so foolish.
HI: मुझे बिल्कुल अंदाज़ा नहीं कि उसने ऐसा मूर्खतापूर्ण काम क्यों कर डाला।
GU: મને બિલકુલ અંદાજ નથી કે તેણે આવું મૂર્ખામીભર્યું કામ કેમ કરી નાખ્યું.

Answer 2 (別冊 p.16–17). Why: Here まるで is followed by a negative and means まったく、ぜんぜん. 1 愚かなことを + 2 してしまったのか go together; 4 そんな comes before 1.

3 Confusion Pairs / Traps

Look-alike patterns and ordering traps from this set. Item numbers in brackets.

FormMeaningReadingKey differenceUse when
〜に足る vs 〜に耐えないni taru / ni taenai worth ~ing vs not worth ~ing にたる／にたえない Both follow V-dict; opposite meaning. 信頼に足る人物 / 見るに耐えない. 推薦するに足る (2) / 聞くに耐えない (3).
きらい (dislike) vs きらいがあるkirai / kirai ga aru dislike vs tendency きらい／きらいがある Same sound, two words: 好ききらい = likes and dislikes; V + きらいがある = a bad habit. 好ききらいを…言いすぎるきらいがある (6).
〜たところで vs 〜としたところでta tokoro de / to shita tokoro de even if one did vs even if one tried たところで／としたところで Both + negative/useless result. としたところで follows a volitional form (〜ようとしたところで). あわてたところで (7) / やめさせようとしたところで (23).
〜からある vs 〜からするkara aru / kara suru as much as (size/weight) vs (price) からある／からする 100キロからある荷物, 100万円からする時計 (the second added, not in book). 100キロからある荷物 (4).
いかんによっては vs 次第でikan ni yotte wa / shidai de depending on いかんによっては／しだいで Same meaning; いかん needs によって(は) or で after it. 答えいかんによっては (5).
〜にともない vs 〜をとりまくni tomonai / o torimaku along with vs surrounding にともない／をとりまく Watch the particles: に + ともない, を + とりまく. They decide where each piece goes. 国際化にともない… (20).
〜ばそれまでだ vs 〜までのことだba sore made da / made no koto da then that's that vs I'll just ~ ばそれまでだ／までのことだ それまで after a conditional = nothing more can be done; までのことだ (問題5 item 15) = I'll simply take another way. と言われればそれまで (10).
Exam trap 1 — ★ isn't always slot 3: item 10 has ★ in slot 2. Write out the whole order first, then read off the ★ slot.
Exam trap 2 — two pieces both seem to fit: in 11 (同じクラスで／机を並べて) and 20 (わが国の言語／とそれ), more than one order looks grammatical. Make sure every piece has a place — a piece left "floating" (1 とそれ in 20) means your order is wrong.
Exam trap 3 — adjective placement: an adjective like 大きな (9) or 心優しい (16) must sit directly before its noun. Place nouns first, then slot the modifiers in front of them.
