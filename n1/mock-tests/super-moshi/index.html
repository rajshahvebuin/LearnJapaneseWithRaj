スーパー模試 N1

Following 日本語能力試験 スーパー模試 N1 (JLPT Super Moshi N1, アルク, 2011) — three full mock exams in real-exam format. Each 回 is split into one page per paper: 言語知識（文字・語彙・文法）, 読解 and 聴解. Every page gives every question with the official 別冊 answer, explanations, and the traps in the wrong options, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji. Reading passages and listening scripts are summarised, not reproduced; audio is referred to by CD and track number only.

How to use these pages

  • Sit each paper under exam conditions in your own copy of the book first (言語知識＋読解 110 min together, then 聴解 60 min without stopping the CD), then check yourself here.
  • Score each section with the book's formula and note your weak section — the book's advice is to do all three tests, then repeat all three, rather than repeating one test straight away.
  • For every wrong answer, read the "Why" and the confusion-pair table: the wrong options are where the vocabulary you still need is hiding.
