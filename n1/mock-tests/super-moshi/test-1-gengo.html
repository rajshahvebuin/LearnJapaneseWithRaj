Q1

Underlined word: 突如totsujo — suddenly

Q45

Exam trap 1 — 漢字読み distractors are real words: in Q4 and Q5 every wrong reading is a genuine word with a related meaning (阻害, 停止, 混乱). If an option "feels right" because of the meaning, check that the reading actually belongs to these kanji.
Exam trap 2 — 用法: check grammar before meaning: many wrong sentences fail on part of speech or structure alone — 手配 used as a な-adjective (Q22), 授ける with no receiver (Q20), カテゴリー used as an adverb (Q24). Rule these out first, then compare meanings.
Exam trap 3 — 問題7: read past the blank: Q43 and Q44 are decided by the sentence after the blank (the list of constraints; the example of a forced marriage), and Q45 by the whole argument. Read to the end of the paragraph before choosing.
