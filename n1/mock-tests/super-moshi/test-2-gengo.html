Exam trap 1 — 漢字読み: the "right meaning, wrong kanji" option: Q1 いわって (celebrate) and Q4 せつび (equipment) fit the sentence in meaning. When a reading feels natural, check that it belongs to the printed kanji.
Exam trap 2 — 用法 adverbs: Q24 とっさに and Q25 てっきり each come with rhyming adverbs (さっと, くっきり, すっきり, ひっそり). Learn each adverb with the exact verb or ending it needs: とっさに + a reflex action, てっきり + 〜と思っていた.
Exam trap 3 — 問題5: look at the end of the sentence: Q32 (〜たところで → no effect), Q31 (ようやく → に至って) and Q34 (〜のだ → ばこそ) are decided by a word after the blank.
