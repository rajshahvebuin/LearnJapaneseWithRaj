Answer 1. The 解説: the passage contrasts awareness before and after "MOTTAINAI" became known. Not paying attention (気にもかけないでいた) is the earlier state → それまでは; after foreigners praised it, people "came to feel proud" — a change from before, so a "but" word (the 解説 writes 「だが」; the option prints ところが) fits b.

Exam trap 1 — small っ, long vowels and ぱ/ば: Q2 ひんぱん, Q4 しゅっせ (not しゅっせい), Q5 こうけん (not こけん). Say the word aloud in a known compound (頻度・繁栄, 世間, 献立) and the right sound pattern follows.
Exam trap 2 — 問題4 wrong sentences borrow the kanji's surface meaning. 加入 looks like "add + enter", so the paper puts it with milk (加える), university (入学) and bank deposits (預金). Ask what the word actually collocates with: 加入 = join a group or scheme.
