完全模試 N1

Following ゼッタイ合格！日本語能力試験 完全模試 N1 (Kanzen Moshi N1, Jリサーチ出版) — three full mock exams, each split into its three papers: 言語知識（文字・語彙・文法）, 読解 and 聴解. Every page gives every question with the book's official answers and explanations, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji. Reading passages and listening scripts are summarised, not reproduced; audio tracks are referred to by CD and track number only.
