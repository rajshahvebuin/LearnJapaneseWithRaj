模試と対策 N1

Following 日本語能力試験 模試と対策 N1 (Nihongo Nouryoku Shiken Moshi to Taisaku N1, アスク出版) — two full JLPT N1 mock tests (模擬試験 第1回・第2回), each split here into its three papers, plus the book's strategy sections (問題の説明 in the main book, 「言語知識」の対策 and 「聴解」の対策 in the 別冊). Every page gives every question with the official 別冊 answer and explanation, and confusion pairs, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Sit each paper against the clock first (110 min for 言語知識・読解, 60 min for 聴解), using your own copy of the book and CDs.
  • Then check every answer on the unit pages. The book's own pass line is 80 %: if you score 48 or less (out of 60 per section), read the explanations and the 対策 pages before trying mock test 2.
  • The 対策 pages summarise the book's advice for each question type — useful before your first attempt, too.
