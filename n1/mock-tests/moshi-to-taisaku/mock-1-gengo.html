問題1 — 漢字読み (items 1–6)

1

彼女は今、出版関係の仕事に携わっている。Kanojo wa ima, shuppan kankei no shigoto ni tazusawatte iru.

Underlined word: 携わるtazusawaru — to be engaged in (work)
EN: She is now working in a publishing-related job.
HI: वह इस समय प्रकाशन से जुड़े काम में लगी हुई है।
GU: તે હાલમાં પ્રકાશન સંબંધિત કામમાં જોડાયેલી છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1かかわってkakawatte= 関わって (be related to)= 関わって (संबंध रखना)= 関わって (સંબંધ રાખવો)
2こだわってkodawatte= 拘って (be particular about)= 拘って (ज़िद रखना)= 拘って (આગ્રહ રાખવો)

Answer 3. Book's 解説: 携わる means "to be working in that field". Written in kanji, option 1 is 関わって and option 4 is 加わって. (added, not in book) Option 2 is 拘る. All four end in 〜わる, which is exactly the trap: the meaning "be involved" pulls you towards かかわる.
