1

彼女は、スポーツジムに通い、身体を鍛えた。Kanojo wa, supootsu jimu ni kayoi, karada o kitaeta.

OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1さかえたsakaeta= 栄えた (prospered)= 栄えた (फला-फूला)= 栄えた (સમૃદ્ધ થયું)

Answer 1. Book's 解説: The writer's claim is that the mind changes through creating. 4 means "doesn't change", so no; 2 〜てしまった carries regret; 3 変化させる would need を before it. So 1.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The traps this paper set, taken from its wrong options:

剖 vs 倍 vs 部bou / bai / bu cut open vs times vs part 解剖 ／ 倍率 ／ 部分 Same right-hand shape, three readings. 遺体を解剖した (Q2).

ようと〜まいと vs ようか〜まいかyou to ~mai to / you ka ~mai ka whether or not (doesn't matter) vs whether or not (I'm torn) V-よう + と ／ V-よう + か と = indifference; か = hesitation. わかってくれようとくれまいと (Q30).
に至った vs に及んだni itatta / ni oyonda finally reached (a result) vs extended to (a scope) 完成するに至った ／ 被害は全国に及んだ Verb + に至る is normal; verb + に及ぶ only in set phrases. ついに完成するに至った (Q33).

Exam trap 1 — the particle gives it away: in 文脈規定, all four words may make "する" verbs; the particle in the sentence (に vs を) picks one, as in Q7. Check the particle before the meaning (added, not in book).
Exam trap 2 — read the first half: in Q34 every ending sounds fine on its own; the 別冊 points you to 〜である以上 in the first half. In 問題5, the clue is often far from the blank (added, not in book).
Exam trap 3 — 別冊 numbering slips: for Q32 the 解説 says "3" but means 則って (option 2). When an explanation and the answer table disagree, trust the reasoning and the table (added, not in book).
