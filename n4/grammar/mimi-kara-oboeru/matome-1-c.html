2 Quiz / Exercise Section

III　（　）に入るのはどれですか。いちばんいいものを一つ選びなさい。(2×20) (Which goes in ( )? Choose the best one. 2 marks each, 40 marks.)

III-1 · point 3

前はいつも車でしたが、今は健康のことを考えて、よく（　）。Mae wa itsumo kuruma deshita ga, ima wa kenkou no koto o kangaete, yoku ( ).

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a歩けるようになりましたarukeru you ni narimashitabecame able to walkचल पाने लगाચાલી શકતો થયો
b歩くつもりですaruku tsumori desuplan to walkचलने का इरादा हैચાલવાનો વિચાર છે
c歩くことができますaruku koto ga dekimasucan walkचल सकता हूँચાલી શકું છું
d歩くようになりましたaruku you ni narimashitahave started walkingपैदल चलने लगा हूँચાલવા લાગ્યો છું

前はいつも車でしたが、今は健康のことを考えて、よく歩くようになりました。Mae wa itsumo kuruma deshita ga, ima wa kenkou no koto o kangaete, yoku aruku you ni narimashita.

EN: I always used to go by car, but now, thinking of my health, I've started walking a lot.
HI: पहले हमेशा गाड़ी से जाता था, पर अब सेहत का सोचकर अक्सर पैदल चलने लगा हूँ।
GU: પહેલાં હંમેશાં ગાડીમાં જતો, પણ હવે તબિયતનો વિચાર કરીને ઘણું ચાલવા લાગ્યો છું.

Book answer (解答 p.3): d. Why: a change of habit (前は…今は) = dictionary form + ようになる (point 3 ②). a means he could not walk before; b is a plan; c ignores the change. Same as Unit 01 練習 IV-2 (answer c there — the options were re-ordered).

III-2 · point 2

「あのレポート、もう書きましたか」「まだです。難しくてなかなか（　）」"Ano repooto, mou kakimashita ka." "Mada desu. Muzukashikute nakanaka ( )."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a書きませんkakimasendon't write (by choice)नहीं लिखताનથી લખતો
b書きましたkakimashitawroteलिख लियाલખી લીધું
c書けませんkakemasencan't writeलिख नहीं पा रहाલખી નથી શકતો

「まだです。難しくてなかなか書けません」"Mada desu. Muzukashikute nakanaka kakemasen."

EN: "Have you written that report yet?" "Not yet. It's hard, and I just can't get it written."
HI: "वह रिपोर्ट लिख ली?" "अभी नहीं। मुश्किल है, लिख ही नहीं पा रहा।"
GU: "તે રિપોર્ટ લખી લીધો?" "હજી નહીં. અઘરો છે, લખી જ નથી શકાતો."

Book answer (解答 p.3): c. Why: まだ + 難しくて + なかなか (just can't) → negative potential (point 2). a sounds like refusing; b and d contradict まだです.

III-3 · question words

「（　）人と結婚したいですか」「やさしい人がいいですね」"( ) hito to kekkon shitai desu ka." "Yasashii hito ga ii desu ne."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだれdarewhoकौनકોણ
bどんなdonnawhat kind ofकिस तरह काકેવું
cどのくらいdono kuraihow much / longकितनाકેટલું
dどうdouhowकैसेકેવી રીતે

「どんな人と結婚したいですか」「やさしい人がいいですね」"Donna hito to kekkon shitai desu ka." "Yasashii hito ga ii desu ne."

EN: "What kind of person do you want to marry?" "Someone kind would be nice."
HI: "किस तरह के इंसान से शादी करना चाहते हैं?" "दयालु इंसान अच्छा रहेगा।"
GU: "કેવી વ્યક્તિ સાથે લગ્ન કરવા માગો છો?" "દયાળુ વ્યક્તિ સારી."

Book answer (解答 p.3): b. Why: the blank is right before a noun (人) and the answer describes a type (やさしい人) → どんな. だれ cannot modify 人 directly (that would need だれの); c and d don't fit before a noun. Unit 02 練習 III-5.

「この赤いバッグとあの黒いバッグ、どちらがいいと思いますか」「赤いバッグ（　）ね」"Kono akai baggu to ano kuroi baggu, dochira ga ii to omoimasu ka." "Akai baggu ( ) ne."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのほどですno hodo desu(not a pattern)(कोई ढाँचा नहीं)(કોઈ માળખું નથી)

「赤いバッグのほうですね」"Akai baggu no hou desu ne."

EN: "Which do you think is better, this red bag or that black one?" "The red one, I'd say."
HI: "यह लाल बैग और वह काला बैग — कौन-सा अच्छा लगता है?" "लाल वाला, मेरे ख़याल से।"
GU: "આ લાલ બેગ અને તે કાળી બેગ — કઈ સારી લાગે છે?" "લાલ વાળી, મારા મતે."

Book answer (解答 p.3): d. Why: the answer to どちら = N のほう (point 12 ④). a and c are not grammatical; b "seems like a red bag" doesn't choose. Unit 02 練習 IV-6.

EN: I like French food, but I wouldn't want to eat it every day.
HI: मुझे फ़्रेंच खाना पसंद है, पर हर दिन खाने का मन नहीं करता।

Book answer (解答 p.3): d. Why: negative intention = 〜(よ)うとは思わない (point 6). a is not the natural negative of つもり (that is 食べないつもり); b guesses about someone else; c is not a natural choice sentence. Unit 01 練習 IV-6 (answer c there).

III-14 · point 12

bどちらがdochira gawhich (of two)कौन-सा (दो में से)કયું (બેમાંથી)

EN: "Which do you like better, spring or autumn?"
HI: "बसंत और पतझड़ में से आपको कौन-सा ज़्यादा पसंद है?"
GU: "વસંત અને પાનખરમાંથી તમને કઈ ઋતુ વધુ ગમે?"

Book answer (解答 p.3): b. Why: two things compared → always どちら (point 12 注意). どれ is for three or more; 何 and どこ don't compare two items.

III-15 · point 14

「飛行機で行きますか、新幹線で行きますか」「そうですねえ、新幹線（　）」"Hikouki de ikimasu ka, shinkansen de ikimasu ka." "Sou desu nee, shinkansen ( )."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aでしますde shimasu(wrong particle)(ग़लत कण)(ખોટો પ્રત્યય)

EN: "Will you go by plane or by shinkansen?" "Let me see… I'll go with the shinkansen."
HI: "हवाई जहाज़ से जाएँगे या शिंकानसेन से?" "हम्म… शिंकानसेन चुनूँगा।"

III-16 · point 4

来週試験があるので、今週はアルバイトを休んで（　）。Raishuu shiken ga aru node, konshuu wa arubaito o yasunde ( ).

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a勉強するつもりですbenkyou suru tsumori desuI plan to studyपढ़ने का इरादा हैભણવાનો વિચાર છે
b勉強できるつもりですbenkyou dekiru tsumori desu(wrong) plan to be able to study(ग़लत) पढ़ सकने का इरादा(ખોટું) ભણી શકવાનો વિચાર

EN: I have an exam next week, so this week I plan to take time off my part-time job and study.
HI: अगले हफ़्ते परीक्षा है, इसलिए इस हफ़्ते पार्ट-टाइम काम से छुट्टी लेकर पढ़ने का इरादा है।

Book answer (解答 p.3): a. Why: a decided plan = dictionary form + つもり (point 4). b: つもり doesn't take the potential form. c (a past thought) and d ("I think I can") do not state a plan, and する／できる alone after 休んで sounds incomplete. Unit 01 練習 IV-4.

The re-used 練習 questions test the same contrasts again. Check yourself on these:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ようになる~you ni naru came to ~ 辞書形／可能形 + ようになる Habit (dictionary) vs ability (potential). III-1.
〜てしまう~te shimau ended up ~ V-て + しまう Accident / regret, not a plan. III-8, III-20.
