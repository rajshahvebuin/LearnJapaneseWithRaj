2 Quiz / Exercise Section

もんだい1 — fill the blank (1–15), p.114–115

（　）に 何を 入れますか。a・b・c・dから いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. a, b, c, d kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in the brackets? Choose the best one from a–d. HI: कोष्ठक में क्या आएगा? a–d में से सबसे सही एक चुनिए। GU: કૌંસમાં શું આવશે? a–d માંથી સૌથી સાચો એક પસંદ કરો. Do the page in your own copy first; the correct row is highlighted.

1-1 · point 69

来週 みんなで 先生の 家へ 行く こと（　）なりました。Raishuu minna de sensei no ie e iku koto ( ) narimashita.

EN: It has been decided that we will all go to our teacher's house next week.
HI: तय हुआ है कि अगले हफ़्ते हम सब शिक्षक के घर जाएँगे।
GU: નક્કી થયું છે કે આવતા અઠવાડિયે અમે બધા શિક્ષકના ઘરે જઈશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aをo(object)(कर्म)(કર્મ)
bにni(ことに なる: it was decided)(ことに なる: तय हुआ)(ことに なる: નક્કી થયું)
cがga(subject)(कर्ता)(કર્તા)
dでdeat, byमें, सेમાં, થી

Book answer (解答 p.8): b に. Why (our explanation): the fixed pattern is 〜ことになる (point 69): a plan decided by the group or by circumstances. を would need する: 行くことにする (point 68, "I decide"), but the verb here is なりました. が and で do not make a pattern with なる.

1-2 · points 79–80

電車に のって いる（　）ずっと 本を 読んで いた。Densha ni notte iru ( ) zutto hon o yonde ita.

EN: The whole time I was on the train, I kept reading a book.
HI: ट्रेन में रहने के पूरे समय मैंने किताब ही पढ़ी।
GU: ટ્રેનમાં હતાં એ આખો સમય મેં પુસ્તક જ વાંચ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあいだaidathe whole time whileपूरे समय जब तकઆખો સમય જ્યાં સુધી
bあいだにaida niat some point whileके दौरान (एक बार)દરમિયાન (એક વાર)
cまでmadeuntilतकસુધી
dまでにmade niby (a deadline)तक (समय-सीमा)સુધીમાં

Book answer (解答 p.8): a あいだ. Why (our explanation): ずっと 〜ていた is an action that fills the whole period, so あいだ (point 79). あいだに (point 80) is for a one-time event inside the period (乗っているあいだに 寝てしまった). まで needs an end point, not のっている (降りるまで), and までに is a deadline (point 78).

1-3 · point 33

母の 手紙には、家族は みんな（　）という ことが 書いてありました。Haha no tegami ni wa, kazoku wa minna ( ) to iu koto ga kaite arimashita.

EN: My mother's letter said that everyone in the family is well.
HI: माँ की चिट्ठी में लिखा था कि परिवार में सब ठीक हैं।
GU: મમ્મીના પત્રમાં લખ્યું હતું કે પરિવારમાં બધા મજામાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a元気なgenki nahealthy (before a noun)स्वस्थ (संज्ञा से पहले)તંદુરસ્ત (નામ પહેલાં)
b元気genkihealthy (bare stem)स्वस्थ (केवल शब्द)તંદુરસ્ત (ફક્ત શબ્દ)
c元気でgenki dehealthy and… (て-form)स्वस्थ और…તંદુરસ્ત અને…
d元気だgenki dais healthy (plain sentence end)स्वस्थ है (साधारण वाक्य)તંદુરસ્ત છે (સાદું વાક્ય)

Book answer (解答 p.8): d 元気だ. Why (our explanation): ということ (point 33) wraps a whole plain-form sentence: 「家族は みんな 元気だ」＋という こと. A な-adjective or noun keeps だ before という. 元気な is for a noun directly (元気な人); 元気 alone and 元気で do not end a sentence.

1-4 · point 32

「春子、早く（　）なさい」"Haruko, hayaku ( ) nasai."

EN: "Haruko, get up quickly!"
HI: "हारुको, जल्दी उठो।"
GU: "હારુકો, જલદી ઊઠ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a起きてokiteget up (て-form)उठकरઊઠીને
b起きろokiroGet up! (command form)उठ! (आदेश)ઊઠ! (આદેશ)
c起きoki(ます-stem)(ます-रूप का मूल)(ます-રૂપનું મૂળ)
d起きようokiyoulet's get upचलो उठेंચાલો ઊઠીએ

Book answer (解答 p.8): c 起き. Why (our explanation): なさい joins the ます-stem: 起き(ます) → 起きなさい (point 32). 起きろ is already a full command and cannot take なさい; 起きて is followed by ください, not なさい; 起きよう is the volitional form (point 5).

1-5 · point 18

おなかが すいている ときは 何（　）おいしいです。Onaka ga suite iru toki wa nan ( ) oishii desu.

EN: When you are hungry, anything tastes good.
HI: भूख लगी हो तो कुछ भी स्वादिष्ट लगता है।
GU: ભૂખ લાગી હોય ત્યારે કંઈ પણ સ્વાદિષ્ટ લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aでもdemo(何でも: anything at all)(कुछ भी)(કંઈ પણ)
bもmo(何も + negative: nothing)(कुछ भी नहीं)(કંઈ જ નહીં)
cがもga mo(not a real form)(ऐसा रूप नहीं होता)(આવું રૂપ નથી)
dとかtokathings likeजैसे किજેમ કે

Book answer (解答 p.8): a でも. Why (our explanation): question word + でも = "any ~ at all" with a positive verb (point 18): 何でも おいしい. 何も needs a negative ending (何も 食べたくない). がも does not exist, and とか lists examples (point 8), not "anything".

1-6 · point 38

この くつは（　）すぎて はけません。Kono kutsu wa ( ) sugite hakemasen.

EN: These shoes are too small, so I can't wear them.
HI: ये जूते बहुत छोटे हैं, इसलिए पहने नहीं जा सकते।
GU: આ જૂતાં બહુ નાનાં છે, એટલે પહેરી શકાતાં નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a小さいchiisaismall (dictionary form)छोटाનાનું
b小さくchiisakusmall (く-form)छोटा (く-रूप)નાનું (く-રૂપ)
c小さchiisa(stem, い removed)(मूल, い हटाकर)(મૂળ, い કાઢીને)
d小さくてchiisakutesmall and…छोटा और…નાનું અને…

Book answer (解答 p.8): c 小さ. Why (our explanation): すぎる joins an い-adjective without い: 小さ＋すぎる (point 38). The other three forms keep い or add く／くて, which never come before すぎる.

1-7 · point 36

「（　）そうですね。何か いい ことが あったのですか」"( ) sou desu ne. Nanika ii koto ga atta no desu ka."

EN: "You look happy. Did something good happen?"
HI: "आपके चेहरे पर ख़ुशी दिख रही है। कुछ अच्छा हुआ क्या?"
GU: "તમે ખુશ દેખાઓ છો. કંઈ સારું થયું કે?"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aうれしいureshii(うれしいそう: I hear you're happy)(सुना है कि ख़ुश हैं)(સાંભળ્યું કે ખુશ છો)
bうれしureshi(うれしそう: look happy)(ख़ुश दिखना)(ખુશ દેખાવું)
cうれしくてureshikutehappy and…ख़ुश और…ખુશ અને…
dうれしかったureshikatta(I hear you were happy)(सुना है कि ख़ुश थे)(સાંભળ્યું કે ખુશ હતા)

Book answer (解答 p.8): b うれし. Why (our explanation): the speaker is looking at the other person's face, so this is そうだ "looks ~" (point 36): い-adjective stem + そう. The full plain form + そうだ (a, d) is hearsay, "I hear that ~" (point 21), which makes no sense when you are face to face. うれしくて cannot take そう.

HI: वादे निभाने ही चाहिए।
GU: વચન પાળવાં જ જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまもれmamore(まもれば: if you keep)(अगर निभाएँ)(જો પાળો)

Book answer (解答 p.8): d それで. Why (our explanation): "it got worse" is the reason, "I went to hospital" is what followed, so それで (so) in a personal story. しかし shows a contrast, which is the wrong direction. そして only adds the next event without the cause. そのために mostly means "for that purpose" (point 10) or a formal written cause, and sounds stiff in this simple story.

3-4 · point 31

Book answer (解答 p.8): a しんぱいしなくても いい. Why (our explanation): the doctor is calming the patient ("you'll get better soon"), so "you don't have to worry" = 〜なくても いい (point 31). b allows worrying and c tells the patient to worry, both the opposite of the doctor's message. 〜と いい (point 61) is used for advice or wishes and does not sit naturally after あまり 〜ない here.
