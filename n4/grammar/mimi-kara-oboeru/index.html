耳から覚える N4 文法

Following 耳から覚える 日本語能力試験 文法トレーニング N4 (Mimi kara Oboeru JLPT Grammar Training N4, アルク) unit by unit. The book teaches 90 grammar points in 10 Units. Each Unit has example sentences (the ones on the CD are also the dictation), a ディクテーション (dictation) page and a 練習 (practice) set. Three まとめテスト (review tests) and four 総合問題 (JLPT-format grammar sections) follow. Every page gives each point's meaning, connection and every example, and every question with the book's answer. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Read each grammar point and its examples, and listen to the CD sentences (Step 1 in the book, p.3).
  • After each Unit, do the ディクテーション and the 練習. After every three or four Units, take the まとめテスト. Check the questions you got wrong and repeat them (Step 2).
  • After まとめテスト3, try the four 総合問題, which use the real JLPT N4 grammar format (Step 3).
  • Each Unit is split into two grammar pages (with their dictation sentences) and one 練習 page, so one page is one study session.
