もんだい1 — fill the blank (1–15), p.117–118

（　）に 何を 入れますか。a・b・c・dから いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. a, b, c, d kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in the brackets? Choose the best one from a–d. HI: कोष्ठक में क्या आएगा? a–d में से सबसे सही एक चुनिए। GU: કૌંસમાં શું આવશે? a–d માંથી સૌથી સાચો એક પસંદ કરો. Do the page in your own copy first; the correct row is highlighted.

1-1 · point 23

タクシーで 行った（　）、間に合いませんでした。Takushii de itta ( ), ma ni aimasen deshita.

EN: Even though I went by taxi, I didn't make it in time.
HI: टैक्सी से जाने के बावजूद समय पर पहुँचना नहीं हो पाया।
GU: ટૅક્સીમાં ગયાં છતાં સમયસર પહોંચી શકાયું નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのにnonieven thoughके बावजूदછતાં
bでもdemobut / evenलेकिन / भीપણ
cのでnodebecauseक्योंकिકારણ કે
dよりyorithanसे (तुलना)કરતાં

Book answer (解答 p.8): a のに. Why (our explanation): a taxi should be fast, but the result was the opposite, so 〜のに "even though" with disappointment (point 23). ので (point 22) would make the taxi the cause of being late. でも cannot follow a た-form directly (行っても would be "even if", point 63), and より compares.

1-2 · point 51

弟は サッカーせんしゅに（　）います。Otouto wa sakkaa senshu ni ( ) imasu.

EN: My younger brother wants to become a soccer player.
HI: मेरा छोटा भाई फ़ुटबॉल खिलाड़ी बनना चाहता है।
GU: મારો નાનો ભાઈ ફૂટબૉલ ખેલાડી બનવા માગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなりたくてnaritakutewant to become, and…बनना चाहकरબનવા માગીને
bなりたくしてnaritakushite(not a real form)(ऐसा रूप नहीं होता)(આવું રૂપ નથી)
cなりたがってnaritagatte(he) wants to become(वह) बनना चाहता है(તે) બનવા માગે છે
dなるとしてnaru to shitesupposing (he) becomesमान लो बन जाएમાનો કે બને

Book answer (解答 p.8): c なりたがって. Why (our explanation): 〜たい is for your own wish. For a third person (弟) you use 〜たがる, and an ongoing wish is 〜たがって いる (point 51). なりたくて cannot take いる; なりたくして and なるとして do not give "wants to".

1-3 · point 47

リンさんは おとといから 学校を 休んでいる。病気（　）。Rin-san wa ototoi kara gakkou o yasunde iru. Byouki ( ).

EN: Lin has been off school since the day before yesterday. It seems they are ill.
HI: लिन परसों से स्कूल से छुट्टी पर हैं। लगता है बीमारी है।
GU: લિન પરમ દિવસથી સ્કૂલમાં રજા પર છે. લાગે છે કે બીમારી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aようだyou daseems (needs の after a noun)लगता है (संज्ञा के बाद の चाहिए)લાગે છે (નામ પછી の જોઈએ)

Book answer (解答 p.8): d らしい. Why (our explanation): a guess based on information (absent for two days) = らしい, which joins a noun directly: 病気らしい (point 47). ようだ also means "seems" but needs の: 病気のようだ (point 46). Hearsay そうだ needs だ: 病気だそうだ (point 21). ことだ does not express a guess.

1-4 · point 59

「山へ（　）、セーターを 持っていった ほうが いいですよ」"Yama e ( ), seetaa o motte itta hou ga ii desu yo."

EN: "If you're going to the mountains, you'd better take a sweater."
HI: "अगर पहाड़ों पर जाना है, तो स्वेटर साथ ले जाना अच्छा रहेगा।"
GU: "જો પહાડ પર જવાનું હોય, તો સ્વેટર સાથે લઈ જવું સારું."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a行けばikebaif you go (then…)अगर जाएँ (तो उसके बाद)જો જાઓ (તો પછી)
b行ったらittarawhen / after you goजाने के बादગયા પછી
c行くならiku naraif you are going to goअगर जाना है तोજો જવાનું હોય તો
d行くとiku towhenever / when you goजाने पर (हमेशा)જઈએ ત્યારે (હંમેશાં)

Book answer (解答 p.8): c 行くなら. Why (our explanation): you take the sweater before you go, and only なら lets the second action happen first ("if that's your plan, then…", point 59). With たら, ば or と (points 56–58) the going comes first, so "take a sweater" would happen after you arrive. と also cannot end in advice (ほうがいい).

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a乗れるにnoreru ni(not a pattern)(ऐसा पैटर्न नहीं)(આવી રચના નથી)

GU: "પરીક્ષા નજીક છે. ચાલો બધા પૂરી મહેનત કરીએ!"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: I can hear a strange sound. I wonder what that sound is.

GU: વિચિત્ર અવાજ આવે છે. શેનો અવાજ હશે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかka(question)(प्रश्न)(પ્રશ્ન)
bのno(casual question to someone)(किसी से अनौपचारिक प्रश्न)(કોઈને અનૌપચારિક પ્રશ્ન)

Book answer (解答 p.8): d かな. Why (our explanation): the speaker is talking to themself in plain style (音が する), so the soft "I wonder" = かな (point 55). A bare か after a noun sounds rough or written; の asks another person (point 54); 何の音でも would mean "any sound at all" (point 18).

1-8 · point 52

雨が（　）ので、せんたくものを 家の 中に 入れた。Ame ga ( ) node, sentakumono o ie no naka ni ireta.

EN: It started to rain, so I brought the washing inside.
HI: बारिश शुरू हो गई, इसलिए मैंने सूखते कपड़े घर के अंदर रख दिए।
GU: વરસાદ શરૂ થયો, એટલે મેં સૂકવેલાં કપડાં ઘરમાં લઈ લીધાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aふりだしたfuridashitastarted to fallगिरने लगीપડવા લાગ્યો
bふるだろうfuru darouwill probably fallशायद गिरेगीકદાચ પડશે

Book answer (解答 p.8): a ふりだした. Why (our explanation): the moment the rain begins is the reason to take the washing in: 〜だす "start (suddenly)" (point 52). だろう is a guess and does not sit before ので (point 15). OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a暑くてatsukuteit's hot, so…गर्मी है, इसलिए…ગરમી છે, એટલે…

Book answer (解答 p.8): d ちこく しないように. Why (our explanation): the verb after と is 注意する, and reported warnings take 〜ように(と) 注意する／言う (point 81). a, b and c are full spoken sentences; c (point 17) could be quoted in 「」, but without quotation marks the book's set pattern is ように＋と.

Exam trap — conditionals: four "if" words appear on this page. Ask two questions: (1) does the advice or action happen before the condition? Then なら (1-4). (2) Does the sentence end in permission, a request or "let's"? Then not と (1-9). For a fixed, automatic result (spring comes → warm), と is fine.
★ questions: start from the piece that must touch the printed words (しよう + と思います, 聞こえ + ます, 待っていて + ください, 何も + 言いませんでした), then fix pairs like こわれ + やすい and 泣いて + ばかり.
