Time and score
This paper has its own 25 minutes (この本の使い方, p.16), then 文法・読解 (50 min). Score (book p.17): at N5, 文字・語彙 and 文法・読解 count as one section, 言語知識・読解 = 120 × (correct in both papers) ÷ 67. A pass needs 80 / 180 overall, at least 38 / 120 in 言語知識・読解 and at least 19 / 60 in 聴解 (p.14). Pacing tip (added, not in book): 35 items in 25 minutes — the 20 kanji items should take a few seconds each.

The N5 reading traps on this paper: number readings (五本 ごほん not ごぼん; 九時 くじ not きゅうじ), a missing ん (おな / おんな) and a dropped voicing mark (みす / みず, ひたり / ひだり). In もんだい2 the wrong kanji are often mis-drawn versions of the right one, so look at every stroke.

Q1

えんぴつを　五本　かいました。Enpitsu o gohon kaimashita.

Underlined word: 五本gohon — five (pencils)
EN: I bought five pencils.
HI: मैंने पाँच पेंसिलें ख़रीदीं।
GU: મેં પાંચ પેન્સિલ ખરીદી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1じっぽんjippon(十本) ten(十本) दस(十本) દસ
2ろっぽんroppon(六本) six(六本) छह(六本) છ
3さんぼんsanbon(三本) three(三本) तीन(三本) ત્રણ
4ごほんgohonfiveपाँचપાંચ

Answer 4 (別冊 p.18). Why (added, not in book): 本 counts long thin things (pencils, bottles, umbrellas). It changes sound: いっぽん, さんぼん, ろっぽん, じゅっぽん/じっぽん — but 五 stays plain: ごほん. All the wrong options are real counts of other numbers.

Q3

けさ　おそく　うちを　出ました。Kesa osoku uchi o demashita.

Underlined word: 出ましたdemashita — left (went out of)
EN: This morning I left home late.
HI: आज सुबह मैं देर से घर से निकला।
GU: આજે સવારે હું મોડો ઘરેથી નીકળ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1でましたdemashitaleft, went outनिकलाનીકળ્યો
2だしましたdashimashita(出しました) took out, sent — another reading of 出(出しました) निकाला, भेजा — 出 का दूसरा पठन(出しました) કાઢ્યું, મોકલ્યું — 出નું બીજું વાંચન
3きましたkimashita(来ました) came(来ました) आया(来ました) આવ્યો
4でりましたderimashitanot a word(कोई शब्द नहीं)(કોઈ શબ્દ નથી)

Answer 1 (別冊 p.18). Why (added, not in book): 出る (でる, intransitive) = go out; 出す (だす, transitive) = take something out. Here there is no object being taken out — うちを でる means "leave the house", and the okurigana is ました directly after 出, so でました. 出しました would be written with し.

OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati

Exam trap 1 — 漢字読み: numbers change sound: Q1 and Q6 test number readings (五本 ごほん, 九時 くじ). Learn counters as sets — いっぽん, にほん, さんぼん…; いちじ, にじ… くじ — not one number at a time.
Exam trap 2 — 表記: zoom in on every stroke: in Q14, Q17, Q19 and Q20 the wrong answers differ from the right one by a single stroke (ン/ソ, 休/体, made-up versions of 長 and 百). Don't pick the first one that "looks right" — compare all four.
Exam trap 3 — 言い換え: same meaning, not the same topic: in Q32 and Q33 the wrong options stay on the topic (speaking, being absent) but change one detail (volume instead of speed, never came instead of left). Ask what the underlined sentence results in.
