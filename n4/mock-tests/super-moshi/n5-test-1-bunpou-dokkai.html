HI: माँ का बनाया खाना स्वादिष्ट होता है।
GU: મમ્મીએ બનાવેલું જમવાનું સ્વાદિષ્ટ હોય છે.

Exam trap 1 — particles: look at the verb first: in Q1–Q4 and Q12 the verb or adjective decides the particle (ほしい → が, とおる → を, する → を). Read to the end of the sentence before choosing.
Exam trap 2 — ★ items: build the noun phrase, then the verb: in Q17–Q21, first join adjectives to their noun (安くて おいしい さかな) and question words to が (どれが), then put the object + verb at the end. The ★ tile falls out once the frame is set.
Exam trap 3 — 情報検索: calculate, don't copy: in Q32 two options reuse the example's 06; the real date comes from the notice date + あさって, and the slot from the person's schedule. Write the date and slot down before looking at the options.
