スーパー模試 N4・N5

Following 日本語能力試験 スーパー模試 N4・N5 (JLPT Super Moshi N4・N5, アルク, 2012) — four full mock exams in real-exam format: two at N4 level and two at N5 level. Each test is split into one page per paper: 言語知識（文字・語彙）, 言語知識（文法）・読解 and 聴解. Every page gives every question with the official 別冊 answer, explanations and the traps in the wrong options, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji. The book itself has no explanations, so all explanations are ours. Reading passages and listening scripts are summarised, not reproduced; audio is referred to by CD and track number only.

How to use these pages

  • Sit each paper under exam conditions in your own copy of the book first (N4: 文字・語彙 30 min, 文法・読解 60 min, 聴解 35 min without stopping the CD), then check yourself here.
  • Score yourself with the book's formula: 文字・語彙 and 文法・読解 count together as one section (言語知識・読解), so add the two papers' correct answers before dividing by 70 (N5: 67).
  • For every wrong answer, read the "Why" and the confusion-pair table: the wrong options show the vocabulary you still need. The book suggests doing both tests, then repeating both; N4 learners can also try the N5 tests as warm-up.
