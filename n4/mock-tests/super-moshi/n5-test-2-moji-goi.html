Q1

ケーキを　八つ　たべました。Keeki o yattsu tabemashita.

Underlined word: 八つyattsu — eight (things)
EN: I ate eight cakes.
HI: मैंने आठ केक खाए।
GU: મેં આઠ કેક ખાધી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1よっつyottsu(四つ) four(四つ) चार(四つ) ચાર
2むっつmuttsu(六つ) six(六つ) छह(六つ) છ
3やっつyattsueightआठઆઠ
4みっつmittsu(三つ) three(三つ) तीन(三つ) ત્રણ

Answer 3 (別冊 p.19). Why (added, not in book): The native counting words: ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ, よっつ, いつつ, むっつ, ななつ, やっつ, ここのつ, とお. よっつ (4) and やっつ (8) are the classic mix-up — four and eight sound alike.

1かいしゃの　しごとは　2じに　はじまります。Kaisha no shigoto wa niji ni hajimarimasu.Work starts at 2 o'clock.काम 2 बजे शुरू होता है।કામ 2 વાગ્યે શરૂ થાય છે.

Answer 4 (別冊 p.19). Why (added, not in book): 2じかん (two hours, a length) vs 2じ (two o'clock, a point). Leaving at 8 and arriving at 10 = a two-hour journey. 1 and 2 confuse じかん with じ; 3 is two hours of work, not travel.

にちようびは　たいてい　いえに　います。Nichiyoubi wa taitei ie ni imasu.

Key word: たいていtaitei — usually, mostly

Q34

わたしは　りょうりが　へたです。Watashi wa ryouri ga heta desu.

GU: મને રસોઈ સારી નથી આવડતી.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1わたしは　りょうりが　すきです。… suki desu.I like cooking.मुझे खाना बनाना पसंद है।મને રસોઈ ગમે છે.
2わたしは　りょうりが　じょうずじゃありません。… jouzu ja arimasen.I'm not good at cooking.मैं खाना बनाने में अच्छा नहीं हूँ।હું રસોઈમાં સારો નથી.

Exam trap 1 — numbers have three reading systems: Q1, Q3 and Q4 test native counting (やっつ), big-number units (まん) and month names (しがつ). Practise counting objects, prices and dates aloud.
Exam trap 2 — 表記: real kanji vs fake kanji: in Q13, Q14 and Q18 most wrong options are invented shapes, and one is often a real similar kanji (毎, 果). Know the strokes of the 80 N5 kanji well enough to spot one stroke missing.
Exam trap 3 — verb + noun pairs: もんだい3 is mostly collocations — かぜが ふく, ギターを ひく, くつしたを はく, くすりを のむ, やまに のぼる. Learn verbs together with their typical nouns.
