1 Points — what each question type tests

(Added revision table, not in the book.) What each もんだい type tests and what to listen for. For more practice, see the site's N5 listening books.

TypeWhat is askedWhat to listen for
もんだい1 課題理解kadai rikaiWhat will the person do / choose (a day, a time, a thing, a bus)?The question comes first, so you know the target. The first plan is often changed: listen for でも, じゃあ, もっと 早く, いいえ、今….
もんだい2 ポイント理解pointo rikaiOne detail: transport, weather, place, what someone did or bought.Read the four printed options during the pause. Several are mentioned; the answer is the one that is finally true (e.g. "no black, only blue — then, no thanks").
もんだい3 発話表現hatsuwa hyougenWhat does the person with the arrow say?Look at the picture: who speaks to whom, and what is happening (leaving home, offering help, asking a child to be quiet). Then pick the set phrase that fits.
もんだい4 即時応答sokuji outouThe natural reply to one line.Nothing is printed. Ask: is it a question (いつ? いくら? 何で?), a request or thanks? The wrong replies often answer a different question word.

もんだい1 1ばん CD2 · Track 02

男の人と女の人が話しています。二人は、いつ映画に行きますか。Otoko no hito to onna no hito ga hanashite imasu. Futari wa, itsu eiga ni ikimasu ka.

EN: A man and a woman are talking. When will they go to the cinema?
HI: एक आदमी और एक औरत बात कर रहे हैं। दोनों फ़िल्म देखने कब जाएँगे?
GU: એક પુરુષ અને એક સ્ત્રી વાત કરે છે. બંને ફિલ્મ જોવા ક્યારે જશે?
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: He invites her to a film. She is free only on Sundays, so he suggests Sunday. She points out that the next Sunday is the 10th and there is a test on the 11th, so they agree to go after the test.

HI: वह उसे फ़िल्म के लिए बुलाता है। वह सिर्फ़ रविवार को ख़ाली है, तो वह रविवार कहता है। वह बताती है कि अगला रविवार 10 तारीख़ है और 11 को टेस्ट है, इसलिए दोनों टेस्ट के बाद जाने पर राज़ी होते हैं।

GU: તે તેને ફિલ્મ માટે બોલાવે છે. તે ફક્ત રવિવારે નવરી છે, એટલે તે રવિવાર કહે છે. તે જણાવે છે કે આવતો રવિવાર 10 તારીખ છે અને 11મીએ ટેસ્ટ છે, એટલે બંને ટેસ્ટ પછી જવા સંમત થાય છે.

The line the answer hinges on

11日はテストですよ。テストが終わってから行きましょう。Juuichinichi wa tesuto desu yo. Tesuto ga owatte kara ikimashou.

EN: "The 11th is the test. Let's go after the test is over." HI: "11 तारीख़ को टेस्ट है। टेस्ट ख़त्म होने के बाद चलते हैं।" GU: "11મીએ ટેસ્ટ છે. ટેસ્ટ પૂરી થયા પછી જઈએ."

学校で、先生と学生が話しています。学生は、このあと何をしますか。Gakkou de, sensei to gakusei ga hanashite imasu. Gakusei wa, kono ato nani o shimasu ka.

EN: A teacher and a student are talking at school. What will the student do next?
HI: स्कूल में शिक्षिका और छात्र बात कर रहे हैं। छात्र इसके बाद क्या करेगा?
GU: શાળામાં શિક્ષિકા અને વિદ્યાર્થી વાત કરે છે. વિદ્યાર્થી હવે શું કરશે?
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: The teacher asks Lin if his essay is finished. He says not yet — he'll write it at home tonight and bring it tomorrow. She says no, write it here now; she'll be at school until 4, so he should bring it to her room when done.

HI: शिक्षिका लिन से पूछती हैं कि निबंध पूरा हुआ या नहीं। वह कहता है अभी नहीं — आज रात घर पर लिखकर कल लाएगा। वे कहती हैं नहीं, अभी यहीं लिखो; वे 4 बजे तक स्कूल में हैं, लिखकर उनके कमरे में ले आओ।

GU: શિક્ષિકા લિનને પૂછે છે કે નિબંધ પૂરો થયો કે નહીં. તે કહે છે હજી નહીં — આજે રાત્રે ઘરે લખીને કાલે લાવશે. તેઓ કહે છે ના, અત્યારે અહીં જ લખો; તેઓ 4 વાગ્યા સુધી શાળામાં છે, લખીને તેમના રૂમમાં લાવો.

Exam trap 1 — the first plan is not the answer: in もんだい1 1ばん, 3ばん, 4ばん and 7ばん the first day, time, stamps or bus number mentioned is changed later (でも, もっと早く, いいえ、ちがいますよ). Keep listening until the end before choosing.
Exam trap 2 — "わたしも そうです" copies the other person's facts: in もんだい2 3ばん and 5ばん the woman agrees with the man and then adds one difference (でも…). Write down both people's facts.
Exam trap 3 — 即時応答: answer the question word: in もんだい4 the wrong replies answer a similar question (いくつ for いくら, how long for 何で) or echo a sound (本田 → 本). Identify the question word first.
