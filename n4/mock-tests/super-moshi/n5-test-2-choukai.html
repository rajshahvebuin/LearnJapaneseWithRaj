1 Points — what each question type tests

(Added revision table, not in the book.) What each もんだい type tests and what to listen for in this paper. For more practice, see the site's N5 listening books.

TypeWhat is asked in this paperWhat to listen for
もんだい1 課題理解kadai rikaiWhat will happen / what is chosen: what the patient takes off, what to do first, what to buy, where to put a vase, which coat, which person.The question comes first, so you know the target. Requests get changed (コーヒーをお願いします instead of お茶), and descriptions add up (white + black buttons + 3 pockets). Collect every condition before you choose.
もんだい2 ポイント理解pointo rikaiOne detail: a price, a shop, transport, days off, a drink, what someone does on the train.Read the four printed options during the pause. Most of them are mentioned; the answer is the one that fits the exact question (the taxi fare, not the fare per person; where they go now, not later).
もんだい3 発話表現hatsuwa hyougenWhat does the person with the arrow say?Look at the picture: visiting a house, refusing sugar, asking to see a photo, offering a seat, asking who someone is. These are set phrases: ごめんください, 〜ないでください, 見せてください, どうぞ, どなたですか.
もんだい4 即時応答sokuji outouThe natural reply to one line.Nothing is printed. Check the type of line: invitation (〜ませんか), greeting (お元気ですか), もう…か question, offer (いかがですか). Wrong replies often repeat a word from the question in the wrong form.

もんだい1 1ばん CD2 · Track 30

病院で、男の人が先生と話しています。男の人は、どうなりますか。Byouin de, otoko no hito ga sensei to hanashite imasu. Otoko no hito wa, dou narimasu ka.

EN: At a hospital, a man is talking to the doctor. How will the man end up (dressed)?
HI: अस्पताल में एक आदमी डॉक्टर से बात कर रहा है। आदमी आख़िर में कैसा दिखेगा?
GU: હૉસ્પિટલમાં એક પુરુષ ડૉક્ટર સાથે વાત કરે છે. પુરુષ છેવટે કેવો દેખાશે?
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: He has had a stomach ache since yesterday. The doctor asks him to take off his jacket and lie on the bed — and to take his shoes off too. He asks about his shirt; the doctor says he may keep it on.

HI: उसे कल से पेट में दर्द है। डॉक्टर कहती हैं कि जैकेट उतारकर बिस्तर पर लेट जाओ — और जूते भी उतारो। वह कमीज़ के बारे में पूछता है; डॉक्टर कहती हैं वह पहने रह सकता है।

GU: તેને ગઈ કાલથી પેટમાં દુખાવો છે. ડૉક્ટર કહે છે કે જૅકેટ ઉતારીને પલંગ પર સૂઈ જાઓ — અને જૂતાં પણ ઉતારો. તે શર્ટ વિશે પૂછે છે; ડૉક્ટર કહે છે તે પહેરી રાખી શકે છે.

学校で、先生が学生に話しています。学生は、はじめに何をしますか。Gakkou de, sensei ga gakusei ni hanashite imasu. Gakusei wa, hajime ni nani o shimasu ka.

EN: At school, a teacher is speaking to the students. What will the students do first?
HI: स्कूल में शिक्षिका छात्रों से बात कर रही हैं। छात्र सबसे पहले क्या करेंगे?
GU: શાળામાં શિક્ષિકા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ સાથે વાત કરે છે. વિદ્યાર્થીઓ સૌથી પહેલાં શું કરશે?
Script summary (not the book's text)

EN: There will be a conversation test. In a box are slips of paper with numbers; each student takes one. The test goes in number order: when the teacher calls your number, you enter the classroom. The test is one at a time, so the others sit and wait.

HI: बातचीत की परीक्षा होगी। एक डिब्बे में नंबर लिखी पर्चियाँ हैं; हर छात्र एक लेगा। परीक्षा नंबर के क्रम से होगी: शिक्षिका नंबर बुलाएँ तो कक्षा में आना है। एक-एक करके परीक्षा होगी, तो बाक़ी बैठकर इंतज़ार करेंगे।

GU: વાતચીતની પરીક્ષા છે. એક ડબ્બામાં નંબર લખેલી ચિઠ્ઠીઓ છે; દરેક વિદ્યાર્થી એક લેશે. પરીક્ષા નંબરના ક્રમે થશે: શિક્ષિકા નંબર બોલાવે ત્યારે વર્ગમાં આવવાનું. એક-એક કરીને પરીક્ષા છે, એટલે બાકીના બેસીને રાહ જોશે.

教室で、女の人と男の人が話しています。女の人は、何を買いますか。Kyoushitsu de, onna no hito to otoko no hito ga hanashite imasu. Onna no hito wa, nani o kaimasu ka.

GU: તે બેન્તો (ડબ્બાનું જમવાનું) ખરીદવા જાય છે અને પૂછે છે કે તેને કંઈ જોઈએ છે. તે પીણું માગે છે. તે કહે છે પોતાના માટે ચા લે છે, એટલે બે ચા લાવશે — પણ તે કહે છે તેનું જમવાનું બ્રેડ છે, એટલે કૉફી જોઈએ.

Exam trap 1 — numbers you can calculate are not the answer: in もんだい2 1ばん the script gives 750, 250 and 210 yen, and 630 (210 × 3) is printed too. Answer exactly what the question asks — the taxi fare — and do not do maths the question does not ask for.
Exam trap 2 — the request changes, and conditions pile up: in もんだい1 3ばん (tea → coffee), 2ばん 4ばん (Friday → Thursday) and 2ばん 5ばん (cold coffee → cold tea) the first request is replaced, while in もんだい1 5ばん and 7ばん each new line adds one more condition. Keep listening to the end.
