OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しけんshikenexamपरीक्षाપરીક્ષા

Answer 2 (別冊 p.16). Why (added, not in book): 用事が ある = have something (to do): the standard polite excuse for saying no — ちょっと 用事が あって… 1 borrows the pattern 〜が わるくて from つごうが わるい／ぐあいが わるい, which are different words.

Q35

はっきりhakkiri

Word: はっきりhakkiri — clearly, distinctly
Correct sentence (3): もっと　はっきり　いって　ください。Motto hakkiri itte kudasai.
EN: Please say it more clearly.
HI: कृपया और साफ़-साफ़ बोलिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને વધુ સ્પષ્ટ બોલો.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1あさごはんを　はっきり　たべました。Asagohan o hakkiri tabemashita.✗ Means "I ate a proper / big breakfast" — needs しっかり (shikkari) or たくさん.✗ अर्थ: "ठीक से/भरपेट नाश्ता किया" — しっかり चाहिए।✗ અર્થ: "બરાબર/પેટ ભરીને નાસ્તો કર્યો" — しっかり જોઈએ.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.16). Why (added, not in book): はっきり = clear, easy to see, hear or understand: はっきり 言う／見える／きこえる; also 天気が はっきりしない (unsettled). It describes clarity, not amount (1) or speed (2, 4). Do not mix it up with しっかり (properly) — see the table below.

Exam trap 1 — 漢字読み: watch っ, long vowels and voicing: in Q1, Q3, Q4 and Q5 the wrong readings differ from the right one only by a small っ (じっけん, いっかい), a long vowel (くき / くうき) or a voiced sound (うんでん, じけん). Say the word slowly in your head before choosing.
Exam trap 2 — 表記: check both kanji: in Q11 and Q15 every wrong option shares one kanji with the answer (以外, 意見, 地理). A word "looks right" if one kanji matches — make sure the other one does too.
Exam trap 3 — 用法: ask "what does this sentence really want?": for each wrong option in もんだい5, try to name the word that should be there (ぬらす, はる, すぐ, しばらく…). If you can name a better word, that option is wrong.
