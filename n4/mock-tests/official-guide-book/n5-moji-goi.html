Q1

新しい くるまですね。Atarashii kuruma desu ne.

EN: That's a new car, isn't it?
HI: यह नई गाड़ी है, है ना?
GU: આ નવી ગાડી છે, ખરું ને?
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1あたらしいatarashiinew ✓नयाનવું
2あだらしいadarashiinot a word (た made voiced)कोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી
3あらたしいaratashiinot a word (ら and た swapped)कोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી
4あらだしいaradashiinot a word (swapped and voiced)कोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી

Answer 1 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): 新しい = あ・た・ら・しい. The wrong options only swap the order of た and ら or add the voicing marks (゛). Say the word aloud slowly: a-ta-ra-shii. Memory hook (ours): あたらしい and あらた (新た, "anew", a higher-level word) use the same two sounds in a different order — that is why the swap is a trap.

Q2

電気を つけて ください。Denki o tsukete kudasai.

EN: Please turn on the light.
HI: कृपया बत्ती जलाइए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને લાઇટ ચાલુ કરો.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1てんぎtenginot a wordकोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી
2てんきtenkiweather (天気) — a different wordमौसम (天気)હવામાન (天気)
3でんぎdenginot a wordकोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી
4でんきdenkielectricity, the light ✓बिजली, बत्तीવીજળી, લાઇટ

Answer 4 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): 電 = でん (as in 電車 でんしゃ, train), 気 = き (as in 元気 げんき, 天気 てんき). So: でん + き. The trap is the voicing marks: て/で and き/ぎ. てんき (天気, weather) is a real word, which makes option 2 the most tempting wrong answer. 電気を つける = turn on the light; 電気を けす = turn it off.

Answer 4 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): そと = 外 = 夕 (evening) + 卜. Two checks: (a) the left part must be 夕 with its small inside stroke — options 1 and 2 draw it as plain ク, which is not a kanji; (b) the right part must be 卜, not 刂 — 列 (れつ, a line of people) has 刂. Only option 4 passes both. Memory hook (ours): in the evening (夕) you go outside (外).

Q4

あの ほてるは ゆうめいです。Ano hoteru wa yuumei desu.

EN: That hotel is famous.
HI: वह होटल मशहूर है।
GU: તે હોટલ પ્રખ્યાત છે.
OptionWritten formEnglishHindiGujarati
1ホラルhorarunot a word (ラ = ra)कोई शब्द नहीं (ラ = रा)કોઈ શબ્દ નથી (ラ = રા)
2ホテルhoteruhotel ✓होटलહોટલ
3ホラハhorahanot a word (ラ, ハ)कोई शब्द नहींકોઈ શબ્દ નથી
4ホテハhotehanot a word (ハ = ha)कोई शब्द नहीं (ハ = हा)કોઈ શબ્દ નથી (ハ = હા)

Answer 2 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): ほてる in katakana = ホテル. テ (te) has three strokes — two short lines on top and a sloping one; ラ (ra) has one short line on top. ル (ru) has two strokes, the right one hooked; ハ (ha) is two strokes that open outwards like the hiragana は without its left side. Foreign words such as hotel are always written in katakana.

EN: Over there, I (　) a taxi.
HI: वहाँ मैंने टैक्सी (　)।
GU: ત્યાં મેં ટૅક્સી (　).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1のりましたnorimashitagot into, took ✓(टैक्सी) ली, बैठा(ટૅક્સી) લીધી, બેઠો
2あがりましたagarimashitawent upऊपर गयाઉપર ગયો
3つきましたtsukimashitaarrivedपहुँचाપહોંચ્યો
4はいりましたhairimashitawent in, enteredअंदर गयाઅંદર ગયો

Answer 1 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): vehicles take のる with に: タクシーに のる, でんしゃに のる, バスに のる. All four verbs can follow に (へやに はいる, かいだんを あがる, えきに つく), so the particle alone does not decide it — the noun タクシー does. You don't "enter" a taxi in Japanese; you "ride" it.

ゆうべ vs おとといyuube / ototoilast night vs the day before yesterdayゆうべ ＝ きのうの よるyuube = kinou no yoruゆうべ is one time (last night); おととい is a whole day (two days ago).Q8.
Exam trap 1 — voicing marks in 漢字読み: N5 reading options often differ only by ゛ (て/で, き/ぎ, た/だ). Learn each word with its exact sound, and notice when a wrong option is still a real word (てんき).
Exam trap 2 — fake kanji in 表記: a wrong option may be a shape that is not a kanji at all (Q3: 夕 drawn as ク). Check every part: the dot inside 夕, and 卜 vs 刂 on the right.
Exam trap 3 — the 2 × 2 grid in 言い換え: when the options combine two choices (きのう / おととい × ひる / よる), translate the underlined word into both halves first, then find the one box that matches.
