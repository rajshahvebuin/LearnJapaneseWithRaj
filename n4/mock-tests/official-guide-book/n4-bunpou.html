2 Every question — もんだい1–3 (p.33–37)

Try each item in your copy first, then check here. The highlighted row is our answer — the book's N4 answer page is not in our scan, so all answers below are ours. Names in the book (大川, 山下, 山田, アリ, 田中) are just example people.

もんだい1 — 文法形式の判断: what goes in the brackets? (1–2, p.33)

（　　）に 何を 入れますか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。(　) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in the brackets? Choose the best one. HI: कोष्ठक में क्या आएगा? सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: કૌંસમાં શું આવશે? સૌથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. — 例: わたしは 毎朝 新聞（を）読みます。Watashi wa maiasa shinbun (o) yomimasu. (I read the newspaper every morning.) Options 1 が 2 の 3 を 4 で — answer 3 を, marked on the sample answer sheet printed in the book.

Q1

A「わたしの けしゴム、見ませんでしたか。」A: "Watashi no keshigomu, mimasen deshita ka."

B「あ、つくえの 下に（　　）よ。」B: "A, tsukue no shita ni (　) yo."

EN: A: "Haven't you seen my eraser?" B: "Oh, it's (　) under the desk."
HI: A: "क्या आपने मेरा रबर देखा?" B: "अरे, मेज़ के नीचे (　)।"
GU: A: "તમે મારું રબર જોયું?" B: "અરે, ટેબલ નીચે (　)."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1おちて いますochite imasuhas fallen and is lying there ✓गिरा पड़ा हैપડેલું છે
2おちて いませんochite imasenis not lying thereनहीं गिरा पड़ा हैપડેલું નથી
3おちますochimasuwill fall / fallsगिरेगा / गिरता हैપડશે / પડે છે
4おちませんochimasenwill not fallनहीं गिरेगाનહીં પડે

Our answer: 1 (our answer, not in book). Why: B has just found the eraser — it fell earlier and is now lying under the desk. With a "change" verb like おちる (fall), あく (open) or こわれる (break), 〜て いる describes the state left after the change, not an action in progress. B's あ、 ("oh!") shows B has spotted it, so the negative (2, 4) does not fit, and おちます (3) would talk about a fall that has not happened yet.

Q2

（病院で）(Byouin de)

A「先生、もう 薬を 飲まなくても いいですか。」A: "Sensei, mou kusuri o nomanakute mo ii desu ka."

B「いいえ、来週の 月曜までは（　　）。」B: "Iie, raishuu no getsuyou made wa (　)."

EN: (At a hospital) A: "Doctor, is it all right if I stop taking the medicine now?" B: "No, until next Monday (　)."
HI: (अस्पताल में) A: "डॉक्टर साहब, क्या अब दवा लेना बंद कर सकता हूँ?" B: "नहीं, अगले सोमवार तक (　)।"
GU: (હૉસ્પિટલમાં) A: "ડૉક્ટર, હવે દવા ન લઉં તો ચાલે?" B: "ના, આવતા સોમવાર સુધી (　)."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1飲んでも いいですnonde mo ii desuyou may take itले सकते हैंલઈ શકો છો
2飲まないで くださいnomanaide kudasaiplease don't take itकृपया मत लीजिएકૃપા કરીને ન લો
3飲んで くださいnonde kudasaiplease take it ✓कृपया लीजिएકૃપા કરીને લો
4飲んでは いけませんnonde wa ikemasenyou must not take itबिल्कुल मत लीजिएબિલકુલ ન લેવી

Our answer: 3 (our answer, not in book). Why: the patient asks "may I stop?" and the doctor says いいえ (no) — so the patient must keep taking it until Monday: 飲んで ください. Options 2 and 4 say "don't take it", which would be "yes, you can stop". Option 1 (you may take it) is too weak — the doctor is giving an instruction, not a permission.

もんだい2 — 文の組み立て: put the pieces in order (3–4, p.34)

＿★＿に 入る ものは どれですか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。Hoshi ni hairu mono wa dore desu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Choose the best one. HI: ★ वाली जगह में कौन-सा टुकड़ा आएगा? सबसे सही चुनें। GU: ★ વાળી જગ્યામાં કયો ટુકડો આવશે? સૌથી સાચો પસંદ કરો.

The book's worked sample (問題例 and 答え方, p.34): つくえの ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ あります。Tsukue no ___ ___ ★ ___ arimasu. Pieces 1 が 2 に 3 上 4 ペン. Step 1 — make the correct sentence: つくえの 上に ペンが あります。Tsukue no ue ni pen ga arimasu. (There is a pen on the desk.) Order 3 → 2 → 4 → 1. Step 2 — blacken the number in the ★ slot (the 3rd) = 4 ペン. EN: build the whole sentence first, then mark only the ★ piece. HI: पहले पूरा वाक्य बनाएँ, फिर सिर्फ़ ★ वाला टुकड़ा चिह्नित करें। GU: પહેલાં આખું વાક્ય બનાવો, પછી ફક્ત ★ વાળો ટુકડો ચિહ્નિત કરો. Full orders below are worked out by us (the book's N4 key is missing from the scan).

Q3

大川「山下さん、おれいの 手紙は もう 出しましたか。」Ookawa: "Yamashita-san, orei no tegami wa mou dashimashita ka."

山下「いいえ、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ です。」Yamashita: "Iie, ___ ___ ★ ___ desu."

Pieces: 1 書いて (kaite)　2 今 (ima)　3 ところ (tokoro)　4 いる (iru)

2 今→1 書いて→4 いる→3 ところ

いいえ、今 書いて いる ところです。Iie, ima kaite iru tokoro desu.

EN: Okawa: "Yamashita-san, have you already sent the thank-you letter?" Yamashita: "No, I'm writing it right now."
HI: ओओकावा: "यामाशिता जी, क्या धन्यवाद-पत्र भेज दिया?" यामाशिता: "नहीं, अभी लिख ही रहा हूँ।"
GU: ઓકાવા: "યામાશિતાજી, આભારપત્ર મોકલી દીધો?" યામાશિતા: "ના, હમણાં લખી જ રહ્યો છું."

Our answer: ★ = 4 いる (order worked out by us; our answer, not in book). Why: 〜て いる ところです = "I'm in the middle of ~ing". 今 goes first (it is a time word, it cannot sit between 書いて and いる), then 書いて いる, then ところ just before the printed です. ★ is the 3rd slot → いる.

Q4

山田さんは ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ です。Yamada-san wa ___ ★ ___ ___ desu.

Pieces: 1 上手 (jouzu)　2 ギターも (gitaa mo)　3 ひけるし (hikeru shi)　4 歌も (uta mo)

2 ギターも→3 ひけるし→4 歌も→1 上手

山田さんは ギターも ひけるし 歌も 上手です。Yamada-san wa gitaa mo hikeru shi uta mo jouzu desu.

EN: Yamada-san can play the guitar, and he is good at singing too.
HI: यामादा जी गिटार भी बजा सकते हैं और गाना भी अच्छा गाते हैं।
GU: યામાદાજી ગિટાર પણ વગાડી શકે છે અને ગાય પણ સરસ છે.

Our answer: ★ = 3 ひけるし (order worked out by us; our answer, not in book). Why: ひく (play) goes with ギター, not with 歌 — you cannot "play" a song with ひく here. So ギターも ひけるし is one block and 歌も 上手 is the other; 上手 must touch the printed です (上手です). ★ is the 2nd slot this time → ひけるし. Note: the ★ is not always in the 3rd slot — read where it is.

文章

The letter — アリさんが 友だちの 田中さんに 書いた 手紙 (Ari-san ga tomodachi no Tanaka-san ni kaita tegami — a letter Ali wrote to his friend Tanaka) (p.36)

Summary (not the book's text): Ali tells Tanaka that he moved last week to a flat near his university. Before, the trip to university took about an hour and a half by train and bus; now it is a 10-minute walk, which is why he moved. The new flat is a bit small but new and clean, with a supermarket nearby and the station close too, and the view from the window is lovely. He is glad he moved and is sending a photo of the view with the letter. (Dated 2010年2月25日.)
HI (सार): अली तानाका को बताता है कि वह पिछले हफ़्ते विश्वविद्यालय के पास एक फ़्लैट में आ गया। पहले ट्रेन और बस से डेढ़ घंटा लगता था; अब पैदल 10 मिनट, इसीलिए उसने घर बदला। नया फ़्लैट थोड़ा छोटा है पर नया और साफ़ है, पास में सुपरमार्केट और स्टेशन भी है, और खिड़की से नज़ारा सुंदर है। वह ख़ुश है और पत्र के साथ नज़ारे की फ़ोटो भेज रहा है।
GU (સાર): અલી તાનાકાને જણાવે છે કે તે ગયા અઠવાડિયે યુનિવર્સિટી પાસેના ફ્લૅટમાં રહેવા આવ્યો. પહેલાં ટ્રેન અને બસથી દોઢ કલાક લાગતો; હવે ચાલીને 10 મિનિટ, એટલે તેણે ઘર બદલ્યું. નવો ફ્લૅટ થોડો નાનો છે પણ નવો અને સ્વચ્છ છે, પાસે સુપરમાર્કેટ અને સ્ટેશન પણ છે, અને બારીમાંથી દૃશ્ય સુંદર છે. તે ખુશ છે અને પત્ર સાથે દૃશ્યનો ફોટો મોકલે છે.
Q5

（前は…1時間半ぐらい かかりました。）でも、今の アパート［5］大学まで 歩いて 10分ぐらいです。(Mae wa … ichijikan han gurai kakarimashita.) Demo, ima no apaato [5] daigaku made aruite juppun gurai desu.

Our answer: 1 (our answer, not in book). Why: Ali is describing his own life after the move, so a change: 〜に なりました. 2 (そうです = hearsay) is wrong because it is his own experience, not something he heard. 3 gives a reason, but nothing in the sentence asks "why". 4 is a doubtful question — but he already lives there and knows.

Q8

だから、ひっこしを して 本当に よかったと［8］。Dakara, hikkoshi o shite hontou ni yokatta to [8].

EN: So I (think) it was really good that I moved.
HI: इसलिए मुझे (लगता है) कि घर बदलना सचमुच अच्छा रहा।
GU: એટલે મને (લાગે છે) કે ઘર બદલ્યું તે ખરેખર સારું થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1思う ようですomou you desu(someone) seems to think(कोई) सोचता हुआ लगता है(કોઈ) વિચારતું લાગે છે

Q9

この 手紙と いっしょに、わたしの へやの まどから 見た けしきの 写真を［9］。Kono tegami to issho ni, watashi no heya no mado kara mita keshiki no shashin o [9].

EN: Together with this letter, (I'm sending) a photo of the view I saw from my room's window.
HI: इस पत्र के साथ, मेरे कमरे की खिड़की से दिखे नज़ारे की फ़ोटो (भेज रहा हूँ)।

Exam trap 1 — answering "no": in Q2 the doctor's いいえ cancels the patient's idea. Work out what "no" means in that exact question before you look at the options. HI: "नहीं" से क्या मतलब निकलता है, पहले यह सोचें। GU: "ના"નો અર્થ શું થાય, પહેલાં તે વિચારો.
Exam trap 2 — the ★ moves: in Q3 the ★ is in the 3rd slot, in Q4 it is in the 2nd. Always build the whole sentence on paper first, then count to the ★. HI: पहले पूरा वाक्य बनाएँ, फिर ★ तक गिनें। GU: પહેલાં આખું વાક્ય બનાવો, પછી ★ સુધી ગણો.
