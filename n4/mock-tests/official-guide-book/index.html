公式ガイドブック N4・N5

Following 新しい「日本語能力試験」ガイドブック 概要版と問題例集 N4, N5 (Official Guide Book for the new JLPT, N4 and N5, 2009) — the official guide that introduced today's five-level test. It is not a full mock test: for each level it gives a small sample of every question type (問題例), usually two items per 問題. The book covers both N4 and N5, so this course has N4 units and N5 units (the N5 ones are marked "N5 level"), plus one unit on the book's overview of the test. Every page gives every question with an answer and simple explanations in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji. The N5 answers are the book's official ones; the N4 answer page is missing from our scan, so N4 answers are worked out by us and marked as ours. The book has no explanations of its own. Reading passages and listening scripts are summarised, not reproduced; audio tracks are referred to by track number only.
