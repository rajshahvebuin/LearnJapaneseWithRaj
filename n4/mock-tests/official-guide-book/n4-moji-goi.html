Notes
This is a guide book, not a full test: it shows two sample items for each question type. On the real N4 paper (as listed in the book's table on p.22) there are 9 漢字読み, 6 表記, 10 文脈規定, 5 言い換え類義 and 5 用法 items, 30 minutes in all (the 2009 figures; check jlpt.jp for today's counts). The book has no explanations: every "Why", every table in §1 and §3, and all English, Hindi and Gujarati on this page are ours (added, not in book). The test sentences are written mostly in kana with spaces between words, as on the real N4 paper. In もんだい2 Q4 two options use a made-up look-alike kanji; it cannot be typed, so we describe it in words.

Q1

あには バスで 会社に 通って います。Ani wa basu de kaisha ni kayotte imasu.

EN: My older brother commutes to his company by bus.
HI: मेरा बड़ा भाई बस से अपनी कंपनी आता-जाता है।
GU: મારો મોટો ભાઈ બસથી કંપનીએ આવ-જા કરે છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1むかってmukatteheading towards (向かって)की ओर जाते हुएતરફ જતાં
2かよってkayottecommuting (通って) ✓आना-जाना करते हुएઆવ-જા કરતાં
3わたってwatattecrossing (渡って)पार करते हुएપાર કરતાં
4もどってmodottegoing back (戻って)लौटते हुएપાછા ફરતાં

Our answer: 2 (our answer, not in book). Why: 通う (かよう) = to go somewhere regularly — to school, to work, to a gym. 〜に 通って います = "goes to ~ regularly". 通 is also read とおる (通る, to pass through); all four options are real て-forms, so only the kanji tells you which one.

Q2

わたしの せんもんは 医学です。Watashi no senmon wa igaku desu.

EN: My speciality (major) is medicine.
HI: मेरा विषय (विशेषज्ञता) चिकित्सा विज्ञान है।
GU: મારો વિષય (વિશેષતા) તબીબી વિજ્ઞાન છે.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1かがくkagakuscience (科学) / chemistry (化学)विज्ञान / रसायनવિજ્ઞાન / રસાયણ
2いがくigakumedicine ✓चिकित्सा विज्ञानતબીબી વિજ્ઞાન

Our answer: 2 (our answer, not in book). Why: 医 = い (as in 医者 いしゃ, doctor), 学 = がく. All four are "〜がく" subjects, so the first kanji decides it. せんもん (専門) = your special field of study.

ふねで にもつを おくります。Fune de nimotsu o okurimasu.

HI: मैं सामान जहाज़ से भेजूँगा।

OptionWritten formEnglishHindiGujarati

Our answer: 4 (our answer, not in book). Why: おくる (send) = 送る. All four kanji share the 辶 "road" part on the left and bottom, so look at the top-right part: 送 has two short strokes over 天. Memory hook (ours): things you send travel along the road 辶.

Q4

毎日 ラジオで おんがくを 聞いて います。Mainichi rajio de ongaku o kiite imasu.

EN: I listen to music on the radio every day.
HI: मैं हर दिन रेडियो पर संगीत सुनता हूँ।
GU: હું દરરોજ રેડિયો પર સંગીત સાંભળું છું.
OptionWritten formEnglishHindiGujarati
1音楽ongakumusic ✓संगीतસંગીત
2(fake 音) + 楽look-alike shape, not a real kanjithe first kanji has 口 at the bottom instead of 日 — not a real kanjiपहला कांजी नक़ली है (日 की जगह 口)પહેલો કાંજી નકલી છે (日 ની જગ્યાએ 口)
3音薬(薬 = kusuri, "medicine") — not a wordsecond kanji is 薬 (medicine), not 楽दूसरा कांजी 薬 (दवा) हैબીજો કાંજી 薬 (દવા) છે
4(fake 音) + 薬look-alike shape + wrong kanjiboth kanji wrongदोनों कांजी ग़लतબંને કાંજી ખોટા

Our answer: 1 (our answer, not in book). Why: おんがく = 音 (sound) + 楽 (enjoy). Two checks: (a) 音 is 立 over 日 — options 2 and 4 put 口 there, a shape that is not 音; (b) 楽 has no grass-crown 艹 on top — 薬 (くすり, medicine) is 楽 with 艹 added. Only option 1 passes both. Memory hook (ours): medicine comes from plants, so 薬 wears the plant crown.

GU: સુપરમાર્કેટમાં મળેલી (　) જોવાથી ખબર પડે કે શું ખરીદ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1レジrejicheckout counter, tillबिलिंग काउंटरબિલ કાઉન્ટર
2レシートreshiitoreceipt ✓रसीदરસીદ

HI: (　) मत कीजिए, कृपया ख़ूब खाइए।
GU: (　) ન કરો, કૃપા કરીને ખૂબ ખાઓ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1しつれいshitsureirudeness; excuse meअशिष्टता; माफ़ कीजिएઅસભ્યતા; માફ કરજો

Q7

だいどころに りょうりが のこって います。Daidokoro ni ryouri ga nokotte imasu.

EN: There is food left over in the kitchen.
HI: रसोई में खाना बचा हुआ है।
GU: રસોડામાં ખાવાનું બાકી રહ્યું છે.
OptionSentenceEnglishHindiGujarati
1りょうりは だいどころに まだ あります。Ryouri wa daidokoro ni mada arimasu.The food is still in the kitchen. ✓खाना अभी भी रसोई में है।ખાવાનું હજી રસોડામાં છે.
2りょうりは だいどころに もう ありません。Ryouri wa daidokoro ni mou arimasen.There is no food left in the kitchen any more.रसोई में अब खाना नहीं है।રસોડામાં હવે ખાવાનું નથી.
3だいどころで りょうりを つくって います。Daidokoro de ryouri o tsukutte imasu.Someone is cooking in the kitchen.रसोई में खाना बना रहे हैं।રસોડામાં રસોઈ બનાવે છે.

Our answer: 1 (our answer, not in book). Why: のこって いる = is left (not eaten yet) = まだ ある (is still there). Option 2 is the exact opposite (もう ない = none left). Options 3 and 4 keep the words だいどころ and りょうり but change the action — the usual 言い換え trap.

Our answer: 3 (our answer, not in book). Why: けんぶつ (見物) is going round a place — a town, a temple, a festival — for fun. One flower (1) or a TV programme (2) is simply 見る. A factory visit for work or study (4) is けんがく (見学, a study visit), not けんぶつ.

Q10

じゅうしょjuusho

EN: address — where someone lives or where a letter is sent
HI: पता — जहाँ कोई रहता है या जहाँ चिट्ठी भेजी जाती है
GU: સરનામું — જ્યાં કોઈ રહે છે અથવા જ્યાં પત્ર મોકલાય છે

1としょかんの となりの じゅうしょは ゆうびんきょくです。Toshokan no tonari no juusho wa yuubinkyoku desu.The "address" next to the library is the post office. (wrong — たてもの)पुस्तकालय के बगल का "पता" डाकघर है। (ग़लत)પુસ્તકાલયની બાજુનું "સરનામું" પોસ્ટ ઑફિસ છે. (ખોટું)

Our answer: 3 (our answer, not in book). Why: じゅうしょ (住所) is a postal address — the place you live, written on forms and letters. A building (1) is たてもの; where a meeting is held (2) is ばしょ (場所); an e-mail address (4) is メールアドレス. English "address" covers all of these; Japanese splits them.

Exam trap 1 — fake kanji in 表記: a wrong option may be a real kanji with a shared part (Q3: all have 辶) or a shape that is not a kanji at all (Q4: 音 with 口 inside). Check every part of the character, not just its outline.
Exam trap 2 — same words, different action in 言い換え: wrong options reuse the nouns of the underlined sentence (だいどころ, りょうり) but change the verb or turn it into its opposite (まだ ある vs もう ない). Match the meaning of the verb, not the nouns.
Exam trap 3 — English thinking in 用法: the wrong sentences in もんだい5 are often what an English speaker would say ("sightsee a flower", "e-mail address" = じゅうしょ). Ask: what kind of thing does this word go with in Japanese?
