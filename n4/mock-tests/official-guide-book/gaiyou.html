1 Points — the overview chapter (概要, p.7–17)

1 新しい「日本語能力試験」について — about the new test (p.8) (Atarashii "Nihongo Nouryoku Shiken" ni tsuite)

EN (summary): The JLPT measures and certifies the Japanese of people whose first language is not Japanese. It has been run by the Japan Foundation (国際交流基金) and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (日本国際教育支援協会) since 1984; in 2008 about 560,000 people took it worldwide. Test-takers had become more varied, and many now took it for jobs, pay rises or promotion, not only to measure their level. So, using 20+ years of research in Japanese teaching and testing and the data from past tests, the organisers revised the test and started the new JLPT in 2010.
HI (सार): JLPT उन लोगों की जापानी भाषा की क्षमता मापता और प्रमाणित करता है जिनकी मातृभाषा जापानी नहीं है। इसे 1984 से जापान फ़ाउंडेशन और JEES चलाते हैं; 2008 में दुनिया भर में लगभग 5.6 लाख लोगों ने यह परीक्षा दी। परीक्षार्थी अलग-अलग तरह के हो गए थे, और कई लोग नौकरी, वेतन-वृद्धि या पदोन्नति के लिए भी परीक्षा देने लगे थे। इसलिए 20 साल से ज़्यादा के शोध और पुराने परिणामों के आँकड़ों के आधार पर परीक्षा बदली गई और 2010 से नया JLPT शुरू हुआ।
GU (સાર): JLPT એવા લોકોની જાપાની ભાષાની ક્ષમતા માપે અને પ્રમાણિત કરે છે જેમની માતૃભાષા જાપાની નથી. તે 1984 થી જાપાન ફાઉન્ડેશન અને JEES ચલાવે છે; 2008 માં દુનિયાભરમાં લગભગ 5.6 લાખ લોકોએ આ પરીક્ષા આપી. પરીક્ષાર્થીઓ જુદા-જુદા પ્રકારના થયા હતા, અને ઘણા નોકરી, પગારવધારો કે બઢતી માટે પણ પરીક્ષા આપવા લાગ્યા હતા. તેથી 20 વર્ષથી વધુના સંશોધન અને જૂનાં પરિણામોના આંકડાના આધારે પરીક્ષા બદલવામાં આવી અને 2010 થી નવી JLPT શરૂ થઈ.

3 認定の目安 — what each level means (p.10) (Nintei no meyasu)

The book describes each level with two language activities, 読む (reading) and 聞く (listening), and says each level also needs the language knowledge behind them. Our short versions:

LevelHeadline (book)EN (our summary)HindiGujarati
N1幅広い場面で使われる日本語を理解することができるhabahiroi bamen de tsukawareru nihongo o rikai suru koto ga dekiruUnderstands Japanese used in a wide range of situations. Reads logically complex or abstract writing (newspaper editorials, critiques) and follows the structure and nuance; follows lectures, news and conversations at natural speed in detail.हर तरह की स्थितियों की जापानी समझता है। जटिल या अमूर्त लेख (संपादकीय, समीक्षा) पढ़ लेता है; सामान्य गति के व्याख्यान, समाचार और बातचीत विस्तार से समझता है।દરેક પ્રકારની પરિસ્થિતિની જાપાની સમજે છે. જટિલ કે અમૂર્ત લખાણ (તંત્રીલેખ, સમીક્ષા) વાંચી લે છે; સામાન્ય ઝડપનાં વ્યાખ્યાન, સમાચાર અને વાતચીત વિગતે સમજે છે.
N2日常的な場面で使われる日本語の理解に加え、より幅広い場面で使われる日本語をある程度理解することができるnichijouteki na bamen de tsukawareru nihongo no rikai ni kuwae, yori habahiroi bamen de tsukawareru nihongo o aru teido rikai suru koto ga dekiruEveryday Japanese plus, to some degree, Japanese in wider situations. Reads clear articles and commentary in newspapers and magazines; follows news and conversations at near-natural speed.रोज़मर्रा की जापानी, और कुछ हद तक व्यापक स्थितियों की भी। अख़बार-पत्रिकाओं के स्पष्ट लेख पढ़ता है; लगभग स्वाभाविक गति की बातचीत और समाचार समझता है।રોજિંદી જાપાની, અને થોડે અંશે વ્યાપક પરિસ્થિતિઓની પણ. છાપાં-સામયિકોના સ્પષ્ટ લેખ વાંચે છે; લગભગ સ્વાભાવિક ઝડપની વાતચીત અને સમાચાર સમજે છે.
N3日常的な場面で使われる日本語をある程度理解することができるnichijouteki na bamen de tsukawareru nihongo o aru teido rikai suru koto ga dekiruUnderstands everyday Japanese to some degree. Reads concrete texts on everyday topics and gets the gist of headlines; follows everyday conversations at near-natural speed.रोज़मर्रा की जापानी कुछ हद तक समझता है। रोज़ के विषयों पर ठोस लेख और सुर्ख़ियों का सार पढ़ लेता है; लगभग स्वाभाविक गति की रोज़ की बातचीत समझता है।રોજિંદી જાપાની થોડે અંશે સમજે છે. રોજના વિષયો પર નક્કર લખાણ અને મથાળાંનો સાર વાંચી લે છે; લગભગ સ્વાભાવિક ઝડપની રોજની વાતચીત સમજે છે.
N4基本的な日本語を理解することができるkihonteki na nihongo o rikai suru koto ga dekiruUnderstands basic Japanese. Reads texts on familiar daily topics written with basic vocabulary and kanji. N5基本的な日本語をある程度理解することができるkihonteki na nihongo o aru teido rikai suru koto ga dekiruUnderstands basic Japanese to some degree. Reads set phrases and sentences in hiragana, katakana and basic kanji. EN (pass/fail, 合否の判定 gouhi no hantei): you pass only if your total reaches the pass mark and each scoring section reaches its own minimum (基準点 kijunten). Miss one section's minimum and you fail, however high the total. The book says the exact figures would be decided in 2010 — it gives no numbers. Score report: it shows ① the score for each section, ② the total, and ③ "reference information" (参考情報 sankou jouhou) — A / B / C grades for parts such as 文字・語彙 and 文法, which do not count towards pass/fail.

FormMeaningExampleKey differenceWhere
大問 vs 小問daimon / shoumonquestion type (block) vs single item大問1 漢字読み — 小問 9daimon ichi kanji yomi — shoumon kyuuA 大問 is a block like もんだい1; 小問 are the numbered items inside it. HI: 大問 = प्रश्न-खंड; 小問 = उसके भीतर के प्रश्न। GU: 大問 = પ્રશ્ન-વિભાગ; 小問 = તેની અંદરના પ્રશ્નો.p.14, p.18–23.
試験科目 vs 得点区分shiken kamoku / tokuten kubuntest section (what you sit) vs scoring section (how you are scored)N4: 科目 3つ ／ 得点区分 2つN4: kamoku mittsu / tokuten kubun futatsuAt N4 you sit three papers but get two scores: 言語知識・読解 (0–120) and 聴解 (0–60).p.11–12.
総合得点 vs 基準点sougou tokuten / kijuntentotal score vs minimum per section総合得点 120/180sougou tokuten hyaku nijuuYou need both: total over the pass line and every section over its minimum.p.13.
得点 vs 参考情報tokuten / sankou jouhouscore vs reference grade (A/B/C)文字・語彙 A ／ 文法 Cmoji・goi A / bunpou CReference grades help you study; they do not decide pass/fail.p.13.
級 vs Nkyuu / enuold levels 1–4 vs new levels N1–N53級 ≒ N4 ／ 4級 ≒ N5sankyuu ≒ N4 / yonkyuu ≒ N5N3 is the new in-between level; old and new scores cannot be compared.p.8, p.17.
現行試験 vs 新試験genkou shiken / shin shiken"current" test vs "new" test現行試験の1級genkou shiken no ikkyuuWritten in 2009: 現行 = the old pre-2010 test, 新 = today's test. Read the book with that in mind.throughout.
◆ vs ◇ vs ○kuro hishigata / shiro hishigata / marunew type vs changed type vs same type文の組み立て ◆ ／ 漢字読み ◇bun no kumitate / kanji yomi◆ types (★ ordering, text grammar, info search, 発話表現, 即時応答) are the ones old textbooks don't practise.p.18.
Note 1 — the numbers have changed: item counts and test times on this page are the book's 2009 plan. The real test has been adjusted since; always check jlpt.jp for the current figures. HI: ये 2009 के आँकड़े हैं — मौजूदा आँकड़ों के लिए jlpt.jp देखें। GU: આ 2009 ના આંકડા છે — હાલના આંકડા માટે jlpt.jp જુઓ.
Note 2 — one weak section can fail you: because of the per-section minimum (基準点), don't skip listening practice to focus only on vocabulary and grammar. HI: एक खंड कमज़ोर हो तो फ़ेल हो सकते हैं — सुनने का अभ्यास न छोड़ें। GU: એક વિભાગ નબળો હોય તો નાપાસ થઈ શકો — સાંભળવાનો અભ્યાસ ન છોડો.
Note 3 — practise the ◆ types: the new question types (★ sentence building, 文章の文法, 情報検索, 発話表現, 即時応答) feel unfamiliar at first — the sample units of this course show each one. HI: नए (◆) प्रश्न-प्रकारों का अभ्यास करें। GU: નવા (◆) પ્રશ્ન-પ્રકારોનો અભ્યાસ કરો.
