もんだい1 — 文法形式の判断: what goes in the brackets? (例 + 1–2, p.55)

（　　）に 何を 入れますか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。(　) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in the brackets? Choose the best one. HI: कोष्ठक में क्या आएगा? सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: કૌંસમાં શું આવશે? સૌથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. — 例 (printed with its answer): これ（　）えんぴつです。1 に 2 を 3 は 4 や → 3 は: これは えんぴつです。Kore wa enpitsu desu. EN: This is a pencil. HI: यह पेंसिल है। GU: આ પેન્સિલ છે.

Q1

弟は へや（　　）そうじを しました。Otouto wa heya (　) souji o shimashita.

EN: My younger brother did the cleaning (　) the room. → cleaned the room
HI: मेरे छोटे भाई ने कमरे (　) सफ़ाई की। → कमरे की सफ़ाई की
GU: મારા નાના ભાઈએ ઓરડા(　) સફાઈ કરી. → ઓરડાની સફાઈ કરી
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1がgasubject markerकर्ता चिह्नકર્તા ચિહ્ન
2をoobject markerकर्म चिह्नકર્મ ચિહ્ન
3にnito, at, inको, मेंને, માં
4のnoof ('s) ✓का / कीનો / ની

Answer 4 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): the sentence already has そうじを しました, so へや cannot take を too (one を per verb). へや and そうじ are two nouns side by side; nouns join with の: へやの そうじ = the cleaning of the room. Compare: へやを そうじしました (without the second を) means the same.

Q2

きのうは うちに（　　）何を しましたか。Kinou wa uchi ni (　) nani o shimashita ka.

EN: Yesterday, after you (got) home, what did you do?
HI: कल घर (पहुँचकर) आपने क्या किया?
GU: ગઈ કાલે ઘરે (પહોંચીને) તમે શું કર્યું?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かえるkaerugo home (plain form)घर जानाઘરે જવું
2かえるからkaeru karabecause (I) will go homeक्योंकि घर जाऊँगाકારણ કે ઘરે જઈશ
3かえってkaettewent home, and (then) ✓घर जाकरઘરે જઈને
4かえったりkaettaridoing things like going homeघर जाना वग़ैरहઘરે જવું વગેરે

Answer 3 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): the て-form joins two actions in order: かえって、何を しましたか = "you went home, and then what did you do?". 1 cannot sit directly before 何を; 2 gives a reason in the future, which clashes with the past しましたか; 〜たり needs a pair (〜たり 〜たり する).

もんだい2 — 文の組み立て: put the pieces in order (例 + 3–4, p.56–57)

＿★＿に 入る ものは どれですか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。Hoshi ni hairu mono wa dore desu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: Which piece goes in the ★ slot? HI: ★ वाली जगह में कौन-सा टुकड़ा आएगा? GU: ★ વાળી જગ્યામાં કયો ટુકડો આવશે? (p.56) — 問題例 (printed with its answer and the こたえかた "how to answer" box): A「あの 人は だれですか。」B「山田さんです。」A: "Ano hito wa dare desu ka." B: "Yamada-san desu." Order 3 あの人 → 2 は → 4 だれ → 1 です; ★ (3rd slot) = 4. Step 1: build the sentence; step 2: blacken the number in the ★ slot. In both items below ★ is the 3rd of four slots.

Q3

これは きょねん わたし ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿ しゃしんです。Kore wa kyonen watashi ___ ___ ★ ___ shashin desu.

Tiles: 1 が (ga)　2 で (de)　3 とった (totta)　4 海 (umi)

1 が→4 海→2 で→3 とった

これは きょねん わたしが 海で とった しゃしんです。Kore wa kyonen watashi ga umi de totta shashin desu.

EN: This is a photo I took at the sea last year.
HI: यह वह फ़ोटो है जो मैंने पिछले साल समुद्र पर खींची थी।
GU: આ એ ફોટો છે જે મેં ગયા વર્ષે દરિયા કિનારે પાડ્યો હતો.

★ = 2 で (正解 p.81; order worked out by us; the book gives only the ★ answer). Why (our explanation): わたし needs a particle at once → わたしが. 海 needs で (the place of the action) → 海で. The verb とった must touch the noun it describes, しゃしん. So: わたしが / 海で / とった + しゃしん — the 3rd slot is で. Inside a describing clause the subject takes が, not は.

Q4

きのう、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿★＿ ＿＿＿。Kinou, ___ ___ ★ ___.

Tiles: 1 買いに (kai ni)　2 日本語の (nihongo no)　3 行きました (ikimashita)　4 じしょを (jisho o)

2 日本語の→4 じしょを→1 買いに→3 行きました

きのう、日本語の じしょを 買いに 行きました。Kinou, nihongo no jisho o kai ni ikimashita.

EN: Yesterday I went to buy a Japanese dictionary.
HI: कल मैं जापानी शब्दकोश ख़रीदने गया।
GU: ગઈ કાલે હું જાપાની શબ્દકોશ ખરીદવા ગયો.

★ = 1 買いに (正解 p.81; order worked out by us; the book gives only the ★ answer). Why (our explanation): 日本語の must come right before a noun → 日本語の じしょを. 買いに 行きました is the set pattern "go to buy" (ます-stem + に + 行く), and the sentence ends with 。 so 行きました goes last. The 3rd slot is 買いに.

もんだい3 — 文章の文法: two self-introductions (5–9, p.58–59)

［5］から ［9］に 何を 入れますか。1・2・3・4から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。[5] kara [9] ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai. EN: What goes in blanks 5 to 9? Choose the best one. HI: खाली जगह 5 से 9 में क्या आएगा? सबसे सही विकल्प चुनें। GU: ખાલી જગ્યા 5 થી 9 માં શું આવશે? સૌથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરો. The setting (p.58): John and Yan will introduce themselves tomorrow and have each written a short self-introduction. The options are on p.59 (scanned upside down).

文章 (1)

Self-introduction (1) — ジョン・スミスさん (Jon Sumisu-san — John Smith) (p.58)

Summary (not the book's text): John Smith says hello and where he is from (America). He likes films and knows American films well, but he does not know Japanese films much, so in Japan he wants to see a lot of them. He ends with どうぞ よろしく おねがいします.

EN: Nice to meet you. I'm John Smith. I (came) from America.
HI: नमस्ते, मैं जॉन स्मिथ हूँ। मैं अमेरिका से (आया हूँ)।
GU: નમસ્તે, હું જૉન સ્મિથ છું. હું અમેરિકાથી (આવ્યો છું).
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1行きますikimasuwill goजाऊँगाજઈશ

Q6

アメリカの えいがは よく 知って います。［6］、日本の えいがは あまり 知りません。Amerika no eiga wa yoku shitte imasu. [6], Nihon no eiga wa amari shirimasen.

EN: I know American films well. (But) I don't know Japanese films much.
HI: अमेरिकी फ़िल्में मैं अच्छी तरह जानता हूँ। (लेकिन) जापानी फ़िल्में ज़्यादा नहीं जानता।
GU: અમેરિકન ફિલ્મો હું સારી રીતે જાણું છું. (પણ) જાપાની ફિલ્મો બહુ જાણતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ではdewawell thenतो फिरતો પછી
2だからdakaraso, thereforeइसलिएએટલે

Answer 3 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): the two sentences are opposites — よく 知って います (know well) vs あまり 知りません (don't know much). A contrast needs でも. だから would need a cause → result; それから adds the next thing in order.

Answer 3 (正解 p.81). Why (our explanation): みなさん、 calls to the listeners, so the next words must be something said to them — a request or invitation — and it must fit "I want many friends". 3 invites them over. 1 is an invitation too, but going to school every day does not follow from "I want friends"; 2 and 4 talk about herself, not to みなさん. あそびに 来る = come over (to visit), not only "come to play".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The trap sets these questions use (all added, not in book):

N の N vs N をno / onoun link vs objectへやの そうじを する ／ へやを そうじするheya no souji o suru / heya o souji suruOnly one を per verb. If を is already there, the other noun joins with の.Q1.

Exam trap 1 — one を per verb: when a sentence already has 〜を する, a second noun in front cannot take を. Look for の (Q1).
Exam trap 2 — ★ questions: build the whole sentence first, then read off the ★ slot. Start with the easy pairs: noun + particle (わたしが, 海で) and the piece that must touch the printed word after the blanks (とった + しゃしん).
Exam trap 3 — same word, different ending (もんだい3): when all four options use the same verb or adjective, decide two things from the text: past or present, and statement or question.
