Notes
This book gives only the answers — it has no explanations. Every "Why", every table in §1 and §3, and all English, Hindi and Gujarati on this page are ours (added, not in book). The test sentences are written with spaces between words, as on the real N4 paper; some kanji carry small furigana in the book, which we do not copy — the romaji line gives the full reading. In 問題2 some wrong options are made-up look-alike kanji that differ by one stroke; they cannot be typed, so we describe them in words. 問題5 (用法, choosing the sentence that uses a word correctly) is new at N4 — it is not in the N5 paper.

Q1

くつに 石が 入って いました。Kutsu ni ishi ga haitte imashita.

EN: There was a stone in my shoe.
HI: मेरे जूते में एक पत्थर था।
GU: મારા જૂતામાં એક પથ્થર હતો.
OptionReadingEnglishHindiGujarati
1いしishistone ✓पत्थरપથ્થર
2すなsunasand (砂)रेतરેતી
3くさkusagrass (草)घासઘાસ
4えだedabranch (枝)टहनीડાળી

Answer 1 (正答表 p.54). Why (our explanation): 石 = いし. All four are nature words that could get into a shoe, so you must know the kanji itself. 砂 (すな, sand) has 石 on its left side — a good way to remember both.

Answer 2 (正答表 p.54). Why (our explanation): ほめる = praise; ほめられる is its passive (be praised). Praise = saying something good → 「とても よかったですよ」. 「気を つけてね」 is a warning, not praise.

Exam trap 1 — sounds, not meanings, in 問題1: most wrong readings differ from the right one by one small thing: a long vowel (しょせつ／しょうせつ), a small っ (にちき／にっき), or a ゛ mark (けいげん／けいけん). Read each option slowly, mark by mark.
Exam trap 2 — fake kanji in 問題2: at N4 two options often look identical at first glance (歩いて／歩いて, 便利／便利). One has a stroke added or changed. Know the real kanji well enough to see it, and check the okurigana too (眠かった, not 眠むかった).
Exam trap 3 — 問題5 tests real usage: each wrong sentence uses the word where a similar word belongs (しんせつ vs べんり, ちこく vs おくれる, わる vs わける). Ask "who or what can be described by this word?" — a person, a taste, a hard object — and pick the sentence that matches.
