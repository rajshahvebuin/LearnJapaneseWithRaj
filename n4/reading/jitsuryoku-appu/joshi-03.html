1 Strategy & Particle Points

How to use this section

正しい 文には ○を、まちがっている 文には ×を つけなさい。Tadashii bun ni wa maru o, machigatte iru bun ni wa batsu o tsukenasai.

EN: Put ○ on correct sentences and × on wrong sentences.   HI: सही वाक्यों पर ○ और ग़लत वाक्यों पर × लगाइए।   GU: સાચાં વાક્યો પર ○ અને ખોટાં વાક્યો પર × મૂકો.

  • Decide fast, then read the right sentence aloud. The book's own advice (構成と使い方, p.4–5): judge quickly whether the particles are used correctly, write ○ or × in the （ ）, then read the correct sentences on the facing page aloud again and again until you remember them.
    HI: जल्दी तय करें कि कण सही हैं या नहीं, （ ） में ○ या × लिखें, फिर सामने वाले पन्ने के सही वाक्य बार-बार ज़ोर से पढ़ें जब तक याद न हो जाएँ। GU: ઝડપથી નક્કી કરો કે કણ સાચા છે કે નહીં, （ ）માં ○ કે × લખો, પછી સામેના પાનાનાં સાચાં વાક્યો યાદ રહી જાય ત્યાં સુધી વારંવાર મોટેથી વાંચો.
  • A cause takes で. じこで, たいふうで, びょうきで: when the noun is the reason something happened, use で, not に. (added, not in book)
    HI: कारण के साथ で: じこで, たいふうで — कारण वाली संज्ञा पर に नहीं। GU: કારણ સાથે で: じこで, たいふうで — કારણવાળા નામ પર に નહીં.
  • Not every × is a particle. In Q60 the book fixes a verb (もうしあげます → もうします). Read the whole sentence, not just the particles. (added, not in book)
    HI: हर × कण की ग़लती नहीं है; Q60 में किताब क्रिया बदलती है। पूरा वाक्य पढ़ें। GU: દરેક × કણની ભૂલ નથી; Q60 માં પુસ્તક ક્રિયાપદ બદલે છે. આખું વાક્ય વાંચો.

2 Exercise — 助詞100問 Q41–60

All 20 sentences from p.28 and p.30, in book order. Cover the answer, decide ○ or ×, then check. The green row is the book's answer from the 解答例 page. For × items the book's corrected sentence follows, with the changed part in bold. The English is the book's; Hindi and Gujarati are ours.

Q41

（　）しゃちょうは しゃいんが はたらかせます。(   ) Shachou wa shain ga hatarakasemasu.

MarkMeaningEnglishHindiGujarati
○ただしいtadashiithe particles are all rightसभी कण सही हैंબધા કણ સાચા છે
×まちがっているmachigatte iruone particle is wrongएक कण ग़लत हैએક કણ ખોટો છે

Book's corrected sentence (解答例 p.29, PDF 15)

しゃちょうは しゃいんを はたらかせます。Shachou wa shain o hatarakasemasu.

EN (book): The company president makes the employees work.
HI: कंपनी के अध्यक्ष कर्मचारियों से काम करवाते हैं।
GU: કંપનીના પ્રમુખ કર્મચારીઓ પાસે કામ કરાવે છે.

Answer × (解答例 p.29, PDF 15). Why (our explanation, not in book): Causative sentence: the person who makes someone act is the subject (しゃちょうは). The person who is made to act takes を with an intransitive verb like はたらく (or に when there is already a を object). が would make the employees the ones doing the "making".

Book's corrected sentence (解答例 p.31, PDF 16)

あしたの かいぎに でますか。Ashita no kaigi ni demasu ka.

EN (book): Will you attend the meeting tomorrow?
HI: क्या आप कल की मीटिंग में शामिल होंगे?
GU: શું તમે કાલની મીટિંગમાં હાજર રહેશો?

Answer × (解答例 p.31, PDF 16). Why (our explanation, not in book): でる has two uses. "Leave (a place)" takes を (へやを でる). "Attend / take part in (an event)" takes に (かいぎに でる, しあいに でる).
