実力アップ！ N4 読む

Following 実力アップ！日本語能力試験N4 読む（文字・語彙・文法・読解） (Jitsuryoku Appu N4 Yomu, ユニコム) block by block. Despite the title, most of this book is grammar: a 100-sentence particle review from N5, then the N4 grammar points one by one (each with a choose-the-word question and a ★ sentence-building question), a "what's the difference?" chapter on similar expressions, review question sets, and finally 文章の文法 cloze passages and 内容理解 / 情報検索 reading. Every page gives the points, every question with the book's own answer and page, and confusion notes, in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • The book's advice (p.4–5): in the particle review, decide quickly whether each sentence is right (○) or wrong (×), then read the correct sentences on the right-hand page aloud until you remember them.
  • For each grammar chapter, fill in the conjugation table [表] on the first page yourself, then read the example sentences until you know them by heart.
  • The reading pages give a short summary of each passage in our own words, not the book's text, and quote only the lines each question needs.
  • The book ends with a 文字・語彙 便利帳 (verb lists, conjugation tables, a kanji vocabulary list, p.267–302). These are reference lists with no questions, so they are not built here. Use them from your own copy.
