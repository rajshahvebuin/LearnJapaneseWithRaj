1 Strategy & Particle Points

How to use this section

正しい 文には ○を、まちがっている 文には ×を つけなさい。Tadashii bun ni wa maru o, machigatte iru bun ni wa batsu o tsukenasai.

EN: Put ○ on correct sentences and × on wrong sentences.   HI: सही वाक्यों पर ○ और ग़लत वाक्यों पर × लगाइए।   GU: સાચાં વાક્યો પર ○ અને ખોટાં વાક્યો પર × મૂકો.

  • Decide fast, then read the right sentence aloud. The book's own advice (構成と使い方, p.4–5): judge quickly whether the particles are used correctly, write ○ or × in the （ ）, then read the correct sentences on the facing page aloud again and again until you remember them.
    HI: जल्दी तय करें कि कण सही हैं या नहीं, （ ） में ○ या × लिखें, फिर सामने वाले पन्ने के सही वाक्य बार-बार ज़ोर से पढ़ें जब तक याद न हो जाएँ। GU: ઝડપથી નક્કી કરો કે કણ સાચા છે કે નહીં, （ ）માં ○ કે × લખો, પછી સામેના પાનાનાં સાચાં વાક્યો યાદ રહી જાય ત્યાં સુધી વારંવાર મોટેથી વાંચો.
  • Look at the verb first. Most mistakes in this set are a particle that does not fit the verb: みつかる / つく / みえる want が; とおる wants を; つとめる wants に. Find the verb, then check the particle in front of the noun it goes with. (added, not in book)
    HI: पहले क्रिया देखें। ज़्यादातर ग़लतियाँ ऐसे कण की हैं जो क्रिया से मेल नहीं खाता: みつかる / つく / みえる के साथ が; とおる के साथ を; つとめる के साथ に। GU: પહેલાં ક્રિયાપદ જુઓ. મોટા ભાગની ભૂલો એવા કણની છે જે ક્રિયાપદ સાથે મેળ ખાતો નથી: みつかる / つく / みえる સાથે が; とおる સાથે を; つとめる સાથે に.
  • Only one particle is wrong in a × sentence. In every × item here the book changes exactly one particle. Don't "fix" the rest of the sentence. (added, not in book)
    HI: × वाले वाक्य में सिर्फ़ एक कण ग़लत है; किताब हर बार ठीक एक कण बदलती है। बाक़ी वाक्य मत बदलिए। GU: × વાળા વાક્યમાં ફક્ત એક જ કણ ખોટો છે; પુસ્તક દર વખતે બરાબર એક કણ બદલે છે. બાકીનું વાક્ય ન બદલો.

2 Exercise — 助詞100問 Q1–20

All 20 sentences from p.20 and p.22, in book order. Cover the answer, decide ○ or ×, then check. The green row is the book's answer from the 解答例 page. For × items the book's corrected sentence follows, with the changed particle in bold. The English is the book's; Hindi and Gujarati are ours.

Q1

（　）このCDは がくせいに にんきが あります。(   ) Kono CD wa gakusei ni ninki ga arimasu.

MarkMeaningEnglishHindiGujarati
○ただしいtadashiithe particles are all rightसभी कण सही हैंબધા કણ સાચા છે
×まちがっているmachigatte iruone particle is wrongएक कण ग़लत हैએક કણ ખોટો છે

The sentence is correct as it is

EN (book): This CD is popular with students.
HI: यह सीडी छात्रों में लोकप्रिय है।
GU: આ સીડી વિદ્યાર્થીઓમાં લોકપ્રિય છે.

Answer ○ (解答例 p.21, PDF 11). Why (our explanation, not in book): 〜に にんきが ある = "popular with / among ~". The people who like it take に (がくせいに); にんき takes が. Both particles are right.

Q2

（　）わたしは すきなのは コーヒーです。(   ) Watashi wa suki na no wa koohii desu.

MarkMeaningEnglishHindiGujarati
○ただしいtadashiithe particles are all rightसभी कण सही हैंબધા કણ સાચા છે
×まちがっているmachigatte iruone particle is wrongएक कण ग़लत हैએક કણ ખોટો છે

Book's corrected sentence (解答例 p.21, PDF 11)

わたしが すきなのは コーヒーです。Watashi ga suki na no wa koohii desu.

EN (book): The one that I like is coffee.
HI: मुझे जो पसंद है, वह कॉफ़ी है।
GU: મને જે ગમે છે તે કૉફી છે.

Answer × (解答例 p.21, PDF 11). Why (our explanation, not in book): わたし＿すきな is a small clause that describes の ("the thing I like"). Inside such a clause the subject takes が, not は. The topic of the whole sentence is already すきなのは.

MarkMeaningEnglishHindiGujarati
○ただしいtadashiithe particles are all rightसभी कण सही हैंબધા કણ સાચા છે
×まちがっているmachigatte iruone particle is wrongएक कण ग़लत हैએક કણ ખોટો છે

いつまで にほんに いますか。Itsu made nihon ni imasu ka.

EN (book): How long will you be in Japan for?
HI: आप कब तक जापान में रहेंगे?

Answer × (解答例 p.21, PDF 11). Why (our explanation, not in book): いる (to stay) goes on over time, so the end point is まで "until". までに "by (a deadline)" goes with a one-time action: いつまでに かえりますか (by when will you go home?).

MarkMeaningEnglishHindiGujarati

×まちがっているmachigatte iruone particle is wrongएक कण ग़लत हैએક કણ ખોટો છે

Book's corrected sentence (解答例 p.21, PDF 11)

うちに はいろうとしたら、かぎが みつかりませんでした。Uchi ni hairou to shitara, kagi ga mitsukarimasen deshita.

EN (book): Just as I was about to enter my house I couldn't find my keys.
HI: जब मैं घर में घुसने ही वाला था, तो चाबी नहीं मिली।
GU: હું ઘરમાં દાખલ થવા જતો હતો ત્યારે ચાવી ન મળી.

Answer × (解答例 p.21, PDF 11). Why (our explanation, not in book): みつかる (to be found) is an intransitive verb, so the thing takes が. The transitive partner みつける (to find) takes を: かぎを みつけました.

HI: मैं कल के कॉन्सर्ट का बेसब्री से इंतज़ार कर रहा हूँ।
GU: હું કાલના કૉન્સર્ટની આતુરતાથી રાહ જોઉં છું.

Answer × (解答例 p.23, PDF 12). Why (our explanation, not in book): Fixed pattern: 〜を たのしみに する "to look forward to ~". The thing takes を (コンサートを); たのしみ takes に. Two を in one short clause is a warning sign.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The pairs below caused the 14 × sentences in this set. (table added, not in book)

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
に / でni / deat, inに = where something is (ある, いる, 〜てある), or where you go / work (つとめる). で = where an action happens (のむ, だす, はたらく).
HI: に = होने/जाने की जगह; で = क्रिया की जगह. GU: に = હોવાની/જવાની જગ્યા; で = ક્રિયાની જગ્યા.		まわりに うえてある (Q19)／どこかで のむ (Q18)／びょういんに つとめる (Q10)
が / をga / overb pairsIntransitive verbs (みつかる, つく, みえる) take が. Their transitive partners (みつける, つける, みる) take を.
HI: अकर्मक + が; सकर्मक + を. GU: અકર્મક + が; સકર્મક + を.		かぎが みつかる (Q4)／でんきが つく (Q5)／ふじさんが みえる (Q8)
まで / までにmade / made niuntil / byまで: the action goes on up to that time (いる, まつ, はたらく). までに: a one-time action happens before that time (かえる, だす).
HI: まで = तक लगातार; までに = उससे पहले एक बार. GU: まで = ત્યાં સુધી સતત; までに = એ પહેલાં એક વાર.		いつまで いますか (Q3)
は / がwa / gatopic / subjectInside a clause that describes a noun, the subject is が (or の), never は.
HI: संज्ञा-वर्णन उपवाक्य में कर्ता が. GU: નામ-વર્ણન ઉપવાક્યમાં કર્તા が.		わたしが すきなの (Q2)
から / をkara / ofrom"Leave / finish a place" verbs (そつぎょうする, でる) take を, even though English says "from".
HI: छोड़ने वाली क्रियाओं के साथ を. GU: છોડવાની ક્રિયાઓ સાથે を.		だいがくを そつぎょうする (Q6)
に / をni / oto / throughに = arrive at a point. を = move along or through a route (とおる, あるく, わたる).
HI: に = पहुँचना; を = रास्ते से गुज़रना. GU: に = પહોંચવું; を = રસ્તેથી પસાર થવું.		しんじゅくを とおる (Q11)
どこへ / どこもdoko e / doko mowhere to / nowhereQuestion word + も + negative = "not … any". どこへ alone is a question.
HI: प्रश्नवाचक + も + नकार = "कहीं नहीं". GU: પ્રશ્નવાચક + も + નકાર = "ક્યાંય નહીં".		どこも いきませんでした (Q16)
Exam trap: a particle that matches the English often hides the mistake: "from university" → から, "interested in anime" → が, "see Mt Fuji" → を. In Japanese the verb chooses the particle: だいがくを そつぎょうする, アニメに きょうみを もつ, ふじさんが みえる. Learn each verb together with its particle. (added, not in book)
