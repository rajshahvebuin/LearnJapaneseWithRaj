にほんごチャレンジ N4 ことば

Following 「日本語能力試験」対策 にほんごチャレンジ N4 ことば (Nihongo Challenge N4 Vocabulary, アスク出版) lesson by lesson. Each of its 32 short lessons is built around an everyday theme (shopping, moving house, a cold, the weather forecast): a short text, 15–20 new N4 words with example sentences, a もうちょっと mini-dialogue and a few practice questions. After every four lessons comes a ふくしゅう問題 revision set in JLPT format, and the book ends with four まとめのテスト and an appendix of reference tables. Every page gives each word with romaji and its meaning in English, Hindi and Gujarati, every question with the answer from the book's key, and confusion pairs.

How to use this book

  • Study one lesson a day: learn the words first, then read the summary of the text, then try the れんしゅう questions before looking at the answers.
  • After every four lessons, take the ふくしゅう問題. It uses the JLPT question types (fill the blank, same meaning, correct usage), so it is good exam practice.
  • Words marked ★ are printed in bold in the book: these are the N4-level words to learn first.
  • The lesson texts are summarised in our own words, not copied from the book. Only short key lines are quoted.
