Notes
The book gives no explanations. Our meanings, the Hindi and Gujarati, the Why notes and the confusion pairs are ours. Main traps in this lesson: ぜんぜん (needs a negative) vs とても (positive), 先輩 (senior) vs 後輩 (junior), 決める (I decide something, を) vs 決まる (something is decided, が), and the different "wear" verbs: 着る (upper body / whole body), はく (feet and legs), つける (accessories), かぶる (hat).
★ = printed in bold in the book = an N4-level word (この本の使い方 p.15). Words without ★ are extra words from the text.
Related = the book's black arrow mark (関連することば, a related word) · ⇔ = the book's white arrow mark (反対の意味のことば, opposite) · ☞p.115 = the book's "see page" mark

1 文章を読みましょう — the text and the words

文章 (Bunshou — the text)

p.18

Summary (our words, not the book's text):

EN: Next month the writer is going to the wedding of a senior colleague from work. She has no suitable clothes, so she went to a department store. She tried on many suits and dresses but could not decide at all. Shopping is fun but a little tiring; there is still a month before the ceremony, so she will take her time looking.

HI: अगले महीने लेखिका ऑफ़िस के एक सीनियर की शादी में जा रही है। उसके पास सही कपड़े नहीं हैं, इसलिए वह डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर गई। उसने कई सूट और ड्रेस पहनकर देखे, पर कुछ भी तय नहीं कर पाई। ख़रीदारी मज़ेदार है पर थोड़ी थकाने वाली भी। समारोह में अभी एक महीना है, इसलिए वह आराम से ढूँढेगी।

GU: આવતા મહિને લેખિકા ઑફિસના એક સિનિયરના લગ્નમાં જવાની છે. તેની પાસે યોગ્ય કપડાં નથી, એટલે તે ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરમાં ગઈ. તેણે ઘણા સૂટ અને ડ્રેસ પહેરીને જોયા, પણ કંઈ નક્કી કરી શકી નહીં. ખરીદી મજાની છે પણ થોડી થકવનારી પણ છે. સમારોહને હજી એક મહિનો છે, એટલે તે નિરાંતે શોધશે.

Key lines:

来月、会社の先輩の結婚式に行く。Raigetsu, kaisha no senpai no kekkonshiki ni iku.

EN: Next month I'm going to the wedding of a senior colleague at my company.
HI: अगले महीने मैं अपनी कंपनी के एक सीनियर की शादी में जा रही हूँ।
GU: આવતા મહિને હું મારી કંપનીના એક સિનિયરના લગ્નમાં જઈ રહી છું.
結婚式 kekkonshiki = 結婚 (marriage) + 式 (ceremony) = wedding · HI: शादी का समारोह · GU: લગ્ન સમારંભ

いろいろ着てみても、ぜんぜん決められない。Iroiro kite mite mo, zenzen kimerarenai.

EN: Even after trying on all kinds of things, I can't decide at all.
HI: तरह-तरह के कपड़े पहनकर देखने के बाद भी, मैं बिल्कुल तय नहीं कर पा रही।
GU: જાતજાતનાં કપડાં પહેરીને જોયા પછી પણ, હું બિલકુલ નક્કી કરી શકતી નથી.
決められない kimerarenai = "can't decide" (potential negative of 決める) · HI: तय नहीं कर पाना · GU: નક્કી ન કરી શકવું (added, not in book)

式まであと一か月あるから、ゆっくり探そう。Shiki made ato ikkagetsu aru kara, yukkuri sagasou.

EN: There's still one month until the ceremony, so I'll take my time looking.
HI: समारोह तक अभी एक महीना बाक़ी है, इसलिए आराम से ढूँढूँगी।
GU: સમારોહ સુધી હજી એક મહિનો બાકી છે, એટલે નિરાંતે શોધીશ.

2 れんしゅう — practice

p.19: れんしゅう 1 (4 items) and 2 (3 items). All answers are from the official 別冊 問題の答え p.12 (PDF page 140). The book gives answers only, so the Why notes are ours.

1 （　）に入ることばをえらんでください。

( ) ni hairu kotoba o erande kudasai.
EN: Choose the word that goes in ( ). · HI: ( ) में आने वाला शब्द चुनिए। · GU: ( ) માં આવતો શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

1-①

あの黒い（　）を着た男の人の名前を知っていますか。Ano kuroi ( ) o kita otoko no hito no namae o shitte imasu ka.

Full sentence: あの黒いスーツを着た男の人の名前を知っていますか。Ano kuroi suutsu o kita otoko no hito no namae o shitte imasu ka.
EN: Do you know the name of that man in the black suit?
HI: क्या आप उस काले सूट वाले आदमी का नाम जानते हैं?
GU: શું તમે પેલા કાળા સૂટવાળા માણસનું નામ જાણો છો?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ポケットpokettopocketजेबખિસ્સું
2スカートsukaatoskirtस्कर्टસ્કર્ટ
3スーツsuutsusuitसूटસૂટ
4ぼうしboushihatटोपीટોપી

Why: The verb is 着た (wore), which is used for clothes on the body, and the wearer is a man, so スーツ. A スカート is "worn" with はく, a ぼうし with かぶる, and you don't wear a pocket. Key: 別冊 p.12 → 3.

1-②

今日の映画は（　）おもしろくなかった。Kyou no eiga wa ( ) omoshiroku nakatta.

Full sentence: 今日の映画は全然おもしろくなかった。Kyou no eiga wa zenzen omoshiroku nakatta.
EN: Today's movie wasn't interesting at all.
HI: आज की फ़िल्म बिल्कुल दिलचस्प नहीं थी।
GU: આજની ફિલ્મ બિલકુલ રસપ્રદ ન હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1全然zenzen(not) at allबिल्कुल (नहीं)બિલકુલ (નહીં)
2とてもtotemoveryबहुतખૂબ
3たくさんtakusana lot, manyबहुत साराઘણું
4必ずkanarazuwithout failज़रूरજરૂર

Why: おもしろくなかった is negative, and 全然 is the adverb that goes with a negative ("not at all"). とても goes with a positive adjective (とてもおもしろかった). たくさん is about quantity, and 必ず is about doing something without fail. Key: 別冊 p.12 → 1.

1-③

彼は、学校の1年上の（　）です。Kare wa, gakkou no ichinen ue no ( ) desu.

Full sentence: 彼は、学校の1年上の先輩です。Kare wa, gakkou no ichinen ue no senpai desu.
EN: He is my senior at school, one year above me.
HI: वह स्कूल में मुझसे एक साल सीनियर है।
GU: તે શાળામાં મારાથી એક વર્ષ સિનિયર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1後輩kouhaijuniorजूनियरજુનિયર
2先生senseiteacherशिक्षकશિક્ષક
3先輩senpaiseniorसीनियरસિનિયર

Why: 1年上 = one year above, so he is a 先輩. 後輩 is the opposite (one year below). A 先生 or 父 is not described as "one year above at school". Key: 別冊 p.12 → 3.

1-④

わたしは女性だけど、（　）をつけるのがあまり好きじゃない。Watashi wa josei da kedo, ( ) o tsukeru no ga amari suki ja nai.

Full sentence: わたしは女性だけど、アクセサリーをつけるのがあまり好きじゃない。Watashi wa josei da kedo, akusesarii o tsukeru no ga amari suki ja nai.

GU: હું સ્ત્રી છું, પણ મને ઘરેણાં-એક્સેસરી પહેરવાનું બહુ ગમતું નથી.

2-②

EN: I have to look for a new room (place to live).
HI: मुझे नया कमरा ढूँढना है।
GU: મારે નવો રૂમ શોધવો પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a探さなければsagasanakereba(must) look forढूँढना (ज़रूरी)શોધવું (જરૂરી)

EN: It has been decided that I'll work in Japan from next year.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

These groups come from this lesson's words and the wrong options in れんしゅう. English, Hindi and Gujarati use one verb "wear / पहनना / પહેરવું" for everything, but Japanese chooses the verb by the body part.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
着るkiruwear (on the body)Shirts, suits, coats, sweaters, dressesスーツを着る
はくhakuwear (legs, feet)Trousers, skirts, shoes, boots, sandals, socksブーツをはく
つけるtsukeruput on, attachAccessories, rings, watches (also する)アクセサリーをつける
かぶるkaburuwear (on the head)Hats, caps, helmetsぼうしをかぶる
ぜんぜんzenzen(not) at allFollowed by a negative at N4 levelぜんぜんおもしろくない
とてもtotemoveryUsually with a positive adjectiveとてもおもしろい
先輩senpaiseniorStarted school / work before you1年上の先輩
後輩kouhaijuniorStarted after you1年下の後輩
決めるkimerudecideTransitive: someone decides something (を)服を決める
決まるkimarube decidedIntransitive: something becomes decided (が)就職が決まった
探すsagasulook forThe search is still going on; you don't have it yet部屋を探す

"Key difference" and "Use when" examples are ours (added, not in book).

Exam trap: In a (　) question about clothes, look at the verb after を first. 着る, はく, つける and かぶる each fit only certain items, so the verb alone often removes three options. For 決める / 決まる, look at the particle: を → 決める, が → 決まる.
