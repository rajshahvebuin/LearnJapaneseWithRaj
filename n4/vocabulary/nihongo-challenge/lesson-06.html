Notes
The book gives no explanations. Our meanings, the Hindi and Gujarati, the Why notes and the confusion pairs are ours. Main traps in this lesson: できる meaning "be built / come into being" (not only "can"), 変わる (i.) vs 変える (t.), ずいぶん (a great deal) vs ちょうど (exactly), 真ん中 (the middle) vs 角 (corner), and 計画 (plan) vs 用事 (errand).
★ = printed in bold in the book = an N4-level word (この本の使い方 p.15). Words without ★ are extra words from the text.
Related = the book's black arrow mark · ⇔ = the book's white arrow mark (opposite) · ≒ = the book's "almost the same meaning" mark · ☞p.N = see page · (t.)/(i.) = transitive / intransitive

1 文章を読みましょう — the text and the words

文章 (Bunshou — the text)

p.28

Summary (our words, not the book's text):

EN: Ten years ago there were almost no buildings in this area, but a big industrial estate was built and the town changed a lot. A surprisingly wide road now runs through the middle, many factories have opened, and trucks carrying the goods they make go back and forth, so the town has become lively. The picture shows a wide road lined with factories and trucks.

HI: दस साल पहले यहाँ लगभग कोई इमारत नहीं थी, पर एक बड़ा औद्योगिक क्षेत्र बना और शहर बहुत बदल गया। अब बीचों-बीच हैरान करने वाली चौड़ी सड़क है, कई फ़ैक्टरियाँ बन गई हैं, और वहाँ बना माल ढोने वाले ट्रक आते-जाते रहते हैं, इसलिए शहर में रौनक़ आ गई है।

GU: દસ વર્ષ પહેલાં અહીં લગભગ કોઈ ઇમારત ન હતી, પણ એક મોટો ઔદ્યોગિક વિસ્તાર બન્યો અને નગર ઘણું બદલાઈ ગયું. હવે વચ્ચોવચ નવાઈ લાગે એવો પહોળો રસ્તો છે, ઘણાં કારખાનાં બન્યાં છે, અને ત્યાં બનેલો માલ લઈ જતી ટ્રકો આવજા કરે છે, એટલે નગરમાં ચહલપહલ થઈ ગઈ છે.

Key lines:

大きな工業団地ができて、ずいぶん変わりました。Ooki na kougyou danchi ga dekite, zuibun kawarimashita.

EN: A big industrial estate was built, and (the area) changed a great deal.
HI: एक बड़ा औद्योगिक क्षेत्र बना, और (इलाक़ा) बहुत बदल गया।
GU: એક મોટો ઔદ્યોગિક વિસ્તાર બન્યો, અને (વિસ્તાર) ઘણો બદલાઈ ગયો.

そこで生産したものを運ぶトラックが行ったり来たりして、にぎやかになりました。Soko de seisan shita mono o hakobu torakku ga ittari kitari shite, nigiyaka ni narimashita.

EN: Trucks carrying the things made there go back and forth, and the town has become lively.
HI: वहाँ बना माल ढोने वाले ट्रक आते-जाते हैं, और शहर में रौनक़ आ गई है।
GU: ત્યાં બનેલો માલ લઈ જતી ટ્રકો આવજા કરે છે, અને નગરમાં ચહલપહલ થઈ ગઈ છે.

2 れんしゅう — practice

p.29: れんしゅう 1 (4 items) and 2 (4 items). All answers are from the official 別冊 問題の答え p.12 (PDF page 140). The book gives answers only, so the Why notes are ours.

1 （　）に入ることばをえらんでください。

( ) ni hairu kotoba o erande kudasai.
EN: Choose the word that goes in ( ). · HI: ( ) में आने वाला शब्द चुनिए। · GU: ( ) માં આવતો શબ્દ પસંદ કરો.

1-①

広い（　）ができて、便利になった。Hiroi ( ) ga dekite, benri ni natta.

Full sentence: 広い道路ができて、便利になった。Hiroi douro ga dekite, benri ni natta.
EN: A wide road was built, and it became convenient.
HI: चौड़ी सड़क बन गई, और आना-जाना आसान हो गया।
GU: પહોળો રસ્તો બન્યો, અને સગવડ થઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1工業kougyouindustryउद्योगઉદ્યોગ
2道路douroroadसड़कરસ્તો
3真ん中mannakamiddleबीचવચ્ચે
4地域chiikiareaइलाक़ाવિસ્તાર

Why: 広い + something that is built (ができる) + makes life convenient = a wide road. 工業 is an abstract field, and 真ん中 or 地域 are not "built". Key: 別冊 p.12 → 2.

1-②

あの工場はコンピューターを（　）している。Ano koujou wa konpyuutaa o ( ) shite iru.

Full sentence: あの工場はコンピューターを生産している。Ano koujou wa konpyuutaa o seisan shite iru.
EN: That factory produces computers.
HI: वह फ़ैक्टरी कंप्यूटर बनाती है।
GU: તે ફેક્ટરી કમ્પ્યુટર બનાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1生産seisanproductionउत्पादनઉત્પાદન
2世話sewacareदेखभालસંભાળ
3相談soudanconsultationसलाहસલાહ
4専門senmonspecialityविशेषज्ञताવિશેષતા

Why: A factory makes products: 生産する. 専門 is a noun that is not used with する (you say コンピューターが専門だ). 世話 and 相談 are for people. Key: 別冊 p.12 → 1.

1-③

船で外国と貿易するとき、（　）を使う。Fune de gaikoku to boueki suru toki, ( ) o tsukau.

Full sentence: 船で外国と貿易するとき、港を使う。Fune de gaikoku to boueki suru toki, minato o tsukau.
EN: When trading with other countries by ship, you use a port.
HI: जहाज़ से विदेशों के साथ व्यापार करते समय बंदरगाह का उपयोग होता है।
GU: જહાજથી વિદેશો સાથે વેપાર કરતી વખતે બંદરનો ઉપયોગ થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1郵便局yuubinkyokupost officeडाकघरટપાલ કચેરી
2建物tatemonobuildingइमारतઇમારત
3港minatoportबंदरगाहબંદર
4駅ekistationस्टेशनસ્ટેશન

Why: 船で (by ship) → ships use a port. A station is for trains; a post office and "a building" don't match ships. Key: 別冊 p.12 → 3.

1-④

来年、新しい駅が（　）らしい。Rainen, atarashii eki ga ( ) rashii.

Full sentence: 来年、新しい駅ができるらしい。Rainen, atarashii eki ga dekiru rashii.
EN: I hear a new station is going to be built next year.
HI: सुना है अगले साल एक नया स्टेशन बनेगा।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે આવતા વર્ષે એક નવું સ્ટેશન બનશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あるaruthere isहैછે
2出るderugo out, appearनिकलनाનીકળવું
3変わるkawaruchangeबदलनाબદલાવું
4できるdekirube built, come into beingबननाબનવું

Why: 来年 (future) + 新しい駅 → something that does not exist yet will be built: できる. ある describes something that exists now; a "new station" can't 変わる; 出る is not used for buildings. Key: 別冊 p.12 → 4.

2 どちらがいいですか。えらんでください。

Dochira ga ii desu ka. Erande kudasai.
EN: Which one is right? Choose. · HI: कौन-सा सही है? चुनिए। · GU: કયું સાચું છે? પસંદ કરો.

2-①

男の子は一人で（a. ずいぶん　b. ちょうど）遠くまで行ってしまった。Otoko no ko wa hitori de (a. zuibun / b. choudo) tooku made itte shimatta.

Full sentence: 男の子は一人でずいぶん遠くまで行ってしまった。Otoko no ko wa hitori de zuibun tooku made itte shimatta.
EN: The boy went quite far all by himself.
HI: लड़का अकेले ही काफ़ी दूर तक चला गया।
GU: છોકરો એકલો જ ખાસ્સો દૂર સુધી જતો રહ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aずいぶんzuibunquite, a great dealकाफ़ीખાસ્સું
bちょうどchoudoexactly, justठीक, बिल्कुलબરાબર

Why: ずいぶん + 遠く = surprisingly far. ちょうど is for exact amounts or times (ちょうど3時, ちょうどいい) and does not go with a vague word like 遠く. Key: 別冊 p.12 → a.

2-②

今、新しい店を作る（a. 用事　b. 計画）がある。Ima, atarashii mise o tsukuru (a. youji / b. keikaku) ga aru.

Full sentence: 今、新しい店を作る計画がある。Ima, atarashii mise o tsukuru keikaku ga aru.
EN: There is now a plan to open a new shop.
HI: अभी एक नई दुकान बनाने की योजना है।
GU: હાલ એક નવી દુકાન બનાવવાની યોજના છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a用事youjierrandकामકામ
b計画keikakuplanयोजनाયોજના

Why: Building a shop is a big project for the future: a 計画. 用事 is a small everyday errand (bank, post office), not something like "opening a shop". Key: 別冊 p.12 → b.

2-③

あの店は（a. びっくりする　b. あいさつする）ような安い服がたくさんおいてある。Ano mise wa (a. bikkuri suru / b. aisatsu suru) you na yasui fuku ga takusan oite aru.

Full sentence: あの店はびっくりするような安い服がたくさんおいてある。Ano mise wa bikkuri suru you na yasui fuku ga takusan oite aru.
EN: That shop has lots of surprisingly cheap clothes.
HI: उस दुकान में हैरान कर देने वाले सस्ते कपड़े बहुत हैं।
GU: તે દુકાનમાં નવાઈ લાગે એટલાં સસ્તાં કપડાં ઘણાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aびっくりするbikkuri surube surprisedहैरान होनाનવાઈ પામવી
bあいさつするaisatsu surugreetनमस्ते करनाનમસ્તે કરવું

Why: 〜ような安い = "so cheap that it makes you …". びっくりするような安さ (surprisingly cheap) is a common phrase, like 町の真ん中にびっくりするような広い道路 in the text. Clothes don't make you greet someone. Key: 別冊 p.12 → a.

2-④

3人の（a. 真ん中　b. 角）がうちの子どもです。Sannin no (a. mannaka / b. kado) ga uchi no kodomo desu.

Full sentence: 3人の真ん中がうちの子どもです。Sannin no mannaka ga uchi no kodomo desu.
EN: The one in the middle of the three is my child.
HI: तीनों में बीच वाला मेरा बच्चा है।
GU: ત્રણેયમાં વચ્ચેનું મારું બાળક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a真ん中mannakamiddleबीच वालाવચ્ચેનું
b角kadocornerकोनाખૂણો

Why: In a row of three people, one is in the middle: 真ん中. 角 is the corner of a street, room or table, not a position among people. Key: 別冊 p.12 → a.

"Key difference" and "Use when" examples are ours (added, not in book), except where they repeat the book's own example sentences.

Exam trap: できる is tested for its "be built / be made" meaning, not only "can". If the subject is a building, road or shop with が and the time is the future or a change (〜ができて), choose できる. Also learn the pair 輸入 (import, 入 = in) ⇔ 輸出 (export, 出 = out): the kanji tell you the direction.
