Notes
The words, their numbers, the sub-headings, the boxes, the questions and the answers are the book's. The English glosses start from the book's 語彙リスト (we fixed one misprint: the list glosses 付ける as "saw"; it means "sew on"). The Hindi, Gujarati, romaji, every "Why" note and §4 are ours. Pictures are described in words, not copied. Pitch-accent marks in the 語彙リスト are not reproduced. Our scan has a previous owner's pencil notes on p.9 ("living alone" over 一人ぐらし, "easy" over 楽); they are not part of the book. Note that the 家の中 page numbers restart: words 1–30 are on p.6 and words 1–22 on p.7.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

English, Hindi and Gujarati use one word ("boil", "cook", "fix") where Japanese uses several. (added, not in book)

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
沸かす ／ ゆでる ／ 煮るwakasu / yuderu / niru all "boil" 沸かす = heat water (or a bath) until hot. ゆでる = cook food in plenty of boiling water. 煮る = cook food in a little liquid with flavour (stew, simmer). お湯を沸かす · 卵をゆでる · 野菜を煮る
焼く ／ いためる ／ 炊くyaku / itameru / taku grill / stir-fry / cook rice 焼く = cook with dry heat (grill, bake, or in a pan without stirring). いためる = stir-fry with oil. 炊く is only for rice. 魚を焼く · 野菜をいためる · ご飯を炊く
冷やす ／ 冷えるhiyasu / hieru chill / get cold 冷やす has a doer (を); 冷える happens by itself (が). Same pair as 温める／温まる. ビールを冷やす · ビールが冷えていない
つける・消す ／ つく・消えるtsukeru, kesu / tsuku, kieru turn on, off / be on, go off The first pair has a doer (を). The second pair describes the light itself (が). With ている they show a state: ついています = it is on. 電気をつける · 電気がついています
開ける・閉める ／ 開く・閉まるakeru, shimeru / aku, shimaru open, close (something) / open, close (by itself) Same transitive / intransitive pattern as above. 窓を開ける · 窓が開いています
直す ／ 治すnaosu / naosu repair / cure Same sound. 直す is for things (a sock, a button, a mistake). 治す (topic 1) is for illness and injury. くつ下を直す · 病気を治す
用意 ／ 世話youi / sewa preparation / care 用意 = getting something ready (meal, documents). 世話 = looking after a living thing (pet, plant, child, patient). 食事の用意 · ペットの世話
飼う ／ 買うkau / kau keep (an animal) / buy Same sound, different kanji. ペットを飼う = keep a pet; ペットを買う = buy one in a shop. 犬を飼っています
片づける ／ 掃除するkatazukeru / souji suru tidy up / clean 片づける = put things back in their place. 掃除する = remove dirt (vacuum, wipe). 本を片づける · 部屋を掃除する
Fixed verb partners: ゴミを出す, 掃除機をかける, ふとんを干す, ボタンを付ける, ボタンがとれる, お風呂を沸かす, お風呂に入る, ペットを飼う. (added, not in book)
Trap for Hindi and Gujarati speakers: "गरम पानी / ગરમ પાણી" is one word in Japanese: お湯. 熱い水 sounds strange. Likewise ぬるい has no exact English word: it is "not hot enough" for tea or a bath, and "not cold enough" for beer. (added, not in book)
