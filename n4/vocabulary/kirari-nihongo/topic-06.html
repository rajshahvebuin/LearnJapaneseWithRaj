3 練習しましょう！ — every question (p.24–25)

ウォーミングアップ：つぎの ことばから おなじ グループではない ものを 一つ えらびましょう。Woomingu appu: Tsugi no kotoba kara onaji guruupu de wa nai mono o hitotsu erabimashou. "Break" is one word in English, Hindi and Gujarati, but four in Japanese, each with a transitive / intransitive pair. (added, not in book)

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
壊す ／ 壊れるkowasu / kowareru break (machines, things with parts) Clocks, phones, cameras, houses. Also "stop working". 時計が壊れた
割る ／ 割れるwaru / wareru break into pieces (hard, flat things) Glass, plates, mirrors, eggs. 窓ガラスを割る · 鏡が割れた
折る ／ 折れるoru / oreru snap (long, thin things) Chopsticks, pencils, bones, branches. はしが折れた · 骨を折る
破る ／ 破れるyaburu / yabureru tear (paper, cloth) Trousers, bags, paper, posters. ズボンが破れた
落とす ／ 落ちるotosu / ochiru drop / fall 落とす: you let it fall (を). 落ちる: it or you fall (が, に). 財布を落とす · 線路に落ちる
ぶつける ／ ぶつかるbutsukeru / butsukaru hit (something against) / bump into 頭をぶつける (you hit your head on something). 木にぶつかる (you walk into a tree). 話しましょう: 木にぶつかってしまった
なくす ／ 忘れる ／ 落とすnakusu / wasureru / otosu lose / leave behind / drop なくす = it's gone, you don't know where. 忘れる = you left it somewhere (you know where). 落とす = it fell from your pocket or hand. 電車に傘を忘れた · 鍵をなくした
危ない ／ 危険 · 大変 ／ 残念 ／ 困るabunai / kiken · taihen / zannen / komaru dangerous · serious / too bad / be in trouble 危険 is the written word on signs. 大変 = a big or serious problem; 残念 = disappointed; 困る = you don't know what to do. exercise 2
火 ／ 火事hi / kaji fire / a fire (accident) 火 = flame, heat for cooking. 火事 = a building burning. 火を消す · 火事だ!
〜てしまう for accidents: ［person］が［thing］を + transitive verb + てしまった = I did it by mistake (時計を壊してしまった). ［thing］が + intransitive verb + てしまった = it happened (時計が壊れてしまった). Japanese people often choose the intransitive form to sound less like they are blaming someone. (added, not in book)
Emergency numbers in Japan: 119 for fire (消防車) and ambulance (救急車), 110 for police (パトカー). Say 火事です or 救急です first. (added, not in book)
