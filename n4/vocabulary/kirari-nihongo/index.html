きらり日本語 N4 語彙

Following 日本語能力試験対応 きらり☆日本語 N4 語彙 (Kirari Nihongo N4 Vocabulary, 凡人社) topic by topic. Part 1, 言葉を増やそう (Vocabulary Builder), teaches words in 12 everyday topics with pictures, then practises them. Three short extra sections cover shapes and positions, verbs, and how the JLPT tests vocabulary. Part 2 has four 19-question practice tests. Every page gives the full word list, every exercise with the answer from the book's 解答 booklet, and confusion pairs, all in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Each topic has two picture "dictionary" pages and two practice pages. Learn the word list first, then do the 練習しましょう (practice) questions, then check the confusion pairs.
  • Words printed in lighter type in the book are above N4 level. The word-list tables mark them, so you can learn the N4 words first.
  • Take the four 模擬問題 (practice tests) after the 12 topics. Each has 19 questions: fill in the blank, same meaning, and correct usage.
  • The book's pictures are described in words, not copied. Short reading texts in the exercises are summarised, with only the key line quoted.
