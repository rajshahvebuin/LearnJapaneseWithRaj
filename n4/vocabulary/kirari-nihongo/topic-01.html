Answer ①1 ②4 ③3 ④2 (book key, 解答 p.1). (added, not in book) Follow the order of the verbs in the text: 上げて (raise) → のばして、上を見て (stretch, look up) → 下ろして (lower) → まわします (roll). The neck roll comes last: つぎに くびを まわします "next, roll your neck". The text uses 下ろす for lowering the arms; 下げる (word 17) would also be fine.

4 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

In English, Hindi and Gujarati several of these words come out the same ("condition", "heal", "doctor"). In Japanese they are different words with different partners. (added, not in book)

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
治る ／ 治すnaoru / naosu get better / cure 治る has no doer: the illness gets better (風邪が治る). 治す has a doer: someone cures it (医者が病気を治す). Same pattern as 上がる／上げる. Patient talking about himself: 早く治るといいですね. Doctor's job: 病気を治す.
気分 ／ 具合 ／ 調子 ／ 顔色kibun / guai / choushi / kaoiro all "how you are" 気分 = how you feel inside (気分が悪い = I feel sick). 具合 = health condition, often what a doctor asks about. 調子 = condition or form, also for machines (パソコンの調子が悪い). 顔色 = how you look from outside. Seeing a pale friend: 顔色が悪いですよ. At the clinic: 具合はどうですか.
熱が ある ／ 熱が 出るnetsu ga aru / netsu ga deru have a fever / get a fever ある = the state now; 出る = the fever starts. Exercise 1 puts ねつ in the 出る group. 今、熱があります · 昨日の夜、熱が出ました
眠る ／ 寝る ／ 眠いnemuru / neru / nemui sleep / go to bed, sleep / sleepy 眠る = be asleep (よく眠る). 寝る = go to bed or lie down, and also sleep. 眠い is an adjective: you feel sleepy. 毎日11時に寝ます · 眠いです
上げる ／ 下げる ／ 下ろすageru / sageru / orosu raise / lower / lower, take down 下げる moves something to a lower level; 下ろす brings something all the way down (arms to your side, a bag from a shelf). Both appear in this topic. Exercise 5: うでを下ろして
歯科 ／ 歯医者 · 眼科 ／ 目医者shika / haisha · ganka / meisha dentistry / dentist · eye department / eye doctor 〜科 is the department name on signs. 歯医者 and 目医者 are everyday words for the doctor or the clinic. Sign: 歯科 4F · Talk: 歯医者に行きます
入院 ／ 退院nyuuin / taiin go into hospital / leave hospital 入 = enter, 退 = withdraw. Going to a clinic and coming home the same day is not 入院; it is 病院に行く. 一週間入院しました · 明日退院します
年齢 ／ 年 ／ 〜歳nenrei / toshi / -sai age 年齢 is the written word on forms; in speech ask おいくつですか or 何歳ですか. 〜歳 is the counter. Form: 年齢 25歳
Fixed verb partners: 風邪をひく (not 風邪をする), 骨を折る, けがをする, マスクをする, 目薬をさす, 熱がある／出る, せき・くしゃみ・鼻水が出る, 寒気がする. These are tested exactly as exercise 2 does. (added, not in book)
Trap for Hindi and Gujarati speakers: we say दवा खाना / દવા ખાવી ("eat medicine"), but Japanese always says 薬を飲む ("drink"), for pills, capsules and liquid alike. 薬を食べる is wrong. (added, not in book)
