もんだい3 語彙「正しい意味」の文 — what goes in the ( )?

（　　）に なにを いれますか。１・２・３・４から いちばん いい ものを ひとつ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: What do you put in the ( )? Choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: ( ) में क्या रखेंगे? 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: ( ) માં શું મૂકશો? 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

もんだい3 Q1

エレベーターで いきますか、（　　）で いきますか。Erebeetaa de ikimasu ka, ( ) de ikimasu ka.

EN: Shall we go by the lift, or by the ( )?
HI: लिफ़्ट से जाएँगे, या ( ) से?
GU: લિફ્ટથી જઈશું, કે ( ) થી?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かいだんkaidanstairs ✓सीढ़ियाँદાદર
2げんかんgenkanentrance hallप्रवेश-द्वारપ્રવેશદ્વાર
3さんぽsanpoa walk, strollटहलनाફરવું, લટાર
4だいどころdaidokorokitchenरसोईરસોડું

Why: The choice is between two ways of going up or down a building: the lift (エレベーター) or the stairs (かいだん). で here means "by means of". さんぽ is a walk, not a way up; げんかん and だいどころ are rooms.

GU: શાળા જૂનથી ઑગસ્ટ સુધી ( ) બંધ છે.

Exam trap: in もんだい3 several options often make a "possible" sentence in your own language (you could wear trousers, an old parent is old). Japanese chooses by fixed word partners: ぼうし + かぶる, めがね + かける, かぜ + ひく, people + わかい. In もんだい4, watch for options that change who does the action (Q3) or swap skill and liking (Q6).
