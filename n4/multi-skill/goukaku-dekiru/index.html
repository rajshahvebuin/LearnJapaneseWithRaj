JLPT N4 · All-in-one · Book

合格できる N4・N5

Following 合格できる日本語能力試験 N4・N5 (Goukaku Dekiru JLPT N4・N5, 市川綾子・瀬戸口彩・松本隆, アルク) unit by unit. One book covers both levels. 第1部 練習問題 (practice) drills every JLPT section, first for N5 and then for N4. 第2部 総復習問題 (review) is one full mock test for each level. Every page gives the tested words or patterns, every question with the answer from the book's 別冊 key, and confusion pairs. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • If you are preparing for N5, work through the 第1部 N5 units, then take the 第2部 N5 mock test. For N4, do the same with the N4 units. N4 learners who feel unsure of the basics can start with the N5 half.
  • The N4 drills are long (up to 86 questions in one book section), so they are split into parts of about 20 questions. Each part stands on its own.
  • Take the 総復習 mock tests in one sitting with a timer, as if it were the real exam, and only then open the answer pages.
  • Reading and listening pages give a short summary of each text or conversation in our own words, not the book's text, and quote only the lines each question needs.
