Notes
This is the N5 mock test (第2部 総復習問題) from the N5 half of the book; the course is filed under N4 because one volume covers both levels. The 別冊 gives only the answer numbers, so every translation, situation description and "Why" note is added by us. In these two もんだい the question page shows only a picture (もんだい3) or nothing at all (もんだい4): the question and the three answer choices are spoken. We quote them from the 別冊 script because each one is a single short line; no longer script is reproduced. The book's pictures are described in words only. The book's audio CD is copyrighted and is not published here — play your own copy by track number. Misprint: on p.190 the badges for もんだい4 1–6ばん say CD① 28–33, although the instructions badge on the same page says CD② 27 and the 別冊 script says CD②. They are CD2 Tracks 28–33 (CD1 28–33 are the practice-part items).

もんだい3 自分から話す (p.187–189) — CD2 Tracks 19–26

CD2, Track 19 もんだい3では、えを みながら しつもんを きいて ください。やじるし（→）の ひとは なんと いいますか。1から3の なかから、いちばん いいものを ひとつ えらんで ください。Mondai 3 de wa, e o minagara shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Yajirushi (→) no hito wa nan to iimasu ka. 1 kara 3 no naka kara, ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: In もんだい3, look at the picture and listen to the question. What does the person with the arrow (→) say? Choose the best answer from 1–3.   HI: चित्र देखते हुए प्रश्न सुनें। तीर (→) वाला व्यक्ति क्या कहता है? 1–3 में से सबसे सही उत्तर चुनें।   GU: ચિત્ર જોતાં જોતાં પ્રશ્ન સાંભળો. તીર (→) વાળી વ્યક્તિ શું કહે છે? 1–3 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

れい (example): a man in a suit (arrow) waves as he leaves home; a woman and a child see him off. "You're going out now. What do you say?" — 行って きます / おげんきで / では、また. The book gives the answer: 1 行って きます. (Summary added, not in book.)

もんだい3 1ばん CD2, Track 20

Picture (described): a person (arrow) sits at a table with empty bowls and plates, smiling, hands together after the meal.

ごはんを 食べました。何と いいますか。Gohan o tabemashita. Nan to iimasu ka.

EN: You have finished eating. What do you say?
HI: आपने खाना खा लिया। आप क्या कहेंगे?
GU: તમે જમી લીધું. તમે શું કહેશો?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1いただきます。Itadakimasu.(said before eating)(खाने से पहले कहते हैं)(જમતાં પહેલાં બોલાય)
2おいしいです。Oishii desu.It's delicious.स्वादिष्ट है।સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે.
3ごちそうさまでした。Gochisousama deshita.Thank you for the meal. ✓खाने के लिए धन्यवाद।જમવા બદલ આભાર.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.6). Why (added, not in book): 食べました (past) = the meal is over → ごちそうさまでした. いただきます is said before eating. おいしいです describes food you are eating now, and it is in present tense.
