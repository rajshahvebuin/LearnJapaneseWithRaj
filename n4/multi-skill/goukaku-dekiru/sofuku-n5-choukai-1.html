Notes
This is the N5 mock test (第2部 総復習問題) from the N5 half of the book; the course is filed under N4 because one volume covers both levels. As in the real exam, item numbers restart at 1ばん in each もんだい. The 別冊 gives only the answer numbers, so every summary, translation, picture description and "Why" note is added by us. The question sentences come from the 別冊 script (on the CD they are spoken). Scripts are not reproduced: each item has a short summary in our own words plus the one line the answer depends on. The book's pictures are described in words only. The book's audio CD is copyrighted and is not published here — play your own copy by track number. Small misprint: in the 別冊 script, the badge for もんだい1 4ばん reads "CD① 06", but the question page (p.180) and the track sequence show it is CD2 Track 6.

もんだい1 「どうするか」を聞く (p.178–182) — CD2 Tracks 2–10

CD2, Track 2 もんだい1では はじめに、しつもんを きいて ください。それから はなしを きいて、もんだいようしの 1から4の なかから、いちばん いいものを ひとつ えらんで ください。Mondai 1 de wa hajime ni, shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Sorekara hanashi o kiite, mondai youshi no 1 kara 4 no naka kara, ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: In もんだい1, first listen to the question. Then listen to the talk and choose the best answer from 1–4 on the question sheet.   HI: पहले प्रश्न सुनें। फिर बातचीत सुनकर प्रश्न-पत्र के 1–4 में से सबसे सही उत्तर चुनें।   GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન સાંભળો. પછી વાતચીત સાંભળીને પ્રશ્નપત્રના 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

れい (example): at the office a man asks a woman to put a 200-yen stamp on an envelope and post it; the stamps are in that desk. The pictures show posting a letter, writing, buying a stamp at a counter, and writing a letter. The book gives the answer: 1 (posting it). (Summary added, not in book.)

HI: आज का विषय जापानी इमारतें हैं। कक्षा में पढ़ने से पहले छात्र कंप्यूटर पर जानकारी खोजेंगे; फिर पुस्तकालय से किताबें लेंगे; फिर कक्षा में लौटकर चर्चा करेंगे।

GU: આજનો વિષય જાપાની ઇમારતો છે. વર્ગમાં ભણતાં પહેલાં વિદ્યાર્થીઓ કમ્પ્યુટર પર માહિતી શોધશે; પછી પુસ્તકાલયમાંથી પુસ્તકો લેશે; પછી વર્ગમાં પાછા આવીને ચર્ચા કરશે.

Script summary (not the book's text)
