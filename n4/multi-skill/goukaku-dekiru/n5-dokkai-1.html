Key idea

(added, not in book) Each text has 3–5 sentences and one question. Read the question first, then find the sentence that answers it. Wrong options re-use words from the text but change one detail: a negative (入りません), a reason (なぜ), a time (あさ / ひる / よる) or a day.

HI: पहले प्रश्न पढ़ें, फिर वह वाक्य ढूँढें जो उत्तर देता है। ग़लत विकल्प पाठ के शब्द लेते हैं पर एक बात बदल देते हैं: नकार, कारण, समय या दिन। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન વાંચો, પછી જવાબ આપતું વાક્ય શોધો. ખોટા વિકલ્પો લખાણના શબ્દો વાપરે છે પણ એક વાત બદલે છે: નકાર, કારણ, સમય કે દિવસ.

[1] · はがき — Anna's postcard from the sea (p.30)

もんだい4 [1]

アンナさんは 毎日 何を して いますか。Anna-san wa mainichi nani o shite imasu ka.

EN: What does Anna do every day?   HI: अन्ना हर दिन क्या करती है?   GU: અન્ના દરરોજ શું કરે છે?

Key line(s) quoted from the text

わたしは 今 海の ちかくに います。毎日 さんぽしたり、本を 読んだり して います。Watashi wa ima umi no chikaku ni imasu. Mainichi sanpo shitari, hon o yondari shite imasu.

でも 水が つめたいから 海には 入りません。Demo mizu ga tsumetai kara umi ni wa hairimasen.

EN: I'm near the sea now. Every day I go for walks and read books. … But the water is cold, so I don't go into the sea.   HI: मैं अभी समुद्र के पास हूँ। रोज़ टहलती हूँ और किताबें पढ़ती हूँ। … पर पानी ठंडा है, इसलिए समुद्र में नहीं जाती।   GU: હું હમણાં દરિયા પાસે છું. રોજ ફરું છું અને પુસ્તકો વાંચું છું. … પણ પાણી ઠંડું છે, એટલે દરિયામાં નથી જતી.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1さんぽしたり、海に 入ったり して います。Sanpo shitari, umi ni haittari shite imasu.She walks and goes into the sea.वह टहलती है और समुद्र में जाती है।તે ફરે છે અને દરિયામાં જાય છે.
2きれいな 海を 見たり、およいだり して います。Kirei na umi o mitari, oyoidari shite imasu.She looks at the beautiful sea and swims.वह सुंदर समुद्र देखती है और तैरती है।તે સુંદર દરિયો જુએ છે અને તરે છે.
3海の ちかくを あるいたり、本を 読んだり して います。Umi no chikaku o aruitari, hon o yondari shite imasu.She walks near the sea and reads books. ✓वह समुद्र के पास चलती है और किताबें पढ़ती है।તે દરિયા પાસે ચાલે છે અને પુસ્તકો વાંચે છે.
4本を 読んだり、海に 入ったり して います。Hon o yondari, umi ni haittari shite imasu.She reads and goes into the sea.वह पढ़ती है और समुद्र में जाती है।તે વાંચે છે અને દરિયામાં જાય છે.

Answer 3 (別冊 p.2). Why (added, not in book): さんぽする = walk around, so "walking near the sea" (海の ちかくを あるく) is the same idea in other words. Options 1, 2 and 4 all include going into the sea or swimming, but the last line says 海には 入りません. The answer re-words the text — this is typical of the exam.

Column ① part ②

（　　）の 中には どんな ことばが 入りますか。( ) no naka ni wa donna kotoba ga hairimasu ka.

EN: What word goes in the ( )?
HI: ( ) में कौन-सा शब्द आएगा?
GU: ( ) માં કયો શબ્દ આવશે?
ItemJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ここで 道（を）わたります。Koko de michi (o) watarimasu.I cross the road here. ✓मैं यहाँ सड़क पार करता हूँ।હું અહીં રસ્તો ઓળંગું છું.
2つぎの かど（を）まがって ください。Tsugi no kado (o) magatte kudasai.Please turn at the next corner. ✓कृपया अगले मोड़ पर मुड़िए।કૃપા કરીને આગલા ખૂણે વળો.
3この バスは 駅の 前（を）とおります。Kono basu wa eki no mae (o) toorimasu.This bus passes in front of the station. ✓यह बस स्टेशन के सामने से गुज़रती है।આ બસ સ્ટેશન સામેથી પસાર થાય છે.
4トイレは かいだん（を）おりた ところに あります。Toire wa kaidan (o) orita tokoro ni arimasu.The toilet is where you come down the stairs. ✓शौचालय सीढ़ियाँ उतरने के बाद वाली जगह पर है।શૌચાલય દાદર ઊતર્યા પછીની જગ્યાએ છે.

Answers (answer printed on p.33): all four are を. Why (added, not in book): with movement verbs (わたる cross, まがる turn, とおる pass, おりる go down, さんぽする walk), を marks the place you move along, across or through. It is not an object. This is the rule from 文法 もんだい1 Q14.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

(added, not in book) The four wrong-answer tricks in this unit, with the words that set them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
〜には 入りません~ni wa hairimasendoes not go inA negative at the end cancels the activity.[1] options 1, 2, 4
それでsoredeso, that's whyWhat comes after それで is the result / purpose; what comes before is the background.[2]

〜までに / 〜に~made ni / ~niby (deadline) / at (time)までに = latest time for something else (a reply), not the event itself.[4] option 1
さんぽする ≈ ちかくを あるくsanpo suru ≈ chikaku o arukutake a walkThe right answer often uses other words for the same idea.[1] option 3
Exam trap: the option that copies the most words from the text is often wrong. In [1] and [3] the correct answer re-words the text (あるいたり for さんぽしたり; しょくどうで ごはん), while the wrong ones copy words like 海, そば and 木よう日 into the wrong place.
