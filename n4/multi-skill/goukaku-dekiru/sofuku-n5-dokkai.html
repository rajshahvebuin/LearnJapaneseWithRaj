[27] · library notice (p.172)

総復習 [27]

今日は 19日 火ようびです。かりて いる 本は どこに かえしますか。Kyou wa juukunichi kayoubi desu. Karite iru hon wa doko ni kaeshimasu ka.

EN: Today is Tuesday the 19th. Where do you return the borrowed books?   HI: आज मंगलवार 19 तारीख़ है। उधार ली हुई किताबें कहाँ लौटाएँ?   GU: આજે મંગળવાર 19 તારીખ છે. ઉછીનાં લીધેલાં પુસ્તકો ક્યાં પાછાં આપવાં?

Key line quoted from the text

かりて いる 本は 入り口の よこの ポストに 入れて ください。Karite iru hon wa iriguchi no yoko no posuto ni irete kudasai.

EN: Please put borrowed books in the box beside the entrance.   HI: उधार की किताबें दरवाज़े के बगल वाले डिब्बे में डालिए।   GU: ઉછીનાં પુસ્તકો દરવાજાની બાજુની પેટીમાં નાખો.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1入り口の 前iriguchi no maein front of the entranceदरवाज़े के सामनेદરવાજાની સામે

[29] · directions to Sakura Park + map (p.173)

せまい みちの つぎの、ひろい 道の かどに あります。Semai michi no tsugi no, hiroi michi no kado ni arimasu.

Above the railway there are two blocks on each side: ウ (left) and エ (right) are the lower blocks, next to the railway; ア (left) and イ (right) are the upper blocks, one road further up. この 人の 国では なぜ「ごはんを 食べましたか」と いう あいさつが できましたか。Kono hito no kuni de wa naze "Gohan o tabemashita ka" to iu aisatsu ga dekimashita ka.
