もんだい1 文法「正しい文法」の文 — what goes in the ( )?

（　　）に 何を 入れますか。１・２・３・４から いちばん いい ものを 一つ えらんで ください。( ) ni nani o iremasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: What do you put in the ( )? Choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: ( ) में क्या रखेंगे? 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: ( ) માં શું મૂકશો? 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

もんだい1 Q1

それは わたし（　　）かさです。Sore wa watashi ( ) kasa desu.

EN: That is my umbrella.
HI: वह मेरा छाता है।
GU: તે મારી છત્રી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1をoobject markerकर्म-सूचकકર્મ-સૂચક
2がgasubject markerकर्ता-सूचकકર્તા-સૂચક
3のno's, of (owner) ✓का / कीનું / ની
4もmoalsoभीપણ

Why: Two nouns side by side (わたし + かさ) are joined with の: わたしの かさ = "my umbrella". を and が must be followed by a verb or adjective, not by another noun.

もんだい1 Q2

じてんしゃ（　　）学校へ 行きます。Jitensha ( ) gakkou e ikimasu.

EN: I go to school by bicycle.
HI: मैं साइकिल से स्कूल जाता हूँ।
GU: હું સાઇકલથી શાળાએ જાઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1でdeby (means) ✓से (साधन)થી (સાધન)
2にnito, at; on (a vehicle: に のる)को, पर; (सवारी) परને, પર; (વાહન) પર
3がgasubject markerकर्ता-सूचकકર્તા-સૂચક
4のno's, ofकाનું

Why: The bicycle is the means of going, so で: じてんしゃで 行く. に is used with のる (じてんしゃに のる, "ride a bicycle"), not with 行く. じてんしゃの 学校 would be "a bicycle's school".

もんだい1 Q3

母は りょうり（　　）じょうずです。Haha wa ryouri ( ) jouzu desu.

EN: My mother is good at cooking.
HI: मेरी माँ खाना बनाने में माहिर हैं।
GU: મારી મા રસોઈમાં હોશિયાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1をoobject markerकर्म-सूचकકર્મ-સૂચક
2がga(good at) ✓(में अच्छा)(માં હોશિયાર)
3でdeat, byमें, सेમાં, થી
4にnito, atको, परને, પર

Why: じょうず, へた, すき, きらい, わかる and ほしい take が for the thing: A は B が じょうず. English "good at" leads learners to choose を or に, but Japanese uses が.

もんだい1 Q4

きょうしつには だれ（　　）いませんでした。Kyoushitsu ni wa dare ( ) imasen deshita.

EN: There was nobody in the classroom.
HI: कक्षा में कोई नहीं था।
GU: વર્ગમાં કોઈ નહોતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にnito, atको, परને, પર
2はwatopic markerविषय-सूचकવિષય-સૂચક
3もmo(nobody) ✓(कोई) नहीं(કોઈ) નહીં
4がgasubject markerकर्ता-सूचकકર્તા-સૂચક

Why: Question word + も + negative = "no one / nothing": だれも いません (nobody is there), なにも ありません (there is nothing). だれが いましたか would be a question ("who was there?"), but this sentence is a statement with a negative verb.

もんだい1 Q5

りょうり（　　）本を 買いました。Ryouri ( ) hon o kaimashita.

EN: I bought a cookbook (a book about cooking).
HI: मैंने खाना बनाने की किताब ख़रीदी।
GU: મેં રસોઈનું પુસ્તક ખરીદ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1とtoandऔरઅને
2のnoof, about (kind) ✓की (विषय)નું (વિષય)
3がgasubject markerकर्ता-सूचकકર્તા-સૂચક
4をoobject markerकर्म-सूचकકર્મ-સૂચક

Why: の also shows the kind or topic of a thing: りょうりの 本 = a cooking book, 日本語の 本 = a Japanese book. を cannot be used twice for one verb (りょうりを 本を). Option 1 りょうりと 本 ("cooking and a book") does not make sense, because you cannot buy "cooking".

もんだい1 Q6

10時（　　）あいましょう。Juuji ( ) aimashou.

EN: Let's meet at 10 o'clock.
HI: चलो 10 बजे मिलते हैं।
GU: ચાલો 10 વાગે મળીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1にniat (time) ✓(समय) पर(સમયે)
2でdeat (place of action), by(जगह) में, से(જગ્યા) માં, થી

もんだい1 Q7

新しい じしょ（　　）ほしいです。Atarashii jisho ( ) hoshii desu.

EN: I want a new dictionary.
HI: मुझे एक नया शब्दकोश चाहिए।
GU: મારે નવો શબ્દકોશ જોઈએ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: I live in Tokyo now.
HI: मैं अभी टोक्यो में रहता हूँ।
GU: હું હમણાં ટોક્યોમાં રહું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: You must not park your car here.
HI: यहाँ गाड़ी खड़ी करना मना है।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

(added, not in book) In this part every blank is a particle. The trick is to look at the word after the blank (a noun? a verb? which verb?) — it decides the particle more often than the word before it.

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
に / で（場所）ni / dein, atに with being verbs (いる, ある, すむ); で with action verbs (たべる, はたらく).東京に すむ／東京で はたらく
に / で（時・手段）ni / deat (time) / by (means)に after a time; で after a tool or vehicle.10時に あう／じてんしゃで 行く
を / がo / gaobject / "liked, wanted" thingを with action verbs; が with じょうず, すき, ほしい, わかる.じしょを 買う／じしょが ほしい
を（通過） / へ（方向）o / eacross / towardsを = you move along/across it; へ = you move to it.道を わたる／学校へ 行く
だれも / だれがdare mo / dare ganobody / who?だれも + negative statement; だれが = question.だれも いない／だれが いますか
どこか / どこdokoka / dokosomewhere / where?どこかへ 行きましたか → はい／いいえ; どこへ 行きましたか → a place.どこかへ／どこへ
Exam trap: English and Hindi/Gujarati can mislead you here. "Good at cooking" and "want a dictionary" feel like objects, so learners choose を — Japanese uses が with じょうず and ほしい (Q3, Q7). "Live in Tokyo" feels like an action place, so learners choose で — すむ takes に (Q9).
