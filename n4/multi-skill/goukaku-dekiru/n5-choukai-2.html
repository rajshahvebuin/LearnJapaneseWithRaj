Notes
This is the N5 half of the book; the course is filed under N4 because one volume covers both levels. The 別冊 gives only the answer numbers, so every translation, situation description and "Why" note is added by us. In these two もんだい the question page shows only a picture (もんだい3) or nothing at all (もんだい4): the question and the three answer choices are spoken. We quote them from the 別冊 script because each one is a single short line, as on our other N5 listening pages; no longer script is reproduced. The book's pictures are described in words only. The book's audio CD is copyrighted and is not published here — play your own copy by track number.

もんだい3 自分から話す (p.56–59) — CD1 Tracks 19–26

CD1, Track 19 もんだい3では、えを みながら しつもんを きいて ください。やじるし（→）の ひとは なんと いいますか。1から3の なかから、いちばん いいものを ひとつ えらんで ください。Mondai 3 de wa, e o minagara shitsumon o kiite kudasai. Yajirushi (→) no hito wa nan to iimasu ka. 1 kara 3 no naka kara, ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: In もんだい3, look at the picture and listen to the question. What does the person with the arrow (→) say? Choose the best answer from 1–3.   HI: चित्र देखते हुए प्रश्न सुनें। तीर (→) वाला व्यक्ति क्या कहता है? 1–3 में से सबसे सही उत्तर चुनें।   GU: ચિત્ર જોતાં જોતાં પ્રશ્ન સાંભળો. તીર (→) વાળી વ્યક્તિ શું કહે છે? 1–3 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય જવાબ પસંદ કરો.

れい (example): the picture shows a student (arrow) walking past a teacher outside the school in the morning. The choices are おはようございます / しつれいします / はじめまして. The book gives the answer: 1 おはようございます, the morning greeting. (Summary added, not in book.)

もんだい3 1ばん CD1, Track 20

Picture (described): an older woman, smiling, holds out a gift box with a ribbon; a young woman (arrow) receives it with a happy face.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ありがとう ございます。Arigatou gozaimasu.Thank you very much. ✓बहुत धन्यवाद।ખૂબ આભાર.
2ごめんなさい。Gomen nasai.I'm sorry.माफ़ कीजिए।માફ કરશો.

Picture (described): two people sit at a long table; there is one empty chair. A young woman with a bag (arrow) stands beside it and points to it.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ここ、いいですか。Koko, ii desu ka.Is it OK (to sit) here? ✓क्या यहाँ (बैठ सकते हैं)?અહીં (બેસી શકાય)?
