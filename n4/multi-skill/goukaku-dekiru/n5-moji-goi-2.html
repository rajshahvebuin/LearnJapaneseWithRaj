もんだい2 文字 文字の書き方 — how do you write the underlined word?

＿＿＿の ことばは どう かきますか。１・２・３・４から いちばん いい ものを ひとつ えらんで ください。___ no kotoba wa dou kakimasu ka. 1・2・3・4 kara ichiban ii mono o hitotsu erande kudasai.

EN: How do you write the underlined word? Choose the best one from 1–4.   HI: रेखांकित शब्द कैसे लिखा जाता है? 1–4 में से सबसे सही एक चुनें।   GU: રેખાંકિત શબ્દ કેવી રીતે લખાય? 1–4 માંથી સૌથી યોગ્ય એક પસંદ કરો.

もんだい2 Q1

まいばん ラジオを ききます。Maiban rajio o kikimasu.

EN: I listen to the radio every evening.
HI: मैं हर शाम रेडियो सुनता हूँ।
GU: હું દરરોજ સાંજે રેડિયો સાંભળું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1語きます(not a real spelling)not a real word (語 = ご, language)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (語 = भाषा)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (語 = ભાષા)
2話きます(not a real spelling)not a real word (話す = はなす, to speak)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (話す = बोलना)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (話す = બોલવું)
3言きます(not a real spelling)not a real word (言う = いう, to say)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (言う = कहना)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (言う = કહેવું)
4聞きますkikimasulisten ✓सुनता हूँસાંભળું છું

Why: All four kanji are about language and talking, so they are easy to mix up. But only 聞 is read き in "listen": 聞く = きく. 聞 has 耳 (ear) inside 門 (gate): you listen with your ears. 語, 話 and 言 all have the 言 "speech" sign and are about speaking.

もんだい2 Q2

えきまで たくしーで いきましょう。Eki made takushii de ikimashou.

EN: Let's go to the station by taxi.
HI: चलो स्टेशन तक टैक्सी से चलते हैं।
GU: ચાલો સ્ટેશન સુધી ટૅક્સીમાં જઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1クタツーkutatsuunot a real wordवास्तविक शब्द नहींસાચો શબ્દ નથી
2クタシーkutashiinot a real word (ク and タ swapped)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (ク और タ उलटे)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (ク અને タ ઊલટા)
3タクシーtakushiitaxi ✓टैक्सीટૅક્સી
4タクツーtakutsuunot a real word (ツ instead of シ)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (シ की जगह ツ)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (シ ની જગ્યાએ ツ)

Why: たくしー → タ・ク・シ・ー = タクシー. Two traps: タ (ta) and ク (ku) look alike (タ has one more short line inside), and シ (shi) and ツ (tsu) look alike. In シ the dots are on the left and the long stroke goes up from the bottom; in ツ the dots are on top and the stroke goes down.

もんだい2 Q3

そこに くるまを とめないで ください。Soko ni kuruma o tomenaide kudasai.

EN: Please don't park (stop) your car there.
HI: कृपया वहाँ गाड़ी खड़ी मत कीजिए।
GU: કૃપા કરીને ત્યાં ગાડી પાર્ક ન કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1上めないで(not a real spelling)not a real word (上 = うえ, up)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (上 = ऊपर)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (上 = ઉપર)
2止めないでtomenaidedon't stop / park ✓मत रोकिए / खड़ी मत कीजिएન રોકો / પાર્ક ન કરો

もんだい2 Q4

かいぎは なんじまで ですか。Kaigi wa nanji made desu ka.

EN: Until what time is the meeting?
HI: मीटिंग कितने बजे तक है?
GU: મીટિંગ કેટલા વાગ્યા સુધી છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1何時nanjiwhat time ✓कितने बजेકેટલા વાગે
2何寺(not a real word)not a real word (寺 = てら, temple)वास्तविक शब्द नहीं (寺 = मंदिर)સાચો શબ્દ નથી (寺 = મંદિર)
3何町nanmachiwhich town (not "what time")कौन-सा मोहल्ला ("कितने बजे" नहीं)કયો વિસ્તાર ("કેટલા વાગે" નહીં)
4何事nanigotowhat matter (N3 word, not "what time")क्या बात (N3 शब्द, "कितने बजे" नहीं)શી વાત (N3 શબ્દ, "કેટલા વાગે" નહીં)

Why: じ "o'clock" is 時. 時 = 日 (sun) + 寺 (temple): the sun tells the time. Option 2 drops the 日 part. 寺 is also read じ in temple names, which is the trap. 町 (まち, town) and 事 (こと, matter) have different meanings.

EN: Today I have no work (it's a day off).

EN: My father is very tall.
HI: मेरे पिता बहुत लंबे हैं।
GU: મારા પિતા ખૂબ ઊંચા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: たべる = 食べる. 食 is 人 (a roof) over 良. Option 3 drops the roof. Option 4 is the meaning trap: 飲 (drink) is also about food, and it even has 食 on the left side, but it is のむ.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

(added, not in book) In this もんだい you already know the sound; the question is which shape is right. The book uses three kinds of trap: kanji that look alike (休／体), kanji with the same sound but another meaning (買／貝, 休／安), and kanji from the same topic (聞／話／言, 父／母). Exam trap: do not choose by meaning alone. In Q7 (父 / 母 / 兄 / 姉) and Q9 (万 / 千 / 百 / 一) every option is a real word that fits the sentence, so you must know the shape. In Q5 and Q14 the wrong option has the same sound (安 やす, 貝 かい) — a sound match is not enough; the meaning must match too.
