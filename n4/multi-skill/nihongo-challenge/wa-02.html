1 The Story (p.30)

Theme of the chapter

マリアとノーイは、はじめて雪を見ました。カルロスと3人で公園で遊びます。Maria to Nooi wa, hajimete yuki o mimashita. Karurosu to sannin de kouen de asobimasu.

EN: Maria and Noi have seen snow for the first time. With Carlos, the three of them have fun in the park.
HI: मारिया और नोई ने पहली बार बर्फ़ देखी। कार्लोस के साथ तीनों पार्क में खेलते हैं।
GU: મારિયા અને નોઈએ પહેલી વાર બરફ જોયો. કાર્લોસ સાથે ત્રણેય બગીચામાં રમે છે.

The picture shows a snowman, Noi in a winter coat and Maria throwing a snowball at Carlos. The first sentence of each example box tells the story: Noi walks through the park watching the snow, the cold friends want warm tea, and Carlos explains that snow sometimes falls on the high mountains of Brazil too.

HI: नोई बर्फ़ देखते हुए पार्क में घूमती है, ठंड में सबको गरम चाय पीने का मन करता है, और कार्लोस बताता है कि ब्राज़ील के ऊँचे पहाड़ों पर भी कभी-कभी बर्फ़ गिरती है। GU: નોઈ બરફ જોતાં જોતાં બગીચામાં ફરે છે, ઠંડીમાં બધાને ગરમ ચા પીવાનું મન થાય છે, અને કાર્લોસ કહે છે કે બ્રાઝિલના ઊંચા પહાડો પર પણ ક્યારેક બરફ પડે છે.

2 Grammar Points (p.30–31, 別冊 p.3–4)

Three points, in the book's order. The connection (接続) is given exactly as the book prints it. The examples table has the story sentence first, then every example ❶–❹ from p.31. The English follows the 別冊 translation.

【1】 〜ながら、…

two actions at once
Reading~nagara, …
Meaning (EN)"… while ~ing": one person does two actions at the same time.
Meaning (HI)"~ करते हुए …": एक ही व्यक्ति एक साथ दो काम करता है।
Meaning (GU)"~ કરતાં કરતાં …": એક જ વ્યક્તિ એક સાથે બે કામ કરે છે.
Connection (接続)Vます＋ながらV(masu) + nagara
Typical useThe same person does two actions at the same time (別冊 p.3). The main action, the one that matters more, comes after ながら (別冊 p.4).
Verb groupFormationExample
Group I歩きます＋ながら歩きながらarukinagara
Group II食べます＋ながら食べながらtabenagara
Group III(added, not in book) します＋ながら・来ます＋ながらしながら・来ながらshinagara / kinagara

雪を見ながら、公園を歩きました。Yuki o minagara, kouen o arukimashita.I walked across the park while watching the snow.मैं बर्फ़ देखते हुए पार्क में से चली।હું બરફ જોતાં જોતાં બગીચામાંથી ચાલી.
❶ 音楽を聞きながら、料理します。Ongaku o kikinagara, ryouri shimasu.I cook while listening to music.मैं संगीत सुनते हुए खाना बनाता हूँ।હું સંગીત સાંભળતાં સાંભળતાં રસોઈ બનાવું છું.
❷ お弁当を食べながら、おしゃべりしましょう。Obentou o tabenagara, oshaberi shimashou.Let's have a chat while we have our bento.चलो, टिफ़िन खाते हुए गपशप करें।ચાલો, ટિફિન ખાતાં ખાતાં ગપ્પાં મારીએ.
❸ 辞書を引きながら、本を読みます。Jisho o hikinagara, hon o yomimasu.I read the book while looking things up in the dictionary.मैं शब्दकोश देखते हुए किताब पढ़ता हूँ।હું શબ્દકોશ જોતાં જોતાં પુસ્તક વાંચું છું.
❹ 毎朝、テレビを見ながら、食事します。Maiasa, terebi o minagara, shokuji shimasu.Every morning I have breakfast while watching television.हर सुबह मैं टीवी देखते हुए नाश्ता करता हूँ।દરરોજ સવારે હું ટીવી જોતાં જોતાં નાસ્તો કરું છું.

ながら never takes the dictionary form or the て form: always the ます-stem. The tense of the whole sentence is decided by the last verb (歩きました, 料理します). Both actions must belong to the same person; for two people, use 〜とき or 〜あいだ (added, not in book).

【2】 ①〜たい ②〜たくない

speaker's wish
Reading~tai / ~takunai
Meaning (EN)① "want to ~". ② "don't want to ~".
Meaning (HI)① "~ करना चाहता हूँ"। ② "~ नहीं करना चाहता"।
Meaning (GU)① "~ કરવું છે / કરવા માંગું છું". ② "~ નથી કરવું".
Connection (接続)① Vます＋たい　② Vます＋たくない① V(masu) + tai　② V(masu) + takunai
Typical useExpresses the speaker's own wish (別冊 p.4). It changes like an い-adjective: たい → たくない → たかった. Do not ask a superior 「〜たいですか」; it sounds rude (別冊 p.4).

Don't want (2)飲みます＋たくないです飲みたくないですnomitaku nai desu

温かいお茶が飲みたいです。…①Atatakai ocha ga nomitai desu.I want to drink warm tea.मैं गरम चाय पीना चाहती हूँ।મારે ગરમ ચા પીવી છે.
❶ 京都に住みたいです。…①Kyouto ni sumitai desu.I want to live in Kyoto.मैं क्योटो में रहना चाहता हूँ।મારે ક્યોટોમાં રહેવું છે.
❷ フィリピンへ帰りたいです。そして、母と話したいです。…①Firipin e kaeritai desu. Soshite, haha to hanashitai desu.I want to go back to the Philippines, and talk to my mother.मैं फ़िलीपींस वापस जाना चाहती हूँ, और माँ से बात करना चाहती हूँ।મારે ફિલિપાઇન્સ પાછું જવું છે, અને મા સાથે વાત કરવી છે.
❸ 雨の日は仕事に行きたくないです。…②Ame no hi wa shigoto ni ikitaku nai desu.On rainy days, I don't want to go to work.बारिश के दिन मैं काम पर नहीं जाना चाहता।વરસાદના દિવસે મારે કામ પર નથી જવું.
❹ 毎日暑いです。あまり食べたくありません。…②Mainichi atsui desu. Amari tabetaku arimasen.It's so hot every day. I don't feel like eating much.रोज़ गर्मी है। ज़्यादा खाने का मन नहीं करता।રોજ ગરમી છે. બહુ ખાવાનું મન નથી થતું.

In the story sentence the object takes が (お茶が飲みたい); お茶を飲みたい is also correct. 〜たい is for "I" (and for asking "you" in a question). For another person's wish, Japanese uses 〜たがっている, taught in 第29話 (added, not in book).

【3】 〜ることがある／〜ないことがある

sometimes
Reading~ru koto ga aru / ~nai koto ga aru
Meaning (EN)"sometimes ~ / occasionally ~"; with ない: "sometimes doesn't ~".
Meaning (HI)"कभी-कभी ~ होता है / करता है"; ない के साथ: "कभी-कभी ~ नहीं होता"।
Meaning (GU)"ક્યારેક ~ થાય છે / કરે છે"; ない સાથે: "ક્યારેક ~ નથી થતું".
Connection (接続)Vじしょ形／Vない形 ＋ことがあるV-jisho-kei / V-nai-kei + koto ga aru
Typical useNot always, but now and then something happens or someone does something (別冊 p.4). It often comes with ときどき or たまに.
FormFormationExample

Not the た form(added, not in book) 降った＋ことがある = "have (ever) fallen", experience, 第3話見たことがあるmita koto ga aru

❹ 1ヵ月、雨が降らないことがあります。Ikkagetsu, ame ga furanai koto ga arimasu.Sometimes it does not rain for a month.कभी-कभी एक महीने तक बारिश नहीं होती।ક્યારેક એક મહિના સુધી વરસાદ નથી પડતો.

The form before こと decides the meaning: dictionary / ない form = "sometimes" (this chapter); た form = "have ever done" (next chapter). In the 練習問題 (Q8–Q10) every blank wants the dictionary form.

Exam trap: all ten questions here test the verb form just before the grammar word. Make a quick rule: ながら and たい → ます-stem (歩き, 食べ, し); こと → dictionary form (使う, 来る). Then check the irregular verbs する and 来る carefully, because the wrong options are often made-up forms of them.
