にほんごチャレンジ N4

Following にほんごチャレンジ N4［文法と読む練習］ (Nihongo Challenge N4: Grammar and Reading practice, アスク出版) unit by unit. PART 1 teaches about 150 N4 grammar patterns through 32 short story chapters (第1話–第32話) about Carlos, a young Brazilian working in Japan, and his friends. A revision set comes after every four chapters, and the part ends with conjunctions, text grammar and a revision test. PART 2 has 15 short reading texts. Every page gives each grammar point with all the book's examples, every question with the answer from the book's 別冊 key, and confusion pairs. Everything is explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Work through the chapters in order. Each 話 has four pages: the grammar points, the examples (their first sentences tell the chapter's story), a 10-question 練習問題 and a もうちょっと page with extra notes and a チャレンジ.
  • After every four chapters, do the ふくしゅう問題: ten fill-in questions and five ★ word-order questions.
  • Reading pages give a short summary of each text in our own words, not the book's text, and quote only the lines each question needs.
