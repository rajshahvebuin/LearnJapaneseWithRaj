Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and four words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. Key oddity: in P35 the answer to 練習1 item 10 (十) is printed as 「とお・じゅう」 right after item 9 without its number "10"; both readings are accepted.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
二人4 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (人 → Lesson 4).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen (ひと-つ, イッ-) marks okurigana or a reading used inside words.
* = a special reading the book marks with an asterisk (一日 *ついたち, 六日 *むいか, 七日 *なのか, 八日 *ようか).

1 Kanji Charts — the 10 kanji and their words

Every number kanji has two families of readings: a Japanese one used for counting things (ひとつ, ふたつ, みっつ …, always with つ) and a Chinese one used with counters, months and money (いち, に, さん …). Dates of the month (ふつか, みっか …) mix both. (added, not in book)

P31 Q1–10

絵を 見て、数を あらわす 漢字を 選びましょう。E o mite, kazu o arawasu kanji o erabimashou.

EN: Look at the pictures and choose the kanji (from the box) that shows each number.
HI: चित्र देखकर हर संख्या दिखाने वाला कांजी (बॉक्स में से) चुनिए।

練習1 Q11

一月一日、あたらしい としが はじまります。Ichigatsu tsuitachi, atarashii toshi ga hajimarimasu.

EN: The new year starts on January 1st.
HI: नया साल 1 जनवरी को शुरू होता है।
GU: નવું વર્ષ 1 જાન્યુઆરીએ શરૂ થાય છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P35)Romaji
いちがつついたちichigatsu tsuitachi

Why: 一月 = いちがつ. The 1st day of the month is the special reading ついたち (marked * in the chart), not いちにち. いちにち (一日) means "one day / a whole day".

練習1 Q12

ともだちと 二人で うみへ いきます。Tomodachi to futari de umi e ikimasu.

EN: I am going to the sea, just the two of us, with a friend.
HI: मैं दोस्त के साथ, हम दोनों, समुद्र जाऊँगा।
GU: હું મિત્ર સાથે, અમે બે, દરિયે જઈશ.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P35)Romaji
ふたりfutari

Why: "Two people" has its own word: ふたり (one person is ひとり). Do not build it from に + にん. From three people on, it is regular: さんにん, よにん … (人 comes in Lesson 4).

練習1 Q13

七月七日は たなばたまつりです。Shichigatsu nanoka wa tanabata matsuri desu.

EN: July 7th is the Tanabata (Star) Festival.
HI: 7 जुलाई को तानाबाता (तारों का) त्योहार होता है।
GU: 7 જુલાઈએ તાનાબાતા (તારાઓનો) તહેવાર છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P35)Romaji
しちがつなのかshichigatsu nanoka

Why: The month uses しち: the chart gives 七月 as しちがつ (never なながつ). The day uses the special reading なのか. So one sentence uses two different readings of 七.

練習1 Q14

パーティーの のみものは 十分 あります。Paatii no nomimono wa juubun arimasu.

EN: There are enough drinks for the party.
HI: पार्टी के लिए काफ़ी पेय हैं।
GU: પાર્ટી માટે પૂરતાં પીણાં છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P35)Romaji
じゅうぶんjuubun

Why: 十分 meaning "enough" is じゅうぶん. The same two kanji meaning "ten minutes" are read じゅっぷん (Lesson 3, 分 chart). The sentence (drinks, あります) tells you it is "enough".

練習1 Q15

たんじょうびは 八月八日です。Tanjoubi wa hachigatsu youka desu.

EN: My birthday is August 8th.
HI: मेरा जन्मदिन 8 अगस्त है।
GU: મારો જન્મદિવસ 8 ઑગસ્ટ છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P35)Romaji
はちがつようかhachigatsu youka

Why: 八月 = はちがつ; 八日 = the special reading ようか. Careful: ようか (8th) and よっか (4th) sound very close. The 8th has a long よう; the 4th has a small っ.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are the two reading families of each number, and the dates, which have their own readings. All pairs come from this lesson's charts and exercises.

FormMeaningKey difference
四日yokka / 八日youka the 4th / the 8th
HI: 4 तारीख / 8 तारीख · GU: 4 તારીખ / 8 તારીખ		 The classic trap. よっか (small っ) = 4th; ようか (long vowel) = 8th.
一日tsuitachi / 一日ichinichi the 1st of the month / one day
HI: पहली तारीख / एक दिन · GU: પહેલી તારીખ / એક દિવસ		 Same kanji, two words. A date (一月一日) is ついたち; a length of time is いちにち.
二日futsuka / 二人futari the 2nd / two people
HI: 2 तारीख / दो लोग · GU: 2 તારીખ / બે લોકો		 Both start with ふ- (from ふたつ), not に-.
四月shigatsu / 七月shichigatsu / 九月kugatsu April / July / September
HI: अप्रैल / जुलाई / सितंबर · GU: એપ્રિલ / જુલાઈ / સપ્ટેમ્બર		 The three months with a "surprise" reading: し (not よん), しち (not なな), く (not きゅう).
四yon / 四つyottsu four / four things
HI: चार / चार चीज़ें · GU: ચાર / ચાર વસ્તુ		 The chart gives よん and よ as other kun readings of 四, besides よっ-つ and the on reading シ.
十too / juu / 十日tooka ten / the 10th
HI: दस / 10 तारीख · GU: દસ / 10 તારીખ		 とお (ten things, and the 10th: とおか) vs じゅう (the number; 十月 じゅうがつ). There is no とおつ.
十分juubun / 10分juppun enough / ten minutes
HI: काफ़ी / दस मिनट · GU: પૂરતું / દસ મિનિટ		 Same kanji. じゅうぶん = enough (な-adjective); じゅっぷん = 10 minutes (Lesson 3 chart).
六本木Roppongi / 八百屋yaoya Roppongi / greengrocer
HI: रोप्पोंगी / सब्ज़ी की दुकान · GU: રોપ્પોંગી / શાકભાજીની દુકાન		 Sound changes: ろく → ろっ before ぽ (ロッ- in the chart). 八百屋 is a special reading (やお).
