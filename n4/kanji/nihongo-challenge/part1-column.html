もんだい1 (p.99) — find the katakana inside the kanji

もんだい1 Q1–5

かんじの なかの カタカナを さがしましょう。Kanji no naka no katakana o sagashimashou.

EN: Find the katakana hidden inside the kanji. (Each cloud of kanji hides one katakana that all of them share.)
HI: कांजी के अंदर छिपा काताकाना ढूँढिए। (हर बादल के सभी कांजी में एक ही काताकाना छिपा है।)
GU: કાંજીની અંદર છુપાયેલો કાતાકાના શોધો. (દરેક વાદળના બધા કાંજીમાં એક જ કાતાકાના છુપાયેલો છે.)

Book's example (れい): a cloud with 北 and 花 → both contain ヒ hi (北 has ヒ on its right side; 花 has ヒ in 化). The answer page shows each cloud again with the hidden katakana printed darker.

Why: Katakana were made from parts of kanji, so many kanji still contain a katakana shape. Spotting エ, ニ, ナ, タ, ハ inside a kanji helps you remember and write it: 左 = ナ + エ, 右 = ナ + 口, 外 = タ + ト. (The history note is added, not in book.)

もんだい2 (p.99) — write the kanji on the line

もんだい2 Q1–4

せんの うえに かんじを かきましょう。Sen no ue ni kanji o kakimashou.

EN: Write the kanji on the line.
HI: रेखा के ऊपर कांजी लिखिए।
GU: લીટી ઉપર કાંજી લખો.

Book's example (れい): 1か月は 30日か 31日です。Ikkagetsu wa sanjuunichi ka sanjuuichinichi desu. EN: A month has 30 or 31 days. HI: एक महीने में 30 या 31 दिन होते हैं। GU: એક મહિનામાં 30 કે 31 દિવસ હોય છે.

ItemSentenceBook answer (こたえ p.100)EnglishHindiGujarati
11＿は 60分です。Ichi ___ wa rokujuppun desu.時間jikanOne hour is 60 minutes.एक घंटा 60 मिनट का होता है।એક કલાક 60 મિનિટનો હોય છે.
21日は 24＿です。Ichinichi wa nijuuyo ___ desu.時間jikanA day is 24 hours.एक दिन 24 घंटे का होता है।એક દિવસ 24 કલાકનો હોય છે.
31＿は 7日です。Is ___ wa nanoka desu.週間shuukanOne week is 7 days.एक हफ़्ता 7 दिन का होता है।એક અઠવાડિયું 7 દિવસનું હોય છે.
41＿は 365日です。Ichi ___ wa sanbyaku rokujuugo nichi desu.年nenOne year is 365 days.एक साल 365 दिन का होता है।એક વર્ષ 365 દિવસનું હોય છે.

Why: 時間 = a length of hours (1時間 いちじかん, 24時間 にじゅうよじかん); 時 alone would be a point in time (4時 = 4 o'clock). 1週間 is いっしゅうかん. For a year the book writes only 年 (1年 いちねん), not 年間. Read 7日 as なのか and 24 as にじゅうよ before 時間. (Readings added, not in book.)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

(added, not in book) The katakana shapes in this column also explain some look-alike pairs from Part 1.

FormMeaningKey difference
左hidari / 右migi left / right
HI: बायाँ / दायाँ · GU: ડાબું / જમણું		 Both start with ナ. 左 = ナ + エ; 右 = ナ + 口.
名na / 外soto / 多ooi name / outside / many
HI: नाम / बाहर / बहुत · GU: નામ / બહાર / ઘણું		 All contain タ (夕). 名 = タ + 口; 外 = タ + ト; 多 = タ + タ.
時ji / 時間jikan o'clock / hours
HI: बजे / घंटे · GU: વાગ્યે / કલાક		 4時 = at 4 o'clock; 4時間 = for four hours.
二ni / ニni two (kanji) / katakana ni
HI: दो / काताकाना नि · GU: બે / કાતાકાના નિ		 Same shape and same sound. 八 and ハ are the same pair.
Most testable point: the length-of-time words from もんだい2: 〜時間 (hours), 〜週間 (weeks), 〜年 (years). 1週間 is read いっしゅうかん.
