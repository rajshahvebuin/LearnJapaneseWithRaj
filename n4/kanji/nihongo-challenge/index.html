日本語チャレンジ N4・N5 漢字

Following 「日本語能力試験」対策 にほんごチャレンジ N4・N5 かんじ (Nihongo Challenge N4–N5 Kanji, アスク出版) lesson by lesson. The book teaches 310 kanji, 10 per lesson: Part 1 has the 110 N5 kanji (Lessons 1–11) and Part 2 has the 200 N4 kanji (Lessons 1–20). Each lesson shows how every kanji was made from a picture, its readings and its key words, then two practice sets (reading and writing). After every few lessons there is a JLPT-style test practice (じっせんれんしゅう). Every page gives the readings, words and every question with the book's answer, explained in English, Hindi and Gujarati with romaji.

How to use this book

  • Study the lessons in order. For each kanji, learn the readings and the must-learn words first (the book shades them: N5 words in Part 1, N4 words in Part 2).
  • Do 練習1 (write the reading) and 練習2 (write the kanji) before you look at the answers. The book says writing kanji helps you remember them, even though the JLPT does not ask you to write.
  • Take each じっせんれんしゅう (test practice) after its lessons. It uses the same question types as the JLPT: choose the reading, choose the kanji.
  • If you already know the N5 kanji, start at Part 2.
