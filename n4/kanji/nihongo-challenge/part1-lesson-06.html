Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. Print slip: in 練習2 (p.62) the item number "15" is printed twice; the second "15" (せんせい／なまえ) is item 16, as the key confirms.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
外国11 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (国 → Lesson 11).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen marks okurigana (うし-ろ) or a sound-changed form (-ザイ, ホッ-).
The lesson covers the four compass points (東 西 南 北), four positions (前 後 左 右), 外 "outside", and 名 "name".

練習1 Q11

先生が 学生の 名前を よびました。Sensei ga gakusei no namae o yobimashita.

EN: The teacher called the students' names.
HI: शिक्षक ने छात्रों के नाम पुकारे।
GU: શિક્ષકે વિદ્યાર્થીઓનાં નામ બોલાવ્યાં.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P61)Romaji
せんせい・なまえsensei · namae

Why: 先生 (Lesson 5) = せんせい. 名前 = な + まえ: both kun readings. 前 here is the まえ of "front".

練習1 Q12

三年後 外国へ いきたいです。Sannen go gaikoku e ikitai desu.

EN: I want to go abroad in three years.
HI: मैं तीन साल बाद विदेश जाना चाहता हूँ।
GU: હું ત્રણ વર્ષ પછી વિદેશ જવા માગું છું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P61)Romaji
さんねんご・がい（こく）sannen go · gai(koku)

Why: After a length of time, 後 = ご "… later" (三年後 "three years later"). 国 has furigana こく; 外 in a compound is the on reading がい.

練習1 Q13

二年前 東南アジアへ いきました。Ninen mae tounan Ajia e ikimashita.

EN: Two years ago I went to Southeast Asia.
HI: दो साल पहले मैं दक्षिण-पूर्व एशिया गया था।
GU: બે વર્ષ પહેલાં હું દક્ષિણ-પૂર્વ એશિયા ગયો હતો.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P61)Romaji
とうなんtounan

Why: Only 東南 is underlined. 東 = とう, 南 = なん (on readings). Again the order is the reverse of English: 東南 "east-south" = Southeast.

練習1 Q14

スーパーの 前で 友だちに あいました。Suupaa no mae de tomodachi ni aimashita.

EN: I met a friend in front of the supermarket.
HI: सुपरमार्केट के सामने मैं एक दोस्त से मिला।
GU: સુપરમાર્કેટની સામે હું એક મિત્રને મળ્યો.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P61)Romaji
まえ・とも（だち）mae · tomo(dachi)

Why: 〜の 前で "in front of …": 前 alone = まえ. 友 = とも (Lesson 5); だち is printed in kana.

練習1 Q15

左右を よくみて みちを わたります。Sayuu o yoku mite michi o watarimasu.

EN: I look carefully left and right and cross the road.
HI: मैं दाएँ-बाएँ ध्यान से देखकर सड़क पार करता हूँ।
GU: હું ડાબે-જમણે ધ્યાનથી જોઈને રસ્તો ઓળંગું છું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P61)Romaji
さゆうsayuu

Why: 左 = さ, 右 = ゆう (on readings). Japanese says "left-right" (左右), the same order as English "left and right".

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are look-alike pairs (左／右, 名／外), kun vs on readings of directions, and the reversed word order of compass pairs. All pairs come from this lesson's charts and exercises unless marked.

FormMeaningKey difference
左hidari / 右migi left / right
HI: बायाँ / दायाँ · GU: ડાબું / જમણું		 Bottom part: 工 (tool) = left; 口 (mouth) = right. Stroke order also differs: 左 starts with 一, 右 with ノ (chart).
後ato / 後ろushiro / 三年後sannen go after / behind / three years later
HI: बाद में / पीछे / तीन साल बाद · GU: પછી / પાછળ / ત્રણ વર્ષ પછી		 あと (time), うしろ (place, with ろ), ご after a time length. Okurigana ろ tells you うしろ.
前mae / 三日前mikka mae / 前半zenhan front / three days ago / first half
HI: आगे / तीन दिन पहले / पहला आधा · GU: આગળ / ત્રણ દિવસ પહેલાં / પહેલો અડધો		 まえ for place and "ago"; ぜん in compounds (前後, 前半).

HI: नाम / बाहर / शाम · GU: નામ / બહાર / સાંજ		 Both start with the same 夕 shape. The book explains 名 as moon (月) + 口 and 外 as 夕 (evening) + 卜. Look at the second part: 口 = 名, 卜 = 外.

Compass pairs: Japanese order (houi) (added, not in book)

Japanese puts 東／西 first in a pair, and 南 before 北. Hindi and Gujarati also say उत्तर-दक्षिण／ઉત્તર-દક્ષિણ, so be careful: the Japanese order is the reverse.
