Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. In the p.71 opener the book draws two example lines itself (3 新 → A あたらしい and 6 多 → G おおい), so the key lists the other eight items.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
新聞10 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (聞 → Lesson 10, 会・社 → Lesson 11).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi). A hyphen (おお-きい) separates the kanji's part from the okurigana (the kana written after it).
* = a special reading: 大人 *おとな.
Nine of the ten kanji are い-adjectives here (大きい, 小さい, 高い …); the kanji covers only the stem, the rest is written in kana.

練習1 Q11

あには 高校生です。Ani wa koukousei desu.

EN: My older brother is a high school student.
HI: मेरा बड़ा भाई हाई स्कूल का छात्र है।
GU: મારો મોટો ભાઈ હાઈસ્કૂલનો વિદ્યાર્થી છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P75)Romaji
こうこうせいkoukousei

Why: 高 = こう (on reading), 校 = こう (Lesson 5), 生 = せい. Two long こう in a row: write both う.

練習1 Q12

少し おまちください。Sukoshi omachi kudasai.

EN: Please wait a moment.
HI: कृपया थोड़ा इंतज़ार कीजिए।

すこ（し）suko(shi)

Why: 少 has two kun readings: すく-ない and すこ-し. The okurigana し tells you it is すこし.

練習1 Q13

これから 社長の おはなしが あります。Kore kara shachou no ohanashi ga arimasu.

EN: Now the company president will speak.
HI: अब कंपनी के अध्यक्ष का भाषण होगा।
GU: હવે કંપનીના પ્રમુખનું ભાષણ થશે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P75)Romaji
しゃちょうshachou

Why: The book prints しゃ above 社 (Lesson 11), so you only need 長 = ちょう "chief". ちょう has a small ょ and a long う.

練習1 Q14

安くて 大きい テレビを かいました。Yasukute ookii terebi o kaimashita.

EN: I bought a cheap, big TV.
HI: मैंने सस्ता और बड़ा टीवी ख़रीदा।
GU: મેં સસ્તું અને મોટું ટીવી ખરીદ્યું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P75)Romaji
やす（く）・おお（きい）yasu(ku) · oo(kii)

Why: 安くて is the て-form of 安い (い → くて); the kanji part stays やす. 大きい = おお + きい.

練習1 Q15

新年おめでとうございます。Shinnen omedetou gozaimasu.

EN: Happy New Year.
HI: नया साल मुबारक हो।
GU: નૂતન વર્ષાભિનંદન.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P75)Romaji
しんねんshinnen

Why: 新 = しん (on reading), 年 = ねん (Lesson 3). The two ん sounds make しんねん; do not drop one (not しねん).

9□さい（ちい）chiisai小（さい）chiisaismallछोटाનાનું
10□きい（おお）ookii大（きい）ookiibigबड़ाમોટું

Why: Items 8 and 10 both have おお above the box. The okurigana decides: おおい (many) = 多, おおきい (big) = 大. Items 6 and 9: すくない = 少 (few), ちいさい = 小 (small).

練習2 Q11

□□（にほん）ごが □（すこ）し わかります。[Nihon]go ga [suko]shi wakarimasu.

EN: I understand Japanese a little. (book's translation)
HI: मुझे थोड़ी जापानी आती है।
GU: મને થોડી જાપાની આવડે છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P76)Romaji
日本（ご）・少（し）Nihon(go) · suko(shi)

Why: 日本 is from Lessons 1 and 5; ご (語) is printed in kana because 語 comes in Lesson 10. すこし = 少し, the same kanji as すくない.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are same sound, different kanji (おお 大／多), look-alike kanji (小／少) and adjectives with two meanings (高い, 安).

FormMeaningKey difference
大きいookii / 多いooi big / many
HI: बड़ा / बहुत · GU: મોટું / ઘણું		 Both begin おお. 大きい = size; 多い = number/amount. The okurigana (きい / い) shows which.
小さいchiisai / 少ないsukunai small / few
HI: छोटा / कम · GU: નાનું / ઓછું		 少 = 小 + one slanting stroke. 小 = size; 少 = amount. Both are read しょう in words (小学校, 少年).
少ないsukunai / 少しsukoshi few / a little
HI: कम / थोड़ा · GU: ઓછું / થોડું		 One kanji, two kun readings: すく-ない, すこ-し.
高いtakai / 安いyasui expensive (also high) / cheap
HI: महँगा (ऊँचा) / सस्ता · GU: મોંઘું (ઊંચું) / સસ્તું		 The opposite of 高い "high" is 低い ひくい (Part 2). The opposite of 高い "expensive" is 安い. (added, not in book)
古いfurui / 新しいatarashii old / new
HI: पुराना / नया · GU: જૂનું / નવું		 古い is for things, not for people's age; an old person is 年をとった人. (added, not in book)
高いtakai / 円高endaka high / strong yen
HI: ऊँचा / येन का मज़बूत होना · GU: ઊંચું / યેનનું મજબૂત થવું		 たか becomes だか after another kanji.
大学daigaku / 大会taikai / 大人otona university / tournament / adult
HI: विश्वविद्यालय / प्रतियोगिता / वयस्क · GU: યુનિવર્સિટી / સ્પર્ધા / પુખ્ત		 大 has two on readings, ダイ and タイ, and the special word 大人 おとな.
早いhayai / 速いhayai early / fast
HI: जल्दी / तेज़ · GU: વહેલું / ઝડપી		 (added, not in book) Same sound はやい. 早い is about time (early); 速い (not in this book) is about speed.
Most testable point: おお has two kanji. 大きい (big, with きい) and 多い (many, with only い). In JLPT 表記 questions the trap is 大い or 多きい; check the okurigana first.
