Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. In the p.65 opener the book draws two example lines itself (3 魚 → A さかな and 10 午 → I ご), so the key lists only the other eight items.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
天気7 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (気 → Lesson 7, 大 → Lesson 8, 国・電 → Lesson 11).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings.
All ten kanji in this lesson are pictographs (象形文字 shoukei moji): animals, weather and things people made, each drawn and then simplified.

練習1 Q11

日本人は 魚が すきです。Nihonjin wa sakana ga suki desu.

EN: Japanese people like fish.
HI: जापानी लोगों को मछली पसंद है।
GU: જાપાની લોકોને માછલી ગમે છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P69)Romaji
にほんじん・さかなnihonjin · sakana

Why: 日本 = にほん (Lesson 1, Lesson 5) and 人 for a nationality is じん (Lesson 4). 魚 alone is さかな; ぎょ appears only inside words like 金魚.

練習1 Q12

六月は たくさん 雨が ふります。Rokugatsu wa takusan ame ga furimasu.

EN: It rains a lot in June.
HI: जून में बहुत बारिश होती है।
GU: જૂનમાં બહુ વરસાદ પડે છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P69)Romaji
ろくがつ・あめrokugatsu · ame

Why: Month names use がつ (六月 ろくがつ, not ろくげつ). 雨 alone is あめ. In Japan June is the rainy season (つゆ), which is why the sentence says "a lot".

練習1 Q13

天気が いいですから、こうえんを さんぽします。Tenki ga ii desu kara, kouen o sanpo shimasu.

EN: The weather is nice, so I will take a walk in the park.
HI: मौसम अच्छा है, इसलिए मैं पार्क में टहलूँगा।
GU: હવામાન સારું છે, એટલે હું બગીચામાં ફરવા જઈશ.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P69)Romaji
てんきtenki

Why: 天 = てん, 気 = き: てんき. Do not lengthen it to てんきい or てえんき; both kanji are short.

練習1 Q14

門の 前で あいましょう。Mon no mae de aimashou.

EN: Let's meet in front of the gate.
HI: चलिए फाटक के सामने मिलते हैं।
GU: ચાલો દરવાજાની સામે મળીએ.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P69)Romaji
もん・まえmon · mae

Why: 門 = もん. 前 (Lesson 6) alone is the kun reading まえ "in front"; inside 午前 it becomes ぜん.

練習1 Q15

学校は 午前9じから 午後3じまでです。Gakkou wa gozen kuji kara gogo sanji made desu.

EN: School is from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.
HI: स्कूल सुबह 9 बजे से दोपहर 3 बजे तक है।
GU: શાળા સવારે 9 વાગ્યાથી બપોરે 3 વાગ્યા સુધી છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P69)Romaji
ごぜん・ごごgozen · gogo

Why: 午前 = ご + ぜん (before noon), 午後 = ご + ご (after noon). Note that Japanese puts 午前／午後 before the time: 午前9じ "9 a.m.".

Why: 気をつける (a must-learn word in the 気 chart) = "be careful". The き here is the 気 of "spirit, attention", not 木 (tree), which has the same sound.

□□（まいにち） □（ぎゅう）にゅうを のみます。[Mainichi] [gyuu]nyuu o nomimasu.

EN: I drink milk every day. (book's translation)
HI: मैं हर दिन दूध पीता हूँ।
GU: હું દરરોજ દૂધ પીઉં છું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P70)Romaji
毎日・牛（にゅう）mainichi · gyuu(nyuu)

Why: 毎日 is from Lesson 5 (毎 = まい). Only the ぎゅう of 牛乳 has a box, so the key writes 牛 with （にゅう） in brackets: にゅう is printed in kana because 乳 is not taught in Part 1.
