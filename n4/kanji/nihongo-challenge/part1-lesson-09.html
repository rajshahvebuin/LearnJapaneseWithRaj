Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. In the p.77 opener the book draws two example lines itself (2 来 → A きます and 6 食 → G たべます).
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
来週10 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (週・読・語 → Lesson 10, 国・店 → Lesson 11).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi). A hyphen (た-べる) separates the kanji's part from the okurigana. シュッ-, リッ- = the on reading before a small っ.
All ten kanji are basic verbs. The book's practice uses the polite ます form (行きます, 来ます …), so learn the okurigana with each kanji.

練習1 Q11

大学に 入学しました。Daigaku ni nyuugaku shimashita.

EN: I entered university.
HI: मैंने विश्वविद्यालय में दाख़िला लिया।
GU: મેં યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ લીધો.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P81)Romaji
だいがく・にゅうがくdaigaku · nyuugaku

Why: 入 inside a kanji word uses the on reading にゅう (small ゅ, long う). 学 = がく in both words.

練習1 Q12

来月 レポートを 出します。Raigetsu repooto o dashimasu.

EN: I will hand in the report next month.
HI: मैं अगले महीने रिपोर्ट जमा करूँगा।
GU: હું આવતા મહિને રિપોર્ટ જમા કરીશ.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P81)Romaji
らいげつ・だ（します）raigetsu · da(shimasu)

Why: 来月 = らい + げつ (like 来年, 来週). 出します is 出す "take out / hand in", read だ, not で: the okurigana し shows it is だす, not でる.

練習1 Q13

新しいタワーを 見学しました。Atarashii tawaa o kengaku shimashita.

EN: I toured the new tower.
HI: मैंने नई मीनार (टावर) देखने का दौरा किया।
GU: મેં નવા ટાવરની મુલાકાત લીધી.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P81)Romaji
あたら（しい）・けんがくatara(shii) · kengaku

Why: 新しい is from Lesson 8. In 見学, 見 uses its on reading けん, not み.

練習1 Q14

かばんに 本を 入れます。Kaban ni hon o iremasu.

EN: I put the book in the bag.
HI: मैं किताब बैग में रखता हूँ।
GU: હું પુસ્તક બેગમાં મૂકું છું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P81)Romaji
ほん・い（れます）hon · i(remasu)

Why: 入 has two kun readings: はい-る (enter) and い-れる (put in). The okurigana れます means 入れます = いれます, not はいれます.

練習1 Q15

出口が わかりません。行き方を おしえてください。Deguchi ga wakarimasen. Ikikata o oshiete kudasai.

EN: I can't find the exit. Please tell me how to get there.
HI: मुझे निकास द्वार नहीं मिल रहा। कृपया वहाँ जाने का रास्ता बताइए।
GU: મને બહાર નીકળવાનો દરવાજો નથી મળતો. કૃપા કરી ત્યાં જવાનો રસ્તો બતાવો.

練習2 Q11

□□（でぐち）に □（た）っています。[Deguchi] ni [ta]tte imasu.

EN: I'm standing at the exit. (book's translation)
HI: मैं निकास द्वार पर खड़ा हूँ।
GU: હું બહાર નીકળવાના દરવાજે ઊભો છું.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P82)Romaji
出口・立（っています）deguchi · ta(tte imasu)

Why: 立っています is the ている form of 立つ (つ → って). 出口 is the opposite of 入口.

練習2 Q12

ごはんを □（た）べて、コーヒーを □（の）みました。Gohan o [ta]bete, koohii o [no]mimashita.

EN: I ate supper and drank a cup of coffee. (book's translation)
HI: मैंने खाना खाया और कॉफ़ी पी।
GU: મેં જમી લીધું અને કૉફી પીધી.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P82)Romaji
食（べて）・飲（みました）ta(bete) · no(mimashita)

Why: Eat = 食, drink = 飲. Both have the same left part (the food bowl); only 飲 has 欠 (open mouth) on the right.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Verb kanji have several kun readings (入 はい／い, 出 で／だ, 来 く／こ／き), and the okurigana tells you which one.

FormMeaningKey difference
入るhairu / 入れるireru to enter / to put in
HI: अंदर जाना / अंदर डालना · GU: અંદર જવું / અંદર મૂકવું		 はい-る (you go in) vs. い-れる (you put something in). 入口 is いりぐち; 入学 is にゅうがく.
出るderu / 出すdasu to go out / to take out, hand in
HI: बाहर निकलना / बाहर निकालना, जमा करना · GU: બહાર નીકળવું / બહાર કાઢવું, જમા કરવું		 で-ます vs. だ-します. 出席 uses しゅっ.
来るkuru / 来ないkonai / 来ますkimasu come / not come / come (polite)
HI: आना / नहीं आना / आता हूँ · GU: આવવું / ન આવવું / આવું છું		 One kanji, three kun readings by verb form. 来年, 来月, 来週 = らい.
行きますikimasu / 言いますiimasu go / say
HI: जाना / कहना · GU: જવું / કહેવું		 Both start with い. Check the next kana: き → 行, い → 言.
食べるtaberu / 飲むnomu eat / drink
HI: खाना / पीना · GU: ખાવું / પીવું		 飲 = 食 (as a left side) + 欠. (added, not in book) Medicine is "drunk" in Japanese: くすりを飲む.
入iru / 人hito enter / person (Lesson 4)
HI: प्रवेश / आदमी · GU: પ્રવેશ / માણસ		 (added, not in book) Mirror images. In 入 the right stroke is on top; in 人 the left stroke is on top.
見学kengaku / 見るmiru study visit / see
HI: अध्ययन भ्रमण / देखना · GU: અભ્યાસ મુલાકાત / જોવું		 On reading けん in words (見学, 意見); kun reading み in verbs (見る, 見せる).
Most testable point: 来 changes its reading with the verb form: くる, こない, きます, and らい in time words (来年, 来月, 来週). JLPT 漢字読み options love くます and こます as traps.
