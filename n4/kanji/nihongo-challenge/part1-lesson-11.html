Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how its parts combine, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. In the p.91 opener the book draws item 1 (会 → c パーティー) as the example.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first. Every kanji in this lesson's words is taught in Part 1 or has furigana, so there are no superscript lesson numbers here.
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen in -ジャ, -ゴク, -ぞら means the sound changes inside a word.
* = a special reading: 今日 *きょう.

練習1 Q11

山の 空気は おいしいです。Yama no kuuki wa oishii desu.

EN: The mountain air is fresh (literally "delicious").
HI: पहाड़ की हवा ताज़ा (शाब्दिक: स्वादिष्ट) है।
GU: પર્વતની હવા તાજી (શાબ્દિક: સ્વાદિષ્ટ) છે.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P95)Romaji
やま・くうきyama · kuuki

Why: 空 alone is そら (sky), but in 空気 it is the on reading くう (empty): くうき "air". 気 = き (Lesson 7). おいしい with air is a set Japanese expression for clean, fresh air.

練習1 Q12

今年は さむいです。Kotoshi wa samui desu.

EN: It is cold this year.
HI: इस साल ठंड है।
GU: આ વર્ષે ઠંડી છે.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P95)Romaji
ことしkotoshi

Why: 今年 is a special reading: ことし, not こんねん. Compare 今月 こんげつ (regular) and 今日 きょう (special).

練習1 Q13

国の りょうしんに 電話しました。Kuni no ryoushin ni denwa shimashita.

EN: I phoned my parents back home.
HI: मैंने अपने देश में माता-पिता को फ़ोन किया।
GU: મેં મારા દેશમાં માતા-પિતાને ફોન કર્યો.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P95)Romaji
くに・でんわkuni · denwa

Why: 国の りょうしん = "my parents in my home country": 国 alone is くに. 電話 = でん + わ (話, Lesson 10).

練習1 Q14

駅の 前に すてきな 店が あります。Eki no mae ni suteki na mise ga arimasu.

EN: There is a nice shop in front of the station.
HI: स्टेशन के सामने एक बढ़िया दुकान है।
GU: સ્ટેશનની સામે એક સરસ દુકાન છે.
Book answer (解答 p.261, P95)Romaji
えき・まえ・みせeki · mae · mise

Why: All three stand alone, so 駅 = えき, 前 = まえ, 店 = みせ (not てん, which is used in 店員, 売店).

練習1 Q15

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This lesson has the most special readings in Part 1 (今日, 今年) and several kanji whose reading changes inside a word (空 そら → くう, 社 しゃ → じゃ, 国 こく → ごく).

FormMeaningKey difference
今月kongetsu / 今年kotoshi / 今日kyou this month / this year / today
HI: इस महीने / इस साल / आज · GU: આ મહિને / આ વર્ષે / આજે		 Only 今月 (and 今週 こんしゅう) are regular. 今年 and 今日 must be learned as whole words.

HI: अभी / मिलना · GU: હમણાં / મળવું		 Same top (人 roof). 今 = 4 strokes; 会 = 6 strokes with 云 under the roof.
