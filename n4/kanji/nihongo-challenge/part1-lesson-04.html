Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. The opener's worked examples (2→C and 1 = c) are not in the key.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
日本5人 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (本 → Lesson 5).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen marks okurigana (た-りる) or a sound-changed form (-ぐち).
* = a special reading the book marks with an asterisk (上手 *じょうず, 下手 *へた). This lesson is about people and the body: eight pictographs, plus 男 (田 + 力), a kanji built from two others.

練習1 Q11

わたしは 一人で にほんへ きました。Watashi wa hitori de Nihon e kimashita.

EN: I came to Japan alone.
HI: मैं अकेला जापान आया।
GU: હું એકલો જાપાન આવ્યો.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P49)Romaji
ひとりhitori

Why: 一人 = ひとり "one person"; 一人で = "alone, by oneself". Never いちにん.

練習1 Q12

ジョンさんは テニスが 上手です。Jon-san wa tenisu ga jouzu desu.

EN: John is good at tennis.
HI: जॉन टेनिस में माहिर है।
GU: જૉન ટેનિસમાં હોશિયાર છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P49)Romaji
じょうずjouzu

Why: 上手 is a special reading (* in the chart): じょうず, not じょうしゅ or うえて. 〜が 上手です = "is good at …".

練習1 Q13

えきの 入口は どこですか。Eki no iriguchi wa doko desu ka.

EN: Where is the entrance of the station?
HI: स्टेशन का प्रवेश द्वार कहाँ है?
GU: સ્ટેશનનું પ્રવેશદ્વાર ક્યાં છે?
Book answer (解答 p.259, P49)Romaji
（いり）ぐち(iri)guchi

Why: 入 has furigana いり (Lesson 9). 口 after another word becomes ぐち (the -ぐち in the chart): いりぐち, ひじょうぐち.

練習1 Q14

こうえんで 男の子が 三人 あそんでいます。Kouen de otoko no ko ga sannin asonde imasu.

EN: Three boys are playing in the park.
HI: पार्क में तीन लड़के खेल रहे हैं।
GU: બગીચામાં ત્રણ છોકરાઓ રમી રહ્યા છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P49)Romaji
おとこ（の）こ・さんにんotoko (no) ko · sannin

Why: 男 = おとこ, 子 = こ; の is printed. From three people on, 人 is counted with にん: さんにん. Compare ひとり, ふたり (Q11, 練習2 Q12).

練習1 Q15

一日目は ディズニーランドへ いきます。Ichinichime wa Dizuniirando e ikimasu.

EN: On the first day we will go to Disneyland.
HI: पहले दिन हम डिज़्नीलैंड जाएँगे।
GU: પહેલા દિવસે અમે ડિઝનીલૅન્ડ જઈશું.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P49)Romaji
いちにちめichinichime

Why: 〜目 after a number means "the …th" (order), read め: 一日目 = the first day (of a trip). 一日 here is いちにち (a day counted), not ついたち (the 1st of the month).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are counting people (ひとり, ふたり, さんにん), special readings (上手, 下手), the sound change くち → ぐち, and some look-alike body kanji. All pairs come from this lesson's charts and exercises unless marked.

FormMeaningKey difference
一人hitori / 二人futari / 三人sannin 1 / 2 / 3 people
HI: एक / दो / तीन लोग · GU: એક / બે / ત્રણ લોકો		 Only 1 and 2 are special (〜り). From 3 on: number + にん.
人hito / 三人sannin / 日本人nihonjin person / three people / Japanese
HI: व्यक्ति / तीन लोग / जापानी · GU: વ્યક્તિ / ત્રણ લોકો / જાપાની		 ひと alone; にん for counting; じん after a country. 人口 じんこう also uses じん.
上手jouzu / 下手heta good at / bad at
HI: माहिर / अनाड़ी · GU: કુશળ / અણઘડ		 Both are special readings (*). The pattern is 〜が 上手／下手です.
口kuchi / 入口iriguchi / 人口jinkou mouth / entrance / population
HI: मुँह / प्रवेश द्वार / जनसंख्या · GU: મોં / પ્રવેશદ્વાર / વસ્તી		 くち alone, ぐち after a word, こう in Chinese-style compounds.

女子joshi / 男女danjo girl, female / man and woman
HI: लड़की / पुरुष और महिला · GU: છોકરી / પુરુષ અને સ્ત્રી		 女 = じょ and 男 = だん in compounds. Short じょ, not じょう.

HI: सूरज / आँख / कान · GU: સૂરજ / આંખ / કાન		 日 has one line inside, 目 two, 耳 has two plus strokes sticking out at the bottom and right.
人 / 入hito / iru person / to enter (Lesson 9)
HI: व्यक्ति / अंदर जाना · GU: વ્યક્તિ / અંદર જવું		 (added, not in book) Mirror images. In 人 the left stroke is on top; in 入 the right stroke is on top.
力 / 刀chikara / katana power / sword
HI: ताक़त / तलवार · GU: તાકાત / તલવાર		 (added, not in book) In 力 the slanting stroke pokes out above the top line; in 刀 it starts below it.
Most testable point: counting people. 一人 = ひとり, 二人 = ふたり, 三人 = さんにん (and 四人 = よにん, added, not in book). JLPT 漢字読み questions offer いちにん, にじん, さんじん as traps. Second place: the special readings 上手 じょうず and 下手 へた.
