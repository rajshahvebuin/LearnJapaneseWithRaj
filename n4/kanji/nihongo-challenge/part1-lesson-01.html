Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 2–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. Stroke order is in the book's charts: practise writing from your own copy.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
日本5 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (本 → Lesson 5).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen (-ガツ) means the reading is used inside words.
All ten kanji in this lesson are pictographs (象形文字 shoukei moji): each one started as a drawing of the thing it means.

練習1 Q11

日曜日、ともだちと ふじ山へ いきます。Nichiyoubi, tomodachi to Fujisan e ikimasu.

EN: On Sunday I am going to Mount Fuji with a friend.
HI: रविवार को मैं दोस्त के साथ फ़ूजी पर्वत जाऊँगा।
GU: રવિવારે હું મિત્ર સાથે ફુજી પર્વત જઈશ.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P29)Romaji
にち（よう）び・（ふじ）さんnichi(you)bi · (Fuji)san

Why: One kanji, two readings in one word: the first 日 in 日曜日 is にち (on reading), the last 日 is び (ひ with a voicing mark). In ふじ山 the 山 is read さん (on reading サン), not やま. Do not confuse it with the honorific 〜さん.

練習1 Q12

がっこうは 月曜日から 金曜日までです。Gakkou wa getsuyoubi kara kinyoubi made desu.

EN: School is from Monday to Friday.
HI: स्कूल सोमवार से शुक्रवार तक है।
GU: શાળા સોમવારથી શુક્રવાર સુધી છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P29)Romaji
げつ（よう）び・きん（よう）びgetsu(you)bi · kin(you)bi

Why: 月 in a day name is げつ; in a month name it is がつ (1月 いちがつ). 金 in 金曜日 is きん (gold), not かね (money). The final 日 is again び.

練習1 Q13

土曜日、山田さんの うちへ あそびに いきます。Doyoubi, Yamada-san no uchi e asobi ni ikimasu.

EN: On Saturday I am going to visit Mr./Ms. Yamada's house.
HI: शनिवार को मैं यामादा जी के घर मिलने जाऊँगा।
GU: શનિવારે હું યામાદાના ઘરે મળવા જઈશ.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P29)Romaji
ど（よう）び・やまだ（さん）do(you)bi · yamada(-san)

Why: 土 in 土曜日 is ど (not つち, and not と as in 土地). In the name 山田, 山 = やま and 田 = だ: た gets a voicing mark when it comes second in a word.

練習1 Q14

ナイル川は せかいで いちばん ながいです。Nairu-gawa wa sekai de ichiban nagai desu.

EN: The Nile is the longest river in the world.
HI: नील नदी दुनिया की सबसे लंबी नदी है।
GU: નાઇલ નદી દુનિયાની સૌથી લાંબી નદી છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P29)Romaji
がわgawa

Why: After the name of a river, 川 is read がわ, not かわ: ナイル川, インダス川 (from the 川 chart). Alone it is かわ.

練習1 Q15

水曜日、テニスを します。Suiyoubi, tenisu o shimasu.

EN: I play tennis on Wednesday.
HI: बुधवार को मैं टेनिस खेलता हूँ।
GU: બુધવારે હું ટેનિસ રમું છું.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P29)Romaji
すい（よう）びsui(you)bi

Why: 水 alone is みず, but in 水曜日 it is the on reading すい (スイ), as in 水田 すいでん.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are mostly one sound, two kanji (ひ) and one kanji, many readings (日, 月, 田, 土). All pairs come from this lesson's charts and exercises.

FormMeaningKey difference
日hi ひ — the sun (also "day")

The seven days of the week (youbi) (added, not in book)

Seven of today's kanji make the days of the week. Japanese names the days after the sun, the moon and five planets, which is the same system as Hindi and Gujarati. If you know रविवार, सोमवार, मंगलवार … you already know the order.
