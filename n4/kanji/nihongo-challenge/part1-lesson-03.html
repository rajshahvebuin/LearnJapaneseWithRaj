Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and four words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. The opener has two worked examples (1→B and 10→I are already joined in the book), so the key lists only items 2–9.
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
万国11 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (国 → Lesson 11).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen marks okurigana (あ-がる) or a sound-changed reading used inside words (-ビャク, -ピャク).
Five of these kanji are numbers or money (百 千 万 円 年); the other five are signs (記号 kigou): simple marks for "above", "below", "middle", "half" and "divide".

練習1 Q11

エレベーターで 七階へ 上がります。Erebeetaa de nanakai e agarimasu.

EN: I go up to the 7th floor by elevator.
HI: मैं लिफ़्ट से सातवीं मंज़िल पर जाता हूँ।
GU: હું લિફ્ટથી સાતમા માળે જાઉં છું.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P41)Romaji
なな・あ（がります）nana · a(garimasu)

Why: 階 (floor) has furigana かい in the book; the 7th floor is ななかい. In 上がります only あ is the kanji's part: がります is okurigana, already printed. 上 = あ-がる here, not うえ.

練習1 Q12

にほんごが すこし 分かります。Nihongo ga sukoshi wakarimasu.

EN: I understand a little Japanese.
HI: मुझे थोड़ी जापानी समझ आती है।
GU: મને થોડું જાપાની સમજાય છે.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P41)Romaji
わ（かります）wa(karimasu)

Why: 分かる = わかる "understand". The kanji covers only わ. The idea: to understand something, you "divide" it into parts.

練習1 Q13

いもうとと おかしを 半分ずつ 分けました。Imouto to okashi o hanbun zutsu wakemashita.

EN: My younger sister and I shared the sweets half each.
HI: मैंने और मेरी छोटी बहन ने मिठाई आधी-आधी बाँटी।
GU: મેં અને મારી નાની બહેને મીઠાઈ અડધી-અડધી વહેંચી.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P41)Romaji
はんぶん・わ（けました）hanbun · wa(kemashita)

Why: 分 twice, two readings: in 半分 it is the on reading ぶん; in 分けました it is the kun reading わ(ける) "divide". 分けました ≠ 分かりました: look at the okurigana (け vs か).

練習1 Q14

来年 中学に はいります。Rainen chuugaku ni hairimasu.

EN: Next year I will start junior high school.
HI: अगले साल मैं मिडिल स्कूल में दाख़िल होऊँगा।
GU: આવતા વર્ષે હું માધ્યમિક શાળામાં દાખલ થઈશ.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P41)Romaji
（らい）ねん・ちゅう（がく）(rai)nen · chuu(gaku)

Why: 来 and 学 have furigana (らい, がく) because they come in later lessons. You write the readings of 年 = ねん and 中 = ちゅう, both on readings because they are inside compounds.

練習1 Q15

きのうは 一日中 家に いました。Kinou wa ichinichijuu ie ni imashita.

EN: Yesterday I was at home all day.
HI: कल मैं पूरे दिन घर पर था।
GU: ગઈકાલે હું આખો દિવસ ઘરે હતો.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P41)Romaji
いちにちじゅうichinichijuu

Why: 一日 here is いちにち "one day" (not ついたち: this is a length of time, not a date). 〜中 after a time word means "all through" and is read じゅう (the -ジュウ in the chart), not ちゅう.

練習2 Q14

ケーキを □□（はんぶん）ずつ □（わ）けました。Keeki o hanbun zutsu wakemashita.

EN: We shared the cake half and half.
HI: हमने केक आधा-आधा बाँटा।
GU: અમે કેક અડધી-અડધી વહેંચી.
Book answer (解答 p.259, P42)Romaji
半分（ずつ）分（けました）hanbun (zutsu) wa(kemashita)

Why: The same kanji 分 fills both the ぶん box and the わ box. This mirrors 練習1 Q13: ぶん (on) inside 半分, わ-ける (kun) as a verb.

Big numbers: Japanese counts in 万 (man) (added, not in book)

English groups digits in thousands, Hindi and Gujarati use हज़ार／હજાર, लाख／લાખ and करोड़／કરોડ. Japanese makes a new unit every four digits: 万 = 10,000. So 100,000 is 十万 (ten 万), and one lakh is also 十万.

Most testable point: the sound changes after さん and ろく: 三百 = さんびゃく, 六百 = ろっぴゃく, 三千 = さんぜん. The JLPT drill for Lessons 1–3 tests exactly this with さんひゃくえん / さんびゃくえん / さっぴゃくえん. And when counting money, 10,000 yen is いちまんえん: the いち is always said (but 100 is just ひゃく and 1,000 just せん).
