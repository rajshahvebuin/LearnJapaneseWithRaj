Notes
The book gives each kanji's readings, stroke count, a picture of how it was made, and 3–4 words, with English, Korean and Portuguese. The Hindi and Gujarati, the romaji, every "Why" note and §3 are added by us. We describe the book's pictures in words; they are not reproduced. Print slip: in 練習2 (p.56) the item number "3" is printed twice; the second "3" (なに) is item 4, as the key confirms (4 何).
must-learn = a word the book shades grey. In Part 1, shaded words are N5 words; learn these first.
大8学 = the small number is the lesson where the other kanji is taught (大 → Lesson 8).
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi, from Chinese) · Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi, Japanese). ／ separates other readings; a hyphen marks okurigana (う-まれる) or a sound-changed form (-ポン, ガッ-).
* = a special reading the book marks with an asterisk (お父さん *おとうさん, お母さん *おかあさん). 學 = the old form of 学 printed in the chart.

練習1 Q11

かぞくは 何人ですか。Kazoku wa nannin desu ka.

EN: How many people are there in your family?
HI: आपके परिवार में कितने लोग हैं?
GU: તમારા પરિવારમાં કેટલા લોકો છે?
Book answer (解答 p.260, P55)Romaji
なんにんnannin

Why: 何 before a counter is なん: 何人 = なんにん "how many people". なにじん would mean "what nationality", a different question.

練習1 Q12

先月 けっこんしました。Sengetsu kekkon shimashita.

EN: I got married last month.
HI: मेरी पिछले महीने शादी हुई।
GU: મારાં ગયા મહિને લગ્ન થયાં.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P55)Romaji
せんげつsengetsu

Why: 先 = せん "previous" + 月 = げつ: せんげつ "last month". Compare 今月 こんげつ "this month" (Lesson 1 chart).

練習1 Q13

毎日 何を たべますか。Mainichi nani o tabemasu ka.

EN: What do you eat every day?
HI: आप हर दिन क्या खाते हैं?
GU: તમે દરરોજ શું ખાઓ છો?
Book answer (解答 p.260, P55)Romaji
まいにち・なにmainichi · nani

Why: 毎 = まい, 日 = にち. Before the particle を, 何 is なに (なにを), not なん.

練習1 Q14

どうぞ お先に。Douzo osaki ni.

EN: Please, go ahead (after you).
HI: कृपया, पहले आप।
GU: કૃપા કરીને, પહેલાં તમે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P55)Romaji
（お）さき（に）(o)saki(ni)

Why: 先 alone is the kun reading さき "ahead". お先に is a fixed polite phrase: "you first" / "excuse me for going first".

練習1 Q15

わたしは 1990年に 日本で 生まれました。Watashi wa sen kyuuhyaku kyuujuu nen ni Nihon de umaremashita.

EN: I was born in Japan in 1990.
HI: मेरा जन्म 1990 में जापान में हुआ।
GU: મારો જન્મ 1990માં જાપાનમાં થયો હતો.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P55)Romaji
ねん・にほん・う（まれました）nen · nihon · u(maremashita)

Why: After a year number, 年 is ねん (Lesson 3). 日本 = にほん (the chart also gives にっぽん). In 生まれました only う is the kanji; まれました is okurigana. 生 has many readings: う(まれる), い(きる), せい (先生).

練習2 Q9

□（ちち）と □（はは）は □□（にほん）に すんでいます。Chichi to haha wa Nihon ni sunde imasu.

EN: My father and mother live in Japan.
HI: मेरे पिता और माँ जापान में रहते हैं।
GU: મારા પિતા અને માતા જાપાનમાં રહે છે.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P56)Romaji
父、母・日本chichi, haha · Nihon

Why: Talking about your own parents to others, use ちち／はは (父／母), not おとうさん／おかあさん. 日本 is 日 (Lesson 1) + 本.

練習2 Q10

□□（まいにち） 8時に □□（がっこう）へ いきます。Mainichi hachiji ni gakkou e ikimasu.

EN: Every day I go to school at 8.
HI: मैं हर दिन 8 बजे स्कूल जाता हूँ।
GU: હું દરરોજ 8 વાગ્યે શાળાએ જાઉં છું.
Book answer (解答 p.260, P56)Romaji
毎日・学校mainichi · gakkou

Why: まいにち = 毎 + 日. がっこう = 学 + 校: the small っ is only a sound change, so you still write 学.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's traps are family words (ちち vs おとうさん), the two sounds of 何, and kanji with many readings (生, 先, 本). All pairs come from this lesson's charts and exercises unless marked.

FormMeaningKey difference
父chichi / お父さんotousan my father / father (polite)
HI: मेरे पिता / पिताजी · GU: મારા પિતા / પપ્પા		 Same kanji. ちち = your own father when speaking to others; おとうさん = someone else's father, or calling your own (added, not in book).
母haha / お母さんokaasan / 父母fubo my mother / mother (polite) / parents
HI: मेरी माँ / माताजी / माता-पिता · GU: મારી માતા / મમ્મી / માતા-પિતા		 はは, かあ (special), ぼ in compounds (父母, 母国, 母校).
何をnani o / 何人nannin / 何時nanji what / how many people / what time
HI: क्या / कितने लोग / कितने बजे · GU: શું / કેટલા લોકો / કેટલા વાગ્યે		 なに before を, が; なん before counters (人, 時, 月) and before です (added rule, not in book).
先saki / 先月sengetsu / 先生sensei ahead / last month / teacher
HI: आगे / पिछला महीना / शिक्षक · GU: આગળ / ગયો મહિનો / શિક્ષક		 さき alone (お先に); せん in compounds.
生まれるumareru / 生きるikiru / 学生gakusei be born / live / student
HI: जन्म लेना / जीना / छात्र · GU: જન્મ લેવો / જીવવું / વિદ્યાર્થી		 Two kun readings decided by okurigana (まれる vs きる), and the on reading せい.
学生gakusei / 学校gakkou student / school
HI: छात्र / स्कूल · GU: વિદ્યાર્થી / શાળા		 がく before せ; がっ before こ (ガッ- in the chart). Also しょうがっこう.

HI: हर साल / महीना / दिन · GU: દર વર્ષે / મહિને / રોજ		 Not regular: 年 → とし or ねん, 月 → つき (not げつ), 日 → にち.

HI: दोस्त / मित्र · GU: મિત્ર / મિત્ર (ઔપચારિક)		 とも (kun) with だち in kana; ゆう (on) in compounds.
母 / 毎haha / mai mother / every
HI: माँ / हर · GU: માતા / દરેક		 The book explains 毎 as a mother with a hairpin: it is the 母 shape with two extra strokes on top (the hairpin). No top strokes = 母.
木 / 本 / 休ki / hon / yasumu tree / book, root / rest (Lesson 10)
HI: पेड़ / किताब / आराम · GU: ઝાડ / પુસ્તક / આરામ		 本 = 木 + a short line marking the root. (added, not in book) 休 = person + tree: a person resting under a tree.
Most testable point: 何 = なに or なん. Before a counter it is always なん: 何人 なんにん, 何時 なんじ, 何月 なんがつ. Before を it is なに: 何を. JLPT 漢字読み options love なにじ, なにがつ and なんを as traps.
