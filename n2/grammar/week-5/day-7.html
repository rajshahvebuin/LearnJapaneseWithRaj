問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

この寺は、あじさいという花で有名な（　）、あじさい寺と呼ばれています。Kono tera wa, ajisai to iu hana de yuumei na (___), ajisai-dera to yobarete imasu.

Full sentence: この寺は、あじさいという花で有名なことから、あじさい寺と呼ばれています。...yuumei na koto kara...
EN: This temple is called "Ajisai-dera" (Hydrangea Temple) because it's famous for a flower called ajisai (hydrangea).
HI: यह मंदिर "अज़ीसाई" नाम के फूल के लिए मशहूर होने के कारण "अज़ीसाई-देरा" कहलाता है।
GU: આ મંદિર "અજિસાઈ" નામના ફૂલ માટે પ્રખ્યાત હોવાના કારણે "અજિસાઈ-દેરા" કહેવાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ことにはkoto ni wa(wrong pattern here)
2ことからkoto karabecause (an objective fact)
3ことだからkoto dakara(needs a person + の, not a fact clause)
4ものならmono nara(wrong pattern here)

Why: Week 5 Day 2's ことから (an objective, observable reason) explains the temple's nickname — exactly this naming-origin use.

Q2

私は背が高い（　）、どこにいても目立ってしまう。Watashi wa se ga takai (___), doko ni ite mo medatte shimau.

Full sentence: 私は背が高いばかりに、どこにいても目立ってしまう。Watashi wa se ga takai bakari ni, doko ni ite mo medatte shimau.
EN: Just because I'm tall, I stand out everywhere I go.
HI: सिर्फ़ मेरे लंबे क़द की वजह से, मैं जहाँ भी रहूँ, नज़र में आ ही जाता हूँ।
GU: ફક્ત મારી ઊંચાઈના કારણે, હું ગમે ત્યાં હોઉં, ધ્યાન ખેંચાઈ જ જાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1わりにwari ni(wrong pattern here)
2かわりにkawari ni(wrong pattern here)
3ばかりにbakari nijust because of (an unwanted side-effect)
4どころかdokoro ka(wrong pattern here)

Why: Week 5 Day 4's ばかりに blames a single trait (being tall) for an inconvenient, unavoidable side-effect (always standing out).

Q3

ご予算（　）お食事が楽しめます。Go-yosan (___) o-shokuji ga tanoshimemasu.

Full sentence: ご予算に応じてお食事が楽しめます。Go-yosan ni oujite o-shokuji ga tanoshimemasu.
EN: You can enjoy a meal that suits your budget.
HI: आप अपने बजट के अनुसार भोजन का आनंद ले सकते हैं।
GU: તમે તમારા બજેટ પ્રમાણે ભોજનનો આનંદ માણી શકો છો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1に対してni taishite(wrong pattern here)
2に際してni saishite(wrong — not a one-time occasion)
3に比べてni kurabete(wrong — a comparison, not adaptation)
4に応じてni oujiteaccording to / depending on

Why: Week 5 Day 6's に応じて scales the dining option to a variable factor — the customer's budget.

Q4

なぜあんなことをしたのか、本人に聞いてみない（　）理由はわからない。Naze anna koto o shita no ka, honnin ni kiite minai (___) riyuu wa wakaranai.

Full sentence: なぜあんなことをしたのか、本人に聞いてみないことには理由はわからない。...kiite minai koto ni wa riyuu wa wakaranai.
EN: Unless you ask the person themselves why they did that, you won't know the reason.
HI: उसने ऐसा क्यों किया, यह उस इंसान से पूछे बिना पता नहीं चलेगा।
GU: તેણે એવું કેમ કર્યું, એ તે વ્યક્તિને પૂછ્યા વગર ખબર નહીં પડે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ことにはkoto ni waunless (a necessary precondition)
2ことからkoto kara(wrong — states a fact, not a precondition)
3ことだからkoto dakara(needs a person + の)
4ものならmono nara(wrong pattern here)

Why: Week 5 Day 2's V-ないことにはV-ない necessary-precondition pattern — you must ask first, otherwise the reason stays unknown.

Q5

この寮に入る（　）、以下の規則をよく読んでおいてください。Kono ryou ni hairu (___), ika no kisoku o yoku yonde oite kudasai.

Full sentence: この寮に入るに際して、以下の規則をよく読んでおいてください。Kono ryou ni hairu ni saishite, ika no kisoku o yoku yonde oite kudasai.
EN: When entering this dormitory, please make sure to read the following rules carefully.
HI: इस छात्रावास में प्रवेश करते समय, कृपया नीचे दिए गए नियमों को ध्यान से पढ़ लें।
GU: આ છાત્રાલયમાં પ્રવેશ કરતી વખતે, કૃપા કરીને નીચેના નિયમો ધ્યાનથી વાંચી લો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1に基づいてni motozuite(wrong — not a factual basis here)
2に応じてni oujite(wrong — not a scaling factor)
3に際してni saishiteat the time of / on the occasion of
4に反してni hanshite(wrong — means "contrary to")

Why: Week 5 Day 6's に際して marks the formal occasion (moving in) at which the rules apply.

Q6

彼は自分でも認めている（　）、確かにあきっぽい。Kare wa jibun demo mitomete iru (___), tashika ni akippoi.

Full sentence: 彼は自分でも認めているだけあって、確かにあきっぽい。Kare wa jibun demo mitomete iru dake atte, tashika ni akippoi.
EN: Just as he himself admits, he really is quick to lose interest in things.
HI: वह ख़ुद भी मानता है, इसी के अनुरूप, वह वाक़ई जल्दी ऊब जाने वाला है।
GU: તે પોતે પણ સ્વીકારે છે, એ મુજબ જ, તે ખરેખર જલદી કંટાળી જનારો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1だけあってdake attejust as one would expect, given
2ばかりかbakari ka(wrong — would need a second added fact)
3ことからkoto kara(wrong — less natural fit for this validation)
4というよりto iu yori(wrong — "rather than," not tested this week)

Why: Week 5 Day 4's だけあって validates a result (being fickle) as fitting a fact already conceded (his own admission).

Q7

税金はこの表（　）計算されています。Zeikin wa kono hyou (___) keisan sarete imasu.

Full sentence: 税金はこの表に基づいて計算されています。Zeikin wa kono hyou ni motozuite keisan sarete imasu.
EN: Taxes are calculated based on this table.
HI: टैक्स की गणना इस तालिका के आधार पर की जाती है।
GU: ટેક્સની ગણતરી આ કોષ્ટકના આધારે કરવામાં આવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1につれてni tsurete(wrong — "as X progresses," not this)
2に応じてni oujite(wrong — a scaling factor, not a fixed basis)
3に比べてni kurabete(wrong — a comparison)
4に基づいてni motozuitebased on

Why: Week 5 Day 6's に基づいて — the table is the fixed factual basis tax calculations rely on.

Q8

よっぱらい運転は、運転者（　）ほかの人々をも危険にさらす。Yopparai unten wa, untensha (___) hoka no hitobito o mo kiken ni sarasu.

Full sentence: よっぱらい運転は、運転者ばかりかほかの人々をも危険にさらす。...untensha bakari ka hoka no hitobito o mo kiken ni sarasu.
EN: Drunk driving endangers not only the driver but other people as well.
HI: शराब पीकर गाड़ी चलाना न सिर्फ़ ड्राइवर, बल्कि दूसरे लोगों को भी ख़तरे में डालता है।
GU: દારૂ પીને ડ્રાઇવિંગ કરવું ફક્ત ડ્રાઇવરને જ નહીં, પણ બીજા લોકોને પણ જોખમમાં મૂકે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1だけにdake ni(wrong — explains a matching result, not "also")
2わりにwari ni(wrong pattern here)
3ばかりかbakari kanot only... but also
4というとto iu to(wrong pattern here)

Why: Week 5 Day 4's ばかりか adds a second party (other people) to the first (the driver) as also being endangered.

Q9

信用できる教育者（　）子どもを学ばせたい。Shin'you dekiru kyouikusha (___) kodomo o manabasetai.

Full sentence: 信用できる教育者の下で子どもを学ばせたい。Shin'you dekiru kyouikusha no moto de kodomo o manabasetai.
EN: I want my child to study under a trustworthy educator.
HI: मैं चाहता हूँ कि मेरा बच्चा किसी भरोसेमंद शिक्षक के मार्गदर्शन में पढ़े।
GU: હું ઈચ્છું છું કે મારું બાળક કોઈ વિશ્વસનીય શિક્ષકના માર્ગદર્શન હેઠળ ભણે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1の下でno moto deunder (guidance/supervision)
2に応じてni oujite(wrong — not a scaling factor)
3に際してni saishite(wrong — not a one-time occasion)
4に基づいてni motozuite(wrong — an educator isn't a "fact" one is based on)

Why: Week 5 Day 6's の下で — studying "under" a mentor's guidance/supervision.

Q10

彼女の考え方や行動は、普通の人からは理解（　）。Kanojo no kangaekata ya koudou wa, futsuu no hito kara wa rikai (___).

Full sentence: 彼女の考え方や行動は、普通の人からは理解しがたい。Kanojo no kangaekata ya koudou wa, futsuu no hito kara wa rikai shigatai.
EN: Her way of thinking and behavior are hard for ordinary people to understand.
HI: उसकी सोच और व्यवहार को आम इंसान के लिए समझना मुश्किल है।
GU: તેની વિચારસરણી અને વર્તન સામાન્ય માણસ માટે સમજવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1しうるshiuru(wrong — "can do," opposite sense)
2しがたいshigataihard to do
3しかねないshikanenai(wrong — "might well," not "hard to")
4しかないshikanai(wrong — "have no choice but to")

Why: Week 5 Day 1's 〜がたい — an inherent difficulty in understanding, fitting the formal register of this sentence.

Q11

黄色い花が畑一面に咲いて、まるで黄色いカーペットをしいた（　）。Kiiroi hana ga hatake ichimen ni saite, marude kiiroi kaapetto o shiita (___).

Full sentence: 黄色い花が畑一面に咲いて、まるで黄色いカーペットをしいたかのようだ。...marude kiiroi kaapetto o shiita ka no you da.
EN: Yellow flowers bloomed across the whole field — it's as if a yellow carpet had been laid out.
HI: पूरे खेत में पीले फूल खिल गए हैं, मानो पीली दरी बिछा दी गई हो।
GU: આખા ખેતરમાં પીળા ફૂલો ખીલી ગયાં છે, જાણે પીળી કાર્પેટ પાથરી દીધી હોય.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ことかkoto ka(wrong — exclamatory "how...!")
2せいだsei da(wrong — blames a cause for a bad result)
3だけあるdake aru(wrong — not the correct fixed phrase)
4かのようだka no you daas if ~

Why: Week 5 Day 5's かのようだ — a vivid simile for the field's yellow-flower carpet appearance.

Q12

明日こそ、皆でわが校サッカー部の試合を応援しに（　）。Ashita koso, minna de waga kou sakkaa-bu no shiai o ouen shi ni (___).

Full sentence: 明日こそ、皆でわが校サッカー部の試合を応援しに行こうではないか。...ouen shi ni ikou de wa nai ka.
EN: Tomorrow, of all days, let's all go cheer on our school's soccer team's match, shall we!
HI: कल तो, चलो सब मिलकर अपने स्कूल की फ़ुटबॉल टीम के मैच का उत्साहवर्धन करने चलें!
GU: કાલે તો, ચાલો બધા સાથે મળીને આપણી શાળાની ફૂટબોલ ટીમની મેચને ઉત્સાહિત કરવા જઈએ!
#JapaneseEnglish
1行くものかiku mono ka(wrong — "as if I'd go!", opposite sense)
2行こうではないかikou de wa nai kalet's go, shall we
3行くこそiku koso(wrong — not a valid form)
4行くんだっけikun dakke(wrong — "was it that we're going?")

Why: Week 5 Day 5's volitional+ではないか rallies the group into a shared action.

Q13

できない約束をしても、（　）。Dekinai yakusoku o shite mo, (___).

Full sentence: できない約束をしても、守れっこない。Dekinai yakusoku o shite mo, mamorekkonai.
EN: Even if you make a promise you can't keep, there's no way you'll be able to keep it.
HI: अगर ऐसा वादा किया जाए जो पूरा नहीं हो सकता, तो उसे निभाया ही नहीं जा सकता।
GU: જો એવું વચન અપાય જે પૂરું ન થઈ શકે, તો તે પાળી જ શકાય નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
1守れがたいmamoregatai(wrong — がたい doesn't combine with the potential form this way)
2守れっこないmamorekkonaithere's no way it can be kept
3守るわけだmamoru wake da(wrong — "it follows that one keeps it")
4守りかねないmamorikanenai(wrong — "might well keep it," opposite sense)

Why: Week 5 Day 1's っこない flatly denies any possibility — you can't keep a promise that's inherently unkeepable.

Q14

会員様の個人情報はお教え（　）。Kaiin-sama no kojin jouhou wa o-oshie (___).

Full sentence: 会員様の個人情報はお教えしかねます。Kaiin-sama no kojin jouhou wa o-oshie shikanemasu.
EN: We regret that we are unable to disclose members' personal information.
HI: सदस्यों की निजी जानकारी बताना हमारे लिए संभव नहीं है।
GU: સભ્યોની અંગત માહિતી જણાવવી અમારા માટે શક્ય નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1するわけですsuru wake desu(wrong — "it follows that we do")
2してなりませんshite narimasen(wrong — not a valid fit here)
3しかねますshikanemasuwe regret we are unable to
4しかねませんshikanemasen(wrong — not the conventional set refusal phrase)

Why: Week 5 Day 1's polite formal refusal 〜かねます — the standard business phrase, not the nonstandard かねません.

Q15

姉は3日も寝ないで机に向かっている。あれでは体を（　）。Ane wa mikka mo nenaide tsukue ni mukatte iru. Are de wa karada o (___).

Full sentence: 姉は3日も寝ないで机に向かっている。あれでは体をこわしかねない。...are de wa karada o kowashikanenai.
EN: My sister has been at her desk for three days without sleep. At this rate, she might well ruin her health.
HI: मेरी बहन तीन दिनों से बिना सोए मेज़ पर बैठी काम कर रही है। इस तरह तो उसकी सेहत बिगड़ भी सकती है।
GU: મારી બહેન ત્રણ દિવસથી ઊંઘ્યા વગર ટેબલ પર બેસીને કામ કરી રહી છે. આ રીતે તો તેની તબિયત બગડી પણ શકે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1こわしようがないkowashiyou ga nai(wrong — "no way to break it," wrong sense)
2こわしっこないkowashikkonai(wrong — "there's no way she'll ruin it," opposite sense)
3こわしかねるkowashikaneru(wrong — "unable to ruin it," wrong sense)
4こわしかねないkowashikanenaimight well ruin (her health)

Why: Week 5 Day 1's かねない — a real risk that continuing this way could damage her health.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

あの鳥は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 。Ano tori wa ___ ★ ___.

Tiles: 1 美しい (utsukushii)　2 姿も (sugata mo)　3 鳴き声 (nakigoe)　4 のみか (nomika)

3 鳴き声→4 のみか→★ 2 姿も→1 美しい

あの鳥は鳴き声のみか姿も美しい。Ano tori wa nakigoe nomika sugata mo utsukushii.

EN: That bird isn't only its chirping that's beautiful — its appearance is too.
HI: उस पक्षी की सिर्फ़ आवाज़ ही नहीं, बल्कि उसका रूप भी सुंदर है।
GU: તે પક્ષીનો ફક્ત અવાજ જ નહીં, પણ તેનું રૂપ પણ સુંદર છે.

Why: Builds on Week 5 Day 4's のみか ("not only... but also") — praising both the bird's call and its appearance.

Q17

彼はイタリアにくわしい。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ ある。Kare wa Itaria ni kuwashii. ___ ★ ___ aru.

Tiles: 1 ことは (koto wa)　2 ずっと (zutto)　3 いるだけの (iru dake no)　4 行きたがって (ikitagatte)

2 ずっと→4 行きたがって→★ 3 いるだけの→1 ことは

彼はイタリアにくわしい。ずっと行きたがっているだけのことはある。Kare wa Itaria ni kuwashii. Zutto ikitagatte iru dake no koto wa aru.

EN: He knows a lot about Italy. No wonder — he's always wanted to go there.
HI: वह इटली के बारे में काफ़ी जानता है। आख़िर, वह हमेशा से वहाँ जाना चाहता रहा है।
GU: તે ઇટાલી વિશે ઘણું જાણે છે. છેવટે, તે હંમેશાં ત્યાં જવા ઈચ્છતો રહ્યો છે.

Why: Week 5 Day 4's だけのことはある — his knowledge is validated as fitting his long-standing desire to visit.

Q18

待ち合わせの時間に遅れそうだ。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ きっと怒るだろう。Machiawase no jikan ni okuresou da. ___ ★ ___ kitto okoru darou.

Tiles: 1 うるさい (urusai)　2 ことだから (koto dakara)　3 彼の (kare no)　4 時間に (jikan ni)

4 時間に→1 うるさい→★ 3 彼の→2 ことだから

待ち合わせの時間に遅れそうだ。時間にうるさい彼のことだから、きっと怒るだろう。Machiawase no jikan ni okuresou da. Jikan ni urusai kare no koto dakara, kitto okoru darou.

EN: It looks like I'll be late for our meeting time. Since he's so strict about punctuality, he'll surely be angry.
HI: लगता है मुझे मिलने के समय पर देर हो जाएगी। वह समय का इतना पाबंद है, तो ज़रूर नाराज़ होगा।

Q20

Why: Week 5 Day 3's 〜も同然だ — the unspoken attitude is equated with actually having said it outright.

Q21

英語が苦手な（　）、せっかく行った海外旅行先で…伝えられなかった。

#JapaneseEnglish
1ことだからkoto dakara(wrong — needs a person + の)
2ばかりにbakari nijust because of (a regrettable cause)
3ことなくkoto naku(wrong — "without doing," wrong sense)
4ことにはkoto ni wa(wrong — a "unless" precondition, not a cause)

Why (per 別冊): The booklet flags that ことだから would require the sentence to end in a probable/plain form like 伝えられないでしょう rather than the actual past 伝えられなかった. Since this is one specific past regret with a single cause (poor English), Week 5 Day 4's ばかりに is the fit.

Q22

そうなると旅の楽しみも半減（　）。

#JapaneseEnglish
1しがたいshigatai(wrong — would mean "hard to halve," i.e. won't halve)
2しかねますshikanemasu(wrong — would mean "unable to halve")
3しかねませんshikanemasenmight well (happen) — a real risk
4しそうにないshisou ni nai(wrong — "doesn't look likely to halve")

Why (per 別冊): The booklet notes options 1, 2, and 4 all effectively mean "won't halve" — the opposite of what the passage needs. しかねません here functions as the polite/formal cousin of Week 5 Day 1's かねない ("might well happen," not "cannot"), so it correctly warns that enjoyment really could be cut in half.

Q23

…すぐには（　）でしょう。

#JapaneseEnglish
1信じて当然shinjite touzen(wrong — "naturally believe," opposite sense)
2信じかねないshinjikanenai(wrong — "might well believe," opposite sense)
3信じがたいshinjigataihard to believe
4信じるだけあるshinjiru dake aru(wrong — not a valid set phrase)

Why (per 別冊): The booklet notes option 2 would only work as 信じかねる (present inability), not 信じかねない (a future-risk form) — since the context is "can't quite believe it right away," Week 5 Day 1's 信じがたい is the natural fit.

Q24
