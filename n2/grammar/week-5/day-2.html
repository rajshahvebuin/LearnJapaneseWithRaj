2日目 — あの人のことだから (Ano hito no koto da kara)

Grammar points covered
富士山が見えることから彼のことだから休むことなくやってみないことにはわからない
Answer key

Notes
None of today's four forms carry a 硬 badge in the source — all are neutral register. The real trap today is surface similarity: ことから vs ことだから, and ことなく vs 〜ないことには, sound alike but require opposite polarity/attachment — see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ことから

neutral register
Reading~koto kara
Meaning (EN)"because / given that ~" — states an objective, observable fact as the reason for a result
Meaning (HI)...होने के कारण / चूँकि ...है, इसलिए
Meaning (GU)...હોવાના કારણે / કારણ કે ...છે, એટલે
Connection (接続)V/い-adj (plain) + ことから　｜　な-adj + な + ことから　｜　N + である + ことから
RegisterNeutral — common in expository/informational writing (e.g. explaining a name's origin)
Typical useCites a visible or well-known fact and draws a conclusion/result from it — has a more "objective observation → outcome" feel than plain から/ので.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV (plain) + ことから見えることからmieru koto kara
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + ことから高いことからtakai koto kara
な-adjective(added, not in book) な-adj + な + ことから有名なことからyuumei na koto kara
NounN + である + ことから名所であることからmeisho de aru koto kara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
富士山が見えることから、この町は富士見町という名前がついた。Fujisan ga mieru koto kara, kono machi wa Fujimichou to iu namae ga tsuita. This town was named Fujimichō because Mt. Fuji can be seen from here. यहाँ से फ़ूजी पर्वत दिखाई देने के कारण, इस शहर का नाम फ़ूजिमीचो रखा गया। અહીંથી ફૂજી પર્વત દેખાવાના કારણે, આ શહેરનું નામ ફૂજિમીચો રાખવામાં આવ્યું.
ここは桜の名所であることから、春には花見客が大勢やってくる。Koko wa sakura no meisho de aru koto kara, haru ni wa hanami-kyaku ga oozei yatte kuru. Since this place is famous for its cherry blossoms, many flower-viewing visitors come in spring. यह जगह चेरी ब्लॉसम के लिए मशहूर होने के कारण, वसंत में बहुत सारे फूल देखने आने वाले लोग आते हैं। આ સ્થળ ચેરી બ્લોસમ માટે પ્રખ્યાત હોવાના કારણે, વસંતમાં ઘણા ફૂલ જોવા આવનારા લોકો આવે છે.

ことから is especially common when explaining how a place, custom, or thing got its name or reputation — the reason clause states a fact anyone could observe, not a personal judgment. Keep it apart from Point 2 below, which needs a person's name/pronoun immediately before の.

【2】 N（人）+ のことだから

neutral register
Reading~no koto da kara
Meaning (EN)"knowing [person] as I/we do, surely ~" — a prediction based on someone's well-known character
Meaning (HI)उसे जानते हुए, ज़रूर... / उस इंसान की बात है तो...
Meaning (GU)તેને ઓળખતા, ચોક્કસ... / એ વ્યક્તિ છે તો...
Connection (接続)N（人）+ のことだから
RegisterNeutral — common in everyday conversation
Typical useAlmost always followed by a speculative ending (だろう／きっと／にちがいない): the speaker predicts behavior purely from the person's well-known personality or habits, not from a general fact.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (person)N + のことだから彼のことだからkare no koto da kara
Verb / い-adjective / な-adjectiveNot a separate attachment point — こそのことだから always needs a person noun (optionally preceded by a descriptive modifier like 親切な林さん) directly before の.—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
いつも遅刻する彼のことだから、もうすぐ現れるだろう。Itsumo chikoku suru kare no koto da kara, mou sugu arawareru darou. He's always late, so — knowing him — he'll surely show up soon. वह हमेशा देर से आता है, तो ज़ाहिर है, वह जल्द ही आ जाएगा (हमेशा की तरह देर से)। તે હંમેશા મોડો આવે છે, એટલે તે ટૂંક સમયમાં આવી જ જશે (હંમેશની જેમ મોડો).
親切な林さんのことだから、頼めば手伝ってくれるよ。Shinsetsu na Hayashi-san no koto da kara, tanomeba tetsudatte kureru yo. Hayashi-san is so kind — if you ask, he'll surely help you. हयाशी जी इतने दयालु हैं, अगर तुम कहोगे तो वे ज़रूर मदद करेंगे। હયાશી સાહેબ એટલા દયાળુ છે, જો તમે કહેશો તો તેઓ ચોક્કસ મદદ કરશે.

The reasoning here is entirely character-based ("because that's the kind of person he/she is"), never a plain objective fact — that's the line separating this from Point 1's ことから.

【3】 〜ことなく

neutral / written
Reading~koto naku
Meaning (EN)"without ~ing" — a somewhat formal equivalent of 〜ないで
Meaning (HI)बिना ...किए / बिना रुके
Meaning (GU)વગર ...કર્યે / અટક્યા વગર
Connection (接続)Vる（plain, dictionary form）+ ことなく
RegisterNeutral-to-formal, mostly seen in writing/narration
Typical useDescribes an action or state continuing uninterrupted, without any pause — common in descriptive/narrative writing (rain, time, effort continuing on).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ことなく休むことなくyasumu koto naku
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets ongoing verb actions)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
雨は休むことなく降り続いた。Ame wa yasumu koto naku furitsuzuita. It rained continuously without stopping. बारिश बिना रुके लगातार होती रही। વરસાદ અટક્યા વગર સતત પડતો રહ્યો.
時は止まることなく流れる。Toki wa tomaru koto naku nagareru. Time flows on without ever stopping. समय बिना रुके बहता रहता है। સમય અટક્યા વગર વહેતો રહે છે.

Note the polarity: ことなく attaches to the plain/dictionary form (休む, not 休まない). This is the exact opposite attachment of Point 4 below, which needs the negative ない-form — a classic mix-up point (see §3).

【4】 V1-ないことにはV2-ない

neutral register
Reading~nai koto ni wa ~nai
Meaning (EN)"unless you ~, you won't/can't ..." — states a necessary precondition
Meaning (HI)जब तक ...नहीं करोगे, तब तक ...नहीं होगा
Meaning (GU)જ્યાં સુધી ...નહીં કરો, ત્યાં સુધી ...નહીં થાય
Connection (接続)V-ない-form + ことには、＋ 第二節も否定形
RegisterNeutral — used in both speech and writing
Typical useA double-negative structure that emphasizes "you must do V1 first, otherwise V2 (usually negative) simply won't happen."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ない-form + ことにはやってみないことにはyatte minai koto ni wa
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book; not shown as a productive attachment point in this section — the book's examples are all verb-based)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
やってみないことには、できるかどうかわからない。Yatte minai koto ni wa, dekiru ka dou ka wakaranai. I won't know if I can do it unless I try. जब तक कोशिश नहीं करूँगा, तब तक पता नहीं चलेगा कि यह हो पाएगा या नहीं। જ્યાં સુધી પ્રયત્ન નહીં કરું, ત્યાં સુધી ખબર નહીં પડે કે થઈ શકશે કે નહીં.
実物を見ないことには、買う気にはなれない。Jitsubutsu o minai koto ni wa, kau ki ni wa narenai. I don't feel like buying it until I actually see the real thing. जब तक असली चीज़ देख नहीं लूँगा, तब तक खरीदने का मन नहीं करेगा। જ્યાં સુધી ખરી વસ્તુ જોઈ નહીં લઉં, ત્યાં સુધી ખરીદવાનું મન નહીં થાય.

Both halves of the sentence lean negative (V1-ない...ことには, and V2 is usually also negative/potential-negative) — logically this "double negative" reads as a strong "you absolutely must do V1 first." Don't confuse the ない here with the plain form required by Point 3's ことなく.

Q1

彼が（　）ことには、会議を始められない。Kare ga (___) koto ni wa, kaigi o hajimerarenai.

Full sentence: 彼が来ないことには、会議を始められない。Kare ga konai koto ni wa, kaigi o hajimerarenai.
EN: Unless he comes, we can't start the meeting.
HI: जब तक वह नहीं आएगा, तब तक बैठक शुरू नहीं हो सकती।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી તે નહીં આવે, ત્યાં સુધી બેઠક શરૂ થઈ શકશે નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a来るkuru(wrong — 〜ないことには needs the negative form)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b来ないkonaidoesn't comeनहीं आएगाનહીં આવે

Why: 〜ないことには requires V1's negative form; 来ることには isn't the pattern being tested here.

Q2

彼女の（　）、30分前には来ているはずだよ。Kanojo no (___), sanjuppun mae ni wa kite iru hazu da yo.

Full sentence: 彼女のことだから、30分前には来ているはずだよ。Kanojo no koto da kara, sanjuppun mae ni wa kite iru hazu da yo.
EN: Knowing her, she's surely already there 30 minutes early.
HI: उसे जानते हुए, वह ज़रूर 30 मिनट पहले ही पहुँच गई होगी।
GU: તેને ઓળખતા, તે ચોક્કસ 30 મિનિટ પહેલાં જ પહોંચી ગઈ હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aことからkoto kara(wrong — states an objective reason, not a character-based prediction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bことだからkoto da karaknowing her (as I do), surely...उसे जानते हुएતેને ઓળખતા

Why: This is a prediction based on someone's known personality/habits, exactly Point 2's job; ことから would instead cite an objective fact as the reason, which doesn't fit the "surely, given who she is" nuance.

Q3

教室で席がとなりだった（　）、友人になった。Kyoushitsu de seki ga tonari datta (___), yuujin ni natta.

Full sentence: 教室で席がとなりだったことから、友人になった。Kyoushitsu de seki ga tonari datta koto kara, yuujin ni natta.
EN: Because our seats in the classroom happened to be next to each other, we became friends.
HI: क्लासरूम में सीटें बगल में होने के कारण, हम दोस्त बन गए।
GU: વર્ગખંડમાં બેઠકો બાજુમાં હોવાના કારણે, અમે મિત્રો બની ગયા.

aことだからkoto da kara(wrong — needs a person/pronoun + の, not an event clause)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This states an objective, factual reason (seats being adjacent) leading to a result — Point 1's ことから. ことだから requires a person's name/pronoun + の directly before it, which isn't the structure here.

Q4

この作業が終わらない（　）、次の作業ができない。Kono sagyou ga owaranai (___), tsugi no sagyou ga dekinai.

Full sentence: この作業が終わらないことには、次の作業ができない。Kono sagyou ga owaranai koto ni wa, tsugi no sagyou ga dekinai.
EN: Unless this task is finished, the next task can't be done.
HI: जब तक यह काम खत्म नहीं होगा, तब तक अगला काम नहीं हो सकता।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી આ કામ પૂરું નહીં થાય, ત્યાં સુધી પછીનું કામ થઈ શકશે નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aことにはkoto ni waunless (finished)जब तक ...नहींજ્યાં સુધી ...નહીં
bことなくkoto naku(wrong — needs the plain form and means "without ~ing", reversing the intent)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The sentence needs the "unless X, Y won't happen" precondition pattern — 終わらないことには. ことなく would require the plain form (終わることなく, "without finishing"), reversing the intended meaning.

Q5

作品が認められるのを知る（　）、彼は死んだ。Sakuhin ga mitomerareru no o shiru (___), kare wa shinda.

Full sentence: 作品が認められるのを知ることなく、彼は死んだ。Sakuhin ga mitomerareru no o shiru koto naku, kare wa shinda.
EN: He died without ever knowing that his work would be recognized.
HI: उसे यह जाने बिना ही कि उसका काम एक दिन सराहा जाएगा, उसकी मृत्यु हो गई।
GU: તેને એ જાણ્યા વગર જ કે તેનું કામ એક દિવસ વખણાશે, તેનું મૃત્યુ થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aことにはkoto ni wa(wrong — needs a negative verb form and a different "unless" meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

毛の色が白い＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 犬や猫は多い。Ke no iro ga shiroi ___ inu ya neko wa ooi.

Tiles: 1 シロという (Shiro to iu)　2 名前が (namae ga)　3 つけられる (tsukerareru)　4 ことから (koto kara)

4 ことから→1 シロという→2 名前が→3 つけられる

毛の色が白いことからシロという名前がつけられる犬や猫は多い。Ke no iro ga shiroi koto kara Shiro to iu namae ga tsukerareru inu ya neko wa ooi.

EN: There are many dogs and cats given the name "Shiro" because their fur is white.
HI: बालों का रंग सफ़ेद होने के कारण जिन कुत्तों और बिल्लियों को "शिरो" नाम दिया जाता है, वे बहुत हैं।
GU: વાળનો રંગ સફેદ હોવાના કારણે જે કૂતરા અને બિલાડીઓને "શિરો" નામ આપવામાં આવે છે, તે ઘણા છે.

Why: Built on Point 1 (ことから) — 白いことから…名前がつけられる = "given that it's white, it's named..."

Q7

あなたが何を悩んでいるのか＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ られない。Anata ga nani o nayande iru no ka ___ rarenai.

Tiles: 1 助け (tasuke)　2 言って (itte)　3 ことには (koto ni wa)　4 くれない (kurenai)

2 言って→4 くれない→3 ことには→1 助け

あなたが何を悩んでいるのか言ってくれないことには、助けられない。Anata ga nani o nayande iru no ka itte kurenai koto ni wa, tasukerarenai.

EN: Unless you tell me what's troubling you, I can't help you.
HI: जब तक तुम मुझे नहीं बताओगे कि तुम्हें किस बात की चिंता है, तब तक मैं तुम्हारी मदद नहीं कर सकता।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી તું મને નહીં કહે કે તને શાની ચિંતા છે, ત્યાં સુધી હું તારી મદદ કરી શકીશ નહીં.

Why: Point 4 (V-ないことにはV-ない) — 言ってくれないことには助けられない = "unless you tell me, I cannot help."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four forms split into two look-alike pairs that N2 loves to test against each other (see Q2/Q3 and Q4/Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ことから~koto kara Because ~ (objective fact) V/A/na+な/N+である + ことから States an observable fact as the reason for a result — no person-judgment involved. Explaining origins/reasons in written or expository style.
N+のことだから~no koto da kara Knowing [person], surely ~ N（人）+ のことだから Reason is a person's known character, not a general fact; needs a person/pronoun directly before の. Predicting someone's likely behavior from personality.
〜ことなく~koto naku Without ~ing Vる（plain form）+ ことなく Formal equivalent of ~ないで; attaches to the affirmative plain form, not a negative. Narrative/descriptive writing about a continuous, uninterrupted state.
V-ないことにはV-ない~nai koto ni wa ~nai Unless ~, ... won't V-ない-form + ことには、＋ 否定の第二節 A necessary precondition — double-negative structure emphasizing "must do X first." Emphasizing a mandatory prerequisite.
Exam trap: ことから and ことだから look almost identical but work completely differently — ことから attaches to a plain descriptive clause stating a fact, while ことだから requires a person/pronoun + の directly before it and predicts behavior from character, not fact (Q2 vs Q3 above). Separately, ことなく (attaches to the plain/dictionary form, means "without doing") and 〜ないことには (attaches to the negative ない-form, means "unless") sound similar but need opposite verb polarity (Q4 vs Q5 above) — always check which form the verb is in before picking.
