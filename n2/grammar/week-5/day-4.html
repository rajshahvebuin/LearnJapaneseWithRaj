Answer key

Notes
日本のみならず外国でも carries the 硬 badge (formal, a literary upgrade of ばかりでなく); the other three are neutral register. The day's trap is that ばかりか (Point 2, "not only... also") and ばかりに (Point 3, "just because of...") share a root but point in opposite directions — see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜だけあって／だけに／だけのことはある

neutral register
Reading~dake atte / dake ni / dake no koto wa aru
Meaning (EN)"as one would expect from ~ / precisely because ~" — cites a status or trait as the fitting reason for a result (can also amplify a negative result)
Meaning (HI)...होने के कारण, जैसी उम्मीद थी वैसा ही / आख़िर ...तो है ही
Meaning (GU)...હોવાના કારણે, અપેક્ષા મુજબ જ / છેવટે ...તો છે જ
Connection (接続)V/い-adj/な-adj+な/N（plain）+ だけあって／だけに／だけのことはある
RegisterNeutral — usable in speech and writing
Typical useだけあって/だけに explain a result as fitting given a known status or quality (a first-rate hotel being comfortable); だけに can also amplify a negative result caused by the same trait (something cheap breaking quickly); だけのことはある validates that something was "worth it."
Word typeFormationExample
NounN（plain）+ だけあって一流ホテルだけあってichiryuu hoteru dake atte
い-adjectiveAい + だけに安いだけにyasui dake ni
Verb (past)V-た + だけのことはある高かっただけのことはあるtakakatta dake no koto wa aru
な-adjective(added, not in book) Naな + だけあって親切なだけあってshinsetsu na dake atte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ここは一流ホテルだけあって快適だった。Koko wa ichiryuu hoteru dake atte kaiteki datta. As you'd expect from a first-rate hotel, it was comfortable. यह एक बेहतरीन होटल है, इसलिए यह आरामदायक था — जैसी उम्मीद थी वैसा ही। આ એક ઉત્તમ હોટેલ છે, એટલે તે આરામદાયક હતું — અપેક્ષા મુજબ જ.
このバッグは安いだけにすぐに壊れてしまった。Kono baggu wa yasui dake ni sugu ni kowarete shimatta. This bag was so cheap that, unsurprisingly, it broke right away. यह बैग इतना सस्ता था कि, जैसी उम्मीद थी, यह जल्दी ही टूट गया। આ બેગ એટલી સસ્તી હતી કે, અપેક્ષા મુજબ, તે જલદી તૂટી ગઈ.
このいすは丈夫だ。高かっただけのことはある。Kono isu wa joubu da. Takakatta dake no koto wa aru. This chair is sturdy. It was worth the high price. यह कुर्सी मज़बूत है। महंगी थी, लेकिन उसकी क़ीमत वसूल है। આ ખુરશી મજબૂત છે. મોંઘી હતી, પણ તેની કિંમત વસૂલ છે.

だけに is flexible: it can praise (a good hotel being comfortable) or, just as often, explain an amplified bad outcome (a cheap item breaking fast) — in both cases it says "precisely because of this trait." だけのことはある is the "it was worth it / it shows" variant, usually closing out a sentence that already stated the impressive/costly fact.

【2】 〜ばかりか／ばかりでなく

neutral register
Reading~bakari ka / bakari de naku
Meaning (EN)"not only ~ but also ~"
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...ही नहीं, बल्कि ...भी
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...જ નહીં, પણ ...પણ
Connection (接続)V/い-adj/な-adj/N（plain）+ ばかりか／ばかりでなく
RegisterNeutral — everyday-usable, slightly more emphatic than だけでなく
Typical useLists a second, often more surprising fact on top of a first one — a pure "addition," not a cause-and-result relationship.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + ばかりか成績がいいばかりかseiseki ga ii bakari ka
NounN + ばかりでなく空気ばかりでなくkuuki bakari de naku
Verb / な-adjective(added, not in book) V(plain)/Naな + ばかりか上手なばかりかjouzu na bakari ka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人は、学校の成績がいいばかりかスポーツもできる。Ano hito wa, gakkou no seiseki ga ii bakari ka supootsu mo dekiru. Not only does he have good grades at school, he's also good at sports. वह न सिर्फ़ स्कूल में अच्छे नंबर लाता है, बल्कि खेलकूद में भी अच्छा है। તે ફક્ત શાળામાં સારા માર્ક્સ જ નથી લાવતો, પણ રમતગમતમાં પણ સારો છે.
このあたりは、空気ばかりでなく水も汚染されている。Kono atari wa, kuuki bakari de naku mizu mo osen sarete iru. In this area, not only the air but the water is polluted too. इस इलाक़े में न सिर्फ़ हवा, बल्कि पानी भी प्रदूषित है। આ વિસ્તારમાં ફક્ત હવા જ નહીં, પાણી પણ પ્રદૂષિત છે.

Keep this apart from Point 3 below — they share the ばかり root but ばかりか/ばかりでなく always add a second fact (a list), while ばかりに (next point) blames a single cause for one bad result.

【3】 〜ばかりに

neutral register
Reading~bakari ni
Meaning (EN)"just because of ~ / all because of ~" — blames a single (often small) cause for an unfortunate result
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...की वजह से (कुछ बुरा हो गया)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...ના કારણે (કંઈક ખરાબ થયું)
Connection (接続)V/い-adj/な-adj+な/N+である + ばかりに
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical useEmphasizes that one specific (often trivial-seeming) reason is entirely to blame for a regrettable outcome; the clause that follows is almost always negative/unfortunate.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)V-た + ばかりに乗ったばかりにnotta bakari ni
な-adjectiveNaな + ばかりに嫌いなばかりにkirai na bakari ni
い-adjective / Noun(added, not in book) Aい／N+である + ばかりに貧しいばかりにmazushii bakari ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの飛行機に乗ったばかりに、彼は死んだ。Ano hikouki ni notta bakari ni, kare wa shinda. Just because he got on that plane, he died. सिर्फ़ उस फ्लाइट में बैठने की वजह से, उसकी मौत हो गई। ફક્ત તે વિમાનમાં બેસવાના કારણે, તેનું મૃત્યુ થયું.
英語の先生が嫌いなばかりに、英語も嫌いになってしまった。Eigo no sensei ga kirai na bakari ni, eigo mo kirai ni natte shimatta. Just because I disliked my English teacher, I ended up disliking English too. सिर्फ़ अंग्रेज़ी के टीचर को नापसंद करने की वजह से, मुझे अंग्रेज़ी भी नापसंद हो गई। ફક્ત અંગ્રેજી શિક્ષકને નાપસંદ કરવાના કારણે, મને અંગ્રેજી પણ નાપસંદ થઈ ગયું.

The sidebar explicitly notes: 「悪いことが起きた理由の強調」("emphasizing the reason a bad thing happened") — the second clause here is essentially always a misfortune, unlike ばかりか's neutral-to-positive "additions."

【4】 〜のみならず／のみか

硬 formal/written
Reading~nominarazu / ~nomika
Meaning (EN)"not only ~ but also ~" — the formal, literary register version of だけでなく/ばかりでなく
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...ही नहीं, बल्कि ...भी (औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...જ નહીં, પણ ...પણ (ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V/い-adj/な-adj+である/N+だ + のみならず／のみか
Register硬 — the sidebar labels this directly as "だけでなく／ばかりでなくの硬い表現" (the formal version of those two)
Typical useSame "not only X, also Y" meaning as Point 2, but reserved for formal writing — news articles, essays, announcements.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + のみならず日本のみならずNihon no minarazu
い-adjectiveAい + のみならず味が良いのみならずaji ga yoi nominarazu
Noun (のみか variant)N + のみか子どものみかkodomo nomika
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
その映画は日本のみならず、外国でもよく知られている。Sono eiga wa Nihon no minarazu, gaikoku demo yoku shirarete iru. That film is well known not only in Japan but overseas as well. वह फ़िल्म सिर्फ़ जापान में ही नहीं, बल्कि विदेशों में भी काफ़ी मशहूर है। તે ફિલ્મ ફક્ત જાપાનમાં જ નહીં, પણ વિદેશમાં પણ ખૂબ પ્રખ્યાત છે.
この料理は味が良いのみならず、見た目も美しい。Kono ryouri wa aji ga yoi nominarazu, mitame mo utsukushii. This dish not only tastes good, it looks beautiful too. यह पकवान न सिर्फ़ स्वादिष्ट है, बल्कि देखने में भी सुंदर है। આ વાનગી ફક્ત સ્વાદિષ્ટ જ નથી, પણ દેખાવમાં પણ સુંદર છે.
子どものみか大人もこのゲームにはまっている。Kodomo nomika otona mo kono geemu ni hamatte iru. Not only children but adults too are hooked on this game. सिर्फ़ बच्चे ही नहीं, बल्कि बड़े भी इस गेम के दीवाने हैं। ફક્ત બાળકો જ નહીં, પણ મોટેરાઓ પણ આ ગેમના દીવાના છે.

Mentally file のみならず／のみか as "ばかりでなく in a suit and tie" — identical logic, just reserved for writing where だけでなく would sound too casual.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

この家具はデザインがいい（　）丈夫だ。Kono kagu wa dezain ga ii (___) joubu da.

Full sentence: この家具はデザインがいいばかりか丈夫だ。Kono kagu wa dezain ga ii bakari ka joubu da.
EN: This furniture is not only good-looking, but sturdy too.
HI: यह फ़र्नीचर न सिर्फ़ दिखने में अच्छा है, बल्कि मज़बूत भी है।
GU: આ ફર્નિચર ફક્ત દેખાવમાં જ સારું નથી, પણ મજબૂત પણ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだけにdake ni(wrong — explains a result fitting a cause, not "not only... also")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bばかりかbakari kanot only... but alsoसिर्फ़ ...ही नहीं, बल्किફક્ત ...જ નહીં, પણ

Why: The sentence lists two additional good qualities (design AND sturdiness) — Point 2's ばかりか. だけに would instead frame one fact as the reason behind a matching outcome, not add a second quality.

あの先生は教え方がうまい（　）親切だ。Ano sensei wa oshiekata ga umai (___) shinsetsu da.

GU: તે શિક્ષક ફક્ત ભણાવવામાં કુશળ જ નથી, પણ દયાળુ પણ છે.

aだけあってdake atte(wrong — explains a result fitting a status/cause, not "not only... also")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

仕事ができる（　）、人の何倍も仕事を頼まれて困る。Shigoto ga dekiru (___), hito no nanbai mo shigoto o tanomarete komaru.

Full sentence: 仕事ができるばかりか、人の何倍も仕事を頼まれて困る。Shigoto ga dekiru bakari ka, hito no nanbai mo shigoto o tanomarete komaru.
EN: Not only am I good at my job — I also get asked to do many times more work than others, which is a hassle.
HI: सिर्फ़ काम में अच्छा होना ही काफ़ी नहीं, बल्कि दूसरों से कई गुना ज़्यादा काम भी मुझे सौंप दिया जाता है, जिससे परेशानी होती है।
GU: ફક્ત કામમાં સારો હોવો પૂરતું નથી, પણ બીજા કરતાં અનેકગણું વધારે કામ પણ મને સોંપાય છે, જેનાથી તકલીફ થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aばかりかbakari kanot only... but alsoसिर्फ़ ...ही नहींફક્ત ...જ નહીં
bばかりにbakari ni(wrong — frames a single reason for a bad outcome, not "not only... also")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This adds an unexpected second consequence (being overloaded with work) to the first fact (being competent) — Point 2's ばかりか. ばかりに would instead need competence to be the sole cause of a single bad result, which doesn't match this "not only... also" listing.

HI: वह न सिर्फ़ ताक़तवर है, बल्कि दिल का भी अच्छा है।

Q5

これはおいしい。並んで買った（　）ことはある。Kore wa oishii. Narande katta (___) koto wa aru.

Full sentence: これはおいしい。並んで買っただけのことはある。Kore wa oishii. Narande katta dake no koto wa aru.
EN: This is delicious. It was worth standing in line to buy.
HI: यह स्वादिष्ट है। लाइन में लगकर ख़रीदने लायक थी।
GU: આ સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે. લાઇનમાં ઊભા રહીને ખરીદવા જેવું હતું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだけのdake noworth it (だけのことはある)...लायक था...જેવું હતું

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

たまたま＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 事故にあってしまった。Tamatama ___ jiko ni atte shimatta.

Tiles: 1 かかった (kakatta)　2 そこを (soko o)　3 ばかりに (bakari ni)　4 通り (toori)

2 そこを→4 通り→1 かかった→3 ばかりに

たまたまそこを通りかかったばかりに事故にあってしまった。Tamatama soko o toorikakatta bakari ni jiko ni atte shimatta.

EN: Just because I happened to be passing by there, I ended up getting into an accident.
HI: बस संयोग से मैं वहाँ से गुज़र रहा था, इसी वजह से एक दुर्घटना का शिकार हो गया।
GU: ફક્ત સંજોગવશાત્ હું ત્યાંથી પસાર થઈ રહ્યો હતો, એ જ કારણે અકસ્માતનો ભોગ બન્યો.

Why: Point 3 (ばかりに) — たまたま通りかかったばかりに = "all because I happened to be passing by," the single unlucky cause of the accident.

Q7

彼は文章表現が上手だ。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Kare wa bunshou hyougen ga jouzu da. ___.

Tiles: 1 新聞記者 (shinbun kisha)　2 ある (aru)　3 のことは (no koto wa)　4 だけ (dake)

1 新聞記者→4 だけ→3 のことは→2 ある

彼は文章表現が上手だ。新聞記者だけのことはある。Kare wa bunshou hyougen ga jouzu da. Shinbun kisha dake no koto wa aru.

EN: He's skilled at written expression. No wonder — after all, he's a newspaper reporter (it shows).
HI: वह लेखन में माहिर है। आख़िर, वह एक पत्रकार तो है ही (इसीलिए)।
GU: તે લેખનમાં કુશળ છે. છેવટે, તે એક પત્રકાર તો છે જ (એટલે જ).

Why: Point 1 (だけのことはある) — 新聞記者だけのことはある = "it shows he's a newspaper reporter," validating the skill as fitting his profession.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four forms split into a "だけ family" (Point 1) and a "ばかり／のみ family" (Points 2–4), and the book deliberately cross-tests them (see Q1–Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜だけあって／だけに／だけのことはある~dake atte / ni / no koto wa aru As expected from ~ / precisely because ~ V/A/na+な/N（plain）+ だけ〜 Cites one cause/status to explain a single, fitting (or ironically amplified negative) result. Explaining why a result matches someone/something's known quality.
〜ばかりか／ばかりでなく~bakari ka / de naku Not only ~ but also ~ V/A/na/N（plain）+ ばかりか〜 Adds a second, often surprising fact — a listing, not a cause-result relationship. Neutral-register "not only X, also Y."
〜ばかりに~bakari ni Just because of ~ (bad result) V/A/na+な/N+である + ばかりに Single cause leading to an unfortunate/regrettable outcome — always negative-leaning. Blaming one small/trivial reason for something bad.
〜のみならず／のみか~nominarazu / nomika Not only ~ but also ~ (formal) V/A/na+である/N+だ + のみならず〜 Same meaning as ばかりか/ばかりでなく but higher, more literary register. Formal writing (news, essays).
Exam trap: ばかりか (Point 2, "not only... also") and ばかりに (Point 3, "just because of...") share the same ばかり root but point in structurally opposite directions — ばかりか adds a second fact to a list, while ばかりに blames a single cause for one bad result (Q3 above tests this directly). Separately, ばかりでなく and のみならず (Points 2 and 4) mean exactly the same thing at different registers — mixing them up is a register mismatch, not a meaning error, but N2 often tests recognizing the formal のみならず in writing (Q2 above).
