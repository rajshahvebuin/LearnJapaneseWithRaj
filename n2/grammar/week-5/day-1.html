Notes
The source marks 覚えられっこない and 言いかねない with a small speech-bubble icon (casual/spoken tone — the book gives no formal legend for this icon beyond context, so it's noted here in words rather than guessed at); わかりかねる and 信じがたい carry the 硬 badge for formal/written register. All four forms circle around one theme — "impossible / risky / hard to do" — which is exactly why the book groups them on the same day; see §3 for the trap.

1 Grammar Points

っこない almost always rides on a potential-form verb (覚えられる → 覚えられっこない), reinforcing the "impossible" meaning already present in the potential form itself. It's emphatic and casual — reach for it in conversation, never in formal writing.

Typical useFlags a real, plausible risk that something undesirable could occur, given a person's character or a situation. Often paired with 「Vたら」「Nなら」.

Despite looking like a plain negative (ない), かねない does not mean "cannot happen" — it means the opposite: a bad outcome plausibly could happen. Keep this apart from Point 3's かねる, which really is a negative-feeling "cannot do."

わかりかねます literally softens "I don't understand/know" into "it's something I'm unable to determine" — a face-saving way to decline without a flat わかりません. This is the polite-register cousin of Point 2, and the two are easy to mix up (see §3).

The source's sidebar lists more がたい compounds worth memorizing as set vocabulary: 言いがたい (hard to say), 理解しがたい (hard to understand), 捨てがたい (hard to give up/discard), 忘れがたい (hard/unforgettable). Treat these as fixed expressions rather than deriving each one from scratch.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, choose the correct option) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.79. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.81.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

休まずに長時間運転したら、事故を起こし（　）。Yasumazu ni chōjikan unten shitara, jiko o okoshi (___).

Full sentence: 休まずに長時間運転したら、事故を起こしかねない。Yasumazu ni chōjikan unten shitara, jiko o okoshikanenai.
EN: If you drive for a long time without resting, you might well cause an accident.
HI: बिना रुके लंबे समय तक गाड़ी चलाई जाए, तो दुर्घटना हो सकती है।
GU: આરામ કર્યા વગર લાંબા સમય સુધી ડ્રાઇવિંગ કરવામાં આવે, તો અકસ્માત થવાની શક્યતા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかねるkaneru(wrong — "unable to cause," nonsensical here)(यहाँ अर्थहीन)(અહીં અર્થહીન)
bかねないkanenaimight well (cause)हो सकता है (हो जाए)કદાચ (થઈ જાય)

Why: 事故を起こしかねない warns of a real risk of causing an accident. 起こしかねる would mean "unable to cause an accident," which makes no sense as a warning.

Q2

その要求は、受け入れ（　）ものであった。Sono youkyuu wa, ukeire (___) mono de atta.

Full sentence: その要求は、受け入れがたいものであった。Sono youkyuu wa, ukeire-gatai mono de atta.
EN: That request was a hard one to accept.
HI: वह मांग स्वीकार करने में मुश्किल थी।
GU: તે માંગ સ્વીકારવી મુશ્કેલ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aっこないkkonai(wrong — casual register, doesn't combine with 〜ものであった)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

EN: If he had been left alone, he might well have taken his own life.
HI: अगर उसे यूँ ही छोड़ दिया जाता, तो वह आत्महत्या भी कर सकता था।
GU: જો તેને એમ જ છોડી દેવાયો હોત, તો તે આત્મહત્યા પણ કરી શકે તેમ હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aがたかったgatakatta(wrong — describes inherent difficulty, not a feared risky outcome)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bかねなかったkanenakattamight well have (happened) — pastहो सकता थाથઈ શકે તેમ હતું

Why: The sentence describes a feared, narrowly-avoided outcome — exactly what かねない (here in past tense かねなかった) expresses. がたい just labels something as "hard to do," not a risky possible result.

Q4

3歳の子どもにそんな難しいことを言っても、わかり（　）よ。San-sai no kodomo ni sonna muzukashii koto o itte mo, wakari (___) yo.

GU: ત્રણ વર્ષના બાળકને આવી અઘરી વાત કહો તો પણ, તે બિલકુલ સમજી શકશે નહીં.

Q5

ご予約金の返金はでき（　）。Go-yoyakukin no henkin wa deki (___).

Full sentence: ご予約金の返金はできかねます。Go-yoyakukin no henkin wa dekikanemasu.
EN: We regret that we are unable to refund the reservation deposit.
HI: बयाना राशि की वापसी करना हमारे लिए संभव नहीं है।
GU: રિઝર્વેશન રકમનું રિફંડ કરવું અમારા માટે શક્ય નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかねますkanemasuwe regret we are unable toसंभव नहीं हैશક્ય નથી

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

車内での携帯電話の使用は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Shanai de no keitai denwa no shiyou wa ___.

Tiles: 1 トラブルの (toraburu no)　2 乗客同士の (joukyaku doushi no)　3 なりかねない (narikanenai)　4 原因とも (gen'in tomo)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, same as N1's non-test-day convention.)

2 乗客同士の→1 トラブルの→4 原因とも→3 なりかねない

車内での携帯電話の使用は乗客同士のトラブルの原因ともなりかねない。Shanai de no keitai denwa no shiyou wa joukyaku doushi no toraburu no gen'in tomo narikanenai.

EN: Using a mobile phone inside the train/vehicle can even become a cause of trouble between passengers.
HI: गाड़ी के अंदर मोबाइल फोन का इस्तेमाल यात्रियों के बीच झगड़े की वजह भी बन सकता है।
GU: વાહનની અંદર મોબાઇલ ફોનનો ઉપયોગ મુસાફરો વચ્ચે તકરારનું કારણ પણ બની શકે છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜かねない) — 原因ともなりかねない = "could even become a cause," warning of a plausible bad outcome.

Q7

いつも＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 信じられっこない。Itsumo ___ shinjirarekkonai.

Tiles: 1 君の話 (kimi no hanashi)　2 言っている (itte iru)　3 うそばかり (uso bakari)　4 なんか (nanka)

3 うそばかり→2 言っている→1 君の話→4 なんか

いつもうそばかり言っている君の話なんか信じられっこない。Itsumo uso bakari itte iru kimi no hanashi nanka shinjirarekkonai.

EN: There's no way I can believe something like what you say, when all you ever do is lie.
HI: तुम तो हमेशा झूठ ही बोलते हो, तुम्हारी बात पर तो बिल्कुल विश्वास नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: તું તો હંમેશા જૂઠ જ બોલે છે, તારી વાત પર તો બિલકુલ વિશ્વાસ કરી શકાય નહીં.

Why: Point 1's っこない again, this time attached to 信じられる (potential of 信じる) — flatly denying any possibility of belief.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today revolve around "impossible / risky / hard to do," and the book pairs them deliberately so learners notice the traps between them (see Q1 and Q3 above, built to trip up かねる vs かねない). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜っこない~kkonai There's absolutely no way ~ V-ます-stem + っこない Casual; a flat, confident denial of a potential ("definitely won't happen"). Everyday speech, denying something with confidence.
〜かねない~kanenai Might well ~ (bad outcome) V-ます-stem + かねない Looks negative but actually flags a real risk of something undesirable happening — not "cannot happen." Warning that a bad result is plausible.
〜かねる~kaneru Cannot bring myself to ~ V-ます-stem + かねる 硬; expresses present inability/reluctance, not a future risk — do not confuse with かねない. Polite formal refusal or admission of inability (business, service).
〜がたい~gatai Hard to ~ V-ます-stem + がたい 硬; describes something as inherently difficult, not "impossible" or "risky." Formal writing about emotional/cognitive difficulty (信じがたい, 忘れがたい, etc.).
Exam trap: かねる and かねない look like a plain verb and its negation, but they are not opposites in meaning. かねる already means "cannot do X" (a negative meaning despite its positive-looking form); its grammatical negative, かねない, flips to mean "there's a chance X (usually bad) might happen" — not "cannot happen." Q1 and Q3 above are built to test exactly this trap: check whether the sentence is a present formal refusal (→ かねる) or a feared, plausible future outcome (→ かねない).
