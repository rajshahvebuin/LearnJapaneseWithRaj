1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に際して／際し／際しては／際してのN

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni saishite
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of ~ / at the time of ~" — marks a formal, often one-time juncture
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / ...करते समय (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)...ના પ્રસંગે / ...કરતી વખતે (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)N（する省略）+ に　｜　Vる + に際して／際し／際しては／際してのN
Register硬 — a formal expression close in meaning to にあたり (cross-referenced by the book at p.72)
Typical useMarks a formal occasion (applying, visiting, entering) as the moment the following statement applies to — common in official announcements and formal speech.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する implied)N + に際してはお申し込みに際してはo-moushikomi ni saishite wa
VerbVる + に際し訪問するに際しhoumon suru ni saishi
い-adjective / な-adjective(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets formal occasions/events, not descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お申し込みに際しては、写真が必要となります。O-moushikomi ni saishite wa, shashin ga hitsuyou to narimasu. When applying, a photograph is required. आवेदन करते समय, एक फ़ोटो आवश्यक होती है। અરજી કરતી વખતે, એક ફોટો જરૂરી છે.
A氏は日本を訪問するに際し、喜びを語った。Ee-shi wa Nihon o houmon suru ni saishi, yorokobi o katatta. On the occasion of his visit to Japan, Mr. A spoke of his joy. श्री ए ने जापान की अपनी यात्रा के अवसर पर, अपनी ख़ुशी ज़ाहिर की। શ્રી A એ જાપાનની પોતાની મુલાકાત વખતે, પોતાનો આનંદ વ્યક્ત કર્યો.

際して marks a formal, bounded juncture (a single application, a single visit) — it does not describe an ongoing or scaling relationship, which is what separates it from Points 2 and 3 below (see §3).

【2】 〜に基づいて／基づき／基づくN／基づいたN

neutral / formal-leaning
Reading~ni motozuite
Meaning (EN)"based on ~ / in accordance with ~" — cites a factual or evidentiary basis
Meaning (HI)...के आधार पर
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે
Connection (接続)N + に基づいて／基づき／基づくN／基づいたN
RegisterNeutral-to-formal — common in business, academic, and explanatory writing
Typical useNames the factual ground (a plan, data, experience) that an action is built on or derived from.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に基づいて計画に基づいてkeikaku ni motozuite
NounN + に基づき経験に基づきkeiken ni motozuki
Noun (modifying, past)N + に基づいたN人間工学に基づいた家具ningen kougaku ni motozuita kagu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この工事は市の計画に基づいて進められます。Kono kouji wa shi no keikaku ni motozuite susumeraremasu. This construction is being carried out based on the city's plan. यह निर्माण कार्य शहर की योजना के आधार पर आगे बढ़ाया जा रहा है। આ બાંધકામ શહેરની યોજનાના આધારે આગળ વધારવામાં આવે છે.
長年の経験に基づき新入社員を教育する。Naganen no keiken ni motozuki shin'nyuu shain o kyouiku suru. I train new employees based on many years of experience. कई सालों के अनुभव के आधार पर नए कर्मचारियों को प्रशिक्षित किया जाता है। ઘણાં વર્ષોના અનુભવના આધારે નવા કર્મચારીઓને તાલીમ આપવામાં આવે છે.

基づいて cites a fixed factual basis — a plan that already exists, data already collected, experience already gained. That's the key line separating it from Point 3's に応じて, which describes adapting to a variable, changing factor rather than resting on a fixed one.

【3】 〜に応じて／応じ／応じたN

neutral register
Reading~ni oujite
Meaning (EN)"according to ~ / depending on ~ / in response to ~"
Meaning (HI)...के अनुसार / ...के हिसाब से
Meaning (GU)...પ્રમાણે / ...અનુસાર
Connection (接続)N + に応じて／応じ／応じたN
RegisterNeutral — usable in both speech and writing
Typical useExpresses that something scales or adapts in proportion to a changing factor — need, situation, amount, age.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に応じて必要に応じてhitsuyou ni oujite
Noun (modifying)N + に応じたN能力に応じたクラスnouryoku ni oujita kurasu
Noun (sidebar list)状況／年齢／金額 + に応じて状況に応じてjoukyou ni oujite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ソフトは、必要に応じてダウンロードしてください。Sofuto wa, hitsuyou ni oujite daunroodo shite kudasai. Please download the software as needed. सॉफ़्टवेयर को ज़रूरत के अनुसार डाउनलोड करें। સોફ્ટવેરને જરૂરિયાત પ્રમાણે ડાઉનલોડ કરો.
テスト結果に基づき、能力に応じたクラスに分けられます。Tesuto kekka ni motozuki, nouryoku ni oujita kurasu ni wakeraremasu. Based on the test results, you will be divided into classes according to your ability. परीक्षा के नतीजों के आधार पर, आपको आपकी क़ाबिलियत के अनुसार क्लास में बाँटा जाएगा। પરીક્ષાના પરિણામોના આધારે, તમને તમારી ક્ષમતા પ્રમાણે વર્ગમાં વહેંચવામાં આવશે.

Notice how the second example uses both Point 2 and Point 3 in the same sentence (テスト結果に基づき...能力に応じたクラス) — a fixed factual basis (test results) determines a variable, scaling outcome (which class, depending on ability). That's the cleanest illustration of the difference between the two.

【4】 〜の下で／下／下に

neutral register
Reading~no moto de
Meaning (EN)"under ~ (guidance / supervision / conditions)"
Meaning (HI)...के मार्गदर्शन में / ...के अधीन
Meaning (GU)...ના માર્ગદર્શન હેઠળ / ...ની હેઠળ
Connection (接続)N + の + 下で／下／下に
RegisterNeutral — usable in both speech and writing
Typical useDescribes an action taking place under someone's guidance/supervision, or under certain ambient conditions or influence (the sky, the sun, the law, affection).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + の下で青空の下でaozora no moto de
NounN + の下ご指導の下go-shidou no moto
Noun (sidebar list)太陽／影響／先生／愛情 + の下で先生の下でsensei no moto de
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子どもたちが青空の下で元気に遊んでいる。Kodomotachi ga aozora no moto de genki ni asonde iru. The children are playing cheerfully under the blue sky. बच्चे नीले आसमान के नीचे ख़ुशी से खेल रहे हैं। બાળકો ભૂરા આકાશ નીચે ખુશીથી રમી રહ્યાં છે.
田中先生のご指導の下、研究論文を書いています。Tanaka-sensei no go-shidou no moto, kenkyuu ronbun o kaite imasu. I am writing my research paper under Professor Tanaka's guidance. मैं तनाका सर के मार्गदर्शन में अपना शोध-पत्र लिख रहा हूँ। હું તનાકા સાહેબના માર્ગદર્શન હેઠળ મારો સંશોધન લેખ લખી રહ્યો છું.

の下で spans both a literal "physically under" (under the sky) and a figurative "under someone's care/authority" (under a mentor's guidance) — both senses are tested in today's Practice Ⅱ below, where the same phrase fills two very different-looking blanks.

Q1

この番組は、皆様からのご意見（　）構成されています。Kono bangumi wa, minasama kara no go-iken (___) kousei sarete imasu.

Full sentence: この番組は、皆様からのご意見に基づいて構成されています。...go-iken ni motozuite kousei sarete imasu.
EN: This program is put together based on opinions from all of you.
HI: यह कार्यक्रम आप सभी की राय के आधार पर तैयार किया जाता है।
GU: આ કાર્યક્રમ તમારા બધાના અભિપ્રાયના આધારે તૈયાર કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのもとでno moto de(wrong — "under," doesn't fit "built on opinions" as well)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: કરાર કરતી વખતે, ધ્યાન રાખવા જેવા મુદ્દાઓ વિશે સમજાવવામાં આવશે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms attach to N + に (or の), and all four can loosely be glossed "in relation to X" — which is exactly why the book packs them into one day full of cross-traps (see Q2, Q3, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に際して~ni saishite At the time of ~ / on the occasion of ~ Vる／Nする + に際して 硬; marks a formal, bounded one-time juncture — not proportional or evidentiary. Formal announcements, official moments (applying, visiting, signing).
〜に基づいて~ni motozuite Based on ~ N + に基づいて Cites a fixed factual/evidentiary basis (data, plan, experience) something is built on. Explaining what factual grounding an action follows.
〜に応じて~ni oujite According to ~ / depending on ~ N + に応じて Describes something scaling/adapting in proportion to a variable, changing factor. Expressing a scaling or adaptive relationship.
〜の下で~no moto de Under ~ (guidance/conditions) N + の下で Describes being under someone's care, supervision, or ambient conditions. Describing supervision or environmental/relational context.
Exam trap: 際して (a one-time formal occasion), 応じて (an ongoing proportional adaptation), and 基づいて (a fixed factual basis) all follow N+に but describe structurally different relationships — Q2, Q3, and Q5 above each isolate one of these three-way mix-ups. A quick test: is the noun a single bounded event (→際して), a variable condition being adapted to (→応じて), or a fixed fact/data/plan being relied on (→基づいて)?
