Notes
None of today's four forms carry a 硬 badge — all are neutral register, everyday-usable expressions. The real trap today is between 当然だ (Point 1, "naturally deserved") and 同然だ (Point 3, "as good as"), which look and sound almost identical (とうぜん vs どうぜん) but mean completely different things — see §3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜て／くて／で + 当然だ

neutral register
Reading~te touzen da
Meaning (EN)"it's only natural that ~ / no wonder ~" — usually a (often negative) consequence someone brought on themselves
Meaning (HI)...होना स्वाभाविक है / यह लाज़मी है कि...
Meaning (GU)...થવું સ્વાભાવિક છે / એ સ્વાભાવિક છે કે...
Connection (接続)Vて / Aくて / Naで + 当然だ／当然のN／当たり前だ／当たり前のN
RegisterNeutral — usable in speech and writing
Typical useFrames a result — often unfavorable — as a natural, deserved consequence of one's own actions or a situation's obvious logic ("you brought it on yourself").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVて + 当然だ嫌われて当然だkirawarete touzen da
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aくて + 当然だ高くて当然だtakakute touzen da
な-adjective(added, not in book) Naで + 当然だ静かで当然だshizuka de touzen da
Noun / 当たり前 variantVて + 当たり前だ勝って当たり前だkatte atarimae da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ひどいことばかり言ったので、彼女に嫌われて当然だ。Hidoi koto bakari itta node, kanojo ni kirawarete touzen da. I said nothing but nasty things, so it's only natural that she'd come to dislike me. मैंने सिर्फ़ बुरी बातें कही थीं, तो यह स्वाभाविक है कि वह मुझे नापसंद करे। મેં ફક્ત ખરાબ વાતો જ કરી હતી, એટલે એ સ્વાભાવિક છે કે તે મને નાપસંદ કરે.
相手のチームは弱い。勝って当たり前だ。Aite no chiimu wa yowai. Katte atarimae da. The other team is weak. Of course we'd win. विरोधी टीम कमज़ोर है। हमारा जीतना तो लाज़मी ही था। સામેની ટીમ નબળી છે. અમારું જીતવું તો સ્વાભાવિક જ હતું.

当然だ and 当たり前だ are interchangeable synonyms here (both = "naturally/of course"); the book uses whichever fits the sentence's rhythm. This point is about a deserved/expected result, not about validating someone's opinion — that's Point 2's job.

【2】 Vるの／Aいの／Naなの + も + もっともだ

neutral register
Reading~no mo mottomo da
Meaning (EN)"it's only natural/reasonable that ~" — validates a reaction, feeling, or argument as justified
Meaning (HI)यह वाजिब/उचित है कि... / ...होना बिल्कुल जायज़ है
Meaning (GU)એ વ્યાજબી/ઉચિત છે કે... / ...થવું બિલકુલ વ્યાજબી છે
Connection (接続)Vるの／Aいの／Naなの + も／は + もっともだ／もっともなN
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical useSays someone's feeling or claim makes complete sense given the circumstances. Sidebar note: もっとも＝当たり前／当然 (a synonym), but this point specifically validates a reaction/opinion, not a deserved consequence.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVるの + も + もっともだ怒るのももっともだokoru no mo mottomo da
い-adjective(example added, connection per book) Aいの + は + もっともだ高いのはもっともだtakai no wa mottomo da
な-adjective(example added, connection per book) Naなの + は + もっともだ静かなのはもっともだshizuka na no wa mottomo da
もっともなNVるの + も + もっともなN彼が悲しむのももっともな話だkare ga kanashimu no mo mottomo na hanashi da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
君が裏切ったのだから、彼女が怒るのももっともだ。Kimi ga uragitta no da kara, kanojo ga okoru no mo mottomo da. You betrayed her, so it's only natural that she's angry. तुमने उसे धोखा दिया, तो उसका गुस्सा होना बिल्कुल जायज़ है। તેં તેને દગો દીધો, એટલે તેનું ગુસ્સે થવું બિલકુલ વ્યાજબી છે.
あなたがそう言うのはもっともだ。Anata ga sou iu no wa mottomo da. You are quite right to say so. तुम्हारा ऐसा कहना बिल्कुल सही/वाजिब है। તમારું આમ કહેવું બિલકુલ સાચું/વ્યાજબી છે.

Even though もっとも is glossed as a synonym of 当然/当たり前, use this point when validating a feeling, reaction, or spoken claim — not for a deserved objective outcome like getting fired or winning a game (that's Point 1). This is the subtle line the confusion table in §3 draws out.

【3】 〜も同然だ

neutral register
Reading~mo douzen da
Meaning (EN)"as good as ~ / practically the same as ~" — equates something to a state it doesn't technically match
Meaning (HI)लगभग ...जैसा ही है / ...के बराबर है
Meaning (GU)લગભગ ...જેવું જ છે / ...ની બરાબર છે
Connection (接続)V-た（plain past）＋も／N（も）／ないも + 同然だ／同然のN
RegisterNeutral — usable in speech and writing
Typical useClaims something is practically equivalent to a different state or thing — a used item that's "as good as new," or a lack of something that's "as good as having none."
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)(added, illustrative) V-た + も + 同然だ忘れたも同然だwasureta mo douzen da
NounN（も）+ 同然だ新品（も）同然だshinpin (mo) douzen da
ない-formない + も + 同然だないも同然だnai mo douzen da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この車は中古車といっても新品（も）同然だ。Kono kuruma wa chuukosha to itte mo shinpin (mo) douzen da. Although this is a used car, it's practically as good as new. यह गाड़ी भले ही सेकंड-हैंड है, लेकिन यह लगभग नई जैसी ही है। આ ગાડી ભલે સેકન્ડ-હેન્ડ છે, પણ તે લગભગ નવી જેવી જ છે.
彼の財産はないも同然だ。Kare no zaisan wa nai mo douzen da. His assets are practically nonexistent. उसकी संपत्ति न के बराबर है। તેની સંપત્તિ નહિવત્ છે (ન બરાબર છે).

Watch the reading: 同然（どうぜん, "as good as/equivalent"）is easy to misread or mishear as 当然（とうぜん, "naturally," Point 1) — they look related but are unrelated in meaning. See the exam trap in §3.

【4】 〜だけましだ

neutral register
Reading~dake mashi da
Meaning (EN)"at least ~ is better than nothing / that much is something to be grateful for"
Meaning (HI)कम से कम ...है, यही ग़नीमत है / ...होना ही काफ़ी है
Meaning (GU)ઓછામાં ઓછું ...છે, એ પણ ગનીમત છે
Connection (接続)V（plain）／Aい／Naだ + だけましだ
RegisterNeutral — common in everyday speech and writing
Typical useFrames one redeeming point as a silver lining against an otherwise unsatisfying or unlucky situation — "it could be worse, and at least this one thing is fine."
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV（plain）+ だけましだあるだけましだaru dake mashi da
Verb (negative)V-ない + だけましだ首にならないだけましだkubi ni naranai dake mashi da
な-adjectiveNaな + だけましだ便利なだけましだbenri na dake mashi da
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい + だけましだ安いだけましだyasui dake mashi da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
君は仕事があるだけましだよ。俺は首になってしまった。Kimi wa shigoto ga aru dake mashi da yo. Ore wa kubi ni natte shimatta. You should be glad you even have a job. I've just been fired. तुम्हारे पास कम से कम नौकरी तो है, यही बहुत है। मुझे तो निकाल ही दिया गया। તારી પાસે ઓછામાં ઓછું નોકરી તો છે, એ જ ઘણું છે. મને તો કાઢી જ મૂક્યો.
給料が減ったけれど、首にならないだけましだ。Kyuuryou ga hetta keredo, kubi ni naranai dake mashi da. My pay has gone down, but at least I wasn't fired — that's something. तनख़्वाह भले ही कम हो गई, लेकिन नौकरी नहीं गई, यही ग़नीमत है। પગાર ભલે ઘટ્યો, પણ નોકરી ન ગઈ, એ જ ગનીમત છે.
僕のアパートは、狭くて高いけれど、便利なだけましだ。Boku no apaato wa, semakute takakute keredo, benri na dake mashi da. My apartment is small and expensive, but at least it's convenient — that counts for something. मेरा अपार्टमेंट भले ही छोटा और महंगा है, लेकिन कम से कम सुविधाजनक तो है, यही काफ़ी है। મારું એપાર્ટમેન્ટ ભલે નાનું અને મોંઘું છે, પણ ઓછામાં ઓછું અનુકૂળ તો છે, એ જ પૂરતું છે.

まし itself is a な-adjective meaning "relatively better," so だけましだ literally reads "just the fact that ~ is already the 'better' part." It always implies an unstated, worse alternative in the background.

Q1

よその家の庭に勝手に入ってうろうろしていたら、どろぼうと間違われて（　）。Yoso no ie no niwa ni katte ni haitte urouro shite itara, dorobou to machigawarete (___).

Full sentence: よその家の庭に勝手に入ってうろうろしていたら、どろぼうと間違われて当然だ。...dorobou to machigawarete touzen da.
EN: If you wander uninvited into someone else's yard, it's only natural you'd be mistaken for a burglar.
HI: अगर तुम किसी और के घर के आँगन में बेवजह घुसकर घूमोगे, तो चोर समझे जाना स्वाभाविक ही है।
GU: જો તું બીજાના ઘરના આંગણામાં વગર પરવાનગીએ ઘૂસીને ફર્યા કરે, તો ચોર સમજાવું સ્વાભાવિક જ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a当然だtouzen danaturally (deserved)स्वाभाविक हैસ્વાભાવિક છે
bましだmashi da(wrong — needs だけ before it; means "better than nothing," unrelated here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 1's 〜て当然だ fits "naturally deserves being mistaken for a thief" given the suspicious behavior. ましだ alone isn't valid here — it needs だけ attached to mean "better than nothing," an unrelated meaning.

Q2

市民にとって役に立つことに税金を使ってほしいという声は（　）だ。Shimin ni totte yaku ni tatsu koto ni zeikin o tsukatte hoshii to iu koe wa (___) da.

Full sentence: 市民にとって役に立つことに税金を使ってほしいという声はもっともだ。...koe wa mottomo da.
EN: The call for tax money to be used for things helpful to citizens is entirely reasonable.
HI: नागरिकों के लिए उपयोगी चीज़ों पर टैक्स का पैसा खर्च करने की माँग बिल्कुल वाजिब है।
GU: નાગરિકો માટે ઉપયોગી બાબતો પર ટેક્સનું નાણું વાપરવાની માંગ સાવ વ્યાજબી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aもっともmottomoreasonable, justifiedवाजिबવ્યાજબી
b同然douzen(wrong — means "as good as," doesn't fit "the opinion is reasonable")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This validates an opinion/demand as reasonable — Point 2's もっともだ. 同然 would instead claim the opinion is "practically the same as" something, which isn't the intended meaning.

Q6

今度のアルバイト、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ です。Kondo no arubaito, ___ desu.

Tiles: 1 だけまし (dake mashi)　2 けど (kedo)　3 交通費が出る (koutsuuhi ga deru)　4 時給は安い (jikyuu wa yasui)

EN: This new part-time job pays low wages, but at least it covers transportation costs — that's something.
HI: इस बार की पार्ट-टाइम नौकरी में तनख़्वाह तो कम है, लेकिन कम से कम आने-जाने का खर्च मिल जाता है, यही ग़नीमत है।

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜だけましだ) — 交通費が出るだけまし = "at least travel costs being covered is something," the redeeming point against the low wage.

Q7

EN: Company A's stock price crashed, and the shares I owned ended up practically worthless (as good as scrap paper).
HI: A कंपनी के शेयर की कीमत धड़ाम से गिर गई, और मेरे पास मौजूद शेयर लगभग रद्दी कागज़ के बराबर हो गए।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points cluster around "naturally/reasonably/practically" and the book deliberately mixes them so learners must distinguish deserved outcomes from validated opinions from equivalences (see Q1–Q3 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜て/くて/で＋当然だ~te touzen da It's only natural that ~ (deserved result) Vて／Aくて／Naで + 当然だ Frames a (often negative) consequence as deserved/expected from one's own actions. Saying someone brought a result on themselves.
〜の＋も＋もっともだ~no mo mottomo da It's only natural/reasonable that ~ Vるの／Aいの／Naなの + も/は + もっともだ Validates a reaction, feeling, or opinion as justified — not a deserved consequence. Agreeing that someone's feeling or claim makes sense.
〜も同然だ~mo douzen da As good as ~ / practically the same as ~ Vた／N（も）／ないも + 同然だ Equates two different things/states as practically equivalent — unrelated to "naturally." Comparing something to a state it technically isn't.
〜だけましだ~dake mashi da At least ~ is better than nothing V／Aい／Naな + だけましだ Frames one point as a silver lining against an (unstated) worse alternative. Expressing gratitude despite an imperfect situation.
Exam trap: 当然だ (Point 1) and もっともだ (Point 2) both translate loosely as "natural/reasonable," but 当然だ frames a deserved outcome (often bad, resulting from one's own actions), while もっともだ validates a person's reaction or opinion as sensible — Q1 vs Q2 test exactly this. Separately, 同然だ (Point 3, "as good as X") is easy to confuse with 当然だ (Point 1, "naturally") purely because they look and sound alike (どうぜん vs とうぜん) despite meaning completely different things — Q3 tests this reading-based mix-up directly. Always check: is this about a deserved result, a validated opinion, or an equivalence?
