Notes
飲もうではないか carries the 硬 badge in the source, glossed explicitly as "強い誘い／強い意志、男性的" (a strong invitation/strong will, masculine-leaning); the other three points are neutral register. Today's title itself, 片づけようがない, is a live example of Point 2 in the wild.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Vよう + ではないか／じゃないか

硬 — strong invitation/strong will, masculine
Reading~(y)ou de wa nai ka / ja nai ka
Meaning (EN)"let's ~! / I will ~!" — a strong invitation to others, or an assertion of strong personal resolve
Meaning (HI)चलो ...करें! / मैं ज़रूर ...करूँगा!
Meaning (GU)ચાલો ...કરીએ! / હું ચોક્કસ ...કરીશ!
Connection (接続)V-よう（volitional form）+ ではないか／じゃないか
Register硬 — a rhetorical-question form used for emphasis (not literal doubt), traditionally felt as masculine speech
Typical useRallies a group into action together, or declares the speaker's own firm resolve when no one else will act.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (volitional)V-よう + ではないか飲もうではないかnomou de wa nai ka
Verb (volitional, casual)V-よう + じゃないかやってみようじゃないかyatte miyou ja nai ka
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern is built specifically on the verb volitional form)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日はお祝いだ。みんなで飲もうではないか。Kyou wa oiwai da. Minna de nomou de wa nai ka. Today is a celebration. Let's all drink together! आज जश्न का दिन है। चलो सब मिलकर पिएँ! આજે ઉજવણીનો દિવસ છે. ચાલો બધા સાથે મળીને પીએ!
だれもやらないなら、ぼくがやってみようじゃないか。Dare mo yaranai nara, boku ga yatte miyou ja nai ka. If no one else will do it, I'll give it a try! अगर कोई नहीं करेगा, तो मैं ही करके देखता हूँ! જો કોઈ નહીં કરે, તો હું જ પ્રયત્ન કરી જોઉં!

The ではないか here is not a real question — it's rhetorical, adding rallying emphasis to a volitional form that already means "let's ~ / I'll ~." Don't confuse this ではないか with the plain volitional stem used in Point 2's formation (見ましょう vs 行きよう below) — see §3.

【2】 V-ます-stem + ようがない／ようもない

neutral register
Reading~you ga nai / you mo nai
Meaning (EN)"there's no way to ~" — no method or means exists to carry out the action
Meaning (HI)...करने का कोई तरीक़ा नहीं है
Meaning (GU)...કરવાની કોઈ રીત નથી
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem + ようがない／ようもない
RegisterNeutral — usable in both speech and writing
Typical useFocuses specifically on the absence of a method — different from other "impossible" expressions (like っこない or そうにない), which focus on likelihood or ability rather than the lack of a means.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-ます-stem + ようがない言いようがないiiyou ga nai
VerbV-ます-stem + ようもない直しようもないnaoshiyou mo nai
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets actionable verbs)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
何と言ったらいいのか、言いようがない。Nan to ittara ii no ka, iiyou ga nai. I really don't know how to put it — there's no way to say it. कैसे कहूँ, समझ नहीं आ रहा — कहने का कोई तरीक़ा ही नहीं है। કેવી રીતે કહું, સમજાતું નથી — કહેવાની કોઈ રીત જ નથી.
この作文は、日本語がめちゃくちゃで直しようがない。Kono sakubun wa, nihongo ga mechakucha de naoshiyou ga nai. The Japanese in this essay is so messed up that there's no way to fix it. इस निबंध की जापानी भाषा इतनी गड़बड़ है कि इसे ठीक करने का कोई तरीक़ा ही नहीं है। આ નિબંધની જાપાનીઝ ભાષા એટલી ગૂંચવાયેલી છે કે તેને સુધારવાની કોઈ રીત જ નથી.

The よう here is the same nominalizing "-you" seen in ~方 (yarikata, "way of doing"), not the volitional よう of Point 1 — attaching it to the ます-stem (言い, 直し) creates a noun-like "the way of ~ing," which がない then negates entirely.

【3】 〜かのようだ／かのように

neutral register
Reading~ka no you da / ka no you ni
Meaning (EN)"as if ~ / as though ~"
Meaning (HI)मानो ...जैसा / जैसे कि...
Meaning (GU)જાણે ...જેવું / જાણે કે...
Connection (接続)V/い-adj/な-adj+である/N+である + かのようだ／かのように〜
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and descriptive writing
Typical useA simile describing something as resembling a state that isn't literally true, often intensified with まるで ("just like").
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV（plain）+ かのようだ泣いているかのようだnaite iru ka no you da
Verb (past)V-た + かのように見たかのようにmita ka no you ni
な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book) Naである／Nである + かのようだ本物であるかのようだhonmono de aru ka no you da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
葬式が始まると雨が降り出した。まるで空が泣いているかのようだ。Sōshiki ga hajimaru to ame ga furidashita. Marude sora ga naite iru ka no you da. When the funeral started, it began to rain. It was as if the sky were crying. जैसे ही अंतिम संस्कार शुरू हुआ, बारिश होने लगी। मानो आकाश रो रहा हो। જેવો અંતિમ સંસ્કાર શરૂ થયો, વરસાદ પડવા લાગ્યો. જાણે આકાશ રડી રહ્યું હોય.
怖いものでも見たかのように、彼女は震えていた。Kowai mono demo mita ka no you ni, kanojo wa furuete ita. She was shivering as if she had seen something frightening. जैसे उसने कोई डरावनी चीज़ देख ली हो, वह काँप रही थी। જાણે તેણે કોઈ ડરામણી વસ્તુ જોઈ લીધી હોય, તે ધ્રૂજી રહી હતી.

Functionally close to みたいだ／ようだ, but かのようだ carries a more literary, slightly more formal flavor and is especially common with まるで for vivid, writerly comparisons.

【4】 V-ます-stem + そうにない／そうもない

neutral register
Reading~sou ni nai / sou mo nai
Meaning (EN)"seems unlikely to ~ / doesn't look like it will ~"
Meaning (HI)ऐसा नहीं लगता कि ...होगा / होने की संभावना कम लगती है
Meaning (GU)એવું નથી લાગતું કે ...થશે / થવાની શક્યતા ઓછી લાગે છે
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem + そうにない　｜　V-れる（可能形）+ そうもない
RegisterNeutral — everyday judgment/prediction language
Typical useNegates the appearance-form そう (looks like ~) to judge, based on the current situation, that something probably won't happen or isn't achievable.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential, ます-stem)V-ます-stem + そうにない来そうにないkisou ni nai
Verb (potential)V-れる + そうもないやみそうにないyamisou ni nai
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book; not a natural fit — this pattern targets verb likelihood judgments)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近仕事がすごく忙しくて、同窓会に行けそうにないよ。Saikin shigoto ga sugoku isogashikute, doukyuukai ni ikesou ni nai yo. I've been so busy with work lately, it doesn't look like I'll be able to make it to the reunion. हाल ही में काम बहुत व्यस्त रहा है, लगता नहीं कि मैं पुनर्मिलन में जा पाऊँगा। તાજેતરમાં કામ ખૂબ વ્યસ્ત રહ્યું છે, લાગતું નથી કે હું પુનર્મિલનમાં જઈ શકીશ.
できそうもないことを、できると言ってしまって後悔している。Dekisou mo nai koto o, dekiru to itte shimatte koukai shite iru. I regret having said I could do something which probably isn't possible for me. जो काम शायद हो ही न सके, वह मैंने "हो जाएगा" कहकर पछता रहा हूँ। જે કામ કદાચ થઈ જ ન શકે, તે મેં "થઈ જશે" કહીને પસ્તાઈ રહ્યો છું.

そうにない／そうもない are the set idiomatic negatives of 〜そうだ (looks like); a literal-sounding そうじゃない is not the conventional form here — see the exam trap in §3.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

交通機関がストップすると、会社へは（　）がない。Koutsuu kikan ga sutoppu suru to, kaisha e wa (___) ga nai.

Full sentence: 交通機関がストップすると、会社へは行きようがない。Koutsuu kikan ga sutoppu suru to, kaisha e wa ikiyou ga nai.
EN: If public transportation stops, there's just no way to get to the office.
HI: अगर सार्वजनिक परिवहन बंद हो जाए, तो दफ़्तर जाने का कोई तरीक़ा ही नहीं बचता।
GU: જો જાહેર પરિવહન બંધ થઈ જાય, તો ઓફિસ જવાની કોઈ રીત જ રહેતી નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a行きようikiyouthe way to go (ます-stem+よう)जाने का तरीक़ाજવાની રીત
b行こうikou(wrong — the plain volitional "let's go," doesn't combine with がない this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 2's ようがない attaches to the ます-stem (行き) + よう, forming a noun-like "the way to go." 行こう is the already-complete plain volitional form and doesn't combine with がない this way.

Q2

友達は自分が（　）かのように喜んでくれた。Tomodachi wa jibun ga (___) ka no you ni yorokonde kureta.

Full sentence: 友達は自分が合格したかのように喜んでくれた。Tomodachi wa jibun ga goukaku shita ka no you ni yorokonde kureta.
EN: My friend was happy for me as if she herself had passed.
HI: मेरी दोस्त ऐसे खुश हुई मानो वह ख़ुद पास हो गई हो।
GU: મારી મિત્ર એવી ખુશ થઈ જાણે તે પોતે જ પાસ થઈ ગઈ હોય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a合格するgoukaku suru(wrong — present/future tense doesn't fit this "as if it had happened" simile)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b合格したgoukaku shitahad passedपास हो गई होપાસ થઈ ગઈ હોય

Why: The friend's joy is described as if she herself had experienced passing — かのように needs the past tense 合格した to match "as if she had passed," Point 3's pattern.

Q5

彼は知らないことも、（　）かのように話す。Kare wa shiranai koto mo, (___) ka no you ni hanasu.

Full sentence: 彼は知らないことも、知っているかのように話す。Kare wa shiranai koto mo, shitte iru ka no you ni hanasu.
EN: He talks as if he knows things even when he doesn't.
HI: उसे जो पता ही नहीं होता, वह भी वह ऐसे बात करता है मानो उसे पता हो।
GU: તેને જે ખબર જ ન હોય, તે વિશે પણ તે એવી રીતે વાત કરે છે જાણે તેને ખબર હોય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a知らないshiranai(wrong — redundant with the already-stated 知らないことも)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

雨の中を走ってきた彼女は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ だった。Ame no naka o hashitte kita kanojo wa ___ datta.

Tiles: 1 川で泳いできた (kawa de oyoide kita)　2 全身ずぶぬれで (zenshin zubunure de)　3 かのよう (ka no you)　4 まるで (marude)

2 全身ずぶぬれで→4 まるで→1 川で泳いできた→3 かのよう

雨の中を走ってきた彼女は全身ずぶぬれで、まるで川で泳いできたかのようだった。Ame no naka o hashitte kita kanojo wa zenshin zubunure de, marude kawa de oyoide kita ka no you datta.

EN: She came running through the rain, completely drenched — as if she had just swum across a river.
HI: बारिश में दौड़ते हुए आई वह पूरी तरह भीग चुकी थी, मानो वह नदी में तैरकर आई हो।
GU: વરસાદમાં દોડીને આવેલી તે સંપૂર્ણ પલળી ગઈ હતી, જાણે તે નદીમાં તરીને આવી હોય.

Why: Point 3 (かのようだ) — describes her drenched state with a vivid simile ("as if she'd swum in a river"), reinforced by まるで for emphasis.

Q7

今の私の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ない。Ima no watashi no ___ nai.

Tiles: 1 言い表し (iiarawashi)　2 言葉では (kotoba de wa)　3 ようが (you ga)　4 気持ちは (kimochi wa)

4 気持ちは→2 言葉では→1 言い表し→3 ようが

今の私の気持ちは言葉では言い表しようがない。Ima no watashi no kimochi wa kotoba de wa iiarawashiyou ga nai.

EN: There's no way to put my current feelings into words.
HI: मेरी अभी की भावनाओं को शब्दों में बयान करने का कोई तरीक़ा ही नहीं है।
GU: મારી અત્યારની લાગણીઓને શબ્દોમાં વર્ણવવાની કોઈ રીત જ નથી.

Why: Point 2 (〜ようがない) — 言い表しようがない = "there's no way to express (it)," attaching to the ます-stem 言い表し.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four forms share surface similarities — two look like they involve よう, and two involve a negated そう/よう — which the book deliberately tests against each other (see Q1 and Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Vよう＋ではないか~(y)ou de wa nai ka Let's ~! / I will ~! V-よう（volitional）+ ではないか 硬; rhetorical-question form for emphasis, not literal doubt — plain volitional, not the ます-stem. Rallying others, or asserting personal resolve.
V-ます-stem＋ようがない~you ga nai There's no way to ~ V-ます-stem + ようがない Focuses on the absence of a method/means — a different よう than Point 1's volitional. Saying an action is impossible because no method exists.
〜かのようだ~ka no you da As if ~ V/A/na+である/N+である + かのようだ A simile describing something as resembling an untrue state; often paired with まるで. Describing an appearance/impression that isn't literally true.
V-ます-stem＋そうにない~sou ni nai Seems unlikely to ~ V-ます-stem + そうにない A judgment, based on the current situation, that something probably won't happen. Predicting an action likely won't be realized.
Exam trap: 行きよう (ます-stem 行き + よう, Point 2's ようがない) and 行こう (the plain volitional, Point 1's ではないか) look nearly identical on paper but plug into completely different patterns — Q1 above tests exactly this. Separately, そうにない (Point 4) is a fixed idiomatic negative; learners often wrongly substitute the more literal-looking そうじゃない, which isn't the conventional set phrase (Q4 above).
