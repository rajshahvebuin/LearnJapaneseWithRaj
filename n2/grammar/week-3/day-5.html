Notes
Only 3 headline expressions today (the week's divider page lists exactly 3 bullets for 5日目), but きり covers two distinct sub-uses side by side in the source, so this page treats it as points 2 and 3. 練習Ⅰ continues the ○/× true-false format. だらけ and っぱなし are both explicitly marked in the book as 否定的な意味 ("a negative/critical meaning") — neither can be used to describe something positive.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜だらけ

negative nuance
Reading~darake
Meaning (EN)"covered in / full of ~" — an undesirable thing is present in large, unwelcome quantity
Meaning (HI)~ से भरा हुआ (कोई अवांछित चीज़ बहुत ज़्यादा मात्रा में)
Meaning (GU)~ થી ભરેલું (કોઈ અનિચ્છનીય વસ્તુ ખૂબ મોટી માત્રામાં)
Connection (接続)N + だらけ
RegisterNeutral in formality, but always negative in meaning (marked 否定的な意味 in the book) — cannot describe a desirable abundance.
Typical useDescribes visible clutter/mess/damage: ごみだらけ, 傷だらけ, どろだらけ, しわだらけ, 血だらけ, 間違いだらけ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + だらけ間違いだらけmachigai darake
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この手紙の日本語は間違いだらけだ。Kono tegami no nihongo wa machigai darake da. The Japanese in this letter is full of mistakes. इस चिट्ठी की जापानी भाषा में ग़लतियाँ ही ग़लतियाँ हैं। આ પત્રની જાપાનીઝ ભાષામાં ભૂલો જ ભૂલો છે.
ちゃんとたたんでおかなかったから、服がしわだらけになってしまった。Chanto tatande okanakatta kara, fuku ga shiwa darake ni natte shimatta. Because I didn't fold it properly, my clothes ended up completely wrinkled. ठीक से तह न करने की वजह से, कपड़े पूरी तरह से सिलवटों से भर गए। બરાબર ગડી ન વાળવાના કારણે, કપડાં કરચલીઓથી ભરાઈ ગયાં.

Since だらけ always carries a negative meaning, it can never attach to a positive quality like 元気 (energetic/healthy) — 元気だらけ is simply wrong; the correct phrase for "full of energy" is 元気いっぱい (see Q1 in the quiz below).

【2】 〜きり／っきり（Vた＋〜＋Vない）

neutral register
Reading~kiri / ~kkiri (+ negative)
Meaning (EN)"~ and that was the last time — hasn't happened since" — an action was never repeated or followed up on
Meaning (HI)~ किया और उसके बाद से फिर कभी नहीं (वह आख़िरी बार था)
Meaning (GU)~ કર્યું અને પછી ક્યારેય નહીં (તે છેલ્લી વાર હતું)
Connection (接続)Vた + きり／っきり + Vない  |  これ／それ／あれ + （っ）きり
RegisterNeutral; っきり is slightly more emphatic/conversational than きり
Typical useAlways paired with a negative verb describing the lack of any follow-up since that one action.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + きり／っきり + Vない行ったきり帰ってこないitta kiri kaette konai
Demonstrativeこれ／それ／あれ + （っ）きりそれ（っ）きり会っていないsore (k)kiri atte inai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
うちの子は遊びに行ったきり帰ってこない。Uchi no ko wa asobi ni itta kiri kaette konai. My child went out to play and hasn't come back home since. मेरा बच्चा खेलने गया और तब से घर वापस नहीं आया। મારું બાળક રમવા ગયું અને ત્યારથી ઘરે પાછું આવ્યું નથી.
彼とは去年の忘年会に会って、それ（っ）きり会っていない。Kare to wa kyonen no bounenkai ni atte, sore (k)kiri atte inai. I met him at last year's year-end party, and haven't seen him at all since. पिछले साल के ईयर-एंड पार्टी में उससे मिला था, और तब से बिल्कुल नहीं मिला। ગયા વર્ષની યર-એન્ડ પાર્ટીમાં તેને મળ્યો હતો, અને ત્યારથી બિલકુલ મળ્યો નથી.

This sense of きり always attaches to a past ta-form verb, not the te-form — 卒業してきり ("having graduated, ...") is ungrammatical; it must be either 卒業してから (a plain "since graduating") or a genuine Vた+きり construction like 卒業するとき会ったきり ("we met when I graduated, and that was the last time"). See Q5 in the quiz below.

【3】 〜きり／っきり（V-ますstem＋〜）

neutral register
Reading~kiri / ~kkiri (stem form)
Meaning (EN)"exclusively / solely doing ~, without doing anything else" — total, undivided focus on one thing
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ~ करते रहना, और कुछ नहीं करना (पूरी तरह से एक ही काम में लगे रहना)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ~ કરતા રહેવું, બીજું કંઈ નહીં (સંપૂર્ણપણે એક જ કામમાં લાગેલા રહેવું)
Connection (接続)V-ますstem + きり／っきり
RegisterNeutral; also survives as fixed set phrases
Typical useCommon fixed phrases: 一人（っ）きり (=一人だけ, all alone), 二人（っ）きり (=二人だけ, just the two of us), これ（っ）きり (=これだけ／今回だけ, this much/this time only).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ますstem + きり／っきりつき（っ）きりで看病するtsuki(k)kiri de kanbyou suru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
母は入院している父をつき（っ）きりで看病している。Haha wa nyuuin shite iru chichi o tsuki(k)kiri de kanbyou shite iru. My mother is looking after my hospitalized father around the clock, with undivided attention. माँ अस्पताल में भर्ती पिताजी की पूरी तरह से, बिना किसी और काम के, देखभाल कर रही है। મમ્મી હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ પિતાજીની સંપૂર્ણપણે, બીજું કંઈ કર્યા વગર, સંભાળ રાખી રહી છે.
一人（っ）きり（＝一人だけ）　二人（っ）きり（＝二人だけ）　これ（っ）きり（＝これだけ／今回だけ）hitori(k)kiri / futari(k)kiri / kore(k)kiri all alone / just the two of us / this much (only this time) बिल्कुल अकेला / सिर्फ़ हम दोनों / बस इतना ही (सिर्फ़ इस बार) સાવ એકલો / ફક્ત અમે બે / બસ આટલું જ (ફક્ત આ વખતે)

Don't mix this up with Point 2's きり — Point 2 attaches to a Vた past-tense form and always pairs with a negative ("hasn't done X since"), while this Point 3 use attaches to the ます-stem and simply emphasizes exclusive focus, with no negation required.

【4】 〜っぱなし

negative nuance
Reading~ppanashi
Meaning (EN)"left in a continuing state of ~, without proper follow-through" — often implies neglect, endurance, or carelessness
Meaning (HI)~ की हालत में यूँ ही छोड़ देना (अक्सर लापरवाही या सहनशीलता का भाव)
Meaning (GU)~ ની સ્થિતિમાં એમ જ છોડી દેવું (ઘણીવાર બેદરકારી કે સહનશીલતાનો ભાવ)
Connection (接続)V-ますstem + っぱなし
RegisterNeutral in formality, but negative in nuance (marked 否定的な意味 in the book, like だらけ)
Typical useCommon examples: しゃべりっぱなし (talking non-stop), 散らかしっぱなし (left in a mess), 水を出しっぱなし (left the water running), 財布を置きっぱなし (left the wallet lying around).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ますstem + っぱなし立ちっぱなしtachippanashi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
電車が混んでいて、東京まで立ちっぱなしだった。Densha ga konde ite, Tokyo made tachippanashi datta. The train was crowded, so I had to stand the whole way to Tokyo. ट्रेन इतनी भरी थी कि टोक्यो तक पूरे रास्ते खड़े ही रहना पड़ा। ટ્રેન એટલી ભરેલી હતી કે ટોક્યો સુધીના આખા રસ્તે ઊભા જ રહેવું પડ્યું.
テレビをつけっぱなしで寝てしまった。Terebi o tsukeppanashi de nete shimatta. I fell asleep with the TV left on. टीवी चालू छोड़कर ही सो गया। ટીવી ચાલુ છોડીને જ સૂઈ ગયો.

Like だらけ, っぱなし is explicitly marked as carrying a negative/critical nuance in the book — it describes something being left in a state that (usually) shouldn't have been left that way, whether from exhaustion (立ちっぱなし), carelessness (つけっぱなし), or neglect.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, mark ○ or × and correct any ×) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain numbered blanks), both from p.55. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.57.

練習Ⅰ — mark ○ (correct) or × (incorrect), and fix any ×

Q1

子どもたちは元気だらけで遊んでいる。（　）Kodomotachi wa genki darake de asonde iru. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 元気だらけで→元気いっぱいでgenki darake de → genki ippai de
EN (corrected): The children are playing full of energy.
HI: बच्चे पूरी ऊर्जा के साथ खेल रहे हैं।
GU: બાળકો સંપૂર્ણ ઉર્જા સાથે રમી રહ્યાં છે.

Why: だらけ is reserved for negative/undesirable abundance (dirt, mistakes, wounds). 元気 is a positive quality, so it cannot pair with だらけ — the correct phrase is 元気いっぱい ("full of energy").

Q2

北海道へ行ったのは1度きりです。（　）Hokkaidou e itta no wa ichido kiri desu. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: I've only been to Hokkaido once.
HI: होक्काइदो सिर्फ़ एक बार गया हूँ।
GU: હોકાઇડો ફક્ત એક જ વાર ગયો છું.

Why: 一度きり (=一度だけ) is a correct, common fixed use of きり meaning "only once."

Q3

弟の部屋はゴミのだらけだ。（　）Otouto no heya wa gomi no darake da. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: ゴミのだらけ→ゴミだらけgomi no darake → gomi darake
EN (corrected): My younger brother's room is full of trash.
HI: छोटे भाई का कमरा कचरे से भरा हुआ है।
GU: નાના ભાઈનો રૂમ કચરાથી ભરેલો છે.

Why: だらけ attaches directly to a bare noun, with no の in between — the の in ゴミのだらけ is an incorrect insertion.

Q4

海外旅行に行っても日本人だらけでがっかりした。（　）Kaigai ryokou ni itte mo nihonjin darake de gakkari shita. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: Even when I went on an overseas trip, it was disappointingly full of Japanese people.
HI: विदेश यात्रा पर जाने पर भी, हर तरफ़ जापानी लोग ही थे, जिससे निराशा हुई।
GU: વિદેશ પ્રવાસ પર જવા છતાં, ચારેબાજુ જાપાની લોકો જ હતા, જેનાથી નિરાશા થઈ.

Why: 日本人だらけ is correctly attached directly to the noun, and the "unwanted overabundance" nuance fits the speaker's disappointment.

Q5

学校を卒業してきり彼女とは会っていない。（　）Gakkou o sotsugyou shite kiri kanojo to wa atte inai. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 卒業してきり→卒業してから／卒業するとき会ったきりsotsugyou shite kiri → sotsugyou shite kara / sotsugyou suru toki atta kiri
EN (corrected): I haven't seen her since graduating from school. / I met her when I graduated, and that was the last time.
HI: स्कूल से स्नातक होने के बाद से उससे नहीं मिला। / स्नातक होते समय उससे मिला था, और वह आख़िरी बार था।
GU: શાળામાંથી સ્નાતક થયા પછીથી તેને મળ્યો નથી. / સ્નાતક થતી વખતે તેને મળ્યો હતો, અને તે છેલ્લી વાર હતું.

Why: The Point 2 sense of きり must attach to a Vた past-tense verb, not the te-form 卒業して — the sentence must either drop きり and use 卒業してから, or keep きり but attach it to a genuine past-tense verb like 会った (会ったきり).

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

今日が最後だ。もう、お酒は ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ つもりだ。Kyou ga saigo da. Mou, osake wa ___ tsumori da.

Tiles: 1 で (de)　2 きり (kiri)　3 これ (kore)　4 やめる (yameru)

3 これ→2 きり→1 で→4 やめる

今日が最後だ。もう、お酒はこれきりでやめるつもりだ。Kyou ga saigo da. Mou, osake wa kore kiri de yameru tsumori da.

EN: Today is the last time. I intend to quit drinking after this, once and for all.
HI: आज आख़िरी दिन है। अब, मैं शराब हमेशा के लिए छोड़ने का इरादा रखता हूँ, बस इतना ही।
GU: આજે છેલ્લો દિવસ છે. હવે, હું દારૂ કાયમ માટે છોડવાનો ઇરાદો રાખું છું, બસ આટલું જ.

Why: Built on Point 3's きり — これきりで (with this being the only/last one) + やめる (quit), using the "exclusively/only this" nuance.

Q7

窓を ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 風邪を引いた。Mado o ___ kaze o hiita.

Tiles: 1 っぱなし (ppanashi)　2 寝たら (netara)　3 にして (ni shite)　4 開け (ake)

4 開け→1 っぱなし→3 にして→2 寝たら

窓を開けっぱなしにして寝たら、風邪を引いた。Mado o akeppanashi ni shite netara, kaze o hiita.

EN: I slept with the window left wide open, and I caught a cold.
HI: खिड़की खुली छोड़कर सोया, तो सर्दी लग गई।
GU: બારી ખુલ્લી છોડીને સૂઈ ગયો, તો શરદી થઈ ગઈ.

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜っぱなし) — 開けっぱなしにして (left it open) + 寝たら (when I slept) sets up the negative consequence (catching a cold) from neglecting to close the window.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

だらけ, きり, and っぱなし all describe an undesired persistent state, which makes them easy to mix up. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜だらけ~darake Covered in / full of ~ N + だらけ Describes visible clutter/mess covering a surface or object; always negative; never for positive traits. Something is physically covered in an undesirable substance or has an undesirable quality in large quantity.
〜きり／っきり（Vた）~kiri (Vta) ~, and that was the last time Vた + きり + Vない Marks that no follow-up action has happened since a single past event; always with a negative verb. Something happened once and was never repeated or followed up on.
〜きり／っきり（V-stem）~kiri (V-stem) Exclusively / solely doing ~ V-ますstem + きり Marks total, undivided focus on one activity, with no built-in negation required. Someone is devoting all their attention/time to just one thing.
〜っぱなし~ppanashi Left in a continuing state of ~ V-ますstem + っぱなし Describes an action left unfinished/uncorrected without proper follow-through; always negative. Something was left running, open, or in a state that should have been changed but wasn't.
Exam trap: だらけ and っぱなし are both explicitly negative-only in this book — neither can describe a positive trait (Q1 above is exactly this trap: 元気だらけ is wrong because 元気 is positive). Separately, きり has two unrelated attachment shapes depending on meaning: Vた + きり + negative verb ("hasn't happened since," Point 2) versus V-ますstem + きり ("exclusively," Point 3) — mixing up which form of the preceding verb to use (Q5) is the other major trap today.
