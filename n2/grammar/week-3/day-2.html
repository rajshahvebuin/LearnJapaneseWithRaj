1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜わけだ

neutral register
Reading~wake da
Meaning (EN)"that would explain it / no wonder ~ / so that means ~" — a logical conclusion drawn from a preceding fact
Meaning (HI)तो इसीलिए ~ / मतलब यह कि ~ (किसी तथ्य से निकाला गया तार्किक निष्कर्ष)
Meaning (GU)તો એટલે જ ~ / એટલે કે ~ (કોઈ હકીકત પરથી કાઢેલું તાર્કિક તારણ)
Connection (接続)Aい／V(plain)／Vている／Vていた／Vられる／Vさせる + わけだ  |  Naな + わけだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useUsed when a fact you just learned makes an earlier observation suddenly make sense, or when stating the logical/arithmetic result of a calculation.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + わけだ寒いわけだsamui wake da
な-adjectiveNaな + わけだ静かなわけだshizuka na wake da
Verb (plain/progressive/passive/causative)Vる／Vている／Vていた／Vられる／Vさせる + わけだ8千円になるわけだhassen'en ni naru wake da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
寒いわけだ。雪が降っている。Samui wake da. Yuki ga futte iru. No wonder it's cold. It's snowing. ठंड तो होगी ही। बर्फ़ गिर रही है। ઠંડી તો હોય જ. બરફ પડી રહ્યો છે.
定価が1万円で、2割引きだから8千円になるわけだ。Teika ga ichiman'en de, niwaribiki dakara hassen'en ni naru wake da. The list price is 10,000 yen, and with a 20% discount, that comes out to 8,000 yen. असली दाम 10,000 येन है, और 20% छूट के साथ यह 8,000 येन बनता है। મૂળ ભાવ 10,000 યેન છે, અને 20% ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ સાથે તે 8,000 યેન થાય છે.

わけだ is the "aha, that explains it" form — it doesn't introduce new information so much as connect a fact you already knew to a conclusion that logically follows from it. It also works for plain arithmetic ("that comes out to...").

【2】 〜わけではない／〜わけでもない

neutral register
Reading~wake dewa nai / ~wake demo nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not that ~ / that doesn't mean ~" — a partial, softened negation (not a full denial)
Meaning (HI)ऐसा नहीं है कि ~ (पूरी तरह से इनकार नहीं, सिर्फ आंशिक खंडन)
Meaning (GU)એવું નથી કે ~ (સંપૂર્ણ ઇનકાર નહીં, ફક્ત આંશિક ખંડન)
Connection (接続)Aい／V(plain)／Vている／Vていた／Vられる／Vさせる + わけではない  |  Naな + わけではない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSoftens or qualifies a statement that might otherwise sound like a blanket denial — "it's not 100% that ~," leaving room for nuance.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (negative base)Aく-ない + わけではないほしくないわけではないhoshikunai wake dewa nai
Verb (progressive)Vている + わけでもない見ているわけでもないmite iru wake demo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ほしくないわけではないけれど、お金がないから買えないんです。Hoshikunai wake dewa nai keredo, okane ga nai kara kaenai n desu. It's not that I don't want it — I just can't afford to buy it. ऐसा नहीं है कि मुझे नहीं चाहिए, बस पैसे नहीं हैं इसलिए नहीं खरीद सकता। એવું નથી કે મારે નથી જોઈતું, બસ પૈસા નથી એટલે ખરીદી શકતો નથી.
テレビはつけてあるが、見ているわけでもない。Terebi wa tsukete aru ga, mite iru wake demo nai. The TV is on, but that doesn't mean I'm actually watching it. टीवी चल रहा है, लेकिन इसका मतलब यह नहीं कि मैं उसे देख भी रहा हूँ। ટીવી ચાલુ છે, પણ એનો અર્થ એ નથી કે હું ખરેખર જોઈ રહ્યો છું.

Note the double negative: ほしくないわけではない = "not [not-wanting]" = "I do want it, at least somewhat." Keep this apart from わけがない (point 3), which is a much stronger, flatter denial.

【3】 〜わけがない／〜わけはない

neutral register
Reading~wake ga nai / ~wake wa nai
Meaning (EN)"there's no way that ~ / absolutely not ~" — a strong, confident denial of possibility
Meaning (HI)ऐसा हो ही नहीं सकता कि ~ (पूरे यक़ीन के साथ इनकार)
Meaning (GU)એવું બની જ ન શકે કે ~ (સંપૂર્ણ વિશ્વાસ સાથે ઇનકાર)
Connection (接続)Aい／V(plain)／Vている／Vていた／Vられる／Vさせる + わけがない  |  Naな + わけはない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useExpresses the speaker's strong conviction that something is impossible or untrue — much stronger than a plain negative sentence.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)Vる + わけがないするわけがないsuru wake ga nai
い-adjective (negative)Aく-ない + わけがないうれしくないわけがないureshikunai wake ga nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
うちの子がそんなことをするわけがない。Uchi no ko ga sonna koto o suru wake ga nai. There's no way my child would do such a thing. मेरा बच्चा ऐसा काम कर ही नहीं सकता। મારું બાળક એવું કામ કરી જ ન શકે.
ほめられて、うれしくないわけがない。Homerarete, ureshikunai wake ga nai. There's no way I wouldn't be happy about being praised. तारीफ़ होने पर खुश न होना नामुमकिन है। વખાણ થાય ત્યારે ખુશ ન થવું અશક્ય છે.

わけがない is much more emphatic than a simple ～ない sentence — it flatly rules out the possibility. Compare うれしくないわけがない (point 3, "there's absolutely no way I'm not happy" = "I'm definitely happy") with ほしくないわけではない (point 2, "it's not that I don't want it, but...").

【4】 〜わけにはいかない／〜わけにもいかない

neutral register
Reading~wake ni wa ikanai / ~wake ni mo ikanai
Meaning (EN)"can't afford to ~ / cannot ~ (due to social, moral, or circumstantial reasons)"
Meaning (HI)सामाजिक/नैतिक/परिस्थितिजन्य कारणों से ~ नहीं कर सकते
Meaning (GU)સામાજિક/નૈતિક/સંજોગોના કારણે ~ કરી શકાય નહીં
Connection (接続)Vる／Vない／Vている／Vさせる + わけにはいかない／わけにもいかない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useUnlike わけがない (a logical impossibility), this pattern is about a socially/morally imposed constraint — the action is physically possible, but circumstances don't allow it.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary)Vる + わけにはいかない休むわけにはいかないyasumu wake ni wa ikanai
Verb (negative)Vない + わけにはいかない払わないわけにはいかないharawanai wake ni wa ikanai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
重要な会議があるので、病気でも会社を休むわけにはいかない。Juuyou na kaigi ga aru node, byouki demo kaisha o yasumu wake ni wa ikanai. Since there's an important meeting, I can't afford to take the day off work even if I'm sick. ज़रूरी मीटिंग है, इसलिए बीमार होने पर भी ऑफ़िस से छुट्टी नहीं ले सकता। મહત્વની મીટિંગ છે, એટલે બીમાર હોવા છતાં ઓફિસમાંથી રજા ન લઈ શકાય.
税金は、高くても払わないわけにはいかない。Zeikin wa, takakute mo harawanai wake ni wa ikanai. Even if taxes are high, there's no getting out of paying them. टैक्स चाहे कितना भी ज़्यादा हो, बिना चुकाए कोई चारा नहीं। ટેક્સ ગમે તેટલો ઊંચો હોય, ચૂકવ્યા વગર છૂટકો નથી.

払わないわけにはいかない is a double negative ("cannot [not-pay]") meaning "must pay." This whole point pairs naturally with わけがない: わけがない says something is logically impossible, while わけにはいかない says something is not permitted/acceptable given the circumstances, even though it's physically possible.

Q1

もう12時か。おなかがすくわけ（　）。Mou juuni-ji ka. Onaka ga suku wake (___).

Full sentence: もう12時か。おなかがすくわけだ。Mou juuni-ji ka. Onaka ga suku wake da.
EN: It's already noon? No wonder I'm hungry.
HI: पहले ही 12 बज गए? भूख लगना तो लाज़मी है।
GU: અરે, 12 વાગી ગયા? ભૂખ લાગવી તો સ્વાભાવિક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだdathat explains it / no wonderतो यह लाज़मी हैતો આ સ્વાભાવિક છે
bではないdewa nai(wrong nuance — this isn't a partial denial)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)

Why: Realizing it's noon explains the hunger — that's exactly わけだ's "that explains it" logic, not a denial.

Q2

あの学生はいつも授業に来ないから、試験の結果がいいわけ（　）。Ano gakusei wa itsumo jugyou ni konai kara, shiken no kekka ga ii wake (___).

Full sentence: あの学生はいつも授業に来ないから、試験の結果がいいわけがない。Ano gakusei wa itsumo jugyou ni konai kara, shiken no kekka ga ii wake ga nai.
EN: That student never comes to class, so there's no way their exam results would be good.
HI: वह छात्र हमेशा क्लास नहीं आता, इसलिए परीक्षा के अच्छे नतीजे आना नामुमकिन है।
GU: તે વિદ્યાર્થી ક્યારેય વર્ગમાં આવતો નથી, એટલે પરીક્ષાનું પરિણામ સારું આવવું અશક્ય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aではないdewa nai(too weak — this needs a strong denial)(यह भाव कमज़ोर है)(આ ભાવ નબળો છે)
bがないga naithere's no way (strong denial)नामुमकिन हैઅશક્ય છે

Why: Never attending class logically rules out a good result — this calls for the strong denial わけがない, not the softer わけではない.

Q3

用事があるので、台風でも出かけないわけには（　）。Youji ga aru node, taifuu demo dekakenai wake ni wa (___).

Full sentence: 用事があるので、台風でも出かけないわけにはいかない。Youji ga aru node, taifuu demo dekakenai wake ni wa ikanai.
EN: I have somewhere to be, so even in a typhoon, I can't afford not to go out.
HI: काम है, इसलिए तूफ़ान में भी बाहर न निकलना मुमकिन नहीं।
GU: કામ છે, એટલે તોફાનમાં પણ બહાર ન નીકળવું શક્ય નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいかないikanaicorrect fixed form of the patternयही सही रूप हैઆ જ સાચું સ્વરૂપ છે
bいけないikenai(wrong verb — the fixed idiom uses 行く, not いける)(ग़लत क्रिया)(ખોટી ક્રિયાપદ)

Why: The idiom is fixed as わけにはいかない (from 行く), not わけにはいけない — a common spelling/conjugation trap.

Q4

毎日牛乳を飲んでいるのは体にいいからで、特に好きなわけ（　）。Mainichi gyuunyuu o nonde iru no wa karada ni ii kara de, toku ni suki na wake (___).

Full sentence: 毎日牛乳を飲んでいるのは体にいいからで、特に好きなわけではない。Mainichi gyuunyuu o nonde iru no wa karada ni ii kara de, toku ni suki na wake dewa nai.
EN: The reason I drink milk every day is that it's good for me — it's not that I especially like it.
HI: रोज़ दूध पीने की वजह यह है कि यह सेहत के लिए अच्छा है, ऐसा नहीं कि मुझे यह बहुत पसंद है।
GU: રોજ દૂધ પીવાનું કારણ એ છે કે તે શરીર માટે સારું છે, એવું નથી કે મને ખાસ ગમે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aではないdewa naiit's not that (partial denial)ऐसा नहीं किએવું નથી કે
bがないga nai(too strong — this isn't ruling out liking it entirely)(यह भाव बहुत तेज़ है)(આ ભાવ ખૂબ મજબૂત છે)

HI: बैटरी नहीं है, तो यह चलेगा ही नहीं।
GU: બેટરી નથી, એટલે તે ચાલશે જ નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

仕事がこんなにたくさんあるんです。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿。Shigoto ga konna ni takusan aru n desu. ___.

Tiles: 1 わけが (wake ga)　2 忙しく (isogashiku)　3 ありません (arimasen)　4 ない (nai)

2 忙しく→4 ない→1 わけが→3 ありません

仕事がこんなにたくさんあるんです。忙しくないわけがありません。Shigoto ga konna ni takusan aru n desu. Isogashikunai wake ga arimasen.

EN: There's so much work — there's no way I'm not busy.
HI: इतना सारा काम है — मैं व्यस्त न होऊँ, यह मुमकिन ही नहीं।
GU: આટલું બધું કામ છે — હું વ્યસ્ત ન હોઉં, એ શક્ય જ નથી.

Why: Built on Point 3 (〜わけがない), in its polite negative form わけがありません — 忙しくない (not busy) + わけがありません (there's no way that's true) = "there's no way I'm not busy."

Q7

手紙を出し忘れていたと父は言った。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿だ。Tegami o dashi wasurete ita to chichi wa itta. ___ da.

Tiles: 1 いくら (ikura)　2 わけ (wake)　3 届かない (todokanai)　4 待っても (matte mo)

1 いくら→4 待っても→3 届かない→2 わけ

手紙を出し忘れていたと父は言った。いくら待っても届かないわけだ。Tegami o dashi wasurete ita to chichi wa itta. Ikura matte mo todokanai wake da.

EN: My father said he forgot to mail the letter. No wonder it never arrives no matter how long I wait.
HI: पिताजी ने कहा कि वे चिट्ठी भेजना भूल गए। तो इसीलिए वह चाहे जितना इंतज़ार करूँ, कभी नहीं पहुँचती।
GU: પિતાજીએ કહ્યું કે તેઓ પત્ર મોકલવાનું ભૂલી ગયા. તો એટલે જ ગમે તેટલી રાહ જોઉં, તે ક્યારેય પહોંચતો નથી.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜わけだ) — learning that the letter was never mailed explains why waiting never results in it arriving.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points today are built on わけ, which is exactly why the book tests them against each other on the same day (see every question above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜わけだ~wake da That explains it / no wonder A／V(plain) + わけだ Draws a logical conclusion from an already-known fact; also used for arithmetic results. A new piece of information makes an earlier observation click into place.
〜わけではない~wake dewa nai It's not that ~ A／V(plain) + わけではない A soft, partial negation — leaves room for nuance, does not fully deny. You want to qualify a statement without a blanket denial.
〜わけがない~wake ga nai There's no way that ~ A／V(plain) + わけがない A strong, confident denial of logical possibility. You're certain something is impossible or untrue.
〜わけにはいかない~wake ni wa ikanai Can't afford to / cannot (due to circumstance) Vる／Vない + わけにはいかない Not a logical impossibility but a social/moral/circumstantial constraint — the action is physically possible but not permitted. Duty, obligation, or social pressure makes an otherwise-possible action unacceptable.
Exam trap: わけがない and わけにはいかない both sound like "cannot," but わけがない denies that something CAN happen (logical/physical impossibility — "there's no way it would work without batteries"), while わけにはいかない says something technically CAN happen but circumstances forbid it ("I could skip work, but my job won't allow it"). When a question gives you this choice, check whether the sentence is about pure possibility or about permission/obligation.
