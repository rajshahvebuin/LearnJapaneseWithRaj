3日目 — かじったとたん (Kajitta totan)

Notes
Deviation from the general pattern: 練習Ⅰ on this day (and the next two days) is not the "circle a or b" format used on Day 1/2/6 — it's a true/false format: mark each sentence ○ (correct) or × (incorrect), and if ×, supply the fix. All four points here describe an event followed immediately/eventually by a consequence, and share strict tense/form requirements that this exercise tests directly.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜たとたん（に）

neutral register
Reading~ta totan (ni)
Meaning (EN)"the moment ~, immediately..." — an unexpected, involuntary consequence follows instantly
Meaning (HI)~ होते ही, तुरंत... (अनजाने में हुआ तुरंत परिणाम)
Meaning (GU)~ થતાં જ, તરત જ... (અનાયાસે થયેલું તાત્કાલિક પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)Vた + とたん(に)  |  Vた。そのとたん〜
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe clause after とたん must describe something involuntary/unplanned — a request, command, or stated intention cannot follow it. Marked ダメ (wrong) in the book: 駅に着いたとたん、電話します／電話してください — both are ungrammatical because they're intentional/directive, not a spontaneous reaction.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + とたん(に)開けたとたんaketa totan
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
窓を開けたとたん、強い風が入ってきた。Mado o aketa totan, tsuyoi kaze ga haitte kita. The moment I opened the window, a strong wind blew in. खिड़की खोलते ही, तेज़ हवा अंदर आ गई। બારી ખોલતાં જ, જોરદાર પવન અંદર આવ્યો.
お酒を飲んだとたん、顔が赤くなった。Osake o nonda totan, kao ga akaku natta. The moment I drank the alcohol, my face turned red. शराब पीते ही, मेरा चेहरा लाल हो गया। દારૂ પીતાં જ, મારો ચહેરો લાલ થઈ ગયો.

Hard rule for exams: とたん needs an unplanned, involuntary result — never a command, request, or stated intention. If the second clause is something the subject deliberately chooses to do next, とたん is the wrong choice (use てから or たら instead).

【2】 〜たあげく（に）

neutral register
Reading~ta ageku (ni)
Meaning (EN)"after (a lot of) ~, in the end..." — a troublesome or drawn-out process finally leads to a (often negative) result
Meaning (HI)बहुत ~ करने के बाद, आख़िरकार... (मुश्किल भरी प्रक्रिया के बाद अक्सर नकारात्मक परिणाम)
Meaning (GU)ઘણું ~ કર્યા પછી, છેવટે... (મુશ્કેલ પ્રક્રિયા પછી ઘણીવાર નકારાત્મક પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)Vた + あげく(に)  |  Nの／するの + あげく(の N)
RegisterNeutral, but always implies the preceding process was troublesome
Typical useRequires a た-form verb before it — never the plain dictionary form, even though the process being described took time.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + あげく(に)騒いだあげくsawaida ageku
Noun/する-verbNの／するの + あげく(の N)苦労のあげくkurou no ageku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は酔っ払って騒いだあげく、寝てしまった。Kare wa yopparatte sawaida ageku, nete shimatta. He got drunk and caused a ruckus, and in the end, fell asleep. वह नशे में धुत होकर हंगामा करता रहा, और आख़िर में सो गया। તે નશામાં ધૂત થઈને હંગામો કરતો રહ્યો, અને છેવટે સૂઈ ગયો.
何度も手術したあげく、治らないと言われた。Nando mo shujutsu shita ageku, naoranai to iwareta. After many surgeries, I was finally told it couldn't be cured. कई बार सर्जरी कराने के बाद भी, आख़िर में कहा गया कि यह ठीक नहीं हो सकता। ઘણી વાર સર્જરી કરાવ્યા પછી પણ, છેવટે કહેવામાં આવ્યું કે તે સાજું નહીં થાય.

Never write the plain-form 行くあげく — it must be 行ったあげく. This is a common exam trap (see Q5 in the quiz below), because あげく always looks back at a completed troublesome process.

【3】 〜た末（に）

neutral register
Reading~ta sue (ni)
Meaning (EN)"after ~ (much deliberation/effort), finally..." — a lengthy process reaches its conclusion
Meaning (HI)बहुत ~ के बाद, आख़िरकार... (लंबी प्रक्रिया/सोच-विचार के बाद निष्कर्ष)
Meaning (GU)ઘણું ~ કર્યા પછી, છેવટે... (લાંબી પ્રક્રિયા/વિચારણા પછીનું તારણ)
Connection (接続)Vた + 末(に)  |  Nの／するの + 末(の N)
RegisterNeutral to slightly formal — more neutral in tone than あげく
Typical useUnlike あげく, 末 does not necessarily carry a negative or troublesome nuance — it simply marks that a long process (thinking, discussing, struggling) led to a final outcome, positive or negative.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + 末(に)悩んだ末nayanda sue
Noun/する-verbNの／するの + 末(の N)話し合いの末hanashiai no sue
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
悩んだ末、進学ではなく就職することにした。Nayanda sue, shingaku dewa naku shuushoku suru koto ni shita. After much agonizing, he decided to get a job instead of continuing his studies. बहुत सोच-विचार के बाद, उसने आगे पढ़ाई करने के बजाय नौकरी करने का फैसला किया। ઘણી વિચારણા પછી, તેણે આગળ ભણવાને બદલે નોકરી કરવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.
住民との話し合いの末、その建物の建設は中止となった。Juumin to no hanashiai no sue, sono tatemono no kensetsu wa chuushi to natta. After discussions with the residents, construction of the building was called off. निवासियों से बातचीत के बाद, उस इमारत का निर्माण रोक दिया गया। રહેવાસીઓ સાથે ચર્ચા પછી, તે ઇમારતનું બાંધકામ બંધ કરવામાં આવ્યું.

Compare with あげく: 末 is the neutral "after all that effort/time, finally..." while あげく leans negative and implies the process itself was a hassle. 考えた末 (after thinking it over) sounds fine either way, but 待たされたあげく (after being made to wait) is inherently a complaint.

【4】 〜たかと思ったら／〜たと思うと

neutral register
Reading~ta ka to omottara / ~ta to omou to
Meaning (EN)"no sooner had ~ than..." — two events happen in surprisingly quick succession
Meaning (HI)~ होने के तुरंत बाद ही... (दो घटनाएँ हैरानी भरी तेज़ी से एक के बाद एक होती हैं)
Meaning (GU)~ થયું ન થયું ત્યાં જ... (બે ઘટનાઓ આશ્ચર્યજનક ઝડપથી બને છે)
Connection (接続)Vたかと + 思ったら／思うと
RegisterNeutral, conversational-leaning
Typical useExpresses the speaker's surprise at how briefly the first event lasted before the second one happened.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + かと思ったら来たかと思ったらkita ka to omottara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人は忙しい人で、来たかと思ったら、もう帰ってしまった。Ano hito wa isogashii hito de, kita ka to omottara, mou kaette shimatta. He's a busy person — no sooner had he arrived than he'd already left again. वह बहुत व्यस्त इंसान है — आते ही, वह फिर से चला भी गया। તે ખૂબ વ્યસ્ત વ્યક્તિ છે — આવ્યો ન આવ્યો ત્યાં જ, તે ફરી ચાલ્યો પણ ગયો.
花が咲いたかと思ったら、もう散ってしまった。Hana ga saita ka to omottara, mou chitte shimatta. The flowers had barely bloomed before they'd already scattered. फूल खिले ही थे कि वे झड़ भी गए। ફૂલ ખીલ્યાં ન ખીલ્યાં ત્યાં જ, તે ખરી પણ ગયાં.

This pattern is purely descriptive/emotive (surprise at the pace of events) — unlike とたん, it isn't blocked from being followed by a stative continuation like もう帰ってしまった, and it doesn't carry あげく/末's implication of a drawn-out process.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, mark ○ or × and correct any ×) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain numbered blanks), both from p.51. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.53.

練習Ⅰ — mark ○ (correct) or × (incorrect), and fix any ×

Q1

この薬は食べたとんたに、飲んでください。（　）Kono kusuri wa tabeta tanta ni, nonde kudasai. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 食べたら／食べてすぐにtabetara / tabete sugu ni
EN (corrected): Please take this medicine right after you eat.
HI: यह दवा खाना खाने के तुरंत बाद लें।
GU: આ દવા જમ્યા પછી તરત જ લો.

Why: とたん cannot be followed by a request/command like 飲んでください — the clause after とたん must be an involuntary reaction, not an instruction. Use たら or てすぐに instead when giving directions.

Q2

その石けんを使ったとたん、手がかゆくなった。（　）Sono sekken o tsukatta totan, te ga kayuku natta. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: The moment I used that soap, my hands started itching.
HI: उस साबुन का इस्तेमाल करते ही, मेरे हाथों में खुजली होने लगी।
GU: તે સાબુનો ઉપયોગ કરતાં જ, મારા હાથમાં ખંજવાળ આવવા લાગી.

Why: An itchy reaction is involuntary, which is exactly what とたん requires.

Q3

考えた末、手術しないことにしました。（　）Kangaeta sue, shujutsu shinai koto ni shimashita. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: After much thought, I decided not to have the surgery.
HI: बहुत सोच-विचार के बाद, मैंने सर्जरी न कराने का फैसला किया।
GU: ઘણી વિચારણા પછી, મેં સર્જરી ન કરાવવાનું નક્કી કર્યું.

Why: 考えた (past form) + 末 correctly marks the conclusion of a deliberation process.

Q4

待たされたあげく、まずい料理を食べさせられた。（　）Matasareta ageku, mazui ryouri o tabesaserareta. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: After being made to wait, I was forced to eat a bad meal (to top it off).
HI: इंतज़ार करवाने के बाद, मुझे मजबूरन बेस्वाद खाना खाना पड़ा।
GU: રાહ જોવડાવ્યા પછી, મારે ફરજિયાત ખરાબ ખાવાનું ખાવું પડ્યું.

Why: 待たされた (past passive-causative) + あげく correctly marks a troublesome wait leading to yet another bad outcome — exactly あげく's negative-buildup nuance.

Q5

あちこちの病院へ行くあげく、やっと元気になった。（　）Achikochi no byouin e iku ageku, yatto genki ni natta. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 行く→行ったiku → itta
EN (corrected): After going from hospital to hospital, I finally got better.
HI: कई अस्पतालों में जाने के बाद, आख़िरकार मैं ठीक हो गया।
GU: ઘણી હોસ્પિટલોમાં ગયા પછી, છેવટે હું સાજો થયો.

Why: あげく must attach to the past ta-form, never the plain dictionary form — 行くあげく is ungrammatical; it must be 行ったあげく.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

あの二人、さっきまで ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ もう仲良くしている。Ano futari, sakki made ___ mou nakayoku shite iru.

Tiles: 1 けんか (kenka)　2 かと (ka to)　3 思ったら (omottara)　4 していた (shite ita)

1 けんか→4 していた→2 かと→3 思ったら

あの二人、さっきまでけんかしていたかと思ったら、もう仲良くしている。Ano futari, sakki made kenka shite ita ka to omottara, mou nakayoku shite iru.

EN: Those two were just fighting a moment ago — before I knew it, they're already getting along.
HI: वे दोनों अभी-अभी लड़ रहे थे, और अब देखो, दोस्ती से रहने लगे हैं।
GU: તે બંને હમણાં જ ઝઘડી રહ્યા હતા, અને હવે જુઓ, સારા મિત્રોની જેમ રહે છે.

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜たかと思ったら) — けんかしていた (were fighting) + かと思ったら (no sooner had I thought that than...) sets up the surprising quick reversal.

Q7

彼は約束の時間に ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿しなかった。Kare wa yakusoku no jikan ni ___ shinakatta.

Tiles: 1 来た (kita)　2 遅れて (okurete)　3 あげく (ageku)　4 あやまりも (ayamari mo)

2 遅れて→1 来た→3 あげく→4 あやまりも

彼は約束の時間に遅れて来たあげく、あやまりもしなかった。Kare wa yakusoku no jikan ni okurete kita ageku, ayamari mo shinakatta.

EN: He showed up late for our appointment, and on top of that, didn't even apologize.
HI: वह तय समय पर देर से आया, और ऊपर से माफ़ी भी नहीं मांगी।
GU: તે નક્કી કરેલા સમયે મોડો આવ્યો, અને ઉપરથી માફી પણ ન માંગી.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜たあげく) — 遅れて来た (came late) + あげく (and on top of that) + あやまりもしなかった (didn't even apologize) — the classic あげく "insult to injury" structure.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points describe "event → consequence," but they differ sharply in timing, required tense, and emotional coloring — exactly what today's ○/× exercise is testing. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜たとたん~ta totan The moment ~, immediately... Vた + とたん(に) Instant, involuntary consequence — cannot be followed by a command/request/intention. Something unexpected happened right after another action, with no gap and no planning.
〜たあげく~ta ageku After (a lot of) ~, in the end... Vた + あげく(に) Implies the process was troublesome; always requires the た-form, never plain form. A long, difficult process finally reaches a (often disappointing) result.
〜た末~ta sue After ~, finally... Vた + 末(に) Neutral in tone — no built-in negative nuance, unlike あげく. A long process (thought, discussion, effort) reaches its natural conclusion.
〜たかと思ったら~ta ka to omottara No sooner had ~ than... Vたかと + 思ったら／思うと Expresses surprise at how quickly two events followed each other; no restriction on the second clause's voluntariness. You want to highlight the surprisingly short gap between two events.
Exam trap: とたん, あげく, and 末 all attach after a た-form verb, but only とたん is banned from being followed by an intentional command/request (Q1). あげく and 末 both describe a drawn-out process, but あげく leans negative/troublesome while 末 stays neutral — compare 待たされたあげく (an annoyance) with 考えた末 (a considered decision, could go either way). Also remember: あげく never attaches to a plain dictionary-form verb (Q5) — it must be the past ta-form even when describing a process that took time.
