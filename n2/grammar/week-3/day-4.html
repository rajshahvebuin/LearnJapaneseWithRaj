Notes
練習Ⅰ continues in the ○/× true-false format from Day 3. All four points share ところ／どころ, which is why the book bundles them — the same root word covers a temporal-aspect marker, a discovery marker, and two "far from it" negation patterns.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ところ（を）

neutral / polite register
Reading~tokoro (o)
Meaning (EN)"while you're in the middle of ~" (polite acknowledgement of an inconvenient moment) or "just at the point of ~ / just now ~ing" depending on the preceding verb's tense
Meaning (HI)ऐसे समय में जब आप ~ कर रहे हैं (विनम्रता से किसी असुविधाजनक पल का ज़िक्र) / ठीक उसी समय जब...
Meaning (GU)તમે ~ કરી રહ્યા છો ત્યારે (નમ્રતાથી અસુવિધાજનક ક્ષણનો ઉલ્લેખ) / બરાબર તે જ સમયે જ્યારે...
Connection (接続)Aい／Nの／Vた／Vている／Vていた + ところ(に／へ／を／だ)
RegisterPolite when used as a set apology formula (お忙しいところ)
Typical useThe tense of the preceding verb changes the meaning: Vている/Vていた+ところ = "right in the middle of ~ing," Vる (plain, non-past)+ところ = "just about to ~," Vた+ところ = "just finished ~ing" (see Point 2 for the related "as a result of ~" reading of Vた+ところ).
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + ところ(を)お忙しいところ（を）oisogashii tokoro (o)
Verb (progressive/past-progressive)Vている／Vていた + ところだ会いたいと思っていたところですaitai to omotte ita tokoro desu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お忙しいところ（を）すみません。Oisogashii tokoro (o) sumimasen. Sorry to bother you while you're busy. आपके व्यस्त समय में बाधा डालने के लिए माफ़ी चाहता हूँ। તમે વ્યસ્ત હોવ ત્યારે ખલેલ પહોંચાડવા બદલ માફી.
会いたいと思っていたところです。Aitai to omotte ita tokoro desu. I was just thinking I wanted to see you. मैं अभी-अभी सोच रहा था कि आपसे मिलना चाहता हूँ। હું હમણાં જ વિચારી રહ્યો હતો કે તમને મળવા માંગું છું.

お忙しいところ(を)すみません is a fixed polite formula worth memorizing whole — it's the standard way to preface a request to someone who looks busy. Keep this "aspect" sense of ところ separate from Point 2's "as a result of doing ~" sense, even though both use Vた + ところ on the surface.

【2】 〜たところ

neutral register
Reading~ta tokoro
Meaning (EN)"when I did ~, (as a result/it turned out)..." — a discovery marker, similar to てみたら
Meaning (HI)जब मैंने ~ किया, तो (परिणामस्वरूप)... (कोई खोज/नतीजा)
Meaning (GU)જ્યારે મેં ~ કર્યું, ત્યારે (પરિણામે)... (કોઈ શોધ/નિષ્કર્ષ)
Connection (接続)Vた + ところ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAlways requires the past ta-form — it reports what was discovered/learned as a direct result of the action just performed.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (past)Vた + ところ検査したところkensa shita tokoro
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
病院で検査したところ、異常はなかった。Byouin de kensa shita tokoro, ijou wa nakatta. When I got examined at the hospital, it turned out there was nothing wrong. अस्पताल में जांच कराई तो, कुछ भी गड़बड़ नहीं निकली। હોસ્પિટલમાં તપાસ કરાવી તો, કંઈ ખોટું નીકળ્યું નહીં.
駅に問い合わせたところ、忘れ物は届いていた。Eki ni toiawaseta tokoro, wasuremono wa todoite ita. When I inquired at the station, it turned out the lost item had already been turned in. स्टेशन पर पूछताछ की तो, खोई हुई चीज़ पहले ही जमा हो चुकी थी। સ્ટેશન પર પૂછપરછ કરી તો, ખોવાયેલી વસ્તુ પહેલેથી જ જમા થઈ ચૂકી હતી.

This ところ is always preceded by a た-form and always introduces something the speaker found out — never a plain-form verb (that would shift to Point 1's "about to ~" meaning instead). See Q4 in the quiz below for exactly this trap.

【3】 〜どころではない

neutral register
Reading~dokoro dewa nai
Meaning (EN)"not even in a state for ~ / far from being able to ~" — circumstances make an action completely out of the question
Meaning (HI)~ करने की स्थिति में बिल्कुल नहीं / ~ की तो बात ही छोड़िए
Meaning (GU)~ કરવાની સ્થિતિમાં જ નથી / ~ ની તો વાત જ છોડો
Connection (接続)Nどころ／Vるどころ／Vているどころ + ではない／じゃない／ではなく〜／じゃなく〜
RegisterNeutral, slightly more casual as じゃない
Typical useEmphasizes that circumstances (an emergency, illness, workload) make the stated activity completely impossible — stronger than a plain negative.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + どころではない仕事どころではないshigoto dokoro dewa nai
Verb (dictionary)Vる + どころじゃない遊びに行くどころじゃないasobi ni iku dokoro ja nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
工場で火事があり、仕事どころではなかった。Koujou de kaji ga ari, shigoto dokoro dewa nakatta. There was a fire at the factory, so it wasn't a situation for working at all. फ़ैक्टरी में आग लग गई, इसलिए काम करने की स्थिति ही नहीं बची। ફેક્ટરીમાં આગ લાગી, એટલે કામ કરવાની સ્થિતિ જ ન રહી.
風邪がひどくて、遊びに行くどころじゃない。Kaze ga hidokute, asobi ni iku dokoro ja nai. My cold is so bad that going out to have fun is completely out of the question. मुझे इतनी बुरी सर्दी है कि घूमने जाने की तो बात ही छोड़िए। મને એટલી ખરાબ શરદી છે કે ફરવા જવાની તો વાત જ છોડો.

Remember: どころ attaches directly to a bare noun (仕事どころ), with no の in between — a common spelling mistake to avoid (see Q3 in the quiz below).

【4】 〜どころか

neutral register
Reading~dokoro ka
Meaning (EN)"far from ~, not even ~" — the reality goes even further than the stated baseline, in the same direction
Meaning (HI)~ की तो बात ही छोड़ो, ~ भी नहीं (हक़ीक़त कही गई बात से भी आगे है)
Meaning (GU)~ ની તો વાત જ છોડો, ~ પણ નહીં (વાસ્તવિકતા કહેલી વાત કરતાં પણ આગળ છે)
Connection (接続)N／Vる／Naな／Aい + どころか (aどころか bも…ない)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe clause after どころか usually escalates in the same direction with も…ない, showing the actual situation is even more extreme than the first (already modest) claim.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + どころか夏休みどころか日曜日も休めないnatsuyasumi dokoro ka nichiyoubi mo yasumenai
い-adjectiveAい + どころか忙しいどころか仕事がなくてisogashii dokoro ka shigoto ga nakute
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
忙しくて夏休みどころか日曜日も休めないよ。Isogashikute natsuyasumi dokoro ka nichiyoubi mo yasumenai yo. I'm so busy that forget a summer vacation — I can't even take Sundays off. इतना व्यस्त हूँ कि गर्मी की छुट्टी तो दूर, रविवार को भी छुट्टी नहीं मिलती। એટલો વ્યસ્ત છું કે ઉનાળાની રજા તો દૂર, રવિવારે પણ રજા મળતી નથી.
うちの会社は忙しいどころか仕事がなくて困っているんだ。Uchi no kaisha wa isogashii dokoro ka shigoto ga nakute komatte iru n da. My company is far from busy — we're actually struggling because there's no work at all. हमारी कंपनी व्यस्त होना तो दूर, काम ही न होने की वजह से परेशान है। અમારી કંપની વ્યસ્ત હોવી તો દૂર, કામ જ ન હોવાના કારણે મુશ્કેલીમાં છે.

どころか is a contrastive escalation, not a flat denial like どころではない — it says "not only is X not the case, the truth is even further in the same direction than X." Compare 夏休みどころか日曜日も (not even a whole day off, let alone a summer vacation) with 仕事どころではない (a single acute situation makes work completely impossible right now).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, mark ○ or × and correct any ×) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain numbered blanks), both from p.53. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.55.

練習Ⅰ — mark ○ (correct) or × (incorrect), and fix any ×

Q1

これから電車に乗ったところです。（　）Kore kara densha ni notta tokoro desu. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 乗った→乗るnotta → noru
EN (corrected): I'm just about to board the train now.
HI: मैं अभी-अभी ट्रेन में चढ़ने ही वाला हूँ।
GU: હું હમણાં જ ટ્રેનમાં ચઢવાનો છું.

Why: これから ("from now on") signals a future/imminent action, which requires the plain form 乗るところ ("about to board"), not the past form 乗ったところ ("just finished boarding") — mixing tenses here is a direct contradiction.

Q2

お話し中のところ、失礼します。（　）Ohanashi-chuu no tokoro, shitsurei shimasu. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: Excuse me for interrupting while you're in the middle of talking.
HI: बातचीत के बीच में बाधा डालने के लिए माफ़ी चाहता हूँ।
GU: વાતચીતની વચ્ચે ખલેલ પહોંચાડવા બદલ માફી.

Why: This is the polite Point 1 formula (Nの + ところ) used correctly, exactly like お忙しいところ.

Q3

風が強すぎて、花見のどころではなかった。（　）Kaze ga tsuyosugite, hanami no dokoro dewa nakatta. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 花見の→花見hanami no → hanami
EN (corrected): The wind was too strong, so flower-viewing was completely out of the question.
HI: हवा इतनी तेज़ थी कि फूल देखने जाने की तो बात ही नहीं बनी।
GU: પવન એટલો જોરદાર હતો કે ફૂલ જોવા જવાની તો વાત જ ન રહી.

Why: どころ attaches directly to the bare noun — 花見どころ, not 花見のどころ. The の here is an incorrect insertion.

Q4

辞書で調べるところ、漢字が間違っていた。（　）Jisho de shiraberu tokoro, kanji ga machigatte ita. (___)

Marked: × (incorrect) — corrected: 調べる→調べたshiraberu → shirabeta
EN (corrected): When I looked it up in the dictionary, it turned out the kanji was wrong.
HI: डिक्शनरी में देखा तो, कांजी ग़लत निकली।
GU: ડિક્શનરીમાં જોયું તો, કાન્જી ખોટી નીકળી.

Why: This is Point 2's "discovery" sense (〜たところ), which always needs the past ta-form — 調べるところ (plain form) would instead mean "about to look it up," which contradicts the "it turned out..." result that follows.

Q5

もっと勉強しないと、N2どころかN3も無理だ。（　）Motto benkyou shinai to, N2 dokoro ka N3 mo muri da. (___)

Marked: ○ (correct)
EN: If you don't study more, forget N2 — even N3 will be impossible.
HI: अगर तुमने और पढ़ाई नहीं की, तो N2 तो दूर, N3 भी नामुमकिन होगा।
GU: જો તું વધુ ભણશે નહીં, તો N2 તો દૂર, N3 પણ અશક્ય થશે.

Why: This correctly uses Point 4's escalating どころか — even the "lesser" goal (N3) is out of reach, let alone the stated one (N2).

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

「もしもし、ちょうど電話 ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ なんです。」"Moshi moshi, choudo denwa ___ nan desu."

Tiles: 1 いた (ita)　2 しよう (shiyou)　3 ところ (tokoro)　4 と思って (to omotte)

2 しよう→4 と思って→1 いた→3 ところ

「もしもし、ちょうど電話しようと思っていたところなんです。」"Moshi moshi, choudo denwa shiyou to omotte ita tokoro nan desu."

EN: "Hello, I was just about to call you."
HI: "हैलो, मैं अभी-अभी तुम्हें फ़ोन करने ही वाला था।"
GU: "હેલો, હું હમણાં જ તને ફોન કરવાનો જ હતો."

Why: Built on Point 1's aspectual ところ — しようと思っていた (was thinking of doing) + ところ (right at that point) captures "I was just about to."

Q7

宿題がたくさん ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ではない。Shukudai ga takusan ___ dewa nai.

Tiles: 1 どころ (dokoro)　2 見る (miru)　3 あって (atte)　4 テレビを (terebi o)

3 あって→4 テレビを→2 見る→1 どころ

宿題がたくさんあって、テレビを見るどころではない。Shukudai ga takusan atte, terebi o miru dokoro dewa nai.

EN: I have so much homework that watching TV is completely out of the question.
HI: मेरे पास इतना होमवर्क है कि टीवी देखने की तो बात ही छोड़ो।
GU: મારી પાસે એટલું બધું હોમવર્ક છે કે ટીવી જોવાની તો વાત જ છોડો.

Why: Built on Point 3 (〜どころではない) — テレビを見る (watching TV) + どころではない (is completely out of the question) given the workload.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points share ところ／どころ, but the tense of the word before it and the presence of a negation change the meaning completely — a classic N2 exam trap zone. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ところ（を）~tokoro (o) While in the middle of ~ / just about to ~ / just did ~ Aい／Nの／Vた／Vている + ところ A pure aspect marker — meaning shifts entirely based on the preceding verb's tense/aspect. Politely referencing an inconvenient moment, or marking exactly where in a timeline an action sits.
〜たところ~ta tokoro When I did ~, (it turned out)... Vた + ところ Always past tense; introduces a discovery/result, similar to てみたら. Reporting what you found out immediately after doing something.
〜どころではない~dokoro dewa nai Not even in a state for ~ N／Vる／Vている + どころではない A blunt denial of feasibility due to circumstance — attaches straight to the noun, no の. An emergency or overwhelming situation rules an activity out entirely.
〜どころか~dokoro ka Far from ~, not even... N／Vる／Naな／Aい + どころか An escalating contrast — the truth is even further in the same direction than the stated baseline. You want to show reality is more extreme than even a modest claim.
Exam trap: ところ's meaning flips entirely with tense — 乗るところ ("about to board") vs 乗ったところ ("just boarded"/"just finished boarding") vs 乗っているところ ("in the middle of boarding"). Getting the tense wrong (Q1, Q4 above) is the single most common mistake with this word family. Separately, remember どころ never takes の before it when attached to a bare noun (Q3) — that's a spelling trap independent of the tense issue.
