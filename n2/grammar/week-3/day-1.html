1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜（た）とおり／どおり

neutral register
Reading~(ta) toori / ~doori
Meaning (EN)"just as ~ / exactly as ~" — the outcome matches what was said, thought, planned, or expected
Meaning (HI)जैसा कहा/सोचा/तय हुआ था, बिल्कुल वैसा ही
Meaning (GU)જેમ કહ્યું/વિચાર્યું/નક્કી કર્યું હતું, બરાબર એવું જ
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた／Nの + とおり(に)  |  N + どおり(に)
RegisterNeutral — everyday and written use
Typical useとおり needs の after a plain noun (予定のとおり), while どおり attaches directly to the noun with no の (予定どおり) — both mean the same thing, just two different attachment shapes.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary/past)Vる／Vた + とおり(に)言ったとおりitta toori
Noun (with の)N + の + とおり(に)思うとおりにomou toori ni
Noun (direct)N + どおり(に/だ)希望どおりkibou doori
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
友達が言ったとおり、日本の物価は高い。Tomodachi ga itta toori, Nihon no bukka wa takai. Just as my friend said, prices in Japan are expensive. जैसा मेरे दोस्त ने कहा था, जापान में चीज़ों के दाम सच में महंगे हैं। મારા મિત્રએ કહ્યું હતું તેમ, જાપાનમાં ભાવ ખરેખર મોંઘા છે.
自分の思うとおりに生きることは難しい。Jibun no omou toori ni ikiru koto wa muzukashii. Living exactly as you think/wish is difficult. जैसा हम सोचते हैं ठीक वैसा जीना मुश्किल है। જેમ આપણે વિચારીએ છીએ તેમ જ જીવવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
希望どおりkibou doori ／ アドバイスどおりadobaisu doori just as hoped / just as advised जैसी उम्मीद थी / जैसी सलाह दी गई જેવી આશા હતી / જેવી સલાહ આપી હતી

Think of とおり as "N/plain-verb + の-linked path" and どおり as the same idea fused directly onto a noun. 予定のとおり and 予定どおり mean the same thing ("as planned") — the difference is purely about whether の is needed, not about meaning.

【2】 〜まま（に）

neutral register
Reading~mama (ni)
Meaning (EN)"as (someone else) says/leads, without exercising one's own will" — passively following an outside instruction or impulse
Meaning (HI)जैसा कोई कहे/करे, बिना अपनी मर्ज़ी के वैसा ही करना
Meaning (GU)કોઈ કહે તેમ, પોતાની ઇચ્છા વગર તે પ્રમાણે કરવું
Connection (接続)Vる／Vられる + まま(に)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useUnlike とおり (which just states a match with a fact/plan), まま often implies the subject had no strong will of their own and simply went along with something — frequently used with られる (passive) forms like 言われるまま.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (passive)V-られる + まま(に)言われるままiwareru mama
Verb (plain)Vる + まま(に)足の向くままにashi no muku mama ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
セールスマンに言われるまま、契約書にサインをしてしまった。Seerusuman ni iwareru mama, keiyakusho ni sain o shite shimatta. Just as the salesman told me, I ended up signing the contract (without really deciding for myself). सेल्समैन ने जैसा कहा वैसा ही करते हुए, मैंने अनुबंध पर हस्ताक्षर कर दिए। સેલ્સમેને કહ્યું તેમ, મેં કરારમાં સહી કરી દીધી.
足の向くままに歩く。Ashi no muku mama ni aruku. To walk wherever your feet happen to lead you. पैर जिधर ले जाएँ, उधर चलना। પગ જ્યાં લઈ જાય ત્યાં ચાલવું.

Don't confuse まま (point 2) with とおり (point 1): とおり confirms that a result matches an objective plan/statement/expectation, while まま highlights that the subject simply drifted along with someone/something else's lead, without applying their own judgement.

【3】 〜ことに

neutral register
Reading~koto ni
Meaning (EN)"to my ~ / ~ly," — a sentence-opening adverbial that announces the speaker's emotional reaction before stating the fact
Meaning (HI)यह ~ बात है कि... (वाक्य की शुरुआत में वक्ता की भावना बताने के लिए)
Meaning (GU)...ની વાત એ છે કે (વાક્યની શરૂઆતમાં બોલનારની લાગણી બતાવવા)
Connection (接続)Aい／Naな／Vた + ことに
RegisterNeutral — common in narrative writing
Typical useAlways sits at the very start of the sentence; the clause that follows states the fact that triggered the emotion. Common set expressions: うれしいことに, 悲しいことに, 残念なことに, 申し訳ないことに, 間抜けなことに.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + ことに驚いたことにodoroita koto ni
な-adjectiveNaな + ことに残念なことにzannen na koto ni
Verb (past)Vた + ことに驚いたことにodoroita koto ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
驚いたことに、彼女は同じ相手と2回も離婚して再婚しているんです。Odoroita koto ni, kanojo wa onaji aite to nikai mo rikon shite saikon shite iru ndesu. To my surprise, she has divorced and remarried the same person twice. हैरानी की बात है कि उसने एक ही व्यक्ति से दो बार तलाक़ लेकर फिर से शादी की है। નવાઈની વાત એ છે કે તેણે એક જ વ્યક્તિ સાથે બે વાર છૂટાછેડા લઈને ફરી લગ્ન કર્યાં છે.
おもしろいことに、隣の家も、その隣の家も、うちと同じ名字なのです。Omoshiroi koto ni, tonari no ie mo, sono tonari no ie mo, uchi to onaji myouji na no desu. Interestingly, both the house next door and the one after that have the same surname as ours. दिलचस्प बात यह है कि पड़ोस का घर, और उसके बगल का घर भी, हमारे घर जैसा ही उपनाम रखते हैं। રસપ્રદ વાત એ છે કે પડોશનું ઘર અને તેની બાજુનું ઘર પણ અમારા ઘર જેવું જ અટક ધરાવે છે.

い-adjectives and た-form verbs attach directly (驚いた, うれしい); な-adjectives insert な (残念な). Note the contrast with point 4 below — あまり often needs the nominalized form of an adjective (うれしさの), but ことに takes the plain adjective form (うれしい) directly.

【4】 〜（の）あまり

neutral register
Reading~(no) amari
Meaning (EN)"because of too much ~ / to such an excessive degree that..." — an excessive cause leads to an unusual/extreme result
Meaning (HI)इतना ज़्यादा ~ कि... (अत्यधिक मात्रा के कारण कोई असामान्य परिणाम)
Meaning (GU)એટલું બધું ~ કે... (વધુ પડતાના કારણે અસામાન્ય પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)Nの／Naな／Vる + あまり
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe book marks that nominalized adjective stems (うれしさ, 悲しみ) also attach directly before の + あまり — e.g. うれしさのあまり, 悲しみのあまり, 怒りのあまり, 急ぐあまり, 恐れるあまり.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + の + あまり緊張のあまりkinchou no amari
Verb (dictionary)Vる + あまり心配するあまりshinpai suru amari
Nominalized adjectiveAい-stem+さ／Naな-stem+さ + の + あまりうれしさのあまりureshisa no amari
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
緊張のあまり、体が震えた。Kinchou no amari, karada ga furueta. I was so nervous that my body was shaking. इतना घबरा गया कि मेरा शरीर काँपने लगा। એટલો બધો ગભરાયો કે મારું શરીર ધ્રૂજવા લાગ્યું.
心配するあまり、母は寝込んでしまった。Shinpai suru amari, haha wa nekonde shimatta. My mother worried so much that she fell ill in bed. माँ इतनी चिंतित हो गई कि बीमार पड़ गई। મમ્મી એટલી ચિંતિત થઈ ગઈ કે બીમાર પડી ગઈ.

A useful memory hook: あまり literally means "excess" — so "N/nominalized-adj の + あまり" and "Vる + あまり" both read as "from the sheer excess of ~." Compare with ことに (point 3): あまり needs a noun-shaped word before it (hence nominalizing adjectives into うれしさ), while ことに takes adjectives in their plain form.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, choose a or b) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items with plain numbered blanks — this book does not mark ★ on non-test days), both from p.47. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.49.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

会は予定（　）行われた。Kai wa yotei (___) okonawareta.

Full sentence: 会は予定どおり行われた。Kai wa yotei doori okonawareta.
EN: The meeting was held exactly as planned.
HI: बैठक ठीक योजना के अनुसार हुई।
GU: સભા યોજના પ્રમાણે જ યોજાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとおりtoori(needs の after a plain noun like 予定)(予定+とおり के लिए の चाहिए)(予定+とおり માટે の જોઈએ)
bどおりdoorias planned (attaches directly to N)योजना के अनुसारયોજના પ્રમાણે

Why: 予定 is a bare noun, so it takes どおり directly. とおり would require 予定の前 (予定のとおり) — without の, とおり alone is ungrammatical here.

Q2

（　）ことに、同じ夢を毎晩見た。(___) koto ni, onaji yume o maiban mita.

Full sentence: 不思議なことに、同じ夢を毎晩見た。Fushigi na koto ni, onaji yume o maiban mita.
EN: Strangely enough, I had the same dream every night.
HI: अजीब बात है कि मुझे हर रात एक जैसा सपना आता रहा।
GU: વિચિત્ર વાત એ છે કે મને દરરોજ રાત્રે એ જ સપનું આવ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a不思議なfushigi nastrange (な-adjective + な before ことに)अजीबવિચિત્ર
b不思議fushigi(missing な — incomplete attachment)(な की कमी)(な ખૂટે છે)

Why: な-adjectives must keep their な before ことに, exactly like before any noun — 不思議なことに, not 不思議ことに.

Q3

そのコンサートでは興奮（　）気を失うファンも続出した。Sono konsaato de wa koufun (___) ki o ushinau fan mo zokushutsu shita.

Full sentence: そのコンサートでは興奮のあまり気を失うファンも続出した。Sono konsaato de wa koufun no amari ki o ushinau fan mo zokushutsu shita.
EN: At that concert, fan after fan fainted from sheer excitement.
HI: उस कॉन्सर्ट में इतना उत्साह था कि एक के बाद एक प्रशंसक बेहोश होते गए।
GU: તે કોન્સર્ટમાં એટલો ઉત્સાહ હતો કે એક પછી એક ચાહકો બેભાન થઈ ગયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあまりamari(missing の after the noun 興奮)(の की कमी)(の ખૂટે છે)
bのあまりno amarifrom sheer excess of excitementअत्यधिक उत्साह सेઅતિશય ઉત્સાહથી

Why: 興奮 is a plain noun, so it needs の before あまり — 興奮のあまり, not 興奮あまり.

Q4

思う（　）自由にかいた絵が評価された。Omou (___) jiyuu ni kaita e ga hyouka sareta.

Full sentence: 思うまま自由にかいた絵が評価された。Omou mama jiyuu ni kaita e ga hyouka sareta.
EN: The picture drawn freely, just as the artist wished, was well received.
HI: जैसा मन में आया वैसे ही स्वतंत्र रूप से बनाई गई तस्वीर की सराहना हुई।
GU: મન થયું તેમ મુક્તપણે દોરેલા ચિત્રની પ્રશંસા થઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aままmamafreely, following one's own inclinationअपनी मर्ज़ी सेપોતાની ઇચ્છા મુજબ
bどおりdoori(wrong nuance — implies matching a fixed external plan)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)

Why: 思うまま describes drawing however one personally feels like, with no outside constraint — the opposite of どおり, which would imply matching some pre-existing external plan or instruction. This is exactly the Point 1 vs Point 2 confusion trap.

Q5

（　）ことに、また負けてしまった。(___) koto ni, mata makete shimatta.

Full sentence: くやしいことに、また負けてしまった。Kuyashii koto ni, mata makete shimatta.
EN: Frustratingly, I lost again.
HI: निराशाजनक बात है कि मैं फिर हार गया।
GU: નિરાશાજનક વાત એ છે કે હું ફરી હાર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aくやしいkuyashiifrustrating (plain い-adjective form)निराशाजनकનિરાશાજનક
bくやしさのkuyashisa no(wrong here — that's the あまり pattern, not ことに)(यह あまり का रूप है, ことに का नहीं)(આ あまり નું સ્વરૂપ છે, ことに નું નહીં)

Why: ことに takes the plain adjective form (くやしい), never the nominalized -さの form. くやしさのあまり would be correct for Point 4 (あまり), but this sentence uses ことに, so the plain form is required.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

料理の本に ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 失敗した。Ryouri no hon ni ___ shippai shita.

Tiles: 1 のに (noni)　2 書いてある (kaite aru)　3 作った (tsukutta)　4 とおりに (toori ni)
(Note: this book marks the blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ on non-test days.)

2 書いてある→4 とおりに→3 作った→1 のに

料理の本に書いてあるとおりに作ったのに失敗した。Ryouri no hon ni kaite aru toori ni tsukutta noni shippai shita.

EN: Even though I made it exactly as written in the cookbook, I still failed.
HI: रेसिपी की किताब में जैसा लिखा था वैसा ही बनाने के बावजूद, मैं असफल रहा।
GU: રસોઈની ચોપડીમાં લખ્યા મુજબ જ બનાવ્યું છતાં, હું નિષ્ફળ ગયો.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜とおりに) — 書いてある (what's written) + とおりに (exactly as) + 作った (I made it), then のに (even though) sets up the surprising failure.

Q7

事件の現場を見てしまった私は ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿。Jiken no genba o mite shimatta watashi wa ___.

Tiles: 1 ショック (shokku)　2 出なかった (denakatta)　3 のあまり (no amari)　4 声も (koe mo)

1 ショック→3 のあまり→4 声も→2 出なかった

事件の現場を見てしまった私はショックのあまり声も出なかった。Jiken no genba o mite shimatta watashi wa shokku no amari koe mo denakatta.

EN: Having seen the scene of the incident, I was so shocked that I couldn't even make a sound.
HI: घटना स्थल देखकर मैं इतना सदमे में था कि आवाज़ भी नहीं निकली।
GU: ઘટનાસ્થળ જોઈને હું એટલો આઘાત પામ્યો કે અવાજ પણ ન નીકળ્યો.

Why: Built on Point 4 (〜のあまり) — ショック (shock, a noun) + のあまり (from sheer excess of) + 声も出なかった (couldn't even make a sound).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The divider page groups these four together because they're easy to mix up — especially とおり vs まま (Q4 above) and あまり vs ことに (Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜（た）とおり／どおり~(ta) toori / ~doori Just as ~ (matches a fact/plan) Vる／Vた／Nの + とおり  |  N + どおり Confirms the result matches something objective (what was said, planned, forecast). You're checking a result against a known plan/statement.
〜まま（に）~mama (ni) As led/told, without one's own will Vる／Vられる + まま(に) Highlights passivity/lack of independent judgement — often with 言われるまま. The subject is simply following someone else's lead or an impulse.
〜ことに~koto ni To my ~, ... Aい／Naな／Vた + ことに Sentence-initial only; announces the speaker's emotional reaction to what follows. Takes plain adjective forms. You want to editorialize your own reaction before stating a fact.
〜（の）あまり~(no) amari Because of too much ~ Nの／Naな／Vる + あまり Cause-and-effect: excess of a state/action leads to an extreme result. Needs nominalized adjective forms (うれしさ). You're explaining an extreme result caused by an excessive state or action.
Exam trap: とおり and まま can both follow a plain verb and both loosely translate as "as ~," but とおり is about objective correctness/match (it matches the plan), while まま is about the subject's lack of independent will (they just went along with it). Also watch adjective attachment: あまり wants the nominalized noun-like form (うれしさの), while ことに wants the plain form (うれしい) — mixing these up is a classic N2 distractor (see Q5).
