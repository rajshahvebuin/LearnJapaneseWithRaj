Notes

Important trap: 一方 appears in two unrelated grammar roles on this same day — 一方（で） as a contrastive conjunction ("on the one hand... but") and 一方だ directly after a change-verb meaning "only keeps ~ing" (an unstoppable trend) — see the Confusion Pairs section below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に反して／反し／反するN

neutral / written register
Reading~ni hanshite
Meaning (EN)"contrary to ~" — the actual outcome clashes with an expectation, prediction, hope, or rule
Meaning (HI)~ के विपरीत / ~ के उलट (उम्मीद, भविष्यवाणी या नियम से टकराता परिणाम)
Meaning (GU)~ થી વિપરીત / ~ થી ઊલટું (અપેક્ષા, આગાહી કે નિયમથી અથડાતું પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)Nに + 反して／反し  |  Nに反するN
RegisterNeutral to slightly formal/written
Typical useCommon set phrases: 予想に反して, 期待に反して, 意志に反して, 希望に反して.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + に反して予想に反してyosou ni hanshite
Noun (attributive)N + に反するN予想に反する結果yosou ni hansuru kekka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
専門家の予想に反して、景気は回復し始めた。Senmonka no yosou ni hanshite, keiki wa kaifuku shihajimeta. Contrary to the experts' predictions, the economy has begun to recover. विशेषज्ञों की भविष्यवाणी के विपरीत, अर्थव्यवस्था में सुधार शुरू हो गया है। નિષ્ણાતોની આગાહીથી વિપરીત, અર્થતંત્રમાં સુધારો શરૂ થયો છે.
我々の期待に反して、新商品はあまり売れなかった。Wareware no kitai ni hanshite, shinshouhin wa amari urenakatta. Contrary to our expectations, the new product didn't sell very well. हमारी उम्मीदों के विपरीत, नया उत्पाद ज़्यादा नहीं बिका। અમારી અપેક્ષાઓથી વિપરીત, નવું ઉત્પાદન વધારે વેચાયું નહીં.

に反して always sets up a clash between an expectation/rule/wish (before に) and an actual outcome (after it) — think of it as "clashing head-on with N."

【2】 〜反面／半面

neutral register
Reading~hanmen
Meaning (EN)"while being ~ on the one hand, on the other hand..." — one and the same subject has two contrasting facets
Meaning (HI)एक तरफ़ ~ होते हुए, दूसरी तरफ़... (एक ही चीज़ के दो विपरीत पहलू)
Meaning (GU)એક બાજુ ~ હોવા છતાં, બીજી બાજુ... (એક જ વસ્તુના બે વિરોધાભાસી પાસાં)
Connection (接続)Nである／なな／なである／Aい／Vる + 反面／半面
RegisterNeutral
Typical useHighlights that the SAME subject has both a good side and a less-good side, unlike に反して, which contrasts an outcome with an outside expectation.
Word typeFormationExample
な-adjectiveNaな + 反面便利な反面benri na hanmen
Noun (である)Nである + 反面優れた学者である反面sugureta gakusha de aru hanmen
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
パソコンは便利な反面、トラブルも多い。Pasokon wa benri na hanmen, toraburu mo ooi. Computers are convenient, but on the other hand, they also cause a lot of trouble. कंप्यूटर सुविधाजनक हैं, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, इनसे कई परेशानियाँ भी होती हैं। કમ્પ્યુટર સુવિધાજનક છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, તેનાથી ઘણી મુશ્કેલીઓ પણ થાય છે.
彼は優れた学者である反面、精神的に弱い人間だ。Kare wa sugureta gakusha de aru hanmen, seishinteki ni yowai ningen da. He's an excellent scholar, but on the other hand, he's psychologically fragile. वह एक उत्कृष्ट विद्वान है, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, वह मानसिक रूप से कमज़ोर इंसान है। તે એક ઉત્કૃષ્ટ વિદ્વાન છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, તે માનસિક રીતે નબળો માણસ છે.

反面 and 半面 are interchangeable here (半面 is just an alternate kanji spelling with the same meaning). Both describe two sides of the same coin, not a clash with an outside expectation.

【3】 〜一方（で）

neutral register
Reading~ippou (de)
Meaning (EN)"on the one hand... (but on the other)" — nearly synonymous with 反面, contrasting two facets of the same subject; can also stand alone as "meanwhile"
Meaning (HI)एक तरफ़... (लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़) — 反面 जैसा ही; अकेले "इस बीच" के रूप में भी
Meaning (GU)એક બાજુ... (પણ બીજી બાજુ) — 反面 જેવું જ; એકલા "એ દરમિયાન" તરીકે પણ
Connection (接続)Nである／なな／なである／Aい／Vる + 一方（で）
RegisterNeutral, slightly more written/formal than 反面 in some contexts
Typical useStandalone bonus use (marked もっと！ in the book): 一方では can open a new sentence entirely as a discourse connective meaning "meanwhile, looking at it another way."
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveAい + 一方水に強い一方mizu ni tsuyoi ippou
な-adjectiveNaな + 一方静かな一方shizuka na ippou
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この布は水に強い一方、熱に弱い。Kono nuno wa mizu ni tsuyoi ippou, netsu ni yowai. This cloth is strong against water, but on the other hand, weak against heat. यह कपड़ा पानी के प्रति मज़बूत है, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, गर्मी के प्रति कमज़ोर है। આ કાપડ પાણી સામે મજબૂત છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, ગરમી સામે નબળું છે.
予算がないと言われているが、一方では予算がむだに使われている。Yosan ga nai to iwarete iru ga, ippou de wa yosan ga muda ni tsukawarete iru. They say there's no budget, but meanwhile, the budget is being wasted elsewhere. कहा जाता है कि बजट नहीं है, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, बजट को बेकार में बर्बाद किया जा रहा है। કહેવાય છે કે બજેટ નથી, પણ બીજી બાજુ, બજેટનો બગાડ થઈ રહ્યો છે.

一方（で） works almost interchangeably with 反面 for contrasting two facets of the same subject — but only 一方 can also stand alone at the start of a new sentence (一方では) as a "meanwhile" connective; 反面 cannot do this.

【4】 〜一方だ

neutral register
Reading~ippou da
Meaning (EN)"only keeps ~ing / just goes on ~ing" — a one-directional, unstoppable trend (=Vるばかりだ)
Meaning (HI)बस ~ होता ही जा रहा है (एकतरफ़ा, रुकने का नाम न लेने वाला रुझान)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ~ થતું જ જાય છે (એકતરફી, અટકવાનું નામ ન લેતું વલણ)
Connection (接続)Vる + 一方だ (＝「Vるばかりだ」)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useOnly attaches to verbs that describe change: 増える一方だ, 減る一方だ, 変わる一方だ, 悪くなる一方だ, 上がる一方だ, 進む一方だ.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb of change (dictionary form)Vる + 一方だ上がる一方だagaru ippou da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大気中のCO2濃度の上昇とともに、地球の温暖化は進む一方だ。Taikichuu no CO2 noudo no joushou to tomo ni, chikyuu no ondanka wa susumu ippou da. Along with the rise in atmospheric CO2 levels, global warming just keeps advancing. वातावरण में CO2 के स्तर के बढ़ने के साथ, ग्लोबल वार्मिंग लगातार बढ़ती ही जा रही है। વાતાવરણમાં CO2 ના સ્તરના વધારા સાથે, ગ્લોબલ વોર્મિંગ સતત વધતું જ જાય છે.
天候のせいで、野菜の値段は上がる一方だ。Tenkou no sei de, yasai no nedan wa agaru ippou da. Because of the weather, vegetable prices just keep going up and up. मौसम की वजह से, सब्ज़ियों की कीमतें लगातार बढ़ती ही जा रही हैं। હવામાનના કારણે, શાકભાજીના ભાવ સતત વધતા જ જાય છે.

This is a completely different grammatical role from Points 2/3's 一方 — despite sharing the same kanji, 一方だ directly after a change-verb (上がる一方だ) means an unbroken continuing trend, not a two-sided contrast. See the Confusion Pairs callout below for how to tell them apart at a glance.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

子どもの成長はうれしい（　）、どこかさびしい。Kodomo no seichou wa ureshii (___), dokoka sabishii.

Full sentence: 子どもの成長はうれしい反面、どこかさびしい。Kodomo no seichou wa ureshii hanmen, dokoka sabishii.
EN: A child's growth is a happy thing, but on the other hand, it's somehow a little sad too.
HI: बच्चे का बड़ा होना ख़ुशी की बात है, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, कहीं न कहीं थोड़ा उदास भी करता है।
GU: બાળકનું મોટું થવું ખુશીની વાત છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, ક્યાંક થોડું ઉદાસ પણ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a反してhanshite(wrong — no outside expectation is being contradicted here)(यहाँ कोई बाहरी उम्मीद नहीं टूट रही)(અહીં કોઈ બહારની અપેક્ષા તૂટતી નથી)
b反面hanmenon the other hand (two facets of the same thing)दूसरी तरफ़બીજી બાજુ

Why: Both feelings (happy and sad) belong to the same subject (the child's growth) — that's 反面's job, not 反して, which needs an outside prediction/expectation to clash with.

Q2

資源を節約しようと言う（　）、電力がむだに使われている。Shigen o setsuyaku shiyou to iu (___), denryoku ga muda ni tsukawarete iru.

Full sentence: 資源を節約しようと言う一方で、電力がむだに使われている。Shigen o setsuyaku shiyou to iu ippou de, denryoku ga muda ni tsukawarete iru.
EN: People say we should conserve resources, but meanwhile, electricity is being wasted.
HI: लोग कहते हैं कि संसाधन बचाने चाहिए, लेकिन इस बीच, बिजली बर्बाद की जा रही है।
GU: લોકો કહે છે કે સંસાધનો બચાવવા જોઈએ, પણ એ દરમિયાન, વીજળીનો બગાડ થઈ રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a一方でippou demeanwhile / on the other handइस बीचએ દરમિયાન
b反してhanshite(wrong — this needs Nに反して, not a full clause + 反して)(यह Nに反して रूप चाहिए)(આ Nに反して સ્વરૂપ જોઈએ)

Why: 一方で here contrasts what's being said (conserve resources) with what's actually happening (waste) — a two-facets contrast, which needs 一方で/反面, not に反して (which requires a plain Nに structure).

Q3

社員の期待に（　）、給料は下がった。Shain no kitai ni (___), kyuuryou wa sagatta.

Full sentence: 社員の期待に反して、給料は下がった。Shain no kitai ni hanshite, kyuuryou wa sagatta.
EN: Contrary to the employees' expectations, salaries went down.
HI: कर्मचारियों की उम्मीदों के विपरीत, वेतन घट गया।
GU: કર્મચારીઓની અપેક્ષાઓથી વિપરીત, પગાર ઘટ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a反してhanshitecontrary to (clashes with an expectation)के विपरीतથી વિપરીત
b反面hanmen(wrong — 期待 isn't a facet of the salary itself)(यहाँ ग़लत, यह एक ही चीज़ के दो पहलू नहीं हैं)(અહીં ખોટું, આ એક જ વસ્તુના બે પાસાં નથી)

Why: The employees' expectation and the actual salary outcome are two separate things clashing — exactly 反して's job (N+に反して), not 反面, which needs one subject with two facets.

Q4

ノートパソコンは手軽である（　）、こわれやすい。Nooto pasokon wa tegaru de aru (___), kowareyasui.

Full sentence: ノートパソコンは手軽である反面、こわれやすい。Nooto pasokon wa tegaru de aru hanmen, kowareyasui.

GU: લેપટોપ હલકા અને સરળ હોય છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, સહેલાઈથી તૂટી પણ જાય છે.

Q5

仕事をしない若者は増える（　）だ。Shigoto o shinai wakamono wa fueru (___) da.

Full sentence: 仕事をしない若者は増える一方だ。Shigoto o shinai wakamono wa fueru ippou da.
EN: The number of young people who don't work just keeps increasing.
HI: बिना काम किए रहने वाले युवाओं की संख्या लगातार बढ़ती ही जा रही है।
GU: કામ ન કરતા યુવાનોની સંખ્યા સતત વધતી જ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a一方ippouonly keeps increasing (unstoppable trend)लगातार बढ़ता ही जा रहा हैસતત વધતું જ જાય છે
b反面hanmen(wrong — 増える一方だ needs the "unidirectional trend" 一方, not a two-sided contrast)(यहाँ ग़लत, दो-पक्षीय तुलना नहीं है)(અહીં ખોટું, બે-બાજુની સરખામણી નથી)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 商品は売れなかった。___ shouhin wa urenakatta.

Tiles: 1 店側の (misegawa no)　2 新しい (atarashii)　3 予想 (yosou)　4 に反して (ni hanshite)

1 店側の→3 予想→4 に反して→2 新しい

店側の予想に反して、新しい商品は売れなかった。Misegawa no yosou ni hanshite, atarashii shouhin wa urenakatta.

EN: Contrary to the store's expectations, the new product didn't sell.
HI: दुकान की उम्मीदों के विपरीत, नया उत्पाद नहीं बिका।
GU: દુકાનની અપેક્ષાઓથી વિપરીત, નવું ઉત્પાદન વેચાયું નહીં.

Why: Built on Point 1 (〜に反して) — 店側の予想 (the store's prediction) + に反して (contrary to) sets up the clash with reality (the product not selling).

Q7

あの日本人は ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 日本語が少しおかしいようだ。Ano nihonjin wa ___ nihongo ga sukoshi okashii you da.

Tiles: 1 得意だ (tokui da)　2 反面 (hanmen)　3 と言われている (to iwarete iru)　4 英語が (eigo ga)

4 英語が→1 得意だ→3 と言われている→2 反面

あの日本人は英語が得意だと言われている反面、日本語が少しおかしいようだ。Ano nihonjin wa eigo ga tokui da to iwarete iru hanmen, nihongo ga sukoshi okashii you da.

EN: That Japanese person is said to be good at English, but on the other hand, their Japanese seems a little off.
HI: कहा जाता है कि वह जापानी व्यक्ति अंग्रेज़ी में अच्छा है, लेकिन दूसरी तरफ़, उसकी जापानी थोड़ी अजीब लगती है।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે તે જાપાની વ્યક્તિ અંગ્રેજીમાં સારો છે, પણ બીજી બાજુ, તેની જાપાનીઝ થોડી વિચિત્ર લાગે છે.

Why: Built on Point 2 (〜反面) — 英語が得意だと言われている (said to be good at English) + 反面 (on the other hand) contrasts with their questionable Japanese, two facets of the same person.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points share the idea of "contrast," but they contrast different things — and 一方 does double duty as two unrelated patterns. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に反して~ni hanshite Contrary to ~ Nに + 反して Contrasts an outcome against an outside expectation/prediction/rule (two separate things). Reality clashes with what was predicted, hoped, or required.
〜反面／半面~hanmen While ~, on the other hand... N／Naな／Aい／Vる + 反面 Contrasts two facets of the SAME subject. One thing has both a good side and a less-good side.
〜一方（で）~ippou (de) On the one hand... (but) N／Naな／Aい／Vる + 一方（で） Nearly identical to 反面; can additionally stand alone as 一方では ("meanwhile") to open a new sentence. Same use as 反面, or you want a standalone "meanwhile" connective.
〜一方だ~ippou da Only keeps ~ing (unstoppable trend) Vる（change verb） + 一方だ A completely different grammatical role from 一方（で） — attaches only to change-verbs, no contrast involved at all. Something is continuously moving in one direction with no sign of stopping.
Exam trap: Don't let the shared kanji 一方 fool you — 一方（で） after a noun/adjective/plain-form verb means "on the one hand, but," while 一方だ stuck directly onto a change-verb (増える一方だ, 上がる一方だ) means "only keeps increasing," with no contrast at all (see Q5 above, where 反面 is tempting but wrong for exactly this reason). Also keep に反して (clash with an OUTSIDE expectation) separate from 反面/一方 (two sides of the SAME subject) — check whether the sentence is comparing two different things or two facets of one thing.
