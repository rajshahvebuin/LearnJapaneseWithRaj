問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

買う＿＿＿借りる＿＿＿、テキストを必ず持参すること。Kau ___ kariru ___, tekisuto o kanarazu jisan suru koto.

Full sentence: 買うにしろ借りるにしろ、テキストを必ず持参すること。Kau ni shiro kariru ni shiro, tekisuto o kanarazu jisan suru koto.
EN: Whether you buy it or borrow it, be sure to bring the textbook.
HI: चाहे ख़रीदो या उधार लो, टेक्स्टबुक ज़रूर लाना।
GU: ભલે ખરીદો કે ઉધાર લો, પાઠ્યપુસ્તક જરૂર લાવવું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にしろ／にしろni shiro / ni shirowhether ~ or ~ (regardless)
2やら／やらyara / yara(wrong pattern — listing examples)
3も／もmo / mo(incomplete without ば/なら)
4し／しshi / shi(wrong pattern — plain listing)

Why: Day 2's にしろ〜にしろ pairs two alternatives (buy/borrow) and states an instruction applies either way.

Q2

入院＿＿＿、保険に入った。Nyuuin ___, hoken ni haitta.

Full sentence: 入院を契機として、保険に入った。Nyuuin o keiki to shite, hoken ni haitta.
EN: Taking hospitalization as an opportunity, (I) took out insurance.
HI: अस्पताल में भर्ती होने को मौक़ा बनाकर, बीमा लिया।
GU: હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ થવાને તક બનાવીને, વીમો લીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を契機としてo keiki to shitetaking ~ as the opportunity
2をもとにしてo moto ni shite(wrong pattern — source material, not a trigger)
3もかまわずmo kamawazu(wrong pattern here)
4にもかかわらずni mo kakawarazu(wrong pattern — despite, not a trigger)

Why: Day 6's を契機として (formal) — hospitalization triggered the decision to get insurance.

Q3

心の＿＿＿贈り物をもらうとうれしいものだ。Kokoro no ___ okurimono o morau to ureshii mono da.

Full sentence: 心のこもった贈り物をもらうとうれしいものだ。Kokoro no komotta okurimono o morau to ureshii mono da.
EN: It's nice to receive a heartfelt gift.
HI: दिल से दिया गया तोहफ़ा पाकर ख़ुशी होती है।
GU: દિલથી અપાયેલી ભેટ મળે તો ખુશી થાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1めぐったmegutta(wrong word — went around)
2こもったkomottaheartfelt (attributive past of こもる)
3通じたtsuujita(wrong word — got through)
4通したtsuushita(wrong word — passed through)

Why: Day 4's bonus note — 心のこもった (attributive past of the intransitive こもる, "to be filled with") directly modifies 贈り物, "a heartfelt gift."

Q4

彼女は耳も＿＿＿目も見えなかった。Kanojo wa mimi mo ___ me mo mienakatta.

Full sentence: 彼女は耳も聞こえなければ目も見えなかった。Kanojo wa mimi mo kikoenakereba me mo mienakatta.
EN: She could neither hear nor see.
HI: वह न सुन पाती थी, न देख पाती थी।
GU: તે ન સાંભળી શકતી, ન જોઈ શકતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1聞こえないとkikoenai to(wrong pattern here)
2聞こえなくてkikoenakute(wrong pattern — plain te-form reason)
3聞こえなければkikoenakerebaneither X nor Y (も〜ば〜も, negative)
4聞こえてもkikoetemo(wrong pattern — even if audible)

Why: Day 2's N1もVばN2もVる frame in its negative form — 耳も聞こえなければ目も見えない, "neither hearing nor seeing."

Q5

服が汚れるの＿＿＿、彼はけがをした人を抱きかかえた。Fuku ga yogoreru no ___, kare wa kega o shita hito o dakikakaeta.

Full sentence: 服が汚れるのもかまわず、彼はけがをした人を抱きかかえた。Fuku ga yogoreru no mo kamawazu, kare wa kega o shita hito o dakikakaeta.
EN: Not caring that his clothes would get dirty, he carried the injured person.
HI: कपड़े गंदे होने की परवाह किए बिना, उसने घायल व्यक्ति को उठाया।
GU: કપડાં ગંદા થવાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, તેણે ઘાયલ વ્યક્તિને ઊંચકી લીધો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1を問わずo towazu(wrong pattern — needs a category noun)
2につけni tsuke(wrong pattern here)
3もかまわずmo kamawazunot caring about ~
4にしろni shiro(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 1's Vるのもかまわず — he disregards his own clothes getting dirty in order to help.

Q6

そのピアニストは、国の内外を＿＿＿活躍している。Sono pianisuto wa, kuni no naigai o ___ katsuyaku shite iru.

Full sentence: そのピアニストは、国の内外を問わず活躍している。Sono pianisuto wa, kuni no naigai o towazu katsuyaku shite iru.
EN: That pianist is active both in Japan and abroad.
HI: वह पियानोवादक देश-विदेश में सक्रिय है।
GU: તે પિયાનોવાદક દેશ-વિદેશમાં સક્રિય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1問わずtowazuregardless of (in/outside the country)
2中心にchuushin ni(wrong pattern here)
3通してtsuushite(wrong pattern here)
4契機にkeiki ni(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 1's を問わず — 国の内外を問わず is one of the book's own listed set phrases for this pattern.

Q7

ボランティア活動＿＿＿社会に貢献する。Borantia katsudou ___ shakai ni kouken suru.

Full sentence: ボランティア活動を通じて社会に貢献する。Borantia katsudou o tsuujite shakai ni kouken suru.
EN: (One) contributes to society through volunteer activities.
HI: स्वयंसेवी गतिविधियों के ज़रिए समाज में योगदान देना।
GU: સ્વયંસેવક પ્રવૃત્તિઓ દ્વારા સમાજમાં યોગદાન આપવું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1に関してni kanshite(wrong pattern — regarding)
2を通じてo tsuujitethrough (means/channel)
3に応じてni oujite(wrong pattern — according to)
4に先立ってni sakidatte(wrong pattern — prior to)

Why: Day 4's を通じて — volunteer activities are the means/channel through which one contributes to society.

Q8

きょうは雷が鳴る＿＿＿地震が起きる＿＿＿、なんだか普通じゃない。Kyou wa kaminari ga naru ___ jishin ga okiru ___, nandaka futsuu ja nai.

Full sentence: きょうは雷が鳴るやら地震が起きるやら、なんだか普通じゃない。Kyou wa kaminari ga naru yara jishin ga okiru yara, nandaka futsuu ja nai.
EN: Today, what with thunder and an earthquake and everything, it's just not normal.
HI: आज बिजली भी कड़की, भूकंप भी आया — कुछ ठीक नहीं लग रहा।
GU: આજે વીજળી પણ ચમકી, ધરતીકંપ પણ થયો — કંઈક બરાબર નથી લાગતું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にしろ／にしろni shiro / ni shiro(wrong pattern here)
2につけ／につけni tsuke / ni tsuke(wrong pattern here)
3やら／やらyara / yarathings piling up (thunder, earthquake...)
4も／もmo / mo(incomplete without ば/なら)

Why: Day 2's やら〜やら — a jumble of unusual, troubling events piling up at once.

Q9

人は無いものを欲しがる＿＿＿。Hito wa nai mono o hoshigaru ___.

Full sentence: 人は無いものを欲しがるものだ。Hito wa nai mono o hoshigaru mono da.
EN: People naturally want what they don't have.
HI: इंसान वही चाहता है जो उसके पास नहीं होता, यह स्वाभाविक है।
GU: માણસ પાસે જે નથી તે જ ઈચ્છે છે, એ સ્વાભાવિક છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ことかkoto ka(wrong pattern here)
2ものだmono dathat's naturally so (general truth)
3ことはないkoto wa nai(wrong pattern — there's no need to)
4ものがあるmono ga aru(wrong pattern — felt quality, not a general truth)

Why: Day 3's general-truth ものだ — this states a universal human tendency.

Q10

この服を着る＿＿＿、母を思い出す。Kono fuku o kiru ___, haha o omoidasu.

Full sentence: この服を着るにつけ、母を思い出す。Kono fuku o kiru ni tsuke, haha o omoidasu.
EN: Every time I wear this clothing, I remember my mother.
HI: यह कपड़ा पहनते ही, माँ की याद आती है।
GU: આ કપડું પહેરું ત્યારે, મા યાદ આવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1やらyara(wrong pattern here)
2につきni tsuki(wrong pattern — reason, not "every time")
3につけni tsukeevery time ~ (single-verb use)
4にそってni sotte(wrong pattern — along/in accordance with)

Why: Day 2's につけ, used here with a single verb rather than a pair — "every time X happens."

Q11

その病気は動物から人間にうつる＿＿＿がある。Sono byouki wa doubutsu kara ningen ni utsuru ___ ga aru.

Full sentence: その病気は動物から人間にうつる恐れがある。Sono byouki wa doubutsu kara ningen ni utsuru osore ga aru.
EN: There is a risk that this disease will spread from animals to humans.
HI: उस बीमारी के जानवरों से इंसानों में फैलने का ख़तरा है।
GU: તે રોગ પ્રાણીઓમાંથી માણસોમાં ફેલાવાનું જોખમ છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1契機keiki(wrong word — trigger/opportunity)
2恐れosorerisk (of disease spreading)
3気味kimi(wrong word — a slight tendency)
4向きmuki(wrong word — suited for/direction)

Why: Day 5's 恐れがある — the spread of disease is exactly the kind of risk this pattern reports.

Q12

体にいいからといって、たくさん食べればいい＿＿＿。Karada ni ii kara to itte, takusan tabereba ii ___.

Full sentence: 体にいいからといって、たくさん食べればいいというものでもない。Karada ni ii kara to itte, takusan tabereba ii to iu mono demo nai.
EN: Just because it's good for you doesn't mean eating a lot of it is good.
HI: सेहत के लिए अच्छा है इसलिए ज़्यादा खाना अच्छा है, यह ज़रूरी नहीं।
GU: સ્વાસ્થ્ય માટે સારું છે એટલે વધારે ખાવું સારું છે, એ જરૂરી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1わけがあるwake ga aru(wrong pattern here)
2わけだwake da(wrong pattern — it follows that)
3次第だshidai da(wrong pattern — depends on)
4というものでもないto iu mono demo naiit's not necessarily true that ~

Why: Day 5's というものでもない denies the overgeneralization "good for you ⇒ more is better."

Q13

この町には心をなごませる＿＿＿。Kono machi ni wa kokoro o nagomaseru ___.

Full sentence: この町には心をなごませるものがある。Kono machi ni wa kokoro o nagomaseru mono ga aru.
EN: There's something calming/soothing about this town.
HI: इस शहर में कुछ ऐसा है जो दिल को सुकून देता है।
GU: આ શહેરમાં કંઈક એવું છે જે દિલને શાંતિ આપે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ものだmono da(wrong pattern — general truth, not felt quality)
2ものかmono ka(wrong pattern — rhetorical denial)
3ものがあるmono ga aruthere's something ~ about it
4ものではないmono de wa nai(wrong pattern — prohibition)

Why: Day 5's ものがある — a soothing quality felt about the town itself.

Q14

この問題集は、実際の授業で使った教材を＿＿＿作られている。Kono mondaishuu wa, jissai no jugyou de tsukatta kyouzai o ___ tsukurarete iru.

Full sentence: この問題集は、実際の授業で使った教材をもとにして作られている。Kono mondaishuu wa, jissai no jugyou de tsukatta kyouzai o moto ni shite tsukurarete iru.
EN: This workbook is made based on materials actually used in class.
HI: यह प्रश्न-संग्रह वास्तविक कक्षा में उपयोग की गई सामग्री के आधार पर बनाया गया है।
GU: આ પ્રશ્નસંગ્રહ ખરેખર વર્ગમાં વપરાયેલી સામગ્રીના આધારે બનાવવામાં આવ્યો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1もとにしてmoto ni shitebased on (source material)
2問わずtowazu(wrong pattern here)
3こめてkomete(wrong pattern — putting a feeling into)
4きっかけにしてkikkake ni shite(wrong pattern — a trigger, not source material)

Why: Day 6's をもとにして — the real classroom materials are the source this workbook is built from.

Q15

インターネットでご予約の＿＿＿には、パスワードが必要となります。Intaanetto de goyoyaku no ___ ni wa, pasuwaado ga hitsuyou to narimasu.

Full sentence: インターネットでご予約の際には、パスワードが必要となります。Intaanetto de goyoyaku no sai ni wa, pasuwaado ga hitsuyou to narimasu.
EN: When making a reservation online, a password is required.
HI: इंटरनेट पर बुकिंग करते समय, पासवर्ड ज़रूरी है।
GU: ઇન્ટરનેટ પર બુકિંગ કરતી વખતે, પાસવર્ડ જરૂરી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1最中saichuu(wrong nuance — mid-action)
2際saion the occasion of ~ / when ~
3たびtabi(wrong word — each time, informal)
4かわりkawari(wrong word — instead of)

Why: Day 6's Nの際に — the formal "on the occasion of" reservations, typical of official instructions.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

彼らが理解できるわけがない。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ だ。Karera ga rikai dekiru wake ga nai. ___ ★ ___ da.

Tiles: 1 無駄 (muda)　2 もの (mono)　3 時間の (jikan no)　4 という (to iu)

3 時間の→1 無駄→★ 4 という→2 もの

彼らが理解できるわけがない。時間の無駄というものだ。Karera ga rikai dekiru wake ga nai. Jikan no muda to iu mono da.

EN: There's no way they'd understand. It's simply a waste of time.
HI: उनके समझने की कोई गुंजाइश नहीं। यह तो बस समय की बर्बादी ही है।
GU: તેમને સમજાવાની કોઈ શક્યતા નથી. આ તો બસ સમયનો બગાડ જ છે.

Why: Built on Day 3's というものだ — 時間の無駄 is firmly labeled "simply a waste of time."

Q17

人の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ ではない。Hito no ___ ★ ___ de wa nai.

Tiles: 1 嫌がる (iyagaru)　2 する (suru)　3 ことを (koto o)　4 もの (mono)

1 嫌がる→3 ことを→★ 2 する→4 もの

人の嫌がることをするものではない。Hito no iyagaru koto o suru mono de wa nai.

EN: You shouldn't do things that others dislike.
HI: दूसरों को नापसंद हो वैसा काम नहीं करना चाहिए।
GU: બીજાને ન ગમે એવું કામ ન કરવું જોઈએ.

Why: Built on Day 3's ものではない — general advice against doing something others dislike.

Q18

通信販売のカタログなどがよく届く。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ のだ。Tsuushin hanbai no katarogu nado ga yoku todoku. ___ ★ ___ no da.

Tiles: 1 にかかわらず (ni kakawarazu)　2 しない (shinai)　3 希望する (kibou suru)　4 送られてくる (okurarete kuru)

3 希望する→2 しない→★ 1 にかかわらず→4 送られてくる

通信販売のカタログなどがよく届く。希望するしないにかかわらず送られてくるのだ。Tsuushin hanbai no katarogu nado ga yoku todoku. Kibou suru shinai ni kakawarazu okurarete kuru no da.

EN: Mail-order catalogs and the like often arrive. Regardless of whether you want them or not, they get sent to you.
HI: मेल-ऑर्डर कैटलॉग अक्सर आ जाते हैं। चाहो या न चाहो, वे भेज ही दिए जाते हैं।
GU: મેલ-ઓર્ડર કેટલોગ ઘણીવાર આવી જાય છે. ઈચ્છો કે ન ઈચ્છો, તે મોકલાઈ જ જાય છે.

Why: Built on Day 1's にかかわらず — pairs "want/don't want" (希望するしない) as two alternatives that don't change the outcome.

Q19

彼に本を貸したら＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 。Kare ni hon o kashitara ___ ★ ___ .

Tiles: 1 貸すものか。 (kasu mono ka)　2 返ってきた。 (kaette kita)　3 もう絶対に (mou zettai ni)　4 汚くなって (kitanaku natte)

4 汚くなって→2 返ってきた。→★ 3 もう絶対に→1 貸すものか。

彼に本を貸したら汚くなって返ってきた。もう絶対に貸すものか。Kare ni hon o kashitara kitanaku natte kaette kita. Mou zettai ni kasu mono ka.

EN: When I lent him a book, it came back dirty. I will never lend to him again!
HI: उसे किताब उधार दी तो गंदी होकर वापस आई। अब कभी उधार नहीं दूँगा!
GU: તેને પુસ્તક ઉધાર આપ્યું તો ગંદું થઈને પાછું આવ્યું. હવે ક્યારેય ઉધાર નહીં આપું!

Why: Built on Day 3's ものか — an emphatic rhetorical denial, "I will never lend again!"

Q20

たばこが＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ したが、失敗した。Tabako ga ___ ★ ___ shita ga, shippai shita.

Tiles: 1 きっかけに (kikkake ni)　2 値上がりした (neagari shita)　3 やめようと (yameyou to)　4 のを (no o)

2 値上がりした→4 のを→★ 1 きっかけに→3 やめようと

たばこが値上がりしたのをきっかけにやめようとしたが、失敗した。Tabako ga neagari shita no o kikkake ni yameyou to shita ga, shippai shita.

EN: Taking the price increase of cigarettes as an opportunity, (I) tried to quit, but failed.
HI: सिगरेट के दाम बढ़ने को मौक़ा बनाकर छोड़ने की कोशिश की, लेकिन नाकाम रहा।
GU: સિગારેટના ભાવ વધવાને તક બનાવીને છોડવાનો પ્રયત્ન કર્યો, પણ નિષ્ફળ રહ્યો.

Why: Built on Day 6's をきっかけに — the price increase (a Vたのを clause) triggered the attempt to quit.

問題3 — reading passage, five blanks (1問4点)
