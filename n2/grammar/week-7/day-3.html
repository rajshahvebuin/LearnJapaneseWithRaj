1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ものだ／もんだ💬

neutral register
Reading~mono da / ~monda (casual)
Meaning (EN)"that's just how it is / that's naturally so" (general truth/tendency) — or "I really wish ~" (heartfelt hope, with Vたい／Vてほしい)
Meaning (HI)ऐसा ही होता है / ...होना स्वाभाविक है — या "...की बहुत इच्छा है"
Meaning (GU)આવું જ હોય છે — અથવા "...ની ખૂબ ઇચ્છા છે"
Connection (接続)① Vる/Vない・Aい/Aくない・naな/naじゃない + ものだ／もんだ💬 (一般的傾向, general trend)  |  ② Vたい/Vたくない・Vてほしい/ほしくない + ものだ／もんだ💬 (希望, hope)
RegisterNeutral; もんだ (💬 speech-bubble icon in the source) is the casual spoken contraction
Typical useTwo related uses in one form: (1) stating a general truth/tendency about how things naturally are, and (2) voicing a heartfelt wish ("I really want to ~" / "I wish someone would ~").
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (general trend)Aい + ものだ苦いものだnigai mono da
Verb / な-adj / Noun (general trend, added, not in book)Vる／naな + ものだ勉強するものだbenkyou suru mono da
Verb (hope/wish)V-たい／V-てほしい(くない) + ものだ行ってみたいものだitte mitai mono da ／ してほしくないものだshite hoshikunai mono da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
薬は苦いものだ。Kusuri wa nigai mono da. Medicine is meant to be bitter. दवा तो कड़वी होती ही है। દવા તો કડવી હોય જ છે.
あなたの国へ行ってみたいものだ。Anata no kuni e itte mitai mono da. I wish I could go to your country. तुम्हारे देश जाने की बहुत इच्छा है। તમારા દેશમાં જવાની ખૂબ ઇચ્છા છે.
娘には私と同じ仕事はしてほしくないものだ。Musume ni wa watashi to onaji shigoto wa shite hoshikunai mono da. I do not want my daughter doing the same work as mine. बेटी को मेरे जैसा काम करते हुए नहीं देखना चाहती। દીકરીને મારા જેવું કામ કરતી જોવી નથી.

もんだ (💬 in the source) is the everyday spoken contraction — expect it in dialogue, not formal writing. Today's four points all revolve around もの/もん, so keep an eye on what comes right before it (bare adjective/verb vs というvs か) — that's the whole game in §3.

【2】 〜ものではない／もんじゃない💬

neutral register
Reading~mono de wa nai / ~monja nai (casual)
Meaning (EN)"one shouldn't ~ / that's just not done" — gentle, general moral advice about proper behavior
Meaning (HI)...करना उचित नहीं / ...नहीं करना चाहिए
Meaning (GU)...કરવું યોગ્ય નથી
Connection (接続)Vる + ものではない／もんじゃない💬
RegisterNeutral, advice-giving tone
Typical useGives general, common-sense advice or a mild moral admonition — the book itself cross-references this to 〜べきではない (p.70) as a near-synonym, though ものではない reads softer, more like a life lesson than a strict rule.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ものではないするものではないsuru mono de wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
目上の人にそんな言い方をするものではない。Meue no hito ni sonna iikata o suru mono de wa nai. You shouldn't speak like that to someone superior. बड़ों से उस तरह बात नहीं करनी चाहिए। વડીલો સાથે એ રીતે વાત ન કરવી જોઈએ.
口の中にものを入れたまましゃべるものではない。Kuchi no naka ni mono o ireta mama shaberu mono de wa nai. You shouldn't speak with your mouth full. मुँह में खाना भरकर बात नहीं करनी चाहिए। મોંમાં ખોરાક ભરીને વાત ન કરવી જોઈએ.

Softer than a rule, closer to "that's just not how it's done." もんじゃない (casual, 💬) shows up often in spoken scolding — a parent or teacher correcting behavior.

【3】 〜というものだ／というもんだ💬

neutral register
Reading~to iu mono da / ~to iu monda (casual)
Meaning (EN)"that is simply X / that's just what X is" — firmly labels a specific situation as a clear instance of a general category, often a judgment (unreasonable, impossible)
Meaning (HI)यह तो बस ...ही है
Meaning (GU)આ તો બસ ...જ છે
Connection (接続)Vる／な-adj／N + というものだ／というもんだ💬
RegisterNeutral
Typical usePasses a firm judgment that the situation just described clearly IS an instance of the following label — very common for calling something 無理 (unreasonable) or 非常識 (senseless).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + というものだ無理というものだmuri to iu mono da
Noun (2nd example)N + というものだ非常識というものだhijoushiki to iu mono da
Verb (added, not in book)Vる + というものだそれは言い過ぎというものだsore wa iisugi to iu mono da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日中にこれを全部終わらせるのは無理というものだ。Kyoujuu ni kore o zenbu owaraseru no wa muri to iu mono da. It is impossible to finish this all today. आज ही यह सब पूरा करना नामुमकिन ही है। આજે જ આ બધું પૂરું કરવું અશક્ય જ છે.
夜中に電話をしてくるのは非常識というものだ。Yonaka ni denwa o shite kuru no wa hijoushiki to iu mono da. It is unreasonable to call at midnight. आधी रात को फ़ोन करना तो बेतुका ही है। મધ્યરાત્રે ફોન કરવો એ તો ગેરવ્યાજબી જ છે.

The という is doing real work here — it's what turns a bare label into "that IS simply X" as a firm characterization of a specific situation, rather than point 1's plain ものだ, which states a general truth about a subject without labeling a specific case.

【4】 〜ものか／もんか💬／ものですか❤／もんですか💬❤

neutral, emphatic/colloquial
Reading~mono ka / ~monka / ~mono desu ka / ~mon desu ka
Meaning (EN)"as if ~! / there's no way ~!" — a rhetorical question that means the flat opposite: an emphatic, often indignant, denial
Meaning (HI)भला ...होगा! / बिल्कुल नहीं!
Meaning (GU)ભલા ...થશે! / બિલકુલ નહીં!
Connection (接続)Vる + ものか／もんか💬  |  な-adj(な)/い-adj + もんか／もんですか💬❤／ものですか❤ (register pairs — see notes)
RegisterNeutral-to-emphatic, colloquial; the ❤ icon (no legend given in the source — inferred from context) marks the ですか-ending examples, which read as more emotionally charged/exclamatory than the plain ものか/もんか forms
Typical useAsks a rhetorical "is it ~?" purely to answer "absolutely not!" Frequently strengthened with 決して〜ものか (never), 絶対〜ものか (absolutely not), 何も〜ものか (nothing at all) placed before the predicate.

VerbVる + ものか行くものかiku mono ka

い-adjective (casual, ですか-form)Aい + もんですか💬❤うれしいもんですかureshii mon desu ka

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms build on もの／もん, which is exactly why the book teaches them together — and why every quiz item above hinges on telling them apart. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ものだ（もんだ）~mono da That's just how it is; or "I really wish ~" V/A/na(plain) + ものだ; Vたい/てほしい + ものだ States a general truth/tendency OR a heartfelt wish — never advice/prohibition. You're describing what's naturally true, or expressing "I really want/wish ~."
〜ものではない（もんじゃない）~mono de wa nai One shouldn't ~ Vる + ものではない Gentle, general moral advice against an action (≈べきではない). You want to say "that's just not done / you shouldn't do that."
〜というものだ（というもんだ）~to iu mono da That is simply X / that's just what X is Vる／な-adj／N + というものだ Firmly labels/characterizes a specific situation as an instance of a category (often judgmental). You want to pass judgment: "that IS simply unreasonable/impossible."
〜ものか（もんか／ものですか）~mono ka As if ~! There's no way ~! Vる+ものか; な/Aい(casual)+もんか／ものですか Rhetorical question that means a flat, emphatic denial — never a real question. You want to angrily/emphatically deny a suggestion or possibility.
Exam trap: everything here contains もの/もん, but the functions split in opposite directions: ものだ affirms a truth or wish, ものではない forbids an action, というものだ judges a specific case as an instance of a broader label, and ものか rhetorically denies with maximum emphasis. When a question gives you a bare adjective/noun before もの-something, check whether という appears (labeling a specific situation, point 3) versus a bare ものだ (general truth, point 1) — that split is exactly what Q1 vs Q2 test above.
