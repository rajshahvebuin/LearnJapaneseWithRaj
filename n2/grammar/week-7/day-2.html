Notes
All four points are "pairing" patterns that name two things and draw one conclusion from the pair — the book's own 練習Ⅰ deliberately mixes them up (see §2, note that ①–⑤ all fill the same blank twice). None of today's forms are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; all four are neutral register.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜やら〜やら

neutral register
Reading~yara ~yara
Meaning (EN)"things like ~ and ~ (among others)" — names representative examples, often with a nuance of clutter or being overwhelmed
Meaning (HI)...वगैरह-वगैरह / ...जैसी कई चीज़ें
Meaning (GU)...વગેરે વગેરે
Connection (接続)Nやら Nやら  |  A/な/V(plain普) やら A/な/V(plain普) やら 【aやらbやら】
RegisterNeutral — conversational-leaning
Typical useLists 2+ representative (not exhaustive) examples, frequently with an undertone of "too much going on" — symptoms piling up, objects strewn everywhere, troubles stacking. Often paired with 大変です／いっぱいだ.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNやら + Nやら本やらノートやらhon yara nooto yara
Verb (plain)V-plainやら + V-plainやら目がかゆいやら鼻水が出るやらme ga kayui yara hanamizu ga deru yara
い-adjective (added, not in book)Aいやら + Aいやら忙しいやらつらいやらisogashii yara tsurai yara
な-adjective (added, not in book)naやら + naやら心配やら不安やらshinpai yara fuan yara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
机の上は本やらノートやらでいっぱいだ。Tsukue no ue wa hon yara nooto yara de ippai da. The desk is covered with books, notebooks and so on. मेज़ पर किताबें, नोटबुक वगैरह से भरा हुआ है। ટેબલ પર પુસ્તકો, નોટબુક વગેરેથી ભરેલું છે.
この季節は、目がかゆいやら鼻水が出るやら、大変です。Kono kisetsu wa, me ga kayui yara hanamizu ga deru yara, taihen desu. My itchy eyes and runny nose, for example, make this season hard for me. इस मौसम में, आँखों में खुजली होती है, नाक बहती है वगैरह — बहुत मुश्किल होता है। આ ઋતુમાં, આંખોમાં ખંજવાળ આવે છે, નાક વહે છે વગેરે — બહુ મુશ્કેલ છે.

Unlike a plain list with と or や, やら〜やら carries a nuance of clutter or being overwhelmed. It's the go-to pattern for describing symptoms piling up, objects scattered everywhere, or several unpleasant feelings hitting at once (see Q5 in §2).

【2】 〜につけ〜につけ

neutral register
Reading~ni tsuke ~ni tsuke
Meaning (EN)"whenever ~ / every time ~ (regardless of which)" — pairs two contrasting perception verbs/adjectives and says the same feeling follows either way
Meaning (HI)चाहे ...हो या ...हो, हर बार
Meaning (GU)ભલે ...હોય કે ...હોય, દર વખતે
Connection (接続)Vるに(つけ) ／Aいに + つけ 【aにつけ(bにつけ)】
RegisterNeutral, slightly literary but common
Typical useRepeats with two related but contrasting verbs of perception (見る／聞く) or evaluative adjectives (いい／悪い) to mean "whichever one happens, the same result/feeling follows." Also forms the fixed idiom 何かにつけ ("at every little opportunity").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (pair)Vるに + つけ写真を見るにつけ（歌を聞くにつけ）shashin o miru ni tsuke, uta o kiku ni tsuke
い-adjective (pair)Aいに + つけいいにつけ悪いにつけii ni tsuke warui ni tsuke
Noun (fixed idiom only)(added, not in book; rare outside 何かにつけ) Nに + つけ何かにつけnanika ni tsuke
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
写真を見るにつけ（、歌を聞くにつけ）、国を思い出す。Shashin o miru ni tsuke, uta o kiku ni tsuke, kuni o omoidasu. Photos (and songs) from my country make me nostalgic. तस्वीरें देखूं तो भी, गाने सुनूं तो भी, अपने देश की याद आ जाती है। ફોટા જોઉં તો પણ, ગીતો સાંભળું તો પણ, મારા દેશની યાદ આવે છે.
いいにつけ悪いにつけ、子は親に似る。Ii ni tsuke warui ni tsuke, ko wa oya ni niru. Children learn good and bad habits from their parents. अच्छा हो या बुरा, बच्चे माता-पिता जैसे ही होते हैं। સારું હોય કે ખરાબ, બાળકો માતાપિતા જેવા જ હોય છે.
(bonus もっと！) 何かにつけnanika ni tsuke at every (little) opportunity हर छोटी बात पर દરેક નાની બાબતે

Limited to perception-type verbs (見る, 聞く, 考える) or evaluative adjectives (いい, 悪い) — that's the key difference from point 3's にしろ〜にしろ, which can pair almost any two alternatives. When the sentence ends in an emotional/experiential observation rather than an instruction, につけ is the natural fit (see §3).

【3】 〜にしろ〜にしろ／〜にせよ〜にせよ

neutral register
Reading~ni shiro ~ni shiro / ~ni seyo ~ni seyo
Meaning (EN)"whether ~ or ~ / regardless of which" — names two (often opposite) options and states a conclusion/obligation that holds for both
Meaning (HI)चाहे ...हो या ...हो
Meaning (GU)ભલે ...હોય કે ...હોય
Connection (接続)V／Aい／な-adj・N (plain, no だ) + に + しろ／せよ 【aにしろ(bにしろ)】／【aにせよ(bにせよ)】
RegisterNeutral-to-slightly formal — にせよ reads more formal/written than にしろ
Typical usePairs two contrasting or complementary options (go/not go, do/not do) and states that an instruction or obligation applies no matter which is true. The source flags な-adjective／noun + だ + にしろ／にせよ as an error, the same "drop the だ" trap seen with 〜にもかかわらず.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (aff/neg pair)Vる-Vないに + しろ行くにしろ行かないにしろiku ni shiro ikanai ni shiro
Verb (single, "whatever you do")Vないに + しろ受験はしないにしろjuken wa shinai ni shiro
な-adjective／Noun (plain, no だ)(added, not in book) な-adj/N (no だ) + にしろ  ✗ +だ+にしろ／にせよ marked as an error in the source冗談にしろ本気にしろjoudan ni shiro honki ni shiro
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
行くにしろ行かないにしろ、連絡してください。Iku ni shiro ikanai ni shiro, renraku shite kudasai. Please contact me regardless of whether you're going or not. जाएं या न जाएं, कृपया संपर्क करें। જાઓ કે ન જાઓ, કૃપા કરી સંપર્ક કરો.
受験はしないにしろ、勉強はしなさい。Juken wa shinai ni shiro, benkyou wa shinasai. Study regardless of whether you take the entrance examinations. प्रवेश परीक्षा न भी दें, तो भी पढ़ाई करो। પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષા ન આપો તોપણ, અભ્યાસ કરો.

にしろ and にせよ are functionally interchangeable; にせよ leans more written/formal. Similar in spirit to 〜ても (even if), but にしろ〜にしろ explicitly names two alternatives rather than a single hypothetical, and typically leads into an instruction or obligation (くださいなさい, なければならない).

【4】 N1も〜ばN2も〜 (both X and Y are ~)

neutral register
ReadingN1 mo ~ba N2 mo ~
Meaning (EN)"both X is ~ and Y is ~ (as well)" — an emphatic double listing of matching qualities/actions, not a real conditional
Meaning (HI)X भी ~है और Y भी ~है
Meaning (GU)X પણ ~છે અને Y પણ ~છે
Connection (接続)N1もVばN2もVる ／ N1もAければN2もAい ／ N1もnaならN2もnaだ ／ N1もNならN2もNだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe ば／なら here is NOT a true conditional (it does not mean "if") — it's a fixed frame for stacking two parallel qualities or actions onto the same subject (or two subjects), each marked with も.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbN1もV-ばN2もV-る勉強もできればスポーツもできるbenkyou mo dekireba supootsu mo dekiru
い-adjective (added, not in book)N1もAければN2もAい頭もよければ性格もいいatama mo yokereba seikaku mo ii
な-adjectiveN1もnaならN2もnaだ歌も下手ならダンスも下手だuta mo heta nara dansu mo heta da
Noun (added, not in book)N1もNならN2もNだ平日も休みなら週末も休みだheijitsu mo yasumi nara shuumatsu mo yasumi da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は勉強もできればスポーツもできる。Kare wa benkyou mo dekireba supootsu mo dekiru. He is outstanding, academically and in sports. वह पढ़ाई में भी अच्छा है और खेल में भी। તે અભ્યાસમાં પણ સારો છે અને રમતગમતમાં પણ.
私は歌も下手ならダンスも下手だ。Watashi wa uta mo heta nara dansu mo heta da. I am bad at singing and at dancing. मैं गाने में भी कमज़ोर हूँ और नाच में भी। હું ગાવામાં પણ નબળો છું અને નાચમાં પણ.

Also works in negative form (もなければ〜もない, "there's neither X nor Y" — see Q2 in §2). Don't read the ば/なら as "if" here; the whole point of the pattern is to state both halves as simultaneously, definitely true.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items — note the book's own instruction: "the same word goes in both blanks," so each item fills one repeating pair) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain underscore blanks, no ★), both from p.113. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.115.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option (same word fills both blanks)

Q1

今日は、銀行（　）郵便局（　）、あちこちに用事がある。Kyou wa, ginkou (___) yuubinkyoku (___), achikochi ni youji ga aru.

Full sentence: 今日は、銀行やら郵便局やら、あちこちに用事がある。Kyou wa, ginkou yara yuubinkyoku yara, achikochi ni youji ga aru.
EN: Today I have errands here and there — at the bank, the post office, and so on.
HI: आज बैंक, डाकघर वगैरह, इधर-उधर काम हैं।
GU: આજે બેંક, પોસ્ટ ઓફિસ વગેરે, અહીં-તહીં કામ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aやらyarathings like ~ (and others)वगैरहવગેરે
bつけtsuke(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 銀行・郵便局 are simple nouns listed as examples of "errands here and there" — that's やら〜やら's job (point 1); につけ needs a verb/adjective pair expressing "whenever X, whenever Y," not a flat noun list.

Q2

この町にはデパート（　）なければ大型スーパー（　）ない。Kono machi ni wa depaato (___) nakereba ougata suupaa (___) nai.

Full sentence: この町にはデパートもなければ大型スーパーもない。Kono machi ni wa depaato mo nakereba ougata suupaa mo nai.
EN: This town doesn't have a department store, nor does it have a big supermarket.
HI: इस शहर में न तो डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर है, न बड़ा सुपरमार्केट।
GU: આ શહેરમાં ન તો ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર છે, ન મોટું સુપરમાર્કેટ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aもmoneither X nor Y (negative も〜ば〜も)न तो...नન તો...ન
bやらyara(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: This is point 4's も〜ば〜も frame in its negative form (もなければ〜もない, "there's neither X nor Y"); やら〜やら would instead suggest a messy list of several examples, not a clean two-item negation.

EN: Whether I'm happy or sad, I listen to this song.
HI: चाहे खुश होऊं या उदास, मैं यह गाना सुनता हूँ।
GU: ભલે ખુશ હોઉં કે ઉદાસ, હું આ ગીત સાંભળું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

HI: समय हो या न हो, आज ही करना होगा।
GU: સમય હોય કે ન હોય, આજે જ કરવું પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

高い＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ならない。Takai ___ naranai.

Tiles: 1 必要なら (hitsuyou nara)　2 安いにしろ (yasui ni shiro)　3 買わなければ (kawanakereba)　4 にしろ (ni shiro)

4 にしろ→2 安いにしろ→1 必要なら→3 買わなければ

高いにしろ安いにしろ、必要なら買わなければならない。Takai ni shiro yasui ni shiro, hitsuyou nara kawanakereba naranai.

EN: Whether it's expensive or cheap, if it's necessary, you have to buy it.
HI: चाहे महंगा हो या सस्ता, ज़रूरी हो तो खरीदना ही होगा।
GU: ભલે મોંઘું હોય કે સસ્તું, જરૂરી હોય તો ખરીદવું જ પડશે.

Why: Built on point 3 (Aいにしろ Aいにしろ) — 高いにしろ安いにしろ frames "whichever the price," then 必要なら買わなければならない states the actual obligation.

Q7

大家さんには＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ になっている。Ooyasan ni wa ___ ni natte iru.

Tiles: 1 つけ (tsuke)　2 何か (nanika)　3 お世話 (osewa)　4 に (ni)

2 何か→4 に→1 つけ→3 お世話

大家さんには何かにつけお世話になっている。Ooyasan ni wa nanika ni tsuke osewa ni natte iru.

EN: I'm always relying on my landlord's help, one way or another.
HI: मकान मालिक की हर बात में मदद लेता रहता हूँ।
GU: મકાનમાલિકની દરેક બાબતમાં મદદ લેતો રહું છું.

Why: Built on point 2's bonus idiom 何かにつけ ("at every opportunity / one way or another") — 何か + につけ + お世話になっている, "indebted to (them) for all sorts of things."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms are "pairing" patterns that name two things and draw a conclusion from the pair — exactly why the book's own 練習Ⅰ fills the same blank twice in every item, forcing you to tell やら, につけ, にしろ, and も apart. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜やら〜やら~yara ~yara Things like X and Y (among others), often overwhelming N/V/A/na(plain) やら 〜やら Lists 2+ representative (often unpleasant/chaotic) examples; not a closed pair. You want "there's X, Y, and more like it — what a lot/mess."
〜につけ〜につけ~ni tsuke ~ni tsuke Whenever X or Y, same result either way V(plain)／Aいに + つけ Pairs 2 opposite perception verbs/adjectives (見る/聞く, いい/悪い); result/feeling is constant regardless. You want "every time, whichever one happens, the same thing follows."
〜にしろ〜にしろ（にせよ）~ni shiro ~ni shiro Whether X or Y, regardless of choice V/Aい/な・N(plain, no だ)に + しろ／せよ Pairs almost any 2 alternatives; typically leads into an instruction or obligation. You want "no matter which of these you pick/is true, do Y."
〜も〜ば〜も~mo ~ba ~mo Both X is ~ and Y is ~ N1もVば/Aければ/naなら N2もVる/Aい/naだ Not really conditional — an emphatic double listing of matching qualities/actions. You want to stack up two qualities/actions as both simultaneously true.
Exam trap: にしろ／にせよ vs につけ look similar (both pair two things with に + verb), but につけ is limited to perception-type verbs (見る, 聞く, 考える) or evaluative adjectives (いい, 悪い) describing "whichever happens, the same feeling results," while にしろ／にせよ works with almost any two contrasting options and typically precedes an instruction or obligation (なければならない, てください). If the sentence continues with a command or must-do, lean にしろ／にせよ; if it continues with an emotional/experiential conclusion tied to perception, lean につけ.
