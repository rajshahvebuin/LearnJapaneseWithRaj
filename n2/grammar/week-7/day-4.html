4日目 — 心をこめて (Kokoro o komete)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Bunpou) — printed p.116–117 (PDF pages 116–117)
Grammar points covered
東京を中心に感謝の気持ちをこめて友人を通じて地図を頼りに
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.119 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points are 「Nを〜」 patterns that superficially look alike but attach to very different kinds of nouns (a focal area, a feeling, a channel, a support) — see §3. None of today's forms are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; all four are neutral register.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜を中心に(して)／〜を中心とした

neutral register
Reading~o chuushin ni (shite) / ~o chuushin to shita
Meaning (EN)"centering on ~ / mainly ~" — names the core focus of an activity, area, or group, with everything else built around it
Meaning (HI)...को केंद्र में रखकर / मुख्य रूप से...
Meaning (GU)...ને કેન્દ્રમાં રાખીને / મુખ્યત્વે...
Connection (接続)Nを + 中心に／中心にして／中心にしたN／中心として／中心としたN
RegisterNeutral — common in both spoken and written scope descriptions
Typical useNames the "center" of an activity/area/group. The pre-noun forms (中心にしたN, 中心としたN) attach directly before a following noun; に／として work adverbially inside a clause, modifying a verb.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (adverbial)N + を中心に東京を中心にToukyou o chuushin ni
Noun (adverbial, alt.)N + を中心として中村さんを中心としてNakamura-san o chuushin to shite
Noun (pre-noun modifier)N + を中心とした + Nスキー用品を中心としたスポーツ専門店sukii youhin o chuushin to shita supootsu senmon-ten
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
東京を中心に関東地方は台風の影響で風が強くなっています。Toukyou o chuushin ni Kantou chihou wa taifuu no eikyou de kaze ga tsuyoku natte imasu. In the Kanto Region, especially the Tokyo area, the winds are strong because of a typhoon. टोक्यो को केंद्र मानकर, कान्तो क्षेत्र में तूफ़ान के असर से हवा तेज़ हो रही है। ટોક્યોને કેન્દ્ર માનીને, કાન્તો વિસ્તારમાં તોફાનની અસરથી પવન તેજ થઈ રહ્યો છે.
この店はスキー用品を中心としたスポーツ専門店です。Kono mise wa sukii youhin o chuushin to shita supootsu senmon-ten desu. This sports store sells mainly ski equipment. यह दुकान मुख्य रूप से स्की के सामान की खेल-विशेष दुकान है। આ દુકાન મુખ્યત્વે સ્કી સામાનની રમત-વિશેષ દુકાન છે.

Cross-referenced by the book to 〜を主とした (p.39) as a near-synonym. Remember the split: 中心とした needs a following noun to modify; 中心に／として work adverbially before a verb — mixing these up is exactly what Q2 and Q5 in §2 test.

【2】 〜をこめて 《込めて》

neutral register
Reading~o komete
Meaning (EN)"putting ~ into it / with ~" — doing something while investing a feeling (gratitude, love, a wish) into the act
Meaning (HI)...भरकर / ...की भावना डालकर
Meaning (GU)...ભરીને
Connection (接続)N + をこめて
RegisterNeutral, often warm/emotional in tone
Typical useAttaches to an abstract feeling-noun (感謝 gratitude, 愛 love, 恨み resentment, 祈り prayer, 願い wish) and describes performing the following action while pouring that feeling into it.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (feeling)N + をこめて感謝の気持ちをこめてkansha no kimochi o komete
Noun (related set from the box)思い／恨み／感謝／祈り／願い + をこめて祈りをこめてinori o komete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
感謝の気持ちをこめて編んだマフラーです。Kansha no kimochi o komete anda mafuraa desu. This is a scarf that I wove as a sign of my gratitude. यह आभार की भावना भरकर बुना हुआ मफ़लर है। આ આભારની ભાવના ભરીને ગૂંથેલો મફલર છે.
愛をこめてカードを贈る。Ai o komete kaado o okuru. Send a card with love. प्यार भरकर कार्ड भेजो। પ્રેમ ભરીને કાર્ડ મોકલો.
(bonus もっと！) 心をこめる→心がこもる→心のこもった手紙kokoro o komeru → kokoro ga komoru → kokoro no komotta tegami put one's heart into it → one's heart is filled with it → a heartfelt letter दिल से करना → दिल भर जाना → दिल से लिखा पत्र દિલથી કરવું → દિલ ભરાઈ જવું → દિલથી લખેલો પત્ર

こめる is transitive ("to put/pour X into Y"); its intransitive partner こもる ("to be filled with/contained") shows up in the attributive past form 込もった when directly modifying a noun, as in 心のこもった手紙 ("a heartfelt letter") — a small transitive/intransitive pair worth memorizing together.

【3】 〜を通じて／〜を通して

neutral register
Reading~o tsuujite / ~o tsuushite
Meaning (EN)"through ~ / via ~" (a means or intermediary) — or "throughout ~" (a continuous span of time)
Meaning (HI)...के माध्यम से / ...के दौरान (पूरे समय)
Meaning (GU)...ના માધ્યમથી / ...દરમિયાન (આખો સમય)
Connection (接続)Nを + 通じて／通して
RegisterNeutral
Typical useTwo related uses: (1) a means/channel/intermediary ("through a friend," "through the news"), and (2) a continuous time span across which something holds true ("all year round," 一年を通じて／通して).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (means/channel)N + を通じて友人を通じてyuujin o tsuujite
Noun (time span)N + を通じて／通して一年を通じてichinen o tsuujite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
友人を通じて彼と知り合った。Yuujin o tsuujite kare to shiriatta. I met him through a friend. एक दोस्त के ज़रिए उससे जान-पहचान हुई। એક મિત્ર દ્વારા તેની ઓળખાણ થઈ.
テレビのニュースを通じてその事件を知った。Terebi no nyuusu o tsuujite sono jiken o shitta. I knew the incident through the news on television. टीवी की ख़बर के ज़रिए उस घटना के बारे में पता चला। ટીવીના સમાચાર દ્વારા તે ઘટના વિશે ખબર પડી.
ここでは一年を通じて美しい花が見られます。Koko de wa ichinen o tsuujite utsukushii hana ga miraremasu. You can see beautiful flowers all year round here. यहाँ पूरे साल सुंदर फूल देखे जा सकते हैं। અહીં આખા વર્ષ દરમિયાન સુંદર ફૂલો જોઈ શકાય છે.

通じて and 通して are largely interchangeable for the time-span use (see Q4 in §2, where the book marks both correct), but この本の例では通じて is preferred for the "means/intermediary" use (友人を通じて) — worth noting as a collocational tendency, not an absolute rule.

【4】 〜を頼りに(して)／〜を頼りとして

neutral register
Reading~o tayori ni (shite) / ~o tayori to shite
Meaning (EN)"relying on ~ / with the help of ~" — doing something using a specific aid or support as a guide
Meaning (HI)...के सहारे / ...की मदद से
Meaning (GU)...ના સહારે
Connection (接続)Nを + 頼りにV／頼りとして／頼りにして
RegisterNeutral
Typical useNames a concrete or abstract aid (a map, a cane, someone's help/connections) that the subject depends on while performing the main action.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + を頼りに地図を頼りにchizu o tayori ni
Noun (alt.)N + を頼りにしてつえを頼りにしてtsue o tayori ni shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
地図を頼りに、友達に教えてもらったレストランに行った。Chizu o tayori ni, tomodachi ni oshiete moratta resutoran ni itta. I went to the restaurant that my friend recommended to me with the help of a map. नक़्शे के सहारे, दोस्त की बताई हुई रेस्टोरेंट गया। નકશાના સહારે, મિત્રએ બતાવેલી રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં ગયો.
祖父はつえを頼りにして歩いている。Sofu wa tsue o tayori ni shite aruite iru. My grandfather walks with the help of a cane. दादाजी छड़ी के सहारे चलते हैं। દાદા લાકડીના સહારે ચાલે છે.

Can describe leaning on something very literal (a map, a cane) or something more abstract (someone's advice or connections). Don't confuse with point 3's を通じて, which names a channel/means for information or a continuous time span, not a support being leaned on.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items — the book's own instruction allows marking both options correct where genuinely both work) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain underscore blanks, no ★), both from p.117. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.119.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option (mark both if both are correct)

Q1

インターネットを（　）世界中の人々と友達になる。Intaanetto o (___) sekaijuu no hitobito to tomodachi ni naru.

Full sentence: インターネットを通じて世界中の人々と友達になる。Intaanetto o tsuujite sekaijuu no hitobito to tomodachi ni naru.
EN: I make friends with people all over the world through the internet.
HI: इंटरनेट के ज़रिए दुनिया भर के लोगों से दोस्ती होती है।
GU: ઇન્ટરનેટ દ્વારા દુનિયાભરના લોકો સાથે મિત્રતા થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a通じてtsuujitethrough (means/channel)के ज़रिएદ્વારા
b中心にchuushin ni(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The internet is the means/channel through which friendships form — point 3's を通じて; を中心に would instead mean the internet is the "main focus," which changes the meaning.

Q2

文法を（　）勉強したい。Bunpou o (___) benkyou shitai.

Full sentence: 文法を中心に勉強したい。Bunpou o chuushin ni benkyou shitai.
EN: I want to study focusing mainly on grammar.
HI: मुख्य रूप से व्याकरण पर ध्यान देकर पढ़ाई करना चाहता हूँ।
GU: મુખ્યત્વે વ્યાકરણ પર ધ્યાન કેન્દ્રિત કરીને અભ્યાસ કરવા માંગુ છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a中心にchuushin nicentering on ~ (adverbial)पर ध्यान देकरપર ધ્યાન કેન્દ્રિત કરીને
b中心としたchuushin to shita(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 勉強したい is a verb, not a noun, so the adverbial 中心に is needed; 中心とした is reserved for directly modifying a following noun (as in スキー用品を中心としたスポーツ専門店).

Q3

平和への祈りを（　）歌を歌う。Heiwa e no inori o (___) uta o utau.

Full sentence: 平和への祈りをこめて歌を歌う。Heiwa e no inori o komete uta o utau.
EN: (They) sing a song, putting a prayer for peace into it.
HI: शांति की प्रार्थना भरकर गाना गाते हैं।
GU: શાંતિની પ્રાર્થના ભરીને ગીત ગાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこめてkometeputting (a feeling) into itभरकरભરીને
b通じてtsuujite(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 祈り (a prayer, an abstract feeling) fits point 2's をこめて ("putting a feeling into the action"); 通じて would misread 祈り as a means/channel rather than an emotional investment.

Q4

この町では一年を（　）様々な行事が行われる。Kono machi de wa ichinen o (___) samazama na gyouji ga okonawareru.

Full sentence: この町では一年を通じて（通して）様々な行事が行われる。Kono machi de wa ichinen o tsuujite (tsuushite) samazama na gyouji ga okonawareru.
EN: In this town, various events are held all year round.
HI: इस शहर में साल भर तरह-तरह के आयोजन होते हैं।
GU: આ શહેરમાં આખું વર્ષ વિવિધ કાર્યક્રમો યોજાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a (also correct)通じてtsuujitethroughout (time span)साल भरઆખું વર્ષ

Why: For the time-span use of point 3, 通じて and 通して are interchangeable — both mean "throughout/across the whole year," so the book marks both a and b correct here.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

入院中、友達が＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 勉強した。Nyuuinchuu, tomodachi ga ___ benkyou shita.

Tiles: 1 ノートを (nooto o)　2 書いてくれた (kaite kureta)　3 たよりに (tayori ni)　4 授業の (jugyou no)

2 書いてくれた→4 授業の→1 ノートを→3 たよりに

入院中、友達が書いてくれた授業のノートをたよりに勉強した。Nyuuinchuu, tomodachi ga kaite kureta jugyou no nooto o tayori ni benkyou shita.

EN: While I was in the hospital, I studied using the class notes my friend wrote for me as my guide.
HI: अस्पताल में भर्ती रहते हुए, दोस्त की लिखी क्लास नोट्स के सहारे पढ़ाई की।
GU: હોસ્પિટલમાં દાખલ હતો ત્યારે, મિત્રએ લખેલી ક્લાસ નોટ્સના સહારે અભ્યાસ કર્યો.

Why: Built on point 4 (Nを頼りに) — 友達が書いてくれた授業のノート (the notes, as a relative clause) + をたよりに + 勉強した ("studied relying on ~").

Q7

大学受験の日、息子が合格＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ お弁当を作った。Daigaku juken no hi, musuko ga goukaku ___ obentou o tsukutta.

Tiles: 1 ようにと (you ni to)　2 こめて (komete)　3 します (shimasu)　4 願いを (negai o)

3 します→1 ようにと→4 願いを→2 こめて

大学受験の日、息子が合格しますようにと願いをこめてお弁当を作った。Daigaku juken no hi, musuko ga goukaku shimasu you ni to negai o komete obentou o tsukutta.

EN: On the day of my son's university entrance exam, I made his lunch box, putting into it my wish for him to pass.
HI: बेटे की यूनिवर्सिटी प्रवेश परीक्षा के दिन, उसके पास होने की कामना भरकर लंच बॉक्स बनाया।
GU: દીકરાની યુનિવર્સિટી પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષાના દિવસે, તે પાસ થાય એવી કામના ભરીને લંચ બોક્સ બનાવ્યું.

Why: Built on point 2 (Nをこめて) — 合格しますように (a wish clause, "may he pass") + と願いを + こめて + お弁当を作った ("made the lunch box, putting the wish into it").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms follow a superficially similar 「Nを〜」 shape, but each attaches to a very different kind of noun — a focal topic, a feeling, a channel, or a support. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜を中心に(として)~o chuushin ni Centering on ~ / mainly ~ Nを中心に(して)／中心としたN Names the main focus/core; とした directly modifies a following noun, に/にして work adverbially. You're describing what's central to an activity, area, or group.
〜をこめて~o komete Putting a feeling into ~ (feeling)Nをこめて Attaches only to abstract feeling-nouns (感謝, 愛, 祈り, 願い); describes investing emotion into an action. You want to say an action was done with heartfelt feeling.
〜を通じて／通して~o tsuujite Through ~ (means/channel) or throughout ~ (time) Nを通じて／通して Names an intermediary/channel OR a continuous time span — not about focus or feeling. You want "via X" or "all through X (time)."
〜を頼りに(して)~o tayori ni Relying on ~ / with the help of ~ Nを頼りに(して) Names a concrete or abstract support the subject leans on while acting. You want "using X as a crutch/guide."
Exam trap: を中心に vs を頼りに look similar (both follow a noun that "helps" the main action), but 中心に means that noun is the central focus/topic everything else revolves around, while 頼りに means the noun is a support/aid being leaned on (a map, a cane, someone's help) — one is about topical focus, the other about dependency for support. Also remember: とした (中心とした) only works directly before a noun, never before a verb, unlike に／にして — exactly the trap tested in Q2 and Q5 above.
