Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.124 (7日目 実戦問題's third page) — confirmed, not guessed. (Because Day 7 spans three pages this week, the answer key lands one page later than the usual "next day's second page.")
Notes
Two of today's four points carry the 硬 formal/written badge in the source (につき, and the 契機 variant of きっかけに); をもとに and 際に carry it only partially or not at all — details in each point below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜をもとに(して)

neutral register
Reading~o moto ni (shite)
Meaning (EN)"based on ~ / using ~ as a basis" — names the source material or foundation something is created or derived from
Meaning (HI)...के आधार पर
Meaning (GU)...ના આધારે
Connection (接続)Nを + もとに／もとにして
RegisterNeutral
Typical useNames underlying facts, materials, or experience that something is built from — common for novels "based on" real events, inventions arising "from" failures, decisions "based on" data.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をもとに事実をもとにjijitsu o moto ni
Noun (alt.)N + をもとにして失敗をもとにしてshippai o moto ni shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この小説は事実をもとに書かれた。Kono shousetsu wa jijitsu o moto ni kakareta. This novel is based on fact. यह उपन्यास तथ्यों पर आधारित है। આ નવલકથા હકીકતો પર આધારિત છે.
失敗をもとにして発明する。Shippai o moto ni shite hatsumei suru. An invention results from failure. असफलता के आधार पर आविष्कार होता है। નિષ્ફળતાના આધારે શોધ થાય છે.

Cross-referenced by the book to 〜に基づいて (p.88) as a near-synonym. をもとに looks at source material feeding INTO a result (a novel built from facts); contrast with point 3's きっかけに, which names a trigger MOMENT for a change, not raw material.

【2】 〜につき

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni tsuki
Meaning (EN)"because of ~ / due to ~" — a formal reason marker, typical of signs and official notices
Meaning (HI)...के कारण
Meaning (GU)...ના કારણે
Connection (接続)N + につき
Register硬 — the book itself notes: 書面や改まった場面でよく使われる ("often used in writing and formal situations")
Typical useStates a reason on official signage or formal announcements — "closed due to maintenance," "closed due to a holiday," etc.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + につき調整中につきchouseichuu ni tsuki
Noun (2nd example)N + につき祭日につきsaijitsu ni tsuki
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この機械はただ今調整中につき、ご使用になれません。Kono kikai wa tadaima chouseichuu ni tsuki, goshiyou ni naremasen. This machine is currently unavailable due to maintenance. यह मशीन अभी रखरखाव के कारण उपयोग में नहीं है। આ મશીન હાલમાં જાળવણીના કારણે વાપરી શકાતું નથી.
本日は祭日につき、休業させていただきます。Honjitsu wa saijitsu ni tsuki, kyuugyou sasete itadakimasu. Due to the holiday, we are closed today. आज छुट्टी के कारण, हम बंद हैं। આજે રજાના કારણે, અમે બંધ છીએ.

Think shop signs, notices, official memos — not casual conversation, where you'd just say 〜だから／〜ので instead.

【3】 〜をきっかけに(して)／〜を契機に(して)

neutral (契機 forms are 硬)
Reading~o kikkake ni (shite) / ~o keiki ni (shite)
Meaning (EN)"taking ~ as an opportunity/trigger" — names an event that prompted a subsequent change or new action
Meaning (HI)...को मौक़ा/कारण बनाकर
Meaning (GU)...ને તક/કારણ બનાવીને
Connection (接続)Nを／Vるのを／Vたのを + きっかけに／きっかけとして／きっかけにして／契機に硬／契機として硬／契機にして硬
Registerきっかけ forms are neutral; the 契機 forms are marked 硬 in the source — more formal, common in news reports
Typical useNames a specific event/moment that triggered a subsequent change — often a positive turning point (illness → quitting a habit, graduation → independence, an incident → a policy review).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + をきっかけに入学をきっかけにnyuugaku o kikkake ni
Noun (formal)N + を契機に 硬卒業を契機にsotsugyou o keiki ni
VerbVたのを + きっかけに病気をきっかけにbyouki o kikkake ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大学入学をきっかけに引っ越す。Daigaku nyuugaku o kikkake ni hikkosu. Because I'll be going to university, I will move. यूनिवर्सिटी में दाख़िले को मौक़ा बनाकर घर बदल रहा हूँ। યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશને તક બનાવીને ઘર બદલી રહ્યો છું.
病気をきっかけに酒をやめた。Byouki o kikkake ni sake o yameta. As a result of an illness, I stopped drinking. बीमारी को कारण बनाकर शराब छोड़ दी। બીમારીને કારણ બનાવીને દારૂ છોડી દીધો.
卒業を契機に独立する。Sotsugyou o keiki ni dokuritsu suru. I'll take the opportunity of graduating from university to become independent. स्नातक होने को मौक़ा बनाकर स्वतंत्र हो रहा हूँ। સ્નાતક થવાને તક બનાવીને સ્વતંત્ર થઈ રહ્યો છું.
昨年の事故を契機として、安全対策が強化された。Sakunen no jiko o keiki to shite, anzen taisaku ga kyouka sareta. As a result of last year's accident, safety measures were strengthened. पिछले साल की दुर्घटना को कारण बनाकर, सुरक्षा उपाय मज़बूत किए गए। ગયા વર્ષની દુર્ઘટનાને કારણ બનાવીને, સલામતી પગલાં મજબૂત કરાયા.

きっかけ and 契機 mean the same thing; 契機 is the more formal/written option, common in news reports about policy changes following an incident. Both always take the particle を, never の — distinguish from point 1's もとに, which is about source material feeding into a result, not a triggering event/moment.

【4】 〜際に／〜際

硬 formal/written
Reading~sai ni / ~sai
Meaning (EN)"at the time of ~ / on the occasion of ~" — the formal version of とき
Meaning (HI)...के समय / ...के अवसर पर
Meaning (GU)...ના સમયે
Connection (接続)Nの + 際に  |  V-plain(dictionary/past) + 際
Register硬 — the book's own cross-reference notes this as a formal counterpart to 「とき」「〜にあたり」(p.72)
Typical useNames the moment/occasion something happens, typically in formal instructions, forms, or announcements — applying for something, checking out, driving, etc.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNの + 際に受験の際にjuken no sai ni
Verb (plain)V-plain + 際申し込んだ際moushikonda sai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
受験の際に、写真が必要です。Juken no sai ni, shashin ga hitsuyou desu. A photo is required when you come to take the examination. परीक्षा के समय, फ़ोटो ज़रूरी है। પરીક્ષાના સમયે, ફોટો જરૂરી છે.
申し込んだ際、住所を間違って書いてしまった。Moushikonda sai, juusho o machigatte kaite shimatta. I wrote a wrong address when I applied. आवेदन करते समय, पता ग़लत लिख दिया। અરજી કરતી વખતે, સરનામું ખોટું લખાઈ ગયું.
(related set from the box) 〜する際／お申し込みの際／お帰りの際／お降りの際 when ~; when applying; when leaving; when getting off ...करते समय; आवेदन के समय; लौटते समय; उतरते समय ...કરતી વખતે; અરજી સમયે; પાછા ફરતી વખતે; ઉતરતી વખતે

際 reads noticeably more formal than とき and shows up constantly in instructions, forms, and public announcements (お帰りの際, お降りの際 — see Q6 in §2).

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

（　）、すばらしい発明が生まれることも多い。(___), subarashii hatsumei ga umareru koto mo ooi.

Full sentence: 失敗をもとに、すばらしい発明が生まれることも多い。Shippai o moto ni, subarashii hatsumei ga umareru koto mo ooi.
EN: Wonderful inventions are often born from failure.
HI: असफलता के आधार पर, अक्सर शानदार आविष्कार जन्म लेते हैं।
GU: નિષ્ફળતાના આધારે, ઘણીવાર અદ્ભુત શોધ જન્મ લે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a失敗のきっかけにshippai no kikkake ni(wrong particle/pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b失敗をもとにshippai o moto nibased on failureअसफलता के आधार परનિષ્ફળતાના આધારે

Why: The failure serves as source material an invention is built from — point 1's をもとに; きっかけに would instead frame the failure as a trigger moment for a change (a subtly different nuance), and its correct particle would be を, not の.

Q2

その事件（　）、商品の検査体制が見直された。Sono jiken (___), shouhin no kensa taisei ga minaosareta.

Full sentence: その事件をきっかけに、商品の検査体制が見直された。Sono jiken o kikkake ni, shouhin no kensa taisei ga minaosareta.
EN: That incident prompted a review of the product inspection system.
HI: उस घटना को मौक़ा बनाकर, उत्पाद जांच प्रणाली की समीक्षा की गई।
GU: તે ઘટનાને તક બનાવીને, ઉત્પાદન તપાસ પ્રણાલીની સમીક્ષા કરવામાં આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aの契機にno keiki ni(wrong particle here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bをきっかけにo kikkake nitaking ~ as the triggerको मौक़ा बनाकरને તક બનાવીને

Why: 契機/きっかけ both need the particle を before them (Nを契機に／Nをきっかけに); の is simply the wrong particle here, regardless of register.

Q3

出産（　）、生き方を考え直した。Shussan (___), ikikata o kangaenaoshita.

EN: Taking childbirth as an opportunity, (I) reconsidered how to live.
HI: बच्चे के जन्म को मौक़ा बनाकर, जीने का तरीक़ा फिर से सोचा।
GU: બાળકના જન્મને તક બનાવીને, જીવવાની રીત ફરી વિચારી.

Why: Childbirth here is a life-changing trigger moment — point 3's 契機として; もとにして (point 1) would misread childbirth as raw material/basis for something constructed, not a turning-point event.

HI: यह फ़िल्म एक सच्ची घटना पर आधारित होकर बनाई गई।
GU: આ ફિલ્મ સાચી ઘટના પર આધારિત બનાવવામાં આવી.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ご注意ください。___ gochuui kudasai.

Tiles: 1 お忘れ物の (owasuremono no)　2 お帰りの (okaeri no)　3 ないよう (nai you)　4 際には (sai ni wa)

2 お帰りの→4 際には→1 お忘れ物の→3 ないよう

お帰りの際には、お忘れ物のないようご注意ください。Okaeri no sai ni wa, owasuremono no nai you gochuui kudasai.

EN: Please be careful not to leave any belongings behind when you go home.
HI: जाते समय, कृपया अपना सामान भूलने से बचें।
GU: જતી વખતે, કૃપા કરી તમારો સામાન ભૂલી ન જાવ તેનું ધ્યાન રાખો.

Why: Built on point 4 (Nの際には) — お帰りの際には ("on the occasion of leaving") sets the scene, then お忘れ物のないようご注意ください is the polite request.

Q7

工事中＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ となっております。Koujichuu ___ to natte orimasu.

Tiles: 1 この先 (kono saki)　2 片側通行 (katagawa tsuukou)　3 つき (tsuki)　4 に (ni)

4 に→3 つき→1 この先→2 片側通行

工事中につき、この先片側通行となっております。Koujichuu ni tsuki, kono saki katagawa tsuukou to natte orimasu.

EN: Due to ongoing construction, the road ahead is one-way from this point.
HI: निर्माण कार्य के कारण, आगे से एक-तरफ़ा यातायात है।
GU: બાંધકામના કારણે, આગળથી એક-તરફી ટ્રાફિક છે.

Why: Built on point 2 (Nにつき) — 工事中に + つき ("due to being under construction"), then この先片側通行となっております states the resulting traffic condition.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points today follow a noun with something that explains a reason, source, trigger, or occasion — easy to blur together, and exactly what Q1–Q4 above test. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜をもとに(して)~o moto ni Based on ~ / using ~ as material Nをもとに(して) Names source material/foundation something is built FROM. You're describing what a creation/decision is built on.
〜につき~ni tsuki Because of ~ (formal reason) Nにつき Formal reason marker for signs/notices; explains why something is currently unavailable/closed. You're writing an official notice explaining a temporary state.
〜をきっかけに（を契機に）~o kikkake ni Taking ~ as a trigger/opportunity Nを(V-たのを)きっかけに／契機に Names a specific event that triggered a subsequent change; 契機 is the formal variant. You want to say "that event is what prompted this change."
〜際に（際）~sai ni On the occasion of ~ / when ~ Nの際に; V-plain+際 Formal version of とき; names a general occasion/moment for a rule or instruction. You're giving formal instructions tied to a specific occasion.
Exam trap: をもとに vs をきっかけに both can follow a noun describing a past event, but もとに treats that noun as raw material a NEW thing is constructed from (a novel from facts, an invention from failure), while きっかけに/契機に treats it as a turning-point MOMENT that triggered a change (a life change after childbirth, a policy review after an incident) — one is "made from," the other "prompted by." Also remember: 契機/きっかけ always take を, never の (Q2), and もとに always needs を before it too, never に directly (Q4).
