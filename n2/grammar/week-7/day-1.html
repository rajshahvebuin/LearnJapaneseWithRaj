1日目 — 国籍を問わず (Kokuseki o towazu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Bunpou) — printed p.110–111 (PDF pages 110–111)
Grammar points covered
人目もかまわず雨にもかかわらず来る来ないにかかわらず年齢を問わず
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.113 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points mean roughly "regardless of / not caring about ~", and the book groups them together precisely because they're easy to mix up — see §3 below. None of today's four forms are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; all four are neutral register, usable in speech and writing alike.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜もかまわず

neutral register
Reading~mo kamawazu
Meaning (EN)"without caring about ~ / paying no mind to ~" — the subject knowingly disregards something about themselves or their situation
Meaning (HI)...की परवाह किए बिना
Meaning (GU)...ની પરવા કર્યા વગર
Connection (接続)Nも／Vるのも + かまわず 《も構わず》
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical useDescribes someone acting (often emotionally or physically) without worrying about how it looks or what it costs them — 人目 ("the eyes of others") is by far the most common noun paired with this pattern.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + もNも + かまわず人目もかまわずhitome mo kamawazu
Verb (potential/plain + のも)Vるのも + かまわず脱げるのもかまわずnugeru no mo kamawazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は人目もかまわず子どものように泣いた。Kanojo wa hitome mo kamawazu kodomo no you ni naita. She cried like a child without worrying about how it would appear. वह दूसरों की नज़र की परवाह किए बिना बच्चे की तरह रोई। તે બીજાની નજરની પરવા કર્યા વગર બાળકની જેમ રડી.
彼は靴が脱げるのもかまわず走り続けた。Kare wa kutsu ga nugeru no mo kamawazu hashiri tsuzuketa. He continued to run without caring about his shoes coming off. वह जूते उतरने की परवाह किए बिना दौड़ता रहा। તે પગરખાં નીકળી જવાની પરવા કર્યા વગર દોડતો રહ્યો.

A more colloquial cousin of 「Nも構わず」— 構う itself means "to mind/care about," so もかまわず literally means "not even minding ~." Because it's about the subject's own indifference to something visible about themselves, it reads very differently from 〜にもかかわらず (point 2) even though both get translated "despite/regardless of" in English — see the confusion table in §3.

【2】 〜にもかかわらず

neutral register
Reading~ni mo kakawarazu
Meaning (EN)"despite ~ / in spite of ~" — concedes one fact/circumstance as an obstacle that didn't stop the outcome
Meaning (HI)...के बावजूद
Meaning (GU)...છતાં
Connection (接続)V／Aい (plain) + にもかかわらず  |  な-adj／N (plain, no だ) + にもかかわらず
RegisterNeutral — slightly more formal-sounding than plain 〜のに, but used freely in speech
Typical useSets up a fact that would normally block/contradict the result, then states the result happened anyway. A common trap: な-adjectives and nouns must attach in their plain (no だ) form — 静かだにもかかわらず is marked wrong in the source; 静かにもかかわらず (or 静かであるにもかかわらず) is correct.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)V-plain + にもかかわらず見たにもかかわらずmita ni mo kakawarazu
い-adjective(added, not in book) い-adj + にもかかわらず高いにもかかわらずtakai ni mo kakawarazu
な-adjectiveな-adj (no だ) + にもかかわらず  ✗ な-adj+だ+にもかかわらず is a marked error in the source(added, not in book) 静かにもかかわらずshizuka ni mo kakawarazu
NounN (no だ) + にもかかわらず雨にもかかわらずame ni mo kakawarazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
雨にもかかわらず、大勢の人々が集まった。Ame ni mo kakawarazu, oozei no hitobito ga atsumatta. A lot of people gathered despite the rain. बारिश के बावजूद बहुत सारे लोग इकट्ठा हुए। વરસાદ છતાં ઘણા લોકો ભેગા થયા.
見たにもかかわらず、彼は見なかったと言った。Mita ni mo kakawarazu, kare wa minakatta to itta. Though he saw it, he said he didn't see it. देखने के बावजूद, उसने कहा कि उसने नहीं देखा। જોયું હોવા છતાં, તેણે કહ્યું કે તેણે જોયું નથી.

Reads as a slightly stiffer, more explicit cousin of 〜のに — good for essays and speeches where you want to flag a contradiction clearly. Note it takes plain forms throughout, including な-adjectives/nouns without だ, which is the trap the source explicitly warns about.

【3】 〜にかかわらず

neutral register
Reading~ni kakawarazu
Meaning (EN)"regardless of whether ~ or not / no matter which of the two" — pairs two opposite options and says the outcome is the same either way
Meaning (HI)चाहे ~ हो या न हो, कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता
Meaning (GU)ભલે ~ હોય કે ન હોય, કંઈ ફરક પડતો નથી
Connection (接続)Vる-Vないに／Aい-Aくないに／Nに + かかわらず (also かかわりなく)
RegisterNeutral (the かかわりなく variant leans slightly more written)
Typical useNeeds two alternatives on the table — an affirmative/negative verb pair, an い-adjective pair, or a pair of opposite nouns (好き・嫌い, 上手・下手) — then says the result doesn't depend on which one holds. This is the form's key difference from point 2's にもかかわらず, which concedes only one fact.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (aff/neg pair)Vる-Vないに + かかわらず来る来ないにかかわらずkuru konai ni kakawarazu
い-adjective (aff/neg pair)(added, not in book) Aい-Aくないに + かかわらず良い良くないにかかわらずyoi yokunai ni kakawarazu
Noun (single)Nに + かかわらず国籍にかかわらずkokuseki ni kakawarazu
Noun (opposite pair)N・Nに + かかわらず好き・嫌いにかかわらずsuki kirai ni kakawarazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
来る来ないにかかわらず、連絡をください。Kuru konai ni kakawarazu, renraku o kudasai. Please contact me regardless of whether you're coming or not. आइए या न आइए, कृपया संपर्क करें। આવો કે ન આવો, કૃપા કરી સંપર્ક કરો.
国籍にかかわらず、歓迎します。Kokuseki ni kakawarazu, kangei shimasu. We welcome anyone regardless of nationality. राष्ट्रीयता चाहे जो भी हो, हम स्वागत करते हैं। રાષ્ટ્રીયતા ગમે તે હોય, અમે સ્વાગત કરીએ છીએ.

The book's own example list adds ある・なしにかかわらず, 好き・嫌いにかかわらず, 上手・下手にかかわらず, 性別にかかわらず — all pairs of opposites. Don't confuse this with point 2 (〜にもかかわらず) just because they share かかわらず — the presence or absence of も is the tell (see §3's confusion table).

【4】 〜を問わず

neutral register
Reading~o towazu
Meaning (EN)"regardless of ~ / not asking about ~" — attaches to a category noun (age, gender, season, experience...) that doesn't affect the matter at hand
Meaning (HI)...चाहे जो भी हो / ...पूछे बिना
Meaning (GU)...ગમે તે હોય
Connection (接続)N + を問わず
RegisterNeutral — very common in formal announcements, job ads, event notices
Typical use問わず literally means "not asking" (from 問う, "to ask/question"), so 年齢を問わず literally reads "without asking age." Attaches only to nouns, never to verb/adjective pairs like point 3 can.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (single)N + を問わず国籍を問わずkokuseki o towazu
Noun (opposite pair)N・Nを + 問わず男女を問わずdanjo o towazu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
年齢を問わず多くの人々が集まった。Nenrei o towazu ooku no hitobito ga atsumatta. Many people of different ages gathered. उम्र चाहे जो भी हो, बहुत सारे लोग इकट्ठा हुए। ઉંમર ગમે તે હોય, ઘણા લોકો ભેગા થયા.
経験の有無を問わず、募集。Keiken no umu o towazu, boshuu. We are accepting applications, regardless of experience. अनुभव हो या न हो, आवेदन आमंत्रित हैं। અનુભવ હોય કે ન હોય, અરજી આમંત્રિત છે.
(bonus もっと！) 性別は問いません。Seibetsu wa toimasen. Regardless of gender. लिंग नहीं पूछा जाता। લિંગ પૂછવામાં આવતું નથી.

Book's own related-phrase box lists: 男女を問わず／性別を問わず, 〜の有無を問わず, 国の内外を問わず, 季節を問わず, 昼夜を問わず — worth memorizing as a set since they show up often in reading passages and formal notices. Close in meaning to point 3's 〜にかかわらず, but を問わず only ever attaches to nouns.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

男女（　）、能力のある人を採用します。Danjo (___), nouryoku no aru hito o saiyou shimasu.

Full sentence: 男女を問わず、能力のある人を採用します。Danjo o towazu, nouryoku no aru hito o saiyou shimasu.
EN: We hire capable people regardless of gender.
HI: लिंग चाहे जो भी हो, हम योग्य व्यक्ति को नियुक्त करते हैं।
GU: લિંગ ગમે તે હોય, અમે લાયક વ્યક્તિની ભરતી કરીએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aを問わずo towazuregardless of (category noun)चाहे जो भी होગમે તે હોય
bもかまわずmo kamawazu(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 男女 is a category noun paired with point 4's を問わず; もかまわず is reserved for idiomatic "not caring about ~" phrases like 人目もかまわず, not a general substitute for "regardless of."

Q2

お忙しいにも（　）、来てくださってありがとうございます。Oisogashii ni mo (___), kite kudasatte arigatou gozaimasu.

Full sentence: お忙しいにもかかわらず、来てくださってありがとうございます。Oisogashii ni mo kakawarazu, kite kudasatte arigatou gozaimasu.
EN: Thank you for coming despite being busy.
HI: व्यस्त होने के बावजूद आने के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: વ્યસ્ત હોવા છતાં આવવા બદલ આભાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかまわずkamawazu(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

雨にぬれるのも（　）歩き続けた。Ame ni nureru no mo (___) aruki tsuzuketa.

Full sentence: 雨にぬれるのもかまわず歩き続けた。Ame ni nureru no mo kamawazu aruki tsuzuketa.
EN: (He/she) kept walking without caring about getting wet in the rain.
HI: बारिश में भीगने की परवाह किए बिना, वह चलते रहे।
GU: વરસાદમાં ભીંજાવાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, તે ચાલતા રહ્યા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかまわずkamawazunot caring about ~परवाह किए बिनाપરવા કર્યા વગર
bかかわらずkakawarazu(wrong pattern here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The subject notices getting wet but chooses to ignore it — that's かまわず's nuance (point 1), not かかわらず, which needs にも and expresses "despite" an unrelated circumstance, not self-directed indifference.

Q4

Full sentence: 合否にかかわらず、結果はお知らせします。Gouhi ni kakawarazu, kekka wa oshirase shimasu.
EN: We will inform you of the result regardless of whether you pass or fail.
HI: पास हों या फ़ेल, हम आपको परिणाम की सूचना देंगे।
GU: પાસ થાઓ કે નાપાસ, અમે તમને પરિણામ જણાવીશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

本当のことを＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ もらえなかった。Hontou no koto o ___ moraenakatta.

Tiles: 1 信じて (shinjite)　2 かかわらず (kakawarazu)　3 にも (nimo)　4 言った (itta)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

4 言った→3 にも→2 かかわらず→1 信じて

本当のことを言ったにもかかわらず、信じてもらえなかった。Hontou no koto o itta ni mo kakawarazu, shinjite moraenakatta.

EN: Even though I told the truth, I wasn't believed.
HI: सच बताने के बावजूद, मुझ पर विश्वास नहीं किया गया।
GU: સાચું કહેવા છતાં, મારા પર વિશ્વાસ ન કરવામાં આવ્યો.

Why: Built on point 2 (〜にもかかわらず) — 言った (plain past) + にもかかわらず concedes "even though (I) said (it)," setting up the negative result 信じてもらえなかった.

Q7

まわりの人たちに＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 歌い続けた。Mawari no hitotachi ni ___ utai tsuzuketa.

Tiles: 1 のも (no mo)　2 聞こえる (kikoeru)　3 歌を (uta o)　4 かまわず (kamawazu)

2 聞こえる→1 のも→4 かまわず→3 歌を

まわりの人たちに聞こえるのもかまわず歌を歌い続けた。Mawari no hitotachi ni kikoeru no mo kamawazu uta o utai tsuzuketa.

EN: Not caring that the people around could hear, (she) kept singing songs.
HI: आस-पास के लोगों को सुनाई देने की परवाह किए बिना, वह गाना गाती रही।
GU: આસપાસના લોકોને સંભળાવાની પરવા કર્યા વગર, તે ગીતો ગાતી રહી.

Why: Built on point 1 (Vるのもかまわず) — 聞こえる ("to be audible") + のも + かまわず, then the object 歌を + 歌い続けた as the main action.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms translate to something like "regardless of / despite" in English, which is exactly why the book groups them into one day (see the divider page's four-bullet preview for this week) — and exactly why Q2–Q5 above are built to trip up かまわず vs にもかかわらず vs にかかわらず vs を問わず. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜もかまわず~mo kamawazu Not caring about ~ Nも／Vるのも + かまわず Subject actively disregards a visible fact about themselves. You want to say someone did something without worrying how it looks/feels.
〜にもかかわらず~ni mo kakawarazu Despite ~ (concession) V/Aい-plain・な/N(no だ) + にもかかわらず Concedes ONE fact/state as an obstacle, then states the result happened anyway. You want to say "even though X, Y happened anyway."
〜にかかわらず~ni kakawarazu Regardless of which of two things is true Vる-Vないに／Aい-Aくないに／Nに + かかわらず Pairs TWO alternatives (aff/neg or two opposite nouns) — the outcome is the same either way. You want to say "no matter which of these two is the case."
〜を問わず~o towazu Regardless of (a named attribute) N + を問わず Attaches only to a category noun (age, gender, season…); common in formal announcements. You're listing an attribute that doesn't affect eligibility/applicability.
Exam trap: 〜にもかかわらず (with も) vs 〜にかかわらず (without も) is the classic N2 trap — にもかかわらず concedes a single circumstance ("despite X"), while にかかわらず (no も) needs paired alternatives (do/don't, like/dislike, pass/fail) and means "it doesn't matter which." When the sentence gives you just one fact, keep the も; when it gives two opposite options (as in 合否, 好き・嫌い, 来る来ない), drop the も.
