1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜恐れがある

硬 formal/written
Reading~osore ga aru
Meaning (EN)"there is a risk/danger that ~" — formally reports the possibility of an undesirable event
Meaning (HI)...का ख़तरा/आशंका है
Meaning (GU)...નું જોખમ/ભય છે
Connection (接続)Vる + 恐れがある  |  Nの + 恐れがある
Register硬 — formal, the standard register for weather warnings, safety notices, and news reports
Typical useReports a risk of something bad happening. Almost never used for a positive outcome — contrast with point 2's ものがある, which can affirm any felt quality, good or bad.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + 恐れがある倒れたりする恐れがあるtaoretari suru osore ga aru
NounNの + 恐れがある上陸の恐れがあるjouriku no osore ga aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
地震の際には、窓ガラスが割れたり壁が倒れたりする恐れがある。Jishin no sai ni wa, mado garasu ga waretari kabe ga taoretari suru osore ga aru. In an event of an earthquake, there is a risk that the windows will break or that the walls will collapse. भूकंप के समय, खिड़की का शीशा टूटने या दीवार गिरने का ख़तरा है। ધરતીકંપ સમયે, બારીનો કાચ તૂટવાનો કે દીવાલ પડવાનો ભય છે.
台風19号は今夜、四国に上陸の恐れがあります。Taifuu juukyuu-gou wa konya, Shikoku ni jouriku no osore ga arimasu. Typhoon no. 19 is threatening Shikoku tonight. तूफ़ान नंबर 19 आज रात शिकोकू में उतरने की आशंका है। તોફાન નંબર 19 આજે રાત્રે શિકોકુમાં ત્રાટકવાની આશંકા છે.

Firmly 硬 (formal) — this is the register of news broadcasts and official warnings, not casual speech (the book's own casual paraphrase is 〜心配がある/〜かもしれない). Don't reach for it to describe a positive or neutral possibility.

【2】 〜ものがある

硬 formal/written
Reading~mono ga aru
Meaning (EN)"there is something ~ about it / it really is ~" — subjectively but firmly affirms that something has a felt quality
Meaning (HI)उसमें कुछ ...जैसा है / सच में ...महसूस होता है
Meaning (GU)તેમાં કંઈક ...જેવું છે
Connection (接続)Vる／Aい／naな + ものがある
Register硬 — formal/literary, but common in reviews, critiques, and reflective writing
Typical useSoftly but firmly affirms a subjective impression — "there's something touching/hard/wonderful about X." Can affirm a positive, negative, or neutral quality, unlike point 1's 恐れがある, which is only for risks.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる + ものがある心にひびくものがあるkokoro ni hibiku mono ga aru
い-adjectiveAい + ものがあるつらいものがあるtsurai mono ga aru
な-adjective (added, not in book)naな + ものがある見事なものがあるmigoto na mono ga aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
満員電車で毎日通勤するのはつらいものがある。Man'in densha de mainichi tsuukin suru no wa tsurai mono ga aru. It is hard commuting on a packed train everyday. रोज़ भरी हुई ट्रेन में ऑफिस जाना सच में कठिन लगता है। રોજ ભરેલી ટ્રેનમાં ઓફિસ જવું ખરેખર અઘરું લાગે છે.
彼の歌には心にひびくものがある。Kare no uta ni wa kokoro ni hibiku mono ga aru. His songs are very touching. उसके गानों में दिल को छू जाने वाली कोई बात है। તેના ગીતોમાં દિલને સ્પર્શે એવું કંઈક છે.

A softer, more literary way to affirm a quality than a flat adjective sentence (つらい vs つらいものがある) — implies the speaker has really felt/noticed this quality. Common in reviews and reflective writing; don't confuse with point 1's 恐れがある, which is strictly about a risk of something bad, never a felt quality.

【3】 〜ものでもない／〜ものではない

硬 formal/written
Reading~mono demo nai / ~mono de wa nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not necessarily true that ~" — softens or pushes back on an over-generalization
Meaning (HI)ऐसा भी ज़रूरी नहीं / हमेशा ऐसा नहीं होता
Meaning (GU)એવું જરૂરી પણ નથી
Connection (接続)Vば／Aければ／naなら／Nなら + Aいという／na(だ)という／N(だ)という + ものでもない／ものではない
Register硬 — formal/written
Typical useFollows a conditional clause stating a common assumption ("if X, then Y") and denies that the assumption always holds — "it's not necessarily the case that Y just because X."
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjective (conditional + claim)Aければ + Aいという + ものでもない多ければいいというものでもないookereba ii to iu mono demo nai
Noun/な-adjective (conditional + claim)Nがあれば + naだという + ものでもないお金があれば幸せだというものでもないokane ga areba shiawase da to iu mono demo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
何でも多ければいいというものでもない。Nandemo ookereba ii to iu mono demo nai. Not everything is good in excess. हर चीज़ ज़्यादा होना अच्छा है, यह ज़रूरी नहीं। દરેક વસ્તુ વધારે હોવી સારી છે, એ જરૂરી નથી.
お金があれば幸せだというものでもない。Okane ga areba shiawase da to iu mono demo nai. Just because you have money, it does not mean that you are happy. पैसा होने से ही ख़ुशी मिलती हो, यह ज़रूरी नहीं। પૈસા હોવાથી જ સુખ મળે, એ જરૂરી નથી.

Builds directly on Day 3's というものだ, but negates it — というものだ firmly asserts "that IS simply X," while というものでもない softens a general claim to "that's not always/necessarily X." A common structure for gently pushing back on a stereotype or oversimplified rule.

【4】 〜ないものか／〜ないものだろうか

硬 formal/written
Reading~nai mono ka / ~nai mono darou ka
Meaning (EN)"isn't there some way to ~? / I wish something could be done about ~" — a wish for a solution, phrased as a soft rhetorical question
Meaning (HI)क्या कोई तरीक़ा नहीं है...? / काश कुछ किया जा सके...
Meaning (GU)શું કોઈ રસ્તો નથી...? / કંઈક થઈ શકે તો સારું
Connection (接続)(どうにか／なんとか／もう少し) + Vないもの(だろう)か／Vれないもの(だろう)か
Register硬 in writing, though the sentiment itself feels conversational/personal
Typical useExpresses a wish that a frustrating situation could somehow be resolved. Often preceded by どうにか (somehow), なんとか (some way), or もう少し (a little more) for extra nuance, though none are required.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential negative)V-れない + ものか（なんとか）直せないものか(nanto ka) naosenai mono ka
Verb (negative)V-ない + ものだろうか直らないものだろうかnaoranai mono darou ka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
最近、変なメールがたくさん来る。どうにかならないものか。Saikin, hen na meeru ga takusan kuru. Dou ni ka naranai mono ka. I get a lot of weird e-mails these days. I wish I could do something about it. आजकल बहुत सारे अजीब ईमेल आते हैं। काश कुछ किया जा सके। આજકાલ ઘણા વિચિત્ર ઈમેલ આવે છે. કંઈક થઈ શકે તો સારું.
デジカメが壊れた。（なんとか）直らないものだろうか。Dejikame ga kowareta. (Nanto ka) naoranai mono darou ka. My digital camera is not working. I wish I knew how to fix the problem. डिजिटल कैमरा ख़राब हो गया। काश किसी तरह ठीक हो जाए। ડિજિટલ કેમેરો ખરાબ થઈ ગયો. કોઈક રીતે સુધરી જાય તો સારું.

Always about the speaker's own frustration and hope for a fix — never a literal question directed at someone else. どうにか／なんとか／もう少し add a "somehow/a bit more" flavor but aren't grammatically required.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Every point today is 硬 (formal), and three of the four share もの as their core — exactly the trap the reading-cloze passage above is built to test. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜恐れがある~osore ga aru There's a risk/danger that ~ Vる／Nの + 恐れがある Reports risk of a negative/undesirable event; classic news/weather register. You're warning about a possible bad outcome.
〜ものがある~mono ga aru There's something (felt quality) about it Vる／Aい／な + ものがある Subjectively affirms a felt quality (positive, negative, or neutral) — not about risk. You want "there really is something ~ about X" (an impression).
〜ものでもない~mono demo nai It's not necessarily true that ~ (conditional)ば/なら + というものでもない Softens/denies an assumed general rule; usually follows an "if X then Y" claim. You want to push back on an oversimplified generalization.
〜ないものか(だろうか)~nai mono ka Isn't there a way to ~? / I wish ~ could be fixed (どうにか/なんとか)+Vない(れない)ものか A rhetorical wish for a solution to a frustrating situation, not a real question. You're expressing frustrated hope that something could be resolved.
Exam trap: ものがある vs 恐れがある both look like a "there is ~" construction, but ものがある affirms a subjectively felt quality that can be positive (人を引きつけるものがある, "has an appeal"), while 恐れがある is reserved specifically for the risk of something bad happening (damage, injury, a storm) — never a positive quality. If the verb/adjective before it names something desirable or neutral, it's ものがある; if it names damage, danger, or a bad event, it's 恐れがある.
