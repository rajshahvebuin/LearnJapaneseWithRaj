2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — fill in the blank (p.138, 15 items, 4-option JLPT format)

Q1

未成年でも入会できます。（　）保護者の許可が必要です。Miseinen demo nyuukai dekimasu. (___) hogosha no kyoka ga hitsuyou desu.

EN: Even minors can join. However, parental permission is required. HI: नाबालिग भी सदस्य बन सकते हैं। परंतु अभिभावक की अनुमति ज़रूरी है। GU: સગીર પણ સભ્ય બની શકે છે. પરંતુ વાલીની મંજૂરી જરૂરી છે.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1するとsurutothen
2要するにyou suru niin short
3ただしtadashihowever (exception)
4したがってshitagattetherefore

Why: Parental permission is an exception/condition attached to the general rule "minors may join" — ただし's job (Day 4).

Q2

A「初めて歌舞伎を見たよ。」B「（　）どうだった?」A: "Hajimete kabuki o mita yo." B: "(___) dou datta?"

EN: A: "I watched kabuki for the first time." B: "So, how was it?" HI: A: "पहली बार काबुकी देखा।" B: "तो, कैसा लगा?" GU: A: "પહેલી વાર કાબુકી જોયું." B: "તો, કેવું લાગ્યું?"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1なおnaoadditionally
2そこでsokodeso (situation→solution)
3さてsatenow (topic change)
4それでsoredeso? (prompting more)

Why: B is prompting A to continue the story about the kabuki experience — それで's conversational use ② (Day 1).

Q3

運転免許証（　）健康保険証など、身分を証明するものをご持参ください。Unten menkyoshou (___) kenkou hokenshou nado, mibun o shoumei suru mono o gojisan kudasai.

EN: Please bring your driver's license or health insurance card, or another form of ID. HI: कृपया ड्राइविंग लाइसेंस या स्वास्थ्य बीमा कार्ड जैसा कोई पहचान पत्र लाएँ। GU: કૃપા કરીને ડ્રાઇવિંગ લાયસન્સ અથવા આરોગ્ય વીમા કાર્ડ જેવું ઓળખપત્ર લાવો.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1あるいはarui waor
2すなわちsunawachinamely
3ただしtadashihowever
4もっともmottomoalthough

Why: Two acceptable ID types are being offered as alternatives — formal あるいは (Day 3), not a rewording (すなわち).

Q4

びんのふたを開けるのにいつも苦労していた。（　）この商品を開発したわけです。Bin no futa o akeru no ni itsumo kurou shite ita. (___) kono shouhin o kaihatsu shita wake desu.

EN: We always struggled to open jar lids. So, that's why we developed this product. HI: जार का ढक्कन खोलने में हमेशा दिक़्क़त होती थी। तो इसीलिए हमने यह उत्पाद बनाया। GU: બરણીનું ઢાંકણ ખોલવામાં હંમેશા તકલીફ પડતી હતી. તો એટલે જ અમે આ ઉત્પાદન બનાવ્યું.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1するとsurutothen
2さてsatenow
3そこでsokodeso (situation→solution)
4なおnaoadditionally

Why: A stated problem leads to a concrete solution (developing the product) — textbook そこで (Day 2).

Q5

A「この子猫の写真、かわいいね。」B「ほんと。あれ、（　）最近お隣の猫、見かけないね。」A: "Kono koneko no shashin, kawaii ne." B: "Honto. Are, (___) saikin otonari no neko, mikakenai ne."

EN: A: "This kitten photo is cute." B: "True. Oh, come to think of it, I haven't seen the neighbor's cat lately." HI: A: "यह बिल्ली के बच्चे की फ़ोटो प्यारी है।" B: "सच में। अरे, वैसे याद आया, पड़ोसी की बिल्ली हाल में नहीं दिखी।" GU: A: "આ બિલાડીના બચ્ચાનો ફોટો ક્યૂટ છે." B: "સાચે. અરે, વૈસે યાદ આવ્યું, પડોશીની બિલાડી હમણાં દેખાઈ નથી."

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1というのはto iu no wathe reason is
2ということはto iu koto wathat means
3もっともmottomoalthough
4そういえばsouiebacome to think of it

Why: The kitten photo triggers an associative memory about the neighbor's cat — そういえば (Day 2).

Q6

人気者のプロゴルファーにはギャラリー（　）観客がぞろぞろついて回っている。Ninkimono no puro gorufaa ni wa gyararii (___) kankyaku ga zorozoro tsuite mawatte iru.

EN: Popular pro golfers are trailed by a "gallery" — in other words, spectators. HI: लोकप्रिय प्रो गोल्फ़र के पीछे "गैलरी", यानी कि दर्शक, कतार में चलते रहते हैं। GU: લોકપ્રિય પ્રો ગોલ્ફર પાછળ "ગેલેરી", એટલે કે દર્શકો, હારમાં ચાલતા રહે છે.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1すなわちsunawachinamely
2あるいはarui waor
3おまけにomake nion top of that
4ちなみにchinaminiby the way

Why: "Gallery" is immediately reworded as "spectators" — the exact same referent restated, すなわち's job (Day 3), not an addition (おまけに) or aside (ちなみに).

Q7

A「手紙、出してくれた?」B「（　）、忘れてしまって、まだ鞄の中に持ってるの。」A: "Tegami, dashite kureta?" B: "(___), wasurete shimatte, mada kaban no naka ni motteru no."

EN: A: "Did you mail the letter?" B: "Well, actually, I forgot, and it's still in my bag." HI: A: "चिट्ठी भेज दी क्या?" B: "असल में तो, भूल गई, अभी भी बैग में रखी है।" GU: A: "કાગળ મોકલી દીધો?" B: "ખરેખર તો, ભૂલી ગઈ, હજુ બેગમાં જ છે."

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1そういえばsouiebacome to think of it
2それがsore gawell, actually
3それはそうとsore wa sou toby the way
4そこでsokodeso

Why: B admits an awkward truth that contradicts A's assumption (that the letter was sent) — それが (Day 2).

Q8

A「どうしてレシートをもらわないの。」B「（　）いらないんだもの。」A: "Doushite reshiito o morawanai no." B: "(___) iranain damono."

EN: A: "Why don't you take the receipt?" B: "Because I don't need it!" HI: A: "रसीद क्यों नहीं लेती?" B: "क्योंकि ज़रूरत नहीं है!" GU: A: "રસીદ કેમ નથી લેતી?" B: "કારણ કે જરૂર નથી!"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1さてsatenow
2だがdagabut (formal)
3だってdattebecause (casual)
4なおnaoadditionally

Why: A casual, slightly defensive reason with the fixed 〜んだもの ending — だって (Day 3).

Q9

あ、左手の薬指に指輪が…。（　）結婚しているのかな。A, hidari te no kusuriyubi ni yubiwa ga…. (___) kekkon shite iru no ka na.

EN: Oh, there's a ring on her left ring finger... That must mean she's married. HI: अरे, बाएँ हाथ की अनामिका में अंगूठी है... तो इसका मतलब वह शादीशुदा है क्या। GU: અરે, ડાબા હાથની અનામિકામાં વીંટી છે... તો એનો અર્થ કે તે પરિણીત છે?

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1ということはto iu koto wathat means
2というのはto iu no wathe reason is
3したがってshitagattetherefore
4それはそうとsore wa sou toby the way

Why: Inferring a conclusion (married) from an observed fact (the ring) — ということは (Day 4).

Q10

私、ここに来られるのは今日が最後なんです。（　）転職するんです。Watashi, koko ni korareru no wa kyou ga saigo nan desu. (___) tenshoku surun desu.

EN: Today is the last day I'll be able to come here. That's because I'm changing jobs. HI: आज यहाँ आने का आख़िरी दिन है। वजह यह है कि मैं नौकरी बदल रहा हूँ। GU: આજે અહીં આવવાનો છેલ્લો દિવસ છે. કારણ એ છે કે હું નોકરી બદલી રહ્યો છું.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1もっともmottomoalthough
2というのはto iu no wathe reason is
3そこでsokodeso
4それでもsoredemoeven so

Why: Explains the reason behind the "last day" statement — というのは (Day 4).

Q11

A「日本の伝統文化について知りたいんだけど。」B「（　）このコースを受けるといいよ。」A: "Nihon no dentou bunka ni tsuite shiritain da kedo." B: "(___) kono koosu o ukeru to ii yo."

EN: A: "I want to learn about traditional Japanese culture." B: "In that case, you should take this course." HI: A: "जापानी परंपरागत संस्कृति के बारे में जानना चाहता हूँ।" B: "तो फिर, यह कोर्स ले लो।" GU: A: "જાપાનની પરંપરાગત સંસ્કૃતિ વિશે જાણવું છે." B: "તો પછી, આ કોર્સ લઈ લે."

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1それでsoredeso
2それならsore narain that case
3それでもsoredemoeven so
4それなのにsore na noniand yet

Why: B reacts to A's statement with a direct suggestion — それなら (Day 1).

Q12

A「昨日は大変でしたね。」B「ええ、ほんとうに疲れました。（　）、高橋選手、メダルとりましたね!」A: "Kinou wa taihen deshita ne." B: "Ee, hontou ni tsukaremashita. (___), Takahashi senshu, medaru torimashita ne!"

EN: A: "Yesterday was tough, wasn't it." B: "Yeah, I'm really tired. Anyway, Takahashi got a medal, didn't he!" HI: A: "कल तो बहुत मुश्किल था ना।" B: "हाँ, सच में थक गया। ख़ैर, ताकाहाशी ने पदक जीता ना!" GU: A: "કાલે તો ખૂબ મુશ્કેલ હતું ને." B: "હા, ખરેખર થાકી ગયો. ખેર, તાકાહાશીએ મેડલ જીત્યો ને!"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1要するにyou suru niin short
2ということはto iu koto wathat means
3それなのにsore na noniand yet
4それはそうとsore wa sou toby the way

Why: "Being tired" and "Takahashi's medal" are unrelated — a deliberate topic switch, それはそうと (Day 2).

Q13

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1さてsatenow
2なおnaoadditionally
3だがdagabut

Q16

地球温暖化が大きく騒がれている。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 言われている。Chikyuu ondanka ga ookiku sawagarete iru. ___ ★ ___ iwarete iru.

Tiles: 1 だが (daga)　2 そんなことはないとも (sonna koto wa nai to mo)　3 ある知識人に (aru chishikijin ni)　4 よれば (yoreba)

1 だが→3 ある知識人に→★4 よれば→2 そんなことはないとも

地球温暖化が大きく騒がれている。だが、ある知識人によれば、そんなことはないとも言われている。Chikyuu ondanka ga ookiku sawagarete iru. Daga, aru chishikijin ni yoreba, sonna koto wa nai to mo iwarete iru.

EN: Global warming is being talked about a great deal. But according to one expert, it's also said that this isn't actually the case.
HI: ग्लोबल वार्मिंग की बहुत चर्चा है। लेकिन एक जानकार के अनुसार, ऐसा नहीं भी है, ऐसा भी कहा जाता है।
GU: ગ્લોબલ વોર્મિંગની ખૂબ ચર્ચા છે. પણ એક નિષ્ણાતના મતે, એવું નથી પણ કહેવાય છે.

★ answer: 4 (よれば). だが opens the formal contrast; ある知識人によれば ("according to one expert") is the fixed evidential phrase, with よれば as its final piece before the quoted claim.

Q17

自治会の清掃を行いますので、ご参加ください。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 意志によるものとします。Jichikai no seisou o okonaimasu node, gosanka kudasai. ___ ★ ___ ishi ni yoru mono to shimasu.

Tiles: 1 義務ではなく (gimu de wa naku)　2 これは (kore wa)　3 もっとも (mottomo)　4 自主的な (jishuteki na)

3 もっとも→2 これは→★1 義務ではなく→4 自主的な

自治会の清掃を行いますので、ご参加ください。もっとも、これは義務ではなく自主的な意志によるものとします。Jichikai no seisou o okonaimasu node, gosanka kudasai. Mottomo, kore wa gimu de wa naku jishuteki na ishi ni yoru mono to shimasu.

EN: We will be holding a neighborhood association cleanup, so please participate. That said, this is not obligatory — it is meant to be based on voluntary will.
HI: निवासी संघ की सफ़ाई होगी, कृपया शामिल हों। हालांकि, यह अनिवार्य नहीं है — यह स्वैच्छिक इच्छा पर आधारित है।
GU: રહેવાસી સંઘની સફાઈ થશે, કૃપા કરીને ભાગ લો. જોકે, આ ફરજિયાત નથી — તે સ્વૈચ્છિક ઇચ્છા પર આધારિત છે.

★ answer: 1 (義務ではなく). もっとも softens the "please participate" call to action by clarifying it's not compulsory — the exact self-correcting-exception job from Day 5.

Q18

何度もこれは試験によく出るから間違えないようにと注意した。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 残念だった。Nando mo kore wa shiken ni yoku deru kara machigaenai you ni to chuui shita. ___ ★ ___ zannen datta.

Tiles: 1 学生が (gakusei ga)　2 それなのに (sore na noni)　3 間違えた (machigaeta)　4 たくさんいたのは (takusan ita no wa)

2 それなのに→3 間違えた→★1 学生が→4 たくさんいたのは

何度もこれは試験によく出るから間違えないようにと注意した。それなのに間違えた学生がたくさんいたのは残念だった。Nando mo kore wa shiken ni yoku deru kara machigaenai you ni to chuui shita. Sore na noni machigaeta gakusei ga takusan ita no wa zannen datta.

EN: I warned the students repeatedly that this comes up often on tests, so not to get it wrong. And yet, it was disappointing that so many students got it wrong anyway.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The full 25-item test is itself the week's biggest confusion-pairs drill. Grouping all 24 connectives by function makes the traps easier to spot at exam speed:

Function groupMembersKey discriminator
Reason → result それで, したがって, というのは (backward), そこで それで = plain/conversational; したがって = formal; というのは explains a reason for what was just said; そこで must lead to a concrete action.
Inference / conclusion ということは, すると (use②), つまり, 要するに ということは/すると② react to someone's statement with a deduction; つまり/要するに summarize a longer point already made.
Contrast / concession それなのに, それでも, だが, もっとも, ただし それなのに = surprise at mismatch; それでも = willful perseverance; だが = formal plain "but"; もっとも = softens speaker's own claim; ただし = formal rule-exception.
Addition / stacking しかも, おまけに, ちなみに, なお しかも/おまけに pile on a same-direction fact (おまけに = casual); ちなみに/なお add a lighter, looser aside (なお = more formal/announcement-flavored).
Topic management そういえば, それはそうと, さて そういえば needs a real associative trigger; それはそうと/さて are deliberate switches (さて = clean segment break; それはそうと = mid-conversation pivot).
Rewording / alternatives すなわち, あるいは, それが すなわち = same fact reworded (never a conclusion); あるいは = genuine either/or; それが = contradicts an assumption/plan.
Register-locked だって (casual only), だが／あるいは／すなわち／したがって／ただし (formal only) These never swap with their neutral cousins (しかし, または, つまり, だから, でも) without a register shift.
Exam trap: Q22 above is the passage's sharpest trap — three of the four options are plausible-sounding continuations, but only 無料で助かった keeps the positive tone that しかも requires (it stacks a second compliment, not a contradiction). More broadly, when several connectives seem to "sort of" fit a blank, check register first (formal notice vs. casual dialogue), then check direction (does B explain A, conclude from A, contrast A, or just add to A) — that two-step filter resolves nearly every item in this test.
