2日目 — そういえば… (Souieba…)

Grammar points covered

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.131 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Like Day 1, all four items are 接続詞 joining two full sentences, not inflectional patterns. そういえば and それはそうと are a close pair (both fire on a "related thought just occurred to me"), while それが and そこで do very different jobs — see §3 below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 それが

neutral register
Readingsore ga
Meaning (EN)Two uses: ① "well, actually…" (introduces an unexpected/awkward answer that goes against what was just asked); ② "but then…" (a plan/expectation turned out differently)
Meaning (HI)① असल में तो… (सवाल के उलट जवाब); ② पर हुआ यह कि… (योजना से अलग नतीजा)
Meaning (GU)① ખરેખર તો… (પ્રશ્નથી ઊલટો જવાબ); ② પણ થયું એવું કે… (યોજનાથી અલગ પરિણામ)
Connection (接続)① 「A?」「それが、B（Aに反する内容）。」  |  ② [plan/expectation A]。それが、[different result B]。
RegisterNeutral, mostly spoken
Typical useUsed specifically when the truth is awkward, disappointing, or embarrassing to admit — softens the blow of contradicting an assumption.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — contrary answer「A?」「それが、B。」「もう卒業されましたでしょう?」「それが、まだなんですよ。」"Mou sotsugyou saremashita deshou?" "Sore ga, mada nan desu yo."
Connective — plan vs. realityA。それが、B。やめると誓った。それが三日でまた吸った。Yameru to chikatta. Sore ga mikka de mata sutta.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「お嬢さん、もう大学をご卒業されましたでしょう?」「それが、まだなんですよ。」"Ojousan, mou daigaku o go-sotsugyou saremashita deshou?" "Sore ga, mada nan desu yo." "Your daughter must have graduated from university by now, right?" "Well, actually, not yet." "आपकी बिटिया अब तक विश्वविद्यालय से स्नातक हो गई होंगी, है ना?" "असल में तो, अभी नहीं हुई।" "તમારી દીકરી હવે યુનિવર્સિટીમાંથી સ્નાતક થઈ ગઈ હશે, ખરું ને?" "ખરેખર તો, હજુ નથી થઈ."
たばこをやめると誓った。それが、たった三日でまた吸ってしまった。Tabako o yameru to chikatta. Sore ga, tatta mikka de mata sutte shimatta. I vowed to quit smoking. But then, after only three days, I ended up smoking again. मैंने सिगरेट छोड़ने की कसम खाई थी। पर हुआ यह कि सिर्फ़ तीन दिन में फिर से पी ली। મેં સિગારેટ છોડવાની પ્રતિજ્ઞા લીધી હતી. પણ થયું એવું કે ફક્ત ત્રણ દિવસમાં ફરી પી લીધી.

Book gloss for ①: (=そう思うでしょうが, "you'd think so, but…"); for ②: (=ところが／それなのに). それが always signals the speaker is about to admit something didn't go as planned or assumed.

【2】 そこで

neutral register
Readingsokode
Meaning (EN)"so / with that in mind" — explains a situation (A) and then introduces the solution/proposal/request (B) taken in response
Meaning (HI)तो / इसी को देखते हुए — स्थिति बताकर उसके समाधान/सुझाव/निवेदन की ओर ले जाने के लिए
Meaning (GU)તો / એ ધ્યાનમાં રાખીને — પરિસ્થિતિ જણાવીને તેના ઉકેલ/સૂચન/વિનંતી તરફ લઈ જવા માટે
Connection (接続)[situation explained, A]。そこで、[solution/proposal/request B]。
RegisterNeutral; the book notes it's "used to develop the conversation" (話を展開するときに使う)
Typical useDo not confuse with それで — そこで must be followed by a new action/proposal, while それで only reports a result or prompts more talk.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — situation→solution[situation A]。そこで[solution B]。車が混むらしい。そこで早く出発する。Kuruma ga komu rashii. Sokode hayaku shuppatsu suru.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
明日は車が混むらしい。そこで我々は朝早く出発するつもりだ。Ashita wa kuruma ga komu rashii. Sokode wareware wa asa hayaku shuppatsu suru tsumori da. The traffic will probably be bad tomorrow. So, we plan to leave early in the morning. लगता है कल ट्रैफिक बहुत होगा। तो हम सुबह जल्दी निकलने वाले हैं। લાગે છે કે કાલે ટ્રાફિક ખૂબ હશે. તો અમે સવારે વહેલા નીકળવાના છીએ.
「今度アメリカに旅行します。そこでお願いがあるのですが……。」"Kondo Amerika ni ryokou shimasu. Sokode onegai ga aru no desu ga……." "I'm going on a trip to America. With that in mind, I have a favor to ask…" "इस बार अमेरिका जा रहा हूँ। तो इसी सिलसिले में एक निवेदन है……।" "આ વખતે અમેરિકા જાઉં છું. તો એ સંબંધમાં એક વિનંતી છે……."

Book gloss: (=そういうわけで). そこで is the "problem → proposed action" connective; unlike それなら it doesn't need to be a direct reply to another speaker, and unlike それで it must be followed by a concrete next step.

【3】 そういえば

neutral / conversational
Readingsou ieba
Meaning (EN)"come to think of it / speaking of which" — a related thought suddenly occurs to the speaker, triggered by the current topic
Meaning (HI)वैसे याद आया / इस पर से याद आया — बातचीत के दौरान अचानक कोई जुड़ा हुआ ख़याल आना
Meaning (GU)વૈસે યાદ આવ્યું / આ પરથી યાદ આવ્યું — વાતચીત દરમિયાન અચાનક સંબંધિત વિચાર આવવો
Connection (接続)A。そういえば、B（Aから連想した関連の話題）。
RegisterNeutral, common in speech
Typical useThe new topic (B) must be genuinely reminded of by A — it's an associative recollection, not a random subject change (compare それはそうと below).
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — associative recallA。そういえば、B。田中君、そういえば元気かな。Tanaka-kun, souieba genki ka na.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「そういえば、田中君、元気かな?」"Souieba, Tanaka-kun, genki ka na?" "Come to think of it, I wonder how Tanaka is doing?" "वैसे याद आया, तनाका कैसा है, पता नहीं?" "વૈસે યાદ આવ્યું, તનાકા કેમ છે, ખબર નહીં?"
「いい家ですね。そういえば、お父様は設計士さんでしたよね。」"Ii ie desu ne. Souieba, otousama wa sekkeishi-san deshita yo ne." "It's a very nice house. Come to think of it, wasn't your father an architect?" "बहुत अच्छा घर है। वैसे याद आया, आपके पिताजी आर्किटेक्ट थे ना?" "બહુ સરસ ઘર છે. વૈસે યાદ આવ્યું, તમારા પિતાજી આર્કિટેક્ટ હતા ને?"

Book gloss: (=思い出したけれど／今思い出しましたが). Trigger: something in the current conversation (the nice house) reminds the speaker of a related fact (the father's profession).

【4】 それはそうと

neutral / conversational
Readingsore wa sou to
Meaning (EN)"by the way / anyway" — deliberately switches to a topic that is unrelated to what was just being discussed
Meaning (HI)ख़ैर, वो छोड़ो / बात बदलते हुए — पूरी तरह से अलग विषय पर जाने के लिए
Meaning (GU)ખેર, એ છોડો / વાત બદલીને — સંપૂર્ણપણે અલગ વિષય પર જવા માટે
Connection (接続)A。それはそうと、B（Aと関係ない話）。
RegisterNeutral, common in speech
Typical useThe book explicitly labels this "used when changing the topic" (話題を換えるときに使う) — unlike そういえば, B does not need to be triggered/reminded by A.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — deliberate topic changeA。それはそうと、B。今度の試験いつだっけ。Kondo no shiken itsu dakke.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「今日の授業、おもしろかったね。」「うん、すごくためになった。それはそうと、今度の試験いつだっけ?」"Kyou no jugyou, omoshirokatta ne." "Un, sugoku tame ni natta. Sore wa sou to, kondo no shiken itsu dakke?" "Today's class was interesting, wasn't it?" "Yeah, I learned a lot. Anyway, when's the next exam again?" "आज की क्लास मज़ेदार थी ना।" "हाँ, बहुत कुछ सीखने को मिला। ख़ैर, अगली परीक्षा कब है भला?" "આજનો ક્લાસ મજાનો હતો ને." "હા, ઘણું શીખવા મળ્યું. ખેર, આગલી પરીક્ષા ક્યારે છે?"
「春になりましたね。それはそうと、田中さんの息子さん、大学受かったでしょうか。」"Haru ni narimashita ne. Sore wa sou to, Tanaka-san no musuko-san, daigaku ukatta deshou ka." "It feels like spring, doesn't it? By the way, did Mr. Tanaka's son get into university?" "बसंत आ गया है ना। ख़ैर, तनाका जी के बेटे को यूनिवर्सिटी में दाखिला मिला क्या?" "વસંત આવી ગઈ ને. ખેર, તનાકાસાહેબના દીકરાને યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ મળ્યો?"

Book gloss: (=話は変わりますが／ところで). Compare with そういえば: "spring" and "exam" (or "spring" and "son's admission") have no logical link — the speaker is simply steering the conversation elsewhere, not recalling something spring reminded them of.

Q1

この周辺は交通渋滞がひどい。（　）新しい道路が作られることになった。Kono shuuhen wa koutsuu juutai ga hidoi. (___) atarashii douro ga tsukurareru koto ni natta.

Full sentence: この周辺は交通渋滞がひどい。そこで新しい道路が作られることになった。Kono shuuhen wa koutsuu juutai ga hidoi. Sokode atarashii douro ga tsukurareru koto ni natta.
EN: Traffic congestion is terrible around here. So, it's been decided that a new road will be built.
HI: इस इलाके में ट्रैफिक जाम बहुत ज़्यादा है। तो एक नई सड़क बनाने का फ़ैसला हुआ है।
GU: આ વિસ્તારમાં ટ્રાફિક જામ ખૂબ છે. તો નવો રસ્તો બનાવવાનું નક્કી થયું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそこでsokodeso (situation → solution)तोતો
bそれがsore ga(wrong — no awkward/contrary answer here)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)

Why: Bad traffic (situation) leads to a concrete solution (a new road) — the textbook そこで pattern.

「来週から旅行でしたよね。」「（　）家の事情で中止になったんです。」"Raishuu kara ryokou deshita yo ne." "(___) ie no jijou de chuushi ni nattan desu."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

一人暮らしのお年寄りの問題と、留学生の受け入れ先の問題があった。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ という案が出された。Hitorigurashi no otoshiyori no mondai to, ryuugakusei no ukeiresaki no mondai ga atta. ___ to iu an ga dasareta.

Tiles: 1 共同生活 (kyoudou seikatsu)　2 お年寄りと (otoshiyori to)　3 そこで (sokode)　4 留学生との (ryuugakusei to no)

3 そこで→2 お年寄りと→4 留学生との→1 共同生活

一人暮らしのお年寄りの問題と、留学生の受け入れ先の問題があった。そこでお年寄りと留学生との共同生活という案が出された。Hitorigurashi no otoshiyori no mondai to, ryuugakusei no ukeiresaki no mondai ga atta. Sokode otoshiyori to ryuugakusei to no kyoudou seikatsu to iu an ga dasareta.

EN: There was the problem of elderly people living alone, and the problem of finding housing for international students. So, a proposal was made for elderly people and international students to live together.
HI: अकेले रहने वाले बुज़ुर्गों की समस्या थी, और विदेशी छात्रों को रहने की जगह देने की समस्या भी थी। तो बुज़ुर्गों और विदेशी छात्रों के साथ रहने का प्रस्ताव रखा गया।
GU: એકલા રહેતા વૃદ્ધોની સમસ્યા હતી, અને વિદેશી વિદ્યાર્થીઓને રહેઠાણ આપવાની સમસ્યા પણ હતી. તો વૃદ્ધો અને વિદેશી વિદ્યાર્થીઓ સાથે રહે એવો પ્રસ્તાવ મુકાયો.

Why: Two stated problems (situation) lead directly to a proposed solution (co-habitation) — classic そこで.

Q7

田中氏はこの地区の空港の必要性を強く訴えて＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿。Tanaka-shi wa kono chiku no kuukou no hitsuyousei o tsuyoku uttaete ___.

Tiles: 1 当選したら (tousen shitara)　2 それが (sore ga)　3 全く反対のことを言い出した (mattaku hantai no koto o iidashita)　4 当選した (tousen shita)

4 当選した→2 それが→1 当選したら→3 全く反対のことを言い出した

田中氏はこの地区の空港の必要性を強く訴えて当選した。それが、当選したら全く反対のことを言い出した。Tanaka-shi wa kono chiku no kuukou no hitsuyousei o tsuyoku uttaete tousen shita. Sore ga, tousen shitara mattaku hantai no koto o iidashita.

EN: Mr. Tanaka strongly campaigned on the need for an airport in this district and got elected. But then, once elected, he started saying the complete opposite.
HI: तनाका साहब ने इस इलाके में एयरपोर्ट की ज़रूरत पर ज़ोर देकर चुनाव जीता। पर हुआ यह कि चुने जाते ही वे बिल्कुल उल्टी बात कहने लगे।
GU: તનાકાસાહેબે આ વિસ્તારમાં એરપોર્ટની જરૂરિયાત પર ભાર મૂકીને ચૂંટણી જીતી. પણ થયું એવું કે ચૂંટાયા પછી તે તદ્દન ઊલટી વાત કરવા લાગ્યા.

Why: それが marks the unexpected/disappointing turn — winning on a promise, then reversing it — exactly its "plan vs. reality" use.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today pairs two "topic-shift" connectives (そういえば／それはそうと) against two "situation-driven" connectives (それが／そこで). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
それがsore ga Well, actually… / but then… 「A?」「それがB。」  |  A。それがB。 B directly contradicts what A (a question or plan) led you to expect. Often softens bad/awkward news. Admitting reality didn't match an assumption or plan.
そこでsokode So / with that in mind A（状況）。そこでB（解決策）。 Must be followed by a concrete next action/proposal — not just any result. You explained a situation and now state what you did/will do about it.
そういえばsouieba Come to think of it A。そういえばB（連想）。 B must be genuinely triggered/reminded by A — an associative link is required. Something in the conversation just reminded you of a related fact.
それはそうとsore wa sou to By the way / anyway A。それはそうと、B（無関係）。 B is deliberately unrelated to A — a conscious topic change, not a triggered memory. You want to move the conversation to something else entirely.
Exam trap: そういえば vs それはそうと is the pair N2 loves to test here (see Q3/Q4 above) — both look like "oh, by the way" phrases. Check whether the new topic is logically connected to what was just said (chocolate → Valentine's Day = そういえば) or genuinely unrelated (weather → documents = それはそうと). Also watch それが vs そこで: それが reports a surprising outcome with no action attached, while そこで must be followed by a deliberate response/solution.
