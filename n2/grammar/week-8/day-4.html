4日目 — ということは… (To iu koto wa…)

Answer key

Notes
したがって and ただし are both tagged 硬 formal/written in the source. The page also includes a bonus もっと！ callout introducing ただ (tada) — a near-homophone of ただし with a different job (adding a problem/caveat rather than a strict exception) — included below as a confusion-pair bonus since the book places it directly beside ただし for that reason.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 ということは

neutral register
Readingto iu koto wa
Meaning (EN)"that means / so that means" — draws a logical inference/conclusion from a stated fact (A)
Meaning (HI)इसका मतलब है / तो इसका मतलब — किसी बात से कोई निष्कर्ष निकालने के लिए
Meaning (GU)એટલે / તો એનો અર્થ — કોઈ વાત પરથી નિષ્કર્ષ કાઢવા માટે
Connection (接続)A。ということは、B（Aから判断した内容）。
RegisterNeutral
Typical useBook gloss: (=つまり). The speaker reasons forward from A to a conclusion B — very close to ~と考えられる in function.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — inferenceA。ということはB。まだ来ない。ということは欠席だ。Mada konai. To iu koto wa kesseki da.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「彼は、まだ来ませんね。ということは、欠席ということですね。」"Kare wa, mada kimasen ne. To iu koto wa, kesseki to iu koto desu ne." "He hasn't come yet, has he. That means he's absent, right." "वह अभी तक नहीं आया ना। तो इसका मतलब वह अनुपस्थित है।" "તે હજુ સુધી નથી આવ્યો ને. તો એનો અર્થ કે તે ગેરહાજર છે."
「私はもうお酒を飲めます。」「ということは二十歳を過ぎてるんだね。」"Watashi wa mou osake o nomemasu." "To iu koto wa hatachi o sugiterun da ne." "I can already drink alcohol." "That means you're over twenty, huh." "मैं अब शराब पी सकता हूँ।" "तो इसका मतलब तुम बीस साल से ज़्यादा के हो चुके हो।" "હું હવે દારૂ પી શકું છું." "તો એનો અર્થ કે તું વીસ વર્ષથી મોટો થઈ ગયો છે."

Use ということは when B is a conclusion you're inferring, not a reason you already know — compare with というのは below, which goes the opposite direction (from result back to reason).

【2】 というのは

neutral register
Readingto iu no wa
Meaning (EN)"the reason is… / that's because…" — follows a statement with its explanation/reason
Meaning (HI)वजह यह है कि… / क्योंकि… — पहले कही बात की वजह बताने के लिए
Meaning (GU)કારણ એ છે કે… / કેમ કે… — અગાઉ કહેલી વાતનું કારણ આપવા માટે
Connection (接続)A。というのは、B（理由）。
RegisterNeutral
Typical useBook gloss: (=なぜかというと／どうしてかというと). Runs in the opposite direction from ということは: A is stated first, then というのは introduces why.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — reason followsA。というのはB。外出できない。というのは父の具合が悪い。Gaishutsu dekinai. To iu no wa chichi no guai ga warui.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「今日は家を出られないんです。というのは父の具合が悪くなりまして……。」"Kyou wa ie o derarenain desu. To iu no wa chichi no guai ga waruku narimashite……" "I can't leave the house today. That's because my father has taken a turn for the worse…" "आज मैं घर से नहीं निकल सकता। वजह यह है कि पापा की तबीयत बिगड़ गई है……।" "આજે હું ઘરેથી નીકળી શકતો નથી. કારણ એ છે કે પપ્પાની તબિયત બગડી ગઈ છે……"
「ぼくは卵を食べないんです。というのは、アレルギーがあるんですよ。」"Boku wa tamago o tabenain desu. To iu no wa, arerugii ga arun desu yo." "I don't eat eggs. That's because I have an allergy." "मैं अंडा नहीं खाता। वजह यह है कि मुझे एलर्जी है।" "હું ઇંડું ખાતો નથી. કારણ એ છે કે મને એલર્જી છે."

Memory hook: ということは points forward to a conclusion; というのは points backward to a reason for what was just said.

【3】 したがって

硬 formal/written
Readingshitagatte
Meaning (EN)"therefore / accordingly" — states a logical/formal result following directly from a stated reason
Meaning (HI)इसलिए / अतः — किसी वजह से सीधा निकलने वाला औपचारिक परिणाम बताने के लिए
Meaning (GU)તેથી / એટલે — કોઈ કારણથી સીધું નીકળતું ઔપચારિક પરિણામ જણાવવા માટે
Connection (接続)A（理由）。したがってB（結果）。
Register硬 — formal, written (reports, academic writing, official statements)
Typical useBook gloss: (=だから／その結果). A far more formal cousin of だから — expect it in reports, regulations, and academic writing rather than daily conversation.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — formal reason→resultA。したがってB。まじめだ。したがって信頼されている。Majime da. Shitagatte shinrai sarete iru.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼はまじめで誠実な人だ。したがってみんなから信頼されている。Kare wa majime de seijitsu na hito da. Shitagatte minna kara shinrai sarete iru. He is a diligent and sincere person. Therefore, he is trusted by everyone. वह मेहनती और ईमानदार इंसान है। इसलिए सब उस पर भरोसा करते हैं। તે મહેનતુ અને પ્રામાણિક વ્યક્તિ છે. તેથી બધા તેના પર વિશ્વાસ કરે છે.
教授は急用で来られません。したがって講義は中止です。Kyouju wa kyuuyou de koraremasen. Shitagatte kougi wa chuushi desu. The professor cannot come due to urgent business. Therefore, the lecture is cancelled. प्रोफ़ेसर एक ज़रूरी काम की वजह से नहीं आ सकते। इसलिए लेक्चर रद्द है। પ્રોફેસર તાત્કાલિક કામને લીધે આવી શકતા નથી. તેથી લેક્ચર રદ છે.

This week's own 実戦問題 test reuses this exact pattern (問題1 Q15: 単位が足りません。したがって君は卒業できない), confirming したがって as a high-frequency formal connective on the actual exam.

【4】 ただし

硬 formal/written
Readingtadashi
Meaning (EN)"however / provided that / except that" — attaches a condition or exception to the sentence just stated
Meaning (HI)परंतु / बशर्ते / सिवाय इसके कि — पिछली बात में कोई शर्त या अपवाद जोड़ने के लिए
Meaning (GU)પરંતુ / શરતે કે / સિવાય કે — અગાઉની વાતમાં કોઈ શરત કે અપવાદ ઉમેરવા માટે
Connection (接続)A。ただしB（条件・例外）。
Register硬 — formal, written (notices, rules, contracts)
Typical useThe standard connective for fine print — discounts, schedules, and rules that need a stated exception.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — condition/exceptionA。ただしB。3割引。ただしこの棚を除く。San-wari biki. Tadashi kono tana o nozoku.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
全商品3割引です。ただし、この棚の商品は除きます。Zen-shouhin san-wari biki desu. Tadashi, kono tana no shouhin wa nozokimasu. All items are 30% off. However, items on this shelf are excluded. सभी सामान पर 30% छूट है। परंतु इस शेल्फ़ के सामान को छोड़कर। તમામ વસ્તુઓ પર 30% ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ છે. પરંતુ આ છાજલી પરની વસ્તુઓ સિવાય.
明日は9時に集合です。ただし、雨の場合は中止です。Ashita wa kuji ni shuugou desu. Tadashi, ame no baai wa chuushi desu. Tomorrow we meet at 9 o'clock. However, in case of rain, it's cancelled. कल सुबह 9 बजे इकट्ठा होना है। परंतु बारिश होने पर रद्द रहेगा। કાલે સવારે 9 વાગ્યે ભેગા થવાનું છે. પરંતુ વરસાદ પડે તો રદ છે.

Bonus (もっと！box, not one of today's 4 headline points but placed right beside ただし for contrast): ただ (tada) looks similar but adds a problem or open question rather than a strict exception — 品はいい。ただ値段が高すぎる。("The quality is good. But/only, the price is too high" — a complaint, not a formal carve-out) and ぼくはかまわない。ただ妻が何と言うか。("I don't mind. But I wonder what my wife would say" — an open question, not a rule). See the confusion table in §3 for the full contrast with ただし.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.133. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.135.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

部屋は気に入りました。（　）駅から遠すぎます。Heya wa ki ni irimashita. (___) eki kara toosugimasu.

Full sentence: 部屋は気に入りました。ただ駅から遠すぎます。Heya wa ki ni irimashita. Tada eki kara toosugimasu.
EN: I liked the room. But it's just too far from the station.
HI: कमरा मुझे पसंद आया। पर स्टेशन से बहुत दूर है।
GU: રૂમ મને ગમ્યો. પણ સ્ટેશનથી ઘણો દૂર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aただtadabut / only (raising a problem)परપણ
bだってdatte(wrong — だって gives a reason for a prior claim, this is a fresh complaint)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)

Why: "Too far from the station" is a drawback being raised about the room, which is ただ's job (adding a problem), not だって (giving a reason in reply to a question).

Q2

私は肉は食べません。（　）菜食主義だからです。Watashi wa niku wa tabemasen. (___) saishoku shugi da kara desu.

Full sentence: 私は肉は食べません。というのは菜食主義だからです。Watashi wa niku wa tabemasen. To iu no wa saishoku shugi da kara desu.
EN: I don't eat meat. That's because I'm a vegetarian.
HI: मैं मांस नहीं खाता। वजह यह है कि मैं शाकाहारी हूँ।
GU: હું માંસ ખાતો નથી. કારણ એ છે કે હું શાકાહારી છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aというのはto iu no wathe reason isवजह यह है किકારણ એ છે કે
bということはto iu koto wa(wrong — ということは infers a conclusion, this sentence already states the reason directly)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)

Why: The sentence gives the reason for not eating meat (だから), which is what というのは introduces — ということは would instead be inferring a new conclusion from a fact, which isn't happening here.

Q3

出席が3分の2以上ない人は最終試験を受けられません。（　）あなたは受験できないということになります。Shusseki ga sanbun no ni ijou nai hito wa saishuu shiken o ukeraremasen. (___) anata wa juken dekinai to iu koto ni narimasu.

Full sentence: …したがってあなたは受験できないということになります。"…" "Shitagatte anata wa juken dekinai to iu koto ni narimasu."
EN: People with less than two-thirds attendance cannot take the final exam. Therefore, that means you cannot take the exam.
HI: जिनकी उपस्थिति दो-तिहाई से कम है वे अंतिम परीक्षा नहीं दे सकते। इसलिए इसका मतलब है कि तुम परीक्षा नहीं दे सकते।
GU: જેમની હાજરી બે-તૃતીયાંશ કરતાં ઓછી છે તેઓ અંતિમ પરીક્ષા આપી શકતા નથી. તેથી એનો અર્થ એ કે તું પરીક્ષા આપી શકતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aただしtadashi(wrong — this states a consequence, not a condition/exception to the rule)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)
bしたがってshitagattetherefore (formal result)इसलिएતેથી

Why: The rule (attendance requirement) leads formally to a consequence for "you" — したがって's exact job; ただし would instead carve out an exception to the rule itself.

Q4

「4位でした。」「（　）メダルは取れなかったということですね、残念。」"Yon-i deshita." "(___) medaru wa torenakatta to iu koto desu ne, zannen."

Full sentence: 「4位でした。」「ということはメダルは取れなかったということですね、残念。」"Yon-i deshita." "To iu koto wa medaru wa torenakatta to iu koto desu ne, zannen."
EN: "I came in 4th place." "That means you didn't get a medal, huh. Too bad."
HI: "मैं चौथे स्थान पर आया।" "तो इसका मतलब पदक नहीं मिला, अफ़सोस।"
GU: "હું ચોથા સ્થાને આવ્યો." "તો એનો અર્થ કે મેડલ ન મળ્યો, અફસોસ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aというのはto iu no wa(wrong — no reason is being given here)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)

Q5

年中無休。（　）年末年始を除く。Nenjuu mukyuu. (___) nenmatsu-nenshi o nozoku.

Full sentence: 年中無休。ただし年末年始を除く。Nenjuu mukyuu. Tadashi nenmatsu-nenshi o nozoku.
EN: Open year-round. However, except for the New Year holiday period.
HI: साल भर खुला। परंतु नववर्ष की छुट्टियों को छोड़कर।
GU: આખું વર્ષ ખુલ્લું. પરંતુ નવા વર્ષની રજાઓ સિવાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aしたがってshitagatte(wrong — this is an exception, not a logical consequence)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

ゴルフは確かに子どものときからやっています。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ なんです。Gorufu wa tashika ni kodomo no toki kara yatte imasu. ___ nan desu.

Tiles: 1 わけではなく (wake de wa naku)　2 ちゃんと習った (chanto naratta)　3 ただし (tadashi)　4 自己流 (jikoryuu)

3 ただし→2 ちゃんと習った→1 わけではなく→4 自己流

ゴルフは確かに子どものときからやっています。ただし、ちゃんと習ったわけではなく自己流なんです。Gorufu wa tashika ni kodomo no toki kara yatte imasu. Tadashi, chanto naratta wake de wa naku jikoryuu nan desu.

EN: I certainly have been playing golf since I was a child. However, it's not that I properly learned it — it's self-taught.
HI: मैं गोल्फ़ ज़रूर बचपन से खेल रहा हूँ। परंतु ऐसा नहीं कि मैंने ठीक से सीखा है, यह ख़ुद-ब-ख़ुद सीखा हुआ है।
GU: હું ગોલ્ફ ચોક્કસ બાળપણથી રમું છું. પરંતુ એવું નથી કે મેં બરાબર શીખ્યું છે, આ જાતે શીખેલું છે.

Why: ただし adds the qualifying exception ("but not properly trained") to the initial claim of long experience.

Q7

最近疲労がたまっています。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿としても寝られないからです。Saikin hirou ga tamatte imasu. ___ toshitemo nerarenai kara desu.

Tiles: 1 心配事があって (shinpaigoto ga atte)　2 忙しすぎる上に (isogashi-sugiru ue ni)　3 寝よう (neyou)　4 というのは (to iu no wa)

4 というのは→2 忙しすぎる上に→1 心配事があって→3 寝よう

最近疲労がたまっています。というのは、忙しすぎる上に心配事があって寝ようとしても寝られないからです。Saikin hirou ga tamatte imasu. To iu no wa, isogashi-sugiru ue ni shinpaigoto ga atte neyou to shitemo nerarenai kara desu.

EN: Lately, fatigue has been building up. That's because, on top of being too busy, I have worries, so even when I try to sleep, I can't.
HI: हाल ही में थकान बढ़ती जा रही है। वजह यह है कि बहुत व्यस्त होने के अलावा चिंताएँ भी हैं, तो सोने की कोशिश करने पर भी नींद नहीं आती।
GU: તાજેતરમાં થાક વધતો જાય છે. કારણ એ છે કે ખૂબ વ્યસ્ત હોવા ઉપરાંત ચિંતાઓ પણ છે, એટલે ઊંઘવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરવા છતાં ઊંઘ આવતી નથી.

Why: というのは introduces the reason for the stated fatigue (busyness + worry preventing sleep).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split into two clean pairs: ということは vs というのは (inference direction), and したがって vs ただし (result vs. exception) — plus the bonus ただ vs ただし contrast from the もっと！box.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
ということはto iu koto wa That means (conclusion) A。ということはB。 Reasons forward: A is a fact, B is the conclusion drawn from it. Inferring an unstated conclusion from a given fact.
というのはto iu no wa The reason is (explanation) A。というのはB。 Reasons backward: A is stated first, B explains why A is true. Justifying/explaining a claim you just made.
したがってshitagatte Therefore (formal result) A（理由）。したがってB（結果）。 States a logical consequence — B must follow necessarily from A. Formal writing needing a "therefore."
ただしtadashi However / except that A（規則）。ただしB（例外）。 Carves an exception into A's rule — B doesn't follow from A, it limits it. Fine print: rules, discounts, schedules with a stated exception.
ただtada (bonus, もっと！box) But / only (a caveat) A。ただB（問題点・疑問）。 Adds a problem or open question, not a formal rule-exception like ただし. Casually noting a drawback or lingering doubt.
Exam trap: したがって vs ただし is the headline trap today (see Q3/Q5): both are formal and both can follow a rule-like first sentence, but ask whether B is a consequence that must follow (→ したがって) or an exception that limits the rule (→ ただし). Separately, ただし vs ただ is a near-homophone trap: ただし is a strict, formal exception clause; ただ is a softer, more conversational "but there's this one problem/thought" — swapping them changes the register, not just the nuance.
