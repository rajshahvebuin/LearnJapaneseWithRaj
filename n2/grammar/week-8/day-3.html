Notes
Sharp register split today: すなわち, あるいは and だが are all tagged 硬 formal/written in the source, while だって is marked with a speech-bubble icon (💬) as distinctly conversational — the most formal and most casual connectives of the week sit side by side on the same page, which is exactly why the book groups them (see §3).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 すなわち

硬 formal/written
Readingsunawachi
Meaning (EN)"namely / in other words" — restates the same thing in different words (a strict rewording, not a summary/conclusion)
Meaning (HI)यानी कि / दूसरे शब्दों में — वही बात दूसरे शब्दों में दोहराने के लिए (निष्कर्ष नहीं)
Meaning (GU)એટલે કે / બીજા શબ્દોમાં — એ જ વાત બીજા શબ્દોમાં ફરીથી કહેવા માટે (નિષ્કર્ષ નહીં)
Connection (接続)A、すなわちA'（Aと同じ内容の言い換え）。
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useThe book stresses すなわち looks similar to つまり but cannot be used when stating a conclusion drawn from A — only for a strict rewording of the exact same fact.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — rewordingA、すなわちA'。母の兄、すなわちおじさんHaha no ani, sunawachi ojisan
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「母の兄、すなわち僕のおじですが……」"Haha no ani, sunawachi boku no oji desu ga……" "My mother's older brother — in other words, my uncle…" "माँ का बड़ा भाई, यानी कि मेरे मामा……" "માતાનો મોટો ભાઈ, એટલે કે મારા મામા……"
このペットボトルには1,000ミリリットル、すなわち1リットルの水が入っています。Kono petto-botoru ni wa sen miririttoru, sunawachi ichi rittoru no mizu ga haitte imasu. This PET bottle contains 1,000 milliliters — in other words, one liter — of water. इस प्लास्टिक की बोतल में 1,000 मिलीलीटर, यानी कि एक लीटर पानी है। આ પ્લાસ્ટિકની બોટલમાં 1,000 મિલિલિટર, એટલે કે એક લિટર પાણી છે.

Book gloss: (=別の言い方では). The book's own ダメ (wrong) example is the clearest warning: 財布をなくした。すなわちお金がない is marked wrong — the correct connective for drawing that conclusion is つまり, not すなわち: 財布をなくした。つまりお金がない。

【2】 あるいは

硬 formal/written
Readingarui wa
Meaning (EN)"or" — presents two options, either of which is acceptable
Meaning (HI)अथवा / या फिर — दो विकल्पों में से कोई एक चुनने के लिए
Meaning (GU)અથવા / અગર — બે વિકલ્પોમાંથી કોઈ એક પસંદ કરવા માટે
Connection (接続)A あるいは B
Register硬 — the formal written equivalent of または
Typical useInterchangeable in meaning with または, but reach for あるいは in formal writing (notices, contracts, official announcements).
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — either/orA あるいは BファックスあるいはメールFakkusu arui wa meeru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ファックス、あるいはメールでお知らせください。Fakkusu, arui wa meeru de oshirase kudasai. Please let us know by fax, or by email. कृपया फ़ैक्स से, या फिर ईमेल से सूचित करें। કૃપા કરીને ફેક્સથી, અથવા ઈમેલથી જાણ કરો.
来週の火曜日の午後はどうですか。あるいは水曜日の午前でもかまいませんが……。Raishuu no kayoubi no gogo wa dou desu ka. Arui wa suiyoubi no gozen demo kamaimasen ga…… How about Tuesday afternoon next week? Or Wednesday morning would also be fine… अगले मंगलवार दोपहर कैसा रहेगा? या फिर बुधवार सुबह भी चलेगा……। આવતા મંગળવારે બપોરે કેવું રહેશે? અથવા બુધવારે સવારે પણ ચાલશે……

Book gloss: (=または). Appears constantly in official notices offering the reader a choice of method (問題1 Q3 in this week's own 実戦問題 test reuses this exact pattern: 運転免許証あるいは健康保険証など).

【3】 だが

硬 formal/written
Readingdaga
Meaning (EN)"but / however" — the formal written equivalent of しかし／けれども
Meaning (HI)लेकिन / परंतु — "しかし／けれども" का औपचारिक लिखित रूप
Meaning (GU)પણ / પરંતુ — "しかし／けれども"નું ઔપચારિક લેખિત સ્વરૂપ
Connection (接続)A。だがB。
Register硬 — formal, written
Typical useA plain contrastive "but" between two full sentences, pitched at essay/speech register rather than everyday conversation.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — formal contrastA。だがB。難しい挑戦だ。だが失敗を恐れるな。Muzukashii chousen da. Daga shippai o osoreru na.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
これは難しい挑戦だ。だが失敗を恐れてはいけない。Kore wa muzukashii chousen da. Daga shippai o osorete wa ikenai. This is a difficult challenge. But you must not be afraid of failing. यह एक कठिन चुनौती है। लेकिन असफलता से डरना नहीं चाहिए। આ એક મુશ્કેલ પડકાર છે. પણ નિષ્ફળતાથી ડરવું ન જોઈએ.
生活は貧しい。だが幸せだ。Seikatsu wa mazushii. Daga shiawase da. Our life is poor. But we are happy. हमारी ज़िंदगी ग़रीब है। लेकिन हम ख़ुश हैं। અમારું જીવન ગરીબ છે. પણ અમે ખુશ છીએ.

Book gloss: (=「しかし」「けれども」の硬い表現). Use だが in essays and speeches; save しかし／けれども for everyday register, and だって (below) for casual spoken excuses.

【4】 だって

conversational 💬
Readingdatte
Meaning (EN)"because / well, it's just that…" — a casual, often defensive way of giving a reason, typically closing with 〜だもの／〜だもん
Meaning (HI)क्योंकि / बात यह है कि… — किसी बात की सफ़ाई या वजह देने का बोलचाल का अंदाज़, अक्सर 〜だもの／〜だもん के साथ
Meaning (GU)કારણ કે / વાત એમ છે કે… — કોઈ વાતનું બચાવ કે કારણ આપવાની બોલચાલની રીત, ઘણીવાર 〜だもの／〜だもん સાથે
Connection (接続)「A?」「だって、B（理由・原因）だもの／だもん。」
RegisterConversational — the casual equivalent of なぜなら／なぜかというと／どうしてかというと
Typical useAlmost always defends or explains a previous action/statement that the listener questioned or criticized — it carries a mildly whiny or self-justifying tone.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — casual reason「A?」「だって、B。」だって約束を破ったじゃないか。Datte yakusoku o yabutta ja nai ka.
Fixed casual patternだって〜だもの／だって〜だもんだってやさしかったんだもん。Datte yasashikattan damon.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「何を怒ってるの?」「だって、約束を破ったじゃないか。」"Nani o okotteru no?" "Datte, yakusoku o yabutta ja nai ka." "Why are you mad?" "Because you broke your promise!" "तुम्हें किस बात का ग़ुस्सा आ रहा है?" "क्योंकि तुमने वादा तोड़ा ना!" "તને શેનો ગુસ્સો આવે છે?" "કારણ કે તેં વચન તોડ્યું ને!"
「テストの点、よかったんだって?」「うん。だってやさしかったんだもん。」"Tesuto no ten, yokattan datte?" "Un. Datte yasashikattan damon." "I heard you did well on the test?" "Yeah. Because it was easy!" "सुना है टेस्ट में अच्छे नंबर आए?" "हाँ। क्योंकि पेपर आसान था!" "સાંભળ્યું છે ટેસ્ટમાં સારા માર્ક્સ આવ્યા?" "હા. કારણ કે પેપર સહેલું હતું!"

Book gloss: (=「なぜなら」「なぜかというと」「どうしてかというと」の会話的表現; fixed pattern だって〜だもの／だって〜だもん, cross-referenced to the book's own p.16). だって sits at the opposite register extreme from today's other three points — never use it in writing or with a superior.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

ご希望の賞品A（　）Bのいずれか一つに○をつけ、ご応募ください。Gokibou no shouhin A (___) B no izureka hitotsu ni maru o tsuke, goouboo kudasai.

Full sentence: ご希望の賞品Aあるいは、Bのいずれか一つに○をつけ、ご応募ください。Gokibou no shouhin A arui wa, B no izureka hitotsu ni maru o tsuke, goouboo kudasai.
EN: Please circle either prize A or B (whichever you prefer) and submit your application.
HI: पसंदीदा इनाम A अथवा B में से किसी एक पर घेरा लगाकर आवेदन करें।
GU: ઇચ્છિત ઇનામ A અથવા B માંથી કોઈ એક પર ગોળ કરીને અરજી કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあるいはarui waor (choosing one of two)अथवाઅથવા
bすなわちsunawachi(wrong — A and B are different prizes, not a rewording)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)

Why: Prizes A and B are alternatives to choose between, which is あるいは's job; すなわち would only fit if B were merely restating A.

Q2

「いつまで寝てるの。早く起きなさい。」「だって（　）。」"Itsu made neteru no. Hayaku okinasai." "Datte (___)."

Full sentence: 「いつまで寝てるの。早く起きなさい。」「だって眠いんだもん。」"…" "Datte nemuindamon."
EN: "How long are you going to sleep? Get up already." "Because I'm sleepy!"
HI: "कब तक सोते रहोगे। जल्दी उठो।" "क्योंकि मुझे नींद आ रही है!"
GU: "ક્યાં સુધી ઊંઘતો રહીશ. જલ્દી ઊઠ." "કારણ કે મને ઊંઘ આવે છે!"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a眠いもんかnemui monka(wrong — もんか is a rhetorical "as if!", the opposite meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)
b眠いんだもんnemuindamonbecause I'm sleepy (casual excuse)क्योंकि नींद आ रही हैકારણ કે ઊંઘ આવે છે

Why: だって pairs with the fixed casual ending 〜んだもん (giving a whiny excuse), not with もんか, which is a completely different rhetorical-negation pattern ("as if I'm sleepy!").

夫の父、（　）義理の父と一緒に暮らすことになりました。Otto no chichi, (___) giri no chichi to issho ni kurasu koto ni narimashita.

Full sentence: 夫の父、すなわち義理の父と一緒に暮らすことになりました。Otto no chichi, sunawachi giri no chichi to issho ni kurasu koto ni narimashita.
EN: My husband's father — in other words, my father-in-law — and I are going to live together.
HI: पति के पिता, यानी कि मेरे ससुर, के साथ रहने का तय हुआ है।
GU: પતિના પિતા, એટલે કે મારા સસરા, સાથે રહેવાનું નક્કી થયું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aすなわちsunawachinamely / in other wordsयानी किએટલે કે

Why: "Husband's father" and "father-in-law" are exactly the same person restated — the strict rewording that すなわち requires.

Q4

よく晴れている。（　）気温は低い。Yoku harete iru. (___) kion wa hikui.

Full sentence: よく晴れている。だが気温は低い。Yoku harete iru. Daga kion wa hikui.
EN: It's very clear and sunny. But the temperature is low.
HI: मौसम बिल्कुल साफ़ है। लेकिन तापमान कम है।
GU: હવામાન એકદમ ચોખ્ખું છે. પણ તાપમાન ઓછું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだってdatte(wrong — だって introduces a reason, not a contrast between two sentences)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)
bだがdagabut / however (formal contrast)लेकिनપણ

Q5

「寒いし、疲れているし、あそこは遠いし……」「（　）行きたくないってことね。」"Samui shi, tsukarete iru shi, asoko wa tooi shi……" "(___) ikitakunai tte koto ne."

Full sentence: 「寒いし、疲れているし、あそこは遠いし……」「つまり行きたくないってことね。」"…" "Tsumari ikitakunai tte koto ne."
EN: "It's cold, I'm tired, and it's far…" "In other words, you don't want to go, right?"
HI: "ठंड है, थका हुआ हूँ, और वो जगह दूर भी है……।" "मतलब तुम जाना नहीं चाहते, है ना?"
GU: "ઠંડી છે, થાકેલો છું, અને એ જગ્યા દૂર પણ છે……" "મતલબ તારે નથી જવું, ખરું ને?"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aつまりtsumariin other words (drawing a conclusion)मतलबમતલબ
bすなわちsunawachi(wrong — this is a conclusion drawn from excuses, not a strict rewording)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીं ખોટું)

Why: This is exactly the trap the book warns about: summarizing a string of excuses into "so you mean you don't want to go" is a conclusion, which only つまり can express — すなわち is reserved for a strict rewording of the same fact, never a conclusion.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

最近はCD、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿。Saikin wa CD, ___.

Tiles: 1 多く販売されている (ooku hanbai sarete iru)　2 すなわち (sunawachi)　3 語学教材が (gogaku kyouzai ga)　4 コンパクトディスク付きの (konpakuto disuku tsuki no)

最近はCD、すなわちコンパクトディスク付きの語学教材が多く販売されている。Saikin wa CD, sunawachi konpakuto disuku tsuki no gogaku kyouzai ga ooku hanbai sarete iru.

EN: Recently, CDs — that is, language-learning materials that come with a compact disc — are selling well.
HI: आजकल सीडी, यानी कि कॉम्पैक्ट डिस्क के साथ आने वाली भाषा सीखने की सामग्री, ख़ूब बिक रही है।
GU: આજકાલ સીડી, એટલે કે કોમ્પેક્ટ ડિસ્ક સાથે આવતી ભાષા શીખવાની સામગ્રી, ખૂબ વેચાય છે.

Why: "CD" is immediately reworded into a fuller description of the same product — すなわち's exact strict-rewording function.

Q7

事故にあったときは＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿。Jiko ni atta toki wa ___.

Tiles: 1 なんともなかった (nantomo nakatta)　2 頭が痛くなった (atama ga itaku natta)　3 だが (daga)　4 そのあくる日に (sono akuru hi ni)

1 なんともなかった→3 だが→4 そのあくる日に→2 頭が痛くなった

事故にあったときはなんともなかった。だがそのあくる日に頭が痛くなった。Jiko ni atta toki wa nantomo nakatta. Daga sono akuru hi ni atama ga itaku natta.

EN: When I had the accident, I was completely fine. But the very next day, I got a headache.
HI: दुर्घटना के वक़्त मुझे कुछ नहीं हुआ था। लेकिन अगले दिन मेरे सिर में दर्द होने लगा।
GU: અકસ્માત વખતે મને કંઈ થયું નહોતું. પણ બીજા દિવસે માથું દુખવા લાગ્યું.

Why: だが provides the plain formal "but" pivot between "fine at first" and "headache the next day."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split cleanly by register — three formal/written, one purely conversational — which is itself the exam trap: pick the connective that matches the sentence's register, not just its rough meaning.

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
すなわちsunawachi Namely / in other words A、すなわちA'。 Strict rewording only — cannot state a conclusion (that's つまり's job). Restating the exact same fact in different words, formally.
あるいはarui wa Or A あるいは B Presents genuine alternatives — A and B are different things, not the same thing reworded. Formal "or" between two real options.
だがdaga But / however A。だがB。 Formal written equivalent of しかし／けれども — plain contrast, no reason-giving nuance. Essay/speech-register "but."
だってdatte Because / well, it's just that… 「A?」「だってB（理由）だもの／だもん。」 Only for spoken excuses/justifications, opposite register extreme from だが. Casually defending yourself when questioned.
Exam trap: すなわち vs つまり is the sneaky one here (see Q5): both translate loosely as "in other words," but すなわち is banned from stating a conclusion — reserve it for pure rewording (母の兄 → おじ), and reach for つまり whenever the second half is a conclusion drawn from the first (寒いし疲れているし… → つまり行きたくない). Also don't let register fool you: あるいは/だが/すなわち will never appear in casual dialogue, and だって will never appear in a formal notice.
