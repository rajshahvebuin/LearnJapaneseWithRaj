Notes
Week 8 is a different flavor of "grammar point" from earlier weeks: all four items today are 接続詞 (conjunctions/connectives) that join two complete, independent sentences — they don't attach to a verb/adjective stem the way inflectional patterns (like N1's 〜こそ series) do. それなら and the conversational で-shortening of それで carry a small speech-bubble icon in the source, flagging spoken/conversational register — noted per point below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 それなのに

neutral register
Readingsore na noni
Meaning (EN)"and yet / despite that" — marks a surprising or disappointing result that goes against what sentence A would lead you to expect
Meaning (HI)फिर भी / ऐसा होने के बावजूद — पिछली बात के बावजूद अप्रत्याशित (अक्सर निराशाजनक) परिणाम बताने के लिए
Meaning (GU)છતાં પણ / એમ છતાં — અગાઉની વાત છતાં અનપેક્ષિત (ઘણીવાર નિરાશાજનક) પરિણામ દર્શાવવા માટે
Connection (接続)[Sentence A]。それなのに、[Sentence B]。
RegisterNeutral — used in both writing and speech
Typical useFunctions like 〜のに, but joins two separate sentences instead of attaching mid-clause. Always signals the speaker's surprise or dissatisfaction that B happened even though A would suggest otherwise.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective (接続詞)[complete sentence A]。それなのに[complete sentence B]。高かった。それなのにすぐ壊れた。Takakatta. Sore na noni sugu kowareta.
(added, not in book) Unlike verb/adjective/noun-attaching patterns, それなのに never conjugates and never attaches inside a clause — it always opens a brand-new sentence.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この時計は高かった。それなのにすぐに壊れた。Kono tokei wa takakatta. Sore na noni sugu ni kowareta. This watch was expensive, and yet it broke right away. यह घड़ी महंगी थी। फिर भी यह जल्दी खराब हो गई। આ ઘડિયાળ મોંઘી હતી. છતાં પણ તે તરત જ ખરાબ થઈ ગઈ.
一生懸命勉強している。それなのに成績はよくならない。Isshoukenmei benkyou shite iru. Sore na noni seiseki wa yoku naranai. I'm studying as hard as I can. And yet my grades aren't improving. मैं जी-जान से पढ़ाई कर रहा हूँ। फिर भी मेरे नंबर अच्छे नहीं हो रहे। હું ખૂબ મહેનતથી અભ્યાસ કરી રહ્યો છું. છતાં પણ મારા માર્ક્સ સુધરતા નથી.

The book glosses それなのに as (=〜のに): 高かった。それなのにすぐ壊れた。 ≈ 高かったのにすぐ壊れた。 Use それなのに when you want the surprise/complaint to land as its own separate sentence — useful for spoken emphasis or when sentence A is long.

【2】 それでも

neutral register
Readingsore demo
Meaning (EN)"even so / still" — concedes a difficult situation (A) and asserts that the speaker's stance or action (B) holds anyway
Meaning (HI)फिर भी / तो भी — कठिन स्थिति के बावजूद अपने रुख़/काम पर कायम रहना बताने के लिए
Meaning (GU)તો પણ / છતાં — મુશ્કેલ પરિસ્થિતિ છતાં પોતાનું વલણ/કાર્ય ચાલુ રાખવા માટે
Connection (接続)[Sentence A — a difficulty/objection]。それでも、[Sentence B — speaker perseveres]。
RegisterNeutral
Typical useClose to それなのに, but それでも carries a stronger flavor of willful determination ("no matter what") rather than surprise at a mismatch.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective (接続詞)[complete sentence A]。それでも[complete sentence B]。大雨だ。それでも出かける。Oo-ame da. Soredemo dekakeru.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
外は大雨だ。それでも出かけないといけない。Soto wa oo-ame da. Soredemo dekakenai to ikenai. It's pouring rain outside. Even so, I have to go out. बाहर मूसलाधार बारिश हो रही है। फिर भी मुझे बाहर जाना ही होगा। બહાર ધોધમાર વરસાદ છે. તો પણ મારે બહાર જવું જ પડશે.
みんなに反対されている。それでも私は彼と結婚したい。Minna ni hantai sarete iru. Soredemo watashi wa kare to kekkon shitai. Everyone is against it. Even so, I want to marry him. सब लोग इसके खिलाफ हैं। फिर भी मैं उससे शादी करना चाहती हूँ। બધા તેની વિરુદ્ધ છે. તો પણ હું તેની સાથે લગ્ન કરવા માંગું છું.

Book gloss: (=だけど／けれども／しかし). それでも is the connective you reach for when the speaker is choosing to push through a stated obstacle, not just noting an odd result.

【3】 それなら

conversational 💬
Readingsore nara
Meaning (EN)"if that's the case / then" — builds a judgment, suggestion, or decision directly on what the other person just said
Meaning (HI)अगर ऐसा है तो / तो फिर — सामने वाले की बात के आधार पर राय या सुझाव देने के लिए
Meaning (GU)જો એમ હોય તો / તો પછી — સામેની વ્યક્તિની વાત પરથી અભિપ્રાય કે સૂચન આપવા માટે
Connection (接続)「[A]。」「それなら(ば)、[judgment/suggestion based on A]。」
RegisterNeutral-to-casual; the book marks conversational shortenings じゃ(あ)／それじゃ(あ) with a small speech-bubble icon (💬), flagging spoken register
Typical useAlmost always appears in dialogue as a direct reaction — it needs a preceding statement (A) to react to, unlike それなのに/それでも which can open a narrative.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective (接続詞)「A。」「それなら、B。」渋滞だとか。それなら電車で行こう。Juutai da toka. Sore nara densha de ikou.
Casual variantじゃ(あ)／それじゃ(あ) 💬(added, not in book) 「じゃ、電車で行こう。」Ja, densha de ikou.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「道がすごく渋滞しているようだよ。」「それなら、電車で行こう。」"Michi ga sugoku juutai shite iru you da yo." "Sore nara, densha de ikou." "The roads look really jammed." "In that case, let's go by train." "लगता है रास्ते में बहुत जाम है।" "तो फिर, ट्रेन से चलते हैं।" "રસ્તા પર ખૂબ ટ્રાફિક જામ હોય એવું લાગે છે." "તો પછી, ટ્રેનમાં જઈએ."
「今、それ、やりたくない。」「それならやらなくていいよ。」"Ima, sore, yaritakunai." "Sore nara yaranakute ii yo." "I don't want to do that right now." "Then you don't have to." "अभी मुझे यह करने का मन नहीं है।" "तो फिर मत करो।" "અત્યારે મને એ કરવાનું મન નથી." "તો પછી ન કરો."

Book gloss: (=じゃ(あ)／それじゃ(あ) 💬). Think of それなら as "given what you just told me…" — it cannot open a conversation cold, it always responds to prior context.

【4】 それで

neutral / conversational 💬
Readingsorede
Meaning (EN)Two uses: ① "so / that's why" (states the result of a stated reason); ② "and then? / so?" (a conversational prompt urging the listener to continue their story) — the shortened spoken form で is common for ②, flagged with a speech-bubble icon (💬) in the source
Meaning (HI)① इसलिए / इस वजह से; ② फिर? / उसके बाद? (बात आगे बढ़ाने के लिए)
Meaning (GU)① એટલે / તેથી; ② પછી? / તો પછી? (વાત આગળ ચલાવવા માટે)
Connection (接続)① [reason A]。それで、[result B]。  |  ② 「A。」「それで／で 💬、[question about A's outcome]?」
RegisterNeutral for ①; casual/conversational for ② (especially the bare で shortening)
Typical useDon't confuse ② それで/で with そこで (Day 2) — それで only asks about or states the continuation/result of what was just said; そこで introduces a new action taken to solve/address a situation.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — reason→result[reason A]。それで[result B]。働きすぎた。それで病気になった。Hatarakisugita. Sorede byouki ni natta.
Connective — prompting more「A。」「それで／で 💬、…?」「面接を受けたんだ。」「で、どうだった?」"Mensetsu o uketan da." "De, dou datta?"
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
父は働きすぎた。それで病気になった。Chichi wa hatarakisugita. Sorede byouki ni natta. My father worked too much. That's why he got sick. पापा ने बहुत ज़्यादा काम किया। इसलिए वे बीमार पड़ गए। પપ્પાએ બહુ વધારે કામ કર્યું. એટલે તે બીમાર પડી ગયા.
「田中君、インフルエンザにかかったんだって。」「それで学校を休んだんだね。」"Tanaka-kun, infuruenza ni kakattan datte." "Sorede gakkou o yasundan da ne." "I heard Tanaka caught the flu." "So that's why he was out of school." "सुना है तनाका को फ्लू हो गया।" "तो इसीलिए वह स्कूल नहीं आया।" "સાંભળ્યું છે તનાકાને ફ્લૂ થયો છે." "તો એટલે જ તે શાળાએ ન આવ્યો."
「田中君にお金を貸してくれって言われたんだ。」「へえー。それで、貸してあげたの?」"Tanaka-kun ni okane o kashite kurette iwaretan da." "Hee. Sorede, kashite ageta no?" "Tanaka asked me to lend him some money." "Oh really. So, did you lend it to him?" "तनाका ने मुझसे पैसे उधार माँगे।" "अच्छा। तो फिर, तुमने उसे दे दिए?" "તનાકાએ મારી પાસે પૈસા ઉધાર માંગ્યા." "અરે વાહ. તો પછી, તેં તેને આપ્યા?"
「今日、面接を受けたんだ。」「で、どうだった?」"Kyou, mensetsu o uketan da." "De, dou datta?" "I had an interview today." "So, how was it?" "आज मेरा इंटरव्यू था।" "तो, कैसा रहा?" "આજે મારો ઇન્ટરવ્યુ હતો." "તો, કેવો રહ્યો?"

Book explicitly labels use ② as "used to get the other person to talk about something" (相手の話を聞き出すときに使う). で alone is simply それで with それ dropped in fast speech — very common in casual conversation.

Q1

「学校は春休みになったね。」「（　）電車の混み具合がいつもよりましなんだね。」"Gakkou wa harayasumi ni natta ne." "(___) densha no komiguai ga itsumo yori mashi nan da ne."

Full sentence: 「学校は春休みになったね。」「それで電車の混み具合がいつもよりましなんだね。」"Gakkou wa harayasumi ni natta ne." "Sorede densha no komiguai ga itsumo yori mashi nan da ne."
EN: "School's on spring break now, huh." "That's why the trains are less crowded than usual, huh."
HI: "स्कूल में स्प्रिंग ब्रेक शुरू हो गया, है ना।" "इसीलिए ट्रेन में हमेशा से कम भीड़ है।"
GU: "શાળામાં સ્પ્રિંગ વેકેશન શરૂ થઈ ગયું, ખરું ને." "એટલે જ ટ્રેનમાં હંમેશા કરતાં ઓછી ભીડ છે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそれでsoredeso / that's why (reason→result)इसलिएએટલે
bそれでもsoredemo(wrong — no obstacle being overcome here)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)

Why: Spring break is the reason trains are less crowded — a plain reason→result link, which is それで's job. それでも would need a contradiction/obstacle, which isn't present.

Q2

毎日節約している。（　）、お金がちっともたまらない。Mainichi setsuyaku shite iru. (___), okane ga chittomo tamaranai.

Full sentence: 毎日節約している。それなのに、お金がちっともたまらない。Mainichi setsuyaku shite iru. Sore na noni, okane ga chittomo tamaranai.
EN: I'm saving money every day. And yet I can't save up any money at all.
HI: मैं रोज़ बचत करता हूँ। फिर भी बिल्कुल पैसा नहीं जुड़ पाता।
GU: હું રોજ બચત કરું છું. છતાં પણ પૈસા બિલકુલ ભેગા થતા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそれでsorede(wrong — no reason→result link, this is a mismatch)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)
bそれなのにsore na noniand yet / despite thatफिर भीછતાં પણ

Why: Saving money daily should lead to savings building up — but it doesn't, which is exactly the surprising/disappointing mismatch それなのに flags.

Q3

あの大学は絶対無理だと言われた。（　）僕は受けるつもりだ。Ano daigaku wa zettai muri da to iwareta. (___) boku wa ukeru tsumori da.

Full sentence: あの大学は絶対無理だと言われた。それでも僕は受けるつもりだ。Ano daigaku wa zettai muri da to iwareta. Soredemo boku wa ukeru tsumori da.
EN: I was told that university is absolutely out of reach for me. Even so, I intend to apply.
HI: मुझे बताया गया कि उस यूनिवर्सिटी में जाना बिल्कुल नामुमकिन है। फिर भी मैं आवेदन करने वाला हूँ।
GU: મને કહેવામાં આવ્યું કે તે યુનિવર્સિટીમાં જવું બિલકુલ અશક્ય છે. તો પણ હું અરજી કરવાનો છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそれでもsoredemoeven so (perseverance despite a difficulty)फिर भीતો પણ
bそれならsore nara(wrong — それなら needs a suggestion built on A, not defiance of it)(यहाँ ग़लत)(અહીં ખોટું)

Why: The speaker is choosing to apply anyway, despite being told it's hopeless — that willful "no matter what" stance is それでも, not それなら (which would suggest a next step based on the objection, e.g. "then try a different school").

Q4

「本は好きだから買いたいけど、高いし、増え続けて邪魔だし……。」「（　）図書館で借りたらいいじゃないですか。」"Hon wa suki dakara kaitai kedo, takai shi, fuetsuzukete jama da shi……." "(___) toshokan de karitara ii ja nai desu ka."

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q5

あのメーカーはいろいろと問題を起こしている。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 私には理解できない。Ano meekaa wa iroiro to mondai o okoshite iru. ___ watashi ni wa rikai dekinai.

Tiles: 1 というのは (to iu no wa)　2 それなのに (sore na noni)　3 どういうことなのか (dou iu koto na no ka)　4 客が減らない (kyaku ga heranai)
(Note: this book marks blanks with plain numbered underscores, not a single ★ blank, for Days 1–6.)

2 それなのに→4 客が減らない→1 というのは→3 どういうことなのか

あのメーカーはいろいろと問題を起こしている。それなのに客が減らないというのはどういうことなのか私には理解できない。Ano meekaa wa iroiro to mondai o okoshite iru. Sore na noni kyaku ga heranai to iu no wa dou iu koto na no ka watashi ni wa rikai dekinai.

EN: That manufacturer has caused all sorts of problems. And yet, I can't understand what it means that their customers haven't decreased.
HI: उस निर्माता कंपनी ने तरह-तरह की समस्याएँ खड़ी की हैं। फिर भी उसके ग्राहक कम नहीं हुए हैं — यह मेरी समझ में नहीं आता।
GU: તે ઉત્પાદક કંપનીએ અનેક પ્રકારની સમસ્યાઓ ઊભી કરી છે. છતાં પણ તેના ગ્રાહકો ઘટ્યા નથી — આ મને સમજાતું નથી.

Why: それなのに opens the surprising twist (customers haven't dropped despite the problems); というのは then frames the whole preceding clause as "the thing that" the speaker can't understand.

Q6

彼はテレビ番組を見ては文句ばかり言っている。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ つもりはなさそうだ。Kare wa terebi bangumi o mite wa monku bakari itte iru. ___ tsumori wa nasasou da.

Tiles: 1 それなら (sore nara)　2 と思うのだが (to omou no da ga)　3 見るのをやめる (miru no o yameru)　4 見なければいい (minakereba ii)

1 それなら→4 見なければいい→2 と思うのだが→3 見るのをやめる

彼はテレビ番組を見ては文句ばかり言っている。それなら見なければいいと思うのだが見るのをやめるつもりはなさそうだ。Kare wa terebi bangumi o mite wa monku bakari itte iru. Sore nara minakereba ii to omou no da ga miru no o yameru tsumori wa nasasou da.

EN: He complains every time he watches TV shows. I think if that's the case, he should just stop watching, but he doesn't seem to have any intention of stopping.
HI: वह जब भी टीवी शो देखता है, बस शिकायत ही करता रहता है। मुझे लगता है कि तो फिर उसे देखना ही बंद कर देना चाहिए, पर लगता नहीं कि उसका देखना बंद करने का कोई इरादा है।
GU: તે જ્યારે પણ ટીવી શો જુએ છે ત્યારે ફક્ત ફરિયાદ જ કરતો રહે છે. મને લાગે છે કે તો પછી તેણે જોવાનું જ બંધ કરી દેવું જોઈએ, પણ તેનો જોવાનું બંધ કરવાનો કોઈ ઇરાદો હોય એવું લાગતું નથી.

Why: それなら reacts to "he keeps complaining" with a reasonable suggestion (見なければいい, "he should just not watch") — again, a suggestion built directly on the preceding statement.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four connectives today link two separate sentences and can look interchangeable at a glance in a "which conjunction fits?" question. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
それなのにsore na noni And yet / despite that A。それなのにB。 Flags a surprising/disappointing mismatch between A and B. No sense of the speaker "fighting" the situation. B contradicts what A would lead you to expect, and it's disappointing.
それでもsoredemo Even so / still A。それでもB。 A is an obstacle/difficulty; B is the speaker's willful decision to push through it anyway. You want to show determination despite a stated difficulty.
それならsore nara If that's the case / then 「A。」「それならB。」 Reactive — needs another speaker's statement (A) to respond to with a judgment/suggestion (B). Replying to what someone just said with advice or a decision.
それでsorede So / and then? A。それでB。  |  「A。」「それで／で…?」 Purely reason→result (①), or a bare prompt to keep talking (②) — never concedes an obstacle the way それでも does. Stating a plain cause-and-effect, or asking "so then what?"
Exam trap: それなのに vs それでも is the classic N2 trap here — both look like "but" connectives following a negative-feeling first sentence. Ask: is the second sentence describing an unwanted result that already happened (→ それなのに) or the speaker's ongoing choice/resolve to act despite the obstacle (→ それでも)? Also watch それなら vs それでも: それなら always needs to be reacting to a preceding statement with a suggestion, while それでも asserts the speaker's own stance regardless of the obstacle.
