1 Grammar Points

【1】 もっとも

neutral register
Readingmottomo
Meaning (EN)"but also / although" — softens or partially retracts the previous statement by attaching an exception or condition
Meaning (HI)हालांकि / बशर्ते कि… — पिछली बात को थोड़ा नरम करते हुए कोई शर्त या अपवाद जोड़ने के लिए
Meaning (GU)જોકે / જો કે… — અગાઉની વાતને થોડી હળવી કરીને શરત કે અપવાદ ઉમેરવા માટે
Connection (接続)A（強い主張）。もっともB（例外・条件で修正）。
RegisterNeutral
Typical useBook gloss: (=ただし). Very close to ただし in meaning, but もっとも specifically walks back a strong statement the speaker just made, as if adding "well, actually, to be fair…"
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — self-correcting exceptionA。もっともB。飲食禁止。もっとも水はいい。Inshoku kinshi. Mottomo mizu wa ii.
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
検査の前夜から飲食禁止です。もっとも水は飲んでもかまいません。Kensa no zen'ya kara inshoku kinshi desu. Mottomo mizu wa nonde mo kamaimasen. No food or drink is allowed from the night before the examination. That said, water is fine. जाँच से एक रात पहले से खाना-पीना मना है। हालांकि पानी पीना ठीक है। તપાસના આગલી રાતથી ખાવા-પીવાની મનાઈ છે. જોકે પાણી પીવું વાંધો નથી.
全員が参加しなければなりません。もっとも病気の場合は別です。Zen'in ga sanka shinakereba narimasen. Mottomo byouki no baai wa betsu desu. Everyone must attend. That said, if you're sick, that's a separate matter. सबको भाग लेना ज़रूरी है। हालांकि बीमार होने की स्थिति अलग है। બધાએ ભાગ લેવો જરૂરી છે. જોકે બીમાર હોય તો એ અલગ વાત છે.

Both examples use もっとも after a rule stated firmly (禁止, なければなりません) to immediately soften it with a real-world exception — a very natural rhetorical move in announcements and instructions.

【2】 なお

neutral register
Readingnao
Meaning (EN)"additionally / also note that…" — appends supplementary information, which may or may not be related to the previous sentence
Meaning (HI)और, यह भी ध्यान दें कि… — पिछली बात से जुड़ी या असंबंधित अतिरिक्त जानकारी जोड़ने के लिए
Meaning (GU)વધુમાં, નોંધ કરો કે… — અગાઉની વાત સાથે સંબંધિત કે અસંબંધિત વધારાની માહિતી ઉમેરવા માટે
Connection (接続)A。なおB（追加情報、無関係でも可）。
RegisterNeutral, common in announcements/notices
Typical useBook explicitly notes B "does not necessarily have to be related to" A — the loosest of today's four connectives in terms of topical continuity.
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — supplementary infoA。なおB。以上です。なお詳細は…Ijou desu. Nao shousai wa…
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「この件の説明は以上です。なお、詳細についてはプリントをご覧ください。」"Kono ken no setsumei wa ijou desu. Nao, shousai ni tsuite wa purinto o goran kudasai." "That's the explanation for this matter. Additionally, please see the printout for details." "इस विषय की व्याख्या इतनी ही है। साथ ही, विवरण के लिए कृपया प्रिंटआउट देखें।" "આ બાબતની સમજૂતી આટલી જ છે. વધુમાં, વિગતો માટે કૃપા કરીને પ્રિન્ટઆઉટ જુઓ."
「今日はこれで終わります。なお次回の日時は……」"Kyou wa kore de owarimasu. Nao jikai no nichiji wa……" "That's all for today. Also, as for the date and time of the next session…" "आज के लिए इतना ही। साथ ही, अगली बार की तारीख़ और समय……।" "આજ માટે આટલું જ. વધુમાં, આગલી વખતની તારીખ અને સમય……"

Book gloss: (=話を付け加えるときに使う。前の文と関係のないことでもいい). Extremely common as the second-to-last line of an official announcement, right before closing details.

【3】 さて

neutral register
Readingsate
Meaning (EN)"well now / so" — signals a deliberate shift to a new subject, usually at a natural break point (end of a lesson, news segment, etc.)
Meaning (HI)अच्छा, अब / तो फिर — किसी स्वाभाविक पड़ाव पर नए विषय पर जाने के लिए
Meaning (GU)સારું, હવે / તો પછી — કોઈ કુદરતી વિરામ પર નવા વિષય તરફ જવા માટે
Connection (接続)A（ここで一区切り）。さてB（新しい話題）。
RegisterNeutral, used in both speech (announcements, class endings) and writing
Typical useBook gloss: (=used when changing to a new subject／話は換わって). Marks the boundary between "finishing up X" and "starting Y."
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — deliberate segueA。さてB。授業を終わります。さて来週は…Jugyou o owarimasu. Sate raishuu wa…
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
「これで授業を終わります。さて来週の予定ですが……」"Kore de jugyou o owarimasu. Sate raishuu no yotei desu ga……" "That's it for today's lesson. Now, as for next week's plan…" "आज की क्लास यहीं ख़त्म होती है। तो अब, अगले हफ़्ते की योजना के बारे में……।" "આજનો ક્લાસ અહીં પૂરો થાય છે. તો હવે, આવતા અઠવાડિયાની યોજના વિશે……"
「以上、今日のニュースをお伝えしました。さて次に天気予報です。」"Ijou, kyou no nyuusu o otsutae shimashita. Sate tsugi ni tenki yohou desu." "That was today's news. Now, next up is the weather forecast." "आज की ख़बरें इतनी ही थीं। तो अब, आगे मौसम का हाल।" "આજના સમાચાર આટલા જ હતા. તો હવે, આગળ હવામાનની આગાહી."

Both examples are "closing one segment, opening the next" — the classic broadcaster/teacher use of さて.

【4】 すると

neutral register
Readingsuruto
Meaning (EN)Two uses: ① "then / thereupon" (event B follows immediately after action A); ② "so… / that means…" (inferring a conclusion from what was just said, close to ということは)
Meaning (HI)① फिर / उसके तुरंत बाद (A के बाद तुरंत B होना); ② तो… (जो अभी कहा गया उससे निष्कर्ष निकालना)
Meaning (GU)① પછી / તરત જ (A પછી તરત B થવું); ② તો… (હમણાં કહેલી વાત પરથી નિષ્કર્ષ કાઢવો)
Connection (接続)① A（きっかけ）。するとB（起こったこと）。  |  ② 「A。」「すると、B（Aから判断した内容）。」
RegisterNeutral
Typical useUse ① for narrating a sequence of events (often in stories); use ② in dialogue when reacting to someone else's statement with an inference — functionally overlapping with ということは (Day 4).
Word typeFormationExample
Connective — immediate sequenceA。するとB。薬を塗った。すると痛みが治まった。Kusuri o nutta. Suruto itami ga osamatta.
Connective — inference (dialogue)「A。」「すると、B。」「外出していました。」「すると誰もいなかったんですね。」"Gaishutsu shite imashita." "Suruto dare mo inakattan desu ne."
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
薬を塗った。すると痛みが治まった。Kusuri o nutta. Suruto itami ga osamatta. I applied the medicine. Then, the pain subsided. मैंने दवा लगाई। फिर, दर्द कम हो गया। મેં દવા લગાવી. પછી, દુખાવો ઓછો થઈ ગયો.
窓を開けた。すると、蛾が入ってきた。Mado o aketa. Suruto, ga ga haitte kita. I opened the window. A moth came in right after. मैंने खिड़की खोली। फिर तुरंत एक पतंगा अंदर आ गया। મેં બારી ખોલી. પછી તરત જ એક શલભ અંદર આવ્યું.
「私は外出していました。」「すると、家には誰もいなかったんですね。」"Watashi wa gaishutsu shite imashita." "Suruto, ie ni wa dare mo inakattan desu ne." "I was out." "So, that means no one was home?" "मैं बाहर गया हुआ था।" "तो इसका मतलब घर में कोई नहीं था?" "હું બહાર ગયો હતો." "તો એનો અર્થ કે ઘરમાં કોઈ નહોતું?"

Book gloss for ①: (=薬を塗って、そのあとすぐに); for ②: (=そこから判断すると／ということは). Use ① strictly for sequential events; ② is essentially a synonym for ということは in reactive dialogue.

Q1

「私には両親と妹が一人います。」「（　）4人家族ですね。」"Watashi ni wa ryoushin to imouto ga hitori imasu." "(___) yonin kazoku desu ne."

Full sentence: 「…」「すると4人家族ですね。」"…" "Suruto yonin kazoku desu ne."
EN: "I have my parents and one younger sister." "So, that's a family of four, huh."
HI: "मेरे माता-पिता और एक छोटी बहन है।" "तो इसका मतलब चार लोगों का परिवार है ना।"
GU: "મારા માતાપિતા અને એક નાની બહેન છે." "તો એનો અર્થ ચાર જણનું કુટુંબ છે ને."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aするとsurutoso (inferring a conclusion)तो इसका मतलबતો એનો અર્થ

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four today are announcement/discourse-management connectives. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
もっともmottomo But also / although A（強い主張）。もっともB。 Softens/partially retracts the speaker's own strong preceding claim. Adding "to be fair…" after a firm statement or rule.
なおnao Additionally A。なおB（無関係可）。 Loosest link of the four — B need not relate to A at all. Tacking on a supplementary note at the end of an announcement.
さてsate Now / well then A（区切り）。さてB（新話題）。 Marks a deliberate, clean break between finishing one segment and starting the next. Ending a segment (class, news item, meeting point) and opening the next.
するとsuruto Then / so, that means A。するとB。  |  「A。」「すると、B。」 ① narrates an immediate real-world event sequence; ② infers a conclusion in dialogue (≈ ということは). Telling a story (①) or reacting to someone's statement with a deduction (②).
Exam trap: もっとも vs ただし (Day 4) is worth re-checking here: they're glossed as near-synonyms, but もっとも specifically softens the speaker's own just-stated claim, while ただし formally carves an exception into any rule, including one someone else stated. Also don't mix up なお (loosely related add-on) with さて (a hard topic break) — なお continues the same announcement, さて starts a new one.
