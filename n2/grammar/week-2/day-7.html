2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

マフラーを編み（　）、途中でやめてしまった。Mafuraa o ami (___), tochuu de yamete shimatta.

Full sentence: マフラーを編みかけて、途中でやめてしまった。Mafuraa o amikakete, tochuu de yamete shimatta.
EN: I started knitting a scarf but stopped partway through.
HI: मैंने मफ़लर बुनना शुरू किया था, पर बीच में ही छोड़ दिया।
GU: મેં મફલર ગૂંથવાનું શરૂ કર્યું હતું, પણ વચ્ચે જ છોડી દીધું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1えてete(not a valid attachment here)
2かけてkaketestarted but left unfinished (Week 2 Day 2)
3きってkitte(would mean "finished completely" — contradicts "stopped partway")
4ぬいてnuite(would mean "carried through" — contradicts "stopped partway")

Why: Day 2's V-stem+かける describes an action begun but left incomplete — exactly "stopped partway" here.

Q2

そのスポーツは、もともと身を守ることを目的（　）考え出されたものだ。Sono supootsu wa, motomoto mi o mamoru koto o mokuteki (___) kangaedasareta mono da.

Full sentence: そのスポーツは、もともと身を守ることを目的として考え出されたものだ。...mokuteki to shite kangaedasareta mono da.
EN: That sport was originally devised with the purpose of self-defense.
HI: वह खेल मूल रूप से आत्मरक्षा को उद्देश्य बनाकर बनाया गया था।
GU: તે રમત મૂળે આત્મરક્ષાને હેતુ બનાવીને બનાવવામાં આવી હતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1とともにto tomo ni(wrong pattern — not a purpose marker)
2につれてni tsurete(wrong pattern — not a purpose marker)
3にとってni totte(means "for/to someone" — wrong sense)
4としてto shiteas, with the purpose of (Week 2 Day 5)

Why: Day 5's 目的として names self-defense as the sport's founding purpose.

Q3

スープ、（　）うちに召し上がってください。Suupu, (___) uchi ni meshiagatte kudasai.

Full sentence: スープ、冷めないうちに召し上がってください。Suupu, samenai uchi ni meshiagatte kudasai.
EN: Please eat the soup before it gets cold.
HI: सूप को ठंडा होने से पहले खा लीजिए।
GU: સૂપ ઠંડું થાય તે પહેલાં ખાઈ લો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1冷めsame(ます-stem alone — wrong shape)
2冷めるsameru(would mean "while it's cold" — wrong sense)
3冷めてsamete(wrong form for うちに)
4冷めないsamenaibefore it cools (Week 2 Day 3)

Why: Day 3's negative Vない+うちに marks the closing window — "while it hasn't yet cooled."

Q4

悩み（　）、結局、彼は真実を話した。Nayami (___), kekkyoku, kare wa shinjitsu o hanashita.

Full sentence: 悩み抜いて、結局、彼は真実を話した。Nayaminuite, kekkyoku, kare wa shinjitsu o hanashita.
EN: After much agonizing, he finally told the truth.
HI: बहुत उलझन के बाद, आख़िरकार उसने सच बता दिया।
GU: ઘણી મૂંઝવણ પછી, છેવટે તેણે સાચું કહી દીધું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かけてkakete(would mean "started worrying" — wrong sense)
2ぬいてnuiteagonized all the way through (Week 2 Day 2)
3えてete(not a valid attachment here)
4かぎってkagitte(wrong pattern)

Why: Day 2's 悩み抜く emphasizes persisting through prolonged worry before finally reaching a decision.

Q5

生徒がやる気を出さない（　）、いくら先生が一生懸命教えても上達しないでしょう。Seito ga yaruki o dasanai (___), ikura sensei ga isshoukenmei oshiete mo joutatsu shinai deshou.

Full sentence: 生徒がやる気を出さないかぎり、いくら先生が一生懸命教えても上達しないでしょう。...dasanai kagiri...
EN: As long as students don't show motivation, they won't improve no matter how hard the teacher works.
HI: जब तक विद्यार्थी उत्साह नहीं दिखाते, टीचर चाहे कितनी भी मेहनत से पढ़ाए, वे बेहतर नहीं हो पाएंगे।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી વિદ્યાર્થીઓ ઉત્સાહ બતાવતા નથી, શિક્ષક ગમે તેટલી મહેનતથી ભણાવે, તેઓ સુધરશે નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かぎりkagirias long as (Week 2 Day 3, ❶)
2ほどhodo(wrong pattern here)
3くらいkurai(wrong pattern here)
4とともにto tomo ni(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 3's conditional 限り sets "not showing motivation" as the continuing condition that blocks improvement.

Q6

勉強していないところ（　）、試験に出る。Benkyou shite inai tokoro (___), shiken ni deru.

Full sentence: 勉強していないところにかぎって、試験に出る。Benkyou shite inai tokoro ni kagitte, shiken ni deru.
EN: It's exactly the part you didn't study that comes out on the exam.
HI: जो हिस्सा पढ़ा ही नहीं होता, वही परीक्षा में आता है।
GU: જે ભાગ ભણ્યા જ ન હોય, એ જ પરીક્ષામાં આવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にとってni totte(wrong pattern here)
2にしたらni shitara(wrong pattern here)
3にかぎってni kagitteof all things, especially this (Week 2 Day 3)
4にともなってni tomonatte(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 3's ironic に限って singles out "of all parts, precisely the one you skipped" — the classic exam-trap flavor of this pattern.

Q7

まだ古いのが残っているから、使い（　）から新しいのを使おう。Mada furui no ga nokotte iru kara, tsukai (___) kara atarashii no o tsukaou.

Full sentence: まだ古いのが残っているから、使い切ってから新しいのを使おう。...tsukaikitte kara...
EN: Since there's still some of the old one left, let's use it up completely before switching to the new one.
HI: पुराना अभी बचा है, तो पूरा इस्तेमाल कर लेने के बाद नया इस्तेमाल करते हैं।
GU: જૂનું હજુ બચ્યું છે, તો સંપૂર્ણ વાપરી લીધા પછી નવું વાપરીએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かけてkakete(would mean "start using" — wrong sense)
2えてete(not a valid attachment here)
3きってkitteuse up completely (Week 2 Day 2)
4ぬいてnuite(wrong verb — 抜く is for perseverance, not "using up"; that's 切る's job)

Why: Day 2's 使い切る (use up completely) is exactly the right verb for finishing the old item before opening a new one — 使い抜く is explicitly marked ダメ in the book.

Q8

夜が明けるか明けないか（　）、彼らは働き始める。Yo ga akeru ka akenai ka (___), karera wa hataraki hajimeru.

Full sentence: 夜が明けるか明けないかのうちに、彼らは働き始める。...no uchi ni...
EN: They start working just around dawn.
HI: भोर होते ही, वे काम पर लग जाते हैं।
GU: પરોઢ થતાંની સાથે જ, તેઓ કામે લાગી જાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1のかぎりno kagiri(wrong pattern here)
2とともにto tomo ni(wrong shape — needs the same-verb affirmative/negative pair first)
3につれてni tsurete(wrong pattern here)
4のうちにno uchi nicompletes Vるか Vないかのうちに (Week 2 Day 3)

Why: Day 3's Vるか Vないかのうちに needs のうちに to complete the "practically simultaneous" pattern — dawn breaking and the workers starting happen almost at once.

Q9

小学生以下のお子さん（　）、プレゼントをさしあげます。Shougakusei ika no okosan (___), purezento o sashiagemasu.

Full sentence: 小学生以下のお子さんに限り、プレゼントをさしあげます。...ni kagiri...
EN: Only for children of elementary school age or younger, we give a present.
HI: सिर्फ़ प्राइमरी स्कूल या उससे छोटी उम्र के बच्चों के लिए, तोहफ़ा दिया जाता है।
GU: ફક્ત પ્રાથમિક શાળા કે તેથી નાની ઉંમરના બાળકો માટે, ભેટ આપવામાં આવે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かぎりkagiri(ダメ — needs に before 限り to restrict a noun)
2にかぎりni kagirionly for, limited to (Week 2 Day 3)
3としてto shite(wrong pattern here)
4にとってni totte(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 3's に限り restricts the offer strictly to children of that age group — の限り (without に) is the ダメ form flagged in the book.

Q10

あなたの健康を考える（　）お酒を飲ませないのです。Anata no kenkou o kangaeru (___) osake o nomasenai no desu.

Full sentence: あなたの健康を考えるからこそ、お酒を飲ませないのです。...kangaeru kara koso...
EN: It's precisely because I'm thinking about your health that I won't let you drink alcohol.
HI: तुम्हारी सेहत की परवाह है इसीलिए तो शराब नहीं पीने देता।
GU: તારા આરોગ્યની ચિંતા છે એટલે જ તો દારૂ પીવા નથી દેતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ほどhodo(wrong pattern here)
2くらいkurai(wrong pattern here)
3からこそkara kosoprecisely because (Week 2 Day 4)
4にかぎってni kagitte(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 4's からこそ names the real reason (concern for health) behind the restriction on alcohol.

Q11

体（　）元気なら、なんとか生活していけるだろう。Karada (___) genki nara, nantoka seikatsu shite ikeru darou.

Full sentence: 体さえ元気なら、なんとか生活していけるだろう。Karada sae genki nara, nantoka seikatsu shite ikeru darou.
EN: As long as my body is healthy, I'll be able to get by somehow.
HI: बस शरीर स्वस्थ रहे, तो किसी तरह गुज़ारा हो ही जाएगा।
GU: બસ શરીર તંદુરસ્ત રહે, તો કોઈ પણ રીતે ગુજારો થઈ જ જશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ほどhodo(wrong pattern here)
2かぎりkagiri(wrong shape — 限り doesn't pair with なら like this)
3さえsaeif only this one thing (Week 2 Day 4)
4としてto shite(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 4's Nさえ〜ば/なら isolates good health as the one sufficient condition for getting by.

Q12

無料体験レッスンは1回（　）です。Muryou taiken ressun wa ikkai (___) desu.

Full sentence: 無料体験レッスンは1回限りです。Muryou taiken ressun wa ikkai kagiri desu.
EN: The free trial lesson is limited to one time only.
HI: मुफ़्त ट्रायल क्लास सिर्फ़ एक बार के लिए ही है।
GU: મફત ટ્રાયલ ક્લાસ ફક્ત એક જ વાર માટે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1かぎりkagirilimited to, only (Week 2 Day 3, N+quantity)
2くらいkurai(wrong pattern here)
3さえsae(wrong pattern here)
4としてto shite(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 3's N〈数量〉+限り (一回限り, "just this one time") directly fits — a quantity noun attaches to 限り without needing に here.

Q13

あんなにいい人が悪いことをするなんて、あり（　）。Anna ni ii hito ga warui koto o suru nante, ari (___).

Full sentence: あんなにいい人が悪いことをするなんて、ありえない。...arienai.
EN: It's impossible that such a good person would do something bad.
HI: इतना अच्छा इंसान कोई बुरा काम करे, यह हो ही नहीं सकता।
GU: આટલો સારો માણસ કંઈ ખરાબ કરે, એ શક્ય જ નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1えるeru(positive — contradicts "impossible")
2うるuru(positive — contradicts "impossible")
3えないenaiimpossible, can't be (Week 2 Day 2)
4うないunai(ダメ — not a valid form)

Why: Day 2's ありえない is the only valid negative form — ありうない is explicitly flagged as wrong in the book.

Q14

これは1週間分の食事のメニューです。このメニュー（　）、料理を作ってください。Kore wa isshuukanbun no shokuji no menyuu desu. Kono menyuu (___), ryouri o tsukutte kudasai.

Full sentence: このメニューにしたがって、料理を作ってください。Kono menyuu ni shitagatte, ryouri o tsukutte kudasai.
EN: This is a week's worth of meal menus. Please cook according to this menu.
HI: यह एक हफ़्ते के खाने का मेनू है। इस मेनू के अनुसार खाना बनाइए।
GU: આ એક અઠવાડિયાના ભોજનનું મેનુ છે. આ મેનુ પ્રમાણે રસોઈ બનાવો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1につれてni tsurete(wrong — a menu is a fixed reference, not a gradually changing quantity)
2にしたがってni shitagattefollowing, in accordance with (Week 2 Day 6)
3にともなってni tomonatte(wrong — no consequence-pairing of two changes here)
4とともにto tomo ni(wrong pattern here)

Why: Day 6's にしたがって is the only one of the four proportional-change expressions that can also mean "following a rule/schedule" — exactly what a fixed menu calls for.

Q15

明日は首相がこの町を訪問します。それに（　）この道路は混雑が予想されます。Ashita wa shushou ga kono machi o houmon shimasu. Sore ni (___) kono douro wa konzatsu ga yosou saremasu.

Full sentence: それにともなって、この道路は混雑が予想されます。Sore ni tomonatte, kono douro wa konzatsu ga yosou saremasu.
EN: Tomorrow the Prime Minister will visit this town. Accompanying that, this road is expected to be congested.
HI: कल प्रधानमंत्री इस शहर का दौरा करेंगे। उसके चलते, इस सड़क पर जाम की आशंका है।
GU: આવતીકાલે વડાપ્રધાન આ શહેરની મુલાકાત લેશે. તેના કારણે, આ રસ્તા પર ટ્રાફિક જામની શક્યતા છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ともなってtomonatteaccompanying that, as a consequence (Week 2 Day 6)
2つれてtsurete(wrong — this needs に first; also not a gradual process)
3したがってshitagatte(possible in some contexts, but the visit isn't a rule/instruction being followed)
4とともにto tomo ni(wrong — needs に, not と, after それ here)

Why: Day 6's にともなって casts the visit as the single triggering event for the traffic consequence — a one-off cause-and-effect pairing, not a gradual process or a rule being followed.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

漢字も聞き取りも読解もまあまあの成績だから、＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿＿ でしょう。Kanji mo kikitori mo dokkai mo maamaa no seiseki da kara, ___ ★ ___ deshou.

Tiles: 1 やれば (yareba)　2 文法 (bunpou)　3 いい (ii)　4 さえ (sae)

2 文法→4 さえ→★ 1 やれば→3 いい

漢字も聞き取りも読解もまあまあの成績だから、文法さえやればいいでしょう。Kanji mo kikitori mo dokkai mo maamaa no seiseki da kara, bunpou sae yareba ii deshou.

EN: Since your kanji, listening, and reading scores are decent, you probably just need to work on grammar.
HI: कांजी, सुनना और पढ़ना सब ठीक-ठाक हैं, तो शायद बस व्याकरण पर काम करना बाकी है।
GU: કાંજી, સાંભળવું અને વાંચવું બધું બરાબર છે, તો કદાચ ફક્ત વ્યાકરણ પર કામ કરવાનું બાકી છે.

Why: Built on Week 2 Day 4's さえ〜ば — 文法さえやればいい (if only you do grammar) isolates grammar as the one remaining sufficient condition for overall improvement.

Q17

ホテルや旅館の料金は、休日のほうが高い。このシステムは＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿＿。Hoteru ya ryokan no ryoukin wa, kyuujitsu no hou ga takai. Kono shisutemu wa ___ ★ ___.

Tiles: 1 旅行できない (ryokou dekinai)　2 人にしたら (hito ni shitara)　3 休日しか (kyuujitsu shika)　4 つらい (tsurai)

3 休日しか→1 旅行できない→★ 2 人にしたら→4 つらい

このシステムは休日しか旅行できない人にしたら、つらい。Kono shisutemu wa kyuujitsu shika ryokou dekinai hito ni shitara, tsurai.

EN: Hotel and inn rates are higher on holidays. From the standpoint of people who can only travel on holidays, this system is rough.
HI: होटल और रयोकान के दाम छुट्टियों में ज़्यादा होते हैं। जो लोग सिर्फ़ छुट्टियों में ही यात्रा कर सकते हैं, उनके नज़रिए से यह व्यवस्था कठिन है।
GU: હોટેલ અને ર્યોકાનના ભાવ રજાઓમાં વધુ હોય છે. જે લોકો ફક્ત રજાઓમાં જ પ્રવાસ કરી શકે છે, તેમના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી આ વ્યવસ્થા મુશ્કેલ છે.

Why: Built on Week 2 Day 5's にしたら (standpoint marker) — 休日しか旅行できない人にしたら frames the sentence from the perspective of people restricted to holiday travel.

Q18

パーティーの準備はすべて終わって、＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿＿ だ。Paatii no junbi wa subete owatte, ___ ★ ___ da.

Tiles: 1 ばかり (bakari)　2 あとは (ato wa)　3 待つ (matsu)　4 お客様を (okyakusama o)

2 あとは→4 お客様を→★ 3 待つ→1 ばかり

パーティーの準備はすべて終わって、あとはお客様を待つばかりだ。Paatii no junbi wa subete owatte, ato wa okyakusama o matsu bakari da.

EN: All the preparations for the party are done; now all that's left is to wait for the guests.
HI: पार्टी की सारी तैयारी हो चुकी है, अब बस मेहमानों का इंतज़ार करना बाकी है।
GU: પાર્ટીની બધી તૈયારી થઈ ગઈ છે, હવે બસ મહેમાનોની રાહ જોવાનું બાકી છે.

Why: Built on Week 2 Day 4's ❷ "nothing left but to ~" sense of ばかりだ — 待つばかりだ names waiting as the one remaining action.

Q19

ここ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿＿ 就職率が大幅に低下した。Koko ___ ★ ___ shuushokuritsu ga oohaba ni teika shita.

Tiles: 1 景気の (keiki no)　2 数年の (suunen no)　3 にともない (ni tomonai)　4 悪化 (akka)

2 数年の→1 景気の→★ 4 悪化→3 にともない
