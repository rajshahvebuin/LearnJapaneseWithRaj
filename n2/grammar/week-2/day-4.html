4日目 — 君さえいれば (Kimi sae ireba)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.36–37 (PDF pages 36–37).
Grammar points covered
これさえあればかわいいからこそ信頼してこそ上がるばかりだ
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.39 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Point 3 (Vてこそ) reuses the exact grammar point already covered in N1 Week 1 Day 1 — the N2 book introduces it here at a lighter level. Point 4 has two distinct senses sharing the same ばかりだ: an ongoing one-way trend (❶) and "nothing left but to ~" (❷, with the more formal のみだ as its written variant).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 これさえあれば

neutral register
Readingkore sae areba
Meaning (EN)"if only ~ / as long as just this one thing (is present/done)" — isolates a minimum sufficient condition
Meaning (HI)बस अगर यह हो तो / सिर्फ़ इतना ही काफ़ी है
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત આટલું જ હોય તો / બસ આટલું જ પૂરતું છે
Connection (接続)Nさえ ＋ Vば／Aければ／naなら／Nなら ／ V-ます-stem ＋ さえすれば
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSingles out one element as the sole necessary/sufficient condition, implying everything else is secondary.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNさえ ＋ Vばこれさえあればkore sae areba
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ さえすれば飲みさえすればnomi sae sureba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
携帯電話を買った。これさえあれば、時計もカメラもいらない。Keitai denwa o katta. Kore sae areba, tokei mo kamera mo iranai. I bought a cell phone. Now that I have this, I don't need a watch or a camera. मैंने मोबाइल फ़ोन ख़रीदा। बस यह हो तो घड़ी और कैमरे की ज़रूरत नहीं। મેં મોબાઈલ ફોન ખરીદ્યો. બસ આ હોય તો ઘડિયાળ અને કેમેરાની જરૂર નથી.
この薬を飲みさえすれば、すぐに治ります。Kono kusuri o nomi sae sureba, sugu ni naorimasu. If you take this medicine, it alone will help you to recover immediately. बस यह दवा ले लो, तुरंत ठीक हो जाओगे। બસ આ દવા લઈ લો, તરત સાજા થઈ જશો.

さえ isolates the single necessary condition ("if only X") — stronger than plain ば/なら alone, implying everything else doesn't matter as long as that one condition is met.

【2】 かわいいからこそ

neutral register
Readingkawaii kara koso
Meaning (EN)"precisely because ~ / it's exactly for the reason that ~" — emphasizes the one true reason, ruling out other possible reasons
Meaning (HI)ठीक इसी वजह से / यही वजह है कि...
Meaning (GU)બરાબર આ જ કારણથી / એ જ કારણ છે કે...
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj／N（普通形）＋ からこそ ／ Vば／Aければ／naであれば／Nであれば ＋ こそ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useStrongly emphasizes the real reason behind an action.
Word typeFormationExample
Plain formV／A／な-adj／N（普）＋ からこそ努力したからこそdoryoku shita kara koso
ConditionalVば／Aければ／naであれば／Nであれば ＋ こそ思えばこそomoeba koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子どもがかわいいからこそ、しかるんです。Kodomo ga kawaii kara koso, shikarun desu. I scold my children because I care for them. बच्चे प्यारे हैं इसीलिए तो डांटती हूँ। બાળકો વ્હાલા છે એટલે જ તો ઠપકો આપું છું.
努力したからこそ、成功したんです。Doryoku shita kara koso, seikou shitan desu. I succeeded by working hard. मेहनत की थी इसीलिए सफलता मिली। મહેનત કરી હતી એટલે જ સફળતા મળી.
厳しく注意したのは、あなたのことを思えばこそです。Kibishiku chuui shita no wa, anata no koto o omoeba koso desu. I warned you because I care for you. सख़्ती से चेतावनी इसलिए दी क्योंकि मुझे तुम्हारी परवाह है। કડક ચેતવણી એટલા માટે આપી કારણ કે મને તારી ચિંતા છે.

からこそ emphasizes "THIS is the real reason, not some other one" — stronger and more pointed than plain から. The Vば/Aければ+こそ variant (without から) does the same job with conditional forms.

【3】 信頼してこそ

neutral register
Readingshinrai shite koso
Meaning (EN)"only by/after doing ~ (does the following become true)" — the same meaning as 〜てはじめて
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ ...करने पर ही (तभी संभव है)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...કરવાથી જ (ત્યારે જ શક્ય છે)
Connection (接続)Vて ＋ こそ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes a necessary precondition — the exact same pattern already introduced in the N1 book, reused here as a review checkpoint at N2 level.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVて ＋ こそ信頼してこそshinrai shite koso
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
お互いに信頼してこそ、悩みも言える。Otagai ni shinrai shite koso, nayami mo ieru. We talk about each other's problems because we trust each other. एक-दूसरे पर भरोसा है इसीलिए तो अपनी परेशानियां बता पाते हैं। એકબીજા પર વિશ્વાસ છે એટલે જ તો પોતાની મુશ્કેલીઓ કહી શકીએ છીએ.
親になってこそ、親の苦労がわかる。Oya ni natte koso, oya no kurou ga wakaru. Now that I am a parent, I realise the difficulties of being one. माता-पिता बनने के बाद ही माता-पिता की मुश्किलें समझ आती हैं। માતાપિતા બન્યા પછી જ માતાપિતાની મુશ્કેલીઓ સમજાય છે.

This is the same 〜てこそ pattern already covered in the N1 book — the N2 book reuses it as an earlier/lighter-level checkpoint. Meaning identical to 〜てはじめて ("only after ~, for the first time").

【4】 上がるばかりだ

neutral register
Readingagaru bakari da
Meaning (EN)❶ "only keeps ~ing / just keeps getting more ~" (one-directional continuous change, usually negative); ❷ "nothing but ~ / all that's left is to ~"
Meaning (HI)❶ लगातार सिर्फ़ ...होता जा रहा है; ❷ सिर्फ़ ...करना ही बाकी है
Meaning (GU)❶ સતત ફક્ત ...થતું જાય છે; ❷ ફક્ત ...કરવાનું જ બાકી છે
Connection (接続)❶ Vる ＋ ばかりだ  |  ❷ （ただ）Vる ＋ ばかりだ／のみだ（硬）
Register❶ neutral; ❷ のみだ leans 硬 formal
Typical use❶ describes a trend that only moves in one direction, usually worsening (prices, situations); ❷ describes that nothing remains to be done except one final thing.
Word typeFormationExample
❶ Verb (trend)Vる ＋ ばかりだ上がるばかりだagaru bakari da
❷ Verb (nothing left)（ただ）Vる ＋ ばかりだ／のみだ祈るばかりだinoru bakari da ／ 忍耐あるのみだnintai aru nomi da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
物価は上がるばかりだ。Bukka wa agaru bakari da. The cost of living just keeps going up. महंगाई लगातार बढ़ती ही जा रही है। મોંઘવારી સતત વધતી જ જાય છે.
状況は悪化するばかりだ。Joukyou wa akka suru bakari da. The situation is just getting worse. हालात बस बिगड़ते ही जा रहे हैं। પરિસ્થિતિ ફક્ત બગડતી જ જાય છે.
事故がないようにとただ祈るばかりだ。Jiko ga nai you ni to tada inoru bakari da. I just pray that there will be no accidents. बस यही दुआ करता हूं कि कोई दुर्घटना न हो। બસ એ જ પ્રાર્થના કરું છું કે કોઈ અકસ્માત ન થાય.
解決策はない。ただ忍耐あるのみだ。Kaiketsusaku wa nai. Tada nintai aru nomi da. There is no solution. I just have to bear it. कोई समाधान नहीं है। बस सहना ही एकमात्र रास्ता है। કોઈ ઉકેલ નથી. બસ સહન કરવું જ એકમાત્ર રસ્તો છે.

❶ describes an unstoppable one-way trend (almost always negative — rising prices, worsening situations). ❷ (ただ〜ばかりだ／のみだ) means "there's nothing left to do but ~" — のみだ is the more formal/written variant of ばかりだ in this specific "nothing but" sense.

練習Ⅰ — circle the correct choice

Q1

出席（a. する　b. して）こそ、授業の意味があるのです。Shusseki (a. suru b. shite) koso, jugyou no imi ga aru no desu.

Full sentence: 出席してこそ、授業の意味があるのです。Shusseki shite koso, jugyou no imi ga aru no desu.
EN: Only by attending does the class have meaning.
HI: कक्षा में उपस्थित होकर ही, क्लास का कोई मतलब बनता है।
GU: વર્ગમાં હાજર રહીને જ, ક્લાસનો કોઈ અર્થ બને છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aするsuru(plain form — wrong attachment for こそ here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bしてshitete-form + こそ, "only by attending" (Point 3)उपस्थित होकर हीહાજર રહીને જ

Why: Point 3's てこそ always attaches to the て-form of the verb, not the plain dictionary form.

Q2

子どもの将来を思えば（a. こそ　b. さえ）一流の大学に入れたいのです。Kodomo no shourai o omoeba (a. koso b. sae) ichiryuu no daigaku ni iretai no desu.

Full sentence: 子どもの将来を思えばこそ、一流の大学に入れたいのです。Kodomo no shourai o omoeba koso, ichiryuu no daigaku ni iretai no desu.
EN: It's precisely because I think about my child's future that I want them to get into a top university.
HI: बच्चे के भविष्य की चिंता है इसीलिए तो उसे एक अच्छे विश्वविद्यालय में भेजना चाहता हूं।
GU: બાળકના ભવિષ્યની ચિંતા છે એટલે જ તો તેને સારી યુનિવર્સિટીમાં મોકલવા માંગું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこそkosoprecisely because (Point 2's Vば+こそ)इसीलिए तोએટલે જ તો
bさえsae(would isolate a minimal condition — wrong sense for stating "the real reason")(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)

Why: The sentence states the true underlying reason for wanting the best for the child, which is exactly Point 2's job (Vば+こそ); さえ (Point 1) would instead isolate a minimum sufficient condition, which doesn't fit this "real reason" context.

Q3

根本的な問題を解決しなければ、財政は苦しく（a. なる　b. なった）ばかりだ。Konponteki na mondai o kaiketsu shinakereba, zaisei wa kurushiku (a. naru b. natta) bakari da.

Full sentence: 根本的な問題を解決しなければ、財政は苦しくなるばかりだ。Konponteki na mondai o kaiketsu shinakereba, zaisei wa kurushiku naru bakari da.
EN: If we don't solve the fundamental problem, our finances will just keep getting tighter.
HI: अगर मूल समस्या हल नहीं की गई, तो वित्तीय हालत बस बिगड़ती ही जाएगी।
GU: જો મૂળભૂત સમસ્યાનો ઉકેલ ન લાવવામાં આવે, તો નાણાકીય સ્થિતિ ફક્ત બગડતી જ જશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aなるnaruplain form + ばかりだ, an ongoing trend (Point 4, ❶)होता ही जाएगाથતું જ જશે
bなったnatta(past tense — wrong attachment for ❶'s ongoing-trend sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 4's ❶ ongoing-trend ばかりだ takes the plain non-past form (なる), describing a trend still in progress — not a completed past change.

Q4

この仕事が好きだ（a. からこそ　b. こそから）つらくても続けられるのだと思う。Kono shigoto ga suki da (a. kara koso b. koso kara) tsurakute mo tsuzukerareru no da to omou.

Full sentence: この仕事が好きだからこそ、つらくても続けられるのだと思う。Kono shigoto ga suki da kara koso, tsurakute mo tsuzukerareru no da to omou.
EN: It's precisely because I love this job that I can keep going even when it's hard.
HI: यह काम पसंद है इसीलिए तो मुश्किल होने पर भी जारी रख पाता हूं।
GU: આ કામ ગમે છે એટલે જ તો મુશ્કેલ હોવા છતાં ચાલુ રાખી શકું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aからこそkara kosoprecisely because (Point 2)इसीलिए तोએટલે જ તો
bこそからkoso kara(wrong word order — こそ must follow から, not precede it)(यहाँ ग़लत क्रम)(અહીં ખોટો ક્રમ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

お互いに率直に＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ し合えるのです。Otagai ni socchoku ni ___ shiaeru no desu.

Tiles: 1 理解 (rikai)　2 合って (atte)　3 話し (hanashi)　4 こそ (koso)

3 話し→2 合って→4 こそ→1 理解

お互いに率直に話し合ってこそ理解し合えるのです。Otagai ni socchoku ni hanashiatte koso rikai shiaeru no desu.

EN: Only by talking to each other honestly can we truly understand one another.
HI: एक-दूसरे से खुलकर बात करके ही हम एक-दूसरे को सही मायनों में समझ पाते हैं।
GU: એકબીજા સાથે ખુલ્લેઆમ વાત કરીને જ આપણે એકબીજાને ખરેખર સમજી શકીએ છીએ.

Why: Built on Point 3's てこそ — 話し合ってこそ (only by talking things through) is the necessary precondition for 理解し合える (being able to understand each other).

Q7

この仕事は＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ だれにでもできます。Kono shigoto wa ___ dare ni de mo dekimasu.

Tiles: 1 あり (ari)　2 やる気が (yaruki ga)　3 すれば (sureba)　4 さえ (sae)

2 やる気が→1 あり→4 さえ→3 すれば

この仕事はやる気がありさえすれば、だれにでもできます。Kono shigoto wa yaruki ga ari sae sureba, dare ni de mo dekimasu.

EN: As long as you have motivation, anyone can do this job.
HI: बस उत्साह हो तो, यह काम कोई भी कर सकता है।
GU: બસ ઉત્સાહ હોય તો, આ કામ કોઈ પણ કરી શકે છે.

Why: Built on Point 1's さえすれば — やる気があり (having motivation, ます-stem of ある) + さえすれば isolates motivation as the one sufficient condition for anyone to do the job.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

さえ and こそ both add emphasis, but to very different things — and ばかりだ has two faces of its own. All four are worth lining up together:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nさえ〜ばN sae ~ba If only this one thing Nさえ ＋ Vば／Aければ／naなら／Nなら Isolates the minimum sufficient condition — everything else is secondary. Saying "as long as just X, it's fine."
からこそ／ばこそkara koso / ba koso Precisely because ~ plain form ＋ からこそ ／ 条件形 ＋ こそ Emphasizes the true reason, excluding other possible reasons. Pointing to the real cause behind an action.
てこそte koso Only after/by doing ~ Vて ＋ こそ A necessary precondition (＝てはじめて), not about reasons or conditions. Saying something only becomes true once an action happens first.
ばかりだbakari da ❶ Only keeps ~ing; ❷ nothing left but to ~ Vる ＋ ばかりだ ／ （ただ）Vる ＋ ばかりだ／のみだ ❶ describes an ongoing one-way trend; ❷ describes the last remaining action. Describing a worsening trend, or that only one thing is left to do.
Exam trap: Don't confuse さえ (isolating a sufficient condition, "if only X") with こそ (emphasizing the true reason among possible reasons) — Q2 tests exactly this: 子どもの将来を思えばこそ is about the real reason for wanting the best for one's child, not about a minimal condition, so こそ is correct and さえ is wrong.
