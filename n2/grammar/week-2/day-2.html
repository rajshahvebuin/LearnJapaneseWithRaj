2日目 — ありえない (Arienai)

Source

Grammar points covered
読みかける読み切るありえる／ありうるやり抜く
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.35 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points are suffixes attached to a verb's ます-stem, each describing a different way an action can play out: incomplete/on the verge (かける), totally finished or exhausted (切る), persisting through hardship (抜く), or simply possible/impossible (える／うる). Watch the ダメ forms flagged in the book: ありうない／ありうます and 使い抜く are all incorrect.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 読みかける

neutral register
ReadingV-masu-stem + kakeru
Meaning (EN)"start to do ~ but leave it unfinished / be partway through doing ~ / be on the verge of ~"
Meaning (HI)...करना शुरू किया पर अधूरा छोड़ दिया / ...होने ही वाला था
Meaning (GU)...કરવાનું શરૂ કર્યું પણ અધૂરું છોડ્યું / ...થવાની જ તૈયારીમાં હતું
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ＋ かける ／ かけの N ／ かけだ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribes an action begun but not completed (読みかけの本 = a half-read book), or an event on the very verge of happening (死にかける = to nearly die).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ かける食べかけるtabekakeru ／ やりかけるyarikakeru ／ 忘れかけるwasurekakeru ／ 枯れかけるkarekakeru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
読みかけの本が何冊もある。Yomikake no hon ga nansatsu mo aru. There are still many books I haven't finished reading. मेरे पास ऐसी कई किताबें हैं जो मैंने पढ़नी शुरू कीं पर पूरी नहीं कीं। મારી પાસે એવા ઘણાં પુસ્તકો છે જે મેં વાંચવાનું શરૂ કર્યું પણ પૂરું ન કર્યું.
彼女は何か言いかけてやめた。Kanojo wa nanika iikakete yameta. She began to say something and stopped. वह कुछ कहने ही वाली थी कि रुक गई। તે કંઈક કહેવાની જ હતી કે અટકી ગઈ.
私は、子どものとき、病気で死にかけたことがある。Watashi wa, kodomo no toki, byouki de shinikaketa koto ga aru. I almost died from an illness when I was a child. बचपन में, मैं बीमारी की वजह से मरते-मरते बचा था। બાળપણમાં, હું બીમારીના કારણે મરતાં મરતાં બચ્યો હતો.

かける can mean either "left incomplete" (読みかけ = half-read) or "right on the verge of" (死にかける = nearly die) — the specific verb decides which of the two nuances applies. Both senses share the same core idea: the action stopped short of completion.

【2】 読み切る

neutral register
ReadingV-masu-stem + kiru / kireru
Meaning (EN)"finish completely / do all the way through" (切る); "〜切れない" = "can't manage to finish / can't do completely"
Meaning (HI)पूरी तरह ख़त्म करना / ...पूरा न कर पाना (きれない)
Meaning (GU)સંપૂર્ણપણે પૂરું કરવું / ...પૂરું ન કરી શકવું (きれない)
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ＋ 切る／切れる ／ 切れない（＝完了しない）
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes total completion of an action, or "using everything up, nothing remaining" (全部使って、残っていない様子); also used idiomatically for an extreme state, e.g. 疲れ切った (utterly exhausted).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ 切る／切れる使い切るtsukaikiru ／ 疲れ切るtsukarekiru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この小説はおもしろくて、1日で読み切った。Kono shousetsu wa omoshirokute, ichinichi de yomikitta. This novel was so interesting that I finished reading it in a day. यह उपन्यास इतना दिलचस्प था कि मैंने इसे एक दिन में पूरा पढ़ लिया। આ નવલકથા એટલી રસપ્રદ હતી કે મેં તેને એક દિવસમાં પૂરી વાંચી લીધી.
彼は疲れ切った顔をしている。Kare wa tsukarekitta kao o shite iru. He looks extremely tired. उसके चेहरे से साफ़ झलकता है कि वह बुरी तरह थका हुआ है। તેના ચહેરા પરથી સ્પષ્ટ દેખાય છે કે તે ખૂબ થાકેલો છે.

切る emphasizes "all the way, completely, nothing left over" — either finishing a task fully or reaching an extreme state. The negative 切れない means "couldn't manage to finish" (used up more than the amount available, or too much of a task remained).

【3】 ありえる／ありうる 《～得る》

neutral register
Readingarieru / ariuru
Meaning (EN)"can happen / is possible"; negative ありえない = "impossible, can't be, no way"
Meaning (HI)हो सकता है / संभव है; ありえない = असंभव है, हो ही नहीं सकता
Meaning (GU)થઈ શકે છે / શક્ય છે; ありえない = અશક્ય છે
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ＋ える／うる ／ えない（＝あるはずがない）
RegisterNeutral — ありえない is extremely common in casual speech ("no way!")
Typical useExpressing possibility or impossibility. ある's potential form is irregular: written あり得る, read either arieru or ariuru — both are correct. ダメ: ありうない and ありうます are both ungrammatical.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ える／うる知りうるshiriuru ／ 起こりうるokoriuru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
博士でも間違うことはありえる。Hakase demo machigau koto wa arieru. It is even possible for doctors to make mistakes. विशेषज्ञ भी ग़लती कर सकते हैं। નિષ્ણાતો પણ ભૂલ કરી શકે છે.
これが私の知りうるすべての情報です。Kore ga watashi no shiriuru subete no jouhou desu. This is all the information that I could get. यही वह सारी जानकारी है जो मुझे मिल सकती थी। આ જ બધી માહિતી છે જે મને મળી શકતી હતી.

える／うる are both legitimate readings of the potential-form kanji 得る attached to ある/知る/起こる and similar verbs — pick whichever reads more naturally. Remember the ダメ forms: ありうない and ありうます are simply wrong; only ありえない (negative) and ありえる／ありうる (positive) exist.

【4】 やり抜く

neutral register
Readingyarinuku
Meaning (EN)"carry through to the end / see something through despite difficulty"
Meaning (HI)अंत तक निभाना / मुश्किलों के बावजूद पूरा करना
Meaning (GU)અંત સુધી નિભાવવું / મુશ્કેલીઓ છતાં પૂરું કરવું
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ＋ 抜く
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes perseverance — continuing an action all the way to the end despite hardship (最後までその状態を続けること). ダメ: 使い抜く is wrong; use 使い切る instead (抜く is for perseverance through difficulty, not "using something up").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ 抜く悩み抜くnayaminuku ／ 考え抜くkangaenuku ／ 耐え抜くtaenuku ／ 守り抜くmamorinuku ／ 戦い抜くtatakainuku ／ 生き抜くikinuku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大変な仕事でも、やり抜く自信があります。Taihenna shigoto demo, yarinuku jishin ga arimasu. I have confidence to finish even difficult jobs. मुश्किल काम भी अंत तक निभाने का मुझे भरोसा है। મુશ્કેલ કામ પણ અંત સુધી નિભાવવાનો મને વિશ્વાસ છે.
彼はあきらめずにゴールまで走り抜いた。Kare wa akiramezu ni gooru made hashirinuita. He ran all the way to the goal line without giving up. उसने बिना हार माने अंत तक दौड़ लगाई। તેણે હાર માન્યા વગર અંત સુધી દોડ લગાવી.

抜く highlights persistence through difficulty — the mirror image of 切る's "completely used up, nothing remaining" nuance. Don't confuse 使い抜く (wrong) with 使い切る (correct, "use up completely").

Q1

Full sentence: やりかけたことは、最後までやりぬこう。Yarikaketa koto wa, saigo made yarinukou.
EN: Let's carry through to the end anything we've started.

GU: જે પણ કામ શરૂ કર્યું છે, તેને અંત સુધી નિભાવીએ.
BlankOptionJapaneseEnglish

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

使い＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ 夢を見た。Tsukai ___ yume o mita.

Tiles: 1 ほどの (hodo no)　2 切れない (kirenai)　3 もらった (moratta)　4 お金を (okane o)

2 切れない→1 ほどの→4 お金を→3 もらった

使い切れないほどのお金をもらった夢を見た。Tsukaikirenai hodo no okane o moratta yume o mita.

EN: I dreamed that I received more money than I could ever spend.
HI: मैंने सपने में देखा कि मुझे इतना पैसा मिला जिसे मैं कभी ख़र्च ही न कर पाऊं।
GU: મેં સપનામાં જોયું કે મને એટલા પૈસા મળ્યા જે હું ક્યારેય ખર્ચી જ ન શકું.

Why: Built on Point 2's negative 使い切れない (can't manage to use it all up) — describing an amount of money so large it can't possibly be spent.

Q7

考え＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ 試してみたが、だめだった。Kangae ___ tameshite mita ga, dame datta.

Tiles: 1 すべての (subete no)　2 を (o)　3 方法 (houhou)　4 うる (uru)

4 うる→1 すべての→3 方法→2 を

考えうるすべての方法を試してみたが、だめだった。Kangaeuru subete no houhou o tameshite mita ga, dame datta.

EN: I tried every method I could think of, but it didn't work.
HI: मैंने जितने भी तरीक़े सोच सकता था, सब आज़मा लिए, पर काम नहीं बना।
GU: મેં જેટલી પણ પદ્ધતિઓ વિચારી શકતો હતો, બધી અજમાવી, પણ કામ ન થયું.

Why: Built on Point 3's 考えうる (every method one could possibly think of) modifying すべての方法 — "all the methods that could possibly be thought of."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points attach to a verb's ます-stem, but each carries a different sense of "how the action plays out" — a natural set for the exam to test against each other:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
V-stem+かける~kakeru Left incomplete / on the verge of V-stem ＋ かける Marks an action stopped short of completion, or right before it happens. Describing something half-done or about to occur.
V-stem+切る~kiru Finish completely / to an extreme V-stem ＋ 切る／切れる Marks total completion, nothing remaining — or an extreme state (exhaustion). Describing something fully finished or a maxed-out state.
V-stem+抜く~nuku Carry through to the end V-stem ＋ 抜く Emphasizes perseverance through difficulty over time, not just finishing. Praising persistence in the face of hardship.
V-stem+える／うる~eru/~uru Possible / can happen V-stem ＋ える／うる（否定形：えない） Pure possibility/impossibility — no notion of completion or effort at all. Judging whether something could happen.
Exam trap: Two ダメ forms are explicitly flagged in the book and worth memorizing outright: ①ありうない is wrong (only ありえない exists as the negative); ②使い抜く is wrong (使い切る is correct — 抜く is for perseverance through hardship, not for "using something up completely"). Also remember that positive える／うる are freely interchangeable (Q4), but the negative only exists as えない, never うない.
