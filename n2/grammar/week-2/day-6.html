6日目 — 高くなるにしたがって (Takaku naru ni shitagatte)

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.44 (7日目's third page) — note: this lands one page later than the usual pattern (Day 7's second page would be p.43; the actual answer box appears on p.44 instead), confirmed directly from the scan, not guessed.
Notes
All four points describe "as X changes, Y changes too," but only にしたがって can also mean "in accordance with a rule/schedule/instruction" — a sense the other three lack entirely (予定表につれて is explicitly marked ダメ in the book).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 家族とともに 《～共に》

neutral register
Readingkazoku to tomo ni
Meaning (EN)"together with N / along with ~ing" — literal accompaniment, or "simultaneously with"
Meaning (HI)N के साथ / ...करने के साथ-साथ
Meaning (GU)N ની સાથે / ...કરવાની સાથે
Connection (接続)N ＋ と ＋ ともに ／ Vる ＋ と ＋ ともに
RegisterNeutral, slightly written/formal (共に)
Typical useExpresses accompaniment (with family) or simultaneity (as X happens, Y happens too).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ とともに家族とともにkazoku to tomo ni
VerbVる ＋ とともに普及するとともにfukyuu suru to tomo ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
家族とともに来日した。Kazoku to tomo ni rainichi shita. I came to Japan with my family. मैं अपने परिवार के साथ जापान आया। હું મારા પરિવાર સાથે જાપાન આવ્યો.
自動車が普及するとともに、事故も増加した。Jidousha ga fukyuu suru to tomo ni, jiko mo zouka shita. With the increase in the number of cars, accidents also increased. कारों के बढ़ने के साथ-साथ, दुर्घटनाएं भी बढ़ीं। કારોના વધારા સાથે, અકસ્માતો પણ વધ્યા.

とともに has two senses: literal "together with" (with a person/group noun) and "as X happens, Y happens simultaneously" (with Vる) — context distinguishes them.

【2】 増加にともなって 《～に伴って》

neutral register
Readingzouka ni tomonatte
Meaning (EN)"accompanying/along with the increase in ~ / as a consequence of ~" — one change is accompanied by another
Meaning (HI)...की वृद्धि के साथ / ...के परिणामस्वरूप
Meaning (GU)...ના વધારા સાથે / ...ના પરિણામે
Connection (接続)Nする ＋ に ＋ ともない ／ Vるの／Vたの ＋ に ＋ ともなって ／ 〜にともなうN
RegisterNeutral, leans slightly formal (written reports, news)
Typical useDescribes one change accompanying/triggered by another — common in formal/news register.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する)Nする ＋ にともない増加にともないzouka ni tomonai
Verb + のVるの／Vたの ＋ にともなって行われるのにともなってokonawareru no ni tomonatte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
人口の増加にともない、資源の消費量も増える。Jinkou no zouka ni tomonai, shigen no shouhiryou mo fueru. As the population increases, the consumption of resources also increases. जनसंख्या बढ़ने के साथ-साथ, संसाधनों की खपत भी बढ़ती है। વસ્તી વધારા સાથે, સંસાધનોનો વપરાશ પણ વધે છે.
マラソン大会が行われるのにともなって、この道路は通行止めになります。Marason taikai ga okonawareru no ni tomonatte, kono douro wa tsuukoudome ni narimasu. Because of the upcoming marathon, this road will be closed. मैराथन आयोजित होने के चलते, यह सड़क बंद रहेगी। મેરેથોન યોજાવાના કારણે, આ રસ્તો બંધ રહેશે.

にともなって is more formal/written than とともに, and always implies a cause-consequence coupling of two changes, not mere simultaneity or literal togetherness.

【3】 年を取るにつれて

neutral register
Readingtoshi o toru ni tsurete
Meaning (EN)"as ~ progresses, gradually ~" — a gradual, proportional change
Meaning (HI)जैसे-जैसे ...होता जाता है, वैसे-वैसे धीरे-धीरे...
Meaning (GU)જેમ જેમ ...થતું જાય છે, તેમ તેમ ધીરે ધીરે...
Connection (接続)Nする ＋ に ＋ つれて ／ Vる ＋ に ＋ つれ
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes a gradual, proportional co-development of two things over time.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる ＋ につれて年を取るにつれてtoshi o toru ni tsurete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
年を取るにつれて、体のいろいろな機能が低下する。Toshi o toru ni tsurete, karada no iroiro na kinou ga teika suru. As you get older, the various functions of your body deteriorate. जैसे-जैसे उम्र बढ़ती है, शरीर के कई कार्य कमज़ोर होते जाते हैं। જેમ જેમ ઉંમર વધે છે, શરીરના વિવિધ કાર્યો નબળા થતા જાય છે.
町の発展につれて、自然が少なくなった。Machi no hatten ni tsurete, shizen ga sukunaku natta. As the town developed, natural areas decreased. शहर के विकास के साथ-साथ, प्रकृति कम होती गई। શહેરના વિકાસ સાથે, પ્રકૃતિ ઓછી થતી ગઈ.

につれて emphasizes gradual, proportional change over an extended process (aging, urban development) — similar to にともなって but with a stronger sense of gradualness/progression rather than a single triggered consequence.

【4】 北へ行くにしたがって 《～に従って》

neutral register
Readingkita e iku ni shitagatte
Meaning (EN)"as ~ progresses, correspondingly ~ / in accordance with ~" — overlaps につれて, and additionally covers "following/in accordance with a rule or instruction"
Meaning (HI)जैसे-जैसे ...वैसे-वैसे / ...के अनुसार (निर्देश/नियम)
Meaning (GU)જેમ જેમ ...તેમ તેમ / ...ના અનુસાર (સૂચના/નિયમ)
Connection (接続)N ＋ に ＋ したがって ／ Vる ＋ に ＋ したがい
RegisterNeutral. ダメ: 予定表につれて (につれて cannot replace にしたがって when following a rule/schedule/instruction)
Typical useTwo overlapping senses — (a) proportional change like につれて; (b) "in accordance with/following" a rule, schedule, or instruction (予定表にしたがい, 指示にしたがって) — only sense (b) cannot be replaced by につれて.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる ＋ にしたがって北へ行くにしたがってkita e iku ni shitagatte
Noun (rule/instruction)N ＋ にしたがい予定表にしたがいyoteihyou ni shitagai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
北へ行くにしたがって、紅葉は早くなる。Kita e iku ni shitagatte, kouyou wa hayaku naru. As you go north, you will get autumn colors earlier. जैसे-जैसे उत्तर की ओर जाते हैं, पत्ते जल्दी रंग बदलने लगते हैं। જેમ જેમ ઉત્તર તરફ જઈએ છીએ, પાંદડા વહેલા રંગ બદલવા લાગે છે.
予定表にしたがい、学習を進める。Yoteihyou ni shitagai, gakushuu o susumeru. I undertake my studies according to the schedule. समय-सारणी के अनुसार पढ़ाई करता हूं। સમયપત્રક પ્રમાણે અભ્યાસ કરું છું.

にしたがって covers both "proportional change" (overlapping with につれて) AND "following a rule/instruction" (a sense につれて does NOT have — 予定表につれて is marked wrong in the book). When a schedule, rule, or instruction is involved, にしたがって is the only correct choice among this group.

Q1

温度が上がる（a. とともに　b. につれて）、湿度も上がった。Ondo ga agaru (a. to tomo ni b. ni tsurete), shitsudo mo agatta.

Full sentence: 温度が上がるとともに／につれて、湿度も上がった。Ondo ga agaru to tomo ni / ni tsurete, shitsudo mo agatta.
EN: As the temperature rises, the humidity also rises.
HI: जैसे-जैसे तापमान बढ़ता है, नमी भी बढ़ती है।
GU: જેમ જેમ તાપમાન વધે છે, ભેજ પણ વધે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとともにto tomo nisimultaneously with (Point 1)के साथ-साथની સાથે સાથે
bにつれてni tsuretegradually as (Point 3)जैसे-जैसे धीरे-धीरेજેમ જેમ ધીરે ધીરે

Why: A simultaneous, proportional rise in temperature and humidity fits both とともに (simultaneity) and につれて (gradual/proportional) equally well — both are accepted.

Q2

引っ越し（a. につれて　b. にともない）、電話番号も変わりました。Hikkoshi (a. ni tsurete b. ni tomonai), denwa bangou mo kawarimashita.

〈ヒント〉 引っ越しのために (Hint: because of moving)

Full sentence: 引っ越しにともない、電話番号も変わりました。Hikkoshi ni tomonai, denwa bangou mo kawarimashita.
EN: Along with moving, my phone number also changed.
HI: घर बदलने के साथ, फ़ोन नंबर भी बदल गया।
GU: ઘર બદલવાની સાથે, ફોન નંબર પણ બદલાયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにつれてni tsurete(wrong — moving isn't a gradual process, it's a single triggering event)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)
bにともないni tomonaias a consequence of (Point 2)...के कारण/साथ...ના કારણે/સાથે

Why: The hint clarifies that this is "because of moving" — a single event causing another single change (phone number), which is にともなって's cause-consequence job, not につれて's gradual-progression sense.

Q6

学生＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ べきだ。Gakusei ___ beki da.

Tiles: 1 教師も (kyoushi mo)　2 成長 (seichou)　3 していく (shite iku)　4 とともに (to tomo ni)

4 とともに→1 教師も→2 成長→3 していく

学生とともに教師も成長していくべきだ。Gakusei to tomo ni kyoushi mo seichou shite iku beki da.

EN: Teachers should grow along with their students.
HI: शिक्षकों को भी विद्यार्थियों के साथ-साथ आगे बढ़ते रहना चाहिए।
GU: શિક્ષકોએ પણ વિદ્યાર્થીઓની સાથે સાથે આગળ વધતા રહેવું જોઈએ.

Why: Built on Point 1's とともに — 学生とともに (together with/alongside students) sets up 教師も成長していくべきだ (teachers should also keep growing).

Q7

係員の＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ください。Kakariin no ___ kudasai.

Tiles: 1 車を (kuruma o)　2 指示に (shiji ni)　3 したがって (shitagatte)　4 止めて (tomete)

2 指示に→3 したがって→1 車を→4 止めて

係員の指示にしたがって、車を止めてください。Kakariin no shiji ni shitagatte, kuruma o tomete kudasai.

EN: Please stop your car in accordance with the staff member's instructions.
HI: कर्मचारी के निर्देशों के अनुसार अपनी गाड़ी रोकें।
GU: કર્મચારીની સૂચનાઓ પ્રમાણે તમારી ગાડી ઊભી રાખો.

Why: Built on Point 4's "following an instruction" sense — 指示にしたがって (in accordance with the instructions) is the one sense につれて cannot cover, confirming にしたがって is required here.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This is the classic N2 "proportional/accompanying change" cluster — four expressions that overlap heavily but each has its own domain:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
とともにto tomo ni Together with / simultaneously with N／Vる ＋ とともに Loosest, most general — literal togetherness or plain simultaneity. Two things happening together, no strong causal link implied.
にともなってni tomonatte Accompanying / as a consequence of Nする／Vるの／Vたの ＋ にともなって Cause-and-consequence pairing of two changes — leans formal/written. One change explicitly triggers or accompanies another.
につれてni tsurete Gradually, proportionally as ~ Nする／Vる ＋ につれて Emphasizes gradual, proportional change over an extended process. A slow, continuous co-development (aging, urbanization).
にしたがってni shitagatte Proportionally as ~ / in accordance with ~ N／Vる ＋ にしたがって Overlaps につれて for gradual change, PLUS uniquely covers "following a rule/instruction." Gradual change, or explicitly following directions/a schedule/an order.
Exam trap: にしたがって is the only one of the four that can mean "following a rule/schedule/instruction" — 予定表につれて is explicitly marked wrong in the book. When a sentence involves following directions, a schedule, or an order, にしたがって is required and the other three are wrong. Conversely, when it's simply a gradual or simultaneous change (like temperature and humidity, or skill and awareness), several of the four may be interchangeable — see Q1 and Q3, where both offered answers are accepted.
