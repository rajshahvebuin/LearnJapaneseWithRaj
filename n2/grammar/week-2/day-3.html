1 Grammar Points

【1】 忘れないうちに

neutral register
Readingwasurenai uchi ni
Meaning (EN)"while ~ / before ~ (a temporary state changes)" — do something within a limited window before it closes
Meaning (HI)...रहते हुए / ...होने से पहले (जब तक स्थिति बनी है)
Meaning (GU)...હોય ત્યાં સુધીમાં / ...થાય તે પહેલાં
Connection (接続)Vない／Vている ＋ うちに ／ Aい ＋ うちに ／ Naな ＋ うちに ／ N ＋ の ＋ うちに
RegisterNeutral
Typical useEmphasizes doing something within a temporary window before a state changes (memory fading, flowers wilting, etc.) — うちに always implies the window will eventually close.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (negative)Vない ＋ うちに忘れないうちにwasurenai uchi ni
な-adjectiveNaな ＋ うちにきれいなうちにkirei na uchi ni
い-adjective(added, not in book) Aい ＋ うちに若いうちにwakai uchi ni
Noun(added, not in book) N ＋ の ＋ うちに学生のうちにgakusei no uchi ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
忘れないうちに、メモしておこう。Wasurenai uchi ni, memo shite okou. I will make a quick note before I forget. भूलने से पहले नोट कर लेता हूँ। ભૂલી જાઉં તે પહેલાં નોંધ કરી લઉં.
桜がきれいなうちに、お花見に行きましょう。Sakura ga kirei na uchi ni, ohanami ni ikimashou. Let's go to see the cherry blossoms while they are still beautiful. चेरी ब्लॉसम खूबसूरत रहते हुए ही फूल देखने चलते हैं। ચેરી બ્લોસમ સુંદર હોય ત્યાં સુધીમાં જ ફૂલ જોવા જઈએ.

うちに always implies the window will close — the action after うちに would become impossible or lose meaning once the state changes. This is what separates it from more neutral time markers like 〜てから.

【2】 終わるか終わらないかのうちに

neutral register
Readingowaru ka owaranai ka no uchi ni
Meaning (EN)"just as ~ / almost the very moment ~" — an action happening right at the boundary of another event's start or end
Meaning (HI)...होते ही, लगभग उसी पल (होने से पहले या ठीक बाद)
Meaning (GU)...થતાંની સાથે જ, લગભગ તે જ ક્ષણે
Connection (接続)Vるか Vないかのうちに (same verb, affirmative + negative)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA stronger, more precise version of plain うちに — stresses that two events happen almost simultaneously.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVるか Vないかのうちに終わるか終わらないかのうちにowaru ka owaranai ka no uchi ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
授業が終わるか終わらないかのうちに、彼は教室を出た。Jugyou ga owaru ka owaranai ka no uchi ni, kare wa kyoushitsu o deta. He left the classroom as soon as the class ended. क्लास ख़त्म होते ही वह कमरे से निकल गया। ક્લાસ પૂરો થતાંની સાથે જ તે ક્લાસરૂમમાંથી નીકળી ગયો.
布団に入るか入らないかのうちに眠ってしまう。Futon ni hairu ka hairanai ka no uchi ni nemutte shimau. I fell fast asleep the moment I got under the covers. बिस्तर में घुसते ही नींद आ जाती है। પથારીમાં પ્રવેશતાંની સાથે જ ઊંઘ આવી જાય છે.

This pattern always repeats the SAME verb in both plain affirmative and negative form (Vるか Vないか) — unlike ordinary うちに, which just takes one form. It stresses precision and immediacy: "practically simultaneous."

【3】 日本にいる限り

neutral register
ReadingNihon ni iru kagiri
Meaning (EN)❶ "as long as ~ (a condition holds)"; ❷ "as far as ~ goes (I know / checked / heard)"
Meaning (HI)❶ जब तक ...है (शर्त बनी रहती है); ❷ जहाँ तक ...है (जानकारी की सीमा में)
Meaning (GU)❶ જ્યાં સુધી ...છે; ❷ જ્યાં સુધી ...ની જાણ છે
Connection (接続)❶ Vる／Vない・Aい／Aくない・Naな／Naである・Nである ＋ 限り（は）  |  ❷ Vる／Vた／Vている ＋ 限り（では）
RegisterNeutral, leaning slightly formal/written
Typical use❶ sets a condition that must continue to hold for a result to remain true; ❷ limits a claim strictly to the speaker's own knowledge or survey (私の知る限り, 彼から聞いた限り, 昨日見た限り).
Word typeFormationExample
❶ Verb (condition)Vる／Vない ＋ 限り（は）日本にいる限りNihon ni iru kagiri ／ 改めない限りaratamenai kagiri
❷ Verb (scope)Vる／Vた／Vている ＋ 限り（では）私の知る限りwatashi no shiru kagiri ／ 調べた限りshirabeta kagiri
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
日本にいる限り、日本語は必要だ。Nihon ni iru kagiri, nihongo wa hitsuyou da. As long as you are in Japan, you will need Japanese. जब तक आप जापान में हैं, जापानी भाषा ज़रूरी है। જ્યાં સુધી તમે જાપાનમાં છો, જાપાનીઝ જરૂરી છે.
悪い生活習慣を改めない限り、健康にはなれない。Warui seikatsu shuukan o aratamenai kagiri, kenkou ni wa narenai. If you don't improve your bad living habits, you will not be able to be healthy. जब तक आप अपनी बुरी आदतें नहीं सुधारते, स्वस्थ नहीं हो सकते। જ્યાં સુધી તમે તમારી ખરાબ ટેવો સુધારતા નથી, તંદુરસ્ત થઈ શકતા નથી.
私の調べた限り(では)、こういう研究はだれもしていない。Watashi no shirabeta kagiri (dewa), kou iu kenkyuu wa dare mo shite inai. I surveyed the literature, but no one so far has conducted this type of research. मैंने जितना पता लगाया, उस हद तक कोई ऐसा शोध नहीं कर रहा। મેં જેટલી તપાસ કરી, ત્યાં સુધી કોઈ આવું સંશોધન કરી રહ્યું નથી.

The same word 限り splits into two distinct uses: ❶ a conditional "as long as" (attaches to plain-form predicates of any word type) and ❷ a limiter on the speaker's own knowledge (attaches only to verbs of knowing/checking/hearing/seeing — 知る／調べた／聞いた／見た). Don't confuse the two when a question tests them side by side.

【4】 70歳以上の方に限り

neutral register
Readingnanajussai ijou no kata ni kagiri
Meaning (EN)"limited to N only" (に限り); "of all things, especially N" — often ironic, e.g. "today of all days" (に限って); "not limited to N, also ~" (に限らず〜も)
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ N तक सीमित (に限り); ख़ासकर N ही, जैसे "आज ही" (に限って); सिर्फ़ N तक सीमित नहीं, बल्कि...भी (に限らず〜も)
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત N પૂરતું મર્યાદિત (に限り); ખાસ કરીને N જ (に限って); ફક્ત N પૂરતું મર્યાદિત નહીં, ...પણ (に限らず〜も)
Connection (接続)N ＋ に ＋ 限り／限って／限らず〜も ／ N〈数量／時間〉＋ 限り
RegisterNeutral. ダメ: 女性限り／東京限り (N + 限り without に) are ungrammatical for this "restricted to" meaning.
Typical useMarking an explicit restriction (only these people/this time), or, with 限って, an ironic "of all the times/people, precisely this one" sense; 限らず negates the restriction, extending the claim further.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (restriction)N ＋ に限り70歳以上の方に限りnanajussai ijou no kata ni kagiri
Noun (quantity/time)N〈数量／時間〉＋ 限り本日限りhonjitsu kagiri ／ 一回限りikkai kagiri
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
70歳以上の方に限り、入場無料。Nanajussai ijou no kata ni kagiri, nyuujou muryou. Admission is free only for those who are 70 years old or older. सिर्फ़ 70 साल या उससे अधिक उम्र के लोगों के लिए प्रवेश मुफ़्त है। ફક્ત 70 વર્ષ કે તેથી વધુ ઉંમરના લોકો માટે પ્રવેશ મફત છે.
最近は、女性に限らず男性も化粧をする。Saikin wa, josei ni kagirazu dansei mo keshou o suru. Recently, not only women but men as well wear make-up. आजकल, सिर्फ़ महिलाएं ही नहीं, पुरुष भी मेकअप करते हैं। આજકાલ, ફક્ત મહિલાઓ જ નહીં, પુરુષો પણ મેકઅપ કરે છે.
雨だ。今日に限ってかさを持ってこなかった。Ame da. Kyou ni kagitte kasa o motte konakatta. It's raining. Today of all days I don't have an umbrella. बारिश हो रही है। आज ही के दिन छाता नहीं लाया। વરસાદ પડે છે. આજે જ છત્રી નથી લાવ્યો.
うちの子に限って悪いことをするはずがない。Uchi no ko ni kagitte warui koto o suru hazu ga nai. (Unlike other children,) our child absolutely would not do bad things. हमारे बच्चे के मामले में तो बुरा काम करने का सवाल ही नहीं उठता। અમારા બાળકના કિસ્સામાં તો ખરાબ કામ કરવાનો સવાલ જ નથી.

Watch the に — 女性限り／東京限り (without に) are marked wrong in the book; it must be 女性に限り／東京に限り. に限って carries a distinct ironic "of all times/people" flavor, quite different from the neutral restriction of に限り.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

いただきます、＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ 食べ始めた。Itadakimasu, ___ tabehajimeta.

Tiles: 1 言わないか (iwanai ka)　2 言うか (iu ka)　3 と (to)　4 のうちに (no uchi ni)

3 と→2 言うか→1 言わないか→4 のうちに

いただきますと言うか言わないかのうちに食べ始めた。Itadakimasu to iu ka iwanai ka no uchi ni tabehajimeta.

EN: He started eating almost before he'd finished saying "itadakimasu."
HI: "इतादाकिमास" कहते-कहते ही उसने खाना शुरू कर दिया।
GU: "ઈતાદાકિમાસ" કહેતાં કહેતાં જ તેણે ખાવાનું શરૂ કરી દીધું.

Why: Built on Point 2's Vるか Vないかのうちに — 言うか言わないかのうちに repeats the same verb 言う in affirmative and negative form to stress how immediate the eating was.

Q7

＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ しなければならない。___ shinakereba naranai.

Tiles: 1 学生 (gakusei)　2 かぎり (kagiri)　3 勉強 (benkyou)　4 である (de aru)

1 学生→4 である→2 かぎり→3 勉強

学生である限り、勉強しなければならない。Gakusei de aru kagiri, benkyou shinakereba naranai.

EN: As long as you're a student, you have to study.
HI: जब तक तुम विद्यार्थी हो, तब तक पढ़ाई करनी ही होगी।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી તું વિદ્યાર્થી છે, ત્યાં સુધી અભ્યાસ કરવો જ પડશે.

Why: Built on Point 3's ❶ conditional 限り — 学生である (noun + である) + 限り sets the condition ("as long as one is a student") that requires the ongoing obligation to study.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points revolve around "a bounded window or condition" — うちに, its stronger cousin, and 限り's two very different uses, plus に限り/に限って/に限らず:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
うちにuchi ni While a state lasts Vない／Vている／Aい／Naな／Nの ＋ うちに General "window before change" — one form, any word type. Doing something before a temporary state ends.
Vるか Vないかのうちに~ka ~nai ka no uchi ni Almost the very instant ~ same verb, affirmative + negative Stronger/narrower than plain うちに — near-simultaneity. Two events happening practically at once.
限り❶ (condition)kagiri As long as ~ plain form (any word type) ＋ 限り（は） A condition that must keep holding for the result to stay true. Stating "the result holds only while X continues."
限り❷ (scope)kagiri As far as I ~ 知る／調べた／聞いた／見た ＋ 限り（では） Limits a claim to the speaker's own knowledge/survey — verbs of knowing only. Hedging a claim as based only on what you personally know.
に限り／に限って／に限らずni kagiri/kagitte/kagirazu Only N / of all N / not only N N ＋ に ＋ 限り／限って／限らず〜も 限り=neutral restriction; 限って=ironic "of all times/people"; 限らず=negates the restriction. Restricting, ironically singling out, or widening a claim about a noun.
Exam trap: 女性限り (no に) is wrong — always insert に before 限り／限って／限らず when restricting a noun. Also don't confuse 限り❶ (a condition, works with any plain-form predicate) with 限り❷ (a scope-limiter, works only with verbs of knowing) — Q4 tests exactly this by putting 知る/知らない side by side, where only the fixed ❷ pattern (知る限り) is grammatical here.
