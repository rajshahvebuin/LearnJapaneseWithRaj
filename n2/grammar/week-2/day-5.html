5日目 — 外国へ行くとしたら (Gaikoku e iku to shitara)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.38–39 (PDF pages 38–39).

Answer key

Notes
This day contrasts に (adopting a standpoint) with と (supposing a hypothesis) across several near-identical-looking patterns — にしたら vs としたら is the day's central trap (see Confusion Pairs).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 子どもにしたら

neutral register
Readingkodomo ni shitara
Meaning (EN)"from N's standpoint / if we consider it from N's position"
Meaning (HI)N के नज़रिए से / N की स्थिति में रखकर देखें तो
Meaning (GU)N ના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી / N ની સ્થિતિમાં જોઈએ તો
Connection (接続)N ＋ にしたら／すれば／してみたら／してみれば
RegisterNeutral
Typical useExpresses "looking at it from that person's/thing's point of view"; also usable as 〜の立場になってみれば ("if I were in ~'s position").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN ＋ にしたら／すれば／してみたら／してみれば子どもにしたらkodomo ni shitara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
大きすぎる親の期待は、子どもにしたら苦痛だ。Ookisugiru oya no kitai wa, kodomo ni shitara kutsuu da. Expectations of parents that are too big become agony for the children. माता-पिता की बहुत ज़्यादा उम्मीदें बच्चों के नज़रिए से एक तकलीफ़ है। માતાપિતાની ખૂબ મોટી અપેક્ષાઓ બાળકોના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી પીડાદાયક છે.
犬や猫はかわいいが、飼っていない人にすれば迷惑なこともある。Inu ya neko wa kawaii ga, katte inai hito ni sureba meiwaku na koto mo aru. Dogs and cats are cute, but for those who do not have them, they can be a nuisance. कुत्ते-बिल्लियां प्यारे होते हैं, लेकिन जो नहीं पालते उनके नज़रिए से कभी-कभी परेशानी भी होती है। કૂતરાં-બિલાડીઓ વ્હાલા હોય છે, પણ જેઓ પાળતા નથી તેમના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી ક્યારેક તકલીફરૂપ પણ હોય છે.

にしたら／にすれば／にしてみたら／にしてみれば are near-interchangeable ways to say "looking at it from that standpoint" — choose whichever fits the sentence's rhythm.

【2】 本当だとしたら

neutral register
Readinghontou da to shitara
Meaning (EN)"if it were the case that ~ / supposing that ~" — a hypothetical conditional
Meaning (HI)अगर मान लें कि ...है / यदि ऐसा हो तो
Meaning (GU)જો માની લઈએ કે ...છે તો
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj／N（普通形）＋ と ＋ したら／すれば
RegisterNeutral
Typical useIntroduces a hypothesis: "supposing X is true, then...".
Word typeFormationExample
Plain form + とV／A／な-adj／N（普）＋ としたら／とすれば本当だとしたらhontou da to shitara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
もし、それが本当だとしたら、夢のようです。Moshi, sore ga hontou da to shitara, yume no you desu. If that is true, it's like a dream. अगर यह सच है, तो यह किसी सपने जैसा है। જો આ સાચું છે, તો આ સપના જેવું છે.
あの人が今も生きているとすれば、もう90歳になっているでしょう。Ano hito ga ima mo ikite iru to sureba, mou kyuujuu-sai ni natte iru deshou. If that person was still living today, he would be 90 years old. अगर वह व्यक्ति आज भी जीवित है, तो वह अब तक 90 साल का हो चुका होगा। જો તે વ્યક્તિ આજે પણ જીવિત છે, તો તે અત્યાર સુધીમાં 90 વર્ષનો થઈ ગયો હશે.

と+したら／すれば is a hypothetical "if we suppose ~" — と marks the supposition being made before the conditional したら／すれば.

【3】 行くとしても

neutral register
Readingiku to shite mo
Meaning (EN)"even if ~ / even granting that ~" — concedes a hypothetical and says the conclusion doesn't change
Meaning (HI)भले ही ...हो फिर भी / मान भी लें कि ...है तो भी
Meaning (GU)ભલે ...હોય તો પણ
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj／N（普）＋ としても／としたって（casual） ／ な-adj／N ＋ にしても／にしたって（casual）
RegisterNeutral; としたって／にしたって are casual/spoken variants
Typical useConcedes a hypothetical possibility while asserting the conclusion holds regardless.
Word typeFormationExample
Plain form + とV（普）＋ としても行くとしてもiku to shite mo
Noun / な-adj + にN／な-adj ＋ にしても監督にしてもkantoku ni shite mo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
行くとしても、旅行者としてしか行けない。Iku to shite mo, ryokousha to shite shika ikenai. If I go, I could only go as a traveler. भले ही जाऊं भी, तो सिर्फ़ एक यात्री के तौर पर ही जा सकता हूं। ભલે જાઉં પણ, ફક્ત એક પ્રવાસી તરીકે જ જઈ શકું છું.
負けてくやしいのは、選手だけでなく監督にしても同じだ。Makete kuyashii no wa, senshu dake de naku kantoku ni shite mo onaji da. It's not just the athletes who regret losing, even the coach feels the same. हारने का मलाल सिर्फ़ खिलाड़ियों को ही नहीं, कोच को भी उतना ही होता है। હારવાનો અફસોસ ફક્ત ખેલાડીઓને જ નહીં, કોચને પણ એટલો જ હોય છે.

としても／にしても both mean "even if/even granting ~"; the difference is mostly about what attaches (と for verbs/plain forms generally, に for nouns/な-adjectives), with としたって／にしたって as their casual spoken equivalents.

【4】 社会参加を目的として

neutral register
Readingshakai sanka o mokuteki to shite
Meaning (EN)"with N2 as N1's purpose/theme/leader/etc." — a family of "N1をN2として/とする/とした" constructions
Meaning (HI)N1 को N2 के तौर पर लेकर (उद्देश्य/विषय/नेता आदि बनाकर)
Meaning (GU)N1 ને N2 તરીકે રાખીને (હેતુ/વિષય/નેતા વગેરે બનાવીને)
Connection (接続)N1 ＋ を ＋ N2 ＋ と ＋ してV／するN／したN
RegisterNeutral, common in formal/written descriptions
Typical useDescribes purpose/theme/role-assignment structures — very common in announcements and descriptions of organized activities.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun + するN1をN2と ＋ してV社会参加を目的として始められたshakai sanka o mokuteki to shite hajimerareta
Noun + する (present)N1をN2と ＋ するN山田さんをリーダーとするサークルYamada-san o riidaa to suru saakuru
Noun + する (past)N1をN2と ＋ したN環境問題をテーマとした番組kankyou mondai o teema to shita bangumi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この祭りは、住民の社会参加を目的として始められた。Kono matsuri wa, juumin no shakai sanka o mokuteki to shite hajimerareta. The goal of this festival was to have residents participate in the local society. यह त्योहार निवासियों की सामाजिक भागीदारी को उद्देश्य बनाकर शुरू किया गया। આ તહેવાર રહેવાસીઓની સામાજિક ભાગીદારીને હેતુ બનાવીને શરૂ કરવામાં આવ્યો.
山田さんをリーダーとするサークルを作る。Yamada-san o riidaa to suru saakuru o tsukuru. We will make a club with Yamada-san as the leader. यामादा जी को नेता बनाकर एक क्लब बनाएंगे। યામાદા સાનને નેતા બનાવીને એક ક્લબ બનાવીશું.
環境問題をテーマとしたテレビ番組を見る。Kankyou mondai o teema to shita terebi bangumi o miru. I watch TV programs about environmental problems. पर्यावरण की समस्याओं को विषय बनाकर बने टीवी कार्यक्रम देखता हूं। પર્યાવરણની સમસ્યાઓને વિષય બનાવીને બનેલા ટીવી કાર્યક્રમો જોઉં છું.

This is a template "N1をN2として/とする/とした" — the tense of する (して／する／した) depends on whether it modifies an ongoing clause, a noun in present/generic form, or a noun via past/completed modification.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.39. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.41.

練習Ⅰ — circle the correct choice

Q1

このクラスでは新聞を教材（a. として　b. にしたら）使います。Kono kurasu de wa shinbun o kyouzai (a. to shite b. ni shitara) tsukaimasu.

Full sentence: このクラスでは新聞を教材として使います。Kono kurasu de wa shinbun o kyouzai to shite tsukaimasu.
EN: In this class, we use newspapers as teaching material.
HI: इस क्लास में, हम अख़बारों को शिक्षण सामग्री के तौर पर इस्तेमाल करते हैं।
GU: આ ક્લાસમાં, અમે અખબારોને શિક્ષણ સામગ્રી તરીકે વાપરીએ છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとしてto shiteas, in the role of (Point 4)के तौर परતરીકે
bにしたらni shitara(would mean "from the newspaper's standpoint" — wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)

Why: This describes newspapers being used in the role of teaching material — Point 4's Nとして, not Point 1's standpoint-marker にしたら.

Q2

彼（a. にしたら　b. にしたって）大したことではないだろうが、私にとっては重大な問題だ。Kare (a. ni shitara b. ni shitatte) taishita koto de wa nai darou ga, watashi ni totte wa juudai na mondai da.

Full sentence: 彼にしたら大したことではないだろうが、私にとっては重大な問題だ。Kare ni shitara taishita koto de wa nai darou ga, watashi ni totte wa juudai na mondai da.
EN: From his standpoint it's probably no big deal, but for me it's a serious problem.
HI: उसकी नज़र में यह शायद कोई बड़ी बात न हो, पर मेरे लिए यह एक गंभीर समस्या है।
GU: તેની નજરે આ કદાચ મોટી વાત ન હોય, પણ મારા માટે આ ગંભીર સમસ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにしたらni shitarafrom his standpoint (Point 1)उसकी नज़र मेंતેની નજરે
bにしたってni shitatte(casual "even granting X" — wrong sense here, this is about a standpoint, not a concession)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)

Why: The sentence contrasts his perspective with the speaker's — exactly Point 1's job. にしたって (casual としても/にしても) would instead mean "even granting it's about him," a concessive sense that doesn't fit here.

Q3

旅行する（a. として　b. としたら）、夏休みにしようと思います。Ryokou suru (a. to shite b. to shitara), natsuyasumi ni shiyou to omoimasu.

Full sentence: 旅行するとしたら、夏休みにしようと思います。Ryokou suru to shitara, natsuyasumi ni shiyou to omoimasu.
EN: If we were to travel, I'm thinking we'd do it during summer vacation.
HI: अगर यात्रा करनी हो, तो मुझे लगता है गर्मियों की छुट्टियों में करेंगे।
GU: જો પ્રવાસ કરવો હોય, તો મને લાગે છે ઉનાળાની રજાઓમાં કરીશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとしてto shite(role/purpose marker — wrong sense; there's no hypothesis here)(यहाँ ग़लत भाव)(અહીં ખોટો ભાવ)
bとしたらto shitaraif we suppose ~ (Point 2)अगर मान लें कि...જો માની લઈએ કે...

Why: The sentence is a hypothesis about a future trip that hasn't been decided yet — exactly Point 2's としたら, not Point 4's role-marker として.

Q4

この集まりは、国際交流を目的（a. として　b. とした）ものです。Kono atsumari wa, kokusai kouryuu o mokuteki (a. to shite b. to shita) mono desu.

Full sentence: この集まりは、国際交流を目的としたものです。Kono atsumari wa, kokusai kouryuu o mokuteki to shita mono desu.
EN: This gathering is one whose purpose is international exchange.
HI: यह सभा अंतरराष्ट्रीय आदान-प्रदान को उद्देश्य बनाकर बनाई गई है।
GU: આ મેળાવડો આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય આદાનપ્રદાનને હેતુ બનાવીને બનાવવામાં આવ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとしてto shite(would need to directly precede a verb, not ものです — wrong shape here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bとしたto shitapast/completed form modifying もの (Point 4)...बनाकर बनाई गई...બનાવીને બનાવવામાં આવેલ

Q5

もし男に生まれた（a. としては　b. としても）、この仕事をしたい。Moshi otoko ni umareta (a. to shite wa b. to shite mo), kono shigoto o shitai.

Full sentence: もし男に生まれたとしても、この仕事をしたい。Moshi otoko ni umareta to shite mo, kono shigoto o shitai.
EN: Even if I had been born a man, I would want to do this job.
HI: अगर मैं पुरुष के रूप में पैदा होता, तब भी यही काम करना चाहता।
GU: જો હું પુરુષ તરીકે જન્મ્યો હોત, તો પણ આ જ કામ કરવા માંગત.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとしてはto shite wa(wrong pattern — not a concessive form)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

ちょっと信じられないことだが、もしそれが＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ だ。Chotto shinjirarenai koto da ga, moshi sore ga ___ da.

Tiles: 1 としたら (to shitara)　2 事実だ (jijitsu da)　3 ものすごい (monosugoi)　4 発見 (hakken)

2 事実だ→1 としたら→3 ものすごい→4 発見

ちょっと信じられないことだが、もしそれが事実だとしたら、ものすごい発見だ。Chotto shinjirarenai koto da ga, moshi sore ga jijitsu da to shitara, monosugoi hakken da.

EN: It's hard to believe, but if that's true, it's an amazing discovery.
HI: यह मानना ज़रा मुश्किल है, लेकिन अगर यह सच है, तो यह एक ज़बरदस्त खोज है।
GU: આ માનવું થોડું મુશ્કેલ છે, પણ જો આ સાચું છે, તો આ એક જબરદસ્ત શોધ છે.

Why: Built on Point 2's としたら — 事実だとしたら (supposing it's a fact) sets up the hypothetical before naming the conclusion (an amazing discovery).

Q7

これは＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ ＿＿＿＿ 作品です。Kore wa ___ sakuhin desu.

Tiles: 1 作られた (tsukurareta)　2 平和を (heiwa o)　3 として (to shite)　4 テーマ (teema)

2 平和を→4 テーマ→3 として→1 作られた

これは平和をテーマとして作られた作品です。Kore wa heiwa o teema to shite tsukurareta sakuhin desu.

EN: This is a work created with peace as its theme.
HI: यह शांति को विषय बनाकर बनाई गई एक कृति है।
GU: આ શાંતિને વિષય બનાવીને બનાવવામાં આવેલી કૃતિ છે.

Why: Built on Point 4's N1をN2として — 平和をテーマとして (with peace as the theme) modifies 作られた作品 (a work that was created).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

に and と look interchangeable in casual speech, but here they mark two completely different things — a standpoint vs. a supposition — with としても adding a third, concessive layer:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
にしたら／にすればni shitara/ni sureba From N's standpoint N ＋ にしたら／すれば／してみたら／してみれば に attaches to a person/thing whose viewpoint is being adopted. Contrasting how a specific party sees things.
としたら／とすればto shitara/to sureba If we suppose ~ V／A／な-adj／N（普）＋ としたら／とすれば と attaches to a proposition being hypothesized as true. Introducing a hypothesis about a fact or situation.
としても／にしてもto shite mo/ni shite mo Even if / even granting ~ V／A／な-adj／N（普）＋ としても ／ な-adj／N ＋ にしても Concedes the hypothesis but asserts the conclusion doesn't change. Saying "even if X is true, Y still holds."
NをNとしてN o N to shite With N2 as N1 N1 ＋ を ＋ N2 ＋ と ＋ してV／するN／したN Assigns a role/purpose/theme — unrelated to standpoint or hypothesis. Describing the purpose or role behind something organized.
Exam trap: にしたら (standpoint) and としたら (hypothesis) look similar but differ completely in what they attach to and mean — に marks a person/thing whose viewpoint you're adopting, と marks a proposition you're supposing true. Q1 and Q3 both hinge on exactly this contrast (として vs にしたら; として vs としたら) — check first whether the sentence is contrasting a perspective or floating a hypothesis.
