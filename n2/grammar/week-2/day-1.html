1日目 — がんばったかいがあって (Ganbatta kai ga atte)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Bunpou) — printed p.30–31 (PDF pages 30–31).
Grammar points covered
努力のかいがあって手術のかいもなくやりがい借金してまで／借金までして
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.33 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Points 1–2 share the same noun かい (worth/effect) in opposite polarity (positive vs. negative); Point 3's がい is a different, unrelated suffix that turns a verb stem into "the value of doing ~". Point 4's てまで carries a built-in nuance of surprise/disapproval at going to an extreme length.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 努力のかいがあって

neutral register
Readingkai ga atte / doryoku no kai ga atte
Meaning (EN)"it was worth the effort / the effort paid off" — an action or effort resulted in a good outcome
Meaning (HI)मेहनत का फल मिला / ...की मेहनत रंग लाई
Meaning (GU)...ની મહેનતનું ફળ મળ્યું
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた ＋ かいがある  |  Nする／Nの ＋ かい（が）あって
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical usePraising that a specific effort led to a good result; very often appears as the clause 〜のかいがあって at the start of a sentence.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVる／Vた ＋ かいがある待ったかいがあったmatta kai ga atta
NounNする／Nの ＋ かい（が）あって努力のかいがあってdoryoku no kai ga atte
い-adjective(added, not in book — not a natural fit; かい pairs with effort-verbs/nouns, not descriptive adjectives)—
な-adjective(added, not in book — same reason)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
努力のかいあって、希望の大学に合格した。Doryoku no kai atte, kibou no daigaku ni goukaku shita. My efforts were rewarded because I got into the university I wanted to. मेरी मेहनत रंग लाई और मुझे मनचाहे विश्वविद्यालय में दाखिला मिल गया। મારી મહેનત રંગ લાવી અને મને મનપસંદ યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ મળ્યો.
すてきな人と結婚できた。この年まで待ったかいがあった。Sutekina hito to kekkon dekita. Kono toshi made matta kai ga atta. I was able to marry a wonderful person. It was worth waiting until this age. मुझे एक शानदार व्यक्ति से शादी करने का मौका मिला। इस उम्र तक इंतज़ार करने का फल मिला। મને એક સરસ વ્યક્તિ સાથે લગ્ન કરવાની તક મળી. આ ઉંમર સુધી રાહ જોવાનું ફળ મળ્યું.

かい means "worth / effect" — it is almost always paired with のかいがあって (noun + の) or a past-tense verb, since the pattern reports on an effort that has already borne fruit. This is neutral register, common in both everyday speech and writing.

【2】 手術のかいもなく

neutral register
Readingshujutsu no kai mo naku
Meaning (EN)"in vain, despite ~ / even though ~, it came to nothing" — the negative counterpart of Point 1
Meaning (HI)...के बावजूद कोई फ़ायदा नहीं हुआ
Meaning (GU)...છતાં કોઈ ફાયદો ન થયો
Connection (接続)Vた ＋ かいがない  |  Nする／Nの ＋ かい（も）なく〜
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribing an effort or action that ended without the hoped-for result — often used for disappointing or tragic outcomes (an operation, hard practice).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVた ＋ かいがない練習したかいがなかったrenshuu shita kai ga nakatta
NounNする／Nの ＋ かい（も）なく〜手術のかいもなくshujutsu no kai mo naku
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
手術のかいもなく、愛犬が死んでしまった。Shujutsu no kai mo naku, aiken ga shinde shimatta. Even though my pet dog had an operation, he died. ऑपरेशन के बावजूद, मेरे पालतू कुत्ते की मौत हो गई। ઓપરેશન છતાં, મારા પાલતુ કૂતરાનું મૃત્યુ થયું.
予選で落ちてしまい、一生懸命練習したかいがなかった。Yosen de ochite shimai, isshoukenmei renshuu shita kai ga nakatta. Even though I practised hard for the competition, I feel like my efforts were wasted because I couldn't win through the prelims. मैंने कड़ी मेहनत से अभ्यास किया, लेकिन प्रारंभिक दौर में ही हार गया — मेरी मेहनत बेकार गई। મેં સખત મહેનતથી પ્રેક્ટિસ કરી, પણ પ્રિલિમિનરી રાઉન્ડમાં જ હારી ગયો — મારી મહેનત નકામી ગઈ.

This is the exact negative mirror of Point 1 — same かい (worth/effect), simply negated. It is often used to express regret or disappointment that a genuine effort did not pay off.

【3】 やりがい

neutral register
Readingyarigai / V-stem + gai
Meaning (EN)"worth doing ~" — a suffix attached to a verb's ます-stem that forms a noun meaning "the value/worth of doing ~"
Meaning (HI)...करने लायक / ...करने का सुकून (जैसे यरिगाइ = करने लायक काम)
Meaning (GU)...કરવા જેવું (જેમ કે યારીગાઈ = કરવા જેવું કામ)
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem ＋ がい
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAttaches to the ます-stem of a verb to form a compound noun meaning "the worth of doing X"; almost always paired with ある (worth doing) or ない (not worth doing).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-stem ＋ がい生きがいを感じるikigai o kanjiru ／ 苦労のしがいがあるkurou no shigai ga aru ／ 作りがいがあるtsukurigai ga aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
やりがいのある仕事Yarigai no aru shigoto work worth doing करने लायक/संतोषजनक काम કરવા જેવું/સંતોષકારક કામ
教えがいのある生徒Oshiegai no aru seito students worth teaching पढ़ाने लायक विद्यार्थी ભણાવવા જેવા વિદ્યાર્થીઓ

がい is a completely different word from Point 1–2's かい, even though they sound similar — がい is a suffix that turns a verb stem into an abstract "worth doing" noun, while かい is a standalone noun meaning "effect/result". Don't mix them up: 作りがいがある ("worth making") vs 作ったかいがあった ("it was worth having made it").

【4】 借金してまで／借金までして

neutral register
Readingshakkin shite made / shakkin made shite
Meaning (EN)"even to the point of ~ (doing something extreme)" — expresses the speaker's surprise or disapproval that someone would go that far
Meaning (HI)यहां तक कि ...करके भी (आश्चर्य/अस्वीकृति दर्शाता है)
Meaning (GU)ત્યાં સુધી કે ...કરીને પણ (આશ્ચર્ય/નાપસંદગી દર્શાવે છે)
Connection (接続)Vて ＋ まで（も）  |  N ＋ まで ＋ Vて
RegisterNeutral, but carries a built-in critical/surprised nuance
Typical useEmphasizes that someone went to an extreme, unexpected length to do something, usually with implied criticism or disbelief. (The book notes: ◆ そんなことまで、という驚きを表す — "expresses surprise that it went that far".)
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVて ＋ まで（も）借金してまでshakkin shite made
NounN ＋ まで ＋ Vて借金までしてshakkin made shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
借金してまで／借金までして海外旅行に行くなんて、私には信じられない。Shakkin shite made / shakkin made shite kaigai ryokou ni iku nante, watashi ni wa shinjirarenai. I can't believe he went on a trip abroad on borrowed money. मैं विश्वास नहीं कर सकता कि उसने कर्ज लेकर भी विदेश यात्रा की। હું માની શકતો નથી કે તેણે દેવું કરીને પણ વિદેશ પ્રવાસ કર્યો.
いやな思いまでして／いやな思いをしてまで、彼女と付き合うことないよ。Iyana omoi made shite / iyana omoi o shite made, kanojo to tsukiau koto nai yo. There is no reason for you to keep going out with her if you don't enjoy it. अगर तुम्हें बुरा लगता है तो उसके साथ रिश्ता जारी रखने की कोई ज़रूरत नहीं है। જો તને ખરાબ લાગે છે તો તેની સાથે સંબંધ ચાલુ રાખવાની કોઈ જરૂર નથી.
親にうそをついてまで遊びに行きたくない。Oya ni uso o tsuite made asobi ni ikitakunai. I don't want to go if I have to lie about it to my parents. मैं नहीं चाहता कि माता-पिता से झूठ बोलकर भी घूमने जाऊं। હું નથી ઈચ્છતો કે માતાપિતાને જૂઠું બોલીને પણ ફરવા જાઉં.

まで here means "even to the extent of," implying the action described is extreme or unexpected — often used to voice surprise or disapproval. Both orders (V-てまで and N＋までVて) are natural and interchangeable.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This week's divider page groups these four expressions together deliberately, because they're easy to mix up — かい/がい look and sound almost identical, and てまで adds a third flavor entirely:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜のかいがあって~no kai ga atte The effort paid off Vる／Vた ／ Nの ＋ かいがある かい = a noun meaning "worth/effect"; reports a specific effort's positive result. Praising that a particular effort led to success.
〜のかいもなく~no kai mo naku In vain, despite ~ Vた ／ Nの ＋ かいもなく Same noun かい, simply negated — a specific effort's result was disappointing. Lamenting that an effort failed to produce the hoped-for result.
V-stem+がいV-stem+gai Worth doing ~ V-ます-stem ＋ がい（がある／ない） A different suffix — turns the verb itself into an abstract "worth of doing X", not tied to one specific past effort. Describing whether an activity itself is inherently rewarding.
Vてまで／Nまで Vて~te made / N made ~te Even to the point of ~ Vて＋まで（も） ／ N＋まで＋Vて Signals an extreme, unexpected length gone to — carries a built-in surprise/critical nuance. Expressing disbelief or disapproval at how far someone went.
Exam trap: かい (Points 1–2) and がい (Point 3) are easy to confuse on sound alone, but they are grammatically unrelated — かい is a standalone noun that follows a conjugated verb/noun-の (作ったかいがあった, "it was worth having made it"), while がい is a suffix fused directly onto the ます-stem (作りがいがある, "it's worth making," describing the activity itself rather than a specific completed effort). When a question gives you a stem-form option next to a past-tense option, check whether the sentence is judging a general activity (→ がい) or a specific completed effort (→ かい).
