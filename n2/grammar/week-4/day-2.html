1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜向け／〜向き

neutral register
Reading~muke / ~muki
Meaning (EN)〜向け = "made/aimed for N" (a deliberate design target); 〜向き = "suited to N" (a natural fit, whether intended or not)
Meaning (HI)〜向け = ...के लिए बनाया गया (लक्षित); 〜向き = ...के लिए उपयुक्त/मुफ़ीद
Meaning (GU)〜向け = ...માટે બનાવેલું (લક્ષિત); 〜向き = ...માટે યોગ્ય/અનુકૂળ
Connection (接続)N + 向け[だ／に／のN]　|　N + 向き[だ／に／のN]
RegisterNeutral
Typical use向け = "N向け = Nを対象とした" (made with N as the target audience); 向き = "N向き = Nに向いている" (naturally suitable for N).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (design target)N + 向け初心者向けの教科書shoshinsha-muke no kyoukasho
Noun (natural fit)N + 向き女性向きだjosei-muki da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
それは、初心者向けの教科書です。Sore wa, shoshinsha-muke no kyoukasho desu. This is a textbook for beginners. यह शुरुआती लोगों के लिए बनाई गई पाठ्यपुस्तक है। આ શરૂઆત કરનારાઓ માટે બનાવેલ પાઠ્યપુસ્તક છે.
この製品の生産は、国内向けも、海外輸出向けも、ともに増加した。Kono seihin no seisan wa, kokunai-muke mo, kaigai yushutsu-muke mo, tomo ni zouka shita. The manufacturing of this product has increased for both domestic and export markets. इस उत्पाद का उत्पादन घरेलू बाज़ार और विदेश निर्यात, दोनों के लिए बढ़ा है। આ ઉત્પાદનનું ઉત્પાદન સ્થાનિક બજાર અને વિદેશ નિકાસ, બંને માટે વધ્યું છે.
この店の料理は、量が少なく見た目がきれいなので、女性向きだ。Kono mise no ryouri wa, ryou ga sukunaku mitame ga kirei na node, josei-muki da. The food at this restaurant comes in small portions and looks nice, so it is suited for women. इस दुकान का खाना मात्रा में कम और देखने में सुंदर है, इसलिए यह महिलाओं के लिए उपयुक्त है। આ દુકાનનું ખાવાનું માત્રામાં ઓછું અને દેખાવમાં સુંદર છે, એટલે તે મહિલાઓ માટે અનુકૂળ છે.

Keep the intention test in mind: 子ども向けの番組 was made *for* children on purpose; 子ども向きの番組 just happens to *suit* children, whether or not that was the maker's plan. In practice the two often overlap, but 向け always implies a deliberate target audience.

【2】 〜次第で

neutral register
Reading~shidai de
Meaning (EN)"depending on ~" — the outcome is entirely contingent on N
Meaning (HI)...पर निर्भर करता है — नतीजा पूरी तरह इसी बात पर निर्भर है
Meaning (GU)...પર આધાર રાખે છે — પરિણામ સંપૂર્ણપણે આના પર નિર્ભર છે
Connection (接続)N + 次第だ／で／では
RegisterNeutral
Typical useN is the deciding factor for whatever comes next — weather, a person's will, circumstances, etc.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + 次第で／だ／では天気次第でtenki shidai de
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
花火大会は天気次第で中止になる場合もあります。Hanabi taikai wa tenki shidai de chuushi ni naru baai mo arimasu. The fireworks display may be cancelled due to the weather. मौसम के अनुसार, आतिशबाज़ी का कार्यक्रम रद्द भी हो सकता है। હવામાન પ્રમાણે, ફટાકડાનો કાર્યક્રમ રદ પણ થઈ શકે છે.
仕事は紹介するが、うまくいくかどうかは本人次第だ。Shigoto wa shoukai suru ga, umaku iku ka douka wa honnin shidai da. I will introduce you to some work, but whether it goes well or not depends on you. मैं काम की सिफ़ारिश कर दूँगा, लेकिन वह सफल होगा या नहीं यह आप पर निर्भर करता है। હું કામ માટે ભલામણ કરી દઈશ, પણ તે સફળ થશે કે નહીં તે તમારા પર આધાર રાખે છે.

次第 attaches directly to a noun with no の in between (天気次第, 本人次第) — a common slip is inserting の, which is wrong here (contrast with point 4's 次第です, which is a different construction built on a verb).

【3】 〜次第

neutral register
Reading~shidai
Meaning (EN)"as soon as ~ happens" — the moment V occurs, the next action follows immediately
Meaning (HI)...होते ही — जैसे ही यह हो, तुरंत अगला काम
Meaning (GU)...થતાં જ — જેવું આ થાય, તરત જ આગળનું કામ
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem + 次第
RegisterNeutral, common in polite/business speech
Typical useDescribes an immediate follow-up action once V happens. Cannot be used for a past event — 過去のことには使えない, per the book's own sidebar.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます-stem + 次第戻り次第modori shidai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
では、田中が戻り次第、お電話させます。Dewa, Tanaka ga modori shidai, odenwa sasemasu. Well, when Tanaka returns, I will get him to call you. ठीक है, तनाका के लौटते ही, मैं उनसे आपको फ़ोन करवाऊँगा। ઠીક છે, તનાકા પાછા આવતાં જ, હું તેમની પાસે તમને ફોન કરાવીશ.
決まり次第、ご連絡いたします。Kimari shidai, gorenraku itashimasu. As soon as it is decided, I will contact you. तय होते ही, मैं आपसे संपर्क करूँगा। નક્કી થતાં જ, હું તમારો સંપર્ક કરીશ.

Since this describes an immediate, future-facing follow-up, it always attaches to the ます-stem — never the past た-form. That's a reliable test: if the sentence describes something that already happened, 次第 (this sense) is the wrong choice.

【4】 〜次第です

neutral register
Reading~shidai desu
Meaning (EN)"that's the reason I am doing ~ / that's why I came to do ~" — a polite, formal way of explaining the background/reason for one's current action
Meaning (HI)इसी वजह से मैं ...कर रहा हूँ — यह बताना कि किसी काम के पीछे क्या कारण है (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)એટલા માટે જ હું ...કરી રહ્યો છું — કોઈ કામ પાછળનું કારણ સમજાવવાની ઔપચારિક રીત
Connection (接続)Vる／Vた／Vている + 次第です
RegisterFormal, polite — common in business greetings and letters
Typical useExplains the background reason behind the speaker's current visit, message, or action — a very formal equivalent of 〜わけです.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (present/past/progressive)Vる／Vた／Vている + 次第です伺った次第ですukagatta shidai desu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
このたび担当が替わりましたので、あいさつに伺った次第です。Kono tabi tantou ga kawarimashita node, aisatsu ni ukagatta shidai desu. I am here to greet you because I'm the new person in charge. इस बार ज़िम्मेदारी बदली गई है, इसीलिए मैं मिलने आया हूँ। આ વખતે જવાબદારી બદલાઈ છે, એટલે જ હું મળવા આવ્યો છું.
日時の変更について、改めてお知らせする次第です。Nichiji no henkou ni tsuite, aratamete oshirase suru shidai desu. I will inform you later of any changes in the time and the place of the meeting. समय व तारीख़ में बदलाव के बारे में, मैं फिर से आपको सूचित कर रहा हूँ। સમય અને તારીખમાં ફેરફાર વિશે, હું ફરીથી તમને જાણ કરી રહ્યો છું.

Don't confuse this with point 3's 次第 (V-stem + 次第, "as soon as"). Point 4 attaches to a full verb (plain form, any tense) and ends the sentence with です — it's a closing explanation, not a time marker. It's especially common in business emails: 〜のご報告をする次第です, 〜ご連絡する次第です.

Q1

GU: તાજેતરની બેઠકની વિગત વિશે, હું ટૂંક સમયમાં લેખિતમાં અહેવાલ આપી રહ્યો છું.

aご報告のgohoukoku no(wrong — 次第です needs a verb, not a bare noun)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

このような＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 次第です。Kono you na ___ shidai desu.

Tiles: 1 事情から (jijou kara)　2 する (suru)　3 利用規則を (riyou kisoku o)　4 改正 (kaisei)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

1 事情から→3 利用規則を→4 改正→2 する

このような事情から利用規則を改正する次第です。Kono you na jijou kara riyou kisoku o kaisei suru shidai desu.

EN: This is why (given such circumstances) we are revising the usage rules.
HI: इन्हीं परिस्थितियों के चलते, हम उपयोग नियमों में संशोधन कर रहे हैं।
GU: આવા સંજોગોને કારણે, અમે ઉપયોગના નિયમોમાં સુધારો કરી રહ્યા છીએ.

Why: Built on point 4 (〜次第です) — explains the background reason (事情から) behind the action (規則を改正する) in a formal, business-letter tone.

Q7

このアパートは女性対象というわけではないが、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ だ。Kono apaato wa josei taishou to iu wake dewa nai ga, ___ da.

Tiles: 1 といえば (to ieba)　2 どちらか (dochira ka)　3 向き (muki)　4 女性 (josei)

2 どちらか→1 といえば→4 女性→3 向き

このアパートは女性対象というわけではないが、どちらかといえば女性向きだ。Kono apaato wa josei taishou to iu wake dewa nai ga, dochira ka to ieba josei-muki da.

EN: This apartment isn't exclusively for women, but if anything, it's better suited to women.
HI: यह अपार्टमेंट सिर्फ़ महिलाओं के लिए नहीं है, लेकिन अगर कहा जाए तो यह महिलाओं के लिए ज़्यादा उपयुक्त है।
GU: આ એપાર્ટમેન્ટ ફક્ત મહિલાઓ માટે નથી, પણ જો કહેવું હોય તો તે મહિલાઓ માટે વધુ અનુકૂળ છે.

Why: Built on point 1's 〜向き (a natural, not necessarily intentional, fit) — どちらかといえば ("if anything") softens the claim into a mild preference.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of today's four points share the reading 次第, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1–Q3 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜向け／向き~muke / ~muki Made for N / suited to N N + 向け／向き 向け = deliberately targeted at N; 向き = happens to suit N naturally. Describing a designed audience vs. a natural fit.
〜次第で~shidai de Depending on ~ N (no の) + 次第で The outcome is contingent on N — a condition, not a time marker. "It all depends on…" — weather, mood, circumstances.
〜次第~shidai As soon as ~ V-ます-stem + 次第 A time marker — the very moment V happens; never for past events. "The instant this happens, I will…"
〜次第です~shidai desu That's the reason I am doing ~ V (plain, any tense) + 次第です A closing explanation of background/reason — not a time or condition marker at all. Formal letters and business greetings, explaining "why I'm doing this."
Exam trap: The three 次第 forms all sound alike but attach differently and mean different things — 次第で needs a bare noun (condition), 次第 (as soon as) needs a ます-stem (timing), and 次第です needs a full verb ending a sentence (reason). When you see 次第 in a blank, first check what comes right before it: a bare noun → 次第で; a ます-stem mid-sentence → 次第 ("as soon as"); a complete verb at the very end → 次第です.
