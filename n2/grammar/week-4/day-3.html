3日目 — 期待にこたえて (Kitai ni kotaete)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.66–67 (PDF pages 66–67).
Grammar points covered
〜にこたえて〜に対して〜により〜にかかわって
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.69 (4日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points today attach with に and describe how one thing relates to, responds to, or is caused by another — see the Confusion Pairs section for the distinctions. 〜により is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other three are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜にこたえて 《に応えて》

neutral register
Reading~ni kotaete
Meaning (EN)"in response to ~" — acting so as to fulfil a request, hope, or expectation
Meaning (HI)...के जवाब में / ...की उम्मीद पर खरा उतरते हुए
Meaning (GU)...ના જવાબમાં / ...ની અપેક્ષા પર ખરું ઉતરીને
Connection (接続)Nに + こたえて／こたえ／こたえるN
RegisterNeutral
Typical useMarks that an action is done specifically to satisfy someone's request, hope, or expectation.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + こたえて客の意見にこたえてkyaku no iken ni kotaete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
客の意見にこたえて、営業時間を延長する。Kyaku no iken ni kotaete, eigyou jikan o enchou suru. In response to our customers' requests, we have extended our business hours. ग्राहकों की राय के जवाब में, हमने कारोबार का समय बढ़ा दिया है। ગ્રાહકોના મંતવ્યના જવાબમાં, અમે વેપારનો સમય વધાર્યો છે.
親の期待にこたえ、がんばった。Oya no kitai ni kotae, ganbatta. So that I wouldn't disappoint my parents, I did my best. माता-पिता की उम्मीदों पर खरा उतरने के लिए, मैंने पूरी कोशिश की। માતાપિતાની અપેક્ષાઓ પર ખરું ઉતરવા માટે, મેં પૂરી કોશિશ કરી.

Common set phrases: 希望にこたえて, 要求にこたえて, リクエストにこたえて — always something that was hoped for or requested, then fulfilled. Note the fixed phrase 期待に応える ("to live up to expectations") shows up again on Day 7's test.

【2】 〜に対して

neutral register
Reading~ni taishite
Meaning (EN)"toward ~ / regarding ~" — marks the target/direction of an attitude or action; with のに, "in contrast to ~"
Meaning (HI)...की ओर / ...के प्रति; (のに के साथ) ...के विपरीत
Meaning (GU)...ની તરફ / ...પ્રત્યે; (のに સાથે) ...થી ઊલટું
Connection (接続)Nに + 対して／対し／対しては／対しても／対するN　|　[文]のに／これに／それに + 対して…
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal
Typical usePoints an attitude/action at its target (respect, complaint, question); with 〜のに対し it draws a contrast between two opposite trends.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + 対して目上の人に対してmeue no hito ni taishite
Clause + のに[文]のに + 対し増えているのに対しfuete iru no ni taishi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
目上の人に対して、そういう言い方は失礼ですよ。Meue no hito ni taishite, sou iu iikata wa shitsurei desu yo. It is rude to speak to your superiors that way. बड़ों के प्रति उस तरह बोलना असभ्यता है। વડીલો પ્રત્યે એ રીતે બોલવું અસભ્ય છે.
都市で人口が増えているのに対し、農村では減っている。Toshi de jinkou ga fuete iru no ni taishi, nouson de wa hette iru. In contrast to the rising population in the cities, the population in the countryside is falling. शहरों में जनसंख्या बढ़ रही है, इसके विपरीत गाँवों में यह घट रही है। શહેરોમાં વસ્તી વધી રહી છે, તેનાથી ઊલટું ગામડાંઓમાં તે ઘટી રહી છે.

Two jobs for one form: plain 〜に対して points at a target (人に対して), while 〜のに対し contrasts two clauses that move in opposite directions (増えているのに対し…減っている). Context tells you which job it's doing.

【3】 〜により

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni yori
Meaning (EN)"by / due to ~" — a formal way to state a method, basis, cause, or circumstance; the formal register of 〜によって
Meaning (HI)...के द्वारा / ...के कारण (औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)...દ્વારા / ...ના કારણે (ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)Nに + より／よるN
Register硬 — a formal version of 〜によって; expresses method, basis, cause, or circumstance
Typical useLegal/formal writing: laws, official notices, cause-and-effect statements in a serious register.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (adverbial)Nに + より法律によりhouritsu ni yori
Noun (attributive)Nに + よるN地震による被害jishin ni yoru higai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
未成年者の飲酒は、法律により禁じられている。Miseinensha no inshu wa, houritsu ni yori kinjirarete iru. The consumption of alcohol by minors is prohibited by the law. नाबालिगों द्वारा शराब पीना क़ानून द्वारा प्रतिबंधित है। સગીરો દ્વારા દારૂ પીવો કાયદા દ્વારા પ્રતિબંધિત છે.
その地震による被害は、過去最大だった。Sono jishin ni yoru higai wa, kako saidai datta. The damage and number of casualties due to the earthquake were the largest in history. उस भूकंप से हुआ नुक़सान इतिहास में सबसे बड़ा था। તે ધરતીકંપથી થયેલું નુકસાન ઇતિહાસમાં સૌથી મોટું હતું.

に より (adverbial, before a verb: 法律により禁じられている) and に よる + N (attributive, directly before a noun: 地震による被害) are just two shapes of the same word — pick より before a verb, よる before a noun.

【4】 〜にかかわって 《に関わって》

neutral register
Reading~ni kakawatte
Meaning (EN)"connected to / involved with ~" — being related to or implicated in N; also used for something affecting a very important matter (life, pride, etc.)
Meaning (HI)...से जुड़ा/संबंधित होना — किसी महत्वपूर्ण चीज़ (जान, इज़्ज़त) को प्रभावित करने के लिए भी
Meaning (GU)...સાથે જોડાયેલું/સંબંધિત હોવું — જીવ, પ્રતિષ્ઠા જેવી મહત્વની બાબતને અસર કરવા માટે પણ
Connection (接続)Nに + かかわって／かかわり／かかわる。／かかわるN
RegisterNeutral
Typical useDescribes involvement or relatedness — often with a negative undertone (implicated in a scandal) or with high-stakes nouns like 命 (life) or 誇り (pride).
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + かかわって汚職事件にかかわってoshoku jiken ni kakawatte
Noun (attributive)Nに + かかわるN命にかかわる病気inochi ni kakawaru byouki
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は汚職事件にかかわって逮捕された。Kare wa oshoku jiken ni kakawatte taiho sareta. He was arrested for being involved in a corruption case. वह भ्रष्टाचार के मामले में शामिल होने के कारण गिरफ़्तार हुआ। તે ભ્રષ્ટાચારના કેસમાં સંડોવાયેલો હોવાથી ધરપકડ પામ્યો.
息子はマスコミにかかわる仕事をしている。Musuko wa masukomi ni kakawaru shigoto o shite iru. My son works in the mass media. मेरा बेटा मीडिया से जुड़ा काम करता है। મારો દીકરો મીડિયા સાથે સંકળાયેલું કામ કરે છે.
命にかかわる病気Inochi ni kakawaru byouki a life-threatening illness जानलेवा बीमारी જીવલેણ બીમારી

The book's own sidebar flags a second sense worth memorizing: with weighty nouns like 命 (life) or 誇り (pride), にかかわる shifts from "connected to" toward "affecting/threatening" — 命にかかわる ≈ "life-threatening," not just "life-related."

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, circle the correct option) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.67. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.69.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

今朝の人身事故（　）ダイヤが乱れております。Kesa no jinshin jiko (___) daiya ga midarete orimasu.

Full sentence: 今朝の人身事故によりダイヤが乱れております。Kesa no jinshin jiko ni yori daiya ga midarete orimasu.
EN: Due to this morning's fatal accident, train schedules are disrupted.
HI: आज सुबह हुई दुर्घटना के कारण, ट्रेन का समय-सारिणी बिगड़ी हुई है।
GU: આજ સવારે થયેલા અકસ્માતના કારણે, ટ્રેનનું સમયપત્રક ખોરવાયું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aによるni yoru(wrong — attributive form, but here it must connect adverbially to a verb clause)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)
bによりni yoridue to (point 3, adverbial)के कारणના કારણે

Why: Here により leads straight into a verb clause (ダイヤが乱れております), so the adverbial により is needed, not the attributive による (which would need to sit directly before a noun).

Q2

国民の政治（　）不満は年々高まっている。Kokumin no seiji (___) fuman wa nen'nen takamatte iru.

Full sentence: 国民の政治に対する不満は年々高まっている。Kokumin no seiji ni taisuru fuman wa nen'nen takamatte iru.
EN: The public's dissatisfaction toward politics is growing year by year.
HI: राजनीति के प्रति जनता का असंतोष साल-दर-साल बढ़ रहा है।
GU: રાજકારણ પ્રત્યે લોકોનો અસંતોષ વર્ષ-દર-વર્ષ વધી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aに対するni taisurutoward, directed at (point 2, attributive)के प्रतिપ્રત્યે
bに応えるni kotaeru(wrong — "responding to politics," not the target of dissatisfaction)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: 不満 (dissatisfaction) needs a target it's directed at — 〜に対する不満 ("dissatisfaction toward ~"), point 2. 応える would mean "responding to politics," which doesn't fit a feeling of dissatisfaction.

Q3

人々の期待（　）、その選手は金メダルをとった。Hitobito no kitai (___), sono senshu wa kinmedaru o totta.

Full sentence: 人々の期待にこたえて、その選手は金メダルをとった。Hitobito no kitai ni kotaete, sono senshu wa kinmedaru o totta.
EN: In response to people's expectations, that athlete won the gold medal.
HI: लोगों की उम्मीदों पर खरा उतरते हुए, उस खिलाड़ी ने स्वर्ण पदक जीता।
GU: લોકોની અપેક્ષાઓ પર ખરું ઉતરીને, તે ખેલાડીએ સુવર્ણ ચંદ્રક જીત્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにこたえてni kotaetein response to, living up to (point 1)पर खरा उतरते हुएપર ખરું ઉતરીને
bにかかわってni kakawatte(wrong — "being involved with expectations" doesn't fit the meaning here)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: Fulfilling people's expectations by winning the gold medal is exactly point 1's にこたえて — a hope was raised, and the action satisfies it.

Q4

子どもが減っているのに（　）、老人が増えている。Kodomo ga hette iru no ni (___), roujin ga fuete iru.

Full sentence: 子どもが減っているのに対して、老人が増えている。Kodomo ga hette iru no ni taishite, roujin ga fuete iru.
EN: In contrast to the decreasing number of children, the elderly population is increasing.
HI: बच्चों की संख्या घट रही है, इसके विपरीत बुज़ुर्गों की संख्या बढ़ रही है।
GU: બાળકોની સંખ્યા ઘટી રહી છે, તેનાથી ઊલટું વૃદ્ધોની સંખ્યા વધી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a対してtaishitein contrast to (point 2, のに対して)के विपरीतથી ઊલટું
bこたえてkotaete(wrong — no request/hope is being fulfilled here)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: Two opposite trends (children decreasing, elderly increasing) — that's the contrastive のに対して pattern from point 2, not point 1's "responding to a hope."

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

彼が何らかの形でその事件と＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 疑いが持たれている。Kare ga nanraka no katachi de sono jiken to ___ utagai ga motarete iru.

Tiles: 1 という (to iu)　2 関わって (kakawatte)　3 ないか (nai ka)　4 いるのでは (iru no de wa)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

2 関わって→4 いるのでは→3 ないか→1 という

彼が何らかの形でその事件と関わっているのではないかという疑いが持たれている。Kare ga nanraka no katachi de sono jiken to kakawatte iru no de wa nai ka to iu utagai ga motarete iru.

EN: There are suspicions that he may somehow be involved with that incident.
HI: ऐसा शक जताया जा रहा है कि वह किसी न किसी रूप में उस घटना से जुड़ा हो सकता है।
GU: એવી શંકા વ્યક્ત કરવામાં આવી રહી છે કે તે કોઈક રીતે તે ઘટના સાથે સંકળાયેલો હોઈ શકે.

Why: Built on point 4 (〜に関わって) — the suspicion (疑い) is that he is "involved with" (関わっている) the incident, wrapped in a tentative "…ではないかという" (a suspicion that maybe…).

Q7

このコースの成績は出席、宿題、各課の試験と＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ されます。Kono koosu no seiseki wa shusseki, shukudai, kakuka no shiken to ___ saremasu.

Tiles: 1 総合的に (sougouteki ni)　2 最終試験 (saishuu shiken)　3 によって (ni yotte)　4 評価 (hyouka)

2 最終試験→3 によって→1 総合的に→4 評価

このコースの成績は出席、宿題、各課の試験と最終試験によって総合的に評価されます。Kono koosu no seiseki wa shusseki, shukudai, kakuka no shiken to saishuu shiken ni yotte sougouteki ni hyouka saremasu.

EN: This course's grade is comprehensively evaluated based on attendance, homework, each unit's test, and the final exam.
HI: इस कोर्स के अंक उपस्थिति, गृहकार्य, हर पाठ की परीक्षा और अंतिम परीक्षा के आधार पर समग्र रूप से मूल्यांकित किए जाते हैं।
GU: આ કોર્સના ગુણ હાજરી, ગૃહકાર્ય, દરેક પાઠની કસોટી અને અંતિમ પરીક્ષાના આધારે સંપૂર્ણ રીતે મૂલ્યાંકિત કરવામાં આવે છે.

Why: This uses によって (a cousin of point 3's により, both meaning "by/based on") to state the evaluation method, with 評価されます ("is evaluated") as the final passive verb.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today attach with に and describe some kind of relationship between two things, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q2, Q3, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にこたえて~ni kotaete In response to ~ Nに + こたえて Fulfils a hope, request, or expectation. Someone asked/hoped for something, and you deliver it.
〜に対して~ni taishite Toward ~ / in contrast to ~ Nに + 対して　|　[文]のに + 対し Points an attitude/action at a target, or contrasts two opposite clauses. Marking a target of a feeling, or two facts moving in opposite directions.
〜により~ni yori By / due to ~ Nに + より（adverbial）／よるN（attributive） 硬 — states a method, basis, or cause in formal register (= によって). Legal/formal statements of cause or method.
〜にかかわって~ni kakawatte Connected to / involved with ~ Nに + かかわって／かかわるN Describes relatedness/involvement — can shift to "affecting" with high-stakes nouns (命, 誇り). Being implicated in something, or something threatening a vital matter.
Exam trap: にこたえて and にかかわって can both loosely translate as "in relation to X" in a rough English gloss, but they are not interchangeable — にこたえて requires a prior request/hope/expectation being satisfied (Q3 above), while にかかわって just states an association or involvement, often neutral or negative (Q5 above). If nothing was "hoped for," にこたえて is wrong.
