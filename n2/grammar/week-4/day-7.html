問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

受験に（　）、次の点に注意してください。Juken ni (___), tsugi no ten ni chuui shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 受験にあたり、次の点に注意してください。Juken ni atari, tsugi no ten ni chuui shite kudasai.
EN: When taking the exam, please pay attention to the following points.
HI: परीक्षा देते समय, कृपया निम्नलिखित बातों पर ध्यान दें।
GU: પરીક્ષા આપતી વખતે, કૃપા કરી નીચેની બાબતો પર ધ્યાન આપો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1次第でshidai dedepending on (wrong — no condition here)
2そってsottein line with (wrong — no guideline given)
3わたりwatariover the span of (wrong — an exam is a single occasion, not a span)
4あたりatarion the occasion of, when about to (Day 6)

Why: Day 6's 〜にあたり marks the formal occasion of an important task — here, taking an exam.

Q2

B国から日本（　）に輸出される農産物の80%が果物である。B-koku kara Nihon (___) ni yushutsu sareru nousanbutsu no hachijuppaasento ga kudamono de aru.

Full sentence: B国から日本向けに輸出される農産物の80%が果物である。B-koku kara Nihon-muke ni yushutsu sareru nousanbutsu no hachijuppaasento ga kudamono de aru.
EN: 80% of the agricultural products exported from Country B to the Japanese market are fruit.
HI: देश B से जापान के बाज़ार के लिए निर्यात किए जाने वाले कृषि उत्पादों में से 80% फल हैं।
GU: દેશ B માંથી જાપાનના બજાર માટે નિકાસ થતાં કૃષિ ઉત્પાદનોમાંથી 80% ફળો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1向きmukisuited to (wrong — this is a deliberate export target, not a natural fit)
2向けmukeaimed/targeted at (Day 2)
3向かいmukai(not a valid fit — a different verb, "facing")
4向こうmukou(not a valid fit — means "the other side/beyond")

Why: Day 2's N向け describes goods deliberately targeted at a market — 日本向け, "for the Japanese market."

Q3

新しい情報が（　）次第、お知らせします。Atarashii jouhou ga (___) shidai, oshirase shimasu.

Full sentence: 新しい情報が入り次第、お知らせします。Atarashii jouhou ga hairi shidai, oshirase shimasu.
EN: As soon as new information comes in, I will let you know.
HI: नई जानकारी मिलते ही, मैं आपको बता दूँगा।
GU: નવી માહિતી મળતાં જ, હું તમને જણાવીશ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1入りhairias soon as (it) comes in (Day 2, ます-stem)
2入るhairu(wrong — 次第 here needs the ます-stem, not the dictionary form)
3入ってhaitte(wrong shape for this pattern)
4入ったhaitta(wrong — 次第 (as soon as) can't attach to the past tense)

Why: Day 2's 〜次第 ("as soon as") needs the ます-stem — 入り次第, not 入る次第 or 入った次第.

Q4

この料理は、作り方が簡単な（　）材料費も安い。Kono ryouri wa, tsukurikata ga kantan na (___) zairyouhi mo yasui.

Full sentence: この料理は、作り方が簡単な上に材料費も安い。Kono ryouri wa, tsukurikata ga kantan na ue ni zairyouhi mo yasui.
EN: Not only is this dish's method of preparation simple, but the ingredient cost is also low.
HI: यह पकवान न सिर्फ़ बनाना आसान है, बल्कि सामग्री की लागत भी कम है।
GU: આ વાનગી ફક્ત બનાવવી સહેલી જ નથી, પણ સામગ્રીનો ખર્ચ પણ ઓછો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1上にue ninot only ~, but also (Day 1)
2上でue de(wrong — implies a deliberate first step, not a stacked fact)
3上はue wa(wrong — no determination/rule follows)
4上ではue de wa(wrong — not a "on paper vs. reality" contrast)

Why: Two matching positive facts stack here (simple to make, cheap ingredients) — Day 1's 〜上に.

Q5

彼女は彼の突然の結婚の申し込みに驚き（　）、うれしそうだった。Kanojo wa kare no totsuzen no kekkon no moushikomi ni odoroki (___), ureshisou datta.

Full sentence: 彼女は彼の突然の結婚の申し込みに驚きながらも、うれしそうだった。Kanojo wa kare no totsuzen no kekkon no moushikomi ni odoroki nagara mo, ureshisou datta.
EN: Though surprised by his sudden marriage proposal, she seemed happy.
HI: उसके अचानक शादी के प्रस्ताव से हैरान होते हुए भी, वह ख़ुश दिख रही थी।
GU: તેના અચાનક લગ્નના પ્રસ્તાવથી નવાઈ પામવા છતાં, તે ખુશ દેખાતી હતી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1もののmono no(not a natural fit for this connection)
2べきもbeki mo(not a valid form)
3ながらもnagara moeven though (Day 4)
4にわたりni watari(wrong — this isn't a span of time/area)

Why: Day 4's concessive 〜ながらも — being surprised doesn't cancel out being happy; both are true at once.

Q6

会議（　）、参加者へのアンケート調査が行われた。Kaigi (___), sankasha e no ankeeto chousa ga okonawareta.

Full sentence: 会議に先立ち、参加者へのアンケート調査が行われた。Kaigi ni sakidachi, sankasha e no ankeeto chousa ga okonawareta.
EN: Prior to the meeting, a survey was conducted among the participants.
HI: बैठक से पहले, प्रतिभागियों के बीच एक सर्वेक्षण किया गया।
GU: બેઠક પહેલાં, સહભાગીઓ વચ્ચે એક સર્વે કરવામાં આવ્યો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1に先立ちni sakidachiprior to (Day 6)
2にわたりni watari(wrong — not a span)
3に沿いni soi(wrong — no guideline being followed)
4に反しni hanshi(wrong — "contrary to," not "before")

Why: The survey happens before the meeting, in preparation for it — Day 6's に先立ち.

Q7

数字（　）では景気は回復しているのだが、私たちにはその実感はない。Suuji (___) de wa keiki wa kaifuku shite iru no da ga, watashitachi ni wa sono jikkan wa nai.

Full sentence: 数字の上では景気は回復しているのだが、私たちにはその実感はない。Suuji no ue de wa keiki wa kaifuku shite iru no da ga, watashitachi ni wa sono jikkan wa nai.
EN: On paper (looking at the numbers), the economy is recovering, but we don't feel it.
HI: आँकड़ों के हिसाब से तो अर्थव्यवस्था सुधर रही है, लेकिन हमें इसका एहसास नहीं होता।
GU: આંકડાઓ પ્રમાણે તો અર્થતંત્ર સુધરી રહ્યું છે, પણ અમને તેનો અહેસાસ થતો નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ぬきnuki(not a valid fit — "excluding")
2の上no ueaccording to, on paper (Day 1)
3のわりno wari(not a valid fit here)
4向けmuke(wrong — "targeted at numbers" makes no sense)

Why: "On paper the economy recovers, but it doesn't feel that way" is exactly Day 1's contrast pattern, 数字の上では…実感はない.

Q8

状況が同じでも、考え方（　）幸せにも不幸にもなる。Joukyou ga onaji demo, kangaekata (___) shiawase ni mo fukou ni mo naru.

Full sentence: 状況が同じでも、考え方次第で幸せにも不幸にもなる。Joukyou ga onaji demo, kangaekata shidai de shiawase ni mo fukou ni mo naru.
EN: Even in the same situation, one can become happy or unhappy depending on how one thinks.
HI: हालात एक जैसे होने पर भी, सोचने के तरीक़े पर निर्भर करते हुए इंसान ख़ुश या दुखी हो सकता है।
GU: પરિસ્થિતિ સરખી હોવા છતાં, વિચારવાની રીત પ્રમાણે માણસ ખુશ કે દુ:ખી બની શકે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1向きでmuki de(wrong — not a "suited to" relationship)
2にそってni sotte(wrong — no fixed guideline being followed)
3次第でshidai dedepending on (Day 2)
4にあたりni atari(wrong — not a single formal occasion)

Why: The outcome (happy or unhappy) is entirely contingent on the variable (how one thinks) — Day 2's 次第で.

Q9

この問題集は、実際の試験形式（　）作られている。Kono mondaishuu wa, jissai no shiken keishiki (___) tsukurarete iru.

Full sentence: この問題集は、実際の試験形式にそって作られている。Kono mondaishuu wa, jissai no shiken keishiki ni sotte tsukurarete iru.
EN: This workbook is made in line with the actual exam format.
HI: यह अभ्यास-पुस्तिका असली परीक्षा के प्रारूप के अनुसार बनाई गई है।
GU: આ પ્રેક્ટિસ પુસ્તક ખરેખરની પરીક્ષાના ફોર્મેટ મુજબ બનાવવામાં આવી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にあたってni atatte(wrong — not a single occasion)
2にそってni sottein line with (Day 6)
3にわたってni watatte(wrong — a format isn't a span/period)
4に先立ってni sakidatte(wrong — nothing is happening "before" the format)

Why: The workbook closely follows an existing format — Day 6's にそって.

Q10

これは6年間（　）観測データをまとめたものです。Kore wa rokunenkan (___) kansoku deeta o matometa mono desu.

Full sentence: これは6年間にわたる観測データをまとめたものです。Kore wa rokunenkan ni wataru kansoku deeta o matometa mono desu.
EN: This is a compilation of observation data spanning six years.
HI: यह छह सालों तक फैले हुए अवलोकन आँकड़ों का संकलन है।
GU: આ છ વર્ષ સુધી ફેલાયેલા અવલોકન માહિતીનું સંકલન છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1に先立つni sakidatsu(wrong — not "before" anything)
2に関するni kansuru(wrong — "related to six years" is odd)
3にそうni sou(wrong — no guideline to follow)
4にわたるni wataruspanning the whole of (Day 6, attributive)

Why: "Six years" is a full period being covered — Day 6's attributive にわたるN.

Q11

実物を（　）上で、買うかどうか決めます。Jitsubutsu o (___) ue de, kau ka douka kimemasu.

Full sentence: 実物を見た上で、買うかどうか決めます。Jitsubutsu o mita ue de, kau ka douka kimemasu.
EN: After actually seeing the item in person, I will decide whether to buy it.
HI: असली चीज़ को देखने के बाद ही, मैं तय करूँगा कि ख़रीदना है या नहीं।
GU: ખરેખરી વસ્તુ જોયા પછી જ, હું નક્કી કરીશ કે ખરીદવું છે કે નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
1見るmiru(wrong — 上で needs the past た-form)
2見てmite(wrong shape for this pattern)
3見たmitaafter doing (deliberately), based on that (Day 1)
4見ないminai(wrong — contradicts the deliberate-viewing meaning)

Why: Day 1's 〜上で needs the た-form — 見た上で, "after having looked."

Q12

子どもには勉強しろと（　）つつ、大人が勉強しないのはよくないと思う。Kodomo ni wa benkyou shiro to (___) tsutsu, otona ga benkyou shinai no wa yokunai to omou.

Full sentence: 子どもには勉強しろと言いつつ、大人が勉強しないのはよくないと思う。Kodomo ni wa benkyou shiro to ii tsutsu, otona ga benkyou shinai no wa yokunai to omou.
EN: While telling children to study, I think it's not good that adults themselves don't study.
HI: बच्चों को पढ़ाई करने को कहते हुए भी, बड़ों का ख़ुद न पढ़ना अच्छा नहीं लगता।
GU: બાળકોને અભ્યાસ કરવાનું કહેતાં છતાં, વડીલોનું જાતે અભ્યાસ ન કરવું સારું નથી લાગતું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1言うiu(wrong — つつ needs the ます-stem)
2言いiiwhile saying, even though saying (Day 4, ます-stem)
3言ってitte(wrong shape for this pattern)
4言ったitta(wrong — つつ never attaches to the past tense)

Why: Day 4's concessive 〜つつ needs the ます-stem — 言いつつ, "even while saying."

Q13

昔からの習慣で、女性はこの中に入ってはいけない（　）。Mukashi kara no shuukan de, josei wa kono naka ni haitte wa ikenai (___).

Full sentence: 昔からの習慣で、女性はこの中に入ってはいけないことになっている。Mukashi kara no shuukan de, josei wa kono naka ni haitte wa ikenai koto ni natte iru.
EN: By long-standing custom, it is the rule that women may not enter this place.
HI: पुरानी परंपरा के अनुसार, यह तय है कि महिलाओं का इस जगह में प्रवेश वर्जित है।
GU: જૂની પરંપરા મુજબ, એવું નક્કી છે કે મહિલાઓનો આ જગ્યામાં પ્રવેશ પ્રતિબંધિત છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1つつあるtsutsu aru(wrong — no gradual change described)
2べきであるbeki de aru(wrong — this states a longstanding rule, not a personal judgment)
3ことになっているkoto ni natte iruit is the established rule that (Day 5)
4わけにはいかないwake ni wa ikanai(wrong — this describes an external custom, not the speaker's own constraint)

Why: "By long-standing custom" signals an established external rule — Day 5's ことになっている.

Q14

日本の美しい伝統は、日本人に（　）。Nihon no utsukushii dentou wa, nihonjin ni (___).

Full sentence: 日本の美しい伝統は、日本人に忘れられつつある。Nihon no utsukushii dentou wa, nihonjin ni wasurerarete tsutsu aru.
EN: Japan's beautiful traditions are gradually being forgotten by the Japanese people.
HI: जापान की ख़ूबसूरत परंपराएँ, जापानी लोगों द्वारा धीरे-धीरे भुलाई जा रही हैं।
GU: જાપાનની સુંદર પરંપરાઓ, જાપાની લોકો દ્વારા ધીમે ધીમે ભુલાઈ રહી છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1忘れざるをえないwasurezaru o enai(wrong — this would mean "have no choice but to forget," an odd fit here)
2忘れられつつあるwasurerarete tsutsu aruis gradually being forgotten (Day 4)
3忘れることになっているwasureru koto ni natte iru(wrong — traditions aren't a scheduled rule)
4忘れるにすぎないwasureru ni suginai(wrong — "is nothing more than forgetting" doesn't fit)

Why: A slow, ongoing societal change (traditions fading) is exactly Day 4's つつある, in its passive form 忘れられつつある.

Q15

確かに、今この商品は人気があるが、一時的な流行（　）。Tashika ni, ima kono shouhin wa ninki ga aru ga, ichijiteki na ryuukou (___).

Full sentence: 確かに、今この商品は人気があるが、一時的な流行にすぎない。Tashika ni, ima kono shouhin wa ninki ga aru ga, ichijiteki na ryuukou ni suginai.
EN: Certainly, this product is popular right now, but it's nothing more than a temporary trend.
HI: यह सच है कि यह उत्पाद अभी लोकप्रिय है, लेकिन यह सिर्फ़ एक अस्थायी चलन है, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं।
GU: એ સાચું છે કે આ ઉત્પાદન અત્યારે લોકપ્રિય છે, પણ તે ફક્ત એક કામચલાઉ ચલણ છે, તેનાથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
1次第だshidai da(wrong — no condition being described)
2にすぎないni suginaiis nothing more than (Day 5)
3せざるをえないsezaru o enai(wrong — a trend being popular isn't a forced action)
4であるわけではないde aru wake de wa nai(wrong — that would mean "isn't necessarily," the opposite implication)

Why: Downplaying the popularity as merely temporary is Day 5's にすぎない.

Q16

うそはつきたくないから、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ えない。Uso wa tsukitakunai kara, ___ ★ ___ enai.

Tiles: 1 たら (tara)　2 ざるを (zaru o)　3 真実を話さ (shinjitsu o hanasa)　4 聞かれ (kikare)

4 聞かれ→1 たら→★ 3 真実を話さ→2 ざるを

うそはつきたくないから、聞かれたら真実を話さざるをえない。Uso wa tsukitakunai kara, kikaretara shinjitsu o hanasazaru o enai.

EN: Since I don't want to lie, if I'm asked, I have no choice but to tell the truth.
HI: चूँकि मैं झूठ नहीं बोलना चाहता, अगर मुझसे पूछा गया तो सच बताने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं होगा।
GU: કારણ કે હું જૂઠું બોલવા માંગતો નથી, જો મને પુછાય તો સાચું કહેવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નહીં હોય.

Why: Built on Day 5's 〜ざるをえない — if asked (聞かれたら), the speaker has no choice but to tell the truth.

Q17

けがのせいで、大好きなテニスがもうできなくなってしまった。残念だが＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 趣味を探そう。Kega no sei de, daisuki na tenisu ga mou dekinaku natte shimatta. Zannen da ga ___ ★ ___ shumi o sagasou.

Tiles: 1 何か (nanika)　2 ほかの (hoka no)　3 上は (ue wa)　4 こうなった (kou natta)

4 こうなった→3 上は→★ 1 何か→2 ほかの

けがのせいで、大好きなテニスがもうできなくなってしまった。残念だがこうなった上は何かほかの趣味を探そう。...zannen da ga kou natta ue wa nanika hoka no shumi o sagasou.

EN: Because of my injury, I can no longer play the tennis I love. It's a shame, but now that this has happened, let's look for some other hobby.
HI: चोट लगने के कारण, मुझे बहुत पसंद टेनिस अब खेलना मुमकिन नहीं रहा। अफ़सोस की बात है, लेकिन अब जब ऐसा हो गया है, तो कोई और शौक़ ढूँढते हैं।
