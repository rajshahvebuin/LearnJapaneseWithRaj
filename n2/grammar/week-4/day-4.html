1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ながら（も）

neutral register
Reading~nagara (mo)
Meaning (EN)"even though ~ / while ~ (is/does)" — a concessive use of ながら: despite one fact being true, the other clause happens anyway
Meaning (HI)...होते हुए भी / जानते हुए भी — एक बात सच होते हुए भी दूसरा काम हो जाता है
Meaning (GU)...હોવા છતાં / જાણવા છતાં — એક વાત સાચી હોવા છતાં બીજું કામ થઈ જાય છે
Connection (接続)N／な-adj／い-adj／V-ます-stem + ながら／ながらも
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA concessive contrast — "although X (is true), Y happens/is true anyway." Very common as 残念ながら ("regrettably") and 今さらながら ("even though it's a bit late now").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます-stem + ながら知りながらshiri nagara
い-adjectiveい-adj + ながらも狭いながらもsemai nagara mo
な-adjective(added, not spelled out on this spread) な-adj + ながら静かながらshizuka nagara
NounN + ながら敵ながらteki nagara
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
悪いことと知りながら、盗みを繰り返した。Warui koto to shiri nagara, nusumi o kurikaeshita. While I knew it was bad, I continued to steal. यह जानते हुए भी कि यह ग़लत है, मैं बार-बार चोरी करता रहा। ખબર હોવા છતાં કે આ ખોટું છે, હું વારંવાર ચોરી કરતો રહ્યો.
「狭いながらも楽しいわが家」という言葉がある。"Semai nagara mo tanoshii waga ya" to iu kotoba ga aru. There is a saying, "a small but happy home." "छोटा होते हुए भी सुखी घर" जैसी एक कहावत है। "નાનું હોવા છતાં સુખી ઘર" જેવી એક કહેવત છે.

Don't confuse this ながら with the "simultaneous action" ながら you already know (食べながら話す, "talk while eating") — here it always signals a contrast/concession, not two actions happening together. The concessive sense can attach to nouns directly, e.g. 敵ながら ("even though [they're] the enemy…").

【2】 〜つつ(も) ／ 〜つつ

硬 (concessive sense)
Reading~tsutsu (mo) / ~tsutsu
Meaning (EN)Two related senses: ❶ V-ます-stem+つつ(も) — a formal version of concessive ながら(も), "even though ~"; ❷ V-ます-stem+つつ — a formal version of simultaneous ながら, "while (doing) ~"
Meaning (HI)❶ ...होते हुए भी (औपचारिक ながら) ❷ ...करते हुए, साथ ही (औपचारिक "एक साथ")
Meaning (GU)❶ ...હોવા છતાં (ઔપચારિક ながら) ❷ ...કરતી વખતે, સાથે (ઔપચારિક "એકસાથે")
Connection (接続)❶ V-ます-stem + つつ(も) [硬い「ながら(も)」]　|　❷ V-ます-stem + つつ [「ながら（＝同時に）」の意味]
Register❶ 硬 — a formal written equivalent of ながら(も); ❷ neutral-to-formal, equivalent of simultaneous ながら
Typical use❶ Concedes one fact while the opposite happens anyway (say one is busy, but keep talking); ❷ Two actions happening at the same time, in more formal writing than ながら.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem, ❶ concessive)V-ます-stem + つつ(も)言いつつii tsutsu
Verb (ます-stem, ❷ simultaneous)V-ます-stem + つつ相談しつつsoudan shi tsutsu
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼女は忙しいと言いつつ、長電話をしている。Kanojo wa isogashii to ii tsutsu, nagadenwa o shite iru. Though she said she was busy, she continued to speak on the phone. वह व्यस्त होने की बात कहते हुए भी, लंबी फ़ोन बातचीत कर रही है। તે વ્યસ્ત હોવાનું કહેતાં છતાં, લાંબી ફોન વાત કરી રહી છે.
今日こそがんばろうと思いつつ、また勉強しなかった。Kyou koso ganbarou to omoi tsutsu, mata benkyou shinakatta. I was going to work hard today, but did not study again. आज ज़रूर मेहनत करूँगा ऐसा सोचते हुए भी, फिर भी पढ़ाई नहीं की। આજે ચોક્કસ મહેનત કરીશ એમ વિચારવા છતાં, ફરી અભ્યાસ ન કર્યો.
先生と相談しつつ、進路を決めたいと思う。Sensei to soudan shi tsutsu, shinro o kimetai to omou. I'd like to decide what to do after graduation in consultation with my teacher. शिक्षक से सलाह लेते हुए, मैं आगे की राह तय करना चाहता हूँ। શિક્ષક સાથે ચર્ચા કરતાં કરતાં, હું આગળનો રસ્તો નક્કી કરવા માંગું છું.

Same word, two jobs — check what the sentence needs. If the second clause contradicts or undercuts the first (said busy, but kept talking), it's the concessive ❶ sense (硬い「ながらも」). If the two actions genuinely happen together (consulting the teacher while deciding), it's the simultaneous ❷ sense — both are more formal/written than plain ながら.

【3】 〜つつある

neutral register
Reading~tsutsu aru
Meaning (EN)"is in the process of ~ing / is gradually ~ing" — describes a change that is currently, gradually underway
Meaning (HI)धीरे-धीरे ...हो रहा है — कोई बदलाव अभी जारी है
Meaning (GU)ધીમે ધીમે ...થઈ રહ્યું છે — કોઈ ફેરફાર હાલમાં ચાલુ છે
Connection (接続)V-ます-stem + つつある
RegisterNeutral, common in news/formal writing about ongoing change
Typical useA gradual, ongoing process — improving, spreading, changing — closer in feel to 〜ている used for a slow trend than a momentary action.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます-stem + つつある進歩しつつあるshinpo shi tsutsu aru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
医療はますます進歩しつつある。Iryou wa masumasu shinpo shi tsutsu aru. Medical care is progressing day by day. चिकित्सा दिन-प्रतिदिन उन्नति कर रही है। તબીબી સારવાર દિવસે ને દિવસે પ્રગતિ કરી રહી છે.
新種のウイルスによる被害は、全国に広がりつつある。Shinshu no uirusu ni yoru higai wa, zenkoku ni hirogari tsutsu aru. Damage caused by the new virus has been spreading across the nation. नई क़िस्म के वायरस से हुआ नुक़सान पूरे देश में फैल रहा है। નવા પ્રકારના વાયરસથી થયેલું નુકસાન સમગ્ર દેશમાં ફેલાઈ રહ્યું છે.

つつある always describes a gradual trend in progress, never a single instantaneous action — you can't use it for something like ドアが開きつつある in the sense of a door opening in one motion; it's for slow, ongoing change (progress, spread, recovery).

【4】 〜くせして

colloquial — marked with a speech-bubble icon (一) in the source (see note below)
Reading~kuse shite
Meaning (EN)"even though ~ / for someone/something that is only ~" — a conversational, critical equivalent of くせに, used to blame or criticize a mismatch between what someone is/knows and how they act
Meaning (HI)...होते हुए भी (व्यंग्य/शिकायत के लहज़े में) — किसी बात और व्यवहार में तालमेल न होने पर ताना
Meaning (GU)...હોવા છતાં (મહેણાં/ટીકાના સ્વરમાં) — કોઈ વાત અને વર્તન વચ્ચે તાલમેલ ન હોય ત્યારે મહેણું
Connection (接続)Nの／な-adj-な + くせして　|　V／い-adj (plain) + くせして
RegisterConversational — a spoken-style version of くせに, carrying a feeling of blame or criticism (非難の気持ち)
Typical usePointing out a contradiction between someone's status/knowledge and their behaviour, usually to scold or tease — "you're a university student and you don't even know that?"
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)V (plain) + くせして知らないくせしてshiranai kuse shite
NounNの + くせして大学生のくせしてdaigakusei no kuse shite
な-adjective(added, not spelled out with its own example on this spread) な-adj-な + くせして下手なくせしてheta na kuse shite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
知らないくせして、知っているようなことを言うな。Shiranai kuse shite, shitte iru you na koto o iu na. Don't talk as if you knew. जानते तो हो नहीं, फिर जानने वालों जैसी बातें मत करो। ખબર ન હોવા છતાં, ખબર હોય એવી વાતો ન કરો.
大学生のくせして、そんなことも知らないの？Daigakusei no kuse shite, sonna koto mo shiranai no? You're in university and you don't even know that? कॉलेज के छात्र होकर भी, तुम्हें यह भी नहीं पता? કૉલેજનો વિદ્યાર્થી હોવા છતાં, તને એ પણ ખબર નથી?

The source book prints a small speech-bubble icon labelled (一) next to this heading (the same visual style as a quotation/spoken-line marker) — read in context, it appears to flag that くせして is the spoken, everyday counterpart of the more neutral くせに, always carrying a note of blame. No formal legend for this icon appears in the pages processed so far, so this reading is contextual, not confirmed from a key.

Q1

宿題をしようと思いながら、まだしていない。Shukudai o shiyou to omoi nagara, mada shite inai.

EN: While I was thinking I should do my homework, I still haven't done it.
HI: यह सोचते हुए भी कि गृहकार्य करना है, अभी तक नहीं किया।
GU: ગૃહકાર્ય કરવાનું વિચારવા છતાં, હજુ સુધી કર્યું નથી.
VerdictEnglish
○ correctValid — concessive ながら (point 1) attaches cleanly to the ます-stem 思い.
× incorrect(not the case here)

Why: 思いながら is a textbook concessive ながら — "even though I meant to, I still haven't."

Q2

天気は回復に向かいつつある。Tenki wa kaifuku ni mukai tsutsu aru.

EN: The weather is gradually heading toward recovery.
HI: मौसम धीरे-धीरे सुधार की ओर बढ़ रहा है।
GU: હવામાન ધીમે ધીમે સુધારા તરફ આગળ વધી રહ્યું છે.
VerdictEnglish
○ correctValid — a gradual trend in progress is exactly what point 3's つつある describes.
× incorrect(not the case here)

Why: 向かいつつある fits perfectly — the weather is in the middle of a slow shift toward recovery.

Q3

買い物に行ったながら、高くて買えなかった。Kaimono ni itta nagara, takakute kaenakatta.

Corrected: 買い物に行きながら／行ったけれど(も)／行ったが、高くて買えなかった。Kaimono ni iki nagara / itta keredo(mo) / itta ga, takakute kaenakatta.
EN: I went shopping, but it was expensive so I couldn't buy it.
HI: ख़रीदारी करने गया था, लेकिन महँगा होने के कारण नहीं खरीद पाया।
GU: ખરીદી કરવા ગયો હતો, પણ મોંઘું હોવાથી ખરીદી ન શક્યો.
VerdictEnglish
○ correct(not the case here)
× incorrectながら (point 1) needs the ます-stem (行き), never the past た-form (行った) — must be corrected to 行きながら, or switched to 行ったけれど(も)／行ったが.

Why: ながら always attaches to the ます-stem, not the た-form — 行ったながら is simply the wrong shape; either fix the verb form (行きながら) or swap in a plain contrastive けれど(も)／が.

Q4

日本人くせして、日本語を間違えちゃだめだよ。Nihonjin kuse shite, nihongo o machigaecha dame da yo.

Corrected: 日本人のくせして、日本語を間違えちゃだめだよ。Nihonjin no kuse shite, nihongo o machigaecha dame da yo.
EN: You're Japanese — you shouldn't be making mistakes in Japanese!
HI: तुम जापानी होकर भी, जापानी भाषा में ग़लती नहीं करनी चाहिए!
GU: તું જાપાની હોવા છતાં, જાપાનીઝમાં ભૂલ ન કરવી જોઈએ!
VerdictEnglish
○ correct(not the case here)
× incorrectくせして needs の after a noun (point 4) — must be 日本人のくせして, not 日本人くせして.

Why: When くせして attaches to a noun it needs の in between (Nの＋くせして) — dropping the の, as in 日本人くせして, is ungrammatical.

相手チームのプレーは、敵ながらすばらしい。Aite chiimu no puree wa, teki nagara subarashii.

GU: સામેની ટીમની રમત, દુશ્મન હોવા છતાં, અદ્ભુત છે.
VerdictEnglish
○ correctValid — concessive ながら (point 1) attaches directly to the noun 敵.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

政治の情勢を＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 計画しよう。Seiji no jousei o ___ keikaku shiyou.

Tiles: 1 つつ (tsutsu)　2 事業の (jigyou no)　3 見守り (mimamori)　4 拡大を (kakudai o)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

2 事業の→4 拡大を→3 見守り→1 つつ

政治の情勢を事業の拡大を見守りつつ計画しよう。Seiji no jousei o jigyou no kakudai o mimamori tsutsu keikaku shiyou.

EN: Let's plan the business's expansion while keeping watch on the political situation.
HI: राजनीतिक हालात पर नज़र रखते हुए, व्यवसाय के विस्तार की योजना बनाते हैं।
GU: રાજકીય પરિસ્થિતિ પર નજર રાખતાં, વ્યવસાયના વિસ્તરણનું આયોજન કરીએ.

Why: Built on point 2's simultaneous ❷ sense (V-stem+つつ, formal "while") — keeping watch (見守りつつ) and planning happen at the same time.

Q7

今度引っ越す家には＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ものがあります。Kondo hikkosu ie ni wa ___ mono ga arimasu.

Tiles: 1 呼べる (yoberu)　2 小さい (chiisai)　3 庭と (niwa to)　4 ながらも (nagara mo)

3 庭と→1 呼べる→2 小さい→4 ながらも

今度引っ越す家には庭と呼べる小さいながらもものがあります。Kondo hikkosu ie ni wa niwa to yoberu chiisai nagara mo mono ga arimasu.

EN: The house I'm about to move to has something that could be called a garden, small as it may be.
HI: जिस घर में मैं अब जाने वाला हूँ, वहाँ कुछ ऐसा है जिसे बगीचा कहा जा सकता है, भले ही वह छोटा हो।
GU: હું જે ઘરમાં હવે જવાનો છું, ત્યાં એવું કંઈક છે જેને બગીચો કહી શકાય, ભલે તે નાનું હોય.

Why: Built on point 1's い-adjective concession (小さいながらも, "even though [it's] small") — the relative clause 庭と呼べる ("that can be called a garden") describes something modest but real.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day is a cluster of look-alike forms all built on the same idea of "concession" or "ongoing state," which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q3 and Q4 above, which are built to trip up verb-form and の-attachment errors). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ながら（も）~nagara (mo) Even though ~ N／な-adj／い-adj／V-ます-stem + ながら（も） Neutral register; attaches to the ます-stem, never the た-form. Everyday concessive contrasts, spoken or written.
〜つつ（も）~tsutsu (mo) Even though ~ (❶) / while ~ (❷) V-ます-stem + つつ（も） 硬 — the formal written cousin of ながら; check context for concessive vs. simultaneous. Formal writing needing either sense of ながら.
〜つつある~tsutsu aru Is gradually ~ing V-ます-stem + つつある Describes an ongoing, gradual trend — not a single momentary action. Reporting a slow-moving change (recovery, spread, progress).
〜くせして~kuse shite Even though ~ (critically) Nの／な-adj-な／V・い-adj (plain) + くせして Conversational, always carries blame/criticism — needs の after a bare noun. Scolding or teasing about a mismatch between status and behaviour.
Exam trap: ながら and つつ mean the same thing but attach exactly the same way (both need the ます-stem, never the た-form) — Q3 above shows what happens when a past tense sneaks in front of ながら. Separately, くせして needs a connecting の when it follows a bare noun (Q4) — the same trap as くせに, so if you already know くせに's connection rules, they transfer directly to くせして.
