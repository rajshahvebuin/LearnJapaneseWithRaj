1日目 — 地図の上では (Chizu no ue de wa)

Source

Grammar points covered

Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.65 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points today are built on the same kanji 上, which is exactly why the book groups them into one day — see the Confusion Pairs section below for how to tell them apart at a glance. 〜上は is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other three are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜上に

neutral register
Reading~ue ni
Meaning (EN)"not only ~, but also ~" / "on top of ~" — adds a second fact or quality that builds on the first (can stack two good things or two bad things)
Meaning (HI)न सिर्फ़ ...बल्कि ...भी — एक बात के ऊपर दूसरी बात भी जोड़ना
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત ...જ નહીં પણ ...પણ — એક વાત ઉપર બીજી વાત ઉમેરવી
Connection (接続)V／い-adj／な-adj／N (plain/dictionary form) + 上に
RegisterNeutral — used in both writing and speech
Typical useStacking a second, matching fact onto the first — both facts point the same direction (both positive or both negative).
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-る + 上にできる上にdekiru ue ni
い-adjectiveい-adj + 上に寒かった上にsamukatta ue ni
な-adjective(added, not spelled out in book; standard な-adj plain form) な-adj + である/な + 上に静かである上にshizuka de aru ue ni
Noun(added, not spelled out in book) N + の/である + 上に学生である上にgakusei de aru ue ni
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は仕事ができる上に優しい。Kare wa shigoto ga dekiru ue ni yasashii. Not only is he good at his job but he's also a nice guy. वह न सिर्फ़ काम में अच्छा है, बल्कि दयालु भी है। તે ફક્ત કામમાં સારો જ નથી, પણ દયાળુ પણ છે.
昨日は寒かった上に、風が強かった。Kinou wa samukatta ue ni, kaze ga tsuyokatta. Yesterday it was cold and windy. कल न सिर्फ़ ठंड थी, बल्कि हवा भी तेज़ थी। ગઈકાલે ફક્ત ઠંડી જ નહોતી, પણ પવન પણ તેજ હતો.

Think of 上に as "and on top of that…" — both clauses must point the same emotional direction (both good, or both bad). Don't mix a positive fact with a negative one; that's what makes it different from a plain "but".

【2】 〜上で

neutral register
Reading~ue de
Meaning (EN)"after doing ~ (deliberately), and based on that" — a considered action happens first, then the main action follows on that basis
Meaning (HI)...करने के बाद, उसके आधार पर — पहले सोच-समझकर वह काम करना, फिर अगला कदम
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા પછી, તેના આધારે — પહેલાં વિચારીને તે કામ કરવું, પછી આગળનું પગલું
Connection (接続)V-た + 上で　|　Nの + 上で／上の／上での／上
RegisterNeutral, often slightly formal in business contexts
Typical useMarks a deliberate first step (thinking it over, consulting someone) that the following decision is based on.
Word typeFormationExample
VerbV-た + 上でよく考えた上でyoku kangaeta ue de
NounNの + 上で／上の／上での／上相談の上soudan no ue
い-adjective／な-adjective(added, not in book — this pattern targets a deliberate action, not a state, so plain adjectives don't attach here)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
よく考えた上で決めます。Yoku kangaeta ue de kimemasu. I will think well and then decide. अच्छी तरह सोचने के बाद फ़ैसला करूँगा। સારી રીતે વિચાર્યા પછી નિર્ણય લઈશ.
家族と相談の上、お返事します。Kazoku to soudan no ue, ohenji shimasu. I will reply after consulting my family. परिवार से सलाह लेने के बाद जवाब दूँगा। પરિવાર સાથે ચર્ચા કર્યા પછી જવાબ આપીશ.

〜上で always implies the first action was deliberate/considered — that's the difference from plain てから ("after doing"). It shows up a lot in business Japanese: 検討の上, ご確認の上, ご了承の上, etc.

【3】 〜上は

硬 formal/written
Reading~ue wa
Meaning (EN)"now that ~ / since ~ (a fixed situation has been reached)" — the same meaning as 以上 or からには; something has become an established fact, so a determination, advice, or prohibition naturally follows
Meaning (HI)अब जब कि ...हो चुका है / चूँकि ...हो गया है — अब यह तय बात है, इसलिए...
Meaning (GU)હવે જ્યારે ...થઈ ગયું છે / કારણ કે ...થયું છે — હવે આ નક્કી વાત છે, એટલે...
Connection (接続)V (plain form, often past) + 上は
Register硬 — formal, mostly written; interchangeable with 以上／からには
Typical useFollowed by words expressing determination, advice, or prohibition — "since this is now settled, you must/should/will…"
Word typeFormationExample
VerbVた／Vる + 上は選ばれた上はerabareta ue wa
い-adjective／な-adjective／Noun(added, not in book — this pattern targets a settled event/decision, so it's almost always built on a verb)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
キャプテンに選ばれた上は、がんばるしかない。Kyaputen ni erabareta ue wa, ganbaru shika nai. Since I was chosen as captain, I will have to try my hardest. चूँकि मुझे कप्तान चुना गया है, अब पूरी मेहनत करने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं। કારણ કે મને કેપ્ટન તરીકે પસંદ કરવામાં આવ્યો છે, હવે પૂરી મહેનત કરવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
入学する上は卒業したい。Nyuugaku suru ue wa sotsugyou shitai. Upon entering school, I would like to graduate. चूँकि दाखिला ले ही रहा हूँ, तो स्नातक भी करना चाहता हूँ। જ્યારે પ્રવેશ લઈ જ રહ્યો છું, તો સ્નાતક પણ થવા માંગું છું.

The book's own sidebar notes that 〜上は is typically followed by words expressing determination, advice, or prohibition (決意を表す言葉やアドバイス、禁止など) — so watch for a strong-willed or imperative-feeling second clause as a clue that 上は (not 上では) is the right choice.

【4】 〜上では／上は／上も

neutral register
Reading~ue de wa / ~ue wa / ~ue mo
Meaning (EN)"according to ~ / from the standpoint of ~ / on paper" — viewed strictly from N's perspective (a map, data, theory, calculation), even though reality may differ
Meaning (HI)...के हिसाब से / ...के अनुसार देखा जाए तो — कागज़ी तौर पर, भले ही असलियत अलग हो
Meaning (GU)...ના હિસાબે / ...મુજબ જોઈએ તો — કાગળ પર, ભલે વાસ્તવિકતા અલગ હોય
Connection (接続)Nの + 上では／上でも　|　N + 上／上は／上も
RegisterNeutral — common in both writing and speech
Typical useContrasts a formal/theoretical perspective (map, calculation, statistics) against actual reality, usually followed by a "but actually…" clause.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (with の)Nの + 上では／上でも天気図の上ではtenkizu no ue de wa
Noun (direct)N + 上／上は／上も理論上はriron-jou wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
天気図の上では春なのに、実際はまだ寒い。Tenkizu no ue de wa haru na noni, jissai wa mada samui. The patterns on the weather map indicate that spring is here, but actually it is still cold. मौसम के नक़्शे के हिसाब से बसंत आ चुका है, लेकिन असल में अभी भी ठंड है। હવામાનના નકશા મુજબ વસંત આવી ગઈ છે, પણ ખરેખર હજુ ઠંડી છે.
理論上はできるはずだったが、実験では失敗した。Riron-jou wa dekiru hazu datta ga, jikken de wa shippai shita. Theoretically it should have been possible, but the experiment failed. सिद्धांत रूप से यह संभव होना चाहिए था, लेकिन प्रयोग में यह असफल रहा। સિદ્ધાંત મુજબ તે શક્ય હોવું જોઈતું હતું, પણ પ્રયોગમાં તે નિષ્ફળ ગયું.

This pattern loves fixed collocations: 計算の上では／計算上は, 書類の上では／書類上は, データの上では／データ上は, 統計の上では／統計上は — 上 attaches straight to a noun of records/data/theory. When 上 follows a noun directly (no の) it's read じょう, e.g. 理論上 (ronron-jou), 計算上 (keisan-jou).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, circle the correct option between two choices) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.63. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.65.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

計算（　）可能だが、実用にはまだ問題がある。Keisan (___) kanou da ga, jitsuyou ni wa mada mondai ga aru.

Full sentence: 計算上は可能だが、実用にはまだ問題がある。Keisan-jou wa kanou da ga, jitsuyou ni wa mada mondai ga aru.
EN: It's possible in calculation, but there are still problems with practical use.
HI: गणना के हिसाब से यह संभव है, लेकिन व्यवहार में इस्तेमाल में अभी भी समस्याएँ हैं।
GU: ગણતરી પ્રમાણે તે શક્ય છે, પણ વ્યવહારુ ઉપયોગમાં હજુ સમસ્યાઓ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a上は-jou wain calculation-terms (point 4)गणना के हिसाब सेગણતરી પ્રમાણે
bの上no ue(wrong — reads as a literal "on top of the calculation")(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: 計算上 (N directly + 上, no の) is point 4's fixed "in N terms" collocation; の上 would suggest something physically resting on top of a calculation, which makes no sense.

Q2

仕事でミスをした（　）、友達とけんかをした。Shigoto de misu o shita (___), tomodachi to kenka o shita.

Full sentence: 仕事でミスをした上に、友達とけんかをした。Shigoto de misu o shita ue ni, tomodachi to kenka o shita.
EN: Not only did I make a mistake at work, but I also had a fight with a friend.
HI: काम में ग़लती करने के अलावा, दोस्त से झगड़ा भी हो गया।
GU: કામમાં ભૂલ કરવા ઉપરાંત, મિત્ર સાથે ઝઘડો પણ થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a上でue de(wrong — implies a deliberate first step, not a stacked misfortune)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)
b上にue nion top of that, in addition (point 1)के अलावा (के ऊपर से)ઉપરાંત

Why: Two bad things pile up here (a mistake, then a fight) — that's exactly point 1's job. 上で would wrongly suggest the mistake was a deliberate, considered step.

Full sentence: 金額を確認の上、サインをお願いします。Kingaku o kakunin no ue, sain o onegai shimasu.
EN: Please sign after confirming the amount.
HI: राशि की पुष्टि करने के बाद, कृपया हस्ताक्षर करें।
GU: રકમની ખાતરી કર્યા પછી, કૃપા કરી સહી કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a上ue(wrong — needs の before 上 when attaching to the noun 確認)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Q4

受験手続きが完了した（　）全力をつくすつもりだ。Juken tetsuzuki ga kanryou shita (___) zenryoku o tsukusu tsumori da.

GU: કારણ કે પરીક્ષાની પ્રક્રિયા પૂરી થઈ ગઈ છે, હવે હું પૂરી તાકાત લગાવવાનો ઇરાદો રાખું છું.

Q6

この機械は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ もわかりづらい。Kono kikai wa ___ mo wakarizurai.

Tiles: 1 上に (ue ni)　2 難しい (muzukashii)　3 説明書 (setsumeisho)　4 操作が (sousa ga)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

4 操作が→2 難しい→1 上に→3 説明書

この機械は操作が難しい上に説明書もわかりづらい。Kono kikai wa sousa ga muzukashii ue ni setsumeisho mo wakarizurai.

EN: Not only is this machine's operation difficult, but its manual is also hard to understand.
HI: यह मशीन चलाना न सिर्फ़ मुश्किल है, बल्कि इसकी नियमावली भी समझने में कठिन है।
GU: આ મશીન ચલાવવું ફક્ત મુશ્કેલ જ નથી, પણ તેની માર્ગદર્શિકા પણ સમજવી અઘરી છે.

Why: Built on point 1 (〜上に) — 操作が難しい (the operation is difficult) and 説明書もわかりづらい (the manual is also hard to follow) are two matching negative facts stacked together.

Q7

彼は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 面接が心配だ。Kare wa ___ mensetsu ga shinpai da.

Tiles: 1 では (de wa)　2 上 (ue)　3 成績の (seiseki no)　4 問題ないが (mondai nai ga)

3 成績の→2 上→1 では→4 問題ないが

彼は成績の上では問題ないが面接が心配だ。Kare wa seiseki no ue de wa mondai nai ga mensetsu ga shinpai da.

EN: Grade-wise he's fine, but the interview is a concern.
HI: उसके नंबरों के हिसाब से तो कोई समस्या नहीं है, लेकिन इंटरव्यू की चिंता है।
GU: તેના ગુણ પ્રમાણે તો કોઈ સમસ્યા નથી, પણ ઇન્ટરવ્યુની ચિંતા છે.

Why: Built on point 4 (Nの上では) — "grade-wise/on paper" (成績の上では) contrasts with the real-world worry (面接が心配だ) that follows.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today are built on 上, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1 and Q4 above, which trip up 上は vs の上 and 上は vs 上では). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜上に~ue ni Not only ~, but also ~ V／A／な-adj／N (plain) + 上に Adds a second matching fact — both clauses point the same emotional direction. You want to stack two good or two bad facts.
〜上で~ue de After doing ~ (deliberately), based on that Vた／Nの + 上で The first action is a considered step the second action builds on. Formal/business steps: 検討の上, ご確認の上, 相談の上.
〜上は~ue wa Now that ~ (a settled fact), so… V (plain, often た) + 上は 硬 — followed by determination, advice, or prohibition; = 以上／からには. A decision/status is now fixed and a strong-willed conclusion follows.
〜上では／上は／上も~ue de wa / ~ue wa / ~ue mo According to ~ / on paper Nの + 上では　|　N + 上／上は／上も Views things from a data/theory/documented standpoint — often contrasted against reality. 計算上, 理論上, 書類の上では, 統計上, etc.
Exam trap: 上は and 上では look almost identical but do very different jobs — 上は (point 3) is causal ("since this is now decided, therefore…") and demands a determination/advice/prohibition afterward; 上では (point 4) is perspectival ("looked at from this angle…") and is usually followed by a reality-check "but actually…". Ask yourself: is the second clause a resolve/rule, or a contrast with reality? That answers which one fits.
