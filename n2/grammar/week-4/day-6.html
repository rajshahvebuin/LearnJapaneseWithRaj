6日目 — 使用にあたって (Shiyou ni atatte)

Answer key

Notes
All four points today are formal, business/announcement-style expressions of timing or basis. 〜にあたり and 〜に先立ち are both tagged 硬 formal/written in the source.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜にあたり

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni atari
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of ~ / when (about to) ~" — marks a formal moment of doing/undertaking something important
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / ...करते समय (औपचारिक रूप से) — किसी ख़ास काम को शुरू करते वक़्त
Meaning (GU)...ના પ્રસંગે / ...કરતી વખતે (ઔપચારિક રીતે) — કોઈ ખાસ કામ શરૂ કરતી વખતે
Connection (接続)Nする／Vる + に + あたって／あたり／あたっては／あたってのN
Register硬 — formal, common in official notices and speeches
Typical useMarks the formal occasion of doing something significant — using a facility, starting a job, giving a speech.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する-verb)Nする + にあたり利用にあたりriyou ni atari
Verb (dictionary form)Vる + にあたって使用するにあたってはshiyou suru ni atatte wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
図書館の利用にあたり、図書カードが必要です。Toshokan no riyou ni atari, tosho kaado ga hitsuyou desu. A library card is required to use the library. पुस्तकालय का उपयोग करते समय, लाइब्रेरी कार्ड ज़रूरी है। પુસ્તકાલયનો ઉપયોગ કરતી વખતે, લાઇબ્રેરી કાર્ડ જરૂરી છે.
アルバイトをするにあたっては、学業や体に無理のないようにすること。Arubaito o suru ni atatte wa, gakugyou ya karada ni muri no nai you ni suru koto. If you are going to work part-time, make sure that it does not interfere with your studies or your health. पार्ट-टाइम काम करते समय, ध्यान रखें कि इससे पढ़ाई या सेहत पर बुरा असर न पड़े। પાર્ટ-ટાઇમ કામ કરતી વખતે, ધ્યાન રાખો કે તેની અસર અભ્યાસ કે તબિયત પર ન પડે.

Reserved for meaningful, one-time occasions — starting a job, using an important facility, giving a speech — not everyday trivial actions. Very close to 上で (Day 1) in feel, but more formal and announcement-like.

【2】 〜に沿って

neutral register
Reading~ni sotte
Meaning (EN)"in line with ~ / following ~" — acting according to a plan, guideline, or wish, without deviating from it
Meaning (HI)...के अनुसार / ...के मुताबिक़ चलते हुए — किसी योजना या इच्छा से हटे बिना
Meaning (GU)...ને અનુસરીને / ...મુજબ — કોઈ યોજના કે ઇચ્છાથી ખસ્યા વગર
Connection (接続)Nに + 沿って／沿い／沿ったN
RegisterNeutral, common in business/service contexts
Typical useFollowing a document, plan, or someone's wishes closely — a guideline that shapes how something is done.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + 沿って資料に沿ってshiryou ni sotte
Noun (attributive)Nに + 沿ったNご希望に沿ったプランgokibou ni sotta puran
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
資料に沿って、ご説明いたします。Shiryou ni sotte, gosetsumei itashimasu. I will explain the content of the handout as I go along. मैं दस्तावेज़ के अनुसार समझाऊँगा। હું દસ્તાવેજ મુજબ સમજાવીશ.
お客様ひとりひとりのご希望に沿った旅行プランをお作りします。Okyakusama hitori hitori no gokibou ni sotta ryokou puran o otsukuri shimasu. We make travel plans according to each customer's wishes. हम हर ग्राहक की इच्छा के अनुसार यात्रा योजनाएँ बनाते हैं। અમે દરેક ગ્રાહકની ઇચ્છા મુજબ પ્રવાસ યોજનાઓ બનાવીએ છીએ.

Picture 沿う literally: "to run alongside" (like a road running alongside a river) — the action tracks closely along a plan/document/wish without straying from it.

【3】 〜に先立ち

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni sakidachi
Meaning (EN)"prior to ~ / ahead of ~" — one formal event happens before another, in preparation for it
Meaning (HI)...से पहले (औपचारिक रूप से) — किसी बड़े काम की तैयारी में उससे पहले कुछ करना
Meaning (GU)...પહેલાં (ઔપચારિક રીતે) — કોઈ મોટા કામની તૈયારીમાં તેની પહેલાં કંઈક કરવું
Connection (接続)Nする／Vる + に + 先立って／先立ち／先立つN
Register硬 — formal, common in official announcements about upcoming events
Typical useA formal precursor step taken in preparation for a bigger upcoming event (an opening, a launch, a survey before a project).
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (する-verb)Nする + に先立ち開店に先立ちkaiten ni sakidachi
Verb (dictionary form)Vる + に先立って開発するに先立ってkaihatsu suru ni sakidatte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
開店に先立ち、関係者だけのパーティーが行われた。Kaiten ni sakidachi, kankeisha dake no paatii ga okonawareta. Prior to opening, a party was held with those involved. दुकान खुलने से पहले, संबंधित लोगों के लिए एक पार्टी हुई। દુકાન ખૂલવા પહેલાં, સંબંધિત લોકો માટે એક પાર્ટી યોજાઈ.
新製品の開発に先立って、アンケート調査を行った。Shinseihin no kaihatsu ni sakidatte, ankeeto chousa o okonatta. We conducted a survey prior to the development of a new product. नए उत्पाद के विकास से पहले, हमने एक सर्वेक्षण किया। નવા ઉત્પાદનના વિકાસ પહેલાં, અમે એક સર્વે કર્યો.

に先立ち is more formal and announcement-like than plain 前に — reach for it in official notices about a launch, opening, or ceremony, where 前に would sound too casual.

【4】 〜にわたって

neutral register
Reading~ni watatte
Meaning (EN)"over the whole span of ~ / throughout ~" — covers an entire period or range, expressing scale/breadth (time or area)
Meaning (HI)...भर / पूरे ...तक — पूरी अवधि या पूरे क्षेत्र को दर्शाना
Meaning (GU)...સમગ્ર / આખા ...સુધી — સંપૂર્ણ સમયગાળો કે વિસ્તાર દર્શાવવો
Connection (接続)Nに + わたって／わたり／わたるN′　（N = period／range）
RegisterNeutral, common in news and formal reports
Typical useEmphasizes the full extent of a period or area — how long or how wide something covers.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (range/area)Nに + わたって広い範囲にわたってhiroi han'i ni watatte
Noun (period)Nに + わたり20年間にわたりnijuu-nenkan ni watari
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この台風の影響で、関東地方は広い範囲にわたって大雨となるでしょう。Kono taifuu no eikyou de, Kantou chihou wa hiroi han'i ni watatte ooame to naru deshou. Because of this typhoon, it is expected to rain heavily in large parts of the Kanto Region. इस तूफ़ान के असर से, कान्तो क्षेत्र के व्यापक हिस्से में भारी बारिश होने की संभावना है। આ વાવાઝોડાની અસરથી, કાન્તો પ્રદેશના વિશાળ ભાગમાં ભારે વરસાદ થવાની શક્યતા છે.
わが社は20年間にわたり、この薬の研究開発に努めてきた。Wagasha wa nijuu-nenkan ni watari, kono kusuri no kenkyuu kaihatsu ni tsutomete kita. Over the last 20 years, our company has endeavored to research and develop this drug. हमारी कंपनी ने पिछले 20 सालों से इस दवा के अनुसंधान व विकास में जुटी रही है। અમારી કંપની છેલ્લા 20 વર્ષથી આ દવાના સંશોધન અને વિકાસમાં પ્રયત્નશીલ રહી છે.

The noun before にわたって is always a period or range (期間・範囲), never a single point — 広い範囲にわたって (across a wide area), 20年間にわたり (over 20 years). Don't confuse with にあたって (point 1), which is about timing/occasion, not span/breadth.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, circle the correct option) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items), both from p.73. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.76.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

祭りは3日間に（　）続いた。Matsuri wa mikkakan ni (___) tsuzuita.

Full sentence: 祭りは3日間にわたって続いた。Matsuri wa mikkakan ni watatte tsuzuita.
EN: The festival continued for a whole three days.
HI: त्योहार पूरे तीन दिनों तक चला।
GU: ઉત્સવ પૂરા ત્રણ દિવસ સુધી ચાલ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあたってatatte(wrong — にあたって is about a single occasion, not a span of days)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)
bわたってwatatteover the whole span of (point 4)पूरे ...तकપૂરા ...સુધી

Why: "3日間" (three whole days) is a period being fully covered — point 4's にわたって, not point 1's にあたって (occasion/timing).

Q2

会社の方針に（　）、計画を立てます。Kaisha no houshin ni (___), keikaku o tatemasu.

Full sentence: 会社の方針に沿って、計画を立てます。Kaisha no houshin ni sotte, keikaku o tatemasu.
EN: We will draw up the plan in line with the company's policy.
HI: कंपनी की नीति के अनुसार, हम योजना बनाएँगे।
GU: કંપનીની નીતિ મુજબ, અમે યોજના બનાવીશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aそってsottein line with, following (point 2)के अनुसारમુજબ
bさきだってsakidatte(wrong — に先立って means "prior to," not "in line with")(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: Making a plan that follows the company's policy is point 2's に沿って — に先立って would wrongly suggest the plan comes chronologically before the policy.

Q3

映画の公開に（　）、試写会が行われた。Eiga no koukai ni (___), shishakai ga okonawareta.

Full sentence: 映画の公開に先立ち、試写会が行われた。Eiga no koukai ni sakidachi, shishakai ga okonawareta.
EN: Prior to the film's release, a preview screening was held.
HI: फ़िल्म रिलीज़ होने से पहले, एक पूर्वावलोकन स्क्रीनिंग हुई।
GU: ફિલ્મ રિલીઝ થતાં પહેલાં, એક પૂર્વાવલોકન સ્ક્રીનિંગ યોજાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aさきだちsakidachiprior to (point 3)...से पहले...પહેલાં
bわたりwatari(wrong — にわたり is for a span, not "before")(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: A preview screening happening before the release is point 3's に先立ち — に先立って always means "ahead of," which is exactly the timing here.

Q4

書類を書くに（　）、次の点に注意してください。Shorui o kaku ni (___), tsugi no ten ni chuui shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 書類を書くにあたり、次の点に注意してください。Shorui o kaku ni atari, tsugi no ten ni chuui shite kudasai.
EN: When filling out the document, please pay attention to the following points.
HI: दस्तावेज़ भरते समय, कृपया निम्नलिखित बातों का ध्यान रखें।
GU: દસ્તાવેજ ભરતી વખતે, કૃપા કરી નીચેની બાબતોનું ધ્યાન રાખો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあたりatarion the occasion of, when about to (point 1)...करते समय...કરતી વખતે
bそってsotte(wrong — nothing has been given yet to "follow along")(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

その国との400年に（　）交流について調べる。Sono kuni to no yonhyaku-nen ni (___) kouryuu ni tsuite shiraberu.

GU: હું તે દેશ સાથેના 400 વર્ષના આદાનપ્રદાન વિશે સંશોધન કરીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

論文を＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ルールがある。Ronbun o ___ ruuru ga aru.

Tiles: 1 べき (beki)　2 にあたって (ni atatte)　3 書く (kaku)　4 守る (mamoru)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

3 書く→2 にあたって→4 守る→1 べき

論文を書くにあたって守るべきルールがある。Ronbun o kaku ni atatte mamoru beki ruuru ga aru.

EN: There are rules that must be followed when writing a thesis.
HI: शोध-निबंध लिखते समय पालन करने योग्य नियम होते हैं।
GU: સંશોધન-લેખ લખતી વખતે પાળવા જેવા નિયમો હોય છે.

Why: Combines point 1 (〜にあたって, "when undertaking [writing a thesis]") with Day 5's 〜べき ("rules that should be followed") describing the formal occasion of academic writing.

Q7

客の＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 望まれる。Kyaku no ___ nozomareru.

Tiles: 1 希望 (kibou)　2 商品の (shouhin no)　3 開発が (kaihatsu ga)　4 にそった (ni sotta)

1 希望→4 にそった→2 商品の→3 開発が

客の希望にそった商品の開発が望まれる。Kyaku no kibou ni sotta shouhin no kaihatsu ga nozomareru.

EN: The development of products that match customers' wishes is desired.
HI: ग्राहकों की इच्छाओं के अनुरूप उत्पादों का विकास वांछनीय है।
GU: ગ્રાહકોની ઇચ્છાઓ મુજબના ઉત્પાદનોનો વિકાસ ઇચ્છનીય છે.

Why: Built on point 2's attributive にそったN — 希望にそった商品 ("products that follow [customer] wishes") is the subject of the passive desire-verb 望まれる.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today are formal, announcement-style expressions that can look interchangeable at a glance, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q3, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜にあたり~ni atari On the occasion of ~ Nする／Vる + にあたり 硬 — marks the formal moment of undertaking something significant. Starting a job, using a facility, giving a speech.
〜に沿って~ni sotte In line with ~ Nに + 沿って Follows an existing guideline/plan/wish closely, without deviating. Following a document, a policy, or a customer's request.
〜に先立ち~ni sakidachi Prior to ~ Nする／Vる + に先立ち 硬 — a formal "before," always implying preparation for the bigger event that follows. Announcing a preview, survey, or ceremony ahead of a launch/opening.
〜にわたって~ni watatte Over the whole span of ~ Nに（期間／範囲） + わたって Emphasizes total duration or breadth — not a point in time at all. Describing how long or how wide something covers.
Exam trap: にあたり and にわたって both start with に and feel "formal-timing-ish," but they answer completely different questions — にあたり asks "at what moment/occasion?" (a point), while にわたって asks "over how long/how wide?" (a span). Q1 above is built exactly to test this: 3日間 (three days, a span) needs にわたって, not にあたって. Similarly, に沿って (following an existing plan) and に先立ち (coming before a plan/event) look similar but point in opposite chronological directions.
