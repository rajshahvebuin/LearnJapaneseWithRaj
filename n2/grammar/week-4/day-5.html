5日目 — せざるをえない (Sezaru o enai)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.70–71 (PDF pages 70–71).
Grammar points covered
〜べきではない〜ざるをえない〜ことになっている〜にすぎない
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.73 (6日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This day's title itself — せざるをえない — is the week's own title pattern (おぼえざるをえない), so it's worth memorizing especially well. 〜にすぎない is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other three are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜べきではない

neutral register
Reading~beki de wa nai
Meaning (EN)"should not ~" — states that an action is not the right/proper thing to do (the negative of 〜べきだ, "should")
Meaning (HI)...नहीं करना चाहिए — यह बताना कि यह काम सही/उचित नहीं है
Meaning (GU)...ન કરવું જોઈએ — આ કામ યોગ્ય નથી એમ જણાવવું
Connection (接続)Vる／する→すべき（＝するべき） + ではない／だった／N
RegisterNeutral
Typical useA judgment about what is/was proper — often used in hindsight (〜べきではなかった, "shouldn't have").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)Vる + べきではないすべきではないsubeki de wa nai
する (irregular)する→すべき（＝するべき）勉強すべきだったbenkyou subeki datta
な-adjective／Noun(added, not spelled out with its own example on this spread) な-adj-である／N-である + べきだ正直であるべきだshoujiki de aru beki da
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
そんなことをすべきではない。Sonna koto o subeki de wa nai. You should not do that. वैसा काम नहीं करना चाहिए। એવું કામ ન કરવું જોઈએ.
もっと勉強するべきだった。Motto benkyou suru beki datta. I should have studied more. मुझे और ज़्यादा पढ़ाई करनी चाहिए थी। મારે વધુ અભ્યાસ કરવો જોઈતો હતો.
あんなこと言うべきではなかった。Anna koto iu beki de wa nakatta. I should not have said such a thing. मुझे ऐसी बात नहीं कहनी चाहिए थी। મારે એવી વાત ન કહેવી જોઈતી હતી.

する is irregular here: it becomes すべき, not するべき — though the book notes するべき (=すべき) is also accepted in casual use. Put it in the past (〜べきではなかった) for hindsight regret, exactly like English "shouldn't have."

【2】 〜ざるをえない

neutral register
Reading~zaru o enai
Meaning (EN)"have no choice but to ~ / cannot help but ~" — circumstances force an action the speaker would rather not do (＝ざるを得ない)
Meaning (HI)...करने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं — मजबूरी में वह काम करना पड़ता है
Meaning (GU)...કરવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી — મજબૂરીમાં તે કામ કરવું પડે છે
Connection (接続)V-ない-form の ない を取って + ざるをえない（例外：する→せざるをえない）
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal
Typical useA reluctant, forced action — "I don't want to, but I have no other option."
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (regular)V-ない-form (drop ない) + ざるをえない続けざるをえないtsuzuke zaru o enai
する (irregular)しない → せ + ざるをえない勉強せざるをえないbenkyou sezaru o enai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
いやな仕事でも、生活のためには続けざるをえない。Iya na shigoto demo, seikatsu no tame ni wa tsuzuke zaru o enai. In order to earn a living, I have to continue my unpleasant job. नापसंद काम होते हुए भी, गुज़ारे के लिए इसे जारी रखने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं। નાપસંદ કામ હોવા છતાં, ગુજરાન માટે તે ચાલુ રાખવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
この戦争は間違いだったと言わざるをえない。Kono sensou wa machigai datta to iwazaru o enai. We must say that this war was a mistake. यह कहने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं कि यह युद्ध एक ग़लती थी। એમ કહેવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી કે આ યુદ્ધ એક ભૂલ હતું.

する is irregular — it goes to せざるをえない (from the classical negative せず), not "しざるをえない." This is exactly the pattern behind this week's own title, おぼえざるをえない ("you have no choice but to remember").

【3】 〜ことになっている

neutral register
Reading~koto ni natte iru
Meaning (EN)"it is arranged/decided/scheduled that ~" — describes an existing rule, plan, or arrangement, not a one-off personal decision
Meaning (HI)ऐसा तय/निर्धारित है कि ... — यह किसी नियम या योजना के बारे में बताता है
Meaning (GU)એવું નક્કી/ગોઠવાયેલું છે કે ... — આ કોઈ નિયમ કે યોજના વિશે જણાવે છે
Connection (接続)Vる／Vない／Nという + ことになっている
RegisterNeutral
Typical useStates a standing rule, schedule, or agreed arrangement — a fact "on the books," not a spontaneous personal choice.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (affirmative/negative)Vる／Vない + ことになっている行われることになっているokonawareru koto ni natte iru
NounNという + ことになっている合格ということになっているgoukaku to iu koto ni natte iru
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
明日、ここで卒業式が行われることになっている。Ashita, koko de sotsugyoushiki ga okonawareru koto ni natte iru. The graduation ceremony will be held here tomorrow. कल यहाँ स्नातक समारोह होना तय है। આવતીકાલે અહીં સ્નાતક સમારંભ યોજાવાનું નક્કી છે.
60点以上が合格ということになっている。Rokujutten ijou ga goukaku to iu koto ni natte iru. A passing grade is 60 points or more. 60 अंक या उससे अधिक को पास माना जाना तय है। 60 ગુણ કે તેથી વધુને પાસ ગણવાનું નક્કી છે.

Compare with 〜ことにしている ("I make it a rule to ~", a personal habit): 〜ことになっている describes a rule or arrangement set by someone/something else (a school, a schedule, a regulation), not the speaker's own personal habit.

【4】 〜にすぎない 《〜に過ぎない》

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni suginai
Meaning (EN)"is nothing more than ~ / merely ~" — downplays something as being no more significant than the stated fact
Meaning (HI)...से ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं / सिर्फ़ ...है — किसी बात को छोटा/मामूली बताना
Meaning (GU)...થી વધુ કંઈ નથી / ફક્ત ...છે — કોઈ વાતને નાની/સામાન્ય ગણાવવી
Connection (接続)V／A／な-adj-である／N（だ／に） + すぎない
Register硬 — formal, common in writing and careful spoken explanations
Typical useMinimizes a claim — "it's only this much, nothing grander." Often paired with 単に…, あくまで(も)…, ただの…, ほんの…
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + すぎない言い間違いにすぎないiimachigai ni suginai
Verb (potential/plain)V + にすぎないできるにすぎないdekiru ni suginai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
単なる言い間違いにすぎない。Tannaru iimachigai ni suginai. It was only a simple slip of the tongue. यह बस एक साधारण ज़ुबान फिसलने की बात थी, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं। આ ફક્ત એક સામાન્ય જીભ લપસવાની વાત હતી, તેનાથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં.
簡単な日常英会話ができるにすぎない。Kantan na nichijou eikaiwa ga dekiru ni suginai. I can only have simple everyday conversations in English. मैं बस सामान्य रोज़मर्रा की अंग्रेज़ी बातचीत ही कर सकता हूँ, इससे ज़्यादा नहीं। હું ફક્ત સામાન્ય રોજિંદી અંગ્રેજી વાતચીત જ કરી શકું છું, તેનાથી વધુ નહીં.

This pattern loves modest, self-deprecating framing — 単に…にすぎない, あくまで(も)…にすぎない, ただのNにすぎない, ほんのNにすぎない are all fixed ways of saying "it's really nothing special."

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

約束は（　）。Yakusoku wa (___).

Full sentence: 約束は守らざるをえない。Yakusoku wa mamorazaru o enai.
EN: I have no choice but to keep my promise.
HI: वादा निभाने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं।
GU: વચન પાળવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a守らざるをえないmamorazaru o enaihave no choice but to keep (point 2)निभाने के अलावा कोई चारा नहींપાળવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી
b守らないべきだmamoranai beki da(wrong — べき doesn't attach to a negative like this; also contradicts the intended meaning)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)

Why: Keeping a promise despite reluctance is exactly point 2's ざるをえない — べきだ would describe an ideal/duty, not a forced circumstance, and 守らないべきだ is also the wrong shape.

Q2

Full sentence: その計画は不可能だと言わざるをえない。Sono keikaku wa fukanou da to iwazaru o enai.
EN: I have no choice but to say that this plan is impossible.
HI: यह कहने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं कि यह योजना असंभव है।
GU: એમ કહેવા સિવાય કોઈ રસ્તો નથી કે આ યોજના અશક્ય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aえるeru(wrong — the fixed set phrase is ざるをえない, not ざるをえる)(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)
bえないenaicompletes the fixed phrase ざるをえない (point 2)चारा नहीं (निश्चित रूप)રસ્તો નથી (નિશ્ચિત સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ざるをえない is a fixed phrase — it's always negative えない, never affirmative える.

Q3

Full sentence: 彼の言うことは理想にすぎない。Kare no iu koto wa risou ni suginai.
EN: What he says is nothing more than an ideal.
HI: वह जो कहता है, वह सिर्फ़ एक आदर्श भर है, इससे ज़्यादा कुछ नहीं।
GU: તે જે કહે છે, તે ફક્ત એક આદર્શ છે, તેનાથી વધુ કંઈ નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aありえないarienai(wrong — "impossible/can't be," not "merely")(ग़लत विकल्प)(ખોટો વિકલ્પ)
bすぎないsuginaiis nothing more than (point 4)सिर्फ़ ...है, इससे ज़्यादा नहींફક્ત ...છે, તેનાથી વધુ નહીં

Why: The sentence downplays his words as merely idealistic — point 4's にすぎない, not ありえない (which would mean the opposite, "impossible").

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

明日の会議は、第一会議室＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ なっています。Ashita no kaigi wa, dai-ichi kaigishitsu ___ natte imasu.

Tiles: 1 こと (koto)　2 で (de)　3 行われる (okonawareru)　4 に (ni)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores rather than a single ★ blank, unlike the official JLPT format.)

2 で→3 行われる→1 こと→4 に

明日の会議は、第一会議室で行われることになっています。Ashita no kaigi wa, dai-ichi kaigishitsu de okonawareru koto ni natte imasu.

EN: Tomorrow's meeting is scheduled to be held in Conference Room 1.
HI: कल की बैठक पहले सम्मेलन कक्ष में होना तय है।
GU: આવતીકાલની બેઠક પ્રથમ કોન્ફરન્સ રૂમમાં યોજાવાનું નક્કી છે.

Why: Built on point 3 (〜ことになっている) — an already-arranged schedule (行われることになっています) for where the meeting takes place.

Q7

法律は＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ と思います。Houritsu wa ___ to omoimasu.

Tiles: 1 ある (aru)　2 正しく (tadashiku)　3 だ (da)　4 べき (beki)

2 正しく→1 ある→4 べき→3 だ

法律は正しくあるべきだと思います。Houritsu wa tadashiku aru beki da to omoimasu.

EN: I think the law should be fair/correct.
HI: मेरा मानना है कि क़ानून निष्पक्ष होना चाहिए।
GU: મારું માનવું છે કે કાયદો ન્યાયી હોવો જોઈએ.

Why: Built on point 1's affirmative べきだ (an い-adjective's ある-form, 正しくある, plus べきだ) — a statement of what a law ideally ought to be.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points all describe different flavors of obligation, necessity, and limitation, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q4, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜べきではない~beki de wa nai Should not ~ Vる（する→すべき） + べきではない A personal/moral judgment of what's proper — the speaker's own opinion. Giving advice, criticizing a choice, expressing regret (〜べきではなかった).
〜ざるをえない~zaru o enai Have no choice but to ~ V-ない-stem（する→せ） + ざるをえない Circumstances force a reluctant action — not about right/wrong, just necessity. "I don't want to, but I must."
〜ことになっている~koto ni natte iru It is arranged/decided that ~ Vる／Vない／Nという + ことになっている Describes an external rule, schedule, or arrangement — not the speaker's opinion or forced action. Stating a school/company/exam rule or a fixed schedule.
〜にすぎない~ni suginai Is nothing more than ~ V／A／な-adj-である／N + にすぎない 硬 — downplays significance; unrelated to obligation, purely about scale/importance. Minimizing a claim: "it's only this, nothing more."
Exam trap: べきではない (opinion) and ことになっている (external rule) both get used for "you shouldn't/can't do X," but they answer different questions — べきではない answers "is this the right thing to do?" while ことになっている answers "what does the rule/schedule say?" Q4 and Q5 above are a matched pair built to test exactly this distinction: an exam regulation takes ことになっている, but a safety judgment based on reasoning takes べきではない.
