Answer key

Notes
Point 1 bundles three near-synonyms under one banner: 決まっている carries a speech-bubble icon (casual/conversational), while 相違ない is tagged 硬 formal/written; 違いない sits in between as the everyday standard. ほかない and にほかならない are both also tagged 硬. Watch the na-adjective/noun connection exception noted in the source's own formation box (drop だ before に for these three forms).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に決まっている／に違いない／に相違ない

💬 決まっている casual; 相違ない 硬
Reading~ni kimatte iru / ni chigainai / ni souinai
Meaning (EN)"bound to be ~ / must be ~" — states strong, confident certainty about a fact or outcome
Meaning (HI)...होना तय है / ज़रूर ...ही है
Meaning (GU)...થવાનું જ છે / ચોક્કસ ...જ છે
Connection (接続)V/A-普通形 + に決まっている／に違いない／に相違ない　|　❶な-adj/N + に（だ抜き）
Registerに決まっている is conversational (speech-bubble icon); に違いない is neutral/standard; に相違ない is 硬 formal/written
Typical useAll three express near-total confidence, ranked roughly from casual to formal: 決まっている（casual) < 違いない（neutral) < 相違ない（formal/written, common in legal or investigative contexts).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)V-普通形 + に決まっている勝つに決まっているkatsu ni kimatte iru
Verb (た-form)V-た + に違いないやったに違いないyatta ni chigainai
Noun (drop だ)N + に相違ない犯人に相違ないhannin ni souinai
な-adjective (drop だ)(added, standard rule) な-adj + に違いない／に相違ない元気に違いないgenki ni chigainai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あのチームが勝つに決まっている。Ano chiimu ga katsu ni kimatte iru. That team is bound to win. वह टीम जीतेगी ही, यह तय है। તે ટીમ જીતવાની જ છે, એ નક્કી છે.
彼がやったに違いない。Kare ga yatta ni chigainai. He must have done it. यह उसी ने किया होगा, इसमें कोई शक नहीं। આ તેણે જ કર્યું હશે, તેમાં કોઈ શંકા નથી.
彼が犯人に相違ない。Kare ga hannin ni souinai. He must be the culprit. वही अपराधी है, इसमें कोई संदेह नहीं। તે જ ગુનેગાર છે, તેમાં કોઈ શંકા નથી.

Notice the connection exception: な-adjectives and nouns drop だ before all three of these (元気に違いない, 犯人に相違ない, not 元気だに違いない) — this is a common slip to watch for.

【2】 〜とは限らない

neutral register
Reading~to wa kagiranai
Meaning (EN)"it's not necessarily the case that ~" — denies that something is always/certainly true, leaving room for exceptions
Meaning (HI)यह ज़रूरी नहीं कि हमेशा ...ही हो
Meaning (GU)એ જરૂરી નથી કે હંમેશા ...જ થાય
Connection (接続)V/A/な-adj/N-普通形 + とは限らない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe direct logical opposite of Point 1's に決まっている — it explicitly rejects certainty, often after 必ずしも／必ず.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain)V-普通形 + とは限らない勝つとは限らないkatsu to wa kagiranai
Verb (negative plain)V-ない + とは限らない出ないとは限らないdenai to wa kagiranai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
相手が弱いチームであっても、必ず勝つとは限らない。Aite ga yowai chiimu de atte mo, kanarazu katsu to wa kagiranai. Even if the opponent is a weak team, it's not necessarily true we'll always win. भले ही प्रतिद्वंद्वी टीम कमज़ोर हो, यह ज़रूरी नहीं कि हम हमेशा जीतें ही। ભલે વિરોધી ટીમ નબળી હોય, એ જરૂરી નથી કે અમે હંમેશા જીતીએ જ.
あまり使わない表現だが、必ずしもテストに出ないとは限らない。Amari tsukawanai hyougen da ga, kanarazushimo tesuto ni denai to wa kagiranai. It's an expression not used much, but that doesn't necessarily mean it will never appear on the test. यह अभिव्यक्ति ज़्यादा इस्तेमाल नहीं होती, लेकिन यह ज़रूरी नहीं कि यह परीक्षा में कभी न आए। આ અભિવ્યક્તિ બહુ વપરાતી નથી, પણ એ જરૂરી નથી કે તે પરીક્ષામાં ક્યારેય ન આવે.

This is a deliberate counterweight to Point 1 in today's lineup — the book pairs "certain" (決まっている／違いない／相違ない) directly against "not necessarily certain" (とは限らない) so learners feel the contrast.

【3】 〜（より）ほかない

硬 formal/written
Reading~(yori) hokanai
Meaning (EN)"have no choice but to ~ / there's nothing to do except ~"
Meaning (HI)...के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं
Meaning (GU)...સિવાય બીજો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી
Connection (接続)V-る（より）+ ほかない／ほかはない　(=〜しかない)
Register硬 — formal, deliberate tone
Typical usePresents the stated action as the sole remaining option once all alternatives are exhausted.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary form)V-る（より）+ ほかない祈る（より）ほかないinoru (yori) hokanai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
全力をつくした。あとは祈る（より）ほかない。Zenryoku o tsukushita. Ato wa inoru (yori) hokanai. I gave it everything I had. Now there's nothing left to do but pray. मैंने पूरी ताक़त झोंक दी। अब बस प्रार्थना करने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं। મેં પૂરી તાકાત લગાવી. હવે ફક્ત પ્રાર્થના કરવા સિવાય બીજો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.
もう後には戻れない。前進する（より）ほかない。Mou ato ni wa modorenai. Zenshin suru (yori) hokanai. There's no going back now. I have no choice but to move forward. अब पीछे मुड़ना संभव नहीं। आगे बढ़ने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं। હવે પાછળ ફરવું શક્ય નથી. આગળ વધવા સિવાય બીજો કોઈ રસ્તો નથી.

より is optional (both ほかない and よりほかない appear), but always attaches to the plain dictionary form of the verb — never a masu-stem or noun without more support.

【4】 〜にほかならない

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni hokanaranai
Meaning (EN)"is nothing other than ~ / is precisely ~" — asserts that something is exactly and only this one thing, ruling out any other explanation
Meaning (HI)...के अलावा और कुछ नहीं / यह ठीक ...ही है
Meaning (GU)...સિવાય બીજું કંઈ નથી / આ બરાબર ...જ છે
Connection (接続)N + にほかならない
Register硬 — formal, common in essays and reasoned argument
Typical useStrongly asserts a single cause/identity, closing off alternative explanations — "this and only this is the true reason."
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + にほかならない努力の結果にほかならないdoryoku no kekka ni hokanaranai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
合格したのは、彼の努力の結果にほかならない。Goukaku shita no wa, kare no doryoku no kekka ni hokanaranai. The fact that he passed is nothing other than the result of his own effort. उसका पास होना उसकी मेहनत का ही नतीजा है, और कुछ नहीं। તેનું પાસ થવું તેની મહેનતનું જ પરિણામ છે, બીજું કંઈ નહીં.
彼があなたをからかうのは、まさに愛情表現にほかならない。Kare ga anata o karakau no wa, masani aijou hyougen ni hokanaranai. The reason he teases you is nothing other than an expression of his affection. उसका आपको छेड़ना, यह उसके प्यार का ही इज़हार है, और कुछ नहीं। તેનું તમને ચીડવવું, એ તેની લાગણીની જ અભિવ્યક્તિ છે, બીજું કંઈ નહીં.

にほかならない is often mistaken for に相違ない (Point 1) since both feel like "definitely" — but ほかならない is about identity/attribution ("this IS the reason/thing, nothing else"), while 相違ない is about certainty of a fact ("this is surely true"). See the confusion table below.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points form two opposing pairs — certainty vs. its denial, and "no other option" vs. "no other identity" — which is exactly why the book groups them together. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningRegisterKey differenceUse when
〜に決まっている~ni kimatte iru Bound to be ~ (casual confidence) 💬 conversational The most casual of the three certainty forms. Everyday speech, confident predictions.
〜に違いない~ni chigainai Must be ~ (standard confidence) Neutral The everyday-standard middle ground of the three. General writing and speech alike.
〜に相違ない~ni souinai Must be ~ (formal confidence) 硬 formal Formal register — investigations, legal/official contexts. Written reports, formal declarations.
〜とは限らない~to wa kagiranai Not necessarily ~ Neutral The direct opposite of all three certainty forms above — denies certainty. You want to leave room for exceptions.
〜（より）ほかない~(yori) hokanai Have no choice but to ~ 硬 formal About lacking any other course of action (a decision/behavior). All alternatives are exhausted; only one action remains.
〜にほかならない~ni hokanaranai Is nothing other than ~ 硬 formal About identity/cause — ruling out any other explanation for what something IS. Asserting the one true reason/identity behind a fact.
Exam trap: ほかない and にほかならない look almost identical in spelling but test completely different ideas — ほかない is about actions/choices ("nothing left to do but X"), while にほかならない is about identity/cause ("this IS X, and nothing else"). Also, に相違ない (certainty about a fact) is easy to confuse with にほかならない (identity of a fact) — ask "is this confirming that something IS true" (相違ない) or "is this saying WHAT something truly is" (ほかならない)?
