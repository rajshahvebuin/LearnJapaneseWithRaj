1日目 — 決めた以上 (Kimeta ijou)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Bunpou) — printed p.94–95 (PDF pages 94–95, no offset)
Grammar points covered
Nに来て以来V-る以上（は）V-たからにはNの折には
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.97 (2日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points mean roughly "since/now that ~", but they differ in what follows logically (a lasting state vs. a determined resolve) and in register — 折には is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; the other three are register-neutral. This day's own quiz format is ○/× true-false judgment (練習Ⅰ) plus a 4-option fill-in-the-blank (練習Ⅱ) — not the two-choice circling format used on most other days this week.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜に来て以来

neutral register
Reading~ni kite irai
Meaning (EN)"ever since ~" — marks the starting point of a state that has continued unbroken up to now
Meaning (HI)...के बाद से (अब तक लगातार)
Meaning (GU)...પછીથી (અત્યાર સુધી સતત)
Connection (接続)N + 以来　|　V-て + 以来　|　それ以来／あれ以来
RegisterNeutral — used in both writing and speech
Typical useThe clause that follows almost always describes a state that has continued without a break (often with ない — "haven't done X since"), not a one-off event.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + 以来来て以来kite irai
NounN + 以来入学以来nyuugaku irai
Demonstrativeそれ／あれ + 以来あれ以来are irai
い-adjective / な-adjective(added, not in book; not a natural fit — 以来 needs a clear starting-point event, not a state description)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
日本に来て以来、母の料理を食べていない。Nihon ni kite irai, haha no ryouri o tabete inai. I haven't eaten my mother's cooking since I came to Japan. जापान आने के बाद से, मैंने माँ के हाथ का खाना नहीं खाया। જાપાન આવ્યા પછીથી, મેં માની રસોઈ ખાધી નથી.
入学以来、一度も授業を休んでいない。Nyuugaku irai, ichido mo jugyou o yasunde inai. I haven't missed a single class since enrolling. दाख़िला लेने के बाद से, मैंने एक भी क्लास नहीं छोड़ी। પ્રવેશ લીધા પછીથી, મેં એક પણ વર્ગ ચૂક્યો નથી.

以来 is close to 〜てから ("ever since ~", 〜てからずっと), but it leans more formal/written and pairs naturally with a negative continuing state. Don't use it for a single completed action — it wants an unbroken state from that starting point to now.

【2】 〜以上（は）

neutral register
Reading~ijou (wa)
Meaning (EN)"now that / given that ~ (it naturally follows that...)" — states a premise and draws an obligation or resolve from it
Meaning (HI)चूँकि ...है, तो ज़ाहिर है कि...
Meaning (GU)...છે એટલે, દેખીતું છે કે...
Connection (接続)V-る／V-た + 以上（は）　|　Nである + 以上（は）
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal — common in speeches, resolutions, essays
Typical useCarries a feeling of 当然 ("of course, naturally") — having accepted the premise, the speaker treats the consequence as unavoidable or a matter of duty. Nearly identical in meaning to 〜からには and 〜上は.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary/た-form)V-る／V-た + 以上（は）来た以上はkita ijou wa
NounNである + 以上（は）(added, not shown this day; standard rule) 学生である以上gakusei de aru ijou
い-adjective / な-adjective(added, not in book; rare — 以上 targets decisions/facts more than descriptive states)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
試験を受ける以上、いい点を取りたい。Shiken o ukeru ijou, ii ten o toritai. Now that I'm taking the exam, I want to get a good score. चूँकि परीक्षा दे ही रहा हूँ, तो अच्छे अंक लाना चाहता हूँ। પરીક્ષા આપી જ રહ્યો છું એટલે, સારા ગુણ મેળવવા માંગુ છું.
日本に来た以上は、日本語ができるようになりたい。Nihon ni kita ijou wa, nihongo ga dekiru you ni naritai. Now that I've come to Japan, I want to become able to speak Japanese. जापान आ ही गया हूँ, तो जापानी भाषा बोलना सीखना चाहता हूँ। જાપાન આવી જ ગયો છું, તો જાપાનીઝ બોલતાં શીખવા માંગુ છું.

Cross-reference: this book treats 〜以上（は）as equivalent to 〜上は (see p.62) — both mark a premise from which a natural, almost obligatory conclusion follows. Compare directly with Point 3 below (〜からには), which shares the same "given that ~" logic in a slightly more everyday register.

【3】 〜からには

neutral register
Reading~kara ni wa
Meaning (EN)"since/now that ~ (one should/must...)" — the same "given-that" logic as 以上（は）, worded a touch more conversationally
Meaning (HI)चूँकि ...कर ही दिया/लिया है, तो...
Meaning (GU)...કરી જ દીધું છે એટલે...
Connection (接続)V-る／V-た + からには
RegisterNeutral — usable in speech as freely as in writing
Typical use=以上／上は; also carries the 当然 nuance of a natural, expected obligation once the premise is accepted.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary/た-form)V-る／V-た + からには約束したからにはyakusoku shita kara ni wa
Noun / adjective(added, not shown this day; standard rule) Nである／A-い + からには—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
約束したからには、守るべきだ。Yakusoku shita kara ni wa, mamoru beki da. Since you made a promise, you should keep it. चूँकि वादा कर लिया है, तो उसे निभाना ही चाहिए। વચન આપી જ દીધું છે એટલે, તે નિભાવવું જ જોઈએ.
試合に出るからには、勝ちたい。Shiai ni deru kara ni wa, kachitai. Since I'll be competing in the match, I want to win. चूँकि मुक़ाबले में उतर ही रहा हूँ, तो जीतना चाहता हूँ। સ્પર્ધામાં ઉતરી જ રહ્યો છું એટલે, જીતવા માંગુ છું.

Functionally interchangeable with 以上（は）and 上は in nearly every sentence on this page — the exam rarely distinguishes between the three, so treat them as a synonym cluster rather than hunting for a subtle difference.

【4】 〜の折には

硬 formal/written
Reading~no ori ni wa
Meaning (EN)"on the occasion of ~ / when ~ happens" — a formal, polite way to say "when"
Meaning (HI)...के अवसर पर / जब भी ...हो
Meaning (GU)...ના પ્રસંગે / જ્યારે પણ ...થાય
Connection (接続)V-る／V-た／Nの + 折（に）／折（に）は
Register硬 — formal, very common in polite business letters, invitations, and postcards
Typical useA courteous substitute for 〜ときに／〜たときに; often paired with polite requests (お立ち寄りください, ご説明申し上げます).
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (dictionary/た-form)V-る／V-た + 折にお目にかかった折にo-me ni kakatta ori ni
NounNの + 折には来日の折にはrainichi no ori ni wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
来日の折には、ぜひこちらにお立ち寄りください。Rainichi no ori ni wa, zehi kochira ni o-tachiyori kudasai. When you come to Japan, please be sure to stop by here. जब भी आप जापान आएँ, ज़रूर यहाँ आइएगा। જ્યારે પણ તમે જાપાન આવો, ચોક્કસ અહીં આવજો.
次にお目にかかった折に、お借りしていた本をお返しします。Tsugi ni o-me ni kakatta ori ni, o-kari shite ita hon o o-kaeshi shimasu. The next time we meet, I'll return the book I borrowed from you. अगली बार जब हम मिलें, तो मैं आपकी उधार ली हुई किताब लौटा दूँगा। આગલી વખતે જ્યારે આપણે મળીશું, ત્યારે મેં ઉછીની લીધેલી ચોપડી પાછી આપીશ.

Reserve 折には for polite written register — letters, invitations, formal spoken announcements. It would sound stiff/out of place in casual daily conversation, where 〜ときに is the natural choice.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 true/false items) and 練習Ⅱ (3 four-option fill-in-the-blank items), both from p.95. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.97.

練習Ⅰ — mark ○ (correct) or × (incorrect)

Q1

入学以上は、もちろん卒業したい。Nyuugaku ijou wa, mochiron sotsugyou shitai.

Judgment: × incorrect — 入学以上→ 入学する以上／入学した以上
Corrected: 入学する以上は、もちろん卒業したい。Nyuugaku suru ijou wa, mochiron sotsugyou shitai.
EN: Now that I'm enrolling, of course I want to graduate.
HI: चूँकि दाख़िला ले ही रहा हूँ, तो ज़ाहिर है स्नातक होना चाहता हूँ।
GU: પ્રવેશ લઈ જ રહ્યો છું એટલે, દેખીતું છે કે સ્નાતક થવા માંગુ છું.

Why: 以上 must attach to a verb (V-る／V-た), never directly to a bare noun like 入学 — the noun needs する added first (入学する以上) or the past form (入学した以上).

Q2

卒業して以来ですから、10年ぶりですね。Sotsugyou shite irai desu kara, juu-nen buri desu ne.

Judgment: ○ correct
EN: Since it's been like this ever since graduation, it's been 10 years, hasn't it.
HI: स्नातक होने के बाद से ऐसा है, तो यह 10 साल बाद है, है ना।
GU: સ્નાતક થયા પછીથી આવું છે, તો આ 10 વર્ષ પછી છે, ખરું ને.

Why: V-て + 以来 is exactly the standard formation — nothing to fix here.

Q3

自分で決めたことからには、大変でもがんばろう。Jibun de kimeta koto kara ni wa, taihen demo ganbarou.

Judgment: × incorrect — 決めたことからには→ 決めたからには
Corrected: 自分で決めたからには、大変でもがんばろう。Jibun de kimeta kara ni wa, taihen demo ganbarou.
EN: Since I decided this myself, I'll do my best even if it's hard.
HI: चूँकि मैंने ख़ुद तय किया है, तो मुश्किल होने पर भी मेहनत करूँगा।
GU: મેં જાતે નક્કી કર્યું જ છે એટલે, મુશ્કેલ હોવા છતાં મહેનત કરીશ.

Why: からには attaches directly to the verb's plain form (決めた) — inserting こと (決めたことからには) is redundant and ungrammatical here.

Q4

この件は後ほど会議折にくわしくご説明申し上げます。Kono ken wa nochihodo kaigi ori ni kuwashiku go-setsumei moushiagemasu.

Judgment: × incorrect — 会議折に→ 会議の折に
Corrected: この件は後ほど会議の折にくわしくご説明申し上げます。Kono ken wa nochihodo kaigi no ori ni kuwashiku go-setsumei moushiagemasu.
EN: I will explain this matter in detail later, at the meeting.
HI: इस मामले पर मैं बाद में बैठक के अवसर पर विस्तार से बताऊँगा।
GU: આ બાબત વિશે હું પછીથી બેઠકના પ્રસંગે વિગતવાર જણાવીશ.

Why: A noun must be linked to 折 with の (会議の折に) — a bare noun directly in front of 折 is not the correct connection.

Q5

会議に出席するからには、自分の意見を言うべきだ。Kaigi ni shusseki suru kara ni wa, jibun no iken o iu beki da.

Judgment: ○ correct
EN: Since you're attending the meeting, you should voice your own opinion.
HI: चूँकि आप बैठक में शामिल हो ही रहे हैं, तो अपनी राय ज़रूर रखनी चाहिए।
GU: બેઠકમાં હાજર રહી જ રહ્યા છો એટલે, પોતાનો અભિપ્રાય જણાવવો જ જોઈએ.

Why: V-る + からには is exactly the standard formation — nothing to fix here.

練習Ⅱ — choose the best option (1〜4)

Q6

「犬を飼ってもいいけど、（　）は自分で世話をするんですよ。」"Inu o katte mo ii kedo, (___) wa jibun de sewa o suru n desu yo."

Full sentence: 「犬を飼ってもいいけど、飼う以上は自分で世話をするんですよ。」"...kau ijou wa jibun de sewa o suru n desu yo."
EN: "You can keep a dog, but once you decide to, you look after it yourself, understand?"
HI: "कुत्ता पाल सकते हो, लेकिन एक बार पालने का फ़ैसला कर लिया, तो उसकी देखभाल ख़ुद करनी होगी।"
GU: "કૂતરો પાળી શકે છે, પણ પાળવાનું નક્કી કરી લીધું એટલે, તેની સંભાળ જાતે રાખવી પડશે."
#JapaneseEnglish
1飼う以上kau ijounow that you've decided to keep one
2飼って以来katte iraiever since keeping one (wrong sense — no ongoing negative state)
3飼ってからにkatte kara ni(incomplete form — needs は)
4飼って以上katte ijou(wrong connection — 以上 needs dictionary/た-form, not て-form)

Why: Point 2's 〜以上（は） — deciding to keep a dog naturally obligates the responsibility of caring for it yourself.

Q7

「週末に外食を（　）、おなかの調子が悪いんです。」"Shuumatsu ni gaishoku o (___), onaka no choushi ga warui n desu."

Full sentence: 「週末に外食をして以来、おなかの調子が悪いんです。」"...gaishoku o shite irai, onaka no choushi ga warui n desu."
EN: "Ever since I ate out over the weekend, my stomach has been off."
HI: "सप्ताहांत में बाहर खाना खाने के बाद से, मेरा पेट ख़राब है।"
GU: "અઠવાડિયાના અંતે બહાર જમ્યા પછીથી, મારું પેટ ખરાબ છે."
#JapaneseEnglish
1した以来shita irai(wrong connection — 以来 needs て-form, not た-form)
2して以来shite iraiever since (doing)
3した以上shita ijounow that (wrong sense — no obligation implied)
4したからにはshita kara ni wasince (wrong sense — same issue as 以上)

Why: Point 1's 〜て以来 — the stomach trouble is an ongoing state that began at that point and hasn't stopped, which is exactly what 以来 flags.

Q8

「先生の大切な本を（　）、難しくても読まなければと思います。」"Sensei no taisetsu na hon o (___), muzukashikute mo yomanakereba to omoimasu."

Full sentence: 「先生の大切な本を貸していただいた以上、難しくても読まなければと思います。」"...kashite itadaita ijou, muzukashikute mo yomanakereba to omoimasu."
EN: "Now that my teacher has been kind enough to lend me this important book, I feel I must read it even if it's difficult."
HI: "चूँकि गुरुजी ने अपनी क़ीमती किताब उधार दी है, तो मुश्किल होने पर भी उसे पढ़ना ही चाहिए, ऐसा लगता है।"
GU: "ગુરુજીએ પોતાનું મહત્વનું પુસ્તક ઉછીનું આપ્યું જ છે, તો અઘરું હોવા છતાં તે વાંચવું જ જોઈએ, એવું લાગે છે."
#JapaneseEnglish
1貸して以来kashite iraiever since lending (wrong subject/sense)
2貸した以上kashita ijou(wrong politeness level — no keigo for the teacher's favor)
3貸していただいた以上kashite itadaita ijounow that (I've had it) kindly lent to me
4借りていただいて以来karite itadaite irai(wrong verb direction — 借りていただく is backwards keigo)

Why: ～ていただく politely marks the teacher's favor of lending the book (receiving the favor of being lent); 以上 then draws the natural obligation — since I've been granted this kindness, I must honor it by reading carefully.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's points translate loosely to "since/now that ~" in English, which is exactly why they're grouped together and easy to mix up. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜に来て以来~ni kite irai Ever since ~ N／V-て + 以来 Marks a starting point; what follows is a continuing state (often negative — "haven't done X since"). You want to describe an unbroken state that started at a clear point and hasn't changed.
〜以上（は）~ijou (wa) Now that ~ (naturally...) V-る／V-た＋以上（は） Draws an obligation/resolve from an accepted premise; slightly formal register. Essays, speeches, resolutions — a considered, somewhat formal conclusion.
〜からには~kara ni wa Since ~ (naturally...) V-る／V-た＋からには Same logic as 以上（は）, but usable just as comfortably in everyday speech. Anywhere 以上（は） fits, especially in conversation.
〜の折には~no ori ni wa On the occasion of ~ / when ~ V-る／V-た／Nの＋折（に）は A polite, formal stand-in for 〜ときに — no obligation/resolve nuance at all. Polite letters, invitations, formal announcements.
Exam trap: 以上（は） and からには are near-perfect synonyms — the test rarely asks you to choose between them, but it does test whether you can tell them apart from 以来 (a continuing state, no obligation nuance) and 折には (a polite "when," no obligation nuance either). Ask yourself: "Does this sentence draw a resolve/obligation from a premise?" If yes → 以上／からには. If it's just marking a starting point → 以来. If it's just a polite "when" → 折には.
