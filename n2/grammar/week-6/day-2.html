2日目 — ぼくから見ると (Boku kara miru to)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.96–97 (PDF pages 96–97, no offset)
Grammar points covered
Nから言うと／言えばNからすると／すれば／いってNからしてNから見ると
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.99 (3日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points are register-neutral (no 硬 tags this day). The one hard grammatical trap: 〜から言うと／言えば cannot take a person directly as its noun (a bare 人 before から言うと is marked ダメ in the source) — you need their standpoint/opinion instead (Nの立場から言うと, Nの考えから言うと). 〜から見ると, by contrast, explicitly can take a person directly (人から見ると is marked OK).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜から言うと／言えば

neutral register
Reading~kara iu to / ieba
Meaning (EN)"speaking from the standpoint of ~" — states which viewpoint the following opinion is based on
Meaning (HI)...के नज़रिए से कहें तो
Meaning (GU)...ના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી કહીએ તો
Connection (接続)Nから + 言うと／言えば　(れい N の考えから言うと、その点から言うと)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useIntroduces the speaker's own judgment/opinion as seen from a specific standpoint. Cannot attach directly to a person — 〈人〉から言うと is marked ダメ in the source; use their立場/考え instead.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (standpoint)Nから + 言うと／言えば客の立場から言うとkyaku no tachiba kara iu to
Person (NG)✗ 〈人〉から言うと is not used — say 〈人〉の立場／考えから言うと instead—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
客の立場から言うと、この店は入り口がせまくて入りにくい。Kyaku no tachiba kara iu to, kono mise wa iriguchi ga semakute hairinikui. From the customer's standpoint, this shop's entrance is narrow and hard to enter. ग्राहक के नज़रिए से कहें तो, इस दुकान का प्रवेश द्वार संकरा है और अंदर जाना मुश्किल है। ગ્રાહકના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી કહીએ તો, આ દુકાનનું પ્રવેશદ્વાર સાંકડું છે અને અંદર જવું અઘરું છે.
しかし、店の側から言えば管理しやすい。Shikashi, mise no gawa kara ieba kanri shiyasui. However, from the shop's side, it's easy to manage. लेकिन, दुकान की ओर से कहें तो, इसका प्रबंधन करना आसान है। પણ, દુકાનની બાજુથી કહીએ તો, તેનું સંચાલન કરવું સહેલું છે.

Treat から言うと／言えば as introducing a stated opinion built on a specific standpoint — it never attaches straight to a person's name; you must route through their 立場 (standpoint) or 考え (way of thinking) first.

【2】 〜からすると／すれば／いって

neutral register
Reading~kara suru to / sureba / itte
Meaning (EN)"judging from ~" — draws a conclusion or guess from visible evidence
Meaning (HI)...को देखते हुए / ...से अंदाज़ा लगाया जाए तो
Meaning (GU)...ને જોતાં / ...પરથી અંદાજ લગાવીએ તો
Connection (接続)Nから + すると／すれば／いって
RegisterNeutral
Typical useBases an inference or guess on observable evidence (symptoms, attitude, appearance) — close to 〜から考えると.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (evidence)Nから + すると／すれば／いって症状からするとshoujou kara suru to
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
症状からすると、心臓の病気かもしれません。Shoujou kara suru to, shinzou no byouki kamo shiremasen. Judging from the symptoms, it could be a heart condition. लक्षणों को देखते हुए, यह दिल की बीमारी हो सकती है। લક્ષણોને જોતાં, આ હૃદયની બીમારી હોઈ શકે છે.
周りの態度からすると、あの方が社長ではないでしょうか。Mawari no taido kara suru to, ano kata ga shachou de wa nai deshou ka. Judging from the attitude of those around him, isn't he probably the president? आस-पास के लोगों के व्यवहार को देखते हुए, वे शायद कंपनी के अध्यक्ष हैं। આસપાસના લોકોના વર્તનને જોતાં, તેઓ કદાચ કંપનીના પ્રમુખ છે.

Unlike から言うと (a stated opinion from a standpoint), からすると draws an inference from evidence — think of it as "based on the clues I can see, I'd guess...".

【3】 〜からして

neutral register
Reading~karashite
Meaning (EN)"judging just from ~ alone" — cites one representative example/detail to support a broader judgment
Meaning (HI)...को देखकर ही (बाक़ी का अंदाज़ा लगाया जा सकता है)
Meaning (GU)...ને જોઈને જ (બાકીનો અંદાજ લગાવી શકાય)
Connection (接続)Nからして
RegisterNeutral
Typical use代表的なものを例にあげる — cites something representative as a single example, implying "and everything else follows the same pattern."
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (representative detail)Nからして服装からしてfukusou karashite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼は服装からしてだらしない。きっと、ほかの面も同じだろう。Kare wa fukusou karashite darashinai. Kitto, hoka no men mo onaji darou. Just his clothes alone are sloppy — surely everything else about him is the same. उसके कपड़े देखकर ही पता चलता है कि वह लापरवाह है — बाक़ी चीज़ें भी वैसी ही होंगी। તેના કપડાં જોઈને જ ખબર પડે છે કે તે બેદરકાર છે — બાકીની બાબતો પણ એવી જ હશે.
あの映画は、題名からして悲しそうだ。Ano eiga wa, daimei karashite kanashisou da. Just the title of that movie alone sounds sad. उस फ़िल्म का नाम देखकर ही लगता है कि यह दुखद होगी। તે ફિલ્મનું નામ જોઈને જ લાગે છે કે તે દુ:ખદ હશે.

からして is narrower than からすると — it singles out one telling detail (clothes, a title) as a representative sample, rather than weighing broad evidence.

【4】 〜から見ると

neutral register
Reading~kara miru to
Meaning (EN)"seen from ~'s viewpoint" — states a viewpoint, and (unlike から言うと) can take a person directly
Meaning (HI)...की नज़र से देखा जाए तो
Meaning (GU)...ની નજરે જોઈએ તો
Connection (接続)Nから + 見ると／見れば／見て／見ても　(OK 〈人〉から見ると)
RegisterNeutral
Typical usePresents how something appears from a given viewpoint — this is the one form in today's set explicitly marked OK to take a person directly.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun / personNから + 見ると／見れば／見て／見ても外国人から見るとgaikokujin kara miru to
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
日本の習慣には、外国人から見ると妙なものもあるだろう。Nihon no shuukan ni wa, gaikokujin kara miru to myou na mono mo aru darou. Some Japanese customs probably seem strange when seen from a foreigner's viewpoint. विदेशियों की नज़र से देखा जाए तो, जापान के कुछ रीति-रिवाज़ अजीब लग सकते हैं। વિદેશીઓની નજરે જોઈએ તો, જાપાનના કેટલાક રિવાજો વિચિત્ર લાગી શકે છે.
昔の人から見ると、現代人の生活のリズムは速すぎるかもしれない。Mukashi no hito kara miru to, gendaijin no seikatsu no rizumu wa hayasugiru kamo shirenai. Seen from the viewpoint of people in the past, the pace of modern life might seem too fast. पुराने ज़माने के लोगों की नज़र से देखा जाए तो, आज की ज़िंदगी की रफ़्तार शायद बहुत तेज़ लगे। જૂના જમાનાના લોકોની નજરે જોઈએ તો, આજની જિંદગીની ગતિ કદાચ બહુ ઝડપી લાગે.

The key distinguishing fact this day drills is precisely this: から見ると welcomes a person directly (外国人から見ると, 人から見ると), while から言うと does not — you must dress it up with 立場/考え first when the source is a person.

Q1

この子は、笑い方（　）母親にそっくりだ。Kono ko wa, warai-kata (___) hahaoya ni sokkuri da.

EN: Just this child's way of laughing alone is exactly like their mother's.
HI: इस बच्चे के हँसने के तरीक़े से ही पता चलता है कि यह अपनी माँ जैसा है।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms attach to Nから and translate loosely as "from ~'s perspective" — this is exactly the cluster the day's own dialogue quiz (Q6–Q9) is built to test. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningCan take a person directly?Key differenceUse when
〜から言うと／言えば~kara iu to / ieba Speaking from the standpoint of ~ No — needs の立場／考え first States an opinion/judgment from a named standpoint. You're voicing your own take, framed by a specific standpoint.
〜からすると／すれば／いって~kara suru to / sureba / itte Judging from ~ (evidence) Yes, but usually broad evidence (symptoms, attitude), not a bare person Draws an inference/guess from visible evidence. You're reasoning from clues to a likely conclusion.
〜からして~karashite Judging just from ~ alone Not typically a bare person Cites one representative sample/detail as proof of the whole. You want to say "even just this one thing tells you everything."
〜から見ると~kara miru to Seen from ~'s viewpoint Yes — explicitly OK with a bare person The one form in this set that welcomes a person directly as the viewpoint. You want to describe how something looks from someone's (or something's) perspective.
Exam trap: The single hardest-tested distinction this day is から言う(と/えば) vs から見ると when the source is a person. から見ると welcomes 〈人〉から見ると directly; から言う(と/えば) does not — you must say 〈人〉の立場／考えから言うと instead. When you see a bare person immediately before the blank, から見ると is very often the safe answer.
