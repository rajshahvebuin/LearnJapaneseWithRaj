2 Quiz / Exercise Section

問題1 — choose the best option for each blank (1問4点)

Q1

ご招待する（　）楽しいパーティーにするつもりです。Go-shoutai suru (___) tanoshii paatii ni suru tsumori desu.

Full sentence: ご招待するからには楽しいパーティーにするつもりです。Go-shoutai suru kara ni wa tanoshii paatii ni suru tsumori desu.
EN: Since I'm inviting people, I intend to make it an enjoyable party.
HI: चूँकि मैं आमंत्रित कर ही रहा हूँ, तो एक मज़ेदार पार्टी बनाने का इरादा है।
GU: આમંત્રણ આપી જ રહ્યો છું એટલે, એક આનંદદાયક પાર્ટી બનાવવાનો ઇરાદો છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1以来iraiever since (wrong sense — no continuing state here)
2からしてkarashitejudging just from this alone (wrong sense)
3だとかda to kaI hear that (wrong sense — not hearsay)
4からにはkara ni wasince ~ (naturally, one intends...)

Why: Week 6 Day 1's からには — having decided to invite people, the natural resolve is to make the party enjoyable.

Q2

あんな失礼な人は、二度と誘う（　）。Anna shitsurei na hito wa, nido to sasou (___).

Full sentence: あんな失礼な人は、二度と誘うまい。Anna shitsurei na hito wa, nido to sasou mai.
EN: I will never invite such a rude person again.
HI: ऐसे असभ्य व्यक्ति को मैं दोबारा कभी नहीं बुलाऊँगा।
GU: આવા અસભ્ય વ્યક્તિને હું ફરી ક્યારેય નહીં બોલાવું.
#JapaneseEnglish
1もんmon(wrong — casual "because," not a resolve marker)
2まいmaiwill never ~ again (negative volition)
3こそkoso(wrong — an emphasis particle, not a resolve)
4ことかkoto ka(wrong — exclamatory, not fitting here)

Why: Week 6 Day 4's negative-volition まい — a firm personal resolve never to invite this person again.

Q3

学校側（　）、家で起こっている問題の世話まではなかなかできない。Gakkou-gawa (___), ie de okotte iru mondai no sewa made wa nakanaka dekinai.

Full sentence: 学校側から言えば、家で起こっている問題の世話まではなかなかできない。Gakkou-gawa kara ieba, ie de okotte iru mondai no sewa made wa nakanaka dekinai.
EN: Speaking from the school's standpoint, it's hard to also take care of problems happening at home.
HI: स्कूल के नज़रिए से कहें तो, घर में हो रही समस्याओं का ध्यान रखना आसान नहीं है।
GU: શાળાના દૃષ્ટિકોણથી કહીએ તો, ઘરમાં થતી સમસ્યાઓની કાળજી રાખવી સહેલી નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からいえばkara iebaspeaking from the standpoint of
2からしてkarashite(wrong sense — not one representative detail)
3からといってkara to itte(wrong sense — no rejected conclusion here)
4からにはkara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve/obligation here)

Why: Week 6 Day 2's から言えば — an opinion voiced from the school's own standpoint.

Q4

本日2時より、駅前の広場（　）候補者の演説があります。Honjitsu ni-ji yori, ekimae no hiroba (___) kouhosha no enzetsu ga arimasu.

Full sentence: 本日2時より、駅前の広場において候補者の演説があります。Honjitsu ni-ji yori, ekimae no hiroba ni oite kouhosha no enzetsu ga arimasu.
EN: Today from 2 o'clock, there will be a candidate's speech at the square in front of the station.
HI: आज 2 बजे से, स्टेशन के सामने वाले चौक पर उम्मीदवार का भाषण होगा।
GU: આજે 2 વાગ્યાથી, સ્ટેશન સામેના ચોકમાં ઉમેદવારનું ભાષણ થશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にあたってni atatte(wrong — "on the occasion of," different nuance)
2に先立ちni sakidachi(wrong — "prior to," different nuance)
3においてni oiteat (formal location marker)
4にかけてni kakete(wrong — needs a から partner for a range reading)

Why: Week 6 Day 6's において — a formal announcement naming the location of the speech.

Q5

梅雨から夏（　）、日本は暑くて過ごしにくい。Tsuyu kara natsu (___), Nihon wa atsukute sugoshinikui.

Full sentence: 梅雨から夏にかけて、日本は暑くて過ごしにくい。Tsuyu kara natsu ni kakete, Nihon wa atsukute sugoshinikui.
EN: From the rainy season through summer, Japan is hot and hard to live comfortably in.
HI: बारिश के मौसम से लेकर गर्मियों तक, जापान में गर्मी की वजह से रहना मुश्किल हो जाता है।
GU: ચોમાસાથી લઈને ઉનાળા સુધી, જાપાનમાં ગરમીને લીધે રહેવું અઘરું બની જાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1以来irai(wrong — no continuing state described)
2をはじめにo hajime ni(wrong — not a representative example)
3にかけてni kaketeover the span from ~ to ~ (paired with から)
4をめぐってo megutte(wrong — no dispute here)

Why: Week 6 Day 3's から〜にかけて — the rainy season and summer together mark an approximate time range.

Q6

準備運動を（　）、泳いではいけません。Junbi undou o (___), oyoide wa ikemasen.

Full sentence: 準備運動をしてからでないと、泳いではいけません。Junbi undou o shite kara de nai to, oyoide wa ikemasen.
EN: Unless you do warm-up exercises first, you mustn't swim.
HI: पहले वॉर्म-अप किए बिना, तैरना नहीं चाहिए।
GU: પહેલાં વોર્મ-અપ કર્યા વગર, તરવું ન જોઈએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1したからにはshita kara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve implied)
2してからでないとshite kara de nai tounless (this happens) first
3した以上shita ijou(wrong sense — no obligation drawn)
4しないからといってshinai kara to itte(wrong sense — not rejecting a conclusion here)

Why: Week 6 Day 3's てからでないと — warming up is the strict precondition before swimming is allowed.

Q7

妹は理解が遅いが、一生懸命さ（　）だれにも負けない。Imouto wa rikai ga osoi ga, isshoukenmei-sa (___) dare ni mo makenai.

Full sentence: 妹は理解が遅いが、一生懸命さにかけてはだれにも負けない。Imouto wa rikai ga osoi ga, isshoukenmei-sa ni kakete wa dare ni mo makenai.
EN: My younger sister is slow to understand things, but when it comes to how hard she tries, she loses to no one.
HI: मेरी छोटी बहन समझने में धीमी है, लेकिन मेहनत करने के मामले में वह किसी से पीछे नहीं।
GU: મારી નાની બહેન સમજવામાં ધીમી છે, પણ મહેનત કરવાની બાબતમાં તે કોઈથી પાછળ નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からにはkara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve here)
2にしてはni shite wa(wrong sense — "for a ~" comparison, not fitting)
3からといってkara to itte(wrong sense — not rejecting a conclusion)
4にかけてはni kakete wawhen it comes to (a superiority claim)

Why: Week 6 Day 3's にかけては — isolating "how hard she tries" as the one area where she's unbeatable.

Q8

共同生活をする（　）、規則やマナーを守らなければならない。Kyoudou seikatsu o suru (___), kisoku ya manaa o mamoranakereba naranai.

Full sentence: 共同生活をする以上、規則やマナーを守らなければならない。Kyoudou seikatsu o suru ijou, kisoku ya manaa o mamoranakereba naranai.
EN: Now that you're living communally, you must follow the rules and manners.
HI: चूँकि साझा जीवन जी ही रहे हैं, तो नियमों और शिष्टाचार का पालन करना ही होगा।
GU: સહિયારું જીવન જીવી જ રહ્યા છો એટલે, નિયમો અને શિષ્ટાચારનું પાલન કરવું જ પડશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1以上ijounow that (naturally, one must...)
2からといってkara to itte(wrong sense — not rejecting a conclusion)
3にわたりni watari(wrong — "over a span of," not fitting)
4からみるとkara miru to(wrong sense — not a viewpoint statement)

Why: Week 6 Day 1's 以上 — communal living naturally obligates following shared rules.

Q9

このスープは色（　）辛そうだ。Kono suupu wa iro (___) karasou da.

Full sentence: このスープは色からして辛そうだ。Kono suupu wa iro karashite karasou da.
EN: Just from the color alone, this soup looks spicy.
HI: इस सूप का रंग देखकर ही लगता है कि यह तीखा होगा।
GU: આ સૂપનો રંગ જોઈને જ લાગે છે કે તે તીખો હશે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からしてkarashitejudging just from this alone
2をはじめとしてo hajime to shite(wrong sense — not a representative group member)
3からにはkara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve here)
4にかわってni kawatte(wrong — "in place of," unrelated meaning)

Why: Week 6 Day 2's からして — the color alone is cited as the one telling detail for the whole soup's spiciness.

Q10

よそから来た人（　）、ここの習慣は受け入れがたいものなのかもしれない。Yoso kara kita hito (___), koko no shuukan wa ukeiregatai mono na no kamo shirenai.

Full sentence: よそから来た人から見ると、ここの習慣は受け入れがたいものなのかもしれない。Yoso kara kita hito kara miru to, koko no shuukan wa ukeiregatai mono na no kamo shirenai.
EN: Seen from the viewpoint of someone from elsewhere, the customs here might be hard to accept.
HI: कहीं और से आए व्यक्ति की नज़र से देखा जाए तो, यहाँ के रीति-रिवाज़ स्वीकार करना मुश्किल लग सकता है।
GU: બીજેથી આવેલા વ્યક્તિની નજરે જોઈએ તો, અહીંના રિવાજો સ્વીકારવા અઘરા લાગી શકે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からにはkara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve here)
2からしてkarashite(wrong sense — not one representative detail)
3からみるとkara miru toseen from the viewpoint of (a person)
4からでないとkara de nai to(wrong sense — not a precondition here)

Why: Week 6 Day 2's から見ると — a person (someone from elsewhere) is explicitly allowed as the viewpoint.

Q11

この小説の主人公のモデルは、あの人（　）。Kono shousetsu no shujinkou no moderu wa, ano hito (___).

Full sentence: この小説の主人公のモデルは、あの人に相違ない。Kono shousetsu no shujinkou no moderu wa, ano hito ni souinai.
EN: The model for this novel's protagonist must be that person.
HI: इस उपन्यास के नायक का आधार वही व्यक्ति है, इसमें कोई शक नहीं।
GU: આ નવલકથાના નાયકનું મોડેલ તે વ્યક્તિ જ છે, તેમાં કોઈ શંકા નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1っぽいppoi(wrong register — too casual/vague for this formal claim)
2にすぎないni suginai(wrong sense — "is nothing more than," downplaying rather than asserting)
3かのようだka no you da(wrong sense — "as if," not a confident assertion)
4に相違ないni souinaimust be (formal certainty)

Why: Week 6 Day 5's に相違ない — a formal, confident assertion identifying who the model must be.

Q12

アジア（　）日本の役割を考えなければならない。Ajia (___) Nihon no yakuwari o kangaenakereba naranai.

Full sentence: アジアにおける日本の役割を考えなければならない。Ajia ni okeru Nihon no yakuwari o kangaenakereba naranai.
EN: We must think about Japan's role in Asia.
HI: हमें एशिया में जापान की भूमिका के बारे में सोचना चाहिए।
GU: આપણે એશિયામાં જાપાનની ભૂમિકા વિશે વિચારવું જોઈએ.
#JapaneseEnglish
1をめぐるo meguru(wrong sense — no dispute framed here)
2にかけてni kakete(wrong — needs a から partner for a range reading)
3におけるni okeruin ~ (formal, before a noun)
4に対するni taisuru(wrong sense — "toward," not "in/within")

Why: Week 6 Day 6's における — a formal "in Asia" modifying the following noun phrase (日本の役割).

Q13

本校では英語（　）とする7ヵ国語が学べます。Honkou de wa eigo (___) to suru nana-kakokugo ga manabemasu.

Full sentence: 本校では英語をはじめとする7ヵ国語が学べます。Honkou de wa eigo o hajime to suru nana-kakokugo ga manabemasu.
EN: At this school, you can learn seven languages, starting with English.
HI: इस स्कूल में, अंग्रेज़ी से शुरू करके, सात भाषाएँ सीखी जा सकती हैं।
GU: આ શાળામાં, અંગ્રેજીથી શરૂ કરીને, સાત ભાષાઓ શીખી શકાય છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1からしてkarashite(wrong sense — not one representative detail)
2をめぐりo meguri(wrong sense — no dispute here)
3をはじめo hajimestarting with (leading example of a group)
4においてni oite(wrong sense — not a location)

Why: Week 6 Day 6's をはじめとするN — English leads the list of seven languages offered.

Q14

昨夜の地震は震度3だった（　）。Sakuya no jishin wa shindo san datta (___).

Full sentence: 昨夜の地震は震度3だったとか。Sakuya no jishin wa shindo san datta toka.
EN: I hear last night's earthquake was a magnitude of 3.
HI: सुना है कि कल रात का भूकंप तीव्रता 3 का था।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે ગઈ રાતનો ધરતીકંપ તીવ્રતા 3નો હતો.
#JapaneseEnglish
1ことkoto(wrong — an incomplete nominalizer, not a sentence-ender here)
2とかto kaI hear that (uncertain hearsay)
3ことかkoto ka(wrong — exclamatory "what a ~!", not fitting)
4ものだmono da(wrong — reflective "that's how it is," not hearsay)

Why: Week 6 Day 4's とか — passing along secondhand information about the earthquake's magnitude without full certainty.

Q15

教えるということは、教わるということ（　）。Oshieru to iu koto wa, osowaru to iu koto (___).

Full sentence: 教えるということは、教わるということにほかならない。Oshieru to iu koto wa, osowaru to iu koto ni hokanaranai.
EN: Teaching is nothing other than being taught (in turn) — precisely that and nothing else.
HI: सिखाना असल में सीखने के अलावा और कुछ नहीं है।
GU: શીખવવું એ ખરેખર શીખવા સિવાય બીજું કંઈ નથી.
#JapaneseEnglish
1にほかならないni hokanaranaiis nothing other than
2よりほかないyori hokanai(wrong sense — that's about lacking alternatives, not identity)
3になっているni natte iru(wrong sense — a mere state, not an identity claim)
4だけのことはあるdake no koto wa aru(wrong — "no wonder," unrelated meaning)

Why: Week 6 Day 5's にほかならない — asserting that teaching truly IS being taught, ruling out any other characterization.

問題2 — sentence ordering: which tile goes at ★? (1問4点)

Q16

私が負けたのは事実だから、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 。Watashi ga maketa no wa jijitsu dakara, ___ ★ ___ .

Tiles: 1 ほか (hoka)　2 より (yori)　3 認める (mitomeru)　4 ない (nai)

3 認める→2 より→★ 1 ほか→4 ない

私が負けたのは事実だから、認めるよりほかない。Watashi ga maketa no wa jijitsu dakara, mitomeru yori hokanai.

EN: Since it's a fact that I lost, I have no choice but to accept it.
HI: मेरे हारने की बात सच है, इसलिए इसे स्वीकार करने के अलावा कोई चारा नहीं।
GU: મારા હાર્યાની વાત સાચી છે, એટલે તેને સ્વીકારવા સિવાય બીજો કોઈ ઉપાય નથી.

Why: Built on Week 6 Day 5's よりほかない — accepting the loss is the only option left once it's an established fact.

Q17

彼は書く練習もせずに、ただ漢字をじっと見ているだけだ。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ きまっている。Kare wa kaku renshuu mo sezu ni, tada kanji o jitto mite iru dake da. ___ ★ ___ kimatte iru.

Tiles: 1 に (ni)　2 おぼえられ (oboerare)　3 ない (nai)　4 そんなんじゃ (sonna n ja)

4 そんなんじゃ→2 おぼえられ→★ 3 ない→1 に

そんなんじゃおぼえられないにきまっている。Sonna n ja oboerarenai ni kimatte iru.

EN: He doesn't even practice writing, just stares at the kanji. If that's how it is, he's bound not to remember them.
HI: वह लिखने का अभ्यास तक नहीं करता, बस कांजी को घूरता रहता है। ऐसे में, वह उन्हें याद नहीं रख पाएगा, यह तय है।
GU: તે લખવાની પ્રેક્ટિસ પણ કરતો નથી, ફક્ત કાન્જીને તાકી રહે છે. આવી રીતે, તે તેને યાદ નહીં રાખી શકે, એ નક્કી છે.

Why: Built on Week 6 Day 5's に決まっている — negating the verb itself (おぼえられない) before attaching に決まっている predicts he's bound NOT to remember, given his lack of practice.

Q18

節約するのはよいことだ。しかし、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 目が悪くなってしまう。Setsuyaku suru no wa yoi koto da. Shikashi, ___ ★ ___ me ga waruku natte shimau.

Tiles: 1 からといって (kara to itte)　2 もったいない (mottainai)　3 勉強したら (benkyou shitara)　4 電気をつけずに (denki o tsukezu ni)

2 もったいない→1 からといって→★ 4 電気をつけずに→3 勉強したら

もったいないからといって電気をつけずに勉強したら目が悪くなってしまう。Mottainai kara to itte denki o tsukezu ni benkyou shitara me ga waruku natte shimau.

EN: Saving is a good thing. But just because it feels wasteful, if you study without turning on the lights, your eyesight will get worse.
HI: बचत करना अच्छी बात है। लेकिन सिर्फ़ बिजली बर्बाद होने के डर से, अगर बिना बत्ती जलाए पढ़ाई करें, तो आँखें ख़राब हो जाएँगी।

Q20

仲間が教室で自分の悪口を言っているのを＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ★ ＿＿＿ 学校に来なくなった。Nakama ga kyoushitsu de jibun no waruguchi o itte iru no o ___ ★ ___ gakkou ni konaku natta.

EN: Ever since he happened to overhear his classmates badmouthing him in the classroom, he stopped coming to school.
HI: कक्षा में सहपाठियों को अपनी बुराई करते सुनने के बाद से, वह स्कूल आना बंद कर चुका है।

Why: Built on Week 6 Day 1's て以来 — overhearing the classmates' badmouthing marks the starting point of an ongoing state (not coming to school) that continues to now.

Q21

…4月の上旬から中旬（　）、チューリップ祭りが開かれます。

#JapaneseEnglish
1にわたってni watatte(wrong — "spanning," slightly different nuance from a from-to range)
2にかけてni kaketefrom ~ to ~ (a range, ="~から~まで")
3にあたってni atatte(wrong — "on the occasion of," not a range)
4につれてni tsurete(wrong — "as ~ progresses," not a fixed range)

Why: Week 6 Day 3's にかけて — "early to mid-April" is a time range, essentially equivalent to から〜まで.

Q22

祭りがある（　）、チューリップを育てている人が多いわけではありません。

#JapaneseEnglish
1からするとkara suru to(wrong sense — not an inference from evidence)
2からにはkara ni wa(wrong sense — no resolve implied)
3からしてkarashite(wrong sense — not one representative detail)

Q23

Test-day strategy: This test deliberately mixes look-alikes from every day this week — から言う vs からする vs からして vs から見る (Day 2, whether a bare person is allowed), からといって vs てからでないと vs から〜にかけて vs にかけては (Day 3, "just because" vs "precondition" vs "range" vs "superiority"), とか vs the three flavors of まい (Day 4, hearsay vs resolve vs conjecture vs indecision), に決まっている／違いない／相違ない vs とは限らない vs ほかない vs にほかならない (Day 5, certainty vs its denial vs no-choice vs pure-identity), and をはじめ vs をめぐって vs において／における vs にて (Day 6, example vs dispute vs formal location, from broad to narrow register). When stuck, first ask "is this asserting certainty, denying a conclusion, marking a precondition, or naming a location/topic?" — narrowing to that category eliminates most wrong options on this test.
