Notes

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜からといって

neutral / casual からって
Reading~kara to itte
Meaning (EN)"just because ~ (doesn't mean...)" — rejects a conclusion that would seem to logically follow from a stated reason
Meaning (HI)सिर्फ़ इसलिए कि ...है, इसका मतलब यह नहीं कि...
Meaning (GU)ફક્ત એટલા માટે કે ...છે, એનો અર્થ એ નથી કે...
Connection (接続)V/A/な-adj/N-普通形 + からといって　|　casual: からって
RegisterNeutral in writing; からって is the spoken, conversational contraction (flagged with a speech-bubble icon in the source)
Typical useAlmost always followed by a negative statement (〜わけではない／〜とは限らない／〜ない) that blocks the expected conclusion.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb / い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun (plain form)普通形 + からといって好きだからといってsuki da kara to itte
Casual/spoken普通形 + からって 💬(added, not shown this day; standard contraction) 好きだからってsuki da karatte
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
好きだからといって、同じ食品ばかり食べるのはよくない。Suki da kara to itte, onaji shokuhin bakari taberu no wa yokunai. Just because you like it doesn't mean it's good to eat only that same food all the time. सिर्फ़ पसंद होने से यह मतलब नहीं कि हमेशा वही एक खाना खाना अच्छा है। ફક્ત ગમે છે એટલા માટે હંમેશા એ જ ખોરાક ખાવો સારો નથી.
日本に住んでいるからといって、日本語がしゃべれるようにはならない。Nihon ni sunde iru kara to itte, nihongo ga shabereru you ni wa naranai. Just because you live in Japan doesn't mean you'll become able to speak Japanese. सिर्फ़ जापान में रहने से यह मतलब नहीं कि आप जापानी बोलना सीख ही जाएँगे। ફક્ત જાપાનમાં રહેવાથી જાપાનીઝ બોલતાં આવડી જ જશે એવું નથી.

からといって is the classic "correlation isn't causation" pattern — a stated reason is acknowledged, but the expected/obvious conclusion is explicitly denied. Almost always look for a negative in the second clause.

【2】 〜てからでないと／てからでなければ

neutral register
Reading~te kara de nai to / te kara de nakereba
Meaning (EN)"unless one does ~ first, (one can't...)" — states a strict precondition that must be satisfied first
Meaning (HI)पहले ...किए बिना, ...नहीं हो सकता
Meaning (GU)પહેલાં ...કર્યા વગર, ...થઈ શકે નહીં
Connection (接続)V-て + からでないと〜できない　|　V-て + からでなければ〜できない
RegisterNeutral
Typical useThe main clause is almost always a negative ability/permission statement — you cannot do Y until X happens first.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + からでないと手続きしてからでないとtetsuzuki shite kara de nai to
Verb (て-form, alternate)V-て + からでなければもらってからでなければmoratte kara de nakereba
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
手続きしてからでないと、図書館の本は借りられない。Tetsuzuki shite kara de nai to, toshokan no hon wa karirarenai. Unless you complete the procedure first, you can't borrow library books. पहले प्रक्रिया पूरी किए बिना, लाइब्रेरी की किताबें उधार नहीं मिल सकतीं। પહેલાં પ્રક્રિયા પૂરી કર્યા વગર, પુસ્તકાલયના પુસ્તકો ઉછીના મળી શકે નહીં.
親の許可をもらってからでなければ、申し込めない。Oya no kyoka o moratte kara de nakereba, moushikomenai. Unless you get your parents' permission first, you can't apply. माता-पिता की अनुमति लिए बिना, आवेदन नहीं किया जा सकता। માતાપિતાની પરવાનગી લીધા વગર, અરજી કરી શકાય નહીં.

Both からでないと and からでなければ mean the same thing; treat them as free variants. This is a stricter, more formal cousin of 〜なければ〜ない — it specifically frames the earlier action as an unskippable prerequisite.

【3】 〜から〜にかけて

neutral register
Reading~kara ~ni kakete
Meaning (EN)"from ~ to ~ / over the range of ~ to ~" — marks an approximate span of time or place, without claiming precise start/end boundaries
Meaning (HI)...से ...तक (लगभग, पूरे दौरान)
Meaning (GU)...થી ...સુધી (લગભગ, આખા સમય દરમિયાન)
Connection (接続)N₁から N₂にかけて　(N₁・N₂＝時／場所 — a time or a place)
RegisterNeutral
Typical useSimilar to 〜から〜まで, but looser/less precise — it describes a general span rather than exact boundaries.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (time)N₁から N₂にかけて2007年から2009年にかけて2007-nen kara 2009-nen ni kakete
Noun (place)N₁から N₂にかけてこの駅からあそこの通りにかけてkono eki kara asoko no toori ni kakete
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は、2007年から2009年にかけて、ロンドンに住んでいました。Watashi wa, 2007-nen kara 2009-nen ni kakete, Rondon ni sunde imashita. I lived in London over the span of 2007 to 2009. मैं 2007 से 2009 तक लंदन में रहा। હું 2007 થી 2009 સુધી લંડનમાં રહેતો હતો.
この駅から、あそこの通りにかけて、再開発されるそうです。Kono eki kara, asoko no toori ni kakete, saikaihatsu sareru sou desu. I hear that the area from this station to that street over there is going to be redeveloped. सुना है कि इस स्टेशन से उस सड़क तक का इलाक़ा फिर से विकसित किया जाएगा। સાંભળ્યું છે કે આ સ્ટેશનથી પેલી શેરી સુધીનો વિસ્તાર ફરીથી વિકસાવવામાં આવશે.

Both N₁ and N₂ must be the same type — either both times or both places — never mixed. This point looks identical in spelling to Point 4's にかけては, so pay close attention to whether there's a から before it (range) or not (superiority claim).

【4】 〜にかけては／にかけても

neutral register
Reading~ni kakete wa / ni kakete mo
Meaning (EN)"when it comes to ~" — singles out one area/skill in which someone is superior/unrivaled
Meaning (HI)...के मामले में तो (कोई मुक़ाबला नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...ની બાબતમાં તો (કોઈ મુકાબલો નથી)
Connection (接続)Nに／Nの A-さに／Nのな-さに + かけては／かけても
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAlmost always paired with a superlative claim ("the best in town," "no one can beat him") — it isolates the one specific criterion the claim is about.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNに + かけては(standard rule) 音楽にかけてはongaku ni kakete wa
Noun + い-adjective-さNの A-さに + かけては足の速さにかけてはashi no hayasa ni kakete wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
足の速さにかけては、彼は町で一番だ。Ashi no hayasa ni kakete wa, kare wa machi de ichiban da. When it comes to running speed, he's the best in town. दौड़ने की रफ़्तार के मामले में तो, वह पूरे शहर में सबसे आगे है। દોડવાની ઝડપની બાબતમાં તો, તે આખા શહેરમાં સૌથી આગળ છે.
歌のうまさにかけては、彼に勝てる人はいない。Uta no umasa ni kakete wa, kare ni kateru hito wa inai. When it comes to singing skill, no one can beat him. गाने की क़ाबिलियत के मामले में तो, उसे कोई हरा नहीं सकता। ગાવાની આવડતની બાબતમાં તો, તેને કોઈ હરાવી શકતું નથી.

Practice item ④ on this page's own quiz shows that a bare にかけて (without は) can also appear before this kind of superlative claim in natural speech — にかけては is the more textbook-standard, emphatic form, but にかけて alone is not marked wrong.

Q1

この花は、春から秋（　）咲き続ける。Kono hana wa, haru kara aki (___) sakitsuzukeru.

Full sentence: この花は、春から秋にかけて咲き続ける。Kono hana wa, haru kara aki ni kakete sakitsuzukeru.
EN: This flower keeps blooming from spring through autumn.
HI: यह फूल वसंत से लेकर शरद ऋतु तक खिलता रहता है।
GU: આ ફૂલ વસંતથી પાનખર સુધી ખીલતું રહે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aにかけてni kaketeover the span from spring to autumnसे लेकर तकથી સુધી
bからといってkara to itte(wrong sense — not a range of time)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 3's N₁から N₂にかけて — spring and autumn are both time nouns marking a span.

Q2

手を洗ってから（　）、食べてはいけません。Te o aratte kara (___), tabete wa ikemasen.

Full sentence: 手を洗ってからでないと、食べてはいけません。Te o aratte kara de nai to, tabete wa ikemasen.
EN: Unless you wash your hands first, you mustn't eat.
HI: हाथ धोए बिना, खाना नहीं चाहिए।
GU: હાથ ધોયા વગર, ખાવું ન જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aでないとde nai tounless (this happens first)बिनाવગર
bといってto itte(incomplete — needs から before it, and からといって has a different meaning entirely)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 2's てからでないと — washing hands is the strict precondition before eating is allowed.

Q3

大変だから（　）、あきらめないでください。Taihen da kara (___), akiramenaide kudasai.

Full sentence: 大変だからといって、あきらめないでください。Taihen da kara to itte, akiramenaide kudasai.
EN: Just because it's difficult, please don't give up.
HI: सिर्फ़ मुश्किल होने की वजह से, कृपया हार मत मानिए।
GU: ફક્ત મુશ્કેલ છે એટલા માટે, કૃપા કરીને હાર ન માનશો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aといってto ittecompletes 〜からといって, "just because"की वजह से (लेकिन)એટલા માટે (પણ)
bでないとde nai to(wrong sense — not a precondition here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Point 1's からといって — the difficulty (大変だ) is acknowledged as a reason, but the expected conclusion (giving up) is explicitly rejected.

Q4

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q6

いくら体にいい ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ よくない。Ikura karada ni ii ___ yokunai.

Tiles: 1 同じもの (onaji mono)　2 からといって (kara to itte)　3 食べるのは (taberu no wa)　4 ばかり (bakari)

2 からといって→1 同じもの→4 ばかり→3 食べるのは

いくら体にいいからといって同じものばかり食べるのはよくない。Ikura karada ni ii kara to itte onaji mono bakari taberu no wa yokunai.

EN: No matter how good it is for your body, it's not good to eat only the same thing just because of that.
HI: चाहे शरीर के लिए कितना ही अच्छा क्यों न हो, सिर्फ़ इसी वजह से हमेशा एक ही चीज़ खाना ठीक नहीं।
GU: શરીર માટે ગમે તેટલું સારું હોય, ફક્ત એટલા માટે હંમેશા એ જ વસ્તુ ખાવી સારી નથી.

Why: Built on Point 1's からといって — being good for the body is acknowledged, but the conclusion "so eat only that" is explicitly rejected.

Q7

事実関係をしっかり認識 ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ コメントできません。Jijitsu kankei o shikkari ninshiki ___ komento dekimasen.

Tiles: 1 その件 (sono ken)　2 してから (shite kara)　3 については (ni tsuite wa)　4 でないと (de nai to)

2 してから→4 でないと→1 その件→3 については

事実関係をしっかり認識してからでないと、その件についてはコメントできません。Jijitsu kankei o shikkari ninshiki shite kara de nai to, sono ken ni tsuite wa komento dekimasen.

EN: Unless I first properly grasp the facts of the matter, I can't comment on that issue.
HI: जब तक मामले की सच्चाई पूरी तरह न समझ लूँ, तब तक उस विषय पर टिप्पणी नहीं कर सकता।
GU: જ્યાં સુધી બાબતની હકીકત બરાબર સમજી ન લઉં, ત્યાં સુધી તે મુદ્દા પર ટિપ્પણી કરી શકતો નથી.

Why: Built on Point 2's てからでないと — understanding the facts first is the strict precondition before any comment is possible.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of today's four points share the root から or にかけて, which is exactly why they're grouped on the same page. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜からといって~kara to itte Just because ~ (doesn't mean...) 普通形＋からといって Rejects an expected conclusion from a stated reason; needs a negative second clause. You want to block a natural-sounding but false inference.
〜てからでないと~te kara de nai to Unless ~ first, (can't...) V-てからでないと／でなければ States a strict precondition — the earlier action is an unskippable requirement. You want to say one thing absolutely must happen before another can.
〜から〜にかけて~kara ~ni kakete From ~ to ~ (a span) N₁からN₂にかけて Marks an approximate time/place range, less precise than 〜から〜まで. You're describing a general span, not exact start/end points.
〜にかけては~ni kakete wa When it comes to ~ Nに／Nの…さにかけては No から before it — isolates one skill/area for a superiority claim, not a range. You want to say "in this one respect, nobody beats them."
Exam trap: にかけて appears in two completely different constructions this day: with a から before it (N₁からN₂にかけて, a time/place range) versus alone after a skill/quality noun (Nにかけては, "when it comes to X, nobody's better"). Check for a から partner first — if there's one, it's a range; if not, it's the superiority pattern.
