4日目 — 行こうか行くまいか (Ikou ka ikumaika)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 — printed p.100–101 (PDF pages 100–101, no offset)
Grammar points covered
V/A/na/N-とかV-まい (negative volition)V-まい (negative conjecture)Vようか Vるまいか
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.103 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
とか is tagged with a speech-bubble icon (conversational, hearsay register); all three まい points are tagged 硬 formal/written in the source. Formation for まい is irregular and worth memorizing carefully: godan (u-)verbs take only the dictionary form (行くまい); ichidan (ru-)verbs and する/来る accept two forms (食べるまい／食べまい, するまい／すまい, 来るまい／来まい). A bonus phrase 夢ではあるまいか ("could this possibly be a dream?") appears as an extra idiomatic note under Point 3.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜とか

💬 conversational / hearsay
Reading~toka
Meaning (EN)"I hear that ~ / apparently ~" — reports secondhand information without full certainty
Meaning (HI)सुना है कि ...
Meaning (GU)સાંભળ્યું છે કે ...
Connection (接続)V/A/な-adj/N-普通形 + とか
RegisterConversational, spoken — flagged with a speech-bubble icon in the source
Typical use「〜そうだ」より「はっきりわからないが」という意味を含む — weaker/vaguer than 〜そうだ; it implies "I'm not entirely sure, but I heard...".
Word typeFormationExample
Noun / na-adjective (plain)普通形 + とか中止だとかchuushi da toka
Verb (plain, progressive)普通形 + とかはやっているとかhayatte iru toka
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今夜の花火大会は、雨で中止だとか。Kon'ya no hanabi taikai wa, ame de chuushi da toka. I hear tonight's fireworks display has been cancelled because of the rain. सुना है कि आज रात का आतिशबाज़ी उत्सव बारिश की वजह से रद्द हो गया है। સાંભળ્યું છે કે આજ રાત્રે ફટાકડાનો કાર્યક્રમ વરસાદને લીધે રદ થયો છે.
今、インフルエンザがはやっているとか。Ima, infuruenza ga hayatte iru toka. I hear the flu is going around right now. सुना है कि आजकल फ़्लू फैल रहा है। સાંભળ્યું છે કે હાલમાં ફ્લૂ ફેલાઈ રહ્યો છે.

とか as hearsay must stand alone as a sentence-final particle — it cannot be tacked onto ことです／のことです (see Q1 in the quiz below); the correct reported-speech ending in that case is とのことです, dropping とか entirely.

【2】 〜まい（意志：もうしない）

硬 formal/written
Reading~mai (negative volition)
Meaning (EN)"I will never ~ again / I've resolved not to ~" — a strong, formal negative intention
Meaning (HI)मैं फिर कभी ...नहीं करूँगा (यह पक्का इरादा है)
Meaning (GU)હું ફરી ક્યારેય ...નહીં કરું (એવો દૃઢ ઇરાદો)
Connection (接続)(ru-verb) V-る＋まい／V-ます＋まい　|　(u-verb) V-る＋まい　|　する→するまい／すまい　|　来る→来るまい／来まい
Register硬 — formal, often written or deliberate/emphatic speech
Typical useOften reinforced with 決して〜まい or 何も〜まい ("never, absolutely not").
Word typeFormationExample
u-verb (godan)V-る（dictionary form）＋まい行くまいiku mai
u-verb (godan)V-る（dictionary form）＋まい飲むまいnomu mai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あんなひどいところ、二度と行くまい。Anna hidoi tokoro, nido to iku mai. I will never go to such an awful place again. ऐसी बुरी जगह पर मैं दोबारा कभी नहीं जाऊँगा। આવી ખરાબ જગ્યાએ હું ફરી ક્યારેય નહીં જાઉં.
海外旅行では、絶対に買った水以外は飲むまいと思った。Kaigai ryokou de wa, zettai ni katta mizu igai wa nomu mai to omotta. On trips abroad, I decided I would absolutely never drink anything other than bottled water I'd bought myself. विदेश यात्रा में, मैंने तय किया कि ख़रीदे हुए पानी के अलावा कुछ भी नहीं पिऊँगा। વિદેશ પ્રવાસમાં, મેં નક્કી કર્યું કે ખરીદેલા પાણી સિવાય બીજું કંઈ નહીં પીઉં.

This is まい's "negative volition" flavor — a firm personal resolve never to repeat an action. Compare with Point 3 below, where まい instead expresses a guess/conjecture about someone else, not the speaker's own resolve.

【3】 〜まい（推量：〜ないだろう）

硬 formal/written
Reading~mai (negative conjecture)
Meaning (EN)"probably won't ~ / surely isn't ~" — the speaker's negative guess about someone/something else
Meaning (HI)शायद ...नहीं होगा / नहीं करेगा
Meaning (GU)કદાચ ...નહીં હોય / નહીં કરે
Connection (接続)同上（ru-V: V-る／V-ます＋まい；u-V: V-る＋まい）
Register硬 — formal, often rhetorical
Typical useA negative counterpart of だろう／と思う — "I don't think X will happen," said about a third party or a general possibility, not the speaker's own resolve.
Word typeFormationExample
ru-verb (ichidan)V-る＋まい／V-ます＋まいわかるまいwakaru mai
ru-verb, potential formV-できる＋まい／V-でき＋まいできまいdeki mai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
彼には私の気持ちはわかるまい。Kare ni wa watashi no kimochi wa wakaru mai. He probably won't understand my feelings. शायद उसे मेरी भावनाएँ समझ में नहीं आएँगी। કદાચ તેને મારી લાગણીઓ સમજાશે નહીં.
上級者でも、この問題はできまい。Joukyuu-sha demo, kono mondai wa deki mai. Even advanced learners probably can't solve this problem. उन्नत स्तर के लोग भी शायद यह सवाल हल नहीं कर पाएँगे। અદ્યતન સ્તરના લોકો પણ કદાચ આ સવાલ ઉકેલી નહીં શકે.

Bonus phrase (もっと！): 夢ではあるまいか (Yume de wa aru mai ka) — "Could this possibly not be a dream? / Am I dreaming?" — a fixed rhetorical pattern (Nではあるまいか) expressing astonished disbelief, built on this same negative-conjecture まい.

【4】 〜ようか〜まいか

硬 formal/written
Reading~you ka ~mai ka
Meaning (EN)"whether to ~ or not" — expresses hesitation/indecision between doing something and not doing it
Meaning (HI)करूँ या न करूँ (यह तय नहीं कर पा रहा)
Meaning (GU)કરું કે ન કરું (નક્કી કરી શકતો નથી)
Connection (接続)V-ようか + V-るまいか　|　する→しようか するまいか／すまいか
Register硬 — formal/written, though the underlying "whether or not" idea is universal
Typical useBoth halves refer to the same verb (do it, or don't do it) and the whole phrase is usually followed by 迷う／考える ("hesitate/think about").
Word typeFormationExample
u-verb / ru-verbV-ようか + V-るまいか話そうか話すまいかhanasou ka hanasu maika
する (irregular)しようか + するまいか／すまいか進学しようか進学すまいかshingaku shiyou ka shingaku su maika
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
本当のことを話そうか話すまいか迷ったが、結局全部話した。Hontou no koto o hanasou ka hanasu maika mayotta ga, kekkyoku zenbu hanashita. I hesitated over whether to tell the truth or not, but in the end I told everything. सच बताऊँ या न बताऊँ, यह तय नहीं कर पा रहा था, पर आख़िरकार सब कुछ बता दिया। સાચું કહું કે ન કહું, નક્કી કરી શકતો ન હતો, પણ છેવટે બધું કહી દીધું.
食べたいけど、太りたくないし、食べようか食べまいか考えているところです。Tabetai kedo, futoritakunai shi, tabeyou ka tabe maika kangaete iru tokoro desu. I want to eat it, but I don't want to gain weight either, so I'm right now thinking about whether to eat it or not. खाना चाहता हूँ, पर मोटा भी नहीं होना चाहता, इसलिए खाऊँ या न खाऊँ, यही सोच रहा हूँ। ખાવા માંગુ છું, પણ જાડું પણ થવા માંગતો નથી, એટલે ખાઉં કે ન ખાઉં, એ જ વિચારી રહ્યો છું.

Note that まいか here uses the same irregular formation rules as Points 2–3: for する, the abbreviated form すまいか is just as common as the full するまいか — see Q7 in the quiz below.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 true/false items) and 練習Ⅱ (2 four-option fill-in-the-blank items), both from p.101. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.103.

練習Ⅰ — mark ○ (correct) or × (incorrect)

Q1

「社長、田中様からお電話で、少し遅れるとかのことです。」"Shachou, Tanaka-sama kara o-denwa de, sukoshi okureru toka no koto desu."

Judgment: × incorrect — とかのことです → とのことです
Corrected: 「社長、田中様からお電話で、少し遅れるとのことです。」"...sukoshi okureru to no koto desu."
EN: "Sir, there was a call from Mr. Tanaka — he says he'll be a little late."
HI: "सर, तनाका जी का फ़ोन आया था, कह रहे थे कि थोड़ी देर हो जाएगी।"
GU: "સાહેબ, તનાકાજીનો ફોન આવ્યો હતો, કહેતા હતા કે થોડું મોડું થશે."

Why: とか cannot combine with のことです — reported speech built on のことです needs plain と (とのことです), not とか. とか must stand alone as a sentence-final hearsay particle.

Q2

子どもには自分と同じ苦労はさせまいと彼女は思った。Kodomo ni wa jibun to onaji kurou wa sasemai to kanojo wa omotta.

Judgment: ○ correct
EN: She resolved that she would not make her child go through the same hardships she herself had.
HI: उसने ठान लिया कि वह अपने बच्चे को अपने जैसी मुश्किलों से नहीं गुज़ारेगी।
GU: તેણે નક્કી કર્યું કે તે પોતાના બાળકને પોતાના જેવી મુશ્કેલીઓમાંથી નહીં પસાર થવા દે.

Why: させまい is the causative-form (させる, ichidan) plus まい, correctly formed as V-る＋まい — a firm resolve not to let something happen. Nothing to fix here.

Q3

「もう梅雨入りしたとか。」「うん、そうだってね。」"Mou tsuyu-iri shita toka." "Un, sou datte ne."

Judgment: ○ correct
EN: "I hear the rainy season has already started." "Yeah, that's what I heard too."
HI: "सुना है बारिश का मौसम शुरू हो चुका है।" "हाँ, ऐसा ही सुना है।"
GU: "સાંભળ્યું છે કે ચોમાસું શરૂ થઈ ગયું છે." "હા, એવું જ સાંભળ્યું છે."

Why: とか stands alone here as a hearsay sentence-ender, exactly as it should — nothing to fix.

Q4

「お昼ごはん、食べに行くまいか。」「うん、行こう。」"Ohirugohan, tabe ni iku maika." "Un, ikou."

Judgment: × incorrect — 行くまいか → 行こうか
Corrected: 「お昼ごはん、食べに行こうか。」「うん、行こう。」"Ohirugohan, tabe ni ikou ka." "Un, ikou."
EN: "Shall we go for lunch?" "Yeah, let's go."
HI: "दोपहर का खाना खाने चलें क्या?" "हाँ, चलते हैं।"
GU: "બપોરનું જમવા જઈએ?" "હા, જઈએ."

Why: A simple invitation ("shall we go?") uses the plain volitional 行こうか alone — まいか only belongs in the paired ようか〜まいか "whether to or not" construction, not as a standalone invitation.

Q5

パーティーに行こうか行きまいか考えています。Paatii ni ikou ka ikimai ka kangaete imasu.

Judgment: × incorrect — 行こうか行きまいか → 行こうか行くまいか
Corrected: パーティーに行こうか行くまいか考えています。Paatii ni ikou ka iku maika kangaete imasu.
EN: I'm thinking about whether to go to the party or not.
HI: मैं सोच रहा हूँ कि पार्टी में जाऊँ या न जाऊँ।
GU: હું વિચારી રહ્યો છું કે પાર્ટીમાં જાઉં કે ન જાઉં.

Why: 行く is a u-verb (godan), so まい must attach to its dictionary form (行くまい) — the masu-stem 行き＋まい pattern is only valid for ichidan verbs and する／来る, not for u-verbs.

Q6

あの先生のようにすばらしい先生には二度と（　）まい。Ano sensei no you ni subarashii sensei ni wa nido to (___) mai.

Full sentence: あの先生のようにすばらしい先生には二度と出会えまい。Ano sensei no you ni subarashii sensei ni wa nido to deaemai.
EN: I probably will never again meet such a wonderful teacher as that one.
HI: उस जैसे शानदार शिक्षक से मुझे शायद दोबारा कभी मुलाक़ात नहीं होगी।
GU: તેમના જેવા શ્રેષ્ઠ શિક્ષક ફરી ક્યારેય મળવાના નથી, એવું લાગે છે.
#JapaneseEnglish
1出会わdeawa(nai-stem of 出会う — wrong; まい doesn't attach here)
2出会いdeai(masu-stem of 出会う — wrong for a plain u-verb)
3出会えdeaethe masu-stem of the potential form 出会える (can meet)
4出会おdeao(volitional stem — wrong; まい doesn't attach here)

Why: The intended verb is not plain 出会う but its potential form 出会える ("to be able to meet") — since potential forms are always ichidan (ru-verbs), まい attaches either as 出会えるまい (full) or, as tested here, the abbreviated ichidan pattern stem＋まい: 出会え＋まい = 出会えまい. This is the same abbreviation pattern as 食べまい／すまい／来まい from Points 2–3.

Q7

進学（　）ようか（　）まいか迷っている。Shingaku (___) you ka (___) maika mayotte iru.

Full sentence: 進学しようか進学すまいか迷っている。Shingaku shiyou ka shingaku sumaika mayotte iru.
EN: I'm undecided about whether to go on to further education or not.
HI: मैं तय नहीं कर पा रहा कि आगे पढ़ाई जारी रखूँ या नहीं।
GU: હું નક્કી કરી શકતો નથી કે આગળ અભ્યાસ ચાલુ રાખું કે નહીં.
#JapaneseEnglish
2し／すshi / suし for the volitional (しよう), す for the abbreviated negative (すまいか)
1す／しsu / shi(reversed — wrong order)
3せ／すse / su(せ is not a valid volitional stem for する)
4す／するsu / suru(する before まいか would need するまいか, not まいか alone after する)

Why: する's volitional is しよう (stem し), and its abbreviated negative-volition/conjecture form is すまい (stem す) — the two blanks need different stems of the same irregular verb, し for the volitional half and す for the まい half.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Three of today's four points are all built on まい, which shifts meaning depending on context — exactly what today's quiz is designed to test. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜とか~toka I hear that ~ (uncertain hearsay) 普通形＋とか Conversational; weaker than 〜そうだ; cannot combine with のことです. Casually passing along something you heard, without full confidence.
〜まい (negative volition)~mai I will never ~ again (my own resolve) u-V: V-る＋まい / ru-V: V-る・V-ます＋まい About the speaker's own firm decision, often with 二度と／決して. Declaring your own resolve never to repeat an action.
〜まい (negative conjecture)~mai Probably won't ~ (a guess about someone/something else) Same forms as above A negative だろう/と思う about a third party, not the speaker's own will. Guessing that someone else won't do/isn't likely to do something.
〜ようか〜まいか~you ka ~mai ka Whether to ~ or not V-ようか＋V-るまいか Pairs both halves of the same verb to show active indecision, not a settled resolve or guess. Describing genuine hesitation over doing something or not.
Exam trap: まい's meaning (resolve vs. conjecture) is disambiguated entirely by context — there's no separate grammatical marker — so always check whose action is being discussed: the speaker's own firm decision (volition) or a guess about someone/something else (conjecture). Separately, watch verb type carefully for formation: u-verbs (godan) take only the dictionary form + まい; potential forms and ichidan verbs can take the abbreviated masu-stem + まい (出会えまい, 食べまい, すまい, 来まい) — mixing these up is exactly what Q6–Q7 test.
