Answer key

Notes
において and にて are both tagged 硬 formal/written in the source; をはじめ and をめぐって carry no register badge (neutral). にて is specifically called out as common in written notices and correspondence (お知らせや通信文など) — including as a postcard sign-off (地名にて　名前より).

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜をはじめ／をはじめとして／をはじめとするN

neutral register
Reading~o hajime / o hajime to shite / o hajime to suru N
Meaning (EN)"starting with ~ (and other similar ~s)" — cites a leading/representative example, then generalizes to a wider group
Meaning (HI)...से शुरू करके (और भी कई ऐसे)
Meaning (GU)...થી શરૂ કરીને (અને બીજા આવા ઘણા)
Connection (接続)Nを + はじめ／はじめとして／はじめとするN
RegisterNeutral
Typical useNames one prominent/representative member of a group as the lead example, implying "and many others of the same kind."
Word typeFormationExample
NounNを + はじめ中国をはじめChuugoku o hajime
Noun (before a clause)Nを + はじめとして野球をはじめとしてyakyuu o hajime to shite
Noun (before another noun)Nを + はじめとするN英語をはじめとする7ヵ国語eigo o hajime to suru nana-kakokugo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
会議には中国をはじめ、アジアの国々が参加した。Kaigi ni wa Chuugoku o hajime, Ajia no kuniguni ga sanka shita. Countries from across Asia, starting with China, took part in the conference. सम्मेलन में चीन समेत एशिया के कई देशों ने भाग लिया। પરિષદમાં ચીન સહિત એશિયાના ઘણા દેશોએ ભાગ લીધો.
わが国では、野球をはじめとして、サッカーやテニスなど、様々なスポーツがさかんである。Waga kuni de wa, yakyuu o hajime to shite, sakkaa ya tenisu nado, samazama na supootsu ga sakan de aru. In our country, various sports are popular, starting with baseball, along with soccer, tennis, and others. हमारे देश में बेसबॉल से शुरू करके, फ़ुटबॉल और टेनिस जैसे कई खेल लोकप्रिय हैं। અમારા દેશમાં બેઝબોલથી શરૂ કરીને, ફૂટબોલ અને ટેનિસ જેવી અનેક રમતો લોકપ્રિય છે.

Think of をはじめ as "leading with the most obvious/famous example, and implying more of the same kind follow." はじめとして bridges into a following clause, while はじめとするN bridges directly into a following noun phrase.

【2】 〜をめぐって／をめぐるN

neutral register
Reading~o megutte / o meguru N
Meaning (EN)"concerning ~ / over the issue of ~" — marks the topic around which a dispute, discussion, or conflict revolves
Meaning (HI)...को लेकर (बहस/विवाद)
Meaning (GU)...ને લઈને (વિવાદ/ચર્ચા)
Connection (接続)Nを + めぐって／めぐるN
RegisterNeutral
Typical useAlmost always paired with words like 論争 (dispute), 対立 (conflict), 話し合う (discuss) — the topic is something people are actively debating or fighting over.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (before a clause)Nを + めぐって憲法改正をめぐってkenpou kaisei o megutte
Noun (before another noun)Nを + めぐるN教育制度をめぐる諸問題kyouiku seido o meguru sho-mondai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
憲法改正をめぐって、長い間、論争が続いている。Kenpou kaisei o megutte, nagai aida, ronsou ga tsuzuite iru. Over the issue of constitutional amendment, a dispute has continued for a long time. संविधान संशोधन को लेकर, लंबे समय से बहस चल रही है। બંધારણ સુધારાને લઈને, લાંબા સમયથી ચર્ચા ચાલી રહી છે.
教育制度をめぐる諸問題について、話し合う。Kyouiku seido o meguru sho-mondai ni tsuite, hanashiau. We discuss the various problems surrounding the education system. शिक्षा प्रणाली से जुड़ी विभिन्न समस्याओं पर चर्चा करते हैं। શિક્ષણ પ્રણાલી સાથે સંકળાયેલ વિવિધ સમસ્યાઓ પર ચર્ચા કરીએ છીએ.

をめぐって literally means "circling around" a topic — unlike をはじめ (a representative example), it always implies active tension, debate, or competing interests focused on that one topic.

【3】 〜において／における

硬 formal/written
Reading~ni oite / ni okeru
Meaning (EN)"in/at ~" — a formal way to mark a place, time, field, or situation
Meaning (HI)...में (औपचारिक)
Meaning (GU)...માં (ઔપચારિક)
Connection (接続)Nに + おいて／おいては／おいても／おけるN
Register硬 — formal, common in speeches, reports, official writing
Typical useNが場所・状況のときは「〜で」の意味 — when N is a place or situation, において means the same as で, just more formal.
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (place, before a clause)Nに + おいて京都においてKyouto ni oite
Noun (before another noun)Nに + おけるN国際社会におけるわが国の役割kokusai shakai ni okeru waga kuni no yakuwari
Noun (topic-marking)Nに + おいてその点においてsono ten ni oite
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
京都において、シンポジウムが行われた。Kyouto ni oite, shinpojiumu ga okonawareta. A symposium was held in Kyoto. क्योटो में एक संगोष्ठी आयोजित की गई। ક્યોટોમાં એક પરિસંવાદ યોજાયો.
国際社会におけるわが国の役割を考える。Kokusai shakai ni okeru waga kuni no yakuwari o kangaeru. We consider our country's role in the international community. अंतरराष्ट्रीय समुदाय में हमारे देश की भूमिका पर विचार करते हैं। આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય સમુદાયમાં આપણા દેશની ભૂમિકા વિશે વિચારીએ છીએ.
Aさんの主張には、その点において疑問があります。A-san no shuchou ni wa, sono ten ni oite gimon ga arimasu. I have a question about Mr./Ms. A's argument, on that particular point. ए के तर्क में, उस बिंदु पर मुझे एक सवाल है। એના તર્કમાં, તે મુદ્દા પર મને એક પ્રશ્ન છે.

において/における is essentially a formal-register upgrade of で/の — reach for it in reports, speeches, and academic writing; in casual conversation, plain で sounds far more natural.

【4】 〜にて

硬 formal/written
Reading~nite
Meaning (EN)"at/by/in ~" — an old-fashioned, very formal equivalent of で, common in written notices and correspondence
Meaning (HI)...में/द्वारा (अति औपचारिक, सूचनाओं/पत्रों में)
Meaning (GU)...માં/દ્વારા (અતિ ઔપચારિક, સૂચનાઓ/પત્રોમાં)
Connection (接続)Nにて
Register硬 — very formal, characteristic of written announcements and correspondence (お知らせや通信文などでよく使われる)
Typical useMarks a place or means (電話・メール) in formal announcements, and is also a set phrase at the end of postcards/letters (地名にて　名前より — "From [name], in [place]").
Word typeFormationExample
Noun (place)Nにて現地にてgenchi nite
Noun (means)Nにて電話かメールにてdenwa ka meeru nite
Letter/postcard sign-off地名にて　名前より京都にて　洋子よりKyouto nite, Youko yori
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
現地にて解散となります。Genchi nite kaisan to narimasu. We will disperse on-site. वहीं स्थान पर सब अलग हो जाएँगे। ત્યાં જ સ્થળ પર બધા છૂટા પડશે.
電話かメールにてご連絡ください。Denwa ka meeru nite go-renraku kudasai. Please contact us by either phone or email. कृपया फ़ोन या ईमेल द्वारा संपर्क करें। કૃપા કરીને ફોન અથવા ઈમેલ દ્વારા સંપર્ક કરો.
京都にて　洋子よりKyouto nite, Youko yori From Yoko, in Kyoto (a typical postcard sign-off) क्योटो से, योको की ओर से (यात्रा से भेजे गए पोस्टकार्ड के अंत में) ક્યોટોથી, યોકો તરફથી (પ્રવાસ પરથી મોકલેલા પોસ્ટકાર્ડના અંતે)

にて looks similar to において at a glance, but is narrower and even more formal/old-fashioned — it's the go-to choice for written notices, official correspondence, and postcard sign-offs, not general academic argument (that's において's territory).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four of today's forms can loosely translate as "in/regarding ~" in English, but they mark very different relationships — exactly what the shared-option quiz above (Q⑥⑦⑧) is built to test. Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningRegisterKey differenceUse when
〜をはじめ（として）~o hajime (to shite) Starting with ~ Neutral Cites one representative/leading example of a larger group. Listing a notable example, implying "and others like it."
〜をめぐって（めぐるN）~o megutte / o meguru N Concerning ~ (a dispute) Neutral Marks the topic of active debate, conflict, or discussion. Something is being argued about or actively discussed.
〜において（おける）~ni oite / ni okeru In/at ~ (formal で) 硬 formal A formal stand-in for で／の marking place, time, field, or topic. Formal writing/speech, reports, academic argument.
〜にて~nite At/by ~ (very formal で) 硬 very formal Narrower than において — specifically for written notices, correspondence, sign-offs. Formal announcements, letters, postcard closings.
Exam trap: において and にて both translate as a formal "at/in," but before a noun phrase (as opposed to a full clause), only における is grammatical — における is the mandatory pre-noun form of において (Xにおける Y, never Xにおいて Y or Xにて Y as a noun-modifier). Meanwhile, をはじめ (a representative example) and をめぐって (an active dispute) are easy to mix up only because both attach directly to a topic noun with を — check whether the sentence lists similar things (をはじめ) or describes conflict/discussion (をめぐって).
