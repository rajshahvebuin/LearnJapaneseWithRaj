Answer key

Notes
All four points share the root もの, which is why the divider page groups them together — but they split into two very different jobs: ものなら/ものだから/んだもの all give a reason or hypothetical condition, while ものの alone means "although" (a concessive, the odd one out). んだもの／もん is explicitly marked in the source as mainly used by women and children (casual, pouty excuse tone) — this book doesn't have a site badge for that register, so it's called out in plain text below.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ものなら

neutral register
Reading~mono nara
Meaning (EN)"if only I could ~ / if it were possible to ~" — a hypothetical that is actually difficult or impossible
Meaning (HI)अगर ...हो सके तो / काश ...कर पाता (व्यावहारिक रूप से कठिन/असंभव)
Meaning (GU)જો ...થઈ શકે તો / કાશ ...કરી શકાત (વ્યવહારમાં મુશ્કેલ/અશક્ય)
Connection (接続)V-れる (potential form) + ものなら／もんなら (casual)
RegisterNeutral; もんなら is the casual/spoken variant
Typical useOnly attaches to a verb already in potential form — expresses "if I actually could do this (which is hard/impossible), I would." Often followed by a wish or a challenge ("try it if you can").
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential)V-れる + ものなら戻れるものならmodoreru mono nara
Verb (potential, casual)V-れる + もんならやれるもんならyareru mon nara
い-adjective / な-adjective / Noun(added, not in book — this pattern is verb-only; it needs a potential form to express "if it were possible")—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
帰れるものなら、今すぐ、国へ帰りたい。（=帰れないが、もしも帰れるなら）Kaereru mono nara, ima sugu, kuni e kaeritai. If I could, I'd like to go back to my country right now. अगर हो सके, तो मैं अभी अपने देश वापस जाना चाहता हूँ। જો શક્ય હોય તો, હું અત્યારે જ મારા દેશ પાછો જવા માંગુ છું.
やれるもんなら、やってみろ。（=できないと思うが、もしできるなら）Yareru mon nara, yatte miro. If you can do it, then let's see you try. अगर कर सकते हो, तो करके दिखाओ। જો કરી શકતા હો, તો કરી બતાવો.

Because ものなら always attaches to a potential form, the underlying meaning is almost always "this is hard or impossible, but IF it were possible..." — it's a wistful or challenging hypothetical, never a plain neutral "if".

【2】 〜ものだから

neutral register
Reading~mono da kara
Meaning (EN)"because ~" — gives a reason, often as an excuse for something already done
Meaning (HI)क्योंकि ...है / ...की वजह से (सफ़ाई/कारण बताते हुए)
Meaning (GU)કેમકે ...છે / ...ના કારણે (કારણ/સફાઈ આપતાં)
Connection (接続)V/A (plain form) + ものだから　|　な-adj + なものだから　|　N + なものだから　|　もんだから (casual)
RegisterNeutral; もんだから is the casual/spoken variant
Typical useExplains a reason for something that already happened or is happening — often used to justify or excuse an action (being late, doing something unusual).

上着を脱いでもいいですか。暑いものですから。（=暑いので）Uwagi o nuide mo ii desu ka. Atsui mono desu kara. Can I take my jacket off? I'm feeling a bit hot. क्या मैं जैकेट उतार सकता हूँ? थोड़ी गर्मी लग रही है। શું હું જેકેટ ઉતારી શકું? થોડી ગરમી લાગે છે.

ものだから reads as a straightforward, adult explanation of a reason — compare with point 3 below, which is the same idea but in a much more childish/whiny tone.

【3】 〜んだもの／〜もん

casual/spoken 💬♥
Reading~n da mono / ~mon
Meaning (EN)"because ~" — a childish, pouty excuse; softer and more emotional than ものだから
Meaning (HI)क्योंकि...है ना (बचकाना/मासूम बहाना देते हुए)
Meaning (GU)કેમકે...છે ને (બાળક જેવું બહાનું આપતાં)
Connection (接続)V/A (plain form) + んだもの／もん　|　な-adj + なんだもの　|　N + なんだもの
RegisterCasual, spoken — the source explicitly notes it's mostly used by women and children (◆女性や子どもがよく使う), marked with a speech-bubble and heart icon in the book (no dedicated site badge for this, so noted here in plain text)

な-adjective / Nounな-adj／N + なんだもんいや（なん）だもんiya (nan) da mon
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
パーティーには行かなかったよ。知らなかった（んだ）もの。（=知らなかったから）Paatii ni wa ikanakatta yo. Shiranakatta (n da) mono. I didn't go to the party, because I didn't know it was on. मैं पार्टी में नहीं गया। मुझे पता ही नहीं था। હું પાર્ટીમાં ગયો નહોતો. મને ખબર જ નહોતી.

Today's title itself, 空を飛びたいんだもの ("because I'd love to fly in the sky!"), is this exact pattern — a childlike, wistful excuse. Keep んだもの／もん strictly for casual conversation; it would sound very odd in a formal or written context.

【4】 〜ものの

neutral register
Reading~mono no
Meaning (EN)"although ~ / even though ~" — a concessive, slightly more formal than けれど
Meaning (HI)हालाँकि ...है, फिर भी... / ...तो है, लेकिन...
Meaning (GU)જોકે ...છે, છતાં... / ...ખરું, પણ...
Connection (接続)V/A (plain form) + ものの　|　な-adj + だものの　|　N + だ／である + ものの
RegisterNeutral, slightly formal-leaning — common in both speech and writing
Typical useConcedes one fact, then states a contrasting result. With an adjective or noun, it's very often used as the fixed phrase 「〜とはいうものの」.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb / い-adjectiveplain form + ものの持っているもののmotte iru monono
な-adjectiveな-adj + だものの静かだもののshizuka da monono
NounN + だ／である + ものの春とはいうもののharu to wa iu monono
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
車の免許は持っているものの、ほとんど運転したことがない。（=持っているけれど）Kuruma no menkyo wa motte iru monono, hotondo unten shita koto ga nai. Although I have a driver's license, I have almost never driven. मेरे पास गाड़ी चलाने का लाइसेंस तो है, लेकिन मैंने लगभग कभी गाड़ी नहीं चलाई। મારી પાસે ડ્રાઇવિંગ લાયસન્સ છે ખરું, પણ મેં લગભગ ક્યારેય ગાડી ચલાવી નથી.
申し込みはしたものの、試験を受けるかどうか未定だ。（=申し込んだけれど）Moushikomi wa shita monono, shiken o ukeru ka dou ka mitei da. Although I applied, I haven't decided whether I take the test or not. आवेदन तो कर दिया, लेकिन परीक्षा दूँगा या नहीं यह अभी तय नहीं है। અરજી તો કરી દીધી, પણ પરીક્ષા આપીશ કે નહીં તે હજુ નક્કી નથી.
春とはいうものの、まだ寒い。（=春だというけれど）Haru to wa iu monono, mada samui. Although it is spring, it is still cold. वसंत ऋतु तो है, लेकिन अभी भी ठंड है। વસંત ઋતુ છે ખરી, પણ હજુ ઠંડી છે.

ものの is the odd one out among today's four もの-forms: the other three (ものなら, ものだから, んだもの) all give a reason or condition, while ものの alone is concessive ("although"). Don't let the shared もの root trick you into treating them as synonyms.

Q1

「また、同じビデオ見てるの。」「だって、おもしろいんだ（a. もん　b. もんだから）。」"Mata, onaji bideo miteru no." "Datte, omoshiroi n da (a. mon　b. mon dakara)."

Full sentence: だって、おもしろいんだもん。Datte, omoshiroi n da mon.
EN: "Are you watching the same video again?" "Because it's fun!"
HI: "फिर वही वीडियो देख रहे हो?" "क्योंकि यह मज़ेदार है!"
GU: "ફરી એ જ વીડિયો જુઓ છો?" "કેમકે એ મજાનું છે!"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aもんmonbecause (it's fun) — childlike excuse, sentence-finalक्योंकि (मासूम अंदाज़ में)કેમકે (બાળક જેવા અંદાજમાં)
bもんだからmon dakara(grammatically needs a following clause — can't end the sentence here)(यह रूप वाक्य को यहीं ख़त्म नहीं कर सकता)(આ સ્વરૂપ વાક્યને અહીં પૂરું કરી શકતું નથી)

Why: もん (point 3) can stand alone at the end of a casual sentence as a whole excuse; もんだから (point 2) needs a continuation clause after it, so it can't close the sentence here.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

学校は ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ついていけない。Gakkou wa ___ tsuiteikenai.

Tiles: 1 休まずに (yasumazu ni)　2 授業には (jugyou ni wa)　3 ものの (monono)　4 行っている (itte iru)

1 休まずに→4 行っている→3 ものの→2 授業には

学校は休まずに行っているものの授業にはついていけない。Gakkou wa yasumazu ni itte iru monono jugyou ni wa tsuiteikenai.

EN: Although I go to school without missing a day, I can't keep up with the classes.
HI: स्कूल तो बिना नागा किए जाता हूँ, लेकिन क्लास के साथ तालमेल नहीं बिठा पाता।
GU: શાળાએ તો રજા પાડ્યા વગર જાઉં છું, પણ વર્ગ સાથે તાલ મેળવી શકતો નથી.

Why: Built on point 4 (ものの) — 行っているものの concedes the fact (attending regularly), then 授業にはついていけない gives the contrasting result.

Q7

今日は早く帰ってきたんです。＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ から。Kyou wa hayaku kaette kita n desu. ___ kara.

Tiles: 1 よくない (yokunai)　2 です (desu)　3 もの (mono)　4 体調が (taichou ga)

4 体調が→1 よくない→3 もの→2 です

今日は早く帰ってきたんです。体調がよくないものですから。Kyou wa hayaku kaette kita n desu. Taichou ga yokunai mono desu kara.

EN: I came home early today. It's because I'm not feeling well.
HI: आज मैं जल्दी घर आ गया। क्योंकि तबीयत ठीक नहीं है।
GU: આજે હું વહેલો ઘરે આવી ગયો. કેમકે તબિયત સારી નથી.

Why: Built on point 2 (ものだから) — 体調がよくないものですから gives a polite, adult-register reason for coming home early.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today share the root もの, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1–Q3 and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ものなら~mono nara If only I could ~ V-れる (potential) + ものなら Only attaches to a potential verb form; expresses a difficult/impossible hypothetical. You're wishing for or challenging something that's hard/impossible to do.
〜ものだから~mono da kara Because ~ (adult explanation) V/A (plain)／な-adj+な／N+な + ものだから A neutral, everyday reason/excuse for something already done. You're giving a plain, adult-toned explanation.
〜んだもの／もん~n da mono/mon Because ~ (childish excuse) V/A (plain)／な-adj+なんだ／N+なんだ + もの／もん Same "reason" job as ものだから, but casual, whiny, mainly women/children. You're excusing yourself the way a child would — informal speech only.
〜ものの~mono no Although ~ V/A (plain)／な-adj+だ／N+だ・である + ものの The only concessive of the four — concedes a fact, then contrasts it. You're conceding one point before stating a contrasting result.
Exam trap: ものなら and ものの look similar at a glance (both attach after a verb and both are somewhat formal-sounding), but ものなら needs a potential verb and sets up a hypothetical wish, while ものの takes a plain form (any tense) and sets up a concession. Meanwhile ものだから and んだもの mean exactly the same thing ("because") but differ only in register — swapping them doesn't change meaning, only tone. When a question gives you a もの-family choice, first ask: hypothetical (→ ものなら), reason (→ ものだから／んだもの, pick by register), or concession (→ ものの)?
