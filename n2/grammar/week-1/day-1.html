Notes
All four points today are suffixes that turn a noun/adjective/verb into a "tendency / appearance" description — げ, がち, っぽい, and 気味 are the four forms N2 loves to test against each other (this week's divider page groups them as one 4-item bullet list). None of the four is tagged 硬 (formal) in the source — they're all neutral, everyday register.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜げ

neutral register
Reading~ge
Meaning (EN)"looks/seems ~" — describes an outward appearance you can visually observe (never your own stated feeling)
Meaning (HI)...जैसा दिखता है / ...जैसी झलक देता है (बाहर से दिखने वाला भाव)
Meaning (GU)...જેવું દેખાય છે (બહારથી દેખાતો ભાવ)
Connection (接続)い-adj (drop い) + げ　|　な-adj (drop な) + げ　|　V-たい (drop い) + げ
RegisterNeutral — common in everyday speech and writing
Typical useDescribing how someone/something looks from the outside — a facial expression, an attitude, a vibe. Two irregulars: よい→よさげ, ない→なさげ (a さ is inserted, not よげ/なげ).
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj (stem) + げさびしげsabishige
な-adjectiveな-adj (stem) + げ自信ありげjishin arige
Verb (たい-form)V-たい (stem) + げ言いたげiitage
Irregularよい→よさげ／ない→なさげ (さ inserted)自信なさげjishin nasage
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
あの人はさびしげな目をしている。（=さびしそうな）Ano hito wa sabishige na me o shite iru. That person has a lonely look in his eye. उस व्यक्ति की आँखों में एक उदास सी झलक है। તે વ્યક્તિની આંખોમાં એક ઉદાસ ઝલક દેખાય છે.
彼は何か言いたげだった。（=言いたそう）Kare wa nanika iitage datta. He looked like he wanted to say something. ऐसा लग रहा था जैसे वह कुछ कहना चाहता था। એવું લાગતું હતું કે તે કંઈક કહેવા માંગે છે.

げ is always about a look you can see from outside — a face, an eye, an attitude — never about how the speaker himself explicitly says he feels. Compare with 〜っぽい (point 3): げ is closer to "looks like ~", while っぽい is closer to "has a lot of the quality of ~".

【2】 〜がち

neutral register
Reading~gachi
Meaning (EN)"tends to ~ / prone to ~ / often ~" — a repeated, usually unwanted, tendency
Meaning (HI)अक्सर ...हो जाता है / ...करने की प्रवृत्ति है (आमतौर पर नकारात्मक)
Meaning (GU)વારંવાર ...થાય છે / ...કરવાનું વલણ ધરાવે છે (સામાન્ય રીતે નકારાત્મક)
Connection (接続)N + がち　|　V-ます (stem) + がち
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useMarks something that happens often, almost always an undesirable pattern (getting sick, being late, taking days off).
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + がち留守がちrusugachi
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます (stem) + がち遅れがちokuregachi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私は子どもの頃、病気がちだった。（=病気になることが多かった）Watashi wa kodomo no koro, byoukigachi datta. I tended to get sick when I was a child. बचपन में मैं अक्सर बीमार पड़ जाता था। બાળપણમાં હું વારંવાર બીમાર પડતો હતો.
最近、彼は仕事を休みがちだ。（=休むことが多い）Saikin, kare wa shigoto o yasumigachi da. He has been taking a lot of days off work recently. हाल ही में वह अक्सर काम से छुट्टी ले रहा है। તાજેતરમાં તે વારંવાર કામ પરથી રજા લે છે.

がち is about frequency — how often something happens over time. Don't confuse it with 気味 (point 4 in Day 4), which is about degree/intensity right now, not frequency.

【3】 〜っぽい

neutral register
Reading~ppoi
Meaning (EN)"~-ish / seems very ~ / has a lot of the quality of ~" — often slightly negative/critical in tone
Meaning (HI)...जैसा / ...की झलक बहुत ज़्यादा है (अक्सर हल्के नकारात्मक भाव में)
Meaning (GU)...જેવું / ...નો ભાવ વધારે પડતો છે (ઘણી વાર થોડું નકારાત્મક)
Connection (接続)N + っぽい　|　V-ます (stem) + っぽい　|　い-adj (stem) + っぽい
RegisterNeutral — casual/everyday, common in speech
Typical useAttaches to nouns, verb stems, or adjective stems to say something strongly resembles or is full of that quality — materials (安っぽい "looks cheap"), behavior (子どもっぽい "childish"), habits (忘れっぽい "forgetful").
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + っぽい男っぽいotokoppoi
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます (stem) + っぽい忘れっぽいwasureppoi
い-adjectiveい-adj (stem) + っぽい安っぽいyasuppoi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
年を取ると、忘れっぽくなる。（=よく忘れるようになる）Toshi o toru to, wasureppoku naru. As you get older, you become more forgetful. उम्र बढ़ने के साथ इंसान भुलक्कड़ होता जाता है। ઉંમર વધવાની સાથે માણસ ભુલકણો થતો જાય છે.
このコート、デザインはいいけれど、生地が安っぽいね。（=安く見える）Kono kooto, dezain wa ii keredo, kiji ga yasuppoi ne. This coat has a nice design, but the material looks cheap. इस कोट का डिज़ाइन अच्छा है, लेकिन कपड़ा सस्ता सा लगता है। આ કોટની ડિઝાઇન સારી છે, પણ કાપડ સસ્તું લાગે છે.

The book's example list also shows 女っぽい, 油っぽい, 水っぽい, 黒っぽい, 白っぽい, 怒りっぽい, あきっぽい — っぽい is one of the most productive suffixes in Japanese, attaching to almost any noun/adjective stem to mean "-ish" or "full of ~ quality".

【4】 〜気味

neutral register
Reading~gimi
Meaning (EN)"a bit ~ / a slight tendency toward ~" — a mild degree of some (usually negative) state, right now
Meaning (HI)थोड़ा ...जैसा / ...की हल्की झलक (अभी की स्थिति)
Meaning (GU)થોડું ...જેવું / ...નો હલકો સંકેત (અત્યારની સ્થિતિ)
Connection (接続)V-ます (stem) + 気味　|　N + 気味
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useDescribes a mild degree of a state at the present moment — tiredness, weight gain, nervousness, being a bit behind schedule.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (ます-stem)V-ます (stem) + 気味太り気味futorigimi
NounN + 気味寝不足気味nebusokugimi
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
残業続きで疲れ気味だ。（=ちょっと疲れている）Zangyou tsuzuki de tsukaregimi da. I've been feeling rather tired, because of continuous overtime work. लगातार ओवरटाइम की वजह से मैं थोड़ा थका हुआ महसूस कर रहा हूँ। સતત ઓવરટાઇમના કારણે હું થોડો થાકેલો અનુભવું છું.
新入社員は緊張気味の顔をしていた。（=ちょっと緊張した）Shinnyuu shain wa kinchougimi no kao o shite ita. The new employee looked a little bit nervous. नया कर्मचारी थोड़ा घबराया हुआ लग रहा था। નવો કર્મચારી થોડો ગભરાયેલો દેખાતો હતો.

気味 is about degree/intensity at this moment, not about how often something happens (that's がち). "疲れ気味" = "a little tired right now"; "休みがち" = "often absent, as a pattern over time".

練習Ⅰ — circle the correct option

Q1

この牛乳は水（a. っぽくて　b. 気味で）、おいしくない。Kono gyuunyuu wa mizu (a. -ppokute　b. -gimi de), oishikunai.

Full sentence: この牛乳は水っぽくて、おいしくない。Kono gyuunyuu wa mizuppokute, oishikunai.
EN: This milk is watery, and not tasty.
HI: यह दूध पानी जैसा है, स्वादिष्ट नहीं है।
GU: આ દૂધ પાણી જેવું છે, સ્વાદિષ્ટ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a水っぽくてmizuppokutewatery (っぽい on a noun)पानी जैसाપાણી જેવું
b水気味でmizugimi de(not natural — 気味 doesn't attach to 水 this way)(यह रूप स्वाभाविक नहीं)(આ સ્વરૂપ સ્વાભાવિક નથી)

Why: "Watery" as a texture/quality description is 水っぽい (N+っぽい), not 水気味 — 気味 only pairs naturally with states like 疲れ／太り／緊張, not with a raw noun like 水 describing taste.

Q2

この時計は20年使っているが、このごろ遅れ（a. っぽい　b. がち）だ。Kono tokei wa nijuunen tsukatte iru ga, kono goro okure (a. -ppoi　b. -gachi) da.

Full sentence: このごろ遅れがちだ。Kono goro okuregachi da.
EN: I've been using this watch for 20 years, but lately it tends to run slow.
HI: यह घड़ी 20 साल से इस्तेमाल कर रहा हूँ, लेकिन आजकल यह अक्सर पीछे रह जाती है।
GU: આ ઘડિયાળ 20 વર્ષથી વાપરું છું, પણ હમણાં તે વારંવાર પાછળ રહી જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a遅れっぽいokureppoi(not idiomatic here)(यहाँ अस्वाभाविक)(અહીં અસ્વાભાવિક)
b遅れがちokuregachitends to run late/behindअक्सर पीछे रह जानाવારંવાર પાછળ રહી જવું

Why: A repeated pattern over time ("it keeps running slow") is exactly what がち marks; っぽい would describe a resemblance/quality, not a recurring tendency.

Q3

二人は親し（a. げ　b. がち）に話している。Futari wa shitashi (a. -ge　b. -gachi) ni hanashite iru.

Full sentence: 二人は親しげに話している。Futari wa shitashige ni hanashite iru.
EN: The two of them are talking in a friendly-looking manner.
HI: दोनों आपस में घनिष्ठता से बात कर रहे हैं।
GU: બંને એકબીજા સાથે ઘનિષ્ઠ રીતે વાત કરી રહ્યાં છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a親しげshitashigelooking friendly/close (visible manner)घनिष्ठता दिखाते हुएઘનિષ્ઠતા દેખાડતાં
b親しがちshitashigachi(not a real word — がち needs a recurring event, not a manner)(यह शब्द मौजूद नहीं)(આ શબ્દ અસ્તિત્વમાં નથી)

Why: "Talking in a manner that looks X" calls for げ (visible appearance/manner); がち only attaches to describe a recurring event or state, and 親しがち isn't idiomatic.

Q4

風邪（a. 気味で　b. がちで）、熱（a. 気味だ　b. っぽい）。Kaze (a. -gimi de　b. -gachi de), netsu (a. -gimi da　b. -ppoi).

Full sentence: 風邪気味で、熱っぽい。Kazegimi de, netsuppoi.
EN: I have a bit of a cold, and I feel feverish.
HI: थोड़ी सर्दी-जुकाम है, और बुखार जैसा महसूस हो रहा है।
GU: થોડી શરદી છે, અને તાવ જેવું લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a, b気味で … っぽい-gimi de … -ppoia slight cold … feeling feverishथोड़ी सर्दी … बुखार जैसाથોડી શરદી … તાવ જેવું

Why: This item has two blanks and both need the "mild degree right now" reading — 気味 for the cold, っぽい for the feverish feeling (as in the day's own title 熱っぽい). がち would wrongly imply a recurring pattern rather than today's one-off state.

Full sentence: 母は忘れっぽくなった。Haha wa wasureppoku natta.
EN: My mother has become forgetful.
HI: माँ भुलक्कड़ हो गई हैं।
GU: મમ્મી ભુલકણી થઈ ગયા છે.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

彼は両親を ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ だ。Kare wa ryoushin o ___ da.

Tiles: 1 休みがち (yasumigachi)　2 仕事も (shigoto mo)　3 悲しげで (kanashige de)　4 亡くしてから (nakushite kara)
(Note: this book marks all four blanks with plain underscores, not a ★ marker, on non-test days.)

4 亡くしてから→3 悲しげで→2 仕事も→1 休みがち

彼は両親を亡くしてから悲しげで仕事も休みがちだ。Kare wa ryoushin o nakushite kara kanashige de shigoto mo yasumigachi da.

EN: Ever since he lost his parents, he has looked sad and has often been missing work too.
HI: जब से उसने अपने माता-पिता को खोया है, वह उदास दिखता है और अक्सर काम से भी छुट्टी लेता है।
GU: જ્યારથી તેણે પોતાના માતાપિતા ગુમાવ્યા છે, ત્યારથી તે ઉદાસ દેખાય છે અને વારંવાર કામ પરથી રજા પણ લે છે.

Why: 悲しげで (point 1, げ = looks sad) describes his visible manner, and 休みがち (point 2, がち = often absent) describes his recurring behavior — both suffixes from today's lesson stack in one sentence.

Q7

男は ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Otoko wa ___.

Tiles: 1 笑いを (warai o)　2 ありげな (arige na)　3 浮かべた (ukabeta)　4 意味 (imi)

4 意味→2 ありげな→1 笑いを→3 浮かべた

男は意味ありげな笑いを浮かべた。Otoko wa imi arige na warai o ukabeta.

EN: The man wore a meaningful-looking smile.
HI: उस आदमी के चेहरे पर एक अर्थपूर्ण सी मुस्कान थी।
GU: તે માણસના ચહેરા પર અર્થપૂર્ણ સ્મિત હતું.

Why: 意味ありげ ("looking like there's a meaning behind it") is a fixed げ-collocation built on point 1 — げ turns the na-adjective-like ありげ into a visible-manner description modifying 笑い.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four suffixes today attach to a noun/adjective/verb to add a "tendency or appearance" nuance, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q2, and Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜げ~ge Looks/seems ~ (visible manner) い/な-adj (stem)／V-たい (stem) + げ About an outward look you observe on someone else — never your own stated feeling. You're describing an expression, attitude, or vibe.
〜がち~gachi Tends to ~ (frequency) N／V-ます (stem) + がち About how often something (usually negative) happens over time. You're describing a repeated pattern (often absent, often sick, often late).
〜っぽい~ppoi ~-ish / full of ~ quality N／V-ます (stem)／い-adj (stem) + っぽい About resemblance or a strong quality — materials, personality traits, habits. You're describing what something resembles or is characterized by.
〜気味~gimi A bit ~ (mild degree, right now) V-ます (stem)／N + 気味 About the intensity of a state at this moment, not how often it recurs. You're describing a slight, present-tense degree of tiredness, weight gain, nervousness, etc.
Exam trap: がち and 気味 both sound like "a bit of a negative tendency," but they answer different questions — がち answers "how often?" (a repeated pattern across time, e.g. 休みがち = often takes days off), while 気味 answers "how much, right now?" (a mild degree at this moment, e.g. 疲れ気味 = a little tired today). When a question gives you a がち/気味 choice, check whether the sentence is about a repeated event (→ がち) or a present-moment degree (→ 気味).
