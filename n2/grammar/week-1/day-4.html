4日目 — かゆくてたまらない (Kayukute tamaranai)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 文法 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Bunpou) — printed p.20–21 (PDF pages 20–21)
Grammar points covered
心配でたまらないひまでしょうがないうるさくてかなわない残念でならない
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the official answer box at the bottom of p.23 (5日目's second page) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
All four points intensify a feeling or state to "unbearably ~ / can't help but ~". うるさくてかなわない and 残念でならない are tagged 硬 formal/written-leaning in the source (each carries a 硬 stamp in the margin); たまらない and しょうがない are register-neutral and common in speech.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜てたまらない

neutral register
Reading~te tamaranai
Meaning (EN)"unbearably ~ / can't stand how ~" — an emotion/sensation so strong it feels unbearable
Meaning (HI)बेहद/असहनीय रूप से ...महसूस होना (सहन नहीं हो पा रहा)
Meaning (GU)અસહ્ય રીતે ...લાગવું (સહન થતું નથી)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くて／な-adj-で／V-たくて + たまらない
RegisterNeutral — common in both speech and writing
Typical useMeans "I can hardly stand ~" — a physical sensation, emotion, or desire pushed to its limit.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj-くて + たまらないかゆくてたまらないkayukute tamaranai
な-adjectiveな-adj-で + たまらない好きでたまらないsuki de tamaranai
Verb (たい-form)V-たくて + たまらない会いたくてたまらないaitakute tamaranai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
子どものことが心配でたまらない。（=とても心配だ）Kodomo no koto ga shinpai de tamaranai. I am so worried about my children. बच्चों की चिंता में मेरा दम घुट रहा है। બાળકોની ચિંતામાં હું બેચેન થઈ ગયો છું.
家族に会いたくてたまらない。（=とても会いたい）Kazoku ni aitakute tamaranai. I can't wait to see my family. परिवार से मिलने के लिए मन बेताब है। પરિવારને મળવા માટે મન અધીરું છે.

「がまんできないぐらい〜だ」("~ to the point of not being able to bear it") is the book's own gloss for たまらない — treat it as the strongest of today's four intensifiers.

【2】 〜てしょうがない

neutral register
Reading~te shou ga nai
Meaning (EN)"can't help but be ~ / ~ to no end" — similarly strong to たまらない, common in speech
Meaning (HI)बहुत ज़्यादा ...होना (रोका नहीं जा सकता)
Meaning (GU)ખૂબ જ ...થવું (રોકી શકાતું નથી)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くて + 仕方がない／しようがない　|　な-adj-で + 仕方がない／しようがない　|　V-て + しょうがない (casual)
RegisterNeutral; しょうがない is the more casual/spoken variant
Typical useWith verbs, only ones expressing an emotion, sensation, or troubled state can be used (寒くて, 気になって, お腹がすいて) — not arbitrary action verbs.
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj-くて + 仕方がない寒くてしょうがないsamukute shou ga nai
な-adjectiveな-adj-で + しようがないひまで仕方がないhima de shikata ga nai
Verb (emotion/sensation only)V-て + しょうがない気になってしょうがないki ni natte shou ga nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
今日は何もすることがなくて、ひまで仕方がない。（=とてもひまだ）Kyou wa nani mo suru koto ga nakute, hima de shikata ga nai. I have nothing to do today. I'm so bored. आज कुछ करने को नहीं है, बहुत बोर हो रहा हूँ। આજે કંઈ કરવાનું નથી, ખૂબ કંટાળો આવે છે.
そんな方法では、時間がかかってしょうがない。（=とても時間がかかる）Sonna houhou de wa, jikan ga kakatte shou ga nai. It will take you forever to do it that way. इस तरीके से तो बहुत ज़्यादा समय लगेगा। આ રીતથી તો ઘણો સમય લાગશે.

仕方がない, しようがない, and しょうがない are three spellings/registers of the same ending — しょうがない is the casual contraction most common in spoken Japanese.

【3】 〜てかなわない

硬 formal/written
Reading~te kanawanai
Meaning (EN)"can't stand ~ / ~ is unbearable" — means "I'm troubled by ~"; only for external conditions/circumstances, never for the speaker's own emotions
Meaning (HI)...बर्दाश्त नहीं हो पा रहा (बाहरी हालात/परिस्थिति के लिए; अपनी भावनाओं के लिए नहीं)
Meaning (GU)...સહન થતું નથી (બાહ્ય પરિસ્થિતિ માટે; પોતાની લાગણી માટે નહીં)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くて／な-adj-で + かなわない
Register硬 — leans formal/written, marked with a 硬 stamp in the margin next to this entry
Typical useMeans literally "〜で困る" ("I'm troubled by ~"). Restricted to external circumstances (noise, distance, inconvenience) — not used for the speaker's own emotions (for that, use たまらない/しょうがない/ならない instead).
Word typeFormationExample
い-adjectiveい-adj-くて + かなわないうるさくてかなわないurusakute kanawanai
な-adjectiveな-adj-で + かなわない不便でかなわないfuben de kanawanai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
隣の工事がうるさくてかなわない。（=うるさくて困る）Tonari no kouji ga urusakute kanawanai. I can't stand the noise from the construction going on next door. पड़ोस के निर्माण-कार्य का शोर बर्दाश्त नहीं हो रहा। પડોશના બાંધકામનો અવાજ સહન થતો નથી.
私の家は、駅から遠くて不便でかなわない。（=不便で困る）Watashi no ie wa, eki kara tookute fuben de kanawanai. I can't stand the inconvenience due to the fact that my house is so far from the station. मेरा घर स्टेशन से दूर होने की वजह से बहुत असुविधाजनक है, बर्दाश्त नहीं हो रहा। મારું ઘર સ્ટેશનથી દૂર હોવાથી ખૂબ અગવડભર્યું છે, સહન થતું નથી.

This is the one intensifier of today's four that is restricted to external, troublesome circumstances — not usable for the speaker's own feelings like joy, worry, or longing (those need たまらない/しょうがない/ならない).

【4】 〜てならない

硬 formal/written
Reading~te naranai
Meaning (EN)"can't help feeling ~ / ~ inevitably" — a feeling that arises naturally and involuntarily
Meaning (HI)...महसूस किए बिना नहीं रह पाता (अपने-आप उठने वाली भावना)
Meaning (GU)...અનુભવ્યા વગર રહી શકાતું નથી (આપોઆપ ઉદ્ભવતી લાગણી)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くて／な-adj-で／V-て + ならない
Register硬 — leans formal/written
Typical useUsed for feelings that arise on their own, involuntarily (自然になる気持ち) — worry, pity, a nagging suspicion — never for physical sensations like itchiness (かゆい), and never as an act of will.
Word typeFormationExample
な-adjectiveな-adj-で + ならない残念でならないzannen de naranai
Verb (て-form)V-て + ならない気になってならないki ni natte naranai
い-adjective(added, not in book, but formed the same way) い-adj-くて + ならないかわいそうくてならないkawaisokute naranai (rare — usually かわいそうでならない, treating かわいそう as na-adjective)
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
親友の結婚式に出席できないのが、残念でならない。（=とても残念だ）Shinyuu no kekkonshiki ni shusseki dekinai no ga, zannen de naranai. I am so disappointed that I won't be able to attend my friend's wedding. अपने ख़ास दोस्त की शादी में शामिल न हो पाने का बहुत अफ़सोस है। મારા ખાસ મિત્રના લગ્નમાં હાજર ન રહી શકવાનો ખૂબ અફસોસ છે.
検査の結果が気になってならない。（=とても気になる）Kensa no kekka ga ki ni natte naranai. I am really concerned about the results of my physical exam. जाँच के नतीजों की चिंता सता रही है। તપાસના પરિણામોની ચિંતા સતાવી રહી છે.

ならない is reserved for feelings that well up on their own (自然になる気持ちの場合に使う) — worry, regret, suspicion. Physical sensations like かゆい ("itchy", today's own title example) use たまらない instead, never ならない.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (5 items, two-choice each) and 練習Ⅱ (2 sentence-ordering items, plain numbered blanks), both from p.21. Answers confirmed from the official answer box printed at the bottom of p.23.

練習Ⅰ — circle the correct option

Q1

試験の結果が（a. 心配に　b. 心配で）たまらない。Shiken no kekka ga (a. shinpai ni　b. shinpai de) tamaranai.

Full sentence: 試験の結果が心配でたまらない。Shiken no kekka ga shinpai de tamaranai.
EN: I'm so worried about the exam results.
HI: परीक्षा के नतीजों की चिंता में दम घुट रहा है।
GU: પરીક્ષાના પરિણામોની ચિંતામાં બેચેની થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a心配にshinpai ni(wrong particle — たまらない needs で after a な-adjective)(ग़लत — यहाँ で चाहिए)(ખોટું — અહીં で જોઈએ)
b心配でshinpai deworried, unbearably soबेहद चिंतितઅત્યંત ચિંતિત

Why: な-adjectives attach to たまらない with で, not に — 心配でたまらない is the fixed pattern.

Q2

一人じゃ（a. さびし　b. さびしい）くてしょうがない。Hitori ja (a. sabishi　b. sabishii) kute shou ga nai.

Full sentence: 一人じゃさびしくてしょうがない。Hitori ja sabishikute shou ga nai.
EN: Being alone, I feel unbearably lonely.
HI: अकेले रहने पर बहुत अकेलापन महसूस होता है।
GU: એકલા રહેવાથી ખૂબ એકલતા લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aさびしsabishiい-adjective stem, correct before くてसही रूप (स्टेम)સાચું સ્વરૂપ (સ્ટેમ)
bさびしいsabishii(dictionary form — wrong before くて)(शब्दकोश रूप, यहाँ ग़लत)(શબ્દકોશ સ્વરૂપ, અહીં ખોટું)

Why: The くて conjugation always drops the plain-form い before attaching くて — さびし + くて, not さびしい + くて.

Q3

外の音が（a. 気になって　b. 気になっては）ならない。Soto no oto ga (a. ki ni natte　b. ki ni natte wa) naranai.

Full sentence: 外の音が気になってならない。Soto no oto ga ki ni natte naranai.
EN: I can't help being bothered by the noise outside.
HI: बाहर की आवाज़ मुझे बेचैन किए जा रही है।
GU: બહારનો અવાજ મને બેચેન કરી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a気になってki ni nattebothered/on my mind, involuntarilyअनायास बेचैन करनाઅનાયાસે બેચેન કરવું
b気になってはki ni natte wa(wrong — the plain て-form attaches directly to ならない, no は)(ग़लत — यहाँ は नहीं चाहिए)(ખોટું — અહીં は ન જોઈએ)

Why: 〜てならない takes the plain て-form directly, without an inserted は — 気になってならない is the fixed pattern.

GU: જ્યારે હું વિદેશમાં રહેતો હતો, ત્યારે બેચેની અનુભવ્યા વગર રહી શકતો નહોતો.

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

セールスの ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ かなわない。Seerusu no ___ kanawanai.

Tiles: 1 何度も (nando mo)　2 人が (hito ga)　3 しつこくて (shitsukokute)　4 訪ねてきて (tazunete kite)

2 人が→1 何度も→4 訪ねてきて→3 しつこくて

セールスの人が何度も訪ねてきてしつこくてかなわない。Seerusu no hito ga nando mo tazunete kite shitsukokute kanawanai.

EN: Sales people keep coming to visit again and again, and it's unbearably persistent.
HI: सेल्स वाले बार-बार आ जाते हैं, यह ज़िद बर्दाश्त नहीं होती।
GU: સેલ્સના લોકો વારંવાર આવે છે, આ જીદ સહન થતી નથી.

Why: Built on point 3 (〜てかなわない) — this is exactly the kind of external, troublesome circumstance (repeated unwanted visits) that かなわない is reserved for.

Q7

いい夢を見ると、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ならない。Ii yume o miru to, ___ naranai.

Tiles: 1 いいことが (ii koto ga)　2 気がして (ki ga shite)　3 起こりそうな (okorisou na)　4 何か (nanika)

4 何か→1 いいことが→3 起こりそうな→2 気がして

いい夢を見ると、何かいいことが起こりそうな気がしてならない。Ii yume o miru to, nanika ii koto ga okorisou na ki ga shite naranai.

EN: When I have a good dream, I can't help feeling like something good is about to happen.
HI: जब अच्छा सपना आता है, तो लगता है कि कुछ अच्छा होने वाला है।
GU: જ્યારે સારું સપનું આવે છે, ત્યારે લાગે છે કે કંઈક સારું થવાનું છે.

Why: Built on point 4 (〜てならない) — 気がしてならない is the fixed collocation for "I can't help but feel/suspect ~", a feeling that wells up naturally rather than being willed.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four forms today intensify a state to "unbearably ~", which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q3, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜てたまらない~te tamaranai Unbearably ~ Aくて／naで／Vたくて + たまらない Broadest use — any strong emotion, sensation, or desire. Your default choice for "I can hardly stand this feeling."
〜てしょうがない~te shou ga nai Can't help but be ~ Aくて／naで／Vて (emotion verbs only) + しょうがない Casual/spoken equivalent of たまらない; verbs restricted to emotion/sensation types. Everyday conversation, same strength as たまらない.
〜てかなわない~te kanawanai Can't stand ~ (troubled by) Aくて／naで + かなわない Only for external circumstances/conditions — never the speaker's own emotions. Complaining about noise, distance, inconvenience, or other outside nuisances.
〜てならない~te naranai Can't help feeling ~ (involuntary) Aくて／naで／Vて + ならない Only for feelings that arise naturally/involuntarily — not physical sensations, not willed actions. Worry, regret, or a nagging suspicion that wells up on its own.
Exam trap: かなわない and ならない both sound formal and both follow the same Aくて／naで connection pattern, but they cover opposite domains: かなわない is strictly for external, troublesome circumstances (never the speaker's own emotion), while ならない is strictly for involuntary feelings (never a physical sensation or external nuisance). たまらない is the safe, broad default that covers both emotions and sensations; しょうがない is its casual twin, but only for emotion/sensation verbs. When a question gives you one of these four, ask: is this an external annoyance (→ かなわない), an involuntary feeling (→ ならない), or a strong sensation/emotion/desire in general (→ たまらない／しょうがない)?
