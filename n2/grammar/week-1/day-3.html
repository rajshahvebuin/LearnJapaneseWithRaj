Notes
All four points today are ways of "setting one thing aside" to focus attention elsewhere — もとより (of course, not to mention), ともかく (regardless of/aside from), まだしも (X would be tolerable, but not this), and 抜きにして (excluding/without). もとより is tagged 硬 formal/written in the source as a stiffer version of はもちろん; the other three are register-neutral.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 Nはもとより〜も

硬 formal/written
ReadingN wa motoyori ~mo
Meaning (EN)"not to mention N, ~ too / N of course, and ~ as well"
Meaning (HI)N की तो बात ही क्या, ...भी (N तो ज़ाहिर है, ...भी वैसा ही)
Meaning (GU)N ની તો વાત જ શું, ...પણ (N તો દેખીતું છે, ...પણ)
Connection (接続)N + はもとより + 〜も
Register硬 — a formal, written-register version of 「Nはもちろん〜も」
Typical useLists a baseline fact (obviously true) then adds a further, sometimes more surprising, point that's also true.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + はもとより + 〜も車はもとより自転車もkuruma wa motoyori jitensha mo
い-adjective / な-adjective / Verb(added, not in book — this pattern attaches to nouns; a verb/adjective phrase would need to be nominalized first, e.g. 食べるのはもとより)—
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
うちには、車はもとより自転車もないんです。（=もちろん車もないし）Uchi ni wa, kuruma wa motoyori jitensha mo nai n desu. My family doesn't have a bicycle, let alone a car. हमारे घर में कार तो है ही नहीं, साइकिल भी नहीं है। અમારા ઘરે કાર તો છે જ નહીં, સાઇકલ પણ નથી.
レタスはサラダで食べるのはもとより、炒めてもおいしい。（=もちろんサラダで食べるのもおいしいし）Retasu wa sarada de taberu no wa motoyori, itamete mo oishii. Lettuce is of course good in salad, but it is good in a stir-fry too. लेट्यूस सलाद में खाना तो अच्छा है ही, भूनकर भी स्वादिष्ट लगता है। લેટ્યુસ સલાડમાં ખાવું તો સારું છે જ, તળીને પણ સ્વાદિષ્ટ લાગે છે.

もとより is stiffer/more written than もちろん — expect it in essays, formal announcements, or news-style writing rather than casual conversation.

【2】 N₁はともかく（として）N₂は／が

neutral register
ReadingN1 wa tomokaku (to shite) N2 wa/ga
Meaning (EN)"regardless of/setting aside whether N1 is ~ or not, N2 ~"
Meaning (HI)N1 की बात छोड़ो / N1 का जो भी हो, N2...
Meaning (GU)N1 ની વાત બાજુ પર રાખો / N1 જે હોય તે, N2...
Connection (接続)N1 + はともかく（として） + N2は／が
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useSets one topic aside as not the point right now, then shifts focus to a second topic that matters more in this context.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN1はともかく（として）N2は／があなたはともかく、私はanata wa tomokaku, watashi wa
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
母の料理は見た目はともかく味はおいしい。（=見た目はどうか、わからないが）Haha no ryouri wa mitame wa tomokaku aji wa oishii. Whether it looks good or not, my mother's cooking tastes good. माँ के खाने का दिखावट चाहे जैसा भी हो, स्वाद बहुत अच्छा होता है। મમ્મીની રસોઈ દેખાવમાં ભલે ગમે તેવી હોય, સ્વાદ ખૂબ સરસ હોય છે.
あの女優は、顔はともかくとして演技がすばらしい。（=顔はどうか、わからないが）Ano joyuu wa, kao wa tomokaku to shite engi ga subarashii. Whether she is good looking or not, she is a great actress. उस अभिनेत्री का चेहरा चाहे जैसा भी हो, उसका अभिनय शानदार है। તે અભિનેત્રીનો ચહેરો ભલે ગમે તેવો હોય, તેનો અભિનય શાનદાર છે.

Unlike point 3 (まだしも), ともかく doesn't rank the two things by tolerability — it simply says "let's not talk about N1 right now; N2 is what matters here."

【3】 Nはまだしも／Nならまだしも

neutral register
ReadingN wa madashimo / N nara madashimo
Meaning (EN)"N might still be acceptable, but ~ (something worse) is not"
Meaning (HI)N होता तो चल जाता, लेकिन...(यह तो हद है)
Meaning (GU)N હોત તો ચાલત, પણ...(આ તો હદ છે)
Connection (接続)N + は／なら + まだしも
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useSets a lower bar of tolerance with N, then implies the actual situation goes well beyond it — often with a tone of complaint or disbelief.
Word typeFormationExample
NounN + は／なら + まだしも旅行はまだしもryokou wa madashimo
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
私の英語力では、旅行はまだしも、留学なんて無理です。（=旅行なら少しはいいが）Watashi no eigo-ryoku de wa, ryokou wa madashimo, ryuugaku nante muri desu. With my English skills, I can handle travelling but not studying abroad. मेरी अंग्रेज़ी के स्तर से यात्रा तो चल जाएगी, लेकिन विदेश में पढ़ाई करना मुमकिन नहीं है। મારી અંગ્રેજીના સ્તરથી પ્રવાસ તો ચાલશે, પણ વિદેશમાં ભણવું શક્ય નથી.
10分や20分ならまだしも、1時間も待てません。（=10分や20分なら少しはいいが）Juppun ya nijuppun nara madashimo, ichijikan mo matemasen. I can wait for ten or twenty minutes, but not for an hour. दस-बीस मिनट होते तो ठीक था, लेकिन एक घंटे तक इंतज़ार नहीं कर सकता। દસ-વીસ મિનિટ હોત તો ઠીક હતું, પણ એક કલાક રાહ જોઈ શકતો નથી.

まだしも always carries an implicit comparison: "X would be fine, but the real situation is worse than X." That built-in judgment/complaint is what separates it from the neutral ともかく in point 2.

【4】 Nは抜きにして／N（を）抜きにしては

neutral register
ReadingN wa nuki ni shite / N (o) nuki ni shite wa
Meaning (EN)"without N / leaving N aside / excluding N" (with は+negative potential: "cannot happen without N")
Meaning (HI)N को छोड़कर / N के बिना (…しては+नकारात्मक के साथ: N के बिना …असंभव)
Meaning (GU)N ને બાદ કરતાં / N વગર (…しては+નકારાત્મક સાથે: N વગર …અશક્ય)
Connection (接続)Nは + 抜きにして／抜きで／抜きに／抜きのN　|　N（を） + 抜きにして（は）／抜きで（は）／抜きに（は） + Vれない
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useExcludes a topic from the conversation/situation. Combined with a negative potential verb, it emphasizes that something is impossible without that excluded element.
Word typeFormationExample
NounNは + 抜きにして仕事の話は抜きにしてshigoto no hanashi wa nuki ni shite
NounN + 抜きにお世辞抜きにoseji nuki ni
Noun (with negative potential)N抜きで（は） + Vれない田中さん抜きで（は）始められませんTanaka-san nuki de (wa) hajimeraremasen
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
皆さん、仕事の話は抜きにして楽しく飲みましょう。（=仕事の話はやめて）Minasan, shigoto no hanashi wa nuki ni shite tanoshiku nomimashou. Everyone, let's enjoy drinking and forget about work. दोस्तों, काम की बातें छोड़कर मज़े से पीते हैं। મિત્રો, કામની વાતો બાજુ પર રાખીને મજાથી પીએ.
お世辞抜きに、君の日本語は本当にうまいよ。（=お世辞ではなく）Oseji nuki ni, kimi no nihongo wa hontou ni umai yo. I am not flattering you. Your Japanese is really good. बिना खुशामद के कहूँ तो, तुम्हारी जापानी सच में बहुत अच्छी है। ખુશામત વગર કહું તો, તારું જાપાનીઝ ખરેખર બહુ સારું છે.
田中さん抜きで（は）パーティーは始められません。（=田中さんがいなくては）Tanaka-san nuki de (wa) paatii wa hajimeraremasen. We can't start the party without Tanaka-san. तनाका जी के बिना पार्टी शुरू नहीं की जा सकती। તનાકા સાહેબ વગર પાર્ટી શરૂ કરી શકાય નહીં.

Note the four interchangeable forms after N: 抜きにして／抜きで／抜きに／抜きのN — all mean roughly "excluding N", differing mainly in what follows grammatically (a clause, a te-form continuation, or a noun modifier).

Q1

この店の品物は、質は（a. もとより　b. ともかく）種類は多い。Kono mise no shinamono wa, shitsu wa (a. motoyori　b. tomokaku) shurui wa ooi.

Full sentence: この店の品物は、質はともかく種類は多い。Kono mise no shinamono wa, shitsu wa tomokaku shurui wa ooi.
EN: Regardless of the quality, this shop's goods come in a great variety.
HI: इस दुकान के सामान की गुणवत्ता चाहे जैसी हो, किस्में बहुत हैं।
GU: આ દુકાનના સામાનની ગુણવત્તા ભલે ગમે તેવી હોય, પ્રકારો ઘણા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aもとよりmotoyori(wrong — もとより adds a further true point, it doesn't set one aside)(ग़लत — यह जोड़ता है, अलग नहीं करता)(ખોટું — આ ઉમેરે છે, બાજુ પર રાખતું નથી)
bともかくtomokakusetting quality aside / regardless of qualityगुणवत्ता की बात छोड़ोગુણવત્તાની વાત બાજુ પર રાખો

Why: The sentence is redirecting focus from quality to variety — that "set this aside, focus on that" job belongs to ともかく, not もとより (which stacks two things that are both true).

GU: પ્રવાસ માટે સમયની તો વાત જ છોડો, પૈસા પણ નથી.

Q6

この家具、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Kono kagu, ___.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four points today are ways of setting one thing aside relative to another, which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q1, Q2, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
Nはもとより〜もN wa motoyori ~mo Not to mention N, ~ too N + はもとより + 〜も Stacks two things that are both true — a formal もちろん. You're adding a further true point on top of an obvious one.
N₁はともかくN₂はN1 wa tomokaku N2 wa Regardless of N1, N2 ~ N1 + はともかく（として） + N2は／が Redirects attention from N1 to N2, no ranking/judgment implied. You're saying "let's not discuss N1 right now; N2 is what matters."
NはまだしもN wa madashimo N might be tolerable, but ~ isn't N + は／なら + まだしも Ranks by tolerability — implies the real situation is worse than N. You're complaining that something exceeds an already-generous limit.
Nは抜きにしてN wa nuki ni shite Without/excluding N Nは + 抜きにして／抜きで／抜きに Literally removes N from the situation — no comparison or ranking at all. You're leaving something out entirely (a topic, an ingredient, a person).
Exam trap: もとより and ともかく can both follow a topic marked with は, but もとより adds a second true fact ("N, and also ~"), while ともかく drops N from consideration to focus on something else ("never mind N, what about ~"). まだしも looks similar to ともかく too, but only まだしも carries a built-in judgment that the real situation is worse than the tolerable baseline. When a question gives you one of these four, check: are two truths being stacked (→ もとより), is attention being redirected without judgment (→ ともかく), is a worse-than-tolerable situation being complained about (→ まだしも), or is something being removed entirely (→ 抜きにして)?
