Notes
帰らねばならない and 忘れてはならない are both tagged 硬 formal/written in the source — formal equivalents of なければならない and てはいけない respectively. 待っていられない and 遊んでばかりはいられない are register-neutral and common in speech.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ねばならない

硬 formal/written
Reading~neba naranai
Meaning (EN)Formal equivalent of 〜なければならない — "must ~"
Meaning (HI)...करना ही होगा (औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)...કરવું જ પડશે (ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V-nai-stem (drop ない) + ねば + ならない／ならぬ　|　irregular: する→せねば
Register硬 — formal, written or careful speech
Typical useSame meaning as なければならない, but stiffer — common in official announcements, essays, and formal instructions.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (nai-stem)V-nai-stem + ねばならない／ならぬ帰らねばならないkaeraneba naranai
Verb する (irregular)する→せねば + ならない確認せねばならないkakunin seneba naranai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
ビザが切れたので、国に帰らねばならない。（=帰らなければならない）Biza ga kireta node, kuni ni kaeraneba naranai. I have to go back to my country as my visa has expired. वीज़ा ख़त्म हो जाने के कारण मुझे अपने देश वापस जाना ही होगा। વિઝા પૂરો થઈ ગયો હોવાથી મારે મારા દેશ પાછા જવું જ પડશે.
それが真実であることを確かめねばならない。（=確かめなければならない）Sore ga shinjitsu de aru koto o tashikameneba naranai. I have to confirm if it is true. यह सच है या नहीं, इसकी पुष्टि करनी ही होगी। આ સાચું છે કે નહીં, તેની ખાતરી કરવી જ પડશે.

ならぬ is an even more classical/literary alternative ending for the same pattern — recognize it in written passages, but ならない is the safer default to produce yourself.

【2】 〜てはならない

硬 formal/written
Reading~te wa naranai
Meaning (EN)"must not ~ / shall not ~" — expresses prohibition
Meaning (HI)...नहीं करना चाहिए / ...करना वर्जित है
Meaning (GU)...ન કરવું જોઈએ / ...કરવું નિષિદ્ધ છે
Connection (接続)V-て + はならない
Register硬 — formal, expresses 禁止 (prohibition)
Typical useA formal prohibition — stiffer than てはいけない, common in rules, laws, and serious warnings.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + はならない忘れてはならないwasurete wa naranai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
この悲惨な体験を決して忘れてはならない。（=忘れてはいけない）Kono hisan na taiken o kesshite wasurete wa naranai. We shall never forget this tragic experience. इस दुखद अनुभव को कभी नहीं भूलना चाहिए। આ કરુણ અનુભવ ક્યારેય ભૂલવો ન જોઈએ.
失敗を人のせいにしてはなりません。（=してはいけません）Shippai o hito no sei ni shite wa narimasen. You should not put blame on others for your own mistakes. अपनी गलती के लिए दूसरों को ज़िम्मेदार नहीं ठहराना चाहिए। પોતાની ભૂલ માટે બીજાને દોષ ન આપવો જોઈએ.

Point 1 (ねばならない = "must") and point 2 (てはならない = "must not") are grammatical opposites built from similar-looking parts — don't let their shared ならない ending cause you to mix up obligation with prohibition.

【3】 〜ていられない

neutral register
Reading~te irarenai
Meaning (EN)"can't keep on ~-ing / can't afford to keep ~-ing" — running out of patience or time to continue an action
Meaning (HI)...करते रहने की गुंजाइश/धैर्य नहीं बचा
Meaning (GU)...કરતા રહેવાની ધીરજ/અવકાશ રહ્યો નથી
Connection (接続)V-て + (は)いられない　|　V-てられない　|　V-てらんない (casual contraction)
RegisterNeutral; てらんない is the casual/spoken contraction
Typical useDescribes reaching the limit of patience or time for continuing some ongoing action.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + いられない見ていられないmite irarenai
Verb (contracted)V-てられない待ってられないmattarerenai
Verb (casual contraction)V-てらんない見てらんないmiterannai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
田中君、遅いなあ。もう待ってられないから先に行こう。（=待ち続けられない）Tanaka-kun, osoi naa. Mou mattarerenai kara saki ni ikou. Mr. Tanaka is very late. I do not want to wait for him forever. Let's go ahead. तनाका बहुत देर कर रहा है। अब और इंतज़ार नहीं कर सकते, चलो आगे बढ़ते हैं। તનાકા બહુ મોડું કરે છે. હવે વધુ રાહ જોઈ શકાય નહીં, ચાલો આગળ વધીએ.
忙しくてテレビなんか見てらんない。（=見ることを続けられない）Isogashikute terebi nanka miterannai. I am so busy I have no time to be watching TV. इतना व्यस्त हूँ कि टीवी देखने की फ़ुर्सत ही नहीं है। એટલો વ્યસ્ત છું કે ટીવી જોવાનો સમય જ નથી.

Today's own title 見ていられない ("I can't bear to watch") uses this exact pattern — running out of the emotional patience to keep watching something distressing.

【4】 〜てばかりはいられない

neutral register
Reading~te bakari wa irarenai
Meaning (EN)"can't just keep on ~-ing (there's something more important to do)"
Meaning (HI)हमेशा सिर्फ़ ...करते नहीं रह सकते (कुछ ज़्यादा ज़रूरी काम है)
Meaning (GU)હંમેશા ફક્ત ...કરતા રહી શકાય નહીં (કંઈક વધુ જરૂરી કામ છે)
Connection (接続)V-て + ばかりは／ばかりも + いられない
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useSays the speaker cannot exclusively/only keep doing one thing, because responsibility or circumstance demands something else.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (て-form)V-て + ばかりは + いられない遊んでばかりはいられないasonde bakari wa irarenai
Verb (て-form)V-て + ばかりも + いられない頼ってばかりもいられないtayotte bakari mo irarenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
連休だけれど、もうすぐ試験があるから、遊んでばかりはいられない。（=いつも遊んでいることはできない）Renkyuu dakeredo, mou sugu shiken ga aru kara, asonde bakari wa irarenai. It is a long holiday but I have an exam coming, so I cannot just play around. लंबी छुट्टी तो है, लेकिन जल्द ही परीक्षा है, इसलिए हमेशा खेलते नहीं रह सकता। લાંબી રજા છે ખરી, પણ ટૂંક સમયમાં પરીક્ષા છે, એટલે હંમેશા રમતો રહી શકાય નહીં.
もう親に頼ってばかりはいられない。（=いつも頼ることはできない）Mou oya ni tayotte bakari wa irarenai. I cannot keep depending on my parents. अब हमेशा माता-पिता पर निर्भर नहीं रह सकता। હવે હંમેશા માતાપિતા પર આધાર રાખી શકાય નહીં.

Compare with point 3: 見ていられない is about running out of patience to continue one specific act, while 遊んでばかりはいられない is about a broader life-responsibility overriding a habitual behavior.

Q1

国民はもう少し政治に関心を（a. 持た　b. 持ち）ねばならない。Kokumin wa mou sukoshi seiji ni kanshin o (a. mota　b. mochi) neba naranai.

Full sentence: 国民はもう少し政治に関心を持たねばならない。Kokumin wa mou sukoshi seiji ni kanshin o motaneba naranai.
EN: Citizens must take a little more interest in politics.
HI: नागरिकों को राजनीति में थोड़ी और दिलचस्पी लेनी ही होगी।
GU: નાગરિકોએ રાજકારણમાં થોડો વધુ રસ લેવો જ પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a持たmotanai-stem of 持つ, correct before ねばसही रूप (नाई-स्टेम)સાચું સ્વરૂપ (નાઇ-સ્ટેમ)
b持ちmochi(ます-stem — wrong before ねば)(ます-स्टेम, यहाँ ग़लत)(ますસ્ટેમ, અહીં ખોટું)

Why: ねば always attaches to the nai-stem (持た, from 持たない), not the masu-stem (持ち).

Q2

Full sentence: 先輩に聞いてばかりはいられない。Senpai ni kiite bakari wa irarenai.

HI: हमेशा सीनियर से पूछते नहीं रह सकता, खुद कर पाना होगा।
GU: હંમેશા સિનિયરને પૂછતો રહી શકાય નહીં, જાતે કરવું પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a聞くkiku(dictionary form — wrong before ばかりは)(शब्दकोश रूप, यहाँ ग़लत)(શબ્દકોશ સ્વરૂપ, અહીં ખોટું)

EN: The wind is so strong I can barely stand.
HI: हवा इतनी तेज़ है कि खड़ा रहना मुश्किल है।
GU: પવન એટલો જોરદાર છે કે ઊભા રહેવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aならないnaranai(wrong — this is not a feeling that wells up on its own; it's an inability to continue standing)(ग़लत — यह अपने-आप उठने वाली भावना नहीं है)(ખોટું — આ આપોઆપ ઉદ્ભવતી લાગણી નથી)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

僕は、他人に迷惑を ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Boku wa, tanin ni meiwaku o ___.

Tiles: 1 ならないと (naranai to)　2 かけては (kakete wa)　3 育ちました (sodachimashita)　4 言われて (iwarete)

2 かけては→1 ならないと→4 言われて→3 育ちました

僕は、他人に迷惑をかけてはならないと言われて育ちました。Boku wa, tanin ni meiwaku o kakete wa naranai to iwarete sodachimashita.

EN: I was raised being told that I must not cause trouble for others.
HI: मुझे यह सिखाते हुए पाला गया कि दूसरों को कभी परेशान नहीं करना चाहिए।
GU: મને એ શીખવીને ઉછેરવામાં આવ્યો કે બીજાને ક્યારેય તકલીફ ન આપવી.

Why: Built on point 2 (てはならない) — 迷惑をかけてはならない is the prohibition being quoted, embedded inside 言われて育ちました ("was raised being told that...").

Q7

恥ずかしくて ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 彼らは大声でしている。Hazukashikute ___ karera wa oogoe de shite iru.

Tiles: 1 話を (hanashi o)　2 いられない (irarenai)　3 ような (you na)　4 聞いて (kiite)

4 聞いて→2 いられない→3 ような→1 話を

恥ずかしくて聞いていられないような話を彼らは大声でしている。Hazukashikute kiite irarenai you na hanashi o karera wa oogoe de shite iru.

EN: They're loudly talking about something so embarrassing I can't bear to listen.
HI: वे इतनी शर्मनाक बातें ज़ोर से कर रहे हैं कि सुना नहीं जाता।
GU: તેઓ એટલી શરમજનક વાતો મોટેથી કરી રહ્યા છે કે સાંભળી શકાતું નથી.

Why: Built on point 3 (Vていられない) — 聞いていられない ("can't bear to keep listening") modifies 話 as a relative clause (〜ような話), describing the embarrassing content of their loud conversation.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split into an obligation/prohibition pair (points 1–2) and a "can't keep doing X" pair (points 3–4), which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q3 and Q4 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ねばならない~neba naranai Must ~ (obligation) V-nai-stem + ねばならない (irregular: する→せねば) Formal equivalent of なければならない — positive obligation. Stating a formal requirement/duty.
〜てはならない~te wa naranai Must not ~ (prohibition) V-て + はならない Formal equivalent of てはいけない — negative prohibition. Stating a formal rule/ban.
〜ていられない~te irarenai Can't keep ~-ing (one ongoing act) V-て + いられない (casual: てられない／てらんない) About running out of patience/time for one specific ongoing action. You've hit your limit continuing a single act (watching, waiting, standing).
〜てばかりはいられない~te bakari wa irarenai Can't just keep ~-ing (habitual pattern) V-て + ばかりは／ばかりも + いられない About a broader life-responsibility overriding a repeated/habitual behavior. Something more important means you can't exclusively keep doing X as a lifestyle.
Exam trap: ねばならない and てはならない share the ending ならない but mean opposite things — ねば (built on the nai-stem) states an obligation ("must do"), while てはならない (built on the て-form) states a prohibition ("must not do"). Separately, ていられない and てばかりはいられない both end in いられない, but ていられない is about a single continuous action reaching its patience limit, while てばかりはいられない is about a recurring/habitual behavior being overridden by a bigger responsibility. When a question gives you one of these four, check whether it's about a duty (→ ねばならない), a ban (→ てはならない), an exhausted-patience single act (→ ていられない), or a habit that must give way to something more important (→ てばかりはいられない).
