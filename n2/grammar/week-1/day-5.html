5日目 — やめられないことはない (Yamerarenai koto wa nai)

Grammar points covered
食べないことはない覚えられないこともない言わないではいられない飲まずにはいられない
Answer key

Notes
The first two points are double-negative softeners ("it's not that ~ isn't the case"); the last two are "can't help but ~" compulsion patterns. 飲まずにはいられない is tagged 硬 formal/written as the stiffer version of 〜ないではいられない — the irregular しない→せず conjugation is worth memorizing on its own.

1 Grammar Points

【1】 〜ことはない

neutral register
Reading~koto wa nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not that ~ isn't the case / it's not like NOT ~" — a double-negative softener meaning something is possible/true, just without enthusiasm
Meaning (HI)ऐसा नहीं है कि ...नहीं होता (यानी होता तो है, पर उतना नहीं)
Meaning (GU)એવું નથી કે ...નથી થતું (એટલે થાય તો છે, પણ એટલું નહીં)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くない／な-adj-じゃない／V-ない／V-られない + ことはない
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useA double negative (not + not) that ends up meaning "yes, to some extent" — softer and less direct than a plain affirmative.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain negative)V-ない + ことはない食べないことはないtabenai koto wa nai
Verb (potential negative)V-られない + ことはないやめられないことはないyamerarenai koto wa nai
い-adjective / な-adjective(added, not in book) Aくない／naじゃない + ことはない安くないことはないyasukunai koto wa nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
食べないことはないが、あまり好きじゃない。（=食べると言っていいが）Tabenai koto wa nai ga, amari suki ja nai. It doesn't mean that I won't eat it, I just don't like it much. ऐसा नहीं कि मैं इसे खाऊँगा ही नहीं, बस इतना पसंद नहीं है। એવું નથી કે હું એ ખાઈશ જ નહીં, બસ એટલું ગમતું નથી.
お酒やタバコをやめるのは難しいが、やめられないことはない。（=やめることは可能である）Osake ya tabako o yameru no wa muzukashii ga, yamerarenai koto wa nai. It is difficult to quit drinking or smoking, but it is possible to quit. शराब और सिगरेट छोड़ना मुश्किल है, लेकिन छोड़ना नामुमकिन नहीं है। દારૂ અને સિગારેટ છોડવું મુશ્કેલ છે, પણ છોડવું અશક્ય નથી.

ことはない reads as "it CAN be done/is true, just not emphatically" — a hedge, not a strong claim either way.

【2】 〜こともない

neutral register
Reading~koto mo nai
Meaning (EN)"it's not entirely impossible that ~ / it's not that ~ can't happen either" — a smaller, more tentative possibility than ことはない
Meaning (HI)ऐसा भी नहीं कि ...बिल्कुल नहीं हो सकता (हल्की सी संभावना)
Meaning (GU)એવું પણ નથી કે ...બિલકુલ ન થઈ શકે (થોડી શક્યતા)
Connection (接続)い-adj-くない／な-adj-じゃない／V-ない／V-られない + こともない
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useNear-synonym of point 1, slightly more tentative — expresses a small, grudging possibility rather than a general truth.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (potential negative)V-られない + こともない覚えられないこともないoboerarenai koto mo nai
Verb (plain negative)V-ない + こともないわからないこともないwakaranai koto mo nai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
毎日、漢字を4つか5つなら、覚えられないこともない。（=覚えられるかもしれない）Mainichi, kanji o yottsu ka itsutsu nara, oboerarenai koto mo nai. It is not impossible to memorize kanji characters if they are 4 or 5 each day. अगर रोज़ 4-5 कांजी सीखने हों, तो याद करना नामुमकिन नहीं है। જો રોજ 4-5 કાંજી શીખવાના હોય, તો યાદ રાખવું અશક્ય નથી.
この会社を辞める人の気持ちがわからないこともない。（=わかるように思う）Kono kaisha o yameru hito no kimochi ga wakaranai koto mo nai. I understand, to some extent, why he/she has quit this company. इस कंपनी को छोड़ने वाले की भावनाएँ समझ में न आएँ, ऐसा भी नहीं है। આ કંપની છોડનારની લાગણી ન સમજાય, એવું પણ નથી.

Note that in the second example, 覚えられないこともない uses the same 4つか5つなら condition that appears with ものなら-type reasoning in Day 2 — a small possibility only under a specific condition.

【3】 〜ないではいられない

neutral register
Reading~nai de wa irarenai
Meaning (EN)"can't help but do ~ / can't refrain from doing ~"
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना नहीं रह सकता
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર રહી શકતો નથી
Connection (接続)V-ない (plain negative) + ではいられない
RegisterNeutral — everyday
Typical useDescribes an impulse so strong the speaker cannot stop themselves from acting on it.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (plain negative)V-ない + ではいられない言わないではいられないiwanai de wa irarenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
態度の悪い店員に、一言文句を言わないではいられなかった。（=言うことをやめられなかった）Taido no warui teniin ni, hitokoto monku o iwanai de wa irarenakatta. I couldn't help giving a piece of my mind to the ill-mannered store clerk. बदतमीज़ दुकानदार से एक शिकायत कहे बिना नहीं रह पाया। અભદ્ર દુકાનદારને એક ફરિયાદ કહ્યા વગર રહી ન શક્યો.
工事の音がうるさくて、耳をふさがないではいられない。（=ふさぐことをやめられない）Kouji no oto ga urusakute, mimi o fusaganai de wa irarenai. The noise from the construction was so loud that I couldn't help covering my ears. निर्माण-कार्य का शोर इतना तेज़ था कि कान बंद किए बिना नहीं रह पाया। બાંધકામનો અવાજ એટલો મોટો હતો કે કાન બંધ કર્યા વગર રહી ન શકાયું.

This is the same compulsion idea as N1's てこそ-family patterns, but at N2 level it's built from the plain negative + ではいられない — an impulse you cannot resist acting on.

【4】 〜ずにはいられない

硬 formal/written
Reading~zu ni wa irarenai
Meaning (EN)Formal version of 〜ないではいられない — "can't help but ~"
Meaning (HI)...किए बिना नहीं रह सकता (औपचारिक रूप)
Meaning (GU)...કર્યા વગર રહી શકતો નથી (ઔપચારિક સ્વરૂપ)
Connection (接続)V-ない (drop ない) + ずには + いられない　|　irregular: する→せず (not しず)
Register硬 — a formal, written-register version of 〜ないではいられない
Typical useSame meaning as point 3, but in a stiffer register — common in essays and more careful speech.
Word typeFormationExample
Verb (nai-stem)V-nai-stem + ずには + いられない飲まずにはいられないnomazu ni wa irarenai
Verb する (irregular)する→せず + にはいられない感動せずにはいられないkandou sezu ni wa irarenai
JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
職場でいやなことがあると、酒を飲まずにはいられない。（=飲むことをやめられない）Shokuba de iya na koto ga aru to, sake o nomazu ni wa irarenai. I can't help having a drink when something unpleasant happens at work. दफ़्तर में कोई बुरी बात हो जाए तो शराब पिए बिना नहीं रह पाता। ઓફિસમાં કંઈક ખરાબ થાય તો દારૂ પીધા વગર રહી શકતો નથી.
あの映画を見たら、だれでも感動せずにはいられないだろう。（=感動することを止められない／感動する）Ano eiga o mitara, dare demo kandou sezu ni wa irarenai darou. Anyone who sees that movie will no doubt be moved. वह फ़िल्म देखकर कोई भी भावुक हुए बिना नहीं रह पाएगा। તે ફિલ્મ જોઈને કોઈ પણ ભાવુક થયા વગર રહી શકશે નહીં.

The irregular しない→せず (not しず) is the single most commonly tested trap for this pattern — memorize it as a set phrase.

Q1

試験の前だから、勉強し（a. ずには　b. ないでは）いられません。Shiken no mae dakara, benkyou shi (a. zu ni wa　b. nai de wa) iraremasen.

Full sentence: 試験の前だから、勉強しないではいられません。Shiken no mae dakara, benkyou shinai de wa iraremasen.
EN: Since the exam is coming up, I can't help but study.
HI: परीक्षा नज़दीक है, इसलिए पढ़ाई किए बिना नहीं रह सकता।
GU: પરીક્ષા નજીક છે, એટલે ભણ્યા વગર રહી શકતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aずにはzu ni wa(wrong — する becomes せず, not し+ずには)(ग़लत — する का सही रूप せず है)(ખોટું — するનું સાચું સ્વરૂપ せず છે)
bないではnai de wacan't help but (study)पढ़ाई किए बिना नहीं रह सकताભણ્યા વગર રહી શકતો નથી

Why: する's irregular ずに form is せず, never し+ずに — so 勉強しずには is wrong; the plain-negative pattern 勉強しないでは is what's needed here.

Q2

テレビは（a. 見る　b. 見ない）ことはないが、1日に1時間ぐらいだ。Terebi wa (a. miru　b. minai) koto wa nai ga, ichinichi ni ichijikan gurai da.

Full sentence: テレビは見ないことはないが、1日に1時間ぐらいだ。Terebi wa minai koto wa nai ga, ichinichi ni ichijikan gurai da.
EN: It's not that I don't watch TV, but it's only about an hour a day.
HI: ऐसा नहीं कि मैं टीवी नहीं देखता, बस दिन में करीब एक घंटा देखता हूँ।
GU: એવું નથી કે હું ટીવી નથી જોતો, બસ દિવસમાં લગભગ એક કલાક જોઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a見るmiru(wrong — ことはない needs a negative verb before it)(ग़लत — यहाँ नकारात्मक क्रिया चाहिए)(ખોટું — અહીં નકારાત્મક ક્રિયાપદ જોઈએ)
b見ないminaiit's not that I don't watch (double negative)ऐसा नहीं कि नहीं देखताએવું નથી કે નથી જોતો

Q3

肉はあまり好きではないが、食べない（a. こともない　b. こともある）。Niku wa amari suki de wa nai ga, tabenai (a. koto mo nai　b. koto mo aru).

Full sentence: 肉はあまり好きではないが、食べないこともない。Niku wa amari suki de wa nai ga, tabenai koto mo nai.
EN: I don't really like meat, but it's not that I never eat it.
HI: मुझे मांस बहुत पसंद नहीं है, लेकिन बिल्कुल नहीं खाता, ऐसा भी नहीं है।
GU: મને માંસ બહુ ગમતું નથી, પણ બિલકુલ નથી ખાતો, એવું પણ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこともないkoto mo naiit's not that (I) never eat itबिल्कुल नहीं खाता, ऐसा नहींબિલકુલ નથી ખાતો, એવું નથી

GU: એટલું રમૂજી હતું કે હસ્યા વગર રહી ન શકાયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいられるirareru(wrong — flips the meaning to "can refrain from laughing")(ग़लत — अर्थ उलट जाता है)(ખોટું — અર્થ ઊલટો થાય છે)

練習Ⅱ — sentence ordering (並べ替え)

Q6

たくさんは無理だが、1日に ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ない。Takusan wa muri da ga, ichinichi ni ___ nai.

Tiles: 1 なら (nara)　2 こと も (koto mo)　3 覚えられない (oboerarenai)　4 4つ (yottsu)

4 4つ→1 なら→3 覚えられない→2 こと も

たくさんは無理だが、1日に4つなら覚えられないこともない。Takusan wa muri da ga, ichinichi ni yottsu nara oboerarenai koto mo nai.

EN: A large number is out of the question, but if it's four a day, it's not entirely impossible to memorize them.
HI: बहुत सारे नामुमकिन हैं, लेकिन एक दिन में 4 हों तो याद करना नामुमकिन नहीं है।
GU: ઘણા બધા શક્ય નથી, પણ દિવસમાં 4 હોય તો યાદ રાખવું અશક્ય નથી.

Why: Built on point 2 (こともない) — 4つなら sets the condition, and 覚えられないこともない gives the tentative "it's not impossible" conclusion.

Q7

そんなことを聞いては失礼かとも思ったが、＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ ＿＿＿ 。Sonna koto o kiite wa shitsurei ka to mo omotta ga, ___.

Tiles: 1 聞かず (kikazu)　2 どうしても (dou shite mo)　3 いられなかった (irarenakatta)　4 には (ni wa)

2 どうしても→1 聞かず→4 には→3 いられなかった

そんなことを聞いては失礼かとも思ったが、どうしても聞かずにはいられなかった。Sonna koto o kiite wa shitsurei ka to mo omotta ga, dou shite mo kikazu ni wa irarenakatta.

EN: I thought it might be rude to ask such a thing, but I couldn't help asking no matter what.
HI: लगा कि ऐसा पूछना असभ्य होगा, फिर भी पूछे बिना नहीं रह पाया।
GU: લાગ્યું કે આવું પૂછવું અસભ્ય હશે, છતાં પૂછ્યા વગર રહી ન શકાયો.

Why: Built on point 4 (ずにはいられない) — 聞かずにはいられなかった is the formal-leaning "couldn't help but ask" pattern, reinforced by どうしても ("no matter what").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's four points split into two families — double-negative softeners (points 1–2) and compulsion patterns (points 3–4) — which is exactly why N2 likes to test them against each other (see Q2, Q3, and Q5 above). Compare them side by side:

FormMeaningConnectionKey differenceUse when
〜ことはない~koto wa nai It's not that ~ isn't the case Aくない／naじゃない／Vない／Vられない + ことはない A general, mild double-negative softener. Making a broad, gentle claim that something is possible/true.
〜こともない~koto mo nai It's not entirely impossible that ~ Aくない／naじゃない／Vない／Vられない + こともない A smaller, more tentative possibility than ことはない — often under a specific condition. Grudgingly admitting a small possibility exists.
〜ないではいられない~nai de wa irarenai Can't help but ~ V-ない + ではいられない Neutral-register compulsion — an impulse the speaker cannot resist. Everyday speech, describing a strong urge acted upon.
〜ずにはいられない~zu ni wa irarenai Can't help but ~ (formal) V-nai-stem + ずには + いられない (irregular: する→せず) Same meaning as the previous form, but 硬 (formal register). Writing, essays, or more careful/formal speech.
Exam trap: ことはない and こともない look almost interchangeable (both are double-negative softeners on a negative verb form), but ことはない makes a broader, more general claim while こともない signals a smaller, more grudging possibility — often tied to a specific condition (Q6's "4つなら" is a good example). Separately, don't confuse the negative-verb-based ないではいられない／ずにはいられない (points 3–4, "I can't resist doing this") with the double-negative ことはない／こともない (points 1–2, "it's not that this can't happen") — both use a negative verb form, but one is about compulsion to act, the other about softened possibility.
