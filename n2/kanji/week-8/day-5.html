2 Quiz / Exercise Section

Each printed page (左 p.132, 右 p.133) has its own 練習 exercise (4 items, "choose the correct option and mark ○"). Answers confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, PDF index 174, sections「5日目 左 (p.132)」and「5日目 右 (p.133)」.

練習 — p.132 (５日目 左)

Q1

あの店は安くておいしいと（a. 評価　b. 評判）だ。Ano mise wa yasukute oishii to (a. hyouka / b. hyouban) da.

Full sentence: あの店は安くておいしいと評判だ。Ano mise wa yasukute oishii to hyouban da.
EN: That shop is reputed to be cheap and delicious.
HI: उस दुकान की चर्चा है कि वह सस्ती और स्वादिष्ट है।
GU: તે દુકાનની પ્રતિષ્ઠા છે કે તે સસ્તી અને સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a評価hyoukaa valuation/assessment (wrong sense here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b評判hyoubanreputation, "said to be"प्रतिष्ठा, चर्चाપ્રતિષ્ઠા, ચર્ચા

Why: 評判だ is the fixed phrase for "is reputed to be / is said to be" (general public opinion); 評価 refers to a formal assessment/valuation, not word-of-mouth reputation.

Q2

人間だから、（a. 賢い　b. 勇ましい）人でも判断を誤ることがある。Ningen dakara, (a. kashikoi / b. isamashii) hito de mo handan o ayamaru koto ga aru.

Full sentence: 人間だから、賢い人でも判断を誤ることがある。Ningen dakara, kashikoi hito de mo handan o ayamaru koto ga aru.
EN: Because we're only human, even wise people sometimes misjudge things.
HI: इंसान हैं, इसलिए बुद्धिमान लोग भी कभी-कभी ग़लत फ़ैसला कर बैठते हैं।
GU: માણસ છીએ, એટલે હોશિયાર લોકો પણ ક્યારેક ખોટો નિર્ણય લઈ બેસે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a賢いkashikoiwise, cleverबुद्धिमानહોશિયાર
b勇ましいisamashiibrave (wrong quality for "misjudging")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 賢い人でも判断を誤る contrasts wisdom with a lapse in judgment; 勇ましい ("brave") describes courage, which has no logical contrast with misjudging.

Q3

（a. 敬語　b. 敬意）を正しく使うのはなかなか難しい。(a. Keigo / b. Keii) o tadashiku tsukau no wa nakanaka muzukashii.

Full sentence: 敬語を正しく使うのはなかなか難しい。Keigo o tadashiku tsukau no wa nakanaka muzukashii.
EN: Using honorific speech correctly is quite difficult.
HI: सम्मानसूचक भाषा को सही तरीक़े से इस्तेमाल करना काफ़ी मुश्किल है।
GU: માનવાચક ભાષાનો સાચો ઉપયોગ કરવો ખૂબ અઘરું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a敬語keigohonorific speech (something you "use")सम्मानसूचक भाषाમાનવાચક ભાષા
b敬意keiirespect, a feeling (not "used" like a language style)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 敬語 is a style of language you 使う ("use"); 敬意 is an abstract feeling of respect that you 表す ("show") or 抱く ("hold"), not "use."

Q4

大臣の発言を国民は（a. 批評　b. 批判）的に受けとめた。Daijin no hatsugen o kokumin wa (a. hihyou / b. hihan) teki ni uketometa.

Full sentence: 大臣の発言を国民は批判的に受けとめた。Daijin no hatsugen o kokumin wa hihanteki ni uketometa.
EN: The public received the minister's remarks critically.
HI: जनता ने मंत्री के बयान को आलोचनात्मक ढंग से लिया।
GU: જનતાએ મંત્રીના નિવેદનને ટીકાત્મક રીતે લીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a批評hihyoureview/commentary (more neutral/evaluative)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b批判hihancriticism (negative judgment)आलोचनाટીકા

Why: 批判的に受けとめる ("to receive critically/negatively") pairs with 批判, which carries a negative/disapproving edge; 批評 is closer to a neutral review or commentary.

練習 — p.133 (５日目 右)

Q1

その帽子、あなたによく（a. 似て　b. 似合って）いますよ。Sono boushi, anata ni yoku (a. nite / b. niatte) imasu yo.

Full sentence: その帽子、あなたによく似合っていますよ。Sono boushi, anata ni yoku niatte imasu yo.
EN: That hat suits you really well!
HI: वह टोपी आप पर बहुत अच्छी लग रही है!
GU: એ ટોપી તમને ખૂબ સરસ લાગે છે!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a似てniteresemble (wrong verb — a hat can't "resemble" a person)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b似合ってniattesuit, matchफबना, सूट करनाશોભવું

Q2

朝早く目が（a. 覚めた　b. 覚ました）のに、起きられなかった。Asa hayaku me ga (a. sameta / b. samashita) noni, okirarenakatta.

Full sentence: 朝早く目が覚めたのに、起きられなかった。Asa hayaku me ga sameta noni, okirarenakatta.
EN: Even though I woke up early in the morning, I couldn't get out of bed.
HI: सुबह जल्दी नींद खुल गई, फिर भी मैं उठ नहीं पाया।
GU: સવારે વહેલા આંખ ખૂલી ગઈ, છતાં હું ઊઠી શક્યો નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a覚めたsametawoke up (intransitive — 目が覚める)नींद खुलनाઆંખ ખૂલવી
b覚ましたsamashitawoke (something) up (transitive — needs an object, wrong here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 目が覚める is the fixed intransitive phrase for "to wake up" (the eyes are the subject); 覚ます is transitive ("to wake someone/something up") and would need a direct object, not 目が as the subject marker.

Q3

あの娘さんは有名な武士の（a. 祖先　b. 子孫）だそうです。Ano musume-san wa yuumei na bushi no (a. sosen / b. shison) da sou desu.

EN: I hear that young lady is a descendant of a famous samurai.
HI: सुना है वह लड़की एक प्रसिद्ध समुराई की वंशज है।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે તે યુવતી એક પ્રખ્યાત સામુરાઈની વંશજ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a祖先sosenancestor (backwards direction — she'd be older than the samurai)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

Full sentence: 犯人の顔や服装を覚えていますか。Hannin no kao ya fukusou o oboete imasu ka.
EN: Do you remember the culprit's face and clothing?
HI: क्या आपको अपराधी का चेहरा और कपड़े याद हैं?
GU: શું તમને ગુનેગારનો ચહેરો અને કપડાં યાદ છે?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today share a kanji but differ sharply in register or grammatical role — exactly what today's 練習 tests:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
批判hihan Criticism (negative judgment) Carries a disapproving, fault-finding edge. "The public reacted critically" (批判的に受けとめた).
批評hihyou A review/commentary Shares 批 but is closer to a neutral evaluative review (of art, writing, performance). A film or book review; art criticism as a genre.
賢明（な）kenmei (na) Wise, prudent A formal na-adjective/noun, often describing a decision or course of action. "A wise decision" (賢明な決断), formal writing.
賢いkashikoi Wise, clever, smart Same kanji, but the everyday i-adjective describing a person's intelligence. "A clever child," everyday conversation.
犯人hannin An offender, criminal (noun) The on-yomi reading — names the person who committed a crime. News reports, police statements.
犯すokasu To commit/perpetrate (verb) Same kanji, kun-yomi verb — describes the act itself, not the person. "To commit a crime" (罪を犯す).
覚めるsameru To wake up (intransitive) The waker is the grammatical subject — 目が覚める, no external agent. "I woke up early" (目が覚めた).
覚ますsamasu To wake (someone/something) up (transitive) Same kanji, but an agent acts on an object — needs a direct object. "The alarm clock woke me up" (目覚まし時計が私を覚ました).
Look-alike trap: 犯人 (hannin, "the offender") and the distractor 判人 in 練習 Q4 above look almost identical because 犯 and 判 are visually similar and both appear as this week's headline kanji. Only 犯 combines with 人 to form a real word; 判 combines with other characters (判断, 裁判, 評判) but never with 人 this way — a good reminder to check the whole compound, not just the general shape.
