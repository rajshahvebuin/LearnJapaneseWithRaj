練習 (p.124) — choose the correct option

Q1

猫がネズミを（a. 捕る　b. 捕まる）。Neko ga nezumi o (a. toru / b. tsukamaru).

Full sentence: 猫がネズミを捕る。Neko ga nezumi o toru.
EN: The cat catches the mouse.
HI: बिल्ली चूहे को पकड़ती है।
GU: બિલાડી ઉંદરને પકડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a捕るtoruto catch (transitive, takes を)पकड़नाપકડવું
b捕まるtsukamaruto be caught (intransitive, wrong for を)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 捕る is transitive "to catch" and takes を + object (ネズミを捕る); 捕まる is intransitive "to be caught," which cannot take a direct object.

Q2

相手は（a. 電話　b. 電波）の届かない所にいるか、電源を切っている。Aite wa (a. denwa / b. denpa) no todokanai tokoro ni iru ka, dengen o kitte iru.

Full sentence: 相手は電波の届かない所にいるか、電源を切っている。Aite wa denpa no todokanai tokoro ni iru ka, dengen o kitte iru.
EN: The other person is either somewhere the signal doesn't reach, or has turned the power off.
HI: दूसरा व्यक्ति या तो ऐसी जगह है जहाँ सिग्नल नहीं पहुँचता, या उसने पावर बंद कर रखी है।
GU: સામેની વ્યક્તિ કાં તો એવી જગ્યાએ છે જ્યાં સિગ્નલ પહોંચતું નથી, અથવા તેણે પાવર બંધ કરી દીધો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a電話denwaa telephone (the device, not a signal)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b電波denpaa radio wave/signalरेडियो तरंग, सिग्नलરેડિયો તરંગ, સિગ્નલ

Why: 電波が届く/届かない describes whether a signal reaches a location; 電話 is the device itself, which cannot be described as "reaching" a place.

Q3

盗みの（a. 疑い　b. 疑問）をかけられる。Nusumi no (a. utagai / b. gimon) o kakerareru.

Full sentence: 盗みの疑いをかけられる。Nusumi no utagai o kakerareru.
EN: He is suspected of theft.
HI: उस पर चोरी का शक किया जाता है।
GU: તેના પર ચોરીની શંકા કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a疑いutagaisuspicion (of wrongdoing)शक, संदेहશંકા
b疑問gimona question/doubt about facts (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 疑いをかけられる ("to be suspected") is the fixed phrase for criminal suspicion; 疑問 is an intellectual question/doubt about the truth of something, not an accusation.

Q4

店長は（a. 絶えず　b. 絶えて）客や店員に気を配らねばならない。Tenchou wa (a. taezu / b. taete) kyaku ya tenin ni ki o kubaraneba naranai.

Full sentence: 店長は絶えず客や店員に気を配らねばならない。Tenchou wa taezu kyaku ya tenin ni ki o kubaraneba naranai.
EN: The store manager must constantly pay attention to customers and staff.
HI: स्टोर मैनेजर को लगातार ग्राहकों और कर्मचारियों का ध्यान रखना चाहिए।
GU: સ્ટોર મેનેજરે સતત ગ્રાહકો અને સ્ટાફ પર ધ્યાન આપવું જોઈએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a絶えずtaezuconstantly, ceaselesslyलगातारસતત

Why: 絶えず is the everyday adverb "constantly"; 絶えて is an old-fashioned form normally paired with a negative verb to mean "not at all," which doesn't fit this affirmative sentence.

Today's kanji are unusually rich in multiple readings of the same character — 捕 alone has four. Compare the pairs that the 練習 questions above are really testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
捕るtoru To catch (fish/prey) Transitive, everyday verb for catching an animal or object. "The cat catches the mouse" (ネズミを捕る).
捕まるtsukamaru To be caught/arrested Intransitive — the subject itself gets caught, cannot take を. "The thief was caught" (泥棒が捕まった).
捕まえるtsukamaeru To catch/arrest (someone) Transitive, everyday verb — an agent actively catches a target. "The police caught the suspect" (容疑者を捕まえる).
逃げるnigeru To escape/flee (intransitive) The subject itself runs away — no external object. "The suspect fled" (容疑者が逃げる).
逃がすnigasu To release/let go (transitive) An agent deliberately lets something escape. "He released the fish" (魚を逃がす).
絶えずtaezu Constantly Everyday adverb describing an ongoing, uninterrupted state. "Must constantly pay attention" (絶えず気を配る).
絶つtatsu To cut off/sever A deliberate verb — someone actively severs a connection. "The boat had lost contact" (連絡を絶っていた).
疑いutagai Suspicion (of wrongdoing) Used specifically for criminal/moral suspicion. "Suspected of theft" (盗みの疑いをかけられる).
疑問gimon A question/doubt (about facts) An intellectual uncertainty about whether something is true. "I have doubts about this theory" (この説には疑問がある).
Reading trap: The five ❶-marked kun-yomi verbs today — 盗む・疑う・絶つ・補う・暴れる — are all everyday verb forms that sit right next to a more common on-yomi compound built from the same kanji (強盗・容疑・絶えず・補助・暴走). On a 漢字読み question, check whether the sentence needs the standalone verb or the noun compound before picking an answer.
