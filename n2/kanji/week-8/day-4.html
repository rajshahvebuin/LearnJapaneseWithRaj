2 Quiz / Exercise Section

Each printed page (左 p.130, 右 p.131) has its own 練習 exercise (4 items, "choose the correct option and mark ○"). Answers confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, PDF index 174, sections「4日目 左 (p.130)」and「4日目 右 (p.131)」.

練習 — p.130 (４日目 左)

Q1

国際（a. 親善　b. 改善）を目的とした試合が行われる。Kokusai (a. shinzen / b. kaizen) o mokuteki to shita shiai ga okonawareru.

Full sentence: 国際親善を目的とした試合が行われる。Kokusai shinzen o mokuteki to shita shiai ga okonawareru.
EN: A match aimed at international goodwill is held.
HI: अंतरराष्ट्रीय मैत्री को लक्ष्य बनाकर एक मैच आयोजित किया जाता है।
GU: આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય મૈત્રીના હેતુથી એક મેચ યોજાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a親善shinzengoodwill, friendshipमैत्री, सद्भावनाમૈત્રી, સદ્ભાવના
b改善kaizenimprovement (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 国際親善 ("international goodwill") is the fixed phrase for a friendly match between nations; 改善 means "improvement," which isn't something a match is held "for" in this sense.

Q2

多少（a. 条件　b. 尊重）が悪くても、雇ってほしいと思っている。Tashou (a. jouken / b. sonchou) ga warukute mo, yatotte hoshii to omotte iru.

HI: भले ही शर्तें थोड़ी ख़राब हों, फिर भी मैं चाहता हूँ कि वे मुझे नौकरी पर रखें।
GU: ભલે શરતો થોડી ખરાબ હોય, હું ઈચ્છું છું કે તેઓ મને નોકરીએ રાખે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a条件joukenconditions/termsशर्तेंશરતો
b尊重sonchourespect (can't be "poor")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 条件が悪い ("the conditions are poor") is the natural collocation before 雇ってほしい; 尊重 ("respect/esteem") isn't a quality that can be described as 悪い.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today share a kanji or a reading but pull in very different directions — exactly what today's 練習 questions test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
荒いarai Rough, wild (i-adjective) Describes an inherent rough/wild quality — a temperament, texture, or wave. "This horse is short-tempered" (気が荒い), "rough seas" (荒い波).
荒れるareru To become stormy/rough (intransitive verb) Same kanji, a state-change verb — land, weather, or skin becomes rough over time, no external agent. "The neglected land became overgrown" (畑が荒れる), "stormy weather" (天気が荒れる).
荒らすarasu To devastate/damage (transitive verb) Same kanji again, but an agent actively damages something else. "Animals damaged the field" (動物が畑を荒らす).
雇用koyou Employment (hiring) The positive/neutral half of the pair — bringing someone onto the payroll. "Employment conditions" (雇用条件), discussing job creation.
解雇kaiko Dismissal (firing) Shares 雇 but is the near-opposite outcome — removing someone from the payroll. "Unjust dismissal" (不当解雇), layoffs during a recession.
情勢jousei Situation, state of affairs A broad, often political/social situation — what a news report describes. "The wartime situation" (戦争の情勢), international affairs.
姿勢shisei Posture, attitude/stance Also built on 勢 but describes a person's physical posture or a figurative stance/attitude, not an external situation. "Sit up straight" (姿勢を正す), "a cooperative attitude" (協力的な姿勢).
双方souhou Both parties The on-yomi reading, used in formal/legal contexts for two negotiating sides. "Both parties agreed" (双方が合意した), diplomacy, contracts.
双子futago Twins Same kanji 双, but the kun-yomi reading used for the everyday family term. "Those twins" (あの双子), everyday conversation about siblings.
Reading trap: 景色 is marked ⊙ in this book — a special/irregular reading けしき that doesn't decompose into 景's regular on-yomi (ケイ) or kun-yomi at all, unlike its family members 風景 (ふうけい), 光景 (こうけい), and 景気 (けいき), which all use the expected ケイ reading. When you see 景色, just memorize it as a whole word rather than trying to sound it out from 景's other readings.
