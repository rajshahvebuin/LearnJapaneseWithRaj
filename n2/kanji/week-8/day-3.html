○軍の兵士死亡で捜査 (○ Gun no heishi shibou de sousa)

headline — p.129

「○軍の兵士死亡で捜査」"○ Gun no heishi shibou de sousa."
"Investigation launched after a soldier of the OO military dies." — headline compression: 「死亡で」stands in for a full clause like 死亡したことにより ("as a result of the death"), a terse causal で common only in headline style.

Q1

（a. 巨額　b. 巨大）魚がどこかの海岸で捕れたそうだ。(a. Kyogaku / b. Kyodai) gyo ga dokoka no kaigan de toreta sou da.

Full sentence: 巨大魚がどこかの海岸で捕れたそうだ。Kyodai-gyo ga dokoka no kaigan de toreta sou da.
EN: I heard a huge fish was caught on some beach.
HI: सुना है किसी समुद्र तट पर एक विशाल मछली पकड़ी गई।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે કોઈ દરિયાકિનારે એક વિશાળ માછલી પકડાઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a巨額kyogakua huge sum of money (describes money, not size)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b巨大（な）kyodai (na)huge/giganticविशालવિશાળ

Why: 巨大 is the adjective for physical size ("a huge fish"); 巨額 specifically describes large sums of money, not physical size.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

競・勝・逆・針 each spread across several readings in today's トピックス box and headlines — exactly what the 練習 questions above are testing. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
競争kyousou A competition/contest On-yomi キョウ, a general noun for rivalry/competing. "Beer sales competition" (販売競争).
❶競馬keiba Horse racing Same kanji 競, but read ケイ here — a completely different on-yomi tied to one fixed word. Only for "horse racing," never generalized.
勝つkatsu To win (kun-yomi verb) The everyday verb for winning an event. "Win the beer sales competition" (競争に勝つ).
勝負shoubu A match, a win-or-lose contest On-yomi noun naming the contest itself, used in the idiom 勝負がつく. "The match remains undecided" (勝負がつかない).
逆さ（に）sakasa (ni) Upside down/inverted Describes physical or visual orientation. "Hang it upside down."
逆らうsakarau To oppose/defy Same kanji 逆, but an action verb about resisting authority or a trend. "Defy one's parents' wishes."
方針houshin A policy/principle On-yomi シン, an abstract plan of action. "The PM won't change the basic policy" (基本方針変えない).
❶針hari A needle (kun-yomi) Same kanji 針, but the concrete physical object. A sewing needle, a clock's hand.
Headline-compression trap: Today's banners drop verbs and conjunctions freely — 「○軍の兵士死亡で捜査」uses a bare で to compress a causal clause (死亡したことにより → 死亡で), and 「首相「基本方針変えない」」quotes the PM directly with no attribution verb (と述べた is implied, never written). Recognizing this terse headline register — not just the vocabulary — is itself part of what this week trains.
