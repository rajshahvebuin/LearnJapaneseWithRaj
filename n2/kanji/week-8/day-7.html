1 問題1 — reading (10 items)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best reading for the underlined word from 1–4.

1

コンサート会場は大勢のファンで超満員になった。Konsaato kaijou wa oozei no fan de choumanin ni natta.

EN: The concert venue became jam-packed with a huge crowd of fans.
HI: कॉन्सर्ट स्थल प्रशंसकों की भारी भीड़ से खचाखच भर गया।
GU: કોન્સર્ટ સ્થળ ચાહકોની મોટી ભીડથી ખીચોખીચ ભરાઈ ગયું.
#Reading
1だいせい
2おおせい
3たいせい
4おおぜい (oozei)

Why: 大勢 (Day 4's 勢 group) is read おおぜい, "a great number of people."

2

母は典型的な古い日本の女性です。Haha wa tenkeiteki na furui nihon no josei desu.

EN: My mother is a typical old-fashioned Japanese woman.
HI: मेरी माँ एक पारंपरिक पुरानी जापानी महिला हैं।
GU: મારી માતા એક લાક્ષણિક જૂના જમાનાની જાપાની સ્ત્રી છે.
#Reading
1もはんてき
2りそうてき
3でんとうてき
4てんけいてき (tenkeiteki)

Why: 典型的 is read てんけいてき, "typical" — a cumulative-review item outside this week's own 14–15 kanji.

3

子供の意志も尊重すべきです。Kodomo no ishi mo sonchou subeki desu.

EN: A child's will should also be respected.
HI: बच्चे की इच्छा का भी सम्मान किया जाना चाहिए।
GU: બાળકની ઇચ્છાનું પણ સન્માન કરવું જોઈએ.
#Reading
1けいじゅう
2そんじゅう
3けいちょう
4そんちょう (sonchou)

Why: 尊重 (Day 4's 尊 group, kanji #697) is read そんちょう, "respect/esteem."

4

現代人は他人を敬う気持ちに欠けているのではないだろうか。Gendaijin wa tanin o uyamau kimochi ni kakete iru no de wa nai darou ka.

EN: Don't modern people lack the feeling of respecting others?
HI: क्या आधुनिक लोगों में दूसरों का सम्मान करने की भावना की कमी नहीं है?
GU: શું આધુનિક લોકોમાં બીજાનું સન્માન કરવાની ભાવનાની ઉણપ નથી?
#Reading
1さからう
2うやまう (uyamau)
3あしらう
4したがう

Why: 敬う (Day 5's 敬 group, kanji #713, marked ❶) is read うやまう, "to show respect."

5

補足説明をさせて頂きます。Hosoku setsumei o sasete itadakimasu.

EN: Let me give a supplementary explanation.
HI: मुझे एक पूरक स्पष्टीकरण देने दीजिए।
GU: મને એક પૂરક સ્પષ્ટતા આપવા દો.
#Reading
1ふそく
2ほそく (hosoku)
3ふぞく
4ほぞく

Why: 補足 is read ほそく, "supplementation" — a cumulative-review item from an earlier week's 補 kanji.

6

兄は県庁で職員として働いています。Ani wa kenchou de shokuin toshite hataraite imasu.

EN: My older brother works as a staff member at the prefectural office.
HI: मेरा बड़ा भाई प्रांतीय कार्यालय में कर्मचारी के रूप में काम करता है।
GU: મારો મોટો ભાઈ પ્રાંતીય કચેરીમાં કર્મચારી તરીકે કામ કરે છે.
#Reading
1けんちょう (kenchou)
2けんてい
3けんしょう
4けんしょ

Why: 県庁 (Day 6's 庁 group, kanji #725) is read けんちょう, "a prefectural office."

7

被告は無罪を主張している。Hikoku wa muzai o shuchou shite iru.

EN: The defendant claims innocence.
HI: प्रतिवादी निर्दोष होने का दावा कर रहा है।
GU: પ્રતિવાદી નિર્દોષ હોવાનો દાવો કરે છે.
#Reading
1はこく
2ひこく (hikoku)
3ふこく
4へこく

Why: 被告 is read ひこく, "the defendant" — a cumulative-review legal term, thematically close to Day 4's 律/法律 group.

8

犯した罪をつぐなってほしい。Okashita tsumi o tsugunatte hoshii.

EN: I want them to atone for the crime they committed.
HI: मैं चाहता हूँ कि वे अपने किए अपराध का प्रायश्चित करें।
GU: હું ઈચ્છું છું કે તેઓ પોતે કરેલા ગુનાનું પ્રાયશ્ચિત કરે.
#Reading
1ほんした
2おこした
3はんした
4おかした (okashita)

Why: 犯す (Day 5's 犯 group, kanji #716, marked ❶) is read おかす, so its past form is おかした, "committed."

9

環境問題について考える。Kankyou mondai ni tsuite kangaeru.

EN: Think about environmental issues.
HI: पर्यावरण संबंधी मुद्दों पर विचार करना।
GU: પર્યાવરણ સંબંધિત મુદ્દાઓ પર વિચાર કરવો.
#Reading
1けんきょう
2かんきょう (kankyou)
3かんきゅう
4けんきゅう

Why: 環境 (Day 6's 環 group, kanji #721) is read かんきょう, "environment."

10

各国の選手たちは技を競い合った。Kakkoku no senshu-tachi wa waza o kisoiatta.

EN: Athletes from each country competed with their skills.
HI: विभिन्न देशों के खिलाड़ियों ने अपने कौशल की प्रतिस्पर्धा की।
GU: વિવિધ દેશોના ખેલાડીઓએ પોતાની કૌશલ્યની સ્પર્ધા કરી.
#Reading
1きそい (kisoi)
2あらそい
3うばい
4うやまい

Why: 競う (from 競技, Day 6's 技 group, kanji #729) is read きそう, so 競い合った is きそいあった, "competed with each other."

2 問題2 — writing (5 items)

＿＿＿の言葉を漢字で書くとき、最もよいものを1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best kanji for the underlined reading.

11

会社の信頼を回復するようつとめる。Kaisha no shinrai o kaifuku suru you tsutomeru.

EN: [I] make efforts to restore the company's trust.
HI: कंपनी का भरोसा फिर से हासिल करने के लिए प्रयास करना।
GU: કંપનીનો વિશ્વાસ પુનઃસ્થાપિત કરવા પ્રયત્ન કરવો.
#Kanji
1務める
2勤める
3努めるtsutomeru
4勉める

Why: 努める (Day 6's 努 group, kanji #724, marked ❶) means "to make efforts/try hard"; 務める (to serve in a role), 勤める (to be employed at), and 勉める (not a standard word) are look-alike distractors with different meanings.

12

日本チームは決勝でやぶれた。Nihon chiimu wa kesshou de yabureta.

EN: The Japan team was defeated in the final.
HI: जापान की टीम फ़ाइनल में हार गई।
GU: જાપાનની ટીમ ફાઇનલમાં હારી ગઈ.
#Kanji
1敗れたyabureta
2破れた
3折れた
4倒れた

Why: 敗れる ("to be defeated," of a team/side in competition) is the correct word; 破れる (to tear/be torn, of paper/cloth) is a homophone-adjacent trap with a completely different meaning, while 折れる ("to break/snap") and 倒れる ("to collapse/fall over") don't fit a match result.

13

湯かげんはいかがですか。Yu kagen wa ikaga desu ka.

EN: How is the temperature of the bath water?
HI: पानी का तापमान कैसा है?
GU: પાણીનું તાપમાન કેવું છે?
#Kanji
1増減
2加減kagen
3削減
4過減

Why: 湯かげん (Day 6's 減 group, kanji #723) means "the temperature/degree of the bath water" — 加減 is the word for a fine-tuned degree/extent; 増減, 削減, and the invented 過減 don't fit this idiom.

14

不況のため社員の半数がかいこされた。Fukyou no tame shain no hansuu ga kaiko sareta.

EN: Due to the recession, half the employees were laid off.
HI: मंदी के कारण आधे कर्मचारियों की छँटनी कर दी गई।
GU: મંદીના કારણે અડધા કર્મચારીઓની છટણી કરવામાં આવી.
#Kanji
1解子
2解放
3解雇kaiko
4解戸

Why: 解雇 (Day 4's 雇 group, kanji #698) means "a dismissal/layoff"; 解放 ("release/liberation") is a real word with a different meaning, and 解子/解戸 are invented non-words.

15

奥さんの料理のうでまえはすごいですね。Okusan no ryouri no udemae wa sugoi desu ne.

EN: Your wife's cooking skill is amazing, isn't it.
HI: आपकी पत्नी की खाना बनाने की कला अद्भुत है, है ना।
GU: તમારી પત્નીની રસોઈ કળા અદ્ભુત છે, ખરું ને.
#Kanji
1腕毎
2腕力
3腕米
4腕前udemae

Why: 腕前 (Day 6's 腕 group, kanji #732) means "skill/ability" (especially a craft like cooking); 腕力 means "physical strength" (a real word, wrong meaning here), while 腕毎/腕米 are invented non-words.

3 問題3 — fill the blank (6 items)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4.

16

新（　）インフルエンザが流行している。Shin(___) infuruenza ga ryuukou shite iru.

Full sentence: 新型インフルエンザが流行している。Shingata infuruenza ga ryuukou shite iru.
EN: A new strain of influenza is spreading.
HI: इन्फ़्लूएंज़ा का एक नया प्रकार फैल रहा है।
GU: ઈન્ફ્લુએન્ઝાનો એક નવો પ્રકાર ફેલાઈ રહ્યો છે.
#Kanji
1米
2型gata → shingata
3兵
4星

Why: 新型 ("a new type/strain") is the fixed word used for new virus strains.

17

外国人には選挙（　）がありますか。Gaikokujin ni wa senkyo(___) ga arimasu ka.

Full sentence: 外国人には選挙権がありますか。Gaikokujin ni wa senkyoken ga arimasu ka.
EN: Do foreigners have the right to vote?
HI: क्या विदेशियों को मतदान का अधिकार है?
GU: શું વિદેશીઓને મતદાનનો અધિકાર છે?
#Kanji
1件
2令
3条
4権ken → senkyoken

Why: 選挙権 ("the right to vote") is the fixed word for suffrage; note option 3 条 is Day 4's own kanji (#699, from 条件), a plausible-looking but wrong distractor here.

18

大雨のため、各地で水（　）が起きている。Ooame no tame, kakuchi de mizu(___) ga okite iru.

Full sentence: 大雨のため、各地で水害が起きている。Ooame no tame, kakuchi de suigai ga okite iru.
EN: Due to heavy rain, flood damage is occurring in various places.
HI: भारी बारिश के कारण कई जगहों पर बाढ़ से नुक़सान हो रहा है।
GU: ભારે વરસાદને કારણે અનેક સ્થળોએ પૂરથી નુકસાન થઈ રહ્યું છે.
#Kanji
1害gai → suigai
2絶
3爆
4暴

Why: 水害 ("flood damage") is the fixed word for water-related disaster damage.

19

米国のカリフォルニア（　）から来ました。Beikoku no Kariforunia(___) kara kimashita.

Full sentence: 米国のカリフォルニア州から来ました。Beikoku no Kariforunia-shuu kara kimashita.
EN: I came from the State of California, USA.
HI: मैं अमेरिका के कैलिफ़ोर्निया राज्य से आया/आई हूँ।
GU: હું અમેરિકાના કેલિફોર્નિયા રાજ્યમાંથી આવ્યો/આવી છું.
#Kanji
1週
2集
3州shuu → Kariforunia-shuu
4宗

Why: 州 ("state/province") is the counter used for US states; option 1 週 ("week") is a pure homophone trap (both read しゅう).

20

野（　）から首相の発言に対して批判の声が上がった。Ya(___) kara shushou no hatsugen ni taishite hihan no koe ga agatta.

GU: વડાપ્રધાનના નિવેદન સામે વિપક્ષ તરફથી ટીકાના અવાજો ઊઠ્યા.
#Kanji
1党tou → yatou
2軍
3童
4覚

Why: 野党 ("the opposition party") is the fixed political term; note option 4 覚 is Day 5's own kanji (#719), a look-alike distractor unrelated in meaning here.

21

4 問題4 — passage fill-in (4 items)

次の（　）に入る言葉を下から選び、漢字で書きなさい（同じ言葉を2回使ってもよい）。Choose the right word from the bank for each blank and write it in kanji (the same word may be used twice). Bank: しゅしょう・ちょうかん・だいじん・せいふ

22–25

会社の長は社長、学校の長は校長、大学の長は学長で、22（　）の長は総理23（　）です。厚生労働省、文部科学省、環境省など各省の長も24（　）ですが、気象庁、警察庁など庁の場合は25（　）と呼ばれます。Kaisha no chou wa shachou, gakkou no chou wa kouchou, daigaku no chou wa gakuchou de, (22) no chou wa souri (23) desu. Kousei Roudou-shou, Monbu Kagaku-shou, Kankyou-shou nado kaku-shou no chou mo (24) desu ga, Kishou-chou, Keisatsu-chou nado chou no baai wa (25) to yobaremasu.

Full passage: 会社の長は社長、学校の長は校長、大学の長は学長で、政府の長は総理大臣です。厚生労働省、文部科学省、環境省など各省の長も大臣ですが、気象庁、警察庁など庁の場合は長官と呼ばれます。Kaisha no chou wa shachou, gakkou no chou wa kouchou, daigaku no chou wa gakuchou de, seifu no chou wa souri daijin desu. Kousei Roudou-shou, Monbu Kagaku-shou, Kankyou-shou nado kaku-shou no chou mo daijin desu ga, Kishou-chou, Keisatsu-chou nado chou no baai wa choukan to yobaremasu.
EN: The head of a company is a 社長 (company president), the head of a school is a 校長 (principal), and the head of a university is a 学長 (university president), while the head of the government is the 総理大臣 (Prime Minister). The heads of ministries (省) like the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Education, and the Ministry of the Environment are also called 大臣 (ministers), but for agencies (庁) like the Meteorological Agency or the National Police Agency, the head is called 長官 (director-general).
HI: कंपनी का प्रमुख 社長 (कंपनी अध्यक्ष) होता है, स्कूल का प्रमुख 校長 (प्रधानाचार्य), विश्वविद्यालय का प्रमुख 学長 (कुलपति), जबकि सरकार का प्रमुख 総理大臣 (प्रधानमंत्री) होता है। स्वास्थ्य-श्रम-कल्याण मंत्रालय, शिक्षा मंत्रालय, पर्यावरण मंत्रालय जैसे प्रत्येक 省 (मंत्रालय) का प्रमुख भी 大臣 (मंत्री) कहलाता है, लेकिन मौसम विभाग, राष्ट्रीय पुलिस एजेंसी जैसी 庁 (एजेंसियों) के प्रमुख को 長官 (महानिदेशक) कहा जाता है।

6 Week 8's Closing Column

[コラム] 上回る・下回る (Uwamawaru, Shitamawaru — "to exceed / to fall below") — printed p.138

The book's final コラム teaches graph- and comparison-reading vocabulary — exactly the skill needed to read the statistical charts that accompany real news articles. It introduces 7 more kanji (book-wide #733–739): 昇shou / noboru (rise: 上昇 joushou, "ascending"), 幅haba (width/degree: 大幅（な） oohaba (na), "substantial/large-scale"), 著chi・arawasu・ichijirushii (❶著しい ichijirushii, "remarkable/considerable"; 著者 chosha, "an author"), 占shimeru・uranau (占める shimeru, "to occupy"; ❶占う uranau, "to tell fortunes"), 比hi・kuraberu (比較 hikaku, "a comparison"), 較kaku (比較的 hikakuteki, "comparative"), and 並nami・naraberu・narabu (平年並み heinen-nami, "an average year"; 並木 namiki, "a row of trees").

「読めるかな？」("Can you read this?") graph exercise — six phrases for describing a line graph's movement:

  1. 増加する／上昇する (zouka suru / joushou suru) — to increase / to rise
  2. 横ばい／一定 (yokobai / ittei) — flat / constant
  3. 大幅に上昇する (oohaba ni joushou suru) — to rise sharply/substantially
  4. 減少する／下降する (genshou suru / kakou suru) — to decrease / to fall
  5. 著しく減少する／下降する (ichijirushiku genshou suru / kakou suru) — to decrease/fall remarkably
  6. AはBを上回る。BはAを下回る。Aが1位を占める。(A wa B o uwamawaru. B wa A o shitamawaru. A ga ichi-i o shimeru.) — A exceeds B. B falls below A. A occupies first place.

A second table exercise (表, printed p.138) applies the same idea to a data table comparing 前年比較 (year-over-year) and 平年比較 (vs. average-year) figures across three districts, using 平年並み ("about average"), やや長い／短い ("a bit longer/shorter"), and やや多い／少ない ("a bit more/fewer") — the same 並 kanji from the vocabulary list above, applied to real comparative data exactly like Day 6's population graph in 練習 Q3.
