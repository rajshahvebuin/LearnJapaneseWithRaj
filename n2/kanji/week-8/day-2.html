OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: Your country and Japan have been trading with each other since long ago.
HI: आपका देश और जापान बहुत पहले से व्यापार करते आ रहे हैं।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

易 and 害 each spawn several compounds with subtly different readings/nuances today, and 命／令 are a classic "looks related, isn't" pair. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
貿易boueki Trade 易 read エキ here — an on-yomi compound for international trade. "Japan and Thailand trade" (貿易をする).
容易（な）youi (na) Easy, simple 易 read イ here, a different on-yomi from 貿易's エキ — same kanji, different sound. Describing a task as simple/uncomplicated.
易しいyasashii Easy, simple (kun-yomi) The everyday adjective form — a homophone trap with 優しい (kind). "An easy problem" (易しい問題).
被害higai Damage (suffered) General word for harm/damage a victim suffers. "Flood damage occurred" (被害が出ている).
公害kougai Pollution Specifically industrial/environmental pollution, not any harm. "Factory pollution," "noise pollution."
命（いのち）inochi Life Kun-yomi, a standalone noun for a living being's life. "Treasure your life" (命は大切に).
命令meirei An order/command 命 here paired with 令 as an on-yomi compound — nothing to do with "life." "The bank was ordered to refund" (払い戻し命令).
Headline-compression trap: 厚労省 (from 厚生労働省) shows how real headlines routinely truncate long government/organization names — expect to mentally expand such abbreviations back to their full reading (こうせいろうどうしょう) even though the printed headline never spells it out. Watch too for 体言止め (headlines ending on a bare noun with no verb, e.g. 「売上高4兆円」, 「農業分野で合意」) — a hallmark of Japanese newspaper style not used in ordinary sentences.
