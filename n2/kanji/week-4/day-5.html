2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (4 items each, from p.68 and p.69). Answers confirmed from the [別冊] booklet, PDF index 167 (booklet p.7).

練習 (p.68) — choose the correct option

Q1

その小説の最初の（a. 章　b. 部）を読み終えたところです。Sono shousetsu no saisho no (a. shou / b. bu) o yomiowatta tokoro desu.

Full sentence: その小説の最初の章を読み終えたところです。Sono shousetsu no saisho no shou o yomiowatta tokoro desu.
EN: I have just finished reading the first chapter of that novel.
HI: मैंने अभी उस उपन्यास का पहला अध्याय पढ़ना ख़त्म किया है।
GU: મેં હમણાં જ તે નવલકથાનો પહેલો પ્રકરણ વાંચવાનું પૂરું કર્યું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a章shoua chapterअध्यायપ્રકરણ
b部bua department/section — wrong sense(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 章 specifically means "a chapter" in a book; 部 refers to a department/section in an organizational sense, not a chapter of a novel.

Q2

健康のために（a. 準備　b. 適度）な運動をするようにしています。Kenkou no tame ni (a. junbi / b. tekido) na undou o suru you ni shite imasu.

Full sentence: 健康のために適度な運動をするようにしています。Kenkou no tame ni tekido na undou o suru you ni shite imasu.
EN: I try to do a moderate amount of exercise for my health.
HI: स्वास्थ्य के लिए मैं उचित मात्रा में व्यायाम करने की कोशिश करता/करती हूँ।
GU: સ્વાસ્થ્ય માટે હું યોગ્ય માત્રામાં કસરત કરવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a準備junbipreparation — wrong meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b適度tekidomoderateसंतुलित मात्रा मेंમધ્યમ માત્રામાં

Why: 適度な運動 ("a moderate amount of exercise") is the set phrase; 準備 means "preparation," which doesn't describe exercise intensity.

Q3

明日行くかどうかは天気（a. 次第　b. 次第に）です。Ashita iku ka dou ka wa tenki (a. shidai / b. shidai ni) desu.

Full sentence: 明日行くかどうかは天気次第です。Ashita iku ka dou ka wa tenki shidai desu.
EN: Whether I go tomorrow or not depends on the weather.
HI: कल जाना है या नहीं यह मौसम पर निर्भर है।
GU: કાલે જવું કે નહીં તે હવામાન પર આધાર રાખે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a次第shidaidepends on (noun + だ)...पर निर्भर है...પર આધાર રાખે છે
b次第にshidai nigradually (adverb) — wrong form(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 天気次第だ ("it depends on the weather") uses 次第 as a noun meaning "depending on"; 次第に is an adverb meaning "gradually," which can't take だ this way.

Q4

Full sentence: 直ちに安全な場所に移動してください。Tadachi ni anzen na basho ni idou shite kudasai.
EN: Please move to a safe place immediately.
HI: कृपया तुरंत सुरक्षित जगह पर चले जाएँ।
GU: કૃપા કરી તાત્કાલિક સુરક્ષિત સ્થળે જાઓ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a正直にshoujiki nihonestly — wrong meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b直ちにtadachi niimmediatelyतुरंतતાત્કાલિક

Why: 直ちに means "immediately," fitting an urgent evacuation instruction; 正直に means "honestly," unrelated despite sharing the kanji 直.

EN: The surrounding residents are opposed to the construction of the apartment building.
HI: आसपास के निवासी अपार्टमेंट के निर्माण का विरोध कर रहे हैं।
GU: આસપાસના રહેવાસીઓ એપાર્ટમેન્ટના બાંધકામનો વિરોધ કરી રહ્યા છે.

Q6

Full sentence: 形式も重要だが、内容はもっと重要だ。Keishiki mo juuyou da ga, naiyou wa motto juuyou da.
EN: Form is important too, but content is even more important.
HI: प्रारूप भी महत्वपूर्ण है, लेकिन विषय-वस्तु और भी महत्वपूर्ण है।
GU: સ્વરૂપ પણ મહત્વનું છે, પરંતુ સામગ્રી વધુ મહત્વની છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a形式keishikiform/formalityप्रारूपસ્વરૂપ

Q7

副社長がよく働いてくれるので、社長は（a. 助けて　b. 助かって）いる。Fukushachou ga yoku hataraite kureru node, shachou wa (a. tasukete / b. tasukatte) iru.

Full sentence: 副社長がよく働いてくれるので、社長は助かっている。Fukushachou ga yoku hataraite kureru node, shachou wa tasukatte iru.
EN: Because the vice president works so hard, the president is being helped a great deal.
HI: उपाध्यक्ष बहुत मेहनत करता है, इसलिए अध्यक्ष को बड़ी मदद मिल रही है।
GU: ઉપપ્રમુખ ખૂબ મહેનત કરે છે, તેથી પ્રમુખને ઘણી મદદ મળી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a助けてtasuketehelping (someone else) — wrong voice(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b助かってtasukattebeing helped/relievedमदद मिलनाમદદ મળવી

Why: 助かる is intransitive, "to be helped/relieved" (the president is the beneficiary); 助ける is transitive, "to help someone," which would need the president as the one doing the helping.

Q8

EN: If you compare her to a flower, she is a white lily.
HI: अगर उसकी तुलना किसी फूल से करें, तो वह एक सफ़ेद लिली है।
GU: જો તેની સરખામણી કોઈ ફૂલ સાથે કરીએ, તો તે એક સફેદ કમળ (લિલી) છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji include several near-synonym pairs the book itself notes side by side — exactly what 練習 Q1–Q8 above are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
適当(な)tekitou Appropriate / reasonable Can also drift toward "random/careless" in casual speech. General suitability, or (casually) "whatever works."
適切(な)tekisetsu Appropriate / proper Always precise and formal — never means "careless." Formal writing: "an appropriate measure/response."
誤解gokai A misunderstanding Names the event of misreading someone's meaning/intent. "There's been a misunderstanding between us."
誤りayamari A mistake, an error A general factual or textual error, not about intent. "Correct the error in the underlined part" (誤りを直す).
正直(な)shoujiki Honest Describes truthfulness in speech/character. "He is an honest person."
素直(な)sunao Obedient, unaffected, docile Describes a compliant, unresisting temperament — not about telling the truth. "A child who listens obediently."
次第だshidai da Depends on ~ Noun usage: "X次第だ" = "it depends on X." "Whether we go depends on the weather" (天気次第だ).
次第にshidai ni Gradually Adverb usage describing a slow change over time. "It gradually got colder" (次第に寒くなった).
❶ reading traps: 次第 itself carries two senses the book marks with ①② — "as soon as" (〜次第、すぐに) and "depending on the circumstances" (〜によって) — don't confuse this with the adverb 次第に above. 人形(にんぎょう) is an irregular-sounding compound reading for "doll," not じん+けい as the individual on-yomi readings might suggest. 最も(もっとも) is a kun-yomi reading distinct from the サイ on-yomi seen in 最初・最近・最後.
