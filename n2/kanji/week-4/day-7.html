Notes
Treat this as a timed drill: try all 25 questions first (15 minutes), then check against the reasoning below. Many items reuse kanji directly from Days 4–6 (打ち合わせ, 素直, 争い, 恥, 感心, 諸問題, 憎らしい...).
⊙ = an unusual/irregular reading (特別な読み)

1 問題1 — reading (10 items)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。＿＿＿ no kotoba no yomikata to shite mottomo yoi mono o, 1-2-3-4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. Choose the best reading for the underlined word from 1–4.

1

自分は間違っていない、と彼は主張した。Jibun wa machigatte inai, to kare wa shuchou shita.

EN: He insisted that he was not mistaken.
HI: उसने ज़ोर देकर कहा कि वह ग़लत नहीं है।
GU: તેણે ભારપૂર્વક કહ્યું કે તે ખોટો નથી.
#Reading
1ちゅしょう
2しゅっちょう
3じゅちょう
4しゅちょう (shuchou)

Why: 主張 is read しゅちょう, "to insist/assert."

2

デパートはお歳暮の買い物をする人たちで込んでいた。Depaato wa o-seibo no kaimono o suru hitotachi de konde ita.

EN: The department store was crowded with people shopping for year-end gifts.
HI: डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर साल के अंत के उपहार ख़रीदने वाले लोगों से भरा हुआ था।
GU: ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર વર્ષના અંતની ભેટ ખરીદનારા લોકોથી ભરેલો હતો.
#Reading
1おさいぼ
2おせいぼ (o-seibo)
3おとしくれ
4おとしだま

Why: お歳暮 ("a year-end gift") is read おせいぼ.

3

本日はお招き頂きまして、ありがとうございます。Honjitsu wa o-maneki itadakimashite, arigatou gozaimasu.

EN: Thank you for inviting me today.
HI: आज मुझे आमंत्रित करने के लिए धन्यवाद।
GU: આજે મને આમંત્રણ આપવા બદલ આભાર.
#Reading
1おなねき
2おまねき (o-maneki)
3おなめき
4おまめき

Why: 招き ("invitation") is read まねき, so お招き = おまねき.

4

信用を失ってはビジネスはできない。Shin'you o ushinatte wa bijinesu wa dekinai.

EN: You can't do business if you lose people's trust.
HI: अगर विश्वास खो दिया तो व्यापार नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: જો વિશ્વાસ ગુમાવો તો વ્યવસાય કરી શકાય નહીં.
#Reading
1うしなって (ushinatte)
2やしなって
3あしらって
4おぎなって

Why: 失って is うしなって, "having lost" — from Day 4's own 失 kanji.

5

#Reading
1めぐまれた
2つつまれた
3かこまれた (kakomareta)
4いとなまれた

Why: 囲まれた is かこまれた, "surrounded" — from Day 5's own 囲 kanji.

6

7

3そっちょく
4すなお (sunao)

9

2たもって

2 問題2 — writing (5 items)

＿＿＿の言葉を漢字で書くとき、最もよいものを1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。＿＿＿ no kotoba o kanji de kaku toki, mottomo yoi mono o 1-2-3-4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. Choose the best kanji for the underlined reading.

11

国によって文化やしゅうかんは異なる。Kuni ni yotte bunka ya shuukan wa kotonaru.

EN: Culture and customs differ from country to country.
HI: संस्कृति और रीति-रिवाज़ देश-देश में अलग होते हैं।
GU: સંસ્કૃતિ અને રિવાજો દેશ-દેશમાં અલગ હોય છે.
#Kanji
1週間
2習慣shuukan
3主観
4週刊

Why: しゅうかん meaning "custom/habit" is written 習慣; 週間 (a week), 主観 (subjectivity), and 週刊 (a weekly publication) are unrelated same-sound distractors.

12

うちあわせは2時からです。Uchiawase wa niji kara desu.

EN: The meeting starts at 2 o'clock.
HI: बैठक/चर्चा 2 बजे से शुरू होगी।
GU: બેઠક/ચર્ચા 2 વાગ્યાથી શરૂ થશે.
#Kanji
1打ち合わせuchiawase
2待ち合わせ
3持ち合わせ
4落ち合わせ

Why: 打ち合わせ ("a prior arrangement/meeting") is Day 4's own 打 kanji; 待ち合わせ ("meeting up at a place"), 持ち合わせ ("what one has on hand"), and 落ち合わせ (not a real word) are look-alikes.

13

明日はさいじつでお休みだ。Ashita wa saijitsu de o-yasumi da.

EN: Tomorrow is a festival holiday, so it's a day off.
HI: कल त्यौहार का दिन है, इसलिए छुट्टी है।
GU: કાલે તહેવારનો દિવસ છે, તેથી રજા છે.
#Kanji
1祝日
2察日
3際日
4祭日saijitsu

Why: さいじつ ("festival day/holiday") is written 祭日 — Day 6's own 祭 kanji; 祝日 is a real word for "national holiday" but read しゅくじつ, not さいじつ; 察日 and 際日 aren't real words.

14

こちらに勤務先とぶしょ名をお書きください。Kochira ni kinmusaki to busho mei o o-kaki kudasai.

EN: Please write your workplace and department name here.
HI: कृपया यहाँ अपना कार्यस्थल और विभाग का नाम लिखें।
GU: કૃપા કરી અહીં તમારું કાર્યસ્થળ અને વિભાગનું નામ લખો.
#Kanji
1部暑
2部所
3部署busho
4部諸

Why: ぶしょ ("department") is written 部署; 部暑, 部所, and 部諸 are near-identical shapes that are not the correct word.

15

幼い子にきちんとあいさつをされてかんしんした。Osanai ko ni kichinto aisatsu o sarete kanshin shita.

EN: I was impressed that the young child greeted me so properly.
HI: छोटे बच्चे ने ठीक से अभिवादन किया तो मैं प्रभावित हुआ।
GU: નાના બાળકે યોગ્ય રીતે અભિવાદન કર્યું તેથી હું પ્રભાવિત થયો.
#Kanji
1喜心
2関心
3寒心
4感心kanshin

Why: かんしん meaning "to be impressed/admire" is written 感心 — Day 6's own 感 kanji; 関心 (interest, also read かんしん) is the classic same-sound trap but means "curiosity," not "being impressed"; 喜心 and 寒心 aren't standard words here.

3 問題3 — fill the blank (7 items)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。(___) ni ireru no ni mottomo yoi mono o, 1-2-3-4 kara hitotsu erabinasai. Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4.

16

この試験に受かった人は二（　）試験が受けられる。Kono shiken ni ukatta hito wa ni-(___) shiken ga ukerareru.

#Kanji
1期
2次ji → niji
3部
4助

Why: 二次試験 ("second-round exam") uses 次 — Day 5's own 次 kanji.

兄は消防（　）に勤めています。Ani wa shoubou(___) ni tsutomete imasu.

19

友達の石田（　）が遊びに来た。Tomodachi no Ishida(___) ga asobi ni kita.

Full sentence: 友達の石田君が遊びに来た。Tomodachi no Ishida-kun ga asobi ni kita.
EN: My friend Ishida came over to visit.
HI: मेरा दोस्त इशिदा मिलने आया।
GU: મારો મિત્ર ઇશિદા મળવા આવ્યો.
#Kanji
1殿
2様
3君kun → Ishida-kun
4奥

Why: 君(くん) is the casual honorific used for a friend/peer; 様 and 殿 are too formal for a friend, and 奥 isn't an honorific at all.

4 問題4 — passage fill-in (3 items)

次の（　）に入る言葉を下から選び、漢字で書きなさい。Tsugi no (___) ni hairu kotoba o shita kara erabi, kanji de kakinasai. Choose the right word from the bank for each blank and write it in kanji. Bank: なさけない・にくらしい・たのもしい・こいしい・あさましい

23–25

兄はいつも私をからかうので23（　）と思うこともある。でも、困ったとき、いつも助けてくれる24（　）人だ。もう半年も会っていないので、兄が25（　）。Ani wa itsumo watashi o karakau node (23) to omou koto mo aru. Demo, komatta toki, itsumo tasukete kureru (24) hito da. Mou hantoshi mo atte inai node, ani ga (25).

Full passage: 兄はいつも私をからかうので憎らしいと思うこともある。でも、困ったとき、いつも助けてくれる頼もしい人だ。もう半年も会っていないので、兄が恋しい。Ani wa itsumo watashi o karakau node nikurashii to omou koto mo aru. Demo, komatta toki, itsumo tasukete kureru tanomoshii hito da. Mou hantoshi mo atte inai node, ani ga koishii.
EN: My older brother always teases me, so sometimes I think he's annoying. But he's a reliable person who always helps me when I'm in trouble. I haven't seen him in half a year now, so I miss him.
HI: भाई हमेशा मुझे चिढ़ाता है, इसलिए कभी-कभी वह नफ़रत के क़ाबिल लगता है। लेकिन मुश्किल में हमेशा मदद करने वाला भरोसेमंद इंसान है। आधे साल से मिला नहीं, इसलिए भाई की याद आती है।
GU: ભાઈ હંમેશા મને ચીડવે છે, તેથી ક્યારેક તે અણગમતો લાગે છે. પરંતુ મુશ્કેલીમાં હંમેશા મદદ કરનારો ભરોસાપાત્ર માણસ છે. અડધા વર્ષથી મળ્યો નથી, તેથી ભાઈની યાદ આવે છે.

5 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This test is itself built on confusion pairs — the wrong options in 問題2/3 above are deliberate look-alikes or sound-alikes. A few worth isolating:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
感心(な)kanshin Admirable, impressed A reaction of admiration toward someone's behavior. "I was impressed by the child's manners."
関心kanshin Interest, concern Same reading, totally different meaning — curiosity/concern about a topic, not admiration. "I have an interest in politics" (政治に関心がある).
頼るtayoru To rely on (verb) Describes the act of depending on someone. "I rely on my wife" (妻に頼っている).
頼もしいtanomoshii Reliable (adjective) Describes the person who can be relied on — built from the same root as 頼る. "He is a reliable person" (頼もしい人).
憎らしいnikurashii Hateful, annoying Often used semi-affectionately about a mildly irritating but loved person. A sibling who teases you but whom you still care about.
恋しいkoishii Missed, longed for Not limited to romance — can describe missing family or home. "I miss my brother" (兄が恋しい), "I miss home" (故郷が恋しい).
Exam trap: 感心 and 関心 are a classic same-reading (かんしん), same-general-topic (a mental/emotional response) pair with unmistakably different meanings — 問題2 Q15 tests exactly this. Whenever かんしん appears, check whether the sentence describes being impressed by something (→感心) or having curiosity/concern about a topic (→関心) before choosing the kanji.
