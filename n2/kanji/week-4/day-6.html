作文「私の夢」② (Sakubun "Watashi no yume" — "My Dream", part 2)

p.71 — kanji #349–358

人間と人間が互いに憎しみ合ったり殺し合ったりするのは悲しく恥ずかしいことだと感じています。Ningen to ningen ga tagai ni nikushimiattari koroshiattari suru no wa kanashiku hazukashii koto da to kanjite imasu.
地球は一つ。Chikyuu wa hitotsu. 私の願いも一つ、それは平和です。Watashi no negai mo hitotsu, sore wa heiwa desu.
世界中の人々が家族のように愛し合い、仲良くすること、それが私の夢です。Sekaijuu no hitobito ga kazoku no you ni aishiai, nakayoku suru koto, sore ga watashi no yume desu.
("I feel that it is sad and shameful for humans to hate and kill one another. The earth is one. My wish is also one: peace. That people all over the world love one another like family and get along — that is my dream.")

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (6 items, printed at the bottom of p.71, covering kanji from both p.70 and p.71). Answers confirmed from the [別冊] booklet, PDF index 167 (booklet p.7).

Q1

弟は野球に（a. 夢中　b. 感動）で、将来はプロの選手になりたいと言っている。Otouto wa yakyuu ni (a. muchuu / b. kandou) de, shourai wa puro no senshu ni naritai to itte iru.

Full sentence: 弟は野球に夢中で、将来はプロの選手になりたいと言っている。Otouto wa yakyuu ni muchuu de, shourai wa puro no senshu ni naritai to itte iru.
EN: My younger brother is absorbed in baseball, and says he wants to become a professional player in the future.
HI: मेरा छोटा भाई बेसबॉल में मगन है, और कहता है कि भविष्य में पेशेवर खिलाड़ी बनना चाहता है।
GU: મારો નાનો ભાઈ બેઝબોલમાં મગ્ન છે, અને કહે છે કે ભવિષ્યમાં વ્યાવસાયિક ખેલાડી બનવા માંગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a夢中muchuuabsorbed/engrossedमगन, तल्लीनમગ્ન
b感動kandoubeing moved emotionally — wrong sense(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 〜に夢中だ means "to be absorbed/obsessed with something"; 感動する means "to be emotionally moved," describing a reaction, not an ongoing passion.

Q2

大学では数学を（a. 専攻　b. 専門）した。Daigaku de wa suugaku o (a. senkou / b. senmon) shita.

Full sentence: 大学では数学を専攻した。Daigaku de wa suugaku o senkou shita.
EN: I majored in mathematics at university.
HI: विश्वविद्यालय में मैंने गणित को मुख्य विषय के रूप में पढ़ा।
GU: યુનિવર્સિટીમાં મેં ગણિતનો મુખ્ય વિષય તરીકે અભ્યાસ કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a専攻senkouto major inमुख्य विषय के रूप में पढ़नाમુખ્ય વિષય તરીકે અભ્યાસ કરવો
b専門senmona specialty (noun) — wrong usage(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 専攻する is the verb "to major in [a subject]"; 専門 is a noun meaning "one's field/specialty," normally used as 専門は〜だ rather than 〜を専門した.

Q3

この建物のトイレは洋式と（a. 和式　b. 正式）のほかに車いす用のもある。Kono tatemono no toire wa youshiki to (a. washiki / b. seishiki) no hoka ni kuruma isu you no mo aru.

Full sentence: この建物のトイレは洋式と和式のほかに車いす用のもある。Kono tatemono no toire wa youshiki to washiki no hoka ni kuruma isu you no mo aru.

HI: इस इमारत के शौचालयों में पश्चिमी और जापानी शैली के अलावा व्हीलचेयर के लिए एक भी है।
GU: આ ઇમારતના શૌચાલયોમાં પશ્ચિમી અને જાપાની શૈલી ઉપરાંત વ્હીલચેર માટેનું પણ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a和式washikiJapanese styleजापानी शैलीજાપાની શૈલી
b正式seishikiformal/official — unrelated(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

Full sentence: あの人と私はオリンピックで金メダルを争った仲だ。Ano hito to watashi wa orinpikku de kin medaru o arasotta naka da.
EN: That person and I are on terms of having competed for the Olympic gold medal.
HI: वह व्यक्ति और मैं ओलंपिक में स्वर्ण पदक के लिए प्रतिस्पर्धा कर चुके हैं।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિ અને હું ઓલિમ્પિકમાં સુવર્ણ ચંદ્રક માટે સ્પર્ધા કરી ચૂક્યા છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a戦ったtatakattafought (too literally combative) — wrong nuance(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This essay pairs several near-synonyms the practice questions above hinge on:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
専門senmon Specialty, field A noun — the field itself. "My specialty is history" (専門は歴史です).
専攻senkou Major (academic) A する-verb — the act of majoring in a subject. "I majored in mathematics" (数学を専攻した).
戦うtatakau To fight (a battle, war, or literal contest) Combative — actual physical or military conflict. "Soldiers fight in a war."
争うarasou To dispute / compete for Broader — includes verbal disputes and competing for a prize. "They competed for the gold medal" (金メダルを争う).
愛情aijou Love, affection (general) Covers any warm bond — family, friends, or romance. A parent's, sibling's, or partner's affection.
恋愛ren'ai Romantic love Specifically romantic/dating relationships. "They're dating" / "a romance novel."
Reading family — 憎: Notice how many distinct okurigana forms one kanji can take: 憎い(にくい, adjective "detestable"), 憎む(にくむ, verb "to detest"), 憎しみ(にくしみ, noun "hatred"), 憎らしい(にくらしい, adjective "detestable/hateful," a slightly softer/more colloquial variant of 憎い). The same pattern applies to 悲しい(かなしい, "sad") vs 悲しむ(かなしむ, "to grieve") — adjective and verb forms of the same feeling, both built on the same kanji.
