JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 5 — つかう② (Tsukau ② — Use ②)

4日目 — 家庭用品（薬） (Katei-youhin (kusuri) — Household Goods: Medicine)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.82–83 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
13 kanji (#414–426) drawn from a home medicine cabinet: a cold-relief patch box labelled "くしゃみ・鼻水・せき・のどの痛み・発熱など風邪の諸症状に効く！" (エルタックV), an anti-itch cream tube "虫刺されに！" (カユノン), and a joint/nerve-pain patch box "肩こり・腰痛・関節・神経痛に" (コリトル55), followed by the products' own usage-warning label text.
Answer key
練習 answers for both p.82 (左) and p.83 (右) are confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet at PDF index 168 (the booklet's own p.8), under the 「4日目　左 (p.82)」and 「4日目　右 (p.83)」headers.
Notes
Two reading traps to watch for today: ❶節 (ふし, "a knot/joint") is an irregular standalone reading of 節 hiding inside the same entry as the ordinary on-yomi compound 調節 (ちょうせつ); and ⊙浴衣 (ゆかた) is a jukujikun — a whole-word reading that doesn't map onto 浴's usual あ(びる) kun-yomi at all.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — an unusual/irregular (jukujikun) reading not derivable from the kanji's usual on/kun readings, e.g. 浴衣 read ゆかた
☞ [number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book (e.g. ☞苦553 = "see kanji #553") — informational only
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi); Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi)

薬の使用上の注意書き (kusuri no shiyou-jou no chuui-gaki — usage warnings on the medicine label, p.83)

The label reads: 「入浴後のご使用が効果的です。汗や水分を拭き取ってから、おはりください。刺激や異常を感じた時は使用を中止し、医師に相談してください。薬の乾燥を防ぐため、開封後は点線を折り曲げて保存してください。」("It's most effective to use after bathing. To keep the medicine from drying out, fold along the dotted line for storage after opening.")

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (4 items from p.82, 4 items from p.83) — 正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the underlined kanji in hiragana. Answers confirmed from [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet p.8 (PDF index 168), sections 「4日目　左 (p.82)」and 「4日目　右 (p.83)」.

練習 — p.82 (左)

Q1

名刺の（a. 品書き　b. 肩書き）によれば、あの人は偉い人のようだ。Meishi no (a. shinagaki / b. katagaki) ni yoreba, ano hito wa erai hito no you da.

Full sentence: 名刺の肩書きによれば、あの人は偉い人のようだ。Meishi no katagaki ni yoreba, ano hito wa erai hito no you da.
EN: According to the title on his business card, that person seems to be someone important.
HI: उसके विज़िटिंग कार्ड पर लिखी पदवी के अनुसार, वह व्यक्ति कोई बड़ा आदमी लगता है।
GU: તેના વિઝિટિંગ કાર્ડ પરના હોદ્દા મુજબ, તે વ્યક્તિ કોઈ મોટા માણસ લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a品書きshinagakia menu / list of items (wrong fit)
b肩書きkatagakia title/position

Why: 肩書き is the professional title/position printed on a business card; 品書き is a menu or itemized list, unrelated to a person's status.

Q2

神社には（a. 上様　b. 神様）が、寺には仏様がいらっしゃいます。Jinja ni wa (a. uesama / b. kamisama) ga, tera ni wa hotokesama ga irasshaimasu.

Full sentence: 神社には神様が、寺には仏様がいらっしゃいます。Jinja ni wa kamisama ga, tera ni wa hotokesama ga irasshaimasu.
EN: At Shinto shrines there are kami (gods), and at temples there are Buddhas.
HI: शिंतो मंदिरों में देवता (कामी) होते हैं, और बौद्ध मंदिरों में बुद्ध होते हैं।
GU: શિન્તો મંદિરોમાં દેવતાઓ (કામી) હોય છે, અને બૌદ્ધ મંદિરોમાં બુદ્ધ હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a上様uesamaa polite address for a customer/recipient (wrong fit)
b神様kamisamaa god/deity

Why: Shinto shrines (神社) enshrine 神様 (kami); 上様 is a polite way of addressing a person (e.g. on a receipt), not a religious deity.

Q3

この腰掛けは高さが（a. 調節　b. 調子）できます。Kono koshikake wa takasa ga (a. chousetsu / b. choushi) dekimasu.

Full sentence: この腰掛けは高さが調節できます。Kono koshikake wa takasa ga chousetsu dekimasu.
EN: This seat's height can be adjusted.
HI: इस कुर्सी की ऊँचाई समायोजित की जा सकती है।
GU: આ ખુરશીની ઊંચાઈ સમાયોજિત કરી શકાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a調節chousetsuan adjustment
b調子choushicondition/tone/rhythm (wrong fit)

Why: 調節できる ("can be adjusted") pairs with 調節 for setting a degree/height; 調子 refers to a condition or rhythm (e.g. how one's body feels), not a settable height.

Q4

虫に（a. 抜かれて　b. 刺されて）かゆい。Mushi ni (a. nukarete / b. sasarete) kayui.

Full sentence: 虫に刺されてかゆい。Mushi ni sasarete kayui.
EN: I got bitten/stung by a bug and it's itchy.
HI: मुझे कीड़े ने काटा और अब खुजली हो रही है।
GU: મને જીવડાએ કરડ્યું અને હવે ખંજવાળ આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a抜かれてnukaretehad (something) pulled out (wrong fit)
b刺されてsasaretegot stung/bitten

Why: 虫に刺される is the fixed expression for being bitten/stung by an insect; 抜く means "to pull (something) out," unrelated to insect bites.

練習 — p.83 (右)

Q5

（a. 汁　b. 汗）をかいたのでシャワーを浴びたい。(a. Shiru / b. Ase) o kaita node shawaa o abitai.

Full sentence: 汗をかいたのでシャワーを浴びたい。Ase o kaita node shawaa o abitai.
EN: I worked up a sweat, so I want to take a shower.
HI: पसीना आ गया है, इसलिए मैं शावर लेना चाहता/चाहती हूँ।
GU: પરસેવો થયો છે, એટલે મારે શાવર લેવો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a汁shirusoup/juice (wrong fit)
b汗asesweat

Why: 汗をかく is the fixed expression "to sweat"; 汁 means soup or juice and doesn't collocate with かく in this sense.

Q6

医師に相談したら（a. 的確な　b. 快適な）助言をしてくれた。Ishi ni soudan shitara (a. tekikaku na / b. kaiteki na) jogen o shite kureta.

Full sentence: 医師に相談したら的確な助言をしてくれた。Ishi ni soudan shitara tekikaku na jogen o shite kureta.
EN: When I consulted the doctor, he gave me accurate advice.
HI: डॉक्टर से सलाह लेने पर उन्होंने सटीक सलाह दी।
GU: ડૉક્ટરની સલાહ લેતાં તેમણે ચોક્કસ સલાહ આપી.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a的確なtekikaku naaccurate/exact
b快適なkaiteki nacomfortable (wrong fit)

Why: 的確な助言 ("on-point advice") fits a doctor's advice; 快適な means "comfortable," which doesn't describe advice.

Q7

指を（a. 折って　b. 祈って）数を数える。Yubi o (a. otte / b. inotte) kazu o kazoeru.

Full sentence: 指を折って数を数える。Yubi o otte kazu o kazoeru.
EN: To count a number by bending down one's fingers.
HI: उंगलियाँ मोड़ते हुए गिनती गिनना।
GU: આંગળીઓ વાળીને ગણતરી કરવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a折ってottefolding/bending
b祈ってinottepraying (wrong fit, sound-alike trap)

Why: 指を折る ("to fold/bend one's fingers") is the idiom for counting on your fingers; 祈る (to pray) sounds similar but has nothing to do with counting.

Q8

この洗濯機には（a. 乾電　b. 乾燥）機能も付いています。Kono sentakuki ni wa (a. kanden / b. kansou) kinou mo tsuite imasu.

Full sentence: この洗濯機には乾燥機能も付いています。Kono sentakuki ni wa kansou kinou mo tsuite imasu.
EN: This washing machine also comes with a drying function.
HI: इस वॉशिंग मशीन में सुखाने का फ़ंक्शन भी है।
GU: આ વોશિંગ મશીનમાં સૂકવવાનું ફંક્શન પણ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a乾電kandennot a standalone word (only inside 乾電池)
b乾燥kansoudrying/dryness

Why: 乾燥機能 ("a drying function") is the correct compound; 乾電 isn't a standalone word — it only exists inside 乾電池 ("dry-cell battery").

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji cluster around transitive/intransitive verb pairs and one same-kanji reading trap:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
刺すsasu To stab/prick (something) Transitive — someone/something does the piercing. "The mosquito bit me" (蚊が刺す).
刺さるsasaru To be pierced/stuck Intransitive — describes the resulting state of a sharp object lodged in something. "A splinter is stuck in my finger" (とげが刺さる).
調節chousetsu An adjustment Regular on-yomi compound — 節 read セツ as part of a two-kanji word. Adjusting a seat height, temperature, volume.
❶節fushi A knot (in wood) / a joint Irregular standalone kun-yomi reading of the same kanji 節 — doesn't appear in ordinary compound readings. Describing a knot in a wooden board or a joint in bamboo.
折るoru To break/fold (something) Transitive. "I folded my fingers to count" (指を折る).
折れるoreru To break/bend (intransitive) Describes the object itself breaking, with no explicit agent. "The branch broke" (枝が折れる).
❶浴びるabiru To pour water over oneself / bask in Regular kun-yomi of 浴, but flagged because it also extends metaphorically (日光を浴びる, "to bask in sunlight"). Showering, or figuratively "receiving" attention/light.
⊙浴衣yukata A summer kimono A jukujikun — the whole word ゆかた doesn't come from 浴's usual あ(びる) reading at all. Naming the garment itself, never as a verb form.
Reading trap: 節 shows up twice in this same entry with two unrelated readings: 調節 (ちょうせつ, "an adjustment," ordinary on-yomi compound) vs. ❶節 (ふし, "a knot/joint," an irregular standalone kun-yomi). And 浴 shows the opposite kind of trap: ❶浴びる keeps a predictable kun-yomi, but ⊙浴衣 (ゆかた) is a jukujikun that must simply be memorized as a whole word, since it can't be sounded out from 浴's normal readings at all.
