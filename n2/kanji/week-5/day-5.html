JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 5 — つかう② (Tsukau ② — Use ②)

5日目 — 食品 (Shokuhin — Food Products)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.84–85 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
13 kanji (#427–439) drawn from food-packaging labels — a sauce bottle and a tofu package showing 賞味期限/製造年月日/消費期限, a frozen-hamburger microwave tray with cooking-time instructions, and (p.85) a dedicated section on instant ramen noodle-cup instructions.
Answer key
練習 answers for both p.84 (左) and p.85 (右) are confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet at PDF index 168–169 (the booklet's own p.8–9), under the 「5日目　左 (p.84)」and 「5日目　右 (p.85)」headers.
Notes
Watch for two irregular kun-yomi standalone readings today: ❶中身 (なかみ, "contents") under 身, and ❶杯 (さかずき, "a sake cup") under 杯 — both sit right beside a completely regular on-yomi/kun-yomi use of the same kanji.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — an unusual/irregular (jukujikun) reading not derivable from the kanji's usual on/kun readings
☞ [number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book (e.g. ☞麦486 = "see kanji #486") — informational only
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi); Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi)

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (4 items from p.84, 4 items from p.85) — 正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the underlined kanji in hiragana. Answers confirmed from [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet p.8–9 (PDF index 168–169), sections 「5日目　左 (p.84)」and 「5日目　右 (p.85)」.

練習 — p.84 (左)

Q1

〜酒造というのは酒を（a. 製造　b. 改造）している会社です。~Shuzou to iu no wa sake o (a. seizou / b. kaizou) shite iru kaisha desu.

Full sentence: 〜酒造というのは酒を製造している会社です。~Shuzou to iu no wa sake o seizou shite iru kaisha desu.
EN: A company named "...-Shuzou" is a company that manufactures sake.
HI: "...-शुज़ो" नाम की कंपनी वह कंपनी है जो साके (जापानी शराब) बनाती है।
GU: "...-શુઝો" નામની કંપની એ કંપની છે જે સાકે (જાપાની દારૂ) બનાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a製造seizouto manufacture
b改造kaizouto convert/remodel (wrong fit)

Why: 製造する ("to manufacture") fits making a product from raw ingredients; 改造する means to remodel something that already exists, not to produce sake from scratch.

Q2

この試合の（a. 費用　b. 賞金）の額は1位と2位とでは全然違う。Kono shiai no (a. hiyou / b. shoukin) no gaku wa ichi-i to ni-i to de wa zenzen chigau.

Full sentence: この試合の賞金の額は1位と2位とでは全然違う。Kono shiai no shoukin no gaku wa ichi-i to ni-i to de wa zenzen chigau.
EN: The prize money for this competition is completely different between 1st and 2nd place.
HI: इस प्रतियोगिता की पुरस्कार राशि पहले और दूसरे स्थान के बीच बिल्कुल अलग है।
GU: આ સ્પર્ધાની ઈનામી રકમ પ્રથમ અને બીજા સ્થાન વચ્ચે તદ્દન અલગ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a費用hiyouexpense/cost (wrong fit)
b賞金shoukinprize money

Why: 賞金 specifically means money awarded as a prize; 費用 means a cost/expense one pays — the opposite direction of money flow.

Q3

「（a. 身長　b. 身体）と体重をここに記入してください。」"(a. Shinchou / b. Shintai) to taijuu o koko ni kinyuu shite kudasai."

Full sentence: 「身長と体重をここに記入してください。」"Shinchou to taijuu o koko ni kinyuu shite kudasai."
EN: "Please fill in your height and weight here."
HI: "कृपया अपना कद और वज़न यहाँ लिखें।"
GU: "કૃપા કરી તમારી ઊંચાઈ અને વજન અહીં લખો."
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a身長shinchouheight
b身体shintaibody (redundant wrong fit)

Why: 身長 specifically means "height," pairing naturally with 体重 ("weight"); 身体 just means "body" in general and would be redundant next to 体重 in a form field.

Q4

この電車は（a. 車庫　b. 金庫）に入りますので、ご乗車になれません。Kono densha wa (a. shako / b. kinko) ni hairimasu node, gojousha ni naremasen.

Full sentence: この電車は車庫に入りますので、ご乗車になれません。Kono densha wa shako ni hairimasu node, gojousha ni naremasen.
EN: This train is heading into the depot, so you cannot board it.
HI: यह ट्रेन डिपो (गैराज) में जा रही है, इसलिए आप इसमें सवार नहीं हो सकते।
GU: આ ટ્રેન ડેપો (ગેરેજ)માં જઈ રહી છે, તેથી તમે તેમાં સવાર થઈ શકતા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a車庫shakoa garage/train depot
b金庫kinkoa safe (wrong fit)

Why: 車庫 is where vehicles like trains and cars are stored when out of service; 金庫 is a safe for valuables, unrelated to trains.

練習 — p.85 (右)

Q5

「開封後はなるべく早く（a. お召し上がり　b. お召しになって）ください。」"Kaifuu-go wa narubeku hayaku (a. o-meshiagari / b. o-meshi ni natte) kudasai."

Full sentence: 「開封後はなるべく早くお召し上がりください。」"Kaifuu-go wa narubeku hayaku o-meshiagari kudasai."
EN: "After opening, please eat it as soon as possible."
HI: "खोलने के बाद कृपया इसे जल्द से जल्द खा लें।"
GU: "ખોલ્યા પછી કૃપા કરી શક્ય તેટલું જલદી ખાઈ લો."
OptionJapaneseEnglish
aお召し上がりo-meshiagaripolite "please eat"
bお召しになってo-meshi ni nattepolite "please wear/put on" (wrong fit)

Why: お召し上がりください is the honorific request form of 食べる (to eat); お召しになる is the honorific form of 着る (to wear clothes) — the same base verb 召す branches into two unrelated polite meanings depending on context.

Q6

「この町の発展と繁栄を願って、（a. 一杯　b. 乾杯）！」"Kono machi no hatten to han'ei o negatte, (a. ippai / b. kanpai)!"

Full sentence: 「この町の発展と繁栄を願って、乾杯！」"Kono machi no hatten to han'ei o negatte, kanpai!"
EN: "Wishing for this town's development and prosperity — cheers!"
HI: "इस शहर की तरक्की और समृद्धि की कामना करते हुए — चियर्स!"
GU: "આ શહેરના વિકાસ અને સમૃદ્ધિની કામના સાથે — ચિયર્સ!"
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a一杯ippaione cup / a lot (wrong fit)
b乾杯kanpaicheers, a toast

Why: 乾杯 is the fixed toast exclamation said before drinking together; 一杯 just means "one cup" or "a lot" and isn't used as the toast word itself.

Q7

「（a. 年末　b. 年未）年始は休業とさせていただきます。」"(a. Nenmatsu / b. Nenmi) nenshi wa kyuugyou to sasete itadakimasu."

Full sentence: 「年末年始は休業とさせていただきます。」"Nenmatsu-nenshi wa kyuugyou to sasete itadakimasu."
EN: "We will be closed for the year-end and New Year holidays."
HI: "साल के अंत और नए साल की छुट्टियों में हम बंद रहेंगे।"
GU: "વર્ષના અંતે અને નવા વર્ષની રજાઓમાં અમે બંધ રહીશું."
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a年末nenmatsuthe end of the year
b年未nenminot a real word (look-alike trap)

Why: 年末年始 ("year-end and New Year") is a fixed set phrase using 末 ("the end"); 年未 with 未 ("not yet") isn't a real word — a visual look-alike trap.

Q8

明日は（a. 粉末　b. 花粉）が多くなりそうです。ご注意ください。Ashita wa (a. funmatsu / b. kafun) ga ooku narisou desu. Go-chuui kudasai.

Full sentence: 明日は花粉が多くなりそうです。ご注意ください。Ashita wa kafun ga ooku narisou desu. Go-chuui kudasai.
EN: Tomorrow there's likely to be a lot of pollen. Please be careful.
HI: कल हवा में पराग-कणों की मात्रा ज़्यादा होने की संभावना है। कृपया सावधान रहें।
GU: આવતીકાલે હવામાં પરાગની માત્રા વધારે હોવાની શક્યતા છે. કૃપા કરી સાવચેત રહો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a粉末funmatsupowder (wrong fit)
b花粉kafunpollen

Why: 花粉 (pollen) is what causes seasonal allergies and what a health advisory would warn about; 粉末 is just generic "powder," not the specific allergen.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today share a kanji but split into opposite or unrelated meanings — this is exactly what 練習 Q1–Q2 and Q6 are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
製造seizou Manufacture Making a product from raw materials. "This factory manufactures sake."
改造kaizou Conversion, remodeling Modifying something that already exists. "They remodeled the old building."
費用hiyou An expense/cost Money you pay out. "The cost of the trip was high."
消費shouhi Consumption Using up a resource (money, energy, goods). "Energy consumption rose this summer."
可能kanou Possible Something can be done. "It's possible to adjust the settings."
不可fuka Unacceptable, not allowed ⇔ opposite of 可 ("acceptable") — a flat prohibition. "Cash payment not accepted" (現金不可).
沸くwaku To boil (intransitive) The water itself reaches a boil. "The water boiled" (お湯が沸いた).
沸かすwakasu To boil something (transitive) Someone actively heats the water. "I boiled some water."
〜杯-hai ...cups (counter suffix) Regular on-yomi counter attached to a number. "Two and a half cups of water" (カップ2杯半).
❶杯sakazuki A sake cup (the object itself) Irregular standalone kun-yomi naming the cup itself, not a counter. Referring to the small ceramic cup used for sake.
Reading trap: 身 carries a plain kun-yomi in 身長 (しんちょう, on-yomi actually — height) and 身体 (しんたい), but ❶中身 (なかみ, "contents") uses an everyday standalone kun-yomi み that a learner might not expect after seeing シン elsewhere in the same entry. Likewise ❶杯 (さかずき) sits right beside the ordinary counter 〜杯 (はい) — same kanji, but one names an object and the other counts a quantity.
