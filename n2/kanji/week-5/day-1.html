Q1

試験に出される問題の（a. 傾向　b. 原因）を調べて受験勉強をする。Shiken ni dasareru mondai no (a. keikou / b. gen'in) o shirabete juken benkyou o suru.

Full sentence: 試験に出される問題の傾向を調べて受験勉強をする。Shiken ni dasareru mondai no keikou o shirabete juken benkyou o suru.
EN: I study for the exam by researching the trends in the questions that come up.
HI: परीक्षा में आने वाले सवालों के रुझान की जाँच करके मैं प्रवेश परीक्षा की तैयारी करता/करती हूँ।
GU: પરીક્ષામાં પુછાતા પ્રશ્નોના વલણની તપાસ કરીને હું પ્રવેશ પરીક્ષાની તૈયારી કરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a傾向keikoutrend/tendencyप्रवृत्ति, रुझानવલણ, ઝુકાવ
b原因gen'incause (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 傾向 ("trend/tendency") fits researching patterns in exam questions; 原因 ("cause") doesn't fit "researching a cause of questions."

Q5

「作品には手を（a. 触れないで　b. 届けないで）ください。」"Sakuhin ni wa te o (a. furenaide / b. todokenaide) kudasai."

Full sentence: 「作品には手を触れないでください。」"Sakuhin ni wa te o furenaide kudasai."
EN: "Please do not touch the artwork."
HI: "कृपया कलाकृति को हाथ न लगाएँ।"
GU: "કૃપા કરીને કલાકૃતિને હાથ ન લગાડો."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a触れないでfurenaidedon't touchहाथ न लगाएँહાથ ન લગાડો

2 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

These kanji come from two unrelated appliances, but several pairs share readings or components and are worth comparing directly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
恐れosore Fear, risk (noun) A neutral noun for danger/risk of something happening. "There is a risk/fear of X" — 〜の恐れがある.
恐ろしいosoroshii Terrifying (adjective) Describes something as frightening in itself. Describing a scary experience, story, or sight.
恐れ入りますosoreirimasu Excuse me / I'm terribly sorry (fixed polite phrase) A set formula of humility, not literally about fear at all. Making a polite request or apologizing formally.
触れるfureru To touch / to mention Softer, can be physical or figurative ("touch on a topic"). Signs/instructions ("please do not touch"), or "to touch on a subject."
触るsawaru To touch Plain, direct physical contact only. "Don't touch that with your hands" (casual/direct).
熱心（な）nesshin (na) Enthusiastic, diligent Describes a person's ongoing attitude/character trait. "An enthusiastic student," "a zealous worker."
熱中netchuu Absorbed, engrossed Describes a temporary state of being completely absorbed in one activity. "So absorbed in a game that you forget the time."
余分（な）yobun (na) Excess (amount) A surplus quantity of something concrete. "Extra money," "an excess amount of salt."
余裕yoyuu Leeway, margin Spare capacity — of time, money, or emotional room. "No time to spare," "financial leeway."
装置souchi Device, mechanism A physical apparatus that performs a function — shares 置 with 位置. "A safety device," "an electronic apparatus."
位置ichi Location, position Where something is, not what it does. "The location of a building," "current position."
Exam trap: 触れる and 触る are both marked ❶ in the book — both mean "touch," but 触れる is the softer/more formal verb (also used figuratively, "to touch on a topic"), while 触る is the plain, direct physical-contact verb. Warning signs like 「作品には手を触れないでください」almost always use 触れる, not 触る.
