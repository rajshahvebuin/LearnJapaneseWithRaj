6日目 — インターホン・パソコン (Intaahon, Pasokon — Intercom, Personal Computer)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.86–87 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
13 kanji (#440–452) split across two devices: an apartment intercom panel (chime, visitor call button, emergency alarm reset instructions) on p.86, and a home PC — a received-email inbox, a dictionary-software launch guide, and a menu's "insert row/column" dialog — on p.87.
Answer key
練習 answers for both p.86 (左) and p.87 (右) are confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet at PDF index 169 (the booklet's own p.9), under the 「6日目　左 (p.86)」and 「6日目　右 (p.87)」headers.
Notes
訪 gives two ❶-marked readings side by side: ❶訪れる (otozureru, "for a time/place to arrive/come," or a more literary "visit") and ❶訪ねる (tazuneru, "to visit a person/place") — easy to mix up since both mean "visit" in English but take different grammatical subjects. 実 also carries a ❶ standalone kun-yomi み ("a fruit/nut") distinct from its usual みの-る verb form.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — an unusual/irregular (jukujikun) reading not derivable from the kanji's usual on/kun readings
☞ [number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book (e.g. ☞島521 = "see kanji #521") — informational only
Katakana reading = 音読み (on-yomi); Hiragana reading = 訓読み (kun-yomi)

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (4 items from p.86, 4 items from p.87) — 正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the underlined kanji in hiragana. Answers confirmed from [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet p.9 (PDF index 169), sections 「6日目　左 (p.86)」and 「6日目　右 (p.87)」.

練習 — p.86 (左)

Q1

訪問販売の人が何度も（a. 訪ねて　b. 栄えて）きてチャイムを鳴らす。Houmon hanbai no hito ga nando mo (a. tazunete / b. sakaete) kite chaimu o narasu.

Full sentence: 訪問販売の人が何度も訪ねてきてチャイムを鳴らす。Houmon hanbai no hito ga nando mo tazunete kite chaimu o narasu.
EN: The door-to-door salesperson keeps coming to visit and ringing the chime.
HI: घर-घर जाकर सामान बेचने वाला व्यक्ति बार-बार आकर घंटी बजाता है।
GU: ઘરે ઘરે જઈને સામાન વેચનાર વ્યક્તિ વારંવાર આવીને ઘંટડી વગાડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a訪ねてtazunetevisiting
b栄えてsakaeteflourishing (wrong fit)

Why: 訪ねてくる ("to come and visit") fits a salesperson calling repeatedly; 栄える means "to flourish/prosper," unrelated to visiting.

Q2

台風が近づいて雨や風が強くなり、注意報が（a. 警報　b. 電報）に変わった。Taifuu ga chikazuite ame ya kaze ga tsuyoku nari, chuuihou ga (a. keihou / b. denpou) ni kawatta.

Full sentence: 台風が近づいて雨や風が強くなり、注意報が警報に変わった。Taifuu ga chikazuite ame ya kaze ga tsuyoku nari, chuuihou ga keihou ni kawatta.
EN: As the typhoon approached and the rain and wind grew stronger, the advisory was upgraded to a warning.
HI: तूफ़ान के नज़दीक आने और बारिश-हवा के तेज़ होने से, सलाह चेतावनी में बदल गई।
GU: તોફાન નજીક આવવાથી અને વરસાદ-પવન તીવ્ર થવાથી, સલાહ ચેતવણીમાં ફેરવાઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a警報keihoua warning/alarm
b電報denpoua telegram (wrong fit, look-alike trap)

Why: 警報 is the escalated weather-alert level above 注意報; 電報 means "telegram," a completely different concept that only shares the kanji 報.

Q3

あの人の言動には（a. 内外　b. 裏表）があって、信用できない。Ano hito no gendou ni wa (a. naigai / b. uraomote) ga atte, shin'you dekinai.

Full sentence: あの人の言動には裏表があって、信用できない。Ano hito no gendou ni wa uraomote ga atte, shin'you dekinai.
EN: That person's words and actions are two-faced, so I can't trust them.
HI: उस व्यक्ति की बातों और व्यवहार में दोहरापन है, इसलिए उस पर भरोसा नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિની વાત અને વર્તનમાં દંભ છે, તેથી તેમના પર વિશ્વાસ કરી શકાય નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a内外naigaiinside and outside (wrong fit)
b裏表uraomotetwo-facedness/duplicity

Why: 裏表がある idiomatically means "to be two-faced/duplicitous"; 内外 just means "inside and outside" (e.g. a location), not a description of someone's honesty.

Q4

お客様に（a. お呼び出し　b. お呼び込み）を申し上げます。O-kyakusama ni (a. o-yobidashi / b. o-yobikomi) o moushiagemasu.

Full sentence: お客様にお呼び出しを申し上げます。O-kyakusama ni o-yobidashi o moushiagemasu.
EN: We would like to page/call for the customer (a PA announcement).
HI: हम ग्राहक महोदय को पुकार कर बुला रहे हैं (उद्घोषणा)।
GU: અમે ગ્રાહકશ્રીને બોલાવી રહ્યા છીએ (જાહેરાત).
OptionJapaneseEnglish
aお呼び出しo-yobidashia page/summons
bお呼び込みo-yobikomitouting customers in (wrong fit)

Why: お呼び出しを申し上げます is the set announcement phrase used over a PA system to page a specific customer; 呼び込み instead describes actively drawing people into a shop — the wrong direction of action.

練習 — p.87 (右)

Q5

（a. 正面　b. 前面）玄関は閉まっていますから、裏口から入ってください。(a. Shoumen / b. Zenmen) genkan wa shimatte imasu kara, uraguchi kara haitte kudasai.

Full sentence: 正面玄関は閉まっていますから、裏口から入ってください。Shoumen genkan wa shimatte imasu kara, uraguchi kara haitte kudasai.
EN: The front entrance is closed, so please come in through the back door.
HI: मुख्य प्रवेश द्वार बंद है, इसलिए कृपया पिछले दरवाज़े से अंदर आएँ।
GU: મુખ્ય પ્રવેશદ્વાર બંધ છે, તેથી કૃપા કરી પાછલા દરવાજેથી અંદર આવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a正面shoumenfront
b前面zenmenthe front surface/aspect (wrong fit)

Why: 正面玄関 ("the front entrance") is the fixed compound for a building's main entrance; 前面 usually describes the front surface/aspect of an object, not a building's main door.

Q6

合格した人は入学（a. 手続き　b. 操作）をしてください。Goukaku shita hito wa nyuugaku (a. tetsuzuki / b. sousa) o shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 合格した人は入学手続きをしてください。Goukaku shita hito wa nyuugaku tetsuzuki o shite kudasai.
EN: Those who passed, please complete the enrollment procedures.
HI: जो उत्तीर्ण हुए हैं, कृपया प्रवेश की औपचारिकताएँ पूरी करें।
GU: જેઓ પાસ થયા છે, કૃપા કરી પ્રવેશની વિધિ પૂર્ણ કરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a手続きtetsuzukia procedure
b操作sousaoperation/manipulation (wrong fit)

Why: 入学手続き ("enrollment procedures") is the fixed term for the paperwork/steps to enroll; 操作 refers to operating a machine or device, unrelated to an administrative process.

Q7

新製品を買いたい人の（a. 行　b. 列）が店の前から交差点まで続いている。Shinseihin o kaitai hito no (a. gyou / b. retsu) ga mise no mae kara kousaten made tsuzuite iru.

Full sentence: 新製品を買いたい人の列が店の前から交差点まで続いている。Shinseihin o kaitai hito no retsu ga mise no mae kara kousaten made tsuzuite iru.
EN: The line of people wanting to buy the new product stretches from in front of the shop all the way to the intersection.
HI: नया प्रोडक्ट ख़रीदने के इच्छुक लोगों की कतार दुकान के सामने से चौराहे तक फैली हुई है।
GU: નવું ઉત્પાદન ખરીદવા ઈચ્છતા લોકોની કતાર દુકાનની સામેથી ચાર રસ્તા સુધી લંબાયેલી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a行gyoua line of text/writing (wrong fit)
b列retsua line/queue of people

Why: 列 is the word for a queue of people; 行 refers to a line of writing/text, not a line of waiting people.

Q8

私は課長に（a. 辞表　b. 裏表）を出した。Watashi wa kachou ni (a. jihyou / b. uraomote) o dashita.

Full sentence: 私は課長に辞表を出した。Watashi wa kachou ni jihyou o dashita.
EN: I submitted my resignation letter to my section chief.
HI: मैंने अपने विभाग प्रमुख को अपना इस्तीफ़ा सौंप दिया।
GU: મેં મારા વિભાગના વડાને મારું રાજીનામું આપ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a辞表jihyoua resignation letter
b裏表uraomotetwo-facedness (unrelated distractor)

Why: 辞表を出す ("to submit one's resignation") is the fixed expression; 裏表 means duplicity, an unrelated word a learner might only confuse by loose sound/shape association.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The intercom half of today's kanji is built around two "visit" verbs that are easy to swap; the PC half has its own same-kanji nuance traps:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
❶訪れるotozureru To visit / for a time to come More literary; the subject is often abstract (a season, an era) as well as a person. "Spring has arrived" (春が訪れた) or formal visiting.
❶訪ねるtazuneru To visit (a person/place) Always has a concrete human or place as the object being visited. "I visited my teacher's house" (先生の家を訪ねた).
警報keihou A warning/alarm An escalated, urgent alert level. A typhoon or flood warning.
電報denpou A telegram A written message sent by wire — shares only the kanji 報 with 警報, otherwise unrelated. An old-fashioned urgent written message.
辞書jisho A dictionary A noun naming a reference book. Looking up a word.
辞めるyameru To resign/quit A verb describing leaving a job or position — same kanji 辞, unrelated meaning. "I quit my job."
続くtsuzuku To continue (intransitive) The situation itself goes on. "The line kept going" (列が続いている).
続けるtsuzukeru To continue (something), transitive Someone actively keeps doing an action. "I kept studying" (勉強を続けた).
Exam trap: 訪れる vs. 訪ねる both translate as "visit" in English, but the book flags both with ❶ because Japanese distinguishes them by subject: 訪れる leans literary/abstract (a season, an era, or a formal visit "arriving"), while 訪ねる is the everyday verb for a person actively going to see someone. 実 hides a similar trap: its usual verb form is 実る (みのる, "to bear fruit"), but the standalone noun ❶実 (み, "a fruit/nut") uses a shorter, unrelated-looking kun-yomi that must be memorized separately.
