Scene

普通乗車券、回数券だけでなく、新幹線の自由席券も自動券売機で買うことができます。お金を入れて、行き先、往復か片道か、枚数などのボタンを押します。Futsuu-joushaken, kaisuuken dake de naku, shinkansen no jiyuusekiken mo jidou-kenbaiki de kau koto ga dekimasu. Okane o irete, ikisaki, oufuku ka katamichi ka, maisuu nado no botan o oshimasu.
(Not just ordinary tickets and commuter-ticket books — you can also buy Shinkansen unreserved-seat tickets from an automatic ticket machine. You insert money, then press buttons for the destination, whether it's round-trip or one-way, the number of tickets, and so on.)

定期券も自動券売機で販売していますが、指定席券は窓口で買います。回数券や定期券など発券に時間がかかるときは「発券中」という表示が出ます。「調整中」の表示があったら、しばらく使用中止です。Teikiken mo jidou-kenbaiki de hanbai shite imasu ga, shiteisekiken wa madoguchi de kaimasu. Kaisuuken ya teikiken nado hakken ni jikan ga kakaru toki wa "hakken-chuu" to iu hyouji ga demasu. "Chousei-chuu" no hyouji ga attara, shibaraku shiyou-chuushi desu.
(Commuter passes are also sold from the machine, but reserved-seat tickets are bought at the window. When issuing a commuter-ticket book or pass takes time, a "issuing in progress" display appears. If an "adjustment in progress" display appears, the machine is temporarily out of use.)

HI: चलिए, जो पढ़ा है उसे एक बार फिर से दोहराते हैं।
GU: ચાલો, જે ભણ્યા છીએ તેનું ફરી એકવાર પુનરાવર્તન કરીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a準備junbipreparation (wrong verb entirely)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: મારી પાસે મોજાંમાંથી ફક્ત એક જ બચ્યું છે. ક્યાં પડી ગયું હશે ખબર નહીં.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today reuse the same kanji with a different reading and a very different meaning — exactly what today's two 練習 sets are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
指すsasu To point at (with a finger) 訓読み さす — a literal, physical pointing gesture. "Point to the map," "the clock hands point to 3."
示すshimesu To show/indicate (information) 訓読み しめす — presenting data, a sign, or an instruction, not a physical gesture. "The display shows the fare," "the sign indicates the exit."
表hyou A table/chart 音読み ひょう — same kanji as 表(おもて), completely different reading and meaning. "Timetable" (時刻表), "chart."
❶表omote Surface/face (of an object) 訓読み おもて — the physical front side of something. "The front side of the coin/fabric."
片付けるkatazukeru To tidy up A full verb built on 片, but the meaning has drifted far from "one side." "Tidy your room."
片道katamichi One-way (of a trip) 片〜 here literally means "one side/direction," its more transparent core meaning. "A one-way ticket," contrasted with 往復 (round trip).
Exam trap: 表 is the day's biggest reading trap — read ひょう it means "a table/chart" (時刻表, 発表), but read おもて (marked ❶ in the book) it means "the surface/face" of an object, an unrelated meaning. Always check whether a 表 in context names a document or a physical surface before deciding the reading.
