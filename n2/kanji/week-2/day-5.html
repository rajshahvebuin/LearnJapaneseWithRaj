Scene (p.36)

私は家に帰るとすぐ留守番電話の伝言を聞き、再生済みの用件を消去する。携帯電話は電車の中や仕事中など、普段はマナーモードに設定している。Watashi wa ie ni kaeru to sugu rusuban denwa no dengon o kiki, saisei-zumi no youken o shoukyo suru. Keitai denwa wa densha no naka ya shigoto-chuu nado, fudan wa manaa moodo ni settei shite iru.

EN: As soon as I get home, I listen to the answering machine's messages and delete matters I've already played. I usually keep my cell phone set to manner (silent) mode, like on the train or while working.

Scene (p.37)

「着信履歴」やメールの「受信箱」を確認して、用件があれば返信する。新しくアドレスを作成する場合は、「新規」で入力する。文字は自分で変換したり並んでいる言葉の中から「選択」したりして、「決定」あるいは「確定」する。"Chakushin rireki" ya meeru no "jushinbako" o kakunin shite, youken ga areba henshin suru. EN: Check the "call history" or the email "inbox," and reply if there's a matter to address. To create a new address, enter it under "New." Convert characters yourself, or "select" from a list of candidate words, then "confirm" or "finalize."

Q1

この件は（a. 保留　b. 停留）にしておいて、また後で考えます。Kono ken wa (a. horyuu / b. teiryuu) ni shite oite, mata ato de kangaemasu.

Full sentence: この件は保留にしておいて、また後で考えます。Kono ken wa horyuu ni shite oite, mata ato de kangaemasu.
EN: Let's leave this matter on hold and think it over again later.
HI: इस मामले को फ़िलहाल टाल देते हैं और बाद में फिर सोचेंगे।
GU: આ બાબતને હાલ પૂરતી મુલતવી રાખીએ અને પછીથી ફરી વિચારીશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a保留horyuuholding off, pendingटालना, लंबित रखनाમુલતવી, પડતર રાખવું
b停留teiryuuonly used in 停留所 "bus stop", not standalone(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day reuses several headword kanji across very different readings/nuances — the exact traps both 練習 sets above are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
保留horyuu Holding off, pending 音読み ほ — a suspended decision. "Let's leave this matter on hold."
⊙留守rusu Absence from home Irregular reading of 留守 as a set word — being away/out. "No one's home," "I'll be away."
保つtamotsu Keep, maintain (a state) 訓読み — describes sustaining a condition. "Maintain a temperature," "keep one's balance."
守るmamoru Protect, obey (a rule) 訓読み — describes defending or following something external. "Obey traffic signals," "protect the environment."
変わるkawaru To change (intransitive) The subject itself undergoes the change. "My phone number has changed."
変えるkaeru To change (transitive) Someone actively changes an object. "I changed my phone number."
選ぶerabu To choose (someone chooses) Requires a chooser as the grammatical subject. "I chose the blue one."
決まるkimaru To be decided (outcome, no chooser named) The decided thing is the subject; no explicit decider. "The team has been decided."
Exam trap: 留守 is flagged with the special-reading mark (⊙) precisely because る+す doesn't obviously spell "absence" the way most 音読み compounds do — memorize 留守 as a fixed unit rather than building it from 留(リュウ)+守(シュ) individually. Likewise 手伝う (てつだう) is a special reading of 伝 that doesn't use either てん listed on-yomi form directly in its verb conjugation pattern.
