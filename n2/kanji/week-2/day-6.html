Scene (p.39)

ワープロソフトで書類を作成するときは「書式」でフォントやサイズを設定する。文字は中央にそろえたり、右寄せ、左寄せもできる。作成したら「名前を付けて保存」する。その後は「上書き保存」できる。印刷は「用紙設定」をして「印刷プレビュー」で確認するとよい。文字が小さくて見づらいときは、「拡大」表示する。Waapuro sofuto de shorui o sakusei suru toki wa "shoshiki" de fonto ya saizu o settei suru. Moji wa chuuou ni soroetari, migiyose, hidariyose mo dekiru. Sakusei shitara "namae o tsukete hozon" suru. Sono ato wa "uwagaki hozon" dekiru. Insatsu wa "youshi settei" o shite "insatsu purebyuu" de kakunin suru to yoi. Moji ga chiisakute mizurai toki wa, "kakudai" hyouji suru.

EN: When creating a document in word-processing software, set the font and size under "Format." Text can also be center-aligned, right-aligned, or left-aligned. Once created, use "save as" to name and save it; after that you can use "overwrite save." For printing, set up the paper under "page setup" and check with "print preview." When the text is too small to read comfortably, display it "enlarged."

EN: Please develop this film.
HI: कृपया इस फ़िल्म को डेवलप कर दें।
GU: કૃપા કરી આ ફિલ્મ ડેવલપ કરી આપો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a現像genzouto develop (film)डेवलप करना (फ़िल्म)ડેવલપ કરવું (ફિલ્મ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several words today are near-homophones or share a kanji with a very different partner meaning — exactly what today's 練習 questions test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
終了shuuryou End, expiration A general event, business hours, or process coming to an end. "Today's business has ended."
修了shuuryou Completion (of a course) Same reading as 終了, but specifically finishing a course of study. "Completed the training program."
現像genzou Developing (film) A verb — the chemical process of developing a photograph. "Please develop this film."
映像eizou Video, footage A noun — the resulting moving image/footage itself. "Security camera footage."
登録touroku Registration Saving data/information into a system. "Register a new address in the phone."
登場toujou Appearance (on stage/scene) A person, character, or product entering a scene. "A new character appears in the story."
保存hozon Save, preserve Keeping a file, food, or record intact. "Save the document."
生存seizon Survival A living thing continuing to live. "Survivors of the disaster."
Exam trap: 終了 and 修了 are perfect homophones (both しゅうりょう) with related but distinct meanings — 終了 is the generic "ended/over" (a shop closing, a meeting ending), while 修了 specifically means completing a course of study or training. When a 漢字選択 question gives you しゅうりょう in a sentence about school/training, check whether it's about the course finishing (修了) versus something simply being "over" (終了) before picking the kanji.
