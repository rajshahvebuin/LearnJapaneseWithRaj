練習（p.34 — air conditioner remote）— choose the correct option

Q1

梅雨の時期はエアコンを（a. 除湿　b. 停電）にすると快適だ。Tsuyu no jiki wa eakon o (a. joshitsu / b. teiden) ni suru to kaiteki da.

Full sentence: 梅雨の時期はエアコンを除湿にすると快適だ。Tsuyu no jiki wa eakon o joshitsu ni suru to kaiteki da.
EN: During the rainy season, it's comfortable to set the air conditioner to dehumidify.
HI: बारिश के मौसम में एसी को डीह्यूमिडिफ़ाई (नमी हटाने) पर सेट करने से आराम रहता है।
GU: ચોમાસાની ઋતુમાં એસીને ડિહ્યુમિડિફાય (ભેજ દૂર કરવા) પર સેટ કરવાથી આરામદાયક રહે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a除湿joshitsudehumidifyनमी हटानाભેજ દૂર કરવો
b停電teidenpower outage (not a remote setting)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 除湿 is a mode button on the air conditioner remote (shown in the illustration); 停電 describes a power blackout, not something you set the AC to.

Q2

Full sentence: 次の駅で地下鉄に乗り換えましょう。Tsugi no eki de chikatetsu ni norikae mashou.
EN: Let's transfer to the subway at the next station.
HI: अगले स्टेशन पर मेट्रो में बदलते हैं।
GU: આગલા સ્ટેશને મેટ્રોમાં બદલીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a乗り換えnorikaechanging trainsट्रेन बदलनाટ્રેન બદલવી
b切り換えkirikaeswitching (a mode/setting, not trains)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 乗り換える is the fixed verb for transferring between trains/subway lines; 切り換える switches a device's mode or setting (like 運転切換 on the remote), not a person's ride.

室内の温度が高くなりすぎたら、（a. 設定　b. 指定）温度を下げましょう。Shitsunai no ondo ga takaku narisugitara, (a. settei / b. shitei) ondo o sagemashou.

年中無休。ただし、年末年始を（a. 包む　b. 除く）。Nenjuu mukyuu. Tadashi, nenmatsu-nenshi o (a. tsutsumu / b. nozoku).

HI: साल भर खुला। हालाँकि, साल के अंत/नए साल की छुट्टियाँ छोड़कर।
GU: આખું વર્ષ ખુલ્લું. જોકે, વર્ષના અંત/નવા વર્ષની રજાઓ સિવાય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day repeatedly reuses the same headword kanji with a different reading for a different nuance — exactly what today's two 練習 sets test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
除湿joshitsu Dehumidify (noun/mode) 音読み じょ — a fixed appliance-mode compound. Naming the AC's dehumidify setting.
除くnozoku To remove/exclude 訓読み のぞく — a standalone verb of general use. "Excluding the New Year holidays," "remove an item."
換気kanki Ventilation 音読み かん — about air/rooms. "Ventilate the room."
乗り換えnorikae Changing trains 訓読み か-える, but fixed to 乗り〜 — about transport only. "Transfer to another line at the next station."
向かいmukai The opposite side, facing Static — describes a fixed spatial relationship. "The shop across from the station."
向こうmukou Over there, beyond Points further away, often beyond a boundary. "Over there," "the other side (of the world)."
Exam trap: Both ❶-marked entries today (掃除/除く and 量る) are the book's own flagged "pay attention" readings — 除 shows up as じょ in 除湿/掃除 but のぞく as a plain verb, and 量 shows up as りょう in every compound (音量・風量・分量・数量) but switches to the kun-yomi はかる only as the standalone verb "to measure." When a 漢字読み question isolates the single kanji as a verb, expect the kun-yomi even if every compound on the page used the on-yomi.
