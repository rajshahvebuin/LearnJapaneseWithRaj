Scene

銀行や郵便局で窓口を使わずに、現金自動支払機でお金を引き出したり、預けたりすることができます。「引き出し」「預け入れ」を「払い戻し」「預金」とも言います。また、「残高照会」で、いくらお金が残っているか調べることができます。Ginkou ya yuubinkyoku de madoguchi o tsukawazu ni, genkin jidou shiharaiki de okane o hikidashitari, azuketari suru koto ga dekimasu. "Hikidashi" "azukeire" o "haraimodoshi" "yokin" to mo iimasu. Mata, "zandaka shoukai" de, ikura okane ga nokotte iru ka shiraberu koto ga dekimasu.
(Without using the counter at a bank or post office, you can withdraw or deposit money using an ATM. "Withdrawal" and "deposit" are also called 払い戻し and 預金. You can also use "balance inquiry" to check how much money remains.)

「硬貨はこちらにお入れください」「よろしければ確認ボタンを押してください」などという指示にしたがってお金を預けたり、引き出したりしますが、間違えてしまった場合は、「取り消し」ボタンを押します。"Kouka wa kochira ni oirekudasai" "Yoroshikereba kakunin botan o oshite kudasai" nado to iu shiji ni shitagatte okane o azuketari, hikidashitari shimasu ga, machigaete shimatta baai wa, "torikeshi" botan o oshimasu.
(You follow instructions like "Please insert coins here" and "If it's OK, press the confirm button" to deposit or withdraw money, but if you make a mistake, you press the "cancel" button.)

Q1

この店は（a. 照明　b. 調整）が明るくて気持ちがいい。Kono mise wa (a. shoumei / b. chousei) ga akarukute kimochi ga ii.

Full sentence: この店は照明が明るくて気持ちがいい。Kono mise wa shoumei ga akarukute kimochi ga ii.
EN: This shop's lighting is bright and pleasant.
HI: इस दुकान की रोशनी तेज़ और सुहावनी है।
GU: આ દુકાનની લાઇટિંગ તેજસ્વી અને સુખદ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a照明shoumeilightingरोशनीલાઇટિંગ
b調整chouseiadjustment (can't be "bright")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 照明が明るい describes bright lighting; 調整 (adjustment) isn't something that can be "bright."

EN: I live supported by my family and friends.
HI: मैं अपने परिवार और दोस्तों के सहारे जी रहा/रही हूँ।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a数えkazoecount (nonsensical here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today reuse the same headword kanji for very different meanings, or look alike enough to swap by mistake — exactly what today's two 練習 sets are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
❶支えるsasaeru To support/hold up 訓読み ささえる — physically or emotionally holding something/someone up. "Supported by family and friends," "support a shelf."
支払うshiharau To pay Same kanji 支, unrelated meaning — about money changing hands. "Pay the bill," "pay a fee."
違いchigai A difference (neutral) Describes two things not being the same — no right/wrong implied. "The difference between A and B."
間違いmachigai A mistake (negative) Adds 間 to 違い — specifically an error to be corrected. "Erase the mistake and rewrite it."
確か(な)tashika (na) Certain (adjective) 訓読み たしか — describes a state of certainty. "I'm certain that...," "a reliable source."
確認kakunin A confirmation (noun) 音読み かくにん — the act of checking/confirming something. "Press the confirm button," "confirmation email."
Exam trap: Today's intro scene mentions the ATM's 「取り消し」(cancel) button — but 取 also builds 書き取り (dictation, writing what you hear) and 聞き取り (listening comprehension), two totally unrelated skills sharing the same base kanji. Don't let the shared 取 lead you to confuse "cancel a transaction" with "a listening/writing test."
