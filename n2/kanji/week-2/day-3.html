Scene

A: 何にする？　温かいお茶？Nani ni suru? Atatakai ocha?
B: 冷たいのがいい。えーっと、緑茶、紅茶……ウーロン茶にしよう。Tsumetai no ga ii. Eetto, ryokucha, koucha... uuroncha ni shiyou.

B: その返却レバーを引けば出てくるよ。Sono henkyaku rebaa o hikeba dete kuru yo.
(A: "What'll you have? Warm tea?" B: "I want something cold. Let's see, green tea, black tea... let's go with oolong tea." A: "Oh, apparently 500-yen coins don't work... 1000-yen bills do, though. Huh? Where's the change?" B: "It comes out if you pull that return lever.")

入場券や入館券の券売機には、団体、一般、学生、小中学生、幼児（〜歳以上〜歳未満）などというボタンがあって、それぞれ料金が違う場合があります。また、老人割引があるところもあります。Nyuujouken ya nyuukanken no kenbaiki ni wa, dantai, ippan, gakusei, shou-chuugakusei, youji (~sai ijou ~sai miman) nado to iu botan ga atte, sorezore ryoukin ga chigau baai ga arimasu. Mata, roujin waribiki ga aru tokoro mo arimasu.
(Admission/entry-ticket machines have buttons for group, general, student, elementary/middle-school student, and infant (~ years old and above, under ~ years old) categories, and prices sometimes differ for each. Some places also offer senior citizen discounts.)

練習（p.32）— choose the correct option

Q1

このデザートは（a. 冷やして　b. 冷えて）食べるとおいしい。Kono dezaato wa (a. hiyashite / b. hiete) taberu to oishii.

Full sentence: このデザートは冷やして食べるとおいしい。Kono dezaato wa hiyashite taberu to oishii.
EN: This dessert tastes good when you chill it before eating.
HI: यह डेज़र्ट ठंडा करके खाने पर स्वादिष्ट लगता है।
GU: આ ડેઝર્ટ ઠંડું કરીને ખાવાથી સ્વાદિષ્ટ લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a冷やしてhiyashitecool (something), -te formठंडा करकेઠંડું કરીને
b冷えてhietebecome cold, -te form (intransitive, wrong here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: You actively 冷やす (chill/cool) the dessert before eating it — a transitive action on the food; 冷える describes something becoming cold on its own, which doesn't fit the deliberate action implied here.

Full sentence: この辺は緑が多くて静かできれいですね。Kono hen wa midori ga ookute shizuka de kirei desu ne.
EN: This area has a lot of greenery and is quiet and beautiful, isn't it?
HI: यह इलाका हरियाली से भरा, शांत और सुंदर है, है ना?
GU: આ વિસ્તાર હરિયાળીથી ભરેલો, શાંત અને સુંદર છે, ખરું ને?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a緑midorigreen(ery)हरियालीહરિયાળી
b紅beni/koucrimson/red (doesn't fit a green, quiet area)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 緑が多い describes an area rich in greenery/trees; 紅 (red/crimson) doesn't fit describing a peaceful, green area.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a返事henjia reply (unrelated to money)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

EN: This vending machine accepts not just coins but 1000-yen bills too.
HI: यह वेंडिंग मशीन सिक्कों के अलावा 1000 येन के नोट भी स्वीकार करती है।
GU: આ વેન્ડિંગ મશીન સિક્કા ઉપરાંત 1000 યેનની નોટ પણ સ્વીકારે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a玉tamaball/coin-shaped object (not paper money)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Full sentence: 「18歳未満」には18歳は入りません。"Juuhassai miman" ni wa juuhassai wa hairimasen.
EN: "Under 18" does not include 18-year-olds themselves.
HI: "18 साल से कम" में 18 साल के लोग शामिल नहीं होते।
GU: "18 વર્ષથી ઓછું" માં 18 વર્ષની ઉંમરના લોકોનો સમાવેશ થતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

冷 alone gives two intransitive/transitive pairs with a subtle difference in what they describe, and 未 stacks with different second kanji to mean very different kinds of "not yet" — both are worth comparing side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
冷えるhieru Become cold (intransitive) About physical temperature dropping — drinks, a room, the weather. "The drink got cold," "it's chilly out."
冷やすhiyasu Cool something (transitive) The deliberate act of chilling something physically. "Chill the dessert/drinks in the fridge."
❶冷めるsameru Cool down (intransitive) Something that was hot loses heat on its own — food, tea, or figuratively enthusiasm. "The tea went cold," "their enthusiasm cooled."
❶冷ますsamasu Cool something down (transitive) Deliberately letting a hot thing lose heat before use. "Let the soup cool before serving."
未満miman Less than (a quantity threshold) About a number/age boundary, strictly excluding the stated value. "Under 18," "less than 1000 yen."
未定mitei Undecided (a decision status) About whether something has been decided yet, no numeric boundary. "The schedule is still undecided."
Exam trap: 二十歳 has two valid readings side by side in the book: the regular compound reading にじゅっさい, and the ⊙-marked irregular reading はたち (used in everyday speech for "20 years old," e.g. 成人の日 turning-20 ceremonies). Both are correct, but はたち is the one JLPT listening sections actually use in natural dialogue — don't be thrown by not hearing にじゅっさい.
