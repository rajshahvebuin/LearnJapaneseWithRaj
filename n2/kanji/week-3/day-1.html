2 Quiz / Exercise Section

正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。 Two separate 練習 blocks (4 items each), one per printed page. Answers confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, p.4 (PDF index 164).

練習 — p.44 (1日目 左)

Q1

「お客様のおかけになった番号は（a. 現在　b. 現代）使われておりません。」"O-kyaku-sama no o-kake ni natta bangou wa (a. genzai / b. gendai) tsukawarete orimasen."

Full sentence: 「お客様のおかけになった番号は現在使われておりません。」"O-kyaku-sama no o-kake ni natta bangou wa genzai tsukawarete orimasen."
EN: "The number you have dialed is not currently in use."
HI: "आपने जो नंबर डायल किया है वह वर्तमान में उपयोग में नहीं है।"
GU: "તમે ડાયલ કરેલો નંબર હાલમાં ઉપયોગમાં નથી."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a現在genzaicurrently, at presentवर्तमान मेंહાલમાં
b現代gendaithe modern era (wrong sense of "now")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 現在 means "the present moment" and fits an automated phone message; 現代 means "the modern age/era" as a historical period, not "right now."

Q2

「（a. 細かい　b. 細い）お金が要るんですが、両替してくれませんか。」"(a. Komakai / b. Hosoi) okane ga irun desu ga, ryougae shite kuremasen ka."

Full sentence: 「細かいお金が要るんですが、両替してくれませんか。」"Komakai okane ga irun desu ga, ryougae shite kuremasen ka."
EN: "I need small change — could you exchange this for me?"
HI: "मुझे छुट्टे पैसे चाहिए, क्या आप बदल देंगे?"
GU: "મારે છૂટા પૈસા જોઈએ છે, શું તમે બદલી આપશો?"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a細かいkomakaismall change (coins)छुट्टे पैसेછૂટા પૈસા
b細いhosoiphysically thin/slender (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

「（a. 金額　b. 金利）が低いときに、お金を借りたほうがいいですね。」"(a. Kingaku / b. Kinri) ga hikui toki ni, okane o karita hou ga ii desu ne."

Full sentence: 「金利が低いときに、お金を借りたほうがいいですね。」"Kinri ga hikui toki ni, okane o karita hou ga ii desu ne."
EN: "It's better to borrow money when interest rates are low, isn't it."
HI: "ब्याज दर कम होने पर पैसा उधार लेना बेहतर है, है ना?"
GU: "વ્યાજ દર ઓછો હોય ત્યારે પૈસા ઉધાર લેવા સારા, ખરું ને?"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a金額kingakua sum of money (wrong sense — not a rate)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

読んだ本の内容を（a. 要約　b. 要点）する。Yonda hon no naiyou o (a. youyaku / b. youten) suru.

Full sentence: 読んだ本の内容を要約する。Yonda hon no naiyou o youyaku suru.
EN: To summarize the contents of the book one read.
HI: पढ़ी हुई किताब की विषय-वस्तु का सारांश बनाना।
GU: વાંચેલા પુસ્તકના વિષયવસ્તુનો સારાંશ બનાવવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a要約youyakuto summarize (verb する)सारांश बनानाસારાંશ બનાવવો
b要点youtenan essential point (a noun that doesn't take する this way)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's realia (bills, receipts, forms) naturally pairs up several same-kanji, different-nuance words:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
曲げるmageru To bend something Transitive — an agent bends an object. "Bend the wire," "twist the rules."
曲がるmagaru To turn/curve Intransitive — the subject itself turns or curves. "Turn right at the corner," "the road curves."
汚すyogosu To soil, make dirty Transitive — standard grammar has this acting on an object, though this book's own short gloss prints "become dirty" for it. "He soiled his shirt with ink."
汚れるyogoreru To become dirty Intransitive — the subject becomes dirty on its own; the book's own short gloss prints "make dirty/pollute" for it, the reverse of the standard textbook pairing. "My shirt got dirty."
様sama Honorific name suffix (everyday) The default polite suffix for anyone's name, in speech or writing. Addressing a letter, a customer, anyone by name.
殿dono Honorific name suffix (formal/official) Reserved for formal or official documents (government notices, certificates) — not casual use. An official letter, a certificate of merit.
細いhosoi Thin, slender Describes physical shape/width. "A thin street," "thin arms."
細かいkomakai Fine, detailed, small denomination Describes fineness of detail, or small change/coins. "Small change," "fine print/details."
Usage caution (❶): 様子 (ようす, "an appearance/situation") is flagged by the book as a reading needing particular attention — don't confuse the compound reading ようす with the individual readings ヨウ/さま listed for 様 elsewhere in the same entry.
