JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 3 — よむ① (Yomu ① — Read ①)

4日目 — ゴミの分別 (Gomi no bunbetsu — Rubbish classification and separation)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.50–51 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan). 14 kanji: #223–236.
Theme
A neighborhood association's ゴミ出しのルール (rubbish disposal rules) notice board — one continuous illustrated sign spanning both pages: burnable/landfill collection days on the left, then can/bottle/paper/plastic recycling days and bulky-rubbish rules on the right.
Answer key
Confirmed from the book's [別冊] 解答・解説 answer booklet, booklet p.5 (PDF index 165) — headers 「4日目　左 (p.50)」and 「4日目　右 (p.51)」.
Notes
This day's kanji are not clustered by shared component/reading (unlike N1 Kanji) — they're grouped by which half of the notice board they appear in, exactly as the book lays them out.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — a special/irregular reading not predictable from the on/kun readings shown
☞[number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book — informational only
Scene

A notice board at a Takara City apartment building explains the rubbish sorting rules.

Left half: 必ず、たから市指定のゴミ袋に入れて指定の曜日に集積所に出してください。Kanarazu, Takara-shi shitei no gomibukuro ni irete shitei no youbi ni shuusekijo ni dashite kudasai.
毎週　月・水・金曜日　燃やせるゴミ（紙くず、生ゴミ、木の葉や枝　等）Maishuu getsu, sui, kin-youbi — moyaseru gomi (kamikuzu, namagomi, ki no ha ya eda nado)
毎週　木曜日　埋め立てゴミ（茶わん、皿、貝がら　等）Maishuu moku-youbi — umetate gomi (chawan, sara, kaigara nado)

Right half: 第1・3・5火曜日　カン類／第2・4火曜日　ビン類Dai ichi, san, go kayoubi — kan-rui / dai ni, yon kayoubi — bin-rui

雑誌、古新聞、ダンボール等の資源ゴミは、子ども会等、資源回収団体へお出しください。粗大ゴミは個別有料収集です。Zasshi, furushinbun, danboru nado no shigen gomi wa, kodomokai nado, shigen kaishuu dantai e o-dashi kudasai. Sodai gomi wa kobetsu yuuryou shuushuu desu.

EN: "Please always put rubbish in Takara City's designated bags and put it out at the collection point on the designated day. Mon/Wed/Fri = burnable rubbish (paper scraps, food waste, leaves/branches); Thu = landfill rubbish (bowls, plates, shells). 1st/3rd/5th Tue = cans, 2nd/4th Tue = bottles; every Tue = other paper containers/wrapping, every Thu = other plastic containers/wrapping. Bulky rubbish is collected individually for a fee." — HI: कचरा हमेशा नगर-निर्धारित थैलों में डालें और तय दिन पर संग्रह स्थल पर रखें; कैन/बोतल/काग़ज़/प्लास्टिक और बड़ी वस्तुओं के लिए अलग-अलग नियम हैं। — GU: કચરો હંમેશાં શહેર-નિર્ધારિત થેલીમાં નાખો અને નક્કી દિવસે સંગ્રહસ્થાને મૂકો; કેન/બોટલ/કાગળ/પ્લાસ્ટિક અને મોટી વસ્તુઓ માટે અલગ નિયમો છે.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the kanji in hiragana. Two 練習 sets — left page (p.50) and right page (p.51), 4 items each. Answers confirmed from [別冊] booklet p.5.

練習 — 4日目 左 (p.50)

Q1

雪が（a.保って　b.積もって）花だんが埋まってしまった。Yuki ga (a. tamotte / b. tsumotte) hanadan ga umatte shimatta.

Full sentence: 雪が積もって花だんが埋まってしまった。Yuki ga tsumotte hanadan ga umatte shimatta.
EN: The snow piled up and the flower bed ended up buried.
HI: बर्फ़ जमा हो गई और फूलों की क्यारी दब गई।
GU: બરફ જમા થયો અને ફૂલોનો ક્યારો દટાઈ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a保ってtamottemaintain, keep (wrong verb for snow)
b積もってtsumottepiled up

Why: Snow accumulating is 積もる; 保つ means "to maintain/keep (a state)," not physically pile up. Confirmed: 別冊 booklet p.5, 「4日目 左 (p.50)」①b.

Q2

落ち葉や木の枝は（a.燃やせる　b.埋め立て）ゴミになります。Ochiba ya ki no eda wa (a. moyaseru / b. umetate) gomi ni narimasu.

Full sentence: 落ち葉や木の枝は燃やせるゴミになります。Ochiba ya ki no eda wa moyaseru gomi ni narimasu.
EN: Fallen leaves and tree branches become burnable rubbish.
HI: गिरे हुए पत्ते और पेड़ की टहनियाँ जलाए जा सकने वाला कचरा हैं।
GU: ખરી પડેલાં પાંદડાં અને ઝાડની ડાળીઓ સળગાવી શકાય તેવો કચરો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a燃やせるmoyaseruburnable
b埋め立てumetatelandfill (this scene's rule puts them under burnable, per the notice board)

Why: Per this notice board's own rule, leaves/branches are grouped under 燃やせるゴミ, not 埋め立てゴミ. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 左」②a.

Q3

この洗剤を使うときは必ずゴム（a.手袋　b.足袋）をしてください。Kono senzai o tsukau toki wa kanarazu gomu (a. tebukuro / b. tabi) o shite kudasai.

Full sentence: この洗剤を使うときは必ずゴム手袋をしてください。Kono senzai o tsukau toki wa kanarazu gomu-tebukuro o shite kudasai.
EN: Always wear rubber gloves when using this cleanser.
HI: इस क्लीनर का उपयोग करते समय हमेशा रबर के दस्ताने पहनें।
GU: આ ક્લીનરનો ઉપયોગ કરતી વખતે હંમેશાં રબરના મોજાં પહેરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a手袋tebukurogloves (for hands)
b足袋tabitabi (traditional footwear — wrong body part)

Why: ゴム手袋 (rubber gloves) protect the hands while cleaning; 足袋 are worn on the feet. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 左」③a.

Q4

植物から自動車の（a.原料　b.燃料）を作る。Shokubutsu kara jidousha no (a. genryou / b. nenryou) o tsukuru.

Full sentence: 植物から自動車の燃料を作る。Shokubutsu kara jidousha no nenryou o tsukuru.
EN: Automobile fuel is made from plants.
HI: पौधों से कार का ईंधन बनाया जाता है।
GU: છોડમાંથી કારનું બળતણ બનાવવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a原料genryouraw material (too generic here)
b燃料nenryoufuel

Why: The sentence names a specific end product — 自動車の燃料 (car fuel) — so 燃料 fits; 原料 is the broader "raw material" a step earlier in production. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 左」④b.

練習 — 4日目 右 (p.51)

Q5

（a.衣　b.器）がきれいだと、料理もおいしく見える。(a. I / b. Ki) ga kirei da to, ryouri mo oishiku mieru.

Full sentence: 器がきれいだと、料理もおいしく見える。Utsuwa ga kirei da to, ryouri mo oishiku mieru.
EN: When the tableware is nice, the food looks more delicious too.
HI: अगर बर्तन सुंदर हों, तो खाना भी स्वादिष्ट लगता है।
GU: જો વાસણ સુંદર હોય, તો ખોરાક પણ સ્વાદિષ્ટ દેખાય છે.

a衣iclothing (wrong noun for tableware)
b器utsuwatableware, a container

Why: 器 (うつわ) is the word for the dish/container that holds food; 衣 is clothing and doesn't fit here. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 右 (p.51)」①b.

Q6

この時計はスイス（a.製　b.作）です。Kono tokei wa Suisu (a. sei / b. saku) desu.

Full sentence: この時計はスイス製です。Kono tokei wa Suisu-sei desu.
EN: This watch is made in Switzerland.
HI: यह घड़ी स्विट्ज़रलैंड निर्मित है।
GU: આ ઘડિયાળ સ્વિટ્ઝર્લેન્ડમાં બનેલી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a製-seimade in/of...
b作-sakunot the fixed suffix for country-of-origin

Why: 〜製 is the fixed suffix for "made in [country]" (スイス製 = "Swiss-made"). Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 右」②a.

Q7

（a.雑用　b.雑音）がひどくて電話がよく聞こえません。(a. Zatsuyou / b. Zatsuon) ga hidokute denwa ga yoku kikoemasen.

Full sentence: 雑音がひどくて電話がよく聞こえません。Zatsuon ga hidokute denwa ga yoku kikoemasen.
EN: The static is so bad I can't hear the phone well.
HI: शोर इतना ज़्यादा है कि फ़ोन ठीक से सुनाई नहीं देता।
GU: ઘોંઘાટ એટલો બધો છે કે ફોન બરાબર સંભળાતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a雑用zatsuyouodd jobs (wrong noun for a sound)
b雑音zatsuonstatic, noise

Why: A phone-line interference sound is 雑音; 雑用 refers to miscellaneous chores, not a sound. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 右」③b.

Q8

この町ではリサイクルのため、第2土曜日に古新聞・古雑誌などを（a.回収　b.包装）します。Kono machi de wa risaikuru no tame, dai-ni doyoubi ni furushinbun, furuzasshi nado o (a. kaishuu / b. housou) shimasu.

Full sentence: この町ではリサイクルのため、第2土曜日に古新聞・古雑誌などを回収します。Kono machi de wa risaikuru no tame, dai-ni doyoubi ni furushinbun, furuzasshi nado o kaishuu shimasu.
EN: In this town, old newspapers and magazines are collected for recycling on the second Saturday.
HI: इस शहर में, रीसाइक्लिंग के लिए दूसरे शनिवार को पुराने अख़बार-पत्रिकाएँ इकट्ठी की जाती हैं।
GU: આ શહેરમાં, રિસાયક્લિંગ માટે બીજા શનિવારે જૂનાં છાપાં-સામયિકો ભેગાં કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a回収kaishuucollection, recovery
b包装housouwrapping (wrong verb for gathering rubbish)

Why: Gathering up recyclables is 回収する; 包装する means "to wrap," unrelated to a collection drive. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「4日目 右」④a.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day pairs several transitive/intransitive verb forms and same-kanji reading traps that the practice exercises above directly test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
積もるtsumoru Pile up (intransitive) Something (snow, dust) accumulates on its own — no direct object. "Snow piled up on the flower bed."
積むtsumu Load, stack (transitive) Someone actively loads/stacks an object. "Load the boxes onto the truck."
埋めるumeru Bury (transitive) Someone buries something. "Bury the time capsule."
埋まるumaru Be buried (intransitive) Something ends up buried, often as a side effect (e.g. of snow piling up). "The flower bed got buried [by the snow]."
包装housou Wrapping (on-yomi ソウ) Regular on-yomi ソウ for 装, used for physically wrapping an object. "Gift wrapping," "packaging."
衣装ishou Costume, dress (on-yomi ショウ, ❶ marked) Same kanji 装, but this word shifts to the irregular reading ショウ instead of ソウ. "A stage costume," "traditional dress."
器-ki (on-yomi) ...-器 in compounds: 食器, 受話器, 楽器 On-yomi キ used inside two-kanji compound words. Building a compound noun for an instrument/appliance.
器utsuwa (❶) A container; a person's caliber Kun-yomi うつわ, used standalone — the book flags this as a reading needing special attention. "A fine piece of tableware," or figuratively "the caliber of a leader."
Special-reading trap: ⊙足袋 (たび, tabi socks) and ⊙紅葉 (もみじ, a maple tree — vs. the regular reading こうよう for "autumn leaves" using the same 紅葉 spelling) are both irregular readings the book flags with its special-reading mark. Don't assume 紅葉 always reads こうよう — context (a general noun for "autumn foliage" vs. a specific "maple tree") decides which reading is meant.
