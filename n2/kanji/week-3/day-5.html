JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 3 — よむ① (Yomu ① — Read ①)

5日目 — いろいろな通知① (Iroiro na tsuuchi ① — Various notices ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.52–53 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan). 13 kanji: #237–249.
Theme
Two real-life posted notices: an evacuation-drill announcement (避難訓練のお知らせ) on the left page, and a water-supply-suspension announcement (断水のお知らせ) on the right page.
Answer key
Confirmed from the book's [別冊] 解答・解説 answer booklet, booklet p.5 (PDF index 165) — headers 「5日目　左 (p.52)」and 「5日目　右 (p.53)」.
Notes
Both notices are formal 掲示 (posted announcement) style — note the polite request forms (〜ください) and the set phrase なお、〜の場合は (as a side note, in the case of...) used to announce a contingency.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — a special/irregular reading not predictable from the on/kun readings shown
☞[number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book — informational only
Scene

Left page — 避難訓練のお知らせ(Hinan kunren no oshirase — Notification of evacuation drills)
地震に備えて避難訓練を行います。できるだけご参加ください。なお、雨天の場合は延期となります。Jishin ni sonaete hinan kunren o okonaimasu. Dekiru dake go-sanka kudasai. Nao, uten no baai wa enki to narimasu.

Right page — 断水のお知らせ(Dansui no oshirase — Notification of water supply suspension)
配水管工事のため、下記の日時に断水となります。大変ご迷惑をおかけしますが、ご理解とご協力をお願いいたします。日時　平成XX年11月20日午後11時〜21日午前5時Haisuikan kouji no tame, kaki no nichiji ni dansui to narimasu. Taihen go-meiwaku o okake shimasu ga, go-rikai to go-kyouryoku o onegai itashimasu. Nichiji: Heisei XX-nen juuichi-gatsu hatsuka gogo juuichi-ji kara nijuuichi-nichi gozen go-ji.

EN: "We will hold an evacuation drill in preparation for earthquakes. Please participate as much as possible. Note that in case of rain it will be postponed." / "Due to water-pipe construction, the water supply will be suspended at the date/time below. We're very sorry for the inconvenience, and appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Date: Heisei XX Nov 20, 11pm – Nov 21, 5am." — HI: भूकंप की तैयारी के लिए निकासी अभ्यास होगा, बारिश में स्थगित होगा। जल-पाइप के काम के कारण पानी बंद रहेगा। — GU: ધરતીકંપની તૈયારી માટે સ્થળાંતર કવાયત યોજાશે, વરસાદમાં મુલતવી રહેશે. પાણીની પાઇપલાઇનના કામને લીધે પાણી પુરવઠો બંધ રહેશે.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the kanji in hiragana. Two 練習 sets — left page (p.52) and right page (p.53), 4 items each. Answers confirmed from [別冊] booklet p.5.

練習 — 5日目 左 (p.52)

Q1

この（a.練習　b.訓練）問題はあまり難しくない。Kono (a. renshuu / b. kunren) mondai wa amari muzukashikunai.

Full sentence: この練習問題はあまり難しくない。Kono renshuu mondai wa amari muzukashikunai.
EN: This practice problem isn't very difficult.
HI: यह अभ्यास प्रश्न ज़्यादा कठिन नहीं है।
GU: આ અભ્યાસ પ્રશ્ન બહુ અઘરો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a練習renshuupractice
b訓練kunrentraining/drill (too formal a collocation with 問題)

Why: 練習問題 ("practice problem/exercise") is the fixed collocation; 訓練問題 isn't natural. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 左 (p.52)」①a.

Q2

「届かないので（a.延期　b.延長）コードを持ってきてください。」"Todokanai node (a. enki / b. enchou) koodo o motte kite kudasai."

Full sentence: 「届かないので延長コードを持ってきてください。」"Todokanai node enchou koodo o motte kite kudasai."
EN: "It doesn't reach, so please bring an extension cord."
HI: "यह पहुँच नहीं रहा, इसलिए एक एक्सटेंशन कॉर्ड ले आइए।"
GU: "આ પહોંચતું નથી, તેથી એક એક્સ્ટેન્શન કોર્ડ લાવો."
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a延期enkipostponement (about time, not a cord)
b延長enchouextension

Why: 延長コード is the fixed term for "extension cord" (physically lengthening a cable); 延期 only applies to postponing a schedule. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 左」②b.

Q3

「あ！（a.火事　b.地震）だ！　揺れてる！」"A! (a. Kaji / b. Jishin) da! Yureteru!"

Full sentence: 「あ！地震だ！揺れてる！」"A! Jishin da! Yureteru!"
EN: "Ah! It's an earthquake! It's shaking!"
HI: "अरे! भूकंप है! हिल रहा है!"
GU: "અરે! ધરતીકંપ છે! ધ્રૂજી રહ્યું છે!"
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a火事kajia fire (doesn't cause shaking)
b地震jishinan earthquake

Why: "揺れてる" (it's shaking) only makes sense with 地震; a fire (火事) doesn't shake things. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 左」③b.

Q4

材料がやわらかくなったら、さとうを（a.加えます　b.凍えます）。Zairyou ga yawarakaku nattara, satou o (a. kuwaemasu / b. kogoemasu).

Full sentence: 材料がやわらかくなったら、さとうを加えます。Zairyou ga yawarakaku nattara, satou o kuwaemasu.
EN: Once the ingredients have softened, add the sugar.
HI: जब सामग्री नरम हो जाए, तो चीनी मिलाएँ।
GU: જ્યારે સામગ્રી નરમ થાય, ત્યારે ખાંડ ઉમેરો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a加えますkuwaemasuadd
b凍えますkogoemasufreeze/go numb with cold (unrelated verb, similar sound)

Why: Adding an ingredient is 加える; 凍える means "to feel numb with cold," a different verb that just sounds superficially similar. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 左」④a.

練習 — 5日目 右 (p.53)

Q5

（a.迷信　b.迷惑）メールが何通も届いて困っている。(a. Meishin / b. Meiwaku) meeru ga nantsuu mo todoite komatte iru.

Full sentence: 迷惑メールが何通も届いて困っている。Meiwaku meeru ga nantsuu mo todoite komatte iru.
EN: I'm having trouble because so much spam mail keeps arriving.
HI: बहुत सारे स्पैम ईमेल आने से परेशानी हो रही है।
GU: ઘણા બધા સ્પામ ઈમેલ આવવાથી તકલીફ થઈ રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a迷信meishinsuperstition (wrong noun for email)
b迷惑meiwakua nuisance

Why: 迷惑メール is the fixed term for "spam/junk mail" (a nuisance email); 迷信 means "superstition," unrelated. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 右 (p.53)」①b.

Q6

（a.平日　b.平成）のこの時間は道路が込んでいる。(a. Heijitsu / b. Heisei) no kono jikan wa douro ga konde iru.

Full sentence: 平日のこの時間は道路が込んでいる。Heijitsu no kono jikan wa douro ga konde iru.
EN: The roads are crowded at this time on weekdays.
HI: कार्यदिवसों में इस समय सड़कें भरी रहती हैं।
GU: કામકાજના દિવસોમાં આ સમયે રસ્તાઓ ભરેલા હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a平日heijitsuweekdays
b平成Heiseithe Heisei era (an era name, not a day-type)

Why: 平日 describes a type of day (weekday, as opposed to weekend); 平成 is a calendar era name and can't modify "this time [of day]" this way. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 右」②a.

Q7

「（a.駐車　b.禁煙）お断り」"(a. Chuusha / b. Kin'en) o-kotowari"

Full sentence: 「駐車お断り」"Chuusha o-kotowari"
EN: "No Parking" (sign)
HI: "पार्किंग वर्जित" (सूचना पट्ट)
GU: "પાર્કિંગ પ્રતિબંધિત" (સૂચનાપાટિયું)
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a駐車chuushaparking
b禁煙kin'enno smoking (a real sign, but doesn't fit this context's expected answer)

Why: 「〜お断り」is a common fixed sign format meaning "[X] is refused/prohibited here" — 駐車お断り = "No Parking," the standard posted sign this exercise is testing. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 右」③a.

Q8

大学に入学（a.願書　b.明細）を郵送する。Daigaku ni nyuugaku (a. gansho / b. meisai) o yuusou suru.

Full sentence: 大学に入学願書を郵送する。Daigaku ni nyuugaku gansho o yuusou suru.
EN: I mail my university admission application.
HI: विश्वविद्यालय में प्रवेश आवेदन-पत्र डाक से भेजना।
GU: યુનિવર્સિટીમાં પ્રવેશ માટેનું અરજીપત્રક ટપાલ દ્વારા મોકલવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a願書ganshoa (written) application
b明細meisaian itemized statement (wrong document type)

Why: 入学願書 is the fixed term for a university admission application form; 明細 refers to a detailed statement/breakdown (e.g. a bill), a different kind of document. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「5日目 右」④a.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's practice sentences are built entirely around these pairs — study them side by side:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
延期enki Postponement Moving an event's date/time later. "The drill will be postponed if it rains."
延長enchou Extension Lengthening something (time or a physical object like a cord). "An extension cord," "extending a deadline."
加えるkuwaeru Add (transitive) Someone actively adds an ingredient/item. "Add the sugar."
加わるkuwawaru Join, be added (intransitive) Something/someone joins on its own — no direct object. "A new member joined the team."
断るkotowaru Refuse (kun-yomi, everyday verb) The common verb "to decline/refuse," also seen in fixed signs like 駐車お断り. "I refused the offer," or a posted "not allowed" sign.
断定dantei A decision, assertion (on-yomi, formal noun) A firm conclusion/statement, used in writing or logical argument. "It's hard to make a firm assertion yet."
平ら(な)taira (na) Flat, even Kun-yomi describing a physically flat surface. "A flat road," "level ground."
平等byoudou Equality Irregular on-yomi ビョウ (not the expected ヘイ) in this one compound — 平 has four readings total in this list (ヘイ／ビョウ／たいら／ひら). "Gender equality," "equal opportunity."
Multi-reading trap: 平 alone carries four readings in this lesson — ヘイ (平成, 平気, 平日), ビョウ (平等 only), たいら (平ら), and ひら (平仮名). 男女平等 (gender equality) is a common JLPT trap because test-takers default to へい and answer へいとう instead of the correct びょうどう — exactly what booklet-confirmed test question 9 on Day 7 checks.
