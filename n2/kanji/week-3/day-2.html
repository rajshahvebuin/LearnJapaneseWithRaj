練習 — p.46 (2日目 左)

Q1

5月の（a. 週休　b. 連休）に両親を旅行に連れていく。Go-gatsu no (a. shuukyuu / b. renkyuu) ni ryoushin o ryokou ni tsurete iku.

Full sentence: 5月の連休に両親を旅行に連れていく。Go-gatsu no renkyuu ni ryoushin o ryokou ni tsurete iku.
EN: I'll take my parents on a trip during May's consecutive holidays.
HI: मैं मई की लगातार छुट्टियों में अपने माता-पिता को यात्रा पर ले जाऊँगा।
GU: હું મેની સળંગ રજાઓમાં મારા માતાપિતાને પ્રવાસ પર લઈ જઈશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a週休shuukyuua weekly day off (not this compound)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b連休renkyuuconsecutive holidaysलगातार छुट्टियाँસળંગ રજાઓ

Why: 連休 names a run of several consecutive days off (like Golden Week in May), matching "taking a trip"; 週休 refers to a fixed weekly rest day (like a two-day weekend), not a multi-day holiday block.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The courier-slip realia this day naturally clusters several same-kanji, opposite-transitivity or opposite-nuance pairs:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
届けるtodokeru To deliver (something) Transitive — an agent (courier) delivers an object. "The courier delivers the parcel."
届くtodoku To arrive, to reach Intransitive — the item itself arrives. "The parcel arrived," "my voice reaches you."
冷蔵reizou Refrigeration (cool, not frozen) Keeps items cold but above freezing. Fresh food, dairy, vegetables.
冷凍reitou Freezing Keeps items below freezing, solid. Ice cream, frozen meat.
担当tantou Being in charge of (a role) Names who is responsible for a whole task/area. "She's in charge of sales."
分担buntan Sharing out a workload Names how one big task is split among several people. "We divided up the chores."
〜等~tou / ~nado Et cetera, and so on Attaches to a list to mean "and others" — read とう in formal writing, など more casually. "Apples, oranges, etc."
等しいhitoshii Equal An adjective describing two things being the same in value/amount — an entirely separate reading of the same kanji. "A is equal to B."
Usage caution (❶): Both ❶等しい (ひとしい, "equal") and ❶気配 (けはい, "a sign/indication") are flagged by the book as readings needing particular attention — neither reading is predictable from 等's or 配's more common on-yomi (トウ／ハイ) shown just above them in the same entry, so memorize each compound as its own unit.
