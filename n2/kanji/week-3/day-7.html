1 問題1 — reading, from a receipt (4 items)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best reading for the underlined word from 1–4. Source realia: a hospital 領収書 (receipt) from 東西大学病院, with boxed blanks [1]–[4] over 領収, 殿, 番号, and 金額.

1

領収書の領収という言葉。Ryoushuusho no ryoushuu to iu kotoba. (word on a receipt's heading)

EN: "Ryoushuu" as printed on the heading of a receipt (領収書).
HI: रसीद के शीर्षक पर छपा "りょうしゅう" (प्राप्ति) शब्द।
GU: રસીદના મથાળા પર છપાયેલો "りょうしゅう" (પ્રાપ્તિ) શબ્દ.
#Reading
1りょしゅ
2りょしゅう
3りょうしゅ
4りょうしゅう (ryoushuu)

Why: 領収 (receipt of payment) is read りょうしゅう. Confirmed: 別冊 booklet p.6, 「7日目 (p.56〜57)」問題1 [1]4.

2

患者氏名欄の「内田　清　殿」。Kanja shimei-ran no "Uchida Kiyoshi -dono".

EN: The honorific "-dono" attached to the patient's name, "Uchida Kiyoshi -dono," on the patient-name line.
HI: मरीज़ के नाम पंक्ति पर "内田　清" नाम के साथ लगा सम्मानसूचक "殿"।
GU: દર્દીના નામની લાઇન પર "内田　清" નામ સાથે લખેલું માનવાચક "殿".
#Reading
1さま
2どの (dono)
3くん
4でん

Why: 殿 as a formal honorific suffix on official documents (like a hospital receipt) is read どの. Confirmed: booklet p.6, 問題1 [2]2.

6 [コラム] 記号的な漢字 未⇔済 (Kigouteki na kanji: mi ⇔ sai — "Ideographic kanji: not-yet ⇔ done")

This week's closing column (printed p.58) explains how some kanji function almost like symbols/icons rather than ordinary words:

GroupWordsMeaning
未mi ⇔ 済sai 未読midoku ⇔ 送信済みsoushin-zumi "未" marks "not yet [done]" (e.g. unread) and "済" marks "already done" (e.g. sent) — used almost like status icons/stamps, plus as prefixes.
昨saku ⇔ 明-／翌yoku ⇔ 前zen 昨日・昨年・昨夜 (kinou = ⊙昨日, kyonen = 昨年, sakuban/yuube = 昨夜) ⇔ 明日・来年・明晩 (ashita/asu = ⊙明日, rainen, myouban) ／ 翌日・翌年・翌朝 (yokujitsu, yokunen/yokutoshi, yokuasa) ⇔ 前日・前年・前夜 (zenjitsu, zennen, zen'ya) 昨〜 = "last..." and 翌〜 = "the following..." work like time-direction prefixes, paired against 明〜/来〜 ("next...") and 前〜 ("the previous..."). (☞晩585)
Counters 〜冊-satsu (☞冊617)・〜本-hon・〜個-ko・〜枚-mai・〜着-chaku・〜台-dai・〜軒-ken Counter suffixes attached to a number, each tied to a shape-category: notebooks(冊)/pens(本)/apples(個)/cards(枚)/suits(着)/TVs(台)/houses or shops(軒).
毒doku 中毒chuudoku、気の毒(な)ki no doku (na)、消毒shoudoku A single kanji (毒, "poison") whose meaning is unmistakable at a glance — e.g. on a warning label, functioning like an ideogram/icon.
涙namida／笑warau, emu 涙namida（＝😢）、笑うwarau・笑顔egao（＝😊） The book notes that kanji like 涙 (tears) and 笑 (laugh/smile) can substitute for kaomoji (emoticons) in casual writing — e.g. 「テストだめだったよ〜(涙) でも、またガンバル！(笑)」in place of (T_T) / (^_^).
