練習 — p.48 (3日目 左)

Q1

この薬は風邪によく（a. 効く　b. 聞く）。Kono kusuri wa kaze ni yoku (a. kiku / b. kiku).

Full sentence: この薬は風邪によく効く。Kono kusuri wa kaze ni yoku kiku.
EN: This medicine is very effective against colds.
HI: यह दवा ज़ुकाम पर बहुत असर करती है।
GU: આ દવા શરદી પર સારી અસર કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a効くkikuto be effectiveअसर करनाઅસર કરવી
b聞くkikuto hear/listen (same reading, unrelated meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Medicine "working" on an illness is 効く (be effective); 聞く shares the exact same reading きく but means "to hear/listen," a classic homophone trap.

Q2

外国の人たちと（a. 交流　b. 交換）するのは楽しいです。Gaikoku no hitotachi to (a. kouryuu / b. koukan) suru no wa tanoshii desu.

Full sentence: 外国の人たちと交流するのは楽しいです。Gaikoku no hitotachi to kouryuu suru no wa tanoshii desu.
EN: Interacting/exchanging with people from other countries is fun.
HI: विदेशी लोगों के साथ मेल-जोल करना मज़ेदार है।
GU: વિદેશી લોકો સાથે આદાનપ્રદાન કરવું આનંદદાયક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a交流kouryuu(cultural/social) exchange, interactionमेल-जोलઆદાનપ્રદાન
b交換koukanexchange (of objects, wrong sense for socializing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 交流する describes people/cultures interacting and mingling; 交換する is specifically swapping objects or information one-for-one, not socializing.

Q3

今までに一度も負けたことのない（a. 相手　b. 相当）に負けてしまった。Ima made ni ichido mo maketa koto no nai (a. aite / b. soutou) ni makete shimatta.

Full sentence: 今までに一度も負けたことのない相手に負けてしまった。Ima made ni ichido mo maketa koto no nai aite ni makete shimatta.
EN: I ended up losing to an opponent I had never once lost to before.
HI: मैं ऐसे प्रतिद्वंद्वी से हार गया जिससे पहले कभी नहीं हारा था।
GU: હું એવા હરીફ સામે હારી ગયો જેની સામે પહેલાં ક્યારેય હાર્યો નહોતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a相手aitean opponentप्रतिद्वंद्वीહરીફ
b相当soutoucorresponding to (wrong sense — not a person)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Only 相手 names a person you compete against (an opponent); 相当 means "equivalent to/corresponding to" a quantity or level, not a competitor.

Q4

卵10個入りパック百円！ お一人様1パック（a. 参り　b. 限り）！Tamago jukko-iri pakku hyaku-en! O-hitori-sama ichi pakku (a. mairi / b. kagiri)!

Full sentence: 卵10個入りパック百円！ お一人様1パック限り！Tamago jukko-iri pakku hyaku-en! O-hitori-sama ichi pakku kagiri!
EN: A pack of 10 eggs for 100 yen! Limit one pack per customer!
HI: 10 अंडों का पैक सिर्फ़ 100 येन में! प्रति व्यक्ति सिर्फ़ 1 पैक!
GU: 10 ઈંડાનું પેક ફક્ત 100 યેનમાં! વ્યક્તિદીઠ ફક્ત 1 પેક!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a参りmairigo/come, humble form (wrong sense)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b限りkagiria limit, restriction ("...limit")सीमा, अधिकतमમર્યાદા, મહત્તમ

Why: A sale sign capping purchases per customer uses 「〜限り」("limited to..."); 参り is the humble verb "to go/come," unrelated to a purchase restriction.

練習 — p.49 (3日目 右)

Two vocabulary callouts printed directly above this page's 練習 in the booklet: 仕上がり日shiagari-bi — 完成する日 (the date a job will be completed); 〜かねます~kanemasu — 〜ことが難しい、できない (a polite way of saying "we're unable to...").

Q5

子どもの勉強のことは（a. 担任　b. 責任）の先生にお任せしています。Kodomo no benkyou no koto wa (a. tannin / b. sekinin) no sensei ni o-makase shite imasu.

Full sentence: 子どもの勉強のことは担任の先生にお任せしています。Kodomo no benkyou no koto wa tannin no sensei ni o-makase shite imasu.
EN: We leave our child's studies to the homeroom teacher.
HI: बच्चे की पढ़ाई का ज़िम्मा कक्षा-अध्यापक पर छोड़ा हुआ है।
GU: બાળકના અભ્યાસની જવાબદારી વર્ગશિક્ષકને સોંપી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a担任tanninthe teacher in charge (homeroom teacher)कक्षा-अध्यापकવર્ગશિક્ષક
b責任sekininresponsibility (a noun, not a person)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 担任の先生 ("the homeroom/in-charge teacher") names the specific teacher responsible for a class; 責任 is the abstract noun "responsibility" itself, not a person you can entrust something to directly like this.

Q6

この薬を飲んでください。1週間（a. 経験　b. 経過）をみましょう。Kono kusuri o nonde kudasai. Isshuukan (a. keiken / b. keika) o mimashou.

Full sentence: この薬を飲んでください。1週間経過をみましょう。Kono kusuri o nonde kudasai. Isshuukan keika o mimashou.
EN: Please take this medicine. Let's observe how things progress for a week.
HI: यह दवा लीजिए। एक हफ़्ते तक स्थिति पर नज़र रखते हैं।
GU: આ દવા લો. એક અઠવાડિયું પરિસ્થિતિ પર નજર રાખીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a経験keikenexperience (wrong sense — not what's being watched)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b経過keikaprogress/course (of a condition over time)प्रगति, हालપ્રગતિ

Q7

HI: काम बहुत ज़्यादा है। कर्मचारियों का बोझ हल्का करना ज़रूरी है।
GU: કામ ખૂબ વધારે છે. કર્મચારીઓનો ભાર ઓછો કરવો જરૂરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a負担futana burden (that can be made light/heavy)बोझભાર
b担当tantoubeing in charge of (a role, not a weight to lighten)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's slips (point card, gift certificate, dry-cleaning claim) surface several same-sound or same-kanji traps:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
効くkiku To be effective A medicine, method, or measure produces its intended result. "This medicine works well."
聞くkiku To hear, to listen, to ask Exact same reading きく, completely different kanji and meaning. "Listen to music," "ask a question."
相手aite An opponent, the other party Reading あい comes straight from 相's own kun-yomi. "My opponent in a match," "the other person in a conversation."
相撲sumou Sumo wrestling A jukujikun (special whole-word reading) — すもう cannot be built from 相 or 撲's individual readings. Naming the sport itself.
負けるmakeru To lose Describes losing a match/contest. "I lost the game."
負うou To bear, carry, be responsible for Same kanji 負, but means shouldering a burden or responsibility, not losing. "Bear the responsibility," "carry a wound (傷を負う)."
経過keika Progress, course (of time/a condition) Something ongoing that is observed over a period. "Watch the patient's progress for a week."
過去kako The past A completed period of time, viewed as already over. "In the past, this town was a fishing village."
Special reading (⊙): 相撲 (すもう) is marked with this book's special-reading symbol because すもう is a jukujikun — a reading assigned to the whole word, not built from 相's individual on/kun-yomi (ソウ／ショウ／あい) or 撲's own reading. Memorize 相撲 as one indivisible unit, the same way one-off readings like 一日＝ついたち are memorized.
