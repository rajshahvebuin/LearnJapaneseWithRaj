JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 3 — よむ① (Yomu ① — Read ①)

6日目 — いろいろな通知② (Iroiro na tsuuchi ② — Various notices ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.54–55 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan). 14 kanji: #250–263.
Theme
A health-checkup results envelope (検診結果) with its "how to open" and "no abnormalities found" notes on the left page, and paired relocation notices (移転のお知らせ — a formal office move; 引っ越しました！— a casual "we moved!" postcard) on the right page.
Answer key
Confirmed from the book's [別冊] 解答・解説 answer booklet, booklet p.5–6 (PDF index 165–166) — headers 「6日目　左 (p.54)」and 「6日目　右 (p.55)」; the right page's full answer sentences ③④ appear at the top of booklet p.6 as a continuation.
Notes
Note the contrast in register: 移転のお知らせ is a formal business notice (事務所が下記に移転しました), while 引っ越しました！ is a friendly personal postcard to friends/neighbors (お近くにお越しの際は是非お立ち寄りください) — same event, two very different tones.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
⊙ = 特別な読み — a special/irregular reading not predictable from the on/kun readings shown
☞[number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book — informational only
Scene

Left page — 検診結果(Kenshin kekka — Results of health checkups)
検診結果は内側にあります。開封方法 [illustration of how to open the sealed flap]Kenshin kekka wa uchigawa ni arimasu. Kaifuu houhou.
受診者各位　異常ありませんでした。来年も必ず受けてください。Jushinsha kakui. Ijou arimasen deshita. Rainen mo kanarazu ukete kudasai.

Right page — 移転のお知らせ ／ 引っ越しました！(Iten no oshirase / Hikkoshimashita! — Relocation notice / We moved!)
事務所が下記に移転しました。電話番号の変更はありません。〒186-XXXXJimusho ga kaki ni iten shimashita. Denwa bangou no henkou wa arimasen.
引っ越しました！　郊外で周りは畑しかありませんが、空気がきれいです。お近くにお越しの際は是非お立ち寄りください。Hikkoshimashita! Kougai de mawari wa hatake shika arimasen ga, kuuki ga kirei desu. O-chikaku ni o-koshi no sai wa zehi o-tachiyori kudasai.

EN: "Your checkup results are inside the flap. [Opening instructions.] To all patients: no abnormalities were found. Please be sure to come again next year." / "Our office has relocated to the address below; our phone number is unchanged." / "We moved! It's in the suburbs and there's nothing around but fields, but the air is clean. If you're ever nearby, please do stop by." — HI: जांच परिणाम अंदर हैं; कोई असामान्यता नहीं मिली। कार्यालय नए पते पर स्थानांतरित हुआ; फ़ोन नंबर वही है। हम शिफ़्ट हो गए! — GU: તપાસનાં પરિણામો અંદર છે; કોઈ અસાધારણતા મળી નથી. ઓફિસ નવા સરનામે ખસેડાઈ; ફોન નંબર એ જ છે. અમે શિફ્ટ થયા!

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the kanji in hiragana. Two 練習 sets — left page (p.54) and right page (p.55), 4 items each. Answers confirmed from [別冊] booklet p.5–6.

練習 — 6日目 左 (p.54)

Q1

手紙に写真を（a.開封　b.同封）する。Tegami ni shashin o (a. kaifuu / b. doufuu) suru.

Full sentence: 手紙に写真を同封する。Tegami ni shashin o doufuu suru.
EN: I enclose a photo with the letter.
HI: पत्र के साथ एक फ़ोटो संलग्न करना।
GU: પત્ર સાથે એક ફોટો બિડાણ કરવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a開封kaifuuunsealing/opening (opposite direction of action)
b同封doufuuenclosing

Why: Putting a photo inside a letter you're sending is 同封する ("enclose"); 開封する means "to unseal/open" a letter you've received — the reverse action. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 左 (p.54)」①b.

Q2

最近、全国（a.各位　b.各地）で異常な事件が起こっている。Saikin, zenkoku (a. kakui / b. kakuchi) de ijou na jiken ga okotte iru.

Full sentence: 最近、全国各地で異常な事件が起こっている。Saikin, zenkoku kakuchi de ijou na jiken ga okotte iru.
EN: Unusual incidents have been happening all over the country recently.
HI: हाल ही में देश भर में असामान्य घटनाएँ हो रही हैं।
GU: તાજેતરમાં દેશભરમાં અસાધારણ ઘટનાઓ બની રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a各位kakui"to all concerned" — addresses people, not places
b各地kakuchieverywhere, many places

Why: 各地 names locations ("in various places nationwide"); 各位 is a form of address used at the end of a letter/announcement (e.g. 関係者各位, "to whom it may concern"), not a place word. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 左」②b.

Q3

朝、（a.果物　b.貨物）を食べると体にいいそうだ。Asa, (a. kudamono / b. kamotsu) o taberu to karada ni ii sou da.

Full sentence: 朝、果物を食べると体にいいそうだ。Asa, kudamono o taberu to karada ni ii sou da.
EN: They say eating fruit in the morning is good for your health.
HI: कहते हैं कि सुबह फल खाना सेहत के लिए अच्छा होता है।
GU: કહેવાય છે કે સવારે ફળ ખાવું આરોગ્ય માટે સારું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a果物kudamonofruit
b貨物kamotsufreight/cargo (not edible, similar sound to 果物)

Why: 果物 (fruit) is edible and fits "eating in the morning"; 貨物 (cargo/freight) is an unrelated homophone-adjacent word for shipped goods. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 左」③a.

Q4

運動靴のひもがほどけそうですよ。しっかり（a.結んで　b.積んで）。Undougutsu no himo ga hodoke-sou desu yo. Shikkari (a. musunde / b. tsunde).

Full sentence: 運動靴のひもがほどけそうですよ。しっかり結んで。Undougutsu no himo ga hodoke-sou desu yo. Shikkari musunde.
EN: Your sneaker laces look like they're about to come undone. Tie them tightly.
HI: आपके जूतों के फीते खुलने वाले हैं। कसकर बाँध लीजिए।
GU: તમારા બૂટની દોરી છૂટવાની તૈયારીમાં છે. મજબૂત રીતે બાંધો.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a結んでmusundetie
b積んでtsundeload/stack (wrong verb for shoelaces)

Why: Shoelaces are 結ぶ (tied); 積む means to load/stack objects, not applicable to laces. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 左」④a.

練習 — 6日目 右 (p.55)

Q5

会社の経理（a.事務　b.任務）を担当しています。Kaisha no keiri (a. jimu / b. ninmu) o tantou shite imasu.

Full sentence: 会社の経理事務を担当しています。Kaisha no keiri jimu o tantou shite imasu.
EN: I'm in charge of the company's accounting office work.
HI: मैं कंपनी के लेखा-दफ़्तर का काम संभालता/संभालती हूँ।
GU: હું કંપનીના હિસાબી ઓફિસકામનું સંચાલન કરું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a事務jimuoffice work
b任務ninmua duty/mission (too weighty a word for routine desk work)

Why: 経理事務 is the set phrase for routine "accounting/clerical work"; 任務 suggests a specific assigned mission or duty, a different register. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 右 (p.55)」①a.

Q6

旅券事務所でパスポートの（a.延期　b.更新）をする。Ryoken jimusho de pasupooto no (a. enki / b. koushin) o suru.

Full sentence: 旅券事務所でパスポートの更新をする。Ryoken jimusho de pasupooto no koushin o suru.
EN: I renew my passport at the passport office.
HI: पासपोर्ट कार्यालय में पासपोर्ट का नवीनीकरण करवाना।
GU: પાસપોર્ટ ઓફિસમાં પાસપોર્ટનું નવીનીકરણ કરાવવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a延期enkipostponement (wrong noun for a document renewal)
b更新koushina renewal

Why: A passport is 更新する ("renewed"); 延期 only applies to postponing a scheduled event/date, not renewing a document. Confirmed: booklet p.5, 「6日目 右」②b.

Q7

女子更衣室は2階に（a.移り　b.写り）ました。Joshi kouishitsu wa nikai ni (a. utsuri / b. utsuri) mashita.

Full sentence: 女子更衣室は2階に移りました。Joshi kouishitsu wa nikai ni utsurimashita.
EN: The women's changing room has moved to the second floor.
HI: महिलाओं का चेंजिंग रूम दूसरी मंज़िल पर स्थानांतरित हो गया है।
GU: મહિલાઓનો ચેન્જિંગ રૂમ બીજા માળે ખસેડાયો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a移りutsurimoved, relocated (移る)
b写りutsuribe photographed/copied (写る — same sound, unrelated meaning)

Why: A room relocating uses 移る; 写る is the homophone verb for "to be photographed/reflected/copied," a classic same-sound kanji trap. Confirmed: booklet p.5–6, 「6日目 右」③a.

Q8

引っ越した家の（a.周り　b.配り）は緑が多い。Hikkoshita ie no (a. mawari / b. kubari) wa midori ga ooi.

Full sentence: 引っ越した家の周りは緑が多い。Hikkoshita ie no mawari wa midori ga ooi.
EN: There's a lot of greenery around the house we moved to.
HI: जिस घर में हम गए, उसके आस-पास बहुत हरियाली है।
GU: અમે જ્યાં રહેવા ગયા તે ઘરની આસપાસ ઘણી હરિયાળી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglish
a周りmawariaround, surroundings
b配りkubaridistributing (配る — not a noun for "surroundings")

Why: "The area around a house" is 周り; 配り is the stem of 配る ("to distribute/hand out"), unrelated. Confirmed: booklet p.5–6, 「6日目 右」④a.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Same-sound and same-topic kanji this day cluster around "moving" and "office" vocabulary:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
移るutsuru Move, relocate (intransitive) A place, role, or person shifts location on its own. "The changing room moved to the 2nd floor."
写るutsuru Be photographed/reflected (intransitive, different kanji, same sound) Something appears in a photo, mirror, or copy — a total unrelated meaning that only sounds like 移る. "I came out well in that photo."
開封kaifuu Unsealing, opening Action performed by the letter's recipient. "Open this envelope to see your results."
同封doufuu Enclosing Action performed by the letter's sender, before mailing. "I've enclosed a photo with this letter."
引っ越すhikkosu Move house Relocating one's home/residence. "We moved to the suburbs."
乗り越すnorikosu Go past your stop Same kanji 越す ("to cross over/past"), but riding a train beyond your intended station — an entirely different everyday meaning. "I fell asleep and rode past my station."
各地kakuchi Everywhere, many places Refers to locations. "Incidents nationwide, in various places."
各位kakui To all concerned Refers to people being addressed, typically at the top/end of a notice. "関係者各位" — a memo's salutation to "all concerned parties."
Reading trap: 果物 is marked with the book's special-reading symbol (⊙) because くだもの cannot be predicted from 果's regular kun-yomi は-たす — it's a fixed, irregular reading you must memorize as a whole word, not sound out kanji-by-kanji.
