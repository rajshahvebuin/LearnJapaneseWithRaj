Notes
This day walks through the paperwork and posted rules around sitting a university entrance exam (受験): the documents you must submit, then the warnings printed on the exam-room instruction sheet. Each of the two printed pages has its own realia scene, its own kanji table, and its own 4-item 練習 — the book does not merge them into one combined exercise.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)

☞ [number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book — informational only

①明日のハイキングには必ず（　）とお弁当を持参すること。Asu no haikingu ni wa kanarazu (___) to obentou o jisan suru koto.
Be sure to bring a [___] and a boxed lunch on tomorrow's hike.		a. 水筒suitou　b. 筒tsutsua — 水筒suitou (a canteen)
②この試験はマークシートではなく、（　）式です。Kono shiken wa maakushiito de wa naku, (___) shiki desu.
This exam isn't a mark-sheet format, it's a [___] format.		a. 前述zenjutsu　b. 記述kijutsub — 記述kijutsu (written-answer)
③この論文は（　）が非常に良い。Kono ronbun wa (___) ga hijou ni ii.
This thesis's [___] is very good.		a. 構内kounai　b. 構成kouseib — 構成kousei (structure)
④彼は（　）が弱くて、お酒がやめられない。Kare wa (___) ga yowakute, osake ga yamerarenai.
His [___] is weak, so he can't quit drinking.		a. 志望shibou　b. 意志ishib — 意志ishi (willpower)

Why: ① 水筒 is the everyday word for a carried drink container; 筒 alone just means "a tube," too generic for a hiking checklist. ② 記述式 ("written-response format") contrasts with マークシート (mark-sheet); 前述 means "mentioned earlier in this document," not a test format. ③ 構成 ("composition/structure") describes how a thesis is organized; 構内 is a physical campus/premises, which a thesis can't have. ④ 意志が弱い ("weak-willed") is the fixed phrase for lacking self-control; 志望 is "a wish/aspiration," which doesn't collocate with 弱い in this sense.

受験の注意 (Juken no chuui)

Exam-day cautions

Realia caption, box 1: 30分以上の遅刻は認めません。受験票を忘れた人は「仮受験票」の発行を申し出てください。(30-pun ijou no chikoku wa mitomemasen. Jukenhyou o wasureta hito wa "kari-jukenhyou" no hakkou o moushidete kudasai. — Latecomers of 30+ minutes will not be admitted. Anyone who forgot their exam ticket, please request a "provisional exam ticket.")
Box 2 (注意): 1. 受験票を机の上に置いてください。2. この冊子は持ち帰ってはいけません。解答はすべてマークシートに書きます。マークがはみ出たり、薄いと採点されません。HBやB等の濃い鉛筆で例のように正しくマークします。(1. Place your exam ticket on your desk. 2. This booklet may not be taken home. Write all answers on the mark sheet. Marks that stray outside the circle or are too faint will not be graded. Mark correctly as shown, using a dark HB or B pencil.)

QSentenceOptionsAnswer
①夏休みの自由研究で植物（　）をした。Natsuyasumi no jiyuu kenkyuu de shokubutsu (___) o shita.
For my summer-vacation independent research, I did plant [___].		a. 採集saishuu　b. 採点saitena — 採集saishuu (collecting)
②前を走っている車には（　）練習中と書いてある。Mae o hashitte iru kuruma ni wa (___) renshuu-chuu to kaite aru.
The car driving ahead has "[___], practicing" written on it.		a. 仮免許kari-menkyo　b. 仮採用kari-saiyoua — 仮免許kari-menkyo (provisional license)
③兄は新聞（　）です。Ani wa shinbun (___) desu.
My older brother is a newspaper [___].		a. 筆者hissha　b. 記者kishab — 記者kisha (reporter)
④ノートは（　）の引き出しに入っています。Nooto wa (___) no hikidashi ni haitte imasu.
The notebook is in the [___]'s drawer.		a. 机tsukue　b. 筆fudea — 机tsukue (desk)

Why: ① 植物採集 ("plant collecting") is a set phrase for a nature-study project; 採点 is "grading," which a student doesn't do to plants. ② 仮免許 ("provisional/learner's license") is what a learner-driver's car displays; 仮採用 means "provisional employment," unrelated to driving. ③ 記者 ("reporter/journalist") is the job title for someone who writes for a newspaper; 筆者 just means "the author of this piece," a role, not an occupation. ④ A drawer (引き出し) belongs to a desk (机); 筆 is a writing brush, which doesn't have a drawer.

2 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji cluster in same-sounding or visually-similar pairs that this day's own 練習 sections test directly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
記述kijutsu Written-response (format) Describes an exam/answer format where you write out the answer. "This exam is 記述式, not multiple choice."
前述zenjutsu Aforementioned Refers back to something already stated earlier in the same document. "As noted above (前述のとおり)..."
構内kounai Campus, grounds A physical area/premises. "Parking is available on campus (構内)."
構成kousei Composition, structure How something (an essay, a team) is organized/put together. "This thesis's structure (構成) is excellent."
志望shibou A wish, aspiration What you want (a school, a job) — a noun naming the goal. "My first-choice (志望) university."
意志ishi Will, willpower Inner resolve/self-control, not tied to a specific goal. "He has weak willpower (意志が弱い)."
採点saiten Grading, marking Scoring someone's answers/work. A teacher grading exam papers.
採集saishuu Collecting, gathering Physically gathering specimens (plants, insects). A nature-study collecting project.
仮定katei Assumption, hypothesis A supposed premise used in reasoning. "Let's assume (仮定して) that X is true."
❶仮kari Temporary, provisional Describes a stand-in/interim arrangement (a ticket, a license, a residence). "A provisional exam ticket (仮受験票)."
Exam trap: 仮 (かり) carries the day's theme — 仮受験票 (provisional exam ticket), 仮免許 (provisional driver's license), 仮の住まい (temporary residence) all use this same "stand-in, not the real/final thing" sense. Don't confuse it with 仮名 (かな), which is a fixed word for the kana syllabary and uses an irregular reading (⊙) unrelated to this かり pattern — 仮名 is never read *かめい in this sense.
