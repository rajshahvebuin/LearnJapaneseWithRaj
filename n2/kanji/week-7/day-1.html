Scene

A convenience-store style "Help Wanted" flyer taped to a shop door, and a language school's student-recruitment poster on a noticeboard.

求人情報 (Kyuujin jouhou): スタッフ急募！　簡単な仕事です。資格：年齢・経験不問。普通自動車免許あればなお可。給与：時給800円〜。勤務：土日祝日できる方歓迎。勤務日・時間は相談に応じます。応募：電話連絡の上、履歴書ご持参ください。面接日は追って連絡します。Sutaffu kyuubo! Kantan na shigoto desu. Shikaku: nenrei, keiken fumon. Futsuu jidousha menkyo areba nao ka. Kyuuyo: jikyuu happyaku-en~. Kinmu: dojitsu shukujitsu dekiru kata kangei. Kinmubi, jikan wa soudan ni oujimasu. Oubo: denwa renraku no ue, rirekisho gojisan kudasai. Mensetsubi wa otte renraku shimasu.
("Staff wanted urgently! Easy work. Qualifications: no age/experience requirement — a regular driver's license is a plus. Pay: from ¥800/hour. Hours: weekend/holiday availability welcome, negotiable. To apply: call first, then bring your résumé. Interview date to be advised later.")

生徒募集 (Seito boshuu): 青山日本語学校　昼間部専門課程募集中。奨学金制度有り。短期集中講座受付中。初級コース　中級コース　上級コース。基礎からていねいに指導します。Aoyama Nihongo Gakkou. Chuukanbu senmon katei boshuuchuu. Shougakukin seido ari. Tanki shuuchuu kouza uketsuke-chuu. Shokyuu koosu, chuukyuu koosu, joukyuu koosu. Kiso kara teinei ni shidou shimasu.
("Aoyama Japanese School — now recruiting for its daytime specialist course. Scholarship system available. Now accepting applications for short intensive courses. Beginner / intermediate / advanced courses. We teach carefully from the basics.")

求人・募集 (kyuujin, boshuu) — this day's theme is the vocabulary of "Help Wanted" job flyers and course/student recruitment posters, the everyday reading you meet on a shop door or a school bulletin board.

Q1

店のドアに「アルバイト求む」と、（a. 求人　b. 応募）の張り紙がしてある。Mise no doa ni "arubaito motomu" to, (a. kyuujin / b. oubo) no harigami ga shite aru.

Full sentence: 店のドアに「アルバイト求む」と、求人の張り紙がしてある。Mise no doa ni "arubaito motomu" to, kyuujin no harigami ga shite aru.
EN: On the shop door there's a "Help Wanted" job-recruitment poster.
HI: दुकान के दरवाज़े पर "पार्ट-टाइम काम चाहिए" लिखा हुआ भर्ती का पोस्टर लगा है।
GU: દુકાનના દરવાજા પર "પાર્ટ-ટાઇમ જોઈએ છે" લખેલું ભરતીનું પોસ્ટર લગાવેલું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a求人kyuujinjob recruitment (fits the flyer itself)भर्तीભરતી
b応募ouboan application (the act of applying, not the flyer)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 求人の張り紙 ("a recruitment poster") describes the flyer itself; 応募 names the act of applying that a jobseeker later performs, not the notice on the door.

Full sentence: 時給800円です。Jikyuu happyaku-en desu.
EN: It's ¥800 per hour.
HI: यह प्रति घंटा 800 येन है।
GU: તે કલાકના 800 યેન છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a支給shikyuua payment (the act of paying out)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b時給jikyuuhourly wageप्रति घंटा वेतनકલાકદીઠ પગાર

Q3

EN: You need the company's permission to park here.
HI: यहाँ पार्किंग करने के लिए कंपनी की अनुमति ज़रूरी है।
GU: અહીં પાર્કિંગ કરવા માટે કંપનીની પરવાનગી જરૂરી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a免許menkyoa license (a formal document, wrong fit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: કામના કલાકો ચર્ચા દ્વારા નક્કી કરી શકાય છે.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's own kanji table already sets a trap (課程 vs 過程) and pairs up on/kun readings of the same kanji (許・導). Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
課程katei A course, curriculum Homophone trap: read かてい, but built from 課 (lesson/section) — about a structured program of study. "The daytime specialist course," an academic curriculum.
過程katei A process, course (of events) Same reading かてい, built from 過 (pass through) instead — about how something unfolds over time, not a study program. "The process of development," "in the course of negotiations."
許可kyoka Permission (on-yomi, noun) The formal, general noun for permission granted by an authority. "You need the company's permission."
許すyurusu To permit / to forgive (kun-yomi, verb) Same kanji 許, but the verb form — can mean either "allow" or "forgive" depending on context. "I can't forgive that," "smoking is not allowed here."
導入dounyuu Introduction (of equipment/systems, on-yomi) Bringing something new into a system or workplace. "Introduce new machinery/software."
導くmichibiku To lead, guide (kun-yomi) Same kanji 導, but about guiding a person or outcome, not installing something. "Lead a team to success."
Exam trap: 課程 and 過程 are a perfect same-reading (かてい), different-kanji, different-meaning pair — exactly like Q7's 制限 vs 過程 contrast. Whenever a listening or reading question gives you かてい, check whether it's about a study curriculum (課程) or an unfolding process (過程) before picking kanji.
