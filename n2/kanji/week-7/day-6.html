練習 — 6日目 右 (p.119)

正しいほうに○をつけ、文の漢字にひらがなで読みを書きなさい。Circle the correct option and write the reading of the underlined kanji in hiragana. Answers confirmed from 別冊 p.13.

QSentenceOptionsAnswer
①（　）記念に校庭に木を植える。(___) kinen ni koutei ni ki o ueru.
To plant a tree in the schoolyard to commemorate [___].		a. 率業(not a real word)　b. 卒業sotsugyoub — 卒業sotsugyou (graduation)
②今日は良いお天気に（　）春のような陽気でした。Kyou wa ii otenki ni (___) haru no you na youki deshita.
Today, [___] with fine weather, it felt spring-like.		a. 恵まれてmegumarete　b. 晴れてharetea — 恵まれてmegumarete (blessed with)
③疲れたときはちょっと休んだほうが（　）が上がる。Tsukareta toki wa chotto yasunda hou ga (___) ga agaru.
When you're tired, resting a bit raises your [___].		a. 利率riritsu　b. 能率nouritsub — 能率nouritsu (efficiency)
④メガネが湯気で（　）何も見えない。Megane ga yuge de (___) nani mo mienai.
My glasses [___] from the steam and I can't see anything.		a. 曇ってkumotte　b. 雲って(not a verb)a — 曇ってkumotte (fogged up)
⑤昔の人は、便利な機械がない代わりに（　）を使って生活した。Mukashi no hito wa, benri na kikai ga nai kawari ni (___) o tsukatte seikatsu shita.
People long ago lived using their [___] instead of convenient machines.		a. 知恵chie　b. 予想yosoua — 知恵chie (wisdom)
⑥都心のマンションから郊外の（　）に引っ越した。Toshin no manshon kara kougai no (___) ni hikkoshita.
I moved from a downtown apartment to a [___] in the suburbs.		a. 一戸建てikkodate　b. 家庭kateia — 一戸建てikkodate (detached house)

Why: ① 卒業 ("graduation") is a real word using 卒; 率業 isn't a word at all — a pure look-alike trap between 卒(ソツ) and 率(リツ/ソツ), which share the ソツ reading and a similar shape. ② 恵まれる ("to be blessed with," taking 天気に as its object) is the fixed pattern for "blessed with good weather"; 晴れて alone can't take 天気に this way. ③ 能率が上がる ("efficiency goes up") is the set phrase for productivity; 利率 is a bank's "interest rate," irrelevant to resting. ④ 曇る is a real verb ("to fog/cloud up"); 雲る isn't a verb in standard Japanese — 雲 is only ever a noun ("clouds"), another 訓読み-alike trap. ⑤ 知恵を使う ("to use one's wisdom") fits "living without modern machines"; 予想 ("forecast/anticipation") isn't something you "use" to live. ⑥ 一戸建て ("a detached, single-family house") is the natural contrast to a downtown マンション; 家庭 means "a household/family," not a building type.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Visually near-identical kanji and one classic reading trap from this day:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
卒業sotsugyou Graduation 卒 (ソツ) — the real, dictionary word. Finishing school.
率業(invalid) — not a word — 率 (リツ/ソツ) looks similar and even shares the ソツ reading, but this compound doesn't exist. Never — a distractor only.
曇るkumoru To become cloudy / fog up (verb) 曇 is usable as a verb stem with る. "My glasses fogged up (曇った)."
雲kumo Clouds (noun only) 雲 never takes る as a native verb — 雲る is not standard Japanese. "There are clouds in the sky (空に雲がある)."
利率riritsu Interest rate A banking/finance term. "The savings account's interest rate (利率)."
能率nouritsu Efficiency How productively work gets done. "Resting improves efficiency (能率が上がる)."
Special reading: 吹雪 (⊙ふぶき, "snowstorm/blizzard") does not build predictably from 吹く(ふく, "to blow") + 雪(ゆき, "snow") — the compound's reading is fused and must simply be memorized, the same irregular-reading pattern flagged elsewhere in this book with the ⊙ mark.
