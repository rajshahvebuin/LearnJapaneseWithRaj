Q1

梅雨の時期は（a. 蒸し　b. 干し）暑い。Tsuyu no jiki wa (a. mushi / b. hoshi) atsui.

Full sentence: 梅雨の時期は蒸し暑い。Tsuyu no jiki wa mushiatsui.
EN: The rainy season is hot and humid.
HI: बारिश के मौसम में उमस भरी गर्मी होती है।
GU: ચોમાસાની ઋતુમાં બફારાવાળી ગરમી હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a蒸し暑いmushiatsuihumid and hotउमस भराબફારાવાળું
b干し暑いhoshi-atsuinot a real word(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 蒸し暑い ("steamy-hot," i.e. humid) is the fixed compound adjective for muggy weather; 干す means "to dry (in the sun)," and there is no such word as 干し暑い.

猫が家の（a. 屋根　b. 羽根）の上を歩いている。Neko ga ie no (a. yane / b. hane) no ue o aruite iru.

Full sentence: 猫が家の屋根の上を歩いている。Neko ga ie no yane no ue o aruite iru.
EN: A cat is walking along the roof of the house.
HI: एक बिल्ली घर की छत पर चल रही है।
GU: એક બિલાડી ઘરની છત પર ચાલી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a屋根yaneroofछतછત
b羽根hanea feather/wing (unrelated homophone-like near-match)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A cat walks on the 屋根 ("roof"); 羽根 means "feather" or "wing," a completely different word that only looks similar in kanji shape.

GU: અમે નવા કર્મચારીઓની સ્વાગત પાર્ટી ઇઝાકાયા (જાપાની પબ)માં કરી રહ્યા છીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a酒屋sakayaa liquor store (you buy from, not a venue to party in)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b居酒屋izakayaa Japanese pubजापानी पबજાપાની પબ

Why: A welcome party is held at an 居酒屋 (a pub where you sit and drink/eat); 酒屋 is a retail liquor store you buy bottles from, not a venue to hold a party in.

海辺で拾った（a. 貝　b. 具）でアクセサリーを作る。Umibe de hirotta (a. kai / b. gu) de akusesarii o tsukuru.

Full sentence: 海辺で拾った貝でアクセサリーを作る。Umibe de hirotta kai de akusesarii o tsukuru.

HI: मैं समुद्र तट पर बीने हुए सीपों से गहने बनाता/बनाती हूँ।
GU: હું દરિયાકિનારે વીણેલા છીપલાંમાંથી ઘરેણાં બનાવું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Full sentence: この価格に消費税は含まれておりません。Kono kakaku ni shouhizei wa fukumarete orimasen.
EN: Consumption tax is not included in this price.
HI: इस क़ीमत में उपभोक्ता कर शामिल नहीं है।
GU: આ કિંમતમાં વપરાશ કર (ટેક્સ) સામેલ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a込まれてkomaretefrom 込む/込める (crowded/packed in — wrong verb here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Menu and ingredient-label vocabulary bundles several near-synonyms and transitivity pairs. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
酒屋sakaya Liquor store A shop where you buy alcohol to take away. "Buy a bottle of sake at the liquor store."
居酒屋izakaya Japanese pub Same 酒 kanji, but a venue where you sit, drink, and eat. "Hold a welcome party at an izakaya."
含むfukumu To be included, to contain Often used in the passive 含まれる ("is included") to describe what a product contains. "This price does not include tax."
含めるfukumeru To include (something) Transitive: the speaker actively adds something to a set. "Including the leader, there were five people."
脂肪／脂質shibou / shishitsu Fat / lipids 脂肪 is the general everyday word for body/food fat; 脂質 is the more technical nutrition-label term for the lipid content. 脂肪 in casual speech, 脂質 on a nutrition facts panel.
油脂yushi Fats and oils (as raw material) Refers to fats/oils as an ingredient category (e.g. 植物性油脂 "vegetable oil"), not the nutrient measurement itself. Reading an ingredients list, not the nutrition table.
Reading trap: This label's own ingredient line uses 植物性油脂 (shokubutsusei yushi, "vegetable oil/fat") right next to the nutrition table's 脂質 (shishitsu, "lipids") — two different 脂-words doing two different jobs on the same package: one names what's inside, the other measures how much fat is in it. Don't read them as the same word just because they share 脂.
