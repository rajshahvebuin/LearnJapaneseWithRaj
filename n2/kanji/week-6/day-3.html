Q1

週末は富士山の近くの温泉（a. 家　b. 宿）に泊まる予定です。Shuumatsu wa Fujisan no chikaku no onsen (a. ie / b. yado) ni tomaru yotei desu.

Full sentence: 週末は富士山の近くの温泉宿に泊まる予定です。Shuumatsu wa Fujisan no chikaku no onsen-yado ni tomaru yotei desu.
EN: This weekend I plan to stay at a hot-spring inn near Mt. Fuji.
HI: इस सप्ताहांत फ़ूजी पर्वत के पास एक झरनेवाली सराय में ठहरने की योजना है।
GU: આ સપ્તાહાંતે ફૂજી પર્વત નજીકની ગરમ પાણીના ઝરાવાળી ધર્મશાળામાં રોકાવાની યોજના છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a家iehouse (doesn't form 温泉家)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b宿yadoan innसराय, धर्मशालाધર્મશાળા, સરાય

Why: 温泉宿 ("a hot-spring inn") is the set compound for lodging built around a hot spring; 家 means "house" and isn't used to form this word.

Q2

冬季オリンピック選手の帰国を空港で（a. 出迎える　b. 迎えに行く）。Touki Orinpikku senshu no kikoku o kuukou de (a. demukaeru / b. mukae ni iku).

Full sentence: 冬季オリンピック選手の帰国を空港で出迎える。Touki Orinpikku senshu no kikoku o kuukou de demukaeru.
EN: To greet the returning Winter Olympics athletes at the airport.
HI: शीतकालीन ओलंपिक के खिलाड़ियों की स्वदेश वापसी पर हवाई अड्डे पर उनका स्वागत करना (लेने जाना)।
GU: શિયાળુ ઓલિમ્પિકના ખેલાડીઓની સ્વદેશ વાપસી પર એરપોર્ટ પર તેમને લેવા જવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a出迎えるdemukaeruto go and meet/greet (arriving people)लेने जाना, स्वागत को जानाલેવા જવું, સ્વાગત કરવા જવું
b迎えに行くmukae ni ikuto go to meet (grammatically fine, but not the fixed single verb the book tests here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 出迎える is the single verb specifically for "going out to meet/welcome someone who is arriving" (matching 出迎え in this day's own list); 迎えに行く is a valid phrase but is the analytical two-part version, not the word being tested.

Q3

この町は自然に囲まれた緑（a. 豊かな　b. 富む）ところです。Kono machi wa shizen ni kakomareta midori (a. yutaka na / b. tomu) tokoro desu.

Full sentence: この町は自然に囲まれた緑豊かなところです。Kono machi wa shizen ni kakomareta midori yutaka na tokoro desu.
EN: This town is a lushly green place surrounded by nature.
HI: यह शहर प्रकृति से घिरा हुआ हरा-भरा (समृद्ध हरियाली वाला) स्थान है।
GU: આ શહેર કુદરતથી ઘેરાયેલું હરિયાળીથી ભરપૂર સ્થળ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a豊かなyutaka naabundant, rich (in greenery)समृद्ध, भरपूरસમૃદ્ધ, ભરપૂર
b富むtomuto be wealthy in (grammatically needs 〜に富む, wrong form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 緑豊かな ("lushly green") is the set adjectival phrase describing a place rich in greenery; 富む needs the particle に (「緑に富む」) to work grammatically and doesn't attach directly to 緑 the way 豊かな does.

明日から（a. 1泊　b. 1晩）2日の旅行に行きます。Ashita kara (a. ippaku / b. hitoban) futsuka no ryokou ni ikimasu.

Full sentence: 明日から1泊2日の旅行に行きます。Ashita kara ippaku futsuka no ryokou ni ikimasu.

HI: कल से मैं एक रात-दो दिन की यात्रा पर जा रहा/रही हूँ।
GU: આવતીકાલથી હું એક રાત-બે દિવસની સફર પર જાઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a1泊ippakuone night's stay (the fixed counter for trip itineraries)एक रात का ठहरावએક રાત્રિનું રોકાણ
b1晩hitobanone night (a plain duration, not the itinerary counter)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

お金が必要になったので、定期預金を（a. 解約　b. 解決）する。Okane ga hitsuyou ni natta node, teiki yokin o (a. kaiyaku / b. kaiketsu) suru.

EN: Since I needed money, I'm cancelling my fixed-term deposit.
HI: पैसे की ज़रूरत पड़ने पर मैंने अपनी सावधि जमा (फिक्स्ड डिपॉज़िट) रद्द कर दी।
GU: પૈસાની જરૂર પડતાં મેં મારી ફિક્સ ડિપોઝિટ રદ કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji cover housing and hospitality vocabulary with several verb/noun pairs and one same-kanji two-reading trap:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
迎えるmukaeru To meet, welcome (plain verb) The general verb for going to receive/welcome a person or a moment in time. "To welcome guests," "to greet the new year."
出迎えdemukae Going to meet someone (noun) The noun form specifically for going out (e.g. to a station/airport) to receive an arriving person. "Airport pickup," "a welcoming party at the gate."
泊まるtomaru To stay overnight (plain verb) The everyday verb for spending the night somewhere. "I'm staying at a hotel tonight."
宿泊shukuhaku Lodging (formal noun) The formal/written noun used on forms, brochures, and ads for "accommodation." "Accommodation fee," "lodging facilities."
豊か（な）yutaka (na) Abundant, rich (in quality) Describes a rich, fulfilling quality — nature, life, emotion. "A town rich in greenery," "an emotionally rich life."
豊富（な）houfu (na) Abundant (in quantity/variety) Describes a large quantity or wide variety of something concrete. "Abundant experience," "a wide variety of dishes."
角kado A corner (kun-yomi, physical) A physical corner of a room, street, or building — the everyday reading. "Turn at the corner," "a corner room (角部屋)."
角度kakudo An angle (on-yomi, compound) The on-yomi reading カク only appears in compounds like 角度, 三角 — never standing alone for "corner." "From a different angle," "a 90-degree angle."
Reading trap: 角 has two unrelated-looking readings taught this day: かど (kun-yomi, "a corner" — as in the flyer's own 角部屋, "a corner room") and カク (on-yomi, only in compounds like 角度/三角). Don't read 角部屋 as かくべや. Similarly, ❶新米 has two completely different meanings depending on context — "new-harvest rice" or "a rookie/newcomer" — decide from context, not from the kanji alone.
