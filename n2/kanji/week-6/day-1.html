練習 — p.92 (スーパーのチラシ)

Q1

消費税は商品に（a. 均等　b. 平均）に課税される。Shouhizei wa shouhin ni (a. kintou / b. heikin) ni kazei sareru.

Full sentence: 消費税は商品に均等に課税される。Shouhizei wa shouhin ni kintou ni kazei sareru.
EN: Consumption tax is levied on goods uniformly/equally.
HI: उपभोग कर सभी वस्तुओं पर समान रूप से लगाया जाता है।
GU: વપરાશ કર બધી ચીજવસ્તુઓ પર સમાન રીતે લાગુ પડે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a均等kintouequally, uniformlyसमान रूप सेસમાન રીતે
b平均heikinan average (a noun, doesn't fit「に」+ manner here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 均等に describes tax applied the same way to everything; 平均 means "an average" (a calculated mean), which doesn't fit describing how tax is applied.

Q2

「広告が事実と異なり、（a. 心得　b. 納得）がいかない場合はこちらにご連絡を。」"Koukoku ga jijitsu to kotonari, (a. kokoroe / b. nattoku) ga ikanai baai wa kochira ni go-renraku o."

Full sentence: 広告が事実と異なり、納得がいかない場合はこちらにご連絡を。Koukoku ga jijitsu to kotonari, nattoku ga ikanai baai wa kochira ni go-renraku o.
EN: "If the advertisement differs from the facts and you're not satisfied, please contact us here."
HI: "यदि विज्ञापन तथ्यों से भिन्न हो और आपको संतोष न हो, तो कृपया यहां संपर्क करें।"
GU: "જો જાહેરાત હકીકતથી અલગ હોય અને તમને સંતોષ ન થાય, તો કૃપા કરીને અહીં સંપર્ક કરો."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a心得kokoroepreparedness/what one should know (wrong fit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b納得nattokubeing satisfied/convincedसंतोष, सहमतिસંતોષ, સંમતિ

Why: 「納得がいかない」("not convinced/satisfied") is the set phrase for a customer complaint; 心得 refers to knowledge one should be aware of, not a feeling of being satisfied.

Q3

「○○ランドの入場者数がすでに百万人を（a. 超え　b. 告げ）ました。」"○○ Rando no nyuujousha-suu ga sude ni hyakuman-nin o (a. koe / b. tsuge) mashita."

Full sentence: ○○ランドの入場者数がすでに百万人を超えました。○○ Rando no nyuujousha-suu ga sude ni hyakuman-nin o koemashita.
EN: "The number of visitors to ○○ Land has already exceeded one million."
HI: "○○ लैंड के आगंतुकों की संख्या पहले ही दस लाख को पार कर चुकी है।"
GU: "○○ લેન્ડની મુલાકાતીઓની સંખ્યા પહેલેથી જ દસ લાખને વટાવી ચૂકી છે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a超えkoeexceededपार कर गईવટાવી ગઈ
b告げtsugeinformed (wrong verb for a number)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 「数を超える」("to exceed a number") is the fixed collocation; 告げる means "to inform/announce" and takes a message as its object, not a number being surpassed.

Q4

世界で最も（a. 定価　b. 物価）の高い都市はどこでしょう。Sekai de mottomo (a. teika / b. bukka) no takai toshi wa doko deshou.

Full sentence: 世界で最も物価の高い都市はどこでしょう。Sekai de mottomo bukka no takai toshi wa doko deshou.
EN: "Which city in the world has the highest cost of living?"
HI: "दुनिया में सबसे ज़्यादा महंगाई वाला शहर कौन-सा है?"
GU: "દુનિયામાં સૌથી વધુ મોંઘવારી ધરાવતું શહેર કયું છે?"
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a定価teikaa fixed price (of one item, not a city-wide measure)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b物価bukkacost of living / general price levelमहंगाई दर, वस्तुओं की कीमतेंમોંઘવારી દર

Why: 物価 describes the general price level of a place (cost of living); 定価 is the fixed retail price of a single product, which doesn't describe a whole city.

子供を（a. 対象　b. 対照）としたテレビ番組を制作する。Kodomo o (a. taishou / b. taishou) to shita terebi bangumi o seisaku suru.

GU: બાળકોને લક્ષ્યમાં રાખીને ટીવી કાર્યક્રમ બનાવવો.

GU: કૃપા કરીને ધ્યાન રાખો — તમારો કોલેસ્ટ્રોલનો આંકડો વધી રહ્યો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Q7

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji cluster around price/value/quantity words that look alike or share a component — exactly what the 練習 above tests:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
価格kakaku A price The concrete amount charged for a specific good. "The price of this item is..."
価値kachi Value/worth An abstract sense of how much something is worth — shares 値 with 数値 but means "worth," not "a measured figure." "This book has great value," "worth the effort."
均等kintou Equality, evenly Describes equal distribution/treatment across items or people. "Tax applied equally to all goods."
均一kin'itsu Uniformity (one flat rate) Describes a single fixed value applied to everything — common in "100円均一" (everything ¥100) sales. Flat-rate pricing, e.g. a "everything's the same price" sale.
超えるkoeru To exceed, go over The general, more common verb for surpassing a number, limit, or barrier. "Visitors exceeded one million," "over the mountain pass."
超すkosu To cross, pass, exceed Near-synonym of 超える, but slightly more common for crossing a point in time or place ("〜を越す/超す"). "Pass the deadline," "get through the winter."
対象taishou An object/target (of study, a policy, an audience) Shares the kanji 象 with 印象 but the meanings are unrelated. "A program aimed at children," "the subject of the survey."
印象inshou An impression A subjective feeling left by something — unrelated to 対象 beyond sharing 象. "He made a good impression."
Reading trap: ❶均一 is read きんいつ, not きんいち — the 一 here keeps its alternate on-yomi いつ (as in 統一 とういつ), so don't default to the more common いち. Likewise ❶値 has two unrelated-looking kun readings: ね (in 値段, "price") and あたい (standalone "value") — the same kanji, read differently depending on whether it's a set compound or standing alone.
