5日目 — 文化財・展示 (Bunkazai / Tenji — Cultural Properties, Exhibits)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.100–101 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
14 kanji (#527–540) for visiting cultural sites: temple statues, national treasures, and a pagoda (p.100), then an art museum's exhibition rooms — paintings, prints, sculpture, publications (p.101).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (p.100, 4 items) and 練習 (p.101, 4 items). Answers confirmed from the [別冊] answer booklet: 「5日目　左 (p.100)」and 「5日目　右 (p.101)」, both on PDF index 171 (booklet's own p.11).

練習 — p.100 (文化財の展示)

Q1

このツアーの（　）コースにパリのエッフェル塔も入っています。Kono tsuā no (___) kōsu ni Pari no Efferu-tō mo haitte imasu.

Full sentence: このツアーの観光コースにパリのエッフェル塔も入っています。Kono tsuā no kankō kōsu ni Pari no Efferu-tō mo haitte imasu.
EN: The Eiffel Tower in Paris is also included in this tour's sightseeing course.
HI: इस टूर के भ्रमण मार्ग में पेरिस का एफ़िल टावर भी शामिल है।
GU: આ ટૂરના પ્રવાસ માર્ગમાં પેરિસનો એફિલ ટાવર પણ સામેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a観光kankōsightseeing (観光コース = a sightseeing route)पर्यटनપ્રવાસ
b観察kansatsuobservation — not a "course" you book(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 観光コース ("sightseeing course/route") is the fixed travel term; 観察 (observation, e.g. of nature or data) doesn't combine with コース.

Q2

この建物は（　）に指定されています。Kono tatemono wa (___) ni shitei sarete imasu.

Full sentence: この建物は国宝に指定されています。Kono tatemono wa kokuhō ni shitei sarete imasu.
EN: This building has been designated a national treasure.
HI: इस इमारत को राष्ट्रीय धरोहर घोषित किया गया है।
GU: આ ઈમારતને રાષ્ટ્રીય ધરોહર જાહેર કરવામાં આવી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a財産zaisanproperty/fortune — not something you're "designated" as(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b国宝kokuhōa national treasure (a formal designation)राष्ट्रीय धरोहरરાષ્ટ્રીય ધરોહર

Why: 国宝に指定される ("to be designated a national treasure") is the fixed legal/cultural phrase; 財産 (property/fortune) is a general asset, not a formal government designation.

Q3

渋谷駅の犬の（　）の前で待ち合わせしましょう。Shibuya-eki no inu no (___) no mae de machiawase shimashō.

Full sentence: 渋谷駅の犬の銅像の前で待ち合わせしましょう。Shibuya-eki no inu no dōzō no mae de machiawase shimashō.
EN: Let's meet in front of the dog's bronze statue at Shibuya station.
HI: शिबूया स्टेशन पर कुत्ते की कांस्य प्रतिमा के सामने मिलते हैं।
GU: શિબુયા સ્ટેશન પર કૂતરાની કાંસાની પ્રતિમા સામે મળીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a銅像dōzōa bronze statue (this is Hachikō's famous statue)कांस्य प्रतिमाકાંસાની પ્રતિમા
b仏像butsuzōa statue of Buddha — not a dog statue(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Shibuya's famous dog statue (Hachikō) is a 銅像 (bronze statue); 仏像 specifically means a statue depicting Buddha, which a dog statue is not.

Q4

あの国には3人の（　）がいます。Ano kuni ni wa san-nin no (___) ga imasu.

Full sentence: あの国には3人の王子がいます。Ano kuni ni wa san-nin no ōji ga imasu.
EN: That country has three princes.
HI: उस देश में तीन राजकुमार हैं।
GU: તે દેશમાં ત્રણ રાજકુમારો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a王子ōjia princeराजकुमारરાજકુમાર
b玉子tamagoan egg — a homophone-shaped trap, unrelated meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 王子 ("a prince") is built from 王 (king), taught this day; 玉子 (an egg, an alternate spelling of 卵) looks visually similar with 王 vs 玉 but is a completely unrelated word for food.

練習 — p.101 (美術館の展示室)

Q5

有名な絵画の（　）を部屋にかざる。Yūmei na kaiga no (___) o heya ni kazaru.

Full sentence: 有名な絵画の複製を部屋にかざる。Yūmei na kaiga no fukusei o heya ni kazaru.
EN: To decorate a room with a reproduction of a famous painting.
HI: किसी मशहूर पेंटिंग की प्रतिकृति से कमरे को सजाना।
GU: કોઈ પ્રખ્યાત પેઇન્ટિંગની પ્રતિકૃતિથી ઓરડો સજાવવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a複写fukushaa photocopy/duplicate document — not decor(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b複製fukuseia reproduction/replica (of art)प्रतिकृतिપ્રતિકૃતિ

Why: 複製 ("a reproduction/replica") is the word for an art print you'd hang as decor; 複写 refers to duplicating documents/paperwork, not artwork for display.

Q6

これに関する説明は複雑なため、ここでは（　）します。Kore ni kansuru setsumei wa fukuzatsu na tame, koko de wa (___) shimasu.

Full sentence: これに関する説明は複雑なため、ここでは省略します。Kore ni kansuru setsumei wa fukuzatsu na tame, koko de wa shōryaku shimasu.
EN: The explanation for this is complex, so we'll omit it here.
HI: इसकी व्याख्या जटिल है, इसलिए यहाँ इसे छोड़ दिया जाएगा।
GU: આનું સ્પષ્ટીકરણ જટિલ છે, તેથી અહીં તેને છોડી દેવામાં આવશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a省略shōryakuto omit, abridge (an explanation)छोड़ देना, संक्षिप्त करनाછોડી દેવું, સંક્ષિપ્ત કરવું
b節約setsuyakuto economize/save (money, resources) — wrong object(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 省略する ("to omit") is what you do to a lengthy explanation; 節約する ("to economize") applies to money, energy, or time, not to an explanation.

Q7

出版社の（　）の案内を見て、注文する。Shuppansha no (___) no annai o mite, chūmon suru.

Full sentence: 出版社の刊行物の案内を見て、注文する。Shuppansha no kankōbutsu no annai o mite, chūmon suru.
EN: To order after looking at the publisher's catalog of publications.
HI: प्रकाशक की प्रकाशित सामग्री की सूची देखकर ऑर्डर करना।
GU: પ્રકાશકની પ્રકાશિત સામગ્રીની યાદી જોઈને ઓર્ડર કરવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a順番junbana turn/order (of sequence) — unrelated to a catalog(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b刊行物kankōbutsupublications (what a publisher's catalog lists)प्रकाशित सामग्रीપ્રકાશિત સામગ્રી

Why: 刊行物の案内 ("a guide/catalog of publications") is what a publisher (出版社) sends out; 順番 (a turn/sequence) has nothing to do with a product catalog.

Q8

昨日、デパートでテレビによく出ている（　）人を見かけた。Kinō, depāto de terebi ni yoku dete iru (___) jin o mikaketa.

EN: Yesterday I spotted a celebrity who's often on TV at the department store.
HI: कल डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर में मैंने टीवी पर अक्सर दिखने वाले एक सेलिब्रिटी को देखा।
GU: ગઈકાલે ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરમાં મેં ટીવી પર વારંવાર દેખાતી એક સેલિબ્રિટીને જોઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a芸能geinōpublic entertainment (芸能人 = a celebrity/entertainer)मनोरंजन कलाમનોરંજન કલા
b役者yakushaan actor — narrower, and doesn't take 人 this way(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 芸能人 ("a TV celebrity/entertainer") is the fixed compound with 人; 役者 (an actor) is a real word but stands alone rather than combining into "〜人" the same way.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several same-kanji pairs here point at very different things — exactly what the 練習 questions above test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
財産zaisan Property, fortune An individual's personal assets/wealth. "He inherited a fortune."
文化財bunkazai Cultural property A nationally recognized cultural/historical asset — shares 財 with 財産 but a formal, public designation. "This temple is a registered cultural property."
観光kankō Sightseeing Touring for leisure; pairs with コース, 客. "A sightseeing tour."
観察kansatsu Observation Careful, often scientific watching — shares 観 with 観光 but a different register. "Observing an insect's behavior."
銅像dōzō A bronze statue A statue of any subject (person, animal) cast in bronze. "The Hachikō statue at Shibuya."
仏像butsuzō A statue of Buddha Specifically a Buddhist devotional statue. "A temple's principal Buddha statue."
複製fukusei A reproduction, replica A copy of an artwork, made to display or sell. "A replica of a famous painting."
複写fukusha A duplicate, photocopy A copy of a document/paper — an office/admin term. "Photocopy this contract."
省略shōryaku Omission, abridgment Leaving out part of an explanation or text. "We'll skip the details here."
節約setsuyaku Economizing, saving Reducing use of money, time, or resources — a different verb pattern from 省略. "Saving on electricity."
Caution & special readings: ❶財布 (さいふ, "wallet") and ❶観音 (かんのん, "Kannon") both break from their kanji's expected on-yomi — don't try to sound out 財布 as ざいふ or 観音 as かんおん. Separately, ⊙日仏 (にちふつ, "Japan–France") is flagged as a true irregular/idiomatic reading, distinct from either kanji's normal readings — this is the same "country-pair" pattern as 日米 (にちべい, Japan–US) elsewhere in the book.
