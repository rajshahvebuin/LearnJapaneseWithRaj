2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 — p.94 (スーパー「マルヨシ」の折り込み広告)

Q1

草花や野菜、果物などを箱に（a. 詰めて　b. 固めて）出荷する。Kusabana ya yasai, kudamono nado o hako ni (a. tsumete / b. katamete) shukka suru.

Full sentence: 草花や野菜、果物などを箱に詰めて出荷する。Kusabana ya yasai, kudamono nado o hako ni tsumete shukka suru.
EN: Flowering plants, vegetables, fruit, and the like are packed into boxes and shipped out.
HI: फूल-पौधे, सब्जियां, फल आदि को बक्सों में भरकर भेजा जाता है।
GU: ફૂલછોડ, શાકભાજી, ફળો વગેરેને બોક્સમાં ભરીને મોકલવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a詰めてtsumetepacking/stuffingभरकरભરીને
b固めてkatametehardening/solidifying (wrong verb for packing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 箱に詰める ("to pack into a box") is the set phrase for shipping goods; 固める means "to harden/solidify" and doesn't describe placing items into a container.

Q2

その作業は（a. 純粋　b. 単純）だが、ミスをすると危険だ。Sono sagyou wa (a. junsui / b. tanjun) da ga, misu o suru to kiken da.

Full sentence: その作業は単純だが、ミスをすると危険だ。Sono sagyou wa tanjun da ga, misu o suru to kiken da.
EN: That task is simple, but it's dangerous if you make a mistake.
HI: वह काम सरल है, लेकिन ग़लती करने पर ख़तरनाक है।
GU: તે કામ સાદું છે, પણ ભૂલ થાય તો ખતરનાક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a純粋junsuipure (describes substances/motives, not a task's difficulty)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b単純tanjunsimpleसरल, सीधा-सादाસાદું, સરળ

Why: 単純 describes a task being simple/uncomplicated; 純粋 describes purity (of a substance, feeling, or motive) — it doesn't apply to how easy or hard a task is.

GU: કોઈ ચીજને વાપરીને કે બનાવીને બતાવીને વેચવાની રીતને "રૂબરૂ નિદર્શન વેચાણ" કહેવાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a実演jitsuenlive demonstrationप्रत्यक्ष प्रदर्शनરૂબરૂ નિદર્શન
b演習enshuudrills/exercises (training, not selling)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

年に2回、住民による地域の（a. 雑草　b. 除草）作業が行われる。Nen ni nikai, juumin ni yoru chiiki no (a. zassou / b. josou) sagyou ga okonawareru.

EN: Twice a year, the residents carry out weeding work in the area.
HI: साल में दो बार, निवासी मिलकर इलाके में निराई का काम करते हैं।
GU: વર્ષમાં બે વાર, રહેવાસીઓ વિસ્તારમાં નીંદણ કરવાનું કામ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a雑草zassouweed (a noun, not an activity)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b除草josouweedingनिराई करनाનીંદણ કરવું

HI: "ऊर्जा बचत" का मतलब है ऊर्जा को संरक्षित करना।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji include several verb/noun pairs and same-kanji nuance traps pulled straight from the day's own list:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
単純tanjun Simple Describes how easy/uncomplicated a task or idea is. "This task is simple."
純粋junsui Pure Describes the purity of a substance, feeling, or motive — shares 純 with 単純 but not the same axis of meaning. "Pure gold," "pure intentions."
固定kotei Fixed, stable An adjective/noun describing something set in place. "A fixed exchange rate," "bolted down."
固まるkatamaru To harden, solidify An intransitive verb describing a change of state from soft/liquid to solid. "The cement hardened," "opinions solidified."
展示tenji Display, exhibit (the act of showing) Focuses on the items being shown/arranged for viewing. "Products on display," "an exhibit hall."
展覧会tenrankai An exhibition (the event) Names the whole organized event, not just the act of displaying. "An art exhibition was held."
破れるyabureru To tear, rip (intransitive) Something tears/rips on its own or as a result of wear. "My sock got a hole in it," "the paper tore."
破るyaburu To tear, break, violate (transitive) Someone deliberately tears/breaks something, or breaks a rule/record. "He tore the paper," "broke the record," "violated a promise."
Politeness trap: ❶承る (うけたまわる) is the humble form service staff use to mean "to accept/receive (a request, an order)" — never confuse it with plain 承知する ("to acknowledge/understand"), which is less formal. Likewise ❶省く (はぶく, "to omit/leave out") is a different word from 省エネ's 省 read しょう — same kanji 省, but はぶく is the kun-yomi verb while しょう is the on-yomi used in compounds.
