JLPT N2 · Kanji · Week 6 — みる② (Miru ② — Look and See ②)

4日目 — 地図 (Chizu — Maps)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) — printed p.98–99 (PDF index = printed page number for this scan)
Theme
14 kanji (#513–526) for reading maps: a station-area map (駅周辺地図) and a sightseeing map (観光地図) — words for buildings, landmarks, and geography you'd see labeled on a Japanese map.
Answer key
練習 (p.98) confirmed from [別冊] 解答・解説, 「4日目　左 (p.98)」, PDF index 170 (booklet's own p.10). 練習 (p.99) confirmed from 「4日目　右 (p.99)」, PDF index 171 (booklet's own p.11).
Notes
Both illustrations are literal maps: a hand-drawn station-area map (elementary school, museum, river, police box, city hall) on p.98, and a sightseeing map of a peninsula (bay, islands, castle, lake, gorge) on p.99. Vocabulary follows the map labels directly.
❶ = 特に注意してほしい読み — a reading requiring particular attention (usage caution)
☞[number] = cross-reference to another kanji's ID number in this book (informational only)
~ = this kanji attaches as a prefix/suffix to another word (e.g. ~湾 = "...Bay")

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (p.98, 4 items) and 練習 (p.99, 4 items), each "choose the correct option and mark ○, then write the reading in hiragana." Answers confirmed from the [別冊] answer booklet: 「4日目　左 (p.98)」(PDF index 170) and 「4日目　右 (p.99)」(PDF index 171).

練習 — p.98 (駅周辺地図)

Q1

この（　）を上ると美しいお寺がありますよ。Kono (___) o noboru to utsukushii otera ga arimasu yo.

Full sentence: この坂を上ると美しいお寺がありますよ。Kono saka o noboru to utsukushii otera ga arimasu yo.
EN: If you climb this slope, there's a beautiful temple.
HI: इस ढलान पर चढ़ोगे तो एक ख़ूबसूरत मंदिर मिलेगा।
GU: આ ઢોળાવ ચઢશો તો એક સુંદર મંદિર મળશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a坂sakaa slope — you climb (上る) a slopeढलानઢોળાવ
b役yakua role/duty — doesn't collocate with 上る(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 坂を上る ("climb a slope") is the natural collocation; 役 (a role/duty) is an abstract noun that can't be "climbed."

Q2

市役所は（　）署の向かいにあります。Shiyakusho wa (___)-sho no mukai ni arimasu.

Full sentence: 市役所は警察署の向かいにあります。Shiyakusho wa keisatsu-sho no mukai ni arimasu.
EN: The city hall is across from the police station.
HI: नगर निगम कार्यालय पुलिस थाने के सामने है।
GU: નગરપાલિકા કચેરી પોલીસ સ્ટેશનની સામે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a警察keisatsupolice (a station: 警察署)पुलिसપોલીસ
b診察shinsatsua medical consultation (no such "署")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 警察署 ("police station") is a fixed compound with 署; 診察 (a medical check-up) never combines with 署 — you'd say 診察室 (examination room) instead.

Q3

日本の科学（　）を学ぶために来ました。Nihon no kagaku (___) o manabu tame ni kimashita.

Full sentence: 日本の科学技術を学ぶために来ました。Nihon no kagaku gijutsu o manabu tame ni kimashita.
EN: I came here to study Japanese science and technology.
HI: मैं जापान का विज्ञान और तकनीक सीखने आया/आई हूँ।
GU: હું જાપાનનું વિજ્ઞાન અને ટેકનોલોજી શીખવા આવ્યો/આવી છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a技術gijutsutechnology (科学技術 = science & technology)तकनीकટેકનોલોજી
b手術shujutsusurgery — wrong sense entirely(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 科学技術 ("science and technology") is a set compound; 手術 (surgery) shares the 術 kanji but means something a doctor performs, unrelated to studying science.

Q4

あのお相撲さんは（　）を引退して解説者になるそうだ。Ano o-sumō-san wa (___) o intai shite kaisetsusha ni naru sō da.

Full sentence: あのお相撲さんは現役を引退して解説者になるそうだ。Ano o-sumō-san wa gen'eki o intai shite kaisetsusha ni naru sō da.
EN: I hear that sumo wrestler is retiring from active competition to become a commentator.
HI: सुना है वह सूमो पहलवान सक्रिय कुश्ती से संन्यास लेकर टिप्पणीकार बनने वाला है।
GU: સાંભળ્યું છે કે તે સૂમો કુસ્તીબાજ સક્રિય કારકિર્દીમાંથી નિવૃત્ત થઈ કોમેન્ટેટર બનવાનો છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a現役gen'ekiactive service — what you "retire from" (引退する)सक्रिय सेवाસક્રિય સેવા
b役目yakumea role/duty — doesn't pair with 引退する(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 現役を引退する ("to retire from active competition") is the fixed phrase for athletes; 役目 (a duty/role) is something you "fulfill" (果たす), not "retire from."

練習 — p.99 (観光地図)

Q5

湖の（　）に白鳥がたくさんいるのが見える。Mizuumi no (___) ni hakuchō ga takusan iru no ga mieru.

Full sentence: 湖の岸に白鳥がたくさんいるのが見える。Mizuumi no kishi ni hakuchō ga takusan iru no ga mieru.
EN: I can see lots of swans on the lakeshore.
HI: झील के किनारे पर बहुत सारे हंस दिख रहे हैं।
GU: સરોવરના કિનારે ઘણા બધા હંસ દેખાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a岸kishishore, bank — where birds gather at a lake's edgeकिनाराકિનારો
b谷tania valley — not part of a lake(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 湖の岸 ("the lake's shore") is where swans would gather; 谷 (a valley) is a landform unrelated to a lake's edge.

Q6

日本（　）を北から南まで旅行してみたい。Nihon (___) o kita kara minami made ryokō shite mitai.

Full sentence: 日本列島を北から南まで旅行してみたい。Nihon rettō o kita kara minami made ryokō shite mitai.
EN: I'd like to travel across the Japanese archipelago from north to south.
HI: मैं जापानी द्वीपसमूह में उत्तर से दक्षिण तक यात्रा करना चाहता/चाहती हूँ।
GU: હું જાપાનના ટાપુસમૂહમાં ઉત્તરથી દક્ષિણ સુધી પ્રવાસ કરવા માંગું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a半島hantōa peninsula — Japan as a whole is not one(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b列島rettōan archipelago — Japan is a chain of islandsद्वीपसमूहટાપુસમૂહ

Why: 日本列島 ("the Japanese archipelago") is the standard term for Japan's chain of islands; 半島 (a peninsula) describes a single landmass jutting into the sea, which doesn't describe the whole country.

Q7

この公園の中に、お（　）や植物園があります。Kono kōen no naka ni, o-(___) ya shokubutsuen ga arimasu.

Full sentence: この公園の中に、お城や植物園があります。Kono kōen no naka ni, o-shiro ya shokubutsuen ga arimasu.
EN: Inside this park there's a castle, a botanical garden, and so on.
HI: इस पार्क के अंदर एक किला और एक वनस्पति उद्यान है।
GU: આ ઉદ્યાનની અંદર એક કિલ્લો અને એક વનસ્પતિ ઉદ્યાન છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a城shiroa castle — a landmark inside a large parkकिलाકિલ્લો
b湾wana bay — a body of water, not a park landmark(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: お城 ("a castle") fits as a landmark you'd find inside a large park alongside a botanical garden; 湾 (a bay) is a coastal feature, not something built inside a park.

Q8

（　）には波で打ち寄せられたごみが落ちていた。(___) ni wa nami de uchiyoserareta gomi ga ochite ita.

Full sentence: 海岸には波で打ち寄せられたごみが落ちていた。Kaigan ni wa nami de uchiyoserareta gomi ga ochite ita.
EN: There was trash washed up by the waves lying on the seashore.
HI: समुद्र तट पर लहरों से बहकर आया कचरा पड़ा था।
GU: દરિયાકિનારે મોજાંથી ધોવાઈને આવેલો કચરો પડ્યો હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a渓谷keikokua gorge — waves don't reach a mountain gorge(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b海岸kaigana seashore — where waves wash things upसमुद्र तटદરિયાકિનારો

Why: 波で打ち寄せられる ("washed up by waves") can only happen at a 海岸 (seashore); 渓谷 (a gorge/ravine) is inland and has no waves.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several words here share a kanji but point at very different things — exactly what the 練習 questions above test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
警察keisatsu Police A law-enforcement body; combines with 署 (station). "The police station is across from city hall."
診察shinsatsu A medical consultation A doctor examining a patient — shares 察 with 警察 but unrelated meaning. "The doctor examined me."
技術gijutsu Technique, technology Skill or applied science — pairs with 科学 (science). "Science and technology," "technical skill."
手術shujutsu Surgery A medical operation — shares 術 with 技術 but a completely different field. "He underwent surgery."
現役gen'eki Active service/duty A status you hold and can 引退する ("retire") from. "A currently active athlete."
役目yakume A role, a duty A task you 果たす ("fulfill") — shares 役 with 現役 but a different verb pattern. "It's my duty/role to..."
半島hantō A peninsula One landmass jutting into the sea. "The Izu Peninsula."
列島rettō An archipelago A whole chain of islands — describes a country like Japan. "The Japanese archipelago."
海岸kaigan A seashore The coast where sea waves reach. "Trash washed up on the shore."
渓谷keikoku A gorge, ravine An inland mountain landform — no waves, no sea. "Hiking through a gorge."
Caution reading: 辺り is marked ❶ in the book — read あたり (kun-yomi), not the on-yomi へん you'd expect from 周辺 (しゅうへん). Both mean roughly "around/vicinity," but 辺り stands alone as a native Japanese word (この辺り = "around here"), while へん appears bound inside compounds like 周辺.
