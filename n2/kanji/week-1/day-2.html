2 Quiz / Exercise Section

Each printed page (左 p.14, 右 p.15) has its own 練習 exercise. Answers confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, PDF index 161, sections「2日目 左 (p.14)」and「2日目 右 (p.15)」.

練習 — p.14 (２日目 左)

Q1

タバコを吸う所＝（a. 禁煙席　b. 喫煙所）Tabako o suu tokoro = (a. kin'enseki / b. kitsuenjo)

EN: A place to smoke = (a. non-smoking seat / b. smoking area).
HI: धूम्रपान करने की जगह = (a. धूम्रपान-निषेध सीट / b. धूम्रपान क्षेत्र)।
GU: ધૂમ્રપાન કરવાની જગ્યા = (a. ધૂમ્રપાન-નિષેધ સીટ / b. ધૂમ્રપાન વિસ્તાર).
OptionEnglish
a禁煙席 — a non-smoking seat (the opposite meaning)
b喫煙所 — a smoking area

Why: 喫煙 (きつえん) means "smoking," so 喫煙所 is a place where smoking is allowed; 禁煙 means "no smoking," the opposite.

Q2

「この（a. 付近　b. 日付）に喫茶店はありませんか。」"Kono (a. fukin / b. hizuke) ni kissaten wa arimasen ka."

EN: "Isn't there a coffee shop around here?"
HI: "क्या इस आसपास कोई कॉफ़ी शॉप नहीं है?"
GU: "શું આ આસપાસ કોઈ કોફી શોપ નથી?"
OptionEnglish
a付近 (fukin) — vicinity
b日付 (hizuke) — date (unrelated meaning)

Why: 付近 means "the area/vicinity around a place," which fits asking about a coffee shop nearby; 日付 means "a calendar date" and doesn't fit spatially.

Q3

「日本語は（a. 日常　b. 非常）会話ならできます。」"Nihongo wa (a. nichijou / b. hijou) kaiwa nara dekimasu."

EN: "I can manage everyday conversation in Japanese."
HI: "जापानी में मैं रोज़मर्रा की बातचीत कर सकता/सकती हूँ।"
GU: "જાપાનીઝમાં હું રોજિંદી વાતચીત કરી શકું છું."
OptionEnglish
a日常 (nichijou) — everyday
b非常 (hijou) — extreme/emergency (wrong meaning here)

Why: 日常会話 ("everyday conversation") is the fixed collocation; 非常 means "extreme" or (as 非常口) "emergency" and doesn't combine with 会話 this way.

Q4

あの人は（a. 常識　b. 御中）がない。Ano hito wa (a. joushiki / b. onchuu) ga nai.

EN: That person has no common sense.
HI: उस व्यक्ति में कोई समझदारी नहीं है।
GU: તે વ્યક્તિમાં કોઈ સામાન્ય સમજ નથી.
OptionEnglish
a常識 (joushiki) — common sense
b御中 (onchuu) — an honorific addressed to an organization (wrong context)

Why: 常識がない ("lacking common sense") is the fixed phrase describing a person; 御中 is only used on envelopes addressed to organizations.

練習 — p.15 (２日目 右)

Q5

A「すみません、化粧室はどこですか。」B「はい、（a. お手洗い　b. 案内所）は、あちらでございます。」A: "Sumimasen, keshoushitsu wa doko desu ka." B: "Hai, (a. otearai / b. annaijo) wa, achira de gozaimasu."

EN: A: "Excuse me, where is the powder room?" B: "Yes, the restroom is over there."
HI: A: "माफ़ कीजिए, प्रसाधन कक्ष कहाँ है?" B: "जी, शौचालय उस तरफ़ है।"
GU: A: "માફ કરશો, પ્રસાધન ખંડ ક્યાં છે?" B: "હા, શૌચાલય તે તરફ છે."
OptionEnglish
aお手洗い (otearai) — restroom, matches the question's meaning
b案内所 (annaijo) — an information desk (wrong referent)

Why: The reply must answer "where is the toilet"; お手洗い restates 化粧室 in the polite everyday equivalent, while 案内所 is an unrelated information counter.

Q6

A「会議室はどこですか。」B「（a. 段階　b. 階段）を上って、すぐ右です。」A: "Kaigishitsu wa doko desu ka." B: "(a. Dankai / b. Kaidan) o nobotte, sugu migi desu."

EN: A: "Where is the meeting room?" B: "Go up the stairs, and it's immediately on the right."
HI: A: "सम्मेलन कक्ष कहाँ है?" B: "सीढ़ियाँ चढ़कर, तुरंत दाईं ओर है।"
GU: A: "સભાખંડ ક્યાં છે?" B: "પગથિયાં ચઢીને, તરત જ જમણી બાજુ છે."
OptionEnglish
a段階 (dankai) — a stage/level (abstract, not a physical stairway)
b階段 (kaidan) — stairs

Why: 階段を上る ("go up the stairs") is the fixed phrase for climbing a physical staircase; 段階 means an abstract "stage" or "level" and cannot be physically climbed.

Q7

「このごろ（a. 一段と　b. 石段と）寒くなってきましたね。」"Kono goro (a. ichidan to / b. ishidan to) samuku natte kimashita ne."

EN: "It's gotten even colder lately, hasn't it?"
HI: "आजकल और भी ठंड बढ़ गई है, है ना?"
GU: "તાજેતરમાં વધુ ઠંડી પડવા લાગી છે, ખરું ને?"
OptionEnglish
a一段と (ichidan to) — even more
b石段と — not a real adverbial phrase (石段 is "a stone step," a noun)

Why: 一段と is a set adverb meaning "even more/a notch further"; 石段 ("stone step") is a concrete noun that cannot modify an adjective this way.

Q8

ベストセラーになった小説が映画（a. 化　b. 案）された。Besutoseraa ni natta shousetsu ga eiga (a. -ka / b. -an) sareta.

EN: The novel that became a bestseller was turned into a movie.
HI: बेस्टसेलर बनी उपन्यास को फ़िल्म में बदल दिया गया।
GU: બેસ્ટસેલર બનેલી નવલકથાને ફિલ્મમાં ફેરવવામાં આવી.
OptionEnglish
a化 (-ka) — -ization suffix: 映画化 = "made into a movie"
b案 (-an) — a plan/proposal (not a suffix meaning "turned into")

Why: 〜化 is the productive suffix for "turning X into Y" (映画化 = "movie-ization"); 案 means "a plan/proposal" and cannot attach to 映画 this way.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji include several same-kanji, different-reading pairs and near-synonyms worth comparing directly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
内uchi The inside Kun-yomi, stands alone as a noun meaning "inside." "The inside of the box" (箱の内).
〜内-nai Inside / within Same kanji 内, on-yomi, attaches as a suffix to another noun. 社内 ("within the company"), 以内 ("within [a limit]").
文化bunka Culture 化 read カ, the everyday on-yomi. 文化, 化学, 〜化 (-ization) — the default reading.
化粧室keshoushitsu Powder room / toilet Same kanji 化, but read ケ only in this one common word. 化粧 (けしょう, makeup) and its compounds — memorize as an exception.
御手洗いotearai Toilet (polite, spoken) The everyday polite word, used when asking or answering aloud. "Where is the restroom?" in conversation.
化粧室keshoushitsu Powder room (formal, written) A more formal/written label, often the word printed on the door or floor map itself. Signage, floor plans, formal writing.
案内annai Information / guidance 案 combined with 内 forms a compound noun about guiding someone. 案内所 (information desk), 道案内 (giving directions).
案an A plan / proposal 案 alone (or in compounds like 提案) means an idea put forward for consideration — unrelated to "guidance." "I have a proposal" (案がある).
Reading trap: ❶日付 (ひづけ, "date") is flagged in the book as a reading needing particular attention — the 付 in 日付 is read づけ (voiced), not つけ, once combined with 日. Compare the everyday つ-ける／つ-く readings of 付 elsewhere (付近, 受付) with this one voiced exception.
