2 Quiz / Exercise Section

Each printed page (左 p.16, 右 p.17) has its own 練習 exercise. Answers confirmed from the [別冊] 解答・解説 booklet, PDF index 161, sections「3日目 左 (p.16)」and「3日目 右 (p.17)」.

練習 — p.16 (３日目 左)

Q1

「あ、（a. 準備中　b. 定休日）だ、まだ早いんだね。また後で来よう。」"A, (a. junbichuu / b. teikyuubi) da, mada hayain da ne. Mata ato de koyou."

EN: "Oh, they're preparing to open — we're too early. Let's come back later."
HI: "अरे, अभी तैयारी चल रही है, हम बहुत जल्दी आ गए। बाद में फिर आते हैं।"
GU: "અરે, હજુ તૈયારી ચાલુ છે, આપણે વહેલા આવી ગયા. પછી ફરી આવીશું."
OptionEnglish
a準備中 (junbichuu) — in preparation, fits "too early, come back later"
b定休日 (teikyuubi) — regular holiday, would mean the shop is closed that whole day

Why: "Too early, come back later" implies the shop will open soon — consistent with 準備中 ("in preparation"), not 定休日 (a fixed closing day when returning later that day wouldn't help).

Q2

「開放厳禁」＝（a. 開けてはいけない　b. 開けておいてはいけない）"Kaihou genkin" = (a. akete wa ikenai / b. akete oite wa ikenai)

EN: "Do not leave open" = (a. you must not open it / b. you must not leave it open).
HI: "खुला छोड़ना वर्जित" = (a. इसे खोलना नहीं चाहिए / b. इसे खुला नहीं छोड़ना चाहिए)।
GU: "ખુલ્લું રાખવું મનાઈ" = (a. તેને ખોલવું ન જોઈએ / b. તેને ખુલ્લું ન છોડવું જોઈએ).
OptionEnglish
ayou must not open it
byou must not leave it open

Why: 開放 means "leaving something open/unrestricted," so 開放厳禁 forbids the state of leaving a door open — opening it briefly to pass through is fine, leaving it propped open is not.

Q3

A「何にする？」B「安くて早いから（a. 定食　b. 洋食）がいいよ。」A: "Nani ni suru?" B: "Yasukute hayai kara (a. teishoku / b. youshoku) ga ii yo."

EN: A: "What will you have?" B: "The set meal is good — it's cheap and quick."
HI: A: "क्या लोगे?" B: "सेट भोजन अच्छा रहेगा, सस्ता और जल्दी मिल जाता है।"
GU: A: "શું લઈશ?" B: "સેટ ભોજન સારું છે, સસ્તું અને ઝડપી છે."
OptionEnglish
a定食 (teishoku) — a set meal
b洋食 (youshoku) — Western-style food (unrelated to "cheap and quick")

Why: 定食 is a fixed-price, fast-serving set meal, matching "cheap and quick"; 洋食 only describes a cuisine style, not speed or price.

Q4

うちのチームは（a. 準急　b. 準決勝）まで勝ち進んだ。Uchi no chiimu wa (a. junkyuu / b. junkesshou) made kachisusunda.

EN: Our team advanced through to the semifinals.
HI: हमारी टीम सेमीफ़ाइनल तक जीतते हुए पहुँची।
GU: અમારી ટીમ સેમીફાઈનલ સુધી જીતતી ગઈ.
OptionEnglish
a準急 (junkyuu) — a semi-express train (wrong domain)
b準決勝 (junkesshou) — semifinals

Why: 準決勝 is the tournament term for "semifinals"; 準急 uses the same 準 (semi-) but names a train category, not a competition stage.

練習 — p.17 (３日目 右)

Q5

「備え付けの紙以外、流さないでください。」＝トイレットペーパー（a. しか　b. だけ）使えない。"Sonaetsuke no kami igai, nagasanaide kudasai." = toiretto peepaa (a. shika / b. dake) tsukaenai.

EN: "Do not flush anything other than the provided paper." = You can(not) use anything but toilet paper.
HI: "उपलब्ध कराए गए कागज़ के अलावा कुछ मत बहाइए।" = केवल टॉयलेट पेपर ही इस्तेमाल किया जा सकता है।
GU: "પૂરો પાડેલો કાગળ સિવાય કંઈ ન વહાવો." = ફક્ત ટોઇલેટ પેપર જ વાપરી શકાય.
OptionEnglish
aしか (shika) — used with a negative verb: "nothing but"
bだけ (dake) — "only," but does not pair with the negative verb 使えない the same way

Why: しか...ない is the grammatical pattern meaning "nothing except X" — the sentence ends in 使えない (negative), which しか requires; だけ pairs with an affirmative verb instead.

Q6

「テストをしますから、本を（a. 閉めて　b. 閉じて）ください。」"Tesuto o shimasu kara, hon o (a. shimete / b. tojite) kudasai."

EN: "We're having a test now, so please close your books."
HI: "अब परीक्षा होगी, इसलिए कृपया अपनी किताबें बंद कर लीजिए।"
GU: "હવે પરીક્ષા થશે, તેથી કૃપા કરી તમારાં પુસ્તકો બંધ કરો."
OptionEnglish
a閉めて (shimete) — close (a door, window; wrong object type)
b閉じて (tojite) — close (a book, eyes, mouth)

Why: 閉じる is the verb specifically used for closing books, eyes, or a mouth; 閉める is used for doors, windows, and stores (閉店).

Q7

「エレベーターは（a. 点検中　b. 検問中）ですから、階段を使ってください。」"Erebeetaa wa (a. tenkenchuu / b. kenmonchuu) desu kara, kaidan o tsukatte kudasai."

EN: "The elevator is under inspection, so please use the stairs."
HI: "लिफ़्ट की जाँच चल रही है, इसलिए कृपया सीढ़ियों का इस्तेमाल करें।"
GU: "લિફ્ટની તપાસ ચાલી રહી છે, તેથી કૃપા કરી પગથિયાંનો ઉપયોગ કરો."
OptionEnglish
a点検中 (tenkenchuu) — under (mechanical) inspection
b検問中 (kenmonchuu) — under (police) checkpoint questioning, wrong domain

Why: 点検 is the routine mechanical/equipment inspection appropriate for an elevator; 検問 refers to a police checkpoint interrogating people, an unrelated context.

Q8

（a. 閉店　b. 閉会）セールを行う。(a. Heiten / b. Heikai) seeru o okonau.

EN: They are holding a store-closing sale.
HI: वे दुकान बंद होने की सेल लगा रहे हैं।
GU: તેઓ દુકાન બંધ થવાનું વેચાણ કરી રહ્યા છે.
OptionEnglish
a閉店 (heiten) — closing of a store
b閉会 (heikai) — closing of a meeting/assembly (wrong domain)

Why: 閉店セール ("store-closing sale") is a fixed retail term; 閉会 applies to meetings, conferences, or ceremonies, not shops.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's kanji supply two classic transitive/intransitive pairs plus a same-kanji domain contrast — all directly tested in the 練習 above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
閉まるshimaru To shut / close (intransitive) The door/store itself closes — no stated agent. "The door closes (by itself)" (ドアが閉まる).
閉めるshimeru To shut / close (transitive) An agent closes the door/store deliberately. "Please close the door" (ドアを閉めてください).
閉じるtojiru To shut / close (a book, eyes, mouth) Reserved for a narrower set of objects — books, eyes, mouths — not doors or shops. "Close your eyes" (目を閉じる), "close your book" (本を閉じる).
流れるnagareru To flow (intransitive) Water/time/information flows on its own. "The river flows" (川が流れる).
流すnagasu To let flow (transitive) An agent causes water/something to flow — e.g. flushing a toilet. "Flush the toilet paper" (紙を流す).
点検tenken A (mechanical/equipment) inspection Routine technical checking of a machine or facility. "The elevator is under inspection" (エレベーターは点検中).
検問kenmon A (police) checkpoint inspection Same 検 kanji, but about questioning people, typically by police. A roadside police checkpoint stopping cars.
Verb-choice trap: This day has no ❶-flagged readings on p.16–17 — the main trap is verb choice among the three "close" verbs (閉まる／閉める／閉じる) and the two "flow" verbs (流れる／流す). When a JLPT question gives you an object (ドア, 本, 目, 水, 紙), match it to the verb that book normally pairs with that object, not just the English translation "close" or "flow."
