2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習 (choose the correct option, mark ○) — 4 items from p.20, 4 items from p.21. Answers confirmed from the [別冊] booklet, 「5日目　左 (p.20)」and「5日目　右 (p.21)」sections.

練習 (p.20) — choose the correct option

Q1

バスなどのおりるほう＝（a. 降車口　b. 乗車口）Basu nado no oriru hou = (a. koushaguchi / b. joushaguchi)

EN: The side of a bus, etc. where you get off = 降車口 (exit door).
HI: बस आदि से उतरने वाला दरवाज़ा = उतरने का द्वार।
GU: બસ વગેરેમાંથી ઉતરવાનો દરવાજો = ઉતરવાનું દ્વાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a降車口koushaguchiexit door (for getting off)उतरने का द्वारઉતરવાનું દ્વાર
b乗車口joushaguchientry door (for getting on) — opposite meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 降車口 uses 降 ("to descend/get off"); 乗車口 (with 乗, "to board") is the opposite door — for getting on, not off.

Q2

お金を細かくしたいときは（a. 両替　b. 運賃）します。Okane o komakaku shitai toki wa (a. ryougae / b. unchin) shimasu.

EN: When you want to break your money into smaller change, you exchange it.
HI: जब आप पैसे को छोटे नोटों/सिक्कों में बदलना चाहें, तो विनिमय करते हैं।
GU: જ્યારે તમારે પૈસાને છૂટા કરવા હોય, ત્યારે વિનિમય કરો છો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a両替ryougaeto exchange moneyमुद्रा विनिमय करनाચલણ વિનિમય કરવો
b運賃unchina fare (a noun, not an action you "do" to money)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 両替する means "to break/exchange money" (e.g. into smaller denominations); 運賃 is simply the fare amount itself, not a verb-forming action.

Q3

○町を通って△駅へ行く＝○町（a. 経由　b. 回送）△駅行き◯-machi o tootte △-eki e iku = ◯-machi (a. keiyu / b. kaisou) △-eki-iki

EN: Going to Station △ by way of Town ◯ = "◯-machi via, bound for △ Station."
HI: ◯ शहर से होकर △ स्टेशन जाना = "◯ शहर के रास्ते, △ स्टेशन के लिए।"
GU: ◯ ગામથી થઈને △ સ્ટેશન જવું = "◯ ગામ થઈને, △ સ્ટેશન માટે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a経由keiyuvia, by way of...के रास्ते होकर...થઈને
b回送kaisou"out of service" (a train not carrying passengers) — wrong meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 経由 marks a route passing through a place before reaching the destination; 回送 is an unrelated railway term for a train running empty/out of service.

Q4

（a. 深刻　b. 深夜）バスで田舎へ帰る。(a. Shinkoku / b. Shin'ya) basu de inaka e kaeru.

EN: To go back to the countryside by overnight/midnight bus.
HI: रात की बस से गाँव लौटना।
GU: રાત્રિની બસથી ગામડે પાછા ફરવું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a深刻shinkokuserious (describes a problem, not a bus)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b深夜shin'yamidnight, late-nightआधी रात, रात्रिમધરાત, રાત્રિ

Why: 深夜バス ("midnight/overnight bus") is a fixed compound naming a bus that runs late at night; 深刻 describes seriousness (of a problem, situation), and can't modify "bus" this way.

Q5

ここはお年寄りや体の不自由な方のための（a. 優先席　b. 自由席）です。Koko wa otoshiyori ya karada no fujiyuu na kata no tame no (a. yuusenseki / b. jiyuuseki) desu.

EN: This is a priority seat for elderly people and people with disabilities.
HI: यह बुज़ुर्गों और शारीरिक रूप से अक्षम लोगों के लिए प्राथमिकता वाली सीट है।
GU: આ વૃદ્ધો અને શારીરિક રીતે અક્ષમ લોકો માટેની અગ્રતા બેઠક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a優先席yuusensekia priority seatप्राथमिकता सीटઅગ્રતા બેઠક
b自由席jiyuusekia non-reserved/open seat (anyone can sit) — wrong meaning(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 優先席 specifically means a seat prioritized for the elderly/disabled/pregnant; 自由席 just means "unreserved, first-come seating" open to anyone.

Q6

深夜はタクシーの運賃が（a. 割増　b. 割引）になります。Shin'ya wa takushii no unchin ga (a. warimashi / b. waribiki) ni narimasu.

EN: Late at night, taxi fares get a surcharge.
HI: देर रात टैक्सी का किराया अतिरिक्त शुल्क के साथ बढ़ जाता है।
GU: મોડી રાત્રે ટેક્સીનું ભાડું વધારાના ચાર્જ સાથે વધે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a割増warimashia surcharge (an extra added on)अतिरिक्त शुल्कવધારાનો ચાર્જ
b割引waribikia discount (opposite direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Late-night taxi fares in Japan go up (a 割増 surcharge), not down; 割引 is a discount, the opposite effect.

Q7

（a. 窓側　b. 通路側）の座席は出たり入ったりしやすい。(a. Madogawa / b. Tsuurogawa) no zaseki wa detari haittari shiyasui.

EN: An aisle-side seat is easy to get in and out of.
HI: गलियारे की ओर वाली सीट से आना-जाना आसान होता है।
GU: પાંસાની બાજુની બેઠકમાંથી અંદર-બહાર જવું સહેલું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a窓側madogawawindow-side (harder to get in/out of)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b通路側tsuurogawaaisle-sideगलियारे की ओरપાંસાની બાજુ

Why: An aisle-side (通路側) seat opens directly onto the walkway, making it easy to get up and down; a window-side (窓側) seat requires climbing past a neighbor.

Q8

これは安全性に（a. 優れた　b. 優しい）車です。Kore wa anzensei ni (a. sugureta / b. yasashii) kuruma desu.

EN: This is a car that excels in safety.
HI: यह सुरक्षा में उत्कृष्ट कार है।
GU: આ સલામતીમાં શ્રેષ્ઠ કાર છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a優れたsuguretaexcelling, excellentउत्कृष्ट, श्रेष्ठશ્રેષ્ઠ, ઉત્કૃષ્ટ
b優しいyasashiikind, gentle (doesn't fit "safety")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 安全性に優れる ("to excel in safety") is a fixed collocation using 優れる; 優しい describes kindness/gentleness toward people, which doesn't combine with an abstract quality like "safety" this way — even though both readings come from the same kanji 優.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs here share the same kanji but split into very different readings/meanings — exactly what 練習 Q6, Q7, and Q8 above are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
降りるoriru To get off, descend A person leaving a vehicle or coming down from somewhere. "Get off the bus at the next stop."
降るfuru To fall (rain/snow) Same kanji 降, but describes weather falling from the sky, not a person moving. "It's raining/snowing."
両替ryougae Currency exchange (the act) The general verb/noun for breaking or exchanging money. "Exchange this bill for coins."
為替kawase Exchange, a money order An irregular (⊙) reading — a financial instrument/mechanism for sending money, not the everyday act of breaking change. "Foreign exchange rate," "a postal money order."
優しいyasashii Kind, gentle Kun-yomi describing a person's gentle character. "A kind teacher."
優れるsugureru To excel Same kanji 優, different kun-yomi — describes superior quality/performance, not personality. "A car that excels in safety," "an outstanding student."
割引waribiki A discount Price goes down. "10% off with this coupon."
割増warimashi A surcharge Same kanji 割, opposite direction — price goes up. "Late-night taxi fares include a surcharge."
Reading trap: 為替 (かわせ) is one of this day's ⊙ irregular readings — nothing about the individual kanji 為 or 替 predicts "かわせ" on sight, so treat it as a fixed vocabulary item to memorize whole, the same way you would 今日(きょう) or 眼鏡(めがね).
