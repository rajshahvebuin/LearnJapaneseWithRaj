1 問題1 — reading (10 items)

＿＿＿の言葉の読み方として最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best reading for the underlined word from 1–4.

1

ここは駐車禁止です。Koko wa chuusha kinshi desu.

EN: This is a no-parking zone.
HI: यह वाहन-स्थल निषेध क्षेत्र है।
GU: આ પાર્કિંગ પ્રતિબંધિત વિસ્તાર છે.
#Reading
1じゅうしゃ
2ちょうしゃ
3ちゅうしゃ (chuusha)
4じょうしゃ

Why: 駐車 is read ちゅうしゃ, "parking (a vehicle)."

2

チューインガムは紙に包んで捨てましょう。Chuuin gamu wa kami ni tsutsunde sutemashou.

EN: Let's wrap chewing gum in paper before throwing it away.
HI: च्युइंग गम को काग़ज़ में लपेटकर फेंकें।
GU: ચ્યુઇંગ ગમને કાગળમાં લપેટીને ફેંકીએ.
#Reading
1はさんで
2つつんで (tsutsunde)
3たたんで
4すすんで

Why: 包んで (Day 6's 包 kanji) is the te-form of 包む, "to wrap."

3

外国為替の窓口は2階です。Gaikoku kawase no madoguchi wa nikai desu.

#Reading
1きがえ
2ためせ
3かわせ (kawase)
4りょうがえ

Why: 為替 (Day 5's 替 group, an ⊙ irregular reading) is read かわせ, "(foreign) exchange."

10

機械でも精算できます。Kikai demo seisan dekimasu.

EN: You can settle payment with the machine too.
HI: मशीन से भी भुगतान समायोजन किया जा सकता है।
GU: મશીનથી પણ ચુકવણી સમાયોજન કરી શકાય છે.
#Reading

2かいさん

2 問題2 — writing (5 items)

＿＿＿の言葉を漢字で書くとき、最もよいものを1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best kanji for the underlined reading.

11

このエレベーターはていいん9名です。Kono erebeetaa wa teiin kyuu-mei desu.

EN: This elevator has a capacity of 9 people.
HI: इस लिफ़्ट की क्षमता 9 लोग है।
GU: આ લિફ્ટની ક્ષમતા 9 લોકોની છે.
#Kanji
1係員
2乗員
3店員
4定員teiin

Why: 定員 means "fixed capacity/quota (of people)" — the correct term for an elevator's maximum occupancy.

12

御手洗いはせいそう中です。Otearai wa seisou-chuu desu.

EN: The restroom is being cleaned.
HI: शौचालय की सफ़ाई हो रही है।
GU: શૌચાલયની સફાઈ ચાલી રહી છે.
#Kanji
1清帰
2清掃seisou
3精掃
4精帰

Why: 清掃 means "cleaning" — 帰 ("return") is an unrelated kanji distractor, and 精 (from Day 4's 精算) is a homophone trap for 清.

13

地デジほうそうというのは何ですか。Chi-deji housou to iu no wa nan desu ka.

EN: What is terrestrial digital broadcasting?
HI: "टेरेस्ट्रियल डिजिटल प्रसारण" क्या है?
GU: "ટેરેસ્ટ્રિયલ ડિજિટલ પ્રસારણ" શું છે?
#Kanji
1郵送
2包送
3放送housou
4発送

Why: 放送 means "broadcasting" — 郵送 (mailing, Day 6's 郵 group) and 発送 (dispatching) are believable distractors built from real kanji taught this week, but the wrong meaning.

14

この手紙はそくたつでおねがいします。Kono tegami wa sokutatsu de onegaishimasu.

EN: Please send this letter by special/express delivery.
HI: कृपया इस चिट्ठी को शीघ्र डाक सेवा से भेजें।
GU: કૃપા કરીને આ પત્ર ઝડપી ટપાલ સેવાથી મોકલો.
#Kanji
1速達sokutatsu
2配達
3発達
4友達

Why: 速達 (Day 6's 達 group) means "special/express delivery" — 配達 (regular delivery), 発達 (development), and 友達 (friend) all share 達 but mean something else entirely.

15

くうこうまでリムジンバスで行きました。Kuukou made rimujin basu de ikimashita.

EN: I went to the airport by limousine bus.
HI: मैं हवाई अड्डे तक लिमोज़ीन बस से गया।
GU: હું વિમાનમથક સુધી લિમોઝિન બસથી ગયો.
#Kanji
1空港kuukou
2航空
3港口
4高校

Why: 空港 (Day 5's 港 group) means "airport" — 航空 (aviation), 港口 (harbor mouth), and 高校 (high school, an unrelated homophone こうこう vs くうこう) are all plausible-looking distractors.

3 問題3 — fill the blank (5 items)

（　）に入れるのに最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。Choose the best word for the blank from 1–4.

16

一万円（　）を両替してくれませんか。Ichiman'en (___) o ryougae shite kuremasen ka.

Full sentence: 一万円札を両替してくれませんか。Ichiman'en-satsu o ryougae shite kuremasen ka.
EN: Could you exchange this 10,000 yen note for me?
HI: क्या आप यह 10,000 येन का नोट बदल देंगे?
GU: શું તમે આ 10,000 યેનની નોટ બદલી આપશો?
#Kanji
1符
2札satsu → ichiman'en-satsu
3付
4段

Why: 一万円札 (Day 4's 札 group) means "a 10,000 yen note/bill."

17

日本からの海外旅行は1964年に自由（　）されました。Nihon kara no kaigai ryokou wa sen-kyuuhyaku-rokujuu-yo-nen ni jiyuu (___) saremashita.

Full sentence: 日本からの海外旅行は1964年に自由化されました。Nihon kara no kaigai ryokou wa sen-kyuuhyaku-rokujuu-yo-nen ni jiyuuka saremashita.
EN: Overseas travel from Japan was liberalized in 1964.
HI: जापान से विदेश यात्रा को 1964 में उदार बनाया गया।
GU: જાપાનથી વિદેશ પ્રવાસ 1964માં ઉદાર બનાવવામાં આવ્યો.
#Kanji
1科
2化ka → jiyuuka
3案
4席

Why: 自由化 ("liberalization," using the suffix 化 "-ization") describes a policy becoming free/open; 科 (Day 6's 科 group, "department/subject") is a homophone-adjacent distractor but doesn't form this word.

18

（　）入国手続きをする。(___) nyuukoku tetsuzuki o suru.

Full sentence: 再入国手続きをする。Sai-nyuukoku tetsuzuki o suru.
EN: To complete re-entry (into the country) procedures.
HI: पुनः-प्रवेश की प्रक्रिया पूरी करना।
GU: ફરી-પ્રવેશની પ્રક્રિયા પૂર્ણ કરવી.
#Kanji
1改
2際
3御
4再sai → sai-nyuukoku

Why: 再入国 ("re-entry into a country," using Day 6's 再 group) is the fixed term for returning after leaving; 際 (Day 6's 際 group, "at the time of") is a plausible-looking distractor sharing the same day but a different meaning.

19

このバスは運（　）を先にはらいます。Kono basu wa un(___) o saki ni haraimasu.

Full sentence: このバスは運賃を先にはらいます。Kono basu wa unchin o saki ni haraimasu.
EN: On this bus, you pay the fare first.
HI: इस बस में पहले किराया चुकाना होता है।
GU: આ બસમાં પહેલા ભાડું ચૂકવવું પડે છે.
#Kanji
1代
2料
3賃chin → unchin
4貸

Why: 運賃 (Day 5's 賃 group) means "a fare" (for transportation specifically); 料 (fee, as in 料金) is a near-synonym but doesn't combine with 運 this way, and 貸 ("to lend," visually similar to 賃) is a distractor.

20

1（　）車は禁煙車です。Ichi(___)sha wa kin'ensha desu.

HI: डिब्बा नंबर 1 धूम्रपान-निषेध डिब्बा है।
GU: ડબ્બો નંબર 1 ધૂમ્રપાન-નિષેધ ડબ્બો છે.
#Kanji
1号gou → ichi-gousha
2両
3番
4階

Why: ～号車 (Day 4's 号 group) numbers a train's individual carriages; 両 (also taught this week, in ～両目) and 番 (as in 番線) are same-week homophone-adjacent distractors that number different things (car count vs. platform).

読み終えた新聞などは、デパートの21（　）紙やトイレットペーパーなどに22（　）されます。ゴミとして23（　）で、リサイクルするように24（　）気をつけているという人が25（　）います。Yomioeta shinbun nado wa, depaato no (21) kami ya toiretto peepaa nado ni (22) saremasu. Gomi to shite (23) de, risaikuru suru you ni (24) ki o tsukete iru to iu hito ga (25) imasu.

Full passage: 読み終えた新聞などは、デパートの包み紙やトイレットペーパーなどに再生されます。ゴミとして捨てないで、リサイクルするように常に気をつけているという人が増えています。Yomioeta shinbun nado wa, depaato no tsutsumigami ya toiretto peepaa nado ni saisei saremasu. Gomi to shite sutenai de, risaikuru suru you ni tsune ni ki o tsukete iru to iu hito ga fuete imasu.
EN: Newspapers and the like that have been read are recycled into things like department-store wrapping paper and toilet paper. The number of people who are always careful to recycle rather than throw them away as trash is increasing.
HI: पढ़े जा चुके अख़बार आदि को डिपार्टमेंट स्टोर के आवरण-काग़ज़ या टॉयलेट पेपर जैसी चीज़ों में पुनर्चक्रित (रीसाइकल) किया जाता है। कचरे के रूप में न फेंककर हमेशा रीसाइकल करने का ध्यान रखने वाले लोगों की संख्या बढ़ रही है।
GU: વાંચી લીધેલા છાપાં વગેરેને ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોરના પેકિંગ કાગળ કે ટોઇલેટ પેપર જેવી વસ્તુઓમાં રિસાયકલ કરવામાં આવે છે. કચરા તરીકે ફેંક્યા વગર હંમેશાં રિસાયકલ કરવાનું ધ્યાન રાખતા લોકોની સંખ્યા વધી રહી છે.

5 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes (week review)

The test above deliberately re-tests same-reading and same-kanji traps from earlier in the week — the three most important to keep straight going into Week 2:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
精算seisan Fare/bill adjustment Settling a shortfall on a specific payment (Day 4; Q10 above). "Pay the fare difference at the machine."
計算keisan Calculation Same 算 kanji, different reading family — general arithmetic, not a payment. "Calculate the total."
科学kagaku Science (broad) Homophone of 化学 — the umbrella field (Day 6). "Science" as a general subject.
化学kagaku Chemistry (specific) Same reading かがく — one narrow branch of 科学. Naming the chemistry subject specifically.
初診shoshin First-time consultation Antonym pair built on 初 vs 再 (Day 6). A brand-new patient's first visit.
再診saishin Follow-up consultation The returning-patient half of the same antonym pair. A repeat visit for the same condition.
[コラム] 看板・立て札・表示 (Kanban, tatefuda, hyouji — Signboards, Notice Boards) — the book's own closing sidebar for Week 1:

第1週「みる①」では、看板・立て札・表示の漢字を勉強しました。こんなものもありますね。Dai-isshuu "Miru ①" de wa, kanban, tatefuda, hyouji no kanji o benkyou shimashita. Konna mono mo arimasu ne.
(In Week 1 "Look and See ①," we studied kanji for signboards, notice boards, and displays. There are things like these too, aren't there?)

The column shows more real-life signage — a street sign (○○町), Narita Airport's Terminal 1/Terminal 2 signs (成田空港 第1ターミナル／第2ターミナル), Haneda Airport's station signs (羽田空港第1ビル駅／第2ビル駅), and a general information desk (総合案内所) — then explains: 成田空港、羽田空港は空港の名前です。成田には第1ターミナル、第2ターミナルがあります。「総」は「たくさんあるものを集める」という意味があって、「総合病院」「総計」などのように使われます。私たちのまわりには漢字がいっぱいです。楽しく勉強しましょう。Narita Kuukou, Haneda Kuukou wa kuukou no namae desu. (Narita Airport and Haneda Airport are airport names. Narita has Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. "総" carries the meaning "to gather many things together," and is used in words like "総合病院" (general hospital) and "総計" (grand total). Kanji are all around us — let's enjoy studying them!)
