Q1

足りない料金を払うところ＝（a. 精算所　b. 清算所）Tarinai ryoukin o harau tokoro = (a. seisanjo / b. seisanjo)

EN: The place where you pay the balance of a fare = 精算所 (fare adjustment office).
HI: बकाया किराया चुकाने की जगह = किराया-समायोजन कार्यालय।
GU: બાકી ભાડું ચૂકવવાની જગ્યા = ભાડું-સમાયોજન કાર્યાલય.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a精算所seisanjofare adjustment officeकिराया-समायोजन कार्यालयભાડું-સમાયોજન કાર્યાલય
b清算所seisanjoa (financial/debt) liquidation office — wrong word here(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 精算 is the fixed word for adjusting/settling a train fare shortfall; 清算 (also せいさん) means liquidating debts/accounts in a broader financial sense — a classic same-sound trap, see confusion pairs below.

Q2

「その切符では（a. 改札口　b. 自動改札）は通れませんから、駅員のいるほうを通ってください。」"Sono kippu de wa (a. kaisatsuguchi / b. jidou kaisatsu) wa toore-masen kara, ekiin no iru hou o tootte kudasai."

EN: "You can't pass through the automatic gate with that ticket, so please go through the one with a station staff member."
HI: "उस टिकट से आप स्वचालित गेट से नहीं गुज़र सकते, इसलिए कृपया स्टेशन कर्मचारी वाले रास्ते से जाएँ।"
GU: "એ ટિકિટથી તમે સ્વયંસંચાલિત ગેટમાંથી પસાર થઈ શકશો નહીં, તેથી કૃપા કરીને સ્ટેશન કર્મચારી હોય તે તરફથી જાઓ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a改札口kaisatsuguchiticket gate (the general term — too broad here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

GU: "જે કંઈ સમજાય નહીં, કૃપા કરીને સ્ટેશનની બારી પર પૂછો."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

GU: "આગલી ઝડપી ટ્રેન પ્લેટફોર્મ 3 પરથી ઉપડશે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

HI: "अप्रैल से ट्रेन का समय-सारणी संशोधित की जाएगी।"

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's kanji cluster around speed, fares, and amendments — several pairs share a reading or a kanji but split into different meanings, exactly what 練習 Q1 and Q7 above test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
精算seisan Fare adjustment Settling a shortfall on a train/bus fare — this book's own 練習 Q1 trap. "Pay the difference at the fare adjustment window."
清算seisan Settling accounts, liquidation Same reading せいさん, unrelated kanji — a broader financial/debt settlement, not a train fare. "Liquidate a company's debts."
快いkokoroyoi Pleasant Kun-yomi of 快 — describes a pleasant feeling or sensation. "A pleasant breeze," "a pleasant reply."
快速kaisoku High-speed (train) On-yomi of the same kanji 快 — describes a train service category, nothing to do with "pleasant." Naming a rapid/express train.
足し算tashizan Addition The operation of adding numbers together. "2 + 2" style problems.
引き算hikizan Subtraction The opposite operation — taking a number away. "5 − 2" style problems.
改めるaratameru To change, to check Kun-yomi verb form of 改 — an action someone performs. "He revised his opinion," "check one's ticket."
改正kaisei An amendment, revision On-yomi noun form of the same kanji 改 — the resulting change itself (e.g. to a law or timetable). "A revision to the timetable/law took effect."
Exam trap: 精算 and 清算 are both read せいさん but are completely different kanji with different scopes — 精算 is specifically about settling a fare/bill shortfall (trains, taxis, utility bills), while 清算 is the broader term for liquidating debts or closing out accounts. When a 漢字読み question gives you せいさん, check whether the context is "paying a small difference" (→精算) or "settling/closing accounts entirely" (→清算).
