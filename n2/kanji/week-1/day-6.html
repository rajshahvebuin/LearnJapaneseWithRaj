練習 (p.22) — choose the correct option

Q1

急ぎの手紙を（a. 小包　b. 速達）で送る。Isogi no tegami o (a. kozutsumi / b. sokutatsu) de okuru.

EN: To send an urgent letter by special/express delivery.
HI: ज़रूरी चिट्ठी को शीघ्र डाक सेवा से भेजना।
GU: તાત્કાલિક પત્ર ઝડપી ટપાલ સેવાથી મોકલવો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a小包kozutsumia parcel (wrong item — a letter isn't a parcel)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b速達sokutatsuspecial/express deliveryशीघ्र डाक सेवाઝડપી ટપાલ સેવા

Why: 速達 is the delivery speed/service you choose for an urgent letter; 小包 names a parcel/package as an object, not a delivery method for a letter.

Q2

国際郵便＝（a. 海外　b. 国内）へ送る郵便Kokusai yuubin = (a. kaigai / b. kokunai) e okuru yuubin

EN: International mail = mail sent abroad.
HI: अंतरराष्ट्रीय डाक = विदेश भेजी जाने वाली डाक।
GU: આંતરરાષ્ટ્રીય ટપાલ = વિદેશ મોકલવામાં આવતી ટપાલ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a海外kaigaiabroad, overseasविदेशવિદેશ
b国内kokunaidomestic (opposite meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 国際郵便 ("international mail") is by definition mail sent 海外 (abroad); 国内 means "domestic," the opposite of international.

Q3

郵便貯金＝郵便局（a. からお金を送る　b. にお金を預ける）Yuubin chokin = yuubinkyoku (a. kara okane o okuru / b. ni okane o azukeru)

EN: Postal savings = depositing money with the post office.
HI: डाक बचत = डाकघर में पैसा जमा करना।
GU: ટપાલ બચત = ટપાલ કચેરીમાં પૈસા જમા કરવા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aからお金を送るkara okane o okuruto send money from (wrong direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bにお金を預けるni okane o azukeruto deposit money withपैसा जमा करनाપૈસા જમા કરવા

Why: 貯金 (savings) means money you deposit/entrust (預ける) to an institution for safekeeping; it isn't money sent away from the post office.

Q4

この紙を（a. 薬局　b. 包帯）に持って行って、薬をもらってください。Kono kami o (a. yakkyoku / b. houtai) ni motte itte, kusuri o moratte kudasai.

EN: Please take this paper to the pharmacy and get the medicine.
HI: कृपया यह काग़ज़ दवाख़ाने ले जाएँ और दवा लें।
GU: કૃપા કરીને આ કાગળ દવાની દુકાને લઈ જાઓ અને દવા લો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a薬局yakkyokua pharmacy, drugstoreदवाख़ानाદવાની દુકાન
b包帯houtaia bandage (not a place you go to)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A prescription slip is taken to a 薬局 (pharmacy) to receive medicine; 包帯 is a bandage, an object, not a destination.

Q5

急病人のために、夜でも開いている入口＝（a. 夜間救急入口　b. 非常口）Kyuubyounin no tame ni, yoru demo hiraite iru iriguchi = (a. yakan kyuukyuu iriguchi / b. hijouguchi)

HI: आपातकालीन मरीज़ों के लिए रात में भी खुला रहने वाला द्वार = रात्रिकालीन आपातकालीन प्रवेश द्वार।
GU: કટોકટીના દર્દીઓ માટે રાત્રે પણ ખુલ્લું રહેતું પ્રવેશદ્વાર = રાત્રિ કટોકટી પ્રવેશદ્વાર.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a夜間救急入口yakan kyuukyuu iriguchithe nighttime emergency entranceरात्रिकालीन आपातकालीन प्रवेश द्वारરાત્રિ કટોકટી પ્રવેશદ્વાર

Q6

初めて診察を受けに来た人は（a. 初診　b. 再診）受付へ行く。Hajimete shinsatsu o uke ni kita hito wa (a. shoshin / b. saishin) uketsuke e iku.

EN: A person coming for a medical consultation for the first time goes to the first-visit reception.
HI: पहली बार जाँच कराने आया व्यक्ति प्रथम-परामर्श काउंटर पर जाता है।
GU: પ્રથમ વખત તપાસ કરાવવા આવેલ વ્યક્તિ પ્રથમ-પરામર્શ કાઉન્ટર પર જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a初診shoshina first-time medical consultationप्रथम चिकित्सकीय परामर्शપ્રથમ તબીબી પરામર્શ
b再診saishina follow-up/repeat consultation (opposite meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 初診 (first visit, using 初 "first") is for someone visiting for the first time; 再診 (using 再 "again") is for someone returning for a repeat visit.

Q7

（a. 科学は化学　b. 化学は科学）の一つです。(a. Kagaku wa kagaku / b. Kagaku wa kagaku) no hitotsu desu.

EN: Chemistry is one (branch) of science.
HI: रसायन विज्ञान, विज्ञान की एक शाखा है।
GU: રસાયણશાસ્ત્ર એ વિજ્ઞાનની એક શાખા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a科学は化学kagaku wa kagaku"Science is chemistry" (backwards — too narrow a claim)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b化学は科学kagaku wa kagaku"Chemistry is (a branch) of science"रसायन विज्ञान, विज्ञान की एक शाखा हैરસાયણશાસ્ત્ર વિજ્ઞાનની એક શાખા છે

Why: Both 科学 and 化学 are pronounced かがく, but 科学 means "science" broadly while 化学 means specifically "chemistry" — chemistry is a subset of science, not the reverse, so only 化学は科学の一つ ("chemistry is one branch of science") makes logical sense.

Q8

手術は（a. 内科　b. 外科）で行います。Shujutsu wa (a. naika / b. geka) de okonaimasu.

EN: Surgery is performed in the surgery department.
HI: ऑपरेशन शल्य चिकित्सा विभाग में किया जाता है।
GU: ઓપરેશન સર્જરી વિભાગમાં કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a内科naikainternal medicine (non-surgical)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b外科gekathe surgery departmentशल्य चिकित्सा विभागસર્જરી વિભાગ

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's vocabulary is full of built-in antonym pairs and same-reading traps — exactly what 練習 Q6, Q7, and Q8 above are testing:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
初診shoshin First-time consultation The patient's very first visit to that doctor/clinic. Registering as a brand-new patient.
再診saishin Follow-up consultation Antonym of 初診 — the patient is returning. A repeat visit for the same condition.
内科naika Internal medicine department Treats illness without surgery. A cold, a fever, a stomach ache.
外科geka Surgery department Antonym of 内科, built from the same 科 — treats conditions via operations. A broken bone, an operation.
科学kagaku Science (broad) Same reading かがく as 化学 but a totally different, broader field. "Science" as an umbrella subject.
化学kagaku Chemistry (specific) A single branch within science — homophone trap with 科学. Naming the chemistry subject specifically.
皮kawa Skin, peel, bark (everyday word) Kun-yomi, used for skin/peel of everyday objects (fruit, animals). "The peel of an apple," "animal hide."
皮膚hifu The skin (medical/formal term) On-yomi compound with 膚 — the formal/medical register for human skin. "A skin specialist," "a skin condition."
Sound-change trap: 局 is read きょく in most compounds (郵便局, 放送局) but becomes っきょく in 薬局 (やっきょく) — a small but easy-to-miss onbin (sound change) that a reading question could test directly.
