Q1

法律を犯したその議員は（　）逮捕されるだろう。Houritsu o okashita sono giin wa (___) taiho sareru darou.

Full sentence: 法律を犯したその議員は近々逮捕されるだろう。Houritsu o okashita sono giin wa chikajika taiho sareru darou.
EN: That lawmaker who broke the law will probably be arrested soon.
HI: क़ानून तोड़ने वाला वह सांसद जल्द ही गिरफ़्तार हो जाएगा।
GU: કાયદો તોડનાર તે ધારાસભ્યની ટૂંક સમયમાં ધરપકડ થશે.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1絶えずtaezuconstantly/incessantly (fits an ongoing action, not a one-time arrest)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2すでにsude nialready (contradicts だろう, which implies it hasn't happened yet)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3せいぜいseizeiat most (fits a maximum limit, not a time prediction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4近々chikajikasoonजल्द हीટૂંક સમયમાં

Why: 近々～だろう ("will probably [happen] soon") fits a future prediction about an arrest; the other options describe an ongoing action, something already completed, or a maximum limit — none of which fit a future one-time event.

Q2

時間がたつにつれて、痛みは（　）消えていった。Jikan ga tatsu ni tsurete, itami wa (___) kiete itta.

Full sentence: 時間がたつにつれて、痛みはしだいに消えていった。Jikan ga tatsu ni tsurete, itami wa shidai ni kiete itta.
EN: As time passed, the pain gradually faded away.

GU: સમય જતાં, દુખાવો ધીમે ધીમે અદૃશ્ય થઈ ગયો.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1たっぷりtappuriamply (a quantity word, not a gradual-process word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2しだいにshidai nigraduallyधीरे-धीरेધીમે ધીમે

4なおnaostill/even more (fits emphasis, not a gradual fade)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Day 4's 雪が次第にとける establishes しだいに as the word for a slow, ongoing process tied to the passage of time — exactly what "as time passed" calls for here.

Q3

Full sentence: ついにわが社は新製品を開発することに成功した。Tsuini waga sha wa shinseihin o kaihatsu suru koto ni seikou shita.

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

Why: Day 5's 思い切って転職する defines 思い切って as firmly deciding and acting on it — a direct match; its near-twin 思い切り instead describes doing something with full force, a different meaning.

Q16

あの事故からもうすぐ1年がたつ。Ano jiko kara mou sugu ichinen ga tatsu.

EN: It will soon be one year since that accident.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1絶えずtaezuconstantly (unrelated to "soon")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2いずれizureeventually, sooner or later (vaguer, less immediate than もうすぐ)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q17

言ってはいけないと思っていたのに、思わず本当のことを言ってしまった。Itte wa ikenai to omotte ita noni, omowazu hontou no koto o itte shimatta.

EN: Even though I thought I shouldn't say it, I ended up blurting out the truth.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1なにしろnanishiroanyway, in any case (unrelated to an involuntary slip)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2ついtsuiunintentionally, before one knows itअनजाने मेंઅજાણતાં
3実にjitsu nitruly, really (an intensifier, not "unintentionally")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4あいにくainikuunfortunately (about bad luck, not an involuntary slip of the tongue)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: つい is the direct synonym for 思わず in this context — both describe doing something involuntarily, "before you know it."

Q19

あんまりおいしくなかったし、高かったし、もうそのレストランには行かない。Anmari oishikunakatta shi, takakatta shi, mou sono resutoran ni wa ikanai.

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1たいしてtaishitenot particularly (with negative)ज़्यादा नहींબહુ નહીં
2はたしてhatashitereally, truly (used in questions, e.g. "is it really...?")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

問題4 — which sentence uses the word correctly? (1問4点)

Q21

まさかmasaka

#JapaneseEnglish
1この犬は頭がよくて、まさかサルか人間のようだ。Kono inu wa atama ga yokute, masaka saru ka ningen no you da."This dog is smart — by no means, it's like a monkey or a human" — まさか needs a context of disbelief at an unwelcome/surprising fact, not a flattering comparison
2まさか彼女が本当は男だったとは、信じられない。Masaka kanojo ga hontou wa otoko datta to wa, shinjirarenai.I can't believe that she was actually a man!
3まさかそれが私の言いたかったことです。Masaka sore ga watashi no iitakatta koto desu."By no means, that's what I wanted to say" — contradicts itself; まさか denies, but the sentence then confirms
4今日は、まさか仕事があるので行けません。Kyou wa, masaka shigoto ga aru node ikemasen."Today, by no means, I have work so I can't go" — まさか doesn't fit stating a mundane, expected reason

Why: まさか typically pairs with とは/とは思わなかった to express disbelief at an unexpected, often unwelcome fact — sentence 2's "I can't believe she was actually a man" fits perfectly; the others misuse it as a flattering comparison, a self-contradiction, or a mundane reason.

Q22

にわかにniwaka ni

#JapaneseEnglish
1急に言われても、にわかには決められない。Kyuu ni iwarete mo, niwaka ni wa kimerarenai.Even if you tell me suddenly, I can't decide right away.
2ショーが7時ににわかに始まった。Shoo ga shichi-ji ni niwaka ni hajimatta."The show suddenly started at 7" — a scheduled 7 o'clock start isn't "sudden/unexpected"
3にわかに100人は集まっているだろう。Niwaka ni hyakunin wa atsumatte iru darou."Probably instantly 100 people have gathered" — doesn't fit にわかに's meaning
4「大変です！にわかに来てください！」"Taihen desu! Niwaka ni kite kudasai!""It's serious! Please come suddenly!" — should be すぐに/急いで, not にわかに

Why: にわかに(は)～ない is a set pattern meaning "cannot readily/immediately [do something]," exactly matching sentence 1's "can't decide right away"; the others misapply にわかに to a scheduled event, an estimate, or an urgent request, none of which fit its "not readily/all of a sudden" sense.

Q23

いっそisso

#JapaneseEnglish
1頼んだピザがいっそ来ていない。Tanonda piza ga isso kite inai."The pizza I ordered rather hasn't come" — doesn't fit; no drastic alternative is being proposed
2あちこち修理するよりも、いっそ家を新しく建て直してしまおう。Achikochi shuuri suru yori mo, isso ie o atarashiku tatenaoshite shimaou.Rather than patching it up here and there, let's just rebuild the house new.
3お酒を加えたら、料理がいっそおいしくなった。Osake o kuwaetara, ryouri ga isso oishiku natta."Adding alcohol made the dish rather delicious" — needs いっそう (even more), not いっそ
4のどがかわいていたので、水をいっそ飲んでしまった。Nodo ga kawaite ita node, mizu o isso nonde shimatta."Since my throat was dry, I rather drank water" — no alternative being weighed, doesn't fit

Why: いっそ proposes a drastic alternative course of action, usually contrasted with a less appealing option via よりも — sentence 2's "rather than patching it up, let's just rebuild" fits exactly; sentence 3 is the classic trap, confusing いっそ with its near-twin いっそう ("even more").

Q24

しょっちゅうshocchuu

#JapaneseEnglish
1そのチケットはしょっちゅう売り切れてしまった。Sono chiketto wa shocchuu urikirete shimatta."That ticket constantly sold out" — a single batch of tickets selling out is a one-time event, not a repeated one
2練習したのでギターがしょっちゅううまくなってきた。Renshuu shita node gitaa ga shocchuu umaku natte kita."Because I practiced, guitar has gotten good all the time" — a gradual-improvement verb needs だんだん/しだいに, not しょっちゅう
3母はしょっちゅう文句を言っている。Haha wa shocchuu monku o itte iru.My mother is complaining all the time.
4単語をしょっちゅう覚えたのに英語の成績が悪かった。Tango o shocchuu oboeta noni eigo no seiseki ga warukatta."Even though I constantly memorized words, my English grade was bad" — 覚えた is a completed one-time action, clashing with しょっちゅう's repeated/ongoing sense

Why: しょっちゅう describes an ongoing, frequently-repeated action — "complaining all the time" fits perfectly; the others attach it to one-time completions or a gradual process, both of which need different words.

Q25

むしろmushiro

#JapaneseEnglish
1あのチンパンジーはサルと言うよりむしろ人間に近いです。Ano chinpanjii wa saru to iu yori mushiro ningen ni chikai desu.Rather than calling that chimpanzee a monkey, it's actually closer to human.
2トレーニングのおかげでむしろ体がきたえられた。Toreiningu no okage de mushiro karada ga kitaerareta."Thanks to training, rather the body got stronger" — no two things are being weighed against each other here
3あの大会社の社長はむしろお金をたくさん持っている。Ano dai-gaisha no shachou wa mushiro okane o takusan motte iru."That big company's president rather has a lot of money" — no comparison is set up, so むしろ has nothing to reverse
4その高層ビルはむしろ完成した。Sono kousou-biru wa mushiro kansei shita."That high-rise rather was completed" — no alternative outcome being weighed, doesn't fit

Why: むしろ requires an explicit or implied comparison between two things, reversing the expected one — Day 4's AよりむしろBのほうがいい pattern, and sentence 1's "rather than X, actually Y" fits exactly; the rest have no comparison for むしろ to act on.
