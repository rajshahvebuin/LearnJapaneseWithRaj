6日目 — いったん覚えたら忘れない！ (Ittan oboetara wasurenai! — You'll never forget once you learn it!)

Source

Theme
決まった言葉があとに来る言葉 (adverbs that call for a fixed following expression — e.g. 別に～ない, おそらく～だろう, いったん～したら), plus a second もっとおぼえましょう table of everyday adverbs
Answer key

Notes
Scene: two girls talking; one announces she's trying out for a singing audition (オーディション), and the other bluntly predicts she'll fail, using とても～ない/たいして/どうせ～だろう from today's own list. The book flags one relation with ⊙ (いったん and ひとまず share a root but diverge into "once you do X" vs. "for now/temporarily").
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression with a different nuance)
Scene

Two girls talk; one is excited about an audition, the other is openly skeptical.

Girl 1: 私、今度歌手のオーディション※を受けるの。Watashi, kondo kashu no oodishon o ukeru no.
("I'm going to try out for a singer audition soon.")

Girl 2: 彼女が歌手になれるとはとても思えない。歌もそううまくないし、顔もたいしてかわいくないし。どうせ落ちるでしょう。Kanojo ga kashu ni nareru to wa totemo omoenai. Uta mo sou umakunai shi, kao mo taishite kawaikunai shi. Douse ochiru deshou.
("I really can't believe she could become a singer. Her singing isn't that great either, and she's not particularly cute. She'll probably fail anyway.")

Girl 2 (to her face): ふーん、がんばってね。Fuun, ganbatte ne.
("Hmm, good luck.")

※オーディション(oodishon) — "audition," a loanword for a tryout for singers, hosts, etc. Today's four fixed-pattern adverbs at work in Girl 2's aside: とても～ない(totemo ~nai) "not at all", そう～ない(sou ~nai) "not that", 大して～ない(taishite ~nai) "not particularly", どうせ～だろう(douse ~darou) "probably anyway".

Q1

これで（a. ひとまず　b. ひとたび）安心です。Kore de (a. hitomazu / b. hitotabi) anshin desu.

Full sentence: これでひとまず安心です。Kore de hitomazu anshin desu.
EN: With this, I can relax for now.
HI: इससे फ़िलहाल मुझे तसल्ली है।
GU: આનાથી હાલ પૂરતી રાહત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aひとまずhitomazufor now, temporarilyफ़िलहालહાલ પૂરતું
bひとたびhitotabionce (something happens) — needs a following ～したら clause, not a standalone statement(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ひとまず describes a temporary sense of relief "for now"; ひとたび is the "once you do X" conditional word and needs a ～したら-type clause to attach to, which isn't present here.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

HI: तुलना करने पर उतना अंतर नहीं था।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとてもtotemocannot possibly/not at all — too absolute a denial for a nuanced comparison result(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's whole lesson is about which negative-degree adverb pairs with which strength of denial — exactly what Q3, Q4, and Q8 above test. Compare the "not ~" family by strength:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
別に～ないbetsu ni ~nai Not particularly (not) Mildest — indifference, "nothing special." "I don't particularly want anything."
そう～ないsou ~nai Not so, not that (much) Softens a claim relative to an implied high bar. "It's not THAT difficult."
大して～ないtaishite ~nai Not much, not particularly Downplays the amount/degree of effort or extent. "I didn't study much."
とても～ないtotemo ~nai Cannot possibly, not at all Strongest — an absolute, emphatic impossibility. "He doesn't look 50 at all."
一切～ないissai ~nai Not at all, none whatsoever Formal, total negation — "zero, full stop." "I have nothing whatsoever to do with it."
⊙ Same word, two meanings: いったん covers two different senses depending on what follows it — いったん約束したら (once you promise...) is the "once X happens" conditional sense, while いったん休んで (take a break for now) is a synonym of ひとまず, "temporarily/for the time being." Only the surrounding sentence tells you which いったん you're looking at, since the word itself is identical.
