JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 4 — 副詞をたっぷり覚えましょう (Fukushi o tappuri oboemashou)

2日目 — いずれ覚えられる！ (Izure oboerareru!)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.62–63
Theme
時に関係がある言葉 (words related to time — まもなく／やがて／たちまち etc.) and 数字や量が後に来る言葉 (words followed by numbers/quantities — ほぼ／およそ／せめて etc.)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "63ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.65 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Opens with narration rather than a spoken dialogue: a woman reflects on an ex-boyfriend and tries calling him. The .vd-warmup section below reproduces that narration.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A woman thinks back on an ex-boyfriend and tries to reach him by phone.

Narration: 恋人と別れてやがて半年になるが、まだ彼のことを考えてしまう。Koibito to wakarete yagate hantoshi ni naru ga, mada kare no koto o kangaete shimau.
("It's soon been half a year since I broke up with my boyfriend, but I still find myself thinking about him.")

せめて声だけでも聞きたいと思い、電話したが、すでに携帯電話の番号も変わってしまっていた。Semete koe dake demo kikitai to omoi, denwa shita ga, sude ni keitai denwa no bangou mo kawatte shimatte ita.
("Thinking I wanted to at least hear his voice, I called him, but his cell phone number had already changed.")

Caption: "You will eventually remember it!" (总会记下来的！／어차피 외워지겠지!)

Q1

申し込みの期限は（a. とっくに　b. そのうち）過ぎてしまった。Moushikomi no kigen wa (a. tokku ni / b. sono uchi) sugite shimatta.

Full sentence: 申し込みの期限は、とっくに過ぎてしまった。Moushikomi no kigen wa, tokku ni sugite shimatta.
EN: The application deadline had already long passed.
HI: आवेदन की समय-सीमा बहुत पहले ही निकल चुकी थी।
GU: અરજીની સમયમર્યાદા ઘણા સમય પહેલા જ પસાર થઈ ગઈ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aとっくにtokku nilong ago, a long time sinceबहुत पहले हीઘણા સમય પહેલા જ
bそのうちsono uchibefore long, someday (a future event, wrong tense/direction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: とっくに ("long ago already") describes something that happened well in the past, matching a deadline that has passed; そのうち points to something happening in the future, the wrong direction here.

HI: इमारत लगभग पूरी हो चुकी है।
GU: ઇમારત લગભગ પૂર્ણ થઈ ગઈ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's words cluster into two confusable groups — "soon/eventually" time words and "approximately" quantity words — exactly what the exercises above test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
そのうちsono uchi Before long, someday (future, vague) Points forward in time, casually. "You'll understand someday."
とっくにtokku ni Long ago already (past) Points backward in time — the opposite direction of そのうち. "The deadline already passed long ago."
ただちにtadachi ni Immediately, at once Urgency with no delay at all. "You should leave right away."
ようやくyouyaku Finally, at long last Relief after a long wait — the opposite pacing of ただちに. "The long rainy season finally ended."
ほぼhobo Almost, nearly (a state close to complete) Describes how close something is to being done. "The building is almost complete."
およそoyoso Roughly, approximately (a number/amount) Precedes a specific figure, not a completion state. "Roughly two weeks."
たったtatta Only, merely (emphasizing smallness) Stresses how surprisingly small/few something is. "All by just one person."
せめてsemete At least, if only (a hoped-for minimum) Expresses a wish for a bare minimum, not a measured fact. "I'd at least like to score 70."
Direction trap: そのうち (future) and とっくに (past) both translate loosely as "at some point," but point in opposite directions along the timeline — mixing them up flips the meaning of a sentence entirely.
