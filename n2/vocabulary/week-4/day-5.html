JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 4 — 副詞をたっぷり覚えましょう (Fukushi o tappuri oboemashou)

5日目 — 取りあえず覚えよう！ (Toriaezu oboeyou! — I'll memorize them for the time being!)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.68–69
Theme
形が似ている言葉 (words similar in form — near-homophone/near-form adverb pairs that are easy to mix up, e.g. まさか vs まさに, 思い切り vs 思い切って, いっそう vs いっそ)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "69ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.71 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Opens with a short first-person narration (someone recounting passing a hard exam) rather than a two-person dialogue, using まさに/まさか/思わず in sequence. The book groups several near-identical-looking adverb pairs together with a bracket to show they share a root but diverge in form/meaning — reproduced below as combined headword rows.
Scene

A first-person narration about passing a difficult exam, reacting with a mix of relief and disbelief.

試験はとても難しかった。Shiken wa totemo muzukashikatta.
("The exam was very difficult.")
まさに先輩の言ったとおりだ。Masani senpai no itta toori da.
("It's exactly as my senpai said it would be.")
まさか自分が合格するとは思わなかった。Masaka jibun ga goukaku suru to wa omowanakatta.
("I never imagined that I myself would pass.")
発表を見に行ったとき、うれしくて思わず大声で叫んでしまった。Happyou o mi ni itta toki, ureshikute omowazu ougoe de sakende shimatta.
("When I went to see the results posted, I was so happy that I unintentionally shouted out loud.")
やったー！Yattaa! ("I did it!")

Three look-alike adverbs in one breath: まさに(masani) "exactly/precisely", まさか(masaka) "never would have imagined", 思わず(omowazu) "unintentionally" — today's theme is telling these near-twins apart.

Q1

（a. 思い切り　b. 思い切って）マンションを買った。(a. Omoikiri / b. Omoikitte) manshon o katta.

Full sentence: 思い切ってマンションを買った。Omoikitte manshon o katta.
EN: I took the plunge and bought a condo.
HI: हिम्मत करके मैंने एक फ़्लैट ख़रीद लिया।
GU: હિંમત કરીને મેં એક ફ્લેટ ખરીદી લીધો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a思い切りomoikiriwith all one's might (a noun/adverb of force, not a decisive-action verb form)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b思い切ってomoikittetaking the plunge, resolutelyहिम्मत करकेહિંમત કરીને

Why: 思い切って describes making a bold, resolved decision (like buying a condo); 思い切り describes doing something with full physical/emotional force (思い切りしかられた, "scolded thoroughly"), which doesn't fit a purchase decision.

GU: આ દુકાનમાં હંમેશની જેમ ગ્રાહકોની અછત છે.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a今にもima ni moat any momentकिसी भी पलગમે ત્યારે

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's entire lesson is built around near-identical-looking adverb pairs — exactly what Q1, Q5, Q7, and Q8 above test. Compare the trickiest ones:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
まさにmasani Exactly, precisely / just about to Confirms something is precisely so, or right on the verge of happening. "That's exactly right." / "Dawn is just breaking."
まさかmasaka Never would have imagined Near-identical spelling to まさに, but expresses disbelief/shock, usually with とは. "I never imagined this could happen."
思い切りomoikiri With all one's might (adverbial noun) Describes the physical/emotional intensity of an action already happening to you. "I got scolded thoroughly."
思い切ってomoikitte Taking the plunge, resolutely The -te form: describes making a bold decision to act. "I took the plunge and changed jobs."
いっそうissou Even more so An intensifier meaning "to a greater degree." "It got even more delicious."
いっそisso Might as well, would rather One character shorter than いっそう, but proposes a drastic alternative action instead. "I'd rather just quit entirely."
今にもima ni mo At any moment An event looks imminent, usually with ～そうだ. "It looks like it's about to rain."
今さらima sara At this late stage It's too late to change course now, often with negative/resigned nuance. "It's too late to back out now."
The 今〜 family: 今に, 今にも, 今さら, 未だに, ただ今, and たった今 all start with the same kanji but point in six different time-directions — 今に ("someday, eventually"), 今にも ("any second now"), 今さら ("too late at this point"), 未だに ("still, even now, unchanged"), ただ今 ("right now"), and たった今 ("just now, moments ago"). Memorize each one anchored to its own example sentence rather than the kanji alone, since the kanji gives almost no clue to which direction in time it points.
