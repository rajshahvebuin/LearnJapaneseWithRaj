JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 4 — 副詞をたっぷり覚えましょう (Fukushi o tappuri oboemashou)

1日目 — なんとか覚えよう！ (Nantoka oboeyou!)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.60–61
Theme
形が似ている言葉 (adverbs whose forms look alike but differ in meaning/use — つい／ついに, どうしても／どうも, なんとか／どうにか, etc.)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "61ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.63 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Week 4's theme is 副詞 (adverbs); this day opens with a short monologue (a student struggling to keep up a Japanese diary) plus an offscreen encouraging voice, rather than a two-person dialogue — the .vd-warmup section below reflects that.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
❶ = 注意しましょう (a usage caution)
Scene

A student struggles to keep up a Japanese-language habit.

Him (thinking): 日本語がどうもうまく話せない。なるべく日記も日本語で書くようにしているが、つい なまけてしまう……Nihongo ga doumo umaku hanasenai. Narubeku nikki mo nihongo de kaku you ni shite iru ga, tsui namakete shimau...
("I really can't speak Japanese well, somehow. I try to write my diary in Japanese too, as much as possible, but I end up being lazy about it without meaning to...")

Offscreen voice (encouraging): あきらめないで なんとか がんばりなさい。Akiramenaide nantoka ganbarinasai.
("Don't give up — somehow, do your best.")

Caption: "I'll try to memorize them!" (想方设法记住吧！／어떻게든 외우자!)

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

お金を貯めて、（a. つい　b. ついに）欲しい車を手に入れた。Okane o tamete, (a. tsui / b. tsuini) hoshii kuruma o te ni ireta.

Full sentence: お金を貯めて、ついに欲しい車を手に入れた。Okane o tamete, tsuini hoshii kuruma o te ni ireta.
EN: I saved up money and finally got the car I wanted.
HI: पैसे बचाकर, आख़िरकार मुझे मनचाही कार मिल गई।
GU: પૈસા બચાવીને, છેવટે મને મનગમતી કાર મળી ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aついtsuiunintentionally (wrong sense — no unintended action here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bついにtsui nifinally, at lastआख़िरकारછેવટે

Why: ついに ("finally, at last") fits reaching a long-awaited goal after saving money; つい means "unintentionally," describing an action done without meaning to — the wrong sense here.

Full sentence: 急げば、なんとか約束の時間に間に合うでしょう。Isogeba, nantoka yakusoku no jikan ni maniau deshou.

HI: अगर हम जल्दी करें, तो किसी तरह समय पर पहुँच जाएँगे।
GU: જો આપણે ઉતાવળ કરીએ, તો કોઈક રીતે સમયસર પહોંચી જઈશું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

HI: लगता है मुझे किसी तरह ज़ुकाम हो गया है।

きょうの一言 (Today's saying): 全部覚えるのは、必ずしも不可能ではありません。Zenbu oboeru no wa, kanarazu shimo fukanou de wa arimasen. — "Memorizing everything isn't necessarily impossible."

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is built around adverbs that "look alike" but diverge in meaning — exactly what Q1–Q8 above test. Compare the key pairs:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
ついtsui Unintentionally, without meaning to An accidental slip — you didn't mean to do it. "I lied without meaning to."
ついにtsui ni Finally, at last A long-awaited outcome is reached. "Finally, I got the car I wanted."
どうしてもdoushite mo Absolutely (not); no matter what Willpower/determination or an absolute impossibility. "I cannot forgive it, no matter what."
どうもdoumo Somehow, it seems; (greeting) thanks A soft hedge on a feeling/impression, not willpower. "It seems I've caught a cold."
なんとかnantoka Somehow, manage to; do something about Effort toward a goal, or fixing a problem. "We somehow made it in time." / "Do something about the trash."
なんとなくnantonaku Vaguely, for no particular reason A fuzzy feeling/impression, no effort involved. "This book seems sort of interesting."
確かtashika If I recall correctly (hazy memory) A tentative recollection — might be wrong. "I believe she got married, but..."
確かにtashika ni Certainly, definitely (firm assertion) A confident, verifiable claim. "I was definitely at the office until 5."
Usage caution (❶): なるほど is a natural way to show you're following along in conversation — but a subordinate saying it to a superior can come across as presumptuous or rude, since it implies you're "evaluating" what they said. Prefer more neutral acknowledgments toward someone above you.
