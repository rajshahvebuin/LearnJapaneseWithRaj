JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 5 — やさしい漢字で書きますが… (Yasashii kanji de kakimasu ga...)

4日目 — 土地・名字・発売 (Tochi, Myouji, Hatsubai — Land, Surname, Sale)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.82–83
Theme
Four kanji roots — 地 (land), 名 (name), 発 (emit/issue), 会 (meet) — that recombine into many everyday N2 words, several with tricky/irregular readings (土地=とち, 名字=みょうじ, 発売=はつばい). This week's whole point: don't assume one kanji always keeps the same reading.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day (p.83) are printed in the "83ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.85 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Opens with a narrator scene (soba shop, missed train) instead of a two-speaker dialogue — this week's days are organized as kanji-family word groups, not situational dialogues. Two book errata caught and corrected below: 地理学's English gloss ("geology") should be "geography"; and the English column for 会計/会合 is swapped in print (confirmed against the Chinese/Korean columns).
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

駅の中にあるそば屋で、この地方の名物のそばを食べたが、会計を済ませている間に、乗ろうと思っていた電車が発車してしまった。
Eki no naka ni aru soba-ya de, kono chihou no meibutsu no soba o tabeta ga, kaikei o sumasete iru aida ni, norou to omotte ita densha ga hassha shite shimatta.

("At a soba stand inside the station, I ate the local specialty soba, but while I was settling the bill, the train I had been planning to catch departed.")

地方・名物・会計・発車 — four everyday words built from this day's four kanji roots (地・名・会・発), used naturally in one unlucky commute

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.83) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.83). Answers confirmed from the "83ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.85.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

東京で開かれる（a. 大会　b. 開会）に出るため上京した。Toukyou de hirakareru (a. taikai / b. kaikai) ni deru tame joukyou shita.

Full sentence: 東京で開かれる大会に出るため上京した。Toukyou de hirakareru taikai ni deru tame joukyou shita.
EN: I went up to Tokyo to attend an assembly/tournament held there.
HI: टोक्यो में आयोजित सम्मेलन में भाग लेने के लिए टोक्यो गया/गई।
GU: ટોક્યોમાં યોજાતા સંમેલનમાં ભાગ લેવા ટોક્યો ગયો/ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a大会taikaian assembly/tournament (something you "attend/出る")સંમેલનસંમેલન
b開会kaikaiopening (a session) — a verb/event, not a thing you "出る"(attend) as a noun here(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 大会に出る ("to attend/take part in an assembly or tournament") is the natural collocation; 開会 names the act of opening an event, not the event itself that one "attends."

Q2

電話の（a. 発明者　b. 発見者）は、一般的にベルだと言われている。Denwa no (a. hatsumeisha / b. hakkensha) wa, ippanteki ni Beru da to iwarete iru.

Full sentence: 電話の発明者は、一般的にベルだと言われている。Denwa no hatsumeisha wa, ippanteki ni Beru da to iwarete iru.
EN: The inventor of the telephone is generally said to be Bell.
HI: टेलीफ़ोन के आविष्कारक को आम तौर पर बेल कहा जाता है।
GU: ટેલિફોનના શોધકને સામાન્ય રીતે બેલ કહેવાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a発明者hatsumeishathe inventor (someone who creates something new)आविष्कारकશોધક (આવિષ્કારક)
b発見者hakkenshathe discoverer (someone who finds something that already existed)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: The telephone was created from nothing, so its maker is the 発明者 ("inventor"); 発見者 ("discoverer") fits something that already existed and was found, like a star or a new species.

Q3

私の（a. 地下　b. 地元）では、この魚は食べない。Watashi no (a. chika / b. jimoto) de wa, kono sakana wa tabenai.

Full sentence: 私の地元では、この魚は食べない。Watashi no jimoto de wa, kono sakana wa tabenai.
EN: In my hometown/local area, we don't eat this fish.
HI: मेरे इलाक़े में यह मछली नहीं खाई जाती।
GU: મારા વિસ્તારમાં આ માછલી ખવાતી નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a地下chikaunderground (a physical location, not "one's home area")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b地元jimotoone's home area/localityસ્થાનિક વિસ્તારસ્થાનિક વિસ્તાર

Why: 地元 ("one's home area") is what a speaker means by "where I'm from"; 地下 is a literal physical location (underground), unrelated to a home region.

Q4

東北（a. 地区　b. 地方）に伝わる昔話を聞いた。Touhoku (a. chiku / b. chihou) ni tsutawaru mukashibanashi o kiita.

Full sentence: 東北地方に伝わる昔話を聞いた。Touhoku chihou ni tsutawaru mukashibanashi o kiita.
EN: I heard a folktale passed down in the Tohoku region.
HI: मैंने तोहोकु क्षेत्र में प्रचलित एक लोककथा सुनी।
GU: મેં તોહોકુ પ્રદેશમાં પ્રચલિત એક લોકકથા સાંભળી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a地区chikua district/zone (a small administrative subdivision, too narrow for "Tohoku")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b地方chihoua region (a large area like Tohoku)પ્રદેશપ્રદેશ

Why: 東北地方 ("the Tohoku region") is the fixed name for that large area of Japan; 地区 names a much smaller zone, like a neighborhood or ward, and doesn't fit a region the size of Tohoku.

Q5

納豆は水戸の（a. 名所　b. 名物）です。Nattou wa Mito no (a. meisho / b. meibutsu) desu.

Full sentence: 納豆は水戸の名物です。Nattou wa Mito no meibutsu desu.
EN: Natto is a specialty of Mito.
HI: नत्तो मिटो की ख़ास चीज़ है।
GU: નાત્તો મિટોની ખાસ વસ્તુ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a名所meishoa famous place/sight (a location you visit, not a food product)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b名物meibutsua local specialty (a product/dish)ખાસ વસ્તુખાસ વસ્તુ

Q6

ピザの（a. 地味　b. 生地）は小麦粉で作る。Piza no (a. jimi / b. kiji) wa komugiko de tsukuru.

Full sentence: ピザの生地は小麦粉で作る。Piza no kiji wa komugiko de tsukuru.
EN: Pizza dough is made from wheat flour.
HI: पिज़्ज़ा का आटा गेहूं के आटे से बनाया जाता है।
GU: પિઝાનો લોટ ઘઉંના લોટમાંથી બનાવાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a地味jimiplain/subdued (describes style, not a physical dough)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q7

GU: નવી ચલણી નોટો જારી કરવામાં આવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1発行hakkouissuing (official documents, currency)जारी करनाજારી કરવું
2発売hatsubaiputting on sale (a product for purchase, not currency)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

The whole point of this week is that a familiar kanji doesn't keep one fixed reading or meaning across compounds. Today's four families show that clearly:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
地方chihou A (large) region Broad geographic area — 東北地方, 関東地方. "The Tohoku region."
地区chiku A (small) district/zone A narrow administrative subdivision — 住宅地区. "A residential district."
名所meisho A famous place A location you visit/sightsee. "A tourist spot."
名物meibutsu A local specialty A product/dish associated with a place. "A regional specialty food."
発明するhatsumei suru To invent Creating something that didn't exist before. "Inventing the telephone."
発見するhakken suru To discover Finding something that already existed. "Discovering a new star."
Reading trap — same kanji, different sound: 地 reads ち in 地方・地区・地理・地下・地元 (all "geography/place" senses), but じ in 地味 ("plain," an unrelated adjective of style) and shows up again, unread as ち or じ but as part of 生地 (きじ, "fabric/dough" — 地 here isn't even pronounced independently). Never assume a kanji's reading carries over between compounds — check each word on its own.
Book erratum — 会計 vs 会合: this page's own English column swaps the two: it prints "hold a meeting" next to 会計 and "pay the bill" next to 会合. The Chinese (结完帐 / 举办聚会) and Korean (회계를 끝마치다 / 회합을 열다) columns agree with each other and with the kanji themselves: 会計 = settle/pay a bill, 会合 = hold a meeting. This page uses the corrected pairing.
