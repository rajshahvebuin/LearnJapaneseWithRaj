2日目 — 夜中・世間・作業 (Yonaka, Seken, Sagyou)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.78–79
Theme
Night, society, work (夜晚、社会、工作) — five easy-looking kanji (大・中・間・作・通) whose common compounds mix on-yomi and kun-yomi readings
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "79ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.81 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
No dialogue today either — the opening illustration shows someone finishing a beaded craft project late at night, relieved, next to a caption sentence using this day's vocabulary (夜中・集中・作業・作品).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Opening

Illustration: a person finishes a beaded craft piece late at night, then wipes their brow, relieved.

Caption: 昨日、夜中まで集中して作業したので、思ったより早く作品が完成した。Kinou, yonaka made shuuchuu shite sagyou shita node, omotta yori hayaku sakuhin ga kansei shita.
("Since I worked with concentration until the middle of the night yesterday, the piece was finished sooner than I expected.")

きょうの一言 (kyou no hitokoto — "today's saying"): もっと集中して勉強しましょう。 Motto shuuchuu shite benkyou shimashou. — "Let's study with more concentration."

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.79) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.79). Answers confirmed from the "79ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.81.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

クラスの（a. 大半　b. 大小）が自転車で通学している。Kurasu no (a. taihan / b. daishou) ga jitensha de tsuugaku shite iru.

Full sentence: クラスの大半が自転車で通学している。Kurasu no taihan ga jitensha de tsuugaku shite iru.
EN: The majority of the class commutes to school by bicycle.
HI: कक्षा के अधिकांश छात्र साइकिल से स्कूल आते-जाते हैं।
GU: વર્ગના મોટાભાગના વિદ્યાર્થીઓ સાયકલથી શાળાએ જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a大半taihanthe majorityअधिकांशમોટાભાગના
b大小daishoubig and small (sizes, unrelated to a proportion of people)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 大半が…している ("the majority ~") describes a proportion of people; 大小 describes a range of sizes (like boxes), not a headcount majority.

Q2

この作品を作るのには、（a. 手間　b. 世間）がかかった。Kono sakuhin o tsukuru no ni wa, (a. tema / b. seken) ga kakatta.

Full sentence: この作品を作るのには、手間がかかった。Kono sakuhin o tsukuru no ni wa, tema ga kakatta.
EN: It took a lot of time and effort to make this piece.
HI: इस कृति को बनाने में बहुत मेहनत लगी।
GU: આ કૃતિ બનાવવામાં ઘણી મહેનત લાગી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手間tematime and effort, troubleमेहनतમહેનત
b世間sekensociety, the public (can't be "taken" the way effort is)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 手間がかかる ("it takes effort/trouble") is the fixed phrase for something requiring time and labor; 世間 means "society/the public," an unrelated noun that doesn't pair with がかかる this way.

Q3

この（a. 作業　b. 集中）が終わったら、次に進みましょう。Kono (a. sagyou / b. shuuchuu) ga owattara, tsugi ni susumimashou.

Full sentence: この作業が終わったら、次に進みましょう。Kono sagyou ga owattara, tsugi ni susumimashou.
EN: Once this task is finished, let's move on to the next one.
HI: यह काम ख़त्म होने के बाद, अगले काम पर चलते हैं।
GU: આ કામ પૂરું થાય પછી, આગળ વધીએ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a作業sagyoua task, piece of workकामકામ
b集中shuuchuuconcentration (a mental state, not a task that "finishes")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 作業 is the countable task/work itself that can "finish" (終わる); 集中 means "concentration," a mental state rather than a task with an endpoint.

Q5

昨日、友人と（a. 夜中　b. 夜間）まで電話で話をした。Kinou, yuujin to (a. yonaka / b. yakan) made denwa de hanashi o shita.

Full sentence: 昨日、友人と夜中まで電話で話をした。Kinou, yuujin to yonaka made denwa de hanashi o shita.
EN: Yesterday, I talked on the phone with my friend until the middle of the night.
HI: कल, मैंने दोस्त से आधी रात तक फ़ोन पर बात की।
GU: ગઈકાલે, મેં મિત્ર સાથે મધરાત સુધી ફોન પર વાત કરી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a夜中yonakamidnight, middle of the nightआधी रातમધરાત

HI: ज़्यादा अच्छी नहीं बनी, फिर भी कृति पूरी हो गई।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart words that share a kanji but split into very different meanings:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
夜中yonaka Midnight, the middle of the night Casual, conversational — a specific late point in the night. "We talked on the phone until midnight."
夜間yakan Nighttime (as a general span) Formal/broad register — used in set phrases and signage. "夜間の外出" (an outing at night), night-shift notices.
中心chuushin The center, a hub A noun naming a place/point. "Tokyo is the center of the economy."
集中(する)shuuchuu (suru) To concentrate; concentration Describes a process — things or attention gathering. "Study with concentration," "population is concentrated here."
手間tema Time and effort, trouble What an activity costs you in labor. "手間がかかる" (it takes effort).
世間seken Society, the public The world/people around you, as a collective. "世間のきびしさ" (how harsh the real world is).
名作meisaku A masterpiece Carries a value judgment — implies high quality. Praising a celebrated novel or film.
作品sakuhin A work, a piece Neutral — no judgment about quality. Any created work, good or not.
Reading trap: 大 alternates between だい (大工, 大小) and たい (大気, 大半, 大金, 大木) with no visible rule from spelling alone — each compound's reading has to be memorized individually, exactly like last lesson's 物/日/年.
