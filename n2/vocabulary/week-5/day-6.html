6日目 — 本気・気楽・目安 (Honki, Kiraku, Meyasu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.86–87
Theme
Four kanji roots — 本 (main/real), 家 (house/professional "-ist"), 目 (eye/goal), 気 (spirit/feeling) — recombined into everyday N2 words. Book erratum: the printed English gloss for this trio reads "Official name, easy going, aim," which doesn't match 本気/気楽/目安 at all — the Chinese (认真、轻松、目标) and Korean (진심·속편함·기준) columns agree on the real meanings: earnestness/seriousness, carefree/relaxed, and a target/rough guideline. We use those corrected meanings throughout this page.
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day (p.87) are printed in the "87ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.90 (Day 7's third page, since Day 7 runs three pages) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Opens with a narrator scene: he tells a strong opponent he'll take today's match casually, while thinking he actually needs to get serious or he'll lose. Same kanji-family word-group structure as 4・5日目 — four "おぼえましょう" tables (本・家・目・気), no dialogue.
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

口では「今日の試合は、気楽にやるよ。」と平気な顔をして言ったが、相手は最近注目されている選手だ。本気を出さないと負けてしまうかもしれない。
Kuchi de wa "Kyou no shiai wa, kiraku ni yaru yo." to heiki na kao o shite itta ga, aite wa saikin chuumoku sarete iru senshu da. Honki o dasanai to makete shimau kamoshirenai.

("Out loud I said, with a straight face, 'I'll take today's match easy,' but my opponent is a player who's been getting a lot of attention lately. If I don't get serious, I might end up losing.")

気楽・本気 — the same speaker's outward calm (気楽) hides an inward need to get serious (本気を出す)

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.87) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.87). Answers confirmed from the "87ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.90.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

赤ちゃんの成長の（a. 注目　b. 目安）は体重の増え方です。Akachan no seichou no (a. chuumoku / b. meyasu) wa taijuu no fuekata desu.

Full sentence: 赤ちゃんの成長の目安は体重の増え方です。Akachan no seichou no meyasu wa taijuu no fuekata desu.
EN: A guideline for a baby's growth is the way its weight increases.
HI: बच्चे के विकास का एक मापदंड वज़न बढ़ने का तरीक़ा है।
GU: બાળકના વિકાસનું એક ધોરણ વજન વધવાની રીત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a注目chuumokupaying attention (an action, not a measurable indicator)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b目安meyasua rough guideline/benchmarkઅંદાજિત ધોરણઅંદાજિત ધોરણ

Why: 目安 ("a rough guideline/indicator") is exactly the kind of measurable benchmark described here; 注目 names the act of paying attention, not a standard you measure something against.

Q2

熱が下がってだいぶ（a. 気味　b. 気分）が良くなった。Netsu ga sagatte daibu (a. kimi / b. kibun) ga yoku natta.

Full sentence: 熱が下がってだいぶ気分が良くなった。Netsu ga sagatte daibu kibun ga yoku natta.
EN: My fever went down and I feel a lot better now.
HI: बुख़ार उतरने से मुझे काफ़ी अच्छा महसूस हो रहा है।
GU: તાવ ઉતરવાથી મને ઘણું સારું લાગે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a気味kimia tinge/tendency of (attaches to a noun/verb stem, e.g. 疲れ気味 — doesn't stand alone as "feeling good")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b気分kibuna feeling/moodમૂડ/અનુભૂતિમૂડ/અનુભૂતિ

Q3

銀行で（a. 本人　b. 本名）の確認のため免許証を見せるように言われた。Ginkou de (a. honnin / b. honmyou) no kakunin no tame menkyoshou o miseru you ni iwareta.

EN: At the bank, I was told to show my license to confirm my identity.
HI: बैंक में पहचान की पुष्टि के लिए लाइसेंस दिखाने को कहा गया।
GU: બેંકમાં ઓળખની ખાતરી માટે લાયસન્સ બતાવવાનું કહેવામાં આવ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a本人honninthe person themself (identity verification)સ્વયં તે વ્યક્તિસ્વયં તે વ્યક્તિ

HI: वह बिना किसी हिचक के झूठ बोलता है, इसलिए उस पर भरोसा नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: તે કોઈ પણ સંકોચ વગર જૂઠું બોલે છે, એટલે તેના પર ભરોસો કરી શકાય નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a平気heikiunfazed, without any qualms (lying shamelessly, unbothered by it)બેપરવાહીથીબેપરવાહીથી

HI: उम्र बढ़ने के साथ मेरे सफ़ेद बाल दिखने लगे हैं।
GU: ઉંમર વધવાની સાથે મારા સફેદ વાળ દેખાવા લાગ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a注目するchuumoku suruto pay attention to (transitive — needs someone doing the noticing; gray hair can't "pay attention")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms built from 本・家・目・気 — exactly what Q4, Q5, and Q6/Q7 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
本気honki Seriousness, earnest effort Genuine, all-out effort toward a goal. "I need to get serious."
平気heiki Unfazed, without qualms Doing something (often wrong) without a second thought. "He lies shamelessly."
気楽（な）kiraku (na) Carefree, relaxed A relaxed attitude toward a task. "Let's take it easy."
目立つmedatsu To stand out (intransitive) The subject itself becomes noticeable, no outside agent needed. "Gray hair stands out."
注目するchuumoku suru To pay attention to (transitive) An outside agent actively watches/focuses on something. "I'll keep watching his progress."
一家ikka A (whole) family/household Attaches to a surname: "the ~ family." "The Tanaka family went on a trip."
大家ooya A landlord An unrelated role — someone who rents out property. "The apartment's landlord."
Book erratum, corrected: the printed English gloss for this day's title trio says "Official name, easy going, aim" — none of which matches 本気 (seriousness), 気楽 (carefree), or 目安 (a rough guideline). The Chinese (认真、轻松、目标) and Korean (진심·속편함·기준) columns agree with each other and with each word's actual usage in the vocabulary table, so this page uses the corrected meanings throughout.
