5日目 — 手品・合図・強気 (Tejina, Aizu, Tsuyoki — Magic, Cue, Aggressive)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.84–85
Theme
Four more kanji roots — 手 (hand), 合 (fit/together), 体 (body), 強 (strong) — recombined into everyday N2 words, several with irregular readings (手品=てじな, 合図=あいず, 強気=つよき).
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day (p.85) are printed in the "85ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.87 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Opens with a narrator scene: overwhelmed by too much work, the speaker is heartened when a coworker offers help, citing his own 体力. Same kanji-family word-group structure as 4日目 — four "おぼえましょう" tables (手・合・体・強), no dialogue.
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

人手が足りなくて困っていたところ、田中さんから「いつでも手伝うよ。ぼくは体力もあるし。」という力強い言葉をもらった。
Hitode ga tarinakute komatte ita tokoro, Tanaka-san kara "Itsudemo tetsudau yo. Boku wa tairyoku mo aru shi." to iu chikarazuyoi kotoba o moratta.

("Just as I was struggling from a lack of manpower, Tanaka-san gave me the encouraging words: 'I'll help out anytime — I've got the stamina for it too.'")

人手・体力・力強い — three 手/体/強-family words woven into one moment of relief at being offered help

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.85) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.85). Answers confirmed from the "85ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.87.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

12時にロビーに（a. 集合　b. 合同）してください。Juuni-ji ni robii ni (a. shuugou / b. goudou) shite kudasai.

Full sentence: 12時にロビーに集合してください。Juuni-ji ni robii ni shuugou shite kudasai.
EN: Please gather in the lobby at 12 o'clock.
HI: कृपया 12 बजे लॉबी में इकट्ठा हो जाएँ।
GU: કૃપા કરીને 12 વાગ્યે લોબીમાં ભેગા થાઓ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a集合shuugougathering/assembling (people converging at a spot)ભેગા થવુંભેગા થવું
b合同goudoujoint/combined (describes merging two groups, not simply meeting up)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 集合する ("to gather/assemble") is exactly what people do when told to meet in a lobby at a set time; 合同 describes two separate groups joining into one activity, not a simple meet-up instruction.

Q2

セールスマンに（a. 強気　b. 強引）に契約させられた。Seerusuman ni (a. tsuyoki / b. gouin) ni keiyaku saserareta.

Full sentence: セールスマンに強引に契約させられた。Seerusuman ni gouin ni keiyaku saserareta.
EN: I was forced by the salesman into signing a contract.
HI: सेल्समैन ने ज़बरदस्ती अनुबंध पर हस्ताक्षर करवा दिए।
GU: સેલ્સમેને બળજબરીથી કરાર પર સહી કરાવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a強気tsuyokiassertive/bullish (an attitude one holds, not how one forces someone else)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b強引gouinforceful/pushy (imposing one's will on someone else)બળજબરીપૂર્વકબળજબરીપૂર્વક

Why: 強引に契約させる ("to force someone into a contract") describes imposing one's will on another person; 強気 describes one's own confident/bullish attitude, not an action done to someone else.

Q3

携帯電話には（a. 強力な　b. 力強い）磁石が使われている。Keitai denwa ni wa (a. kyouryoku na / b. chikarazuyoi) jishaku ga tsukawarete iru.

Full sentence: 携帯電話には強力な磁石が使われている。Keitai denwa ni wa kyouryoku na jishaku ga tsukawarete iru.
EN: A powerful magnet is used in cellphones.
HI: मोबाइल फ़ोन में एक शक्तिशाली चुंबक इस्तेमाल किया जाता है।
GU: મોબાઇલ ફોનમાં એક શક્તિશાળી ચુંબક વપરાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a強力なkyouryoku napowerful (describes physical strength/force of an object)શક્તિશાળીશક્તિશાળી
b力強いchikarazuyoipowerful/reassuring (describes speeches, allies — an emotionally reassuring quality, not a magnet's pull)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 強力な磁石 ("a powerful magnet") describes raw physical force in an object; 力強い is reserved for things that feel reassuring or inspiring, like a speech or an ally, not a magnet's strength.

Q4

運動を続けているのに（a. 体力　b. 体重）が増えてしまった。Undou o tsuzukete iru noni (a. tairyoku / b. taijuu) ga fuete shimatta.

Full sentence: 運動を続けているのに体重が増えてしまった。Undou o tsuzukete iru noni taijuu ga fuete shimatta.
EN: Even though I've kept exercising, my weight has gone up.
HI: व्यायाम जारी रखने के बावजूद, मेरा वज़न बढ़ गया।
GU: કસરત ચાલુ રાખવા છતાં, મારું વજન વધી ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a体力tairyokustamina (would logically go up with exercise, not clash with "increased despite exercising")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b体重taijuubody weight (the ironic thing that rose despite exercise)વજનવજન

Why: The sentence's irony ("despite exercising, ~ increased") only works with 体重 ("weight"), since exercise is expected to raise 体力 ("stamina") but lower or maintain weight — 体重 increasing anyway is the surprising part.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms built from 手・合・体・強 — exactly what Q2/Q3, Q4, Q5/Q6, and Q7 test:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
強引（な）gouin (na) Forceful, pushy Imposing one's will onto someone else. "He forced me into it."
強気（な）tsuyoki (na) Assertive, bullish One's own confident stance/attitude. "A bold/assertive remark."
強力（な）kyouryoku (na) Powerful (physical force) Raw strength of an object/substance. "A powerful magnet/glue."
力強いchikarazuyoi Powerful, reassuring Emotionally inspiring — speeches, allies. "A reassuring, inspiring ally."
体力tairyoku Physical stamina The body's capacity for exertion. "I've lost my stamina."
気力kiryoku Willpower/drive Mental motivation, independent of the body. "I've lost the will to work."
手入れteire Maintenance/upkeep Routine care of an object, garden, machine. "Garden/camera maintenance."
手当てteate First aid/treatment (or: an allowance) Care specifically for an injury or ailment. "Treating a wound."
Reading trap: 手 reads て in 手当て・手入れ・手前・手作り but た in 手品 (from a fossilized older reading), and じな (音読み) rather than a hand-related 訓読み at all — 手品 literally parses as "hand + goods/item" historically but is now a fixed, non-compositional word for "a magic trick." Similarly, 合 reads ごう in 合計・合理的・合同・集合 but あい in 合図・合間 — always confirm a compound's reading rather than assuming consistency.
