1日目 — 物事・日中・年月 (Monogoto, Nicchuu, Nengetsu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.76–77
Theme
Things, daytime, years (事物、白天、年月) — three easy-looking kanji (物・日・年) whose common compounds shift between on-yomi and kun-yomi in ways that trip learners up
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "77ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.79 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Week 5 opens a new format: instead of a situational dialogue, each day picks 2–3 everyday kanji and walks through the (surprisingly varied) compounds built from them. The opening illustration shows a middle-aged woman happily eating cake and a parfait while a caption warns that her habit isn't healthy — no dialogue, just a scene-setting sentence using this day's vocabulary.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Opening

Illustration: a middle-aged woman happily eats cake, a parfait, and other rich food at the table.

Caption: 中年になってから、年中カロリーが高い食物を取り続けるのは、体に良くありません。Chuunen ni natte kara, nenjuu karorii ga takai shokumotsu o tori-tsuzukeru no wa, karada ni yoku arimasen.
("Now that she's middle-aged, continuing to eat high-calorie food all the time isn't good for her health.")

Q1

スキーの道具は庭の（a. 入れ物　b. 物置）に入れてあります。Sukii no dougu wa niwa no (a. iremono / b. monooki) ni irete arimasu.

Full sentence: スキーの道具は庭の物置に入れてあります。Sukii no dougu wa niwa no monooki ni irete arimasu.
EN: The ski equipment is put away in the shed in the yard.
HI: स्की के उपकरण आंगन के स्टोररूम में रखे हुए हैं।
GU: સ્કીના સાધનો આંગણાના સ્ટોરરૂમમાં મૂકેલા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a入れ物iremonoa container (too small/generic a term for a storage building)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's real lesson is reading variation — the same kanji switches between on-yomi and kun-yomi from word to word, and a few near-synonyms trip up the exercises above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
年月nengetsu Years, a long span of time (on-yomi reading) The more literary/formal reading, e.g. as a standalone noun phrase. "長い年月" (a long span of years).
年月toshitsuki Years, a long span of time (kun-yomi reading, same kanji/meaning) Common in the set phrase 年月がたつ ("time/years pass"). "年月がたつのは早い" (the years go by fast).
入れ物iremono A container (a box, bottle, jar) Something you put items into — an object. "A plastic container," "a container for caught bugs."
物置monooki A storage shed/closet A room or small structure for storing things — a place, not a handheld object. "Put the ski gear away in the shed."
生き物ikimono A living creature (everyday word) Kun-yomi, used casually for animals/insects/pets. "Take care of the animals/insects."
⊙生物seibutsu A living organism (technical/academic word) On-yomi, used in science contexts (biology, "marine organisms"). "Research on marine organisms," biology class.
今日konnichi Present-day, nowadays (formal reading) Same spelling as "today" (きょう) but read differently for the abstract "present era" sense. "Japan's economy today/in the present day."
日中nicchuu During the daytime Both kanji read on-yomi together — a fixed time-of-day word. "I won't be home during the day tomorrow."
Reading trap: 日 alone can be にち (日時, 元日, 日中), じつ (今日 → こんにち, 後日 → ごじつ), or ひ (日の出, 日の入り) depending on the compound — there's no single rule, so each compound has to be memorized as its own reading, not derived from a pattern.
