3日目 — 一生・用心・見事 (Isshou, Youjin, Migoto)

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

私はあなたを（a. 一生　b. 人生）愛し続けます。Watashi wa anata o (a. isshou / b. jinsei) aishi tsuzukemasu.

Full sentence: 私はあなたを一生愛し続けます。Watashi wa anata o isshou aishi tsuzukemasu.
EN: I will continue to love you for my whole life.
HI: मैं तुम्हें जीवनभर प्यार करता रहूँगा।
GU: હું તને આખી જિંદગી પ્રેમ કરતો રહીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a一生isshouone's whole life (works adverbially, "for a lifetime")जीवनभरઆખી જિંદગી
b人生jinseilife (a noun/topic — doesn't slot in adverbially before a verb)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 一生 can function adverbially ("for one's whole life, continuously"), fitting directly before 愛し続けます; 人生 is a noun meaning "(one's) life" as a topic, and doesn't slot into the same adverbial position.

Full sentence: あー、こんなところにもカビが生えちゃったよ。Aa, konna tokoro ni mo kabi ga haechatta yo.
EN: Ugh, mold has grown even in a place like this.
HI: अरे, ऐसी जगह पर भी फफूंद उग आई!
GU: અરે, આવી જગ્યાએ પણ ફૂગ ઊગી ગઈ!
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a生じちゃったshoujichattaarose (used for abstract things like problems, not organic growth)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's headline trap is 生じる vs 生える (the exact Q2 test above); the rest are near-synonyms that share a kanji but split by register or object type:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
生じるshoujiru To arise (abstract) Subject is something intangible — a problem, a difference, a need. "問題が生じる" (a problem arises).
生えるhaeru To grow (organic) Subject is something physically growing — plants, hair, mold. "カビが生える" (mold grows).
一生isshou One's whole life (adverbial) Modifies a verb directly — "for a lifetime, continuously." "一生忘れない" (never forget as long as I live).
人生jinsei Life (as a noun/topic) Names life itself as a subject to describe. "つらい人生" (a harsh life).
見学kengaku An observational tour Physically visiting and observing a place (a factory, a school). "工場を見学する" (tour the factory).
見方mikata A viewpoint, way of seeing A mental stance/perspective, not a physical visit. "物の見方を変える" (change one's outlook).
用心youjin Caution, wariness An ongoing state of being careful/alert. "すりに用心する" (be wary of pickpockets).
急用kyuuyou Urgent business, an errand A specific matter that has come up and needs attending to. "急用があるので帰ります" (I have urgent business, so I'll go).
Reading trap: 生 alone covers five readings across this group — しょう (生じる), せい (人生, 生産), は (生える), い (生け花), なま (生) — the widest reading-spread of any kanji this week. There's no shortcut: each compound's reading has to be memorized on its own.
