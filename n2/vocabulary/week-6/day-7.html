Q1

お忙しいようですので、日を（　）また来るようにします。Oisogashii you desu node, hi o (___) mata kuru you ni shimasu.

Full sentence: お忙しいようですので、日を改めてまた来るようにします。Oisogashii you desu node, hi o aratamete mata kuru you ni shimasu.
EN: Since you seem busy, I'll make sure to come back another day.
HI: आप व्यस्त लग रहे हैं, इसलिए मैं किसी और दिन दोबारा आने की कोशिश करूँगा।
GU: તમે વ્યસ્ત લાગો છો, એટલે હું બીજા દિવસે ફરી આવવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરીશ.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1すべてsubeteall (doesn't fit modifying "day")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2改めてaratameteon another occasionकिसी और दिनબીજા દિવસે
3新たにarata nianew (fits renewing resolve, not rescheduling a visit)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4どけてdoketemove aside (fits a physical object, not a day)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 日を改めて ("changing the day, on another occasion") is a fixed business idiom built from Day 4's 改める, seen in that day's own example 改めて、また来ます ("I'll come again another time").

Q2

兄は（　）関係の会社に勤めています。Ani wa (___) kankei no kaisha ni tsutomete imasu.

Full sentence: 兄はマスコミ関係の会社に勤めています。Ani wa masukomi kankei no kaisha ni tsutomete imasu.
EN: My older brother works at a media-related company.
HI: मेरा बड़ा भाई मीडिया से जुड़ी एक कंपनी में काम करता है।
GU: મારો મોટો ભાઈ મીડિયા સંબંધિત કંપનીમાં કામ કરે છે.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ホテルのフロントhoteru no furontohotel front desk (a job role, not an industry that combines with 関係)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2マスコミmasukomimass mediaमीडियाમીડિયા
3ビジネスbijinesubusiness (too generic — nearly every company is "business-related")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4プロpuroprofessional (doesn't name an industry)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: マスコミ関係の会社 ("a media-industry company") is a standard, specific collocation for naming an industry sector; ビジネス関係 is too generic to describe any particular field, and the other two options aren't industries at all.

Q3

私は、卵に（　）があるので、卵の入ったものは食べないようにしています。Watashi wa, tamago ni (___) ga aru node, tamago no haitta mono wa tabenai you ni shite imasu.

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1エネルギーenerugiienergy (sounds similar but unrelated meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2ブームbuumua boom/trend (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3オーバーoobaaover/exceeding (unrelated to a medical reaction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

彼女の部屋があまりに汚くて（　）しまった。Kanojo no heya ga amari ni kitanakute (___) shimatta.

Full sentence: 彼女の部屋があまりに汚くてあきれてしまった。Kanojo no heya ga amari ni kitanakute akirete shimatta.
EN: Her room was so dirty that I was appalled.
HI: उसका कमरा इतना गंदा था कि मैं हैरान रह गया/गई।
GU: તેનો ઓરડો એટલો ગંદો હતો કે હું અચંબિત થઈ ગયો/ગઈ.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1あきてakitetired of (fits weariness from repetition, not a one-time shock)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2あきれてakireteappalled/shockedहैरानઅચંબિત
3あきらめてakirametegiven up (no clear object of what was given up)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4あきられてakirarete(an unnatural passive form here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Day 6's ひどい態度にあきれる ("be appalled by rude behavior") extends naturally to being appalled by an extremely dirty room; あきる describes weariness building up over repeated exposure, not a sudden shock.

Q5

大丈夫、この症状は（　）風邪ですからしばらくすれば治りますよ。Daijoubu, kono shoujou wa (___) kaze desu kara shibaraku sureba naorimasu yo.

Full sentence: 大丈夫、この症状は単なる風邪ですからしばらくすれば治りますよ。Daijoubu, kono shoujou wa tannaru kaze desu kara shibaraku sureba naorimasu yo.
EN: Don't worry, these symptoms are just a common cold, so you'll be fine soon.
HI: चिंता मत करो, ये लक्षण सिर्फ़ एक साधारण ज़ुकाम हैं, थोड़ी देर में ठीक हो जाएँगे।
GU: ચિંતા ના કરો, આ લક્ષણો ફક્ત સામાન્ય શરદીનાં છે, થોડા સમયમાં સારું થઈ જશે.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1単なるtannarumere/just (attaches directly to the noun, no particle needed)सिर्फ़/साधारणફક્ત/સામાન્ય
2ただtadajust (normally needs ただの before a noun; ungrammatical as given here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3意外にigai niunexpectedly (wrong meaning — implies surprise, not reassurance)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4案外angaisurprisingly (same problem as 意外に — wrong sense here)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 単なる is a special attributive-only form from Day 5 (単なる不注意, "mere carelessness") that directly precedes a noun with no particle — 単なる風邪 ("just a common cold") reassures that it's nothing serious; ただ would need ただの to work the same way, and 意外に／案外 both wrongly introduce a note of surprise instead of reassurance.

Q6

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ポイントpointopointsपॉइंट्सપોઇન્ટ્સ
2バランスbaransubalance (an account figure, not something you accumulate for rewards)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3サービスsaabisuservice (not something measured/accumulated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4アポイントapointoappointment (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

問題4 — which sentence uses the word correctly? (1問4点)

Q21

特徴tokuchou

#JapaneseEnglish
1最新の冷蔵庫は、省エネの特徴があります。Saishin no reizouko wa, shou-ene no tokuchou ga arimasu."The newest fridge has an energy-saving [tokuchou]" — an energy-saving edge is a selling point over competitors, which calls for 特長, not neutral 特徴
2キリンは首が長いのが特徴です。Kirin wa kubi ga nagai no ga tokuchou desu.A giraffe's long neck is its distinguishing trait.
3東京は人口が特徴に多い。Toukyou wa jinkou ga tokuchou ni ooi."Tokyo's population is [tokuchou]-ly large" — 特徴 isn't an adverb modifying 多い; should be 非常に (extremely)
4彼を特徴にあつかってはいけません。Kare o tokuchou ni atsukatte wa ikemasen."You mustn't treat him [tokuchou]-ly" — not a real collocation; should be 特別に (specially)

Why: Day 6's own note distinguishes 特徴 (a neutral defining trait, good or bad) from 特長 (a point of superiority) — a giraffe's long neck is purely descriptive, the textbook example of plain 特徴; sentence 1 is the classic trap where a genuine selling point should use 特長 instead, and 3–4 misuse 特徴 grammatically as an adverb.

Q22

改めるaratameru

#JapaneseEnglish
1彼は、先生に注意されて、態度を改めた。Kare wa, sensei ni chuui sarete, taido o aratameta.He was warned by his teacher and corrected his attitude.
2テレビ番組がつまらないから、チャンネルを改めよう。Terebi bangumi ga tsumaranai kara, channeru o aratameyou."Since the show is boring, let's [aratameru] the channel" — switching channels needs 変える, not 改める
3味を改めたので、おいしくなった。Aji o aratameta node, oishiku natta."The flavor was [aratameru]-ed, so it got tastier" — adjusting a taste needs 変える, not 改める
4答えが間違っているかどうか改めた。Kotae ga machigatte iru ka dou ka aratameta."[Aratameru]-ed whether the answer was wrong" — checking needs 確認した/調べた, not 改めた

Why: Day 4's 欠点を改める ("correct one's shortcomings") is exactly the pattern in sentence 1 — formally correcting a fault after being pointed out; 2–4 all substitute 改める for a plain "change" or "check," which needs 変える or 確認する instead.

Q23

しゃぶるshaburu

#JapaneseEnglish
1突然雨がしゃぶってきた。Totsuzen ame ga shabutte kita."Suddenly rain [shaburu]-ed" — nonsensical; rain doesn't "suck"
2ラーメンをしゃぶって食べました。Raamen o shabutte tabemashita."I sucked and ate ramen" — noodles are eaten/slurped (すする), not sucked on like candy
3別れた彼からの手紙をしゃぶって捨てた。Wakareta kare kara no tegami o shabutte suteta."Sucked on a letter from my ex and threw it away" — nonsensical object for しゃぶる
4風邪でのどが痛いので、あめをしゃぶった。Kaze de nodo ga itai node, ame o shabutta.Since my throat hurts from a cold, I sucked on a piece of candy.

Why: This week's own vocabulary example is あめをしゃぶる ("suck on candy") — sentence 4 matches it exactly; 1–3 apply しゃぶる to objects (rain, noodles, a letter) that don't fit the "hold in the mouth and suck" meaning.

Q24

うなるunaru

#JapaneseEnglish
1赤ちゃんがうなってすやすや寝ている。Akachan ga unatte suyasuya nete iru."The baby is [unaru]-ing, sleeping peacefully" — うなる's growling/straining sense contradicts "sleeping peacefully"
2雷がうなっています。Kaminari ga unatte imasu."Thunder is [unaru]-ing" — possible but less standard; 雷が鳴る is the far more common way to describe thunder
3問題の答えがわからずうなってしまった。Mondai no kotae ga wakarazu unatte shimatta.Not knowing the answer to the problem, I groaned (in frustration).
4父に大きな声でうなられてしまった。Chichi ni ookii koe de unararete shimatta."I was [unaru]-ed at loudly by my father" — an angry parent is more naturally 怒鳴られた ("yelled at"), not うなられた

Why: Day 4's 犬が怒ってうなる ("a dog growls in anger") extends idiomatically to a person groaning under mental strain — 難問にうなる ("to groan over a hard problem") is a textbook-standard extension, matching sentence 3; 1 and 4 misapply the word's threatening undertone to contexts that contradict it (peaceful sleep, a parent's scolding, better said as 泣く/怒鳴る), and 2, while not impossible, is much less idiomatic than 雷が鳴る. (This item carries slightly more uncertainty than the others on this page — see the notes below.)

Q25

ワクチンwakuchin

#JapaneseEnglish
1ワクチン不足で、肌が荒れてしまった。Wakuchin busoku de, hada ga arete shimatta."Due to vaccine shortage, my skin got rough" — no logical link between vaccine supply and skin condition
2若いから彼はワクチンにあふれている。Wakai kara kare wa wakuchin ni afurete iru."Because he's young, he's overflowing with vaccine" — should be 若さ/エネルギー (youth/energy), not ワクチン
3パソコンがワクチンに感染してしまった。Pasokon ga wakuchin ni kansen shite shimatta."The computer got infected with a vaccine" — reversed logic; computers get infected with ウイルス, and a vaccine prevents infection rather than causing it
4インフルエンザのワクチンを注射してもらった。Infuruenza no wakuchin o chuusha shite moratta.I got a flu vaccine shot.

Why: ワクチンを注射する／接種する ("to get vaccinated") is the standard collocation; sentence 3 is this test's mirror-image trap of Q9 — a computer is infected by a ウイルス (virus), never by a ワクチン (vaccine), which does the opposite job of preventing infection.
