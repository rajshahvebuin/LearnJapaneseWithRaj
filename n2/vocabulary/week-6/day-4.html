4日目 — 似ている言葉① (Niteiru kotoba ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.98–99
Theme
意味が似ている言葉 (words with similar meanings that are easy to mix up) — verb/adjective pairs describing closely related actions and states
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "99ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.101 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This is one of Week 6's まとめ (review/consolidation) days: unlike other weeks, the day's whole word list is itself a set of confusable near-synonyms, so it prints as a single continuous "おぼえましょう" table rather than several themed sub-groups. Opens with two women chatting about a dog outside, using several of the day's own words.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression — often the transitive/intransitive partner of the headword)
Scene

Two women are talking on the street; a dog nearby overhears and looks hurt.

Her: 私、猫は好きだけど、犬は苦手。ほえるとうるさいし、大きい犬が跳ねたりするとこわいし。なめられたりするのもいや。Watashi, neko wa suki dakedo, inu wa nigate. Hoeru to urusai shi, ookii inu ga hanetari suru to kowai shi. Namerareta ri suru no mo iya.
("I like cats, but I'm not good with dogs. They're noisy when they bark, and it's scary when a big dog jumps around, and I don't like being licked either.")

Dog (thinking): 傷つくなぁ。Kizutsuku naa.
("Ouch, that hurts (my feelings)...")

The dialogue itself uses four of today's headwords in one breath: ほえる, 跳ねる, なめる, 傷つく — a preview of how tightly these "similar words" cluster in real speech.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

（a. 先ほど　b. 先に）田中さんからお電話がありました。(a. Sakihodo / b. Saki ni) Tanaka-san kara odenwa ga arimashita.

Full sentence: 先ほど田中さんからお電話がありました。Sakihodo Tanaka-san kara odenwa ga arimashita.
EN: Mr./Ms. Tanaka called a little while ago.
HI: थोड़ी देर पहले तनाका जी का फ़ोन आया था।
GU: થોડી વાર પહેલાં તનાકાનો ફોન આવ્યો હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a先ほどsakihodoa little while agoथोड़ी देर पहलेથોડી વાર પહેલાં
b先にsaki nibeforehand/ahead of time (wrong here — doesn't mark a specific recent moment)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 先ほど specifically means "a little while ago," pinpointing a recent moment — exactly right for reporting a call that just came in; 先に means "ahead of time/first," which doesn't mark a specific recent point.

Q2

天井から雨が（a. こぼれている　b. もれている）。Tenjou kara ame ga (a. koborete iru / b. morete iru).

Full sentence: 天井から雨がもれている。Tenjou kara ame ga morete iru.
EN: Rain is leaking (through) from the ceiling.
HI: छत से बारिश का पानी रिस/टपक रहा है।
GU: છત પરથી વરસાદનું પાણી ટપકે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこぼれているkoborete iruspilling over (fits liquid overflowing a container, not seeping through a ceiling)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bもれているmorete iruleakingरिस रहा हैટપકે છે

Why: 雨がもれる describes water seeping through a gap or crack (like a ceiling); こぼれる is for liquid spilling out of/over a container, which doesn't fit a ceiling leak.

Q3

ゴルフ場の芝生が（a. かれて　b. しぼんで）しまった。Gorufujou no shibafu ga (a. karete / b. shibonde) shimatta.

Full sentence: ゴルフ場の芝生がかれてしまった。Gorufujou no shibafu ga karete shimatta.
EN: The grass at the golf course has died (withered).
HI: गोल्फ़ कोर्स की घास सूख गई।
GU: ગોલ્ફ કોર્સનું ઘાસ સુકાઈ ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかれてkaretedied/witheredसूख गईસુકાઈ ગયું
bしぼんでshibondewilted (fits a flower's petals closing up, not turf drying out)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 枯れる applies broadly to plants (trees, grass/turf) drying out and dying; しぼむ specifically describes a flower's petals wilting and closing, which doesn't fit lawn grass.

EN: The new car already has a scratch on it.
HI: नई कार पर पहले से ही खरोंच आ गई।
GU: નવી કારને પહેલેથી જ ખંજવાળ પડી ગયું.

a傷ついてkizutsuitegot (emotionally) hurt — fits feelings, not a car body(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

HI: छींकते समय हाथ से मुँह ढका।

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

EN: Because of the snow, the exam's start time was pushed back 30 minutes.
HI: बर्फ़ के कारण, परीक्षा का शुरू होने का समय 30 मिनट खिसका दिया गया।
GU: બરફના કારણે, પરીક્ષાનો શરૂઆતનો સમય 30 મિનિટ ખસેડવામાં આવ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aどけたdoketamoved (an object) out of the way — fits a chair, not a time(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

HI: तालाब में मछलियाँ उछल रही हैं।
GU: તળાવમાં માછલીઓ કૂદી રહી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ころんでkorondefalling/tripping (fits people/objects, not fish)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2はねてhanetejumpingउछल रहीકૂદી રહી
3とんでtondeflying (fits birds, not a fish's leap in water)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

1どいてdoitemove out of the wayहट जाओખસી જા

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This entire day is a confusable-pairs list by design — the book groups every headword next to its closest near-synonym. Here are the trickiest clusters, drawn straight from today's own vocabulary and exercises:

PairMeaningKey differenceUse when
飛ぶtobuto flyMoving through the air (birds, planes, thrown objects)."A bird flies."
跳ねるhaneruto jump/leapSpringing upward off a surface — legs or a bouncing motion, not sustained flight."A rabbit/fish jumps."
転ぶkorobuto fall overA person or animal losing their footing."I tripped and fell."
転がるkorogaruto rollAn object rotating along a surface."The ball rolled away."
ちぎるchigiruto tear off (a piece)Pulling a small piece free by hand, often to eat."I tore off a piece of bread."
破るyaburuto tear/breakRipping material, or breaking an abstract promise/record."I tore the paper / broke my promise."
ほえるhoeruto barkThe default sound a dog makes."The dog is barking."
うなるunaruto growlA low, angry/threatening sound."The dog is growling."
もれるmoreruto leakLiquid or gas escaping through a gap/crack."Rain is leaking through the ceiling."
こぼれるkoboreruto spillLiquid overflowing from/out of a container."Milk spilled on the table."
埋めるumeruto fill inClosing up a hole or gap with material."Fill in the hole."
ふさぐfusaguto cover/blockBlocking an opening (ears, mouth, a doorway)."I covered my mouth."
つるすtsurusuto hang (up)Suspending something from a fixed point above."Hang a curtain."
ぶらさげるburasageruto dangle/carry hangingSomething hangs and swings from you as you carry it."He wore a camera around his neck."
なめるnameruto lickA single pass of the tongue over a surface."The cat licked the plate."
しゃぶるshaburuto suck (on)Holding something in the mouth and sucking repeatedly."He sucked on a candy."
ずらすzurasuto shift/postponeDeliberately moving a time or position slightly."I pushed the meeting back 30 minutes."
どけるdokeruto move (something) asideRelocating a physical object out of the way."Move the chair aside."
枯れるkareruto wither/die (plant)A whole plant (tree, grass) drying up."The tree died."
しぼむshibomuto wiltA flower's petals closing/shrinking specifically."The flower wilted."
傷つくkizutsukuto be (emotionally) hurtFeelings are wounded by words/actions."His words hurt me."
傷がつくkizu ga tsukuto get a scratch/markA physical object is physically damaged."My bag got scratched."
先にsaki nipreviously/aheadMarks sequence — something that happened before another event."the previous meeting"
先ほどsakihodoa little while agoMarks a specific, recent point in clock time."I called you earlier."
Transitive/intransitive pattern: Several ⊙-marked pairs above are really the same event seen from two grammatical sides — 転がる(intr.)／転がす(tr.), 破ける(intr.)／破る(tr.), ずれる(intr.)／ずらす(tr.), どく(intr.)／どける(tr.). If the subject is the thing itself moving/changing on its own, use the intransitive (が) form; if someone is deliberately acting on an object, use the transitive (を) form.
