Theme
ホテル・仕事・日常生活でよく使うカタカナ語の正しい使い方 (katakana words for hotels, work, and daily life, with attention to correct usage)
Answer key

Notes
Scene: a young man imagines the hotel restaurant he wants to take someone to — a set-course dinner at night, but an all-you-can-eat buffet lunch. One continuous おぼえましょう list titled 使い方に注意 (watch how it's used) spans both printed pages, covering hotel, work, media, and daily-life katakana words rather than being split into separate sub-groups.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A young man daydreams about a hotel restaurant while talking to two friends.

Him (thinking): お昼はホテルのフロントの向かいにあるレストランに行こうよ。夜はコース料理しかないけれど、お昼はバイキング形式だから、おなかいっぱい食べられるよ。Ohiru wa hoteru no furonto no mukai ni aru resutoran ni ikou yo. Yoru wa koosu ryouri shika nai keredo, ohiru wa baikingu keishiki dakara, onaka ippai taberareru yo.
("Let's go to the restaurant across from the hotel's front desk for lunch. At night there's only set-course meals, but lunch is buffet-style, so you can eat until you're full.")

Today's single, continuous list covers katakana words for hotels (フロント, シングル, ツイン), work (コピー機, オーバー), media (タレント, クレーム), and describing people (ドライ, ハーフ, ベテラン, ユニーク).

Q1

父はキャリア30年の（a. ビジネス　b. ベテラン）パイロットです。Chichi wa kyaria sanjuu-nen no (a. bijinesu / b. beteran) pairotto desu.

Full sentence: 父はキャリア30年のベテランパイロットです。Chichi wa kyaria sanjuu-nen no beteran pairotto desu.
EN: My father is a veteran pilot with a 30-year career.
HI: मेरे पिता 30 साल के करियर वाले अनुभवी पायलट हैं।
GU: મારા પિતા 30 વર્ષની કારકિર્દી ધરાવતા અનુભવી પાયલોટ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aビジネスbijinesubusiness (doesn't describe a person's experience)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bベテランbeteranveteran, expertअनुभवीઅનુભવી

Why: ベテラン describes a person with a lot of experience, fitting "30-year career"; ビジネス just means "business" as a noun and cannot modify パイロット this way.

Q2

Full sentence: 書類を2部ずつコピーしておいてください。Shorui o nibu zutsu kopii shite oite kudasai.
EN: Please make two copies each of the documents in advance.
HI: कृपया हर दस्तावेज़ की दो-दो प्रतियाँ बना कर रखें।
GU: કૃપા કરીને દરેક દસ્તાવેજની બે-બે નકલો કરી રાખો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aコピーkopiicopy, photocopyप्रति, कॉपीનકલ
bサービスsaabisuservice (unrelated to duplicating documents)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: コピーする ("to photocopy") is the natural verb for duplicating documents; サービス means "service" and doesn't apply to making copies.

Q3

彼女は学校中の（a. アイドル　b. タレント）です。Kanojo wa gakkoujuu no (a. aidoru / b. tarento) desu.

Full sentence: 彼女は学校中のアイドルです。Kanojo wa gakkoujuu no aidoru desu.
EN: She is the idol of the whole school.
HI: वह पूरे स्कूल की चहेती है।
GU: તે આખી શાળાની માનીતી છે.
aアイドルaidoruidol, someone widely adoredचहेता/चहेती व्यक्तिમાનીતી વ્યક્તિ
bタレントtarentoa TV personality (a broadcasting job, not "adored by everyone")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Full sentence: とてもユニークなデザインの衣装ですね。Totemo yuniiku na dezain no ishou desu ne.
EN: That's a very unique/original design of costume, isn't it.
HI: यह पोशाक का डिज़ाइन बहुत ही अनोखा है, है ना।
GU: આ પોશાકની ડિઝાઇન ખૂબ જ અનોખી છે, ખરું ને.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart words that look loosely related but aren't interchangeable — exactly what Q2/Q4/Q8 and Q3/Q1 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
コピーkopii To copy/photocopy Duplicating a physical document. "Please make two copies of the documents."
サインsain Signature, autograph A handwritten mark of identity or consent — on a poster, receipt, or contract. "A poster signed by the celebrity" / "Sign here to consent."
コメントkomento Comment, opinion Neutral: voicing (or declining to voice) a view on a matter. "No comment on that matter."
クレームkureemu Complaint Specifically negative — a grievance filed against poor service/a product. "He filed a complaint about the service."
アイドルaidoru Idol — someone widely adored Describes being beloved/admired by many, in any setting (a school, a fandom). "She's the idol of the whole school."
タレントtarento TV personality — a job title A specific broadcasting profession, not a measure of popularity. "He works as a TV personality."
ベテランbeteran Veteran, expert (from experience) Describes a person and their accumulated skill/experience. "A veteran pilot with 30 years' experience."
ビジネスbijinesu Business (the activity/field) A plain noun for commerce — cannot describe a person's experience level. "He works in business."
Wasei-eigo trap: ビジネスマン looks like it should mean any "businessman," but in Japanese it's most often used simply to mean 会社員 (a company employee) — it doesn't necessarily imply someone who owns or runs a business. Likewise, オーバーする can mean "to go over/exceed" (予算がオーバーする, over budget) or "to exaggerate" (オーバーに話す) — the object tells you which sense is meant, not the verb itself.
