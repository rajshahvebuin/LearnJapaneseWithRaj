JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 6 — まとめて覚えましょう① (Matomete oboemashou ①)

3日目 — カタカナで書く言葉③ (Katakana de kaku kotoba ③)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.96–97
Theme
和製カタカナ語・その他の紛らわしいカタカナ語 (katakana words coined in Japan itself, and other easily-confused katakana terms)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "97ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.99 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a young man complains to a friend that a girl he met at a group dating mixer (合コン) turned out different from what he imagined once they actually talked — the conversation's "tempo" felt off. Two おぼえましょう tables: 日本でできたカタカナ語 (wasei-eigo — katakana words coined in Japan itself, e.g. 合コン, Uターン, マイホーム) and その他の注意したいカタカナ語 (other katakana words to watch, e.g. セロテープ, タイプ, イメージ, テンポ).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A young man tells a friend about a disappointing group date.

Him: 合コンで好みのタイプの女の子に出会ったんだけど、話してみたら、会話のテンポが合わなくて、ちょっとイメージが違ったよ。Goukon de konomi no taipu no onna no ko ni deatta n da kedo, hanashite mitara, kaiwa no tenpo ga awanakute, chotto imeeji ga chigatta yo.
("I met a girl who was just my type at a group dating mixer, but when I actually talked with her, the conversation's tempo didn't match, and she was a bit different from what I'd imagined.")

Today covers two groups: wasei-eigo — katakana words Japan coined itself from English-looking parts (合コン, ワンパターン, Uターン) — and other katakana words worth watching closely (タイプ, イメージ, テンポ, リズム).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.97) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.97). Answers confirmed from the "97ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.99.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

子どものころ、よく父と（a. ボールキャッチ　b. キャッチボール）をしたものです。Kodomo no koro, yoku chichi to (a. boorukyacchi / b. kyacchi booru) o shita mono desu.

Full sentence: 子どものころ、よく父とキャッチボールをしたものです。Kodomo no koro, yoku chichi to kyacchi booru o shita mono desu.
EN: When I was a child, I often used to play catch with my father.
HI: बचपन में मैं अक्सर अपने पिता के साथ कैच-बॉल खेला करता था।
GU: બાળપણમાં હું ઘણીવાર મારા પિતા સાથે કેચ-બોલ રમતો હતો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aボールキャッチboorukyacchinot the fixed word order used in Japanese(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bキャッチボールkyacchi booruplaying catchकैच-बॉलકેચ-બોલ

Why: Japanese fixed this wasei-eigo term as キャッチボール (catch + ball), not the reverse order ボールキャッチ — the word order is fixed by convention, not by English grammar.

HI: मैंने पर्चे को सेलोफ़ेन टेप से दीवार पर चिपकाया।
GU: મેં પત્રિકાને સેલોફેન ટેપથી દીવાલ પર ચોંટાડી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aホチキスhochikisustapler (staples, doesn't "paste/stick" onto a wall)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bセロテープserouteepucellophane tapeसेलोफ़ेन टेपસેલોફેન ટેપ

HI: उस दुकान के सारे कपड़े सस्ते जैसे दिखने वाला प्रभाव देते हैं।
GU: તે દુકાનનાં બધાં કપડાં સસ્તાં લાગે એવી છાપ આપે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aタイプtaiputype/category (doesn't pair with がする, "to give off a feeling")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q5

（a. テンポ　b. リズム）が早くて、会話についていけない。(a. Tenpo / b. Rizumu) ga hayakute, kaiwa ni tsuite ikenai.

Full sentence: テンポが早くて、会話についていけない。Tenpo ga hayakute, kaiwa ni tsuite ikenai.
EN: The pace is fast, and I can't keep up with the conversation.
HI: रफ़्तार बहुत तेज़ है, मैं बातचीत के साथ तालमेल नहीं बिठा पा रहा।
GU: ગતિ ખૂબ ઝડપી છે, હું વાતચીત સાથે તાલ મેળવી શકતો નથી.
aテンポtenpotempo, pace (of speech/conversation)रफ़्तारગતિ
bリズムrizumurhythm (a repeating pattern, not speed)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: テンポ describes the speed/pace of a conversation, matching 会話のテンポについていけない from this day's vocabulary; リズム describes a repeating rhythmic pattern (like tapping a beat), not speaking speed.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on words that share a prefix, a sound, or a rough theme but aren't interchangeable — exactly what Q2/Q3/Q5/Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
セロテープserouteepu Cellophane/adhesive tape Sticks/pastes things onto a surface. "Tape a poster/flyer to the wall."
ホチキスhochikisu A stapler Binds sheets of paper to each other, not to a wall. "Staple the handouts together."
コインランドリーkoin randorii A coin-operated laundromat A place to wash clothes, not to buy/sell items. "Wash clothes at the laundromat."
リサイクルショップrisaikuru shoppu A secondhand/recycle shop A place to sell or buy used goods/appliances. "Sell an old washing machine at a recycle shop."
テンポtenpo Tempo, pace (of speech, action) About speed — how fast something proceeds. "The conversation's pace is too fast to follow."
リズムrizumu Rhythm (a repeating beat/pattern) About a recurring pattern, not raw speed. "Tap your foot to the beat."
フリーダイヤルfurii daiyaru A toll-free phone number フリー here means "free of charge" for a phone call. "Order by calling the toll-free number."
フリーサイズfurii saizu One-size-fits-all (clothing) フリー here means "unrestricted/universal" fit, about sizing. "A one-size-fits-all belt."
Word-order trap: キャッチボール (kyacchi booru, "catch" + "ball") is fixed in that order in Japanese — saying ボールキャッチ is not natural, even though both parts are recognizable English loanwords. Wasei-eigo compounds like this one follow Japanese convention, not English word order, so they have to be memorized as fixed units rather than reconstructed from their English parts.

False-friend trap: スマート(な) does not mean "intelligent" in Japanese — it means "slim/slender" (ダイエットしてスマートになった, "I lost weight and became slim"). The English sense of "smart" (clever) is a different word in Japanese (賢い／頭がいい).
