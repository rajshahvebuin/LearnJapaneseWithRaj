JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 6 — まとめて覚えましょう① (Matomete oboemashou ①)

5日目 — 似ている言葉② (Niteiru kotoba ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.100–101
Theme
意味が似ている言葉 (words with similar meanings) — scope/degree adverbs, manner adverbs, and grade adjectives that overlap in translation but differ in nuance
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "101ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.103 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Second of Week 6's まとめ days on confusable words — one continuous "おぼえましょう" table again. Opens with two illustrated vignettes: a father scolding a child about leaving without telling anyone, and two men gossiping at a luxury hotel while a woman imitates/pretends nearby.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
ダメ = このような使い方はだめです (this usage is NOT correct)
Scene

Two vignettes on one page. First, a father lectures his son:

Father: 無断で外出してはいけません。それから……Mudan de gaishutsu shite wa ikemasen. Sorekara...
("You must not go out without telling anyone. And also...")

Second, at a hotel lounge, one man remarks on the room while another (visibly pretending to listen) imitates a woman behind them:

Man: 高級なホテルだけど、案外狭いな。Koukyuu na hoteru dakedo, angai semai na.
("It's a high-class hotel, but it's surprisingly cramped.")
(Labels in the illustration: まねをしている "imitating," 聞いているふり "pretending to listen.")

Both scenes plant today's confusable pairs directly: 無断で (vs 勝手に), 高級な (vs 上等・高度・高等・高価), 案外 (vs 意外), and the illustration literally acts out まね vs ふり side by side.

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.101) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.101). Answers confirmed from the "101ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.103.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

友人に（a. 高度な　b. 高級な）ワインをもらった。Yuujin ni (a. koudo na / b. koukyuu na) wain o moratta.

Full sentence: 友人に高級なワインをもらった。Yuujin ni koukyuu na wain o moratta.
EN: I received a high-class (luxury) wine from a friend.
HI: दोस्त से एक शानदार (महँगी) वाइन मिली।
GU: મિત્ર પાસેથી ઉચ્ચ કક્ષાનો (મોંઘો) વાઇન મળ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a高度なkoudo naadvanced/high-level (fits technology/skill, not a wine's grade)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b高級なkoukyuu naluxury/high-classशानदारઉચ્ચ કક્ષાનો

Why: 高級 describes a product's luxury grade (wine, hotels, clothing); 高度 describes an abstract level of sophistication (technology, skill), not the class of a beverage.

Q2

医者へ行ったら（a. 余計に　b. 余分に）症状が悪化した。Isha e ittara (a. yokei ni / b. yobun ni) shoujou ga akka shita.

Full sentence: 医者へ行ったら余計に症状が悪化した。Isha e ittara yokei ni shoujou ga akka shita.
EN: When I went to the doctor, my symptoms got even worse.
HI: डॉक्टर के पास जाने पर लक्षण और भी बिगड़ गए।
GU: ડોક્ટર પાસે ગયા પછી લક્ષણો વધુ બગડ્યાં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a余計にyokei nieven more (than expected)और भी ज़्यादाવધુ
b余分にyobun nian extra amount (fits countable things like food, not degree of worsening)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 余計に means "even more than expected," fitting an unwanted worsening of symptoms; 余分に describes making/using an extra quantity of something countable, which doesn't fit "symptoms."

Q3

その映画はみんなはつまらないと言っていたが、（a. ただ　b. 意外と）面白かった。Sono eiga wa minna wa tsumaranai to itte ita ga, (a. tada / b. igai to) omoshirokatta.

Full sentence: その映画はみんなつまらないと言っていたが、意外と面白かった。Sono eiga wa minna tsumaranai to itte ita ga, igai to omoshirokatta.
EN: Everyone said that movie was boring, but it was surprisingly interesting.
HI: सब कह रहे थे कि वह फ़िल्म बोरिंग है, पर वह उम्मीद से कहीं ज़्यादा दिलचस्प निकली।
GU: બધા કહેતા હતા કે એ ફિલ્મ કંટાળાજનક છે, પણ તે અનપેક્ષિત રીતે રસપ્રદ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aただtadajust/only (no sense of contradicting an expectation)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b意外とigai tosurprisingly/unexpectedlyउम्मीद से ज़्यादाઅનપેક્ષિત રીતે

Why: 意外と signals that reality contradicted a prior expectation ("everyone said boring, but..."); ただ simply means "just/merely," carrying no sense of surprise.

Q4

クモは危険を感じると死んだ（a. まね　b. ふり）をする。Kumo wa kiken o kanjiru to shinda (a. mane / b. furi) o suru.

Full sentence: クモは危険を感じると死んだふりをする。Kumo wa kiken o kanjiru to shinda furi o suru.
EN: When a spider senses danger, it pretends to be dead.
HI: मकड़ी को ख़तरा महसूस होने पर वह मरने का नाटक करती है।
GU: કરોળિયો ભય અનુભવે ત્યારે મરી ગયો હોવાનો ડોળ કરે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまねmaneimitate/mimic (copying an external model, not feigning one's own state)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bふりfuripretendनाटक करनाડોળ કરવો

Why: 死んだふりをする ("pretend to be dead") uses ふり for feigning one's own state/condition; まね is for imitating something external, like a sound or someone else's action.

Q5

地震のとき、教室から（a. 第一　b. 真っ先）に逃げ出したのは先生だった。Jishin no toki, kyoushitsu kara (a. daiichi / b. massaki) ni nigedashita no wa sensei datta.

Full sentence: 地震のとき、教室から真っ先に逃げ出したのは先生だった。Jishin no toki, kyoushitsu kara massaki ni nigedashita no wa sensei datta.
EN: When the earthquake hit, the teacher was the very first one to run out of the classroom.
HI: भूकंप के समय, कक्षा से सबसे पहले भागने वाले शिक्षक थे।
GU: ભૂકંપ વખતે, વર્ગખંડમાંથી સૌથી પહેલાં ભાગનાર શિક્ષક હતા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a第一daiichifirst of all (for listing points, not physically being first)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b真っ先massakithe very first (to act/move)सबसे पहलेસૌથી પહેલાં

Why: 真っ先に describes literally being first to do or act; 第一に is for listing points in an argument ("first of all, ..."), not describing who physically moved first.

Q6

1900年（a. 以前　b. かつて）の記録はもう残っていません。Sen kyuuhyaku-nen (a. izen / b. katsute) no kiroku wa mou nokotte imasen.

Full sentence: 1900年以前の記録はもう残っていません。Sen kyuuhyaku-nen izen no kiroku wa mou nokotte imasen.
EN: Records from before 1900 no longer remain.
HI: 1900 से पहले के रिकॉर्ड अब मौजूद नहीं हैं।
GU: 1900 પહેલાંના રેકોર્ડ હવે બચ્યા નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a以前izenbefore (a specific reference point)से पहलेપહેલાં
bかつてkatsuteonce, in the past (a vague era, can't attach to "before 1900" directly)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 以前 attaches directly to a specific reference point ("before 1900"); かつて describes a vague bygone era without pinning to an exact date.

Q7

彼は（　）勉強して医者になりました。Kare wa (___) benkyou shite isha ni narimashita.

Full sentence: 彼は必死に勉強して医者になりました。Kare wa hisshi ni benkyou shite isha ni narimashita.
EN: He studied desperately (with all his might) and became a doctor.
HI: वह जीजान से पढ़ाई करके डॉक्टर बना।
GU: તે જીવ રેડીને અભ્યાસ કરીને ડોક્ટર બન્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1無理にmuri niby force (fits forcing something physically, not studying)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2必死にhisshi nidesperately, with all one's mightजीजान सेજીવ રેડીને

4勝手にkatte nion one's own, selfishly (fits acting without permission, not effort)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 必死に勉強する ("study desperately/with all one's might") is the natural collocation describing great effort leading to success; the other options describe force, an unrelated adjective, or selfish behavior.

Q8

GU: ફ્લૂ ફેલાવાથી દર્દીઓ સતત વધી રહ્યા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1続々とzokuzoku toone after another, continuouslyलगातारસતત
2案外とangai tosurprisingly (doesn't describe a rate/frequency of increase)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

案外(と)angai (to)surprisingly (contrary to appearance)Contradicts how something looks/seems on the surface."looks easy but is surprisingly hard"
意外(に/と)igai (ni/to)unexpected(ly)Contradicts a prior expectation/prediction; also works alone as a predicate ("was unexpected")."their marriage was unexpected"
余計にyokei nieven more (degree)An unwanted or unexpected increase in intensity/degree."symptoms got even worse"
余分にyobun niextra (quantity)A countable/measurable surplus amount."cook extra food"
ダメ trap: The book explicitly flags 「彼らが結婚したのは案外だった」as wrong — 案外 must be followed by と/に modifying another word (案外難しい, "surprisingly difficult"); only 意外 can stand alone as the predicate itself ("それは意外だった," "that was unexpected"). Mixing them up is one of this day's most common test traps (see Q3 above and Q18 of the 実戦問題 test).
