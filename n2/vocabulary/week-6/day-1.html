カタカナ略語・外来語 (shortened katakana words, and loanwords borrowed from German/Latin, that need careful handling)

Notes
Scene: a man warns a woman that flu is going around this winter and she should get vaccinated; she says she's fine because she's careful about nutrition, takes vitamins, and has plenty of energy stored up. Two おぼえましょう tables: 省略に注意 (watch the abbreviation — everyday katakana words that are shortened forms) and 発音に注意 (watch the pronunciation — loanwords that came into Japanese from German or Latin, not English).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

A man warns a woman about the flu going around this winter; she says her nutrition, vitamins, and stored-up energy will protect her.

Him: 今年の冬、インフルエンザがはやるらしいから気をつけて。ワクチンを打ったほうがいいかも。Kotoshi no fuyu, infuruenza ga hayaru rashii kara ki o tsukete. Wakuchin o utta hou ga ii kamo.
("This winter, the flu seems to be spreading, so be careful. You might want to get vaccinated.")

Her: 私はだいじょうぶ。栄養に気をつけてるし、ビタミン剤もたくさん飲んでいるし、エネルギーを十分に蓄えているから。Watashi wa daijoubu. Eiyou ni ki o tsuketeru shi, bitamin zai mo takusan nonde iru shi, enerugii o juubun ni takuwaete iru kara.
("I'll be fine. I'm careful about my nutrition, I take lots of vitamin pills, and I've stored up plenty of energy.")

Today's list groups katakana words two ways: words that get shortened in everyday speech (デジカメ ← デジタルカメラ), and loanwords whose origin isn't English (ワクチン, from German "Vakzin").

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.93) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.93). Answers confirmed from the "93ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.95.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼は（a. ゼミ　b. プロ）のカメラマンです。Kare wa (a. zemi / b. puro) no kameraman desu.

Full sentence: 彼はプロのカメラマンです。Kare wa puro no kameraman desu.
EN: He is a professional cameraman.
HI: वह एक पेशेवर कैमरामैन है।
GU: તે એક વ્યાવસાયિક કૅમેરામૅન છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aゼミzemiseminar (unrelated to a job)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bプロpuroprofessionalपेशेवरવ્યાવસાયિક

Why: プロのカメラマン ("a professional cameraman") is the natural phrase for someone who does photography as a job; ゼミ is a university seminar, unrelated to a profession.

Q2

インフルエンザの（a. ウイルス　b. ワクチン）が不足している。Infuruenza no (a. uirusu / b. wakuchin) ga fusoku shite iru.

Full sentence: インフルエンザのワクチンが不足している。Infuruenza no wakuchin ga fusoku shite iru.
EN: The flu vaccine is in short supply.
HI: फ्लू के टीके की कमी है।
GU: ફ્લૂની રસીની અછત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aウイルスuirusuvirus (spreads, isn't "in short supply")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bワクチンwakuchinvaccineटीकाરસી

Why: A vaccine is something manufactured and stocked, so it can be 不足している ("in short supply"); a virus isn't a supply that runs short — it spreads or is present, so ウイルス doesn't fit.

Q3

緑黄色野菜には（a. ビタミン　b. ビニール）が多い。Ryokuoushoku yasai ni wa (a. bitamin / b. biniiru) ga ooi.

Full sentence: 緑黄色野菜にはビタミンが多い。Ryokuoushoku yasai ni wa bitamin ga ooi.
EN: Green and yellow vegetables are rich in vitamins.
HI: हरी-पीली सब्ज़ियों में विटामिन भरपूर होते हैं।
GU: લીલા-પીળા શાકભાજીમાં વિટામિન વધુ હોય છે.
aビタミンbitaminvitaminविटामिनવિટામિન
bビニールbiniiruvinyl/plastic (a similar-sounding but unrelated word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ビタミン (vitamin) is the nutrient found in vegetables; ビニール (vinyl) only sounds similar — it names a plastic material and has nothing to do with nutrition.

EN: Electricity is generated using solar energy.
HI: सौर ऊर्जा का उपयोग करके बिजली बनाई जाती है।
GU: સૌર ઊર્જાનો ઉપયોગ કરીને વીજળી ઉત્પન્ન કરાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aアレルギーarerugiiallergy (a similar-sounding but unrelated word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bエネルギーenerugiienergyऊर्जाઊર્જા

Why: 太陽エネルギー ("solar energy") is the fixed phrase for power generation; アレルギー (allergy) only sounds similar to エネルギー and has no connection to electricity.

Q7

Full sentence: 明日の12時に面会のアポをとった。Ashita no juuni-ji ni menkai no apo o totta.

HI: मैंने कल 12 बजे मिलने का समय तय कर लिया।
GU: મેં આવતીકાલે 12 વાગ્યે મળવાનો સમય નક્કી કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1アポapoappointmentमुलाक़ात का समयમુલાકાતનો સમય
2ミスmisumistake (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3アマamaamateur (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ミスmisumistake (doesn't stop trains)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today only sound alike — this is exactly what Q3/Q4/Q6 above are testing, plus a couple of near-synonym traps. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
ビタミンbitamin Vitamin (a nutrient) Sounds close to ビニール but is completely unrelated in meaning. "Green and yellow vegetables are rich in vitamins."
ビニールbiniiru Vinyl / plastic (a material) A plastic material, e.g. a plastic umbrella — not a nutrient. "A plastic umbrella."
エネルギーenerugii Energy Sounds close to アレルギー but is completely unrelated in meaning. "Generate electricity using solar energy."
アレルギーarerugii Allergy A medical reaction to a substance — not a power source. "An allergic reaction."
ホームhoomu A train platform Names a physical place; doesn't describe crowding or a lecture's subject. "Meet on the station platform."
テーマteema Theme, topic The subject of a talk/paper — the fixed partner of ～に講義する／～について書く. "Lecture with this issue as the theme."
ウイルスuirusu Virus (a pathogen) Something that spreads/infects — not manufactured or stocked. "The flu virus."
ワクチンwakuchin Vaccine (a product) Something manufactured and stocked — can be "in short supply." "The flu vaccine is in short supply."
Antonym trap: インフレ (inflation, rising prices) and デフレ (deflation, falling prices) are opposites (⇔) — both are shortened forms (インフレーション／デフレーション) and are easy to swap by mistake under time pressure.
