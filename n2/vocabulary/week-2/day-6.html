Answer key

Notes
Scene: a child complains about a botched print job (wrong paper size, no margins, wrong orientation, wasted ink) while a parent shrugs it off as a first attempt. Vocabulary splits into a printer-troubleshooting table (p.38) and a security/online-activity table (p.39).

Scene

A child inspects a badly printed page while a parent looks on, and a sibling shrugs it off.

Child: あーあ、用紙のサイズも違うし、余白も全然ないし、印刷の向きも違うし、インクのむだだよ。直す手間もかかるし……。Aaa, youshi no saizu mo chigau shi, yohaku mo zenzen nai shi, insatsu no muki mo chigau shi, inku no muda da yo. Naosu tema mo kakaru shi...
("Ugh, the paper size is wrong, there's no margin at all, the print orientation is off too — what a waste of ink. And it'll take effort to fix, too...")

Sibling (thinking): 初めてやったんだからしょうがないでしょ。Hajimete yattandakara shouganai desho.
("Well, it was their first time doing it, so it can't be helped.")

きょうの一言：プリンターの用紙が切れていませんか。(Purintaa no youshi ga kirete imasen ka.) — "Isn't the printer out of paper?"

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.39) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.39). Answers confirmed from the "39ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.42.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

ファイルを（a. 閉じて　b. 完了して）パソコンを終了させた。Fairu o (a. tojite / b. kanryou shite) pasokon o shuuryou saseta.

Full sentence: ファイルを閉じてパソコンを終了させた。Fairu o tojite pasokon o shuuryou saseta.
EN: I closed the file and shut down the computer.
HI: फ़ाइल बंद करके कंप्यूटर बंद किया।
GU: ફાઇલ બંધ કરીને કમ્પ્યુટર બંધ કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a閉じてtojiteclosingबंद करकेબંધ કરીને
b完了してkanryou shitecompleting (fits a download finishing, not manually closing a file)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ファイルを閉じる ("to close a file," from the ファイルメニュー's 閉じる icon) is the action you take before shutting down; 完了する describes something like a download reaching completion on its own, not a deliberate close action.

Q2

コンピューターウイルスに（a. 感染　b. 接続）したらしく、正しく動かない。Konpyuutaa uirusu ni (a. kansen / b. setsuzoku) shita rashiku, tadashiku ugokanai.

Full sentence: コンピューターウイルスに感染したらしく、正しく動かない。Konpyuutaa uirusu ni kansen shita rashiku, tadashiku ugokanai.
EN: It seems to have been infected by a computer virus, and it's not working properly.
HI: लगता है यह कंप्यूटर वायरस से संक्रमित हो गया है, इसलिए ठीक से काम नहीं कर रहा।
GU: લાગે છે કમ્પ્યુટર વાયરસથી સંક્રમિત થયું છે, એટલે બરાબર ચાલતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a感染kansengetting infectedसंक्रमित होनाસંક્રમિત થવું
b接続setsuzokuconnecting (wouldn't by itself explain a malfunction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: ウイルスに感染する ("to get infected by a virus") is the natural explanation for a computer misbehaving; 接続する ("to connect") is neutral and doesn't by itself explain malfunction.

Q3

1行ずつ入力するのは（a. 手間を省く　b. 手間がかかる）。Ichigyou zutsu nyuuryoku suru no wa (a. tema o habuku / b. tema ga kakaru).

Full sentence: 1行ずつ入力するのは手間がかかる。Ichigyou zutsu nyuuryoku suru no wa tema ga kakaru.
EN: Typing one line at a time takes a lot of effort.
HI: एक-एक पंक्ति करके टाइप करना बहुत मेहनत का काम है।
GU: એક-એક લીટી કરીને ટાઈપ કરવું ઘણી મહેનતનું કામ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a手間を省くtema o habukusaves effort (the opposite of what a slow, line-by-line method does)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b手間がかかるtema ga kakarutakes a lot of effortमेहनत लगती हैમહેનત લાગે છે

Why: Typing line-by-line is slow and laborious, so 手間がかかる ("takes a lot of effort") fits; 手間を省く ("saves effort") describes the opposite outcome — a shortcut, not a tedious method.

Q4

このソフトは迷惑メールの受信を（a. 消去　b. 拒否）します。Kono sofuto wa meiwaku meeru no jushin o (a. shoukyo / b. kyohi) shimasu.

Full sentence: このソフトは迷惑メールの受信を拒否します。Kono sofuto wa meiwaku meeru no jushin o kyohi shimasu.
EN: This software rejects/blocks the receiving of junk mail.
HI: यह सॉफ़्टवेयर स्पैम मेल प्राप्त होने से रोकता है।
GU: આ સોફ્ટવેર સ્પામ મેલ મળવાનું અટકાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a消去shoukyodeleting (data already received, not blocking it from arriving)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b拒否kyohirejecting/refusingअस्वीकार करनाનકારવું

Q7

Full sentence: 紙がずれてしまって、印刷が上手にできない。Kami ga zurete shimatte, insatsu ga jouzu ni dekinai.

HI: काग़ज़ खिसक गया, इसलिए प्रिंटिंग सही से नहीं हो रही।
GU: કાગળ ખસી ગયો, એટલે પ્રિન્ટિંગ બરાબર થતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かかってkakattetaking (time/effort — wrong verb for paper)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2ずれてzuretegetting misalignedखिसककरખસીને

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart words for printer trouble and data/security problems — exactly what Q4, Q7, and Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
印刷がずれるinsatsu ga zureru The printing is misaligned Paper/text shifted out of position during printing. Output looks crooked/offset.
用紙が切れるyoushi ga kireru The printer runs out of paper No paper left at all — a supply problem. Printer stops because the tray is empty.
受信を拒否するjushin o kyohi suru To reject incoming messages Blocks unwanted mail before/as it arrives. Setting up a spam filter.
消去するshoukyo suru To delete/erase Removes data that already exists on the system. Clearing out old files.
データが消えるdeeta ga kieru Data is lost (by accident) An unwanted, often sudden loss — e.g. from a power cut. "Everything I typed disappeared."
ウイルスに感染するuirusu ni kansen suru To get infected by a virus A malware-caused malfunction, not a power/hardware issue. Explaining why a PC behaves strangely.
Register note: This day's closing line 「初めてやったんだからしょうがないでしょ。」("It was their first time, so it can't be helped") uses しょうがない — a casual, spoken equivalent of the more formal 仕方がない — reflecting the informal sibling-to-sibling tone of the scene, not something you'd write in a business email.
