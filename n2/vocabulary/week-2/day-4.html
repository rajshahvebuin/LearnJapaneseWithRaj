4日目 — パソコンを使っています① (Pasokon o tsukatte imasu ①)

Source

Theme
パソコンの部品・インターネット接続に関する言葉 (PC hardware, internet connectivity)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "35ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.37 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a man's internet connection acts up and a friend suggests restarting. Opens with an "絵をみておぼえましょう" (look at the picture and learn) labeled diagram of a desktop computer's parts, then a "もっとおぼえましょう" table of PC/internet action verbs.

Scene

A man at his desktop computer complains that something has gone wrong while online; a friend suggests a fix.

Him: インターネットに接続してたら、なんかおかしくなったよ。Intaanetto ni setsuzoku shite tara, nanka okashiku natta yo.
("While I was connected to the internet, something went weird.")

Friend: 使ってるソフトを終了して再起動してみたら？Tsukatteru sofuto o shuuryou shite saikidou shite mitara?
("Why don't you close the software you're using and try restarting?")

終了して再起動してみたら？(shuuryou shite saikidou shite mitara?) — the classic "turn it off and on again" fix

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.35) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.35). Answers confirmed from the "35ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.37.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

インターネットからそのソフトを（a. アクセス　b. ダウンロード）した。Intaanetto kara sono sofuto o (a. akusesu / b. daunroodo) shita.

Full sentence: インターネットからそのソフトをダウンロードした。Intaanetto kara sono sofuto o daunroodo shita.
EN: I downloaded that software from the internet.
HI: मैंने इंटरनेट से वह सॉफ़्टवेयर डाउनलोड किया।
GU: મેં ઇન્ટરનેટ પરથી તે સોફ્ટવેર ડાઉનલોડ કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aアクセスakusesuaccessing (fits a website, not bringing software onto your PC)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bダウンロードdaunroododownloadingडाउनलोडડાઉનલોડ

Why: ダウンロードする ("to download") is the action of bringing software from the internet onto your own computer; アクセスする is for reaching/visiting a site or system, not the act of retrieving a file.

Q2

フリーズしたので、パソコンを（a. 起動　b. 再起動）した。Furiizu shita node, pasokon o (a. kidou / b. saikidou) shita.

Full sentence: フリーズしたので、パソコンを再起動した。Furiizu shita node, pasokon o saikidou shita.
EN: Since it froze, I restarted the computer.
HI: हैंग हो जाने के कारण मैंने कंप्यूटर को पुनः चालू किया।
GU: હેંગ થઈ જવાથી મેં કમ્પ્યુટર ફરી ચાલુ કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a起動kidoustarting up (for a computer that's already off, not a frozen one)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b再起動saikidourestartingपुनः चालू करनाફરી ચાલુ કરવું

Why: A frozen computer needs 再起動 ("restart" — turning it off and back on to fix a problem); 起動 is simply the first-time startup of a machine that was off, not the fix for a freeze.

Q3

家でインターネットが使えるように、プロバイダーと（a. 契約　b. 接続）した。Ie de intaanetto ga tsukaeru you ni, purobaidaa to (a. keiyaku / b. setsuzoku) shita.

Full sentence: 家でインターネットが使えるように、プロバイダーと契約した。Ie de intaanetto ga tsukaeru you ni, purobaidaa to keiyaku shita.
EN: So that I could use the internet at home, I signed a contract with a provider.
HI: घर पर इंटरनेट इस्तेमाल करने के लिए, प्रोवाइडर से अनुबंध किया।
GU: ઘરે ઇન્ટરનેટ વાપરી શકાય એ માટે, પ્રોવાઇડર સાથે કરાર કર્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a契約keiyakusigning a contractअनुबंध करनाકરાર કરવો
b接続setsuzokuconnecting (what you do to the internet itself, not to a provider company)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: プロバイダーと契約する ("to sign a contract with a provider") is the fixed collocation for the business arrangement with と; 接続する is what you do to the internet itself (インターネットに接続する, with に), not to the provider company.

Q4

パソコンが（a. 起動　b. フリーズ）したのでカーソルが動かない。Pasokon ga (a. kidou / b. furiizu) shita node kaasoru ga ugokanai.

Full sentence: パソコンがフリーズしたのでカーソルが動かない。Pasokon ga furiizu shita node kaasoru ga ugokanai.
EN: The computer froze, so the cursor doesn't move.
HI: कंप्यूटर हैंग हो गया, इसलिए कर्सर हिलता नहीं है।
GU: કમ્પ્યુટર હેંગ થઈ ગયું, એટલે કર્સર હલતું નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a起動kidoustarting up (would make the cursor work, not freeze it)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bフリーズfuriizufreezingहैंग होनाહેંગ થવું

Why: フリーズする ("to freeze") explains why the cursor stops responding; 起動する ("to start up") is the opposite direction — it's what makes a computer usable, not what stops the cursor.

HI: फ़ाइल खोलने के लिए माउस क्लिक करता/करती हूँ।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for "starting/restarting" a computer and "reaching/getting" something online — exactly what Q1, Q2/Q4, and Q3/Q6 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
起動するkidou suru To start up (from off) The first-time boot of a machine that was powered off. "I turned on the computer."
再起動するsaikidou suru To restart Turning an already-running (often malfunctioning) machine off and back on. "I restarted it because it froze."
ダウンロードするdaunroodo suru To download Bringing a file/program from the internet onto your device. "I downloaded the software."
アクセスするakusesu suru To access Reaching/visiting a site or system — no file necessarily changes hands. "I accessed the company's website."
〜と契約する~ to keiyaku suru To sign a contract with (a company) と marks the other party in a business agreement. "I signed up with a provider."
〜に接続する~ ni setsuzoku suru To connect to (a network) に marks the network/system you're plugging into. "I connected to the internet."
Particle trap: プロバイダーと契約する uses と (with a business partner), while インターネットに接続する uses に (into a network) — swapping the particles produces nonsense (プロバイダーに契約する or インターネットと接続する are both unnatural), so learn the verb and its particle together, not separately.
