Q2

EN: I'm Tanaka — I was transferred here from the Osaka branch office.

GU: હું તનાકા છું, ઓસાકા શાખાએથી બદલી થઈને આવ્યો/આવી છું.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1転送tensouforwarding (a call/data, not a person)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2派遣hakendispatching (transitive — a company dispatches someone; awkward for self-introduction without されて)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

不景気で、ボーナスを（　）のはなかなか難しい。Fukeiki de, boonasu o (___) no wa nakanaka muzukashii.

Full sentence: 不景気で、ボーナスをかせぐのはなかなか難しい。Fukeiki de, boonasu o kasegu no wa nakanaka muzukashii.
EN: With the recession, it's quite difficult to earn a bonus.
HI: मंदी के कारण, बोनस कमाना काफ़ी मुश्किल है।
GU: મંદીના કારણે, બોનસ કમાવવું ઘણું મુશ્કેલ છે.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1もうかるmoukaruto turn a profit (intransitive — cannot take を as in "ボーナスをもうかる")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2かせぐkaseguto earn (transitive, takes を)कमानाકમાવવું
3支給するshikyuu suruto pay out (from the company's side, not what the employee does to earn it)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q21

こなすkonasu

#JapaneseEnglish
1ミーティングをこなしています。Miitingu o konashite imasu."I am [konasu]-ing a meeting" — こなす is for handling a volume/flow of tasks, not simply holding one meeting
2彼は早めに昼食をこなした。Kare wa hayame ni chuushoku o konashita."He [konasu]-ed lunch early" — doesn't fit eating a meal
3電話の保留をこなした。Denwa no horyuu o konashita."He [konasu]-ed the phone hold" — doesn't fit a single brief action
4スケジュールどおり仕事をこなしていった。Sukejuuru doori shigoto o konashite itta.He worked through his tasks efficiently, according to schedule.

Why: Day 3's 仕事を順調にこなす defines こなす as working through a volume of tasks efficiently — sentence 4 matches that exactly, while 1–3 misapply it to single events/actions (a meeting, a meal, a phone hold) that don't involve processing a workload.

Q22

ゴマをするgoma o suru

#JapaneseEnglish
1部長は正直にゴマをすった。Buchou wa shoujiki ni goma o sutta."The manager honestly flattered" — flattery and honesty (正直に) contradict each other
2上司にゴマをすらないように、相談にのった。Joushi ni goma o suranai you ni, soudan ni notta."So as not to flatter the boss, I gave advice" — the two clauses don't logically connect
3彼は上司にゴマをすりながら出世した。Kare wa joushi ni goma o suri nagara shusse shita.He advanced his career while flattering his boss.
4疲れたのでちょっとゴマをすってもいいでしょうか。Tsukareta node chotto goma o sutte mo ii deshou ka."Since I'm tired, may I flatter a little?" — nonsensical connection to tiredness

Why: 上司にゴマをする ("to flatter one's boss") is a cynical remark about currying favor for career gain — sentence 3's "advanced his career while flattering his boss" is the classic, natural usage; the others pair it with contradictory or unrelated context.

Q23

席を外すseki o hazusu

#JapaneseEnglish
1課長の鈴木はあいにく今、席を外しております。Kachou no Suzuki wa ainiku ima, seki o hazushite orimasu.Unfortunately, Section Chief Suzuki has stepped away from his desk right now.
2課長の鈴木が席を外してお伺いいたします。Kachou no Suzuki ga seki o hazushite oukagai itashimasu."Suzuki will step away from his desk and come visit" — unnatural combination

Q24

長引くnagabiku

#JapaneseEnglish
1電車に乗り遅れて、待ち合わせに長引いてしまった。Densha ni noriokurete, machiawase ni nagabiite shimatta."I missed the train and the meet-up 'ran long'" — being late isn't "prolonging," should be 遅れて

4打ち合わせが長引いて、次の約束に遅れてしまいました。Uchiawase ga nagabiite, tsugi no yakusoku ni okurete shimaimashita.The meeting ran long, and I ended up late for my next appointment.

Why: 会議／打ち合わせが長引く ("a meeting runs long") is the correct usage, matching Day 3's own vocabulary and diary entry; 1–3 misapply 長引く to lateness, sentence length, and a fixed daily duration — none of which involve an event unexpectedly dragging past its expected end.
