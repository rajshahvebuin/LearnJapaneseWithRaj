3日目 — 仕事をしています② (Shigoto o shite imasu ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.32–33
Theme
会議・電話応対・キャリアの変化に関する言葉 (meetings, phone handling, career changes)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "33ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.35 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This day opens with a handwritten diary entry (5月24日 金) instead of a comic dialogue — a new format for this book. The writer reflects on a long, unproductive meeting, and notes a new employee's nervous-but-enthusiastic first meeting.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
(V) = 動詞 (verb)
Scene — diary entry

5月24日（金）Gogatsu nijuuyokka (kin)

今日は会議が長引いて疲れた。自分の意見を述べたが、具体的な案を出すことができなかった。結局、今日の問題について、結論が出ないまま終わった。意見をまとめるのは本当に大変だと思った。Kyou wa kaigi ga nagabiite tsukareta. Jibun no iken o nobeta ga, gutaiteki na an o dasu koto ga dekinakatta. Kekkyoku, kyou no mondai ni tsuite, ketsuron ga denai mama owatta. Iken o matomeru no wa hontou ni taihen da to omotta.
("Today the meeting ran long and I got tired. I stated my own opinion, but I couldn't come up with a concrete proposal. In the end, regarding today's problem, it ended without reaching a conclusion. I thought summarizing everyone's opinions is really tough.")

4月に入社したばかりの中村君も出席したが、初めての会議で意見を求められて緊張したようだ。でも、資料を配ったり、メモを取ったりして張り切っていた。Shigatsu ni nyuusha shita bakari no Nakamura-kun mo shusseki shita ga, hajimete no kaigi de iken o motomerarete kinchou shita you da. Demo, shiryou o kubattari, memo o tottari shite harikitte ita.
("Nakamura, who just joined the company in April, also attended, and he seemed nervous being asked for his opinion at his first meeting. But he was full of enthusiasm, handing out materials and taking notes.")

結論が出ないまま (ketsuron ga denai mama) — "without a conclusion emerging," the frustrating outcome of a meeting that ran long for nothing

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.33) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.33). Answers confirmed from the "33ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.35.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

明日の会議で新しい商品の（a. 案　b. メモ）を出さなければならない。Ashita no kaigi de atarashii shouhin no (a. an / b. memo) o dasanakereba naranai.

Full sentence: 明日の会議で新しい商品の案を出さなければならない。Ashita no kaigi de atarashii shouhin no an o dasanakereba naranai.
EN: Tomorrow at the meeting, I have to present a proposal for a new product.
HI: कल की बैठक में मुझे नए उत्पाद का प्रस्ताव देना होगा।
GU: આવતીકાલની બેઠકમાં મારે નવા ઉત્પાદનનો પ્રસ્તાવ રજૂ કરવો પડશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a案ana plan/proposalप्रस्तावપ્રસ્તાવ
bメモmemoa note (something you take, not present as a proposal)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 具体的な案を出す ("to suggest a concrete plan/proposal") is the fixed collocation for presenting an idea at a meeting; メモ is what you take during a meeting, not something you "present."

Q2

明日から2日間、東京へ（a. 転勤　b. 出張）します。Ashita kara futsuka-kan, Toukyou e (a. tenkin / b. shucchou) shimasu.

Full sentence: 明日から2日間、東京へ出張します。Ashita kara futsuka-kan, Toukyou e shucchou shimasu.
EN: Starting tomorrow, I'll be on a business trip to Tokyo for two days.
HI: कल से 2 दिन के लिए टोक्यो व्यापारिक यात्रा पर जा रहा/रही हूँ।
GU: આવતીકાલથી 2 દિવસ માટે ટોકિયો બિઝનેસ ટ્રિપ પર જાઉં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a転勤tenkina permanent job transfer (not a 2-day trip)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b出張shucchoua business tripव्यापारिक यात्राબિઝનેસ ટ્રિપ

Why: A short, temporary 2-day visit is 出張 ("business trip"); 転勤 means being permanently reassigned to work at a different location, not a brief trip.

Q3

Full sentence: 来月のスケジュールを組んでから、またご連絡します。Raigetsu no sukejuuru o kunde kara, mata go-renraku shimasu.
EN: After I put together next month's schedule, I'll contact you again.
HI: अगले महीने का शेड्यूल बनाने के बाद, मैं फिर संपर्क करूँगा/करूँगी।
GU: આવતા મહિનાનું શેડ્યુલ બનાવ્યા પછી, હું ફરી સંપર્ક કરીશ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a取ってtottetaking (fits 休みを取る, not スケジュール)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: スケジュールを組む ("to put together a schedule") is the fixed collocation; 取る pairs with 休み (time off), not with スケジュール.

Q4

会議で資料を（a. 述べた　b. 配った）。Kaigi de shiryou o (a. nobeta / b. kubatta).

Full sentence: 会議で資料を配った。Kaigi de shiryou o kubatta.
EN: I handed out materials at the meeting.
HI: बैठक में सामग्री बांटी।
GU: બેઠકમાં સામગ્રી વહેંચી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a述べたnobetastated (pairs with 意見, not 資料 as a physical object)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b配ったkubattadistributed/handed outबांटाવહેંચ્યું

Why: 資料を配る ("to distribute materials") is the physical act of handing out documents; 述べる ("to state") is for stating opinions/conclusions verbally, not distributing physical handouts.

Q5

最後に結論が（a. 述べられた　b. 長引かれた）。Saigo ni ketsuron ga (a. noberareta / b. nagabikareta).

Full sentence: 最後に結論が述べられた。Saigo ni ketsuron ga noberareta.
EN: In the end, the conclusion was stated.
HI: आख़िर में निष्कर्ष बताया गया।
GU: છેવટે નિષ્કર્ષ જણાવવામાં આવ્યો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a述べられたnoberaretawas statedबताया गयाજણાવવામાં આવ્યો
b長引かれたnagabikaretaan unnatural passive of an already-intransitive verb; 長引く describes meetings dragging on, not a conclusion itself(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 結論を述べる ("to state a conclusion") passivizes naturally to 結論が述べられる; 長引く ("to be prolonged") describes a meeting or event dragging on in time, not something that happens "to" a conclusion itself.

Q6

「その仕事は私が（a. 引き受けます　b. 打ち合わせます）。」"Sono shigoto wa watashi ga (a. hikiukemasu / b. uchiawasemasu)."

Full sentence: 「その仕事は私が引き受けます。」"Sono shigoto wa watashi ga hikiukemasu."
EN: "I'll take on that job."
HI: "वह काम मैं संभाल लूँगा/लूँगी।"
GU: "એ કામ હું સંભાળી લઈશ."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a引き受けますhikiukemasuwill take on/acceptसंभाल लूँगाસંભાળી લઈશ
b打ち合わせますuchiawasemasuwill arrange/discuss in advance (about coordinating, not accepting a task)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 仕事を引き受ける means "to take on/accept a job" — volunteering to handle a task; 打ち合わせる means to arrange or discuss something in advance with someone, not to accept responsibility for it.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart verbs for handling opinions/materials at a meeting, and on scale-of-absence words for career changes — exactly what Q1/Q4/Q5, Q2, and Q7 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
意見を述べるiken o noberu To state an opinion A verbal/written expression of a viewpoint. "I stated my opinion at the meeting."
資料を配るshiryou o kubaru To distribute materials A physical handing-out of documents/objects. "He handed out the handouts."
出張shucchou A business trip Temporary — you return to your normal post afterward. "I'm going to Tokyo for two days."
転勤tenkin A job transfer Permanent (or long-term) — your work location itself changes. "I was transferred to the head office."
出世する／昇進するshusse suru / shoushin suru To advance/get promoted Positive career movement, staying at the same company. Describing a career success.
首になる／リストラされるkubi ni naru / risutora sareru To get fired/laid off Involuntary loss of a job — the opposite of career advancement. Describing a job loss.
Passive-form trap: Not every verb passivizes naturally into a meaningful new word — 述べる → 述べられる ("is stated") is a normal, useful passive, but forcing 長引く (already intransitive, "to drag on by itself") into a passive like 長引かれる produces an unnatural form that isn't used to describe a conclusion or any single item; 長引く always takes an event (会議, 話し合い) as its subject, not a result like 結論.
