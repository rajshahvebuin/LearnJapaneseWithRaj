JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 2 — 仕事は順調ですか？ (Shigoto wa junchou desu ka?)

1日目 — 仕事を探しています (Shigoto o sagashite imasu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.28–29
Theme
就職活動・求人・面接に関する言葉 (job hunting, job listings, interviews)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "29ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.31 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a young woman reads a "home helper wanted" job flyer, thinking she needs to earn money. Day opens with a "□にいれてよみましょう" (fill in the box and read) substitution drill — a sentence pattern with a list of job/role words to slot in — instead of a dialogue.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A young woman looks at a flyer for a nursing-home job while thinking about money.

Her (thinking): お金かせがなきゃいけないし、面接に行ってみようかな。Okane kasenakya ikenai shi, mensetsu ni itte miyou kana.
("I need to earn money, so maybe I should go to the interview.")

Flyer: ホームヘルパー募集！経験は問いません。50歳くらいまでの方。お気軽にお問い合わせください。やすらぎ老人ホームHoomuherupaa boshuu! Keiken wa toimasen. Gojussai kurai made no kata. Okigaru ni otoiawase kudasai. Yasuragi Roujin Hoomu.
("Home helper wanted! No experience necessary. Applicants up to around age 50. Feel free to inquire. Yasuragi Elderly Home.")

□にいれてよみましょう (kuchi ni irete yomimashou) — "fill in the box and read": お仕事は？ — [　　]をしています／[　　]です。 (What's your job? — I do [　], I am a [　].)

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

パソコンの辞書に自分の名前を（a. 採用　b. 登録）した。Pasokon no jisho ni jibun no namae o (a. saiyou / b. touroku) shita.

Full sentence: パソコンの辞書に自分の名前を登録した。Pasokon no jisho ni jibun no namae o touroku shita.
EN: I registered my own name in the computer's dictionary.
HI: मैंने कंप्यूटर की डिक्शनरी में अपना नाम दर्ज किया।
GU: મેં કમ્પ્યુટરની ડિક્શનરીમાં મારું નામ નોંધ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a採用saiyouhiring/adopting (about people/proposals, not dictionary entries)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b登録tourokuregisteringदर्ज करनाનોંધવું

Why: 登録する ("to register") is the natural verb for adding an entry (like your name) into a computer dictionary/database; 採用する means "to hire/adopt," used for people or proposals, not data entries.

Q2

給料に加えて食事代として一人2,000円（a. 引き落とし　b. 支給）された。Kyuuryou ni kuwaete shokujidai toshite hitori nisen-en (a. hikiotoshi / b. shikyuu) sareta.

Full sentence: 給料に加えて食事代として一人2,000円支給された。Kyuuryou ni kuwaete shokujidai toshite hitori nisen-en shikyuu sareta.
EN: In addition to salary, 2,000 yen per person was provided as meal money.
HI: वेतन के अलावा, भोजन ख़र्च के रूप में प्रति व्यक्ति 2,000 येन दिए गए।
GU: પગાર ઉપરાંત, ભોજન ખર્ચ તરીકે વ્યક્તિદીઠ 2,000 યેન અપાયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a引き落としhikiotoshiautomatic deduction (money going out, not being given)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b支給shikyuubeing provided/paid outदिया गयाઅપાયું

HI: होम हेल्पर्स की संख्या अभी भी कम है।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for hiring, earning, and fitting an occasion — exactly what Q1/Q7, Q2, and Q6 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
採用するsaiyou suru To hire (a specific candidate) The company's decision to bring on one particular person. "He was hired by the company."
募集するboshuu suru To recruit/advertise for candidates The company's act of posting a job opening — before anyone is chosen. "The company is advertising for staff."
お金をかせぐokane o kasegu To earn money (a person, from labor) An individual's wages from working. "She earns 100,000 yen a month part-time."
店がもうかるmise ga moukaru A business turns a profit A business/store as the subject, not a person's wage. "The shop is turning a good profit."
ふさわしいfusawashii Suitable, appropriate (for an occasion) Matches the formality/purpose of a specific situation. "Clothes suitable for a wedding/interview."
気軽なkigaru na Casual, easygoing Describes a relaxed attitude or manner, not fitness for an occasion. "Feel free to ask casually."
Fixed-phrase trap: 経験を問わない and 年齢を問わない are both fixed job-listing phrases meaning "regardless of experience/age" — 問う here means "to ask about/require," not "to inquire" (that's 問い合わせる, a different verb entirely, used for what an applicant does to a company, not what a job posting itself does).
