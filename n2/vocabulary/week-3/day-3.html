3日目 — どんな動作ですか？ (Donna dousa desu ka?)

Source

Theme
体の部位ごとの動作を表す言葉 (words for physical actions/gestures, grouped by body part)
Answer key

Notes
Scene: a dotted-line "mystery" panel narrates a small street incident — a man with a magazine under his arm and a cigarette in his mouth leans on a wall; a woman passing by trips and falls, then glares at him for having watched. A curious boy reading a book wonders what's going on. Vocabulary is grouped exactly as the book groups it: by body part (目/口/頭・首・肩・腕/手/足/全身).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A dotted-line panel narrates a small incident on the street, watched by a curious boy holding a book.

Narration: 雑誌をわきに抱えた男が、たばこをくわえて壁にもたれている。その前を通り過ぎようとした女がつまずいてころんだ。その様子を見ていた男を、女はにらんだ。Zasshi o waki ni kakaeta otoko ga, tabako o kuwaete kabe ni motarete iru. Sono mae o toorisugiyou to shita onna ga tsumazuite koronda. Sono yousu o mite ita otoko o, onna wa niranda.
("A man with a magazine tucked under his arm leans against the wall with a cigarette in his mouth. A woman who tried to pass in front of him tripped and fell. The woman glared at the man who had been watching this happen.")

Boy (thinking): ？
("What kind of behaviors are they talking about?")

The narration itself previews five of today's key phrases: わきに抱える (carry under one's arm), たばこをくわえる (leave a cigarette in one's mouth), 壁にもたれる (lean against a wall), つまずく (stumble), and にらむ (glare at).

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.49) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.49). Answers confirmed from the "49ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.51.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

車のドアで指を（a. はさんだ　b. ふれた）。Kuruma no doa de yubi o (a. hasanda / b. fureta).

Full sentence: 車のドアで指をはさんだ。Kuruma no doa de yubi o hasanda.
EN: I caught/pinched my finger in the car door.
HI: कार के दरवाज़े में उँगली दब गई।
GU: કારના દરવાજામાં આંગળી ભીંસાઈ ગઈ.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aはさんだhasandacaught/pinched (between two things)दब गईભીંસાઈ ગઈ
bふれたfuretatouched lightly (doesn't describe getting caught/pinched)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 指をはさむ ("to catch/pinch one's finger," matching this day's パンにハムをはさむ) fits something getting caught between two closing surfaces; ふれる means a light touch, not getting pinched.

Q2

変な虫が床を（a. はっている　b. はがしている）。Hen na mushi ga yuka o (a. hatte iru / b. hagashite iru).

Full sentence: 変な虫が床をはっている。Hen na mushi ga yuka o hatte iru.
EN: A strange bug is crawling on the floor.
HI: एक अजीब कीड़ा फ़र्श पर रेंग रहा है।
GU: એક વિચિત્ર જીવડું ફ્લોર પર સરકી રહ્યું છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aはっているhatte iruis crawlingरेंग रहा हैસરકી રહ્યું છે
bはがしているhagashite iruis peeling off (something), wrong verb entirely for a bug's movement(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 地面をはう ("to crawl on the ground") from this day's vocabulary is the verb for an insect's crawling movement; はがす means "to peel/strip off," an unrelated action.

Q3

この道はせまくて、車と車が（a. すれちがう　b. ぶつかる）のは大変だ。Kono michi wa semakute, kuruma to kuruma ga (a. surechigau / b. butsukaru) no wa taihen da.

Full sentence: この道はせまくて、車と車がすれちがうのは大変だ。Kono michi wa semakute, kuruma to kuruma ga surechigau no wa taihen da.
EN: This road is so narrow that it's hard for cars to pass each other.
HI: यह सड़क इतनी सँकरी है कि गाड़ियों का एक-दूसरे के पास से गुज़रना मुश्किल है।
GU: આ રસ્તો એટલો સાંકડો છે કે ગાડીઓ માટે એકબીજાની બાજુમાંથી પસાર થવું મુશ્કેલ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aすれちがうsurechigauto pass by (each other), without collidingबग़ल से गुज़रनाબાજુમાંથી પસાર થવું
bぶつかるbutsukaruto crash/bump into (the opposite of successfully passing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: すれちがう ("to pass by each other without colliding," matching this day's 自転車とすれ違う) fits two cars safely passing on a narrow road; ぶつかる means "to crash into," the very outcome the narrow road makes hard to avoid.

Q4

ひざを（a. ついたり　b. 曲げたり）伸ばしたりする。Hiza o (a. tsuitari / b. magetari) nobashitari suru.

Full sentence: ひざを曲げたり伸ばしたりする。Hiza o magetari nobashitari suru.
EN: to bend and stretch one's knees
HI: घुटनों को मोड़ना और सीधा करना
GU: ઘૂંટણ વાળવા અને સીધા કરવા
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aついたりtsuitariputting/resting on (pairs with ひじ — elbows — not ひざ knees)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b曲げたりmagetaribendingमोड़नाવાળવું

GU: મેં સ્કેટિંગમાં પાછળની તરફ લપસવાનો અભ્યાસ કર્યો.

HI: डिब्बे में क्या है, यह देखने के लिए झाँका।
GU: બોક્સમાં શું છે તે જોવા ડોકિયું કર્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aのぞいたnozoitapeekedझाँकाડોકિયું કર્યું
bにらんだnirandaglared (an angry stare at someone, not curious peeking)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q7

いらないポスターを（　）。Iranai posutaa o (___).

Full sentence: いらないポスターをはがす。Iranai posutaa o hagasu.
EN: to peel/take down a poster you don't need
HI: बेकार पोस्टर को उतारना/हटाना
GU: બિનજરૂરી પોસ્ટર ઉખાડવું/હટાવવું
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はがすhagasuto peel/strip offउतारना, उखाड़नाઉખાડવું
2ねじるnejiruto twist (fits 体をねじる, not removing a flat poster)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for looking, moving past something, and gripping/carrying — exactly what Q3/Q6 and Q1/Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
のぞくnozoku To peek/look in Curious, neutral looking into an enclosed space. "I peeked inside the box to see what was there."
にらむniramu To glare at An angry, pointed stare directed at a person. "She glared at the man who'd been watching."
すれちがうsurechigau To pass by (each other) Two things moving past one another without colliding. "Cars can barely pass each other on this narrow road."
ぶつかるbutsukaru To bump/crash into Actual collision — the failure mode すれちがう avoids. "He crashed into the wall."
はさむhasamu To catch/pinch between two things Something getting squeezed between two closing surfaces. "I caught my finger in the car door."
かつぐkatsugu To carry on one's shoulder A specific carrying posture — shoulder-borne, distinct from わきに抱える (under the arm) or 手でつかむ (gripped in the hand). "He shouldered the heavy pack."
Body-part organization: This day's vocabulary is grouped by which body part performs the action (目・口・頭/首/肩/腕・手・足・全身) rather than by meaning — when a question is ambiguous between two options, check which body part the sentence's subject uses (e.g. ひざ → 曲げる, not つく; ひじ → つく, not 曲げる).
