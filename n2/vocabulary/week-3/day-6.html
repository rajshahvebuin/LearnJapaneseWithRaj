Scene

Wife: あなた、顔が真っ青よ。どうしたの？Anata, kao ga massao yo. Doushita no?
("Honey, your face is so pale. What's wrong?")

Husband: さっきからずっと痛みをこらえてたんだけど、頭が割れるように痛くて……。Sakki kara zutto itami o koraetetan dakedo, atama ga wareru you ni itakute...
("I've been enduring the pain since earlier, but my head hurts like it's splitting...")

Husband: ううっ、吐き気もしてきた……。ちょっとトイレで吐いてくる……。Uu, hakike mo shite kita... Chotto toire de haite kuru...
("Ugh, I'm starting to feel nauseous too... I'm going to go throw up in the bathroom for a bit...")

顔が真っ青だ(kao ga massao da) — "looks very pale," the visible sign that something is wrong before any words are exchanged

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

肩を（a. こって　b. もんで）あげましょう。Kata o (a. kotte / b. monde) agemashou.

Full sentence: 肩をもんであげましょう。Kata o monde agemashou.
EN: Let me massage your shoulders.
HI: चलो, तुम्हारे कंधे दबा देता/देती हूँ।
GU: ચાલો, તારા ખભા દબાવી આપું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこってkottebecoming stiff (intransitive — something you experience, not do to someone)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bもんでmondemassagingदबाकरદબાવીને

Why: Offering to do something FOR someone (〜てあげる) needs a transitive action — 肩をもむ ("to massage shoulders"); 肩がこる ("shoulders become stiff") is what happens TO the shoulders, not an action you perform on someone else.

Q2

蚊にさされたところが（a. かゆい　b. だるい）。Ka ni sasareta tokoro ga (a. kayui / b. darui).

Full sentence: 蚊にさされたところがかゆい。Ka ni sasareta tokoro ga kayui.
EN: The spot where the mosquito bit me is itchy.
HI: मच्छर के काटने वाली जगह खुजली कर रही है।
GU: મચ્છર કરડ્યું ત્યાં ખંજવાળ આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかゆいkayuiitchyखुजलीખંજવાળ
bだるいdaruisluggish/heavy (describes whole-body fatigue, not a bite mark)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A mosquito bite spot is かゆい ("itchy"); だるい describes a general sluggish, heavy-bodied feeling — a mismatch for a localized bite.

Q3

階段を降りるとき、（a. ひざ　b. ひじ）が痛む。Kaidan o oriru toki, (a. hiza / b. hiji) ga itamu.

Full sentence: 階段を降りるとき、ひざが痛む。Kaidan o oriru toki, hiza ga itamu.
EN: My knee hurts when I go down stairs.
HI: सीढ़ियाँ उतरते समय घुटने में दर्द होता है।
GU: સીડી ઉતરતી વખતે ઘૂંટણમાં દુખાવો થાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aひざhizakneeघुटनाઘૂંટણ
bひじhijielbow (not weight-bearing on stairs)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Knees bear the weight and strain of going down stairs, so ひざ ("knee") is the joint that would ache; ひじ ("elbow") is a near-homophone trap unrelated to leg movement.

Q6

薬が効いたようで、だんだん痛みが（a. こらえて　b. おさまって）きた。Kusuri ga kiita you de, dandan itami ga (a. koraete / b. osamatte) kita.

Full sentence: 薬が効いたようで、だんだん痛みがおさまってきた。Kusuri ga kiita you de, dandan itami ga osamatte kita.
EN: The medicine seems to have worked — the pain has gradually subsided.
HI: दवा असर कर गई लगती है, धीरे-धीरे दर्द कम होता गया।
GU: દવાએ અસર કરી હોય એમ લાગે છે, ધીરે ધીરે દુખાવો ઓછો થતો ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aこらえてkoraeteenduring (something YOU do to pain, not what the pain itself does)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bおさまってosamattesubsiding/settling downकम होनाઓછું થવું

Why: Once medicine takes effect, the pain itself gradually おさまる ("subsides"); こらえる ("to endure") describes what the sufferer actively does to bear pain, not something the pain does on its own.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1症状shoujousymptomsलक्षणલક્ષણો

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's near-synonyms cluster around "what a body does under stress" and "what someone does about it" — exactly what Q1 and Q6 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
肩がこるkata ga koru Shoulders become stiff Intransitive — a state your shoulders are in. "My shoulders are stiff from the desk work."
肩をもむkata o momu To massage shoulders Transitive — an action performed on someone. "Let me massage your shoulders."
痛みをこらえる／に耐えるitami o koraeru / ni taeru To endure/bear pain What the sufferer actively does — a willed effort. "I gritted my teeth through the pain."
痛みがおさまるitami ga osamaru Pain subsides What the pain itself does — no effort involved. "The pain gradually went away."
熱中症necchuushou Heat stroke Overheating from environmental heat/exertion. "He collapsed from heat stroke in summer."
花粉症kafunshou Hay fever A seasonal pollen allergy — itchy eyes, stuffy nose. "My eyes itch every spring from hay fever."
Body-part trap: ひざ (knee) and ひじ (elbow) are a classic near-homophone pair that this book (and Q3 above) deliberately tests — ひざ is the leg joint that bends when sitting/climbing stairs, ひじ is the arm joint you rest on a table.
