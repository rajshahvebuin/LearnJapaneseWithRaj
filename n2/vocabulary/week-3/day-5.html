JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 3 — いろいろ表現しましょう (Iroiro hyougen shimashou)

5日目 — どんな気持ち・態度ですか？② (Donna kimochi / taido desu ka? ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.52–53
Theme
人にどう接するか・どう思うかを表す言葉 (words for how you treat someone or think of them — trust, betrayal, comfort, blame) と、態度を表す言葉 (words for attitude/conduct)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "53ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.55 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a monologue about an unreliable, joking coworker who talks behind people's backs and can't be trusted with important work. Three groups: 「田中さんを＿＿＿」(things you do to someone), 「田中さんに＿＿＿」(things you do toward someone), then "もっとおぼえましょう" (more attitude/conduct words). Includes two "ダメ" (wrong-usage) flags.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
"ダメ" スタンプ = このような使い方はだめです (this usage is NOT correct)
Scene

The illustration shows one man talking about a cheerful, joking coworker (the listener's raised eyebrows show mild alarm) — a third-person description of someone you can't quite trust.

彼は、よく冗談を言う明るい人ですが、ふざけてばかりいます。Kare wa, yoku joudan o iu akarui hito desu ga, fuzakete bakari imasu.
("He's a cheerful person who often jokes, but he's always fooling around.")

また、よく人の悪口を言うので、信用できません。だから、大事な仕事を任せることはできません。Mata, yoku hito no waruguchi o iu node, shinyou dekimasen. Dakara, daiji na shigoto o makaseru koto wa dekimasen.
("Also, since he often speaks ill of others, he can't be trusted. So I can't entrust him with important work.")

信用できない(shinyou dekinai) — someone who jokes and gossips too much loses others' trust

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.53) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.53). Answers confirmed from the "53ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.55.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

彼は自分の失敗をみんなに（a. 許した　b. わびた）。Kare wa jibun no shippai o minna ni (a. yurushita / b. wabita).

Full sentence: 彼は自分の失敗をみんなにわびた。Kare wa jibun no shippai o minna ni wabita.
EN: He apologized to everyone for his own mistake.
HI: उसने अपनी ग़लती के लिए सबसे माफ़ी माँगी।
GU: તેણે પોતાની ભૂલ માટે બધાની માફી માંગી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a許したyurushitaforgave (wrong direction — you forgive someone ELSE's mistake, not apologize for your own)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bわびたwabitaapologizedमाफ़ी माँगीમાફી માંગી

Why: Owning up to your own mistake in front of everyone is わびる ("to apologize"); 許す ("to forgive") is what the other party does in response, not what the person at fault does.

Q2

「また、がんばればいいよ」と親友に（a. からかわれた　b. なぐさめられた）。"Mata, ganbareba ii yo" to shinyuu ni (a. karakawareta / b. nagusamerareta).

Full sentence: 「また、がんばればいいよ」と親友になぐさめられた。"Mata, ganbareba ii yo" to shinyuu ni nagusamerareta.
EN: I was comforted by my close friend, who said "It's fine — just try again."
HI: "फिर से कोशिश कर लेना" कहकर मेरे ख़ास दोस्त ने मुझे सांत्वना दी।
GU: "ફરી પ્રયત્ન કરી લેજે" કહીને મારા ખાસ મિત્રએ મને સાંત્વના આપી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aからかわれたkarakawaretawas teased (a warm, supportive line isn't teasing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bなぐさめられたnagusameraretawas comfortedसांत्वना दी गईસાંત્વના અપાઈ

Why: A kind, encouraging remark from a close friend after a setback is なぐさめる ("to comfort"); からかう ("to tease/make fun of") would clash with the supportive tone of the quoted line.

Q3

「車の運転は、あなたに（a. まかせる　b. まねをする）わ。」"Kuruma no unten wa, anata ni (a. makaseru / b. mane o suru) wa."

Full sentence: 「車の運転は、あなたにまかせるわ。」"Kuruma no unten wa, anata ni makaseru wa."
EN: "I'll leave the driving to you."
HI: "गाड़ी चलाना तुम पर छोड़ती हूँ।"
GU: "ગાડી ચલાવવાનું તારા પર છોડું છું."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまかせるmakaseruto entrust/leave (a task) to someoneछोड़ना, सौंपनाસોંપવું
bまねをするmane o suruto imitate (doesn't fit "handing over" driving duty)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Handing a task like driving over to someone else is まかせる ("to entrust"); まねをする ("to imitate") describes copying someone's action, unrelated to delegating a task.

Q4

生徒を疑うような先生を（a. 尊敬　b. 文句）できません。Seito o utagau you na sensei o (a. sonkei / b. monku) dekimasen.

Full sentence: 生徒を疑うような先生を尊敬できません。Seito o utagau you na sensei o sonkei dekimasen.
EN: I cannot respect a teacher who would doubt his own students.
HI: अपने ही छात्रों पर शक करने वाले शिक्षक का सम्मान नहीं किया जा सकता।
GU: પોતાના જ વિદ્યાર્થીઓ પર શંકા કરતા શિક્ષકનું સન્માન કરી શકાય નહીં.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a尊敬sonkeirespectसम्मानસન્માન
b文句monkucomplaint (文句する isn't a real verb — must be 文句を言う)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 尊敬できません ("cannot respect") directly fits a を-marked object with できる/できない; 文句 needs the fixed phrase 文句を言う and cannot take できません on its own — it's the same "ダメ" trap flagged in this day's vocabulary.

EN: He tricked the customer into buying a fake.
HI: ग्राहक को धोखा देकर नक़ली सामान ख़रीदवा दिया।
GU: ગ્રાહકને છેતરીને નકલી વસ્તુ ખરીદાવી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aだましてdamashitetricking/deceiving (into a specific false transaction)धोखा देकरછેતરીને
b裏切ってuragittebetraying (breaking an existing bond of trust, not a one-off scam)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Tricking a customer into a fake purchase is だます ("to deceive/con"); 裏切る ("to betray") specifically means breaking an existing relationship of trust (a friend, an ally), not swindling a stranger in a transaction.

Q6

子「お父さん、変な虫がいるよ。（a. いじめて　b. やっつけて）。」父「こらこら、何も悪いことをしてないのに、かわいそうだよ。」Ko: "Otousan, hen na mushi ga iru yo. (a. Ijimete / b. Yattsukete)." Chichi: "Kora kora, nani mo warui koto o shitenai noni, kawaisou da yo."

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aいじめてijimetebully it (implies prolonged tormenting, not the child's blunt "deal with it")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's list is full of near-synonyms for "wronging" and "supporting" someone — exactly what Q1, Q5/Q6, and the "ダメ" flags test above. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
だますdamasu To deceive/con Tricking someone (often a stranger) in a one-off scheme. "He conned the customer into buying a fake."
裏切るuragiru To betray Breaking an existing bond of trust with someone close. "Even my best friend betrayed me."
いじめるijimeru To bully Repeated, drawn-out tormenting of a person. "He was bullied at school."
やっつけるyattsukeru To beat/get rid of One decisive act of finishing something off. "Get rid of that bug!"
頼る／頼りにするtayoru / tayori ni suru To depend/rely on Leaning on someone as a general source of support. "I rely on my parents."
任せるmakaseru To entrust Actively handing over a specific task's responsibility. "I'll leave the driving to you."
"ダメ" trap: 冗談する and 文句する are both marked with the book's "ダメ" (wrong usage) stamp — 冗談 and 文句 are nouns that must pair with 言う (冗談を言う, 文句を言う), never take する directly. Q4 above tests exactly this trap.
