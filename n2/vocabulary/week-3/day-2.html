JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 3 — いろいろ表現しましょう (Iroiro hyougen shimashou)

2日目 — どんな様子ですか？ (Donna yousu desu ka?)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.46–47
Theme
状態・様子を表す言葉、および顔色・様子の変化を表す「真っ〜」表現 (words describing states/circumstances, plus "makk-" color/emotion-change expressions)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed as "47ページの答え" in the box at the bottom of p.49 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: an old man dozes in a chair; his thought bubble recalls a poor, deprived childhood of plain meals and a miserable life, contrasted with his ordinary but comfortable life now — "I'm a lucky man!"
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

An old man naps in an armchair; in his thought bubble, his younger self eats a meager meal.

Old man (thinking): 子どもの頃は貧しくて、粗末な食事しかできないみじめな暮らしだった。今はありふれた生活だが、快適な暮らしをしている。幸せなことだ！Kodomo no koro wa mazushikute, somatsu na shokuji shika dekinai mijime na kurashi datta. Ima wa arifureta seikatsu da ga, kaiteki na kurashi o shite iru. Shiawase na koto da!
("As a child I was poor, living a miserable life where I could only afford plain meals. Now I live an ordinary life, but a comfortable one. What a lucky man I am!")

豊かな国 (yutaka na kuni) — "a wealthy country" ⇔ 貧しい (mazushii) — "poor," setting up today's words for describing conditions and circumstances.

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.47) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.47). Answers confirmed from the "47ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.49.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

子どものころの記憶が（a. 豊か　b. あいまい）になっている。Kodomo no koro no kioku ga (a. yutaka / b. aimai) ni natte iru.

Full sentence: 子どものころの記憶があいまいになっている。Kodomo no koro no kioku ga aimai ni natte iru.
EN: My memories of childhood have become vague.
HI: बचपन की यादें धुंधली हो गई हैं।
GU: બાળપણની યાદો ઝાંખી થઈ ગઈ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a豊かyutakawealthy, abundant (doesn't describe fading memory)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bあいまいaimaivagueधुंधला, अस्पष्टઝાંખું, અસ્પષ્ટ

Why: あいまいになる ("to become vague") fits fading childhood memories; 豊か describes abundance/wealth, unrelated to memory clarity.

Q2

恥ずかしくて顔が（a. 真っ青　b. 真っ赤）になった。Hazukashikute kao ga (a. massao / b. makka) ni natta.

Full sentence: 恥ずかしくて顔が真っ赤になった。Hazukashikute kao ga makka ni natta.
EN: I was so embarrassed my face turned bright red.
HI: शर्म के मारे चेहरा बिल्कुल लाल हो गया।
GU: શરમના માર્યા ચહેરો સાવ લાલ થઈ ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a真っ青massaodeathly pale (fits fear/shock, not embarrassment)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b真っ赤makkabright red, flushedबिल्कुल लालસાવ લાલ

Why: 顔が真っ赤になる ("face turns bright red") is the fixed expression for a flushed, embarrassed face; 真っ青 describes a face turning pale from fear or shock, a different emotion.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati

GU: શિયાળાના ડિસેમ્બરમાં ચેરી બ્લોસમ ખીલવું ખૂબ નવાઈની વાત છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあわただしいawatadashiibusy, hectic (about a pace of activity, not rarity)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for difficulty/trouble, and on which "真っ〜" color-change word fits which emotion or situation — exactly what Q3/Q5/Q6 and Q2/Q7 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
やっかいなyakkai na Troublesome (a task/problem) Describes something fiddly or bothersome to deal with. "This is a troublesome problem to solve."
荒っぽいarappoi Rough, coarse (manner/language) Describes a person's rough way of speaking or acting, not task difficulty. "He uses rough language."
みじめなmijime na Miserable (an inner feeling) Describes how someone feels inside. "I feel miserable after failing."
みにくいminikui Ugly (appearance/conduct) Describes how something looks or how conduct appears to others. "An ugly, public dispute."
顔が真っ赤になるkao ga makka ni naru Face turns bright red Embarrassment or anger — a "hot" emotion. "She blushed bright red from embarrassment."
顔が真っ青になるkao ga massao ni naru Face turns deathly pale Fear or shock — a "cold," draining emotion. "His face went pale with fear."
頭の中が真っ白になるatama no naka ga masshiro ni naru Mind goes completely blank Freezing up mentally under pressure/nerves. "I froze up and forgot everything mid-speech."
Double meaning: やかましい covers both literal loudness (スピーカーの音がやかましい, "the speaker is too loud") and being fussy/particular about something (彼はファッションにやかましい, "he's picky about fashion") — the same word for "noisy" can describe a nitpicky attitude, not just volume.
