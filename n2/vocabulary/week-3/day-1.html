JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 3 — いろいろ表現しましょう (Iroiro hyougen shimashou)

1日目 — どんな人ですか？ (Donna hito desu ka?)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.44–45
Theme
性格・人柄を表す言葉 (words describing personality and character)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed as "45ページの答え" in the box at the bottom of p.47 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: two women separately think about their boyfriends — one describes a serious, considerate man whose looks she isn't keen on; the other describes a good-looking man who is selfish, proud, and short-tempered. The book notes that in phrases like 思いやりが／の ある, が can be swapped for の with no change in meaning.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

Two women, each picturing her boyfriend in a thought bubble, compare notes on what he's like.

Woman A (thinking): 私の彼は、まじめで、思いやりがあって、センスもマナーもいいんだけど、顔があんまりね……Watashi no kare wa, majime de, omoiyari ga atte, sensu mo manaa mo iindakedo, kao ga anmari ne...
("My boyfriend is serious and considerate, and has both good taste and good manners, but his face isn't up to much...")

Woman B (thinking): 私の彼は、かっこいいんだけど、わがままで、プライドが高くて、気が短くて……Watashi no kare wa, kakkoii n da kedo, wagamama de, puraido ga takakute, ki ga mijikakute...
("My boyfriend is good-looking, but he's selfish, has a lot of pride, and is short-tempered...")

田中さんはどんな人ですか。——［　　］人です。(Tanaka-san wa donna hito desu ka. — [___] hito desu.) — "What kind of person is Tanaka? — He/she is a [___] person." Note: 「が」→「の」でも OK — 思いやりがある人 and 思いやりのある人 are both fine.

Q1

母はいつも同じことを言って（a. あつかましい　b. くどい）。Haha wa itsumo onaji koto o itte (a. atsukamashii / b. kudoi).

Full sentence: 母はいつも同じことを言ってくどい。Haha wa itsumo onaji koto o itte kudoi.
EN: My mother always says the same thing — she's so repetitive.
HI: मेरी माँ हमेशा वही बात कहती है, बहुत उबाऊ है।
GU: મારી માતા હંમેશા એ જ વાત કહે છે, ખૂબ કંટાળાજનક છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあつかましいatsukamashiiimpudent, shameless (about brazen behavior, not repetition)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bくどいkudoilong-winded, repetitiveउबाऊ, दोहराने वालाકંટાળાજનક, દોહરાવનારું

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for repetitiveness, cheekiness, and forcefulness, plus fixed adjective+noun collocations — exactly what Q1/Q2/Q6 and Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
くどいkudoi Long-winded, repetitive About saying/explaining the same thing over and over. "She always says the same thing — so repetitive."
あつかましいatsukamashii Impudent, shameless About brazen behavior/demands, not repetition. "He shamelessly asked for more."
生意気なnamaiki na Impertinent, cheeky Speaking/acting above one's place, especially from someone younger/junior. "Don't talk back cheekily — you're just a kid."
無邪気なmujaki na Childlike, innocent A positive, guileless innocence — the opposite feel from 生意気な. "A child's innocent question."
強引にgouin ni Forcefully, pushily Describes pressuring someone into an action against their will. "He forcefully made me buy it."
下品にgehin ni Vulgarly, crudely About coarse manners or taste, not pressure tactics. "He laughed vulgarly."
プライドが高いpuraido ga takai To have high pride Describes a person's self-regard; pairs fixedly with 高い, not いい. "He's very proud/full of himself."
センスがいいsensu ga ii Tasteful, good sense Describes taste/judgment quality; pairs fixedly with いい, not 高い. "Her idea/outfit shows good taste."
Fixed-collocation trap: プライド and センス each lock onto one partner adjective — プライドが高い ("high pride") and センスがいい ("good taste/sense") — swapping them (プライドがいい, センスが高い) is unnatural even though 高い and いい can both mean roughly "high/good" elsewhere.
