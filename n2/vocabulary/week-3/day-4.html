JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 3 — いろいろ表現しましょう (Iroiro hyougen shimashou)

4日目 — どんな気持ち・態度ですか？① (Donna kimochi / taido desu ka? ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.50–51
Theme
くやしい・仕方がない・納得がいかないなど、結果に対する気持ちを表す言葉 (words for how you feel about an outcome — frustration, resignation, disbelief) と、その他の感情表現 (other feeling words)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "51ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.53 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a diary-style monologue — an employee who admired this company but is constantly scolded by his section chief. Opens with a "□にいれてよみましょう" (fill in the blank) pattern box built around 結果 (result), then a second "もっとおぼえましょう" table of feeling verbs/adjectives.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

The illustration shows a man bowing his head while his angry section chief scolds him — a diary-style monologue about the frustration of working under a boss you can't agree with.

この会社にあこがれて入ったけれど、毎日、課長に納得がいかないことを言われる。Kono kaisha ni akogarete haitta keredo, mainichi, kachou ni nattoku ga ikanai koto o iwareru.
("I joined this company because I admired it, but every day my section chief says things I can't accept.")

がまんするのはくやしいけど、上司だから仕方がないと思う。Gaman suru no wa kuyashii kedo, joushi dakara shikata ga nai to omou.
("It's frustrating to put up with, but I think it can't be helped since he's my superior.")

仕方がない(shikata ga nai) — resign yourself to what a superior says, even when it's hard to accept

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

「神に（a. ちかって　b. いのって）、うそはついていません。」"Kami ni (a. chikatte / b. inotte), uso wa tsuite imasen."

Full sentence: 「神にちかって、うそはついていません。」"Kami ni chikatte, uso wa tsuite imasen."
EN: "I swear to God, I'm not lying."
HI: "मैं ईश्वर की क़सम खाकर कहता/कहती हूँ, मैं झूठ नहीं बोल रहा/रही।"
GU: "હું ઈશ્વરના સોગંદ ખાઈને કહું છું, હું જૂઠું નથી બોલી રહ્યો/રહી."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aちかってchikatteswearing (an oath)क़सम खाकरસોગંદ ખાઈને
bいのってinottepraying (asking for something, not backing a claim)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 神にちかう ("to swear by God") is the fixed way to back up a claim of honesty; 神にいのる ("to pray to God") asks for something instead — it doesn't support "I'm not lying."

Q2

大阪まで車で行くか、電車で行くか、（a. ためらう　b. なやむ）。Oosaka made kuruma de iku ka, densha de iku ka, (a. tamerau / b. nayamu).

Full sentence: 大阪まで車で行くか、電車で行くか、なやむ。Oosaka made kuruma de iku ka, densha de iku ka, nayamu.
EN: I'm agonizing over whether to go to Osaka by car or by train.
HI: ओसाका कार से जाऊँ या ट्रेन से, यह सोचकर उलझन में हूँ।
GU: ઓસાકા કારથી જવું કે ટ્રેનથી, એ વિચારીને મૂંઝવણમાં છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aためらうtamerauhesitating (holding back from acting)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bなやむnayamuagonizing/being troubled over a decisionउलझन में होनाમૂંઝવણમાં હોવું

Why: Weighing two concrete options against each other (car vs. train) is なやむ, "to agonize/be troubled over a choice"; ためらう describes hesitating right before a single action, not deliberating between two alternatives.

Q3

もうすぐ受験だけれど、気持ちが（a. あせって　b. あわてて）勉強できない。Mou sugu juken dakeredo, kimochi ga (a. asette / b. awatete) benkyou dekinai.

Full sentence: もうすぐ受験だけれど、気持ちがあせって勉強できない。Mou sugu juken dakeredo, kimochi ga asette benkyou dekinai.
EN: My exam is coming up soon, but I feel so anxious/rushed that I can't study.
HI: परीक्षा नज़दीक है, लेकिन मन इतना बेचैन है कि पढ़ाई नहीं हो पा रही।
GU: પરીક્ષા નજીક છે, પણ મન એટલું અધીરું છે કે અભ્યાસ થઈ શકતો નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあせってasettefeeling impatient/anxious (an inner state)बेचैन होकरઅધીરા થઈને
bあわててawatetepanicking/flustered (usually a visible, sudden reaction)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 気持ちがあせる describes the ongoing inner pressure of "time is running out" that blocks concentration; あわてる is a sudden, visible fluster reacting to a single event, not a sustained state of mind.

Q4

こんなに働いているのに給料が減らされるのは、（a. みっともない　b. 納得がいかない）。Konna ni hataraite iru noni kyuuryou ga herasareru no wa, (a. mittomonai / b. nattoku ga ikanai).

Full sentence: こんなに働いているのに給料が減らされるのは、納得がいかない。Konna ni hataraite iru noni kyuuryou ga herasareru no wa, nattoku ga ikanai.
EN: Working this hard and still having my pay cut — I can't accept it.
HI: इतनी मेहनत करने के बावजूद तनख़्वाह कम कर दी जाए, यह बात समझ में नहीं आती।
GU: આટલી મહેનત કરવા છતાં પગાર ઘટાડી દેવાય, એ વાત સમજાતી નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aみっともないmittomonaishameful/unbecoming (about appearance/behavior, not a policy)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b納得がいかないnattoku ga ikanaiunable to acceptसमझ में नहीं आताસમજાતું નથી

Why: An unfair pay cut is something you can't intellectually/emotionally accept — 納得がいかない; みっともない judges appearance or behavior as shameful, not the fairness of a decision.

Q5

好きなものでも、毎日食べていると（a. あきる　b. あきれる）。Suki na mono demo, mainichi tabete iru to (a. akiru / b. akireru).

Full sentence: 好きなものでも、毎日食べているとあきる。Suki na mono demo, mainichi tabete iru to akiru.
EN: Even something you like, if you eat it every day, you get tired of it.
HI: पसंदीदा चीज़ भी अगर रोज़ खाई जाए तो उससे ऊब हो जाती है।
GU: ગમતી વસ્તુ પણ રોજ ખાવાથી કંટાળો આવી જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aあきるakiruto get bored/tired of (through repetition)ऊब जानाકંટાળી જવું
bあきれるakireruto be dumbfounded (at someone's shocking behavior, not food)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Losing interest in food through repetition is あきる ("to get tired of"); あきれる describes being appalled/flabbergasted at a person's shocking words or actions, not fading interest in a taste.

Q6

毎日遅刻する彼が会社を辞めさせられたのは、（a. やむを得ない　b. おしい）ことだ。Mainichi chikoku suru kare ga kaisha o yamesaserareta no wa, (a. yamu o enai / b. oshii) koto da.

Full sentence: 毎日遅刻する彼が会社を辞めさせられたのは、やむを得ないことだ。Mainichi chikoku suru kare ga kaisha o yamesaserareta no wa, yamu o enai koto da.
EN: It couldn't be helped that he, who's late every day, was forced to leave the company.
HI: जो हर दिन देर से आता था, उसे कंपनी छोड़नी पड़ी — यह तो लाचारी की बात थी।
GU: રોજ મોડો આવતો હતો એ, કંપની છોડવી પડી — એ તો લાચારીની વાત હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aやむを得ないyamu o enaiunavoidable, can't be helpedलाचारी हैલાચારી છે
bおしいoshiia shame to lose (fits losing someone valuable, not someone chronically late)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Firing an employee who is habitually late is やむを得ない, "an unavoidable/justified outcome"; おしい expresses regret over losing something worth keeping, the opposite feeling from justifying a firing.

Q7

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's near-synonyms cluster around "giving up on accepting something" and "reacting under pressure" — exactly what Q2/Q3, Q4, and Q5 test above. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
あきるakiru To get bored/tired of Losing interest through repetition of something originally liked. "I got tired of eating the same food every day."
あきれるakireru To be dumbfounded A negative shock at someone else's outrageous words/actions. "I was appalled at his excuse."
あせるaseru To feel rushed/impatient An internal, sustained state of pressure (often about time). "I feel anxious because the exam is close."
あわてるawateru To panic/fluster A sudden, visible reaction to one triggering event. "I panicked when the phone rang."
仕方（が）ない／しょうがないshikata (ga) nai / shouganai It can't be helped Everyday, casual register. Speaking with friends/family.
やむを得ないyamu o enai There's nothing that can be done The same idea, in formal/written register. Reports, news, formal writing.
納得がいかないnattoku ga ikanai Unable to accept An emotional/moral refusal to accept something as fair. "I can't accept this pay cut."
不思議だfushigi da It's strange/mysterious Bewilderment with no judgment attached — just puzzling. "It's mysterious that the light turned on by itself."
Usage caution: 申し訳ない is almost always used about your own conduct toward someone else (an apology register, as in 申し訳ないことをした); it does not describe how you feel about an external situation the way 残念だ or 情けない can.
