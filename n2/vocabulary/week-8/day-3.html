JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 8 — まとめて覚えましょう③ (Matomete oboemashou ③)

3日目 — よく使われる表現① (Yoku tsukawareru hyougen ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.128–129
Theme
体の名称を使った表現① — 目・耳・口・顔・頭 (idiomatic expressions built on body-part names: eye, ear, mouth, face, head)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "129ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.131 — confirmed, not guessed. (p.131 is Day 4's second page, outside this build's scope, but the answer box itself was fetched and checked.)
Notes
Opens a new 3-day sub-unit, 「よく使われる表現」("frequently used expressions"), distinct from Days 1–2's compound-verb "組み合わせの言葉". Instead of one flat word list, entries are grouped under five body-part headers exactly as the book presents them (目/め, 耳/みみ, 口/くち on p.128; 顔/かお, 頭/あたま on p.129) — preserved here as five separate sub-tables rather than flattened into one.
(＝...) = 言い換え (a same-meaning paraphrase given in-book)
Scene

Two friends talk; one teases the other about getting caught texting in class, and the other's reply itself uses one of today's ear idioms.

Friend A: 授業中に先生の目を盗んで携帯のメールをして見つかったんだって？Jugyou-chuu ni sensei no me o nusunde keitai no meeru o shite mitsukattandatte?
("I heard you got caught texting on your phone behind the teacher's back during class?")

Friend B: 耳が早いね。そうだよ、すごくしかられて、ひどい目にあったよ。Mimi ga hayai ne. Sou da yo, sugoku shikararete, hidoi me ni atta yo.
("Word travels fast to you, huh. Yeah, I got scolded badly — it was a terrible experience.")

Three of today's idioms appear in this exchange: 目を盗む (do something behind someone's back), 耳が早い (to hear news quickly), ひどい目にあう (to have a terrible experience).

1 Vocabulary List

おぼえましょう — 体の名称を使った表現① (karada no meishou o tsukatta hyougen ①) — grouped by body part exactly as printed.

目 (me — eye)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujaratiNote
彼の日本語は目に見えて上達している。Kare no nihongo wa me ni miete joutatsu shite iru.His Japanese is improving very fast/visibly.उसकी जापानी भाषा तेज़ी से सुधर रही है।તેની જાપાનીઝ ભાષા ઝડપથી સુધરી રહી છે.
環境問題に目を向けよう。Kankyou mondai ni me o mukeyou.Let's pay more attention to environmental problems.चलो पर्यावरण की समस्याओं पर ध्यान दें।ચાલો પર્યાવરણની સમસ્યાઓ પર ધ્યાન આપીએ.
歩き始めの幼児は目が離せない。Aruki hajime no youji wa me ga hanasenai.You must keep an eye on toddlers who've just started walking.चलना सीख रहे बच्चे से नज़र नहीं हटानी चाहिए।ચાલવાનું શરૂ કરેલા બાળક પર નજર ન હટાવવી.
目に付くところに置く(＝見える)。Me ni tsuku tokoro ni oku (=mieru).Put ... in a place where people will easily notice it.ऐसी जगह रखना जहाँ आसानी से नज़र आए।એવી જગ્યાએ મૂકવું જ્યાં સહેલાઈથી નજરે પડે.
甘いものに目がない。Amai mono ni me ga nai.Has a sweet tooth.मीठे का बहुत शौक़ीन होना।મીઠાઈનો ખૂબ શોખીન હોવું.
目が回るように忙しい。Me ga mawaru you ni isogashii.Extremely busy.बेहद व्यस्त (सिर घूमने जैसा)।અત્યંત વ્યસ્ત (માથું ફરે એવું).
母の姿が目に浮かぶ。Haha no sugata ga me ni ukabu.My mother's image comes to mind.माँ की छवि आँखों के सामने उभर आना।માતાની છબી આંખો સામે તરવરવી.
学生のレポートに目を通す。Gakusei no repooto ni me o toosu.Go through the students' papers.छात्रों की रिपोर्ट पर सरसरी नज़र डालना।વિદ્યાર્થીઓના રિપોર્ટ પર નજર ફેરવવી.
親の目を盗んで遊びに行く(＝隠れて)。Oya no me o nusunde asobi ni iku (=kakurete).Go and have a good time without your parents knowing.माता-पिता की नज़रों से बचकर खेलने जाना।માતાપિતાની નજર ચૂકવીને રમવા જવું.
ひどい目にあう。Hidoi me ni au.Have a terrible experience.बहुत बुरा अनुभव होना।ખૂબ ખરાબ અનુભવ થવો.⊙大変な目にあうtaihen na me ni au — a tough experience; 危険な目にあうkiken na me ni au — a dangerous experience

耳 (mimi — ear)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujaratiNote
彼が結婚するといううわさを耳にした。Kare ga kekkon suru to iu uwasa o mimi ni shita.I have gathered/heard that he is getting married.सुना है कि उसकी शादी हो रही है।સાંભળ્યું છે કે તેના લગ્ન થઈ રહ્યા છે.
欠点を言われて耳が痛い。Ketten o iwarete mimi ga itai.It hurts me when I am reminded of my shortcomings.कमियाँ सुनकर बुरा लगना।ખામીઓ સાંભળીને ખરાબ લાગવું.
祖母は耳が遠い。(＝耳がよく聞こえない)Sobo wa mimi ga tooi. (=mimi ga yoku kikoenai)My grandmother is hard of hearing.दादी/नानी को कम सुनाई देता है।દાદી/નાનીને ઓછું સંભળાય છે.
ちょっと耳を貸して。(＝聞いて)Chotto mimi o kashite. (=kiite)Let me tell you this! (lit. "lend me your ear")ज़रा सुनो तो।જરા સાંભળો તો.
そんなことを知っているとは、耳が早いね。Sonna koto o shitte iru to wa, mimi ga hayai ne.You are on top of things if you already knew this!यह पहले से जानते हो, तुम तक ख़बर जल्दी पहुँचती है!આ પહેલેથી ખબર છે, તને સમાચાર બહુ ઝડપથી મળે છે!
事件のことを聞いて、耳を疑った。Jiken no koto o kiite, mimi o utagatta.I couldn't believe it when I heard about the crime.घटना सुनकर अपने कानों पर यक़ीन नहीं हुआ।ઘટના સાંભળીને પોતાના કાન પર વિશ્વાસ ન થયો.

口 (kuchi — mouth)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujaratiNote
セールスマンは口がうまいので気をつけよう。Seerusuman wa kuchi ga umai node ki o tsukeyou.Watch out for salesmen who are very good at flattery/smooth talk.सेल्समैन मीठी-मीठी बातें करते हैं, सावधान रहें।સેલ્સમેન મીઠી મીઠી વાતો કરે છે, સાવધાન રહો.
彼は口がかたいから信用できる。Kare wa kuchi ga katai kara shinyou dekiru.I can trust him to keep a secret.वह राज़ रखने वाला है, उस पर भरोसा किया जा सकता है।તે રહસ્ય રાખનારો છે, તેના પર ભરોસો કરી શકાય.
彼は口が悪いけれど、やさしい。Kare wa kuchi ga warui keredo, yasashii.He has a sharp tongue but is actually kind.उसकी ज़बान तीखी है, पर दिल अच्छा है।તેની જીભ તીખી છે, પણ દિલ સારું છે.
これは私の口に合わない。(＝好きな味じゃない)Kore wa watashi no kuchi ni awanai. (=suki na aji ja nai)This is not to my taste.यह मेरे स्वाद के अनुसार नहीं है।આ મારા સ્વાદને અનુરૂપ નથી.
口にする(＝食べる／話をする)。Kuchi ni suru (=taberu / hanashi o suru).Eat / talk about.खाना / किसी विषय पर बात करना।ખાવું / કોઈ વિષય પર વાત કરવી.
そんなことを口に出すべきではない。Sonna koto o kuchi ni dasu beki de wa nai.You should not talk about such things.ऐसी बातें ज़ुबान पर नहीं लानी चाहिए।આવી વાતો બોલવી ન જોઈએ.

顔 (kao — face)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujaratiNote
ぼくはその店では顔がきく。Boku wa sono mise dewa kao ga kiku.I have a lot of influence in the shop.उस दुकान में मेरा अच्छा प्रभाव/पहचान है।તે દુકાનમાં મારો સારો પ્રભાવ/ઓળખાણ છે.
彼は顔が広いから、聞いてみよう。Kare wa kao ga hiroi kara, kiite miyou.He has a lot of contacts so I will ask him if he could help.उसकी जान-पहचान बहुत है, उससे पूछते हैं।તેની ઓળખાણ ઘણી છે, તેને પૂછી જોઈએ.
ちょっと顔を貸して。Chotto kao o kashite.Can't you come even for a short time?थोड़ी देर के लिए साथ चलो।થોડી વાર માટે સાથે આવો.

頭 (atama — head)

JapaneseEnglishHindiGujaratiNote
学生の態度に頭に来た(＝怒っている)。Gakusei no taido ni atama ni kita. (=okotte iru)I got mad at the student's attitude.छात्र के रवैये से गुस्सा आ गया।વિદ્યાર્થીના વર્તનથી ગુસ્સો આવ્યો.＝腹が立ったhara ga tatta — same meaning, "got angry"

Q1

この料理はちょっと（a. 口がうまくない　b. 口に合いません）。Kono ryouri wa chotto (a. kuchi ga umakunai / b. kuchi ni aimasen).

Full sentence: この料理はちょっと口に合いません。Kono ryouri wa chotto kuchi ni aimasen.
EN: This dish doesn't quite suit my taste.
HI: यह व्यंजन मेरे स्वाद के अनुसार नहीं है।
GU: આ વાનગી મારા સ્વાદને અનુરૂપ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a口がうまくないkuchi ga umakunainot a smooth talker (about a person's speech, not food)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b口に合いませんkuchi ni aimasendoesn't suit my tasteस्वाद के अनुसार नहींસ્વાદને અનુરૂપ નથી

Why: 口に合わない ("doesn't suit one's taste") is the fixed idiom for food not matching one's preference; 口がうまい/うまくない is about a person's persuasive speech, an unrelated idiom that happens to share the word 口.

Q2

この曲はだれもが一度は（a. 耳にした　b. 耳を疑った）ことがあると思う。Kono kyoku wa dare mo ga ichido wa (a. mimi ni shita / b. mimi o utagatta) koto ga aru to omou.

Full sentence: この曲はだれもが一度は耳にしたことがあると思う。Kono kyoku wa dare mo ga ichido wa mimi ni shita koto ga aru to omou.
EN: I think everyone has heard this song at least once.
HI: मुझे लगता है कि हर किसी ने यह गाना कम से कम एक बार सुना होगा।
GU: મને લાગે છે કે દરેકે આ ગીત ઓછામાં ઓછું એક વાર સાંભળ્યું હશે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a耳にしたmimi ni shitaheard (happened to hear)सुनाસાંભળ્યું
b耳を疑ったmimi o utagattacouldn't believe one's ears (shock at hearing something, not a plain "heard")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q3

今日の飲み会には、ちょっとだけ顔を（a. 貸す　b. 出す）予定です。Kyou no nomikai ni wa, chotto dake kao o (a. kasu / b. dasu) yotei desu.

Q6

彼女は男性を見る（a. 目がない　b. 目にあう）。Kanojo wa dansei o miru (a. me ga nai / b. me ni au).

Full sentence: 彼女は男性を見る目がない。Kanojo wa dansei o miru me ga nai.
EN: She has no eye for men (she's a poor judge of men).
HI: उसे पुरुषों को परखने की समझ नहीं है।
GU: તેને પુરુષોને પારખવાની સમજ નથી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a目がないme ga nailacks a discerning eye (completing the fixed compound 見る目, "an eye for judging")परखने की समझ न होनाપારખવાની સમજ ન હોવી

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day packs several near-synonymous "ear" idioms and two "face/social standing" idioms into a small space — exactly what the exercises above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
耳にするmimi ni suru To hear/happen to hear (a rumor, news) A neutral, passive catching-wind of something. "I heard a rumor he's getting married."
耳を疑うmimi o utagau To not believe one's ears Shock/disbelief at hearing something. "I couldn't believe it when I heard about the crime."
耳が痛いmimi ga itai It stings to hear (criticism) Discomfort at hearing about one's own flaws. "It hurts to be reminded of my shortcomings."
耳が遠いmimi ga tooi To be hard of hearing A literal, physical hearing impairment. "My grandmother is hard of hearing."
顔がきくkao ga kiku To have influence/pull (somewhere) People do you favors — real leverage, not just acquaintance. "I have a lot of influence at that shop."
顔が広いkao ga hiroi To have a wide circle of contacts Simply knowing many people, without implying leverage. "He knows a lot of people, so let's ask him."
Same-ending, different idiom trap: 目がない shows up twice with unrelated meanings — after a plain noun+に it means "to have a weakness for/love something uncontrollably" (甘いものに目がない, "has a sweet tooth"), but in 見る目がない it instead negates the separate compound noun 見る目 ("an eye for judging"), giving "to lack good judgment (about ___)." Don't assume every 目がない is the "weakness for" idiom — check whether it's attached to a plain noun or to a "verb + 目" compound first.
