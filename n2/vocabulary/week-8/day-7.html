Q1

彼にじっと見（　）と、どきどきして話せなくなる。Kare ni jitto mi (___) to, dokidoki shite hanasenaku naru.

Full sentence: 彼にじっと見つめられると、どきどきして話せなくなる。Kare ni jitto mitsumerareru to, dokidoki shite hanasenaku naru.
EN: When he stares at me intently, my heart races and I can't speak.
HI: जब वह मुझे टकटकी लगाकर देखता है, तो दिल धड़कने लगता है और बोल नहीं पाती/पाता।
GU: જ્યારે તે મારી સામે એકીટશે જુએ છે, ત્યારે ધબકારા વધી જાય છે અને બોલી શકાતું નથી.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1かけられるkakerarerufrom 見かける, "to happen to see" (not "stare fixedly")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2なれられるnarerarerufrom 見なれる, "to get used to seeing" (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3つめられるtsumerarerufrom 見つめる, "to gaze/stare fixedly at"टकटकी लगाकर देखनाએકીટશે જોવું
4なおされるnaosarerufrom 見なおす, "to reassess/take another look" (unrelated)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 見つめる ("to gaze/stare fixedly") is exactly the intense, unbroken kind of looking (じっと見る) that would make someone's heart race and leave them unable to speak; the other 見〜 compounds mean "to happen to see," "to get used to seeing," and "to reassess" — none convey a fixed stare.

Q2

残念ながら、当店ではその商品は取り（　）おりません。Zannen nagara, touten de wa sono shouhin wa tori (___) orimasen.

Full sentence: 残念ながら、当店ではその商品は取り扱っておりません。Zannen nagara, touten de wa sono shouhin wa toriatsukatte orimasen.
EN: Unfortunately, we do not carry that product at this store.
HI: अफ़सोस, हमारी दुकान में वह उत्पाद नहीं रखा जाता।
GU: દુઃખની વાત છે કે અમારી દુકાનમાં એ ઉત્પાદન રાખવામાં આવતું નથી.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1上げてagetefrom 取り上げる, "to take up/confiscate" (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2付けてtsuketefrom 取り付ける, "to install" (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
3組んでkundefrom 取り組む, "to tackle/engage with" (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
4扱ってatsukattefrom 取り扱う, "to carry/deal in (a product)"रखना/बेचनाરાખવું/વેચવું

Why: 取り扱う ("to handle/carry" a product) is the standard verb for a shop's product lineup; 取り上げる, 取り付ける, and 取り組む don't fit describing a store's inventory.

Q3

Why: 光を取り入れる ("to bring in/let in light") is the fixed collocation for architectural light; the other compounds mean "to accept/welcome" (for people/ideas) or aren't standard words, and don't fit physical light.

Q5

Full sentence: ドライブに出かけたが、忘れ物をして途中で家に引き返した。Doraibu ni dekaketa ga, wasuremono o shite tochuu de ie ni hikikaeshita.

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1取りtorifrom 取り返す, "to get back/recover (an object)" (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2追いoifrom 追い返す, "to send someone away/turn them back" (wrong meaning)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q21

腕がいいude ga ii

#JapaneseEnglish
1最近年のせいか右の腕がいい。Saikin toshi no sei ka migi no ude ga ii."Maybe due to age, my right arm is [ude ga ii] lately" — 腕がいい describes skill, not a physical arm's condition
2長袖のシャツはちょっと腕がいいですね。Nagasode no shatsu wa chotto ude ga ii desu ne."A long-sleeve shirt is a bit [ude ga ii]" — doesn't fit a piece of clothing
3腕がいい生活は長続きしない。Ude ga ii seikatsu wa nagatsuzuki shinai."A [ude ga ii] life doesn't last long" — 腕がいい doesn't modify "a life"
4あそこの歯医者さん腕がいいと評判です。Asoko no haisha-san ude ga ii to hyouban desu.That dentist has a reputation for being very skilled.

Why: 腕がいい describes someone's professional skill (a skilled dentist/carpenter), exactly as Day 4's vocabulary and Scene establish; sentences 1–3 misapply it to a physical arm's condition, a piece of clothing, and "a life," none of which take 腕がいい.

Q22

目にあうme ni au

#JapaneseEnglish
1ほめられて、かっこいい目にあっている。Homerarete, kakkoii me ni atte iru."Being praised, I'm having a cool [me ni au]" — 目にあう pairs with bad experiences (大変な目／悪い目), not "cool"
2なまけて遊んでばかりいると、今に大変な目にあうよ。Namakete asonde bakari iru to, ima ni taihen na me ni au yo.If you keep slacking off and playing, you'll have a rough time of it soon.
3あの子のいたずらは本当に悪い目にあった。Ano ko no itazura wa hontou ni warui me ni atta."That child's mischief really [experienced] a bad eye" — grammatically backwards; the affected person, not the mischief, undergoes the experience
4食べたことがなかったのに、食べる目にあった。Tabeta koto ga nakatta noni, taberu me ni atta."Even though I'd never eaten it, I [experienced] an eating eye" — nonsensical combination

Why: 大変な目にあう ("to have a rough/hard time of it") is the standard idiom, where a person undergoes a bad experience — warning someone about the consequences of laziness is textbook usage; sentences 1, 3, and 4 misapply the idiom to a positive experience, an inanimate subject, and a nonsensical phrase.

Q23

割り引くwaribiku

#JapaneseEnglish
1洋服を割り引いてもらって、着やすくなった。Youfuku o waribiite moratte, kiyasuku natta."Getting clothes [discounted] made them easier to wear" — a discount doesn't affect wearability
2同じものを2つ買ったら割り引いてくれた。Onaji mono o futatsu kattara waribiite kureta.Buying two of the same item, they gave me a discount.
3大きな石を割り引いて運んだ。Ookina ishi o waribiite hakonda."I [discounted] a big rock and carried it" — nonsensical, should be 割って ("broke")
4わからない漢字を辞書で割り引いて調べた。Wakaranai kanji o jisho de waribiite shirabeta."I [discounted] an unknown kanji in the dictionary to look it up" — nonsensical

Why: 割り引く correctly means "to give a price discount," matching buying two of the same item and receiving a discount; sentences 1, 3, and 4 misuse it where "wearability," "breaking a rock," and "looking up a kanji" are meant, confusing 割り引く with 割る ("to break/split").

Q24

立て替えるtatekaeru

#JapaneseEnglish
1会社の体制を立て替えた。Kaisha no taisei o tatekaeta."I [tatekae]-ed the company's structure" — nonsensical, should be 立て直した ("restructured")
2銀行から給料を立て替えた。Ginkou kara kyuuryou o tatekaeta."I [tatekae]-ed my salary from the bank" — nonsensical; salary isn't "advanced" from a bank this way
3電車賃は私が立て替えて払っておきました。Densha-chin wa watashi ga tatekaete haratte okimashita.I covered the train fare for now and paid it (to be reimbursed later).
4玄関に人が立て替えています。Genkan ni hito ga tatekaete imasu."A person is [tatekae]-ing at the entrance" — nonsensical, should be 立っています ("standing")

Why: 立て替える specifically means "to pay a cost on someone else's behalf, to be reimbursed later" — covering someone's train fare is the textbook example; sentences 1, 2, and 4 confuse it with unrelated homophone-adjacent words (立て直す "to rebuild," and 立つ "to stand").

Q25

口が軽いkuchi ga karui

#JapaneseEnglish
1彼女は口が軽いので、ないしょ話はしないほうがいいよ。Kanojo wa kuchi ga karui node, naisho hanashi wa shinai hou ga ii yo.She can't keep a secret, so you'd better not tell her anything confidential.
2口が軽いので、おやつを食べた。Kuchi ga karui node, oyatsu o tabeta."Because I [kuchi ga karui], I ate a snack" — nonsensical cause-and-effect
3彼は口が軽く自分のことしか話さなかった。Kare wa kuchi ga karuku jibun no koto shika hanasanakatta."He [kuchi ga karui] and only talked about himself" — 口が軽い is about revealing others' secrets, not talking about oneself
4このお酒は飲んだ後の口が軽い。Kono osake wa nonda ato no kuchi ga karui."This alcohol's mouth is light after drinking" — nonsensical, confusing it with a taste description like 口当たりが軽い

Why: 口が軽い describes someone who can't keep a secret and blabs information to others — warning someone not to share confidential matters with such a person is the natural usage; sentences 2–4 misapply it to snacking, self-centered talk, and a drink's mouthfeel.
