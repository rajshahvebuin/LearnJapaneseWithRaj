1日目 — 組み合わせの言葉① (Kumiawase no kotoba ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.124–125
Theme
動詞の組み合わせ表現・複合動詞 (compound verbs formed by combining two verb stems)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "125ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.127 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This 3-day mini-unit (「組み合わせの言葉」, Days 1–2) departs from the usual dialogue-scene format: instead of a two-character scene, Day 1 opens with a single instruction-giving speech bubble whose verbs are themselves today's vocabulary. The word list runs as one continuous "おぼえましょう" table across both printed pages (the book does not split it into sub-groups here).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

A woman gives her husband a string of DIY instructions while he sits looking put-upon; each verb she uses is one of today's combination words.

Her: ねえ、その絵を取り外して、この棚を取り付けて。それから、これを組み立てて。あ、持ち上げるときに、壁にぶつけないようにしてね。Nee, sono e o torihazushite, kono tana o toritsukete. Sorekara, kore o kumitatete. A, mochiageru toki ni, kabe ni butsukenai you ni shite ne.
("Hey, take down that picture, and put up this shelf. Then, assemble this. Oh, and when you lift it, be careful not to bump it into the wall.")

Four combination verbs appear in one breath: 取り外す (take down), 取り付ける (put up/install), 組み立てる (assemble), 持ち上げる (lift) — all covered in today's list.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's word list is dense with near-synonymous "sales" verbs and two "receive/take charge of" verbs that the exercises above hinge on telling apart. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
売り出しuridashi A product's market launch/release A one-time event — the moment something goes on sale. "A new PC model is coming onto the market."
売れ行きureyuki How well something is currently selling An ongoing trend, good or bad, not a single event or figure. "CD sales have been poor lately."
売り上げuriage Sales revenue/turnover A concrete figure of money earned. "Sales reached 1,000,000 yen today."
売り切れるurikireru To sell out completely Describes stock running out, a state not a trend or figure. "Tickets are sold out."
受け取るuketoru To receive (a physical object) An item changing hands. "Receiving a parcel."
受け持つukemotsu To take charge of (a class/duty) A role or responsibility, not an object. "Teaching an advanced class."
Look-alike trap: 組み合わせる and 組み立てる both follow the "verb stem + auxiliary verb" combination pattern this whole unit is named for, but they differ in what happens to the pieces: 組み合わせる combines two separate things that remain distinguishable (visuals + music into a film), while 組み立てる assembles physical parts into a single finished object (furniture from its pieces).
