JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えましょう② (Matomete oboemashou ②)

4日目 — 言葉の前につく語 (Kotoba no mae ni tsuku go — Terms which precede other words)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.114–115
Theme
Prefixes attached to the front of words (negation: 不/無/非/未; repetition: 再; degree: 超/高; and more: 名/全/総/各/長, plus a "terms attached to people" set: 現/前/元/故/副)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "115ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.117 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This is a word-formation day, not a dialogue-scene day: it opens with a short illustrated exchange (a furniture shop scene) demonstrating several of today's prefixes in the wild, then presents prefix + example-word tables instead of the usual situational vocabulary lists.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

A furniture salesman shows a cabinet to a couple; the husband grumbles about the price while the wife pushes back.

Salesman: これなんかいかがですか？Kore nanka ikaga desu ka?
("How about this one?")

Husband: 低収入の我々には高くて買えないよ。だいたい、そんなもの不必要だと思うな。Teishuunyuu no wareware ni wa takakute kaenai yo. Daitai, sonna mono fuhitsuyou da to omou na.
("For us low-income folks, it's too expensive to buy. Honestly, I think something like that is unnecessary.")

Wife: でも、いいものを買うと長持ちするでしょう。Demo, ii mono o kau to nagamochi suru deshou.
("But if you buy a good one, it'll last a long time, won't it?")

低収入(teishuunyuu) "low income", 不必要(fuhitsuyou) "unnecessary", and 長持ち(nagamochi) "long-lasting" all use today's prefixes in a natural argument about whether to buy the furniture.

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.115) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.115). Answers confirmed from the "115ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.117.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

新しいソフトが（a. 総　b. 全）世界で同時発売された。Atarashii sofuto ga (a. sou / b. zen) sekai de doujihatsubai sareta.

Full sentence: 新しいソフトが全世界で同時発売された。Atarashii sofuto ga zensekai de doujihatsubai sareta.
EN: The new software was released worldwide at the same time.
HI: नया सॉफ़्टवेयर पूरी दुनिया में एक साथ जारी किया गया।
GU: નવું સોફ્ટવેર આખી દુનિયામાં એકસાથે રિલીઝ થયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a総soutotal/combined (pairs with countable totals like 総人数, not "world")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b全zenwhole/allपूरी/सारीઆખી/બધી

Why: 全世界 ("the whole world") is the fixed, natural word for global scope; 総〜 ("combined total") pairs with countable totals (総人数, 総収入), not with "world."

Q2

急病の社長のかわりに（a. 副　b. 故）社長が出席した。Kyuubyou no shachou no kawari ni (a. fuku / b. ko) shachou ga shusseki shita.

Full sentence: 急病の社長のかわりに副社長が出席した。Kyuubyou no shachou no kawari ni fukushachou ga shusseki shita.
EN: In place of the president who suddenly fell ill, the vice-president attended.
HI: अचानक बीमार पड़े अध्यक्ष के बदले उपाध्यक्ष ने भाग लिया।
GU: અચાનક બીમાર પડેલા પ્રમુખને બદલે ઉપપ્રમુખે હાજરી આપી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a副fukuvice-/deputyउप-ઉપ-
b故kothe late (deceased) — would mean the president died, contradicting "suddenly fell ill"(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 副社長 ("vice-president") is exactly the person who fills in for an ill president; 故〜 ("the late ~") would mean the president had died, which contradicts 急病 ("a sudden illness" — implying they are alive but unwell).

Q3

Full sentence: その事件はいまだに未解決です。Sono jiken wa imada ni mikaiketsu desu.
EN: That incident is still unsolved.
HI: वह घटना अब भी अनसुलझी है।
GU: તે ઘટના હજુ પણ વણઉકેલાયેલી છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a無muwithout (無解決 isn't a real word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b未minot yetअभी तक नहींહજુ સુધી નહીં

Q4

ノックせずにドアを開けるのは（a. 非　b. 不）常識です。Nokku sezu ni doa o akeru no wa (a. hi / b. fu) joushiki desu.

Full sentence: ノックせずにドアを開けるのは非常識です。Nokku sezu ni doa o akeru no wa hijoushiki desu.
EN: Opening a door without knocking shows a lack of common sense.
HI: बिना दस्तक दिए दरवाज़ा खोलना सामान्य समझ की कमी दिखाता है।
GU: ટકોરા માર્યા વગર દરવાજો ખોલવો એ સામાન્ય સમજની ખામી બતાવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a非hilacking (in)की कमीની ખામી
b不funot (不常識 isn't the standard word — 常識 fixes with 非, not 不)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 常識 ("common sense") specifically pairs with 非〜 to form the fixed word 非常識 ("lacking common sense"); 不〜 doesn't attach to 常識 this way.

Q5

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a超chouextremelyबेहदઅત્યંત
b高kouhigh (高〜 attaches to measurable quantities like カロリー/収入/気圧, not to a state like "fullness")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's whole point is telling apart four different "not ~" prefixes (tested directly in Q3, Q4, Q6, and Q8) and four different ways to talk about a past/current office-holder (tested in Q2 and used throughout the "人につく言葉" table):

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
不〜fu~ Not ~ The plain, most common negator for Sino-Japanese nouns/na-adjectives. 不可能 "impossible", 不必要 "unnecessary"
無〜mu~ Without ~ / no ~ Stresses a total absence of a quality, rather than simple negation. 無関心 "indifferent", 無意識 "unconscious"
非〜hi~ Not ~ Locked to specific fixed compounds describing what something is not. 非常識 "lacking common sense", 非公式 "unofficial"
未〜mi~ Not yet ~ Marks a state that hasn't happened/finished yet, implying it still could. 未完成 "incomplete", 未解決 "unsolved (so far)"
現〜／前〜gen~ / zen~ Current / immediately preceding 現〜 is whoever holds the position now; 前〜 is specifically the one right before them. 現社長 "the (current) president", 前社長 "the (immediately) former president"
元〜／故〜moto~ / ko~ A past one (living) / the late (deceased) 元〜 is any past holder, not necessarily the last one, and they're alive; 故〜 specifically means the person has died. 元社長 "a former president (alive)", 故田中社長 "the late President Tanaka"
Usage trap: 前〜 and 元〜 both translate as "former/ex-", but they're not interchangeable: 前社長 can only refer to the person immediately preceding the current one, while 元社長 can refer to any past president — including one from decades ago, well before 前社長. Picking the wrong one implies the wrong number of people held the position in between.
