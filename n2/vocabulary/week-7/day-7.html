HI: मैं बेखयाली में नाखून चबा बैठता/बैठती हूँ।
GU: હું બેધ્યાનપણે નખ કરડી બેસું છું.
#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1不funot (不意識 isn't a real word)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

#JapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1発売hatsubaiputting on sale (a permit isn't "sold")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

ごとにgoto ni

#JapaneseEnglish
1一日ごとにお風呂に入ります。Ichinichi goto ni ofuro ni hairimasu."I take a bath [goto ni] a day" — grammatically fine but an unusual way to say "every day" next to the plainer 毎日
