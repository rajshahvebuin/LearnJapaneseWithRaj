JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えましょう② (Matomete oboemashou ②)

2日目 — 意味がたくさんある言葉② (Imi ga takusan aru kotoba ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.110–111
Theme
意味がたくさんある動詞 — かかる／かける・取れる／取る・伸びる／伸ばす (verbs with many meanings: "hang/depend/take out," "take/win/get," "grow/extend")
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "111ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.113 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Continuing 第7週's 意味がたくさんある言葉 theme. Each headword group below is shown sense-by-sense through the book's own example collocations, kept as separate rows rather than one flattened gloss. Scene: a man with a cold thinks he should take a sick day for caution's sake (大事を取って) but can't, because he needs to grow this month's sales (売り上げを伸ばす) or risk having to take responsibility (責任を取って) and quit.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A man wearing a face mask holds his head, thinking, while a thought bubble shows a rising sales graph.

He (thinking): 風邪を引いたみたいだ。本当は大事を取って仕事を休みたいけど、休めない。今月の売り上げを伸ばさないと大変だ。責任を取って、会社を辞めることになるかもしれないし……。Kaze o hiita mitai da. Hontou wa daiji o totte shigoto o yasumitai kedo, yasumenai. Kongetsu no uriage o nobasanai to taihen da. Sekinin o totte, kaisha o yameru koto ni naru kamoshirenai shi...
("I think I've caught a cold. Honestly I'd like to play it safe and take the day off work, but I can't. If I don't grow this month's sales, it'll be bad. I might end up taking responsibility and quitting the company...")

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's headwords each carry several senses across the intransitive/transitive pair — mixing them up, or mixing up which sense of 取る is meant, is the main trap in Q1–Q8 above. Compare:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
保険をかけるhoken o kakeru To take out insurance Fixed collocation — 保険 never pairs with 買う or 取る. "She insured her instrument."
責任を取るsekinin o toru To take responsibility (voluntarily) The subject actively accepts responsibility. "He resigned, taking responsibility."
税金を取られるzeikin o torareru To have tax imposed on you (passive) Something is taken from the subject by an outside force. "An extra tax is charged when you buy a house."
売り上げが伸びるuriage ga nobiru Sales go up (by themselves) Intransitive — describes the result, no explicit actor. "Sales have grown this quarter."
売り上げを伸ばすuriage o nobasu To grow/increase sales (deliberately) Transitive — a person or company actively drives the increase. "We need to grow sales this month."
場所を取るbasho o toru To take up space The bulky object itself is the grammatical subject of 取る. "This desk takes up a lot of room."
Fixed-phrase trap: 大事を取って入院する ("to be admitted to hospital as a precaution") uses 大事を取る in the sense of "playing it safe/erring on the side of caution" — a different, idiomatic sense of 取る from 責任を取る ("to take responsibility") or 記録を取る ("to keep a record"), even though all three use the same verb 取る.
