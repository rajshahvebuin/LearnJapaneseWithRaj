JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えましょう② (Matomete oboemashou ②)

5日目 — 言葉の後ろにつく語① (Kotoba no ushiro ni tsuku go ① — Terms which follow other words ①)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.116–117
Theme
Suffixes attached to the back of words: money-related (~金/~料/~費/~代/~賃), range (~内/~外), manner/impression (~的/~風/~感/~性/~製), and miscellaneous (~化/~目)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "117ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.119 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Word-formation day: opens with an illustrated interior monologue about money-related suffixes, then presents suffix + example-word tables grouped by the book's own category headers (お金/範囲/様子・印象/その他) instead of the usual situational vocabulary lists.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
Scene

A young woman sits at a desk with a calculator and scattered coins/bills, mentally tallying up her monthly costs (illustration shows floating yen figures around her).

Her: 交通費、高くつくなあ。電車賃はそうでもないけど、とにかくバス代が高い。Koutsuuhi, takaku tsuku naa. Densha-chin wa sou demo nai kedo, tonikaku basu-dai ga takai.
("Transportation costs really add up. Train fare isn't so bad, but bus fare is expensive no matter what.")

家賃も値上がりするっていうし、奨学金をもらわないと生活費がきついなあ。Yachin mo neagari suru tte iu shi, shougakukin o morawanai to seikatsuhi ga kitsui naa.
("They say rent is going up too, so if I don't get a scholarship, living expenses will be tight.")

交通費(koutsuuhi), 電車賃(denshachin), バス代(basudai), 家賃(yachin), 奨学金(shougakukin), and 生活費(seikatsuhi) pack five different money-suffixes (~費/~賃/~代/~金) into one budgeting monologue.

Q1

その事件は予想（a. 外　b. 内）の結果を迎え、だれもが驚いた。Sono jiken wa yosou (a. gai / b. nai) no kekka o mukae, dare mo ga odoroita.

Full sentence: その事件は予想外の結果を迎え、だれもが驚いた。Sono jiken wa yosougai no kekka o mukae, dare mo ga odoroita.
EN: That incident met with an unexpected result, surprising everyone.
HI: उस घटना का परिणाम अप्रत्याशित निकला, जिससे सब हैरान रह गए।
GU: તે ઘટનાનું પરિણામ અણધાર્યું આવ્યું, જેનાથી બધા આશ્ચર્યચકિત થયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a外gaioutside of, beyondके बाहरની બહાર
b内naiwithin (予想内 would mean "as expected," the opposite of "surprising everyone")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's five money-suffixes (~金/~料/~費/~代/~賃) all translate loosely as "cost," but each locks to its own set of nouns — exactly what trips up Q3–Q6 above:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
〜金~kin A named fee/fund A specific, often one-time sum with an official name. 入学金 "admission fee", 奨学金 "scholarship"
〜料~ryou A fee for a service Paid for the right to use/enter/receive something. 入場料 "entrance fee", 拝観料 "temple admission"
〜費~hi An expense category A broader, often recurring category of spending. 生活費 "living expenses", 医療費 "medical expenses"
〜代~dai A bill/charge for goods or utilities What you owe for a specific consumable or utility. 電気代 "electricity bill", 修理代 "repair bill"
〜賃~chin Fare or wage Payment for transportation or for someone's labor. 電車賃 "train fare", 手間賃 "labor charge"
Fixed-collocation trap: Only one suffix is "correct" for each noun even though several might seem to fit logically (医療費 not 医療金; 拝観料 not 拝観代; 修理代 not 修理料) — these are fixed vocabulary items to memorize individually, not a productive rule you can apply freely to any new noun.
