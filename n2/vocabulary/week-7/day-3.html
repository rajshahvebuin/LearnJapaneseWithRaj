JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えましょう② (Matomete oboemashou ②)

3日目 — 意味がたくさんある言葉③ (Imi ga takusan aru kotoba ③)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.112–113
Theme
意味がたくさんあるイ形容詞 — 軽い・暗い・高い・きつい (i-adjectives with many meanings: "light/minor," "dark/bleak/uninformed," "high/proud," "tight/severe/strong")
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "113ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.115 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Final day of 意味がたくさんある言葉, this time covering い-adjectives instead of verbs. The book itself supplies each adjective's antonym (⇔) or a paraphrase (＝) right inside the example phrase — those are reproduced in the Note column so each row's specific sense stays distinguishable, rather than collapsing "light/dark/high/tight" into single flat glosses. Scene: a politician's dark past comes to light on the news, making his future outlook bleak (暗くなった) — a man watching reacts with a gloomy face (暗い顔), saying the situation is tough (きついなあ).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

A TV screen shows a masked, shadowy politician figure; a man watching rubs his forehead with a gloomy expression.

Narration: 彼の暗い過去が明らかになり、政治家としての将来の見通しは暗くなった。Kare no kurai kako ga akiraka ni nari, seijika toshite no shourai no mitooshi wa kuraku natta.
("His dark past came to light, and his outlook for the future as a politician became bleak.")

Man watching: ああ、きついなあ。Aa, kitsui naa.
("Ah, that's rough / that's tough.")

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day's adjectives each cover several distinct senses that English speakers often lump together (especially "tight," which covers five unrelated Japanese nuances). Compare:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
きつい（けいこ）kitsui (keiko) Gruelling, hard to endure ＝つらい／厳しい — physical or mental hardship. "This training is brutal."
きつい（酒／日差し）kitsui (sake / hizashi) Strong, intense ＝強い — intensity of a substance or stimulus. "This liquor/sunlight is strong."
きつい（性格）kitsui (seikaku) Strong-willed, forceful ＝気が強い — a personality trait. "She has a forceful personality."
きつい（ベルト／ふた）kitsui (beruto / futa) Physically too tight Literal physical fit, ⇔ゆるい. "This belt/lid is too tight."
暗い（過去／見通し）kurai (kako / mitooshi) Bleak, troubled, dim (figurative) Describes an outlook or history, not literal darkness. "Her past is troubled." / "The outlook is bleak."
～に暗い~ ni kurai Uninformed, out of touch (about a topic) Takes に + a topic, ⇔～に明るい ("well-informed about ~"). "He's out of touch with politics."
Fixed-idiom trap: 鼻が高い（＝自慢だ, "to be proud"）is a fixed idiom using 鼻 ("nose") — do not confuse it with プライドが高い ("to have high self-esteem/be prideful," often with a negative nuance of arrogance) or 理想が高い ("to have high ideals"); all three use 高い but attach to entirely different nouns with different connotations (one positive/neutral pride in an achievement, one about ego, one about aspirations).
