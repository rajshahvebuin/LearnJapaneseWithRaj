JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 7 — まとめて覚えましょう② (Matomete oboemashou ②)

6日目 — 言葉の後ろにつく語② (Kotoba no ushiro ni tsuku go ② — Terms which follow other words ②)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.118–119
Theme
More suffixes attached to the back of words: inclusiveness (~ごと/~ごとに), intervals (~おき/~ぶり), difficulty (~がたい/~づらい), manner (~づかい/~こなす/~つき), and freshness (~たて)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "119ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.122 (one page further than the usual two-day gap, since 7日目 runs three pages instead of two) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Word-formation day: opens with an illustrated dinner-table dialogue about eating fish whole and freshly cooked rice, then presents suffix + example-word tables instead of the usual situational vocabulary lists.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
*硬い表現 = a stiff/formal expression
Scene

A couple sits down to dinner; the husband praises the freshly cooked rice and asks about eating a whole fish, while the wife points out a practical problem.

Husband: 炊きたてのごはんはおいしいなあ。この魚、丸ごと食べられる？Takitate no gohan wa oishii naa. Kono sakana, marugoto taberareru?
("Freshly cooked rice is delicious. Can this fish be eaten whole?")

Wife: 食べてもいいけど、骨が固くて食べづらいんじゃない？Tabete mo ii kedo, hone ga katakute tabezurain ja nai?
("You can eat it, but isn't it hard to eat because the bones are hard?")

炊きたて(takitate) "freshly cooked", 丸ごと(marugoto) "whole", and 食べづらい(tabezurai) "hard to eat" pack three of today's suffixes into one dinner-table exchange.

EN: Freshly boiled udon is very delicious.
HI: ताज़ी उबली हुई उदोन बहुत स्वादिष्ट होती है।
GU: તાજી બાફેલી ઉદોન ખૂબ સ્વાદિષ્ટ હોય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aゆでたてyudetatefreshly boiledताज़ा उबला हुआતાજું બાફેલું

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on two clusters of near-synonyms: "hard to do" (〜がたい vs 〜づらい, tested in Q2/Q4) and "interval/repetition" words (〜ごとに vs 〜おき vs 〜ぶりに, tested in Q3/Q6/Q8):

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
〜がたい~gatai Hard to do (formal) A stiff, written-register word for deep moral/emotional difficulty. 許しがたい "unforgivable", 信じがたい "unbelievable"
〜づらい~zurai Hard/uncomfortable to do (everyday) Casual, practical difficulty — physical or situational awkwardness. 座りづらい "uncomfortable to sit on", 書きづらい "hard to write with"
〜ごとに~goto ni Every time / each A recurring event tied to each instance/unit (often with a verb). 失敗するごとに "with each failure", 1問正解するごとに "each time you get one right"
〜おき~oki At fixed intervals of A regular, mechanical gap between repeated occurrences (often with a number). 5分おきに来る "comes every 5 minutes", 1行おきに書く "write on every other line"
〜ぶりに~buri ni For the first time in ~ A one-time event happening again after a long gap since it last occurred. 5年ぶりに会った "met for the first time in 5 years", 100年ぶりに更新された "broken for the first time in 100 years"
Register trap: 〜がたい appears in formal/written contexts (marked *硬い表現 "a stiff expression" in the book) and shouldn't be swapped freely with the everyday 〜づらい just because both mean "hard to ~" — using 座りがたい for an uncomfortable bench would sound oddly weighty, the same way using 許しづらい for a serious crime would sound too casual.
