1日目 — アパートを探しています (Apaato o sagashite imasu)

Source

Theme
賃貸アパート探し・引っ越しに関する言葉 (words for apartment-hunting and moving)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "13ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.15 (two pages later, same pattern as N1) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
This book's days are situational, not one continuous word list: the day opens with an illustrated dialogue between a real-estate agent and a prospective tenant, then groups vocabulary into small themed tables. Word order/grouping below follows the book exactly.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
❶ = 注意しましょう (usage caution)
ダメ = このような使い方はだめです (this usage is incorrect)
Scene

A real-estate agent is showing a prospective tenant a 賃貸アパート (rental apartment) listing.

Agent: ベランダも広々していますし、ながめもいいでしょう？Beranda mo hirobiro shite imasu shi, nagame mo ii deshou? 真ん前にコンビニがあるので便利ですし……Man mae ni konbini ga aru node benri desu shi...
("The veranda is nice and spacious too, and the view is good, right? There's a convenience store right out front, so it's convenient...")

Tenant: でも人通りが多すぎてちょっとうるさくないかなあ。Demo hitodoori ga oosugite chotto urusakunai ka naa. 近所付き合いが大変じゃないといいんだけど……Kinjo-zukiai ga taihen janai to iindakedo...
("But isn't there too much foot traffic — won't it be a bit noisy? I hope the relations with the neighbors won't be difficult...")

賃貸アパート (chintai apaato) — the listing shown: 駅徒歩5分・1DK・家賃5万円・敷金2ヵ月・礼金なし・管理費3000円・南向き・3階建ての3階・築10年・自転車置き場無料

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.13) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.13). Answers confirmed from the "13ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.15.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

私は10階（a. 建て　b. 建ち）のマンションに住んでいます。Watashi wa jukkai (a. date / b. dachi) no manshon ni sunde imasu.

Full sentence: 私は10階建てのマンションに住んでいます。Watashi wa jukkai-date no manshon ni sunde imasu.
EN: I live in a 10-story apartment building.
HI: मैं 10 मंज़िला अपार्टमेंट में रहता हूँ।
GU: હું 10 માળના એપાર્ટમેન્ટમાં રહું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a建て-date-story (counter suffix for buildings)-मंज़िला (भवन के लिए प्रत्यय)-માળનું (ઈમારત માટે પ્રત્યય)
b建ち-dachinot a real counter suffix here(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 「〜階建て」is the fixed counter for a building's number of stories (10階建て = "a 10-story building"); 建ち isn't used this way.

Q2

引っ越ししてから（a. 荷造り　b. 近所付き合い）が大変になった。Hikkoshi shite kara (a. nizukuri / b. kinjo-zukiai) ga taihen ni natta.

Full sentence: 引っ越ししてから近所付き合いが大変になった。Hikkoshi shite kara kinjo-zukiai ga taihen ni natta.
EN: Since I moved, relations with the neighbors have become difficult.
HI: घर बदलने के बाद पड़ोसियों से रिश्ते निभाना मुश्किल हो गया है।
GU: ઘર બદલ્યા પછી પડોશીઓ સાથેના સંબંધો મુશ્કેલ બની ગયા છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a荷造りnizukuripacking (a one-time task, not ongoing)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b近所付き合いkinjo-zukiairelations with neighborsपड़ोसियों से रिश्तेપડોશીઓ સાથેના સંબંધો

Why: 「引っ越ししてから〜大変になった」describes an ongoing state after moving; 荷造り (packing) is a one-off task done before/during the move, not something that becomes 大変 afterward.

Q3

ベランダには（a. 物干し　b. 洗面所）も付いているからよかった。Beranda ni wa (a. monohoshi / b. senmenjo) mo tsuite iru kara yokatta.

Full sentence: ベランダには物干しも付いているからよかった。Beranda ni wa monohoshi mo tsuite iru kara yokatta.
EN: It was great that the veranda also came with a clothesline.
HI: अच्छा हुआ कि बालकनी में कपड़े सुखाने की जगह भी थी।
GU: સારું થયું કે બાલ્કનીમાં કપડાં સૂકવવાની જગ્યા પણ હતી.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a物干しmonohoshiclotheslineकपड़े सुखाने की जगहકપડાં સૂકવવાની જગ્યા
b洗面所senmenjowashroom (not found on a veranda)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: A veranda (ベランダ) is an outdoor space where a clothesline (物干し) makes sense; a washroom (洗面所) is an indoor fixture, not something on a veranda.

Q4

友達に付き（a. 合って　b. 回って）買い物に行った。Tomodachi ni tsuki (a. atte / b. mawatte) kaimono ni itta.

Full sentence: 友達に付き合って買い物に行った。Tomodachi ni tsukiatte kaimono ni itta.
EN: I went along with a friend to go shopping.
HI: मैं एक दोस्त के साथ ख़रीदारी करने गया।
GU: હું એક મિત્રની સાથે ખરીદી કરવા ગયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a付き合ってtsukiattewent along with, accompaniedसाथ गयाસાથે ગયો
b付き回ってtsuki-mawattenot a real compound verb(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 「〜に付き合う」("to accompany/go along with someone") is the fixed verb; 付き回る isn't a word — 回す/回る attach to different vocabulary (like 回覧板を回す) in this same lesson, but not to 付き.

Q5

（a. 礼金　b. 敷金）は後で返してくれることになっている。(a. Reikin / b. Shikikin) wa ato de kaeshite kureru koto ni natte iru.

Full sentence: 敷金は後で返してくれることになっている。Shikikin wa ato de kaeshite kureru koto ni natte iru.
EN: The deposit is supposed to be given back later.
HI: ज़मानती राशि बाद में वापस कर दी जाती है।
GU: ડિપોઝિટ પછીથી પરત કરવામાં આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a礼金reikinkey money (never refunded)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b敷金shikikindeposit (refundable)ज़मानती राशि (वापस होने वाली)ડિપોઝિટ (પરત મળે તેવી)

Why: 敷金 is a refundable deposit; 礼金 is a one-time non-refundable "key money" gift to the landlord — only 敷金 fits "returned later."

Q6

近くに大学があるので、このアパートには（a. 一人住まい　b. 人通り）が多い。Chikaku ni daigaku ga aru node, kono apaato ni wa (a. hitorizumai / b. hitodoori) ga ooi.

Full sentence: 近くに大学があるので、このアパートには人通りが多い。Chikaku ni daigaku ga aru node, kono apaato ni wa hitodoori ga ooi.
EN: Since there's a university nearby, there's a lot of foot traffic around this apartment.
HI: पास में विश्वविद्यालय होने से इस अपार्टमेंट के आसपास लोगों की आवाजाही ज़्यादा है।
GU: નજીકમાં યુનિવર્સિટી હોવાથી આ એપાર્ટમેન્ટની આસપાસ લોકોની અવરજવર વધારે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a一人住まいhitorizumailiving alone (about a resident, not a street)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b人通りhitodoorifoot trafficलोगों की आवाजाहीલોકોની અવરજવર

Why: A university nearby means lots of people passing by (人通りが多い); 一人住まい describes how a single resident lives, not the street's traffic — the two words share 人 but mean very different things.

Q7

ベランダから見る外の（　）は最高です。Beranda kara miru soto no (___) wa saikou desu.

Full sentence: ベランダから見る外のながめは最高です。Beranda kara miru soto no nagame wa saikou desu.
EN: The view of outside from the veranda is the best.
HI: बालकनी से बाहर का नज़ारा बेहतरीन है।
GU: બાલ્કનીમાંથી બહારનો નજારો શ્રેષ્ઠ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1辺りatarivicinity, areaआस-पासઆસપાસ
2日当たりhiatarisunlight exposureधूपતડકો
3住まいsumairesidence, dwellingनिवासરહેઠાણ

Why: 「見る外の（　）」("the [　] of outside that you see") calls for ながめ ("view"); the other three describe a place, sunlight, or a dwelling, not what you see when you look out.

Q8

ぼくは、教室でいつもリンさんの（　）に座っている。Boku wa, kyoushitsu de itsumo Rin-san no (___) ni suwatte iru.

Full sentence: ぼくは、教室でいつもリンさんの真後ろに座っている。Boku wa, kyoushitsu de itsumo Rin-san no massushiro ni suwatte iru.
EN: In the classroom, I always sit right behind Rin.
HI: मैं क्लासरूम में हमेशा रिन के ठीक पीछे बैठता हूँ।
GU: હું વર્ગખંડમાં હંમેશાં રિનની બરાબર પાછળ બેસું છું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1直後chokugoimmediately after (about time, not place)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
2直後ろchoku-ushironot a real word(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Several pairs today look alike or share a kanji but behave very differently — exactly what 練習Ⅰ Q5, Q6 and Q8 above are testing. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
敷金shikikin Deposit paid to a landlord Refundable — the landlord returns it (minus damages) when you move out. Talking about money you get back later.
礼金reikin "Key money" gift to a landlord Never refunded — a one-time thank-you payment; some listings waive it entirely. Talking about money that's gone for good.
一人暮らし／一人住まいhitorigurashi / hitorizumai Living alone About a person's household situation. Describing how a resident lives.
人通りhitodoori Foot traffic About a street/area's pedestrian volume — nothing to do with living arrangements, despite sharing 人. Describing how busy a street is.
真ん前mammae Right in front Directly facing the reference point. "The convenience store is right in front of the building."
真後ろmassushiro Right behind Directly opposite 真ん前 — the back side of the reference point. "I sit right behind so-and-so."
斜め前naname mae Diagonally/kitty-corner in front Off to one side of 真ん前, not directly facing it. "The station is kitty-corner from here."
Idiom trap: 付き合う changes meaning entirely with its particle: 彼と付き合う ("to date/go out with him") vs. 友達に付き合う ("to go along with/accompany a friend somewhere"). Same verb, but と marks a romantic partner while に marks someone you're tagging along with — don't swap the particle or the meaning flips from "dating" to "accompanying."
