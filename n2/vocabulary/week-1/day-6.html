6日目 — お金を使いすぎました (Okane o tsukaisugimashita)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.22–23
Theme
銀行・お金の出し入れ・家計に関する言葉 (banking, money in/out, household budgeting)

練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "23ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.26 (which is Day 7's last test page, since Day 7 has no exercises of its own to answer 2 pages later) — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a young man realizes he's overspent this month, with paper bills sprouting wings and flying away and his hobby/dinner plans crossed out. Vocabulary splits into a banking/bills table (p.22) and a budgeting/frugality table (p.23), both marked with the same ※「が」→「の」でもOK note as Day 3.
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
(N) = 名詞 (noun)
※ = 「が」can be swapped for 「の」 (e.g. バランスが取れた＝バランスの取れた)
Scene

A young man sits slumped at his desk surrounded by bills and a few coins, watching paper money sprout wings and fly away, while his hobby and his dinner plans are crossed out.

Him (thinking): ああ、赤字だ。Aa, akaji da. 今月はバイトの収入は特に少なかったからなあ。Kongetsu wa baito no shuunyuu wa toku ni sukunakatta kara naa. 銀行へ行ってお金を下ろさないと……。Ginkou e itte okane o orosanai to... 授業料も払い込まなくちゃいけないし……。Jugyouryou mo haraikomanakucha ikenai shi... ああ、旅行する余裕なんてないなあ。Aa, ryokou suru yoyuu nante nai naa. 外食もやめないと。Gaishoku mo yamenai to.
("Ugh, I'm in the red. My part-time job income was especially low this month. I need to go withdraw money from the bank... I have to pay my tuition too... Ugh, there's no way I can afford to travel. I should stop eating out too.")

紙幣／お札 (shihei / osatsu) — paper bills, shown here sprouting wings and flying away; 請求書 (seikyuusho) — a bill/invoice; 小銭 (kozeni) — coins

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.23) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.23). Answers confirmed from the "23ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.26.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

銀行に（a. 預けてある　b. 振り込んである）お金が減ってきた。Ginkou ni (a. azukete aru / b. furikonde aru) okane ga hette kita.

Full sentence: 銀行に預けてあるお金が減ってきた。Ginkou ni azukete aru okane ga hette kita.
EN: The money that's deposited in the bank has been decreasing.
HI: बैंक में जमा पैसा घटता जा रहा है।
GU: બેંકમાં જમા પૈસા ઘટતા જાય છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a預けてあるazukete aruis deposited (a resulting state)जमा हैજમા છે
b振り込んであるfurikonde aruhas been transferred in (describes a one-time transfer, not a standing balance)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 預ける describes money sitting in an account as a deposit — the natural state that "decreases" over time; 振り込む describes the one-time act of transferring money in, not an ongoing balance.

Q2

電気代を（a. 不足　b. 節約）するためにエアコンを止めた。Denkidai o (a. fusoku / b. setsuyaku) suru tame ni eakon o tometa.

Full sentence: 電気代を節約するためにエアコンを止めた。Denkidai o setsuyaku suru tame ni eakon o tometa.
EN: I turned off the air conditioner to save on the electricity bill.
HI: बिजली का बिल बचाने के लिए एसी बंद कर दिया।
GU: વીજળીનું બિલ બચાવવા એસી બંધ કરી દીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a不足fusokushortage (a noun describing a lack, not an action you take)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b節約setsuyakuto economize/saveबचत करनाબચત કરવી

Why: 電気代を節約する ("to save on the electricity bill") is the fixed collocation for a deliberate money-saving action; 不足する describes a shortage happening to something, not an action someone takes to prevent it.

EN: I think going to a restaurant every day is extravagant.
HI: मुझे लगता है रोज़ रेस्तराँ जाना फ़िज़ूलख़र्ची है।
GU: મને લાગે છે રોજ રેસ્ટોરાંમાં જવું એ મોજશોખ છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aぜいたくだzeitaku dais extravagant/luxuriousफ़िज़ूलख़र्ची हैમોજશોખ છે
b余裕がないyoyuu ga naithere's no room to spare (describes a lack of means, not a judgment about the habit itself)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: Calling a daily habit "ぜいたく" ("extravagant/spending beyond what's necessary") is a judgment about the behavior itself; 余裕がない describes not having the financial means, a different (though related) statement.

Q4

今月分の家賃の（a. 支払い　b. 支出）を済ませた。Kongetsu-bun no yachin no (a. shiharai / b. shishutsu) o sumaseta.

Full sentence: 今月分の家賃の支払いを済ませた。Kongetsu-bun no yachin no shiharai o sumaseta.
EN: I finished paying this month's rent.
HI: इस महीने का किराया चुका दिया।
GU: આ મહિનાનું ભાડું ચૂકવી દીધું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a支払いshiharaipayment (the specific act of paying)भुगतानચુકવણી
b支出shishutsuexpenditure (a broader accounting category, not "finishing" a single payment)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Why: 支払いを済ませる ("to finish making a payment") describes completing a specific payment like rent; 支出 is the broader accounting term for money going out overall, which doesn't pair naturally with "finishing" one bill.

Q5

HI: फ़ोन का बिल खाते से स्वतः कट गया।

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on telling apart near-synonyms for "spending/lacking money" and "paying" — exactly what Q2/Q3, Q4/Q5, and Q7/Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
節約するsetsuyaku suru To economize/save (money) A deliberate action taken to spend less. "I turned off the AC to save on electricity."
不足するfusoku suru To be insufficient/short Describes a lack that has occurred — not an action you take. "My living expenses are running short."
支払いshiharai Payment (a specific act) One concrete bill or transaction being paid. "I finished paying this month's rent."
支出shishutsu Expenditure (a category) The broader accounting side of money going out — the opposite of 収入 (income). "My expenditures this month were high."
引き落とされるhikiotosareru To be automatically deducted A bank/system-driven, recurring automatic charge. "The phone bill was auto-deducted."
下ろす／引き出すorosu / hikidasu To withdraw (money) A manual action you take at a bank/ATM. "I need to go withdraw cash."
Figurative extension: かたよる／つりあう aren't only about money or diet — 栄養がかたよる ("nutrition is unbalanced") and 考え方がかたよる ("a way of thinking is biased") reuse the same verb for physical and mental imbalance, while 収入につりあう生活 ("a lifestyle that matches one's income") extends つりあう ("to balance/match") from a physical scale metaphor to a whole way of living within one's means.
