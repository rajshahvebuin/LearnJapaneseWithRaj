3日目 — 友人を招きました (Yuujin o manekimashita)

Notes
Scene: a visitor arrives with a small gift while another pair of friends admire a new sofa. Vocabulary splits into a set fixed-phrase "greetings" table (p.16) and a second "もっとおぼえましょう" table of comfort/relaxing words (p.17), where a shared ※ note says が can be swapped for の in all of that table's 心地 expressions.

Scene

A visitor arrives at a friend's home bearing a small gift, while in another room two friends admire a newly bought sofa.

Visitor: こんにちは。ご無沙汰しております。あのー、これ、つまらないものですが……Konnichiwa. Gobusata shite orimasu. Anoo, kore, tsumaranai mono desu ga...
("Hello. It's been a while. Um, this is just a small thing, but please take it...")

Host: すみません。遠慮なくいただきます。今日はゆっくりしていってくださいね。Sumimasen. Enryo naku itadakimasu. Kyou wa yukkuri shite itte kudasai ne.
("Thank you so much, I'll gladly accept it. Please make yourself at home and take your time today.")

Friend 1: このソファー、半額セールで3万円だったんだよ。Kono sofaa, hangaku seeru de san-man'en dattan da yo.
("This sofa was 30,000 yen at a half-price sale!")

Friend 2: そりゃ、お買い得だね。座り心地もいいし。Sorya, okaidoku da ne. Suwarigokochi mo ii shi.
("Wow, what a bargain. And it's comfortable to sit on too.")

つまらないものですが (tsumaranai mono desu ga) — the humble set phrase used when handing someone a gift, lit. "this is a boring/trifling thing, but..."

2 Quiz / Exercise Section

練習Ⅰ (6 items, from p.17) and 練習Ⅱ (2 items, from p.17). Answers confirmed from the "17ページの答え" box printed at the bottom of p.19.

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

大きいソファーがあって、とても（a. 座り心地　b. 居心地）がいい部屋ですね。Ookii sofaa ga atte, totemo (a. suwarigokochi / b. igokochi) ga ii heya desu ne.

Full sentence: 大きいソファーがあって、とても居心地がいい部屋ですね。Ookii sofaa ga atte, totemo igokochi ga ii heya desu ne.
EN: There's a big sofa, and it's such a comfortable room to be in, isn't it.
HI: यहाँ एक बड़ा सोफ़ा है, और यह कमरा रहने के लिए बहुत आरामदायक है, है ना।
GU: અહીં મોટો સોફા છે, અને આ રૂમ રહેવા માટે ખૂબ આરામદાયક છે, ખરું ને.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a座り心地suwarigokochicomfort of sitting (describes the sofa itself, not the room)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
b居心地igokochicomfort of being (in a place)रहने का आरामરહેવાનો આરામ

Why: The sentence describes 部屋 ("the room") as comfortable, which calls for 居心地 (comfort of being in a place); 座り心地 specifically describes the comfort of sitting on something like a chair or sofa, not a whole room.

Q2

昨日は家でゆっくり（a. くつろいだ　b. 暮らした）。Kinou wa ie de yukkuri (a. kutsuroida / b. kurashita).

Full sentence: 昨日は家でゆっくりくつろいだ。Kinou wa ie de yukkuri kutsuroida.
EN: Yesterday I relaxed leisurely at home.
HI: कल मैं घर पर आराम से सुस्ताया।
GU: ગઈકાલે હું ઘરે નિરાંતે હળવો થયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aくつろいだkutsuroidarelaxedसुस्तायाહળવો થયો
b暮らしたkurashitalived (an ongoing lifestyle, not a single leisurely day)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's exercises hinge on three "comfortable" words that pick different things to be comfortable, and on telling greeting phrases apart by exactly when they're said — exactly what Q1, Q3 and Q6 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
座り心地（が/の）いいsuwarigokochi ga/no ii Comfortable to sit on About the physical feel of a seat — sofa, chair, car seat. Describing furniture you sit on.
寝心地（が/の）いいnegokochi ga/no ii Comfortable to sleep on About the physical feel of a bed/mattress. Describing a bed or mattress.
居心地（が/の）いいigokochi ga/no ii Comfortable to be in About the overall atmosphere of a place — a room, house, café — not any single piece of furniture. Describing a room, home, or hangout spot as a whole.
お買い得okaidoku A great deal, a bargain Describes the purchase/item as good value — doesn't directly modify a price noun. "That sofa was a steal."
お手ごろ（な）otegoro (na) Affordable, reasonably priced Directly describes a price (値段/価格) as within reach. "This place has affordable-priced lunches."
Fixed-phrase trap: つまらないものですが ("this is a trifling thing, but...") is a humility formula said while handing over a gift — it doesn't literally mean the gift is boring, and swapping in a literal synonym like 面白くない sounds bizarre. Likewise 遠いところまでよくいらっしゃいました is only said as a greeting on arrival, while ゆっくりしていってください is only said once a guest has settled in — the two "welcome" phrases are not interchangeable at the same moment.
