JLPT N2 · Vocabulary · Week 1 — 楽しく暮らしていますか？ (Tanoshiku kurashite imasu ka?)

5日目 — 夏休みを海で過ごしています (Natsuyasumi o umi de sugoshite imasu)

Source
日本語総まとめ N2 語彙 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Goi) — printed p.20–21
Theme
海水浴・日焼け・魚釣り・ボートに関する言葉 (swimming, sunbathing, fishing, boats)
Answer key
練習Ⅰ・Ⅱ answers for this day are printed in the "21ページの答え" box at the bottom of p.23 — confirmed, not guessed.
Notes
Scene: a beach comedy — someone fell out of a fishing boat and needs rescuing, snorkelers dive nearby, a fish refuses obvious bait, and a shark eyes the swimmers as its own "bait." Vocabulary splits into a sunbathing/swimming/fishing table (p.20) and a boats/danger table (p.21).
⊙ = 関連する語彙 (a related word/expression)
⇔ = 反対の意味 (antonym)
Scene

At the beach: a fishing boat has capsized and someone's in the water, snorkelers dive nearby, and a shark lurks below eyeing the swimmers.

Person in the water: ああ、眼鏡を外せばよかった……Aa, megane o hazuseba yokatta...
("Ugh, I should have taken my glasses off...")

Person on the boat (throwing a rope): これにつかまれ！Kore ni tsukamare!
("Grab onto this!")

Boy in the water: 助けてー！Tasuketee!
("Help!") — label: 行方不明のボート (yukue fumei no booto — "the missing boat")

A fish near the hook (thought bubble): そんなものには、引っかからないぞ。Sonna mono ni wa, hikkakaranai zo.
("I'm not falling for something like that.")

The shark below (thought bubble): あちこちに、うまそうなえさがあるなー。Achikochi ni, umasou na esa ga aru naa.
("There's tasty-looking bait everywhere~")

これにつかまれ (kore ni tsukamare) — imperative of つかまる ("grab onto/hang onto"), thrown as a rope to someone in danger of drowning

練習Ⅰ — choose the correct option

Q1

この自転車は古いので（a. かく　b. こぐ）と変な音がする。Kono jitensha wa furui node (a. kaku / b. kogu) to hen na oto ga suru.

Full sentence: この自転車は古いのでこぐと変な音がする。Kono jitensha wa furui node kogu to hen na oto ga suru.
EN: This bicycle is old, so it makes a strange noise when you pedal it.
HI: यह साइकिल पुरानी है, इसलिए पैडल मारने पर अजीब आवाज़ आती है।
GU: આ સાયકલ જૂની છે, એટલે પેડલ મારતાં વિચિત્ર અવાજ આવે છે.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aかくkakuto sweat/scratch/write (wrong verb for pedaling)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bこぐkoguto pedal/rowपैडल मारनाપેડલ મારવું

Why: こぐ is the verb for pedaling a bicycle or rowing a boat — the same word covers both, as seen in ボートをこぐ／自転車をこぐ; かく has entirely different meanings (汗をかく "to sweat," 絵をかく "to draw") that don't fit here.

泥棒が（a. つかまれた　b. つかまえられた）。Dorobou ga (a. tsukamareta / b. tsukamaerareta).

Full sentence: 泥棒がつかまえられた。Dorobou ga tsukamaerareta.
EN: The thief was caught.
HI: चोर पकड़ा गया।
GU: ચોર પકડાયો.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aつかまれたtsukamaretawas grabbed (passive of つかむ, "to grab" — a single hand-grip, not an arrest)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bつかまえられたtsukamaeraretawas caught (passive of 捕まえる, "to catch/capture")पकड़ा गयाપકડાયો

Why: "The thief was caught (by the police)" needs the passive of 捕まえる ("to catch/apprehend"); つかむ is about physically grabbing/gripping something with your hand, which isn't what happens when police "catch" a criminal.

EN: The lights went out and it became pitch dark.
HI: बत्ती चली गई और चारों तरफ़ अंधेरा हो गया।
GU: લાઈટ ગઈ અને ચારેકોર અંધારું થઈ ગયું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a真っ暗makkurapitch-dark (absence of light)घुप्प अंधेराસાવ અંધારું
b真っ黒makkurodeep black (a color, e.g. of skin/objects, not "the state after lights go out")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q4

「あ、大変だ！あそこで子どもが（a. もぐっている　b. おぼれている）。」"A, taihen da! Asoko de kodomo ga (a. mogutte iru / b. oborete iru)."

Full sentence: あ、大変だ！あそこで子どもがおぼれている。A, taihen da! Asoko de kodomo ga oborete iru.
EN: "Oh no! A child is drowning over there!"
HI: "अरे, बड़ी बात है! वहाँ एक बच्चा डूब रहा है।"
GU: "અરે, મોટી વાત છે! ત્યાં એક બાળક ડૂબી રહ્યું છે."
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aもぐっているmogutte iruis diving (a deliberate, safe action — contradicts 大変だ "this is bad!")(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)
bおぼれているoborete iruis drowningडूब रहा हैડૂબી રહ્યું છે

GU: ખાઈને જોયું તો એ સ્વાદમાં ખરાબ નીકળ્યું.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
aまずかったmazukattatasted bad (an actual, experienced judgment)बेस्वाद निकलाસ્વાદમાં ખરાબ નીકળ્યું

Q6

HI: मेरे दादा एक बैंक-ट्रांसफ़र धोखाधड़ी में फँस गए।
GU: મારા દાદા બેંક-ટ્રાન્સફર છેતરપિંડીમાં ફસાઈ ગયા.
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
a引っかけてhikkaketehooking (something) — transitive, would mean grandfather ran the scam(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

Q7

その犬にあまり（　）とかまれるよ。Sono inu ni amari (___) to kamareru yo.

Full sentence: その犬にあまり近寄るとかまれるよ。Sono inu ni amari chikayoru to kamareru yo.

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1近づけるchikazukeruto bring (something) closer — transitive, needs an object(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1はずしてhazushitetaking off/removing (doesn't fit a cup)(यहाँ ग़लत रूप)(અહીં ખોટું સ્વરૂપ)

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

This day is full of transitive/intransitive pairs that look almost identical but swap who's doing what to whom — exactly what Q2, Q6, Q7 and Q8 above test. Compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
捕まえるtsukamaeru To catch (someone/something) Transitive — someone actively catches a target. "The police caught the thief." (thief = object)
捕まるtsukamaru To get/be caught Intransitive — the subject itself ends up caught. "The thief got caught." (thief = subject)
引っかけるhikkakeru To hook (something), or to con someone Transitive — an agent doing the hooking/conning. Describing the scammer's action.
引っかかるhikkakaru To get hooked/caught, or to fall for a con Intransitive — the subject is the one who gets snagged or conned. Describing the victim's experience.
ひっくり返すhikkurikaesu To overturn (something) Transitive, takes を + object. "I knocked over the cup."
ひっくり返るhikkurikaeru To capsize/tip over (by itself) Intransitive, takes が + subject. "The boat capsized."
Figurative extension: 引っかかる／引っかける aren't only about physical hooks and nets — 詐欺に引っかかる ("to fall for a scam") reuses the exact same image as a fish getting caught on a hook, applied to being tricked out of money. まずい／うまい work the same figurative-vs-literal way as taste words, but note the register gap: うまい (for "delicious") skims more male/casual than the neutral おいしい, so swapping them changes tone, not just meaning.
