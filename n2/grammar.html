〜あげく

"after all that... (in the end, unfortunately)" — a bad or troublesome result after a long process.

さんざん悩んだあげく、旅行をキャンセルすることにした。
After agonizing over it for a long time, I ended up cancelling the trip.
Formation & notes

Verb (た-form) / Noun + の + あげく. Usually followed by a negative or disappointing outcome.

〜からといって

"just because... (doesn't mean...)" — rejects an assumption that would naturally follow from a stated reason.

日本語が上手だからといって、漢字も全部読めるとは限らない。
Just because someone is good at Japanese doesn't mean they can read every kanji.
Formation & notes

Plain form + からといって, typically paired with 〜とは限らない or a similar denial in the second clause.

〜において

"in / at / regarding..." — a formal way to mark the setting, field, or point of a statement.

この分野において、彼は第一人者だ。
In this field, he is a leading expert.
Formation & notes

Noun + において(は)／における + Noun. More formal than で; common in writing, presentations, and official documents.
