JLPT N2 · Module

Kanji

Following 日本語総まとめ N2 漢字 (Nihongo Sou-Matome N2 Kanji) week by week, day by day — this book teaches kanji through real-life situations (signs, forms, ads, newspapers) rather than by shared component or reading.

💡
Module complete — all 8 weeks, 56 days Every week is built, each including a Day 7 実戦問題 review test. This book's own [別冊] answer booklet is included in the scan, so every answer — regular day and test day alike — is confirmed from the official key, not reasoned out.
