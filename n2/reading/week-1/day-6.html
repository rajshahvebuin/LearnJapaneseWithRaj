Notes
This day's theme is レシピ (recipes) — the ✿ tip warns to pay close attention to words expressing order (①②③... and いったん, 仕上げに) and degree (焦がさないように, 軽く). The main 問題 compares two similar-but-different recipes (A: a stew; B: a shrimp-macaroni gratin).

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

順番や程度を表す表現に注意！Junban ya teido o arawasu hyougen ni chuui!

Pay attention to expressions that show order (順番 — numbered steps, いったん "once/temporarily," 仕上げに "as the finishing touch") and degree (程度 — 軽く "lightly," 焦がさないように "so as not to burn," 弱火で "on low heat"). Recipes hide their trickiest details in exactly these small connector words, not in the ingredient list.

HI: क्रम (順番 — क्रमांकित चरण, いったん "एक बार/अस्थायी रूप से," 仕上げに "अंतिम चरण के रूप में") और मात्रा/तीव्रता (程度 — 軽く "हल्के से," 焦がさないように "जलने न पाए," 弱火で "धीमी आँच पर") दिखाने वाले शब्दों पर ध्यान दें। नुस्खों की सबसे मुश्किल जानकारी अक्सर इन्हीं छोटे जोड़ने वाले शब्दों में छिपी होती है, सामग्री सूची में नहीं। GU: ક્રમ (順番 — ક્રમાંકિત પગલાં, いったん "એક વાર/હંગામી ધોરણે," 仕上げに "અંતિમ પગલા તરીકે") અને માત્રા (程度 — 軽く "હળવેથી," 焦がさないように "બળે નહીં તેમ," 弱火で "ધીમા તાપે") દર્શાવતા શબ્દો પર ધ્યાન આપો. રેસિપીની સૌથી અઘરી વિગતો ઘણી વાર આ નાના જોડાણ-શબ્દોમાં જ છુપાયેલી હોય છે, સામગ્રી યાદીમાં નહીં.

例えばグラタンの作り方にはこんな言葉が出てきます。Tatoeba guratan no tsukurikata ni wa konna kotoba ga dete kimasu. — Words that typically appear in a recipe like gratin:

練習 (Warm-up — answer confirmed on the next page)

母：あ、玉ねぎも薄く切らないのよ。みんな同じくらいの大きさに切るの。Haha: A, tamanegi mo usuku kiranai no yo. Minna onaji kurai no ookisa ni kiru no.

A mother is guiding her daughter through cooking. She corrects the daughter's onion-cutting technique (don't slice it thin — cut everything to a similar size). The daughter admits they have no butter or meat; the mother says to just use what's on hand. She asks about adding shrimp, but the daughter refuses. After stir-frying in oil, they add milk last, then water. When it starts boiling, something floats to the surface — the mother tells the daughter to scoop it out and discard it, then lower the heat, waiting until the roux (ルー) has softened.

#StatementTrue/False
1母が料理をして娘に見せている。Haha ga ryouri o shite musume ni misete iru. — The mother is cooking and showing the daughter.False
2母が娘に料理をさせて教えている。Haha ga musume ni ryouri o sasete oshiete iru. — The mother is having the daughter cook while teaching her.True
3娘は玉ねぎを薄切りにしてしまった。Musume wa tamanegi o usugiri ni shite shimatta. — The daughter (already) cut the onion into thin slices (by mistake).False
4母は肉の代わりにエビを使おうと思った。Haha wa niku no kawari ni ebi o tsukaou to omotta. — The mother thought to use shrimp instead of meat.True
5母は娘にアクの取り方を指示した。Haha wa musume ni aku no torikata o shiji shita. — The mother instructed the daughter on how to skim the scum.True

Why: The mother is correcting and directing the daughter's cutting technique in real time ("あ、〜切らないのよ") while the daughter does the actual cutting/cooking — a teaching-while-doing setup, matching #2 rather than #1 (mother demonstrating alone). The mother's correction is a preemptive instruction, not a comment on an already-made mistake, so #3 (daughter already cut it thin) is false. The mother explicitly proposes shrimp ("エビ入れる？") once she notices there's no meat, matching #4. When something floated up as the mixture boiled, the mother told the daughter exactly what to do about it ("それ、すくって捨てて"), matching #5.

2 Passage Questions

The main 問題 (p.23) gives two full recipes side by side — A: シチュー (stew, serves 8) and B: エビマカロニグラタン (shrimp macaroni gratin, serves 4) — each with an ingredient list and numbered steps, then asks questions comparing them (and comparing recipe A to the 練習 dialogue above). Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Q1

Recipe A — シチュー（8皿分）(Shichuu, 8-plate serving)

①厚手の鍋を熱してバターを溶かし、一口大に切った肉・野菜を焦がさないように炒めます。※バターのかわりにサラダ油で炒めてもかまいません。① Atsude no nabe o nesshite bataa o tokashi, hitokuchi-dai ni kitta niku/yasai o kogasanai you ni itamemasu. ※Bataa no kawari ni sarada-abura de itamete mo kamaimasen.

②水を加え、沸騰したらアクをとります。③材料が柔らかくなるまで弱火で約20分間煮込みます。④いったん火を止めてルーを割り入れ、よく溶かします。⑤再び弱火で煮込み、仕上げに牛乳を加えてさらに軽く煮込みます。② Mizu o kuwae, futtou shitara aku o torimasu. ③ Zairyou ga yawarakaku naru made yowabi de yaku nijuppun-kan nikomimasu. ④ Ittan hi o tomete ruu o wariire, yoku tokashimasu. ⑤ Futatabi yowabi de nikomi, shiage ni gyuunyuu o kuwaete sara ni karuku nikomimasu. — Ingredients: クリームシチューの素1箱, 肉(鶏肉か豚肉)300g, タマネギ中2個(500g), にんじん1/2本(150g), じゃがいも中2個(300g), バター大さじ1, 水900ml, 牛乳200ml.

問1　左ページの「練習」の会話について、正しいものはどれか。Toi 1: Hidari peeji no "renshuu" no kaiwa ni tsuite, tadashii mono wa dore ka.

EN: Regarding the 練習 conversation above (mother and daughter making a stew without butter or meat, and without shrimp), which statement is correct?
OptionJapaneseEnglish
1肉の代わりにエビでＡを作っている。Niku no kawari ni ebi de A o tsukutte iru.They're making A with shrimp instead of meat.
2野菜だけでＡを作っている。Yasai dake de A o tsukutte iru.They're making A with vegetables only.
3エビを使わずにＢを作っている。Ebi o tsukawazu ni B o tsukutte iru.They're making B without shrimp.
4バターを使わずにＢを作っている。Bataa o tsukawazu ni B o tsukutte iru.They're making B without butter.

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問1: 2): The 練習 dialogue is clearly about recipe A (a stew with a roux/ルー finished with milk), not B. The daughter explicitly refused shrimp ("エビはいやだ"), and they had no meat or butter, using oil and "whatever's on hand" instead — so this is A made with vegetables only. Options 3 and 4 are about recipe B, which the dialogue never discusses; option 1 contradicts the daughter's direct refusal of shrimp.

Q2

Recipe B — エビマカロニグラタン（4皿分）(Ebi makaroni guratan, 4-plate serving)

①厚手のなべにサラダ油を熱し、タマネギとエビを焦がさないように炒めます。②いったん火を止め、ソースミックス・水・牛乳の順に入れてよく混ぜます。③マカロニを加えて中火にかけ、かき混ぜながら約5分煮ます。④耐熱皿に移し、チーズをのせてオーブントースターで5〜6分、焦げ目がつくまで焼きます。① Atsude no nabe ni sarada-abura o nesshi, tamanegi to ebi o kogasanai you ni itamemasu. ② Ittan hi o tome, soosu-mikkusu/mizu/gyuunyuu no jun ni irete yoku mazemasu. ③ Makaroni o kuwaete chuubi ni kake, kakimazenagara yaku go-fun nimasu. ④ Tainetsuzara ni utsushi, chiizu o nosete oobun-toosutaa de go-roku-fun, kogeme ga tsuku made yakimasu. — Ingredients: ホワイトソースミックス1袋, マカロニ1袋, 冷凍エビ150g, タマネギ(薄切り)小1個(150g), サラダ油大さじ2, 水400ml, 牛乳300ml, とろけるチーズ適量.

問2　ＡとＢについて正しいものはどれか。Toi 2: A to B ni tsuite tadashii mono wa dore ka.

EN: Which statement about recipes A and B together is correct?
OptionJapaneseEnglish
1ＡもＢも野菜などをまず炒める。A mo B mo yasai nado o mazu itameru.Both A and B stir-fry the vegetables (etc.) first.
2ＡもＢも焦げ目をつけないようにする。A mo B mo kogeme o tsukenai you ni suru.Both A and B avoid browning the food.
3ＡもＢもいろいろな野菜を煮込む。A mo B mo iroiro na yasai o nikomu.Both A and B simmer a variety of vegetables.
4ＡもＢも仕上げに牛乳を使う。A mo B mo shiage ni gyuunyuu o tsukau.Both A and B use milk as a finishing touch.

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問2: 1): Step ① in both recipes starts by heating a heavy pan and stir-frying the meat/vegetables (A) or onion/shrimp (B) without burning them — both begin the same way. Option 2 is wrong because B's final step deliberately browns the top (焦げ目がつくまで焼きます) — the opposite of avoiding browning. Option 3 is wrong because B only has one vegetable (onion), not "various vegetables" like A's carrot/potato/onion mix. Option 4 is wrong because B never adds milk as a finishing step — its milk goes in early with the sauce mix and water (step ②), while A does add milk right at the end (仕上げに) — this is true for A only, not both.

Q1 Match the 練習 dialogue's specific details (no meat, no butter, no shrimp) to the right recipe (A, not B) The dialogue only ever discusses recipe A's ingredients (roux, milk finish) — B is a red herring here Options 3–4 describe B, which the 練習 conversation never mentions — don't let a plausible-sounding option about the wrong document distract you.
Q2 Compare step order and technique across two documents side by side Where A and B truly overlap (step 1: stir-fry first) vs. where they diverge (browning, vegetable variety, when milk is added) A "both X and Y" question fails the instant ONE recipe contradicts it — B's final browning step alone rules out option 2.
