問1

左ページの会話から、Aさんの条件に合わないものを除くと、どれが残るか。Hidari peeji no kaiwa kara, A-san no jouken ni awanai mono o nozoku to, dore ga nokoru ka.

EN: From the conversation on the left page, if you remove the listings that don't match A-san's conditions, which ones remain?
HI: बाईं ओर की बातचीत के अनुसार, जो सूचियाँ A-सान की शर्तों से मेल नहीं खातीं उन्हें हटाने पर, कौन-सी बचती हैं?
GU: ડાબી બાજુની વાતચીત મુજબ, A-સાનની શરતો સાથે ન મળતી યાદીઓ કાઢી નાખતાં, કઈ બાકી રહે છે?
OptionAnswer
1CとE C to E
2CとD C to D
3DとE D to E
4DとG D to G

Answer: 3 (D と E) (confirmed — booklet: "問1:3"). This question depends entirely on conditions A-san states in the missing p.16 conversation, so the elimination logic that rules out A, B, C, F, G, H, I can't be reconstructed here — only the confirmed final answer (D: 映画館スタッフ / cinema staff, and E: カフェ調理補助・ホール係 / café kitchen & floor staff) is cited.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare what each question actually requires, given the scan gap:

QuestionWhat it needsWhat we haveTrap to avoid
練習 (p.16) The full dialogue and 5 true/false statements Only one line's gloss: 向いて(い)ない＝合わない; confirmed answer 2・3 Don't reconstruct the missing dialogue from the gloss alone — a single line isn't enough context to safely infer the rest.

問2 What B-san reacts to or asks about in the p.16 conversation Only the final answer, I Same caution — I (weekends, high wage, phone shop) is plausible on its own merits, but the actual textual evidence for "B-san is interested" is on the missing page.
Reading-table skill, independent of the missing page: Even without the left-page conversation, practice scanning the table itself by column — 曜日・時間帯 (days/hours) first to rule out schedule conflicts, then 時給 (wage) and 交通費 (transport) to compare take-home value. That column-by-column scan is the transferable N2 skill this exercise is built around, regardless of which two rows the missing conversation ultimately points to.
