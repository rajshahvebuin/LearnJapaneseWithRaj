1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

有効期限に注意！Yuukou kigen ni chuui! — Pay attention to the date of expiry!

例えばこんな項目をチェックします。Tatoeba konna koumoku o chekku shimasu. ("For example, check items like these.") A coupon/discount ticket always answers these questions somewhere on its face — find them before trusting the discount:

  • いつまで？Itsu made? ("Until when?") → 有効期限：XX年○月×日まで／XX年○月×日まで有効Yuukou kigen: XX-nen ○-gatsu ×-nichi made / XX-nen ○-gatsu ×-nichi made yuukou. ("Valid until: [date]" / "valid until [date]")
    HI: "समाप्ति तिथि: XX वर्ष X महीना X तारीख तक" जैसे शब्द ढूँढें। GU: "સમાપ્તિ તારીખ: XX વર્ષ X મહિનો X તારીખ સુધી" જેવા શબ્દો શોધો.
  • いくら？Ikura? ("How much?") → 無料／半額／○割引／○%OFFMuryou / hangaku / ○-waribiki / ○-paasento OFF. ("free / half price / X-discount / X% OFF")
    HI: मुफ़्त, आधी क़ीमत, या कितने प्रतिशत की छूट है, यह देखें। GU: મફત, અડધી કિંમત, અથવા કેટલા ટકાનું ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ છે તે જુઓ.
  • どこで？Doko de? ("Where?") → ○○店のみ（＝○○店だけ）／全店（＝どの店でも）○○-ten nomi (= ○○-ten dake) / zenten (= dono mise demo). ("only at __ store" / "any store")
    HI: क्या यह सिर्फ़ एक दुकान पर चलता है या सभी दुकानों पर? GU: શું આ ફક્ત એક દુકાન પર ચાલે છે કે બધી દુકાનો પર?
  • いつ出す？Itsu dasu? ("When to present it?") → ご注文の際（＝注文するとき）／お会計前に（＝会計するとき）Go-chuumon no sai (= chuumon suru toki) / o-kaikei mae ni (= kaikei suru toki). ("when ordering" / "before paying")
    HI: ऑर्डर करते समय देना है या बिल चुकाते समय — यह सबसे बड़ी ग़लतफ़हमी की जगह है। GU: ઓર્ડર કરતી વખતે આપવું કે બિલ ચૂકવતી વખતે — આ સૌથી મોટી ગેરસમજની જગ્યા છે.
  • 何人？Nan-nin? ("How many people?") → お一人様一回（＝持っている人が一回だけ）／同伴の方お一人まで（＝一緒に行った人も一人だけ）O-hitori-sama ikkai (= motte iru hito ga ikkai dake) / douhan no kata o-hitori made (= issho ni itta hito mo hitori dake).
    HI: केवल कूपन रखने वाले के लिए एक बार, या साथ आए एक व्यक्ति तक भी लागू? GU: ફક્ત કૂપન ધરાવનાર માટે એક વાર, કે સાથે આવેલી એક વ્યક્તિ માટે પણ લાગુ?
  • ほかの券も使える？Hoka no ken mo tsukaeru? ("Can other tickets also be used?") → 他のクーポンとは併用できません（＝他の割引券とは一緒に使えない）Hoka no kuupon to wa heiyou dekimasen (= hoka no waribikiken to wa issho ni tsukaenai).
    HI: "併用できません" का मतलब है कि इसे किसी और कूपन के साथ नहीं जोड़ा जा सकता। GU: "併用できません" નો અર્થ છે કે તેને બીજા કોઈ કૂપન સાથે જોડી શકાય નહીં.

✿ 練習 (Renshuu — Practice)

次の会話文を読んで、後の文から正しいものを選ぼう。Tsugi no kaiwabun o yonde, ato no bun kara tadashii mono o erabou. ("Read the following dialogue, then choose the correct statements below.") Answer confirmed printed on the next page (p.13): 1・2・5.

妹：おいしいね、このチーズケーキ。Imouto: Oishii ne, kono chiizukeeki.

姉：うん。あ！チーズケーキといえば、割引券があったの、忘れてた。……ほら！Ane: Un. A! Chiizukeeki to ieba, waribikiken ga atta no, wasureteta. ...Hora!

妹：なんだー。早く気がつけばよかったのに。もう食べちゃったよ。Imouto: Nanda-. Hayaku ki ga tsukeba yokatta noni. Mou tabechatta yo.

姉：会計のとき出せばいいんじゃないの？えーっと、あ、注文のときに出すんだって。Ane: Kaikei no toki daseba ii n janai no? Eeto, a, chuumon no toki ni dasu n datte.

妹：えー。半額になるはずだったんでしょ。二人だから240円も安くなるんだったのに。Imouto: Ee. Hangaku ni naru hazu datta n desho. Futari dakara 240-en mo yasuku naru n datta noni.

姉：一人分だけだって。でも、それでももったいないよね。店員さんに聞いてみようか。Ane: Hitori-bun dake datte. Demo, sore demo mottainai yo ne. Ten'in-san ni kiite miyou ka.

妹：ちょっと見せて。……あれ？なんだ、これ期限過ぎてるじゃない。Imouto: Chotto misete. ...Are? Nanda, kore kigen sugiteru janai.

EN: Sister: This cheesecake is good, isn't it. — Older sister: Yeah. Oh! Speaking of cheesecake, I forgot — I had a discount coupon. ...Here! — Sister: Aw, I wish you'd noticed sooner. I already ate it. — Older sister: Can't you just give it at checkout? Um... oh, it says to give it when ordering. — Sister: Ugh. It was supposed to be half price — that's 240 yen cheaper for the two of us. — Older sister: It says it's only for one portion. But still, what a waste. Should we ask the staff? — Sister: Let me see... huh? What, this has already expired.
HI: बहन: यह चीज़केक स्वादिष्ट है ना। — बड़ी बहन: हाँ। अरे! चीज़केक की बात हो रही है तो याद आया, मेरे पास एक छूट कूपन था, भूल गई थी। ...देखो! — बहन: अरे यार, जल्दी बताना था। मैं तो खा भी चुकी। — बड़ी बहन: बिल चुकाते समय दे सकते हैं ना? उम्म... अरे, इसमें लिखा है ऑर्डर करते समय देना है। — बहन: उफ़्फ़। यह तो आधी क़ीमत का होना था — दोनों के लिए 240 येन कम हो जाते। — बड़ी बहन: लिखा है सिर्फ़ एक व्यक्ति के लिए। पर फिर भी अफ़सोस है। कर्मचारी से पूछें? — बहन: ज़रा दिखाओ... अरे? यह तो समाप्त हो चुका है।
GU: બહેન: આ ચીઝકેક સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે ને. — મોટી બહેન: હા. અરે! ચીઝકેકની વાત નીકળી તો યાદ આવ્યું, મારી પાસે એક ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ કૂપન હતું, ભૂલી ગઈ હતી. ...જો! — બહેન: અરે યાર, વહેલા કહેવું હતું. મેં તો ખાઈ પણ લીધું. — મોટી બહેન: બિલ ચૂકવતી વખતે આપી શકાય ને? ઉમ્મ... અરે, આમાં લખ્યું છે ઓર્ડર કરતી વખતે આપવાનું છે. — બહેન: ઉફ્ફ. આ તો અડધી કિંમતનું થવાનું હતું — બંને માટે 240 યેન ઓછા થાત. — મોટી બહેન: લખ્યું છે ફક્ત એક વ્યક્તિ માટે. પણ તોય અફસોસ છે. કર્મચારીને પૂછીએ? — બહેન: જરા બતાવ... અરે? આ તો સમય પૂરો થઈ ગયો છે.
  • 1 姉も妹もチーズケーキを注文した。Ane mo imouto mo chiizukeeki o chuumon shita. — Both the older sister and the younger sister ordered cheesecake.
  • 2 この店のチーズケーキは240円である。Kono mise no chiizukeeki wa 240-en de aru. — This store's cheesecake is 240 yen.
  • 3 割引券は会計のときに出せばいい。Waribikiken wa kaikei no toki ni daseba ii. — You can present the discount coupon when paying the bill.
  • 4 割引券は一緒に行った人の分も安くなる。Waribikiken wa issho ni itta hito no bun mo yasuku naru. — The coupon also makes the companion's portion cheaper.
  • 5 今回、この割引券は使えない。Konkai, kono waribikiken wa tsukaenai. — This time, this coupon can't be used.

Why: 240 yen is confirmed as the cheesecake's regular price because it matches Coupon A on the next page (¥240→¥120, exactly a 50%-off/half-price ticket) — the "240円も安くなる" for two people works out to 120 yen off each. Statement 3 is wrong because the sisters correct themselves: it must be given when ordering, not at checkout. Statement 4 is wrong because the ticket says "one portion only" (一人分だけ), so a companion's food isn't discounted. Statement 5 turns out true only because of the twist ending — the ticket had already expired.

Answer: 1 (confirmed — 第1週 answer booklet, PDF index 113: "1日目 (p.12〜p.13) 問1:1"). Both A (breakfast hours excluded, other coupons excluded) and B (prescriptions/cigarettes/postcards/stamps/magazines excluded) restrict which items or times they cover — neither is a blanket, use-on-anything ticket. Option 2 is false (A works at any Berry's branch nationwide); option 3 only fits A, not B (B is a flat 500-yen voucher, not a percentage discount); option 4 only fits A (B has no time restriction, only item restrictions).

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which strategy-box checklist item each question actually turns on:

QuestionChecklist item usedWhat the trap testsTrap to avoid
練習 Q3 いつ出す？ (when to present it) Whether you register that the coupon must be given at ordering, not payment The dialogue itself first guesses "at checkout" and self-corrects — read to the end before answering.
練習 Q4 何人？ (how many people) Whether "one portion only" also covers a companion's order "一人分だけ" (only one portion) sounds generous but explicitly excludes a second diner's food.
問1 どこで？ / いくら？ (where / how much) Finding what's common between two very different ticket types (A = %-off, B = flat-value voucher) Don't assume both tickets work the same way just because they're both "discount documents" — check each ticket's own restriction list.
問2 いつ出す？ (when to present it) Spotting the one plausible-sounding statement that contradicts a printed rule "会計の際に見せればよい" reads naturally for most coupons, but Ticket A's own text overrides that general expectation.
Exam trap: Both A and B look similar at a glance (discount tickets from a shop), but A is a percentage-off ticket restricted by time-of-day, headcount, and combinability, while B is a flat-value voucher restricted only by item category. Never transfer one ticket's rules onto the other just because they're printed on the same page.
