問題1 (Mondai 1) — p.24–25

田中さん（男性）は、今週の土曜日（6月26日）に出張で一晩泊まるホテルを探しています。夜7時半ごろに森山駅に着く予定なので、ホテルは駅から近くて、できるだけ安いのがいいと考えています。また、朝ごはんがついていることを希望しています。Tanaka-san (dansei) wa, konshuu no doyoubi (roku-gatsu nijuuroku-nichi) ni shucchou de hitoban tomaru hoteru o sagashite imasu. Yoru shichi-ji han goro ni Moriyama-eki ni tsuku yotei na node, hoteru wa eki kara chikakute, dekiru dake yasui no ga ii to kangaete imasu. Mata, asagohan ga tsuite iru koto o kibou shite imasu.

Q1

List I — 森山駅周辺 ホテル情報 (Moriyama-eki shuuhen hoteru jouhou)

#ホテル名(hotel)部屋数(rooms)1部屋ごとの料金（S）(single, from)チェックIN/OUT備考(remarks)
Aセントラルホテルみどり野92室S 5,600円〜IN 15:00 / OUT 10:00森山駅徒歩10分。ビジネス・観光に最適。
Bホテルむらかわ46室S 5,800円〜IN 16:00 / OUT 10:00森山駅徒歩1分。
Cターミナルホテル森山134室S 6,900円〜IN 15:00 / OUT 10:00森山駅直結で交通至便。全室無線LAN完備。
Dホテルグリーンシティ110室S 7,200円〜IN 15:00 / OUT 10:00森山駅徒歩3分。ビジネスに最適。

1　リストⅠだけを見た場合、田中さんの条件に最も合うものはどれか。1: Risuto I dake o mita baai, Tanaka-san no jouken ni mottomo au mono wa dore ka.

EN: Looking only at List I, which hotel best matches Tanaka's conditions (near the station, as cheap as possible)?
OptionHotel
1A
2B
3C
4D

Why (booklet-confirmed, [1]: 2): Tanaka wants both proximity AND low price. B (ホテルむらかわ) is only a 1-minute walk from the station — the closest option after C's direct connection — and its rate (¥5,800~) is the second-lowest, just above A. C is the closest structurally (directly connected) but noticeably pricier (¥6,900~); D is close (3 min) but the most expensive (¥7,200~); A is the cheapest (¥5,600~) but a 10-minute walk, too far given how close the other three options are. B best balances "near" and "cheap" together.

Q2

List II — 森山駅周辺の宿泊情報（プラン別）(Moriyama-eki shuuhen no shukuhaku jouhou, puran-betsu)

ホテル名プラン名朝食(breakfast)料金(price/night)備考
ターミナルホテル森山【お日にち限定】格安プランー4,800円※6月22〜25日限定

ホテルむらかわ安さいちばん！素泊まりプランー4,500円素泊まり（食事なし）
ホテルグリーンシティレイトチェックインでオトクプラン○5,300円※18時以降のチェックインの場合に限ります。

2　リストⅡも見た場合、田中さんの条件に合うものはどれか。2: Risuto II mo mita baai, Tanaka-san no jouken ni au mono wa dore ka.

EN: Now also considering List II's discount plans, which one fits Tanaka's conditions (checking in around 7:30pm on Sat. June 26, wants breakfast included, as cheap as possible)?
OptionJapaneseEnglish
1ターミナルホテル森山の「【お日にち限定】格安プラン」Terminal Hotel Moriyama's date-limited discount plan
2セントラルホテルみどり野の「7日前早割プラン」Central Hotel Midorino's 7-day-advance early-bird plan
3ホテルむらかわの「安さいちばん！素泊まりプラン」Hotel Murakawa's cheapest room-only plan
4ホテルグリーンシティの「レイトチェックインでオトクプラン」Hotel Green City's late-check-in deal plan

Why (booklet-confirmed, [2]: 4): Option 1 is restricted to June 22–25, but Tanaka's stay is June 26 — it doesn't apply to his date. Option 3 has no breakfast (素泊まり = room only), failing his stated wish. Option 2 requires booking 7 days in advance, which isn't confirmed as met ("今週の土曜日" suggests a short-notice booking). Option 4 includes breakfast (○) and specifically requires checking in after 18:00 — which matches Tanaka's ~19:30 arrival exactly, at a competitive price (¥5,300) — satisfying every one of his conditions at once.

問題2 (Mondai 2) — p.26

下のハガキは、クリーニング店からのダイレクトメールである。Shita no hagaki wa, kuriiningu-ten kara no dairekuto meeru de aru. — The postcard below is a direct-mail flyer from a dry cleaner.

Q3

Postcard — 衣替え応援セール！しろくまクリーニング (Koromogae ouen seeru! Shirokuma Kuriiningu)

5点までなら10%OFF／6点以上で20%OFF／10点以上で30%OFF
ゆっくりクリーニング（出来上がりは9月）はさらに5%OFF
セール期間：本状が届いた日から20XX年5月31日（日）まで
※本状をご持参ください。セール中ご利用の方には次回の割引券をプレゼント。Go-ten made nara juppaasento ofu / roku-ten ijou de nijuppaasento ofu / juk-ten ijou de sanjuppaasento ofu. Yukkuri kuriiningu (dekiagari wa ku-gatsu) wa sara ni go-paasento ofu. Seeru kikan: honjou ga todoita hi kara nijuu-XX-nen go-gatsu sanjuuichi-nichi (nichiyoubi) made. ※Honjou o go-jisan kudasai. Seeru-chuu go-riyou no kata ni wa jikai no waribikiken o purezento.

注1 衣替え：洋服を季節に合わせて替えること Chuu 1: koromogae: youfuku o kisetsu ni awasete kaeru koto — "changing one's wardrobe with the season" 　·　注2 〜点：クリーニングに出すものの数え方 Chuu 2: ~ten: kuriiningu ni dasu mono no kazoekata — "点 is the counter for items sent to be cleaned"

3　正しいのはどれか。3: Tadashii no wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
19月はクリーニング料金が5％OFFになる。Ku-gatsu wa kuriiningu ryoukin ga go-paasento ofu ni naru.Cleaning fees are 5% off in September.
2このセールの始まりは人によって違う。Kono seeru no hajimari wa hito ni yotte chigau.This sale's start date differs from person to person.
3受け取りが3ヵ月先でもいい人は5割引になる。Uketori ga san-kagetsu-saki demo ii hito wa go-wari-biki ni naru.People fine with pickup 3 months later get a 50% discount.
4案内状を見せるだけで割引券がもらえる。Annaijou o miseru dake de waribikiken ga moraeru.Just showing the flyer gets you a discount coupon.

Why (booklet-confirmed, [3]: 2): The sale period is defined as "本状が届いた日から" (from the day this postcard arrives at your home) until a fixed end date — since postcards arrive on different days for different people, each recipient's sale actually starts on a different date, matching option 2. Option 1 misreads the extra 5% off for the slow "ゆっくりクリーニング" service (finished in September) as a blanket September discount. Option 3 invents a "50% off for 3-month pickup" that doesn't exist — the slow service only adds 5%, on top of the item-count discount, not 50%. Option 4 is wrong because the coupon is a reward for using the service during the sale, not simply for showing the flyer.

OptionDiscount

Why (booklet-confirmed, [4]: 2, with the booklet's own explanatory note): The booklet itself notes: "シャツもズボンもコートも服の種類には関係なく数えればいい" — shirts, pants, and coats all count as one 点 each regardless of clothing type. So 3 shirts + 2 pants + 1 coat = 6点 total, which falls in the "6点以上で20%OFF" tier — 2割引.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Balance two conditions (distance + price) rather than optimizing either alone The single cheapest option (A) and the single closest option (C) are both traps — the best-balanced option (B) wins Don't default to the lowest price or the shortest distance alone — "近くて、できるだけ安い" asks for both together.
Q2 Check every plan's fine-print restriction against the specific scenario (date, arrival time, booking lead time) Each of the 4 plans fails on exactly one condition except the correct one A plan that's cheap or has breakfast can still fail on a date range, lead time, or missing breakfast — check ALL conditions, not just price.
Q3 Distinguish who the discount/date applies to from a fixed calendar date "本状が届いた日から" makes the sale's start person-specific, not a fixed date for everyone Don't assume a sale's start date is universal — read exactly what event triggers the countdown.
Q4 Apply a single counting rule (点 = 1 per garment, regardless of type) before checking the discount tier 3+2+1 = 6 items total, crossing the "6点以上" threshold into the 20% tier Don't split the count by garment type (shirts vs. pants vs. coats) — the postcard counts every garment the same way.
