Notes
This day opens with a ✿ tip about reading rent listings (always add the management fee to the base rent to get the real monthly cost), then a themed vocabulary box of words that show up in apartment/mansion flyers, then a short 練習 dialogue, then the main 問題 using a rental listing table plus a train-line map.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

家賃は管理費を入れた金額で考えようYachin wa kanrihi o ireta kingaku de kangaeyou

Always think of the rent as the total amount including the management fee (管理費) — a listing's headline rent number often looks cheaper than what you'll actually pay every month, because 管理費 is billed separately but is just as mandatory.

HI: किराया देखते समय हमेशा प्रबंधन शुल्क (管理費) को भी जोड़कर सोचें — लिस्टिंग में दिखने वाला किराया अक्सर असल मासिक खर्च से कम लगता है, क्योंकि 管理費 अलग से लिया जाता है पर उतना ही ज़रूरी है। GU: ભાડું જોતી વખતે હંમેશા મેનેજમેન્ટ ફી (管理費) પણ ઉમેરીને વિચારો — યાદીમાં દેખાતું ભાડું ઘણી વાર ખરેખરા માસિક ખર્ચ કરતાં ઓછું લાગે છે, કારણ કે 管理費 અલગથી લેવાય છે પણ એટલી જ જરૂરી છે.

練習 (Warm-up — answer confirmed on the next page)

学生Ａ：俺は、敷金・礼金なしで、月3万5千円ぐらいまで……。あるかな……。Gakusei A: Ore wa, shikikin/reikin nashi de, tsuki san-man go-sen en gurai made... Aru ka na...

Student A and Student B are looking at apartment listings together. A wants no deposit/key money, budget up to about ¥35,000/month. B has the same conditions, finds a listing with a corner room, A/C, near the station — but A points out the nearest station has no express-train stop, so A prefers an express-stop station. B counters that a non-express station might be cheaper/nicer. B then rejects a very cheap listing because it requires a bus (expensive fare, long walk if on foot, bad in rain) — B wants to stay near the station, at most a 10-minute walk. A says walking distance doesn't bother him/her; a bicycle could be bought.

#StatementTrue/False
1AさんもBさんも敷金・礼金なしの部屋を探している。A-san mo B-san mo shikikin/reikin nashi no heya o sagashite iru. — Both A and B are looking for a room with no deposit/key money.True
2Bさんはバスが苦手なので、バスを使わないところがいい。B-san wa basu ga nigate na node, basu o tsukawanai tokoro ga ii. — B dislikes buses, so prefers a place without one.False
3Aさんは特急の止まる駅を利用するところに住みたい。A-san wa tokkyuu no tomaru eki o riyou suru tokoro ni sumitai. — A wants to live somewhere served by an express-stop station.True
4Bさんは10分でも歩くのは嫌だ。B-san wa juppun demo aruku no wa iya da. — B dislikes walking even 10 minutes.False
5Aさんは自転車があるので、遠くても気にならない。A-san wa jitensha ga aru node, tookute mo ki ni naranai. — A already has a bicycle, so distance doesn't matter.False

Why: B's objection to the bus listing is about cost/inconvenience (expensive fare, a long walk, trouble in the rain) — not a personal dislike of buses — so #2 mischaracterizes the reason. B explicitly sets a 10-minute walking limit as acceptable ("歩くとしても10分前後までだな"), so #4 is false. A only says he/she "could buy" a bicycle (a hypothetical future purchase), not that one is already owned, so #5 is false. #1 and #3 both restate things A and B say directly.

2 Passage Questions

The main 問題 (p.19) gives a table of 6 rental listings ("みどり市内 賃貸アパート・マンション情報") along with a small map of the private railway line showing which stations are express-stop (◎) stations, then asks which listings best match A's and B's conditions from the 練習 dialogue above. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

The map shows 私鉄川島線 (a private railway line) running 北山方面 ← 青田・寺町・白田・黒田・下川 → 川島中央方面, with 青田駅 and 黒田駅 marked ◎ (特急停車駅, express-stop stations); 寺町駅 and 白田駅 are not express stops.

EN: Looking only at List I (the table above), which listing(s) best match A's wish (no deposit/key money, budget up to ~¥35,000/month total, express-stop station)?
OptionListings

33と4 (3 to 4)

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問1: 1): A wants no deposit/key money, an express-stop station, and a total monthly cost (rent + management fee) around ¥35,000 or under. Listings 1 (黒田駅, ◎, 3.2万+2,000円=3.4万) and 6 (青田駅, ◎, 2.85万+1,500円≈3.0万) are both on express-stop stations, have no deposit/key money, and fit comfortably within A's budget. Listing 2 is far over budget (5.55万); listing 5 is also over budget (5.7万) despite being on an express line; listings 3 and 4 are on 白田駅, which is not an express stop — they fit B's needs (see Q2), not A's.

Same listing table as Q1

EN: Which listing(s) best match B's wish (no deposit/key money, prefers not to rely on a bus, at most about a 10-minute walk from the station, is fine with — or even prefers — a non-express station if it's cheaper/nicer)?
OptionListings

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問2: 2): Listings 3 (白田駅, 11分, no deposit/key money) and 4 (白田駅, 5分, corner room + A/C, no deposit/key money) are both an easy walk from a non-express station B was open to, need no bus, and fit within B's ~10-minute walking limit and budget. Listing 1 is 25 minutes on foot from the station (far beyond B's limit) and listing 6 is 27 minutes (also far beyond B's limit) — both effectively require the kind of long walk or bus trip B rejected.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
練習 Careful attribution of who said what Distinguishing B's stated reason (cost/rain/distance) from an unstated one (personal dislike of buses) Don't infer a reason (#2 "B is bad with buses") that sounds plausible but was never actually said.
Q1 Combine two conditions from the dialogue (budget total + express-stop station) with the table Total cost = rent + management fee, checked against both the price column and the map's ◎ marks Don't compare rent alone — management fee changes which listings are actually within A's ¥35,000 budget.
Q2 Match B's walking-distance limit against the table's 徒歩 column "No bus" + "~10 minutes on foot" rules out anything with a long walk, even if the price is great Listing 6 looks attractive (cheap, express station) but its 27-minute walk fails B's stated limit — don't let price alone decide.
