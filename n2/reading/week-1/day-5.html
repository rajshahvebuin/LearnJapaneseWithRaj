Notes
This day's theme is 利用案内 (user/usage guides) — the kind of notice posted at a facility (here, a bicycle parking lot) explaining hours, fees, and rules. The ✿ tip stresses reading the notices about facility use carefully, since fee tables often split into a flat/tiered rate and a separate discounted rate.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

施設などの利用に関する注意事項をよく読もうShisetsu nado no riyou ni kansuru chuui jikou o yoku yomou

Read the notices about using a facility (parking lot, gym, library, etc.) carefully — the fee table usually has more than one column (e.g. a monthly/定期 rate vs. a per-use/一時 rate), and there's often a hidden student or member discount that only applies if you ask or show ID.

HI: किसी सुविधा (पार्किंग, जिम, लाइब्रेरी आदि) के इस्तेमाल संबंधी सूचनाएँ ध्यान से पढ़ें — शुल्क तालिका में अक्सर एक से ज़्यादा कॉलम होते हैं (जैसे मासिक दर बनाम एकल-उपयोग दर), और अक्सर एक छिपी हुई छात्र या सदस्य छूट होती है जो तभी लागू होती है जब आप पूछें या पहचान-पत्र दिखाएँ। GU: કોઈ સુવિધા (પાર્કિંગ, જિમ, લાઇબ્રેરી વગેરે) ના ઉપયોગ અંગેની સૂચનાઓ ધ્યાનથી વાંચો — ફી ટેબલમાં ઘણી વાર એક કરતાં વધુ કૉલમ હોય છે (જેમ કે માસિક દર વિરુદ્ધ એક-વખતના ઉપયોગની દર), અને ઘણી વાર એક છુપાયેલ વિદ્યાર્થી કે સભ્ય છૂટ હોય છે જે ફક્ત પૂછવાથી કે ઓળખપત્ર બતાવવાથી લાગુ પડે છે.

例えば駐輪場の利用案内にはこんな言葉が出てきます。Tatoeba chuurinjou no riyou annai ni wa konna kotoba ga dete kimasu. — Words that typically appear in a bicycle-parking usage guide:

練習 (Warm-up — answer confirmed on the next page)

妹：お兄ちゃん、いつも自転車どこに止めてる？Imouto: Onii-chan, itsumo jitensha doko ni tometeru?

A younger sister asks her brother where he always parks his bicycle. He says: usually the free spot in front of the station; when that's full, the north exit lot. She's surprised — she thought the north exit was paid. He explains: ¥100 for 6 hours, but the first 2 hours are free, and it's close and convenient. She admits she didn't know that — since the station-front lot was full that day, she had to go all the way back to the park's free lot and nearly was late. He teases her for being inefficient and suggests a monthly (月極) contract, noting a student discount applies. She agrees, since she goes almost every day.

#StatementTrue/False
1妹は北口の駐輪場を利用したことがない。Imouto wa kitaguchi no chuurinjou o riyou shita koto ga nai. — The sister has never used the north-exit lot.True
2兄はいつでも無料駐輪場を利用している。Ani wa itsudemo muryou chuurinjou o riyou shite iru. — The brother always uses a free lot.False
3兄も妹も駅前の無料駐輪場を利用したことがある。Ani mo imouto mo ekimae no muryou chuurinjou o riyou shita koto ga aru. — Both brother and sister have used the free station-front lot.True
4兄は妹に学生割引の使える駐輪場をすすめている。Ani wa imouto ni gakusei waribiki no tsukaeru chuurinjou o susumete iru. — The brother is recommending a lot where a student discount applies.True
5北口の駐輪場は、駅ビルで買い物をすれば8時間100円で止められる。Kitaguchi no chuurinjou wa, ekibiru de kaimono o sureba hachi-jikan hyaku-en de tomerareru. — The north-exit lot is ¥100 for 8 hours if you shop at the station building.False

Why: The sister didn't know the north exit was paid — meaning she'd never used it before. Since she went straight to the station-front lot by habit and only detoured to the park's free lot because the station-front one was full that day, she (like her brother) is a regular user of the station-front free lot. The brother's suggestion (月極 + 学割) directly confirms #4. #2 is false because the brother himself said he uses the paid north-exit lot when the free one is full. #5 invents a shopping-discount condition never mentioned — the real deal is simply ¥100 for 6 hours with the first 2 hours free.

2 Passage Questions

The main 問題 (p.21) gives a real bicycle-parking usage notice ("みどり駅北口駐輪場") with hours, a fee table (定期利用/月極 vs. 一時利用/時間貸, split by bicycle vs. moped), and posted rules. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Q1

Notice — みどり駅北口駐輪場 (Midori-eki Kitaguchi Chuurinjou)

ご利用時間：5時〜翌1時（1〜5時の間は自転車の出し入れができません。）Go-riyou jikan: go-ji kara yoku ichi-ji (ichi-ji kara go-ji no aida wa jitensha no dashiire ga dekimasen.)

定期利用〔月極〕の場合は前の月の25日までに申込書を駐輪場窓口に提出してください。お申し込みの際は料金（契約月数分）および身分証明書（学割を受ける場合は学生証）・印鑑が必要です。Teiki riyou (tsukigime) no baai wa mae no tsuki no nijuugo-nichi made ni moushikomisho o chuurinjou madoguchi ni teishutsu shite kudasai. Moushikomi no sai wa ryoukin (keiyaku tsukisuubun) oyobi mibun shoumeisho (gakuwari o ukeru baai wa gakuseishou), inkan ga hitsuyou desu.

料金(fee)定期利用〔月極〕(monthly)一時利用〔時間貸〕(pay-per-use)
自転車jitensha (bicycle)2,000円（学割 1,500円）最初の2時間は無料／以降6時間あたり100円
原付（50cc以下）gentsuki (moped, 50cc or under)2,600円（学割 2,000円）最初の2時間は無料／以降6時間あたり200円

Posted rules include: no littering; always lock up to prevent theft; the lot takes no responsibility for accidents/theft/damage; unauthorized parking or anything left over 2 days in the pay-per-use area is subject to removal.

問1　正しいものはどれか。Toi 1: Tadashii mono wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1月極で利用したい場合には、市役所に申し込まなければならない。Tsukigime de riyou shitai baai ni wa, shiyakusho ni moushikomanakereba naranai.To use it monthly, you must apply at city hall.
24月から定期利用したい場合は3月末までに申し込まなければならない。Shi-gatsu kara teiki riyou shitai baai wa san-gatsu-matsu made ni moushikomanakereba naranai.To start a monthly lease in April, you must apply by the end of March.
3一時利用で1時間止めた場合は無料である。Ichiji riyou de ichi-jikan tometa baai wa muryou de aru.If you park for 1 hour on the pay-per-use rate, it's free.
4時間貸で24時間止めた場合、自転車が別の場所に移動される。Jikangashi de nijuuyo-jikan tometa baai, jitensha ga betsu no basho ni idou sareru.If parked 24 hours on the pay-per-use rate, the bicycle gets moved elsewhere.

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問1: 3): The notice states the first 2 hours of pay-per-use parking are free, so 1 hour costs nothing — option 3 is correct. Applications go to the 駐輪場窓口 (the lot's own counter), not city hall generally (option 1 is wrong — the lot is merely run by 都市計画課, but applications go to the parking lot's window, not "市役所" as a blanket requirement). The deadline is the 25th of the previous month, so for an April start you'd apply by March 25th, not "the end of March" (option 2 is wrong on the exact date). Removal applies after 2 days of being left in the pay-per-use area, not automatically at 24 hours (option 4 misstates the threshold).

OptionAmount
12,000円

36,000円

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問2: 3, with the booklet's own explanatory note): The moped's student (学割) monthly rate is ¥2,000, and the notice says the fee required at application time covers the number of months contracted (契約月数分) — so for 3 months: ¥2,000 × 3 = ¥6,000. ¥2,000 (option 1) is just one month's student rate; ¥2,600 (option 2) is one month's non-student rate; ¥7,800 (option 4) would be 3 months at the non-student rate (2,600×3=7,800), which doesn't apply here since the question specifies a student.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
練習 Track who has/hasn't used which lot The sister's surprise at the north exit being paid implies she's never used it, but she's a regular at the free station-front lot Don't assume "never used the paid lot" means "never used any lot" — she still uses the free one regularly.
Q1 Match each option's specific number/threshold against the notice's exact wording "25th of the previous month," "2 hours free," "2 days before removal" are all precise thresholds, not round numbers Options 2 and 4 sound almost right (deadlines/thresholds nearby) — always check the notice's exact figure, not an approximation.
Q2 Combine the correct row (原付) + correct column (学割/定期) + multiply by contract length Fee tables cross two axes (vehicle type × student/non-student) plus a multiplier (number of months) ¥7,800 is a trap for forgetting the student discount applies; ¥2,000/¥2,600 are traps for forgetting to multiply by 3 months.
