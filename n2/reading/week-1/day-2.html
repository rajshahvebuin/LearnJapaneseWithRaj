1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

期間や特典を受ける条件に注意！Kikan ya tokuten o ukeru jouken ni chuui! — Pay attention to the dates and the conditions for receiving bonuses!

例えばセールのお知らせなら、こんな内容が書いてあります。Tatoeba seeru no oshirase nara, konna naiyou ga kaite arimasu. ("If it's a sale notice, for example, this is the kind of content that's written on it.")

  • 期間は？Kikan wa? ("What's the period?") → 日時、閉店日もチェック。Nichiji, heiten-bi mo chekku. — Check the date/time, and closing days too.
  • 対象となる商品は？Taishou to naru shouhin wa? ("Which products are covered?") → 新製品は？○%OFF、○割引、半額…Shinseihin wa? ○-paasento OFF, ○-waribiki, hangaku... — Are new products included? X% OFF, X-discount, half price...
  • どのぐらい安くなる？Dono gurai yasuku naru? ("How much cheaper?") → 店頭表示価格からさらに割引？買い物の合計額によって割引率が異なる？Tentou hyouji kakaku kara sara ni waribiki? Kaimono no goukei-gaku ni yotte waribiki-ritsu ga kotonaru? — A further discount off the price already displayed in-store? Does the discount rate change depending on the total amount purchased?
    HI: दुकान में लगे मूल्य से आगे और छूट मिलेगी, या कुल ख़रीद राशि के हिसाब से छूट अलग-अलग होगी? GU: દુકાનમાં લગાવેલ ભાવ કરતાં વધુ ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ મળશે, કે કુલ ખરીદીની રકમ પ્રમાણે ડિસ્કાઉન્ટ અલગ-અલગ હશે?
  • 当日必要なものは？Toujitsu hitsuyou na mono wa? ("What's needed on the day?") → 届いたハガキ？会員証？Todoita hagaki? Kaiin-shou? — The postcard that arrived? A membership card?

This week's running joke: 安い！30%で買えるんだ！Yasui! Sanjuppaasento de kaeru n da! — "Cheap! I can buy it for 30%!" ／ 違うよ！30%引きを！Chigau yo! Sanjuppaasento-biki o! — "No! [It means] 30% OFF!" — a reminder that ○%で買える (buy it for X% of the price) and ○%引き (X% off the price) mean very different discounts, and DM flyers rely on you telling them apart.

✿ 練習 (Renshuu — Practice)

次の会話文を読んで、後の文から正しいものを選ぼう。Tsugi no kaiwabun o yonde, ato no bun kara tadashii mono o erabou. Answer confirmed printed on the next page (p.15): 4・5.

夫：今日も暑くなりそうだなあ……お、セールのハガキか。Otto: Kyou mo atsuku narisou da naa... O, seeru no hagaki ka.

妻：うん、昨日来てた。毎年来るけど。Tsuma: Un, kinou kiteta. Maitoshi kuru kedo.

夫：なんだ、3日間だけか。……しかも、平日。うちなんか行けるわけないよな。Otto: Nanda, mikka-kan dake ka. ...Shikamo, heijitsu. Uchi nanka ikeru wake nai yo na.

妻：そうなのよ。土日だったらね……。あ、もうこんな時間。はい、お弁当。えっと、今日はちょっと遅くなるけど、あなたよりは早く帰れると思うから。Tsuma: Sou na no yo. Do-nichi dattara ne... A, mou konna jikan. Hai, obentou. Etto, kyou wa chotto osoku naru kedo, anata yori wa hayaku kaereru to omou kara.

夫：了解。じゃ、行ってくる。Otto: Ryoukai. Ja, ittekuru.

妻：いってらっしゃい。私も急がないと。お弁当持ったし、窓は閉めたし……。Tsuma: Itterasshai. Watashi mo isoganai to. Obentou mottashi, mado wa shimeta shi...

EN: Husband: Looks like it'll be hot again today... oh, a sale postcard? — Wife: Yeah, it came yesterday. It comes every year though. — Husband: What, only three days?... And on weekdays too. There's no way we could go. — Wife: That's right. If only it were the weekend... oh, look at the time already. Here's your lunch. Um, I'll be a little late today, but I think I'll get home before you. — Husband: Got it. Well, I'm off. — Wife: Take care. I'd better hurry too — I've got the lunch, and I closed the windows...
HI: पति: आज भी गर्मी लगने वाली है... अरे, सेल का कार्ड आया है क्या? — पत्नी: हाँ, कल आया था। हर साल आता है। — पति: क्या, बस 3 दिन का है?... वो भी हफ़्ते के दिनों में। हम तो जा ही नहीं सकते। — पत्नी: सही कहा। वीकेंड होता तो... अरे, इतना समय हो गया। लो, टिफ़िन। हाँ, आज मुझे थोड़ी देर होगी, पर लगता है मैं तुमसे पहले घर पहुँच जाऊँगी। — पति: ठीक है। चलता हूँ। — पत्नी: संभल के जाना। मुझे भी जल्दी करनी है — टिफ़िन ले लिया, खिड़कियाँ भी बंद कर दीं...
GU: પતિ: આજે પણ ગરમી લાગશે લાગે છે... અરે, સેલનું કાર્ડ આવ્યું છે? — પત્ની: હા, ગઈકાલે આવ્યું હતું. દર વર્ષે આવે છે. — પતિ: શું, ફક્ત 3 દિવસનું છે?... વળી અઠવાડિયાના દિવસોમાં. આપણે તો જઈ જ ન શકીએ. — પત્ની: સાચી વાત. વીકેન્ડ હોત તો... અરે, આટલો સમય થઈ ગયો. લે, ટિફિન. હા, આજે મને થોડું મોડું થશે, પણ લાગે છે હું તારા પહેલાં ઘરે પહોંચી જઈશ. — પતિ: ભલે. જાઉં છું. — પત્ની: સંભાળીને જજે. મારે પણ ઉતાવળ કરવી પડશે — ટિફિન લઈ લીધું, બારીઓ પણ બંધ કરી દીધી...
  • 1 今週末、3日間セールがある。Konshuumatsu, mikka-kan seeru ga aru. — This weekend, there's a 3-day sale.
  • 2 夫は今日帰宅が遅くなるかもしれない。Otto wa kyou kitaku ga osoku naru kamo shirenai. — The husband might come home late today.
  • 3 妻は今日仕事の帰りにセールに行くつもりだ。Tsuma wa kyou shigoto no kaeri ni seeru ni iku tsumori da. — The wife plans to go to the sale on her way home from work today.
  • 4 夫はセールに興味がある。Otto wa seeru ni kyoumi ga aru. — The husband is interested in the sale.
  • 5 二人はセールに行きたくても行けない。Futari wa seeru ni ikitakute mo ikenai. — Even though they want to go to the sale, the two of them can't.

Why: Statement 1 is false — the sale is 3 weekday days, not this weekend (the husband explicitly wishes it were 土日). Statement 2 is a classic N2 trap: the wife says she'll be a little late but still get home before him, which sounds like it implies he'll be later still — but the dialogue never actually states anything about the husband's schedule changing today, so it can't be confirmed and isn't picked. Statement 3 is false — no plan to visit the sale is ever mentioned. Statements 4 (he keeps engaging with the topic — asking about it, reacting to the dates) and 5 (both sigh that a weekday sale is useless to two working people) are supported directly by the dialogue.

Answer: 4 (confirmed — booklet: "問1:4"). The flyer explicitly says 新商品も...20%OFF ("new products too...20% OFF") — new items ARE included, so option 4 is the incorrect statement being asked for. Options 1–3 all restate the flyer accurately: discounted items still get the extra 20%, 10,000-yen-plus purchases get 30% off, and 特典2 requires bringing 本状 (this notice) to claim the gift.

Answer: 1 (7,000円) (confirmed — booklet: "問2:1", with the worked calculation: "割引商品も店頭表示価格（＝10,000円）から」「1万円以上ご購入の際は30%OFF」とあるので10,000円×70%＝7,000円" — "since even discounted items count from the displayed store price (=¥10,000), and purchases of ¥10,000+ get 30% OFF, that's ¥10,000 × 70% = ¥7,000."). The starting point is the already-reduced 特価¥10,000 tag (not the crossed-out ¥14,000) — this single item's price exactly meets the ¥10,000 threshold, so the 30%-off tier applies, not just the base 20%.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — compare which strategy-box checklist item unlocks each question, and note the recurring "don't over-infer" trap:

QuestionChecklist item usedWhat the trap testsTrap to avoid
練習 Q2 (none — trap item) Distinguishing what the text implies from what it actually states "She'll get home before him" sounds like proof he's late, but the dialogue never confirms anything unusual about his schedule today — resist the urge to over-infer.
問1 対象となる商品は？ (which products are covered) Whether "新商品も" (new products too) was read correctly もmarks "new products ALSO" as included — missing that も flips the statement's truth value.
問2 どのぐらい安くなる？ (how much cheaper — layered discounts) Which price is the real starting point for a further discount The crossed-out ¥14,000 is a distractor; ¥10,000 is the "店頭表示価格" (displayed price) that all further discounts are calculated from.
Idiom trap: ○%で買える ("can buy it for X%") and ○%引き ("X% off") describe opposite-sized discounts — buying something "for 30%" of its price is a much bigger discount than getting "30% off." DM flyers count on shoppers reading these carelessly.
