1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

助詞に注意して文の意味を予想しようJoshi ni chuui shite bun no imi o yosou shiyou — Let's guess the meaning of the sentence by paying attention to the particles!

例えばこんなふうに助詞で終わる見出しも多いです。Tatoeba konna fuu ni joshi de owaru midashi mo ooi desu. ("For example, many headlines end on a bare particle like this.") The dangling particle tells you what's coming, even though the verb itself is cut off: 助詞から動詞を推測しよう！Joshi kara doushi o suisoku shiyou! — Guess the verb from the particle!

  • 製品事故　安全基準見直しもSeihin Jiko — Anzen Kijun Minaoshi mo ("Product accident: a review of safety standards, too")
    → Full sentence: 安全基準の見直しも検討されている。Anzen kijun no minaoshi mo kentou sarete iru.
    EN: A revision of the safety standards is also being discussed. HI: सुरक्षा मानकों में बदलाव पर भी विचार किया जा रहा है। GU: સલામતી ધોરણોમાં ફેરફાર અંગે પણ વિચારણા થઈ રહી છે.
    The trailing も ("also/too") signals there's more than one response under discussion — here it hints "a review is also being considered [alongside other measures]," so the missing verb is something like 検討されている (being considered).
  • ABCカンパニー会長　退任へABC Kanpanii Kaichou — Tainin e ("ABC Company chairman: toward resignation")
    → Full sentence: 退任という方向へ動いている。Tainin to iu houkou e ugoite iru.
    EN: The general sentiment is leaning towards him resigning. HI: मामला उनके इस्तीफ़े की दिशा में बढ़ रहा है। GU: મામલો તેમના રાજીનામાની દિશામાં આગળ વધી રહ્યો છે.
    へ ("toward") always signals movement/direction — a headline ending on へ means something is heading that way, not that it's already settled; the missing verb is a soft "moving toward" (動いている), not a hard "has resigned."
  • 経済　他国よりも国内救済をKeizai — Takoku yori mo Kokunai Kyuusai o ("Economy: domestic relief over other countries'")
    → Full sentence: 他の国よりも国内経済の救済をすべきだ。Hoka no kuni yori mo kokunai keizai no kyuusai o subeki da.
    EN: The domestic economy should be saved rather than those of other countries. HI: दूसरे देशों की बजाय घरेलू अर्थव्यवस्था को बचाया जाना चाहिए। GU: બીજા દેશો કરતાં ઘરેલુ અર્થતંત્રને બચાવવું જોઈએ.
    を marks a direct object still waiting for its verb — a headline ending on を almost always implies a strong recommendation/demand verb like 〜べきだ (should do) or 求める (to call for).

✿ 練習 (Renshuu — Practice)

次の会話文を読んで、後の文から正しいものを選ぼう。Tsugi no kaiwabun o yonde, ato no bun kara tadashii mono o erabou. ("Read the following dialogue, then choose the correct statements below.") Answer confirmed printed on the next page (p.81): 2・4.

客：あら、もうお中元の商品が並んでるのね。Kyaku: Ara, mou o-chuugen no shouhin ga narande'ru no ne.

店員：いらっしゃいませ。今年は「エコ」をテーマに当店でしかお求めになれないギフトをご用意させていただいております。ぜひ、ご利用くださいませ。Ten'in: Irasshaimase. Kotoshi wa "eko" o teema ni touten de shika o-motome ni narenai gifuto o go-youi sasete itadaite orimasu. Zehi, go-riyou kudasaimase.

客：エコだなんて、安いものという感じがしない？ 贈り物としてどうかしら……。Kyaku: Eko da nante, yasui mono to iu kanji ga shinai? Okurimono to shite dou kashira...

店員：エコと申しますのは、環境に配慮し注1、また産地や素材注2、製法などが特別なもの、という意味でございますので、手間をかけた注3当店自慢の商品でもございまして、贈り主様の意識の高さが相手様に伝わることと存じます。Ten'in: Eko to moushimasu no wa, kankyou ni hairyo shi (chuu 1), mata sanchi ya sozai (chuu 2), seihou nado ga tokubetsu na mono, to iu imi de gozaimasu node, tema o kaketa (chuu 3) touten jiman no shouhin de mo gozaimashite, okurinushi-sama no ishiki no takasa ga aite-sama ni tsutawaru koto to zonjimasu.

EN: Customer: Oh, the mid-year gift items are already out, aren't they. — Clerk: Welcome! This year, themed around "eco," we've prepared gifts you can only get at our store. Please do take a look. — Customer: "Eco" doesn't exactly sound premium, does it? I wonder if it's really suitable as a gift... — Clerk: What we mean by "eco" is that the item shows consideration for the environment, and that its production area, materials, and manufacturing method are all something special — so these are products our store takes real pride in, having put in extra care and effort, and we believe the thoughtfulness of the giver will come through to the recipient.
HI: ग्राहक: अरे, मध्य-वर्ष के उपहार वाले सामान तो पहले से ही सज गए हैं। — कर्मचारी: स्वागत है! इस साल "इको" थीम पर हमने ऐसे उपहार तैयार किए हैं जो सिर्फ़ हमारी दुकान पर ही मिलेंगे। ज़रूर देखिए। — ग्राहक: "इको" सुनकर तो सस्ता-सा एहसास होता है ना? उपहार के लिए ठीक रहेगा क्या... — कर्मचारी: "इको" का मतलब है कि यह सामान पर्यावरण का ध्यान रखता है, और इसका उत्पादन क्षेत्र, सामग्री, बनाने की विधि — सब कुछ ख़ास है। इसलिए यह हमारी दुकान का गर्व करने लायक़, मेहनत से बना उत्पाद है, और देने वाले की सोच-समझ पाने वाले तक ज़रूर पहुँचेगी।
GU: ગ્રાહક: અરે, મધ્ય-વર્ષની ભેટની ચીજવસ્તુઓ તો પહેલેથી જ ગોઠવાઈ ગઈ છે. — કર્મચારી: સ્વાગત છે! આ વર્ષે "ઇકો" થીમ પર અમે એવી ભેટો તૈયાર કરી છે જે ફક્ત અમારી દુકાનમાં જ મળશે. જરૂર જુઓ. — ગ્રાહક: "ઇકો" સાંભળીને તો સસ્તું લાગે છે ને? ભેટ માટે યોગ્ય રહેશે કે કેમ... — કર્મચારી: "ઇકો" નો અર્થ છે કે આ વસ્તુ પર્યાવરણનું ધ્યાન રાખે છે, અને તેનું ઉત્પાદન ક્ષેત્ર, સામગ્રી, બનાવવાની રીત — બધું જ ખાસ છે. એટલે આ અમારી દુકાનનું ગર્વ કરવા લાયક, મહેનતથી બનેલું ઉત્પાદન છે, અને આપનારની સમજદારી મેળવનાર સુધી જરૂર પહોંચશે.

注1 配慮する：to give some considerations Chuu 1: hairyo suru　·　注2 素材：materials/ingredients Chuu 2: sozai　·　注3 手間をかける：to spend more time and effort Chuu 3: tema o kakeru

  • 1 エコというのはエコノミー、すなわち経済的だという意味だ。Eko to iu no wa ekonomii, sunawachi keizaiteki da to iu imi da. — "Eco" means "economy," i.e. inexpensive.
  • 2 エコというのは環境に配慮するという意味だ。Eko to iu no wa kankyou ni hairyo suru to iu imi da. — "Eco" means being considerate of the environment.
  • 3 エコをテーマにした商品はどこでも買うことができる。Eko o teema ni shita shouhin wa doko demo kau koto ga dekiru. — Products themed around "eco" can be bought anywhere.
  • 4 この店では今年は「エコ」をテーマにしたギフトを用意している。Kono mise de wa kotoshi wa "eko" o teema ni shita gifuto o youi shite iru. — This store has prepared gifts themed around "eco" this year.
  • 5 この店では素材や製法にかかる費用をおさえて商品を買いやすい値段にしている。Kono mise de wa sozai ya seihou ni kakaru hiyou o osaete shouhin o kaiyasui nedan ni shite iru. — This store keeps material/manufacturing costs down to make its products affordably priced.

Why: The clerk directly defines エコ as 環境に配慮し...という意味 ("means being considerate of the environment"), confirming statement 2 and refuting statement 1 (nothing about "economy/cheap" — in fact the customer's worry that "eco sounds cheap" is exactly what the clerk is correcting). The opening line ("today's mid-year gift items with an 'eco' theme, only available at our store") confirms statement 4, while directly contradicting statement 3 (でしかお求めになれない = "can only be obtained here," not "anywhere"). Statement 5 inverts the clerk's actual pitch — 手間をかけた ("extra time/effort spent") implies higher cost, not a cost-cutting affordable price.

2 Passage Questions

問題：次の新聞記事を読んで、後の問いに答えなさい。Mondai: Tsugi no shinbun kiji o yonde, ato no toi ni kotaenasai. ("Read the following newspaper article and answer the questions that follow.") From p.81. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Headline

お中元もエコを主力にO-chuugen mo Eko o Shuryoku ni

"Mid-year gifts, too, focus on 'eco'" — お中元 is the traditional mid-summer gift-giving season in Japan (a counterpart to the year-end お歳暮), a major sales event for department stores.

Article

お中元商戦を目前に、百貨店の○○と△△は目玉商品を公開した。両百貨店とも、「エコ」をテーマに商品を並べ、産地や素材へのこだわり注1を強調している。○○百貨店はお中元用商品2500品から主力商品として25品を選び、展示した。有機農法注2によるジュース、レストランなどで余った食材をエサとして飼育した豚の肉などもあった。△△百貨店でも地下入り口に「人と地球にやさしいグリーンギフト」と10品並べ、農薬を減らして栽培した注3野菜の冷製スープなどの商品を主力としている。両百貨店とも「ほかでは買えない」ものを演出し、客に財布のひもをゆるめてもらうのがねらいだ。*O-chuugen shousen o mokuzen ni, hyakkaten no ○○ to △△ wa medama shouhin o koukai shita. Ryou-hyakkaten tomo, "eko" o teema ni shouhin o narabe, sanchi ya sozai e no kodawari (chuu 1) o kyouchou shite iru. ○○ hyakkaten wa o-chuugen-you shouhin ni-sen-go-hyaku-hin kara shuryoku shouhin to shite ni-juu-go-hin o erabi, tenji shita. Yuuki nouhou (chuu 2) ni yoru juusu, resutoran nado de amatta shokuzai o esa to shite shiiku shita buta no niku nado mo atta. △△ hyakkaten de mo chika iriguchi ni "hito to chikyuu ni yasashii guriin gifuto" to juppin narabe, nouyaku o herashite saibai shita (chuu 3) yasai no reisei suupu nado no shouhin o shuryoku to shite iru. Ryou-hyakkaten tomo "hoka de wa kaenai" mono o enshutsu shi, kyaku ni saifu no himo o yurumete morau no ga nerai da.

注1 こだわり：particular preferences kodawari　·　注2 有機農法：organic farming yuuki nouhou　·　注3 栽培する：to grow saibai suru

* 客に財布のひもをゆるめてもらうのがねらいだ。Kyaku ni saifu no himo o yurumete morau no ga nerai da. — book's own footnote: "They aim to get customers to loosen their wallets."

EN: Ahead of the mid-year gift sales battle, department stores ○○ and △△ have unveiled their flagship products. Both stores are lining up products themed around "eco," emphasizing their particular attention to production regions and materials. ○○ Department Store selected 25 items as its flagship products out of 2,500 mid-year gift items and put them on display — including pork raised on feed made from leftover ingredients from juice makers and restaurants using organic farming methods. △△ Department Store, too, has lined up 10 items at its basement entrance under a "Green Gifts kind to people and the planet" banner, featuring items such as cold vegetable soup made from produce grown with reduced pesticide use. Both stores are aiming to create "things you can't buy anywhere else" so as to get customers to loosen their purse strings.
HI: मध्य-वर्ष उपहार बिक्री सीज़न से पहले, ○○ और △△ डिपार्टमेंट स्टोरों ने अपने मुख्य आकर्षण उत्पाद पेश किए हैं। दोनों स्टोर "इको" थीम पर सामान सजा रहे हैं, और उत्पादन क्षेत्र व सामग्री पर ख़ास ध्यान देने पर ज़ोर दे रहे हैं। ○○ स्टोर ने 2500 उपहार वस्तुओं में से 25 को मुख्य उत्पाद के तौर पर चुनकर प्रदर्शित किया — जिसमें जैविक खेती के तरीक़ों से जूस बनाने वालों और रेस्तराँओं के बचे हुए खाद्य पदार्थों को चारे के रूप में इस्तेमाल कर पाले गए सुअर का माँस भी शामिल है। △△ स्टोर ने भी अपने बेसमेंट प्रवेश द्वार पर "लोगों और धरती के लिए अनुकूल ग्रीन गिफ़्ट" नाम से 10 वस्तुएँ सजाई हैं, जिनमें कम कीटनाशक से उगाई गई सब्ज़ियों से बना ठंडा सूप शामिल है। दोनों स्टोरों का मक़सद "कहीं और न मिलने वाली चीज़ें" पेश कर ग्राहकों को खर्च करने के लिए प्रेरित करना है।
GU: મધ્ય-વર્ષ ભેટ વેચાણ સીઝન પહેલાં, ○○ અને △△ ડિપાર્ટમેન્ટ સ્ટોર્સે પોતાના મુખ્ય આકર્ષણ ઉત્પાદનો રજૂ કર્યા છે. બંને સ્ટોર્સ "ઇકો" થીમ પર વસ્તુઓ ગોઠવી રહ્યા છે, અને ઉત્પાદન ક્ષેત્ર તથા સામગ્રી પર ખાસ ધ્યાન આપવા પર ભાર મૂકી રહ્યા છે. ○○ સ્ટોરે 2500 ભેટ વસ્તુઓમાંથી 25 ને મુખ્ય ઉત્પાદન તરીકે પસંદ કરી પ્રદર્શિત કરી — જેમાં ઓર્ગેનિક ખેતી પદ્ધતિઓથી જ્યુસ બનાવનારાઓ અને રેસ્ટોરાંના વધેલા ખાદ્ય પદાર્થોને ખોરાક તરીકે વાપરી ઉછેરેલા ડુક્કરનું માંસ પણ સામેલ છે. △△ સ્ટોરે પણ પોતાના બેઝમેન્ટ પ્રવેશદ્વાર પર "લોકો અને પૃથ્વી માટે અનુકૂળ ગ્રીન ગિફ્ટ" નામે 10 વસ્તુઓ ગોઠવી છે, જેમાં ઓછા જંતુનાશકથી ઉગાડેલા શાકભાજીમાંથી બનેલો ઠંડો સૂપ સામેલ છે. બંને સ્ટોર્સનો હેતુ "બીજે ક્યાંય ન મળે તેવી વસ્તુઓ" રજૂ કરી ગ્રાહકોને ખર્ચ કરવા પ્રેરવાનો છે.

The article says ○○百貨店はお中元用商品2500品から主力商品として25品を選び — this is the same set of items the headline/opening line calls the store's 目玉商品: the small selection singled out as the store's highlight, out of thousands of general 中元用商品 (option 1, too broad — the whole catalogue, not the highlights). エコ商品 (2) and ギフト商品 (3) both describe the *theme/category* of these products, not the fact that they were specifically singled out as headliners.

Answer: 2 (confirmed — booklet: "問2:2"). 両百貨店とも、「エコ」をテーマに商品を並べ、産地や素材へのこだわりを強調している directly states both stores share this "eco" focus. Option 1 is never claimed — no prices are given at all, and 手間をかけた ("extra effort put in") in Day 2's own strategy dialogue suggests the opposite of cheap. Option 3 overstates "same-ish lineup" as literally identical, when the actual products differ (pork vs. vegetable soup). Option 4 invents a quantity contrast — ○○ narrowed 2,500 down to 25, but △△'s 10-item count is never compared against any larger pool, so no such contrast is established.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's near-synonyms for "the products a store is pushing" are easy to mix up — compare them:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
目玉商品medama shouhin A flagship/highlight ("eyeball") product The headline term — a product deliberately singled out to catch attention. Talking about what makes the news/ad stand out.
主力商品shuryoku shouhin A flagship/core product The article body's near-synonym — the small selected set a business is pushing hardest. Describing which items a store actually chose to lead with, out of a much bigger catalogue.
中元用商品chuugen-you shouhin Mid-year-gift-use products (general) The whole catalogue (2,500 items) — not singled out at all. Talking about the full range on offer, before any selection happens.
エコ商品eko shouhin Eco-themed products Describes the *theme*, not the fact of being a highlighted item — a product can be "eco" without being the store's headline pick. Talking about the environmental angle specifically, separate from marketing prominence.
Exam trap: A headline ending on a bare particle (も／へ／を) is not incomplete by accident — it's a stylistic compression, and each particle points to a predictable family of missing verbs: も → "this too is being [done]," へ → "moving toward [state]," を → "should/must [do]." Learn the particle-to-verb mapping rather than memorizing individual headlines.
