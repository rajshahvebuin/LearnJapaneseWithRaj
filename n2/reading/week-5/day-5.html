1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

グラフでよく使われる表現を覚えよう Gurafu de yoku tsukawareru hyougen o oboeyou — "Let's learn expressions often used in graphs!"

例えばこんな表現がよく使われます。Tatoeba konna hyougen ga yoku tsukawaremasu. "For example, expressions like these are often used:"

ExpressionMeaningEN example
〜割（%）を占める~wari (%) o shimeruto make up ~% of (a total)"A occupies 70% (of the total)."
大半を占めるtaihan o shimeruto occupy the majority"A makes up the majority."
（〜%／〜円など）に達する(~% / ~en nado) ni tassuruto reach/come to ...% or ...yen"It reached 90%."
AはBを上回るA wa B o uwamawaruA exceeds B"A exceeds B."
AはBを下回るA wa B o shitamawaruA falls short of B (⇔ opposite of 上回る)"A falls short of B."
わずかに／ややwazuka ni / yayaslightly / somewhat"A is slightly higher than B."
はるかに／大きくharuka ni / ookikuby far / by a lot"A is by far higher than B."
割合wariaia ratio, a proportion"the ratio of A to B"
◯◯率◯◯-ritsuthe ◯◯ rate"the casualty rate"
総◯数sou-◯-suuthe gross/total number of ◯"the total number of cases"

The book illustrates 「はるかに」vs「わずかに」with a joke: one character boasts 「ボクの点数はキミよりはるかにいい！」(Boku no tensuu wa kimi yori haruka ni ii! — "My score is far better than yours!") while holding a 100-point paper next to a friend's near-zero score; the friend, holding a slightly-better-than-last-time paper, mutters 「これでも前回よりわずかにいい。」(Kore demo zenkai yori wazuka ni ii. — "Even this is only slightly better than last time.") — the two adverbs mark a huge gap vs. a tiny one.

HI: はるかに = बहुत ज़्यादा अंतर से; わずかに = बहुत मामूली अंतर से — दोनों तुलना करते हैं, पर अंतर के आकार में बहुत फ़र्क होता है। GU: はるかに = ઘણા મોટા તફાવતથી; わずかに = ખૂબ નાના તફાવતથી — બંને સરખામણી કરે છે, પણ તફાવતના કદમાં મોટો ફરક છે.

Dialogue

妻：え？　またライターの火事？　また、子どもたちが犠牲になったって。かわいそうに。親がよく管理しないとね、子どもがいじらないように。Tsuma: E? Mata raitaa no kaji? Mata, kodomotachi ga gisei ni natta tte. Kawaisou ni. Oya ga yoku kanri shinai to ne, kodomo ga ijiranai you ni.
夫：昔はこんなにライターの火事ってなかったような気がするな。車の中の火事か……。車の中は火の回りが速いし、今は燃えやすい素材が身の回りに多いんだろうなあ。Otto: Mukashi wa konna ni raitaa no kaji tte nakatta you na ki ga suru na. Kuruma no naka no kaji ka... Kuruma no naka wa hi no mawari ga hayai shi, ima wa moeyasui sozai ga mi no mawari ni ooi n darou naa.
妻：簡単に火がつかないようなライターにすべきよね。Tsuma: Kantan ni hi ga tsukanai you na raitaa ni subeki yo ne.
夫：そういう製品もあったんだけど、コストが高くつくし、使いにくくて売れなかったんだって。今また子どもが簡単に操作できないような安全基準を設けようという声が上がってるよ。Otto: Sou iu seihin mo atta n da kedo, kosuto ga takaku tsuku shi, tsukainikukute urenakatta n datte. Ima mata kodomo ga kantan ni sousa dekinai you na anzen kijun o mokeyou to iu koe ga agatteru yo.

#Statement (Japanese)EnglishTrue/False
1子どもがライターをいじって起きる火事が続いている。Kodomo ga raitaa o ijitte okiru kaji ga tsuzuite iru.Fires caused by children playing with lighters keep happening.True — "また、子どもたちが犠牲になった" (again, children became victims).
2車の中は物が燃えやすく、火が広がりやすい。Kuruma no naka wa mono ga moeyasuku, hi ga hirogariyasui.Inside cars, things catch fire easily and flames spread easily.True — matches the husband's line about fire spreading fast in cars with flammable materials nearby.
3昔に比べて今は、車の中にライターを置く人が多い。Mukashi ni kurabete ima wa, kuruma no naka ni raitaa o oku hito ga ooi.Compared to the past, more people now leave lighters in their cars.False — not stated; the husband only notes that car fires are new, not why lighters end up in cars.
4子どもが簡単に使えないようなライターが値上がりしている。Kodomo ga kantan ni tsukaenai you na raitaa ga neagari shite iru.Child-proof lighters have been rising in price.False — such lighters existed and cost more, but there's no mention of prices currently rising.
5子どもが簡単に操作できない安全基準が設けられた。Kodomo ga kantan ni sousa dekinai anzen kijun ga mokerareta.A safety standard preventing easy child operation has already been established.False — 声が上がってる means calls for the standard are rising now, not that it's been established.

Answer (confirmed, printed at the bottom of p.87): 1・2.

問い　グラフの説明として正しいものはどれか。Toi: Gurafu no setsumei toshite tadashii mono wa dore ka. — "Which explanation of the graph[s] is correct?"

Full sentence (option 1, correct): 合計件数526は全年齢の総件数1,319の39.9%（≈約4割）にあたり、526件は約1,300件に近い。Goukei kensuu 526 wa zen-nenrei no sou-kensuu 1,319 no 39.9% (≈ yaku yon-wari) ni atari, 526-ken wa yaku 1,300-ken ni chikai.
EN: The 12-and-under total of 526 cases is close to "about 1,300," and 526 is 39.9% (≈ about 4/10) of the all-ages total of 1,319 — both figures in option 1 check out.
HI: 12 साल या उससे कम उम्र के कुल 526 मामले "लगभग 1,300" के करीब हैं, और 526, सभी उम्र के कुल 1,319 का 39.9% (≈ लगभग 4/10) है — विकल्प 1 के दोनों आँकड़े सही बैठते हैं।
GU: 12 વર્ષ કે તેથી ઓછી ઉંમરના કુલ 526 કેસ "લગભગ 1,300" ની નજીક છે, અને 526, બધી ઉંમરના કુલ 1,319 ના 39.9% (≈ લગભગ 4/10) છે — વિકલ્પ 1 ના બંને આંકડા સાચા બેસે છે.

Why (answer confirmed: booklet index 117, "第5週" → "5日目 (p.86～p.87)" → 問い:1): The booklet spells out each distractor directly:

  • 選択肢をひとつひとつゆっくり読もう。Sentakushi o hitotsu hitotsu yukkuri yomou. "Read each option slowly, one at a time."
  • 2は「約半数」でなく約7割（69.2%）。2 wa "yaku hanbun" de naku yaku nana-wari (69.2%). "Option 2 should say 'about 70% (69.2%)', not 'about half.'"
  • 3は「約7割」でなく約半数（53%）。3 wa "yaku nana-wari" de naku yaku hanbun (53%). "Option 3 should say 'about half (53%)', not 'about 70%.'"
  • 4は「はるかに上回っている」でなくわずかに上回っている（53%：47%）。4 wa "haruka ni uwamawatte iru" de naku wazuka ni uwamawatte iru (53%:47%). "Option 4 should say 'slightly exceeds', not 'far exceeds' — the gap is only 53% to 47%."

Options 2 and 3 simply swap the two figures from the pie chart and the data table (53% ↔ 69.2%); option 4 correctly identifies which side is bigger but overstates the size of the gap with はるかに (by far) — this is exactly the わずかに vs. はるかに distinction from today's strategy box.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

All four options recycle the same two data points (53%/47% and 69.2%/18.9%/29.1%) with small but critical wording changes — exactly what today's strategy box warns about:

OptionReal figureWhat it wrongly claimsTrap to avoid
1 (correct) 526 / 1,319 = 39.9% ≈ 4割; 526 ≈ 1,300 Nothing — both numbers check out —
2 69.2% (under-5 casualty rate) "約半数" (about half, i.e. ~50%) 約半数 and 約7割 are easy to swap between the pie chart's 53% and the table's 69.2% — always match the number to its actual row.
3 53% (lighter-caused share nationwide) "約7割" (about 70%) Same swap in the other direction — 53% is close to half, not to 70%.
4 53% vs. 47% — a gap of only 6 points "はるかに上回っている" (far exceeds) Getting the direction (which side is bigger) right isn't enough — check the size of the gap too; a slight lead needs わずかに/やや, not はるかに/大きく.
Exam trap: When a graph question recycles the same handful of numbers across all four options, the trap is almost always a swapped or exaggerated degree word (約半数 vs 約7割, わずかに vs はるかに) rather than a made-up statistic — always re-check the exact figure in the source before trusting a "roughly ~" restatement.
