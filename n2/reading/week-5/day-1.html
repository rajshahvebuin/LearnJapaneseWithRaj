1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

見出しから文を作って理解しようMidashi kara bun o tsukutte rikai shiyou — Make a sentence from the headline that demonstrates the main point!

例えばこんなふうに見出しを文にします。Tatoeba konna fuu ni midashi o bun ni shimasu. ("For example, here's how to turn a headline into a sentence.") A headline crams the story into a few nouns with no particles or verb endings — restore them in your head before you even read the article body:

  • A鉄道新線 2030年に延期　収益悪化のためA Tetsudou Shinsen 2030-nen ni Enki — Shuueki Akka no Tame ("'A' Railway new line: postponed to 2030 — due to worsening revenue")
    → Full sentence: A鉄道は新線の開通を、収益悪化のため2030年に延期した。A Tetsudou wa shinsen no kaitsuu o, shuueki akka no tame ni-sen-sanjuu-nen ni enki shita.
    EN: "A" Railway decided to postpone the opening of its new line until 2030 due to worsening revenue.
    HI: A रेलवे ने राजस्व में गिरावट के कारण नई लाइन का उद्घाटन 2030 तक टाल दिया। GU: A રેલવેએ આવકમાં ઘટાડાને કારણે નવી લાઇનનું ઉદ્‌ઘાટન 2030 સુધી મુલતવી રાખ્યું.
  • ○○劇場　さよなら公演に　長蛇の列○○ Gekijou — Sayonara Kouen ni Chouda no Retsu ("○○ Theater: a long line for the farewell performance")
    → Full sentence: ○○劇場の最後の公演（を見たい人たちが）とても長い列を作って並んだ。○○ Gekijou no saigo no kouen (o mitai hitotachi ga) totemo nagai retsu o tsukutte narande.
    EN: Those who wanted to see the last performance at ○○ Theater formed a long line.
    HI: ○○ थिएटर का आख़िरी शो देखना चाहने वालों ने एक बहुत लंबी क़तार बना ली। GU: ○○ થિયેટરનો છેલ્લો શો જોવા ઈચ્છનારાઓએ ખૂબ લાંબી લાઇન બનાવી.
  • 裁判員裁判　判決 → 自宅放火被告に　懲役1年6ヵ月Saibanin Saiban Hanketsu → Jitaku Houka Hikoku ni Choueki Ichi-nen Roku-kagetsu ("Jury-trial verdict: 18 months in prison for defendant who set fire to own home")
    → Full sentence: 裁判員裁判で、自宅に放火した被告に懲役1年6ヵ月の判決が出た。Saibanin saiban de, jitaku ni houka shita hikoku ni choueki ichi-nen roku-kagetsu no hanketsu ga deta.
    EN: At the jury trial, the defendant was sentenced to 18 months in prison for setting fire to his own home.
    HI: जूरी वाली अदालत में, अपने ही घर में आग लगाने वाले अभियुक्त को 1 वर्ष 6 महीने की सज़ा सुनाई गई। GU: જ્યુરી ટ્રાયલમાં, પોતાના જ ઘરમાં આગ લગાડનાર આરોપીને 1 વર્ષ 6 મહિનાની જેલની સજા આપવામાં આવી.

✿ 練習 (Renshuu — Practice)

次の会話文を読んで、後の文から正しいものを選ぼう。Tsugi no kaiwabun o yonde, ato no bun kara tadashii mono o erabou. ("Read the following dialogue, then choose the correct statements below.") Answer confirmed printed on the next page (p.79): 2・4.

女：あ、あの人たちやっと帰れるのね。Onna: A, ano hitotachi yatto kaereru no ne.

男：え？ ああ、ずっと空港で寝袋にくるまって飛行機が飛ぶの待ってた人たちか。Otoko: E? Aa, zutto kuukou de nebukuro ni kurumatte hikouki ga tobu no matteta hitotachi ka.

女：1週間よ。気の毒に。でもしょうがないね、自然災害じゃ。Onna: Isshuukan yo. Ki no doku ni. Demo shouganai ne, shizen saigai ja.

男：よかったよ、飛べるようになって。乗客だけじゃなくて、商品も足止め注1されていたからビジネスにも影響があっただろうし、航空業界の損失注2は2,000億円とか3,000億円とか、とにかく、すごい額らしいから。Otoko: Yokatta yo, toberu you ni natte. Joukyaku dake ja nakute, shouhin mo ashidome sarete ita kara bijinesu ni mo eikyou ga atta darou shi, koukuu gyoukai no sonshitsu wa nisen-oku-en toka sanzen-oku-en toka, tonikaku, sugoi gaku rashii kara.

EN: Woman: Oh, those people can finally go home now. — Man: Huh? Oh, the ones who were wrapped up in sleeping bags at the airport this whole time, waiting for flights to resume? — Woman: A whole week. Poor them. But it can't be helped — it's a natural disaster. — Man: I'm just glad flights are running again. It wasn't only passengers — cargo was stranded too, so it must have hit businesses as well; they say the airline industry's losses are something like 200 or 300 billion yen — anyway, apparently a huge amount.
HI: महिला: अरे, वे लोग आख़िरकार घर जा सकेंगे। — पुरुष: क्या? अरे, वही लोग जो हवाई अड्डे पर स्लीपिंग बैग में लिपटे, उड़ान शुरू होने का इंतज़ार कर रहे थे? — महिला: पूरा एक हफ़्ता। बेचारे। पर क्या करें, प्राकृतिक आपदा है। — पुरुष: अच्छा हुआ कि उड़ानें फिर से शुरू हो गईं। सिर्फ़ यात्री ही नहीं, माल भी फँसा रहा, तो व्यापार पर भी असर पड़ा होगा; कहते हैं विमानन उद्योग का नुक़सान 2000 या 3000 अरब येन जैसा कुछ है — बहरहाल, बहुत बड़ी रक़म लगती है।
GU: સ્ત્રી: અરે, એ લોકો છેવટે ઘરે જઈ શકશે. — પુરુષ: શું? અરે, એ જ લોકો જે એરપોર્ટ પર સ્લીપિંગ બેગમાં લપેટાઈને, ફ્લાઇટ શરૂ થવાની રાહ જોતા હતા? — સ્ત્રી: આખું અઠવાડિયું. બિચારા. પણ શું કરીએ, કુદરતી આપત્તિ છે. — પુરુષ: સારું થયું ફ્લાઇટ્સ ફરી શરૂ થઈ. ફક્ત મુસાફરો જ નહીં, માલસામાન પણ અટવાયો, એટલે વેપાર પર પણ અસર પડી હશે; કહે છે ઉડ્ડયન ઉદ્યોગનું નુકસાન 2000 કે 3000 અબજ યેન જેવું કંઈક છે — ગમે તેમ, બહુ મોટી રકમ લાગે છે.

注1 足止め：being trapped Chuu 1: ashidome: being trapped — "stuck in place, unable to move on"　·　注2 損失：a loss Chuu 2: sonshitsu: a loss

  • 1 空港で催し物があったので、観光客は出発を遅らせた。Kuukou de moyooshimono ga atta node, kankoukyaku wa shuppatsu o okuraseta. — There was an event at the airport, so tourists delayed their departure.
  • 2 自然災害の影響で予定の飛行機に乗れない人たちがいた。Shizen saigai no eikyou de yotei no hikouki ni norenai hitotachi ga ita. — Due to the natural disaster, there were people who couldn't board their scheduled flights.
  • 3 乱れていた空のダイヤはまだもとに戻らない。Midarete ita sora no daiya wa mada moto ni modoranai. — The disrupted flight schedules still haven't returned to normal.
  • 4 この災害で観光客だけでなく輸出や輸入の商品も影響を受けた。Kono saigai de kankoukyaku dake de naku yushutsu ya yunyuu no shouhin mo eikyou o uketa. — This disaster affected not only tourists but also exported and imported goods.
  • 5 この災害の影響でホテル業界は多額の利益を得た。Kono saigai no eikyou de hoteru gyoukai wa tagaku no rieki o eta. — Because of this disaster, the hotel industry earned a large profit.

Why: The man explicitly says 「よかったよ、飛べるようになって」("I'm glad flights are running again") — flights have already resumed, so statement 3 (still not restored) is false, and statement 2 (people couldn't board due to the disaster) is confirmed true by the whole first exchange. He also says 「乗客だけじゃなくて、商品も足止めされていた」("not just passengers — cargo was stranded too"), directly confirming statement 4 (exports/imports affected, not just tourists). Nothing in the dialogue mentions an airport event (statement 1) or the hotel industry profiting (statement 5) — both are invented distractors.

2 Passage Questions

問題：次の新聞記事を読んで、後の問いに答えなさい。Mondai: Tsugi no shinbun kiji o yonde, ato no toi ni kotaenasai. ("Read the following newspaper article and answer the questions that follow.") From p.79. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Headline

A国　火山噴火注1　「空の足」正常化A-koku Kazan Funka (chuu 1) — "Sora no Ashi" Seijouka

Literally "Country A volcanic eruption — 'the sky's legs' return to normal." 空の足 ("the sky's feet/legs") is a metaphor for air transportation/flight services — 足 ("feet, legs") is commonly used in Japanese to mean "a means of getting around" (as in 通勤の足 — "commuter transport").

Article

A国火山噴火による影響で、大混乱となっていた欧州の航空輸送網注2が22日中に正常化する見通しを発表した。15日に火山灰注3が拡散して注4以来、10万便以上が欠航、世界中で700万人以上が足止めされた。航空業界の受けた損失は15億〜25億ユーロにのぼるとみられ、観光業を含めるとさらに損失は拡大するとみられている。*A-koku kazan funka ni yoru eikyou de, dai-konran to natte ita Ousiuu no koukuu yusou-mou (chuu 2) ga nijuuni-nichi-juu ni seijouka suru mitooshi o happyou shita. Juugo-nichi ni kazanbai (chuu 3) ga kakusan shite (chuu 4) irai, juu-man-bin ijou ga kekkou, sekaijuu de nana-hyaku-man-nin ijou ga ashidome sareta. Koukuu gyoukai no uketa sonshitsu wa juugo-oku kara nijuugo-oku yuuro ni noboru to mirare, kankougyou o fukumeru to sarani sonshitsu wa kakudai suru to mirarete iru.

注1 火山噴火：a volcanic eruption kazan funka　·　注2 航空輸送網：an air transportation network koukuu yusou-mou　·　注3 火山灰：volcanic ashes kazanbai　·　注4 拡散する：to diffuse kakusan suru

* 航空業界が受ける損失は15〜25億ユーロにのぼり、観光業を含めるとさらに拡大するとみられる。Koukuu gyoukai ga ukeru sonshitsu wa juugo kara nijuugo-oku yuuro ni nobori, kankougyou o fukumeru to sarani kakudai suru to mirareru. — book's own footnote: "The financial damage to the airline industry may amount to between 1.5–2.5 billion euros, and more, if you include the tourist industry."

EN: Because of the effects of the volcanic eruption in Country A, Europe's air transportation network — which had fallen into major chaos — announced that it expects to return to normal by the end of the 22nd. Since volcanic ash spread on the 15th, more than 100,000 flights have been cancelled and more than 7 million people worldwide have been stranded. The airline industry's losses are estimated to reach 1.5–2.5 billion euros, and if the tourism industry is included, the losses are expected to grow even further.
HI: A देश में हुए ज्वालामुखी विस्फोट के असर से, यूरोप का हवाई परिवहन नेटवर्क — जो भारी अफ़रा-तफ़री में पड़ गया था — 22 तारीख़ तक सामान्य होने की उम्मीद जताई गई है। 15 तारीख़ को ज्वालामुखी की राख फैलने के बाद से, 1 लाख से अधिक उड़ानें रद्द हो चुकी हैं और दुनिया भर में 70 लाख से अधिक लोग फँसे रह गए हैं। विमानन उद्योग का नुक़सान 1.5–2.5 अरब यूरो तक पहुँचने का अनुमान है, और पर्यटन उद्योग को शामिल करने पर यह नुक़सान और बढ़ने की आशंका है।
GU: A દેશમાં થયેલા જ્વાળામુખી વિસ્ફોટની અસરથી, યુરોપનું હવાઈ પરિવહન નેટવર્ક — જે ભારે અંધાધૂંધીમાં પડી ગયું હતું — 22 તારીખ સુધીમાં સામાન્ય થવાની ધારણા જાહેર કરવામાં આવી છે. 15 તારીખે જ્વાળામુખીની રાખ ફેલાયા પછીથી, 1 લાખથી વધુ ફ્લાઇટ્સ રદ થઈ ચૂકી છે અને દુનિયાભરમાં 70 લાખથી વધુ લોકો ફસાયેલા રહી ગયા છે. ઉડ્ડયન ઉદ્યોગનું નુકસાન 1.5–2.5 અબજ યુરો સુધી પહોંચવાનો અંદાજ છે, અને પ્રવાસન ઉદ્યોગનો સમાવેશ કરવામાં આવે તો આ નુકસાન વધુ વધવાની શક્યતા છે.

Answer: 4 (confirmed — 第5週 answer booklet, PDF index 117: "1日目 (p.78〜p.79) 問1:4"). 足 ("feet/legs") is a common Japanese metaphor for "a means of getting around" (as in 通勤の足 for commuter transport); 空の足 ("the sky's feet") means the flights themselves — the actual air services that were grounded and then restored. The airline industry (3) is the business that operates those flights, and tourism (1) is merely one industry affected by their disruption — neither is what was literally "stopped" and then "normalized."

Answer: 4 (confirmed — booklet: "問2:4"). Ash spread on the 15th, and normalization was announced for "within the 22nd" — 22 − 15 = exactly 7 days, one week. Option 1 misreads "15〜25億ユーロ" (a range that could still grow further) as a hard ceiling of 25億. Option 2 wrongly generalizes this one incident ("always" whenever any volcano erupts anywhere). Option 3 invents a claim about tourists rerouting to Asia that never appears anywhere in the article.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary confusion cluster today — instead, compare how each question is unlocked by decoding the headline metaphor versus reading the numbers precisely:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
練習 (Q2, Q4) Headline-to-sentence expansion Reconstructing what "自然災害" and "足止め" actually caused The man's closing remark ("flights are running again") retroactively tells you the disruption is over — don't answer statement 3 before reaching that line.
問1 Metaphor decoding (足 = a means of transport) Distinguishing the thing itself (flights) from the industry that runs it (airlines) or the industry affected by it (tourism) 航空業界 and 観光業 both appear right there in the following sentences, tempting you to pick whichever "business" word you saw most recently — but 空の足 is the flights, not a business.
問2 Precise number-reading 15日→22日 date arithmetic, and reading a range (15〜25億) as still-open rather than fixed とどまった ("stayed capped at") contradicts とみられ／さらに拡大するとみられている ("estimated to... expected to grow further") in the very same sentence.
Exam trap: Japanese headlines love figurative shorthand — 空の足, 通勤の足, 買い物客の足 all use 足 for "a means of getting somewhere." When a headline's key word doesn't literally make sense (a "sky" doesn't have actual "feet"), assume it's this kind of idiom and look at the article body for what it stands in for.
