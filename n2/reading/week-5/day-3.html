Notes
Days 1–6 in this book print furigana under every kanji; reproduced here with full romaji regardless. Crime/accident reports in Japanese newspapers reuse a small, fixed set of expressions (逮捕, 容疑, 通報, etc.) article after article — this day builds a vocabulary base for recognizing them instantly.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

事件や事故の記事に慣れようJiken ya jiko no kiji ni narerou — Try to familiarize yourself with articles dealing with crimes and accidents!

事件や事故の記事は、ほぼ同じ表現が使われます。Jiken ya jiko no kiji wa, hobo onaji hyougen ga tsukawaremasu. ("Crime and accident articles use nearly the same expressions every time.") Here's a sample crime article, followed by the standard vocabulary that recurs across this genre:

Sample headline

女子中学生徒が所持
大麻譲渡容疑で男らを逮捕Joshi Chuugakusei ga Shoji — Taima Jouto Yougi de Otoko-ra o Taiho

("Female junior-high student found in possession — Men arrested on suspicion of supplying marijuana")

Sample article

○市の市立中学校3年の女子生徒（14）が大麻を所持したとして逮捕された事件で、この生徒に大麻を渡したとして○市の男二人が大麻取り扱い法違反の疑いで逮捕されていたことがわかった。逮捕されたのは18歳の少年と21歳の男で、いずれも大麻所持を認めているという。○-shi no shiritsu chuugakkou san-nen no joshi seito (juuyon) ga taima o shoji shita to shite taiho sareta jiken de, kono seito ni taima o watashita to shite ○-shi no otoko futari ga taima toriatsukai-hou ihan no utagai de taiho sarete ita koto ga wakatta. Taiho sareta no wa juuhassai no shounen to nijuuissai no otoko de, izure mo taima shoji o mitomete iru to iu.

EN: In connection with the case in which a third-year female student (14) at a municipal junior high school in City ○ was arrested for possessing marijuana, it has come to light that two men from City ○, suspected of having given the marijuana to the student, had themselves been arrested on suspicion of violating the marijuana control law. The two arrested are an 18-year-old boy and a 21-year-old man; both reportedly admit to possessing marijuana.
HI: शहर ○ के एक नगरपालिका मिडिल स्कूल की तीसरे वर्ष की एक छात्रा (14) को भांग रखने के आरोप में गिरफ़्तार किए जाने के मामले में, यह सामने आया है कि इस छात्रा को भांग देने के शक में शहर ○ के दो पुरुषों को भी भांग नियंत्रण क़ानून के उल्लंघन के शक में गिरफ़्तार किया गया था। गिरफ़्तार किए गए लोगों में एक 18 वर्षीय किशोर और एक 21 वर्षीय पुरुष शामिल हैं, और दोनों ने भांग रखने की बात स्वीकार कर ली है।
GU: શહેર ○ ની મ્યુનિસિપલ મિડલ સ્કૂલની ત્રીજા વર્ષની એક વિદ્યાર્થિની (14) ને ગાંજો રાખવાના આરોપસર ધરપકડ કરવામાં આવેલા કેસમાં, એવું બહાર આવ્યું છે કે આ વિદ્યાર્થિનીને ગાંજો આપવાની શંકામાં શહેર ○ ના બે પુરુષોની પણ ગાંજા નિયંત્રણ કાયદાના ઉલ્લંઘનની શંકામાં ધરપકડ કરવામાં આવી હતી. ધરપકડ થયેલા લોકોમાં એક 18 વર્ષનો કિશોર અને એક 21 વર્ષનો પુરુષ સામેલ છે, અને બંનેએ ગાંજો રાખવાની વાત સ્વીકારી લીધી છે.

✿ 練習 (Renshuu — Practice)

次の会話文を読んで、後の文から正しいものを選ぼう。Tsugi no kaiwabun o yonde, ato no bun kara tadashii mono o erabou. ("Read the following dialogue, then choose the correct statements below.") Answer confirmed printed on the next page (p.83): 3・5.

《Aさんは新聞を見ながらBさんと話しています。》(A-san wa shinbun o minagara B-san to hanashite imasu.) — "A is talking with B while looking at the newspaper."

A：またひき逃げだって。多いね。A: Mata hikinige datte. Ooi ne.

B：どうして逃げるのかな。すぐに通報すれば、助かったかもしれないのに。B: Doushite nigeru no kana. Sugu ni tsuuhou sureba, tasukatta kamo shirenai noni.

A：怖くなって逃げるとか、もしかしたら、飲酒運転していたかもしれないね。A: Kowaku natte nigeru toka, moshikashitara, inshu unten shite ita kamo shirenai ne.

B：ああ、飲酒運転の罰則が厳しいから、お酒を飲んでいたことがわからなくなるように逃げるんだよね。ひき逃げの罰則がまだまだ甘いんじゃないかな。B: Aa, inshu unten no bassoku ga kibishii kara, osake o nonde ita koto ga wakaranaku naru you ni nigeru n da yo ne. Hikinige no bassoku ga madamada amai n ja nai ka na.

A：うん、幼児3人が亡くなる事件があってから、両方の罰則が厳しくなったんだよね。でも飲酒運転はずいぶん減ったのにひき逃げはあんまり減ってないんでしょ？ もっと厳しくしないと、なくならないんじゃないかな。A: Un, youji san-nin ga nakunaru jiken ga atte kara, ryouhou no bassoku ga kibishiku natta n da yo ne. Demo inshu unten wa zuibun hetta noni hikinige wa amari hettenai n desho? Motto kibishiku shinai to, nakunaranai n ja nai ka na.

EN: A: Another hit-and-run. They happen a lot, don't they. — B: I wonder why people flee. If they'd reported it right away, the victim might have survived. — A: Maybe they panicked and ran, or maybe they'd been drunk-driving. — B: Ah, the penalty for drunk driving is so strict now that people flee so no one finds out they'd been drinking. I feel like the penalty for hit-and-run itself is still too lenient. — A: Yeah, after that incident where three small children died, both penalties got stricter. But drunk driving has dropped a lot, while hit-and-runs haven't really decreased, right? I don't think they'll go away unless the penalty gets even stricter.
HI: A: फिर से टक्कर मारकर भागने का मामला। ऐसे मामले बहुत होते हैं ना। — B: पता नहीं लोग क्यों भाग जाते हैं। अगर तुरंत सूचना दी होती, तो शायद पीड़ित बच जाता। — A: शायद डर के मारे भाग गए, या हो सकता है शराब पीकर गाड़ी चला रहे हों। — B: हाँ, शराब पीकर गाड़ी चलाने की सज़ा अब इतनी सख़्त है कि लोग यह छुपाने के लिए भाग जाते हैं कि उन्होंने शराब पी थी। मुझे लगता है टक्कर मारकर भागने की सज़ा अभी भी हल्की है। — A: हाँ, जब से वह घटना हुई जिसमें तीन छोटे बच्चों की मौत हो गई थी, तब से दोनों तरह की सज़ाएँ सख़्त हो गई हैं। पर शराब पीकर गाड़ी चलाना काफ़ी कम हो गया है, जबकि टक्कर मारकर भागने के मामले ज़्यादा कम नहीं हुए, है ना? जब तक सज़ा और सख़्त नहीं होगी, यह ख़त्म नहीं होगा।
GU: A: ફરી એકવાર અથડાઈને ભાગી જવાનો કિસ્સો. આવું ઘણું થાય છે ને. — B: ખબર નહીં લોકો કેમ ભાગી જાય છે. જો તરત જાણ કરી હોત, તો કદાચ પીડિત બચી ગયા હોત. — A: કદાચ ડરીને ભાગી ગયા, અથવા દારૂ પીને વાહન ચલાવતા હોય. — B: હા, દારૂ પીને વાહન ચલાવવાની સજા હવે એટલી કડક છે કે લોકો એ છુપાવવા ભાગી જાય છે કે તેમણે દારૂ પીધો હતો. મને લાગે છે અથડાઈને ભાગી જવાની સજા હજુ પણ હળવી છે. — A: હા, જ્યારથી એ ઘટના બની જેમાં ત્રણ નાનાં બાળકોનાં મોત થયાં, ત્યારથી બંને પ્રકારની સજા કડક થઈ ગઈ છે. પણ દારૂ પીને વાહન ચલાવવાનું ઘણું ઘટ્યું છે, જ્યારે અથડાઈને ભાગી જવાના કિસ્સા ખાસ ઘટ્યા નથી, ખરું ને? જ્યાં સુધી સજા વધુ કડક નહીં થાય, ત્યાં સુધી આ બંધ નહીં થાય.
  • 1 Aさんは飲酒運転による交通事故の記事を読んでいる。A-san wa inshu unten ni yoru koutsuu jiko no kiji o yonde iru. — A is reading an article about a traffic accident caused by drunk driving.
  • 2 Aさんはひき逃げで幼児3人が死亡した記事を読んでいる。A-san wa hikinige de youji san-nin ga shibou shita kiji o yonde iru. — A is reading an article about a hit-and-run in which three small children died.
  • 3 Bさんはひき逃げをする人の気持ちが理解できない。B-san wa hikinige o suru hito no kimochi ga rikai dekinai. — B cannot understand the mindset of people who commit hit-and-runs.
  • 4 AさんとBさんは飲酒運転の罰則をもっと厳しくすべきだと言っている。A-san to B-san wa inshu unten no bassoku o motto kibishiku subeki da to itte iru. — A and B say the penalty for drunk driving should be made even stricter.
  • 5 AさんとBさんはひき逃げ事件の罰則をもっと厳しくすべきだと言っている。A-san to B-san wa hikinige jiken no bassoku o motto kibishiku subeki da to itte iru. — A and B say the penalty for hit-and-run cases should be made even stricter.

Why: B's opening line 「どうして逃げるのかな」("I wonder why people flee") is a genuine puzzlement at the mindset, confirming statement 3. Both close by saying ひき逃げの罰則がまだまだ甘い／もっと厳しくしないと、なくならない ("the hit-and-run penalty is still too lenient" / "won't stop unless it's made stricter") — that's specifically about hit-and-run penalties, confirming 5 and refuting 4 (drunk-driving penalties are described as already 厳しい/having worked, not needing more toughening). The 幼児3人 incident (statement 2) is mentioned only as the past trigger that made both penalties stricter already — not the article A is currently reading — and nothing confirms drunk driving actually caused today's incident (statement 1 — it's only A's guess, "かもしれない").

2 Passage Questions

問題：次の新聞記事を読んで、後の問いに答えなさい。Mondai: Tsugi no shinbun kiji o yonde, ato no toi ni kotaenasai. ("Read the following newspaper article and answer the questions that follow.") From p.83. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Headline

ひき逃げか　女性死亡
横浜・港北区Hikinige ka — Josei Shibou (Yokohama, Kouhoku-ku)

"Possible hit-and-run — woman dead (Yokohama, Kouhoku Ward)." The か after ひき逃げ marks this as still unconfirmed at headline stage — "hit-and-run?" — matching this week's Day 1/2 lesson that headlines often compress a hedge like 〜とみられる into a single question-mark-like か.

Article

18日午後9時ごろ、横浜市港北区○○町のJR横浜線の踏切近くの市道で、女性が血を流し、うつぶせ注1で倒れているのを近くに住む男性が見つけ、一一〇番通報した。女性は病院に運ばれたが間もなく死亡が確認された。付近の路面に約170メートルにわたって引きずられたような跡や血痕注2が残っていたことから、港北署ではひき逃げ事件とみて捜査している。また、女性は60代ぐらいとみられ、身元注3や死因の特定を急いでいる。*Juuhachi-nichi gogo ku-ji goro, Yokohama-shi Kouhoku-ku ○○-chou no JR Yokohama-sen no fumikiri chikaku no shidou de, josei ga chi o nagashi, utsubuse (chuu 1) de taorete iru no o chikaku ni sumu dansei ga mitsuke, hyaku-touban tsuuhou shita. Josei wa byouin ni hakobareta ga mamonaku shibou ga kakunin sareta. Fukin no romen ni yaku hyaku-nana-juu-meetoru ni watatte hikizurareta you na ato ya kekkon (chuu 2) ga nokotte ita koto kara, Kouhoku-sho de wa hikinige jiken to mite sousa shite iru. Mata, josei wa rokujuu-dai gurai to mirare, mimoto (chuu 3) ya shiin no tokutei o isoide iru.

注1 うつぶせ：lying on one's stomach utsubuse　·　注2 血痕：血のあと kekkon: chi no ato — "blood traces: marks left by blood"　·　注3 身元：one's identity mimoto

* 女性の身元や死亡の原因を特定しようと急いでいる。Josei no mimoto ya shibou no gen'in o tokutei shiyou to isoide iru. — book's own footnote: "They are trying to identify the victim and the cause of her death."

EN: At around 9pm on the 18th, on a public road near a JR Yokohama Line railway crossing in ○○-chou, Kouhoku Ward, Yokohama City, a man living nearby found a woman lying face-down and bleeding, and called the emergency number (110). She was taken to hospital, but her death was confirmed soon after. Because marks resembling drag trails and blood traces were found stretching roughly 170 meters along the nearby road surface, Kouhoku Police Station is investigating this as a hit-and-run case. The woman is believed to be in her 60s, and police are hurrying to confirm her identity and cause of death.
HI: 18 तारीख़ रात लगभग 9 बजे, योकोहामा शहर के कोहोकु वार्ड, ○○-चो में JR योकोहामा लाइन के एक रेलवे क्रॉसिंग के पास की सड़क पर, पास में रहने वाले एक पुरुष ने एक महिला को ख़ून बहाते हुए मुँह के बल गिरी हुई पाया और आपातकालीन नंबर (110) पर सूचना दी। महिला को अस्पताल ले जाया गया, लेकिन जल्द ही उसकी मृत्यु की पुष्टि हो गई। आस-पास की सड़क पर लगभग 170 मीटर तक घसीटे जाने जैसे निशान और ख़ून के धब्बे मिलने के कारण, कोहोकु पुलिस थाना इसे टक्कर मारकर भागने का मामला मानकर जाँच कर रहा है। महिला की उम्र लगभग 60 वर्ष अनुमानित है, और पुलिस उसकी पहचान व मृत्यु के कारण का पता लगाने में जुटी है।
GU: 18 તારીખે રાત્રે લગભગ 9 વાગ્યે, યોકોહામા શહેરના કોહોકુ વોર્ડ, ○○-ચોમાં JR યોકોહામા લાઇનના રેલવે ક્રોસિંગ નજીકના રસ્તા પર, નજીકમાં રહેતા એક પુરુષે એક સ્ત્રીને લોહી વહેતું અને ઊંધા મોઢે પડેલી જોઈ અને ઇમર્જન્સી નંબર (110) પર જાણ કરી. સ્ત્રીને હોસ્પિટલ લઈ જવામાં આવી, પણ ટૂંક સમયમાં તેનું મૃત્યુ થયાની પુષ્ટિ થઈ. નજીકના રસ્તા પર લગભગ 170 મીટર સુધી ઘસડાયાના નિશાન અને લોહીના ડાઘ મળી આવતાં, કોહોકુ પોલીસ સ્ટેશન આને અથડાઈને ભાગી જવાનો કિસ્સો ગણી તપાસ કરી રહ્યું છે. સ્ત્રીની ઉંમર આશરે 60 વર્ષની હોવાનો અંદાજ છે, અને પોલીસ તેની ઓળખ અને મૃત્યુનું કારણ શોધવામાં ઝડપ કરી રહી છે.
問1

警察がひき逃げ事件と考えているのはなぜか。Keisatsu ga hikinige jiken to kangaete iru no wa naze ka.

EN: Why do the police believe this is a hit-and-run case?
HI: पुलिस इसे टक्कर मारकर भागने का मामला क्यों मान रही है?

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

Today's standard incident-report vocabulary is easy to mix up under time pressure — compare these pairs:

FormMeaningKey differenceUse when
容疑yougi Suspicion (not yet proven) Describes what someone is *suspected* of — always paired with 〜の疑いで／〜容疑で before an arrest. "Arrested on suspicion of [violation]" — before guilt is established.
認めているmitomete iru To admit (reportedly) A stronger, confirmed statement — the suspect has personally acknowledged the act, narrowing the case from mere suspicion. After an arrest, when the article reports the suspect's own confession.
踏切fumikiri A railway (level) crossing A place name/landmark used to locate the road in this article — the crime happened near it, not on it, and did not involve a train. Describing where something happened, not what type of accident it was.
ひき逃げhikinige A hit-and-run The actual incident type here: a vehicle hits someone and the driver flees, evidenced by drag marks/blood trail. Don't substitute a nearby landmark's name (踏切事故) for the crime type actually described.
Exam trap: A wrong option often borrows a real word straight out of the article's scenery (like 踏切, mentioned only to help locate the street) and turns it into a false claim about the incident itself (踏切事故, "a railway-crossing accident"). Always check whether a word describes the *setting* or the *event* before matching it to an option.
