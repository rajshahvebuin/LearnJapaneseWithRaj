問題1 (Mondai 1) — p.90, 次の記事はある写真展の紹介記事である (an article introducing a photo exhibition)

Passage

Article

現在、古川市美術館で震災注1後10年を記念した写真展が開かれている。カメラマン注2はプロではなく、一般の人たち。地震直後の町のようすを撮った写真約100点が展示されて注3いる。「この家は、亡くなった主人が好きだった家なんです。壊れたままの形でもいいから、とにかく写真に撮って残しておきたいと思って。そしたら、お隣の家も、その隣の家も残したいと思って、この辺りを夢中で撮りました。」と話すのは、60代の主婦の安川さん。復興注4が進み、新しい町並み注5が広がる古川。10年前、確かにここで起こった出来事が昨日のことのように思い出される。今月末まで開催されて注6いる。Genzai, Furukawa-shi bijutsukan de shinsai-go juu-nen o kinen shita shashinten ga hirakarete iru. Kameraman wa puro de wa naku, ippan no hitotachi. Jishin chokugo no machi no yousu o totta shashin yaku hyaku-ten ga tenji sarete iru. "Kono ie wa, nakunatta shujin ga suki datta ie nan desu. Kowareta mama no katachi de mo ii kara, tonikaku shashin ni totte nokoshite okitai to omotte. Soshitara, otonari no ie mo, sono tonari no ie mo nokoshitai to omotte, kono atari o muchuu de torimashita." to hanasu no wa, 60-dai no shufu no Yasukawa-san. Fukkou ga susumi, atarashii machinami ga hirogaru Furukawa. Juu-nen mae, tashika ni koko de okotta dekigoto ga kinou no koto no you ni omoidasareru. Kongetsu-matsu made kaisai sarete iru.

注1 震災：地震によって起こる災害 — "a disaster caused by an earthquake"　·　注2 カメラマン：写真を撮る人 — "a person who takes photos" (a 和製英語/Japanese-English word; it just means "photographer," professional or not)　·　注3 展示する：並べて多くの人に見せる — "to arrange and show to many people"　·　注4 復興：再び盛んになること — "to flourish again"　·　注5 町並み：町でいろいろな建物が並んでいるようす — "the way various buildings line up in a town"　·　注6 開催する：行う、開く — "to hold, to open (an event)"

EN: A photo exhibition marking 10 years since the disaster is currently being held at the Furukawa City Art Museum. The photographers aren't professionals, but ordinary people. About 100 photos of the town right after the earthquake are on display. "This house was one my late husband loved. Even in its broken state, I just wanted to keep a photograph of it. Then I found myself wanting to keep photos of the neighboring house too, and the one next to that, so I ended up photographing this whole area, absorbed in it," says Ms. Yasukawa, a housewife in her 60s. Furukawa, where reconstruction has progressed and a new townscape now spreads out. Ten years ago, the event that undeniably happened here is recalled as vividly as if it were yesterday. The exhibition runs through the end of this month.
HI: आपदा के 10 साल पूरे होने पर फुरुकावा शहर के कला संग्रहालय में एक फोटो प्रदर्शनी चल रही है। फ़ोटोग्राफ़र पेशेवर नहीं, आम लोग हैं। भूकंप के तुरंत बाद के शहर की करीब 100 तस्वीरें प्रदर्शित हैं। "यह घर मेरे दिवंगत पति को बहुत पसंद था। टूटी हुई हालत में भी मैं बस इसकी एक तस्वीर रखना चाहती थी। फिर पड़ोस के घर की, और उसके बगल के घर की भी तस्वीर रखने का मन हुआ, तो मैं पूरे इलाके की तस्वीरें खींचने में डूब गई," 60 की उम्र की गृहिणी सुश्री यासुकावा बताती हैं। फुरुकावा, जहाँ पुनर्निर्माण आगे बढ़ चुका है और नया शहर-दृश्य फैल रहा है। 10 साल पहले यहाँ जो हुआ, वह कल की बात जैसा याद आता है। यह प्रदर्शनी इस महीने के अंत तक चलेगी।
GU: આપત્તિના 10 વર્ષ પૂરા થવા પર ફુરુકાવા શહેરના કલા સંગ્રહાલયમાં એક ફોટો પ્રદર્શન ચાલી રહ્યું છે. ફોટોગ્રાફર વ્યાવસાયિક નહીં, સામાન્ય લોકો છે. ભૂકંપ પછી તરત જ શહેરની લગભગ 100 તસવીરો પ્રદર્શિત છે. "આ ઘર મારા સ્વર્ગસ્થ પતિને ખૂબ ગમતું હતું. તૂટેલી હાલતમાં પણ હું ફક્ત તેની તસવીર રાખવા માંગતી હતી. પછી પડોશના ઘરની, અને તેની બાજુના ઘરની પણ તસવીર રાખવાનું મન થયું, તો હું આખા વિસ્તારની તસવીરો લેવામાં ડૂબી ગઈ," 60ની ઉંમરના ગૃહિણી સુશ્રી યાસુકાવા કહે છે. ફુરુકાવા, જ્યાં પુનર્નિર્માણ આગળ વધ્યું છે અને નવું શહેર-દૃશ્ય ફેલાઈ રહ્યું છે. 10 વર્ષ પહેલાં અહીં જે થયું, તે ગઈકાલની વાત જેવું યાદ આવે છે. આ પ્રદર્શન આ મહિનાના અંત સુધી ચાલશે.
Q1

1　ここで起こった出来事とは、どんな出来事か。Koko de okotta dekigoto to wa, donna dekigoto ka. — "What is 'the event that happened here'?"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1地震があったことJishin ga atta kotoThat an earthquake occurred
2安川さんの家が地震で壊れたことYasukawa-san no ie ga jishin de kowareta kotoThat Ms. Yasukawa's house was destroyed by the earthquake
3安川さんの夫が亡くなったことYasukawa-san no otto ga nakunatta kotoThat Ms. Yasukawa's husband passed away
4写真展を開いたことShashinten o hiraita kotoThat the photo exhibition was held

Why (answer confirmed: booklet index 117, "第5週" → "7日目 (p.90～p.93)" → 問題1 [1]:1): The whole paragraph is framed around the震災 (the earthquake disaster) itself — the exhibition, the photos, and Ms. Yasukawa's account are all about documenting its aftermath. "ここで起こった出来事" refers back to that shared, town-wide event (the earthquake), not to any one resident's personal loss (options 2–3) or to the exhibition itself, which is the response to the event, not the event (option 4).

Q2

2　この文章の内容と合っているものはどれか。Kono bunshou no naiyou to atte iru mono wa dore ka. — "Which matches the content of this passage?"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1安川さんは、地震が起きているときに写真を撮った。Yasukawa-san wa, jishin ga okite iru toki ni shashin o totta.Ms. Yasukawa took photos while the earthquake was happening.
2安川さんは、地震から10年たった町の写真を撮った。Yasukawa-san wa, jishin kara juu-nen tatta machi no shashin o totta.Ms. Yasukawa photographed the town 10 years after the earthquake.
3安川さんは、地震の直後の町を写真に撮った。Yasukawa-san wa, jishin no chokugo no machi o shashin ni totta.Ms. Yasukawa photographed the town right after the earthquake.
4安川さんは、地震のあとプロのカメラマンになった。Yasukawa-san wa, jishin no ato puro no kameraman ni natta.After the earthquake, Ms. Yasukawa became a professional photographer.

Why (answer confirmed: booklet index 117, "第5週" → "7日目 (p.90～p.93)" → 問題1 [2]:3): The article states directly: "地震直後の町のようすを撮った写真約100点が展示されている" (about 100 photos of the town right after the earthquake are on display), and Ms. Yasukawa is one of the amateur "カメラマン" who took them. The booklet flags two vocabulary traps here: line 1's カメラマン is 和製英語 (Japanese-English) simply meaning "photographer" — it never implies "professional," so option 4 is wrong (all photographers here are 一般の人, amateurs). And line 6's 「復興が進み、新しい町並みが広がる古川」describes Furukawa today, ten years on — that sentence is about the present-day town, not about what's in the photos, so option 2 wrongly attaches "10 years later" to the photos themselves.

Q3

3　この記事からはっきりわかることはどれか。Kono kiji kara hakkiri wakaru koto wa dore ka. — "What is clearly known from this article?"

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1事故で死亡した人は自殺だった。Jiko de shibou shita hito wa jisatsu datta.The person who died in the accident died by suicide.
2人をはねた電車の乗客にけがはなかった。Hito o haneta densha no joukyaku ni kega wa nakatta.None of the train's passengers were injured.
3事故による開廷の遅れは裁判に影響した。Jiko ni yoru kaitei no okure wa saiban ni eikyou shita.The court-opening delay caused by the accident affected the trial.
4死亡した人の身元はまだわかっていない。Shibou shita hito no migen wa mada wakatte inai.The identity of the deceased is not yet known.

Why (answer confirmed: booklet index 117, "第5週" → "7日目 (p.90～p.93)" → 問題2 [3]:4): The article's last line states directly: "身元確認を進めている" (identity confirmation is ongoing) — a plainly stated fact, unlike option 1 (a witness only saw her lift the barrier and walk in — suggestive, but never confirmed as suicide in the article's own words), option 2 (train passengers are never mentioned at all), or option 3 (the delay affected the trial's start time, which is a fact, but "影響した裁判" is vaguer than what's literally stated — the clean, unambiguous fact is the still-unconfirmed identity).

Why (answer confirmed: booklet index 117, "第5週" → "7日目 (p.90～p.93)" → 問題2 [4]:1): The article gives the accident time (「8日午前7時5分ごろ」, option 2), the suspension count (「85本が運休」, option 3), and the deceased's gender (「女性」, option 4) — all directly stated. It only tells us the trial started 25 minutes late (開廷した), never when it ended, so option 1 is the one detail genuinely missing.

問題3 (Mondai 3) — p.92–93, 次のAとBは、それぞれ「おさかなポスト」についてかかれた記事である (A and B are each articles about the "fish drop-off box")

Passage A

川崎「おさかなポスト」に捨て魚、年1万匹Kawasaki "Osakana Posuto" ni sute-zakana, nen ichi-man-biki

自宅で飼えなくなった魚を引き取る川崎市多摩区の「おさかなポスト」に、年間約1万匹ものペットが捨てられている。外来魚注1が川に捨てられるのを防ぐポスト本来の目的は実現しつつあるが、「最後まで面倒を見るのが飼い主の責任なのに」と管理者は残念がっている。ポストは縦約60センチ、横約120センチ、深さ約100センチの網のかごで、捨てられた魚は近くの小中学校や老人ホームなどに贈られる。捨てられる魚は過去3年、年間約1万匹。飼えなくなった最近の理由は、「マンションからアパートに引っ越した」「えさ代が払えなくなった」など不況注2によるものが多いという。Jitaku de kaenakunatta sakana o hikitoru Kawasaki-shi Tama-ku no "Osakana Posuto" ni, nenkan yaku ichi-man-biki mo no petto ga suterarete iru. Gairaigyo ga kawa ni suterareru no o fusegu posuto honrai no mokuteki wa jitsugen shitsutsu aru ga, "Saigo made mendou o miru no ga kainushi no sekinin na noni" to kanrisha wa zannen gatte iru. Posuto wa tate yaku 60-senchi, yoko yaku 120-senchi, fukasa yaku 100-senchi no ami no kago de, suterareta sakana wa chikaku no shou-chuu gakkou ya rou-jin hoomu nado ni okurareru. Suterareru sakana wa kako san-nen, nenkan yaku ichi-man-biki. Kaenakunatta saikin no riyuu wa, "manshon kara apaato ni hikkoshita" "esadai ga haraenaku natta" nado fukyou ni yoru mono ga ooi to iu.

注1 外来魚：日本にいなかった外国の魚 — "a foreign fish species not native to Japan"　·　注2 不況：景気が悪いこと — "an economic slump"

Article B

多摩川の「タマゾン」対策に「おさかなポスト」Tamagawa no "Tamazon" taisaku ni "Osakana Posuto"

いま多摩川が「タマゾン」などと呼ばれている。これは、アマゾン川にすむようなピラニアやアロワナ、グッピー、エンゼルフィッシュといった様々な外来種が増えたため、もともといた魚が外来種に食べられて少なくなってしまったからである。この「タマゾン」対策に、「おさかなポスト」というものが効果を上げている。このポストは2005年に設置されたが、そのきっかけは2004年の夏のこと。「世話をしないなら捨てなさい」と母親に叱られて泣きながら金魚を捨てに来た男の子に「川に捨てないで。ときどき会いに来なさい。」と言って金魚を預かったのが始まりで、「生き物の命を大切にすること」と「生態系を乱すのを防ぐこと」に役立っていると管理者は言う。Ima Tamagawa ga "Tamazon" nado to yobarete iru. Kore wa, Amazon-gawa ni sumu you na piranha ya arowana, guppii, enzerufisshu to itta samazama na gairaishu ga fueta tame, moto moto ita sakana ga gairaishu ni taberarete sukunaku natte shimatta kara de aru. Kono "Tamazon" taisaku ni, "Osakana Posuto" to iu mono ga kouka o agete iru. Kono posuto wa nisen-go-nen ni setchi sareta ga, sono kikkake wa nisen-yon-nen no natsu no koto. "Sewa o shinai nara sutenasai" to hahaoya ni shikararete nakinagara kingyo o suteni kita otoko no ko ni "Kawa ni sutenaide. Tokidoki ai ni kinasai." to itte kingyo o azukatta no ga hajimari de, "Ikimono no inochi o taisetsu ni suru koto" to "Seitaikei o midasu no o fusegu koto" ni yakudatte iru to kanrisha wa iu.

EN: A "Fish Drop-off Box" as a countermeasure against the Tama River's "Tamazon" problem. The Tama River is now sometimes nicknamed "Tamazon." This is because various non-native species that normally live in the Amazon River — piranhas, arowanas, guppies, angelfish — have increased, and native fish have dwindled as a result of being eaten by them. The "Fish Drop-off Box" is proving effective as a countermeasure against this "Tamazon" problem. The box was installed in 2005, but the trigger for it was back in the summer of 2004: a boy came crying to abandon his goldfish after his mother scolded him, "If you won't take care of it, get rid of it" — and when told "Don't throw it in the river. Come visit it sometimes," he left the goldfish there, which is how it all began. The manager says it now helps both "cherish the lives of living creatures" and "prevent the disruption of the ecosystem."

[コラム] 「統合問題」に慣れよう！② "Touhou mondai" ni nareyou! Ni — "Getting used to 'integrated' questions! (2)"

統合問題では、説明や解説文だけではなく、次のようなものも出題されます：相談に対するアドバイス／同じ物や事に対する意見や評価／複数の人の意見や感想。Tougou mondai de wa, setsumei ya kaisetsubun dake de wa naku, tsugi no you na mono mo shutsudai saremasu: soudan ni taisuru adobaisu / onaji mono ya koto ni taisuru iken ya hyouka / fukusuu no hito no iken ya kansou. "Integrated" (multi-passage) questions don't just test explanatory writing — they can also present: advice given in response to a consultation; opinions or evaluations of the same thing from different angles; or the opinions/impressions of multiple people (exactly what 問題3's two paired articles above practice).

統合問題のポイント② Tougou mondai no pointo ni — Point #2 for integrated questions: 筆者の意見の入っている文を読むときは……何が言いたいのかに注意！Hissha no iken no haitte iru bun o yomu toki wa... nani ga iitai no ka ni chuui! "When reading a sentence that contains the writer's own opinion... pay attention to what they're actually trying to say!"
  • ★肯定しているのか否定しているのかを見分けましょう！Koutei shite iru no ka hitei shite iru no ka o miwakemashou! — "Tell apart whether they're affirming or denying something!"
  • ★意見や評価によく使われる表現に注意しましょう！Iken ya hyouka ni yoku tsukawareru hyougen ni chuui shimashou! — "Watch for expressions commonly used in opinions/evaluations!"
    • 意外と（⇒予想とは違う）igai to (⇒ yosou to wa chigau) — "surprisingly (⇒ different from what was expected)"
    • いかがなものか（⇒いいと思わない）ikaga na mono ka (⇒ ii to omowanai) — "is that really acceptable? (⇒ I don't think it's good)"
    • もう一つ／いま一つ（⇒あまりよくない）mou hitotsu / ima hitotsu (⇒ amari yokunai) — "not quite there (⇒ not very good)"
    • 可もなく不可もなく（⇒良くも悪くもなく普通だ）ka mo naku fuka mo naku (⇒ yoku mo waruku mo naku futsuu da) — "neither good nor bad (⇒ just average)"
    • 〜べきだ（⇒したほうがいい）~beki da (⇒ shita hou ga ii) — "should ~ (⇒ it would be better to ~)"
HI: "समेकित प्रश्नों" में सलाह, राय/मूल्यांकन, या कई लोगों की राय भी आ सकती है — लेखक की राय वाले वाक्य पढ़ते समय हमेशा देखें कि वे हामी भर रहे हैं या इनकार कर रहे हैं, और "इगाई तो", "इकागा ना मोनो का" जैसे मुहावरों के असली मतलब को पहचानें। GU: "સંકલિત પ્રશ્નો" માં સલાહ, અભિપ્રાય/મૂલ્યાંકન, અથવા અનેક લોકોના મંતવ્યો પણ આવી શકે છે — લેખકના મંતવ્યવાળા વાક્યો વાંચતી વખતે હંમેશા જુઓ કે તેઓ સંમતિ આપે છે કે ના પાડે છે, અને "ઇગાઈ તો", "ઇકાગા ના મોનો કા" જેવા રૂઢિપ્રયોગોનો સાચો અર્થ પારખો.
