Notes
This day's main exercise shows a newspaper-style headline article next to four similar-looking stacked bar graphs (options 1–4) and asks which graph matches the article's claims. Since the source graphs print no exact numeric labels (only 20%-interval gridlines), the reproduction below gives visually-approximated percentages rounded to the nearest 5%, clearly marked as approximate.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

グラフの違いを見つけよう Gurafu no chigai o mitsukeyou — "Let's find the differences between the graphs!"

比べてどこが違うか考えましょう。Kurabete doko ga chigau ka kangaemashou. "Let's compare [the graphs] and think about where they differ." The book gives two paired examples showing how easy it is to misread a graph if you don't compare carefully:

ExampleWhat the bars showConclusion (Japanese)
Bar chart, version 1 Three columns A, B, C — B is the tallest, A is the shortest Bが最も高い、Aが最も低いB ga mottomo takai, A ga mottomo hikui — "B is highest, A is lowest"
Bar chart, version 2 (same A/B/C labels!) Same three columns A, B, C — but now A is the tallest, B is the shortest Aが最も高い、Bが最も低いA ga mottomo takai, B ga mottomo hikui — "A is highest, B is lowest"
Stacked % bar, version 1 A=70%, B=20%, C=10% (left to right) Aが7割、Bが2割A ga nana-wari, B ga ni-wari — "A is 70%, B is 20%"
Stacked % bar, version 2 (same labels!) A=20%, B=70%, C=10% — the proportions have flipped Aが2割、Bが7割A ga ni-wari, B ga nana-wari — "A is 20%, B is 70%"

★ グラフが2つ以上あるときはどこが違うかよく見比べてみましょう。Gurafu ga futatsu ijou aru toki wa doko ga chigau ka yoku mikurabete mimashou. "When there are two or more graphs, take a close look at where they differ."

HI: जब दो या ज़्यादा ग्राफ़ हों, तो ध्यान से देखें कि वे कहाँ अलग हैं — लेबल (A, B, C) एक जैसे होने पर भी अनुपात पूरी तरह बदल सकता है। GU: જ્યારે બે કે વધુ ગ્રાફ હોય, ત્યારે ધ્યાનથી જુઓ કે તે ક્યાં અલગ છે — લેબલ (A, B, C) સરખા હોવા છતાં પ્રમાણ સાવ બદલાઈ શકે છે.

Dialogue

母親：また、違う時計しているのね。一体いくつもってるの？Hahaoya: Mata, chigau tokei shite iru no ne. Ittai ikutsu motteru no?
息子：5つくらいあるけど、お気に入りは3つくらいかな。仕事用、おしゃれ用、遊び用……。あれ、そういえば、母さんは時計していないの？Musuko: Itsutsu kurai aru kedo, o-ki ni iri wa mittsu kurai kana. Shigoto-you, oshare-you, asobi-you... Are, sou ieba, kaa-san wa tokei shite inai no?
母親：最近、携帯電話で見るから。要らないの。Hahaoya: Saikin, keitai denwa de miru kara. Iranai no.
息子：へえ、若い子みたいだね。Musuko: Hee, wakai ko mitai da ne.
母親：でも、携帯って面倒なのよね、意外と。落としたり、忘れたり。だから、携帯電話が時計みたいに腕につけられるといいんだけど。Hahaoya: Demo, keitai tte mendou na no yo ne, igai to. Otoshitari, wasuretari. Dakara, keitai denwa ga tokei mitai ni ude ni tsukerareru to ii n da kedo.
息子：それなら、時計に通信機能がつけばいいわけだよね。そういうのあるらしいよ。Musuko: Sore nara, tokei ni tsuushin kinou ga tsukeba ii wake da yo ne. Sou iu no aru rashii yo.
母親：まあ、便利ね。だったら、ついでに万歩計とか健康診断の機能もつけてほしいわ。Hahaoya: Maa, benri ne. Dattara, tsuide ni manbokei toka kenkou shindan no kinou mo tsukete hoshii wa.

（注）万歩計：a pedometer Chuu: manbokei: a pedometer — a step-counting device.

Answer (confirmed, printed at the bottom of p.85): 1・4. Statements 2, 3, and 5 all misattribute a detail (mendou describes the phone, not the watch; the "connected watch" is something the son heard about, not his own; and such watches are said to already exist).

2 Passage Questions

Main 問題 from p.85: a short newspaper article plus 4 candidate graphs; choose the graph that matches the article. Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Q1

Article

腕時計に付加機能注1　7割が「不要」Udedokei ni fuka kinou　nana-wari ga "fuyou"

時計メーカーが実施した調査で、腕時計に付加機能はいらないと答えた人が7割もいたことがわかった。「機能が増えると操作が複雑になる」「デザイン性が損なわれる注2」という理由からのようだ。IT分野から次世代型機器として腕時計型のパソコンなどさまざまな製品の提案がされており、今後腕時計のIT化、多機能化が進むと思われている中での意外な調査結果だったが、50代からは通信機能や健康診断機能、20代からはデジカメ機能が少数ながら支持されている注3。金額やサイズの面で手軽な注4機器が出てくれば、支持はさらに広がるかもしれない。Tokei meekaa ga jisshi shita chousa de, udedokei ni fuka kinou wa iranai to kotaeta hito ga nana-wari mo ita koto ga wakatta. "Kinou ga fueru to sousa ga fukuzatsu ni naru" "Dezain-sei ga sokonawareru" to iu riyuu kara no you da. IT bun'ya kara jisedai-gata kiki toshite udedokei-gata no pasokon nado samazama na seihin no teian ga sarete ori, kongo udedokei no IT-ka, takinouka ga susumu to omowarete iru naka de no igai na chousa kekka datta ga, 50-dai kara wa tsuushin kinou ya kenkou shindan kinou, 20-dai kara wa dejikame kinou ga shousuu nagara shiji sarete iru. Kingaku ya saizu no men de tegaru na kiki ga dete kureba, shiji wa sara ni hirogaru ka mo shirenai.

注1 付加機能：an additional feature Chuu 1: fuka kinou: an additional feature　·　注2 損なわれる：to spoil Chuu 2: sokonawareru: to spoil　·　注3 支持する：to support Chuu 3: shiji suru: to support　·　注4 手軽な：handy and affordable Chuu 4: tegaru na: handy and affordable　·　（資料：シチズン意識調査）(Shiryou: Shichizun ishiki chousa) — "Source: Citizen [watchmaker] consciousness survey"

EN: A survey by a watch manufacturer found that as many as 70% of respondents said they don't need extra features on a wristwatch, apparently reasoning that "more features make it harder to operate" and "it spoils the design." This was a surprising result given that the IT sector has been proposing next-generation wearable PCs and other products, with watches expected to become increasingly "smart" and multifunctional. That said, communication and health-check functions are supported by a small share of people in their 50s, and a digital-camera function by a small share of people in their 20s. If handier, more affordable devices appear, that support might spread further.
HI: एक घड़ी निर्माता के सर्वे में पता चला कि 70% लोगों ने कहा कि कलाई घड़ी में अतिरिक्त फ़ीचर की ज़रूरत नहीं — वजह: "फ़ीचर बढ़ने से चलाना मुश्किल हो जाता है" और "डिज़ाइन बिगड़ जाता है।" यह चौंकाने वाला नतीजा है क्योंकि आईटी क्षेत्र से स्मार्टवॉच जैसे उत्पाद सामने आ रहे हैं। फिर भी, 50 की उम्र वालों में कुछ लोग संचार और स्वास्थ्य-जाँच फ़ीचर चाहते हैं, और 20 की उम्र वालों में कुछ लोग डिजिटल कैमरा फ़ीचर चाहते हैं।
GU: એક ઘડિયાળ ઉત્પાદકના સર્વેમાં જાણવા મળ્યું કે 70% લોકોએ કહ્યું કે કાંડા ઘડિયાળમાં વધારાના ફીચરની જરૂર નથી — કારણ: "ફીચર વધવાથી ચલાવવું અઘરું બને છે" અને "ડિઝાઇન બગડે છે." આ આશ્ચર્યજનક પરિણામ છે કારણ કે IT ક્ષેત્રમાંથી સ્માર્ટવોચ જેવા ઉત્પાદનો આવી રહ્યા છે. છતાં, 50ની ઉંમરના કેટલાક લોકો સંચાર અને આરોગ્ય-તપાસ ફીચર ઈચ્છે છે, અને 20ની ઉંમરના કેટલાક લોકો ડિજિટલ કેમેરા ફીચર ઈચ્છે છે.

問い　この記事の内容と合うグラフはどれか。Toi: Kono kiji no naiyou to au gurafu wa dore ka. — "Which graph matches the content of this article?"

Each of the four graph options shows five 年代 rows (全体/20代/30代/40代/50代〜) as 100%-stacked bars split into six categories: 通信(携帯電話)機能, パソコン機能, ゲーム機能, デジカメ機能, 健康診断機能, and 付加機能はいらない. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead, compare how each wrong graph option fails to match the article, and which reading strategy catches the mismatch:

Graph optionStrategy usedWhat it gets wrongTrap to avoid
1 Match the overall headline number first Shows majority wanting a feature, not majority saying features are unnecessary Don't just check that "70%" appears somewhere — check which category it's attached to.
2 (correct) Match overall % first, then check the two named exceptions Nothing — matches the 7-wari figure and both named age-group exceptions —
3 Match the overall headline number first Same core problem as option 1 — inverts which side of the split is majority A graph "in the right shape" isn't enough if the majority segment is the wrong category.
4 Match the article's specific named age groups Elevates the wrong age groups (e.g. 30代 instead of 50代) Read every named detail (each specific age group and its feature) — don't stop once the overall shape looks plausible.
Exam trap: When a reading question asks "which graph matches the article," always check the overall headline claim first (here, the 7-wari/"unnecessary" split), then verify every specific named detail one at a time (here, the two age-group exceptions) — a graph can get the overall shape right while still misplacing a specific detail, or vice versa.
