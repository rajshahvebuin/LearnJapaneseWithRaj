Notes
This is the first of three 通知 (notices) days in Week 2. Today's focus is the e-mail format itself — how it differs from a formal letter — a theme that carries into Days 5–6.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

メールの形式に慣れようMeeru no keishiki ni nareyou

Let's get used to the proper e-mail format! Business e-mails follow different conventions from formal letters:

  • 1. メールでは形式的な挨拶などは省略されることが多いです。Meeru de wa keishikiteki na aisatsu nado wa shouryaku sareru koto ga ooi desu. Formal greetings (拝啓…敬具, etc.) are often left out entirely in e-mails.
    HI: ईमेल में औपचारिक अभिवादन अक्सर छोड़ दिए जाते हैं। GU: ઈમેલમાં ઔપચારિક અભિવાદન ઘણીવાર છોડી દેવામાં આવે છે.
  • 2. 普通の手紙では最後に来る所属や名前が、メールの場合は最初に来ます。Futsuu no tegami de wa saigo ni kuru shozoku ya namae ga, meeru no baai wa saisho ni kimasu. In an ordinary letter, your affiliation and name come at the very end; in an e-mail, they come first.
    HI: सामान्य पत्र में सबसे अंत में आने वाला संगठन और नाम, ईमेल में सबसे पहले आता है। GU: સામાન્ય પત્રમાં છેલ્લે આવતું જોડાણ અને નામ, ઈમેલમાં સૌથી પહેલાં આવે છે.
  • 3. 連絡先や取引の詳細などの情報は普通後ろにまとめてあります。Renrakusaki ya torihiki no shousai nado no jouhou wa futsuu ushiro ni matomete arimasu. Information like contact details and transaction specifics is usually gathered together at the end.
    HI: संपर्क जानकारी और लेन-देन का विवरण आमतौर पर सबसे अंत में एक साथ दिया जाता है। GU: સંપર્ક માહિતી અને વ્યવહારની વિગતો સામાન્ય રીતે છેલ્લે એકસાથે આપવામાં આવે છે.

例えば商品の納品に関するメールなら、こんな言葉が出てきます — words that show up in a delivery-related e-mail: Tatoeba shouhin no nouhin ni kansuru meeru nara, konna kotoba ga dete kimasu.

B: હા સાચી વાત. બપોર સુધીમાં લોકો પાછા આવી જશે લાગે છે, પણ ચોક્કસ સમય ખબર નથી. 19 તારીખ કરાવી લઈએ? A: ના, વહેલું હોય તો સારું — સાંજનો સમય ન મળે? B: ભલે, સૌથી મોડો સમય માંગી લઈશ.

1プリンターは最初の予定では今日届くはずだった。Purintaa wa saisho no yotei de wa kyou todoku hazu datta.The printer was originally supposed to arrive today.False
2プリンターは19日の遅い時間に届けてもらうことにした。Purintaa wa juuku-nichi no osoi jikan ni todokete morau koto ni shita.They decided on late delivery on the 19th.False
3AさんもBさんも18日の午前中は会社にいない。A-san mo B-san mo juuhachi-nichi no gozenchuu wa kaisha ni inai.Neither A nor B is at the office on the morning of the 18th.True
4AさんもBさんも19日は午後にしか会社にいない。A-san mo B-san mo juuku-nichi wa gogo ni shika kaisha ni inai.Both are only at the office in the afternoon on the 19th.False
5プリンターは最初の予定より1週間遅れて届くことになる。Purintaa wa saisho no yotei yori isshuukan okurete todoku koto ni naru.The printer will now arrive a week later than first planned.True

Why (confirmed on p.35): B explicitly says 「1週間遅れるんだそうです」— a week's delay — matching item 5. A's line about the 18th health checkup, followed by B agreeing ("あ、そうでしたね"), confirms neither is at the office that morning — matching item 3. The conversation never returns to "today," and it ends with B keeping the 18th but picking its latest slot (not moving to the 19th), so items 1, 2, and 4 don't hold.

問1　商品の受け取りの希望について、正しくないものはどれか。Toi 1: Shouhin no uketori no kibou ni tsuite, tadashikunai mono wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
15月17日以前に希望することはできない。Gogatsu juu-shichi-nichi izen ni kibou suru koto wa dekinai.Cannot request delivery before May 17.
25月17日に希望する場合、夜遅くしかできない。Gogatsu juu-shichi-nichi ni kibou suru baai, yoru osoku shika dekinai.Requesting May 17 is only possible late at night.
35月18日の午前中に希望する場合、連絡しなくてもよい。Gogatsu juu-hachi-nichi no gozenchuu ni kibou suru baai, renraku shinakute mo yoi.Requesting the morning of May 18 needs no contact.
45月18日の午後に希望する場合、連絡しなければならない。Gogatsu juu-hachi-nichi no gogo ni kibou suru baai, renraku shinakereba naranai.Requesting the afternoon of May 18 requires contact.

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 2): The e-mail only ever discusses dates from May 18 onward — nothing suggests May 17 delivery is offered at all, let alone "only late at night," so option 2 is simply invented and unsupported by the text. Options 1, 3, and 4 all track the e-mail exactly: the earliest offered slot is May 18 morning (default, no reply needed — matching "ご連絡がない場合は、予定通りの発送となります"), and anything else needs a reply.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Check every option against the text directly Whether a specific claim about dates is actually written in the e-mail Don't assume unstated details (like "May 17 only at night") are implied just because they sound plausible — if the text never says it, it isn't correct.
Q2 Attribution check — who caused what Whether blame/cause is assigned to the right party メーカーの都合 (manufacturer's fault) vs. the shop's own fault are easy to mix up when skimming — the e-mail exists precisely because the shop is apologizing for someone else's delay.
