1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

改まった手紙の形式を覚えようAratamatta tegami no keishiki o oboeyou

Try to remember the format of formal letters! The book splits a formal letter into parts you can skim past, and parts that actually carry the content:

  • 読み飛ばしてもいい部分 (parts you can skip):
    ① 始めと終わりの言葉 → 拝啓……敬具・前略……草々 Haikei……keigu, zenryaku……souso — opening/closing set phrases, always written at the very start/end.
    ② 始めの挨拶 → 例：時下ますますご清栄（ご健勝）のこととお喜び申し上げます。Jika masu-masu goseiei (gokenshou) no koto to oyorokobi moushiagemasu. — "I hope this letter finds you well" — a fixed opening greeting.
    ③ 終わりの挨拶 → 例：今後のご活躍をお祈り申し上げます。Kongo no gokatsuyaku o oinori moushiagemasu. — "I wish you all the best in your future" — a fixed closing greeting.
    HI: शुरुआत और अंत के तयशुदा अभिवादन वाक्य अक्सर सिर्फ़ औपचारिकता होते हैं — इन्हें तेज़ी से पढ़कर आगे बढ़ सकते हैं। GU: શરૂઆત અને અંતના નિશ્ચિત અભિવાદન વાક્યો મોટે ભાગે ફક્ત ઔપચારિકતા હોય છે — તેમને ઝડપથી વાંચીને આગળ વધી શકાય.
  • 重要な部分 (the parts that matter):
    ❶ キーワード → 書類選考・一次試験・二次試験・面接・履歴書 — the keywords marking each hiring stage.
    ❷ 結果 → 「不合格」「不採用」→ だめだった (rejected). 「採用」「合格」→ pay close attention to the next interview's place, date/time, and what to bring.

田中：わあ、おめでとうございます！それ、採用通知ですか。Tanaka: Waa, omedetou gozaimasu! Sore, saiyou tsuuchi desu ka.
森：まだだよ。一次が通っただけで、これから二次の面接があるんだから。あれ、この日、大学の補講があるんだった。どうしよう。Mori: Mada da yo. Ichiji ga tootta dake de, kore kara niji no mensetsu ga aru n da kara. Are, kono hi, daigaku no hokou ga atta n datta. Dou shiyou.
田中：先生にお願いして休ませてもらうとか、会社に別の日にしてもらうとか？Tanaka: Sensei ni onegai shite yasumasete morau toka, kaisha ni betsu no hi ni shite morau toka?

Mori: Not yet — I only passed the first stage, there's still a second interview to come. Oh wait, there's a university makeup class that same day. What should I do? Tanaka: Maybe ask the teacher to excuse you, or ask the company for a different day? Mori: In a case like this, which should I ask first? I'll go consult the student affairs office.
HI: तानाका: सेम्पाई, नौकरी की तलाश कैसी चल रही है? मोरी: अरे, जॉब हंटिंग? एक में असफल हुई, एक में स्क्रीनिंग पास कर ली। तानाका: वाह, बधाई हो! क्या वो नियुक्ति सूचना है? मोरी: अभी नहीं — सिर्फ़ पहला चरण पास हुआ है, दूसरा इंटरव्यू अभी बाकी है। अरे, उसी दिन तो यूनिवर्सिटी की मेकअप क्लास है। क्या करूँ? तानाका: शायद शिक्षक से छूट माँग लो, या कंपनी से दूसरा दिन माँग लो? मोरी: ऐसे मामले में पहले किससे पूछना ठीक रहेगा? स्टूडेंट अफेयर्स ऑफ़िस में जाकर सलाह लेती हूँ।
GU: તાનાકા: સેમ્પાઈ, નોકરીની શોધ કેવી ચાલે છે? મોરી: અરે, જોબ હન્ટિંગ? એકમાં નિષ્ફળ ગઈ, એકમાં સ્ક્રીનિંગ પાસ કરી. તાનાકા: વાહ, અભિનંદન! શું એ નિમણૂક સૂચના છે? મોરી: હજુ નહીં — ફક્ત પહેલો તબક્કો પાસ થયો છે, બીજું ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ હજુ બાકી છે. અરે, એ જ દિવસે તો યુનિવર્સિટીનો મેકઅપ ક્લાસ છે. શું કરું? તાનાકા: કદાચ શિક્ષકને છૂટ માંગી લે, અથવા કંપનીને બીજો દિવસ માંગી લે? મોરી: આવા કિસ્સામાં પહેલા કોને પૂછવું યોગ્ય રહેશે? સ્ટુડન્ટ અફેર્સ ઓફિસમાં જઈને સલાહ લઉં છું.
#StatementEnglishTrue/False
1田中さんは森さんの後輩である。Tanaka-san wa Mori-san no kouhai de aru.Tanaka is Mori's junior.True
2森さんは就職が決まった。Mori-san wa shuushoku ga kimatta.Mori's job is settled.False
3森さんにとって二次試験の日は都合が悪い。Mori-san ni totte niji shiken no hi wa tsugou ga warui.The second-exam day is inconvenient for Mori.True
4森さんは補講を休んで面接を受ける。Mori-san wa hokou o yasunde mensetsu o ukeru.Mori will skip the makeup class for the interview.False
5森さんは面接の日を別の日にしてもらう。Mori-san wa mensetsu no hi o betsu no hi ni shite morau.Mori will get the interview moved to another day.False

Why (confirmed on p.37): Tanaka addresses Mori as 先輩 (senpai), so Tanaka is the 後輩 (junior) — item 1 true. Mori explicitly says 「まだだよ」("not yet") about the hiring notice — item 2 false. The makeup-class clash ("どうしよう") shows the exam date is inconvenient — item 3 true. Mori ends the dialogue undecided, planning only to ask the student affairs office first — so neither item 4 nor item 5 (both of which assume a decision was already made) hold.

Jika, masu-masu gokenshou no koto to, oyorokobi moushiagemasu. Kono tabi wa heisha no shain saiyou shiken ni goubo itadaki, arigatou gozaimashita. 問1　ＡとＢに共通して書かれていることは何か。Toi 1: A to B ni kyoutsuu shite kakarete iru koto wa nani ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1面接試験の結果Mensetsu shiken no kekkaResult of the interview exam
2採用試験の結果Saiyou shiken no kekkaResult of the hiring exam
3面接試験の日程Mensetsu shiken no nitteiSchedule of the interview exam
4採用試験の日程Saiyou shiken no nitteiSchedule of the hiring exam

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 2): A reports the final hiring-exam result (不採用, not hired); B reports an intermediate hiring-exam result (第一次選考に合格, passed the first round) and schedules the next step. Both are, at their core, reporting a stage of the 採用試験's result — not a schedule (only B has one) and not specifically "interview exam" results (A never mentions an interview taking place, only that its outcome was decided).

Passage B only — see Q1 above

問2　Ｂの通知の内容と合うものはどれか。Toi 2: B no tsuuchi no naiyou to au mono wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1二次面接に行けない人は、前日に連絡しなければならない。Niji mensetsu ni ikenai hito wa, zenjitsu ni renraku shinakereba naranai.Those who can't attend the second interview must contact the day before.
2森さんがこの会社に就職できるかどうかはわからない。Mori-san ga kono kaisha ni shuushoku dekiru ka dou ka wa wakaranai.Whether Mori will get this job is still unknown.
3面接を受ける人は5月20日の9時までに行かなければならない。Mensetsu o ukeru hito wa gogatsu hatsuka no ku-ji made ni ikanakereba naranai.Interviewees must arrive by 9:00 on May 20.
4この会社の面接試験は社内で行われる予定だ。Kono kaisha no mensetsu shiken wa shanai de okonawareru yotei da.This company's interview will be held in-house.

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 2): B never labels the coming interview "二次面接" — it only says 第一次選考に合格 and schedules "面接試験," so option 1's specific "二次" label overreaches what's written. The 9:00 AM time is when reception opens (受付を開始いたします), not a hard arrival deadline before the 9:30 start — so option 3 overstates it. The venue is みどりシティホール, a public hall, not the company's own office — so option 4 is wrong. Since the outcome of an interview that hasn't happened yet is by definition unknown, option 2 is the safe, correct read.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Find the shared theme, not shared wording Both letters, at different stages, report a result of the same underlying process (採用試験) A mentions no interview and B has no final result — don't pick an option that only half-applies to one letter.
Q2 Don't add details the text never says Distinguishing what B states outright from what sounds plausible "二次面接" and a "9:00 deadline" both sound reasonable but are reader assumptions, not text — the only 100%-safe statement is the general, unresolved one.
