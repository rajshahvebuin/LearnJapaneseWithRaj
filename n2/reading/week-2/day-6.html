Notes
The last of Week 2's three 通知 days — today's skill is recognizing polite, indirect Japanese phrasing and translating it back to its blunt real meaning, a very common N2 reading-comprehension trap.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

遠回しな表現に慣れようToomawashi na hyougen ni nareyou

Let's become familiar with indirect (euphemistic) expressions! Business Japanese often softens blunt statements — the book gives two direct conversions to memorize:

  • 入金の確認がとれません。Nyuukin no kakunin ga toremasen. ("We are unable to confirm receipt of payment.") → really means 入金されていないNyuukin sarete inai ("Payment has not been made.")
    HI: "भुगतान की पुष्टि नहीं हो पा रही" का असली मतलब है "भुगतान हुआ ही नहीं है।" GU: "ચુકવણીની પુષ્ટિ થઈ શકતી નથી" નો ખરો અર્થ છે "ચુકવણી થઈ જ નથી."
  • ○○していただければ幸いです。〇〇 shite itadakereba saiwai desu. ("It would be a happiness if you could do ○○.") → really means ○○してください〇〇 shite kudasai ("Please do ○○.")
    HI: "अगर आप ऐसा कर दें तो अच्छा होगा" का असली मतलब है "कृपया ऐसा करें।" GU: "જો તમે આમ કરો તો સારું રહેશે" નો ખરો અર્થ છે "કૃપા કરી આમ કરો."

支払いを催促する手紙にはこんな表現が出てきます — words that show up in payment-demand letters: Shiharai o saisoku suru tegami ni wa konna hyougen ga dete kimasu.

B：え？どれどれ、……あ、これ、督促状じゃない！B: E? Dore dore, ……a, kore, tokusokujou ja nai!
A：え？とくそくじょう？何、それ？A: E? Tokusokujou? Nani, sore?
B：代金がまだだから、早く払えって。B: Daikin ga mada dakara, hayaku harae tte.
A：え？あれ？インターネットで……クレジットカードで払ったよ。A: E? Are? Intaanetto de……kurejitto kaado de haratta yo.
B：そのときのメールをもう一度確認したほうがいいよ。僕も前にカードで払ったと思ってたのに、実はコンビニの後払いを選択してて、商品が来たときにその払い込み用紙をうっかり捨ててしまったことがあるから。とにかく、ここに早く連絡したほうがいいね。B: Sono toki no meeru o mou ichido kakunin shita hou ga ii yo. Boku mo mae ni kaado de haratta to omotteta noni, jitsu wa konbini no atobarai o sentaku shitete, shouhin ga kita toki ni sono haraikomi youshi o ukkari sutete shimatta koto ga aru kara. Tonikaku, koko ni hayaku renraku shita hou ga ii ne.

EN: A: Hey, I want to ask you something — what's this? B: Huh? Let me see… oh, isn't this a payment demand notice! A: A "tokusokujou"? What's that? B: It says the payment hasn't come through yet, so pay up quickly. A: Huh? That? I paid online… by credit card. B: You should double-check that e-mail again. B: कहता है भुगतान अभी नहीं आया, इसलिए जल्दी चुकाओ। A: अरे? वो? मैंने तो ऑनलाइन...क्रेडिट कार्ड से चुकाया था। B: उस समय का ईमेल फिर से देख लेना चाहिए। मैंने भी एक बार सोचा था कार्ड से चुकाया, पर असल में मैंने कन्वीनियंस स्टोर पर बाद में भुगतान चुना था, और सामान आने पर वह भुगतान पर्ची लापरवाही से फेंक दी थी। बहरहाल, जल्दी वहाँ संपर्क कर लेना बेहतर होगा।
GU: A: સાંભળ, કંઈક પૂછવું હતું — આ શું છે? B: શું? બતાવ તો...અરે, આ તો ચુકવણી તકાદા-પત્ર છે! A: તકાદા-પત્ર? એ શું હોય? B: કહે છે ચુકવણી હજુ આવી નથી, એટલે જલદી ચૂકવો. A: અરે? એ? મેં તો ઓનલાઇન...ક્રેડિટ કાર્ડથી ચૂકવ્યું હતું. B: તે વખતનો ઈમેલ ફરી ચકાસી લેવો જોઈએ. મેં પણ એકવાર વિચાર્યું હતું કાર્ડથી ચૂકવ્યું, પણ ખરેખર મેં કન્વીનિયન્સ સ્ટોર પર પછીથી ચુકવણી પસંદ કરી હતી, અને સામાન આવ્યો ત્યારે એ ચુકવણી સ્લિપ બેદરકારીથી ફેંકી દીધી હતી. કોઈ પણ સંજોગોમાં, જલદી ત્યાં સંપર્ક કરવો સારું રહેશે.
#StatementEnglishTrue/False
1Ａさんはインターネットで買い物をした。A-san wa intaanetto de kaimono o shita.A shopped online.True
2Ｂさんは Ａさんに貸したお金を早く返してもらいたい。B-san wa A-san ni kashita okane o hayaku kaeshite moraitai.B wants A to quickly repay money B lent A.False
3Ａさんはクレジットカードで代金を支払ったと思っている。A-san wa kurejitto kaado de daikin o shiharatta to omotte iru.A believes they paid by credit card.True
4ＢさんはＡさんの払い込み用紙を間違って捨ててしまった。B-san wa A-san no haraikomi youshi o machigatte sutete shimatta.B mistakenly threw away A's payment slip.False
5Ａさんには、よく督促状が届く。A-san ni wa, yoku tokusokujou ga todoku.A often receives demand notices.False

Why (confirmed on p.39): A says they paid 「インターネットで……クレジットカードで」— shopped online by card, matching items 1 and 3. The notice is about an unpaid product bill, not a personal loan between A and B, so item 2 is false. It was B, in a past incident of B's own, who threw away B's own slip (「僕も前に…捨ててしまったことがある」) — not A's slip — so item 4 misreads whose slip it was. Nothing in the dialogue suggests this happens often to A, so item 5 doesn't hold either.

問1　この文書からわからないことはどれか。Toi 1: Kono bunsho kara wakaranai koto wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1代金の支払い方法Daikin no shiharai houhouThe method for paying the bill
2支払わなければならない金額Shiharawanakereba naranai kingakuThe amount that must be paid
3商品の内容Shouhin no naiyouWhat the product is
4商品が届いた日Shouhin ga todoita hiThe date the product arrived

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 1): The letter states the item (コーヒー豆セット), the delivery date (2月7日), and the exact amount due (3,150円) — but nowhere does it specify how to pay (bank transfer, in person, etc.), so the payment method is the one thing this document doesn't tell you.

Q2

Same letter as Q1 above

問2　この文書といっしょに送られている文書は何か。Toi 2: Kono bunsho to issho ni okurarete iru bunsho wa nani ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1督促状TokusokujouDemand notice
2請求書SeikyuushoInvoice
3送信状SoushinjouFax cover sheet
4納品書NouhinshoDelivery slip

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 2): The letter itself says 「改めて請求書をお送りいたします」— "we are sending the invoice again" — meaning a fresh 請求書 (invoice) is enclosed with this letter. A 送信状 is specifically the cover sheet used when sending a FAX (listing receiver, sender, subject, page count) — irrelevant here since this isn't a fax transmission, which rules out option 3.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Inventory what's stated vs. what's assumed Spotting the one detail category the letter never covers It's tempting to assume "surely it says how to pay somewhere" — but absence of a payment method is exactly the gap the question is testing for.
Q2 Match the letter's own self-reference The letter names its own enclosure directly (請求書) 督促状 sounds like the obvious answer (since this letter *is* a payment reminder), but the question asks what's sent with it, not what it itself is — a subtle but important distinction.
