1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

No new strategy box today — 実戦問題 (practice test) days apply everything from Days 4–6 under light time pressure. The book gives a 制限時間 (time limit) of 15分 (juugo-fun) for both problem sets combined (4 questions, 25点 each, 100点満点). As you work through each question below, try to identify which Week 2 skill unlocks it — reading an illustrated flyer for concrete facts (Day 4-style e-mail/notice reading), or spotting a euphemistic/precise-wording trap (Day 6-style) — before checking the bp-why note.

HI: आज कोई नई रणनीति नहीं — 実戦問題 (अभ्यास परीक्षा) वाले दिन पिछले तीन दिनों में सीखी हर बात को हल्के समय-दबाव में लागू करने के लिए होते हैं। किताब दोनों प्रश्न-सेट (कुल 4 प्रश्न) के लिए 15 मिनट की समय-सीमा देती है। GU: આજે કોઈ નવી રણનીતિ નથી — 実戦問題 (અભ્યાસ કસોટી) વાળા દિવસો પાછલા ત્રણ દિવસમાં શીખેલી દરેક બાબત હળવા સમય-દબાણમાં લાગુ કરવા માટે હોય છે. પુસ્તક બંને પ્રશ્ન-સેટ (કુલ 4 પ્રશ્નો) માટે 15 મિનિટની સમય-મર્યાદા આપે છે.

②　みどり市のスーパーで働いている62歳の女性が習うことができるのはどれか。Midori-shi no suupaa de hataraite iru rokujuuni-sai no josei ga narau koto ga dekiru no wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1空手だけKarate dakeKarate only
2ヨガだけYoga dakeYoga only
3空手とヨガKarate to yogaBoth karate and yoga
4どちらも習えないDochira mo naraenaiNeither

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 3): Karate's only eligibility rule is 「小学生以上」(elementary school age or up) — no residency requirement, no upper age limit, so a 62-year-old qualifies. Yoga requires 「みどり市にお住まい・またはお勤めの18歳以上の方」— living OR working in Midori City, 18+ — and she works at a Midori City supermarket, satisfying "お勤めの," plus she's well over 18. She qualifies for both.

③　この文書について正しいものはどれか。Kono bunsho ni tsuite tadashii mono wa dore ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1会費納入のお願いという通知は初めて来た。Kaihi nounyuu no onegai to iu tsuuchi wa hajimete kita.This is the first such payment-request notice.
2この人は3ヵ月分の会費が未納になっている。Kono hito wa sankagetsu-bun no kaihi ga minou ni natte iru.This person has 3 months of dues unpaid.
3このまま会費を払わなければ会員ではなくなる。Kono mama kaihi o harawanakereba kaiin de wa naku naru.If dues go unpaid, they'll no longer be a member.
4会員は会費を毎月払わなければならない。Kaiin wa kaihi o maitsuki harawanakereba naranai.Members must pay dues every month.

Why (booklet-confirmed answer: 3): The letter says 「以前より数回にわたりご通知しております」— notices have already been sent several times before — ruling out option 1. It states the dues are 6ヵ月 unpaid, not 3 — ruling out option 2. The fee is described as a 年度 (fiscal-year) due, not a monthly one — ruling out option 4. Only option 3 matches: 会員規約9条に基づき除名の措置を取る (membership will be revoked under Article 9) if unpaid.

3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead, compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat the answer choice testsTrap to avoid
Q1 Read the fine print, not just the price table A flat monthly fee vs. a per-session fee Seeing "twice a week" makes it tempting to multiply — but the flyer's own note ("any number of sessions") overrides that instinct.
Q2 Check eligibility rules independently for each document "Or" conditions (住まい・または勤め) are easy to misread as "and" Working in Midori City is enough for yoga eligibility even without living there — don't assume both conditions are required together.
Q3 Cross-check every option against a specific line Each wrong option contradicts one exact detail (count of notices, months unpaid, billing frequency) 6ヵ月 vs. 3ヵ月 is a classic number-swap trap — always re-verify the exact figure in the text, don't rely on memory of skimming.
Q4 Anchor a relative date ("this month") to the letter's own dateline "今月末日" only resolves once you find the letter's date, printed at the top Don't default to "the day you're reading it" — the letter's own dateline (10月10日) is what fixes what "今月" means.
