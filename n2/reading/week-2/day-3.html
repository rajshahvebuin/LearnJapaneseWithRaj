1 Reading Strategy

Skill focus

✿常識を働かせて必要な情報だけ読み取ろうJoushiki o hatarakasete hitsuyou na jouhou dake yomitorou — Use common sense to select only the necessary information!

例えば日帰り旅行のお知らせにはこんな言葉が出てきます。Tatoeba higaeri ryokou no oshirase ni wa konna kotoba ga dete kimasu. — For example, a notice about a day trip will contain words like these. A trip/excursion notice always answers the same seven questions — scan for them instead of reading every line with equal weight.

Dialogue

教師：じゃあ、早く申し込みをしたほうがいいですよ。40人しか行けませんから。Kyoushi: Jaa, hayaku moushikomi o shita hou ga ii desu yo. Yonjuu-nin shika ikemasen kara.

生徒：わかりました。でも、先生、20日は月曜日ですね。月曜日は授業がありますね。Seito: Wakarimashita. Demo, sensei, hatsuka wa getsuyoubi desu ne. Getsuyoubi wa jugyou ga arimasu ne.

4代金は旅行の当日に払う。Daikin wa ryokou no toujitsu ni harau.The fee is paid on the day of the trip.False

Why: #3 is false because the trip falls on 海の日 (Marine Day), already a school holiday — nobody is "skipping" class, there simply is none that day. #4 is false because the student plans to submit the application today and bring the ¥2,500 tomorrow, not on the day of the trip itself.
