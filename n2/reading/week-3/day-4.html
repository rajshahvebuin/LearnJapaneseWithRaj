Notes
This day's theme is 意見文 (opinion pieces). The ✿ tip teaches a two-track reading habit: for a "why" question, hunt for the reason-clause; for a "what is that" question, hunt for what the demonstrative (これ／それ／その etc.) is pointing back to.

1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

問われている部分に焦点を当てて読もうTowarete iru bubun ni shouten o atete yomou

Try to focus on the main ideas as you read! Once you know what kind of question is coming, read to answer that question specifically instead of absorbing the whole passage evenly.

HI: जैसे-जैसे आप पढ़ें, मुख्य विचारों पर ध्यान केंद्रित करने की कोशिश करें! एक बार जब आपको पता चल जाए कि किस तरह का सवाल आने वाला है, तो पूरे पाठ को समान रूप से पढ़ने के बजाय उसी सवाल का जवाब देने के लिए पढ़ें। GU: વાંચતી વખતે મુખ્ય વિચારો પર ધ્યાન કેન્દ્રિત કરવાનો પ્રયાસ કરો! એકવાર ખબર પડે કે કેવો પ્રશ્ન આવવાનો છે, પછી આખો ફકરો સરખી રીતે વાંચવાને બદલે એ જ પ્રશ્નનો જવાબ આપવા માટે વાંચો.

例えばこんなことを問われたら？Tatoeba konna koto o towaretara? — For example, if you're asked something like this:

  • ◆ ○○なのはなぜか。→ 理由を表す部分を探そう！→ ……であるから、○○だ。◆ Maru-maru na no wa naze ka. → Riyuu o arawasu bubun o sagasou! → ...de aru kara, maru-maru da. "Why is it [OO]?" → Hunt for the part expressing the reason → "...because ..., it's [OO]."
    HI: "यह [OO] क्यों है?" → उस हिस्से को खोजें जो कारण बताता है → "...क्योंकि..., यह [OO] है।" GU: "આ [OO] કેમ છે?" → કારણ દર્શાવતો ભાગ શોધો → "...કારણ કે..., આ [OO] છે."
  • ◆ 何が△△なのか。→ 指示語のさす部分を探そう！→ ……。それは△△だ。◆ Nani ga sankaku-sankaku na no ka. → Shijigo no sasu bubun o sagasou! → ... Sore wa sankaku-sankaku da. "What is [XX]?" → Hunt for what the demonstrative (これ／それ／この／その, etc.) points back to → "..., that is [XX]."
    HI: "[XX] क्या है?" → सूचक शब्द (यह/वह) किस चीज़ की ओर इशारा कर रहा है, उसे खोजें → "...वह [XX] है।" GU: "[XX] શું છે?" → સૂચક શબ્દ (આ/તે) શેની તરફ ઈશારો કરે છે એ શોધો → "...તે [XX] છે."

問いから読んで早く答えを探す読み方です。Toi kara yonde hayaku kotae o sagasu yomikata desu. — "This is a reading style where you read the question first, so you can quickly hunt down the answer."

練習 (Warm-up — answer confirmed on the next page)

妻：もう、まったく、あの子ったら、毎日だらだらと過ごして……しょうがないんだから。パパからも叱ってよ。今からきちんと勉強しておかないと！ほんと、もったいない。私があの子だったらと思うけどな。若い時は二度ないんだし。Tsuma: Mou, mattaku, ano ko ttara, mainichi darari to sugoshite... shouganain dakara. Papa kara mo shikatte yo. Ima kara kichinto benkyou shite okanai to! Honto, mottainai. Watashi ga ano ko dattara to omou kedo na. Wakai toki wa nido nain dashi.

Wife: "Honestly, that kid — dawdling through every single day... it's hopeless. You scold him too, dear. If he doesn't sit down and study properly now! Really, what a waste. I keep thinking, if only I were that kid. After all, you're only young once."

夫：あいつはあいつでちゃんと考えているさ。それより、君はどうなの？Otto: Aitsu wa aitsu de chanto kangaete iru sa. Sore yori, kimi wa dou nano?

妻：え？私？私はもう……。Tsuma: E? Watashi? Watashi wa mou...

夫：もう、何？終わったと思ってるの？若いときだけが大事だとか思ってないよね。俺たちだって同じだよ。ただ毎日を忙しく過ごせばいいってわけじゃない。勉強したり、楽しんだり、泣いたり笑ったりしないと。人生にはその年その年で特有注の喜びや悲しみがあるんだから。だから、あいつにも今をただ老後の準備にはしてほしくないね、俺は。Otto: Mou, nani? Owatta to omotteru no? Wakai toki dake ga daiji da to ka omottenai yo ne. Oretachi datte onaji da yo. Tada mainichi o isogashiku sugoseba ii tte wake ja nai. Benkyou shitari, tanoshindari, naitari warattari shinai to. Jinsei ni wa sono toshi sono toshi de tokuyuu no yorokobi ya kanashimi ga arun dakara. Dakara, aitsu ni mo ima o tada rougo no junbi ni wa shite hoshikunai ne, ore wa.

注 特有：unique Chuu: tokuyuu: yunikku — Husband: "He's got his own way of thinking about it. More to the point, what about you?" — Wife: "Huh? Me? I'm already..." — Husband: "Already what? You think it's over for you? Don't tell me you think only youth matters — it's the same for us too. It's not just about getting through each busy day. You've got to study, enjoy things, cry, laugh. Every age of life has its own particular joys and sorrows. So — I don't want him treating 'now' as nothing but preparation for old age, either."

#StatementTrue/False
1妻は若い人は若い時を大事に過ごすべきだと思っている。Tsuma wa wakai hito wa wakai toki o taisetsu ni sugosu beki da to omotte iru. — The wife thinks young people should treasure their youth.True
2夫は大事に過ごすのは中年も老年も同じだと思っている。Otto wa taisetsu ni sugosu no wa chuunen mo rounen mo onaji da to omotte iru. — The husband thinks treasuring life applies equally to middle age and old age.True
3妻は老後をどう過ごすかが大事だと思っている。Tsuma wa rougo o dou sugosu ka ga daiji da to omotte iru. — The wife thinks how you spend old age is what matters.False
4夫は子どもにあまり勉強してほしくないと思っている。Otto wa kodomo ni amari benkyou shite hoshikunai to omotte iru. — The husband doesn't want his child to study much.False
5妻は子どもより自分と夫の老後のことを心配している。Tsuma wa kodomo yori jibun to otto no rougo no koto o shinpai shite iru. — The wife worries more about her and her husband's old age than about the child.False

Why: The wife's opening lines ("若い時は二度ない" — youth only comes once) show she treats youth as uniquely precious (#1 true). The husband directly counters that it's "the same for us" (middle-aged people) too — every life stage has its own joys and sorrows (#2 true). Neither of them ever frames old age itself as the important thing to plan for (#3, #5 false — the husband explicitly rejects treating "now" as mere old-age preparation), and the husband never says he wants the child to study less (#4 false — he never comments on the child's studying at all, only on how his wife views her own life stage).

2 Passage Questions

The main 問題 (p.51) is a short opinion essay arguing against the saying "youth only comes once." Words with a dotted underline are clickable/tappable (or hover on desktop) for a popup with romaji + English/Hindi/Gujarati meaning.

Passage

若い時は二度ない (Wakai toki wa nido nai)

若い時は二度ない――と言う。だから、若い時代を大事にせよ、といった意味である。Wakai toki wa nido nai — to iu. Dakara, wakai jidai o daiji ni seyo, to itta imi de aru.

なるほど、その通りである。だが、皮肉屋注1のわたしは、このことばに反論したい。たしかに若い時は一度しかないが、中年だって、老年だって一度しかないのである。われわれは若い時代を大事にすべきであるが、同様に中年を大事にすべきであるし、老年を大事にしなければならない。①若い時代だけを特別視する必要はないのである。Naruhodo, sono toori de aru. Da ga, hinikuya no watashi wa, kono kotoba ni hanron shitai. Tashika ni wakai toki wa ichido shika nai ga, chuunen datte, rounen datte ichido shika nai no de aru. Wareware wa wakai jidai o daiji ni subeki de aru ga, douyou ni chuunen o daiji ni subeki de aru shi, rounen o daiji ni shinakereba naranai. ① Wakai jidai dake o tokubetsushi suru hitsuyou wa nai no de aru.

わたし自身は先ごろ、五十三歳になった。昔の呼称だと、もう立派な"老年"である。だから、ひがんで注2言っているのではない。わたしは、老年には老年のよさがあると思っている。（中略）人生のそれぞれの段階には、それぞれに違った人生のこくがある。わたしはそう思っている。わたしたちは、それぞれの段階に特有な人生の喜びと悲しみを味わいながら生きたい。Watashi jishin wa sakigoro, gojuusan-sai ni natta. Mukashi no koshou da to, mou rippa na "rounen" de aru. Dakara, higande itte iru no de wa nai. Watashi wa, rounen ni wa rounen no yosa ga aru to omotte iru. (Chuuryaku) Jinsei no sorezore no dankai ni wa, sorezore ni chigatta jinsei no koku ga aru. Watashi wa sou omotte iru. Watashitachi wa, sorezore no dankai ni tokuyuu na jinsei no yorokobi to kanashimi o ajiwainagara ikitai.

にもかかわらず、どうして若い時代だけが特別視されるのか!? わたしには不思議である。思うに、人々は若い時代を準備段階と考えているようだ。若い時にしっかりと学問や体験の蓄積をしておかないと、後になって困る。だから、若いうちから遊びほうけて注3いてはいけない。と、結局は、若者に自制と禁欲注4を呼びかけているのである。Ni mo kakawarazu, doushite wakai jidai dake ga tokubetsushi sareru no ka!? Watashi ni wa fushigi de aru. Omou ni, hitobito wa wakai jidai o junbi dankai to kangaete iru you da. Wakai toki ni shikkari to gakumon ya taiken no chikuseki o shite okanai to, ato ni natte komaru. Dakara, wakai uchi kara asobihouketeite wa ikenai. To, kekkyoku wa, wakamono ni jisei to kinyoku o yobikakete iru no de aru.

でも、わたしは、②それはまちがいだと思う。若い時代に、若い時代に特有の人生の喜び・悲しみを体験しておかないと、中年や老年になって、その段階での人生の喜び・悲しみが味わえない。若い時代は決して準備段階ではない。若者はそのことを銘記すべき注5である。Demo, watashi wa, ② sore wa machigai da to omou. Wakai jidai ni, wakai jidai ni tokuyuu no jinsei no yorokobi, kanashimi o taiken shite okanai to, chuunen ya rounen ni natte, sono dankai de no jinsei no yorokobi, kanashimi ga ajiwaenai. Wakai jidai wa kesshite junbi dankai de wa nai. Wakamono wa sono koto o meiki subeki de aru.

注1 皮肉屋：a sarcastic person　注2 ひがむ：to be jealous　注3 遊びほうける：to play around and not work　注4 自制と禁欲：self-control and abstinence　注5 銘記する：to engrave (in one's mind) — （ひろさちや『まんだら人生論（下）』読売新聞社）(Hiro Sachiya, "Mandara Jinsei-ron (Ge)," Yomiuri Shinbunsha)

EN: "Youth only comes once" — so the saying goes, meaning: treasure your youth. Fair enough. But I, a sarcastic sort, want to push back on this. It's true youth only happens once — but middle age and old age each only happen once too. We should treasure youth, but we should equally treasure middle age, and we must treasure old age. There's no need to single out youth alone as special. I myself recently turned fifty-three — by old terminology, already a proper "old person." I'm not saying this out of envy: I believe old age has its own goodness. Each stage of life has its own distinct flavor, and I believe we should live tasting the joys and sorrows unique to each stage. So why is youth alone singled out? It puzzles me. I suspect people think of youth as a "preparation stage" — if you don't build up learning and experience properly while young, you'll struggle later. So, don't fritter your youth away playing. In the end, that's just calling on young people for self-restraint and abstinence. But I think that's a mistake. If you don't experience the joys and sorrows unique to youth while you're young, you won't be able to taste the joys and sorrows of middle age or old age when you get there either. Youth is never a mere preparation stage. Young people should keep that firmly in mind.
HI: "जवानी दोबारा नहीं आती" — यानी अपनी जवानी को क़ीमती समझो। ठीक है। पर मैं, एक व्यंग्यकार होने के नाते, इस कहावत का विरोध करना चाहता हूँ। सच है कि जवानी एक ही बार आती है — पर अधेड़ उम्र और बुढ़ापा भी एक-एक बार ही आते हैं। हमें जवानी को क़ीमती समझना चाहिए, पर उतना ही अधेड़ उम्र को और बुढ़ापे को भी समझना चाहिए। सिर्फ़ जवानी को ख़ास मानने की ज़रूरत नहीं। मैं ख़ुद हाल ही में तिरपन साल का हुआ हूँ — पुराने हिसाब से तो अब भला-चंगा "बुज़ुर्ग" हूँ। मैं ईर्ष्या से यह नहीं कह रहा — मेरा मानना है कि बुढ़ापे की अपनी अच्छाई है। जीवन के हर पड़ाव का अपना अलग स्वाद होता है, और हमें हर पड़ाव के अपने ख़ास सुख-दुख चखते हुए जीना चाहिए। तो फिर सिर्फ़ जवानी को ही ख़ास क्यों माना जाता है? यह मेरे लिए अजीब है। मुझे लगता है लोग जवानी को "तैयारी का दौर" मानते हैं — अगर जवानी में ठीक से पढ़ाई और अनुभव इकट्ठा न करो, तो बाद में मुश्किल होगी। इसलिए जवानी में खेल-कूद में डूबे मत रहो। आख़िर में यह बात युवाओं से सिर्फ़ आत्म-संयम और त्याग की माँग करने जैसी है। पर मुझे लगता है यह ग़लत है। अगर जवानी में जवानी के ख़ास सुख-दुख न अनुभव किए, तो अधेड़ उम्र या बुढ़ापे में उस पड़ाव के सुख-दुख भी नहीं चखे जा सकेंगे। जवानी कभी भी सिर्फ़ तैयारी का दौर नहीं होती। युवाओं को यह बात अच्छी तरह याद रखनी चाहिए।
GU: "જુવાની ફરી નથી આવતી" — એટલે કે પોતાની જુવાનીને કીમતી ગણો. બરાબર છે. પણ હું, એક કટાક્ષકાર હોવાના નાતે, આ કહેવત સામે વિરોધ કરવા માંગું છું. સાચું છે કે જુવાની એક જ વાર આવે છે — પણ આધેડ ઉંમર અને ઘડપણ પણ એક-એક વાર જ આવે છે. આપણે જુવાનીને કીમતી ગણવી જોઈએ, પણ એટલી જ આધેડ ઉંમર અને ઘડપણને પણ ગણવી જોઈએ. ફક્ત જુવાનીને જ ખાસ ગણવાની જરૂર નથી. હું પોતે તાજેતરમાં ત્રેપન વર્ષનો થયો છું — જૂના હિસાબે તો હવે ભલો-ચંગો "વૃદ્ધ" છું. હું ઈર્ષ્યાથી આ નથી કહી રહ્યો — મારું માનવું છે કે ઘડપણની પોતાની સારપ છે. જીવનના દરેક તબક્કાનો પોતાનો અલગ સ્વાદ હોય છે, અને આપણે દરેક તબક્કાના પોતાના ખાસ સુખ-દુઃખ ચાખતાં જીવવું જોઈએ. તો પછી ફક્ત જુવાનીને જ કેમ ખાસ ગણવામાં આવે છે? આ મને વિચિત્ર લાગે છે. મને લાગે છે લોકો જુવાનીને "તૈયારીનો તબક્કો" ગણે છે — જો જુવાનીમાં બરાબર ભણતર અને અનુભવ ભેગો ન કરો, તો પછીથી મુશ્કેલી પડશે. એટલે જુવાનીમાં રમતગમતમાં ડૂબેલા ન રહો. છેવટે આ વાત યુવાનો પાસેથી ફક્ત આત્મ-સંયમ અને ત્યાગની માંગ કરવા જેવી છે. પણ મને લાગે છે આ ખોટું છે. જો જુવાનીમાં જુવાનીના ખાસ સુખ-દુઃખ અનુભવ્યા નહીં, તો આધેડ ઉંમર કે ઘડપણમાં એ તબક્કાના સુખ-દુઃખ પણ ચાખી નહીં શકાય. જુવાની ક્યારેય ફક્ત તૈયારીનો તબક્કો નથી હોતી. યુવાનોએ આ વાત સારી રીતે યાદ રાખવી જોઈએ.
Q1

問1　①若い時代だけを特別視する必要はないとあるが、それはなぜか。Toi 1: ① Wakai jidai dake o tokubetsushi suru hitsuyou wa nai to aru ga, sore wa naze ka.

OptionJapaneseEnglish
1老年のほうが人生のよさがあるから。Rounen no hou ga jinsei no yosa ga aru kara.Because old age has more of life's goodness.
2自分が皮肉屋だから。Jibun ga hinikuya da kara.Because he himself is a sarcastic person.
3若い時代と同様に、中年、老年も大事だから。Wakai jidai to douyou ni, chuunen, rounen mo daiji da kara.Because, just like youth, middle age and old age matter too.
4若いときは一度しかないから。Wakai toki wa ichido shika nai kara.Because youth only happens once.

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問1: 3): The booklet flags the second paragraph's second line as the key: "たしかに若い時は一度しかないが、中年だって、老年だって一度しかないのである" ("youth only happens once, but middle age and old age each only happen once too") — directly building to "so there's no need to single out youth," matching option 3. Option 4 restates the premise the author is arguing against, not his reason; option 1 overclaims (he never says old age is "better," only that it has its own goodness); option 2 confuses his self-description with his actual reasoning.

Q2

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問2: 4): The booklet's hint is important here: "筆者の意見であり、世間の常識ではない" ("this is the writer's own opinion, not common social wisdom") — a warning not to pick option 2, which sounds like ordinary good advice ("build up learning and experience") rather than the specific claim the author calls "a mistake." What the author actually rejects is the surrounding demand that young people restrain and deny themselves (自制と禁欲) in the name of "preparation" — that's option 4. Option 1 describes the opposite extreme (idling), which the author never defends; option 3 restates his own thesis, not the "mistake" he's attacking.
