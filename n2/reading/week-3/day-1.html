1 Reading Strategy

Skill focus

✿文末表現に注意！Bunmatsu hyougen ni chuui! — Be careful with words/phrases at the end of the sentence!

In an 意見文 (opinion piece), the writer's real stance often hides in the last few characters of a sentence, not in its content words. This book highlights two families of sentence-ending patterns to watch for:

PatternEquivalentWhat it signals
〜一方だ~ippou da／〜つつある~tsutsu aru＝〜の状態だ= ~no joutai daIndicates the present, ongoing condition ("it is in the state of ~ing")
〜に至る~ni itaru／〜次第だ~shidai da／〜始末だ~shimatsu da＝〜という結果になった= ~to iu kekka ni nattaIndicates a resulting consequence — 始末だ in particular usually marks a regrettable outcome

The book's mascot gives two example sentences:

ボクは日本語をマスターしつつある！Boku wa nihongo o masutaa shitsutsu aru! — "I'm in the process of mastering Japanese!"

ボクは遊びすぎて試験に落ちる始末だ……Boku wa asobisugite shiken ni ochiru shimatsu da... — "I played around too much, and it ended up with me failing the exam..."

練習 (Renshuu — practice: read the dialogue, then judge each statement)

Dialogue

男の人：また病院のミスで患者が死んだんだって。看護師注1が患者に間違った薬を点滴注2したらしいよ。Otoko no hito: Mata byouin no misu de kanja ga shindan datte. Kangoshi ga kanja ni machigatta kusuri o tenteki shita rashii yo.

女の人：え？薬の確認は当然するでしょう？Onna no hito: E? Kusuri no kakunin wa touzen suru deshou?

男の人：確認はしたけど患者の名前が間違って書かれていたんだって。Otoko no hito: Kakunin wa shita kedo kanja no namae ga machigatte kakarete itan datte.

女の人：信じられない。そんなケアレスミス注3で人の命が奪われる注4なんて……。Onna no hito: Shinjirarenai. Sonna kearesu misu de hito no inochi ga ubawareru nante...

男の人：ああ、ひどいよね。ニュースにならないミスはもっともっとあるということなんだろうね。Otoko no hito: Aa, hidoi yo ne. Nyuusu ni naranai misu wa motto motto aru to iu koto nan darou ne.

注1 看護師：a nurse　注2 点滴：an intravenous drip　注3 ケアレスミス：a careless mistake　注4 命が奪われる：to be killed

EN: Man: "I heard a patient died again because of a hospital mistake. Apparently a nurse gave a patient the wrong IV drip." Woman: "What? Surely they check the medicine, don't they?" Man: "They did check, but apparently the patient's name had been written down wrong." Woman: "I can't believe it — that a person's life could be taken by a careless mistake like that..." Man: "Yeah, it's awful. There must be even more mistakes than this that never make the news."
HI: पुरुष: "फिर से अस्पताल की ग़लती से एक मरीज़ की मौत हो गई। लगता है नर्स ने मरीज़ को ग़लत दवा की ड्रिप दे दी।" महिला: "क्या? दवा की जाँच तो ज़रूर करते होंगे?" पुरुष: "जाँच तो की, पर मरीज़ का नाम ग़लत लिखा हुआ था।" महिला: "यक़ीन नहीं होता — ऐसी लापरवाही की ग़लती से किसी की जान चली जाए..." पुरुष: "हाँ, बहुत बुरा है। जो ग़लतियाँ ख़बर नहीं बनतीं, वो तो और भी ज़्यादा होंगी।"
GU: પુરુષ: "ફરી હોસ્પિટલની ભૂલથી એક દર્દીનું મૃત્યુ થયું. લાગે છે નર્સે દર્દીને ખોટી દવાની ડ્રિપ આપી દીધી." સ્ત્રી: "શું? દવાની ચકાસણી તો જરૂર કરતા હશે ને?" પુરુષ: "ચકાસણી તો કરી, પણ દર્દીનું નામ ખોટું લખાયેલું હતું." સ્ત્રી: "વિશ્વાસ નથી થતો — આવી બેદરકારીભરી ભૂલથી કોઈનો જીવ જતો રહે..." પુરુષ: "હા, ખૂબ ખરાબ છે. જે ભૂલો સમાચાર નથી બનતી, એ તો હજુ વધારે હશે."
#StatementEnglishTrue / False
1患者が死んだのは、病院のせいである。Kanja ga shinda no wa, byouin no sei de aru.The patient's death is the hospital's fault.True
2病院の不注意で、患者が違う薬を飲んでしまった。Byouin no fuchuui de, kanja ga chigau kusuri o nonde shimatta.Due to the hospital's carelessness, the patient drank the wrong medicine.False
3看護師は薬の確認をしなかった。Kangoshi wa kusuri no kakunin o shinakatta.The nurse didn't check the medicine at all.False
4女の人は看護師たちが薬の名前を知らなかったことが信じられない。Onna no hito wa kangoshi-tachi ga kusuri no namae o shiranakatta koto ga shinjirarenai.The woman can't believe the nurses didn't know the medicine's name.False
5男の人は、病院の不注意はたくさんあると思っている。Otoko no hito wa, byouin no fuchuui wa takusan aru to omotte iru.The man believes the hospital's carelessness happens a lot (beyond just this case).True

Why: #2 misdescribes the incident — the wrong medicine was given by IV drip (点滴), not drunk (飲んだ), and the root problem was a mislabeled name, not the drug itself. #3 is wrong because the nurse did check (確認はした) — the label itself was wrong. #4 misreads what shocks the woman: it's that a careless mistake could cost a life, not that the nurses were ignorant of a drug's name. Answer confirmed in-book at the bottom of p.45: "（左ページの答え→1・5）".

2 Passage Questions

問題 from p.45: a newspaper-style opinion piece about rising medical errors, followed by 2 comprehension questions with 4 options each.

Opinion piece

増え続ける医療ミスFue-tsuzukeru iryou misu — "Ever-Increasing Medical Errors"

ニュースを見たとたん、思わず「またか！」と声を上げてしまった。病院のケアレスミスで、また一人の患者が死に至った。Nyuusu o mita totan, omowazu "mata ka!" to koe o agete shimatta. Byouin no kearesu misu de, mata hitori no kanja ga shi ni itatta.

今回のミスは、似た名前の別人に間違った薬を点滴してしまったというものである*。ちょっと気をつけて①確認すれば、簡単に避けられるようなミスだ。責任者である院長が、どんなに謝っても失われた命は二度と帰らない。Konkai no misu wa, nita namae no betsujin ni machigatta kusuri o tenteki shite shimatta to iu mono de aru. Chotto ki o tsukete kakunin sureba, kantan ni sakerareru you na misu da. Sekininsha de aru inchou ga, donna ni ayamatte mo ushinawareta inochi wa nido to kaeranai.

最近、このような事故が増える一方である。いや、これは事故ではなく、「殺人」である。よく「人間は間違う動物だ」などと言うが、大切な命を預かる**病院で、ケアレスミスは許されないことだ。すべての病院関係者はこのことを②人ごととはせずに重く受け止め注2、自分の病院のシステム注1を見直す努力をしてほしい。Saikin, kono you na jiko ga fueru ippou de aru. Iya, kore wa jiko de wa naku, "satsujin" de aru. Yoku "ningen wa machigau doubutsu da" nado to iu ga, taisetsu na inochi o azukaru byouin de, kearesu misu wa yurusarenai koto da. Subete no byouin kankeisha wa kono koto o hitogoto to wa sezu ni omoku uketome, jibun no byouin no shisutemu o minaosu doryoku o shite hoshii.

システム注1の見直しを！Shisutemu no minaoshi o! — "Overhaul the system!"

注1 システム：a system　注2 重く受け止める：to take a matter very seriously　* the mistake this time involved an IV drip being mistakenly given to a patient with a similar name　** to be responsible for saving people's lives

EN: The moment I saw the news, I couldn't help exclaiming, "Again?!" Another patient died due to a hospital's careless mistake. This time, the mistake was that a different patient with a similar name was mistakenly given the wrong IV drip. It's the kind of mistake that could easily be avoided with a bit of care and checking. No matter how much the director in charge apologizes, a lost life never comes back. Lately, accidents like this keep increasing. No — this isn't an "accident," it's "murder." People often say "humans are creatures that make mistakes," but at a hospital entrusted with precious lives, careless mistakes are unforgivable. I want every single person connected to a hospital to take this seriously instead of treating it as someone else's problem, and to make the effort to review their own hospital's systems. Overhaul the system!
HI: ख़बर देखते ही मेरे मुँह से निकल पड़ा, "फिर से?!" अस्पताल की लापरवाही की ग़लती से एक और मरीज़ की जान चली गई। इस बार की ग़लती यह थी कि मिलते-जुलते नाम वाले किसी और मरीज़ को ग़लत दवा की ड्रिप दे दी गई। थोड़ी सावधानी और जाँच से आसानी से टाली जा सकने वाली ग़लती है यह। ज़िम्मेदार निदेशक चाहे जितनी माफ़ी माँग ले, गई हुई जान वापस नहीं आती। हाल ही में ऐसी दुर्घटनाएँ बढ़ती ही जा रही हैं। नहीं, यह दुर्घटना नहीं, "हत्या" है। अक्सर कहा जाता है "इंसान ग़लती करने वाला जीव है", लेकिन क़ीमती जानों की ज़िम्मेदारी सँभालने वाले अस्पताल में लापरवाही की ग़लती माफ़ी के लायक़ नहीं है। मैं चाहती हूँ कि अस्पताल से जुड़ा हर व्यक्ति इसे "मुझसे कोई मतलब नहीं" समझने की बजाय गंभीरता से ले, और अपने अस्पताल की व्यवस्था को फिर से जाँचने की कोशिश करे। व्यवस्था की समीक्षा हो!
GU: સમાચાર જોતાં જ મોઢામાંથી નીકળી ગયું, "ફરી?!" હોસ્પિટલની બેદરકારીભરી ભૂલથી વધુ એક દર્દીનો જીવ ગયો. આ વખતની ભૂલ એ હતી કે મળતા નામવાળા બીજા દર્દીને ખોટી દવાની ડ્રિપ આપી દેવાઈ. થોડી કાળજી અને ચકાસણીથી સહેલાઈથી ટાળી શકાય તેવી ભૂલ છે આ. જવાબદાર નિયામક ગમે તેટલી માફી માંગે, ગયેલો જીવ પાછો નથી આવતો. તાજેતરમાં આવા બનાવો વધતા જ જાય છે. ના, આ અકસ્માત નથી, "હત્યા" છે. ઘણીવાર કહેવાય છે "માણસ ભૂલ કરનારું પ્રાણી છે", પણ કીમતી જીવોની જવાબદારી સંભાળતી હોસ્પિટલમાં બેદરકારીભરી ભૂલ માફ કરી શકાય તેમ નથી. હું ઇચ્છું છું કે હોસ્પિટલ સાથે જોડાયેલ દરેક વ્યક્તિ આને "મારે શું લેવાદેવા" સમજવાને બદલે ગંભીરતાથી લે, અને પોતાની હોસ્પિટલની વ્યવસ્થા ફરી તપાસવાનો પ્રયત્ન કરે. વ્યવસ્થાની સમીક્ષા થાય!
問1

①確認すればとあるが、何を確認するのか。① Kakunin sureba to aru ga, nani o kakunin suru no ka.

EN: It says "①if [they] check" — check what?
HI: "①अगर जाँच करें" लिखा है — क्या जाँचें?
GU: "①જો ચકાસણી કરે" લખ્યું છે — શું ચકાસવાનું?
OptionJapaneseEnglishHindiGujarati
1ケアレスミスkearesu misua careless mistakeलापरवाही की ग़लतीબેદરકારીભરી ભૂલ
2患者の状態kanja no joutaithe patient's conditionमरीज़ की हालतદર્દીની સ્થિતિ

Why: The sentence right before ①確認すれば explains exactly what went wrong: "似た名前の別人に間違った薬を点滴してしまった" — the drip itself wasn't wrong in dosage or type, the patient identification was wrong because of a similar name. So what "checking carefully" would have caught is the patient's name, not the dose, condition, or the general idea of a careless mistake. 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat it testsTrap to avoid
練習 Match each statement to one exact clause Whether a statement swaps a detail (drip vs. drink) or misplaces the blame (drug name vs. patient name) The dialogue never says the patient "drank" anything, and the nurse did check — she just checked a mislabeled name.
問1 Read the sentence immediately before the underline Pinpointing exactly what "checking" would have caught 点滴の量 (dose) and 患者の状態 (condition) sound like plausible things a nurse checks in general — but this passage's error was specifically about the name.
問2 Translate a set phrase (人ごと) literally, then match register Recognizing 人ごと as "someone else's business," feeding into the writer's call to action Don't let 人 in 人ごと pull you toward an answer about "humans" or "the patient" — the phrase is about ownership/responsibility, not about who is human.
