1 Reading Strategy

Key idea

指示語に注意！②　— 指示語の内容が後に来るものに注意！Shijigo ni chuui! Ni — Shijigo no naiyou ga ato ni kuru mono ni chuui!

Pay attention to demonstratives (この／その／あの, これ／それ／あれ, etc.) whose content comes AFTER them, not before — the opposite of the usual "point backward" habit. The book gives this example:

「この人を知りませんか。」僕はいきなり、女性の写真を目の前に突きつけられた。"Kono hito o shirimasen ka." Boku wa ikinari, josei no shashin o me no mae ni tsukitsukerareta.
「私はこういう者です。」そう言うと男は警察手帳を見せた。刑事だった。"Watashi wa kou iu mono desu." Sou iu to otoko wa keisatsu techou o miseta. Keiji datta.
EN: "Do you know this person?" — I was suddenly shown a photo of a woman thrust in front of my face. ("この人" points forward to "女性"/the photo, revealed right after.) / "I'm someone like this." Saying that, the man showed his police ID. He was a detective. ("こういう者" points forward to "刑事," revealed right after.)
HI: इस/उस जैसे संकेत-शब्द कई बार आगे आने वाली बात की ओर इशारा करते हैं, पीछे की बात की ओर नहीं — यानी असली जवाब वाक्य के बाद में आता है। GU: આ/તે જેવા સંકેત-શબ્દો ઘણી વાર આગળ આવનારી વાત તરફ ઈશારો કરે છે, પાછળની વાત તરફ નહીં — એટલે કે ખરો જવાબ વાક્ય પછી આવે છે.

芥川龍之介『少年』〈会話部分のみ抜粋〉(Akutagawa Ryuunosuke, "Shounen" — kaiwa bubun nomi bassui)

宣教師注1：お嬢さんはおいくつですか？Senkyoushi: Ojou-san wa o-ikutsu desu ka?

少女：あたし？　あたしは来年12。Shoujo: Atashi? Atashi wa rainen juuni.

少女：きょう？　きょうはもう家へ帰るところなの。Shoujo: Kyou? Kyou wa mou ie e kaeru tokoro na no.

宣教師：きょうは何日だかご存知ですか？Senkyoushi: Kyou wa nan-nichi da ka go-zonji desu ka?

少女：12月25日でしょう。Shoujo: Juuni-gatsu nijuugo-nichi deshou.

宣教師：ええ、12月25日です。12月25日は何の日ですか？　お嬢さん、あなたはご存知ですか？Senkyoushi: Ee, juuni-gatsu nijuugo-nichi desu. Juuni-gatsu nijuugo-nichi wa nan no hi desu ka? Ojou-san, anata wa go-zonji desu ka?

少女：ええ、それは知っているわ。Shoujo: Ee, sore wa shitte iru wa.

宣教師：ではきょうは何の日ですか？　言ってごらんなさい注2。Senkyoushi: Dewa kyou wa nan no hi desu ka? Itte goran nasai.

少女：きょうはあたしのお誕生日。Shoujo: Kyou wa atashi no o-tanjoubi.

注1 宣教師：a priest 　·　注2 言ってごらんなさい：言ってみなさい Chuu 2: itte goran nasai: itte minasai — "go on, try saying it" 　·　出典: 芥川龍之介『少年』(Akutagawa Ryuunosuke, "Shounen")

#StatementTrue/False
1宣教師と少女はとても親しい。Senkyoushi to shoujo wa totemo shitashii. — The priest and the girl are very close.False
2少女はこれから教会へ行く。Shoujo wa kore kara kyoukai e iku. — The girl is about to go to church.False
3今日はクリスマスである。Kyou wa kurisumasu de aru. — Today is Christmas.True
4少女は今日が何日か知らなかった。Shoujo wa kyou ga nan-nichi ka shiranakatta. — The girl didn't know what day it was.False
5宣教師は少女とクリスマスについて話したかった。Senkyoushi wa shoujo to kurisumasu ni tsuite hanashitakatta. — The priest wanted to talk with the girl about Christmas.True

Why: The dialogue reads like two strangers making polite small talk on a vehicle (see the fuller context in the 問題 below) — nothing suggests closeness, so #1 is false. The girl says she's "on my way home," not heading anywhere new like a church, so #2 is false. December 25th is stated directly and confirmed as widely known as a special day — #3 is true (this is Christmas Day). The girl correctly names the date immediately ("12月25日でしょう"), so #4 (didn't know the date) is false. The priest's whole line of questioning — pressing "what day is it," "what is December 25th," "go on, say it" — is clearly steering toward getting the girl to say "Christmas," so #5 is true, even though her actual answer ("my birthday") surprises him.

Why (booklet-confirmed, 問2: 3): The passage's own footnote states it directly: "Yasukichi found all the beauty of Christmas in his (the priest's) happiness-filled grey eyes." The text describes the priest's eyes shining with "笑い涙" (tears of laughter) — not disappointment — right after the girl's unexpected "my birthday" answer, and Yasukichi reads that expression as beautiful and joyful, matching option 3. Nothing suggests the girl is unintelligent (1) or that Yasukichi pities the priest (2) — the priest is depicted as delighted, not let down. Option 4 introduces a cynical motive about religious conversion that the warm, tearful description doesn't support.
