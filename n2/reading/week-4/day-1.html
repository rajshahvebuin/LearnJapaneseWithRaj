1 Reading Strategy

Skill focus

✿答えが書いてある部分を見つけようKotae ga kaite aru bubun o mitsukeyou — Let's focus on locating where the answer is written!

例えば問いが次のような場合Tatoeba toi ga tsugi no you na baai — For example, when the question looks like this:

Question patternWhere the answer usually sits nearby
○○とは何のことか。○○ to wa nan no koto ka. — "What does ○○ refer to?"……。それは、○○のようなものであった。...... Sore wa, ○○ no you na mono de atta. — "...It was something like ○○."
△△したのはいつか。△△ shita no wa itsu ka. — "When did △△ happen?"……ころが△△したといえるのかもしれない。...... koro ga △△ shita to ieru no kamoshirenai. — "...it might be fair to say that △△ happened around that time."
何のために××したのか。Nan no tame ni ×× shita no ka. — "What purpose was ×× done for?"……ためにしか××しなかった。...... tame ni shika ×× shinakatta. — "...did ×× only for the purpose of ......"

問いを先に読むと、早く答えが見付けられるね！Toi o saki ni yomu to, hayaku kotae ga mitsukerareru ne! — "If you read the question first, you can find the answer faster!"

問いの○○、△△、××を探すと、近くに答えの「……」が見つかります。Toi no maru-maru, sankaku-sankaku, batsu-batsu o sagasu to, chikaku ni kotae no "......" ga mitsukarimasu. — "If you search for the question's ○○/△△/×× wording in the passage, you'll find the answer '......' right nearby."

HI: पहले सवाल पढ़ लें, फिर पाठ में उसी शब्द (○○, △△, ××) को खोजें — जवाब आमतौर पर उसके ठीक पास लिखा होता है। GU: પહેલાં પ્રશ્ન વાંચી લો, પછી લખાણમાં એ જ શબ્દ (○○, △△, ××) શોધો — જવાબ સામાન્ય રીતે તેની બરાબર નજીક લખેલો હોય છે.

練習 (Renshuu — practice: read the dialogue, then judge each statement)

Dialogue

娘：最近、本を読まなくなったなあ、私。Musume: Saikin, hon o yomanaku natta naa, watashi.

母：え？いつも読んでいるじゃないの。Haha: E? Itsumo yonde iru ja nai no.

娘：ああ、仕事関係の本ばかりなの。そういう、必要にせまられて注っていうのは違うでしょ。中学のころからかな、こんなふうになったのは。レポート書くためとか。子どものころは本当にただ本を読んでいたのにね。楽しかったなあ……。Musume: Aa, shigoto kankei no hon bakari na no. Sou iu, hitsuyou ni semararete (chuu) tte iu no wa chigau desho. Chuugaku no koro kara ka na, konna fuu ni natta no wa. Repooto kaku tame to ka. Kodomo no koro wa hontou ni tada hon o yonde ita no ni ne. Tanoshikatta naa......

母：そうね、うれしそうに、よく図書館に通っていたわね。あんまり買ってやれなかったしね。Haha: Sou ne, ureshisou ni, yoku toshokan ni kayotte ita wa ne. Anmari katte yarenakatta shi ne.

注 必要にせまられて：out of necessity Chuu: hitsuyou ni semararete

EN: Daughter: "Lately I've stopped reading books, huh." Mother: "Eh? You're always reading, aren't you?" Daughter: "Ah, it's just work-related books. That kind of 'forced by necessity' reading is different, isn't it. I think it's been like this since junior high — for writing reports and things. When I was a kid I really just read books for their own sake. That was fun..." Mother: "That's right, you happily used to go to the library a lot. We couldn't buy you that many books, either."
HI: बेटी: "हाल ही में मैंने किताबें पढ़ना बंद कर दिया है, माँ।" माँ: "अरे? तुम तो हमेशा पढ़ती रहती हो, है ना?" बेटी: "हाँ, बस काम से जुड़ी किताबें ही होती हैं। वो 'मजबूरी में पढ़ना' अलग बात है ना। शायद मिडिल स्कूल से ही ऐसा होने लगा — रिपोर्ट लिखने के लिए वगैरह। बचपन में तो मैं सच में सिर्फ़ किताबें पढ़ती थी, अपने मज़े के लिए। वो मज़ा आता था..." माँ: "हाँ, तुम खुश होकर पुस्तकालय जाया करती थी। हम तुम्हें ज़्यादा किताबें ख़रीद कर भी नहीं दे पाते थे।"
GU: દીકરી: "તાજેતરમાં મેં પુસ્તકો વાંચવાનું બંધ કરી દીધું છે, મા." મા: "અરે? તું તો હંમેશાં વાંચતી જ હોય છે ને?" દીકરી: "હા, બસ કામને લગતાં પુસ્તકો જ હોય છે. એ 'મજબૂરીથી વાંચવું' જુદી વાત છે ને. કદાચ મિડલ સ્કૂલથી જ આવું થવા લાગ્યું — રિપોર્ટ લખવા માટે વગેરે. બાળપણમાં તો હું ખરેખર ફક્ત મારી મજા માટે પુસ્તકો વાંચતી હતી. એ મજા આવતી હતી..." મા: "હા, તું ખુશ થઈને પુસ્તકાલય જતી હતી. અમે તને વધારે પુસ્તકો ખરીદી પણ નહોતાં આપી શકતાં."
#StatementEnglishTrue / False
1娘は大人になってから本を読んでいない。Musume wa otona ni natte kara hon o yonde inai.The daughter hasn't read books since becoming an adult.False
2母は娘が子どものころ、よく本を買ってやった。Haha wa musume ga kodomo no koro, yoku hon o katte yatta.The mother often bought books for her daughter as a child.False
3娘は中学のころ、よく好きな本を読んで楽しんだ。Musume wa chuugaku no koro, yoku suki na hon o yonde tanoshinda.In junior high, the daughter often enjoyed reading books she liked.False
4母は、娘が子どものころ、図書館で働いていた。Haha wa, musume ga kodomo no koro, toshokan de hataraite ita.The mother worked at the library when her daughter was a child.False
5娘は子どものころの読書が楽しかったと思っている。Musume wa kodomo no koro no dokusho ga tanoshikatta to omotte iru.The daughter thinks that her childhood reading was enjoyable.True

Why: #1 is wrong — she still reads, just only work-related books. #2 is wrong — the mother explicitly says she "couldn't buy her many books" (あんまり買ってやれなかった). #3 is wrong — junior high is exactly when reading stopped being for pleasure and became report-driven, per the daughter's own words. #4 is wrong — "図書館に通っていた" describes the daughter going to the library, not the mother working there. Only #5 matches "楽しかったなあ" (that was fun). Answer confirmed in-book at the bottom of p.61: "（左ページの答え→5）".

2 Passage Questions

問題 from p.61: a short essay about how the writer's relationship with reading changed from childhood to adulthood, followed by 2 comprehension questions with 4 options each.

Essay

子どものころ、よく図書館へ行って本を借りたものだ。自分では楽しんで本を読んでいるという意識はなかった。読み終えた本を返しに行くと、そこに当然のことだが未知の本があり、それをまた借りる、ということを繰り返していたのだ。中学に入学したころからは、学校のレポート提出などのためにしか本を読まなくなった。それは一種の作業のようなものであった。社会人になってから、読みたい本ぐらいはいくらでも買えるようになったが、仕事がらみ注で必要にせまられてのことが多い。今思うと、図書館通いをしていたあのころが、ほんとうに読書を楽しんでいたと言えるのかもしれない。Kodomo no koro, yoku toshokan e itte hon o karita mono da. Jibun de wa tanoshinde hon o yonde iru to iu ishiki wa nakatta. Yomioeta hon o kaeshi ni iku to, soko ni touzen no koto da ga michi no hon ga ari, sore o mata kariru, to iu koto o kurikaeshite ita no da. Chuugaku ni nyuugaku shita koro kara wa, gakkou no repooto teishutsu nado no tame ni shika hon o yomanaku natta. Sore wa isshu no sagyou no you na mono de atta. Shakaijin ni natte kara, yomitai hon gurai wa ikura demo kaeru you ni natta ga, shigoto-garami (chuu) de hitsuyou ni semararete no koto ga ooi. Ima omou to, toshokan-gayoi o shite ita ano koro ga, hontou ni dokusho o tanoshinde ita to ieru no kamoshirenai.

注 仕事がらみ：work related Chuu: shigoto-garami — "work related"

EN: As a child, I often went to the library and borrowed books. I wasn't even conscious that I was enjoying the reading itself. When I went to return a book I'd finished, there would naturally be unfamiliar books there, and I'd borrow one of those again — I kept repeating that cycle. From around the time I started junior high, I stopped reading books except for things like submitting school reports. That became a kind of chore. Since becoming a working adult, I've become able to buy as many books as I want, but more often than not it's because I'm forced to by work-related necessity. Thinking about it now, maybe it was actually back in those days when I used to go to the library that I was truly enjoying reading.
HI: बचपन में, मैं अक्सर पुस्तकालय जाकर किताबें उधार लेता था। मुझे यह एहसास भी नहीं था कि मैं पढ़ने का आनंद ले रहा हूँ। पढ़ी हुई किताब वापस करने जाता तो वहाँ स्वाभाविक रूप से कुछ अनजानी किताबें होतीं, और मैं फिर उनमें से एक उधार ले लेता — यही सिलसिला चलता रहता। मिडिल स्कूल में दाख़िले के आसपास से, मैंने स्कूल की रिपोर्ट जमा करने जैसी ज़रूरतों के अलावा किताबें पढ़ना बंद कर दिया। वह एक तरह का काम-सा बन गया। कामकाजी बनने के बाद, अब मैं जितनी चाहूँ उतनी किताबें ख़रीद सकता हूँ, लेकिन ज़्यादातर काम से जुड़ी मजबूरी के कारण। अब सोचता हूँ तो शायद वही दिन, जब मैं पुस्तकालय जाया करता था, वही असली मायने में पढ़ने का आनंद लेने के दिन थे।
GU: બાળપણમાં, હું ઘણીવાર પુસ્તકાલયમાં જઈને પુસ્તકો ઉછીના લેતો હતો. મને એ પણ ખ્યાલ નહોતો કે હું વાંચવાનો આનંદ માણી રહ્યો છું. વાંચેલું પુસ્તક પાછું આપવા જાઉં ત્યારે ત્યાં સ્વાભાવિક રીતે કેટલાંક અજાણ્યાં પુસ્તકો હોય, અને હું ફરી તેમાંથી એક ઉછીનું લઈ લેતો — આ સિલસિલો ચાલતો રહેતો. મિડલ સ્કૂલમાં દાખલ થયા પછીથી, મેં શાળાનો રિપોર્ટ સુપરત કરવા જેવી જરૂરિયાતો સિવાય પુસ્તકો વાંચવાનું બંધ કરી દીધું. તે એક પ્રકારનું કામ જેવું બની ગયું. નોકરી કરતો થયો ત્યારથી, હવે હું ઇચ્છું તેટલાં પુસ્તકો ખરીદી શકું છું, પણ મોટેભાગે કામને લગતી મજબૂરીને કારણે. હવે વિચારું છું તો કદાચ એ જ દિવસો, જ્યારે હું પુસ્તકાલયમાં જતો હતો, એ જ ખરેખર વાંચનનો આનંદ માણવાના દિવસો હતા.

Why: "一種の作業のようなもの" directly follows the sentence "学校のレポート提出などのためにしか本を読まなくなった" (using this day's own strategy — the answer sits right next to the paraphrased wording). 3 Confusion Pairs / Nuance Notes

No vocabulary cluster today — instead compare which reading strategy unlocks each question:

QuestionStrategy usedWhat it testsTrap to avoid
練習 Match each statement to one exact clause Whether a statement inverts a detail from the dialogue The mother says she "couldn't buy many books" — the opposite of statement #2 — don't let matching keywords (本, 買う) substitute for checking polarity.
問1 ○○とは何のことか → find the paraphrase right next to it Whether you can locate "作業" pinned to the report-writing clause just before it 図書館 and レポート both appear near the underlined phrase — pick the clause immediately adjacent to the target wording, not just any nearby noun.
問2 Trace a demonstrative ("あのころ") to its explicit referent Recognizing that the essay names its own answer in the closing line Junior high and adulthood both involve reading, but the essay frames both as "forced" — don't confuse "reads a lot" with "enjoys reading."
